# The Tether's answer: how institutions reshape habitual thinking

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the five domain findings, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

R is the gap between habitual thinking without sustained institutional contact and habitual thinking after it. "Habitual" means the cheaply retrievable molecules a frame composes over without re-deriving them: what a situation affords, what counts as progress, what goes without saying. The question is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Four institution types, three mechanisms, one membrane.

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

1. **An institution is a maintained habitat with a seat, not a belief in a head.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A rule that leaves contact with the ground unchanged changes the convention, not the thinking. `general`, triangulated.
2. **The person's closure moves through contact with a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Internal reflection alone cannot add a composeable atom. `general`, triangulated with Fig 8 :45.
3. **What crosses the membrane is a method and a head start, never a recording.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :34: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* Each person remakes what they receive, which is why the same institution yields different habits. `general`, triangulated with Fig 12 :104.
4. **Institutional agreement about shared meaning is the product, not a verdict about the world.** Fig 2 :41: *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. The two valences must not be swapped. `general`, triangulated with Fig 7 :35 and Fig 10 :47.
5. **Effects are per person and per habit, never averaged away.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A global average near zero with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). `general`, triangulated with Fig 3 :27.
6. **A habit reads as a part that survives its own use.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* An installed rule reads as a functional group (Fig 12 :123), which is how rule-following without adopting the assumption is possible. `specified`, single-source.
7. **The institution-to-habit mapping is a gap, not a silence.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. No figure line licenses "schools produce X, markets produce Y". A specific mapping would be a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test, and none was supplied. `gap` for the name.

These verdicts are not averaged. A school result cannot be pooled with a market result to make a stronger claim about institutions in general.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named institution causes any named cognitive change. That is the gap in §2.7.
- Not that all four institution types work by the same mechanism. They are a union with overlaps (Fig 9 :57).
- Not that agreement inside an institution is false or worthless. It is the product when the question is about shared meaning (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that an institution is a ground. Its measures are conventions or instruments; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).
- Not a causal-identification result. That is question COG-0082, a separate loop with a separate ground.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` here.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would refute it

The ground, not agreement. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a value *"cannot comment on the source"*, and an instrument that cannot distinguish cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. The spectrometer resolved the colour case, not an argument.

**Ground tests that would close the shape:**

1. **Contact test.** Hold contact constant while varying the institutional rule. If the habitual change persists, §2.1 is broken.
2. **Closure test.** Look for a frame whose composeable reach expands with no import from a differing closure. If found, §2.2 is broken.
3. **Round-trip test.** Measure `R_T` for a habit carried from one generation of a community to the next across changed landing conditions. A zero gap breaks §2.3.
4. **Per-slot test.** Compare a population average with a pre-registered per-person, per-habit reading. If the average locates the live slot as well, §2.5 is broken.
5. **Contrast test.** Select contrasting rooms against the residual and range each separately against a ground value. That settles the causal question left open in §2.7.

**Standing refuters** (written before the run, `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5): agent-only change with contact fixed; internal closure expansion; average that beats a per-slot reading; within-institution agreement that settles a world question; zero round-trip gap. Any one fires against the corresponding finding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing item is not a primitive but a discriminating instrument. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of what current instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. Prestige or consensus is not an instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name which institution produces which habit, and it does not claim a single mechanism. It says that an institution is a maintained habitat with a seat, so it works by changing what a person is in contact with; that the person's frame moves when it meets a frame whose closure differs, since internal reflection alone cannot add a new composeable term; that what passes down the individual-to-population boundary is a method and a head start rather than a recording, so each person remakes it; that agreement about shared meaning is the product of an institution and is not a verdict about the world; and that the effect must be read per person and per habit, never as a population average. A habitual way of thinking behaves like a part that survives its own use, which is why an installed rule can be followed without being believed. The framework returns that shape and five written refuters. It stops before naming the institution-to-habit mapping, because that would take a discriminating instrument, not a better story.
