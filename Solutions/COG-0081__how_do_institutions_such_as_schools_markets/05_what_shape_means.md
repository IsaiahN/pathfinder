## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question, the shape is:

> a maintained habitat, a differing closure, a directional crossing that carries a method and a head start, a per-slot residual, and a valence that separates shared meaning from a verdict about the world.

That is a shape. "Schooling produces X" is a name, and it is not licensed.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on the stop: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the bootstrap vocabulary came from institutional theory, developmental psychology and cultural evolution, not from the framework. The residual was stated in effect terms first; the figures were chosen second (Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question that means a contrast between institutions and a pre-registered, per-slot measure, with a value the ground returns independently.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode. An instrument that reads a value *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can yield the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: observing that people in an institution share a habit does not name the source. The same habit can come from habitat contact, from a selected population, from a shared prior, or from several at once. Naming the institution as the source from the shared reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating design, not a name.

## 4. Why the union must be split before it is searched

*Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

Fig 13 :136 says the same structurally: *"the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again"*. The source question bundles four institution types and at least three mechanisms. Searching it as one question would return a shape vague enough to fit anything, which is the projection failure Fig 9 :27 names: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I first wrote that a name is forbidden absolutely here. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Γ. The issue in this question is not a missing primitive. It is the absence of an instrument that can discriminate among sources that yield the same habit reading. The *Hard Problem* essay makes the same distinction: the answer is *"unreached until a better instrument exists"*, not unknowable.

So the correct statement is: naming a specific institution-to-habit mapping is **unreached at this budget**, and the honest verdict is `gap` for that mapping, not a ban on ever naming it. By Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Both the earlier and the corrected readings are recorded so neither is silently lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of well-known institutional researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: an LLM *"can simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
