# The Tether's answer: what establishes empirical grounding

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A model M is called "empirically grounded" when its outputs are corrected by something outside M that does not change its answer because of what M's author thinks. It is called "an illustrative story" when it is a self-consistent narrative that reproduces a preferred pattern and is scored by quantities M produces about itself. The residual is the gap between M's predictions and the world's returns. The question asks which standards narrow that gap in the right way. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Grounding is a compound, so the answer is a set of conditions, not a single test.

## 2. The shape

1. **A non-updating anchor is required.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Calibration data reused as validation, a stylised fact chosen after seeing the output, and an expert panel picked for likely agreement all fail this. The standard must name a reading M's construction could not change.
2. **The residual must be reported per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A global fit near zero with one live slot is a legal state, not an inert one. Report misses by agent, subgroup, time window, and outcome.
3. **Self-produced quantities are disqualified.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Coverage, compression, rule count, ODD completeness, badges, and prestige cannot establish contact. Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."
4. **Evidence must be low-overlap.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Held-out data from a different source, a checker who has not seen the interior, and a declared stake. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
5. **Observationally equivalent alternatives need a discriminating instrument.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." If two mechanisms fit the same pattern, name the observation that separates them. If none is nameable, the honest verdict is "unreached until a better instrument exists."
6. **The refuter is pre-registered.** Formula "Direction": "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."
7. **The lineage is part of the claim.** Fig 13 :127: "The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable." Calibration data, hold-out dates, pre-run refuter, sensitivity sweeps, and the provenance of any imported mechanism.
8. **Existing documentation standards are gates, not witnesses.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." ODD, TRACE, FAIR, code peer review, and reproducibility are necessary and insufficient. The CoMSES Net review criteria are the checkable real-world case: they grade execution, documentation, and code quality, and explicitly do not require reviewers to assess output validity.
9. **No standard certifies completeness.** Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A model is grounded to the resolution of the instruments brought against it, on the slots named, as of a date. The verdict stays `candidate`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that ODD, TRACE, FAIR, code review, or sensitivity analysis are useless. They are necessary gates for auditability. The claim is that they are not sufficient for grounding.
- Not that a peer-reviewed publication establishes grounding. Publication is a filter on what gets tried, not a witness.
- Not that more reviewers establish grounding. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not that a failed falsification confirms the model. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not that a synthetic test proves grounding. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- Not a name for the one true standard. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The named standards here are bindings from world frames, provenanced as such, single-source and ungrounded.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles which standard works best.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A standard becomes `grounded` only when a real checking arrangement pays for it. The discriminating observations are:

- A held-out dataset from a different instrument, fixed before the model is tuned, on which the model's per-slot residual is reported. Does the fit survive?
- A discriminating measurement where two mechanisms fit the same training pattern. Does one fail?
- A registered replication in which the verifier did not see the model's interior. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
- A standard whose passing demonstrably changes contact, with no self-produced criterion in it. This is refuter 1 in `01` §4 and the first thing to try.
- A measurement showing a named anchor moves when the model is re-calibrated. Then the claim fails and the standard is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-condition shape is a constraint on any standard that could establish grounding. It does not tell us which institutional standard best implements it; that needs a ground reading of actual checking arrangements. "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring a standard does not make it neutral. Fig 9 :51: "evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> "Empirically grounded" is not a property a model's interior can certify. Code that runs, an ODD document, a code archive, and a reproduced stylised fact are gates on auditability: the CoMSES model review criteria are honest enough to say they do not assess output validity. A grounding standard must do more. It must name an anchor that does not move when the model is re-calibrated; report the residual per slot instead of hiding a live miss in an average; disqualify quantities the model produces about itself; require low-overlap evidence so calibration is not reused as validation; demand a discriminating observation where two mechanisms fit the same pattern; state a refuter before the run; and keep a lineage. It must also admit that no standard certifies completeness. What this gives you is a shape any grounding standard must have, a falsifier written in advance, and the instruments that would close it, with the verdict left open until a ground pays. That is a checkable candidate, not a certification.
