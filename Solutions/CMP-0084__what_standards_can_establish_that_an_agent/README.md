# Solution CMP-0084: what standards establish that an ABM is empirically grounded?

**Question:** What standards can establish that an agent-based model is empirically grounded rather than an illustrative story?

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement in this derivation settles which standard is best, and this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## The residual in effect terms

An agent-based model M and a claim that M is "empirically grounded" rather than "an illustrative story." The residual R is the gap between what M predicts and what the world returns. The question asks which standards narrow R in a way that distinguishes contact with a ground from a self-consistent narrative that merely reproduces a preferred pattern. Option A: treat a checklist (code available, ODD written, paper peer reviewed, pattern reproduced) as proof of grounding. Option B: treat such checklists as gates on the budget and require a non-updating anchor, a per-slot residual, low-overlap evidence, and a pre-registered refuter before calling M grounded. The derivation below takes B.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The JASSS contact page publishes institutional and personal addresses; neither is reproduced here. Public submission portals and institutional addresses only.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Seat Map :163).
- No bulk-send plan. One-to-one or public opt-in submission only.
- No claim that this derivation settled the question. Every substantive claim is `specified`, the source discipline is a simulation, and the verdict stays `candidate` until a ground pays.

## Three reasons from the figures for that refusal

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for agreement is a mirror.
2. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A submission venue is a filter on where the claim gets tried, not a witness that it is true.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the model's interior before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission and contact points avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
