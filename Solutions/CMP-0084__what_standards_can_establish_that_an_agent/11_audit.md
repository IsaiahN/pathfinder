Arity: a vector, because the claim attaches to slots (agents, subgroups, time windows, outcomes). Symmetry: asymmetric; a live miss on one slot is not interchangeable with a hit on another, and the decisive comparison is model against an external reading rather than frame against frame. Scale: two scales with a lossy membrane, agents inside the model and observations outside it. Question type: a union compound, because "grounded" bundles reproducibility, documentation, calibration, independent validation, and mechanism discrimination; a single lever would clear the region where only one condition holds and leave the intersections.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim constraints, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22, :27; Fig 2 :29, :31; Fig 9 :60; Fig 10 :42, :52; Fig 12 :117; Fig 13 :127.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`, "a grounded ABM has a non-updating anchor, per-slot residuals, independent evidence, a pre-registered refuter, and a lineage; documentation standards are gates."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a real paper's claim, "our model is empirically grounded because it reproduces four stylised facts and the code is archived."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **discrimination test** for observationally equivalent mechanisms (Fig 12 :117), which the shorthand compresses into "independent evidence";
- the **operand type** of the isomer claim at the model level (Fig 12 :104 permits settled molecules to become atoms, but whether a whole ABM is settled enough needs an instrument);
- the **verifier non-access condition** (Fig 10 :42), which is easily reported as "we had independent reviewers" while they read the code;
- the **pre-registration date** of the refuter, which collapses into "we stated limitations".

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: documentation and reproducibility gates. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "validated = documented and reproducible" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
