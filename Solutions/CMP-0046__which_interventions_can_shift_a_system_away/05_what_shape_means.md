## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* `Fig 9 :32` "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". `Fig 13 :134` "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- a shape: "any acceptable intervention must have its habitat enumerated to the cascade horizon, must be measured per slot, and must be treated as a union with the harm condition";
- a name: "reduce nutrient loading by 30 percent in this lake".

The first is derivable. The second is owed from the ground. The difference matters because substituting the name for the shape is how an intervention gets asserted before its side effects have been enumerated.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."
- `Fig 9 :27-28` "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Here: describe the residual (distance from threshold per slot plus harm per slot) before choosing an intervention family.
- `Fig 6 :30` "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

For this question the "effect terms" are: contact cascades, per-slot residual, independence of measurement, and the asymmetry of introduction versus exclusion. These are readable by an ecologist, a network engineer and a clinician alike, which is why the corroboration in `09_watson_answer.md` crosses fields.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8`). Here that means a measurement, experiment or dataset that predicts the shape of the residual and shows no unenumerated cascade.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that forbids skipping this step: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". A system-level improvement reading cannot comment on its source. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." For intervention work the analogue is direct: a global resilience indicator that improves after an intervention does not tell you which slot improved or what else changed. The remedy is a per-slot instrument, not a narrative.

## 4. Why the question cannot be answered with a list

*The Library of Babel* names the failure directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the world-side version of `Fig 9 :54-55` (unbundle a union before searching). The question "which interventions" bundles at least two problems: moving the target variable, and not moving harm elsewhere. A list answers the first and silently assumes the second.

`Fig 11 :49` "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Even if a list were produced, its entries would change valence when the residual changes. So the honest answer is a rule for validating a candidate, not a roster of candidates.

## 5. Answer to the shaping question

**What licenses a particular intervention in a particular system?** Nothing in the figures. A named intervention is licensed only by the ground settling a measured claim, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, by construction: `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10 records the absence of a figure-derived name as a gap, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` offers the shape and the test only. The error would be to present "enumerate the habitat and measure per slot" as if it were itself an intervention. It is a validation procedure.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any concrete thing. The canonical README qualifies it: the phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like."* Here the missing thing is the system-specific intervention and the source-discriminating instrument, both genuinely absent from the frame. So the rule applies with full force to this question. What is **not** forbidden is naming atoms the frame already holds, such as "contact", "cascade" or "per-slot residual", when describing the shape. I overstated the ban in the first draft and narrow it here. Both readings remain visible.

## 6. Why this entire folder is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any intervention and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. This is the honest stop the figures require: the lineage is recorded, the refuter is pre-registered, and the name is left owed.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For outreach this means a prominent ecologist's endorsement of the shape claim gives it a budget to be tried, not a verdict. The first contact asks for a refutation; it does not collect endorsements.
