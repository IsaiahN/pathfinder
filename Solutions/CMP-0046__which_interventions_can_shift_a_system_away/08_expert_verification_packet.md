# Expert verification packet: the Tether on interventions near a tipping point

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist, resilience scientist, systems analyst or risk engineer can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions, not `grounded`.** No intervention was measured. This is a shape and a test, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

There is no standing list of interventions that are safe near a tipping point, because an intervention's valence is relative to the residual it is applied to. Any acceptable intervention must have its habitat enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering, must have its effect measured per slot rather than in a global average, and must be treated as one condition of a two-condition union whose intersection with "harm elsewhere" is unbundled before a lever is chosen. Because introduction is available and exclusion is not, this is permanent monitoring work rather than a solved state. The figures do not name a system-specific intervention; that name requires an import from the ground with provenance shown. What is offered is the shape, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 11

- **Statement:** harm "elsewhere" is a per-slot, later-order residual, and a global indicator can hide it; the habitat must be enumerated outward to the cascade horizon.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 1 :22` "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." `Fig 1 :13` "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". `Fig 11 :44` "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." `Fig 11 :52` "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a global system metric proven to preserve every slot-level residual at all stated horizons; or show a system in which all harm appears at first order and no third-order residual exists where the figure predicts one.

### C2 - ecology facing; checkable by a resilience scientist or ecosystem manager

- **Statement:** the power to introduce an actor is not the power to remove the habitat, so an intervention is permanent work and habitat substitution carries unchosen actors.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 11 :61` "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." `Fig 11 :63` "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." `Fig 11 :66` "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." `Fig 11 :65` "Introducing an actor for its cascade is ecosystem engineering, after Jones, Lawton and Shachak, 1994. Cane toads and kudzu are the same move with the cascade unenumerated."
- **What to check:** does the cited ecological record support the asymmetry and the cane-toad/kudzu comparison? Does the claim survive a case where an introduced actor was later successfully removed?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate at the ground that an introduced actor's harm can be subtracted without substituting a habitat, verified over a stated horizon.

### C3 - decision facing; checkable by a systems analyst or intervention designer

- **Statement:** the two requirements form a union; one lever leaves the intersection standing; cheap ranking heuristics are budget filters, never a safety verdict; independence must be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 9 :55` "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." `Fig 9 :60` "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." `Fig 2 :42` "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **What to check:** is there a class of cases where the intersection is provably empty, so unbundling buys no safety? Does the single-lever failure described actually occur in the recipient's domain?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single lever for which zero harm elsewhere is verified across the enumerated habitat by independent frames, with no unbundling required.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of named interventions, and not a numerical threshold or dose.
- Not that expert agreement establishes safety; agreement from one evidence pool is one witness.
- Not that a global stability indicator is sufficient. It is a filter.
- Not that an intervention can be undone by removing the action. Exclusion is not available.
- Not that monitoring ends when the system has moved away from the threshold.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is asserted as settled by this packet.
- Not that this simulated derivation can validate itself. An external ground is always required.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

1. A per-slot, per-horizon observation that makes a third-order residual visible and a global average insufficient.
2. A measured intervention that moves a system away from a threshold with zero residual in every enumerated downstream slot over a stated horizon, with independent frames ranged against the same ground.
3. A measured demonstration that introduced harm can be removed without habitat substitution.
4. A named, system-specific intervention derived from the figures alone and surviving independent measurement (this would refute the shape claim, not confirm it).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 and Fig 11 :43-44, :52, :61-63. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know well: was an introduced intervention's harm removable, or did it require permanent management?
3. Name one instrument that would show a third-order residual before it becomes a system-level signal, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
