# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape (the form any acceptable intervention must have), `specified` on the per-domain predictions. Nothing here is `grounded`: no system, threshold, or intervention was measured in this run. All claims are candidate.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pipeline, not by this folder).

**Question:** Which interventions can shift a system away from a tipping point without producing harmful side effects elsewhere?

**Source:** `complexity science.txt` line 57. **Section:** 5. Critical transitions, tipping points, and resilience.

---

## The question restated as an effect

Two requirements bind at once: (1) move a system's state away from a critical threshold, and (2) leave no residual harm in another slot or at a later time. Option A ranks candidate interventions by their direct effect on the target variable and picks the largest. Option B refuses the armchair list, enumerates the habitat outward until the cascade stops mattering, measures every slot against a ground with independent frames, and keeps monitoring. Option A is how first-order scoping produces the DDT-to-eagles result and the cane-toad result. Option B is the shape the figures license. Neither is a measurement.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No list of named interventions presented as figure-derived. The figures return a shape and a test, not a prescription. Naming an intervention requires the ground.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** `Fig 2 :35` "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies agreement.
2. **Receptiveness is forbidden as a selection criterion.** `Fig 2 :40` "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **Sending the interior spends independence.** `Fig 10 :42` "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
