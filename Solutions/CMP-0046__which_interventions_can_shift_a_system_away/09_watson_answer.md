## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what answer the Tether licenses to the question: which interventions move a system away from a tipping point without harmful side effects elsewhere. Option A: rank candidate interventions by direct effect on the target variable and treat harm elsewhere as a checked side constraint. Option B: refuse the armchair list, enumerate the habitat outward to the cascade horizon, measure per slot against a ground with independent frames, unbundle the two conditions, and keep monitoring. What each leads to: A produces interventions whose harm appears three steps out and hides in an average; B produces a checkable shape plus a pre-registered refuter and an honest declaration that the name is owed from the ground.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two conditions bind: distance from the threshold, and harm in another slot or later time. Arity: two, so a union compound. Symmetry: broken, because the target slot and the "elsewhere" slots are not interchangeable. Scale: first, second and third order in the habitat cascade. Fig 9 names a case in two conditions a specific compound, not a harder case of either condition.
- **Domain 1, habitat and ecology.** Actors: the system, habitat actors, conditions, relations, the introduced intervention, the residual. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, meaning what actually happens to the system and downstream slots. Figure family: Fig 11.
- **Domain 2, measurement and verification.** Actors: instruments, frames, the ground maintainer's channel. Currency: residual per slot and independence. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Figure family: Figs 1, 2, 10.
- **Domain 3, decision and search.** Actors: the designer, candidate interventions, filters, two budgets. Currency: bits and actions. Anchor: the ground settles; filters only buy budget. Figure family: Figs 3, 8, 9, 12, 13, 6.
- **Domain 4, modelling (the seam where D1 and D2 overlap).** Actors: models, aggregates, shared priors. Currency: description length. Anchor: the ground, never model agreement. Fig 10 seam, so D1 and D2 are triangulated rather than negotiated.
- **Union:** yes. D1 and D2 share the field; the two question conditions bind jointly. A ∩ B is "effective here and harmless elsewhere at horizon H", with its own loop. Unbundle before search.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The break usually sits between vocabulary and objective.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure verdict is the shape plus the procedure, and a plain declaration that the name is not derivable. An intervention is acceptable only if its habitat has been enumerated outward to a stated cascade horizon, its effect measured per slot against an independent ground, the two conditions unbundled before a lever is chosen, and permanent monitoring accepted as the cost of introduction. No list of named interventions is licensed; a system-specific name requires an import from the ground. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The quantitative price of enumeration and the round-trip gap `R_T` remain an open gap, recorded rather than smoothed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded but real. The packet states its mode, its refuters and its lineage, so an error is findable. The main cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a list instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is acting on an unenumerated cascade, which is how DDT reached the eagles.
- **undoable:** the analysis is fully undoable; no term or atom was withdrawn, and the corrections in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6 are visible. The intervention itself is not generally undoable: `Fig 11 :61` "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." So the undoability of the *claim* must not be confused with the undoability of the *action* it describes.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument that makes third-order residuals visible before they reach a global average; a measured intervention with zero residual in every enumerated slot over a stated horizon and independent frames; a demonstrated way to subtract introduced harm without habitat substitution; or a named intervention derived from the figures alone and surviving independent measurement (which would refute the no-name claim). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
