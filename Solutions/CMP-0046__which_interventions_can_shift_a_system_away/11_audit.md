## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the two-condition failure of an intervention near a threshold. Arity: two, so a **union compound**. Symmetry: broken, because "elsewhere" is a different slot and a later time, not a permutation of the target. Scale: first, second and third order in the cascade (the DDT case is third order). Union, not partition: a single lever leaves the intersection standing.
- **Domain 1, habitat and ecology.** Actors: system, habitat actors, conditions, relations, introduced intervention. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: what actually happens to the system and downstream slots. This is Fig 11 territory.
- **Domain 2, measurement and verification.** Actors: instruments, frames, the channel. Currency: residual per slot and independence. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Figs 1, 2 and 10.
- **Domain 3, decision and search.** Actors: designer, candidates, filters, two budgets. Currency: bits and actions. Anchor: the ground settles. Figs 3, 8, 9, 12, 13, 6.
- **Domain 4, the modelling seam.** A model is not an anchor. D1 and D2 must be triangulated, not negotiated (Fig 10).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Break expected between vocabulary and objective, where no instrument exists.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 11's DDT example and Fig 1's per-slot residual, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-44, :52, :61-63 (habitat enumeration, DDT cascade, asymmetry), Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-55 (union), Fig 2 :42 (independence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "enumerate the habitat, measure per slot, unbundle the union, keep monitoring, and do not name the intervention".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete intervention proposal in a specific system, for example a nutrient-loading reduction in a lake with a stated downstream monitoring plan.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **cascade horizon**, which the shorthand leaves as "until the cascade stops mattering" but which must be an explicit number and an explicit set of slots in any real proposal;
- the **independence specification**, which the shorthand compresses to "independently measured" but which requires naming the frames, their evidence pools and the instrument that can move the ground reading;
- the **two currencies**, which the shorthand keeps separate in words but cannot price, so a proposal may be cheap to describe and unaffordable to verify (Fig 12 :126-127);
- the **asymmetry**, which the shorthand states but which a concrete proposal tends to reinterpret as "the action can be reversed if needed".

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a three-step harm cannot be seen in a global average, so per-slot measurement is required. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
