## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A local practice meets a ground: the steel heat either turns or it does not, the apprentice's cut either fits or it does not, the patient either recovers or does not. Corrections arrive during practice, and the practitioner's competence lives in the loop that reads those corrections. A textbook, database, or policy document is a description that can travel without the practitioner. The move is a transform from one scale (the practitioner and their immediate ground) to another (a population of readers who will act later, elsewhere).

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot. Here the description is `Γ`, the reader's belief is `b`, the reader's action on the job is `a`, and `o'` is what the ground returns. The residual is the gap between what the document's terms predict the reader can do and what the ground actually returns. It is indexed per slot: which specific act failed, not a general "knowledge loss" score. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

**Option A (the document-defect reading):** the loss is an omission in the document, curable by writing more, structuring better, or adding metadata. **Option B (the union reading):** the loss is a union of conditions at four different places, only one of which is the document itself, so one lever cannot clear all of them. What each leads to: A produces more documentation and mistakes a frame-internal quantity for a verdict (Fig 1 :27). B produces a per-domain verdict, a measured round trip, and a refusal to promise a universal percentage.

**The sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term before the run.** An answer is acceptable only if it (1) names each loss class with a nameable anchor, (2) cites a verbatim figure line that governs it, (3) states a refuter, and (4) measures or honestly declines to measure the round trip gap `R_T`. This is the formula's "Direction" discipline. Written here, before the findings, so it can be refused by the same test as any other claim.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** multiple conditions, not one. At least four things can go missing at different places: the arrangement (bond), the generator status (method versus recording), the receiving habitat (contact), and the anchor (a ground that does not update on the document). This is a **union** of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*), not a partition. One lever, for example "add more detail to the manual", clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Symmetry:** the move is **ordered**, not symmetric. Practice to document is not the same relation as document to competent practice. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The inverse trip requires a different operation (rebuilding contact) and pays a gap. Treating the document as two names for one referent (identity, `≡`) is the symmetric-bond error.
- **Scale:** the move crosses a **scale membrane** (one practitioner to a population of readers) and is **recursive** (a textbook becomes an atom for the next textbook). Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."*

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

A domain exists only if it has a nameable anchor. Where the document cannot supply one, that is recorded, not hidden.

- **D1, the practice and habitat domain.** Actors: practitioner, apprentice, tool, situation, conditions and relations. Currency: corrections from the ground during practice (trial, error, correction). Anchor: what the practice pays off against, a working machine or a healed patient. Nameable. The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43). Question type: contact and capability.
- **D2, the transform and codification domain.** Actors: the author or compiler, the method, the description. Currency: description length versus residual removed, and loss across scales. Anchor: the round trip gap `R_T`, measured by carrying the description back down to a real act (Fig 4 :30). Nameable. Question type: a directed membrane with a measured loss.
- **D3, the document and closure domain.** Actors: terms in the library `Γ`, bonds, the reader of the document. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)`, description cost. Anchor: **none inside the document**. A document cannot certify its own completeness (Fig 8 :34); its anchor must be imported from D1 or D2. It is a domain only by borrowing that anchor. Question type: closure idempotence, flat notation.
- **D4, the reader and reception domain.** Actors: reader with a history, the document, the ground the reader later meets. Currency: agreement versus correction. Anchor: the ground, ranged separately by reader. Nameable if and only if the reader acts and the ground pays. Question type: a room that must match the residual it surfaces (Fig 7 :36) and a habitat that was substituted (Fig 11 :66).

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union, not partition. The four domains overlap: the transform (D2) happens inside a practice (D1), the document (D3) is the output of the transform and the input to reception (D4), and the same term can be lost at two places for two different reasons. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A "bad manual" case can be a bond loss plus a habitat substitution, and is not just a worse case of either alone. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22).

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

Order: **D1 (practice meets ground) → D2 (extract a method, measure the loss) → D3 (document composes inside a closure) → D4 (reader rebuilds contact).** The break is most often between vocabulary and objective: the document gives terms for what things contain, but the reader has no term for what counts as progress on the job. Fig 3 :25: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* More documentation spends effort above the break.

**Strain test applied before findings.** The tempting line "shape, not a name" governs the naming of a *missing primitive*. This question asks for a list of losses, not the name of a missing atom. Applying it as a ban on listing would be a strain, so it governs another quantity and sends us back to the census (see §4 and the correction in `05` §5b). What it does govern here is the universal percentage: no figure returns "X percent of knowledge is lost."

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The loss is a directed, lossy transform, not a copy with accidental omissions

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."** Fig 4 :31: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."**

A textbook or policy document is the upward product of a membrane. What legitimately crosses is a method that can be reapplied. What usually crosses instead is a recording of one occasion dressed as knowledge. That is why the loss is structural and not a proofreading failure. The honest measure is the round trip: Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

- **Mode:** `general` for the transform rule; `specified` for the claim that a given document carries a recording rather than a method.
- **Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 4 (membrane) and Fig 12 :107 (settling compresses the description, not its cost). Low overlap between the scale rule and the description-cost rule.
- **Refuter:** a round trip on a named document that returns exactly what was sent (`R_T = 0` for the relevant slot), or a demonstration that the document transmits the reapplicable generator rather than a replay of one occasion.

### F2 — The document records the atoms but not the bonds, and nothing signals the loss

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** The Melt / Freeze / Boil case, Fig 12 :13: **"three different changes, one written formula"**; Fig 12 :14: **"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"**; Fig 12 :15: **"nothing downstream can tell them apart, and nothing signals that anything was lost"**; Fig 12 :17: **"the parts are not the thing"**.

Fig 12 :46: **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

A list of steps, ingredients, or fields names a state. It cannot name the typed relation that made the practice work. Because the notation cannot represent the bond, a reader cannot tell from the document that anything was left out. This is the decisive content loss, and it is silent by construction.

- **Mode:** `general` for the notation rule, `specified` for any particular document's missing bond.
- **Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 12 (bond) and the Operators table :16 (the only bond in a flat formula is `+`), two separate statements of the notation limit.
- **Refuter:** a document whose atom list plus explicit bond labels fully determines the competent act at the ground, with no residual on the slot. If the bond is written and the reader still fails, the loss is not the bond (see F4).

### F3 — The ordered bonds and the refusal are the ones that fail to cross

Operators table :16: *"the only bond in a flat formula, which is why a formula records ingredients and not arrangement"*. Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."** Operators :73: *"A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."*

The symmetric bonds (`+`, `∥`, `≡`) survive a flat document. The ordered bonds (`→`, `⇒`, `−`, `⋛`) and the unary negation (`¬`) are exactly what carries a route, a goal, progress, a threshold, and the right to refuse a claim. Losing them is not losing detail; it is losing the grammar that turns a state description into a way of getting somewhere. Fig 12 :113: **"A system holding only ingredients can say what a situation contains and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it."**

- **Mode:** `general` (structural consequence of the bond table).
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 and the Operators table, with the topology worked out in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- **Refuter:** a flat document that nevertheless lets a reader correctly order steps, state a threshold, and refuse an out-of-date rule at the ground without extra contact. If it can, the ordered-bond claim is narrowed to the class that actually fails.

### F4 — The reader's habitat is substituted, with two silent failures

Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :67: **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."** Fig 11 :29: **"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"** (the full line is the second of the two silent failure modes). Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Giving a reader a document substitutes a habitat. The two failures are invisible until the goal fails: what the document failed to reproduce from the original practice, and what it introduced from the new setting that was not chosen. The practitioner's competence was agent-and-habitat; the document transports an agent-shaped description without the habitat. This is why a training manual can be followed perfectly and still not produce a practitioner.

- **Mode:** `specified` for reception, `general` for the substitution law.
- **Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 11 (habitat) and Fig 4 :34 (a seed carries no small tree), two different figures on the same point with low content overlap.
- **Refuter:** a reader who, from the document alone with the same ground and no practitioner contact, reliably produces the competent act with no residual on the slot. That would show the substituted habitat preserved what the goal required.

### F5 — The pretext and history do not travel; only effect descriptions do

Fig 4 :34: **"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."** Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."**

The governing essay (*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session) states the same limit in the vocabulary of the field: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* And: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Fig 12 :107: **"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."** Where the record does not cross a boundary, Fig 12 :108: **"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."**

What is lost is the built history that makes a compressed message unpackable. The world literature converges here: Polanyi's *"we can know more than we can tell"* (as quoted in the Wikipedia source and the essay), Collins' three kinds of tacit knowledge (relational, somatic, collective), Clark and Brennan's repair loop in conversation, and Bartlett's schema-driven omissions in the War of the Ghosts transmission chain. These are corroboration only; the figures decide.

- **Mode:** `specified` (loss of pretext at reception), supported by a `general` structural rule.
- **Status:** single-source on the figure side for the explicit "effect terms" instruction (Fig 6 :30, Fig 9 :30 are two figures, so triangulated); world corroboration is broad but shares a tacit-knowledge evidence pool, counted as one witness.
- **Refuter:** a written effect description alone that reproduces skilled action at the ground without contact. The essay's own claim would be falsified by that; so would this finding.

### F6 — The document is a closure with no internal anchor

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

A database or policy document can be searched exhaustively and still not yield what it did not encode. It cannot certify its own completeness, because that certificate would require reaching its own metaframe (Fig 8 :34). The anchor must come from outside: a ground, a measurement, or a differing frame. Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

- **Mode:** `general` (closure is definitional).
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8, and Fig 9 (three separate figures with different diagrams).
- **Refuter:** a document that internally proves its completeness, or a search inside a closed document that adds a genuinely new atom. The first would break the metaframe theorem; the second would break closure.

### F7 — A declared meaning is not a grounded one; the document can drift coherently

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**; Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."** Fig 2 :13: **"no anchor; each updates on the others"**; Fig 2 :14: **"they converge, and what they converge on"**; Fig 2 :15: **"is whoever had the most influence"**. Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."** Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

A policy document that defines its own terms and is checked only against itself has turned a convention into a verdict. Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**. The channel by which the document stays in touch with practice decays (Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*), while the ground keeps paying elsewhere. The loss here is not content but the immovability of the reference against which content is checked.

- **Mode:** `specified` for policy documents, `general` for the drift law.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 10 (independent diagrams: the three panels versus the seam and channel).
- **Refuter:** a document whose terms are pinned to a non-updating measurement that continues to correct readers separately, with the anchor demonstrably unmoved by the document's own reports.

### F8 — The union needs one lever per condition, and the universal percentage is a gap

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

There is no figure line that returns "X percent of knowledge is lost in codification." Percentages are filters for how much documentation effort to fund; they are not witnesses for which loss occurred. Fig 13 :135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and Fig 13 :136: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** A universal percentage is a name the framework does not return. This is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget, not an omission and not an invitation to invent a number.

- **Mode:** `general` for the union structure; `gap` for the percentage.
- **Status:** triangulated for the union (Fig 9 and Fig 1); the percentage is `gap` after a genuine re-census.
- **Refuter (for the gap):** an instrument that measures, per slot and per document, the round trip gap and yields a stable, reproducible loss function. An instrument, not an argument, would close it.

### F9 — What survives, and with which valence

Not everything is lost, and the distinction is figure-governed. Fig 2 :39: *"Correlation has valence."* Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Shared names, definitions, interfaces, and stated effects cross well because agreement is the product for a convention. The same agreement is disqualifying for a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :40). A textbook is good at conventions and dangerous when its convention silently decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).

Fig 5 :72: *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* Adding documents grows the library; it does not grow what a reader can do unless the channel to a ground is kept reachable and the round trip is measured.

- **Mode:** `general` for the two-valence rule, `specified` for a given document's use.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 (:39 to :43) and Fig 5 :72.
- **Refuter:** evidence that agreement about a shared meaning also settles a contested fact about the world without an independent ground, which would collapse the convention/verdict distinction.

## 4. Strains, re-census, and corrections

**S1, "shape, not a name" applied to a list of losses.** First pass tried to use the rule as a ban on enumerating what is lost. Strain: the rule governs naming a *missing primitive*, not describing a known loss class. Re-census (README, Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134) narrows it: enumeration of loss classes is a binding, permitted; only the universal percentage is refused. Kept visible here rather than erased.

**S2, "moves from practice into documents" sounds like removal.** Fig 11 :66 says isolation is substitution, not removal. Re-census: the question's phrasing is the ordinary term; the figure governs the receiving side. Codification substitutes a habitat for the reader. No strain after re-census; the loss is what failed to be reproduced plus what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :29).

**S3, Fig 4 (between-scale membrane) versus Fig 8 (closure wall).** Apparent conflict: one says a filter transmits methods across scales, the other says a closure cannot be escaped. Re-census: Fig 4 governs *what crosses a scale boundary*, Fig 8 governs *what a frame can derive from its own atoms*. Different quantities. Both retained.

**S4, the bond table applied to a prose document.** A textbook is not a typed DSL. Re-census: the bond analysis is used as a *structural diagnostic*, not a claim that books are formal molecules. The question is which relational information the notation can carry at all. Stated as a binding, kept visible in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**S5, world/figure tension on codifiability.** The Nonaka Takeuchi model presents tacit knowledge as both uncodifiable and codifiable (the Wikipedia SECI page records this as an ambiguity in the literature). Conflict rule: a conflict sends us back to the census, it never flips the verdict. Re-census assigns "codifiable" to externalization (an import that may carry a method) and "cannot be codified" to the pretext that stays behind (F5). Both readings are retained in their own slots. No verdict flipped.

**After re-census: zero hidden strains.** S1 and S2 dissolved by narrowing the object; S3 and S4 dissolved by assigning each figure to its proper quantity; S5 dissolved by splitting the SECI ambiguity into two domains. S1's correction is also recorded in `05` §5b and `11_audit.md`.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the framework's own rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds on a named document and a named slot:

1. A round trip that returns exactly what was sent, with `R_T = 0` on that slot, against a ground that pays.
2. A document whose atom list plus explicit bond labels fully determines the competent act, with no residual.
3. A reader who, from the document alone, reliably produces the competent act with no practitioner contact and no residual.
4. A written effect description that reproduces skilled action at the ground without contact.
5. A document that internally proves its own completeness, or a search inside a closed document that adds a new atom.
6. Agreement about the document's meaning that also settles a contested fact about the world without an independent ground.
7. An instrument that measures a stable universal loss percentage across domains, which would close the declared gap.

Any one firing retracts the corresponding finding. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Treat the move as a directed membrane, not a copy (F1). Measure `R_T`; do not assume it is zero.
2. Audit documents for missing bonds, especially ordered bonds and negation, not just missing facts (F2, F3).
3. Keep the receiving habitat and the channel to the ground in scope: a document without a maintained channel is a substituted habitat with two silent failures (F4).
4. Where contact is impossible, send effect descriptions, not internal shorthand (F5, Fig 6 :30).
5. Keep an external, non-updating anchor and a seat able to refuse the document's own claim (F6, F7).
6. Refuse the universal percentage as a shape-not-a-name gap; price the budget with it, never decide the verdict with it (F8, Fig 9 :60).
7. Send the generator (the residual, the refuter, and the round trip method), not the conclusion (Fig 4 :25).

### The three questions in formula step 7, kept distinct

- **SEARCHED:** Is the universal loss percentage in the figure closure and merely unfound? No line in the extract returns it. The search was run first and was cheap.
- **INWARD:** Sharpen locally by measuring `R_T` per document and per slot with a round trip to a real act. This is the instrument owed. It must be improved from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32), not specified from a description.
- **OUTWARD:** Find a frame whose closure predicts the lost quantity, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14). The tacit-knowledge literature and the SECI model are candidate frames, but their agreement with this derivation shares an evidence pool and is not a second witness.

Inward and outward were not read against one another. Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts; the percentage is the latter, and importing an atom against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives and is specific: codification is a directed, lossy membrane; the document records atoms but not the bonds, especially the ordered bonds and negation; the reader's habitat is substituted with two silent failures; pretext does not travel and only effect descriptions can cross without contact; the document is a closure with no internal anchor; and agreement is good for conventions, disqualifying for verdicts. What does not survive is any universal loss percentage and any claim that a better document alone repairs the union. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structure, `gap` on the percentage. Not `grounded`. Strains after re-census: none hidden, one correction preserved (S1).
