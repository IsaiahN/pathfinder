## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's findings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; union or partition; order by dependency. I took a different entry point from the primary, starting from the membrane direction and the operator table rather than from the union figure, to reduce the chance of simply restating its route.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot, the gap between what a document's terms predict a reader can do and what the ground returns. Arity: four conditions can fail at different places, so a **union compound**, not one hard defect; Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Symmetry: the move is **directed**, because practice to document and document to competent practice are different operations with different cargo; Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Scale: it crosses a **size membrane** from one practitioner to a population of readers and is recursive when a textbook becomes an atom for a later textbook; Fig 12 :103 "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible." Per-slot indexing is required: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1, practice and habitat.** Actors: practitioner, apprentice, tool, situation, conditions and relations. Currency: corrections from the ground during practice. Anchor: the working machine or the healed patient. Nameable. Question type: contact and capability, with the habitat enumerated rather than composed.
- **D2, the transform and codification.** Actors: the author or compiler, the method, the description. Currency: description length versus residual removed, and loss across scales. Anchor: the round trip gap `R_T`, measured by carrying the description back to a real act. Question type: a directed membrane with a measured loss.
- **D3, the document and closure.** Actors: terms in `Γ`, bonds, reader of the document. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)` and description cost. Anchor: **none inside the document**. A document cannot certify its own completeness; the anchor must be imported from D1 or D2. Question type: closure idempotence, flat notation.
- **D4, the reader and reception.** Actors: reader with a history, the document, the ground the reader later meets. Currency: agreement versus correction. Anchor: the ground, ranged separately by reader, nameable only when the reader acts and the ground pays. Question type: a substituted habitat and a room that must match the residual it surfaces.
- **Union, not partition.** D2 happens inside D1; D3 is the output of D2 and the input to D4; the same term can be lost twice for different reasons. A case in two conditions is categorically different, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). Verdicts stay per domain.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 to D2 to D3 to D4. The break sits between vocabulary and objective, where no instrument exists (Fig 3 :25 to :27). Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :20).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading and its dependency order. Where the primary derived D3 from closure figures first, I derived it from the operator table's statement that a flat formula has one bond. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, but still one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak and not circular in a way that would make it a witness.

Strain test applied before findings. The 'shape, not a name' rule governs the naming of a missing primitive. Applying it as a ban on enumerating loss classes would be a strain, so I narrowed it to the universal percentage, which no figure line returns. This is the primary's S1 correction, reached here independently.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as "The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold." Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :22-:27 (directed, lossy membrane), Fig 12 :5 and :112 (atoms, bonds, ordered bonds and negation), Fig 11 :39 and :66 (agent-and-habitat, substitution), Fig 6 :21 and Fig 8 :32 (closure), Fig 2 :39-:41 (valence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 2 and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` section 1: codification is a directed membrane; the document records atoms but not bonds; the habitat is substituted; pretext does not travel; the document is a closure; agreement helps conventions and harms verdicts.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete answer a reader would act on: a given textbook, database or policy document loses the route, threshold and refusal; a reader following it alone will fail in specific ways; a universal percentage is unavailable.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down to a real document:

- **Which bond is missing in a named document.** The shorthand says 'bonds'. The concrete claim must name the slot and the typed relation, and no such measurement exists in this folder. The bond loss stays `specified`, not `grounded`.
- **The two silent failures at reception.** Fig 11 :29 holds both what was not reproduced and what was introduced. A shorthand that says 'habitat substitution' can drop either half, and a concrete check must enumerate both against a named goal.
- **The valence flip.** Saying 'codification loses knowledge' drops the fact that for a convention agreement is the product (Fig 2 :41). A concrete document used only to share a vocabulary does not suffer the loss the shorthand predicts.
- **The status of the percentage.** The shorthand 'no percentage' can be read as a measured zero. Fig 13 :134 makes it a declared `gap`, which is a different fact. Setting R_T to zero here would be exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

Two things survive cleanly: the direction of the membrane (methods and head starts cross, recordings do not) and the closure limit (searching inside a closed document cannot add what it did not encode). Those are the residue worth sending, and they are what `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sends.

R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading and no instrument exist for it in this folder. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as a lossless copy of the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the error the membrane figure warns against.

Strain: the rule governs naming a *missing primitive*; the losses listed in `01` are bindings onto held terms. Re-census narrows it: enumeration of loss classes is a binding and is permitted, while the universal percentage is refused. Correction preserved in `05` section 5 and `01` section 7. I reached the same narrowing from the README phrase and accepted it.
- **S2, 'moves from' read as removal.** Fig 11 :66 makes isolation a substitution of habitat, not a removal. Re-census: the question's phrasing is the ordinary term; the figure governs the receiving side. No strain after re-census, but the two silent failures (Fig 11 :29) stay live.
- **S3, Fig 4 membrane versus Fig 8 closure wall.** Apparent conflict: one says a filter transmits methods across scales, the other says a closure cannot be escaped. Re-census: Fig 4 governs what crosses a scale boundary, Fig 8 governs what a frame can derive from its own atoms. Different quantities, both retained.
- **S4, bond table applied to a prose document.** A textbook is not a typed DSL. Re-census: the bond analysis is a structural diagnostic for what a notation can carry, not an ontology of books. Stated as a binding in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- **S5, world/figure tension on codifiability.** The Nonaka-Takeuchi model records tacit knowledge as both uncodifiable and codifiable. Conflict rule: a conflict sends us back to the census, it never flips a verdict. Re-census assigns 'codifiable' to externalization (D2, an import that may carry a method) and 'cannot be codified' to pretext (D4). Both readings retained in their own slots.
- **Isomer fixed-atom qualifier.** The claim that practice and document are isomers holds only over a shared atom set. If the document imports a genuinely new atom, the relation is composition plus import, not isomerism. This qualifier is live, named in `06`, `08` and `09`, and left open rather than hidden.
- **After re-census: zero hidden strains.** S1 and S2 dissolved by narrowing the object; S3 and S4 dissolved by assigning each figure to its proper quantity; S5 dissolved by splitting the ambiguity into two domains. One correction (S1) and one qualifier (fixed atoms) are preserved visibly.
