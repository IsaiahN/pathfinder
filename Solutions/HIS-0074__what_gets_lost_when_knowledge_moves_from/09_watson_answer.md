## QUESTION

State the residual in effect terms. A local practice meets a ground and corrects against it; the steel heat turns or it does not, the cut fits or it does not, the patient recovers or does not. A textbook, database or policy document is a description that travels without the practitioner. `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot: the gap between what the document's terms predict a reader can do and what the ground returns when the reader acts.

Option A: the loss is one defect in the document, curable by writing more or adding metadata. Option B: the loss is a union of conditions at four different places, only one of which is the document, so one lever cannot clear all of them. A spends effort above the break (Fig 3 :20); B keeps the channel, measures the round trip and binds the losses per slot.

The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run: an answer is acceptable only if it names each loss class with a nameable anchor, cites a verbatim figure line that governs it, states a refuter, and measures or honestly declines to measure the round trip gap `R_T`. This makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, union or partition, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least four conditions, so a **union**, not a single defect (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: the move is **ordered**, not symmetric, because practice to document is not document to competent practice (Fig 4 :22). Scale: it crosses a **scale membrane** (one practitioner to a population of readers) and is **recursive** (a textbook becomes an atom for the next textbook, Fig 12 :103). Fig 1 :19 'The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.' Fig 1 :22 'Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.'
- **D1, practice and habitat.** Actors: practitioner, apprentice, tool, situation, conditions and relations. Currency: corrections from the ground during practice. Anchor: what the practice pays off against, a working machine or a healed patient. Nameable. Question type: contact and capability; the habitat is enumerated, never composed.
- **D2, the transform and codification.** Actors: author or compiler, method, description. Currency: description length versus residual removed, and loss across scales. Anchor: the round trip gap `R_T`, measured by carrying the description back down to a real act. Nameable. Question type: a directed membrane with a measured loss.
- **D3, the document and closure.** Actors: terms in the library `Γ`, bonds, the reader of the document. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)` and description cost. Anchor: **none inside the document**. A document cannot certify its own completeness; its anchor must be imported from D1 or D2. It is a domain only by borrowing that anchor. Question type: closure idempotence, flat notation.
- **D4, the reader and reception.** Actors: reader with a history, the document, the ground the reader later meets. Currency: agreement versus correction. Anchor: the ground, ranged separately by reader, nameable if and only if the reader acts and the ground pays. Question type: a room that must match the residual it surfaces, and a habitat that was substituted.
- **Union, not partition.** The four domains overlap: the transform happens inside a practice, the document is the output of the transform and the input to reception, and the same term can be lost at two places for two reasons. Fig 9 :57 'What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.' Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 (practice meets ground) goes to D2 (extract a method, measure the loss) to D3 (document composes inside a closure) to D4 (reader rebuilds contact). Fig 3 :20 'The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.' The break sits at vocabulary and objective, where no instrument exists (Fig 3 :25, Fig 3 :27).

Strain test applied before findings. The rule 'shape, not a name' governs naming a *missing primitive*, not enumerating a known loss class; using it as a ban on listing would be a strain and sends us back to the census. It governs the universal percentage instead, because no figure line returns one. Strains and the correction are kept visible in section 5 of `01` and in `11_audit.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Codification is a directed, lossy membrane, not a copy. What legitimately crosses is a method or a head start; what usually crosses is a recording of one occasion that looks like knowledge. The document records atoms but not the typed bonds, so it cannot carry a route, a goal, progress, a threshold or the right to refuse, and nothing in the notation signals the absence. The reader's habitat is substituted, with two failures that stay invisible until the goal fails: what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced. Lived history does not travel, so only an effect description can cross without contact. The document is a closure with no internal certificate of completeness, and a declared meaning is not a grounded one. Agreement about a shared definition is efficient for a convention and disqualifying as a verdict about competent action. Losses are indexed per slot, kept per domain and never averaged. The universal percentage is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer and not silence. Mode `specified` per domain, `general` for the structural shape, `gap` for the percentage, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the generator travels with the claim. Each finding carries a verbatim figure line and a refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a conclusion or a percentage instead of the residual, the refuter and the round trip method (Fig 2 :42). A second cost is treating a substituted habitat as a faithful copy and mistaking a synthetic solve for capability (Fig 11 :70). Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter, and both are recoverable by a superseding packet.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive must be withdrawn. The four domain verdicts, the refuters and the round trip instruction can be restated without touching the derivation, and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already sends that generator. A sent claim can be corrected by a later packet with provenance; Fig 13 :129 requires the lineage to be the proof. What is not undoable after the fact is the independence spent by asking readers to agree before they have checked.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement or prestige. Specifically: a round trip on a named document returning exactly what was sent with `R_T = 0` on the slot against a ground that pays; a document whose atom list plus explicit bond labels fully determines the competent act; a reader who from the document alone produces the competent act with no practitioner contact; a written effect description that reproduces skilled action without contact; a document that internally proves its completeness or a search inside a closed document that adds a new atom; agreement about a shared meaning that also settles a contested world fact without an independent ground; or an instrument that measures a stable universal loss percentage and closes the declared gap. Until one of these fires, every finding stays `candidate`. A second seat that shares this evidence pool cannot reverse anything; by Fig 2 :35 a mirror cannot correct you.
