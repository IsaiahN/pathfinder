# The Tether's answer: what is lost when knowledge enters a document

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal loss percentage. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A local practice meets a ground and corrects against it. A textbook, database, or policy document travels without the practitioner. `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot: the gap between what the document's terms predict a reader can do and what the ground returns when the reader acts. The question asks "what gets lost." Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** The move is therefore a union of conditions at four places, not one defect in the document. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22).

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, practice and habitat. `specified`, triangulated.** Capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*). What the practice holds is not a stock of facts but a maintained loop of corrections against a ground. This domain sets the reference for all the others.

**D2, the transform. `specified`, triangulated.** The move is a directed, lossy membrane, not a copy. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."** What is owed is the round trip and its measured gap, not a better word count (Fig 4 :30).

**D3, the document. `specified` on the bond loss, `general` on closure.** A flat description records atoms but not bonds. Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 12 :15: **"nothing downstream can tell them apart, and nothing signals that anything was lost"**. The specific casualties are the four ordered bonds and negation: the route, the goal, progress, the threshold and the right to refuse (Fig 12 :112, Operators :16). The document is a closure with no internal certificate of completeness (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :32, Fig 9 :44).

**D4, reception. `specified`, triangulated.** Giving a reader a document substitutes a habitat (Fig 11 :66). Two failures stay invisible until the goal fails: what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :29). Lived history does not travel, so only an effect description can cross without contact (Fig 6 :30, Fig 9 :30). The governing essay puts it in the field's vocabulary: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."*

**Cross-cutting valence.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. So codification helps where the goal is a shared meaning and harms where the goal is competent action with no channel back to the ground. A convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention (Fig 10 :47).

**The percentage is a `gap`.** No figure line returns "X percent of knowledge is lost." Fig 13 :135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and Fig 13 :136: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** An instrument is owed. Percentages are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that documents are useless. Shared meanings and stated effects cross well (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that tacitness is mystical or unnameable. The figure returns a shape; it names no missing primitive (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that a universal loss percentage exists. It is a declared gap, not a hidden answer.
- Not that the practice is copying itself perfectly into the document. The transform is lossy by definition (Fig 4 :27).
- Not `grounded`. No textbook, database, or policy document was measured and no round trip was executed.
- Not that agreement among readers is evidence. A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

An instrument and a round trip, not a further argument. `*The Hard Problem is About Source*` gives the discriminator logic: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

1. **Round-trip test.** Carry the document's description back down to a named act on a named slot, and measure `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. An instrument must be improved from one already returning something (Fig 6 :32), not specified from a description.
2. **Bond test.** Does the document explicitly carry the ordered bonds and negation a reader needs, or only co-presence? If it carries them and the reader still fails, the loss moves to the habitat, not to notation.
3. **Habitat test.** Enumerate what the goal required and check what the substituted habitat failed to reproduce and what it introduced (Fig 11 :66 to :69).
4. **Anchor test.** Does the document's definition move when the reader pushes on it? If it does, it is a frame, not an anchor (Fig 2 :29, :33 to :34).
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §5.** Any one firing retracts the corresponding finding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set is a human's careful guess at which distinctions matter; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The round trip has not been run on any real document in this folder, so `R_T` is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`, as the enumerated set of constraints the shorthand drops. Treating the loss as "a percentage" would set that gap to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a fixed share of knowledge is lost when practice is written down, and it does not claim that documents are useless. It says that codification is a directed, lossy membrane: methods and head starts cross, recordings of one occasion do not, yet recordings are what usually get sent. A flat document records ingredients but not the typed relations between them, so it cannot carry a route, a goal, progress, a threshold, or the right to refuse an out of date rule, and nothing in the notation signals the absence. The reader's habitat is substituted, with two failures that stay invisible until the goal fails: what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced. Lived history does not travel in text, so only a description of effects can cross without contact, and a document that defines its own terms has no anchor unless one is kept outside it. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, seven failure conditions written in advance, and a round trip that would measure the gap. The percentage stays open until an instrument settles it.
