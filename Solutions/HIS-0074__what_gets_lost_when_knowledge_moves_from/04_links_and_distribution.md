## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are corroboration only. The figures decide. The tacit-knowledge and knowledge-management sources share one broad evidence pool and count as one witness (Fig 2 :40); grounding in communication and Bartlett's transmission-chain work are lower-overlap fields.

1. **Polanyi, *The Tacit Dimension* (1966).** *"we can know more than we can tell."* Predicts that a description cannot exhaust the competence. Corroborates F5.
2. **Collins, *Tacit and Explicit Knowledge* (2010), and the sapphire-Q study (2001).** Relational, somatic and collective kinds; laboratory visits were needed to move a published experimental result. Predicts that contact, not text, closes the gap. Corroborates F4 and F5.
3. **Nonaka and Takeuchi (1990, 1995), SECI.** Externalization (tacit to explicit) and internalization (explicit to tacit) name the two directions. The model's own ambiguity about codifiability is split across two figure domains in `01` §4 S5. Corroborates the transform shape, does not decide the verdict.
4. **Clark and Brennan (1991), grounding in communication.** A conversational contribution is not complete until the partner shows understanding. Predicts that a document has no acceptance phase and therefore cannot detect its own failure. Corroborates F1 and F4.
5. **Bartlett (1932), War of the Ghosts.** Details that do not fit the listener's schema are omitted or made familiar. Predicts silent substitution in any chain of retelling. Corroborates F2 and F4.
6. **Bessemer (patent 1856), as reported in the Bessemer process entry.** A patented, published process still failed in the hands of licensees until the missing step was supplied by another practitioner. A history of technology case, low overlap with the management pool. Corroborates F1 and F4.
7. **Lave and Wenger (1991), Brown and Duguid (1991), communities of practice.** Learning is legitimate peripheral participation; practitioners turn to colleagues more than to manuals. Corroborates the habitat claim at a different stratum.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict after re-census. The SECI ambiguity was returned to the census and split rather than flipping a verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the loss claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27:** *"describe it before you go looking"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the bond-loss claim were true before giving them the essay.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :41:** agreement about a shared meaning is efficient for a convention. The first contact is about a convention (what would count as a failure), not about belief.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure on a document they know; (3) record their verdict before any theory is shown; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the round-trip method as something to reuse on their own case, with their own refuter written first.

## The two-stage offer, with stage 3 as a method

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a generator, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The round-trip method is reusable; a result is not.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per document.** Fig 4 :30 requires the gap to be measured, not assumed. Each user must state what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01` §5 and `08` §2 are the test.
