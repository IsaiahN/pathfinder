## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel* (D): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the loss that can be stated before any percentage is measured. It constrains: the transform is directed, the bond is unrecorded, the habitat is substituted, the pretext does not travel, the document is a closure. It does not fill: it names no universal "X percent lost" figure.

## 2. The governing essay: context, subtext, pretext

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (D) is the essay that maps directly onto this question. Its claim is that every message is understood through three things the listener brings:

- **context**, what is going on around the words;
- **subtext**, what the words are for, though they do not say it;
- **pretext**, the history and assumptions the listener had before the words arrived.

The decisive sentence for codification: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* And: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

The essay maps its three terms onto the figures explicitly: *"Pretext is the history (the library) ... Context points the way (search) ... Subtext is the meaning you arrive at (the interpretation)."* It then gives the figure lines that govern: Fig 6 :23 (the bound moves only on contact), Fig 8 :54 (*"underivable is not unknowable"*), Fig 11 :40 (*"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing"*), and Fig 12 :108 to :110 (where the record is not carried, every lineage pays again).

Its sharpest line for this question: *"A manual outlives the craft when no one is left who can read it as it was meant."* That is a loss at the receiving habitat, not a loss in the document. This is why `01` F4 and F5 are separate domains rather than one complaint about bad writing.

The correction loop is two-way: *"The loop only closes if the learner acts; the highlighted step is where the correction arrives, unstated."* And: *"One-way subtext can't correct anything, because the teacher never sees how it was understood."* A textbook, database, or policy document is a one-way channel. That is a structural limit, not an accident of format.

## 3. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (D): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 (verbatim): **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**
- Fig 9 :30 (verbatim): **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."**
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (D): *"Anyone can check that against the world, whatever background they bring, so it's the one kind of message that still works without contact."*

For this question the shape is obtained by describing the loss in effect terms: what fails at the ground when a reader acts on the document. That is why each finding in `01` carries a refuter stated as an observable failure, not as a theoretical disagreement.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14, Fig 8 :50 to :52).

*They're Scanning Books* (C, carried from the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (D) gives the discriminator rule. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources produce one reading, so *"behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. This is the reason `01` F8 ends in an instrument owed rather than an invented percentage: a reading of "how much was lost" does not determine which loss occurred.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid listing what is lost? CORRECTION KEPT VISIBLE

My first pass used the rule as if it forbade enumerating loss classes. That was an overstatement, and it is corrected here rather than erased.

- The README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`.
- The losses enumerated in `01` are not new primitives. They are bindings onto terms the framework already holds: the membrane (Fig 4), the bond (Fig 12), the habitat (Fig 11), the anchor (Fig 2, Fig 10), the closure (Fig 6, Fig 8).
- Naming these losses is therefore **description**, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is the universal percentage, because no instrument or figure line returns it (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135).
- The correction does **not** weaken F5. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (D) still says the pretext does not travel, and Fig 12 :108 still says the deriving does not cross the boundary. Both claims stand; only the reason was misassigned.

Status: re-census required by the figures README rule that a contradictory reading has misidentified its domain. Triangulated across the README, Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for the answer

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (C, carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names agreeing that knowledge is tacit is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a refuter on a document the reader knows.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (C, carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any document and cannot settle anything it reports on.

**A note from *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (C, carried):** the Society of Agents room holds methods in common under a shared ground. That is the positive form of this answer. Codification works when what is shared is a method and the ground stays reachable; it fails when what is shared is a recording and the room has no ground of its own (Fig 7 :35).
