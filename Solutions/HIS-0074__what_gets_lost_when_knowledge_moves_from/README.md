**Standing of this folder.** Every substantive file below is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*), this package cannot settle the question it reports on. ## The question restated as an effect

Local practice reliably produces action against a ground: a machine that turns, a heat that yields steel, a patient who recovers. A textbook, database, or policy document is a description that can travel without the practitioner. The residual `R` is the gap between what the description predicts a reader can do and what the ground returns when the reader acts. What is being decided is whether the loss is a single defect in the document, or a union of conditions at the transform, in the arrangement, at the receiving habitat, and at the anchor. Option A (a better document) assumes one defect. Option B (keep the channel, measure the round trip, and bind the losses per slot) assumes a union. Keeping each option's consequence visible is the point of the census.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term.** A list of loss classes, each with a named anchor, a verbatim figure line, a refuter, and a measured round trip gap `R_T`. Binding this in advance is the formula's "Direction" step: it makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else instead of a preference the frame cannot see.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Gathering more readers of a manual does not test the manual.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the full line begins with that clause). A list of people chosen because they already accept a codification framework is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Show the residual and its refuter first.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That practical failure sits on top of the structural one.
