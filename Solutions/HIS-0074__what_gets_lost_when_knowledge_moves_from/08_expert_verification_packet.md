# Expert verification packet — the Tether on codification loss

**What this is:** a claim in a form a knowledge-management researcher, information scientist, or science-studies scholar can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal loss percentage. Not `grounded`.** No document was measured and no round trip was executed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Codification is a directed, lossy membrane, not a copy. What crosses well is a method or a head start; what usually crosses is a recording of one occasion that looks like knowledge. A flat document records atoms but not the typed bonds between them, so it cannot carry a route, a goal, progress, a threshold, or a refusal, and the notation does not signal that anything was lost. The reader's habitat is substituted, with two failures that stay invisible until the goal fails: what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced. Lived history does not travel, so only effect descriptions can cross without contact. The document is a closure with no internal certificate of completeness, and a declared meaning is not a grounded one. Agreement about a shared definition is efficient for a convention and disqualifying as a verdict about competent action. Losses are indexed per slot, kept per domain, and never averaged into a percentage.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal plus field-facing; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 12

- **Statement:** the move is directed and lossy, and the document records ingredients but not arrangement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. - **What to check:** on a document you know, can you point to the typed relations (sequence, production, subtraction, threshold, refusal) that the reader needs and the document does not carry? Is the document transmitting a reapplicable method or one recorded success?
- **Refuter:** a round trip on a named document that returns exactly what was sent, with `R_T = 0` on the relevant slot, against a ground that pays; or a document whose atom list plus explicit bond labels fully determines the competent act with no residual.

### C2 — reception; checkable against apprenticeship, communities of practice and grounding studies

- **Statement:** giving a reader a document substitutes a habitat, and pretext does not travel. Two failures are silent until the goal fails: what you failed to reproduce and what you brought with you.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :29 *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"*. Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- **What to check:** does the learner in your setting need two-way correction, co-presence or a return to the ground before the description works? Clark and Brennan's acceptance phase and Lave and Wenger's legitimate peripheral participation are the test frames.
- **Refuter:** a reader who, from the document alone, reliably produces the competent act with no practitioner contact and no residual; or a written effect description that reproduces skilled action at the ground without contact.

### C3 — framework-internal plus measurement; checkable against your own method

- **Statement:** the document is a closure with no internal anchor, so the loss cannot be settled by more searching inside it; an anchor and a round trip are owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 10 :18 *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*. Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** can the document's own terms settle a dispute about what a reader can do, or must you go to an outcome, a measurement, or a differing frame? Is the reference movable by the document's reports?
- **Refuter:** a document that internally proves its own completeness; a search inside a closed document that adds a new atom; or an instrument that measures a stable universal loss percentage across domains (which would close the declared gap).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that documents are useless. Agreement about a shared meaning is the product for a convention (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that every loss is a bond loss. D1 through D4 have different anchors and different refuters.
- Not that the document and the practice are unconditionally isomers. The isomer claim needs a shared atom set; a genuinely new import breaks it. This qualifier is live and named.
- Not a universal percentage, and not that any figure names one. It is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence (Fig 13 :135).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured, and the second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this derivation's evidence pool.
- Not a claim about any named author or institution. The world sources are secondary corroboration only.

## 5. What would settle it

Ground and instrument, not agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Run the round trip per document and per slot and measure `R_T`.
2. Apply the bond test (are sequence, production, subtraction, threshold and refusal represented, or only co-presence?).
3. Enumerate the habitat at reception: what the goal required, what was not reproduced, what was introduced.
4. Apply the anchor test: does the definition move when the reader pushes on it?
5. If any standing refuter fires, withdraw the corresponding finding.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
