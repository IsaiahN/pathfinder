## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. It characterises what is missing in effect terms and stops.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The same essay gives the stopping paradox: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form a dwarf-galaxy answer must take before any mechanism is named. "Union of assembly and shutdown", "open habitat list", "threshold on a spendable gas supply", "sign-flip cycle", "isomers over a shared atom set": each is a shape. "Ram pressure quenches satellites below 10^7 solar masses" is a name with a number, and it is a world claim, not a figure result.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, proximity bootstrapping: use *"the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*; then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* So describe the residual (which galaxies stay dark, which quench and when), not the preferred mechanism.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is F1 in `01`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. A new observational probe or a genuinely new physical input can move the wall; a name without provenance cannot.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. It could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* What resolved it was the spectrometer, not an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a quenched dwarf with no H I and an old stellar population is a reading. Ram pressure, reionization, tidal stripping and stellar feedback are four possible sources of that reading. Naming one from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (resolved star formation history, H I distribution, spatial structure), not a name.

## 4. Q of this question: does "shape, not a name" forbid mechanism names?

**No, not in that strong form.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Ram pressure, reionization, tidal stripping and stellar feedback are not primitives in the six-term set; they are world actors in the habitat (Fig 11), already named by the physics frame. Using those names is a binding or an import with provenance, not an invention of a seventh primitive and not a figure derivation.

What remains forbidden:

- claiming the figures select among those mechanisms (Fig 9 :32);
- claiming a world binding moves the closure wall (Fig 6: import adds an atom only when the atom is genuinely new to the frame);
- presenting a world-measured threshold (near 10^7 solar masses) as a figure result.

This is the same correction the exemplar made in its own `05` §5b about naming Gradient: the strong reading was overstated, the provenance requirement survives. Both versions are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. Why the record's tempting single-mechanism answer is the forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* A simulation family tuned to the quenched fraction cannot validate the mechanism it assumed. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

Fig 11 :50 adds the structural reason a single answer is wrong: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and malfactor to another"*. The actor list has no fixed valence, so any single-name answer is a projection of one occasion onto all residuals. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous dwarfs researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried against the ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any astrophysical claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.
