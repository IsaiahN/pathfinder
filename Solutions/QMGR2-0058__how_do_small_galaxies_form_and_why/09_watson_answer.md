## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single mechanism does not. The question is a union compound that must be split. The habitat is enumerated from the world and stays open at the instrument edge. Shutdown is a threshold comparison on a spendable gas supply and a cycle whose sign decides closure, so the same actor can strip or reignite. Mechanisms over shared atoms are isomers or functional-group members, distinguishable by measurement, not by agreement inside one simulation frame. The transition mass near 10^7 solar masses is a world result, not a figure result. Where the dependency chain breaks is `gap`. Everything here is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded, and `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a mechanism name instead of a shape; Fig 2 :42 prices that as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. The mechanism slot is empty rather than occupied, so a wrong world binding can be replaced without touching the derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. The only irreversible part is a reader who has already absorbed the framework before giving a verdict; that is why the first contact carries only the residual and refuter.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing which mechanism operates in a given mass and environment regime would name the slot; a smooth single-mechanism fit with no transition would refuse the threshold shape; a measurement of fixed actor valence would refuse Fig 11 :49; a proof that two mechanisms are indistinguishable in principle would reduce the isomer claim to a functional group; an instrument built with no ancestor returning a signal would break Fig 6 :32. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
