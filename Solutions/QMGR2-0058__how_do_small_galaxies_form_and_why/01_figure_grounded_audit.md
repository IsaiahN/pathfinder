## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Two observations are not predicted together by one frame.

- **Formation.** Given a low-mass dark matter halo, a gas supply and a cosmological environment, whether a luminous galaxy forms at all, and with what stellar mass, size and star formation history. The residual is the scatter and the threshold: some haloes of similar mass make galaxies and others stay dark.
- **Shutdown.** Given a galaxy that was forming stars, what removes or heats the cold gas, on what timescale, and why the same process quenches one galaxy and leaves another star forming.

They are not independent. A quenched dwarf's present structure is the product of both its assembly and its shutdown, and its shutdown epoch depends on its assembly history (early infall, backsplash, preprocessing). The compound has its own loop and its own ground.

**Bound sought-for shape (Formula step "Direction").** Write down what an answer would look like before searching: (a) the two conditions are unbundled; (b) the list of actors that can touch the gas is enumerated from the world and stays open at the instrument edge; (c) shutdown is a threshold comparison and a feedback cycle whose sign can flip, so the same actor can strip or reignite; (d) distinct mechanisms are isomers over a shared atom set or members of one functional group; (e) the slot `WHICH MECHANISM` is named only by a source-discriminating observation, never by agreement inside a simulation frame. This bound is an ordinary term and can be refuted by the pre-registered tests in §4.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least two conditions (assembly, shutdown), with a third operand, the measurement that distinguishes them. This is a union compound, not a single hard question.
- **Symmetry:** ordered, not symmetric. The governing relations are comparisons and thresholds (mass near 10^7 solar masses, ram pressure versus restoring force, virial temperature versus photoheating), and the sign of a feedback loop matters. A symmetric join would erase the direction that decides strip versus reignite.
- **Scale:** recursive across at least three nested scales, molecular cloud (parsecs), halo (kiloparsecs) and cosmic web (megaparsecs), plus lookback time (gigayears). Fig 12 :103 *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."*

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle the habitat and assembly vocabulary (D1) before asking what cold gas affords (D2); settle what would count as progress (predict quenched fraction and resolved star formation history) before ranking mechanisms (D3); range every model against the ground last (D4). Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Where the chain in this field currently stops is a measurement, not a figure reading; see F10, which is honestly recorded as `gap`.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — Split the question before searching it (decisive, `general`)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :57-58:

> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

"How do small galaxies form" and "why do some stop forming stars" are two conditions with different actors and different anchors. A single lever (more resolution, a different feedback prescription) optimises one link and leaves the compound standing. The world literature agrees on the shape without deciding it: Cortese, Catinella and Smith (2021) write that *"different physical mechanisms may act simultaneously and cannot always be easily separated"*; Sales, Wetzel and Fattahi (2022) list *"the diversity of dwarf galaxies"* and *"star-formation quenching"* as separate remaining tensions.

- **Mode:** `general`, structural.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 split rule plus Fig 11 habitat enumeration, two figures with different subject matter), world corroboration low weight because it is the same physics community.
- **Refuter:** one continuous mechanism with one parameter fits formation and shutdown across the full dwarf mass and environment range with no residual left. Then the union split was unnecessary and this finding is refused.

### F2 — The mechanism list is enumerated from the world, not composed (decisive, `general`)

Fig 11 :43-44:

> **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
> **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**

A reader of the world list (Hai et al. 2026) finds hot halo gas, ram pressure, tidal stripping, preprocessing in low-mass groups, stellar feedback, reionization, backsplash orbits and the cosmic web, and explicitly adds that *"a large sample of galaxies in different environments with precisely measured properties is needed to disentangle the relative effects of environmental and internal processes."* That list is read off, not derived from the six-term substrate. - **Mode:** `general`, structural.

### F3 — Valence is relative to the residual (`specified`)

Fig 11 :49-50:

> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
> **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**

The world picture is explicit. Hai et al. (2026) write that *"ram pressure from the cosmic web can both strip gas from these systems ... and reignite their star formation"* and that *"feedback from nearby galaxies may also facilitate star formation by compressing the dwarf galaxy's gas."* A 2020 MNRAS study of ram pressure in dwarfs reports that *"SF can be either suppressed or triggered by RP depending on the ICM density and the DGs mass"* (search abstract). The same actor, ram pressure, is malfactor for a gas reservoir and benefactor for a compressed cloud.

- **Mode:** `specified`, a falsifiable structural prediction about sign, not a claim about which object.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 valence plus Fig 12 cycle sign); world sources overlap heavily on ram-pressure literature, so they count as one witness.
- **Refuter:** measure one actor with fixed valence for every residual it touches, stripping only or compressing only, across the mass and environment range. F3 is then false.

### F4 — Quenching is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes (`specified`)

Fig 12 :121:

> **"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Fig 13 :22:

> **"a difference that can be spent"**

Fig 13 :76:

> **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

Fig 13, gradient row of the minimality table:

> **"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"**

Stars form from a spendable gradient in cold gas; feedback returns energy to the gas, which may cool and re-accrete (loop closes, sustained star formation) or be expelled or heated so no remainder returns (loop opens, quenching). The figure gives the sign structure. It does not say which feedback channel wins at a given mass.

- **Mode:** `specified`, ungrounded.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 cycle plus Fig 13 gradient/flow, different figures); world corroboration from the reignition literature.
- **Refuter:** a galaxy that keeps forming stars over a Hubble time with no re-entry of gas into the reservoir, or a quenched galaxy that retains a spendable gradient with no mechanism removing it. Either breaks the sign reading.

### F5 — Distinct mechanisms are isomers or functional-group members (`specified`)

Fig 12 :117:

> **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :124:

> **"A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Fig 12 :119:

> **"A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

Two quenched dwarfs can share the same atoms (cold reservoir, halo, stars) and differ in the bond (ram pressure shock, tidal heating, photoheating, outflow), so they are isomers: same formula, different behaviour in H I content, star formation history and spatial structure. Where the full atom set is not shared, they are members of one functional group (the `state → structure → state` quenching module) with different qualifiers. A hot halo or a dense dark matter core is a catalyst in Fig 12 :119's sense, a part that survives its own use and keeps the loop from reclosing.

- **Mode:** `specified`, structural; the observational discrimination is owed, not claimed.
- **Status:** single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), with world corroboration from Cortese et al. 2021 and Slater and Bell 2014. Because both world sources read the same stripping literature, this is one witness.
- **Refuter:** if two mechanisms over the same atoms give indistinguishable distributions in H I, star formation history and structure even in principle, the isomer claim is empty and the functional-group reading stands alone.

### F6 — A threshold shape, not a threshold value (`specified`)

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Operators table :48 gives comparison ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. Fig 13 :134:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

The figures license the shape "there is a comparison threshold on the gas supply". They do not license the number. The world supplies a number, and it is mass dependent: Slater and Bell (2014) find *"a clear transition near 10^7-10^8 solar masses from nearly ubiquitous quenching of satellites at low mass to much lower quenched fractions at higher masses"*; Sales et al. (2022) show the quiescent fraction of satellites versus stellar mass as an open figure. Simpson et al. (2018) report a sharp rise in quenched fraction below about 10^7 solar masses even beyond 600 kpc. The value is world-grounded in those papers; the shape is figure-derived. They must not be presented as the same kind of claim.

- **Mode:** shape `general`, numeric threshold `grounded` in the cited world sources only, not in the Tether derivation.
- **Status:** single-source for the figure shape (Operators table and Fig 9), world value triangulated across independent samples (Local Group plus SDSS in Slater and Bell).
- **Refuter:** a smooth single-mechanism fit with no transition mass that reproduces both the low-mass satellite quenched fraction and the higher-mass field fraction. Then the threshold shape is refused.

### F7 — More search inside one frame adds no mechanism (`general`)

Fig 8 :32:

> **"no search, however long, adds an atom"**

Fig 8 :39:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 8 :50:

Adding resolution or another realisation to the same simulation physics explores the existing closure more thoroughly. A genuinely new atom (a new observational discriminator, a different physical input such as baryon-dark-matter scattering, or a different dark matter model) enters only from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test. Sales et al. (2022) say resolution *"or validation as actual problems with ΛCDM, will probably require both refining of galaxy-formation models and improving numerical accuracy in simulations"*, which is two tracks, not one. A null detection in one survey is not proof that a mechanism is absent (Fig 8 :39).

- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 8 plus Fig 6).
- **Refuter:** derive a genuinely new quenching mechanism inside one simulation frame without changing subgrid physics or adding an observational input, and show it was not already in the closure. Then the import rule is wrong for this case.

### F8 — The discriminator is an instrument descended from a worse one (`general`)

Fig 6 :32-33:

> **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
> **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."**

Fig 10 :24:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

The mechanisms are not separated by another argument. They are separated by instruments that already return a signal and can be sharpened: resolved star formation histories from HST colour-magnitude diagrams, H I masses from radio surveys, far-UV star formation rates, and the spatial distribution of quenched versus star-forming dwarfs. Weisz et al. (2015) obtained a real discriminator from such histories: *"Inside of virial radius there is no correlation between a satellite's current proximity to a massive host and its quenching epoch"*, which strains any single distance-only environmental story without touching the shape claim. Hai et al. (2026) used three independent probes (CMD fitting, archival FUV, H I upper limits) on one isolated quenched dwarf, showing what a discriminator looks like in practice.

- **Mode:** `general` on the rule, `specified` on which instruments are relevant here.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 plus Fig 10), world corroboration from Weisz et al. and Hai et al., two different datasets.
- **Refuter:** an instrument built from a pure description with no prior instrument already returning a signal on that quantity settles the mechanism question. Fig 6 :32 would then be false.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds. Written in advance per the Fig 5 direction discipline: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

1. **Union split.** One continuous mechanism with one parameter fits formation and shutdown across mass and environment with no residual, so the unbundling in F1 is unnecessary.
2. **Valence.** One actor is shown to have fixed valence for every residual it touches, never both stripping and reigniting, so F3 is false.
3. **Isomer discrimination.** Two mechanisms over the same atoms are indistinguishable in H I content, star formation history and spatial structure even in principle, so F5 is empty.
4. **Threshold.** A smooth single-mechanism fit with no transition mass reproduces both low-mass satellite and higher-mass field quenched fractions, so F6's threshold shape is refused.
5. **Instrument ancestry.** An instrument built from a pure description, with no prior instrument returning a signal on the quantity, settles the mechanism question, so Fig 6 :32 and F8 fail.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the remaining findings are re-censused, not silently adjusted. Until one is run, no part of this derivation is `grounded`.
