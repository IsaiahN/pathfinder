## What is being decided, in effect terms

An institution predicts that compliance is safe. A political actor who expects future exclusion or retaliation observes that the prediction does not describe its future. The residual is the gap between the institution's assurance and the actor's expected treatment, indexed per actor, not as a national peace score.

- Option A: assert that institutions can prevent violence by design, that is, by writing and enforcing sufficient rules.
- Option B: keep only what the figures license, namely the shape of the conditions under which an assurance becomes a settled, non-updating prediction for the actor, with the specific mechanism left as a `gap` until a ground settles it.
- What each leads to: A states a `general`-mode verdict without a measurement and treats agreement among designers as evidence. B states a falsifiable `specified` shape, keeps its two conditions separate, and records what would reverse it.

The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the Formula's "Direction" section: *the answer would be a pair of conditions whose conjunction makes 'compliance is safe' a settled prediction for the actor, plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of the institution to bind itself from a failure of the channel carrying the assurance.* This is in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map field guide, failure genera).
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map law: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must see the residual and its refuter, not the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in routes.
