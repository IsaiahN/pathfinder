# Expert verification packet - INS-0065

**What this is:** a claim in a form a violence-prevention researcher, conflict scholar or institutional designer can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No violence measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

For a political actor who expects future exclusion or retaliation, an institution's assurance can prevent violence only if two conditions hold and are kept separate: C1, the assurance is a commitment that does not change when a party to the conflict pushes on it; C2, the channel carrying the assurance stays reachable at that actor over time and is not pooled into a national average. C1 comes before C2 by dependency. Prevention is a property of the institution and its habitat, a mechanism that once produced peace can change sign when expectations change, and an institution cannot mint a mechanism its own rules do not contain. The specific mechanism that fills the credibility slot is not named by the framework; it is a `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. What is offered is the shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus domain-facing; checkable by reading Figure 2 and by a case check

- **Statement:** an assurance that updates under pressure from a party is not a ground. It is another frame, so the actor's expectation of exclusion is correctly predicted.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :30 - *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :33 - *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :34 - *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
- **Outside corroboration (secondary):** Wikipedia, *Commitment device* (redirect target of "Credible commitment"), 2026, fetched 2026-11-23: *"a commitment device is a mechanism or arrangement that implements precommitment by deliberately restricting one's own future options in order to make a commitment credible."* Low-overlap field (game theory and behavioural economics), so it corroborates the shape rather than importing it.
- **What to check:** find a case where the institution altered its own guarantee under pressure from a party. Did measured violence per actor-slot rise, and was the guarantee the operative ground before it bent?
- **Refuter:** a case where the guarantee bent under pressure, measured violence did not rise, and the guarantee is shown not to have been operative; or a case where a non-updating assurance coexists with sustained violence in the same slots. Either breaks C1 as stated.

### C2 - framework-internal plus domain-facing; checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 7

- **Statement:** a formally sound assurance can fail because the channel to the actor degraded, not because the ground changed. The named decay modes are stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :28 - *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. Fig 7 :37 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*; Fig 7 :38 - *"the bracket’s fidelity across the handoff"*; Fig 7 :39 - *"the ground’s pressure on the room"*.
- **Outside corroboration (secondary):** Wikipedia, *Institutional memory*, 2026, fetched 2026-11-23: *"Institutional memory may be ingrained to the point that it becomes hard to challenge, even the conditions that caused it to arise have changed."* Low-overlap field (organizational studies).
- **What to check:** did the signal of continued commitment actually reach each faction, or was it reported only at the centre? Was a national average used where a per-faction series was needed?
- **Refuter:** a case where compliance collapsed while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining. If both stay high, C2's mechanism is not the cause.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about structure and order; checkable by reading Figures 9, 11 and 12

- **Statement:** C1 and C2 are a union of two conditions with different instruments, not one score; C1 is prior to C2 by dependency; a settled assurance becomes an atom for the next handoff; and a peace mechanism changes valence when the residual changes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **What to check:** are the failure signatures of a bent guarantee and a dead channel actually different in your data? Can one lever fix both without leaving a categorically different residue?
- **Refuter:** a single intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types with no categorically different residue breaks the union claim; a mechanism whose measured valence is invariant across a documented change in expectations breaks the valence claim.

**Status of the naming `gap`:** which specific mechanism (veto, independent court, external guarantor, monitoring mission) fills the credibility slot is **not** asserted. Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Any such name is a binding or import and carries zero evidentiary weight until a source gate and a shadow test are supplied (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The union stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not that institutions cannot prevent violence. The verdict is conditional on both conditions and on habitat.
- Not that any named institution or policy is the answer.
- Not that deterrence never works. It is one possible mechanism inside D3, with valence relative to the residual.
- Not a derivation of one condition from the other; their order is dependency, not sufficiency.
- Not `grounded`. No violence event data were analysed in this package.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, fetched 2026-11-23): a color instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

1. **Binding test:** does the assurance still hold when the most powerful party is inconvenienced by it?
2. **Channel test:** do signals reach each actor-slot, or have they been pooled into a national average?
3. **Per-slot outcome:** is violence measured per faction and per time rather than as a national index?
4. **Standing refuters:** the eight items in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. Any one firing retracts or narrows the shape.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :30, :33-34 and Fig 10 :24-26 (five short passages).
2. Check C1 and C2 against one case you know: where was the assurance bent, and where did the channel die?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish "bound itself" from "merely believed" at the actor level, or say why none is possible.
4. Name the failure class you would expect if both conditions held and violence still occurred.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
