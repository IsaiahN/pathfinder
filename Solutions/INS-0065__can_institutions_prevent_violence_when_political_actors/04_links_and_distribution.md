## Wider literature actually opened, and what it bears on

These are secondary witnesses. They corroborate the shape of the mechanism, never the framework's authority, and none of them settles the question.

Quote: *"a commitment device is a mechanism or arrangement that implements precommitment by deliberately restricting one's own future options in order to make a commitment credible."* Predicts that an assurance which can be changed for the powerful party is not a commitment. Corroborates F3 and Fig 2 :30. Field overlap with the framework is low.

2. **Power sharing and elite accommodation (political science).** Wikipedia contributors, *Consociationalism*, 2026, fetched 2026-11-23. 3. **Groupthink (social psychology and human cognition).** Wikipedia contributors, *Groupthink*, 2026, fetched 2026-11-23. Quote: *"concurrence-seeking becomes so dominant in a cohesive ingroup that it tends to override realistic appraisal of alternative courses of action."* Also Janis's prevention rule: *"The organization should set up several independent groups, working on the same problem"* and *"The group should invite outside experts into meetings."* Predicts that elite agreement is not evidence and that an outside closure is required. ## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the two conditions and the refuter. Offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; and Seat Map law: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :73** - *"The residual selects the members, but only from what is available."* Choose venues from the residual (violence prevention, institutional design, conflict resolution), then let the venue's own public rules select reviewers. Do not build a private list of believers.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the two-condition shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) offer the method for reuse, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Record the stage-1 verdict before showing any framework material and never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** The Formula's "Direction" section: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it, and labels the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading. State that plainly in the first message; do not let a recipient assume a measurement was taken.
