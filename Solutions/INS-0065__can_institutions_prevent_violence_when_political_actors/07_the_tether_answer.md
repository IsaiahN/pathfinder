# The Tether's answer on INS-0065

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A political actor expects to be excluded or retaliated against if it complies or loses power. The institution predicts that compliance is safe. The gap between those two, indexed per actor and per time, is the residual. It is not a national peace score: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19).

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57). It unbundles into C1 (credible, non-updating assurance) and C2 (maintained channel). Each has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The assurance must not update under pressure from the parties.** Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* A rule that bends for the powerful faction is not a ground. The actor's fear of exclusion was then well founded.
2. **The channel carrying the assurance must stay reachable at the actor.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."* A rule that holds but whose signal never reaches the faction has lost contact, not the ground.
3. **Prevention is a property of the institution and its habitat, not of the institution alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*; :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
4. **Order is C1 then C2.** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* One cannot maintain a channel to an assurance that never bound. Monitoring before binding is effort spent above the break.
5. **The actor's expectation is a refusal, not an absence.** Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*; Fig 12 :139: without a unary operator a system *"cannot write that any of those is false."* Repeating the same assurance does not repair `¬φ`.
6. **A mechanism that produced peace can change sign when the residual changes.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
7. **Renewal requires contact with a closure that differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* An institution cannot mint a mechanism its rules do not contain.
8. **The specific mechanism is not named by the framework.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Vetoes, courts, monitoring missions and external guarantors are bindings or imports, admitted only with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).

The two conditions are kept separate and never averaged. A blended "institutional strength" number is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) and destroys the diagnostic.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of everything, and no final room. The union of C1 and C2 stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not that any particular constitution, veto, power-sharing deal or monitoring mission *is* the answer. Those are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that institutions cannot prevent violence. The verdict is conditional: they can when both conditions hold and the habitat keeps paying for compliance.
- Not that deterrence never works. Coercive capacity is absorbed into D3 as one possible mechanism of credibility; its valence is relative to the residual (F7).
- Not a derivation of one condition from the other. Their order is a dependency, not a proof of sufficiency.
- Not `grounded`. No violence event data were analysed here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the answer *"is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

- **Binding test:** does the assurance still hold when the most powerful party is inconvenienced by it? A violation that is quietly accommodated is evidence C1 failed.
- **Channel test:** do signals of continued commitment arrive at each actor-slot, or have they been pooled into a national average (Fig 10 :26)?
- **Per-slot outcome:** is violence measured per faction and per time, not as a national index (Fig 1 :19)?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** habitat substitution with no change in prevention; agreement high while violence rises; a bending guarantee with no violence and shown not to be operative; high fidelity and high ground pressure with compliance lost; one intervention restoring both conditions with no residue.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument that separates "the institution bound itself" from "the actor merely believed it did", both of which can produce a quiet year. It is characterized in effect terms but not held here. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

A round trip from the verbatim figures down to a concrete policy claim carries a nonzero gap `R_T`, enumerated in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q5 and checked in `11_audit.md`. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would be dead reckoning (Fig 4 :30).

## 6. Cost if wrong, undoable, reversed by

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. An institution failing C2 would be sold more rules, or an institution failing C1 would be told to improve its monitoring, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 amendment :42-43). The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. The binding and channel tests above, applied per actor-slot, would move a domain verdict; any of the eight falsifiers firing would break the shape; a figure re-census that admits an eighth substrate member or fails idempotence would break the framework itself. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which institution will keep the peace, and it does not claim a final answer. It says that an assurance only works as a ground if it does not change when a powerful party pushes on it, that the channel carrying the assurance must stay reachable at each actor and not be pooled into a national average, and that these are two conditions rather than one score, to be checked in that order. Prevention is a property of the institution and its political habitat, a mechanism that once worked can change sign when actors' expectations change, and an institution cannot mint a mechanism its own rules do not contain. The framework returns a shape, not a name: which specific mechanism occupies the credibility slot is left open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. What it offers is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it.
