## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0065:

- *"An assurance must not update when the actor pushes"* is a shape.
- *"A veto gives that assurance"* is a name.
- *"The channel must stay reachable"* is a shape.
- *"An international monitoring mission is what keeps it reachable"* is a name.

The package keeps the first of each pair and records the second as a provenanced binding, not a derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (actor expects exclusion; assurance predicts safety; observed treatment decides) before any figure line was selected. That ordering is Fig 9 :27-28, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the first. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* What resolved the case was the spectrometer, not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

For INS-0065: a year without violence is a reading, not a source. It is consistent with a non-updating assurance (C1), with a maintained channel (C2), with pure deterrence, with exhaustion of the parties, and with an external guarantor holding the line. Naming one of those as *the* cause from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. This is why F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a declared `gap`.

## 4. Union, bundle, and the shape of a two-condition answer

*Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The shape for this question therefore has two parts kept separate:

| condition | shape | would-be name (withheld) | discriminator owed |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 credibility | the assurance does not update under pressure from a party | veto, independent court, external guarantor | does the rule bind the powerful actor when tested? |
| C2 channel | the assurance remains reachable at the actor over time | monitoring mission, early-warning system, local media | do signals arrive at the faction, or have they been pooled into a national average? |

A single "institutional quality" number is a blended reading. Fig 10 :26 names the failure: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous peacebuilding experts who endorse the shape is a prestige loop, not a check. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried against measured violence and asks what would refute it. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are chosen for public, opt-in, ground-visible routes, not for the eminence of the people who might answer.

The same essay supplies the drift warning: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states it in one line: *"two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."*

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay also says *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a measurement of institutional performance and **not** a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on. Every verdict in this folder is labelled `specified` or `general`, and the `gap` at F10 is left open.

## 7. Correction to my own first reading

I initially wanted to write *"the framework cannot name any mechanism, so naming a veto or a monitoring mission is forbidden."* That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. "Veto", "court", "monitoring mission" are not missing primitives of the framework; they are bindings from the institutional-science frame onto conditions the framework can already describe in effect terms.

The correct statement is narrower: naming such a mechanism is a **binding or import**, and it carries zero evidentiary weight until a source gate and a shadow test are supplied (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52). What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh substrate member or claiming that the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
