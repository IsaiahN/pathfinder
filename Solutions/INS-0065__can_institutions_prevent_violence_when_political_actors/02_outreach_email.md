# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 3 and 4 have been checked for internal consistency by a second seat (`11_audit.md`). A two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about institutions and political violence, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a small, checkable output and would like your verdict on it before I show you where it came from. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to read the framework first. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** take one political actor who expects to be excluded or retaliated against if it complies or loses power. Write the institution's assurance as a prediction that compliance is safe. The question is whether that prediction matches what the actor actually experiences. Under the framework's ground rule, an assurance can do that work only if it is a commitment that does not change when the actor pushes on it, and only if the channel carrying the assurance stays reachable over time. These are two conditions, not one score. The specific mechanism that fills the credibility slot is left open; the framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> Concretely: a rule that bends for the powerful faction is not an anchor, it is another frame, and the actor's fear was well founded. A rule that holds but whose signals never reach the faction has lost the channel, not the ground.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named institution, veto, power-sharing deal, monitoring mission or repression strategy *is* the answer. Those are bindings from other fields onto atoms already in the vocabulary. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. Until provenance is shown they carry zero evidentiary weight. I am also not claiming a national "peace index" settles anything; the residual is indexed per actor, never averaged.
>
> **What would refute this:**
> 1. An institution's capacity to prevent violence is unchanged across a habitat substitution that removes the ground's payment for compliance.
> 2. Expert agreement and institutional self-report stay high while measured violence rises per actor, and the field counts the agreement as the verdict.
> 3. An institution changes its own guarantee under pressure from a party to the conflict, measured violence does not rise, and the guarantee is shown not to have been the operative ground.
> 4. An institution loses compliance while both the fidelity of the assurance and the ground's pressure are independently measured as high and not declining.
> 5. A single intervention restores both conditions in both failure types with no categorically different residue left over.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the public sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures README and citation rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Local verbatim figure text used for every quote: local `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - CDC Division of Violence Prevention, public page and funded research: https://www.cdc.gov/violence-prevention/index.html
> - Stability: International Journal of Security and Development, open-access submission route: https://www.stabilityjournal.org/
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the figures are linked above. Run the loop on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether your claim is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Contact discipline

- One recipient at a time. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Use the public institutional or opt-in routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Do not use personal emails found on a webpage.
- Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 **before** any framework material is shown. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have it re-evaluated as if it came from any other source. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
