## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a predicted interaction web and the web the ground returns. Arity: many slots, one per species pair under an interaction type. Symmetry: mixed; some relations are symmetric (competition, mutualism as `+`), others ordered (predation, parasitism as `->` or `=>`), so an undirected adjacency has lost the bond. Scale: pairwise detection at first order, network property at second order, lossy crossing between. My starting figures here are Fig 12 :5 (bond is the missing information) and Fig 4 :30 (round trip), whereas the primary started from Fig 1 :19 and Fig 3 :20.
- **D1, observation and instruments.** Actors: observers, traps, sequencers, assays. Currency: observations that reduce R per slot. Anchor: the actual interaction web. Question type: reachability and perception.
- **D2, habitat.** Actors: species, conditions, relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact and influence. Anchor: what actually interacts. Question type: enumeration from the world, never composition from a catalogue.
- **D3, inference and verification.** Actors: method frames, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the same non-updating web. Question type: triangulation. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so D3 independence is zero.
- **D4, budget and composition.** Actors: replicates, depth, time, cost. Currency: actions, distinct from bits. Anchor: what the budget settles. Question type: two-currency pricing and filters versus witnesses.

Order by dependency: settle perception before vocabulary, objective before planning (Fig 3 :20), refuse a standing method list before minting a binding (formula step 2), describe the residual before choosing the frame (Fig 8 :53), settle first-order detections before composing a network property (Fig 12 :104).

**My census matches the primary's four domains and the union reading.** Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-57, I derived it from Fig 9 :4-5 (effort that does not shrink means several questions) and Fig 12 :5 (ingredient lists are not molecules). Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular inside the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
