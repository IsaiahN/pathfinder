## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about observing ecological networks when many interactions are rare, hidden, or difficult to measure. Option A: treat the three adjectives as one problem and answer with more sampling and one method. Option B: split the union, route each condition and each intersection to its own instrument, and keep the method name open until a ground settles it. What each leads to: A spends budget where the closure already works and mistakes non-detection for absence; B sends a per-slot shape, a written refuter, and an honest list of what each instrument would have to close. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: many slots, one per species pair under an interaction type. Symmetry: mixed, competition and mutualism approximate the symmetric bond `+`, predation and parasitism are ordered (`->` or `=>`). Scale: first order is a pairwise detection; second order is a network property composed from settled links. What cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read (Symbols table :38-40).
- **D1, observation and instruments.** Actors: field observers, camera traps, eDNA and metabarcoding, diet DNA, stable isotopes, telemetry, experiments. Currency: observations o' that reduce R per slot. Anchor: the interaction web, which does not move when a method or a reviewer changes its mind. This is the perception and instrument-reach domain.
- **D2, habitat and ecology.** Actors: species, conditions (season, temperature, water, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact, energy flow, influence. Anchor: what actually interacts and survives. The habitat is enumerated from the world, not composed from a catalogue.
- **D3, inference and verification.** Actors: independent method frames, observers, reviewers, archives. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the same non-updating interaction web, never agreement. Two instruments pointed at the same thing are two frames; two people reading the same report are one.
- **D4, budget and composition.** Actors: sampling effort, PCR replicates, sequencing depth, assay cost, time. Currency: actions, a second currency that does not add to bits. Anchor: what the budget can actually settle. The verdict is not a threshold on a filter.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Rare, hidden and hard to measure overlap, so one lever leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts stay per condition and per intersection and are never averaged into one completeness score.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, then vocabulary, then objective, then planning, then learn and carry. The chain usually breaks between perception and vocabulary, where a hidden interaction has no instrument. Effort lands above the break because that is where the measurements exist.

Strain test applied: the six-term substrate governs the held network atom; the habitat enumeration governs the species and interaction list. Different objects, so no strain after re-census (see `01` section 6, S1). The "shape, not a name" phrase governs missing primitives, so naming a detection channel is a binding, not a forbidden invention (S2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the method name does not. Observation error is per interaction slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). Rare, hidden and hard to measure are a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55). Rare owes distributed independent search (Fig 6 :49-50); hidden owes an imported instrument with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :21/:23/:32); hard to measure owes two prices held apart (Fig 12 :125-126). The habitat and its second-order effects are enumerated from the world (Fig 11 :43/:48/:52-53), methods are ranged separately against a non-updating web (Fig 2 :29/:35/:31), and what crosses scales is a method with a measured round-trip gap (Fig 4 :22/:30). No figure line names a winner, so the method is a `gap`. Status: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states per-domain verdicts, a pre-registered refuter and the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending a field budget on more search where an import was owed, or importing a costly method where independent search would have sufficed; Fig 8 :42 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives, and Fig 2 :42 says to buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Both costs are avoidable by splitting the union first.
- **undoable:** yes. Named methods are removable bindings, not primitives. A misrouted sampling campaign can be superseded by a corrected allocation, and no atom has to be withdrawn. The split, the refuter and the per-slot ledger survive a failed method.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument registering a previously hidden slot, with provenance and a shadow test, would close one gap. Demonstrating a partition with no intersection, a hidden slot resolved by search alone, a global coverage number that preserves every live slot, agreement from one evidence pool changing the web, or a lossless network-property round trip would each break a specific clause. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
