## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for ECO-0091 a **shape** is the form an adequate observation strategy must have, statable before any method is named. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape derived in `01` has five clauses: per-slot residual; union split before method lookup; search for rare, import for hidden, two-currency pricing for hard to measure; habitat enumerated outward; instruments ranged independently against a non-updating anchor. "Use metabarcoding" is a name. "An instrument whose closure includes molecules rather than hard remains" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach in `02` asks what a failure would look like in the recipient's own data.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," with the paradox that the searcher has "composed the answer through the process of locating it." For this question, the finish line is a per-slot residual vector that stops registering against the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The method must explain a gap the old one left, and the record must show where it came from.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning a chosen detection channel into *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a single method name is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For ecology, a detection event is a projection. A DNA read, a camera image, and an isotope ratio can each indicate a trophic link, and none by itself determines the interaction's strength, direction, or timing. Naming one method as *the* way to observe the network is a source named from a reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument plus an independent frame, not a name.

The world-side pages corroborate the mechanism without settling the framework's claim: the diet-DNA page reports that secondary consumption confounds interpretation and that sequence counts do not straightforwardly give prey abundance; the isotope page reports that compound-specific analysis resolves sources that bulk values cannot. Those are two instruments discriminating where one projection was ambiguous. They count as one broad ecology-methods pool, not independent witnesses for the framework.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So the output of this derivation cannot validate itself.

## 5. Q: does the framework name a method? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular instrument for a slot?** Nothing in the framework alone. The framework returns a shape. A particular instrument is licensed only by the ground settling a candidate or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does `01` commit the error the README forbids?** No, after the correction in section 6. `01` keeps every named method as a candidate binding and reports the specific method as a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). The earlier draft did name metabarcoding as the answer for hidden interactions; that was retracted in the `01` retraction log.

## 6. Correction to my own reading (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming any method. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive or atom not held in Gamma. Detection channels such as imaging, molecules, and isotopes are already available atoms in the observer's library; they are not missing primitives. Naming one is a **binding** from the methods frame onto a held shape, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong if someone simply asserts the binding: claiming that naming the method moves the wall. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." A binding to an already-held channel adds no atom. What can add an atom is a genuinely new detection principle imported from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: what is owed is a discriminator, and that is a reason to keep the slot open, not a ban on naming held atoms.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous ecologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a real network and a ground that decides.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is simulacra. So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
