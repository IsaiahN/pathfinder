# The Tether's answer on observing complex ecological networks

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the identity of any particular method. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An observer predicts an interaction web and the ground returns another. The gap is indexed per interaction slot, not per study. "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" (Fig 1 :19). The question names three reasons a slot fails to register: rare, hidden, hard to measure. They are a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition, so the answer is a set of per-condition verdicts with the intersections treated as specific compounds (Fig 9 :54-57). They are never averaged into one network-completeness score.

## 2. The shape

1. **Report residuals per slot.** A global coverage number near zero with one live interaction slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22). What cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read (Symbols table :38-40).
2. **Split before you search.** Fig 9 :4-5: effort that does not shrink with effort is several well-formed questions, not one hard one. "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones" (Fig 9 :55). A single lever clears only the region where one condition holds.
3. **Rare: distribute independent search.** The interaction is in the closure and unfound. Fig 6 :49: "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change." Fig 6 :50: "Distribute the search, and keep the searchers independent." More trap nights, more replicates, more depth, with searchers who do not share one evidence pool. Importing a new method against this budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 8 :42).
4. **Hidden: import an instrument.** The interaction has never registered at any resolution on the current frame. "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom" (Fig 6 :21); "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs" (Fig 6 :23). The new instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32); it is not specified from a description of the target. It needs a source gate and a shadow test: where it came from, and which gap it explains (Fig 8 :9-13). "Residual first, frame second" (Fig 8 :53).
5. **Hard to measure: price both currencies.** "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add" (Fig 12 :125). Bits price the description; actions price finding out whether it holds (Fig 12 :126). A sensitivity threshold is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
6. **Enumerate the habitat outward.** "The habitat is enumerated, never composed" (Fig 11 :43). Read the interaction list off the world, not off a standing catalogue (Fig 11 :48). Include second-order effects: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out" (Fig 11 :52-53). Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so no standing list of good actors survives a change of question (Fig 11 :51).
7. **Triangulate against a non-updating anchor.** The anchor is whether the interaction occurred, which does not move when a reviewer thinks it did (Fig 2 :29). "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you" (Fig 2 :35). Two observers reading the same report are one frame; two instruments pointed at the same interaction are two frames (Fig 2 :31). "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" (Fig 2 :42).
8. **Carry methods, not recordings, across scales.** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down" (Fig 4 :22). A per-slot detection settles at first order; a network property is a second-order composition, and the crossing is lossy. "So take the round trip and measure the gap" (Fig 4 :30). The round-trip gap R_T is enumerated qualitatively in `11_audit.md`.
9. **The method name stays open.** "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). Naming metabarcoding, camera trapping, or isotope analysis is a binding from the methods frame onto a held shape, provenanced as such (`05` section 6), not a framework output.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one universal observation protocol, and not a final method list. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6) and no last route (Fig 13 :90). Each habitat re-opens the residual.
- Not "more sampling always helps." True for rare; false for hidden, where a new instrument is owed, and for budget-limited hard-to-measure, where cost is the binding quantity.
- Not that non-detection is absence. "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that a single network metric (connectance, nestedness, robustness) settles observation quality. Those are second-order compositions with a measured R_T, not first-order ground readings.
- Not a derivation of any named instrument from the figures, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No network measurement was taken here, and no slot was settled.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator instrument plus independent frames ranged against the same non-updating web, not a further argument (the metamerism point in `05` section 4). The checkable questions:

- For a slot that was called rare: does distributed independent search drive its per-slot residual down, or does every new searcher draw on the same pool?
- For a slot that was called hidden: does an imported instrument register it at some resolution, and can the import's provenance and shadow test be shown? Would a null result from the old instrument have been a proof of absence (it is not, Fig 8 :39)?
- For a slot that was called hard to measure: what are the description cost and the action cost, held apart, and which one actually binds?
- Does enumerating to second order reveal an effect that a direct-contact-only design missed?
- Round trip: take a per-slot detection vector up to a network property and back down to site-level recommendations; measure the gap rather than assuming zero (Fig 4 :30).

**Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: global coverage preserving every live slot; a hidden interaction resolved by search alone with no imported frame; a partition with no residual intersection; method agreement from one pool changing the web; lossless recovery of a network property. Any of these fires against a specific clause.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The methods library is a visible guess at which channels can carry an interaction, not nature's own list. Another sensorium's method set need not be these four. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. The specific instrument that fills a slot is reported as a `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a universal way to observe ecological networks. It says the observation error must be reported per interaction slot, because one live slot can hide inside a good average; that rare, hidden, and hard-to-measure are overlapping conditions requiring different instruments, so the union must be split before any method is chosen; that rare means distributed independent search while hidden means importing an instrument from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test; that a hard-to-measure link has a description cost and an action cost that do not add; that the habitat and its second-order effects are enumerated from the world rather than taken from a standing list; and that methods must be ranged separately against a web that does not move when a reviewer thinks it did. It returns that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It refuses to name the winner, because it returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. The slots stay open until the ground settles them.
