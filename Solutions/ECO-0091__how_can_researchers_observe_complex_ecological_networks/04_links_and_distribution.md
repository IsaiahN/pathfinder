## Three things in the fetched literature that bear directly on the question

1. **Non-detection is not absence.** The eDNA and diet-DNA pages both describe false negatives and methods that reveal items invisible to morphology. The diet-DNA page reports coral-reef trophic partitioning as "likely underestimated" after metabarcoding. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." It does not prove the figure; it corroborates the shape.
2. **One accuracy number hides four outcomes.** Signal detection theory separates hit, miss, false alarm and correct rejection, and states that bias is independent of sensitivity. That is a low-overlap witness for Fig 1 :19 (residual per slot, not aggregated) and Fig 9 :60 (a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict).
3. **Direct contact is not enough.** The food-web and pollination-network pages describe indirect effects, trophic cascades and loss of interactions preceding species loss. The habitat must be enumerated outward, the world-side counterpart of Fig 11 :52-53.

Overlap to declare: the seven ecology/method pages come from one broad ecological-methods evidence pool, not seven independent witnesses. Wikipedia is a secondary synthesis and is used only as corroboration and for venue verification, never as the authority. The framework essays in `05` share the framework author with the figures. The signal-detection page is the only genuinely low-overlap witness, from psychophysics.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a full field report before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot residual and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in their own system if the shape claim were true, before showing the method literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in their own data; (3) record their verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.
- **No stage asks for trust.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
