## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before a reader has described a residual sends the interiors, not the claim. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40**, agreement inside one evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula may be given away as a method, but each user must bind their own sought-for shape and write their own refuter.
- **Fig 9 :60**, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A journal's impact factor or a society's prestige is a filter for where to spend effort, never a reason to accept a claim.
