# Expert verification packet - the Tether on monsoons under climate change

**What this is:** a claim in a form a climate dynamics researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the scenario-conditioned world numbers, not `grounded`.** No measurement in this packet is claimed as settled by this derivation.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"The monsoon response to climate change" is not one question. It is a union of three overlapping conditions, strength, location and timing, in each of several regional systems. The figure-governed shape is that a structure must sit between the surface states, that a gradient over space and one over time are different terms, that the habitat must be enumerated two or more steps out, and that the ground settles the numbers while ensembles are only working frames. The figures do not produce a sign or a magnitude; the regional numbers are scenario-conditioned world results reported separately. Four refuters were written before the run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading the figure extract

- **Statement:** a direct bond between two states is not composable, and a gradient over space is a different term from a gradient over time. Therefore strength, location and timing cannot be one scalar.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74, *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75, *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Fig 13 :76, *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*
- **Refuter:** derive any one of the six from the other five; or show the framework must admit a state-state or structure-structure composition it cannot refuse; or derive all three observables from one bond onto the same state so they collapse under Fig 12's symmetry test.

### C2 - regional-science-facing; checkable by a climate dynamicist

- **Statement:** the habitat of a monsoon extends at least two steps beyond the land-sea contrast, and the residual that matters can be remote. Aerosols, ocean heat uptake, Eurasian cloud feedback and ice belong in the census.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :44, *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52-53, *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** is the two-step habitat claim faithful to how the response works? The IPCC AR6 SPM (A.3.3) attributes the mid-20th-century monsoon decline to Northern Hemisphere aerosols. Chen, Hwang and Lu (2024) attribute the northward shift of South Asian rain to *"positive cloud feedback over the Eurasia Continent and heat uptake in the Southern Ocean"*. Are those first-order or second-order contacts in your frame?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a two-step enumeration that predicts every regional strength, location and timing change without residual, or show a remote process that cannot be in contact with the monsoon at any order. The first would make the cascade requirement idle; the second would bound it.

### C3 - verification-facing; checkable against ensemble practice

- **Statement:** agreement among models is not a verdict unless independence and ground contact are shown. Ensembles are filters; gauges, reanalysis and proxies are witnesses.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the full extracted line begins *"Correlation has valence"* and then the disqualifying clause). Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- **What to check:** Abramowitz et al. (2019, Earth System Dynamics) write that *"research groups share literature, ideas for representations of processes, parameterisations, evaluation data sets and even sections of model code"*, so *"nominally different models might have similar biases"*. IPCC AR6 Chapter 4 lists model independence among its ensemble evaluation methods. Does a 30-model consensus in your field survive an out-of-sample independence test?
- **Refuter:** present a set of projections with verified zero shared code, parameterisation or evaluation data whose agreement survives out-of-sample testing and is confirmed by an anchor that cannot move. That would be genuine triangulation and would narrow this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not one global monsoon verdict; a global index is a filter for effort.
- Not a figure-derived sign or magnitude for any region. That is a `gap` at the figure level.
- Not a derivation from climate science. The world sources are corroboration only, read and provenanced.
- Not a theory of everything, and no final room.
- Not that a model consensus is many witnesses.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

The ground, not agreement. Concretely:

1. A per-region, per-observable index with explicit onset and retreat thresholds, checked against gauges and reanalysis (the missing middle link of Fig 3).
2. An independence test on any ensemble used as a witness, with out-of-sample validation.
3. A source-discriminating mechanism test separating thermodynamic from dynamic control, in the spirit of He et al. (2025), who use paleo proxies to reconcile past strengthening with future weakening.
4. The four refuters in section 2, each of which would narrow or remove a sub-claim.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :74-76, Fig 11 :44 and :52-53, Fig 2 :35 and :40, Fig 7 :13. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: is the remote cascade claim (aerosols, ocean heat uptake, Eurasian clouds) correctly placed as two or more steps out?
3. Name one instrument or test that would discriminate thermodynamic from dynamic control for a specific regional monsoon, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
