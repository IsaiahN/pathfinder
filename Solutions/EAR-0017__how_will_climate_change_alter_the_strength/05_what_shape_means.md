## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; :135 adds, *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the constrained form of an answer you can state before you can fill in the regional numbers. It constrains; it does not fill. *"A monsoon response is a union of strength, location and timing, per region, with a cascade habitat and a ground-triangulated witness"* is a shape. *"Monsoon rain increases by 1.3 to 2.4 percent per degree"* is a measured number from a world source, not a figure output.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The analogue here is starting with "land-sea thermal contrast" and adding moisture convergence, energy flux equator, aerosol forcing and ocean heat uptake.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on bundle failures: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, written before that essay was opened and then confirmed by it.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means gauges, reanalysis and proxies, not model agreement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test. The paleo frame in domain 3 is such an import, so its result must carry its provenance.

The four essays add a third caution: *Library of Babel* warns that if the final book does not answer the question, *"the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed"*, and a bundle must be split. That is exactly why the numerical question is recorded as a `gap` at the figure level rather than answered by force.

## 4. Why a single global number is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or an undiscovered scheme can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A global monsoon index reads one green. The same index can arise from a strengthened South Asian monsoon with a weakened North American one, or from a uniform moderate increase. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a regional instrument, not a bigger average. This is the substantive reason behind Fig 1 :22 and F2.

## 5. ANSWERED: what does "shape" license here?

**Nothing in the framework licenses the sign or magnitude of a regional monsoon change.** The framework returns a shape. A number is licensed only by the ground settling a term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is a scenario-conditioned world result and must be recorded as such. F7 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` states this as `gap`.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, with one qualification. It does not name a missing primitive, and it does not promote a model consensus to a witness. The qualification is that the shape itself is a preference until it is bound as an ordinary term with a residual; section 0 of the audit performs that binding, and the pre-registered refuter in section 4 makes the preference refusable.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own standing

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of readers chosen for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim and a refutation in return.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the monsoon claim and cannot settle anything it reports on. This document says so on every substantive claim.
