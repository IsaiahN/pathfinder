## 0. The question as a residual, in effect terms

The question asks for the change in a monsoon under a change in forcing. State what fails to resolve before choosing any figure line.

- **R (the residual).** A monsoon is a seasonal reversal of wind and rainfall produced by the land-ocean thermal contrast. The observer holds the historical monsoon and the historical forcing, but the projected monsoon under changed forcing does not resolve to a single value. It resolves to a *distribution* whose sign is region-dependent and whose magnitude depends on the scenario and on how rapidly aerosols decline. The residual is the gap between what a given frame predicts and what the ground (gauges, reanalysis, paleo proxies) will actually return.
- **Arity.** At least three observables (strength, location, timing) crossed with several regional systems (South Asian, East Asian, West African, North and South American, Australian). This is a high-arity residual indexed by slot.
- **Symmetry.** None of the three observables is a symmetric rearrangement of another. Strength is a magnitude (an ordering, more or less). Location is a position in space. Timing is a position in the yearly cycle. They answer different questions about the same underlying gradient, so they are different terms, not three spellings of one.
- **Scale.** Regional (a single monsoon domain) versus global (an aggregate index), and first order (the direct response to forcing) versus second and third order (teleconnections through ocean heat uptake, cloud feedback, ice, aerosols, land use). The residual that matters is often several steps out.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term:** the sought-for answer is a per-slot statement with a stated ground and a refuter. Binding it now lets it accrue a residual like any other term (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## 1. Domain census, before choosing a figure line

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

- **Domain 1, physical mechanism of the monsoon.** Actors: land, ocean, atmosphere, cryosphere, aerosols, greenhouse gases, topography, ENSO. Currency: energy and moisture fluxes, the land-sea thermal gradient, precipitation. Anchor: observations, reanalysis and proxies (the ground). Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, the modelling and ensemble frame.** Actors: CMIP models, SSP scenarios, initial-condition members, weighting schemes. Currency: projections and ensemble spread. Anchor: the real climate system, never model agreement. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 3, the paleoclimate frame.** Actors: proxy records, warm intervals (mid-Pliocene, Last Interglacial, mid-Holocene), PMIP simulations. Currency: reconstructed rainfall and circulation. Anchor: the geological record. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 4, the verification and outreach frame.** Actors: climate dynamics readers. Currency: residuals and refutations. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all frames draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero and its agreement is internal consistency.

**Partition or union.** This is a **union**, not a partition. The three observables are overlapping conditions on one physical system, not three independent problems (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). A single lever that raises strength will not thereby settle location or timing, because the second condition still binds. The union must be unbundled before each part is searched (Fig 9 :54). The regional systems are a further union, so each region x observable pair gets its own loop.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception first: do gauges, reanalysis and proxies actually resolve each observable in each region (Fig 3 :27, *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*). Vocabulary second: thermal contrast, moisture convergence, energy flux equator. Objective third: define strength, location and timing as measurable indices; this is the middle link with no instrument and it attracts the least attention (Fig 3 :24-26). Planning fourth: scenario-conditioned projections. Learn and carry fifth: verify on the historical period before carrying forward.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - The question is a union of three conditions, not one hard question (decisive for structure)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*

Strength, location and timing are three conditions on one circulation. The IPCC assessment itself reports them in separate sentences: precipitation increases, circulation weakens, and onset and retreat shift (IPCC AR6 SPM B.3.3 and Chapter 4 Executive Summary, both opened). Treating "the monsoon response" as one scalar is the bundle the figure warns against. **Refuter:** a single figure-governed term that derives all three observables with the same bond and the same residual would collapse the union and refute this finding.

### F2 - The residual is indexed by slot, never averaged

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A global monsoon index is useful as a budget filter but cannot be a witness for a regional decision. Moon and Ha (2020, opened) split the global monsoon into nine regional domains precisely because *"Individual atmosphere–ocean–land interactions, land–ocean configurations, orography, and diverse monsoon variations across individual regions bring regional characteristics"*, and they find the Southern African monsoon decreasing in the near term while the Indian monsoon increases. One global mean would erase that. **Refuter:** a global index that predicts each regional sign and magnitude without a per-region residual would refute the slot rule.

### F3 - Strength, location and timing are different terms, not three values of one term

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*

Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."*

Fig 13 :76: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*

A monsoon reads as a state-structure-state chain: a thermal gradient (structure) sits between the land-ocean states, and its seasonal reversal is a second gradient. Location is the space-bonded gradient, timing is the time-bonded gradient, and strength is the magnitude of the moisture and energy flux the composition carries. Because the bond and the state it is attached to are part of the term, the three cannot be swapped. This matches the literature: Moon and Ha (2020) find a *"highly positive correlation"* between season length and rainfall increase yet treat them as separate diagnostics, and Chen et al. (2024, opened) separate a *"weakening component"* from a *"northward shift component"* of the same circulation. **Refuter:** show that location and timing compose from the same bond onto the same state, so they are one term under Fig 12's symmetry test.

### F4 - The habitat must be enumerated, and the residual that matters is several steps out

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*

Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

Fig 11 :46: *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"*

Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

The naive habitat is the land-sea contrast. The enumerated habitat also contains aerosols, ocean heat uptake, cloud feedback, ice, soil moisture and topography. Two opened sources show why the cascade matters: the IPCC SPM (A.3.3) attributes the mid-20th-century decline in monsoon rain to Northern Hemisphere aerosols, which then partly reversed as aerosol emissions fell; and Chen et al. (2024) trace the robust northward shift of South Asian rain to *"positive cloud feedback over the Eurasia Continent and heat uptake in the Southern Ocean"*, both far from the monsoon itself. A first-order census would miss both. **Refuter:** a demonstration that a two-step enumeration predicts every regional strength, location and timing change without residual would refute the cascade finding.

### F5 - The ground settles it; agreement inside one evidence pool does not

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

CMIP ensembles are the working frames for monsoon projections, but they are not independent witnesses. Abramowitz et al. (2019, opened) write that research groups *"share literature, ideas for representations of processes, parameterisations, evaluation data sets and even sections of model code"*, so *"nominally different models might have similar biases"*. The IPCC Chapter 4 (opened) explicitly includes *"assessment of model performance and independence"* among ensemble evaluation methods. A 30-model consensus that monsoon rain increases is therefore one evidence pool speaking, not thirty checks. The anchor remains gauges, reanalysis and proxies. **Refuter:** demonstrate a set of monsoon projections whose members have verified zero shared code, parameterisation or evaluation data and whose agreement then survives out-of-sample testing; that would be genuine triangulation.

### F6 - Instruments extend what can be represented; paleo is a low-overlap frame, not a talisman

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

Paleo proxies are instruments already returning something: speleothems, lake records and ice cores register past monsoon rainfall. He, Zhou and Guo (2025, opened) compile proxy records with simulations across the mid-Pliocene, Last Interglacial and mid-Holocene and show a coherent pattern that *"reconciles past strengthening with future weakening"*. This is an outward import in Formula step 7: describe R in effect terms and find the frame whose closure predicts its shape. It is not a name for a missing primitive, and a null result in one proxy would not be proof of absence. **Refuter:** show the paleo frame draws on the same evidence pool as the CMIP projections and adds no independent constraint, so the supposed low-overlap witness collapses.

### F7 - The figures return a shape, not the direction or magnitude of change

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

No figure line yields "plus 1.3 to 2.4 percent per degree" or "onset delays by three pentads". Those are results from the ground, reported by IPCC AR6 (opened) and Moon and Ha (2020, opened). The figure-governed claim is only the shape any answer must have: per slot, cross-kind, enumerated habitat, cascade-aware, ground-triangulated, with a recorded stop. The specific numbers are `specified`, scenario-conditioned world material, not figure derivations. The figure-level verdict on the numerical direction is therefore **`gap`**, stated plainly. **Refuter:** derive the sign and magnitude of a regional monsoon change from the six terms and seven bonds alone, with no world measurement, and the gap finding is withdrawn.

### F8 - Any condensation into a slogan has a measurable round trip gap

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

"Monsoons strengthen under warming" is the condensed form in circulation. Carrying it up from the figure text and back down loses at least four constraints: per-slot indexing, the distinction among strength, location and timing, the multi-step habitat, and the independence requirement on witnesses. `11_audit.md` enumerates this R_T. Treating the slogan as identical to the figure set would set the round trip gap to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error the figure names. **Refuter:** exhibit a lossless condensation that preserves all four constraints in both directions, so R_T is zero.

### F9 - Feedback and chaos are structural, not decorative

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*

Fig 13 :32: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."*

Cloud, ice, soil moisture and ocean feedbacks re-enter the monsoon as input, so the composition contains cycles and cannot be a plain chain. The IPCC Chapter 4 (opened) reports that near-term monsoon change is uncertain because of *"model uncertainty and internal variability such as Atlantic Multi-decadal Variability and Pacific Decadal Variability"*. Chaos is what a compounding remainder looks like from inside. This is why near-term projections are `specified` with low confidence and why timing, the most seasonal of the three observables, is the least certain. **Refuter:** a figure-governed derivation in which every feedback loop is absent and the residual still closes would refute the cycle finding.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before any conclusion or outreach, per Fig 5 :66: *"Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A monsoon is shown to compose as a direct bond between two states with no structural intermediary (breaks Fig 13 :75).
2. A single global monsoon index predicts every regional strength, location and timing change without a per-region residual (breaks Fig 1 :19 and :22).
3. Strength, location and timing are derivable from one bond onto the same state, so they collapse to one term under Fig 12's symmetry test (breaks Fig 13 :76 and F3).
4. Agreement among models, with no independence test and no ground reading, is accepted as settling a regional projection (breaks Fig 2 :40 and Fig 7 :13).

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn and the failure is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
