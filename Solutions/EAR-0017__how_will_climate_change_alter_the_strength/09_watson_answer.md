## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the numerical direction is a `gap` at the figure level. "The monsoon response" is a union of strength, location and timing in each of several regions. A structure must sit between the surface states, the space-bonded and time-bonded gradients are different terms, the habitat must be enumerated at least two steps out, and the ground settles the numbers while ensembles are working frames. Regional precipitation is reported by IPCC as likely to increase, with circulation weakening, a northward shift in South Asia, regional divergence in timing, and asymmetric confidence between onset and retreat. Those are secondary, scenario-conditioned results, not figure outputs. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the numbers, `candidate` until the ground settles them.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries four written refuters and names its gap, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a global number or a model consensus instead of a per-slot claim with a refuter. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The regional numbers are removable without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already keep them separate. A superseded claim can be corrected, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent packet can be replaced by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-region index with explicit onset and retreat thresholds checked against gauges and reanalysis would settle timing; an independence test with out-of-sample validation would tell us whether an ensemble is a witness; a source-discriminating mechanism test of the kind He et al. (2025) attempt would separate thermodynamic from dynamic control; and a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a direct state-state monsoon bond, would break the shape claim. Prestige or the size of a consensus is not such an instrument.
