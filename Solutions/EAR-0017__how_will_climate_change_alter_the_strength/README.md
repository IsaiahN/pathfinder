## Restating the question as an effect

The decision is what to expect from a monsoon, a seasonal reversal of wind and rainfall driven by the land-ocean thermal contrast, when the forcing changes. Three observables are named: strength (how much rain and how strong the circulation), location (where the rain band and convergence sit), and timing (onset, retreat, duration). Each option leads somewhere different. A wetter-but-weaker monsoon changes flood and drought risk differently from a shifted monsoon or a longer season. An answer that averages the three into one global number throws away the signal that decides anything (Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula step "Direction"):** a per-region, per-observable statement of change, with a stated scenario, a stated ground, and a refuter written before the run. The sought-for shape is bound so it can accrue a residual like anything else, rather than left as an invisible preference.

## Boundary and standing

This folder contains a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap says LLM output is *"simulacra"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Nothing here can settle the question it reports on. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6.

Nothing outside this solution folder was modified. Figure quotes are cited as `Fig N :line` against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, never from a summary. World sources were opened and are recorded with author, venue, year, accessed date, and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a projection.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40) CMIP models share literature, parameterisations and code, so a large ensemble is closer to one witness than to many (Abramowitz et al., 2019, read and cited in `10_source_ledger.csv`).
3. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels are used instead.
