## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether one scalar can meaningfully order systems by intelligence across tasks and contexts. Bind the sought shape first: a function from a vector of per task observations to one real number, invariant to which tasks are sampled and predictive of a held out task (direction discipline, `THE_FORMULA.md`). Option A: accept the scalar as a ground verdict about capability. Option B: split it, keeping the scalar as ordering and convention while refusing it as a verdict, and reframe the object as agent-and-habitat. What each leads to: A discards the per slot residual and spends the independence of the people checking it; B keeps a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes as ordering and convention; no as a ground verdict from an aggregated scalar; and the question is reframed as capability in a habitat. Per domain: D1 triangulated no, D2 gap left to the ground, D3 triangulated yes, D4 single-source reframing. The empirical existence of a general factor is not decided here. This is `general` on the shape, `specified` on the verdicts, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, binds the candidate scalar, and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking as a verdict instead of a shape with its refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the verdict split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The verdict split is removable without touching the figure text, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the empirical g question stays open for instruments.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A portable scalar that preserves per slot residuals and predicts held out tasks reverses D1. An instrument separating g from sampling accounts settles D2. An agent only intervention that moves the ground across habitats reverses D4. Zero round trip gap collapses the aggregate and the profile into one term. A commensurable unit for description and action cost clears the second currency finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
