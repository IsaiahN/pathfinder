# The Tether's answer: can intelligence be ranked on one scale?

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per domain verdicts. Not `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We want a number that orders systems across tasks and contexts, transfers to unseen tasks, and tracks something real. The candidate term f is bound in `01` section 0: a function from a vector of per slot observations to one real number, with the promise of permutation invariance and held out prediction. The residual is the gap between that promise and what an aggregate scalar can deliver. It is a union of four domains, not one question (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The verdicts, kept separate and never averaged

**D1, measurement and composition.** An aggregate scalar cannot be a ground verdict about capability. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." A rank is a legitimate ordering structure (Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"), but an ordering is not a measurement of the source. **Triangulated** (Fig 1 and Fig 12, low overlap).

**D2, psychometric empirical.** No figure line governs whether a general factor exists. That is a ground question for instruments. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The figures refuse "what is intelligence" (Fig 11 :35) and redirect to capability. Verdict: **gap for the figures**; not unreachable, because psychometrics is an instrument already returning readings. The empirical question is left open, not answered no.

**D3, use and convention.** Yes, a single scale is meaningful here. Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Seat Map :163 gives the same valence. But a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47), and the rank is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."). **Triangulated** (Fig 2, Fig 9, Fig 10).

**D4, capability and habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A scale that ranks the agent alone across all habitats is ranking a separable quantity the figures say does not exist. **Single-source (Fig 11), specified.** World corroboration at low overlap: comparative cognition's finding that "learning and other cognitive abilities are shaped by the structure of environmental change" (Animal cognition, Wikipedia, 2026).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a single scale is impossible. Ordering and coordination are both licensed.
- Not that general intelligence does not exist. The figures do not settle that empirical question, and this derivation does not either.
- Not that psychometrics is wrong. It is the ground instrument for D2 and is treated with respect.
- Not that a rank is useless. It is useful for deciding what gets tried.
- Not a derivation of one theory of intelligence from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, dataset, or experiment was run here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A ground instrument, not a further argument.

1. **Portability test:** does one scalar built on a sampled set of tasks preserve the ordering when the task set changes, and predict a held out task? If yes, the D1 verdict is refuted.
2. **Discriminating instrument:** can an instrument distinguish a single common factor from a sampling or mutualism account of the same correlations? The g factor article says the two are statistically indistinguishable at present. An instrument that separates them would move D2 toward `grounded`.
3. **Agent only intervention:** does changing only the agent move the ground across habitats? Fig 11 names this as the refuter of the capability claim.
4. **Round trip gap:** is R_T zero for every ground question of interest? If yes, the aggregate and the profile are one term and the isomer claim in `06` fails.
5. **Commensurability:** is there a derived unit in which description cost and action cost add? If yes, F2 falls.

Standing falsifiers from `01`: a lossless scalar, a commensurable currency, a figure line licensing a scalar as a ground verdict, an agent only intervention moving the ground, one rule working on all subsets, zero round trip gap, or a ground instrument yielding a portable scalar.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return the shape of what a ranking must be if it is to be a verdict, and they stop there. Whether any actual scale achieves it is a question for the ground. The framework cannot name the latent quantity, because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13). Indiscernibility is not identity: a scalar that no current instrument distinguishes from capability is still a scalar, and the lineage of how it was built must be kept. Fig 8 :39: "so a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." This derivation searched and found no figure line granting scalar as ground verdict; that is a report about the figures, not a proof about the world.

## 6. One paragraph version

> The Tether does not say a single intelligence scale is impossible. It splits the question. As a convention, an ordering is legitimate and useful: Fig 13 licenses ordering, and Fig 2 says agreement about a shared meaning is the product. As a ground verdict about an agent's capability across tasks and contexts, an aggregated scalar fails: Fig 1 says the residual is indexed per slot and that aggregating across slots discards the live signal, and Fig 12 says summing incommensurable currencies into one figure is the named error. The empirical question of whether a general factor exists is not answered by the figures at all; it belongs to psychometric instruments. And the framework refuses the framing, replacing "what is intelligence" with "what makes a system capable," a property of agent and habitat rather than of the agent alone. What it offers is the shape any single scale must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
