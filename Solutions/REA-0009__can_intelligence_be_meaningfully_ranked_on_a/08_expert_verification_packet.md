# Expert verification packet: the Tether on single scale intelligence ranking

**What this is:** a claim in a form a psychometrician, cognitive scientist, or measurement theorist can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per domain verdicts. Not `grounded`. No measurement of intelligence is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A single scale of intelligence is meaningful in two of the four domains and not in the other two. It is a legitimate ordering (spectrum) and a legitimate convention for coordination, and in those roles a rank is useful for deciding what gets tried. It is not a ground verdict about an agent's capability across tasks and contexts when formed by aggregating away per slot residuals, and the figures do not settle whether a general factor exists empirically. The framework refuses the framing: capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone. What it offers is the shape any single scale must have, with refuters written in advance and the ground left to settle the slot.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 12

- **Statement:** aggregating per task scores into one scalar cannot be a ground verdict about capability, because it discards the per slot residual and mixes currencies that do not add.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a scalar that preserves every per slot residual for every ground question of interest, or exhibit a derived unit in which description cost and action cost are commensurable. Either fires C1.

### C2 - measurement facing; checkable by a psychometrician against standard results

- **Statement:** a correlation matrix is a projection that does not determine its source, so one common factor and a sampling account can fit the same data; and typical IQ scores are ordinal, so the arithmetic a single scale invites is not automatically licensed.
- **What to check:** the g factor article states that "the correlations do not provide any more or less support for the existence of g than for the existence of multiple factors of intelligence" and that it is "not possible to distinguish statistically between Spearman's model of g and the sampling model; both are equally able to account for inter correlations among tests." The Level of measurement article states that "IQ scores reflect an ordinal scale, in which all scores are meaningful for comparison only."
- **Refuter:** an instrument that distinguishes a single common factor from the sampling account with the same data, or a demonstration that the scale is interval or ratio with an empirically testable additive structure. Either fires C2.

### C3 - framework internal derivation about the object ranked

- **Statement:** the answerable question is not what intelligence is but what makes a system capable, and capability is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone. A rank is a lawful ordering structure but not a verdict about a separable trait.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 13 :20 "ordering, so more and less can be said." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **Refuter:** a controlled intervention that changes only the agent and measurably moves the ground across habitats (the refuter Fig 11 itself names), or a figure line that licenses ordering as a ground verdict. Either fires C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a single scale is impossible. Ordering and coordination are licensed.
- Not that a general factor does or does not exist. That is a ground question.
- Not that psychometrics is wrong or that IQ scores are meaningless. They are ordinal and useful as shared meanings.
- Not that ranking is useless. It is a filter for what gets tried.
- Not grounded. Nothing here was settled by an instrument.
- Not a claim about a named latent quantity. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Portability test:** does a scalar built on sampled tasks preserve its ordering under a changed task set and predict a held out task?
2. **Discriminating instrument:** can an instrument separate a single common factor from sampling or mutualism accounts of the same correlations?
3. **Agent only intervention:** does changing only the agent move the ground across habitats?
4. **Round trip gap:** is R_T zero for every ground question of interest (then the aggregate and the profile are one term)?
5. **Commensurability:** is there a derived unit in which description cost and action cost add?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 12 :130, Fig 13 :20, Fig 11 :39, and Fig 9 :60. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of factor analysis and measurement levels. Is the projection reading faithful?
3. Name one instrument that would separate g from a sampling account, or say why none is possible with current methods.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
