## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: `you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself`. Fig 13 :134: `Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence`. The figures README: the framework reports `what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)`.

From *The Library of Babel*: `You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question.`

So for ECN-0033 a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name the coefficient: a union of regimes, separate slots for the two effects, an anchor that does not update, thresholds used as filters, and a recorded loss where a flow becomes a stock. It constrains. It does not fill. "One coefficient for deficits" is a name. "A per-regime table with a refuter per row" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then let recurring terms narrow the space. `With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary.` Here the bootstrap vocabulary was deficit, inflation, interest rate, accommodation, debt/GDP, term premium, expectations anchor.
- Fig 6 :30: `The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms.` Fig 9 :30: `your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause`. The residual was stated as a change in two observed quantities under named conditions, not as "fiscal dominance" first.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: `When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world.` That is the stage-1 email in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: `the finish line will also be the starting place`. Here the initial effect description and the final shape are the same object at different resolution: conditions in, conditions out.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: `the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground.` A measured estimate from a real dataset is a ground reading.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. A coefficient borrowed from another field must say where it came from and which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: `The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space.` It also says `A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six`, and `Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible.` The choice to treat inflation and rates as separate slots is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why the question's "both" is a metamerism trap

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and `cannot comment on the source`: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. `If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information.` It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The parallel here: a rise in long yields is a single reading with several sources. It can come from term premium, expected real rates, expected inflation, or a supply shift in Treasuries. Plante, Richter and Zubairy attribute roughly three quarters of their estimated rise to term premium. Naming the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument (a decomposition), not a name. The essay states the stop plainly: `What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists.`

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid reporting the 3 basis point estimate? ANSWERED, with a correction to my own §3

**First reading:** yes, any number is a name and must be withheld. **Correction, recorded and not silently erased:** the README's phrase governs `a missing thing`, that is a primitive not held in the library. The 3 basis point estimate is not a primitive invented by the framework. It is a ground reading from a published dataset with a stated instrument (five-year-ahead five-year Treasury rate versus CBO debt/GDP projections). Reporting it as world corroboration is allowed; reporting it as a figure-derived verdict is not. What remains wrong would be to let that one estimate stand for the whole union compound, because that would read the source off a single projection.

So: numbers are allowed as secondary ground reports with provenance. They are not allowed to decide the shape. Both claims are now recorded.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: `Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly` and `Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige.`

For this field, the failure is a list of well-known economists assembled to agree that "deficits cause inflation" or that "deficits do not matter." Either list buys a conclusion with prestige and never consults the ground. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the per-regime claim a budget to be checked, let a measurement refuse it, and decline to pool later agreement.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* puts the same point in data terms: `Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times.` Its ground rule is `Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it` (Fig 2 :45, quoted in that essay).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: `the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself.` *How Intelligence Actually Works*: `Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself.` *Arrival of the Fittest* warns that a simulated output `could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula`.

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the deficit claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. That limitation is stated on every substantive file in this folder.

## 8. Consequence for the outreach order

The shape is a set of conditions, and conditions are cheap to state and expensive to color with authority. Therefore: stage 1 carries the residual, the shape and the refuter; stage 2 carries the links; stage 3 carries the method. *How Intelligence Actually Works* says contact resolves the question: `the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted`. A recipient who is handed the full framework before being asked for a verdict is not making contact with the residual; they are joining the frame.
