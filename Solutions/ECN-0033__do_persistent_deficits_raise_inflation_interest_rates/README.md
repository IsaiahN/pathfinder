## Residual in effect terms

What changes in two observed quantities, the rate of change of the general price level (inflation) and the price of borrowing (interest rates), when a government runs a deficit persistently, and under which conditions does that change become large, small or absent? The four offered answers (inflation, rates, both, limited in some circumstances) are not four competing measurements. Figure 9 :54 says *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* So the residual is a union compound and the fourth answer is the instruction to split, not a fourth outcome.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: `A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes.` Adding agreeable recipients raises vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: `agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool`. A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: `A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.` The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
