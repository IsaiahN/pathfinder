# The Tether's answer: do persistent deficits raise inflation, interest rates, or both?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The four offered answers are a union, not a list. Fig 9 :54  "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :56  "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The fourth answer, "limited in some circumstances," is the instruction to split. It is not a fourth magnitude to be averaged with the first three.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**Domain 1, fiscal financing.**
Actors: treasury, legislature, voters, issuers. Currency: budget balance as flow, debt as stock. Anchor: the government budget constraint, which does not move because a model predicts otherwise. Verdict: the deficit alone cannot fix the effect; the financing instrument does work. Friedman's crowd-out/crowd-in result and Bordo and Levy's wartime cases both turn on *how* the deficit was financed, not on its size. Mode `specified`. **Triangulated** (Fig 9 union rule plus two low-overlap empirical frames).

**Domain 2, monetary and price formation.**
Actors: central bank, bond market, households, firms. Currency: money, price level, nominal yields. Anchor: measured inflation and measured yields. Verdict: the inflation response is conditional on the monetary and expectations regime. Fig 2 :33  "The anchor must not update." The inflation *target* revises, so it coordinates expectations and cannot settle the verdict. Catão and Terrones find no strong association among low-inflation advanced economies; Bordo and Levy find the link in wartime and its absence after 2007-2008; Angeletos, Lian and Wolf state two testable conditions. Mode `specified`. **Triangulated** with declared field overlap.

**Domain 3, interest-rate formation.**
Actors: bond market, issuers, term-premium traders. Currency: yields, maturity, risk premia. Anchor: observed Treasury rates. Verdict: a rate response exists but is bounded and mostly a term premium. Fig 1 :19  "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22  "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The 3 basis point estimate from Plante, Richter and Zubairy is a ground report, not a figure-derived number. Mode `specified`. **Triangulated** (Fig 1 plus the high-frequency estimate).

**Domain 4, measurement and verification.**
Actors: datasets, econometricians, historical episodes. Currency: estimates, standard errors. Anchor: the data, with instrument limits. Verdict: agreement among studies using the same pool is one witness. Fig 2 :40  "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 8 :39  "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No figure line supplies a coefficient, so magnitudes are a `gap`, not a refusal of the question. Mode `general` for the limit. **Single-source** (Fig 8 plus the figures README).

**Across domains, never averaged.** Fig 13 :20  "ordering, so more and less can be said" (inflation as spectrum); Fig 13 :22  "a difference that can be spent" (rates as gradient). They are two kinds of quantity, so "both" means two bonds, not one effect at two strengths. The flow-to-stock crossing is lossy: Fig 4 :30  "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Thresholds are filters: Fig 9 :60  "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one coefficient for the general case. No figure line governs a magnitude; that is a `gap` (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that deficits are harmless. "Limited in some circumstances" is a conditional finding, not a blanket exemption.
- Not that the 90 percent debt/GDP cut is a verdict. It is a comparison, a filter for search budget.
- Not that the central bank's target is the anchor. It is a convention that revises (Fig 2 :33).
- Not that inflation "causes" the rate or the rate "causes" inflation. Under `06_isomers_and_order.md` they are a functional group, and the stronger isomer claim needs its own falsifier.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a new measurement.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

1. A low-overlap natural experiment isolating a deficit shock under a fixed monetary regime, with inflation and yields measured separately. A natural experiment in a different closure from the panel and time-series literature would buy the independence Fig 2 :42 requires: `Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.`
2. A source-discriminating decomposition of long-yield moves, separating term premium, expected real rates and expected inflation. This is the spectrometer step from *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that can comment on the source, not just the green reading.
3. An out-of-sample test of the condition variables. If monetary accommodation, currency denomination, slack and expectations regime do not predict where the effect lands, the conditional shape fails.
4. A test of the flow-to-stock crossing: a deficit with no debt accumulation versus one with a large stock, holding financing mix fixed. This measures `R_T` instead of assuming it.

Standing refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 apply. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, not the number.** The figures give a union, separate slots, a non-updating anchor, filters not witnesses and a lossy crossing. They do not give a coefficient. The record stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and keeps the lineage so the stop is checkable. `Indiscernibility is not identity`: a conditional shape is not the measured world.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce one number for the effect of persistent deficits. It says the question is a bundle of overlapping conditions, so it must be split before it is searched; that inflation and interest rates are different quantities and must be reported in separate rows, never averaged; that the measured data are the anchor while a central bank's inflation target is a convention that can revise; that a debt/GDP threshold is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what the deficit did; and that "persistent" names a lossy flow-to-stock crossing whose gap must be recorded. Per regime, the world says the inflation link is strong in high-inflation and emerging-market episodes and weak or absent in low-inflation advanced economies, while the rate link is real but small and mostly a term premium. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The coefficient stays open until a low-overlap ground reading settles it.
