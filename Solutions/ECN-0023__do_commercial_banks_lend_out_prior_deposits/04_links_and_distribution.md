## What bears directly on the question

1. **The bond test can be read off a ledger.** The Bank of England article's wording that bank lending creates deposits, together with the double-entry rule from the accounting source, gives a discriminator that is checkable without accepting any theory: look at the origin of the deposit entry. That is the world-side counterpart to Fig 12 :79.
2. **The reported experiment is the one genuine ground contact.** Werner reports monitoring a cooperating bank's internal records during a loan. It is a single reported reading. The BoE article, its IDEAS record and Jakab/Kumhof form one overlapping pool, not three witnesses.
3. **There is a real conflict to preserve.** Nelson accepts the mechanism and rejects the framing. By the conflict rule this sends us back to the census, which separates the creation leg from the settlement leg. It does not flip the bond verdict.
4. **The multiplier is strained in both theory and reported data.** Disyatat and Carpenter/Demiralp attack it from monetary policy implementation and from VAR evidence respectively. They are different instruments, so their agreement is weaker than a shared-pool echo, though both are economics-frame sources.
5. **A failure to find the attribution share is not a proof of absence.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* That is why the attribution question is recorded as `gap`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** says *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the bond test and the refuter, not the full framework.
- **Fig 10 :42** says *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* An outside frame that has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** says *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would count as a failure before showing them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** says agreement from a shared pool is worthless for a verdict. A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 7 :35** says *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* No number of reviewers upgrades the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the bond test and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
