# Expert verification packet: the Tether on bank lending and deposits

**What this is:** a claim in a form a banking or monetary economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the bond shape and the union split, `specified` on the domain applications, `gap` on attribution and constraint mix. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken by this packet.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The two familiar stories assert different typed bonds between a loan L and a deposit D. "Banks lend out prior deposits" asserts sequence, `D → L`: the deposit is a prior stock. "Lending creates deposits" asserts production, `L ⇒ D`: the deposit is absent until the loan fires. The framework separates these bonds with a test, *"is there a B before A fires? No."* On a bank ledger the deposit is credited as a new liability when the loan is disbursed, so production fits the reported ground. The forced "or" is nevertheless malformed: existing-deposit funding and lending-created deposits are both real at different scales, and the money multiplier is a strained system-level import. Creation and settlement funding form a signed loop with different constraints; the deposit share attributable to lending at a moment is declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** the two options are distinguished by bond type, and the production bond, not sequence, is the one whose test matches a loan disbursement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :77 *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*; Fig 12 :79 *"is there a B before A fires? No."*; Fig 12 :72 *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*; Operators table :40 *"different from sequence: the second operand is not waiting, it is absent until the first fires"*.
- **Refuter:** a ledger trace showing the universal mechanism is a pure transfer of a pre-existing deposit with no new deposit liability created anywhere; or a bond re-census showing sequence is correct (R1, R3).

### C2: banking-facing, checkable against bank accounting and payment settlement

- **Statement:** when a loan is disbursed the bank credits a new deposit liability; when that deposit is spent to another bank, reserve settlement is a separate loop. The constraint set is capital, liquidity, profitability, the policy rate and credit demand, not a prior stock of deposits.
- **What to check:** is this faithful to double-entry practice and to the central bank's operating framework in the jurisdiction you know? Does the reserve leg reverse the bond, or is it a separate composition?
- **Source, verbatim for the separate-currency claim:** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* and Fig 12 :121 *"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **World report for cross-check:** Bank of England, McLeay, Radia and Thomas, 2014, states that banks do not simply lend out deposits nor multiply up central bank money. Werner, 2014, reports a monitored-loan experiment.
- **Refuter:** data showing a stable reserve → deposit → loan multiplier with reserves exogenous (R2); or an attribution instrument showing the lending share negligible (R4).

### C3: framework-internal derivation about question form

- **Statement:** the "or" is a union compound and must be split; the theories of banking are isomers over a shared atom set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*; Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that one of the theories needs an atom the others lack, which reduces the claim to a functional group; or a demonstration that only one funding mechanism is real in a given system, which makes it a partition (falsifiers in `06`).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that banks are unconstrained or that capital and liquidity rules do not bind.
- Not that the money multiplier is false in every regime. Per-slot regime data are a `gap`.
- Not that this packet takes a ground reading. It reads reports of other people's instruments.
- Not that the Bank of England, RePEc records and commentators are independent witnesses. They share a pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No claim here is asserted as settled by this packet's own measurement.
- Not a policy prescription. The framework returns a shape, not a recommendation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* reports that metamerism was resolved by a spectrometer, not by a debate. Here:

1. **Ledger Test:** an origin field on the deposit entry at loan disbursement, showing whether a new liability was created.
2. **Payment Test:** a trace of the outflow and the reserve acquisition that settles it.
3. **Multiplier Test:** a per-slot test of whether reserve changes precede proportional loan changes.
4. **Attribution Instrument:** a decomposition of deposit growth by source at a moment.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :72, :77, :79, :94 and :117, and Operators table :40. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of bank accounting: is the loan credit to the borrower's deposit a new liability, and is reserve settlement a separate leg?
3. Name one instrument that would attribute deposit growth to lending at a moment, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
