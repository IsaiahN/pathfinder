## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :23, :27, Fig 6 :25, Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 2 :29-30 and Fig 10 :24-26.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: disclosure is a term not an atom; description and behaviour are two channels; ratings are instruments; the union must be unbundled; a shadowing study would settle it.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a firm or a sample, for example "firms adopting SASB standards reduce pollution, therefore disclosure changes behaviour."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **counterfactual and self-selection** requirements. Bochkay/Choi/Hales report outcomes after adoption, and the MIT assurance study reports self-selection. The shorthand "disclosure changes behaviour" drops both and would turn a correlation into a production claim (`⇒`), which `06` explicitly refuses.
- the **unit of the comparison**. "Mainly" is a comparison bond over two quantities in different units. The shorthand does not carry the declared scale, so concretising it silently substitutes a rating for an outcome.
- the **boundary-revision case**. The assurance study shows the reported number can move while the physical measurement is merely revealed. The shorthand "channel repaired" can be concretised as "behaviour changed" unless the boundary test is carried explicitly.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the disclosure act adds a term, not an atom, and the two channels need separate instruments. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
