## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that voluntary ESG disclosure changes company behaviour rather than mainly changing self-description. Option A: the disclosure act itself changes behaviour in the world. Option B: the disclosure act mainly changes description, with any behavioural effect a separate causal chain. What each leads to: A requires a causal chain through changed action and changed contact, measured at verified outcomes; B is the structural default of the act considered alone, because a term added to a library is not an atom added to the world. The figures decide the shape of the check, not which branch occurs in a sample.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The differential response of two channels to a voluntary disclosure event. Arity: one act, two consequents, indexed by firm and time. Symmetry: asymmetric; firm and ground are not interchangeable, and tonnes of CO2 and rating points are not. Scale: the single firm loop, the population of reporting firms, and the time channel across reporting years. Fig 9 names it a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the disclosure act.** Actors: managers, standard setters, report text. Currency: description, predicates minted, credibility accrued. Anchor: the dated public filing, for the act only; not for its truth. Question type: frame-internal, cannot self-score.
- **Domain 2, the physical and social ground.** Actors: emissions, injuries, incidents, workers, communities. Currency: verified physical and social outcomes. Anchor: independently verified measurement (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **Domain 3, the reading frame.** Actors: ESG agencies, investors, analysts, business press. Currency: ratings, rankings, capital flows. Anchor: capital actually committed; ratings are instruments. Admitted with ratings demoted.
- **Domain 4, the assurance channel.** Actors: independent assurers, audit committees, regulators. Currency: verification and correction. Anchor: independently verified restatement. Admitted.
- **Excluded X1, sincerity of intent.** No anchor independent of the report and the manager's own account. Not a domain yet.
- **Partition or union:** union. D1/D2, D2/D3 and D3/D4 overlap, so each overlap is a compound with its own loop. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency:** perceive real outcomes, acquire vocabulary for what disclosure affords, state the objective in physical units, plan the counterfactual, learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse the proxy before minting a new outcome claim (Formula step 2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical question stays open. A voluntary ESG disclosure adds a term to the firm's and its observers' libraries, not an atom to the world. A change in description is therefore the structural default of the act alone, while a change in behaviour is a separate causal chain that must change contact with a paying ground and be measured at independently verified outcomes. The two effects are a union of conditions, never averaged. Ratings are instruments, not anchors. The world literature supplies both branches from one evidence pool, which is exactly why neither is a figure verdict. The framework returns the shape any check must have: per-firm indexing, an anchor on verified outcomes, a union unbundled before a lever is chosen, a declared scale for "mainly", and a source-discriminating instrument that this package does not hold. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a blended "ESG score" or a receptive list instead of the two-channel shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused readings (rating as anchor, identity of score and outcome, the isomer name) are removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A prospectively registered two-channel design with verified physical outcomes and a blinded description measure, a counterfactual and no pooled average, would settle the branch. Also: a rating tracking verified outcomes at credit-rating-level correlation and unmoved by stakeholder pressure (breaks the instrument reading); one instrument collapsing the union with no residue (breaks Fig 9 :57); a disclosure whose valence is invariant across a residual change (breaks Fig 11 :49); or a figure-level failure. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. The live strain is the operand scale for the comparison bond: it needs an instrument, not an argument.
