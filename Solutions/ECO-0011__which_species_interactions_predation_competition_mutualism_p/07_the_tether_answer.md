# The Tether's answer: which species interaction most strongly shapes ecosystem resilience?

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a refusal, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

**Tether status: `gap` for the ranking as asked.** "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a total order over predation, competition, mutualism, parasitism and facilitation by how strongly each shapes ecosystem resilience. The residual is the mismatch between a predicted recovery after a perturbation and the recovery actually measured, plus the unstated demand that this mismatch be explained by one interaction type. The ranking is a union compound (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*), not one hard question with five candidates. It has its own ground: a resilience measurement that does not move when a frame changes its mind (Fig 2 :29).

## 2. The shape

1. **The candidates are relations, not kinds.** Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* So no type carries a fixed strength that a ranking could record.
2. **The habitat is enumerated, never ranked.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :47 lists relations as examples of what to enumerate: *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency."* The figure supplies the list procedure, not a strength order.
3. **Resilience is per slot, not one average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global champion would hide the very slot that carries the live signal.
4. **The dominant effect may be indirect.** Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* So even a well-measured first-order ranking would miss the case where the residual lives three steps out.
5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Resilience belongs to the ecosystem together with its habitat and contact structure, not to one interaction isolated from them.
6. **The comparison needs a currency that the question does not supply.** Fig 12 :86 calls ⋛ *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Five heterogeneous interaction types are not two commensurable quantities. A common currency must be imported and provenanced. This is a live strain, named in F8, not smoothed over.
7. **What the framework returns.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The shape is a procedure: name the failure and its metric, enumerate contacts outward, and identify which alteration most reduces R.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that competition, predation, mutualism, parasitism or facilitation is the winner. No type is named.
- Not that the five types do not matter. They are the relations the habitat enumeration must include; the refusal is of a *standing ranking*, not of their causal role.
- Not that resilience cannot be measured. It can, with an anchor; the point is that no anchor is specified in the question.
- Not a claim that all contexts are different. A context-invariant order is exactly refuter 1 and would withdraw the verdict.
- Not `grounded`. No ecological measurement was taken in this derivation.
- Not a derivation from Fig 13's physical six-term set. That earlier reading was retracted (R1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A ground reading, not agreement. The four pre-registered refuters, written before the conclusion:

1. A standing total order of the five types invariant across residuals and habitats (refutes points 1, 2 and 5).
2. An anchor measurement that does not move when the observing frame changes, under which a stable one-currency order appears across two independent perturbation types (refutes points 3 and 6).
3. A case where one direct named pairwise interaction carries strictly more of the residual than every second- or third-order contact path (refutes point 4).
4. An interaction-only improvement that moves the ground while leaving contact unchanged (refutes point 5).

Candidate instruments already in the world literature: scaled recovery rates and distances to tipping points (Zhang et al. 2022), multi-layer robustness and emergent keystone rankings (Domínguez-García and Kéfi 2024), and context-resolved interaction outcomes (Pringle 2016, Chamberlain et al. 2014). These are the instruments that could fire; none was fired here.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figures stop where the instruments stop. A ranking might be reachable once a common resilience currency is declared and an anchor settles it, or it might be a genuine union that must always be split. From inside this derivation the two look the same, and Fig 8 :39 says *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* What is recorded is the lineage: the census, the re-census, the one live strain, and the four refuters. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a champion among predation, competition, mutualism, parasitism and facilitation, and it does not claim such a champion exists. It says that these are relations whose effect depends on the specific resilience failure being tracked, that the habitat must be enumerated rather than ranked, that the residual must be read per species or patch rather than as one global average, and that the effect that matters can be three steps out in a contact cascade. The result is a shape and a refusal: name the failure and its metric, enumerate contacts outward, and identify which alteration most reduces the residual. The framework does not supply the common currency needed to compare the five types, so that strain is recorded rather than hidden. What it offers is the shape any acceptable answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that could close it. The ranking stays open until the ground settles it.
