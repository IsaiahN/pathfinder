## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no receptiveness column, no prestige-weighted evidence.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's derivation.
3. **Contact cost is not evidence cost.** Prestige and accessibility may choose *how* to approach a checker; they never decide whether the answer counts.

Legally: public opt-in submission and contact routes avoid the failure mode of unsolicited bulk email under GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
