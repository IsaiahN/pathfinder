## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: *for a specified resilience failure, enumerate the habitat's relations and indirect contacts outward, and identify which alteration most reduces the residual.* A ranking such as "competition > mutualism > predation" would be a name, and the figures do not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. Opened this session: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar's 2026-11-22 reading): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- The same essay names the malformed-question case directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. That is the precise situation in F5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar's reading): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

A resilience metric imported from ecology into this derivation is such a binding. It must be recorded as a binding, with its source, rather than presented as something the figures named. This is exactly the F8 strain: to compare five interaction types, a common currency must be imported and provenanced.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried from the exemplar's reading) gives the mechanism: metamerism, where an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can create the same reading, so the reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

Applied here: a global resilience score is a reading. Naming "competition" or "mutualism" as its source, when several interaction configurations and cascade paths can produce the same score, is the metamerism error. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The ground anchor is the other half. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A recovery-rate measurement or a distance-to-tipping estimate is a candidate anchor; a consensus of ecologists is not.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does this question have?** A refused ranking plus a positive procedure. Three figure lines close it: Fig 11 :50 makes the candidates relations rather than kinds, Fig 11 :43-44 prescribes enumeration rather than ranking, and Fig 11 :52 makes cascades part of the object. Fig 9 :54-57 classifies the question as a bundled union requiring a split. The framework returns the shape of any acceptable answer: per-residual, per-slot, cascade-aware, one declared currency, with a refuter written in advance.

**Does the world literature supply the missing name?** No. It supplies the same structural message from independent fields (context dependence, multi-interaction robustness, diversity of types). Agreement between those fields and the figures is corroboration of the mechanism, not a licence to nominate a winner. Two sources that share the same evidence pool count as one witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected ecologists to agree with a ranking is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let an anchor settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not an ecological measurement and cannot settle the question it reports on.
