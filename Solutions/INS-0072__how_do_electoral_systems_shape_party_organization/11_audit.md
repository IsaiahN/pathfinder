## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 12 :5 and :117 (bond and isomer), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot), Fig 7 :35 and :36 (rooms), Fig 10 :47, :24 and :42 (anchor, channel, refusal).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: one rule, four shapes, four anchors, a `gap` for the system and the numbers.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about plurality versus PR, a disproportionality index, a polarization score, and an accountability rating.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim (real systems gain and lose parties, so the rearrangement is sometimes composition plus import);
- the **per-slot indexing** (a national index discards the live slot the shorthand insists on keeping);
- the **non-updating anchor** (an accountability rating built from the institution's own reports breaks the condition);
- the **room independence** requirement (a polarization score computed from one evidence pool is a mirror, not a verdict).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the four outcomes are a union with different anchors, and the rule is a bond over held atoms. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
