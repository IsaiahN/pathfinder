# The Tether answer: how electoral systems shape four outcomes

**Mode: `specified` on the four per-domain shapes, `general` on the union skeleton, `gap` on the identity of a best system or any numerical effect size. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 5), not a ground reading. One loop per domain; verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles one rule with four outcomes. In effect terms: given a change in the ballot to seat rule, which downstream quantity changes, through what contact, and against what anchor that does not move when the institution reports on itself? Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* So the answer must be four verdicts, not one.

## 2. The four shapes, one loop each

### D1, the rule: a bond, not an atom

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* The rule fixes a typed threshold relation between voters, candidates, parties and seats. Two rules over the same atoms are **isomers** (Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*), under the fixed-atom condition kept visible in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.** Refuter: show a reform that adds a genuinely new primitive.

### D2, party organization: method versus recording

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* The rule changes the unit of competition and the cost of coordination. What persists is a re-applicable method or a head start, never the recording of one win. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.** The particular organization that forms in a given country is a `gap`, unreached rather than silence (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*). Refuter: show a recording generating a new outcome without a method.

### D3, representation: per-slot comparison, not a score

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Operators :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* Representation is the comparison of vote share to seat share, indexed per party slot. A single national disproportionality number is a filter for the budget, not a witness for a cause (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict"*). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.** No figure returns an optimal proportionality coefficient: `gap`. Refuter: show a single index that preserves the per-slot live signal.

### D4, polarization: room difference selected against ground

Fig 7 :35: *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Polarization carries information only when rooms differ and are selected against a ground that does not update. Otherwise it is a mirror chain. **Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure side** (Fig 7 with Fig 2, distinct objects but related independence theme), with low-overlap world corroboration from quorum sensing and Duverger's usage. No polarization optimum: `gap`. Refuter: show two rooms drawing on one evidence pool correcting each other independently.

### D5, accountability: anchor, channel, refusal

Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Accountability requires a non-updating ground, a channel that stays reachable, and a seat able to refuse. A rule that fixes the outcome has become a verdict and stopped being a neutral convention. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 10 plus Operators plus two low-overlap mathematical witnesses). No accountability score: `gap`. Refuter: show accountability improving while the reference updates on the institution's own reports and no seat can refuse.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any system (FPTP, list PR, mixed member) is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).
- Not that Duverger's law is exceptionless. The field itself calls it a tendency, not an invariant rule.
- Not a proportionality number, polarization index, accountability score, or universal effect size.
- Not a derivation of one party system from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by measurement.
- Not that agreement among experts settles any domain. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator and an independent frame, not more argument. The formula's step 7 asks SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, kept distinct. Concretely:

1. **Per-slot instrument.** Can we measure the vote-to-seat comparison for each party slot without collapsing it into a national index? A source-discriminating instrument, not an index, would name D3.
2. **Anchor test.** Is the reference used for accountability a distribution that does not move when the institution reports on it? A case where it moves breaks D5.
3. **Room test.** Do the competing rooms draw on different evidence, or one pool? Independent low-overlap readings would corroborate D4; shared-pool readings are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
4. **Standing refuters from `01`:** a figure line selecting a system or a number; a new primitive; accountability without anchor and refusal; a cross-field measure that changes form; two shared-pool rooms correcting each other.

Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* The four shapes are stated; the specific system and the numbers are owed and cannot be manufactured by more analysis inside the same evidence pool. The record stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and keeps the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank electoral systems and does not claim a universal effect. It says the electoral system is a typed threshold bond over atoms a polity already has (voters, candidates, parties, seats), so systems over the same atoms are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The four named outcomes are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not four effects of one lever. Party organization turns on whether what crosses a boundary is a re-applicable method or a recording of one win; representation is a threshold comparison indexed per party slot, not a national score; polarization is a real difference between rooms only when the rooms differ and are selected against a ground that does not move; accountability needs that non-updating ground, a reachable channel, and a seat able to refuse. Where the rule itself decides the outcome it has stopped being a neutral convention. The framework returns those shapes, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close them. It does not name a best system. That question stays open until the ground settles it.
