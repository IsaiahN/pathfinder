## What the wider literature does and does not give us

1. **The field already has its own shape-not-a-name warning.** Duverger's law is a tendency, not an invariant, and its effect depends on context, geography and strategy. That is exactly the figure verdict in F8: no rule returns a universal outcome. Cite this first when someone asks for "the answer."
2. **The field also has a per-slot aggregation warning.** The effective number of parties is smaller than the raw count unless all parties are equally strong, and the same formula appears in economics, ecology, physics and information theory. That is the isomer finding (F3) in the field's own notation, and it is a genuinely low-overlap corroborator because it comes from outside the framework.
3. **The barrier is known locally too.** Arrow and Gibbard are impossibility results: no ranked rule satisfies a small set of fair axioms, and no rule is strategy-proof in general. These corroborate the figure claim that no arrangement inside a fixed set adds a new atom (Fig 6 :21, Fig 13 :115) without being derived from the framework.
4. **The anchor question is empirical.** Condorcet's jury theorem flips sign depending on whether voters are more likely right than wrong, and a 2022 result says the theorem fails almost surely under an unbiased prior unless competence is evidenced. That is the "ground decides, not the rule" claim in a different vocabulary.
5. **A threshold rule changes collective state without a new actor.** Quorum sensing is the lowest-overlap witness here: a concentration crosses a threshold and gene expression changes. It corroborates the comparison/threshold reading of representation and polarization (Operators :51, Fig 12 :87) without borrowing any political-science evidence.

**Overlap declaration.** The political-science sources (Duverger's law, Electoral system, Effective number of parties, and the journal venue pages) share one evidence pool and count as one witness. Arrow, Condorcet and quorum sensing are genuinely low overlap. The framework essays, where used in `05_what_shape_means.md`, share the framework author's pool completely and carry no independent verdict weight.
