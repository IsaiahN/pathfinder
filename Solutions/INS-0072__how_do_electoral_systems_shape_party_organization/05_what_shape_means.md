## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel* (mapping carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "One lever with four effects" is not a shape, it is an unexamined binding of the question's grammar.
- "Four overlapping conditions with four anchors, each with a refusal condition" is a shape.
- "First past the post produces two parties" is a name, and the figures do not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (mapping carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to 31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (mapping carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 9 :27 to 28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* This is why the residual in `01` is stated in effect terms before any electoral-system typology is chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means a measurement with a non-updating anchor, not a cross-national correlation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (mapping carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The voter, candidate, party and seat atoms are a visible guess about political compression axes. Naming one electoral system as *the* answer to "how do systems shape outcomes" turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source error, and why it matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (mapping carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a disproportionality index reads the projection (votes versus seats) and cannot say which arrangement produced it. Two different electoral rules can yield the same index. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name for the source. This is the reason F5 treats a national representativeness score as a filter for the budget and not a witness for a cause (Fig 9 :60).

## 5. The honest status of this derivation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened and verified this session, 2026-11-22):

> *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

> *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not ground readings. They cannot settle how electoral systems shape anything. What they can do is state the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and why that governs the outreach

From the same essay (verified this session):

> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious political scientists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let the ground settle it, and keep verdict weight away from reputation. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Seat Map :171: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* The author's interest in the shape surviving is structural and is declared here rather than hidden.

## 7. Correction to my own first-pass reading, kept visible

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name, forbids naming any institution." That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Voters, parties, seats and the comparison bond are already held; naming a binding onto them is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is a verdict stated in `general` mode (Seat Map :281), a system name where the figures return a shape (Fig 9 :32), and an import with no source gate or shadow test (Fig 8 :12 to 13). Both the original overstatement and its narrowing are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The correct output is not a ranking of electoral systems. It is:

- a per-domain shape for each of the four outcomes, with its anchor and its refusal condition;
- a `gap` for the specific system and the numbers, reported as unreached rather than as silence;
- a pre-registered refuter;
- a request that the reader check the shape before reading the theory.

That is what the remaining files do.
