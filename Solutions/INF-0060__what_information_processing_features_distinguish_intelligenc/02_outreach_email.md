# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01` and `08` are stable. The output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about intelligence and pattern recognition, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "what information-processing features distinguish intelligence from sophisticated pattern recognition?" does not have an answer in the frame I am using. The frame replaces it. Its own words: *"What is intelligence? The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* The replacement is that capability is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone, and that an improvement which does not change contact changes nothing.
>
> The narrow internal features that do survive the check are these: the system ranges against something immovable that does not update on the system's own account of itself; it carries a term that can refuse a regularity it holds; it tracks a residual per case rather than folding it into a global accuracy number; it can import from a frame whose closure differs; and it produces methods that cross up rather than replays of a past success. The features that do not survive are accuracy, compression achieved, coverage and prediction score, because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I have a definition of intelligence; that a benchmark score measures capability; that the list above is sufficient. It is a shape with a refuter, not a result. A test harness is a substituted habitat, and a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability.
>
> **What would refute it, written before I sent this:**
> 1. an improvement to the agent in isolation that moves the ground or changes contact;
> 2. a system with no way to refuse any held regularity that settles a novel residual against an immovable ground, with no import;
> 3. a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces, and that score settling a verdict;
> 4. a search inside a fixed closure that adds an atom;
> 5. a benchmark solve that transfers to an unenumerated habitat without new contact.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would like from you:** what would you count as a failure here? Which of the five would you try to fire first? Please answer that before looking at the sources below, so your verdict is recorded first.
>
