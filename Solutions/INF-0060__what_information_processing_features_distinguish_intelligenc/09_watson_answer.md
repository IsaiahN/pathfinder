## VERDICT (from the figures)

The literal question is a `gap` after a genuine census. The replacement shape survives as `specified`, ungrounded and candidate: capability is agent-and-habitat contact, not an agent-only property; the system must range against an immovable ground; it must be able to refuse a held regularity; it must keep the residual per case; it must import from a differing closure and produce methods rather than replays; and a test harness is a substituted habitat, not the ground. Each condition is necessary and none is sufficient, and nothing here is averaged into a single score. Accuracy, compression, coverage and prediction score are refused as discriminators because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. The strongest single point is canonical and low-overlap: Turing in 1950 and the SEP in 2026 both replace an internal-substrate question with a contact question, independently of the framework.

- **cost if wrong:** we would steer evaluation toward grounds, refusal and transfer when the real discriminator is an internal architectural quantity. The cost is bounded because every claim is a shape with a refuter attached, so an error is locatable. The larger avoidable cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending the architecture before they have described a residual. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive was minted, no term deleted, no import committed. The literal question stays `gap` rather than being forced, and the shape can be narrowed without withdrawing the rest of the package. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An agent-only improvement that moves the ground; a no-negation system that settles a novel residual; a self-scored frame that settles a verdict; a synthetic solve that transfers to an unenumerated habitat; or a search that adds an atom inside a fixed closure. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments, and a source-discriminating instrument would be required to move anything to `grounded`.
