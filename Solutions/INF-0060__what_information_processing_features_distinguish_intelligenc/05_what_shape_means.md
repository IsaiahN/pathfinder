## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The README adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question: *"a capable system ranges against an immovable ground and can refuse a held regularity"* is a shape. *"Intelligence is feature X"* is a name. The answer this solution returns is the shape and the refuters, not a definition.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for recurring new terms that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms."* With each new term, *"you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* This is the method used in `01` §1: start from the residual in effect terms, then let the figures narrow the candidate features.
- *Library of Babel* on the searcher's ignorance: *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed."* For INF-0060 the second possibility was taken seriously: Fig 11 :35 calls the question *"the wrong question."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the rule behind `02_outreach_email.md`: send the effect and the refuter, not the architecture.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Applied here: the shape is composed by enumerating the candidate features and discarding the frame-internal ones, not by consulting a definition of intelligence.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens the first: *"Unless you have an unmovable ground outside yourself and the replicator that can validate the outputs (RLVR)"*, the outputs and the steering are unverifiable by you. And: *"The replicator needs to know what a dish is and what comprises it in order to craft it in higher fidelity."* A shape without a ground is a replicator with no recipe.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discriminator test: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* For INF-0060 this is why a benchmark reading cannot license the name "intelligent": the reading does not determine the source.

## 4. Why "intelligence" is the wrong bound term here

*How Intelligence Actually Works* opens: *"The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments."* And: *"The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence."* This is the same move as Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes the operational consequence explicit: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."* That settles the apparent conflict with Turing's 1950 imitation game noted in `01` §7: fluency under interrogation and accuracy against a ground are different readings, and only the latter discriminates capability.

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* lists the claim *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* under *"A note on what is not cited"*, with the comment: *"Absence of a citation is not a claim of originality. It means a search was made and returned nothing."* This is an honest limit of the central verdict in this solution. It is a framework claim, and outside corroboration is weak. The same essay notes a near neighbour, Chollet's *On the measure of intelligence* (2019), as *"a parallel"*: Chollet measures skill-acquisition efficiency against stated priors, while Fig 11 locates capability in agent and habitat. Those are *"a different quantity in the same neighbourhood"*, not the same result.

## 5. Q - What licenses the list of internal features in `01` F7?

**Answer:** only as a necessary shape, never as a sufficient definition. The essays return a shape. Nothing in them names an internal feature that is sufficient for intelligence. The features that survive in `01` F7 are constrained by figure lines and by the ground, and they remain candidates. A particular feature earns the name "the discriminator" only if the ground settles it (condition 1 above) or a provenanced import from a differing closure supplies it (condition 2). Neither has happened.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** In one place it comes close. `01` F7 names refusal, per-slot residual, import and generation as features, and the literal question asks for "features." The canonical check narrows this: those names are figure-grounded constraints on the shape, not a definition of intelligence, and the literal question is recorded as `gap`. Correction recorded in `01` §3b. Both readings are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous AI researchers selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuters.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay adds that LLM outputs under this demo *"must be seen as simulacra"* because the training library is a black box. So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
