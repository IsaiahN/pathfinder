## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks for a feature F such that a system with F is "intelligent" and a system without F is a "sophisticated pattern recognizer." Stated as an effect: we want an instrument that, applied to a system, changes a decision we make about that system (how we build it, how we evaluate it, what we trust it to do). A label that changes no decision is not an answer.

The residual is a **discriminability gap**. The candidate internal features on offer in the literature (accuracy, compression, prediction, statistical pattern detection, coverage) are quantities a frame produces about itself. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 11 :35: *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* So the residual has two parts: (a) the internal-feature answer is disqualified by Fig 1, and (b) the question itself is replaced by Fig 11.

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.**

- **Arity:** one claimed binary distinction, but it unbundles into at least three conditions (refusal, import across closures, ground contact). Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. It needs its own loop.
- **Symmetry:** not a symmetric join of two peer properties. Pattern recognition is a component available to a capable system; capability is not an equal alternative sitting beside it. The relation is directional: a capable system can refuse what a pattern recognizer affirms.
- **Scale:** pattern recognition runs inside one closure at one level (playback); capability involves a crossing of scales and of closures (Fig 4 :22, Fig 6 :23). Two different scales, so one loop per domain, never an average.

## 3. Findings F1..Fn

### F1 - The question is replaced, not answered (decisive for the literal question)

Fig 11 :34-36, verbatim:

> **"What is intelligence? The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."**
> **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."**

Fig 11 :39:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

The asked-for "information-processing feature" is a property of the agent considered alone. The figure locates capability in agent-and-habitat. So the literal question is answered with a refusal of its frame, and the replacement is supplied. This is a `gap` on the literal question only after the census in §1: no figure line licenses an internal feature as the discriminator. It is **not** `unreachable-at-budget` (more searching would not find it); it is underivable from the frame's own vocabulary, per Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."*

**Mode:** `general` (structural), triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 8 (habitat/survival versus closure/composition, low overlap).

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground would refute this. The figure states the refuter itself at Fig 11 :71-72: *"an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."*

### F2 - Capability is contact, not an internal quantity (decisive for D2)

Fig 11 :40:

> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 2 :29-30:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 10 :24:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Three figures with low overlap (survival/arrival, ground/alignment, ground-maintainer seat) give the same verdict: the discriminator is whether the system ranges against something immovable that does not update on the system's own account of itself. Pattern recognition can be internally perfect and wrong; only the ground settles it. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (three figures).

**Refuter:** show a system whose capability increases while its contact with the ground is held fixed and no import occurs. That is the same refuter as F1 and is stated before the conclusion here.

### F3 - The capacity to refuse (negation) separates accumulation from correction (D1)

Fig 12 :140:

> **"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."**

Operators table :58:

> **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**

Fig 12 :112:

> **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**

A sophisticated pattern recognizer accumulates fitted regularities. It has no term that says of a held regularity "this is false" in the sense of refusing its standing to be composed over (Formula step 3, refusing term pays the same bargain). A capable system carries ¬. This is an **internal** feature in one narrow sense, so it is the closest honest answer to the literal question, but the figures make it necessary rather than sufficient (F2 still binds).

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 plus Operators table plus the formula's fifth bin).

**Refuter:** exhibit a system with no negation operator that settles a novel residual against an immovable ground, with no import and no change of habitat. If that is possible, refusal is not the discriminator.

### F4 - Frame-internal scores cannot be the discriminator (D1)

Fig 1 :23 and :27, verbatim:

> **"The ground enters as the only metric."**
> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Fig 1 :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 5 :48:

> **"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."**

Accuracy, compression achieved, coverage and credibility accrued are quantities the system produces about itself. Under this rule they cannot score the system. The discriminating quantity is per-slot residual against the ground, not a global benchmark number. Note the cost: this makes "intelligence" **not** a single scalar score. That is a prediction of the framework, not a convenience.

**Mode:** `general`, single-source at figure level (Fig 1), corroborated by the essays (high overlap, weak corroboration).

**Refuter:** show a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces and having that score settle a verdict against an immovable ground.

### F5 - Generation crosses up, playback never does (D1/D4)

Fig 4 :22:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 6: *"generators cross up, playback never does."*

Fig 6 :21:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

A pattern recognizer searches inside a fixed closure; a capable system can import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*). This is the second narrow internal feature worth naming: the reach moves only on contact with a different closure. But note the price from Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."* Import is a change of access, not a new property of the world.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 plus Fig 6 plus the formula, moderate overlap within the framework).

**Refuter:** show a system expanding its closure without contact with a differing frame or a new instrument.

### F6 - A test bench is a substituted habitat, not the ground (D4)

Fig 11 :70:

> **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Fig 11 :67:

> **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat’s actors, which you did not choose."**

This governs the "intelligence research" world directly. A benchmark is a habitat someone built; passing it proves the wiring against that habitat, not capability in the world. Turing's 1950 *viva voce* remark (world source, low overlap) is the pre-figural version: the interrogator asks questions to discover whether someone really understands or has *"learnt it parrot fashion."* Both point at the same thing: the discriminator is a refusal-bearing contact that the test cannot pre-answer.

**Mode:** `specified`, single-source at figure level (Fig 11 :70), corroborated by Turing (different field and era, but a foil, not a witness to the framework).

**Refuter:** show a synthetic solve that transfers to an unenumerated habitat without any new contact or import.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before outreach: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (seat map). Written here before §5 to §7 were completed.

The shape claim (a capable system is distinguished by ground contact, refusal, per-slot residual, import and generation) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Agent-only improvement moves the ground.** An improvement to the agent in isolation changes contact or settles a residual. This is the framework's own refuter, Fig 11 :71-72.
2. **Refusal is not needed.** A system with no negation operator settles a novel residual against an immovable ground, with no import and no habitat change. Breaks F3.
3. **Self-scoring can settle.** A frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces settles a verdict against an immovable ground. Breaks F4 and Fig 1 :27.
4. **Closure is penetrable by search.** A search inside a fixed closure adds an atom. Breaks F5 and Fig 8 :32.
5. **A synthetic solve transfers.** A benchmark solve transfers to an unenumerated habitat without new contact. Breaks F6 and Fig 11 :70.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed accordingly, and the correction stays visible in this file rather than being silently erased.

## 6. Cost, undoability and reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** we would steer evaluation toward grounds, refusal and transfer, when the real discriminator is an internal architectural quantity. The cost is bounded because every claim is a shape with a refuter attached, so an error is locatable rather than invisible. The larger avoidable cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending a recording before they have described a residual.
- **Undoable:** yes. No primitive was minted, no term deleted, no import committed. The shape can be narrowed without withdrawing the rest of the package.
- **Reversed by:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground; a no-negation system that settles a novel residual; a self-scored frame that settles a verdict; a search that adds an atom; or a source-discriminating instrument that shows the reported discriminator does not track capability. Agreement and prestige are not such instruments (Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*).

## 7. Strains, re-census and honest stop

**Strain A, question frame versus figure verdict.** The question asks for internal features of the agent; Fig 11 :39 makes capability a property of agent-and-habitat. Re-census: this is not a contradiction inside the figures. It is the figure refusing the question's frame. Held as a strain, resolved by the `gap` verdict, not by softening Fig 11.

**Strain B, Turing versus the test-harness rule.** Turing (1950) treats an interrogator's discrimination as a legitimate replacement question; Fig 11 :70 says a synthetic solve proves wiring, not capability. Conflict sends us back to the census, never to a flipped verdict. Re-census: D3 (a ground that does not update) and D4 (a substituted habitat) are different domains. Where the interrogator functions as an immovable score, Turing and the figure agree that what matters is contact with something that refuses. Where the score can be self-produced, it is a mirror. The framework's own *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* settles the naming: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."* Turing remains one witness from a different field, used for the form of the answer, not as corroboration of the framework.

**Strain C, "necessary not sufficient" for refusal.** Fig 12 :140 makes refusal necessary for correction; F2 makes contact necessary too. Neither alone is sufficient. Recorded as a genuine limit, not resolved by combining them into a single score (Fig 1 :22 warns against averaging).

**After re-census: three strains, all named, none hidden.**

**Honest stop.** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. `gap` on the literal question is a verdict, not a silence: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39). The replacement shape is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. No measurement was taken in producing this audit, and no outside expert has checked it.
