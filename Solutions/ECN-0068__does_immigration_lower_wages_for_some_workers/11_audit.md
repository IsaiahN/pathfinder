## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a one-sign headline and the measured outcomes on individual cells. Arity: four or more, skill cell by region by wage percentile by horizon, so a compound. Symmetry: asymmetric, because a supply shock lowers the wage of competing workers and can raise the wage of complementary workers; the two cells cannot be swapped without changing the claim. Scale: local versus national, and short run versus long run. This is a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), not a list of separate questions.
- **Domain 1, labour market as habitat.** Actors: workers by cell, firms, capital, institutions. Currency: wages, employment, hours, output. Anchor: measured data. Question type: union compound.
- **Domain 2, research programme.** Actors: teams, designs, instruments, meta-analysts. Currency: estimates. Anchor: reproducible data and identification. Question type: union of estimands; a relative effect and a total effect are not one quantity (this point came to me from the estimand taxonomy, not from my reading of the primary).
- **Domain 3, verification and distribution.** Actors: readers and referees. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground of domain 1, never agreement. Question type: partition. In this simulated run its independence is zero because it shares the primary's pool.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle estimand and anchor before reading a sign (Fig 3); read slots before the aggregate (Fig 1); measure the round trip gap (Fig 4); choose the frame last (Fig 8 :53).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. I arrived from Fig 9's union/partition split and the estimand taxonomy, whereas the primary arrived from Fig 1's slot indexing and Fig 3's dependency chain. Different starting points, same boundaries: low overlap between routes within one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak but not circular in the narrow sense. It is still one pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 (slot-indexed residual), Fig 9 :55 (union compound), Fig 4 :30-32 (lossy scale change), Fig 13 :134 (unreachability as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, "read R per slot, unbundle the union, keep estimands apart, let the ground decide the signs".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claim, "low-skill workers lose, overall productivity rises, effects change after a decade".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the slot**: "low-skill" in Borjas (2003) is an education-experience cell, while "low-skill" in Dustmann, Frattini and Preston (2013) is a position below the 20th percentile of the wage distribution. The same phrase names different objects;
- the **estimand**: the positive productivity result of Peri (2009) is a total-factor-productivity reading, not a wage reading, and it cannot be brought down as if it were the same quantity;
- the **period and skill mix**: Borjas (2003) and Caiumi and Peri (2024) differ in sign partly because the immigrant skill composition changed, which the shorthand does not carry;
- the **investment cost**: the shorthand is cheap to state, while the instruments that would settle each cell (border distance, pre-1960 communities, natural experiments) are expensive. Fig 12 :125 keeps these two currencies apart.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a distributional pattern exists and no single aggregate sign can represent it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading of a cell-level dataset was taken here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
