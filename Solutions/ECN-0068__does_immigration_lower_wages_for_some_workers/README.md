# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** Does immigration lower wages for some workers, raise overall productivity, or produce different effects across skill groups and time horizons?

**Source:** economics questions.txt line 90

**Section:** 7. Labor markets and employment

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the checkable pattern reported by the literature. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement was taken in this derivation, and the whole package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## The question restated as an effect

One lever, an immigration inflow, is said to produce three candidate reads: a loss on some workers, a gain in an aggregate, and a pattern that changes with skill group and horizon. The effect to decide is whether a single sign governs all slots, or whether the residual lives in the differences between slots and scales. What an answer would look like is a table of signs indexed by skill cell, region, wage percentile and horizon, with a stated separation between relative and total wage effects, and an anchor in measured data. That description is bound here as an ordinary term, `slot-indexed effect`, so it can accrue a residual and be refused rather than remaining an invisible preference (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a conclusion about immigration.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". A list of agreeable recipients is a larger room, not another anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

## Honest status of this package

This is a simulation, not a ground reading. The framework says LLM output is simulacra and that the truly testable output has to be derived from a real architecture or by working the formula yourself (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6). The figures decide the *shape* of an answer here: per-slot residual, union compound, ground-settled signs. They do not decide the empirical sign of any wage effect. That part is recorded as a `gap` from the figures and a candidate from the world literature, not as a result. The package cannot settle the question it reports on.
