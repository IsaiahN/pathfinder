## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a number

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0068 the shape is not "wages fall by 1 percent" or "wages rise". It is the constraint that any admissible answer must be **slot-indexed, union-compounded and ground-settled**. A claim that assigns one sign to all workers at all horizons fails the shape before any data is consulted. A claim that lists signs by cell but never names the anchor fails it too.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the closest vocabulary is "per-slot residual", "estimand", "horizon".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the claim sent outward is "here is the pattern you would see in the data", not "here is our theory of labour markets".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly the stage-1 offer in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." That is not a claim that the empirical question is answered, only that the shape has stopped moving.

## 3. What licenses a name, or a sign

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question the ground is measured wages, employment and output with a defensible identification strategy, not agreement among researchers.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The estimand distinction from JEP 2016 is such an import: it did not come from the six-term set, and its provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Applied here: a single average wage effect is a reading that cannot comment on the source of the loss, which cell produced it, or whether capital had adjusted. What is owed is a finer instrument, meaning a cell-level estimand and an explicit horizon, not a new name for the average.

## 4. Why "some workers" is not a name for a group

Fig 11 :49-50: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another. *The Library of Babel* makes the parallel point: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not." Which workers count as "some" is decided by the residual being measured, not by a standing list. A low-skill native in a high-immigration city and a low-skill native in a low-immigration city are different slots for this question even if they have the same education code.

## 5. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the immigration claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

The relevant failure here is citing a famous economist as if the name settled the sign. Borjas (2003) and Ottaviano and Peri (2006/2012) are both checkable records, not authorities. A campaign that gathers prestigious names to endorse one sign is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried: hand a reader the slot-indexed shape and its refuter, and let the data decide.

## 7. The shape for ECN-0068, stated once

> Any admissible answer to the immigration-wage question must report signs by slot (skill cell, region, wage percentile, horizon), must keep relative and total wage effects as different estimands rather than averaging them, must treat the aggregate as a lossy description with a measured round trip gap, and must name the measured anchor that would settle each slot. The figures do not supply the signs. They supply the form the signs must take.
