# Outreach draft - check the output first, then decide about the frame

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter have survived your own check. If the stage-1 output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One check on an immigration wage claim, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a small claim about the shape of the immigration-wage question, not on the sign of any estimate. I am not asking you to agree, and I am not asking you to read the framework first. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the pattern in the literature):** the question as usually asked bundles several conditions into one headline. Measured effects live in slots, education-experience cells, local labour markets, wage percentiles, and short versus long horizons. The figures' rule is that the residual is indexed by slot, not aggregated. A near-zero overall reading with one live slot is a legal state. So "overall productivity rose" and "this group of workers lost" are not contradictory readings, and a single sign should not be expected to cover all cells. The framework does not tell us which sign any cell has. That part is owed to data.
>
> **What would refute this, written before I sent it:**
> 1. one aggregate statistic fully predicts every slot's wage trajectory, with no live slot residual;
> 2. all credible identification strategies, on disjoint data with different instruments, converge on one sign and magnitude for every skill group, region, percentile and horizon;
> 3. the estimates are shown to track the researcher's preferred model rather than the measured data;
> 4. the figures are shown to license a determinate wage sign from the six-term set alone.
>
> If any of those holds I will withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the public sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the Project Pathfinder workspace (local file; share the file, not a claim about it).
> - Dustmann, Schonberg and Stuhler, "The Impact of Immigration: Why Do Studies Reach Such Different Results?", Journal of Economic Perspectives 30(4), 2016: https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.30.4.31
> - Foged, Hasager and Yasenov, "The role of labor market institutions in the impact of immigration on wages and employment", Scandinavian Journal of Economics 124(1), 2022: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/scandj/v124y2022i1p164-213.html
> - Edo, "The Impact Of Immigration On The Labor Market", Journal of Economic Surveys 33(3), 2019: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jecsur/v33y2019i3p922-948.html
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that immigration lowers wages in general, that it always raises productivity, or that the framework settles either. I am not claiming a new estimate, and I am not asking you to trust the source. The literature above disagrees with itself in ways that matter, and I want to know whether the disagreement is a union of different slots and estimands rather than one hard question.
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the eight-step formula is in the figures folder. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether your output is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Shape claim and refuter before any source | Fig 10 :52 - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | Formula step 2 and the standing rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No verdict on the sign | After re-census, no figure line governs an empirical wage sign. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." |
| Sources only after a stage-1 verdict | Fig 2 :45 - "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). |
| No request for trust or prestige | Fig 2 :42 - "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Asking for trust buys the wrong thing. |
| Offer the formula as a reusable method | Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |
| One-to-one, public channels only | Fig 7 :12-13 - "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" - and Fig 2 :31 on independence. |
