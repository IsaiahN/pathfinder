# Outreach draft - check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one only, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have decided what you would count as a failure of the shape claim. If you cannot state a failure, there is nothing to check.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about small reserves in altered landscapes

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a small protected area cannot be the unit of viability. A viable population belongs to a population-and-habitat system, not to the hectares inside a boundary. When the surrounding landscape is heavily altered, the small reserve is a substituted habitat: it has lost imports of individuals, mutualists and resources it did not choose to lose, and it has inherited matrix actors it did not choose at all. The shape of a surviving answer is therefore a **connected habitat network**, large enough to reproduce the actors, conditions and relations the focal population requires, with the boundary still admitting the crossings that system needs. The framework returns this shape. It does not return a hectare number, and I am not claiming one.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that every small reserve fails; that several small reserves always beat one large one; that corridors alone fix anything; that a universal minimum area exists; or that a model of viability is the same thing as a count of animals. Habitat here is a technical term for the field of contact with a specific residual, not the ecological niche. The ecological mapping is a structural analogy, and you are entitled to reject it.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. A small reserve in a non-permeable, heavily altered matrix maintains a viable focal population over a stated period with zero imports and no managed reproduction of missing actors.
> 2. An area-only lever restores viability while every matrix condition and external contact stays unchanged.
> 3. One reserve-wide aggregate metric preserves every per-species local-extinction signal it averages.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the ecological claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The eight-step formula and mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - The habitat figure, Figure 11, in the public article: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and the architecture article warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Two-stage rule for the reply

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and the four refuters, and ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Never pool their later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

A Track B reply, if one ever comes, is a conversation and never a witness. Its verdict weight is zero by definition.
