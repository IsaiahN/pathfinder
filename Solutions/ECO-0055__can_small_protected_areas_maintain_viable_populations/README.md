## What this solution is

It is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading of any reserve. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap says LLM output can simulate an approximate answer but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* and calls such output *"simulacra"*. This package therefore cannot settle the question it reports on. It states the shape an answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

**Verdict in one line:** the figures do not permit an area-only answer. Viability is a property of a population and its habitat (Fig 11 :39), and when the surrounding landscape is heavily altered the small reserve is a **substituted habitat** (Fig 11 :66) whose inside lacks the imports that cross a membrane (Fig 4 :21, Fig 6 :23). The answer is a **connected network large enough to carry the population's required actors, conditions and relations**, returned as a shape, not a hectare number. This is `specified`, triangulated, ungrounded. The universal area threshold is a `gap` after re-census, not a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
3. **A checker who holds the interior cannot check.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the theory.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
