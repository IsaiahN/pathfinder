## VERDICT (from the figures)

The area-only question is refused; the contact-and-habitat shape survives. A population is viable in a population-and-habitat system, not in hectares. A heavily altered matrix is a substituted habitat with unchosen actors, so the field of contact must be enumerated outward before viability is assessed. What moves a closed reserve toward persistence is import through a membrane, measured per focal population, not an increase in interior quality that leaves all contact unchanged. Connectivity parses as space bonded to network, a cross-kind structure; a viable network is a cycle in which local extinction is answered by recolonisation; one large reserve and several connected small ones are isomers over a shared atom set. The framework returns this shape and a set of refuters, not a universal area threshold. That slot is `gap`. The ecological instantiation is a declared structural binding, refusable under R4, and nothing in this answer is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its four refuters, its mapping caveat and its provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the full framework before they have recorded a verdict on the shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The ecological binding can be withdrawn without touching the figure derivation if R4 fires; the threshold `gap` can be filled by a measured import without changing the shape; a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. No primitive has to be abandoned. Introductions are available to a manager, but exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61), so a wrongly introduced actor cannot simply be deleted from the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Boundary-level mark-recapture or genetic assignment showing persistence with zero imports (R1), an area-only lever that restores persistence with all contacts fixed (R2), a single aggregate metric that preserves every per-species extinction signal (R3), a conservation biologist showing the Fig 11 mapping changes the figure's definition of habitat (R4), a recolonisation record showing a chain rather than a cycle (F6a), identical outcomes under every different arrangement (F6b), or a measured universal threshold with provenance and a shadow test. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
