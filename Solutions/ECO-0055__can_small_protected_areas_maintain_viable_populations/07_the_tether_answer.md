# The Tether's answer on small protected areas in an altered landscape

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A small reserve is predicted to hold a viable population; the ground returns persistence or local extinction. The gap is measured **per focal population and its habitat**, not as a reserve-wide average (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question bundles two conditions, a small area and a heavily altered matrix, which form a union compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55-59). A lever that clears one condition leaves the intersection standing.

## 2. The shape

1. **Viability is not a property of the area.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The unit is a population and its habitat. "Is this reserve big enough" is the wrong question in the same way "is this seed capable" is the wrong question.
2. **The altered matrix is a substituted habitat, not a backdrop.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* The reserve owes a re-enumeration of actors, conditions and relations, and inherits matrix actors it did not choose (Fig 11 :67-69). Contact cascades beyond the fence (Fig 11 :52-53).
3. **Contact decides persistence.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Inside a closure, the only operator that moves the wall is import (Fig 6 :14, :23). Two differing closures together reach what neither reaches alone (Fig 8 :45).
4. **Connectivity is a structure, not an amount.** Space (a state) bonded to network (a structure), a legal cross-kind composition (Fig 13 :74-75, :23-24). It is not a hectare count.
5. **A viable network is a cycle.** Local extinction must re-enter as recolonisation; a chain cannot close by construction (Fig 12 :121). One large reserve and several connected small ones are isomers over a shared atom set (Fig 12 :117), not different kinds of object.
6. **The framework returns the shape, not the threshold.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* There is no universal minimum area or MVP number to be had from these figures. That slot is `gap`.

The ecological instantiation in steps 2 to 5 is a **binding** from conservation biology onto the figure's structure, not a derivation. The canonical article explicitly declines the habitat-as-niche mapping; the mapping is declared, checkable and refusable (R4 in `01` §3).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that every small reserve fails. Whether one persists is a ground question for a named species over a stated period.
- Not that several small always beat one large (SLOSS). The structural claim is that they are isomers; which member persists depends on movement, spreading of risk and heterogeneity, and the world literature is context-dependent.
- Not that corridors alone fix anything. A corridor is an import path; whether it carries the required actor is measured, not assumed.
- Not that a habitat-suitability model is an anchor. Models are frames; a count is the anchor (Fig 2 :29).
- Not that "habitat" in the Tether means the ecological niche. It means the field of contact with a residual, enumerated.
- Not `grounded`. No census, mark-recapture study, genetic assignment or field experiment was performed for this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. The corresponding questions here:

- **R1, contact:** does a focal population persist with zero imports and no managed reproduction of missing actors in a non-permeable altered matrix? A boundary-level mark-recapture or genetic assignment test fires this refuter.
- **R2, compound:** does an area-only lever restore persistence while all matrix conditions stay fixed? A controlled before-after comparison fires it.
- **R3, slot:** does a single reserve-wide metric preserve every per-species local-extinction signal? A per-species time series fires it.
- **R4, mapping:** can a conservation biologist instantiate Fig 11's structure without changing the figure's definition of habitat? If not, the ecological binding is withdrawn.
- **F6a and F6b** in `06_isomers_and_order.md`: no recolonisation of emptied patches (the network is a chain, not a cycle), or identical outcomes under different arrangements (the isomer claim fails).

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A species with a high cost of wrong verdict deserves an independent count and an independent checker, not a second reading of the same survey.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (They're Scanning Books, 2026), and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The Tether habitat is not the ecological niche. The framework returns a shape, and the number that would complete it must be imported from a frame whose closure differs, with source provenance and a shadow test. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (How Intelligence Actually Works, 2026). A search that finds no universal threshold is reported as `gap`, not hidden (Fig 8 :39).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer the area-only question, because a population is not viable in hectares; it is viable in a population-and-habitat system. When the surrounding landscape is heavily altered, the small reserve is a substituted habitat: it has lost the imports of individuals, mutualists and resources it needed, and inherited matrix actors it did not choose. What survives is a shape, not a number: a connected habitat network whose boundary still admits the crossings the focal population requires, with every actor, condition and relation enumerated outward through the matrix. Connectivity is a structure (space bonded to network), not an amount of area; a viable network is a cycle in which local extinction is answered by recolonisation, and one large reserve and several connected small ones are isomers over the same habitat atoms. The framework does not supply a universal minimum area and explicitly declines to map its technical "habitat" onto the ecological niche. What it offers is the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the discriminating instruments that would close it. The reserve stays open until the ground settles it.
