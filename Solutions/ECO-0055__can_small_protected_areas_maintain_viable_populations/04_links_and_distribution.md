## What the wider literature contributes

These are **secondary corroboration only**. They cannot overrule a figure line; a conflict sends the derivation back to the domain census.

1. **Fahrig et al. 2022** gives the world-facing version of the union finding: no context-free answer to SLOSS, and a specific set of three conditions under which a single large patch wins. It corroborates F5, and it refuses the area-only verdict that Option A would deliver.
2. **Haddad et al. 2015** supplies the anchor-style result: effects are greatest in the smallest and most isolated fragments and grow with time. It corroborates F2 and F4, but also supplies a *conflict*: a matrix may not be unsuitable for some species. The conflict was resolved by re-census (Fig 11 :49, valence is relative to the residual), not by choosing one side.
3. **Smith et al. 2025** sharpened the meaning of "contact" to include the immediate matrix, which changed the reading of Fig 11 :40 without changing the verdict. This is recorded as strain 2 in `01` §2b.
4. **Wilson and Primack 2019** state the mechanism a reserve checker would actually look for: persistence in small reserves depends on dispersal through the surrounding habitat, isolation creates sinks, and connectivity is an important strategy for small reserves. This corroborates F4 at maximum independence from the framework author.
5. **Protected Planet 2024** supplies the policy-scale anchor: connectivity is measured, and almost all land that is protected is not connected. It prevents the shape claim from being dismissed as merely theoretical.

Overlap: sources 1 to 5 are all conservation biology and share a substantial evidence pool (several cite Haddad and Fahrig). Their agreement is **one witness**, not five. The essays all share one author with the figures, so their agreement is internal consistency, not independent corroboration. This is declared in the source ledger.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer links after a verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163:** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (context: the second collapse). Agreement from people who all read the same summary is the pool talking.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3, opt-in:** use the formula recursively on another problem, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method; the Formula's "Direction" section says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a conservation biologist returns R1, R2, R3 or R4 from `01` §3, the ecological claim is withdrawn or narrowed before any theory is shown.
