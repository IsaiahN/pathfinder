## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its missing part. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is not "reform X is good." It is the two-part condition: contact with an external ground, plus an unauthored and low-overlap participant frame, with R read per slot and bonds supplied. That shape can be checked against any mechanism before anyone claims which mechanism satisfies it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on nearby new terms. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names the structural move that governs this question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :54-57 in narrative form, and it is why the two conditions are never averaged.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the conditions are stated as effects: does the vote move when pushed, does the pool select itself, does the aggregate hide a slot - not as labels like "populism" or "elite capture."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the rule that sets the outreach order in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README adds that the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the institutional vocabulary this solution uses (votes, polls, assemblies, lotteries) is a visible human choice, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming sortition as an instance of an unauthored pool is a binding from the political-science frame, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not a derivation from the figures.

## 4. The room is not the ground (the decisive essay reading for this question)

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the failure mode of any deliberative or participatory room: *"if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on)."* And, explicitly: *"The problem is that the room session only produces an outcome that you agree on, having come from diverse frames with different vantage points of the problem. It does not mean that the output was objective truth."*

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* carries the same rule into a group: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* and *"The group’s agreement is never the verdict."* On filling the room: *"A carefully chosen room is still a chosen room."* On thresholds: *"Deadlines are filters, never verdicts."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the drift mechanism: *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* The remedy is *"a ground that both are corrected by, separately"* and *"Separate experience"*, which are Fig 2 :45-46 and Fig 10 :80 in narrative form.

This is why the solution refuses to treat participation volume or consensus as evidence of policy improvement. Agreement inside a room is the product of the room, not a verdict about the world.

## 5. Source discrimination: why the aggregate is not enough

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism behind Fig 1 :22: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* The resolution in that essay was *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a vote total is a reading of an aggregate. It cannot say which slot produced it. Several distinct slot-level arrangements (one organized majority plus a harmed minority, or an even distribution) can produce the same total. What is owed is a better instrument, per-slot outcome measurement, not a rhetorical argument about which group really owns the result.

## 6. Q: What is the shape for this question?

**Answered.** The shape has four constraints and one refusal:

1. **Contact constraint:** the mechanism must change contact with an external ground, not merely improve the participant in isolation (Fig 11 :40).
2. **Pool constraint:** the participant set must be unauthored (lottery, everyone who qualifies, a pre-existing set, or enumerated habitat), and the frames must remain low-overlap (Fig 10 :76-78, Fig 2 :40).
3. **Measurement constraint:** R is reported per slot, and the live slot is the object of the next loop, never averaged into a single number (Fig 1 :19-28).
4. **Grammar constraint:** the mechanism must supply sequence, comparison and progress, because a list of options can name a state and cannot name a route (Fig 12 :111-115).
5. **Refusal:** the shape does not name which concrete reform wins. It refuses to say "sortition always works" or "referenda always fail." A specific mechanism is licensed only by a ground reading in a particular polity, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test.

This is a shape because any candidate answer that violates one of the four constraints is excluded before the answer is known, and a candidate that satisfies all four is still not settled until the ground pays.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

In §3 I first wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming a mechanism such as sortition or deliberative polling. That was too strong. The canonical README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Sortition, random sampling and per-slot measurement are already held terms in the public institutional vocabulary. Naming them is a **binding** from an outside frame, with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong if a solution names one of them as *the* answer is different: it would turn a visible choice into an invisible one and skip the ground. *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Both versions are recorded so the correction is not silently erased.

## 8. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against outcomes.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture article adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the institutional question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle what they report on.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the general limit plainly: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* Agreement among readers of this folder would be one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
