## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: four constraints and one refusal, with two condition sets read per domain.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional recommendation, for example replacing an open hearing with a sortition panel and publishing per-group outcome series.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which ground is legitimate** for a given policy (Fig 2 :36-38 requires the anchor to be constitutively about the question). The shorthand says "external ground" and does not say which one.
- **Whether frames remain low-overlap over time.** Fig 10 :80-81 says independence is renewed by separate experience and otherwise can only be rotated. A panel that deliberates for six months may become one frame wearing many names.
- **Whether the habitat permits introduction.** Fig 11 :61 says exclusion is unavailable, so adding a panel does not remove the interests already present.
- **The two currencies stay separate in implementation.** A reform that is cheaper to access may still leave the description cost unpaid; Fig 12 :130 forbids summing them.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a mutable vote cannot be the anchor for policy quality, and an aggregate cannot show which slot is live. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. `06` section Q6 records the same gap from the supporting derivation.
