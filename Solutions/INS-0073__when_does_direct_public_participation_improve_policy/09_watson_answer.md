## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, which conditions make a direct-participation mechanism reduce the policy residual and which make it reduce that residual for an organized slot while leaving or increasing it for others. Option A: participation is an improvement mechanism and capture is a malfunction to be designed out. Option B: the question is a union compound with two condition sets, read per domain and never averaged. What each leads to: A searches for one best reform and treats a higher score as evidence of success, which spends the independence of the people checking it (Fig 2 :40). B delivers a shape with four constraints and one refusal, written refuters, and the instruments that would close it. The verdict recorded here is B in `specified` mode.

- **Domain 1, decision mechanism.** Actors: voters, ballots, agenda setters, quorum rules. Currency: preference aggregation and coordination. Anchor: outcomes external to the vote, because *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor"* (Fig 2 :30). Partition: convention or verdict.
- **Domain 2, pool and frames.** Actors: participants, campaigns, media, expert frames, affected groups. Currency: evidence overlap. Anchor: outcomes, read separately by each frame. Union: a surplus exists only where closures differ.
- **Domain 3, measurement.** Actors: citizen groups, policy domains, minority slots. Currency: live residual per slot. Anchor: measured outcomes per slot. Partition: per slot, never aggregated.
- **Domain 4, habitat and contact.** Actors: interest groups, institutions, rules, money, the reformer. Currency: contact with the ground, introduction versus exclusion. Anchor: outcomes, which do not decay while the channel does. Union with cascades.
- **Domain 5, vocabulary.** Actors: voters' affordance terms and objective terms. Currency: bonds versus bare atoms. Anchor: the middle link of the dependency chain (Fig 3 :24). Dependency order, not importance.

Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20): perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound. Direct participation improves policy when the vote stays a convention under an external ground, the pool is unauthored, frames are low-overlap and range against the ground separately, R is reported per slot, the reform changes contact with the habitat and is maintained, and the mechanism supplies sequence, comparison and progress. It privileges the best-organized groups when the vote is taken as the settlement, the room selects itself, participants share one evidence pool, the aggregate hides the live slot, and the ballot supplies atoms while the organized supply the bonds. The organized group is a relation to a residual, not a kind, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate shape. The strongest single outreach point is canonical: the vote is a convention, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four constraints, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters. A second cost is practical: telling a polity to replace open participation with sortition on the strength of a simulation, before a per-slot measurement, would be an unsourced import.
- **undoable:** yes. The four constraints can be re-tested one at a time without touching the derivation. Each domain finding has its own refuter in `01` section 6. No primitive has to be withdrawn to reverse a domain verdict, and a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. What is not undoable is a reader's independence once they have absorbed the framework before giving a verdict (Fig 10 :42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A push test showing the vote does not move under framing and agenda control would narrow the first finding; a self-selected pool statistically indistinguishable from the affected population with per-group outcomes improving would fire the second refuter; a reform that lowers action and description costs by a common measured factor would fire the third; a case where all per-slot residuals fall with no live slot would fire the fourth; a participation reform that improves outcomes while the habitat is measurably unchanged and needs no maintenance would fire the fifth; an atom-only ballot that works across domains without supplied bonds would fire the sixth. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Agreement among readers of this folder is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40) and cannot reverse or confirm it.
