# The Tether's answer: how to compare a complex new theory with a simpler model that already fits

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the six checks, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A simple model M already fits many observations; a complex theory T is offered. The residual is the per-slot gap between prediction and observation, not an aggregate fit score. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The comparison is a **union compound** of at least description economy, live-slot ground gap and provenance, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape: six ordered steps

1. **Split and describe.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Do not pick a simplicity yardstick after seeing the fit. Keep the three sub-verdicts separate; never average them (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

2. **Route the gap before scoring.** Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. If a held term of M is false, what is owed is a refusal, not a larger explanation. The fifth kind is reached by no arrangement of the other four.

3. **Apply the description bargain under one declared code.** Fig 5 :43-44: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before. A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Admit T only if cost(T) plus left(R,T) is less than cost(M).

4. **Price the testing actions in a separate column.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Bits and actions are two readings, held apart.

5. **Demand a ground discriminator, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29-30 defines the anchor: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* And Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* If nothing at the available resolution is failing to resolve, report `unreachable-at-this-budget`.

6. **Record the lineage.** Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. A theory fitted after the data cannot count those data as corroboration, and a clean record does not make a theory right.

The round trip ties it together: Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The simpler model is a coarse description; the gap between what it holds and what the ground returns is exactly what extra complexity must buy.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "prefer the simpler theory". Simplicity is a code choice, a filter for the budget, not a verdict (Fig 1 :24-27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*).
- Not "the complex theory wins if it fits one more observation". A term that explains one occasion perfectly fails the bargain (Fig 5 :44).
- Not a single scalar ranking. Bits and actions do not add (Fig 12 :130); sub-verdicts are never averaged (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that a crucial experiment can always be designed from a description of the difference (Fig 6 :32-35).
- Not grounded. No measurement at any ground was taken in this simulated derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

1. A per-slot measurement in which M's residual persists and does not trend down while T's pays for it, under a declared code, with a discriminator that already returns a failing reading.
2. A demonstration that T's extra primitive is outside closure(Gamma), with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :14), rather than a term already in the closure that was merely unfound.
3. A re-census that derives one of the held figure terms from the others, or shows that the two prices can be added by a figure-derived rule. Either would break the shape claim.
4. Run-level refuter written before any data: if a discriminator at the available resolution returns identical per-slot residuals for M and T on every live slot, the claim that T buys a round-trip gap is withdrawn for that budget. Absence of a finding is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The figure set can say what a defensible comparison must look like and what would reverse it. It cannot name the winning theory for any real case, because naming it would require a ground reading. The lineage is the proof: the record of what was routed, what was imported, what was refused and what would have to be true for the verdict to differ. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not pick a winner between a complex new theory and a simpler model that already fits. They give the shape any defensible comparison must have. Split the question into description economy, live-slot ground gap and provenance, and keep those verdicts apart. Route the residual first: if a held term is false, owe a refusal rather than a bigger explanation. Then apply the description bargain, admitting the complex theory only if its stated cost plus what it still leaves unexplained is less than the simpler model's unexplained residue, and price the actions needed to test it in a separate column because bits and actions do not add. Demand a discriminator that is already returning a reading that fails to resolve, not a vote or an elegance judgment, and record whether the theory was derived before or fitted after the data. Where no such reading exists, report a budget limit rather than a verdict. What is offered is a checkable procedure, a pre-registered refuter, and the instruments that would close it. The choice of theory stays open until the ground settles it.
