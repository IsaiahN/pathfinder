## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the conclusion.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room of agreeing methodologists is not an anchor. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
2. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. Sending the full framework before asking for a check spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
