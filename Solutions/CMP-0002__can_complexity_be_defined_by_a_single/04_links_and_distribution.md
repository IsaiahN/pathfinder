## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **A proposed single scalar is still a proposal, not a ground reading.** Integrated information theory defines Φ as a quantity, yet its exact calculation is intractable, its proxies give "radically different results," and its status is disputed. This is the world-side picture of the figure claim: a single number arrives with a declared code and an instrument bill, and neither is settled by wanting the number to exist. Cite this when explaining why Option A is not refused out of conservatism.
2. **The averaging failure is already known in engineering.** Beer's observation that average patient temperature misses a local fever is a clean, low-overlap instance of Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." It is the strongest non-framework witness in this folder.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described their residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the measure shape and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source links.
- **Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** If the cost of a wrong definition is high (it is, for a field's standard of measurement), spend the effort to find a genuinely different closure.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the measure shape and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and whether they know of a single scalar that clears the four conditions; (3) only then the three source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a verdict. Record it before showing anything else (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figures, the canonical README and the formula. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method reuse if asked.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. That is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` exists as a separate, sendable artifact with the mode and refuters in front of the links.
