# Expert verification packet: can complexity be one measurable quantity?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complexity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on per-domain bindings, `gap` on a universal index. Not `grounded`.** No instrument is claimed to settle which axes a particular system should use. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A single scalar cannot define complexity across systems. The figures price any composition in two currencies that do not add (bits for the description, actions for the settling) and call the merging of them an error; they hold that the primitive axes are "not three readings of one quantity" and that two readings count as two measures only when they draw on different evidence; they index the residual per object slot and forbid aggregation; and they disqualify a frame that scores itself. The general form of a complexity report is therefore fixed, while its binding is per system and must be provenanced: a universal index is a declared `gap`, unreachable from the held measure set rather than proved impossible everywhere. What is offered is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12
- **Statement:** a composed report has at least two prices in different units; summing them into one number is the named error, not the fix.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them" Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." The Operators table defines + as "both hold at once," not a unit conversion.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a quantity that prices description and settling in one unit for the same system, preserving both readings with no loss, and show why Fig 12 :125-130 was aimed at a different quantity.

### C2 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13, 1 and 2
- **Statement:** the axes are not readings of one quantity; readings are per slot, not aggregated; a frame cannot score itself; two measures require independence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :35 "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network." Fig 13 :36-37 "If two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information, and one of them would be redundant rather than minimal." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27 "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
- **Check:** do these four constraints together force the multi-axis shape, or does one of them govern a different quantity so that a scalar survives? In particular, is there a known complexity scalar that preserves the per-slot location of a residual and whose anchor does not move when the measuring frame changes?
- **Refuter:** any one of: (a) two proposed complexity measures drawing on exactly the same evidence yet carrying distinct non-redundant information; (b) a scalar that preserves per-slot residuals and is settled by a ground outside the measuring frame; (c) a demonstration that Fig 1 :19-27 governs a different quantity in complexity measurement.

### C3 — framework-internal with a world-facing check
- **Statement:** the universal index is a `gap` (unreachable from the held set), and the per-system binding is a provenanced choice, not a derivation of nature's own variable list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The framework essays add: "a minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."
- **World check (secondary):** the Complexity article reports "even among scientists, there is no unique definition of complexity"; Integrated information theory proposes a single Φ but its proxies give "radically different results"; cybernetic variety is "measured in bits" and requires the observer's "powers of discrimination" to be specified. These corroborate the open, multi-axis picture; they do not decide the figure verdict.
- **Refuter:** an imported primitive from a frame whose closure genuinely differs that makes bits and actions commensurable without loss, with source gate and shadow test recorded. That would move the wall and clear the `gap`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that entropy, Kolmogorov complexity, statistical complexity, integrated information Φ, cybernetic variety or energy rate density is wrong. Each is a binding onto an axis, admissible for a declared purpose with a code and a ground.
- Not that every system needs a wholly ad hoc measure. The measurement *form* is general; only the cast, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :20).
- Not that the six-term minimum set is nature's own complexity list. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral.
- Not a proof that no scalar can ever exist. The closure argument proves underivability from this set, not impossibility everywhere (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at a ground is claimed here.
- Not a claim that anyone who disagrees has misunderstood the framework.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Specifically:

1. **Commensurability test:** an instrument or proof that description cost and settling cost are one quantity in one unit for the same system, with no loss of either reading.
2. **Independence test:** a demonstration that two proposed complexity measures are convergent derivations (maximum independence) or one shared pool (one witness). Provenance records decide.
3. **Per-slot test:** a scalar that preserves the location of a live residual and is settled by a ground outside the measuring frame.
4. **Boundaries:** any one of the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 firing moves the verdict. Prestige, number of endorsements and computational convenience are filters, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :125-130 and Fig 13 :35-37 and Fig 1 :19-27, four short passages in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.
2. Name one complexity scalar you know, then mark which of the four constraints (two currencies, axis independence, per slot, external ground) it violates. If it violates none, C1 or C2 is refuted.
3. Decide whether entropy and description length, as used in your field, are convergent derivations or a shared evidence pool. That decides whether they are two measures or one.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
