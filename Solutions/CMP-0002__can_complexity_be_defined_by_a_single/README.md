## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not evidence.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list of people who already read the same complexity literature is one pool talking.
3. **Do not spend the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Boundary and honesty

This folder was produced by a simulated derivation, not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay states that LLMs "can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Every substantive claim states its mode. This solution cannot settle the question it reports on; it returns a checkable shape, written refuters, and the instruments that would close it.
