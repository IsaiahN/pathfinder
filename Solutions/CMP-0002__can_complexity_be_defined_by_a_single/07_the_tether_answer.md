# The Tether's answer: complexity cannot be one scalar, but the measure shape is general

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on per-domain bindings, `gap` on any universal index. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether one measurable quantity can define complexity across systems. In effect terms, Option A (one scalar) gives a cheap ranking but discards where a difference lives and mixes incommensurate units; Option B (different measures) keeps readings separate and checkable at the cost of refusing a single global score. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Two currencies, no sum.** Any compositional report is priced in bits for the description and actions for the settling, and these "do not add" (Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."). The figures call merging them an error, not a fix. So a scalar `complexity = bits + actions` is a notation error (Operators table: + means "both hold at once," not a unit conversion).
2. **Axes are not readings of one quantity.** Fig 13 :35 "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network." Two measures count as two only if they draw on different evidence (Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."). Agreement from one pool is one witness (Fig 8 :47 "Independence is one quantity read twice.").
3. **Per slot, never an average.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global complexity score is the average that throws away the where-to-look signal (Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."). The cybernetics literature supplies a low-overlap witness: a patient's average temperature cannot detect a local fever.
4. **The frame cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :27 "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A measure must range against a ground that does not move when the frame thinks (Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks.").
5. **The form is general, the binding is per system.** Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." So different systems legitimately use different measures, but they do not use different *logic*: keep currencies separate, keep readings per slot, check independence, range against the ground. The particular measures are a provenanced binding, not a derivation of nature's own list. The six-term minimum set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."
6. **The universal index is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." No amount of arranging produces a new kind of measurement, and a scalar that merged currencies and axes would be a new kind. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134). This is not a claim that no scalar can ever be useful for a narrow declared purpose; it is a verdict that no scalar is figure-licensed as a definition of complexity across systems.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that entropy, Kolmogorov complexity, statistical complexity, Φ, variety or energy rate density is wrong. Each is a binding from a world frame onto an axis, admissible for a declared purpose with a stated code and ground.
- Not that every system needs a wholly ad hoc measure. The measurement *form* is general; only the cast, currency and anchor change.
- Not that the six-term set is nature's own complexity list. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral.
- Not a proof that no scalar exists. The closure argument proves underivability from this set, not impossibility everywhere, and "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles which axes a particular system should use.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Commensurability test:** an instrument or proof showing that description cost and settling cost are one quantity in one unit for the same system, preserving both readings. That would fire refuter 1.
- **Independence test:** show that two proposed complexity measures draw on disjoint evidence (convergent derivation, Fig 8 :50) or on one pool (adopted import). That decides whether we have two measures or one.
- **Per-slot test:** show that a scalar preserves the location of a live residual and is settled by a ground outside the measuring frame. That would fire refuter 3.
- **Boundaries:** the standing refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written in advance. A ground reading that clears any one of them moves the verdict. Agreement, prestige and tractability do not.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreachable, not silent.** The framework returns a shape, not a name: the measure shape, with refuters, and a declared `gap` for the universal index. It stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." This is a `general` structural result with `specified` bindings and an empirical strain left open: whether any two proposed complexity measures are genuinely independent is a question for the ground, not for the figures.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a single number for complexity, and it does not say every system needs its own ad hoc recipe. It says the *shape* of an admissible complexity report is fixed: a composition is priced in two currencies that do not add, bits for the description and actions for the settling; the axes are not readings of one quantity and count as two only when they draw on different evidence; the residual is read per component, never averaged into a global score; and the measuring frame cannot score itself, because it must range against a ground that does not move when the frame thinks. What changes from system to system is the cast, the currency and the anchor, so the particular measures are a provenanced binding rather than a discovery of nature's own list. A universal scalar is reported as `gap`: unreachable from the measure set we hold, not proved impossible everywhere. What is offered is the shape any candidate definition must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question stays open until the ground pays.
