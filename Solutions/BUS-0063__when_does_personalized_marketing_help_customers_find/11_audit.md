## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among firm-library Γ_f, action a and customer outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by occasion. Three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (model, click) loses the customer's judgment, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The person's judgment is the anchor and does not update on the model's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Useful" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is a quantity compared against a second, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a customer, but "manipulation" and "help" are judgments that exist only at the person level, while the ranking that produces them runs at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the customer's step loop and the campaign's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

This seat at first proposed splitting D2 into "information environment" and "person's judgment", on the grounds that the habitat updates while the anchor settles (Fig 11 :73). On re-census they share one anchor, the person's considered judgment, and their overlap is a compound, so they are one domain B. This seat also tested merging A and C because both live inside the firm; it declined, because the anchors differ in kind: A is settled by realized customer outcome, C by settlement of a composable term. The primary kept them separate for the same reason without stating the test. Both censuses arrive at four admitted domains.

**Partition or union:** union. The two named conditions are anchor maintenance (A and D) and reach renewal (C, with B supplying the differing closure). Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).

**Dependency order:** perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3). ## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *personalization helps when the person stays the judge and the system shows them something new, and manipulates when the metric decides and the options narrow.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific product decision. Result: *ask the user what they think and add some exploratory recommendations.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It loses the anchor rule. "Ask the user what they think" does not discriminate considered judgment from a click that the system itself shaped (Fig 2 :30, :37-38). Coming back down, it can install the very proxy it was meant to exclude.
2. It loses the union. "Something new" collapses C1 and C2 into one lever, losing Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a C1-failure case to explore more, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
3. It loses the valence flip. "New is good" has no place for the possibility that the same recommendation changes sign with the customer's residual (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :117).
4. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot name the instrument that would distinguish a useful discovery from a habit, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
