## QUESTION

When does personalized marketing help customers find useful products, and when does it become manipulation?

Restated as an effect: when does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′_c| move toward what the customer would judge useful, and when does the firm's own prediction replace that judgment? R is indexed per customer, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the customer's considered judgment under a real option to decline, because the question is constitutively about what is good for a person (Fig 2 :36-38).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among firm model Γ_f, action a and customer outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by occasion; carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "useful" is a threshold relative to this customer's situation (Fig 12 :87). Scale: single customer loop, population ranking, and the campaign channel across occasions; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** personalized marketing helps when C1 (the person's considered judgment stays the anchor) and C2 (the recommendation imports a differing closure that expands the customer's reach) both hold, and becomes manipulation when the firm's engagement proxy takes the checking seat and the loop narrows into a mirror chain. The two conditions are kept separate per domain, with four distinct cells, never averaged. Which named tactic manipulates which person is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`. No world source flipped a verdict; the apparent conflict among marketing sources was returned to the census and re-ranged against different anchors.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A customer failing C2 would be sold novelty when the real problem is a proxy in the seat, or a customer failing C1 would be offered more exploration when the metric itself is deciding outcomes. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and reversible. The larger avoidable cost is spending an expert's independence by sending a theory before the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) proxy in the seat while ex post judgment, welfare and reach all improve, (2) durable useful discovery from pure replay with no import, (3) invariant technique valence across a change of residual, or (6) considered judgment failing the anchor test with no substitute nameable. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-customer outcomes are fully predicted by campaign-level CTR would also reverse it. A click-reading instrument that names its source would close the gap rather than reverse the shape.
