# The Tether's answer on personalized marketing

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No person-level welfare measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would say when a firm's model-driven action on a customer's information environment reduces the gap between what the customer would judge useful and what they actually encounter, and when it replaces that judgment with the firm's own prediction. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′_c| is indexed per customer, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about the **valence of the intervention**, not the size of the click lift.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the customer's considered judgment, taken where they can decline, stays as the anchor; engagement metrics stay as instruments and never decide the outcome.
- **C2:** the recommendation imports a differing closure so the customer's reach moves outward rather than replaying their past behaviour.

## 2. The shape

1. **A person's judgment is the anchor, not the metric.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* CTR, conversion and lifetime value are instruments that *"can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :38) and are quantities the frame produces (Fig 1 :27). The customer's considered judgment under a real option to decline is what settles the question.
2. **Manipulation is a convention that has started deciding the outcome.** Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. When the engagement proxy stops measuring and starts choosing what the customer sees, the loop becomes the mirror collapse of Fig 2 :14-15, where *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"*. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. The customer's preferences and the firm's model agree more and more while nothing new is learned about what helps the person.
3. **Help requires an import, not better replay.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* A recommendation that only composes inside the customer's past clicks can never add the product they would not have searched for (Fig 6 :21).
4. **A filter bubble is a substituted habitat, not a missing one.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* The firm can introduce what the customer did not choose and cannot subtract the customer's circumstances (Fig 11 :61). That is why the relevant question is not "were options removed?" but "which closure supplied what the customer encountered?".
5. **The same technique changes sign with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* The same data and ranking function, bonded to the customer's welfare in one system and to CTR in another, are two substances. No list of tactics can settle a named case by inspection.
6. **The break is at the objective link.** Fig 3 :24: *"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Firms can measure perception and revenue; they have almost no instrument for what would count as progress for this person. Drift into manipulation therefore does not require an intention to manipulate.
7. **The boundary is graded and timed, not maximal.** Fig 7 :37-39 gives two factors: bracket fidelity and ground pressure; zero either stops the chain. The world agrees at low overlap: the information-gap account finds curiosity peaks at a moderate gap, and recommender-systems work finds that exploration is *"a bounded process with user-specific limits"*. Neither "always personalize harder" nor "always show something new" survives.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No person-level measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which retargeting tactic, recommender or nudge is manipulative in a named case. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage lift of personalization over generic ads. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that personalization is harmful, or that covert collection alone is manipulation. Covert collection corrupts the channel (Fig 10 :26); the manipulation condition additionally requires the proxy to occupy the checking seat and the reach to narrow.
- Not that engagement metrics are useless. They are instruments for budget and for channel health; they are barred from the checking seat, not from the firm.
- Not corroboration by agreement. The applied marketing sources share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The cognition, recommender-systems and social-critique sources corroborate the mechanism at different strata, not the per-customer verdict.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the reading is a click or a purchase; the candidate sources are a useful discovery, a habit, a price response, a curiosity gap, social proof, or compliance under an undisclosed vulnerability.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. engagement metric demonstrably in the seat while ex post informed judgment and welfare improve and options expand;
2. pure replay with no outside import yet durable ex post judged-useful discoveries;
3. a technique whose valence is invariant across a documented change of customer residual and trust;
4. an averaged campaign metric that predicts per named customer who will be manipulated;
5. identical depth and timing paying equally across all customers and search stages with no decay;
6. considered judgment failing the anchor test with no substitute anchor nameable.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per customer, records the objective bond as well as the model ingredients, and reports which condition failed before the harmful outcome would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, characterized in effect terms but not held. Naming a winner from a click would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The seven claims in section 2 are a shape; the eighth thing, which named tactic manipulates this customer, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the manipulative tactic and it does not rank platforms. It says that personalization helps only when two conditions hold: the customer's own considered judgment, taken when they can decline, stays as the thing that settles whether a product was useful, and the recommendation brings them into contact with something outside their own past behaviour instead of replaying it. Those are two failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. A comfortable mirror is unhelpful without being manipulative; novelty in the service of someone else's metric is manipulation even when it is new. Manipulation is structurally a metric that has stopped measuring and started deciding, so the customer and the model converge on whoever had the most influence while agreement compounds and evidence does not. The same data and model bonded to a different objective are a different substance, which is why a list of tactics cannot settle a named case. What is offered is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close the gap. Which tactic manipulates which person stays open until a reading can name its source, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
