## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the total amount of water on Earth, but the **shape of the residual between what a water system expects and what a glacier-fed basin actually delivers**, per basin, over the coming decades. In effect terms:

- **b** is the belief held by water managers, irrigators and communities, formed from a historical gauging record under the assumption of stationarity (the past is a guide to the future).
- **a** is what they do: reservoir operating rules, irrigation scheduling, crop choice, infrastructure investment, land-use planning, water allocation.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the planning model predicts will happen: a seasonal hydrograph, a design flood, a dependable yield.
- **o′** is what the basin actually returns after glacier mass loss: changed magnitude, changed timing, changed interannual variability.
- **s** is the basin, community or agricultural district. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed by basin, never aggregated into a single global water-supply number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

- Option A, "glacier retreat means less water everywhere, and the fix is more storage": leads to a single lever applied uniformly and to a prediction that no basin gets more water in the near term.
- Option B, "the answer is a union of distinct conditions whose dominance depends on where a basin sits relative to peak water": leads to per-basin verdicts, different instruments for each condition, and a shape that declines to name which basin peaks when.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): **a decomposition of the residual into the conditions that govern different basins at different times, plus an instrument that would discriminate which condition governs a given basin.** Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` section 4 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the earth-systems question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of glacier-fed water supply.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
