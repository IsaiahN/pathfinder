## QUESTION

How will mountain glaciers' retreat affect water supplies for communities and agriculture?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| grow in glacier-fed basin slots, where b is a plan built on a historical hydrograph, a is allocation and infrastructure, Γ(b,a) is the predicted yield and o′ is the delivered water? R is indexed per basin and per season, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: measured glacier mass balance and gauged streamflow, named as the kind of anchor in Fig 2 :29.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among glacier state, climatic forcing, hydrological routing and human use, indexed by basin and ordered by time; carries an ordered bond (sequence) and a comparison bond (threshold), not symmetric ones. Symmetry: asymmetric in time; committed mass loss persists, and adequacy is relative to demand. Scale: glacier mass balance, basin streamflow, managed water system, and the multi-step downstream cascade; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** glacier retreat delivers a union of three conditions kept separate per basin and never averaged: magnitude and phase governed by the accumulation → peak → decline sequence, seasonal timing governed by a supply-demand threshold, and variability and hazard governed by the sign-changing buffer relation. The planning residual grows where a stationarity closure lacks the imported glacier atom and where the monitoring channel has degraded, not because the ground changed. The downstream consequence reaches agriculture and communities several steps out and is enumerated, not assumed. Which basin peaks when and which crop fails where is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`. The glaciology and assessment sources share a pool and corroborate the shape as one witness; the low-overlap witness from water-resource policy corroborates the closure mechanism. The one genuine conflict (UNESCO Box 2.2) was returned to the census and re-mapped, and it improved the shape by forcing basin and distance specification.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected adaptation. A basin failing the timing condition would be sold storage, which fixes magnitude and leaves the growing-season threshold standing; a basin failing the channel condition would be told to import more physics while its gauges are dead. A single lever applied to a union is the named failure in Fig 9 :55. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real, bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** any of the pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) supply unchanged after the glacier contribution is removed, (2) a stationarity model predicting peak water with no imported atom, (3) mass balance and streamflow stable while supply changes for internal reasons, (4) one intervention restoring all three conditions with no categorically different residue, or (5) a basin with monotone decline and no intervening increase. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-basin delivery is fully predicted by a global aggregate would also reverse it. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
