## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for EAR-0039 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about glacier-fed water supply that can be stated before any particular basin, peak-water date or crop is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a union of magnitude/phase, seasonal timing and variability/hazard conditions, indexed per basin, with the peak-water sequence and a supply-demand threshold, and with an instrument required for each condition.*
- Name, withheld: *the Indus peaks in 2045; the Swiss Alps have passed peak water; potato farmers in Huaraz will lose 30 percent of irrigation water.* The first two are bindings from the hydrology frame onto a shape; the third is a basin-level forecast requiring a ground reading that is not held here.

The distinction matters here because the public literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Huss and Hock name 56 basins and a peak-water year; the IPCC names regions; the WWDR names the Tropical Andes and the Alps. Those are bindings and scope filters, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "peak water", "water tower", "drought buffering", "baseflow", "GLOF", "stationarity is dead". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (the growing gap between a planned yield and a basin's actual delivery, indexed per basin and season) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating EAR-0039 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question. It is also the reason the WWDR caution (F10) was a useful conflict: it refused the single aggregate and forced the basin- and distance-specified reading.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the three conditions are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For EAR-0039, neither has happened at the level of a named basin date. There is no maintained, public, per-basin ground reading here that pays for "this basin crossed peak water in this year and this crop failed because of it". The peak-water concept is a provenanced import from the glaciology frame, recorded as such. Hence the `gap` row in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a water-policy explanation makes when it reads declining summer streamflow and names its source. A low summer flow is a reading. It is consistent with a basin past peak water, with a hotter and drier year, with upstream diversion, with groundwater decline, with a reservoir rule change, or with several of these together. The reading does not determine the source. The IPCC SROCC itself lists multiple contributors: *"Total amounts of river runoff will then depend mainly on rainfall, snowmelt, ground water and evaporation."* Naming glacier retreat as the sole source from the streamflow reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not hold. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to glacier and water research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about basins.

For outreach this means: a list of famous glaciologists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside, against a basin they know and can measure. Note also that the major assessment chapters are themselves prestige-loaded in this field; their role here is scope and corroboration, never the verdict (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular basin or peak-water year is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive or a conclusion not held in Γ. A basin name is a binding from the hydrology frame onto a slot, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall or that it is settled by the framework (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). A basin-level binding still needs provenance and a per-basin ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

A second correction: I first treated the UNESCO Box 2.2 caution as simple disagreement to be set aside. It is not. It is a constraint that generated re-census R2 and improved the shape by forcing basin and distance specification. Retracted as "noise", retained as a governing source.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about glacier-fed water systems. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For EAR-0039 the canonical shape is: **a three-condition union with an ordered peak-water sequence, a seasonal timing valve, a sign-changing buffer/hazard relation, and a per-basin index, with no basin date named.** It is not a global water total, not a peak-water calendar, not a crop forecast, and not a ranking of vulnerable regions. The names that appear in the glaciology and water literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
