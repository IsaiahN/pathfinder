## Three things in the literature that bear directly on the shape claim

1. **The anchor is not the photometric mass.** Boylan-Kolchin 2023 frames the test as a bound, M* <= epsilon f_b M_halo, and explicitly lists the ways an observation-derived mass can move: flux calibration, break confusion, IMF, AGN contribution, lensing, sample variance. This is Fig 2 :29 in astronomical language: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
2. **The reading can be settled upward.** Carniani 2024 turns a photometric candidate previously considered a low-redshift interloper into a spectroscopically confirmed z = 14.32 galaxy. Arrabal Haro 2023 turns another candidate into z = 4.9. Both directions are recorded. The channel is not a side issue; it is a first-order domain with its own ground.
3. **The model response is not one voice.** Ceverino 2024 and Donnan 2025 reach compatible bright-end behaviour by different adjustments; Shen 2024 imports early dark energy; McGaugh 2024 questions the structure-formation framework itself. Agreement among any two of them drawn from the same JWST data set is one evidence pool, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

## The two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1: validate the output against the ground (a spectrum, a baryon-bound test, a dust discriminator). Stage 2, opt in: read the framework that produced the shape. Stage 3: use the formula on another residual, with the user's own refuter written in advance.

Stage 1 before stage 2 follows Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45. Stage 2 is opt in by construction because a reader who has absorbed the theory can no longer serve as an outside frame. Stage 3 sends a generator, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22, but each user must name their own ground and refuter, since a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 works only if the shape claim survives. As written, it does not claim a mechanism at all, which is the point. If a recipient's answer is "you must name high efficiency / EDE / black holes before I can check it," that is a request to convert a shape into a name, and Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" is the reason to decline.
