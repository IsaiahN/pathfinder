## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named astronomers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among readers selected for receptiveness is one evidence pool talking.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraped personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

This package does not settle why early galaxies look bright. It returns a shape and a route: index the residual per object, range the reading against a non-updating spectroscopic anchor, re-fit inside the model closure before importing an atom, enumerate the habitat instead of composing it, and keep the union of conditions separate. Which of the candidate physical causes occupies a given slot is a declared `gap`, not a silence and not an invented name. A ground reading (a spectrum, a baryon-bound test, a dust discriminator) is owed before any cause is labelled `grounded`.
