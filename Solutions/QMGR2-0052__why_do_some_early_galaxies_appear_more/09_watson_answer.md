## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the reported excess: some high-redshift galaxies look brighter, more massive or more evolved than pre-JWST models predicted. Option A: accept a single mechanism as the explanation (models are wrong, or efficiency did it, or early dark energy did it). Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse to name a cause for a slot until a ground reading discriminates among the sources. What each leads to: A imports a name that may be one evidence pool talking, spends the independence of outside readers, and treats a per-slot reading defect or a model re-fit as a cosmological result; B sends a per-slot residual, an ordered test route, written refuters, and an open cause until a spectrum and a source discriminator settle it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For each high-redshift candidate slot, a positive gap between a model prediction and an instrument reading: flux, redshift, stellar mass, UV luminosity or apparent age exceeding the predicted luminosity function or mass function. Arity: at least three operands, reading, model, habitat, plus the observer's aim behind "developed". Symmetry: ordered, since "brighter than predicted" is a threshold comparison, not an identity; a population correlation is symmetric and cannot choose the arrow. Scale: first order inside one reading or one model, second order at their union. Fig 9 calls this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, instrument / reading.** Actors: NIRCam photometry, NIRSpec spectroscopy, ALMA, photometric-redshift codes, SED fitting. Currency: flux, redshift, inferred mass, UV luminosity. Anchor: a spectrum with a Lyman break, which does not update on a model's priors. Question type: partition boundary, which slots are real.
- **Domain 2, model closure.** Actors: semi-analytic models, hydro simulations, empirical luminosity-function models. Currency: predicted number density and stellar mass density. Anchor: the same measured luminosity function and mass function. Question type: re-fit inside the closure versus import from a differing closure.
- **Domain 3, habitat / physics.** Actors: halo mass, baryon fraction, gas density, feedback, black holes, dust, IMF, cosmic time. Currency: baryons converted to stars, and contact. Anchor: the baryon bound M* <= f_b M_halo, which does not move when a model changes its mind. Question type: union of overlapping conditions; valence relative to R.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers, reviewers, public venues. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run every seat draws on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle perception with a spectrum before re-fitting; state the objective before choosing an import; refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 8 :53).

Strain test applied: Fig 11's valence-relative habitat and Fig 2's non-updating anchor govern different quantities (actor selection versus settlement), so there is no contradiction after re-census. Fig 12's isomer vocabulary is an analogue over an abstract atom set, not a claim that galaxies are molecules; this is recorded as live strain S1 in `01` §7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a single mechanism does not. The excess is a per-slot residual that must first be ranged against a non-updating anchor. Some slots close under spectroscopy as reading defects; others survive as real z > 10 to z > 14 objects. Held model terms (efficiency, burstiness, IMF, dust, feedback, black holes) are re-fits and come before any import from a differing cosmological closure. The causes form a union of overlapping conditions with an enumerated habitat, so verdicts remain separate per domain and are never averaged. No figure line names the dominating physical cause: the literal "why" is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, with a source-discriminating instrument owed. Overall mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`, candidate. The framework returns the shape of the answer and the route to it, then stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim names no mechanism, so the cost is not a false physical commitment. The real costs are importing a wrong atom into cosmology, which Fig 6 prices above starting from primitives, and spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before the residual, which Fig 2 prices with "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Because no cause was named, no physical claim has to be withdrawn. A later import carries provenance and a shadow test and can be replaced; a re-fit can be superseded when the ground pays for a better term. The pre-registered refuter makes an error findable rather than invisible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A clean spectrum that removes a candidate's excess closes D1 for that slot; a confirmed mass above the baryon bound forces an outward import and fires R1; a source-discriminating instrument that separates efficiency from IMF from dust from black-hole light names the cause for a slot; a single lever that clears all intersections refutes the union; a figure re-census that supplies a cause-selecting line would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and a `gap` remains a verdict, not a silence.
