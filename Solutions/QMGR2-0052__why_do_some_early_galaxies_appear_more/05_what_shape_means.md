## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."

For QMGR2-0052 the shape is not "high star-formation efficiency" and not "early dark energy". It is the set of constraints any answer must satisfy:

- the excess is indexed per candidate slot (Fig 1 :19);
- the reading must be ranged against something that does not update on the model's own inference (Fig 2 :29);
- the instrument that settles a slot is built from a worse instrument already returning something, photometry before spectroscopy (Fig 6 :32);
- re-fitting a held term is distinct from importing a new atom (Fig 5 :13, Fig 6 :22);
- the causes form a habitat to enumerate, not a standing list (Fig 11 :43, :51);
- the conditions overlap, so verdicts stay separate (Fig 9 :57);
- the name of the dominating cause is not returned (Fig 9 :32).

A filled answer would be "JWST broke ΛCDM" or "efficiency did it". A shape is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the cause is named.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The Library of Babel method is proximity: describe the effect with the vocabulary you have, then let recurring terms narrow the space. The residual was described in effect terms first ("a positive per-slot gap between a reading and a model prediction"), and only then were frames sought. That order matters: Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

The vocabulary bootstrapped in this session, in order: residual per slot, channel, anchor, re-fit, import, habitat, union, provenance, shadow test, source-discriminating instrument. Each term narrowed the search: from "why are galaxies bright" to "which instrument settles which slot, and what is the provenance of the term that closes it". That is the Library of Babel recursion: "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A spectrum, a baryon-bound test, or an ALMA dust measurement can name the cause for a slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). Shen 2024's early dark energy is such an import, carried with its own cost in S8 and age tensions; it is not a free name.

Until one of those, a cause named for a slot is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is why the ledger records author, venue, year and overlap for every source and why the outreach packet asks for a refutation instead of an endorsement.

## 4. Metamerism: why the reading does not determine the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism directly, and it is the sharpest essay for this question. The essay's central claim: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An instrument that detects green "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"; the source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, "or a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered". The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists," and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

That is exactly the early-galaxy situation. A bright red point in NIRCam is a reading. Its source could be a massive stellar population with high efficiency, a dust-reddened lower-redshift interloper, an AGN, a top-heavy IMF, or some combination. Arrabal Haro 2023 shows the reading does not determine the source: a suggested z approximately 16 candidate became z = 4.9. Carniani 2024 shows a better instrument (NIRSpec plus morphology plus UV slope) excluding an AGN source for one object. *The Hard Problem is About Source* names what is owed: "The True Hard Problem is about determining the Source," and it is settled "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming efficiency or dust? Correction to my own first pass

**No, and I overstated it in the first pass of `01`.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Star-formation efficiency, dust, IMF and feedback are already atoms in the galaxy-formation frame. Naming one as the binding for a slot is a **binding from the astronomy frame onto a held term**, not the invention of a new primitive and not a wall-moving import (Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure.").

What remains forbidden:

- inventing a seventh kind where the six-term substrate is the object (not the case here);
- claiming that binding a held atom moves the wall (it does not);
- naming a cause as `grounded` when no instrument has discriminated it from the alternatives (the metamerism error of §4).

So the correct statement is narrower than my first one: **the framework refuses to name the source of a reading without a discriminating instrument; it does not refuse to name atoms it already holds.** Both the original overstatement and this correction are kept visible here and in `01` §4. Triangulated (README plus Fig 6 plus Fig 9).

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In astronomy terms: a result being published in Nature, having many citations, or coming from a well-known team gives it a budget to be tried. It does not make the photometric redshift correct. The first contact asks for a check, not for deference. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The output is "only as good as the validator"; "the agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the astronomical claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. The settled part of this folder is the provenance of the sources, not the verdict on early galaxies.

## 8. The honest stop

The three opened essays agree with the figures because they share an author and an evidence pool. Their agreement is expected and carries no independent witness weight (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). What survives the orbit is the discipline: describe the residual in effect terms, range it against a ground that does not update, refuse a name where no instrument discriminates, and record the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."
