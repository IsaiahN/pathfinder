# The Tether's answer: why some early galaxies look bright or developed

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on which single physical cause fills a given slot. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For each high-redshift candidate slot s, a positive gap between a model prediction `Γ(b,a)` and an instrument reading `o'`: the object looks brighter, more massive, older or more compact than the model's luminosity function or stellar mass function predicts. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symbols table :38-40 "It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."

The question as posed bundles three conditions with different anchors into one "why". Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." So the answer is three verdicts, not one.

## 2. The shape

1. **The excess is per slot, not a population verdict.** Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A bright-end offset in a luminosity function is a filter for where to point the next instrument (Fig 9 :60), not a witness for a cause.

2. **The reading must be settled before the model is blamed.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Photometry is the worse instrument already returning something; spectroscopy is the sharper one built on its residual: Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Arrabal Haro 2023 showed one z approximately 16 candidate was actually at z = 4.9; Carniani 2024 confirmed two at z = 14.32 and 13.90. The same apparent excess can be a channel defect in one slot and a real object in another.

3. **A non-updating anchor is required.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A spectral break and the baryon bound M* <= f_b M_halo are candidate anchors. A photometric stellar mass is not; it moves with the SED code, dust law and redshift prior. Fig 2 :30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."

4. **Re-fit held terms before importing new atoms.** Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent". Efficiency, burstiness, dust geometry, IMF and early black holes rearrange atoms the model already holds (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). Shen 2024's early dark energy is an import from a differing closure, allowed only with provenance, a shadow test and a debit against independence (Fig 6 :23, :24). Ceverino 2024 and Donnan 2025 are re-fits that reach compatible bright-end behaviour from different directions; their agreement is one evidence pool, not two witnesses.

5. **The causes are a habitat to enumerate, not a standing list.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Gas density, dust, feedback, black holes and IMF change valence with the slot: the same gas density that raises luminosity also raises dust production; black holes add light in some accounts and are excluded as dominant in Carniani 2024 by the blue UV slope and 260 parsec extent.

6. **The conditions overlap.** Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections. Verdicts stay separate and are never averaged into a single "the models are off by X".

7. **The framework does not name the dominating cause.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The literal "why" is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that ΛCDM is broken. Boylan-Kolchin 2023 says the candidates "lie at the very edge of these limits" and frames an "unresolved issue" spanning observation-derived properties, galaxy formation and the cosmology itself. An edge is not a refutation.
- Not that all the bright candidates are real. Arrabal Haro 2023 refuted one; Carniani 2024 confirmed two.
- Not that high efficiency, burstiness, IMF, dust, black holes or early dark energy is the answer. These are bindings or imports awaiting a ground reading for each slot.
- Not that the framework settles its own claim. "the output is only as good as the validator" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it, and the honest stop

The ground is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour reading "cannot comment on the source"; several paths create the same behaviour; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

- **Spectrum first:** does the slot have an unambiguous Lyman break, no AGN lines, and a stellar-continuum UV slope? This closes D1.
- **Baryon-bound test:** for a spectroscopically confirmed slot, does the inferred stellar mass exceed the maximum allowed by the available baryons? This is the R1 refuter.
- **Source discriminator:** do UV slope, morphology, ALMA dust emission and emission lines separate high efficiency from dust from AGN for that slot? Carniani 2024 is an instance of this move.
- **Union check:** is there one lever that closes every slot including the intersections? If yes, the union verdict is refuted.

Until such readings exist for a slot, the answer stops at the shape, records the lineage, and reports the gap. Fig 13 :138 "library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." The stop is unreached, not a claim that no cause will ever be found.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter (carried from `01` §5)

R1: a confirmed z > 10 galaxy above the baryon bound with all systematic alternatives excluded. R2: a sample whose excess vanishes entirely under clean spectra. R3: two readings shown to share calibration or SED libraries. R4: a claimed import shown to be a held atom. R5: one lever clearing all intersections. R6: a source-discriminating instrument that settles a slot at the ground. Written before the conclusion; if any fires, the relevant verdict is withdrawn.

## 7. Cost if wrong, undoable, reversed by

- **Cost if wrong:** importing a wrong atom into cosmology costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24), and spending the independence of outside readers costs more than a delayed answer (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both are avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **Undoable:** yes. The shape claim names no mechanism, so no physical commitment has to be withdrawn; an import, if one is later made, carries provenance and can be replaced. The refuter makes any error locatable rather than invisible.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading. A spectral confirmation, a baryon-bound violation, a source-discriminating instrument, or a demonstration that the conditions partition rather than overlap. Not by agreement, not by citation count, not by prestige.

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that JWST broke cosmology, and it does not name a winning mechanism. It says that each apparently too-bright early galaxy is a positive residual on one object slot, that the reading must be settled by a spectrum before the model is blamed, and that re-fitting terms the model already holds (efficiency, dust, IMF, burstiness) comes before importing a new atom such as early dark energy. The instrumental, model and habitat conditions overlap, so they form a union and their verdicts are kept separate: some candidates were reclassified by spectroscopy, others were confirmed even earlier than expected, and the same gas density can make a galaxy brighter and dustier at once. The framework returns that shape, the order of tests, and six refuters written in advance. It refuses to name the dominating cause until a source-discriminating instrument settles the slot. That refusal is a declared gap and a verdict, not a silence.
