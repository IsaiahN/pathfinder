## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether macroscopic behavioural equivalence (one universality class) can hold across systems whose microscopic components and mechanisms differ. Option A: yes, the class is fixed by coarse structure. Option B: no, the components fix the behaviour. What each leads to: A licenses cross-domain transfer of methods and predictions; B makes every system its own frame. A third requirement emerged from the census: the question bundles two conditions, different components and different mechanisms, which the figures treat differently and which must be unbundled before any answer is given.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the fine components we can name and the coarse class we want to predict. Arity: two systems compared plus the candidate class (φ*). Symmetry: class membership is an equivalence over coarse descriptors, decided by a threshold comparison of exponents; the relevant symmetry lives in the bond, not the atom. Scale: the class lives at the scaling limit, the components at the microscopic scale, so this is a membrane crossing (Fig 4, Fig 10).
- **Domain 1, statistical physics / critical phenomena.** Actors: microscopic degrees of freedom (spins, molecules, lattice sites). Currency: energy, free energy, fluctuations. Anchor: measured critical exponents. Anchor present.
- **Domain 2, framework kernel / membrane.** Actors: agents, one or a thousand. Currency: the method that survives the crossing. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Anchor present. Governs what may change while the class stays the same.
- **Domain 3, composition and structure.** Actors: atoms and bonds. Currency: arrangement. Anchor: the behaviour of the substance, checked against the world. Anchor present. Governs components versus arrangement.
- **Domain 4, verification / triangulation.** Actors: two frames with differing closures. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Anchor present.
- **Union or partition.** Domains 1 to 3 are a union (object level plus two structural rule sets). Inside Domain 3 there is a real partition between condition C1 (different components) and condition C2 (different mechanism/bond), which must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the class by arity, symmetry and scale; fix the criterion before looking at either system; compare each system separately; check overlap; only then assign and label the mode. Reversing this is apophenia (Fig 9 :27).
- **Strain test applied.** Fig 12 isomer versus Fig 13 "different routes" is not a strain after re-census (bond inside one composition versus routes across a lineage). "Components do not matter" versus "the bond is decisive" is not a strain (actors versus bond). One open strain remains: whether the class is a primitive or a settled molecule at the next level. Named in `01` section 8, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes, conditionally. Two systems with very different components can belong to one universality class when their coarse descriptors (symmetry, dimensionality, interaction range) match, because the kernel is invariant while the actors change and different routes arrive at the same substance. The condition is load bearing: if the mechanism difference changes the coarse arrangement, the systems are in different classes, because the same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance. "Same class" is an identity relation over the descriptors, and the class is a projection with lossy information: it says the readings match, not the mechanisms. The unbundled conditions C1 and C2 are kept separate and never averaged. This is a `general` shape with a `specified` application, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim is a criterion plus a conditional yes with a pre-registered refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost would be licensing cross-domain method transfer where the coarse descriptors do not match, or treating two Wikipedia pages as two witnesses when they are one pool (Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both are avoidable by leading with the criterion and the refuter and by declaring overlap.
- **undoable:** yes. The verdict is a shape, so tightening the criterion or narrowing it to a specific class does not require withdrawing the kernel claim. A refutation would redraw the class boundary, not break the figures. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled scaling measurement showing matched descriptors but different exponents would break the affirmative half; a microscopic detail outside the three descriptors controlling the exponents would break the criterion; a symmetry-relevant mechanism difference with coincident exponents would break the constraint; an instrument that recovered the mechanism from the class reading would break the projection claim. A derivation showing the class is not a settled molecule at the next level would close the one open strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or a larger pool of agreement is not such an instrument.
