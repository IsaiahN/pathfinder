## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :19-20 (kernel invariant, actors change), Fig 9 :22 (arity, symmetry, scale), Fig 12 :117 and Fig 13 :124 (bond decisive), Fig 13 :121 (different routes converge), Fig 1 :19 (per slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: yes when coarse descriptors match; no when the mechanism changes the arrangement; the class is a projection; keep verdicts separate.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a claim about two concrete systems, for example a magnet and a fluid in the Ising class, or two network-dynamics models in one Barzel-Barabasi class.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the three descriptors** in a concrete system. "Symmetry, dimensionality, range" is a coarse reading; deciding which microscopic detail instantiates "range" for a given model needs a ground reading, not the figure;
- the **bond/actor boundary**. In a concrete model it can be unclear whether a difference is a change of component or a change of interaction rule, which is exactly the unbundling the census demands;
- the **source discrimination**. The class reading cannot say which mechanism produced the exponents. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source";
- the **status of the class**. Fig 12 :104 allows a settled molecule to become an atom at the next level, but whether the class is primitive or settled is not decided.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a change of actors need not move the kernel, and a change of bond that alters the arrangement does move the substance. That residue is what is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed figure set" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
