## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The criterion is already public and matches the figures.** The Universality class page says the exponents "depend on dimensionality, symmetry, and range of interactions", which is the same three-item list the figure gives as "arity · symmetry · scale" (Fig 9 :22). This is not a second derivation; it is a low-overlap field arriving at the same coarse descriptors. Record it as corroboration, not as the source of the verdict.
2. **The network-dynamics paper demonstrates the unbundling.** Barzel and Barabasi place biochemical, birth-death, regulatory and epidemic dynamics into a small set of classes derived from the leading terms of the dynamics, while showing that the network topology can vary without moving the class. That is the figure's "different routes arrive at the same substance" (Fig 13 :121) in a primary research setting. Their two experimentally collected systems, email communication and yeast gene expression, are assigned a class even "in the absence of an appropriate continuum theory", which is exactly the projection-with-lossy-information point from the source essay: the class is the reading, not the mechanism.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full framework before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the criterion and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the criterion were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* - "You may all agree and be wrong." Agreement is not an instrument.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the question and the conditional shape; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure and record the verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

## Legal and structural note

Public institutional contact and submission routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid two failures at once. Scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40). Use opt-in channels, one-to-one only, with an opt-out line in every message. Nothing here sends anything. Hosting and sending are yours to do.
