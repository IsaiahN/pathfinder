## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *a criterion that decides class membership from coarse descriptors, plus a conditional yes*. That is a shape. A list of which physical systems are in the Ising class, or a claim about which microscopic mechanism a system "really" has, is a name and is outside what this derivation returns.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*, proximity: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Library of Babel*, steering: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."
- Fig 9 :22 gives the three descriptors the search converged on: "arity · symmetry · scale". The world literature converges on the same three (dimensionality, symmetry, range of interactions). That convergence is the narrowing.

## 3. Universality is itself a projection with lossy information

This is the essay that bears most directly on the question, and it is the reason "shape" matters here.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*:

> "You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."
>
> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"
>
> "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."

In metamerism, an instrument reads green and cannot say whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. Universal behaviour has the same form: many microscopic paths produce one macroscopic reading, the critical exponents. The class is the reading. The mechanism is the source, and the reading "cannot comment on the source".

Consequences for the answer:

1. Saying two systems are in the same class says something real about the reading and **nothing** about the underlying mechanism being the same. Indiscernibility is not identity (figures README).
2. The honest question is not "are these systems the same" but "what instrument would discriminate among the sources that give this reading". *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."
3. The figure-side parallel is Fig 10 :68, "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." The transform crosses scales and loses information; that is the point.

## 4. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself (consistent with `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5):

1. **The ground settles it.** "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, a measured exponent set settles a particular class assignment. This derivation takes no such measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Wikipedia criterion and the Barzel and Barabasi paper are imports of this kind; they are recorded with source, venue, year and quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They corroborate the figure-derived criterion; they do not replace it.

Naming a specific class from the coarse descriptors alone would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. The six-term substrate is described in the source essays as "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring the descriptors makes the choice visible, not neutral.

## 5. The unbundling the shape requires

The question bundles two conditions: different components and different mechanisms. The shape keeps them apart because the figures do:

- Different components, different routes, different links: same class is permitted. Fig 13 :121, "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."
- Different bond, different arrangement: different class. Fig 13 :124, "the same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance."

This is the same move as *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." Fig 9 :57 adds: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

## 6. Prestige is not action; this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."
> "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
> "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. The relevant instrument would be a controlled scaling measurement, not a larger pool of agreement. As the same essay puts it: "You may all agree and be wrong."

## 7. When to stop

*Library of Babel*: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is: the criterion is stated, the two conditions are unbundled, the refuter is written, and the mechanism remains unnamed because the reading cannot comment on the source. What remains open is recorded, not hidden. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
