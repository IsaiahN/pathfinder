## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

*The Library of Babel* (Isaiah N, 2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for CMP-0095 a **shape** is the set of properties any checkable ethical principle must have, stated before we can name the distribution it would produce:

- graded per affected party, not by a net aggregate (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- valence re-evaluated against the residual, not fixed as a roster (Fig 11 :49-51);
- habitat enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53);
- intervention treated as permanent maintenance (Fig 11 :61, :63);
- grading metric unmovable by the intervention (Fig 2 :33-34);
- stakes declared, including the stake in inaction (Fig 10 :59-60);
- description cost and action cost held in separate accounts (Fig 12 :125-127);
- rulings recorded with lineage (Fig 10 :62-65).

`Grade by total utility minus total harm` is a name. `Grade per slot, keep the ground unmovable, declare the stake` is a shape. The eight essays and the figures support the second, not the first.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

*The Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay describes bootstrapping from vocabulary closest to the thing sought, watching for recurring terms that overlap or correlate, then recursing.

Applied to this question:

- starting vocabulary (closest to the residual): "uneven", "benefit", "harm", "intervention", "distribution";
- recurring new terms found in the figure text and the world literature: "slot", "residual", "anchor", "valence", "cascade", "contact", "stake", "provenance", "two currencies";
- the narrowed space: the eight properties in section 1, plus the explicit `gap` on the substantive distributive rule.

Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." That is why the shape is stated as testable properties (what you would observe if the principle were violated), not as moral vocabulary the author prefers.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason `02_outreach_email.md` sends an effect description and a refuter, not the framework's interior.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **A ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question: a measured per-party outcome after an intervention, with a metric that did not move under the intervention.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question: a substantive moral theory imported explicitly as a premise, with its provenance recorded, not smuggled in as a derivation. This folder does not make that import.

*The Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". The paradox is that by the time you can name the book, you have composed the answer through the search. Here the search composed a shape and a falsifier, and the name of the just distribution is still absent. That absence is the honest stop.

## 4. Why the framework cannot pick a distributive rule

The figures contain no selector among equality, maximin, desert and welfare aggregation. SEP's Distributive Justice entry lists all of them as live competitors, so the world literature does not supply a selector either. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." But Fig 13 :133 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The correct label is `gap` after a genuine re-census, which is what `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10 records.

This is not an excuse to avoid the question. It is the distinction between answering "what form must a checkable principle have" (answered) and answering "which distribution is just" (not answered by these instruments).

## 5. Prestige is not an ethical credential

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-19): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In ethics of intervention this matters twice:

- A principle is not made right by the reputation of its author or by the number of institutions that endorse it (Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict").
- Prestige can legitimately give a claim a budget to be tried through a public venue. That is the only role it plays in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.

The legitimate ask is: give the shape claim a check, and let the ground settle it. Not: gather endorsements.

An earlier pass described `05`'s job as finding the single correct moral principle by narrowing the library. That overstated the method.
