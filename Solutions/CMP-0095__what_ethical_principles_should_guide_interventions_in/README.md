## Residual in effect terms

An intervention is an action `a` taken by a seat into a habitat. The distribution of benefits and harms is the per-slot residual `R_s`, one value per affected party or object. The question asks for principles that keep `R_s` visible, correctable and settled by a ground, rather than principles that make the vector disappear into a single verdict. Option A: give a substantive moral rule (maximin, equality, utility maximisation) and treat it as the answer. Option B: give the figure-grounded shape that any checkable principle must have, refuse to name a distribution the framework cannot derive, and state what would settle it. This folder takes B and records A as a `gap` rather than a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
