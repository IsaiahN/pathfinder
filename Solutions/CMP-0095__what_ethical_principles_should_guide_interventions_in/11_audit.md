## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-party distribution of benefit and harm after an intervention. Arity: a vector over many slots, because "unevenly" is a statement about components, not a mean. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symmetry: benefit and harm are not symmetric operands; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Scale: local action with a cascade that reaches two or three orders out (Fig 11 :52-53). Question type: **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Domain 1, intervention and habitat.** Actors: seat, introduced actor, habitat, affected parties. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground after introduction. Union with the other two domains.
- **Domain 2, evaluation.** Actors: slots, metric, evaluator. Currency: per-slot residual, plus bits and actions as separate currencies. Anchor: the ground as the only metric, which must not update. Partition: which slot carries live mass.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: checkers, stakeholders, maintainer. Currency: agreement and residual, apart. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition: who may grade.
- **Domain 4, substantive distribution.** No actors, no currency, no nameable anchor held. Not yet a domain. `gap`.

Order by dependency: describe `R_s` per slot, enumerate the habitat, fix the anchor, declare stakes, then introduce and read. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's three domains plus the fourth-as-gap. Where the primary derived from Fig 1's per-slot rule and Fig 11's habitat rule, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13, :19, :22; Fig 2 :33-34; Fig 11 :43-44, :49-51, :61-67; Fig 12 :125-127; Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :133.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the ten-item shape in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 2, the compact form "grade per slot, keep the anchor fixed, declare stakes, name no rule".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** what a policy reader would actually write, for example "use a distributional impact assessment with per-group outcomes, an independent metric, a stakeholder stake register, and an explicit statement that the assessment does not decide which distribution is just".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **instrument requirement** at each link (Fig 3 :27). The shorthand says "enumerate the habitat"; the concrete assessment must name which second and third-order contacts have instruments, and often cannot;
- the **two-currency separation** (Fig 12 :125-127). A policy assessment naturally wants one score. Keeping bits and actions apart survives only if the author refuses the single-sum format explicitly;
- the **no-exclusion asymmetry** (Fig 11 :61, :66). A concrete policy tends to offer an "opt out" as if it removed the residual. The figure says it substitutes a habitat;
- the **`gap` on the substantive rule**. A reader of the shorthand will hear "the framework has no view on justice", whereas the figure verdict is stronger and narrower: no selector is held in the library, so the question is unreachable with these instruments, not meaningless.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: grade per party rather than by a net total, with a metric the intervention cannot move. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the ten-item shape is the figures in fewer words" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
