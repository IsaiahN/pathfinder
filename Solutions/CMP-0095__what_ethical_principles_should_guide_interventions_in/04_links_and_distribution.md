## What the wider literature contributes

World sources are secondary corroboration. They do not outrank the figures and they never hand us the verdict.

1. **Measurement under pressure.** Goodhart and Campbell are a single evidence pool (they cite each other and share the unintended-consequences frame). Together they predict that the metric used to grade an uneven intervention will be corrupted if the intervention can move it. This corroborates Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." One witness, not two.
2. **Distinctness of persons.** SEP's summary of the objection to utilitarianism corroborates Fig 1 :19's per-slot rule. It also corroborates the `gap`: the entry lists seven competing distributive principles with no selector, which is what an unreachable substantive question looks like from inside a rich literature.
3. **Second and third-order effects.** The perverse-incentive article's documented cases corroborate Fig 11 :52-53's cascade. The anecdote in its title is historically disputed; the documented cases carry the point.
4. **Irreversibility and the limit of precaution.** The Precautionary principle entry corroborates Fig 11 :61-67 (introduction available, exclusion not; exit is substitution). Its own criticism that a strong principle is "self-cancelling" is a **conflict** with any naive attempt to turn precaution into an absolute rule. The conflict was sent back to the census, which produced F8: hold two incommensurable costs apart and do not collapse them into an absolute. The verdict was not flipped by the conflict.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict in a way that would change it. Where overlap is high, it is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot claim and its refuter, offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the per-slot claim were true before giving them the figure text.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the local figure extract and the audit, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a reviewer shows that a single aggregate is lossless, or that a movable metric can still settle a verdict, or that actor valence can be fixed across residuals, the shape claim is rewritten before any send.
