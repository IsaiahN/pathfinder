## What the opened sources contributed

1. **The driver is measured, not derived.** NSIDC's record since 1979 and the NOAA Sea Ice essay give D0 an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29).
2. **The ecosystem domain is a cascade with mixed valence.** Higher productivity and losses of ice-obligate species appear together in Frey et al.; borealization appears in Wise et al. This is the valence-relativity Fig 11 :49-50 predicts, not a single averaged impact.
3. **The weather domain has a mechanism and a contested magnitude.** Meier et al. give the albedo/barrier mechanism; Ballinger et al. give the heat-release and circulation context and explicitly flag divergent consensuses on midlatitude winter weather.
4. **The human domain is asymmetric.** Marine traffic and security reevaluation grow (Meier et al.) while food security, subsistence and culture are stressed (Meier et al., Wise et al.).

Overlap warning: the NOAA Sea Ice essay is authored by NSIDC staff and uses the NSIDC Sea Ice Index as its data product. Those two rows are one measurement lineage. The IPCC chapter and the NOAA essays also share assessment literature. Agreement among them is a pool talking, not independent corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
