**Mode:** `specified` on the figure-derived shapes, `grounded` on the observed sea-ice record and on the measured within-domain directions taken from the public sources actually read. **Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## Effect restatement

The question is not one question. It is a union of three receiving domains (ecosystems, weather, human activity) plus one upstream driver (the changing sea-ice condition). Option A treats them as one subject and returns a single averaged impact. Option B enumerates the contact of each domain with the driver, derives each verdict separately, and stops where the ground has not settled the magnitude. A hides a live slot in an average (Fig 1 :22). B returns three checkable shapes and three refuters, with the ground deciding the names and numbers.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term:** for each domain, a statement of (i) the actors in contact with sea ice, (ii) the currency in which the effect is measured, (iii) the anchor that would settle it, and (iv) a refuter that would break the claim. Anything that cannot supply an anchor is not yet a domain.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No numeric forecast of sea-ice loss or of any downstream quantity attributed to the figures.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* And Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
3. **Sending the interior before asking for a verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat Map :171: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
