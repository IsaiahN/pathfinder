## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the effect of shrinking sea ice on three receivers. Option A: treat ecosystems, weather and human activity as one subject and return one averaged impact. Option B: treat them as a union of one measured driver and three receiving domains, enumerate each domain's contact and anchor, derive each verdict separately, and stop where the ground has not settled the magnitude. What each leads to: A hides a live slot in an average and mixes incompatible currencies; B returns three checkable shapes, three anchors and written refuters, with the ground deciding names and numbers.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A condition in contact with a habitat (sea ice) is being removed faster than dependent frames can re-fit. Arity: one driver, three receivers, two feedbacks, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55: *"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Symmetry: asymmetric, valence relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Scale: first-order contact, second/third-order cascade, cross-scale surface-to-circulation transform. R is indexed by slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).
- **D0, sea-ice state (upstream driver).** Actors: extent, concentration, thickness, age, melt onset, albedo; conditions light, water, temperature, season. Currency: area, days, metres. Anchor: the passive-microwave satellite record since 1979 and the NSIDC Sea Ice Index. Question type: measured link, not figure-derived.
- **D1, marine ecosystems.** Actors: ice algae, under-ice phytoplankton, Calanus, benthic infauna, Arctic cod, seabirds, walruses, ice seals, boreal taxa; relations predation, competition, dependency. Currency: biomass, population index, phenology, composition. Anchor: long-term monitoring (DBO, EcoFOCI, NOAA groundfish surveys), stable isotopes. Question type: union, own loop.
- **D2, weather and atmosphere.** Actors: albedo, ocean heat uptake and release, water vapour, pressure, blocking, storm track, poleward heat transport. Currency: energy flow, temperature gradient, hPa. Anchor: radiosondes, ERA5 reanalysis, GISTEMP, controlled attribution. Question type: union, overlaps D0 through the ice-albedo loop.
- **D3, human activity.** Actors: shipping, fisheries, tourism, extraction, security, Indigenous communities, subsistence, infrastructure. Currency: cost, risk, access, food security, cultural continuity. Anchor: transit records, markets, community records, policy, and human judgment where the question is about people. Question type: union, overlaps D1 and D2.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** D0 is reached and upstream; D1 and D2 receive from it in parallel; D3 receives from both; feedbacks run from D2 and D3 back to D0. The weakly instrumented link is the objective link (saying what counts as progress in each currency), not the measurement link.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 habitat language strains on D2 and was re-censused to Fig 13 and Fig 12; Fig 12 cycle strains on D1 and was re-censused to Fig 11. No strain remains after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; an averaged answer does not. One measured driver feeds three receivers, and each receiver has its own currency, anchor and figure-governed structure. Ecosystems: contact cascade with second/third-order reach and valence relative to the residual. Weather: cross-kind energy/gradient flow plus a signed ice-albedo loop, with the midlatitude magnitude withheld as `specified` and contested. Human activity: asymmetric, valenced, permanent work, with value anchored in human judgment. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The observed decline of the driver is `grounded` from the satellite anchor; the shapes are `specified`; the numeric magnitudes are out of reach of the figures by construction and owed to the ground.

- **cost if wrong:** averaging the three receivers hides a live slot (Fig 1 :22) and would misdirect monitoring and adaptation away from ice-obligate species and subsistence communities. Treating a contested midlatitude magnitude as settled spends the independence of the people asked to check it (Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both costs are avoidable by leading with the split, the shapes and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The three-domain package can be re-split or re-censused without withdrawing any substrate term. The retracted readings in `01` Section 5 are visible, and `07` and `08` already carry the corrected form. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A contact census that finds no cascade, a controlled attribution showing zero sea-ice contribution to midlatitude circulation, a demonstration that the albedo loop is net damping, a domain with no nameable anchor, or a figure re-census that removes valence relativity would each break the affected verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
