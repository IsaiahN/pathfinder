## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before the numbers are known. It is not "sea-ice loss causes X". It is: enumerate the habitat, follow contact outward, compose the cross-kind flow, respect the signed loop, keep valence relative to the residual, and stop at the anchor. A completed numeric table of impacts is the answer itself, which the framework does not return.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the exact move made in `01` Section 1.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish *"will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name and a number

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework stops where instruments stop distinguishing and records the lineage.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and shadow test.

For EAR-0037 the names (Calanus, Arctic cod, Greenland Blocking Index, shipping routes) and the numbers (extent trends, productivity percentages) are imports from cryosphere and climate science, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They are not minted by the figure loop, and they do not add a primitive to it (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

## 4. Why the three-domain answer is a shape and not an answer

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."*

The figure set can say that the receiving domains must be split, that effects are indexed by slot, that the ecosystem reach is a cascade, that the weather mechanism is a cross-kind flow with a signed loop, and that the human effect is valenced and permanent work. It cannot say how much biomass will move, whether a given winter will see a teleconnection, or what a fishery is worth. Those are ground questions. Claiming otherwise would be a verdict stated beyond the instrument and would violate the mode rule.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially read *"a shape, not a name"* as a ban on naming any observed actor. That is too strong. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. The six substrate terms and the bond set are already held; naming an observed actor such as Arctic cod is not minting a new primitive, it is importing a term from the ecological frame with provenance.

What remains forbidden is: (a) claiming the figures derive the observed decline, (b) claiming a figure-produced numeric magnitude, and (c) presenting an import as if it were a derivation. Both the earlier and the corrected reading are recorded so neither is silently lost. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige is not equal to actions."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous Arctic institutes asked to endorse the shape is the named collapse. A public, opt-in venue that lets a domain specialist refute it is the legitimate ask.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the shape and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
