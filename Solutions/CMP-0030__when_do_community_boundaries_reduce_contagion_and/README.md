## What is being decided, in effect terms

A community boundary is the same structural object in both halves of the question: dense ties inside a set of nodes, sparse ties across the cut. The residual is that this same object produces opposite effects on one quantity, the spread and persistence of contagion. One answer must say which contacting conditions select the barrier regime and which select the reservoir regime, without pretending the two are points on one continuous scale.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the result.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No numerical crossover value for the modularity or bridge density at which the sign flips. The figures return a shape, not a number, and no instrument in this derivation measures one.

Three figure reasons for the first three omissions:

1. Fig 2 :35 — "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for agreement is a mirror.
2. Fig 2 :40 — "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." This whole package is one evidence pool.
3. Fig 10 :52 — "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The first contact is the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
