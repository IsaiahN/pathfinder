## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for CMP-0030 a shape is the form of the rule that can be stated before the crossover number is measured. The shape here has three slots:

1. **what crosses the boundary** (a head start or a recording, Fig 4 :22);
2. **the order of the composition** (first order inside a group, second order between groups, Fig 12 :104);
3. **the overlap of the closures** on either side (Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice").

A numerical modularity threshold is a filler for the third slot, not a shape. `boundary reduces spread when Q is above 0.6` is a name where the framework can only give a kind of thing.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The ordinary terms available at the start were "boundary", "community", "spread". The recurring new terms that overlapped were "filter with a direction", "head start", "catalyst", "cycle", "contact cascade".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So the shape is stated as what would be observed, not as framework vocabulary. "Does a signal alone start a chain?" is an effect description. "Does a recording cross the membrane?" is framework shorthand and belongs in stage 2.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are all written this way.
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the direct essay-level warrant for the four-cell split in F6.

## 3. What would license a name or a number here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For CMP-0030 that means a controlled cross-boundary transmission measurement, not another modularity computation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. The mesoscale metapopulation result and the household decomposition are imports of this kind. They corroborate; they do not become framework primitives.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A global attack rate is such a projection. It cannot say which group reseeded the outbreak, and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The resolution in that essay came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." That is exactly the outstanding need in F8.

## 4. Why this question cannot be answered with one number

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The Wikipedia modularity page records that modularity maximisation is not statistically consistent, finds communities in its own null model, and has a resolution limit. Those are instrument limits on the first slot, not reasons to give up the question.

So the answer shape is a **regime table**, not a curve. Four cells, two verdicts, one declared gap on the number.

## 5. This derivation cannot settle what it reports on

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here."

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not ground readings and cannot settle the question they report on.

## 6. Prestige and agreement are not action

From the same essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Applied here: a highly cited modularity paper can fund a study by making it fundable, but its citation count cannot decide whether a boundary slowed a particular outbreak. The correct ask of a checker is a refutation, not an endorsement.
