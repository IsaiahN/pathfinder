**Overlap warning.** These world sources are not ten independent witnesses. They cite one another and share the Scheffer / Boettiger / Dakos evidence pool. Boettiger and Hastings, Gsell, Contosta and O'Brien draw on the same family of summary statistics and the same lakes or lake-method lineage. Their agreement is one pool talking (`Fig 2 :40`). The ledger records overlap per row. Where two results disagree (44 percent failed versus 58 percent declining), that is a genuine difference of habitat and definition, not a refutation.

## 3. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a failure converts a generator into a recording. Lead with the taxonomy and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the interior before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would count as a failure before naming the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Prefer a venue whose reviewers work in a different closure, for example a journal or conference rather than a list of people who already use the framework.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the failure taxonomy and the pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record that answer first; (3) only then the three framework links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a reusable method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual and the four refuters from `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. Ask for the recipient's own failure criterion. Record the verdict before showing the framework.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the three framework links. Later agreement is never counted as corroboration.

**Stage 3, on request.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user names their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and labels the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the checkable sub-claims survive. The single most likely failure at contact is a post-hoc window or variable choice (Fig 10 :47). If a reviewer points one out, that is the useful outcome, and the claim is narrowed rather than defended.

No archive is created here and nothing is sent. If the packet is hosted, prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. That keeps the recipient in the position of an outside frame rather than a member of the author's room.
