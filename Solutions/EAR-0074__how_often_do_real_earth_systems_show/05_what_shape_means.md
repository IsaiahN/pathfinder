## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local rule: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops at the limit of the instruments. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For question 74 the shape is not a percentage and not a named indicator. It is this:

- the system's crossing is an anchor and the indicator is a channel;
- the failure modes are channel states, not a list of statistical complaints;
- the question is a union of three loops, not one search;
- the population frequency is an enumeration over habitats, not a derivation.

A shape constrains any acceptable answer. It does not supply the answer. In particular it rules out an answer of the form "EWS work 60 percent of the time", because that answer aggregates slots (`Fig 1 :22`) and lets a filter hand you a verdict (`Fig 9 :60`).

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So describe the residual as "an indicator that stops matching the crossing", not as "an AR(1) trend with a window of 10 years".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (recorded in the exemplar, not re-fetched): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends a failure taxonomy that anyone can test.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the searcher has composed the answer while locating it.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is the direct warrant for splitting detection, false positive and false negative before searching.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means a replicated, pre-registered comparison against a realised crossing or a stable control.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8 :50-52`). An empirical frequency imported from a specific enumeration is licensed for that habitat only.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) supplies the sharper reason: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. A rising variance can come from an approaching fold, from a chance excursion, from flickering, or from a detrending choice. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* That is the metamerism error applied to early-warning signals: naming the source (approaching tipping point) from one projection (rising variance).

The same essay also forecloses an easy objection. The answer is *"unreached"*, not *"unknowable"*: what is missing is a discriminating instrument, and the spectrometer, not an argument, is what resolves the case. Here the analogue is replication plus a pre-registered detector, which Boettiger and Hastings 2012 and Contosta et al. 2023 both call for.

## 4. Why the frequency request is the specific forbidden shape

The question asks "how often". That is a request for a number. The figures permit three readings of that request:

| reading | status | why |
|---|---|---|
| a count over an enumerated habitat, with the habitat named | permitted, `grounded` by that enumeration only | Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* |
| a prior for planning a monitoring budget | permitted as a filter | Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* |
| a universal percentage that settles whether a given system is tipping | forbidden | Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* |

The exemplar's `05` §5b records a correction relevant here: the rule *"shape, not a name"* governs a primitive **not held** in the library, not the binding of an already-held atom. For this question no one is asking to name a missing primitive. The gap is about a count and a discriminating instrument, so the correction does not change the verdict; it prevents overstating the ban. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (recorded in the exemplar, not re-fetched): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous tipping-point researchers who agree with the taxonomy is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground: a replicated experiment, a stable control, or a pre-registered hindcast. `03_public_contact_points.csv` therefore contains institutional venues and opt-in channels, not a ranking of likely believers.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The empirical counts it quotes are grounded by the studies that measured them, not by this derivation.
