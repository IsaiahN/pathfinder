- **Domain 2, the ground.** Actors: the realised crossing or its absence, replicated experiments, controls. Currency: the realised transition. Anchor: itself. It settles and does not update on the indicator.

- **Domain 4, the habitat.** Actors: lakes, forests, reefs, climate subsystems and their conditions. Currency: frequency counts. Anchor: each system's own ground. Enumerated, never composed.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 and :45 (anchor and ranging), Fig 9 :60 (filters versus witnesses), Fig 10 :24 and :25 (channel failures), Fig 11 :43 (habitat enumerated).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: a signal is a channel, not an anchor; failure is channel decay; frequency is a filter; the three loops stay separate.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a working EWS study, for example Gsell et al. 2016 with four indicators on 16 lake series, or O'Brien et al. 2023 with nine lakes.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **the identity of the ground.** The shorthand says "the realised crossing". In a real study the ground is often a breakpoint in a single series, chosen by three statistical tests and justified by expert knowledge, with no control and no counterfactual. Gsell et al. 2016 say explicitly that they lacked records to serve as controls. The shorthand's clean anchor becomes a best guess at an anchor.
- **the per-slot rule.** The shorthand says aggregate nothing. The actual studies aggregate over rolling windows, trophic levels and lakes because series are short. Fig 1's rule remains true and becomes harder to satisfy, which is a loss of reach, not a contradiction.
- **the enumeration.** The shorthand says "enumerate the habitat". Each study enumerates a different habitat with a different definition of transition, so the count does not transfer between them.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a signal must be ranged against a ground and fails when the channel loses contact. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
