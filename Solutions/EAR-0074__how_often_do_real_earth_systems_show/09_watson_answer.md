## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about two things the question bundles: the frequency with which real Earth systems show early-warning signals, and the conditions under which those signals fail. Option A: treat the signals as a generic detector and report a success percentage across systems. Option B: treat each signal as a channel that must be ranged against the realised crossing per system, name the channel failure states, and report frequencies only as habitat-specific filters. What each leads to: A hands a filter a verdict and averages away the live slot; B gives a checkable failure taxonomy with refuters written in advance and records a `gap` where the figures cannot produce a number.

**Pre-registered refuter, written before the verdict below:** (1) a pre-registered detector separated by replicated bifurcation and stable systems at fixed error bounds; (2) proof that two indicators from one series are independent evidence; (3) an instrument reading transition mechanism from the pre-tip series; (4) one protocol over an enumerated habitat returning a stable frequency across independent enumerations; (5) a case where the ground itself changes after settlement; (6) a pre-registered window and threshold that survive stable controls. If any fires, the corresponding verdict is withdrawn.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: one residual per system and per realization, `R_s = |Gamma(b,a)_s - o'_s|`, where Gamma is the indicator's reading and o' is the realised crossing or its absence. Symmetry: the indicators are ordered and threshold-like, not symmetric conjunctions; the decision is a comparison of a statistic against a threshold. Scale: within one series, across realizations of one system, and across a population of systems. Union, not partition: detection, false positive and false negative overlap in the same indicator and the same series. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Unbundle before searching.
- **Domain 1, the indicator frame.** Actors: AR(1), SD or variance, skewness, kurtosis, density ratio, recovery-rate estimators, flickering. Currency: a trend statistic computed from a detrended series, a description of the series. Anchor: none of its own; it borrows domain 2. A frame is not an anchor.
- **Domain 2, the ground.** Actors: the realised crossing or its absence, replicated experiments, controls, palaeo records. Currency: the realised transition. Anchor: itself; it settles the question and does not update on the indicator. Hard to reach prospectively, which is a budget limit rather than underivability.
- **Domain 3, the channel.** Actors: sampling interval, window length, detrending, seasonal adjustment, variable choice, truncation, number of replicates. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: inherited from domain 2. The ground holds; the channel decays.
- **Domain 4, the habitat.** Actors: lakes, forests, reefs, climate subsystems, their conditions and relations. Currency: frequency counts over systems. Anchor: each system's own ground. Enumerated, never composed.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance. The break is at vocabulary, where no instrument reads whether slowing down means a bifurcation or general instability; more statistics above the break cannot rescue it.

Strain test applied. The apparent conflict between "the ground does not decay" and moving thresholds resolves by re-census: what moves is the habitat and the frame's model of the threshold, while the realised crossing is settled once it has happened. A prospective forecast with no measured ground is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle. Neither strain was smoothed over; see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide the shape, not the count. An early-warning signal is a channel, not an anchor: it must be ranged per slot against the realised crossing, and the realised crossing is the ground that does not update on the indicator. Signals fail in four named channel states: a window or threshold chosen after the outcome, a fast excursion missed by sampling, a variance or autocorrelation statistic saturated by noise or detrending, and an average across slots that erases the live signal. The four classic indicators are isomers of one reading, split into critical-slowing-down and flickering kinds, and their agreement is one evidence pool, not independent witnesses. The universal frequency is a `gap`: it is a filter for a monitoring budget and an enumeration over habitats, never a verdict for a system and never an average across systems. Published enumerations disagree because their habitats differ. This is a `general` shape with `specified` per-domain verdicts, ungrounded by this run.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim states its mode, its refuters and its provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording or an averaged percentage instead of the failure taxonomy and its refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The gap is removable by one protocol applied to an enumerated habitat with signal and ground defined in advance, and the taxonomy is refinable by naming a fifth channel state. No primitive must be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. A raw percentage cannot be undone once it has been averaged into a decision, which is why it is not asserted here.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Replicated bifurcation and stable systems with a pre-registered detector and fixed error bounds would close the indicator verdict; an instrument distinguishing a fold approach from a chance excursion, flickering and a detrending artefact would close the vocabulary link; one protocol enumerated over a stated habitat and repeated independently would close the frequency gap; a case where the ground itself changes after settlement would require re-census of the anchor rule. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
