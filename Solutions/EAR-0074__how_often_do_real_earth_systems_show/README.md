## What is deliberately not here

- No personal email addresses of named individuals. The public contact form at `global-tipping-points.org/contact` is an institutional role inbox, not a personal address, and the webform is the route.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. `Fig 2 :40` disqualifies a verdict built from a shared evidence pool, and selecting for agreement manufactures that pool.
- No bulk-send plan. `Fig 2 :35`: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- No claim that the figures return a percentage. `Fig 9 :60`: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
