## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census from the question and residual before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; dependency order.

- **R.** The observed decline across slots. Arity: three candidates plus an overlap, so not a three-way partition. Symmetry: broken. Climate and direct pressure have different actors, currencies and timescales; the interaction is not a symmetric join of two peers but a case in two conditions. Scale: the measured decline is at one scale, the drivers at several, and the residual that matters can be several links from the pressure.
- **Domain 1, the measurement frame.** Actors: species, sites, instruments, series. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: the measured state, which must not move when a driver frame pushes. Genuine domain.
- **Domain 2, the candidate frame.** Actors: climate variables, human pressures, their compound, models. Currency: counterfactual prediction. Anchor: experiment or counterfactual. Genuine domain.
- **Domain 3, the contact and common-source frame.** Actors: conditions, relations, indirect effects. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, not a correlation between two driver series. Genuine domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union with an intersection. The word "interaction" in the question is itself the tell: a third candidate that is not a peer of the first two.
- **Dependency order.** Establish and describe the decline, split or unbundle, then anchor and buy independence, then enumerate outward, then let the ground decide.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-58 and the cascade from Fig 11, I reached the union from the semantic asymmetry of the question itself and the cascade from the need for a non-first-order anchor. **Different starting points, same domain boundaries.** Within one framework and one evidence pool that is weak agreement, not independence, but it is not circular at the level of the two routes.

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires that it be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29/:31/:40 (anchor and independence), Fig 9 :54-58 (union compound), Fig 11 :52-53 (cascade).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: keep `R` per slot, describe first, split and unbundle, anchor, buy independence, enumerate outward, filters not verdicts.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete attribution protocol for a decline: choose species and sites, run a manipulation or counterfactual, compare independent candidate frames, map the cascade.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the slots** (Fig 1 says "object slot" but does not say which species or sites count for a given decline; that is an ecological decision, not a figure decision);
- the **bond type of the interaction** (Fig 9 gives the compound, Fig 12 :5 says a bond carries the missing information, but no figure selects which bond, so the glyph is undetermined at concretisation);
- the **choice of anchor** (Fig 2 :29 gives examples, not a rule for choosing which experiment is admissible for which decline).

One thing survives cleanly: the ordered requirement that the decline be measured and described before a candidate is chosen, and that the overlap be treated as a compound. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this package; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
