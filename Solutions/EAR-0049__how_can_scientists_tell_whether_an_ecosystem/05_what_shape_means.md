# What "shape" means for EAR-0049

## Source discipline first

The canonical eight-essay set for "shape" is listed in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md`. Of those eight, **three were opened again this session (2026-11-22) and are quoted below**: *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, and *The Hard Problem is About Source*. The other five are part of the canonical set but were **not** opened in this session, so no quote from them appears here and none is recorded in the ledger as read. ## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

*The Library of Babel*, opened this session, gives the search-side version: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0049 this means the deliverable is not "the driver is climate" or "the driver is fishing." It is the constrained form a warranted attribution must take: per-slot residual, described before a frame is chosen, split where the disagreement does not shrink, union unbundled where both conditions bind, anchored against something that does not update, independent between candidate frames, enumerated outward through the contact cascade, with fit kept out of the verdict column.

A shape constrains; it does not fill. An unmeasured decline with three candidate drivers is the exact case where filling the slot would be a category error.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, recursion, and stopping

*The Library of Babel* describes bootstrapping vocabulary by proximity: use the terms you already have that are closest to the thing, then look for *"commonly used terms that correlate with the shape of what you have been describing"*, and *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: start from the vocabulary of the observed decline (which species, which site, which season, what shape of change), then move outward to the vocabularies of climate variability, direct pressure and interaction. The order matters. Starting from the driver vocabularies and mapping the decline onto them is the inversion that Fig 9 :27-28 forbids.

On stopping, the essay says *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Fig 6 :34-35 states the operational version: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* For this question the stop is the limit of what the available experiments and counterfactual designs can discriminate, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 3. A bundled question must be split before the shape appears

This is the part of the essay set that bears most directly on "or their interaction." *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*.

Fig 9 :54-58 says the same thing in the framework's terms, and adds that when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, the intersection becomes *"a specific compound"* with its own loop. So "their interaction" is not the residual after subtracting two clean drivers. It is a positive object: the region where both conditions bind, with its own habitat, its own anchor requirement and its own refuter.

## 4. What would license a name (a driver), and why this package does not have it

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For an ecosystem, that means a manipulation, an exclusion experiment, a natural experiment, or a declared counterfactual whose control world does not move when a driver frame pushes on it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test.

This package has neither for any named ecosystem. So it returns the shape of a sound attribution and stops. That stop is a `gap` at this budget on the case question, and Fig 13 :134 gives the correct word for it: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 5. Metamerism: why a correlated reading does not name a source

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, is the sharpest tool for this question. Its metamerism case: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The same reading could come from one green light, yellow plus blue, red plus green plus blue, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's rule: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: an ecosystem decline is a reading. Climate, direct human pressure and their interaction are candidate sources that can produce the same reading. A monitoring dataset that shows a decline cannot, by itself, comment on which source produced it. The spectrometer, in the essay, resolved the case not by argument but by being a better instrument. In ecology the analogous move is a discriminating experiment or counterfactual design, which is exactly what F2 and F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` require. The thing to notice is that this is an instrument claim, not an argument claim: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a check

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The essay also warns *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

For outreach this rules out gathering authoritative ecologists to endorse the procedure. Fig 7 :35 agrees: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, ask what would refute it, and record the answer before any theory is shown.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this session: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any ecosystem and cannot settle the question they report on. This is stated on every substantive file rather than once here.

## 8. Correction to my own reading

**Initial reading (retracted):** "shape, not a name" forbids naming climate or human pressure as the driver of a decline, full stop.

**Corrected reading:** the phrase governs what the framework can *return*. The framework returns a shape, not a name, for whatever is missing or underdetermined. Here the underdetermined thing is the warranted attribution procedure, so the shape is the set of constraints in §1. A scientist studying a real decline is, of course, entitled to name a driver once the ground pays for it (§4). The retraction matters because the first reading would have falsely barred ecology from ever attributing a decline, which is not what the figures say. Both readings are kept so the correction is visible rather than silently applied.
