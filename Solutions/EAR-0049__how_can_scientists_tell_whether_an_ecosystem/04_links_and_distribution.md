## Wider literature that bears on the question, read this session

1. **Counterfactual anchors.** Extreme event attribution shows that the answerable form is not "did X cause this decline" but "how much did X change the probability or intensity of a decline of this kind." This is F5 and F2 corroborated from an independent field.
2. **Fingerprints and discrimination.** *Causes of climate change* shows that unique signatures for all candidate causes are compared against observed patterns and internal variability, rather than one cause being fitted and the others assumed. This is F6 corroborated from an independent field.
3. **Unbundling the compound.** Both the regime-shift literature and Global Tipping Points report that a combination of gradual drivers and shocks, or climate plus deforestation, produces the decline. That is the union compound of F4, corroborated twice from different sources.
4. **Aggregation loses information.** Earth System Dynamics publishes work on information loss from common aggregation approaches to indirect human effects. This is F1 corroborated with low overlap.
5. **Cascades go several steps out.** *Silent Spring* and Fig 11 independently give the DDT-to-eagles case. Note the overlap: the figure cites Carson, so the historical case and the framework share a source. Treat this as one witness restated in two places, not two witnesses.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the claim and the refuter. The figures are offered only after a stage 1 verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :45** — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Do not negotiate agreement.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask the reader to characterise the decline before showing any candidate mapping.
- **Fig 7 :35** — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A set of agreeing ecologists is one room, not a check.
- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, never as a finished answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the procedure claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then, the figures and the four attribution links; (4) if asked, the formula as a reusable method with the instruction to state mode and write a fresh refuter.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** The residual and the procedure claim with its pre-registered refuter. The reader checks it without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, the reader gets the three framework links and the attribution literature. Their stage 1 verdict is already recorded, so their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, on request.** The formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

**Hard precondition:** this works only if stage 1 survives. If a reader fires any of the five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, stop and withdraw the affected claim before offering anything else.

## Hosting

No file in this folder is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's to do. Prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. Fig 4's rule about recordings versus generators is the reason: a download that a person chooses is closer to a head start than a recording pushed at them.
