## What this solution actually concludes

The figures do **not** name a winner among climate, direct human pressure and their interaction for any given ecosystem. That is a ground question and this is a simulated derivation, so it cannot settle it. What the figures do constrain is the **shape of a sound way to tell**: keep the decline per slot, describe the residual before choosing a driver frame, split a bundled question before searching, unbundle the interaction as a union compound, require an anchor that does not update, buy independence between frames, enumerate contact outward past the first order, and let the ground, not model fit, hand down the verdict. Which driver governs a real decline stays a `gap` here in the precise sense that the instrument was never run, not a silence.
