# Expert verification packet — attributing an ecosystem decline (EAR-0049)

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist, conservation scientist or attribution researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the procedure, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No ecosystem was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result about any decline.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The figures do not name a driver for any particular ecosystem decline. They constrain the shape of a warranted attribution: keep the decline per slot; describe it in effect terms before choosing a candidate; split a bundled question and treat the climate-plus-human intersection as its own compound; range each candidate against an anchor that does not update and that does not share an evidence pool with the others; enumerate the contact cascade past the first order; keep fit statistics in the budget column and let the ground decide. The question of which driver actually governs a real decline stays open until those instruments run. What is offered is that shape, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — observation frame; checkable by reading Fig 1 and by inspecting an aggregation method
- **Statement:** a driver-specific signal can be destroyed by averaging a decline across slots, so `R` must be indexed per species, population, site or function.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 — *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Check:** take a real decline dataset. Does a single ecosystem-wide index preserve the differences among species or sites that the candidate drivers predict? If it does, this sub-claim fails for that dataset.
- **Refuter:** a declared code for `R` under which aggregation preserves rather than removes the driver-specific signal.

### C2 — attribution frame; checkable by an attribution researcher
- **Statement:** an anchor that does not update is required; agreement between candidate frames drawn from one evidence pool is not corroboration.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 — *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :40 — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 2 :31 — *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
- **Check:** for a published attribution, identify the anchor. Does it change its answer when a driver frame pushes on it? Do the climate and human-pressure analyses draw on genuinely different evidence, or the same monitoring record tuned twice?
- **Refuter:** a warranted driver attribution with no anchor, counterfactual, manipulation or independent instrument; or two frames with a declared common pool whose agreement still raises the warrant.

### C3 — union and cascade; checkable by an ecologist
- **Statement:** the interaction is a union compound with its own loop, not a third peer or an error term; and contact must be enumerated outward because the residual that matters can be several steps from the pressure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 — *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57-58 — *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Fig 11 :52-53 — *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **Check:** in a case where warming and a direct pressure both bind, does removing one pressure clear only the single-pressure region and leave the intersection standing? Is the observed decline first-order, or is it an intermediate effect several links out?
- **Refuter:** a case where two binding pressures are strictly additive with an empty intersection; or a decline whose complete causal chain is first-order direct contact with measured zero second-order effect.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named driver for any named ecosystem. The case-level verdict is a `gap` at this budget, not a silence and not an underivability claim.
- Not that correlation settles attribution. Several sources can produce the same reading.
- Not that climate and direct human pressure are isomers. They are members of one functional group with different atoms (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1).
- Not that the figures select a bond type for the ecological interaction. They do not; that is a recorded `gap` (`06` Q2).
- Not that averaging is harmless, or that a fit statistic is a verdict.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken for this package.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not more agreement. Concretely: a manipulation or exclusion experiment with per-slot results; a declared factual-versus-counterfactual comparison; a fingerprint-style discrimination of all candidate causes against observed patterns and internal variability; or an instrument that distinguishes sources producing the same decline reading. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-22, the colour case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are the standing failure conditions. If one fires, withdraw the affected part rather than reinterpreting it.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 2 :29 and :40, Fig 9 :54 and :57-58, Fig 11 :52-53. Eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against one attribution study you know: was there an anchor, and were the candidate frames independent?
3. Check C3 against one decline you know: was the measured effect first-order, or several links from the pressure?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate climate from direct pressure for that decline, or say why none is available.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
