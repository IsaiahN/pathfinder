## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about telling which candidate drives an observed ecosystem decline. Option A: treat the three candidates as three competing single answers and pick the best-fitting one. Option B: treat the question as a union with a compound, require a non-updating anchor and independent frames, and return the shape of a warranted attribution while leaving the case-level answer to the ground. What each leads to: A spends independence on a fit statistic and can silently absorb the interaction into whichever driver was tested first; B gives a checkable method, written refuters and an honest stop at the limit of the instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R.** An ecosystem decline indexed per slot (species, population, site, function). Arity: three candidates plus their union. Symmetry: not symmetric. Climate and direct human pressure differ in actors, currency and scale; their interaction is a compound, not a peer. Scale: climate broad and slow, human pressure local and fast, interaction cross-scale; the residual that matters can be several links from the pressure.
- **Domain 1, observation frame.** Actors: species, sites, survey instruments, monitoring series. Currency: per-slot residual against a baseline. Anchor: the measured state, which does not change because a driver frame prefers an answer. Genuine domain, anchor named.
- **Domain 2, driver-attribution frame.** Actors: climate variables, direct human pressures, the interaction compound, the models representing them. Currency: counterfactual prediction and explanatory residual. Anchor: manipulation, natural experiment or declared factual-versus-counterfactual comparison. Genuine domain, anchor named.
- **Domain 3, contact and confounding frame.** Actors: conditions and relations, plus indirect effects arriving several steps out. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, never a correlation between two driver series. Genuine domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union with overlaps. A case in two conditions is a specific compound, not a harder case of either driver.
- **Dependency order.** Establish the decline per slot, describe it in effect terms, split or unbundle, then anchor and buy independence, then enumerate contact outward, then let the ground decide. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2) and describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the case-level answer is not available here. A warranted attribution must keep the decline per slot, describe it before choosing a candidate, split a bundled question and unbundle its union compound, range candidates against a non-updating anchor with real independence between frames, enumerate contact past the first order, and keep fit out of the verdict column. The interaction is a specific compound with its own loop, not a third peer or an error term. Which driver actually governs any named decline is a `gap` at this budget: the instrument was not run. That is not a claim that the question is unanswerable; attribution science produces ground readings for particular events. This is a `specified` procedure and a `general` shape, not a `grounded` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished framework instead of a characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter. A second cost is practical: a manager who trusts a fit-only attribution may apply the wrong lever to a decline that is actually a union compound.
- **undoable:** yes. The case-level gap can be closed by an experiment, a counterfactual design or a discriminating instrument without revising the method shape. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. No primitive is withdrawn. If a refuter fires, the affected part of the procedure is withdrawn while the rest stands.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A manipulation with per-slot results that isolates one candidate; a declared factual-versus-counterfactual comparison; a fingerprint-style discrimination of all candidates against internal variability; an instrument that separates sources producing the same decline reading. A figure re-census that selects a bond type for the ecological interaction would also close the one recorded glyph gap. Until one of these fires, the method verdict stands as `candidate` and the case verdict stands as `gap`.
