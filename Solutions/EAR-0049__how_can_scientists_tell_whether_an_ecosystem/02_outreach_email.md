# Outreach draft — check the procedure first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the claim in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survives the reader's own refuter. The whole offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about ecosystem decline attribution, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual.** An ecosystem declines across a set of slots (species, populations, sites, functions). We want to tell whether climate, direct human pressure, or their interaction chiefly drives it. The claim I want checked is about the *procedure*, not about any named ecosystem.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A driver attribution counts as warranted only when: (1) the decline is kept per slot rather than averaged into one index; (2) the decline is described in effect terms before a candidate driver is chosen; (3) a bundled question is split, and the climate-plus-human intersection is treated as its own compound rather than folded into whichever driver was tested first; (4) each candidate is ranged against an anchor that does not change its answer because a frame pushes on it, such as a manipulation, a natural experiment, or a declared factual-versus-counterfactual comparison; (5) independence between candidate frames is real, meaning they do not draw on the same evidence pool; and (6) fit statistics, significance and model weights stay in the budget column, while the ground hands down the verdict.
>
> This is a shape for an attribution, not a verdict about your ecosystem. It is derived from a set of figures and checked only for internal consistency, so it is explicitly **not** a ground reading. I take no position here on which driver governs any particular decline.
>
> **What would refute it (written down before this message):**
> 1. a warranted attribution from a single observational frame with no anchor, counterfactual or independent instrument;
> 2. a case where averaging across slots preserves the driver-specific signal;
> 3. a case where two drivers both bind but their effects are strictly additive, with no intersection to unbundle;
> 4. two frames with a declared shared evidence pool whose agreement still raises the warrant of a driver claim.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the affected part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - The figures and formula, public repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The attribution methods literature this was checked against: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_event_attribution and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regime_shift
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a decline you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two deliberate omissions

There is no request for trust and no statement that this procedure is the only one. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that *"You may all agree and be wrong."* A claim that cannot be refused by the reader is not a claim worth sending.
