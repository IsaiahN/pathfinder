# Structure and order in a three-driver attribution (EAR-0049)

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7). No ecosystem was measured.

## RESIDUAL

The primary audit says the interaction is a union compound and that contact cascades several steps out. Two structural questions are owed: (1) what kind of structure is the compound, and (2) what is the order of the links, so that effort is not spent above a break.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are climate and direct human pressure isomers, or variants of one functional group?

Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

Climate and human pressure do **not** share an atom set. They differ in actors (temperature and precipitation regimes versus harvest, land conversion, nutrient loading), in currency (biophysical forcing versus direct removal or alteration), and in timescale. By Fig 12's definition they are therefore **not isomers**. They are two candidate structures that occupy the same causal role relative to the residual, which is the weaker **functional-group** relation: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* The group here is "candidate driver of `D_s`"; the qualifiers are climate and direct pressure.

This matters because the tempting analogy, treating climate and human pressure as two spellings of one thing, is figure-wrong. If they were isomers, a single shared atom set would let one be rearranged into the other and the attribution would be a notation problem. It is not: they are different things that can produce the same reading, which is the metamerism case from `05` §5.

### Q2 — What is the structure of the interaction compound?

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

The interaction is not `C + H` in the sense of a flat list of ingredients. It is the region where both bind and where the effect of one depends on the level of the other. That relation carries information the operands do not, which is exactly what Fig 12 :5 says the bond records.

**Strain test and honest stop.** The figures define seven bonds and give tests for each, but they do not say which bond an ecological interaction instantiates. Asserting one (for example calling it the symmetric conjunction `+`, *"both hold at once"*, or the comparison `⋛`) would force the case onto a glyph the figures did not select for it. That is a strain, so it sends the question back to the census rather than producing a mapping. The correct report is: the compound is bond-bearing, the bond type is **undetermined here**, and a discriminating instrument would be needed to name it. This is a `gap` on the glyph, not on the compound. Reduced to a falsifiable form: if an interaction can be written as a flat list of pressures with no relation, Fig 12 :5 does not govern it.

### Q3 — What is the order of the links in a cascade?

Fig 11 :52-53: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

The order is therefore:

1. **First order:** the pressure at its point of application (spraying on land; harvesting of a predator; nutrient input to a lake).
2. **Second order:** the intermediate effect (biomagnification through the food chain; release of urchins after predator removal; algal bloom after nutrient loading).
3. **Third order and beyond:** the residual that actually mattered (eggshell thinning and bird decline; loss of kelp forest; hypoxia and fish collapse).

The order is not cosmetic. A study that measures only the first order will find a weak or absent signal at the place where the decline is observed, and may conclude that the pressure is not the driver. A study that measures only the third order will find the decline but cannot name its source, which is the metamerism error again. Fig 12 :104 gives the recursion rule that lets a settled intermediate become an operand for the next link: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*

**Mode note:** the order is figure-derived (`general`/`specified`). The particular DDT chain is a historical case, corroborated by *Silent Spring*, opened this session. The figure and the history share Carson as a source, so this is one witness restated twice, not two independent witnesses.

### Q4 — Which links must be settled before an interaction term can be fitted?

Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

The dependency order for this question is:

| link | status required before moving on | common failure |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Is there a decline, per slot? | measured | averaging it away (F1) |
| 2. What shape does it have? | described in effect terms | choosing the driver first (F5) |
| 3. Is the question one, several, or a union? | split or unbundled | fitting an interaction over a bundle (F3, F4) |
| 4. What anchor ranges each candidate? | declared, non-updating | correlating two series and calling it attribution (F2) |
| 5. Are the frames independent? | provenance declared | pooling one dataset twice (F6) |
| 6. Which intermediate links carry the effect? | enumerated outward | first-order-only scoping (F7) |

Fitting a six-way interaction at link 3 when link 1 has no instrument is effort above the break. The link below a break cannot be read, so the interaction result would be a reading of nothing, not a weak reading of it.

---

## CONCLUSION

Climate and direct human pressure are **not isomers**; they are members of one functional group of candidate drivers with different atoms. Their interaction is a **bond-bearing compound**, not a flat list, but the figures do not select a bond type for it, so the glyph is honestly left open. The causal chain is **ordered**: first-order pressure, second-order and further effects, and the residual that matters several steps out. Dependency order requires that the decline be measured and described before any interaction term is fitted. These are structural results about a method, not a claim about which driver governs any real decline.
