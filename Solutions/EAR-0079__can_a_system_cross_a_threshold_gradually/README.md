## The question restated as an effect

Three things are being decided. (1) Can the quantity being compared with a threshold change continuously, so that the approach looks gradual? (2) Is the crossing itself an event with a location, or is it spread out? (3) Can the impact that registers at the ground be separated in time from the crossing, so that it appears much later? Option A treats the question as one continuous event, a threshold that is itself crossed slowly. Option B treats it as a union of two loops: a continuous approach in an ordering, and a delayed registration of a residual that propagates across slots and scales. A collapses three different quantities into one word. B keeps them separate, states a shape for each, and says where the ground would have to be read to close it. What an answer would look like is a shape: an ordered comparison, a continuous ordering, a per-slot residual, and a cascade delay, with the boundary between them marked rather than filled. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else (formula step "Direction").

## Boundary and honesty

**This is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output can *"simulate an approximate answer/output"* while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Every substantive claim below is marked `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. Nothing is labelled `grounded`. This package cannot settle the question it reports on. A real measurement, a bifurcation experiment, a palaeorecord or an instrument would have to settle it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Adding recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list ranked by likely assent is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing a reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
