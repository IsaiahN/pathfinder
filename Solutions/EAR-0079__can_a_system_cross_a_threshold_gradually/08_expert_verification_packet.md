## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Can a system cross a threshold gradually, with impacts appearing only much later?" is a union of three conditions, not one event. A threshold is an ordered comparison between two quantities; the comparison fires at a value, and the gradual part belongs to the ordering being compared. The impact is a separate term: the residual is indexed by slot, a near-zero global reading with one live slot is a legal state, and the impact that matters can be three steps out in the habitat, arriving much later elsewhere. If it is read at another scale, the bridge is lossy and the round-trip gap must be measured. The framework supplies that shape, not a threshold value, a lag or a named system.

- **Statement:** a threshold is an ordered comparison, not a duration; smoothness is derived, not primitive.

### C2: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 11

- **Statement:** the impact is not the crossing. The residual is per slot, a global near-zero reading with one live slot is legal, and the impact that matters can be three steps out.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 1 :20, *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*; Fig 11 :52-53, *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** is per-slot indexing actually load-bearing in the framework, and does the DDT example really license a three-step cascade rather than a one-step effect?
- **Refuter:** show R is a global scalar with no slot index; or exhibit a late impact in direct contact with no intermediate actor and no scale transform.

### C3: framework-internal, with a scale bridge; checkable by reading Figure 4

- **Statement:** a delay read across scales carries a round-trip gap that must be measured, not assumed zero.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30, *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :27, *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*
- **Refuter:** show the transform between the forcing scale and the impact scale is lossless, or demonstrate a measured round-trip gap of zero for a threshold crossing.
