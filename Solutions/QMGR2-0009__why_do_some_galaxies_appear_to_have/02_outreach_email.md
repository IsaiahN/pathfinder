> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Treat one galaxy as one slot. The residual is the difference between the mass a prediction frame expects from the luminous matter and distance, and the mass an instrument returns from globular-cluster or stellar velocities. Three things follow from the structure of that residual, before any physics is named:
>
> 1. The residual is per galaxy, never a population average. DF2 and DF4 earn separate verdicts. In 2024 Gemini imaging found tidal tails in DF4 and none in DF2 down to about 30.9 mag per square arcsecond. A single sentence about "UDGs lacking dark matter" would have thrown away that difference.
> 2. The live link is the measurement channel, chiefly distance and the velocity tracer. The distance is the contested anchor. Groups using different indicators have argued for about 13 Mpc and for about 19 to 20 Mpc, and those two values swap the verdict from an ordinary dwarf to a genuine deficit. An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not move when a theory pushes on it; agreement among models is not an anchor.
> 3. The gap must be sorted before a mechanism is minted. A wrong distance is a mis-attached gap: refit what you have. A real stripped halo or a modified force law is a mechanism-broken gap: something new is owed. Those are different checks and they are not interchangeable.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not claiming the figures say which mechanism is right. They return a shape, not a name. So I am not asserting tidal stripping, a distance error, MOND, or a collision as the answer. I am not pooling agreement among simulations as corroboration, and I am not offering a theory of everything. The physical cause is an open `gap` for this method, to be settled by a measurement, not by a vote.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before I sent this):**
> 1. An independent distance ladder or mass tracer that does not move with theory preference settles the residual to nothing, so there is no live slot to explain.
> 2. A galaxy with a genuine deficit is found in the field with no neighbour in contact, breaking the habitat import.
> 3. Averaging across galaxies is shown to preserve the directional signal, breaking the per-slot claim.
> 4. A physical mechanism is derived from the method's primitives with no import and no measurement, closing the gap I am reporting.
