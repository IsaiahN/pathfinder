## 0. The residual in effect terms

Take one galaxy as one slot `s`. Let `Γ(b,a)` be what a prediction frame says the dynamical mass should be, given a belief `b` (stellar mass, distance, halo relation) and an observation action `a` (spectroscopy of globular clusters or stars). Let `o'` be what the instrument returns (a velocity dispersion converted to a mass inside a radius). Then:

`R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`

For NGC 1052-DF2 the discovery paper reported a velocity dispersion below 10.5 km/s at 90 percent confidence, a mass inside 7.6 kpc below 3.4 x 10^8 solar masses, and a halo-to-stars ratio of order unity, *"a factor of at least 400 lower than expected"* (van Dokkum et al., Nature 2018, arXiv:1803.10237, opened 2026-11-23). That is a live `R_s`, not a global average. "Appear to have less dark matter than expected" is the observation; the question is which link carries the residual.

Four candidate carriers, kept separate:

1. the measurement channel (distance, tracer sampling, dynamical-equilibrium assumption);
2. the prediction frame (abundance matching under Lambda-CDM);
3. a competing frame (modified gravity, MOND with the external-field effect);
4. an actor in the habitat (tidal stripping, ram pressure, a collision).

The task is not to pick a favourite. It is to census the domains, quote the figures that bear, and state which verdict is triangulated, which is single-source, and which is a gap.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: one slot per galaxy, with several channels touching that slot (kinematics, distance indicator, lensing where available, environment). Symmetry: none. This is a directed comparison of a predicted quantity against an observed one, not a symmetric join of two peers. Scale: one stratum (a galaxy), with the group or cluster as the containing habitat that must be enumerated outwards (Fig 11 :44). The residual is per object:

> Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

> Fig 1 :28 "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."

So averaging the deficit across a UDG population would destroy the only signal that says which galaxy to probe.

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor).** A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | verdict type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Measurement channel | HST, Keck, Gemini, globular clusters, stars, SBF/TRGB/megamaser distance indicators, dispersion estimators | velocity dispersion, distance modulus, luminosity | an independent measurement that does not move when a theory pushes on it: a distance ladder, an independent tracer | the live link; refit before reminting |
| D2 Standard prediction frame | dark matter halos, N-body simulations, abundance matching, the stellar-mass-halo-mass relation | predicted halo mass | simulation plus cosmological parameters, legitimate because the question is constitutively about them (Fig 2 :36) | a frame prediction, not a verdict |
| D3 Modified-gravity frame | the modified force law, the external-field effect of a host | acceleration, predicted velocity dispersion | rotation-curve fits and the same spectral data, a different closure | a competing frame, independence partial |
| D4 Habitat and environment | neighbouring massive galaxies (NGC 1052, NGC 1035, NGC 1042), tidal field, ram pressure, group membership | stripped halo mass, tidal tails | deep imaging (tidal tails), group kinematics | an actor in contact with the residual |

**Partition or union.** The four carriers are not a list of separate causes. They overlap: distance changes the stellar mass and therefore the predicted halo (D1 touches D2); the external-field effect depends on distance and host (D1 touches D3); tidal stripping needs a neighbour in projection, whose reality depends on distance (D1 touches D4). This is a union compound with its own loop:

> Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

> Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Establish D1 first (distance and dispersion), because D2, D3 and D4 all consume it. Then sort the residual into its four kinds (Fig 5), then enumerate the habitat before importing an actor (Fig 11), then refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* A better simulation (D2) cannot rescue a wrong distance (D1). Winning arguments at the top of a broken chain arrive at a step that cannot use them.

**Strain test applied before concluding.** An early reading wanted Fig 13's cross-kind composition rule to decide the physics of DF2. That was a strain: Fig 13 governs the six-term substrate, not the mass budget of a galaxy. Re-census: no figure line governs the physical cause. That is recorded as F7 and as `gap`, not smoothed into an answer.

## 3. Findings, each with a verbatim figure line and a refuter

### F1 - The residual is per galaxy, never an average (triangulated)

> Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

> Fig 1 :28 "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."

**Reading.** DF2 and DF4 must get separate verdicts. The 2024 Gemini study found tidal tails in DF4 and none in DF2 (Golini, Montes et al., A&A 684 A99 2024, arXiv:2402.04304). Averaging them into "UDGs lack dark matter" would have hidden exactly the difference that matters.

**Refuter.** Show that the per-slot residual can be replaced by a population mean without losing the signal that directs the next observation, or exhibit a live slot whose positive mass disappears into the aggregate. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 1 :19 and :28, two low-overlap statements in the same figure).

### F2 - The anchor is an independent measurement, not agreement (triangulated)

> Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

> Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

**Reading.** The distance is the contested anchor here. Trujillo et al. (MNRAS 2019, arXiv:1806.10141) argued that five independent indicators give about 13 Mpc, so *"they consistently indicate a much shorter distance (13 Mpc) than previously indicated (20 Mpc)"*, which makes DF2 an ordinary dwarf with room for dark matter. van Dokkum et al. (ApJL 2018, doi:10.3847/2041-8213/aada4d) defended 19-20 Mpc with a megamaser-TRGB-SBF ladder, reporting a result *"consistent with our previous measurement and with the distance to the elliptical galaxy NGC 1052."* Both are attempts to pin the anchor. Which one survives is a ground question, not a vote.

**Refuter.** Show that a proposed distance indicator moves when the theorist's preference changes, that is, that it is a frame rather than an anchor. Or show an independent mass tracer that changes the kinematics verdict without touching the distance. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 2 :29 anchor test plus Fig 2 :35 triangulation rule).

### F3 - Sort the gap before minting a mechanism (triangulated)

> Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"

> Fig 5 :14 "mechanism broken: a new term is owed"

**Reading.** The distance-error explanation is literally the mis-attached bin: the prediction was right, a parameter was wrong. Tidal stripping or modified gravity is the mechanism-broken bin: something new is owed, either an actor from the habitat or a different force law. Fig 5 :33 adds *"The three guards are a product, not a checklist."* You cannot mint a new mechanism while the support guard (a real residual in a live slot) is still unset by a distance refit.

**Refuter.** Show that the gap is held (*"held: the model was right, nothing owed"*, Fig 5 :11), or that a mis-attached gap was treated as mechanism-broken and a new actor was minted to explain a distance error. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 5 four-kind sort plus Fig 5 product-of-guards rule).

### F4 - Enumerate the habitat before importing an actor (triangulated)

> Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

> Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

**Reading.** Neighbouring massive galaxies, the tidal field, ram pressure and group membership are actors in contact with DF2 and DF4. Ogiya (MNRAS Letters 2018, arXiv:1804.06421) showed by N-body simulation that *"tidal stripping by NGC 1052 can play an important role in forming a DM-deficient UDG"* on a cored halo and a radial orbit, and predicted such objects are rare and *"not present in the field."* Montes et al. (ApJ 2020, arXiv:2010.09719) detected tidal tails around DF4 from interaction with NGC 1035. The ESA/Hubble release (2020, heic2019) states that *"the gravity forces of the neighbouring galaxy NGC 1035 stripped the dark matter from NGC 1052-DF4 and are now tearing the galaxy apart."* These are imports from the habitat, and the source gate is the tail detection, not the simulation's plausibility.

**Refuter.** Show that the putative neighbour is not in contact (a foreground or background projection at a different distance), or exhibit a genuinely isolated field galaxy with the same deficit, which breaks the habitat import. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 11 :43 and :44 plus the world tail detections).

### F5 - Contact cascades; first-order enumeration is not enough (triangulated)

> Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades."

> Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

**Reading.** The residual that mattered for DF4 was three steps out: neighbour, tidal field, stripped halo, observed dispersion. A neighbour is a benefactor or a malfactor only relative to a residual, never by kind. Ogiya's 2018 result required the orbit and halo core to be in the tail of distributions; the 2021 follow-up (Jackson et al., arXiv:2111.12104) found the remnant can match DF2 on a not-particularly-extreme orbit, so which actor carries the residual is a live question, not a settled valence.

**Refuter.** Show that enumerating direct contact alone accounts for the residual, or that the same actor has a fixed valence independent of which residual is tracked. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 11 :52 cascade and :49 relative valence).

### F6 - Independence is partial, so agreement is weak corroboration (single-source for structure, with a named strain)

> Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."

> Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**Reading.** Lambda-CDM simulations (D2) and MOND (D3) are different closures, so in principle they can triangulate. Famaey, McGaugh and Milgrom (MNRAS 2018, doi:10.1093/mnras/sty1884) showed that *"the correct prediction of MOND depends on both the internal field of the dwarf and the external field caused by its proximity to the giant elliptical NGC1052"*, giving about 13.4 km/s, close to the observed value. But both frames consume the same distance and the same globular-cluster velocities. Their agreement is partly one evidence pool talking.

> Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."

Only the record of where each prediction came from separates them. **Refuter.** Show that the two frames use genuinely independent measurements (a distance from one, a dispersion from another), in which case the corroboration rises; or show they share the same input data, in which case it falls to one witness. **Status: single-source** for the independence rule (Fig 2), with the shared-input strain named and not hidden.

### F7 - The figures return a shape, not a physical cause (gap)

> Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

> Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

**Reading.** No figure line derives tidal stripping, a distance error, MOND, or a collision. The six-term set says nothing about halo mass budgets. After a genuine re-census the figure contribution is the shape of the inquiry (F1 to F6, F8 to F10), not the name of the mechanism. That is a `gap`, and it is a verdict rather than a silence.

**Refuter.** Derive a specific physical mechanism from the six-term set with no world import and no measurement, and show the derivation composes under Fig 13. Until that is done, the mechanism question is left to the ground. **Status: gap, canonical limit**, triangulated across Fig 9 :32 and the canonical figures README (opened 2026-11-23), which says the framework reports *"a shape, not a name."*

### F8 - Instruments are extended from worse instruments, not designed from descriptions (triangulated)

> Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

**Reading.** The tail claim for DF4 came from progressively deeper imaging: shallow surveys showed nothing, IAC80 showed an excess, Gemini and Hubble resolved the tails (Golini et al. 2024; ESA/Hubble heic2019 2020). That is a worse-instrument-to-better-instrument chain, exactly the figure's rule. DF4's tail depth is about 30.6 mag/arcsec^2, and the 2024 data reached about 30.9 mag/arcsec^2 for DF2 with no detection.

**Refuter.** Point to an instrument for the residual that was specified from a description alone and still returned something at first light, with no prior reading to extend from. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 6 :32 plus the world imaging sequence).

### F9 - A null result is not proof of absence (triangulated)

> Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

> Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

**Reading.** The 2024 result *"see no evidence for tidal effects in NGC1052-DF2"* down to a limiting surface brightness. That constrains ongoing or very recent stripping; it does not prove no past interaction, and the authors say so: other possibilities such as an incorrect distance or a rotating system could alleviate the problem. Treating the null as a proof would be the figure's named error.

**Refuter.** Show that the search covered a sealed frame, that is, that the instrument could in principle have detected every possible history of the residual, not just tails above a surface-brightness limit. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44).

### F10 - Agreement and prestige cannot settle it (triangulated)

> Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."

> Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**Reading.** A list of researchers selected because they already accept one explanation is a room, not an anchor. The outreach artifacts in this folder therefore ask for a failure, never an endorsement, and keep the residual and refuter in front of the links.

**Refuter.** Show that a consensus of frames sharing a pool added evidence rather than agreement, or that an anchor moved because of a vote. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 7 :35 and Fig 2 :42, plus the *Prestige Is Not Action* passage in the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim in this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Anchor reversal.** An independent distance ladder or an independent mass tracer that does not move with theory preference settles the residual to held (Fig 5 :11), so there is no live slot to explain. Specifically: a distance near 13 Mpc making DF2 ordinary, or a near-20 Mpc distance with a stellar-based dispersion that leaves room for a halo, either dissolves the original deficit as a mis-attached gap.
2. **Habitat failure.** A galaxy with a genuine deficit is found in the field with no neighbour in contact, breaking the habitat import and Ogiya's *"not present in the field"* prediction.
3. **Frame falsification.** MOND with the external-field effect is shown to fail at low overlap with the standard frame, or the standard abundance-matching prediction is shown to succeed once the anchor is fixed. Both would remove a competing frame from the census.
4. **Direct derivation.** A physical mechanism is derived from the six-term set with no import and no measurement, closing F7's gap.
5. **Per-slot collapse.** Averaging across galaxies is shown to preserve the directional signal, breaking F1.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed, and the earlier reading is kept visible in the retraction log. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1 to F3, the shape and the anchor test. The physical mechanism is a gap and must be stated as such (F7).
2. Keep DF2 and DF4 separate. The 2024 deep imaging makes them different slots with different verdicts (F1).
3. Sort each slot into mis-attached (distance or dispersion refit) before minting a stripping or modified-gravity mechanism (F3).
4. Enumerate the habitat and demand a tail detection as the import's source gate before naming a neighbour (F4, F8).
5. Treat a null tail detection as a constraint, not a proof (F9).
6. Do not pool the two theory frames' agreement. Check their independence explicitly (F6).
