## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when a galaxy's inferred dynamical mass is less than a prediction frame expects from its luminous mass and distance. Option A: name a physical cause (tidal stripping, a distance error, modified gravity, a collision). Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and mark the specific cause a `gap` until a discriminator settles it. What each leads to: A presents a mechanism the figures do not derive and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, a pinned anchor requirement, written refuters, and an open slot that names no cause.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Predicted mass minus measured mass for one galaxy. Arity: one slot per galaxy with several channels touching it (kinematics, distance indicator, environment). Symmetry: none, it is a directed comparison of prediction against observation. Scale: one galaxy stratum, with the group as the containing habitat enumerated outwards. The residual is per object, never an aggregate. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The four candidate carriers form a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two or more conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, measurement channel.** Actors: HST, Keck, Gemini, globular clusters, stars, SBF/TRGB/megamaser distance indicators, dispersion estimators. Currency: velocity dispersion, distance modulus, luminosity. Anchor: an independent measurement that does not move with theory preference. This is the live link. Question type: a refit before any mint.
- **Domain 2, standard prediction frame.** Actors: dark matter halos, N-body simulations, abundance matching, the stellar-mass-halo-mass relation. Currency: predicted halo mass. Anchor: simulation plus cosmological parameters, legitimate because the question is constitutively about them (Fig 2 :36). Question type: a frame prediction that defines the "expected" value.
- **Domain 3, modified-gravity frame.** Actors: the modified force law, the external field effect of a host. Currency: acceleration and predicted velocity dispersion. Anchor: rotation-curve fits and the same spectral data, a different closure. Question type: a competing frame whose independence is partial because it consumes the same distance and dispersion.
- **Domain 4, habitat and environment.** Actors: neighbouring massive galaxies (NGC 1052, NGC 1035, NGC 1042), tidal field, ram pressure, group membership. Currency: stripped halo mass, tidal tails. Anchor: deep imaging and group kinematics. Question type: an actor in contact with the residual, to be enumerated before import.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle D1 first, because D2, D3 and D4 all consume it. Then sort the gap into held / new / mis-attached / mechanism-broken (Fig 5), then enumerate the habitat (Fig 11), then refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Optimising a link above a broken one cannot rescue it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the physical cause is a `gap`. Keep the residual per galaxy (DF2 and DF4 are separate slots). Pin the anchor before choosing a frame, because distance and tracer choice swap the verdict. Sort the mis-attached gap before minting a stripping or modified-gravity mechanism. Enumerate the habitat and require a tail detection as the import's source gate. Treat the null tail search around DF2 as a depth-limited constraint. Count the standard and MOND frames as one witness where they share inputs. The figures do not derive any specific cause, so naming one would present a frame contribution as a framework output. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `gap` at the physics.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, keeps claims per slot, and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism claims are withheld, so nothing physical has to be withdrawn. A revised distance, a deeper tail search, or a stellar-based dispersion supersedes a slot verdict without touching the shape. Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." A settled measurement becomes an atom for the next loop.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent distance ladder or stellar-based dispersion that does not move with theory preference would settle whether the residual is held or live; deep imaging covering the candidate disruptor with a stripped-mass budget would name an import's source; a field galaxy with a real deficit would break the habitat import; a MOND prediction at low overlap, or a standard prediction succeeding after the anchor is fixed, would change the frame verdict; a derivation of a physical mechanism from the six-term set with no import would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
