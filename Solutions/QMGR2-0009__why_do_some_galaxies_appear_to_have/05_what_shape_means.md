## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 (line 132 in the extract): *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form an answer must have, statable before the answer can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: per-slot residual, pinned anchor, sorted gap, enumerated habitat, null as constraint, mechanism withheld. "Galaxy X lost its halo to a radial encounter with neighbour Y" is a name. The shape does not include it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." For this question: describe the residual as predicted mass minus observed mass, not as "missing dark matter," because the latter already names a cause.
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: when the source cannot be measured directly, *"the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see"* is the honest form; here that means a tail at a stated surface-brightness limit, a distance from a stated ladder, a dispersion from a stated tracer.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* and by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For a `gap` verdict, the stop is the honest report that the discriminating instrument is missing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles it, verified only by what pays; a gate is not the ground. Here: a distance ladder that does not move with theory preference, a stellar-based dispersion, a tail at a measured depth.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here: a neighbour imported into the habitat only after a tail detection, not because a simulation can reproduce the mass profile.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the first: a color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can produce the same reading. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. For DF2 the analogue is: several mechanisms produce a low dispersion, and the discriminator is an instrument, not a preference.

## 4. Why this question is especially prone to naming too early

The phrase "less dark matter than expected" already embeds a frame and a cause. It assumes the expected value is right and that the missing quantity is dark matter. The figures require the residual to be described before the frame is chosen:

> Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

The word "appear" in the question is the honest part. It marks the residual as an observation under a projection. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A low dispersion is such a projection. Distance, tracer population, equilibrium, halo content and modified gravity are candidate sources of it.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular mechanism here?

**Answer: nothing in the figures.** A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the mechanism is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is F7's `gap`, and it is triangulated across Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README.

**Does the literature commit the error the README forbids?** Some readings do. A simulation that reproduces a mass profile shows that a mechanism is possible, not that it is the source; naming it as the cause from the profile alone is the metamerism error. The 2024 Gemini result is the better practice: it reports what was seen (tails in DF4, none in DF2 to a limit) and withholds the causal claim where the discriminator is absent.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with one mechanism is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be checked.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any mechanism and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction recorded

An earlier framing of this section treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any entity. That is too broad. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Applied here: the shape claim does not name a mechanism because no figure line derives one, not because naming mechanisms is categorically forbidden to a searcher. The ban is on presenting a chosen mechanism as the framework's output. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README plus Fig 9 :32).
