## Two things in the literature that bear directly on the shape claim

1. **The DF2 and DF4 results are not interchangeable.** The 2024 Gemini data confirm tails in DF4 but find none in DF2 to a deeper limit. Fig 1 :19 says the residual is indexed by slot, so the two galaxies must not be averaged into one statement. Cite Golini et al. 2024 for the split.
2. **The same reading has several possible sources.** The same low dispersion is read as no halo by van Dokkum 2018, as an ordinary dwarf at a shorter distance by Trujillo 2019, as consistent with MOND by Famaey et al. 2018 when the external field is included, and as a stripping remnant by Ogiya 2018 and Montes et al. 2020. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument *"cannot comment on the source."* What is owed is a discriminator (an independent distance, stellar kinematics, deeper imaging), not a faster argument among frames.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks F1 to F3 without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the figure text and the four papers. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3:** offer the method as a generator. Each user states their own residual, names their own anchor and writes their own refuter before running it. Report failures, not endorsements.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the physical mechanism is deliberately withheld (F7 gap). If a recipient demands a mechanism, that is the correct moment to say the method does not have one yet.
