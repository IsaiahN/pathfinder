# The Tether's answer on dark-matter-deficient galaxies

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the physical cause, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

For one galaxy, predicted dynamical mass from luminous mass and distance minus measured dynamical mass from kinematics is a live per-slot residual. DF2 was reported with a halo-to-stars ratio of order unity, *"a factor of at least 400 lower than expected"* (van Dokkum et al., Nature 2018). The question is which link carries that residual. The four carriers (measurement channel, standard prediction frame, modified-gravity frame, habitat actor) form a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-55). The compound has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape, per domain, never averaged

1. **Keep the slots separate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The 2024 Gemini imaging found tails in DF4 and none in DF2 to about 30.9 mag per square arcsecond. Those are two verdicts, not one population statement.
2. **Pin the anchor before choosing a frame.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Distance and the velocity tracer are the contested anchor. About 13 Mpc makes DF2 an ordinary dwarf with room for a halo; about 19 to 20 Mpc keeps the deficit real. Which wins is a ground question. Agreement among simulations is not an anchor.
3. **Sort the gap before minting.** Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent" versus Fig 5 :14 "mechanism broken: a new term is owed." A wrong distance is the first; a stripped halo or a modified force law is the second. Order matters: Formula step 2 owes the refusal of an unfounded mechanism before any mint.
4. **Enumerate the habitat.** Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Neighbours, tidal field and ram pressure are actors in contact with these galaxies. Tidal stripping becomes a candidate import only after a tail detection supplies the source gate.
5. **A null is a constraint, not a proof.** Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No tails in DF2 constrains ongoing stripping; it does not exclude a past interaction or a distance error.
6. **Two frames, partial independence.** The standard simulation frame and MOND-with-external-field are different closures and can in principle triangulate, but they consume the same distance and dispersion. Their agreement is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :31).
7. **No mechanism is named.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figures return the shape above and stop. The physical cause is a `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a verdict on which explanation is true. The figures do not derive tidal stripping, MOND, a distance error or a collision.
- Not a claim that DF2 and DF4 have the same cause. The 2024 imaging separates them.
- Not a claim that no tidal stripping occurred in DF2. A null tail search is bounded by its depth.
- Not a claim that the 13 Mpc distance is correct, nor that the 19 to 20 Mpc distance is correct. Both are frame-dependent readings awaiting a firmer anchor.
- Not a derivation from the six-term substrate. Fig 13 governs a different object here.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. The corresponding checks here:

- **Anchor test:** an independent distance ladder (TRGB, SBF, megamaser calibration) or a stellar-based dispersion that does not move with theory preference. This decides whether the residual is held or live.
- **Habitat test:** deep imaging at fainter limits around DF2, and imaging that covers NGC 1035 in the same pointing, to identify the disruptor and the stripped mass. Tails plus a mass budget would name the import's source.
- **Field test:** a genuinely isolated galaxy with a real deficit, which would break the habitat import and the *"not present in the field"* prediction.
- **Frame test:** a MOND prediction with the external field at low overlap with the standard prediction, or a standard prediction that succeeds once the anchor is fixed.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** anchor reversal, habitat failure, frame falsification, direct derivation from the primitives, per-slot collapse.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instrument that would discriminate among the candidate sources at the required depth does not yet exist in the record I read. This is underivable from the figures in principle, not merely unreachable at this budget: no amount of rearranging the six terms yields a halo mass budget. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity: two mechanisms giving the same low dispersion are not thereby the same mechanism.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell us which mechanism stripped or prevented dark matter in DF2 and DF4. It tells us what any answer must look like. The residual belongs to each galaxy separately, not to a population average; the live link is the measurement channel, chiefly distance and the velocity tracer, which must act as an independent anchor and not move with theoretical preference; the gap must be sorted into a refit (a wrong distance) before a new mechanism (stripping or modified gravity) is minted; and the habitat of neighbouring galaxies must be enumerated before any neighbour is named. A null tail detection in DF2 is a constraint, not a proof that no stripping ever happened, and the standard and MOND frames share enough input that their agreement counts as one witness. The physical cause stays open until a discriminator arrives. That is a verdict of `gap`, not a silence, and the refuters were written before the run.
