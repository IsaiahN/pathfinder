# WATSON answer, INS-0004

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Why do similar institutional designs produce different outcomes in different societies? Restated as an effect: why does R_S = |Gamma_S(b_S,a) - o'_S| differ persistently across society-slots when the written design D and the action a are the same? Option A: divergence is implementation noise around a portable design quality. Option B: divergence is structural, the design is a convention whose effect belongs to design-and-habitat, and the residual is a union of two conditions that must be kept separate. What each leads to: A produces a single "institutional quality" score that averages away the live slot signal; B produces a shape, five pre-registered refuters, and an honest gap where the discriminating instrument is missing.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale. Arity: at least four-place (design, society-slot, action, observation), indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the written form is exchangeable across societies, the outcome is not; the symmetry is broken by habitat, weighting, anchor and channel, which is the isomer condition. Scale: step (one official), cycle (one agency), level (one society), population (cross-society comparison), lineage (the history that made the belief), with a lossy membrane between scales.

| X2 culture, X3 geography and disease | inside D2 and D3 | absorbed | absorbed | absorbed, not separate domains |

Union, not partition: D1 and D2 meet at the enactment seam, D2 and D3 form the inherited-habitat compound, and D4 runs across all of them. Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Dependency order (Fig 3): perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry; describe the residual before choosing a frame; refuse before mint; settle before composing over a result.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :40 governs an improvement to the agent in isolation, while a successful import changes contact (Fig 6 :23) and is debited against independence. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any single-factor verdict does not. A written design is a convention whose effect is a property of design-and-habitat; same atoms under different bonds are isomers; identical architecture diverges because histories and weightings diverge; the residual is a union of a maintained channel and a local valence fit, never one score; a transplant substitutes a habitat. The specific instrument that discriminates the two conditions is a declared gap. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result, and the five pre-registered refuters are the route to reversing it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-factor story instead of a generator, which Fig 2 prices in "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that measures channel contact after a shock to the formal text, a matched pair showing the same formal role recruiting actors of different valence, a pair with identical formal atoms and identical ordered bonds that still diverges, any of the five pre-registered refuters, or a source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-society residuals are fully predicted by a single score. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
