# Expert verification packet - the Tether on INS-0004

**What this is:** a claim in a form a comparative institutional analyst can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement on a society pair is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Similar institutional designs produce different outcomes because the written design is an ingredient list and the bond is the unrecorded half. Two societies with the same formal rules are isomers under Fig 12: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. The effect is a property of design-and-habitat, so improving the text alone does not change contact; two systems with identical architecture diverge because their histories and weightings diverge. The residual is a union of two conditions kept separate: C1, a channel that stays connected to a ground that can refuse the rule, and C2, a valence and weighting fit with the actors the design actually recruits. A transplant substitutes one habitat for another and inherits actors nobody chose. The framework does not hold an instrument that discriminates a C1 failure from a C2 failure; that is a declared gap, not a hidden answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and notation-level; checkable by reading Figures 10 and 12

- **Statement:** a written design is a convention, not a ground, and the arrangement carries the information the text does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :18 - *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*; Fig 10 :19 - *"install what can be violated"*; Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- **Refuter:** show a written rule that constrains an outcome with no interpretation or enforcement channel and no ground contact, or show that the formal ingredient set alone predicts outcomes with no contribution from bonds. If either holds, C1 falls.

### C2 - institution-facing; checkable by a comparative analyst against matched cases

- **Statement:** two societies with the same formal rules but different ordered and threshold bonds are isomers, and actor valence is relative to the residual rather than carried by the actor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 - *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Operators table :30-33 defines sequence as *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*, with the test *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*
- **What to check:** in a matched pair of societies with the same statute, does the enforcement *order* (for example monitor-then-elect versus elect-then-monitor) or the sanction *threshold* differ? Does the same formal role recruit actors who behave differently when the residual changes?
- **Refuter:** identical formal rules plus demonstrably identical informal bonds, sequencing and enforcement, with a persistent outcome difference. That kills the isomer claim and leaves only the weaker functional-group reading (Fig 12 :124).

### C3 - the union and the channel; checkable against measurement practice

- **Statement:** the divergence is two conditions, never one score. The channel decays even though the ground does not, and a saturated metric is a lost channel, not a settled outcome.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** does the study report channel fidelity separately from valence fit, or does it collapse both into one "institutional quality" index? Does a country average preserve the live per-society signal out of sample?
- **Refuter:** one intervention restores both conditions with no categorically different residue, or a cross-society average predicts an individual society out of sample. Either means the union reading was wrong for that case.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking of designs, a best-practice list, or a verdict on any country.
- Not that culture, geography, disease or elite power alone explains the divergence. Each is absorbed into the habitat or the weighting.
- Not that agreement among experts settles the question. A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).
- Not that a famous adopter validates a reform. Prestige can fund an action but cannot settle it.
- Not that the framework names the missing instrument. It returns a shape and records the gap (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does a channel measure still return fresh readings after a shock to the formal text, or is it saturated (Fig 10 :26)?
2. Does the same formal role recruit actors of different valence when the residual changes (Fig 11 :49-51)?
3. Do two matched societies share their formal atoms while differing in ordered and threshold bonds (Fig 12 :117, Operators table :30)?
4. Do any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 fire?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :18-19, :24-26 and Fig 12 :5, :117, :124 - four short passages.
2. Check C2 against one matched country pair you know: are the formal rules the same and the bonds different?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a channel failure from a valence failure in that pair, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record the verdict before reading the framework.
