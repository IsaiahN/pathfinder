## 0. The question in effect terms

Source line 29 asks: "Why have some institutions encouraged open criticism while others discouraged challenges to established claims?"

Stated as an effect, not a topic: two observable institutional policies, and an outcome each leads to.

- **Encouragement**: the institution keeps a channel by which a claim it holds can be refused by a frame that ranges the claim against something that does not move when the institution changes its mind about it.
- **Discouragement**: the institution protects the standing of an established claim by making its own claim the reference others update on, so disagreement is absorbed before it reaches anything that could settle it.

The question asks what selects between them. Two options are possible and must be kept distinct: (A) the policy is a fixed institutional character, a trait of an academy or a culture; (B) the policy is a relation between the institution, its ground, its refusal seat, its critics and its habitat, so the same institution can encourage in one slot and discourage in another. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": **a shape naming the condition that keeps a refusal channel reachable**, not a name of a winning institution and not a moral ranking. If a figure line returns that shape, the answer is `specified`; if no line governs the historical "why this one", that part is a `gap`.

## 1. Residual, described by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Two policies, but not a two-item list. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." An institution can be open on method and closed on doctrine at the same time, so the policies are conditions that can co-occur, not opposite answers to one question.
- **Symmetry.** "Challenge" is ordered: a critic addresses a claim already held, so the operands are not interchangeable. "Criticism" as an operation is a refusal, which is a unary operator, not a symmetric bond. Fig 12 :112 "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." No figure line licenses treating criticism as `+` or `∥`.
- **Scale.** First order: a single claim and a single critic. Second order: an institution's standing across many claims, which Fig 10 :61 calls "standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." The second order is where discouragement becomes attractive, because protecting a held claim protects the standing built on it.
- **Slot indexing.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single verdict such as "institutions are open" or "institutions are closed" destroys the signal. Verdicts are kept per slot and per domain.

## 4. Findings, with verbatim lines and refuters

### F1: encouragement is triangulation, discouragement is coherent drift (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."
Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

An institution that encourages criticism keeps the thing that settles its claims outside the influence of its own claims. Critics range against that reference; the institution ranges against it separately. An institution that discourages challenges makes its own established claim do the work of the reference. By Fig 2 :34 the reference has become another frame, and the drift is coherent, which is why it looks like health. This is not a moral reading. It is a statement about which object occupies the anchor position.

Fig 1 :27 "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." An institution that publishes its own openness score and then uses that score to settle whether it is open is the named frame-internal failure.

**Refuter:** show a case where the reference genuinely does not update on the institution's opinion, criticism is freely expressed, and error correction is no better than in a case where the institution's own claim is the reference. That would break the claimed link between anchor position and channel use.

### F2: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."
Fig 10 :61 "standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."

The established claim starts as a convention, a shared rule for how members read the world. When discouraging a challenge protects the claim because of the standing already built on it, the convention decides an outcome. By Fig 10 :47 it has stopped being a convention. The institution's keeper is then a ground with a reputation to protect, which Fig 10 :93 disqualifies. The relevant interest is not having an outcome at all; everyone has that. It is having an interest in **which reading the ground returns**. That is the disqualifying one, and it must be declared rather than denied (Fig 10 :59).

**Refuter:** show a keeper of an established claim whose standing is unaffected by which reading the ground returns today, and whose refusal of a challenge is still explained by that standing. If such a keeper exists, the stake clause needs re-census.

### F3: without a refusal term, the institution can only accumulate (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 12 :112 "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."
Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"
Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"

Discouraging challenges is not merely a policy about manners. It removes the unary operator that lets a system say that something it holds is false. A vocabulary with only the seven bonds can say what a situation contains and what follows what, but it cannot carry the correction (Fig 12 :112, Operators table :58). It can still be corrected from outside, but it cannot record the correction as part of its own operation. An institution that has no internal form of "this established claim is refused" can add findings forever and never withdraw the standing of a claim.

This also fixes a strain before it becomes one: "criticism" is not a symmetric joining of two equal frames. It is a refusal aimed at a held term, so Fig 8's symmetric-bond discussion does not govern it. No re-census is owed on that point.

**Refuter:** a figure re-census that places criticism under a symmetric bond (`+`, `∥`, `≡`) rather than under negation, with the operators table's test showing that swapping operands changes nothing about the criticism. Until then the negation reading stands.

### F4: rooms that differ are where the ground votes (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."
Fig 7 :37 "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"
Fig 7 :38 "the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"
Fig 7 :39 "the ground's pressure on the room"
Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"

Encouragement is a chain with selection between rooms. A critic is a different room, picked against the residual the last room surfaced, and the handoff advances only if the bracket is faithful and the ground presses on the room. Discouragement is the mirror chain: same closure handed along, agreement compounds, evidence does not. This is why a large, harmonious community of specialists can be less corrective than one outsider with a different closure and a measurable residual.

Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." A critic pool drawn from institutions that share the same training, the same journals and the same funders is one room spelling itself many ways. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**Refuter:** show a chain of rooms with low overlap between successive rooms and real ground pressure in which agreement nonetheless compounds and evidence does not. That would falsify the two-factor claim as applied here.

### F5: independence is one quantity read twice, and selecting for agreement buys a convention (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 8 :47 "Independence is one quantity read twice."
Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"
Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

The same parameter that makes a critic useful also makes agreement with them uninformative, if the agreement comes from a shared pool. An institution that hand-picks critics likely to agree purchases a convention, not corroboration. Fig 8 :51 supplies the discriminate: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in their contents, so only the record of where each came from separates them. That is why this audit records, for each world source, which pool it draws from, and why the second seat in `11_audit.md` is declared to share the primary's pool.

**Refuter:** show two frames with demonstrably zero shared evidence whose agreement still carries no information about the world, with the overlap measured rather than asserted.

### F6: the same institutional actor changes valence with the residual (`specified`, triangulated at shape level)

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

A patron, a journal, a society or a state can fund the instrument that settles a claim in one case and suppress the same kind of claim in another. Asking "are patrons good for criticism" is the standing-list error Fig 11 :51 refuses. The residual decides valence. This is why the world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are read as cases with different residuals, not as votes for or against institutions in general.

Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Adding an internal review committee that never reaches a frame outside the institution's closure changes no contact and therefore changes no capability.

**Refuter:** identify an actor whose valence toward criticism is invariant across two residuals with different grounds, with both residuals described in effect terms.

### F7: the question is a union compound, not a partition (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

The four conditions in F1 to F4 can be present or absent independently. An academy may have a non-updating reference (condition 1) and no refusal seat (condition 2). A journal may have both and still draw its reviewers from one evidence pool (condition 3). A society may satisfy all three and still be captured by a patron whose interest tracks the reading (condition 4). One lever, such as "publish your peer review", clears only the region where one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts are therefore never averaged into an "openness index".

**Refuter:** exhibit a single variable whose value determines all four conditions across a set of cases where the conditions otherwise vary independently.

### F8: suppression is a channel failure, not a change in what settles claims (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"
Fig 10 :27 "the output is not a verdict, it is"
Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Discouraging challenges does not abolish the ground. It degrades the channel to it: dissent becomes a dropped transient, the metrics saturate on internal agreement, and readings are pooled until no single critic can be heard. The output is then not a verdict about the claim but a report that contact has been lost. Fig 6 :23 adds that no frame escapes its own closure on its own, so the external critic is not decoration; it is the contact that moves the wall.

**Refuter:** show an institution whose channel to a non-updating ground is intact by measurement and whose correction rate still collapses when criticism is silenced, with the collapse explained by something other than the four conditions.

### F9: no figure line returns the historical cause (`gap` at figure level, `general` shape only)

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

A genuine census was run over all thirteen figures, the operators and symbols tables, the formula and the canonical metalaws note. No line names which historical cause selected which policy, nor how much ideology, patronage, professional interest or disciplinary structure contributed in a given case. The figures return the shape: a condition on anchor, refusal seat, room independence and habitat contact. They do not return the name of a cause. The honest report is `gap` for the historical attribution, of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle: a comparative historical instrument could in principle measure it, and none was run here. `06_isomers_and_order.md` explains why the gap is not filled by a list of famous cases.

**Refuter:** exhibit a verbatim figure line that assigns a specific historical policy to a specific cause, with the line quoted from `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. If one is found, this `gap` is withdrawn and the line governs D4.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any world source was opened for corroboration. Per Fig 5 :65 "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM", the sought-for shape was bound in section 0 so that it can accrue a residual like anything else.

The four-condition separator is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An institution with a demonstrably non-updating reference and an empowered refusal seat still suppresses criticism for a cause outside the four conditions, with that cause named and measured.
2. An institution with no independent critic ranges claims against a ground and corrects error at the same rate as one with an open channel, measured on the same residual.
3. A re-census of the operators table places criticism under a symmetric bond rather than under negation, so Fig 12 :112 and Operators table :58 do not govern it.
4. A comparative historical instrument assigns one cause to the policy across cases where the separator predicts different domains, with the assignment grounded in records rather than in prestige.

If any fires, the separator is withdrawn or re-censused. Until one is run, no part of the historical attribution may be labelled `grounded`.
