## The question restated as an effect

Something is being decided: two institutional policies toward criticism. Encourage means keep a channel by which a held claim can be refused by a frame that ranges against a ground which does not update on what the institution thinks. Discourage means protect the standing of an established claim by making the institution's own claim the reference others update on. An answer is wanted in the form of a **shape**: the condition that keeps the refusal channel reachable, not a list of historical institutions and not a moral verdict. written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction".

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that open criticism is good.
- No bulk-send plan.

Figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the agreement that would disqualify the verdict.
2. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" A list chosen to agree is one frame wearing many names.
3. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework's interior.
