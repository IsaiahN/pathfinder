# Expert verification packet, the Tether on institutional criticism

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian of science or a science-studies scholar can check. Mode, derivation and refuters come before the conclusion, so the reader knows what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on D1 to D3, `general` and `gap` on D4. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Encouragement and discouragement of criticism are not opposite values of an institutional character. They are outcomes of four conditions that can co-occur, so verdicts stay per slot and per domain. Criticism is a channel to ground only when (1) the reference that settles a claim does not change its answer because the institution thinks about it, (2) a maintained seat can refuse a claim the institution already holds, with its interest declared, (3) the critic comes from a closure that differs from the institution's rather than from a hand-picked agreeable set, and (4) the habitat is enumerated rather than composed into a standing list of good or bad actors. When condition 1 fails, the institution's own claim takes the anchor position and the drift is coherent. When condition 2 fails, the vocabulary can accumulate but cannot carry the correction. When condition 3 fails, agreement is the pool talking. When condition 4 is skipped, the same actor is praised in one case and condemned in another with no way to say why. No figure line returns which historical cause selected the policy in a given case; that attribution is a `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** a non-updating reference is required for criticism to count as a channel. Protecting a held claim by protecting the standing built on it turns a convention into an outcome and disqualifies its keeper.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."
- **Refuter:** show a case where the reference demonstrably does not update on the institution's opinion, criticism is expressed, and error correction is no better than in a case where the institution's own claim is the reference.

### C2, framework-internal plus operator check, checkable by reading Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** criticism is a negation, not a bond. A vocabulary without it can only accumulate. Over a fixed atom set, an encouraging and a discouraging institution are isomers: same atoms, different bond at the refusal slot (Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour.").
- **Sources, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate" Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"
- **Refuter:** a re-census placing criticism under a symmetric bond, with the operators table's swap test showing that operand order does not matter; or a case where the negation bond is present and the claim is still unrefusable.

### C3, frame-facing, checkable by a historian against cases

- **Statement:** correction advances by selection between differing rooms under ground pressure, and the same independence parameter that makes a critic useful makes agreement with that critic worthless when the evidence pool is shared.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 8 :47 "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
- **What to check:** in a case you know well, was the critic pool drawn from a closure that differed from the institution's, and was there anything non-updating the criticism was ranged against? Was the correction rate measured, or was the volume of criticism counted as a proxy?
- **Refuter:** a chain with low overlap between rooms and real ground pressure in which agreement still compounds and evidence does not; or an institution with no independent critic that corrects at the same measured rate.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that open criticism is always right, nor that every dissenter is a ground.
- Not that discouraging criticism abolishes the ground. The channel decays; the ground does not (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does").
- Not that a hand-picked panel of agreeable critics is a check. It is a convention (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a name for the historical cause. That is a `gap`, reported rather than filled.
- Not `grounded`. No comparative instrument was run, and no historian's verdict was pooled as corroboration.
- Not a claim that any particular academy, society or journal occupies a given cell. Naming one is a binding with provenance, not a figure-derived selection.

## 5. What would settle it

A comparative instrument, not a further argument. Measure on the same residual: whether the reference changes with the institution's opinion; whether the refusal seat survives its own use across more than one claim; the evidence overlap between critic pool and institution; and the error-correction rate rather than the volume of criticism. Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." The author's interest in the separator surviving is declared here and handled by the refuters written before the conclusion.
