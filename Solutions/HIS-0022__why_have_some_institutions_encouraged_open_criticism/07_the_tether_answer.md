# The Tether's answer on institutional criticism

**Mode: `specified` on the four-domain separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical attribution. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observed facts are two policies. Some institutions kept a channel by which their own claims could be refused by outsiders. Others discouraged exactly that. The question asks what selects between them, not which is morally preferable. "Criticism" is not a symmetric exchange here: it is a refusal aimed at a claim an institution already holds, so it is a unary operator (Fig 12 :112 "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."). The residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."), so an institution can be open on method and closed on doctrine.

## 2. The shape

1. **Encouragement is a relation to a non-updating reference.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Critics range against that reference separately; the institution ranges against it separately. That is triangulation.
2. **Discouragement makes the institution's own claim the reference.** Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." It looks like harmony because it is coherent drift.
3. **A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." The disqualifying interest is not having an outcome but having an interest in which reading the ground returns (Fig 10 :59).
4. **The operator that is missing is negation.** Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". An institution with no internal form of "this claim is refused" can add findings forever and never withdraw the standing of a claim.
5. **The critic must come from a differing room.** Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
6. **Suppression is a channel failure, not the abolition of ground.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings (Fig 10 :26) are how a channel dies while looking healthy.
7. **The habitat is enumerated and never composed into a character.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The same patron can fund the instrument in one case and suppress the challenge in another.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that open criticism is always right, nor that every dissenter is a ground.
- Not that discouraging criticism eliminates the ground. It degrades the channel to it.
- Not that a hand-picked panel of agreeable critics is a check. It is a convention (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- Not a name for the cause in any particular institution. That is the `gap`.
- Not a verdict of `grounded` anywhere. No comparative instrument was run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating comparative instrument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the instrument would measure, on the same residual:

1. whether the reference changes its answer when the institution changes its mind;
2. whether a refusal seat exists and survives its own use across more than one claim;
3. the overlap between the critic pool's evidence and the institution's evidence;
4. the error-correction rate, not the volume of published criticism.

Standing refuters from `01` section 5: a case outside the four conditions with the same channel behaviour; an institution with no independent critic that corrects at the same rate; a re-census putting criticism under a symmetric bond; a historical instrument assigning one cause across cases where the separator predicts different domains.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The figures were searched for a historical-cause line and none was found. SEARCHED was asked first; INWARD sharpened the four conditions; OUTWARD imported history of science and human cognition sources, which corroborate the mechanism shape but do not return a universal cause. The three routes were kept distinct. Another frame's minimum vocabulary need not be these four conditions, and declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank institutions as open or closed. It says the policy tracks a relation, not a character: criticism is a channel to ground only while the thing that settles a claim does not change its answer when the institution thinks about it, while someone is seated who can refuse the institution's own held claim, while the critic comes from a room that differs from the institution's, and while the habitat is read rather than composed into a verdict about institutional character. When the institution's own claim takes the anchor position, agreement is coherent drift that nobody notices, a convention becomes an outcome, and the vocabulary loses its negation, so it can accumulate but not correct. The framework returns that shape, with four failure conditions written in advance. It does not return which patron, ruler or discipline caused a given academy to choose one policy. That remains a gap until a comparative instrument, not a list of famous cases, settles it.
