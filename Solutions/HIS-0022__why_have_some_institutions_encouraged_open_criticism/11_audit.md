## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** Arity two policies, but a union rather than a partition, because the same institution can encourage in one slot and discourage in another. Symmetry: a challenge is ordered, since a critic addresses a claim already held; the critical act itself is a refusal, so unary, not a symmetric bond. Scale: first order for a claim and a critic, second order for an institution's standing across claims. Slot indexed, not averaged.
- **D1, epistemic frame.** Actors: claims, methods, instruments. Currency: settlement versus internal coherence. Anchor: a reference that does not move when the frame thinks. Partition boundary: what may settle a claim.
- **D2, checking arrangement.** Actors: refuter seat, keeper, verifier. Currency: verdict versus agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition boundary: is the negation operator available and can its seat survive reuse.
- **D3, room chain.** Actors: successive rooms and the next room selected against a surfaced residual. Currency: independent variation per handoff. Anchor: ground pressure on the room. Partition boundary: mirror chain versus selection between rooms.
- **D4, historical habitat.** Actors: patrons, rulers, journals, societies. Currency: resources and standing. Anchor: none nameable for the causal claim. Union of overlapping conditions.

Order by dependency: check the anchor first, refuse before mint, then build the channel, then settle at the ground. Effort at D4 cannot rescue a break at D1.
