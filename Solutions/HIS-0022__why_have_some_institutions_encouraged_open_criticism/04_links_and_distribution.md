## 3. Which world sources bear, and how much

- **Lowest overlap, genuinely different fields:** red team (engineering and security), groupthink (human cognition), critical rationalism (philosophy of science), Shapin and Schaffer (history of science). These support the mechanism shape from different evidence pools. Shapin and Schaffer's own reception section is used as a check on taking any single historical account as settled.
- **One broad pool:** Merton 1942, the Mertonian-norms page, Mitroff's counter-norm and Popper are all sociology or philosophy of science. They count as one witness for "criticism is an institutional norm", not four.
- **Cases, not votes:** Lysenkoism and the Royal Society motto are opposite poles of the same historical axis. They are read as cases with different residuals (Fig 11 :49), not as evidence that institutions in general are open or closed.
- **Framework essays:** all by the framework author, maximal overlap with the figures, zero independent witness weight. They are used only for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05`.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying" is the reason the pools are declared rather than hidden.

## 4. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded rules for offering this material:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Stage 1 carries the four conditions and the refuter. The framework interior goes only after the reader has recorded a verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what the reader would expect to see in an institution with a real refusal channel before naming any historical case.
- **Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** Do not pool later agreement as corroboration. One-to-one contact only.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Stage 3 offers the formula as a method to reuse, not as a conclusion to accept.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** The cost here is a wrong historical attribution, so the venues in `03` are chosen for their ability to refute, not for their likely agreement.

## 5. The two-stage offer, repeated for convenience

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the four conditions and the pre-registered refuter from `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record the verdict.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the figure text and the two framework links. State that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes a refuter and states a mode before running it.

No request for trust, no request for agreement, no bulk send, no receptiveness rank. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
