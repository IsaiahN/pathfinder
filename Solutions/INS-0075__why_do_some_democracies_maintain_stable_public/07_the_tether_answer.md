# The Tether's answer on INS-0075

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints. Not `grounded`.** This is
a shape and a route, not an empirical result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question compares two outcomes under severe disagreement: stable public support with an intact
capacity to remove a government by registered means, versus vulnerability to breakdown. The
residual is not the amplitude of disagreement. It is the persistent, per-slot gap between a
polity's prediction that support continues and what its ground returns, while its internal frames
disagree. Fig 9 :4-5 says a disagreement that does not shrink with effort, where each rule works on
a different subset, is a compound to be split rather than one question to be searched. So the
residual is a **union compound**, not one national malady.

## 2. The shape

1. **An anchor that does not update.** Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :29-30:
   *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any
   frame thinks."* When the arbiter updates on the claims of the frames it arbitrates, Fig 2 :34
   says the anchor becomes another frame and *"everything drifts together, and the drift is
   coherent, which is why nobody notices."* An approval poll is a reading of this anchor, not the
   anchor itself.
2. **Frames that are independent, not merely numerous.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two
   frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 10 :80: *"And divergence regenerates
   unevenly. Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained; frames
   whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated, because no amount of use makes them
   less alike and replacement is the only refresh."* Agreement from one evidence pool is the pool
   talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Selection between rooms.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict.
   Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long
   room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* A polity
   that hands the same closure along looks stable while producing motion without progress.
4. **A readable channel, per slot.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*;
   Fig 10 :25: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 1 :19:
   *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A national average near zero can
   coexist with one live slot (Fig 1 :13). Failure of the channel reports *"you have lost touch"*,
   not a measurement of zero support (Fig 10 :27-28).
5. **A working refusal.** Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary
   without it can only accumulate."* A polity must be able to say that a held claim is false, not
   merely that an operand is absent. And a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a
   convention (Fig 10 :47).

These five constraints are the shape. The institution that fills each role is **not** derived. The
framework returns a shape, not a name; a specific institution is an import from political science
with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that disagreement causes breakdown. Agreement within a room is variation, not a verdict
  (Fig 7 :35). The relation to the ground is the variable.
- Not that any named country is stable or doomed. No political ground reading was taken.
- Not that one institution *is* the anchor. Mutual toleration, forbearance, judicial independence
  and disaggregated indicators are world claims, held as secondary corroboration only.
- Not that more civil society, more parties, more polls or more institutions help by count.
  Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), valence is relative to the residual
  (Fig 11 :49), and independence is renewed by separate experience, not by headcount (Fig 10 :80).
- Not that isolation removes a cleavage. *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is
  substitution of one habitat for another"* (Fig 11 :66).
- Not `grounded`. No election, survey, court record or protest was read as ground for this
  derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*:
an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here
the corresponding questions are:

- **Anchor movement test:** does the legitimacy arbiter change its answer when a frame pushes on it,
  with the push and the answer both recorded by someone who cannot be moved by either? This is
  R1 in `01` section 5.
- **Channel test:** are elections, measurement and courts independently reachable per slot, and do
  they register the ground without updating on the governing frame? A 2011 study of 163 countries
  reported that established independent judiciaries prevent drift while new courts are associated
  with collapse; that is a world claim, to be checked, not a figure line.
- **Room-difference test:** how much does the next governing arrangement differ from the last, and
  was it selected against the residual the last one surfaced?
- **Overlap test:** how much do the opposing frames draw on the same evidence? Effective sample size
  under correlation is the standard arithmetic (Fig 2 :44), and Fig 8 :48 makes it the surplus test.
- **Refusal test:** can a failed claim actually be refused, and does the refusal survive the next
  cycle, or is it absorbed?

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5: R1, R2 and R3 as written there. Any one firing withholds
the shape claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of
absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed
room."* The six-term substrate is a physical composition law and returns no verdict for this
question; that is a recorded `gap`, not a hidden failure (Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict
here, not a silence"* without its trailing comma). The answer stops at the limit of the available
instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*:
*"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a stable democracy or a single institution that saves one. It constrains
> the shape any answer must have. Under severe disagreement, stable support requires an anchor that
> does not change its answer because a party claims it should, frames that draw on different
> evidence, a real selection between successive governing rooms against the problem the last one
> left, a channel that still registers public judgment group by group, and a working way to refuse a
> failed claim. When the arbiter becomes another frame, when the same closure is handed along, or
> when the channel decays while the ground stays put, the system loses touch and breakdown becomes
> possible; how much people disagree is not by itself the cause. The framework cannot name the
> institutions that fill those roles, so the slots stay open. What it offers is the shape, three
> refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question stays open
> until a ground reading settles it.
