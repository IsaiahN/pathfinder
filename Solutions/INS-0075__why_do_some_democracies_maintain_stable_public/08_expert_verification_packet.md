**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints, not `grounded`.** No

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Severe disagreement does not by itself explain why some democracies keep stable support and others
become vulnerable to breakdown. The figure-derived shape requires five things at once: an anchor
that does not change its answer because a frame claims otherwise; frames with low evidence overlap;
a real selection between successive governing rooms against the problem the last one surfaced; a
channel that registers the ground group by group; and a working operator for refusal. When the
arbiter becomes another frame, when the same closure is handed along, or when the channel decays
while the ground stays put, contact is lost and breakdown becomes possible. The framework does not
name which institution fills any of those roles. It offers the shape, three refuters written in
advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 7

- **Statement:** a stability answer that locates the cause in disagreement amplitude is a category
  error. The anchor must not update on frames, and the next room must differ from the last and be
  selected against the surfaced residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :34 *"If it does, panel 3 turns
  into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is
  coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a
  verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one
  long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
- **Refuter:** R1 in `01` section 5. Show a polity sustaining stable, independently measured support
  and an intact removal capacity while its legitimacy arbiter demonstrably updates on frames over a
  period long enough to exclude a transient. Also: show stability maintained with low between-room
  difference and no selection step.

### C2: political-science-facing; checkable against the measurement literature

- **Statement:** the failure is in the channel, not in the ground, and the residual is per group. A
  national average can hide a live signal in one group, and a captured information environment can
  produce a stable reading from a failing source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :25
  *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 1 :19 *"The
  residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how
  a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** does the V-Dem style multidimensional disaggregation actually resolve a signal
  that a single national index hides? Is there an instrument that discriminates genuine support from
  a captured information environment? The backsliding page reports a 2011 study of 163 countries in
  which established independent judiciaries prevent drift while new courts are associated with
  collapse; does that hold as an anchor test, not merely a correlation?
- **Refuter:** R2 in `01` section 5. Show breakdown with a demonstrably reachable channel:
  independent elections, free per-group measurement, courts that do not update on the governing
  frame, and low-overlap opposing frames. Also: show that a single aggregate index predicts the
  outcome as well as the per-group reading.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, with a world-facing consequence

- **Statement:** a polity needs a separate negation operator to refuse a failed claim, and two
  polities with the same formal constitution but different unwritten norms are Fig 12 isomers, not
  variants of one thing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary
  without it can only accumulate"*; Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different
  substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 12 :14
  *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"*; Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides
  an outcome has stopped being one"*
- **What to check:** are mutual toleration and forbearance relations (bonds) rather than extra
  offices (atoms)? Is there a polity that refuses failed claims only by absorbing them into existing
  terms? Does the unwritten relation carry outcome information that the written text does not?
- **Refuter:** show a polity sustaining refusal over time with no separate negation path, or show
  that two polities with the same formal atoms but different norms have no systematic outcome
  difference after a ground reading controls for channel reachability. That kills the isomer claim
  and leaves only the functional-group claim.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, a reading that
cannot comment on its source demonstrates its own inadequacy. The checks are:

1. **Anchor movement:** does the arbiter change its answer when frames push, recorded by someone
   who cannot be moved by either?
2. **Channel reachability:** are elections, measurement and courts independent per slot, and do they
   register the ground without updating on the governing frame?
3. **Room difference:** how much does the next arrangement differ from the last, and was it selected
   against the surfaced residual?
4. **Overlap:** how much do opposing frames draw on the same evidence (Fig 2 :44, Fig 8 :48)?
5. **Refusal:** can a failed claim be refused, and does the refusal survive the next cycle?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29-34, Fig 7 :35-36 and Fig 10 :24-25. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against one dataset you know well: does the per-group signal differ from the national
   average in a way the average hides?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish genuine support from a captured information

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
