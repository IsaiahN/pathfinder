## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions, using

anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

  and vulnerability to breakdown under severe disagreement. Arity: at least two conditions, so a
  **union compound** until a common mechanism is shown (Fig 9 :4-5). Symmetry: disagreement is
  relational among frames; agreement among correlated frames disqualifies (Fig 2 :40). Scale: first
  order inside each frame, second order at the polity level where frames are selected.
- **Domain 1, alignment and legitimacy.** Actors: public as ground, parties, courts, media,
  agencies as frames. Currency: legitimacy and residual. Anchor: something that settles who
  governs and does not update on frames. Question type: partition, anchors versus frames.
- **Domain 2, disagreement topology.** Actors: claims, cleavages, conditions. Currency: shrink with
  effort, evidence overlap. Anchor: the ground. Question type: union or partition, before search.
- **Domain 3, channel and measurement.** Actors: elections, polls, courts, media, protest. Currency:
  signal fidelity and residual per slot. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Question type:
  dependency; a link with no instrument is not a working link.
- **Domain 4, institutional habitat and refusal.** Actors: norms, opposition, associations,
  separation of powers. Currency: introduction available, exclusion not, refusal possible. Anchor:
  the ground. Question type: introduction asymmetry plus negation.
- **Domain 5, six-term substrate.** Actors: the six physical terms. Currency: cross-kind composition.
  Anchor: the figures as metalaws. Verdict: **gap** for this question.

Order by dependency: register the ground (link 1); then vocabulary and objective (the middle
links); then selection between rooms; then learning and carrying. Refuse before mint (Formula step

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the difference between what was sent up
and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round
trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-34, Fig 7 :35-36, Fig 10 :24-25,
   Fig 1 :19 and Fig 11 :39-49.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: an anchor that does not update,
   low-overlap frames, selection between rooms, a per-slot channel, and a refusal operator.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete political claim, for example "an independent judiciary is
   the non-updating anchor and free media is the channel."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **legitimacy of the anchor** (Fig 2 :36, the question must be constitutively about it). A
  court that is legitimate for "is this law constitutional" is only an instrument for "does the
  public support this government," and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure;
- the **provenance of an institutional name**. A specific institution is an import from political
  science with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The shorthand permits a
  name only after that gate; the concrete claim tends to state the name first;
- the **union/partition split**. The shorthand says "describe before looking"; a concrete
  institutional package tends to be chosen first and then fitted to the residual.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the ground does not decay and the channel does, and the
residual must be read per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured
numerically, because no political ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the
enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the
metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
