## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the observed contrast: some democracies keep
stable public support through severe disagreement (Option A), while others become vulnerable to
breakdown (Option B). What each leads to: treating disagreement amplitude as the cause sends a
`general`-mode story that confuses a room's agreement with a verdict and cannot say which live slot
is failing; treating the contrast as a residual about contact with the ground sends a `specified`
shape with five constraints, three pre-registered refuters, and an open institutional slot until an
instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor;
partition or union; order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (the residual).** The persistent gap between a polity's prediction that support continues and
  what its ground returns at specific slots, while internal frames disagree strongly. Arity: at
  least two conditions, so a **union compound** until shown otherwise (Fig 9 :4-5). Symmetry:
  disagreement is relational among frames, not a symmetric join; adding an agreeing frame adds no
  evidence. Scale: first order inside each frame, second order at the polity level where frames are
  selected against the ground.
- **Domain 1, alignment and legitimacy.** Actors: public as ground; parties, courts, media and
  agencies as frames. Currency: agreement versus residual, legitimacy. Anchor: the registered
  capacity to remove a government under conditions no frame can alter at will. Question type:
  partition, which things are anchors and which are frames.
- **Domain 2, disagreement topology.** Actors: competing claims, cleavages, conditions. Currency:
  whether disagreement shrinks with effort and how much frames share evidence. Anchor: the ground
  that decides which claims survive. Question type: union or partition, decided before search.
- **Domain 3, channel and measurement.** Actors: elections, polls, free media, courts, protest,
  budgets. Currency: signal fidelity and residual per slot. Anchor: the ground, which does not
  decay. Question type: dependency, a link with no instrument is not a working link.
- **Domain 4, institutional habitat and refusal.** Actors: norms, separation of powers, opposition,
  civil associations. Currency: what can be introduced but not subtracted, and refusal. Anchor: the
  ground, again, never agreement. Question type: introduction available, exclusion not; negation
  required.
- **Domain 5, six-term substrate.** Actors: the six physical terms. Currency: composition across two
  kinds. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. Verdict: **gap** for this question. Applying Fig 13 to
  parties and polls requires replacing terms by analogy, which is a strain, so re-census moves it
  out.
- **Order by dependency.** Register the ground first (link 1), then vocabulary and objective (the
  middle links), then selection between rooms, then learning and carrying. Effort above a broken
  middle link cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test: the apparent conflict between "civil society helps" and "civil society helped destroy
Weimar" was resolved by re-census to Fig 11 :49, not by choosing a side. Valence is relative to the
residual, so bonding and bridging associations are different domains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the names do not. Severe disagreement is not the decisive variable. Stable
support under disagreement requires a non-updating anchor, low-overlap independent frames,
selection between rooms against the surfaced residual, a channel that registers the ground per slot,
and a working refusal operator. The arbiter becoming a frame, the mirror chain, channel decay and
the absence of negation are the failure modes. Two polities with the same written constitution but
different unwritten norms are Fig 12 isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. Which
institution fills any role is an import with provenance, not a derivation. This is a `specified`,
ungrounded result, and Domain 5 is a recorded `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes refuters before the
  conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost
  is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator,
  which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is
  avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No political primitive has to be withdrawn. A claimed institutional binding is
  removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and
  `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold every binding. A sent packet can be
  superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that measures anchor movement,
  per-slot channel reachability, between-room difference, frame overlap and the survival of a
  refusal would settle the shape. R1, R2 and R3 in `01` section 5 are the standing reversers: an
  updating anchor that still sustains support, breakdown with a reachable channel, or one
  irreducible disagreement resolved without selection between rooms. A re-census that admits the six
  physical terms as political primitives would also change the verdict. Until one fires, the
  verdict stands as `candidate`.
