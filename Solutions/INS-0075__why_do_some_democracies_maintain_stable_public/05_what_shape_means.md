## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is that the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a
shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13
:134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (without its trailing comma).

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It
constrains; it does not fill. For this question `STABILITY(???)BREAKDOWN` is a shape. "A
constitution with an independent judiciary, a free press and proportional representation is what
keeps a democracy stable" is a name, and a name the framework did not derive.

The same essay names the failure mode that makes the shape discipline necessary: *"Perhaps your
problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the
union warning in `01` F4. The library is not the problem; the searcher's vocabulary is.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing,
  look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term
  gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` is stated as removal capacity and per-slot support,
  not as "trust in institutions."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The
  same essay supplies the closest world analogue to the anchor in F1: *"Courts of law rule on
  evidence, which neither side can move."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place."* The paradox is
  that by then the answer has been composed through the search. This folder stops earlier, at the
  shape, because there is no political ground reading here.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on why a room can mislead: *"the room will be swayed by
  negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift"* and *"It does not mean that the output was
  objective truth."* That is Fig 2's first collapse, restated in a room of people.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."*
   Here that would mean an instrument that returns per-slot support, anchor movement and channel
   reachability without updating on the frames being graded.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. A political institution named as the anchor is an import from
   political science. The institutional names we actually read (mutual toleration, forbearance,
   independent judiciaries, disaggregated V-Dem indicators) come from that frame, not from the
   figures.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason an instrument is needed: *"If several paths
create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A stable approval
reading can come from genuine support or from a captured information environment. The same essay:
*"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"* and *"The
answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* So what is owed for INS-0075 is a
source-discriminating instrument, not a larger pile of opinion polls.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* adds the provenance limit: *"outputs
do not contain their generator."* A set of stable institutions is the output of a historical
process; reading the output does not tell you which process produced it. And: *"The real import is
not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* This is why importing a
new poll into the same frame does not help; what is owed is a different axis of measurement.

## 4. The shape for this question, stated concretely

`STABILITY(???)BREAKDOWN` is five constraints and one open slot:

1. **An anchor that does not update.** Something settles who governs and does not change its answer
   because a frame claims it should (Fig 2 :33, Fig 2 :29-30). The parentheses hold the name of that
   something.
2. **Low-overlap frames.** Independence is *"how little they draw on the same evidence"* (Fig 2 :31);
   agreement from one pool is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Selection between rooms.** The next governing arrangement must differ from the last and be
   picked against the residual the last one surfaced (Fig 7 :36), not handed along as the same
   closure.
4. **A readable channel per slot.** The ground does not decay but the channel does (Fig 10 :24);
   the residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19).
5. **A working refusal.** A vocabulary without negation can only accumulate (Operators table :58).
   Failed claims must be refused, not merely absorbed.

None of these five names an institution. Each is a constraint that a candidate institution must
satisfy, and each has a refuter in `01` section 5. That is the operational meaning of "shape" here.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (a too-strong reading of "shape, not a name")

I first wrote that the framework forbids naming any of the five items. That is too strong, and the
correction is recorded here rather than erased.

- The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like
  (a shape, not a name)."* The exemplar's own correction (`QMGR-0037`, `05` §5b and `06` Q1) narrows
  it: naming an atom already held in the library is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive.
- The political case is different again. The framework does **not** hold a fixed six-term political
  library at all. "Anchor," "channel," "refusal" and "room" are roles, not primitives the framework
  owns. So naming a specific institution in those roles is not naming a held atom; it is an
  **import** from the political-science frame. Imports are permitted only with a source gate and a
  shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).
- Therefore the honest statement is: the framework forbids *deriving* an institutional name from
  itself, and permits *importing* one with provenance. It does not forbid talking about
  institutions. It forbids presenting an imported name as a figure-derived result.
- *They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It
  makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and
  an emergent one cannot."* Visibility is not neutrality. Our five roles are a visible choice.

My §3 metamerism point is **not** retracted. An approval reading does not determine its source, and
source-discriminating instruments are owed. What is retracted is only the blanket ban on naming.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an
action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground
can settle it."* The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* A campaign that gathers
prestigious names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The
legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let an instrument settle
it.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly
testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula
yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* states the limit directly: *"Because it is a metatheory, it
cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* It also warns that outputs *"could never be
verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated
derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the political
claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
