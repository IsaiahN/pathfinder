# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can researchers identify the most important links in the Earth system without oversimplifying the rest?

**Source:** `earth systems.txt` line 10

**Section:** 1. Earth as a coupled system

**Field for public contact points:** earth system modeling research

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No single measurement settles the ranking rule itself, so nothing here is `grounded`. Every substantive claim carries its mode inline in `01`, `07`, `09` and `11`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pass, not by this folder)

## What this folder is

A derive-first answer to a yes-or-no question that turns out to be two questions in one. The derivation starts from the residual in effect terms, censuses the domains before choosing any figure line, quotes the figures verbatim from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, and keeps the three verdicts separate rather than averaging them. The world sources are secondary corroboration only, and most of them are independent of the framework author. The whole thing is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## Short answer

Yes to the weak reading, no to the strong reading. A ranked shortlist of links is obtainable without discarding the rest, but only if "important" is bound to a stated residual, the habitat is enumerated outward past first order, residuals stay indexed per link slot and are never aggregated, and the ranking is spent as a budget filter whose verdict is still owed by the ground. The strong reading, a single canonical list that is also a verdict about the system, is a final room and an averaged signal, and the figures forbid both.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." An outside frame that has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
