## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request for a shortlist of the most important links in the Earth system that does not discard the rest. Option A: treat "importance" as an intrinsic property of links and deliver a canonical ranked list. Option B: bind importance to a stated residual, keep residuals per slot, enumerate outward, spend the ranking as a budget filter and leave the verdict to the ground; refuse a final list. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal, misses the cascade, and lets a filter hand down a verdict; B delivers a checkable shape with written falsifiers and an open list until a discriminator settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is how to rank link slots by contribution to a stated R while keeping the unranked slots legible. Arity: a link is a binary relation, so R is indexed per slot and there are many slots. Symmetry: links are directed and ordered (→, ⇒, −, ⋛), so importance is not symmetric and a single scalar imposes a symmetry on an ordered field. Scale: first-order direct links, second-order links that alter other links, and cascades three steps out. Fig 9 :22 names arity, symmetry and scale as the description of R.
- **Domain 1, Earth system as ground.** Actors: components (atmosphere, ocean, cryosphere, land, biosphere) and relations (energy, water, carbon and momentum fluxes, gradients, networks, feedbacks, teleconnections). Currency: flux magnitude and residual against observation. Anchor: observations, reanalysis, experiments, paleo records. Question type: a union of coupled components whose links are the cross-boundary relations.
- **Domain 2, the modeling and method frame.** Actors: models, parameters, sensitivity indices, centrality measures, importance scores. Currency: description length, residual, budget. Anchor: the ground through validation, never the metric. Question type: a filter over D1, not a new domain of the world.
- **Domain 3, the verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers and public submission venues. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one framework evidence pool, so this domain's internal independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** The two halves of the question are a union: "rank the links" and "preserve the rest" are two conditions over one residual field, and the lever for one does not satisfy the other. Fig 9 :5 splits it; Seat Map :211 unbundles it and notes the payoff that "the residue is finally describable."
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Split the union, bind the residual and the sought-for shape, enumerate the habitat outward, keep per-slot residuals, rank as a filter, then let the ground settle. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test applied: Fig 3 :20 ("ordered by dependency, not by importance") appeared to conflict with the question. Re-census resolved it: Fig 3 governs the internal order of a chain; the question's ranking is a budget operation under Fig 9 :60. Different objects. One live strain remains, at D1 scale: a continuous residual field may not be finitely enumerable at a fixed budget. That is unreachable-at-budget, not underivable, and is reported in `11_audit.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The weak reading is licensed, the strong reading is refused. A shortlist of important links can be produced without discarding the rest provided four conditions hold: the residual is named, the habitat is enumerated outward, residuals stay per slot and unaggregated, and the ranking is spent as a budget filter whose verdict is owed by the ground. The strong reading, a canonical final list that is itself a verdict, is a last room and an averaged signal. Domain 1 is `specified` triangulated; Domain 2 is `specified` triangulated; Domain 3 is `general` single-source internally with an external `gap`. The three are not averaged into one status. No particular link is named and no measurement is claimed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent in the right order. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished list or a framework summary instead of a residual and its refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable. The scientific cost of believing the strong reading is higher: an averaged score can hide a live slot and a first-order list can omit the cascade, which is how a wrong shortlist becomes an unexamined assumption rather than a visible error.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions and the five falsifiers are removable or checkable without touching the derivation, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. What is not undoable is an outside reader's independence once they have absorbed the framework before giving a verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A change of residual that reorders the ranking and is confirmed by observations would break D1's invariance claim; a lossless aggregate that preserves all live slots would break D2; a first-order enumeration that reliably finds a third-order residual would break both; a ranking that predicts an observed outcome with no separate ground check would break the filter rule; and a figure re-census that admits a final room would break D3. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
