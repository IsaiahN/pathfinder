**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 to F5 is stated with an explicit residual in your own words. The recipient must be able to refute the output without holding the framework's interior.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about ranking links in the Earth system

> Hi [name],
>
> I work on [your field, one line]. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know from earth system modeling. I am not asking you to read any framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output, stated as `specified`, not `grounded`:** a shortlist of "the most important links" in the Earth system is only well defined after the residual is named. Importance is relative to the question being asked of the model, not a property a link carries. Two consequences follow. First, a single aggregated importance score throws away the per-link signal that tells you where to look next, so the unranked links must remain individually addressable. Second, the link that carries the residual is often two or three steps out, not a direct exchange, so a first-order enumeration is not a smaller version of the right shortlist, it is a different object.
>
> The claim is that a shortlist can be produced without discarding the rest, provided four conditions hold: the residual is stated in effect terms; the habitat is enumerated outward past first order; residuals stay indexed per link and are never averaged into one background score; and the ranking is used to decide where to spend measurement effort, while the verdict about what actually matters is left to observations, reanalysis, experiments or paleo constraints.
>
> What I am not claiming: that there is a fixed canonical set of important links; that an importance metric is itself a ground truth; that links without instruments are unimportant rather than unassessed; or that this note reports a measurement. It is a shape with its refuter attached, nothing more.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this email:**
> 1. a link ranking that stays the same under two residuals the field treats as different questions;
> 2. a single aggregated score that preserves every per-link live residual and predicts the next measurement location just as well;
> 3. evidence that first-order enumeration reliably finds the residual carried three steps out;
> 4. a ranking that predicts the observed outcome without any separate ground check and survives a change of residual;
> 5. a ranking procedure that no further observation could ever change.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because ..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would like from you:** name the failure class you would expect this to hit in your own work. If you think the phrase "most important links" is already well defined in your field without a residual, please say so and give me the definition, because that would be the strongest possible refutation.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

> - Canonical usage rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether your output is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, stated explicitly

**Stage 1.** Send only the residual, the shape claim, the four conditions and the refuter. Record the recipient's verdict *before* any framework material is shown. Ask what they would count as a failure.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not this folder's summaries, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they have seen the interior (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user state their own ground, write their own refuter before running, and label the mode of what they get. The framework's own warning applies: a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" when the sought-for shape is held as a preference (Fig 5 :62-64).

**Hard precondition repeated:** stages 1 through 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Nothing in this packet has been checked against an Earth system observation by this agent. That check is the recipient's to make, and it is the whole point of the first contact.
