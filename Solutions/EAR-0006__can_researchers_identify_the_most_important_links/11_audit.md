## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual, no aggregation), Fig 11 :49-54 (valence relative to R, cascade enumeration), Fig 9 :60 (filter versus verdict), Fig 7 :6 (no last room).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: importance is residual-relative; keep the rest addressable; enumerate outward; rank as a budget filter; no final list.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a researcher's concrete action: produce a ranked shortlist of Earth system links for a funding or measurement decision.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four constraints held in the shorthand do not survive the trip back down unless they are explicitly enforced:

- the **residual binding**. A shortlist produced without naming R silently restores importance as intrinsic. This is the first thing dropped under time pressure.
- the **per-slot field**. A ranked list naturally carries only the top entries; the unranked slots disappear from the artifact even if they remain in the analyst's mind.
- the **cascade reach**. A shortlist scoped to direct exchanges looks complete and is categorically different from the cascaded one.
- the **filter status**. A ranking handed to a decision maker is read as a result unless the verdict is explicitly deferred to a ground check.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure sits between the ranked shortlist and any claim about importance, and the ground, not the ranking, settles it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
