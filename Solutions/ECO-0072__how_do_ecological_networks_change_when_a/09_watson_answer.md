## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a newly arrived species with no local predators or competitors. Option A: the network loses enemies, so the change is a subtraction of checks and the network simplifies. Option B: the network gains an actor, so the change is a count of nodes. Option C: the arrival is an introduction into an enumerated habitat, the newcomer has unopposed valence, the effect travels outward through relations, and the ground settles the sign. What each leads to: A gives a static inventory with a missing entry and an unmeasured sign; B gives a count that cannot see rewiring; C gives an enumerable habitat, a checkable cascade, four written refuters and an honest stop at the sign. The figures decide for C as a shape and refuse to decide A's sign.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor. Partition or union. Order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The shape of the network change after an enemy-free arrival. Arity: compound, with newcomer, residents, conditions and relations as co-operands. Symmetry: relations are directed, and valence is relative to R rather than carried (Fig 11 :49). Scale: first order is direct contact, second order is the contact of those, third order is where the residual that matters can sit (Fig 11 :52). Fig 9 :54 unbundles it as a union, not a partition: the arrival condition and the recipient-community condition overlap.
- **Domain 1, ecological habitat.** Actors: newcomer, residents, light, water, season, terrain, pollination, predation, competition, dependency. Currency: survival, reproduction and who influences whom. Anchor: measured population and interaction data. Question type: enumerate by contact, never compose.
- **Domain 2, management / seat.** Actors: managers, engineers, control agents. Currency: introduction versus exclusion, effort and reversibility. Anchor: the same ground, which does not move when a manager pushes it. Question type: who has standing to add an actor.
- **Domain 3, verification / outreach.** Actors: ecologists, reviewers, public readers. Currency: residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat (Fig 11 :43), locate unopposed valence (Fig 11 :49), trace the cascade outward until it stops mattering (Fig 11 :52), then let the ground settle the sign (Fig 11 :73). Measuring the sign first is effort above the break (Fig 3).

Strain test applied before concluding. Two strains were found. "No predators or competitors" cannot mean literally zero contact, because an actor with no contact is not in the habitat; it is read as no effective local check, which the world sources corroborate. The sign of the complexity change is withheld by every figure line in the census and by the unsettled ecology literature. Both are named in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6, not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the sign and the literal reading do not. An enemy-free arrival is an addition of an unopposed actor to a habitat that must be enumerated outward, with the network changing in its relations and conditions and possibly forming a re-entrant loop whose sign is a measurement question. The newcomer is in contact, not outside the habitat. A first-order inventory is insufficient, and a species list cannot see a rewiring. In the unmaintained mode selection ran, and "fittest" is a retrospective label; in the maintained mode a seat acts but exclusion is unavailable, so the work is permanent. The figures withhold the sign of the complexity change, and the world literature is itself unsettled there. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest corroboration is the low-overlap cascade and network literature; the weakest is the shared-source ecosystem-engineering pair.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a signed conclusion instead of a shape and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The literal "no enemies" reading, any signed complexity claim and the "outside the habitat" reading are removable without touching the enumeration or cascade derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A field measurement showing that a first-order inventory fully predicts the change with an instrument capable of seeing second and third order effects would break the cascade claim; an actor with zero contact that changes the network would break the contact census; a verbatim figure line returning a sign would end the honest stop; a managed introduction reaching a solved state with no maintenance would break the permanence claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
