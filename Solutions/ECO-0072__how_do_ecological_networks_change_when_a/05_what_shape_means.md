## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 (local extract) adds that it *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"*.

For this question the shape is: **arrival is an introduction into an enumerated habitat; the newcomer's valence is unopposed but its contact is real; the change travels outward through relations; the ground settles the sign.** That narrows the answer space without filling it. "Connectance falls by 12 percent" is a number, not a shape. "The network simplifies" is a sign, not a shape.

The *Tether Architecture* essay gives the nearby warning directly. Its section "Three mappings offered and declined" refuses the easy identification: *"The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual."* A reader who reaches for the familiar ecological term gets a resemblance, not the definition.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* You read the list off the world.
- Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The stopping rule is the cascade's disappearance, not a preferred list size.
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* Describe the effect before choosing the discipline.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The *How Intelligence Actually Works* essay describes the recursion as a web rather than a tree: the mapping *"is not a tree with roots and branches, it is a web, with no canonical order and no single path through."* For an ecological network this is a structural warning, not a metaphor: there is no privileged first link to trace, so enumeration is outward from the residual rather than down a ladder.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape.

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means field measurement of population and interaction change.
2. **A provenanced import with a shadow test** from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8). Naming a particular newcomer or a particular sign is the ecologist's binding, recorded as such.

The canonical rule stops at instrument limits and keeps the lineage. The second essay states the reason: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A description that no available instrument distinguishes from the measured outcome is still a description. The lineage is what keeps the result checkable.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Fig 11 :73: *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*

An invasive species updates the habitat. It does not update the ground. This separates two claims that are easy to merge:

- the newcomer changes which relations hold (habitat, updates);
- whether the change is good, bad, simple or complex is settled by measurement (ground, does not update).

The *How Intelligence Actually Works* essay makes the same separation in ecological language: *"when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."* The unpredictability is a statement about our enumeration, not about the ground. Fig 11 :52 gives the instrument rule: enumerate outward, because the residual that matters was three steps out.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework license us to say about an enemy-free arrival?** A shape, not a sign and not a species name: an addition of an unopposed actor to a habitat enumerated by contact, a re-wiring of directed relations and conditions over two or more steps, and a measured sign that the figures deliberately withhold.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it keeps the distinction in §3. It does not name a missing primitive (the newcomer is already an enumerated actor), and it does not return a sign (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*). It would commit the error if it claimed that enemy release *must* simplify the network or that the framework identifies the "fittest" invader.

**Correction to a tempting reading.** The phrase *"shape, not a name"* governs *missing* things. Here nothing is missing in the primitive set: the newcomer is an actor in contact. So naming the species, the pathway or the measured sign is permitted as an ecologist's binding, as long as it is provenanced and not presented as a framework output. The shape is still not a name: it does not tell you which species will arrive or whether connectance will rise.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From the *Tether Architecture* essay's citations and the master plan's authority order, prestige can give a claim a budget to be tried and cannot settle it. Only the ground settles it. A list of sympathetic ecologists is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The *Tether Architecture* essay says an LLM given the figures and formula produces *"a plausible simulated output"* and that such outputs must be seen as *"simulacra"* because the library is a black box. The *How Intelligence Actually Works* essay adds that the recursion *"stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it"*, which is operational convergence, not truth. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
