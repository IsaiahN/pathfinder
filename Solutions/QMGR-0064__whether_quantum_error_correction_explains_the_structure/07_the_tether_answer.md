# The Tether's answer: does quantum error correction explain the structure of spacetime?

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Explains the structure of spacetime" packs three residuals that stay separate: what occupies the structure slot between space and time; whether a QEC construction can occupy it without importing a new atom; and what ground reading would discriminate that occupant from network, spectrum or gradient alternatives. D2 (QI) and D3 (gravity) form a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Each has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Spacetime is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." So "the structure of spacetime" is the middle slot of a cross-kind derivative, not a substance QEC can explain as if it were a primitive. Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
2. **Continuity is a leftover, not an ingredient.** Fig 13 :43: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this" and Fig 13 :124: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." A QEC account does not earn its place by supplying smoothness.
3. **QEC is a candidate binding onto held atoms, not a derivation and not a seventh member.** The network atom is "that entities relate, and influence travels" (Fig 13 :24), and error correction also resembles a functional group over a code subspace (Fig 12 :123). The framework returns a shape, not a name: Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Selecting QEC is a provenanced binding from the QI frame, recorded as such.
4. **The binding is an import with a source gate and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall" and Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The code literature passes the shadow test in one direction (bulk locality, area law), but the stronger claim has no recorded source gate, so it carries zero evidentiary weight until the route is stated (Fig 8 :50-52).
5. **"Explains" is second-order production, not identity.** Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." Both accounts are settled first-order molecules; their comparison composes at second order. The Operators table defines the relevant bond as production ("A produces B; B did not exist before"), not identity ("two names, one referent"). And even a successful account is not a final room: Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that spacetime *is* a quantum error correcting code as a final theory. The no-last-room rule forbids a terminal explanation.
- Not that QEC adds a new primitive or moves the wall. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world."
- Not that the metric tensor or the RT formula *is* a specific framework atom. That is a naming of the source of a reading without a discriminating instrument.
- Not a derivation of gravity from quantum information, and not a unified field.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the relevant scale settles the left-over residual.
- Not that the expert community's agreement settles anything. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot test:** name a measurement or proof that discriminates network, spectrum and gradient as occupants of the space-structure-time slot. Until one exists, the metamerism error is live.
- **Shadow test:** show a gap the geometric account leaves open that the QEC construction actually closes, with the route and provenance recorded. Fig 8 :50-52: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents.
- **Flat-formula test:** specify the bond on the code side (which code, which reconstruction map), not just the ingredients. Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused; show no QEC construction can occupy the slot; derive one theory from the other with no residual left.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A `gap` on the specific identification is a verdict, not a silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether quantum error correction explains spacetime, and it does not claim the final answer. It says that spacetime is not a primitive: space and time cannot bond directly, so a structure must sit between them, and smoothness is what a composition leaves behind rather than an ingredient. QEC is a serious candidate for that slot, but it is a provenanced binding onto atoms the framework already holds, not a new primitive and not a derivation from the figures. "Explains" can only mean second-order composition over two already settled accounts, not identity and not a last room. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, the code literature's genuine shadow test, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the slot. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
