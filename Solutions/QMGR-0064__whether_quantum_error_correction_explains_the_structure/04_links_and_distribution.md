## What the wider literature contributes

W1 to W4 are one evidence pool (holographic QEC and entanglement/spacetime), so their agreement is one witness, not four (Fig 2 :40). Read together they establish that the code account has real support inside D2: it explains bulk locality, an area law and subregion reconstruction. That is the shadow test of F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` succeeding in one direction. None of them supplies a ground reading that discriminates QEC from alternative occupants of the structure slot, so F5 stands. Their existence is also a budget filter: the field is crowded, which is a reason to be precise about the residual, never a reason to infer the answer (Fig 9 :60).

W5 is low overlap (philosophy and complex systems). Its weak/strong emergence distinction corroborates the shape claim at the mechanism level: a weakly emergent geometric description can be deduced from the code substrate, but the deduction does not name which held atom plays the structure role. The "overdetermination" criticism and the Carroll/Parola taxonomy both support keeping the three residuals separate rather than collapsing "explains" into identity. W5 does not bear on the specific QEC binding and is not used as a witness for it.

The canonical framework essays (local files and the public figures repository) are framework-internal. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight. They are used for the canonical reading in `05_what_shape_means.md`, not as world witnesses.
