## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The phrase "explains the structure of spacetime" packs three different residuals that must not be averaged:

1. **Composition residual.** If space and time are states, what sits in the structure slot that composes them? The question "what is the structure of spacetime" is exactly the question of that slot. Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."
2. **Binding residual.** Does the quantum error correction (QEC) story occupy that slot, or does it bind onto an atom already held, and if so which one? This is a provenance question, not a derivation question.
3. **Settlement residual.** What ground reading would discriminate QEC from a competing occupant (entanglement alone, a network, an entropy gradient, a spectrum)? Without such a reading, "explains" is a `specified` hypothesis, not a verdict.

Arity and symmetry of R: two conditions in contact (a quantum information frame and a geometric frame), so a union compound under Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The claim "QEC explains spacetime" is a production or sequence claim, not a symmetric identity, so the bond is ordered (⇒ or →), not ≡. Scale: first order inside each frame, second order at their contact.

What an answer would look like is bound in the README (Direction). That binding is what makes "explains" refusable.

## 3. Findings

Verdicts are kept per domain and are never averaged. `triangulated` means two figure lines with low overlap; `single-source` means one figure line; `gap` means no figure line governs after a genuine re-census.

### F1 — Spacetime is not a primitive; the question must be restated (D1, triangulated)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

The phrase "the structure of spacetime" is therefore not a name for a primitive object. It is the middle slot of a cross-kind derivative. The question "does QEC explain the structure of spacetime" is well formed only as "does QEC characterize what occupies the structure slot between space and time, and how that composition is licensed." A claim that QEC explains a primitive spacetime commits the category error the figure forbids. Mode `general` for the structural claim; `specified` for the application.

Refuter: a figure re-census that admits a direct state-state composition, or a derivation of one of the six from the other five.

### F2 — Continuity and smoothness are leftovers, not a structure to be explained by naming (D1, triangulated)

Fig 13 :43: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"
Fig 13 :124: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."

So a QEC account cannot settle the question by supplying continuity or smoothness as an ingredient. Those are what a composition leaves behind. This is a second, independent reason (low overlap with F1) that "spacetime structure" must be read as the slot plus its arrangement, not as a substance.

Refuter: a derivation of smoothness from the six that also makes smoothness a member of the set, which the figure explicitly refused.

### F3 — QEC is a candidate binding, not a derivation and not a new atom (D1 and D2, single-source for the atom, triangulated for the limit)

Fig 13 :24: "that entities relate, and influence travels"
Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

The network structure is "that entities relate, and influence travels." QEC is about how logical information remains recoverable across many physical subsystems, so network is the closest held atom. But it is a strain to say QEC *is* the network atom: error correction also looks like a functional group (Fig 12 :123, "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."), and an entropy gradient is another candidate. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Selecting network or a code functional group is a binding from the QI frame onto held atoms, with provenance recorded as such. It is not a new member of the six, and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :25, "An import adds no atom to the world.").

Mode `specified`, ungrounded. Status single-source for the network mapping (Fig 13 :24); triangulated for the shape-not-a-name limit (Fig 9 :32 with the canonical figures README).

Refuter: an instrument that discriminates which held atom a QEC construction occupies in the space-structure-time slot, or a proof that no QEC construction can occupy it.

### F4 — The QEC to spacetime step is an import and needs a source gate and shadow test (D2 to D3, triangulated)

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

The QEC results are derived inside the QI frame and then offered to the gravity frame. That is an import, and imports are priced: source gate (provenance) and shadow test (explains a gap). The literature partly supplies the shadow test in one direction: the code account explains bulk locality and an area law (world sources W1, W2, W4 below). The stronger claim that QEC explains *the structure of spacetime* has no recorded source gate in the framework, so it carries zero evidentiary weight until the route is stated. Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

Refuter: a demonstration that the QEC construction explains no gap the geometric account leaves open, so the shadow test fails.

### F5 — No ground reading discriminates the binding at the relevant scale (D3, gap on the specific identification, unreached at the ground)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

D2 has genuine anchors: theorem proofs and table-top quantum experiments. D1 has the figures as metalaws. But the cross-frame binding "QEC is the structure of spacetime" has no instrument that would change its answer if it were false. This is not silence. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." Verdict: `gap` for a figure line that decides QEC specifically; `unreached` at the ground. No argument in this folder moves it to `grounded`.

Refuter: name and run an instrument or proof whose outcome would discriminate QEC from entanglement-only, network-only or gradient accounts of the slot.

### F6 — "Explains" is licensed only as second-order composition, not as a last room (D1 and D3, triangulated)

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."
Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room"

Both first-order molecules (the code account inside D2 and the geometric account inside D3) are settled in their own frames. Each can become an atom for a second-order composition. That is the one figure-licensed meaning of "explains": composition over settled terms, not reduction to a final substrate. A claim that QEC is the final explanation of spacetime would be a terminal room, which Fig 7 forbids. The canonical figures README makes the same point in its own section titled "This is not a Theory of Everything - and that is the point."

Refuter: a derivation of one theory from the other that leaves no further residual, which would contradict the no-last-room rule.

### F7 — The bargain: QEC pays inside D2, but "spacetime is a code" risks the flat formula (D1 and D2, triangulated)

Fig 5 :43: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing"
Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."

The QEC hypothesis passes the support guard inside D2: it predicts and retrodicts concrete gaps (W1 to W4). But the slogan "spacetime is a QECC" names ingredients (subsystems, qubits) without recording the bond (which code, which reconstruction map, which subregion duality). That is the flat-formula collapse. Fig 5 :29-31 names the inert case: "the machinery worked; the answer was already known."

Refuter: a version of the claim that states the bond explicitly and still fails to reduce the residual; or a version with no bond that nevertheless pays the bargain.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map :287). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One of the six terms is derived from the other five (breaks Fig 13 indivisibility and F1).
2. A figure re-census admits a direct state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused, so no structure slot is required (breaks F1).
3. An instrument or proof discriminates the structure slot and shows that no QEC construction can occupy it, or that a specific alternative occupant is forced (settles or breaks F3 and F5).
4. The QEC construction explains no gap left open by the geometric account, so the shadow test fails (breaks F4 and F7).
5. A derivation of one theory from the other leaves no further residual, contradicting the no-last-room rule (breaks F6).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn and the lineage records where the error was. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. Seat Map :171: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* My stake is recorded here rather than asserted as truth.
