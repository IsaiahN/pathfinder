## The question restated as an effect

The question asks whether the hypothesis "spacetime geometry emerges from the error-correcting structure of a boundary quantum theory" closes the residual at the contact between quantum theory and gravity. Two options and their consequences:

- **Option A, assert yes.** Spacetime is a quantum error correcting code (QECC), and error correction is the explanation of geometric structure. This delivers a naming of the structure slot and a reduction story. It also risks treating "spacetime" as a primitive, naming a slot the framework says it can only characterize by shape, and importing a result from the quantum information frame without a source gate (Fig 13 :75, Fig 9 :32, Fig 8 :50-52).
- **Option B, keep the shape and refuse the naming.** A structure must sit between space and time, and a continuum is not a primitive. QEC is a candidate binding onto a held atom (most plausibly network, possibly a functional group over a code subspace), and the word "explains" is only licensed as second-order composition over settled terms, not as a final theory. This yields a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction".** By "an answer" I mean: a figure-licensed characterization of what must occupy the space-structure-time slot, a statement of whether QEC can be that occupant without importing a new atom, and a named instrument or proof that would discriminate it from alternative occupants. Binding this down makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else (Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that QEC explains spacetime.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not supply an anchor. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
