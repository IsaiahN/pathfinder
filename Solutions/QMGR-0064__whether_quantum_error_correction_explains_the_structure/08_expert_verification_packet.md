## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Space and time cannot bond directly, so spacetime is not a primitive but a cross-kind derivative with a structure in the slot, and smoothness is a leftover of that composition rather than an ingredient. The QEC account and the geometric account are each first-order molecules of the form `state - structure - state`; over the full shared six-term set they stand as isomers, while as usually written they share only a functional group. "QEC explains spacetime" can only be licensed as second-order production over two already settled accounts, not as identity and not as a final theory. The framework does not select QEC for the structure slot. It returns a shape, together with written falsifiers and a named source-discriminating instrument that would close the slot.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by a researcher against the two accounts

- **Statement:** each account can be written as `state - structure - state` at first order. Over the full shared atom set `{Space, Time, Energy, Spectrum, Gradient, Network}` they are **isomers**, Fig 12 :117, "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. As usually written they share only the cross-kind motif, hence the weaker **functional-group** claim, Fig 12 :123, "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful? In the geometric account the metric relates space and time into an interval, with area and entropy supplying structure. In the code account a code subspace over physical subsystems supports recoverability of logical information. Does a structure sit between two states in each case, or is one of them a background parameter rather than a composed term?
- **Refuter:** either account cannot be written as `state - structure - state` at first order; or one requires an atom the other does not, which kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim standing.
