## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the two claims bundled in "can a system reason about its own assumptions and detect when those assumptions no longer apply?" Option A: treat this as one capacity, answer yes or no, and use the system's own confidence as the evidence. Option B: split it into representation and detection, keep the verdicts separate per domain, require a non-updating anchor for detection, refuse whole-set self-certification, and record the missing instrument as a `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a self-certifying frame, which is the last room the figures forbid, and makes coherent drift invisible; B yields a checkable shape with five failure conditions written in advance, a located witness and an honest stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two conditions plus a limit object. Arity: representation and detection, with the metaframe appearing as the object that would certify the whole set. Symmetry: "reason about" is reflective and lossy across scales (Fig 10 :68, Fig 4 :27-29); no-longer-applies is ordered, since a term was held and then refused by the unary operator negation (Operators table :70-74). Scale: one loop inside Γ, one contact with the ground, one cross-scale seat. Fig 9 :54-58 names it a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1, loop / self-model.** Actors: Γ, atoms(Γ), bound terms, belief b, weighting w, per-slot residual. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ) and residual per slot. Anchor: the figures as metalaws; no world measurement in this folder. Genuine domain.
- **D2, ground / anchor.** Actors: prediction Γ(b,a), action a, observation o′, the anchor. Currency: R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot. Anchor: a measurement, experiment, counter or outcome that does not update (Fig 2 :29). Genuine domain.
- **D3, metaframe / certification.** Actors: the frame, its metaframe, negation. Currency: proof inside closure(Γ). Anchor: none inside the frame; the witness is imported (Fig 9 :19). Genuine domain, verdict `no` at this scale.
- **D4, verification / other-frame seat.** Actors: ground maintainer, outside frame, monitor, record. Currency: residual, independence, provenance. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 9 :60, Fig 10 :24). Genuine domain.
- **D5, the instrument that binds the unbound direction.** Actors: a preference, an ordinary term. Currency: residual accrual after binding. Anchor: **none nameable in this folder**. Not a domain yet, so `gap`. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D1 and D2 overlap on the residual; D3 and D4 overlap on the imported witness. An overlap is a compound with its own loop, so the four verdicts are never averaged (Fig 9 :55-58).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (per-slot residual) then vocabulary (bind the assumption as a term) then objective (persistent residual against the ground) then planning (aim the probe) then learn and carry (refuse, settle, compose). The chain breaks at vocabulary when the load-bearing object is an unbound preference. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

Strain test applied: "reason about" as inner self-observation and "certify the whole set" are different objects (S2); an externally specified runtime monitor is not a verifier reconstructing the claim (S3). Both dissolved by re-census and kept visible in `01` §6. One live type question remains, S4: what is the ground for a formal assumption.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A system can reason about the subset of its assumptions that are bound, predicting terms, and can rank them by a residual that persists and does not trend down. It cannot see the unbound preference that gives its own question direction. It can detect that a held assumption no longer applies only by ranging it against a reference that does not update on the system's own report, with the residual indexed per assumption and a refusal term added rather than a parameter re-fitted. It cannot certify the whole set from inside: the metaframe is underivable and the witness is imported. A second frame can carry the correction only if it cannot reconstruct the claim and does not share the evidence pool. The instrument that would turn a preference into a refusable term is a declared `gap`, reported as `unreached` rather than as silence. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `specified` on the domain verdicts, `general` on the structure, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** if the bound/unbound split is false, a system could certify its own direction and the last-room restriction would be an unnecessary constraint; if the anchor condition is false, internal confidence would settle assumption failure and the external-frame requirement would waste a budget. The cost is bounded and locatable because five failure conditions were written before the run in `01` §4 and repeated in `08`. The larger practical cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape names no system, architecture or specific failing assumption, so nothing concrete has to be withdrawn if a case fails. A committed prediction can be replaced by a better one; an imported external frame can be swapped for a sharper one; a refused term is added to the record rather than deleted (Fig 6 :42-46). `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already keep the unverified parts out of the claim.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A consistent system that proves its own consistency by its own rules would break D3; a system whose confidence tracks correctness across novel tasks with no external reading, no second frame and no change of contact would break D2 and Fig 1 :27; a frame that binds its unbound preference as a term, accrues residual for it and refuses it internally, with the refusal confirmed against a ground, would close D5; a verifier that is unable to reconstruct the claim and verifies it soundly would sharpen D4; a derivation of the missing instrument from the existing figure set would move D5 from `gap` to a domain. Prestige, headcount and agreement from a shared pool are not such instruments.
