## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing it with the primary's conclusions. I used the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between a state's claim to rightful rule and what the governed do. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not one axis. Symmetry: introduction available, exclusion not (Fig 11 :61); the two currencies, actions and contact, do not add (Fig 12 :125). Scale: slot, population aggregate, time channel; scale change is lossy (Fig 4 :27-32).
- **Domain 1, state as frame.** Actors: agencies, officials, budgets, self-description. Currency: actions and information. Anchor: the governed's live response. Type: partition inside closure, then ground contact.
- **Domain 2, governed as frames.** Actors: citizens, groups, informal authorities. Currency: belief and compliance. Anchor: their lived conditions. Type: triangulation with D1 against one ground.
- **Domain 3, channel and instruments.** Actors: censuses, surveys, audits, aggregates. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Type: instrument question (Fig 3 :27).
- **Domain 4, checking seat.** Actors: researchers and public record. Currency: agreement versus ground contact. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence zero in this simulated run.
- **Refused:** prestige or international reputation as an anchor, because it moves when pushed (Fig 2 :30). **Absorbed:** the national legitimacy score, as a lossy D3 transform.

Order by dependency: perceive the per-slot residual, acquire vocabulary for what the state affords, state the objective, plan, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); keep the anchor reachable before spending on capacity (Fig 10 :29); describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

**My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading.** Where the primary derived from Fig 2's anchor rule and Fig 12's two currencies, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 4's scale membrane. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes inside one evidence pool. That is weak internal agreement, not independent corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29/:34/:41/:43 (anchor, drift, convention), Fig 11 :39/:40/:61 (contact and introduction), Fig 10 :24/:61/:93 (channel and stake), Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :55 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: two anchors, per-slot residual, contact and introduction for weak states, stake and pooled metrics for capable states, convention not cashed, no reform named.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about actual states, for example "state X lost legitimacy because its officials became invested in their own standing and its surveys averaged away a dissenting region".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor test itself**, that the ground must not move when pushed. A concrete case always tempts a substitute anchor (election result, GDP, international ranking), which is X1 refused in the primary census;
- the **per-slot discipline**. The shorthand says "per slot"; the concrete claim reaches for a national trust figure because that is the data that exists;
- the **asymmetry of introduction**. The concrete claim reaches for exclusion (remove an actor, ban a voice), which Fig 11 :61 refuses;
- the **separation of the two loss loops**. The concrete claim merges stake and metrics into "elite capture", which is a name, not a shape.

One thing survives cleanly: a state that loses the per-slot channel and cashes its convention as a credential is losing legitimacy regardless of its capacity. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the full law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
