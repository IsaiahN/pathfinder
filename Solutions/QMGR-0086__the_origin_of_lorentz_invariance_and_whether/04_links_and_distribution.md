## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22-25 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only if asked, the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only if they then want the method, the formula, with the instruction to state their own mode and refuter.

## The two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1:** check the output (symmetry as derivative; null search does not close exactness) against the ground. Ask for a refutation, not a verdict on the framework.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, read the figures and formula. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled as later agreement.
3. **Stage 3:** use the formula on another problem, with a self-written refuter and a stated mode.

Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." This is why the first contact names the existing edge (GRB lags, birefringence, coefficient tables) rather than asking someone to imagine a hypothetical detector.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. It is deliberately modest: a shape and a refusal, not a claim about which symmetry is realised in nature.
