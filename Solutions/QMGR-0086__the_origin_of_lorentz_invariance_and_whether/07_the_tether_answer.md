# The Tether's answer on Lorentz invariance

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two residuals, kept separate. **Origin:** what produces the observed space-time symmetry? **Exactness:** does it hold at every energy? They form a union compound (Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"), not one hard question, and they have different anchors: derivation for the first, a detector for the second.

## 2. The shape

1. **A symmetry is a derivative, not a primitive.** Fig 13 :124-125: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." Fig 13 :43-44: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." So the origin question, under this substrate, receives a categorical answer about *kind*: a symmetry is a leftover of composition. It does not receive the name of a mechanism.
2. **Space and time are independent terms, so the symmetry relates distinct frames.** Fig 13 :35-37: "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network. Each answers a question the other two cannot: adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction." The minimality table ties symmetries to the states: Fig 13 :62 "no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything", Fig 13 :65 "no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder."
3. **No direct space-time bond.** Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one"; :75 "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." A Lorentz symmetry over space and time is a higher-order derivative, not a floor term.
4. **Exactness at all energies is a ground question.** Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
5. **No finite null search closes it.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." The published bounds are real, but they are bounds. The verdict on "exact at all energies" is **unreached**, not confirmed and not refuted.
6. **The remedy is instruments, not a new principle.** Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." We are budget-limited, not principle-limited. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The existing edge is GRB arrival-time lags, birefringence limits and coefficient tables.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Lorentz invariance is broken. A phenomenological hint (Song and Ma 2025, "rejected at a significance level of 3.1σ") and a null result (Du et al. 2025, "no significant evidence for LIV signatures") conflict within the world pool; the figures do not choose between them, and neither is averaged into the verdict.
- Not that Lorentz invariance is exact at all energies. That would be a convention unless a sealed closure is searched to the end.
- Not a derivation of special relativity, or of a particular dispersion relation, or of a numeric LIV scale. Those are bindings from the physics frame and would need provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).
- Not that relativity is wrong. The symmetry is very well tested; that is why a null result cannot be upgraded into universality.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A GRB lag can be intrinsic emission or vacuum dispersion. What would fire:

- **Threshold Test:** a measurement that separates intrinsic emission lag from propagation lag at the relevant energy scale, so a residual either arrives or does not.
- **Sealed-closure test:** an exhibited closed frame in which exactness could be searched to the end. Without one, "exact" stays a convention.
- **Standing framework falsifiers:** derive symmetry, smoothness or discreteness as a seventh member; admit a direct space-time bond that cannot be refused; show the two sub-questions are a partition. Any of these breaks the shape claim.
- **Falsifier that would end the open question:** a return at every relevant energy, or a demonstrated principle-level obstruction showing no instrument at any resolution could register the dependence. The first grounds a verdict; the second moves the case from unreachable-at-budget to underivable-in-principle and changes the instruction.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say Lorentz invariance is broken, and it does not say it is exact at all energies. It says that a symmetry is not a primitive ingredient: under a six-term substrate it is what a composition leaves behind, and the minimality table makes symmetries depend on the states they range over. So the "origin" of the symmetry is a categorical fact about composition, while the name of the specific symmetry remains a binding from physics, not a derivation. On exactness the answer is an honest stop: modern null searches are real bounds, but "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence," so a finite set of null results cannot close a claim about all energies. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument that separates intrinsic emission lag from propagation effects, not another interpretation of the same lag. The framework offers the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The high-energy end stays open until the ground settles it.
