## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and it cannot name the missing primitive, "because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (read this session): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Also: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For QMGR-0086 the shape is:

> A space-time symmetry is a derivative of a composition, not a member of the substrate; and its exactness at all energies is a ground question that no finite null search closes.

That shape says "a structure sits between the states" and "the detector decides", not "the symmetry is X" and not "it holds to 10^19 GeV".

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The search is steered by the question, which is "a tether and anchor to reality".
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (recorded in exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was cast in effect terms (what composes, what a detector can resolve) before the physics frame was named. That ordering is the discipline; skip it and any field seems to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A Lorentz-violation coefficient becomes a name only when a detector pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (recorded in exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Calling one of them *the* origin of the symmetry turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "exact at all energies" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." Several causes can give one reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A GRB arrival-time lag can be intrinsic emission or vacuum dispersion; the reading does not determine the source. The essay's own conclusion: "An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case.

Here the corresponding error is upgrading a null time-of-flight bound into "exact at all energies". That names a source from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another interpretation of the same lag.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So this essay, and the figures it accompanies, cannot certify their own reading of Lorentz invariance.

## 5. Q: What is the shape here, and what is refused?

**Shape (kept):** a derivative relation between states, plus an instrument requirement. Both are ordinary terms that can be refused.

**Refused as unsupported:** (a) a specific symmetry mechanism as *the* origin; (b) a numeric Lorentz-violation scale as *the* answer; (c) "exact at all energies" as a settled verdict; (d) "Lorentz invariance is broken" on the strength of one phenomenological fit. Items (c) and (d) are opposite claims that share the same error: each turns a reading into a source verdict.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade saying anything specific about the symmetry at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing" not held in Γ. Here the relevant atoms (space, time, energy, structures) are already in the six, and "symmetry" is a leftover of their composition rather than a missing primitive. So saying "a symmetry is a derivative" is within the shape; it is a structural claim, not the invention of a seventh member. What remains forbidden is naming a specific mechanism or scale as though the framework returned it. The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted: a lag still does not determine its source. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names agreeing that Lorentz invariance is exact is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a detector.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of Lorentz invariance and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
