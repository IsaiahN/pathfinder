# Expert verification packet: the Tether on Lorentz invariance

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at any energy is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Under a six-term substrate, composition runs across two kinds (states and structures) and never within one. A symmetry is not a member of that set; it is what a composition leaves behind, so the "origin" of a space-time symmetry is a categorical fact about composition rather than the name of a mechanism. Space and time are independent terms whose symmetry depends on their presence, and no direct state-state bond is licensed. On the second half of the question, a finite set of null Lorentz-violation searches cannot establish exactness at all energies: a null bound is a bound, not a closed room. The verdict there is unreached, and the remedy is a source-discriminating instrument, not a new principle. Four falsifiers are stated below.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** symmetry is a composition leftover, not a primitive member; no direct space-time bond is composable; space and time are independent terms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :124-125 "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." Fig 13 :43-44 "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." Fig 13 :75 "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Fig 12 :92 "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."
- **Refuter:** derive symmetry, smoothness or discreteness as a seventh indivisible member; or show the framework must admit a direct space-time or structure-structure composition it cannot refuse; or show space and time draw on one underlying fact so their agreement carries no information.

### C2 — ground-facing; checkable by a physicist against the test literature

- **Statement:** a null Lorentz-violation bound is not a proof of exactness at all energies; the verdict is unreached, and intrinsic emission lag is a degenerate source of the same reading.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
- **What to check:** Du et al. (2025) report "no significant evidence for LIV signatures in current GRB spectral lag observations" while Song and Ma (2025) report the dispersion-free vacuum hypothesis "rejected at a significance level of 3.1σ or higher." Do these actually conflict at the level of data, or only at the level of modelling intrinsic lag? Would you count either as settling exactness at all energies?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a sealed closure in which the symmetry could be searched to exhaustion, or a source-discriminating measurement that returns the relevant energy dependence at every scale. Either moves R2 from unreached to a grounded verdict.

### C3 — framework-internal with a scope condition; checkable against Figures 8, 9 and 6

- **Statement:** origin and exactness form a union compound with separate loops; the case is unreachable-at-this-budget, not underivable-in-principle, so the instruction is better instruments rather than a new atom.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"; Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 8 :40-41 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **Refuter:** show the two sub-questions are a partition (one condition, not two), or demonstrate that no instrument at any resolution could register the relevant energy dependence, which would move the case to underivable and change the instruction.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that Lorentz invariance is broken, and not that it is exact at all energies. Opposite claims that share the same source-from-reading error.
- Not a derivation of special relativity, a dispersion relation, or a numeric LIV scale. Those are bindings from the physics frame and need provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them.").
- Not that relativity is wrong. The symmetry is extremely well tested; a null result is not a proof of universality precisely because it is a finite search.
- Not a claim about which phenomenological fit wins. The world sources conflict; the figure verdict is about the shape of the question, not the fit.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument was read in producing this packet.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating instrument at the relevant energy scale: does the lag separate into intrinsic emission and propagation components? *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"; the resolution came from a spectrometer, not an argument.
