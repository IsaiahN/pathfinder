**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have checked the shape claim and its refuter against your own knowledge. If the stage-1 output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

**Subject:** One checkable output on Lorentz invariance, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** Two things are usually bundled together: where the space-time symmetry comes from, and whether it holds at every energy. I kept them separate because they are decided by different things.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Under a six-term substrate (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures; composition only across the two kinds), a symmetry is not a primitive member. It is what a composition leaves behind. So "Lorentz invariance" is offered as a derivative, not a floor term, and the framework does not name which specific symmetry or mechanism occupies the slot. The second half of the output is a refusal: no finite set of null Lorentz-violation searches establishes exactness at all energies. A null bound is a bound, not a closed room. The question at Planck-scale energies is unreached, not answered.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this derives special relativity, that it predicts a particular Lorentz-violation scale, that Lorentz invariance is broken, or that it is exact. Those are detector questions. I am also not claiming that relativity is wrong; the symmetry is extremely well tested, and that is exactly why a null result cannot be upgraded into a proof of universality.
>
> **What would refute the output:** (1) deriving symmetry, smoothness or discreteness as a seventh indivisible member of the substrate; (2) showing the framework must admit a direct space-time bond with no structure between; (3) exhibiting a sealed closure in which exactness at all energies could be searched to the end, or a measurement that returns the relevant energy dependence at every scale; (4) showing the two sub-questions are one condition rather than a union of two. I wrote these down before contacting you. If any holds, I will retract the claim.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
