## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question bundles two residuals. Kept separate, never averaged (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").

**R1, origin.** What produces the observed symmetry of space and time under boosts and rotations? Enumerable options: (a) a primitive input, a postulate that stops the regress by stipulation; (b) a derived leftover of a deeper composition; (c) a contingent selection effect among observers. What each leads to: (a) gives a law with no further explanation and no prediction about other symmetries; (b) predicts that symmetries are not members of the substrate and that any symmetry depends on the terms composed; (c) makes exactness contingent on where observers sit, which is a different claim from universality.

**R2, exactness.** Is there an energy at which the symmetry fails? Enumerable options: (a) exact at all energies; (b) broken at a finite scale; (c) unreachable at this budget. What each leads to: (b) is a ground question, settled by an instrument at that scale; (a) is a convention unless a sealed closure can be searched to the end; (c) requires better instruments, not a new atom.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity two, so a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again" and :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Symmetry: the residual itself concerns a symmetry, so "symmetry" is both the subject and a property we must not smuggle into the explanation. Scale: R1 is at the level of what composes; R2 spans all energies, including regimes no current instrument resolves.

**Direction commitment (formula step "Direction").** Written down before the run so it can be refused: *an answer would look like "a space-time symmetry is a derivative composition, not a primitive; and its exactness at all energies is an instrument question that no finite null search closes."* Bind this as an ordinary term `S*`. It accrues a residual like anything else. If a figure line refuses it, the refusal is recorded, not smoothed over.

## 3. Findings, each with a verbatim quote and a refuter

### F1 — A symmetry is a leftover, not a member of the substrate (governs R1, decisive for shape)

Fig 13 :124-125: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."

Fig 13 :43-44: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."

The six are two states-and-structures sets; symmetry is explicitly listed among what a composition *leaves behind*. Under this substrate, the origin of Lorentz invariance cannot be "a seventh member that was always there"; the framework excludes it definitionally. The legitimate `general`-mode statement is: *if space and time compose at all, the symmetry is a derivative of the composition, not a primitive.* This is a shape, not a name of which symmetry arises.

Mode `general` (structural), triangulated across Fig 13 :124-125 and :43-44 (two separate figure lines, low overlap within the figure set).

**Refuter, written now:** derive "symmetry" as a seventh indivisible member of the six-term set, or show a composition that produces no symmetry at any level where the states are present. Either fires and F1 is withdrawn.

### F2 — The symmetry relates distinct terms, not redundant readings of one quantity (governs R1)

Fig 13 :35-37: "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network. Each answers a question the other two cannot: adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."

Fig 13 :38: "Which is Figure 2’s condition applied to the set itself: independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence, and it is what makes their agreement mean anything."

A boost mixes space and time, but they are independent members, not two labels for one underlying fact. So the symmetry is a relation between distinct terms, exactly the kind of cross-kind composition Fig 13 :74 permits. The physics-facing reading (metric, interval) is a binding onto these atoms, not a discovery of a new member.

Fig 13 :62: "no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything." Fig 13 :65: "no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder." The substrate's own minimality table makes symmetries depend on the presence of the states: remove space, spatial symmetry goes; remove time, time-translation symmetry goes. This is the closest the figures come to an "origin" statement, and it is a dependency claim, not a mechanism.

Mode `general` (structural), `specified` where it touches Noether rows, single-source for the specific table (Fig 13), triangulated with F1 for the general point.

**Refuter:** derive a symmetry while removing the state the table says it needs, or show space and time drawing on one underlying fact such that their agreement carries no information.

### F3 — No direct state-state bond; a structure sits between (governs the form of the composition)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

So "spacetime" is not a primitive; it is a derivative with a structure in the slot. A Lorentz symmetry over space and time is therefore a higher-order derivative, not a floor term. Mode `general` (structural), triangulated across Fig 13 :74 and :75.

**Refuter:** admit a direct space-time or structure-structure composition that the framework cannot refuse, or measure one at the ground.

### F4 — Exactness at all energies is a ground question, not an argument (governs R2)

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

Whether the symmetry holds at 10^19 GeV is settled by something that returns a reading at that scale, not by the coherence of a theory. The figure's "anchor" is the detector, never the agreement of physicists. Mode `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 :29-30 and :45.

**Refuter:** produce a closed derivation from the six-term set that fixes exactness at every energy without any instrument reading. That would make the claim `grounded` by construction, which the figures forbid (step 5: "A gate is not the ground").

### F5 — A finite null search cannot establish exactness (governs R2, decisive for the honest answer)

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

Modern LIV searches return bounds, not a proof of exactness. Du et al. (2025) report E_QG,1 >= 4.37 x 10^16 GeV and conclude "no significant evidence for LIV signatures in current GRB spectral lag observations"; that is a null bound, not a closed room. So the Tether verdict on "exact at all energies" is **unreached, not refuted and not confirmed**. Mode `specified`, triangulated across Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44 (two figures, low overlap).

**Refuter:** exhibit a sealed closure in which the symmetry could be searched to exhaustion, or a measurement at every energy (which is the same thing in the limit). Without one, any "exact" claim is a convention, and Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict" applies to agreement about it.

### F6 — Underivable and unreachable-at-budget are different facts (scope note, prevents an error)

Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."

"Exact at all energies" is unreachable-at-this-budget: we lack Planck-scale time-of-flight resolution. It is not underivable-in-principle: a detector at the relevant scale could in principle return a residual. The instruction is therefore better instruments, not a new symmetry principle. Importing an atom against a budget limit is exactly the mistake Fig 8 :42 warns about: "Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed."

Mode `specified`, single-source (Fig 8 :40-43), corroborated by the world source Mattingly (2005) on experimental advances.

**Refuter:** show that no instrument at any resolution could register the relevant energy dependence, which would move the case to underivable-in-principle and change the instruction to "go outside."

### F7 — Instruments extend from worse instruments already returning something (governs the remedy)

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

The extant edge is real: GRB arrival-time lags, vacuum birefringence limits, interferometer and accelerator constraints. The hard problem of *source* (the essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*) matters here: a lag can come from LIV or from intrinsic emission, and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another interpretation of the same lag. Mode `general` on the instrument rule, `specified` on its application here, triangulated across Fig 6 :32-35 and the essay.

**Refuter:** identify a domain where nothing at any resolution returns a reading that could be sharpened, and show the search for a proto-instrument has no edge to extend from.

### F8 — The two sub-questions cannot be averaged; the compound has its own loop

Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"; :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not."

Verdict D1 (symmetry is a derivative, `general`/`specified`) and verdict D2 (exactness open at all energies, `specified`/unreached) are kept in separate columns in `09_watson_answer.md`. A phenomenology paper that finds a hint and a paper that finds a null are not averaged into a "maybe"; they are separate frames ranged against the same ground.

Mode `general`, triangulated across Fig 9 :54-58 and Fig 7 :13.

**Refuter:** show that origin and exactness are a partition (one condition, not two) such that answering one answers the other without a further ground reading.

### F9 — Provenance, not agreement, decides what the world sources mean

Fig 8 :50-52: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

The Einstein 1905 source and the SEP essay are records of a **postulate** being installed (D4, no anchor). Mattingly and Kostelecky-Russell are records of **tests** (D3, anchor = experiment). They look like they are saying the same thing about "Lorentz invariance," but one has no ground and the other does. Treating all four as agreeing witnesses would be one evidence pool wearing four names.

Mode `specified`, single-source for the figure line, applied as a discipline to the ledger.

**Refuter:** show that a postulate frame and a detector frame draw on genuinely independent evidence about the same residual, in which case their agreement would be corroboration at maximum independence rather than one pool.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (before the conclusion, as required)

Required by the discipline ("a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument"). The shape claim `S*` is refused if any of the following holds:

1. Symmetry, smoothness or discreteness is derived as a seventh indivisible member of the six-term set, or a composition is shown that produces none at any level where the states are present (kills F1).
2. A space-time or structure-structure composition is admitted that the framework cannot refuse (kills F3).
3. A closed derivation fixes exactness at every energy without an instrument reading, or a sealed closure is exhibited that can be searched to the end (kills F4 and F5).
4. The two sub-questions are shown to be a partition, not a union compound (kills F8).
5. A source-discriminating instrument returns a reading that separates LIV from intrinsic emission at the relevant scale, which would convert R2 from `unreached` to a grounded verdict either way.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn in the retraction log below and the final answer is reissued. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
