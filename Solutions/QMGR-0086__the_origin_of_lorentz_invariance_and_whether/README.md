## Restated as an effect

Two decisions are bundled in the question. **Origin:** is the observed space-time symmetry a primitive input, a derived leftover of a deeper composition, or a contingent selection effect? Each option leads somewhere different: a postulate stops at a stipulation; a derivative predicts symmetries are not members of the substrate; a selection effect makes exactness contingent. **Exactness:** is there an energy at which the symmetry fails, is it exact at all energies, or is the question beyond the reach of current instruments? A broken symmetry is settled by measurement; an exactness claim is a convention unless a sealed closure is available; an unreachable-at-budget question needs better instruments, not a new principle.

**What an answer would look like (formula step "Direction"):** a shape, namely "a space-time symmetry is a composition leftover, and its exactness at all energies is an instrument question that a finite null search cannot close." This is bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0.
