# Outreach draft — check the shape before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four slots are separated and the anchors are named in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 3 and 5. If the output cannot survive on its own, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one output about multilingual development before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a claim about what any answer to "how does growing up multilingual influence attention, memory, identity and social understanding" must look like. It has four parts.
>
> 1. **There is no single bilingual advantage.** A residual is indexed by the thing being tracked, not averaged across things. The framework states: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* So attention, memory, identity and social understanding get four separate verdicts. A global effect near zero is a legal state even when one slot is live.
> 2. **What changes is contact, not the child in isolation.** The framework states: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* "Growing up multilingual" is a change in habitat, interlocutors and contexts. The comparison that matters is between children whose contact differs, not between children whose labels differ. The sharpest available natural check is the bimodal case: speakers of a spoken language plus a signed language do not have to select between competing languages, and in the published flanker comparison they performed like monolinguals rather than like spoken-language bilinguals.
> 3. **The four slots need different anchors.** For attention and memory an instrument is the right anchor. Because the identity question is constitutively about a person, structured human judgment is the right anchor there, not a task score and not a consensus. The framework states: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."*
> 4. **The mechanisms are candidates, not a merged cause.** Executive function, metalinguistic awareness and sociolinguistic awareness are written over the same atom set with different bonds, and different children may reach the same task through different routes. The literature itself reports that language ability predicts social understanding in some bilingual groups while executive function predicts it in others.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not claiming a bilingual advantage, a bilingual deficit, a critical period, or a specific identity outcome. I am not claiming that the framework names which mechanism operates for a given child. It returns a shape, not a name. I am not claiming any effect size is settled: the best meta-analysis I found (152 studies, 891 effect sizes) reports that the small advantages disappeared after publication-bias correction.
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. a single aggregate factor predicting held-out outcomes better than the four-slot decomposition, with no quiet slot hidden by the average;
> 2. the same per-slot effect for children whose contact does not differ as for children whose contact does;
> 3. identity settled by a task score alone, or attention settled by self-report alone, with the swapped readings agreeing;
> 4. the three mechanisms reducing to one latent factor with no interaction by language history;
> 5. a pre-registered, independent, low-overlap sample returning a nonzero per-slot effect after correction (this one would not kill the shape, it would close a gap I am openly reporting).
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any of the first four holds, I will withdraw the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures and the formula are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

## What was deliberately withheld

- No claim that bilingualism is good or bad. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*).
- No mechanism named as *the* mechanism. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 13 :135).
- No personal emails, no receptiveness ranking, no bulk-send plan. Public opt-in venues only (Fig 2 :40, Fig 9 :60).
- No pool of agreeing studies presented as corroboration (Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*).
