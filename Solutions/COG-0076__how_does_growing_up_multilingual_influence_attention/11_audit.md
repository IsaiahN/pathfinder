## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :39-40 (capability is agent-and-habitat), Fig 2 :36-37 (person-level anchor), Fig 9 :54-58 (union), Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: four slots, contact not label, instruments for attention and memory, persons for identity, three competing routes, open directions.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an empirical study design that would test the claim: a composite bilingualism score, a single task battery, and a headline "bilingual children perform better".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down into the concrete design:

- **per-slot indexing:** the design collapses four slots into a composite;
- **contact over label:** the design recruits by self-reported bilingual status rather than by contact structure, losing the bimodal discriminator;
- **anchor legitimacy:** the design treats a task score as an identity measure;
- **route competition:** the design measures one mechanism and treats it as the cause.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be tracked per slot rather than averaged, and the contact question is answerable by a design. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
