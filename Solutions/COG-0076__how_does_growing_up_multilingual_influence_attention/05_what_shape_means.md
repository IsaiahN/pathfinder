## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can fill it

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For COG-0076, a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer to "how does growing up multilingual influence attention, memory, identity and social understanding" that can be stated before any effect size is measured. It says:

- four slots, not one score;
- contact and habitat, not an isolated agent upgrade;
- question-specific anchors, with persons as the anchor where the question is about a person;
- competing mechanisms over a shared atom set, not a merged cause.

It does **not** say which direction any effect goes, how large it is, or which mechanism fires for a given child. Those are names and measurements, and they are gaps in `01` section 6.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*; :31 *"rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the outreach in `02` describes an expected pattern per slot instead of a mechanism story.

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (a condition M acting on four tracked slots) **before** any frame was chosen. Only then were the cognitive-science frames opened. Had the frames been chosen first, Fig 9 :28 *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* would have made the fit inevitable.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A per-slot effect direction is licensed by an instrument, not by a derivation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming metalinguistic awareness as a candidate mechanism is a provenanced import from the developmental literature; naming it as *the* mechanism is not licensed.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from canonical mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The candidate mechanisms are a visible guess by the field, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why a source-naming error is the specific risk here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the reading is compatible with one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The multilingualism record shows the same error in a different costume. A false-belief score (the reading) is compatible with an executive-function source, a metalinguistic-awareness source, a sociolinguistic-awareness source, or a combination (the sources). Yu et al. 2021 report that studies measuring one account rarely measure the others. Naming executive function as *the* source from the task score is the metamerism error: several sources give the reading, and the reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q2 — What licenses a particular mechanism or a particular direction? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism or direction is licensed only by a ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this solution commit the error the README forbids?** No, and here is the exact boundary. In `01`, executive function, metalinguistic awareness and sociolinguistic awareness are introduced as **candidates drawn from the literature**, with the literature named and the overlap declared. They are not presented as terms the framework derived, and none is selected. The framework's own contributions are the four-slot split, the contact requirement, the anchor rule and the union/isomer reading. Those are structural, not empirical.

## 5b. Correction to my own first reading (kept visible)

I first wrote the blanket sentence: "The framework returns a shape, not a name, so this solution may not name any mechanism." That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Fig 13 :135 confirms it: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

The three candidate mechanisms are **not** missing primitives. They are terms already circulating in the target literature and imported with provenance. Naming them as candidates is permitted. What remains forbidden is (a) presenting them as framework-derived, (b) selecting one as the answer, and (c) naming a direction for a slot before an instrument settles it. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reading still does not determine the source, and a discriminator is still owed. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous bilingualism researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle each slot.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any claim about multilingual children and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
