## Boundary and standing of this work

Nothing in this folder is in the Tether repository. No repository file was read for the purpose of editing and no repository file was changed. Figure quotes come from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, the verbatim extract with line numbers, cited as `Fig N :line`. The formula, the canonical figures README and the metalaws note were read locally. World sources were opened on 2026-11-22 and are recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

**This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output can *"simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the empirical question it reports on. It states the shape any answer must have, the anchors each sub-question needs, and what would refute it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Many studies agreeing inside one evidence pool is one witness with many names.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in and is positioned to refute the shape claim, not a list of people already inclined to believe it.
