## 3. What the wider literature adds that the figures do not

1. **The discriminator is real and physical, not a metaphor.** Bose et al. and Marletto and Vedral independently give the same inference: entanglement implies a nonclassical mediator. This is low-overlap corroboration for Fig 2's anchor reading (F1) and Fig 6's closure argument (F3), because neither paper draws on the framework.
2. **The inference is contested inside physics.** Huggett et al. call the witness claim equivocal; Marchese et al. generate the same entanglement under classical Newtonian time evolution. This is a genuine world-source conflict. Per the WATSON rule it sends the derivation back to the census, where the observation, the shape and the name were separated (F2). It never flips the figure verdict.
3. **Status is a budget fact, not an absence proof.** The 2026 rotor result shows the entanglement-to-decoherence ratio improving toward the required regime, which is what "unreached at this budget" looks like operationally (F7). GQuEST is a second tabletop route with a different target, which supports the scale correction in F11.

## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the shape claim, the exclusion condition and the refuter. Do not send the full architecture first.
- **Fig 10 :42**  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**  -  *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them the papers.
- **Fig 2 :35**  -  *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as evidence.
- **Fig 2 :40**  -  *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* All the framework essays share an author with the figures. Their agreement is one pool talking, which is why the physics papers lead the source list.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure, and record the verdict; (3) only then the three public paper links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer, and the third stage

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22  -  *"Only methods cross up."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter, per the formula's "Direction" section: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Refuter 1 in `01` F5 is currently live in the literature, so the honest stage-1 output is the *conditional* shape claim, not an unconditional one.
