## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The residual is a **missing discriminating observation**. Two frames make the same predictions for every tabletop phenomenon already measured, but differ on one unobserved quantity: whether the gravitational interaction can transmit quantum information between two quantum systems.

- **Question it is a residual of:** can gravity entangle quantum systems?
- **What is being decided:** whether the mediator of the gravitational interaction has at least one nonclassical degree of freedom, so that two initially separable masses can become entangled through gravity alone.
- **Arity:** two object slots, the two masses and their embedded spins. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* There is no single global "is gravity quantum" reading; there is a correlation between two specific slots.
- **Symmetry:** the Newtonian interaction is symmetric under exchange of the two masses. Fig 12 :23: *"this bond is symmetric, so the four are one term."* Swapping the masses does not produce a new substance. The entanglement witness is likewise swap-invariant. This matters for F6.
- **Scale:** mesoscopic, roughly micron masses at micron separation in the original proposals, with milligram-scale mechanical control now demonstrated. This is nowhere near the Planck scale. Scale is a separate quantity from arity and symmetry; conflating it with "quantum gravity" is a public misstatement, addressed in F11.
- **What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula "Direction"):** `W = (spin correlation above threshold) AND (every classical channel excluded)`. Until both conjuncts are observed, the sought-for shape is a preference, not a term, and cannot accrue a residual.

Two outcomes and what each leads to:

| outcome | what it leads to |
|---|---|
| Entanglement observed with classical channels excluded | Import of a new atom into the physics frame: gravity is a quantum-capable channel. The framework gives a shape, not a name of a theory. |
| No entanglement observed | The classical-gravity frame is left standing but is **not** settled, because the experiment may simply have failed to reach the regime (Fig 8 :39). |

## 3. Findings F1 to F12

### F1  -  The experiment is a genuine anchor, and agreement is not one (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29-30:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."**

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

The spin-correlation readout is an anchor: it is a measurement, and it does not move when a theorist changes their preferred interpretation. Theorists' agreement about what a witness would mean is not an anchor. This question is therefore unusually well positioned: the ground is a real instrument, not a committee. Corroborated at low overlap by Bose et al. (2017), which specifies *"a prescription for witnessing this entanglement... through simple correlation measurements between two spins"* (physics, independent of the framework author). Status: **triangulated** across Fig 2 and the physics literature.

**Refuter:** show that the spin-correlation readout is adjusted to agree with the prediction being tested, so that the anchor updates on the frame. Then it is panel 1 of Fig 2, not panel 3.

### F2  -  The witness is a filter, not the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 9 :6-8 separates the verdict, the lookup and the narrowing: *"1 · the verdict: is φ\* in here at all? 2 · the lookup: which frame holds it? 3 · the narrowing: which one is it?"*

An entanglement threshold is a filter. It narrows the space of frames that can still describe the world: a frame with only a local classical channel is eliminated once entanglement is observed with that channel excluded. But the filter does not deliver the name of the theory. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities,"* and using it to describe the witness threshold is a **binding** from the physics frame, not a derivation from the framework.

Three distinct outputs must not be collapsed:

1. the observation (spin correlation above threshold);
2. the shape (a nonclassical gravitational channel exists);
3. the name (gravitons, a specific quantum gravity theory).

The experiment can deliver 1. With assumptions stated, it licenses 2. It does not deliver 3. This is why the public phrase "tabletop test of quantum gravity" needs the qualification in F11.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a case where the filter itself decides the verdict, so that crossing the threshold entails a unique theory. Under Fig 9 this cannot happen, so the refuter would be a figure re-census, not a new measurement.

### F3  -  The quantum-channel atom is not derivable inside classical gravitational closure (triangulated)

Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :14 names *"the only operator that moves the wall"* as IMPORT. Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*

A frame containing only the Newtonian potential, classical trajectories and local operations cannot compose its way to "the mediator can transmit quantum information." No amount of refining that frame adds the atom. The atom must arrive from a frame whose closure differs, and here it arrives with an instrument: the experiment is the OUTWARD lookup. Marletto and Vedral (2017) state the general theorem at low overlap with the framework: *"any system (e.g. a field) capable of mediating entanglement between two quantum systems must itself be quantum."* Bose et al. (2017) state the same discriminator as *"the principle that two objects cannot be entangled without a quantum mediator."*

**Correction recorded:** the framework's six already include network (*"that entities relate, and influence travels"*), so the framework itself does not need a new primitive. The import is into the **physics frame**, and the record must show the source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13: *"source gate: provenance"*, *"shadow test: explains a gap"*). Saying "the Tether imports the quantum mediator" would be wrong twice: it adds no framework atom, and it skips provenance.

**Refuter:** derive the quantum-channel property from classical Newtonian dynamics plus local operations with no new assumption. Marchese et al. (2025) come close to firing this refuter and are treated as live in F5, not smoothed over.

### F4  -  The residual is per mass and per condition; averaging destroys it (single-source in the figures, corroborated by method)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The witness is a correlation between two slots. Reporting only a global visibility, or averaging over many uncontrolled noise sources, throws away the only signal that says where to look. Symbols table: R *"is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

**Refuter:** show a protocol whose readout is a single global scalar with no per-slot decomposition, and which still identifies which channel mediated the correlation. If none exists, the per-slot rule stands.

### F5  -  "Entanglement observed" is a union compound, and the second condition is currently unmeasured (single-source for the figure split; world-confirmed compound)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

Unbundle `W` into:

- C1: two masses become entangled through the interaction under study;
- C2: every classical channel between the masses is excluded.

A lever that improves coherence clears C1 but leaves C2 binding. This is exactly the live difficulty in the field. Marchese, Plávala, Kleinmann and Nimmrichter (2025) report that *"one can generate the same amount of entanglement in this setup by using classical time evolution given by Newton's laws of motion,"* so the same readout can arise without the intended inference. Huggett, Linnemann and Schneider (2022) conclude that *"the positive claim of witness is equivocal"* and that it *"decisively depends on which out of two legitimate modelling paradigms one finds oneself in."*

These are not reasons to abandon the experiment. They are reasons to refuse the shortcut from the observation to the name. The figures' own dependency rule applies: effort above the break cannot rescue the link below it (Fig 3 :19).

**Refuter:** produce a single measurement that simultaneously delivers C1 and C2 without a separate exclusion argument. If the exclusion is always a separate argument, the compound reading stands.

### F6  -  Enumerate the habitat; the symmetry of the bond is what makes the inference work (triangulated)

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. The habitat here includes: electromagnetic patch potentials, Casimir-Polder forces, blackbody and thermal decoherence, seismic and tidal gradients, laser phase noise, and the measurement back-action itself. Bose et al. (2017) explicitly design the geometry so the masses are *"placed far apart enough to keep Casimir-Polder forces at bay"*, which is an enumeration move, not a derivation. The 2026 milligram-rotor result (Mishra et al.) reports *"inferred entanglement-to-decoherence rate ratios nearly two orders of magnitude beyond the state of the art for tests of gravity at the quantum-classical interface"*, which is habitat work: it changes contact, so it changes capability (Fig 11: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*).

Fig 12 :23: *"this bond is symmetric, so the four are one term."* The gravitational interaction and the witness are both symmetric under exchange of the two masses. So a claimed asymmetry between the masses is a signature of a non-gravitational channel, and a useful diagnostic inside the enumeration.

**Refuter:** find a second-order cascade actor that was omitted from the enumeration and that alone reproduces the correlation. The DDT case is the precedent for why such actors matter.

### F7  -  This is unreached at this budget, not unreachable in principle (triangulated)

Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

There is an edge. Matter-wave interferometry, optomechanics and spin readout already return unresolved readings in exactly this regime: Bose et al. propose two micron-size masses in adjacent interferometers; Mishra et al. (2026) demonstrate a suspended milligram rotor with a damping rate of only 7 nHz and conditional entropic temperatures as low as 140 nK; GQuEST (Vermeulen et al., 2025) uses tabletop Michelson interferometers with photon counting. So the instrument can be improved from a worse instrument already returning something. Fig 8 :40-43 keeps the two facts apart:

> **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions. Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."**

Verdict: the witness is **underivable** inside classical gravitational closure (F3), but **unreached** at the current instrument budget. Importing a new theory atom to fix a budget limit would be the error Fig 8 prices above starting from primitives.

**Refuter:** show that no resolution of any existing instrument could ever return the two-slot correlation, i.e. that there is no edge to extend from. The 2026 rotor result is evidence against that.

### F8  -  A null result is not a proof that gravity is classical (triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 5 amendment: a refusal is owed when a held term is false, not when a gap is merely empty; *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*

If the witness does not fire, the correct statement is that the compound `W` was not achieved. It does not identify which conjunct failed. The classical frame is therefore **not** confirmed; it is simply not yet refuted at this instrument. Reading a null as "gravity is classical" converts an unreached budget into an absence proof, which Fig 8 forbids. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*

**Refuter:** exhibit a sealed frame, i.e. a closed set with a complete search, in which a null result does exclude the atom. No tabletop experiment is such a room.

### F11  -  Scale is a separate quantity; the word "quantum gravity" is being used too loosely (re-census, no strain)

Re-census of §0: arity is two slots, symmetry is an exchange-symmetric bond, scale is mesoscopic. The six-term set is domain-agnostic across scale (Fig 4: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*), so Fig 13 applies at any scale. What fails is the public statement, not the derivation. The experiment probes **quantum properties of the gravitational interaction in the low-energy, non-relativistic regime**. It does not probe Planck-scale spacetime structure. GQuEST, for example, uses *"tabletop-scale Michelson laser interferometers to probe for fluctuations in space-time"* within specific models; that is a different target from the two-mass entanglement witness.

After re-census there is no strain: scale was never the same quantity as arity or symmetry. The correction is to wording, and it is kept visible here rather than silently adopted.

**Refuter:** show that the two-mass witness at micron separation is sensitive to the Planck scale in a way not mediated by a model assumption. If the sensitivity always arrives through a model, the scale distinction stands.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: Seat Map :287  -  *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim, *a positive two-slot witness with classical channels excluded licenses the shape 'gravity is a quantum-capable channel'*, is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **Discriminator failure.** A classical local channel, electromagnetic, Casimir or otherwise, is shown to reproduce the same spin correlations in the exact experimental geometry while the claimed gravitational contribution is absent. This is the Marchese et al. challenge in its strongest form.
2. **Exclusion failure.** A positive result is reported while a second-order habitat actor (Fig 11 :52) remains in contact and unexplained, so that C2 of F5 is not actually satisfied.
3. **Aggregation failure.** The reported signal is shown to be a global average with no per-slot decomposition, so Fig 1 :19 is breached and the live slot was never isolated.
4. **Ground failure.** The spin-correlation readout is found to have been tuned to the predicted answer, so the anchor moved with the frame (Fig 2 :35).
5. **Figure re-census.** A verbatim figure line is found that makes an entanglement threshold a name-bearing verdict rather than a filter (reversing Fig 9 :60).

If any fires, the claim is narrowed or withdrawn, and this file is updated rather than silently rewritten. Refuter 1 is **live in the literature today**, which is why the verdict is `specified` and not stronger.
