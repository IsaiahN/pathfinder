# Expert verification packet  -  the Tether on tabletop gravity entanglement tests

**What this is:** a claim in a form an experimentalist or quantum-information theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`  -  not `grounded`.** No tabletop experiment has returned the two-slot correlation with classical channels excluded. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The spin-correlation readout in a two-mass entanglement witness is the anchor, so agreement among theorists about what it means is not evidence. The witness is a filter, not a verdict: a correlation above threshold, with every classical channel between the masses excluded, licenses only the shape "the gravitational mediator has at least one nonclassical degree of freedom". It does not license a theory name. The observation is a union of two conditions, `C1 correlation above threshold` and `C2 classical channels excluded`, and C2 is the live bottleneck: classical Newtonian time evolution can generate the same entanglement, so the correlation alone does not discriminate. A null result is not an absence proof. The experiment is unreached at the current budget, not unreachable in principle, because existing interferometers and mechanical rotors already return unresolved readings in this regime.

### C2  -  physics-facing; checkable against the experiment and the exclusion argument

- **Statement:** the observation is a union compound. C1 alone is insufficient; classical Newtonian time evolution reproduces the same amount of entanglement (Marchese et al., 2025, PRA 111, 042202), and the witness claim is called equivocal by Huggett et al. - **What to check:** in a proposed or performed experiment, is C2 established by an independent argument with stated bounds, or merely asserted from the geometry? Is there an exchange-asymmetry test between the two masses? The gravitational interaction is exchange-symmetric (Fig 12 :23, *"this bond is symmetric, so the four are one term"*), so an observed asymmetry flags a non-gravitational actor.
- **Refuter:** produce a single measurement that delivers both C1 and C2 without a separate exclusion argument; or show that the cited classical-channel result does not apply to the geometry in question. If the latter holds, this sub-claim is narrowed accordingly.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that gravity is quantum, and not that it is classical on a null result.
- Not a Planck-scale test, not a graviton detection, not a theory of quantum gravity. Those are names from other frames; Danielson et al. (2022) argue the graviton version, and that is recorded as a world-source tension, not adopted here.

1. **Threshold (C1):** does the two-spin correlation cross the witness threshold in the intended geometry?
2. **Exclusion (C2):** with the habitat enumerated to second order (electromagnetic patch potentials, Casimir-Polder, blackbody and thermal decoherence, tidal gradients, measurement back-action), does the correlation survive? This is the live bottleneck.
