## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill.

For this question the exemplar's `SPACE(???)TIME` has a direct analogue. Write the sought-for answer as an ordinary term (formula, "Direction"):

```
W = (spin correlation above threshold) AND (every classical channel excluded)
```

`W` is a shape. `W = gravity is quantum and the mediator is the graviton field of theory X` is a name. The whole discipline of this solution is to keep `W` unfilled until the ground pays for it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the thing. *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* With each new term, *"you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email leads with a refuter rather than a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: describe the residual as a two-slot correlation with an exclusion requirement, not as "quantum gravity". Then the frame that already predicts its shape (quantum information theory) is recognisable, and *"matching is a one-pass check, not a search"* (Fig 9 :29). Pick the frame first and any frame will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, a name would require the two-slot witness **plus** the habitat exclusion argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (2026-09-06) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. In this question the temptation is to name the mediator as "quantum gravity" from a correlation reading. That is a source named from a projection.

## 4. The metamerism point, and the horizon that makes it honest

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, so *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the projection is the spin correlation. Several sources can produce it: a quantum-capable gravitational mediator (the intended one), an unmodelled electromagnetic or Casimir channel, or classical Newtonian time evolution as Marchese et al. (2025) show. The correlation alone cannot comment on which. What is owed is a **better discriminator**, which is exactly the exclusion argument in `W`, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16) adds the stopping rule: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* And: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* So the field's current inability to discriminate is a status report, not a proof that gravity is classical. This is the essay-level version of Fig 8 :39.

The same essay's own `SPACE(???)TIME` passage (*"a testable theory"*, *"we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered"*) is the precedent for keeping `(???)`, and for not inventing an order. Here the analogous ignorance is which physical channel actually mediated the correlation until C2 is measured.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (carried forward from the exemplar's discipline)

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade saying anything about the mediator. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in Γ. The framework's six already hold **network** (*"that entities relate, and influence travels"*), so the structural form of a mediator is not missing. What is missing is a **property of a particular physical channel in a different frame**: whether the gravitational network is quantum-capable.

Therefore:

- The rule does not ban naming the mediator in the physics frame. It bans (a) claiming the framework derived that name, and (b) claiming the observation delivers a full theory.
- The README's *"a shape, not a name"* applies here by analogy to the **physics frame's closure**: what the experiment returns is the shape `nonclassical channel`, not the name `graviton` or any specific theory. Danielson et al. (2022) argue the stronger version, that *"Newtonian entanglement implies the existence of graviton entanglement."* That is a claim from a different frame with its own derivation. It is recorded, not adopted as the figure verdict.
- My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: naming a source from a reading that several sources can produce is still the error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a general ban on naming held atoms.

Both readings are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of distinguished people who already agree with a quantum-gravity interpretation is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the experiment a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The canonical article is blunter: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula,"* and LLM products must be seen as *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. The real test is the instrument, whose current status is *"unreached at this budget"*, not *"unreachable in principle."*
