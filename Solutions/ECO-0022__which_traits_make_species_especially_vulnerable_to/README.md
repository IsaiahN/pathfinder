# Solution ECO-0022

**Question:** Which traits make species especially vulnerable to extinction in rapidly changing environments?

**Source:** ecology.txt line 29 (question 22 in the source file).

**Section:** 3. Species loss, extinction, and community change.

**Field for public contact points:** extinction vulnerability research.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain ecological claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal standing list of traits, not `grounded`. Every substantive file is labelled a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

## What this package answers, in effect terms

The question asks for a standing list of traits that make a species vulnerable. Restated as an effect: for each species-slot s, the residual R_s is the gap between predicted persistence under a trait-and-environment model and observed persistence when the environment moves. The figures refuse a standing list, because the valence of a trait is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51). What survives derivation is a separator, not a roster: vulnerability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), it is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), it is a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), and the candidates are small library and closure (D1), narrowed habitat contact and severed relations (D2), a non-updating ground whose channel decays (D3), and a generational loop that runs slower than the moving optimum (D4). Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged into a universal percentage.

The literal list is a declared `gap`: no figure line names which traits occupy the slots, and Fig 11 :51 structurally refuses the request for a standing list. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (with the leading clause *"disqualifying for a verdict"*). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
3. **Do not ask for trust before a check.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
