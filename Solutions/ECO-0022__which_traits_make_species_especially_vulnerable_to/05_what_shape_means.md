## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* **Fig 9 :32:** *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The figure extract states the corresponding decidability limit: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0022 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name a universal trait list. It constrains: it says the answer must be agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), per-slot (Fig 1 :19), a union (Fig 9 :55), and residual-relative (Fig 11 :49). It does not fill: it does not say "large body size" or "low dispersal" is the answer. A four-part separator is a shape; a roster of traits is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- **Fig 6 :30:** *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The residual was described in effect terms in `01` §0 before any figure line was chosen: R_s is a per-species gap between predicted and observed persistence.
- **Fig 9 :30-31:** *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* (spans two lines in the extract). Applied here: describe "contact with a non-updating ground" rather than "habitat quality," because the latter is a term borrowed from the ecology frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what the checker would expect under residual-relative valence.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* with the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In this question the finish line is the separator, and starting there is exactly what the refuter does.

## 3. What licenses a concrete trait name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A per-species persistence measurement that survives a habitat substitution would license a name for that species-slot. The canonical limit says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (**Fig 6 :23:** *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with a source gate and a shadow test. A trait coefficient imported from a macroecological regression must be marked as a binding from that frame, with its provenance and overlap declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six-term substrate in this derivation is a visible guess applied across scale, not a discovery of ecology's own list (Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*).

## 4. Why the standing-list reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The standing-list reading makes the same error. A pooled correlation between body size and extinction is a reading of the artifact; naming "large body size" as the vulnerability factor is naming a source from a projection, when several mechanisms (area requirement, low reproductive rate, trophic position, slow adaptation) could give the same correlation. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the species-slot, not a roster. Cardillo et al. 2005 corroborate the need: the same body-size variable changes its driver composition above a threshold, which a single pooled coefficient would hide.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is why this folder cannot certify its own shape.

## 5. Correction to my own §3: the "shape, not a name" limit is narrower than I first wrote it

I initially treated the limit as forbidding the naming of any concrete trait. That overstates it. The canonical phrase has a subject: *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Body size, dispersal capacity, generation time, range size, and diet breadth are all already observable terms in the ecology frame; naming them is a **binding** from that frame onto the derivation, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is treating such a binding as an import that moves the wall. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The standing-list reading still names a source from a reading without an instrument that discriminates sources. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming available terms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This correction is the ecological twin of the one in the imported exemplar's `05` §5b, and it is preserved visibly for the same reason.

## 6. Consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From the essay opened directly: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known reviewers agreeing that a trait matters is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the separator a budget to be tried and let a ground, a census, a substitution experiment, settle it.

**Selection with no maintainer.** The same essay: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector."* This is the precise figure-parallel for the unmaintained habitat mode (Fig 11 :56). Conservation adds the maintained mode (Fig 11 :58), but does not replace the underlying selection process.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From the essay opened directly: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any extinction claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What shape means for ECO-0022, in one paragraph

The shape is a four-domain separator with per-domain verdicts: library and closure, habitat contact, the non-updating ground, and the generational channel. It says that vulnerability is a relation, not a stock; that trait valence flips with the residual; that the habitat is enumerated and contact cascades; that a pooled macroecological correlation is a budget filter and not a per-species witness; and that a Red List category is a frame reading while persistence is the ground. It does not say which concrete traits occupy the slots or what their effect sizes are. That is a `gap`, reported as a verdict and not as silence. The missing instrument is a source-discriminating, per-species persistence measurement under a documented habitat substitution. Until one is run, the result is `candidate`, not `grounded`.
