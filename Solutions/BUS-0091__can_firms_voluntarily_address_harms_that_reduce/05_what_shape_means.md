## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **A shape:** a harm-reducing action is durable only if the harm is reachable in the firm's own loop and something keeps ground pressure on the decision. The institution that supplies those two conditions stays marked `???`.
- **Names** (withheld): "regulation", "consumer pressure", "investor incentives", "purpose-driven firm". Those are bindings from the business frame onto the shape, not derivations.

The question as posed already names three candidates and asks us to pick. That is the inversion Fig 9 :27-28 warns about: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly the case here. The two conditions (reachability, pressure) were conflated into one "voluntary or regulated?" question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A measured harm trajectory that persists after pressure is withdrawn would name the condition.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "regulation" is licensed only as a provenanced binding from the institutional frame, not as a derivation from the six terms.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring that "investor incentives" are the answer makes a choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. Why the question's own framing is a source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* Several paths can create the same reading: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a firm's emissions curve (the reading) does not determine its source (conviction, mandate, customer loss, or capital cost). Fig 8 :50 says the same thing in framework terms: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. What is owed is the provenance record and, where the cause matters, a discriminator. A public statement of "we chose to do this" is a self-report from inside the frame, not an instrument.

## 5. Q2, answered: what licenses the word "voluntary"?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. "Voluntary" is a claim about the origin of an action, and origin is precisely what a harm trajectory cannot show. It is licensed only by a record with a source gate: who decided, under what constraint, with what violated condition attached. Until such a record exists, the adjective is the speaker's own contribution and must be marked as such.

**Does the question commit the forbidden error?** Yes, in both directions. It asks us to choose among three names before the shape is described (the Fig 9 inversion), and it treats "voluntary" as observable from outcomes (the metamerism error). The correct form is: *a harm reduction is durable when the harm is reachable in the firm's loop and pressure stays on the room; which institution supplies those two conditions is a provenanced binding, not part of the shape.*

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of respected business-ethics authorities who agree that regulation is required is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried against a ground, and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is simulacra. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the business question and must be labelled as such. *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

## 7. A correction carried from the Ostrom re-census

The same RSI essay contains a first-person provenance record: the author encountered Elinor Ostrom's work on communities managing shared resources without central authority and wrote, *"The paper I derived and hers were not a carbon copy. They rhymed."* That is relevant to BUS-0091 because it is exactly the low-overlap import used in `01` F8. Two cautions follow, and both are recorded rather than smoothed over:

1. The framework author's account of the convergence is a **framework-internal provenance claim**, not independent corroboration that the mapping is right. It carries maximal overlap with the figures.
2. The figure-level invariant in `01` F5 was still sharpened by actually reading Ostrom this session (publiccommons.ca excerpts, 2026-11-22). The sharpening is from the source, not from the essay. The correction stands: the necessary condition is reachability plus pressure, not the name "regulation".
