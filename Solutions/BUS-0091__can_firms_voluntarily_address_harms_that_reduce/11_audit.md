- the **institution is not named**. The framework returns the function (reachability plus pressure), not "regulation". Turning the shorthand into a mandate is the naming error the README forbids.
- the **union is flattened**. A single lever applied to C1 and C2 leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :56). The shorthand says "both conditions"; the concrete answer often keeps only one.
- the **frequency limit is dropped**. "Generally" is a `gap` in the abstraction and silently becomes a verdict in the concretisation.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an unpriced harm produces no live signal in the firm's own loop, and a change without pressure decays into agreement. That is the residue worth sending. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
