## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that firms can voluntarily address harms that reduce short-term profit, or that durable change generally requires regulation, consumer pressure or investor incentives. Option A: treat the three listed pressures as three rival mechanisms and pick one. Option B: describe the residual first, split the union, and report the conditions each pressure supplies. What each leads to: A names an institution before the shape is described, so any frame picked will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28), and it turns an empirical frequency into a verdict; B returns a reachability-plus-pressure shape, four named instances rather than three, five written refuters, and a declared `gap` for "generally".

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the harm's actual trajectory and what the firm's loop registers, ordered by time (durability). Arity: three-place (firm Γ, action a, harm h) per slot and time. Symmetry: ordered, because durability is a sequence, with a comparison bond for "reduces short-term profit". Scale: single firm loop, industry field, wider habitat; the membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4). Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1 Firm loop.** Actors: decision makers, routines, Γ. Currency: profit, actions, residual reduction. Anchor: realized market return (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D2 Harm's ground.** Actors: emissions, water, workers, communities, future generations. Currency: harm level, exposure, health. Anchor: a measurement, a counter, a patient outcome (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D3 Pressure instruments.** Actors: regulators, consumers, investors, courts, auditors. Currency: mandates, purchases, capital, liability. Anchor: a rule that can be violated and a ground reading (Fig 10 :19). Admitted.
- **D4 Verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **X1 Pure corporate benevolence as free-floating property.** No anchor independent of what the ground returns. Not a domain yet, excluded before any figure line is applied.
- **X2 Shareholder value as sole cause.** Already inside D1 and D3; absorbed, not separate.

Union, not partition: D1/D2 overlap at the point of production and registration of the harm; D3/D4 overlap where the instrument is read by the verifier. Order by dependency: perception of the harm → terms for the harm → objective for the harm → plan → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Strain test applied: the "no direct link" rule in the six-term set governs primitives, not institutions; no figure was forced to name an institution. After the Ostrom re-census, the strain moved from "regulation required" to "reachability plus pressure", and is recorded in `01` F8.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and is sharper than the question's framing. A firm can voluntarily reduce a profit-reducing harm only when that harm is already reachable in its own loop, and the reduction is durable only while something keeps ground pressure on the decision. Regulation, consumer pressure and investor incentives are three names for the import that supplies both; self-organised community governance is a fourth, so no single institution is the framework's answer. "Voluntary" is a provenance claim that a harm trajectory cannot settle. Whether such change *generally* requires pressure is a `gap`, not a hidden verdict. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with five written falsifiers.

- **cost if wrong:** a firm failing reachability would be sold a pledge campaign when it needs a harm instrument, and a firm failing pressure would be told to import more when it needs a violable condition. The union would be treated as one lever and the intersection left standing (Fig 9 :56). The avoidable outreach cost is spending an expert's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 prices it, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42). The shape, its falsifiers and the provenance of the Ostrom correction are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a durable, ground-measured reduction where the harm was invisible to the firm's anchor and no contact or pressure was present, (2) a reduction sustained after all pressure and agreement activity ceased, (3) a self-organised arrangement durable without monitoring or graduated sanctions, (4) an instrument that discriminates voluntary from pressured origin from the outcome alone, or (5) a figure re-census yielding a frequency line. Agreement among receptive readers is not such a reversal (Seat Map :163).
