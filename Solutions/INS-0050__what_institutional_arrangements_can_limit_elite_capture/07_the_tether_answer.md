# The Tether's answer: limiting elite capture without stopping decisions

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A public decision should be settled by something the process cannot move. Capture is the family of cases where that reference starts updating on the reading of whoever has power, so decisions converge on influence and the drift is coherent enough that nobody inside notices. The residual is indexed per decision slot, never as a national average (Fig 1 :19, :22). The workability half of the question is a real constraint: a rule no real occupant can meet is an idealisation, not a position (Fig 10 :54).

Elite is not a kind of person here. It is a relation between an interest in the reading and the power to move the frame (Fig 11 :49, :51).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named country has solved this.
- Not that a named institution is the answer. Sortition, audit courts, independent media, transparency registries and judicial review appear as bindings onto held terms, each provenanced, none declared the winner.
- Not that "the elite" is a fixed class. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not that influence can be eliminated. An interest in outcomes is unavoidable (Fig 10 :59).
- Not that formal independence is real independence. Overlap decides (Fig 8 :48-49).
- Not that a room of agreeable experts corroborates anything (Fig 7 :13, :35).
- Not `grounded`. No decision record was measured.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

The honest stop is **unreached, not unreachable**. A source-discriminating instrument is owed, in the sense of *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: a reading does not determine its source. The discriminator should be describable in effect terms as follows.

1. **Pin test.** For each decision slot, take an outcome the process cannot revise, a published count, a court finding, a price. Push on the frame by changing who argues the case. Does the reference move? A reference that moves is not a reference (Fig 2 :29-30).
2. **Non-authorship test.** Can the checker write the rule, supply the data and certify the result? If yes, it is not a check (Fig 10 :42-43).
3. **Overlap test.** Measure the evidence pools of two checkers. If they draw on one pool, their agreement is one witness (Fig 2 :40, Fig 8 :48-49). Effective sample size, not headcount.
4. **Refusal test.** Can a captured standing be refused, not merely supplemented with a new disclosure rule? A vocabulary without negation can only accumulate (Operators :58).
5. **Workability test.** Do decisions still resolve within a defined interval, and can the office be filled and held by a real occupant with a declared interest? Fig 10 :53-60.
6. **Maintenance test.** After one cycle, what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69)? A design that only works at commissioning is a test harness (Fig 11 :70).

Nothing here declares which country or which reform passes. That is the `gap`.

## 5. Standing falsifiers

From `01` §4, written before this conclusion: a process with no pinned reference whose drift does not trend down; a sound check authored by the checker; agreement across one evidence pool carrying verdict weight; an unbiased pool composed by an interested party; a working office with no interest and no standing; a maintenance-free design; a refusal-free vocabulary that removes a captured standing; a single lever clearing the union intersections. Any one firing withdraws the corresponding claim.

## 6. The honest stop

The shape stops where instruments stop distinguishing. Indiscernibility is not identity. A clean decision record is compatible with several institutional sources, and naming one from the reading alone is the metamerism error. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: what was unreachable, what was imported and from where, and what would reverse it. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the institution that will defeat elite capture. It gives the shape any working one must have. The reference that settles a decision must not move when the body being checked pushes on it, because a moving reference makes the drift coherent and invisible. The checker must be unable to author the rule, the data and the certification, and must draw on evidence separate from the body it grades. The pool from which decision-makers and checkers are drawn must not be composed by an interested party. A captured standing must be refusable, not merely surrounded by new disclosure rules. Interests in outcomes are unavoidable, so interests in readings are declared rather than abolished, and every rule must be one a real occupant can keep. Because contact cannot be subtracted from a habitat, this is maintenance rather than a solved state. Which package satisfies all of this in a given place is left open, with the tests that would close it written in advance.
