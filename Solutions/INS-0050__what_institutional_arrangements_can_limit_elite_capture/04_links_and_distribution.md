## Wider literature actually opened, and what each bears on

These are secondary witnesses. Overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Elite capture** (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23). *"Elite capture is a form of corruption whereby public resources are biased for the benefit of a small group of people with superior social status (the elite)."* Also: *"Elite capture of public resources by local elites can be relatively lessened by the presence of media to balance the asymmetry of information."* This is a map of the field, not an authority. It places the question and corroborates the separate-checker point from development studies.
2. **Why Nations Fail** (Acemoglu and Robinson 2012, via Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23). *"Institutions are 'inclusive' when many people have a say in political decision-making, as opposed to cases where a small group of people control political institutions and are unwilling to change."* Corroborates the anchor and unauthored-pool points. Shares the institutional-economics pool with items 3 and 4.
3. **Elinor Ostrom, Nobel facts** (Nobel Foundation 2009). *"She showed that when natural resources are jointly used by their users, in time, rules are established for how these are to be cared for and used in a way that is both economically and ecologically sustainable."* Corroborates workability and permanent maintenance.
4. **Federalist No. 51** (Hamilton or Madison 1788, Yale Avalon Project). *"Ambition must be made to counteract ambition."* Also *"If men were angels, no government would be necessary."* Corroborates the declared-interest reading.
5. **Sortition** (Wikipedia, citing Manin 1997, Dahl 1989, Fishkin 2009, OECD 2020). *"In governance, sortition is the selection of public officials or jurors at random, i.e., by lottery."* The Venetian case: *"The use of lot to select nominators made it more difficult for political sects to exert power, and discouraged campaigning."* Corroborates the unauthored-pool mechanism.
6. **Byzantine fault tolerance** (Lamport, Shostak and Pease 1982, via Wikipedia). *"Without message signing, Byzantine fault tolerance can only be achieved if the total number of generals is greater than three times the number of disloyal (faulty) generals."* Low-overlap engineering corroboration for buying independence in proportion to cost.
7. **Principal-agent problem** (Jensen and Meckling 1976, via Wikipedia). *"The principal-agent problem typically arises where the two parties have different interests and asymmetric information."* Also *"the multiple principal problem is particularly serious in the public sector."* Business and public administration corroboration.
8. **Confirmation bias** (Wikipedia, citing Nickerson 1998). *"Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for, interpret, favor and recall information in a way that confirms or supports one's prior beliefs, values, or decisions."* Human cognition corroboration for coherent drift without a pinned reference.
9. **Arrow's impossibility theorem** (Wikipedia, Arrow 1950 and 1963). *"no group decision-making procedure based on ordinal utilities ... can satisfy the requirements of rational choice."* Mathematics and social choice corroboration for the workability constraint.
10. **Dominance hierarchy** (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23). *"dominant animals must maintain alliances with subordinates and grant them favours to receive their support in order to retain their dominant rank."* Low-overlap animal behaviour corroboration for limiting rather than eliminating power.

Four framework essays were opened directly this session for the canonical reading in `05_what_shape_means.md`: *The Library of Babel*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*. Four other essays in the set of eight (*How Intelligence Actually Works*, *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, and the Substack *Arrival of the Fittest*) were **not** opened this session and are not quoted.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a failure condition sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the three checkable sub-claims and the refuters.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Seat Map :163 *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not a check.
- **Fig 7 :35** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Prefer several different closures over a large agreeing audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the claim and its refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the public links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only on request, the formula as a method with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. The verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a written refuter for their own problem.

**Hard precondition:** all three stages work only if the stage-1 claim survives expert checking. If it does not, withdraw it and keep the shape that does survive. Nothing here is sent by this agent.
