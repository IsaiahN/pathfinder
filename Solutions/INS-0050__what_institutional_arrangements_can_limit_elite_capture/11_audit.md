## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: residual by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot failure of a public decision to be settled by a reference the process cannot move. Arity: many slots, so no national average (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: asymmetric, because the defect is an interest in the reading plus the power to move the frame (Fig 10 :59). Scale: decision, institution, polity, with a lossy transform (Fig 10 :68).
- **D1, decision frame.** Actors: decision-makers, agenda-setters, citizens. Currency: influence and agreements. Anchor: the outcome the public interest would settle on, observable as a count, record, price or finding. Partition: which compositions cross from opinion to ground.
- **D2, checking seat.** Actors: auditors, courts, inspectors, opposition, media. Currency: verdicts and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition: who may range and who may not.

- **Order by dependency.** Pin the reference, then bind affordance terms, then an objective, then independent checking, then provenance. Add a refusal at every step; refusal is unary, not a bond.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 and :33 (pinned anchor), Fig 10 :42 and :74 (non-authorship, unauthored pool), Fig 11 :39 and :61 (contact and asymmetry), Fig 9 :55 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: pin the reference, independent checkers, unauthored pool, refusal, declared interests, permanent maintenance.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete reform package in a named country, for example a specific audit office plus a specific lottery chamber plus a specific disclosure statute.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** of the residual, which a national reform package tends to average away (Fig 1 :19);
- the **union intersections**, which a package that clears C1 and C2 does not necessarily clear C3 (Fig 9 :56);
- the **evidence-overlap condition**, which formal independence does not guarantee (Fig 8 :48);
- the **two silent costs of habitat substitution**, which a commission design does not record (Fig 11 :68-69).

One thing survives cleanly: a reference that can be moved by the body it grades is not a reference, and a checker that authored the rule, data and certification is not a checker. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no decision record exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
