## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the winner

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of a working anti-capture arrangement that can be stated before the winning constitution is named. The three conditions and the workability constraint in `01` are a shape. "Adopt sortition plus an independent audit plus a free press in country X" is a name, and the framework does not produce it.

A correction kept visible: the README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive. The mechanisms named in `01` (an unpinned reference, low-overlap checkers, an unauthored pool, a power of refusal, declared interests) are not new primitives in this derivation. They are bindings onto terms the world already supplies, provenanced as such. What is refused is the step from a binding to a uniqueness claim.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the thing and bootstrap by proximity. Applied here: the closest ordinary vocabulary was "independence", "transparency", "checks and balances". Each was tested against the figure text and narrowed. *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the shape is written as effects: the reference does not move when pushed; checkers cannot author the rule, data and certification; the pool is not composed by an interested party; a captured standing can be refused.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach in `02` sends failure conditions rather than theory.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual was described before the world sources were chosen. That is why the source list spans engineering, cognition, mathematics, animal behaviour, business and institutional economics rather than one favourite school.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means decision records showing that the reference stayed pinned, that decisions still resolved, and that drift did not compound.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A constitutional design imported from another polity is exactly such an import. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, sharpens the first point. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can produce one reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Applied here: a clean decision record does not by itself identify which arrangement produced it. Several institutional paths can yield the same clean reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. That is the declared `gap` in `01` F10.

## 4. Why "limit" and not "eliminate" is the figure-correct verb

Fig 10 :58: *"A seat with no stake at all does not exist."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*

This is the canonical reason the question's own wording, "limit elite capture", is the right one. An arrangement that promised to eliminate influence would be promising a seat with no interest at all, which the figures refuse as an idealisation. The workable target is to limit an *interest in the reading* while allowing interests in outcomes, and to make the latter declare themselves.

Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* There is no clean subtraction of the habitat. Anti-capture is maintenance, not a solved state (Fig 11 :63).

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: the reputation of a reform, a Nobel prize on a reformer, or the number of signatories to a declaration can give the claim a budget to be tried. None of them settles whether the reference stayed pinned. A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 7 :13, Seat Map :163).

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay calls such output *"simulacra."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any institutional arrangement and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. A real check would require decision records and a discriminator that separates the arrangements producing them.
