**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been checked against the figures by you. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about limiting elite capture, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If this does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Public decisions are supposed to be settled by something the decision process cannot move: an outcome, a published count, a court finding, a price. Capture is what happens when that reference starts updating on the reading of whoever has power, so decisions converge on influence rather than on the ground. The claim is that an arrangement limits capture only along three separate axes, and that one lever does not clear all three:
>
> 1. **The reference must stay pinned.** If the thing that should settle the decision can be revised by the body being checked, the check has drifted into the thing it grades. The drift is coherent, so nobody notices from inside.
> 2. **The checker must not be able to author what it checks**, and must draw on evidence separate from the body it grades. Two reviewers reading the same briefing are one reviewer with two names.
> 3. **The vocabulary must be able to refuse.** A system that can only add disclosures and add oversight bodies can be corrected but cannot carry the correction.
>
> Two further constraints come with it. Interests in outcomes are unavoidable, so the requirement is to declare an interest in the reading, not to find disinterested occupants. And the arrangement must be operable by real occupants: a rule no real occupant can meet is an idealisation, not a position.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named country has solved this, that any single institution is the answer, that sortition or independent audits or transparency rules work by themselves, or that this is grounded. I am also not claiming that "the elite" is a fixed kind of person. It is a relation that changes when the residual changes.
>
> **What would refute the claim:**
> 1. a decision process that settles questions with no pinned reference whose drift against an independent record does not trend down;
> 2. a check that is sound while the checker authored the rule, the data and the certification;
> 3. two bodies drawing on one evidence pool whose agreement still carries verdict weight after the effective-sample-size correction;
> 4. a vocabulary with no power of refusal that nonetheless removes a captured standing.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - The figures and formula, verbatim with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public figures folder: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1.** Send the claim and its four refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the answer before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, give the three links and note the figures are the authority.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
