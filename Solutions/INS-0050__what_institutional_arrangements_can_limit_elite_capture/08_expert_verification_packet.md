# Expert verification packet: the Tether on elite capture and workability

**What this is:** a claim in a form a political scientist, institutional economist, public administrator or governance practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No decision record was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Elite capture is an anchor failure before it is a moral failure: the reference that should settle a decision starts updating on the reading of whoever has power, so outcomes converge on influence and the drift is coherent. A working arrangement must satisfy three separable conditions and one constraint. The reference must stay pinned against the frame it grades. The checker must be unable to author what it checks and must draw on separate evidence. The vocabulary must be able to refuse a captured standing. Interests in outcomes are unavoidable, so an interest in the reading is declared rather than abolished, and every rule must be satisfiable by a real occupant. Because contact cannot be subtracted from a habitat, this is permanent maintenance, not a solved state. Which package satisfies all of this in a given place is left open, with the tests that would close it written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 2 and Figure 10

- **Statement:** a check is not a check if the thing that should settle the decision can be moved by the body being checked. The requirement is a pinned reference, not a virtuous occupant.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a decision process with no pinned reference whose drift against an independent record does not trend down. Or exhibit a sound check in which the checker wrote the rule, supplied the data and certified the result.

### C2: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 8, Figure 10 and Figure 7

- **Statement:** independence is low evidence overlap, not separate letterhead; the pool must not be composed by an interested party; a room of agreeable experts cannot corroborate a design.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :48 *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."* Fig 10 :74 *"A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second."* Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- **What to check:** are these four lines about the same quantity, or are two of them about different things? Our reading is that :31 and :48 govern verdict weight, :74 governs membership, and :13 governs the failure of a room to add an anchor. If they collapse into one rule, the sub-claim needs re-census.
- **Refuter:** show two bodies drawing on one evidence pool whose agreement still carries verdict weight after an effective-sample-size correction, or a pool composed by an interested party with no residual bias after overlap is measured.

### C3: framework-internal and workability-facing, checkable by reading Operators and Figure 10 and Figure 11

- **Statement:** a vocabulary without a unary refusal can only accumulate; interests in outcomes cannot be abolished, only declared; prevention is permanent work and isolation substitutes a habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Fig 10 :59 *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :54 *"A rule no real occupant could meet describes an idealisation, not a position."* Fig 11 :61 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a decision vocabulary with no power of refusal that nonetheless removes a captured standing; or a real office whose occupant has no interest in any outcome and no standing and can still be filled and held; or an anti-capture regime shown to work once with no maintenance and no substituted habitat.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named country has solved elite capture.
- Not that a named institution is the answer. Sortition, audit bodies, judicial review, free media and disclosure are bindings onto held terms, provenanced as such, none declared the winner.
- Not that "the elite" is a fixed kind of person. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51).
- Not that influence can be eliminated. Fig 10 :58: *"A seat with no stake at all does not exist."*
- Not that formal independence is real independence; overlap decides.
- Not that a room selected for agreement corroborates anything.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a decision record.
- Not a claim that the figures settle an empirical dispute about which reform works. They narrow the shape and name the tests.

## 5. What would settle it

A decision record and a discriminator, not agreement. In effect terms:

1. **Pin test:** does the reference move when who argues the case changes?
2. **Non-authorship test:** can the checker write the rule, supply the data and certify the result?
3. **Overlap test:** how much evidence do the two checkers share?
4. **Refusal test:** can a captured standing be refused rather than supplemented?
5. **Workability test:** do decisions still resolve, and can the office be filled by a real occupant with a declared interest?
6. **Maintenance test:** after one cycle, what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29, :33; Fig 10 :24, :42, :54, :59, :61, :66, :74; Fig 8 :48; Fig 7 :13; Operators :58. Twelve short passages.
2. Check C2's internal question: are the overlap rule, the authorship rule and the room rule about one quantity or three? Mark the answer, do not smooth it.
3. Name one instrument that would separate a pinned reference from a moving one in a decision record, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
