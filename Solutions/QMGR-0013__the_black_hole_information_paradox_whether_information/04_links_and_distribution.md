## Three things in the literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **Page's caution is a depth/absence statement, not a resolution.** Page 1993 says the information "would never show up in an analysis perturbative in M_Planck/M". That is a prediction about the limits of D1, and it is exactly Fig 6 :27's point: depth and absence look the same from inside. Do not quote Page as if he had measured the correlations.
2. **The 2019-2020 Page-curve derivations are a new instrument, not a better search.** Penington's phase transition and Engelhardt and Wall's quantum extremal surface are built from the semiclassical entropy calculation that was already returning something. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." That is why the import is legitimate rather than a free invention.
3. **The disagreement is still live.** Unruh and Wald 2017 present arguments for information loss; Raju 2020 warns that the bath models may misstate the extraction rate for asymptotically flat black holes. These are not noise to be averaged away. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Send the shape claim and the refuter first.
- **Fig 9 :27:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source links.
- **Fig 2 :35:** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask whether they agree that information is preserved.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for people who already accept holography produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. The recording of a particular success does cross as knowledge unless the round trip is measured.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter, with the request "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict before showing anything else; (4) only then the three local figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with their own written refuter.

## The two-stage offer, stated plainly

- **Stage 1.** Check the output against the ground, without holding the framework's interior. Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the figures. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3, opt-in again.** Offer the loop as a reusable method. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so each user must write down the sought-for shape as an ordinary term and state their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** none of this works if the stage-1 output does not survive. That is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` puts its three checkable sub-claims and their refuters before the conclusion.
