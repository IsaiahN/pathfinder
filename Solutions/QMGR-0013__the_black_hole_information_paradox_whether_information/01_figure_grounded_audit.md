## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question is framed as a binary: is the information lost, or is it encoded in the Hawking radiation? Stated as an effect, the residual is a prediction gap at the contact of two closures:

- Semiclassical gravity (general relativity plus quantum field theory on a curved background) returns a thermal spectrum fixed by mass, charge and angular momentum. Because a pure initial state maps to a mixed final state, the map is not invertible. Call this the "lost" output.
- Quantum mechanics holds that evolution is unitary, so the map is invertible. Call this the "encoded" output.

The residual is not "where did the information go"? It is: which closure governs the evaporation process, and what would have to be imported for a term to be composable that both closures can settle? The binary itself is a candidate framing, and the first job of the audit is to test whether it is one question or a bundle.

**Bound shape (written before the run).** An acceptable answer must state: (i) the question type (partition or union), (ii) whether "not found" and "absent" are distinguishable from inside the semiclassical closure, (iii) what operator moved the wall if one did, (iv) a shape for the missing term, not a name, and (v) what instrument could settle it. This is the "make the sought-for shape an ordinary term" discipline from `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" and Fig 5 :65-66: "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** two conditions, not one. The residual needs both a thermal-spectrum claim and a unitarity claim to exist. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric under swapping the operands. "Lost" and "encoded" are not two names for one frame. Fig 8 :13: "their union adds nothing" is the collapse case, and this is not it, because the closures differ. The Page turnover is a threshold relation, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities").
- **Scale:** first order inside each closure (a semiclassical prediction; a unitary CFT evolution), second order at their union (the fine-grained entropy of the radiation as a function of time, the Page curve).

**Question type: union, not partition.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The overlap is the evaporation process itself, where both conditions bind. A lever that clears only unitarity leaves the semiclassical condition standing, and a lever that clears only semiclassical gravity leaves a different compound. The binary in the question is therefore a compressed union, and answering it as a single either/or is the first failure mode.

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

A domain is admitted only if it has a nameable anchor, per the standing method.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Semiclassical gravity | black hole, horizon, no-hair theorem, stress-energy tensor, Hawking quanta, background metric | thermal spectrum, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, temperature | yes: the semiclassical calculation itself, settled for large holes where horizon curvature is small |
| D2 Quantum information | density matrix, pure/mixed states, von Neumann entropy, entanglement, no-cloning, decoding | fine-grained entropy, unitarity, recoverability | yes: unitarity, constitutive of quantum theory and experimentally supported in laboratory QM |
| D3 Holographic / quantum-gravity import | AdS/CFT boundary theory, quantum extremal surfaces, entanglement wedge, replica wormholes, islands | fine-grained entropy via the island formula | yes: the Page curve as a calculational target, reproduced in 2019-2020. Not an experimental anchor |
| D4 Verification / ground | instruments, correlation measurements, observers at future infinity | measured correlation, measured fine-grained entropy | **no reachable anchor for a fully evaporated astrophysical black hole** |

D4 has no nameable, reachable anchor, so it is not yet a domain that can return a verdict. It is recorded as an empty seat, not smuggled in as a source of agreement.

### Partition or union, and dependency order

- D1 and D2 form a union compound. D3 is a witness frame with a different closure, imported against the residual. D4 is the empty ground seat.
- Dependency order (Fig 3 :20): "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Settle the semiclassical prediction (D1) before asking about its correction; hold the unitarity requirement (D2) before deriving the Page target; the Page target (D3) before any mechanism name; and the ground (D4) last. The chain stops at D4, because there is no instrument there. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

## 3. Findings

### F1. The binary is a bundled union, not one hard question (structural, `general`)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The question asks for a choice between "lost" and "encoded". But both options are defined against a background where the semiclassical calculation and unitarity are jointly assumed. If unitarity is dropped, "lost" stops being a paradox and becomes a modification of quantum theory. If the semiclassical result is dropped, "encoded" stops being surprising. The two conditions overlap in the evaporation process, so this is a union with its own loop and its own ground.

**Refuter:** show that the two conditions are not jointly required for the residual to arise, that is, exhibit the paradox with only one condition in play. Then it is a partition and can be split into two separate searches.

### F2. "Lost" is a refuted-kind gap, not a missing-kind gap (`specified`, conditional on the unitarity anchor)

Fig 5 :51: "The four kinds sort a gap by what is missing. None of them sorts a gap by what is wrong." Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."

If the unitarity anchor is held, the content of the residual is not "we have not yet found the information". It is "a regularity we hold, the pure-to-mixed map, is false". What is owed is a refusal (¬), not another explanation of where the information went. `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 puts the same point as: "REFUTED a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."

**This is conditional, and the condition is declared.** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The question is constitutively about information, and information preservation is constitutive of quantum theory. But a real minority rejects unitarity as absolute (Unruh and Wald 2017 review the case; Penrose's objective-collapse programme is another). A conflict sends us back to the census, never to a side-swap: the dissenters are not making a mistake inside D2, they are denying that D2's anchor governs D1. So the refusal is `specified` under a declared anchor, not `general`.

**Refuter:** a self-consistent account in which the pure-to-mixed map is accepted at the ground and the rest of the physics is retained (an objective-collapse or information-loss theory that passes its own tests). That would show the refusal was aimed at a term the anchor does not actually hold.

### F3. From inside the semiclassical closure, depth and absence look the same (structural, `general`; triangulated)

Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

This is the central figure-grounded result for the question as asked. Hawking's calculation returns a thermal spectrum. Failing to decode the initial state from that spectrum is compatible with two states of affairs:

1. **Depth.** The information is present in correlations of order beyond the perturbative calculation, so it is not yet found. Page 1993 predicted exactly this: the information "may come out initially so slowly, or else be so spread out, that it would never show up in an analysis perturbative in M_Planck/M".
2. **Genuine absence.** The information is not there at all, because the map is non-invertible.

From inside D1 these are indiscernible. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (canonical figures README, applied here): the honest report is that D1 cannot distinguish the two, so D1 cannot decide the question.

Supporting lines, low overlap with Fig 6: Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."; Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

**Refuter:** exhibit a discriminator inside the semiclassical closure that returns different readings for depth and for absence, and show it works on a case where the answer is independently known. Page's point is that the required correlations are non-perturbative, so this refuter has not fired.

### F4. The wall moved by import, and the figure names this case (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall".

The history of the field is a sequence of imports into the evaporation problem: the holographic principle (Susskind 1994, following 't Hooft), AdS/CFT (Maldacena 1997), quantum extremal surfaces (Engelhardt and Wall 2014), entanglement wedge reconstruction and the island formula (Penington 2019), and replica wormholes (the 2019-2020 derivations summarised by Almheiri et al 2020). None of these is derivable by searching D1 harder; each arrives from a frame whose closure differs.

Fig 8 :56: "Wiles and Frey, Montgomery and Dyson, Bekenstein and holography" is the figure's own list of known triangulation cases. The case at hand is named in the figure.

**But read the next clause.** Fig 8 :56 continues "the triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward." The exemplar's `04_links_and_distribution.md` records that the framework's own article tags the Bekenstein/Hawking/'t Hooft/Susskind entry as "an instance", a case arrived at independently by someone else. So this is a **retrodiction**: the framework recognises a triangulation that happened, it did not produce it. Reporting it as a prediction would be the adopted-import error. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

**Refuter:** show that the resolution was derived inside D1's closure without importing a new atom, then the import claim is wrong (it would also weaken F3). Or show that the figure's "Bekenstein and holography" entry refers to a different case than this one, then the retrodiction claim falls.

### F5. The framework returns a shape, not a mechanism (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

Describe R in effect terms before picking a frame: the missing term must (a) keep evolution unitary, (b) leave the leading-order thermal spectrum intact, since that calculation works where curvature at the horizon is small, (c) change the fine-grained entropy at a threshold, the Page time, and (d) be nonlocal enough that interior and radiation are not independent subsystems. That is a shape. It is not the name "fuzzball", "soft hair", "firewall", "remnant", "island" or "replica wormhole". Each of those is a binding from a particular physics frame onto the slot and must be provenanced as such.

The essays add the same limit from a different field. *The Library of Babel*: a search may be cut short if the returned set is "small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question". *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

**Refuter:** a figure line that selects one mechanism over another, or a demonstration that the four effect conditions determine a unique mechanism. None is in the extract.

### F6. The Page turnover has the shape of the comparison bond (specified, single-source for the figure)

Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities". Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

The Page curve is a comparison between two quantities, the fine-grained entropy of the radiation and the remaining black-hole entropy. Its turnover at the halfway point is a threshold. That is the ⋛ shape under Fig 12, with no strain: the operands are two quantities, as the Operators table requires.

This is a structural observation, not a physics result. The world sources supply the content: Page 1993 argued that a unitary evaporation requires the entropy to "initially increase and then decrease back to zero"; Wikipedia's public reference entry states that "After the Page time, correlations appear and the radiation becomes increasingly information rich." Almheiri et al 2020 report "large corrections needed for the entropy to be consistent with unitary black hole evaporation."

**Refuter:** show that the turnover is not a threshold relation between two quantities but a different bond type under Fig 12's six tests, or show that the correct fine-grained entropy never turns over. The latter would also fire F2's refuter.

### F7. The round-trip gap is nonzero (`general`)

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :27 (depth and absence), Fig 8 :32 (no search adds an atom), Fig 9 :32 (a kind of thing, never the answer).
- **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in this folder: the binary is a union; "not found" is not "absent"; the wall moved by import; the answer is a shape with a threshold at the Page time.
- **Concretisation (T_E):** any specific mechanism, for example the island formula or the fuzzball proposal.

Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down: which nonperturbative correction is the right one, whether the interior copy is state-dependent, and whether the Page curve computed in AdS coupled to a bath is the right rate for an asymptotically flat black hole (Raju 2020 explicitly warns the latter models "may provide an inaccurate picture of the rate at which information can be extracted"). R_T is therefore nonzero, and is recorded qualitatively, not numerically, since there is no ground reading.

**Refuter:** a mechanism whose concretisation returns exactly the sent-up constraints with no residue. Then R_T is zero for that mechanism and the warning is moot for it.

### F8. Consensus cannot be a witness here (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

The public reference states that "It is now generally believed that information is preserved in black-hole evaporation." That is a report about a community, not a measurement. The physicists who agree on it read the same papers, use the same AdS/CFT machinery and the same 2019-2020 calculations. Their agreement is one evidence pool talking. This simulated second seat shares the same pool too (see `11_audit.md`), so its agreement with this audit is also not a witness.

**Refuter:** two independent experimental groups obtaining the same correlation result with instruments that do not share a calibration chain. That would be genuine triangulation at D4.

### F9. "Information" is not a primitive; it is a frame currency (`general`, with a standing strain)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." The six terms are space, time, energy (states) and spectrum, gradient, network (structures). "Information" is not among them, and deriving it as a seventh would break minimality.

The honest reading is a **binding**, not a derivation: the physics frame's "information" is its distinguishability of states, which is an ordering, hence spectrum-shaped (Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"), and the residual is the Symbols-table `R` for that frame's slots. This is a cross-frame binding that needs provenance, like the metric-tensor-to-Gradient binding in the exemplar. It is **not** import in the Fig 6 sense: it adds no atom to the six.

**Standing strain, named not hidden:** the framework's object is the structure of a composition, whereas this question is about a quantity that the substrate does not list. Applying the framework here yields constraints on the shape of any resolution, not a statement about information as a substance. Re-census does not remove the strain; it locates it in the binding, so the verdict stays `general`/`specified` and never `grounded`.

**Refuter:** derive information from the other five terms, or show a seventh term is needed. Either breaks the substrate, and with it the whole derivation.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing rule, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Ground refutation.** A measurement of the fine-grained entropy of Hawking radiation from a fully evaporated black hole that rises monotonically to the end, with no turnover, or a direct measurement showing the final radiation state is mixed. This kills the "encoded" shape at the only anchor that could settle it.
2. **Anchor refutation.** A self-consistent, experimentally adequate theory in which pure states genuinely evolve to mixed states and the rest of the physics is retained. This kills the "refuse lost" move in F2.
3. **Closure refutation.** A discriminator inside the semiclassical closure that distinguishes depth from absence and works on a known case. This kills F3.
4. **Census refutation.** A demonstration that the two conditions in F1 are not jointly required, so the question is a partition and not a union. This kills F1 and the whole unbundling.
5. **Standing substrate refutation.** Derive any one of the six terms from the other five, or exhibit a seventh. This kills F9 and the framework used throughout.

If any fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn, not softened. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union finding (F1) and the depth/absence finding (F3). They are the parts that answer the question as asked, and neither requires choosing a physics side.
2. State the anchor before the refusal (F2). "Information is not lost" is a `specified` statement under unitarity, not a general verdict.
3. Present the imports with provenance and dates (F4). The framework retrodicts the holographic triangulation; it did not generate it.
4. Never name a mechanism as the answer. Give the four effect conditions and let the ground choose (F5, F7).
5. Do not present community agreement as evidence (F8). Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
