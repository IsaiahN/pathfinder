# The Tether's answer on black hole information, lost or encoded

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the shape claim and the refusal of 'lost'. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of the fine-grained correlations in radiation from a fully evaporated black hole exists. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Semiclassical gravity returns a thermal spectrum fixed by mass, charge and spin, so a pure initial state maps to a mixed final state. Quantum mechanics holds that evolution is unitary, so the map is invertible. The question asks for a choice between the two. The Tether's first answer is that this is not one question: it is a union compound (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.") whose two conditions overlap in the evaporation process itself. A lever that clears unitarity alone leaves the semiclassical condition standing, and a lever that clears semiclassical gravity alone leaves a different compound.

The second answer is about what the semiclassical calculation can report. Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Failing to decode the initial state from a thermal spectrum is therefore compatible with two states of affairs, information present in non-perturbative correlations, or information genuinely absent. From inside the semiclassical closure they are indiscernible.

## 2. The shape

1. **The binary must be unbundled before it is searched.** Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The residual is a union of a thermal-spectrum claim and a unitarity claim, not a single hard either/or.
2. **Failure to find is not proof of absence.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Page 1993's caution that the correlations are non-perturbative is exactly this point, not a resolution.
3. **Under the declared unitarity anchor, 'lost' is refuted, not missing.** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." What is owed is a refusal (¬), not another explanation of where the information went. The anchor is declared, and dissent from it is recorded rather than dismissed (Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it.").
4. **The wall moved by import, and the case is named.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Holography, AdS/CFT, quantum extremal surfaces and replica wormholes arrived from frames whose closures differ. Fig 8 :56 names "Bekenstein and holography" among four known triangulation cases while stating "the triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward". This is a **retrodiction**: the framework recognises a triangulation that happened; it did not generate it. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a mechanism.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The shape has four conditions: evolution unitary; leading thermal spectrum intact; fine-grained entropy turning over at a threshold; interior and radiation not independent subsystems. No mechanism name is selected by the figures. Each candidate (fuzzball, soft hair, firewall, remnant, island, replica wormhole) is a binding from a physics frame onto that slot and must be provenanced as such.
6. **The turnover has the comparison shape.** Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities". The Page curve compares the fine-grained entropy of the radiation with the remaining black-hole entropy. That is a ⋛ relation with no strain on the operand type. The figure licenses the arrangement; Page 1993 and the 2019-2020 calculations supply the content.
7. **The answer stops at the missing instrument.** Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". There is no reachable anchor for a fully evaporated astrophysical black hole, so the verdict is unreachable at the ground, not silence and not a confirmed physics result.
8. **Consensus is not the ground.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The wide agreement that information is preserved is one evidence pool talking, and this simulated derivation shares that pool too.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the paradox is solved, and not that information preservation has been measured.
- Not that the framework derived the Page curve. It recognises a ⋛ shape; the curve comes from Page and from the 2019-2020 derivations.
- Not that the island formula, replica wormholes, fuzzballs, soft hair or any other mechanism is the answer.
- Not that information loss has been refuted without a ground. The refusal is `specified` under a declared anchor, and a self-consistent information-loss theory remains live pressure on that status.
- Not that the six-term substrate contains information as a primitive. It is a frame currency bound to distinguishability of states, a strain recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9 and `11_audit.md`.
- Not a final room. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". No last mechanism may be declared.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The corresponding questions:

- **Ground refutation.** A measurement of the fine-grained entropy of radiation from a fully evaporated black hole that rises monotonically to the end with no turnover, or a direct measurement showing the final radiation state is mixed. This kills the encoded shape at the only anchor that could settle it.
- **Anchor refutation.** A self-consistent, experimentally adequate theory in which pure states genuinely evolve to mixed states and the rest of the physics is retained. This kills the 'refuse lost' move.
- **Closure refutation.** A discriminator inside the semiclassical closure that separates depth from absence and works on a case where the answer is independently known. This kills the indiscernibility finding.
- **Census refutation.** A demonstration that the two conditions are not jointly required, so this is a partition and not a union.
- **Substrate refutation.** Derive one of the six terms from the other five, or exhibit a seventh. This breaks the framework used throughout.

These are the pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, restated so each can fire. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## 5. The honest stop

The stop is at D4, the empty verification seat. Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Nothing at any resolution is currently returning the fine-grained correlation reading for a fully evaporated black hole. The round trip from figures to shorthand and back carries a nonzero gap R_T, recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`: the shorthand does not recover which non-perturbative correction is right, whether the interior copy is state-dependent, or whether the AdS-with-bath Page curve gives the right rate for an asymptotically flat hole. Indiscernibility is not identity, and the lineage is what makes the stop checkable.

It names no mechanism, and it stops at the missing instrument. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.
