## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :135 adds the reason: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the difference is sharp:

- A name would be "fuzzball", "soft hair", "firewall", "remnant", "island" or "replica wormhole".
- A shape is the set of conditions any resolution must meet: unitary, leading thermal spectrum intact, fine-grained entropy turning over at a threshold, interior and radiation not independent subsystems.

The first column is a physics binding from a particular frame. The second is what the framework can return. Fig 5 :65-66: "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

That is exactly what the bound shape in `README.md` §0 does. It is written before the run so that it can be refused.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: describe the effect, not the cause

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

Applied here: do not describe the residual as "the missing information carrier". Describe it as an effect, a map from a pure initial state to a final radiation state whose fine-grained entropy must return to zero if a held regularity is to survive. That description travels to a frame that may hold the missing term. The description itself picks no mechanism.

Fig 9 :32 ends the passage with the standing limit: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." A shape is a kind of thing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question the relevant ground would be a measurement of the fine-grained correlations in radiation from a fully evaporated black hole. No such measurement exists.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 6 :14, Fig 8 :51-52). Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".

*They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." It adds: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

This matters because the framework's six terms do not include "information" (Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."). The physics frame's information is its distinguishability of states, which is ordering, hence spectrum-shaped (Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"). That is a **binding**, like the metric-tensor-to-Gradient binding in the exemplar, not a derivation, and not an import that adds an atom. It is recorded as a strain in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9 and `11_audit.md`.

## 4. The metamerism that matters here: a thermal reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The black hole case is the same shape. The semiclassical calculation reads a thermal spectrum. That reading is compatible with at least two sources:

1. **Depth.** The information is present in correlations of order beyond the perturbative calculation. Page 1993 predicted exactly this: the information "may come out initially so slowly, or else be so spread out, that it would never show up" in a perturbative analysis.
2. **Genuine absence.** The map is non-invertible and the information is not there.

Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

The honest report is therefore not "the radiation is thermal, so the information is gone". It is: from inside the semiclassical closure, depth and absence are indiscernible, so that closure cannot decide the question. This is the central result of the whole solution, and it comes from the figures and Page's caution together, not from a preference for one physics side.

## 5. The shape for this question, stated before the run

Written as an ordinary term (Fig 5 :65-66), the sought-for shape has four conditions:

| condition | effect term | why the figure set requires it |
|---|---|---|
| keep evolution unitary | the map is invertible | under the declared anchor, a pure-to-mixed map is a refuted regularity (Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so") |
| leave the leading thermal spectrum intact | the semiclassical calculation works where horizon curvature is small | Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." |
| change the fine-grained entropy at a threshold | the Page turnover | Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities" |
| make interior and radiation non-independent | the radiation can carry the interior state | Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." |

The fourth condition is about the bond, not the ingredients. The ingredients of the evaporation problem were on the table in Hawking's calculation; what changed was the relation between them. That is Fig 12's point, and it is why a name like "replica wormhole" is a binding onto a bond rather than the bond itself.

The shape is thus a constraint set, not an answer. Fig 9 :32 still governs: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 6. Why the binary is a compressed union, not a shape

The question asks for a choice between 'lost' and 'encoded'. But both options are defined against a background in which the semiclassical calculation and unitarity are jointly assumed. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

Drop unitarity and 'lost' stops being a paradox and becomes a modification of quantum theory. Drop the semiclassical result and 'encoded' stops being surprising. The two conditions overlap in the evaporation process, so answering with a single either/or is the first failure mode. The essays give the same warning from a different field. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered." The order of the unbundling is part of the answer, not a presentational detail.

A further correction from the bond figure: 'lost' and 'encoded' are not isomers under Fig 12 :117, because they are related by the negation of a held claim, not by a different bond over the same atoms. Fig 12 :135: "The seven are bonds, and a bond is a relation between two things. Negation is not one: it takes a term and yields a term, and joins nothing." The full derivation is in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
