## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question as posed is not one binary but a union of two conditions, a semiclassical thermal-spectrum claim and a unitarity claim, overlapping in the evaporation process (Fig 9 :55). From inside the semiclassical closure, 'the information is there but not decoded' and 'the information is genuinely gone' are indiscernible, so failure to find information in the radiation is not evidence that it is lost. Under a declared unitarity anchor, 'lost' is a refuted regularity rather than a missing item; what is owed is a refusal, not another explanation. The wall moved historically by import from frames whose closures differ, and the figure names this case as a known triangulation while saying the claim has not been tested forward, so the framework retrodicts the holographic resolution and did not generate it. What is returned is a shape: unitary, leading thermal spectrum intact, fine-grained entropy turning over at a threshold, interior and radiation not independent. No mechanism is named. The stop is at the missing instrument, not at a final answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. The binary is a union compound, not one hard question (structural, `general`)

- **Statement:** the two conditions are jointly required for the residual to arise. Answering the question as a single either/or clears only the region where one condition holds.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **What to check:** can the paradox be stated with only one condition in play? If unitarity is dropped, 'lost' becomes a modification of quantum theory, not a paradox. If the semiclassical result is dropped, 'encoded' is not surprising. That is the overlap.
- **Refuter:** exhibit the paradox with only one condition in play. Then the question is a partition, and it can be split into two separate searches. The unbundling claim is withdrawn.

### C2. Depth and absence are indiscernible from inside the semiclassical closure (`general`, triangulated)

- **Statement:** a thermal spectrum does not discriminate between information present in correlations beyond the perturbative calculation and information genuinely absent. More of the same calculation cannot separate them.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Low-overlap support: Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
- **World check:** Page 1993, https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9306083, abstract: the information "may come out initially so slowly, or else be so spread out, that it would never show up" in an analysis perturbative in M_Planck/M. That is a prediction about the limit of D1, not a measurement of the correlations.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a discriminator inside the semiclassical closure that returns different readings for depth and absence, and show it works on a case where the answer is independently known. Page's non-perturbative point means this refuter has not fired.

### C3. The wall moved by import, and the output is a shape, not a mechanism (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the retrodiction)

- **Statement:** no amount of search inside the semiclassical closure manufactures the missing structure; the historical moves came from frames with different closures, and the figures do not select a mechanism.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Provenance clause: Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". Scope clause: Fig 8 :56: "The triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward".
- **What to check:** holography, AdS/CFT, quantum extremal surfaces and replica wormholes were imported, not derived by refining Hawking's calculation. The figure's own list includes Bekenstein and holography among the known cases. The claim made here is a retrodiction of that history. No sentence above says which mechanism fills the slot.
- **Refuter:** show that the resolution was derived inside the semiclassical closure without importing a new atom, or show that the figure's triangulation entry refers to a different case than this one. The first kills the import claim, the second kills the retrodiction claim. A figure line that selects one mechanism over another kills the shape claim.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A measurement of the fine-grained entropy of radiation from a fully evaporated black hole that rises monotonically with no turnover, or a direct measurement that the final radiation state is mixed.
2. A self-consistent, experimentally adequate theory in which pure states evolve to mixed states and the rest of the physics is retained.
3. A discriminator inside the semiclassical closure that separates depth from absence on a case where the answer is independently known.
4. A demonstration that the two conditions are not jointly required, so this is a partition.
5. A derivation of any one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or the exhibition of a seventh.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55 and :57, Fig 6 :27, and Fig 9 :32. Four short passages.
2. Check C1 against their own view of whether unitarity and semiclassical gravity are jointly required for the paradox.
3. Check C2 against Page 1993. Is the non-perturbative caution a statement about the limit of the calculation, as claimed, or has it been superseded by a measurement?
4. Check C3 against the historical record. Was any of the 2019-2020 machinery derived inside Hawking's closure, or imported?
5. Name one instrument that would discriminate depth from absence in the radiation, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. No trust or agreement is requested.
