## The residual as an effect

Something is being decided: whether the map from an initial pure state to the final radiation state is invertible. Option A ("lost") leads to a non-unitary evolution and a mixed final state, which breaks a constitutive rule of quantum theory. Option B ("encoded") leads to a unitary evolution in which the information is present in correlations that the leading semiclassical calculation does not see. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a statement about (i) which closure governs evaporation, (ii) whether "not found" can be distinguished from "absent" from inside that closure, (iii) what had to be imported to move the wall, and (iv) what instrument would settle it. That bound shape is the thing this package tests, not a report of what physics has settled.
