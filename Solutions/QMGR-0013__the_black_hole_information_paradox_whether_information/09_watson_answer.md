## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: is information that falls into a black hole lost, or encoded in Hawking radiation? Option A: treat it as one binary and choose a physics side. Option B: characterise the residual first, report a shape under a declared anchor, and name the instrument that would settle it. What each leads to: A sends a `general` verdict that spends the independence of its readers and overstates what the figures can return. B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and a stop at the missing instrument. The bound shape was written before the run in `README.md` §0: a statement of question type, of whether 'not found' and 'absent' are distinguishable from inside the closure, of what moved the wall, of a shape rather than a name, and of an instrument that could settle it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The contact failure between a thermal semiclassical spectrum and unitarity. Arity: two conditions. Symmetry: not symmetric under swapping the operands, because 'lost' and 'encoded' are related by negation, not by a symmetric join. Scale: first order inside each closure, second order at the union (the fine-grained entropy as a function of time). Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The binary in the question is a compressed union.
- **D1 Semiclassical gravity.** Actors: black hole, horizon, no-hair theorem, stress-energy tensor, Hawking quanta, background metric. Currency: thermal spectrum, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, temperature. Anchor: the semiclassical calculation itself, settled for large holes where horizon curvature is small. Question type: one first-order closure.
- **D2 Quantum information.** Actors: density matrix, pure and mixed states, von Neumann entropy, entanglement, no-cloning, decoding. Currency: fine-grained entropy, unitarity, recoverability. Anchor: unitarity, constitutive of quantum theory and experimentally supported in laboratory QM. A real minority rejects it as absolute; that dissent is recorded, not dismissed.
- **D3 Holographic or quantum-gravity import.** Actors: AdS/CFT boundary theory, quantum extremal surfaces, entanglement wedge, replica wormholes, islands. Currency: fine-grained entropy via the island formula. Anchor: the Page curve as a calculational target, reproduced in 2019-2020. Not an experimental anchor.
- **D4 Verification and ground.** Actors: instruments, correlation measurements, observers at future infinity. Currency: measured correlation, measured fine-grained entropy. Anchor: none reachable for a fully evaporated astrophysical black hole. Empty seat.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 form a union compound. D3 is a witness frame with a different closure, imported against the residual. D4 is the empty ground seat. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Order by dependency.** Settle the semiclassical prediction before asking about its correction; hold the unitarity requirement before deriving the Page target; derive the Page target before naming a mechanism; ground last. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The chain stops at D4 because there is no instrument there.
- **Strain test applied.** Fig 13 excludes 'information' from the six-term substrate, yet the question is about information. Re-census locates it in a frame binding to distinguishability of states, which is ordering and therefore spectrum-shaped, not in a seventh primitive. The strain is named, not smoothed away.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Per domain, kept separate: D1 cannot distinguish depth from absence, so it cannot decide the question (`general`, triangulated). D2 refuses 'lost' only under a declared unitarity anchor (`specified`, triangulated, with the information-loss literature as live pressure). D3 shows the wall moved by import and reports the holographic triangulation as a retrodiction (`specified`, mixed status as marked). D4 has no reachable anchor and returns `gap`, not silence. The union verdict is `single-source` in Fig 9, and the shape-not-a-name limit is `triangulated`. No verdict is `grounded`. The result is a four-condition shape with pre-registered falsifiers and a named missing instrument, not a physics result and not a mechanism name.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, anchor and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a mechanism name before asking for a check. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. If the anchor is wrong, the refusal in D2 is withdrawn and the rest of the shape still stands.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim can be superseded without touching the figure text. A sent claim can be corrected by the next version of `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, no primitive has to be withdrawn, and the falsifiers make the specific point of failure visible. A `grounded` measurement at D4 would either settle the shape or retire it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement of the fine-grained entropy from a fully evaporated black hole that rises monotonically with no turnover, or a direct measurement that the final state is mixed, kills the encoded shape. A self-consistent, experimentally adequate pure-to-mixed theory kills the D2 refusal. A discriminator inside the semiclassical closure that separates depth from absence on a known case kills D1. A demonstration that the two conditions are not jointly required kills the union. A derivation of one substrate term from the other five, or a seventh term, breaks the framework used throughout. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
