# The Tether's answer: thresholds in systems that cannot be pushed

**Mode: `specified` on the method, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A researcher wants φ*, the location of a threshold in a system where the control action that would sweep the boundary cannot be applied. R is the gap between prediction and the system's own record, indexed per system and per candidate threshold (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The ground is that recorded trajectory. It settles a crossing after the fact and no frame's opinion moves it (Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*). The missing thing is the discriminating observation, not a harder push. This is a reachability problem, and it is a union compound with the short-record problem (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*).

## 2. The shape

1. **Treat it as reachability, with two opposed tracks.** Fig 6 :28: *"Which makes reachability two questions, not one. The instruments are opposed."* Inward sharpens the reading already available; outward finds a frame whose closure predicts the shape. Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*
2. **Find the proto-instrument, do not imagine a sensor.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The candidates are natural and historical perturbations, the paleo archive, spatial transects and small fluctuations, whatever in the system is already returning a reading that fails to resolve.
3. **Describe the residual before choosing the analogue.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Transfer methods and provenance, with a measured round-trip gap, not donor threshold values (Fig 4 :22).
4. **Use early-warning signals as filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Rising autocorrelation, variance and slower recovery say where to look first. They do not fix φ* and they can be channel degradation (Fig 10 :26).
5. **Deliver a narrowed shape, not a point.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* A rank among systems, a bound, or an interval plus the discriminator that would close it. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
6. **Triangulate across independent frames.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Models, the observational record and analogues must range separately against the ground. Agreement among models that share a model family is one witness.
7. **Bind the shape and write the refuter before the run.** Fig 5 :65-66: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a numeric threshold for any named Earth tipping element. That is a `gap` (F12), not a hidden answer.
- Not that the system has no threshold, or that no threshold can ever be estimated. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not that early-warning signals are useless. They are useful filters; only their verdict standing is refused.
- Not that an analogue threshold value transfers. Methods cross; recordings do not (Fig 4 :22).
- Not that agreement among experts settles the location. Prestige funds a trial; it does not settle it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any system's boundary is claimed here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. The framework essay analogy: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Which discriminator separates mechanisms that produce the same rising autocorrelation?** An observation that distinguishes a bifurcation-induced from a noise-induced or rate-induced transition would name the kind of threshold.
- **Does a natural or historical perturbation already provide a crossing reading at adequate resolution?** If yes, the inward track has a proto-instrument and φ* becomes a shape with an interval.
- **Does an analogue method survive the round trip with a measured R_T?** If yes, the outward track has a licensed import.
- **Standing refuters from `01`:** one instrument under two names; a discriminator returning a unique φ*; an indicator that fixes φ* deterministically; one lever closing both union conditions; a ground that moves with frame opinion; a transfer with R_T = 0.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A null result inside one model closure is reported as unreachable-at-this-budget, never as absence. This package cannot settle any particular system; it states the shape any acceptable estimate must have and the observations that would close it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for a threshold you cannot push through. It treats that situation as a reachability problem, not a failed measurement. Run two tracks and keep them apart: inward, sharpen whatever reading the system already returns that fails to resolve (natural perturbations, the paleo archive, small fluctuations); outward, describe the residual in effect terms first and then find a frame whose closure predicts its shape, importing the method with provenance and a measured round-trip gap. Early-warning signals such as slower recovery and rising autocorrelation rank systems for attention, but they do not carry the verdict, because the ground does. What is delivered is a narrowed shape, a rank or a bound, plus the instrument that would close it, with a refuter written in advance. If no such reading exists anywhere, the honest answer is unreachable at this budget, not "no threshold".
