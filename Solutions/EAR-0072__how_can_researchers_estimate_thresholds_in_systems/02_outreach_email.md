**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter for a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checks in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been run against the specific system you are asking about. A two-stage offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output does not survive.

**Subject:** Check one output about unpushable thresholds before you decide whether the method is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual.** A researcher wants the threshold φ* of a system that cannot be experimentally pushed through it. The gap is between what a model predicts and what the system's own record shows, indexed per system, not averaged across systems. The ground is the recorded trajectory: it settles the question once a crossing occurs, and no model's opinion moves it. What is missing is not a harder push but the discriminating observation.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** This is a reachability problem with two opposed tracks. Inward: sharpen the reading we already have, from something that is already returning a signal that fails to resolve (natural and historical perturbations, the paleo archive, spatial transects, small fluctuations). Outward: find a frame whose closure already predicts the shape of the threshold, characterise the residual in effect terms first, and import the *method* with provenance. Run the two tracks separately; do not blend them. The deliverable is a narrowed shape, a rank among systems or a bound, plus the name of the instrument that would close it, not a point value. Early-warning signals such as slower recovery, rising lag-1 autocorrelation and rising variance are useful for deciding which systems to watch first. They do not by themselves carry the verdict that a threshold was crossed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any specific Earth tipping element has a known threshold value; that a rising early-warning signal proves proximity to a threshold; that a threshold estimated in one system transfers as a value to another. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them, so an analogue carries zero evidentiary weight until its source gate and shadow test are shown.
>
> **What would refute this output.** I wrote these down before writing to you:
> 1. the inward and outward tracks are shown to be one instrument under a different name;
> 2. a source-discriminating instrument returns a unique φ* across systems, so the shape collapses to a name;
> 3. an early-warning indicator fixes φ* deterministically across differing mechanisms, so it is a witness rather than a filter;
> 4. one lever closes both the non-manipulability and the short-record conditions with no categorically different residue left standing.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding part of the claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the material is linked below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.

>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage boundaries

- **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* showing any framework material.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the links, with the note that the figures are the authority.
- **Stage 3:** the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a fresh mode.

A Stage 2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A Stage 1 refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated like any other source.
