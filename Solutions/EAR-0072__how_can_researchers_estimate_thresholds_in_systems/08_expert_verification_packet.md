# Expert verification packet: thresholds in systems that cannot be pushed

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the method, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No threshold value for any named system is claimed.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A threshold in a system that cannot be experimentally pushed through it is a reachability problem, not a broken measurement. Characterise the residual in effect terms, then run two opposed tracks separately: inward, sharpen whatever reading the system already returns that fails to resolve; outward, find a frame whose closure predicts the shape of the threshold and import its *method* with provenance and a measured round-trip gap. The ground is the system's recorded trajectory, which settles a crossing after the fact and does not move when frames change their minds. Early-warning signals (slower recovery, rising lag-1 autocorrelation, rising variance) rank systems for attention; they do not carry the verdict that a threshold was crossed, because they are filters for budget and can also reflect channel degradation. The deliverable is a narrowed shape, rank or bound plus the discriminator that would close it, with a refuter written in advance. A null result inside one model closure is reported as unreachable-at-this-budget, never as proof of absence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 6

- **Statement:** for an unpushable threshold, there are two opposed instruments (inward and outward), and they must be run as separate tracks.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :28 *"Which makes reachability two questions, not one. The instruments are opposed."*; Fig 6 :31 *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*; Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **What to check:** is the opposition real for the system you know? Identify one reading that already exists and fails to resolve (a natural perturbation, a paleo interval, a spatial transect, a small fluctuation). If none exists at any resolution, say so.
- **Refuter:** show that inward and outward are one instrument under different names, or that the system has no reading at any resolution that fails to resolve, independently replicated.

### C2: world-facing; checkable against early-warning literature and your own data

- **Statement:** early-warning indicators are filters for budget, not witnesses for the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*; Fig 10 :26 *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Operators table :52 *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*
- **World check:** Scheffer et al., Science 2012: slowing down is *"neither a universal warning signal for shifts nor specific to an approaching tipping point"* and is a *"broad spectrum"* indicator requiring further diagnosis. Dakos et al., PLoS ONE 2012: variance can decrease near a transition and detrending choices change the result. These corroborate the figure line; they do not set any threshold value.
- **Refuter:** an indicator whose value deterministically fixes φ* across systems with differing mechanisms and instruments, so it can carry the verdict rather than only the ranking.

### C3: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the deliverable is a shape, and a null result is not proof of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
- **What to check:** does the proposed output name an interval, rank or bound plus a discriminator, rather than a single value? Does it distinguish underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-this-budget (Fig 8 :40-41)?
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument that returns a unique φ* for the system, so the shape legitimately collapses to a name; or a proof that the threshold is underivable in principle, so importing a frame is wasted budget.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a threshold value for any named Earth tipping element. That is a `gap`.
- Not that early-warning signals are useless. They are useful triage filters; only their verdict standing is refused.
- Not that the system has no threshold, or that thresholds can never be estimated.
- Not that an analogue threshold value transfers between systems. Methods cross, recordings do not (Fig 4 :22).
- Not that agreement among experts settles the location. Prestige can fund a trial; it cannot settle it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any boundary is asserted.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument:

1. An observation that distinguishes mechanisms producing the same rising autocorrelation, which would name the kind of threshold.
2. A natural or historical perturbation providing a crossing reading at adequate resolution, which would give the inward track a proto-instrument.
3. An analogue method that survives the round trip with a measured R_T, which would license the outward import.
4. Any of the six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 firing.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :28-32, Fig 9 :60, Fig 8 :39 and Fig 13 :134, five short passages.
2. Name one reading in your system that already fails to resolve, or say that none exists.
3. Say whether rising autocorrelation in your system could have a cause other than proximity to a threshold. If yes, C2 stands.
4. Name the observation that would distinguish two mechanisms giving the same signal. If you can, the shape has its discriminator.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
