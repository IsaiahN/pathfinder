## 0. The residual under audit

Question: how can researchers estimate thresholds in systems that cannot be experimentally pushed through them?

Restated in effect terms. Let φ* be the location of a threshold in a system Σ and let R be the gap between what a model predicts and what is observed, per system-slot and per candidate threshold, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The usual way to reduce R is to apply a control action that sweeps the system across the boundary and observe the response. Here that action is unavailable: the system cannot be pushed. The residual is therefore not *"we have not pushed hard enough"* but *"the action that would generate the discriminating observation is absent from the frame."*

What each option leads to:

- **Option A (measurement reading):** wait for a better push or a finer time series. This refines on an axis that may not carry the quantity (Fig 6 :29), and it can refine forever.
- **Option B (reachability reading):** characterise R in effect terms, run the two opposed tracks, triangulate across frames whose closures differ, and report a shape with a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop.

The sought shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: a **narrowed region, rank or bound for φ* plus the name of the instrument that would close it**. It is not a point estimate. This binding is what makes the preference refusable (formula step "Direction", Fig 5 :65-66).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: a threshold is a two-place comparison between a system feature and a control variable, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Fig 12 :87); R is indexed per system-slot and per candidate threshold. Symmetry: the comparison ⋛ is ordered, not symmetric (*"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*, Fig 12 :89); the same observation read at two different forcing levels is not the same evidence. Scale: a single tipping element, a cascade across elements, and the lossy transfer between them (Fig 4). This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: not being able to push AND having short observations are two conditions (Fig 9 :57).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Observed system | the system Σ, its state variables, its observed trajectory | observations o', residuals R | the system's own recorded trajectory (a ground that cannot be manipulated) | admitted |
| D2 Model frame | models, parameters, ensembles,hindcasts | predictions Γ(b,a), reach closure(Γ) | settlement against D1's record | admitted |
| D3 Analogue frame | other systems that already crossed (paleo record, other basins, lab analogues, other fields) | transferred *methods* and structural relations, not recordings | each analogue's own ground | admitted, low overlap with D1/D2 |
| D4 Early-warning-signal frame | time-series indicators (recovery rate, lag-1 autocorrelation, variance) | statistical signal strength | the ground, but only as a filter on where to look | admitted as filter, not witness |
| X1 "the exact threshold as a manipulable measurement" | none nameable | none | none nameable | **not a domain**; this is the residual itself |
| X2 "consensus among experts as the threshold" | the experts | agreement | none that does not move when frames move | absorbed into D4; disqualified as verdict |

No nameable anchor for X1, so it is not admitted as a domain. This is the crux: the ground exists (the system's trajectory) but it is not manipulable, so the instrument question is about how a frame gains access to it, not about how hard to push.

**Partition or union.** Union. D1/D3 and D2/D4 form two compounds; the whole is a union of "the ground cannot be pushed" and "the record is short", so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57). **Dependency order (Fig 3):** perception (what is already returning something that fails to resolve, Fig 6 :34) → vocabulary (describe R in effect terms) → objective (bind the sought shape, Fig 5 :65) → planning (two opposed tracks, Fig 6 :31) → learn and carry (provenance, Fig 10 :62).

Strain test applied during census: reading *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24) as if the ground were unavailable is a strain. Re-census: non-manipulability is an instrument property, not an anchor property. The anchor criterion is that it does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29), which the recorded trajectory satisfies. No strain after re-census.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The question is reachability, not measurement force (decisive for method)

Fig 6 :28: *"Which makes reachability two questions, not one. The instruments are opposed."*
Fig 6 :29: *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."*
Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*

The unpushable threshold is exactly the reachability question. "Cannot be pushed" does not tell us whether the threshold is underivable in principle or merely unreachable at this budget; those are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-41). Inward means sharpening the observation we already have; outward means finding a frame whose closure already predicts the shape. Running them as a single blended track is the collapse Fig 6 :31 forbids.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the two tracks are the same instrument under a relabelling (so the opposition is spurious), or that pushing is after all possible and the question reduces to an ordinary experiment.

### F2: A proto-instrument is already returning something that fails to resolve

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."*
Fig 6 :34: *"It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

For an unpushable system the search is not for an imagined sensor but for a reading that already exists and fails to resolve: natural and historical perturbations, the paleo archive, spatial transects across analogous regimes, and the system's own small fluctuations. This is a `general` structural claim; which concrete proto-instrument exists in a given system is `specified` and unmeasured here.

**Refuter:** a system for which nothing, at any resolution, is returning a reading that fails to resolve, with the search independently replicated. That would move the case toward genuine absence rather than depth.

### F3: The anchor is the recorded trajectory, not the push

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

A common error in this field is to conclude that because the system cannot be pushed there is no ground. There is a ground: once a crossing has occurred, the record settles it, and no frame's opinion moves it. What is lost is manipulability, not the anchor. Hence the legitimate route is triangulation of separately ranging frames, not a vote among modellers who share a model family.

**Refuter:** a case where the supposed ground visibly changes its answer when frames change their prior, so it is a frame rather than an anchor.

### F4: Underivable is not the same as unreachable; absence is not proved by silence

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."*
Fig 8 :54: *"underivable is not unknowable"*

If the threshold cannot be derived by pushing, the remedy is not more search inside the same model family. Either extend the instrument (inward) or import a frame with a differing closure (outward). A null result inside one closure cannot be reported as "no threshold".

**Refuter:** a proof that the threshold is underivable in principle for this system class, so that importing a frame is wasted budget; or a demonstration that additional search inside the closure does close R.

### F5: This is a union compound, so split before searching

Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*
Fig 9 :53: *"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."*
Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

"Cannot push" and "short record" are separate conditions with separate remedies. A single lever that improves the record does nothing for non-manipulability, and a better analogue does not lengthen the local series. Unbundle before choosing a method.

**Refuter:** a single intervention that demonstrably closes both conditions at once, leaving no categorically different residue.

### F6: Early-warning signals are filters for budget, not witnesses for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*
Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*
Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*
Operators table :52: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*
Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*

Critical slowing down, rising lag-1 autocorrelation, rising variance and flickering are genuine generic signals, but they indicate *proximity*, not the crossing and not its location. They tell a researcher which systems to watch first (a budget decision). The verdict about whether a threshold was crossed belongs to the ground. A rising indicator can also be channel degradation (Fig 10 :26), so a positive signal is not a witness.

**Refuter:** an indicator whose value deterministically fixes φ* across systems with differing mechanisms and instruments, so that it can carry the verdict rather than only the ranking.

### F7: Describe the residual before choosing the analogue frame

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* / *"rather than the cause"*
Fig 8 :50-52: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* / *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*

The analogue must be selected by the effect description of R, not by the researcher's preferred theory. Otherwise any system will seem to fit, and an import will be mistaken for a convergence. Provenance, not resemblance, separates them.

**Refuter:** a documented case where frame choice preceded residual description and the resulting mapping still survives an independent source gate.

### F8: The deliverable is a shape, not a point

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*
Fig 13 :137: *"It can characterise what is missing in effect terms, which is the description that travels to a frame that may hold it."*
Fig 13 :138-139: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."*
Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* / *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

The honest output is a narrowed interval, a rank among systems, or a bound, with the missing discriminator characterised in effect terms. A single number claimed as *the* threshold would be a name where only a shape is available.

**Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument that returns a unique φ* for the system, so the shape collapses legitimately to a name.

### F9: Bind the sought shape before the run so it can be refused

Fig 5 :65-66: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*
Fig 5 :63-64: *"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"*
Fig 5 :69-70: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."*

A frame cannot check the direction of its own question. The remedy is a discipline: write down the shape, commit the prediction and the refuter before the run, then let the same shrinking test that selects terms refuse it if it persists and does not trend down.

**Refuter:** a claimed pre-registration whose refuter was written after seeing the data, or one that cannot in principle fire.

### F10: Transfer methods and provenance, with a measured round-trip gap

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

An early-warning method imported from depression research or finance into an ice-sheet problem carries a round-trip gap R_T. What crosses legally is the method and the provenance, not the recorded threshold value of the donor system. This is the Symbols table definition: R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).

**Refuter:** a transfer shown to preserve every quantity that settled the donor case, so R_T is zero, or a transfer with no record of source and shadow test.

### F11: The analogue habitat is enumerated, and contact cascades

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*
Fig 11 :52-54: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

List everything in contact with the threshold, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. The relevant signal for a tipping element may be three steps out (a teleconnected driver, a human pressure), so a first-order-only habitat will miss the residual that matters.

**Refuter:** a threshold whose determinants are demonstrably exhausted by first-order contacts, with the cascade test run and negative.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The inward and outward tracks are shown to be one instrument under relabelling, so the opposition at Fig 6 :31 is spurious.
2. A source-discriminating instrument returns a unique φ* across systems, collapsing the shape to a name and removing the gap at F12.
3. An early-warning indicator is shown to fix φ* deterministically across differing mechanisms, so it is a witness and not merely a filter (F6 fails).
4. A single lever closes both the non-manipulability and the short-record conditions with no categorically different residue (F5 fails).
5. The recorded trajectory is shown to move with frame opinion, so it is not an anchor (F3 fails).
6. A transfer of an analogue method is shown to preserve every donor-side settling quantity, so R_T = 0 (F10 fails).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the reachability reading and the two opposed tracks (F1); do not lead with a quest for a better push.
2. Inventory proto-instruments already returning a failing-to-resolve reading before designing new ones (F2, F11).
3. Name the ground as the recorded trajectory and keep frames ranging separately against it (F3).
4. Report a narrowed shape plus the missing discriminator, never a point estimate (F8).
5. Treat EWS as a triage filter and price the channel that can degrade them (F6).
6. Record the source gate, shadow test and R_T for every analogue import (F10).
7. Keep the refuters in front of the links in any outreach (F9).

### Updated verdict
