Canonical framing: the figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a shape is: a narrowed region, rank or bound for φ*, plus the identity of the observation that would close it. It is not a point value. `SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar is the analogous form; here the analogous form is `THRESHOLD(???)SYSTEM` with a stated interval and a stated discriminator. A shape constrains: it says what any acceptable estimate must look like (a comparison between a feature and a control, a rank among systems, a bound), and it refuses a single number until a source-discriminating instrument exists.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary is the residual in effect terms: feature, control, comparison, trajectory, record.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the union reading in `01` F5: cannot push and short record must be unbundled.
- *Library of Babel* on cross-scale resemblance: *"the pattern of the shape of answers from these searches starts to look like the shape of problems you already worked out on a different scale"* This licenses the outward track, provided the transfer is of structure and method, not of value.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A refuter written in effect terms is checkable by someone who has never read the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* In this question the ground is the recorded trajectory, which cannot be pushed but does settle a crossing after the fact.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing the *method* of lag-1 autocorrelation from depression research is legitimate; importing the depression threshold value is not.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* Here a rising autocorrelation reads "loss of resilience" but cannot comment on which mechanism produced it or where the boundary lies. Several mechanisms give the same reading. The essay's conclusion: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* That is the honest stop in F8 and F12.

## 4. Why a single threshold value is the forbidden error

The error is metamerism: naming a source from a projection. Lenton 2008 reports threshold ranges and marks several as unidentified or missing, with the explicit note that values are preliminary expert assessments. A single value quoted without that provenance turns a visible choice into an invisible one. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

This is also why consensus is not a name. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* An expert elicitation is a budget for a claim to be tried, not a settlement of it.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote as if the shape-not-a-name rule forbade reporting any specific threshold. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, and the missing thing here is the source-discriminating instrument, not the threshold itself. Reporting a bounded interval, a rank or a bound is reporting a shape. What is forbidden is presenting a point value as settled when the discriminator has not run. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 13 :133-139 + *Hard Problem*).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** Quoted above. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an observation, a record or a natural experiment, settle it. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree is the named collapse.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any threshold and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
