## Three findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The published thresholds are ranges, not points.** Lenton 2008 reports critical values as intervals and marks several as *"Unidentified"* or *"Missing"*, with a footnote that numbers are *"preliminary and derive from assessments by the experts at the workshop."* This is what a shape looks like in practice, and it is honest about its provenance. Treat any single-number claim as owing a source gate.
2. **Early-warning signals are explicitly non-specific.** Scheffer 2012: slowing down is a *"broad spectrum"* indicator and *"Further diagnosis of what might be coming up requires additional information."* Dakos 2012 shows variance can decrease near a transition and that preprocessing choices change the result. This corroborates Fig 9 :60 rather than overturning it.
3. **Low-overlap fields supply the method, not the value.** Depression (PNAS 2014) and financial crises (Empirical Economics 2018) reuse the same indicators on different grounds. That is an outward import with a source gate and a shadow test. It corroborates the transferability of the *method* and carries no evidence about any Earth-system threshold.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the links.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13:** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* More recipients do not add an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) residual, shape claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 (always):** the residual and the pre-registered refuter, with a request for a failure. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
- **Stage 2 (opt-in only):** the figure text and the canonical README. Stage 2 never feeds the Stage 1 verdict.
- **Stage 3 (method reuse):** the eight-step loop. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** Stage 1 only works if the shape claim survives the checks in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` for the specific system in question. If it does not, withdraw it rather than sending a recording.
