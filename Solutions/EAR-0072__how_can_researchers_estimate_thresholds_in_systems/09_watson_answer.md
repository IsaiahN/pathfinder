# WATSON answer: thresholds in systems that cannot be experimentally pushed through them

**Mode: `specified` on the method, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No Earth-system measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

How can researchers estimate thresholds in systems that cannot be experimentally pushed through them?

Restated as an effect: how does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| get reduced for φ* when the action a that would sweep the system across the boundary is unavailable? R is indexed per system-slot and per candidate threshold, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Option A: treat this as a broken measurement and wait for a better push. Option B: treat it as a reachability problem, run two opposed tracks, triangulate against an unpushable but real ground, and report a narrowed shape with a pre-registered refuter. A can refine forever on an axis that cannot carry the quantity; B produces a checkable shape and an honest stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale: a threshold is an ordered comparison between a system feature and a control variable (Fig 12 :87), arity two, not symmetric (Fig 12 :89), indexed per system; scale runs from a single tipping element through a cascade to the lossy transfer between analogue frames (Fig 4).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Observed system | the system, its state variables, its recorded trajectory | observations o', residuals R | the recorded trajectory, real but not manipulable | admitted |
| D2 Model frame | models, parameters, ensembles, hindcasts | predictions Γ(b,a), reach closure(Γ) | settlement against D1 | admitted |
| D3 Analogue frame | systems that already crossed (paleo, other basins, other fields) | transferred methods and structural relations | each analogue's own ground | admitted, low overlap |
| D4 Early-warning frame | autocorrelation, variance, recovery rate, skewness | indicator strength | the ground, but only as a filter | admitted as filter |
| X1 exact threshold as a manipulable measurement | none nameable | none | none nameable | not a domain; the residual itself |
| X2 expert consensus as threshold | experts | agreement | none that frames cannot move | absorbed into D4, disqualified |

Union, not partition: "cannot push" and "short record" are two conditions (Fig 9 :57), so unbundle before searching. Dependency order: perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Strain test: reading non-manipulability as absence of an anchor strains Fig 2 :29; re-census shows non-manipulability is an instrument property (Fig 6), not an anchor property, so no strain remains.

**D2 Model frame, status triangulated (Fig 8 closure and Fig 7 room chain, distinct roles).**

- Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." -> more search inside one model family cannot manufacture the discriminating observation.
- Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not" -> a panel of agreeing modellers is one long room, not corroboration. Mode `general` for idempotence, `specified` for the model frame. Refuter: additional search inside the closure demonstrably closes R.

**D3 Analogue frame, status triangulated (Fig 8 union surplus, Fig 6 import, Fig 4 methods, three distinct roles).**

- Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." -> a low-overlap analogue frame supplies what the local closure lacks.
- Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." -> the inward and outward tracks are not the same operation.
- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." -> import the method and provenance, not the donor threshold value. Mode `specified`. Refuter: the analogue frame draws on the same evidence as the local frame, so the union has no surplus.

**D4 Early-warning frame, status triangulated (Fig 9 budget rule and Fig 10 channel decay, different objects).**

- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." -> indicators rank systems for attention; they do not carry the crossing verdict.
- Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" -> a rising indicator can be channel degradation, so a positive signal is not a witness.
- Operators table :52 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one" -> the indicator is comparison-like and goal-like, not a measurement of φ*. Mode `specified`. Refuter: an indicator deterministically fixes φ* across mechanisms.

**Union and method, status triangulated (Fig 6 two tracks, Fig 9 split, Fig 5 direction).**

- Fig 6 :28 "Which makes reachability two questions, not one. The instruments are opposed."
- Fig 6 :31 "Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."
- Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." -> seek a proto-instrument already returning a failing-to-resolve reading, not an imagined sensor.
- Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" -> split non-manipulability from short record.
- Fig 5 :65 "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like" -> bind the shape and the refuter before the run.

**Naming a specific φ*, status `gap`.** No figure line yields a number for any named system. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held. This is a verdict, not a silence.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

An unpushable threshold is a reachability problem with two opposed tracks, not a broken measurement. Sharpen a reading that already fails to resolve; find a low-overlap frame whose closure predicts the shape and import its method with provenance and a measured round-trip gap; treat early-warning signals as filters for budget, never as witnesses; triangulate separately against the recorded trajectory, which is a real but unpushable ground; deliver a narrowed shape, rank or bound plus the discriminator that would close it; and report a null result as unreachable-at-this-budget, never as absence. Mode `specified` on the method, `general` on the structural shape, ungrounded. A concrete φ* for any named Earth system is a declared `gap`.

- **Cost if wrong:** a researcher is sent looking for a better push when the missing thing is a discriminating observation, or treats a rising autocorrelation as a verdict and acts on a threshold that was never crossed. The cost is bounded because the packet names its refuters and its evidence pool. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
- **Undoable:** yes. A failed shape claim is refused, not deleted; the record grows and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The corrected packet supersedes the sent one, and no primitive is withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, in particular a discriminator returning a unique φ*, an indicator that fixes φ* deterministically across mechanisms, one lever closing both union conditions, a ground that moves with frame opinion, or a transfer with R_T = 0. A world source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that the inward and outward tracks are one instrument would also reverse it. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
