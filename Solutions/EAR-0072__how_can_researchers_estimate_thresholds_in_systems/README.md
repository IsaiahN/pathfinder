**Question:** How can researchers estimate thresholds in systems that cannot be experimentally pushed through them?

**Source:** `earth systems.txt` line 97

**Section:** 8. Tipping points, thresholds, and early warning

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No measurement settles the binding, so nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Standing of this derivation.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"), it cannot settle the question it reports on. Every substantive claim carries a mode label.

## Restatement as an effect

What is being decided: a researcher wants the location of a threshold φ* in a system where the control action that would sweep the system across that boundary cannot be applied. Option A: treat this as a measurement problem and wait for a better push. Option B: treat it as a reachability problem, characterise the residual in effect terms, and triangulate across frames that already bear on it. A leads to an unbounded wait for an instrument that may not exist; B leads to a bounded, checkable shape (an interval, a rank, a bound) with a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop.

What an answer would look like: not a point value, but a narrowed region plus the identity of the instrument that would close it. Bound that as an ordinary term before the run so it can accrue a residual and be refused (formula step "Direction", Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35)
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40)
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42)

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Note also that the Earth System Dynamics submission page requires authors to disclose AI tool use, which this simulated package must do if any part of it is ever submitted.
