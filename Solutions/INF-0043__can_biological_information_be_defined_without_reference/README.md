## The question restated as an effect

Two frames make incompatible claims about the same term, "biological information."

- Option A, the statistical or correlational frame: define information over a sequence or signal using probability, entropy, mutual information, or program length. No appeal to evolution, function, or survival is needed. This yields a usable, measurable quantity.
- Option B, the teleological or semantic frame: the properties that make a signal count as *biological* information (aboutness, asymmetry, accuracy conditions, normativity) require a function, and on the etiological account of function a selected effect, hence evolution, with survival or fitness as the relevant currency. Without those, the definition reaches only correlation, not information "for" an organism.

What each option leads to: A gives a definition that is clean, general, and blind to the very features (asymmetry, normativity, non-ubiquity) that biological practice treats as central; B gives a definition that captures those features but owes an import from the biological frame and, on one theory of function, an appeal to selection history. The residual is whether the semantic term is reachable in the closure of A's primitives, and whether "evolution", "function", and "survival" are one condition or three.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a per-slot verdict, one for the correlational sense, one for the semantic sense, one for the function-theory sense, each with a mode, a figure line, and a refuter. The sought-for shape is "a set of per-slot reachability results, not a single yes or no." That shape is written down here before the derivation so it can accrue a residual like any other term (Fig 5 :62-65).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a definition.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Simplicity, fame, or popularity of a definition is a budget filter, never evidence that the definition is right.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
