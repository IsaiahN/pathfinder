# Expert verification packet: the Tether on defining biological information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a philosopher of biology, information theorist, or philosopher of information can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** No measurement or discriminating experiment is claimed here.

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, NOT A GROUND READING.** It is checked for internal consistency against the figures and cannot settle the conceptual question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can biological information be defined without reference to evolution, function, or an organism's survival?" is a union of three slots with three different answers. A correlational measure over sequences and signals is definable inside a statistical closure with no appeal to any of the three. A semantic notion carrying aboutness, asymmetry, and accuracy conditions is not reachable from correlation alone, because mint composes inside a closure and never adds an atom; it needs a function atom imported from the biological frame with its provenance recorded. Once function is required, evolution, function, and survival remain distinct: organizational accounts of function make no appeal to selection history, etiological accounts do, and survival is one currency inside the etiological frame. The framework returns this shape, not a name for a winning theory, and it stops where the discriminating cases run out.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 13

- **Statement:** inside the statistical frame's closure, the correlational notion is composable but the semantic notion is not; the function atom must cross from a frame whose closure differs.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
- **What to check:** are entropy, mutual information, and description length in fact compositions over the statistical atoms (symbols, probabilities, channels, code lengths), and are asymmetry, normativity, and non-ubiquity absent from those atoms?
- **Refuter:** derive asymmetry, normativity, and non-ubiquity from correlation or complexity alone, with no added atom and no import. If this works, C1 fails.

### C2 - field-facing; checkable against the definition literature

- **Statement:** the two senses are structurally different in their bond, not just their vocabulary. Correlational information is symmetric; semantic information is asymmetric. This makes them isomers only when written over a shared atom set, and otherwise different molecules with a shared functional-group pattern.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- **What to check:** is it true that correlational information is symmetric while biological semantic information is asymmetric? Marc Artiga, "Biological Information", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2024, §1, states: "correlational information is symmetrical: if A carries information about B, then B carries information about A", and characterizes the biological notion as "asymmetric, normative, and non-ubiquitous." If that survey is wrong, C2 changes.
- **Refuter:** show a flat symbol-frequency measure that discriminates arrangements differing only in bond type and receiver relation, or show that the symmetry difference is a notational artifact. Then the bond is already recorded and C2 fails.

### C3 - field-facing; checkable against theories of biological function

- **Statement:** "evolution", "function", and "survival" are not one condition. A function can be attributed without selection history on organizational or causal-role accounts; etiological accounts require it; survival or fitness is a currency inside the etiological frame rather than a synonym for either.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
- **What to check:** Colin Allen, "Teleological Notions in Biology", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, revision 2026, states that organizational accounts "make no appeal to the selection history of the trait." Peter Schulte and Karen Neander, "Teleological Theories of Mental Content", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, revision 2022, state that teleological theories "usually understand these functions to be what the items with the functions were selected for, either by phylogenetic natural selection or by some other similar process." Are those characterizations faithful? If organizational accounts do in fact smuggle in selection history, the split collapses.
- **Refuter:** give a single account of function that is both non-evolutionary and non-selection-based and still yields normativity and aboutness, so the etiological and organizational slots are not genuinely distinct.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that Shannon information is irrelevant to biology, and not that it is explanatorily sufficient. It is measurable and useful; the claim is about which vocabulary can define which sense.
- Not that semantic information is unreal, and not that function or evolution are dispensable in biology. Fig 6 :27 says depth and genuine absence look the same from inside a frame; this is a reachability result relative to the statistical frame.
- Not a decision among teleosemantics, biosemantics, causal-informational, hybrid, or organizational accounts. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. No discriminating case has been run here.
- Not a claim that popularity, simplicity, or prestige of a definition is evidence. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating case, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The relevant tests are:

1. A signal with the right statistics but no biological function: is information present?
2. A trait with a function but no informational role (a heart pumping blood, Artiga 2024): is information present?
3. A molecule-by-molecule duplicate with no selection history (Swampman, Artiga 2024): does it carry information?
4. A case of functional indeterminacy (Dretske's magnetosomes, Schulte and Neander 2022): which content is the signal's?

If a definition survives these, it has ground contact. If none can discriminate, C1 and C2 remain structural claims only.
