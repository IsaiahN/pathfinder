## What the wider literature contributes

1. **The split is already there in the field.** Artiga (2024) separates correlational from teleological information and asks whether the expressions refer to the same entity. Adriaans (2023) reports three influential and largely incompatible definiens. This is world corroboration of the union finding in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1, from a pool independent of the framework.
2. **Function does not always entail evolution.** Allen (2026) distinguishes direct natural selection accounts from organizational and causal-role accounts, which "make no appeal to the selection history of the trait." This is a genuine conflict with a crude reading of the question's phrase "reference to evolution, function, or survival" as if the three were one condition. Per the WATSON rule, the conflict sends us back to the census, never flips the verdict: it is the reason S3 exists as a separate slot and why "function" and "evolution" are kept distinct in the answer.
3. **The Swampman case is a candidate anchor.** Artiga (2024) reports that on teleological theories a molecule-for-molecule duplicate with no selection history would carry no information, and "many regard this consequence as unpalatable." Schulte and Neander (2022) note that standard etiological theories do not entail that all functions are grounded in phylogenetic natural selection (meme selection, learning, conditioning). These are the discriminating cases a checker should range against any proposed definition.
4. **The "shape, not a name" limit has an independent analogue.** The Library of Babel essay's bundle passage and the Hard Problem essay's metamerism passage both describe the same discipline: split conflated questions, and ask for an instrument that discriminates sources rather than a name read off a projection. These are framework-author essays, so they are high-overlap corroboration, not independent witnesses.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a verdict on the output before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." No request for agreement, no request for trust.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure extract and the canonical README. Their later agreement with the framework is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they hold the interiors (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a generator to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter before the run and states the mode of the result. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay warns that LLM output is "simulacra", so a user must derive the result from the real architecture or work the formula themselves.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Do not send until `08_expert_verification_packet.md` has been checked line by line against `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.
