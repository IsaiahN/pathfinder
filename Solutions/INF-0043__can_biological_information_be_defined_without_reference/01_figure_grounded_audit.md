## 0. The residual in effect terms

"Biological information" is a name shared by at least two frames that do not use it the same way.

- The **statistical or correlational frame** (Shannon 1948, Kolmogorov complexity, mutual information) defines a quantity over symbols, probabilities, and sequences. It needs no function, no selection history, and no survival. It is measurable and frame-transparent.
- The **semantic or teleological frame** (function, selected effect, sender and receiver, normativity) defines a relation that is asymmetric, normative, and non-ubiquitous: an alarm call carries information about a snake, but the snake does not carry information about the call (Marc Artiga, "Biological Information", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2024, §1). On the etiological account of function this reaches selection history and fitness.

R, stated as an effect: **a term is wanted whose defining conditions apply to biological signalling and coding, and two frames disagree about whether those conditions can be stated inside a purely statistical vocabulary.** What is owed is not a cleverer measure of entropy. It is a decision about reachability: is the semantic term in `closure(Γ)` of the statistical frame, or does the wall have to move? And are "evolution", "function", and "survival" one condition or three?

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." So the verdict is kept per slot, never averaged into a single yes or no. The three slots are:

| slot | question | candidate frames |
|---|---|---|
| S1, correlational | can a measure of sequence or signal information be defined with no function, evolution, or survival? | statistics, coding theory, algorithmic complexity |
| S2, semantic | can aboutness, asymmetry, and accuracy conditions be defined with no function? | teleosemantics, sender-receiver theory, causal-informational accounts |
| S3, function theory | if function is required, must it be evolutionary and survival-linked? | etiological vs organizational/causal-role accounts of function |

The shape sought, written down before the run (Formula, "Direction"): **a per-slot reachability verdict with a mode and a refuter for each slot, plus a finding on whether the slots form a union or a partition.** A single unqualified "yes" or "no" would be the aggregation Fig 1 forbids.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

Describe R by arity, symmetry, scale. Then one line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet. Partition or union. Order by dependency (Fig 3).

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**
- *Arity*: at least three (two senses of information crossed with three candidate reference conditions). The question is compound, not binary.
- *Symmetry*: the semantic information relation is asymmetric (alarm about snake, not snake about alarm). Correlational information is symmetric (mutual information has no direction). The two senses differ structurally, so they cannot be one term without strain.
- *Scale*: molecular (sequences, codons), cellular (signalling pathways), organismal (survival and fitness), evolutionary (selection history). Each scale has its own ground contact.

**Domain 1, the statistical or correlational frame.**
- Actors: source, sender, channel, receiver, noise, symbols, probability distributions, programs.
- Currency: entropy, bits, mutual information, description length.
- Anchor: proof and computation. A measure is settled by calculation, not by what any biologist thinks. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- Question type: partition boundary. Shannon information is inside `closure(Γ)` here.

**Domain 2, the semantic or teleological frame.**
- Actors: signals, senders, receivers, traits, functions, selected effects, consumers.
- Currency: aboutness, asymmetry, normativity, accuracy and malfunction, fitness.
- Anchor: the biological ground, meaning what the receiver does and what selection preserved. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- Question type: semantic content is a compound of correlational information plus a function atom; it is not derivable from the statistical frame alone.

**Domain 3, the definitional or conceptual frame (philosophy of information and biology).**
- Actors: definitions, necessary and sufficient conditions, counterexamples (Swampman, misrepresentation, functional indeterminacy), rival theories of function.
- Currency: conceptual coherence, counterexample resistance, explanatory role.
- Anchor: conceptual adequacy checked against a ground case, not against agreement among definers. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- Question type: union, because the frame currently runs several definitions under one word.

**Union or partition.** Union. The two senses overlap (both can be measured on the same DNA sequence) but their conditions are not the same, so this is a compound with its own loop, not a list of separate questions (Fig 9 :54, :55). Evidence from the field: Adriaans and van Benthem report three influential definiens for information, "Shannon-probability; Kolmogorov-computation; Floridi-truth", with "next to nothing in common" ("Information", SEP, 2023). Artiga opens "Biological Information" (SEP, 2024) by asking "Do all these expressions refer to the same or to different entities?"

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Settle S1 first (the statistical measure is the link that works). Then S2, which composes S1 with a function atom. Then S3, because the answer to "must it be evolutionary?" depends on which theory of function is adopted. A definition of S2 attempted before S1 is settled will inherit an unmeasured statistical base; a decision on S3 before S2 will decide which atom is owed by preference rather than by the residual.

### F1 - The question is a union compound; split it before searching

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

Fig 9 :57-58 adds that the residue after a split "stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Applied here: asking one yes/no about "biological information" mixes two definitions of information with three candidate reference conditions. The honest answer is the per-slot table in §0.

**Status: triangulated.** Low overlap between the figure route (Fig 9 union/partition) and the essay route (*The Library of Babel*, 2026-10-02: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"). Two different descriptions converge on the same split. World corroboration (Adriaans 2023; Artiga 2024) is discussed in `09_watson_answer.md`.

**Refuter:** a single set of necessary and sufficient conditions is given that defines both the correlational and semantic senses with no residue left over, so a single lever clears the whole region and the union was a partition after all. If that is produced, F1 falls.

### F2 - Keep the residuals separate; a single averaged verdict is forbidden

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

**Status: single-source** (Fig 1). It governs the method, not a world fact. The world literature agrees in practice by treating correlational and semantic information as distinct entries (Artiga 2024 §1.1 and §1.2), but that is corroboration from a different pool, not a second figure.

**Refuter:** show that S1, S2, and S3 residuals trend together under one definition, so indexing by slot adds no information and the aggregation is harmless.

### F3 - MINT cannot manufacture the semantic atom inside the statistical closure (the barrier result)

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."
Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

Inside the statistical frame's `closure(Γ)` the available atoms are symbols, probabilities, channels, and description lengths. Composition over them yields entropy, mutual information, and complexity. "Function", "selected effect", "aboutness", and "normativity" are not among those atoms and cannot be minted from them, because mint composes and never adds an atom. The syntactic notion of information is therefore reachable in that closure; the semantic notion is not. The wall moves only on import from a frame whose closure differs, here the function frame.

This is a barrier-theorem result about reachability, not a claim that semantic information is unreal or unmeasurable in the world. Fig 6 :27 notes that "some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." The verdict is relative to the frame named, and it says so.

**Status: triangulated.** Fig 6 (what moves the wall) and Fig 13 (closed set, open arrangements) are two figures with low overlap, one about the operator and one about the set. The world literature supplies a third, independent witness: Artiga (2024) states that correlational information is symmetric, non-normative, and ubiquitous, while the biological notion is "asymmetric, normative, and non-ubiquitous", and concludes "many think that the relevant concept of biological information cannot straightforwardly be understood in terms of Shannon's approach." That is a world description of the same barrier, not a figure quote.

**Refuter:** derive asymmetry, normativity, and non-ubiquity from entropy, mutual information, or program length alone, inside the statistical closure, with no added atom and no import. Equivalently: show that the statistical frame already holds "function" as an atom, in which case there is no wall to cross. If either is done, F3 falls and the semantic slot changes to reachable.

### F4 - The import needs provenance and a shadow test

Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."
Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures"
Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

An etiological definition of biological information imports "function" from the philosophy-of-biology frame and "selected effect" from evolutionary biology. Those are atoms the statistical frame did not hold. Since convergent derivation and adopted import look the same in the content, the definition must carry a record of where each atom came from. A hybrid account is exactly the union of two frames with a surplus (Artiga 2024 §1.2: "a growing tendency to provide hybrid accounts that combine functional elements with correlational information"). That surplus is real only if the closures differ, which F3 establishes.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 8 and the world description of hybrid accounts (different evidence pool). The figure supplies the provenance rule; the literature supplies the instance.

**Refuter:** show that the imported function atom adds no surplus, that is, the statistical and functional frames' closures are identical with respect to the residual. Then the "import" is a redundant name and the provenance requirement adds nothing.

### F5 - From inside the syntactic frame, the framework can report a shape, not a name

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"
Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

The canonical usage rule says the same: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" (FIGURES_README_canonical.md). So the correct output of this derivation is not "biological information *is* selected-effect information" and not "it *is* mutual information." It is the shape of what would close the residual: an atom that supplies asymmetry, normativity, and non-ubiquity over a correlational base. The framework can report that the semantic term is out of reach from the statistical closure and characterize the missing atom in effect terms, but it cannot name it without already holding it.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 9, Fig 13, and the canonical figures README (local). Note the correction recorded in §5: this limit governs a *missing* atom. "Function" is not missing from the world literature; it is missing from the statistical frame. The shape claim is correctly scoped to that frame.

**Refuter:** name a substrate primitive that already carries aboutness and normativity in every frame at every level, so the missing atom is held after all and "unreachable" is the wrong verdict.

### F6 - A list of symbols is not the information; the bond carries part of it

Fig 12 :5: "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

The flat formula records ingredients and not arrangement. A Shannon-style count over a sequence records the symbol inventory and its statistics; it does not record which typed relation joins the symbols to a source, a receiver, and a state of the world. Two biological systems with the same base composition and different bonding or regulatory relations are different substances in Fig 12's sense. This is the figure-level reason for the world finding that correlational information is "a rather weak concept" (Adriaans 2023: "the string 0000011111 contains just as much Shannon information as the string 1001011100 because it has the same number of ones and zeros").

**Status: single-source for the figure definition** (Fig 12), with a world corroboration from a different pool (Adriaans 2023; Artiga 2024 on the failure of pure correlation). The two agree but do not share an author.

**Refuter:** show a flat symbol-frequency measure that discriminates two arrangements differing only in bond type and receiver relation. If such a measure exists, the bond is already recorded and F6 falls.

### F7 - The anchor for a definition is a discriminating case, not agreement among definers

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

For this question the anchor is a case that discriminates definitions: a signal with the right statistical profile but no function (noise, or a correlational accident), a trait with a function but no information (Artiga: "Hearts are supposed to pump blood, but they don't play an informational role"), or a molecule-for-molecule duplicate with no selection history (the Swampman case reported by Artiga 2024). A count of how many philosophers prefer a definition is not such an anchor. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

**Status: single-source** (Fig 2) for the anchor rule. The world cases that could serve as anchors are named in the SEP entries, but no measurement was taken here, so none has fired.

**Refuter:** exhibit a definition whose correctness varies with which community endorses it, with no discriminating case able to change that. Then the anchor cannot be pinned and F7 falls.

### F8 - Simplicity is a budget filter, never a witness

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

"Occam's razor" arguments of the form "this definition needs no function, therefore function is not required" use a budget filter as a witness. They decide which theory to try first, not whether the term is reachable. Likewise "function is needed because otherwise alarm calls would be mere correlations" uses explanatory satisfaction as a witness. Both are filters until a discriminating case is ranged against them.

**Status: single-source** (Fig 9). It is a methodological rule this framework imposes on itself, not a finding about biology.

**Refuter:** show a case where a definition's having fewer primitives is itself the ground that settles it, with no further discriminating contact.

### F9 - Dependency order: settle the link that works before rescuing the one that does not

Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Improvements to the statistical measure (sharper entropy estimators, better compression bounds) do not rescue the semantic definition, because the break is between correlation and content, not inside the correlation link. Fig 3 :26-27 notes that the middle link, "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress," has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests, so it attracts the least attention. Here the middle link is the function atom.

**Status: single-source** (Fig 3).

**Refuter:** exhibit a statistical refinement that yields normativity without adding an atom, which would move the break and make F3 and F9 fall together.

### F10 - Send the generator, not a recording

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

A finished definition sent to a checker before the checker has described its own residual is a recording of one occasion. The first contact therefore carries the residual, the per-slot shape, and the refuter. Links to the full framework come only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

**Status: single-source** (Fig 4, corroborated by Fig 10 on verifier non-access). Governs outreach only, not the verdict.

**Refuter:** show a case where sending the complete theory first leaves a checker's independence intact. By Fig 10 this should not be possible, but the rule is recorded as refutable rather than assumed.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the discipline stated in `THE_FORMULA.md` ("Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working") and by the outreach rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The verdict that follows from §2 is: **S1 reachable in the statistical closure; S2 not reachable without an imported function atom; S3 split into etiological and organizational readings rather than answered with one word.** This is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Barrier failure.** A definition of semantic biological information (asymmetric, normative, non-ubiquitous) is derived within the statistical closure from correlation or complexity alone, with no new atom (breaks F3, F6).
2. **Union collapse.** Correlational and semantic information are shown to be one concept with identical necessary and sufficient conditions, leaving no residue after a single lever (breaks F1, F2).
3. **Function-theory collapse.** A single account of function is shown to yield normativity and aboutness while being both non-evolutionary and non-selection-based, so the etiological and organizational slots are not genuinely distinct (breaks F1 at S3 and changes the scope of the verdict, but does not by itself establish S2).
4. **Substrate revision.** A re-census of Fig 13 admits aboutness or normativity as a primitive, so nothing is missing from any frame and the reachability claim is vacuous (breaks F5).
5. **Anchor failure.** A discriminating case (Swampman, mismatched-correlation signal, function-without-information trait) is shown to be indiscernible in principle between the competing definitions, so no ground could ever settle the choice (breaks F7).

If any fires, the affected slot is withdrawn and re-censused. Until one is run, no part of the answer is `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the split, not with a definition. The union finding (F1) is the strongest figure-grounded result and it is the thing a checker can verify in twenty minutes.
2. Report the three slot verdicts separately with modes attached. Do not compress them into a single "yes" or "no" (F2).
3. Mark the semantic slot as requiring an import, and record the provenance of the function and selection atoms (F4).

5. Use discriminating cases as the anchor (F7). Never count preferences as evidence (F8).
