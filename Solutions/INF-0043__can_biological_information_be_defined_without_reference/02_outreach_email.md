**Subject:** One question about "biological information" that may be three questions

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The question "can biological information be defined without reference to evolution, function, or survival?" does not have one residual. It has at least three slots, and the answer differs by slot.
>
> 1. A **correlational** measure over sequences and signals (Shannon entropy, mutual information, Kolmogorov complexity) is definable with no function, evolution, or survival. That slot is reachable inside a purely statistical vocabulary.
> 2. A **semantic** notion, one that is asymmetric, normative, and non-ubiquitous, carrying aboutness and accuracy conditions, is not reachable from correlation alone. It needs an added function atom imported from a different frame. On an etiological account of function that atom brings selection history and fitness; on an organizational or causal-role account it need not.
> 3. So "evolution", "function", and "survival" are not one condition. They are three different things a definition might appeal to, and they have to be answered separately.
>
> This is a structural claim about what can be composed from what, not a claim that any of these definitions is false or unreal. I am reporting a shape, not naming a winner.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that Shannon information is irrelevant to biology. It is useful and measurable. Not that function or evolution are dispensable in biology. Not that this derivation settles a philosophical debate by calculation. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against a set of figures, and it is labelled as such.
>
> **What would refute the output.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. Derive aboutness, normativity, and asymmetry from entropy, mutual information, or program length alone, with no added atom. That would collapse the barrier between slots 1 and 2.
> 2. Show that the correlational and semantic senses are one concept with identical necessary and sufficient conditions. That would collapse the three slots into one.
> 3. Show a single account of function that is both non-evolutionary and non-selection-based and still delivers normativity, so the etiological and organizational split is not real.
> 4. Name a primitive in the base set that already carries normativity, so nothing is missing and there is no import to report.
>
> If any of those holds, I will retract the affected slot. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
