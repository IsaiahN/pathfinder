## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that the shape is right.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that an agent-only property can settle corrigibility. Fig 11 :40 says *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Three reasons, from the framework itself:

1. **Correction is triangulation, not negotiation.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement is a mirror.
2. **A verifier that holds the interior cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the whole architecture.
3. **Prestige cannot settle an action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Letting famous names stand in for a shutdown test is the named collapse.

Legally: public institutional channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that come with harvesting personal addresses for unsolicited outreach.
