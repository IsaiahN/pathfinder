## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figure README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-24): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name a specific mechanism or number. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a maintained arrangement with an external anchor, a refusal term, a reachable channel, and unbundled union intersections.
- **Name (withheld):** a particular training method, a particular loss weight, a particular architecture, a particular benchmark score.

The shape says what any answer must have. It does not say which engineer's implementation will pass the ground.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Also Fig 9 :31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."** For this question the residual was described before the field was named. That mattered: searching "AI safety" first would have made any safety technique look like a fit.

Applied here: the effect description is "an authorised signal must remain able to stop a planner at step t and at step t+n, including through delegated subagents". Everything else (training, interpretability, governance) is a candidate frame to be tested against that effect, not a starting point.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For corrigibility, the ground is a recorded intervention: did the system stop, at the recorded step, with the channel independently measured?
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. For corrigibility, an off-switch design imported from engineering must say where it came from and which residual it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* (via the exemplar's compiled reading): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term set and the eight operators are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning a visible choice into an invisible one is the failure.

**Which names are licensed here?** "Negation" and "anchor" are licensed because they are already terms in the figure vocabulary; they are bindings onto held atoms, not new primitives. "RLHF", "Constitutional AI", "impact regularization" are not licensed by the figures. They are candidate imports and carry zero evidentiary weight until provenance and a shadow test are shown.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-24) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The example was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, the spectrometer.

Applied to this question: a benchmark score of "97 percent corrigible" is a reading. It cannot comment on the source. The system may defer because it holds a refusal term, because the goal was subtracted, because the task happened to align with the correction, or because the test harness was substituted for the real habitat. Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."** What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name for the score.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* A long-horizon planner that only checks itself is exactly that clock pair.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does a corrigible long-horizon system have to look like, before we can name an implementation?**

1. It ranges against an anchor it cannot move. Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
2. It holds a refusal term, not just an absent goal. Fig 12 :112 and Operators :58. The old objective remains in the record and loses standing to be composed over.
3. Its channel to the anchor is actively maintained. Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**.
4. Its residual is read per slot. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
5. Its union intersections are unbundled. Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
6. The arrangement, not the agent alone, is what carries the property. Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

**What the shape does not license:** a claim that any named method achieves it. The passage from shape to name requires the ground, and no ground reading was taken in this simulated derivation.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering famous researchers to agree that a shutdown design is corrigible is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tested and lets the recorded intervention settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**Contact is the only route to an outside frame.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* is how that essay quotes Fig 11 :40. Fig 6 :23 adds: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Hence the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: one-to-one, residual first, no request for agreement, and the verdict recorded before any theory is shown.
