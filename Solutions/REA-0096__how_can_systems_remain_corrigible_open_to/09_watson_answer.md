## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about corrigibility under long-horizon planning. Option A: treat corrigibility as an agent property installed by training or a learned scalar objective. Option B: treat it as a maintained arrangement with an outside anchor, a refusal term, a reachable channel, and unbundled union conditions. What each leads to: A improves a link that already works and, if contact is unchanged, changes nothing (Fig 11 :40); B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and the instruments that would close it. The residual is per slot: off switch, objective, channel, delegated subagents.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the implementation claims do not. Corrigibility under long horizon is a maintained arrangement, not an agent property: an external anchor that does not update, a refusal term that makes an old objective lose standing to be composed over, a channel actively kept reachable against a ground that does not decay, per-slot residuals, and unbundled union conditions. The break is at objective and vocabulary, not planning. Refusal and subtraction are isomers, so a disabled goal is not the same substance as a refused goal. Corrections are imports from a closure that differs and need provenance and a shadow test. There is no terminal safe state. What remains `specified`, ungrounded, and candidate is every claim about which method, weight, architecture, or benchmark achieves this. This is a simulated derivation; it has not been settled.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (the full architecture) instead of a generator (the residual and refuter). Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Here the cost is a deployed planner that resists shutdown, so the price is high and the mistake is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and nothing is deleted. A refused term stays in the record and loses standing to be composed over, so a defective implementation can be superseded by a corrected packet without erasing the lineage. A sent claim can be retracted with its refuter intact. A training method that changed contact can be replaced; a claim that did not change contact can simply be dropped.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A recorded intervention showing a pinned internal estimate predicting external shutdown outcomes would reverse F1; an adversarial battery in which subtraction matches refusal would reverse F3 and the isomer claim; a degraded channel that leaves metrics accurate and the switch passing would reverse F6; an agent-only change that moves the ground reading with contact unchanged would reverse F5 and the capability claim as Fig 11 :72 anticipates; a derivation of a refusal term wholly inside the planner's closure would reverse F7. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
