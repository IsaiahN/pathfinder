## QUESTION

Should public companies face different incentives and obligations from private-equity-owned or founder-controlled firms? Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| differ by ownership slot, and what design rule should bind incentives and obligations to each slot? Option A, one uniform regime, leads to a pooled reading that hides which slot is losing contact (Fig 1 :22). Option B, a different regime asserted per form without a ground test, leads to an authored convention that decides its own outcome (Fig 2 :43). The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: per-slot indexing, one kernel re-bound, unbundled union, reachable ground, written falsifiers.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The difference between what a governance regime predicts and what its ground returns, indexed by firm and by ownership form. Arity: four-place (regime, belief, library, outcome), indexed by slot and ordered by time; carries an ordered bond (`→`) because governance acts before outcomes, and a comparison bond (`⋛`, "a threshold relation between two quantities", Fig 12 :87) because "should" is a threshold judgment. Symmetry: asymmetric; "better" is relative to the ground, not absolute (Fig 12 :85-89). Scale: firm loop, population of firms, regulatory frame; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, firm governance loop.** Actors: managers, board, owners, incentives, obligations. Currency: decisions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized firm outcomes per form. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, ownership habitat.** Actors: dispersed public shareholders, PE sponsors, founder, creditors, workers, entrants, regulators. Currency: control, capital, time horizon, information. Anchor: market verdict and counted stakeholder outcomes. Admitted. Ownership form is a slot here.
- **Domain 3, governance convention library.** Actors: rules, incentive devices, disclosure and obligation regimes as conventions. Currency: reach, |φ| cost. Anchor: settlement against the ground (Fig 5 :32). Admitted.
- **Domain 4, verification and regulatory frame.** Actors: regulators, legislators, standard setters, researchers, outside checkers. Currency: rulings, conventions, evidence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement; the seat's stake must be declared (Seat Map :171). Admitted.
- **X1, ownership form as a free-floating good or bad property.** Actors: the form itself. Currency: presumed virtue. Anchor: none independent of the residual. Not a domain yet, excluded by the anchor test (Fig 11 :49-51).
- **X2, one uniform global rule as the only serious option.** Its anchor is administrative comparability, an instrument, not the ground (Fig 2 :30). Absorbed into the union analysis.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D3 and D2/D4 form overlapping compounds (Fig 9 :54-57). The bundle splits three ways: incentives versus obligations; three ownership forms; contact condition C1 versus reach condition C2. Unbundle before searching. **Order by dependency:** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20, :24). The middle link has no instrument and breaks first.

**Strain test applied.** An early attempt to apply Fig 13's six physical terms to governance only fit by calling incentives gradients and duties networks; a strain, so Fig 13 is relegated to the honest-stop role (S1). An early attempt to use ⋛ as a boundary between public and private ownership was a type error (Fig 12 :87); re-census keeps ⋛ for the threshold in "should" (S2). The Steel versus Filatotchev conflict was resolved by layer separation: standardization at the convention layer, heterogeneity at the binding layer (R2). No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures return a design shape, not a yes or a no. **Measure R per ownership form and per firm; keep one governance kernel with re-bound actors, currency and anchor; treat public, PE and founder firms as isomers over a shared atom set; unbundle incentives from obligations, the three forms from one another, and contact failure from reach failure; keep governance conventions from becoming targets; keep the channel to the ground reachable and renew reach from closures that differ.** Refuse a standing ranking of forms and refuse a blanket uniformity of rules. Which concrete incentive or obligation belongs to which form is a `gap`, not a hidden answer: the framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Mode `specified` for the shape, `general` for the structural laws. The verdict is kept separate per domain and never averaged.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected reform. A form whose real failure is a dead channel to outcomes would be given more reach, or a form whose reach has stopped moving would be given better metrics, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs several. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded by the written refuters and the provenance trail, which make an error locatable rather than invisible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no governance primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: per-form residual measurement showing identical residuals after controlling for habitat; a demonstration that the governance loop changes kind between forms; a form whose measured valence is invariant across a change of residual; two forms reducing to the same bond with the same residual; a convention that decides an outcome and stays uncorrupted against an unmoved ground; a single lever restoring both conditions with no different residue; or a figure re-census that breaks per-slot indexing or the invariance of the kernel. A genuinely low-overlap source showing that per-form differences are fully predicted by a form-blind average would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
