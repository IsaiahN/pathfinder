# Expert verification packet: the Tether on multiple origins and a single survivor

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the survivorship prediction. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of the number of origins is claimed.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Distinct arrival routes are composable, so the figures do not exclude more than one origin. In the unmaintained mode, selection keeps what the ground kept, so a single surviving lineage does not imply a single arrival. The count cannot be read off one survivor's molecular record, because the artefact does not carry which route produced it, and finding no second genesis is not a proof of absence. "More than one environment" splits into one origin using several venues and several independent origins; these are different molecules under the bond rule, not variants of one term. The framework returns this shape, states its falsifiers in advance, and refuses the number until an instrument returns it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** arrangements over held atoms are not closed, so more than one distinct arrival route is composable; and an environment is part of the term, so one origin across several venues differs from several independent origins.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Fig 13 :76 "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Fig 12 :95 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."
- **Refuter:** prove that early-Earth prebiotic chemistry admits exactly one arrival route, or that venue exchange changes no observable. The first breaks plurality, the second breaks the term distinction.

### C2: biology-facing; checkable by a researcher against the phylogenetic and instrument record

- **Statement:** the unity of the genetic code settles common descent among extant life, not the number of origins; one survivor among many arrivals is the expected shape when selection runs.
- **What to check:** is that separation defensible? Crapitto et al. (2022) state that the LUCA lineage was the sole survivor of multiple contemporaneous lineages. Mariscal et al. (2019) state that the single-origin assumption "may simply be because this situation is currently indistinguishable from the scenario in which life originated several times". Cleland and Copley (2005) state that the standard microbial tools could not detect an alternative form of life if it existed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **Refuter:** show that multiple arrivals cannot collapse to one survivor, so that one lineage entails one origin; or produce a source-discriminating assay that returns a definite count.

### C3: framework-internal derivation with one live strain

- **Statement:** the count is a `gap`, not a silence; the missing thing is an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :128 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument built from a description alone, with no prior failing reading, that resolves the count. That breaks the instrument rule. Or close the gap directly with a ground reading.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that life arose once, and not that it arose many times. No event count is asserted.
