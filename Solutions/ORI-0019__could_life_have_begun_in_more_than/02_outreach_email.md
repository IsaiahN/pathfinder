# Outreach draft: check one shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the sender has read `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and can state the pre-registered refuter without looking. If the shape does not survive validation, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about multiple origins, and what would refute it

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** Every organism we know descends from one last universal common ancestor, with one genetic code, one chirality and one energy currency. That is a reading from one survivor. It is compatible with one origin in one place, and also with several origins in several places followed by the extinction of all but one lineage. The observed unity of life does not by itself count the arrivals.
>
> **The shape claim (`general` on structure, `specified` on the survivorship prediction, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. Distinct arrival routes are composable. The framework's minimum set is "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists" (Fig 13 :115), and it states that "Different routes can arrive at the same substance" (Fig 13 :121). So the figures do not forbid more than one origin, and they do not add up the number that actually happened.
> 2. One survivor among many arrivals is the expected shape when selection runs. "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept" (Fig 11 :56). Also: "variation inside, selection between" (Fig 7 :33). A single lineage therefore does not imply a single arrival.
> 3. The count itself is not read off the survivor. The record of a composed thing does not carry which route produced it: "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available" (Fig 13 :128). Searching one survivor and finding no second genesis is not proof of absence: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before writing this note:
>
> 1. A demonstration that, under early-Earth boundary conditions, the closure of plausible prebiotic chemistry admits exactly one arrival route. That would break the plurality step.
> 2. A demonstration that many arrivals cannot collapse to one survivor, so that one lineage would entail one origin. That would break the survivorship step.
> 3. An instrument already returning a reading that fails to resolve source, which then returns a definite number of origins from surviving molecular architecture. That would close the evidence gap.
>
> If any one holds, the shape claim fails and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Stage 1 question:** what would you count as a failure of this shape, and what would you expect to see if it were true? I will record your answer before showing you any source material.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three links to the figures, the formula and the canonical usage rule. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
>
> **Only if you then want the method:** the formula can be reused on other problems. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Sender discipline

1. Send to a public, opt-in channel only. One recipient or one venue per message.
2. Record the stage-1 verdict in your own notes before replying with links.
3. Never pool later agreement as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
4. A refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated. It is never deleted.
