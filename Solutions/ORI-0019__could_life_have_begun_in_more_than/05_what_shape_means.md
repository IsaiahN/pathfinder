## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The canonical README reads: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is honest. Fig 9 :32 puts it in the figures' own words: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

*The Library of Babel* gives the operational version: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is a narrowed form of an answer that constrains without filling. For this question, the shape is: distinct arrivals are composable, a filter can reduce many to one, one survivor's reading does not count its sources, and a source-discriminating instrument is owed. "One origin", "two origins", "vent origin", "hot-spring origin" are names, not shapes.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- The same essay names the failure mode that governs this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is F1 in `01`: the question is a union compound, not one hard question.
- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Describe the effect (one survivor's reading, several possible sources), not the cause (a preferred venue).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach artifact sends a prediction and its refuter, not the architecture.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." Here that would be a discriminating measurement, not a review article.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second item: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And it refuses neutrality: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot. But visibility is not neutrality." The six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." So a figure-grounded answer here is a visible choice of axes, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. The four thought experiments, applied to origins

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives four cases that map exactly onto the difficulty here.

1. **The colour green.** The projection says green; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet known. *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the general law: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The instrument "cannot comment on the source". Applied here: the universal genetic code is a reading, and common descent is one source among several that could produce it.
2. **Video frame versus photograph.** The two artefacts are indistinguishable and carry different warrants; nothing in the image records which. Applied here: the surviving lineage is the frame, not the video. Its provenance was lost in the transformation.
3. **Lab-grown versus mined diamonds.** Here provenance *is* recoverable, but only when isotope ratios become readable with a tool that can perceive the trace. This is the hopeful case: a trace may exist and a better instrument may resolve it. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" and that the colour case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."
4. **The replicator.** Each recursive pass adds real material from the practitioner's expertise. Applied here: an expert who supplies measurements narrows the venue question; the framework alone supplies the shape and not the venue.

The essay's summary rule governs all four: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A single survivor's record that is indiscernible from one origin's record is not thereby proof of one origin.

## 5. Direct answer to the "what shape?" question for ORI-0019

**The shape has four edges.**

- **Plurality is not excluded.** The arrangements over the held kinds are not closed, so more than one distinct arrival route is composable. This is a claim about what is *licensed*, not what happened.
- **The filter explains the one-sided observation.** In the unmaintained mode nothing maintains anything and what survives is what the ground kept. Many to one is the expected shape, so one survivor carries no information about the number of arrivals.
- **The record is lossy in a specific way.** The artefact carries the atoms and bonds of the survivor, not the route that produced it. The lineage would carry the route, but the pre-LUCA lineages are gone.
- **The missing thing is an instrument with a particular shape.** It must already be returning a reading that fails to resolve source, so it can be improved from a worse instrument. Naming a specific assay would be a preference, and Formula step 2 says a preference "predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."

The canonical limit applies to the count exactly as it does to a missing primitive: the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it". Here it cannot say which count is right, because asserting one would mean already holding the discriminator.

## 6. Prestige, and the status of this audit

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of eminent origins researchers who favour one origin is a budget, not a verdict.

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay calls such output "simulacra". The canonical article repeats the caveat: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula".

**Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading and cannot settle the question it reports on.** No experiment was run, no sample was measured, and no count was inferred.
