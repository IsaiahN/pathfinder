## The residual, stated as an effect

All extant life shares one genetic code, one chirality, one energy currency, and one last universal common ancestor. The question asks whether that single surviving lineage settles how many origin events and how many environments there were. Option A: read the survivor as proof of one arrival in one place. Option B: read the survivor as the output of a filter that ran over an unknown number of arrivals in possibly different places. A leads to closing the search for a second genesis and to forcing a choice between competing venue hypotheses. B leads to a checkable shape, an open count, and a named instrument gap.

The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: an answer to this question must say (i) whether distinct arrival routes are composable at all, (ii) why arrivals outnumber survivors if they do, and (iii) what instrument could discriminate sources from a single survivor's reading. That binding is the refusable object in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence."
3. **A verifier must not hold the interior.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the shape and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in routes in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
