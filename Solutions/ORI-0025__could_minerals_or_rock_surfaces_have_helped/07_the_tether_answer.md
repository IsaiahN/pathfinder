# The Tether's answer: could minerals or rock surfaces have helped prebiotic assembly?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Prebiotic monomers in bulk aqueous solution stay dilute, short, unoriented and short-lived. A genetic or catalytic system needs the opposite. The gap is a **union compound** of at least four overlapping conditions: concentration, alignment, bond formation by a part that survives its own use, and protection or release; with a fifth condition (chirality) appearing on some minerals. Fig 9 :55: *"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Each condition has its own loop and its own ground. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Capability belongs to the habitat, not to the mineral alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A mineral is an actor in a habitat that also contains feedstock, water activity, UV, wet-dry cycling and gradients. A claim of "minerals instead of" is the exclusion error (Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*).
2. **The habitat must be enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Read what was in contact before choosing montmorillonite, pyrite or colemanite (Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*).
3. **A catalyst is a role, and it survives its own use.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A mineral surface can hold this role; so can ions released by a dissolving mineral. Which actor holds it is an empirical question. Ferris 1996 shows reuse across feedings; Rimoldi 2025 shows the catalytic role partly in solution.
4. **"Organize" is a bond claim.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Products differing only in monomer order are different substances. Rimoldi 2025's AGG, GAG and GGA (8:47:45) and Li 2024's enantiomeric excess are arrangement results, not yield results.
5. **The framework returns a kind of thing, never a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Naming a mineral is a binding from the experimental frame, provenanced as such, not a figure derivation and not an import that adds an atom.
6. **A laboratory result is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Every cited experiment proves that a mechanism is wired. None proves that the Hadean Earth ran it.
7. **The ground decides; agreement does not.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The anchor here is an instrument that discriminates surface from solution chemistry, not the number of agreeing papers.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named mineral *is* the answer. Montmorillonite, pyrite, colemanite and layered double hydroxides are candidate occupants of the roles, each with a provenance and a measured condition.
- Not that minerals were necessary. A wet-dry or hydrothermal pathway that closes the four slots without mineral contact remains possible; failing to find it is not proof it does not exist (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that a flask proves a Hadean setting. Fig 11 :70.
- Not that the solid surface rather than a dissolved ion did the catalysis in any particular experiment. Rimoldi 2025 explicitly puts the surface *"in the background."*
- Not a derivation of life from minerals, and no new primitive. The bindings of spectrum, gradient and network onto mineral ordering, dehydration and scaffolding add no atom (Fig 6 :22).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of the early Earth is claimed here, and this agent took no measurement.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer that resolved the case. Here:

- **The discriminator:** an experiment that runs the same feedstock, concentration and cycle with (a) an intact mineral surface, (b) the dissolved ions that mineral releases, (c) no mineral at all, and (d) a wet-dry cycle without mineral, and measures chain length, sequence distribution and enantiomeric excess for each. HPLC-MS plus TPD plus controls for vortex and stirrer field (as in Li 2024) already exist as the worse instrument to improve from (Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*).
- **The escape test:** can the oligomer leave the surface and compose further at the next level (Fig 12 :104)? Lambert's question, quoted by Erastova 2017: *"If the initial steps of life really occurred on surfaces, how then did life escape surfaces at a later stage?"*
- **The field test:** a geological trace tying a specific product distribution to a specific mineral assemblage in a setting whose conditions are independently established. That moves the claim from substituted habitat toward capability.

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6: a mineral-free pathway closing all four slots; irreversible binding under plausible conditions; a re-census breaking the catalyst role; an instrument showing the reported alignments or excesses are artefacts.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The question of *which* mineral, and *whether* it happened, is unreachable from the figures: naming it would mean already holding it. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes."* Declaring the six makes the choice visible, not neutral. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a prebiotic mineral and does not claim minerals were necessary. It says that prebiotic assembly is a union of at least four conditions, not one question: concentration, alignment, bond formation by something that survives its own use, and protection or release; that capability belongs to the whole habitat, so minerals sit beside wet-dry cycles, UV and feedstock rather than replacing them; that "organize" is a bond claim, so products differing only in order are different substances; and that every laboratory demonstration is a substituted habitat that proves the mechanism is wired, not that the early Earth ran it. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the discriminating instrument that would close the shape. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
