## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the actor holding the catalyst role**. Fig 12 :119 gives a role; Rimoldi 2025 gives dissolved ions plus a surface with a concentration function. The shorthand's "a catalyst survives its use" does not determine the material.
- the **equivalence of lab capability and field capability**. Fig 11 :70 says a test harness proves wiring only; the shorthand's "minerals can do the work" is true of a substituted habitat and not established of a Hadean setting.
- the **number of live slots**. Fig 1 :19 indexes the residual per slot; the shorthand's "minerals help" averages concentration, alignment, catalysis and protection into one lever.
- the **source of the product reading**. Fig 12 :46 and the source essay say the reading does not determine which bond or which actor produced it; the shorthand's "organize" can be read as naming a surface.

Conversely, three things survive cleanly: capability belongs to agent-and-habitat; arrangement is the substance; and the framework returns a kind of thing, never a name. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
