## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Yes in shape, open in name, ungrounded as history.** The figures license a shape with four slots: a habitat condition that concentrates, a structure that aligns, a part that survives its own use to catalyze, and an instrument that decides which actor held each role. Mineral surfaces and mineral-derived ions are figure-consistent candidates for those roles, and world experiments show they can do parts of the work under laboratory conditions. The figures cannot name a mineral, cannot establish that minerals were necessary, and cannot turn a substituted habitat into a Hadean history. The name and the history are `gap` from the figures and `candidate` from the world sources. All domain verdicts remain separate and are never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The corrected packet is mode-labeled, states four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a mineral name and a literature review instead of a shape and a discriminator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective readings (mineral named, surface assumed catalytic, lab treated as history, agreement pooled) are removable without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A four-arm experiment (intact surface, mineral-released ions, no mineral, wet-dry without mineral) at matched feedstock that discriminates length, sequence and enantiomeric excess would name the actor; a demonstration that the product cannot leave the surface would break the recursion at Fig 12 :104; a geological trace tying a product distribution to a specific assemblage in an independently characterized setting would move the history toward `grounded`; a figure re-census that breaks the catalyst-survives-use role would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
