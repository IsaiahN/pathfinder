## Restatement in effect terms

What is being decided is not whether minerals exist, but whether contact with a mineral surface closes a specific gap: the gap between a dilute, disordered, short-lived mixture of prebiotic monomers and the concentrated, aligned, longer, information-bearing oligomers that a genetic or catalytic system needs. Option A (minerals helped) leads to a habitat in which a persistent surface concentrates, orients and catalyzes monomers. Option B (they did not) leads to a habitat in which the same work is done by wet-dry cycles, hydrothermal gradients, ice eutectics, meteorite delivery or some other condition. The figures decide neither option by experiment. They decide the **shape** an answer must have and refuse the **name** of the mineral.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a checkable statement of which actor in the habitat performs concentration, which performs alignment, which survives its own use as a catalyst, which instrument discriminates surface from solution chemistry, and what would refute the binding. That binding is written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 and in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §5 before the verdict is stated.

It can only state the shape a checkable answer must have and the refuter that would break it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that a named mineral (montmorillonite, pyrite, colemanite, layered double hydroxide) is *the* answer. The figures return a kind of thing, never a name, and the ground decides the name.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement among people selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **An interest in the reading is disqualifying.** Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.
3. **A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42. Sending the full architecture before asking for a verdict spends the independence of the person checking it.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
