## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

For ORI-0025, a **shape** is the four-slot constraint stated in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0: something concentrates dilute monomers, something aligns them, something survives its own use to form bonds, and an instrument decides which actor did each job. "A mineral surface" is a candidate occupant of the first three slots. "Montmorillonite" or "pyrite" is a name. The shape constrains; it does not fill.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. This is why the residual was described in effect terms (dilute to concentrated, disordered to ordered, short to long) before any mineral was named.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* "Catalysis by clay" is a cause-word; "oligomers grow from 10 to 55 monomers in contact with a surface" is an effect-description anyone can check.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` sends exactly that: what you would see if surface templating were doing the work, and what would refute it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done because *"the finish line will also be the starting place."* For this question the start and finish are the same residual: the gap between a dilute mixture and an ordered polymer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The experiments of Ferris 1996, Erastova 2017, Rimoldi 2025 and Li 2024 pay for specific bindings under specific conditions. They have not paid for a Hadean history.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming montmorillonite as *the* prebiotic surface is an import from mineralogy, not a figure result.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six-term set is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Binding spectrum, gradient and network onto mineral ordering, dehydration and scaffolding (F9 of `01`) makes that guess auditable; it does not turn it into a discovery about minerals.

## 4. Why the source question matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* — one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same error is live in this question. A product distribution (55-mers, or an 8:47:45 Gly-Ala isomer ratio, or 42% D-glutamic acid) is a reading. The source could be a solid surface, dissolved ions released by a dissolving mineral, a wet-dry cycle, a vortex, a stirrer field, or a combination. Rimoldi 2025 is unusually honest about exactly this: their reproducible result came from colemanite-derived borate in solution, with the mineral surface in the background. Naming "the surface" from the product reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction in its own terms: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. ... And nothing in the image records which."* A product mixture is the frame; the route that made it is the video. Fig 12 :46 agrees: *"which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*

## 5. Q answered: what licenses a particular mineral?

**Nothing in the framework.** A particular mineral is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the mineral name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. That is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks the reader to name an instrument, not to pick a favourite clay.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any mineral at all. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`. The mineral names here are not claimed as new primitives; they are bindings from the experimental frame onto roles the figure already names (catalyst that survives its use, arrangement as substance, contact in a habitat). Naming a mineral as a *candidate occupant of a role* is therefore permitted, with provenance. What is forbidden is claiming the framework derives the name, or that the binding adds an atom and moves the wall (Fig 6 :22).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The product reading still does not determine the source; that is why the discriminator, not the mineral name, is the thing owed. Both statements are now on the record so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree with the mineral story is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the four-slot claim a budget to be tried, and let an instrument settle it.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the prebiotic claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. One-paragraph version

A shape here is a constraint you can state before you can name its occupant: something must concentrate prebiotic monomers, something must align them, something must survive its own use to form bonds, and an instrument must decide which actor did each job. The figures give that shape and the canonical limit that they return a kind of thing, never a name. The eight essays add that a name needs a ground or a provenanced import, and that a product reading cannot comment on its source, so the discriminating instrument is owed before any mineral is crowned. Every derivation in this folder is a simulation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.
