## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether the origin of life is one identifiable transition or many gradual steps. Option A: one event and a boundary that can be located. Option B: a route of many small steps with no privileged event. What each leads to: A owes a name for a boundary, a criterion that decides it, and an instrument that returns a discriminating reading; B owes a typed route, a level crossing, and an account of where the surplus over closed chemistry comes from. The figures are asked which shape is licensed before either option is chosen.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what closed chemistry composes and what a living system composes. Arity: five or more conditions (replication, catalysis or metabolism, compartmentalization, heredity with variation, unit of selection), so a union, not a binary partition. Symmetry: ordered, chemistry before biology, so sequence and production, not a symmetric join. Scale: molecular through protocellular to cellular and population, crossing at least one scale membrane. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **D1, chemistry / geochemistry frame.** Actors: molecules, catalysts, autocatalytic sets, mineral surfaces, gradients, compartments. Currency: reaction flux and free energy. Anchor: wet-lab experiment and the geochemical record. Question type: which bonds compose a route, and what crosses in from a differing frame.
- **D2, evolutionary / biology frame.** Actors: replicators, collectives, units of selection, lineages. Currency: heredity, variation, differential persistence. Anchor: selection, which runs when no maintainer exists. Question type: which level crossing changes the unit of selection.
- **D3, the categorising frame.** Actors: the term "life", the boundary predicate, the researcher. Currency: comparison and agreement. Anchor: an instrument that discriminates the boundary, currently missing. Question type: what kind of thing a "transition" is. This domain has no nameable anchor yet, so it is a gap, not a settled domain.
- **D4, verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so agreement between them is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Cycle before replicator, replicator before unit of selection, unit of selection before retrospective label, and discriminating instrument before any boundary name. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test at the census: the six-term physical substrate does not map directly onto origin-of-life conditions. Re-census assigns the six to what composes at any level and the origin conditions to bindings onto held atoms. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Life did not begin in one identifiable transition in the sense of a nameable event atom. In the world it began through many gradual steps: a bonded route in a closed chemistry, with the surplus supplied by contact between differing frames and the unit of selection changing in phases. The "single identifiable transition" is the other side of a union: a threshold relation and a convention imposed by a categorising frame, with life as a retrospective label on a lineage that met its ground. The exact boundary is a `gap`, reported as unreached, not as proof of absence and not as silence. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode: `specified` on domains D1 and D2, `specified` analogue on the life-label transfer in D3, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the boundary. Not `grounded`, and a simulated derivation only.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, six refuters and the provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name where a shape is owed, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the route, the cut and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused name and the union framing can be withdrawn without touching the chemistry or evolution findings. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already contain the corrected form. No primitive has to be given up, and no world source has to be discarded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument returning a boundary event with a non-updating anchor would close the D3 gap; a measured MLS1 to MLS2 shift dated to a process would tighten D2; a demonstration that the route is recoverable from the end artefact alone would break Fig 13 :129; a figure re-census admitting "life" as a primitive or collapsing the union to a partition would break the structural verdict; a maintainer selecting the first living system before selection runs would break the analogue. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
