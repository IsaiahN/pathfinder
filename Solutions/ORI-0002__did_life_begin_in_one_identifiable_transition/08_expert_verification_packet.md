# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the origin of life

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the exact boundary. Not `grounded`.** No instrument is claimed to have located a boundary. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "one identifiable transition or many gradual steps?" is a union, not a partition. The world side is a route: many bonded steps (sequence, production, catalyst, cycle), a closed chemistry whose own compositions add no atom, and a surplus supplied by contact between differing frames, with the unit of selection changing in phases. The question side asks for a cut: a transition is a threshold relation between two quantities, and a boundary defined by a researcher is a convention, not an object in the world. Life reads as a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground; what ran was selection. The route is not recoverable from a modern cell, only from its lineage, and the exact boundary is a `gap` because no instrument returns a discriminating reading. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the either/or is a union of a route and a cut, not one question with two competing answers. The residual is per condition, not one global predicate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **Refuter:** show that all origin-of-life conditions share one anchor and one currency, so the union collapses to a partition, or show that a single global "alive" residual is sufficient and per-slot indexing adds nothing.

### C2. Framework-internal, checkable against Figures 6, 8 and 12

- **Statement:** gradual steps are a bonded route, and composition inside a closure never adds a primitive; the surplus comes from a union of frames. RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first are isomers over a shared atom set and members of a functional group over their narrow sets.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **What to check:** is a gradual route in the field actually described by typed bonds (cycles, catalysts, production), or is the bond language only a redescription after the fact? Does a purely internal chemistry composition ever add a primitive without contact with a differing frame?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a route expressible as an unordered ingredient list with the same predictions, or demonstrate an atom added by internal composition alone.

### C3. Ground-facing, checkable by an instrument or a record

- **Statement:** the exact boundary is a `gap`; the route is not recoverable from the end artefact, and life is a retrospective label on a lineage.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :129 *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* Fig 11 :36 *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **What to check:** name any instrument, at any resolution, that returns a reading distinguishing a first living state from its immediate non-living predecessor, with a reference that does not move when the definition of life changes. If none is known, the `gap` stands. If one exists, the gap closes and the claim changes.
- **Refuter:** a discriminating measurement, or a demonstration that the route is recoverable from the modern artefact alone without a lineage.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a name for the first living thing, first replicator or first cell.
- Not that gradualism wins a debate or that a single event is impossible. The union is the verdict.
- Not that the boundary is unknowable in principle. It is unreached at this budget (Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).
- Not that the figure's "intelligence" line is literally about life. Fig 11 :36 is transferred as an analogue and marked `specified`, with its own refuter.
- Not `grounded`. No laboratory measurement settled any sub-claim.

## 5. What would settle it

1. An instrument returning a discriminating boundary reading, with a non-updating anchor.
2. A measured MLS1 to MLS2 shift in a protocell or equivalent system, dated to a process.
3. A demonstration that the route is recoverable from the artefact alone.
4. A figure re-census naming "life" as a primitive or collapsing the union to a partition.
5. A demonstrated atom added by internal composition alone.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes
