# Solution ORI-0002

**Question:** Did life begin in one identifiable transition, or through many gradual steps from chemistry to biology?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 6

**Section:** 1. What counts as "life," and what counts as its origin?

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the exact boundary. Not `grounded`. Everything in this folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What this folder is for

It takes the either/or question in effect terms and asks what the figures say its shape must be. The question asks for a name of a boundary event; the framework returns a shape, a route, and a refuter. The verdict is kept per domain and never averaged into a single "one or many" answer.

- The world side (chemistry, geochemistry, protocells, evolution) supplies a **route**: many gradual steps, a lineage, a non-invertible transform between levels.
- The question side (the demand for one identifiable transition) asks for a **cut**: a threshold relation, a bond between quantities, a convention that a frame imposes.
- These live in different domains with different anchors. The either/or is a **union of two conditions**, not a partition with one winning answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163, carried in the exemplar). Selecting people for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless. And *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42): a recipient who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
