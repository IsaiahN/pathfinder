## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0002 a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name a boundary event. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape here has four parts:

1. the residual is per condition, not a global "alive";
2. the world side is a route with typed bonds, not a list of molecules;
3. the cut side is a threshold relation and a convention, not an atom;
4. the route is recoverable only from the lineage, and the exact boundary is a `gap`.

"Life began through many bonded steps and the single transition is a cut we impose" is a shape. "Life began when the first self-replicating RNA appeared 4.1 billion years ago" is a name. The framework cannot license the name from what it holds.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (`carried`): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: we did not begin by choosing RNA-first, metabolism-first or membrane-first and then read the figures for support. We began with the effect, the gap between what chemistry composes and what biology composes, and let the figures narrow it. This is Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For ORI-0002 this would be an instrument that returns a discriminating reading of a boundary, with an anchor that does not move when the definition changes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. Naming a specific first replicator would be an import from the chemistry frame onto a held atom, and it would have to be recorded as a binding, recorded with its provenance.

*They're Scanning Books* (`carried`): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

**Correction to my own §1, kept visible.** I initially wrote that *"the framework returns a shape, not a name"* forbids naming any specific molecule or event. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Bond types, thresholds, cycles and catalysts are already held, so naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is naming a **specific first event** and claiming the framework derived it. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This matches the exemplar's own correction in its `05` section 5b.

## 4. Why reading the boundary off a cell is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

A modern cell is the reading. RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first are candidate sources that could produce the same reading. Fig 13 :129: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the source. This is why F4 and F7 are separate findings: F4 says the route is not in the artefact; F7 says no instrument currently returns it.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So this derivation cannot validate its own boundary claim. That is why the boundary is a declared `gap`.

## 5. Q1 and Q2 answered

**Q1. Does the framework return a single identifiable transition?** No. It returns a shape: a route, a threshold, a lineage, and an owed instrument. The `gap` is a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

**Q2. Why is the either/or refused rather than decided?** Because the two options live in different domains with different anchors. The route is in D1/D2, anchored by experiment and selection. The cut is in D3, anchored by an instrument we do not yet have, and its current definition is a convention. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A definition that changes with the definer is not that anchor. Verdicts stay separate and are never averaged.

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree with a boundary is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
