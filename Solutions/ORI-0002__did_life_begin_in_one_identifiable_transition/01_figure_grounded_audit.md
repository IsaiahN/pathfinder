## 0. The question in effect terms

> Did life begin in one identifiable transition, or through many gradual steps from chemistry to biology?

Restated as an effect, with no theory: we are asked to choose between two shapes for the origin of life.

- **Option A, one identifiable transition.** There is a single event or boundary, locatable in principle, such that before it there is chemistry and after it there is biology. This leads to a name for a slot, a cut, and a criterion that decides the cut.
- **Option B, many gradual steps.** There is a route of many compositions, each small, with no single privileged event. This leads to a lineage, a sequence of bonds, and a retrospective label.

What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the Formula's "Direction"): either a **name** for the transition event, or a **route** with its order and its bonds, plus the reason the other option fails. If neither is available, the honest result is the **shape** the answer must have and a statement of what instrument would close it.

**Residual R, stated in effect terms.** The gap between what chemistry can compose and what biology can compose. It is not a single global yes/no; it is per slot. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The live slots here are at least five: self-replication, catalysis or metabolism, compartmentalization, heredity with variation, and a level of selection. A global predicate "alive" flattens all five into one number and throws away the only signal that says where to look.

**Arity, symmetry, scale.**
- Arity: five or more conditions, so a **union**, not a two-item partition (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*).
- Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric. Chemistry precedes biology; `→` is *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :70). A symmetric bond would make "chemistry then biology" and "biology then chemistry" one term, which they are not.
- Scale: molecular, protocellular, cellular, and population. The question therefore crosses at least one scale membrane (Fig 4).

## 3. Findings

### F1. The global predicate is an aggregation error (decisive for the framing)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

The question's "life" is one global predicate. The live slots are the conditions listed in section 0. If one slot (say self-replication) is satisfied and another (say a unit of selection) is not, a global "alive" reading hides exactly the signal that tells us where the residual still is. This is not a stylistic objection. Fig 1 :13 says *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*, so a system can look settled while one condition is still open.

**Verdict, D1/D2:** the origin is not one slot flipping. It is several slots, each with its own closure and its own ground. `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 plus the conditions enumerated from the world sources in D1/D2; low overlap between the framework line and the biological conditions).

**Refuter:** show that all five conditions share one closure and one ground, so a single global residual is sufficient, or measure a single slot whose satisfaction closes the other four. Neither is available in the sources read.

### F2. The either/or is a union, not a partition

Fig 9 :4-5: **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**
Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

"One transition" is well formed for D3 (the cut imposed by a categorising frame). "Many gradual steps" is well formed for D1/D2 (the route in the world). Each rule keeps working on its own subset. The question asks them to compete for one answer, so it does not shrink with effort. It must be split.

**Verdict, whole question:** the either/or is refused as written. Both descriptions survive in their own domains and are never averaged. `general` on the structural split, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts. Triangulated: Fig 9 plus Fig 12 (route versus cut) plus Fig 2 (different anchors).

**Refuter:** produce one domain with one anchor in which both "single event" and "gradual route" are the same quantity, so the union collapses to a partition.

### F3. A "transition" is a threshold relation, not a thing

Operators table :48: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"** (comparison, ⋛).
Fig 12 :87: **"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."**
Operators table :51: **"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"**.

The demand for "one identifiable transition" asks for an atom, a thing whose arrival can be pointed at. The figures give a bond for a transition: ⋛ compares two quantities and produces a threshold. So "the transition" is a relation between quantities at a level, not a primitive that enters the world at an instant. Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** The phrase "the moment life began" reifies a bond as an atom.

This also fixes the common failure of drawing a line at a fossil or a molecule: that is setting a threshold on a measurement (a comparison), not finding an event. A threshold is legitimate as a convention, but Fig 10 :47 says **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**. A definition of life that decides whether a system is alive has become a verdict, not a convention.

**Verdict, D3:** `specified` and triangulated (Fig 12 plus Operators plus Fig 10 :47). The transition is a priced bond, not an atom.

**Refuter:** exhibit a figure line in which a transition is admitted as a primitive atom rather than as a bond or a level crossing.

### F4. The route is not recoverable from the artefact

Fig 13 :129: **"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."**
Fig 13 :142: **"The seed was always the seedline."**
Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

A living cell is a composed term. Looking at it cannot tell you which route produced it. "Identify the transition" tries to read the route off the end state, which the figures say is impossible: only the lineage carries the order. This is why origin-of-life research reconstructs pathways rather than points at a first cell. The world agrees: the abiogenesis source says the transition *"has not been observed experimentally, but many proposals have been made for different stages of the process."*

**Verdict, D2/D3:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 and Fig 12, with the world source as secondary corroboration, not as the deciding witness). "One identifiable transition" is not identifiable from the outcome; at best from a recorded lineage.

**Refuter:** show that the route is recoverable from the end artefact alone, so that a boundary event can be read back from a modern cell without a lineage. That would break Fig 13 :129 directly.

### F5. Gradual steps are necessary but not sufficient; the surplus comes from a union of frames

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**
Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"** (import).
Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**

"Many gradual steps" is a correct description of the route in the world, but composition alone never adds a primitive. If chemistry is a closed set, no amount of gradual chemistry manufactures the first unit of selection. Something has to cross from a differing frame: a gradient, a mineral surface, a compartment, a code. That is an import, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12 *"source gate: provenance"*, :13 *"shadow test: explains a gap"*). Szathmáry 2015 supplies the world-side counterpart: transitions have formation, maintenance and transformation phases, and protocell formation is a transition in individuality (MLS1 to MLS2).

**Verdict, D1/D2:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 6 plus Fig 8 plus Fig 10 :68 *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."*). The origin is a union of frames, not a single closed sequence.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a purely internal composition of one closure that adds a new atom without contact with a differing closure. Fig 8 :32 makes this definitional, so such a demonstration would break the framework, not merely this reading.

### F6. Life is a retrospective label on a lineage that met its ground

Fig 11 :36: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."**
Fig 10 :70: **"The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."**
Fig 10 :68: **"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."**

The figure about intelligence transfers to the ordinary term "life" by the same move: where no maintainer exists, "alive" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. What ran was selection. The origin of life is therefore a level crossing seen from outside (non-invertible), and the question "when did it begin" asks for the location of a label on a lineage, not for an event in the world. Fig 4 :34-35: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*

**Verdict, D2/D3:** `specified` on the transfer, triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 10 plus Fig 4). The "identifiable transition" is an observer's cut on a lineage, not an actor in it.

**Refuter:** show a maintainer in the origin-of-life setting that selects the first living system before selection runs, so the label is applied prospectively rather than retrospectively. No source read supplies such a maintainer.

### F7. The boundary link has no instrument: this is a `gap`, reported as unreached

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 6 :34: **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

No source read reports an instrument that discriminates a unique first-living state from its immediate predecessor. The world source is explicit that the transition has not been observed experimentally. This is the objective link failing, not the planning link. The honest report is: **gap of the unreached-at-this-budget kind**, not underivable in principle and not silence. Fig 13 :134 directs us to say what the answer would have to look like and stop.

**Verdict, D3:** `gap`. Not `single-source`, not `triangulated`, because no figure line selects a boundary and no instrument exists. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.

**Refuter (also the closing instrument):** an experiment that returns a discriminating reading at some resolution, i.e. a measurement in which a specific state crosses the chemistry-to-biology boundary and a neighbouring state does not, with an anchor that does not update on the observer's definition.

### F8. Many gradual steps are a route (bonds), not a list of ingredients

Fig 12 :70: **"A then B; B needs A to have happened"** (sequence).
Fig 12 :77: **"A produces B; B did not exist before"** (production).
Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**
Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
Fig 12 :97: **"This is not an analogy. Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced"**

The gradual route is not "more and more molecules". It is a bonded arrangement: autocatalytic cycles (a cycle whose output re-enters), catalysts (parts surviving their own use), production bonds (B not present before A fires), and sequence. Fig 12's own example is chemistry: isomerism forced structure into notation. A flat list of prebiotic molecules cannot say which route to life it contains, because the bond carries the information the ingredients do not.

**Verdict, D1:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 plus Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*). Many steps is the right world-side shape, with bonds, not a count.

**Refuter:** show that the route from chemistry to biology is expressible as an unordered ingredient list with no typed bond, so that order and closure carry no information.

### F9. "One or many" is a budget filter masquerading as a witness

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**
Fig 2 :39: **"Correlation has valence."**
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

The preference for "one clean transition" (or for "pure gradualism") is a shape held as a preference, not a term that predicts anything. Formula step 2 warns: *"a frame searching for an answer of a given SHAPE holds that shape as a PREFERENCE, not a term. Unbound, predicting nothing, no residual - so nothing above can see it."* A researcher who has already chosen one side will find it. Agreement between RNA-first and metabolism-first advocates about "gradualism" is one evidence pool if both read the same reviews.

**Verdict, D4:** `general` on the structural rule, `specified` on the warning for this question. Triangulated (Fig 9 plus Fig 2 plus the Formula).

**Refuter:** show a case where a held shape predicted a residual and accrued error like an ordinary term, so the preference becomes a witness. The Formula says this cannot happen by construction.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line is produced that names a single primitive or event "life" whose admission closes the chemistry-to-biology residual (breaks Fig 13 :134 and Fig 6 :21).
2. An instrument returns a discriminating reading of a unique boundary event at some resolution, with an anchor that does not update on the observer's definition (breaks Fig 3 :27 and Fig 6 :34).
3. The route and the cut are shown to share one anchor and one currency, so the union collapses to a partition (breaks Fig 9 :54-57 and Fig 2 :31-32).
4. A purely internal composition of one closure is shown to add a new atom without contact with a differing closure (breaks Fig 6 :21 and Fig 8 :32).
5. Fig 13 :129 is shown false: the route is recoverable from the end artefact alone, so a boundary event can be read back without a lineage.
6. A maintainer is shown to select the first living system before selection runs, so the label is prospective rather than retrospective (breaks Fig 11 :36).

If any fires, the derivation is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 8. Conclusion of the audit

The figures do not hand back "one" or "many". They hand back a split. In the world the origin is a route: many gradual steps with typed bonds (sequence, production, cycle, catalyst), a closed chemistry that gains nothing from its own compositions, and a surplus supplied by contact with differing frames, ending in a non-invertible level crossing whose order lives in the lineage. In the question's frame the "single identifiable transition" is a threshold bond and a convention, not an atom; the exact boundary is unreached because no instrument returns a discriminating reading. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode: `specified` on the domain findings, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the boundary. Not `grounded`.
