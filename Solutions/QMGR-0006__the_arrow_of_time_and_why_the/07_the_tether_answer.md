# The Tether's answer on the arrow of time and the low-entropy early universe

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific reason for the low-entropy start. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`: the time-symmetric microscopic laws predict no preferred temporal direction; observation supplies a universe with a thermodynamic arrow, records of a lower-entropy past, and an exceptionally low-entropy initial state. R is indexed by three slots and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*): s1 direction, s2 the low-entropy boundary, s3 arrow alignment. It is a **union compound**: a lever that fixes one condition leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54, :57). Dependency order: establish directedness, then ask for the boundary, then explain alignment (Fig 3).

## 2. Per-domain verdicts, kept separate and never averaged

**D4, framework-internal composition: `specified`, triangulated.**
Time is *"the ordered succession of differences"* (Fig 13 :12) and energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14). Directedness lives in the gradient, *"a difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22), whose defining property is that *"what makes it a gradient is directedness"* (Symbols table). Fig 13 :45-47 argues that *"neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness"*, and Fig 13 :76 distinguishes a gradient over time from one over space. So the thermodynamic arrow is a **gradient-over-time composition of held terms**, not a seventh primitive. Refuter: derive directedness from energy alone, or exhibit a state–state or structure–structure bond carrying the arrow without an opposite-kind term.

**D1, statistical mechanics: `specified`, triangulated.**
Entropy is a spectrum-over-network composition over finite energy (Fig 13 :56, :59, :68), and the gradient is load-bearing: remove it and there is *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"* (Fig 13 :71). Boltzmann's phase-space asymmetry explains increase once a low start is given; this is ground-supported by the second law and corroborated by Lynn et al. (2022) at retinal scale. Refuter: a measured entropy decrease in a genuinely isolated macroscopic system, or a derivation of irreversibility from time-symmetric dynamics with no boundary condition and no import.

**D2, cosmology and the low-entropy boundary: `gap`, unreached at this budget.**
The time-symmetric frame cannot generate its own boundary: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs"* (Fig 6 :23), and *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* (Fig 8 :32). There is a measurement of the early state's smoothness, but no instrument returning the reason: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there"* (Fig 6 :34-35). From inside the frame, deep-but-hidden and genuinely-absent look the same (Fig 6 :27), so the honest report is unreached, not underivable-proved (Fig 6 :37; Fig 8 :39). The framework returns a shape: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32), and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134). Refuter: a source-discriminating cosmological instrument that closes the slot, or a derivation of the boundary from the held frame with no import.

**D3, arrow alignment: `specified`, triangulated.**
*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24) plus Fig 2 :35 (*"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*): records can form only in the direction in which contact with a non-updating ground is maintained, which aligns the psychological arrow with the thermodynamic one without identifying them. Lynn et al. find that neurons can define an arrow even when the stimulus does not; Gu et al. find the front/back representation of past and future is language- and culture-sensitive, so the representation is a binding. Refuter: a reliable record of future events, or independence of the psychological arrow from entropy production.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a mechanism name. Inflation, a bounce, a no-boundary proposal and Boltzmann fluctuations are candidate imports requiring a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14, Fig 9 :27-28). Picking one before describing the residual would make any frame seem to fit.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*; a time-symmetric ultra-large-scale model is a direction of travel, not a terminal explanation.
- Not a derivation of the thermodynamic arrow from the weak-interaction arrow. The weak arrow barely points and is at a different scale.
- Not a single global entropy verdict. The three slots have different anchors; averaging them would hide the live one (Fig 1 :22).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement. The low-entropy "why" is a declared `gap`.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The situation is the metamerism case from *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

- **Boundary discriminator:** a cosmological observation that distinguishes candidate sources of the low-entropy start with provenance and a shadow test, and that closes s2. Carroll and Chen's own limit, *"we do not have a reliable understanding of entropy in the context of quantum gravity; the fundamental degrees of freedom themselves remain unclear,"* names what such an instrument would have to overcome.
- **Direction test:** a measurement of entropy decrease in a genuinely isolated macroscopic system, or a derivation of irreversibility from time-symmetric dynamics with no boundary condition (Loschmidt's challenge, via the SEP).
- **Bond test:** an instrument that discriminates a gradient-over-time composition from a mere comparison of distributions (the live type strain S4 in `01` §7).
- **Alignment test:** evidence that psychological arrow alignment survives removal of entropy production, which would break D3.
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a state–state or structure–structure composition that cannot be refused; show a remainder that does not re-enter the next turn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not underivable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not a discovery of nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The `gap` is a verdict, not a silence, and no amount of prose in this folder reduces it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a mechanism for the low-entropy early universe, and it does not claim one. It says that entropy is a composition of held terms: ordering over configurations, made finite by energy and given direction by a gradient bonded to time, so the arrow is a gradient-over-time and not a seventh primitive or a new field. That is ground-supported for the *direction* of entropy increase once a low start is given, and the thermodynamic, radiative and psychological arrows align by maintained contact with a ground that does not update, not by being the same arrow. The reason the start was low is a separate slot, and after a genuine search it is recorded as a `gap`: there is no instrument returning it, and searching the time-symmetric frame longer adds no new atom. Any named mechanism would be an import needing provenance and a shadow test, and the framework returns a shape rather than a name. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the gap. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
