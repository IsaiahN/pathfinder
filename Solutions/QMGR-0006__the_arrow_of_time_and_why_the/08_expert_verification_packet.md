# Expert verification packet — the Tether on the arrow of time

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape claims, `general` on structural rules, `gap` on the low-entropy reason. Not `grounded`.** No cosmological measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Entropy is not a single primitive: under the six-term set it is a spectrum-over-network composition over finite energy, with the gradient supplying direction. The thermodynamic arrow is therefore a gradient bonded to time, a composition of held terms rather than a seventh primitive or a new field. The three questions the user asks are a union with different anchors: the *direction* of increase is ground-supported once a low start is given; the *reason the start was low* is unreached at this budget and is reported as a `gap`, not answered by a named mechanism; and the *alignment* of the physical and psychological arrows is maintained contact with a non-updating ground, not an identity. Any candidate mechanism (inflation, a bounce, a no-boundary proposal, a fluctuation) is an import that needs a provenance and a shadow test before it can move anything.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** directedness is not in the energy term; the arrow is a gradient-over-time composition, not a seventh primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14 — *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (energy). Fig 13 :22 — *"a difference that can be spent"* (gradient). Fig 13 :45-47 — *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent"* and *"neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."* Fig 13 :76 — *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Symbols table: *"what makes it a gradient is directedness."*
- **Refuter:** derive directedness from energy alone; exhibit a state–state or structure–structure bond that carries the arrow with no opposite-kind term between; or add a seventh primitive that generates it. Any one breaks C1.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by a physicist or philosopher of physics against the literature

- **Statement:** the direction of entropy increase is accounted for by the statistical asymmetry of phase-space volumes plus a low-entropy initial condition; the reason *for* that initial condition is a separate, unreached slot. The two must not be averaged.
- **Verbatim figure lines:** Fig 13 :68 — *"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate."* Fig 13 :71 — *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step."* Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 8 :32 — *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 9 :32 — *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- **What to check against the literature:** the SEP entry on thermodynamic asymmetry records that Boltzmann's phase-space argument gives increase *given* a low start, and that Loschmidt's reversal and Zermelo's recurrence objections block a derivation from time-symmetric dynamics without one. Carroll and Chen (2004) state: *"The origin of the Second Law is traced back to initial conditions: the early universe had an extremely low entropy, allowing it to continue to increase thereafter,"* and concede *"we do not have a reliable understanding of entropy in the context of quantum gravity; the fundamental degrees of freedom themselves remain unclear."* Do you agree that the "why" remains open, or do you have a source-discriminating result that closes it?
- **Refuter:** a measured entropy decrease in a genuinely isolated macroscopic system; a derivation of irreversibility from time-symmetric dynamics with no boundary condition and no import; or a cosmological observation that returns the reason for the low start with provenance and a shadow test.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation, with an empirical limb

- **Statement:** the alignment of the arrows is maintained contact with a ground that does not update, not an identification of the arrows.
- **Verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 — *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 2 :35 — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 12 :124 — *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **What to check:** Lynn, Holmes, Bialek and Schwab (2022, *Physical Review Letters* 129:118101) find *"the neuronal dynamics can define an arrow of time, even when the stimulus does not."* Gu, Zheng and Swerts (2019, *Cognitive Science* 43:e12804) find *"Chinese speakers can produce sagittal gestures not only directing the past to their back but also to their front."* Does maintained contact, plus the language-sensitive representation, account for the alignment without making the arrows identical?
- **Refuter:** a reliable record of future events; evidence that psychological alignment survives removal of all entropy production; or a demonstration that the arrows cannot be written as members of one functional group.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a mechanism name. Inflation, a bounce, a no-boundary proposal and a Boltzmann fluctuation are candidate imports, not conclusions.
- Not a theory of everything; no last room (Fig 7 :6).
- Not a derivation of the thermodynamic arrow from weak-interaction T violation.
- Not a single global entropy verdict; the three slots have different anchors.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the cosmological initial state is claimed.
- Not a claim that "shape, not a name" forbids mentioning held atoms or candidate imports. It governs a missing primitive; binding Gradient or proposing an import is provenance, not invention (see `05` §3 correction).
