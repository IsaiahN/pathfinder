## VERDICT (from the figures)

The direction and alignment slots survive as `specified` shapes: entropy is a spectrum-over-network composition over finite energy with a gradient bonded to time, the arrow is that gradient-over-time rather than a new primitive or a new field, and the arrows align by maintained contact with a non-updating ground rather than by identity. The low-entropy reason does not survive as an answer: after a genuine search it is a declared `gap`, unreached at this budget, owed a source-discriminating instrument and a provenanced import with a shadow test. The three slot verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. A named mechanism would invert the figure order (frame before residual) and would spend the independence of the reader. This is a `specified` and ungrounded result, and the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, keeps slots separate and prints refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a mechanism name before the residual; Fig 2 :42 prices that as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No mechanism is asserted, so nothing must be retracted to correct the answer. A source-discriminating cosmological observation, a direction test, a bond test or an alignment test can move the corresponding verdict without disturbing the others. The `gap` is explicitly revisable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A cosmological observation that distinguishes candidate sources of the low-entropy start with provenance and a shadow test would close s2; a measured entropy decrease in an isolated system or a derivation from time-symmetric dynamics with no boundary would break D1 and D4; evidence that psychological alignment is independent of entropy production would break D3; deriving one of the six from the other five would break the substrate. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
