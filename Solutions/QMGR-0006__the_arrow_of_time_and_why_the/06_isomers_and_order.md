# Entropy, bonds and the order of the arrow question

**Mode: `specified` on the structural derivations, `general` on the bond rules, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## RESIDUAL

The question asks for (i) the structure that makes entropy a directed magnitude and (ii) the order in which the direction question, the boundary question and the alignment question must be answered. Three derivations are owed: what entropy is made of under the six-term set, what kind of bond the arrow is, and whether competing cosmological mechanisms stand as isomers or as different compounds. Each carries its own falsifier.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: space, time, energy (states); spectrum, gradient, network (structures). Bonds held: the seven, including ordered `→`, production `⇒`, comparison `⋛`, subtraction `−`, and the unary negation `¬`. No new primitive is available without an import (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). The statistical-mechanics frame supplies a coarse-graining measure and a phase-space volume as bindings, not as new atoms.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — What is entropy made of under the six-term set?

- Fig 13 :56: **"no ordering, so no equivalence over thermal state, so no transitivity"** (spectrum removed, zeroth law fails).
- Fig 13 :59: **"no relation between systems, and no configurations to count"** (network removed, zeroth and second laws fail).
- Fig 13 :68: **"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"** (energy removed, second law fails).
- Fig 13 :71: **"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"** (gradient removed, second and third laws fail).

Reading: a Boltzmann entropy is not a single atom. It is a **spectrum-over-network composition over finite energy**: ordering supplies "more and less", network supplies configurations to count, finiteness makes the count terminate, and the gradient supplies the directedness that lets the count have a sign in time. The world source corroborates the shape at a different scale: Lynn et al. (2022) find that irreversibility decomposes into non-negative contributions from interactions of increasing order, with pairwise terms dominant in retinal data. That is a composition result, not a cosmological one.

- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if entropy can be derived with the network atom removed (no configurations to count), or if a gradient-free composition yields a signed entropy increase, Q1 fails.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated (four independent Fig 13 rows + Lynn et al. at low overlap).

### Q2 — What kind of bond is the arrow?

- Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
- Fig 12 :46: **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands."**
- Fig 13 :76: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**
- Operators table :33: `→` is *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*, an ordered bond. Operators table :51: `⋛` *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, used for comparisons.

Reading: the arrow is not an extra ingredient. It is the **bond plus its operand**: a gradient bonded to time rather than to space. The information that makes it an arrow is carried by the bond, which is exactly the half a flat ingredient list omits. "Entropy increases" compares magnitudes (`⋛`), but "increase" is ordered (`→`); a correlation alone is symmetric and cannot choose the direction, which is the Operators table's symmetry point. This is why the weak-interaction T violation cannot be the thermodynamic arrow: it *"barely"* points (Wikipedia overview, opened this session), while the thermodynamic arrow is a composition-level directedness.

- **Falsifier:** if the same four atoms with the operand order reversed yield the same arrow (symmetry holds), the bond claim fails and the arrow would have to be a magnitude, not a direction.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12, Fig 13, Operators table).

### Q3 — Cycle, recurrence and the Loschmidt objection

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- Fig 13 :31: **"each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next"**; Fig 13 :32: **"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos."**

Reading: the recurrence objection (Zermelo, via the SEP) and the reversal objection (Loschmidt, via the SEP) are two ways of saying that a time-symmetric chain can be read backwards. Fig 12's cycle entry names the missing piece: a chain cannot close by construction, but a **cycle** can, and whether it closes depends on the sign of the loop. The remainder that re-enters the next turn is the cycle's input. So the arrow is a cycle-like composition in the figures' sense, not a one-way ingredient, and the reason our world has a sign is the boundary condition (s2), which is the open slot. The figure gives the shape of the loop and not its sign.

- **Falsifier:** a demonstration that a time-symmetric chain, with no boundary condition and no imported sign, closes into a directed cycle.

**Status:** `general` (structural analogue). The mapping of Poincaré recurrence onto Fig 12's cycle is an analogue, not a figure-derived identity; recorded as such.

### Q4 — Are competing cosmological mechanisms isomers or different compounds?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Reading: over the shared narrative atom set (an initial state, a dynamical law, a coarse graining, a boundary condition), inflation, a bounce and a no-boundary proposal can be written as **isomers**: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance, with the difference invisible in a flat list of ingredients. That is the claim worth testing. It fails in the weaker form, where one proposal requires an atom another lacks (for example a scalar inflaton field that a pure no-boundary proposal does not need); then they are different compounds, not isomers. Separately, the thermodynamic, radiative and psychological arrows share a **functional group** (a directed composition over a state pair), with different qualifiers naming the members (Fig 12 :124). This is why aligning the arrows is a composition over a shared kind, not an identity.

- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if either proposal can be written without an atom the other requires, the isomer claim fails and the different-compound reading stands; if any arrow cannot be written as a directed composition over a state pair, the functional-group claim fails.

**Status:** `specified` but unmeasured for the cosmological proposals; `specified` for the arrow functional group, corroborated at low overlap by Gu et al. (2019) on the representational side.

### Q5 — Two currencies: describing the shape is not settling it

- Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
- Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**

Reading: the shape in `01` is cheap in bits (a few figure lines) and expensive in actions (a source-discriminating cosmological instrument). The two prices do not add, so a long, well-described argument cannot substitute for the measurement, and a cheap description is not thereby wrong. This is the structural reason the low-entropy slot stays a `gap`: what is missing is an action budget against a ground, not more bits. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

- **Falsifier:** a demonstration that description length and settlement cost share a single unit and can be summed.

**Status:** `general`, single-source for the figure rule (Fig 12), corroborated conceptually by Carroll and Chen's admission that the degrees of freedom are unknown.

## CONCLUSION

Entropy is a spectrum-over-network composition over finite energy, with the gradient supplying direction; the arrow is a gradient-over-time bond, not an extra atom; recurrence is a cycle question whose sign is owed to the boundary condition; the competing mechanisms are isomers over a shared atom set only if none requires an atom another lacks; and the per-slot structure is a union whose direction and boundary conditions have different currencies. The alignment of arrows is a functional group, not an identity. What survives is a checkable shape with stated falsifiers. What does not survive is any claim to have named the reason the early universe was low entropy.
