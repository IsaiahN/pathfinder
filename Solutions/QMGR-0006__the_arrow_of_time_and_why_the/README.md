## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No named answer to the low-entropy question. The figures return a shape, not a name, and the specific mechanism is recorded as a `gap`.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words.

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"Correlation has valence."* (Fig 2 :39); *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42), and *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
