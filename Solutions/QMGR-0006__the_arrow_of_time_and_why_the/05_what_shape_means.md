## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *"a gradient over time composes the arrow"* is a shape;
- *"the arrow was produced by spontaneous eternal inflation"* is a name.

The first is licensed by Fig 13's rows and gradient argument (F1, F2 of `01`). The second is a candidate import that has not passed a source gate or a shadow test (F6).

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction that governs this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 (local): *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical local rule: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

### Correction to my own first reading (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any cosmological mechanism. That is too broad. The canonical phrase's subject is a **missing primitive**, one not held in `Γ`. Gradient and Spectrum are already among the six (Fig 13); a *cosmological mechanism* is not a seventh primitive either, it is a candidate frame whose closure differs. So:

- naming **Gradient** as the structure that supplies directedness is a **binding** from the physics frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a missing one (this is the same narrowing the exemplar records in its `05` §5b);
- naming **inflation or a bounce** as the reason for the low-entropy start is an **import** and is allowed as a provenanced claim with a shadow test, but it is not a derivation and it does not move the wall until those gates pass;
- what remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *returns* the mechanism name. It returns the shape of what the mechanism must do: supply a boundary condition that is not derivable from the time-symmetric dynamics and that explains the compositional gradient's starting value.

Both the original overstatement and its narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why "low entropy" as a reading does not name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or a scheme not yet discovered). *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: CMB smoothness and large-scale structure read a low-entropy early state. Several candidate sources could produce that reading (inflation, a bounce, a no-boundary proposal, a fluctuation, a time-asymmetric law). The reading does not determine the source. Carroll and Chen's own admission in the paper opened this session, *"we do not have a reliable understanding of entropy in the context of quantum gravity,"* is an admission that the discriminator is missing. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is F5's `gap`, and it is a metamerism gap.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A candidate mechanism from inside the same cosmology pool cannot validate its own explanation of the boundary.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

The temptation here is strong, because the low-entropy question has famous names attached to famous proposals. A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with one proposal is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a source-discriminating cosmological observation, settle it. An expert's job in stage 1 is to name a failure, not to endorse. Fig 7 :35 (local): *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
