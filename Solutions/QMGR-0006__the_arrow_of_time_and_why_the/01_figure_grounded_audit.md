## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Let `Γ` be the library of time-symmetric microscopic dynamical laws (classical mechanics, GR, unitary quantum mechanics). Let `b` be the inherited belief that these laws are time-reversal invariant apart from small weak-interaction violations. Let `a` be any microscopic evolution. Let `o′` be what is observed: a universe with a thermodynamic arrow (entropy increases toward one temporal direction), records and memories of a lower-entropy past, and an initial cosmological state of exceptionally low gravitational entropy.

Then `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`, indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19):

| slot | effect to be accounted for | name in the literature |
|---|---|---|
| s1 | why entropy increases toward one temporal direction at all | the direction problem, Loschmidt's paradox |
| s2 | why the initial state was so far from equilibrium | the past hypothesis, the low-entropy initial condition |
| s3 | why the thermodynamic, cosmological, radiative, causal, psychological and quantum arrows point the same way | the alignment or master-arrow question |

*"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"* (Fig 1 :13), and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22). So there is no single "arrow of time number" to be maximised or minimised. Verdicts are kept per slot and never averaged.

Restating the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": an answer would look like a typed composition that (i) supplies directedness without adding a seventh primitive, (ii) states which boundary condition is owed and with what provenance, and (iii) explains the alignment by contact rather than by identifying the arrows. Binding that shape lets it accrue a residual like anything else, so the preference is refusable (§4).

## 2. Domain census

### 2.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Three slots (s1, s2, s3), so a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :4-5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Symmetry.** Entropy is a comparison: *"ordering, so more and less can be said"* (Fig 13 :20). The arrow is directedness: *"A difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22), and the Symbols table sharpens it, *"what makes it a gradient is directedness."* So the operative relation is ordered (`→`, `⋛`), not the symmetric `+`. A correlation between entropy and time is symmetric and cannot choose the arrow on its own.
- **Scale.** Microscopic (phase-space cells, coarse graining), macroscopic (the thermodynamic arrow), cosmological (the initial condition of the observable universe). The transform between scales is lossy: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* (Fig 4 :27).

X1 fails the anchor test (Fig 2 :29): no instrument is named that returns the reason for the boundary condition. *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* The figures do not give X1 a seat.

### 2.3 Partition or union, and dependency order

**Union, not partition.** s1 (direction) and s2 (boundary) share a fraction: the second law only defines "increase" relative to a low starting entropy, so a lever that fixes one can leave the intersection standing. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single lever (inflation, a bounce, a no-boundary proposal) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the compound.

**Order by dependency** (Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*): establish the gradient/directedness that constitutes the arrow (D4, then D1); then ask for the boundary condition (D2); alignment (D3) composes over both. Effort spent on D2 before D1 is effort above a break: Fig 3 :19, *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The arrow is a gradient-over-time term, not a seventh primitive (decisive)

Quote before concluding. Fig 13 :12: **"the ordered succession of differences"** (time). Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (energy). Fig 13 :20: **"ordering, so more and less can be said"** (spectrum). Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"** (gradient). Symbols table, on the gradient: **"what makes it a gradient is directedness."**

Fig 13 :45-47 argues the thin case directly: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent"**, and **"neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."** The whole has a property neither part has, so the whole is not the parts arranged. Fig 13 :76: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

Reading: the thermodynamic arrow is a gradient over time, a bonded composition of energy, ordering and time, not a primitive and not a new kind of thing. The framework is entitled to say *what kind of thing* the arrow is, and not to name which cosmological process produced the gradient's starting value.

**Mode:** `specified` (structural). **Status:** triangulated across Fig 13's state rows, structure rows, the gradient argument, and the Symbols table (low overlap within the figure set). **Refuter:** derive directedness from energy alone; exhibit a state–state or structure–structure bond that carries direction with no opposite-kind intermediary; or add a seventh primitive that generates the arrow. Any of these breaks F1.

### F2 — Direction is not in the energy term, and the thermodynamic rows are load-bearing on the gradient

Fig 13 :66-68 places energy in the second law: **"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate."** Fig 13 :56: **"no ordering, so no equivalence over thermal state, so no transitivity."** Fig 13 :59: **"no relation between systems, and no configurations to count."** Fig 13 :71 places the gradient in the second and third laws: **"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step."**

Fig 13 :28: **"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."** Fig 13 :31: **"each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next"**; Fig 13 :32: **"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos."**

Reading: remove the gradient and you lose exactly the two things the question asks about, direction to drive the flow and a remainder left after each step. The arrow is what the alternating chain leaves, not a member of the six. This is the strongest single figure-grounded point in the folder. Note the honest limit: F2 gives the *source of directedness in the composition*, not the *value of the initial entropy*.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 rows plus the Symbols table). **Refuter:** derive the second and third laws with the gradient removed, or show a remainder that does not re-enter the next turn.

### F6 — Any named mechanism is an import and needs a source gate and a shadow test

Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"** (import). Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."** Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."**

Reading: inflation, a bounce, a no-boundary proposal, a Weyl-curvature hypothesis and a Boltzmann fluctuation are *candidate frames*, not atoms already in `Γ`. Each may move the wall only on contact with a source whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test (does it explain a gap we already have?). This is why the package withholds a mechanism name even though the world literature discusses several.

**Mode:** `general` (method rule). **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 9). **Refuter:** exhibit a mechanism derived inside the held frame with no imported atom and with a shadow test already passed.

### F7 — The alignment of the arrows is a contact relation, not an identity

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**; :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Reading: a memory or a record can form only in the direction in which the channel to the ground is maintained. That fixes why the psychological arrow points the same way as the thermodynamic one without identifying the two. Lynn, Holmes, Bialek and Schwab (2022, *Physical Review Letters*) find that retinal neurons break detailed balance even when the visual stimulus does not, and state that **"the neuronal dynamics can define an arrow of time, even when the stimulus does not."** Gu, Zheng and Swerts (2019, *Cognitive Science*) find that the front/back mapping of past and future is shaped by language and culture, with Chinese speakers producing past-in-front gestures at rates far above English speakers. So the *representation* of the arrow is a binding, while the *direction* is fixed by contact.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10 plus two genuinely low-overlap world sources). **Refuter:** a reliable record of future events, or a demonstration that psychological arrow alignment survives the removal of all entropy production.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Directedness is derived from energy alone, or a state–state or structure–structure bond carries the arrow with no opposite-kind intermediary (breaks F1 and Fig 13 :74-75).
2. A measurement shows entropy decreasing in a genuinely isolated macroscopic system, or irreversibility is derived from time-symmetric dynamics with no boundary condition and no import (breaks F2/F4 at the ground, Loschmidt's challenge).
3. A source-discriminating cosmological instrument returns a specific mechanism for the initial low entropy, with provenance and a shadow test, and the residual s2 closes (breaks F5's `gap`).
4. The psychological arrow is shown to be independent of entropy production and maintained contact (breaks F7).
5. The low-entropy boundary is derived from the time-symmetric frame alone with no import (breaks Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :32).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the honest stop moves. Until one is run, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there an arrow to explain?** Yes at the ground (Fig 1 :23; measured entropy increase), and it is three slots, not one (Fig 1 :19).
2. **Where can direction come from?** Not from energy (Fig 13 :14), not from ordering alone (Fig 13 :45-47); from a gradient composed with time (Fig 13 :22, :76).
3. **Is the gradient a seventh primitive?** No. It is one of the six; the arrow is a composition (Fig 13 :115; Fig 12 :103).
4. **Why does entropy increase toward one direction?** Statistical asymmetry of phase-space volumes plus a low start (world: SEP; Fig 13 :56/:59/:68). `specified`, ground-supported for s1.
5. **Why was the start low?** Unreached. No proto-instrument returns the reason (Fig 6 :32-35; Fig 3 :27); more search in the time-symmetric frame adds no atom (Fig 8 :32). `gap`.
6. **Can a named mechanism close it?** Only as an import with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14; Fig 9 :27-28); the framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
7. **Why do the arrows align?** Maintained contact fixes which direction can carry records (Fig 10 :24); representation of that direction is a binding (Gu et al. 2019).
