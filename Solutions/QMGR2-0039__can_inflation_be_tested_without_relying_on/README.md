## The question restated in effect terms

The question is not really asking whether a telescopes can be pointed at the early universe. It asks whether the *link* from an inflationary model to a number a telescope can return can be made without inserting auxiliaries, initial conditions, a measure, a prior or a reheating history, that no instrument can check. Option A: treat inflation as a single testable hypothesis and answer yes. Option B: treat the question as a union of conditions, test each separately, and answer yes only for the conditions whose channel to the ground is assumption-light. What each leads to: A lets a model class absorb any outcome by adjusting an unverified auxiliary, which is the coherent-drift failure; B yields a short list of genuinely testable items and an honest `gap` for the rest.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that inflation is testable.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **A checker who holds the interiors cannot check.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid both.
