## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that inflation can be tested without relying on assumptions that are difficult to verify. Option A: treat inflation as one hypothesis and answer yes if the data fit a model. Option B: treat the question as a union of overlapping conditions, test each against its own anchor, and answer yes only where the path from sky reading to claim is assumption-light. What each leads to: A lets a model class absorb any outcome by adjusting an unverified auxiliary, which is coherent drift; B yields a short list of genuinely testable items, one partial assumption-light constraint, and an explicit `gap` for the physical truth.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union, not one test. Specific inflationary models are testable once their priors, measures and foreground models are declared, and real data have already disfavoured some of them. Inflation as an assumption-free class is not testable: the auxiliaries that carry a sky reading to an early-universe claim are not returned by any instrument, and where a measure decides the result a convention has become a verdict. A partial assumption-light constraint exists in the kinematic past-incompleteness result, which needs no initial condition. Which model (if any) is true is a `gap` for the figures and is left to the ground. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices directly. That cost is avoidable by leading with the union and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The per-domain verdicts are separable and reversible. A new observable clears the channel finding without touching the union finding; a measure-invariance result clears the convention finding without touching the import. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no claim is irrecoverable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A model-independent observable returned by an instrument with no initial-condition, measure, potential or reheating auxiliary would reverse the no-for-the-class verdict; a demonstration that the four conditions form a partition would reverse the union verdict; measure invariance or a measured measure would reverse the convention finding; a past-eternal model satisfying the BGV conditions would reverse the import. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
