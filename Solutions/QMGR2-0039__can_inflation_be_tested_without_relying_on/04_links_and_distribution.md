## 2. Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **The debate is about two different objects.** Guth et al. (2017 letter, quoted by Dawid and McCoy): "Inflation is not a unique theory but rather a class of models based on similar principles," and "the desirable process of using observation to thin out the set of viable models." Ijjas et al. reply that the flexible contemporary framework "cannot be evaluated using the scientific method." Dawid and McCoy reconstruct both and find the defence successful against the literal claims while a real paradigm-level confirmation problem remains. This is the per-slot versus aggregate split (Fig 1 :19 and :22) in the actual literature. Tell a reader this before offering any theory.
2. **The dust case is the source-discrimination instance.** Planck at seven frequencies and BICEP/Keck at one are two instruments pointed at the same patch. The 2015 joint paper says "using a prior on the frequency spectral behavior of polarized dust emission from previous Planck analysis of other regions of the sky," then tests model variations and finds the r constraint stable. That is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, not an assumption smuggled in. Use it as the concrete example of what "settled or provenanced" means.
3. **The measure problem has no instrument yet.** Dawid and McCoy: "the measure problem ... is the problem of supplying a probability measure that can be used to make concrete predictions within the multiverse. Without an appropriate and justified measure, one cannot make predictions of observations across the bubble universes." Guth et al. agree the problem is real but say it need not block model-level prediction. The figure-side reading preserves both: r has an edge to extend from, the measure does not (Fig 6 :32-35). Do not turn "no instrument today" into "never"; Fig 8 :39 says "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-23** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework interior before judging stage 1 has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would count as a failure of the separator before showing the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not collect endorsements from people who all read the same debate.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong verdict about the early universe, so independent instruments, not more signatories, are what to buy.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its six refuters; (2) "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) only then the three public links, with the note that the figure text, not this folder's summary, is the authority; (4) only if asked, the method itself, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
