**What this is:** a claim in a form an earth system scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement in this packet ranks the interactions. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question asks which interactions among atmosphere, oceans, ice, land and ecosystems most strongly shape long-term Earth behavior. The framework cannot return a single winner. It returns a shape: the five realms are an enumerated habitat; each interaction is a gradient-driven flow embedded in a signed feedback cycle; the slots form a union with a threshold compound where two or more bind at once; and fast and slow slots are separated by a measured round-trip gap. Because the residual is indexed per slot and an actor's valence is relative to the residual, a single ranked list would average away the live signal and freeze a relative weight into an absolute one. Verdicts must be per slot, mode-labelled and anchored to an instrument. The ranking stays open until a ground reading returns the same slot order across scales.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 11 and 9

- **Statement:** a single aggregated ranking is not licensed. Verdicts must be per slot and valence must be relative to a named residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." - **Refuter:** one interaction carrying positive residual mass in every slot under one fixed residual, with an anchor returning the same order at local, regional and global scale; or the ten pairs forming a partition with each realm in exactly one edge.

### C2 — Earth-system-facing; checkable against observations and models

- **Statement:** the slots are gradient-driven flows in signed cycles, and the overlapping conditions form a compound, not a sum. Thresholds are relations over slots, not a sixth realm.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **What to check:** hold bond, operands and feedback sign fixed for a given interaction. Does the long-term outcome stay fixed? If it changes, the bond is not carrying the difference. Then test a compound case (for example ice loss plus circulation change) against the sum of its single-condition cases. If the compound is just the sum, the union claim fails.
- **Refuter:** a long-term trajectory fully accounted for by a linear chain with no output re-entering as input, under an instrument that would detect a cycle if present; or a compound behaving exactly as the sum of its single-condition cases.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with an instrument consequence

- **Statement:** fast and slow slots must be separated by a measured round-trip gap, and the ranking is unreached rather than refused.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- **What to check:** name an instrument that resolves both a storm-scale flux and a millennial ice-sheet response on one axis without loss, and demonstrate that the abstraction/concretisation round trip returns the original description. If such an instrument exists, the gap claim fails.
- **Refuter:** a verbatim figure line naming a single strongest interaction among the five realms, or a ground measurement returning a stable slot ranking across all named scales and residuals.
