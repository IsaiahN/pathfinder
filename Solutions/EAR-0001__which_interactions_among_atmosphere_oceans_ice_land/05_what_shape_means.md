## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"a shape, not a name"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 13 :138 to :139: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."

From *The Library of Babel*, read this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0001 the shape is: *an enumerated union of per-slot gradient-driven flows, each in a signed feedback cycle, with threshold relations over overlapping slots, and an anchor instrument per slot*. The name of a winning slot is not in that shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that would be an instrument returning a stable ranking of slots across the named scales and residuals. No such reading is present in this folder.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A ranking imported from a single model intercomparison would have to declare its closure and its shared evidence pool.

The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* That is what §7 of the audit does.

## 4. The metamerism warning applies to a ranking, not only to QMGR-0037

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, read this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

Applied here: a single scalar "interaction strength" is a projection. Several different slot configurations (a strong ice-albedo cycle plus a weak carbon sink, or the reverse) can produce the same global temperature trajectory. A scalar ranking cannot comment on which source produced the reading. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case; here the corresponding need is a per-slot instrument with enough discrimination to separate the configurations. This is why the answer stays a shape and why the refuter in `01` §4 asks for a ground reading, not for more agreement.
