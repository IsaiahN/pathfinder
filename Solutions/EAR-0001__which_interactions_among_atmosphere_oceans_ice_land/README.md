**Question:** Which interactions among atmosphere, oceans, ice, land, and ecosystems most strongly shape Earth's long-term behavior?

**Source:** `Questions/earth systems.txt` line 5

**Section:** 1. Earth as a coupled system

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement in this package ranks the interactions. Every substantive claim is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry owner, not by this agent)

**Field for public contact points:** earth system science research.

## What this answer does

It refuses the question's demand for a single ranked list. The figures require that the residual be indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22), that the habitat be enumerated rather than composed (Fig 11 :43), and that a union of overlapping conditions be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). The reachable result is therefore a per-slot, enumerated union of interactions with its feedback cycles named, not a winner. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134), and this is recorded as a verdict about the form of the answer, not as a failure to try.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words.

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :29 names the anchor as "A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome," not a vote of readers.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."
3. **Sending the framework before the claim destroys the only thing an outside reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. The public opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
