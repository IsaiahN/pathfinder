**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. ## QUESTION

Which interactions among atmosphere, oceans, ice, land, and ecosystems most strongly shape Earth's long-term behavior? Option A: return a single ranked list of interactions. Option B: return a per-slot shape with the union, the cycles, the scales, the anchors and a refuter. What each leads to: A averages live slot signals, freezes relative valence into an absolute property and gives the reader a scalar that cannot discriminate among source configurations; B keeps the signal, states what would settle each slot and stops honestly at the instrument limit.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: an edge set over five realms, ten unordered pairs plus higher-order overlaps, so a network rather than a scalar. Symbols table :43: "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." Symmetry: bonds are typed and not interchangeable; valence is relative to the residual being tracked. Scale: local to global in space, hours to millennia in time, with a lossy transform between scales.
- **D1 Atmosphere / ocean.** Actors: winds, currents, SST. Currency: heat, momentum and carbon flux. Anchor: Argo profiles, ocean heat content, SST record.
- **D2 Ice / ocean / atmosphere.** Actors: sea ice, ice sheets, albedo, meltwater. Currency: reflected radiation, freshwater flux, mass balance. Anchor: satellite albedo and extent, ice mass balance.
- **D3 Land / atmosphere.** Actors: soil moisture, vegetation, dust, land use. Currency: water, energy and carbon flux. Anchor: flux towers, satellite land products.
- **D4 Ocean / ecosystems / atmosphere.** Actors: solubility and biological pumps, phytoplankton. Currency: carbon flux and storage. Anchor: ocean carbon inventory, atmospheric CO2 record.
- **D5 Ecosystems / land / atmosphere.** Actors: vegetation, fire, permafrost, canopy. Currency: carbon, water and albedo. Anchor: vegetation indices, CO2 and CH4 records.
- **D6 Thresholds and cascades.** Actors: all of the above in state transition. Currency: regime shift, cascade, hysteresis. Anchor: paleo records, detected transitions, model intercomparison. Admitted as a **compound**, not a sixth realm.
- **X1 "the single strongest interaction".** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet. It is the sought-for shape held as a preference (Fig 5 :65).
- **X2 "a tipping point as a separate cause".** Absorbed into D6 as a comparison relation, not a realm. The source question file itself warns that the term's meaning and application vary.
- **Union, not partition.** Each realm occurs in several edges. D6 is the compound formed by overlap, with its own loop and ground (Fig 9 :55).
- **Order by dependency.** Observations → flux vocabulary → objective ("long-term behavior" as a comparison) → Earth system models → validation and lineage (Fig 3 :20). The middle link is the one most likely to break and least likely to have an instrument.

Strain test applied: the closed six-term substrate and the five named realms govern different census levels. Re-census dissolved the apparent conflict rather than softening either figure (see `11_audit.md`).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not license a single strongest interaction. They license a per-slot, enumerated union in which each interaction is a gradient-driven flow embedded in a signed feedback cycle, thresholds are comparison relations over overlapping slots, fast and slow slots are separated by a measured round-trip gap, and each verdict carries an anchor and a mode. Because R is indexed per slot and valence is relative to the residual, the asked-for ranking would average away the live signal and freeze a relative weight into an absolute one. Any named winner is the searcher's binding from the Earth-system frame, provenanced as such, not a result of the framework. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, per-slot verdicts and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is asking outside readers to endorse a scalar that cannot discriminate source configurations, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter and by never pooling later agreement as corroboration.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no realm is retracted. If a ground instrument returns a stable slot ranking across scales, the ranking can be added as a new settled term composed over the existing per-slot results; the shape stays intact. If the union reading fails, D6 is withdrawn and the ten slots are re-censused as a partition, with the earlier reading preserved in `01` §6.2.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An anchor returning the same slot order at local, regional and global scale under one fixed residual would settle the ranking; an instrument detecting no feedback cycle where the shape requires one would break F5; a partition of the ten pairs with each realm in one edge would break the union; a verbatim figure line naming a winner would break F7. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts (Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."), so more searching inside the same closure is not the remedy.
