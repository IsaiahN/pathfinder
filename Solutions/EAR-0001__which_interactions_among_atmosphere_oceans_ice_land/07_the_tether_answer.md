# The Tether's answer on EAR-0001

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Which interactions among atmosphere, oceans, ice, land and ecosystems most strongly shape Earth's long-term behavior? Restated as an effect: for each interaction slot `s`, what residual mass `R_s` does the slot carry against an observation of long-term behavior? The requested single ranking aggregates across slots; the figures forbid that aggregation (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The answer is therefore about the **form** a ranking would have to take, not about a winner.

## 2. The shape

1. **The five realms are a habitat, enumerated from contact, not the six substrate primitives.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Read actors, conditions and relations outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). The census is in `01` §1.2, with X1 and X2 excluded or absorbed visibly.
2. **Each slot is a gradient-driven flow, and the network is the governing structure.** Fig 13 :24 names network as "that entities relate, and influence travels"; Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Heat fluxes, freshwater-driven overturning and carbon uptake are different bonds over different states, not interchangeable magnitudes.
3. **Feedbacks are cycles, and the sign is relative to a named residual.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." A slot's "strength" cannot be reported without reporting the residual it is strong *for*.
4. **The interactions form a union with a compound residue.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The ten pairs overlap; thresholds and cascades are a second-order compound (D6), not a sixth realm. Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
5. **Fast and slow slots are separated by a measured round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Hours-to-weeks atmospheric slots and centennial-to-millennial ice and ocean slots are not one axis.
6. **The framework names no winner.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Any particular ranking is the searcher's binding from the Earth-system frame, provenanced as such, not a result of the framework.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "ice-albedo is the strongest interaction", nor any other winner. That is Option A, refused in `01` F1.
- Not that "tipping point" is a sixth realm. It is a threshold relation over slots and a compound (D6).
- Not that the five realms are the six substrate terms, or that one can be derived from the other. Different census levels (`01` §6.3).
- Not that agreement among Earth system models settles a slot. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not that the ranking scores or tiers used to budget this work are evidence. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not grounded. No measurement in this folder ranks the slots.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A per-slot instrument program, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

- **Slot invariance test:** an anchor returning the same slot order at local, regional and global scale under one fixed residual. If it returns a different order per scale, the ranking is scale-relative and the single-list claim fails.
- **Cycle detector:** an instrument that would detect a feedback cycle if one were present. If none is present and a linear chain accounts for the outcome, the cycle claim fails.
- **Valence test:** measure the same interaction under two different residuals. If sign and magnitude are invariant, the valence-relative claim fails.
- **Discriminating instrument:** show that two distinct slot configurations cannot produce the same long-term trajectory. Currently a scalar "interaction strength" is a projection with lossy information; a ranking from such a scalar is a metamerism error.
- **Standing refuters from `01` §4:** partition instead of union; invariant valence; a verbatim figure line naming a winner; a stable ground ranking across scales. As written, a "more data will settle it" claim is dead until the instrument is named, because underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts (Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is reachable and recorded. The ranking is unreached because no instrument in this package discriminates among the slot configurations that produce the same long-term trajectory. The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :138 to :139: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."

The decision is also a choice about what counts as an answer. If a user insists on a single ordered list, that is a different question, and by Fig 9 :54 it must be unbundled before it is searched again. That is a verdict about the question, not a silence from the framework.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not return a winner. They return a shape: the five realms are an enumerated habitat, each interaction is a gradient-driven flow embedded in a signed feedback cycle, the slots form a union with a threshold compound where two or more bind at once, and fast and slow slots are separated by a measured round-trip gap. Because the residual is indexed per slot and an actor's valence is relative to the residual, a single "strongest interaction" list would average away the live signal and freeze a relative weight into an absolute one. So the honest answer is per slot, with an anchor instrument and a mode attached to each verdict, and with the refuters written down in advance. The ranking stays open until a ground reading returns the same slot order across scales. That is `specified`, not `grounded`, and it is a verdict about the form of an answer rather than a refusal to look.
