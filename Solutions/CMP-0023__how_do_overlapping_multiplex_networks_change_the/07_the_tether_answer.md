# The Tether's answer: overlapping, multiplex networks and spread

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For each node slot, the gap between the predicted spread state and what is observed, R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot. The effect to explain: the same nodes, edges and transmission rule produce a different final set when a second overlapping layer is added. This is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **A multiplex is a union with its own loop, not a bigger single network.** Fig 9 :55-56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Collapsing the layers into one adjacency matrix discards the compound (Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*).
2. **Layer independence, not layer count, governs the change.** Fig 8 :48-49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* High overlap adds no path, no reach, no threshold change. Low overlap adds reachable pairs that neither layer has. Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
3. **Overlap is provenance as much as coefficient.** Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* The same shared edge is real corroboration if reached independently and redundancy if copied. Record the lineage.
4. **Spread is a multi-hop flow over a network structure.** Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*. Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* The relevant measure is compound reachability at the cascade horizon, per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22).
5. **The four currencies keep separate verdicts.** Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Information, disease, risk and cooperation differ in sign and in anchor; an averaged verdict would hide the live signal (Fig 1 :22). Coupled awareness can inhibit disease, interdependent risk can collapse abruptly, and cooperation can be enhanced only if coordination holds.
6. **The framework returns a shape, not a number.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. No threshold value, reproduction number or winning layer follows from the figures. Those are bindings from the network-science frame, provenanced as such.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that adding any layer always increases spread. It does not; a high-overlap layer adds nothing, and an inhibitory coupled process can reduce disease spread.
- Not that the four currencies share one verdict or one mechanism.
- Not that a single scalar overlap coefficient is sufficient. Provenance of the shared edges is part of the quantity.
- Not a numeric threshold, a reproduction number, or a named winning layer.
- Not `grounded`. No simulation or measurement was run; nothing here is settled by a ground.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A per-slot, per-layer measurement of the compound's reachable set, threshold and final size, with the edge provenance recorded, is the ground. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The standing refuters, written before the run (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4):

1. near-identical layers that change spread beyond the best single-layer projection;
2. independent layers that add no new reach;
3. reversal or failure of the overlap rule in two of the four currencies after anchors are controlled;
4. a one-source and a multi-source contagion responding identically to overlap;
5. a change from convergence to copy in a shared edge's provenance leaving surplus unchanged.

An instrument that discriminates convergence from copy at fixed edge sets is the analogue of the spectrometer in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the aggregate reading cannot tell you the source.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish convergence from copying, and where no compound simulation has been run. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (figures README). Agreement from the world papers is corroboration from separate pools, not a verdict; agreement from the framework essays is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40). Nothing is promoted to `grounded` by consensus.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single number for how multiplex networks change spread. It says that the change is governed by the independence of the layers, not by their count: layers with almost the same edges are one network wearing two names and add no reach, while independent layers create a union surplus of paths that neither layer alone has. Because the compound is categorically different from either layer, single-layer results cannot be averaged into it, and the shared edges must be recorded with their provenance, since convergent derivation and copying look identical in the adjacency matrix. Spread itself is a multi-hop flow over a network structure, so the relevant measurement is compound reachability per node, not a layer average. Information, disease, risk and cooperation keep separate verdicts because their anchors differ, and the same coupling can inhibit one and accelerate another. What the framework offers is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
