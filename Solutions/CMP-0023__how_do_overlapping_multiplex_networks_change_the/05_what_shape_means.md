## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :137-138: *"it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* And Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is: the relation between layer independence and union surplus, plus the prohibition on averaging the compound into a single layer. It constrains any candidate answer. It does not fill in a threshold, a reproduction number, or a winning layer. "Independence governs surplus" is a shape. "Layer B raises R0 from 2.1 to 3.4" is a measurement, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably"*, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here the effect is "new reachable node pairs appear when the second layer is added", not "inter-layer coupling", which is the network-science frame's own term.
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* Describe the spread residual before choosing to read graph theory, epidemiology, or behavioural ecology. Otherwise any field will seem to fit.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here that means a simulation or observation that actually measures the compound's reachable set, threshold, or final size and records the overlap and provenance of the edges.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A published threshold from network physics is an import: it must be recorded as coming from that frame, and it does not move the framework's wall.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, the spectrometer. Applied here: a measured final size does not determine which layer caused it. High-overlap redundancy and low-overlap convergence can produce the same aggregate statistic. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources (per-slot, per-layer, with provenance), not a name for the source.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The four currencies have different grounds, so a single name would be a metamerism error across domains:

| currency | reading | what the reading cannot tell you |
|---|---|---|
| information | adoption or recall counts | whether reinforcement came from one source or many (Centola and Macy) |
| disease | case counts | whether awareness inhibited spread or the geometry did (Granell) |
| risk | realised failures | whether the overlap was the intersection or the remainders (Radicchi) |
| cooperation | contribution rates | whether coordination between layers held (Wang, Szolnoki, Perc) |

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* The anchor here is the measured spread outcome, per slot and per layer, never the agreement of readers.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

The shape for CMP-0023 is: **layer independence sets union surplus, the compound is not the average of its layers, and the four currency verdicts stay separate.** The framework cannot name a numeric threshold, a reproduction number, a winning layer, or a single mechanism that covers all four currencies. Any such name is a binding from a world frame, provenanced as such, and is `specified` until the ground pays for it.

A common mistake to record: reading *"shape, not a name"* as a ban on naming held atoms. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive. Network and flow are already held (Fig 13 :23-24, :76), so naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh term. What is forbidden is claiming the framework returns the number or the specific layer.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of well-known network scientists assembled to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, however *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
