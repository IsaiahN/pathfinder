## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the change in a spread process when the contact structure is several overlapping layers instead of one network. Option A: the layers are one richer network, aggregate them and use one adjacency matrix. Option B: the layers form a union compound whose change is set by their independence, not their count. What each leads to: A discards the compound and can hide a live node slot; B yields a shape (independence governs surplus, no averaging, four separate currency verdicts) with five written refuters and an open numeric slot until a ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and is specific: multiplexity changes spread through the independence of its layers, not through their count. High overlap is one network wearing two names and adds no reach; low overlap creates a union surplus of paths neither layer holds. The compound is categorically different from either layer, so single-layer results cannot be averaged, and shared edges need a provenance record because convergence and copying look identical in the adjacency matrix. Spread is a multi-hop flow over a network structure, measured per node slot. Information, disease, risk and cooperation keep separate verdicts because their anchors differ and their signs can oppose. The framework returns no numeric threshold, reproduction number or winning layer. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, five refuters and the provenance of every figure quote, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, a cost Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters. A practical cost of acting on the wrong verdict is intervention on the wrong layer: treating a high-overlap redundant layer as a new transmission route, or treating a low-overlap surplus as redundant.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn; the shape is derived from held atoms (network, gradient, flow, union). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected version, and the four currency verdicts can be updated independently as each ground pays. If a refuter fires, the specific claim is retracted while the method stays.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot, per-layer measurement with edge provenance showing that near-identical layers change the threshold, that disjoint layers add no reach, that provenance at fixed edges leaves surplus unchanged, or that two currencies share a sign and an anchor would reverse the verdict. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Until a refuter fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
