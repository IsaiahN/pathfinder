## Restatement as an effect

What is being decided is whether a spreading process run on several overlapping layers is just a richer version of one network (aggregate the layers), or a categorically different compound whose behaviour is set by the overlap between layers. Option A (aggregate) leads to the prediction that layer identities can be discarded and one adjacency matrix suffices. Option B (compound) leads to the prediction that the overlap itself is a governing quantity: near-identical layers add nothing, independent layers add reach. An answer would look like a rule mapping a measured overlap property to a change in reachability, threshold, or final size, with a written refuter.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term:** "the union surplus S is the part of a spread's reachable set that exists only in the compound and not in either layer alone." Binding it makes the sought-for shape refusable, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction".

- No verdict taken from a filter. "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." (Fig 9 :60)

## Verdict in one line

Multiplexity changes spread through the independence of its layers, not through their count: high overlap is one network wearing two names and adds no reach, low overlap creates a Fig 8 union surplus that neither layer alone reaches. The four currencies keep separate verdicts because their anchors differ. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.
