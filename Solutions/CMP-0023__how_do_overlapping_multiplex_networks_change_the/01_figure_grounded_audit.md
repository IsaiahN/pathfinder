## 0. The residual in effect terms

We compare a prediction of the spread with what is observed, per node slot. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Formula step 1). The slots are the individual nodes: who is informed, infected, failing, or cooperating. *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19) And: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22)

The effect to explain is this: the same nodes, the same single-layer edges, and the same transmission rule can produce a different final set when a second, overlapping layer is added. What property of the overlap causes the difference? Options: (A) none, the layers are one network in disguise; (B) the independence of the layers sets how much new reach the compound has. What an answer would look like is bound in the README as the union surplus S.

Question type: a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57-58: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: two or more conditions in a compound, a spread process and a layer-overlap structure. Symmetry: overlap is a correlation read twice, *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* (Fig 8 :47); it is not a symmetric join of two equal layers, because swapping layers with different degree distributions is not inert. Scale: intra-layer (one hop), inter-layer crossing (a change of role, not a step upward, Fig 13 :4-5), and cascade (multi-hop). Union, not partition.
- **Domain 1, network topology.** Actors: nodes, intra-layer edges, inter-layer edges. Currency: adjacency, paths, reachability. Anchor: the measured contact data, who is connected to whom in each layer, which can be read off and does not move when a frame opines. This is the compound boundary question. Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*. Verdict: `general`, triangulated across Fig 8, Fig 9, Fig 2.
- **Domain 2a, information spread.** Actors: people or agents. Currency: messages, beliefs, adoptions. Anchor: observed adoption or recall data. Verdict `specified`, ungrounded; single-source for the figure transfer with world corroboration.
- **Domain 2b, disease spread.** Actors: hosts and pathogens. Currency: infections. Anchor: surveillance and confirmed case data. Verdict `specified`, ungrounded; same.
- **Domain 2c, risk or failure spread.** Actors: firms, infrastructure, components. Currency: losses and failures. Anchor: realised loss or outage records. Verdict `specified`, ungrounded; same.
- **Domain 2d, cooperation spread.** Actors: players. Currency: contributions and payoffs. Anchor: contribution rates in experiments or field data. Verdict `specified`, ungrounded; same.
- **Domain 3, verification / outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero and its agreement is not a witness (Fig 2 :40).

**Partition or union.** The layers are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition of independent cases (Fig 9 :54-58). The four currencies are a partition of outcome types with different anchors; they must not be pooled into one averaged verdict (Fig 4 :20, Fig 13 :38-39).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe the overlap in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53, Fig 9 :27-28). Establish topology reachability before any dynamic verdict. In a spread, intra-layer contact precedes inter-layer crossing precedes cascade (Fig 11 :52). The objective (what counts as spread) must be stated per currency before planning an intervention; a link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27).

**Strain test applied before concluding:** one apparent conflict was found and re-censused (F10). After re-census no strain is hidden.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The compound is a union with its own loop, not a bigger single network (decisive, `general`)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 9 :57-58: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

A multiplex network is exactly a union of overlapping conditions: a node pair may be joined in layer A only, layer B only, or both. The intersections are not "more of the same edge"; they are a specific compound. Single-layer results cannot be averaged into the compound verdict. Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*

World corroboration: De Domenico, Granell, Porter and Arenas (Nature Physics, 2016) write that *"Throwing away or aggregating available structural information can generate misleading results and be a major obstacle towards attempts to understand complex systems."* This is a low-overlap field check (a physics review, not the framework) with the same shape. It corroborates; it does not decide.

**Refuter:** a controlled pair of layers where aggregating into one adjacency matrix reproduces the compound's reachability, threshold, and final size exactly, with no residue in the intersections, breaks the compound claim.

### F2 — Overlap is independence read twice: the governing quantity (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Fig 8 :48-49: *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

Applied to networks: two layers whose edges are almost the same are one network wearing two names. Their union adds no path, no reach, no threshold change. Two layers whose edges are independent have a surplus, because the compound contains paths that neither layer holds. This is the answer's central dependency: it is the overlap, not the layer count, that governs spread.

World corroboration, two independent groups: Radicchi and Arenas (Nature Physics, 2013) state that an effective robustness strategy is *"to avoid the repetition of edges among layers, and thus bring the superposition of the layers as close as possible to an all-to-all topology."* Radicchi (Nature Physics, 2015) decomposes real interdependent networks into *"the intersection among the layers, and the remainders of the layers"* and finds that when the intersection dominates the remainders the transition is smooth, and when the remainders dominate it is abrupt. Both predict that overlap, not layer count, sets the outcome. Low overlap between these two authors' methods is partial (shared Arenas/Radicchi lineage in one case), recorded as such in the ledger.

**Refuter:** a pair of near-identical layers that measurably changes spread beyond the best single layer, or a pair of disjoint layers that produces no new reach, breaks this finding.

### F3 — Shared edges are not the signature; provenance separates convergence from adoption (`specified`)

Fig 8 :50-51: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

Two layers can share an edge because both independently discovered the same contact (convergence, maximum independence, real corroboration), or because one was copied from the other (adoption, zero surplus). The adjacency matrices look identical. Only the record of origin separates them. So a measured overlap coefficient is not enough; the lineage of the edges is part of the quantity that decides whether the union has surplus. Finding F10 re-census is required here and is done below.

**Refuter:** show two frames with the same shared edge and the same provenance record yet different surplus, or show that provenance never changes the predicted reach after overlap is controlled.

### F4 — Spread is a flow over a network and cascades multi-hop (`general`)

Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*. Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

A spread is a flow driven by a gradient over a network structure. It is not a one-step property of a node; the residual that matters is often several hops out. So the relevant measure for a multiplex compound is reachability across the union at the multi-hop horizon, not a per-layer average or a first-neighbour count. Fig 1 :19 and :22 govern the per-slot measurement.

World corroboration: Granell, Gomez and Arenas (Physical Review Letters, 2013) model an epidemic on physical contacts coupled to awareness on virtual contacts and find a metacritical point at which the epidemic threshold moves. Ward, Herbert-Read, Sumpter and Krause (PNAS, 2011) find that fish shoals *"achieve greater decision-making efficiencies through division of labor combined with social information transfer."* The animal-behaviour result is deliberately low-overlap (behavioural ecology, not network physics) and corroborates the cascade-and-transfer shape.

**Refuter:** a spread measured only at first hop that fully predicts the compound's final size would remove the cascade requirement.

### F5 — Four currencies, four anchors, four verdicts (`specified`, keep separate)

Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Fig 13 :38-39: *"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network."* By the same rule, information, disease, risk and cooperation are four currencies with different anchors, not four readings of one quantity. The loop is the same; the verdicts are not pooled.

The world literature confirms the divergence rather than merging it: Buldyrev and colleagues (Nature, 2010) find that in interdependent risk networks *"a broader degree distribution increases the vulnerability of interdependent networks to random failure, which is opposite to how a single network behaves."* Wang, Szolnoki and Perc (Scientific Reports, 2013) find cooperation can be promoted past isolated-network limits but *"requires simultaneous formation of correlated cooperator clusters on both networks"*, and collapses if coordination is disturbed. Granell and colleagues show information awareness can inhibit disease. Same structure, opposite signs per currency. Averaging would destroy the signal (Fig 1 :22).

**Refuter:** a demonstration that one scalar parameter predicts all four currency outcomes with the same sign and the same anchor would break the separation.

### F6 — Cycles and feedback belong to the compound (`general`)

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Coupled awareness and disease, and correlated cooperator clusters, are feedback loops: the output of one layer re-enters as input to the other. A single chain representation cannot express this. The sign (enhancing or inhibiting) decides whether the compound closes into an outbreak, a collapse, or a stable cooperative state.

**Refuter:** a coupled two-layer system whose dynamics are exactly the composition of two independent one-way chains, with no re-entry, would remove the cycle finding.

### F7 — Aggregation loses a live slot (`specified`)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* De Domenico and colleagues make the same point in their own field. Collapsing layers into one matrix can make a live node slot disappear into the average, which is precisely the failure mode a multiplex representation is meant to fix.

**Refuter:** show an aggregation that preserves the per-slot residual exactly for all layers and all four currencies.

### F8 — The framework returns a shape, not a number (`general`)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 6 :29-31 distinguishes sharpening in our own terms from casting in effect terms: *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? ... Sharpen in our own terms."* and *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. ... Cast it in effect terms."* What the figures license here is the shape (independence governs surplus; the compound is not the average). They do not license a numeric threshold, a specific reproduction number, or a named winning layer. Those are bindings from the network-science frame and must be provenanced as such, not presented as derivations.

**Refuter:** a figure-derived numeric threshold for a four-currency compound would break the shape-not-a-name limit and require a re-census.

### F9 — Filters are not witnesses (`general`)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Ground availability, tractability and tier are budget filters. A tier-A label says what gets tried first, not whether the compound claim is true. No score in the ranking spreadsheet may enter this verdict.

### F10 — Re-census of an apparent Fig 8 strain (`general`, resolved)

**Apparent strain.** Fig 8 :48-49 says two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names, so their union adds nothing. Fig 8 :50-51 says two frames can hold the same atom by convergence and that this is corroboration at maximum independence, so a shared atom can be a good thing. Read as one sentence, these conflict.

**Re-census.** They govern different quantities. The first is about **shared evidence** (correlation of the pools the frames draw on). The second is about a **shared atom reached by separate routes** (independent derivation of the same result). Convergence keeps independence maximal because no evidence pool is shared; copying destroys it. Fig 2 :31-32 defines independence as *"how little they draw on the same evidence"*, which resolves the two: the quantity is the evidence pool, not the atom's name. Same node pair, different lineage, different surplus. Matches the canonical rule that a reading making figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

**Verdict after re-census:** no strain. The overlap that suppresses surplus is shared provenance, not a shared edge label. This is why F3 requires the record of origin.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A pair of near-identical layers (high edge overlap, same nodes) measurably changes reachability, threshold or final size beyond the best single-layer projection. That breaks "one network wearing two names" (Fig 8 :48-49).
2. A pair of independent layers (disjoint edges, no shared evidence pool) produces no new path or reachable node beyond each layer alone. That breaks "two frames together reach what neither reaches alone" (Fig 8 :45).
3. The overlap-to-surplus relation reverses sign, or fails entirely, in at least two of the four currency domains after their anchors are controlled. That breaks the domain-transfer claim (Fig 4 :20).
4. A simple one-source contagion and a multi-source complex contagion respond identically to layer overlap. That breaks the currency distinction corroborated by Centola and Macy (2007) and the relation-not-kind rule (Fig 11 :50).
5. A provenance record that changes from convergence to copy, holding edge sets fixed, leaves the measured surplus unchanged. That breaks Fig 8 :50-52.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **What is R?** A per-slot spread residual on a compound of a process and a layer overlap, union not partition (Fig 9 :54-58).
2. **Which figure governs?** Fig 8 union surplus, with Fig 2 independence and Fig 9 unbundle. Low overlap between the union-surplus frame and the dependency-chain frame gives a triangulated structural verdict.
3. **What composes a spread?** A flow, energy and gradient over a network structure (Fig 13 :24, :76), multi-hop (Fig 11 :52-53).
4. **Does aggregation work?** No as a verdict; it can hide a live slot (Fig 1 :19, :22) and loses the compound (Fig 9 :55-56).
5. **Is a new term owed?** No. Network, gradient, flow, cycle and union are already in Γ. No mint and no import that moves the wall (Fig 6).
6. **What is unreachable here?** The numeric threshold and the named winning layer. Outward import from network science gives a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).
7. **Route.** SEARCHED first (is it in the closure?), then INWARD (sharpen measurement per slot), then OUTWARD (cast the residual in effect terms and find the frame that predicts its shape). Keep the three distinct (Formula step 7).

### Updated verdict

The shape survives and is now sharper than the first reading: multiplexity changes spread through the independence of its layers, not through their count; high overlap is redundancy, low overlap is surplus; the compound is categorically different from either layer and must not be averaged; the four currencies keep separate verdicts. Everything numeric is withheld. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**, one resolved (F10). Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.
