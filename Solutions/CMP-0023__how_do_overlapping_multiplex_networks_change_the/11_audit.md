Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: high overlap is redundancy and low overlap is surplus, with the compound categorically different from either layer. That is the residue worth sending. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
