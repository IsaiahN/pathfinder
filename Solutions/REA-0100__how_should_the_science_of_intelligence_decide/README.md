## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks for a decision procedure. One branch, "what machines can learn to do", ends in a benchmark result, a reward total, or a demonstrated capability. The other branch, "what machines ought to do", ends in an objective, a refusal, or a choice of which ground is allowed to settle the outcome. These are different effects with different anchors. The answer derived below does not pick a moral theory. It specifies what any candidate answer must look like, what instrument is missing, what would refute the shape, and what is owed before the specific content can be named.
