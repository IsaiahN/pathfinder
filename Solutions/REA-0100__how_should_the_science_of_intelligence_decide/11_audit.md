## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before re-reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an instrumented capability science and an uninstrumented objective link. Arity: at least three conditions plus a checking frame, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54), not a two-way choice. Symmetry: "more capable than" is a ranking that does not choose an arrow, while "ought" is ordered and needs comparison, subtraction and negation to be stated at all (Operators :51, :46, :58). A pooled score cannot choose the direction it will serve. Scale: link 3 inside one agent, selection between rooms, and per-slot values across people.
- **Domain 1, capability science.** Actors: learner, habitat, benchmark, instrument. Currency: ground-paid accuracy, *"The ground enters as the only metric"* (Fig 1 :23). Anchor: something that does not move when the model reports (Fig 2 :29). Partition boundary: can or cannot under a test.
- **Domain 2, objective and direction.** Actors: frame, sought-for shape, bound ordinary term. Currency: a residual the shape cannot accrue until bound (Fig 5 :62-66). Anchor: absent until binding, then the ground. Union with D1, a different quantity. Upstream of planning and learning (Fig 3 :20).
- **Domain 3, normative pluralism.** Actors: people, stakeholders, value frames. Currency: agreement as convention versus ground contact as verdict (Fig 2 :40-41). Anchor: human judgment for person-relative questions only (Fig 2 :37), indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). Union; no standing list (Fig 11 :51).
- **Domain 4, verification and governance.** Actors: ground maintainer, outside frame, reward channel, convention. Currency: independence (Fig 2 :31-32). Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45). A seat, not a content domain.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down"*, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 3 :10/:20/:25-27 (objective link and missing instrument), Fig 5 :62-66 (direction limit and binding discipline), Fig 2 :36-43 (anchors and convention guard), Fig 10 :42-49 (seat and convention rules), Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: bind the shape, name the anchor per domain, keep verdicts per slot, keep specifications refusable, report the gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete lab decision, for example adopting a reward model plus a constitution as the machine's objective and declaring that settled.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **verdict/convention distinction** (Fig 2 :40-41): the shorthand says "keep specifications refusable"; a lab can hear that as "write a constitution and treat it as the answer";
- the **seat rule** (Fig 10 :42-49): a verifier that can author the specification it checks has crossed into the frame it grades, and the shorthand's "per domain" does not by itself prevent that;
- the **source gate and shadow test** on value imports (Fig 8 :50-54): "name the anchor per domain" does not record where a value frame came from;
- the **per-slot non-aggregation** (Fig 1 :19/:22): a single alignment score reinstates exactly the average that hides the live slot.

One thing survives cleanly: a direction cannot be checked by the loop that cannot see it, and the objective sits above learning in the dependency chain. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
