## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be refuted before it can be named

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

For REA-0100 the sought-for thing is an "ought" that is not held in Γ. A shape here is: the objective link is bindable; the anchor does not update; verdicts are per slot; specifications stay conventions; the specific content is a stated gap. Each clause constrains any candidate answer and each clause has a refuter. None supplies a utility function.

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote from the exemplar mapping): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* A shape narrows; it does not fill.

## 2. The alignment essay is the direct canonical source for this question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* contains a section headed "Alignment to What?", opened this session:

> *"What does it mean to align an AI to human values when human values are a moving target? You cannot align to something that changes."*

> *"The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback. To the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement. A board that pays or does not."*

This is the framework's own answer to why a preference-based target is unstable: the target moves when the people move, so the machine is tethered to a channel rather than to a ground. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."* So the four-part separator in this folder is a candidate, not a verdict, and it needs an external validator.

## 4. What licenses a name, and why the "ought" content is a gap

Only two things license a name in this framework:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-54).

For REA-0100, neither has happened. No instrument discriminates among candidate objectives at the required level, and no value frame has been imported with a source gate and shadow test. So the specific content stays a `gap`, and by Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried quote from the exemplar mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A value frame is exactly such a new dimension. Importing one into a capability lab and calling it a derived objective would erase the dimension and keep the word.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 and §4: what "shape, not a name" actually governs

The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. It does not forbid naming an atom already in the library. In the exemplar this distinction was applied to Gradient and Spectrum; here it applies differently.

- The objective link, the anchor rule, the per-slot separator and the convention rule are not held as ordinary terms in a typical ML system's Γ. They are missing from the loop's vocabulary, so the shape-not-a-name limit governs them and the gap on specific content stands.
- Human judgment, reward channels, benchmarks and human-feedback data are already present in the lab's Γ. Naming them is a **binding** from the lab frame onto held terms, not an invention of a seventh primitive and not a derivation of an objective. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as something the framework derived.

So my first pass overstated the rule by writing "the framework never names anything". Retracted and narrowed. The reason the specific "ought" is a gap is that the discriminating ground is missing, not that names are categorically banned. Both statements are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Source discrimination: why a score cannot stand in for the objective

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A reward total or a benchmark score is such a projection. It can be produced by genuine alignment to the intended outcome, by specification gaming, by reward tampering, or by an unrecognised pathway. The score cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument about which score to use. This is F1 and F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` in essay form.

## 7. Two consequences for this answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous endorsers of a particular "ought" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the output against a ground, and a recorded verdict before any theory is shown.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. None of them can settle what a science of intelligence ought to decide.

## 8. Why the shape includes a stop

*How Intelligence Actually Works*, opened this session: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A candidate answer can become indistinguishable from the answer under the available instruments and still not be it. Hence the lineage must be recorded, the gap must be stated, and the stop must be honest. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The round-trip gap for this folder is enumerated in `11_audit.md`.
