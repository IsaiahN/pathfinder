## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is a three-condition union, not a named recipe. Generalization beyond familiar patterns occurs when a training regime leaves a re-applicable generator so reach grows (D1), when the habitat brings the learner into contact with conditions whose closures differ so an import is possible, with provenance for what crosses (D2), and when an unmoved ground returns a small per-slot residual on novel situations (D3). A representation carries bonds, not just ingredients (Fig 12 :5, :117); capability is agent and habitat, not agent alone (Fig 11 :39-40). No figure line ranks named algorithms or returns a threshold number, so that part is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind. Mode `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the name. Not `grounded`. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged into a single "best training method."

- **Cost if wrong:** a misdirected research programme. A lab that fails D2 would be sold a better optimizer, and a lab that fails D3 would be told to collect more data; a single lever would be applied to a union with three intersections. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, and it is avoidable by leading with the shape claim, the per-slot anchor and the refuter. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the four refuters and the lineage are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet without withdrawing a primitive.
- **Reversed by:** any of the four pre-registered refuters: (1) a recording-only learner with small, non-declining novel-slot residual against a non-updating ground; (2) an agent-only change that moves the OOD reading with contact fixed; (3) averaged accuracy reproducing the per-slot verdict on a held-out probe; (4) transfer fully predicted by an ingredient list with the bond term at zero. Also reversing: a figure re-census that finds a line naming an algorithm or returning a threshold number (closes the gap), or a source-discriminating instrument that shows the capacity came from a different structure than the one characterized. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
