## What the question asks in effect terms

It asks which training regimes produce a representation whose residual stays small on situations the training did not contain. The residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` indexed per situation slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, :22). "Generalize beyond familiar patterns" is a threshold comparison: small R inside the training closure, small R outside it. Two readings are possible. Option A: a named recipe (a specific objective, architecture or augmentation) is the answer. Option B: what can be said is the shape of any generalizing regime, the conditions under which reach grows and contact is maintained, plus a declared gap where the figures return no name. The derivation below reaches B.
