# Outreach draft: validate the shape first, then decide about the method list

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the live type question in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9 is stated in the recipient's own terms. The offer collapses on contact if the residual is smuggled in as a method preference.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about out-of-distribution generalization before you decide what to read

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the conditions, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the named method):** a representation generalizes beyond familiar patterns when three conditions hold together. First, the training leaves a re-applicable generator in the learner, so what can be built grows, not only what is stored. Second, the training habitat brings the learner into contact with conditions whose structure differs, so something outside the original closure can enter. Third, the evaluation ground does not change its answer when the model changes, and the residual is read per situation, not as one averaged score. These are three conditions on a union, not a ranking of training methods.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named recipe (contrastive, self-supervised, augmentation, meta-learning, invariant risk minimization) is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and after a genuine census no figure line selects an algorithm. Which specific method wins is a declared open item, not a hidden answer. I am also not claiming that more data or a larger model is the cause; those are budget filters, not witnesses.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a learner that only records familiar patterns still shows small, non-declining error on a genuinely novel situation measured by a ground that did not train on it;
> 2. an agent-only change that leaves the training habitat and contact unchanged moves the out-of-distribution reading;
> 3. a regime is counted as generalizing when its evaluation set is inside its training contact, or when an averaged score reproduces the per-situation verdict;
> 4. a representation's transfer behaviour is fully determined by its ingredient list with the arrangement term set to zero across a change of situation.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the corresponding condition. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract, path supplied on request): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula, public repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Shape claim and refuter before any theory | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | The exemplar's seat rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Named methods withheld | Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* A method preference is a search direction, not a term the loop can check. |
| Habitat condition stated before method | Fig 11 :39 - *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* |
| Per-slot evaluation, not averaged accuracy | Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* |
| Ground that does not update | Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* |
| Links only after stage 1 | Fig 10 :42 - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |

**Distribution rule:** one-to-one. Do not select recipients by likelihood of agreement; that manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Record any stage-1 verdict before showing the links, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
