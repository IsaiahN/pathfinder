## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for REA-0017 a **shape** is the narrowed form of a generalizing training regime that can be stated before a winning algorithm can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. The three conditions:

> a re-applicable generator, contact with differing closures, an unmoved per-slot ground

are a shape. "Use contrastive learning with augmentation strength 0.3" is a name, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* (carried mapping)
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` is stated as small error on a novel slot, not as "a disentangled latent space." The latter is the field's vocabulary, the former is the effect.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The four refuters in `02` and `08` are exactly such a description.
- Fig 9 :53: *"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A controlled comparison of training regimes on a held-out, non-updating OOD benchmark with per-situation reporting would settle which named method wins in that setting. No such measurement is in this folder.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. If a method from causal inference (invariant prediction) is brought into representation learning, the record must show where it came from and which gap it explains. IRM's own paper does this explicitly, citing ICP; that is why its import is legitimate while a bare "use IRM" recommendation is not.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For REA-0017, the six held atoms are not being redeclared; the question is which arrangement and which contact produce transfer. Naming a method as the winner would turn a provenanced binding into a verdict the framework cannot deliver.

## 4. Why "the learned vector is the representation" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried mapping) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several different physical sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A learned representation is a reading. Transfer behaviour is a projection of that reading onto a novel situation. Several training histories (a generator, a memorized table, an imported binding) can produce the same projection. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error.

Fig 11 :129: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A feature vector records ingredients; the arrangement that carries transfer is the half not recorded in the vector's magnitude list.

So the specific error is not "having a representation." It is reading the cause off the artifact. What is owed is a better instrument (an intervention that discriminates among sources) and a lineage record, not a name.

## 5. Q - "Then what does the question even mean by 'kinds of training'?"

**Answer:** "kinds" is an enumeration over habitats and generators, not a ranking over algorithm names. Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The three conditions in `01` are read off the contact structure of the learning problem: what the learner can build, what it is in contact with, and what settles the reading. The named methods in the literature are candidate bindings onto those conditions, and each needs a provenance record.

**Does this folder commit the error the README forbids?** No, provided the three conditions stay conditions. If a later reader turns "multiple differing environments" into "therefore IRM is the answer," that reader has named where the framework returns a shape, and has ignored the Kamath et al. 2021 caveat recorded in `01` F10. The correct form remains the union plus the gap.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote in §3 that naming any specific method is "forbidden." That overstated the rule, exactly as the exemplar's own §3/§4 once overstated it about naming Gradient. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. Contrastive objectives, augmentations and invariant penalties are already in the machine-learning frame's library; naming one is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is claiming the figure derived the name, or treating the binding as an import that moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22 - *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*). The `gap` in F9 is therefore about verdict authority, not about a ban on using method names. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
