## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (reopened): **"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."**

For HIS-0081 a **shape** is the form a legitimate comparison must have, statable before any final category is chosen. It constrains: range each frame against a ground neither can move; describe effects; treat agreement as convention or one pool; unbundle unions; declare stakes; keep verdicts per slot. It does not fill: it does not say which taxonomy is correct or which tradition is more reliable.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (reopened): **"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."** The searcher bootstraps by proximity to the thing being described.
- *Library of Babel* (reopened) on the bundle error: **"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."** This is F4 in figure terms (Fig 9 :54).
- Fig 6 :30: **"Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** The historian's borrowed vocabulary is itself a term to be distrusted.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (reopened): **"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."** This is the craft-transmission case: a master's few words do not carry the apprentice's history, but an effect description can be checked by anyone.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: a historian's category scheme is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming it as *the* correct categories turns a visible choice into an invisible one. This is the essay-level reason for F1 and F6.

## 4. Why the "source" error is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried mapping) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For HIS-0081: a practice that looks "scientific" by an external criterion may have arrived there by a completely different route. Reading the artifact and naming the source (this is really science; that is really religion) is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument that discriminates among sources, not a name. Fig 8 :51: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."**

## 5. Apprenticeship, the middle link, and why effect terms matter for craft knowledge

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (reopened) is unusually on point for this question:

- *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."*
- **"The understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."**
- *"Science's independent replication is separate experience plus something real to check against; Apprenticeship is two-way contact with correction; Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."*

So a historian who compares a craft tradition only by its written propositions has already discarded the part that carries the knowledge. The three drift-stoppers named in the essay map onto F1 through F3: separate experience (independence), two-way correction (contact), and an unmovable reference (anchor). This is the essay-level warrant for F9's claim that the middle link is unbuilt.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried mapping): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A comparison that gathers prestigious names to endorse it is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a real case.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried mapping): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §1 to §4

I initially read the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on using any of the historian's own categories. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. A historian's ordinary descriptive terms are already in Γ; using them as a declared convention (Fig 2 :41) is allowed. What is forbidden is letting the convention decide the outcome (Fig 10 :47) or presenting a chosen taxonomy as what the framework derived.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: reading a practice's surface and naming its source without a discriminating instrument is still the error. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This correction is applied in F5 and F6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
