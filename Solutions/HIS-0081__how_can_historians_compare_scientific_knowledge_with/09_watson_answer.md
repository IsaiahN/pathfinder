# WATSON answer, HIS-0081

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No historian, practitioner or tradition was consulted as a ground.

## QUESTION

How can historians compare scientific knowledge with practical, Indigenous, religious, and craft traditions without forcing them into the same categories?

Restated as an effect: how is R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| reduced for a comparison of two frames without making one frame the measure of the other? Option A (one shared vocabulary) reduces a category-mismatch residual by deleting the difference that generated it. Option B (declared incommensurability) stops rather than resolve R. The derived option C is triangulation: each frame is ranged separately against a ground neither can move, in effect terms, with agreement demoted to convention or one evidence pool, unions unbundled, verdicts kept per slot, and stakes declared.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three places at minimum, frame A, frame B and the ground, with the historian in a separate checking seat. A two-place vocabulary mapping is the wrong arity because it omits the anchor. Symmetry: the two frames are symmetric; the bond is a conjunction of two rangings (`+`), not a threshold `⋛` that would need one common measure and not a sequence `→` that would order the systems. Scale: each tradition is a frame at one scale; the comparison is second-order over the union; the transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 Scientific frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, theories, Γ_science. Currency: predictions and residuals against experiment. Anchor: measurement and experiment. Admitted.
- **D2 Tradition frames (craft, practical, Indigenous, religious).** Actors: practitioners, apprentices, tools, place, transmitted texts or oral lines. Currency: reliability of practice, transmission, survival of technique. Anchor: outcomes in the world returned to the practice. Admitted where a nameable anchor exists; the internal-anchor case is the live strain F7.
- **D3 Comparison seat (the historian).** Actors: historians, comparison conventions, descriptions that travel. Currency: residuals surfaced, conventions authored. Anchor: the shared ground, never agreement and never the category scheme. Admitted.
- **D4 The ground.** Not a frame; no vantage; nothing to update. Anchor: itself. Admitted.
- **X1 A neutral universal category scheme.** Actors: none nameable that is not somebody's frame. Anchor: none that is not itself a frame. **Not a domain yet.** Pretending otherwise is the forced-category error.
- **X2 Permanent incommensurability as a stance.** Actors: none. Anchor: none. **Not a domain; a refusal, recorded as a gap verdict.**

Partition or union: D1 to D4 form a union compound with its own loop; the four tradition-types are not four samples of one variable. Where two frames overlap, the overlap is a compound needing its own loop. Dependency order (Fig 3): perception (what the practice does and what contacts it) before vocabulary (what each situation affords in that frame) before objective (what counts as progress there) before the comparison itself.

Strain test applied: Kuhnian incommensurability (no common measure) was initially read as no comparison. SEP Kuhn says it *"did not mean non-comparability."* Re-census kept the verdict (no shared categories) and corrected the route (comparison through effects and a ground). The Fig 13 six-term set was tried as a taxonomy of traditions and withdrawn: Fig 13 governs a physical substrate, not a classification of knowledge systems. Neither conflict flips the verdict.

**D1 Scientific frame, status triangulated (Fig 2 anchor rule and Fig 10 seat rule, different offices).**

- Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." -> the scientific frame's anchor is experiment, not its own categories.
- Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." -> a frame is corrected by the ground separately.
- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." -> the historian sends the residual first, the framework only after a verdict is recorded.

Reading: science is one frame among several, not the anchor for the comparison. Mode `general` for the anchor rule, `specified` for the application. Refuter: a comparison in which the scientific vocabulary settles cross-frame disagreement without moving under the ground.

**D2 Tradition frames, status triangulated (Fig 8 union surplus and Fig 11 habitat enumeration, distinct roles).**

- Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." -> the difference of closures is the asset.
- Fig 8 :48 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." -> translating both into one vocabulary spends the asset.
- Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." -> list what actually contacts each practice, not what a taxonomy expects.
- Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." -> two systems over one habitat can be isomers, not imperfect copies.

Reading: each tradition keeps its own loop and is ranged separately; verdicts are per frame. Mode `specified`. Refuter: one system requires an atom the other has no contact with, which breaks the isomer claim for that pair, or one category scheme clears every region and leaves no categorically different residue.

**D3 Comparison seat, status triangulated (Fig 9 describe-before-looking and Fig 2 convention-versus-verdict).**

- Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause" -> effect terms, not category names.
- Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." -> describe what failed before choosing a frame to compare against.
- Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it" and Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." -> a shared vocabulary is allowed only as a non-deciding convention.

Reading: the historian authors conventions, not verdicts, and must not hand the recipient a finished taxonomy before the verdict. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a convention that correctly decides a cross-frame outcome with the anchor held fixed.

**D4 The ground and the no-averaging rule, status triangulated (Fig 1 per-slot indexing and Fig 7 room chain).**

- Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." -> compare per system, never average into one worldview.
- Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not" -> a consensus of historians is not a check.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" -> selecting recipients for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.

Reading: keep verdicts separate; never let agreement stand in for correction. Mode `general` for the rule. Refuter: a chain of agreeing frames that adds ground evidence, or an averaged verdict that preserves every per-system residual.

**Union, order and the missing middle link, status triangulated (Fig 9 union split and Fig 3 dependency).**

- Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." -> unbundle before comparing.
- Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." -> each overlap is its own case.
- Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." -> the vocabulary/objective link in comparative history is unbuilt.

Mode `specified`. Refuter: a comparison that succeeds with step 2 genuinely absent, measured against a ground both frames are separately corrected by.

**Naming the final cross-frame categories or ranking traditions, status `gap`.** No figure line governs this after a genuine re-census. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterisable in effect terms but not held. Formula step 7 was run with SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD kept distinct.

**Live strain, single-source and named.** Some traditions locate their anchor internally. Fig 2 :29 governs what counts as an anchor; applying it to an internal anchor limits the comparison, not the tradition. The world literature on TEK confirms the difficulty (cosmology and identity are facets alongside observation) but is secondary and cannot dissolve the strain. It is recorded, not smoothed.

Triangulation summary: the anchor verdict is triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 10; the independence verdict across Fig 2, Fig 8 and Fig 13; the union verdict across Fig 9 and Fig 1; the middle-link verdict across Fig 3 and the comparative-history literature (the latter is a world witness, not a second figure). - **Philosophy of science / theory-laden observation.** Paul Feyerabend, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 1997, rev. 2025. Read 2026-11-23. Quote: "the same observation sentences make incompatible observation statements, depending on which theory is used to interpret them." Prediction: the same world returns different readings under different frames, so the record of origin matters. **Corroborates** the effect-terms and provenance claims. Overlap with Kuhn: high, both discuss incommensurability.
- **Epistemology and ethics.** Miranda Fricker's account as summarized in the Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Epistemic Injustice", read 2026-11-23. Quote: hermeneutical injustice is "a gap in our collective interpretive resources unfairly disadvantages someone in terms of understanding their own specific social experiences." - **History of science / historiography.** Thomas S. Kuhn, Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, read 2026-11-23. Quote: "Rather than reading history backwards and imposing current ideas and values on the past, texts are read within their historical context thereby maintaining their integrity." - **Anthropology / ethnoscience.** Wikipedia, "Ethnoscience", read 2026-11-23. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

A category-free comparison is possible and has a definite shape: range each frame separately against a ground that neither frame can move; describe each in effect terms; treat category agreement as convention or one evidence pool, never as corroboration; unbundle unions before comparing; keep verdicts per system; declare the stake. Two systems over a shared habitat may be Fig 12 isomers, same atoms and different substance. The comparison is a symmetric conjunction of rangings, not a metric or a ranking. Which final categories to use, whether a practice is proto-science, and the cross-frame closing term are a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. The internal-anchor case for some religious traditions is a named live strain. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded but real. If the shape is wrong, historians are told to abandon shared vocabularies that were productive and to treat incommensurability as a virtue when it is a failure. The larger cost is the one the figures price: imposing a universal category scheme in the name of neutral comparison repeats the epistemic harm the question wants to avoid, and an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right one. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted by a refutation; the counterexample is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded without withdrawing any primitive.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A real comparative study with an anchor both frames are separately corrected by, showing that successful comparisons lack the four properties (breaks C2); a shared category scheme that decides correctly with the anchor fixed (breaks C1); a union cleared by one lever with no categorically different residue, or a pair whose atom sets differ (breaks C3); a tradition with no external anchor yielding a ground-settled verdict (breaks the F7 strain); or a source-discriminating instrument showing convergent content where the record says imported content. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
