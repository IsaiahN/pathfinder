# Expert verification packet - the Tether on data versus human judgment

**What this is:** a claim in a form a decision researcher or organizational scholar can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions, not `grounded`.** No decision outcome is claimed to have been measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Balancing data and judgment" is not a scalar to tune. A company decision is a union of a factual prediction, a value question about people and a legitimacy question, and each needs its own anchor. The legitimate anchor is the realized outcome, which neither the model score nor the manager's override can move. The model and the human should range against that anchor separately, with residuals indexed per decision slot, rather than being averaged into one score. Human judgment is the legitimate anchor only where the question is constitutively about people, and is otherwise a fallible instrument whose positive contribution is to be a genuinely different frame. Incomplete or biased data are a channel fault, so the remedy is to sharpen an instrument already returning something and to change the habitat if needed, not to replace the model with intuition.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the decision is a union of distinct questions, and both frames must range against one immovable ground, with residuals per slot. A weighted blend is not a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** show a decision whose ground provably moves when a frame pushes on it and which nevertheless settles correctly; or show a single global ratio that closes every per-slot residual in a live biased-data case.

### C2 - question-type-dependent; checkable by a decision researcher against the distinction between prediction and value

- **Statement:** human judgment is the legitimate anchor for the constitutively people-facing part of a decision, and only an instrument for the factual part. Its positive contribution requires low overlap with the model's evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :37-38 - *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 8 :45 - *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
- **Refuter:** show a people-facing value question where a non-human instrument is constitutive; or a domain where model and human frames are necessarily correlated so independent elicitation buys no surplus; or a case where a manager who shares the model's evidence still adds surplus that survives a ground check.
- **Live strain to check explicitly:** if the "human anchor" is elicited only after seeing the model's recommendation, it is a moving anchor and this sub-claim fails for that slot. This is disclosed in `01` §5, S1.

### C3 - organizational / habitat-facing; checkable against research on biased data and automation

- **Statement:** incomplete or biased data are channel decay, so the remedy is to improve an instrument already returning something and to change the habitat where needed. A model-only improvement in an unchanged habitat changes nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 6 :32 - *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **Refuter:** show an agent-only improvement that changes contact with the ground while habitat and instruments stay fixed; or a case where no proto-instrument exists at any resolution, in which case the honest verdict is `unreachable at this budget`, not a call for intuition.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a percentage split, an optimal weight, or a universal decision rule.
- Not that human judgment beats data, or that data beat human judgment.
- Not that committee agreement is a verdict.
- Not that the framework settles any actual company decision. It returns a shape.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the human role is unconditional. Where the frames overlap, the human role is empty and the claim says so.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A decision slot where an experimental holdout or field audit closes the residual that the dashboard hides.
2. A provenance record that separates the model's atom from the manager's atom and shows which one paid at the ground.
3. An independent pre-model elicitation of the people-facing anchor, so it can be checked for movement.
4. A habitat change (feedback returned, contested decisions allowed) that measurably changes contact.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29-30, :37-40, :45 and Fig 9 :54. Four short passages.
2. Say whether the prediction/value/legitimacy split is a real unbundling for a decision domain you know, or an artificial one.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish model residual from human residual at a real decision slot, or say why none exists.
4. Check C2 against the AOM/Bath finding on managerial judgment under Gen-AI: does over-reliance raise the overlap between the frames?
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
