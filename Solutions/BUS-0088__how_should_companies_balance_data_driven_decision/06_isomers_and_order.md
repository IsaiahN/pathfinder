# Is "balance" a quantity? Structure, order and the union of conditions

**Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the order claim. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).**

## RESIDUAL

The question is phrased as a balance, which invites a single number. Two supporting questions are owed: (1) is a scalar ratio the right *structure* for joining a model frame and a human frame, and (2) is "data versus judgment" a partition into two cases or a union of overlapping conditions?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: two frames (data/model, human/judgment), one ground (realized outcome), residuals indexed per slot, instruments, and a policy level that governs future decisions. Relations available: the seven bonds plus negation. No new primitive is needed. The question is which relation actually holds and in what order the links are checked.

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Is a balance ratio a term or a flattening?

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :106: *"Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."*

A model score and a manager's view are two operands. Saying "70/30" records the ingredients and their weights, but the thing that decides whether the decision is good is the relation each frame has to the ground. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The bond that matters is frame-to-ground, repeated for each frame, not a frame-to-frame blend. A blended score is Fig 12's flat formula collapse: the arrangement is discarded and nothing downstream can tell which relation held.

Candidate relations from the operators table:

- `+` conjunction (both hold at once): symmetric; would make model and human simultaneous co-authors of one answer. This is the blend, and it hides which frame produced which atom.
- `∥` disjunction (either suffices): symmetric; would let whichever frame is louder settle the slot.
- `⋛` comparison (a threshold relation between two quantities): ordered, but both operands are frames, not quantities at the same level, and the comparison that matters is against the outcome, not between the frames.
- `¬` negation (refusal of a claim): needed when the persistent residual shows a held regularity is false. Fig 5 :58-60 ranks the candidate: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at."*

**Result:** none of the frame-to-frame relations is the right object. The correct structure is two separate ranges against one anchor. A ratio is not a different arrangement of the same atoms; it omits the anchor, which is the load-bearing operand. Triangulated for the structure (Fig 12 + Fig 2); single-source for the operator inventory.

### Q2. Partition or union?

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

The company decision is a union of at least three conditions: (a) a factual prediction where a ground exists and data may be degraded, (b) a value question about people where the people are constitutively the anchor, and (c) a legitimacy question about whether the decision should be automated at all. A single lever tuned on the aggregate clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds.

The residue after unbundling is smaller and categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound (Fig 9 :57-58). This is why §2 of `01` gives each domain its own anchor and refuses to average verdicts.

**Falsifier for Q2:** show a decision where the three conditions are separable in fact, so a single rule tuned on the aggregate closes every slot residual. Then the union reading fails and the partition reading stands.

### Q3. What is the order of the chain?

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

For a decision under incomplete data the links are:

1. **Perception:** is the channel returning a readable signal for this slot? Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*
2. **Vocabulary:** do we have a term for what this situation affords? Without it, more data are just numbers.
3. **Objective:** what counts as progress on this slot, in its own terms?
4. **Planning:** which frame acts where, and what is elicited before what?
5. **Learn and carry:** name what worked, verify it against the ground, carry the method.

A reading taken below the break is not a weak reading; it is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23). So the order of action is: repair perception and vocabulary first; only then ask whether to weight the model or the manager. Most organizations do the reverse because measurements exist above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).

### Q4. What crosses between one decision and the policy level?

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

A method of ranging two frames against an outcome can cross from one decision to the policy that governs future decisions. A recording of one decision cannot: it is an instance, and replayed downward it produces a company that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one. The gap between the method and its instantiation is the round trip gap `R_T`; it is not zero and must be measured where possible. In this simulated derivation it is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`, not numerically.

## ANALOGUES (corroboration only, never the verdict)

- **Two navigators and one landmark.** From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark, and nobody can talk the landmark into moving."* This is the operational picture of triangulation.
- **Courts and apprenticeship.** The same essay names the three drift stoppers: *"Science's independent replication is separate experience plus something real to check against; Apprenticeship is two-way contact with correction; Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."*
- **Procedural protections.** The fairness textbook says bureaucracies incorporate protections ensuring decisions are made *"on the basis of the right and relevant information, and with the opportunity for challenge and correction."* This is the organizational analogue of keeping the ground reachable and the frames separate.
- **Group collapse.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."*

These analogues come from the same author for the first two (high overlap with the figures) and from independent management/fairness research for the last two. Agreement from the shared pool is one witness, not two.

## FALSIFIER

Written before the conclusion: if a decision can be shown where a single scalar ratio closes every slot residual against the ground, with no separate ranging of frames and no per-slot tracking (breaking Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :45, Fig 12 :94), then Q1 is refuted and the blend is the right structure. If the three conditions can be shown to be separable in fact (Q2), the union reading is refuted. If effort above the break can be shown to rescue a dead perception link (Q3), the dependency order is refuted.

## CONCLUSION

"Balance" is not a quantity in the operator inventory. It is a mis-naming of an arrangement: two separate ranges against one immovable ground, with residuals indexed per slot and a method, not a recording, carried up to the policy level. The decision is a union of conditions and must be unbundled before a frame is chosen. The order is perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry. Every one of these is a shape claim with a stated falsifier, not a measurement.
