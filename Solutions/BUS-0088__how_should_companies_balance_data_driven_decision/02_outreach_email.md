# Outreach draft - check the residual before you decide about the framing

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have stated your own ground and your own failure condition for the claim below. If you cannot name what would refute it, you are not in a position to check it, and the framework's own rule says a check that reconstructs the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

---

**Subject:** One decision-residual claim to check, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about organizational decision-making that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms (`general` shape, `specified` predictions):** a company commits an action on a decision slot, a hire, a loan, a launch. It holds a prediction from a data model and an observation after acting. The residual is the gap between them, measured per slot. When data are incomplete or biased, the model's residual on some slot persists and does not trend down. The question is which frame, the model or the manager, settles which part of that gap.
>
> **The shape claim:** "balance" is not a ratio to tune. It is a union of different questions, a factual prediction, a value question about people, and a legitimacy question, and each needs its own anchor. The company should make both frames range separately against the same immovable ground, which is what actually happened, and keep the residual per slot. It should not average a model score and a manager's override into one number. Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that managers are more trustworthy than models, that intuition beats data, that any particular percentage split is right, or that this settles the question. Human judgment is the legitimate anchor only when the question is constitutively about people. For a factual prediction it is an instrument with blind spots, and it can be biased in ways the model does not measure.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. A decision where the ground provably moves when a frame pushes on it, yet the decision still settles correctly.
> 2. A domain where model and human frames are necessarily correlated, so independent elicitation is impossible and triangulation buys no surplus.
> 3. A live biased-data case where a single global accuracy figure closes every per-slot residual.
> 4. A model-only improvement that changes contact with the ground while habitat and instruments stay fixed.
> 5. A people-facing value question where a non-human instrument is constitutive.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then, I will send the sources.** The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority. Please record your verdict on the claim above before I send anything else, so that your agreement later cannot be mistaken for corroboration.
>
> **If you want the method:** I can send the eight-step formula to reuse on a decision problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]

## Why the offer is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory withheld | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Asking for belief manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Seat Map :163). |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287 - *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Verdict on stage 1 recorded before any theory | Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Seat Map :35, the outside frame must not hold the interiors. |
| Links come only after | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A full framework sent before a verdict is a recording, not a generator. |
| Method offered last, with its own refuter required | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* |
| No personal emails, no receptiveness rank | Seat Map :163 and :171. Public institutional or opt-in channels only; see `03_public_contact_points.csv`. |

## Opt-out line for every message

> If you would prefer not to be contacted about this again, tell me and I will not follow up.

No automation, no bulk send, no misleading subject line. A reply to stage 1 is a conversation, never corroboration. Any refutation returned is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
