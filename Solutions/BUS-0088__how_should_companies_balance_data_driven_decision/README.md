## The residual in effect terms

A decision slot `s` (a hire, a loan, a launch) carries a residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`: the gap between what the data/model frame predicts and what the ground returns. The question "how should companies balance data and judgment?" asks which frame settles which part of `R_s` when the channel from the ground is degraded. The answer derived below is that "balance" is not a scalar to be tuned: the decision is a union of distinct questions, each with its own anchor, and the only legitimate balancing is triangulation of two low-overlap frames against a common immovable ground.
