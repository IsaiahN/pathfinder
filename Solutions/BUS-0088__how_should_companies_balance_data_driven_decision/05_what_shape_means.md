## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says the system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"* and can instead narrow, saying the question is not one question and must be split.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *"The decision has a ground, two frames that must range against it separately, and residuals kept per slot"* is a shape.
- *"Use a 70/30 split of model and manager"* is a name, and a name for something the derivation does not license.

The same essay supplies the reason the bundle has to be split first: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the library version of Fig 9 :4-5 and it is the opening move of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Applied here: describe the decision residual (a prediction that misses on a named slot whose data are degraded), not the cause (an algorithm, a bias type, a department).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` opens with the residual and the refuter, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* In a company, this is the realized outcome, the experiment, the market, the patient, the counter, not the model's confidence and not the meeting's consensus.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The company's chosen metric set is a visible guess about what counts, not nature's own list. Naming a metric as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

A ratio such as 70/30 has neither license. No ground settles it in general, and it is not an import from a frame whose closure differs; it is a preference dressed as a parameter.

## 4. Why the "balanced score" answer is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A blended decision score is exactly this projection. A model score, a manager's override, and a committee average can all produce the same number 72. The number does not tell you which source produced it, and several institutional processes can produce that 72 with very different failure modes. What is owed is a discriminator (provenance of each frame's contribution, plus the ground reading), not a more elaborate blending formula.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point about who may read the result: *"If you are not an expert, the outputs and the steering would be unverifiable by you, and likely lead you to the wrong conclusions and outputs unless you have an unmovable ground outside yourself and the replicator that can validate the outputs (RLVR)."* A non-expert manager handed an AI recommendation without a ground is in precisely that position.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

## 5. Two roles for the human frame, from the essays

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* The manager contributes only while their closure differs from the model's. Long collaboration on the same dashboard spends that difference. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says it directly: *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."*
- The same essay says: *"Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark, and nobody can talk the landmark into moving."* This is the operational meaning of balance in this solution.

This does not make human judgment sovereign. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that human values are a moving target and that alignment to preferences drifts; the framework ties alignment to the ground, *"not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback."* Fig 2 :36-37 narrows this: human judgment is the legitimate anchor only where the question is constitutively about people, and an instrument elsewhere.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A "data-driven company" award, a famous chief data officer, or a well-cited vendor case study is a budget and a signal of where to look. It is not evidence that a decision was good. The legitimate ask of an outside reviewer is a refutation of the shape claim, not an endorsement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter about the demo: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own §1 and §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that naming any role for human judgment violates the "shape, not a name" rule. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in the frame. Both "model frame" and "human frame" are already present in the company; what the derivation does is **bind** them to roles (instrument on some slots, anchor on others) and record the binding with provenance. That is not inventing a missing primitive.

What remains forbidden, and is not retracted: treating the 70/30 ratio as the answer. A ratio is not a binding onto a held atom with a source gate; it is a preference with no residual of its own. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 9. What would make this more than a shape

An instrument that discriminates sources at the decision slot: an experiment, a holdout, a field audit, an affected person's account, a provenance record of which frame produced which atom. Until one of these pays, the answer stays a shape and the verdict stays `candidate`.
