## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form any incentive arrangement must have before we can name a single metric. It says: the anchor must be unmovable by opinion; residuals must be read per slot; the arrangement must carry the information; the verifier must be outside the maker's interior. It does **not** say "use relative TSR over three years" or "use adjusted EBITDA". The first is a shape. The second is a name.

The distinction matters because this question is usually answered with a longlist of metrics. A longlist is an ingredient list, and Fig 12 :5 says *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity, then recurse. Use the terms closest to the thing you cannot yet name, look for new terms that overlap with them, and narrow. For this question the near terms are "pay for performance", "target", "benchmark", "clawback", "restatement", "peer group". Their overlap points to a missing term: an instrument for "did the reading move without the ground moving".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the question is restated as an effect: what would we see if pay tracked long-term value, and what would we see if it tracked a movable reading?
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` uses exactly this form.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, and the seeker *"leave[s] as the author"*. Here: once you can state what a non-movable anchor, per-slot residual, priced arrangement and independent verifier would look like, you have composed the answer shape through the search.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this solution refuses to name a metric

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A particular firm's metric is licensed by outcomes that refuse it, over a horizon the contract actually spans.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A regulator's pay-versus-performance table, an independent economic reconstruction, or a restatement series can cross as a new reading, provided its origin and its gap-explaining role are recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A metric family (accounting earnings, market price, peer rank) is a visible choice, not nature's own list. Declaring TSR "the long-term metric" makes that choice visible, which is useful, but it does not make it neutral or settled.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: metamerism of the pay reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a reported revenue increase can come from durable demand, from a pull-forward of sales, from an acquisition, or from a change in recognition policy. The reading (revenue) does not determine the source (long-term value creation). Calling the reading "performance" names a source from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a better adjective in the proxy statement.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance side: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants."* Two firms can report the same adjusted EPS, one from operations and one from adjustments; the artifacts match and the warrants do not. The essay's conclusion, *"indiscernibility is not identity"*, applies directly.

## 5. Q  -  what does "shape" mean for this question? ANSWERED

**A shape here is a four-part constraint plus a stop rule:**

1. an anchor that settling outcomes can change but opinions cannot;
2. residuals indexed per metric and per horizon, never collapsed into a single alignment score;
3. a priced arrangement (bonds: vesting, hurdles, comparison basis, discretion, clawback) rather than an ingredient list;
4. a verifier with different evidence and no access to the maker's interior;
5. an honest `gap` when no instrument in the closure can discriminate the reading from the ground.

Nothing in that list names a metric. That is the point. If a reader wants a particular metric, they must supply the ground measurement and the provenance themselves.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in §3. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame's library. Accounting metrics, stock prices, peer groups, clawbacks and disclosure tables are all already in the governance library. Naming one of them is a **binding** from the compensation frame onto a held term, not the invention of a new primitive.

What is genuinely missing is the instrument that discriminates source from reading (§4). That missing thing is correctly reported as a shape, and it is the thing the framework may not name. Both claims are now recorded: selecting an existing metric is a provenanced binding, while the discriminator remains an unreached shape. The earlier §3 wording is not silently erased; it is narrowed here.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known compensation scholars who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against real contracts, and a check against outcomes that can refuse it.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any compensation contract and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

One more guard from *Arrival of the Fittest*: the architecture article warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* Its own "Three mappings offered and declined" section is the precedent for refusing a mapping that resembles the framework without aligning with it. The mapping "executive effort = energy, metric = spectrum" was offered and declined in `01` §1 for exactly that reason.
