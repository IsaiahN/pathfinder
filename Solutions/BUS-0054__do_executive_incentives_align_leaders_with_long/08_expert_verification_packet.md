# Expert verification packet  -  the Tether on executive incentives

**What this is:** a claim in a form an executive compensation or corporate governance researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`  -  not `grounded`.** No contract or outcome was measured in this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question's "or" is a union, not a partition. A contract contacts long-term value only if four conditions hold together: its anchor is an outcome no party can move by opinion; residuals are read per metric and per horizon and never averaged into one alignment score; the arrangement (vesting, hurdles, comparison basis, discretion, clawback) carries the information that the ingredient list drops; and the verifier has different evidence and no access to the maker's interior. It rewards easy-to-measure outcomes when the metric is produced by the same system it scores, the channel saturates, the verifier shares management's evidence pool, or the bond makes the measurable target the objective. The framework returns that shape, not a named metric. Which metric works for a particular firm is a provenanced binding that only a ground measurement can settle.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 1

- **Statement:** the anchor of an incentive contract must not update on the metric; a firm-produced metric cannot score the firm. When the reported number is used as the ground, the design is a mirror chain, not a triangulation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29  -  *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 1 :27  -  *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :35  -  *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **What to check:** is the anchor in a real contract an outcome (realized over the horizon) or a reading (a date-specific quote or an adjusted figure)? Does the metric move when the committee wants it to, or only when value changes?
- **Refuter:** show a contract whose anchor is a movable metric that still tracks long-term value out of sample across horizons; or a firm-produced metric that discriminates value on held-out firms after removing the producing frame's influence.

### C2  -  governance-facing; checkable by a researcher against real contracts

- **Statement:** packages with identical ingredients and different bonds are isomers under Fig 12 and behave differently at the ground. The arrangement, not the list, is the contract.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5  -  *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117  -  *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :126  -  *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
- **What to check:** take two firms with the same salary, bonus, options and restricted-stock ingredients but different vesting, hurdle basis, comparison group or clawback. Do their long-run outcomes diverge in the way the bond structure predicts? Is the disclosure recording the bond or only the ingredients?
- **Refuter:** show matched pairs with identical ingredients and different bonds whose realized long-term outcomes are indistinguishable; or show that a bond has no effect on behavior (a notation-only difference).

### C3  -  verification-facing; checkable by an auditor, regulator or investor

- **Statement:** independence requires different evidence and non-access to the maker's interior. A second reader of the same adjusted figures is one evidence pool, not a second frame. A stake in the reading disqualifies the reader even when a stake in the outcome is unavoidable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :42  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 2 :31  -  *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 10 :59  -  *"The condition is that nothing about which value the ground returns today serves the occupant: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
- **What to check:** does the verifier use management's adjusted figures or an independent reconstruction? Is the committee's own standing tied to the reported number? Can the verifier detect a restatement or a metric-only movement without reconstructing the claim from management's interior?
- **Refuter:** show a verifier with full access to the maker's interior and evidence pool that detects metric-only movement as reliably as one with independent evidence; or show a decider whose reward is independent of the reading yet who cannot be checked against the ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that incentives never align, nor that they always reward manipulation. All four slots are live; the verdicts are separate.
- Not that any specific metric is correct for all firms. That is a binding with provenance, not a derivation.
- Not that more pay, more KPIs, or more outside directors automatically improve contact. New KPIs in the same closure add no atom (Fig 6 :21), and readers of the same report share one pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that an aggregate pay-for-performance correlation settles a specific contract. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken; nothing here is settled by experiment.
- Not an endorsement by any of the venues listed in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. A submission route is not an agreement.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating measurement per firm: does an independent reconstruction separate durable value creation from a pull-forward, an acquisition or a recognition-policy change in the reported metric?
2. A metric-only movement event: a restatement or clawback where the reading moves and long-term outcomes do not.
3. Matched-pair bond test on identical ingredients with different vesting, hurdles and clawbacks, checked on long-run outcomes.
4. A verifier comparison: independent evidence versus management's adjusted figures, measured on detection of metric-only movement.
5. The six standing falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, written before the run.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :27, Fig 12 :117 and Fig 10 :42 (four short passages).
2. Name one real contract where the anchor is a reading rather than an outcome, or say why none exists in your sample.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate source from reading in a reported metric, or say why none is possible at the relevant horizon.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
