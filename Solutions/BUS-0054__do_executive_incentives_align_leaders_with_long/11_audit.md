## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the residual line in the question and the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap is between a pay-triggering reading and the long-term outcome the firm needs. Arity: several slots (metric, horizon, governance check). Symmetry: the `effort → metric → pay` chain is directed; the `metric → value` instrument relation can fail one-way, so metric movement is not evidence of value movement. Scale: three levels, with a lossy transform from the reporting cycle to the board contract and from the contract to the assurance regime.
- **Domain 1, action and reporting.** Actors: executives and reporting systems. Currency: effort and reported outcomes. Anchor: realized long-term outcomes, nameable and not moved by committee opinion. Question type: does the reading contact the anchor?
- **Domain 2, contract design.** Actors: committee, consultants. Currency: targets, benchmarks, payout rules. Anchor: long-term shareholder interest, nameable; fails where the committee's standing depends on today's reading. Question type: union of horizon mismatch and stake in the reading.
- **Domain 3, measurement.** Actors: standards, analysts, auditors, data vendors. Currency: reported and adjusted figures. Anchor: underlying economics, reachable only through fallible instruments. Question type: union of self-scoring and channel saturation.
- **Domain 4, assurance.** Actors: auditor, regulator, shareholders, courts. Currency: votes, opinions, restatements, clawbacks. Anchor: realized outcomes, which do not vote in the room. Question type: verification, independence and non-access.
- **Union and order.** Four overlapping conditions, so separate loops, never an average. Dependency order: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → carry. The likely break is at the vocabulary-to-objective link, where no instrument exists.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading, and its dependency order. I derived them from a different starting point: the primary arrived at the domains by listing actors around the pay setting, while I arrived at them by splitting the residual's arity (metric / horizon / check) first and then asking which anchor each slot could have. Different routes within the same evidence pool; the agreement is weak but not circular in the mapping. It is still one pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:27, Fig 2 :29/:31/:42, Fig 10 :24/:42/:59, Fig 12 :5/:94/:117.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-part shorthand carried in `07` §2 and `08` §1: unmovable anchor, per-slot residuals, arrangement-carried information, independent verifier.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a real compensation contract for a real firm: a three-year relative TSR hurdle, annual bonus on adjusted EPS, options with one-year vesting, and a committee that reviews the same analyst pack management uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot distinction** collapses: "per-slot residuals" becomes one headline alignment figure in the annual report;
- the **stake in the reading** is dropped: "independent verifier" is instantiated as more outside directors who read the same adjusted EPS pack, which is Fig 2 panel 2;
- the **bond price** is dropped: "arrangement carries the information" becomes an ingredient list in the disclosure table;
- the **source-discriminating instrument** is absent: no element of the concretised contract separates pull-forward from durable demand;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit** is breached if the shorthand is read as naming relative TSR as the answer rather than as one binding among many.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the anchor must be an outcome rather than a reading, and the verifier must not reconstruct the claim from the maker's interior. That residue is worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `01` §6 and `09` now state.
