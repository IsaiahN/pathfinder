# The Tether's answer on executive incentives

**Mode: `specified`  -  not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The contract rewards a reading; the firm needs long-term outcomes. `R` is the gap between them, indexed per metric and per horizon. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The question's "or" is a union of four conditions (horizon mismatch, metric manipulability, shared evidence pool, wrong bond structure), so the answer is four domain verdicts, not one averaged binary.

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor must be an outcome no party can move by opinion.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Realized long-term outcomes qualify; the reporting-date stock quote, an adjusted EPS figure, and a peer group drawn from the same reporting culture do not. Where the metric is used as the ground, Fig 2's mutual-update collapse is live and the drift is coherent. **D2 verdict: `triangulated`** on anchor and arrangement, with the middle-link break `single-source`.

2. **Incentives can contact value, but sensitivity is not contact.** World evidence (Hall and Liebman) shows large pay-to-performance sensitivity once equity is counted, while noting that options also reward market-wide movement. Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Raising the pay multiplier does not restore a dead channel. **D1 verdict: `triangulated`** on per-slot and contact findings.

3. **A metric the firm produces cannot score the firm.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* Reported figures are the system's own output. The channel decays even though the ground does not (Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*), so targets saturate and peer pools stop discriminating. **D3 verdict: `triangulated`** on frame-internal and channel-decay findings; adding KPIs inside the same closure adds no atom (Fig 8 :32).

4. **The arrangement, not the ingredient list, is the contract.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Two packages with the same salary, bonus, options and restricted stock but different vesting, hurdles, comparison basis and clawback are different substances. Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Disclosure that lists ingredients prices the description and leaves the action cost unstated.

5. **Verification must be a different frame, not a mirror.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* An auditor working only from management's adjusted figures is not a second frame. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* **D4 verdict: `triangulated`** on independence and non-access.

6. **The missing instrument sits at the vocabulary-to-objective link.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Boards have vocabulary for metrics and plans for payout; they lack an instrument for "what would count as long-term progress" and "did the reading move without the ground". Most reform arrives at the payout link, above the break. **Single-source** in the figures; world corroboration from the SEC Chief Economist's attention remark is weak and declared.

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a named metric.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. No compensation metric is named here. A particular metric is licensed only by a ground measurement on a specific firm and horizon, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that incentives never align, and not that they always reward manipulation. Both are live in different slots; the union is kept unbundled.
- Not that a particular metric (TSR, adjusted EPS, ROIC, ESG score) is the right one. That is a binding with provenance, not a derivation.
- Not that more pay, more metrics, or more outside directors automatically improve contact. More metrics inside the same closure add no atom; more readers of the same report are one evidence pool.
- Not that a pay-for-performance correlation settles a verdict. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not `grounded`. No contract was measured and no long-term outcome was read here.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* shows that an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, not a debate. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Source discrimination:** does an independent reconstruction separate durable value creation from pull-forward, acquisition, or recognition-policy change in the reported metric? An instrument that does would name the slot for that firm.
- **Metric-only movement:** a restatement or clawback event in which the reported figure moves and realized long-term outcomes do not. That is a live per-slot residual, not a convention.
- **Bond test:** matched pairs of firms with identical compensation ingredients and different bonds (vesting, hurdle, clawback), checked against long-run outcomes. This would fire the isomer falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- **Verifier test:** a checker with independent evidence compared with one holding management's adjusted figures, measured on detection of metric-only movement.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §3:** a movable-metric-only contract that still tracks value out of sample; one correlation that preserves slot information; metric and value shown to be the same quantity; identical-bond-behavior result; verifier non-access shown irrelevant; a figure line naming a metric.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The compensation library already contains many metrics and many controls; what it lacks is a discriminator between a reading and its source, and the instrument that states what would count as long-term progress. Until one exists and is read, the answer stops at the shape and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *They're Scanning Books* warns that *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Our four-part shape is a visible choice, not nature's own list.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "either align or manipulate". It splits the question into four slots and keeps the verdicts separate. An incentive contract can contact long-term value only if its anchor is an outcome no party can move by opinion, its residuals are read per metric and per horizon instead of being averaged into one alignment score, its arrangement (vesting, hurdles, comparison basis, discretion, clawback) carries the information that an ingredient list drops, and its verifier has different evidence and no access to management's interior. Incentives reward easy-to-measure outcomes when the metric is produced by the same system it scores, the channel saturates, the peer group shares the same evidence pool, or the bond makes the measurable target the objective. The framework returns this shape, not a named metric: which metric works for a firm is a provenanced binding that only a ground measurement can settle. What it offers is the shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
