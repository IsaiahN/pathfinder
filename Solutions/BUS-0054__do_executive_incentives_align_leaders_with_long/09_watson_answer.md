## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms whether executive incentives track long-term company performance (Option A) or reward outcomes that are easy to measure and manipulate (Option B). What each leads to: A implies the metric is an instrument pointed at a ground; B implies the metric is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. Fig 9 :54 says to unbundle a union before searching: the "or" here is a union of four overlapping conditions, not a partition. Therefore the question is answered as four domain verdicts that are never averaged. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term in `01` §0 before the run, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Gap between what the contract rewards (a reading) and what the firm needs (realized long-term outcomes). Arity: many slots, one per metric, horizon and governance check. Symmetry: directed; `effort → metric → pay` is a chain, while `metric → value` is an instrument relation that can fail in one direction. Scale: three levels (executive action, board design, assurance regime) with lossy transforms between. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, executive action and the reporting cycle.** Actors: CEO, CFO, business units, reporting systems. Currency: effort, strategic choices, accounting and stock outcomes. Anchor: realized long-term firm outcomes, nameable and not moved by committee opinion. Union/partition: partition boundary, does the metric contact value? Dependency: first.
- **Domain 2, board contract design.** Actors: compensation committee, directors, pay consultants. Currency: contracts, targets, benchmarks, discretion, peer groups. Anchor: long-term shareholder interest, nameable as a standard; fails where the committee's standing depends on today's number. Union: horizon mismatch plus stake in the reading. Dependency: third, after perception and vocabulary.
- **Domain 3, measurement and metric production.** Actors: accounting standards, analysts, auditors, data vendors. Currency: reported numbers, adjusted figures, indices. Anchor: underlying economics, reachable only through instruments that can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Union: frame-internal reading plus channel saturation. Dependency: second, supplies what the objective can name.
- **Domain 4, assurance and governance.** Actors: external auditor, regulator, shareholders, proxy advisors, courts. Currency: opinions, votes, restatements, clawbacks, filings. Anchor: realized outcomes, and the ground never votes in the room. Verification: independence and non-access. Dependency: fifth, learn and carry.
- **Partition or union and order.** Union of four conditions; keep separate loops. Order by dependency per Fig 3: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → carry. Strain test applied: the attempted mapping of action and metric onto Fig 13's physical six-term substrate was retracted at census (domain mismatch); re-census landed on the domain-agnostic figures. No strain after re-census except the declared `single-source` status of the middle-link finding.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The binary framing is refused as a union error. Both effects are live, in different slots. Incentives contact long-term value when the contract has an unmovable outcome anchor, per-slot residuals, an arrangement whose bonds carry the information, and a verifier with different evidence and no access to management's interior. They reward easy-to-measure outcomes when the metric is produced by the same system it scores, the channel saturates, the verifier shares the maker's pool, or the bond makes the measurable target the objective. Domain 1 and 3 verdicts are `triangulated` on frame-internal and channel findings; Domain 2 is `triangulated` on anchor and arrangement with the middle-link break `single-source`; Domain 4 is `triangulated` on independence and non-access. No metric is named. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result; the world sources are secondary corroboration with the overlaps declared above.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished conclusion instead of a characterised residual (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four-part shape can be revised without withdrawing the derivation: a new discriminator can be imported with a source gate, the middle link can be instrumented, and a failed bond claim can be refused. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the record keeps the lineage either way.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement per firm that names the reading's source; a metric-only movement event (restatement or clawback) where value does not move; matched-pair evidence that bond differences do or do not change long-run outcomes; a verifier comparison showing independent evidence makes no difference; or any of the six pre-registered falsifiers in `01` §3. Prestige, a large comment campaign, or a pay-for-performance correlation is not such a reading.
