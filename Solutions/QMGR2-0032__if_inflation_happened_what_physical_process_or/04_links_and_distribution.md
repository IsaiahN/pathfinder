## Three things the wider literature bears directly on this question

1. **The name is explicitly unsettled.** Both Wikipedia entries and the Planck 2018 inflation paper use language of conjecture and reconstruction. Planck finds slow-roll concave potentials *"increasingly favoured"* but reports no detection of primordial tensors. That is a narrowing of the shape (C1 and C2), not a naming of D. It corroborates F3 and F5 at low overlap with the figures.
2. **Several drivers share the same shape.** The Wikipedia inflaton entry lists single-field slow-roll, Higgs inflation with non-minimal coupling, and the JCAP issue page carries Starobinsky and EFT reheating work. These are candidate bindings onto the same held atoms (Energy, Gradient, Time) with different arrangements, which is exactly the isomer/functional-group question in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. This is a corroboration of the union reading F6, not of any one model.
3. **Underdetermination is a standard result outside the framework.** The SEP entry gives both the holist (Duhem, Quine) and contrastive (Mill, van Fraassen) forms. Its finite-data/infinite-curves passage says no finite amount of data narrows to one function. That is the same limit Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README state in framework terms, reached from philosophy of science with a different evidence pool. It corroborates the gap verdict but does not decide it; the figures decide.

**Overlap discipline.** The Wikipedia, Planck, and SEP sources do not draw on the Tether figures, so they are genuine low-overlap witnesses for the *shape* of the conclusion. The two Substack essays are by the framework author and share the figure evidence pool; their agreement is expected and carries no independent weight. Two web pages quoting the same Planck abstract are one witness, not two.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.

So the stage-1 verdict must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
