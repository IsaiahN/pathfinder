**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken. ## QUESTION

If inflation happened, what physical process or field drove it? Restated as an effect: what must the driver be shaped like so that R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| closes across the flatness, horizon, relic, scalar-perturbation, and tensor slots? R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the CMB measurement, named as a kind of anchor in Fig 2 :29. Option A: assert that the driver was the inflaton field. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and record the name as a provenanced binding with the specific field left as a `gap`. What each leads to: A presents a `general`-mode verdict with no figure line selecting the name, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it. The verdict recorded here is B.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not follow. If inflation happened, its driver must be a spendable, directed energy difference over time that persists like a part surviving its own use and then ends by conversion into radiation. The driver is not bare energy, because energy is directionless alone and only energy bonded to a gradient composes a flow. "Inflaton", slow-roll potentials and Starobinsky R-squared gravity are bindings from the physics frame onto held atoms, provenanced as such, and do not add a primitive. The source and persistence conditions form a union whose verdicts stay separate, and which field actually did it is a declared `gap` pending a source-discriminating instrument. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The question of whether inflation occurred at all is not decided here.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The five pre-registered refuters are written before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added. The shape, the binding record, and the refuters are in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A primordial B-mode or non-Gaussianity measurement that discriminates a unique potential would name the slot; a demonstration that accelerated expansion needs no directed spendable difference would break the shape; a figure derivation that uniquely names a field would upgrade the binding to a mint; one lever that closes both union conditions with no categorically different residue would dissolve the union; or the judgement that the shape is vacuous would retire the whole packet. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
