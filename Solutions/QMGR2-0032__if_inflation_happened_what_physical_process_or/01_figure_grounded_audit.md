## 0. The residual in effect terms

The loop defines R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| per slot s (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`). The Symbols table calls R *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it."* Here:

- **b**: the pre-inflationary belief, a radiation-dominated hot Big Bang under the Friedmann equations with initial conditions supplied from outside the model.
- **a**: the observation, measuring the CMB, large-scale structure, and any primordial tensor background.
- **Gamma(b,a)**: the prediction of that belief for those measurements.
- **o'**: what is returned. Near-flatness, isotropy, no magnetic monopoles, nearly scale-invariant adiabatic scalar perturbations (n_s = 0.9649 +/- 0.0042), and a tensor-to-scalar ratio bound r < 0.056 (Planck Collaboration 2018, arXiv:1807.06211).
- **s**: the slot, indexed by observable rather than pooled. Slots: flatness, horizon, relic abundance, scalar spectral shape, tensor amplitude.

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

The phenomenon to explain is the persistence and shape of the gap: standard Big Bang evolution returns o' only from initial conditions fine-tuned to roughly one part in 10^62 (flatness), and it supplies no mechanism for the observed perturbations. Call the unknown driver D.

State the residual as: *what must the early-universe driver be shaped like so that a bounded, directed expansion closes the flatness, horizon, relic, and perturbation slots without fine-tuning, and then ends?* This is an effect description. It names no field, no "inflaton", no "false vacuum", no "scalar potential". Those are candidate names and are withheld until the census.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (Formula, "Direction"), before the run: the answer would be a molecule with (i) a state carrying energy density, (ii) a structure with directedness that can be spent, (iii) a persistence condition that survives many uses, and (iv) an end condition that returns the universe to radiation domination, plus an instrument that discriminates a scalar-field driver from a curvature-squared or modified-gravity driver. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in section 5 can fire on it.

**Anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* The CMB measurement is a named kind of anchor. Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Model preference, theoretical elegance, and the number of papers citing a model move when pushed; they are instruments at best. Viable anchor: CMB temperature and polarization spectra, B-mode limits, and large-scale structure, held fixed against any frame's opinion.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 Arity, symmetry, scale of R

- **Arity.** R is a many-place relation: model Gamma, action a, observed o', indexed by slot s and ordered by cosmic time t. It carries an **ordered** bond (`→`, sequence: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*, Fig 12 :72), because inflation must precede reheating and structure formation. It carries a **comparison** bond (⋛) for threshold questions such as the number of e-folds and which model fits n_s and r (Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). It is not symmetric: driver and expansion are not interchangeable.
- **Symmetry.** Accelerated expansion has a time arrow; it is not reversible by swapping operands. The estimate of a spectral index is a threshold report against data, not an absolute quantity.
- **Scale.** Two scales are in view: the first-order composition inside the early universe (field or curvature plus geometry producing expansion), and the second-order comparison of candidate models once each is settled or not. The membrane between them is *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction"* (Fig 4 :21), and only methods cross up (Fig 4 :22). The transform is lossy, so the round-trip gap R_T must be measured (Fig 4 :30).

### 1.3 Partition or union

D1 and D2 overlap: the physical field is a binding onto framework atoms, so their overlap is a compound with its own loop. D3 and D4 overlap: the instrument is read by a community, so that overlap is a second compound. The whole question is a **union**, not a partition.

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** (Fig 9 :54)
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** (Fig 9 :55)
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** (Fig 9 :57)

The unbundled conditions are: (C1) a source of energy density that behaves as a directed, spendable difference over time, and (C2) a persistence and end condition, that the source survives many e-folds and then converts to radiation. A driver with C1 but not C2 spends itself at once and gives no prolonged smoothing. A driver with C2 but not C1 is a constant with no directedness and cannot end. These are different compounds and need different instruments (a tensor amplitude for C1, a spectral index and reheating constraint for C2).

> **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** (Fig 3 :20)
> **"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."** (Fig 3 :24)

Order for this question: 1 perception (do the data show a residual at all?) → 2 vocabulary (do we have a term for a directed, spendable energy difference inside closure(Gamma)?) → 3 objective (what counts as progress: enough e-folds plus the observed n_s and r?) → 4 planning (which instrument discriminates the candidates?) → 5 learn and carry. ## 3. Findings

Mode tag on each: `general` if structural, `specified` if a falsifiable prediction about the early universe, `grounded` only if settled by a measurement. None is `grounded`.

### F1: The driver must be a gradient bonded to energy, and a gradient over time (decisive shape)

**Mode: `general` for the law, `specified` for the early-universe application. Domains D1, D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 13 and the Symbols table, low overlap: the minimum-set derivation versus the notation definition).**

> **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (Fig 13 :14)
> **"a difference that can be spent"** (Fig 13 :22)
> **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."** (Fig 13 :76)
> **"A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."** (Symbols table :97)

Energy alone is directionless. Accelerated expansion is a flow with a time direction. Therefore the driver, in framework terms, is a state (energy density) bonded to a structure (a gradient over time) that can be spent. That is a **shape**, not a name: a directed, spendable energy difference. It does not say "scalar field", "false vacuum", or "R-squared gravity". Those are bindings from D2 onto the held atoms.

The physics frame is consistent with the shape without confirming the name. Wikipedia (opened this session) reports that Guth found *"a positive-energy false vacuum would, according to general relativity, generate an exponential expansion of space."* A positive-energy vacuum is the state; the rolling or tunnelling is the directedness; the expansion is the flow. Planck 2018 X reports that *"slow-roll models with a concave potential are increasingly favoured by the data"*, which is a constraint on the shape of the spendable difference, not a naming of a field.

**Refuter:** a model in which accelerated expansion is driven by energy with no directed, spendable difference at all, or by a gradient over space with no time-directedness, that still closes the flatness, horizon, and perturbation slots, would break F1.

### F2: The driver must persist, behaving like a part that survives its own use

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1, D2. Status: single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), corroborated by the physics frame at low overlap (Wikipedia's statement about roughly constant inflaton energy density).**

> **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."** (Fig 12 :119)
> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** (Fig 12 :121)

Slow-roll keeps the potential energy of the driver roughly constant, so the driver acts like a catalyst: it is not spent in one use. Wikipedia records that *"During inflation, the energy density in the inflaton field is roughly constant."* That is the catalyst shape in the physics vocabulary. Reheating is the point where the spent part becomes a different term: *"the energy content of the field driving inflation condensed into hot, dense particles, a process called reheating."* The cycle figure warns that a chain cannot close by construction; reheating is not a re-entry of the same driver but a conversion, so the loop is broken by a ground event, not continued indefinitely.

**Refuter:** a driver whose energy density falls as fast as radiation from the outset, yet still yields at least sixty e-folds and the observed scalar spectral index, would break the persistence shape in F2.

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** (Fig 9 :32)

> **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** (Fig 13 :135)
> **"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"** (canonical figures README, local copy)

"Inflaton" is a name supplied by the physics frame. The figures say what a missing thing would look like (a directed, spendable, persistent energy difference), not which thing it is. The proper correction, recorded in `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5b of the exemplar and repeated here in section 7, is that the shape-not-a-name rule governs a **missing primitive**. Energy, Gradient, and Time are already held atoms, so naming a *binding* onto them is not forbidden; it must simply be provenanced as a binding and not presented as a derivation.

**Refuter:** a figure line that uniquely selects one of Energy, Gradient, Spectrum, or Network for the driver slot, or a composition from the six alone that yields a specific scalar potential, would break F3 and upgrade the answer from shape to name.

### F4: Naming the field is a binding or import with a source gate, not a mint

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 8, distinct roles: what moves a wall versus how a surplus is recognised).**

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** (Fig 6 :23)
> **"An import adds no atom to the world."** (Fig 6 :25)
> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."** (Fig 8 :51-52)

Mapping "inflaton" onto Energy plus Gradient plus Time is a binding from the physics frame. It adds no seventh atom (Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*). What crosses the boundary is the record of which physics term is attached to which held atom, with a source gate and a shadow test. Without that record, a convergent derivation and an adopted import are indistinguishable in the contents (Fig 8 :51-52). The Wikipedia inflation article supplies exactly the provenance a binding needs: it attributes the first proposal to Guth in 1979, with the 1981 Physical Review D paper, and lists the later Linde, Albrecht, Steinhardt, and Starobinsky variants.

**Refuter:** a derivation from the six-term set alone that uniquely produces V(phi) for a scalar field, with no physics-frame term imported, would upgrade this binding to a mint and break F4.

### F5: The ground is the CMB, and the specific name is a declared gap

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D3, D4. Status: triangulated (Fig 2, Fig 8, Fig 10, low overlap across anchor, absence, and channel roles).**

Planck 2018 X is the anchor in action: it reports n_s = 0.9649 +/- 0.0042, r < 0.056 when combined with BICEP2/Keck BK15, *"slow-roll models with a concave potential are increasingly favoured by the data"*, and *"two different methods for reconstructing the inflaton potential find no evidence for dynamics beyond slow roll."* That narrows the shape. It does not name the field. The Wikipedia inflaton entry states the gap plainly: *"The nature of the inflaton field is currently not known."* So the correct status for "which field" is `gap`, with the missing thing characterised in effect terms: a source-discriminating instrument sensitive to tensor modes or primordial non-Gaussianity.

**Refuter:** a measurement of primordial B-modes or non-Gaussianity that discriminates a unique potential, or its absence at a sensitivity that excludes the whole slow-roll class, would close the gap and either name or refuse the field at the ground.

### F6: The question is a union compound, so verdicts stay per slot and per domain

**Mode: `general`. Domain D4. Status: triangulated (Fig 9 and Fig 1, union rule versus slot rule).**

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** (Fig 9 :55)

"Which field drove inflation?" bundles C1 (a directed, spendable energy difference) with C2 (persistence and end). A single lever that establishes a field does not automatically establish that the field persisted for sixty e-folds and reheated correctly. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A model's popularity is a filter for which paper to read first, not a witness that the model is right.

**Refuter:** an instrument or argument that closes both C1 and C2 with one measurement and leaves no categorically different residue would make this a partition rather than a union and break F6.

**Refuter:** the delivery of a ground reading that names the field, or a proof that the shape is vacuous (fits every candidate equally and predicts nothing), would end the honest stop. Until then the stop stands.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

1. A measurement shows accelerated expansion with no directed, spendable energy difference (breaks F1).

If any fires, the claim is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
