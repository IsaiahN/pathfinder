## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether to accept or reject the word "inflaton". It is what an answer must be shaped like to close the residual R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| in the early-universe slot.

- **b** is the belief about early-universe dynamics, namely a radiation-dominated hot Big Bang evolving under the Friedmann equations from specified initial conditions.
- **a** is the act of observing, that is measuring the cosmic microwave background (CMB), large-scale structure, and any primordial tensor signal.
- **Gamma(b,a)** is what that belief predicts for those measurements.
- **o'** is what is actually returned: near-flatness, isotropy, no observed magnetic monopoles, and nearly scale-invariant adiabatic scalar perturbations with n_s about 0.965 and an upper bound on the tensor-to-scalar ratio r below 0.056 (Planck Collaboration 2018, arXiv:1807.06211).
- **s** is the slot, indexed by observable (flatness, horizon, perturbations, tensors), never aggregated into a single yes-or-no "did inflation happen" score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

The residual is this: the standard model matches o' only for extremely fine-tuned initial conditions, so something must have driven a brief epoch of accelerated expansion. Call the unknown driver D.

- Option A, "D is a new scalar field named the inflaton": leads to a claim whose name comes from the physics frame, with no figure line selecting that name.
- Option B, "D is a shape: a spendable, directed energy difference that persists and then ends": leads to a checkable structural claim with a stated refuter and an open slot.

**What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula, "Direction"): (i) a state carrying energy density, (ii) bonded to a structure with directedness that can be spent over time, (iii) persistent long enough to smooth and flatten, and (iv) an instrument that discriminates among candidate drivers. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, with the specific name "inflaton" recorded as a provenanced binding from the physics frame, not as a figure derivation. Which field actually did it is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles which field drove inflation, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of the early universe.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
