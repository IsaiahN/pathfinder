# Expert verification packet - the Tether on the driver of inflation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or particle physicist can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement at the inflationary scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

If inflation happened, its driver cannot be bare energy: energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14), and *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow"* (Fig 13 :76). The driver is therefore a spendable, directed energy difference over time, a gradient being *"a difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22) whose defining feature is directedness. It must persist across many e-folds like *"a part that survives its own use"* (Fig 12 :119), then end by conversion into radiation, a sequence rather than a cycle. The framework returns that shape, not the name of the field (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). "Inflaton", concave slow-roll potentials, and Starobinsky R-squared gravity are bindings from the physics frame onto held atoms; *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* (Fig 8 :51-52). Which field actually drove inflation is a declared gap pending a source-discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** energy alone yields no directed flow; a gradient over time must be bonded to it, and the driver must persist then end.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14 *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*; Fig 13 :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*; Fig 13 :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **Refuter:** show a directed expansion driven by energy with no spendable difference, or by a space gradient with no time-directedness, that still closes the flatness, horizon and perturbation slots; or show a driver whose energy density falls as fast as radiation that still yields sixty e-folds and the observed n_s.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the data and the models

- **Statement:** the current ground narrows the shape but does not name the field. Planck 2018 X reports n_s = 0.9649 +/- 0.0042 and r < 0.056 with BK15, finds slow-roll concave potentials *"increasingly favoured by the data"*, and reports that *"two different methods for reconstructing the inflaton potential find no evidence for dynamics beyond slow roll."* The Wikipedia inflaton entry states *"The nature of the inflaton field is currently not known."*
- **What to check:** do those statements support "narrowed shape, open name", or does some result already discriminate a unique field? Are the candidate bindings (slow-roll, Higgs inflation, Starobinsky) genuinely different arrangements of the same atom set, or do some require an atom the others lack (which would break the isomer claim in `06_isomers_and_order.md`)?
- **Refuter:** a measurement that uniquely selects one potential or one driver class; or a candidate that requires an atom absent from the others (kills the isomer reading, leaves the functional group); or the judgement that the shape is vacuous, fitting every candidate and predicting nothing.

### C3 - framework-internal; the import and union claims

- **Statement:** the name is a provenanced binding, not a mint and not an import that adds an atom; and the source and persistence conditions form a union, so their verdicts stay separate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."*; Fig 8 :51-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*; Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Refuter:** a derivation from the six-term set alone that uniquely yields a scalar potential (would upgrade binding to mint); or one lever that closes both the source and persistence conditions with no categorically different residue (would make it a partition, not a union).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The scope is the shape of an inflating driver, conditional on inflation having happened.
- Not a derivation of the inflaton, of slow-roll, of R-squared gravity, or of any specific potential. Those are physics-frame bindings, recorded as such.
- Not a claim that inflation occurred. The premise is conditional; the frame does not decide it.
- Not a claim that the CMB reading determines the source field. One projection can have several sources.
- Not a derivation of one candidate model from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and the colour case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

1. **Source Test:** does a primordial B-mode or non-Gaussianity measurement discriminate a unique potential or class of drivers?
2. **Persistence Test:** do n_s, the tensor bound, and a reheating constraint close the persistence-and-end condition?
3. **Vacuity Test:** does the shape actually rule anything out? If it fits every candidate equally, refuter 5 fires and the packet is withdrawn.
