# The Tether's answer: demand, supply, or interacting shocks?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal single main cause. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a separator, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which one of three candidate mechanisms is "mainly" responsible for recessions. The residual is the fall in activity that the frame read from one aggregate series and cannot assign to a mechanism without a discriminator. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The question is posed at the level where that signal has been averaged away.

## 2. The answer

1. **The trichotomy is not a partition; it is a union of overlapping conditions.** Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." The question must be split per episode and per slot. Interaction is the intersection plus a contact cascade (Fig 11 :52-53), not a third cause.

2. **There is no standing main cause.** Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A shock's status as demand or supply is relative to the slot and the residual (Fig 11 :49). The oil price rise that is a supply disruption for an importer is a demand boost for an exporter. What the figures return is a relation, not a rank.

3. **Demand-recession and supply-recession are isomers over a fixed atom set.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Same atoms, different typed arrangement; invisible in GDP, decisive in which instrument pays. The fixed-atom condition carries a live falsifier (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1).

4. **The universal percentage is a budget filter, never a verdict.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A decomposition telling us that demand contributes somewhere between 40 and over 95 per cent at a four-quarter horizon is information about where to spend identification effort, not an answer to "which is the main cause".

5. **What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The broken link is between having terms for demand and supply and being able to say what would count as progress on attributing the source. The instrument would have to be imported from a low-overlap frame with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23), and its independence debited against the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).

6. **A single main cause is a `gap`, and that is a verdict.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :136: "What it can do is narrow. It can say the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again." The figures return the shape of the discriminator and the split of the question. They do not name the cause a future instrument will find, because that primitive is not held.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that demand never matters, or that supply never matters. Both rules work on different subsets; the claim is about the *form* of the answer, not a denial of mechanisms.
- Not that "interacting shocks" is a third kind of cause. It is a union intersection plus a cycle (Fig 9 :55, Fig 12 :121).
- Not a universal percentage, not a ranking of recessions, and not a best single explanation.
- Not that the discriminator is impossible. It is unreachable at this budget with the held instruments (Fig 8 :39). Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget are different facts with different instructions.
- Not grounded. No natural experiment, dataset or market was run in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The discriminator would have to show, in one episode, that the output fall persists when the demand bond is held fixed and the capacity bond varies, or vice versa. Standing refuters, written before the conclusion:

1. One mechanism accounts for all episodes with a non-updating anchor that discriminates it.
2. The three conditions form a partition, with no episode satisfying two.
3. A demand-recession requires an atom a supply-recession never uses (kills the isomer claim).
4. A source-discriminating instrument returns one named cause across episodes and sectors.
5. A per-slot reading is unnecessary because one aggregate series already carries the source information.

## 6. The honest stop

The economics atoms are a level-relative binding onto the held substrate (Fig 12 :104), and declaring them makes a choice visible, not neutral. Another frame's minimum set may carve the economy differently. The result stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A future discriminator can move it; agreement among readers cannot.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single main cause of recessions, and it does not average the candidates into a percentage. It says the either/or is a union of overlapping conditions, so the question must be split per episode and per sector: demand rules fit some episodes, supply rules fit others, and "interaction" names the intersection plus a contact cascade, not a third cause. Over a fixed set of atoms a demand-recession and a supply-recession are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, invisible in GDP and decisive in which policy pays. A variance share is a filter for how much identification effort to buy, never a witness for a cause. What is owed is a discriminator with a non-updating anchor and low-overlap frames; the figures can describe its shape but cannot name what it will find. That is a reported gap, with refuters written in advance, not a silence and not a claim to have settled the matter.
