# Solution ECN-0001

**Question:** Are recessions mainly caused by declines in aggregate demand, disruptions to supply, or interacting shocks?

**Source:** economics questions.txt line 5

**Section:** 1. Macroeconomic fluctuations and recessions

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain verdicts and the union shape, `general` on the structural claim that the trichotomy is not a partition, `gap` on a single universal main cause. Not `grounded`: no measurement is taken here.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## Effect reading of the question

Decide which object the phrase "mainly caused by" picks out. Option A (aggregate demand) predicts that a demand-management instrument closes the residual. Option B (supply disruption) predicts that a supply-side or relative-price instrument closes it. Option C (interacting shocks) predicts that neither single lever clears the overlap and that a compound with its own loop is owed. The ordinary term bound for the loop is "main cause": a single residual-owning mechanism with a nameable, non-updating anchor such that, if it were removed, the recession would not have occurred. The derivation tests whether the figures license any such term. They do not license one; they license the split and the separator.

## Boundary and status of the work

Everything in this folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. No experiment, dataset, or market was run. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*) it cannot settle the question it reports on. The figures are primary and decide; world sources are secondary corroboration only. Nothing was modified outside this folder.

Sources used: the canonical figure text at `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, the eight essays as mapped in `05_what_shape_means.md`, and the public world sources listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`, each opened on 2026-11-22.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of economists by how likely they are to agree with the verdict. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163).
- No bulk-send plan and no request for trust or agreement before a check.
- No universal percentage of recessions attributed to demand versus supply. The figures return a shape and a separator, not that number.
