## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (VERIFIED): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0001 the shape is: a per-slot, union-structured attribution with a non-updating discriminator, where the discriminator's name is not supplied. The single main cause is the filler the question wants and the figures refuse. A shape here narrows the search to mechanisms that (a) can be indexed per sector, (b) occupy one or more overlapping conditions, and (c) would be settled by an instrument that does not update on the economy's own reports.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (VERIFIED): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- The same essay (VERIFIED) on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the exact diagnostic applied in `01` F1 and F2.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is a many-to-one reading of falling output; the cause is not in the reading.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (CARRIED): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Translated: describe what a discriminator would have to show, not what cause it will name.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (VERIFIED): "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means an instrument or natural experiment that returns the same cause across episodes with a non-updating anchor. The variance-share decompositions are gates on the budget, not settlements.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The inverse-problem and attribution-bias sources are such differing frames, but they corroborate the *shape* of the gap; neither names a macro cause.

*They're Scanning Books* (CARRIED): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A source-discriminating instrument would add a dimension (a new reading that can separate demand from supply); a new variance share would only add a term in the existing space.

## 4. Why "the main cause" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (CARRIED) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source; an instrument with more resolving power resolved it.

The aggregate GDP reading is exactly this projection. Falling output plus rising prices is consistent with an adverse supply shock; falling output plus falling prices with an adverse demand shock; and a sufficiently nonlinear network can produce either sign from the same primitive (see the network source in `04`). Naming one cause from the projection is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

The inverse-problem source opened this session (VERIFIED, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problem) is the mathematical version: "calculating from a set of observations the causal factors that produced them", with "the solution of the system p=F−1dobs is not unique" when the forward map is rank deficient. It corroborates the shape; it does not decide the macro verdict.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming demand or supply? ANSWERED, with a correction

**No.** The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Demand decline, supply disruption, capacity, credit and spending are bindings onto atoms already present in the economics frame (and, via the level-relative binding in `06`, onto held substrate atoms). Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh cause.

**Correction to my own first pass, kept visible.** I initially wrote that the figures forbid naming any cause at all, so even "demand" and "supply" had to stay as blanks. That overstates the rule and is narrowed here: what the figures forbid is (a) inventing a new primitive to hold the answer, and (b) claiming that a binding from the economics frame moves the wall (Fig 6 :25, "An import adds no atom to the world"). Both claims are withdrawn; their withdrawal is recorded in `01` §7.

What remains forbidden and what remains owed: it is still a metamerism error to name *the* cause from the aggregate reading without a discriminator (§4). The "shape, not a name" limit survives on the *discriminator's* output, not on the vocabulary used to describe the conditions.

## 6. Why the group-level story matters here

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (CARRIED) and *How Intelligence Actually Works* (CARRIED): rooms differ in closure; variation inside a room is not selection between rooms, and selection is where the ground votes (Fig 7 :35). Macroeconomic schools that read the same aggregate series are one long room. Booking another room that reads the same series is motion, not progress. The lower-overlap rooms in this solution (inverse problems, social cognition) are different closures, which is why they are worth importing despite being non-economics.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (VERIFIED): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous economists who agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it. The same essay (VERIFIED): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (VERIFIED): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Every substantive file in this folder is labelled accordingly. The derivation cannot settle the question it reports on, and it does not claim to.
