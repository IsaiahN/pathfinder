## 5. The two-stage offer, with the third stage recorded

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape C, and the five refuters. Ask: what would count as a failure in your system, and what instrument would show it coming? Record the verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the three primary links (figure text, canonical usage rule, formula). Say plainly that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator; `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" forbids assuming a frame can check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only mean anything if the stage-1 output survives. The numeric part does not (F7 is `gap`), so no stage-1 message may contain a threshold, an exponent or a universality class. If a reader asks for one, the honest answer is that the figures do not carry it.
