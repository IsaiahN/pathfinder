## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement is in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session:

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for CMP-0014 a **shape** is: the narrowed form of an answer about *when* a small rule change becomes a discontinuous collective response, statable before any critical exponent, model class or specific mechanism is named.

- **Shape:** a small change is abrupt when it lands on a load-bearing bond inside a re-entrant amplifying loop, its per-slot residual compounds through the chain, and a functional group fixes which state wins; the anchor is a measured discontinuity, not agreement.
- **Names withheld:** "the Ising universality class", "β = 0.325", "self-organized criticality in the Manna sandpile", "the E/I ratio in cortex". Each is a binding from a world frame or a specific system, not an output of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

*The Library of Babel* gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "phase transition", "tipping point", "critical slowing down", "avalanche", "cascade", "bifurcation", "self-organized criticality". None was used as the answer. Each was ranged against the residual (Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second"*), and the figure census was run before the world search. The descriptive work came first: a binary bond change, a k-ary collective composition, a slot-indexed residual, three scales. Only then were figures selected.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the warrant for the union reading in `01` section 1.3: physical critical phenomena, network cascades, neural dynamics and engineered systems are partitioned by anchor, but the cascade mechanism is a union that needs unbundling (Fig 9 :54-57).

The stopping rule:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Here the stop is reached when the shape is stated, an instrument is named for each clause, and five refuters are written. Further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 4. The source error that would be easy to make here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

The reading "the collective state jumped" is consistent with several sources: a bond swap, a sign flip in a re-entrant loop, a functional group crossing a threshold, or a reporting artifact from averaging across slots. The reading does not determine the source. Naming "criticality" from the jump alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is the instrument that discriminates among those sources: the slot-indexed residual, the loop sign, and the baseline-referenced spatial statistic (Bandara et al. 2026, fetched). The essay's closing principle applies: when several paths give the same behaviour, a better discriminator, not a better argument, is what settles it.

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For CMP-0014 the canonical shape is: **a location-and-loop criterion with a slot-indexed instrument and an open numeric slot.** It is not a critical exponent, not a threshold, not a universality class, and not a list of famous tipping points. The names that appear in the world literature are bindings with provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer exist in the world (susceptibility, avalanche exponents, baseline-referenced spatial variance, stability margins) but were not operated here, so the answer stops at `specified` with an explicit `gap`, and the stop is recorded.
