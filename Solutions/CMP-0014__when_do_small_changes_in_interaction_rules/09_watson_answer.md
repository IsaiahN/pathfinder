## VERDICT (from the figures)

A small rule change is abrupt when it lands on a load-bearing bond inside a re-entrant amplifying loop, its per-slot residual compounds through the chain, a functional group fixes which state wins, and a persisting part holds the new state; a pure feed-forward chain should respond smoothly to the same change. The jump must be measured per slot against a fixed anchor, and the micro-to-macro claim carries a round trip gap R_T that must be enumerated. The figures give no threshold, exponent or universality class, so that part is `gap` and is not filled with a borrowed name. The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The shape is differentiable across four domains with nameable anchors, but no instrument was operated here, so nothing is settled.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a theory before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The numeric part is withheld rather than asserted, so no threshold claim needs retraction. The shape can be narrowed domain by domain if a refuter fires, and `01` section 3 and the retraction log preserve the two earlier overstatements (chaos equated with criticality; "not an analogy" read as a cross-domain ban) so they can be corrected without erasing the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled loop-versus-chain comparison holding atoms and δ magnitude fixed, measured per slot, would pay for or fire the cycle clause; a sign flip that leaves the response smooth would fire it; a magnitude-only explanation would fire the location rule; a flat slot residual with a jumping mean would fire the instrument clause. A verbatim figure line predicting a numeric threshold would close F7 in the opposite direction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
