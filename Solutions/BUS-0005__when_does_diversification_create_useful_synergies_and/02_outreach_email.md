# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about when diversification helps before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about corporate diversification that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** whether diversification creates a surplus or makes a firm harder to manage is decided by two conditions that have to hold at once, not by whether the businesses are "related" on a list.
>
> 1. The added unit must differ from the existing portfolio in the evidence it actually uses, so that the union reaches a return neither reaches alone, and that difference must be aimed by a stated residual and paid for by a real link (shared activity, transferred skill, a priced bond), not by a synergy narrative.
> 2. The organization must be able to carry the composition: methods must travel from units to the center rather than recordings of one success, the round trip between the center's abstract view and the unit's actual situation must be measured, and the readings of demand, price and share must not be stale, saturated or pooled into an average.
>
> These are two different failure modes with two different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. A firm whose units are "two names for one frame" needs a different fix from a firm with a real surplus that its center cannot handle. The further claim is that the capability which made the first business win can change sign when the new unit changes what the market rewards, so the strength and the rigidity can be the same thing.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which businesses or which industries will produce this, that any particular diversified firm is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of diversification. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. two units with the same evidence pool and the same terms still produce a sustained, market-verified surplus after a control for selection;
> 2. a diversification with no stated residual and no priced link pays prospectively, per unit, after a control for selection;
> 3. the number of units doubles while communication load per unit and metric pooling do not increase, and performance stays high;
> 4. one prospective metric predicts both the cost of describing the move and the cost of verifying it, and the market confirms it;
> 5. a capability's measured value stays the same across a documented change in what the market rewards caused by diversification;
> 6. one intervention restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Two failure modes kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended synergy score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Residual before target | *"Residual first, frame second."* (Fig 8 :53) and *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :28) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning businesses | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
