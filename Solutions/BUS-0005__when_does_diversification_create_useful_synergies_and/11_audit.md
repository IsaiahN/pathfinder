A two-place reading (portfolio, valuation) would lose the action and the prediction, so it is rejected. The time index crosses the membrane between the unit's step loop and the portfolio's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the membrane is part of the object, not an implementation detail.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *diversification works when the parts stay different and the center keeps in touch.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific decision. Result: *buy different businesses and keep talking to the unit managers.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into two separate slogans and loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a firm failing C2 (unmanageable surplus) merely to find more different businesses, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Keep in touch" does not specify that the market, not internal fit claims, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a center to measure its own success with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the aim-and-price condition. "Different" does not carry the residual described first (Fig 8 :21) or the priced bond (Fig 12 :99). Coming back down, it licenses exactly the *"imagined synergy"* Porter warns about.
4. It loses the lossy-transform measurement. "Keep talking" has no place for R_T (Fig 4 :30), saturated metrics or pooled readings (Fig 10 :26).
5. It loses the valence flip. "Different businesses" has no place for the possibility that the old good capability changed sign (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
