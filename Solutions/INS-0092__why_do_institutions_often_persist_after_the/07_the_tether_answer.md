# The Tether's answer on institutional persistence

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An institution (a frame with Γ, rules, routines, a seat and an anchor) was settled against conditions C0. Conditions move to C1. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| grows per slot, yet Γ and the seat stay fixed. The question is what keeps them fixed while the residual persists and does not trend down. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## 3. The shape

An institution persists after its conditions changed when the room kept its form while the question moved, with no new atom entering its closure and a channel no longer able to report contact loss. The successor, when one is owed, is a room that differs from the last and matches the residual it surfaced, reached by a source-gated import. That is a shape. It does not name the successor.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that institutions are irrational or that a collective will sustains them. Fig 11 :36: *"Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."*
- Not that culture alone explains persistence. Culture is already inside Γ as inherited prior w (X2 in the census).
- Not that "path dependence" is a mechanism. It is a label for the closure's internal dynamics, absorbed into D1 and D3.
- Not that a single lever (leadership, restructuring, a new mission statement) is sufficient. The problem is a union.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles which condition holds in any real institution.

## 5. What would settle it, and the honest stop

A ground reading, not agreement. The checks are: (1) per-slot residual trend for the institution against the new conditions; (2) a fresh, non-saturated instrument that can report "you have lost touch"; (3) a provenance record for any atom imported from a differing closure; (4) a record of which institutional parts are spent versus regenerating (Fig 12 :119).

The stop is honest because the successor is not named and R_T is not measured. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* This derivation records the lineage instead.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the institution that should replace a persisting one, and it does not claim to have measured any real institution. It says that an institution is a room, and when the conditions it was settled against move, a room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure named in Fig 10. Persistence is a union of two conditions: no new atom enters the institution's closure, so internal reform cannot add what the new ground needs; and the channel to the ground decays, so the signal that should say "you have lost touch" reads as business as usual. Standing earned on the old conditions survives them, which is why the occupants are the least able to re-fit the room. What the framework offers is that shape, three refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The successor stays unnamed until the ground settles it.
