## QUESTION

Why do institutions often persist after the conditions that produced them have changed? Restated as an effect: an institution is a frame with a library Γ (rules, routines, categories, a seat, an anchor) settled against conditions C0. Conditions move to C1, so R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| grows per slot while Γ and the seat stay fixed. What keeps them fixed while R persists and does not trend down? Option A: explain persistence by an internal property of the institution (will, culture, inertia as a substance). Option B: explain it as a union of a closure condition and a channel condition, each with a refuter. A gives a name with no anchor. B gives a checkable shape and an open successor slot. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the conditions, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Institutions persist after their conditions changed when a room stayed fixed while the question moved, inside a union of two conditions: no new atom entered the institution's closure (so internal reform could not supply what the new ground requires), and the channel to the ground decayed (so the signal that should say "you have lost touch" was read as normal operation). Standing earned on the old conditions survives them, which makes the seat structurally unable to re-fit the room. The founding success is replayed as a recording; agreement compounds while evidence does not. This is `specified` and ungrounded. The specific successor institution is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Verdicts are kept separate per domain, never averaged. The world sources corroborate the mechanism shape after re-mapping Staw to the standing term; none overrules a figure line.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode labelled and states its refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording (a name, a prestige list) instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the refuters and the provenance are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot residual series trending down after the conditions moved with no import; an institution with fresh per-slot instruments that still fails to register the change (which would kill the channel condition); identical Γ and habitat with divergent persistence and no standing difference; a record marking each part spent or regenerating showing no conversion; or a re-census that admits a figure line naming the successor form. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
