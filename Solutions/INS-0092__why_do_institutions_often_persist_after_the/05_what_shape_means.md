## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is:

> An institution that persists after its ground moved is a room that stayed fixed while the question moved, inside a union of two conditions: no new atom entered its closure, and the channel to the ground decayed. The successor room must differ from the last and match the residual it surfaced.

"Restructure the bureaucracy" is a name. "Import an atom from a closure that differs and re-open a channel that can say 'you have lost touch'" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap by proximity. *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Then: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual was stated as R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot, not as "bureaucratic inertia."
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."* Fig 2 :29 names the institutional anchor: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the point at which both conditions are checkable and the successor is still unnamed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measurement that shows the institution's routines still pay against C1 would license a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

Until one of those, a named successor is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."*

## 4. Why the "will" reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the mechanism: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* This is exactly Fig 2 :33-34's coherent drift, applied to an institution whose own measures became its reference.

Fig 11 :36 removes the need for a mental entity: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* So "the institution willed its own persistence" is a label applied afterwards. The checkable claim is about contact and closure, not about will.

## 5. The correction to keep visible

My first reading treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade all naming. That is too broad. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the atoms (a frame, a library, a ground, a channel, a seat) are already held; what is missing is the specific successor arrangement and a source-discriminating instrument. So naming the two conditions is legitimate; naming the successor institution is not. Both readings are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of distinguished institutional theorists who agree with the shape is a prestige loop, not a ground. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the institutional claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
