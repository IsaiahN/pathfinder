**Subject:** One checkable output on inflation: shape, field name, and what the energy scale does not tell us

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, stated in effect terms (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The hot big-bang model fits the CMB and light elements only if it is handed initial conditions it cannot explain. I am tracking five separate slots, not one average: horizon (distant patches at the same temperature to about 1 part in 100,000), flatness (Ω within about 0.4 percent of critical), the absence of relic monopoles, the nearly scale-invariant perturbation spectrum with n_s about 0.965, and reheating. These are the slots the driver has to reduce.
>
> **The shape claim.** A mechanism term is owed for those slots, and under a six-term substrate a structure must sit between space and time, so expansion cannot be a bare space-time bond. The licensed shape is a flat-potential, nearly constant energy density whose spendable gradient drives a flow; the hot plasma is produced at reheating, and the episode re-enters as input to standard cosmology. I am explicitly **not** claiming the framework derives which field this is. The word "inflaton" is a placeholder binding from particle physics, not a figure result, and the detailed particle physics mechanism is still unknown in the literature.
>
> **On testability.** The characteristic energy scale of order 10^16 GeV is a fact about the budget of a direct accelerator test, not a verdict about testability. I am treating it as a filter. The same physics is already registered by a different frame: COBE saw the anisotropy, Planck measured n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042, and BICEP/Keck constrains r_0.05 < 0.036. So the indirect test is live now, direct production is unreachable at this budget, and a null tensor signal would eliminate large-field models rather than prove absence.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. primordial perturbations that are not a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic ordering (for example a large non-Gaussianity or isocurvature inconsistent with single-field slow roll);
> 2. early expansion shown to work with no flat-potential, nearly constant energy-density mediator;
> 3. a source-discriminating instrument identifying a field that is not of that shape, or proving no such degree of freedom existed;
> 4. proof that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. If you answer, I would like your verdict on stage 1 recorded before I show you anything else.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - TASI Lectures on Inflation (Baumann, 2009/2012): https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.5424
> - Planck 2018 results X, Constraints on inflation: https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06211
> - BICEP/Keck XIII, improved tensor constraints (2021): https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00483
