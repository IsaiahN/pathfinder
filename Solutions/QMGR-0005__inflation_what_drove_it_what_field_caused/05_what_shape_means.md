## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, **"a shape, not a name"**. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. It can say that something is owed and what it would have to look like; it cannot say which primitive it is, because naming would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Also: "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `driver = SPACE ( ??? ) TIME` with the slot constrained to a spendable gradient of a nearly constant energy density, is a shape.
- `driver = SPACE ( INFLATON FIELD ) TIME` is a name.

The canonical wording is about a **missing** thing. Here what is missing from the discriminating instrument is the microphysical source, so the rule applies directly to the field identity (see section 5, correction).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 and F6).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

For inflation the bootstrap is visible in the record: horizon and flatness (borrowed terms) lead to causal contact and a smooth patch, then to a nearly constant energy density, then to a flat potential, then to a spectrum of perturbations. Each step narrows the allowed source without naming it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." A source-discriminating instrument at the inflationary scale would settle the name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test. A result from particle physics that predicts a specific field and survives the CMB ground would be such an import, with provenance recorded.

The inverse-problem reference (mathematics, low overlap) gives the structural reason a name is not forced: a rank-deficient forward map yields a non-unique solution. Several source descriptions project onto the same measured spectrum. Without an instrument that discriminates among sources, choosing one is the searcher's contribution.

## 4. The metamerism point, applied

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": it could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Here the instrument (CMB temperature and polarization spectra) reads a nearly scale-invariant spectrum with n_s about 0.965 and a bound on r. It cannot comment on the source. Chaotic, plateau, hybrid and f(R) realizations can project onto the same reading. The answer is unreached until a source-discriminating instrument exists. Naming the inflaton from the reading is the metamerism error.

## 5. Q: does the "shape, not a name" rule forbid naming the field? ANSWERED with a correction

**No, not because of the reason I first gave.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Scalar fields, potential energy and gradients are **already terms** in the physics library Γ. So naming one is a **binding from the physics frame onto held terms**, not the invention of a missing primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

What remains correct is the metamerism point: the reading does not determine the source, and an instrument that cannot distinguish sources is inadequate to answer the name question. The **reason** to withhold the name is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held terms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

This mirrors the exemplar's correction in its `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5b, reached here independently from the same canonical wording.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous cosmologists who agree is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground to settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the inflation claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape, stated once

A candidate answer must have this shape: a mechanism term that reduces the horizon, flatness, monopole and perturbation slots at once; a structure in the slot between space and time, constrained as a spendable gradient of a nearly constant energy density; a production at reheating whose output re-enters as input to standard cosmology; a field identity left open until a discriminating instrument fires; and a test that ranges this shape against the CMB while treating the energy scale as a budget filter rather than a verdict. Anything that claims more than this is reporting a name where the framework returns a shape.
