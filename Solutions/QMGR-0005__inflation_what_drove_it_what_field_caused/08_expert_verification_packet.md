# Expert verification packet: the Tether on inflation

**What this is:** a claim in a form an inflation cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation, refuters and provenance are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the inflationary scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status note:** it cannot settle the cosmology question it reports on. If any sub-claim below fails, the packet is to be retracted rather than defended.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The three sub-questions in the prompt are a union and must be unbundled. The hot big-bang model needs initial conditions it cannot derive, so a mechanism term is owed against five named slots (horizon, flatness, monopoles, the perturbation spectrum, reheating). The figures license a **shape** for the driver: space and time cannot bond directly, so a structure sits between them, and the licensed shape is a nearly constant energy density whose spendable gradient produces a flow, with reheating as a production whose output re-enters standard cosmology. They do **not** license a field name; "inflaton" is a binding from particle physics onto terms already held, and the detailed particle physics mechanism is still unknown in the literature. On testability, the characteristic scale of order 10^16 GeV is a price in actions, a budget filter for direct production, not a verdict. The same physics is already registered by the CMB, a frame whose closure differs, so indirect testing is live now, while a null tensor signal eliminates large-field models rather than proving absence. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Structure and order of the driver (framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12)

- **Statement:** expansion of space over time cannot be a bare state-state bond. A structure sits in the slot. The driver reads as energy bonded to a spendable gradient, and reheating is a production, not a sequence, with the episode forming a cycle at the next level.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75 "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Also Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent", Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before", and Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input."
- **What to check:** is there a typed structure between the two states in the figure's grammar, and is the hot bath genuinely a substance that did not exist before reheating? Check whether the bond is ⇒ and not → or +.
- **Refuter:** a measurement showing expansion driven by a direct space-time bond with no structural mediator; the hot bath shown to pre-exist reheating; or the perturbations shown not to be a spectrum-like, nearly scale-invariant ordering.

### C2. A shape is licensed, a name is not (framework-internal plus an independent mathematical check)

- **Statement:** the figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself, so the field identity stays open. Naming it is a binding onto held terms, not a mint and not an import that moves the wall. The reason for withholding is the missing source-discriminating instrument, not a blanket ban on naming held terms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself", Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.", and Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **Independent corroboration, secondary:** the inverse-problem reference (mathematics and engineering, low overlap with cosmology) states that a rank-deficient forward map gives a non-unique solution. The cosmic-inflation reference entry states that the detailed particle physics mechanism responsible for inflation is unknown. Both are recorded with URLs, accessed dates and quotes in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They predict that several microphysical fields can project onto one CMB spectrum; they corroborate the figure verdict and are not the ground for it.
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument at the inflationary scale that identifies a specific field not of the licensed shape, or that proves no such degree of freedom existed. A detection of a field *of* that shape would settle the name and leave the shape standing; that is a different outcome and is not a refutation.

### C3. Testability is two facts (framework-internal plus the observational ground)

- **Statement:** direct production at order 10^16 GeV is unreachable-at-budget, while the same physics is already registered by another frame, so the indirect test is live now. The energy scale is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. A null tensor signal is not proof of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.", Fig 6 :34-35 "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve.", Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone.", and Fig 8 :40-41 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts".
- **Ground numbers, secondary:** COBE registered the anisotropy; Planck reports n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042 and finds no evidence for dynamics beyond slow roll; BICEP/Keck reports r_0.05 < 0.036 at 95 percent confidence. Planck and BICEP/Keck share Planck maps in the tensor analysis, so they are one evidence pool for that bound, not two independent witnesses.
- **Refuter:** proof that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual, which would convert unreachable-at-budget into underivable and reverse the verdict; or a demonstration that the two frames draw on one evidence pool, which would remove the triangulation claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the figures derive a scalar field, and not that the framework names the inflaton. "Scalar field" and "inflaton" are bindings provenanced from the physics frame (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §5).
- Not that inflation is untestable because the energy scale is high, and not that it is directly producible at current accelerators.
- Not that a null tensor result ends the question, and not that the number of published models is evidence. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not a new theory of the early universe. The figures say what a missing thing would have to look like and stop at the limit of what the instruments distinguish.
- Not `grounded`. The derivation is internally consistent with the figures; that is not the same as being right about the universe.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. In order of cost:

1. A tensor detection or a tighter bound that discriminates the large-field plateau region (running now).
2. A significant primordial non-Gaussianity or isocurvature signal inconsistent with single-field slow roll (would break the shape).
3. A source-discriminating instrument at the inflationary scale (would settle the name).
4. A proof that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual (would reverse the testability verdict).

A written objection to C1, C2 or C3 is more useful than an endorsement. If one fires, the packet is retracted and the retraction stays visible in the folder.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read the five short figure passages in C1 to C3 against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Each is a verbatim extract, not a paraphrase.
2. Check C1 against their own knowledge: is reheating a production of the hot bath, and is the episode a cycle whose output is handed to standard cosmology?
3. Check C2 against the inverse-problem point: can several potentials project onto one measured spectrum, so that the name is not forced by the data?
4. Check C3 against the Planck and BICEP/Keck numbers, and state whether the null tensor bound rules out the shape or only part of the model family.
5. Name one instrument that would discriminate sources at the inflationary scale, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
