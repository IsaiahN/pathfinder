# The Tether's answer on inflation

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is three conditions that overlap, not one question. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The hot big-bang model reproduces the CMB and light elements only when it is handed initial conditions it cannot derive: causal contact across the horizon, flatness to better than a percent, the absence of relic monopoles, a nearly scale-invariant perturbation spectrum, and a mechanism for reheating. One term that reduces all five slots at once pays the bargain in Fig 5. Answering "what drove it" with a field name silently answers "what field caused it" by a binding, and "is it testable" then ranges that binding against a ground. Keep the three loops and their verdicts separate; never average them.

## 2. The shape

1. **A structure must sit between space and time.** Fig 13 :75 "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Expansion of space over time is therefore not a bare bond of two states. The slot is constrained, not filled.
2. **The driver reads as energy bonded to a spendable gradient, producing a flow.** Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent". The licensed shape is a nearly constant energy density with a flat enough potential that its gradient can be spent. The figures return a kind of thing, not the thing: Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
3. **Reheating is a production, and the episode is a cycle.** Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before". The hot plasma was not there before reheating. Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input." The reheated bath becomes the input to standard cosmology. The supporting derivation in `06_isomers_and_order.md` records the bond tests.
4. **The field identity is withheld.** Naming the degree of freedom "the inflaton" is a binding from the physics frame onto terms already held in Γ. It is not a figure derivation and not an import that adds an atom: Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." What licenses withholding is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held terms (the correction in `05_what_shape_means.md` §5 is preserved). Chaotic, plateau, hybrid and f(R) realizations project onto the same measured spectrum; the instrument cannot comment on the source. The literature itself says the detailed particle physics mechanism is unknown.
5. **Testability is two facts, not one.** The characteristic scale of order 10^16 GeV is a statement about the budget of a direct accelerator test. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Direct production is unreachable-at-budget. But an edge already exists: Fig 6 :34-35 "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." COBE registered the anisotropy, Planck measured n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042, and BICEP/Keck constrains r_0.05 < 0.036. The same physics is held in a second frame whose closure differs. Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." So the indirect test is live now, with low overlap between particle theory and microwave photons. Fig 8 :40-41 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts" issues the instruction: more search and a second frame, not an imported exotic atom.
6. **A null tensor signal is not an absence.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Tightening r eliminates large-field models. It does not refute the shape.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures derive a scalar field. The shape is a flat-potential, nearly constant energy-density mediator; "scalar field" is a provenanced binding.
- Not that "inflaton" is a discovered primitive. It is a placeholder for a term the discriminating instrument cannot yet single out.
- Not that inflation is untestable because the energy scale is high, and not that it is directly testable at current accelerators. The two facts are kept separate.
- Not that a null r result ends the question, and not that model proliferation corroborates it. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor is the measurement, not the count of agreeing papers.
- Not `grounded`. No number in this derivation was measured by this agent.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. The concrete routes:

- **Tensor bound.** A detection of primordial B modes, or a bound that squeezes the remaining large-field plateau region, discriminates among the model family. This is already running through BICEP/Keck and planned CMB polarimeters.
- **Non-Gaussianity and isocurvature.** A significant primordial signal inconsistent with single-field slow roll would break the licensed shape, since the perturbations would no longer be the spectrum-like ordering the figure permits.
- **Source discrimination.** An instrument able to distinguish which microphysical degree of freedom produced the same spectrum would settle the name and close the shape question.
- **Reachability proof.** A proof that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual would flip the outward route from unreachable-at-budget to underivable and reverse the testability verdict.
- **Structural break.** A measurement showing expansion driven with no flat-potential mediator, or a direct space-time bond, would break the shape claim itself.

The pre-registered refuter is in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5, written before the conclusion. Any one firing fires a retraction. A direct detection of the field would settle the name rather than refute the shape; those outcomes are recorded separately.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The six-term set is a careful compression guess, not a neutral fact about the world; declaring it makes the choice visible. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing sources, and the lineage is recorded so the stop can be checked. Indiscernibility is not identity. This derivation is a simulation checked for internal consistency. It cannot settle the cosmology question it reports on, and it does not claim to.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the field that drove inflation, and it does not call inflation untestable because the energy scale is high. It says that space and time cannot bond directly, so a structure must sit between them; that the driver has the shape of a nearly constant energy density whose spendable gradient produces a flow, with reheating as a production whose output re-enters standard cosmology; and that rival models are members of a functional group rather than separate discoveries. What the framework returns is a shape, not a name, because the CMB spectrum registers the effect but cannot discriminate the source. Direct production at 10^16 GeV is unreachable at this budget, while the same physics is already registered by a different frame, so the indirect test is live now and a null tensor signal is not a proof of absence. What is offered is the shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
