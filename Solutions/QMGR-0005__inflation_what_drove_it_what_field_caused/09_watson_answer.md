## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether licenses about three overlapping sub-questions: what drove inflation, what field caused it, and whether it is testable at the energy scales involved. Option A: hand down a single verdict that names the inflaton and calls the question untestable because the scale exceeds accelerators. Option B: unbundle the union, return a shape for the driver, withhold the name pending a discriminating instrument, and split testability into a direct budget fact and a live indirect test. Option A spends the independence of the reader, hands a filter a verdict, and presents a binding as a derivation. Option B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an open slot. The figures decide for B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** The hot big-bang model reproduces the CMB and light elements only when handed initial conditions it cannot derive. Arity: five named slots (horizon, flatness, monopoles, perturbation spectrum, reheating), and three overlapping conditions (driver, field, testability), so a **union**, not a partition. Symmetry: the operative bond is ⇒ production, ordered, not the symmetric +. Scale: first order for the mechanism, second order for its registration by an instrument. The figure rule is Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Domain 1, field-theoretic mechanism frame.** Actors: a scalar degree of freedom, its potential, vacuum energy density, pressure, slow-roll parameters. Currency: energy density and its rate of change, plus description length of a model. Anchor: none inside this frame; it composes models but cannot settle them. It becomes a domain only when ranged against Domain 2.
- **Domain 2, cosmological observation frame.** Actors: CMB temperature and polarization maps, B modes, galaxy surveys. Currency: n_s, r, f_NL, Ω. Anchor: the instruments, COBE, WMAP, Planck, BICEP/Keck, which do not update on what model builders think.
- **Domain 3, reachability and instrument frame.** Actors: accelerators, CMB polarimeters, the characteristic scale of order 10^16 GeV. Currency: accessible energy (a budget) and residual per slot. Anchor: whether anything at any resolution is already returning something that fails to resolve.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers in inflation cosmology. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero; its agreement is not a witness.
- **Partition or union and dependency order.** Driver, field and testability are a union. Describe R first before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."), then vocabulary (scalar field, potential, vacuum equation of state), then the objective (reduce five slots with one term), then planning (mint the mechanism term), then settle and carry: a settled molecule becomes an atom for the next level. The classic break is between vocabulary and objective.

Strain test applied: Fig 13 governs composition among the six primitives and does not forbid a mediated energy-driven flow, because it explicitly composes energy and a gradient. The catalyst line governs a part that survives its use, which is residual vacuum energy, not the inflaton spent at reheating. Both re-census notes are kept visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8 and F9.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name and the single testability verdict do not. A mechanism term is owed against five separate slots. The driver must be a structure between space and time, constrained as a nearly constant energy density whose spendable gradient produces a flow; reheating is a production and the episode a cycle. The field identity is withheld as a figure result, kept as a provenanced binding. Testability splits into two facts: direct production at order 10^16 GeV is unreachable-at-budget, and an indirect CMB test is live now through a frame whose closure differs. A null tensor signal is not proof of absence. Rival models read as members of a functional group, with a stronger isomer claim left checkable (`06_isomers_and_order.md`). This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. It cannot settle the cosmology question it reports on.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a field name or a filter-as-verdict instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the residual, the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective moves are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the field-name claim and the single testability verdict. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one; the retraction stays visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the inflationary scale identifying a field not of the licensed shape, or proving no such degree of freedom existed, would break the shape; one identifying a field of that shape would settle the name. Primordial non-Gaussianity or isocurvature inconsistent with single-field slow roll would break the spectrum-like ordering. A direct space-time bond or a mediated-flow failure would break the driver verdict. A proof that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual would convert unreachable-at-budget into underivable and reverse the testability verdict. A demonstration that the particle-theory and CMB frames share one evidence pool would remove the triangulation claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`.
