## The result in brief

The question is a union of three sub-questions, not one: what drove inflation, what field caused it, and whether it is testable at the energy scales involved. The figures license a **shape** for the driver (a nearly constant energy density supplied by a flat-potential degree of freedom, mediated by a structure between space and time) and refuse the **name** (the particular field is a binding from the physics frame, not a figure derivation). On testability the figures separate two facts that are usually merged: direct production at the inflationary scale is unreachable at this budget, while the same physics is already registered by a different frame (the CMB), so it is testable indirectly now. A null tensor signal is not a proof of absence.
