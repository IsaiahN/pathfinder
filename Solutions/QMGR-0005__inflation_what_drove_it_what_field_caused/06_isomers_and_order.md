# Isomers, order and the inflaton: supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6). No measurement is claimed here. It supports the verdicts in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`; it does not add a new one.

## RESIDUAL

Three things in the question remain uncounted after the primary audit, and an answer cannot be assembled without them.

1. **Order.** Is the driving of expansion a bare succession of states, or a production that brings a new substance into being? These are different bonds and they issue different tests.
2. **Structure.** What do rival inflation models actually share? Are they two names for one thing, members of one family, or different substances whose notation hides the difference?
3. **Currency.** What does it cost to *state* a model, and what does it cost to *settle* one? The question names an energy scale of order 10^16 GeV, and that number has to be placed in the right ledger before it can be called a verdict.

## Q1. Order: sequence is not production

Fig 12 :72-73 defines sequence as "A then B; B needs A to have happened". Fig 12 :77 defines production as "A produces B; B did not exist before". The test given in the figure is the same: "is there a B before A fires? No."

The hot thermal bath did not exist before reheating. Reheating is therefore ⇒, a production. Writing it as → (sequence) would record only that the bath came later, which is not the claim and does not carry the same cost. Writing it as + (conjunction) would make the order symmetric and lose the direction entirely. Under Fig 12 :46-47, "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." The bond here is the difference the formula would otherwise drop.

Fig 12 :121 names the next level: "cycle. The output re-enters as input." The reheated bath is the input to standard big-bang cosmology, so the whole episode is a cycle at that level, and the sign of the loop decides whether it closes. **Verdict, `specified`:** reheating is a production, and the inflationary episode is a cycle whose output is handed to standard cosmology. Refuter: a demonstration that the hot bath was already present before the episode, or that the output does not re-enter as input.

## Q2. Isomer or functional group?

Fig 12 :117 gives the strict test: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :123 gives the weaker one: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

Apply both to chaotic, plateau, hybrid and f(R) realizations as they are written in the literature.

- As written, they do **not** share an atom set. Each binds a different potential, and often a different field content. Under Fig 12's definition they fail the "same atoms" condition, so calling them isomers is too strong.
- They **do** share a functional group. Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent" together with a nearly constant energy density and a flat enough potential fixes the kind of behaviour: slow roll, a nearly scale-invariant spectrum, dilution of relics. The particular potential is the qualifier that says which member. Fig 12 :123 governs this reading exactly.
- The stronger claim is still available and is worth testing: if two models can be rewritten over the **full shared six-term set** {space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network} and differ only in which structure mediates which state pair, then they are isomers under Fig 12 :117. Fig 13 :121-124 explicitly permits different routes to the same substance and the converse, same ingredients under a different bond giving a different substance.

**Verdict, `specified`, ungrounded:** the family reading (functional group) is licensed as written; the isomer reading is a checkable stronger claim, not a settled fact. Refuter, written before this conclusion: exhibit two models written over the same full atom set that differ only in bond arrangement and show identical behaviour (kills the isomer claim, leaves the functional-group claim), or show that no shared substructure fixes the outcome (kills both).

## Q3. The catalyst re-census

Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." A first reading would make the inflaton a catalyst, because the term appears throughout the episode. That reading strains. The inflaton is spent at reheating, so it does not survive its own use. The catalyst line governs a different object, the residual vacuum energy that does survive. This is not a correction to the figure but a re-census of which quantity it governs, kept visible rather than erased (also recorded as F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`). **Refuter:** a model in which the driving field survives reheating unchanged would restore the catalyst reading and break the production reading of Q1.

## Q4. Two currencies, held apart

Fig 12 :125-127 says a composition costs in two currencies: bits price the description and actions price finding out whether it holds, and they are in different units. A model with a flat potential is cheap to state in bits and may take a whole experimental budget to settle. The characteristic scale of order 10^16 GeV is therefore a price in **actions**, a budget for a direct production test. It is not a price in bits and not a verdict about testability. This is the structural reason behind Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 8 :40-41 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts" issues the different instruction: more search and another frame, not an imported exotic atom.

## ANALOGUES (corroboration only, never the anchor)

- **Chemistry.** Fig 12 :97-98 records silver fulminate and silver cyanate, same composition, different properties, which is what forced structure into the notation. This is the figure's own analogue for the isomer test, not independent evidence from chemistry.
- **Source discrimination.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Applied here, the CMB reads a spectrum and cannot comment on which field produced it. This is an analogue from perception science with low overlap with cosmology.
- **Inverse problems.** The inverse-problem reference records that a rank-deficient forward map gives a non-unique solution. Several microphysical fields can project onto one measured spectrum. This is a mathematical analogue of the name-withholding limit, not a derivation of it.

These analogues agree with the figure verdict. By Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", their agreement is recorded but not counted as a second witness where they share the framework's author.

## PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIER

Written before the conclusion, as required by the seat map. The supporting derivation fails if any of the following fires:

1. The hot bath is shown to pre-exist the episode, or the output fails to re-enter as input (breaks Q1).
2. Two models are shown to share the full six-term atom set, differ only in bond arrangement, and yet behave identically (breaks the isomer form of Q2).
3. No shared substructure is found that fixes the slow-roll outcome across models (breaks the functional-group form of Q2).
4. The driving field is shown to survive reheating, restoring the catalyst reading (breaks Q3).
5. The 10^16 GeV scale is shown to be a limit on what can be represented at all, rather than a limit on accessible energy (breaks Q4 and forces a re-census of the testability verdict).

## CONCLUSION

Order: the driver is a production (⇒), the episode is a cycle, and the reheated bath is a handed-over input, not a later state. Structure: inflation models as written are members of a functional group, with the isomer reading left as a stronger test over a shared atom set. Currency: the energy scale is an action price, a filter for the budget, and is not allowed to hand down the testability verdict. None of this names the field. The name stays open until a source-discriminating instrument fires.
