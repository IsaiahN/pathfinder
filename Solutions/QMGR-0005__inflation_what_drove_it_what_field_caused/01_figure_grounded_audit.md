## 0. The question under audit, stated as an effect

> "Inflation: what drove it, what field caused it, and whether it is testable at the energy scales involved"

Restated in effect terms: the hot big-bang model reproduces the CMB and light-element abundances only if it is handed initial conditions it cannot itself explain. We must decide what leads to what under three options:

- **Driver:** (A) an unexplained brute initial condition, or (B) a mechanism term that reduces several residuals at once. B leads to a minted term with a stated bargain; A leads to a stopped census.
- **Field:** (A) a named microphysical field, or (B) a shape with the name withheld pending a ground reading. A leads to a binding presented as a derivation; B leads to a checkable shape and an open slot.
- **Testability:** (A) "the energy scale is beyond accelerators, therefore untestable", or (B) the energy scale is a budget filter, while a different frame already registers the effect. B leads to an indirect ground test now; A leads to a verdict handed to a filter, which the figures forbid.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a candidate answer must (i) name a mediator that makes expansion of space over time a legal cross-kind composition, (ii) state whether the field identity is derived or bound, and (iii) point to an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve. Fig 5 :65: *"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

## 2. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain.

- **Domain 1, field-theoretic mechanism frame.** Actors: a scalar degree of freedom, its potential V(φ), vacuum energy density ρ, pressure p, slow-roll parameters. Currency: energy density and its rate of change, plus description length of a model. Anchor: **none inside this frame**. It composes models; it cannot settle them. It becomes a domain only when ranged against Domain 2.
- **Domain 2, cosmological observation frame.** Actors: CMB temperature and polarization maps, B modes, galaxy surveys. Currency: n_s, r, f_NL, Ω. Anchor: **the instruments**, COBE, WMAP, Planck, BICEP/Keck. They do not update on what model builders think (Fig 2 :29).
- **Domain 3, reachability and instrument frame.** Actors: accelerators, CMB polarimeters, the characteristic energy scale of order 10^16 GeV. Currency: accessible energy (budget) and residual per slot. Anchor: **the question whether anything at any resolution is already returning something that fails to resolve** (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers in inflation cosmology. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: **the ground, never agreement**. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

**Partition or union.** Driver, field and testability are a **union**, not a partition. The driver and the field are the same term seen from two frames, and testability ranges that term against a ground. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (so residual first, frame second: Fig 8 :53).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3), upstream first.** (1) Perception: the CMB residual is already registered (COBE, 1992). (2) Vocabulary: scalar fields, vacuum energy and potential gradients are already terms in Γ. (3) Objective: reduce the five slots with one term (the MDL bargain). (4) Planning: mint a mechanism term in Domain 1 and range it against Domain 2. (5) Learn and carry: each settled model becomes an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104). The classic break is between (2) and (3): having the terms is not the same as saying what would count as progress. Fig 3 :26: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**Habitat (enumerated, never composed), Fig 11 :43-44:** *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Actors in contact with the residual: the inflaton candidate, the metric, standard-model fields, foreground dust and synchrotron (for B modes), Boltzmann codes, lensing. Conditions: energy scale, horizon crossing, reheating temperature. Relations: production at reheating, dilution of monopoles, quantum fluctuation to classical perturbation. Contact cascades, so second-order actors (interstellar dust polarimetry) bear on a first-order slot (r).

## 4. Findings

### F1 - The three sub-questions are a union and must be unbundled (decisive for method)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

The three sub-questions overlap. Answering "what drove it" with a field name already answers "what field caused it" by a binding, and "is it testable" ranges that binding against a ground. A single search over the bundled question would let whichever frame is named first seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28). **Verdict:** derive driver, field and testability as three loops with separate verdicts, then recombine. Triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 8 (low overlap between the split rule and the residual-first rule).

### F2 - The residual has real mass on named slots, so support is satisfied

Fig 5 :20-21: *"is there anything to explain?"* and *"the gap has real mass somewhere"*. Fig 1 :19 and :22 (quoted in section 1) forbid aggregating the five slots. The CMB and monopole searches give positive mass on s1 to s4. **Verdict:** SUPPORT gate passes. Single-source for the figure rule; the numeric values are world sources (Planck, Wikipedia) and are corroboration only.

### F3 - A new mechanism term is owed, and it pays

Fig 5 :14: *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. The big-bang model does not lack vocabulary; its mechanism for the initial conditions is broken, so this is a mint, not a refit.

The three guards, from Fig 5 :22-26, are a product:
- REACHABILITY: *"it composes from what we already have."* Scalar fields, potential energy and vacuum equations of state are already in Γ.
- NOVELTY: a phase of accelerated expansion dominated by nearly constant energy density is not in the prior cosmological library.
- BARGAIN, Fig 5 :26: *"does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"* and Fig 5 :43-44: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* One term removes four or five slot residuals.

**Verdict:** `general` structural claim, mint licensed. Refuter written before the run: if the five slots are shown to be independent brute facts that no single mechanism can reduce (left(R,φ) does not fall), the bargain fails and no mint is owed. Triangulated across Fig 5 and Fig 1.

### F4 - What drove it: a cross-kind mediator, a production, and a cycle

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* So expansion of space over time is not a bare bond of two states. A structure must sit between them.

Fig 13 :22 names the relevant structure: *"a difference that can be spent"*. Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* The driver is therefore energy bonded to a spendable gradient, producing a flow. Fig 12 :76, on the ⇒ bond: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*. The hot plasma did not exist before reheating, so reheating is production (⇒), not mere sequence (→) and not conjunction (+). Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input."* The reheated bath is the input to standard big-bang cosmology, so the whole episode is a cycle at the next level.

Fig 13 :19-20 on the observational residue: *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*. The nearly scale-invariant perturbation spectrum is a spectrum-like ordering left by the crossing, not a seventh primitive.

**Verdict:** `specified`, ungrounded. The shape is a flat-potential, nearly constant energy density whose gradient drives a flow; the identity of the degree of freedom is not given. Refuter: a measurement showing the expansion was driven by a direct space-time bond with no structural mediator, or that the perturbations are not a spectrum-like ordering, breaks the shape. Triangulated across Fig 13 and Fig 12 (substrate grammar versus bond semantics).

### F5 - What field caused it: a shape is licensed, a name is not

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The canonical README states the limit as a shape, not a name (see `05_what_shape_means.md`). What the figures license is: a degree of freedom whose potential energy dominates, whose potential is flat enough for slow roll, and whose gradient can be spent. Naming it "the inflaton" is a binding from the physics frame onto terms already in Γ. It is **not** an import that adds an atom: Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*, Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

World corroboration, secondary: the cosmic-inflation reference entry states "The detailed particle physics mechanism responsible for inflation is unknown" and calls it "the hypothetical field thought to be responsible for inflation". The inverse-problem reference states that when a forward map is rank deficient "the solution of the system will be undetermined", which is the mathematical form of the same non-uniqueness: several microphysical fields can project onto the same CMB observables. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information", and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the source.

**Verdict:** `specified`. Shape licensed; name withheld. The word "inflaton" is recorded as a placeholder binding, not a settlement. Refuter: an instrument that discriminates sources at the inflationary scale and identifies a specific field would settle the name; a demonstration that no nearly constant energy density mediator was involved would break the shape. Triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 6, with the source-discrimination point single-source for the figure set and corroborated by two low-overlap world fields (mathematics and perception science).

### F6 - Testability: the energy scale is a filter, not a witness

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The characteristic scale of order 10^16 GeV is a statement about the budget of a direct accelerator test. It is not a verdict about testability.

Formula step 7 and Fig 6 :31 require the two instruments to be run as opposed tracks and never read against one another.

- **INWARD**, Fig 6 :29: *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access."* Direct production of the field at 10^16 GeV is unreachable at this budget. But Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* The CMB is already returning something that fails to resolve: COBE found the anisotropy, Planck measured n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042, and BICEP/Keck constrains r_0.05 < 0.036. There is an edge to extend from. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*, and :33 *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."*
- **OUTWARD**, Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The cosmology frame already holds the effect of the field-theory frame's cause. Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* The union reaches a testable claim neither frame reaches alone. This is triangulation with low overlap: particle theory versus microwave photons.
- **SEARCHED first** is the cheapest track and is what the BICEP/Keck progression does: the same closure, more of the space visited, r bound tightened from 0.10 to 0.036. No new atom was imported.

Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts"*. Direct production is the second, so more search and a second frame are the remedy, not an imported exotic atom. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A null tensor signal eliminates large-field models; it does not refute inflation as a shape.

**Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 9. Indirect testability now; direct production unreachable-at-budget; null is not absence. Refuter: proof that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual would flip the outward route to genuinely unreachable and reverse the verdict.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. A measurement shows the primordial perturbations are not a spectrum-like, nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic ordering (breaks the s4 shape and F4). A significant primordial non-Gaussianity or isocurvature signal inconsistent with single-field slow roll would fire this.
2. A measurement shows the early expansion was driven without any flat-potential, nearly constant energy-density mediator (breaks F4 and the cross-kind requirement).
3. A source-discriminating instrument at the inflationary scale identifies a specific field that is **not** of the licensed shape (breaks F5), or proves no such degree of freedom existed.
4. It is proven that no instrument at any resolution can register the primordial residual, so the outward route fails and "unreachable at this budget" becomes "unreachable in principle" (breaks F6).
5. The five slots are shown to be independent brute facts for which left(R,φ) does not fall (breaks F3's bargain).

A direct accelerator detection of the field at the inflationary scale would **settle the name**, not refute the shape. These are different outcomes and are recorded separately.

Until one of 1 to 5 is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
