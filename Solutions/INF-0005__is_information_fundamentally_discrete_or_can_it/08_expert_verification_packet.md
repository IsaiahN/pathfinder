# Expert verification packet: the Tether on discrete versus continuous information

**What this is:** a claim in a form an information theorist, physicist or philosopher of information can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** No instrument at a physical scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Neither "information is fundamentally discrete" nor "information can be continuous at the physical level" can be cashed out at the primitive level. Under a six-term generating set (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network), the continuum was explicitly removed because it is what a spectrum without gaps produces, and discreteness is explicitly named as something a composition leaves behind, not as a seventh member. Irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world, so there is no bottom stratum at which the binary finally stops. The discrete bit-price of a description belongs to the description layer and does not decide the physical question. Which leftover a physical carrier returns is owed to an instrument, and no such instrument is claimed here. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The continuum is a derivative, not a primitive

- **Statement:** the generating set does not contain a continuum; it contains a spectrum that can produce smoothness when bonded to space and time.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43-44, "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." Also Fig 13 :49-50, "Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none."
- **Mode:** `specified`, figure-derived.
- **Refuter:** derive spectrum-without-gaps from the other five and show it is indivisible; or show an established law that loses its derivation when the continuum is removed from the set. Either makes it a seventh member.

### C2: Discreteness is a leftover by the same rule

- **Statement:** discreteness is not a floor beneath the six terms. It is what a bounded, counted composition leaves behind, in the same sentence as smoothness, symmetry and chaos.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :124-125, "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." Also Fig 13 :41, "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it." And Fig 13 :68, "finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate".
- **Mode:** `specified`, figure-derived.
- **Refuter:** derive discreteness as an indivisible seventh member, or show that removing it from the six costs an established law. The bit-price of a description (Fig 12 :43, "one bond type costs nothing; seven cost 2.8 bits each") is **not** such a refuter: it prices the description layer, and using it as a physical witness is the error named in Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

### C3: "Fundamental" cannot name a privileged stratum

- **Statement:** irreducibility is relative to a level; there is no bottom and no top. A bit can be atomic for coding theory without being a primitive of the world.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :103, "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible." Fig 12 :104, "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom". Fig 4 :33, "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."
- **Mode:** `specified` / `general`.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a stratum that is irreducibly atomic for all levels, with a ground that settles it, so irreducibility becomes world-level rather than level-level.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that bits are unreal. They are units of description priced by a chosen logarithmic base (Shannon 1948, read 2026-11-23).
- Not that Shannon entropy or differential entropy is wrong. Differential entropy is a continuous generalization with an offset that depends on units; that is a level-local fact.
- Not that physical spacetime is discrete, and not that it is smooth. This packet makes no Planck-scale measurement.
- Not "both are true". That would be a verdict in `general` mode with no ground.
- Not that discreteness is false at a level. At a level it can be exactly what the composition leaves behind.
- Not `grounded`. Everything here is `candidate`.
- Not a claim that the six-term set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess at compression axes.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The F6 gap is explicit: no Planck-scale instrument is claimed. Concretely:

1. A countdown test returning a clean termination at a named scale names the discrete leftover at that level.
2. A gap test returning no gaps at that scale names the smooth leftover at that level.
3. One instrument returning both readings for the same carrier shows metamerism, so the instrument is inadequate rather than the carrier being both.
4. A derivation of either term as a seventh indivisible member breaks C1 or C2.
5. A level-independent atom breaks C3.

Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`. Agreement from readers does not fire any of them (Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.").
