## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, between Option A (information is fundamentally discrete, so a bit-like atom exists and continuity is a coarse description over counts) and Option B (information can be continuous at the physical level, so a carrier admits arbitrary fine-grained values and discreteness is a coarse description over a smooth quantity). What each leads to: A licenses a search for the atom; B licenses a search for the smooth carrier. Both treat "discrete" and "continuous" as candidates for a primitive and "fundamental" as a privileged stratum. A third reading, derived before the world search, is that both terms are level-relative composition leftovers and that a ground decides which appears at a level.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Neither option is primitive. The continuum was removed because it is what a spectrum without gaps produces; discreteness is named as a composition leftover in the same sentence as smoothness, symmetry and chaos. Both are arrangements over the same six terms, so at the primitive level they cannot form a partition. Irreducibility is a fact about a level, with no bottom and no top, so "fundamental" cannot name a privileged stratum. The discrete bit-price of a description is recorded as a layer distinction and is not promoted to a physical witness. Which leftover a physical carrier returns is a `gap` owed to an instrument. The verdict is `specified` / `general`, `candidate`, and simulated. Per-domain verdicts are not averaged: Domain 1 `single-source`, Domain 2 leftovers `triangulated`, Domain 2 measurement `gap`, Domain 3 `triangulated`, Domain 4 `gap`, Domain 5 `triangulated`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three refuters and the layer distinction before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is promoting the bit-price to a physical verdict, which would settle the question wrongly and prematurely.
- **undoable:** yes. The physical binding is not asserted anywhere in the package, so nothing physical has to be withdrawn if an instrument returns the opposite reading. The layer distinction in F5 can be revised without touching the two removal findings. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be struck from the set.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument returning a terminating count or a gap at a named scale would name the leftover and move that domain to `grounded`; one instrument returning both for the same carrier would show metamerism; a derivation of either term as an indivisible seventh member would break Fig 13 :41 and F1 or F2; a level-independent atom would break Fig 12 :104; a figure re-census showing the bit-price is a physical witness would clear the F5 strain in the other direction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
