## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question forces a binary:

- **A, discrete:** there is a smallest unit of information, a bit-like atom that cannot be composed further. Anything continuous is a coarse description over counts. This leads to a search for the atom and treats the continuum as derivative.
- **B, continuous:** the physical carrier admits arbitrarily fine-grained values. Anything discrete is a coarse description over a smooth quantity. This leads to a search for the smooth carrier and treats the bit as derivative.

What fails to resolve is not which option wins. It is the standing of both options. Each treats "discrete" and "continuous" as candidates for a primitive, and each treats "fundamental" as naming a privileged stratum where the answer finally stops. Both moves are governed by figure lines, so the residual is closeable in shape form before any world search.

**The sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"):** a statement of which compositions over the six-term set leave discreteness behind, which leave smoothness behind, and what ground would decide which leftover appears at a given level. Binding it lets it accrue a residual like anything else, so the preference is refusable by the same test as every other term. This paragraph is the binding. It was written before the findings below.

## 1. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Two named options, but the figure census shows they are not two members of one partition at the primitive level. Both are derivatives. The true arity is a union compound: multiple conditions (primitive status, composition, level, instrument) bundled as one binary (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").
- **Symmetry.** The question presents discrete and continuous as symmetric alternatives. Under the six-term set they are not symmetric peers. Smoothness comes from ordering over space and time, discreteness comes from counting over finiteness. Different compositions, different operands, so swapping them changes the derivation.
- **Scale.** "Fundamental" claims a privileged stratum. Fig 12 :104 says "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"; Fig 4 :33 says "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress." A privileged bottom or top is not available to this framework.

## 2. Domain census before choosing any figure line

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

- **Domain 1, information theory and coding.** Actors: symbols, probabilities, channels, codewords, bits. Currency: description length in bits, chosen by the logarithmic base. Anchor: the coding theorems and channel capacity, which can be checked. Question type: partition of a message space into symbol slots. This domain is real, but it does not decide what is primitive about the world.
- **Domain 2, fundamental physics.** Actors: spacetime, fields, metric, quantum fluctuations, Planck scale. Currency: physical measurement. Anchor: an instrument. At the Planck scale the anchor is not yet reachable, which makes the physical half of the question a genuine ground gap, not a figure gap.
- **Domain 3, framework substrate.** Actors: the six terms (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures), seven bonds, negation. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)`, priced in bits and actions. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement. This is the domain where the discrete/continuous question is actually decidable from the figures.
- **Domain 4, cognition and engineering.** Actors: observers doing subitizing or estimation, analog and digital machines. Currency: what can be discriminated or computed. Anchor: behavioural reaction times, instrument readouts, engineering practice. Shows both modes as level-local achievements, not cosmological primitives.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run every domain draws on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.

**Partition or union.** Domain 3 is a partition question at the primitive level (which terms belong in the generating set). The user's binary is a union compound (Fig 9 :54), so it must be unbundled before search. **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** settle primitive status first (F1, F2), then level relativity (F4), then instrument reach (F6), then verification and outreach. Effort spent on the physical measurement before the primitive question is effort above the break, because it assumes exactly what F1 and F2 decide.

## 4. Findings

### F1: The continuum was explicitly removed from the generating set (decisive)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

Fig 13 :49-50:

> **"Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none."**

The continuum is not missing from the framework, and it is not banned from the world. It is a composition that the six produce: a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce. So Option B, as stated ("information is continuous at the physical level" with continuity as a primitive), is not composable under the six-term set. The continuity that exists is a derivative, and it is exactly the derivative the figure names.

**Mode:** `specified`, figure-derived. **Status:** triangulated across the removal sentence and the load-bearing test (Fig 13 :43-44 and :49-50), low overlap between the two.

**Refuter:** derive spectrum-without-gaps from the other five alone and show it is indivisible, or show an established law that loses its derivation when the continuum is removed. Either would make the continuum a seventh member and break the set.

### F2: Discreteness is also a leftover, and not a seventh member (decisive)

Fig 13 :124-125:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

Fig 13 :41:

> **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**

The same sentence that names smoothness as a leftover names discreteness as a leftover. The framework does not hold a primitive bit. Discreteness is what a composition leaves behind, alongside smoothness, symmetry and chaos. Option A, as stated ("information is fundamentally discrete" with the bit as a primitive), is therefore the same category error as Option B, made on the other side.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 :124-125 plus Fig 13 :41), low overlap between the leftover list and the indivisibility rule.

**Refuter:** derive discreteness as a seventh indivisible member, or show that removing discreteness from the six costs an established law. Until one fires, the bit is an arrangement, not a floor.

### F3: The forced binary is a union compound, not a partition (structural)

Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 13 :115:

> **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

Fig 13 :116-117:

> **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."**

The set of kinds is closed; the arrangements are not. "Discrete" and "continuous" name two arrangements, so they cannot be two members of the primitive partition. They are not even two values of one primitive. They are two different routes through the arrangement space. The binary must be unbundled into: primitive status, level of composition, and instrument reach. A single lever cannot clear a compound (Fig 9 :55-58).

**Mode:** `general` (structural). **Status:** single-source on the figure text (Fig 9 + Fig 13 are the same framework, but low overlap in what they govern: split logic versus closure).

**Refuter:** show that discrete and continuous are two values of one indivisible primitive over the same operands, so the split really is a partition and one lever can clear it.

### F4: "Fundamental" assumes a privileged stratum that the figures forbid

Fig 12 :103:

> **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

Fig 4 :33:

> **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

Irreducible is a fact about a level, never about the world. There is no bottom and no top. So the word "fundamentally" in the question cannot be cashed out as "at the bottom stratum". At every stratum, what is atomic is what settled at that level, and a settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. A bit can be atomic for coding theory without being a primitive of the world.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 :103-104 and Fig 4 :33 (two figures, different governing claims, low overlap).

**Refuter:** exhibit a stratum that is irreducibly atomic for all levels, with a ground that settles it, so irreducibility becomes world-level rather than level-level.

### F5: The price is discrete bits over counted atoms and bonds, and that is a strain to name, not a verdict

Fig 12 :43:

> **"one bond type costs nothing; seven cost 2.8 bits each"**

Fig 13 :68:

> **"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"**

Fig 13 :20:

> **"ordering, so more and less can be said"**

The framework prices descriptions in bits, over integer counts of atoms and bond types. Spectrum is "ordering, so more and less can be said", which is where a continuum-like reading enters. This creates a real tension worth stating plainly: the *price* of a term is discrete, but neither discreteness nor smoothness is a *primitive*. The discrete accounting belongs to the description layer, not to the generating set. Treating the price as evidence for a discrete world would be the metamerism error named in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads a value and cannot comment on the source.

**Mode:** `specified` for the price claim, `general` for the caution. **Status:** triangulated on each half separately; the bridging inference is explicitly refused.

**Refuter for the refusal:** show a ground reading in which the bit-price of a description discriminates between a discrete and a continuous carrier. Until then the price does not hand us a verdict, which is exactly Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
