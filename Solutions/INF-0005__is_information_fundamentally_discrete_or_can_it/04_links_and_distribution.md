## 2. Wider literature that bears on the question

Three points worth carrying into a conversation, all from sources actually opened:

1. **Both descriptions of information exist at the level of theory.** Shannon's bit is introduced by a choice of logarithmic base, while differential entropy generalizes Shannon entropy to continuous distributions and carries an offset that depends on units. Neither fact establishes a primitive; both establish that the choice of description is priced and level-local. This corroborates F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and does not settle the physical question.
2. **The concepts do not form one clean kind.** The SEP entry says three influential proposals have "next to nothing in common". That is the union-compound reading of F3 from an independent field. Agreement here is structural, not a pooled evidence claim, because the SEP is not drawing on the figures.
3. **Physical discreteness is conjectural.** The Planck-units entry says quantum fluctuations "might make the familiar notion of distance inapplicable below the Planck length" and that a theory of quantum gravity "does not yet exist". That is exactly F6's `gap`. It is a verdict about instrument reach, not a silence about the answer.

None of these sources conflicts with the figure verdict. If one did, the rule is to return to the domain census, not to flip the verdict.
