# The Tether's answer: discrete, continuous, or neither at the primitive level

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6). No instrument was built and no physical measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question forces a choice between a smallest unit of information and a continuous physical carrier. Both options treat "discrete" and "continuous" as candidates for a primitive, and both treat "fundamental" as naming a privileged stratum. Fig 9 :54 calls this a union compound: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The residual splits into primitive status, level of composition, and instrument reach.

## 2. The answer

1. **The continuum is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :43-44: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." Fig 13 :49-50: "Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none." Continuity exists in the framework, but as a derivative, not as a member of the generating set.

2. **Discreteness is not a primitive either.** Fig 13 :124-125: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." Fig 13 :41: "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it." The bit is an arrangement, not a floor.

3. **The binary is therefore a false partition at the primitive level.** Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :116-117: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held." Discrete and smooth are two arrangements, not two members of the primitive set.

4. **"Fundamental" cannot name a bottom stratum.** Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible." Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom". Fig 4 :33: "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress." A bit can be irreducible for coding theory and derivative for the substrate. There is no level at which the question finally stops for all levels.

5. **The discrete price of a description does not decide the physical question.** Fig 12 :43: "one bond type costs nothing; seven cost 2.8 bits each". Fig 13 :68: "finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate". The accounting is in bits over counted atoms and bond types, but that is the description layer. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." This tension is named, not smoothed.

6. **Which leftover a physical carrier returns is an instrument question.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The physical half is `gap`: unreached, not unreachable, and not a win for either side.

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The shape is: both terms are level-relative composition leftovers over the same six-term set, and the ground decides which appears at a level. F1 and F2 keep the two refuters separate, so neither side is averaged with the other.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that bits do not exist. They exist, as units of description priced by a chosen logarithmic base.
- Not that Shannon entropy is wrong, or that differential entropy is wrong. Both are level-local theories with their own ground.
- Not that physical spacetime is smooth, or that it is discrete. No Planck-scale measurement is claimed.
- Not that "both are true". That would be a verdict stated in `general` mode with no ground.
- Not that discreteness is false at a level. At a level it can be exactly what the composition leaves behind.
- Not a derivation of one option from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Every claim above is `candidate` until an instrument pays.

## 4. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not a debate, settled the colour case. Here:

- **Countdown test:** does a count terminate at a named scale, with a bounded region that makes the counting stop? An instrument that returns a clean termination names the discrete leftover at that level.
- **Gap test:** does an ordering remain without gaps at that scale? An instrument that returns no gap names the smooth leftover at that level.
- **Discriminator:** does one instrument return both readings for the same carrier? That would be metamerism and would show the instrument is inadequate, not that the carrier is both.
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive either term as an indivisible seventh; show an established law that loses its derivation without it; exhibit a level-independent atom; show the framework naming a leftover without ground or provenance. Any one breaks the shape claim.

The world sources report that Planck-scale discreteness is conjectural and that a quantum theory of gravity does not yet exist. That is the honest location of the stop.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list. Fig 13 :134 gives the form: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The answer stops at the limit of what is available and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. If an instrument returns a reading tomorrow, the shape claim can be revised without withdrawing the derivation, because the derivation never claimed a physical property.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the discrete-versus-continuous debate, and it does not claim the final answer. It says that a continuum was explicitly removed from the minimum generating set because it is what a spectrum without gaps produces, and that discreteness is named in the same breath as smoothness, symmetry and chaos as something a composition leaves behind. Neither is a primitive. "Fundamental" cannot name a bottom level, because irreducibility is a fact about a level and there is no bottom and no top. What the framework returns is a shape: both terms are level-relative leftovers over the same six terms, and an instrument, not an argument, decides which one appears at a physical level. The discrete bit-price of a description is recorded as a tension, not promoted to a verdict. What is offered is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The physical question stays open until the ground settles it.
