# The Tether's answer: exploration versus acting on what is known

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separators and the routing rule, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal numeric fraction. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

On each slot the system can consume a prediction that already pays, or aim a probe at what the library cannot yet predict. `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot. The ordinary question asks for one ratio. Fig 9 :4-5 refuses the one-question reading: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The object is a union of operations with different currencies, not one dial with a right setting.

## 2. The shape

1. **Route the gap per slot, never by a global rate.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Symbols table :43 *"One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."* Where `R⁺_s` has positive mass, a probe is owed (Symbols :47); where nothing is scoring, the library may be consumed.
2. **The gap has kinds, not weights.** Fig 5 :11 *"held: the model was right, nothing owed"*; Fig 5 :12 *"new: extend perception, aim the probe here"*; Fig 5 :13 *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"*; Fig 5 :14 *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. There is a fifth bin, a refusal, which no arrangement of the other four reaches (Formula step 2).
3. **Exploration is an aimed drive, and its activity is not its success.** Fig 5 :46 *"When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient."* Fig 5 :48 *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* The aim is the gap that is large and compressible (Fig 5 :18).
4. **Two currencies that do not add.** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them and will not notice the shortfall."* Information gain is a price, not a verdict. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
5. **The amount is a bargain, not a threshold.** Fig 5 :42 *"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."* A probe pays when stating it plus what remains unexplained costs less than what was unexplained before (Fig 5 :43). The three guards are a product, so a system minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting (Fig 5 :41).
6. **"Explore" is three opposed operations.** Fig 6 :29 *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."* Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 6 :49 *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."* Fig 6 :31 *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*
7. **Independence is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong.** Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 6 :50 *"Fails by spending budget on a region already swept. Distribute the search, and keep the searchers independent."*
8. **Acting on known is contact, not storage.** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :25-26 *"stale percepts, dropped transients,"* and *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. If the channel has decayed, the stored prediction is a recording, not knowledge (Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*).
9. **No standing winner.** Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
10. **The universal fraction is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The framework returns the routing rule, the payment test and the probe modes. It does not return a number.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not an optimal ε, temperature, exploration bonus or universal allocation. None is figure-governed.
- Not that information gain alone decides. Information is a price in bits, and the ground settles whether the action paid (Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*).
- Not that exploration is always good or exploitation always bad. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not that random search and structured probe are the same operation. Fig 6 :31 keeps them apart.
- Not grounded. No measurement ranged all slots and scales at once.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not another argument. The questions that would close the shape:

- **Slot discriminator:** an instrument that shows whether a live `R⁺_s` is a compressible gap, an irreducibly noisy observation (the noisy TV case), or a decayed channel. The information reading alone does not separate these.
- **Currency conversion with provenance:** a measured common unit for bits and actions, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23), before ⋛ is well typed (Operators table: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*).
- **Probe-mode discriminator:** a case in which SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD return different verdicts. If they cannot be told apart, Fig 6 :31 is wrong.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a figure line that returns the universal fraction; a genuinely global residual; information gain settling the choice with no ground; the three probe modes collapsing into one; exploration that never changes contact improving capability.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The fraction may exist in some decision frame; the framework does not hold it and cannot name it. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce an optimal exploration fraction. It says that the choice is routed per slot by the live residual, not by a global rate; that the gap falls into kinds, including a refusal that no amount of sampling reaches; that the two currencies, bits for describing and actions for settling, do not add, so information gain cannot score the choice by itself; and that the word "explore" hides three opposed operations, more search inside the same closure, extension of an instrument already returning something, and import from a frame whose closure differs. Acting on what is known is contact with a non-updating ground, not storage of a recording, and nothing is intrinsically good or bad across residual changes. What the framework offers is the routing shape, a payment test, the three-mode separator and five refuters written in advance. The universal number stays marked as a `gap` until a ground settles it.
