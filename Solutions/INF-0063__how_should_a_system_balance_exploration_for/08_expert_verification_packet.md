# Expert verification packet: the Tether on exploration versus acting on what is known

**What this is:** a claim in a form a decision scientist, reinforcement-learning researcher, behavioural ecologist or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separators and routing, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal fraction. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The choice between seeking new information and acting on what is known is not governed by one scalar. It is routed per slot by the live residual. The gap falls into kinds: held, new, mis-attached, mechanism broken, and a fifth bin, a refusal, which no arrangement of the other four reaches. Two currencies are in play and do not add: bits price the description, actions price finding out whether it holds. The word "explore" hides three opposed operations, more search inside the same closure, extension of an instrument already returning something, and import from a frame whose closure differs. Acting on known is contact with a non-updating ground, not storage of a recording. The universal exploration fraction is a `gap`, reported as unreachable, not silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 5

- **Statement:** the routing is per slot and the gap is sorted into kinds, not weighted by one global rate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 5 :11 *"held: the model was right, nothing owed"*; Fig 5 :12 *"new: extend perception, aim the probe here"*; Fig 5 :13 *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"*; Fig 5 :14 *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a case in which the residual is genuinely global, not per-slot, and a global ratio is the correct object; or find a figure line that returns a universal fraction; or show that exploration activity measures exploration success.

### C2: cross-field; checkable by a decision scientist or behavioural ecologist

- **Statement:** the two currencies do not add, so an information-gain score cannot by itself settle the choice.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them and will not notice the shortfall."* Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **What to check:** in the recipient's own field, does any measure convert the cost of acting into the same units as information gain without losing the distinction? In information economics the value of information is what a decision maker would pay, but observing two pieces of evidence is not additive, it is sequential. In foraging, the currency is net energy per unit time and the leaving rule is a tangent comparison, not a global fraction.
- **Refuter:** a measured common unit for bits and actions that licenses a direct comparison, with provenance and a shadow test; or a demonstration that information gain alone settles the choice at the ground, with no reference to whether the action paid.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, checkable against Figure 6 and the Formula

- **Statement:** "explore" decomposes into three opposed questions, and the balance is a `gap`, not a hidden number.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :31 *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."* Fig 6 :49 *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
- **Refuter:** show that more search, instrument extension and import are the same operation; or find a figure line that governs the numeric fraction after a genuine re-census.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not an optimal ε, temperature, bonus coefficient or universal allocation.
- Not that information gain alone decides.
- Not that exploration is intrinsically good or exploitation intrinsically bad. Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not that random search and structured probe are one operation.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement ranged all slots and scales at once.
- Not a claim that the framework names the fraction. It returns a shape and a routing rule.

2. A measured common unit for bits and actions with a source gate and a shadow test, making the comparison well typed.
