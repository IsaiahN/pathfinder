# Outreach draft: check the routing claim before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim, the refuter and the gap have survived the recipient's own check. The offer collapses on contact if the first stage is a theory dump.

---

**Subject:** One claim to check about exploration versus acting on what is known

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "how much exploration versus exploitation" asks for one dial, but the object that actually routes the choice is the residual measured per slot, not a global rate. The framework sorts each gap into kinds: the model was right and nothing is owed; the gap is new and a probe is owed; the attachment is wrong and a refit is owed; the mechanism is broken and a new term is owed; and a held term is false and a refusal is owed. A curiosity drive fires when nothing is scoring, aimed at the gap that is large and compressible. Its own output is never a measure of success: a system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing.
>
> Three further separations follow:
> 1. **Two currencies that do not add.** Bits price the description; actions price finding out whether it holds. Summing them into one number is the named error, not the fix.
> 2. **Three different things hidden inside "explore."** More search inside the same closure; extending an instrument from a worse one already returning something; and importing from a frame whose closure differs. They are opposed and must be run as separate tracks.
> 3. **Acting on what is known is contact, not storage.** It is valid only while the channel to a non-updating ground is still reachable. A saturated metric or a pooled reading means you have lost touch, not that the ground changed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I do not have an optimal ε, a temperature, or a universal fraction. No figure line returns one. That number is recorded as a `gap`, which is a verdict here and not a silence. I also do not claim that information gain alone settles the choice: information is a price in bits, and the ground settles whether the action paid.
>
> **What would refute the claim:**
> 1. a figure line that returns a universal exploration fraction for all slots and scales;
> 2. a case where the residual is genuinely global and a global ratio is the right object;
> 3. a demonstration that information gain alone settles the choice with no reference to the ground;
> 4. a demonstration that more search, instrument extension and import are the same operation;
> 5. a measurement showing exploration that never changes ground contact nevertheless improves capability.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the routing claim is withdrawn or narrowed. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Berger-Tal, Nathan, Meron and Saltz 2014, PLOS ONE: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3995763/
> - Cohen, McClure and Yu 2007, Philosophical Transactions B: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2430007/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, because the framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> [name]
