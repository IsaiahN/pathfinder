## Effect reading of the question

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :39 *"Correlation has valence."* and Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients by likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
