## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a number

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: per-slot routing, two currencies held apart, three probe modes kept separate, and a marked `gap` where the universal fraction would sit. A single ε is a name, and it is the wrong kind of name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay: the question anchors the search. *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."*
- The same essay, on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level version of Fig 9 :54-57 and it governs the present question directly.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

## 3. What licenses a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a universal exploration fraction would have to be either settled by a measurement that ranges all slots and scales at once, or imported from a decision frame that already holds it. The world sources do not supply it: Cohen et al. 2007 state that no general optimum is known and a general solution may not be possible. So the number is a `gap`, not an omission.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens the import rule: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Importing an ε into the framework would not add a dimension; it would rename an existing budget filter.

## 4. Why "information gain settles it" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several different sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

An information-gain reading has the same defect. Large information gain can arise from a live compressible gap, from an irreducibly noisy observation (the noisy TV problem recorded on the exploration-exploitation page), or from a channel that has decayed. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a scalar.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is why the routing claim cannot score itself by how much it explores.

## 5. Correction to my own first reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming the exploration fraction at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive. The budget and the residual are already held terms (Symbols table :38, Formula step 3); a fraction over them is a **binding** from the decision frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is treating that binding as figure-derived and as the verdict. The honest form is: the fraction is a filter for the budget, recorded as a `gap` for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Both the narrowed form and the original overstatement are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A canonical bandit algorithm's reputation can get the routing claim tried; it cannot settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Why the essays matter for "balance" specifically

The Library of Babel essay says the searcher's ignorance is the obstacle, not the size of the library, and that the search narrows as vocabulary deepens (opened this session). Transposed to this question: the obstacle is not the number of arms or the size of the space; it is the lack of a term for the bond between gap and probe. Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* A vocabulary that has only "explore" and "exploit" can name two states and cannot name the route between them. That is the shape the essays converge on.
