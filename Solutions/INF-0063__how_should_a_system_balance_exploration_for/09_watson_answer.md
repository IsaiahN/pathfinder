## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the ordinary request for a balance between seeking new information and acting on what is known. Option A: ship one scalar fraction, temperature or bonus coefficient as the answer. Option B: refuse the scalar as the verdict, route the choice per slot by the live residual, hold the two currencies apart, keep the three probe modes separate, and mark the number as `gap`. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** The gap between prediction and observation, `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`, always a slice and per slot. Arity: at least three, because there are two operations plus a routing assignment and a refusal bin, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Symmetry: both operations are ordered, not interchangeable; one consumes a settled prediction, the other aims a probe at a live gap. Scale: steps 1 to 5 close within one scale, step 6 crosses with `R_T`.
- **Domain 1, the single-agent loop.** Actors: Γ, b, a, o′, R per slot, boundary diff. Currency: residual and settled terms, priced in bits. Anchor: the ground as the only metric (Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric."). Question type: a partition of the gap into bins.
- **Domain 2, instrument and search.** Actors: instruments, closure(Γ), searchers, SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD. Currency: reach, independence and actions. Anchor: the ground, plus a proto-instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."). Question type: a union of three distinct questions that must not be read against one another.
- **Domain 3, the world decision frame.** Actors: arms, foragers, brains, niches. Currency: reward, energy, fitness, value of information. Anchor: the reward distribution, fitness or a measured outcome. This is a different closure and corroborates only.
- **Domain 4, habitat and distributed search.** Actors: searchers, habitat, introduced actors. Currency: independence and surplus. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."). Question type: union with Domain 2, with independence as one quantity read twice.
- **Order by dependency.** Route the gap before choosing the operation (Fig 5 bins precede the drive). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Settle before composing (Formula step 5). Describe R before picking a frame (Fig 9 :28 "any frame you pick will seem to fit"). Keep INWARD and OUTWARD as separate tracks (Fig 6 :31).

Strain test applied: the word "explores" in Fig 6 :26 describes search inside a closure, not the policy term, so no strain after re-census. The four bins plus a refusal bin in Fig 5 are different quantities from the two currencies in Fig 12, so no strain there either. One live strain remains, the operand type of the comparison operator, and it is named in the verdict section rather than smoothed over.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The scalar is refused as the verdict. The object that routes the choice is the residual per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22), sorted into kinds with a fifth refusal bin that no amount of sampling reaches (Fig 5 :11-14, Formula step 2). Bits and actions are two currencies that do not add, so information gain cannot score itself (Fig 12 :125-126, Fig 1 :27). The amount is set by a bargain rather than a threshold (Fig 5 :42-43). The word "explore" names three opposed operations that must be run as separate tracks (Fig 6 :29-31, :49-50). Acting on known is contact with a non-updating ground, not storage of a recording (Fig 10 :24, Fig 4 :25), and no operation is intrinsically good across residual changes (Fig 11 :49-51). The universal fraction is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than as silence (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). Mode `specified` on the separators, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and gap in front of the claim, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by handing them a filter dressed as a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the routing claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar reading can be withdrawn without touching the routing derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withdraw it. No primitive has to be removed from the library, since a refused term adds a refusal and removes only the standing to compose over it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates a compressible gap from an irreducibly noisy observation or a decayed channel would route slots that the current reading cannot; a measured common unit for bits and actions with provenance and a shadow test would make the comparison well typed; a figure re-census that finds a line governing the fraction would convert the `gap` to `specified`; a case in which SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD return the same verdict would break the three-mode separator. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
