Order by dependency: route the gap before choosing the operation; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle before composing (Formula step 5); describe R before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :28); keep INWARD and OUTWARD apart (Fig 6 :31).

One thing survives cleanly: the gap falls into kinds and the choice is a routing question rather than a mixture. That is the residue worth sending.
