# The Tether's answer: can biodiversity preserve ecosystem function under turnover?

**Mode: `specified` on the ecological claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the identity of any particular preserving species or trait. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not an ecological result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The residual is a measured function reading that must stay within a band while the species producing it and the relations among them change. R is indexed per function and per site, never pooled. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The question's own wording ("identities and interactions are continually changing") puts the changing things on the habitat side of Fig 11 :73 and the function reading on the ground side.

## 2. The shape

1. **The function reading is the ground.** The question is constitutively about it, and it must not move when frames reclassify which species matter (Fig 2 :29, :36, :30). The species and the interactions are the habitat, which updates and is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43, :73).
2. **Preservation is carried by an arrangement, not by a standing list.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49-50 makes valence relative to the residual and makes mutualism and parasitism relations rather than kinds. What holds is a functional group: Fig 12 :123 *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Members can turn over while the group and its bonds hold.
3. **The verdict is per function and per site.** A pooled index can hide a collapsing function (Fig 1 :22). The mechanisms form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition, so no single lever returns a universal verdict and verdicts are kept separate (Fig 9 :55-57).
4. **Richness is a filter, not a witness.** A count tells you how much search was spent, not what arrangement pays for the function (Fig 9 :60). The same goes for the literature: reviews of the same grassland experiments are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
5. **Managed preservation is permanent work.** The seat can introduce an actor but cannot subtract the habitat (Fig 11 :61-62), and the cascade of an introduction must be enumerated before it is made (Fig 11 :44, :65).
6. **The channel can fail while the ground does not.** Saturated metrics and pooled readings sever contact (Fig 10 :24, :26); the remedy is a better per-function instrument, not another review.
7. **The specific preserving species or trait is not returned.** This is a declared `gap`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32), and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134). What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a new primitive.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal "yes, diversity preserves function". The result is conditional on the arrangement and is a union, so an unqualified yes is the averaged verdict the figures forbid.
- Not a standing list of good or important species. Fig 11 :51 refuses it.
- Not that species are interchangeable in general. They are exchangeable only at the level of a named functional group and a named function; `06` Q1 separates this from strict isomerism.
- Not that richness is irrelevant. It is a legitimate budget filter. It is not a witness for the verdict.
- Not that an introduced species fixes a system permanently. Managed habitat work is asymmetric and ongoing.
- Not `grounded`. No site measurement was taken, and none of the world sources was used to settle the verdict.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the discriminators are:

- **Function discriminator:** does the per-function reading stay in band through a documented identity turnover while a comparator with the same richness but different functional groups fails? If the two never diverge, the arrangement claim is not doing work.
- **Source discriminator:** does a response-trait and interaction measurement identify which member was actually paying for the function in each interval, rather than inferring it from the flux alone? This is the spectrometer analogue; without it the species name is a metamerism error.
- **Anchor test:** does the verdict change when the set of "important" species is reclassified but the measured flux does not? If so, the anchor moved (Fig 2 :30) and the whole derivation is void.
- **Aggregation test:** do pooled multifunctionality scores and every per-function score agree through a function failure? If they never diverge, per-slot indexing is unnecessary here.
- **Union test:** does one intervention restore function where two or more conditions were missing, with no categorically different residue? If so, the union claim fails.
- **Standing-list test:** is there a universal list of named species that preserves a given function across sites and residuals? If so, Fig 11 :51 is refuted and the shape is withdrawn.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** all five conditions in the pre-registered refuter, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The functional-group vocabulary is held in the figures; the identity of the member that pays for a function in a particular place is not. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. A reviewer who wants the species name is asking for a ground reading, which this folder explicitly did not take.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "does biodiversity preserve ecosystem function?" with a universal yes. It says that the function reading is the ground and the species and interactions are the habitat, so a result about the habitat cannot be reported as a result about the ground. What survives turnover is not a standing list of good species (none survives a change of residual) but an arrangement: a functional group whose presence fixes the outcome, with the qualifier saying which member fills the slot. Members can change while the kind and its bonds hold. The verdict is per function and per site, the mechanisms form a union of overlapping conditions so no single lever settles them, and a richness count is where to look rather than what closes the question. In managed systems this is permanent work, since an actor can be introduced and the habitat cannot be subtracted. The framework does not name the species that pays for the function in your site; that is a gap to be closed by a measurement, not by a review. What it offers is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
