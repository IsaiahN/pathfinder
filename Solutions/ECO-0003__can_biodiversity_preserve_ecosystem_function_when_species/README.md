## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether diversity is good for ecosystems in the abstract. It is whether a measured function reading stays near its reference level, per function and per site, while the actors that produce it (species identities) and the relations between them (interactions) turn over.

- Option A, "function is preserved by the presence of particular good species": leads to the prediction that a standing list of species can be named and that preservation fails when those names disappear. Fig 11 :51 refuses a standing list of good actors.
- Option B, "function is preserved by an arrangement that survives turnover": leads to the prediction that preservation is a property of the functional group and the bond structure, that the identities filling the slots can change, and that the verdict is per function, never a single average (Fig 12 :123, Fig 1 :19).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a shape with three parts, (1) a non-updating ground reading of function per slot, (2) an arrangement (functional group plus response diversity) that remains across actor turnover, and (3) a discriminator that would distinguish preservation by arrangement from preservation by a lucky constant identity. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged, with the identity of the preserving species or trait declared a `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the ecological question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of biodiversity and ecosystem function.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Most of the biodiversity literature cited here draws on the same grassland experiments, so it is one witness, not many.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
