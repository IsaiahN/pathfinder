## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the preserving species. It constrains; it does not fill. Concretely, the shape is:

- a function reading that is a ground, per function and per site, and that does not move when the species list is revised;
- an arrangement (functional group plus independently varying responses) that survives a change of members;
- a union of overlapping conditions, so that no single lever returns a universal verdict;
- a maintainer seat in managed cases, with introduction available and exclusion not;
- an open slot where the species or trait would go.

`FUNCTION(???)under turnover` is a shape. `FUNCTION(bee species X)under turnover` is a name, and the framework did not produce that name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already known that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* In ecology terms: describe what would be observed if function were preserved under turnover, not the name of the species thought to cause it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks the reader which function they would measure before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

The residual was described in effect terms in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 before any ecological frame was chosen. That order is Fig 9 :27-28 and it is deliberate: had "diversity" been chosen first, any diversity metric would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question that means a site-level measurement that a given species or trait was what kept a named function in band through a documented turnover event, with a comparator that rules out a stable dominant and an outside subsidy.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Borrowing "response diversity" from ecology into this derivation is such a binding; it is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv` with author, venue, year and quote, and it does not become a figure result.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the error mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*, and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a stable function reading can be produced by several different species sets (similarity, complementarity, a temporarily dominant performer, an external subsidy). Naming one source from the function reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which in ecology means a response-trait and interaction measurement through the turnover, not another pooled regression.

## 4. Q1 — What licenses a particular species or trait in the slot?

**Nothing in the figures.** The framework returns a shape. A particular species or trait is licensed only by a ground settlement at the site or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is declared a `gap` in `01` F10, not hidden as a finding.

## 5. Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible)

I initially stated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming *any* element of an ecological answer. That was too broad. The figures README says *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. In this derivation the functional group and the bond vocabulary are already available from Fig 12 :123 and Fig 11 :47, so describing the arrangement as a functional group is a **binding onto held vocabulary**, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains ruled out is naming the *member* that fills the slot, because that member is not determined by the figures and would require the source-discriminating instrument of section 3. My metamerism point is **not** retracted: the function reading still does not determine which species set produced it. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming arrangement kinds. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers highly cited reviews to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried against a function measurement, and let that measurement settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. One consequence for the verdict

The frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Fig 5 :62: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."* The remedy is the discipline in Formula step "Direction": bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term and write down what would refute it before the run. That is what the five refuters in `01` section 5 and `08` section 3 do. An ecologist reading this packet is being asked to check those, not to endorse the framework.
