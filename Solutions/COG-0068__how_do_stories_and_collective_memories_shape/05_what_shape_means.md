## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the instance

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :138-139: *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."*

*The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The essay also says the searcher's own ignorance, not the size of the library, is the obstacle, and that the finish line is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* because the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For COG-0068 that means the deliverable is **not** a theory of which founding myth, which commemoration or which family story is the important one. The deliverable is a constraint any such account must satisfy. The shape here has four parts: a directional membrane, a bond structure, a valence split between verdict and convention, and a contact test. Each is checkable without naming a myth.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the target, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary was residual, membrane, import, bond, anchor and convention, and the world literature supplied the closer terms: hourglass filter, master narrative, provenance, lossy transform.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual in `01` was written as "what changes when a story is told", not "how does collective memory work".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the outreach email leads with five falsifiable effects rather than with the framework.
- Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The first shape decision was to refuse the single-question reading.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question Fig 2 :36-37 is unusually important: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Because the question is about people, structured human judgment and behaviour are a legitimate ground here, not merely an instrument. That does not make any particular researcher's opinion an anchor; it makes checkable judgments of conduct the anchor.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a specific story as the carrier is an import from the history or memory-studies frame. It is allowed if its provenance is recorded, and it adds no atom to the framework.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* A beautiful shared narrative is not a validator.

## 4. Why this question is unusually exposed to the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (not re-fetched; cited in the exemplar's canonical mapping, no quote used here) names metamerism: several different sources can produce the same reading. The same structure is here, and the framework's own opened essay says it directly. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* That is Fig 2 :40-41 applied to exactly this question, written by the framework's author before this derivation. My F2 converged on it independently from the figure lines, but this is **not** a second witness: same author, same evidence pool. It is a confirmation of internal consistency only.

The same essay gives the mechanism of transmission: Bartlett's retellings *"lost what didn't fit and became 'more familiar forms'"* until *"each participant's report of the story mirrored his or her own culture."* Nobody lied. The errors compounded in one direction. This is Fig 7 :12-13 *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* in human terms, and it is why F7 asks for a room that differs from the last and matches the residual the last one surfaced.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

My first draft treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming anything in this answer. That was an over-reading. The rule governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. The six-term substrate already holds states and structures, and the Symbols table already defines a person as a frame. Naming an existing concept such as "a reapplicable method" or "a convention" is not inventing a new primitive. What remains forbidden is:

- presenting a world-frame binding (this myth, this commemoration) as a figure derivation;
- claiming that such a binding moves the wall, when Fig 6 :21 says *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*;
- treating the shape as if it were the answer, when Fig 9 :32 says otherwise.

Both the over-reading and its correction are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequence for how to read the rest of this folder

`01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` return a shape and a route. They do not return a diagnosis of any community. Where they say a story is an import carrying bonds, that is a structural claim with a refuter, not an empirical finding about a named group. Any specific claim about a real memory, myth or institution would need a ground reading, a provenance record and a stated mode, and would have to be added by a frame that is actually in contact with that ground.
