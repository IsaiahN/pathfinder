## 0. The residual in effect terms

The formula defines the residual as `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot `s` (step 1). Translated into the effects named by this question:

- **Γ (the library)** is the person's currently expressible terms about what is right, what is wrong, who counts as "we", and what the world is like. Symbols table :111: *"A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."* A person is a frame in this framework, not a metaphor for one.
- **b (the belief)** is what the person takes to be the case before acting, formed from private history and inherited prior (Symbols :20-22).
- **a (the action)** is the telling, the ritual, the commemoration, the archive entry, the family story at dinner: another frame acting on this one.
- **o' (the observation)** is what actually happens when the person subsequently judges or aligns: who they help, who they exclude, which coalition they join, which ground they check.
- **R per slot** is the gap between what the story predicts about those things and what they turn out to be. It must not be averaged: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The slot "moral identity" and the slot "political belonging" are kept apart all the way through.

**What is being decided.** Two options. Option A: stories and collective memories reshape identity and belonging by supplying content, so the mechanism is one of belief replacement. Option B: they reshape it by changing which atoms and bonds the person's frame can compose with, and by relocating the person in a habitat, with the two slots governed by opposite rules about agreement. Each leads to different instruments and different failures.

**Arity / symmetry / scale of R.**

- **Arity: two conditions.** Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* This is a **union compound**, and Fig 9 :54 adds: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **Symmetry: ordered.** A story is a sequence, not a bag of themes. Fig 12 :71 *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* and :74 *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Retelling the same events in a different order is a different term, not a spelling of the same one.
- **Scale: crosses a membrane.** The story originates at the population scale and arrives at the person scale. Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* The transform is lossy (Fig 4 :27) and its round trip must be measured (Fig 4 :30).

**Partition or union overall: union.** The three domains are not three versions of one question. They are a dependency chain. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

**Dependency order (Fig 3).**

1. D1: the story must cross the membrane and be admitted before it can alter Γ.
2. D2: an altered Γ must be settled and must change contact before it can select or re-select a room.
3. D3: the room and habitat are re-selected last.

Dependency warning from Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* For D1 the instrument is a recall or behaviour measure; for D2 a structured judgment of moral action; for D3 an observable change in alliance, boundary or institution. If any of these is absent, that link is not weak, it is unread.

**Strain test applied before conclusions.** An apparent contradiction was noted between Fig 2 :40 (agreement disqualifies a verdict) and Fig 2 :41 (agreement is the product for a convention). Re-census shows they govern different quantities in different domains, not one quantity read twice. It is resolved by the union split, not smoothed over. A second apparent tension between Fig 4 :25 (a replayed recording produces no new success) and Fig 13 :130 (the record *is* the term) is also resolved by re-census: Fig 13's record is the lineage of atoms, bonds and route, while Fig 4's recording is the inert artefact of one occasion. Different objects. Both are named in §6.

## 2. Findings (figure line first, conclusion after)

### F1 — The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (mode: `general`)

> Fig 9 :4-5: **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**
>
> Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
>
> Fig 9 :57-58: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

A single search for "how stories shape moral identity and political belonging" will find whichever literature is loaded first and make it fit. Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** The two slots have different currencies and different anchors, so they get two loops and are never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Verdict: **union, unbundled**. Triangulated: Fig 9 (split rule) and Fig 2 (valence split, F2) are two different figure lines with low conceptual overlap. **Refuter:** if persistent effort makes both slots collapse into one residual with the same anchor and the same valence of agreement, the union claim fails.

### F2 — Correlation has opposite valence in the two halves (mode: `specified`, decisive for the shape)

> Fig 2 :39: **"Correlation has valence."**
>
> Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
>
> Fig 2 :41: **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**
>
> Fig 2 :43: **"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**

This is the governing line of the whole question. A shared founding story that unites a group is doing exactly what a convention should do: it produces shared meaning. Its agreement is the product, not a defect. The same story used to settle a verdict about the world, who is morally worthy, whose suffering counts, who may vote, has crossed the guard at Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** It is then a frame pretending to be an anchor, and Fig 2 :33 applies: **"The anchor must not update."**

Verdict: the apparent conflict between the two Fig 2 lines is not a strain after re-census, it is the answer. The two halves are governed by opposite valences of agreement. **Triangulated:** Fig 2 and Fig 10 are distinct figures; the venue is different (alignment versus ground maintainer). **Refuter:** exhibit a case where agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool raises the truth of a world-verdict at maximum independence. Under Fig 2 :40 this cannot count as corroboration; if it did, the valence rule fails.

### F3 — Stories shape by crossing a directional membrane as imports; a recording is not a method (mode: `specified`)

> Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
>
> Fig 4 :23: **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**
>
> Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**
>
> Fig 4 :25-26: **"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."**
>
> Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"**
>
> Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
>
> Fig 8 :45-46: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone. Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."**

A person cannot derive another person's memory from inside their own closure. The story is the contact with a frame whose closure differs, and the surplus it carries is an import. But what crosses is two different things depending on how it is carried. A compressed, reapplicable rule (a "head start") makes the recipient's next search cheaper. A fixed recording makes the recipient repeat an occasion. So the same story can increase reach or merely increase conformity, and the difference is not in its content but in what was carried across the membrane.

Fig 6 :12-13 names the two gates an import needs: **"source gate: provenance"** and **"shadow test: explains a gap"**. Without them, a borrowed value is indistinguishable from an independently reached one (F6).

Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 4 (membrane direction) and Fig 6/Fig 8 (import operator and surplus), which are low-overlap figure lines. **Refuter:** show a collective memory that changes a person's reachable moral terms without any difference between a replayable record and a reapplicable method, or show an import moving the wall with no contact with a differing closure. Either breaks F3.

### F4 — The bond carries the moral content, not the theme list (mode: `specified`)

> Fig 12 :46: **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**
>
> Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**
>
> Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**
>
> Fig 12 :112-113: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**
>
> Fig 12 :140: **"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."**

A story that lists traits ("we are brave, we are just") has vocabulary. It can name a state. It cannot name a route, because a route is a sequence (→); it cannot state a goal, because a goal is a comparison (⋛); it cannot say how far along it is, because progress is a subtraction (−); and, if it lacks negation (¬), it cannot carry a correction at all. Fig 12 :140 says what such a vocabulary can do: accumulate. For moral identity this predicts that a narrative with no refusal operator can only add obligations and grievances, never revise them. For political belonging it predicts that a master narrative without a correction path is rigid by construction.

Verdict: `specified`. The figure definition is single-source (Fig 12), but the prediction is independently anticipated by two low-overlap world sources (Bouizegarene et al. 2024 on narrative prediction and master-narrative rigidity; Garland et al. 2021 on the hierarchical sequence of whale song), so the *prediction* is corroborated while the *definition* remains single-source. **Refuter:** exhibit a story that changes observable moral behaviour and alliance while changing only vocabulary, with no change in sequence, comparison, subtraction or refusal. That would break the bond claim.

### F5 — Shaping is real only if contact changes (mode: `specified`)

> Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**
>
> Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
>
> Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
>
> Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**

A vivid identity narrative that changes how a person describes themselves but changes nothing they do, no relation they enter, no boundary they draw, has not changed anything in the framework's terms. Fig 11 :40 is a hard test, not a rhetorical preference. And the valence of a story is not intrinsic: the same founding myth that binds one group to its ground can bind another group against it. Benefactor and malfactor are relations to a residual, not kinds of story.

This also supplies the legitimate anchor. Fig 2 :36-37 makes human judgment the right anchor when the question is constitutively about people, and this question is. So the ground here is structured judgment of what people actually do, not the researcher's theory and not consensus among tellers. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11 (contact) and Fig 2 (anchor legitimacy). **Refuter:** show an identity change that reliably changes behaviour and alliance while leaving contact with the ground unchanged, or show a story whose valence is intrinsic to its content across different residuals. Either breaks F5.

### F6 — Collective memory is the lineage that makes the identity term checkable (mode: `general`)

> Fig 13 :127: **"The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable."**
>
> Fig 13 :128-129: **"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."**
>
> Fig 13 :130-131: **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."**
>
> Fig 13 :142: **"The seed was always the seedline."**
>
> Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

You cannot read a person's current self-story and tell which route produced it. Two people can hold the same value by convergent derivation (corroboration at maximum independence, Fig 8 :50) or by adopting the same import (authority). The contents are identical; only the lineage separates them. So "collective memory" has a precise figure reading: it is the record of atoms, bonds and route that makes an identity term verifiable, not the current statement of identity. This is the exact point where the framework's notion of record and the world literature's notion of memory source meet (Shaw 2016, Loftus: *"You need independent corroboration to know whether you're dealing with an authentic memory, or something that is a product of some other process."*).

Verdict: `general`, triangulated across Fig 13 (lineage) and Fig 8 (provenance), two different figures. **Refuter:** exhibit a case where a person's present identity is fully checkable from its current statement with no route record, or where an adopted import and a convergent derivation are distinguishable by content alone. Either breaks F6.

### F7 — A chain of agreeing tellers is one long room (mode: `specified`)

> Fig 7 :12-13: **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
>
> Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
>
> Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**

A collective memory passed down a chain of tellers who all agree gains vocabulary and loses nothing it did not already have. It is a mirror chain. Fig 7 :36 requires the next room to differ from the last and to match the residual the last one surfaced. So a healthy tradition is not one that repeats perfectly: it is one where each retelling is selected against what the previous telling failed to resolve. Cordonnier et al. (2022) supply the empirical shape: detail-rich memories are lost by the third generation while *"feelings of victimisation can seep from one generation to the next"*, exactly the lossy transform with valence surviving that Fig 4 and Fig 7 predict.

Verdict: `specified`. Single-source for the figure line (Fig 7); the world prediction is corroborated by a low-overlap field. **Refuter:** show a chain of retellings in which agreement at each step adds new ground-bearing evidence without any step differing in closure or matching a surfaced residual. That breaks F7.

### F8 — When a belonging convention decides an outcome, it becomes a false ground (mode: `specified`)

> Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
>
> Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**
>
> Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
>
> Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."**

This is the failure mode of political belonging. "We have always told this story, therefore this person is not one of us, therefore they may not decide" converts a convention into an authority that cannot be updated, which is Fig 2 :33's dead anchor. The world literature calls the same move the boundary-making function of myth: it strengthens bonds inside a group and draws borders against other groups at the same time. The figures say the same thing in different terms: the convention is efficient for shared meaning and fatal when it decides an outcome.

Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 10 and Fig 2, with an independent low-overlap corroboration from political sociology (Zubrzycki and Woźny 2020: collective memory in the *"creation, legitimation, and maintenance of national identities and nation-states"*). **Refuter:** exhibit a convention that continues to function as a convention, remaining revisable and non-decisive, while being used as the final authority on an outcome. That breaks F8.

### F9 — The framework cannot name the story (mode: `general`)

> Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
>
> Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**
>
> Fig 13 :138-139: **"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."**
>
> Fig 8 :39: **"A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

The derivation returns the shape: a directional import of bonds, a valence split between verdict and convention, a contact test, a lineage requirement. It does not return which story, which myth, which commemoration. Selecting one is a binding from the world frame onto a held shape, provenanced as such, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall. Where the search stops, it stops honestly: *"Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."* Verdict: **gap on the name, verdict on the shape**. Single-source for the figure phrasing (Fig 9), corroborated by the canonical usage rule. **Refuter:** derive a specific required story from the figures alone with no world import. Under Fig 9 :32 that is not available; if it were produced, the shape-not-a-name limit fails.

### F10 — Filters are not witnesses (mode: `general`)

> Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**
>
> Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."**
>
> Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

The number of people who find an account of collective memory moving, the number of citations it gathers, and the number of institutions willing to host it are all frame-internal quantities. They can buy the claim a budget to be tried. They cannot settle it. The same applies to this audit: its tidy shape is not evidence that the shape is true. Verdict: `general`. **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the popularity or institutional uptake of a memory narrative raises the truth of its world-verdict without a ground reading. That breaks F10.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion, carried unchanged)

Required by seat reasoning: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is withdrawn if any of the following fires:

1. **Union refuter.** Persistent effort collapses moral identity and political belonging into one residual with one anchor and one valence of agreement, contrary to Fig 9 :4-5 and Fig 9 :54.
2. **Valence refuter.** A shared narrative's agreement is shown to raise the truth of a world-verdict while the agreeing frames draw on one evidence pool, contrary to Fig 2 :40.
3. **Contact refuter.** A story changes a person's observable behaviour, alliances and boundaries while leaving contact with the ground unchanged, contrary to Fig 11 :40.
4. **Bond refuter.** A story changes moral and political conduct by altering vocabulary alone, with no change in sequence, comparison, subtraction or refusal, contrary to Fig 12 :111-113.
5. **Lineage refuter.** A person's identity is fully checkable from its current statement without the route that produced it, contrary to Fig 13 :128-130.
6. **Convention refuter.** A belonging convention used as the final authority on an outcome remains revisable and non-decisive, contrary to Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47.

None of these was fired in this simulated run. The run cannot fire them: it has no ground. They are recorded so an outside frame can.

## 4. Recommendation

1. **Unbundle before searching** (F1). Run one loop on the verdict half, one on the convention half, and never average them.
2. **Lead with the valence split** (F2). It is the strongest figure-grounded point: the same agreement is the product in one half and disqualifying in the other.
3. **Ask what crossed the membrane** (F3). Distinguish the reapplicable method from the replayable record before asking whether a story is good or bad.
4. **Demand the ground test** (F5). If nothing observable changed, report "no change", however vivid the narrative.
5. **Ask for the lineage, not the conclusion** (F6). Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them.
6. **Name the refusal operator** (F4). A narrative that cannot refuse can only accumulate. Ask what in a tradition is allowed to be falsified.
7. **Do not gather agreement as evidence** (F10). Use public, opt-in venues, ask what would refute the shape, and record the verdict before offering the theory.

## 7. Verdict of the primary seat

The shape survives. Stories and collective memories shape moral identity and political belonging by crossing a directional membrane as imports that carry bonds, not merely vocabulary; the two slots form a union with opposite valence of agreement; nothing has changed unless contact changed; and the identity term is checkable only through the lineage. What does not survive is any claim to name the story, any claim that agreement among tellers corroborates a world-verdict, and any claim that a belonging convention can decide an outcome and remain a convention. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The second seat's independent census is in `11_audit.md`.
