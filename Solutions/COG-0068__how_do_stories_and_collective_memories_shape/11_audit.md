## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold (Symbols :75-78). Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :39-43 (valence split and guard), Fig 4 :21-26 (directional filter, method versus recording), Fig 7 :12-13 and :36 (mirror chain, selection between rooms), Fig 12 :111-113 and :140 (bonds and refusal), Fig 13 :128-131 (lineage).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: 'stories import bonds across a filter, agreement splits by valence, contact decides, lineage verifies'.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a real community, for example 'this founding myth made us moral and binds us together'.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **refusal operator** (Fig 12 :140, Fig 5 :53) needed for a narrative to be corrigible; the shorthand says "bonds" and drops the one operator that is not a bond;
- the **contact requirement** (Fig 11 :40), which the shorthand compresses to "contact decides" and which a concrete story easily replaces with "people felt changed";
- the **lineage requirement** (Fig 13 :128-131), which the shorthand compresses to "lineage verifies" and which a concrete claim routinely drops in favour of the current self-report;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit** (Fig 9 :32), which a concrete claim about a named myth breaches unless the import is provenanced.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the two halves keep opposite valence of agreement and must not be averaged. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula shorthand as identical to the figure set would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
