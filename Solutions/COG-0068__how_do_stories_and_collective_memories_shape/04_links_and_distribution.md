## 2. What these sources do and do not do for the derivation

- **No source shares the framework's evidence pool.** None of these authors cites the Tether figures. Their agreement with the figure predictions is therefore cross-pool corroboration, not one pool talking. This is different from the exemplar's essays, which are by the framework author.
- **All nine corroborate predictions; none was allowed to set a verdict.** The verdicts in `09_watson_answer.md` are derived from figure lines first. The world sources were then asked what they predict here. If any had conflicted, the conflict would have sent the derivation back to the domain census.
- **One apparent conflict was found and resolved by re-census.** Cordonnier et al. report that for a narrative to thrive, *"factual accuracy is less important"*. That looks like a challenge to the figure rule that the ground decides. Re-census dissolved it: the world finding governs the convention half of the union (shared meaning is the product), while the figure rule governs the verdict half (agreement about the world is disqualifying). Different quantities, not a flipped verdict.
- **Two clusters share an evidence pool with each other.** Cordonnier, Zubrzycki and Lopez all draw on the memory-studies literature. Their mutual agreement is one witness for the belonging half, not three. The whale-song study, the SLSA spec, the SE folklore interviews and the information-bottleneck review are independent pools and are the stronger corroboration for the mechanism claims.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before someone has described the residual sends a recording. Lead with the split claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and **Fig 9 :60** — *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A receptiveness-ranked mailing list is a filter, not a witness.
- **Fig 2 :42** — *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is high for people whose belonging is at stake, so one-to-one contact through opt-in venues is the right spend.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-loop shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) only if asked, the method.

## 4. The two-stage offer, with the third stage

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as failure. This is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the three source links (figures text, formula, one worked packet). Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, on request.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. But `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output survives only if its refuters are honest and falsifiable. If a reader fires one, the shape is withdrawn. The point of the contact is to find that out, not to collect approval.
