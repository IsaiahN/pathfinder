## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; the system *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again"*.

For this question a **shape** is the set of conditions any candidate answer about who develops advanced AI must satisfy, stated before we can name which condition binds in which slot. The shape is: hardware, energy supply, data access and infrastructure are habitat, import channel, budget filters and channel to the ground; capability is agent-and-habitat; per-slot verdicts only. Filling the slot with "the best-funded lab wins" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Describe the effect, not the cause. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Enumerate the habitat rather than composing it. Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* List everything in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. For this residual that enumeration gives chips and fabs, power and cooling, corpora and curators, networks and instruments, not a league table of labs.
- Import, do not invent. From *They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A new data axis extends what can be perceived at all. This is why data access is not reducible to data volume.
- Stop where instruments stop distinguishing. The canonical rule: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* An instrument that distinguishes which of the four conditions binds in a given slot would name it. The CSET survey's self-report is not such an instrument: it is a channel reading from one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8). A physics result (Landauer) imported into the energy condition must carry its provenance, and it does; it corroborates, it does not decide.

Until one of these, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why naming a winning lab or region is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*, and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A benchmark score or a frontier-model announcement is the same kind of reading. It cannot distinguish a capability that came from hardware from one that came from energy, data or channel contact. Naming the winner from the score is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument per slot, not a name.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the data case: *"A pile of human reasoning's outputs is not an engine."* More of the same corpus cannot be mistaken for a new generator; this is Fig 8 :32 (idempotent closure) in the essay's vocabulary.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figure text. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
