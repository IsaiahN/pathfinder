## What an answer would look like (Direction)

Restated as an effect: `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` is indexed per developer-slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The question asks which slots can keep R small on advanced-AI capability tasks, and what the four named constraints do to that capacity. Option A: the constraints are chiefly a property of the agent (talent, cleverness), so who develops advanced AI is decided by who is smart enough. Option B: the four constraints are the habitat, the instruments, and the budget filters of the loop, so capability is a property of agent-and-habitat and the constraints decide contact and reach, not truth. Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term now: *the four constraints bound how much of the reachable space a developer can search and what the developer can represent at all; they do not settle whether a claim is correct*. That binding is written before the run and is refusable by the pre-registered falsifier in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3.
