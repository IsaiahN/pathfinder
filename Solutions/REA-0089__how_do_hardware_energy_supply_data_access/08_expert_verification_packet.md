# Expert verification packet — the Tether on AI infrastructure constraints

**What this is:** a claim in a form an infrastructure researcher, ML engineer or science-policy analyst can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to name which condition binds in which slot. This is a shape and a route, not a ranking.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Hardware, energy supply, data access and infrastructure constrain who can develop advanced AI by fixing the developer's habitat, budget and channel, not by deciding who is right. Capability is a property of developer-and-habitat, never of the developer alone, so an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing however much it improves. Hardware and energy price actions and bound how much of the search space is reached; data prices the description and is the only import that can add a primitive; infrastructure is the channel to a ground that does not decay while the channel does. The four conditions overlap into a union compound, so a single lever leaves intersections standing. Which condition binds in which slot is left open; the framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Capability is agent-and-habitat; the four conditions are the habitat

- **Statement:** an agent-only improvement, with hardware, energy, data and infrastructure contact held fixed, cannot move the ground on an advanced-AI capability task.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Also Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* The habitat is enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43).
- **Refuter:** produce an agent-only improvement, with the four contact conditions held fixed and measured, that moves an independent ground reading.

### C2 — Hardware and energy are budget filters, not verdict witnesses

- **Statement:** compute and energy bound how much of the reachable space is searched. They price actions; data prices the description; the two prices do not add and neither settles a claim's truth.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :127 *"and the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all."* Energy is a finite substrate state: Fig 13 :14 *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* and Fig 13 :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*
- **What to check:** does a change in compute or power, with ground and closure held fixed, change only how much was searched, or does it flip the verdict? Is the dollars accounting being silently summed with a token accounting?
- **Refuter:** a budget change shown to determine the ground verdict rather than only the extent of the search; or a single accounting demonstrated to predict both prices with no residual.

### C3 — Data is the wall-moving import, and infrastructure is the channel

- **Statement:** more of the same corpus cannot add a primitive, because closure is idempotent; a low-overlap data frame can. Infrastructure failure is channel failure against a ground that does not decay.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :46 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :49 *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*.
- **What to check:** did the claimed capability gain come from a genuinely new data axis (a source gate and a shadow test), or from more of the same corpus? Was the failure a saturated or pooled metric rather than a change in the ground?
- **Refuter:** reach expanding by internal composition alone with no imported atom; or a high-overlap corpus giving the same union surplus as a low-overlap one; or capability rising solely because a channel degraded.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking of labs, firms or regions, and not a prediction of who wins. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that compute, energy, data or infrastructure is the single binding constraint. The conditions form a union (Fig 9 :55).
- Not that talent is irrelevant. Capability is agent-and-habitat, never either alone (Fig 11 :39).
- Not that a bigger model or corpus is thereby a better answer (Fig 8 :32).
- Not `grounded`. No slot-level measurement settles which condition binds.
- Not that this simulated derivation can validate itself. *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument per slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Binding test:** vary each of the four conditions with the others held fixed and read R against an independent ground. A researcher self-report is a channel reading, not this instrument.
2. **Filter test:** does the budget change only the extent of search, or flip the verdict?
3. **Import test:** does a low-overlap data frame add a primitive while a high-overlap corpus does not?
