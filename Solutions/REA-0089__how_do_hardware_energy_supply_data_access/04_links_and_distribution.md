## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the shape claim and its refuter. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any article.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. Do not select recipients for receptiveness.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A list chosen for fame or likely assent is a budget filter, not a witness.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The method may be given away; a recording of a conclusion sent to a list is a description of one occasion, not a generator.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the three falsifiers; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then, if curious, the three source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
