# Outreach draft — check the shape first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 before showing any source or theory.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 2 survives the pre-registered refuter in section 3. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

---

**Subject:** Check one shape claim about AI infrastructure before you decide whether the theory is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** hardware, energy supply, data access and infrastructure constrain who can develop advanced AI by fixing the habitat and the budget of the developer, not by deciding who is right. Three pieces of it are checkable:
>
> 1. Capability is a property of the developer-and-habitat, never of the developer alone. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves. In this case the habitat is the four named conditions.
> 2. Compute and energy are budget filters. They price actions and bound how much of the reachable space gets searched; they do not settle a verdict. Data is the import that moves the wall, and more of the same corpus adds no new primitive.
> 3. Infrastructure is a channel to a ground that does not decay. The channel does decay, and a lab's failure to convert resources into capability is usually a channel failure, not a truth failure.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any one of the four is the single binding constraint; that a bigger model or a bigger cluster is thereby a better answer; that a named lab, firm or region wins. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Which condition binds in which slot is left open until a source-discriminating instrument says so.
>
> **Written down before this message, so they are real falsifiers:**
> 1. An agent-only improvement, with hardware, energy, data and infrastructure contact held fixed, that moves the ground on an advanced-AI capability task.
> 2. A budget filter, with ground and closure held fixed, shown to flip the verdict of a claim rather than only how much of the space was searched.
> 3. Reach expanding by internal composition alone with no imported atom, or a high-overlap corpus giving the same surplus as a low-overlap one.
>
> If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority. Please tell me what you would count as a failure before I send anything else.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** it is a loop with a pre-registered refuter. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, verdict recorded before theory | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for agreement would manufacture the disqualifying correlation. |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Per-slot, never averaged | Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* |
| No single binding lever | Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* |
| No named winner | Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; the canonical rule is a shape, not a name. |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask anyone to endorse it. |
| Offer the method, not the answer | Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask recipients to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check *"the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* |
