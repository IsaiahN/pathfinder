## QUESTION

How do hardware, energy supply, data access and infrastructure constrain who can develop advanced AI? Restated as an effect: why does `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` stay small on advanced-AI capability tasks in some developer-slots and grow in others? Option A: the four constraints are chiefly a property of the agent, so who develops advanced AI is decided by talent. Option B: the four constraints are habitat, import, budget filters and channel, so they decide contact and reach, not truth. What each leads to: A sends a talent-only verdict with no nameable anchor and refuses Fig 11 :40; B sends a checkable union shape, per-slot verdicts and a declared gap on which condition binds. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: four overlapping conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55), not one hard problem. Symmetry: capability is a threshold relative to competitors, an ordered comparison bond rather than a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87). Scale: single developer loop, organization population, global supply chain, with a lossy membrane between scales.
- **Domain 1, developer loop.** Actors: researchers and their Γ. Currency: residual reduction and minted terms. Anchor: the ground, benchmarks and real-world tasks. Admitted, not sufficient alone.
- **Domain 2, compute and energy substrate.** Actors: chips, fabs, GPUs, memory, grid, cooling. Currency: energy as a finite state and gradient as a spendable structure, composing into flow; bits and actions as two non-additive prices. Anchor: thermodynamics, the physical ground. Admitted as budget filter.
- **Domain 3, data and imports.** Actors: datasets, corpora, sensors, curators. Currency: atoms imported into closure(Γ); independence between frames. Anchor: the world's ground. Admitted as the only wall-moving operator.
- **Domain 4, infrastructure and channel.** Actors: networks, cloud, review, distribution, instruments. Currency: contact and bracket fidelity. Anchor: the ground maintained, which does not decay. Admitted as channel.
- **X1 talent as free-floating property.** Actors: the leader. Currency: talent. Anchor: none nameable. Absorbed into D1's agent-only reading, refused by Fig 11 :40. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 compute as single all-purpose lever.** Absorbed into D2 and D4; a filter, not a verdict (Fig 9 :60).

Union, not partition: D2, D3 and D4 overlap, so each overlap is a compound with its own loop and a single lever leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :57). Dependency order: data access -> hardware and energy -> channel -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Strain test applied: the CSET survey self-report conflicts with a compute-first reading; re-census shows it is a channel reading from one evidence pool, not a competing ground, so no figure line was strained and no verdict flipped.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The four constraints decide who gets to run the search and what the search can represent; they do not decide who is right. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so hardware, energy supply, data access and infrastructure are the habitat that determines who can develop advanced AI. As budget filters they bound reach, not truth (Fig 9 :60). Energy is a finite, spendable substrate (Fig 13 :14, :76). Data is the only import that moves the wall, and low-overlap data gives a union surplus (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :46). Infrastructure is the channel to a ground that does not decay (Fig 10 :24). The conditions form a union ordered by dependency, so one lever leaves intersections standing. Which condition binds in which slot is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Verdicts were kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected policy and a wasted budget. A compute-only intervention is applied to a channel- or data-bound lab, or a talent-only intervention to an energy-bound one, and a single lever is used on a union that needs the right compound. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, and it can concentrate power if the intervention is another large centralised cluster.
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing; the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its three falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). No substrate term has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** an agent-only improvement with the four contact conditions fixed that moves the ground; a budget change shown to flip a verdict rather than only the extent of search; reach expanding by internal composition with no imported atom; or a source-discriminating instrument naming which condition binds in a slot. Agreement or prestige is not such an instrument.
