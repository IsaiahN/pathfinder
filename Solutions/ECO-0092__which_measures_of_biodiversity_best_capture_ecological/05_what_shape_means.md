## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Its companion line :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of a measurement protocol that can be stated before a winning index is named. It constrains: three readouts, one per target, per slot, low overlap, ground-anchored, provenance recorded. It does not fill: it does not say "use Shannon" or "use Faith's PD."

The same essay names this question's exact failure mode: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Ecological difference, evolutionary history and functional importance are such a bundle. That is the single most directly applicable essay sentence in the whole set.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the closest held vocabulary is "index," "profile," "facet," "slot" and "ground."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect description of the residual is: a readout that moves when lineage changes but not when counts change, and another that moves when a process changes but not when either first readout changes.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what the recipient would expect to see before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For this question that would mean the three readouts jointly leave no residual on any target for the communities in question. No dataset here reaches that condition.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A monitoring outcome, experiment or before-and-after measurement on the target process licenses the name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Calling a Hill number of order 1 "the diversity" is an import from information theory into ecology; Jost 2006 supplies the provenance and the shadow test is whether it explains the gap the richness-only readout left.

*They're Scanning Books* (mapped, not re-opened): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also says *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* That applies here: the three targets are a human's careful guess at which axes matter in biodiversity measurement, not nature's own list. Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. Why the scalar index is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism directly. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved it. The essay's conclusion: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

A single diversity score reads a projection and names its source as "biodiversity." That is the metamerism error: several underlying configurations (richness up and evenness down, or lineage depth up and abundance concentrated) can give the same scalar, so the scalar cannot say which happened. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, that is, the three separate readouts, not a better argument for one number.

Jost 2006 is the closest real instrument in the literature. Its order q turns one reading into a profile: *"The order of a diversity indicates its sensitivity to common and rare species."* It does not name a single winner; it makes the loss visible by showing which part of the frequency distribution a given index emphasizes. That is precisely Fig 6 :32's instrument-improvement pattern.

## 5. Q2: what licenses a particular index for a target?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular index is licensed by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

This question differs from QMGR-0037 in one useful way: there is no "which of the six?" puzzle here. The ecology frame already holds the index names. So the honest form of an answer is *"for target T, under condition C, we bind index I, provenance P, ground reading G owed"* rather than *"the best measure of biodiversity is I."*

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3 and to F10 of `01`

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade mentioning any index. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the frame. Richness, Hill numbers, Faith's PD and Rao's Q are already in the ecology frame, so naming one is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). Retracted as applied to those index names.

I also withdrew in `01` F10 a mapping of the three targets onto network / spectrum / gradient. That mapping was an adopted import with no source gate or shadow test, and it is recorded rather than erased. What remains is the structural rule: frames that answer different questions and draw on different evidence can be counted separately; frames that overlap are one witness.

Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well known names to agree that one index is best is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a monitoring programme or an experiment, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any index and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
