## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :5 and :55 (split the union), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per slot), Fig 13 :35-39 (member independence) and Fig 11 :49 (valence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: split the question, keep three low-overlap readouts, report per slot, bind names with provenance, let the ground decide.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete monitoring programme that must choose three named indices, set an overlap threshold, define a focal process and buy a field campaign.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **which three indices** to use for the three targets (no figure line names one);
- **the overlap threshold** at which two readouts count as one witness (Fig 2 :31 defines the quantity, not the cutoff);
- **the focal process and habitat** that define functional importance (Fig 11 :49 makes importance relative but does not choose a residual);
- **the instrument** that would settle a binding (Fig 6 :32 requires an existing proto-instrument, none is supplied by the figures).

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must be kept per slot and the readouts must be counted by shared evidence, not by name. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
