**Source:** ecology.txt line 113

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that a named index (Shannon, Simpson, Faith's PD, Rao's Q) is *the* winner the framework derives.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Teaching the framework before asking for a verdict spends the one thing the outside frame contributes.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid that practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute the shape claim, not a purchased list of believers. Send the residual and the refuter first. Record their verdict before showing the theory. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
