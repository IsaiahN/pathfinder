## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: which measure of biodiversity best captures ecological difference, evolutionary history and functional importance? Option A: name one winning index and report it. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the name until a ground reading licenses a binding. What each leads to: A collapses three conditions into one ordering, hides the live slot signal, and treats correlated readouts as corroboration; B sends a checkable protocol shape, written refuters, and an open name slot until the ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A readout chosen for one biodiversity target fails to register a change in another, and the failure is invisible when the three are averaged or ranked into one score. Arity: three conditions over one species list. Symmetry: the targets are not readings of one quantity, so there is no lossless symmetric conversion between them. Scale: first order within one community and one target, second order at the decision about which combination to report. Fig 9 :55 names it a union compound: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Domain 1, the measured community.** Actors: species, individuals, lineages, traits, functions. Currency: presence, abundance, branch length, trait values. Anchor: a field measurement that does not move when the index is changed (Fig 2 :29). Question type: partition of slots within one habitat.
- **Domain 2, the measurement frame.** Actors: indices (richness, Shannon, Hill numbers of order q, Faith's PD, Rao's Q, functional richness / evenness / divergence). Currency: description cost versus residual removed (Fig 5 :42-44). Anchor: the ground pays for a term; a gate is not the ground (formula step 5). Question type: binding from the ecology frame onto held terms, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: external ecology reviewers, monitoring programmes, journals. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35, :40). In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before choosing an instrument (Fig 11 :43-44); state the objective before the index (Fig 3 :20); refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

No domain ended in `gap` after the census. The only unreachable item is the name of a winning index, which is a reach verdict of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported in the VERDICT rather than as silence (Fig 13 :134).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. The question is a union of three conditions over one species list, so split before choosing an instrument. Keep three readouts, one per target, reported per slot, counted as separate witnesses only when their overlap is low and measured. Treat functional importance as a relation between a species and a named process in a named habitat, with the habitat enumerated by contact and followed outward. Over the shared species set the facets stand as isomers or at minimum one functional group, and a diversity profile parameterised by order q is a spectrum term, not a fourth facet. The framework returns a shape, not the name of an index; which index wins is a `gap` owed to the ground. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A wrong binding is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold the name. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet and no primitive has to be withdrawn. If one of the three readouts is shown redundant, drop it and re-run the overlap test.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A representative community dataset in which no two targets ever move in opposite directions dissolves the union; an injective aggregate map from the joint slot vector refutes the per-slot rule; a species whose importance ranking is invariant across habitats refutes the valence rule; a monitoring outcome or experiment naming one index that settles all three targets with no residual left would fill the name slot and close the gap. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
