## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried from the exemplar): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form of a coupled-process answer that can be stated before any process is named. It constrains a candidate answer without filling the list. Three constraints are already visible:

- the answer must treat the residual as a union compound, not a list;
- it must locate the missed effect in a typed coupling bond, not in a process name;
- it must describe the per-slot residual before choosing a frame, because an undirected union is mute.

A named coupling such as ice-albedo feedback is a **name**. "A persistent per-slot coupled residual with differing closures whose decisive cause may be third order" is a **shape**. The frame returns the second, not the first.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* For Earth-system coupling this is the method that turns a vague "combined effect" into a checkable list of contacts.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

The three-question close in `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 is kept distinct here. SEARCHED asks whether the discriminator is already in the closure (it is: Fig 5, Fig 8, Fig 9). INWARD sharpens the per-slot instrument from one already returning something. OUTWARD imports a frame whose closure predicts the residual's shape. INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and are never read against one another. Importing against a budget limit would add an atom not needed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For a coupled pair, this means a discriminating measurement that fails if the claimed coupling is absent.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning one into *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. This is why the solution never maps atmosphere to space and ocean to energy; re-census assigns the six to the physical substrate and uses Fig 4 :19-20 (*"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*) as the carrier.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; it could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a single "combined effect" reading can arise from several distinct couplings. A science that reports the reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources has named a source from a projection. What is owed is a better per-slot instrument, not a confident name. The external literature supplies the same warning in a different vocabulary: Zoller et al. 2026 report *"the risk of information loss inherent in commonly used aggregation approaches."* That is one witness from a different pool, corroboration only.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* Prestige is not action: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework give for "which Earth-system processes are still studied too separately"?** It gives the separator, not the list:

- a union-compound reading, so the answer is not a set of independent entries;
- a bond reading, so the missed effect is a typed relation rather than a process;
- a mute-union warning, so "study them together" is not an answer without a described residual;
- a per-slot discriminator: persistent, non-trending coupled residual plus frames that do not share an evidence pool;
- a habitat enumeration outward to the third order;
- a `gap` for the literal names, with an instrument owed.

**Does the solution commit the error the README forbids?** No, with one qualification. It does not name a process as figure-derived. It does mention named couplings as bindings from the Earth-system frame, provenanced as such. The qualification is section 5b.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any coupling at all. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. A coupling built from already-settled component terms is not a missing primitive; Fig 12 :104 says *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* Naming such a binding is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). What remains forbidden is claiming that the figures themselves select that binding. Both the narrowed rule and the earlier overstatement are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact and for this document's status

**Prestige is not action.** A campaign that gathers respected names to agree that a coupling is understudied is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an instrument or a published record, settle it. Public institutional and opt-in channels are used in `03_public_contact_points.csv`; no personal emails, no receptiveness rank, no bulk send.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulated approximate answer. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the shape and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on. Fig 8 :37: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit. Chaitin: beyond a fixed constant, no system proves that any object exceeds its own complexity."*

**Indiscernibility is not identity.** The README's closing discipline: the route is recorded so the stop is checkable. A process that has not yet registered as a coupled residual is not proven absent; it is unreached at this budget.
