Currency: within-slot prediction error. Anchor: a measurement or reanalysis in that discipline. Question type: partition boundary inside each discipline. - **Domain 2, coupled modelling and assessment.** Actors: coupled Earth-system models, scenarios, assessment bodies, intercomparison projects. Currency: joint-state prediction skill and reproduced feedbacks. Anchor: the observed combined outcome, which does not move when a model changes its mind (Fig 2 :33). Question type: union compound built out of Domain 1 components.
- **Domain 3, the habitat / field of contact.** Actors: conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency), cascades. - **Partition or union:** union. Component models and coupled models overlap; the habitat is sampled by both. A single-discipline lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator survives; the list does not. The combined-effect question is a union compound whose residual lives in a typed coupling bond, indexed per slot and enumerated outward through the habitat because the decisive effect can be third order. An undirected interdisciplinary union is mute until a residual is described, so "study them together" is not an answer. The substitute is a two-part discriminator: a coupled residual that persists and does not trend down under component-only work, plus frames that do not share an evidence pool. The literal process list is a `gap` with a per-slot discriminating instrument owed, reported as unreached rather than silence. One live type strain is named (Fig 12 :87 versus :104). Verdicts per domain are separate and are never averaged into a global integration score. Mode `specified` on the shape, `general` on the separator, `gap` on the list, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and the gap before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is handing a filter a verdict, for example ranking processes by publication count (Fig 9 :60), and spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 4 :25). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no figure line is reinterpreted. The literal list can be withheld without touching the separator, and a discriminating measurement can later close one slot and narrow the gap with provenance. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument that discriminates one named coupling would close a member of the gap; a demonstration that a combined effect is fully recovered by a sum of separate predictions (Fig 12 :5 refuted), that all putatively coupled frames share one evidence pool (Fig 8 :49 refuted), or that the decisive residual is always first order (Fig 11 :52 refuted) would break the shape; a figure-derived rule naming a process would close the gap; a figure re-census showing the union rules govern a different object would re-census the domain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
