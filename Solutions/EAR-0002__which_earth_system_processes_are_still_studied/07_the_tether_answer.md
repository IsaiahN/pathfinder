# The Tether's answer: which Earth-system processes are still studied too separately?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A joint Earth-system prediction fails when the contributing processes are represented separately, even though each separate representation predicts its own slot well. The missed effect is not recovered by listing, summing or averaging the parts. The question's own form, "which processes," asks for a list; the figures return a separator.

The residual is indexed per coupled slot: which outcome, which place and season and time window, which pair of processes. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global "integration score" is a frame-internal quantity, not a verdict (Fig 1 :27).

It is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a list. Fig 9 :55-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* It has its own loop and its own ground. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

The residual must be described before the frame is chosen (Fig 9 :27-28).

3. **The discriminator is per-slot and two-part.** A process pair counts as "studied too separately" when (a) a coupled prediction for that slot keeps failing and does not trend down under more component-only work, and (b) the two frames do not draw on the same evidence at source. 4. **Enumerate the habitat outward; the decisive residual can be third order.** Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52: *"And contact cascades. ## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer that resolved it, not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

- **Per-slot discriminator.** A measurement that fails when a claimed coupling is absent and succeeds when it is present, for one named place, season and variable pair. This is the owed instrument for the `gap`.
- **Persistence test.** Does the coupled residual keep failing and refuse to trend down under component-only work (Fig 5 :58-60)? If it trends down, that slot is not "still studied too separately."
- **Independence test.** Do the two process frames draw on different evidence at source (Fig 8 :48-49), or are they one frame with two names?
- **Cascade test.** Does the residual lie beyond direct first-order contact, and where does the cascade stop mattering (Fig 11 :44, :52)?
- **Standing refuters from `01`:** full bond recovery by a sum of parts; universal shared evidence pool; direct-contact sufficiency; a figure-derived list; re-census failure.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

It says that the combined-effect question is a union compound, not a list: a single-discipline lever clears one condition and leaves the intersections standing. The missed effect lives in the typed coupling bond, which an ingredient list does not record, so a feedback cycle and a forcing-then-response are different coupled outcomes over the same processes. The literal list is a declared gap, with a per-slot discriminating instrument owed. Refuters were written before the run, and nothing here is settled by agreement.
