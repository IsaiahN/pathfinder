## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold."* Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union compound), Fig 12 :5 and :46-47 (bond carries the arrangement), Fig 8 :19-21 (mute union), Fig 11 :43-54 (habitat enumeration and cascade), Fig 13 :134 (gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in the folder: treat the question as a union, locate the residual in a bond, describe it per slot, enumerate the habitat, report the list as a gap, ask for a refuter.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete answer to the original question: a ranked list of named processes with a coupling index.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **the mute-union condition.** The shorthand "study them together" loses the requirement that a residual be described first; without it, the union is mute (Fig 8 :21).
- **the per-slot indexing.** A global coupling index collapses the slot structure that Fig 1 :19 and :22 say is the only signal (corroborated from a different pool by Zoller et al. 2026).
- **the habitat cascade.** A direct-pair list loses the third-order residual that Fig 11 :52 makes decisive.
- **the gap itself.** A request for names tends to convert a declared `gap` into an invented list, which is exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a combined effect is carried by a typed bond over several processes, and the question is a union compound. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
