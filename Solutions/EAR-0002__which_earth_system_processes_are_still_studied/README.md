## The answer in one line

The residual is a union compound, not one hard question and not a list. The combined effect lives in the coupling bond, which a list of separately studied processes does not record. An undirected interdisciplinary union is mute until a residual is described, so "studied too separately" cannot be answered by a ranked list of process names: no figure line returns that list. What the figures return is a discriminator, a shape: a persistent per-slot coupled residual whose two process frames do not share an evidence pool, enumerated outward through the habitat until the cascade stops mattering. Verdict: `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the literal list, `general` on the structural separator, not `grounded`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35)
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163; the figure text states the equivalent at Fig 2 :40)
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42)

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
