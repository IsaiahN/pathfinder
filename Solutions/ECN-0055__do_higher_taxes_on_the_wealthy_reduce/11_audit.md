## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unassigned change in the composition of effort and capital at the top of the distribution when the top rate rises. Arity: at least four overlapping channels (real effort, avoidance, bargaining, rent-sector entry), so a **union compound**, not a two-way choice. Symmetry: broken, because real investment, rent capture and concentration are different readings with different instruments. Scale: slot, habitat, rule stratum, with a lossy transform between. This matches the primary's R.
- **Domain 1, effort and investment.** Actors: top earners, executives, inventors, investors per slot. Currency: after-tax return, hours, capital committed. Anchor: observed real investment. Question type: per-slot effect, union member.
- **Domain 2, rent extraction.** Actors: rent-seekers, incumbent firms, lobbyists, bargaining teams. Currency: rents, pay for luck, concentration. Anchor: measured rent activity and market structure. Question type: per-slot with habitat dependence, union member.
- **Domain 3, rule and habitat.** Actors: legislature, tax authority, enforcement, governance. Currency: rules and conventions. Anchor: the ground the policy claims to serve. Question type: habitat to be enumerated, never composed.
- **Domain 4, measurement.** Actors: estimators and datasets. Currency: elasticities and intervals. Anchor: a ground that must not update on the frame. Question type: instrument, upstream by dependency.

Order by dependency: instrument before habitat before per-slot readings before aggregate. I derived this from Fig 3's dependency chain and Fig 11's enumeration rule; the primary derived it from the same figures plus Fig 9's residual-first rule. Different entry figures, same ordering.

**Where my census differs from the primary's.** I initially treated Domain 3 as a fixed background condition, so the rule stratum sat outside the loop. The primary treats the rule change as moving the room itself (Fig 10 :71-72). Re-census: the intervention *is* a rule change, so D3 cannot be a fixed background. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-58 (union), Fig 1 :19/:22 (per slot), Fig 12 :125 (two currencies), Fig 3 :27 (instrument), Fig 10 :72 (room follows question), Fig 13 :134-135 (gap as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: it is a union, effects are per slot, currencies do not add, direction is a gap, the instrument is missing, investment and rent-seeking are isomers over a fixed atom set.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the answer a reader might try to bring back down: a number for how much investment falls, or a verdict that one channel dominates.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim. The shorthand says "isomers"; the concretisation quietly treats the atom set as given in the world. If enforcement or a new market enters, the relation is composition plus import, not isomerism.
- the **habitat dependence** of actor valence. The shorthand says "per slot"; the concretisation easily turns this into a fixed taxonomy of taxpayer types.
- the **regime condition** on any measured response. The shorthand says "recordings, not generators"; the concretisation reaches for a historical elasticity as if it were structural.
- the **source gate**. The shorthand says "primary over summary"; the concretisation reaches for the most readable secondary claim, which in this case was the one that inverted the primary.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union structure with separate currencies and a recorded direction gap. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
