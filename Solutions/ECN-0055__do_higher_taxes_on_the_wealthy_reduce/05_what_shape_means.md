## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened directly): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The same essay gives the bundle passage that governs ECN-0055 directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> a union of overlapping channels, indexed per slot, with incommensurable currencies, whose relative magnitude is not reachable with the instruments held here.

The name "higher taxes mainly reduce rent-seeking" would be a filling, not a shape, and it is refused by F4 of `01`.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start from the ordinary terms "investment", "rent", "avoidance", "bargaining", then narrow by the slot and currency structure rather than picking a side.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect description here is "change in composition of effort and capital at the top when the rate changes", not "incentives" or "supply-side response".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach asks for the instrument rather than for agreement.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the gap in `01` F4, stated as a verdict.

## 4. Why the scalar "mainly" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* — one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The elasticity of taxable income is exactly such a projection. It reads one green: reported income fell when the rate rose. It cannot say whether the source was real effort, avoidance, bargaining, or rent capture. Saez, Slemrod and Giertz's critical review (opened 2026-11-23) says the elasticity is a sufficient statistic only under assumptions and that other parameters must be estimated when it is not. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a verbal argument about which source is more plausible.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: Why does naming the two channels not violate "shape, not a name"?

**Answer: it does not.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. "Productive investment" and "rent-seeking" are both already available bindings from the economics frame; they are atoms in the census, not new primitives the derivation invented. Naming them is provenance. What would be forbidden is inventing a seventh channel and claiming the framework derived it, or claiming that naming one moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

## 5b. Correction to my own reading (kept visible)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the very act of calling the two effects by their ordinary economic names. That was an overstatement, the same overstatement corrected in the exemplar's `05` §5b. The narrowed rule is: shape-not-a-name governs **missing primitives**, not **bindings onto atoms already held**. The genuine error in this folder is not naming the channels; it is any attempt to name the *dominant* channel before an instrument discriminates them. That error remains refused in F4.

The metamerism point of §4 is **not** retracted by this correction. The reason the scalar answer is refused is still the missing discriminator, not a general ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of prominent tax economists who agree with a shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried, and to name the instrument that would settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the tax claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
