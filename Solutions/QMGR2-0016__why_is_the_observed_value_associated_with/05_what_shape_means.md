## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the four-part constraint stated in the manifest: which side is the anchor, how many conditions are bundled, what the cross-scale transform drops, and which instrument would discriminate the sources. It constrains an acceptable answer without supplying a value. "Observed value is the anchor, the question is a union, the comparison is lossy" is a shape. "Lambda equals such and such a number" or "the mechanism is unimodular gravity" is a name, and the framework does not return names.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *The Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay form of Fig 9 :4-5.
- Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."**
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a name would be a specific value of the cosmological constant or one of the competing mechanisms. World sources show those mechanisms are live and mutually disputed: the dedicated Wikipedia page lists unimodular gravity, trace-free Einstein gravity, light-front quantisation, the Cohen-Kaplan-Nelson bound, fluctuating-vacuum models and anthropic selection, with no figure line selecting among them. The shape is what all acceptable answers must satisfy; the name awaits a ground that currently fails to discriminate.

## 4. Why a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument, is owed

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several underlying causes return the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What resolved the colour case was the spectrometer, not an argument; the essay says *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the projection is the single headline number. A Planck cutoff, a dimensional regulator and a properly renormalised free theory all return a "vacuum energy", but the reading does not determine which source computation produced it. The measured Lambda is not a source; it is a projection of the expansion history onto one parameter. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among the candidate sources, not another fit to the same projection.

This is why F7 in `01` reports `gap` on the value rather than a mechanism. A gap in this sense is a narrowed place where a discriminator must go, not an excuse to stop.

## 5. Q1 and Q2 - answered from the shape

**Q1: Does the framework imply the theoretical estimate is simply wrong?** No. It implies the estimate is frame-internal and regulator-dependent, while the observation is the anchor (Fig 2 :29-30; `01` F1). A frame-internal return can be perfectly correct *within its frame* and still not compose into the IR reading without a lossy transform and a source-gated import. "Wrong" is the wrong category; "not the same quantity at the same scale" is the figure-governed one.

**Q2: Does the framework imply dark energy must be a cosmological constant rather than a field?** No. Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."** That is a constraint on how a term composes; it does not select which term nature realised. Dynamical dark energy, modified gravity and a true constant are all names, and naming one would exceed what the figures return. The world sources record that the observations still fail to discriminate (DESI's public press release is titled *"New DESI Results Strengthen Hints That Dark Energy May Evolve"*), which is the empirical form of the same stop.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
