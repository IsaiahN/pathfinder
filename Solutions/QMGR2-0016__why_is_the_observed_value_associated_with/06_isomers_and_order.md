## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are U1, U2 and U3 a partition or a union?

- Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**; it is a specific compound.
- U1 (why the vacuum gravitates at all), U2 (why the renormalised value is so small relative to the cutoff) and U3 (why the density is comparable to matter today) overlap: the same measured Lambda appears in all three, but the grounds differ. A vacuum-cancellation mechanism clears U2 while leaving U1's coupling and U3's coincidence standing; an anthropic bound addresses U3 while leaving the mechanism of U1 open.
- **Result: union, not partition.** Fig 9 :58: *"It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Each condition needs its own loop and its own ground, and the verdicts must never be averaged.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if one mechanism demonstrably shrinks all three residuals together in a measured way, the union reading fails and the partition reading stands.

### Q2 - Are the regulator-indexed estimates isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- The Planck-cutoff estimate, the dimensional-regularisation estimate and the properly renormalised free-theory estimate are not different theories with different atoms. They are the same field content arranged under a different regulator (a different way of bounding the same sum). Under Fig 12 they share atoms and differ in the treatment of the cutoff.
- Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** All three estimates share the functional group "sum the vacuum modes of the field content up to a scale"; the qualifier is the regulator. They are therefore members of one functional group, not three independent predictions of nature.
- **Why the distinction matters.** Two sources quoting the same 120-order number are one witness, not three (Fig 2 :40), because they share the same regulator and the same review literature. The 56-order and renormalised values are different members of the same group, and the spread between them is the measured round-trip gap, not corroboration of any one value.
- **Falsifier:** if two regulators are shown to differ in their atoms (different field content, different symmetry assumptions) rather than only in the bounding treatment, they are not members of one functional group and the spread cannot be read as a transform gap. Then F3 of `01` must be re-censused.

### Q3 - Is there a seventh atom for dark energy?

- Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."*
- "Dark energy" as a name for the observed density is a derived quantity: a state (Energy) arranged under a structure (a spectrum-like ordering of the expansion history). It is not a seventh primitive. Dynamical dark energy would be a further composition of the same six, not a new member; modified gravity would change the binding, not add an atom to the figure set.
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"** is the recursion that lets the measured Lambda serve as an operand at the next level without being a primitive.
- **Falsifier:** derive a seventh member from the figure set, or show that the observed density cannot be written as a composition over the six. Either would break the substrate reading here.

### Q4 - What is the dependency order?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**
- Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
- The order forced by the census:
  1. Fix the anchor, the observed Lambda, which does not update on a frame's re-description (Fig 2 :29-30).
  2. Index the residual per slot: each field, each phase transition, each regulator, each independent probe (Fig 1 :19, :22).
  3. Unbundle U1, U2 and U3 and assign a ground to each (Fig 9 :54-58).
  4. Measure the round-trip gap between the UV description and the IR reading (Fig 4 :30).
  5. Seek an import only if no inside closure closes the residual, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-53).
  6. Name the discriminator instrument before naming a mechanism (Fig 6 :32-33).
- Proposing a mechanism at step 1 is effort above the break: it arrives at a step that cannot use it, because the anchor and the slots have not been fixed.
- **Falsifier:** show that the order can be reversed without loss, that is, that a mechanism can be settled before the anchor is fixed and still constrain the residual. If so, the dependency claim is withdrawn.

### Q5 - Is the comparison bond a legitimate operator here?

- The Operators table defines comparison as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* with the test *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*
- The vacuum-to-Lambda comparison is a threshold relation between a predicted density and a measured density. It is about which is larger, so it passes the operator test.
- Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* is not the right structure here: the regulator does not survive as an untouched part of the observed value, it is a bookkeeping choice in the prediction. The correct reading is the priced bond plus a lossy transform (Fig 4), not a catalyst.
- **Falsifier:** show that the vacuum-to-Lambda relation is not about magnitude but about identity of referent (a definitional equivalence, the symmetric bond `=`), in which case the comparison operator is mistyped and F4 of `01` is rewritten.

## CONCLUSION

U1, U2 and U3 are a union compound and must be unbundled before any mechanism is proposed. The regulator-indexed estimates are members of one functional group, not independent predictions; their spread is the measured footprint of a lossy transform. No seventh primitive is owed for dark energy, and the dependency order runs from the anchor to the slots to the unbundling to the round-trip gap to any import. The comparison operator is legitimate. What this derivation does **not** produce is the value of the cosmological constant or the name of the mechanism. Those remain `gap` in `01` F7.
