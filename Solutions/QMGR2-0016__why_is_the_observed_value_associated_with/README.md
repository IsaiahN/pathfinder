## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the numerical value of the cosmological constant and not which mechanism nature realised. In effect terms: one frame (quantum field theory) predicts a vacuum energy density from its own vocabulary and cutoff, and returns a number many orders of magnitude above the value another frame (cosmology) reads off the expansion history. The residual is the gap between those two returns, `R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` (Symbols table, definition of R).

- Option A, "the theoretical estimate is a prediction that the observation refutes": leads to the expectation that one frame should be corrected until its number matches the other.
- Option B, "the two numbers come from different closures joined by a lossy cross-scale transform": leads to the expectation that the gap is the round-trip error of a condensation, that the observed value is the anchor, and that a single averaged number hides which slot is live (Fig 4 :27, :30; Fig 1 :19, :22).

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a shape stating (i) which side of the gap is the ground, (ii) how many conditions the question bundles, (iii) what the cross-scale transform drops, and (iv) which instrument would discriminate the candidate sources. The shape is the sought-for term; until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, with the numerical value and the mechanism declared a `gap` after a genuine re-census. Per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it computes the cosmological constant, no claim that the framework picks between a cosmological constant, dynamical dark energy, modified gravity, and selection effects, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of the vacuum.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
