# The Tether's answer on the smallness of the dark-energy value

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A QFT construction returns a vacuum energy density many orders of magnitude above the density inferred from the expansion history. The question asks why the observed value is so small relative to that estimate. The answer is not a number. It is the shape of the correction: which side is ground, how many conditions are bundled, what the cross-scale transform drops, and which instrument would discriminate the sources.

## 2. The shape

1. **The observed value is the anchor; the estimate is a projection.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :30: **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."** The measured Lambda does not move when a theory re-describes it. The estimate changes by roughly 64 orders as the regulator changes (120 orders with a Planck cutoff, about 56 with dimensional regularisation, lower still for the properly renormalised free-theory value). A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27). The comparison is therefore asymmetric: one side is ground, the other is a family of frame-internal returns.
2. **The question is a union compound, not one hard problem.** Fig 9 :4-5 splits a disagreement that does not shrink with effort into several well-formed questions, and Fig 9 :54-58 requires a union to be unbundled before searching. The three conditions U1 (why vacuum gravitates at all), U2 (why the value is so small relative to the cutoff) and U3 (why it is comparable to matter today) have different grounds and need separate verdicts, never averaged. A single lever that clears one leaves the others standing.
3. **The headline gap is a lossy cross-scale transform, not a same-level contradiction.** Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."** Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** The prediction is formed at the UV end and the observation at the IR end. Treating the map between them as lossless is dead reckoning. The honest report is the measured round-trip gap `R_T`, whose footprint is the spread among regulator-dependent estimates.
4. **Two closures meet at the vacuum, and the union is mute until a residual is described.** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."** The binding "vacuum energy gravitates as Lambda" is an import between physics frames with no source gate in the standard treatment. It needs provenance and a shadow test before it carries verdict weight.
5. **No inside closure supplies the missing cancellation.** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."** Weinberg's no-go result is the physics-face version: under general conditions the contributions do not cancel without fine tuning. That is a barrier, not a proof that no answer exists; Fig 8 :39 says a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.
6. **The single "120 orders" is an aggregate that hides the live slot.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The slots are each field, each phase transition, each regulator and each independent cosmological probe. The number grew historically as new slots were counted, not as one slot was measured better.
7. **An unbounded frame-first selection account fails the mint bargain.** Fig 5 :43-44: a term that explains everything by saying nothing fails the acceptance test. But a pre-registered bound such as Weinberg's anthropic limit, stated before the measurement and then checked, does constrain the space of readings and is not refused by this rule. The distinction is whether a shadow test was supplied.
8. **The question is unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Supernovae, the CMB, BAO, lensing and DESI are already returning readings that fail to discriminate at the required precision. The missing thing is discrimination, not first contact.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a value of the cosmological constant and not a calculation of Lambda. No figure line yields one.
- Not a mechanism. The figures do not select among unimodular gravity, trace-free Einstein gravity, light-front quantisation, holographic bounds, fluctuating-vacuum models or anthropic selection.
- Not that dark energy must be a constant rather than a field. Fig 13 :74 constrains composition, not which term nature realised.
- Not that the standard theoretical estimate is "wrong" in its own frame. It is frame-internal and regulator-dependent; the defect is the ungated binding and the assumed lossless transform.
- Not a dismissal of all anthropic reasoning. Only the unbounded frame-first version is refused.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* records that green *"cannot comment on the source"* and that *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the absolute vacuum energy return from a direct ground instrument, or only differences (Casimir, Lamb shift)? A direct absolute reading would make the estimate an anchor and break the asymmetry claim.
- Can an instrument discriminate among the regulator-indexed source computations at a fixed IR reading, so that the round-trip gap is measured rather than inferred from the spread?
- Do the expansion probes at higher precision separate a true constant from dynamical dark energy or modified gravity? DESI's public pages and press releases are the instruments already returning something that fails to resolve.
- Does one lever move U1, U2 and U3 together in a measured way, or do they move independently?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5: a frame-dependent observation, a lossless UV-to-IR map, a single mechanism shrinking all three residuals, a direct absolute vacuum instrument, or a figure line that determines the value or selects a mechanism.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, on the instruments. `gap` on the value and the mechanism.** The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32). Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39 adds that a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence. So the absence of a figure line for the number is reported as a `gap`, with the shape of what is missing written down, rather than hidden behind a plausible mechanism. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
