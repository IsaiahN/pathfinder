## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The observed dark-energy density is an anchor; the "theoretical estimate" is a family of regulator-dependent returns produced inside a frame, so the comparison is asymmetric. The question bundles three conditions with different grounds (why the vacuum gravitates at all, why the renormalised value is so small relative to the cutoff, and why the density is comparable to matter today), and those conditions must be judged separately and never averaged. The headline 120-order gap is the footprint of a lossy transform between a UV construction and an IR reading, not a same-level contradiction; treating the transform as lossless is dead reckoning. No figure line yields the number or selects a mechanism, and that is reported as a `gap`, not silence. What is offered is the shape any acceptable answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the discriminating instrument still owed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a value of the cosmological constant and not a derivation of Lambda.
- Not a mechanism. The framework does not select among unimodular gravity, trace-free Einstein gravity, light-front quantisation, holographic bounds, vacuum fluctuation models, anthropic selection, modified gravity or dynamical dark energy.
- Not that dark energy must be a constant rather than a time-varying field.
- Not that the standard QFT estimate is wrong in its own frame. It is frame-internal and regulator-dependent; the defect is the ungated binding and the assumed lossless transform.
- Not that all anthropic reasoning is empty. A pre-registered bound is treated as a prediction.
