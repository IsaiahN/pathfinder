## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The estimate is regulator-dependent, so it is not an anchor.** A Planck cutoff gives a 120-order mismatch; dimensional regularisation gives about 56; Martin's properly renormalised free-theory value is *"far from being 122 orders"*. That is three different returns from three framings of the same calculation. It corroborates `01` F1 and F3 at the level of world evidence. It does not by itself solve the problem.
2. **The no-go result is a barrier, not a proof of absence.** Wikipedia reports Weinberg's 1989 result that *"the different contributions to the cosmological constant cannot, under general conditions, cancel without fine tuning their values"*. That is the physics-face version of closure idempotence: search inside the effective-field-theory closure does not manufacture the cancellation. It corroborates `01` F8.
3. **The old, new and coincidence problems move separately.** The sources distinguish the old problem (why the vacuum contributes so little), the new problem (why nonzero and so tiny), and the coincidence problem (why comparable to matter today). Anthropic arguments address the third; cancellation mechanisms address the first two. That corroborates the union split in `01` F2.
4. **The instruments are already returning readings.** DESI's public pages report a completed five-year survey with more than 47 million galaxies and quasars observed. CMB, supernova, BAO and lensing constraints are already returning values that fail to discriminate among mechanisms at the required precision. That corroborates `01` F9: unreached, not unreachable in principle.
5. **The framework's own limit is repeated in its canonical README.** It returns a shape, not a name, and stops where instruments stop discriminating. So the solution must not end with a mechanism or a number.

**Overlap warning.** The two Wikipedia pages share the same review literature (Weinberg 1989, Martin 2012, Carroll 2001). They count as one evidence pool on that literature, not three independent witnesses. The two Isaiah N essays share the framework author with the figures and count as one pool. There are no genuinely low-overlap world sources in this folder apart from the institutional venue pages, which are contact points and are not used as verdict witnesses.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the asymmetry, the union split and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article or the reviews.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A mass send selected for agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - a later agreement is never pooled as a second witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three physics links and the figures link, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is the residual and refuter. Stage 2 is opt-in only. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method to reuse, with each user stating their own ground and writing their own refuter before the run. The rationale is Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45 and Fig 4 :22, set out in `02_outreach_email.md`.
