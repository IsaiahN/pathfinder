## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number and the mechanism do not. The observed dark-energy density is the anchor and the estimate is a regulator-dependent projection, so the comparison is asymmetric. "Why so small" is a union of three conditions with different grounds, judged separately and never averaged. The 120-order headline is the footprint of a lossy UV to IR transform, not a same-level contradiction, and the next thing owed is a source-discriminating instrument with a measured round-trip gap. No inside closure supplies the cancellation; any import needs a source gate and a shadow test. On the value and the mechanism the verdict is `gap` after a genuine re-census, reported plainly. The question is `unreached` on instruments, not `unreachable` in principle. This is `specified` and ungrounded, candidate only.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separates the four domains, writes four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The value and mechanism claims were never made, and the shape claims are removable independently: if the estimate is an anchor, if one lever moves all three conditions, or if a lossless cross-scale map exists, the corresponding finding is withdrawn without touching the other two. No primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A direct instrument returning the absolute vacuum energy would break the anchor asymmetry; a single lever moving U1, U2 and U3 together would break the union verdict; a lossless UV to IR map with `R_T` zero would break the transform verdict; a figure line that determines Lambda or selects a mechanism would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and readership are not such instruments (Fig 7 :35).
