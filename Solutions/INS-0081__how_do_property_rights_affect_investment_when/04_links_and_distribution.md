## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The institutional economics pool is one witness, not four.** Besley, Goldstein and Udry, Field, Ali and Dercon, and Fenske all work on land tenure and investment, and they cite one another (Fig 2 :31). Their agreement corroborates the two-condition shape only weakly. Field's result is the most useful because it reports what changed (labour allocation), not what the programme claimed.
2. **There is a real conflict, and it is informative.** Banerjee and Ghatak 2004 is the low-overlap theoretical item inside the same broad field: eviction threats can raise investment effort. It does not overturn the derived shape; it narrows it to long-horizon, immobile investment (strain S2 in `01`). A failure that narrows a claim is more useful than an endorsement that broadens it.
3. **The title monocause is contested in the public record.** The de Soto page records both the claimed labour effects and the counter-observation that credit access did not change. This is why the packet never says "title causes investment" (F7).
4. **Low-overlap corroboration comes from outside economics.** Ostrom's commons work and the tragedy-of-the-commons record show that security is a relation that can be built without private title; the endowment-effect record shows that ownership changes the belief term `b` as well as the ground; animal territoriality shows that investment in defence is conditional on defendability. These fields do not share the development-economics evidence pool, so they corroborate the mechanism shape at low overlap. None supplies a group-level measurement, so none is `grounded`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the two conditions and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - a list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is real: a wrong shape could misdirect land policy. Independent reviewers are worth more than a large audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two conditions and the pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which instrument would discriminate; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, it does survive as a shape, but only after the two conflicts are disclosed: the contested de Soto claim (F7) and the non-monotonic sign result (F8). Disclose both before asking for a verdict.
