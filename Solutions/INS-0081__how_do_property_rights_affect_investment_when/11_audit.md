## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the systematic difference in investment return across groups with different ownership security. Arity: four places (group, tenure condition, action, return), indexed by plot and ordered by time. It carries an ordered bond, because investment now is repaid later, and a comparison bond, because secure and precarious are a threshold relation between two quantities (Fig 12 :87). Symmetry: not symmetric, group and market are not interchangeable; "secure" is relative to a threat. Scale: single group loop, population with a shared market, and the time channel across seasons.
- **Domain 1, group decision loop.** Actors: decision makers, routines, Γ. Currency: actions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized return per plot. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, tenure habitat.** Actors: holders, state, courts, neighbours, creditors, market. Currency: security, enforcement, transferability. Anchor: observed investment and output per plot. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, legal instrument frame.** Actors: titles, statutes, registries. Currency: legal legitimacy. Anchor: none independent of Domain 2, because a title's validity is internal to the legal frame and the frame moves when pushed. Not a domain yet; absorbed into Domain 2.
- **Domain 4, temporal channel.** Actors: expectations, memory of dispossession, instruments. Currency: contact with a future return. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **Exclusions.** A free-floating "culture of thrift" has no nameable anchor. A "title monocause" is measurable only through Domain 2 outcomes. Both excluded before any figure line was applied.

Union, not partition: the group loop is inside the habitat and the future return is pursued inside the habitat, so two overlapping compounds. Unbundled conditions: C1 (channel to a future return maintained) and C2 (reach to a wider set of investments renewed). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry; the middle link carries no instrument.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-57 (union unbundling), Fig 11 :39-40 (capability is agent-and-habitat), Fig 10 :24 (channel versus ground), Fig 6 :23 (import from a differing closure) and Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: investment has two separable conditions, a channel to a future return and a reach to composable investment; precarious ownership degrades both; index per group.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy claim of the kind found in the wider literature: issuing formal title unlocks investment, ideally through credit.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **scope of the investment category**. The shorthand says investment; the discriminating literature says long-horizon immobile investment. The broad claim is lost in the concretisation, and Banerjee and Ghatak 2004 shows why it must be lost;
- the **sign of the effect**, which the figures do not determine and the register of eviction-threat models can reverse;
- the **source of the reading**, which a post-titling rise in activity does not identify. Naming the title is the metamerism error described in `05` section 4.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: investment is group-and-habitat, and the residual is per group and per plot, never an average. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no group-level ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
