## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :132-135: a system with a closure can answer whether a term was reachable before it searches, and what it returns *"is a shape rather than a name"*; it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* though it cannot say which primitive is missing, because *"naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0081 the shape is: *two conditions must hold for investment to keep paying across a horizon longer than one season, a maintained channel to the future return and a renewed reach to a wider set of investments, and the residual must be indexed per group.* Naming "formal title" as the whole answer is a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` section 0 is written as `Γ(b,a)` versus `o′` per slot, and not as "title versus no title". The words of the literature are borrowed; the effect is described first.
- The same essay states the stop condition directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This governs the unbundling into C1 and C2 in `01` section 1.3.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The honest form here is a shape plus a registered gap, not a finished causal story.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means a per-group, per-plot measurement with an instrument that discriminates channel failure from reach failure.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Bringing in " collateral" from the finance frame or "psychological ownership" from cognition is allowed only when its provenance is recorded and it explains a gap the group's closure could not.

A crucial narrowing, preserved from the exemplar's own correction and re-derived here: the README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. In this solution nothing claims that "security" or "title" is a new primitive of the six-term physical substrate. Fig 13 was re-censed out of this domain (strain S1 in `01` section 6). Naming a title is instead a **binding** from the legal frame onto a habitat relation, recorded as such. What remains wrong is treating that binding as an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 :25 says *"An import adds no atom to the world."* The title adds no atom; contact with a credit market, a court, or a differing closure may.

## 4. Why "title causes investment" is the named measurement error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: an observed rise in investment after a titling programme is a reading, not a source. The same reading can be produced by a maintained channel to a future return (C1), by a new reach through credit or markets (C2), by a shift in the group's belief term `b` through the endowment effect, or by a change in the political hierarchy that made the title usable in the first place (Goldstein and Udry 2008). Naming "title" as the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the figure-grounded reason the packet asks for an instrument in stage 1.

## 5. Answer on the two questions this raises

**What licenses a particular institutional arrangement in the "security" slot?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. An arrangement is licensed only by the ground settling a term for a named habitat, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The de Soto record is exactly this: a name ("formal title") attached to a reading (more work hours), with a counter-observation that the claimed credit mechanism did not appear. Both are kept visible in `01` F7.

**Does the literature commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, when "dead capital" is read as if the framework named the source. The correct form is the shape in section 1 plus an open slot: `SECURE??? → (channel maintained + reach renewed) → long-horizon investment`. The `???` is not laziness; it is the recorded limit of discrimination.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with a property-rights claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground per group settle it.

**Agreement is not a validator.** Same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The development-economics papers cited in this folder share an evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the institutional claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The same limit applies to the second seat in `11_audit.md`: it shares this evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
