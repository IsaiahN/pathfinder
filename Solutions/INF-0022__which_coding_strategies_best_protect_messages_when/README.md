## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

An answer has two parts. (1) A strategy class: the transformation applied to redundancy and symbol order before transmission. (2) A stopping rule: what measurement on the channel would make one strategy the right one. Written as a term to accrue residual:

> `ANSWER := (arrangement of redundancy, channel statistic that licenses it, cost paid in rate and latency, refuter that fires if the arrangement buys nothing)`

The sought-for shape is bound this way so it can be refused by the same test as anything else (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*). A bare leaderboard of code names is not an answer under this binding: it supplies part (1) without part (2) and cannot accrue a residual.

Restated in effect terms. A receiver treats symbol errors as independent witnesses. When errors are correlated, adjacent symbols fail together because they share a noise source, so they are one pool talking, not many witnesses. The independent-error assumption aggregates that structure away (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question asks which coding strategy protects the message once that assumption fails.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a particular code (Reed-Solomon, Fire, LDPC, turbo) is the figure-derived winner. Naming one is a `gap`, not a finding.

Three reasons, from the figures themselves:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Agreement is not the anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
3. **The source must not reach the checker before the verdict.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Key verdict in one line

Correlated errors are a shared-evidence-pool collapse, not a harder version of independent errors (Fig 2 :31, :20-21); the compound must be split before a code is chosen (Fig 9 :5); the figure-licensed lever is **arrangement**, so interleaving and burst-targeted symbol grouping are the shape of the winning strategy (Fig 12 :94, :117); the channel must be measured before "best" means anything (Fig 2 :29); and no figure names a unique best code (Fig 9 :32).
