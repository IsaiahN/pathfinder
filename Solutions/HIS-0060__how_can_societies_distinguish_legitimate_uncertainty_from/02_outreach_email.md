# Outreach draft: check the test before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter for a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the checklist and its refuter are stated plainly and the
sender has recorded the recipient's verdict on stage 1 before any framework is shown. If stage 1
cannot be checked in a few lines, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One question about a test, not a theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one operational question I would like you to check. I am not asking you to read a
> framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the test does not survive,
> there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` checks, not `grounded`):** a public claim of
> "uncertainty" gets one of three values. (i) A live residual on a named slot, with positive mass
> and a ground that shrinks it under inquiry: legitimate. (ii) A preference that imitates a
> residual, with no named slot, no advance falsifier and no ground contact: manufactured doubt.
> (iii) Undecidable at this budget.
>
> The checks behind it are short. Which slot failed to resolve, and what is the positive mass
> there? Can the claimant say in advance what observation would make them concede? Are the
> agreeing voices independent, or do they draw on one pool? Does the asserted uncertainty trend
> down as evidence accumulates, or does it stay flat? Is the ground being ranged against, or has
> it been made one party to a balanced debate? Is the funding stake declared? And is this a single
> question, or a bundle that should be split before it is searched?
>
> The first two are the strongest. A claim that cannot name a slot has no positive mass to check,
> and a claim that cannot state a falsifier is a preference, not a term. Everything else is a
> corroborating check, not a verdict on its own.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a funding source proves a claim false, that a skeptical expert
> is by kind a doubt merchant, or that this checklist names which real campaigns are manufactured.
> Provenance shows how a ruling was reached, never whether it was right. The same person can be a
> legitimate questioner on one residual and a doubt merchant on another, so there is no permanent
> list of good and bad actors. Applying the checklist to a named case needs that case's history and
> a ground reading, which I have not done here.
>
> **What would refute the test, written down before this message:**
> 1. A case that passes every check above and is nevertheless strategically manufactured, or a
>    case that fails them and is nevertheless legitimate.
> 2. A case where the "ground" for deciding turns out to be another frame that updates on opinion,
>    so there is no anchor for the question at all.
> 3. A class of claims where no independence is possible because every voice shares one evidence
>    pool, so no amount of checking can separate the two.
> 4. A single rule that separates them cleanly in all cases, making this checklist unnecessary.
>
> If any holds, I will narrow or withdraw the test. A one-line "this fails because..." is more
> useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the three sources below. The figures, not my
> summary, are the authority.
> - Local figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README (canonical usage rule): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Eight essays and the worked method, indexed in `04_links_and_distribution.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** write down what an answer would look like before you run it,
> state which ground would settle it, and write your own refuter first. An output that cannot name
> what would refute it is not a result.
>
> Thanks for checking.

## Two-stage discipline

- **Stage 1:** send the test, the three-valued output and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient
  would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any framework.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the source links. Later agreement is never
  pooled as corroboration, because by then the reader has seen the interior (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3, opt-in only:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own
  refuter and states their own mode and ground.

Nothing is sent by this agent. The human host sends, one to one.
