## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity,
symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order
by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the classification of a public uncertainty
  claim. Arity: four terms, a knowledge frame, a doubting frame, a ground and a deciding society.
  Sub-questions: slot, independence, term versus preference, trend. Symmetry: the claim presents
  as two balanced sides, but the anchor is not a party to a disagreement, so the symmetry is the
  phenomenon, not a premise. Scale: slot, closure, room chain.
- **Domain 1, knowledge production.** Actors: researchers, instruments, peer review. Currency:
  residuals settled against the ground. Anchor: measurement. Union member.
- **Domain 2, doubt production.** Actors: funders, think tanks, contrarian experts, media
  intermediaries. Currency: doubt as output, attention, funding. Anchor: none valid for the truth
  question; a sponsor interest that moves when pushed. Common cause and habitat member, not an
  anchor.
- **Domain 3, public decision.** Actors: public, journalists, policymakers. Currency: agreement.
  Anchor: should be the ground, often becomes agreement.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert leads. Currency: residuals and refutations. Anchor:
  the ground. In this simulated run independence is zero because we share one evidence pool.
- **Question type.** Union compound, because a real case can be a genuine residual and a funded
  campaign at once. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency.** Per-slot support before vocabulary, vocabulary before objective,
  objective before trend and independence. A step below the break cannot be diagnosed, only its
  predecessor can (Fig 3 :22-23).

My census reaches the same four domains and the same union reading as the primary. I derived them
from Fig 2's collapse panels and Fig 9's union/partition split, whereas the primary started from
Fig 1's slot indexing and Fig 5's support gate. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low
overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak rather than circular within the framework. It is

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

  about a social decision rather than a physical measurement? Fig 2 :36-38 limits anchor legitimacy
  to questions constitutively about it, and the shorthand does not settle which anchor applies;
- the **sign of the loop** (Fig 12 :121): the shorthand asserts a positive-feedback cycle but no
  instrument here measures its sign in a real campaign;
- the **radical-uncertainty limit**: a flat residual may be genuinely unresolvable, and the
  shorthand's three-value output can only flag this, not prove manufacture;
- the **source-discriminating instrument**: metamerism (from *The Hard Problem is About Source*)
  requires an instrument that can tell honest and funded sources apart; no such instrument is
  supplied, only the requirement for one.

Conversely, what survives cleanly is the core: a global aggregate hides a slot signal; a preference
is not a term; a shared pool is not two witnesses; and a ground made a party has become a frame.
That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading
exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the
