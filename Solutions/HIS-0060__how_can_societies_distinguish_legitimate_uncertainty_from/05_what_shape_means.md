## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it
does not fill. For this question the shape is: *a three-valued, per-slot, ground-referenced test
that any uncertainty claim can be run through, together with a refuter stated in advance.* Naming
"Big Tobacco", "ExxonMobil" or any particular campaign is a name, not the shape, and needs a
separate ground reading.

From *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it
validate itself."* The shape is a generator of tests, not a court that pronounces on cases.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you
  are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast
  it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather
  than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the
  paradox that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating
  it."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several
  problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level twin of Fig 9's split rule and it is the
  reason the question here is treated as a union compound, not one hard question.

Applied here: the effect terms are **slot, positive mass, falsifier, independence, trend, stake,
habitat**. None of them requires knowing which side is right in a political dispute, so anyone can
check them.

## 3. What would license a name: source, not reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs this question most directly.
An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several causes can produce the
same reading: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with
lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"It was
proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The public claim "we are uncertain" is exactly such a projection. An honest live residual and a
funded doubt machine can produce the same sentence, the same press conference and the same number
of expert voices. The reading does not determine the source. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Two frames can hold the
same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence.
Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where
each came from separates them."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works* names the same limit as **provenance loss**: *"often, very little
about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* The video
frame and the photograph are *"indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants.
And nothing in the image records which."* It also states the stop rule: *"The recursion converges
toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

So the discriminator cannot be the content or the tone of the uncertainty claim. It has to be a
source-discriminating instrument: the advance falsifier, the provenance, the independence of the
frames and the residual trajectory. Where no such instrument exists, the honest output is the
third value, undecidable at this budget, not a guess dressed as a verdict.

## 4. Agreement, drift and deception

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the social mechanism. Two people who check only against each
other drift together: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody
notices."* And the key line for manufactured doubt: *"Agreement is the whole point when you're
settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the
same sources."* It also names deception precisely: *"Deception runs on substitution: a false
context, a false pretext, a false subtext. And knowledge can survive in writing while the people
who could understand it die out."*

Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence
pool"*. So a public "debate" in which ten contrarian outlets repeat one briefing is not ten frames.
It is what *How a Society of Agents Would Work* calls it: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are
one opinion said ten times."* The same essay adds: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."*

But note the caution from Fig 8 :50: convergence at maximum independence can produce the same
shared atom. Two genuinely independent teams can reach the same conclusion. So the test is not
"do they agree?" but "would they still agree if one pool were removed?", which is why provenance
and separate experience matter more than a disagreement count.

## 5. Outputs are not generators: why counting voices fails

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: *"A written sentence is a lossy
compression of the reasoning that produced it."* And the load-bearing move: *"You cannot accumulate
your way from the category of outputs to the category of generators; they are different kinds of
thing, and no quantity of the first sums to the second."*

A doubt campaign is an output factory. Its volume of "uncertainty" statements is a count of
outputs, not evidence that a live residual exists. Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure
of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* The same essay adds
that *"a committee regresses toward consensus, and consensus is the average, which is duller than
the best member"*, which is Fig 1 :22's warning that *"Aggregating across slots is how a live
signal disappears into an average."*

The same source adds the honest caveats that bind this derivation: *"A minimum viable set of six
terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set
need not be those six"* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the
choice visible."* So the checklist here is a visible choice of discriminators, not nature's own
list. It can be revised.

## 6. The answer to the shape question

**What shape does an answer to HIS-0060 have?** It is a three-valued test, not a two-sided verdict:

1. `legitimate uncertainty`: a live residual on a named slot, positive mass, an advance falsifier,
   ground contact, independent frames, a trend that shrinks with effort.
2. `manufactured doubt`: a preference imitating a residual, no named slot, no falsifier, no ground
   contact, a common evidence pool, a flat residual under effort and a declared or traceable stake.
3. `undecidable at this budget`: the discriminating instrument does not yet exist.

**What licenses applying value 2 to a named case?** Only a ground reading of that case: its
funding provenance, the independence history of its experts, the trajectory of its asserted
residual, and a source-discriminating instrument. The framework itself supplies the shape and the
refuter, not the verdict about a named campaign.

**Does this record commit a forbidden error?** No if it stays in shape form. It would commit an
error if it named a specific real campaign as manufactured without the ground reading, because
that would be a name where the framework only returns a shape. The outreach draft and packet
therefore withhold any such naming (see `02_outreach_email.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`).

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action
directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an
action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The
Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: a famous scientist, a famous institution or a famous journal saying "there is
uncertainty" has given the claim a budget to be tried. That is all. It has not settled the claim.
A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with a classification is the named collapse,
not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the test a budget to be tried, let a
ground settle each case, and report the failure.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or
the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output
with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real
architecture or working the formula yourself."*

*Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter: LLM outputs generated with the formula are *"simulacra"*, and
the approach *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the
formula."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for
internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the question and must be labelled
as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 9. Corrections visible in this file

- My first framing treated "false balance" as a single mechanism. The reading of *Decoding Tacit
  Knowledge* shows it covers at least two: a shared evidence pool (common cause) and a norm that
  treats agreement about words as agreement about the world (mutual update). Both are retained;
  neither is promoted to the whole explanation.
- My first framing treated "no anchor" as absence of any anchor. It is now stated as absence of a
  *valid* anchor for the truth question: a sponsor interest is an anchor that moves when pushed,
  which Fig 2 :30 rules out. The correction is recorded in `01` §5 and is not silently erased.
- The source-discriminating point in §3 is **not** retracted by the shape limit in §6. They are
  different claims: the shape is a test; applying the test to a case requires a source instrument.
