**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on each check, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This WATSON run synthesizes material already

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a public claim of uncertainty. Option A: treat
it as a live residual on a named slot that shrinks under inquiry. Option B: treat it as a
strategically manufactured appearance of a residual that stays flat regardless of measurement. What
each leads to: calling manufactured doubt legitimate buys delay and puts the ground on trial as one
party among many; calling legitimate uncertainty manufactured closes a live slot early and hides a
real gap in an average. A third value, undecidable at this budget, is required because the two
sources can produce the same public reading.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Four terms: a knowledge frame K, a doubting frame D, a ground G,
  and a society S that decides. Arity of the compound: four sub-questions (is there a live slot;
  is D independent; is D's claim a term or a preference; does D track G over effort). Symmetry:
  manufactured doubt presents as two balanced sides, but the anchor is not a party to a
  disagreement, so the apparent symmetry is the phenomenon to explain, not a premise. Scale: slot,
  closure, room chain.
- **Domain 1, knowledge production.** Actors: researchers, instruments, peer review, funders.
  Currency: residuals settled against the ground. Anchor: measurement and experiment. Union
  member, not a standalone answer.
- **Domain 2, doubt production.** Actors: funders, think tanks, contrarian experts, media
  intermediaries. Currency: doubt as output, attention, funding. Anchor: none valid for the truth
  question, because its sponsor interest moves when pushed. A common cause (Fig 2 :6-7) and part of
  the habitat, not an anchor.
- **Domain 3, public decision.** Actors: public, journalists, policymakers. Currency: agreement,
  attention, votes. Anchor: should be the ground; often becomes agreement.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert leads in uncertainty and doubt research. Currency:
  residuals and refutations. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four
  domains draw on one evidence pool, so their independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** A union compound: a case can be both a genuine residual and a funded
  campaign. Each overlap is its own loop and must be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (named slot, positive mass) before vocabulary
  (bound term with advance falsifier) before objective (what would count as settlement) before plan
  and learn (residual trend, independence, stake). A reading below the break is a reading of
  nothing.

Strain test applied: the SUPPORT gate (Fig 5) governs per-slot existence before search; Fig 9's
split rule governs compounds after no single residual closes. Different quantities, so no strain
after re-census. One live strain remains: Fig 12 :121 names the sign of a loop but no instrument
here measures that sign in a social campaign.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures license a shape, not a name: a three-valued, per-slot, ground-referenced conjunction of
checks with a falsifier written in advance. Name the slot and positive mass; require the claimant to
state what would settle the question; keep the ground out of a balanced debate; count independent
frames, not voices from one pool; track the residual over effort and split compounds; declare
stakes without letting them refute; enumerate the habitat instead of blacklisting actors. Over the
shared atom set the two kinds are isomers whose bond carries the difference. This is a `general`
shape with `specified` checks, ungrounded and candidate. No named campaign is classified here.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the mode, the three checkable
  sub-claims, the verbatim lines and the refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable
  rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a
  dossier of conclusions instead of a test, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion
  to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The classification can be withdrawn or narrowed without touching the figures.
  A published checklist can be superseded by a corrected one, no figure line is amended, and no
  actor is permanently blacklisted. The one strain (Fig 12 :121 loop sign) and the one gap (no
  single-line separator) are recorded rather than hidden, so the stop can be revisited.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument for a named
  case, a case that passes all checks yet is manufactured (or fails yet is legitimate), an
  "anchor" that turns out to update on opinion, a class where all frames share one pool, or a single
  figure line that separates the two cleanly would narrow or break the shape. Until one fires, the
  verdict stands as `candidate`.
