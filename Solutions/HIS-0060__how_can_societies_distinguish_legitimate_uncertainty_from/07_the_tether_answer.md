# The Tether's answer: how a society tells the two apart

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on each check, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated
derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading and not a
verdict on any named campaign.

## 1. The residual it answers

A public claim of uncertainty arrives with two possible sources: a live residual on a named slot
that shrinks under inquiry, or a funded appearance of a residual that stays flat regardless of what
measurements return. The two produce the same public sentence. The society must decide what to do
with the sentence, and both errors are costly: treating manufactured doubt as legitimate buys
delay and puts the ground on trial; treating legitimate uncertainty as manufactured closes a live
slot early and hides a real gap in an average. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot,
not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an
average."*

## 2. The answer

**A society distinguishes them by a per-slot, ground-referenced conjunction of checks, with a
three-valued output and a falsifier written in advance. It does not distinguish them by the
wording, the volume or the prestige of the claim.**

1. **Name the slot and the positive mass.** Fig 5 :20-21: *"is there anything to explain?"* /
   *"the gap has real mass somewhere"*. Legitimate uncertainty can say what failed to resolve and
   where. A global "the science is unsettled" is an aggregate that can hide a settled ground.
2. **Turn the doubt claim into a term.** Fig 5 :65: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM.
   Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything
   else."* Ask the claimant what observation would make them concede. An unbound preference that
   predicts nothing and accrues no residual (Fig 5 :62-64) is a manufactured product, not a
   finding.
3. **Keep the ground out of the debate.** Fig 2 :47: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has
   already become a frame."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror
   cannot correct you."* Both frames range against the ground separately; the ground is not one
   side and not a tie-breaker.
4. **Count independent frames, not voices.** Fig 2 :40:
   *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*;
   Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Ten outlets repeating one briefing are
   one witness with ten names. Convergence from separate experience is still corroboration
   (Fig 8 :50), so provenance decides, not the fact of agreement.
5. **Track the residual over effort and split compounds.** Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact
   here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a
   different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than
   search."* Legitimate uncertainty trends down; manufactured doubt stays flat. Non-shrinking also
   covers genuinely unresolvable questions, so the test flags, it does not convict.
6. **Declare the stake, but do not let it refute.** Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed,
   declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*
   Fig 10 :65-66: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was
   right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."*
7. **Enumerate the habitat, never a permanent blacklist.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good
   actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the
   residual, not a property it carries."* The same expert can be a legitimate questioner on one
   residual and a doubt merchant on another.
8. **Record one of three values.** `legitimate`, `manufactured`, or `undecidable at this budget`.
   The third is a verdict, not silence: Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a
   silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The bond, not the wording, carries the difference. Over the shared atom set the two are Fig 12
:117 isomers: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the
formula and decisive in the behaviour."* See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a verdict on any named person, campaign, industry or country. That needs a ground reading of
  that case, which is not performed here.
- Not that funding, prestige or institutional affiliation makes a claim false. Prestige can fund a
  trial; it cannot settle one. Provenance is a declared stake, not a refutation.
- Not that every skeptic is a doubt merchant or every consensus is right. Valence is relative to a
  residual, and a ground with a reputation to protect is disqualified (Fig 10 :93).
- Not that a flat residual proves manufacture. It can be genuinely radical uncertainty, or merely
  unreachable at this budget (Fig 9 :40-43).
- Not a single-line test. The figures license a conjunction, and Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of
  thing, never the answer itself"*.
- Not `grounded`. No case was settled by measurement in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would refute it

**To settle a case:** a source-discriminating instrument for that claim, its funding and publication
provenance, the independence history of its experts, and the residual trajectory under inquiry.
*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*;
the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved that case.

**Pre-registered falsifiers of the shape (from `01` §3):**

1. A case passing all checks that is nevertheless strategically manufactured, or a case failing
   them that is nevertheless legitimate.
2. A "ground" for the decision that turns out to be an opinion-updating frame, so no anchor exists
   (Fig 2 :33-34).
3. A class where every voice shares one pool, so independence cannot be established at all
   (Fig 2 :40).
4. A single figure line that separates the two cleanly in every case, making the conjunction
   unnecessary.

## 5. The honest stop

**Shape reached, case undecided.** The output is a checkable procedure with its own refuter, not an
answer about the world. One strain remains visible: Fig 12 :121 says the sign of the loop decides
whether it closes, but no instrument here measures that sign in a social campaign. One gap remains:
no single figure line closes the question; the discriminator is a conjunction and its application
is a ground task. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The lineage is recorded so the stop is
checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A society does not tell legitimate uncertainty from manufactured doubt by reading the claim,
> counting the experts or weighing their prestige. It asks which slot failed to resolve and whether
> there is positive mass there; it demands that the claimant state in advance what would settle the
> question, so doubt becomes a checkable term rather than a hidden preference; it keeps the ground
> out of the "two sides" format; it counts independent frames, not voices from one pool; it watches
> whether the asserted residual shrinks under effort and splits compounds that do not shrink; it
> declares funding stakes without treating them as refutations; and it enumerates the habitat
> instead of keeping a permanent list of good and bad actors. The result has three values,
> legitimate, manufactured, and undecidable at this budget, and the third is a verdict rather than
> a silence. The figures license the shape of that test and its falsifiers, not a verdict about any
> named campaign, and this derivation is a simulation checked for internal consistency, not a
> ground reading.
