# Expert verification packet  /  skepticism toward experts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a philosopher of skepticism, historian of science or science-studies researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the conditions, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape with falsifiers, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Skepticism toward experts carries no intrinsic valence. It is a negation applied to a held term, and its effect depends on three relations: the aim of the refusal, the independence of the doubting frame, and the state of the channel to the ground. The three conditions form a product, not a checklist, and bind in dependency order: aim, independence, channel. When all three hold, the refusal is a selection between rooms and knowledge improves. When they are inverted, the ground is promoted to a party, the pool is shared, or the channel is cut, and reliable knowledge becomes harder to establish. The two outcomes are isomers over one atom set, not kinds of skepticism. The answer is offered with five pre-registered falsifiers.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  /  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 12 and 5

- **Statement:** skepticism's valence is relational and the three conditions are a product.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist."
- **What to check:** is the refusal correctly classed as a negation, and does the framework really define independence as a quantity rather than a virtue?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the same refusal, aimed at the same term from the same frame, changes valence with no change in aim, overlap or contact; or show that one condition at zero can be compensated by the other two.

### C2  /  the improving regime; checkable against a case the reader knows

- **Statement:** a low-overlap frame selected against the residual the expert frame surfaced adds reach that the expert frame could not derive internally.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".
- **What to check:** in a case you know, did the productive doubt come from different evidence, or only from a different tone? If the evidence pool was the same, Fig 2 :40 says the agreement is the pool talking.
- **Refuter:** a documented case where a genuinely independent frame, selected against the surfaced residual, produced no improvement in contact with the ground.

### C3  /  the degrading regime; checkable against an institution the reader knows

- **Statement:** when the refusal aims at the ground or the channel, agreement is drawn from a shared pool, or a stake in the reading is undeclared, reliable knowledge becomes harder to establish even if the doubters are numerous.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :47 "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying". Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."
- **What to check:** can the parties move the reference point? Is the correction instrument independent of both? Is the skeptic's stake declared as carefully as the expert's credentials?
- **Refuter:** a documented case where knowledge improved after the measuring instrument or the settled record was treated as a negotiable party, or where a declared stake in the reading improved contact with the ground.

## 3. Non-claims

- Not that experts are usually right, or that skepticism is usually wrong.
- Not that credentials are meaningless; they can fund an action being tried, they cannot settle it.
- Not that any particular historical episode has been settled by this derivation.
- Not that critical rationalism supplies the verification seat. That mapping is explicitly declined in the canonical article (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).
- Not a field-wide or public-wide verdict. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Five falsifiers were written before the conclusion and are recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. The decisive one: a documented case in which the skeptical frame shares the expert's evidence pool, aims at the channel rather than a settleable claim, and declares a stake in the outcome, and knowledge nevertheless improves. If that case is real, the shape fails.

The instruments available are: independent replication (separate experience plus something real to check against), two-way correction, and a court-like anchor neither party can move. These are named in the *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* essay and are figure-consistent.
