## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms when a skepticism event S toward an expert claim E improves knowledge and when it makes reliable knowledge harder to establish. Option A: skepticism has an intrinsic valence and the answer is a degree of doubt. Option B: S is a negation whose valence is set by its relations. What each leads to: A gives the instruction "doubt more" or "trust more" and cannot explain why the same act produces both outcomes; B gives a three-condition shape with a pre-registered falsifier and a per-domain verdict. The derivation takes Option B. Source: `Questions/history of science and knowledge.txt` line 23.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unexplained variation in the valence of a refusal directed at expert standing or an expert claim. Arity: a relation among skeptic frame B, expert claim or frame A, ground G, and channel C. Symmetry: S is a unary negation with an asymmetric effect. Scale: first order a claim, second order the expert's standing, third order the certifying institution.
- **Domain 1, claim and ground.** Actors: the held term, the claim, the ground (measurement, experiment, counter, outcome, replicated record). Currency: settlement. Anchor: G, which does not move when a frame pushes on it. Question type: is the term settleable?
- **Domain 2, frames and triangulation.** Actors: expert frame, skeptic frame, their evidence pools, the room chain. Currency: independence and residual. Anchor: the ground as selection pressure between rooms, with independence as the measurable quantity. Question type: selection between rooms, not negotiation.
- **Domain 3, channel and habitat.** Actors: the verifier office, instruments, journals, peer review, credentials, the public. Currency: contact and conventions. Anchor: ground reachability. Question type: maintenance of the channel.
- **Partition or union.** A union of overlapping conditions. One event can be improving in D1 and degrading in D2 and D3 at the same time, which is a compound with its own loop, not a middle value. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Order by dependency.** Aim (D1), then independence (D2), then channel (D3). Fig 3 :20-21 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces".

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Skepticism toward experts has no intrinsic valence. It is a refusal, and the aim, independence and channel of that refusal decide the outcome. D1: a refusal aimed at a settleable held term with a pre-set criterion improves knowledge; promoting the ground to a party degrades it. D2: a low-overlap frame selected against the surfaced residual improves knowledge; a shared pool adds polarization, not evidence. D3: an act that keeps the anchor reachable and declares both stakes improves knowledge; cutting the channel without a replacement instrument substitutes a habitat and degrades it. The three conditions form a product and bind in that order. The two outcomes are isomers over one atom set, not kinds of skepticism. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate shape with five pre-registered falsifiers.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the falsifiers are used. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a theory instead of a checkable condition set. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." If the shape is wrong and it is sent as a verdict, readers may reject useful doubt or accept useless doubt, and the error compounds because Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not".
- **undoable:** yes. A refusal adds a term to the record rather than deleting one, so the error is reversible without erasing anything (Fig 6 amendment). The condition set can be corrected condition by condition; nothing has to be withdrawn wholesale except the claim that skepticism has an intrinsic valence.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A documented case in which all three conditions are inverted yet knowledge improves would break the shape; a demonstrated compensation between conditions would break the product claim; a measurement of overlap ρ between belief systems would close the D2 gap; a derivation showing the refusal changes contact with aim, overlap and channel held fixed would restore intrinsic valence. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
