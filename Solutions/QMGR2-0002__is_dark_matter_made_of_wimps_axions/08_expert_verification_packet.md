# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the composition of dark matter

**What this is:** a claim in a form a dark matter researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural limits, not `grounded`.** No detector result is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a particle name.

**Standing:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The missing-mass residual registers gravitationally, so the question is hard rather than foreclosed. The four offered options form a union compound, not a partition and not a set of isomers: "several components" makes the other candidates non-exclusive, so each needs its own loop and its own ground. Null results from direct, indirect and collider searches are budget filters, not a verdict of absence, because insufficient exposure and genuine absence look the same from inside the detector frame. The framework does not name the occupant; it returns the shape any occupant must have and records the identity as a gap. The verdict belongs to a source-discriminating measurement, never to agreement among theorists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the four options form a union compound and must be unbundled before search; the residual is indexed per observation slot, not averaged into one global mass number.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** are the lines present verbatim in `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`? Does "several components" logically make the other options non-exclusive? Is there any reading under which the four options are disjoint?
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that the observations force exactly one occupant with disjoint conditions, so one lever closes every slot; or a measurement showing that a single global mass number predicts every slot within error, so slot indexing carries no information.

### C2 : physics-facing; checkable by a dark matter researcher against the experimental record

- **Statement:** current null results constrain parameter space but do not prove absence for a candidate. From inside the detector frame, depth (insufficient exposure or sensitivity) and genuine absence are indistinguishable. The correct next moves are more search and better instruments, not a new particle category imported just because WIMPs have not been found.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27 *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :40-41 *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."*
- **What to check:** is this a fair reading of the xenon (XENONnT, LZ), axion (ADMX) and LHC null results as reported in the public record? Does any collaboration claim its result proves WIMPs or axions do not exist at all, rather than closing a declared mass window under assumptions? Are the candidates already held terms in the theoretical vocabulary, so no new atom is required to compose them?
- **Refuter:** a detector result that closes the entire allowed mass window for a candidate under declared assumptions and is reported as proof of absence; or a genuinely external candidate with a discriminating prediction, a provenanced source and a shadow test, which would license an import (Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"*).

### C3 : framework-internal limit; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the figures cannot name which particle occupies the slot. Naming WIMP, axion or PBH is a binding from the particle-physics frame onto the residual, not a figure derivation and not a wall-moving import. The identity is a `gap`, reported, not filled.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **What to check:** is there any line in the verbatim figure text that selects a mass scale, a boson, a fermion or a production mechanism? If not, the name is necessarily external to the framework. Does the candidate list being already held in the physics frame change the limit? (Answer given in `05` §5b: it makes the name a binding, not a prohibition on naming held atoms.)
- **Refuter:** a figure line that directly governs particle identity. If one is produced, C3 is false and the gap claim is withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that dark matter is WIMPs, axions, primordial black holes, or any other named candidate.
- Not that null results prove a candidate does not exist.
- Not that several components is the actual answer; the union reading licenses unbundling, not multiplicity at the ground.
- Not that the four options are isomers. Fig 12 :117 defines isomers as *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*; the candidates are different atoms, so the term is refused here.
- Not a derivation of particle physics from the six-term substrate. A mapping of a particle onto a substrate term fails the strain test and was retracted.
- Not a theory of everything; no claim to finality.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a ground reading. This is a simulation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

1. **Source Test:** a detection measuring a mass and an interaction strength that distinguishes a WIMP from an axion from a black hole population, rather than a gravitational excess alone.
2. **Component Test:** per-slot measurements that decompose the missing mass into contributions, settling the union empirically.
3. **Window Test:** exclusion limits that close the remaining allowed mass range for a candidate under declared assumptions, reported with those assumptions.
4. **Standing falsifiers:** a figure line governing particle identity; a partition proof with one occupant; a result making null results prove absence. Any of these refutes the shape claim.
