# The Tether's answer: what is dark matter made of?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural limits, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a particle name. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Observed gravitation exceeds what baryons plus general relativity predict, in several independent slots: rotation curves, cluster dynamics, lensing, CMB acoustic peaks, large-scale structure, nucleosynthesis. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The options offered (WIMP, axion, another particle, several components) are four ways of filling the slot, but they are not four mutually exclusive answers. One of them changes the type of the question.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**D1, gravitational observation (the ground end).** The residual registers gravitationally. Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Here there is an edge: the missing mass has already been read by rotation curves and lensing. So the question is hard, not foreclosed. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 6 plus CERN and Wikipedia).

**D2, particle theory.** WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino and primordial black hole are already held terms. No new primitive is needed to compose them. But the figures do not select among them: Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* **Verdict: `gap` on the name, `general`, triangulated.** The candidate set is a **union compound**, not a partition (Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). The options are not isomers under Fig 12 :117 (*"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*), because they are different atoms, not one atom set differently arranged (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2). **Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure definition.**

**D3, detector and instrument.** Null results from xenon detectors, axion cavities and the LHC are budget filters, not verdicts of absence. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* The correct triage is SEARCHED (more exposure, independent searchers), then INWARD (better instruments extended from ones already returning something), then OUTWARD (import only with provenance and a shadow test). Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* **Verdict: unreachable-at-this-budget for the standard WIMP window, not underivable; `specified` from world sources, `general` for the figure rule, triangulated.**

**D4, verification.** The anchor is a measurement, not agreement. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* **Verdict: zero verdict weight for consensus; `general`, triangulated.**

Verdicts are kept separate by domain. They are not averaged into a single answer about what dark matter "really is."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "dark matter is WIMPs," not "dark matter is axions," not "dark matter is primordial black holes." All are bindings from D2, ungrounded here.
- Not "null results prove WIMPs do not exist." Fig 8 :39 forbids that inference.
- Not "several components is the answer." The union reading licenses unbundling before search; it does not assert multiplicity at the ground.
- Not a derivation of particle physics from the six-term substrate. The substrate contains no particle-mass term; a mapping of axion onto Spectrum or WIMP onto Gradient fails the strain test and was retracted (`01` §6).
- Not a theory of everything, and not grounded. No detector result settles `left(R,φ)` for any candidate in this folder.
- Not a ground reading. This is a simulation checked for internal consistency.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A **source-discriminating instrument**, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read this session: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The spectrometer resolved the colour case, not an argument.

The corresponding checks here:

- **Source Test:** a detection that measures a mass and an interaction strength sufficient to distinguish a WIMP from an axion from a black hole population, rather than a gravitational excess alone.
- **Component Test:** per-slot measurements that decompose the missing mass into contributions, so the union can be settled empirically rather than by preference.
- **Window Test:** exclusion limits that close the remaining allowed mass range for a candidate under declared assumptions. This is a filter that becomes a verdict only when the window is genuinely closed, and it must be reported with its assumptions.
- **Standing falsifiers from the record:** a figure line governing particle identity; a partition proof with one occupant; a detector result making null results prove absence. Any of these refutes the shape claim (`01` §4).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, and partly ungoverned by the figures.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The shape is reached; the name is a gap. The held candidate list is *"a human's careful guess"*, not nature's own list, and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The LICENSE to name arrives only from a ground reading or a provenanced import. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you whether dark matter is made of WIMPs, axions, another particle, or
> several components. It tells you the shape any answer must have. The missing mass already
> registers gravitationally, so there is an edge from which to improve an instrument. The four
> options are not four answers to one question: "several components" makes the others
> non-exclusive, so they form a union that must be unbundled into one loop per candidate before any
> single lever is applied. Null results from xenon tanks and axion cavities are budget filters, not
> proof of absence, because from inside the detector frame too little exposure and a genuinely
> absent particle look the same. Which particle occupies the slot is a gap the figures report
> rather than fill. Only a source-discriminating measurement settles it, and agreement among
> theorists is not such a measurement. What is offered is the shape, the falsifiers written in
> advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
