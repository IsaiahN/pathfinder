## 0. The residual, in effect terms

**R = (observed gravitational effect) minus (prediction from baryons plus general relativity).** It is indexed by object and by scale, not aggregated into one global number. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The residual registers in at least these slots: galaxy rotation curves; velocity dispersions in clusters and ellipticals; strong and weak gravitational lensing (the Bullet Cluster separates the lensing peak from the visible gas); the acoustic peak structure of the CMB; large-scale structure and baryon acoustic oscillations; Big Bang nucleosynthesis bounds on baryon density. Each slot is a separate reading of the same missing-mass effect. A candidate that fits one slot and not another has not closed R.

The question asks which term occupies the slot: a WIMP, an axion, an as-yet-unbound particle, or several occupants. The options are not four answers to one question. At least one option (several components) makes the others non-exclusive, so the structure is a **union compound** before it is a multiple-choice question. That distinction is derived in F1 and `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**What an answer would look like, bound before the run (Formula "Direction"):** a named occupant plus a detector result that discriminates it from the alternatives. Binding this as an ordinary term lets it accrue a residual like anything else and makes the search preference refusable. The figures return the shape the occupant must have, not the occupant's name (F2).

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

R described by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity.** The missing-mass slot is one effect measured in many slots. The candidate set has arity k ≥ 1, because "several components" is on the table. Arity is therefore variable, not fixed at one.
- **Symmetry.** WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino, primordial black hole and other candidates are not interchangeable under swap. They differ in mass, interaction strength, production mechanism and the instrument that could see them. The symmetric reading would be a union of identical components, which the world does not give us. The comparison of candidates is ordered by which instrument discriminates them, not symmetric.
- **Scale.** The residual registers at galactic and cosmological scale. The candidate answers live at particle scale (sub-eV axions through GeV to TeV WIMPs) or at astrophysical scale (primordial black holes). A cross-scale transform is required; the two ends are not the same frame (Fig 10 :68, *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."*).

One line per domain, giving actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet:

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, gravitational observation** | galaxies, clusters, CMB photons, lensing geometry | measured velocities, lensing masses, peak heights | instruments and published data; they do not move when a theorist changes their mind | where R is registered; the ground end |
| **D2, particle theory** | WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino, primordial black hole, other beyond-Standard-Model terms | predicted cross sections, relic abundance, mass windows | internal consistency plus eventual detector confirmation; no current anchor of its own for the identity | a set of held terms in Γ; a union candidate set |
| **D3, detector and instrument** | liquid xenon (XENONnT, LZ), resonant cavities (ADMX), gamma-ray (Fermi-LAT), collider (LHC) | exclusion limits, exposure, null results, candidate events | the ground: a detection or a closed mass window | the instrument that would settle which term pays |
| **D4, verification and outreach** | expert readers in dark matter candidates research | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | correction on shape, named failure classes |

**Partition or union.** D1 is a partition of independent observations of one effect. D2 plus D3 form a union when "several components" is allowed: the conditions overlap, because two candidates can each contribute to the same slot. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The union must be unbundled into one loop per component before any single lever is applied.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The chain is ordered by what consumes what, not by importance:

1. **perception** (D1): the residual must register gravitationally before anything can be sought. It does.
2. **vocabulary** (D2): candidate terms must be held before a probe can be aimed. WIMP and axion are already in Γ; PBH and sterile neutrinos are also held.
3. **objective** (D2 and D3): state what counts as progress, in the frame's own terms, a detection that discriminates candidates or an exclusion that closes a declared mass window.
4. **planning** (D3): build and run instruments aimed at each candidate.
5. **learn and carry** (D3 to D2): exclusion limits feed back and shrink the candidate space.

Optimising a link above a break cannot rescue one below it (Fig 3 :19). A candidate list that cannot be discriminated by any instrument is a break at link 3, not a hard problem at link 4.

## 3. Findings

### F1 : The question is a union compound, not four answers to one question

Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

The options WIMP, axion, another particle and several components are not mutually exclusive. The world sources confirm this: Wikipedia states *"Mixtures of the above are also possible: a theory of mixed dark matter was popular in the mid-1990s, but was rejected following the discovery of dark energy"* and a 2024 review is cited for a scenario where a component *"could explain the entirety of dark matter, or coexist with other candidates in a mixed dark matter scenario."* The Library of Babel essay names the exact failure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

**Verdict (D2, structural): union.** Unbundle into one loop per candidate, each with its own instrument and its own ground. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Mode:** `general` (structural). **Status:** triangulated, Fig 9 and Fig 1 have low overlap (union rule versus slot indexing). World corroboration from a physics reference and an essay.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the four options form a genuine partition with exactly one occupant forced by the observations, so a single lever closes all slots. Until then, the union reading stands.

### F2 : The figures return a shape, not a particle name; the identity is a gap

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The canonical figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*

The WIMP and the axion are not missing primitives in the sense Fig 13 describes. They are terms already held in the particle-physics frame (D2). Naming either one as the occupant is a **binding** from D2 onto the missing-mass slot, not a derivation from the figures. The figures can say the occupant must gravitate, must be non-baryonic and must be long-lived on cosmological timescales, because those constraints follow from the observations in D1. They cannot say *which* particle satisfies them.

**Verdict (framework level): gap on the name.** "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The honest report is the shape plus the stated gap. A guess presented as a figure result would breach the mode rule and would be a `general` verdict where no ground has paid.

**Mode:** `general` (limit). **Status:** triangulated, Fig 9, Fig 13 and the canonical README (three routes, low overlap between the figure limit and the usage rule).

**Refuter:** a figure line that directly governs particle identity (for example, a figure rule that selects a weak-scale mass or a boson), which would falsify this gap claim. No such line exists in the extract.

### F3 : Null results are not proof of absence

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

World evidence: Wikipedia records that liquid xenon experiments (XENON, LUX, PandaX, LZ) *"all reported null results for WIMPs across the standard GeV–TeV mass range"* and that LHC non-detection of supersymmetry has constrained WIMP parameter space; ADMX has reached sensitivity to the DFSZ axion in the micro-eV range, but has not excluded the full axion mass window. From inside the detector frame, a null result is compatible with (a) the candidate existing below current sensitivity (depth) and (b) the candidate not existing (genuine absence). The two look the same. Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*

The logic source agrees: the Argument from ignorance entry quotes Rumsfeld, *"Simply because you do not have evidence that something exists does not mean that you have evidence that it doesn't exist"*, and Sagan's *"absence of evidence is not evidence of absence."* It also notes a *"Null result is a term often used in science to indicate evidence of absence"* and gives the dry-ground example, which is exactly the interpretation a detector collaboration reports when it closes a mass window. The distinction is a budget filter, not a verdict (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

**Verdict (D3, reachability): unreachable-at-this-budget for the standard WIMP window, not underivable in principle.** Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* The remedy is more exposure and better instruments, not the import of a new category.

**Mode:** `specified` (about particular experiments, from world sources) and `general` (the figure rule). **Status:** triangulated, Fig 8 and Fig 6 are separate figures with different arguments; world corroboration from a logic/philosophy source with low overlap.

**Refuter:** a detector result that closes the entire allowed mass window for a candidate under declared assumptions, or a figure reading that makes a null result settle absence. The first would change the verdict for that candidate at the ground; the second would falsify the figure rule.

### F4 : There is an edge to extend from: this is not a foreclosed question

Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Dark matter has already registered, gravitationally, in rotation curves and lensing. CERN: *"researchers have been able to infer the existence of dark matter only from the gravitational effect it seems to have on visible matter."* Wikipedia: *"Dark matter is implied by gravitational effects that cannot be explained by general relativity unless more matter is present than can be observed."* So there is a wandering point in the sky, to use Fig 6 :33's image (*"A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."*). Inward instrumentation is legitimate: xenon detectors were improved from worse xenon detectors, and cavity haloscopes from lower-sensitivity cavities.

This does **not** license claiming a particle has been seen. The gravitational reading is a projection with lossy information. The Hard Problem is About Source: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what it is being measured or what causes it."* Many candidate sources produce the same gravitational green reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

**Verdict (D3): hard, not foreclosed.** **Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** single-source for the figure line (Fig 6), corroborated by CERN and Wikipedia and by the source essay.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the gravitational residual is fully explained by baryons plus a modification of gravity, so no missing-mass slot exists to extend from. The world sources note that no modified gravity theory currently fits all the evidence, but this is a live empirical question, not a figure verdict.

### F5 : The verdict is owned by the ground, never by agreement

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*

The anchor here is a measurement: a detection with a mass and a cross section, or an exclusion that closes a declared window. It does not move when the theoretical community changes its mind. Wikipedia reports that the failure of WIMP detection *"has led to a divergence in consensus"* and shifted focus toward axions and primordial black holes. That is a change in the frames, not a change in the ground. Theorists agreeing that axions are now more plausible would be a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*).

**Verdict (D4): agreement has zero verdict weight.** **Mode:** `general`. **Status:** triangulated, Fig 2 and Fig 10 and the Seat Map (three separate rules).

**Refuter:** a case where community agreement, with no new measurement, correctly settles which particle exists. None is known; the figure rule would be falsified by such a case.

### F6 : Search first, then extend the instrument, then import; never import against a budget limit

Fig 9 :6: *"the verdict: is φ* in here at all?"* Fig 6 :14 calls IMPORT *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 8 :40-43: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions. Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."*

The candidates WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino and primordial black hole are already in Γ. No new atom is required to compose them. The correct next moves are SEARCHED (more exposure, distributed independent searchers) and INWARD (better detectors), in that order. A claim that dark matter must be a fundamentally new kind of thing, introduced only because WIMPs have not been found, is an import against a budget limit: Fig 6 :24 warns *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* If a genuinely external candidate is proposed, it owes a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (it must explain a gap), per Fig 8 :9-13 and Fig 6 :11-14.

**Verdict (D2 and D3): more search and better instruments, not a minted category.** **Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source for the figure procedure; the world move toward axions and PBHs is a change of candidate within Γ, not an import that moves the wall.

**Refuter:** a candidate that genuinely lies outside closure(Γ) with a discriminating prediction and a provenanced source, which would license an import. Until then, importing is premature.

### F7 : The round-trip gap is nonzero: do not treat a condensed answer as the answer

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down"*, written as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, which measures what the coarser description cannot hold.

The condensation *"dark matter is made of WIMPs"* does not survive the return trip. At least three constraints are dropped: (1) the union character of the candidate set, (2) the shape-not-a-name limit, (3) the null-results-are-not-absence clause. What survives cleanly is: the missing mass registers gravitationally, the candidates are held terms, and only a source-discriminating instrument settles the identity. R_T is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`; it is not measured numerically because there is no ground reading.

**Verdict: the honest report is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, not an identity claim.** **Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source (Fig 4), corroborated by the metalaws note that a condensation is a generator, not a recording.

**Refuter:** a round trip in which nothing is lost. Then R_T would be zero and the condensation would be reversible. No such round trip is demonstrated here.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim (F1 to F5, F8) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line is found that directly governs particle identity, for example a rule selecting a weak-scale mass, a boson, or a specific production mechanism. That would falsify F2 (the gap on the name).
2. The four options are shown to form a genuine partition with one occupant forced by the observations, so a single lever closes every slot. That would falsify F1 (the union reading).
3. A detector result establishes that null results at all sensitivities constitute proof of absence for a candidate within the framework. That would falsify F3.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Note what would **not** refute it: an actual detection of a particle. A detection would ground one of the D2 bindings and move that binding from `candidate` to `grounded`; it would not falsify the claim that the figures return a shape. The distinction is between a ground update and a refutation.

Until one of the three fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
