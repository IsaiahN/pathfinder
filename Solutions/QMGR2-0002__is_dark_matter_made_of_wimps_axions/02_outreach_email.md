# Outreach draft: check the shape of the answer before you decide about a candidate

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the mode labels and refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are intact. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the shape of a dark matter answer, before any candidate

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am also not
> going to tell you which particle dark matter is made of, because the framework I use does not
> return names. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms:** the observed gravitational effects (rotation curves, lensing,
> cluster dynamics, CMB acoustic peaks, large-scale structure) exceed what baryons plus general
> relativity predict. Something gravitates that we cannot see. The question asks what occupies that
> slot: a WIMP, an axion, another particle, or several components.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the shape, `general` on the limits, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. The residual already registers gravitationally, so this is a hard question, not a foreclosed
>    one. There is an edge from which to improve an instrument.
> 2. The four options are not four answers to one question. "Several components" makes the others
>    non-exclusive. It is a union compound and must be unbundled into one loop per candidate before
>    any single lever is applied.
> 3. Null results from xenon detectors and axion searches are budget filters, not a verdict of
>    absence. From inside the detector frame, too little exposure and a genuinely absent particle
>    look the same.
> 4. Which particle occupies the slot is **not** decided by the framework. The framework returns a
>    shape, not a name. I am recording that as a gap, not filling it with a guess.
> 5. The verdict belongs to a source-discriminating measurement, not to agreement among theorists.
>    A poll of people who now prefer axions would be a change of fashion, not a change of ground.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework picks WIMPs, or axions, or primordial black holes;
> that null results prove any candidate does not exist; that several components is the actual
> answer; or that this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for
> internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a figure line that directly governs particle identity (a rule selecting a mass scale, a boson,
>    or a production mechanism), which would falsify the gap on the name;
> 2. a demonstration that the four options form a partition with one occupant forced by the data,
>    so one lever closes every slot, which would falsify the union reading;
> 3. a detector result establishing that null results at all sensitivities prove absence.
>
> Note what would **not** refute it: an actual detection. That would ground one binding and move it
> from candidate to settled. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than
> agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - The figures and formula, verbatim and public (local canonical extract):
>   `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the Project Pathfinder repository.

>   https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-library-of-babel-locating-the

## Stage sequence

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the three refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three figure/essay links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A Stage 2 or Stage 3 agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
