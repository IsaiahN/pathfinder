## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: **a non-baryonic, gravitationally registering, cosmologically long-lived occupant of the missing-mass slot, which may be more than one component, and whose identity is settled only by a source-discriminating measurement.** "WIMP", "axion" and "primordial black hole" are names. `MISSING(???)SLOT` is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, read this session: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Then: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question: describe the effect (extra gravitation, no electromagnetic coupling), not the cause (a particular particle).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (CARRIED): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle case, read this session, and directly relevant to the "several components" option: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A detector result with a mass and an interaction cross section would name the occupant. A null result does not.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A genuinely new candidate kind would need both.

*They're Scanning Books* (CARRIED): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The held candidate list (WIMPs, axions, PBHs and so on) is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Promoting one to *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why naming a source from the gravitational reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, read this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can all produce the same reading. Then: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what it is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: the gravitational instrument reads "missing mass" and cannot say whether the source is a WIMP, an axion, a black hole population, or a mixture. Several candidate sources produce the same gravitational reading. Naming one from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is why the outreach packet asks for a detector result and not for agreement.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (CARRIED): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q2 answered: what licenses a particular candidate?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular particle is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No. `01`, `07`, `08` and `09` all record the identity as a `gap` and bind the sought-for answer as an ordinary term (*"a named occupant plus a discriminating detector result"*) so the preference is refusable. The one thing that could look like an error is saying "a non-baryonic, gravitationally registering occupant" earlier in this file; that is a shape constraint read off the observations, not a particle name, and it is labelled `specified` where it is empirical and `general` where it is structural.

## 5b. Correction to my own framing (kept visible)

I initially considered writing that the framework "cannot name any dark matter candidate" as a blanket prohibition. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. For this question, WIMP, axion and PBH are already held in the particle-physics frame. Naming one is a **binding** from that frame onto the slot, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as a figure derivation or as a wall-moving import (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none). Both readings are kept: the narrow rule is correct; my broader statement is retracted.

I also initially treated the union reading as a figure verdict that "several components is the answer." That is wrong. Fig 9 :54 licenses **unbundling before search**; it does not assert that multiple components exist. The empirical question of how many components there are stays with the ground.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (CARRIED): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that axions are now favored is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a detector settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any candidate and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
