## The question restated as an effect

Something gravitates but does not emit, absorb or reflect light. Galaxy rotation curves, cluster velocity dispersions, gravitational lensing, the cosmic microwave background acoustic peaks and large-scale structure all require more gravitating mass than baryons supply. The residual is the gap between those observations and the prediction from visible matter plus general relativity. The four options are four different ways of filling that slot:

| option | what it leads to |
|---|---|
| WIMP | a new weak-scale particle; direct detection, indirect detection and collider searches are the ground |
| axion | an ultralight boson from the strong CP problem; resonant cavity and helioscope searches are the ground |
| another undiscovered particle | a candidate not yet in the held vocabulary; needs a provenanced import and a shadow test |
| several components | a union of overlapping candidates, each with its own loop and its own ground |

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: **a named occupant of the missing-mass slot, with a detector result that discriminates it from the other candidates**. The figures return the shape that any such occupant must have and the rule that null results do not prove absence. They do not return the name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a candidate.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A poll of dark matter theorists does not settle which particle exists.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must see the residual and the refuter, not the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure and the structural one.
