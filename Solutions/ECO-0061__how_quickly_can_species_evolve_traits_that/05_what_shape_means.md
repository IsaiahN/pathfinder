## 1. A shape is a narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it

The canonical local rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

*The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether*: "The framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."

For ECO-0061 a shape is: **a per-trait rate that is a product of transmission fidelity and environmental pressure, stalls at zero on either factor, compounds across generations, and trails a moving optimum.** A number of generations for a named species would be a name, not a shape, and is not claimed.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, proximity and recursion: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for the expectation and the failure condition before showing theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here that means the loop stops when the per-trait residuals stop registering against measurements of `h²` and `S`, not when an attractive number appears.
- *Library of Babel* on bundled questions, the most directly relevant passage: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level warrant for unbundling "how quickly can a species evolve traits" into per-trait loops (F2, F9 of `01`).

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this question stays a shape

Only the ground settles a name. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

For this question the missing instrument is a measurement of `h²` and `S` for a named trait in a named population. The figures and the breeder's equation tell us the form; only a field measurement names the rate. The source essay on metamerism is the reason a generic "adaptation rate" cannot be read off an observation that several causes could produce.

## 4. The provenance correction, recorded explicitly

I initially treated the match between Fig 7 :26 and the quantitative genetic breeder's equation as a case of convergent derivation, where two frames arrive at the same shape separately and only the record distinguishes them (Fig 8 :50-51). **That is now corrected.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* says, in its own words:

> "Figure 7's progress formula, R = h² × S, borrowed from breeding, reads directly: how faithfully partial results pass between agents, times how hard the checker selects. 'Zero either and the chain runs in place.'"

The word "borrowed from breeding" is the framework author's own provenance record. By the figures' rule, an adopted import and a convergent derivation look identical in content and differ only in the record; here the record says **import**. So:

- The equation `R = h²S` belongs to quantitative genetics. This solution does not attribute its discovery to the Tether and does not claim the figure predicted it.
- What the framework contributes is the **placement**: per-slot (Fig 1 :19), two-factor product with a stall at zero (Fig 7 :37-39), dependency order (Fig 3 :19), contact-dependence (Fig 11 :39-40), and bounded lag under a moving optimum (Fig 7 :30-31). Those are the parts that extend beyond the breeding equation, and they are the parts offered for checking.
- This correction was found only after the essays were opened. It is kept visible rather than being silently folded into F8. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7 records the matching change to the retraction log.

## 5. Variation, hedge, and the prerequisite link

Two essays bear directly on the biology, not just on method.

- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The test that environment applies is what we call fitness." And, on why variance is not noise: "variation ... is a robust property that hardens an entire population against something that would affect them en masse if they were homogeneous. Diversity is a natural hedge." This is the essay form of Fig 7's "the bracket's fidelity" and Fig 3's prerequisite link: without spread in the trait, there is nothing for selection to work on.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, on the same point from outside biology: "Evolution. Fisher's fundamental theorem says a population adapts at a rate set by its variance in fitness. With no spread, it cannot adapt." It also records the aggregation warning in figure language: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Both corroborate F2 and F4.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and the simulation limit

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A campaign that gathers respected evolutionary biologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a measurement settle it.

The same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* adds: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So this audit is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**. It is not a test of the claim, cannot settle it, and must be labelled as such wherever the claim is stated.

## 7. The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Also: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

This bounds the answer. The two factors in `R = h² × S` are not claimed to be the only things biology could say about adaptation rate; they are the shape this framework's primitives can compose. A frame holding different primitives could compose a different shape, and that would be a reason to re-census, not a reason to call this one false. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence": the numeric generation count is unreachable from these primitives without a ground measurement, and that is reported openly in `01` §6 and `07` §5.

## 8. Correction to my own §4 (shape, not name)

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any particular quantity here. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a primitive *not held* in the library. Heritability and selection differential are not missing primitives of the Tether; they are already imported into it, as the Society essay's "borrowed from breeding" record confirms. What remains forbidden is asserting a species-specific number as if the framework derived it. Both statements are kept: the import is legitimate and provenanced; the number is not derived and is refused.
