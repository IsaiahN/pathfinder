## The honest status of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap rule applies: LLM output is simulacra and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." It cannot settle the question it reports on. The world sources are independent of the framework, which makes their corroboration stronger than the exemplar's author-overlapping essays, but they still do not make this a measurement.

The governing figure is Fig 7 :26, `progress per handoff: R = h² × S`. The answer the figures license is a **shape**, not a number: a per-trait response that is a product of a transmission-fidelity term and an environmental-pressure term, which stalls if either factor is zero, and which trails a moving optimum by a lag. The number of generations for any particular species is ground work and is not claimed here.

## What is deliberately not here

Reason, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.
