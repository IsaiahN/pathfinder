# The Tether's answer on the rate of adaptive evolution

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a number. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A changing environment moves the trait values that keep a population away from the ground (mortality, failure to replace itself). The residual is the gap between the population's current trait distribution and those values, indexed per trait and per environmental axis. The question asks for the rate at which that gap closes. It is a **union compound**: several conditions bind at once, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It must be unbundled before it is searched.

## 2. The shape, with verdicts kept separate

1. **The rate is per trait, not per species.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global average can be near zero while several trait-level responses are live. `triangulated` (Fig 1 plus the Symbols table's definition of `R` as always a slice).
2. **Each per-trait rate is a product of two factors.** Fig 7 :26: "progress per handoff: R = h² × S". Fig 7 :37-39: "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:" / "the bracket's fidelity across the handoff" / "the ground's pressure on the room". The first factor is the fidelity of transmission across the generational handoff (narrow-sense heritability in the breeding literature); the second is the environmental pressure (the selection differential). If either is zero, there is no directional response. `triangulated` between Fig 7 and the independent *Heritability / Breeder's equation* source.
3. **The product is an imported law, placed by the figure.** The author's own record says Fig 7's formula is "borrowed from breeding" (*How a Society of Agents Would Work*). The framework's surplus is the placement: per-slot, stall-at-zero, dependency-ordered, contact-dependent and bounded. The equation is credited to quantitative genetics. `triangulated` (Fig 8 :50-51 plus the dated essay record).
4. **Heritable variation comes before pressure.** Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." A population with no variance for the trait cannot be made to adapt by increasing selection. `triangulated` (Fig 3 plus Alexander et al. 2014's list of determining parameters and Fisher's theorem as quoted in the Society essay).
5. **Vulnerability is a contact property.** Fig 11 :39-40: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" and "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." `triangulated` (Fig 11 plus the evolutionary-rescue literature's extrinsic and human-impact factors).
6. **Adaptation is incremental and lagging.** Fig 7 :30-31: "every room closes some of the gap;" / "no finite number of them ever arrives". Under a moving optimum the population trails by a lag rather than catching up, and beyond a critical rate set by variance and fitness width the outcome is extinction, not faster adaptation. `triangulated` (Fig 7 plus *Evolutionary rescue* and Alexander et al. 2014).
7. **Settled traits become substrate.** Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." Each generation's settled distribution is a term available to the next, so the loop recurses without a new primitive. `single-source` for the figure rule; corroborated in spirit by the breeding literature's multi-generation selection, not by this figure's recursion claim.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **No number of generations for any named species.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The numeric rate is ground work.
- **No species-level scalar.** Aggregating across traits is how the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
- **Not a new biological law.** `R = h²S` is the breeder's equation, imported and credited.
- **Not a claim that adaptation always rescues a population.** Evolutionary rescue is conditional on variance, population size, the timing of resistant variants and the rate of environmental change.
- **Not a claim that all trait change is genetic.** Phenotypic plasticity and movement to a new habitat are different residuals; the question says "evolve traits".
- **Not grounded.** Nothing here was settled by a measurement in this derivation.
- **Not a final theory.** No last room, no unique framework claim.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor here is a measurement, not agreement. The checks, stated so they can fire:

1. **Product test:** estimate narrow-sense heritability `h²` and the selection differential `S` for one named trait in one population, then compare the observed per-generation response with `h²S`. Additive rather than multiplicative behaviour refutes the product.
2. **Stall test:** remove additive variance for the trait under sustained pressure; a heritable directional response refutes the stall condition.
3. **Vector test:** show that one scalar rate predicts every trait's response as well as the per-trait vector.
4. **Lag test:** show a population holding a continuously moving optimum with zero lag, or adapting past the critical rate given its variance and fitness width.
5. **Contact test:** demonstrate reduced vulnerability from a change that alters no contact with any habitat actor or condition.

These are the same five as the pre-registered refuter in `01` §4. They are written before any outreach, so a failure is usable.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give a shape: product, stall, vector, order, contact, lag. They do not give a number, and no amount of searching inside the closure will add one. *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* warns that "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number of generations for any species, and it does not claim to have discovered the breeder's equation. It places that imported law inside a shape: the response to selection is indexed per trait, not averaged into one species-wide rate; each per-trait response is a product of transmission fidelity and environmental pressure, so adaptation stalls when either factor is zero; heritable variance comes before pressure in the dependency order, so more pressure cannot rescue a population with nothing to select on; vulnerability is a property of the organism and its habitat together, not of the organism alone; and a population tracking a moving optimum trails by a lag rather than arriving. Each generation's settled distribution becomes the substrate for the next. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and a clear statement of what only a measurement can close: the actual heritability, the actual selection differential and the actual response per generation for a named trait in a named place.
