# The Tether's answer: when industrial policy corrects and when it protects

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a separator and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An intervention is applied at a named market slot on a belief that a failure is there. "Correct" means the residual falls against a market counterfactual that does not move when the policy frame announces success. "Protect" means the same action keeps a firm alive because the reference was changed to the supported room's own report. The two outcomes are a **union compound** with different anchors, not one dial (Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**). Each domain gets its own loop and its own verdict; they are never averaged into "industrial policy works somewhat."

## 2. The separator, one loop per domain

**Loop D1, the market-failure frame, verdict `specified`, triangulated.**
A case enters only if a real failure is measured at the slot. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 5 :21: **"the gap has real mass somewhere"** A national average cannot supply support because Fig 1 :22 says **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Refuter: a correction established from an aggregate with no per-slot residual and no counterfactual.

**Loop D2, the policy-instrument frame, verdict `specified`, triangulated.**
The instrument is a mint. It passes only if support, reachability and the bargain all hold and the term can be refused. Fig 5 :42: **"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."** Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Description cost and action cost are charged separately; an effect size is a filter for the budget, not a witness (Fig 9 :60). Refuter: a policy whose full cost plus leftover residual exceeds the uncorrected failure yet is still admitted.

**Loop D3, the political-maintenance frame, verdict `specified`, triangulated.**
This is where protection lives. Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."** When the success metric is written by the supported room, the metric becomes a target and stops discriminating. Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** so no instrument is good or bad in itself. Refuter: a room-written metric that still tracks an independently measured fall in the failure.

**Loop D4, the habitat and time channel, verdict `specified`, triangulated / single-source for the ecological instance.**
The head start must be a method, not a recording. Fig 4 :26: **"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."** Fig 11 :61: **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."** A policy with no withdrawal condition is an introduction without exclusion, which is why a temporary measure becomes a permanent institution (Operators :58: **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**). The cascade must be enumerated outward, because Fig 11 :69 says **"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"** Refuter: a policy with no exit that closes the residual and exits on its own with the counterfactual intact.

**Loop on the intersection, verdict `general`, triangulated.**
A case that closes one failure and entrenches one incumbent is a specific compound with its own loop, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57, Fig 13 :137). Its verdict is the pair of domain verdicts, stated together, never averaged.

**Loop on the parameter question, verdict `gap`.**
No figure line returns a tariff rate, subsidy level, optimal duration or sector pick. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"** This is a verdict, not a silence, and unreachable is reported rather than filled with a number.

## 3. The five conditions, in one place

1. **Measured support at a named slot.** No national average as a witness.
2. **A paying instrument.** `cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R)`, with description and action costs kept in separate currencies.
3. **A non-updating ground.** The counterfactual is external to the supported room. If the metric becomes a target, it stops measuring.
4. **A seat able to refuse.** A written exit, a performance condition and an operator for "this no longer holds."
5. **A method, not a recording.** What crosses to the firm is a head start that makes its next search cheaper, and the cascade is enumerated before the introduction.

Drop 1 or 2 and the case is not a correction. Drop 3, 4 or 5 and it is a protection case even if the stated objective was correction. The intersection keeps both results visible.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that industrial policy is always good or always bad. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so a standing list is refused by Fig 11 :51.
- Not that any sector should be picked, or that any instrument rate is optimal. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not that a negative correlation between subsidies and performance proves rent-seeking. The same negative correlation is produced by a welfare-maximizing government that subsidizes the worst-performing activities (Juhász, Lane and Rodrik 2024). Correlation cannot choose the arrow.
- Not `grounded`. No counterfactual measurement was taken in this derivation.
- Not that a policy which announces a sunset is therefore temporary. Whether the refusal actually fires is a ground question (Fig 12 :119 catalyst).

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating counterfactual at the slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and it was the spectrometer that resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Externality test:** does a measured, slot-specific externality or coordination failure exist before the intervention, with an instrument at link 3 of Fig 3?
- **Counterfactual test:** does the residual fall against an observation that does not update on what the supported firms report?
- **Withdrawal test:** does the competitive sector exist after the support ends, making the bond production rather than sequence, and is the subsidy a spent part rather than a catalyst?
- **Independence test:** does the monitoring reading come from a frame whose evidence pool differs from the supported room's?
- **Cascade test:** are second-order residuals enumerated outward from the slot and measured, not assumed zero?

The six pre-registered refuters in `01` §4 state the same tests as failure conditions.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The counterfactual instruments for many sectors are hard to build, but the figures do not say they could never exist; they say a search for something that has never registered anywhere has no edge to extend from (Fig 6 :34). Where a sector's externality has never been measured at any resolution, the honest report is `gap` with a characterized instrument owed. Where it has been measured, INWARD (sharpen our own instrument) and OUTWARD (import a counterfactual from a differing frame with provenance and a shadow test) are two different instruments and must not be read against each other. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether industrial policy is good or bad. It says the question is a union of two conditions with different anchors, so the verdict must be given per sector, not as a national average. A policy corrects a failure when five things hold at that slot: a real failure is measured there, the instrument pays its own way, success is judged against a counterfactual that does not move when the supported firms report success, the support can actually be withdrawn, and what crosses to the firm is a head start rather than a payment that must be repeated forever. It protects an inefficient firm when the success metric becomes a target set by the supported room, when the temporary measure cannot be refused and becomes a permanent institution, or when an introduced actor's cascade was never enumerated. Correct and protect are isomers over the same instrument set: the ingredient list is identical and only the arrangement differs, which is why an effect size cannot tell them apart. What is offered is the separator, the failure conditions written in advance, and the counterfactual instruments that would close it. The sector-specific numbers stay open until a ground reading settles them.
