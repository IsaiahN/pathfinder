## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a shape constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0073 the shape is the five-condition separator (measured support, MDL-paying instrument, non-updating ground, withdrawable support, head start rather than recording), with a written sixth set of refuters. A national "industrial policy works, on average" percentage is not a shape; it is a filler that aggregates away the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"** (quoting the continuous passage). The separation between "correct a failure" and "protect a firm" is described in effect terms, as a residual against an external counterfactual versus a reference that updates, before any economics vocabulary is borrowed.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02` asks what the reader would expect to see if the separator were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For ECN-0073 the ground is a measured counterfactual at a named slot. Without one, the separator is a `specified` candidate.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Goodhart / Strathern measurement frame and the cane-toad ecology frame are imports of this kind: they describe the mechanism shape from a different closure, and they are debited against independence in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the five conditions are a visible guess about what closes the residual, not a discovery of nature's list of policy levers. Turning one of them into "the" answer to a slot would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the metric-as-target error is the named forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow and blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: an employment or export metric reads one green. It cannot comment on whether a cost-discovery externality was closed or an incumbent was kept alive, because several sources produce the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating counterfactual, not a better slogan. The low-overlap measurement literature (Strathern 1997, CNA 2022) independently names the same error for targets in general.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A separator derived from the figures cannot certify its own correctness; that requires the ground.

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular instrument in a slot? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular instrument is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. `01` F10 records that as a declared gap, not a hidden answer.

**Does this folder commit the error the README forbids?** No, after correction: it never names a sector or a tariff rate. It does name conditions such as sunset clauses and performance tests, which the correction in §6 shows are bindings onto held atoms, not newly invented primitives. The provisional first draft did call these "the answer"; that overstatement is preserved as a retraction in `01` §5.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §5

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Withdrawal conditions, monitoring and external counterfactuals are not missing primitives of the framework: Fig 11 already holds introduction without exclusion, Fig 10 already holds channel decay, and Fig 2 already holds the anchor. Naming them is a **binding** from the economics frame onto held atoms, a provenanced candidate, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong would be calling such a binding an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 :21 says **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Both the corrected reading and the overstatement are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists who agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried against a counterfactual.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the separator and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. The honest stop

The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six, and another economics tradition's set of policy levers need not be the standard four failures. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* For ECN-0073 that means: the separator is returned, the sector-specific parameters are owed, and the gap is reported rather than filled.
