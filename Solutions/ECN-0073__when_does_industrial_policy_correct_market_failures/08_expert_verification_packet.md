**What this is:** a claim in a form an economist or policy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No counterfactual measurement is claimed. This is a separator and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Does industrial policy correct market failures or protect inefficient firms?" is a union of two conditions with different anchors, not one dial. A policy counts as correcting a failure at a named slot only when five conditions hold: a real failure is measured there; the instrument pays its own way after its leftover residual is charged; success is judged against a counterfactual that does not update on the supported firms' reports; the support can be refused and withdrawn; and what crosses to the firm is a reapplicable head start rather than a recording of one occasion. It protects when the metric becomes a target set by the supported room, when withdrawal cannot fire and a temporary measure becomes permanent, or when an introduced actor's cascade was never enumerated. Over a fixed instrument set the two outcomes are isomers, same atoms and different bonds, which is why an ingredient-list or an average effect size cannot tell them apart. No figure returns a sector, tariff rate or duration; that is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Framework-internal and economic; checkable against Fig 1, Fig 2 and Fig 9

- **Statement:** the two outcomes have different anchors and must be indexed per slot; a national average cannot decide either one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**
- **What to check:** in a case you know, can you name the slot, the externality and the counterfactual separately? Does the same instrument close one failure and keep one incumbent alive? If the two outcomes cannot co-occur, the union claim fails.
- **Refuter:** a correction established from a national aggregate with no per-slot residual and no counterfactual, still tracking the ground at each slot; or one variable that fully predicts both outcomes with no leftover residual and shows they are mutually exclusive.

### C2: The protection mechanism; checkable against Fig 2, Fig 10 and the measurement literature

- **Statement:** if the success metric is written by the supported room, it becomes a target, the reference updates, and the drift is coherent, which is why the failure is invisible from inside. This is the figure-grounded form of Goodhart's law.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."** Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"** Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**
- **World corroboration, secondary:** Strathern 1997, *"When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure."* CNA 2022, *"when we use a measure to reward performance, we provide an incentive to manipulate the measure in order to receive the reward."* Different field from industrial economics, so this is a low-overlap witness for the mechanism shape only.
- **Refuter:** a metric written by the supported room, with no external observation, that still tracks an independently measured fall in the failure at the same slot.

### C3: The withdrawal and arrangement test; checkable against Fig 4, Fig 11, Fig 12 and Operators

- **Statement:** a temporary support must be a head start that can be refused. Without a refusal operator the policy can only accumulate; a support that survives its own use is a catalyst, and correct versus protect are isomers over the same instrument set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :26: **"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."** Fig 11 :61: **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."** Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."** Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Operators :58: **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**
- **What to check:** after the supposed learning period, does the sector exist without the measure? Is the subsidy a spent part or a catalyst? Are the two cases made of the same instruments with different bonds? The Juhász and Lane record of Mill's 1868 letter and the EBRD sunset-clause finding are world corroboration, not authority.
- **Refuter:** a policy with no exit that closes the residual and exits at the market-determined time with the counterfactual intact; or an instrument whose sign is invariant across two documented changes of residual.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that industrial policy is inherently good or bad, and not a standing list of good or bad instruments (Fig 11 :51).
- Not a sector pick, subsidy rate, duration, or optimal amount. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that a negative subsidy-performance correlation proves rent-seeking. A welfare-maximizing government that subsidizes the worst-performing activities generates the same correlation.
- Not that a stated sunset clause is a real withdrawal. Whether the refusal fires is a ground question.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured against a counterfactual.
- Not a claim that the ecology or measurement sources settle the economics. They corroborate the mechanism shape from low-overlap frames; the figures decide.

## 5. What would settle it

A counterfactual instrument at a named slot, not a further argument. The five ground tests are the externality test, the counterfactual test, the withdrawal test, the independence test and the cascade test in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §5. The source-discriminating instrument is the analogue of the spectrometer in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."*

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :29/:34, Fig 9 :57, Fig 11 :51/:61, Fig 12 :117/:119 and Operators :58. Ten short passages.
2. Pick one industrial policy you know well. Name the slot, the failure, the counterfactual and the exit. Does the case clear all five conditions or fail one?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish "corrected the failure" from "kept the incumbent alive" at that slot, or say why none exists.
4. If you know the empirical literature: does the subsidy-performance correlation you would cite distinguish a welfare-maximizer from a rent-seeking government? If not, it is a budget filter, not a verdict.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record your verdict on stage 1 before looking at the figures.
