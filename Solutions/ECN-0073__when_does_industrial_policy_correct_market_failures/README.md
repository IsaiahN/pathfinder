## Restating the question as an effect

The ordinary wording asks for a single line between two outcomes. In effect terms the question is: for a policy intervention `a` applied at market slot `s` on a belief `b` that a failure exists there, when does the residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` fall against a market counterfactual that does not move when the policy frame announces success, and when does the same action instead keep an inefficient firm alive by replacing that counterfactual with the supported room's own agreement? Option A treats the two outcomes as opposite ends of one dial. Option B treats them as two overlapping conditions in a union with different anchors, so the verdict is indexed per slot and kept separate per domain. The derivation below derives Option B from the figures and records why Option A is refused.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

A usable answer is a separator, not a percentage: a list of conditions each of which can be checked at a named slot, plus a written failure condition. The sought-for shape is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 as the conjunction of a real measured residual, an MDL-paying instrument, a non-updating ground, a seat able to refuse support, and a method rather than a recording of one occasion. Binding it makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else (Formula step "Direction", Fig 5 :65).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the separator.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige is not a verdict.** Contact cost and evidence are recorded in separate axes; a famous name funds a hearing and cannot settle a claim.
