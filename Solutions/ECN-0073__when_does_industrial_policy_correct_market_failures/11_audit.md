## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :22-26 (method versus recording), Fig 9 :55/:57 (union), Fig 11 :49/:51/:61 (valence and asymmetric power), Fig 12 :117/:119 (isomer and catalyst).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` §3 and `07` §3: measure at the slot, pay for the instrument, keep the ground external, keep a withdrawal, cross a method not a recording.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a country's actual industrial-policy decision, with named sectors, rates, durations, monitoring agencies and political constraints.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **renaming of the anchor across scales** (a market counterfactual at the firm scale becomes an institutional monitoring instrument at the policy scale; Fig 4 :20 licenses the change of name but the two are not the same object);
- the **refusal operator type** (Fig 11 :61 and Operators :58 require a negation, but a real withdrawal seat is a political office, not a unary logical operator);
- the **source gate on the counterfactual** (Fig 8 :51 requires provenance and a shadow test when importing from a differing frame; the shorthand just says "use a counterfactual");
- the **numeric parameters**, which the shorthand declares as a gap and the concrete decision must supply from a ground reading.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a policy that updates its own success metric, cannot withdraw support, and replays a one-occasion support falls in the protection column, while a policy with measured support, an external counterfactual, a real exit and a head start falls in the correction column. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no counterfactual reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
