## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** An intervention applied at slot `s` on belief `b` that a failure is there. Arity: three place, `(action, slot, counterfactual observation)`, indexed by slot and ordered by time. Symmetry: "correct" is an ordered comparison against a fixed ground and "protect" is the persistence of an actor through an introduction that cannot be un-made, so they are not symmetric inverses and no single scalar can choose between them. Scale: firm and sector loop, policy-instrument loop, and political-maintenance loop, with a lossy membrane between them. Fig 9 names the compound a union, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1, market-failure frame.** Actors: firms, consumers, entrants, externalities, knowledge spillovers. Currency: prices, quantities, residual reduction at slot `s`. Anchor: the market counterfactual, which does not update on the ministry's report. Status: admitted.
- **D2, policy-instrument frame.** Actors: policymakers, tariffs, subsidies, R&D credits, loan guarantees, procurement rules. Currency: `|φ|`, leftover residual, reachability. Anchor: settlement, meaning whether the ground pays for the instrument. Status: admitted.
- **D3, political-maintenance frame.** Actors: interest groups, rent-seekers, regulators, oversight bodies. Currency: agreement, influence, maintenance effort. Anchor: should be the market ground; becomes a frame when the reference updates on the room's own report. Status: admitted.
- **D4, habitat and time channel.** Actors: entry and exit, sunset clauses, performance conditions, monitoring instruments, institutional capacity. Currency: contact and duration. Anchor: the ground does not decay, the channel does. Status: admitted.
- **X1, industrial policy as a free-floating good or bad property.** No nameable anchor, because valence changes with the residual. **Not a domain yet**, refused.
- **X2, "pick winners" as the sole cause of protection.** Already inside D2 and D3. Absorbed, not a separate domain.
- **Union, not partition.** D1/D2 and D3/D4 form two compounds; their overlap is a specific compound with its own loop. Order by dependency: perceive the failure, have terms for what the instrument affords, state a measurable objective, choose a route, then learn and carry.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is a per-slot separator, not a general answer. Industrial policy corrects a market failure when five conditions hold at a named slot: a real failure is measured there against an external counterfactual (Fig 1 :19, Fig 5 :21); the instrument passes the MDL bargain with description and action costs kept apart (Fig 5 :42, Fig 12 :125); the anchor does not update on the supported room's reports (Fig 2 :34, Fig 10 :47); support can be refused and withdrawn (Fig 11 :61, Operators :58); and what crosses to the firm is a head start rather than a recording of one occasion with an unenumerated cascade (Fig 4 :26, Fig 11 :69). It protects inefficient firms when the metric becomes a target set by the supported room, when withdrawal cannot fire and a temporary measure becomes a permanent institution, or when an introduced actor's cascade was never enumerated. The two outcomes are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :57), and over a fixed instrument set they are isomers (Fig 12 :117), so no average effect size can decide between them. No figure returns a sector, rate or duration; that is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The economics evidence pool corroborates the union and capacity claims; the measurement and ecology sources corroborate the protection mechanism from genuinely different closures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable but real. A failing separator would send a diagnostic to look for a missing counterfactual when the actual failure is an unmeasured externality, or would treat a self-sustaining sector as protected because a subsidy lingered. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The larger avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before the refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. No instrument or atom is deleted if a finding fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out. The six refuters and the lineage are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 provenance rule). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured counterfactual at a slot that falls when the supported room's metric does not; a withdrawal test showing a competitive sector exists after support ends; a derivation of a numeric intervention rule from the six-term set; or any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` §4 firing. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot correction status is fully predicted by a national average, or that the same instrument's valence is invariant across changes of residual, would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
