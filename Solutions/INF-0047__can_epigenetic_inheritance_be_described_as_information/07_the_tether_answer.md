# The Tether's answer: can epigenetic inheritance be described as information transmission, and what distinguishes it from inherited state or bias?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a separator and a route, not a biological result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Offspring resemble ancestors in ways not accounted for by DNA sequence. The same observed reading is compatible with a sent-and-read message, a persisting part or loop, a head start that changes starting conditions, and a bias in the receiver or its habitat. The residual is the **unassigned source** of that reading. It is a union compound (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*), not a two-way partition, and it is indexed per mark/locus/slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). A global "amount of information" is how the signal disappears into an average (Fig 1 :22).

## 2. The shape

1. **A carried mark is an operand, not a message.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Listing inherited molecules answers the state question and leaves the route question open.
2. **"Transmission" is an ordered, typed, priced bond to a receiver.** Operators :30: `→`, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*; Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* The receiver must show an evolved, discriminating response (Jablonka's account, SEP 2024), and the response must be tested against an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29).
3. **A symmetric correlation cannot choose the arrow.** Operators :12-15 (`+`, symmetric) versus :30-33 (`→`, ordered); SEP: *"First, correlational information is symmetrical: if A carries information about B, then B carries information about A."* A mutual-information number is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
4. **The negative cases are four, not one.** Persisting state is a catalyst or cycle (Fig 12 :119/:121); head start is a cheaper search, not a replay (Fig 4 :22/:24); prior bias is a belief weighting (Symbols :20-24); habitat substitution changes contact without removing a habitat (Fig 11 :66). Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
5. **The mark alone cannot discriminate among them.** Fig 12 :46 (bond not recoverable from operands); Fig 13 :129: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* This is a metamerism problem: several sources, one reading (*The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried canonical quote).
6. **Over a fixed atom set, transmission and state are isomers.** Same atoms, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :117). The difference is invisible in the ingredient formula and decisive in the behaviour. That is why "is it information?" cannot be answered by chemistry alone.
7. **The framework returns a separator shape, not a molecule name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Naming methylation or a small RNA as the transmitter is a provenanced binding from the biology frame (Fig 8 :12-13), a candidate, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that epigenetic inheritance is or is not information, full stop. The framework gives the condition under which the word is licensed; the ground has not settled any instance.
- Not that any particular molecule is the transmitter, and not that a mark is automatically a message.
- Not that correlation, mutual information or entropy settles the question. Those are symmetric and ubiquitous (SEP).
- Not that "state or bias" is one negative condition. It is at least four.
- Not that this is a partition. It is a union; a single lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57).
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating experiment was read in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further definition. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Mark-erase test:** remove or prevent the mark while holding habitat and receiver fixed. If the offspring reading changes, the mark is load-bearing; if not, the case is bias or habitat.
- **Habitat-substitution test:** cross-foster or transfer embryos while holding the mark fixed. If the reading follows the habitat, habitat contact was doing the work (Fig 11 :66-67).
- **Receiver-variation test:** vary the mark while holding the receiver's other inputs fixed and measure whether an evolved response changes with it. This is the Jablonka interpreter condition. Without it, "message" is a name for a correlation.
- **Anchor test:** check that the classification does not change when the observer is told what answer is expected. Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* If it does, the test is a mirror chain (Fig 2 :35).
- **Per-slot report:** score each mark/locus separately (Fig 1 :19, :22), never as a global "information content" number.
- **Figure-level falsifiers from `01` §4:** bond recoverable from operands; partition demonstrated; symmetric statistic sufficient; anchor shown to update; a figure line naming the transmitter; settlement without a discriminator.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The live gap is S4 in `01` §5: who counts as the receiver in a multicellular organism. Fig 11 :44 requires the habitat to be enumerated and notes that contact cascades (*"first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out"*). The responding system may be several steps from the mark. That is an instrument to be built, not a term already held. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that epigenetic inheritance is or is not information. It says that a methyl mark, a histone state, a small RNA or a prion conformation is a carried item, and that calling it "transmission" adds a claim the item does not carry by itself: an ordered bond from a parent carrier to an offspring reader, of a type you cannot recover by looking at the item, plus a reader whose response changes when the item varies, plus a comparison that does not move when we decide what to call it. A correlation cannot supply the order, because a correlation is symmetric and transmission is not. The same observed resemblance can also come from a part that persists, a head start that cheapens the offspring's search, a prior bias in the receiving system, or a substituted habitat, and these four are isomers over the same ingredients rather than versions of one thing. The framework returns that separator shape, not the name of a molecule. Which mark, if any, is a transmitter in a given case is left open until an experiment varies the mark with the habitat fixed, varies the habitat with the mark fixed, and checks that the reader test does not update on the answer we expect. Nothing here is measured; the separator is written with its refuters in advance, and the ground decides.
