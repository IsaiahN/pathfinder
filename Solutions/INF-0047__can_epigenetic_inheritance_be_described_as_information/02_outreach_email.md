# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide whether the theory is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the audit findings in `01` are repaired and the refuter in §3 below is final. The stage-1 output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One separator for "information" versus "state or bias" in epigenetic inheritance

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`):** a methyl mark, a histone state, a small RNA or a prion conformation is a carried item. Calling it *information transmission* adds a claim the item does not carry by itself: an ordered relation from a parent carrier to an offspring reader, of a type that cannot be recovered by looking at the item, plus a reader whose response changes when the item varies, plus a test that does not move when we decide what to call it.
>
> The problem is that the same observed offspring resemblance is compatible with at least four different accounts: a sent-and-read message; a persisting part or self-reinforcing loop; a head start that changes the offspring's starting conditions; and a bias in the offspring's developmental system or habitat, which can produce the same outcome with no carried item at all. The mark alone does not tell you which account is true. A correlation does not tell you either, because a correlation is symmetric while transmission is ordered.
>
> **What would count as transmission under this output:** an ordered bond, typed and priced, to a reader whose response varies with the mark, checked against a comparison that holds the habitat fixed and one that holds the mark fixed. If the reader test or the fixed comparison fails, the correct description is inherited state, head start, bias or habitat contact, and I will refuse the word "transmission" for that case.
>
> **What this output does not say:** that any particular molecule is *the* transmitter; that all epigenetic inheritance is information; that a symmetric statistical association decides the question; or that "state or bias" is one negative condition. The framework returns a separator shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. A procedure that reads sender, receiver and bond type directly from the mark, with no separate reader assay.
> 2. A controlled series showing the four conditions are mutually exclusive rather than overlapping.
> 3. A symmetric statistic shown sufficient to settle the transmission/state distinction with no ordered intervention.
> 4. A demonstration that the proposed reader test updates on the observer's classification, so it is a mirror and not a check.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the separator. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, verbatim with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as Fig N :line.
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - Public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you can check against a ground, write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
