## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5/:46/:111 (operand versus bond, route versus state), Operators :30-33 (ordered bond), Fig 4 :22 (head start), Fig 11 :66 (habitat substitution), Fig 9 :55 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: a mark is an operand, transmission is an ordered typed bond to a discriminating receiver, the negative cases are four, the question is a union.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete biological claim, for example "DNA methylation is information transmission because methylation is copied and a phenotype follows."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **receiver condition**. The shorthand requires a reader whose response varies with the mark. The concretisation supplies a copied mark and a phenotype, with no reader assay, so the ordered bond is asserted rather than tested;
- the **non-updating anchor**. The shorthand requires a comparison that does not move when the classification changes. The concretisation typically reads the same observational dataset that generated the hypothesis, which is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40);
- the **union structure**. The shorthand keeps four conditions separate. The concretisation collapses them into "methylation causes the phenotype," which is a single lever applied to a compound and leaves the intersections standing against it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a mark alone names a state, not a route, and a symmetric correlation cannot supply the order. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
