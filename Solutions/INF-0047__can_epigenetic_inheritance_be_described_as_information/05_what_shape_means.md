## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the separator stated before any molecule is named: an ordered bond, typed and priced, to a receiver whose response varies with the mark, checked against an anchor that does not update. It constrains. It does not fill. "A carried item plus a reader" is a shape. "DNA methylation is the transmitter" is a name.

The ordinary-language word "information" is itself a filler here. It is used for at least three different objects: a statistical association, a semantic message and a persisting state. The shape discipline binds the sought-for object as an ordinary term before the run so that the ambiguity is refusable (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question the effect is a parent-offspring resemblance unexplained by sequence; describing it that way avoids presupposing either message or bias.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The separator is written as an expectation: vary the mark with habitat fixed and the receiver should change; vary the habitat with mark fixed and the receiver should not, if transmission is the right description.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the finishing shape (ordered typed bond to a receiver) is also the starting description of the residual.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So naming DNA methylation, a small RNA or a prion conformation as *the* transmitter is a binding from the biology frame, provenanced as such. It is a candidate. It is not a derivation, and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*).

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

That is exactly the situation in `01` F2. The offspring phenotype is the green reading. Message, persisting loop, head start, bias and habitat substitution are the sources. Calling the reading "transmission" names a source from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating experiment (mark-erase with habitat fixed; habitat substitution with mark fixed; receiver variation with both fixed), not a better definition.

The world literature confirms the instrument gap: the Wikipedia transgenerational inheritance page reports that observational studies cannot isolate biological transmission from shared environmental and cultural confounders. That is the inadequate spectrometer, stated in the field's own language.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does "information transmission" have here, and what is refused?**

The shape is the four-clause separator in `01` F5. It is `general` as a structure and `specified` when applied to a given mark/receiver pair. It explicitly refuses:

- a symmetric correlation as a sufficient criterion (Operators :12-15 versus :30-33; SEP),
- a molecule list as an answer (Fig 12 :5, :111),
- a two-way partition between transmission and state/bias (Fig 9 :55-57),
- any `grounded` claim, since no source-discriminating experiment was read in this derivation.

**Correction to my own §3.** I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any mechanism. It does not. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an already-available molecular binding is a provenance question, not a prohibition. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework derived the name or that the binding moves the wall. Both the overstatement and this narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Compare `06` Q1.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of respected reviewers who agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**The verifier must not hold the interior.** *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* The two-stage offer in `02` keeps stage 1 independent for exactly this reason.

## 7. Shape summary for the packet

> Shape: an ordered, typed, priced bond from a carrier to a receiver whose response varies with the mark, checked against an anchor that does not update on the classification. The four negative cases (persisting loop, head start, prior bias, substituted habitat) are kept separate. Which molecule fills the carrier slot is a provenanced import, not a derived name, and is left open.
