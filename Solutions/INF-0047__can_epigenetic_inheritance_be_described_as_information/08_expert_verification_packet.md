# Expert verification packet: the Tether on epigenetic inheritance as information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the separator shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** No biological measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05` §6), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A methyl mark, histone state, small RNA or prion conformation is a carried item. Calling its inheritance "information transmission" adds a bond claim the item does not carry by itself: an ordered relation from carrier to reader, typed so that which relation holds is not recoverable from the operands, plus a receiver whose response varies with the mark, plus a comparison that does not update on the classification. A symmetric correlation cannot supply the order. The same observed parent-offspring resemblance is also compatible with a persisting part or self-reinforcing loop, a head start that cheapens the offspring's search, a prior bias in the receiving system, and a substituted habitat. These conditions are isomers over a shared atom set: same ingredients, different bond, different substance. The framework returns this separator shape and withholds the name of any molecule. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal and conceptual; checkable by reading Figures 12, the Operators table and the SEP entry

- **Statement:** the mark is an operand; "transmission" is an ordered, typed bond, and a symmetric correlation cannot choose the arrow.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 — *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :46 — *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* Operators :30-31 — `→`, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*, symmetric `no`; Operators :15 — `+`, *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."* Fig 12 :111 — *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **World point to check:** SEP Biological Information (2024) states that correlational information is *"symmetrical"* and *"ubiquitous"* while biological information is standardly *"asymmetric, normative, and non-ubiquitous"*; Jablonka makes the interpreter the seat of information.
- **Refuter:** a procedure that reads sender, receiver and bond type from the mark with no separate receiver assay (breaks Fig 12 :46), or a symmetric statistic shown sufficient to settle transmission versus state (breaks Operators :30-33 versus :12-15).

### C2 — biology-facing; checkable by an epigeneticist against the four conditions

- **Statement:** the same offspring phenotype can arise from (a) a sent-and-read mark, (b) a persisting catalyst/cycle, (c) a head start, (d) receiver bias or substituted habitat; these are not mutually exclusive and the four are isomers over the shared atom set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 — *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :119 — *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121 — *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 11 :66 — *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 9 :57 — *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful? Is a self-sustaining methylation loop a cycle rather than a message? Is maternal mRNA a head start rather than a decoded signal? Do cross-fostering data show habitat contact masquerading as transmission? Are the four conditions exclusive in any known controlled series?
- **Refuter:** a controlled series in which the four conditions are mutually exclusive (breaks the union), or a case where transmission requires an atom the state case does not (breaks the isomer claim).

### C3 — instrument-facing; checkable by anyone designing the experiment

- **Statement:** the discriminator is a mark-erase test with habitat and receiver fixed, a habitat-substitution test with the mark fixed, a receiver-variation test with both fixed, and an anchor test; without them the source is unassigned. The live gap is who counts as the receiver when contact cascades.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 — *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 13 :129 — *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* Fig 11 :44 — *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
- **World point to check:** the transgenerational epigenetic inheritance review reports that observational studies cannot isolate biological transmission from shared psychosocial, environmental and cultural confounders; Uller et al. 2018 show bias is an evolving property, so a resemblance needs no mark.
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that the proposed reader test updates on the observer's classification (breaks the anchor), or a ground reading that settles a case as transmission while all source-discriminating experiments remain unrun (breaks Formula step 5).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that epigenetic inheritance is information, or is not information, full stop.
- Not that any molecule is *the* transmitter. That is a provenanced binding from the biology frame, a candidate, not a derivation (Fig 8 :12-13).
- Not that correlation, mutual information or entropy settles the distinction. They are symmetric and ubiquitous.
- Not that "state or bias" is one negative case. It is at least four, and the four are kept separate.
- Not that this is a partition. It is a union compound (Fig 9 :55-57).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is asserted. The second seat shares this author's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Mark-erase:** remove/prevent the mark with habitat and receiver fixed; does the offspring reading change?
2. **Habitat substitution:** cross-foster or transfer embryos with the mark fixed; does the reading follow the habitat?
3. **Receiver variation:** vary the mark with other inputs fixed; does an evolved response change with it? This is the Jablonka interpreter condition.
4. **Anchor test:** does the classification change when the expected answer changes? It must not (Fig 2 :33).
5. **Per-slot scoring:** report each mark/locus separately, not as a global "information content" (Fig 1 :19, :22).
6. **Falsifier set:** bond recoverable from operands; partition demonstrated; symmetric statistic sufficient; anchor updates; a figure line naming the transmitter; settlement without a discriminator (`01` §4).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, :46, :111, :117 and Operators :12-15, :30-33 (six short passages).
2. Check C2 against your own field: are the catalyst, head start, bias and habitat readings faithful to the cases you know?
3. Name one experiment that would vary the mark while holding habitat and receiver fixed, or say why none is possible.
4. Say what you would count as a failure of the separator. Send that, not an endorsement.
