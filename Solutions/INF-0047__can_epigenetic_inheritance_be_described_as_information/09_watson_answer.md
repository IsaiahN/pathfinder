## QUESTION

Option A: accept that a carried mark is a message and that state or bias is the negative case where no message exists. - **Domain 1, mechanism and inheritance.** Actors: methylation marks, histone states, small RNAs, prions, structural templates, germline, zygote. Currency: molecular copies and mark persistence. Anchor: cross-fosting, embryo transfer, germline-specific erase, mark rescue. Nameable, so a genuine domain. Question type: partition at first order, is a mark carried across the boundary.

Anchor: an evolved discriminating receiver response and an intervention that varies the mark. Nameable. Question type: ordered bond versus flat co-presence.

Currency: heritable phenotypic variation and propensity to vary. Anchor: mutation accumulation lines, experimental evolution, habitat substitution. Nameable. Question type: union with D1 and D2, since bias can produce the same reading with no mark.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator survives; the two-way partition and the molecule name do not. A carried mark is an operand. "Information transmission" is licensed only for an ordered, typed, priced bond to a receiver whose response varies with the mark, tested against an anchor that does not update on the classification. A symmetric correlation cannot supply the order. Persisting part, head start, prior bias and substituted habitat are four distinct conditions, isomers over the shared atom set, and the observed resemblance is a union of them, not a harder case of one. The framework returns this shape and withholds the molecule name; naming a carrier is a provenanced binding, a candidate, not a derivation. This is `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, ungrounded, candidate. The live gap is who counts as the receiver when contact cascades more than one step.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter. A second cost is conceptual: treating a symmetric correlation as a message would let any physical correlation be called information, which the SEP entry calls ubiquitous.
- **undoable:** yes. The molecule names are withheld and the negative cases are kept separate, so a refutation removes a bond assignment without forcing a primitive to be withdrawn. A sent packet can be superseded by the corrected one. The union reading is reversible per slot: a lever that clears a single-condition region does not need to be applied to the intersections.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A mark-erase experiment with habitat and receiver fixed, a habitat-substitution experiment with the mark fixed, and a receiver-variation experiment with both fixed would assign the source. A demonstration that the bond is recoverable from the operands, that the four conditions are mutually exclusive, that a symmetric statistic suffices, or that the anchor updates would break the separator. A verbatim figure line naming a transmitter, or a figure line returned by re-census that governs the receiver identity, would change the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
