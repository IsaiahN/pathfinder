## What the wider literature bears on ECN-0088

1. **The union is the field's own position.** The NBER survey names three non-exclusive explanations and says no single one accounts for all facts. That is the strongest single corroboration available, and it comes from a source whose authors are inside the field. It is still one witness, because it shares the compensation evidence pool with the Wikipedia syntheses and the governance sources (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The question as posed is the standard framing.** Both Wikipedia compensation articles open with the same split, competition for scarce talent versus self-dealing. The derivation does not invent the dichotomy; it refuses it as a bundle (Fig 9 :4-5, :54).
3. **The mirror chain has a name in the field.** Peer benchmarking to the 75th or 90th percentile, with a "Ratchet, Ratchet and Bingo" consultant culture, is the mutual-update collapse in the vocabulary of practitioners (Fig 2 :14-15, Fig 7 :36).
4. **The common cause has a name in statistics.** Firm size and industry predict both pay and governance, and the confounding literature specifies exactly when such a variable blocks a causal reading. This is the low-overlap witness for the anchor rule (Fig 2 :18-21).
5. **The talent camp is not one source.** Spence signalling separates a costly signal from a marginal product payment, so even a perfect talent story needs a discriminator (Fig 9 :32).
6. **The same reform has different valence under different bindings.** Say-on-pay evidence differs between binding and advisory forms, which is Fig 11 :49-51 in the language of corporate law.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Where a source could have been read as a conflict, for example the finding that highly paid CEOs improved profitability, it was kept as subset evidence for the union rather than used to flip the verdict (Fig 9 :4-5).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the survey.
- **Fig 2 :39-40** - *"Correlation has valence."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. A list of people who already favour one camp produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59** - *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Every participant in this debate has a stake. Record their verdict on stage 1 before any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as evidence.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which instrument would separate the sources; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a refutation. Record the verdict before showing any theory. Stage 1 precedes stage 2 because Fig 10 :52 says *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and Fig 2 :45 says *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figures extract, the NBER survey, and the submission venue. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method only:** offer the formula as a generator, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question because the sought-for shape is not a term it holds.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. As drafted it does, but only after the two double-counting corrections in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 are honoured. Do not restore "talent and productivity" or "power and weak governance" as four independent witnesses when adapting this for a new audience.
