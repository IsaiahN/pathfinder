## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and the same section says it *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0088, the shape is: a union of at least three conditions, kept separate, each with its own anchor, plus an instrument that discriminates their sources at the slot level. "Seventy percent talent and thirty percent power" is a name with no instrument behind it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The words "talent" and "power" are borrowed from the debate being examined, so the derivation restates them as a priced external outcome and a room whose reference updates on the payer.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, carried from the exemplar mapping: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, decisive here: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* The either/or in ECN-0088 is such a bundle.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here the obvious import is an exogenous identification strategy from econometrics or a rule change from corporate law. It must arrive with provenance and must explain a gap the compensation frame could not close. Simply saying "natural experiment" is not an import; it is a label for one.

*They're Scanning Books*, carried from the exemplar mapping: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list, which is why section 6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` retracts the attempt to force compensation terms onto it.

## 4. Source discrimination: why a salary cannot name its own cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the governing mechanism for this question:

- *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*
- An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, whether the source is one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered.
- *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*
- *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A reported executive salary is exactly such a reading. Marginal product, a costly signal of unobservable quality, and a rent from a captured room can each produce the same high number. The compensation dataset, however large, is the spectrometer reading green; it is not the instrument that separates the sources. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, described in effect terms: an outcome the executive did not move, read separately from the room's own benchmark.

## 5. Q2, answered: what licenses a particular cause? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework names a cause for a particular slot.** A cause is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** Not in this folder, but the surrounding debate does. Both Wikipedia compensation articles open by naming two causes and inviting the reader to choose. The framework's own rule says the answer is a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32), and the missing thing is an instrument, not a verdict. The correct form for ECN-0088 is the union plus the instrument description, not a chosen camp.

## 5b. Correction to the "shape, not a name" reading (kept visible)

An earlier reading of this rule, as applied in the exemplar, overstated it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. In ECN-0088 the analogous point is sharper: no new primitive is being sought at all. The atoms (pay, outcome, benchmark, contract, rule) are all already available. What is missing is a **discriminator**, which is an instrument, not a primitive and not a cause. So the `gap` in F8 is of the instrument kind, not the missing-primitive kind. This correction does not weaken the stop; it says precisely what is owed at the stop.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

Applied here: an appeal to a famous economist, a prestigious journal, or a large dataset can fund a check of the shape. It cannot decide whether a named executive's pay is priced or extracted. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list of readers selected because they already favour one camp buys a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :39-40).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, read directly: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the economics question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The honest output is a shape, its falsifiers, and the instrument that would close it.
