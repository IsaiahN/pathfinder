## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's conclusion, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the difference between what the pay-setting process awards and what an independently read outcome supports. Arity: at least three-place (executive slot, contract, outcome), indexed by slot and ordered by time; an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "excessive" and "scarce" are threshold relations, not properties carried by a person. Scale: one slot, the boardroom, the population, the career channel.
- **D1 Executive labour market.** Actors: executives, rival firms, recruiters. Currency: pay as a talent price. Anchor: an exogenous contribution measure. Admitted, though no specific instrument is held.
- **D2 Governance room.** Actors: board, committee, CEO, consultants. Currency: pay as bargaining output. Anchor: a CEO-recused decision or an imposed rule. Admitted.
- **D3 Measurement frame.** Actors: researchers and datasets. Currency: pay-outcome sensitivity. Anchor: data, but low independence. Admitted with flag.
- **D4 Institutions.** Actors: regulators, tax and accounting rules. Currency: rule changes. Anchor: an enacted change. Admitted.
- **X1 Talent as free-floating property.** No anchor independent of firm outcome. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Firm size or industry.** Already habitat conditions in D1 and D2. Absorbed, with common-cause status.

Union, not partition: D1 and D2 overlap in the same executive, D2 and D4 overlap through rule making, D3 reads all three. The chain order is perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3 :20), with the break at vocabulary and objective. My census matches the primary's four admitted domains and two exclusions. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 and the NBER survey, I derived it from Fig 2's two collapses and the confounding source: a common cause makes the two camps agree without independent evidence, which is only meaningful if the conditions are overlapping rather than exclusive. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low topical overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` section 8 and `07` section 2: three conditions, per-slot indexing, non-updating anchor, two witnesses not four, valence relative to residual, percentage is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ordinary debate answer, for example "pay is mostly rent because boards are weak, so cap it" or "pay is mostly talent because markets are efficient, so stop regulating it".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **union to partition:** the concreteness of a reform proposal collapses three overlapping conditions into one lever, losing Fig 9 :57;
- **per-slot to average:** the practical answer reaches for an industry ratio, losing Fig 1 :22;
- **anchor test to price reading:** the practical answer treats share price or median peer pay as the ground, losing Fig 2 :30 and :37-38;
- **shape to name:** the practical answer names a cause and a number, losing Fig 9 :32 and the instrument requirement.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a gap measured per executive needs an outcome that does not update on the executive's own report, and the room's benchmark is not such an outcome. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
