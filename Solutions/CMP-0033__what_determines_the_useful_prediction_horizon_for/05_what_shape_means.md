## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." `Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"`. So for this question a shape is the narrowed form of the horizon answer that can be stated before a system-specific number can be licensed. It constrains; it does not fill.

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So `per-slot boundary + four limiter classes + budget/absence split` is a shape. `This system is predictable for 14 days` is a name attached to a number, and no figure returns it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on bootstrapping vocabulary: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the ordinary terms already held (residual, slot, ground, closure, contact, cycle) and let the residual narrow which additional terms are owed.
- `Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."` and `Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"`. "Prediction horizon" is borrowed vocabulary from many fields; the derivation therefore describes the effect (residual crossing a tolerance at a per-slot boundary) rather than assuming the cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach artifact states what would fail, not what to believe.
- *Library of Babel*, on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" - the shape is recognized when the vocabulary used to search is the vocabulary the answer needs.

## 3. What licenses a name, or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a horizon, that means running the system and measuring residual growth against a ground. The ecology source (Wesselkamp et al.) makes the same demand explicit: forecast limits need "A verification reference, a scoring function, and a predictive error tolerance."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 provenance and shadow test), with the source recorded.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that forbids naming from a reading alone: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." In that essay the instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and the resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." For a horizon, a declining forecast score is a reading; it does not by itself say whether the cause was amplification, abstraction, new atoms or channel decay. Naming the cause from the score is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating measurement, not a name.

## 4. Why "one global horizon number" is the specific forbidden error

The question is phrased as if a single quantity were being asked for. Three separate rules refuse that framing:

- `Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."` The horizon is a vector of per-slot boundaries.
- `Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."` A global number is not merely imprecise; it is the operation that hides the live slot.
- `Fig 9 :54`: the problem is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question. A global number would average over conditions that require different remedies (import vs more search).

The shape holds all three visible and refuses to compress them into a scalar.

## 5. Q2 answered: what does "shape" mean here?

**A shape is a per-slot boundary condition on the residual, with a four-way limiter census and a budget/absence distinction, stated before any system-specific number is claimed.** It says what any acceptable answer must contain. It does not say when any given system fails. That requires a ground reading and is therefore `specified` here, never `grounded`.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like", that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Two things in this derivation are not missing primitives:

1. The **tolerance** is an ordinary term already available in the verification domain (D3). It is the scoring function the decision actually uses, exactly as the ecology source states. Binding it is legitimate; it is a decision convention, and a convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention, so it must be declared rather than assumed.
2. The **sign of a feedback loop** is not a name for a missing atom. `Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."` The figure supplies the term and the test.

What remains forbidden is naming the *cause* of a particular failure from a score alone (the metamerism point in section 3), and producing a system-specific number with no ground (the mode rule). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of agreeable recipients is the named collapse, not corroboration. Give the claim a budget to be tried against a ground; do not gather endorsements. The same essay adds: "You may all agree and be wrong."

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
