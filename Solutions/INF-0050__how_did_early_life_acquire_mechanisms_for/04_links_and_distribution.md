## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The bootstrap bind is alive, not a historical footnote.** Robertson and Joyce 2012 and Joyce and Szostak 2018 both state that the origin of the RNA world is not the same as the origin of life and that the details remain obscure. Walker and Davies 2013 add that a monomolecular replicator may be logically incapable of the hardware/software separation seen in known life. These are three different frames, but they cite overlapping experimental literature, so treat them as one pool when they agree.
2. **"Function before information" is a real result, not a metaphor.** Bernhardt and Tate 2010, quoting de Duve, argue that the conformational role of proto-mRNA preceded its informational role. That is a world-frame corroboration of Fig 12's functional group and the middle-link warning in Fig 3.
