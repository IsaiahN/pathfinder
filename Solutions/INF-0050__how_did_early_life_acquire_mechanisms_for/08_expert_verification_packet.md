# Expert verification packet: the Tether on early information mechanisms

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origin-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters come before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the origin of life is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Storage, copying, interpretation and compartmentalization are four conditions of one union, not four stages of a timeline. Storage must be a non-repeating bonded arrangement; copying must cross a fidelity threshold with a term that closure alone cannot supply; interpretation is a substructure whose presence fixes a consequence that preserves the arrangement; and the boundary is part of the capability. No designer or seventh primitive is licensed. The specific chemistry (RNA, PNA, cyanosulfidic chemistry, fatty-acid vesicles) is a provenanced binding from the chemistry frame, not a derivation from the figures. The historical route is `gap`, and the framework offers the shape any answer must have plus refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 12

- **Statement:** the residual is a union compound with four overlapping conditions; storage is a bonded arrangement and interpretation is a functional group, not a new primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 (line 502) *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 12 :117 (line 815) *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 (line 821) *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Refuter:** show one condition sufficient for all four across generations with no residue at the intersections; or admit a seventh primitive to the six-term set; or show a strictly periodic arrangement carrying heritable novelty.

### C2 — chemistry-facing; checkable by an origin-of-life researcher against the two main frames

- **Statement:** as written, RNA-first and cyanosulfidic protometabolism are not isomers under Fig 12's definition because their starting atom sets differ; the isomer claim survives only over the full shared atom set. Copying cannot improve inside a closed set without an import or a better instrument.
- **What to check:** Robertson and Joyce 2012 begin from activated nucleotides and a template; Patel et al. 2015 derive nucleotide, amino acid and lipid precursors together from HCN and H2S. Do those starting sets differ at the first step? And is the bootstrap bind real: with no replicase, can evolution search for a replicase, or must something copy first?
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 (line 414) *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :23 (line 292) *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** a ground-visible demonstration that a copy mechanism gains a new atom by pure composition inside a closed set, or that one frame requires exactly the same starting atoms as the other at the first step (which would clear the isomer claim as written).

### C3 — assay-facing; checkable against in vitro work on protocells and translation

- **Statement:** interpretation arrives as a functional consequence before it arrives as a code, and the compartment is part of the capability, not an accessory.
- **What to check:** in Bernhardt and Tate 2010, de Duve is quoted: *"the conformational function preceded the informational one and that it was, in fact, instrumental in bringing about the latter."* In Joyce and Szostak 2018: *"Compartmentalization enables the positive feedback required for Darwinian evolution by keeping useful products close to the catalysts that generated them and by keeping molecules related by descent physically closer, on average, compared with more distantly related molecules."* Does a well-mixed solution without compartments sustain open-ended evolvability despite exploitable shared products?
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 (line 657) *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 4 :21 (line 164) *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."*
- **Refuter:** an interpreted sequence with no substructure that fixes a measurable outcome; or open-ended evolvability in a well-mixed system with no spatial isolation despite parasitic templates.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that RNA, PNA, TNA or any other named polymer was the first informational molecule.
- Not that metabolism-first or RNA-first is the true route. Both are bindings with provenance and no source gate.
- Not that selection alone explains the origin, or that a designer was involved.
- Not that the genetic code's mapping was dictated by physics or chemistry.
- Not that this derives a conference abstract, a synthesis route, or an experiment. It gives a shape and a falsifier.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken by this author.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Specifically:

1. A synthesis under one prebiotic condition set that yields only one of the three precursor classes, with the others excluded, would close the simultaneous-origin route.
2. A copying experiment that crosses an error threshold from activated monomers under plausible conditions, with fidelity measured, would name the copying slot.
3. An assay showing a substructure that fixes a consequence preserving its own sequence, with the consequence measured, would settle the interpretation slot.
4. A protocell experiment that suppresses parasites and couples genotype to phenotype in the same compartment would settle the boundary slot.

2. Check C2 against Robertson and Joyce 2012 and Patel et al. 2015: do the starting atom sets differ at step one? Is the bootstrap bind stated as claimed?
