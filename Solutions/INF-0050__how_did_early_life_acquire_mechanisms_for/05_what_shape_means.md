## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

For INF-0050 the shape is: four conditions that must be met, with each slot left open for a chemistry to be named from outside the framework. A shape is what can be said before the occupant is known. "Some molecule held a non-repeating difference" is a shape. "RNA was first" is a name.

The same essay gives the stopping rule: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Applied here, that means stopping when the four slots are described well enough that any candidate chemistry can be checked against them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The modern DNA sequence is a projection; its route of arrival is not written in it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends the refuters, not a chemistry.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* Two routes that end in the same molecule are not the same route, so provenance has to be recorded rather than reconstructed.

The essays introduce two horizons relevant to this question. A **distinction horizon** is where one observer can no longer tell two generation routes apart. A **union horizon** is where no observer with any available instrument can. Crucially, being below the union horizon is *"never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* That is exactly the status of the historical route in F8.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* An in vitro experiment that shows a specific polymer copying itself under prebiotic conditions pays for that specific name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming RNA as the occupant of the storage slot is an import from the chemistry frame, and it must be marked as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Promoting a guessed primitive into the answer to a historical question would hide the guess.

## 4. The source problem, applied to the origin of life

This is the deepest reason the historical question cannot be read off the figures or off modern biology. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Metamerism gives one reading from several sources. The essay's resolution: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here, a modern cell reads as a system with DNA storage, RNA intermediates, protein catalysts and a lipid membrane. That reading does not say how the four arrived, because several routes could produce it. The spectrometer analogy says what is owed: an instrument that discriminates among routes, not a better argument about which route feels more likely. Until then the route is `gap`, and a `gap` is a verdict, not a silence.

## 5. Q: what licenses naming RNA as the first information polymer?

**Answer: nothing in the framework.** Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README return a shape. RNA is licensed only by a ground reading that settles it, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Neither has been supplied in this package. The sources actually read include strong evidence that RNA can fold, catalyse and carry information (Robertson and Joyce 2012; Joyce and Szostak 2018) and equally strong evidence for precursor polymers and for simultaneous synthesis of all three subsystems (Patel et al. 2015; Walker and Davies 2013). Their agreement is a shared evidence pool, not corroboration from independent frames.

**Correction to my own §3.** I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming any specific molecule because it returns a shape, not a name. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like,"* a primitive not held in Γ. Naming a molecule from an outside frame is permitted as a binding with provenance. What remains forbidden is presenting that binding as a figure derivation or as the framework's answer. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a chemical story is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a trial by someone who can refuse the shape.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the caveat that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any origin-of-life hypothesis and cannot settle the question it reports on.
