## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question is not "when did life begin" but: before a modern cell existed, what had to be in place for three capacities to appear together? Restate each as an effect:

- **Storage:** something must hold a difference that can outlast the event that made it, and the difference must not be mere repetition.
- **Copying:** something must make a further instance of that difference with enough fidelity that the copy outruns decay, and the copying must be able to improve.
- **Interpretation:** something must let the held difference fix a consequence in the world, so that the consequence feeds back and preserves the difference.

A fourth condition is forced by the coupling: the three must belong to the same entity, or the product of a catalyst diffuses away and benefits a neighbour instead (Joyce and Szostak 2018). Call it compartmentalization.

R is indexed per capacity, never averaged. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A single number like "life arose" hides which of the four slots is live, which is exactly the question. Arity: four conditions, joined as a union. Symmetry: none of the four is a symmetric join; copying is ordered (`→`, `⇒`), interpretation is a comparison or a functional group, storage is a bonded arrangement. Scale: molecular, protocell, cell, crossing a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :21).

**Question as tether (Direction).** Write down what an answer would look like before searching: an answer names, for each of the four slots, (a) the actor that occupies it, (b) the ground reading that settles it, and (c) why the other three conditions are still bound when that slot changes. Anything less is a story, not an answer. This binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, per Formula step "Direction".

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The residual is a union compound, not a linear origin story (decisive for method)

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The standard narrative orders the question as a timeline: first monomers, then a replicator, then a code, then a cell. That is a partition reading of a union problem. Sutherland's group found that precursors of RNA, protein and lipid can be made together, and explicitly framed the old question as *"did the subsystems emerge together, or one after the other at the origin of life?"* The figure verdict is that one lever cannot clear a union.

**Refuter:** show one condition sufficient for all four across generations, with no residue in the intersections (for example, open-ended evolvability with no heritable difference, or heredity with no functional consequence and no parasite crash).

**Mode:** `general` for the union rule, `specified` for its application here.

### F2 — Storage is a bonded arrangement; periodicity cannot carry novelty

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

Schrödinger's aperiodic crystal is the historical form of this finding: the Nobel history page records that a gene could be stable if it is *"something with a regular but non-repeating structure."* A perfect crystal is information poor because repetition carries no novelty. The figure version is stricter: same atoms under a different bond are different substances, and the bond, not the ingredient list, carries the difference. A phosphodiester linkage is a typed relation; a 2',5' linkage and a 3',5' linkage are different substances from the same nucleotides.

**Important mapping caveat (strain, named).** Fig 12's "bond" is a typed *logical* relation between operands; a phosphodiester bond is a *chemical* relation between nucleotides. The mapping is plausible, not derived. Naming which chemistry occupied the slot is a binding from the chemistry frame, not a figure result.

**Refuter:** a strictly periodic arrangement carrying heritable novelty, or two arrangements with the same atoms and the same bond type showing different heritable behaviour.

**Mode:** `general` for the arrangement rule, `specified` for the chemistry mapping. Triangulated (Fig 12 plus the Nobel/Schrrödinger history, low overlap).

### F3 — Copying needs a mint that closure alone cannot supply

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Robertson and Joyce state the bind directly: *"Without evolution it appears unlikely that a self-replicating ribozyme could arise, but without some form of self-replication there is no way to conduct an evolutionary search for the first, primitive self-replicating ribozyme."* In formula terms this is step 7: no term in `closure(Γ)` closes R. Ask SEARCHED first (is it in here and unfound?), then INWARD (a better instrument, for example the imidazolium-bridged dinucleotide mechanism in Joyce and Szostak 2018), then OUTWARD (import a different closure: mineral surfaces, p-RNA/TNA/GNA, or a metabolism-first analogue frame). These three are distinct and INWARD and OUTWARD are never read against each other.

Formula step 3 gates any candidate: SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0`, REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)`, NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`, *"gated by a product; any factor at zero forces inertness."* Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A proposed ribozyme is a candidate until an experiment pays for it.

**Refuter:** a copying mechanism that gains a new atom by pure composition inside a closed Γ, or a fidelity improvement that persists with no ground reading.

**Mode:** `general` for idempotence, `specified` for the prebiotic case.

### F4 — Interpretation is a functional group settled by the ground, not a fourth primitive

Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

"Interpreting information" invites a homunculus: something inside the molecule that reads the sequence. The figures refuse a new member for this. Fig 13 :3: *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* There is no seventh term called "meaning." What is required is a substructure whose presence fixes an outcome: an aminoacylated 3' CCA end, an anticodon loop, a folding ribozyme. Walker and Davies (2013) make the same point in information terms: biological information has a *"functionality"* that is *"decisively non-local"* and context-dependent, so the bits alone do not determine what is meaningful. Bernhardt and Tate (2010), quoting de Duve, report that *"the conformational function preceded the informational one and that it was, in fact, instrumental in bringing about the latter."* That is Fig 12's functional group arriving before the code is named.

This is the middle link of Fig 3. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The instrument here is an in vitro assay that shows a sequence changes a consequence.

**Refuter:** a sequence called "interpreted" where no substructure fixes an outcome that can be measured, or a seventh primitive admitted to the six-term set.

**Mode:** `specified`. Triangulated for the structure (Fig 12 plus Fig 3, low overlap); the chemistry mapping remains single-source and unmeasured.

### F5 — Before a seat existed, selection ran

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."*

The origin-of-life setting is exactly Fig 11's unmaintained mode: no maintainer, no live frame in the seat, habitat as pure contact. This removes the need for a designer, a first reader, or a bootstrapper that "knew" the code. It also removes the comfort of asking who chose the first useful sequence: nobody did. The metalaws note says the figures predate evolution and are not a product of it, so applying them to pre-biotic chemistry is legitimate rather than anachronistic.

**Refuter:** evidence of a maintaining seat acting before the first heritable difference was settled at the ground, in a system where the seat's action was necessary for the difference to persist.

**Mode:** `general` for the unmaintained-mode rule, `specified` for its application here.

### F6 — Compartmentalization is the membrane that makes the union work

Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Joyce and Szostak: *"Compartmentalization enables the positive feedback required for Darwinian evolution by keeping useful products close to the catalysts that generated them and by keeping molecules related by descent physically closer, on average, compared with more distantly related molecules."* A naked replicator is not a capable system. The vesicle is a filter with a direction: monomers cross, lineages stay, parasites are contained. Fig 13 :22 names the spendable difference that drives it: *"a difference that can be spent."* England's dissipation-driven adaptation, as reported by Wolchover (2014), is a low-overlap corroboration: *"A great way of dissipating more is to make more copies of yourself."*

**Refuter:** open-ended evolvability sustained in a well-mixed solution with no compartment and no spatial isolation, despite parasites that exploit shared products.

**Mode:** `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 4 plus Fig 11 plus the protocell experiments).

### F7 — The framework returns a shape, not a name: the RNA world is a binding

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The canonical README says a missing thing would have to look like *"a shape, not a name."*

So the figure verdict cannot be "RNA was first," nor "metabolism was first," nor "PNA came before RNA." Those are bindings from world frames onto the four slots. Robertson and Joyce themselves say the RNA-first view *"inevitably triggers 'The Prebiotic Chemist's Nightmare'"* and that *"it is fruitful to consider the alternative possibility that RNA was preceded by some other replicating, evolving molecule."* Walker and Davies argue the same at the informational level: monomolecular systems are divided from known life by a logical gap. The honest figure output is the shape: a non-periodic arrangement that is copied with fidelity, has a consequence that preserves it, and is coupled to a compartment. Which molecule occupied each slot is owed to the ground.

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a specific prebiotic polymer as the occupant of a slot. None exists after re-census; a line one is found, this finding is withdrawn.

**Mode:** `general` for the limit, `specified` for its application. Triangulated (Fig 9 plus canonical README).

### F8 — Which route actually happened is unreachable at this budget, not silence

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The historical route is underdetermined by the figures and by the sources actually read. There are at least three live frames: RNA-first (Robertson and Joyce 2012), cyanosulfidic protometabolism where all three subsystems arise simultaneously (Patel and Sutherland 2015), and an algorithmic transition where information gains causal efficacy only after a hardware/software separation (Walker and Davies 2013). These draw on overlapping chemical evidence, so their agreement is one pool, not three witnesses. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget are different facts; here it is the latter, and the remedy is more search with independent instruments, not a new primitive imported to make the story feel complete.

**Refuter:** a ground-visible experiment that closes one route and excludes the others, for example a prebiotic synthesis that yields only RNA-like polymers under the same conditions that exclude peptide and lipid precursors.

**Mode:** `specified` for the verdict `gap / unreached`; the honest label is not "no answer."

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A heritable difference persists with no spendable gradient and no cross-kind composition (breaks Fig 13 :22 and :75).
2. A copy mechanism gains a new atom by pure composition inside a closed Γ (breaks Fig 8 :32).
3. A sequence is called interpreted while no substructure fixes a measurable outcome (breaks Fig 12 :123 and Formula step 5).
4. A strictly periodic arrangement carries heritable novelty (breaks Fig 12 :117 and the aperiodic-crystal finding).
5. One of the four conditions suffices for all four, with no residue at the intersections (breaks Fig 9 :55-57).
6. A seventh primitive is admitted to the six-term set to carry "meaning" (breaks Fig 13 :3).
7. Open-ended evolvability is demonstrated in a well-mixed system with no compartment and no spatial isolation despite exploitable shared products (breaks F6).

If any fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn, not softened. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union and its four slots, not with a chemical name. A single-first-cause story fails Fig 9 :55-57.
2. Treat "interpretation" as an assay question: which substructure fixes which outcome, and what reads it? If there is no instrument, say so (Fig 3 :27).
3. Report bindings as bindings. RNA, p-RNA, HCN chemistry and fatty-acid vesicles are imports with provenance, not figure derivations.
4. Distinguish underivable from unreached. The historical route is unreached at this budget; importing a favorite polymer to finish the story adds an atom that was not needed.
5. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, not this audit, to a checker.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is one first cause enough?** No. Fig 9 :55-57 gives a union with four overlapping conditions.
2. **What carries storage?** A bonded arrangement, not an ingredient list (Fig 12 :94). Aperiodic, not repeating.
3. **Can copying improve itself inside one closure?** No (Fig 8 :32). SEARCHED first, then INWARD or OUTWARD; a mint needs SUPPORT, REACH, NOVELTY and settlement.
4. **Is interpretation a new member?** No (Fig 13 :3). It is a functional group (Fig 12 :123) whose outcome is settled at the ground.
5. **Is a designer needed to set the first meaning?** No (Fig 11 :36). Unmaintained mode: selection ran.
6. **Can a naked replicator do it alone?** No (Fig 11 :39-40). Compartment and habitat are part of the capability.
7. **Which chemistry won?** Not derivable. Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README return a shape; the route is `gap / unreached` (Fig 13 :134).

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: a four-condition union in which a non-periodic bonded arrangement is copied past a fidelity threshold, fixes a measurable consequence that preserves it, and is coupled to a compartment; all of this is settled by experiments, not by agreement, and no designer or seventh primitive is licensed. Every chemical name is a provenanced binding. Strains after re-census: one, the logical-bond to chemical-bond mapping in F2, named and not hidden. Mode `specified`, nothing `grounded`.
