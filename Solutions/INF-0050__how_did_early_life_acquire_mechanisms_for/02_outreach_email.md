# Outreach draft: check the shape first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four slots and the refuters below are stated exactly as written. If the shape cannot survive a twenty-minute check, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** Four conditions for early information mechanisms, and what would break them

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [your name]. I have one specific shape I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the shape does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** Before modern cells existed, something had to store a non-repeating difference, copy it with enough fidelity to outrun decay, let that difference fix a consequence that preserved it, and keep all three in one place. Call these storage, copying, interpretation and compartmentalization. The claim is that these are four conditions of one union, not four stages of a single timeline. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A lever that clears only one leaves the intersections standing.
>
> **The shape (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Any answer must name, for each of the four slots, the actor that occupies it and the ground reading that settles it. The framework does not name the chemistry. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* RNA-first, metabolism-first and precursor-polymer accounts are bindings from their own frames, not deductions from the shape.
>
> **What is explicitly not claimed:** that RNA was the first information polymer; that a single replicator did all four jobs; that "meaning" is a new physical primitive; that we know which historical route occurred. Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The route is recorded as unreached, not as no answer.
>
> **What would refute the shape. I wrote these down before sending this:**
> 1. A heritable difference persists with no spendable gradient and no cross-kind composition.
> 2. A copying mechanism gains a new atom by pure composition inside a closed set, with no import.
> 3. A sequence is called interpreted while no substructure fixes a measurable outcome.
> 4. A strictly periodic arrangement carries heritable novelty.
> 5. One of the four conditions suffices for all four across generations, with no residue at the intersections.
> 6. A seventh primitive has to be added to carry "meaning."
> 7. Open-ended evolvability is shown in a well-mixed system with no compartment and no spatial isolation despite exploitable shared products.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding part of the shape is wrong and I will withdraw it. A one-line "this fails because..." is far more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode (general, specified or grounded) of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
