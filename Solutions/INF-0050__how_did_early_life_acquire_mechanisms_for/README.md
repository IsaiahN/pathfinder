## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe this.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A list of agreeable readers is a convention, not corroboration.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* FIG 2 :40 (line 90): *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 (line 558): *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must read the residual and the shape only, not the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk mail to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
