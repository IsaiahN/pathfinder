## QUESTION

How did early life acquire mechanisms for storing, copying and interpreting information before modern cellular systems existed? Restated as an effect: before a modern cell existed, what had to be in place for a non-repeating difference to persist, be copied with improving fidelity, fix a consequence that preserved it, and stay inside one lineage? Option A: name a chemistry as the answer (RNA-first, or metabolism-first, or a precursor polymer). Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the chemical names until a ground settles them. Option A sends a name where the framework returns a shape and spends the independence of the people checking it. Option B sends a checkable shape, seven written refuters, and an open slot.

- **D2 Heredity and copying.** Actors: templates, base pairs, imidazolium-bridged dinucleotide, ligases, replicase ribozymes. Currency: sequence difference, fidelity, error threshold. Anchor: in vitro copying (Robertson and Joyce 2012; Joyce and Szostak 2018). Status: admitted.

- **D4 Compartmentalization.** Actors: fatty-acid vesicles, porous vents, mineral grains, coacervates. Currency: containment, genotype/phenotype coupling, parasite suppression. Anchor: protocell growth and competition (Joyce and Szostak 2018). Status: admitted.

- **X2 Vital force.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain; Fig 11 :36 removes the label.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the chemical names do not. Storage, copying, interpretation and compartmentalization form a union compound whose slots must each be occupied by something and settled by a ground reading. Storage is a non-repeating bonded arrangement; copying cannot improve inside one closure; interpretation is a functional group whose outcome settles a sequence, not a seventh primitive; the boundary is part of the capability; and no designer is licensed. RNA-first, metabolism-first and precursor-polymer accounts are provenanced bindings from their own frames, and which route actually occurred is `gap / unreached`, not no answer. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, seven refuters and its lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a chemical name before the shape has been checked. Fig 2 :42 (line 93): *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Undoable:** yes. The chemical bindings are removable without touching the shape derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing, so the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :43, line 314), and the record keeps the route.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A synthesis under one condition set that excludes the other precursor classes; a copying experiment that crosses an error threshold with measured fidelity; an assay that names the substructure fixing a self-preserving consequence; a protocell experiment that suppresses parasites and couples genotype to phenotype; or any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
