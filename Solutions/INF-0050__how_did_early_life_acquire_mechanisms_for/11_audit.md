## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. The residual line gives three named capacities plus a coupling, so the census starts with four slots and refuses to average them.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four conditions. Storage holds a difference that outlives its cause. Copying makes a further instance that outruns decay and can improve. Interpretation lets the difference fix a consequence that preserves it. Compartmentalization keeps the three in one lineage. Fig 1 :19 says *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Symmetry: none of the four is a symmetric join. Copying is ordered, production runs one way; interpretation is a comparison or a functional group; storage is a bonded arrangement; containment is a filter with a direction. Scale: molecular, protocell, cell, crossing a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :21). Verdict: a union compound, not one hard question, exactly as Fig 9 :55 puts it, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **D1 Prebiotic physical substrate.** Actors: nucleotides and analogues, HCN and its derivatives, UV light, mineral surfaces, fatty acids, hydrothermal gradients. Currency: bond formation and dissipated free energy. Anchor: laboratory prechemistry. A nameable anchor exists, so this is a domain. It is the unmaintained habitat of Fig 11.
- **D2 Heredity and copying.** Actors: templates, base pairs, an imidazolium-bridged dinucleotide, ligases, replicase ribozymes. Currency: sequence difference, replication fidelity, error threshold. Anchor: in vitro copying experiments. Admitted.
- **D3 Interpretation and function.** Actors: aptamers, ribozymes, aminoacylated hairpins, proto-tRNAs, proto-mRNA. Currency: a functional consequence, binding or catalysis or peptide product. Anchor: an assay showing a sequence produces the function. Admitted, and the middle link of Fig 3 with the least instrumentation.
- **D4 Compartmentalization.** Actors: fatty-acid vesicles, porous vents, mineral grains, coacervates. Currency: containment, genotype/phenotype coupling, parasite suppression. Anchor: protocell growth and competition experiments. Admitted.
- **X1 A first replicator alone.** One actor, one currency (replication rate), but no nameable anchor for the other three slots. Not a domain yet. Absorbed into the D2 and D4 union.
- **X2 Vital force or life force.** No nameable actors, no currency, no anchor. Not a domain. Fig 11 :36 removes the label.

**Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :57 says *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A lever that clears storage alone leaves the intersection where copying or interpretation still binds. There is no single first cause to find.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Chemical contact before vocabulary (an information bearing molecule) before objective (a copy that persists) before planning (selection among variants) before learn and carry (heredity). This orders dependency, not historical arrival. Fig 3 :19 says *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* D3 is the middle link that Fig 3 :27 calls *"a link nobody has looked at."*

I reached the same four admitted domains and the same two rejected pseudo domains as the primary, but by a different route: I started from the three named capacities in the residual line plus the diffusion objection, whereas the primary started from a figure census and worked back. Low overlap between routes, still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 12 :94 and :117 (bonded arrangement), Fig 12 :123 (functional group), Fig 8 :32 and Fig 6 :23 (closure and import), Fig 11 :36 and :39-40 (unmaintained selection, capability in the habitat), Fig 4 :21 (filter with direction), and Fig 13 :134 (unreachable is a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: four conditions of one union, non repeating arrangement, fidelity threshold with a term closure cannot supply, consequence that preserves, boundary as part of capability, no designer, chemistry is a binding.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete origin of life claim, for example RNA world first, or cyanosulfidic protometabolism with all three precursor classes at once, or an algorithmic transition where top down causation appears.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the occupants**, which is the whole historical question and which the shorthand deliberately leaves open;
- the **striped status of each claim**, general for the figure rules, specified for the chemistry mapping, nothing grounded, which a retelling as a result would erase;
- the **seven falsifiers**, which are conditions on a ground reading and vanish if the shape is reused as a slogan.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a four condition union in which a non repeating arrangement must be copied, fix a preserving consequence, and stay inside a boundary. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## 5. Strains and re-census

- **Fig 12 logical bond versus chemical phosphodiester bond (live, named).** Fig 12 :117 says same atoms, different bond, different substance. A 2',5' and a 3',5' linkage are different chemical substances, but the figure's bond is a typed logical relation between operands. The mapping is plausible, not derived. Re-census leaves this a `specified` binding with a falsifier, never a figure verdict. The primary names it the same way in F2; no hidden smoothing.
- **Selection without direction (dissolved on re-census).** Fig 11 :36 has selection running with no maintainer. An apparent strain with the need for direction is removed by Fig 13 :22, the spendable difference: the gradient supplies direction without a seat. Not a conflict after re-census.
- **Function before code versus code before function (not a strain).** Bernhardt and Tate 2010, quoting de Duve, put conformational function before informational function. That is Fig 12 :123's functional group arriving before a code is named, so the chemistry and figure readings point the same way.
- **One route or simultaneous union (a budget question, not a figure conflict).** Patel et al. 2015 derive three precursor classes together; Robertson and Joyce 2012 begin from activated nucleotides and a template. Under Fig 8 these are competing frames whose starting atom sets differ. The figures do not pick between them; that is `gap / unreached` under Fig 13 :134, not a contradiction.
- **After re-census: one strain, named, none hidden.**
