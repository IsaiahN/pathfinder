# The Tether's answer: how early life acquired information mechanisms

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and per-domain verdicts, `general` on structural constraints, nothing `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Before modern cells, three capacities and their coupling had to arise: a non-repeating difference that persists (storage), a copy of it that outruns decay and can improve (copying), a consequence that preserves it (interpretation), and a boundary keeping the three in one lineage (compartment). R is indexed per capacity, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). It is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55-57).

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **The four conditions are a union.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A lever that clears storage alone leaves the intersection where copying or interpretation still binds. There is no single first cause to identify.
2. **Storage is a bonded arrangement, not an ingredient list.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* Repetition is information poor; a difference must be carried by the bond type or the order. The chemistry that did this is a provenanced binding, not a figure result.
3. **Copying needs a term that closure alone cannot supply.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Robertson and Joyce 2012 state the bootstrap bind plainly. Ask SEARCHED first, then INWARD (a better instrument) or OUTWARD (import a different frame). Any candidate is gated by SUPPORT, REACH and NOVELTY and is still only a candidate until settled (Formula step 5).
4. **Interpretation is a functional group settled at the ground.** Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* No seventh primitive is admitted for "meaning" (Fig 13 :3). Bernhardt and Tate 2010, quoting de Duve, report that the conformational function preceded the informational one. Walker and Davies 2013 add that functionality is context-dependent and non-local, so the bits alone do not decide what is meaningful.
5. **Before a maintainer existed, selection ran.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* No designer, first reader or bootstrapper is licensed.
6. **The boundary is part of the capability.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* A naked replicator is not a capable system; parasites crash well-mixed solutions.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* RNA-first, metabolism-first and precursor-polymer accounts are bindings from their own frames. Which route actually occurred is `gap`, and unreachable is a verdict (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that RNA was first, or that any other named polymer was first.
- Not that a single replicator did all four jobs, or that the four appeared in a fixed chronological order. Fig 3 orders dependencies, not dates.
- Not that "meaning" is a new physical primitive, or that the genetic code's mapping was dictated by physics.
- Not that selection alone explains the origin. Fig 11 :36 says selection ran in the unmaintained mode; it does not say this was a maintainer's garden.
- Not that a room of agreeable experts settles anything. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- Not `grounded`. No experiment in this package was run by this author.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The historical route needs a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a prebiotic synthesis under one set of conditions yield nucleotides, amino acids and lipids together, or only one class? Patel et al. 2015 is one reading; it needs independent replication under different conditions before it closes the route.
- Can a copying mechanism be shown to cross an error threshold from monomers under plausible conditions, with the fidelity measured rather than assumed? Robertson and Joyce 2012 and Joyce and Szostak 2018 set the instrument.
- Does a sequence change a consequence that preserves it, with the substructure named and the assay stated? That is the Fig 12 :123 functional-group test.
- Does compartmentalization suppress parasites and couple genotype to phenotype in the same experiment? That is Joyce and Szostak 2018's protocell test.
- Standing falsifiers from §4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: no spendable gradient; a new atom from pure composition; an "interpreted" sequence with no measurable consequence; a periodic carrier of novelty; one condition sufficient for all four; a seventh primitive for meaning; open-ended evolvability in a well-mixed system.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the six makes the choice visible, not neutral. The route stops at the limit of the instruments actually read and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A story that ends in a modern cell is not the route that got there.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a first molecule and does not claim to have solved the origin of life. It says that storage, copying, interpretation and compartmentalization are four conditions of one union, not four stages of a single timeline; that storage must be a non-repeating bonded arrangement, copying must cross a fidelity threshold with a term that closure alone cannot supply, interpretation is a substructure whose presence fixes a consequence that preserves it, and the boundary is part of the capability rather than an accessory. No designer or seventh primitive is licensed, and the specific chemistry is a binding from the chemistry frame, not a figure derivation. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The historical route stays open until a source-discriminating experiment settles it.
