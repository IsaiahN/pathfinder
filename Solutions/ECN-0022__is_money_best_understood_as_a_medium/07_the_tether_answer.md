# The Tether's answer on the nature of money

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Both candidate accounts predict the same reading: a thing is accepted in exchange, discharges a debt, and holds value. What is undecided is the source of that standing. The offered "both" reveals that the question is a bundle. Fig 9 :4-5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* So the Tether does not pick institution, commodity, or a blend. It gives the shape any correct answer must have, and keeps the per-domain verdicts separate.

## 2. The shape

1. **The question splits, and "both" is a union.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The institutional account governs the creation of modern money and the tax-and-clearing anchor; the commodity account governs the anchor function that a non-monetary demand can supply and the collapsed-trust regimes. The both-cell (gold coin as legal tender, deposit held as an asset) is a compound with its own loop, not a centrist average. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
2. **Money is an anchored convention.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* Collective acceptance is what money is, not a defect to be explained away. But Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* and Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* A legal-tender declaration names a thing; it does not settle its value. The standing must be ranged against something that does not move with what frames think: Fig 2 :29-30, *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* That is the market clearing, the payment system, the tax demand.
3. **"Medium" is a structure; "asset" is an operand.** Fig 13 :24 lists network among the structures: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"* A medium of exchange is that structure. A commodity-like asset is the state-like thing the structure carries. Asking which one money "really is" mixes a structure with its operand. Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
4. **The reading does not determine the source.** Commodity value, state taxation, bank credit creation and network effect all produce "accepted as money." An instrument that reads acceptance *"cannot comment on the source"* (*The Hard Problem is About Source*). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* So the framework returns a shape, not a source name. A source-discriminating observation is owed before any one account is crowned.
5. **The substrate mapping is a binding, not a derivation.** Calling commodity money the energy term and credit money the network term adds no atom and moves no wall (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*). It is recorded as a visible choice with provenance, and retracted as a derivation (`01` F7).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one account is the single "best" essence. The question is a union and must be unbundled.
- Not that agreement about money's value is corruption. For a convention it is the product (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that legal tender grounds value. Declaring a meaning does not ground it (Fig 10 :49).
- Not that the framework can name the source of money's standing. It returns a shape (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that commodity money is the framework's energy term. That binding was retracted (`01` F7).
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating monetary observation was used or claimed.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved green. Here that would be an observation in which acceptance picks out one source. The four standing falsifiers are in `01` §6 and `08` §2. Restated so each can fire:

- **One-account test:** a single account governing every regime with no residual left in any subset (breaks the split).
- **Anchor test:** a regime whose standing is pure mutual belief, no market, clearing or tax anchor, that does not drift (breaks the anchor requirement).
- **Source test:** acceptance uniquely determines commodity, state or credit as the source (breaks the metamerism claim).
- **Structure test:** money is a state with no network structure required (breaks the medium-as-structure reading).

The world record supplies half the ground already: bank deposit creation is a published, checkable fact (Bank of England, 2014), and the anthropological record does not support a pure barter origin (Humphrey, Graeber, as reported). Neither alone settles the source of money's standing across regimes.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the medium as network makes a choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available monetary instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Absence of a source-discriminating observation is not proof that no source could be discriminated.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner between the institutional and commodity accounts of money, and it does not blend them. It says the question is a bundle: each account governs a different subset, and "both" is a union of overlapping conditions, so the two verdicts must be kept separate. What any correct account must look like is an anchored convention and a network medium. Agreement about money's value is the product, not a corruption; but declaring a meaning does not ground it, so its standing must be ranged against something that does not move with belief, a market clearing, a payment system, a tax demand. The commodity account and the institutional account each supply one such anchor. Acceptance alone cannot tell you which source produced a money, so the framework stops at a shape, records the lineage, and names the four observations that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
