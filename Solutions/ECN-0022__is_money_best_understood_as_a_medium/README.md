## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks which account of money is "best." In effect terms: a thing is accepted in exchange, settles debts, and holds value. Both candidate accounts predict that same reading. What is undecided is the *source* of that standing: a legal and institutional act (banks create deposits by lending; a state declares legal tender and demands taxes), or an intrinsic property (a scarce, durable, desirable commodity). The third option, "both," asks whether these are rival names or overlapping conditions. What an answer would look like is a shape that any correct account must have, plus the domains in which each candidate governs. Bind that as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction") and let it accrue a residual like anything else.

## What this solution does and does not claim

It derives a shape from the figures, not a new theory of money. It does not settle the question. By *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, LLM output that uses the formula is "simulacra" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
