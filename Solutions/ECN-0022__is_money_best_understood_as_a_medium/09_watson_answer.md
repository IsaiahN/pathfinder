## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that money is best understood as a medium created by banks and public institutions, as a commodity-like asset, or both. Option A: one of the two accounts is the single best essence. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the single-essence framing. What each leads to: A delivers a `general`-mode verdict that averages across slots, hides where each account governs, and ignores that acceptance cannot discriminate sources; B delivers a checkable shape, separate per-domain verdicts, written refuters, and an open source until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The disagreement about what gives money its standing. Arity: three offered options, structurally two conditions plus their intersection. Symmetry: asymmetric, one account is a legal and institutional fact, the other a physical and intrinsic property. Scale: a single transaction (first order) against the monetary system (second order). Fig 9 :4-5 names it several well-formed questions rather than one hard question: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The two conditions overlap, so it is a **union**, not a partition.
- **Domain 1, the ground of monetary value.** Actors: buyers, sellers, banks, clearing houses, tax offices. Currency: acceptance and settlement. Anchor: a market clearing, a payment system, a tax demand. It does not move with belief. Question type: which anchor settles value in this regime.
- **Domain 2, the source of that standing.** Actors: commodity, state, banks, the accepting network. Currency: the identical reading, accepted as money. Anchor: a source-discriminating observation; absent one, the source stays open. Question type: many sources, one reading (metamerism).
- **Domain 3, the kind of thing money is.** Actors: the six substrate terms, chiefly network. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ). Anchor: the figures. A medium reads as a network structure; an asset reads as an operand. Question type: structure versus state. Binding terms onto held atoms is provenance, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 4, the institutional and credit frame.** Actors: banks, central banks, treasuries. Currency: credit, debt, legal tender. Anchor: the clearing system and the tax office.
- **Domain 5, the commodity frame.** Actors: gold, silver, grain, cigarettes. Currency: commodity market price. Anchor: the market for that commodity.
- **Domain 6, the verification / outreach frame.** Actors: monetary theorists and historians. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle the ground before the source (Fig 3); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9); unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's cross-kind composition rule governs the six primitives; the mapping of "commodity" onto an energy-like state is a binding from the economics frame, not a derivation of a seventh term. Re-census resolves the apparent contradiction by separating the levels (see `01` F7). One strain remains named, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Split, do not average. The question is a bundle whose two conditions form a union, and the both-cell is a compound with its own loop. The figure-derived shape is that money is an anchored convention and a network medium: agreement about value is the product for a convention, but a declaration does not ground value, so standing must be ranged against a market, clearing system or tax demand that does not move with belief. The commodity account and the institutional account each supply one such anchor, and the reading of acceptance cannot discriminate between their sources. So "both" is right only as the union of overlapping conditions, not as a single averaged essence. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the split/union rule of Fig 9; the strongest world corroboration is the existence of competing, regime-indexed theories of money.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states four refuters before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-essence doctrine instead of a shape with refuters, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The retracted substrate identity claim is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating monetary observation showing that acceptance picks out commodity, state or credit uniquely would name the source; a non-drifting pure-belief regime with no external anchor would break the anchor requirement; a re-census showing money is a state with no network term would break the medium-as-structure reading; a single account governing all regimes with no residual would break the split. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
