## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: money is an anchored convention and a network medium, and its source is not determined by the reading of acceptance. The three offered answers (institution, commodity, both) are not yet a shape; they are candidate names for a source the reading cannot discriminate.

## 2. How a shape is obtained, and the specific warning for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, which is the decisive move here: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* The question's "or both" is the tell. It is a bundle masquerading as one question.

## 3. The source question is a metamerism case, not a definition dispute

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The source could be green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay's conclusion: *"This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* restates the same point with the video-frame example: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* And on green: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."*

Money's acceptance is that green reading. Commodity value, state taxation, bank credit creation and network effect are four sources that yield it. So the honest output is a shape, not a source name. The world sources corroborate that there are competing theories of money (credit theory and commodity theory) without either being established across all regimes.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Binding "commodity" onto an energy-like state and "medium" onto the network structure is a visible choice with provenance, not a derived identity. Calling it a derivation would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 5. Q2 — does "shape, not a name" forbid naming what money is?

**Answered with a narrowing, following the exemplar's own correction.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Network and energy are already among the six (Fig 13). No primitive is missing here. So:

- Naming **network** as the structure a medium requires is not forbidden. Fig 13 :75 requires a structure between states but does not select among the three structures it holds. Selecting one is a binding from the economics frame onto an atom already in Γ, provenanced as such.
- Claiming that binding **moves the wall** is forbidden. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Adding an economics label to a held atom adds no atom.
- Naming the **source** of money's standing (commodity, state, credit) is a different matter. None of these is a primitive of the six; they are economics-frame terms. The reading does not discriminate among them, so no name is licensed by the ground.

So the framework can say "network medium" (a binding onto a held atom) and cannot say "money is really credit" (a source name the reading cannot discriminate). Both halves of that sentence are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists selected to agree with one account is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let the market, the clearing system, the tax office, or a source-discriminating observation settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any theory of money and must be labelled as such.

## 7. Two further essay constraints that bear here

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And it quotes Fig 2: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* A panel of monetary theorists who all read the same textbooks is one frame wearing two names; their agreement about "the best account" is the pool talking.
- *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* The essay also records "Three mappings offered and declined" and warns that results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So this solution offers a shape and a refuter, not a validated theory of money, and the derivation is written down before the verdict so a later reader can check whether it was fitted.
