## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: "split, don't average; anchored convention; source undetermined by acceptance; medium is a structure."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about money: gold is both commodity and legal tender; a bank deposit is institution-created and held as an asset; value must settle against a market, clearing or tax anchor; acceptance cannot name the source.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down cleanly:

- the **union condition**. The shorthand "split" alone does not carry that the both-cell is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54 and :55-58). A reader who hears only "split" will produce a partition. This is the largest dropped constraint.
- the **convention-valence rule**. "Agreement is the product" applies to a convention, but Fig 2 :40 is disqualifying for a verdict when frames share an evidence pool. The shorthand, used carelessly, could license pooling agreement as corroboration. The WATSON answer explicitly blocks that.
- the **binding versus derivation distinction**. "Medium is a structure" is a binding onto a held atom; it does not license "commodity money is the energy term." The F7 retraction carries this, but the shorthand does not.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the question must be split, each account governs a subset, and the standing must be ranged against an anchor that does not update on belief. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
