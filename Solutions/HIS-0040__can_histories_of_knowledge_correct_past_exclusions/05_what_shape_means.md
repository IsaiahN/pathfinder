## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, a shape and not a name. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

For this question, a shape is the form a correction must have before anyone can name the tradition, the representative, or the recovered author. The shape constrains in four ways:

- corrections keep the residual per slot (Fig 1 :19);
- the community is a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55);
- the repair imports access rather than inventing a new primitive (Fig 6 :25, :26);
- the exclusion claim is refused, not merely outnumbered (Fig 12 :140).

A correction that satisfies all four has the right shape. It still does not settle which contributor, which archive, or which attribution is owed. Those names require a ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".

The first move is to describe the failure in effect terms: the record predicted that this slot would be empty or misattributed; the artefact, instrument, testimony or practice returned something else. The description is cast in effect terms because it has to travel to a frame with a different closure. A description written only in the correcting historian's vocabulary will be sharpened into terms only that frame can read (Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms.").

The essays opened this session give the same discipline from the source-discrimination side. In The Hard Problem is About Source, the instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and the essay states that if several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information. Its conclusion is checkable and relevant: the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists. Applied here, an archive reading that a community made no contribution does not license a claim about the source of that silence. The instrument cannot comment on the source; a different instrument is owed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles it, verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature sharpens the second. It says that outputs do not contain their generator and that a pile of recorded results is not the process that produced them. For histories of knowledge this is the difference between a list of recovered names and the practice that made them knowledge. It also says that a minimum viable set of six terms is a human careful guess at reality's compression axes and that another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six, and that declaring the primitives does not make them neutral but makes the choice visible. A corrective history that declares its primitives, its inclusion rules and its provenance makes its choices auditable. That is the most the shape can do; it cannot make the choices neutral.

The same essay says the real import is not a new term in the existing space but a new dimension of the space. That is the sense in which a correction can move the record's wall without pretending the community was newly invented.

## 4. Why the internally uniform community is the specific forbidden error

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :95-96: "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."

A uniform community is a flat formula. It records the ingredients, meaning the list of persons and contributions, and omits the bonds, meaning the relations of teaching, instrument use, dispute, transmission and repair that made those contributions knowledge. Because the bond is omitted, nothing downstream can tell two different arrangements apart, and nothing signals that anything was lost.

Fig 11 :43 and :51 add the habitat rule: enumerate, never compose; no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. A representative voice appointed in advance is a bond authored by the historian rather than read off the habitat. The source-discrimination essay gives the mechanism from another field: a reading that cannot discriminate among sources demonstrates its own inadequacy. A history that reports one community voice where several paths could produce the same record is using an instrument that cannot comment on the source.

## 5. Answered question

**What does the framework license a corrective history to assert?** The shape of the missing slot and the conditions under which a name could fill it. It does not license naming the tradition's authentic voice, and it does not license treating a recovered list as a repair.

**Does the world literature conflict?** No. The epistemic injustice sources, the historiography sources and the decolonial sources all corroborate that exclusion is structural and that repair needs more than insertion. They are at low overlap with the framework and with each other, so they raise the confidence in the shape without deciding it. Where they share an evidence pool, as the two Wikipedia entries do, their agreement is counted as one witness.

## 6. Consequences for outreach and honesty

**Prestige is not the ground.** A correction does not become true because a famous historian endorses it. The claim gives a checkable shape and a refuter; the ground settles it.

**This document is a simulation.** The source-discrimination essay notes that simulated outputs are not the testable thing when it says the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists, and the framework's own Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, not opened this session and therefore not quoted here, makes the same point about validator dependence. This file is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading and cannot settle the question it reports on.

**The round trip must be measured.** Any condensation of the figures into a slogan such as just add the missing voices carries a nonzero round-trip gap. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The gap is enumerated in `11_audit.md`.
