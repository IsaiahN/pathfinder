## The verdict in one line

Yes, conditionally. A history of knowledge can correct a past exclusion without treating a community or tradition as internally uniform only if it keeps the residual indexed per slot, treats the community as a union of overlapping conditions rather than a single frame, imports a provenanced atom from a frame whose closure differs, refuses the exclusion with a negation rather than merely accumulating recovery entries, and triangulates against a ground that does not update. Drop any one of those and the correction replaces one monolith with another.
