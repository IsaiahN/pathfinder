## Wider literature that bears on the question

The fetched sources fall into three low-overlap fields. No source from the history of science was used to overrule a figure; each corroborates a shape that the figures already decide.

- **Human cognition and social epistemology.** The epistemic injustice sources corroborate that exclusion is a credibility and interpretive failure, not merely an omission of names. Their distinction between testimonial and hermeneutical injustice is a low-overlap analogue of the framework's per-slot residual versus flat formula distinction.
- **Historiography of science and of tradition.** The history of science entry corroborates that recovered contributions became a recognized practice and that the field now sees itself as a global history of exchange. The invented tradition entry corroborates the warning that a tradition presented as ancient and uniform may be recent and constructed, and its criticism records that all traditions change.
- **Decolonial epistemology.** De Sousa Santos corroborates that the destruction of rival knowledges is a structural process and that the answer is an ecology of knowledges with intercultural translation rather than a replacement canon. His own caution that the point is not to ascribe the same validity to every kind of knowledge is important: it prevents the yes from becoming an anything-goes verdict.

Overlap is declared rather than hidden. The two Wikipedia entries share an encyclopedia and an evidence pool; their agreement is one witness, not two. The de Sousa Santos chapter and the 2014 quote page share an author. The two Substack essays share an author with the figures and are high overlap, so they corroborate only weakly.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons for the sequence:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :45** "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output against their own ground first.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a method, so it may be given away; the conclusion is a recording and should not lead.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" Agreement among readers of the same literature cannot be pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is high, because a bad correction hardens into a new canon.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question of what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the verbatim figures, not any summary, are the authority. A later expression of agreement is never pooled as a witness (Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes.").

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1, before anything else.** Validate the output against the ground. Ask whether the six conditions in `02_outreach_email.md` are load-bearing and whether any of the five refuters fires. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the output survives and the reader is curious, offer the canonical figure text and the essays. Their agreement after seeing the framework is not a second witness; it is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, reuse.** Offer the method for the reader's own problem, with the requirement that they write down what would refute their result before running it. The framework's own direction rule says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so a result contrived to fit the formula is worthless.
