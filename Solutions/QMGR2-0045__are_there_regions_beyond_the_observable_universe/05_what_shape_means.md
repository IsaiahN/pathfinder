## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before any region is named. It says: the two conditions have different grounds; the horizon is a frame wall, not a world wall; the beyond is observer-relative; a finite signature can fire and an infinite whole cannot be confirmed. "There is something out there" is a name-shaped claim. "The observable patch is not a sealed final room" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the closest in-horizon terms (particle horizon, event horizon, curvature, topology) and let the vocabulary narrow toward what can bear on the residual.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe "no signal returns beyond this boundary" rather than "there is a wall".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The outreach email in `02` is exactly this: a description of what would count as a failure.
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay's version of Fig 9 :54 and it is what drives the union split in `01`.
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is reached when the question has been split into an anchored part and an unanchored part and each has its own verdict.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For a named region beyond the horizon neither condition holds. The CMB and galaxy surveys are anchors for global properties (D4); they are not anchors for a specific region (D3). *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the reason: an instrument "cannot comment on the source", and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." We have no source-discriminating instrument for a beyond-horizon region, so naming one is the metamerism error: reading a source off a projection that several sources could produce.

*They're Scanning Books* (via exemplar) sharpens the import rule: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A bubble universe imported from eternal inflation is a claim from a different frame; without a source gate it carries no evidentiary weight in this derivation.

## 4. The asymmetry is part of the shape

This question's shape has a feature the QMGR-0037 case did not: **confirmation and refutation are not symmetric across a horizon.** Luminet (opened): "no physical measure can ever prove that space is infinite, but a sufficiently small, finite space model could be testable." Fig 8 :39 adds: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

So the correct shape is not "we cannot know anything about the beyond". It is: a bounded signature can return and an unbounded whole cannot be confirmed; a negative result constrains, it does not erase. That is a constraint on the *form* of any admissible answer, and it is independent of which cosmology turns out true.

## 5. Why the horizon is a shape-level notion, not a physical object

ScienceInsights (opened, public synthesis, lower weight): "Our observational limit is not a wall or a boundary in space. It is a boundary in information, set by the finite speed of light and the finite age of the cosmos." Ord (opened) makes the same point structurally: every observer has their own observable universe. Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

In figure terms the horizon is `closure(Γ)`: the reach of what a frame can say given what it holds. It is not an atom in the world. Treating it as a physical wall is the error of reading a frame's limit as a limit of the world. The shape therefore includes an observer-relative index: every "beyond" is relative to a light cone. This is why asking "what is beyond the universe" without naming an observer is malformed in the same way a query without a slot index is malformed under Fig 1 :19.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A famous name agreeing with the shape is not an instrument.

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." and "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of cosmology and cannot settle the question they report on.
