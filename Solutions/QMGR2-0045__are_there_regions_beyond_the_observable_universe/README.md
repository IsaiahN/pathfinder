## Residual in effect terms

Our cosmological library predicts regions beyond the particle horizon. For slots inside the horizon, an observation returns and the per-slot residual closes. For slots beyond it, the prediction exists but no observation returns, so the residual is unreadable rather than zero. The question bundles two conditions that do not share a ground: (1) do those slots carry content (existence), and (2) can any observation be made to bear on them (testability). Fig 9 :54 calls a conjunction of this kind a union and requires it to be unbundled before searching.
