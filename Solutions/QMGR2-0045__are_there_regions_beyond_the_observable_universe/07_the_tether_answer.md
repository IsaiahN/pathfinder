# The Tether's answer: regions beyond the observable universe

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on direct properties of a named beyond-horizon region. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Our cosmology predicts regions beyond the particle horizon. For slots inside it, a signal returns and the per-slot residual closes. For slots beyond it, there is a prediction and no observation, so the residual is unreadable rather than zero (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question bundles two conditions with different grounds: existence and testability. Fig 9 :54 requires the bundle to be split before searching.

## 2. The shape, per domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

**D1, in-horizon cosmology.** Anchored by the CMB and large-scale structure. The residual closes here for local geometry and contents. `specified`. This is the only domain where cosmology currently has a ground.

**D2, the horizon.** A boundary, not a domain with its own anchor. Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." The horizon is `closure(Γ)`, a limit of the frame's reach, not a wall in the world. It is observer-relative: every observer has their own sphere. "Beyond" is a relation between an observer and a light cone. `general`.

**D3, a named beyond-horizon region.** No anchor exists. Direct properties: `gap`. Fig 8 :40-43: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Regions beyond the event horizon are underivable by light; regions merely beyond the current particle horizon are unreachable at this budget. Fig 6 :34-35: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Existence is neither asserted nor denied. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

**D4, global properties with in-horizon traces.** Reachable in principle. Anchored by CMB maps and galaxy surveys. Luminet: "no physical measure can ever prove that space is infinite, but a sufficiently small, finite space model could be testable." Copi et al.: "Whether cosmic topology is observable remains uncertain, but current and future data offer an unprecedented opportunity to probe the global structure of the Universe." No positive detection exists yet. `specified`.

**D5, verification.** One evidence pool in this run, so agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). `general`.

**The asymmetry is part of the answer.** A bounded signature can fire; an unbounded whole cannot be confirmed. Negative results constrain, they do not erase. That is why the verdict is not "we can know nothing" and not "something must be out there". It is a shape with an index, a split, an asymmetry, and a refused name.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any specific region exists, and not that none does. The D3 gap is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence").
- Not that the horizon is a physical edge or that conditions change there.
- Not that the observable universe is all there is. That would be a final room, and Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room". But note the mode: this is a shape about closures, not a measurement of spatial extent (see `01` F8).
- Not that the universe is infinite, or finite, or a bubble in a multiverse. Those are names from physics frames with no source gate here.
- Not that global topology has been detected. It has not.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement beyond a horizon is claimed or used.

## 4. What would settle it (the ground)

An instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the color case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Concretely:

1. **A returned signal from a specific beyond-horizon region**, distinguishable from any in-horizon process. This closes the D3 gap.
2. **A matched pair of CMB circles, topological lensing signal, or bubble-collision signature**, which would settle a D4 property at the ground. It would not by itself settle D3.
3. **A dark-energy equation-of-state measurement** showing whether an event horizon exists. If `w >= −1/3`, the underivable class collapses into the unreachable class (Fig 8 :40-43).
4. **A proof of infinite space from measurement**, which Luminet says cannot be done. If it were, the asymmetry in F4 would be retracted.
5. **Standing falsifiers:** a completed search of a sealed frame; a working instrument built from a bare description with nothing previously registering; a single anchor that settles existence and testability together.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, for D4. Unreachable for D3 at this ground.** The shapes returned by the figures are constraints, not a cosmology. "Indiscernibility is not identity": from inside the frame, "there but out of reach" and "nothing there" look the same (Fig 6 :27). The record stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and keeps the lineage so the stop is checkable. Prestige adds no anchor: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say what lies beyond the observable universe, and it does not claim nothing does. It says the question is two questions with different grounds. Inside the horizon, signals return and cosmology has a ground. For a named region beyond the particle horizon, our theories predict but nothing returns, so any direct property is a `gap`, not a yes or a no. The horizon itself is a limit of what a frame can reach, not a wall in space, and it is relative to whoever is looking. Some properties of the whole, such as cosmic topology, do leave traces in the cosmic microwave background and are testable in principle, though none has been detected; and a finite universe could be detected where an infinite one never could be. A search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, a refuter written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The gap stays open until the ground settles it.
