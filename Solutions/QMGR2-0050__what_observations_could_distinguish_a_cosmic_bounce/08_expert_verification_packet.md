# Expert verification packet: the Tether on bounce versus inflation tests

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the channel list, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A bounce or cyclic model and standard inflation both reproduce the observed nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic scalar spectrum, so that spectrum is a shared **functional group** rather than a discriminant. A valid answer to "what observations could distinguish them" is therefore a finite, ranked, unbundled list of observational slots where the two prediction functions separate *and* an instrument already returns a reading that fails to resolve the difference. The names currently supplied by the physics frame are primordial tensors, the bispectrum, tilt and features, a stochastic gravitational-wave background, shear, and BBN consistency; each is `specified`, model-dependent, and imported with provenance rather than derived from the framework. A null search is indiscernibility at a stated instrument set, not equivalence. The figure set shapes the answer; the ground decides it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 6, 8 and 9

- **Statement:** the discriminating residual is per slot and unbundled; a candidate discriminant must be something an instrument already returns; a null result is not a proof of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:**

  - Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

  - Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit one statistic that closes the residual across all channels; name a listed channel with no instrument at any resolution; or show a null search proves equivalence.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the published literature

- **Statement:** the scalar spectrum does not separate the families, and the named channels are *candidate* discriminants, not universal ones. The tensor case is explicitly conditional: *"If primordial gravitational waves are observed, certain bouncing cosmologies, such as the cyclic scenario, are in trouble, while others remain valid."* (Battefeld and Peter, Phys. Rep. 569, 2015). The bispectrum case has sources pointing in different directions (Planck 2019, no detection; Delgado et al. 2021, in-principle detectability at low `ℓ`).
- **What to check:** (a) is the tensor claim correctly stated as "certain cyclic models", not "all bounces"? (b) are the Planck 2019 `f_NL` numbers and the Delgado et al. signal-to-noise claim represented fairly? (c) is the NANOGrav signal correctly described as a correlated background whose source is not yet settled? (d) is any listed channel actually model-independent inside the bounce family? If yes, the "candidate only" qualifier is too weak and should be strengthened, not weakened.
- **Refuter:** a bounce model that predicts observable tensors in the inflationary range; a bounce model with `f_NL` inside the single-field range; or a proof that the scalar spectrum determines the mechanism uniquely. Any of these removes or narrows a channel.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** the two families are weak isomers at the phenomenological level: shared observable atoms, different histories, difference visible only at other slots. Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Refuter:** show that either family requires an observable the other cannot in principle produce (isomer claim collapses), or that both have identical theoretical atoms and bonds (they become identical, not isomeric). Also: show that the Fig 12 cycle bond entails a physical bounce, which would overturn the re-census in `06` Q2.
