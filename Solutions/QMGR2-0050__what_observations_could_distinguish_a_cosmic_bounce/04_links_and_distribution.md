## Wider literature that bears (recorded, not relied on)

- The two review sources disagree in emphasis: Battefeld and Peter stress the fine tuning and non-Gaussianity problems of bounces; Ijjas and Steinhardt argue ekpyrotic models fit Planck2015 well. Fig 1 :22 says *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The disagreement is kept as two witnesses, not averaged into a consensus.
- Planck 2019 and Delgado et al. 2021 pull in opposite directions on the bispectrum channel (no detection versus in-principle detectability at low `ℓ`). Both are kept with their own falsifiers in `01` F5.
- NANOGrav's signal is a detection of *correlations*, not yet of a cosmological source. It is exactly the metamerism case: a reading whose source is not settled by the reading.
