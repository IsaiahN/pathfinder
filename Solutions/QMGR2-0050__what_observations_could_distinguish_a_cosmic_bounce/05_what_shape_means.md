## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133 *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the stopping rule: *"the finish line will also be the starting place."*

For this question the shape is **the form a valid list of discriminating observations must have**: per slot, unbundled, instrument-returned, refutable, and provenance-labelled. It is **not** the name of a single decisive observable. "CMB B-modes" is a name supplied by the physics frame. "A per-slot residual where an instrument already returns a reading that fails to resolve the source" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: describe the residual in effect terms, bootstrap by proximity, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *How a Society of Agents*: *"Everyone who qualifies... a lottery... the habitat itself, everything in contact with the residual, enumerated rather than composed."* This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` lists instruments, archives and venues in contact with the residual, and why no list of people was hand-picked for agreement.

Applied here: describe the residual as "one spectrum, several histories", not as "the bounce problem"; then let the channels in contact with that residual be enumerated (B-mode bandpowers, bispectra, PTA correlations, shear constraints, abundances) rather than composed from a favourite theory.

## 3. Two essay terms that govern this question directly

### Distinction horizon and union horizon

*How Intelligence Actually Works* defines the exact limit this question runs into:

> *"There exists a 'distinction horizon' for every individual observer, the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another."*

> *"A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."*

> *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

So "observations could distinguish" is a question about the **current union horizon of cosmology**, not about the identity of the models. A null result places the bounce/inflation distinction below the horizon for the present instrument set. It does not prove identity. This is the essay-level twin of Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* and of the canonical README's *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

### Provenance traces: the diamond case

*How Intelligence Actually Works* contrasts the colour case (no trace, no instrument can recover the source) with lab-grown versus mined diamonds:

> *"In the gemstone case, provenance is recoverable: isotope ratios genuinely differ, so if you have a tool that can perceive the trace, you regain distinction. The tool only works because a trace existed, and not because a better tool was created."*

This is the criterion F3 applies. The question "what observations could distinguish" is answered by finding a **trace that exists in the sky**: a tensor amplitude, a bispectrum shape, a stochastic-background spectral slope, a shear floor. If no trace exists, no instrument improvements recover the history. If a trace exists, some instrument may read it. The essays do not tell us which case cosmology is in; the physics literature supplies candidates, and the ground settles them.

## 4. Why the metamerism reading is the right one here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*

> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works*, Rule 4:

> *"you can find instances where similar paths of varying derivatives could create similar or potentially identical derivations. Only their provenance chains/lineage would be able to distinguish them"*

These two lines say the same thing from perception and from composition. A CMB power spectrum that both inflation and a bounce reproduce is a projection that cannot comment on its source. The owed object is a discriminator with a trace to read.

## 5. What licenses a name, and what is forbidden

From *They're Scanning Books When They Should*:

> *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."*

> *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. It does not license naming a cosmological mechanism. The figure set can say that a valid discriminator must be per-slot and instrument-returned; it cannot say "the answer is B-modes." That name is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such.

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*, section "Three mappings offered and declined", declines mappings that resemble the framework without aligning with it. Bayesian updating was declined because *"there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism."* The channel list in `01` F5 is offered in the same spirit: a list of measured quantities is not a derivation of the framework, and the framework is not a cosmological model.

## 6. Q: does the framework name the decisive observation? ANSWERED, no

Nothing in the figure set or the essays names a decisive cosmological observation. A particular observation is licensed only by the ground returning a difference at that slot. Until then, the name is the searcher's contribution and is recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, with one caveat recorded. It does not promote any channel to "the test". It does, however, list seven channels with names. That list is an **import with provenance** (Fig 6 :12 to :13, Fig 8 :50 to :52), each with its own falsifier, and it is explicitly `specified` and model-dependent. The caveat: an import of names is still an import, and it is debited against independence. It must not become a verdict.

## 7. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I first wrote (in the draft of section 1) that the rule forbids naming any observable at all. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. The physics channels are already held in the world-facing frame; naming them is a **binding from that frame**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the framework derived the name, (b) claiming one name closes all slots, and (c) claiming the name is settled without a ground reading. Both the earlier and the narrowed reading are recorded here so neither is silently erased.

## 8. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous cosmologists who prefer one model is not a discriminant. The legitimate ask is for a checkable null or detection at a named instrument.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The framework's own architecture post adds that LLM outputs must be seen as *"simulacra"* and that they *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any cosmology and cannot settle anything it reports on.
