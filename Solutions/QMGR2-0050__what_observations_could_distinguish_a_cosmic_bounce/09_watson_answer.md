## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no winner is named. Both families share the scalar spectrum as a functional group, so that spectrum does not discriminate. A valid answer is an unbundled, per-slot list of channels where prediction functions separate and an instrument already returns a reading that fails to resolve the source, with provenance for every imported name, a pre-registered losing condition on each, and nulls reported as indiscernibility at a stated instrument set. The concrete channels from the literature are `specified` candidates, model-dependent inside the bounce family, and none is sufficient alone. The question of which history the universe had is unreached, not unreachable; it is settled by a source-discriminating instrument, not by agreement, prestige, or this derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a list of channel names as if they were figure-derived verdicts, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by keeping the shape claim and the refuter in front of the links.
- **undoable:** yes. The channel list is a provenanced import and can be narrowed or replaced without touching the structural derivation. A wrong tensor or bispectrum claim can be corrected by a new measurement, and no figure line has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A primordial B-mode detection at a level that excludes the cyclic models named in the source, a bispectrum detection attributable to a bounce, an identified primordial component in the stochastic background, or a demonstration that the scalar spectrum uniquely determines the mechanism would each move a slot or break the shape claim. A null across all channels at a stated instrument set would place the distinction below the current union horizon, which is a revision of the horizon, not a verdict of identity. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
