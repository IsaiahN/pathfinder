## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0064 the shape is: a per-slot signal, a conditional decision link, an asymmetric habitat substitution, a cascade, a union tail and two non-updating anchors. A number of hectares is not a shape; it is a measurement.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So describe "a change in contact that cannot be subtracted" rather than reaching first for a named policy instrument.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A shape is exactly such a description.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect description of the residual is "a per-place yield reading changes a land contact that cannot be subtracted, and the damage is found three steps downstream against two different grounds." That is checkable by anyone with a land-cover time series and a species survey, whatever vocabulary they use for the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried, not re-opened) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, naming a particular policy lever (a subsidy reform, a yield-gap programme, a land-sparing scheme) as *the* answer would be a binding from the economist's or agronomist's frame onto an already-available slot. It is allowed as provenance, and it must be recorded as the searcher's own contribution. What is not allowed is presenting it as something the figures derived.

## 4. The source problem: why a reading is not a cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is the exact failure mode in F2 and F8. A global yield index and a global land-cover total are a projection: many decision regimes give the same two readings. An intensification that spares land in one place and an intensification that shifts production to a frontier can look identical in a national production statistic. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: a per-slot decision record, a tenure map, a supply-chain trace, not a better argument about which narrative is right.

## 5. Answered question: what licenses a particular magnitude?

Nothing in the framework. The figures return a shape. A magnitude is licensed only by a ground reading (an instrument, a dataset counted honestly, a survey) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. EAR-0064 declares that number a `gap` (F9), reported as unreached, not as silence: Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I need to narrow the "shape, not a name" rule, and the retraction stays visible. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Gamma. Land-cover types, yield indices, prices and policy levers are not missing primitives; they are bindings from the agronomy, economics and ecology frames onto slots the framework already has. Naming them is therefore not forbidden. What remains forbidden is:

- claiming the binding was derived from the figures when it was imported (Fig 8 :50-52);
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :23), when it adds no atom;
- naming the source of a reading where several sources could give the same reading (the metamerism error in §4).

My §4 point is **not** retracted. It is a claim about the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held bindings. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names agreeing that yield change causes conversion is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against data and a seat able to refuse it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. One-paragraph version

A shape here is the constrained form of an answer that can be checked before it can be numerised: yield change registers per place, acts through a conditional decision link, changes a contact that cannot be subtracted, produces its material biodiversity effect in a cascade three steps out, and faces two different grounds for biodiversity and climate. The framework returns this, not a hectare count. A particular lever or number is licensed only by a ground reading or a provenanced import, and the source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument, is what would settle which regime applies. This document is a simulated derivation, not that instrument.
