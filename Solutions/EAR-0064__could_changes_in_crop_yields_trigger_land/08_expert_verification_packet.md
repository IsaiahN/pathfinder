# Expert verification packet  -  the Tether on yield change and land conversion

**What this is:** a claim in a form a land-change researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on magnitude. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at a specific place and period is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The chain from a change in crop yield to worsened biodiversity loss and climate change is not one causal law. It is a four-link dependency chain with a union tail. Yield change registers per place and per crop. It changes land cover only through a decision frame whose terms select whether land is spared or converted. Conversion is an introduction and a substitution of habitat, not a reversible subtraction. The material biodiversity loss is typically a second- or third-order cascade, while the climate limb is a finite carbon stock spent as a flux. Biodiversity and climate are settled by different anchors, so they need two verdicts, never an average. The figures return this shape, not a number of hectares, species or parts per million.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 3 and 11

- **Statement:** the trigger is per slot, mediated by a decision link, and the resulting conversion is an asymmetric substitution rather than a subtraction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19  -  *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 3 :27  -  *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 11 :61  -  *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about"* Fig 11 :66  -  *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
- **What to check:** does a yield change reach land cover only through price, tenure, policy and demand in the data you know? Is restoration a return of the original habitat or another substitution?
- **Refuter:** show conversion is a reversible subtraction with exclusion available; or demonstrate a direct yield-to-conversion law with no decision-frame mediation; or a per-slot null result.

### C2  -  biodiversity-facing, checkable by an ecologist against cascade evidence

- **Statement:** the biodiversity effect that matters is not first order. The habitat must be enumerated to at least second order around the conversion.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52  -  *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three"* Fig 11 :43  -  *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :49  -  *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What to check:** in a conversion you know, was the material loss at the field edge or three steps out? Does the same actor change valence between the food and biodiversity residuals?
- **Refuter:** show the effect is strictly first order with no material cascade; or exhibit one lever that improves yield and biodiversity at the same place with no displaced burden.

### C3  -  earth-system-facing, checkable by a land-climate researcher

- **Statement:** conversion spends a finite carbon stock as a flux, and the two downstream outcomes have different anchors, so they form a union and cannot be averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14  -  *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*; Fig 13 :22  -  *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 10 :24  -  *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 9 :55  -  *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **What to check:** does the carbon accounting at a converted site show net stock loss and sink removal, read against an atmospheric anchor independent of the model? Do biodiversity and climate actually have different grounds in your assessment practice?
- **Refuter:** show a conversion with no net carbon flux; or that biodiversity and climate share one anchor and one lever clears both without displacement.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal "yes": the yield-to-conversion link is conditional on decision-frame terms (F8).
- Not a magnitude: no hectares, species count or ppm is asserted (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a standing list of good or bad crops, policies or actors (Fig 11 :51).
- Not one averaged outcome: biodiversity and climate are two verdicts (F6).
- Not reversible by exclusion: conversion substitutes a habitat (Fig 11 :66).
- Not a derivation of any particular policy lever from the figures. Naming a lever is a binding from the economics or agronomy frame, provenanced as such (see `05` section 6).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Specifically:

1. A per-slot record pairing yield change with land-cover change and the decision variables that select the regime.
2. A cascade enumeration to second and third order around a conversion, with species responses measured.
3. A flux measurement of net stock change and sink removal, with an atmospheric anchor read independently.
4. Any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 firing.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 3 :27, Fig 11 :52 and :61  -  four short passages.
2. For one conversion you know: name the decision variable that selected the regime and say whether the material biodiversity loss was first order or further out.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish "land spared" from "production displaced" in the same yield-and-land dataset, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
