# Solution EAR-0064  -  figure-grounded package

**Question:** Could changes in crop yields trigger land conversion that worsens biodiversity loss and climate change?

**Source:** earth systems.txt line 86

**Section:** 7. Food, agriculture, and land use

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on any numerical magnitude. Not `grounded`. Every substantive file is labelled a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## Restating the question as an effect

What is being decided is whether a change in a per-place quantity (yield) can change a physical contact (land cover), and whether that contact change then moves two different grounds (biodiversity and climate forcing) in a worsening direction. Option A: yes, as a general causal law with a knowable magnitude. Option B: yes as a structural possibility whose realisation is conditional on a decision frame, with the magnitude left to measurement. An answer would look like a shape: which links exist, which anchors each outcome uses, which conditions select the regime, and what would refute the shape. That shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, so the preference can accrue a residual like anything else (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words: a room is not an anchor (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*); selecting for agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*); and a recipient who holds the framework's interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

## Boundary

Nothing in this folder was modified outside it. Local framework files were read, not edited. World sources were opened over HTTP and are recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The result cannot settle the question it reports on: no measurement was taken, and no figure line returns a number of hectares, species, or parts per million.
