## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22,** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42,** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Record the stage-1 verdict first.
- **Fig 9 :27,** *"describe it before you go looking, or"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 9 :60,** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement. Select for public ground contact: publications, datasets, code, formal reviews, public Q&A.
- **Fig 2 :42,** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Conversion is hard to reverse, so prefer independent frames, not more of the same one.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that answer; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule and the formula. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a shared evidence pool makes agreement worthless as a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Each stage-3 user still owes a ground per problem, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. The pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are the test. If one fires, withdraw the claim and keep the record of the failure.

Nothing here is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the author's to do. If the packet is hosted, prefer a page that lets a visitor decline contact.
