## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 5 :43 (MDL bargain), Fig 6 :14 and :23 (import), Fig 8 :32 and :50 (idempotence and provenance), Fig 2 :40 (independence), Fig 11 :40 (capability).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, that the decision is a per-slot threshold, that a differing closure is required, that agreement needs independence, and that the cue name is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete consumer claim, such as "choose the brand with the most five-star reviews" or "price is the quality signal for used cars."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot rule** is lost when a five-star average is used as the whole reading (Fig 1 :19, Fig 10 :26);
- the **provenance requirement** is lost when a cue is named without showing its source (Fig 8 :50);
- the **independence requirement** is lost when a single-pool average is treated as corroboration (Fig 2 :40);
- the **shape-not-a-name limit** is breached when the framework is said to identify the diagnostic cue for a category (Fig 9 :32).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a threshold decision whose local library cannot predict quality needs an import from a differing closure. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no consumer ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set `R_T` to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a stated gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now says.
