## What the wider literature contributes, and what it does not

The world sources are secondary corroboration only. Figures decide.

1. **Low-overlap mechanism witnesses.** Mate choice copying (animal behaviour) corroborates the import-and-independence shape: naive choosers use public information when private discrimination is costly, and the source records both the benefit (fewer errors) and the cost (following a demonstrator that was itself wrong). This is a different field from marketing, so it is a genuinely low-overlap witness for the mechanism shape, not for the consumer-level fact.
2. **Human cognition witnesses.** Anchoring and choice overload corroborate F3 and F4: a reference point sets the starting belief, and a large equivalent option set pushes the chooser to a coping rule. Anchoring is also a warning about "independence": a shared anchor makes two choosers agree without adding evidence (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Information-economics witnesses.** Akerlof and the SEC classification corroborate F1 and F5: when quality is unobservable before or after use, sellers have no credible disclosure technology and price can become the only indicator. Note that *"Branding and detailed product specifications act to transform a product from an experience good into a search good"* is exactly a wall-moving import, and therefore maps onto Fig 6 :14. It does not prove the framework; it is a case in a different field with the same shape.
4. **Overlap warning.** All of the business and cognition sources share the broad behavioural-science evidence pool. Agreement among them is one witness, not several (Fig 2 :40). Akerlof's lemons model and the credence-goods literature are close neighbours, not independent confirmations.
5. **Framework essays.** The three essays by Isaiah N are framework-internal and overlap maximally with the figures. Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict after re-census. The one apparent conflict, price as a quality signal versus Fig 2 :40 on shared-pool agreement, was resolved by distinguishing a seller-set price (inside the seller's closure) from a price formed by independent buyers (a different closure). It was returned to the census, not used to flip the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - agreement disqualifies a verdict when the frames share a pool. A send list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Independent readers are worth more for a high-stakes credence category than for a low-stakes purchase.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter per problem.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1: send the residual, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory. Stage 2 is opt in: only if curious, offer the three source categories above. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method, never as an answer. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` keeps the F10 gap open and states the simulation status on the surface; both must stay in the packet.
