# Expert verification packet - the Tether on customer decisions under opaque quality

**What this is:** a claim in a form a consumer researcher, judgement researcher, or information economist can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No consumer measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A customer's decision is a per-slot threshold comparison against "good enough," with a belief that mixes private history and inherited prior and updates after outcomes. When quality cannot be read before purchase and the local information cannot predict it, more of the same information adds nothing; what is required is an import from a differing closure, with provenance and a shadow test, before any cue or review can count. Agreement among customers is evidence only when they are independent instruments, and decision quality belongs to the customer and the habitat together. The figures give the shape such a diagnostic cue must have, not its name for any particular category.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and cognition-facing; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5, and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** the decision is a per-slot threshold with a learnable belief weight, not an aggregate maximization.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Operators table :51 *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* Symbols table :24 *"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings."*
- **What to check:** does the predicted stopping rule match observed choice behaviour when the aspiration level is stated before the task? Is the updated weight observable after a bad purchase?
- **Refuter:** a decision made on an aggregate residual with no loss of prediction; or a protocol in which `w` never updates after outcomes; or a chooser who maximizes over the full option set with a complete utility function and no threshold.

### C2 - information-economics-facing; checkable by an information economist or marketing researcher

- **Statement:** more search inside one evidence pool adds no quality atom; a useful signal must come from a differing closure with provenance and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :50 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*
- **What to check:** does a provenanced independent test reduce the prediction error against experienced quality while the same cue stripped of provenance does not? Does reading more reviews from the seller's pool leave the error unchanged?
- **Refuter:** a cue with no provenance and no shadow test that predicts experienced quality out of sample as well as an independent instrument; or new search inside one pool demonstrably adding a predictive atom.

### C3 - social and habitat-facing; checkable by a judgement researcher

- **Statement:** agreement counts only under independence, and decision quality is customer-and-habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **What to check:** do ratings from purchasers with no exposure to the seller's claims and no coordination predict experienced quality out of sample while a single-pool average does not? Does accuracy move with the habitat and stay flat with consumer-only training?
- **Refuter:** independent raters who still fail to predict; or accuracy improving under training in a habitat that supplies no new quality feedback.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that customers are irrational, and not a universal cue list.
- Not that price, brand, or reviews are figure-derived names. They are provenanced bindings from the world frame.
- Not that a five-star average is evidence of quality before its source is discriminated.
- Not that thought experiments, analogues, or this packet settle anything. They are a simulation checked for internal consistency.
- Not `grounded`. No consumer measurement was taken.
- **Declared gap:** no figure line names the diagnostic cue for a specific product category. Characterising what such a cue must do is the limit of this derivation.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 5 :43, Fig 8 :32, Fig 2 :40, and Fig 11 :40 (five short passages).
2. Check C1 against one study you know: was the stopping rule a threshold, and did the belief weight move after outcomes?
