## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the next line says the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *a per-slot threshold decision* is a shape;
- *choose the brand with the most five-star reviews* is a name;
- *a cue that must come from a differing closure and explain a gap* is a shape;
- *price is the signal for used cars* is a name.

The whole package stays on the left side of that line. F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records the right side as a gap.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then bootstrapping: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the starting vocabulary is residual, slot, anchor, import, independence, threshold. Each world source (lemon, credence, anchor, copy) narrows what a diagnostic cue would have to be.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as "quality cannot be read before purchase and the local information cannot predict it" before choosing between economics, psychology, or animal behaviour.
- The exemplar's canonical reading of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Used here only as the canonical reading already recorded in the exemplar, not as an independently opened source this session.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The shape stops when the vocabulary returns to the residual it started from: per-slot, threshold, imported, independent. It does not stop with a list of named cues.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For a customer, an instrument or a market that predicts experienced quality pays; an argument does not.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A cue is licensed when its provenance is shown and it explains a gap the buyer could not close.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved the case. Applied here: a five-star average is a projection. It cannot say whether the source was independent experience or a coordinated campaign. Naming "good quality" from the average is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. Why a universal cue list is the specific forbidden error

A list of "the ten cues customers use" reads the artifact (the observed choice) and names its source (the cause) without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Three sources create the same green: a genuine reputation built on repeat purchases, a price that signals cost, and a coordinated review campaign. The behaviour is the same; the source is not. Fig 2 :40 says agreement is worthless when the frames share an evidence pool. Fig 11 :49-51 says the valence of a cue is relative to the residual and no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.

This is not a claim that cue research is useless. It is a claim about what the framework can and cannot deliver. The framework returns the constraint a diagnostic cue must satisfy; a study with a ground can name the cue.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming price or brand at all? ANSWERED

**No, and the scope matters.** The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in the frame. Price, brand, reviews, and certification are already in the customer's and the market's vocabulary. Naming one is a **binding** from the world frame onto an available term, not the invention of a missing primitive. What is forbidden is claiming that the binding is a figure derivation, or that it moves the wall without provenance. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* A cue that only re-labels what the seller already claims adds no atom.

So the honest form is: *"A cue is admissible only if it comes from a differing closure, has provenance, and explains the quality gap; which cue satisfies this in a given category is a ground question."* It is not *"customers use price."*

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to this question

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A well-known brand, a famous reviewer, or a large star rating is prestige in this question. It can give a product a budget to be tried (a customer can afford to test it), but it cannot settle whether the product is good. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a product claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any consumer decision claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md` exists so that a real outside ground can be consulted before the framework is shown.
