**What is deliberately not here**

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words: a room of agreeing readers is not an anchor (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*); selecting for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40); and a reader who has already absorbed the framework's interior cannot verify its output (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.

**Suggested next step.** Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute the residual, not a list of believers.
