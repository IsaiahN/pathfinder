# The Tether's answer on customer decisions under opaque quality

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A customer bets on a product from a belief formed before purchase, then observes quality only after use. The gap between predicted and experienced quality belongs to one customer-product-occasion, not to an average (Fig 1 :19). Complex products, hard-to-judge quality, and incomplete information are three ways that gap fails to close: the vocabulary lacks the atoms, no instrument returns a failing reading, or the needed atom is absent from the closure. The answer says what the customer does next and what would settle it.

## 2. The shape

1. **The decision is a per-slot threshold comparison.** "Good enough" is a ⋛ comparison against an aspiration level, not a maximization over every option. Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* Fig 5 :43: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* A satisficing rule is admitted on that bargain. Mode `specified`.
2. **Belief is a learnable mix of private history and inherited prior.** Symbols table :24: *"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings."* With little private history, weight leans on brand, price, advertising, and others' choices; a bad purchase can move it.
3. **More of the same information adds nothing.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* The tenth review from the same pool is not the missing atom. What is owed is contact with a differing closure: Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
4. **Signals are imports and require provenance and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Price, brand, certification, expert tests, and warranties count only when their source is shown and they explain a gap the buyer could not close. A cue that repeats the seller's claim moves nothing. Warranties and return rights are a different import: they change the cost of being wrong rather than adding a quality atom.
5. **Agreement is evidence only under independence.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A thousand reviews tracing to one narrative are one witness. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
6. **Decision quality is a property of customer and habitat.** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Labels, tests, disclosure, warranties, and returns are levers; exhortations to decide better are not.
7. **The channel can be lost while the ground does not decay.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."* An averaged star rating hides the per-slot signal. Fig 1 :19 requires it to be kept.
8. **The diagnostic cue for a category is a gap.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework gives the constraint, not the name. Which cue works for used cars or dental work is a ground question.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that customers are irrational. The weighting `w` is a learnable state, not a defect.
- Not a universal list of cues. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not that any named cue is a figure result. Price, brand, and reviews are bindings from the world frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that a high average rating is evidence. It is a projection until the source is discriminated.
- Not that markets settle personal satisfaction. D1 and D2 have different anchors (Fig 2 :36-37 and :29).
- Not `grounded`. No consumer measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

- **Threshold Test:** does measured choice behaviour stop at an aspiration level rather than maximize, with the threshold stated before the task?
- **Import Test:** does a provenanced cue from a genuinely different evidence pool reduce the prediction error against experienced quality, and does the same cue stop working when its provenance is removed?
- **Independence Test:** do ratings from purchasers with no exposure to the seller's claims and no coordination predict experienced quality out of sample, while ratings from one pool do not?
- **Habitat Test:** does decision accuracy move when the information habitat changes (labels, tests, returns) and stay flat when only the customer is trained?
- Standing falsifiers from the audit: aggregate residual with no loss of prediction, new atom from more same-pool search, unprovenanced cue as good as an independent test, consumer-only improvement with unchanged habitat.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, on the instrument questions; `gap` on the cue name.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments discriminate and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a list of the ten cues customers use. It says that the decision is a per-slot threshold, not a full optimization; that a belief mixes private experience with inherited prior and updates the weight after outcomes; that when quality cannot be read, more of the same information adds nothing and a signal must come from a genuinely different source to help; that reviews count as evidence only when the reviewers are independent instruments; that better decisions are a property of the customer and the information habitat together; and that no framework can name the diagnostic cue for a product category, only the shape such a cue must have. What it offers is that shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The cue stays open until the ground settles it.
