## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A community holds claims whose currency is repeated successful contact with a particular place and a long history of experience: where the fish run, when a slope will slide, which star marks a landfall, what a wound needs. The question asks what makes the gap between those claims and their outcomes **visible and corrigible**, rather than invisible and self-confirming.

- **What is being decided.** Option A: such knowledge is tradition, maintained socially and not genuinely testable. Option B: it is tested by the same loop as any knowledge, with a specific anchor and specific failure modes. A makes it mere belief. B makes it checkable and names the conditions under which it is reliable or drifts into dogma.
- **R stated as an effect.** R is the per-slot gap between what the community's library predicts on acting and what the ground returns. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* One bay, one season, one route is one slot. A global "the tradition works" near zero with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13), and averaging across slots is exactly how the signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
- **What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, per Formula "Direction").** A *checking arrangement* has four parts we can write down before looking for any example: (1) a nameable anchor that does not update on what the community thinks; (2) frames that keep separate contact with that anchor; (3) an operator that can refuse a term whose residual persists; (4) a membrane that carries methods up and head starts down without pretending a recording is the method. We bind this four-part shape as the sought-for term so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

R by arity, symmetry and scale (Fig 9 :21 to :22: *"arity · symmetry · scale"*).

- **Arity.** Three conditions are named: place, identity, long experience. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single lever clears the place-only region and leaves the intersections, where identity and experience still bind.
- **Symmetry.** The knower/place relation is not a symmetric identity: the same practice is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"*). And the ground is pinned: it does not range against the frame (Fig 2 :25).
- **Scale.** First order within a practitioner's lifetime; second order across generations through the membrane (Fig 4). Contact cascades outward: the residual that matters can be three steps out (Fig 11 :52 to :54, the DDT to eagles case).

**Partition or union.** Union, with overlap between D1 and D2 (shared contact) and between D2 and D3 (shared evidence when researchers and practitioners use the same observations). Each overlap is a compound with its own loop. Unbundle the three-way question:

- **place** -> anchor question (D1);
- **identity** -> frame-membership and independence question (D2);
- **long experience** -> contact and membrane question (D1 and D2 across scale).

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :4 to :16). The middle links, terms for what a situation affords and a statement of what counts as progress, are where chains usually break (Fig 3 :24 to :27). *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not"* (Fig 3 :19). A community cannot revise what it cannot perceive, has no term for, or has no stated objective for.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The anchor of place-based knowledge is the place, and that is legitimate (decides D1)

Fig 2 :29: > *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
Fig 2 :30: > *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."*
Fig 2 :36: > *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

Fig 1 :23: > *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

The question "does this burn bring the birds back, does this star lead to land" is constitutively about the place and the outcome of acting there. The catch, the yield, the landfall, the healed patient are anchors in exactly the sense of Fig 2 :29: they do not change their answer because the guild agrees. Fig 2 :36 licenses them. There is no strain: identity is not being used as the anchor here, the outcome is.

**Mode: `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for the prediction that a community whose practice survives repeated unfavourable outcome returns is not testing its knowledge.** Triangulated across Fig 2 (anchor) and Fig 1 (ground as only metric), two figures with low conceptual overlap.

**Refuter:** show a case where a practice persists and guides action with no ground return at all, where the anchor is purely "we have always done this" and failures produce no change in the practice. That would break the anchor reading, not merely a particular community.

### F2 - Identity is a frame property, not an anchor (decides the "identity" limb of the union)

Fig 2 :25: > *"pinned: it does not update on A or B"*
Fig 2 :33 to :34: > *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
Fig 10 :24: > *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*

If "this is who we are" is what settles a dispute about a practice, the anchor has been replaced by a frame, and the result is coherent drift that nobody inside notices. The tacit-knowledge essay gives the mechanism in ordinary terms: a story passed person to person loses what does not fit and becomes *"more familiar forms"* (Bartlett, described in that essay), so *"each participant's report of the story mirrored his or her own culture."* Nobody lied. The channel drifted.

Identity does have a legitimate role: it carries the method and the pretext that makes compressed messages unpack (tacit essay: *"writing ... almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part"*). What it cannot do is settle truth. Fig 2 :45: > *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

**Mode: `specified`.** Single-source for the figure rule (Fig 2), triangulated with the tacit essay's independent mechanism of reconstruction drift (same author and evidence pool, so weak corroboration, declared).

**Refuter:** exhibit a community where identity genuinely functions as a non-updating reference for empirical disputes, that is, where "who we are" returns a different answer tomorrow when the fish disappear, without any argument. Under the figures that would not be an anchor but a frame, so the refuter is: show a frame that changes its answer with the ground while still being the thing that settles the question.

### F3 - Revision needs a refusal operator, not just more observation (decides what "revise" costs)

Fig 5 :59: > *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*
Fig 5 :55: > *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*
Operators table :58: > *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*

"Revise" is not adding a new observation to a pile. It is minting a refusal of a held term. The candidate for refusal is not intuited: it is the bound term whose residual is flat and persistent. Fig 6 :42: > *"A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted."*

This is where many place-based traditions are said to fail by outsiders, and where the figures say the failure is structural, not cultural: a vocabulary with only accumulation and no negation can be corrected in behaviour and cannot carry the correction. That is why the written record of a craft can outlive the practitioners who could read it as refusal.

**Mode: `general` (structural), `specified` where it predicts which communities can carry corrections.** Triangulated across Fig 5, Fig 6 and the Operators table, three loci with distinct roles (ranking, reach, operator).

**Refuter:** show a system in this domain that revises a persistent false term with no negation operator and no refusal term, and whose library only grows. That breaks Operators :58 and the Fig 5 amendment.

### F4 - Agreement inside the community is a convention, not a verdict (decides D2)

Fig 7 :13: > *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
Fig 7 :35: > *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
Fig 7 :36: > *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
Fig 2 :35: > *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

A community that only checks against itself is a room chain with rho near one: it travels without progress. Testing requires the next room to differ from the last and to match the residual the last surfaced. That is what a deliberate test, a bad season, a rival guild's route, an instrument, or an outside replication can supply. Consensus is efficient for settling what a word means (Fig 2 :41, *"efficient for a convention"*) and disqualifying for settling what is true.

The tacit essay makes the same point with two navigators: *"Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark, and nobody can talk the landmark into moving."*

**Mode: `specified`.** Triangulated across Fig 7 and Fig 2 (low overlap: chain dynamics vs anchor dynamics).

**Refuter:** show a case where agreement among frames that share one evidence pool changes the truth-value of an empirical place claim, with the ground held fixed. Under Fig 2 :40 that cannot happen; if it does, the anchor model is wrong.

### F5 - Long experience renews independence only if experience is separate (decides the "long experience" limb)

Fig 10 :80: > *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained"*
Fig 2 :31: > *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
Fig 2 :42: > *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
Fig 8 :48: > *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."*

Long shared experience is ambiguous. It builds the compressor that lets a few words carry paragraphs (the tacit essay: *"a highly compressed message only unpacks for someone with the right history"*), but long close contact also pulls frames together and spends the extra reach that made two frames useful (Fig 8 :48, quoted above). The remedy in the essay is concrete: *"The apprentice needs time alone at the bench as well as time beside the master."*

So the answer to "does long experience make the knowledge reliable" is: only when it is **separate** experience, renewed against the same unmoving ground. Shared pretext alone produces the drift of F2.

**Mode: `specified`.** Triangulated across Fig 10, Fig 2 and Fig 8. **Strain named, not hidden:** the very factor that makes the knowledge rich (shared history) disables it as a check. Recorded as a tension, not smoothed into a slogan.

**Refuter:** show two frames with fixed-at-origin correlation whose independence is renewed by use alone, with no separate contact. Fig 10 :80 predicts rotation, not refresh.

### F6 - Codifying place knowledge carries a description, not the method (decides the membrane, Fig 4)

Fig 4 :22: > *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
Fig 4 :23: > *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*
Fig 4 :25: > *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
Fig 4 :30: > *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

Writing a practice into a database or textbook (the neighbouring question 74 in the source file) is a recording carried upward. It looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. What can cross is a reapplicable method and a head start, never the experience itself; the tacit essay says *"Lived experience however, does not travel, so each learner rebuilds it through contact."*

**Re-census note (strain dissolved).** An initial reading set Fig 4 :22 against the fact that long experience plainly is transmitted in guilds and apprenticeships. Re-census resolves it: Fig 4 governs what crosses a **scale boundary** as a reusable method; experience is rebuilt through contact inside a lineage, not transported as an object. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. This matches the canonical rule that a reading making figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

**Mode: `general` for the membrane rule, `specified` for the prediction about codification.** Single-source for the figure rule (Fig 4); the tacit essay corroborates at high overlap.

**Refuter:** show a recording of a single occasion being carried upward and reapplied at a new place without a new contact loop, with the round-trip gap zero. That would break Fig 4 :25 and :30.

### F7 - An outside frame helps only after the residual is described (decides D3)

Fig 8 :45: > *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
Fig 8 :53: > *"Residual first, frame second."*
Fig 9 :27 to :28: > *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
Fig 6 :23: > *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Formal science can be a low-overlap frame that adds a surplus (an instrument that detects what the practitioners detect, a model that discriminates causes). But if researchers arrive with a preferred frame and map the practice onto it, they commit the inversion Fig 9 :27 to :28 names; any frame will seem to fit. The residual must be stated in effect terms first, and the import must carry a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6, *"source gate: provenance"*, *"shadow test: explains a gap"*). Fig 8 :39: > *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Absence of a scientific explanation is not absence of knowledge.

**Mode: `specified`.** Triangulated across Fig 8 and Fig 9 (union surplus vs search order), with Fig 6 for the gate. **Strain named:** the effect description is written in the outside frame's words, and Fig 9 :30 warns *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This needs a check, not an argument.

**Refuter:** demonstrate an import from an outside frame that moves the wall with no provenance and no stated gap it explains, and that is accepted by the figures' own tests. That would break the source gate.

### F8 - A test harness is a substituted habitat, not a test of place knowledge (decides what would settle it)

Fig 11 :66: > *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
Fig 11 :70: > *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
Fig 11 :73: > *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*
Fig 11 :40: > *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

The concrete case is Polynesian wayfinding, read this session. Knowledge passed *"by oral tradition from master to apprentice, often in the form of song"*, kept as guild secrets, and tested not by argument but by voyages: David Lewis sailing without instruments, Ben Finney's Polynesian Voyaging Society and the Hōkūleʻa, Mau Piailug's methods, Nainoa Thompson's reconstructed system. Those re-creations are contact with the same ground, not a simulation of it. A laboratory encryption of a navigational song would prove wiring and never capability.

**Mode: `specified`.** Single-source for the figure rule (Fig 11); the Polynesian navigation source corroborates the pattern at low overlap with the framework essays.

**Refuter:** show a synthetic or laboratory reproduction of a place-tied practice that transfers to the place with the contact loop omitted. That breaks Fig 11 :70.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim "place-tied knowledge is testable when the place stays the anchor, frames keep separate contact, refusal is available, and methods rather than recordings cross scales" is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A practice persists and guides action with **no ground return at all**, where "who we are" is the thing that settles empirical disputes and unfavourable outcomes produce no revision (breaks F1 and F2, Fig 2 :29 and :25).
2. Two frames with **fixed-at-origin correlation** and no separate experience converge on the same place claim without ranging against the place, and that convergence changes the claim's truth-value (breaks F4 and F5, Fig 2 :40 and Fig 10 :80).
3. A system in this domain **revises a persistent false term with no negation operator**, its library only growing (breaks F3, Operators :58 and Fig 5 :55).
4. A recording of one occasion is carried upward and **reapplied at a new place with a zero round-trip gap** and no new contact loop (breaks F6, Fig 4 :25 and :30).

If any fires, withdraw the shape. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and its refuter, never with the theory: Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. Unbundle the three conditions into three loops before searching (Fig 9 :54 to :58). Do not treat "place, identity, experience" as one hard question.
3. Protect separate contact: more than one observer, each ranging against the same unmoving ground, with independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
4. Keep the anchor reachable rather than merely recorded: Fig 10 :29, *"keep the anchor reachable"*; the channel decays even when the ground does not.
5. When codifying, state the round-trip gap and send the method, not the occasion (Fig 4 :22, :30).
6. Use public, opt-in institutional venues (`03_public_contact_points.csv`). Ask what would refute the shape; record the verdict before showing any theory.
