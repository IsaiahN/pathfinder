## What the wider literature adds (secondary, never decisive)

1. **The residual is per place, not per tradition.** Houde's six facets separate factual observations from ethics, identity and cosmology, so a single verdict on "a tradition" aggregates away the live slot. This matches Fig 1 :19 (*"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"*) at low overlap with the framework essays.
2. **Testing already has an institutional name.** Adaptive management defines itself as testing assumptions against monitoring data. It is a retrodiction of the figure loop, not something the framework produced. Do not present it as a result.
3. **A real refutation exists in the record.** The Sharp versus romantic-voyaging debate about Polynesian settlement was resolved partly by re-creation voyages that put the knowledge back in contact with ocean and star, not by more text. That is Fig 11 :70 (*"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability"*) illustrated from outside the framework.
4. **The failure mode has an independent description.** Bartlett's reconstructive memory and the tacit-knowledge essay's drift account both say that a chain checking only against itself drifts toward the familiar. That is Fig 2 :34, corroborated at low overlap (cognitive science vs the figure text).

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The framework essays and the canonical README share the author and evidence pool with the figures, so where they agree it is one pool talking, not two witnesses.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the shape and its four refuters; offer the architecture only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any framework link.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mail merge selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, not an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if they ask, the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The three-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the shape against the ground and against what the reader knows. No framework shown. Verdict recorded first.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the three source links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a reusable generator. Each user supplies their own ground, refuter and mode (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives the four refuters. It is a simulated derivation, so it cannot settle the question; that limitation is stated in the first line of the email.
