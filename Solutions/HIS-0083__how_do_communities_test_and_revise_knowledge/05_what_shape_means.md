## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for HIS-0083 a **shape** is the four-part checking arrangement stated before looking for examples: an unmoving anchor at the place, separately maintained frames, a refusal operator for flat persistent residuals, and a membrane that carries methods not recordings. Naming a particular community (the navigators, the midwives, the burners) as *the* answer would be a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for terms that overlap or correlate, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The essay also names the formal stopping condition: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the bridge between the framework and this specific question: place-based knowledge is precisely the kind of thing whose description travels badly and whose effects can be checked by anyone.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level source for unbundling place, identity and long experience (Fig 9 :54).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: an outside researcher who reads a community's practice and names its "real" cause (resource management, adaptation, superstition) is doing metamerism. Several histories can produce the same practice. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 4. Why consensus is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

That is Fig 2 :34 (*"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"*) in plain language. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the social version: *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* The safeguards it lists match the figure's three drift-stoppers: independent replication, two-way apprenticeship with correction, and courts ruling on evidence neither side can move.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape"

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming anything in the answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"a **missing** thing"*, a primitive not held in the library. For HIS-0083 the four-part checking arrangement is not a missing primitive; it is a composition of terms the figures already hold (anchor, frame, negation, membrane, habitat). So naming those parts is legitimate derivation, not invention. What remains forbidden is:

- naming **which community or practice** is correct (that is a ground question, and no ground reading was taken here);
- naming the **cause** of a practice from a reading of the practice alone (the metamerism error of §3);
- claiming the arrangement is `grounded` without an instrument that would show the round-trip gap closing.

Both the original claim and this narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated for the narrowing (README + Fig 13 + Fig 9 :32), at shared-author overlap.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question the trap has two forms. The first is the famous, well-funded institution whose verdict is treated as the answer about a local practice. The second, less obvious, is the reverse: treating the community's own standing ("the elders know") as proof. Both substitute a record of past success for a ground reading. What the four-part arrangement asks for is a budget to try the claim against the place, not an endorsement from either side.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture post adds: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed ... Anything else is drift."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of which community is right and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
