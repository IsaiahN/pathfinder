# Outreach draft - check the shape first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written down and understood. The offer must survive their question or the two-stage structure collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check a shape claim about local knowledge before reading any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question in local knowledge systems research: how a community can test and revise knowledge tied to a place, to identity, or to long experience. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the claim "place, identity, or long experience" is not one question. It is a union of three conditions, each with its own loop. What makes any of them testable is a four-part checking arrangement: (1) a nameable anchor that does not change its answer because the community agrees, here the outcome return of acting at the place; (2) frames that keep **separate** contact with that same anchor, so their agreement is triangulation rather than one evidence pool talking; (3) a way to refuse a held term whose residual stays flat and does not trend down, because revision is a negation and not just another observation; (4) a path across generations that carries **methods** upward and head starts downward, never a recording of one occasion mistaken for the method. This is a shape, not a measurement, and I am not naming any community as the correct one.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that consensus is a test, that identity can settle an empirical dispute, that a written database preserves a practice, or that formal science validates local knowledge on contact. Agreement inside a closed group is a convention; it is disqualifying as a verdict. Long shared experience builds the ability to compress meaning and at the same time consumes the independence that makes agreement informative. A recording of one occasion looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. An outside frame can add a surplus only after the residual is described in effect terms and with a stated source and gap it explains.
>
> **What would refute the shape (written before this message):**
> 1. a practice that persists and guides action with no ground return at all, where "who we are" settles empirical disputes and failures produce no revision;
> 2. two frames with fixed-at-origin correlation and no separate experience that converge on the same place claim without ranging against the place, where that convergence changes the claim's truth-value;
> 3. a system that revises a persistent false term with no negation operator, its library only growing;
> 4. a recording of one occasion reapplied at a new place with a zero round-trip gap and no new contact loop.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract, branch-independent): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this package.
> - Full architecture (Medium, 19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Worked narrative, including the tacit-knowledge essay that bears directly on this question: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/decoding-tacit-knowledge-why-context
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a place or practice you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether what you get is general, specified, or grounded. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| State the union before the frame | Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No claim that consensus tests | Fig 7 :35 - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* |
| No claim that identity anchors | Fig 2 :25 - *"pinned: it does not update on A or B"*; a frame that updates on the room is a mirror. |
| No claim that codification carries the method | Fig 4 :25 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* |
| Sources only after a stage-1 verdict | Fig 10 :42 - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| Shape, not a name | Canonical figures README: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Hence no community is named as the answer. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* |

## Two subjects to avoid

- Anything containing "validates," "proves the tradition true," or "finally a scientific basis for." Those are verdicts stated in `general` mode with no ground reading, and they spend the recipient's independence on contact.
- Anything that selects the recipient for likely agreement. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
