# The Tether's answer: how communities test and revise place-tied knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Knowledge tied to place, identity, or long experience" is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Its three limbs have different anchors and different failure modes, so their verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. R is indexed by slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"*), so "does the tradition work" is the wrong question; "does this burn work in this valley in this season" is the right one.

## 2. The shape: four parts, three loops

1. **The place is the anchor, and that is legitimate.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* This question is constitutively about the place. The outcome return, the catch, the landfall, the healed patient, does not update on the community's opinion. **Verdict for the place limb: testable.** `general` structurally, `specified` where it predicts that a practice surviving repeated unfavourable returns is not being tested.

2. **Identity carries method and pretext; it cannot settle an empirical dispute.** Fig 2 :25: *"pinned: it does not update on A or B"*. A frame that updates on the room is a mirror (Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*). Identity is a frame property, not the identity bond ≡ and not an anchor (`06` Q2). **Verdict for the identity limb: it is the channel, not the check.** `specified`.

3. **Long experience helps only when it is separate.** Fig 10 :80: *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained"*. Shared history builds compression and consumes independence at the same time (Fig 8 :48, *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."*). The apprentice needs time alone at the bench as well as time beside the master. **Verdict for the experience limb: reliable only under separate contact.** `specified`, with the enrichment/check tension named, not smoothed.

4. **Revision is refusal, not accumulation.** Fig 5 :59: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. Fig 5 :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. A vocabulary that can only add observations can be corrected in behaviour and cannot carry the correction. **Verdict for "revise": it costs a negation operator.** `general` + `specified`.

5. **Across generations, methods cross and recordings do not.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Writing a practice into a database is not carrying the method (source question 74 in the same section). The round-trip gap must be stated: Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that consensus is a test. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35)
- Not that a famous institution validates a local practice, or that the community's standing validates it internally. Prestige funds a trial and cannot settle it.
- Not that formal science "confirms" local knowledge. An outside frame can add a surplus only after the residual is described in effect terms, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :45, :53; Fig 6 :23).
- Not which community is right. That is a ground question and is `gap` here: no measurement was taken.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: a spectrometer, not a debate). Concretely:

- **Anchor test:** does the practice change when the outcome return changes, with identity and narrative held constant? If not, F1's refuter has fired.
- **Independence test:** do two practitioners who learned separately, with no shared report, converge on the same place claim by ranging against the place? If they converge without contact with the place, F4's refuter has fired.
- **Refusal test:** is there an observable term in the community's vocabulary that was pointed at a flat persistent residual and refused, with the reach falling? If corrections never take this form, F3's refuter has fired.
- **Round-trip test:** take a codified version of the practice back to the place and measure the gap. A zero gap with no new contact loop would break F6.
- **Harness test:** a laboratory reproduction of a place-tied practice proves wiring and never capability (Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*). The re-creation voyages of Polynesian wayfinding are the non-synthetic version.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework returns the shape of a checking arrangement, not the name of a correct community. The canonical README says the six-term substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Two strains are carried forward rather than hidden: shared history enriches a frame and disables it as a check; the effect description used to recruit an outside frame is written in borrowed words (Fig 9 :30 to :31). Both need an instrument, not an argument.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which local tradition is right, and it does not say that tradition is mere belief. It says the question is three questions. Place-based knowledge is testable because the place returns an outcome that does not change when the community agrees; identity carries the method and the shared history but cannot settle an empirical dispute; and long experience is a check only when it is **separate** experience, renewed against the same unmoving ground. Revision is not another observation added to a pile. It is a refusal aimed at the term whose residual kept being wrong and did not trend down. What crosses generations is a reapplicable method and a head start, never a recording of one occasion mistaken for the knowledge. Consensus inside one group is a convention, not a verdict; the next test must differ from the last and match the residual it surfaced. What is offered is the shape any such checking arrangement must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
