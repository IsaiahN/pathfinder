## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 (anchor and independence), Fig 5 (refusal), Fig 4 (membrane), Fig 9 (union) and Fig 11 (habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-part checking arrangement carried in `01` and `07`: unmoving anchor, separate frames, refusal of flat persistent residual, methods not recordings.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete test of a real community, for example "this guild tests its star compass by sailing the route and comparing the landfall."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the anchor reading** at a real place: "the outcome return" is a kind, not a measured quantity, and no instrument here discriminates a genuine test from a post-hoc story about one (the metamerism problem from *The Hard Problem is About Source*);
- the **operand type of refusal** in a particular vocabulary: Fig 5 requires a negation operator aimed at a bound term, and whether a community's correction practice has that form needs observation, not an argument;
- the **source gate** for an outside import: the effect description is written in borrowed words (Fig 9 :30-31), and the gap cannot be assumed zero.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a non-updating anchor is required for a place claim to be testable, and agreement inside a closed frame cannot supply it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the four-part arrangement is the figures in fewer words" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a stated gap.
