## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a community's testing and revision of knowledge tied to place, identity, or long experience. Option A: treat the three conditions as one question and answer "tradition, maintained socially, not genuinely testable". Option B: unbundle the union and derive the shape of a checking arrangement for each limb. What each leads to: A averages away the live slot and names no anchor, so it cannot say where a check failed; B states an anchor, the independence condition, the refusal requirement and the membrane rule, and records four refuters. The verdict comes from the figures, not from the number or standing of the sources.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity 3: place, identity, long experience, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Symmetry broken: the ground is pinned and does not range against the frame, while the knower does. Scale: first order in a practitioner's lifetime, second order across generations through the membrane. R is indexed by slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."), so a global "the tradition works" near zero with one live slot is a legal state.
- **Domain 1, practitioner loop.** Actors: practitioner, apprentice, tool, place. Currency: predictions paid by outcomes, and correction size. Anchor: the outcome return of acting at the place, nameable and legitimate because the question is constitutively about it. Type: partition boundary (what composes across contact).
- **Domain 2, community frame.** Actors: peers, elders, guild. Currency: agreement plus residual. Anchor: the same ground reached separately by several practitioners, not agreement. Type: union with D1 through shared contact.
- **Domain 3, outside frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, rival knowledge systems. Currency: predictions statable in effect terms. Anchor: its own ground readings. Different closure. Type: union with D2, low overlap possible only if the residual is described first.
- **Domain 4, verification frame.** Actors: checkers and this derivation. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so D4 independence is zero (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Then: describe the residual, keep the anchor reachable, keep frames separate, refuse the flat persistent term, carry the method across scales. Refusal precedes minting.

Strain test applied: identity is not the identity bond of Fig 12, because substitution loses history; it is a frame property. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of three conditions, and the verdicts stay separate. **Place:** testable, because the outcome return is a legitimate non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :29, :36). **Identity:** a carrier of method and pretext, not an anchor and not a check; treating it as the anchor is coherent drift. **Long experience:** a check only when it is separate and renewed against the same ground; shared history both enriches and disables. **Revision:** it requires a negation term aimed at the flat persistent residual; a vocabulary that can only accumulate cannot carry a correction. **Transmission:** methods and head starts cross scales; recordings do not, and the round-trip gap must be measured. **Consensus:** a convention, not a verdict. **Outside frames:** a possible surplus only after the residual is described and with a source gate. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The verdict on which community is right is `gap`, not silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a wrong verdict imported into a real community: telling practitioners their knowledge is untested when their checking loop is intact, or telling outsiders that a recording preserves a practice when it does not. Fig 2 :42 ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.") prices the independence that would catch that error. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The four-part arrangement is a check on any checking arrangement, not a primitive that has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No community is named as right, so no standing verdict about a people has to be retracted. What cannot be undone is a contact already spent asking people to trust before checking, which is why the offer defers the sources.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An anchor test showing a practice does not change when the outcome return changes; an independence test showing two separately trained practitioners converge without contact with the place; a refusal test showing corrections never take the form of a negation aimed at a flat residual; a round-trip test showing zero gap with no new contact loop; or a discriminator, like the spectrometer, that names the source of a practice reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
