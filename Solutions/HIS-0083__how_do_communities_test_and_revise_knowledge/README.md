## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not become an anchor.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) A list selected for agreement is a room, not a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* calls LLM output *"simulacra"* and says *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. It states the shape a checkable answer must have and the instruments that would close it.
