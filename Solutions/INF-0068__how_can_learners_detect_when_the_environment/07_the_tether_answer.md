# The Tether's answer: detecting invalidation

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural invariants. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a detector and not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A learner holds terms that once paid against the ground. After a change, its predictions keep missing, and it must decide whether "enough" has accumulated to refuse a held term rather than to re-fit a binding, extend perception, mint a mechanism, or do nothing. Fig 9 :54 calls such a bundle a union, not one hard question: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** The residual is therefore not a single number to threshold. It is a decision over several conditions, each with its own remedy and its own anchor.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep R per slot. A global average is not sufficient.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 1 :13: **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**. A learner can be wrong on one slot while every headline metric is green.

2. **The invalidation signature is a residual that persists and does not trend down, on a term the learner already holds.** Fig 5 :59: **"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"**; Fig 5 :60: **"The selector asks which residual is improving; this asks which one keeps being wrong and is not."** This is the shrinking test read with the opposite sign. A transient that decays is noise; a miss removed by a re-fit is a broken binding. Only the flat, persistent residual points at a term to refuse.

3. **Five bins, not two.** Fig 5 :50: **"AMENDMENT · the gap has a fifth kind, and the pipeline has no branch for it."** New conditions, a broken binding and a broken mechanism are sorted by what is missing; the fifth is sorted by what is wrong: Fig 5 :53 **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**. The other four cannot reach the fifth, so a learner equipped only with re-fit and mint can be corrected but cannot carry the correction. The operator owed is negation, not subtraction.

4. **The anchor must not update on the learner.** Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."** Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."** Held-out readings, fresh experiments and external counters are ordinary pins. If the criterion moves with the learner, the detector is a mirror, and Fig 2 :35 says **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

5. **What changes is the habitat, not the ground; the channel can decay.** Fig 11 :73: **"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**. A flat residual can mean the world changed or that contact was lost. Those are different verdicts, and only the first licenses a refusal of held terms.

6. **Independence, not detector count, decides whether a check means anything.** Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 8 :50: **"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."** More monitors on the same stream buy spelling, not search.

7. **No finite search certifies absence.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."** The honest output is a bounded statement with its reach and window recorded. "No alarm fired" is not "no drift".

8. **Bind the sought-for threshold before the run.** Fig 5 :65: **"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."** An unwritten threshold is a preference, invisible to every internal check. The pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 is what makes it refusable.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a detector name. ADWIN, DDM, Page-Hinkley and statistical process control charts are bindings from the machine-learning frame with their own provenance. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**
- Not a threshold number, window length or significance level. Those are preferences until bound and settled.
- Not "a global error rate is enough". That erases the one-live-slot case, which Fig 1 :13 calls a legal state.
- Not "any drift means all learning is invalid". New conditions, broken bindings and broken mechanisms have their own bins.
- Not "no alarm means no drift". Absence of detection is not a certificate.
- Not a theory of everything, and no last detector. The stop is at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of a learner was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's spectrometer resolved one green reading into several sources; here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the flat residual persist **per slot**, after a re-fit has been tried, against a **pinned** anchor? A per-slot record with those three properties is the observation the shape predicts.
- **Contact test:** is the channel still returning anything? If not, the correct output is *"you have lost touch"*, not a claim about the habitat (Fig 10 :27-28).
- **Source test:** can one instrument separate habitat change from channel decay from term falsity? If the three remain metameric, the isomer claim in `06` stays structural only.
- **Standing refuters:** a global aggregate that detects as well as the per-slot reading; an updating anchor that works as well as a pinned one; a persistent residual reliably removed by re-fit; a finite proof of no drift. Any one firing breaks the corresponding finding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the instruments stop. Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."** Whether a given learner has drifted is a ground question for its anchor, not a question this derivation can answer. The answer returned here is the form any check must have, with the lineage recorded so the stop is checkable. Agreement among these files is one evidence pool talking, not corroboration.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a drift detector and does not give a threshold. It gives the shape any detector of invalidation must have. Keep the residual per slot, because a global average can be flat while one tracked thing is failing. Treat "enough" as a residual that persists and does not trend down on a term the learner already holds, which is the opposite sign of the usual shrinking test. Split the decision into five bins, because new conditions, a broken binding, a broken mechanism and a false held term are different verdicts and only the last owes a refusal. Range against an anchor that does not move with the learner, or the drift becomes coherent and nobody notices. Remember that what changes is the habitat, not the ground, and that a dead channel can look exactly like a changed world. Buy independence, not more copies of the same monitor. And never claim that no alarm means no drift: a finite search cannot certify absence. What is offered is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the remaining choices.
