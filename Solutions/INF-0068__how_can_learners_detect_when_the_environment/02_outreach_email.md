# Outreach draft: check the shape before deciding about the detector

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 has been written down and dated. The figure's own rule is that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, and that a sought-for shape held as a preference is invisible to the loop until it is bound as an ordinary term.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about drift detection, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific shape I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the shape does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** A learner holds terms that used to pay against the ground. After a change, its predictions keep missing. How much miss, and of what kind, before a held term should be refused rather than re-fitted, extended or replaced?
>
> **The shape claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A detector of invalidation has five structural requirements, not a threshold number:
>
> 1. The residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` stays indexed by the object or region being tracked. A global average is not sufficient: a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state.
> 2. The invalidation signature is a residual that persists and does not trend down on a term the learner already holds. That is the opposite sign of the usual shrinking test. A spike that decays after a re-fit is a broken binding, not invalidation.
> 3. The decision has five distinct bins, not two. New conditions, a broken binding, a broken mechanism and a false held term are different verdicts; only the last owes a refusal.
> 4. The detector ranges against an anchor that does not update on the learner's own output. If the criterion moves with the learner, drift becomes coherent and nobody notices. Held-out readings, fresh experiments and external counters are the ordinary ways to pin it.
> 5. Independence, not the number of monitors, decides whether a check means anything. More detectors on the same stream are one detector wearing many names.
>
> **What this is not.** It is not an argument for ADWIN, DDM, Page-Hinkley or any named algorithm. The framework returns a shape, not a name; those detectors are bindings from your field with their own provenance. It is not a claim that a global error rate is enough, and it is not a claim that "no alarm fired" proves no drift. A finite search cannot certify absence.
>
> **What would refute the shape.** I wrote these down before writing the conclusion:
>
> 1. A learner detects invalidation as early and as reliably with the residual aggregated across slots as with the per-slot reading, while one slot is live and the global residual is near zero.
> 2. A learner whose anchor updates on its own predictions detects drift as reliably as one with a pinned anchor, under the same change.
> 3. A persistent, non-trending-down residual is reliably removed by re-fitting or extending perception, with nothing left over.
> 4. A finite inside-the-frame procedure certifies that no drift of any shape is present.
>
> If any holds, the shape is wrong in that place and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Gama et al., "A Survey on Concept Drift Adaptation", ACM Computing Surveys 46(4), 2014: http://eprints.bournemouth.ac.uk/22491/
> - River drift detector documentation (ADWIN, PageHinkley, DDM): https://riverml.xyz/latest/api/drift/ADWIN/
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run it on a drift problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## What is deliberately absent

No request for trust, no prestige claim, no "bombshell", no list of likely believers, and no personal email addresses. Fig 2 :42 prices the correct ask: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A request to check a refuter buys an independent reading; a request to endorse a theory spends it.
