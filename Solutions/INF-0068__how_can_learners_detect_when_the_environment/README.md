**Residual in effect terms.** A learner holds terms that predict observations. After a change in the environment the predictions keep failing. The decision is whether "enough" failure has accumulated to refuse a held term, rather than to re-fit a binding, extend perception, or mint something new. A false alarm discards still-valid knowledge; a miss keeps acting on a term that is no longer paid for by the ground.
