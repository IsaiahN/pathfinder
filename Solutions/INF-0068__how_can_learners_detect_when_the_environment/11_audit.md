## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid this before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what a learner's held terms predict and what the observation returns, after an environmental change. Arity: at least two conditions, and a three-way split underneath (habitat change, channel decay, false term), so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54), not one hard question. Symmetry: anisotropic, indexed by object or region, so one live slot can coexist with a near-zero global residual (Fig 1 :13). Scale: the signal is within one scale, but the decision changes the controller's vocabulary and the habitat/channel split crosses the membrane.
- **D1, the environment / habitat.** Actors: actors, conditions, relations in contact with R. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which settles and does not update (Fig 11 :73). Union with D2 at the observation; categorically distinct from the ground.
- **D2, the learner's loop and channel.** Actors: Gamma, b, w, a, o-prime, slots s, the instrument reading the anchor. Currency: R per slot; two incommensurable prices (Fig 12 :126). Anchor: the same ground, reached through a decaying channel (Fig 10 :24). Partition of R into five bins.
- **D3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: held-out readings, second closures, outside frames. Currency: independence (Fig 2 :31). Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 7 :35). Separate frame; never pooled.

Order by dependency: contact before verdict (Fig 3 :19); describe before look (Fig 9 :27-28); unbundle before search (Fig 9 :54); refuse before mint (formula step 2); SEARCHED before the opposed instruments (formula step 7); ground last (Fig 5 :70).

My census matches the primary's three domains, its union reading and its dependency order. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 (per slot), Fig 5 :50-60 (five bins, persistent-and-flat), Fig 2 :33-34 (anchor), Fig 11 :73 and Fig 10 :24 (habitat/channel).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: per-slot, persistent-and-flat, five-bin, pinned anchor, independent, no certificate of absence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete detector specification, for example a per-slot ADWIN with a significance level and a window.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **five-bin distinction** collapses if a single alarm answers new, binding, mechanism and refusal alike;
- the **habitat/channel split** is lost if the detector reads one pooled stream and cannot say whether contact died;
- the **no-certificate-of-absence limit** is lost the moment a report says "no drift" instead of "no drift found within this reach and window."

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: keep the residual per slot and look for a non-improving residual on a held term against a pinned anchor. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
