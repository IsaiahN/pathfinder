## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 says **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 says *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INF-0068, the shape is:

> per-slot residual, persistent-and-flat on a held term, routed to a refusal bin that the other four bins cannot reach, ranged against an anchor that does not update, checked by frames with disjoint evidence, and never claiming to certify absence.

A named detector, a numeric threshold and a window length are **names**, not shapes. The shape is what any candidate detector must satisfy before it is worth testing.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the terms you already have that are closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual here was deliberately described in effect terms (predictions keep missing; how much before a term is refused) before any figure line was chosen.
- The essay on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*
- The essay on bundles is directly load-bearing: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. Fig 9 :54 states the same rule as **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** The question's single phrase "changed enough" is such a bundle.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, ADWIN, DDM and Page-Hinkley are admitted only by route 2. They are field objects with their own provenance and their own ground. The figures neither derive nor exclude them. What the figures do govern is the instrument condition: Fig 6 :32 **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** A detector is eligible to be sharpened because it already returns a failing residual, not because it was described in advance.

## 4. The source problem: why one residual reading is not one cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism this question needs. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's general claim:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*

Apply that to drift without letting it decide the verdict. A habitat shift, a decayed channel and a falsely held term can all present as the same flat residual. A single residual reading is a projection; it does not determine the source. The essay's resolution is also the figure's: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger threshold. Fig 12 :117 names the structural form: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** `06_isomers_and_order.md` works this out.

**Correction to my own §3.** I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any detector at all. The README's phrase governs *"a **missing** thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. Here the shape constrains the relation between held things; the detector names are imports, not missing primitives. So naming ADWIN is not forbidden, it is **unlicensed until provenance and a shadow test are shown**. Both claims are recorded: the shape governs the relation, and the specific name is a binding with a source gate, not a derivation.

## 5. Q answered: what does "shape" mean for INF-0068?

**The framework returns the invariants a detector must have, not the detector.** Concretely:

- It returns the *arrangement* of the decision: per slot, then five bins, then a refusal operator, then an anchor that does not update. That arrangement is the substance, in Fig 12's sense that *"the arrangement is the substance."*
- It does **not** return a window size, a significance level, a warning threshold, or an algorithm name. Those are held as preferences until written down and ranged against a ground (Fig 5 :65).
- It does **not** certify that the detector has found every drift. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

The honest answer to "how can learners detect..." is therefore a specification of what a correct detection procedure would have to look like, plus the instruments that would settle the remaining choices. It is not a procedure that can be run, and saying otherwise would be a mode error.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question the failure would be citing a famous drift survey as if its authority settled whether the shape is right. The survey can give the claim a budget to be tried. It cannot settle it. A refutation from an unknown practitioner with a checkable experiment outranks an endorsement from a famous name, because Fig 2 :40 makes disqualifying correlation the very thing a verdict must avoid.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any learner, and they cannot settle the question they report on. This must stay visible on every substantive claim.
