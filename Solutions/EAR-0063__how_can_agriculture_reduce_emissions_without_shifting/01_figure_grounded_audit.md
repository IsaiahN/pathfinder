## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

We are asked for a change in agricultural practice with two effects at once:

- **E1.** Emissions attributed to the practice fall, as counted in the adopting region's inventory.
- **E2.** Total environmental harm, counted across all regions, all media, and the relevant time horizon, does not rise.

The residual R is the gap between E1 as a bookkeeping result and E2 as a property of the ground. The named failure is that E1 is achieved by moving the burden: the inventory improves while the ground does not. That is not a measurement error inside one frame. It is a **union compound**, because E1 and E2 are two conditions with their own loops and their own grounds. A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

Three consequences follow immediately, before any figure is chosen:

1. R cannot be one scalar. It is indexed by region, by harm currency, and by order of contact.
2. The phrase "without shifting" is a **comparison** between a before-state and an after-state across the full habitat, not a property of a practice considered alone.
3. The adopting region's own inventory is a **frame** in the sense of the Symbols table, not the ground. It cannot certify E2 about itself.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** at least three operands, emissions here, harm there, and the food or fibre service that must be preserved. The union of these is not a list; the intersections are the actual problem.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. `more yield here` and `more land elsewhere` are not interchangeable, and the bond between them is ordered (`→` production / sequence) and comparative (`⋛` comparison). Under Fig 12, the same atoms under a different bond are a different substance.
- **Scale:** first order inside the farm or region; second order across the trade and land-use network; third order along the cascade (fertilizer to nitrous oxide to ozone, or feed demand to pasture displacement to forest conversion). The residual that matters is often not at first order.

**One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Farm and field production | soil, crop, livestock, fertilizer, machinery, farmer | kg CO2e per hectare or per kg output, a local inventory | a measured flux: chamber, eddy covariance, fuel and input records | a candidate partition bounded by the farm, but the boundary is chosen, not given |
| D2 Trade and land-use frame (the elsewhere) | commodity markets, importers, other regions' producers, forests, peatlands | land area, embodied emissions, commodity flow | consumption-based accounts, remote sensing, paired with independent trade data | union with D1; closure differs, so the union has surplus |
| D3 Environmental-harm frame (non-carbon) | water, pollinators, soil biota, downstream communities, wildlife | nutrient load, water quality, species abundance, soil organic matter | measured ecological indicators, not the carbon metric | union with D1 and D2; harm is not denominated in CO2 |
| D4 Verification and governance | certifiers, regulators, buyers, standards bodies, auditors | agreement, compliance, residuals | the ground, never agreement; stake must be declared | a frame that currently often holds D1 only, so it cannot see D2 or D3 |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. D1 has a plausible anchor (a flux measurement) but it is an anchor for E1 only, not for E2. D2 and D3 have candidate anchors, and the honest status is that they are often absent from the deciding frame rather than absent from the world. D4 has no anchor of its own by construction: if its verdict moves when a buyer or a region pushes on it, it is a frame, not an anchor.

**Question type.** Union, not partition. Unbundle before searching. A single lever (for example, yield intensification on existing land) clears the region where only E1 binds and leaves the intersections (E1 and E2 together) standing, because E2 still binds.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** (1) perceive: measure every slot; (2) vocabulary: name flows, embodied emissions, and non-carbon harm currencies; (3) objective: state the comparison of total net burden before and after; (4) planning: choose a practice under incomplete information; (5) learn and carry: verify, then carry a method. The chain usually breaks at link 3, and a reading taken below the break is a reading of nothing. Most improvement effort lands above the break, where the emissions measurements already exist.

## 3. Findings

Mode is stated on every finding. `triangulated` means two figure lines with low overlap, or a figure line plus a low-overlap world source; `single-source` means one figure line only. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two.

### F1 - The residual is indexed by region slot, not aggregated

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 and the Symbols table, with Fig 2 on the anchor).**

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Symbols table :43: "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

A national emissions total is a legal aggregate for some purposes, but it is the wrong instrument for this question. The live slot is the region that receives the displaced production, and it can carry positive mass while the global average looks flat. Aggregation is exactly how the failure named in the question becomes invisible.

**Refuter:** a per-region, per-medium accounting, measured against an anchor independent of the adopting frame, that shows no positive residual in any slot after the change. If that is produced, F1 does not apply to that case.

### F2 - Isolation is substitution, not removal (the governing finding)

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 and Liu et al. 2013, plus Searchinger et al. 2008 on the land-use case).**

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."

Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."

Fig 11 :68: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"

Fig 11 :69: "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"

This is the structural statement of leakage. A region that meets its emissions target by importing feed, shifting processing, or converting land elsewhere has not removed its emissions habitat; it has substituted another habitat. It inherits that habitat's actors (other producers, other land markets, other hydrology) without choosing them. The reduction it failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails; the harm it introduced is invisible until that harm acts. Both silences are structural, not accidental.

World corroboration, low overlap: Searchinger et al. 2008 (*Science*, DOI 10.1126/science.1151861, abstract read via Crossref 2026-11-22) state that prior analyses *"have failed to count the carbon emissions that occur as farmers worldwide respond to higher prices and convert forest and grassland to new cropland to replace the grain (or cropland) diverted to biofuels."* That is the substitution law in an agricultural case, reached from a worldwide agricultural model, not from these figures.

World corroboration, low overlap: Liu et al. 2013 (*Ecology and Society* 18(2):26) describe *"sending, receiving, and spillover systems"* and note that forest transitions *"often occur at the cost of forests in other countries through trade of forest and agricultural products."* Different field, same structure.

**Refuter:** a documented case where the practice's full habitat is reproduced at the new location, with the same conditions and no unchosen actors, and where the goal still holds after the cascade finishes. If that exists, substitution is not the governing law for that case.

### F3 - Contact cascades: the residual that matters is three steps out

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 and Liu et al. 2013).**

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

Fig 11 :52: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

The figure's own case is agricultural in substance: DDT applied to crops, biomagnified through the food chain, thinning eggshells in birds of prey. Fig 11 :54 cites Carson, *Silent Spring*, 1962. The method is enumeration, not composition: read the list off the world, including what you cannot yet measure. For agriculture the analogues are direct (a fertilizer decision has a nitrous-oxide flux, an ozone and nitrate cascade, and a downstream water consequence), and indirect (a yield or mandate decision has a price signal, a land conversion in another region, and a biodiversity consequence). Scoping to the farm gate is first-order enumeration with the residual left three steps out.

**Refuter:** a complete cascade enumeration, with instruments at each order, that shows the residual decays to zero at first order and never reappears at second or third order.

### F4 - Valence is relative to the residual, not carried by the practice

**Mode: `specified`. Status: single-source for the figure definition, corroborated by Liu et al. 2013 on trade-offs and synergies.**

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

Intensification, irrigation, legume rotations, biofuel crops, and precision fertilizer are not good or bad practices in themselves. Each is a benefactor to one residual (local emissions per kilogram) and can be a malfactor to another (regional water, land, or nutrient loading). A verified practice list is a head start for the next search, never a permanent list of good actors.

**Refuter:** a practice whose sign relative to every relevant residual is invariant across regions and years. That would falsify the relativity claim.

### F5 - A frame cannot score itself with the quantity it produces

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 10).**

Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."

Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."

A region, a certifier, or a buyer that uses its own territorial inventory to certify that it has not shifted harm is scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. The stake cannot be removed, so it must be declared. The anchor for E2 must be something that does not move when that frame pushes on it: a measured flux, an independent consumption-based reconstruction, a remote-sensing land-cover series. A market price for a certified commodity is not such an anchor; it is inside the frame it is meant to check.

**Refuter:** an anchor whose answer is invariant under pressure from the adopting frame, demonstrated by an actual perturbation rather than asserted.

### F6 - Two low-overlap frames are required, and two copies of one data set are one frame

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 2).**

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."

Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A territorial inventory and a consumption-based accounting built from trade flows draw on different evidence, so their union has surplus. Two regional reports generated from the same emission factor table and the same activity data draw on one evidence pool; their agreement is the pool talking. The cost of being wrong here includes a wrong claim of "no leakage" applied across a whole supply shed, so independence should be bought in proportion to that cost.

World corroboration, low overlap: Ritchie 2019 (*Our World in Data*, opened 2026-11-22) writes that for consumption-based emissions *"we need to track which goods are traded across the world, and whenever a good was imported we need to include all CO2 emissions that were emitted in the production of that good."* This is a different frame from a territorial inventory by construction.

**Refuter:** evidence that the two frames said to corroborate were built from the same activity data and factors, so their overlap is high and the surplus is zero.

### F7 - This is a union compound; unbundle before searching

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 9, with the domain census).**

Fig 9 :4: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset,"

Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

The question bundles at least three conditions: emissions down, harm not moved, service preserved. A single lever clears the region where only the first binds, and the intersections remain because the second and third still bind. The honest move is to unbundle, give each condition its own loop and ground, then search the compound that remains at the intersections. This is why no single practice can be named as the answer from inside the frame.

**Refuter:** a single demonstrable lever that closes all three conditions together, with each condition measured separately and shown to fall.

### F8 - The objective is a comparison, progress a subtraction, refutation a negation

**Mode: `specified`. Status: single-source (Fig 12 and the Operators table).**

Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."

Operators table :51: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"

Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."

"Reduce emissions without shifting harm" is a `⋛` comparison between total net burden before and after, with progress as the subtraction, not a property named by an ingredient list such as "less fertilizer, more efficiency." A vocabulary that can only name ingredients can describe a farm and cannot describe getting somewhere from it. Negation is required so a failed candidate can be refused rather than merely accumulated.

**Refuter:** a candidate objective with an explicit threshold and a explicit refusal condition, shown to be equivalent to the comparison above; or a demonstration that `⋛` cannot range over the two before/after states at this level.

### F9 - Methods cross between regions; recordings do not

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 4 and Fig 6).**

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

A practice that worked in one region, carried to another as a recipe, is a recording of one occasion. The new habitat remakes it: different soils, prices, hydrology, and neighbours. What can cross is the method (enumerate the habitat, measure each slot, state the comparison, check with a low-overlap frame, record the gap). Fig 6 :30 adds: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

**Refuter:** a practice that transfers unchanged into a new habitat with no loss of fit and no new unchosen actors, verified after the cascade finishes.

### F10 - Any single metric carries a round-trip gap R_T

**Mode: `specified`. Status: single-source (Fig 4).**

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

Compressing E1 and E2 into one headline number (for example, "net emissions savings") loses the information the coarser description cannot hold: which region, which medium, which order of contact, and which stake. The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. The gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the honest report of what was given up. Treating the metric as identical to the ground sets R_T to zero and is the dead-reckoning error.

**Refuter:** a round trip in which the per-slot detail survives compression and returns unchanged. That would make R_T zero for that metric.

### F11 - Reachability: search first, then extend inward, then import outward

**Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 8, with Fig 9).**

Fig 6 :29: "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."

Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

From inside D1, "no harm was shifted" is **underivable**, not merely unfound: the closure of a territorial inventory does not contain the atoms of another region's land conversion. Formula step 7 orders the three questions. **SEARCHED first**, cheapest and assumed by the others: is the evidence already in trade statistics and existing datasets but not assembled? **INWARD** second: extend the instrument from one already returning something, for example adding consumption-based reconstruction to an existing inventory. **OUTWARD** third: import from a frame whose closure differs, casting the residual in effect terms so the description travels. Inward and outward are opposed and must never be read against each other; importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

**Refuter:** a derivation of the leakage evidence from D1's own atoms, showing it was reachable inside the closure and only unfound.

### F12 - Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence

**Mode: `general`. Status: triangulated (Fig 13, Fig 8, Fig 9).**

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

If no instrument can register the harm moved to another region, the honest output is `gap` or `unreachable-at-this-budget`, with the missing thing described in effect terms. It is not a claim that the harm did not occur, and not a claim that a particular practice is clean. The figures return a shape, not a name; a name requires either a ground settlement or a provenanced import with a shadow test.

**Refuter:** an instrument that registers the moved harm at any resolution, which converts the verdict from unreachable to reached. Absence of such an instrument is not evidence of absence.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat-map discipline, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim of this solution is:

> No claimed reduction in agricultural emissions is admissible as "without shifting harm" unless the full habitat is enumerated, the residual is reported per slot and per harm currency, the objective is a comparison against a pinned anchor checked by at least two low-overlap frames, and the round-trip gap is recorded.

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A per-slot, per-medium accounting across the full cascade shows total net burden falling with no positive residual in any region slot after the change, measured by an anchor independent of the adopting frame.
2. The substitution law fails: a practice moves its residual to another habitat while reproducing every condition its goal required, inheriting no unchosen actors, with no invisible failure and no invisible introduction.
3. Two independent low-overlap frames converge on the same total, both pinned to the same ground, and the agreement is shown to be convergent derivation rather than a shared evidence pool.
4. A single lever closes all three bundled conditions together, each measured separately and each shown to fall.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the shape is revised. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Enumerate before choosing.** List everything in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :43-44). Do not start from a list of approved practices.
2. **Report per slot.** Publish the emissions residual and the non-carbon harm residual for each affected region and medium; never as a single global total (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. **Pin the anchor.** Use a measured flux and an independent consumption-based reconstruction; declare the stake of any certifier or buyer (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :60).
4. **State the objective as a comparison.** Write the before/after total net burden and the refusal condition before any practice is chosen (Fig 12 :112, Operators :51, :58).
5. **Buy two low-overlap frames.** Territorial inventory plus consumption-based or remote-sensing evidence, with the overlap recorded so agreement is not mistaken for corroboration (Fig 8 :45, :49; Fig 2 :40).
6. **Unbundle the union.** Split emissions, shifted harm, and service preservation into separate loops before searching the intersections (Fig 9 :54-55).
7. **Carry methods, not recipes.** Each region re-enumerates its habitat; the transferred item is the checking procedure (Fig 4 :22-23).
8. **Record the gap.** Publish R_T for any headline metric; treat it as a measurement, not as zero (Fig 4 :30).
9. **Say unreachable plainly** when no instrument registers the moved harm (Fig 13 :134).
