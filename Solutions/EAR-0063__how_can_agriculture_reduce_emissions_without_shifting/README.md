## The question restated as an effect

An answer is wanted for a change in agricultural practice such that two things happen together: (1) the greenhouse-gas emissions attributed to the practice fall, and (2) the total environmental burden, counted across every region and every medium, does not rise. The failure the question names is that (1) is obtained by buying the reduction with (2): emissions fall inside the adopting region's accounting boundary while land conversion, nutrient loading, water depletion, or biodiversity loss appears in a different region, a different medium, or a later time. The two halves are not one scalar, so a single number cannot be the answer.
