## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are secondary witnesses only. Figures decide; world sources corroborate and never overrule. Overlap with the framework is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Land-use change and indirect emissions.** Searchinger et al., "Use of U.S. Croplands for Biofuels Increases Greenhouse Gases Through Emissions from Land-Use Change," *Science*, 2008, DOI 10.1126/science.1151861. Abstract opened via the Crossref API on 2026-11-22: *"These analyses have failed to count the carbon emissions that occur as farmers worldwide respond to higher prices and convert forest and grassland to new cropland to replace the grain (or cropland) diverted to biofuels."* This is the substitution law in a real agricultural case. It predicts that a saving counted inside one boundary can be reversed by a response outside it. Corroborates Fig 11 :66-69. **Low overlap** with the figures.
2. **Telecoupling.** Liu et al., "Framing Sustainability in a Telecoupled World," *Ecology and Society* 18(2):26, 2013, opened 2026-11-22. *"telecoupling, an umbrella concept that refers to socioeconomic and environmental interactions over distances"*; the framework covers *"sending, receiving, and spillover systems"* and helps *"detect hidden costs and untapped benefits."* It predicts that a farm-gate boundary misses the receiving and spillover systems. Corroborates Fig 11 :43-52 and Fig 1 :19. **Low overlap.**
3. **Consumption-based versus territorial accounting.** Hannah Ritchie, "How do CO2 emissions compare when we adjust for trade?", *Our World in Data*, 2019, opened 2026-11-22. *"whenever a good was imported we need to include all CO2 emissions that were emitted in the production of that good, and vice versa to subtract all CO2 emissions that were emitted in the production of goods that were exported."* This is the second low-overlap frame the derivation requires. Corroborates Fig 8 :45 and Fig 2 :31-32. **Low overlap.**
4. **What agriculture actually emits.** Hannah Ritchie, "Food production is responsible for one-quarter of the world's greenhouse gas emissions", *Our World in Data*, 2019, opened 2026-11-22. *"the release of nitrous oxide from the application of fertilizers and manure; methane emissions from rice production; and carbon dioxide from agricultural machinery"*, and *"Ruminant livestock – mainly cattle – for example, produce methane through their digestive processes (in a process known as 'enteric fermentation')."* These are the per-slot currencies the enumeration must hold; they are not interchangeable with CO2 from fuel. Corroborates Fig 11 :44-48. **Low overlap.**
5. **Carbon leakage as a known policy failure.** IPCC AR6 WGIII Chapter 11 (Industry), Bashmakov et al. 2022, opened 2026-11-22. *"Industry has so far largely been sheltered from the impacts of climate policy and carbon pricing due to concerns for competitiveness and carbon leakage"* and the accounting identity distinguishes *"territorial and consumption-based emissions"* with *"Trade policies including carbon leakage issues."* Different sector, same accounting structure; used as an analogue only, not as an agricultural result. Corroborates Fig 1 :27 and Fig 8 :45. **Low overlap with the framework, different domain from the question.**
6. **Eight essays by Isaiah N** (Substack, 2026, all opened 2026-11-22): Library of Babel, Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, The Hard Problem is About Source, Decoding Tacit Knowledge, How a Society of Agents Would Work, They're Scanning Books When They Should, Arrival of the Fittest, How Intelligence Actually Works. These give the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md`. **High overlap** with the figures (same author and evidence pool), so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight.

No world source conflicts with a figure verdict, so the WATSON conflict rule was not triggered. Searchinger et al. and Liu et al. are not treated as two readings of one dataset: they address different cases and corroborate the figure structure separately.

2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three public links above. The figures, not this summary, are the authority.
