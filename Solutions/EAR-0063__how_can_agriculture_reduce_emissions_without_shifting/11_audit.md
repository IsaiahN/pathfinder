## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before using the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a fall in emissions counted inside an adopting region and the total environmental burden across all regions and media. Arity: three operands (emissions here, harm there, service preserved), so a **union compound**, since a case in three conditions is not a harder case of one. Symmetry: not symmetric; the bonds are ordered production and comparison, and the same atoms under a different bond are a different substance. Scale: first order at the farm, second order across the trade network, third order along the cascade.
- **Domain 1, farm and field production.** Actors: soil, crop, livestock, fertilizer, machinery, farmer. Currency: CO2e per hectare or per kilogram. Anchor: a measured flux, valid for the emissions condition only. Question type: a chosen partition, not a given one.
- **Domain 2, trade and land use.** Actors: markets, importers, other producers, forests, peatlands. Currency: land area, embodied emissions, flows. Anchor: consumption-based accounts and remote sensing with trade data. Question type: union with D1 because the closures differ.
- **Domain 3, non-carbon harm.** Actors: water, pollinators, soil biota, downstream communities. Currency: nutrients, water quality, species, soil organic matter. Anchor: measured ecological indicators. Question type: union with D1 and D2; the currencies do not add to carbon.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: certifiers, regulators, buyers, auditors. Currency: agreement, compliance, residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: a frame that often holds D1 only and cannot see D2 or D3.

Order by dependency: perceive per slot, acquire vocabulary for flows and harm currencies, state the comparison and refusal, plan, verify and carry a method. Unbundle the union before searching. Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the substitution finding from Fig 11, I reached the same boundary from Fig 8 :45 (union surplus requires differing closures) and Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 11 :66-69 and :44-52, Fig 2 :29, Fig 8 :45, Fig 12 :112.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `README.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md`: report per slot, enumerate the habitat, pin the anchor, use two low-overlap frames, state a comparison, carry a method, refuse a name.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a hypothetical concrete programme: a regional certification that a crop was grown with lower emissions and no deforested land in its supply shed.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **operand type of `⋛`** at the level of whole before/after total burden states (the Operators table restricts it to quantities);
- the **order of unbundling before search** (Fig 9 :54-55), which a certification scheme routinely reverses by naming the practice before describing the compound;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**, which a certification breaches by treating a label as if it fixed the member, where Fig 12 :124 says the group says what kind and the qualifier says which member.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a residual must be reported per slot and the habitat must be enumerated outward. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating a single headline metric as identical to the underlying figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
