## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," with the next line explaining that the missing primitive cannot be named because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a candidate is admissible only if the full habitat is enumerated, the residual is per slot and per harm currency, the objective is a before/after comparison against a pinned anchor, two low-overlap frames check it, and a method rather than a recipe crosses regions.
- **Name:** "use nitrification inhibitor X in region Y" or "certification Z proves no leakage." These are bindings from the agricultural frame onto a held practice. They are not the answer the framework returns.

The distinction matters because the question as posed invites a name. It asks "how can agriculture reduce emissions," which sounds like a request for a practice. The derive-first census shows it is a request for a shape with an opened slot.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-22): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by the time you have the exact search terms you have *"already composed the answer."*

Applied here: describe the failure in effect terms first, "emissions fall here while land, water, or biodiversity harm appears there," then go looking for the frames that predict its shape. If a practice is chosen first and the habitat is drawn around it afterwards, any practice will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README adds that the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question that means a measured cross-region dataset with its provenance intact, not a claim imported dressed as a derivation.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (opened 2026-11-22) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six. Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the choice of "emissions" as the primary currency is a visible guess about what to compress. Adding the non-carbon harm currencies is the new dimension, not merely another term in the same space.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The agricultural analogue: a fall in a regional emissions inventory is a reading, not a source. Several paths create that reading, including genuine abatement, imported feed, displaced processing, and land conversion elsewhere. Naming "the practice worked" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources: consumption-based accounting paired with remote-sensing land-cover change, or an equivalent source-discriminating measurement.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is why this folder does not claim that its shape is true.

## 5. Q2: what licenses a particular practice in the slot?

**Answered.** Nothing in the framework licenses a particular practice. A specific practice is licensed only by the ground settling a candidate, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question as posed commit the forbidden error?** It invites it, but does not commit it until an answer names a practice. The correct form keeps the slot open: `EMISSIONS DOWN -> (??? ) -> HARM NOT MOVED`, with the `(???)` standing for the enumeration and checking procedure, not for a technology.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any specific practice. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame. Practices are not missing primitives; they are already in the agricultural frame's library. Naming one is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong is treating that binding as a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall. Fig 6 :23 says no frame escapes its own closure except on contact with a frame whose closure differs. A practice already in the library adds no atom. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The practical consequence is unchanged: a named practice needs provenance and a shadow test, not a ban.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and method

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well known names to agree that a practice is clean is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**The room is per problem; the commons holds methods only.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (opened 2026-11-22): *"there are two stores: The room, one per problem. Agents share everything, recordings included, to solve that one problem faster. The commons, across problems. It holds only methods distilled from finished rooms."* And: *"The ground sits under all three: no store's agreement replaces it."* This is the figure-level reason a practice list cannot be universal: each agricultural habitat is its own room, and only the checking method crosses to the commons.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator... An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any agricultural claim and cannot settle the question it reports on.
