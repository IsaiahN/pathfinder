# The Tether's answer on reducing agricultural emissions without shifting harm

**Mode: `specified` on the structural findings, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result about any particular supply chain. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A proposed agricultural change has two effects that are being treated as one: emissions fall inside the adopting region's boundary, and environmental harm must not appear elsewhere. The contact between these two is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"). It has its own loop and its own ground. The failure named in the question is that the first effect is bought by the second: the inventory improves while the ground does not.

## 2. The shape

1. **The residual is per slot, not an aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The region that receives displaced production is the live slot; a global total hides it. Report emissions and non-carbon harm separately for each affected region and medium.
2. **Moving the residual substitutes a habitat; it does not remove it.** Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose." The two silences follow: Fig 11 :68, "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails", and Fig 11 :69, "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts".
3. **Enumerate the cascade, do not draw a boundary.** Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." A farm-gate boundary is first-order enumeration with the material residual left outside.
4. **The adopting frame cannot certify this about itself.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor for the no-shift condition must be outside the inventory that produced the saving. Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."
5. **Two low-overlap frames are required.** Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." A territorial inventory and a consumption-based reconstruction built from trade flows have surplus; two reports from one emission-factor table do not. Fig 2 :40 disqualifies a shared pool as a verdict.
6. **The objective is a comparison, not an ingredient list.** Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Write total net burden before and after, with a threshold and a refusal condition, before choosing any practice (Operators table :51).
7. **Carry methods, not recipes.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Each new habitat re-enumerates; what transfers is the checking procedure. Record the round-trip gap of any headline metric: Fig 4 :30, "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
8. **Say unreachable plainly when no instrument registers the moved harm.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **Not a named practice.** No inhibitor, cultivar, certification, diet, or technology is nominated. A name needs ground settlement or a provenanced import with a shadow test.
- **Not a claim that any real supply chain did or did not shift harm.** No measurement was taken.
- **Not that local emissions reductions are worthless.** E1 is a legitimate first-order result in its own frame; the error is promoting it to a verdict about E2.
- **Not that all harm is carbon.** The harm currencies include nitrogen, water, land cover, and biodiversity; they do not add to CO2 in one figure (Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add").
- **Not that a certification or a border adjustment subtracts the habitat.** Fig 11 :61 gives introduction, not exclusion; a seat can add a condition but cannot subtract contact.
- **Not grounded.** Nothing here is settled by an instrument.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22), an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer that resolved the cases. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the emissions fall come from genuine abatement, or from imported feed, displaced processing, or land conversion elsewhere? An instrument that discriminates these sources would name the slot. Concretely: a territorial inventory paired with a consumption-based reconstruction and an independent land-cover series, with provenance for each.
- **Threshold test:** does total net burden across the full enumerated habitat fall from before to after, per region slot and per harm currency, against an anchor that does not move when any adopting frame pushes on it?
- **Substitution test:** are the conditions the goal required reproduced at the new location, and are the actors inherited there few and declared? If unchosen actors arrive with the relocated production, the substitution law stands.
- **Cascade test:** does the residual reappear at second or third order after the programme has run long enough for those effects to act?
- **Standing refuters:** a per-slot accounting with no positive residual anywhere; a practice that reproduces its full habitat elsewhere; two low-overlap frames pinned to one ground and shown to be convergent derivation; or one lever closing all three bundled conditions. Each is written in `01` §4 before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The set of currencies the frame currently holds is a visible choice, not a neutral fact (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, opened 2026-11-22: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*). Another sensorium's accounting need not be these currencies. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). If no instrument registers the moved harm, the verdict is `gap`, not a clean bill of health.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a farming practice and does not claim any real supply chain is clean. It says that the residual must be reported per region and per harm currency, not as one global total; that moving production substitutes a habitat rather than removing one, so you inherit actors you did not choose and inherit failures that stay invisible until the goal fails or the new harm acts; that a farm-gate boundary is first-order enumeration with the residual left three steps out; that the adopting region's own inventory cannot certify the result because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it produces; that a territorial account and a consumption-based account are two low-overlap frames whose agreement is worth something only if their evidence pools differ; that the objective is a before/after comparison with a refusal condition, not an ingredient list; and that whatever crosses between regions is a checking method, not a recipe. What it offers is the shape any admissible answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
