# WATSON answer: can agriculture reduce emissions without shifting environmental harm to other regions?

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself"), it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a change in agricultural practice claimed to reduce emissions without moving environmental harm elsewhere. Option A: accept a named practice plus a single emissions total as the answer. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the name and the aggregate until a ground settles them. What each leads to: A sends a `specified` claim as if it were settled, scores the adopting frame with a quantity it produces, and hides the receiving and spillover slots; B sends a checkable shape, four written refuters, and an open slot until an instrument closes it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between E1 (emissions fall inside the adopting region's inventory) and E2 (total environmental burden across all regions, media and the relevant horizon does not rise). Arity: three operands, emissions here, harm there, service preserved, forming a union compound. Symmetry: not symmetric; the bonds are ordered production `→` and comparison `⋛`, and the same atoms under a different bond are a different substance. Scale: first order at the farm, second order across the supply shed, third order along the cascade. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Domain 1, farm and field production.** Actors: soil, crop, livestock, fertilizer, machinery, farmer. Currency: kg CO2e per hectare or per kg output. Anchor: a measured flux, valid for E1 only. Question type: a candidate partition whose boundary is chosen, not given.
- **Domain 2, trade and land-use frame.** Actors: commodity markets, importers, other producers, forests, peatlands. Currency: land area, embodied emissions, commodity flows. Anchor: consumption-based accounts and remote sensing paired with trade data. Question type: union with D1; the closures differ, so the union has surplus.
- **Domain 3, environmental-harm frame.** Actors: water, pollinators, soil biota, downstream communities. Currency: nutrient load, water quality, species abundance, soil organic matter. Anchor: measured ecological indicators, not the carbon metric. Question type: union with D1 and D2; harm is not denominated in CO2.
- **Domain 4, verification and governance.** Actors: certifiers, regulators, buyers, auditors. Currency: agreement, compliance, residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement; stake declared. Question type: a frame that often holds D1 only and therefore cannot see D2 or D3.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive per slot, acquire vocabulary for the flows and harm currencies, state the comparison and refusal condition, plan the practice, then verify and carry a method. The chain usually breaks at the objective link, and a reading below that break is a reading of nothing. Unbundle the union before searching the intersections. Describe the residual in effect terms before naming a frame.

Strain test applied: Fig 11's substitution law (introduction available, exclusion not) governs moving a residual between habitats; a certification or border adjustment is an introduced condition, not a subtraction of contact. No contradiction; re-census keeps the two objects separate.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name and the aggregate do not. The residual must be reported per region slot and per harm currency; moving production substitutes a habitat rather than removing one, so the new actors are inherited and both failures are invisible until they act; the cascade must be enumerated outward; the adopting frame cannot certify its own inventory; two frames with low evidence overlap are required; the objective is a before/after comparison with a refusal condition; and a method, not a recipe, crosses regions. If no instrument registers the moved harm, the verdict is `gap`, not a clean result. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. No practice, certification or single metric is admitted as an answer.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim is mode-labelled, carries four refuters written before the run, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a named practice or a headline metric) instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A specific practice or metric can be withdrawn without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement that separates genuine abatement from displaced production; a per-slot accounting across the full cascade with no positive residual anywhere against an independent pinned anchor; two low-overlap frames pinned to one ground and shown to be convergent derivation; a practice that reproduces its full habitat elsewhere with no unchosen actors; or one lever closing all three bundled conditions. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
