# Outreach draft: check one output before deciding whether to engage

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** this email carries a claim in `specified` mode with a pre-registered refuter. Do not add a named practice, a certification, or a single headline metric after this draft is approved; that would reverse the order of the offer and spend the recipient's independence (Fig 10 :42).

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about agricultural emissions and shifted harm

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question and would value a check from someone who knows the ground. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A claimed reduction in agricultural emissions is not admissible as "without shifting harm" unless five things hold. First, the habitat in contact with the residual is enumerated, including second- and third-order contacts, rather than bounded at the farm gate. Second, the residual is reported per region and per harm currency, not as one aggregated total. Third, the objective is written as a comparison of total net burden before and after against an anchor that does not move when the adopting region pushes on it. Fourth, at least two frames with low evidence overlap agree on that comparison, with the overlap stated. Fifth, what crosses between regions is the checking method, not a recipe from one occasion, and the round-trip gap of any headline metric is recorded.
>
> The reasoning in effect terms: a region that meets an emissions target by importing feed, shifting processing, or converting land elsewhere has substituted a habitat rather than removed one. It inherits actors it did not choose. What it failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails, and what it unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts. A local inventory cannot certify this about itself, because it scores itself with a quantity it also produces.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular practice is the answer; that a certification proves no leakage; that a single metric closes the question; that the reduction did or did not occur in any real supply chain. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I have taken no measurement.
>
> **What would refute the claim (written before this message):**
> 1. A per-region, per-medium accounting across the full cascade shows total net burden falling with no positive residual in any slot, measured by an anchor independent of the adopting frame.
> 2. A practice moves its residual to another habitat while reproducing every condition its goal required, inheriting no unchosen actors, with no invisible failure and no invisible introduction.
> 3. Two independent low-overlap frames converge on the same total, both pinned to the same ground, and the agreement is convergent derivation, not a shared evidence pool.
> 4. One lever closes all three bundled conditions (emissions, shifted harm, service preserved), each measured separately and each shown to fall.
>
> If any holds, I retract the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` (local extract; public repo `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures`).
> - Canonical figures README: `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md`.
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` (local).
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The framework's own limit is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name / role]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| Two low-overlap frames required | Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 2 :40 disqualifies a shared evidence pool. |
| Links only after the verdict | Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." |
