Wet-dry, heating-cooling and freeze-thaw are candidate closures because the product of one pass is the input of the next. A single dry-down is a chain.

- that vents beat hot springs, or that any one name belongs in the slot;

## 4. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. Concretely:
