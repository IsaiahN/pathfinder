## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not follow. Four constraints are held as a union compound: a spendable gradient, a spatial concentration gradient, a closed temporal succession and a bonded reacting network, with a recorded handoff between stages. No named setting is licensed by the figures, and no shortlist of names can be corroborated by agreement inside a shared evidence pool. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate. The strongest checkable point is the written refuter set in `01` and `08`, not any list of settings.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries refuters before its conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking instead of a generator. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter, as `02` does.
- **undoable:** yes. A wrong name can be withheld or withdrawn without touching the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A refuted sub-claim is removed from the reach by adding its refusal, not by deleting a record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement showing one setting satisfying all four slots across all stages would settle the application at the world frame. A directionless energy term driving a sustained network, a variable that is both a spatial gradient and a temporal succession with no change of bonded state, or a one-off event re-entering its own output would break a figure sub-claim. A figure re-census admitting a setting name from the six terms alone would clear the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
