## The question as an effect

Decide between two things. Option A: produce a ranked list of named settings (hydrothermal vents, hot springs, ice interfaces, tidal pools), treating energy, concentration, stability, and ingredients as four checkboxes a setting can tick. Option B: derive the shape any setting must have before a name is admitted, keep the four constraints as separate per-slot residuals, and leave the naming to a ground (an experiment, a measured gradient, an ancient rock). A leads to a list whose agreement is an evidence pool, not a verdict. B leads to a checkable shape, written refuters, and an honest open slot.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** `THE_SEAT_MAP_Field_Guide_amended.md` :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list of believers is a convention, not a witness.
3. **Asking for trust spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one.

## Honest standing
