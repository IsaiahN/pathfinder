## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question names four wanted properties and asks for the settings that combine them. Restated as an effect: something is being decided about where chemistry can keep going rather than stall. Each candidate answer leads somewhere different.

- **Energy leads to** a flow only if the energy is a spendable difference. Otherwise it is conserved, finite, and directionless alone.
- **Concentration leads to** reactions only if monomers are locally dense and held there. Otherwise dilution wins.
- **Stability leads to** persistence only if the ordered succession of differences continues and the loop closes. Otherwise a single dry-down damages the templates.
- **Ingredients lead to** a substance only if they are bonded in a recorded arrangement. An ingredient list is not a molecule.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before searching: **a setting is a candidate only if it simultaneously (1) supplies a spendable gradient, (2) establishes a spatial gradient or containment that holds reactants, (3) persists as a closed succession over time, and (4) carries a network of reacting entities whose bonding is recorded.** This is the sought-for shape written down so it can accrue a residual like anything else (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

Option A would answer with names and treat the four properties as checkboxes. Option B derives the shape first and leaves the name to the ground. B is what the figures license.

## 3. Findings

### F1: "Right energy" is a spendable gradient, not energy by itself

Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone."** Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent."** Fig 13 :76: **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

Energy is one of the six states. Alone it has no direction, so it cannot drive a reaction network. What a setting must supply is a gradient bonded to a state. Fig 12 :126 adds the second currency: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** A named setting that merely has heat, light, or redox potential, with no measured gradient that can be spent, fails the energy condition.

This is a `specified` structural claim, triangulated across the substrate definition (Fig 13 :14, :22) and the cross-kind composition rule (Fig 13 :76). World corroboration: Martin, Hoffmann and Schlikker (2026) report native metals in serpentinizing vents and a *"new source of energy at the origin of metabolism"*; Kato (2026) calls vents plausible *"owing to their steep redox gradients and the availability of chemical energy."*

**Refuter:** show a case where a directionless energy term (no spendable difference) drives a sustained reaction network, or derive the gradient from the other five substrate terms.

### F2: Concentration is a spatial gradient; stability is a temporal succession

Fig 13 :10: **"near, far, between, contained."** Fig 13 :46: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent."** Fig 11 :46: **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain."**

Concentration is a gradient bonded to space, containment and proximity. Stability is Fig 13 :12: **"the ordered succession of differences."** They are different terms. Fig 13 :76 says a gradient over space and one over time are different because the bonded state is part of what the term is. So a hot spring with strong wet-dry concentration but weak geological persistence, and a vent with long persistence but poor retention of products, are not variants of one condition. They are different compounds.

World corroboration: Simonis et al. (2026) show that *"a single dehydration event leads strictly to template damage and degradation"* and that *"it is exclusively through the environmental filtering of repeated wet-dry cycling that this regime acts as a kinetic trap, fostering polymerization."* Lu, Li and Liang (2026) note that minerals act by *"adsorbing and concentrating reactants, catalyzing key reactions, stabilizing intermediates, and providing protective microenvironments."*

**Refuter:** demonstrate one physical variable that simultaneously is a spatial gradient and an ordered temporal succession with no change of bonded state, or measure a setting whose concentration persists without any temporal structure.

### F3: A cycle closes; a single dry-down does not

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."**

Stability is not "the setting lasts a long time." It is the loop re-entering its own output. Wet-dry, heating-cooling, freeze-thaw, and tidal cycles are candidate closures because the product of one pass is the input of the next. A catalyst is the part that survives its own use, which is why mineral surfaces matter in the world literature. A single dehydration is a chain, and a chain cannot close by construction.

This is `specified` and single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), with world corroboration from Simonis et al. (2026) and Lu et al. (2026).

**Refuter:** show a one-off event that re-enters its own output across geological time without a cycle, or show that the loop's sign does not decide closure.

### F4: Ingredients are a bonded arrangement, not a list

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 13 :24: **"that entities relate, and influence travels."**

The fourth condition is a network of relating entities with typed bonds. Listing CHNOPS, minerals, or monomer concentrations records the atoms and omits the relation. This is the flat-formula failure of Fig 12, and it is why "chemical ingredients" cannot be a checkbox.

**Refuter:** produce a term whose bonded arrangement makes no difference to its behaviour, or show that the network term is derivable from the spectrum term.

### F5: Four conditions form a union compound, not a partition

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**.

A setting that satisfies only energy is not a harder case of the answer. It is a different compound, because the other conditions still bind. Fig 9 :56: a single lever *"leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Therefore the question's four properties must be held as four live slots (Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**), not summed into one setting score.

World corroboration is strong and independent: Lu, Li and Liang (2026) state that *"no single geological setting could simultaneously satisfy the diverse environmental requirements of all reaction stages"* and that *"prebiotic evolution probably proceeded through complementary reactions distributed among multiple geological settings, including hydrothermal vents, terrestrial hot springs, and potentially extraterrestrial niches."* Kato (2026) hypothesises *"transitions between environments, rather than on processes within any single environment alone."*

**Refuter:** show a single setting that satisfies all four conditions simultaneously and across all stages, measured, not asserted. That would defeat the union conclusion at the world frame, though not the figure shape itself.

### F6: The framework returns a shape, not a name

Fig 13 :133: **"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."** Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 9 :42: **"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"**.

So the figures can deliver the four constraints and the union rule. They cannot deliver "hydrothermal vents" or "tidal pools." A name is a binding from the world frame onto a held atom, not a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :26: **"An import adds no atom to the world."**).

**Refuter:** derive the name of a specific setting from the six-term set alone, with no world-frame binding and no measurement.

### F7: The anchor is an experiment, not agreement among researchers

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."** Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Candidate settings are enumerated from the world, never composed from the six. Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** UV light is a driver for one chemistry and a degrader for another; a vent is a source for precursors and a destroyer of polymers above 300 °C. The label "right setting" is a relation to a residual, not a kind.

**Refuter:** show that a setting's valence is intrinsic to the setting, independent of which residual is being tracked, against a measured case.

### F8: Filters are not witnesses

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."** A ranking of settings by how many checkboxes they tick is a budget filter. It cannot decide which setting actually worked. A search that finds no single setting is not a proof that none exists: Fig 8 :39, **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the run, per the seat-map rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five (breaks the minimum set).
2. A single physical variable is shown to be both a spatial gradient and an ordered temporal succession with no change of bonded state (breaks F2).
3. A measured setting satisfies all four conditions simultaneously and across all reaction stages (breaks F5's union conclusion at the world frame).
4. A directionless energy term, with no spendable difference, is shown to drive a sustained reaction network (breaks F1).
5. A setting's valence is shown to be intrinsic rather than relative to the residual, in a measured case (breaks F7).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the four constraints and the refuter, not with a list of settings.
2. State the mode on every substantive sentence (F1 to F7 are `specified`; the no-last-room structural claim is `general`).
3. Treat candidate settings as an enumerated habitat (Fig 11 :43), with valence relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
4. Ask what would refute each sub-claim before showing any theory (Fig 10 :52).
5. Use filters (tractability, accessibility) for the budget only (Fig 9 :60).
6. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Verdict of this audit.** The shape survives: a spendable gradient, a spatial concentration gradient, a closed temporal cycle, and a bonded network, held as a union compound. The name does not follow from the figures. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate.
