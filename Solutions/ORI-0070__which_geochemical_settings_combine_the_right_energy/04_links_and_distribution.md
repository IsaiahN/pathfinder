## 2. Wider literature actually read

All six papers below were found through the Europe PMC REST API and their abstracts were read on 2026-11-23. Quotes are verbatim from those records. They are secondary corroboration. They cannot decide a figure reading, and they do not name a setting.

- **Lu, Li and Liang 2026, Fundamental Research** (mineralogy review). *"prebiotic evolution probably proceeded through complementary reactions distributed among multiple geological settings, including hydrothermal vents, terrestrial hot springs, and potentially extraterrestrial niches"*. This is the nearest world-frame statement of the union reading. It corroborates Fig 9 :54 on unbundling a union. It is one witness, not two, because the other five papers work the same origins-of-life question.
- **Simonis et al. 2026, Scientific Reports** (wet-dry chamber). *"These findings demonstrate that thermodynamic control within an environmental simulation chamber can drive the formation of complex, enzyme-resistant biopolymers without the use of activating agents"*. Corroborates the need for a repeated, controlled cycle, and it is a ground-shaped result: a chamber, not a vote. It does not show that wet-dry is the only closure.
- **Martin, Hoffmann and Schlikker 2026, Trends in Microbiology** (serpentinizing vents). *"Native metals also phosphorylate organic compounds in water using phosphite, uncovering a new source of energy at the origin of metabolism."* Corroborates that a spendable difference, not energy in the abstract, is what a setting supplies.
- **Altair et al. 2026, Chemical Communications** (vent mineral barriers). *"Redox gradients across vent minerals driving electron flow have long been proposed as a prebiotic means for chemical reductions."* Its companion finding, that ion conductivity may limit CO2 reduction, is a live refuter-shaped detail rather than a corroboration.
- **Van Kranendonk et al. 2026, Astrobiology** (hot spring fields). *"A key challenge for permanently submerged hydrothermal vents is the inevitable dilution that occurs when fluids are ejected from deep-sea hydrothermal vents into a relatively uniform oceanic reservoir"*. Corroborates that concentration is a spatial condition with a different failure mode from energy supply.
- **Spoto 2026, Life** (planetary screening model). *"The index is not calibrated as a probability of life."* Corroborates Fig 9 :60: a score is a budget filter, not a witness.

**Overlap statement.** These six papers are drawn from one broad evidence pool, origins-of-life geochemistry. They are from different subfields (mineralogy, polymer chemistry, microbial metabolism, electrochemistry, field geochemistry, planetary modelling), so the overlap is low at the method level and high at the question level. Their agreement is therefore one witness with six faces, not six independent witnesses. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 3. Distribution rules

1. **One to one, or to a public venue.** Use `02_outreach_email.md` as a cover note attached to a submission through one of the fetched institutional routes. Never bulk send, never scrape personal addresses.
2. **Two stages, in that order.** Stage 1 carries the four constraints and the pre-registered refuter, with no framework vocabulary and no theory. Record the reader's verdict on stage 1 before showing anything else. Only if the output survives does stage 2 release the figure text and the world links.
3. **Ask for a failure, not agreement.** A one-line *this fails because* is the wanted reply. Agreement is recorded as a convention, not pooled as corroboration.
4. **Mode on every claim.** `specified` for the shape, `general` for the no-last-room structure, `grounded` for nothing in this folder.
5. **Filters for the budget only.** Tractability, access and venue fit decide where to spend time. They never decide whether the shape is true. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
6. **No trust request.** The first contact does not ask the reader to trust the framework or its author. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
7. **Keep the lineage.** Record which sub-claim was checked, by whom, against what ground, and what would reverse it.

## 4. The two-stage offer, in brief

**Stage 1.** Here is a four-part shape claim about what any setting must supply, and four things that would refute it. What fails? No framework, no links, no theory.

**Stage 2, only after stage 1 is recorded.** Here are the verbatim figure lines and the six world records. Here is what I claimed they predicted before I looked. Here is where I was strained, and what I retracted in `01`.

This ordering is not politeness. Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* A reader who sees the architecture first can reconstruct the claim, and a verifier that can reconstruct the claim is not a witness.

## 5. What this distribution cannot do

It cannot settle the question. No number of expert readers, public venues or fetched abstracts adds a measurement of a setting that satisfies all four conditions at once. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The most this distribution can produce is a checkable shape, a recorded refuter, and an honest open slot for the name.
