## What an answer would look like

The question is a yes/no prompt, but the effect it describes has three parts: a decision rule that is symmetric on its face, a distribution of outcomes that is persistently asymmetric, and the field of contact that carries actors into the rule with unequal starting positions. An answer is a shape that says whether these can be composed in one frame, which quantity each claim actually governs, and what instrument would close the residual. It is bound here as the ordinary term **form/outcome/habitat split**, so it can accrue a residual like anything else instead of remaining an invisible preference (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No verdict about whether any particular institution is just or unjust. That is a ground question and needs an instrument, not this derivation.
- No normative "ought" claim. The figures decide what can be composed; they do not decide values.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
2. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Asking for endorsement before a check converts possible outside frames into believers.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in or institutional submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Isaiah N, Substack, 2026): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This solution cannot settle the question it reports on.
