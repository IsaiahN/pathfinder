## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

*The Library of Babel* (opened): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the source. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** "rule form, habitat and outcome distribution are three quantities with different anchors; a symmetric rule over an asymmetric habitat can yield an asymmetric distribution." This is what `01` derives.
- **Name:** "this institution is unjust", or "the disparity is caused by bias". Both require a ground reading and a source-discriminating instrument that this solution does not have.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on a malformed question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the exact situation in INS-0046 and is the warrant for F1.

Applied here: describe the effect (a rule that swaps labels without changing decisions, and a distribution that stays skewed across groups), then look for the frame whose closure predicts it. The legal frame (disparate impact) was found *after* the residual was described, not before. Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism for why a reading is not a source: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." A disparity reading (one green) does not tell you whether the source is the rule, the habitat, or both (RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case. Here the corresponding instruments are audit studies, disparity statistics and confounder-controlled regressions.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (opened) sharpens the status of the vocabulary: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The categories "formal", "substantive", "impartial", "disparate" are a visible human choice, not nature's list. Recording the choice is what keeps the answer checkable.

## 4. The specific forbidden error for this question

The error is **promoting the convention to a verdict**. "We are formally impartial" is a claim about the form of the rule (D1). "Therefore our outcomes are fair" is a claim about the distribution (D3). The second does not follow from the first, and asserting it makes the convention decide an outcome, which Fig 10 :47 forbids: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one".

A second error is **averaging away the live slot**: reporting one impartiality score while a group-level residual persists. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A third error is **naming the source from the reading**: calling a disparity "bias" without an instrument that discriminates among rule, habitat and confounder. That is the metamerism error above.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid calling a disparity discrimination?

**Narrowed answer: no, but it requires provenance.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. "Discrimination" is not a missing primitive being invented here; it is a binding from the legal frame onto a concept already in common use. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as something the framework derived, or as something that moves the wall. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

I overstated this in an earlier reading of the phrase. Earlier form: "the framework returns a shape, not a name, so nothing about the source may be said." Retracted as applied to bindings onto held concepts. The correct form: the framework returns a shape for the *missing* thing; a binding onto a held concept is legitimate provenance work, and the metamerism duty to find a discriminating instrument remains.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prestigious signatories who agree that institutions are (or are not) impartially unequal is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried against an outcome.

**Agreement is not ground.** Same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The Medium article adds that LLM outputs must be seen as "simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. One-paragraph reading of "shape" for INS-0046

The shape this question can return is a three-term split: a symmetric convention at the level of one decision, an asymmetric habitat that supplies the cases, and a per-slot outcome distribution measured at the ground. It does not return the name of a guilty institution, a verdict on justice, or the source of a particular disparity. Those require an instrument, and until one is applied the honest report is a constraint plus a list of what would reverse it. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).
