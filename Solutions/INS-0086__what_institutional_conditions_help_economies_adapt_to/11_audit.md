## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I chose different entry figures from the primary where possible, starting from Fig 4's membrane and Fig 9's split rather than from Fig 1's loop.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Two conditions, not one hard question: the technique must be absorbed, and the gain must not concentrate. Arity two. Symmetry broken: the concentration residual is indexed by slot, so any sum over slots destroys the signal. Scale: a technology arrives locally and composes upward into an economy, with the gain read back down at the slot. Fig 9 :55 to :56: **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** This is a union compound; the intersection is categorically different, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57 to :58).
- **Domain 1, production and adoption.** Actors: firms, workers, technologies, capital. Currency: capability, whether the technique gets used and prediction matches output. Anchor: a measured adoption and productivity reading. Question type: partly maintained habitat; the ground is partly a market.
- **Domain 2, distribution and measurement.** Actors: households, groups, slots, statistical agencies. Currency: per-slot gain. Anchor: an instrument that resolves the top of the distribution and does not move when a frame pushes on it. Question type: the channel is the vulnerable quantity. Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**.
- **Domain 3, institutions as habitat maintainer.** Actors: rule-makers, regulators, courts, social partners. Currency: rules, instruments, head starts. Anchor: the ground, which must remain reachable. Question type: seat, acting across Domains 1 and 2. Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: researchers, models, expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking frame. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.

**Partition or union.** Domains 1 and 2 are a union, not a partition, because the overlap (adoption with concentration) is a specific compound with its own loop. Domain 3 is the seat acting across both; Domain 4 checks. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing.

**Order by dependency.** Domain 2's per-slot instrument first, because the objective cannot be stated without it; then Domain 1's terms for what the technology affords; then the objective itself (both conditions); then Domain 3's rules and planning; then Domain 4's verification. Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."** A rule that arrives before the measurement that would test it lands above the break and cannot reach the step that needs it.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading and its dependency order. Where the primary derived from Fig 1's per-slot loop and Fig 11's habitat, I derived from Fig 4's membrane (methods cross up, head starts down) and Fig 9's union split. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, reading it as the difference between x and the concretisation of its abstraction. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete answer the question literally asks for, a named list of institutions that close both conditions for a technology.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union compound** of F1: the shorthand's single phrase drops that the two conditions have a specific intersection which is not a harder case of either;
- the **cascade** of F5: distributional effects are second and third order, and the habitat needs permanent maintenance, neither of which is carried by "keep the channel independent";
- the **arrangement over ingredients** of F8 and the **middle-link priority** of F9: a named list is a flat formula, and Fig 12 :111 says a vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one;
- the **import debit** of F7: a borrowed arrangement buys surplus and spends the witness, which a recipe cannot record.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: read the gain at the slot and do not let the measurement body own the reading. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
