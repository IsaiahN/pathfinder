## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The distribution half is not a footnote.** Acemoglu and Restrepo measure technology's effects at the local labour-market level and find concentrated losses; Our World in Data shows the measurement instrument itself has a blind spot at the top. Together they corroborate F2 and F3: read per slot, and maintain the channel. They do not corroborate a specific institutional list.
2. **Adaptation is systemic.** Lundvall's innovation-systems entry and the Resilience Alliance's social-ecological framing both describe capability as a property of a system of interacting parts, not of a single firm. They corroborate F4 and F6 from fields with low overlap with each other.
3. **The union is visible in policy practice.** The Flexicurity page describes flexibility and security as deliberately combined rather than traded off, and ties the need to technological change. That is a named instance of F1's compound. It is a binding from the policy frame, `specified`, not a figure derivation.
4. **Why Nations Fail corroborates the capture finding** (F3 and F5): a group that gains from the current arrangement has an interest in blocking the creative destruction that would redistribute the gain. It also records a live objection (reverse causality), which is why this is corroboration, not settlement.

Overlap is declared, not hidden. The two NBER pages share authors and a paper, so they are one witness. Wikipedia summaries are tertiary, so they are weaker than the primary abstracts they describe. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; if one did, it would send the derivation back to the domain census, never flip it.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration later.
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** The formula's "Direction" note says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.
