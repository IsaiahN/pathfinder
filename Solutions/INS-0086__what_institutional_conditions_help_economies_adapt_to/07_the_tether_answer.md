# The Tether's answer: What institutional conditions help economies adapt to technological change without concentrating its gains?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on its application to economies, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. ## 1. The residual it answers

An economy faces a technological change. Two things can fail: it cannot absorb the change (`R_adapt`), or it absorbs it while the gain is captured by one group (`R_dist`). The question asks for conditions that close both. Written as an ordinary term before the run (formula, "Direction"): find C such that under C `R_adapt` trends down and the per-slot residual of `R_dist` does not concentrate in one slot.

Describe R first (Fig 9 :21 to :22): arity two, symmetry broken because the distribution residual is indexed by slot, scale local to economy and back to slot, anchor an instrument that reads both components and does not move when a frame pushes on it. Option A treats the question as one lever and reports success when aggregate output rises. Option B treats it as a union compound and requires both components to close against an anchor. The figures select B.

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not name the institutions that close both conditions. It says the question is a union compound: adaptation and non-concentration must both close, and a lever that raises output while concentrating the gain leaves the second condition binding. Its distribution half must be read per slot, because an aggregate is how a live signal disappears into an average. The measurement channel, not the ground, is the vulnerable quantity: it can pool readings, saturate at the top, or be captured by someone who benefits from the verdict, so independence and maintenance are institutional conditions. Capability belongs to the economy and its habitat rather than to firms in isolation; contact cascades beyond first order, so the work is permanent; adaptation needs differing rooms and selection between them, and contact with differing closures is the only operator that expands what is reachable. Finally, institutions are bonded arrangements rather than ingredient lists, so two economies with the same formal rules can be different substances, and the first condition is a term and an instrument for the objective, not a spending programme. What is offered is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and an open slot until an instrument settles it. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself.
