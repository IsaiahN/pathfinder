## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, *"two conditions whose intersection is a different case"* is a shape. *"Invest in lifelong learning and strengthen competition policy"* is a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point sharply with `SPACE(???)TIME`: *"we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered."* The question mark is the shape. Replacing it with a chosen structure is an act by the practitioner, not an output of the framework. The essay says so: *"the practitioner has built a library of embedded understanding of their domain of expertise"*, so *"the content result is theirs, not the framework's."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The same essay names the failure this question invites: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* That is F1 in `01`, arrived at independently in the essays.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* And the paradox: *"you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author."* Here that means the shape author is the person who supplies the per-slot instrument and the technology-specific measurements, not this folder.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :48 to :52).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this. *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question: naming education policy, competition policy, tax design, or flexicurity as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one. The Flexicurity source in `04` is a real, fetchable binding from the policy frame, but it is a binding, not a derivation.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or *"a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered."* The essay's conclusion: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information,"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the analogous error is: read an aggregate such as GDP growth, then name its source as "successful institutions." Several institutional arrangements can produce the same aggregate; several distributions can hide behind the same mean. What is owed is a distributional instrument with top-end resolution (Our World in Data calls the gap the "missing rich" problem), not a name chosen from the aggregate. Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."*

## 5. Q: do the essays forbid naming institutions? ANSWERED, with a correction

**What is the shape of the institutional answer?** The shape is: a union compound whose distribution half is slot-indexed; a maintainable, uncaptured measurement channel; a habitat view in which capability belongs to the economy and its habitat; cascade-aware and permanently maintained arrangements; differing rooms with selection between them; contact with differing closures; and arrangement over ingredients.

**Does the framework supply the list?** No. *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). The specific list is a binding from the economics frame, `specified`, unmeasured.

### Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially read *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding any naming in the answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Named institutions are not new primitives; they are bindings from an outside frame onto the shape. What remains wrong in such a naming is claiming it as a figure derivation, or claiming it moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not retracted**. The error there is naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The same essay: *"The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious endorsements for the claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the operational version: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different,"* and the group's *"agreement is never the verdict."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the demo caveat: because the training library is a black box, *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the institutional claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. That is the honest stop, and it is the reason this folder ends with a refuter rather than a conclusion.
