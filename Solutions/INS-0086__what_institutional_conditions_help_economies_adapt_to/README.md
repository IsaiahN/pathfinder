## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No list of named institutions asserted as the figure-derived answer. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list ranked for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier who holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Filters are for the budget, witnesses for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue's prestige is a contact cost, not evidence.

Legally: public opt-in submission channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM class of failure that personal-email harvesting creates. Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the parent session's to do.
