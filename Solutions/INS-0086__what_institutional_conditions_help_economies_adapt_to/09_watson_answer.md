## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: which institutional conditions help economies adapt to technological change without concentrating its gains. Option A treats it as one lever and reports success when aggregate output rises. Option B treats it as a union compound and requires both components to close against an anchor. What each leads to: A produces a metric that hides the concentration the question asks about and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it. The figures select B. The sought-for shape is made an ordinary term before the run: conditions C under which `R_adapt` trends down and the per-slot residual of `R_dist` does not concentrate in one slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a technology could deliver and what the economy absorbs and broadly distributes. Arity: two conditions (`R_adapt`, `R_dist`). Symmetry: broken, because `R_dist` is indexed by slot and summing over slots destroys the signal. Scale: a technology arrives at a local slot and composes upward into an economy, and the gain is read back down at the slot. Fig 9 :54 to :58 governs it as a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, production and adoption.** Actors: firms, workers, technologies, capital. Currency: capability, whether the technique gets used and prediction matches output. Anchor: measured adoption and productivity, a real instrument. Question type: maintained habitat, partly unmaintained market.
- **Domain 2, distribution and measurement.** Actors: households, groups, slots, statistical agencies. Currency: per-slot gain, residual indexed by slot. Anchor: a distributional instrument that neither pools nor saturates. Question type: the channel is the vulnerable quantity.
- **Domain 3, institutions as habitat maintainer.** Actors: rule-makers, regulators, courts, social partners. Currency: rules, instruments, head starts. Anchor: the ground, which must remain reachable. Question type: seat, acting across Domains 1 and 2.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: researchers, models, expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking frame. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Domains 1 and 2 form a union compound. Their overlap (a technology that raises output while concentrating the gain) is a specific compound, not a harder case of either. Domain 3 is the seat acting across both; Domain 4 checks. The union must be unbundled before it is searched again.
- **Order by dependency.** Domain 2's per-slot instrument comes first, because without it the objective cannot be stated. Then Domain 1's terms for what a technology affords. Then the objective itself (both conditions). Then Domain 3's rules and planning. Then Domain 4's verification. A rule that arrives before the measurement that would test it lands above the break and cannot reach the step that needs it.

Strain test: an early draft mapped technology onto Gradient and institutions onto Network among the six substrate terms of Fig 13. Re-census: those are bindings from the economics frame onto held atoms, not a derivation and not an import that adds an atom. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the named institutional list does not. The question is a union compound, its distribution half must be read per slot, the measurement channel is the vulnerable quantity, capability belongs to the economy and its habitat, adaptation requires differing rooms and selection between them, contact with differing closures is the only operator that expands what is reachable, and arrangement carries what an ingredient list omits. The first institutional condition is a term and an instrument for the objective, not a spending programme. What is refused is any claim to a grounded, named list: that list is a binding from the economics frame, specified, unmeasured, and owed a refuter. Fig 9 :32 governs the limit: the framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. This verdict is `candidate`, not `grounded`. Per-domain verdicts: Domain 1 to 2 triangulated; Domain 2 triangulated; Domain 3 triangulated for the figure claim and specified for its economic application; Domain 1 to 3 single-source for the figure definitions; Domain 4 triangulated; the named list is a gap.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and withholding the sources until the verdict is recorded.
- **undoable:** yes. No named institution is asserted, so no economic claim has to be retracted. The withdrawn Gradient and Network mapping is recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `11_audit.md` as specified rather than erased. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and the refuters are already written. What cannot be undone is a reader who has absorbed the theory before being asked for a verdict, which is why sources come last.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that reads the per-slot gain distribution with top-resolution on a named technology and shows both components closing against an anchor would move a row toward grounded. A demonstration that the two conditions are separable, that slot indexing adds no information over the aggregate, that a channel cannot degrade or be captured, or an agent-only improvement that moves the ground for an economy would refute the corresponding finding and reverse the verdict. A figure re-census that makes the union reading contradict the per-slot reading would send the derivation back to the census, never flip it by vote.
