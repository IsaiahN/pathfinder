## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks for institutional conditions such that two things hold at once:

- **A.** An economy absorbs a technological change: the new technique is used, prediction of its effects matches observed outcomes, and the composition paths it opens are reachable.
- **B.** The gains from that change are not captured by one slot: no single group or factor takes the residual that the change releases.

Restated as a residual R: the gap between what a technology could deliver and what the economy actually absorbs and broadly distributes. R has two live components, `R_adapt` and `R_dist`.

Describe R before choosing any figure line (Fig 9 :26 to :31):

- **Arity:** two conditions. Not one hard question.
- **Symmetry:** broken. `R_dist` is indexed by slot; summing over slots destroys the signal (Fig 1 :19, :22). There is no symmetric aggregation that preserves it.
- **Scale:** a technology arrives at a local slot (a firm, a task, a region) and composes upward into an economy; the gain is read back down at the slot. The two conditions bind at different scales, which is why the union needs a cross-scale channel (Fig 4).
- **Anchor needed:** an instrument that reads both components and does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29, :33).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run:** a set of conditions C such that, under C, `R_adapt` trends down and the per-slot residual of `R_dist` does not concentrate in one slot. This binding is written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else (formula, "Direction"). It is not itself a figure line.

**Options in effect:** option A treats the question as a single lever ("more skills", "more competition") and reports success when aggregate output rises. Option B treats it as a union compound and requires both components to close against an anchor. A leads to a metric that hides the very thing asked about. B leads to a shape with a stated refuter and an open name.

Then the objective itself (both conditions). Then D3's rules and planning. Then D4's verification. A rule that arrives before the measurement that would test it lands above the break and cannot reach the step that needs it (Fig 3 :20 to :23).

**No domain is admitted on a name alone.** D1's anchor is a measure, not the word "productivity". D2's anchor is an instrument, not the word "inequality". ## 3. Findings

### F1  -  The question is a union compound, and "without concentrating" is a second binding condition (mode: `general`)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
Fig 9 :55 to :56: **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
Fig 9 :57 to :58: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

"Adapt to technological change without concentrating its gains" is not adaptation with a soft qualifier. It is two conditions whose intersection is a different case. Institutions that raise adoption while allowing incumbents to capture the gain (stronger exclusivity, capital-biased subsidies, deregulation that raises the return to the already powerful) clear the first condition and leave the second binding. That is why a single-lever answer repeatedly looks successful and fails the question.

**Refuter:** show, over a stated institutional set, that the two conditions are separable, so that one lever closes both and the intersection is empty. If the intersection is empty, the union reading is wrong and this finding is withdrawn.

### F2  -  "Gains" must be read per slot; an aggregate hides the concentration (mode: `general`; `specified` when applied to a named metric)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 1 :28: **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."**

Symbols table on R: **"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."**

"Did the economy adapt?" answered by aggregate growth cannot register whether the gain concentrated. The live signal is per slot. An institutional condition that follows from this is a measurement condition: the gains must be read at the slot level with an instrument that can resolve the top of the distribution. This is **triangulated** across Fig 1 (the agent's per-slot loop) and the Symbols table (the definition of R). The two have low overlap: one is a loop diagram, the other a symbol definition. The world source from Our World in Data (2026) corroborates the same shape from a different field: survey data understate top concentration, so an instrument can fail to see the very thing asked about.

**Refuter:** exhibit a case in which the per-slot gain distribution is a deterministic function of the aggregate, so slot indexing buys no information. Then the aggregate is sufficient and this finding is withdrawn.

### F3  -  The channel, not the ground, is what institutions must keep reachable (mode: `general`)

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :25 to :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 10 :27 to :28: **"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"**
Fig 10 :29: **"→ keep the anchor reachable"**
Fig 10 :93: **"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."**

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."**

Institutions are the channel between an economy and its ground. A statistical system that pools readings or saturates at the top loses the signal of F2; a measurement body whose reputation depends on a favourable verdict is no longer an anchor. This makes independence and maintenance, not the mere existence of data, the institutional condition. **Triangulated** across Fig 10 (channel decay and disqualification) and Fig 2 (anchor must not update), two figures with different subject matter. The Our World in Data page corroborates that top incomes are undercounted by household surveys and that methodology changes create breaks in comparability, which is a channel failure in the figure's sense.

**Refuter:** exhibit a channel whose readings cannot degrade, cannot be pooled, cannot saturate, and cannot be captured. Then no maintenance condition is owed and this finding is withdrawn. Equivalently, show that the economic ground itself decays, which contradicts Fig 10 :24.

### F4  -  Capability is a property of the economy and its habitat, not of firms in isolation (mode: `general`; the specific institutions are `specified`)

Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

This is the direct answer to the question's form. "Which institutions help" is asked as if there were a standing list of good institutions. The figure refuses that form: capability belongs to the agent-and-habitat, and valence is relative to the residual. What is owed is an enumeration of the habitat in contact with *this* residual (the technology and its distribution), outward until the cascade stops, not a recipe. The Lundvall (2022) entry on national innovation systems corroborates from innovation studies: innovation *"is not the outcome of a single entity"* and the system is *"social and dynamic"*. The Resilience Alliance (after Holling, Gunderson, Walker, Biggs) corroborates from ecology that resilience is *"a property of these linked social-ecological systems"*. **Triangulated** across Fig 11 and two low-overlap fields.

**Refuter:** Fig 11 :72 states the falsifier itself: *"an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."* Produce such an improvement for an economy and this finding is withdrawn.

### F5  -  Contact cascades, so distributional effects are second and third order, and management is permanent work (mode: `general`)

Fig 11 :52 to :53: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**
Fig 11 :61 to :62: **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."**
Fig 11 :63: **"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."**

A technology's effect on the distribution of gains is rarely first order. It reaches the tax base, then the capacity to fund the channel of F3, then the political coalition that can change the rules. Scoping to direct contact is how the residual that matters is missed. And because exclusion is not available, an institution is not a solved design; it is continuous work against a habitat that keeps changing. World corroboration: Why Nations Fail (Acemoglu and Robinson, 2012) describes a self-reinforcing loop in which extractive institutions concentrate gains and thereby protect themselves against the creative destruction that would redistribute them.

**Refuter:** show a technology whose distributional effects are all first order and whose institutional habitat requires no ongoing maintenance to keep both conditions closed. Then the cascade and permanence claims are withdrawn.

### F6  -  Adaptation needs variation and selection between rooms, not one policy handed along (mode: `general`)

Fig 7 :12 to :13: **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**
Fig 7 :42: **"The next room you book is the direction you travel, and booking a room like the last one is how a chain stops travelling at all."**

An economy that applies one policy uniformly and hands it along a chain of agencies gets motion without progress. The shape is: many rooms whose closures differ, low overlap between them, and selection of the next room against the residual the last one surfaced. The Resilience Alliance's seven principles include *"promoting polycentric governance systems"* and *"maintaining diversity and redundancy"*, which corroborate this from ecology. **Triangulated** across Fig 7 and the resilience source (low overlap).

**Refuter:** exhibit a single uniform institution that sustains both conditions over a long horizon without any selection between differing rooms. Then the room claim is withdrawn.

### F7  -  Openness moves the wall, but a borrowed recipe is debited against independence (mode: `general`)

Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"** (import)
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
Fig 6 :24: **"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."**
Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**
Fig 8 :48 to :49: **"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."**

An economy that learns only from its own firms stays inside its closure and cannot compose a response the closure lacks. Contact with different closures (trade, migration, cross-border research, imported instruments) is the only operator that expands what is reachable. But copying a live peer's exact arrangement debits independence, so the same fact that gives the surplus also consumes the witness. The Lundvall (2022) abstract corroborates: a central policy issue is *"how governments should intervene in relation to domestic innovation processes and regulate the openness of the innovation system"*, and *"global challenges cannot be effectively tackled without transnational co-operation in production, science and technology."*

**Refuter:** exhibit an economy that sustained both conditions with zero contact with a differing closure (a genuine autarky case, not a short autarky episode). Then the import claim is withdrawn.

### F8  -  Same formal rules, different arrangement: institutions are isomers (mode: `general`)

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**
Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**

This is why the question cannot be answered by listing ingredients (property rights, education, competition policy, social protection). The arrangement carries the information the list omits. Two economies with the same formal rules can be different substances. And a vocabulary of ingredients can name a state (a rich economy) but cannot name the route to one. The specific bond structure is `specified` and unmeasured here; the claim that arrangement matters is `general`.

**Refuter:** exhibit two economies with identical formal ingredients and identical outcomes under every arrangement, so the bond carries nothing. Then the isomer claim is withdrawn and the ingredient list is sufficient.

### F9  -  The usual break is the middle link: terms for what a technology affords and what counts as broad gain (mode: `general`)

Fig 3 :24: **"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."**
Fig 3 :25 to :26: **"Perception has obvious failures and obvious tests. Planning has obvious failures and obvious tests. The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
Fig 3 :29 to :30: **"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."**

Most adaptation policy lands above the break, where measurements exist: skills, R&D, infrastructure. Those improve links that already work. The link that lacks an instrument is the one that states what a technology affords to whom and what would count as a broadly shared gain. By F2 and F3, that is exactly the per-slot reading. So the first institutional condition is not a spending programme; it is a term and an instrument for the objective. **Triangulated** with Fig 1 (per-slot signal) and the OWID measurement page (the "missing rich" blind spot), which have low overlap with Fig 3's dependency chain.

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where the binding constraint is demonstrably planning rather than vocabulary and objective, and where that constraint has a working instrument. Then the middle-link priority is withdrawn.

### F10  -  Prestige and agreement cannot settle this; the ground does (mode: `general`)

Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**
Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."**
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

A list of "successful countries" ranked by outcomes is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what is true. A campaign that collects prestigious endorsements manufactures the disqualifying correlation. The legitimate ask is a refutation against an instrument. This finding governs the outreach files, not the economics.

**Refuter:** exhibit an institutional question settled correctly by expert agreement alone, with no ground reading and no independent instrument. Then this finding is withdrawn for that class of question.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Over a stated institutional set, the adaptation and non-concentration conditions are separable, so one lever clears both and the intersection is empty (refutes F1).
2. A per-slot gain distribution is a deterministic function of the aggregate, so slot indexing adds no information (refutes F2).
3. A channel exists whose readings cannot degrade, pool, saturate, or be captured (refutes F3).
4. An agent-only improvement moves the ground for an economy (refutes F4; the falsifier is stated in Fig 11 :72).
5. All distributional effects of a technology are first order and the habitat needs no maintenance (refutes F5).
6. A single uniform institution sustains both conditions over a long horizon with no selection between differing rooms (refutes F6).
7. An economy sustains both conditions with zero contact with a differing closure (refutes F7).
8. Two economies with identical formal ingredients have identical outcomes under every arrangement (refutes F8).
9. The binding constraint is demonstrably planning, with a working instrument, in a case where vocabulary and objective are already in place (refutes F9).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until an instrument actually closes both components, no row in F11 may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1 and F2: the question is a compound, and its distribution half needs a per-slot instrument. These are the two findings most likely to change how the question is asked.
2. State the mode on every claim (F10 and the mode rule). Do not present a `specified` shape as a settled list.
3. Do not answer with a list of named institutions. Answer with the shape an answer must have, and record named institutions as bindings from the economics frame with provenance.
4. Put the measurement institution ahead of the spending programme (F9). Optimising skills policy cannot rescue a missing distributional instrument.
5. Offer the generator, not the conclusion. Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** The first contact is the residual and its refuter.
6. Send to public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask for a refutation, never an endorsement.

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives. The question must be split into a union compound; its distribution half must be read per slot; the measurement channel is the vulnerable quantity; capability belongs to the economy-and-habitat; adaptation requires differing rooms and selection between them, and contact with differing closures; and arrangement carries what an ingredient list omits. What does not survive is any claim to a named, grounded list of institutions. That list is a binding from the economics frame, `specified`, unmeasured, and owed a refuter. Strains after re-census: none hidden. The one standing limit is the shape-not-a-name boundary, which is recorded as a limit rather than as a gap in the figures.
