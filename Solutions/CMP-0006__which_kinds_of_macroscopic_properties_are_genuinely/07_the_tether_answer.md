# The Tether's answer: which macroscopic properties are genuinely irreducible

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal standing list. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for property kinds that are "genuinely irreducible" to components. It is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition into irreducible and reducible boxes (Fig 9 :54-58). Four conditions have to be applied per slot per level: the level index, the bond-carried content, the whole-not-parts test, and the cross-level stability test. One lever clears only the region where a single condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape

1. **Irreducibility is a fact about a level, never about the world.** Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 12 :104: *"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. Fig 4 :33 gives no top. So no property is *genuinely* irreducible without a level index. The word "genuinely" is bound as an ordinary term and is refusable by the same test as everything else.

2. **What is irreducible at a level is the arrangement, not the inventory.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

3. **The kinds are arrangements, not physical quantities.** Fig 12 :117-123 names four: isomer (*"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*), catalyst (*"A part that survives its own use"*), cycle (*"The output re-enters as input"*), and functional group (*"A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome"*). These are what a flat component list cannot write. In the condensed matter frame, states with the same symmetry and different long-range entanglement are the isomer case read at a physical level (world corroboration, not a derivation).

4. **The discriminator against aggregates is the whole-not-parts test.** Fig 13 :47: *"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."* Sums and counts pass through to components. Gradients, networks and directed orderings do not. The test is per slot: Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

5. **Turn-leftovers are not new primitive kinds.** Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* They can be irreducible to an ingredient list in the route sense (Fig 12 :111, *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one"*) while still not being members of the set.

6. **The context-free standing list is a `gap`.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The framework gives the criterion a member of the list must meet, not the member.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a standing list of physical properties that are irreducible at every level. That list is a declared `gap`.
- Not that "emergence" or "irreducibility" is a seventh primitive. Fig 13 :43-44 removes a candidate seventh for failing the generating-set rule.
- Not that a property is irreducible everywhere because it is surprising, novel or hard to predict. Surprise is frame-internal; Fig 1 :24-27 disqualifies self-scores.
- Not that the world sources settle the claim. They corroborate the shape from one encyclopedia evidence pool; the verdict comes from figures.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement distinguishes an arrangement-carried property from a component-inventory property was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried in `05`: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Level test:** at a stated level L, can the property be promoted to an atom for a composition at L+1? If yes, its irreducibility is indexed to L and the absolute claim fails (Fig 12 :104).
- **Bond test:** does the property change when the typed bond changes while the ingredient multiset is held fixed? If not, it is a component inventory, not an irreducible arrangement (Fig 12 :95-96).
- **Whole test:** does the whole carry a property neither part has, and is the bond type decisive for it (Fig 13 :47)?
- **Standing-list test:** does any proposed property keep its verdict across a change of level and a change of residual? If not, the list is refused (Fig 11 :51).
- **Instrument owed:** a measurement that discriminates same-ingredient different-arrangement at a fixed level, in the sense of the topological-order case where symmetry was insufficient. Until such a discriminator runs, bindings remain `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the separator, unreached for the list.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (carried in `05`); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a list of properties that are irreducible everywhere. It says that irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world, so every "irreducible" verdict needs a level index. At a fixed level, what resists reduction is the typed arrangement, not the ingredient list: the four kinds a flat list cannot write are isomer, catalyst, cycle and functional group. Smoothness, symmetry and chaos are what a composition leaves behind, not new primitive properties. The discriminator against mere aggregates is whether the whole carries a property neither part has. A context-free standing list is a gap, reported as a verdict rather than silently filled. What is offered is the separator, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would settle a particular binding. The list stays open until the ground settles it.
