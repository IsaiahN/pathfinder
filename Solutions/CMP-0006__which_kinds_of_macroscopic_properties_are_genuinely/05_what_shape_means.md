## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, carried from the exemplar: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its member. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0006 the shape is:

> A property is irreducible at a level when its content is carried by the typed arrangement rather than the component inventory, when the whole carries something neither part has, and when the verdict is indexed to that level. A context-free list of such properties is not available.

`ARRANGEMENT(???)` is the analogue of the exemplar's `SPACE(???)TIME`: a slot with a criterion and no occupant. Naming topological order as *the* irreducible property is the analogue of naming Gradient as *the* intermediate structure: a binding, not a derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, carried: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why section 0 of `01` starts from the effect of the two readings rather than from the word "emergence."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, carried: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly what the outreach email asks for: what would you expect to see if the separator were true?
- *Library of Babel* on stopping, carried: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the finishing shape (a level-indexed separator) is also the starting description of the residual.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For CMP-0006 the discriminating instrument is a measurement that distinguishes an arrangement-carried property from a component-inventory property at a stated level.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A condensed-matter result imported into the framework must say where it came from and which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books*, carried: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Declaring a seventh member, such as "emergence" or "irreducibility" as a primitive property kind, turns a visible choice into an invisible one. That is the error named at Fig 13 :43-44.

## 4. The source-discriminator problem applies here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Apply to CMP-0006: two systems with different micro-components can show the same critical exponent (Wikipedia Critical phenomena, opened this session). The exponent reading *"cannot comment on the source."* Naming the source, for example "this exponent is the irreducible property of the material," is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator at the level, not a name. The world example corroborates the framework's Fig 13 :121, *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance,"* but the verdict comes from the figure.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, carried: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q answered: what would license calling a given property irreducible?

Nothing in the framework licenses a level-free name. A particular property is licensed only by:

- the ground settling a minted term at a stated level, or
- a provenanced import with a shadow test from a frame whose closure differs.

Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. Topological ground-state degeneracy, critical exponents and order parameters are all **candidate bindings** onto the arrangement slot. Their status in this folder is `specified`, candidate, never `grounded`.

**Does `01` commit the error the README forbids?** No, after the correction in section 5b. `01` does not name an occupant for the slot; it names the *kinds of arrangement* (Fig 12 :117-123) and reports the physical properties only as world-side corroboration with overlap declared.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading (kept visible)

An earlier reading treated the phrase *"shape, not a name"* as a ban on naming any property at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in the library. Isomer, catalyst, cycle and functional group are **already in the figure set** (Fig 12 :116-123); naming them is selecting held structure, not inventing a seventh. What remains forbidden is (a) converting a turn-leftover such as smoothness into a new primitive (Fig 13 :43-44) and (b) claiming that a binding from the physics frame moves the wall (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; such a binding adds none). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

The same narrowing was made in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and `06` Q1. It is a correction to the *reading of the rule*, not a retraction of the rule.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, carried: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous emergentists assembled to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let a ground settle it (Fig 2 :29).

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, carried: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
