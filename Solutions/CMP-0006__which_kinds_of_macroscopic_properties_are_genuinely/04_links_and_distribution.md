## Wider literature that bears on the question, read as above

1. **Lewes 1875, via Wikipedia Emergence.** The original discriminator between a resultant (sum or difference of co-operant forces, traceable in components) and an emergent (co-operation of things of unlike kinds, *"cannot be reduced to their sum or their difference"*). This is the pre-history of the whole-not-parts test at Fig 13 :47. Field: philosophy of science. Low overlap with the framework.
2. **Anderson 1972, More is Different, via the pages above.** The canonical condensed-matter statement that constructionism breaks down at scale and complexity. Predicts level-relative new properties. Corroborates Fig 12 :104.
3. **Carroll and Parola taxonomy, via Wikipedia Emergence.** Type 0 featureless, type 1 direct and incompressible, type 2 nonlocal, type 3 augmented (genuinely novel macro variables). This is the closest world-side answer to "which kinds," and it is a taxonomy of *relation of macro to micro*, not a standing list of physical quantities. Corroborates the separator and the gap.
4. **Kivelson and Kivelson, via Wikipedia Emergence.** *"An emergent behavior of a physical system is a qualitative property that can only occur in the limit that the number of microscopic constituents tends to infinity."* Corroborates the level index; note the strain below.
5. **Topological order, Wikipedia.** Same symmetry, different long-range entanglement; new quantum numbers needed because symmetry was insufficient. Corroborates Fig 12 :95-96 (same ingredients, different substance, difference carried by the arrangement).
6. **Critical phenomena, Wikipedia.** Critical exponents shared across very different systems, explained by renormalization group. Corroborates Fig 13 :121 (different routes to the same substance) and is a **filter** for which properties to study, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
7. **Bedau 1997 and Chalmers 2007, via Wikipedia Emergence and Downward causation.** Weak emergence is simulable after the fact; strong emergence is not deducible even in principle. The framework agrees with the level-relativity and refuses the absolute, so this is partial corroboration with a stated limit.
8. **Crutchfield, via Wikipedia Emergence.** Structure detection depends on the observer's computational resources: an observer's notion of what is ordered *"depends directly on its computational resources."* Corroborates the caution that "surprise" is frame-internal (Fig 1 :24-27), not an anchor.

## Overlap declaration

All five Wikipedia pages are one encyclopedia evidence pool. Their agreement with each other is not independent corroboration. They are opened because they are public, checkable and span philosophy, physics and cognition, but they are secondary to the figures. The framework essays are not used as world witnesses in this question; where they are read for the canonical meaning of "shape" in `05`, they share the framework author's pool completely.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52**  -  *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the separator and its refuter. Send the framework only after the reader has recorded a verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42**  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the interior before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**  -  *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would expect to see if the separator were true before offering any article.
- **Fig 2 :35**  -  *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not select venues for likely agreement.
- **Fig 4 :22**  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted; it does not send an answer.
- **Fig 2 :40**  -  *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A later agreement with the framework is never pooled as corroboration.

## The two-stage offer in practice

1. Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` only, to one public venue at a time.
2. Ask: which of the three refuters would you expect to fire, and what would you count as a failure?
3. Record the answer before sending any link.
4. If the reader opts in, send the three public links, noting that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
5. If they ask about the method, offer the formula with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.
