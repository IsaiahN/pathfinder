## Residual in effect terms

A reader asks for a list of property kinds that are "genuinely irreducible" to individual components. Option A: return a standing list of property kinds (phase, order parameter, critical exponent, topological order, symmetry) that are irreducible in principle. Option B: return a level-relative separator and report the standing list as a `gap`. A leads to an absolute that the figures refuse and to a list that cannot survive a change of level. B leads to a checkable criterion, per-domain verdicts that are never averaged, and an honest stop. The word "genuinely" is the load-bearing term: it asks for an absolute where the framework only returns a level-relative fact (Fig 12 :104).

## Honest standing of the derivation

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** By the framework's own rule, LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, as carried in the completed exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md`, which was read this session; the essay itself was not re-opened at source). It cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken and no experiment was run.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons from the figures: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict"* (Fig 7 :35); *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40); and *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42). The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares the primary evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. That is stated rather than hidden.

## Key result

Irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world (Fig 12 :104). What is irreducible at a level is the information carried by the typed arrangement, not the component list: isomer, catalyst, cycle and functional group (Fig 12 :117-123), plus the derived quantities smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness that a turn leaves behind rather than members of the set (Fig 13 :124-125). The aggregate/whole test is Fig 13 :47. A context-free standing list is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134), reported as a verdict, not as silence. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
