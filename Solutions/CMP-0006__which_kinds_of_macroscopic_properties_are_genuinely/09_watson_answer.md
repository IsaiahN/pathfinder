## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request for property kinds that are "genuinely irreducible" to components. Option A: return a standing list of physical property kinds that are irreducible in every system and at every level. Option B: return a level-indexed separator, keep verdicts per domain, and report the standing list as a `gap`. What each leads to: A delivers an absolute with no level index, converts turn-leftovers into primitive kinds, and cannot survive a change of residual; B delivers a checkable criterion, a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop at the instrument limit.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The reader's request for a level-free list, driven by the qualifier "genuinely." Arity: two place (whole to components) plus an unbound qualifier, so a union signal. Symmetry: "irreducible to" is ordered, not a symmetric join; the direction of dependence is part of the question. Scale: explicitly cross-scale, macro versus micro, so the membrane and the level rule govern. Fig 9 :22 names the descriptors: *"arity · symmetry · scale"*.
- **Domain 1, framework composition and levels.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds plus negation, atoms, molecules, levels, `closure(Γ)`. Currency: composition inside the closure then settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws plus the ground for a measurement. Question type: partition boundary (what composes across the two kinds). Dependency position: first, because a level must be fixed before irreducibility can be judged.
- **Domain 2, bond-carried arrangement.** Actors: ingredients, typed bonds, molecules, the four arrangement structures. Currency: description length in bits and the bond information. Anchor: the six bond tests and the minimum description length bargain. Question type: identity of arranged things over a fixed ingredient set. Dependency position: after D1.
- **Domain 3, whole versus aggregate.** Actors: parts, whole, their relation. Currency: whether the whole carries a property neither part has. Anchor: the gradient argument, a test that can fail. Question type: partition of candidate properties at a fixed level, using Fig 13 :47.
- **Domain 4, condensed matter and emergence frame.** Actors: spins, electrons, atoms, order parameters, critical exponents, topological invariants, phases, coarse-graining maps. Currency: physical prediction and measurement. Anchor: experiment, an instrument that does not update on the theory. Question type: the world frame where the question is normally asked and where bindings are proposed.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.

**Union rather than partition.** The four conditions (level index, bond-carried, whole-not-parts, cross-residual stability) are overlapping, not mutually exclusive. A total particle count fails all four; a critical exponent passes three; an order parameter passes two; topological ground-state degeneracy passes the first three but its cross-residual standing is a question for an instrument. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

**Order by dependency.** Refuse the absolute reading before minting a list (Formula step 2); fix the level before judging irreducibility (D1); settle the arrangement before promoting it to an atom (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :47 (whole versus aggregate) and Fig 12 :117 (same atoms, different bond) govern different objects, so there is no strain after re-census. The world claim of in-principle irreducibility is returned to the census rather than used to flip the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world (Fig 12 :104), so every irreducibility verdict needs a level index. At a fixed level, the property kinds that resist reduction to components are the arrangement kinds the flat component list cannot write: isomer, catalyst, cycle and functional group (Fig 12 :117-123), with the discriminator at Fig 13 :47 and the turn-leftovers at Fig 13 :124-125. A context-free standing list of physical properties is a `gap`, reported as a verdict and not silently filled (Fig 11 :51, Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). The world frame corroborates the shape from one encyclopedia pool; the first three figure domains are `specified` and triangulated, the physical bindings are `single-source` and `candidate`, and nothing is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is telling a reader that irreducibility is absolute when it is level-relative, or handing them a standing list when the framework refuses one. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the level index and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The physical bindings are removable without touching the separator; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already keep them separate. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` stays open without a fabricated member.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator that separates an arrangement-carried property from a component-inventory property at a stated level would ground a binding; a property shown irreducible at every level, a terminal level, a seventh primitive, or a context-free list surviving a change of residual would break the separator; an instrument showing that the bond type makes no difference in a named physical case would refute that binding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
