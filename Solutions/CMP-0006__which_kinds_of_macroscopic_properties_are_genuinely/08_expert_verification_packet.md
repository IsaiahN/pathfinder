## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Irreducibility to components is not an absolute property of the world; it is a fact about a level (Fig 12 :104). At a fixed level, the content that resists reduction is carried by the typed arrangement, not by the component inventory: same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance, and the difference is decisive in the behaviour (Fig 12 :95-96). The four arrangement kinds a flat component list cannot write are isomer, catalyst, cycle and functional group (Fig 12 :117-123). Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are what a composition leaves behind, not new primitive kinds (Fig 13 :124-125). A context-free standing list of irreducible physical properties is a `gap`, reported rather than filled (Fig 11 :51, Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 4

- **Statement:** irreducibility is level-relative. There is no level at which a property is irreducible in every possible composition, and no top or bottom level.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :103 *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*. Fig 4 :33 *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*
- **What to check:** do the recursion and the no-top claim actually follow from the two passages, or is a level-absolute reading still open in the text?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a property whose irreducibility holds at every level, so it cannot become an atom for any composition over it; or identify a terminal level with a nameable anchor.

### C2  -  framework-internal and physics-facing; checkable against Fig 12 and the named physical cases

- **Statement:** the irreducible content is the typed bond, and the four arrangement kinds are isomer, catalyst, cycle and functional group. A physical example is a candidate binding onto a kind, not a derivation of it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :95-96 *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."* Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :119 *"A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121 *"The output re-enters as input."* Fig 12 :123 *"A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome."*
- **What to check:** is the physical case genuinely one of same ingredients with a different typed relation? In topological order, states with the same symmetry but different long-range entanglement are the isomer-like case; is that a faithful reading, or is the symmetry-based description sufficient after all? Does the world case add an atom to the framework, or only bind an arrangement already held?
- **Refuter:** show a macroscopic property fully determined by the component multiset under every admissible bond while the separator calls it irreducible; or show that the bond type makes no difference to the outcome in the named physical case; or show that the physical case requires a new primitive rather than a binding.

### C3  -  framework-internal discriminator and gap; checkable against Figures 13, 9 and 11

- **Statement:** aggregates are separated from whole-properties by Fig 13 :47, turn-leftovers are not new members, and the context-free standing list is a `gap` rather than silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :47 *"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."* Fig 13 :124-125 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
- **What to check:** does the whole-not-parts test actually separate a critical exponent from a particle count at a stated level? Does the refusal of the standing list follow from residual-relative valence, or could a level-indexed list survive?
- **Refuter:** produce a context-free list of irreducible physical property kinds that survives both a change of level and a change of residual, with each member shown to be bond-carried and whole-not-parts; or show that a turn-leftover passes the generating-set test at Fig 13 :41-44 and is a genuine seventh member.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument.

1. **Bond test:** at a stated level, does the property change when the typed bond changes and the ingredients are held fixed (Fig 12 :95-96)?
2. **Whole test:** does the whole carry a property neither part has, and is the bond type decisive (Fig 13 :47)?
3. **Level test:** can the property be promoted to an atom one level up, and does its irreducible verdict then become indexed (Fig 12 :104)?
4. **Cross-residual test:** does any candidate keep its verdict after the residual changes (Fig 11 :51)?
