## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about two requests: which process produced the Moon, and what the Moon's composition legitimately carries about the early Earth. Option A: read the composition as a direct sample of early Earth and assert that the figures derive a specific formation event. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, treat composition as a lossy reading, treat the early-Earth claim as a head start, and hold the variant as a `gap`. What each leads to: A delivers a name where the figures return a shape, commits the metamerism error of naming a source from a reading, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an instrument that would close the open slot.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the naive readings do not. The question is a union of two conditions with different anchors, so per-domain verdicts stay separate. The compositional reading fixes a shape: a high-energy, late, mantle-derived, volatile-depleted, small-iron-core event whose debris re-accretes. It does not name the variant, because several paths yield the same reading and a lossy reading cannot recover its source. The variants are isomers over a fixed atom set, and persistent isotope similarity selects a re-fit at the variant level rather than the collapse of the family. The Moon is a head start about early Earth, not a replay, so it cannot be read as a direct sample. The specific impactor, angle and date are a `gap` of the unreached-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle and not silence. Mode `specified` on D1 to D3, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The naive claims are removable without touching the derivation: `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` remains declared rather than filled with a guessed number.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. New samples from different terranes that discriminate among variants would name the mechanism; a chronometer result that ranks the models would close the timing slot; a compositional signature uniquely produced by one path would move D2 from shape to name; a variant requiring a new atom would break the isomer claim; a figure line governing an impact parameter would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
