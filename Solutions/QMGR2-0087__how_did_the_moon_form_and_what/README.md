## The question restated as an effect

Two things are being decided. First, a set of observed features of the Moon (Earth-like oxygen and titanium isotopes, volatile depletion, a small iron core, an anorthositic crust that implies a deep magma ocean, an anomalously high Earth-Moon angular momentum) is a *reading*. We want the *source* that produced it: capture, fission, co-accretion, or a giant impact, and if the last, which variant. Second, the Moon's composition is offered as a *record* of early Earth. We want to know what it legitimately carries about the pre-impact Earth, and what it does not.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a shape with (a) a constraint set that any candidate formation mechanism must reproduce, (b) a stated reason why the reading does or does not uniquely name the mechanism, (c) a stated reason why composition is a head start about early Earth rather than a replay of it, and (d) a discriminator that would move a slot from shape to name. That binding is written down here so it can accrue a residual like anything else, instead of remaining an invisible preference that no check can see (Fig 5 :65, :68).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors (Seat Map, Seat 4).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
