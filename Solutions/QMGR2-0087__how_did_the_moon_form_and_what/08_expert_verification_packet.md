# Expert verification packet: the Tether on lunar formation and early Earth

**What this is:** a claim in a form a lunar or planetary scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific variant and date, not `grounded`.** No formation measurement is claimed by this agent. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of two conditions with different anchors, not one question with one answer. The Moon's composition is a real, non-updating reading that constrains a shape: a high-energy, late, mantle-derived, volatile-depleted, small-iron-core event whose debris re-accretes. It does not name the variant, because several formation paths yield the same compositional reading and a lossy reading cannot recover its source (Fig 13 :129, Fig 9 :32). The candidate paths are isomers over a fixed atom set (Fig 12 :117). The Moon's composition is a head start about the pre-impact Earth, not a replay of it (Fig 4 :22), so it cannot be read as a direct sample of early Earth's bulk state. Competing paths are rooms and only ground selects between them (Fig 7 :35); new sample returns from different terranes are the discriminators. The specific impactor, angle and date are a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 9

- **Statement:** the compositional reading fixes a shape but cannot name the source. Nothing about a composed artefact tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :129 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."
- **World corroboration (secondary):** the four named giant-impact variants all reproduce the same Earth-Moon isotope similarity; the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on underdetermination states that conflicting hypotheses can fit the same phenomena.
- **Refuter:** a compositional signature shown to be uniquely produced by one path, with exclusivity demonstrated and the measurement made, moves the slot from shape to name. If no such signature can exist even in principle and no other ground is reachable, restate as unreachable rather than underdetermined.

### C2. Physics-facing; checkable by a lunar scientist against the constraint set

- **Statement:** the five constraint groups (small iron core, volatile depletion, anorthositic magma ocean, Earth-like O and Ti isotopes, high angular momentum) are real slots that any formation frame must reproduce. They are many-to-one with respect to the mechanism.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** are those five groups the right slots, and does the current favored frame reproduce all of them? Are the compositional difficulties recorded in the public literature (FeO intermediate between Mars and the terrestrial mantle; Ti isotopes within 4 ppm; volatile ratios unexplained by the simple model) genuine live residuals rather than settled details?
- **Refuter:** a sixth constraint group that no current frame reproduces and that splits the family, or a demonstration that two of the five are actually one slot so the census is wrong. Either sends the derivation back to the census; it does not flip the verdict by assertion.

### C3. Framework-internal derivation with a world-facing consequence

- **Statement:** the variants are isomers, and the early-Earth inference is second-order and head-start-only.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- **What to check:** do the equilibration, direct-collision, synestia and terrestrial-magma-ocean models use the same atom set with different arrangements, or does any require a new reservoir or object? Is the Moon's composition legitimately a head start about early Earth only under a model of impact and mixing, as the FeO and isotope difficulties imply?
- **Refuter:** a variant that requires an atom absent from the shared set kills the isomer claim for that variant (this is the standing test for the synestia's transient object). A demonstration that composition matches early Earth under incompatible early-Earth states with no discriminator kills the head-start reading.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a derivation of Theia, an angle, a date, or a mixing percentage. Those are world results with their own provenance, recorded under WORLD in `09`.
- Not "the giant impact is proven." It is favored in the world literature. Favorability is a budget filter, not a verdict witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the Moon is a direct sample of early Earth. That is the metamerism error this packet refuses.
- Not that the figures govern geochemical observables literally. Fig 13's cross-kind rule was re-censused into a shape analogue, not applied as a substrate law.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this agent.
- Not a claim that the specific variant is unreachable in principle. It is unreached at this budget; the ground is reachable with new samples.

## 5. What would settle it

1. New samples from the far side or poles, as NASA describes for Artemis, that discriminate among variants rather than reproduce the Apollo pool (Fig 7 :36).
2. Higher-precision isotope and volatile results that separate the equilibration, direct-collision, synestia and magma-ocean predictions. This is an action-cost item (Fig 12 :125), not another model description.
3. A chronometer result that fixes the timing of the last impact tightly enough to rank models.
4. The standing refuters in `01` section 5 and C1 to C3 above.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :129, Fig 9 :32, Fig 12 :117 and Fig 4 :22. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: are the five constraint groups the right slots, and are the listed difficulties still live?
3. Name one measurement on a future sample that would discriminate two named variants, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
