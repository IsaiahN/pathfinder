## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From The Library of Babel, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill.

For this question the shape is:

> A high-energy, late, mantle-derived, volatile-depleted, small-iron-core event whose debris re-accretes into a Moon, with substantial mixing between impactor and proto-Earth material, and with the specific variant, impactor identity, angle and date left open.

That is a shape. "Theia struck Earth at 45 degrees 4.5 billion years ago and contributed 20 percent of the debris" is a name, and it is a world claim, not a figure result.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- **Library of Babel**, opened this session: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The stopping condition: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."
- **Fig 6 :30**: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- **Decoding Tacit Knowledge** (carried mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Here contact *is* possible, so the appropriate ask is a measurement, not a plain description alone.
- **Library of Babel** on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the canonical warrant for splitting R1 from R2 in `01` section 1.

Applied here, the effect terms were: (a) a small iron core, (b) volatile depletion, (c) an anorthositic crust implying a deep magma ocean, (d) Earth-like O and Ti isotopes, (e) high angular momentum. Only after listing these did the audit name the giant-impact family. Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

## 3. Shape and source: the governing essay for this question

The Hard Problem is About Source, opened this session, is the closest essay to the lunar question. It reframes a hard problem as a measurement problem:

> "You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."
>
> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."
>
> "the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."

The Moon's composition is such a reading. It says what it measured. It cannot comment on the source. Several formation paths (equilibration, direct hit, synestia, terrestrial magma ocean) create the same compositional reading. The essay's resolution is instrumental, not argumentative: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." For lunar formation, the better instruments are the new sample returns NASA describes, and the source-discriminating measurements they enable.

Fig 13 :129 is the figure-level statement of the same thing: "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 12 :104 adds the recursion: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."

**Consequence for this question.** The answer to "how did the Moon form" cannot be a name read off the composition. It can only be a shape plus a stated instrument that would discriminate among routes. That is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` ends with an instrument rather than a verdict.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

They're Scanning Books (carried mapping) sharpens the first: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, the world's favored giant-impact frame is a provenanced import from the planetary-science frame, not a derivation from the six-term substrate. It is recorded as such. The source gate is the public literature; the shadow test is that it explains the five constraint groups; the debit against independence is that the planetary-science sources share an evidence pool.

## 5. Q2 answered: what would name the variant?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A specific variant is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The world literature supplies names (Theia, synestia, equilibration) and measurements (angles, dates, percentages). These are world facts with provenance, not figure outputs. `09_watson_answer.md` lists them under WORLD and keeps them out of TETHER.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The honest stop for "shape"

The framework can say: the answer must have the five constraint groups, must treat composition as a lossy reading, must keep the early-Earth inference downstream of the source question, and must name an instrument that would discriminate among routes. It cannot say which impactor, which angle, which date. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Indiscernibility is not identity. The lineage is the proof (Fig 13 :127, :130).
