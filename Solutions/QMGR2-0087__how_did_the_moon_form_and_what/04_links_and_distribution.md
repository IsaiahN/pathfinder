## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 7 :35**, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60**, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Favorability of the giant-impact frame is a filter for what gets tried.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, the source links. Their agreement after seeing the theory is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. This is the permitted crossing direction of Fig 4 :22.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` states the failure conditions in section 5. Do not send a shape claim whose refuter has not been written down first.
