# The Tether's answer on lunar formation and what composition tells us about early Earth

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific variant and date, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is a union of two conditions with different anchors, not one question with one answer (Fig 9 :54 to :57). R1 asks which process produced the observed Moon; its anchor is returned samples, remote spectra and laser ranging. R2 asks what the Moon's composition carries about the pre-impact Earth; its anchor is independent early-Earth constraints. They overlap in the impact event itself, but a single lever clears the intersection only. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22).

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, the reading (`specified`, substantially ground-supported at the reading level).** The compositional feature set is a real anchor: it does not update on what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). It fixes five constraints: a small iron core, volatile depletion, an anorthositic crust implying a deep magma ocean, Earth-like oxygen and titanium isotopes, and an anomalously high Earth-Moon angular momentum. These register per slot; a single "Moon-likeness" score would throw away the signal (Fig 1 :22).

**D2, the source (`specified` on the family, `gap` on the variant).** The figure-consistent shape is a high-energy, late, mantle-derived, volatile-depleted, small-iron-core event whose debris re-accretes. That is a shape, not the name of a variant. Fig 13 :129: "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Several paths yield the same compositional reading, so the reading does not by inspection select one (Fig 9 :32). The variants are isomers over a fixed atom set (Fig 12 :117), differing in arrangement rather than ingredients. The persistent isotope-similarity residual selects a re-fit at the variant level, not the abandonment of the family (Formula step 2, Fig 5 :50).

**D3, the early-Earth inference (`specified`, triangulated).** The Moon's composition is a head start about the pre-impact Earth, not a replay (Fig 4 :22). Earth's active geology erased its own formation record; the Moon preserved a slower one. But the Moon is a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :25) and a lossy transform, so it cannot be read as a direct sample of early Earth's bulk state. The FeO and titanium difficulties in the world literature independently block the direct reading. Inference about early Earth is therefore second-order, downstream of a model of the impact and mixing, licensed by Fig 12 :104's settled-molecule rule.

**D4, verification (`general` structural).** Competing formation paths are rooms, and only the ground selects between them (Fig 7 :35). Agreement among models trained on the same Apollo pool is one evidence pool talking, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). New samples from different terranes are genuinely different rooms and can move the verdict (Fig 7 :36).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a derivation of Theia, an impact angle, a date, or a percentage of proto-Earth material. Those are world measurements and model outputs, not figure results. Their spread in the literature (about 4.4 to 4.47 billion years; 20 percent of the impactor in a ring; 80 percent proto-Earth in one variant) is recorded under WORLD, never under TETHER.
- Not "the giant-impact hypothesis is proven." It is the favored world frame. Favorability is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not "the Moon is a sample of early Earth." That is the metamerism error the framework refuses.
- Not a derivation of one formation variant from another. Variants are isomers, not consequences.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this agent; the ground here is reachable but it is other people's instruments that reach it.
- Not a claim that the figures govern geochemical observables literally. Fig 13's cross-kind rule governs the six substrate terms; its use here is a shape analogue assigned by re-census (see `01` strain S4).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The hard-problem essay's spectrometer is the model: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source" until a better instrument arrives. For lunar formation the discriminators are:

1. **New samples from different terranes**, far side and polar regions, as NASA proposes for Artemis. If they reproduce the Apollo signature they are another mirror; if they discriminate among variants they are a new room (Fig 7 :36).
2. **Higher-precision isotope and volatile measurements** that separate the equilibration, direct-collision, synestia and magma-ocean predictions. This is an action-cost item (Fig 12 :125), not another description.
3. **A chronometer result** that fixes the time of the last impact relative to solar-system formation tightly enough to rank the models.
4. **Standing refuters from `01` section 5:** a uniquely discriminating compositional signature; two independent sample sets with non-shrinking disagreement; composition matching early Earth under incompatible states; variants requiring different atoms; a figure line governing an impact parameter.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The ground is reachable here, unlike the Planck-scale case in the exemplar: returned samples and ranging already exist. What is unreached is the specific variant from the figures. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Indiscernibility is not identity. The lineage is the proof (Fig 13 :130).

The six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (They're Scanning Books, carried mapping). Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. No seventh term was invented to force a formation answer.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the Moon's formation event, and it does not claim the favored world theory is proven. It says the question is two conditions with different anchors: which process produced the Moon, and what its composition carries about early Earth. The composition fixes a shape, a high-energy, late, mantle-derived, volatile-depleted, small-iron-core event whose debris re-accretes, but it does not pick out one variant, because several paths yield the same reading and a lossy reading cannot recover its source. The variants are isomers over the same atoms, arranged differently, and the isotope similarity is a persistent residual that selects a re-fit at the variant level, not the collapse of the family. The Moon is a head start about early Earth, not a replay of it, so it cannot be read as a direct sample. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the sample-return instruments that would close the open slot. The specific impactor, angle and date stay open until the ground settles them.
