## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No single universal merger percentage asserted from the figures.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
2. **An anchor must not move with the frames.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The ground here is imaging, kinematics and simulation, not consensus.
3. **Do not spend the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public, institutional and opt-in.
