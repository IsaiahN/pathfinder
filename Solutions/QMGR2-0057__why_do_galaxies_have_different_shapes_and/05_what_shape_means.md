## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local figures README states the limit with the framework's own wording: the framework gives "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (read 2026-11-23): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "Morphological types are different arrangements of a shared component set" is a shape.
- "An elliptical is the Spectrum and a spiral is the Gradient" is a name, and the framework does not license it.

The shape also has a stop condition. *The Library of Babel*: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For "how often," that stop is not reached inside the figures. It is reached only after an OUTWARD import, which is why the numeric answer is marked `gap` at the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read 2026-11-23): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

Applied here: describe R1 and R2 in effect terms (what would we see if types were arrangements? what would we see if the merger contribution were conditional?) before choosing any figure line. That is what section 1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` does.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here the ground is imaging, kinematics and merger trees, not agreement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

That is the precise situation for the merger question. A disturbed morphology is a reading; naming "merger" as its source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. Kormendy and Kennicutt (2004) supply the competing source (secular evolution), and Conselice (2014) reports that structural merger indicators catch only about half of real mergers because asymmetry lasts roughly a third of the merger lifetime. What is owed is a better discriminator (kinematics, merger trees, multiple indices), not a name asserted from morphology.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23): "The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback. To the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement. A board that pays or does not."

And: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

So a public list of people who already think mergers dominate cannot settle "how often." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are chosen for ground-visibility and public opt-in, never for likely agreement.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" mean for this question?** It means the form of an answer that is true before the astronomical content is supplied: morphological types are arrangements over a shared component set (isomers and functional groups), the arranging channels are enumerated from a habitat, and no channel has a fixed valence. That is the whole of what the figures give. They do not give the Hubble roster, they do not assign components to slots, and they do not give a merger frequency.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** Not after the correction in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 8. The earlier attempt to map the Hubble sequence onto space, time and energy was retracted as a strain (Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"). The corrected record keeps the shape and imports the astronomical content with provenance.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading

I initially treated Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one" as if it governed the Hubble classification directly. That was a strain. ## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that mergers dominate is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried: send the shape and its refuter to ground-visible people and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**What would move it from candidate to grounded:** a measurement that discriminates the source of a morphology (kinematic or merger-tree evidence), or a derivation from the figures of a frequency. Neither is present in this folder.
