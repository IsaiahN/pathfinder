## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0063 the shape is: a polymer is a bonded arrangement; a cycle is a signed loop; the cycle's valence is habitat-relative; the arrangement, not the ingredient list, carries function. The name of a particular monomer, salt or sequence is not returned by the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." In this question a measured length distribution or a function assay would settle a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a deliquescent salt or a hydroxy acid as the operative binding is a provenanced import from the chemistry frame, not a figure derivation.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list; this is why the audit does not force the chemistry onto it (see the retracted strain in `01` section 6).

## 4. Why bulk yield is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried mapping) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here a total oligomer yield is the green reading. It cannot comment on the source: condensation during drying, selective survival of longer chains, or suppression of hydrolysis could produce the same bulk number. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." A sequence-discriminating instrument, not a larger yield table, is what is owed. This is why the audit declines to treat a higher yield as a verdict (F5 and F8).

## 5. Question answered: what licenses a particular functional sequence?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape: cycling explores arrangements over a fixed alphabet, and the arrangement carries information the ingredients do not. Which sequence is functional is licensed only by an assay that settles a named chain, or by a provenanced import from a frame that already holds the sequence. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The shape here is not "wet–dry cycles work". It is a four-part separator with a declared `gap` on function (see `01` findings F1 to F6).

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming any chemical binding. It is not. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Glycine, hydroxy acids, peptide bonds and cycle structures are bindings onto what the chemical frame already holds. Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive, and it does not move the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom"; this adds none). The correction is recorded rather than silently applied, matching the exemplar's own §5b correction.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous advocates for wet–dry cycling would fund the question; it would not settle it.

Same essay, opened directly: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** ground readings and cannot settle the question. ## Falsifier for this reading

The shape reading is refuted if a figure re-census shows that the framework does return names for missing primitives, or if a measurement predicts functional sequence directly from bulk length without a sequence-discriminating instrument. Either result would collapse the distinction this section relies on.
