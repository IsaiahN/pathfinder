## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Kinetics versus equilibrium.** Higgs 2016 and Boigenzahn et al. 2023 are the two modelling sources. They agree that cycling is a kinetic, non-equilibrium effect. They share a conceptual pool (prebiotic polymerisation kinetics), so this is one witness, not two.
- **Water activity as a control variable.** Campbell et al. 2019 gives the clearest anchor: limited rehydration helps, overhydration flips the valence. This corroborates the figure claim that valence is residual- and habitat-relative (Fig 11 :49), but it is a single experimental system (glycine plus chloride/phosphate salts).
- **Sequence space.** Forsythe et al. 2017 gives the discriminator missing from bulk-yield studies: hundreds of sequence isomers in four days, with order invisible to mass alone. This corroborates Fig 12 :94 and :117. It does not tell us which sequences function.
- **Field mapping.** The setting most often proposed for these cycles is terrestrial hot springs with fluctuating pools (Damer and Deamer, partial). That is a habitat enumeration (Fig 11 :46), not a derivation of the chemistry.

## Distribution rules

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the separator and the refuter first. The figure text and formula links come only after a reader has recorded a verdict on stage 1.
- **Fig 2 :35:** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement; ask what the reader would count as a failure.
- **Fig 8 :50-52:** convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. Where a laboratory result is borrowed, keep its author, venue and year attached.
- **Fig 9 :60:** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A higher yield selects a regime to try; it is not proof of a pathway to life.
- **Fig 11 :43:** "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." When describing the setting, list the measured conditions rather than inventing an idealised cycle.
- **Legal and practical.** Public submission portals, society contacts and opt-in mailing lists avoid unsolicited bulk email and the associated GDPR or CAN-SPAM style exposure. The ISSOL contact page publishes personal institutional addresses; they are deliberately not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv`. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject lines.

## The two-stage offer (carried from the exemplar, adapted)

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the four-condition separator and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the reader would expect to see if it were true and what would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the verified links above, with the note that the figure text is the authority and that the laboratory papers can be checked independently.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user state their own mode and write their own refuter before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This folder is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading; it cannot certify the chemistry. The anchor is the experiment.
