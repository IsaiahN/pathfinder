I redid the census from the residual line alone, before adopting the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

Symmetry: identical monomers under one bond type give a symmetric arrangement with no new substance, while a heteropolymer sequence is ordered; the cycle is not time-symmetric because building and hydrolysing are different operators. Scale: bond, chain, population over N cycles.
- **D1, chemical composition.** Actors: monomers, bonds, water, salts. Currency: net bonds per chain. Anchor: a controlled dry-down measurement. Question type: composition within closure.

- **D4, sequence and function.** Actors: sequence, bond arrangement, assay. Currency: arrangement description length. Anchor: a function assay on a named chain. Question type: arrangement with a declared gap.
- **D5, verification.** Actors: this derivation, readers, laboratories. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: experiment. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: monomers and bond vocabulary, then an objective (length or function), then cycle engineering, then retained chains. Refuse the "one question" framing before minting a verdict (Formula step 2); describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :48/:94/:117/:121, Fig 8 :32, Fig 9 :54-55 and Fig 11 :49.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: polymerisation is bond composition; cycles add no monomer; the loop sign decides; water valence is residual-relative; arrangement beats ingredient list.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete laboratory claim, for example "deliquescent salts raised glycine oligomer yield tenfold and produced chains up to Gly13" or "hundreds of sequences formed in four days".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**: a bulk yield cannot tell which length class or which sequence moved (Fig 1 :22), yet the shorthand says "yield" as if it were one quantity;
- the **function discriminator**: the shorthand says the arrangement carries information the ingredients do not, but the concrete measurement of which sequence functions is not supplied (Fig 12 :94, Fig 9 :32);
- the **independence debit**: the shorthand cites four chemistry papers as if they were four witnesses; they are one pool (Fig 2 :40).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the chain is a one-bond-type molecule and a cycle is a signed loop whose product re-enters as input. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken by this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
