## Wider literature that bears on the question

All of the following were read inside the SEP entries above, so they are second-hand here and are recorded as one encyclopedia witness, not independent confirmations.

1. **Theory-ladenness, Hanson 1958.** The SEP entry says analogues of Hanson's claim that seeing is a theory-laden undertaking "apply just as well to equipment generated observations (Hanson 1958, 19)". Predicted by the figures: assumptions sit in the prediction, hence in what is noticed. Corroborates F1.
2. **Data selection, Longino 2020.** "even the primary, original, state of data is not free from researchers' value- and theory-laden selection and organization" (as quoted in SEP). Predicted: recording is a channel property, not neutral transcription. Corroborates F4.
3. **Model and data, Lloyd 2012.** "no pristine separation of model and data" (as quoted in SEP). Predicted: the library `Γ` is inside the observation, not outside it. Corroborates F1.
4. **Outlier rejection, Franklin 1986 via SEP.** Millikan marked the second oil drop's data "Publish", then "Won't work" after computing a value aberrant against expectation. The SEP entry adds: "When data are excluded from analysis, there ought to be some explanation justifying their omission over and above lack of agreement with the experimenters' expectations." Predicted: disregard is sorted by the held bin (Fig 5 :11). Corroborates F1 and F6.
5. **Expectation and noticing, Mack and Rock via Wikipedia.** The page reports that "once observers are made aware of the importance of the stimuli to be presented, for example stating that one will later be tested on it, the phenomenon essentially disappears." Predicted: changing `b` changes which slots carry positive residual. Corroborates F2. Different field pool from the SEP sources (cognition rather than HPS), so this is the one genuinely low-overlap witness.
6. **Paradigm and puzzles, Kuhn 1962 via SEP Scientific Method.** Normal science commits the community to "the puzzles to be solved and the acceptable ways of solving them". Predicted: at community scale, assumptions set the research agenda and hence what counts as evidence. Corroborates F6.

**Pool check.** Entries 1 through 4 and 6 come from two SEP entries, one encyclopedia and one editorial pool; they are one witness. Entry 5 comes from a tertiary cognition page. Effective independent witness count is two, not six. The figures decide the verdict; these sources only corroborate it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the generator (the residual, the three domain claims and the refuter), not the full architecture, before anyone has described a case.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not select recipients for likely agreement.
- **Fig 9 :60** - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A contact-score or a venue's prestige is a budget filter, never evidence.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three domain claims and the pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure in a case they know; (3) record their stage-1 verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** The reader checks the shape claim against a historical or cognitive case they can verify in an archive or an experiment. They do not hold the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text and the two encyclopedia or cognition links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": the loop "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The three refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 and in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.
