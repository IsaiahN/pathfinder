# The Tether's answer on background assumptions and evidence

**Mode: `general` on the mechanism shape, `specified` on each domain, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two researchers, the same ground, the same evidence field, different observation reports. The difference is per slot, not global: Fig 1 :19 says "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The question "how do background assumptions shape which evidence is noticed, recorded or disregarded" therefore asks for a mechanism at three linked places, not for a verdict about any community.

## 2. The shape, per domain

### D1, notice: assumptions are terms in the prediction

The belief `b`, its weighting `w` between private history and inherited prior, and the library `Γ` enter the prediction `Γ(b,a)`. The residual is `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot. Positive live mass `R⁺_s` is what is noticed; the Symbols table defines `R⁺_s` as "something happened there the prediction missed". Where the prediction already explains the slot, the gap is sorted as "held: the model was right, nothing owed" (Fig 5 :11), so nothing is registered as evidence. The deepest case is no tracker at all: Fig 6 :34-35, "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution." Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 1, Symbols table, Fig 5).

### D2, record: a record is what survives the channel

"the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24). "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25-26) are channel failures, and their honest report is "you have lost touch" (Fig 10 :27-28), not a claim about the world. "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :37-38). New instruments are not deduced from assumptions; "every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced" (Fig 6 :33). Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 10, Fig 6, Fig 2).

### D3, disregard: a shared pool turns agreement into a convention

At community scale, assumptions operate as a shared source. Fig 2 :18-21: "one evidence pool / they never talk, and still agree / so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." That agreement is still "efficient for a convention" (Fig 2 :41), so shared meaning is a real product; it is just not evidence. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A datum with no category in the pool is the one most easily labelled artifact or omitted. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so there is no standing list of good evidence. Verdict: **triangulated** for the pool claim (Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 8), **single-source** for the valence sub-claim (Fig 11 only).

### Across all domains

The ground does not move. Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Assumptions change contact, not truth. The answer is therefore not relativism and not a claim that evidence is whatever a community says it is.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named historian, laboratory or period was irrational. The mechanism is structural: "Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge" (Symbols table, `w`).
- Not that observation is subjective in the strong sense. The ground stays pinned; frames are corrected by it separately.
- Not which assumption mattered in a specific episode. The framework returns a shape, not a historical name (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
- Not that the three operations are one lever. They are a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58) with separate loops.
- Not `grounded`. No archive, blind re-test or replication was run in this package.

## 4. What would settle a specific case - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried reading): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and it was the spectrometer that resolved the case. For a historical episode the corresponding questions are:

1. **Slot test.** Which object slots did the frame track, and was there a slot for the datum now said to have been missed? A private notebook, an instrument log, or a protocol is the ground here. If no slot existed, the no-tracker branch (F3) is the explanation.
2. **Channel test.** Did the datum survive into the record, or was it dropped by a saturated metric or a pooled reading? Compare raw logs with published tables. This distinguishes "not noticed" from "noticed and not recorded".
3. **Pool test.** Did the community's agreement come from independent contact or from a shared source? Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Lines of evidence that never talked but still agree point to the pool, not to corroboration.
4. **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 5: matched `b`, `w`, `Γ` and `o'` with divergent noticing; positive live mass at a tracked slot that is not noticed; a same-closure chain that increases evidence.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The three-domain shape is figure-governed and checkable now. The historical instantiation is not: it needs archives, blind re-tests and independent lines of evidence, and this package has none. The canonical README's limit applies: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." "Indiscernibility is not identity." The record of which discriminator is owed is the result.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that researchers are irrational or that evidence is whatever a community agrees on. It says that background assumptions are terms in the prediction, and the prediction is indexed per object slot. A datum is noticed when its slot carries positive residual; where the prediction already explains the slot, the gap is "held" and nothing is owed, and where no slot exists the datum cannot be read at all. Whether a noticed datum survives into a record is a channel property: the ground does not decay, the channel does, through stale percepts, saturated metrics and pooled readings. At community scale, researchers who share an evidence pool can agree without ever talking, and that agreement is a convention rather than corroboration; a datum with no category in the pool is easily labelled artifact. The same recorded items under different assumptions can be isomers, same atoms and different substance, because the assumption is the bond a list of ingredients omits. The framework returns this shape and its refuters, not the name of the assumption in any particular episode; naming that requires an archive, a blind re-test or an independent line of evidence, and until one is run the verdict stays `candidate`.
