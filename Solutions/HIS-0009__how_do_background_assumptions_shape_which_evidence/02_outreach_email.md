# Outreach draft - check one output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the stage-1 output below survives the reader's check. If it does not, there is nothing else to look at, and the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Field:** public, opt-in history of science and cognition research venues. No personal emails, no purchased lists, no receptiveness ranking.

---

**Subject:** Check one shape claim about evidence and assumptions before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against a case you already know well. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on each domain, not `grounded`):** background assumptions do not act as a filter applied after evidence arrives. They are terms in the prediction. In the framework's notation the residual is `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per object slot, where `b` is the belief, weighted between private history and inherited prior, and `Γ` is the library of what can be expressed. That residual is indexed per slot, never aggregated. Three consequences follow, and they are separate claims, not one score:
>
> 1. **Notice.** A datum is noticed when its slot carries positive residual, that is, when the prediction missed something there. Where the prediction already explains the slot, the gap is sorted as "held: the model was right, nothing owed" and no evidence is registered. A slot with no tracker at all is the deeper case: nothing there can be perceived yet, so it "simply cannot be read".
> 2. **Record.** Once noticed, survival into a record is a channel property. The ground does not decay, the channel does. Stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings are channel failures; the honest report from them is "you have lost touch", not a conclusion about the world.
> 3. **Disregard.** At community scale, researchers who share an evidence pool can agree without ever talking. That agreement is excellent for a convention and worthless as corroboration. A datum the pool has no category for is readily recorded as artifact or not recorded at all. The same datum changes valence when the residual changes; there is no standing list of good evidence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular historian, laboratory or decade was irrational; that observation is merely subjective (the ground stays pinned and frames are corrected by it separately); that the framework names which assumption mattered in a specific case. It returns a mechanism shape, not a historical name. Naming a specific assumption is a binding from your frame, and until provenance is shown it carries no weight here.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. Two frames with identical belief, weighting, library and observation inputs that still notice, record or disregard different evidence at the same tracked slots.
> 2. A slot with positive residual, tracked by an intact channel, that fails to be noticed.
> 3. A chain of same-closure rooms that increases evidence rather than only agreement.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. The load-bearing lines are Fig 1 :19 and :28, Fig 2 :18-21 and :40, Fig 10 :24-28, and the Symbols table entries for `b`, `w`, `R` and `R⁺_s`.
> - Theory and Observation in Science, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/science-theory-observation/
> - Inattentional blindness, Wikipedia (tertiary overview with primary citations): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inattentional_blindness
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run it on a historical case you can check against an archive. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Delivery rules

- One-to-one only. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Record the reader's verdict on stage 1 before any theory is shown. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- If a reader returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluate it the same way as any other source.
- Prefer the public submission routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv` over unsolicited individual messages.
