## Effect statement of the question

Two researchers face the same ground and the same evidence field, yet one notices a datum, records it, and treats it as decisive while the other does not register it, records it as noise, or never tracks it at all. The effect to be explained is a difference in observation reports under a shared ground. In the loop's notation, the residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot `s`: `b` is the belief, `Γ` the library, `o'` the observation, and `s` the object slot (THE_FORMULA step 1; Symbols table). "Background assumptions" are the contents of `b` and `Γ`, including the weighting `w` that mixes private history with inherited prior. "Notice" is positive live mass `R⁺_s` at a tracked slot. "Record" is survival through the channel. "Disregard" is a slot sorted into the held bin, a slot with no tracker, or a datum the surrounding community agrees to treat as artifact. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a mechanism with three stages, assumption setting prediction, prediction setting residual slots, and the channel plus the peer pool setting what persists. That binding is the sought-for shape, written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the framework.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle a particular historical episode. The figures decide the mechanism shape; a historian's archive decides the episode.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the agreement that would disqualify their verdict.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list of people who already accept the framework cannot corroborate it.
3. **The ground, not prestige, decides.** Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." The ask is for a refutation against a case the reader knows, not for endorsement.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions; that is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid both.
