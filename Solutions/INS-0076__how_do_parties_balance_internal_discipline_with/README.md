## The verdict in one line

The question asks for a single dial, but the figures return a separator. Discipline and representation have different anchors and currencies, so they are not opposite settings of one control: agreement is *"efficient for a convention"* (Fig 2 :41) and *"disqualifying for a verdict"* (Fig 2 :40). The answer is a union of conditions, kept per domain and never averaged. No figure line returns a universal percentage, so that is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not supply an anchor.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list chosen for likely agreement is a list chosen to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping private emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Only public institutional submission and contact pages are listed.
