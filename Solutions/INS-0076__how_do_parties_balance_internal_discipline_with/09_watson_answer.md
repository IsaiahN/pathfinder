## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A party must act as one and keep a live signal from heterogeneous members and constituencies. Arity: at least two overlapping conditions plus the false binary framing. Symmetry: discipline and representation are not symmetric coordinates; they have different currencies and anchors (Fig 2 :39-41). Scale: per member and per constituency slot, plus party versus ground at the electoral scale. Fig 9 :55 names the bundle a union: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Domain 1, convention and coordination.** Actors: whips, caucus, leadership, party brand. Currency: agreement about shared meaning and coordinated action. Anchor: the party's capacity to act as one, eventually checked by an election that does not update on the line. Union condition, not a partition cell.
- **Domain 2, verdict and world-facing judgment.** Actors: factions, backbenchers, internal critics, outside peer frames. Currency: independent evidence and residuals per slot. Anchor: a non-updating election or constituency outcome. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Union condition with D1, overlapping where members share evidence.
- **Domain 3, habitat and room composition.** Actors: members, prospective members, activist factions, electoral habitat. Currency: introduction of actors; valence relative to the residual. Anchor: the ground selects and the room is a function of the question. Union condition.
- **Domain 4, budget and measurement.** Actors: analyst and institution designer. Currency: description cost and settling cost, held apart. Anchor: none for a universal ratio. This is a declared limit, not a domain with a hidden anchor.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse the one-dial framing first (formula step 2), then the anchor test, then the convention role, then room composition, then the gap. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :40 and Fig 2 :41 seem to contradict on agreement, but govern world-facing verdicts and shared-meaning conventions respectively, so re-census dissolves it. Fig 8 :45 and Fig 7 :35 seem to contradict on diversity, but one requires low overlap and a described residual while the other describes agreeing rooms in a chain. Two live constraints remain and are named: the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim, and negation versus its institutional analogue.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator survives; the dial does not. Discipline and representation are two quantities with different anchors and currencies, so agreement is both efficient for a convention and worthless as a verdict when frames share evidence. The answer is a union of conditions with per-domain verdicts kept separate: convention (D1) efficient until it fixes an outcome; independence (D2) bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong and only at low overlap; room composition (D3) a function of the question with no standing list of good factions; and a `gap` (D4) where no figure line sets a number. A refusal seat is required so the party can carry a correction, the residual stays indexed per member and constituency, and disciplined and pluralist parties over fixed atoms are isomers rather than amounts. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the two-valence rule at Fig 2 :39-41.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, four refuters and the live analogues before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a number or a theory before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The dial framing is removable without touching the domain census; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No mechanism has to be withdrawn, and the gap is recorded rather than filled with a guess.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that separates the sources of a cohesion reading, a constituency verdict that moves when the line changes, a refusal seat that fails to enter the record, a fixed room that still pays after the question changes, a figure line that returns a universal ratio, or a demonstration that description cost and settling cost trade in one unit, any of these would move the verdict. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
