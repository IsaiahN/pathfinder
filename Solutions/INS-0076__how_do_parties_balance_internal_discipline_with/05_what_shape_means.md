## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): a search can be cut short when the returned set *"provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The essay also notes that if a final exhaustive search fails, *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is precisely the situation here. The party question is a bundle; the shape is the unbundled separator.

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the missing term. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0076 the shape is: two quantities with different anchors, a union of conditions, a per-slot residual, a refusal operator, a room that tracks the question, no universal ratio. It is not a number and it is not a named optimal party structure.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual in `01` section 0 is stated in effect terms (coordination, independence, refusal, anchoring), not in discipline/diversity jargon.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This governs the outreach order in `02`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the stop is `gap`, which is a real stopping place rather than a failure to look hard enough.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground would be a party-specific measurement: an election, a recorded vote, a membership decision, a constituency outcome that does not update on the whip's count.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :53), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Condorcet and Page results are such imports. They explain a gap (why independence matters) and they name their source and year.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So calling a whip, a faction, or a free vote the answer to the party question would turn a visible institutional choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a single number is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several distinct sources can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* What resolves it is a spectrometer, not an argument.

Applied here: a cohesion score reads one thing (how often members vote together). It cannot comment on the source of that cohesion. The same 99.6 percent can be produced by shared conviction, by a shared evidence pool, by career incentives, or by a whip with a strong sanction. A "balance" computed from the reading would name the source from the projection, which is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a number. Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

This is why `01` F11 records a `gap` rather than a best guess. The gap is not an admission that the search was short. It is the report that the available instruments cannot distinguish the cases the question asks us to distinguish.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming whip, faction or free vote?

**No, with a boundary.** The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Whips, caucuses, factions, primaries, conscience votes and minority reports are already-held institutional atoms in the political frame. Naming one as the binding for a slot is a **provenanced binding**, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains forbidden:

- Inventing a seventh mechanism and calling it framework-derived.
- Claiming a binding moves the wall. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* A free vote added to a party's rules does not add a new kind of institution to the world; it re-arranges what is already available.
- Naming the mechanism as *the* answer. The framework returns the shape (a refusal seat is needed); which institution fills it is chosen by the ground and recorded as a choice.

It governs missing primitives, not the selection of atoms already held. The present form narrows the claim and keeps the earlier overstatement on record rather than erasing it. ## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result."* And the operative sentence: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A campaign that gathers well-known political scientists to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let an election or a recorded vote settle it, and do not pool later agreement as evidence. This is also why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no personal emails and no receptiveness rank.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim about parties and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
