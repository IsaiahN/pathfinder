## Independent domain census (written before reading the primary conclusions)

I redid the census using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Two conditions with overlapping intersections plus a false binary frame. Arity: at least two. Symmetry: the two sides of the question are not symmetric, because one is a convention and the other a verdict relation to a ground; a pooled amount assumes a symmetry not derived. Scale: per slot (member, constituency) and party versus ground.
- **Domain A, the coordinating frame.** Actors: leadership, whips, members. Currency: agreement about shared meaning and speed of action. Anchor: only an election outside the frame; an internal cohesion score updates on what the party thinks and is not an anchor.
- **Domain B, the ranging frame.** Actors: factions, backbenchers, critics, outside frames. Currency: independent evidence. Anchor: a constituency outcome that does not move when the line changes.
- **Domain C, the habitat of actors.** Actors: current and potential members, the wider electoral habitat. Currency: introduction; valence relative to the residual. Anchor: the ground selects; the room is a function of the question.
- **Domain D, the accounting limit.** Two currencies (bits of description, actions to settle), no anchor for a universal ratio. Not a domain with a hidden anchor, a declared stop.

Union, not partition. Dependency: refuse the false binary, fix the anchor, then the convention role, then the room, then the stop. This matches the primary's three-plus-limit structure, though I reached it through Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 11 :49 (valence) and Fig 12 :125 (two currencies) rather than through the primary's Fig 2 route. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low route overlap within one evidence pool. The agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a party needs both a convention and independent ranging, and equal emphasis on an unexamined single dial is not a result.
