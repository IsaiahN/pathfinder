## 0. The claim under audit

> When do people comply with rules because they believe the rules are legitimate rather than because they fear punishment?
>
> Read as an effect: one observed compliant act o', two candidate sources. Source L (legitimacy) is an internalized norm that persists when detection probability p = 0 and sanction S = 0. Source D (sanction fear) is a threshold comparison whose compliance collapses as p times S falls. The question asks for the conditions under which L rather than D is the operative source.
>
> Option A, "compliance reveals the source": read the source off the observed act.
> Option B, "compliance is a lossy projection of the source": the act does not determine the source; a discriminating instrument is owed.

What an answer would look like, bound before the run (Formula, "Direction"): the shape of a discriminating condition and an instrument that returns a person-level source attribution.

## 1. Domain census

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor. A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three-place (person, rule, context), carrying one ordered comparison bond (deterrence) and one identification bond (legitimacy). Symmetry: the two sources are not interchangeable, because they respond differently to removal of surveillance. Scale: micro decision, meso rule-application encounter, macro institutional legitimacy; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30). Fig 1 :19 applies: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **D1 Individual decision frame.** Actors: the person, belief b, library Gamma of internalized norms, weighting w. Currency: action a and residual R. Anchor: behavior under the no-surveillance condition (p = 0, S = 0). Nameable and admitted.
- **D2 Sanction and enforcement frame.** Actors: the rule, the monitor, the sanction, p, S. Currency: expected cost p times S. Anchor: the enforcement apparatus, audits, courts, fines. Nameable and admitted.
- **D3 Normative community frame.** Actors: peers, the authority, procedural justice, social identity. Currency: normative acceptance and internalization. Anchor: compliance persistence when p = 0 and S = 0, plus controlled variation of procedural justice. Nameable and admitted.
- **D4 Research and measurement frame.** Actors: experiments, surveys, audits, instruments. Currency: agreement versus residual; independence. Anchor: observed behavior under manipulated p and S, which does not move because a frame thinks otherwise (Fig 2 :29-30). Nameable and admitted.
- **X1 Compliance as a fixed trait of the person.** No nameable anchor that survives a habitat substitution. Not a domain yet; absorbed into the D1/D3 relation (Fig 11 :39).
- **X2 Pure deterrence as the sole cause.** Absorbed into D2 as a limiting case when no residual remains under p = 0. Not a separate domain.

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D3 overlap in the same person, and D2 acts on both. The two conditions bind jointly, so a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-57). **Dependency order (Fig 3):** perception of the rule, vocabulary for "fair", "obligation" and "penalty", objective (what counts as compliance), planning (an instrument to discriminate sources), learn and carry. The missing link is the discriminating instrument (Fig 3 :27).

## 2. Findings

### F1 The question is a source question, not an observation question (decisive)

Fig 8 :50:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"**

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

The Hard Problem is About Source (essay, opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A compliant act is the green reading. Legitimacy and sanction fear are two sources that can produce it. Reading the source off the act is the metamerism error.

**Mode `specified`.** **Status triangulated** (Fig 8 convergence claim and Fig 9 shape limit, low overlap) with the source-discrimination essay as secondary. **Refuter:** an instrument that returns the operative source directly from a single compliance observation without manipulating p or S. If such an instrument exists and is verified, F1 fails.

### F2 Compliance is a property of person-and-habitat, not of the person alone

Fig 11 :39 and :40:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Cross-figure corroboration at low overlap, Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43): *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*

**Mode `specified` for the compliance application, `general` for the structural claim.** **Status triangulated** (Fig 11 and Fig 4). **Refuter:** a person-level compliance measure invariant across a documented habitat substitution that removes the rule's legitimacy anchor. Such an invariance would break the capability claim.

### F3 The two sources carry different bonds and are isomers over one observed act

Fig 12 :117:

> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :112 and the Operators table :51:

> **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**
> **"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"**

The Operators table :48 defines comparison as *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Sanction fear fits this bond: it compares expected cost against benefit. Legitimacy fits an identification with the norm, not a threshold. Fig 12 :119 adds the asymmetry:

> **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

An internalized norm is catalyst-like; it survives its own use. A paid fine is a spent part. So the two sources are isomers: same observable compliance, different bond, different long-run behavior.

**Mode `specified`.** **Status single-source for the bond definition (Fig 12), triangulated with the Operators table for the comparison typing; the empirical discrimination is unmeasured.** **Refuter:** showing both sources can be written with the same bond, for example that legitimacy is also a threshold comparison, or that the norm is spent by compliance rather than regenerated.

### F4 Sanctions can change sign and crowd out legitimacy (second-order cascade)

Fig 11 :49, :50, :51:

> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
> **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**
> **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

Fig 11 :52: *"And contact cascades."* A sanction is not a fixed deterrent; it can build compliance in one frame and displace an internalized norm in another. The overjustification literature (opened 2026-11-23) records the same move in a different field: *"providing expected external incentive, such as money or prizes, for an already intrinsically rewarding activity can reduce a person's intrinsic motivation to perform that activity."*

**Mode `specified`.** **Status triangulated** (Fig 11 valence rule and Fig 12 catalyst/spent distinction, low overlap) with the overjustification literature as a low-overlap witness. **Refuter:** a within-subject experiment showing that raising p times S leaves no-surveillance compliance unchanged, with no displacement of the internalized norm. That would falsify the crowd-out reading.

### F5 A self-report cannot score the verdict

Fig 1 :26 and :27:

> **"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"**
> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Fig 2 :40:

> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

A survey of the same population whose behavior is being explained produces the compliance and the legitimacy report from one pool. By Fig 1 :23, *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

**Mode `specified`.** **Status triangulated** (Fig 1 and Fig 2). **Refuter:** a self-report instrument that predicts behavior under p = 0 and S = 0 as well as the behavioral discriminator, verified on a held-out sample. Then it is not merely frame-internal.

### F6 The discriminator is variation of detection and sanction, ranged per slot

Fig 2 :29:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 3 :27:

> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Fig 6 :29 and :30 name the two opposed instruments:

> **"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."**
> **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**

The inward move is to sharpen the no-surveillance condition and the procedural-justice manipulation. The outward move is to describe the residual in effect terms to a frame that already holds a discriminator. They are never read against each other. Fig 2 :37 makes the anchor constitutive here: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."*

**Mode `specified`.** **Status triangulated** (Fig 2, Fig 3, Fig 6). **Refuter:** an alternative instrument that returns a person-level source attribution without manipulating p or S, for example a validated physiological or behavioral marker of internalization. That would demote the p/S manipulation from necessary to merely sufficient.

### F7 The union must be unbundled; one lever cannot clear both conditions

Fig 9 :55 and :57:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

A person can comply from both sources. Raising the fine clears the region where only deterrence binds and leaves the intersection, where legitimacy still binds, standing. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A compliance rate is a filter for budget, never a witness for source.

**Mode `specified`.** **Status triangulated** (Fig 9 union rule and Fig 11 valence rule). **Refuter:** an intervention that restores both conditions and leaves no categorically different residue, or a demonstration that the two conditions are independent and additive with no overlap.

### F8 The person-level switching threshold is a declared gap

Fig 13 :134 and :135:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**
> **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

No figure line, and no source opened, returns the number or parameter at which a given person's compliance flips from L-dominant to D-dominant. That number needs a source-discriminating instrument characterized in effect terms but not yet held.

**Mode `general` for the limit, `gap` for the quantity.** **Status gap after a genuine re-census.** **Refuter:** a calibrated instrument that returns a stable person-level threshold across two independent low-overlap samples.

### F9 The observer's stake must be declared

Fig 10 :59 and :60:

> **"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."**

**Mode `general`.** **Status single-source (Fig 10 plus Seat Map).** **Refuter:** a measurement design in which the party whose legitimacy is at stake has no role in instrument design, reading or publication, and the result is unchanged.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A controlled experiment shows that no-surveillance compliance (p = 0, S = 0) is statistically indistinguishable from zero across every person-slot tested, leaving no live legitimacy residual anywhere.
2. A within-subject manipulation of procedural justice fails to change no-surveillance compliance, breaking the legitimacy mechanism.
3. Sanctions and legitimacy are shown to be additive and independent, with no displacement and no sign change, breaking the union and cascade reading.
4. A person-level compliance measure is invariant across a habitat substitution that removes the rule's legitimacy anchor, breaking person-and-habitat.
5. An instrument returns a stable source attribution from a single compliance observation without manipulating p or S, breaking the lossy-projection claim.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1, the source question. Do not present compliance as evidence of legitimacy without a discriminator (F1, F5).
2. Design the stage-1 test as variation of p and S, indexed per person-slot, never as a pooled compliance rate (F6, F7, Fig 1 :19).
3. Treat legitimacy and sanction fear as isomers with different bonds, not as points on one continuum (F3).
4. Put the refuter before the result and declare the institution's stake (F9, Seat Map :287).
5. Publish the threshold as a `gap` until an instrument returns it. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, the discriminating design and its refuter, not the conclusion.

- `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule: an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure, not a verdict. The five-bin route in step 2 sorts residual by what is missing; a refused term is a negation, not a mint.

### Seven-question recursive derivation

1. **Is this one question?** No. Fig 9 :55 makes it a union of overlapping conditions; unbundle before searching.
2. **What is the residual?** The source-attribution gap, not the compliance level. Fig 8 :50, metamerism essay.
3. **Which frame predicts its shape?** The enforcement frame predicts D; the normative community frame predicts L. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
4. **What instrument discriminates them?** Variation of p and S, sharpened inward; described in effect terms for an outward frame. Fig 6 :29-30.
5. **Is a mint owed?** No. The atoms (norm, sanction, comparison, identity) are already held; the shape is bound, not a new primitive. Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
6. **Is anything settled?** No. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Status `candidate`.
7. **Route?** REFUTED is not triggered, because no held term has been shown false. The honest route is SEARCHED first, then INWARD to sharpen the discriminator. OUTWARD is a separate track only if a frame that already holds a discriminator is described in effect terms.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: compliance is a lossy projection whose source is discriminated by variation of detection and sanction, ranged per slot; legitimacy and sanction fear are isomers with different bonds; sanctions can displace legitimacy; the threshold is a declared gap. Strained after re-census: the attempt to use Fig 13 :75 (*"Two states never bond directly"*) as if it governed the legitimacy/sanction distinction. That is retracted in section 6. No other strain remains.
