## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *amount* of compliance but the *source* of a single observed compliant act. In effect terms: a person holds a belief b and a library Gamma of internalized norms; the rule context offers an action a (comply or defect); the observation o' is the behavior actually produced; R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is the discrepancy we are trying to attribute. Two candidate sources can produce the same o':

- Option A, legitimacy: the norm is internalized; the person complies when detection probability and sanction are both zero. The bond is an identification with the rule, and it survives its own use.
- Option B, sanction fear: the person performs a threshold comparison of expected cost (detection probability times sanction severity) against benefit. When the expected cost falls below the benefit, compliance ceases.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* naming the discriminating condition that separates the two sources, plus an instrument that would return a person-level source attribution. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is that the question is a **source question**, not an observation question: compliant behavior is a lossy projection that does not determine its source; the two sources are isomers over the same observed act; and the discriminator is variation of detection and sanction, which must be ranged against the ground per person-slot and never averaged. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the institutional question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of rule compliance.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Seat Map :163).
2. A self-report is a frame-internal quantity. *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27). A survey of the same population that produced the compliance cannot score the source of that compliance.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
