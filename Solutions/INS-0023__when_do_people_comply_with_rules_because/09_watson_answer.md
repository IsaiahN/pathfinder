# WATSON answer, INS-0023

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

When do people comply with rules because they believe the rules are legitimate rather than because they fear punishment?

Restated as an effect: one observed compliant act o', two candidate sources. Option A reads the source off the act, so compliance is evidence of legitimacy. Option B treats the act as a lossy projection whose source is not determined by the reading, and owes a discriminator. What each leads to: A commits the metamerism error and spends the independence of the people checking it; B yields a checkable shape, written refuters, and a declared gap until the ground settles the attribution. The anchor is behavior under manipulated detection probability and sanction, which is constitutive here because the question is about people (Fig 2 :37).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three-place (person, rule, context), carrying one ordered comparison bond and one identification bond. Symmetry: the two sources are not interchangeable because they respond differently to removal of surveillance. Scale: micro decision, meso rule-application encounter, macro institutional legitimacy; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4). Fig 1 :19 requires per-slot indexing, never aggregation.
- **D1 Individual decision frame.** Actors: person, belief b, library Gamma, weighting w. Currency: action and residual. Anchor: behavior under p = 0 and S = 0. Admitted.
- **D2 Sanction and enforcement frame.** Actors: rule, monitor, sanction, p, S. Currency: expected cost p times S. Anchor: the enforcement apparatus. Admitted.
- **D3 Normative community frame.** Actors: peers, authority, procedural justice, social identity. Currency: normative acceptance and internalization. Anchor: compliance persistence under no surveillance plus procedural-justice variation. Admitted.
- **D4 Research and measurement frame.** Actors: experiments, surveys, audits, instruments. Currency: agreement versus residual; independence. Anchor: observed behavior under manipulated p and S. Admitted.
- **X1 Compliance as a fixed trait of the person.** No nameable anchor surviving habitat substitution. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D1/D3.
- **X2 Pure deterrence as sole cause.** Absorbed into D2 as a limiting case when no residual remains at p = 0.

Union, not partition: D1 and D3 overlap in the same person and D2 acts on both (Fig 9 :55-57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Strain test applied: Fig 13 :75 governs the six physical primitives, not social motivation; the attempt to use it here was retracted and re-censused to Fig 12 and the Operators comparison bond.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the naive answer does not. A compliant act does not reveal its source; legitimacy and sanction fear are different-bonded sources over one observed compliance, with sanctions able to displace legitimacy; the discriminator is per-slot variation of detection and sanction, and a self-report from the same frame cannot score it. The two sources form a union of overlapping conditions, so one lever cannot clear both. The person-level switching number is a declared `gap`. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`. The world sources corroborate the mechanism but do not settle the institutional fact, and one of them (tax compliance) holds both sources in the same field rather than arbitrating between them.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis with a second-order cost. A legitimacy failure treated as a deterrence failure invites heavier sanctions, which can displace the remaining internalized norm (Fig 11 :52); a deterrence-only case treated as a legitimacy failure can collapse when enforcement is absent. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its five refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered refuters: no no-surveillance residual anywhere; no procedural-justice effect on no-surveillance compliance; additive and independent sources with no displacement; invariance across a habitat substitution removing the legitimacy anchor; an instrument returning the source from a single act without manipulating p or S. A genuinely low-overlap second instrument with a different evidence pool showing that per-slot source is fully predicted by pooled compliance rates would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
