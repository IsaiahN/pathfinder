## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: *a source-discriminating condition over an observed compliance, indexed per person-slot and per rule-slot, with a refuter written in advance*. The person-level switching number is a name, and the framework does not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the effect terms are: detection probability, sanction severity, compliance persistence when both are zero, and perceived procedural justice. Saying "legitimacy" is using a borrowed word from institutional theory; describing the effect is what travels to a different frame.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The same essay adds that declaring primitives does not make them neutral. So naming "legitimacy" as *the* source of a given act is a binding from the institutional frame onto a held term; until a ground settles it, it is recorded as such.

## 4. Why "compliance reveals legitimacy" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Observed compliance is the green reading. Legitimacy and sanction fear are two sources that can produce it. A study that reads legitimacy off compliance commits the metamerism error: a source named from a projection, where several sources could give the same projection. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q2 answered: what would license a particular source attribution?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular attribution is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the attribution is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The correct form is therefore not "this person complied because the rule was legitimate" but "under p = 0 and S = 0 this slot's compliance persisted, which is the shape the legitimacy source predicts, and which a detected absence would refuse."

## 6. Correction to my own section 3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially treated *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding the use of the words "legitimacy" and "sanction" at all. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Gamma. Legitimacy (an internalized norm) and sanction (a comparison) are terms already available in the institutional frame; naming them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in the naive reading is treating the binding as if it moved the wall. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Adding a label for an already-held arrangement does not expand the closure. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A compliance study that gathers famous names to agree that legitimacy matters is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the discriminator a budget to be tried, and let the ground (an experiment, an audit, a court record) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the compliance question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Retraction on a commonly repeated phrase

The phrase "LLM output is simulacra" appears in secondary summaries of the essay. On 2026-11-23 the fetched text of *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* did not contain the word "simulacra" (or "simulacrum"). I therefore do **not** quote it as verbatim in this folder. The verified sentences about the validator and about deriving the output from the real architecture are used instead. This correction is left visible rather than silently substituted, so a reader can see which wording was verified and which was dropped.
