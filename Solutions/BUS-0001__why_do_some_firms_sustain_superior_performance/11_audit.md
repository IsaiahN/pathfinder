## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among firm-library Γ, action a and market return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. It has at least three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (firm, performance) would lose the action and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the firm's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Superior" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is a quantity compared against a second, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a firm, but the ranking of "superior" exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the firm's step loop and the population's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *sustained performance requires maintaining a relational loop between the firm and its market.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific firm situation. Result: *keep listening to customers and keep bringing in new practices.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a firm failing C2 merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Relational loop" does not specify that the market, not internal agreement, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a firm to measure its own success with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the valence flip. "Keep bringing in new practices" has no place for the possibility that the old good practice changed sign (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
4. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a protected resource from a paying habitat, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
