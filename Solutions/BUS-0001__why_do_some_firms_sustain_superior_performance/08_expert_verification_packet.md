# Expert verification packet - the Tether on sustained firm performance

**What this is:** a claim in a form a strategy researcher or empirically minded manager can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No prospective market measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Superior performance lasting for decades is a relation between a firm and its market, not a stock the firm owns. Two conditions have to hold at once: the firm's channel to a market that still pays it stays reachable, meaning its readings of demand, price, share and survival are not stale, saturated or pooled into an average; and its reach keeps moving outward through contact with situations and competitors whose ways of working differ from its own, so it is not simply re-running the same successful routine at a larger scale. These are two different failure modes with two different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. The routine that produced the advantage can change sign when the market changes, so a strength and a rigidity can be the same object. The market, not internal agreement, is the anchor. The claim is a shape that any candidate answer must have; it does not name which routines, leaders or resources win.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 10 and 2

- **Statement:** capability is a property of firm-and-habitat, so persistence cannot be explained by the firm alone; quick loss is a failure of the channel, not a decay of the market ground; the anchor is the market, not internal agreement.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
  - Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
  - Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same line names *"a market"* as an instance.
- **Refuter:** a firm whose sustained performance is unchanged across a habitat substitution that removes the market's payment for what it does; or a firm that loses advantage while both bracket fidelity and ground pressure measure high and non-declining. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one business-facing check

- **Statement:** internal agreement is not evidence, and a firm that keeps the same closure stops progressing; renewal of reach requires contact with a closure that differs, because composition inside a closure cannot add an atom.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :36 - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Teece, Pisano and Shuen 1997 write that *"Whether and how a firm’s competitive advantage is eroded depends on the stability of market demand, and the ease of replicability (expanding internally) and imitability (replication by competitors)"*. Does that sentence not already name both the ground-pressure factor and the closure factor, and is the dynamic-capabilities remedy anything other than the import described in Fig 6 :23? A "no" to both questions is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a firm that keeps the same closure across two decades and keeps producing new, market-verified readings without any import from a differing closure; or a demonstration that a firm renewed its reach purely by internal composition with no new atom. Either breaks idempotence and C2 with it.

### C3 - Structure and competing explanations

- **Statement:** the union of conditions must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; two firms with identical atoms but different bonds are isomers, so a resource inventory alone cannot predict persistence; the strength-to-rigidity flip follows from valence being relative to the residual.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 11 :50 - *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
  - Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves no categorically different residue; or two firms with identical atoms and identical bonds whose persistence diverges with no difference in contact or imported atoms, which would move the explanation out of composition and into habitat alone.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a theory of everything about business, and no final or unique theory claim.
- Not an average. No claim about the percentage of variance attributable to firm, industry or year.
- Not that resources, culture, leadership or talent are irrelevant. They are Γ, the library operand; the claim is that Γ alone does not settle persistence.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-firm study that measures C1 and C2 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline. Concretely:

1. **Channel test:** does the firm's reading of the market degrade while the market keeps paying someone? Indicators: stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings (Fig 10 :25-26).
2. **Pressure and fidelity test:** are bracket fidelity and ground pressure both nonzero at each handoff? Fig 7 :37-39 says zero either stops the chain.
3. **Import test:** did any new atom enter the firm's reach from a closure that differs, before the decade of persistence? If not, C2 is refuted.
4. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish a protected resource from a paying habitat from luck when all three produce the same profit reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.
