# The Tether's answer on sustained firm performance

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No market measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a firm's routines predict the market will return and what the market actually returns stays small for decades in some firm-slots and grows quickly in others. R is indexed by firm, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **duration of contact**, not about the level of performance.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the firm's channel to a ground that still pays it stays reachable.
- **C2:** the firm's reach keeps moving outward through contact with a closure that differs from its own.

## 2. The shape

1. **Advantage is not a stock the firm owns.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A list of firm attributes is Γ, a candidate library whose reach must be tested against the ground, not the answer.
2. **Quick loss is channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. The firm stops hearing what pays, usually by pooling the very readings that would say where to look. Fig 7 :37-39 gives the two-factor form: bracket fidelity times ground pressure, and zero either stops the chain.
3. **Long persistence is a chain of differing rooms.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* A firm that reappoints the same room keeps agreeing with itself and stops travelling.
4. **Renewal needs an import.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal search adds an atom. Competitors, entrants, adjacent technologies, customers and regulators are not distractions from the core; they are the only operators that move the wall.
5. **The strength and the rigidity are the same object.** Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual and no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and *"nothing records which a term is"*. When the market changes, the formerly winning routine can become the thing that prevents the next win.
6. **The market is the anchor, not internal agreement.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a market"* as an anchor; :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Analyst consensus and internal metrics are instruments, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure.
7. **The break is usually in the middle of the chain.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary/objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. More planning budget lands above the break.
8. **Two firms with the same resources can still differ.** Under Fig 12 :117 they are isomers if the bonds differ: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance."* This is why a resource inventory alone cannot predict persistence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective market measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which routines, which leaders, which industries or which capabilities produce a decade of advantage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about what percentage of returns is "firm effect" or "industry effect". Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that leaders, culture or resources are irrelevant. They are Γ. The claim is that Γ does not settle persistence on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not a theory of everything about business. It stops where the instrument stops.
- Not corroboration by agreement. The strategy sources Barney, Rumelt and Teece share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap sources from ecology, complexity and cognition corroborate the mechanism shape, not the firm-level fact.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are profit, share and survival; the candidate sources are a protected resource, a paying habitat, a temporary asymmetry or simple luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. sustained performance unchanged after the market stops paying for what the firm does;
2. advantage lost while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining;
3. the same closure held for two decades while new ground-verified readings keep arriving with no import;
4. a capability's valence invariant across a documented change of residual;
5. one intervention restoring both conditions with nothing categorically different left over.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per firm, records the bonds as well as the atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is owed. Naming a winner from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The six conditions in section 2 are a shape; the seventh thing, which routine actually works next, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank firms or name the resource that wins. It says that superior performance lasting for decades is a relation, not a stock: the firm has to keep its channel to a market that still pays it reachable, and it has to keep moving its reach outward by contact with ways of working that differ from its own. Those are two conditions with two different failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. Quick loss is usually the channel going stale, saturated or pooled into an average; long persistence is a chain of rooms that each differ from the last and are selected against the residual the last one surfaced. The capability that produced the advantage can change sign when the market changes, so the strength and the rigidity can be the same object. The market, not internal agreement, is the anchor. What is offered is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which particular routines occupy the slots stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
