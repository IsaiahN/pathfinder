## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** Barney, Rumelt and Teece are all strategic management. They agree on a firm-specific reading of advantage, but they draw on the same conferences, the same citation chain and the same FTC line-of-business and interview datasets lineage. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not three.

- Barney 1991 starts from resources *"heterogeneously distributed across firms and that these differences are stable over time"* and names value, rareness, imitability and substitutability. This supports the existence of firm-level differences and conflicts with the figures' agent-and-habitat claim as written. It was re-mapped to Γ, not used as the verdict (F1, F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).
- Rumelt 1991 reports *"very large stable business-unit effects"*. This is exactly why R must be indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), but a variance decomposition is a budget filter, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Teece, Pisano and Shuen 1997 define the reconfiguration of *"internal and external competences to address rapidly changing environments"* and note that erosion depends on *"the stability of market demand"*. These are the business-pool names for the two conditions C2 and C1 respectively. They corroborate the shape, but only as one witness.
- Leonard-Barton's title, *"Core Capabilities and Core Rigidities: A Paradox in Managing New Product Development"*, matches the figure claim that valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Only the bibliographic record was opened, so no substantive quote is used.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each read directly:

1. **Ecology and social-ecological resilience.** MathisonSlee et al. 2022 describe a *"rigidity trap"* where a system becomes *"highly connected, rigid, and inflexible"* and loses *"capacity for novelty"*. They also report that *"Cross-scale interactions critically determine the form of the subsequent adaptive cycle at any one focal scale"*. This corroborates F5 and the membrane rule (Fig 4) without sharing a pool with management strategy.
2. **Complex systems and the philosophy of modelling.** Marks, Gerrits and Marx 2019 warn that transferring a biological fitness landscape to the social domain requires changing the semantics while keeping the syntax, and that *"the more the model is tailored towards the target domain, the more exploratory and explanatory power it will have, the less it will resemble the original model."* This corroborates the WATSON discipline of describing the effect before choosing the frame (Fig 9 :28, :31) and refuses a naive fitness-landscape answer.
3. **Human cognition and expertise.** Trinh 2019 reports that *"Nokia did not do anything wrong; it was expert in what it was doing. It only failed to catch up with change"*, and that expert schemas grow *"more interrelated"* and *"more stable"* through repeated practice. This is an individual-level instance of the same closure/reach mechanism. Fig 4 :20 requires the anchor to be re-named at the new scale, so it corroborates the mechanism shape, not a firm-level measurement.
4. **Business practice.** The Stratrix VRIO page makes the same point operationally: *"A rival can copy the product in a cycle. It cannot copy the years that piled up around it."* It also records the failure mode of dating "temporary advantages" and re-running the test when rivals move. This is a practitioner restatement of bracket fidelity across handoffs (Fig 7 :38).

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The AOM page states: *"Artificial Intelligence: Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
