## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame receives the residual and its refuter first, never the interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk mail to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this folder

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From the author's own essay, read 2026-11-23: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So this package cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns a shape and a refuter, and it stops there.
