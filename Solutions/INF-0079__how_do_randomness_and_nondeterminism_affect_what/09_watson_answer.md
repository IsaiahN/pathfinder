## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what randomness and nondeterminism change about (a) what can be computed and (b) what can be predicted. Option A: they extend the computable class and supply a new primitive, so prediction is limited only by budget. Option B: they reorganise a closed reach, changing coverage, cost and independence, while in prediction they are the residual a turn leaves, with the ontic question left open. What each leads to: A predicts a seventh kind, an unbounded reach and a sealed room that could be searched to the end; B predicts an unchanged wall, a finite predictability horizon, and a `gap` where no discriminating instrument exists. The question is a union of two conditions and is unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54-57).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Randomness and nondeterminism do not move the wall of what is composable, because arranging a closed set never adds a kind (D1). They change how much of the reach is visited, what that costs, and whether independent searchers buy anything (D1 and D3). In prediction they are the residual itself: a compounded remainder is chaos and discarded crossing detail is noise, so deterministic systems can have finite predictability horizons and a failure to predict is not proof of a new primitive (D2). A frame cannot certify its own limit or prove arbitrary incompressibility beyond a constant (D1). Whether the randomness is ontic is explicitly open, and the answer is `gap` rather than a guess (D4). The whole result is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each domain has a named anchor and a written refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The D1 to D3 verdicts are removable one at a time without touching the others, and D4 is already recorded as open. A superseded packet can replace a sent one. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the ontic slot was never filled with a guess.
- **reversed by:** ground readings, not agreement. A machine that moves the compositional reach under randomization, a finding that correlated searchers gain as much as independent ones, a source-discriminating instrument that settles ontic versus epistemic randomness, or a proof exceeding the Chaitin constant from inside a fixed system would each reverse a specific verdict. Until one fires, the verdicts stand as `candidate`.
