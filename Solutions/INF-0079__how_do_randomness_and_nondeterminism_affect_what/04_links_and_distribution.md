## Wider literature that bears on the derivation

1. **Computability versus complexity is the central level distinction.** The nondeterministic Turing machine article states the reach result directly: any problem solvable by a deterministic machine is solvable by a nondeterministic one and vice versa, while time may differ. The randomized algorithm article states the resource result: randomness is like a resource, and whether it changes polynomial-time power is the open P versus BPP question. These are not one question, and the figure's "closure" is compositional expressibility, not a resource-bounded oracle model. The mismatch is recorded as a vocabulary strain, not smoothed.
2. **Algorithmic randomness is already inside the figure's accounting.** Fig 5 :45 names "minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding". The CCC 2026 paper extends this to nondeterministic and probabilistic time-bounded Kolmogorov complexity. So this is not an outside analogue imported to make the answer look good; it is a low-overlap corroboration of the figure's own vocabulary.
3. **Prediction has a finite horizon even without chance.** The chaos article reports that systems can be fully deterministic and still unpredictable, with a horizon set by tolerated error, measurement accuracy and the Lyapunov time. This matches the figure's amplified-remainder mechanism and is independent of the framework's author.
4. **Ontic versus epistemic is not decidable by definition.** SEP separates chance from randomness and treats chaotic dynamics and classical indeterminism as distinct cases. This corroborates the framework's own `gap` verdict at Fig 13 :143-144 rather than overriding it.

## The two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1 is the residual and the refuter with no theory attached. Stage 2 is opt-in: if the checker is curious, the figure links follow. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). Stage 3 offers the formula as a generator, and each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it. Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the parent session's to do; an opt-in download page is preferable to an attachment pushed to a list.
