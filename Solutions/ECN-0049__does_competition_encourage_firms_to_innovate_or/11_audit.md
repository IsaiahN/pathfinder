## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before accepting the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the change in new settled terms at a firm-slot when the habitat condition changes. Arity: two conditions plus intersection, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), not a dichotomy. Symmetry: the relevant bond is comparison, ordered and threshold, so a pooled symmetric combination is the wrong operator. Scale: first order at the firm-slot, second order at industry composition.
- **Domain 1, the firm's innovation loop.** Actors: R&D team, budget, library Γ. Currency: settled terms priced in bits and actions (Fig 12 :125-127). Anchor: realized market purchases, which do not move when the firm reports R&D. Question type: partition of what composes inside the closure.
- **Domain 2, the market habitat.** Actors: rivals, entrants, customers, suppliers. Currency: profit, rent, survival. Anchor: realized transactions (Fig 2 :29). Competition is a relation (Fig 11 :47), not an actor or a stock. Question type: union, because pressure and shelter coexist in different slots of one firm.
- **Domain 3, the policy and antitrust frame.** Actors: regulators, courts. Currency: legal permission. Anchor: consumer welfare and independently measured innovation. Failure genus: a convention that decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47). Question type: a binding onto the habitat.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive R per slot, build the vocabulary for pressure and slack, state the objective, plan, carry. The policy question tries to set the plan before the vocabulary exists, work above the break (Fig 3 :19, :27).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-57 and the valence from Fig 11 :49-51, I derived the same boundaries from Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and Fig 12 :87 (ordered comparison). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :49-51 (residual-relative valence), Fig 4 :22-26 (head start not recording), Fig 12 :87 (threshold), Fig 13 :134 (gap as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: union, per-slot, valence-relative, head start not recording, threshold, gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy claim of the form "competition raises innovation, so lower concentration," or its mirror "rent funds R&D, so grant protection."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot indexing** collapses into a pooled policy effect (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- **the union and its intersection** collapse into an either/or lever (Fig 9 :54-57);
- **the head-start / recording boundary** collapses into a permanent right (Fig 4 :24-25, Fig 10 :47);
- **the gap itself** collapses into a number somebody invents (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a spendable difference routed through a method to a settled term is a structural requirement of innovation, and the sign of the effect is relative to the slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
