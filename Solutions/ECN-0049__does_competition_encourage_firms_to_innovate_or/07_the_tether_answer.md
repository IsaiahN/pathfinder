# The Tether's answer: competition, market power and innovation

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical intensity, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which condition reduces R at a firm-slot: rival pressure or a sheltered rent. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The two conditions are a union compound with an unassignable shared fraction (Fig 9 :54-57), not two answers to one question. Each has its own loop and its own anchor.

## 2. The shape

1. **Neither option wins globally; the effect is per slot.** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Competition is a habitat relation (Fig 11 :47), so the same pressure helps a neck-and-neck firm that can take the next step and hurts a laggard that cannot recover the catch-up cost. Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

2. **Shelter is a spendable difference, not a primitive.** Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"**; Fig 13 :45-47: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."** Rent can pay the action cost of a search, but under Fig 5 :33 (*"The three guards are a product, not a checklist."*) a bigger budget does not mint anything unless the live-residual, reachability and novelty guards all pass. Fig 5 :48: **"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."**

3. **The sign flip is a threshold, not a blend.** Fig 12 :87 / Operators :49: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**. Pressure and rent are compared; the result changes sign at a crossing. A symmetric sum would return exactly the pooled "competition helps somewhat" answer the figures refuse.

4. **"Temporary" is the whole policy variable.** Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :24: **"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."** A rent is legitimate while it is a head start that funds the next search; it becomes a recording when it defends one past success. Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**.

5. **Rivals are one import source, not the only one.** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** A sheltered firm can still import from science, suppliers and users. What permanent isolation removes is the surplus of differing closures (Fig 8 :45), and the count of competitors is not a proxy for differing closures (Fig 8 :47-49).

6. **The turning point is a `gap`, a verdict and not a silence.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**; Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**. No figure line returns an optimal intensity or a patent term.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal pro-competition or pro-monopoly verdict. Both are refused at the ground.
- Not an optimal concentration ratio, patent length or subsidy rate. That is a name, not a shape.
- Not that a monopolist cannot innovate. It can import from other differing frames.
- Not that R&D spending or patent counts settle the source. They are frame-internal readings (Fig 1 :24-27).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of firms was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator imposed from outside the firm's own reporting (the instrument owed in `05` section 4). Concretely:

- **Position test:** within one market, does the sign of the competition effect flip across distance-to-frontier classes while the innovation outcome is read independently (patent grants, measured TFP, product launches verified by customers)? Refuter 1 in `01` fires if it does not.
- **Union test:** find the intersection slots where shelter and pressure are simultaneously present. If the intersection is empty, Fig 9 :54-57 is broken.
- **Head-start test:** track a sheltered rent across several innovation cycles. If it keeps funding new searches with no entry threat and no other differing frame, Fig 4 :22-26 and Fig 6 :23 are broken.
- **Guard test:** vary the budget with residual, reachability and novelty held at zero. If settled terms still arrive, Fig 5 :33 is broken.
- **Import test (single-source figure claim):** a firm in contact with no differing frame mints a new primitive settled by the ground would break Fig 6 :23.

The world literature already contains partial versions of the first test (Aghion et al. 2014 laboratory result), but one evidence pool is one witness, not a ground settlement of the policy question.

## 5. The honest stop

The shape stops where the instruments stop. The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*, carried in `05`): declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The binding from frames/filters to firms/markets is a transfer, marked `specified` and listed as a strain in `01` section 8. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The numerical answer remains `unreachable-at-this-budget`, which is a verdict and not a silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the competition-versus-market-power argument. It says the question is a union of two conditions, not an either/or, and that the effect has to be read per firm-slot and never averaged: the same rival pressure helps a firm close to the frontier and hurts one too far behind to use it. Profit is a spendable difference that can fund a search, but only while it crosses as a head start that makes the next search cheaper, not as a recording of one past success that blocks the next one. The sign flip is a threshold comparison, not a blend, and the framework refuses to name the intensity at which it happens. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
