## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the set of properties any answer must have, stated before the number is known:

- per firm-slot, not pooled (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- a union of two conditions, not an either/or (Fig 9 :54-57);
- valence relative to the residual, so the same condition helps one slot and hurts another (Fig 11 :49-51);
- a threshold comparison, not a blend (Fig 12 :87);
- "temporary" is the head-start / recording boundary (Fig 4 :22-26);
- the turning point is a `gap`, a verdict and not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

A specific competition intensity, a specific patent term and a specific concentration threshold are **names**, not shapes. The framework does not return them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual is written as "what happens to new settled terms at a slot when pressure changes," not "the effect of Herfindahl index on patents."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* If the frame (industrial organization, antitrust law, evolutionary biology) is picked first, any of them will seem to explain the result.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here that means an independent reading of innovation, not a self-reported R&D figure.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52) with a source gate and a shadow test. The Brockhurst et al. result is such an import: different field, stated provenance, and it explains a gap in the naive competition story (section 4 of `01`).

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Naming "profit" or "stability" as the mechanism is a binding from economics onto held atoms (a spendable gradient and a maintained channel), not the invention of new primitives. Section 5b below narrows the rule accordingly.

## 4. Why a self-reported input cannot settle the shape

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here: a high R&D spend can be a response to rival pressure, a deployment of sheltered rent, a signal to investors, or an accounting relocation. A patent count can be a new settled term or a strategic blocking move. The reading (R&D, patents) does not comment on the source (pressure versus shelter). What is owed is a discriminator, an exogenous variation in one condition holding the other fixed, not a larger dataset of the same readings.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the mechanism here? ANSWERED with a correction

**What licenses a particular binding in the slot?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular binding (pressure is the driver, or rent is the driver) is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the binding is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, but it came close in the first pass. The first pass wrote "competition drives innovation" as a verdict. That is a binding stated as a figure result. It was retracted in `01` section 7 and replaced with a per-slot, union, valence-relative shape.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in §3. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. "Profit," "rent," "pressure" and "stability" are not missing primitives; they are bindings from economics onto the held set (gradient, comparison, channel, network). Naming them is therefore **not** forbidden. What remains wrong would be claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - this one adds none) or that the figure itself selected one binding over the other. My §3 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reason a self-reported input fails is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the competition-innovation question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The honest stop

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*, carried): declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The numerical intensity remains unclaimed.
