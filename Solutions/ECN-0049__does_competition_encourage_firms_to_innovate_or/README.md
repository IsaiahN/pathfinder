## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not a single dial with a right setting. It is what a change in a habitat relation does to the arrival rate of new settled terms at a particular firm-slot. In effect terms: a firm's library Γ predicts a market return Γ(b,a); the market returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is measured per firm-slot, not averaged across firms or across industries (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The two options are two conditions with different anchors:

- Option A, "competition drives innovation": rivals pressure the slot, so the predicted return fails and the firm must mint. Leads to: the sign of the effect is positive for slots whose closure already differs from the frontier, and negative for slots too far behind to catch up.
- Option B, "temporary market power funds research": a sheltered rent provides a spendable difference, so the firm can pay the action cost of a search that perfect competition would starve. Leads to: the sign of the effect is positive for slots that can convert slack into a new settled term, and negative where the rent becomes a permanent exclusion.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **shape** that (i) keeps the verdict per firm-slot and refuses a pooled average, (ii) treats the two options as a union of overlapping conditions rather than a partition, (iii) assigns valence relative to the residual (distance to the frontier and the kind of slack), and (iv) names the instrument that would discriminate a pressure failure from a slack failure. The exact numerical intensity at which the sign flips is not returned by any figure line and is recorded as a `gap`.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is: neither option wins globally. Competition is a habitat relation whose valence is residual-relative; pressure and shelter are two conditions in a union with different anchors; "temporary" is load-bearing and is the head-start/recording boundary. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles an antitrust or innovation-policy question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of firm innovation.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
