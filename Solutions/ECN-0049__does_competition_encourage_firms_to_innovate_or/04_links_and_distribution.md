## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The sign flips by position, not by ideology.** The experimental paper is the cleanest result: more competition increases R&D among neck-and-neck firms and decreases it among laggards, with a short horizon making the latter worse. That is exactly the valence-relative claim from Fig 11 :49, arrived at from a different instrument.
2. **The policy question is a union, not a dichotomy.** The inverted U means two mechanisms share one axis; it does not mean one of them is false. Fig 9 :57 governs.
3. **"Temporary" is doing real work.** The Schumpeterian account makes the rent a phase of a cycle. Fig 4 :22-26 turns that into a test: spendable head start versus replayed recording.
4. **The low-overlap biological witness narrows rather than flips.** Competition among similar rivals tends to character displacement, not endless escalation; antagonistic interaction with a differing counterpart sustains change. That is Fig 8 :47-49 and Fig 6 :23 in a different field, and it is the one world-level conflict encountered. It was returned to the census and resolved by narrowing "competition" to "contact with a differing closure."
