# Expert verification packet: the Tether on competitive strategy

**What this is:** a claim in a form a strategy or marketing scholar can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` for the shape, `specified` for any one firm. Not `grounded`.** No market measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a commercial result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The three options in the question are not one ranked list. Lower cost and differentiation compose inside the game whose rules are already accepted; changing the rules is the only move that can move that game's boundary. "Primary" is a budget decision made after per-customer-slot choices, not a verdict about which lever is true. The market, not the internal strategy process, settles which move paid, and a rule that decides the outcome has stopped being a rule and has become a verdict the firm authored. The framework returns this shape and a provenance test for any claimed new game; it does not name a winner for any particular firm.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1
- **Statement:** the three levers form a union with overlap, not a partition; "primary" averages away the per-slot signal and must be treated as a budget filter.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 — *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :60 — *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single global ranking of the three levers that predicts customer choice across all slots with no live residual in any slot.

### C2 — structural; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8, and 13
- **Statement:** cost and differentiation compose inside the current closure and cannot add a new basis of competition; rule change is the import, the only operator that moves the wall, and it is real only with provenance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 — *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 — *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :14 — *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 13 :115 — *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 8 :51 — *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*
- **Refuter:** show a firm escaping a saturated closure by cost or differentiation composition alone, with no new basis of competition and no provenanced import, and the market paying. Or show a "new game" that re-arranges only the old atoms, which makes it an isomer, not an import.

### C3 — ground and seat; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7, and 10
- **Statement:** the market is the anchor and settles the lever; internal agreement has zero verdict weight; a rule that decides the outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 — *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 7 :35 — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 10 :47 — *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*
- **Refuter:** show a lever choice settled by internal agreement alone, with no market reading, and the market paying anyway. Or show a firm that authors a rule and legitimately settles the outcome with no external anchor, with customers paying.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a global winner among the three levers, and not a winner for any named firm.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment or market reading.
- Not that Porter is refuted or that Blue Ocean Strategy is confirmed. World sources corroborate the figure-derived split; they never decide it.
- Not that changing the rules is automatically better. It is the only wall-moving operator, but it requires a source gate, a shadow test, and an external anchor.
- Not that the five-line proposal header or the outreach artifacts are part of the verdict. They are discipline, not evidence.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating market reading, not a further argument. In the source essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Per customer slot, does the chosen lever change contact with customer choice?
2. Is the claimed new game a real import, with a named new atom, a source, and a gap it explains, or a renamed old game?
3. Does the new rule get paid by an anchor the firm cannot move, or does it decide the outcome itself?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54 and :60, Fig 1 :19, Fig 6 :21 and :14, Fig 8 :32 and :51, Fig 2 :29, Fig 7 :35, Fig 10 :47. Nine short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: is there a case where cost or differentiation alone moved the basis of competition and the market paid? If yes, the import claim is refuted.
3. Name one instrument or reading that would discriminate a real new game from a renamed old one in their field, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
