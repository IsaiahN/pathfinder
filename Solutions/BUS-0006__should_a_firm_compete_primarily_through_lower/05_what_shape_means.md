## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the missing primitive cannot be named, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (essay 5, read this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0006 the shape is: *a per-slot, market-anchored ordering of moves in which within-game positions are distinguished from game change, and game change requires provenance and an external anchor.* Naming "cost leadership wins" would be a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (essay 5, read this session): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level source for F1 in `01`.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (essay 3, carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the ground is a market reading, customer choice, an experiment, or a counter. Fig 2 :29 names *"a market"* as an anchor.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Blue Ocean Strategy is such an outside frame: it already holds the distinction between a given game and a game whose rules await setting. It corroborates; it does not decide.

*They're Scanning Books* (essay 6, carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the vocabulary makes a choice visible; it does not make it neutral.

## 4. Why "the framework names the winner" would be the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay 7, carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several paths can create the same behaviour, so *"behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

Applied here: a firm's profit is a projection. Several strategies can produce the same profit, and several rule-changes can produce the same apparent "new game." Naming the source (this lever won because of X) from the reading (profit rose) is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator that separates the sources, not a confident name. *How Intelligence Actually Works* (essay 1, read this session) calls this the distinction horizon: *"There exists a 'distinction horizon' for every individual observer, the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another."* It adds the crucial limit: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay 2, carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* An internal strategy review cannot validate the strategy it produced.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a lever?

**No, but it constrains what the name can be.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the frame. "Cost leadership," "differentiation," and "changing the rules" are names already in the business vocabulary. Using them is a binding from the business frame onto the derivation, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is two different moves:

- inventing a seventh structure of competition that the derivation did not license (a name where a shape is owed);
- claiming that naming the lever settles which one pays (a verdict where only the market can settle).

I overstated this rule in an earlier draft of `01` and corrected it there. The correct record is: binding held terms is allowed; claiming a worldwide winner from the binding is not. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 2 :29).

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay 2, carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A well-known author or a famous firm is not a reason to accept a lever. Give the claim a budget to be tried; let the market settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the commercial claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
