## 4. Round trip and `R_T`

Fig 4 :27-32 requires the gap to be measured rather than assumed: *"Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought down will not match what was sent."*

- `T_A` (up): each census compressed to one line, *"per slot, market-anchored, import when the closure fails, with provenance on any new game."*
- `T_E` (down): that line expanded back into domain verdicts and figure lines.
- `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`: the round trip recovered all three verdict domains and the governing lines, but **lost** the Fig 12 isomer detail (same atoms, different bond) and the Fig 11 contact claim (an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing). Those are real losses, so `R_T` is non-zero but bounded: the missing content is arrangement and contact detail, not the verdict structure. It is recorded as a shape, not a fabricated number, consistent with Fig 4 :31-32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold."*
