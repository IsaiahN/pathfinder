## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **Interpretability is not one thing.** Lipton shows the field holds *"diverse and sometimes non-overlapping motivations for interpretability"* and competing notions of it. This corroborates the figure-derived split between legibility and ground contact, from an independent field.
2. **Confidence is not correctness.** Guo et al. show modern networks are poorly calibrated, so a model's own probability estimate is not the true correctness likelihood. This is the independent counterpart of Fig 1 :27: a frame-internal quantity cannot score the frame.
3. **An explanation can be independent of the model.** Adebayo et al. show some saliency maps are independent of both model and data. This is the independent counterpart of the metamerism point in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading does not determine its source.
4. **Reasoning traces are not faithful windows.** The Nature editorial says reasoning tokens are not accurate explanations of internal computations. This is a current, non-framework statement of the same limit.
5. **Overlap warning.** All five framework essays share an author with the figures. Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries little independent weight. The four independent sources above are the real outside witnesses, and they address different sub-claims, so they should not be pooled into one vote.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape claim and its refuter first. The full architecture is a recording, and a recording sent before anyone has described a residual cannot be checked.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :51** - *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. Do not tell the checker which model made the claim or how it was trained before the stage-1 verdict.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A mass send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue's prestige is a filter for where to submit; it is not a witness for the claim.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, which is permitted, never a finished answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: frames range against the ground separately. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded first and never pooled later (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem: `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` §2. Send the refuter, not a request for belief.

## Hosting

Nothing is uploaded or sent by this agent. Hosting the packet on an opt-in page (a repository release, a download page that lets a recipient decline contact) is closer to the figures' rule than an attachment pushed to a list. Keep the shape claim and the refuter in front of the links.
