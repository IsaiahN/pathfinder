## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The replay failure is real and general.** The Einstellung result is the same shape as Fig 4 :25: a recording of one occasion replayed downward. Warning people to pay attention often does not remove it; changing the task structure does. That matches Fig 3 :25-27, where the middle link's problem is an instrument problem, not an effort problem.
2. **The help condition is conditional, not absolute.** Transfer learning gives both outcomes under one vocabulary: positive transfer when source and target are related, negative transfer when the assumption fails. This is the figure's "method versus recording" split expressed in an engineering field: what crosses decides the outcome. It corroborates F5 and F1 without changing the verdict.
3. **The learning-set result is a method, not a stock.** Harlow and Medin show monkeys acquiring a procedure that makes subsequent new problems nearly errorless after one trial, even though the specific rewarded attributes change between problems. That is a generator that crosses, matching Fig 4 :22 and Formula step 6, not a stored answer replayed.
4. **Lock-in has a temporal signature.** The competency-trap literature predicts exactly the short-run gain, long-run self-destruction pattern: exploitation refines faster than exploration. Fig 7 :12 says the same in figure terms: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*

These four fields are low overlap with each other (human problem solving, machine learning, animal behaviour, organizational studies) and with the framework. They are four witnesses, not one witness quoted four times. Source overlap is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A cheap one-to-one refuter request is worth more than a large agreeable audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the verdict; (3) only then the three public framework links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula, "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a recipient fires one of the four refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, stop and narrow the claim before showing any theory.
