## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 10 :24 (channel/ground), Fig 11 :50 (valence relative to residual), Fig 8 :45 and :50 (convergent derivation).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` and `07`: four conditions, per slot, same actor with different valence, no number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete case description: a government crackdown in one country, a fall in visible protest, a later opposition coalition, a claim that the crackdown caused one or the other.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor test**, that the thing the state and the opposition both range against must not update on either side's reports. A case narrative usually names the government's own account of stability as the reference, which is a frame, not an anchor;
- the **source gate and shadow test** on the new shared atom (Fig 6 :12-14), because a protest event cited as the cause of coalition formation is often adopted from the same news pool as the coalition, so convergent derivation and adopted import are indistinguishable in the contents (Fig 8 :51);
- the **per-slot indexing**, because a case narrative reports a national total and reads the sign from it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the same event can have different valence for different residuals, and the two outcomes are different bond arrangements over held conditions. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
