## 3. Overlap declaration

The four political-science and sociology sources are **one broad evidence pool with internal differences**, not four independent witnesses: deMeritt, Honari and Earl et al. all review or cite the same repression literature (Lichbach, Davenport, Opp and Roehl, Rasler, Francisco). Bartusevicius et al. is the most distinct of the four because it contributes original survey and experimental data at the individual level. The shoaling and reactance sources are genuinely low-overlap corroborators of the *shape* of the mechanism (threat increases grouping, restriction increases counter-assertion), not of the political finding. The four framework essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.
