# Expert verification packet — the Tether on repression and collective action

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher of repression and resistance can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator, `general` on the union shape, not `grounded`.** No country verdict is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question's deterrence/unification binary is a union of overlapping conditions, not two outcomes of one dial. The separating conditions are: (1) the channel to a ground that does not update on the state's reports; (2) whether the opposition can still compose an action after the intervention; (3) overlap between the frames the repression touches; (4) whether the state's legitimacy claim is corrected by anything outside itself. Repression deters where the channel decays, the opposition stays one room, frames already share an evidence pool, and the anchor updates on the state's own reports. It unifies where the same event becomes a ground-visible atom that low-overlap frames each range against, so their coalition is triangulation rather than negotiation. The same actor has different valence for different residuals, so verdicts are per group and per time slice and are never averaged. The universal severity number is a declared gap. Over a fixed habitat the two outcomes are isomers, same atoms with a different bond.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — The binary is a union, so no single lever or severity number can settle it

- **Statement:** the cases where repression deters and the cases where it unifies are overlapping conditions. A single lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57, *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** in cases you know, can you find both effects under the same severity, differing only in channel state, frame overlap or anchor? If the two effects separate cleanly by severity alone with those conditions fixed, the union claim is wrong.
- **Refuter:** a within-slot, time-resolved case in which the same event drives action in one direction while all four conditions are held fixed.

### C2 — Valence is relative to the residual, so no standing verdict attaches to repression

- **Statement:** the same repression actor is a cost for one group and a shared grievance for another. The difference is in the residual and the anchor, not in the event.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :50, *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** does a single national or cross-national coefficient hide two opposite per-slot effects? The experimental literature reports a positive overall association with wide between-sample variation (43 of 64 samples for intentions, 63 of 101 for self-reports, in Bartusevicius et al. 2023). Is that variation noise, or is it the signal the claim says it is?
- **Refuter:** a case where the same event has the same valence for two groups with different residuals and different anchors, measured per slot.

### C3 — The channel and the anchor, not the expressed emotion, decide the effect

- **Statement:** deterrence is channel closure with a non-updating grievance behind it; unification needs a new ground-visible atom that frames can hold by convergent derivation. Anger and fear are currencies inside a frame, and they move when framing moves, so they cannot be the anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26, *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 2 :33, *"The anchor must not update."*; Fig 8 :50, *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*
- **What to check:** was the ground measured independently of both the state's report and the opposition's narrative? Is channel state distinguished from organizational reach? If the same observed quiet supports both "deterred" and "latent", the instrument cannot discriminate and the claim stays `candidate`.
- **Refuter:** a case where the ground settles the grievance itself, measured by an anchor that does not update on the state's reports, and the opposition response is unchanged; or a case where frames sharing one evidence pool produce equally strong triangulation.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a severity threshold, probability or country verdict. That is a `gap`, not silence.
- Not "repression always backfires" and not "repression works". The verdict is conditional and per slot.
- Not that anger, fear, anger-management or identity is *the* mechanism. World sources predict these; the figures do not name them.
- Not that silence is deterrence. Successful preemptive repression leaves neither repression nor dissent observed.
- Not that a state can subtract a grievance. Introduction is available, exclusion is not; isolation substitutes a habitat.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not an isomer claim without a fixed habitat. If the unification case imports an atom the deterrence case lacks, the relation is composition plus import instead.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not an argument. Per *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): a reading cannot name its source without an instrument that distinguishes the paths that produce it. The needed design measures per slot and per time slice, fixes an anchor neither side can move, separates channel from reach, records frame overlap, and handles censored observations. Six pre-registered refuters are listed in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 and `07_the_tether_answer.md` §6: union collapse, ground moved, independence broken, threshold derived, valence invariant, observability cleared.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55 and :57, Fig 1 :19, Fig 11 :50 and :51, Fig 10 :24, Fig 2 :33 and Fig 8 :50. Eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own cases. Can you name one group for whom the same repression was a cost and another for whom it was a shared grievance? If not, say so; that is a failure.
3. For one case you know well, name the thing that did not move when the state and the opposition disagreed. If you cannot, the anchor is missing and the case cannot settle the claim.
4. Say which of the six refuters you would try first, and why. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
