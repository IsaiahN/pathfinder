## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit, from the figures README (local copy opened): the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway."*

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0067 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the specific coefficient or country verdict is known. "A union of four conditions, indexed per group and per time slice, with valence relative to the residual" is a shape. "Repression above severity 0.7 deters" is a name, and the figures do not return it. The distinction matters operationally: the first can be checked by a researcher in their own cases; the second would have to be imported from a ground that has not been shown.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

This is the essay-level source for the union verdict in F1, and it is independent of the figure-level source (Fig 9 :55-57) in wording though not in author.

Applied here: the question's ordinary terms ("deter", "unify") were treated as borrowed words. They were recast in effect terms: channel reachable or decayed, closure composable or broken, frames low-overlap or one pool, legitimacy claim updating or not. That is the vocabulary that narrowed the search.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For INS-0067 the needed instrument is a per-slot, time-resolved design with an anchor that does not update on the state's reports.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. If a specific field finding (for example the reactance result) is used to name a mechanism, that is a binding from the psychology frame and must be recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (OPENED) gives the failure mode. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"; and the case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument". For this question, an observed fall in visible protest is the green reading. Fear, fatigue, latent organization and successful preemptive repression are several possible sources. Naming "fear" from the fall alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 4. The correction to the "shape, not a name" reading, kept visible

I initially wrote this section as if the rule forbade naming any mechanism in the separator. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The conditions used here, channel, anchor, overlap and valence, are already held terms in the figures (Fig 10 :24, Fig 2 :29/:31, Fig 11 :49). Naming a specific world mechanism such as reactance or cross-sector coalition formation is a **binding** from another frame onto held terms, not the invention of a seventh substrate.

What remains wrong in any reading that names a mechanism without provenance is the metamerism point in §3: the source is named from a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Both claims are now recorded. The corrected form of the answer stays at the level of the four-condition separator; particular mechanisms are admitted only with author, venue, year and a statement of what they predict here.

## 5. Why this question resists a number

*The Library of Babel* (OPENED) says that the search ends when the answer is reached or when "the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed", and that a bundle must be split. Fig 9 :4-5 says the same at figure level: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The repression literature's contradictory findings are exactly this signature: each theory works on a different subset. So the inability to return a severity number is not a failure of search effort. It is the finding.

Fig 13 :134 is explicit: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the next line adds "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The missing thing here is an instrument, a discriminator between demobilization, latent deterrence and tactical shift. Its shape is describable: per-slot, time-resolved, with an independently fixed anchor. Its name is not.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For outreach this means a list of distinguished signatories is not a check on the separator. It gives the claim a budget to be tried, nothing more. Fig 2 :40 and the accessibility rule agree: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. The first contact therefore asks what would fail, never what would be believed.

## 8. The one-paragraph version of "shape" for this question

A shape here is the four-condition separator that can be stated before any country coefficient: channel to a non-updating ground, reachability of the opposition's composition, overlap between the frames touched, and whether the state's legitimacy claim updates on its own reports. It constrains what a candidate answer must look like, and it refuses to fill the severity slot. A number there would be a name, and names require a ground or a provenanced import. The discriminator that would license one is owed and is described in effect terms in `07` and `08`, not invented here.
