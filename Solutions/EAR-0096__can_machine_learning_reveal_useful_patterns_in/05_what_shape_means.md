## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "ML can narrow a candidate set of regularities that must be ranged against a ground" is a shape. "This model has discovered the causal driver of variable X" is a name, and is not licensed by the derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the operational form of the guard: a predicted signature that anyone can check.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: returned books *"need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search"* and *"the finish line will also be the starting place."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument-level reason. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several paths can create the same reading, so *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a correlation reads "X and Y move together" and cannot comment on the source. Declaring the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (causal discovery, intervention, an independent measurement), not a name.

## 4. Why the residual must be described first

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the failure directly: *"Projection is the trap in Fig 9 :27-28: 'describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit.'"* And on shared evidence: *"Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* That is F2 of the audit, stated in the essay's own terms. Vigen's spurious-correlations gallery (independent world source) is the mechanical demonstration of the same trap.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a pattern found by ML?

**No, with a condition.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. An ML pattern is not a candidate primitive of the six-term set; it is a candidate regularity over data. Naming the pattern (for example, "a warm-season teleconnection between region A and index B") is like naming a molecule that composes from held atoms, not inventing a seventh kind. What is forbidden is naming the *source* or *mechanism* from the reading alone, or claiming the pattern has been settled by the ground when only the frame has paid for it.

So: pattern name, allowed with provenance. Causal name, requires a source-discriminating instrument. Verdict of usefulness, requires the ground. Recorded both ways here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that the pattern is real is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an instrument or an observation, settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any ML pattern and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 8. Two consequences for this question

1. **The capability half is `general`, the guard half is `specified`.** That ML can narrow a shape is a structural claim from Fig 9 :32 and the README. That a particular guard fires in a particular dataset is a `specified` claim needing a ground reading.
2. **The honest stop is a recorded gap, not a silence.** No named pattern is asserted useful in this folder. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of a misleading correlation in Earth-system decision-making is high, so independence is worth buying. Until it is bought, the answer stays `candidate`.
