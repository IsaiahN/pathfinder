## The question restated as an effect

Decide what follows from each option:

- **Option A, strong reading:** ML delivers causal knowledge of the Earth system directly from data. What it leads to: a model output treated as a verdict, with the frame's own metrics standing in for the ground, and agreement among correlated models counted as confirmation.
- **Option B, figure-grounded reading:** ML narrows a shape, a candidate set of regularities that must still be ranged against an independent ground. What it leads to: a checkable output, refuters written in advance, and guards that keep correlation from being promoted to a verdict.

An answer would look like: a statement of what ML can return (a shape, not a causal name), plus a list of guards each traceable to a verbatim figure line, plus the instrument that would have to pay before any pattern is called useful.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A list of people selected for receptiveness cannot corroborate a model output.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients who already agree manufactures the exact correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
