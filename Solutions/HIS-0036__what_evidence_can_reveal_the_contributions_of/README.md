## The question restated as an effect

A contribution was made, a route through a research space was actually taken, but the record naming who took it is missing or was reassigned. Three options:

- **A:** read the published artefact and infer the author from it. This leads to a source-discrimination error: more than one route can produce the same artefact.
- **B:** reconstruct the lineage from surviving traces that cross a frame boundary (a dated document held outside the crediting institution). This leads to an attributed contribution with a stated refuter.
- **C:** report `unreachable` where no trace survives. This leads to an honest stop instead of a manufactured credit.

An answer would look like a *shape*: a kind of evidence (lineage-bearing, independently held) with a refuter, not a list of names. The shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that a contribution was erased.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless. The verdict belongs to a surviving trace checked by a frame that did not inherit the same archive, not to a number of historians who already agree.
