## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 adds: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For HIS-0036 a shape is therefore:

> lineage-bearing evidence, held in a closure different from the one that assigned the credit, whose presence would change the attribution and whose absence is reported as `unreachable` rather than as no contribution.

That constrains which evidence counts. It does not name a person, and it does not name a specific document in a specific archive. "A dated letter held outside the institution" is a shape. "The Pauline Cowan letter at Churchill College" is a ground finding, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap by proximity. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the closest available terms (credit, byline, citation, archive, custody) and let recurring new terms (notebook, lab record, seminar note, grant file, instrument log) narrow the search.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Say "a trace that would have to exist if the work had been done this way" before saying "the missing letter".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach in `02_outreach_email.md` sends the shape and its refuter, not an archive tour.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". The search ends when the residual stops registering, not when a satisfying name turns up.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For attribution, that means a trace actually located and checked.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6, Fig 8), with a source gate and declared overlap.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* gives the load-bearing distinction: "A pile of human reasoning's outputs is not an engine." A publication list is outputs. The person who generated them is not recoverable by counting the outputs; you need the lineage.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the positive and negative cases side by side. Negative: a video frame and a photograph "are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." Positive, for lab-grown versus mined diamonds: "The tool only works because a trace existed, and not because a better tool was created." So a name is licensed by a trace plus an instrument that can read it, never by the artefact alone.

## 4. Why the artefact-reading approach is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism in the colour case: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Green could be one light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. What resolved the case was "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument" (the spectrometer).

Reading a 1953 paper and inferring who contributed is the same metamerism error. The paper's byline is a reading; the route that produced it is the source. Several routes can print the same byline. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources (a dated external record), not a further reading of the byline.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* sharpens why the record is thin: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext". Most of what made the contribution possible never entered the published text. Fig 12 :107: "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."

## 5. Q: can the shape name a kind of evidence? ANSWERED with a correction

**Earlier reading (now narrowed):** I first wrote this as "the framework returns a shape, so it cannot say what evidence would reveal the contribution." That was an overstatement.

**Correction (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1):** the README's phrase governs a *missing primitive*, one not held in the library. The evidence classes here (documents, custody chains, dated traces) are already ordinary terms in the historian's library. Naming a class of evidence is a binding onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What the shape genuinely cannot do is name *which person* a given trace belongs to before the ground settles it.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The narrow claim is the one used in `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

## 6. Two further consequences

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous erased scientists is a funding argument, not evidence. Elsewhere in the same essay the author describes proving independent derivation by a different route: "I could prove I had not copied her because my paper trail was different, and my vocabulary was different." That is the attributed version of Fig 8 :50-51, and it is the method this question needs.

**A record must be kept of the record.** Same essay: "This essay is my provenance record. It is proof of the process of how I came about this through many fields of reasoning and research." If the person doing the reattribution does not record their own route, their correction becomes another unprovenanced claim.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any attribution claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. The honest stop

*How Intelligence Actually Works* names two horizons. A "distinction horizon" is where one observer cannot tell two routes apart; a "union horizon" is where no observer with any available instrument can. Crucially: "is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet. Which is why provenance must be recorded rather than trusted to depth." And for the empty case: "can never be confirmed by better instruments. A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach."

This matches Fig 8 :39 ("So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence") and Fig 13 :134 ("Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"). The answer to HIS-0036 stops at the limit of what surviving traces can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. It does not manufacture a culprit when the trace is gone.
