## 3. Overlap warning (read before counting witnesses)

These sources are not independent in the strong sense. IPCC AR6 Chapter 9 synthesizes the same literature that Garbe 2020, Schaefer 2014 and Cui 2025 draw on. Schuster 2025 cites Garbe 2020 and the Global Tipping Points report. Stroeve and Notz 2018 and the IPCC sea-ice section draw on the same observational record and the same Notz and Stroeve lineage. Mathiot 2026 cites Garbe 2020 and Reese et al. Agreement among them is one pool seen from several angles, not several witnesses. Fig 2 :40 applies: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The verdict still comes from the figures; the world sources only corroborate the shape and supply the numbers.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the three-class shape and its refuter. Send the links only after the reader records a verdict on stage 1.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the asymmetry claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Select venues by ground contact, never by likely agreement.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Contact cost and venue size decide where to spend attention, never what counts as true.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the three-class shape and the four refuters. Ask one question: what observation would you count as a failure of this shape? Record the answer before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer three links: the IPCC chapter, the Antarctic hysteresis paper, and the permafrost stabilization paper. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
