## What an answer would look like

Restate the question as an effect. A settled model (ΛCDM) is held against the ground. An observation arrives. Either it is re-absorbed by re-fitting inside the current closure, or it forces a new term (revision), or it removes the standing of a held term (abandonment). The answer is a classification of observations by which of those three they produce, with a diagnostic that tells the three apart. Bind that classification as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused (formula step "Direction"), rather than leaving it as an unexamined preference for a particular new physics.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that ΛCDM is in trouble.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: `Fig 2 :35` "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (`Fig 2 :40`: agreement from a shared evidence pool is not a verdict). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework interior (`Fig 10 :52`: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."). A reply to a Track B style message would be a conversation, never corroboration.

## Honest standing

Every substantive file is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* essay, opened 2026-11-23, says LLM output can "simulate an approximate answer/output" while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This package therefore cannot settle the question it reports on. It reports the shape of an answer and the refuters that would have to fire before the community's verdict moves.
