# The Tether's answer: what evidence would move the standard cosmological model

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the operator-class claims, `candidate` and ungrounded on every named example. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## 1. The residual it answers

The scientific community holds ΛCDM as a settled term paid for by the ground: CMB acoustic peaks, BBN abundances, large-scale structure, supernova acceleration. The question asks which observations would move it. The trap is to answer with a list of anomalies. An anomaly whose residual shrinks under a re-fit is rebinding; the model has absorbed it and nothing is owed. The answer has to distinguish that from a genuine mint (revision) and a refusal (abandonment).

## 2. The answer in six tests

1. **Persistence, not surprise.** `Fig 5 :59` (verbatim): *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. A single counter-instance is rebinding's material; the SEP Popper entry agrees that in practice one conflicting instance is never sufficient. The first test is whether the residual refuses to shrink as the fit improves.

2. **Independence, not accumulation.** `Fig 2 :31` (verbatim): *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* More data from the same instruments reduced with the same calibration explores the same closure (`Fig 8 :32`: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."*). The Hubble tension matters because it opposes determinations from genuinely different physics and different telescopes, and because the discrepancy grew as errors shrank (Wikipedia Hubble tension). If the two sides share a calibration, the disagreement is one frame talking to itself.

3. **Split before you search.** `Fig 9 :4-5` (verbatim): *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The most conservative reading of a tension is a systematic in a subset, not a new force. Only after the split fails is one residual named.

4. **A source-discriminating instrument, built from one already returning something.** `Fig 6 :32` (verbatim): *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* `Fig 2 :29`: the anchor *"does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The high-redshift galaxy episode is the worked case: photometric candidates challenged cosmology, then spectroscopy showed AGN contamination and overestimated masses, and the confirmed objects matched pre-JWST ΛCDM simulations (Wikipedia Lambda-CDM). A better instrument, not an argument, closed it.

5. **The replacement must pay.** `Fig 5 :43-44` (verbatim): *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* A proposal that fixes one tension while breaking the acoustic peaks, BBN and lensing has not paid. Wikipedia Lambda-CDM records that dozens of proposals exist and *"None of these models can simultaneously explain the breadth of other cosmological data as well as ΛCDM."* The SEP Scientific Revolutions entry quotes Popper's requirement that a new theory *"must always be able to explain fully the success of its predecessor."*

6. **Revision and abandonment are different operators.** `Fig 5 :55` (verbatim): *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Revision mints a molecule inside the closure; abandonment negates a held term. A vocabulary that can only accumulate parameters can revise indefinitely and never abandon (Operators table: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*).

**Ordering.** Dependency order from `Fig 3 :20-21`: persistence first, independence second, split third, discriminator fourth, replacement bargain fifth, community verdict last. Optimising telescopes cannot rescue a missing criterion for progress (`Fig 3 :19`).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- It does not name the new physics. The figures return a shape, not a name (`Fig 9 :32`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*).
- It does not say the Hubble tension is already decisive. It is a union candidate until the split is run.
- It does not say ΛCDM is false, final, or uniquely best. It is a settled term with no last room (`Fig 7 :6`: *"the limit: no last room"*).
- It does not let consensus, prestige or elegance decide. `Fig 7 :13`: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; `Fig 9 :60`: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."*
- It is not `grounded`. No measurement here moved a verdict.

## 4. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement (`Fig 9 :42`: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*):

- A source-discriminating instrument that separates a genuine model-level residual from a calibration systematic, described in effect terms (F3).
- A replacement model that fits the full observation suite at lower statement cost and is adopted because the ground paid for it (F5).
- A case that fires any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, which would break the classification in the named part.
- For abandonment specifically: a term that refuses a held model's standing to be composed over, with the fall in reach computed from the record rather than stipulated (`Fig 6 :42-46`).

## 5. The honest stop

The answer is a reached stop at this budget, not a proof of absence (`Fig 8 :39`: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*). The discriminator that would separate early- and late-universe systematics is described in effect terms only; no source read for this solution names one that settles the tension. Any imported new-physics atom is debited against the independence of the frame that supplied it (`Fig 6 :24`: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."*). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* (`Fig 13 :134`)

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A surprising observation is not the same as a model-changing one. Most anomalies are re-absorbed by re-fitting, and ΛCDM has done this before. What would actually move a settled cosmological model is an observation whose residual persists and does not shrink as the fit improves, that comes from instruments drawing on genuinely different evidence, that survives a better instrument built on the same signal, and whose replacement still explains everything the old model explained. Before new physics is named, the disagreement should be split to see whether it is several systematic-limited questions rather than one. And abandoning a model is not the same act as revising it: revision adds a term inside the current framework, while abandonment refuses a term the framework was built on. The framework returns that ordered checklist of evidence kinds, not the name of the theory that would win. It is a candidate shape, written with refuters in advance, and it stops at the limit of the instruments available.
