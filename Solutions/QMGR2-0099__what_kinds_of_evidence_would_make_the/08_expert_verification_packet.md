# Expert verification packet: what evidence would move ΛCDM

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the operator-class claim, `candidate` and ungrounded on the named examples. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to have moved anyone's verdict. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A settled cosmological model is moved by observations with four properties, ordered by dependency: the residual persists and does not trend down under re-fitting; the disagreeing determinations draw on independent evidence rather than a shared calibration; the discrepancy survives a source-discriminating instrument; and a replacement fits the full suite at lower statement cost. Before any of that is read as new physics, an effort-resistant disagreement must be split to see whether it is several well-formed questions. "Substantially revise" and "abandon" are different operations: revision mints a term inside the closure, abandonment refuses a held term and lowers the reach. The framework returns this ordered checklist of evidence kinds, not the name of a winning theory.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 5 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** re-binding is not revision. The discriminator between them is a residual that persists and does not trend down, and the frames that disagree must be independent.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - `Fig 5 :59` — *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*
  - `Fig 2 :31` — *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
  - `Fig 2 :40` — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
  - `Fig 8 :32` — *"no search, however long, adds an atom."*
- **Refuter:** show a case where the community treated an observation as model-changing although the residual trended down under an in-closure re-fit, or where the two disagreeing determinations shared a calibration so independence was zero.

### C2 — Physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the public record

- **Statement:** the Hubble tension is a candidate union compound, not a single decisive lever, and the high-redshift galaxy anomaly is an example of a candidate that dissolved under a better instrument rather than requiring new physics.
- **What to check:** Wikipedia Hubble tension reports that the estimated uncertainties shrank while the discrepancy did not, and that the most conservative proposed resolution is an unknown systematic, with new physics as one further option among many. Wikipedia Lambda-CDM reports that early JWST photometric candidate massive galaxies were found, most were AGN whose brightness caused overestimated masses, and confirmed objects are consistent with pre-JWST ΛCDM simulations. Are those descriptions faithful? Is the independence of early- and late-universe probes as claimed?
- **Figure bearing (secondary to the physics check):** `Fig 9 :4-5` — *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* And `Fig 6 :32` — *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **Refuter:** show that the disagreement shrinks when the data are partitioned by instrument or calibration, yet the community still demanded new physics; or produce a candidate anomaly that survived a source-discriminating instrument while the model stood unchanged.

### C3 — Framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** "abandon" and "substantially revise" are different operator classes, not isomers on one scale. Revision is a binary-bond mint inside closure; abandonment is a unary negation that removes a held term's standing to be composed over.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - `Fig 5 :55` — *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*
  - `Fig 12 :49` — *"The seven bonds, and whether the order of the operands matters. Negation is the eighth operator and is not a bond."*
  - Operators table — *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
- **Refuter:** show that refusing a held term is expressible as a binary bond between two operands so the six symmetry tests apply, or show a term and its refusal both settled with the reach unchanged. Then the operator-class distinction fails.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that ΛCDM is false, final, or uniquely best. No last room (`Fig 7 :6`).
- Not that the Hubble tension is already decisive. A union candidate until the split is run.
- Not a named new particle, not a named cause of any tension. The figures return a shape, not a name (`Fig 9 :32`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*).
- Not that consensus, prestige or elegance settles anything (`Fig 7 :13`: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; `Fig 9 :60`: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating instrument, described in effect terms, that separates a model-level residual from a calibration systematic.
2. A replacement that fits the full suite at lower statement cost and is adopted because the ground paid for it (`Fig 5 :42-44`).
3. Any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 firing, which breaks the classification in the named part.
4. For the abandonment claim specifically: a term that refuses a held model's standing, with the fall in reach computed and recorded (`Fig 6 :42-46`).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read `Fig 5 :59`, `Fig 2 :31`, `Fig 8 :32`, `Fig 9 :4-5`, `Fig 6 :32`, `Fig 5 :55` and `Fig 12 :49`. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: is the persistence-under-shrinking-errors description of the Hubble tension accurate, and is the JWST high-redshift sequence accurate?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a systematic from a model-level residual at the ground, or say why none is available. An effect description is enough; no name of new physics is requested.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
