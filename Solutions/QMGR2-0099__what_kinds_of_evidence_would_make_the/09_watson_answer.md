## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what kind of evidence would move a settled cosmological model. Option A: a list of candidate anomalies or a named new physics. Option B: an ordered checklist of evidence properties with a persistence diagnostic, an independence requirement, a source-discriminating instrument and a replacement bargain, with abandonment kept distinct from revision. What each leads to: A mistakes rebinding for revision and produces a recording of past surprises; B produces a generator that can be refuted in advance and that returns a kind of discriminator, never a name.

- **Domain 1, model frame.** Actors: the six parameters, the cosmological principle, the component inventory. Currency: fit quality and parameter adjustment. Anchor: the ground of observations, never internal coherence. Question type: partition (rebinding, mint or refusal).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

What moves a settled cosmological model is an observation whose residual persists and does not trend down under re-fitting, that comes from frames drawing on independent evidence, that survives a source-discriminating instrument grown from a signal already returning, and whose replacement pays for the observations the old model explained. An effort-resistant disagreement is split into well-formed questions before new physics is named. Substantial revision mints a term inside the closure; abandonment negates a held term and lowers the reach. The framework returns this ordered checklist of evidence kinds, never the name of a winning theory. All named cosmological examples are world bindings with provenance, not figure derivations. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The classification is written with six pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 and three checkable sub-claims in `08`, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording of anomalies instead of the diagnostic generator (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the persistence test and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The named bindings (Hubble tension as decisive, high-z galaxies as a threat) are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates a model-level residual from a calibration systematic would close the named gap; a replacement fitting the full suite at lower cost and adopted because the ground paid for it would move a revision from candidate to settled; a case firing any of the six refuters would break the classification in the named part; a term and its refusal both settled with the reach unchanged would break the operator-class claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
