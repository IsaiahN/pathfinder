## 0. The residual under audit

> What kinds of evidence would make the scientific community abandon or substantially revise the standard cosmological model (ΛCDM)?

Stated in effect terms. ΛCDM is a **settled molecule**: decades of measurement paid for it (CMB acoustic peaks, BBN light elements, large-scale structure, supernova acceleration). It is an atom at the next level of cosmology. An observation arrives as a residual `R` between prediction and measurement. Three outcomes are possible:

1. **Rebinding.** The residual is absorbed by adjusting a parameter or adding an in-closure extension. Nothing is owed beyond a re-fit. The model is unchanged in kind.
2. **Revision (a mint).** A new term is owed that composes from what the frame already holds. The reach grows. This is "substantially revise".
3. **Abandonment (a refusal).** A term held is false. What is owed is a negation, a term that refuses another, not an explanation. The reach *falls* because something was added. This is "abandon".

The question asks for the evidence classes that select outcome 2 or 3 over outcome 1. The answer cannot be "any surprising datum", because surprising data are rebinding's native material. The diagnostic must be sharper.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula "Direction"): a **structured checklist** of observation properties, ordered by dependency, such that an observation passing the checklist forces a mint or a refusal and cannot be quietly re-fit. The checklist is the sought-for shape. Writing it down lets it accrue a residual like anything else, so it is refusable.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

Describe R by arity, symmetry and scale. Then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

**R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: at least two conditions, because "the standard model" is a compound of the cosmological principle, GR on cosmological scales, the six-parameter fit and the component inventory (Λ, CDM, baryons). Symmetry: a tension is a comparison between two determinations, not a symmetric join; the comparison bond ⋛ is ordered (Operators table: "a threshold relation between two quantities"). Scale: first order inside each probe, second order at the model that mediates between probes. Fig 9 :4-5 says a disagreement that will not shrink under effort is not one hard question but several well-formed ones, so this is a **union compound** until the split is run, not a single lever.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition / union | dependency order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Model frame | the six parameters, the cosmological principle, GR, Λ, CDM | fit quality; parameter adjustments | the ground (observations), never internal coherence | union of at least four conditions | after D2; a model is revised only against a residual that arrived |
| D2 Instrument / observation frame | CMB, distance ladder, BAO, BBN, weak lensing, JWST high-z galaxies | measurements with uncertainties | the ground; instruments described as "already returning something" (Fig 6 :34) | partition by probe, union by shared calibration | first: perception and instrument |
| D3 Community / verification frame | researchers, referees, editors, collaborations | agreement and residuals | the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45) | partition of frames; collapses if they share a pool | last: the verdict is recorded after the ground moves |
| D4 Philosophy-of-science frame (world, secondary) | theories, paradigms, conventionalist stratagems | empirical content, explanatory success | the same ground (observations) | low overlap with the figures by author and field | corroborates, never decides |

Dependency order (Fig 3 :20-21): an observation must first register on an instrument already returning something (D2), then be named as rebinding / mint / refusal (D1 vocabulary), then count as progress by a residual that does not trend down (D1 objective), then aim the next probe (D2 planning), then be carried into the community record (D3). The chain usually breaks at link 3, "what would count as progress here", because that link has neither obvious failure nor obvious test (Fig 3 :25-27). That is where the answer to this question actually lives.

Strain test before selecting lines: a line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity. Two candidate strains were caught and re-censused (section 7): a first attempt to map the cosmological principle onto the six-term substrate, and a first attempt to read Fig 8 :32 as a claim that accumulating same-kind data must eventually add new physics.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 — One anomaly is rebinding, not revision (decisive for the diagnostic)

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2, quote attributed to the formula (not a figure line):

> BROKEN · rebinding — re-fit, do not mint
> REFUTED — a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation

`Fig 5 :10-14` (verbatim extract) names the four bins: *"held: the model was right, nothing owed"*, *"new: extend perception, aim the probe here"*, *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"*, *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. The fifth kind is added at `Fig 5 :53`:

> **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**

The discriminator is the shrinking test read backwards, `Fig 5 :59`:

> **"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"**

World corroboration (low overlap, opened 2026-11-23): the SEP entry on Karl Popper (Stephen Thornton, 1997, rev. 2026) notes that in practice *"a single conflicting or counter-instance is never sufficient methodologically for falsification"*, and the SEP entry on Scientific Revolutions (Thomas Nickles, 2009, rev. 2017) reports Kuhn's criterion: *"If and when persistent efforts by the best researchers fail to resolve the anomalies, the community begins to lose confidence in the paradigm and a crisis period ensues."* These are secondary witnesses from philosophy of science, not figure lines. They predict a persistence requirement and corroborate Fig 5 :59. Overlap between the two SEP entries is moderate (same encyclopedia, different authors and entries), so they count as nearly one witness here.

ΛCDM's history is the rebinding case in the world literature: the Wikipedia Lambda-CDM entry states that *"dark matter was added after the observations of anomalous galaxy rotation rates"* under the conventionalism charge. That is a re-fit that earned its standing by the full-data fit, not a single anomaly by itself.

**Refuter:** show a case where a single observation, whose residual trends down under an in-closure re-fit and owes no new term, was nevertheless treated by the community as a model-changing revision. Then F1 fails.

### F2 — Independence, not accumulation, is what makes the residual count

`Fig 2 :31` (verbatim):

> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**

`Fig 2 :40` (verbatim):

> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

`Fig 8 :32`:

> **"no search, however long, adds an atom."**

So more observations of the same kind, reduced with the same calibration and the same model, explore the same closure and cannot move it (`Fig 6 :49`: "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change."). What moves the verdict is a disagreement between frames whose closures differ, with the disagreement surviving an independence audit.

World corroboration: the Wikipedia Hubble tension entry (opened 2026-11-23) reports that *"as techniques have improved, the estimated measurement uncertainties have shrunk, but the discrepancies have not, to the point that the disagreement is now highly statistically significant."* It also lists independent late-universe methods (distance ladder, TRGB, megamasers, time-delay cosmography, kilonova) and early-universe methods (CMB, BAO), and states that *"both the early-universe and late-universe observations come from several different telescopes."* This is precisely a residual that persists while shrinking tests fail, and a low-overlap frame structure. Corroborates F2. Overlap with the philosophy sources is low: different field, different authors.

**Refuter:** show that the two disagreeing determinations draw on a shared calibration or a common systematic, so their disagreement is one frame talking to itself and the independence is zero. Then F2 does not apply to that case.

### F3 — The observation must be an anchor, and instruments grow from instruments

`Fig 2 :29`:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

`Fig 6 :32`:

> **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

`Fig 6 :34`:

> **"It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**

A candidate anomaly is only evidence if it is a reading from something already returning signal, and if it survives a better instrument built on it. A photometric candidate that dissolves when spectroscopy improves was never a residual at the ground; it was a confound in the channel. `Fig 10 :24`: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* with stale percepts and pooled readings as the named failure modes.

World corroboration, two independent witnesses:

1. Wikipedia Lambda-CDM, high-redshift galaxies section (opened 2026-11-23): early JWST photometry selected candidate massive galaxies whose existence *"would challenge standard cosmology"*; follow-up spectroscopy showed most were AGN whose brightness had caused masses to be overestimated, and *"the high redshift galaxies which have been spectroscopically confirmed... are much less massive and are consistent with the predictions from ΛCDM simulations run before JWST."* A better instrument, not an argument, closed the candidate anomaly. It also states that galaxy tests are *"less direct, as they require assumptions about how galaxies form"*, which is the source-discrimination caveat.
2. The *Hard Problem is About Source* essay (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28, opened 2026-11-23): *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* This is framework-author material, high overlap with the figures, so it corroborates the shape only and carries little independent weight. The Wikipedia case is the low-overlap witness.

**Refuter:** exhibit a candidate cosmological anomaly that survived a source-discriminating instrument without requiring new physics while the model nevertheless stayed standing, or exhibit a new instrument specified from a description alone with no prior returning signal. Either breaks F3.

### F4 — Split before search: a tension may be several questions

`Fig 9 :4-5` (verbatim):

> **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

`Fig 9 :54-58`: when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before searching again; a case in two conditions "is not a harder case of either" but "a specific compound". The Hubble tension's most conservative proposed resolution is an unknown systematic, and the literature explicitly canvasses which instrument or calibration could produce it. That is the split being run, not new physics being assumed. Only after the split fails does the residual name one question.

`Fig 9 :32`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework returns the **kind of evidence**, not the name of the new physics. Any answer that says "the evidence that would kill ΛCDM is detection of X particle" is past what the figures license.

World corroboration: Wikipedia Hubble tension, "Possible resolutions": *"The most conservative is that there is an unknown systematic error affecting either early-universe or late-universe observations."* It then lists new-physics options as one further possibility among several, and notes *"A problem faced by all these theories is that both early-universe and late-universe measurements rely on multiple independent lines of physics, and it is difficult to modify any of those lines while preserving their successes elsewhere."* Corroborates F4 (split first, new physics last). Low overlap with the philosophy sources.

**Refuter:** show that a cosmological disagreement does in fact shrink when the datasets are partitioned by instrument or calibration, yet the community still treated it as one residual demanding new physics. Then F4's ordering is wrong for that case.

### F5 — The replacement must pay the description-length bargain

`Fig 5 :42-44` (verbatim):

> **"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."**
> **"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before. A term that explains everything by saying nothing"**

(the line continues: "fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly.") `Fig 5 :48`: *"Its own output is never a measure of success."* A proposed replacement that fits one tension while breaking the acoustic peaks, BBN abundances and lensing has not paid the bargain.

World corroboration, two sources:

1. Wikipedia Lambda-CDM, Hubble tension section (opened 2026-11-23): *"Dozens of proposals for modifications of ΛCDM or completely new models have been published to explain the Hubble tension... None of these models can simultaneously explain the breadth of other cosmological data as well as ΛCDM."* This is the MDL verdict stated in cosmological language. Low overlap with the figures.
2. SEP Scientific Revolutions (Nickles, 2009, rev. 2017), quoting Popper 1975: *"a new theory, however revolutionary, must always be able to explain fully the success of its predecessor."* Corroborates the same requirement from a different field. Overlap with source 1 is low.

**Refuter:** exhibit a replacement model that fits the full suite at lower statement cost and is still refused by the community on no ground reading. Then F5's sufficiency direction fails.

### F6 — Abandonment is a negation, not a mint

`Fig 5 :55` (verbatim):

> **"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."**

`Fig 5 :56`: *"Requires an operator for negation, which the seven bonds do not contain."* The Operators table entry for ¬ (verbatim): *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. So "abandon" and "substantially revise" are **two different operations**, not two points on one scale. Revision composes a new molecule inside the closure; abandonment refuses a held term's standing to be composed over. A framework that can only add parameters can revise forever and never abandon.

This is a `general` structural claim about the question's own wording, not a prediction about any particular cosmology.

**Refuter:** show that a community can withdraw a settled model's standing to be composed over without any term that refuses it, or show a term and its refusal both settled with the reach unchanged. Then F6 fails.

### F7 — Prestige and consensus are not the ground

`Fig 7 :13`:

> **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

`Fig 7 :35`: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* `Fig 2 :42`: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A large collaboration's consensus, or the fact that a model is "standard", is a prestige proxy. It gives a claim a budget to be tried and cannot settle it. The *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23) states: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* High overlap with the figures, so weak corroboration only.

**Refuter:** identify a cosmological question settled by consensus or authority with no measurement that would have falsified the alternative. Then F7 fails.

### F8 — Elegance is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict

`Fig 9 :60` (verbatim):

> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Mathematical elegance decides which proposal gets tried first, not whether the ground paid for it. This bears directly on section J of the source question (adjacent question 98 asks how much elegance should matter). `Fig 2 :29`: if the "evidence" moves when you push on the aesthetics of the community, it is not an anchor.

**Refuter:** show a case where elegance alone flipped a model's standing with no new measurement. Then F8 fails.

### F9 — The honest stop: unreached, not silence, and no last room

`Fig 7 :6`: *"the limit: no last room"*. `Fig 8 :39`:

> **"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

`Fig 13 :134`: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. `Fig 6 :24`: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* So when no observation currently closes a residual, the correct report is a reached stop at this budget, with the missing thing characterised in effect terms (a kind of discriminator instrument, not a named particle), and with any imported new-physics atom debited against the independence of the frame that supplied it.

World corroboration: Wikipedia Lambda-CDM closes its "Challenges" section with *"cosmologists believe that the model may be an approximation of a more fundamental model"* while its successes section records that the six parameters predict TE and EE spectra *"to few-percent precision with no further adjustments allowed."* An approximation with no last room is the honest standing, not a model awaiting a single killer datum.

**Refuter:** a ground reading that closes the residual, or a demonstration that the framework's own "no last room" rule forbids a well-posed theory-revision question. Then F9 narrows or fails.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before the conclusion: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument"* (Seat Map, 2026-11-22 amendment). Written here before sections 5 to 9.

The shape claim is: **what moves a settled cosmological model is an independent, persistent, non-shrinking residual at the ground that cannot be re-bound inside the model's closure, whose replacement pays the description-length bargain, and whose abandonment additionally requires a refusal of a held term.** It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A case where the community treats an observation as model-changing while the residual trends down under an in-closure re-fit and owes no new term (breaks F1).
2. A case where a model changes on agreement or authority with no independent ground reading that could have gone the other way (breaks D3 and F7).
3. A case where a single instrument sharing the model's evidence pool changes the model, with independence zero (breaks F2).
4. A case where a new term is admitted and kept although it explains one occasion perfectly and breaks the rest of the fit (breaks F5).
5. A case where a settled model is abandoned without any term that refuses it, or where a term and its refusal are both settled with the reach unchanged (breaks F6).
6. A demonstration that mathematical elegance alone flipped a verdict without a new measurement (breaks F8).

If any fires, the classification is withdrawn in whole or in the named part. Until one is run, no part of it is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the persistence diagnostic (F1) and the independence requirement (F2), not with a list of candidate anomalies. A list of anomalies is a recording; the diagnostic is a generator (`Fig 4 :22-25`: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").
2. Run the split (F4) before naming any new physics. A tension that partitions by instrument is several questions.
3. Demand the source-discriminating instrument (F3), and state what it would have to show. "We need better data" is not an instrument; "a probe already returning a reading whose discriminator would separate early- and late-universe systematics" is.
4. Require the replacement to fit the full suite (F5). Do not let a proposal count because it fixes one tension.
5. Distinguish revision from abandonment in the wording (F6). They are different operators.
6. Keep prestige and elegance out of the verdict column (F7, F8).
7. Say "unreached at this budget" rather than "no evidence exists" (F9), and debit any import against independence (Fig 6 :24).

### Strain check after re-census

- Fig 8 :32 versus the fact that more data sometimes does move cosmology. Resolved: same-kind data stay in the closure; a *different instrument* is an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), so this is not a contradiction.
