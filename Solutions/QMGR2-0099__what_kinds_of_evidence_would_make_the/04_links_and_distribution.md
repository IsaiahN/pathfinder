## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **Falsifiability and the timing of rejection.** The Popper and Scientific Revolutions SEP entries disagree in emphasis: Popper's logic gives a single counter-instance the power to refute, while his own methodology and Kuhn's account both require persistence and a replacement. This is a genuine world-source conflict. It was returned to the domain census (see `01` section 6): the figures' shrinking test (Fig 5 :59) and refusal operator (Fig 5 :53) resolve it by separating the logical falsifier from the community's methodological verdict. The verdict was not flipped by the conflict.
2. **Cosmology's own account of its anomalies.** The two Wikipedia entries are not authorities for the philosophy, but they are a compact, public record of the ground-facing facts: the tension's persistence under shrinking errors, the multi-instrument independence, the conventionalism charge, and the spectroscopy case that dissolved a photometric anomaly. All four bear directly on findings F1 to F5.
3. **Provenance limit.** All three framework essays are by the framework author. They share the same evidence pool as the figures and each other. Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries little independent weight; the SEP and Wikipedia sources are the low-overlap witnesses.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the classification and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would count as a failure before giving them any theory.
- **Fig 2 :40** — agreement from a shared evidence pool is not a verdict. A list selected for people who already think ΛCDM is in trouble produces a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35).

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the diagnostic and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a model-changing observation; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures and the public sources, not this summary, are the authority.
