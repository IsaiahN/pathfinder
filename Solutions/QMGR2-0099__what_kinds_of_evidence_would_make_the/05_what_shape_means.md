## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local copy, opened this session) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. `Fig 9 :32` says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* `Fig 13 :134-135`: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. For this question:

- A **name** would be "detection of a specific particle X would kill ΛCDM" or "the Hubble tension is caused by early dark energy". Those are bindings from the physics frame, not figure outputs.
- A **shape** is: an observation must be independent of the model's evidence pool, its residual must persist and not trend down under re-fitting, it must survive a source-discriminating instrument, and any replacement must pay for the observations ΛCDM already explains.

The shape constrains; it does not fill. It returns a **kind of discriminator**, never a named new physics.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the ordinary terms "tension", "anomaly", "systematic", "fit", and narrow toward "persistent non-shrinking residual", "independent closure", "source-discriminating instrument".
- `Fig 6 :30` (verbatim figure line): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* `Fig 9 :31`: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So "an observation that a better instrument would confirm or dissolve" is an effect description; "a fifth-force boson" is a cause name and is not licensed.
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level parallel to `Fig 9 :4-5` and it is why F4 runs the split before new physics is named.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8 :50-52`), with a source gate and a shadow test. Any specific new-physics proposal must carry provenance and explain a gap that ΛCDM leaves. Without it, the proposal is the searcher's own contribution, recorded as such.

For this question, a name would be licensed only when a measurement plus a replacement model that fits the whole suite pays the MDL bargain (F5), or when a frame with a genuinely different closure supplies a provenanced import that survives the independence debit (Fig 6 :24). Until then, the answer stays a checklist of evidence kinds.

## 4. The source-discrimination point

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism for why a reading does not name its source: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* Its green-light example shows one reading with many possible sources, resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied to the Hubble tension: an H0 value is a reading. Several chains (CMB under ΛCDM, distance ladder, TRGB, time delays, kilonova) can produce readings near it or away from it. The reading alone cannot name the source of the discrepancy. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This corroborates findings F3 and F4. The essay is by the framework author, so it is high-overlap corroboration; the low-overlap witness is the Wikipedia high-redshift galaxy case in `04_links_and_distribution.md`, where spectroscopy dissolved a photometric anomaly.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a discriminator? ANSWERED

No. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. The discriminator asked for in F3 is not a new primitive of the framework; it is an instrument described in effect terms (something already returning a reading whose resolution would separate two candidate systematics). Describing it is a binding from the observation frame onto a held capability, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is naming the new physics that the discriminator would reveal. That is the source, not the reading.

I overstated this rule in an earlier pass, treating any concrete description as a name. Narrowed and recorded: the shape/not-a-name limit applies to the missing thing, not to the effect description of the instrument that would find it. Triangulated across the canonical README, `Fig 9 :32` and `Fig 6 :34`.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* That a model is called "standard", or that a result comes from a large collaboration, gives it a budget to be tried. It is not an anchor. `Fig 2 :29`: if a claim moves when the community's reputation is pushed on it, it is not an anchor.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any cosmological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.
