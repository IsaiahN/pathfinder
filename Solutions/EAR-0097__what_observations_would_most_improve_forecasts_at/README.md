## What this package is for

The question is an effect question in disguise. Something about the present observing system fails to settle forecasts at the scale at which a road, a levee, a reservoir, or a land-use zoning decision is actually made. The residual is the gap between what a model predicts at that decision slot and what happens there. The question asks which observations, if added, would shrink that residual most.

Read as an ordinary term, "observations" is treated as a stock to be enlarged. The figures do not allow that. Fig 6 :26 says *"instruments extend what can be represented at all"*, and Fig 11 :48 says *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* So the answer cannot be a free-standing shopping list of instruments. It has to be a shape that a candidate observing system must have if it is to bear on this residual, plus the conditions under which a world source could name an actual one.

The verdict, stated once here and derived in the files below: **no figure line names which observation wins.** The figures return a four-condition separator (per-slot residual, a non-updating local anchor, a scale round trip that can be measured, and a proto-instrument already returning something that fails to resolve, enumerated from the habitat with its cascade). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. The literal ranked list is a declared `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as `unreached` rather than silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No named "top observation" that the figures do not license.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mailing list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **The outsider must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The two-stage offer sends the residual and its refuter first; the framework is offered only after the first verdict is recorded.
3. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Naming a specific sensor as "the answer" would be a binding from the observation frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03` are institutional and opt-in.
