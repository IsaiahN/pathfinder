## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *an observation must reduce a live per-slot residual, be ranged against a local non-updating anchor, survive the global-to-local round trip, and extend an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve.*
- A name: *deploy more soil-moisture gauges in the upper catchment.*

The shape is what the figures return. The name is a binding from the observation frame and must be provenanced as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, connect it to *"commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with"*, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual in `01` is stated as prediction minus observation at a decision slot, not as "model structural error."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet in `08` carries effect descriptions, not framework vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A world source that names a specific observation as high-value is such an import. It carries zero verdict weight until provenance and the gap it explains are shown.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) sharpens this. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The reading does not determine which physical mixture produced it. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a global skill score is a reading of many possible observing-system sources. It cannot say which observation produced the improvement. Naming one from that score alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator at the decision slot, not an argument.

## 4. Why the ranked list is the forbidden error

The question asks for "what observations would **most** improve forecasts." Read naively, it asks for a ranked list. Three essay/figure results refuse that reading:

- No standing list survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*). An observation's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- The residual must stay per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). A single ranking is an aggregate, and aggregation is how the live signal disappears.
- The framework returns a kind of thing, not the answer (Fig 9 :32; canonical README, *"a shape, not a name"*).

What survives is a **separator**: the conditions an observation must satisfy, applied per slot class, with an enumeration rule for the habitat and a refuter written in advance. This is the same move the exemplar makes when it refuses to name Gradient in the QM/GR slot.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: a well-known observing system, a prestigious review, or a famous modelling centre is not evidence that its observations are the ones that most improve decision-scale forecasts. It is a reason to give the claim a budget to be tried. The try is an independent local reading against an anchor the model cannot move (Fig 2 :29, :35).

## 6. Correction to my own §1 to §4: "shape, not a name" is narrower than I first wrote it

I initially applied the canonical phrase to all naming, as if the framework forbade naming any observation. That overstates it. The README says *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Observation channels, gauges, reanalysis products, and station networks are not missing primitives of the framework; they are bindings from the earth-observation frame onto things the framework can already describe as instruments and channels (Fig 6 :26: *"instruments extend what can be represented at all"*).

So:

- Naming a specific observation is **not** forbidden. It is a binding, provenanced as coming from the observation frame.
- What **is** forbidden is presenting that binding as a figure-derived derivation, or claiming it moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).
- My §3 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason is still the missing discriminator: a global skill reading cannot name its source. The correction is that the ban is on an unprovenanced derivation, not on naming per se.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the exemplar's own correction in `05` section 5b.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01` through `11` in this folder are a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any observing-system claim and must be labelled as such. The solution cannot settle EAR-0097. It can say what a settling observation would have to look like, and what would refute that description.
