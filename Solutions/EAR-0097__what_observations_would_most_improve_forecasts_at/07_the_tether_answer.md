# The Tether's answer: which observations most improve decision-scale forecasts

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ranked list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Forecasts inform decisions at a catchment, a coast, a watershed, a parcel. The error that matters is the gap at that slot between what the predicting system says and what happens there. Global skill averages can improve while one live slot stays wrong (Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**). The question asks which observations would shrink that live residual most. "Most" is a threshold comparison in two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125, :87), not a single score.

## 2. The shape: four conditions plus an enumeration rule

1. **Per-slot effect.** The observation must reduce forecast error on at least one decision slot with positive live mass. A global improvement with a still-wrong slot fails (Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**).

2. **An anchor that does not update.** The observation must be read against a local outcome that cannot be moved by the model's own output (Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**). Verification inside one evidence pool is one long room, not a verdict (Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**).

3. **A measured round trip.** The value must survive the global-to-local transform. The gap between the coarse projection and the local outcome is measurable and is the thing to report, not an error to erase (Fig 4 :31, substring: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"**). Instrumenting the boundary is therefore part of the answer, not overhead.

4. **An edge to extend, or a sourced import.** New capability is of two kinds. Sharpening an instrument that already returns something that fails to resolve is INWARD. A genuinely new modality is OUTWARD, an import needing a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**; Fig 6 :31: **"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."**).

5. **Enumeration rule.** The candidate set is read off the world's contact network and followed outward until adding another causal step stops changing the decision residual (Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**). The driver can be three steps out (Fig 11 :52, substring: **"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles"**). There is no standing list of valuable observations (Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**).

Kept separate and never averaged: a flood-siting residual, a water-allocation residual, a land-use zoning residual, and a coastal-protection residual. Each has its own slots, its own anchor and its own habitat.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranked list of sensors, networks, variables, cadences, or budget shares. No figure line governs that; it is a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**).
- Not "more data everywhere." Volume is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**).
- Not that global models, ensembles, or downscaling are useless. They are the frame whose residual is being measured. The claim is about where the witness must come from.
- Not that channel maintenance is overhead. A decayed channel reports "you have lost touch" (Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**).
- Not `grounded`. No decision-scale measurement was taken in producing this answer.
- Not a derivation of any specific observation from the six-term substrate. An observation is a binding from the earth-observation frame, provenanced as such (see `05` section 6).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The corresponding tests here:

1. **Slot test.** Measure forecast error before and after the candidate observation at each live decision slot, with a global mean reported separately so aggregation cannot hide a still-wrong slot.
2. **Anchor test.** Show that the verification outcome does not change when the model's own output changes; use an independent local reading.
3. **Round-trip test.** Compute gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)) between the coarse projection and the local outcome with a declared metric. A zero gap at the relevant slots would refute condition 3.
4. **Edge test.** For a sharpened instrument, show what earlier reading it extends. For a new modality, show the source frame, the provenance and the gap it explains. A sensor built from a description alone that later reduces the slot residual would refute condition 4.
5. **Cascade test.** Enumerate the habitat to the second and third order and show where adding another step stops changing the residual.
6. **Standing refuters from `01` section 4:** one observation winning on every slot class after a residual change; a lossless global skill metric; an improvement with no independent reading; a zero round-trip gap; a description-built sensor; a figure line that names the winner.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreached for the name.** The four conditions and the enumeration rule are figure-derived and internally consistent. Which specific observation wins for a given decision is `unreached at this budget`, not `unreachable in principle`: decision-scale observing systems exist and are studied (Earth System Dynamics, Earth System Science Data, GEO, WMO, all opened). What is owed is a measurement and a discriminator, not another argument. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning sensor. It says what any decision-scale-improving observation must be: it must shrink forecast error at the actual decision slot, not just in a global average; it must be checked against a local outcome the model cannot move; its value must survive the lossy trip from a global projection to the decision site, with that loss measured rather than assumed away; and it must either sharpen an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve or arrive as a provenanced import with a stated source and a stated gap it explains. The candidate set is read off the world's contact network, not the model's variable list, and followed outward because the driver can be several causal steps from the thing the decision touches. Verdicts are kept separate per decision class and never averaged. Which observation actually wins is left open, with the five refuters written in advance and the instrument that would close it named. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
