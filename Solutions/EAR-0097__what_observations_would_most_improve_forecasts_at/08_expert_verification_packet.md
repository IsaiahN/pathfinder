# Expert verification packet: the Tether on decision-scale observations

**What this is:** a claim in a form an earth-system observation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ranked list. Not `grounded`.** No decision-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Forecast skill must be indexed per decision slot, not reported only as a global average, because aggregation hides the live local residual that an infrastructure or land-use decision actually pays for. An observation counts as decision-scale-improving only if four conditions hold together: it reduces error at a live slot; it is ranged against a local anchor that does not update on the model's own output; its value survives the measured global-to-local round trip; and it either sharpens an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve or arrives as a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test. The candidate set is enumerated from the world's contact network and followed outward because the driver can be several causal steps away. Verdicts are kept separate per decision class. No observation is named as the winner; that is a declared `gap` with a stated instrument to close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus field-facing; checkable by reading Figure 1 and the ensemble literature

- **Statement:** a global skill average cannot decide which observation improves a decision-scale forecast. The residual must be indexed per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Also Fig 1 :22 - **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Symbols table :38-40 (substring): R *"is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*
- **World anchor to check against:** *Earth System Dynamics* (Copernicus/EGU, 2026) recently published work on *"the risk of information loss inherent in commonly used aggregation approaches"* (Zoller et al., ESD 17, 1719). *Value of information* (Wikipedia, 2026) states that the value of two new evidence variables *"is not additive."*
- **Refuter:** show a global metric that preserves the live per-slot residual with no information loss, or show all relevant slots have zero residual, and C1 falls.

### C2 - the anchor and round-trip condition; checkable by an observing-system researcher

- **Statement:** verification must use a local reading independent of the model that produced the forecast, and the information lost between the coarse projection and the decision site must be measured, not assumed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :35 - **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 4 :30 - **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
- **World anchor to check against:** *Downscaling* (Wikipedia, 2026): *"Global Climate Models (GCMs) used for climate studies and climate projections are typically run at spatial resolutions of the order of 150 to 200 km and are limited in their ability to resolve important sub-grid scale features such as convection clouds and topography. As a result, GCM based projections may not be robust for local impact studies."* *Ensemble forecasting* (Wikipedia, 2026): *"It is common for the ensemble spread to be too small, such that the observed atmospheric state falls outside of the ensemble forecast. This can lead the forecaster to be overconfident in their forecast."*
- **Refuter:** measure a zero round-trip gap at the relevant slots with an independent local reading, or show an improvement verified entirely inside one evidence pool that is real at the slot, and C2 falls.

### C3 - the instrument / import condition and the enumeration rule; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 11

- **Statement:** new observing capability is either INWARD sharpening of an instrument already returning something, or OUTWARD import with provenance and a shadow test. The candidate set is enumerated from the world's contact network and can be several causal steps from the decision variable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 - **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Fig 6 :31 - **"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."** Fig 11 :43 - **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :51 - **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**
- **World anchor to check against:** *Trophic cascade* (Wikipedia, 2026): *"Trophic cascades are powerful indirect interactions that can control entire ecosystems, occurring when a trophic level in a food web is suppressed."* The cross-boundary subsidy case reports *"a 62% decline in forest tree sapling density over a 24-year study period"* from a change two steps away. *Data assimilation* (Wikipedia, 2026) notes that *"Dealing with biased data is a serious challenge in data assimilation."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a decision residual whose habitat closes at first-order contact with a measured demonstration that second- and third-order actors do not move it; or exhibit an observation of a never-registered quantity built from a description alone that later reduces the slot residual; or show a convention-free total ranking of observations invariant across slot classes. Any of these breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranked list of sensors, networks, variables, cadences or budget shares. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**).
- Not that global models, ensembles, reanalysis or downscaling are useless. They are the frame whose residual is being measured.
- Not that "more data everywhere" is the answer. Volume is a budget filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an experiment or a decision outcome.
- Not that the Tether names an observation. It returns a shape.
- Not that the DDT/eagles figure is a literal infrastructure case. It is a mechanism analogue for contact cascades, corroborated at mechanism level by the trophic-cascade source.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator and a measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

1. **Slot test:** error before and after the candidate at each live decision slot, with the global mean reported separately.
2. **Anchor test:** the verification outcome must not move when the model's own output changes.
3. **Round-trip test:** gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)) at the slot with a declared metric. Zero gap refutes C2.
4. **Edge test:** for a sharpened instrument, name the earlier reading it extends; for a new modality, name the source frame, the provenance and the gap explained.
5. **Cascade test:** enumerate to the second and third order and show where adding another step stops changing the residual.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 2 :29 and :35, Fig 4 :30, Fig 6 :31 and :32, Fig 11 :43 and :51. Nine short passages.
2. Check C1 against your own experience of skill verification: does a global score ever hide a still-wrong decision slot?
3. Check C2 against a downscaling or observing-system study you know: is the gap between projection and local outcome measured, or assumed?
4. Check C3 against a case you know: was the valuable observation a sharpened existing channel or a new provenanced modality, and was the driver within direct contact or several steps out?
5. Name one instrument that would discriminate the four conditions at your decision scale, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
