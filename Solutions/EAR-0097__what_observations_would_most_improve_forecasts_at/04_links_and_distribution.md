## 2. What the wider literature does and does not do

The world sources above are secondary corroboration. They do not decide the verdict.

- **Low overlap, real corroboration.** *Trophic cascade* (ecology), *Anchoring effect* (human cognition), *Value of information* (decision theory) and *Downscaling* (climate method) come from different evidence pools and independently support the mechanism shapes in F7, F9, F2 and F4.
- **One broad pool, not several witnesses.** *Data assimilation*, *Ensemble forecasting*, the *Earth System Dynamics* journal and *Earth System Science Data* all belong to the earth-system-modelling community and often cite the same centres (ECMWF, NCEP, CMIP, CORDEX). Their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Maximal overlap, zero independent weight.** The four framework essays share the framework author with the figures. They govern the canonical reading of "shape" and the simulation-status declaration; they are not corroboration.
- **No conflict triggered.** No world source contradicted a figure verdict. An apparent tension, ensemble spread as a measure of uncertainty versus Fig 2's warning that agreement is not a verdict, was returned to the census and dissolved: spread is a within-frame budget filter, independence is a between-frame witness rule (S5 in `01`).

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape claim and its refuter first. An observer who has absorbed the full architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A recording of one successful observing campaign is not a generator. Lead with the conditions and the enumeration rule.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the architecture link.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Infrastructure and land-use decisions are expensive and slow to reverse, so independent local readings are worth real cost. That is a budget rule, not a verdict.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A tier or a ranking of venues says what gets tried first, never whether the claim is true.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-condition shape and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter.

## 4. Submission caution

Both Earth System Dynamics and Earth System Science Data publish an explicit AI policy, visible in their site navigation. Copernicus Publications also notes that first contact for APC invoicing must have an affiliation and invoice address outside Russia and Belarus. This package is LLM-assisted and is a simulated derivation. It is to be used as a private checking instrument. A human researcher owns and writes anything submitted, and checks the current AI policy of the target journal before submitting. The same caution applies to WMO and GEO channels: public institutional routes are for questions and checkable claims, not for unreleased material the sender needs protected.
