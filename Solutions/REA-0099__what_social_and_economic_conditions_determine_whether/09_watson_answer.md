## DOMAIN CENSUS

`R` by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot discrepancy between the predicted and observed effect of an introduced AI system on a person's capability to reach what they take to be their own good. Arity: two overlapping conditions, social and economic, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: ordered, because autonomy up and dependence up are opposite signs of a comparison against a ground, not two names for one state. Scale: micro to macro, related by the membrane rule. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, person plus AI tool (micro).** Actors: one person, one AI system, the task set. Currency: actions and outcomes relative to the person's own goals. Anchor: human judgment about that person's own good (Fig 2 :37). Question type: partition boundary, does the intervention change contact?
- **Domain 2, social habitat (meso).** Actors: workers, managers, peers, teams, norms, organizations. Currency: roles, skills, trust, decision authority. Anchor: per-slot human judgment, collected by an instrument; each person ranges against it separately (Fig 2 :45-46). Question type: one arm of the union.
- **Domain 3, economic habitat (macro).** Actors: firms, workers, consumers, owners, capital. Currency: money, prices, wages, employment, ownership. Anchor: a market settles questions constitutively about prices or allocation; here it is an instrument and can be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). Question type: other arm of the union.
- **Domain 4, governance / maintainer seat (compound).** Actors: regulators, standards bodies, law, property rights. Currency: rules, permitted introductions, liability. Anchor: human judgment about people's good, with the seat's interest declared (Fig 10 :59). Question type: the union where a maintainer exists; its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 first, then D2 and D3, then D4 which acts on all three (Fig 3, Fig 11 :61). Within D1, refuse the agent-only reading before minting any condition list (Formula step 2), and instrument the vocabulary/objective link before reading anything below it (Fig 3 :24-27).

All four domains have a nameable anchor. Strain test applied: the symmetric `+` of "social and economic" and the ordered `⋛` of the sign govern different quantities, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the requested list does not. The direction of AI's effect on autonomy is a relation between a person, a system and a habitat, measured per person against human judgment about that person's own good. Conditions have no fixed sign, social and economic conditions form a union compound, governance can introduce but not exclude, benchmarks prove wiring and not capability, and the names of the conditions that decide the sign are a `gap` until an import passes a source gate and shadow test and a ground reading pays for it. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list, or a recording of the framework, instead of the shape and its refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The retracted list is already removed from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 with the reason visible. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a better instrument can fill the gap without changing the shape. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled study showing agent-only improvement reliably raises per-slot capability with contact held fixed would break the habitat claim; a condition whose sign is invariant across two goals for the same person would break the valence claim; an aggregate or benchmark score settling a person's own account of autonomy would break the ground claim; a demonstration that the social and economic condition sets are disjoint would break the union claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
