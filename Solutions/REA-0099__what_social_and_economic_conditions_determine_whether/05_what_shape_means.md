## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 (canonical README wording): the system "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: per-slot, habitat-relative, ground-anchored in human judgment, union-aware across social and economic conditions, limited by benchmark substitution, and with a maintainer whose interest is declared. The list of named conditions is not part of the shape. It is the thing not yet held.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *The Library of Babel* on steering: the returned books "need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." And on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."
- The derive-first procedure applied here: state residual in effect terms, census domains before choosing a figure line, quote verbatim before concluding, strain-test, keep verdicts separate. That is how the shape in `07_the_tether_answer.md` was obtained, and it is why the first-draft list was retracted.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a measurement in a real habitat of a person's capability against their own judgment, not a benchmark and not an aggregate.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A candidate condition vocabulary from HCI, health informatics or governance research is an OUTWARD candidate; it becomes an import only when its provenance is recorded and it is shown to explain the residual.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* sharpens the first condition: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Nothing in this folder validates itself.

**Correction to the first draft of this solution.** I initially wrote "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any condition at all. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* thing, one not held in the closure. Held atoms may be bound and named, with provenance. What is forbidden here is presenting a named list of conditions as if the figure set had derived their signs. The list is unreached, not unsayable.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Fig 2 :38 adds that instruments "can be wrong on axes they do not measure." That is the source problem for this question in one line: a metric reads throughput or benchmark success and cannot comment on whether a person's own effective freedom changed. Many sources could give the same reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the general rule: "The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback. To the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement. A board that pays or does not." This is why the verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` comes from the figures and from a future ground reading, never from the surveys cited as world corroboration.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does an answer to REA-0099 have?** It is a rule mapping an enumerated habitat to the sign of a per-person capability change, judged against that person's own good, with social and economic conditions treated as a union compound, with the maintainer's interest declared, and with benchmark results demoted to wiring checks. It is not a list with fixed signs, because valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Concretely, any candidate answer must be able to say, for a named person and a named goal: what changed in the contact, what instrument measured the change, who declared an interest, and what would refute the sign. If it cannot, it is a preference dressed as a result.

**Does the derivation commit the error the README forbids?** No about the shape, yes about the first draft's list. The shape is stated as a kind of thing. The first draft named conditions with signs; that was retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5. The corrected form is the gap plus the measurement protocol.

## 6. Consequences for contact

Two consequences govern outreach.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous AI researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter and an instrument to measure the sign.

**This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So all files in this folder are labelled simulated. They cannot settle the question they report on, and the outreach never asks anyone to treat them as settled.
