## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Direction is domain-specific, not a property of the tool.** Wheeler et al. found automation preference varied across nine life domains and that people preferred bounded autonomy: substantial AI execution with human oversight or final authority retained. This corroborates Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and Fig 11 :49-51 (valence relative to the residual). It does not settle the question, because it measures stated preference, not capability against a ground.

2. **Dependence can be a habitat effect.** Lyell and Coiera found overreliance was associated with verification complexity and with automation that was constantly highly accurate, not with the aid in isolation. Sisodia found that productivity assistance can weaken apprenticeship when assisted output improves faster than unaided capability. Both are instances of Fig 11 :40: change the contact and you change the result; improve the agent alone and you may change nothing.

3. **Current evaluation cannot see the quantity.** Pallikala and Hariprasad found that agent benchmarks measure capability but systematically omit respect for human autonomy. This corroborates Fig 1 :24-27 (frame-internal quantities cannot score the frame) and Fig 9 :60 (filters are not witnesses).

4. **Governance is a live maintainer seat.** NIST's public, consensus-driven risk process and FAccT's stated concern with power and welfare distribution are real-world instances of Fig 11 :56-61: a seat that can introduce instruments and rules into a habitat, though it cannot subtract contact. The AI RMF is explicit about risks to individuals and society, which is the same anchor as Fig 2 :37.

5. **Augmentation research is the nearest outside frame.** The Augmented Humans conference frames its object as technologies that enhance human capabilities beyond the individual and at societal scale. That is a low-overlap frame whose closure may hold the condition vocabulary this derivation lacks, but its claim is a venue description, not a ground reading. It is an OUTWARD candidate, not an import that has passed a source gate and shadow test.

**Conflict rule:** none of these sources conflicts with the figure verdict. All corroborate the shape. They come from different fields (HCI survey, finance and education synthesis, health informatics, standards, conference scope), but several are surveys or venue descriptions rather than direct measurements of autonomy change. They are one set of witnesses at most, not independent corroboration of the specific gap.
