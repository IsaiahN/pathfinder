## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. No scraped contact lists. No receptiveness ranking of people.
- No bulk-send plan. All contact is one to one through public, opt-in or submission channels.
- No list of social and economic conditions with fixed signs. Fig 11 :51 says "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The answer is a shape plus a measurement protocol, and the specific list is declared a `gap` at the world level.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question. It is a shape, written before the ground speaks.

## Headline result

The figures refuse the question's framing as written. Capability is a property of agent and habitat, not of the AI in isolation (Fig 11 :39-40); an actor's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51); the ground for a question about people is human judgment (Fig 2 :37); "social" and "economic" form a union compound, not a list (Fig 9 :55-57). The shape that survives is per-slot, ground-anchored, union-aware and maintainer-declared. The names of the particular conditions that decide the sign are **unreached**: they require an import from a frame whose closure differs and a ground reading. Unreached is a verdict here, not a silence.
