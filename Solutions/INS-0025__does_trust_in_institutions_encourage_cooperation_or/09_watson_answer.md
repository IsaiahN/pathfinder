## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, between reading A: trust in institutions encourages cooperation, and reading B: institutions work well mainly where trust already exists. Option A leads to spending budget on changing trust and predicting cooperation will follow. Option B leads to enumerating the trust habitat and predicting that an institution transplanted without it fails. What the figures can decide is the shape of a legitimate answer and what would order the two readings; they do not decide which reading is true. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run in `01` §0.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced choice is refused as a single question; the shape survives. Four conditions are in a union: trust enabling cooperation, institutions producing trust, a shared habitat cause, and a possible null in some slots. The direction of the bond is `gap`, not `grounded`. Institutional capability is institution-and-habitat, so the B-like transfer warning holds as a constraint and not as a causal verdict. Covariance cannot order the two readings because the readings can update on each other or share a source. The residual is per slot. World sources corroborate the individual branches and disagree with one another at the level of a universal direction, which is what the union reading predicts.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than absorbed. The larger cost is spending a checker's independence by sending the full framework before their verdict (Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The direction can be filled without touching the shape: a randomized direction test in two low-overlap frames would order A or B for named slots. A failed habitat transplant would remove the transfer constraint. No primitive has to be withdrawn to make either repair.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. Specifically: the direction intervention in C3 of `08_expert_verification_packet.md`; a transplant result that produces cooperation without a habitat change; a within-slot time series naming the sign of the loop; or a figure re-census that admits a simple chain whose direction is derivable without an anchor. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
