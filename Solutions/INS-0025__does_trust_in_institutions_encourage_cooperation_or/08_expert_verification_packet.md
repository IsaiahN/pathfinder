# Expert verification packet: the Tether on trust in institutions and cooperation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in trust, institutional quality or political behaviour can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the domain claims, `gap` on the direction. Not `grounded`.** No measurement in this derivation ranks the two readings. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The offered choice between trust causing cooperation and institutions working only where trust exists is a chain-shaped framing of a union compound. Trust, institution and cooperation can form a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot represent that by construction. Capability is a property of institution-and-habitat, never of the institution alone, so moving a design into a low-trust setting substitutes a habitat and inherits actors you did not choose. Covariance cannot order the direction: two readings can update on each other, or both draw on one shared source. The residual must be read per slot. The figure set constrains this shape but does not decide which reading is true; that requires an anchor that does not move when a frame's opinion changes.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 9

- **Statement:** a forced A-or-B choice between two directed bonds is not licensed when the relation may be a cycle; the problem is a union and must be unbundled before it is searched.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Refuter:** a re-census showing the trust/cooperation relation is a simple chain, that its bond is one of the seven, and that its direction is derivable without an anchor. If that holds, this sub-claim is withdrawn.

### C2: Institution-and-habitat; checkable by an institutional researcher against field evidence

- **Statement:** an institution carries no cooperation in isolation; its capability is a property of institution-and-habitat, and transplanting it substitutes a habitat with unchosen actors.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
- **What to check:** does the field evidence support a habitat-specific mechanism (for example Goist and Kern 2018 on Buganda, where the mechanism runs through access to social elites, not peer effects; Murtin et al. 2026 on repeated positive interactions with public services)? Or is there a case where an institution produces cooperation with no habitat change at all?
- **Refuter:** a field transplant into a low-trust habitat producing sustained per-slot cooperation with no reproduction of the trust habitat and no imported actors. That breaks the capability claim.

### C3: Measurement; checkable by a methodologist or experimentalist

- **Statement:** covariance between trust and cooperation does not order their direction; an unmoved anchor and two low-overlap frames are required, and the residual is read per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** do the published designs actually separate the sources, or do they re-use the same survey item and the same laboratory game across frames? Kim et al. 2022 say "it is hard to identify the effect of beliefs regarding others' trustworthiness on cooperation in natural settings"; Bauer et al. 2019 found "no relationship between trust and cooperation" in a real data-sharing situation; Martinangeli et al. 2023 and Murtin et al. 2026 report the institution to trust direction. These are four different slots and instruments, not four votes on one question.
- **Refuter:** a randomized intervention on trust with institution and habitat held fixed, producing a monotone per-slot change in measured cooperation in two low-overlap frames ranging against the same unmoved anchor, with no reverse path. If it fires, the covariance gap closes for those slots.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that trust causes cooperation, and not that institutions work only where trust exists. The direction is a `gap`, not a verdict.
- Not that there is no relation. A null in one situation is a per-slot result (Fig 1 :19), not a universal law.
- Not that the figures settle an empirical question. They constrain the shape; the ground settles the direction.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that survey trust is the ground. It is an instrument, and Fig 2 :37 says instruments "can be wrong on axes they do not measure".
- Not that this derivation validates itself. It is a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Direction test:** the intervention in C3. If it fires, A is ordered in those slots and the union reading for them is withdrawn.
2. **Transplant test:** the field transplant in C2. If it fires, the habitat constraint is wrong.
3. **Sign test:** a within-slot time series showing whether cooperation depletes or regenerates the next trust reading (Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use.").
4. **Figure re-census:** a simple chain whose direction is derivable without an anchor would break C1.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :121, Fig 11 :39-40 and :66, Fig 2 :29 and :39-40, and Fig 1 :19. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one field case you know: is the mechanism habitat-specific, or is the design sufficient on its own?
3. Check C3 against one design you know: did it actually hold a third factor fixed, and were the two frames drawing on separate evidence?
4. Name one instrument that would separate trust-driven cooperation from institution-driven cooperation, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
