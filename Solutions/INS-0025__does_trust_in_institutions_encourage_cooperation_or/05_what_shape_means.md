## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

*The Library of Babel* (re-read): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form an answer must have before it can order the direction. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *the relation among trust, institution and cooperation is a union compound; its direction is not orderable by covariance; its capability is habitat-bound; its loop sign is open.*
- A name masquerading as a shape: *trust causes cooperation* or *institutions only work where trust exists*. Both fill the slot that the figures leave open.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* (re-read): "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* (re-read), the bundle test: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the direct warrant for F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described in effect terms (§0 of the audit) before any world field was chosen.
- Fig 6 :30 (as quoted in the metalaws note): "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (re-read): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends refuters, not architecture.

## 3. What licenses a name (here: a direction)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a reading that does not update on what the frames think (Fig 2 :29, :33), not a survey correlation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. World sources here are such frames, but they disagree, and they share a broad evidence pool; they corroborate constraints, not a direction.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-read) gives the mechanism for why a reading cannot name its source: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The trust/cooperation correlation is that projection. The remedy in the essay is an instrument: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

## 4. Why "A or B" is the specific forbidden error

The question's own form commits the direction before the run. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." The remedy there is a discipline: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

I did that in §0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: the sought-for shape is bound as a term with a refuter. As a result, "trust drives cooperation" and "institutions need trust" are ordinary claims under test, not the frame's invisible preference. The Library of Babel (re-read) names the same trap: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I first wrote that world sources could license a direction if they were "experimental." That overstates it. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-read): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed." Randomization is an instrument, not a validator by itself; it still has to range against a ground that does not update. And Fig 2 :40 disqualifies agreement from a shared pool.

Corrected form: a randomized result licenses an ordering only when (a) the anchor does not update on the frames, (b) two low-overlap frames separately range against it, and (c) the residual is read per slot. Until then, world sources corroborate the shape's constraints and stop there. Both the first reading and this correction are kept visible.

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-read): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. In every stable system, the two are separated. The thing that earns trust is not the same as the thing that gets to act." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of well known trusting researchers assembled to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuters.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (re-read): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Honest stop

*Library of Babel* (re-read): "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The stop here is where the figures cease to discriminate and the instruments have not run. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The gap is recorded; the line is held open.
