## What is being decided, in effect terms

Two readings are offered. Reading A says trust in institutions is a driver: raise trust and cooperation follows. Reading B says institutions work where trust already exists: the trust ecology is a precondition and an imported institution without it fails. Each leads to a different lever. A spends budget on changing reported trust. B spends budget on enumerating and rebuilding the habitat. The residual is the observed covariance of trust, institutional quality and cooperation, whose structure (chain, cycle, common cause, or anchor-and-frames triangulation) is not given by the covariance itself.

**What an answer would look like:** a shape statement about which bonds can hold among trust, institution and cooperation, plus a refuter, plus the name of the ground reading that would settle the direction. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction".
