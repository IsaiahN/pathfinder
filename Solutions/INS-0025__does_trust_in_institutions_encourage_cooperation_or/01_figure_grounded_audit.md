## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question offers two readings of one observed covariance, trust in institutions and cooperative behaviour:

- **A:** trust encourages cooperation. Raise trust and cooperation follows.
- **B:** institutions work well mainly where trust already exists. The trust ecology is a precondition; an institution moved into a low-trust setting is expected to fail.

**What each leads to.** A spends budget on changing trust (transparency, information, performance messaging) and predicts cooperation will follow. B spends budget on enumerating the habitat (history of corruption, enforcement consistency, inequality, local association) and predicts that transplanting the institution alone fails. They are not just different opinions; they select different levers and different grounds.

**R (the residual).** The structure of the relation among trust, institution and cooperation: is it a one-way sequence, a two-way cycle, a shared common cause, or a compound of these? Arity: at least three terms. Symmetry: the question assumes an ordered bond; the observed covariance is symmetric and cannot by itself order anything. Scale: individual (micro), group and agency (meso), society (macro).

**Fig 1 :19** "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." **Fig 1 :22** "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." So the residual must be held per slot: per institution, per community, per intervention. A global "does trust encourage cooperation" average is exactly the signal-killing move the figure forbids.

**Direction bound before the run.** Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", I bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term now: *a figure-licensed answer must name the bond structure among the terms, the anchor that would order it, and the per-slot residual that would register against that anchor; if no line orders the structure, the answer is a gap, not a filled slot.* It is refusable by the same test as everything else (§3).

## 3. Findings F1 to F7

### F1: The forced choice is chain-shaped; the residual is a union compound (general)

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

*The Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

The question "A or B" assumes a single directed bond between trust and cooperation. The figure set has both a directed bond (→ sequence) and a cycle whose sign decides closure. A chain cannot close by construction, so if the real relation is trust → cooperation → institutional performance → trust, the question's own form makes it unanswerable. Four separable conditions are present:

1. trust enabling cooperation (Kim et al. 2022; Kuipers 2022);
2. institutional quality producing trust (Martinangeli et al. 2023; Murtin et al. 2026);
3. a third factor producing both (Dinesen et al. 2020);
4. a possible non-relation (Bauer et al. 2019 found none in a real data-sharing situation).

**Verdict:** refuse A-or-B as one question. Keep the union, unbundled, with its own loop. Mode `general`. Triangulated across Fig 12, Fig 9 and the essay's bundle test (low overlap between a bond definition, a search rule and a search narrative).

**Refuter (F1):** a figure re-census that shows the trust/cooperation relation is a simple chain, that its bond is one of the seven, and that its direction is derivable without an anchor. If that holds, the union reading is wrong and should be withdrawn, not softened.

### F2: Covariance is not a direction verdict (specified; gap on direction)

Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

The two collapses are both live here:

- Fig 2 :13 "no anchor; each updates on the others": trust and perceived institutional performance can co-produce each other. A person who complies because they expect others to comply then reports higher trust; the next compliance decision uses that higher trust.
- Fig 2 :19 "they never talk, and still agree": both can be driven by a shared condition such as local inequality or ethnic diversity (Dinesen et al. 2020), with no causal path between them.

World sources do not break the tie. Kim et al. 2022: "it is hard to identify the effect of beliefs regarding others' trustworthiness on cooperation in natural settings." Bauer et al. 2019: "We found that there is no relationship between trust and cooperation." Martinangeli et al. 2023: "Our contributions are the first causal experimental evidence on the link between institutional quality and trust." Murtin et al. 2026: trust "hinges primarily on perceived governance quality." These are compatible with A, with B, with a cycle, and with no link; the disagreement is real, not a matter of emphasis.

**Verdict:** `gap` on the direction from covariance alone. Not silence: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The gap says what is owed, not that no answer exists. Mode `specified` on the claim that covariance cannot order. Triangulated (Fig 2 collapses + Fig 9 witness rule + four independent world designs).

**Refuter (F2):** a randomized intervention that moves trust while holding institution and habitat fixed, produces a monotone per-slot change in measured cooperation, in two low-overlap frames ranging against the same unmoved anchor, with no reverse path detected. If both frames fire, covariance plus intervention has ordered the direction and the gap closes.

### F3: Institutional capability is institution-and-habitat (specified)

Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable"
Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
Fig 4 :34-35 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

Goist & Kern 2018 find that traditional institutions in Buganda increase cooperation through access to social hierarchies, not through peer effects: "This suggests that traditional institutions increase cooperation, because individuals expect that their cooperation and investment will be rewarded by traditional social elites." That is a habitat-specific mechanism, not a property of "institution" as such. Murtin et al. 2026 find trust is built "through repeated, positive interactions with well-functioning public services," also a habitat claim. Dinesen et al. 2020 find a negative relation between ethnic diversity and social trust across 87 studies, a condition of the habitat.

**Verdict:** the B-like reading survives as a *constraint on transfer*: an institution carries no cooperation in isolation; moving it substitutes a habitat and you inherit actors you did not choose (Fig 11 :67 "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose"). This is not proof that B's causal direction is the true one. Mode `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 4 + three world designs).

**Refuter (F3):** a field transplant of an institution into a low-trust habitat yields sustained per-slot cooperation with no reproduction of the trust habitat and no imported actors. That would break the habitat constraint; B's transfer warning would be wrong.

### F4: The ground must not update on the frames (general)

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Using reported trust to explain cooperation and then using cooperation to confirm the trust report is a mirror pair. Kuipers 2022, writing from AI and robotics, warns that a poorly formulated model "may be uniquely harmful, in cases where the action it recommends deliberately exploits the vulnerability and violates the trust of cooperative partners"; widespread use can "erode the overall levels of trust in the society." That is a mutual-update path across a low-overlap field, and it corroborates the figure's warning rather than deciding this case.

**Verdict:** any answer that orders A or B using only mutually updating readings is disqualified as a verdict. Mode `general`. Triangulated (Fig 2 + Seat Map law on correlation + the AI/robotics source).

**Refuter (F4):** demonstrate an anchor for the direction question that changes its answer when a frame's opinion changes, yet still settles the question. By definition that is a frame, not an anchor; if someone shows the definition is wrong, F4 falls.

### F5: Per-slot residuals, never a global average (general)

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Bauer et al. 2019 found no trust/cooperation relation in one real situation while the laboratory literature is mixed; Dinesen et al. 2020 found the diversity/trust relation is stronger for local measures; Kim et al. 2022 stress natural settings. A cross-society average can be near zero while one institution or one community carries a live residual. That is a legal state per Fig 1, not an inert one.

**Verdict:** the question as phrased at the level of "institutions" and "cooperation" is under-specified. The answer must be per slot. Mode `general`. Single-source for the figure rule (Fig 1), corroborated by three world designs that disagree at different slots.

**Refuter (F5):** a measurement showing that a global trust/cooperation residual predicts per-slot cooperation better than the per-slot residuals themselves, across both low-overlap frames. Then aggregation is carrying signal and Fig 1's slot rule has been misapplied here.

### F6: Trust may be a catalyst or a spent stock; the sign is not given (specified)

Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."
Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."

The two offered readings differ in the sign of the loop. If trust is a catalyst, cooperation regenerates it; if trust is a spent stock, cooperation depletes it. The figures name both possibilities (spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is) but do not select one. Kuipers 2022 supplies the enabling reading, "Where that trust exists, cooperation is possible"; the encyclopedic overview records the asymmetry between building and destroying trust. Neither is a ground reading.

**Verdict:** loop sign is an open instrument question. Mode `specified`. Single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), with world sources only showing that both signs are discussable.

**Refuter (F6):** a within-slot time series in which repeated cooperation monotonically depletes or monotonically regenerates measured trust, replicated in the two low-overlap frames. That would name the sign and close this sub-gap.

### F7: What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (specified)

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

The trust/cooperation correlation is a projection of several possible sources (A, B, common cause, cycle, none). The remedy in the essay is a spectrometer, not an argument. Here the corresponding instruments are randomized interventions with separate experience in two frames whose closures differ (Fig 8 :29 "low overlap: B does not draw on A's evidence"; Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone").

**Verdict:** the figures say what would close the question; they do not close it. Mode `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 8 + the source essay + the paired world designs).

**Refuter (F7):** a single instrument that, in one run and at one slot, separates trust-driven cooperation from institution-driven cooperation from common-cause covariance, with a recorded lineage. Then the " spectrometer is owed" claim is too strong.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter for the whole shape claim

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is *the two readings form a union compound whose direction cannot be ordered by covariance; capability is habitat-bound; the ground must not update*. It is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **R1 (kills the union/shape claim):** a randomized intervention on trust, holding institution and habitat fixed, produces a monotone per-slot change in measured cooperation in two low-overlap frames ranging against the same unmoved anchor, with no reverse path.
2. **R2 (kills the habitat claim):** an institution transplanted into a low-trust habitat produces sustained per-slot cooperation with no reproduction of the trust habitat and no imported actors.
3. **R3 (kills the figure reading):** a re-census shows the trust/cooperation relation is a simple chain whose bond is one of the seven and whose direction is figure-derivable without an anchor.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the audit is rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
