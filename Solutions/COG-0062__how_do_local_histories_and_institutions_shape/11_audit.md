## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historian's claim, for example "the 1840 council caused the shift in authority judgments because it was a prestigious institution".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source-discriminating instrument** required before a cause may be named. The shorthand says "gap"; the concrete claim names a body as if the reading settled the source. This is the metamerism error in `05` section 4.
- the **convention/verdict seam**. The shorthand says codes are conventions until the ground pays. A concrete argument that cites the code as the verdict has crossed the seam silently (Fig 10 :47).
- the **valence condition**. The shorthand says an institution changes sign with the residual. A concrete claim that the council is the cause treats its valence as a property, which Fig 11 :49 forbids.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the four slots must be indexed separately, the habitat must be enumerated, and the anchor is observed human judgment. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
