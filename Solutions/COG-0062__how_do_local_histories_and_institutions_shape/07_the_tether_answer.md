# The Tether's answer on local histories, institutions, and moral judgment

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The gap between what a person's local moral library predicts and what the local community actually returns varies systematically by place, by institution, by historical moment, and by which of four judgment slots is in play. The four slots are fairness, harm, loyalty, and authority. R is indexed per slot and never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The question is a **union compound**, not four independent questions: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor is observed human judgment.** The question is constitutively about people. Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Local practice and sanctions, measured per case and per slot, settle. Moral theory, institutional self-description, and prestige do not.
2. **Judgments are a property of person-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* So the answer to "how" is not a list of individual traits. It is an enumeration of who acts, what conditions bind, and what relations carry sanction and trust.
3. **The habitat is enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Actors, conditions, relations, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). The deliverable for a case is that list, read off records, not a coefficient.
4. **Institutions change valence with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The same council is protector under one local situation and predator under another. There is no standing list of fairness-producing institutions (Fig 11 :51).
5. **History enters as a chain of handoffs and imports, not as a recording.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A tradition that only repeats its own closure drifts coherently (Fig 2 :34). New judgmental reach arrives through contact with a differing closure, which is the only operator that moves the wall (Fig 6 :23).
6. **The four slots stay separate and are treated as union compounds.** Nepotism is fairness plus loyalty; an order to injure is authority plus harm. These are specific compounds, not mixtures. A single moral score has already thrown away the signal (Fig 1 :22).
7. **Declared codes are conventions until practice settles them.** Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* A written norm is useful for coordination and makes no claim about the world until the ground pays for it.
8. **The specific cause is not named.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The shape is returned; the name awaits a source-discriminating instrument (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal moral psychology that dissolves cultural difference. The claims are structural and `specified`, not a theorem about human nature.
- Not a replacement for cross-cultural moral psychology or institutional history. Those fields supply the ground readings and the enumerations this derivation cannot.
- Not a claim that any named institution (council, temple, court, guild) caused a particular historical shift. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not a claim that the moral-foundation kinds are the framework's six-term substrate. That was a strain, dissolved by re-census in `01` section 2.
- Not a derivation of o' from b alone. An agent-only explanation that changes nothing about contact is refused (Fig 11 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No local judgment distribution was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating reading of local records, plus per-slot measurement of actual judgment. The relevant questions:

- **Slot test:** does the locality's fairness, harm, loyalty, and authority pattern remain distinct under a measurement that does not pool them? A single score that predicts each slot prospectively would falsify the indexing claim.
- **Habitat substitution test:** does the pattern change when actors, conditions, relations, and sanctions are independently measured as changed, with contact maintained? Invariance falsifies F1.
- **Handoff test:** does the locality's tradition stay the same closure while still producing new ground-verified discriminations without import? If yes, Fig 7 :36 is broken.
- **Code-versus-practice test:** does the written code alone predict the judgments with no reading of practice? If yes, Fig 10 :47 is broken.
- **Standing falsifiers from the record:** the six in `01` section 5, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The enumerated habitat is drawn from what the instruments of institutional and cultural history can currently read. What those instruments cannot yet discriminate is the residual, not absence. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The derivation stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (How Intelligence Actually Works). A search that finds no specific cause is never a proof that none existed (Fig 8 :39).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a new universal moral psychology and does not name the institution that changed a people's judgments. It says that fairness, harm, loyalty, and authority are four separate slots whose residual must never be averaged; that the ground for a question about people is observed human judgment, not theory or prestige; that judgment is a property of person-and-habitat, so the way to explain a local difference is to enumerate that habitat from records rather than compose a general coefficient; that institutions change sign when the local situation changes; and that a tradition is a chain of handoffs and imports, not a stored recording. It keeps declared codes as conventions until practice settles them, and it leaves the specific historical cause open until an instrument can discriminate among sources that produce the same reading. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument-shaped description that would close it.
