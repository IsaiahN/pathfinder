## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not *whether* local histories and institutions matter but *how* the residual stays small or grows. In effect terms:

- **b** is a person's belief about what a local situation affords morally, formed from private history and inherited priors, mixed by `w` (Symbols table).
- **a** is the judgment or act: condemn, excuse, obey, disobey, distribute, punish.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the person's library of moral vocabulary, stories, and precedents predicts they will judge.
- **o′** is what the local ground returns: actual practice, sanctions, rulings, and the observed distribution of judgments in that community.
- **s** is the case slot. There are four named slots in the question: fairness, harm, loyalty, authority. R is indexed per slot and never averaged across them (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

- Option A, "judgments are the output of a universal moral faculty that local colouring merely decorates": leads to the prediction that the four slots move together and that a person's pattern is invariant across a habitat substitution that changes actors, conditions, and sanctions.
- Option B, "judgments are a property of person-and-habitat, and local history is the habitat's lineage": leads to the prediction that the pattern changes with the enumerated habitat, that the four slots move differently, and that the relevant history is a chain of handoffs and imports rather than a stored recording (Fig 11 :39, Fig 7 :12-13, Fig 6 :23).

**What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula, "Direction"): (i) an enumeration of the habitat variables, actors, conditions, and relations, that must be read off a locality to make R small prospectively and per slot; (ii) an instrument that discriminates a failure of contact or channel from a failure of reach or closure; and (iii) a statement of which of the four slots form a union compound. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the pre-registered refuter in `01` section 5 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. The specific attribution of which local institution caused which judgment is declared a `gap`, not filled with a name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the cognitive-science question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of moral judgment.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
