## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: enumerate the habitat, keep the four slots separate, use human judgment as the anchor, expect imports from differing closures, and do not name the specific cause until an instrument can discriminate sources. A name would be "the temple council caused the shift in authority judgments". The shape is "an institution now serving as a room handed along, with a valence relative to the residual".

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The essay names the untransmittable part as **pretext**, the history the listener brings before the words arrive: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* That is the local history this question is about.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the union unbundling in `01` section 1.3.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, Fig 2 :37 makes the ground explicit: human judgment, because the question is about people.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* This is why the universal moral foundations cannot be treated as the framework's substrate: they are a visible guess from one discipline, and the framework's own six are already a visible guess from another.

## 4. Why the specific-cause answer is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The reading does not determine the source: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from the spectrometer, not from an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a historian observes that a community's authority judgments shifted in 1840 and names the cause as the new council. Several paths can produce the same shift (the council, a concurrent market change, an imported legal code, a generational handoff with a broken bracket). The reading does not discriminate among them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument over the records, not a confident name. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q - What licenses a particular institution as the cause? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular cause is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test and a source-discriminating instrument. Until one of those, the name is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does `01` commit the error the README forbids?** No. F9 declares the specific attribution a `gap` and points to the missing instrument. Naming a cause from the record alone would be the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources give the same reading.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any institution. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive or instrument not held in Γ. A body already present in the local habitat (a council, a court, a guild) is **not missing**; naming it as present is an enumeration, which Fig 11 :48 requires. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derived* which body was causal, or that the binding moves the wall.

So the precise rule is: enumerate freely; attribute causally only with provenance and a discriminating instrument. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reason a cause cannot be named from the reading alone is the missing discriminator, not a ban on mentioning held actors. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious historians to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let the ground, observed local judgment per slot, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay is blunter: outputs produced by prompting an LLM with the figures *"must be seen as simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
