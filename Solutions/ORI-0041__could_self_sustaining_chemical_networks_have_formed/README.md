## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether some metabolism-first story is attractive, but whether the residual of a chemical network can stay small across successive turnovers while the network holds no heritable genetic template. In effect terms: a network library Γ (molecules, catalysts, reactions, a boundary) predicts a return Γ(b,a) under an ambient flux a of food molecules and energy; the environment returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per network-and-compartment slot, not averaged over all prebiotic chemistry (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The ground enters as the only metric (Fig 1 :23).

- **Option A, genetics first:** a network cannot keep R small across generations without a genetic molecule that stores heritable sequence and also catalyzes. The residual only closes once the template exists.
- **Option B, metabolism first:** a catalytic cycle plus a spendable gradient plus a compartment can keep R small with no template at all. The genetic molecule arrives later as a provenanced import onto an already settled network.
- **What an answer would look like**, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* stating the conditions that must co-hold for a slot's R to stay small across turnover without a template, plus an instrument that would discriminate a failure of one condition from a failure of another. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing, and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in its structural form, kept per domain and never averaged. The literal historical question, which came first on Earth, is recorded as `gap`: the framework returns a shape, not a name and not a date.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL public contact page publishes institutional addresses; they are not copied here and are not to be used for unsolicited bulk outreach.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a metabolism-first claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles whether self-sustaining networks actually preceded genetic molecules on Earth, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of life's origin.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
