## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that self-sustaining chemical networks could have formed before genetic molecules. Option A: a heritable genetic template is a structural prerequisite, so a network cannot close its residual before the template exists. Option B: a catalytic cycle, a spendable gradient, a maintained compartment and a compounding selection channel can close the residual without a template, so the template can later compose over a settled network. What each leads to: A makes "before" a chronological claim the figures have no instrument for; B returns a four-condition shape, a compositional order and a stated `gap` for the historical sequence, plus refuters written in advance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three-place in base form (Γ network, a ambient flux, o′ environmental return), indexed per network-and-compartment slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, :22). In practice it is a four-condition conjunction. Symmetry: "self-sustaining" is a threshold (Fig 12 :87) and catalytic closure is an ordered cycle (Fig 12 :121), not a symmetric join. Scale: molecular reactions, network or autocatalytic set, compartment or protocell, population and habitat; each crossing is lossy (Fig 4 :30). Two meanings of "before" follow: compositionally prior within a scale, or temporally prior across scales. They are not the same claim.
- **D1 Reaction network.** Actors: molecules, catalysts, reactions, food set. Currency: mass and electron flux, catalytic closure. Anchor: does the cycle close and keep producing under flux. Admitted.
- **D2 Energy and gradient environment.** Actors: redox couples, metal sulfides, proton and thermal gradients. Currency: spendable free energy. Anchor: a gradient that can be spent and does not update on any frame's opinion (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D3 Compartment and boundary.** Actors: vesicles, mineral pores, surfaces. Currency: containment, concentration, contact. Anchor: continued contact between products and catalysts (Fig 11 :39). Admitted.
- **D4 Variation-and-selection channel.** Actors: network and compartment variants, turnover, drift. Currency: differential persistence. Anchor: the ground, what survives is what the ground kept (Fig 11 :36). Admitted.
- **X1 Genetic molecule as a priori prerequisite.** No anchor named for why it must precede D1 to D3. When the genetics-first frame names one (a molecule that stores heritable sequence and catalyzes), it belongs in D4 as a later import, not a precondition. Absorbed into D4.
- **X2 "First metabolism resembled a modern pathway".** A retrodiction already inside D1 and D2, not a further domain. Absorbed.

**Union, not partition.** The four conditions overlap; one lever clears only the region where one condition binds and leaves the intersections (Fig 9 :55, :57). **Dependency order:** D2 gradient, D1 cycle, D3 compartment, D4 compounding channel (Fig 3 :20). The middle link, terms for what a situation affords and what counts as progress, has no instrument in most prebiotic discussion (Fig 3 :27), so claims below that break are readings of nothing until a discriminator is named.

**Strain test.** Fig 13 :75 ("two states never bond directly") was tried on reaction networks and declined: it governs the six substrate kinds, not molecular species. No strain remains after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is Option B in its structural form, kept per domain and never averaged. A self-sustaining network is a cycle of catalysts that survive their own use, driven by a spendable gradient, held in a compartment that maintains contact, and subject to differential persistence that can compound without a genetic author. Once settled, such a network becomes an atom for whatever composes over it, so a genetic molecule can compose over an already persistent network. That is the metabolism-first ordering as a composition relation, not a date. The historical sequence, the identity of the first network and whether this actually happened on Earth are a `gap`, reported as unreached at this budget rather than as silence. Candidate instantiations are isomers over a shared atom set, or members of a functional group if the set is not shared; the figure does not select among them. World sources corroborate the shape and the dependency order; the two conflicts constrain particular instantiations and were returned to the census without flipping the verdict. Mode `specified` on the shape, `general` on the recursion, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the shape is wrong, the error is in one of four named conditions with a named refuter, so it is findable rather than invisible. The larger avoidable cost is spending an outside checker's independence by sending a finished origin story instead of a residual and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 10 :42 - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the four refuters and the lineage are recorded in provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a narrowed one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A closure test showing template-free network R growing without bound while all four conditions measure present; a channel test showing differential persistence cannot compound without a template; an ordering test deriving a heritable template inside a template-free closure; a measurement settling the historical sequence in the genetics-first direction; or a figure re-census showing a settled network cannot become an atom at the next level. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
