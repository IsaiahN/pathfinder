# Expert verification packet - the Tether on pre-genetic chemical networks

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the dependency and recursion structure, `gap` on the historical sequence. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of Hadean chemistry is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Could a self-sustaining chemical network form before a genetic molecule?" is not one historical question with two answers. It is a union of four conditions with different anchors. A network can close its residual only if there is a spendable gradient, a catalytic cycle whose parts survive their own use, a boundary that maintains contact between products and catalysts, and a channel in which differential persistence can compound. Selection does not need a genetic author: where nothing maintains anything, selection ran. Once such a network is settled it becomes an atom for whatever composes over it, so hereditary molecules can compose over an already persistent network. That is the metabolism-first ordering as a **composition relation**, not a date. Which candidate (an iron-sulfur surface, an alkaline vent pore, a vesicle, an abstract autocatalytic set) is right, and whether any of them actually preceded genetics on Earth, are `gap` questions that need an instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 11

- **Statement:** a self-sustaining network is a cycle of catalysts that survive their own use, and selection can run without a maintainer or a genetic template.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 11 :36 - *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."*
- **Refuter:** show a persistent template-free network that is a chain rather than a cycle; show a catalyst consumed in its own use while persistence continues; or show that differential persistence of compartments cannot compound a lineage without a template.

### C2 - chemistry-facing; checkable against autocatalytic-set and geochemical work

- **Statement:** the conditions in C1 are not empty. Reflexively autocatalytic food-generated networks realize the cycle and surviving-catalyst structure, and geochemical gradients realize the spendable-difference condition. Over the shared atom set, candidate instantiations are isomers (same atoms, different bond, different substance); if the atom set is not shared, the weaker functional-group claim holds.
- **What to check:** is the RAF definition (every reaction catalyzed from within the network or the food set; every reactant food-generated) a faithful instance of Fig 12 :119 and :121? Does the reported asymmetry (networks generate RNA monomers, but monomers alone do not regenerate networks) hold? Is the gradient in a vent or surface setting genuinely external to the network and non-updating?
- **Source, verbatim for the isomer test:** Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Refuter:** a candidate that requires an atom absent from the others kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim; a demonstration that a template-free catalytic network's residual grows without bound while all four conditions are measured present kills C2 outright.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** a settled network becomes an atom at the next level, so a genetic molecule can compose over a network that already persists. If the genetic molecule is a new kind relative to the network's atoms, it is an import, not a minted term, and it carries a price.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*. Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :22 - *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*
- **Refuter:** show a settled chemical network that cannot serve as an atom for a hereditary composition; or derive a heritable template entirely inside a template-free closure, in which case it is not an import and this sub-claim falls.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that metabolism first is historically true. The date and the identity of the first network are a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that RNA or genetics was absent early, not that RNA cannot catalyze, and not that the RNA world is refuted. The claim only removes a claimed structural necessity that a template precede selection.
- Not that a particular geochemical setting was the first one. Naming one is a provenanced binding from the world frame, not a figure derivation.
- Not that the first metabolism resembled a modern pathway. That is a retrodiction from modern metabolism, a recording rather than a generator.
- Not that any figure line names a first network, and not that this derivation is `grounded`.
- Not a claim that field agreement or a publication count settles anything. Agreement in one evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. A reading cannot name its own source when several paths produce the same reading. Here:

1. **Closure test:** a flow reactor or vent mimic in which a template-free network's residual stays flat across turnovers while gradient, compartment and channel are measured present.
2. **Threshold test:** production versus catalyst loss across repeated cycles, the sign of the Fig 12 :121 loop.
3. **Channel test:** differential persistence of compartments compounded over multiple rounds with no heritable template.
4. **Ordering test:** derivation of a heritable template entirely inside a template-free closure.
5. **Figure refuters:** a settled network that cannot become an atom; a cycle that cannot close; a union collapsed into one lever.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :119, :121, :104, :117 and :123, plus Fig 11 :36 and Fig 6 :21-22. Eight short passages.
2. Say whether the RAF definition and the geochemical gradient examples are faithful instances of C1 and C2, or whether one of them smuggles in a template.
3. Name one measurement that would distinguish persistence by catalytic closure from persistence by a heritable template, or say why none is possible.
4. Say which of the four conditions you would test first and why.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
