## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 to :135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0041 a **shape** is the narrowed set of conditions any answer must satisfy before a candidate is named or a date is asserted:

> A self-sustaining pre-genetic network must have a spendable gradient (D2), a catalytic cycle whose parts survive their own use (D1), a boundary that maintains contact (D3), and a channel in which differential persistence can compound (D4).

That is a shape. "The iron-sulfur world did it at 4.1 Ga" is a name plus a date, and the framework cannot produce either. A "yes, networks came first" answer with no conditions is not even a shape; it is a preference disguised as a result.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, read this session: *"You should then be able to connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with."* The method is proximity and recursion, not a leap to a vocabulary you do not have.
- Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and :31 *"rather than the cause"*. So "self-sustaining" is described as a residual staying small across turnovers, not as "life" or "metabolism" in a borrowed sense.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried mapping): when contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world. This is why the outreach email asks what a checker would expect to see, before showing any theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by the time you have the exact search terms *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question, the stop is the discriminator in F11, not a list of named ancient environments.

## 3. The question is a bundle, and that is a figure result

*The Library of Babel*, read this session, asks directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

Fig 9 :55 and :57 say the same thing in the framework's vocabulary: a bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions, and what remains after one lever is *"categorically different"*. So the historical either/or in ORI-0041 is not one hard question with two answers. It is four conditions with different anchors, and the right move is Fig 9's unbundling, not a vote between "metabolism first" and "genes first".

## 4. What licenses a name, and what is owed instead

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, read this session, gives the mechanism for why a reading cannot name its own source: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the color case where an instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Applied here: observing that a modern metabolic network contains an autocatalytic set does not tell you which pre-geochemical path produced it. Several paths could leave the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried mapping): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So the figure-derived shape in this folder cannot certify its own historical reading.

## 5. Narrowing the "shape, not a name" rule for this question

A correction, kept visible as in the exemplar. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. In ORI-0041 the atoms in play (a spendable gradient, a catalyst, a cycle, a compartment) are already among the six terms or their direct compositions; Fig 13 :22 and :46 name the gradient, Fig 12 :119 and :121 name the catalyst and the cycle, Fig 11 names the habitat. So:

- Naming a candidate instantiation (an FeS surface, an alkaline vent micropore, a vesicle) is a **binding** from the world frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive.
- What is forbidden is treating that binding as a figure derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* The binding adds none).
- If a genuinely new kind is required (a heritable template as a new kind), it is an import with a price (Fig 6 :24), and it needs provenance and a shadow test.

My first pass overstated this rule and treated every named candidate as forbidden. That overstatement is retracted in section 6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and the narrowed form is used everywhere in this folder.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried mapping): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous origin-of-life researchers to agree with a metabolism-first story is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets an experiment settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried mapping): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement of Hadean chemistry and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What this means in one paragraph

For ORI-0041, "shape" is the four-condition conjunction that any self-sustaining pre-genetic network must satisfy, stated in effect terms before any geochemical setting is named. The essays and the figures agree that the framework can narrow this shape but cannot name the first network or date it; the ground, a source-discriminating instrument, must do that. Naming a candidate setting is a provenanced binding, not a derivation. The historical either/or is a bundle of overlapping conditions, so it is unbundled rather than voted on. Everything in this folder is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.
