## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figure on the minimum set says it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, but "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for COG-0057 a **shape** is the form an enforcement arrangement must have before we can say which community, which norm, or which sanction. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape is: a ground that does not move with agreement, a refusal operation, a method that crosses between people and scales, and a habitat in which capability lives. It is not: "use graduated sanctions", "elect a council", or "build trust".

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Hence the residual was stated as a missing enforcement function, not as a deficiency of "civic culture".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly what the pre-registered refuter in `01` is: a description of what failure would look like.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then "you have composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Importing "reputation" as a mechanism extends what a frame can say; importing the ground discipline extends what it can perceive.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." For this question that means a measured change in compliance when a monitoring or sanction function is altered, not a theoretical agreement that a norm "should" work.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Ostrom's design principles are a visible guess from a different closure, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a single lever cannot fill this shape

*How Intelligence Actually Works* describes the failure of a room whose members only check against one another: "the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on). ... It does not mean that the output was objective truth. That's where RLVR steps in." The ground must be immovable by persuasion. The essay also notes that "Diversity is a natural hedge", which is the practical reason independent monitors matter.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the operational version for a group: "The group's agreement is never the verdict." It lists four rules that keep a shared store honest: votes come from outcomes and not popularity, independent discoveries count most, things fade so they can be tried again, and "Bad news travels in the same lane as good news." These four are a group-level restatement of the shape, and they map onto the four slots in `01` without being derived from them.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a mere reading cannot settle a source: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." Transposed carefully: observing that a community complies does not tell you which enforcement arrangement produced the compliance. Several sources can give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is an analogue, `specified` mode, not a ground result about this question.

## 5. The specific forbidden error

The forbidden move is to take the observed regularity (compliance) and name its source (a particular norm, a particular sanction, "trust") as if the reading determined the cause. That is the same metamerism error the source essay identifies, and it is why F1 and F5 in `01` refuse to let agreement or declaration stand as enforcement. *Arrival of the Fittest* records "Three mappings offered and declined", including Bayesian updating and the habitat as an ecological niche, as the author's own precedent for refusing a mapping that resembles the framework without aligning with it. The Ostrom convergence is handled the same way: it is accepted only with its provenance record.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially treated "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as forbidding any mention of a specific mechanism. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Γ. Monitoring, reputation (a spectrum), and influence (a network) are already among the six terms in Fig 13, as is the refusal operator in the Operators table. Naming them is therefore a **binding onto held atoms**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is calling that binding a figure-derived list of institutions, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6: import adds an atom; this binding adds none). Both claims are now recorded so neither is silently lost.

I also initially read the Ostrom convergence as independent corroboration. Narrowed: the framework author reached the matching structure first and read Ostrom afterwards. Fig 8 :50 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." The record is the evidence, and it is a record of non-copying, not of two isolated witnesses.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question, the famous name on a norm-enforcement paper gives the claim a budget to be tried, not a verdict. The relevant audience is people whose public, checkable work touches the four slots, reached through the opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, and asked what would refute the shape. In the author's own multi-agent work the same separation was installed as a "PrestigeFirewall": "If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture." That is a design sketch, not a measurement of human communities, and it is cited here only as an analogue.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula" and that, with a language model, "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
