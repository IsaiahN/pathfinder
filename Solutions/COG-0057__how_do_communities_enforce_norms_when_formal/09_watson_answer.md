## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the prediction that defection pays without formal sanction and the observation that compliance often persists. Arity: two or more actors, often three when a third party punishes a transgression it did not suffer. Symmetry: mixed, peer monitoring close to symmetric, reputation ordered as a spectrum. Scale: local face-to-face up to nested multi-level arrangements. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either", so this is a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, detection and monitoring.** Actors: fellow appropriators, accountable monitors, observers. Currency: observations, gossip, reputation, previous history with a given player. Anchor: observed change in behavior after a violation is registered. No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain.
- **Domain 2, refusal and sanction.** Actors: victims, third-party punishers, norm entrepreneurs. Currency: graduated sanctions, shaming, ostracism, reputation loss. Anchor: observed decline in defection when a sanction is applied.
- **Domain 3, transmission and origin.** Actors: imitators, conformists, newcomers, neighbouring groups. Currency: w, conformist transmission, cultural lineage. Anchor: persistence across a generation or migration with contact intact.
- **Domain 4, scale and membrane.** Actors: local groups, nested enterprises, weak or absent higher authority. Currency: methods that cross scales, not recordings of particular sanctions. Anchor: compliance still changing with ground pressure after growth.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D2 consumes what D1 produces, D3 supplies the norm, D4 modulates all three. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Verdicts stay separate.
- **Order by dependency.** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Detect, name the violation, refuse, settle the method, carry it. `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 adds refuse before mint.

Strain test applied: Fig 12 :140 governs the operator set of a term vocabulary, not community institutions. Its application here is a cross-level analogue and is marked as such. Re-census resolves the apparent conflict between scale invariance (Fig 4) and the empirical magnitude findings: different objects, so no contradiction after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single lever does not. Informal enforcement is triangulation against a ground that does not move with agreement, not negotiation among peers. It requires an operation that refuses a violation, not a growing list of rules. It is a property of the person and the habitat together. Information about a transgression travels through a network and is ranked by history, and what crosses between people and scales is a method, not a replay of one sanction. Detection, refusal, transmission and scale are four separate verdicts and are never averaged into "norm strength". Ostrom's principles and Axelrod's results are provenanced imports that corroborate the shape, not figure deductions. The framework does not name which norm is right. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `general` structural claim.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states four separate verdicts and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending an institutional list as if it were a deduction, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the slots and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-lever reading and the claimed derivation of Ostrom's principles are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn; the four domains remain as separate verdicts.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An observation that changes monitoring or sanction function and measures compliance before and after would name the source. A community sustaining compliance with no detection, no refusal and no registering ground would break D1 and D2. A norm surviving a scale change by replayed recording with no contact would break D3. A cross-cultural result showing costly punishment absent wherever formal authority is absent would break the world corroboration. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
