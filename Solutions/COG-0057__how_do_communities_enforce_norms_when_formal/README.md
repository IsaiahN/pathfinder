## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Frames must range against a ground, not against each other.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the very agreement that would disqualify it as evidence.
2. **Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". This simulated derivation and its second seat share one pool, so their agreement is internal consistency only.
3. **A reader who holds the whole framework before judging has joined the frame.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Hence the residual and its refuter are sent first.

Legally, public opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that come with unsolicited bulk mail, as well as the structural one.
