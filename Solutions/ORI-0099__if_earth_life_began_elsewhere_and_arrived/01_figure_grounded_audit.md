## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The residual is not "where did life come from" as a historical curiosity. It is a **source discrimination failure at the artefact**. Two routes, A (origin elsewhere, arrival here) and B (origin in situ on Earth), converge on one present reading: a biosphere whose biochemistry, genetic code and phylogeny are what they are. The reading under A and the reading under B are the same kind of green. The contact failure is between what an instrument can read now and what it would need to read to assign a source.

- **Arity.** Two conditions (two origin routes) bearing on one observation. This is a union compound in the Fig 9 sense: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57). It is not two versions of one hard problem to be averaged.
- **Symmetry.** The observation is symmetric under exchange of A and B. That symmetry is exactly the difficulty: a symmetric reading carries no information about which member of the pair produced it. Fig 13 :35-39: members of the six-term set are distinct frames because "if two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information." The same test applies here: if the two routes draw on the same reading, the reading cannot discriminate them.
- **Scale.** The origin event is first order (a historical transition and a transport); the artefact is the settled output; the discriminator is second order, a comparison between routes by their lineage rather than by their contents (Fig 12 :104).

**What an answer would look like, written down before the run.** A route-discriminating character: a quantity, signature, or record whose value flips when A and B are exchanged, and which an instrument already returning something can read (Fig 6 :32-35). Anything else, including a transport feasibility result, a similarity score, or a list of organic molecules found in meteorites, is a filter on what might have happened, not a witness for which route did. "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict" (Fig 9 :60).

**Partition or union.** The residual is a union of D1 through D4, not a partition: both routes are live at once and the discriminator must clear the intersection (Fig 9 :54-58). D5 is an overlay on all four, not a fifth route. Unbundle before searching: a single lever that clears D1 alone leaves the D2 and D4 intersections standing because the other conditions still bind.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance: (1) read the artefact and say what is actually observed (perception and vocabulary, Fig 3 :5, :7); (2) say what would count as progress, namely a route-discriminating character (objective, Fig 3 :10); (3) enumerate the habitat of the residual from the world, not from a theory (Fig 11 :43-48); (4) pick a frame and an instrument that already returns something (Fig 6 :32-35); (5) let the ground settle the character before composing over it (Formula step 5, Fig 12 :104). The likely break is at links 2 and 3, where there are fewest instruments: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at" (Fig 3 :27).

No domain lacks an anchor, so there is no `gap` at census. The named discriminator remains a `gap` later, because no figure returns a name (Fig 9 :32).

## 3. Findings

### F1 The artefact does not carry its own route (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 13 :128-129:

> "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."
> "the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."

Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."

The present biosphere is the composed term. Its contents (shared biochemistry, a tree rooted in LUCA, a genetic code) name the state, not the route that produced it. Under A the immigrant would have brought an alien lineage that, after four billion years of ground contact, would still read as a tree rooted in something; under B the local chemistry would also read as a tree. Both give the same green. The framework's source discipline is stated in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source." The resolution in that essay is instrumental, not argumentative: the green case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

**Refuter:** a measurement showing that some present-day character carries its route in its value, so that A and B predict different readings of the same character without a lineage record. Until one is run, contents carry zero standalone verdict weight.

### F2 Arrival is a habitat substitution, and that predicts where a trace could live (triangulated)

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."

Fig 11 :28-30:

> "two silent failure modes
> what you failed to reproduce,
> and what you brought with you"

Panspermia is literally a move of the residual from one habitat to another. Fig 11 predicts two classes of route-dependent consequence: something the immigrant failed to reproduce in the new habitat (invisible until the goal fails), and something it brought with it that the destination did not choose (invisible until it acts). These are not generic; they are the specific trace classes a discriminator would read. Wikipedia's "Panspermia" entry concurs from a different field: "if biosignatures could be detected on two (or more) adjacent planets, that would provide evidence that panspermia is a potentially required mechanism for abiogenesis. As of yet, no such discovery has been made." A second, adjacent instance is a "what you brought with you" trace at planetary scale.

The "something brought with you" class includes the trace Crick and Orgel proposed for directed panspermia: Wikipedia reports that it "could, in theory, be demonstrated by finding a distinctive 'signature' message had been deliberately implanted into either the genome or the genetic code." That is a Fig 11 :69 trace, with the same caveat the world source records: mutation and natural selection would erode it over four billion years.

**Refuter:** show that the substitution law does not apply to microbial transport (that a transferred lineage can be copied into a new habitat with no unreproduced condition and no inherited actor), or show that neither failure class leaves any readable residue. This would remove the predicted trace classes, not merely fail to find them.

### F3 Two arrival modes, two different predictions (triangulated)

Fig 11 names two modes. Unmaintained: "nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept" (Fig 11 :15). Maintained: "arrival worked on" (Fig 11 :7), a seat that can "instrument, distil, introduce" while "the ground still decides" (Fig 11 :22).

Ordinary panspermia (radiopanspermia, lithopanspermia) is the unmaintained mode: no selector guides the transport, the ground keeps what survives ejection, radiation and entry. The Wikipedia entry's account of the experimental stages (planetary ejection, survival in transit, atmospheric entry) is a census of that ground contact, not a selection. Directed panspermia is the maintained mode: a seat introduces a message, and "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not" (Fig 11 :61). The two modes predict different discriminators. An unmaintained arrival should show the footprints of filtering by the ground (what survived only because the ground kept it); a maintained arrival should show an introduced, arbitrary record. They must not be averaged: "Keep verdicts per domain separate, never averaged" is the standing instruction, and here the two arrival modes are separate loops inside D1.

**Refuter:** a result showing that a single mode covers both cases (for example, that what looks like a ground filter is always an introduceable record), which would collapse the two predictions into one and require a re-census of D1.

### F4 The null is unreached, not absence, and the instrument must already register (triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

Fig 6 :32-35: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something. Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."

A failure to find a discriminator today is `unreached at this instrument and budget`. It is not evidence for an Earth-based origin, just as a positive similarity reading is not evidence for arrival. Fig 6 :27: "some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." The distinction between underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget "issue different instructions" (Fig 8 :42-43). Importing a favorite test against a budget limit only adds an atom that was not needed.

**Refuter:** a sealed-room proof that no route-dependent character can exist for these two hypotheses. Fig 9 :44 says no frame is a sealed room, so such a proof would itself require a figure re-census. Absent that, the null stays unreached.

### F5 The anchor is a source-discriminating instrument or a recovered lineage, not agreement (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

Ten scientists reading the same meteorite are one frame wearing ten names. Agreement among them is efficient for a convention about how to describe the artefact (Fig 2 :41), not a verdict about its source. An anchor here must do what the spectrometer did in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: return a value that changes with the source while the projection stays green. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." The rooms that can vote are the independent instruments and the independent lineage records, not additional readers of one reading.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the candidate instruments draw on the same evidence pool as the artefact reading (for example, that an isotopic signature used as a discriminator is fully determined by local chemistry under both routes), in which case the instrument is a mirror and contributes no verdict weight.

### F6 Describe the discriminator in effect terms before naming a test (triangulated)

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."
Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

The effect-term description already given is: a reading whose value flips under exchange of A and B, available to an instrument already returning something, with a stated failure condition. That description is the expensive part; "leaving, looking up and recognising are one step each, and cheap only in that order" (Fig 9 :45). Starting instead from a favored test (chirality, an isotopic ratio, a genome message) and then arguing that it fits arrival is the apophenia the figure forbids. The canonical figures README states the limit as "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."

**Refuter:** a figure-grounded derivation that returns a specific named signature without an import from a differing closure. Since the framework returns a kind of thing and not a name, such a derivation would require a re-census showing the signature is already a held atom with provenance, which is a binding, not a derivation.

### F7 Index the discriminator per character slot, never aggregate it into an origin score (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."

A "total evidence for panspermia" score, or a single similarity index between Earth life and a candidate organism, is exactly the averaging the figure disqualifies. One character (an isotopic residue, a non-functional sequence quirk, an adjacent biosignature) can carry positive mass while the global score sits near zero. Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." The discriminating evidence, if it exists, will be reported per character, with the rest of the biosphere recorded as non-discriminating rather than silently pooled.

**Refuter:** a proof that all route-dependent characters are statistically independent and their aggregate is sufficient, which would make the per-slot requirement idle. No such proof is in the opened sources; the standard survey warning behind Fig 2 :44 ("the effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard") points the other way.

### F8 The middle link is where this question breaks (triangulated)

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."
Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 3 :25-26: perception and planning have obvious tests, but "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress" has neither.

Here perception works: we can sequence genomes, measure isotope ratios, return samples. The missing middle is the objective: what would count as progress on a source question? Most effort in the opened literature lands on the transport stages (ejection, transit, entry), where measurements already exist, which is "the link that already works." The source-discriminating objective is the link with fewest instruments. Effort spent above the break "arrives at a step that cannot use it" (Fig 3 :20-21).

**Refuter:** an instrument or criterion that already states what would count as a route determination, with a published failure condition. Then the break moves and this finding is withdrawn.

### F9 The figure-to-answer condensation has a nonzero round trip gap (general)

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

Carrying Fig 11, Fig 13 and Fig 8 up into the shorthand "contents do not decide route, look for a trace" and bringing it back down to a concrete instrument drops at least three constraints: the two silent failure modes become one undifferentiated "signature"; the unmaintained versus maintained arrival split disappears; and the null discipline (unreached, not absence) is lost. The gap is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names.

**Refuter:** a check showing the shorthand preserves all three constraints under a stated transform. Not attempted; the gap is recorded, not assumed away.

### F11 A note on feasibility results (retraction of a first reading)

The first pass of this audit read the lithopanspermia experimental literature as evidence bearing on the question. It is not. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict." A result showing that spores survive ejection or that space dust can loft biological particles (Berera 2017, arXiv:1711.01895: "This paper will assess the ability for this space dust collision mechanism to propel some of these biological constituents into space") raises the prior plausibility of route A; it does not discriminate A from B in a given case. Same for the well-supported pseudo-panspermia result that organic molecules form in space: Wikipedia's "Pseudo-panspermia" entry says "life then emerged on Earth," so it is compatible with B. **Retracted as a witness; retained as a filter.** This retraction is kept visible rather than erased.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The answer shape is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A present-day character is shown to carry its route in its value, with A and B predicting different readings of it without a lineage record (breaks F1).
2. The habitat substitution law is shown not to apply to microbial transport, or neither silent failure class leaves a readable residue (breaks F2).
3. Unmaintained and maintained arrival are shown to make the same predictions, collapsing D1 (breaks F3).
4. A sealed-room proof is produced that no route-dependent character can exist (breaks F4 and Fig 9 :44).
5. A source-discriminating instrument is shown to draw on the same evidence pool as the artefact reading (breaks F5).
6. All route-dependent characters are shown independent with a sufficient aggregate (breaks F7).
7. An instrument or criterion already states what counts as a route determination with a published failure condition (breaks F8).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn, not softened. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
