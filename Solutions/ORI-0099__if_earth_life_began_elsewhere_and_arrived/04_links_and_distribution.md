## Two things in the fetched sources that bear directly on this question

1. **The Wikipedia "Panspermia" entry separates mechanism from discrimination.** It reports that the experimental stages of lithopanspermia (ejection, transit, entry) "have become amenable to experimental testing," but also that "there is no concrete evidence that lithopanspermia has occurred in the Solar System." The same entry records exactly the kind of discriminator this solution predicts: if "biosignatures could be detected on two (or more) adjacent planets, that would provide evidence that panspermia is a potentially required mechanism," and no such discovery has been made. Use this to explain why transport results are filters and why the null is unreached rather than absence.
2. **The source essay gives the instrumental precedent.** The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the green metamerism. The essay also says "The full set of ways to produce a reading is not known to be closed or determined." This is the low-overlap corroboration for Fig 13 :128-130: contents do not determine route, and the stop is at the instrument, not at the argument.
