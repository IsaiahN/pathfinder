# Expert verification packet: the Tether on panspermia evidence

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in panspermia evidence can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the trace-class prediction. Not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A source question cannot be settled by reading an artefact's contents. The present biosphere is the same reading under an arrival origin and an Earth origin, so shared biochemistry, a common genetic code and a rooted tree carry zero standalone verdict weight. An answer needs a route-discriminating character: a reading whose value flips when the two hypotheses are exchanged, available to an instrument already returning something, with a failure condition written in advance. Arrival is a habitat substitution, so the trace classes are what the arriving lineage failed to reproduce in the new habitat and what it brought that the destination did not choose. Unmaintained and maintained arrival are separate loops with different predictions. Evidence is read per character, never as an origin score, and a null is unreached at this instrument and budget, not proof of absence. The framework does not name the discriminator.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** two routes can produce one substance, and the route is not recoverable from the artefact. Contents therefore cannot decide between arrival and in-situ origin.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :128-129: "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage." Also Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Also Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."
- **Refuter:** a present-day character whose value differs between the two routes without a lineage record (breaks F1); or a figure re-census showing contents can carry route.

### C2 Domain-facing; checkable by a panspermia researcher against the transport literature

- **Statement:** arrival is a habitat substitution with two trace classes, and the unmaintained and maintained modes make different predictions. Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :28-30: "what you failed to reproduce" and "what you brought with you" are the two silent failure modes. Fig 11 :15: unmaintained arrival has "nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :7: maintained arrival is "arrival worked on."
- **What to check:** does the transport literature support the substitution framing (ejection, transit, entry as ground contact rather than selection)? Do the two predicted trace classes correspond to things researchers actually look for, for example adjacent biosignatures or an implanted genome message? Are unmaintained and maintained arrival habitually conflated in the field's own summaries? The Wikipedia "Panspermia" entry records both an adjacent-biosignature discriminator and the Crick-Orgel signature proposal with the mutation caveat, and states no such discovery has been made.
- **Refuter:** show that the substitution law does not apply to microbial transport, or that neither failure class leaves a readable residue, or that unmaintained and maintained arrival make the same predictions (breaks F2 and F3).

### C3 Framework-internal derivation with a measurement consequence

- **Statement:** a null is unreached, not absence, and the anchor must be source-discriminating rather than agreement. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- **What to check:** is a reported null in the field treated as evidence against arrival, or as a limit of current instruments? Are multiple readers of the same sample counted as independent confirmations? The source-discrimination essay gives the positive precedent: the green metamerism "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."
- **Refuter:** a sealed-room proof that no route-dependent character can exist; or a candidate instrument shown to draw on the same evidence pool as the artefact reading (breaks F4 and F5).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that panspermia happened, and not that it did not. No verdict on which route obtained.
- Not that a named signature (isotopic ratio, chirality, genome message, adjacent biosignatures) settles the question. Each is a binding from the literature with provenance; the specific discriminator is a `gap`.
- Not that transport feasibility, organic molecules in meteorites, or pseudo-panspermia is evidence for arrival. Those are filters (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that a null is evidence for an Earth origin.
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a source-discriminating measurement.
- Not that the two routes are strict isomers. They are two routes to one substance (Fig 13 :121); see `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument or a recovered lineage, not a further argument.

1. Is there a character whose value differs between an arrival origin and an Earth origin without a lineage record?
2. Does the habitat substitution law hold for microbial transport, and do the two silent failure classes leave readable residues?
3. Is there an instrument already returning something that could be sharpened into a source discriminator (Fig 6 :32-35)?
4. Can a candidate instrument be shown independent of the artefact's evidence pool, so it is an anchor and not a mirror?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :128-129, Fig 12 :111, Fig 11 :66-70 and Fig 8 :39. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of the panspermia literature: are the two trace classes and the two arrival modes real distinctions in the field, or an imported partition?
3. Name one instrument already returning something that could be sharpened into a source discriminator, or say why none is possible (Fig 6 :34-35).
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
