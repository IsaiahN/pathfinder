The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot requirement**: concrete candidates become a single plausibility tally, which Fig 1 :22 disqualifies;
- the **null discipline**: a null is re-read as evidence against arrival, which Fig 8 :39 forbids.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: contents do not carry route, and the trace classes are what failed to reproduce and what was brought. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
