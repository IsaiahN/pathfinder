## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using
the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and
anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unexplained shape of the flow response to a
  heat increment. Arity: three place (forcing, contacted configuration, response), indexed by
  glacier or grounding-line slot. Symmetry: non-symmetric. There is an ordered forcing-response bond
  and a comparison for magnitude, not a symmetric join. Scale: ocean basin, cavity, grounding line,
  ice stream, decadal to millennial. The membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, ocean heat delivery.** Actors: currents, water masses, heat content. Currency: heat and
  temperature. Anchor: an independent temperature or heat-content measurement. Question type: a
  state and a gradient, not yet a flow.
- **Domain 2, contact and grounding zone.** Actors: warm intrusion, basal melt, buttressing,
  grounding line. Currency: melt rate and resistive stress. Anchor: borehole melt and radar or
  altimetry position. Question type: the contact where a spendable gradient must exist.
- **Domain 3, ice flow.** Actors: ice streams, bed, sliding law. Currency: velocity and grounding-line
  flux. Anchor: satellite interferometry and altimetry. Question type: energy plus gradient composing
  a flow, with the possibility of a closing feedback.
- **Domain 4, threshold and detection.** Actors: variance, autocorrelation, hysteresis, reversal
  experiment. Currency: recovery time and persistence of R. Anchor: the ground, tested by reversal.
  Question type: instrument, not verdict.
- **X1, heat as sole cause.** No nameable anchor independent of the ice configuration, so not a
  domain yet. Absorbed into the delivery condition.
- **X2, one global tipping point.** Already inside Domains 2 and 3, and aggregation destroys the live
  signal. Absorbed.

Order by dependency: perceive heat at the cavity and flow at the slot; acquire terms for buttressing
and hysteresis; define what counts as large and persistent; design the reversal experiment; record
and carry (Fig 3). The middle link is the likely break because it has no obvious instrument (Fig 3
:27).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I
ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :40
   (contact), Fig 12 :87 and :121 (comparison and cycle), Fig 9 :55-57 (union) and Fig 9 :32 (shape,
   not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: per slot, the response
   is large or persistent only where heat reaches the contact and the feedback closes; the threshold
   number is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a named ice stream and a specific heat increment,
   as a reader might import it from Hill 2024 or Rosier 2021.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **predicate split**: "large" from the comparison bond and "persistent" from the cycle. In a
  concrete claim the two are routinely merged, and a steep recoverable response is called a tip
  (Rosier 2021 Figure 1 is the discriminator).
- the **union structure**: the shorthand holds C1 and C2 as jointly required, but a concrete forcing
  experiment varies heat while holding configuration fixed, and a concrete configuration study does
  the reverse. Each returns a result that is then reported as if it were the whole claim (Fig 9 :55).
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**: the shorthand returns conditions, but a concrete claim names a
  threshold and a glacier. That is an import from the ice-sheet frame and is priceable only by the
  record (Fig 8 :51).

One thing survives cleanly: a structure-and-contact condition must sit between a heat forcing and a
flow response, and the response must be indexed per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was
**not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the
enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures"
as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
