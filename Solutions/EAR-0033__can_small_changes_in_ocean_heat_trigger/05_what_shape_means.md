## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to
look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which
primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and
descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For this question, the shape is:

> per slot, a large or persistent flow response obtains only where (C1) ocean heat reaches the
> ice-ocean contact and grounding zone, and (C2) the contacted ice is configured so that retreat
> re-enters as input with a positive sign.

That is a shape. "Thwaites tips at 1.2 °C of ocean warming" is a name, and it belongs to the ice-sheet
frame, not to this derivation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the
  words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* The closest held vocabulary here is
  contact, gradient, comparison and cycle, not "tipping point" taken as a label.
- *Library of Babel*: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your
  vocabulary."* Each world source read narrowed "small heat, big flow" into delivery, contact,
  feedback and detector.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is
  why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with what a check would look like, not with the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the
  answer better."* Here the stop is the two-condition shape plus the reversal test.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A
   gate is not the ground."* In this question: a reversal experiment at a named slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source
   gate and a shadow test. The 1.2 °C result is such an import from Rosier et al. 2021, and it is
   recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*Library of Babel* adds a practical cause of failure: *"If you don't have the answer to a question,
it's because you are lacking certain things in the library (instruments, information) that would
help, or perhaps you are asking the wrong question altogether."* That is the `gap` in F6 of
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: the threshold number is an instrument-and-information absence, not a
silence to be filled with a guess.

## 4. Why naming a threshold from a model reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and
*"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*; the
source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the
same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't
distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about
what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that
resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a modelled velocity change or a modelled threshold temperature is a reading. Several
configurations (warm-water access plus retrograde bed; basal-channel amplification; subglacial
discharge; surface melt and hydrofracture) can produce the same reading. Naming "ocean heat" as the
source of a particular observed acceleration from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is
owed is a source-discriminating instrument: the reversal experiment plus a detector that can
separate forcing-driven from configuration-driven retreat. This is a live issue, not a rhetorical one:
Clarke et al. 2026 show early-warning detectors have false positives and lose skill under strong
spatial coupling.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action
directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it
cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to
prestige."*

A list of famous glaciologists who agree with a shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.
The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried: a check, a reversal experiment, a public
refutation. That is what `03_public_contact_points.csv` and `02_outreach_email.md` are for.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the
architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get
otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would
need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay also says
*"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the
frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`,
`08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations
checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ice-ocean claim
and cannot settle it. Only a ground reading can.
