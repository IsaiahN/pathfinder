## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what evidence would distinguish option A, a large-scale pattern has causal power over its constituent parts, from option B, the pattern merely summarizes them. A leads to the demand for a per-slot, directed, cross-scale, triangulated discriminating test. B leads to the demand for nothing beyond a good global fit. What each leads to: if the wrong standard is applied, a symmetric correlation or an average fit is mistaken for causal power, and the question is answered by a convention rather than by a ground.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a part-only model's prediction for each part and the observation under an intervention on the pattern. Arity: two conditions, so a union compound with its own ground (Fig 9). Symmetry: broken, the claim runs pattern to parts, not the reverse. Scale: two adjacent strata separated by a lossy transform. Per-slot: R is indexed by part, never as a single global number (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain 1, composition and levels (framework internal).** Actors: atoms, bonds, molecules, the membrane. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ), settlement, round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus a ground for measurement. Partition question: which compositions cross levels and kinds.
- **Domain 2, intervention and measurement (empirical).** Actors: experimenter, instrument, pattern variable, part variable. Currency: interventions and per-slot residuals. Anchor: an experiment that does not change its answer because of what a frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). Discrimination question: does the intervention leave a live residual?
- **Domain 3, observation and agreement (social).** Actors: observers, communities. Currency: agreement, correlation, prestige. Anchor: none for the causal question. Agreement is constitutively about observers, so this domain cannot decide A versus B and must never hand a verdict (Fig 9 :60). Included to record where a false anchor would be installed.
- **Order by dependency:** describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the symmetric-correlation proxy before minting a causal term (Formula step 2); settle the part model before composing across levels (Fig 12 :104); measure the round trip before claiming a surplus (Fig 4 :30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape of the evidence survives; no claim about any real pattern does. Evidence that would show a large-scale pattern has causal power over its parts must be per slot, directed (intervention, not correlation), cross-scale with a measured round-trip gap R_T, and triangulated between two low-overlap instruments against a ground that does not move. Irreducibility is level-relative, so a level boundary must be named. The weak reading (sequence and constraint) is the one this test can address; the strong reading (production of genuinely novel part-level powers) remains unsupported in the sources read. A closed round trip, a symmetric relation, or consensus-based agreement would each return the pattern to "merely summarizes". This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion rather than a test; the figures price that at Fig 2 :42 ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be revised without withdrawing any primitive; the strong reading can be dropped while the weak one stands. Nothing here asserts a fact about a particular system, so there is no empirical claim to retract. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled intervention that leaves a live per-slot residual under every macro perturbation, a stable nonzero R_T, and reproduction by a second independent instrument would move the weak reading toward `grounded` for that system. A closed round trip, a symmetric or common-cause relation, or a surplus that vanishes under the second instrument would return the pattern to a summary. A derivation of the strong reading from a source with a clean provenance and its own ground would move that reading off `candidate`. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
