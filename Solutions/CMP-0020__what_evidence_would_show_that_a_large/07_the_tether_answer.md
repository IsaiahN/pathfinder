# The Tether's answer: what evidence would show a large-scale pattern has causal power over its parts

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape of evidence and a route to it, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No real system is claimed here to have been shown to possess top-down causal power.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a discriminating instrument, not a general position on emergence. Two hypotheses about a pattern P and its parts: A, varying P under intervention changes the parts beyond what a parts-only model predicts; B, P is a summary and nothing new happens at the part level. The residual is the per-slot gap between the part-only prediction and the observation under a macro intervention (Fig 1 :19). It is a union compound with its own ground (Fig 9), not one hard question.

## 2. The shape of the evidence

1. **A level boundary must be named.** Fig 12 :103 *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* and :104 *"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. There is no level-free verdict. State which pattern, which parts, which boundary.
2. **Per slot, not per average.** Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global fit improving is the signature of the very summary the question excludes. Require a residual that stays live on individual parts.
3. **Directed, not a correlation.** Fig 12 :59 *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."* versus :74 *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."* A correlation is symmetric and cannot distinguish P to parts from parts to P or from a common cause. Require an intervention on P with parts held fixed.
4. **A measured round trip.** Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Carry the pattern up, bring it down, measure R_T. A closed gap means summary; a nonzero, locatable gap is the honest report of what the coarse description holds that the parts do not (Fig 4 :31).
5. **A fixed ground and two low-overlap witnesses.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* and :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Agreement among observers is a convention here, not evidence (Fig 7 :13, Fig 9 :60).
6. **The arrangement is where the surplus lives.** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* and :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* A pattern can carry a property no part carries without a new substance; the information is in the bond, which is priced and recorded.

The weak reading of top-down power (sequence and constraint) is testable first. The strong reading (production of a novel part state) requires its own additional ground and currently has no empirical support in the sources read (SEP: *"a lack of clear empirical evidence for strong emergence"*).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any real pattern has been demonstrated to have causal power over its parts. The result is a shape, not a finding.
- Not that strong or ontological emergence is established, or that microphysics is causally incomplete. The weak/strong distinction is reported, not settled.
- Not that correlation, prediction, or expert agreement is sufficient. All three are filters, never witnesses.
- Not that the framework names the instrument or the system. It returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any level was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not a debate, resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a controlled intervention on the pattern variable, with part state held fixed, leave a live per-slot residual? (F2, F3)
- Does the round trip close, R_T = 0, or does a stable, locatable gap survive every macro perturbation? (F5)
- Is the apparent surplus reproduced by a second instrument from a different evidence pool, or is it one pool talking? (F6)
- Does the pattern merely constrain part behaviour in sequence, or does it produce a part state with no prior existence? (strong reading, `06` Q2)

Standing refuters from `01` section 4: a zero per-slot residual under all interventions; a symmetric or common-cause relation; a closed round trip; a surplus that vanishes under the second instrument; a ground that moves when a frame changes its expectation.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns the shape a discriminating test must have and stops at the limit of the instruments available. Which instrument actually closes the gap is a ground-level binding or a provenanced import, not something the figures name. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. A search that finds no such instrument is not a proof that none exists.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not declare whether emergence is real. It specifies what evidence would discriminate causal power from mere summary. Such evidence must be per slot, because a global average is exactly how a live signal disappears. It must be an intervention, because a correlation is symmetric and cannot tell pattern-to-parts from parts-to-pattern. It must include a measured round trip between scales: carry the pattern up, bring it down, and measure the gap. A closed gap means the pattern is a summary; a stable, locatable gap is the honest report of what the coarse description holds that the parts do not. And it must be anchored to a ground that does not move, with two independent instruments, because agreement among observers is a convention, not evidence. The framework returns this shape, not a name for the system or the instrument. The strong claim that a pattern produces genuinely novel part states remains unsupported; the weak claim, that it constrains them, is the one the test can actually address.
