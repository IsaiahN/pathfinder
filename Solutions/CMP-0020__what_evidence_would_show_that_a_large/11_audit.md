## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-property shape in `07` section 2, with the rider that irreducibility is level-relative and the strong reading is not established.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a lab claim of the form "pattern P has causal power over parts p in system S, measured by a macro/micro EI comparison under intervention".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **level-relative qualifier**. "The pattern has causal power" invites a level-free verdict; the figures require a named boundary (Fig 12 :103-104). This is the largest single drop.
- the **source-discrimination requirement**. The word "causal" invites a name for the cause; what the figures and the source essay demand is an instrument that discriminates cause from projection (Fig 4 :30; the metamerism essay).
- the **two-witness requirement**. Concretisation tends to reduce to one instrument, which turns agreement into a single pool (Fig 2 :31, :40).
- the **weak/strong split**. Concretisation slides toward the strong reading (novel part-level powers), which the sources do not support. The primary correctly refuses it in `01` F10 and `07` section 3.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the per-slot, directed, cross-scale intervention with a measured round trip. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
