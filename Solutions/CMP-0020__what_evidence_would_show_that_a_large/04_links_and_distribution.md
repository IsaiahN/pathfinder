## What the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The intervention requirement is the live methodological point.** The causal-emergence literature measures causation by perturbing a system through its possible states and comparing effective information across grains. In Hoel, Albantakis and Tononi (2013): *"the macro may be causally superior to the micro even though it supervenes upon it, leading to genuine causal emergence."* This corroborates the figure-derived F3 and F5: a symmetric association is not enough; the comparison has to be intervention-based and cross-scale. It is the closest world analogue of the round trip with a measured gap. Overlap: this paper and the 2024 survey draw on the same causal-emergence literature, so they are one witness, not two.

2. **The philosophical literature refuses to overclaim.** The SEP entry on Emergent Properties records *"a lack of clear empirical evidence for strong emergence"* and quotes McLaughlin's *"there is not a scintilla of evidence"* for fundamental novelty. That corroborates the folder's honest stop: the figures give the shape of evidence, not a demonstration that any real pattern has it. A claim that this derivation settles strong emergence would conflict with the source, and with the figures' mode rule.

3. **The biology literature supplies the constitutive challenge.** Eronen and Brooks (SEP) report Craver and Bechtel's argument that apparent top-down causation is often "mechanistically mediated" and constitutive rather than causal, and the reply that in many biological cases higher-level variables such as membrane potential are distinct from lower-level variables such as ion-channel state. This is exactly the F3 discrimination: if the higher-level and lower-level variables are not distinct, the do-test cannot be fired. It corroborates the requirement that the pattern variable be independently manipulable.

4. **The multidisciplinary literature asks for the same missing thing.** Ellis, Noble and O'Connor (2012): *"What is crucially needed is predictions of new tests that will confirm the hypothesis that top-down causation is real, and not just an epiphenomenon."* The folder's stage-1 shape is a specification of such a test, not a result. This is corroboration from a different field, not authority.

5. **Conflict check.** No source conflicts with the figure verdict. The SEP entry is a caution about evidence, not a contradiction. The causal-emergence papers support the intervention and cross-scale requirements. The survey explicitly records that strong emergence remains open. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered and no re-census was owed.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the shape of evidence against the ground. The recipient checks it without holding the framework's interior. Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if curious, read the figures and the world literature. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. The shape here is `specified` and ungrounded. It is offered to be refuted, not believed.
