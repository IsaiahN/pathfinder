## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the sought-for shape is **the shape of the evidence**, not a verdict on emergence. It constrains what an acceptable test must look like: per slot, directed, cross-scale with a measured gap, and triangulated. It does not fill in which real system passes.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* This is the bootstrapping convergence the residual section of `01` performs on "causal power": the closest held terms are bond, residual, membrane, round trip, and anchor.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the question is restated as "what changes the parts under an intervention on the pattern", not "what is the nature of the pattern".
- *Library of Babel* on steering: the returned books must be ranged against the problem, asking whether the search is getting warmer or colder. That is the pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4, written before the run so it can steer the search.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the answer has been composed in the locating.

## 3. What licenses a name (here: an instrument or a real system)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The effective-information measure from causal-emergence research is such an import: it predicts the shape of a discriminating test and must be recorded as imported, not as figure-derived.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the sharpest reason. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several distinct sources can produce the same reading, so *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. For this question: a stable pattern-to-parts correlation is a projection that cannot comment on whether the pattern is a cause or a summary. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a finer verbal account.

## 4. Why a global fit is the specific forbidden error

The same essay applies directly. A summary of the whole system is a reading; the causal relation to parts is the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* Averaging the residual across parts is exactly such a non-discriminating instrument (Fig 1 :22). So F2 of `01` is not a stylistic preference: it is the source-discrimination rule applied to this residual.

## 5. Q: Does the framework name which pattern has causal power? ANSWERED

No. The framework returns a shape of evidence. Which pattern, in which system, with which instrument, is a ground-level binding or a provenanced import. Until one of those fires, naming a real system as causally powerful over its parts is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The correct standing claim is the four-property shape in `01` F9, not "system X has downward causation".

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that top-down causation is real is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether any pattern has causal power and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

I initially wrote that *"shape, not a name"* forbids naming any particular instrument here. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in Γ.
