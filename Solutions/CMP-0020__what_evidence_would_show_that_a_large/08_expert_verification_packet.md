# Expert verification packet - what evidence would show top-down causal power in a pattern?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complex-systems researcher can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape of evidence and a route to it, not a result about any real system.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The evidence that would distinguish "the pattern causes its parts" from "the pattern merely summarizes them" has four properties. It is **per slot**, because averaging is how a live signal disappears. It is **an intervention**, because a correlation is symmetric and cannot establish direction. It includes **a measured cross-scale round trip**, with the gap R_T reported rather than minimised; a closed gap means summary, a nonzero locatable gap means the coarse description holds something the parts do not. And it is **triangulated** between two instruments with different evidence pools against a ground that does not move. Irreducibility is level-relative, so the level boundary must be named. The framework returns this shape, not a name for a system or an instrument, and the strong claim of genuinely novel part-level powers is not established.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 12
- **Statement:** a global measure of fit is not the evidence; the residual must remain live per part, and the relation must be ordered rather than symmetric.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 12 :59 *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."* Fig 12 :74 *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled intervention that leaves the per-slot residual at zero under every macro perturbation; or a macro-to-micro relation that is symmetric under swapping operands or fully accounted for by a common cause.

### C2 - cross-scale; checkable by reading Figure 4 and the Symbols table
- **Statement:** the cross-scale signature is a measured round-trip gap, not a better fit. Carry the pattern up, bring it down, compare. R_T = 0 everywhere means summary; a stable, locatable nonzero gap is the surplus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31 *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."* Symbols table: R_T is `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`.
- **Refuter:** a round trip that closes with R_T = 0 under all interventions; or a gap that vanishes under a second instrument drawn from a different evidence pool.

### C3 - verification arrangement; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7 and 9
- **Statement:** the ground is an intervention that does not move when frames disagree; agreement among observers is a filter, not a witness, and two witnesses must have low evidence overlap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **Refuter:** show the alleged ground changes its answer when a frame's expectation changes, or that the two witnesses draw on one evidence pool; or produce a verdict that does not change when a well-argued refutation arrives from a low-overlap frame.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any real pattern has been shown to have causal power over its parts, and not that this packet settles the emergence debate.
- Not that strong emergence or causal incompleteness of microphysics is established. The weak reading (sequence and constraint) is the one this test addresses; the strong reading needs its own ground.
- Not that correlation, predictability, or expert agreement would be sufficient evidence.
- Not that the framework names the instrument or the system. It returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. The provenance below is documentary, not a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that cannot distinguish sources *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; the spectrometer resolved the colour case. Here:

1. Does a macro intervention, with parts held fixed, leave a live per-slot residual?
2. Does the cross-scale round trip close, or does a stable, locatable R_T survive every perturbation?
3. Is the surplus reproduced by a second instrument from a different evidence pool?
4. Does the pattern merely constrain part behaviour in sequence, or produce a part state with no prior existence?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 12 :59, :74; Fig 4 :30, :31; Fig 2 :29, :31; Fig 7 :13; Fig 9 :60. Ten short passages.
2. Check C1 against your own field: is a symmetric pattern/part association ever enough to establish a causal direction? If yes, name the case.
3. Check C2: would you accept a cross-scale gap R_T as evidence rather than as model error? State the condition under which you would not.
4. Name one instrument that would fire the per-slot intervention test, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
