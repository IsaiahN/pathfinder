## 0. The claim under audit, stated as a residual in effect terms

The question asks for a **discriminating instrument**, not a general essay on emergence. Two hypotheses about one pattern P and its parts {p_i}:

- **A, causal power:** varying P while holding the parts and their local rules fixed changes the behaviour of the parts. P makes a difference that is not recoverable from a part-only model.
- **B, mere summary:** P is an aggregate or encoding. Varying P changes only the description; part behaviour was already fixed by {p_i} and their local rules.

**R in effect terms.** R is the gap between what a part-only model predicts for each p_i and what is observed under an intervention on P. Arity: two conditions (A and B), so this is a **union compound** with its own loop, not one hard question (Fig 9). Symmetry: asymmetric in the claimed direction P to parts, so evidence must be ordered, not a symmetric association. Scale: cross-scale, two strata separated by a lossy transform (Fig 4). Per-slot: R is indexed by part, not by the pattern as a whole (Fig 1 :19).

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction").** Before searching we write down what evidence would look like. The shape is: per-slot, directed, cross-scale with a measured round-trip gap, and triangulated against a ground that does not move. Until it is bound, the shape is a preference that accrues no residual and no check in the loop can see it.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

R by arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

- **R.** Arity two (causal pattern / summary), union compound. Symmetry broken: P to parts is ordered; parts to P is the reverse claim. Scale: two adjacent strata, macro pattern and micro parts. Fig 9 governs the compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

- **Domain 1, composition and levels (framework internal).** Actors: atoms, bonds, molecules, the membrane between sizes. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ), settlement against the ground, round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus a ground for any measurement. Nameable anchor: yes. Question type: partition, which compositions cross between kinds and levels.

- **Domain 2, intervention and measurement (empirical).** Actors: experimenter, instrument, pattern variable, part variable. Currency: interventions and per-slot residuals. Anchor: a measurement or experiment that does not change its answer because a frame thinks otherwise (Fig 2 :29). Nameable anchor: yes. Question type: discrimination at the ground.

- **Domain 3, observation and agreement (social).** Actors: observers, frames, communities. Currency: agreement, correlation, prestige. Anchor: **none for the causal question**. Agreement is constitutively about the observers, not about the pattern's causal relation to its parts. This domain cannot decide A versus B and must never be allowed to hand a verdict (Fig 9 :60). It is included only to record where a false anchor would be installed.

- **Partition or union.** The A/B pair is a union compound in Domain 3 (two conditions can both be present in an observation), but at the ground in Domain 2 the test is a partition: an intervention either discriminates or it does not.

- **Dependency order (Fig 3).** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Refuse the symmetric-correlation proxy before minting a causal term (Formula step 2, negation). Settle at the ground with an intervention before composing across scales. Measure the round-trip gap before claiming a surplus (Fig 4 :30). A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - The question is level-relative, so the evidence must name a level (decisive, general)

Fig 12 :103 *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."*

Fig 12 :104 *"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*

Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*

There is no level-free answer to "does the pattern have causal power." Irreducibility is a fact about a level, never about the world. So the evidence must specify which pattern, which parts, and which level boundary. A claim stated as "the whole is more than the parts" without a level is a `general` structural remark, not a testable specification. **Verdict: `general` (structural), triangulated** between Fig 12 and Fig 13 at low overlap (they derive from different figure roles). **Refuter:** show a composition where irreducibility is fixed independently of any level, or derive the level boundary from the pattern alone.

### F2 - "Merely summarizing" is aggregation; the evidence must be per-slot (specified)

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A global measure of fit between pattern and parts is a summary in the very sense the question excludes. If a macro model's average error falls while individual parts carry a live residual, the signal has disappeared into an average. The first signature of causal power is therefore a **per-slot residual** that remains after the parts-only model has done its best. **Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure line (Fig 1), triangulated with the Symbols table definition of R as a slice.** **Refuter:** a controlled intervention that leaves R_s = 0 on every slot under every macro perturbation.

### F3 - The evidence must be an intervention, not a correlation (specified, decisive)

Fig 12 :59 *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*

Fig 12 :74 *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*

Fig 13 :47 *"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."*

The bond table separates **symmetric** bonds (swap operands, no change) from **ordered** bonds (swap operands, different thing). A correlation between pattern and parts is symmetric: it does not distinguish P causing parts from parts causing P, or from a common cause. The claimed relation is ordered (P to parts), so the evidence must be an **intervention** on P with parts held fixed, the do-style test that Pearl's hierarchy and the causal-emergence literature use (world sources, `10_source_ledger.csv`). An association cannot fire the ordered bond. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** between Fig 12's bond tests and Fig 13's directedness argument. **Refuter:** show the observed macro/micro relation is symmetric under swapping operands, or that a common cause fully accounts for it.

### F4 - The arrangement carries information the parts do not (general, structural)

Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

Fig 12 :17 *"the parts are not the thing"*

Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*

Fig 13 :47 *"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."*

This is the figure-level content of "rather than merely summarizing them." A list of parts plus a list of their properties is not the composed thing. The bond, the arrangement, is the other half of the information. So a pattern can carry a property no part carries without importing a new substance: the surplus is in the arrangement, which is priced and recorded (Fig 12 :44 *"the arrangement is priced or it is free"*). This is a **structural** claim and does not by itself demonstrate any real system has such a property. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** across Fig 12 and Fig 13. **Refuter:** exhibit a composition whose arrangement is fully recoverable from the operand list with no bond information, and show the whole has no property beyond the parts in every such case.

### F5 - The round trip with a measured gap is the cross-scale signature (specified, decisive)

Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*

Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

Fig 4 :31 *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."*

Carry the pattern up (abstract), bring it back down (concretise), compare. If the coarse pattern description, returned to the part level, reproduces part behaviour exactly under all interventions, the round-trip gap R_T is zero and the pattern is a summary. If R_T is nonzero, stable, and locatable to particular slots, the coarse description holds something the part-only description does not, and that gap is the honest report of the surplus. The gap is not noise to be tuned away; it is the measurement. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** between Fig 4 and the Symbols table definition of R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. **Refuter:** a closed round trip (R_T = 0 everywhere) under every macro perturbation, or a gap that vanishes under a second independent instrument.

### F6 - The anchor is the intervention, not the agreement (specified)

Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

The ground here is an experiment or measurement that does not move when observers disagree. If the "evidence" offered is a community agreeing that the pattern is causal, the anchor has been replaced by a frame (Fig 2). Two instruments pointed at the same thing are two frames; two people reading the same paper are one. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 9. **Refuter:** show the alleged ground changes its answer when a frame's expectation changes, or that the two witnesses draw on one evidence pool.

### F7 - Filters are not witnesses; consensus cannot decide (general)

Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

A tier, a citation count, a prestige load, or a show of hands is a filter for where to look. None of them is a witness for whether the pattern has causal power. Selecting checkers for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement (Fig 2 :40). **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** between Fig 7 and Fig 9. **Refuter:** produce a verdict that changes when a well-argued refutation arrives from a low-overlap frame; if it does not change, agreement was the verdict.

### F8 - Strain named and re-censed: "only head starts come down" is not a ban on physical top-down causation

Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

Read literally against this question, Fig 4 :22 seems to forbid downward causation: nothing comes down but head starts. That reading is strained. **Re-census:** Fig 4 governs what crosses the membrane *in a learning system between sizes of agent*, namely methods upward and head starts downward, never recordings. It says nothing about physical causal influence between strata of a complex system. Applying it to physical top-down causation is the wrong domain. What Fig 4 *does* govern for this question is the lossy transform and the round trip (:27 to :31), which F5 uses. **Strain after re-census: none**, but the near-miss is recorded so a later reader does not "fix" it by forcing the line. Matches the canonical rule that a reading which makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

### F10 - What would not count as evidence (negative findings)

- **A stable correlation** between pattern and parts: symmetric, cannot fire the ordered bond (F3).
- **A better global fit**: aggregation hides the live per-slot signal (F2).
- **Predictive success without intervention**: a convenient coarse-graining can compress without doing causal work; the world literature requires interventions to measure causal effectiveness (world sources).
- **"We cannot derive the parts from the pattern"**: computational irreducibility is not causal power. The survey source records this distinction.
- **Agreement that the pattern is real**: a convention, not an anchor (F6, F7).
- **An agent-only improvement in the measuring frame**: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." (Fig 11 :40). Better theory without a new measurement does not move the ground.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat rule, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A controlled intervention on the pattern variable, with part-level state held fixed, leaves R_s = 0 on every slot under every macro perturbation (pure summary, no live signal).
2. The observed macro to micro relation is symmetric under swapping operands, or is fully accounted for by a common cause (no ordered bond).
3. The round trip closes with R_T = 0 everywhere under all interventions (the coarse description holds nothing the parts do not).
4. The apparent surplus disappears under a second instrument drawn from a different evidence pool (one pool talking, not two witnesses; Fig 2 :40).
5. The alleged ground changes its answer when a frame's expectation changes (no anchor).

If any fires, the pattern is a summary for that level and the claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the **shape of the evidence**, not with an assertion that emergence is real or false (F1, F9).
2. Require an intervention with per-slot measurement; reject correlation-only evidence in the write-up before it is offered (F2, F3).
3. Report R_T as a measured gap, not as an error term to be minimised (F5).
4. Name two low-overlap instruments and declare their evidence pools; treat agreement as a filter, never a witness (F6, F7).
5. Use SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD explicitly for the remaining work: **SEARCHED**, is the instrument already in the closure and unfound? **INWARD**, extend an instrument already returning something per slot. **OUTWARD**, import a causal measure from a frame whose closure differs (effective information, intervention calculus), with a source gate and a shadow test. Do not read INWARD and OUTWARD against one another (Fig 6).

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there a fact here?** Yes, a union compound with its own ground, not one hard question (Fig 9).
2. **What is the residual?** The gap between a part-only prediction and the observed part behaviour under a macro intervention (Fig 1 :19).
3. **Which bond?** Ordered, not symmetric, so intervention rather than correlation (Fig 12 :59, :74).
4. **Where is the surplus?** In the arrangement, which is priced information the operands do not carry (Fig 12 :5, :94).
5. **How is it measured?** A round trip with a measured gap R_T (Fig 4 :30); a closed gap means summary.
6. **Who decides?** A ground that does not move, with two low-overlap witnesses; agreement is a filter (Fig 2 :29, :31; Fig 9 :60).
7. **What is still outside?** Which real instrument closes the gap. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Status `candidate` until a ground fires.
