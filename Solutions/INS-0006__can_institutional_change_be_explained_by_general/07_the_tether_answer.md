# The Tether's answer on general principles versus historical events

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical split, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks the reader to choose between two explanatory currencies for institutional change: a repeatable structure, or a singular route. The residual is not "which one is right." It is the unstated assumption that they are rival answers to one question. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Once unbundled, the two candidates are different quantities with different anchors, and the allocation between them is learnable.

## 2. The shape

1. **There is a general kernel.** The invariant is the loop and the rule for naming what worked, while the cast changes. Fig 4 :19-20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* What recurs is the relation between a rule, a ground it ranges against, and a record of what paid. No particular constitution or design principle is thereby universal.
2. **The historical side is in the composition, not an extra variable.** Fig 4 :34-35: *"The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* The general principle is the seed; the habitat, the order of events and the accident of timing are the conditions. The same essay that governs this question says that even with the rules and a simulation *"you would not get the same result without the exact events playing out"* (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened 2026-11-23).
3. **The specific cannot be derived from the general by search.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* A particular dated event is an atom held in a different frame, the historical record. It arrives by import, with a source gate and a shadow test, never by enumerating principles more thoroughly.
4. **The route is carried by the bond, not by the ingredient list.** Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* A critical juncture is not an additional factor. It is an ordered selection under uncertainty, after which alternatives close. Persistence is a separate bond: a loop whose sign decides whether it closes or opens. Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
5. **Path dependence is the recursion rule applied to a settled institution.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* What survives a settlement becomes a building block for the next layer. This is general, not mysterious, and it is why a prior institution is not merely background.
6. **No standing list of decisive events.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Whether a shock is critical depends on the residual being tracked and the system it hits. The same event can open a reform window for one residual and strengthen entrenchment for another.
7. **The allocation is a learnable parameter, not a fixed percentage.** The Symbols table's `w` measures how far a belief leans on private history versus inherited prior, is learnable from outcomes, and is the reason *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."* No figure returns the value for a named institution. That is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than as silence.
8. **The anchor is the material outcome, not the scholarly account.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A narrative that moves when a new interpretation is published is a frame. Agreement among historians or among rival theories is one evidence pool if they cite one another. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal percentage, a design cookbook, or a list of critical junctures that work everywhere. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not that history is "just noise" around a deterministic core, and not that principles are "just rhetoric" over a unique sequence.
- Not that a surviving institution is thereby explained. Fig 5 :69-70: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."*
- Not that this analysis settles any actual country, reform or revolution. Nothing here is `grounded`.
- Not that the figure text gives a seventh primitive. The physical six are the physical substrate; the institutional stratum inherits the loop and the naming rule, with a new currency and anchor (Fig 10 :68, *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."*).

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

What is owed is an instrument, not another argument. The material outcome must be read independently of the account of it, so that the channel to the anchor cannot decay silently (audit S4). Concretely, for a named institution:

- **Per-slot outcome reading:** did the rule persist, did it constrain the actor it was meant to constrain, was it complied with, and what distribution of power resulted, measured without using the historian's narrative as the instrument?
- **Bond discriminator:** can the ordered selection event be separated from the self-reinforcing loop, so that the loop's sign is measured rather than inferred from the outcome?
- **Import test:** can the proposed critical juncture be shown to explain a gap that the general-principles frame leaves open, with a source gate and a provenance record? Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*
- **Learnability test:** does the weighting actually move when outcomes arrive, and if not, why not?
- **Standing falsifiers:** the six conditions in audit section 5, written before the conclusion.

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is returned. The value, the name and the case verdict are not. The framework *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again"* (Fig 13 :136-138 in the local extract). Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The result stops where the instruments stop, and the lineage is recorded so that the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

The further limit is that this derivation and its second seat share one evidence pool. By Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The agreement between `01` and `11` is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not choose between general principles and historical events, because the question bundles two different quantities. General principles govern the kernel: a system keeps a loop and a rule for naming what worked while its actors, currency and anchor change. Historical events are not an extra variable inside that kernel. They are the conditions the seed lands in, the ordered selection of one path among alternatives, and the imported atom that no closed set of principles can derive by searching harder. A settled institution then becomes a building block for the next layer, which is what path dependence is in this framework, and a shock's importance depends on the residual being tracked, so there is no standing list of critical junctures. The split between kernel and route is a learnable per-case weighting, not a universal percentage; the framework gives the shape and the refuters, and stops where a material outcome, read independently of any narrative, would be needed to settle a case.
