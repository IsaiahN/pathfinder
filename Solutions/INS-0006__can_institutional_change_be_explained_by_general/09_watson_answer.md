## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the choice offered by INS-0006: is institutional change explained by general principles, or largely shaped by historically specific events? Option A: take the either/or at face value and pick one. Option B: re-census the residual, split the union, and report per-domain verdicts with a learnable per-case allocation. What each leads to: A selects a theory before describing the residual and therefore imports a frame that will seem to fit whatever was picked (Fig 9 :27-28); B returns a shape, a set of refuters and a stated instrument, and refuses the percentage no figure governs.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unexplained variation in institutional trajectories, and the unstated assumption that the two candidate explanations are rival answers. Arity: three overlapping conditions, a compositional structure, a historical route, and a material outcome, so a **union compound** and not a two-item partition. Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Symmetry: the question wording is symmetric, the explanation relation is ordered, so the two cannot be averaged. Scale: the kernel is invariant across cases, the route is singular, and the transform between them is lossy.
- **Domain 1, the compositional frame.** Actors: the invariant loop, the rule for naming what worked, the seven bonds and the reach. Currency: description length and composability inside closure. Anchor: the figures as metalaws for internal questions, the ground for settlement. Question type: partition, what composes across kinds and what does not.
- **Domain 2, the historical frame.** Actors: events, critical junctures, conditions, relations, the order of arrival. Currency: influence, increasing returns, the bond that carries the route. Anchor: the reconstructed sequence plus its material trace, read separately from any single account. Question type: union, a case in several conditions.
- **Domain 3, the outcome frame.** Actors: implementations, compliance, persistence, distribution of power. Currency: whether the rule pays at the slot. Anchor: the material outcome, which does not update on what a theorist thinks. Question type: per-slot verdict, never global.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: researchers, theories, citations. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero. Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the sequence first, then acquire vocabulary for what the events afford, then state what counts as change, then choose a lever, then carry what worked. Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* A principle applied before the sequence is perceived reaches a step whose input never arrived.

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between "structure constrains" and "events select" is resolved by assigning them to different quantities (closure and reach versus bond order and landing conditions). One live design constraint remains, the absence of an instrument that reads outcomes independently of narratives.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is a union of conditions with different anchors. The kernel is general: the loop, the rule for naming what worked, and the rule that a settled composition becomes an atom for what composes over it. The route is specific: the landing conditions, the ordered selection under uncertainty, the bond order, and the imported event from the historical record. The two are not rivals and cannot be averaged. The allocation is a learnable per-frame weighting, not a universal number; the number is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreachable rather than as silence. No standing list of critical junctures survives a change of residual, and the material outcome, not the scholarly narrative, is the anchor. Mode: `specified` on these per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. The strongest single statement is the seed and its landing conditions: the principles carry what will grow, and the conditions decide what it becomes.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a separator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its failure conditions.
- **undoable:** yes. The separator is removable without touching the figure text or the world sources. A case-level verdict can be added later by an instrument that reads outcomes, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and the retraction log in `01` keeps that correction visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that reads a material outcome independently of the account of it would settle a case; a derivation of a dated event from a closed set of principles with no import would break the import finding; a fixed weighting across all frames would break the learnability finding; a validated context-independent list of critical junctures would break Fig 11 :51; and an outcome that moves when the describing theory changes would break the anchor. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
