## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 says it "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands" and "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0003 the shape is:

> a temporal gradient, a typed and ordered cross-scale bond, a loop sign, a threshold comparison, and a measured round-trip gap between the fast and slow descriptions, indexed per place and process.

A coupling coefficient, a critical timescale ratio, or a named winning process would be a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, opened this session: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay's own instruction for EAR-0003, which the figures answer with the union compound of Fig 9 :54-58.
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly what the pre-registered refuter in `01` is: a description of what would fail, checkable against the world.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping, opened this session: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The search stops when the vocabulary and the residual agree, not when a number is produced.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." It also says "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as the whole answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

**Correction to my own step 3, kept visible.** The phrase "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. In EAR-0003 the items I name (season, temporal gradient, production, sequence, comparison, cycle) are already held in the figure set, Fig 11 :46 "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain", Fig 13 :76 on the gradient over time, Fig 12 :46 on bonds. Naming them is a **provenanced binding from the Earth-science frame onto held atoms**, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). I stated the rule too broadly in the first pass; it is narrowed here and the earlier overstatement is preserved rather than erased.

## 4. Why a reading, not a coefficient, is the honest output

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source", one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a global correlation between storm frequency and soil loss reads the projection and cannot comment on the source. Several cross-scale paths (production, integration, threshold, cascade) can give the same correlation. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a fitted coefficient. This is the same point as Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point" and Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does".

## 5. Q answered

**Does a shape reading forbid naming the temporal gradient or the bonds?** No. With the §3 correction, they are held atoms. What is refused is naming a *coefficient* or a *winner* as though the framework produced it.

**Does the framework produce a timescale ratio?** No. It says a comparison bond holds when a cumulative forcing crosses a threshold (Operators table :48 "a threshold relation between two quantities"), but it does not say where the threshold lies. Börner and Dijkstra 2026, opened this session, confirm that in most cases "the system and forcing timescales are not cleanly separated" and that crossing a threshold need not coincide with observing the event.

## 6. Why this file is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous referees assembled to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument or a long record, settle it.

**Multi-model agreement is variation, not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (carried) is the essay mapping for this; no verbatim quote was recorded in the exemplar, so none is claimed here. The practice is to ask each outside frame separately what it would count as a failure, before it sees the interiors.
