**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pass, not by this folder).

## Residual in effect terms

A prediction made at the event scale (a storm, a fire, a season) does not match the accumulated state it leaves in the slow frame (a soil horizon, an ice sheet, an ocean basin), and the reverse: a changed slow baseline makes the fast event's effect mispredicted. The residual is indexed per place and process pair, never averaged into a single global sensitivity (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). What an answer would look like: a typed relation between two scales, a direction for that relation, a sign for the loop they form, and the instrument that would settle it. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's Direction rule.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public institutional submission and opt-in venues are used instead.
