## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the interaction between fast processes (storms, fires, seasonal cycles) and slow processes (soil formation, ice-sheet change, ocean circulation). Option A: treat this as one interaction strength to be measured and reported as a number. Option B: treat it as a union compound whose answer is a typed, ordered cross-scale shape with a loop sign and a round-trip gap, with the coefficient left as a declared gap. What each leads to: A delivers a global average that discards the per-place signal, assumes a symmetric bond where the figure requires an ordered one, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B delivers a checkable shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and an open slot until an instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a prediction at the event scale and the slow accumulated state it leaves, and the reverse gap when a changed slow baseline makes the next fast event's effect mispredicted. Arity: four conditions, fast forcing, slow state, habitat of conditions and relations, and measuring ground. Symmetry: asymmetric, fast to slow is production, slow to fast is a head start and sequence. Scale: two scales joined by a lossy transform with round-trip gap `R_T`; the axis is a gradient over time, not a size membrane. Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, fast-event frame.** Actors: individual storms, fires, seasons. Currency: event frequency and intensity, per-event residual. Anchor: instrument reading of the event. Question type: many slots, registered per slot.
- **Domain 2, slow-state frame.** Actors: soil horizon, ice sheet, ocean basin. Currency: accumulated state, trend and hysteresis. Anchor: the long observational or palaeo record, which does not update on what models think. Question type: accumulated remainder.
- **Domain 3, coupling and membrane (the question itself).** Actors: processes in contact plus conditions and relations outward through the cascade. Currency: what crosses the scale boundary, a reapplicable method or head start, never a recording of one occasion. Anchor: ground settlement of a cross-scale prediction. Question type: union compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 4, research and verification frame.** Actors: researchers, models, instruments. Currency: agreement and residual, kept separate. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.

Order by dependency: perceive the event, acquire terms for what the slow state affords, state what counts as a cross-scale effect, route a probe, then name and carry what worked. The chain breaks between vocabulary and objective, where there is no instrument (Fig 3 :27). Refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).

Strain test applied: Fig 4's membrane is drawn between sizes, while fast and slow is a temporal gradient (Fig 13 :76). Re-census assigns the membrane's lossy-transform *rule* to the temporal axis as an analogue, not as a direct figure verdict, and records the strain as S1. No figure-to-figure contradiction remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the coefficient does not exist in the framework. Fast and slow are different terms of one temporal gradient, not different kinds of process. The coupling is three ordered bonds, fast to slow production, slow to fast sequence and head start, crossing a threshold comparison, closed into a cycle whose sign decides stabilization versus amplification. The transform between scales is lossy, so the round trip gap `R_T` is owed. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged: D1 `specified` triangulated; D2 `specified` triangulated; D3 `specified` on structure and `gap` on coefficient; D4 `general` structural. The claim is indexed per place and process. It is `specified`, ungrounded and a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a scalar or a summary instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The coefficient is not asserted, so nothing specific has to be withdrawn; the four domain verdicts can be revised independently when an instrument bears on one of them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be removed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing which bond is live under a given baseline, a threshold test at a measured forcing level, a loop-sign measurement, an exact round trip, or a demonstration that a standing fast-is-disturbance list survives a change of residual. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. A figure re-census that produces a line governing the coefficient would also change the `gap` verdict, but the conflict rule sends any apparent conflict back to the census rather than flipping the verdict.
