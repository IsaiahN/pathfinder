# The Tether's answer: when does consensus reflect strong evidence?

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a discriminator and a route, not a verdict on any named historical case. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe agreement on one conclusion C. The same observable is produced by independent confirmation and by social influence, so agreement alone cannot tell us which process ran. R is the missing discriminator between a report from the ground and a report about the community. It is indexed per frame, not per field: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* (Fig 1 :19). The question is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58), so it must be unbundled before it is searched.

## 2. The shape

**1. Agreement is not evidence; independence plus an immovable anchor is.** Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The anchor *"does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :30). Verdict: **triangulated** across Fig 2, Fig 8 :49 and Fig 13 :39.

**2. Three distinct mechanisms make agreement without evidence, and they are not interchangeable.** Mutual update with no anchor converges on *"whoever had the most influence"* (Fig 2 :15) and the drift is coherent, *"which is why nobody notices"* (Fig 2 :34). Common cause produces people who *"never talk, and still agree"* because *"the agreement is the pool talking"* (Fig 2 :19-21). A mirror chain produces *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). Each line is single-source for its mechanism; the claim that agreement cannot discriminate among them is triangulated across Figs 2 and 7.

**3. Adding agreeers from the same closure increases agreement and not evidence.** Fig 7 :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 8 :15: *"two names for one frame."* The remedy for fixed correlation is replacement, not more use: *"frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated... and replacement is the only refresh"* (Fig 10 :80-81), with any import debited against independence (Fig 6 :24). Verdict: **triangulated** across Figs 7, 8 and 10.

**4. Correlation has valence, and the valence depends on what the agreement is about.** Fig 2 :40-41: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* versus *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 2 :43, Fig 10 :47). This is the hinge of the question: the same agreement is good for a definition and worthless for a fact. Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 2, Fig 10, Seat Map :163).

**5. Exclusion is an authored room and a substituted habitat, not a vacuum.** *"A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned"* (Fig 10 :74); *"A conscientious selection is still a selection"* (Fig 10 :75). Stakes survive the outcome: *"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on"* (Fig 10 :61), and *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from"* (Fig 10 :93). Isolation is substitution, and *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* (Fig 11 :66, :68). Verdict: **single-source** in the figures (Fig 10 + Fig 11 share a pool); corroborated by two independent sociology-of-science sources, which count as one witness each, not as a figure-level triangulation.

**6. A consensus score is frame-internal.** *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"* (Fig 1 :27), and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22). A minority with a live residual is a signal, not noise. Verdict: **single-source** (Fig 1), with the survey-statistics arithmetic as corroboration.

**7. The ground can go quiet without moving.** *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24); the output of a dead channel is *"you have lost touch"*, not a verdict (Fig 10 :28). More agreement cannot repair a saturated or pooled reading. Verdict: **single-source** (Fig 10).

Verdicts are kept separate by domain and never averaged. D1 (epistemic) triangulated; D2 (social dynamics) triangulated for the failure of agreement as a discriminator, single-source per mechanism; D3 (institutional) single-source in the figures with outside corroboration; D4 (verification) simulated and non-corroborating.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that consensus is worthless. Agreement about a shared meaning is efficient and is the product of a convention (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that a dissenting minority is automatically right. Dissent is a live slot, not a verdict.
- Not that any named historical consensus was conformity. Naming a case needs a source-discriminating instrument for that case, which this run does not have.
- Not a derivation of the social from the epistemic or the reverse. They are two domains with separate loops.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any ground was taken in this simulated run.
- Not that exclusion is a vacuum or a conspiracy. It is a substituted habitat and an authored room, and no intent is required (Fig 10 :75).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved that case. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. **Independence test:** can we show, case by case, which evidence each agreeing party actually drew on? If the pools coincide, the agreement has no verdict weight.
2. **Anchor test:** is there something none of the parties moved, and does the channel to it still return a reading? If the channel is stale or pooled, the agreement is not contact (Fig 10 :25-29).
3. **Authorship test:** was the set of voices assembled before the question existed, by a lottery, by "everyone who qualifies", or from the habitat itself (Fig 10 :75-79)? If a participant selected it, it is an authored room.
4. **Refresh test:** can the room's independence be renewed by replacement from a frame whose closure differs, with the import recorded and debited (Fig 6 :23-24)? Rotation is not refresh.
5. **Standing test:** does anyone involved gain from which answer the ground returns today (Fig 10 :59-61)? An undeclared interest is modelled anyway.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework gives a shape and a route, not a name. It can say what any answer must look like and what would reverse it, then stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* (Fig 13 :134). Indiscernibility is not identity: a consensus that looks like triangulation and one that looks like a cascade are indiscernible from the agreement reading alone, and that is a limit of the reading, not a claim that the two are the same thing.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that consensus is worthless or that the minority is always right. It says that agreement is a symmetric join and therefore silent about its own source. Consensus reflects strong evidence when independent frames, drawing on different evidence, each range against an anchor none of them can move, from a pool that was assembled before the question was asked, with each import recorded. It reflects conformity when the agreement comes from people updating on each other with no anchor, from one shared evidence pool nobody needed to talk about, or from a chain of rooms handing the same closure along: agreement compounds, evidence does not. It reflects exclusion when the room was authored by someone with a stake, when standing outlives the result it was earned on, or when a habitat was substituted and the losses are invisible from inside. The same agreement about a shared meaning is a convention and is fine; the same agreement about the world, with a shared pool, carries no verdict weight. What the framework offers is the discriminator, the tests that would close it, and refuters written in advance. It does not name a historical culprit, and no measurement in this run settles any case.
