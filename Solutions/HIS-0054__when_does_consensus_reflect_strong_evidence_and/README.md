## What an answer would look like

Bound the term now, as a shape rather than a name: an answer is a **discriminator** that tells, for a given consensus, whether the agreeing parties drew on different evidence and ranged against an anchor none of them could move, or whether the agreement came from a shared pool, a mirror chain, or an authored room. An answer that only counts agreeers is not of the right shape. This is written down before the run so the preference is refusable by the same test as everything else (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

## Boundary

Nothing here was sent to anyone. No personal emails of named individuals, no ranking of people by likelihood of agreement, no bulk-send plan. Selecting recipients for receptiveness would manufacture the correlation the framework says disqualifies a verdict (Seat Map :163).

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on. See `05_what_shape_means.md` §7.
