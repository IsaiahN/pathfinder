## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence. It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is not a list of historical episodes that were conformity, nor a list that were evidence. It is the set of properties any answer must have before it can count as one or the other.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Then look for new terms that overlap or correlate, and recurse: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* This is Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*, stated as a search discipline.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The essay's own warning about this question's structure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :54-58's union rule, arrived at from a different direction.

## 3. The shape for this question

It is a discriminator with three checks and a scope condition:

1. **Independence.** Do the agreeing parties draw on different evidence? Fig 2 :32: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."*
2. **Immovable, reachable anchor.** Is there something none of them can move, and is the channel to it still live? Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*
3. **Unauthored pool and recorded provenance.** Was the set of voices assembled before the question existed, and can each import be told apart from a convergent derivation? Fig 10 :75: *"A conscientious selection is still a selection."* Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*
4. **Scope.** Whether the agreement is about a shared meaning (a convention) or about the world (a verdict). Fig 2 :40-41 gives opposite valences for the two. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 2 :43).

That is all. The shape does not name a culprit, does not rank any school, and does not declare a settled consensus wrong. Naming a particular case would be a binding from the history-of-science frame onto an instance, and would need its own provenance and ground.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :24: *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*

For this question, naming a historical consensus as "conformity" or "strong evidence" would require an instrument that discriminates those sources in that case. Agreement in the literature is not such an instrument; it is the thing being discriminated.

## 5. Why the source question governs this one

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism directly: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Consensus is the projection. A field can agree because independent instruments converged, because one pool fed everyone, because members updated on each other, or because the room was authored. The reading is the same in all four cases. Fig 8 :51-52 is the figure-level version: *"Only the record of where each came from separates them."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another tally of votes.

## 6. Prestige, standing and the ground's reputation

The exemplar records the rule *"Prestige can fund an action... but it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it"* from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*. I did not re-open that essay this session, so I do not present that sentence as a first-hand quote. The figure-level equivalent, which I did read, is stronger for this question: Fig 10 :93 - *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* And Fig 10 :61: *"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."*

A consensus built by appealing to who said it is a review of standing, not a review of the ground. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same standing as the exemplar: LLM output is simulacra, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (as recorded in the exemplar from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*; not re-opened this session). `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any historical consensus and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Consequence for outreach

Stage 1 carries the discriminator and its refuter, never the essay set. The recipient checks the shape before holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*). The essays go out only in stage 2, opt-in, and the figures remain the authority. Never pool a later agreement as corroboration: Fig 2 :40 says agreement about the world is worthless when the frames share an evidence pool, and these essays all share one author.
