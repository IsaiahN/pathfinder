## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the missing discriminator between agreement that reports the ground and agreement that reports the community. Arity: a union of conditions (Fig 9 :54-58), not one hard question. Symmetry: agreement itself is symmetric (Fig 12 :59, *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*), while the range to the ground is asymmetric (Fig 2 :45). Scale: first order at a judgement, second at the room, third at the chain and the institution.
- **Domain 1, epistemic.** Actors: inquirers, instruments, models. Currency: residuals. Anchor: a measurement that does not update (Fig 2 :29-30). Question type: triangulation or one pool.
- **Domain 2, social dynamics.** Actors: majority, peers. Currency: influence. Anchor: absent unless installed. Question type: mutual update, common cause, mirror chain. The absence of an anchor is a finding, not a missing name, so this is a genuine domain.
- **Domain 3, institutional.** Actors: editors, funders, panels. Currency: credit. Anchor: the ground only if kept reachable; otherwise reputation. Question type: authored room, substituted habitat, standing.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: outside checkers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is near zero.
- **Union, not partition.** Evidence and conformity overlap; a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds (Fig 9 :55-58).
- **Dependency order.** Split the bundle; keep the anchor reachable; test independence; test authorship and provenance; grade the agreement last.

Order by dependency is also the order of the checks in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` C1 to C3, and the primary and my census agree on that ordering.

I derived my census starting from Fig 12's bond symmetry and Fig 9's union rule, where the primary started from Fig 1's per-slot residual and Fig 2's anchor. Different starting figures, same four domain boundaries. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :31-32 and :40-43, Fig 7 :13 and :35-36, Fig 8 :49 and :51-52, Fig 10 :74-75, Fig 11 :66-68.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: independence plus immovable anchor makes agreement evidence; shared pool, mirror chain or authored room makes it conformity or exclusion; correlation has opposite valence for conventions and verdicts.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a named historical case: "this consensus was conformity because the field cited the same review", or "that consensus was strong evidence because 98% of experts agreed".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **case-level source evidence** needed to show which material each agreeing party actually drew on (the shorthand names independence, the case needs an instrument);
- the **authorship procedure** needed to show the pool was unauthored, which the shorthand compresses into one clause (Fig 10 :75-79 gives four distinct mechanisms);
- the **scope of the agreement**, whether it is about a meaning or about the world, which decides the valence and is not recoverable from the agreement level (Fig 2 :40-41);
- the **state of the channel**, since a stale channel and a settled ground look identical from inside (Fig 10 :24-28).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a headcount cannot discriminate, and replacing a captured frame requires a frame whose closure differs with the import recorded. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
