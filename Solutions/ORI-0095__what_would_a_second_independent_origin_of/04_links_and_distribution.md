## What the wider literature adds

1. **The independence condition is already explicit in the field, not imported by the framework.** The Drake equation page states that independent life on Mars, Europa, Enceladus or Titan would raise fl toward one and move the count from zero to one degree of freedom, while adding: "the chance they are not really independent". This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 2 :31-32. It is corroboration, not the verdict.
2. **The denominator matters.** The same page notes the anthropic bias: Earth "was not chosen randomly, but by the living organisms that already inhabit it (ourselves)". The Rule of succession page makes the dependence on observed trials explicit. These are two different fields (astrobiology and probability) but both are secondary witness only.
3. **The reading cannot name the source.** The Panspermia page warns that two detections on adjacent planets may point to panspermia rather than two independent origins. The source essay's metamerism point gives the mechanism: an instrument reading life does not comment on its source. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 8 :50-52.
4. **Arrival and intelligence come apart.** Kipping (2020) favors frequent re-emergence of life but not inevitable intelligence; the Great Filter page places the filter question at a specific step. Both corroborate the D1 verdict without deciding it.

**Overlap warning:** the Abiogenesis, Panspermia, Drake equation and Great Filter pages are all Wikipedia astrobiology/SETI pages and share an evidence pool. Their agreement is one witness, not four. The Rule of succession page and the Kipping paper are lower-overlap fields. The three essays share an author with the figures and are high-overlap by construction.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22,** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full derivation before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the independence claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42,** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28,** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would expect if independence were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40,** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* No list selected for receptiveness; no pooling of later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42,** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a false "life is common" claim entering the literature, so independent checkers are worth the effort of finding.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the independence claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The reader checks the claim without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). Any stage 1 verdict must be recorded before theory is shown.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage 1 claim survives. It currently does as a shape with a stated `gap`; it does not survive as a frequency number. Keep the shape and the refuter in front of the links.
