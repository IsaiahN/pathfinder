# The Tether's answer: what a second independent origin would reveal

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the derived constraints, `gap` on the frequency value. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We have one known origin and want a rate. A rate needs a count of **independent** origins and a count of **opportunities**. The question bundles detection with independence, so it is a union compound (Fig 9): a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Until independence is discriminated, a second detection adds a spelling, not a data point.

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

**D1, arrival.** A genuine second origin bears on the arrival step, not on the later evolution of intelligence. Fig 11 :56: *"nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* Before a biosphere exists there is no Darwinian ground. Kipping (2020, secondary) predicts the same split: life may frequently re-emerge while intelligence may not be inevitable. **Verdict `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 11 plus Fig 10).

**D2, inference.** A genuine second independent origin refuses one term: the singleton hypothesis that Earth life is the only origin. Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* What follows is a lower bound on the fraction of suitable worlds that develop life, not a number. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Counting must be per slot: Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* **Verdict `general` for the shape, `gap` for the value, triangulated for the shape.**

**D3, detection and lineage.** Independence is the governing quantity. Fig 2 :31-32: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Same-looking biochemistries can be convergence or common source: Fig 8 :50-52, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* With no lineage instrument, the link is `unreachable-at-this-budget`, which is a verdict and not a silence. **Verdict `specified`, triangulated for the independence requirement; `single-source` (Fig 3) for the ordering claim.**

These three verdicts are not averaged. "Life is common" would need all three to close, and D2 does not close.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that life is common, and not a numeric frequency. The value is a `gap`.
- Not that two detections are two independent origins. Shared ancestry is a common-cause collapse (Fig 2).
- Not that biochemistry resembling Earth's proves either independence or common ancestry. The reading cannot name its source.
- Not that current non-detection on Mars, Europa, Enceladus or Titan shows life is rare: Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not that the result is `grounded`. No measurement settled a lineage or enumerated the denominator.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here:

1. **Lineage test.** Does the second biosphere have a different genetic code, handedness and core molecular machinery beyond what convergence plausibly produces? If yes, independence is established; if no, n stays one.
2. **Habitat enumeration.** List the opportunities (early Mars, Europa, Enceladus, Titan, early Venus, and whatever else contacts the residual), per Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Without a denominator there is a lower bound only.
3. **Third-world test.** With two independent origins confirmed, does a third suitable world remain sterile across the relevant window? This fires refuter 4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and refuses the "fl close to 1" reading.
4. **Standing refuters.** Shared ancestry confirmed; no discriminator in principle; one opportunity only. Any one withdraws the corresponding claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, for the count; `gap` for the value.** The lineage link has no instrument yet. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* We do not know which this is. The answer stops at the shape, records the `gap`, and keeps the lineage so a later reader can locate the error. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a second origin would prove life is common. It says that the word "independent" is doing almost all the work. If the second biosphere shares an ancestry with Earth life, two detections are one event read twice and the frequency estimate does not move. If it is genuinely independent, the observation refuses the idea that Earth life is the only origin and puts a floor under the fraction of suitable worlds that develop life. It does not return a number, because a number also needs an enumerated set of opportunities and an instrument that can tell shared source from convergent origin, and neither has paid yet. What it offers is the shape the answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the name of the instrument that would close it. The value stays open until the ground settles it.
