## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what follows for an estimate of how common life is from observing a second origin that is independent of Earth life. Option A: read the detection as a claim that life is common and publish a frequency. Option B: treat independence as the governing condition, refuse the singleton-origin hypothesis, cut the result down to a lower bound and a shape, and report a `gap` on the value until a lineage instrument and an enumerated denominator pay. Option A leads to a `general`-mode verdict presented as a measurement and to one event counted twice. Option B leads to a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A second independent origin would reveal less than a bare claim that life is common suggests and more than a bare lower bound. It refuses the singleton hypothesis that Earth life is the only origin and bounds the life fraction away from zero, but only if independence is discriminated. If the second biosphere shares an ancestry, the two detections are one event read twice and the estimate does not move. Even when independence is real, the value stays a `gap`: a frequency also needs an enumerated set of opportunities and a lineage instrument, and neither has paid. The result is `general` on shape, `specified` on the independence constraint, `gap` on value, ungrounded, candidate. Domain verdicts remain separate and are not averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** if a shared-ancestry detection is treated as independent, one event is counted twice and a false claim that life is common enters the literature; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." If a real second origin is missed, the singleton hypothesis stays unrefused and fl is underestimated. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the lineage refuter and the denominator.
- **undoable:** yes. The claim is a shape with a `gap`, not a numeric commitment, so it can be revised when an instrument pays without withdrawing any primitive. A published lineage result is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A lineage-discriminating measurement establishing separate origin or shared ancestry; a complete enumeration of opportunities; a third-world test; or a figure re-census that admits a frequency inside closure(Γ) from two counts alone. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` and the value as `gap`.
