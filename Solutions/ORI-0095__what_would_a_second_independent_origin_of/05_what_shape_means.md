## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The canonical figures README adds that the framework can say what is unreachable and therefore owed, and that it stops at the limit of the available instruments.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the form a frequency answer must take before the value is available. For ORI-0095 the shape is: an independent-origin count, an enumerated opportunity set, a refused singleton hypothesis, and a lineage instrument. "Life is common" is not a shape; it is a value asserted in the mode of a measurement. "At least two independent origins occurred, so the singleton hypothesis is refused and fl is bounded below" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Describe the effect: a second count that does not share a source with the first. Do not begin with the word "common", which imports a frequency verdict before the count exists.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The stop here is the shape above, not a number.

## 3. What licenses a value

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: the ground settles a term, verified only by what pays. For this question that means a lineage-discriminating measurement and an enumerated habitat.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. A second biosphere is an external frame; importing its distinctions into our library adds atoms to our library (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*), but adds no atom to the world.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a value cannot be read off the detection: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. It was a spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Here the equivalent instrument is a lineage discriminator. Until one pays, the count is a reading with an unnamed source.

## 4. Why "two detections" is the specific forbidden error

The Drake equation source (world, secondary) already states the failure mode: *"While this would raise the number of degrees of freedom from zero to one, there would remain a great deal of uncertainty on any estimate due to the small sample size, and the chance they are not really independent."* The framework's own reason is Fig 2 :31-32: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Two biospheres with one ancestry are one report read twice.

Fig 7 :13 adds the arithmetic: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I first wrote that a value can be licensed by "a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs" without qualification. That overstates it. The figures README's phrase governs a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. A second biosphere is not a missing primitive of our library; it is an external source whose distinctions can be imported. What the import buys is reach in our library, not a frequency in the world. The value still needs the ground: an enumerated denominator and a lineage measurement. Retained, narrowed, recorded.

I also initially read "shape" as merely vague. It is not. It is a hard constraint with four parts (§1) and a `gap` marker on the value. Refusing a number is a verdict here, not an omission.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A claim supported by the number of people who find it plausible is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to test the independence claim, and a ground to settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, cannot settle it, and every file in this folder says so in its mode line.
