## Three findings in the wider literature that bear directly on the shape

1. **Enumeration depth is the operative variable.** Kaufman and Wright 2017 find that prediction quality depends on the completeness of the data from the agent's place of origin. Culshaw-Maurer et al. 2020 describe the first step of a programme as describing food webs in both native and invaded ranges. This is the world-language analogue of Fig 11 :43-48. It corroborates the figure; it does not replace it.
2. **A safety record does not remove the need for a ground reading.** Suckling and Sforza 2014 report a greater-than-99 percent biosafety track record while also stating that observed ecological changes were not predicted before release in at least a few cases. Both statements are true. The first is a filter for what gets tried; the second is why each case still needs a ground. This is Fig 9 :60 in world language.
3. **A named failure class matches the figure's own example.** The PestSmart account of the cane toad documents no pre-release testing to determine whether the toads ate the target beetle, and the figure names cane toads as "the same move with the cascade unenumerated." Convergence here is high-overlap with the figure's own cited case, so it is one witness, not two.
