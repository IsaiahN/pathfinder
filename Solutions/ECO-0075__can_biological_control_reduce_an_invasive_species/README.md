## The residual in effect terms

An invasive species I is in contact with a habitat H and reduces a valued ecological state V (native abundance, diversity, process rates). A manager proposes to introduce a control agent A so that A suppresses I. The residual is the gap between the predicted post-release state (I down, V restored) and the observed post-release state. Per slot, the target slot may close while other slots in the contact cascade open (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The question is therefore a union of two conditions, not one quantity: can the target slot close while every other slot stays within tolerance?

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

A shape, not a number: the set of constraints any defensible biological-control proposal must satisfy before the ground is consulted. The slot for "which agent and which habitat" stays open until a field reading settles it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The institutional role addresses on the IOBC contacts page are public organisational channels, not private contacts, and the first contact is the residual and its refuter.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that biological control is safe or unsafe.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Agreement among practitioners is variation, not a verdict, because the ground votes between rooms (Fig 7 :35).
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** A verifier handed the full framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in and submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
