## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The shape survives; any single yes or no is refused.** Biological control can suppress an invader, and the figures do not forbid it: the agent is a legitimate import, the only operator that moves the wall. But "without creating new ecological risks" is not a one-time property. The two conditions are a union compound; the agent owes provenance; risk tracks the depth of the contact list; valence is relative to the residual; introduction is available while exclusion is not, so managed ecosystems are permanent work; and a quarantine test substitutes a habitat and proves wiring, never capability. A fall in target abundance cannot comment on its source. Each case is settled by a source-discriminating field reading, not by agreement. This is a `general` shape and a `specified` route, ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the shape is used as written. It states its mode, its refuters and its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is sending a recording of past success instead of the generator, which spends the independence of the outside reader. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter and withholding links until stage 2.
- **undoable:** yes. No commitment to a particular agent or habitat is made, so no primitive is withdrawn if this shape is wrong. The four written refuters can fire and the shape can be replaced without touching the world sources. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. One instrument settling both conditions would break the union verdict; a provably complete finite enumeration would break the depth verdict; exact subtraction of an introduced actor and all its contacts would break the permanence verdict; an actor with residual-invariant valence would break the valence verdict; a control agent derived from the invaded habitat's own closure that moves the wall would break the import verdict. Prestige, citation count and practitioner consensus are none of these.
