## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form of a checkable separator that can be stated before a tipping point can be measured. It constrains: it says trust must be indexed per claim, must be ranged against a non-updating ground, must have independent frames, must have a source-discriminating instrument. It does not fill: it gives no percentage and no named trustworthy expert.

For this question, then:

- **Shape:** a weighted mixture of observation-against-ground and inherited prior, indexed per claim, with a reachable anchor, independent frames and a source discriminator.
- **Not a shape:** "trust is 73 percent evaluation", "trust universities", "distrust institutions". Those are bindings or numbers, and they are owed a ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- The residual was described before any field was chosen: a trust reading, a weighting, a possible anchor, a possible shared pool. Only then were cognitive science, science studies and sociology of science brought in as frames. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: naming a particular expert, institution or credential as the trusted one is a binding from the world frame. It is allowed as a candidate with provenance. It is not a derivation from the figures and it is not a new primitive.

## 4. The source problem, opened this session

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument that governs F7. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the reading could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or an undiscovered scheme. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The colour case was settled by a spectrometer, "not by argument".

For public trust: confidence, credentials, affiliation and citation counts are all projections of an expert's contact with a ground. They cannot comment on the source. A public that has no source-discriminating instrument is in the position of the eye without the spectrometer. What is owed is an instrument, not a louder reputation signal and not a new argument about who to believe.

## 5. Prestige is not action, opened this session

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* is the governing essay for this question. Its words:

- "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."
- "The thing that earns trust is not the same as the thing that gets to act. The record of past success is not a license to skip the ground."
- "The prestige loop confirms itself. The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige. The action is assumed to work because the person is believed. The ground is never consulted. The error compounds. The lineage is corrupted."
- "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."
- "If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."
- "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

This is F2, F3 and F8 in the author's own terms. It also sets the limit on outreach: reputation may buy the public's attention (a budget to be tried), but it may not be used as the reason a claim is accepted.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially treated the rule as a ban on naming any institutional or expert quantity. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", and Fig 13 :135 ties it to a primitive not held in Γ. "Credential", "reputation", "standing" and "open data" are world quantities already in the habitat; they can be named and inspected. What the rule forbids is promoting one into a primitive, or announcing a named expert as the framework's answer. The wrong move in a solution is not naming a credential; it is treating the name as an anchor. Both statements are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, from the section above: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. None of them can settle whether a particular public's trust is careful or reputation-driven. Only a ground reading, an instrument that discriminates the source of the trust signal, can do that.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The honest answer is a shape with a refuter and an owed instrument. It says what any instance of careful public trust must look like and what any instance of reputation-driven trust looks like, keeps the two verdicts separate, and refuses to supply the number or the name the question seems to ask for. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).
