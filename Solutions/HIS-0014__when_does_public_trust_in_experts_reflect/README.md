## The residual in effect terms

We observe a public trust reading t(s) directed at an expert or an expert claim on slot s. The question asks which process produced that reading. Option A: the reading was produced by careful evaluation, meaning the public's belief leans on observations that are independently ranged against a ground that does not change its answer because anyone thinks it should. Option B: the reading was produced mainly by institutional reputation, meaning the belief leans on an inherited prior, credentials, affiliation, standing, that is manufactured inside the same frames that then use it as evidence. A leads to a claim that can be refuted by a ground reading; B leads to a self-confirming loop in which agreement feels like truth and the ground is never consulted. The question is posed as an either/or, but the Symbols table already gives the mechanism as a weighting w with w_A + w_B = 1, so the honest object is a separator between conditions, not a percentage split.

An answer would look like a list of conditions, each with a nameable anchor, that predict whether the trust reading moves with ground contact or with the reputation signal. Bind that list as an ordinary term (formula step, Direction) so it can accrue a residual and be refused. That list is the deliverable of `01`, `06`, `07` and `09`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that expertise should be trusted or distrusted.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the checker contributes. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
