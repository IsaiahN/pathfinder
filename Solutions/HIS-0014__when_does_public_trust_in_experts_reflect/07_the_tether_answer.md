# The Tether's answer: when public trust is careful, and when it is reputation-driven

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical tipping point and on any standing list of trusted experts. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe a public trust reading toward an expert claim. We do not observe its cause. The two candidate causes are a belief that leans on observation ranged against a non-updating ground, and a belief that leans on inherited institutional prior. The Symbols table already gives the shape of the answer: Symbols :24: "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings." So the question is not which of two boxes a public is in, but which conditions push the learnable weighting toward observation and which push it toward the prior.

## 2. The shape: a separator of conditions

**A. Trust reflects careful evaluation when all of the following hold.**

1. **Indexed per claim.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global "trust in experts" number hides the slot where trust moved with the ground.
2. **A reachable, non-updating anchor.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." For a claim about an expert, that anchor is the claim's ground contact, not the expert's standing. Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."
3. **A source-discriminating instrument.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Confidence and credentials "cannot comment on the source"; the *Hard Problem is About Source* essay requires an instrument that can.
4. **Independent frames, corrected separately.** Fig 2 :31: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
5. **A verifier that cannot reconstruct the claim.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The public must be able to see the record without inhabiting the institution's framing of it.
6. **A refusal path.** Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A trusted claim must be refusable without erasing the reputation record.

**B. Trust relies mainly on institutional reputation when the following hold.**

1. **The reference updates on the frames.** Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Institutional standing that moves when the institution is praised is not an anchor.
2. **One evidence pool.** Fig 2 :18-21: "one evidence pool", "they never talk, and still agree", "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other". Repetition of one signal is not corroboration.
3. **The signal is frame-internal.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Credibility accrued is one of the quantities Fig 1 :26 names.
4. **Standing survives outcomes.** Fig 10 :61: "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
5. **A room or chain supplies the verdict.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not".
6. **The object is a standing class.** Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." "Experts" is not a permanent trustworthy class; trust is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).

These are two verdicts per domain, kept separate and never averaged. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." One lever clears only the region where one condition binds.

## 3. The powerful one-line version of the prestige case

From the opened essay: "The prestige loop confirms itself. The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige. The action is assumed to work because the person is believed. The ground is never consulted." And the limit: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." So reputation is a legitimate way to get a hearing and an illegitimate reason to accept a claim.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that all institutional trust is misplaced. Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Shared institutional meaning is useful; using it to decide a world claim is the failure.
- Not that credentials never correlate with competence. Correlation is not settlement (Fig 2 :39-40).
- Not that scepticism is evidence. A refused claim needs a ground, not a contrary attitude.
- Not a percentage. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
- Not a named trustworthy expert or institution. Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of a public's weighting w was taken.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The discriminators owed are:

1. A reading that separates **observation-against-ground** from **inherited prior** in a trust judgment, at the slot level. Without one, w is inferred from its outputs and cannot be checked.
2. A reading that separates **low-overlap triangulation** from **one evidence pool repeated**. Provenance of each source, recorded before agreement, is the minimum instrument (Fig 8 :51-52).
3. A reading that tells whether an anchor **updates on the frames**. Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update."
4. A **refutation** of a specific trusted claim that can be added to the record, so the reach falls without deleting the reputation (Fig 6 :42-43).

The pre-registered refuter (written before the run, `01` section 4) names seven conditions that would break the separator: no anchor needed; reputation can anchor; shared-pool agreement predicts the ground; standing survives residual change; a reconstructible verifier still checks; reputation alone discriminates source; the producers are a partition. If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The separator is reachable from the figures. The numerical tipping point and the named trustworthy expert are not, and reporting them would be inventing a primitive. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Public trust is a belief about a claim, not a stock of confidence in a class of people. It reflects careful evaluation when the reading is indexed per claim, when the belief leans on observations ranged against a ground that does not change because anyone thinks it should, when independent frames are corrected separately by that ground, when an instrument can tell the source of the signal from the signal, and when a trusted claim can be refused without erasing the record. It relies mainly on institutional reputation when the reference updates on the frames, when the frames share one evidence pool so agreement is the pool talking, when the reputation signal is something the same frames produce and then grade themselves by, and when standing survives the outcomes it was earned on. Reputation can buy a claim a hearing; it cannot settle the claim. The framework returns a separator with refuters written in advance, not a percentage and not a list of who to trust. The number and the name stay open until an instrument closes them.
