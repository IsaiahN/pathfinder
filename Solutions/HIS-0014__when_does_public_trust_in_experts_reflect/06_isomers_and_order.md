# Isomers and order: the structure of careful evaluation and reputation-driven trust

**Mode: `specified` on the mapping, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## RESIDUAL

The question asks "when A, when B". We need to know whether A and B are two different substances over the same atoms (isomers), two members of one kind (functional group), or an ordered dependency where one must be settled before the other can be assessed. A fourth possibility is that they are different cycles with different signs. The weighting w from the Symbols table is a mixture, so a pure partition is already suspect.

## DERIVATION

### Q1: Are careful evaluation and reputation-driven trust isomers?

Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."

Bind the two readings over the same atom set `{b, o', p, g, F1, F2}`:

- **Careful evaluation E.** b is bonded to o' by comparison against g; F1 and F2 are each bonded to g separately (triangulation, low mutual overlap); p is present but weakly weighted. Bonds: frame-to-ground, frame-to-frame low overlap, prior-to-belief small.
- **Reputation-driven trust R.** b is bonded to p; F1 and F2 are bonded to each other by a shared signal (common cause) while g is absent or updates on the frames; o' is unobserved or saturated. Bonds: frame-to-prior, frame-to-frame high overlap, prior-to-belief large.

Same atoms, different bonds, different behaviour: E is correctable by a ground reading, R is not. Under Fig 12's definition they are **isomers**, provided the atom set is genuinely fixed. In the world, R often has an extra atom (the prestige signal itself), in which case it is composition plus import, not an isomer. State the condition: **isomers only over the fixed atom set where the prestige signal is a binding of p, not a new kind.**

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either reading can be written without an atom the other requires, for example reputation with no prior, or evaluation with no ground, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading below stands instead.

### Q2: If not isomers, are they a functional group?

Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

Both E and R share the substructure "public belief formed from private history and inherited prior, mixed by w" (Symbols :24-26). That substructure fixes the *kind*: a public trust belief. The qualifier, which bond dominates, says which member: triangulation or prestige loop. So even where the fixed-atom condition fails, the honest weaker claim is: E and R are members of one functional group, differing in the bond, not in the ingredient list.

Using only ingredient names ("expertise", "credentials", "evidence", "trust") cannot tell them apart. That is the flat-formula collapse of Fig 12 :6-17: the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not.

### Q3: What is the order? Dependency, not chronology

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it." Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

The dependency chain for a trust reading:

1. **Ground contact** for the claim (D2). Is there anything that settles it and does not update?
2. **Instrument** that already returns something source-discriminating (Fig 6 :32-34).
3. **Objective link**: can the evaluator say what would count as progress on this claim? Fig 3 :10 "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms". This is the middle link with no obvious instrument (Fig 3 :25-27), so it is the likely break.
4. **Independent frames** corrected separately by the ground (Fig 2 :31-32, :46).
5. **Weighting w** applied and declared.

A lever applied at step 5 (change attitudes to expertise) cannot rescue a break at step 2 (no source-discriminating instrument). This is why "more trust" and "less trust" are both the wrong lever.

**Falsifier:** show a trust reading whose ground contact can be changed independently of its instrument and whose instrument can be changed independently of its objective link, with the residual at each link measured. If the links are not ordered, the dependency claim fails.

### Q4: Reputation is a cycle; evaluation is a chain that closes only at the ground

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

The prestige loop is a positive cycle: standing produces belief, belief produces more standing, and the ground is never consulted. Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "The prestige loop confirms itself. The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige." Careful evaluation is a chain that ranges against an anchor and stops when the ground settles; it closes only at the anchor, not back into the frame. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

A refutation of a trusted claim is not another cycle. Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". Negation is the eighth operator and not a bond, so the correction adds a refusal rather than reversing a bond. The record keeps the reputation and loses the claim's standing to be composed over (Fig 6 :42-43).

**Falsifier:** show a prestige loop with a negative sign that reliably drives trust toward the ground without an external anchor. Then the sign claim fails.

### Q5: Two currencies, and they do not add

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

A reputation signal is cheap to describe and expensive to test against the ground; an open method with raw records is expensive to describe and may cost one action to refute. Neither figure predicts the other (Fig 12 :128-129). "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" (Fig 2 :42) is a budget rule, not a verdict rule (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.")

**Falsifier:** produce a single scalar that prices both the description cost of a trust signal and the action cost of checking it, such that ranking by that scalar predicts which trust readings survive ground contact. Then the two-currency claim fails.

### Q6: Catalyst and spent parts in expertise

Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."

A credential that keeps its force after the claim it supported was refuted is a catalyst: it survives its own use. A track record keyed to particular claims is a spent-and-regenerating part. The public cannot tell them apart from the ingredient list, because nothing records which a term is. This is a concrete reason to demand provenance on every trust signal (Fig 8 :51-52).

**Falsifier:** show a record that marks, for every reputation signal, whether it survives its own use, and that this marker predicts refutation outcomes. Then the invisibility claim fails for that record.

## CONCLUSION

Over a fixed atom set, careful evaluation and reputation-driven trust are **isomers**: same atoms, different bonds, different behaviour. Where the prestige signal is a genuinely new atom, the relation is composition plus import, and the weaker **functional-group** reading holds. The dependency order runs ground contact, instrument, objective link, independent frames, weighting; levers applied downstream cannot rescue an upstream break. Reputation is a positive cycle, careful evaluation a chain that closes only at the anchor, and the correction of a trusted claim is a negation, not a reverse bond. Two currencies price the description and the check and do not add. All modes are `specified` or `general`, none `grounded`.
