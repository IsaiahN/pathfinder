## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :27, Fig 2 :29 to :42, Fig 6 :32, Fig 10 :42 and :61, Fig 11 :51, Fig 12 :117, and the Symbols w definition.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`, "index per claim, non-updating ground, low-overlap frames, source discriminator, refusal path; reputation is the opposite bond".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field claim such as "public trust in scientists reflects careful evaluation when the public consults open data and independent sources".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The anchor test.** "Open data" is a reading, not automatically a non-updating ground. The source essay requires an instrument that can comment on the source; the shorthand silently promotes a projection to an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **The per-slot indexing.** A field claim about "public trust in scientists" is usually an aggregate. The shorthand's "index per claim" is dropped and the live slot signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
- **The independence parameter.** "Independent sources" in a survey can mean different outlets drawing on one report. Without a provenance record, low overlap is asserted, not established (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :51-52).
- **The union structure.** The shorthand reads as two boxes; the field almost always contains both conditions in the same trust reading, so a partition reading is reintroduced (Fig 9 :55-57).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a non-updating ground plus separate correction is what distinguishes the careful case, and a self-confirming shared prior is what distinguishes the reputation case. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
