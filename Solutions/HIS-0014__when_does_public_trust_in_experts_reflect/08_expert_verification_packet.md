# Expert verification packet: the Tether on public trust in expertise

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in public trust in expertise, science communication or the sociology of expertise can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical tipping point and the standing list. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of a public's weighting w is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Public trust in an expert is a belief about a particular claim, indexed per slot, and its cause is a learnable weighting between observed contact with a non-updating ground and inherited institutional prior. Trust reflects careful evaluation when the reading is indexed per claim, leans on observations ranged against an anchor that does not update, is checked by at least two low-overlap frames corrected separately, has a source-discriminating instrument, and permits refusal of a trusted claim without deleting the record. It relies mainly on institutional reputation when the reference updates on the frames, the frames share one evidence pool, the status signal is frame-internal, standing survives the outcomes it was earned on, and the verdict is supplied by a room or a chain. Reputation can fund a hearing; it cannot settle a claim. The framework returns the separator and its refuters, not a percentage or a named trustworthy expert.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** a trust reading must be indexed per claim, and an anchor that settles the claim must not change its answer because any frame thinks it should. Reputation is a frame-internal quantity and cannot score the claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
- **Refuter:** show a single aggregate trust index that predicts per-claim corrections against a ground better than per-slot readings; or an institutional signal that does not update on its own holders' standing and settles claims as well as a ground reading; or a credibility measure that is not produced by the frame it grades.

### C2: world-facing, checkable against research on trust in science and expertise

- **Statement:** trust that tracks low-overlap readings against an independent ground is correctable; trust that tracks a shared signal and a shared prior is self-confirming even when it looks like evaluation. The two are isomers over a fixed atom set (same belief, observation, prior, ground and frames, different bonds).
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 8 :51-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** in the literature on public trust in experts, does the apparent agreement of many sources usually reduce to one evidence pool, and do the Wynne-type cases of lost trust follow failed ground contact rather than changes in credentials? Does any study hold the shared pool fixed and vary independence, so the two quantities separate?
- **Refuter:** shared-pool agreement that predicts ground outcomes as well as low-overlap frames across several domains; or a standing list of trusted experts that survives a change of residual; or a reputation-only signal that separates true from false claims without ground contact.

### C3: framework-internal derivation about the instrument and the refusal

- **Statement:** careful evaluation needs an instrument already returning a source-discriminating reading, and a correction to trust is a negation added to the record, so a claim loses standing to be composed over without the reputation being deleted.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 6 :42-43: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall. A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted."
- **Refuter:** a reputation-only instrument that reliably comments on the source, or a correction to trust that requires erasing the prior record rather than adding a refusal.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that all institutional trust is wrong, or that scepticism is evidence.
- Not that credentials never correlate with competence. Correlation is not settlement (Fig 2 :39-40).
- Not a percentage, a threshold value of w, or a named trustworthy expert (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135).
- Not that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess, not a discovery.
- Not that public trust is one quantity that can be averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not `grounded`. No public was measured and no instrument was built in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The *Hard Problem is About Source* essay: a reading that detects green "cannot comment on the source". Here the discriminators owed are (1) a reading that separates ground contact from inherited prior at the slot, (2) a provenance record that separates low-overlap convergence from one pool repeated, (3) a test of whether the anchor updates on the frames, and (4) a refusal path that adds a correction without deleting the record.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :27, Fig 2 :29 and :40, Fig 6 :32, and Fig 12 :117. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own field: in a study of public trust in experts, are the "independent" sources actually independent at the evidence level, and did trust move with ground contact or with standing?
3. Name one instrument, already returning something, that would separate the source of a trust signal from the signal itself, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
