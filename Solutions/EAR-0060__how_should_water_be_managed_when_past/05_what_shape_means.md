## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And from *How Intelligence Actually Works*: the framework *"cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."*

For EAR-0060 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of a management regime that can be stated before any particular dam, permit, model, or allocation rule is chosen. It constrains; it does not fill. "Keep the ground reachable per slot, refuse the stationarity term, hold thresholds, sequence actions, revise against residuals" is a shape. "Build three desalination plants and a new reservoir" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained: the searcher's method

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: if the final book does not answer, one possibility is that *"your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*. This is the essay-level source for F4 in `01`.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: we began from the effect words already in the question (past patterns, future supply, manage) and narrowed to the vocabulary of channels, anchors, slots, refusals, thresholds and pathways. The residual was described before the RAND or DMDU frames were chosen, per Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or"* *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In water terms: a measured threshold crossing, a reservoir that actually performs, a basin that actually recovers.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So calling "robust adaptive management" *the* answer to EAR-0060 would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. It is a name borrowed from a world frame. The figures return a shape and stop.

## 4. Why "the model says so" is the specific error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A hydrologic model that reproduces past flows is a projection. Several generators can produce the same hydrograph: stationary climate with one extraction regime, shifted climate with another, a gauge change, a land-use change. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator instrument, not a name for the source.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A forecast validated only by its own fit to history is exactly this failure.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid choosing any management method?

**Answered: no, with a boundary.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. A method that already exists in the world literature is not a missing primitive of the framework; it is a candidate from another frame. Naming one as *the* answer is a binding, and it needs provenance and settlement (section 3). It is not forbidden to mention; it is forbidden to present as figure-derived.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No. `01`, `07`, `08` and `09` keep the shape open and explicitly refuse to name a policy. The world literature in `04` is labelled secondary and its names (RDM, DAPP, adaptive management) are recorded as bindings from those frames, never as outputs of the derivation.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous hydrologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried against real basins.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The Substack architecture post is blunter: such outputs *"must be seen as simulacra."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What the society essay adds for D4

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the verification discipline directly: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"*, and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* It repeats the two collapses of Fig 2: mutual update toward *"whoever had the most influence"*, and a shared source where *"the agreement is the pool talking."* For outreach this means: independent gauges and models, recorded before agreement, and later agreement never pooled as corroboration. It also warns that *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times"*, which is the exact failure of sending one shape claim to a list selected for receptiveness.
