## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :36 (anchor legitimacy), Fig 5 :53 (refuted kind), Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 7 :36 (room chain) and Fig 11 :40 (contact).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** "keep the ground reachable; refuse stationarity; unbundle; hold thresholds and sequences; revise against residuals."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a basin operating rule, an allocation permit, a monitoring signpost, a capital investment pathway.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot indexing.** A portfolio average can swallow a live aquifer residual; the shorthand says "per slot" but does not carry the inventory of slots, which must be read off the world each time (Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*).
- **the identity of the refusal target.** The shorthand says "refuse stationarity", but Formula step 2 ranks bound terms by persistent, flat residual. Until a term is bound and ranked, the refusal has no object. The shorthand can be read as refusing a preference rather than a term, which Fig 5 :62 warns is invisible to the mechanism.
- **signpost fidelity.** "Revise against residuals" does not carry the requirement that the signpost be faithful to the threshold it watches. Fig 7 :37-39 makes bracket fidelity and ground pressure the two factors that can zero progress.

One thing survives cleanly: the ground is not the record, and a false regularity needs a refusal. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
