## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I started from the residual line alone (the gap per copy or trust decision between what the selection delivers and what the ground pays for).

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three or more overlapping selections (copy a model, trust a testimony, credit a name), so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Symmetry: "affect" is symmetric while "whose" is directed, so the arrows between status and trust run both ways; only a non-updating ground breaks the circle. Scale: individual, group, institution, recursive across strata with different actors, currency and ground (Fig 10 :68).
- **Domain 1, copying.** Actors: model and copier. Currency: fidelity and adoption frequency. Anchor: whether the behavior pays against the habitat when practiced. Where no outcome test exists, the copy is a convention or a mirror, not a verdict.
- **Domain 2, testimony.** Actors: authority and evaluator. Currency: credibility and obedience. Anchor: an outcome that does not update on the source's standing. If the anchor is the reputation, the arrangement is collapse 1.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: frames and checkers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is the load-bearing quantity. This is the only domain where trust becomes a verdict.
- **Domain 4, credit dynamics.** Actors: high and low prestige contributors, funders, citers. Currency: credit and funding. Anchor: the accumulated record of actions, nameable but structurally liable to outlive the result. A stake, not an independent verdict domain.
- **Awaiting a domain:** charisma, cultural fit, intuition about expertise. No nameable anchor as stated; labels applied after a selection. Not admitted.
- **Partition or union:** union. A title-only lever leaves the intersection where title, following and reputation all bind.
- **Order by dependency:** refuse status-as-ground before minting (Formula step 2); describe the residual before the frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle first-order decisions before composing credit (Fig 12 :104); bracket the round trip (Fig 4 :30); rotate the room (Fig 10 :72).
