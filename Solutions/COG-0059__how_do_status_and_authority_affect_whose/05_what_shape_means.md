## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a union of selection conditions, a valence that flips with the product, an anchor that must not update, independence as the load-bearing quantity, and prestige separated from settlement. A rank order of trustworthy people is a name, not a shape, and is refused.

## 2. The governing essay for this question: Prestige Is Not Action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly, contains a section titled "Prestige Is Not Action." Its verbatim claims:

- *"Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"*
- *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
- *"In every stable system, the two are separated. The thing that earns trust is not the same as the thing that gets to act. The record of past success is not a license to skip the ground."*
- *"someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."*
- *"The prestige loop confirms itself. The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige. The action is assumed to work because the person is believed. The ground is never consulted. The error compounds. The lineage is corrupted. Nobody notices because the prestige loop looks like agreement, and agreement feels like truth."*
- *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
- *"If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."*

This is the essay's own one openly shared finding, and it maps directly onto Fig 10 :61 (*"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on"*) and Fig 10 :59 (*"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*). It gives the mechanism by which status affects whose knowledge is trusted: not a bias bolted on, but a substitution of the record of success for the test of success.

The same essay gives the social-epistemology version: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* That is Fig 2 collapse 1 in plain language.

## 3. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The words "status," "authority," "trust," and "bias" are borrowed from everyday usage. The derivation describes the effect (a selection with or without an anchor) rather than treating those words as causes.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the verification packet asks what would be observed if the claim were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, having *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52) with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: the six-term substrate does not contain a "trustworthiness" atom. Calling a source trustworthy is a binding from the social frame onto held atoms, not the discovery of a seventh primitive. Until an outcome settles that binding, it is a candidate, not a verdict.

## 5. Why a rank order of experts is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK may produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An instrument that cannot distinguish those cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

A credibility cue is a reading. A uniform, a title, a follower count, and a citation count can all produce the same "this source looks credible" reading by different paths. Naming the source as trustworthy from that reading is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (an outcome that does not update on reputation), not a name.

## 6. Societies of agents, methods, and rooms

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (carried) supplies the multi-agent reading: a commons of methods over a shared ground, with rooms per problem. The RSI essay opened here gives the engineering version from Ouroboros: the dual economy separating Prestige from Action Budget, with a PrestigeFirewall at runtime. The author writes: *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."* This is Fig 7 :12-13 (*"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*) implemented as a design rule.

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up."* So in outreach and in organizations, what should be copied is the method and the refuter, not a recording of one occasion's success.

## 7. Status of this derivation: simulacra, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So every file in this folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**. It is not a test of the claim, it took no measurement, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. Mode is stated everywhere: `specified` on the substantive separations, `general` on the structural shape, nothing `grounded`.

## 8. Correction to my own §4: "shape, not a name" governs missing primitives

An earlier pass treated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming any trusted source. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. A trusted source is not a missing primitive; it is a binding from the social frame onto atoms already held (a model, a method, a copier, an outcome). Naming one is provenance work, not the invention of a new atom, and it is not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this adds none).

What remains wrong in naming a source from a credibility cue is the §5 metamerism point: the source is named from a reading without a discriminator. Both the narrowed rule and the metamerism point are visible here so neither is quietly lost.
