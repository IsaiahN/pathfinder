## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: three slots, display is convention, recognition is ground, norm is a head start, valence is residual-relative, display modes are isomers, occupant is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete world claim of the kind a researcher might make after reading the shape, for example "culture makes people suppress anger, and suppression is harmful, so recognition training should remove display rules."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Slot indexing** is lost when the three operations are folded into one outcome measure (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- **The anchor rule** is lost when perceiver agreement is substituted for an independently measured target state (Fig 2 :33-35, :40).
- **The two-kind norm split** is lost when a sanction-backed norm that fixes an outcome is treated as a pure display convention (Fig 10 :47).
- **The shape-not-a-name limit** is lost when a specific norm or emotion is promoted to the answer to a slot (Fig 9 :32).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a convention and a verdict need different treatments of agreement, and a norm crossing scale is a head start rather than a replay. That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
