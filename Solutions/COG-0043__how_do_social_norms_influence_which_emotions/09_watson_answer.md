## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the shape of the route by which a social norm changes three operations on an emotion: expression, concealment and recognition in another person. Option A: treat "influence" as one mechanism with one verdict and pool agreement across a group as corroboration. Option B: unbundle the three operations into slots with different verdict kinds, keep per-slot results separate, and close the open part only with a source-discriminating instrument. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal, lets a display convention decide a verdict about a felt state, and manufactures the disqualifying correlation; B returns a checkable shape with five written failure conditions and an honest `gap` for the named occupant.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union and order by dependency follow.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three slots (express, conceal, recognize). Symmetry: broken between first-person action on one's own emotion and third-person reading of another's display. Scale: the norm is population level, the operation is person level, so this is a cross-scale membrane problem. Anchor: observed display for the first two slots, independently measured target state for the third. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling when the split lands on a union rather than a partition.
- **Domain 1, display control.** Actors: person, felt emotion, audience. Currency: displayed behaviour and audience reaction. Anchor: coded display plus an independently measured felt state. Question type: convention about shared meaning. Order: downstream of the vocabulary link.
- **Domain 2, the norm frame.** Actors: group, norm holders, sanctioners. Currency: shared expectations, display rules, social meaning. Anchor: the convention for coordination rules, and the outcome it fixes for sanction-backed rules. Question type: two kinds after re-census, not one.
- **Domain 3, recognition.** Actors: perceiver, target, target's expression. Currency: recognition judgment and accuracy. Anchor: the target's actual emotion, which must not move when the perceiver moves. Question type: verdict about the world.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception of emotion and audience (1) to vocabulary, where the norm sits (2) to social objective (3) to chosen display (4) to learning (5). Fig 3 :24 and :27 name link 2 as the break with no instrument. Perceivers run the same chain with the expression as percept.
- **Partition or union.** A union of display control and recognition with an overlap where a display becomes a percept. One lever leaves the intersection standing.

Strain test applied: the Fig 2 convention rule and the Fig 10 outcome-fixing guard strain when forced onto one idea of "norm". Re-census splits coordination rules from sanction-backed norms (Domain 2), and the strain dissolves (see `01` F9).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of three slots, and the figures return a shape rather than an occupant. Expression and concealment are convention slots; recognition is a ground slot whose anchor must be independently measured. The norm crosses the scale membrane as a head start at the least instrumented link. Concealment is missing or replaced display, not a refused claim, and the display modes are isomers over held atoms. The valence of a norm is relative to the residual, and a sanction-backed norm that fixes an outcome has stopped being a convention. Which norm and which emotion fill a slot, and what the magnitude is, remain a declared `gap`. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. All of this is `specified` on the mechanism and `general` on the shape, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, records provenance and puts five failure conditions before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the three slots and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific bindings are removable without touching the shape, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates felt state from display would test the concealment and recognition readings; criterion-equivalent recognition across norm groups against an independently labelled target set would break the recognition verdict; audience-invariant display with felt emotion held fixed would break the head-start verdict; a norm that is harmful or beneficial for every residual would break the valence verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
