## Wider literature that bears on the derivation

1. **The sign flip is real, not rhetorical.** Ford and Mauss 2015 is the clearest statement that the well-being cost of suppression depends on cultural orientation. This is what residual-relative valence means in this domain, and it is why the figure line Fig 11 :49 is load-bearing rather than decorative.
2. **The norm acts upstream, not only at display.** De Leersnyder et al. 2013 argue that cultural regulation often occurs at emotion elicitation through situation selection and appraisal, and that response-focused suppression may be *"culture's last resort."* This moves the norm's point of action from the response slot to the vocabulary and objective links of Fig 3. It is corroboration of F10, not a conflicting verdict.
3. **Production is the neglected half.** Gallant et al. 2026 show that cross-cultural differences are larger for posed than spontaneous expressions and that culturally driven regulation appears *"primarily after an initial universal reaction."* That is a head start applied after the first pass, consistent with Fig 4 :22 and :24 rather than with a norm that rewrites the emotion itself.
4. **Recognition is tuned by group membership.** Elfenbein and Ambady 2002 provide the in-group advantage with an objective accuracy criterion. Chiao 2015 provides a neural fingerprint: the amygdala responds preferentially to fear faces from one's own cultural group. These are two readings of the same phenomenon and count as one evidence pool, not two witnesses.
5. **Conventions are not norms.** The SEP entry's distinction between empirical expectations and normative expectations is the world-side reason for the F9 re-census. A display convention is sustained by the first; a sanction-backed norm adds the second.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual and the shape claim with its pre-registered refuter. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure here? Record the answer before anything else is shown. A Track B reply is a conversation, never corroboration; verdict weight is zero.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the three local framework links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they have now seen the interior (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes down what an answer would look like before running it, states a mode, and names a ground that does not update on their own report. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap warns that a simulated output *"cannot validate itself"*; the user's own refuter is the remedy.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. The five refuters in `02` are the test.
