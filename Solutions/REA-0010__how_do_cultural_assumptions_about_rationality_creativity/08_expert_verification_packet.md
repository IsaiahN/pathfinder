# Expert verification packet: cultural assumptions and definitions of intelligence

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cross-cultural intelligence researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the capability claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No measurement in this folder is claimed to settle the question.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A definition of intelligence is a convention whose terms are supplied by what a culture values in rationality, creativity and autonomy. That shaping is legitimate: agreement about shared meaning is the product of a convention, not a corruption of it. But a definition cannot settle a capability question. The figures replace "what is intelligence" with "what makes a system capable", which is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone. When a culturally agreed definition is used to decide who is capable, it stops being a convention and becomes an undeclared verdict. The three assumptions form a union compound, and a single scale aggregates the live slot away. What is offered is the separation, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. No new definition is proposed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - convention term, framework-internal and psychology-facing

- **Statement:** cultural assumptions shape definitions of intelligence by supplying the terms of a convention; this is the convention's product, not a defect.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* and Fig 10 :49 *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 10 :18 *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."*
- **What to check, against the literature:** does the cross-cultural record show cultural values entering *implicit* and *explicit* definitions of intelligence, rather than only performance on tests? Relevant checks: the APA task force report's cultural-variation section; Nisbett et al. (2001) for rationality; Morris and Leung (2010) for creativity norms.
- **Refuter:** a community whose definition is demonstrably independent of what it values in rationality, creativity and autonomy, measured without borrowing the community's own vocabulary (Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*).

### C2 - capability term, the decisive separation

- **Statement:** no definition, however widely agreed, settles capability; capability is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :34 *"What is intelligence?"* and :35 *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* Fig 11 :39 **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40 **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** And Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*
- **What to check against the ground:** find any case where a change of definition alone, with the habitat held fixed, changed a capability measurement. The 2025 NSF workshop report is a useful stress case: it records niche-adapted abilities across eight species while also arguing for domain-general intelligence. Does the framework's agent-and-habitat claim survive that internal tension?
- **Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground (named in Fig 11 :72), or a definition change with no habitat change that moves a capability measurement. Either breaks the separation.

### C3 - measurement term, per-slot and union

- **Statement:** a single-scale definition hides the live slot; the three assumptions are a union compound, not three independent questions or one lever.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and :22 **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **What to check:** does any single-scale instrument preserve rationality, creativity and autonomy as separate, live residuals that then change a ground reading? If yes, the aggregation claim is false in that setting.
- **Refuter:** a single-scale definition shown to preserve per-slot residuals which then change a ground reading; or one cultural variable predicting all three definitional effects with no interaction terms (which would make it a partition).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a new definition of intelligence, and not a ranking of cultures.
- Not that cultural variation makes capability undefined. Domain 2 stays answerable.
- Not that cultural agreement is worthless. Fig 2 :41 gives it positive valence for a convention; only verdict use is disqualified.
- Not that rationality, creativity and autonomy are one lever or three independent questions.
- Not `grounded`. The mode is stated on every substantive claim.
- Not corroborated by agreement. Every world source listed below is secondary, and the essays share an author with the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not an argument and not a consensus.

1. A capability instrument that separates convention from contact, so that a definition change with fixed habitat can be seen to move (or fail to move) the reading.
2. An instrument that does not borrow the community's vocabulary, able to test whether a definition tracks what the community values.
3. A per-slot measurement that keeps rationality, creativity and autonomy live in a single design, with a ground reading at the end.
4. Any of the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 firing. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :41, Fig 10 :47/:49 and Fig 11 :34-40. Six short passages.
2. Say which of C1 and C2 your own field would treat as the definition of intelligence, and why. That is the convention/capability split in practice.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a convention change from a capability change in your data, or say why none exists yet.
4. Try to break C3 with a single-scale measure that preserves the three slots.
5. Send the refutation. A one-line failure is more useful than agreement, and it will be copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
