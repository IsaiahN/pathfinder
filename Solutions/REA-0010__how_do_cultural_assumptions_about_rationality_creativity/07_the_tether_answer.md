# The Tether's answer: cultural assumptions and definitions of intelligence

**Mode: `specified` for the capability claims, `general` for the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A community defines intelligence using assumptions about rationality, creativity and autonomy. The question is what those assumptions *do*. The figures locate the residual on the vocabulary-to-objective link of the dependency chain (Fig 3 :25-27), the one link with no instrument. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not three independent ones (Fig 9 :54-58). Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A definitional stalemate is not evidence that no capability answer exists.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**Domain 1, the convention frame. Verdict: cultural assumptions legitimately shape definitions. `general`, triangulated.**

A definition of intelligence is a convention, and shared meaning is the convention's product. Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* and Fig 10 :49 *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Rationality, creativity and autonomy are the terms a culture uses to build the convention, so a change in what a culture values changes the definition in exactly the way a convention should change. This is not a defect to be corrected. It is the thing the question is about.

**Domain 2, the capability frame. Verdict: no definition settles capability. `specified`, single-source decisive, corroborated.**

Fig 11 :34-35: *"What is intelligence?"* *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* Fig 11 :39:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** Changing a definition does not change contact. When a culturally agreed definition is used to decide who is capable, Fig 10 :47 applies: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* The mechanism was stated at Fig 2 :36-38: human judgment is the legitimate anchor for "is this a good answer for a person", and only an instrument for "is this code correct", and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. The same judgment moves from anchor to instrument when the question moves from convention to capability.

**Domain 3, the verification frame. Verdict: a single scale hides the live slot. `general`, triangulated.**

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and :22 **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A definition that ranks on one scale aggregates across rationality, creativity and autonomy and across contexts. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* There is no culture-independent, residual-independent ranking of which assumption makes a definition good.

**Dependency note.** Settle the convention term (Domain 1) before the capability term (Domain 2). The loop cannot check the direction of its own question unless the sought-for shape is made an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"), which is why §0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` binds it before any figure is quoted.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new definition of intelligence. The framework returns a shape, not a name, for what is missing, and here the atoms are already held.
- Not that cultural variation makes capability undefined. Domain 2 remains answerable; the answer is agent-and-habitat, not agent alone.
- Not that cultural agreement is worthless. Fig 2 :41 gives it positive valence for a convention. Only agreement used as a capability verdict is disqualified.
- Not that the three assumptions are one lever or three independent questions. They are a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58).
- Not a ranking of cultures, and not a claim that any culture's definition is closer to the ground.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles the capability question.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

- **Capability:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, or a definition change with no habitat change that moves a capability measurement. Fig 11 :72 names the first as the refuter of the capability claim.
- **Convention:** a community's definition shown to be independent of what it values in the three assumptions, using an instrument that does not borrow the community's own vocabulary (Fig 9 :30-31).
- **Aggregation:** a single-scale definition that preserves per-slot residuals which then change a ground reading.
- **Union:** one cultural variable predicting all three effects with no interaction terms.
- None of these is "a famous reviewer agrees". From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* Two definitions may be indiscernible to current instruments without being the same. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* One strain remains named and unresolved: the exact overlap among the three union conditions, and the isomer claim of `06_isomers_and_order.md`, are unmeasured.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Cultural assumptions shape definitions of intelligence in the same way any convention gets its content: they supply the terms a community agrees to count, and the figures explicitly say that agreement about a shared meaning is the product of a convention, not a corruption of it. So that part of the question has a real answer and we should keep it. But the figures replace "what is intelligence" with "what makes a system capable", which is a property of an agent and its habitat, never of the agent alone. Changing a definition changes the label, the slots that get measured and who gets standing by agreement; it does not change contact. When a definition is used to decide who is capable, it has stopped being a convention and become an undeclared verdict. Rationality, creativity and autonomy are a union compound rather than three independent levers, and a single scale aggregates the live slot away. What is offered here is not a new definition but a shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The convention term is legitimate; the capability term stays open until a habitat settles it.
