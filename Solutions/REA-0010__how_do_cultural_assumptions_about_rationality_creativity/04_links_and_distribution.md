## Wider literature that bears on the question

Three findings from the world sources change what a careful reader should expect, and are recorded here rather than buried in the packet:

1. **No standard definition.** Legg and Hutter (2007) catalogue more than 70 definitions and open with the observation that there is no standard definition. This corroborates the framework's claim that a definition is a convention. It does not corroborate a capability claim.
2. **Culture enters definitions explicitly.** The APA task force report devotes a section to cultural variation and reports that immigrant parents in San Jose rated motivation, social skills and practical school skills as at least as important as cognitive characteristics for an intelligent first-grade child. That is the cultural shaping in F2, measured from outside the framework.
3. **Capability is niche-shaped in an independent field.** The 2025 NSF workshop report presents intelligence across humans, macaques, ferrets, crows, octopuses, bees and flies in the context of each species' ecological niche. It also records a tension of its own: it argues for domain-general intelligence while describing niche-adapted abilities. That tension is a conflict inside the world source, not a figure conflict; the re-census in `09_watson_answer.md` §WORLD explains why.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The output is the separation of a convention term from a capability term, with the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. The recipient's verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then the reader holds the frame's interior (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, also opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on. A failure found in stage 1 is more useful than an endorsement of the whole package.
