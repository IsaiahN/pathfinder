## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 explains why: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

For REA-0010 the shape is not a new definition of intelligence. It is the separation of two terms that a definition quietly fuses:

> a definition of intelligence is a **convention term** (what a community agrees to count) bonded to a **capability term** (what meets the ground in an agent-and-habitat system), and the two are settled by different instruments.

That is a constraint. It rules out any answer that treats a cultural difference in definitions as a difference in capability, and it rules out any answer that treats capability as fixed by definition. It does not name a new primitive, propose a new definition or rank cultures.

From *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16, read this session): *"The framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."* A shape is the narrowed form you can state before you can settle it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is observable: a community rewards certain behaviours, builds tests around them and calls the resulting profile "intelligence." The cause ("their culture believes x about rationality") is the thing borrowed from the frame.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* So the residual was described as "a definition confers standing by agreement, not by contact", not as "culture biases cognition."
- From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: the mapping account is the closest held vocabulary. *"The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments."* and *"The test that environment applies is what we call fitness."* These are used as proximity for the capability term, then checked against Fig 11 :39, which is the authority.
- The same essay on stopping: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* The shape stops where the available instruments stop discriminating. Indiscernibility of two definitions is not their identity.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For capability, the ground is a habitat and a measurement of contact (Fig 11 :39-40).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). For definitions, the import is the community's own convention, which must be recorded as coming from that frame, not from the framework.

Anything else is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. In this question the temptation is to name one of rationality, creativity or autonomy as "the real intelligence." The figures do not license that; Fig 11 :49-51 makes valence relative to the residual and says no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.

## 4. The source error this question is prone to

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the mechanism with its green-light thought experiment. An instrument reads green; the reading *"can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* Several sources produce the same projection, and the reading does not determine the source. The essay names the failure: provenance loss, where *"very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."*

Applied here: a test score reads "intelligent." The reading cannot tell you whether it came from a habitat-shaped capacity, from a culturally valued style of reasoning, from familiarity with the test format, or from several of these. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. The world literature supplies a concrete case: the APA task force report records that Zambian children excelled at reproducing patterns in wire and English children in pencil and paper, with both equal in clay. One reading, several routes. What is owed is a discriminator, not a new definition.

The same essay adds the honest limit: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* So "no instrument currently separates convention from capability" is a status report, not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid giving a definition of intelligence?

**No, with a correction to my own first reading.** The canonical phrase is *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. In this question the atoms are already held: rationality, creativity and autonomy are ordinary terms in the vocabulary, as are convention, agreement, habitat and contact. Naming them is not inventing a new primitive. What the figures forbid is (a) inventing a seventh term to settle the dispute, and (b) claiming that a binding of the held atoms moves the wall. Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

So I overstated the rule when I first drafted this file. The correct statement: **the framework returns a shape for what is missing; for what is held, it can only report the arrangement and the provenance.** The arrangement of rationality, creativity and autonomy into a definition is the searcher's or the culture's contribution, and it must be recorded as such. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b. Triangulated (README phrasing plus Fig 6 :21 plus Fig 13 :115).

The consequence for outreach: never offer "our new definition of intelligence." Offer the separation, the per-slot residual and the refuter. That is the generator.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19, read this session):

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous intelligence researchers who agree with a definition is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried: one checkable separation, four refuters, and a habitat that can refuse it. The same essay states the alignment point that bears directly on this question: *"The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback."* A cultural preference is a legitimate convention and an illegitimate ground.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, verbatim:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."*
> *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim. They cannot settle the question they report on, and no agreement with a world source is a substitute for a ground reading.
