## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that cultural assumptions about rationality, creativity and autonomy shape definitions of intelligence. Option A: a definition is a report of a capability, so a culturally different definition is a competing capability claim and the disagreement needs settling. Option B: a definition is a convention that supplies the terms of a label, so cultural variation is expected and legitimate, while the capability claim is a separate question answered by contact with a habitat. What each leads to: A sends the field into a definitional dispute with no anchor that can settle it, because human judgment is the thing in dispute. B locates the cultural shaping in the convention domain and keeps the capability question open to a ground.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Cultural assumptions shape definitions of intelligence exactly as conventions shape shared meaning: they supply the terms a community uses to name what it values, and Fig 2 :41 makes that agreement the convention's product. That shaping is real and should be kept. It cannot be exported into a capability verdict. Fig 11 :34-40 replaces the definitional question with "what makes a system capable", which is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone, and Fig 10 :49 says declaring a meaning does not ground it. A culturally loaded definition fixes which slots are measured, aggregates residuals across slots and contexts, and confers standing by agreement rather than by ground. None of that changes contact (Fig 11 :40). Rationality, creativity and autonomy are a union compound (Fig 9 :54), not one lever; a single scale aggregates the live slot away (Fig 1 :22). Domain 1 is `general` and triangulated; Domain 2 is `specified`, single-source decisive and corroborated; Domain 3 is `general` and triangulated. Nothing here is `grounded`. This is a shape and a set of refuters, not a new definition and not a ranking of cultures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent as designed. Mode, refuters and lineage are stated before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is using a definition as an undeclared verdict, which Fig 10 :47 names, and buying agreement instead of independence, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both are avoidable by leading with the separation and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The capability and convention claims are kept separate, so a refutation of one does not require withdrawing the other. No new primitive was introduced and no definition was installed. A superseding packet can be issued without retracting a ground claim, because none was made.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that shows a definition change with fixed habitat moving a capability measurement would break Domain 2. An instrument that does not borrow a community's vocabulary and shows its definition independent of its values would break Domain 1. A single-scale measure that preserves per-slot residuals which then change a ground reading would break Domain 3. One cultural variable predicting all three effects with no interaction terms would dissolve the union. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
