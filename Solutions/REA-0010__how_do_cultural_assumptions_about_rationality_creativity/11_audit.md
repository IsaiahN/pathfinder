## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran it on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :34-40, Fig 2 :41, Fig 10 :47/:49, Fig 1 :19/:22 and Fig 9 :54.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: definitions are conventions, capability is agent-and-habitat, agreement is not a verdict, one scale hides the slot, three assumptions are a union.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete cultural definition of intelligence, for example one that ranks people on a single score composed of reasoning, novelty and self-direction, and uses that score to decide who is capable.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot residual**. The shorthand says "one scale hides the slot"; a concrete test can still report a single number and no per-slot reading, so the claim has to be re-established at the bottom rather than assumed.
- the **union overlap**. The shorthand says "unbundle"; a concrete instrument must still decide whether reasoning, novelty and self-direction interact in this setting. The framework does not supply that measurement.
- the **anchor swap**. The shorthand says human judgment is an anchor for conventions and an instrument for capability; a concrete review panel can drift between the two roles without anyone noticing, which is Fig 2 panel 1, arrived at by a different route.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a definition is a convention and changing it does not change contact. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
