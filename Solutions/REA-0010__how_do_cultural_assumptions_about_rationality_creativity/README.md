## The residual in effect terms

A definition of intelligence is a convention: it tells a community what to count as smart. The question asks how cultural assumptions about rationality, creativity and autonomy get into that convention. Two options. Option A treats a definition as a report of a capability, so a culture with a different definition is making a different claim about what intelligence is, and the disagreement needs settling. Option B treats a definition as a convention that supplies the terms of a label, so cultural variation is expected and legitimate, while the capability claim is a different question answered only by contact with a habitat. Option A sends the field into a definitional dispute with no anchor that can settle it. Option B locates the cultural shaping in the convention domain and keeps the capability question open to a ground. See `09_watson_answer.md` §QUESTION.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term** (formula step "Direction"): a sentence that names which part of a definition of intelligence is convention, which part is a claim about agent-and-habitat capability, and which instrument settles each. Until that sentence is written down the sought-for shape is a preference, not a term, and no check in the loop can see it.

## Key verdict (from the figures, not from the world sources)

Cultural assumptions shape definitions of intelligence exactly as conventions shape shared meaning: they supply the terms a community uses to name what it values, and Fig 2 :41 says that agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it. That shaping is real inside the convention domain. The figures forbid exporting it into a capability verdict: Fig 11 :34-40 replaces "what is intelligence" with "what makes a system capable", which is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone, and Fig 10 :49 says declaring a meaning does not ground it. A culturally loaded definition fixes which slots are measured, aggregates residuals across slots and contexts, and confers standing by agreement rather than by ground. None of that changes contact. Triangulated for the convention/ground split; single-source for the decisive Fig 11 capability claim.
