## 0. The claim under audit, stated as an effect

The question is not asking for a new definition of intelligence. It is asking what a definition of intelligence is *made of*, and what a cultural assumption *does* when it enters one. Restated in effect terms:

> R: a community supplies a definition of intelligence using assumptions about rationality, creativity and autonomy. What is the residual between what that definition predicts (who will be counted as smart, in which setting) and what the habitat actually settles (who meets its ground)?

Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A.** A definition is a report of a capability. A culture with a different definition is making a competing claim about intelligence, and the disagreement needs settling. Leads to: a definitional dispute with no anchor, because human judgment is the thing in dispute.
- **Option B.** A definition is a convention that supplies the terms of a label. Cultural variation is expected and legitimate, while the capability claim is a separate, ground-answerable question. Leads to: the cultural shaping is located and kept, and the capability question stays open to a habitat.

The sought-for shape is written down now so it can accrue a residual (formula step "Direction"): **an answer is a sentence that separates a convention term from a capability term and names the instrument that settles each.** This is an ordinary term in the audit, not a preference smuggled in at the end.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: three named conditions (rationality, creativity, autonomy) are bundled into one question. Fig 9 :54 says *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* So R is a **union compound**, not one hard problem and not three independent ones. Symmetry: the three are not interchangeable. Under the bond set they are distinct atoms, and different arrangements give different definitions (Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*). Scale: the convention lives at the social and room scale; the capability claim lives at the agent-and-habitat scale. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A recording of a definition does not cross as a method.

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor).** A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, convention frame | communities, definition-makers, assumptions about rationality/creativity/autonomy | shared meaning, agreement, definitions | human judgment, legitimate here because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36) | union member |
| D2, capability frame | agent and habitat | contact, residual, settlement | the ground (Fig 11 :39, Fig 2 :29) | union member |
| D3, verification frame | researchers, tests, benchmarks | residuals and measurements | the ground, never agreement | union member |

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (can we tell what a definition is doing?) then vocabulary (terms for what rationality, creativity and autonomy afford) then objective (what would count as progress?) then planning then learn and carry. The break is at vocabulary to objective. Fig 3 :25-27: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments. A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* A definition of intelligence sits exactly on that unmeasured link, which is why cultural assumptions can operate there invisibly.

**Strain test applied before concluding.** An early reading wanted Fig 11 :36 to forbid all talk of intelligence. Re-census below (F5) shows it governs only the unmaintained mode; the maintained mode is described as real work. No strain survives the census.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The figures call the question the wrong one (decisive, `specified`)

Fig 11 :34 *"What is intelligence?"* and :35 *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* The replacement is stated at Fig 11 :39:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

And at Fig 11 :40:

> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

So "define intelligence" is not the question the figures answer. The answerable version is "what makes a system capable", and it is answered at the level of the agent **and** its habitat. A definition of intelligence is upstream of that. When a cultural assumption changes a definition, it changes the label and the slot selection, not the contact.

- **Mode:** `specified` for "capability is agent-and-habitat"; `general` for the structural claim that a definition is upstream of capability.
- **Status:** single-source for the decisive sentence (Fig 11), triangulated with Fig 2 :29 (the anchor) and Fig 6 :21 (composition does not add an atom).
- **Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground. Fig 11 :72 names this exact test: an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim. A second refuter: a show that a definition change, with no habitat change, moved a capability measurement.

### F2 - The cultural shaping is real and legitimate inside the convention domain (`general`)

Cultural assumptions about rationality, creativity and autonomy are the *content* of the convention. Fig 2 :41 names the valence:

> **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**

Fig 10 :18 says *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"* and Fig 10 :49 says *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Together these separate two legitimate things: making a meaning (a convention, the product of a culture) and grounding a claim (a different operation, requiring a non-updating anchor).

The world literature corroborates this from outside the framework. Neisser et al. (1996) write that *"Others emphasize the role of culture, both in establishing different conceptions of intelligence and in influencing the acquisition of intellectual skills."* Nisbett et al. (2001) show that assumptions about formal logic and rationality vary by culture. Morris and Leung (2010) show the same for creativity. These are secondary witnesses only; they do not decide the verdict.

- **Mode:** `general` for the mechanism, `specified` for the prediction that a community's definition will mirror its valued assumptions.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 :41 (valence of agreement), Fig 10 :18/:49 (meaning versus ground), Fig 1 :19 (slot selection), with low overlap between the correlation argument and the convention argument.
- **Refuter:** a community whose definition of intelligence is demonstrably independent of what it values in rationality, creativity and autonomy, measured by a method that does not itself use the community's own vocabulary (Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*).

### F3 - The precise mechanism: a legitimate anchor becomes an instrument

Fig 2 :36-38 is the sharpest statement of how the shaping crosses the boundary:

> **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
> **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."**

Applied here: for "is this a good definition for our people", human judgment and cultural agreement are the legitimate anchor, because the question is constitutively about people. For "is this system capable", the very same judgment is only an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. A cultural assumption shapes a definition legitimately; it cannot settle the capability question without changing what it measures.

Fig 10 :47 completes it: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* When a definition is used to decide who is capable, the convention has silently become a verdict.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 :36-38, Fig 10 :47, Fig 2 :29-30.
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that a culturally agreed definition, used as a capability verdict, cannot be distinguished from the ground by any instrument available to the community (in which case the convention/ground distinction fails here).

### F4 - A single scale aggregates the live slot away

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

A definition of intelligence that ranks on one scale aggregates across rationality, creativity and autonomy and across contexts. Fig 11 :49-51 gives the reason this is not neutral: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Rationality is a benefactor to a residual defined as exam performance and a malfactor to one defined as exploration; there is no standing cultural list that survives a change of residual.

- **Mode:** `general` for the aggregation result, `specified` for the prediction that a single-scale definition will hide at least one live slot.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 :19/:22 and Fig 11 :49-51, near-zero overlap between a per-slot measurement rule and an ecology valence rule).
- **Refuter:** a single-scale definition shown to preserve per-slot residuals in a setting where they then change the ground reading.

### F5 - Strain test and re-census: the word "intelligence" does work in one mode

**The strain.** Fig 11 :36 says *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Read alone, this appears to forbid any serious study of definitions of intelligence, including this question.

**Re-census (required by the canonical rule that a strained reading has misidentified its domain).** Fig 11 has two modes. The sentence above is explicitly restricted to *"where nothing maintains anything"*, the unmaintained mode (Fig 11 :56 *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."*). The maintained mode is Fig 11 :37:

> **"Where a seat exists, something is doing work, and it is exactly what the seat does: enumerate the habitat, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, read the ground."**

and :38 *"That is not a metaphor for intelligence. It is a description of the practice."* Definitions of intelligence are exactly the work of that seat: they enumerate and rank what a culture counts. So the word does work, as a convention, in the maintained mode. It does no work as an explanation of why a lineage met its ground.

- **Resolution:** not strained after re-census. Fig 11 :36 governs the unmaintained mode; Fig 11 :37-38 govern the maintained mode. Different objects. F1 and F2 are both kept.
- **Refuter for the re-census:** a showing that the two Fig 11 modes are the same mode under a different name, in which case the "no work" sentence would apply to cultural definition work too.

### F6 - Unbundle before searching (the three assumptions are not one lever)

Fig 9 :54 (above) and :55-58: a bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions, and a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds while leaving the intersections standing. Rationality, creativity and autonomy are three such conditions. A culture may privilege reasoning in one setting, novelty in another and self-direction in a third. Treating "cultural assumptions" as one lever will clear one region and leave the compound standing. `06_isomers_and_order.md` does the unbundling and states its falsifier.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Status:** single-source for the union reading (Fig 9 :54-58), corroborated weakly by the world sources on cross-cultural creativity showing different norms for novelty versus usefulness.
- **Refuter:** a single cultural variable that predicts all three definitional effects with no interaction terms.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Fig 5 direction discipline and the exemplar's rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Capability claim:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, or a definition change with no habitat change that moves a capability measurement (breaks Fig 11 :39-40).
2. **Convention claim:** a community's definition shown to be independent of what it values in rationality, creativity and autonomy, using an instrument that does not borrow the community's vocabulary (breaks F2 and Fig 9 :30-31).
3. **Aggregation claim:** a single-scale definition that demonstrably preserves per-slot residuals which then change a ground reading (breaks Fig 1 :19/:22).
4. **Union claim:** one cultural variable that predicts all three definitional effects with no interaction terms (breaks Fig 9 :54-58).
5. **Mode claim:** a definition of intelligence settled by an instrument rather than agreed by a convention (would move the claim from `general` to `grounded` and require a rewrite of F2).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1 and F3, not with a proposed new definition. The figures replace the definitional question with the capability question (Fig 11 :34-39).
2. Keep the cultural shaping (F2). Do not treat it as noise or as a corruption; Fig 2 :41 makes that the convention's product.
3. Stop a definition from deciding an outcome (Fig 10 :47). Where a definition must be used, state its mode and name the ground that settles capability separately.
4. Report per slot, not as one score (Fig 1 :19/:22). Rationality, creativity and autonomy keep their own residuals.
5. Send the generator, not the conclusion. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The pre-registered refuter in §4 is the thing worth handing over.
