## Independent domain census (re-derived from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three or more, because the pair I and P is always accompanied by at least one candidate common cause (disease environment, geography, human capital, colonial settlement). Symmetry: the observed association is exchangeable in its operands, while "causes" is not; this is a structural mismatch, not a sample-size problem. Scale: the pair behaves differently at country, region, decade and century scales, so scale is a slot index. I reached these three properties from Operators :15 (the symmetric test) and Fig 1 :19 (slot indexing), before reading the primary's D1-D4 lines.
- **Domain 1, statistical / observational frame.** Actors: variables, coefficients, standard errors. Currency: association and prediction. Anchor: none inside it; a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it produces. Not a domain until an anchor is imported. Question type: convention, not verdict.
- **Domain 2, identification frame.** Actors: instruments, natural experiments, random assignment, temporal and process evidence. Currency: exogenous variation and counterfactual contrast. Anchor: variation that does not move with the outcome. Question type: import.
- **Domain 3, institutional habitat.** Actors: rules, rulers, citizens, external conditions. Currency: persistence of rules and contact with outcomes. Anchor: material outcomes that do not read the researcher's preference. Question type: valence relative to the residual, per Fig 11 :49.
- **Domain 4, verification / review.** Actors: reviewers and replicators. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is zero in this simulated run because all four domains share one pool.

**Partition or union:** union. I derived this independently from Fig 9 :55 and the Library of Babel essay's "bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one." The five conditions (direction, institution slot, prosperity slot, time scale, level) overlap; one lever leaves intersections standing. This matches the primary.

**Order by dependency:** perceive slots, build vocabulary for what each institution affords, bind the objective (a direction discriminator), choose a frame, carry the method. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete country-level claim, for example "secure property rights caused growth in country X between 1980 and 2000, so reforming them will cause growth in country Y."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **exclusion assumption** on the imported asymmetry (S1): the shorthand says "import an asymmetry with a source gate"; the concrete claim needs an exclusion restriction that the distribution cannot test, and the shorthand does not carry its content;
- the **method versus recording distinction** (Fig 4 :22-25): a direction discriminator is a generator, but a fitted coefficient for country X is a recording of one occasion; the shorthand can be read as licensing the recording;
- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19/:22): the shorthand says "per-slot verdicts" but a country-level claim re-aggregates them, and the live slot disappears into the average;
- the **loop-sign question** (Fig 12 :121): the shorthand includes "cycle" but a single-coefficient concretisation silently reverts to a chain.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a symmetric association cannot assign an ordered direction on its own. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no country-level ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09` states.
