# The Tether's answer on institutions and prosperity

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the identity of any named cause. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe an institution I and a prosperity measure P together and cannot assign a direction. The observation is symmetric; the claim "causes" is ordered. The residual is the unassigned direction, indexed per institution-slot and per prosperity-slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It persists because a joint distribution carries no arrow, and because the field's standard institutional indices move with the outcome they are supposed to explain. Fig 9 :4-5: a disagreement that does not shrink with effort, where each rule works well on a different subset, "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

## 2. The shape

1. **A symmetric association cannot choose an ordered bond.** Operators :15: "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." Operators :33: "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." Fig 12 :92: "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing." So no coefficient, significance level or ranking assigns direction by itself. Correlation is "efficient for a convention" and "disqualifying for a verdict" (Fig 2 :41 and :40).

2. **The asymmetry must be imported from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom." Natural experiments, discontinuities, random assignment, temporal-ordering and process-trace frames are the imports that can carry an arrow. Each needs provenance and a shadow test, because "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :51).

3. **The anchor must not update.** Fig 2 :29: an anchor "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." An index whose value rises with current income fails this test and becomes another frame (Fig 2 :34). The exclusion assumption of an instrument must be declared (Fig 10 :60).

4. **The question is a union, not a partition.** Five overlapping conditions: direction, institution-slot, prosperity-slot, time scale, level. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Verdicts are per slot and never averaged into "institutions matter somewhat" (Fig 9 :60).

5. **The two causal stories are isomers; the feedback case is a cycle.** Over a fixed atom set they are Fig 12 :117, "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." "Precedes" is sequence, "produces" is production (Operators :29-40). If both directions hold, Fig 12 :121 applies: "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

6. **No standing list of good institutions.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Contact cascades, so a cause three steps out (disease environment, colonial settlement, trade integration) can be the operative one (Fig 11 :52-53).

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It says what any valid direction check must contain. It does not say which institution occupies the cause slot in a given country. That is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, and unreachable is a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that institutions do not matter. It says a symmetric reading cannot assign direction.
- Not that property rights, education, courts or democracy are the cause. That is a `gap`, and substituting a favourite would be inventing a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that instrumental variables are invalid. They are imports that need a source gate, a shadow test and a declared exclusion assumption; a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24).
- Not that AJR refutes Glaeser et al. or the reverse. They are two frames with different anchors; their disagreement is evidence for the union verdict.
- Not that a p-value or a coefficient size is a verdict. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not `grounded`. No country-level direction was measured in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour-meter "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding checks are:

- **Anchor test:** is the candidate instrument's value invariant to current prosperity and to the researcher's preferred theory, while still predicting later prosperity out of sample?
- **Production test:** was the prosperity measure absent in the same form before the institution fired, and present after, under a counterfactual contrast? Sequence alone is insufficient.
- **Shadow test:** does the imported frame explain the gap the association left, rather than merely rename it? Fig 8 :51.
- **Loop-sign test:** if both directions hold, does the cycle settle or run away? A chain cannot answer this (Fig 12 :121).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** the six conditions in the pre-registered refuter, including a symmetric statistic that assigns direction, an invariant-and-predictive institutional measure, and a harness that reproduces direction without an import.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Institutional indices are "a careful guess at" the field's compression axes, not nature's own list. Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 10 :65-66: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name an institution as the cause of prosperity, and it does not pick a winner between the institutions-first and prosperity-first literatures. It says that the observed association is symmetric while the claim is ordered, so no coefficient or ranking can assign direction on its own; that the arrow must be imported from a frame whose evidence differs, with provenance and an anchor that does not move with the outcome; that the question is a bundle of direction, institution, prosperity measure, time scale and level, so verdicts must stay per slot and never be averaged; that the two causal stories are isomers over a fixed atom set, with the feedback case a cycle a chain cannot close; and that no standing list of good institutions survives a change of the residual. What it offers is the shape a valid direction check must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The cause slot stays open until the ground settles it.
