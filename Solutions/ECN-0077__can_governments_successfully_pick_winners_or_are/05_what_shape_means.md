## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0077 the shape is the four-part separator: per-slot residual, non-updating ground, low-overlap frames, pre-registered refuter. `SUCCESS = 1` is a name. `Which firm in this sector will still be paying customers in five years` is a residual that can be registered per slot. `A selection whose reference does not move when the selector pushes on it` is a shape. The framework returns the second and third, not the first.

**Falsifier for this section:** a figure line that returns a numerical success rate for government selection, with a derivation from the six-term set to the number. Until then the statement "the framework returns a shape" stands.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* In this question that means describing the selection failure as a checking-arrangement failure (an anchor that updates, frames that share a pool) rather than as a moral failure of officials.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the finish line is the point at which an outside checker can state the failure condition without knowing the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name (and the correction that matters here)

Only two things license a name:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A selected firm that survives an unsubsidized market test has been named by the ground, not by the frame.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). An empirical finding about a policy can enter the library, but only with its origin and its gap-explanation recorded.

### Correction to the first pass, kept visible

I initially wrote this section as if *"shape, not a name"* forbade naming any firm, sector, or instrument. That is an overstatement. The canonical phrase has a subject: a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Firms, capital, demand, regulations, and a paying market are already in the habitat of this question. Naming one as a candidate selection is a **binding** from the world frame onto an atom already held, not the invention of a seventh primitive. Fig 11 :43-48 requires the habitat to be enumerated from the world rather than composed: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*

What remains forbidden is treating the binding as a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**). My earlier claim is retracted here rather than deleted, matching the exemplar's own §5b correction.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens the point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Choosing "profit" rather than "patents" as the ground for a biotech program is a visible choice, and it must be recorded as such.

## 4. The source-discrimination problem for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the governing mechanism. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a firm that grows after receiving a subsidy produces the same observable reading (growth, employment, market share) whether the cause was a genuine underlying advantage the selector found or the subsidy itself. The reading cannot comment on the source. The claim "this proves governments can pick winners" names the source from a projection, and several sources give the same projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a verdict.

World corroboration from a genuinely different field: the Wikipedia *Survivorship bias* article, opened this session, describes the Wald aircraft problem, where reinforcing the areas where returning planes were hit overlooks *"where non-surviving planes were fatally hit."* A policy portfolio that looks like a set of winners has the same defect if the failed selections were publicly invisible. The *"advice business is a monopoly run by survivors"* line (McRaney, quoted in that article) is the industrial-policy success-story problem in one sentence.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A government agency's own evaluation of its picks is exactly a frame validating itself.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and this question is full of prestige

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

The industrial-policy debate runs on prestige: Nobel laureates on both sides, celebrated agencies, famous national cases. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list of prestigious economists selected for how likely they are to agree with a position on picking winners manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried, with blind criteria, and let the ground settle it.

Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."** An advocate of industrial policy wants the policy to work. That is an interest in the outcome and is unavoidable. An evaluator whose conclusions would change their standing in the debate has an interest in the reading and is disqualified from that reading.

## 6. Why this audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened this session): LLMs can *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The model's output is a simulation. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** an econometric test of industrial policy and cannot settle anything it reports on. This label appears on every substantive file.
