## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :36/:51, Fig 2 :13-15, Fig 9 :55/:57, Fig 7 :35/:36, Fig 12 :117/:121.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-part separator in `01` and `02`, and the one-paragraph version in `07` section 6.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim such as "agency X picked winners" or "the program created Y jobs."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot indexing.** A national gain cannot name a slot; Fig 1 :22 warns that aggregation erases the live signal.
- **provenance and shadow test.** A cited success is a recording until its origin is shown (Fig 8 :50-52); the one-paragraph version drops this.
- **the source-discriminating instrument.** Growth does not say whether the cause was advantage or support; the spectrometer analogy in *The Hard Problem is About Source* is the governing case.
- **the interest-in-the-reading declaration.** Fig 10 :59-60 requires it; the outreach version compresses it to one line and risks losing the disclosure.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an unanchored reading converges on influence, and a winner is a label assigned after the ground pays. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
