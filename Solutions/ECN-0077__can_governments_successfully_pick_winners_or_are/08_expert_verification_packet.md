# Expert verification packet: the Tether on picking winners

**What this is:** a claim in a form an industrial-policy or innovation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated **before** the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical rate. Not `grounded`.** No welfare measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. **Status:** simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of conditions, not a binary with two opposite answers. A "winner" is a label assigned after a lineage meets its ground, so no standing pre-payout list is figure-licensed. A selector whose reference updates on the selector converges on whoever had the most influence; with a real anchor the same arrangement can triangulate. Misallocation follows from a union of sufficient conditions, including unenumerated cascades, substituted habitats, aggregated signals and conventions promoted to verdicts. Selection itself is permitted only as ground-voted selection between genuinely differing rooms. The usable output is a four-part separator: per-slot residual, a non-updating ground, low-overlap frames, and a failure condition written before the run. No numerical success rate is returned.

### C3: the transfer and loop boundary, checkable by reading Figures 4, 11 and 12

- **Statement:** a cited success is a recording of one occasion until provenance and a shadow test are shown; protection substitutes a habitat rather than removing one; the rent mechanism is a positive cycle invisible in chain notation; market-led and state-led allocation are isomers over a fixed atom set.
- **Sources, verbatim:**

- **What to check:** does a successful transferred policy carry its binding arrangement intact, with provenance recorded? Was the evaluation run in the original habitat or a substituted one, and were second- and third-order cascades enumerated? Does the allocation comparison hold the atom set fixed?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a transferred policy that keeps its arrangement and still fails in a way the figure does not predict; or show that both allocators can be written with identical typed bonds over identical atoms and differ only by a scalar parameter; or exhibit a rent process whose output does not re-enter as input.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a position for or against industrial policy. No country, agency or program is endorsed or condemned.
- Not that governments never succeed, nor that every connected firm is undeserving. The no-anchor mechanism is structural, not an accusation.
- Not a numerical success rate, an optimal subsidy level, or a sectoral target. That is a `gap`, and no figure line returns it.
- Not that market allocation is automatically grounded. Survivorship bias can hide failed firms in a market frame just as it hides failed selections in a public one.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, dataset or experiment was run in this derivation.
- Not a claim that the framework names the right firm. It returns a shape; naming a candidate is a provenanced binding, not a derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement.

1. **Slot discriminator:** does an unsubsidized market test separate underlying advantage from subsidy effect for each supported firm?
2. **Anchor test:** does the evaluation ground move when the selecting office pushes on it? If it moves, it is not an anchor.
3. **Loop test:** does selection output re-enter as political input and close a positive cycle?
4. **Habitat test:** was the evaluation in the original habitat or a substituted one, and were the second- and third-order effects enumerated?
5. **Standing refuter:** any of the seven conditions in `01` section 7 firing.

2. Say whether your own field's evidence on picking winners is one evidence pool or several low-overlap frames. If it is one pool, its agreement carries no verdict weight here.
