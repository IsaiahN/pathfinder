# Expert verification packet: what data suffice for causal identification under feedback, confounding and hidden variables?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No claim is made that any particular X,Y pair is identified. This is a shape of sufficient data and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A joint distribution records the ingredients of a system and not the arrangement that produced them, so observational data alone can narrow the space to a class of observationally equivalent structures but cannot select a member. Four properties are what sufficient data must have: an anchor (a setting mechanism that does not update on the system), independence (frames that do not share the hidden source), loop resolution (time or intervention that separates the two passes of a feedback cycle), and an imported atom (a measurement or design that measures or replaces the unmeasured variable, with provenance recorded). The framework returns this shape, not a named instrument or latent, and stops where the instruments stop. Falsifiers are written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** the distribution does not carry the typed bond, and shared hidden sources make agreement worthless.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :46 "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
- **Refuter:** show a procedure that returns the typed bond from a joint distribution alone with no intervention, instrument or temporal asymmetry; or show two frames with one hidden source giving independent corroboration.

### C2 - the anchor and the import; checkable against intervention practice

- **Statement:** a sufficient setting mechanism must not update on the system, and only an import from a frame with a different closure supplies the missing atom.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **What to check:** is a randomized or do-operation setting genuinely outside the loop in your system? Does the instrument's value depend on the latent? If yes, the anchor condition fails. Does the new environment supply a distribution not derivable from the first? If no, no atom was imported.
- **Refuter:** an intervention whose assignment depends on the hidden confounder that still identifies the effect; or a closed frame deriving a new atom by composition alone.

### C3 - feedback and hidden variables; checkable by a dynamical-systems or complex-systems specialist

- **Statement:** feedback is a cycle that a chain cannot close, and a hidden common cause is a missing operand three steps out rather than a name read off the projection.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **What to check:** does your dataset separate the passes of the loop, or does it collapse them? Does enumeration out to the cascade find the hidden cause, or is the latent being named from a projection? Convergent cross mapping is a relevant outside instrument: "Cross mapping is generally asymmetric" (Wikipedia page, Sugihara et al. 2012).
- **Refuter:** a cyclic influence signed from a single time slice with no interventional or temporal asymmetry; or a latent named from the projection and confirmed by a discriminating instrument as the unique source (this would settle the slot rather than refute the method, and the claim would be revised).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a large sample, a small p-value, or a good fit is sufficient. Those are budget filters. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not a universal identification algorithm, and not a named estimator.
- Not that any specific latent has been identified here, or that causal direction is knowable in principle from observation alone with no extra assumptions. The claim is about what data must supply when identification succeeds.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, and this packet is a simulation.
- Not corroborated by agreement. The three essays cited share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Specifically:
1. An anchor test in which the alleged intervention stays fixed when the system is pushed.
2. An independence test showing the two frames do not share the hidden source.
3. A source-discriminating instrument that splits the equivalence class, as the spectrometer split green.
4. A loop test that reads the sign of the cycle from separated passes or a severed edge.

If these fail in a given system, the four-part set is not sufficient for that system and must be revised.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, Fig 12 :46, Fig 2 :29, Fig 6 :23 and Fig 12 :121, five short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own practice: name one intervention in your field whose setting is genuinely not driven by the hidden cause, or say why none exists.
3. Check C3 against a system you know: does the data separating the loop actually exist, or is the sign being assumed?
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
