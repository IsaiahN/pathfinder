## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters most here. Metamerism: the instrument "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The essay's conclusion is instrumental: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a mass-to-light ratio or a mass within the half-light radius is a reading. It does not, by itself, name the source (cold dark matter halo, warm dark matter halo, self-interacting profile, or a modified-gravity description). Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. Errani et al. 2024 make the same operational point from the physics side: micro galaxies are best distinguished from globular clusters "either directly, by measuring their velocity dispersion, or indirectly, by examining their tidal resilience." The discriminator is an instrument or a tidal test, not an argument about the shape.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* supplies the standing warning: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This file, and the whole folder, is a simulated derivation. Its shape can be checked for internal consistency; its number cannot be settled by it.
