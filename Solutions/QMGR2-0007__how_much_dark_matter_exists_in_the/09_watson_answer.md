## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number is a `gap`. Dark matter in the smallest systems is to be reported as a per-slot mass function over a network of halos, with a low-mass cutoff treated as a threshold comparison and imported from particle microphysics with provenance and a shadow test. "Smallest" is level-relative; the framework's "no bottom" rule does not yield a physical minimum halo mass, and forcing it to do so is the F2 strain, declared and re-censed rather than hidden. The figures supply no mass value. The answer is `unreached` in the world because stellar kinematics, profile fits and tidal tests are already returning readings, and `gap` in the figure frame because no figure line governs a number. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
