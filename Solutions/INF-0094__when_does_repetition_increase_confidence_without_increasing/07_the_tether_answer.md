# The Tether's answer: when does repetition increase confidence without increasing evidence?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a condition and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A claim is presented again. Confidence in it (C) rises; evidence for it (E) does not. The question asks for the separator: under which conditions do C and E come apart, and under which would they move together? The answer must not name a brain process, because the framework returns a shape rather than a name. It must give a condition that a researcher could try to break.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**Domain 1, the individual cognitive frame.** Verdict `specified`, triangulated.

1. C is exactly the quantity the figures disqualify as a metric. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Push by repeating; C moves, so C is not the anchor.
2. More presentations of the same claim visit more of the same space. Fig 6 :49: "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 13 :118: "infinite effort inside a finite reach."
3. C is channel state, not a verdict. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." If the repetition does not change contact, no amount of it changes E: Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

**Domain 2, the social transmission frame.** Verdict `specified`, triangulated.

4. Handing the claim along compounds agreement, not evidence. Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Ten repetitions by one person and ten by a synchronized crowd are one witness counted ten times, because the frames share a pool. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The remedy is the opposite of volume: Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**Domain 3, the ground and verification frame.** Verdict `general` structural, triangulated.

5. The only thing that can move E is contact with the ground: a diagnostic cue, an independent measurement, a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test. Repetition that does any of those is no longer repetition in the disqualified sense; it is a new term or a new frame. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**The condition, stated once.** Repetition increases confidence without increasing evidence exactly when each presentation is the same claim again (identity) at a later time (sequence), with no new diagnostic contact and no independent frame added. Equivalently: the repetition stays inside one closure and ranges against the channel rather than the ground.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that repetition is always harmful. When each presentation is an independent measurement, confidence and evidence should move together; that is the boundary case, not an exception.
- Not that familiarity or processing fluency is a discovered framework primitive. It is a binding imported from cognitive-science research, with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The framework returns a shape; the name comes from the field.
- Not that the effect is unconditional. The opened primary source reports that reliance on fluency falls when diagnostic information is available; the shape predicts exactly that boundary.
- Not a neurological mechanism, not a measured effect size, and not a claim that any particular study has been refuted.
- Not grounded. No instrument was built and no experiment was run.

## 4. What would settle it, and the standing falsifiers

The ground here is a source-discriminating instrument: one that tells whether a rise in confidence came from a diagnostic cue or from repeated exposure to the same content. A better interpretation of the same confidence reading will not do; a reading cannot comment on its own source.

1. A controlled study with an independent ground reading at each repetition, where C and E move together with no new diagnostic information and no independent frame introduced. That refutes the shape.
2. A figure re-census showing that accrued credibility is a ground metric, or that handed-along agreement does add evidence. That refutes the figure verdict.
3. A presentation that is both the same claim under identity and a production of new diagnostic content under `⇒`. That breaks the bond analysis in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
4. A source-discriminating instrument showing that confidence reliably indexes diagnostic evidence when contact is fixed. That refutes the channel reading.

The four were written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. None has been fired.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure verdict says what cannot be composed from what the figures hold: repetition inside a closure adds no atom. It does not tell us the neural implementation, the exact repetition count at which a person's reliance on familiarity changes, or the size of the effect in any population. Those are instruments and measurements, not further arguments. The stop is recorded here with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Indiscernibility is not identity: a fluent, confident answer is not thereby a checked one.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that repeated claims become true, and it does not name a brain mechanism. It says that confidence is a readout the frame produces for itself, so it cannot serve as the metric that settles the claim. More presentations of the same claim visit more of the same space without adding an atom; handed around a group, agreement compounds while evidence does not. The condition is therefore precise: repetition raises confidence without raising evidence when each presentation is the same claim again, with no new diagnostic contact and no independent source added. When a repetition becomes an independent measurement, or imports a genuinely different frame, confidence and evidence can move together. What is offered is the separator, the falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. Until that instrument runs, the result is `specified` and open.
