## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks for the *condition* under which two quantities come apart:

- **C**, confidence in a claim: a readout the perceiver can feel and report. It rises with repetition.
- **E**, evidence for the claim: whatever would settle whether the claim is true. It is reported to stay flat.

State the residual R as the gap that opens between C and E when a claim is presented again. In effect terms, R is not "repetition works"; it is "a quantity the frame produces for itself (C) separates from the quantity that would settle the claim (E), and the separation is systematic in the repetition count." No term in the question has been named as a mechanism. *Processing fluency*, *familiarity*, and *mere exposure* are bindings from the cognitive-science frame, not figure-derived primitives. They are recorded as such in section 6.

Three things follow before any figure is chosen:

1. The question cannot be answered by measuring C more carefully. C is the thing that moves; it is not the anchor.
2. The answer must say where the boundary lies, that is, under what new condition C and E would move together again.
3. The answer is a shape (a separator), not a name (a neurotransmitter, a brain region, a single named bias).

## 1. Domain census

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: at least three positions, a claim, a receiver, and an ordered series of presentations. Symmetry: each presentation is the *same* claim, so the compound is identity across an ordered sequence, not production of new content (identity `≡` is symmetric, sequence `→` is ordered; Fig 12 :67, Fig 12 :72). Scale: first order inside one perceiver, second order across a chain of perceivers who hand the claim along.

One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor. A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, individual cognitive frame** | the claim, the memory trace, the confidence judgment, the repetition count | C (confidence, a frame-internal readout) | none inside the frame. The ground enters only as the only metric (Fig 1 :23) | partition: which presentations change contact with the ground and which do not |
| **D2, social transmission frame** | speakers, hearers, the claim handed along | agreement and accrued credibility | the ground, never agreement (Fig 7 :35) | partition: a chain of mirrors versus selection between rooms |
| **D3, ground and verification frame** | the claim, the diagnostic cue, the instrument that would settle it | E (evidence about truth) | the anchor, which does not update (Fig 2 :29) | a measurement question, not an agreement question |

**Partition or union.** The primary cut is a partition of repetition events by whether they change contact with the ground. A repetition that both raises C and adds E (each presentation is an independent measurement) is a union compound with its own loop, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :54-58). One lever, more repetition, clears only the region where E does not bind; the intersection stands until it is unbundled.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Link 1: can the perceiver tell this presentation from the previous one (perception)? Link 2: does it have a term for what the repetition affords, familiarity or diagnostic value (vocabulary)? Link 3: can it say what would count as progress, C or E (objective)? A link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27). The middle link is where the failure sits: having a term for "feels familiar" was taken for a dictionary, when it is a competing objective (Fig 12 :115).

## 3. Findings

Every figure quote below is verbatim from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Each finding carries a mode and a refuter.

### F1 - Confidence is a frame-internal quantity, not evidence (`general` structural, `specified` for C)

Fig 1 :26: *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"*. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

C is accrued credibility, a quantity the frame both produces and scores itself with. The figures disqualify such a quantity as a metric. E is the ground metric. The divergence is therefore not a paradox; it is what happens when a frame-internal readout is mistaken for the metric.

**Refuter:** show that the figures treat accrued credibility as a ground metric, or produce a measurement in which C changes only when E changes. Either would collapse the separation.

**Triangulation:** triangulated with Fig 11 :40 and Fig 10 :24 (low overlap: scoring rule, contact rule, channel rule).

### F2 - A quantity that moves when you push it is not an anchor (`general`)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*

Push on the claim by repeating it: C moves, E does not. By the figure's own test C fails to be an anchor and E remains one. This is the separator the question asks for, stated as an ordinary term.

**Refuter:** a controlled case in which repetition of the claim leaves C unchanged while E changes, or in which C is invariant to pushing while still failing to settle the claim. The first would break the empirical claim, the second the figure's test.

**Triangulation:** triangulated with Fig 1 :23 and Fig 11 :40.

### F3 - Repetition inside one closure is more search at the same reach (`specified` cross-level application)

Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 13 :113-114: *"More compositions never add a member, and no chain reaches outside what the six and their bonds already span."* Fig 13 :118: *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."*

Each additional presentation of the same claim visits more of the same space. It does not add an atom, so it cannot add E. It can add C, because C is a readout of how much of that space has been visited.

**Refuter:** an experiment in which repeating a claim changes a diagnostic cue (a genuinely new atom, not a new spelling) and E moves. That would be an import, not more search at the same reach, and it would mark the boundary condition.

**Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 13 (closure algebra, wall operator, finite reach). Low overlap within the framework; one evidence pool across it.

### F4 - In the social domain, agreement compounds and evidence does not (`specified`)

Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Fig 2 :19-21: *"they never talk, and still agree so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"*. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

When a claim is repeated by many people who draw on the same pool, their agreement is the pool talking. Ten repetitions by one person and ten by a synchronized crowd are the same witness counted ten times. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Refuter:** show two frames with genuinely low overlap (different instruments or separate experience) whose agreement about a claim tracks E. That is triangulation and it is the case where repetition *does* add evidence, so it bounds the verdict rather than contradicting it.

**Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 7 and Fig 2 (room-chain dynamics versus anchor/independence rule).

### F5 - C lives in the channel, and the channel decays (`general`)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."*

A confidence judgment is channel state. It can be saturated, pooled, or saturated by repeated presentations. Treating a high C as a verdict is the named failure: the output is a report of contact, not a report of the ground.

**Refuter:** a channel reading that cannot be saturated by repetition while still responding to E. That instrument would be a ground-visible confidence measure and would falsify the channel claim.

**Triangulation:** triangulated with Fig 1 :27 and Fig 11 :40.

### F6 - The structure of repetition is identity over sequence (`general` structural)

Fig 12 :67: *"two names, one referent"*. Fig 12 :72: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*.

Under identity, the n-th presentation has the same referent as the first; it is one term with n spellings, not n new terms. Under sequence, the order is part of the term, so presentation 3 is not interchangeable with presentation 1. This asymmetry is the whole "when": repetition changes the *number of ordered accesses* to one referent, and changes nothing about the referent's support. A production bond (`⇒`, "A produces B; B did not exist before", Operators table :38) would be needed for a repetition to add evidence, and identity is not production.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a presentation that is a production of new content while remaining the same claim under identity. If such a compound is admitted, the bond analysis fails and C and E are not separable by structure.

**Triangulation:** single-source for the bond types (Fig 12 and Operators table are one evidence pool), triangulated with Fig 6 :49 for the *more of the same space* consequence.

### F7 - Repetition count is a filter, not a witness (`general`)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

Repetition tells you how much budget has been spent visiting a region, not whether the claim is in the region. A tier or a count decides what gets tried first; it says nothing about truth. This governs the outreach artifacts: a list of people who repeat the claim is a filter, never corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Refuter:** a case in which the repetition count of a claim, with no other information, changes the probability that the claim is true. That would make the filter a witness and break the rule.

**Triangulation:** triangulated with Fig 2 :40 and Fig 7 :13.

### F8 - The confidence readout cannot comment on its own source (essay corroboration, `specified`)

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the source.

C is such a projection. A rise in confidence is compatible with a new diagnostic cue and with familiar processing; the reading does not say which. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument about the reading. This finding is essay corroboration, not a figure line, and is labelled as such.

**Refuter:** an instrument that reads C and simultaneously identifies its source, with the two readings independent. That would close the gap the essay names.

## 3b. The three questions kept distinct (Formula step 7)

The formula forbids merging these. Each has a different instruction.

- **SEARCHED**: is the evidence already in the receiver's closure and merely unfound? Same atoms, same wall, more of the space visited (Fig 6 :49). Instruction: distribute the search and keep searchers independent.
- **INWARD**: sharpen the instrument already returning something. Here, an instrument that discriminates the source of a confidence reading (F8), built from a worse instrument already returning a fluent/disfluent signal. Instruction: refine in our own terms.
- **OUTWARD**: import from a frame whose closure differs. Here, mere exposure and truth-effect research. Source gate: the five external sources were opened, with author, venue, year and quote recorded. Shadow test: they explain exactly the gap the figures leave, why a frame-internal readout rises when contact does not change. Debited against independence: the Wikipedia pages share an encyclopedia pool and are counted as one witness where they agree.

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and are never read against each other. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts: the figure answer is structural (no new atom), the empirical answer is a budget question about how much diagnostic contact a study supplies.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A controlled study repeats a claim, an independent ground reading is taken at each repetition, and C and E move together with no new diagnostic information and no independent frame introduced. That would collapse the frame-internal/ground distinction.
2. A figure re-census shows that *"credibility accrued"* (Fig 1 :26) is a ground metric in the figures, or that *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13) was misread. That would break the figure verdict itself.
3. A presentation is demonstrated that is both the same claim under identity and a production of new diagnostic content under `⇒`. That would break the bond analysis in F6.
4. A source-discriminating instrument shows that a rise in confidence reliably indicates a rise in diagnostic evidence when contact is held fixed. That would falsify F8 and the channel reading in F5.

Until one of these is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State C and E as two separate quantities in any instrument, dashboard or workflow. Never report one in the units of the other (F1, Fig 9 :60).
2. When the aim is truth, buy independence rather than volume: new diagnostic contact, a low-overlap frame, or a source-discriminating instrument (Fig 2 :42, F4, F8).
3. When the aim is to *detect* a channel effect, count repetitions as a filter on how much of the same space has been visited, not as a witness (F3, F7).
4. Offer the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` one-to-one. Ask what would refute the separator before showing the framework (Fig 10 :42).
5. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator (the refuter and the shape), not the conclusion.
