## 1. Independent domain census, redone from the residual line alone

I redid the census using the WATSON order before relying on the primary's conclusions.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between two quantities: confidence C, which rises when a claim is presented again, and evidence E, which is reported to stay flat. Arity: claim, receiver, ordered presentations, so three positions. Symmetry: each presentation is the same claim under identity, which is symmetric, while the series is ordered under sequence; production would be needed to add new content. Scale: first order inside one perceiver, second order across a chain of perceivers. Question type: a partition by whether a presentation changes contact with the ground. A presentation that both raises C and adds E is a union compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-58), not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, individual cognitive frame.** Actors: claim, trace, confidence judgment, count. Currency: C. Anchor: none inside the frame; the ground enters only as the only metric (Fig 1 :23). This is a partition question about contact.
- **Domain 2, social transmission frame.** Actors: speakers, hearers, the handed-along claim. Currency: agreement and accrued credibility. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: mirror chain versus selection between rooms.
- **Domain 3, ground and verification frame.** Actors: claim, diagnostic cue, instrument. Currency: E. Anchor: the anchor, which does not update. This is a measurement question, not an agreement question.

Order by dependency: settle the bond (identity over sequence) before the molecule; the molecule before the loop; refuse "count as witness" before minting a mechanism (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a source frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
