## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its implementer. It constrains; it does not fill.

For this question the shape is:

> Repetition increases confidence without increasing evidence exactly when each presentation is the same claim again (identity) at a later time (sequence), with no new diagnostic contact and no independent frame added. It stops doing so when a repetition becomes an independent measurement or imports a low-overlap frame that predicts the residual.

That is a condition with a falsifier. `SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's shape; `confidence(???)evidence` is the analogue here. The two quantities are held apart, and the slot between them is marked unknown.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet sends an effect description and asks what would refute it, not the architecture.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* And the paradox: by the time you have the exact terms, you have already composed the answer through locating it.

The question-as-tether here is the C/E separation. Naming a brain process would not close it; it would substitute a mechanism for the condition.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A source-discriminating instrument, not an argument about fluency.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

The empirical vocabulary imported here (fluency, familiarity, source monitoring) is a visible choice from the cognitive-science frame. Its provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. It is not a discovered primitive of the framework.

## 4. Why the confidence reading cannot settle the question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

A confidence rating is the same kind of projection. A high rating is compatible with fresh diagnostic evidence and with repeated exposure to the same claim; the reading does not carry its own source. The metamerism point is retained: what is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a more confident interpretation of the rating.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"The output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* This is why a chain of repeated agreement about a claim cannot serve as evidence for it.

## 5. The social shape: why number does not become truth

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times. *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the drift case: two people who only check against each other *"drift together, and they drift together, so neither notices."* The ground must be *"immovable by persuasion"*.

These are the social counterpart of the figure verdict in D2. Repetition across a crowd with one pool is one witness wearing many names (Fig 8 :48-49). This is not a side point: it is the field's own "social information, reliability and misinformation" context from which the source question was drawn.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming *processing fluency* as the mechanism. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Fluency is not a framework primitive at all; it is an atom from the cognitive-science frame that has been imported to fill a slot the figures leave open. Naming it is allowed once its provenance and shadow test are shown. What would be forbidden is pretending the framework derived it.

I also initially treated the confidence rating as if a better calibration of the rating would settle the question. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says the opposite: a better reading of the same projection does not reveal its source. Both the first reading and its correction are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous researchers who agree that repetition is a channel effect is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture post is blunter: LLM outputs are *"simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
