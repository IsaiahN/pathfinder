## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a claim that is presented repeatedly: confidence in it rises while evidence for it does not. Option A: treat the rise in confidence as evidence that the claim is better supported. Option B: keep confidence and evidence as two separate quantities and state the condition under which they come apart, and the condition under which they would move together. What each leads to: A scores a frame-internal readout with itself and mistakes a filter for a witness; B gives a checkable separator, refuters written in advance, and a boundary case that a researcher can try to produce.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between C, a confidence readout that rises with repetition, and E, the ground-anchored support for the claim. Arity: a claim, a receiver and an ordered series of presentations (three positions). Symmetry: each presentation is the same claim under identity, while the series is ordered under sequence; identity is symmetric, sequence is not, and production would be required for a repetition to add new content. Scale: first order inside one perceiver, second order across a chain of perceivers. Fig 9 names a partition question: does a given repetition change contact with the ground or not. A repetition that does both is a union compound with its own loop, not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, individual cognitive frame.** Actors: the claim, the memory trace, the confidence judgment, the repetition count. Currency: C. Anchor: none inside the frame; the ground enters only as the only metric. Type: partition by contact.
- **Domain 2, social transmission frame.** Actors: speakers, hearers, the claim handed along. Currency: agreement and accrued credibility. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Type: partition between a chain of mirrors and selection between rooms.
- **Domain 3, ground and verification frame.** Actors: the claim, a diagnostic cue, the instrument that would settle it. Currency: E. Anchor: the anchor, which does not update. Type: a measurement question, not an agreement question.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle the bond structure (identity over sequence) before the molecule; settle the molecule before the loop; refuse the "count as witness" route before minting a mechanism (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a source frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: applying Fig 8's idempotent closure to human memory is a cross-level move, not a literal neural claim. Re-census shows the actors changed (claim and frame) while the rule did not, so the strain is named and the mode is `specified`, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any claim to have named the mechanism does not. Confidence is a frame-internal readout and fails the anchor test; repetition inside one closure visits more of the same space without adding an atom, and handed-along agreement compounds while evidence does not. The condition is therefore precise: repetition increases confidence without increasing evidence exactly when each presentation is the same claim again (identity) at a later time (sequence), with no new diagnostic contact and no independent frame added. It stops being true when a repetition is an independent measurement, or imports a low-overlap frame with a source gate and a shadow test, or when an instrument discriminates the source of the confidence reading. The identity/sequence analysis is `specified` and single-source within the framework. The neural implementation is an honest gap. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a recording or a request for agreement instead of a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical bindings (fluency, familiarity, source monitoring) are removable without touching the figure verdict, and a reader can substitute a different frame. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing that a rise in confidence tracks a diagnostic cue when contact is fixed; a controlled study in which repetition changes a diagnostic cue and evidence moves; a figure re-census showing accrued credibility is a ground metric or that handed-along agreement does add evidence; or a demonstration that a presentation is both the same claim under identity and a production of new content. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
