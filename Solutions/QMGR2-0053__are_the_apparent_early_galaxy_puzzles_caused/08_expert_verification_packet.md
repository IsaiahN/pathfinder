# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the early-galaxy puzzles

**What this is:** a claim in a form an early-galaxy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the union result, `specified` on the attribution, not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken by this agent.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "measurement, modeling assumptions, or a real theory problem?" is a partition request, but the conditions overlap. The correct output is a union sorted per object slot. In the observed early-galaxy excess, a measurement bin is real and already partly swept (interlopers, biased photometric redshifts, MIRI-driven mass revision); a modeling bin is open (IMF, dust law, M/L ratio, star-versus-AGN source for compact red objects); a theory bin survives within LCDM (feedback and efficiency, not a new primitive); and a held bin exists (a minimal model already reproduces the bright-end abundance). No new cosmological primitive is licensed until the cheap bins are swept and a term pays its bargain. The framework returns this shape and the search order, not a verdict on the data.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Structural, checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the three causes are a union, and the residual must be read per slot rather than aggregated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Also Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **What to check:** does a single lever move more than one condition? Wang et al. report that adding MIRI both lowers stellar masses by ~0.4 dex and lowers the LRD fraction from ~32% to ~13%, which is a union signature.
- **Refuter:** a full per-slot census in which each of the three conditions is disjoint, so a partition is licensed; or a global residual that trends down while every per-slot residual is flat.

### C2. Measurement and modeling, checkable against published follow-up

- **Statement:** the anchor is spectroscopy and rest-frame near-IR; the modeling terms are mis-attached and still open.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent".
- **What to check:** Arrabal Haro et al. find a confirmed-interloper rate of 8.3% and photometric z overestimated by 0.50 +/- 0.12; Arai et al. identify a pre-JWST z ~ 10 candidate as a z = 2.54 ULIRG; Wang et al. reduce number densities by ~36% with MIRI; Casey et al. conclude for LRDs that "this suggests stars do not dominate." Are those characterizations faithful? Is the LRD source still unresolved?
- **Refuter:** a spectroscopic or rest-frame near-IR result that leaves per-slot masses unchanged while templates vary; or a source-discriminating instrument that assigns all LRDs uniquely to one class.

### C3. Theory, checkable against simulations and models

- **Statement:** the surviving theory-bin residual is a feedback and efficiency gap inside LCDM, not a new primitive; a held bin already exists.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :14 "mechanism broken: a new term is owed". Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist."
- **What to check:** Weller et al. find up to ~2 dex underprediction of quenched z > 3 galaxies and "the need for improved physical models of AGN feedback"; Wang et al. find epsilon ~0.3 at z ~ 8 within standard LCDM; Ferrara et al. present "a minimal physical model that explains the observed galaxy abundance at z=10-14". Do these together remove the need for a new cosmological term?
- **Refuter:** a per-slot residual that persists and does not trend down after the cheap bins are swept, plus a proposed new term that costs less to state than the confusion it removes under Fig 5 :42 "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that JWST broke LCDM. No ground reading here supports a new cosmology.

- Not a derivation of galaxy formation from the figures.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an instrument this agent handled.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating observation, not an argument.

1. Spectroscopic z and rest-frame NIR for every contested slot.
2. An instrument that separates LRD source classes and one that fixes the IMF at z > 7.
3. An unmodified LCDM simulation reproducing the full per-slot abundance, including quenched z > 3 galaxies.
