## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a table of stellar masses before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in each bin before giving them the essay set.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The ten papers partly share CEERS and PRIMER data; do not present their agreement as ten witnesses.
- Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send selected for people already inclined to say "LCDM is in crisis" produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union claim and the three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in each bin; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual and the refuter. Ask for the recipient's verdict on the sorting before any framework is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule, and the source ledger. Their agreement after this point is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Stage 3 sends a generator, which Fig 4 :22 permits.

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 only works if the sorting survives. As currently written it is a shape, not a filled table. Fill the per-slot table from real catalog data before sending.
