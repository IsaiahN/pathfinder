## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the cause

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a shape is: **a union of overlapping conditions, sorted per object slot, with the anchor run before the term is minted, and an honest `unreachable` where no discriminator exists.** That is a form of the answer, not the answer. It does not tell us which candidate is an interloper or which IMF is right.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For early galaxies: start from "too massive too early", add "z_phot", "Balmer break", "MIRI rest-frame NIR", "LRD", "AGN fraction", "IMF slope", each narrowing the slot.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay-level precedent for Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A compact red SED can be a dusty starburst, an AGN, or a quenched interloper at lower z. The reading does not name the source. Casey et al. find the star-to-dust ratio "a factor ~100x larger than expected from dust formation models if one assumes the rest-optical light is dominated by stars; this suggests stars do not dominate", which is a metamerism result: the SED alone cannot discriminate, so a better instrument is owed, not a name.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*, opened this session: "indiscernibility is not identity." Its distinction horizon and union horizon are the precise status of the LRD question: below a given observer's horizon now, not settled for all observers and all instruments.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A spectroscopic z, a MIRI mass, an ALMA dust limit.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

So naming "measurement error" for a specific candidate is licensed only by a follow-up spectrum. Naming "new physics" is licensed only by a residual that persists after the cheap bins are swept and a term that pays its bargain (Fig 5 :42 "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold.").

## 4. Why the aggregate answer is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." The early-galaxy analogue is a number density. It is a reading of many projections. It cannot comment on whether the excess came from wrong redshifts, wrong masses, a wrong IMF, or a wrong efficiency. Naidu et al. make the point sharply: both redshift solutions for CEERS-1749 "hold the potential to challenge existing models of early galaxy evolution, making spectroscopic follow-up of this source critical." The aggregate is below the union horizon for source discrimination.

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Wang et al. show the mass bias is concentrated above 10^10 solar masses; an all-mass average would have thrown away the only signal that says where to look.

## 5. Does the framework name a new cosmology? No

Question answered: **nothing in the framework returns the name of a new cosmological primitive.** What it returns is a shape (union, per-slot, anchor-led, mint-last) and a route (SEARCHED, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7). A specific cause is licensed only by the ground settling a term or by a provenanced import. There is no ground reading in this folder, so no cause is `grounded` here.

The record's own answer is therefore careful: the theory bin's surviving residual is a **modeling** gap (AGN feedback, efficiency) inside LCDM, not a cosmological one. Ferrara et al. show that a minimal model already explains the z = 10-14 abundance. Weller et al. locate the shortfall in feedback prescriptions. Neither requires a new primitive.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names agreeing that "JWST broke cosmology" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the sorting, and the ground decides.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
