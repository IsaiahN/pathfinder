# The Tether's answer on the early-galaxy puzzles

**Mode: `general` on the union result, `specified` on the attribution, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The reported early universe contains more stellar mass, earlier quiescence, and more compact red sources than current models predict. The question asks whether this is measurement, modeling assumptions, or a real theory problem. The figures refuse the either/or before answering it: Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The residual is a vector over object slots, not an average (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union, so unbundle before judging.** A photometric redshift error changes a fitted mass, which changes the theory comparison. A lever that fixes one condition leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").
2. **Measurement is the `new` bin.** Spectroscopy and rest-frame near-IR extend perception. Interlopers are real (Arai et al.), photometric z is slightly overestimated (Arrabal Haro et al.), and adding MIRI cuts mass by ~0.4 dex and density by ~36% (Wang et al.). Fig 5 :12 "new: extend perception, aim the probe here".
3. **Modeling assumptions are the `mis-attached` bin.** The IMF, dust law, M/L ratio, and the star-versus-AGN question are terms already held that need refitting. Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent". van Dokkum and Conroy show lighter IMFs reduce the tension; Casey et al. show stars likely do not dominate LRD light. This bin is open, not settled.
4. **The theory bin is `mechanism broken` inside LCDM, not a new primitive.** Weller et al. find simulations underpredict quenched massive galaxies at z > 3 by up to ~2 dex and locate the gap in AGN feedback; Wang et al. find epsilon ~0.3 at z ~ 8 within standard LCDM. Fig 5 :14 "mechanism broken: a new term is owed" licenses a term, not a cosmology.
5. **A `held` bin exists.** Ferrara et al. reproduce the z = 10-14 abundance with a minimal model: decreasing dust attenuation compensates the shortage of halos. Part of the puzzle was never a puzzle.
6. **No new cosmological primitive is licensed as written.** Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist." SUPPORT is not yet shown for a new primitive because the cheap bins are not exhausted; REACHABILITY fails for a new primitive by definition (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."); the import has no source gate or shadow test.
7. **The anchor is the spectrum, not the consensus.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." A room of agreeing photometric selections is variation, not a verdict.
8. **Where no discriminator exists, report `unreachable`.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." For direct dynamical mass at the highest z, the inward track may have no instrument already returning something to extend.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a verdict that "JWST broke LCDM." No ground reading here supports a new cosmology, and the held bin plus the feedback result argue against the need for one.
- Not that the puzzle is solved. The LRD source and the z > 7 IMF remain open, and the quenched-galaxy shortfall remains in the simulations.
- Not that measurement error is the whole story. The literature moved the size of the anomaly, not every component of it.
- Not that the three causes are mutually exclusive. That is the framing being refused.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this agent.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A per-slot table, not a further argument:

1. Anchor: spectroscopic z and rest-frame NIR for every candidate in the contested slots. Does the residual move when the anchor is sharpened?
2. Source discriminator: an instrument that separates dusty star formation from AGN-dominated LRD light, and one that fixes the IMF at z > 7. Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
3. Theory: a simulation within unmodified LCDM that reproduces the full per-slot abundance, including quenched z > 3 galaxies. If it succeeds, the theory-bin residual falls to `held`.
4. Standing falsifiers from `01`: disjoint conditions, anchor-invariant residuals, a persisting non-trending residual with a cheap new term, a named LRD source, or a fully successful LCDM simulation.

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops at the limit of what the instruments currently distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." The union shape is robust; the per-slot attribution is empirical and unfinished. A second seat that reads the same papers would be a mirror with a different name, not a witness (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The early-galaxy puzzles are not three competing answers, they are one union of overlapping conditions. The figures say to unbundle them and sort per object, not to average them into "too many galaxies too early." Spectroscopy and rest-frame near-IR are the anchor and have already moved many candidates out of the puzzle; IMF, dust law and the question of whether little red dots are stars or black holes are mis-attached terms that need refitting; and the residual that survives inside galaxy-formation models is a feedback and efficiency gap, not a demand for new cosmology. Part of the excess was never there, because a minimal LCDM model already reproduces the bright-end abundance. What is owed now is a per-slot table, a source-discriminating instrument, and a refuter written in advance. No new primitive is licensed until the cheap bins are swept and the bargain pays. That is a shape and a route, not a verdict.
