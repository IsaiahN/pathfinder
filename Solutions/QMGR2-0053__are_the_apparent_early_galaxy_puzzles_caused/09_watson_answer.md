## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is refused. The puzzles form a union of overlapping conditions, sorted per object slot. A measurement bin is real and partly swept: spectroscopy and rest-frame near-IR have moved candidates and masses. A modeling bin is open: IMF, dust law and LRD source are mis-attached terms. A theory bin survives inside LCDM as a feedback and efficiency gap, not a new primitive. A held bin exists, since a minimal model reproduces the bright-end abundance. No new cosmological primitive is licensed because the guards have not passed and the arrangements of held atoms are not exhausted. This is a `general` structural result with a `specified`, ungrounded attribution.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, sorts per slot and records its refuters, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a "crisis" narrative instead of a sorting method; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The bins are revisable without touching the figures. A source-discriminating observation moves the LRD slot; a full per-slot table moves the measurement and modeling slots; an LCDM simulation that reproduces the abundance moves the theory slot to `held`. Nothing has to be withdrawn en bloc.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A full per-slot census showing disjoint conditions reverses the union verdict. Anchor-invariant residuals reverse the measurement verdict. A named LRD source or a fixed z > 7 IMF reverses the modeling verdict. A persisting non-trending residual with a cheap new term reverses the no-new-primitive verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
