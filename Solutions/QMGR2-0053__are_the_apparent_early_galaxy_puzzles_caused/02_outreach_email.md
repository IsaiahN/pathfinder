**Subject:** One checkable claim about the early-galaxy puzzles, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` structurally, `specified` in attribution, not `grounded`):** the question "measurement, modeling assumptions, or a real problem?" is a partition request, but the three conditions overlap. A photometric redshift error changes a fitted stellar mass, which changes the comparison to a model. So the first output is a union, not a winner. The sorting I would like checked is:
>
> 1. Measurement. A subset of the surprise is `new` in the figures' sense: extend perception. Spectroscopy and rest-frame near-IR have already moved reported redshifts and masses, and some z ~ 10 candidates were low-z interlopers.
> 2. Modeling assumptions. A subset is `mis-attached`: refit what you have, do not invent. IMF, dust law, mass-to-light ratio, and whether a compact red source is stars or an AGN change the mass without changing the photons.
> 3. Theory. A subset is `mechanism broken`: a new term is owed, but inside the existing cosmology so far. Simulations underpredict quenched massive galaxies at z > 3, and the missing physics is in feedback and efficiency, not yet in the cosmological model.
> 4. Held. A portion is already explained. A minimal model with a halo mass function and an obscured-star-formation prescription reproduces the bright-end abundance at z = 10-14 without new physics.
>
> **What would refute this:**
> 1. show that the three conditions are disjoint across a full per-slot catalog, so the union claim is false;
> 2. show that spectroscopically confirmed masses do not move while photometric selections are varied, so the anchor claim is false;
> 3. show a residual that persists and does not trend down after measurement and modeling are swept, and whose proposed new term costs less than the confusion it removes.
>
> I wrote these down before asking anyone. If any one holds, I will retract the attribution. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
