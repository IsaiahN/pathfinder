# The Tether's answer on shared pattern versus shared mechanism

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural claim that a reading cannot decide its own source, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A mathematical form P is observed in two domains. Researchers must decide whether one generator produced it in both or whether different generators produced the same reading. The residual is the gap between what the proposed attribution predicts and what a second domain, an intervention or a source-discriminating instrument actually returns, indexed per pattern-slot and never averaged. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case that fails source-discrimination is not a harder case of one that fails independence (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **A reading is not a source.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." The equation on the page records the ingredient list. The bond and the route that produced it are not recoverable from the ingredients (Fig 8 :51). So a shared pattern is a symmetric identity claim while a shared mechanism is an ordered production claim. The question slides from one to the other without paying for the change.

2. **The decision is a union of four conditions, with different anchors and instruments.**
   - **C1, a discriminator.** Does anything, at any resolution, return different readings for the two sources? Fig 6 :34 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it." If nothing has ever registered a difference, the honest verdict is `unreachable` at this budget, not "same mechanism" and not "no mechanism".
   - **C2, independence.** Two frames with differing closures, low evidence overlap, each ranged against its own unmoved ground. Fig 2 :31 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Agreement from one pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
   - **C3, composition and provenance.** The mechanism is a priced arrangement of atoms and typed bonds, and the record separates convergence from import. Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 8 :51 separates convergent derivation from adopted import by the record alone.
   - **C4, habitat.** Capability is mechanism-and-habitat. Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A mechanism must be re-tested in each habitat, and the loss across transfer measured (Fig 4 :30).

3. **Both outcomes are real.** Superficial resemblance is the verdict under common cause, convergent routes, equifinality or an unpriced import. Shared mechanism is the verdict when a discriminator identifies the coarse generator and the record shows either convergence or a shared fixed point. Universality classes are the positive boundary: different microscopic models share critical exponents because a renormalization-group flow is the instrument that says which details are irrelevant. Refusing that case would be a strain, not a check.

4. **What transfers is a method, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A formula carried as a finished answer can be replayed but cannot generate new readings in a substituted habitat unless its bonds and conditions crossed too. The round trip must be measured, not assumed zero (Fig 4 :30).

5. **Which mechanism actually produced a given pattern is a declared `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns a shape, not a name, and the discriminator that would settle the source is not held here. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The gap is of the source-discriminator kind, not a silence and not an invitation to invent a mechanism.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal diagnostic test. Fig 11 :51 refuses a standing list of tell-tale features: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not that shared patterns are always superficial. The universality case is admitted as a genuine positive.
- Not that an isomorphism or a shared exponent proves a shared mechanism. Structure preservation is a symmetric claim; the source is ordered (Fig 12 :92).
- Not that parsimony decides. Description cost and verification cost are in different units and do not add (Fig 12 :125-130). Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating instrument was run here.
- Not a single averaged cross-domain verdict. Verdicts are kept per pattern-slot and per domain, never pooled.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground and its instruments

A source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. In the terms of *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not the argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Discriminator test:** what perturbation changes the pattern in candidate mechanism A but not in B, and has that perturbation been run in both domains?
- **Intervention test:** remove the proposed generator and re-read the pattern. Fig 12 :78: *"is there a B before A fires? No."* If the pattern was already waiting, the production claim fails.
- **Independence test:** do the two groups share a dataset, a review and a fitting convention? If so they are one witness, however many papers they wrote.
- **Provenance test:** which field got the term first, by which route, and did either borrow it? Fig 8 :51: only the record separates the two.
- **Habitat and round trip:** carry the method into the second domain and measure what the transfer dropped (Fig 4 :30).
- **Standing falsifiers from the record:** the seven failure conditions in `01` §4, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The source-discriminating instrument is owed, and at this budget none is held for any given pattern pair. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The six-term substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where instruments can no longer distinguish sources and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give you a universal test for whether a shared mathematical pattern means a shared mechanism. It says the pattern is a reading, and a reading does not carry the bond or the route that made it, so the question has to be decided by four things: a source-discriminating instrument, two independent frames ranged against grounds that do not move, a priced arrangement of parts plus a record of where each part came from, and a re-check in each domain's habitat with the transfer loss measured. Shared mechanism is a real verdict, as in universality classes where a coarse generator can be identified; superficial resemblance is also real, as in convergent evolution or a common cause. What the method returns is the shape of a check and its written failure conditions, not the name of the mechanism behind any particular pattern. That name stays open until an instrument pays for it. Unreached is a verdict, not a silence.
