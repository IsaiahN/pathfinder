# Expert verification packet - the Tether on shared pattern versus shared mechanism

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complex systems researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural claim that a reading cannot decide its own source, not `grounded`.** No source attribution is claimed as settled. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A shared mathematical pattern is a reading, and a reading does not carry the bond or the route that produced it, so the form alone cannot decide whether one mechanism generated it in two domains or different mechanisms generated the same reading. The honest answer is a four-condition separator with four distinct failure modes: a source-discriminating instrument that returns different readings for the candidates (C1); two frames with differing evidence pools, each ranged against a ground that does not update (C2); a priced composition of atoms and typed bonds with a record that separates convergent derivation from adopted import (C3); and a re-check in each habitat with the loss across transfer measured (C4). Shared mechanism is a real verdict when a discriminator identifies the coarse generator, as in universality classes; superficial resemblance is a real verdict under a common cause, convergent routes or an unpriced import. Which mechanism actually produced a given pattern in a given pair of domains is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 8
- **Statement:** the ingredient list of a composed term does not carry its bond or its route; two entries with the same ingredients can be different substances, and convergence and import look identical in the contents.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 8 :51 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 13 :128 - *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."*
- **Refuter:** show a mathematical form that determines the intervention which will break it, with no ground reading and no lineage; or show two mechanisms with identical atoms and identical bonds that diverge under intervention with no habitat difference and no unmeasured bond.

### C2 - method-facing, checkable against practice in the recipient's own field
- **Statement:** agreement between two groups that share evidence is one witness, not two; independence is one quantity read twice, deciding both whether agreement means anything and whether a union of frames has surplus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 - *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 8 :50 - *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*
- **What to check:** take a published case where two teams confirmed a shared exponent. Did they use the same dataset, review or fitting convention? If yes, count them once. Then find a case where two genuinely different instruments reached the same term separately and ask whether a record shows the route.
- **Refuter:** a replicated case in which one shared-pool pair gets the source right and a low-overlap pair gets it wrong.

### C3 - instrument-facing, checkable by asking what would break the pattern
- **Statement:** a discriminator descends from an instrument already returning something, and where no instrument at any resolution registers a difference, the verdict is unreached, not "same mechanism".
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 - *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34 - *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
- **What to check:** for a shared pattern the recipient knows well, name one perturbation that changes it under candidate mechanism A but not B, and one that does the reverse. If neither can be named, say so; that is data about the gap, not an objection to the packet.
- **Refuter:** an instrument that returns one reading for two mechanisms and still identifies which produced it; or a correct attribution later confirmed by an instrument not built from any prior reading.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal diagnostic test, and not a standing list of which patterns are diagnostic (Fig 11 :51).
- Not that shared patterns are always superficial. Universality classes are admitted as a genuine positive case where a coarse generator is identifiable.
- Not that an isomorphism, a shared exponent or a good fit proves a shared mechanism. Isomorphism preserves structure; it does not name a source. Dimensionless equivalence is an instrument for modelling (Buckingham pi theorem), not an anchor for mechanism.
- Not that parsimony or description cost settles the verdict. Bits and actions are different currencies (Fig 12 :125-130). A fit statistic is a filter for the budget, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that this packet can name the mechanism behind a particular pattern. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not grounded. No discriminator was run here.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. **Discriminator test:** name the perturbation that changes the pattern under A but not B, and run it in both domains.
2. **Intervention test:** remove the proposed generator and re-read the pattern. If the pattern was already waiting, the production claim fails (Fig 12 :78).
3. **Independence test:** establish whether the two groups share a dataset, review or fitting convention.
4. **Provenance test:** establish which field got the term first, by which route, and whether either borrowed it (Fig 8 :51).
5. **Habitat and round trip:** carry the method into the second domain and measure what transfer dropped (Fig 4 :30).
