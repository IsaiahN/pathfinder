## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**No world source flips a verdict.** The figures are primary; the literature is corroboration only. The fields below are deliberately low-overlap.

1. **Statistical physics, genuinely shared pattern with a shared coarse mechanism.** The *Universality class* page says models in a class differ at finite scales but share a scale-invariant limit, and that *"The critical exponents are independent of microscopic details of the model, but depend on dimensionality, symmetry, and range of interactions."* This is the positive case: same pattern, different microscopic atoms, one shared generator at a coarser level, identifiable because the theory predicts which perturbations change the exponent and which do not. It corroborates the level-relative claim in Fig 12 :104 and is the boundary that stops the answer from becoming a blanket "resemblance only".
2. **Evolutionary biology, same form from different routes.** *Convergent evolution*: analogous structures have similar form or function but were not present in the last common ancestor; the page also distinguishes pattern-based from process-based measures of inference. This corroborates Fig 12 :95-97: the composition is not recoverable from the ingredients, and process evidence is needed.
3. **Allometry, same pattern with contested mechanism.** *Kleiber's law* reports a 3/4 scaling across animals and plants while stating that *"the law currently lacks a single theoretical explanation that is entirely satisfactory."* WBE, thermodynamic models and the MMLE framework each derive the exponent from different structures; the page also records Hulbert's description of it as *"neither a law nor a rule but rather an empirical approximation"*. This is the `gap` in F10 observed in the world: the pattern is settled, the source is not.
4. **Engineering and applied mathematics, pattern equivalence without mechanism.** The *Buckingham pi theorem* page states that systems sharing the same dimensionless description are equivalent for the purposes of the equation, and that similarity theory reproduces curves *"even absent the physical model"*. This corroborates the anchor test (Fig 2 :29-30): a dimensionless group is an instrument for equivalence, not an anchor for source.
5. **Human cognition and mathematics.** The *Analogy* page reports Gentner's structure-mapping theory and the systematicity principle: people prefer mappings that align relationships rather than objects, and the strongest version is an isomorphism that preserves structure. Isomorphism is a precise mathematical notion of same-structure-different-stuff. This corroborates C3 from an evidence pool far from physics, while also warning that object-level matches can fool a comparison.
6. **Statistics, same pattern from a hidden common cause.** The *Spurious relationship* page gives the common-cause structure directly: two variables associated but not causally related because of an unseen third factor. This is the figure's collapse 2 (*"common cause"*, *"hidden shared source"*, Fig 2 :6-7) observed in a different field.
7. **Systems theory, same end state from many means.** *Equifinality* defines the principle and distinguishes process equifinality from model equifinality, where different parameter sets give equally acceptable simulations. This corroborates the union reading and the warning that a good fit is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
8. **Framework-internal essays, used for the canonical reading only.** *The Hard Problem is About Source*, *The Library of Babel* and *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* were opened directly. Their author wrote the figures, so overlap is maximal and they carry no independent witness weight. They are used in `05_what_shape_means.md` only.

**Overlap declaration.** The seven substantive Wikipedia pages span genuinely different fields, but they share one venue and some citation chains. They count as about four witness pools, not seven: statistical physics and critical phenomena (one); evolutionary biology and allometry (one); engineering similarity, statistics and systems theory (one, with partially shared mathematical ancestry); cognition and analogy (one). Agreement among them is therefore weaker than independent primary-source corroboration. The framework essays are a fifth pool with maximum overlap with the figures and zero independent weight.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the separator and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the separator were true before showing the source essay.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the separator and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable journal policy.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the separator and its pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
