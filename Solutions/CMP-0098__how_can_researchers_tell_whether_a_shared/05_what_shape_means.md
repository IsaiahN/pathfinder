## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the source

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish, and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for CMP-0098 a shape is: **the narrowed form of a decision procedure that can be stated before the mechanism behind any particular shared pattern is named.** It constrains; it does not fill.

- Shape: *same form plus no discriminator plus one shared evidence pool is resemblance until proven otherwise; same form plus a discriminator that predicts what would break the pattern plus independent frames plus a recorded route is a candidate shared mechanism.*
- Name, withheld: *this exponent recurs because of percolation / because of preferential attachment / because of a universal fixed point / because of a common ancestor.* Each is a binding from a domain frame, not an output of the loop.

The distinction matters here because cross-domain science is almost entirely in the naming business. Universality, convergence, isomorphism, equifinality, scaling and analogy are candidate names. F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` withholds all of them until a discriminator and a lineage are shown.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "universality class", "analogy", "isomorphism", "convergent evolution", "equifinality", "spurious correlation", "scaling law" and "structure mapping". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (a reading that cannot decide its own source, indexed per pattern-slot) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating CMP-0098 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with a single test. The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when C1 to C4 are stated, an instrument is named for each, and the refuters are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).

The essay also explains why analogies work in the first place: *"they are translating structural relationships and problems through language and scenarios that you are already familiar with, so that the knowledge transfers."* That is exactly the promise and the danger of cross-domain pattern matching. The transfer is of structure, not of source.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For CMP-0098 neither has happened inside this solution. No source-discriminating instrument was run, and the cross-domain names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. A name is licensed when an intervention shows which generator an exponent belongs to, or when a dated record shows two fields reached the same term separately. Not before.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"*
> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a cross-domain pattern argument makes when it reads a shared exponent and names its source. A power law is a reading. It is consistent with criticality, preferential attachment, cumulative distributions of many other origins, or a measurement artefact. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* What is owed is a discriminator, not a better argument about the form. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The positive mirror of the same essay matters too: the spectrometer was an instrument, and it was the instrument, not a argument, that resolved the metamerism. Universality classes are the analogous positive case in physics: the renormalization group is the instrument that says which microscopic details are irrelevant and which perturbations change the exponent. That is why the answer here cannot be "resemblance only".

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to cross-domain research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The essay's own Ostrom passage is directly relevant: the author reached a governance structure from bacteria, biology and game agents, while Ostrom reached it from committees, fisheries and forests, and *"because we came from different fields, I was able to explain things she left vague, and vice versa."* With a different paper trail and different vocabulary, the structures matched. That is convergence at maximum independence, not a copy. It is also framework-internal corroboration, so it carries no independent witness weight.

For outreach this means: a list of famous complex-systems researchers who agree with the separator would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any candidate mechanism is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive or discriminator, one not held in Γ. If a mechanism or a bond is already available in a field's library, naming it is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall by itself (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) and claiming the form determines the source without an instrument. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `general` for the reading and `specified` where it makes claims about research practice. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the separator and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For CMP-0098 the canonical shape is: **a four-condition conjunction with four distinct failure modes, plus an instrument owed at the point where the pattern is read. It is not a standing diagnostic list, not a name for any particular mechanism, and not a score aggregating domains.** The names that appear in the literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instrument that would convert the shape into a grounded answer is not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` with a declared `gap`, and the stop is recorded.
