## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a cross-domain attribution and what a discriminator returns. Arity: four places (pattern, two domains, candidate mechanism), indexed per pattern-slot. Symmetry: the observed equality is symmetric, the mechanism attribution is ordered, so the two cannot be the same bond (Fig 12 :92). Scale: pattern within a domain, generator at a deeper stratum, comparison across domains, with a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :27).
- **Domain 1, reading.** Actors: instruments, fits, exponents, motifs. Currency: description cost in bits. Anchor: the held-out measurement in each domain. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, generator.** Actors: atoms and typed bonds, interventions. Currency: actions to verify. Anchor: a discriminator or intervention. Admitted only conditionally, because for many patterns no such anchor is held.
- **Domain 3, comparison.** Actors: two frames with differing closures. Currency: independence. Anchor: each frame's own unmoved ground. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, provenance.** Actors: dated records and routes. Currency: provenance and shadow test. Anchor: the record of origin. Admitted.
- **X1, elegance as self-evident.** No anchor independent of the reading. Not a domain yet. Excluded.
- **Union, not partition.** C1 discrimination, C2 independence, C3 composition/provenance, C4 habitat are four conditions with different instruments. One lever clears the C1-only region and leaves the intersections (Fig 9 :55).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The vocabulary link, having a term for the source of a reading, is the one with no instrument.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5, :95, :117 (bond invisible in ingredients; isomer), Fig 8 :50-51 (convergence versus import), Fig 2 :31, :40 (independence) and Fig 4 :22-25, :30 (method versus recording; measured gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` §5, `07` §2 and `08` §1: a reading cannot decide its source; a four-condition separator; both outcomes real; the source itself is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a researcher would act on: run this discriminator, check pool overlap, record the bond, re-test in the second habitat, and only then say "shared mechanism".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **mechanism type at the coarse level**: saying "shared generator" collapses the isomer/convergence distinction, which only an intervention or a dated lineage restores;
- **which independence is being bought**: the shorthand says "independent frames", but the figure requires low evidence overlap with each frame ranged against its own unmoved ground, and a shared dataset fails it;
- **the provenance test**: the shorthand says "record the route", but the figure separates convergence from import by the record, and a bare citation of priority is not a route;
- **the habitat conditions required by the goal**: the shorthand says "re-test in the second domain", but Fig 11 :66-69 says a substituted habitat has two silent failures, what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a shared pattern is a reading and cannot decide its own source, and the decision needs a discriminator plus independence plus a record. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
