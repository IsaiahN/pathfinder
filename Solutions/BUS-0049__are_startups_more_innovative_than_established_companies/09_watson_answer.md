## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that startups are more innovative than established companies, and about the alternative that established firms have different strengths and constraints. Option A: assert one scalar ranking of two firm classes on innovation. Option B: refuse the scalar, split the question into conditions, and read the two forms as arrangements in two habitats. What each leads to: A produces a league table and a policy that funds one class, built by aggregating away the very signal that says where to look; B produces a shape with stated refuters and an open magnitude to be closed by a ground reading.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A ranking that does not resolve and a set of measurement rules each working on a different subset. Arity: two conditions, initiation and implementation. Symmetry: the comparison is ordered, not symmetric, and presumes two quantities on one axis, which is exactly what is owed. Scale: first order at the firm and its habitat, second order at the population of firms. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" makes this a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a partition.
- **Domain 1, measurement of innovation.** Actors: patents, R&D counts, new products, initiation versus implementation. Currency: counts, citations, description length. Anchor: a defined metric that pays on the ground for both classes, unbound until one is supplied. Question type: union, not partition.
- **Domain 2, organizational size and habitat.** Actors: hierarchy, slack, process, specialization, team size. Currency: coordination cost, exploration versus exploitation. Anchor: survival and adaptation in that habitat. Question type: habitat-relative capability, with valence relative to the residual.
- **Domain 3, verification of the comparison.** Actors: researchers and meta-analyses. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run the two literatures share an evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Domain 4, outreach after a verdict.** Actors: expert readers in innovation studies. Currency: a correction on shape. Anchor: the ground. Contact is never evidence.

Order by dependency: split the union before searching (Fig 9 :54); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53); settle the first condition in its habitat before the second-order class comparison (Fig 4 membrane); refuse the scalar before minting a policy lever (Formula step 2).

Strain test applied: the comparison bond ⋛ is Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities". Forcing it onto the class comparison needs two quantities on one axis, which the census denies. The strain says ⋛ governs a settled quantity inside one condition, not the union, so the reading was re-censused rather than smoothed. One strain is named and kept.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the scalar ranking does not. The question is a union of initiation and implementation; capability belongs to firm plus habitat; structural features have opposite valences for the two conditions; the two forms are arrangements of shared atoms (isomers over the full atom set, or a functional group as usually written). A numerical ranking is a `gap` to be closed by a discriminating instrument, not a figure-derived result. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single point is Fig 1 :19: aggregating per-slot residuals is how the live signal disappears.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes three refuters before the conclusion, and carries provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a league table instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar ranking is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. Nothing has to be withdrawn from the record, and no primitive is lost. A refutation adds a term that refuses rather than deleting one, so the correction can be carried.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A metric that discriminates initiation from implementation and pays for both classes on one ground would close the gap; a demonstration that relocating the same people and resources leaves capability unchanged would break Fig 11 :39; a feature carrying the same valence for both conditions would break Fig 11 :49; a measure that distinguishes the two arrangements from ingredient counts alone would clear the Fig 12 :5 objection. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
