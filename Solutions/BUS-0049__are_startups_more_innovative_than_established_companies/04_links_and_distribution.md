## Three findings from the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The empirical sign is method-dependent.** Damanpour 1992 finds a positive size-innovation relation but a stronger size effect for implementation than initiation. Camison-Zornoza et al. 2004 attribute contradictory results to how variables were operationalized. So "startups are more innovative" is not a settled empirical finding even inside the business literature, which is what the figure-level refusal predicts.
2. **The two conditions are independently supported.** March 1991 supplies the exploration/exploitation tension from organizational learning; Wu et al. 2019 supplies it from 65 million bibliometric records. They come from different closures and agree on the split, which is why they are kept as two low-overlap witnesses (Fig 8 :56 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone").
3. **The ecological analogue is genuinely independent.** r/K selection was derived in island biogeography, not in management studies. Its match to the fast-initiator versus competitive-developer shape is corroboration from a different substrate, with a round-trip gap that must be stated, not a business result.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Sending the full framework before someone has described what they would count as a failure sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union claim and the three refuters.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the union claim were true before giving them the Damanpour or Wu sources.
- **Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"** and Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Never pool later agreement as corroboration, especially from readers of the same literature.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A venue's prestige is a contact-cost filter. It is not a witness for the shape claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with their own ground and their own refuter.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Send the residual and the refuter. The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded first and never pooled later.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each reader writes down what would refute their result before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if the stage-1 output survives. In this folder the scalar ranking does not survive (F1 and F2 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`); only the union shape does. Send the union shape, not a league table.
