# Expert verification packet: the Tether on startups versus established firms

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in innovation studies or organizational size can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No innovation metric is claimed to settle the comparison. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Are startups more innovative?" is a bundle of at least two conditions, initiation and implementation, that do not collapse into one quantity, so a global ranking aggregates away the signal. Innovative capacity is a property of the firm and its habitat, not of the firm class alone, and the same structural feature (hierarchy, slack, process discipline) has opposite valences for the two conditions. Startups and incumbents are two arrangements of the same organizational atoms, genuine isomers over a shared atom set or at least members of one functional group, with the difference carried by the arrangement and invisible in the word "innovative". No numerical ranking is claimed. What is offered is the shape, three falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close the magnitude.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 1

- **Statement:** the question is a union and the comparison is a refused aggregation, not a scalar verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Also Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." And Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** does the empirical literature behave as a union? Damanpour 1992 reports a stronger size effect for implementation than initiation; Camison-Zornoza et al. 2004 attribute contradictory results to measurement operationalization; Wu et al. 2019 report small teams disrupt and large teams develop.
- **Refuter:** produce one measure that the ground settles for both classes, on the same basis, on which one class dominates across habitats, with a residual that trends down. Then the aggregation was legitimate and C1 fails.

### C2 — organizational-size facing; checkable against the literature

- **Statement:** capability is firm-plus-habitat, and structural features carry opposite valences for initiation and implementation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 4 :19 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
- **What to check:** is the Christensen account habitat-relative (good firms are aware but their environment will not fund the new thing), and does organizational ecology show inertia with age-dependent hazard that reverses when size is controlled? Both should hold if capability is not a class property.
- **Refuter:** show that moving the same people and resources between habitats leaves capability unchanged, or that a structural feature has the same valence for both conditions on one ground. C2 then fails.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with one open type question

- **Statement:** the two forms are isomers over the shared atom set, or at minimum a functional group, and the difference is in the bond.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- **What to check:** can both organizational forms be written over the shared set {search, bet, observation, settled result, budget, habitat, seven bonds}, differing only in arrangement? If as usually written one requires an atom the other lacks, the functional-group reading stands and the isomer claim is withdrawn. This is the one naming question still open; it is recorded, not hidden.
- **Refuter:** show that a patent count or other ingredient-only measure discriminates the two arrangements. By Fig 12 :5 it cannot, but if an instrument did, the metamerism objection would fall.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that startups are more innovative. That ranking is refused as an aggregation.
- Not that incumbents do not innovate; they implement and develop.
- Not a policy of funding one class over the other.
- Not a derivation of one organizational form from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is asserted as settled.
- Not that the ecology analogue is a business result. r/K selection is low-overlap corroboration with a nonzero round-trip gap across substrates.
- Not that agreement among scholars settles anything. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it, not a debate. Here:

1. **Discriminator test:** does the proposed metric separate initiation from implementation at all?
2. **Habitat test:** does relocating the same people and resources between habitats change the output?
3. **Valence test:** does a structural feature carry opposite signs for the two conditions on one ground?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :4, Fig 1 :19, Fig 11 :39, Fig 11 :49, Fig 12 :117. Five short passages.
2. Say whether Damanpour's initiation/implementation split and Wu et al.'s disrupt/develop split are faithful descriptions of those literatures.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate initiation from implementation on one ground, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
