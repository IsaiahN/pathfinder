### Q4 — The renormalization analogue: convention versus ground

- Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
- Carroll 2001: the effective cosmological constant is a bare term plus potential energies plus zero-point energies. Setting the total to the observed value is a choice of where to put the constant, not a prediction of it.
- Koberinski 2021: "renormalized quantities in QFTs aren't taken as predictions of some physical quantity"; the one-loop value is "tuned to give the measured value."
- So the tuning is a **convention that has become an outcome**. By Fig 10 it has stopped being a convention. This is the analogue of a seat authoring a constant and then treating it as a ground reading.
- **Falsifier:** a parameter-free derivation of the observed value would move the tuning from convention to settlement.

### Q5 — The metamerism analogue: a reading is not its source

- From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (reopened 2026-11-23): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved which source produced a green reading.
- Here the reading is a small smooth energy density inferred from expansion. The proposed source is vacuum energy. Several sources could produce the same reading: a true cosmological constant, a slow field, or a modification of gravity (NASA 2025 lists all three). Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error.
- **Falsifier:** an instrument that discriminates among those sources at the relevant scale would close this analogue. NASA's Roman, Euclid and Rubin programs are aimed at parts of it, but none is a direct measurement of ⟨rho⟩.
- Triangulated for the reading-source distinction (essay + Fig 9 :30-31); the instrument claim is single-source (NASA) and unmeasured.

## CONCLUSION

"Connection" is ⋛, a threshold comparison, not ≡. The two frames are not isomers as written, and the stronger isomer claim over a shared atom set is a checkable hypothesis, not a result. The compound is a union that must be unbundled before it is searched. The dependency order is describe, refuse, split, settle, import, then evaluate. Renormalization is a convention that has crossed into outcome-deciding, and the source of the observed reading is not determined by the reading. No new primitive is minted, and no mechanism is named.
