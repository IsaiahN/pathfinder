# The Tether's answer: dark energy and vacuum energy

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a physics result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Quantum field theory returns a **family** of estimates for the energy density of the vacuum, from electroweak and QCD contributions up to a Planck-cutoff estimate about 120 orders of magnitude above the observed value. Cosmology observes a smooth component of order (10^-3 eV)^4 that drives accelerated expansion. The standard move declares them one quantity with a tuned counterterm. The residual is that the claimed identity fails the identity test, the magnitude gap is ordered and not shrinking with effort, and the two sides do not even have the same kind of anchor: D2 has observation, D1 has no instrument returning ⟨rho⟩ itself (Weinberg 1989/2000; Carroll 2001; Koberinski 2021).

## 2. The shape

1. **The relation is comparison, not identity.** Fig 12 :67-69: identity is "two names, one referent" and its test is "substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No." Substitution loses everything, so the identity fails. Fig 12 :87 defines the bond that fits: "a threshold relation between two quantities", ordered, "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."
2. **The question is a union compound, not one question.** Fig 9 :4-5 says to split a disagreement that does not shrink with effort; Fig 9 :54 says to unbundle a union before searching again. The bundle contains at least four questions with different anchors: does a vacuum contribution gravitate, why is the observed value small, is the acceleration that contribution, and is the vacuum number a prediction or a convention.
3. **The shared word is a convention, not corroboration.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Carroll 2001 shows the identification of Lambda with vacuum energy arises from a formal equivalence of stress-energy tensors. That is a shared meaning, which is efficient for a convention and worthless as a verdict.
4. **The counterterm grounds nothing.** Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Tuning the total to the observed value makes the discrepancy statable; it does not settle it.
5. **Per slot, not in aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Each known vacuum contribution (electroweak, QCD, Planck) fails by tens to 120 orders on its own. The problem is structural, per field and per phase transition.
6. **A mechanism is owed and is not in the closure.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." SEARCHED has been extensive; INWARD sharpens the ground reading; OUTWARD would require an import with a source gate and a shadow test. Status: `gap` at the mechanism level, **unreached at this budget**, not unreachable in principle. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The shape is a term that suppresses every vacuum contribution to the observed scale without a hand-set counterterm, survives sharper measurements of w, and does not turn a convention into an outcome. Which term that is, the framework does not say.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that dark energy is definitely *not* vacuum energy. The identity fails **as stated**, under the figure's test; whether a common physical substrate exists is a ground question.
- Not that supersymmetry, sequestering, unimodular gravity, quintessence or anthropic selection is the answer. These are candidate imports with no source gate supplied here.
- Not a new physical theory, not a derivation of one theory from the other, and not a new primitive.
- Not a claim about which of the four sub-questions is correct. They are split, not answered.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation, and no instrument returns a regulator-independent ⟨rho⟩.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

- **Vacuum value test:** an instrument or regulator-free calculation that returns a value of ⟨rho⟩ equal to the observed density. This would let the identity test pass.
- **Substitution test:** show that substituting the field-theory estimate reproduces the observed expansion history and structure formation. It currently predicts no galaxies.
- **Source test:** an observation that discriminates among a true cosmological constant, a dynamic field and a modification of gravity at the relevant scale. NASA's Roman, Euclid and Rubin programs constrain parts of this (NASA 2025), but none measures ⟨rho⟩ directly.
- **Import test:** a candidate import with provenance and a shadow test, settled by the ground as the term that closes R.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** see the pre-registered refuter there. If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say what dark energy is and does not claim that vacuum energy and dark energy are the same thing. It says that the word "connection" is being asked in the wrong bond: identity is ruled out by the figures' own test, because swapping the field-theory estimate for the observed value destroys the universe we see, while an ordered comparison fits and is exactly what the 120-order gap asks about. It says the question is a bundle of four questions with different anchors, so it must be split before it is searched; that tuning a counterterm to the observed value is a convention that has quietly become an outcome; and that each known vacuum contribution fails separately, not just their total. It refuses to name a mechanism. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the observations that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
