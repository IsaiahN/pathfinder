## VERDICT (from the figures)

The connection, if any, is an ordered comparison, not an identity. The identity fails the figure's own substitution test, so Option A is refused as stated. The question is a union compound (Option C) whose four sub-questions must be split before they are searched (Option B), because their anchors differ. The counterterm tuned to observation is a convention that has become an outcome, and each known vacuum contribution fails separately. A mechanism is owed and is not in the closure: `gap` at the mechanism level, unreached at this budget, not unreachable in principle. The framework returns the shape of what would close the residual, never the name. This verdict is `specified` and ungrounded; it does not say what dark energy is, and it does not rule out a common physical substrate to be found by an instrument.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim is mode-labelled, states four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the comparison finding and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A supplied import with provenance and a shadow test, settled by the ground, would replace the `gap` without touching the structural findings. The comparison claim survives even if a mechanism is found, because an ordered gap can close without becoming an identity. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A regulator-independent measurement of the vacuum density equal to the observed value; a demonstration that substituting the QFT estimate reproduces expansion history; an observation discriminating among a true constant, a dynamic field and modified gravity; or a figure re-census that makes identity the correct bond.
