## 0. Residual, in effect terms

Two frames return a quantity with the same name and the same units, an energy density of the vacuum.

- The **quantum field theory frame** returns a family of values from the vacuum expectation value of the stress-energy tensor: electroweak contributions of order (200 GeV)^4, QCD contributions of order (0.3 GeV)^4, Planck-cutoff contributions of order (10^18 GeV)^4 (Carroll 2001, §1.3). The estimate depends on the cutoff and on the regularization scheme.
- The **cosmological frame** returns one observed number from supernovae, the cosmic microwave background and large-scale structure: of order (10^-3 eV)^4. It is named dark energy, is smooth, and is close to constant in time.
- The **standard move** declares them the same quantity with a bare term and a counterterm tuned to the observed value.

The residual R is not "the number is big." It is this: **a claimed identity between two frames fails the identity test, and the magnitude gap is ordered, enormous, and not shrinking with effort.** Fig 9 :4-5 names the shape of the problem: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Options and what each leads to:

| option | claim | leads to |
|---|---|---|
| A | dark energy is identical to QFT vacuum energy | the cosmological constant problem as a wrong calculation; a counterterm tuned by hand; a convention presented as a result |
| B | they are two quantities that can be compared but not substituted | a union compound, split into separate loops, each ranged against the same ground |
| C | the question is a bundle of identity, magnitude, source and convention claims | unbundle before searching; refuse before minting |

The derivation adopts B and C, and refuses A as stated. It does not assert that no common substrate exists. That is a physics question for the ground.

## 1. Domain census

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain. A domain needs actors, a currency and a nameable anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: two frames plus one ground reading. Symmetry: the operative relation is ordered (which is larger), not symmetric; the identity relation would be symmetric but fails its test (F1). Scale: the density gap is ~10^120, or ~10^30 when written as a mass scale to the fourth power (Carroll 2001, eq. 54). This is a **union compound**, not one question: Fig 9 :57-58, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."
- **D1, the QFT vacuum frame.** Actors: scalar, fermion and gauge fields; zero-point modes; electroweak and QCD condensates; regulators and cutoff scales. Currency: the vacuum expectation value of energy density, ⟨rho⟩. Anchor: particle physics measurements of particle masses, couplings and vacuum effects such as the Casimir force, but **no instrument returns a value of ⟨rho⟩ itself**. For the quantity being compared the anchor is not nameable. Re-census required (F4): this is a frame that generates a family of regulator-dependent estimates, not a frame that predicts a measured number.
- **D2, the cosmological frame.** Actors: the cosmological constant Lambda, the metric, the stress-energy tensor, the scale factor a(t), the equation-of-state parameter w. Currency: an energy density that gravitates. Anchor: astronomical observations of expansion and structure. This anchor does not update on theory, so D2 is a genuine domain: Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
- **D3, the relation and verification frame.** Actors: the two frames and an outside checker. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground (observation), never agreement. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero (see `11_audit.md`).
- **X1, "dark energy equals vacuum energy" as a bare assertion.** Actors: none beyond a naming convention. Currency: none. Anchor: none nameable apart from the convention itself. Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." **Not a domain yet.** It is a declared meaning (F3).

**Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The overlap is the shared phrase "energy of empty space," which is a shared vocabulary, not a shared atom (F8). Unbundled, the compound contains at least three questions: (i) does a vacuum contribution gravitate at all, (ii) why is the observed value so small, (iii) is the observed acceleration that contribution rather than a field or a modification of gravity.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle the ground reading in D2 before comparing D1 to it; refuse the identity claim before minting a mechanism (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2: a refusal is owed first, and negation is not a bond); then, and only then, ask what import would be needed (Fig 6, Fig 8).

**Strain test applied.** The 120-order number is often quoted as if it were a single predicted value compared with a single observed value. Two different objects are being conflated: a regulator-dependent family of estimates, and a ground reading. The re-census above separates them before any figure line is chosen. No figure line is strained to make them one.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The identity test fails; the right bond is comparison (decisive for the relation)

Fig 12 :67-69 defines identity:

> **"two names, one referent"**
> **"substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No."**

Apply the test. Substituting the QFT estimate for the observed dark energy density loses everything: the universe would expand so fast that no stars or galaxies form (NASA, *What is Dark Energy?*, 2025; Weinberg 1989/2000). Under the figure's own test the two are **not** one referent under two names.

What bond fits instead? Fig 12 :87 and :89 define comparison:

> **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**
> **"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."**

The question "how does the predicted value compare with the observed value" is exactly a threshold comparison, ordered and about which is larger. So the connection, if any, is ⋛, not ≡. Fig 12 :94 adds: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Two things can share the name "vacuum energy" and differ in what the name is bonded to.

- Mode: `specified`.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 12 (bond table) and Fig 2 (anchor rule), low overlap between a notation rule and a settlement rule.
- Refuter: an instrument that returns a value of ⟨rho⟩ with no regulator choice, equal to the observed dark energy density, would let the identity test pass. So would a demonstration that substituting the QFT value reproduces the observed expansion history. Neither exists in the sources read.

### F2 — The question is a union compound and must be split

Fig 9 :4-5 (quoted in section 0) and Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."** The unbundled list read off the world is: (i) does a vacuum contribution gravitate, (ii) why is it small, (iii) is the acceleration that contribution, (iv) is "vacuum energy" a prediction or a renormalization convention. Each has its own anchor and its own loop. A single lever applied to the bundle clears one condition and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).

- Mode: `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for the split chosen here.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 9 and Fig 8 (union surplus), low overlap between splitting rule and surplus rule.
- Refuter: a demonstration that all four sub-questions share one anchor and one closure, so that one lever clears all of them.

### F3 — The counterterm tuning is a declared meaning, not a ground

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."** Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**

The standard procedure writes the effective cosmological constant as a bare term plus a counterterm chosen so that the total equals the observed value. Carroll 2001 describes this as a bare constant plus the potential and the zero-point contributions, and notes that "there is no obstacle to imagining" a cancellation, "other than the fact that it seems ridiculous." The tuning makes the discrepancy statable; it does not settle it. By Fig 10 it has crossed from convention into outcome-deciding and so has ceased to be a convention.

World corroboration from a genuinely different pool: Koberinski (2021, *Regularizing (away) vacuum energy*) argues that the one-loop vacuum energy density is not a prediction in the effective-field-theory sense, because "renormalized quantities in QFTs aren't taken as predictions of some physical quantity" and the value is "tuned to give the measured value."

- Mode: `specified`.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 10 and Koberinski (philosophy of QFT), low overlap.
- Refuter: a derivation from the Standard Model, with no free counterterm, that yields the observed density. No such derivation appears in the sources read.

### F4 — The QFT value has no anchor: re-census of D1

Fig 2 :29-30 defines the anchor and adds **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."** The estimated ⟨rho⟩ moves when the regulator moves: lattice regularization gives a term proportional to the fourth power of the cutoff, dimensional regularization gives a pole in epsilon, and the two differ in equation of state (Koberinski 2021, §2). No instrument returns ⟨rho⟩. So the quantity being substituted in F1 has no nameable anchor.

This is a **conflict** in WATSON terms. Weinberg, Carroll and NASA treat the 120-order gap as a real physics problem between theory and observation. Koberinski argues the problem "is standardly" mis-stated and that ⟨rho⟩ "is not a physically significant quantity in the standard model." Per the project rule, a conflict returns the derivation to the census and never flips the verdict. Re-census: D1 is a frame that returns a **family of regulator-dependent estimates**, not a single predicted number. F1 is unchanged, because the identity test fails for every member of that family. The honest record is that the comparison is between a family and a ground reading, not between two numbers.

- Mode: `specified`.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 2 and Koberinski; the conflict with the standard framing is recorded, not smoothed.
- Refuter: a regulator-independent measurement or calculation of ⟨rho⟩ that agrees with the observed density.

### F5 — Per-slot residual, not an aggregate

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Carroll 2001 lists the vacuum contributions separately: electroweak ~3 x 10^47 erg/cm^3, QCD ~1.6 x 10^36 erg/cm^3, Planck ~2 x 10^110 erg/cm^3, against an observational bound ~2 x 10^-10 erg/cm^3. **Each slot fails by tens to 120 orders on its own.** Collapsing them into "the vacuum energy prediction" hides that the problem is structural, per field and per phase transition, not one arithmetic mismatch. Weinberg 1989/2000 makes the same point for QCD alone: cancellation would have to be accurate to about 41 decimal places, and to 118 or 120 for the Planck contribution.

- Mode: `general` for the figure principle, `specified` for the physics numbers.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 1 and the physics reviews, low overlap between a per-slot rule and a table of contributions.
- Refuter: a single field whose vacuum contribution already matches the observed density, with all other slots negligible, would remove the per-slot finding.

### F6 — No term in the closure closes R; mechanism is `gap`

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 asks three questions, kept distinct: SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD.

- **SEARCHED.** Has the closure been swept? Yes, extensively: Weinberg 1989/2000 surveys five approaches, Padilla 2015 reviews no-go theorems, scalar cancellations, symmetries, sequestering and large-distance modifications. Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** Fig 7 :13: **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."**
- **INWARD.** Extend the instrument from one already returning something. Observations of w and the expansion history are improving (NASA, *Expand our Knowledge of Dark Energy*, 2025, on Roman, Euclid and Rubin). This sharpens the ground reading. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** INWARD cannot manufacture the missing mechanism.
- **OUTWARD.** Import a frame whose closure predicts the shape: supersymmetry, unimodular gravity, sequestering, anthropic selection. Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Any import owes a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (explains the gap). Padilla's sequestering is recorded as a **candidate import**, not an accepted one.

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. And Fig 8 :40-43 distinguishes underivable in principle from unreachable at this budget. The honest state is **unreached at this budget and with these instruments**, with a `gap` at the level of mechanism. Not `unreachable in principle`, not `no answer`.

- Mode: `general`.
- Status: **single-source** for the figure rule (Fig 6, Fig 8), with world sources corroborating that the searches have indeed been run.
- Refuter: a settled import with provenance and a shadow test that closes R against the ground.

### F7 — The shape of the missing thing, without a name

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** The figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 13 :134 (above) says unreachable is a verdict.

The shape is a term that would have to: (i) make the vacuum contribution to the stress-energy cancel or be sequestered to of order 10^-47 GeV^4 without a hand-set counterterm; (ii) do so across every vacuum slot in F5, not just the largest; (iii) remain settled against the ground as instruments sharpen w; (iv) avoid turning the convention of F3 into the outcome (Fig 10 :47). What it is, the framework does not say. Naming supersymmetry or sequestering as *the* answer would be a name where only a shape is licensed.

- Mode: `general`.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 9 and the canonical figures README, low overlap between figure text and usage rule.
- Refuter: a derivation from the six-term set alone that names the primitive and settles it against the ground. That would move the claim from `gap` to `grounded`.

### F8 — The shared word "vacuum energy" is a convention, not corroboration

Carroll 2001: "This equivalence is the origin of the identification of the cosmological constant with the energy of the vacuum." That is a statement about a shared meaning, which is a convention. Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**. The two frames agree on a word because the word was defined to make them agree. This is efficient for a convention and worthless as a verdict.

Fig 8 :50: **"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."** No such convergent derivation exists here. The two frames have different anchors (D1 has none for ⟨rho⟩, D2 has observation), so their "agreement" is a naming decision, not maximum independence.

- Mode: `general`.
- Status: **triangulated**, Fig 2 and Fig 8, low overlap.
- Refuter: two independent instruments converging on the same value of the vacuum contribution.

### F9 — Energy is a state; the structure in any composition is not named by the figures

Fig 13 :35-39: **"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network. Each answers a question the other two cannot: adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."** Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Dark energy bonded to a cosmological constant term and dark energy bonded to a dynamical field are different molecules, not spellings of one thing (Fig 12 :94, :117). The six-term set itself already removed a candidate seventh member: Fig 13 :43-44, **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."** So the framework does not license adding a new primitive to make vacuum energy and dark energy one.

- Mode: `general`.
- Status: **single-source** for the figure reading; the physics mapping is unmeasured.
- Refuter: a figure re-census that derives a new member from the six, or a measurement that shows a direct state-state bond is required.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An instrument returns a regulator-independent value of ⟨rho⟩ equal to the observed dark energy density. The identity test (F1) would then pass.
2. Substituting the QFT estimate for dark energy reproduces the observed expansion history and structure formation.
3. A candidate import is supplied with provenance and a shadow test, and is settled by the ground as the term that closes R. The `gap` verdict would then be replaced.
4. The four sub-questions in F2 are shown to share one anchor and one closure, so the compound was a single question after all.
5. A figure re-census shows that the correct bond between the two frames is ≡ rather than ⋛, or that negation or subtraction, not comparison, governs the residual.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the retraction log below is updated. Until one is run, no part of the structural verdict may be labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there a fact here?** Not one. Fig 9 :4-5 splits the bundle into four questions.
2. **Are the two identical?** No under Fig 12's test: substitution loses everything. Refuse first (Formula step 2).
3. **What bond governs?** ⋛, a threshold relation between two quantities, ordered (Fig 12 :87, :89).
4. **Does the QFT side have an anchor for ⟨rho⟩?** No. Regulator-dependent, no instrument (Fig 2 :29-30; Koberinski).
5. **Does the ground move?** No. Observations anchor D2 and do not update on either theory (Fig 2 :29).
6. **What closes R?** Nothing in the closure. SEARCHED yes, INWARD sharpens the ground, OUTWARD owes an import with gates. `gap` (Fig 6, Fig 8, Formula step 7).
7. **What is owed?** A shape and a refuter, not a name and not a consensus. The lineage is the proof (Fig 13 :127-129).
