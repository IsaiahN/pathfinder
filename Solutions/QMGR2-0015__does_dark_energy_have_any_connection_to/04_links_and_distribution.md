## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The comparison is between a family and a number, not two numbers.** Koberinski (2021) shows that lattice regularization gives a term proportional to the fourth power of the cutoff, dimensional regularization gives a pole in epsilon, and the two even differ in equation of state. The one-loop finite value is regulator-insensitive only because higher-order sensitivities are hidden by truncation. This is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4 re-censuses D1 as a family of estimates and records a conflict rather than a clean corroboration.
2. **The observational side is sharpening, without yet naming a mechanism.** NASA's program pages make the ground explicit: Roman, Euclid and Rubin will constrain whether dark energy is consistent with a cosmological constant, a dynamic field, or a failure of general relativity on cosmic scales. By Fig 6 :32, "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." INWARD sharpens the ground reading; it does not import the missing atom.
