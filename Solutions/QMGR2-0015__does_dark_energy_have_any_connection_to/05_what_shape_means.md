## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :138-139: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."

From *The Library of Babel* (reopened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of what would have to be true for dark energy and vacuum energy to be connected, stated before anyone can name the mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape is: a term that suppresses every vacuum contribution to the observed scale without a hand-set counterterm, that is settled by observation, and that does not turn a convention into an outcome (Fig 10 :47). A *name* would be "supersymmetry" or "sequestering". The shape is not the name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (reopened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The search is recursive and proximity-driven.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles (reopened): "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay-level version of Fig 9 :4-5 and governs F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as an ordered magnitude gap between a family of estimates and a ground reading, not as "the cosmological constant problem".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (reopened): "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the loop stops earlier, at the limit of what instruments distinguish, and records the lineage.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For vacuum energy, an instrument that returned a regulator-independent ⟨rho⟩ would be such a settlement. None exists in the sources read.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A candidate such as sequestering is licensed only thus, and even then as a candidate until settled.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens the import point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list, so deriving a seventh term to force an identity is not licensed by the framework.

## 4. The metamerism point: a reading is not its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (reopened) gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." The same green reading can come from one green light, from yellow plus blue, from red plus green plus blue, from cyan magenta yellow black, or from a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

Applied here: a small, smooth energy density inferred from expansion is a **reading**. Naming its source as the vacuum expectation value of the stress-energy tensor is naming a source from a projection. The figure is about the source, but the instrument reads the projection. By the essay's own example, the answer stays unreached until a better instrument arrives; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the color case. This is why F1 refuses the identity *as stated* without claiming to know what dark energy is. The right response is a source-discriminating observation, not a better argument.

## 5. Q — What licenses naming the missing term here?

**Answer:** nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling an imported term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

Correction to my own first pass: I initially wrote "the framework cannot say whether dark energy and vacuum energy are the same thing." That overstates the limit. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing. Here the relation between two things already in the library can be tested with Fig 12's bond rules. What the framework cannot do is name the primitive that would close the discrepancy (F7). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 12 + Fig 9).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree that dark energy is vacuum energy is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against the ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The numbers quoted from Weinberg, Carroll and NASA are their measurements and estimates, not outputs of this derivation.
