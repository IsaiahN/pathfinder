## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `f = unknown frequency` is a placeholder, not a shape.
- `retention is habitat-dependent survival of a cohort, ordered in time, across a threshold, indexed per planet` is a shape.
- `f = 0.12` is a name, and the framework cannot name it. The ground must.

The figure text itself refuses the extra step: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"* (Fig 13 :135). Here the missing item is not a primitive but a measurement, and that makes the refusal stronger, not weaker.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* In this question the starting vocabulary is "retain", "lose", "billions of years"; the acquired terms are escape velocity, cumulative XUV, transition zone, occurrence bound, survivorship curve.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is a count over a cohort. The causes are escape processes. Describing the effect first is what keeps the counting question from being swallowed by the mechanism question.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the answer has been *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For a ground question like this one, the analogue is: the shape is complete when it names the instrument that would close it. Our shape names an occurrence-rate survey over a declared sample. That is where we stop.
- Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Spectra already return something. The path to `f` is better spectra and larger declared samples, not a better slogan.

## 3. What licenses a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* An occurrence rate over a declared sample is exactly such a settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A published upper bound can be imported as a constraint on `f`, but its provenance is the paper, its sample and its confidence interval. Without those, the number carries no weight.

This matters because the shoreline literature already supplies candidate numbers: an upper bound of one in ten from 27 bare rocks (Ih et al. 2026), a posteriors-based upper bound that tightens with observing time, and composition-dependent critical instellations (Ji et al. 2025). Those are imports with provenance, not figure outputs. They are recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 4. The specific error this record would commit

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

The error to avoid here is **reading a threshold model as a counting law**. A cosmic shoreline separates airless worlds from worlds with atmospheres in a parameter space; it does not, by itself, report how many planets fall on each side of the line in a real sample. Turning the slope of the shoreline into a retention frequency skips the ground. That is the same shape as the metamerism error in the exemplar: a reading taken where a discriminating instrument is required.

Two concrete ways the error appears:

- Quoting a model occurrence rate as a measured frequency, without the sample and the confidence interval.
- Reading a spectral non-detection as an airless planet. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Kreidberg and Stevenson 2025 list three live interpretations of a flat transmission spectrum: a high cloud deck, a high-mean-molecular-weight atmosphere, or no appreciable atmosphere. The reading does not determine the source.

What is owed is a better instrument and a declared sample, not a name.

## 5. Q: What does "shape" license here?

**Nothing about the frequency.** The framework licenses the constraints in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and nothing more. A particular number is licensed only by a ground measurement over a declared population, or by a provenanced import with its sample and interval attached. Until one of those, any number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the literature commit the forbidden error?** Some of it is careful and some invites it. Kreidberg and Stevenson write *"it is unknown which planets have retained their atmospheres"* and report bounds, which is the correct form. A summary that turns those bounds into "a fraction X retain atmospheres" would commit the error. This solution does not do that.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well known reviewers agreeing with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget for the measurement and a refutation of the shape.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figure text. It is not a test of atmospheric retention physics and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the frequency it reports on.
