## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The shoreline is a threshold with a transition zone, not a sharp law.** Ji et al. 2025 and the 2026 empirical shoreline paper both find a steeper dependence on escape velocity than the classical value and both report that composition, initial volatile inventory and stellar history blur the boundary. That is the figure's ⋛ comparison operating on a distribution, not a blend. Cite it when explaining F4 and when refusing to collapse the curve into a single fraction.
2. **The current observational ground is a set of bounds, not a detection.** Kreidberg and Stevenson 2025 report no definitive rocky-planet atmosphere detection; Ih et al. 2026 show what an observing campaign could bound. This is the anchor that would close the D2 gap, and it is the reason a non-detection must be reported as a bound (Fig 8 :39). Do not present model occurrence estimates as measured frequencies.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before a reader has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the paper.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people likely to agree manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." One careful outside check is worth more than a hundred agreeable replies.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, validation of the output.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuters. The question for the recipient is what they would count as a failure, not whether they like the framework.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the figure link and the two literature links. Their agreement is never pooled as corroboration because it comes after exposure to the source.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, not the conclusion.
