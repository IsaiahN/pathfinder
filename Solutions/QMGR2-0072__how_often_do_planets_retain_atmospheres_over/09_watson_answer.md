## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number does not exist in the figure library. Atmospheric retention over billions of years is unmaintained survival under contact, a property of planet and habitat, a threshold between binding and loss, ordered in time, and indexed per planet. The figures forbid treating a non-detection as an absence. They do not yield a retention frequency, so the D2 verdict is `gap` with an import instruction: measure an occurrence rate over a declared sample and report the bound, the confidence interval and the observing cost with it.
