## Independent domain census

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the effect of a declared legal exclusion on the production of settled new terms. Arity: two conditions with overlap, because the same grant funds the holder and restricts followers. That is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :4-5, :55), not one question with two opposite answers. Symmetry: a grant is one-way; an exclusion is ordered. The two cannot be treated as symmetric operands and subtracted (Fig 12 :125, :130). Scale: first order inside one search; second order at the union of holder and follower closures (Fig 12 :104).
- **D1 Reward / budget.** Actors: inventor, granting office, market. Currency: quasi-rents and action budget. Anchor: a market or adoption outcome that does not update on the holder's report (Fig 2 :29). Question type: does budget change contact?
- **D2 Exclusion / follow-on.** Actors: holder, followers in other closures, licensees. Currency: access to the claimed atom. Anchor: subsequent settled terms that would not exist but for access. Question type: union, because closures overlap at the atom (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45).
- **D3 Measurement / verdict.** Actors: economists and counters. Currency: patent counts, R&D spend, citations. Anchor: ground-level outcomes. Question type: union of overlapping proxies; counts are produced by the system they would grade (Fig 1 :24-27).
- **D4 Disclosure / bond.** Actors: holder, readers, followers. Currency: head start versus recording (Fig 4 :22/:25). Anchor: compose without a license. Question type: isomer over a fixed atom set (Fig 12 :117).

Order by dependency: split the union first (Fig 9 :54); fix the bond before comparing arrangements (Fig 12 :5); settle the first-order term before it serves as an atom (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before selecting frames (Fig 9 :27-28).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5/:54-57 (union), Fig 2 :29 and Fig 11 :40 (anchor and contact), Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :45 (crossing and surplus), Fig 4 :22/:25 and Fig 12 :5/:117 (bond and isomer), Fig 12 :125/:130 (two currencies), Fig 1 :19/:22 (per slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: a grant is a budget, an exclusion prices a crossing, disclosure is the bond, the union is per slot, the net is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete answer a reader expects, "patents help" or "patents hurt", and a single effect size for policy.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing** is dropped when the reader asks for one answer. The average absorbs the live slot (Fig 1 :22).
- **Two currencies** are dropped when the reader adds reward to restriction. The price in money and the price in access are in different units (Fig 12 :125).
- **The bond is dropped** when a patent is treated as a single atom with a fixed valence. Same atoms with different disclosure are different substances (Fig 12 :117), and a patent count cannot see the bond.
- **The non-updating anchor is dropped** when a count or an R&D total is used as the outcome. That is a frame grading itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 from the figure text).
- **The gap itself is dropped** when an unsupported number is supplied to satisfy the question. That is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a patent's valence is relative to the residual, so a per-slot verdict with a named disclosure arrangement is the right output shape. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the error the canonical README warns against. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
