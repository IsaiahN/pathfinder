## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 from the figure text: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0048 the shape is: *a per-slot verdict under a stated disclosure arrangement, with an anchor that does not update and a refuter written in advance.* The filler the question asks for, a single "yes patents help" or "no patents hurt", is not a shape. It is a name for a quantity nobody has measured in the terms the question uses.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, then splitting

*Library of Babel*, opened this session: if you do not know the right terms, use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary"* (carried from exemplar mapping, same essay).

For this question the starting vocabulary is "reward", "restriction", "innovation". The nearest figure terms are "action budget", "reach across closures", "settled term", "bond", "anchor". Each new term narrows the search:

- "innovation" narrows to settled new terms that pay against a ground, not patents minted.
- "reward" narrows to an action budget, not contact.
- "restriction" narrows to a priced or blocked crossing between differing closures.
- "the two together" narrows to a union of overlapping conditions, not an average.

*Library of Babel*, opened this session, gives the decisive move when a question refuses to narrow: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* It then says to search the problems individually and combine them. That is exactly the F1 census in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: four domains, separate verdicts, never averaged.

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". If we had gone looking for "the economics of patents" first, any paper we picked would have seemed to answer the question. We described the residual first, then opened Moser, Sampat and Williams, Hall, and Bloom et al.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, carried from the exemplar mapping: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` sends exactly that: what you would expect to see if the separator were true.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For patents, that means an adoption outcome or a follow-on settled term measured against an anchor that does not update on the holder's own report. A patent count is not such an instrument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The disclosure arrangement is imported from the patent document itself, which is a different frame from the counted patent. That import needs provenance.

*They're Scanning Books*, carried from the exemplar mapping: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also says: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring that "innovation" is the quantity to be maximised makes that choice visible; it does not make it the ground.

### Correction to an overstatement (kept visible)

A first reading of "shape, not a name" would forbid naming "reward" or "exclusion" as the mechanisms. That is too strong. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Reward and exclusion are ordinary terms in the economics frame, already available. Naming them is a binding from a world frame onto terms we hold, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall: Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied. Same narrowing as the exemplar's section 5b.

## 4. Why the global net is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried from the exemplar mapping, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A patent count is that green reading. A patent can be granted because the invention would not have been made without appropriation (the reward path), or because the holder wants to block followers (the exclusion path), or because both, or because the office's examiners were lenient (Sampat and Williams use examiner leniency as an instrument precisely because grant is not randomly assigned). The count cannot comment on the source. Fig 1 from the figure text: "predicates minted, and credibility accrued. They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."

What is owed is a discriminator, not a name for the source. The discriminator in this case is a slot-level reading of follow-on settled terms under contrasting disclosure arrangements, which is why D4 exists.

## 5. Q: What licenses the per-slot separator?

Nothing in the framework returns "patents help" or "patents hurt". The separator is licensed by three structural constraints:

1. Fig 9 :4-5 splits a disagreement that does not shrink with effort into several well-formed questions. *Library of Babel* gives the same instruction for a bundle of problems.
2. Fig 11 :49 and :51 make valence relative to the residual and refuse a standing list of good actors. The patent's valence is therefore a property of the relation, not of the patent.
3. Fig 12 :125/:130 refuse to add the two currencies. So a single net is not an unmeasured number; it is a category error.

The separator is a shape, not a verdict. It does not decide the welfare question. It says what a checkable answer must look like.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

This bears directly on the reward half of the question. Quasi-rents are a budget. They can fund a search. They are not the ground. A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree that patents help or hurt is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried, and let an instrument settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this session: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and the WATSON answer are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of whether patents help or hurt innovation and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The world sources were opened and quoted, but their agreement with the figure shape is corroboration of shape only, and the essays share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures, so that agreement carries no independent witness weight.

## 8. The honest stop

The separator stops where the instruments stop. There is no figure line that names the net effect, no ground reading here that measures a per-slot follow-on term, and no source-discriminating instrument for the legal-exclusion versus habitat-contact boundary in `01` F9. The record says so, records the lineage, and leaves the center open. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (canonical figures README, opened locally).
