## Restating the question as an effect

A patent is a declared legal convention: a time-limited right to exclude. The question asks what that convention does to the production of settled new terms (innovation). Option A says it funds the search (a reward). Option B says it blocks the crossing needed for cumulative follow-on work (an exclusion). Stated in effect terms, the residual is: *what happens to contact with a non-updating ground when a declared exclusion right is introduced into a habitat of searchers whose closures differ?*

An answer would look like a per-slot verdict: for a given technology slot, under a given disclosure arrangement, with a given ground that does not update on the holder's own report, does the exclusion leave the channel to follow-on ground reachable or not? The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 before any conclusion is drawn, per the formula's "Direction" rule.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that patents help or hurt.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for the side of the debate we expect to agree manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A larger audience raises vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29, an anchor is "a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome").
3. **Sending the source before the residual spends independence.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03` are institutional and opt-in or public submission routes.
