Currency: gas fluxes, partial pressures, isotope fractionations. Anchor: rock proxies. Question type: union of overlapping conditions; each proxy constrains a slice.

- **Domain 4, verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Preserved rock and proxy first; then pressure; then composition conditional on pressure; then climate consistency; then synthesis.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the numbers are imports, not figure outputs; the oldest end is `gap`. Any answer must be a cross-kind composition with two unbundled, coupled slots, and the figures name neither a gas nor a pressure. For the Archean the ground reports an anoxic N2-CO2 dominated atmosphere at a few tenths of a bar near 2.7 Ga, with CO2 uncertain across three orders of magnitude and a live dissenting H2-CH4-NH3 scenario. The three paleobarometers give weak triangulation on pressure; the composition consensus is `specified` with a live conflict; the anoxia negation is world-triangulated. Nothing here is `grounded` by this derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three refuters and the lineage before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is a wrong prebiotic pathway built on a degenerate CO2-pressure pair, which is why the Lehmer coupling is named rather than smoothed.
- **undoable:** yes. The numerical layer is detachable from the shape without touching the derivation. A corrected pressure or a fired anoxia negation can be inserted into `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` while Fig 13 :3 and :74 stand. A sent claim can be superseded, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating proxy that breaks the 0.23 bar versus 1 bar CO2 degeneracy would collapse R_T; dated Hadean inclusions would close the `gap`; two independent proxy systems showing O2 at or above 10^-5 present atmospheric level before oxygenic photosynthesis would fire the negation; a derivation of a gas from the six terms would break the shape claim; a demonstration that the three paleobarometers share one evidence pool would drop the pressure verdict from triangulated to single-source. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
