## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it be measured rather than assumed. I ran the trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the proxy readings themselves. A vesicle size distribution at 2.74 Ga; fossil raindrop imprints at 2.7 Ga; N2/36Ar in fluid inclusions at 3.5 to 3.0 Ga; oxidized iron fractions in 2.7 Ga micrometeorites; sulfur isotope mass-independent fractionation.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: "anoxic N2-CO2 atmosphere, a few tenths of a bar, CO2 uncertain." Detail is thrown away, which is what makes the shorthand reusable.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a specific claim such as "total pressure was 0.23 bar and CO2 was above 70 percent by volume."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- The **CO2-pressure coupling**. Lehmer et al. show that >70 percent CO2 means >0.7 bar in a 1 bar atmosphere but >0.16 bar in a 0.23 bar atmosphere. The shorthand "CO2 was high" does not determine the partial pressure.
- The **age coupling**. The shorthand merges 3.5 Ga nitrogen inclusions, 2.74 Ga vesicles and 2.7 Ga raindrops into one period. Fig 1 :19 forbids that aggregation: each is a slot with its own age.
- The **gap at the oldest end**. The shorthand says "early atmosphere"; the concretisation for 4.5 to 4.0 Ga has no measured values at all. Ohmoto and Ferry's numbers are computed from an fO2 assumption, not read from a rock.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an anoxic atmosphere with N2 and CO2 as bulk gases over liquid water, at a pressure well below the modern bar, is consistent with every proxy read. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no source-discriminating ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensation of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
