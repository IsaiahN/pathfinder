## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes officer addresses; those are deliberately not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Use the public meeting and abstract submission routes instead.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room is not an anchor. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
