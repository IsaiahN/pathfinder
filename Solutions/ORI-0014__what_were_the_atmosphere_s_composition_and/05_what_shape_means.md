## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ORI-0014 a **shape** is therefore the form that any answer about the early atmosphere must have, stated before the gas list is known. It says:

- the answer is a cross-kind composition (Fig 13 :3, :74), not a single species;
- composition and pressure are two slots with different anchors, so they are a union to be unbundled (Fig 9 :54);
- the pressure slot sets the partial pressures, so mixing ratio alone is not an answer;
- the anoxia slot is a negation, not an ordinary gas abundance (Operators table :57-58);
- the pre-4.0 Ga window is a `gap` on measured values, not a blank.

`N2-CO2, 0.23 bar` is a name, not a shape. The shape is what survives if that name is withdrawn.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the effect the atmosphere had to serve, then narrow toward proxies that register that effect.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question: an anoxic, thin atmosphere leaves S-MIF in sediments and small vesicles in sea-level basalt; anyone with the rocks can check.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the starting place is the rock record; the finish line is a reading of the same rocks with a narrower vocabulary.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "Contact Is the Distinction Resolver." Contact is with the ground (the proxy), not with other reviews of the proxy.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own gas list. Naming N2 as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. But naming a gas that a rock proxy actually measures is a different act: it is an import with provenance, and it is legitimate when the source, the instrument and the age are recorded.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs this question most directly. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

This is exactly the Lehmer et al. (2020) result: oxidized iron micrometeorites are consistent with a 1 bar atmosphere containing more than 0.7 bar CO2 **and** with a 0.23 bar atmosphere containing more than 0.16 bar CO2. The proxy is a reading of oxidation state, not of pressure and CO2 separately. Naming one source from that reading without an instrument that discriminates sources is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating proxy, not a confident number.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance version: "the projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." And: "the output is only as good as the validator" is stated in *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* as: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Answer to the shape question (with a correction)

**What licenses a particular gas list or pressure for this question?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns the shape. A particular composition or pressure is licensed only by a ground proxy that discriminates among candidate atmospheres, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Correction to my own §3.** In §3 I wrote that naming N2 or CO2 "turns a visible choice into an invisible one." That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Gas species and total pressure are **not** primitives of the six-term set, so naming one from a measured proxy is not inventing a missing primitive. What remains forbidden is deriving a gas from the six terms, or claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" - a gas binding adds none). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the proxy still fails to discriminate sources, so the specific CO2 and pressure pair stays `specified`. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

**Does the world literature commit the error the README forbids?** Not usually. Catling and Zahnle (2020) give each constraint with its age and its basis, and state "because of limited proxy data, major uncertainties remain about the exact levels of atmospheric gases over time." Ohmoto and Ferry (2026) declare their mantle fO2 assumption and show that the conclusion follows from it. Declared priors are auditable priors. A single headline number without its proxy would be the forbidden act.

## 6. Two consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous reviewers agreeing that the early atmosphere was N2-CO2 rich is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to test a proxy, and the rock decides.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and the truly testable output "would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* says the same outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01`, `06`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the composition claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**One more consequence.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "a group is only worth having while its members stay different" and "the group's agreement is never the verdict." Ten reviews of the same Catling and Zahnle table are one frame wearing ten names. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42): here the cost is a wrong prebiotic scenario built on an untested pressure, so seek a different instrument, not a tenth endorsement.
