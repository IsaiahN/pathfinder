# The Tether's answer: early atmosphere composition and pressure

**Mode: `specified` on the shape; the numerical claims are `grounded` by the cited authors, not by this derivation.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

What were the atmosphere's composition and pressure during the window in which prebiotic chemistry could operate and life appeared, roughly late Hadean to early Archean (4.5 to 3.5 Ga)? The residual is the gap between what outgassing and impact scenarios predict and what the rock proxies measure, and it spans roughly three orders of magnitude in CO2 and a wide range in total pressure. It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Composition and pressure have different anchors and must be unbundled before either is searched.

## 2. The shape (from the figures, verbatim)

1. **The answer is a composition, not a species.** Fig 13 :3: "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds." Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." So a gas vector is a legal answer; a single gas name is not, even if it dominates.
2. **The figures do not name the gases or the pressure.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Names are imports from the geochemical frame, with provenance, not derivations from the six-term set.
3. **The ground is the rock record, not model agreement.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
4. **Per-slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Each proxy is a slot; averaging the proxies destroys the signal that says which one is broken.
5. **The pre-4.0 Ga window is a `gap` on measured values.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A shape can still be stated there; a number cannot.
6. **Pressure and composition couple.** The same mixing ratio at 0.23 bar and 1 bar is a different climate. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The round-trip gap R_T is nonzero and is recorded qualitatively.

## 3. The numbers (from the ground, not from the figures)

These are the answers the cited authors give, with their modes and their anchors:

| slot | reported value | anchor | mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total pressure at 2.74 Ga | 0.23 ± 0.23 bar (2σ); about 0.5 bar upper limit to late Archean | vesicles in sea-level basalt (Som et al. 2016) | `grounded` by the authors; triangulated across three instruments |
| Total pressure at 2.7 Ga | <0.52 to 1.1 bar; earlier <2.1 bar | fossil raindrop imprint size (Catling and Zahnle 2020 table) | `grounded` by the authors |
| pN2 at 3.5 to 3.0 Ga | <1.1 bar, possibly as low as 0.5 bar | N2/36Ar in fluid inclusions (Marty et al. 2013) | `grounded` by the authors |
| Bulk composition | N2 and CO2 dominant; minor H2, CO, CH4, H2S, SO2; anoxic | outgassing models plus rock proxies (Kasting 2013; Catling and Zahnle 2020) | `specified` consensus, `grounded` for anoxia |
| O2 before oxygenic life | <10^-6 present atmospheric level (>2.4 Ga) | S-MIF and redox proxies (Catling and Zahnle 2020) | `grounded` by the authors; a negation |
| CO2 | 0.003 to 0.75 bar across paleosols; >70 percent by volume from micrometeorites | paleosols and micrometeorite oxidation (Catling and Zahnle 2020; Lehmer et al. 2020) | `specified`, span of three orders of magnitude |
| Pre-4.0 Ga window | no measured composition or pressure | no preserved rocks | `gap` |
| Dissenting scenario | H2-CH4-NH3 rich, CO2 poor | mantle fO2 thermodynamic model (Ohmoto and Ferry 2026) | `specified`, single-source, in conflict with the consensus |

**Best short answer for the Archean:** an anoxic atmosphere dominated by N2 and CO2, with minor reducing gases and methane after methanogens evolved, at a total surface pressure well below the modern bar, plausibly around a few tenths of a bar near 2.7 Ga. Best short answer for the Hadean: a shape plus a `gap`, with a live thermodynamic scenario that is not ground-settled.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures derive N2, CO2, methane or 0.23 bar. They do not.
- Not a single number for "the relevant period." The period spans hundreds of millions of years and the proxies have different ages.
- Not that the N2-CO2 consensus is settled and Ohmoto and Ferry are wrong. Different anchors, live conflict, sent back to the census (Fig 2 :35).
- Not that the absence of Hadean rocks proves there was no atmosphere (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulation.
- Not that a published number is `grounded` because it is published. `grounded` means the ground paid for the claim; here the authors paid, and the proxy is the anchor.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. The analogues here are:

1. A source-discriminating proxy for pressure and CO2 that is not degenerate across the 0.23 bar and 1 bar cases. The micrometeorite oxidation reading currently admits both.
2. More preserved early Archean or Hadean material with datable fluid inclusions or vesicles. This would close the `gap` at the oldest end.
3. An independent redox proxy in a different rock system that confirms or fires the anoxia negation (R2 in `01`).
4. A photochemical or thermodynamic model that predicts the shape of a proxy reading before measuring it, so a convergent derivation can be distinguished from an adopted import (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached at the oldest end, not unreachable in principle.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the rock record stops and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (README). The live conflict and the nonzero R_T are part of the result, not stains on it.

## 7. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not tell you what the early atmosphere was made of, and it does not pick a pressure. It says that any answer must be a composition across kinds rather than a single gas, that composition and pressure are two slots with different anchors and must be unbundled, that the rock record and not model agreement settles each slot, and that the pre-4.0 Ga window is a `gap` on measured values rather than a silence. The ground supplies the names: for the Archean, an anoxic atmosphere dominated by N2 and CO2 with minor reducing gases, at a total pressure well below the modern bar and plausibly around a few tenths of a bar near 2.7 Ga, with CO2 uncertain across three orders of magnitude and a live dissenting scenario that puts H2, CH4 and NH3 in charge. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate must have, the refuters written in advance, and the record of which anchors are still missing. The oldest end stays open until the ground speaks.
