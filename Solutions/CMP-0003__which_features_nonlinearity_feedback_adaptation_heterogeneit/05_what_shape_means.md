## 1. A shape is a narrowed constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a shape is therefore: **the form any answer about necessary features must take, stated before you can name a single empirical feature that always does the work.** The shape here is the four-way classification of the candidates (primitive member, derivative of composition, the loop itself, frame-relative relation) plus the dependency order among those classes. It is not "feedback is the necessary feature."

Concretely: `the five candidates are not one kind of thing` is a shape. `network structure is the one necessary feature` is a name, and the framework does not return names.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the target, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."* Applied here: start with the words the question already gives (nonlinearity, feedback, adaptation, heterogeneity, network), then let the figure lines replace them with structural categories.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe "what a feature does", not "what a feature is".
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The residual was described in §1 of `01` before any world source was opened. That order is deliberate.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: a failed search may mean *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the direct essay backing for F1 in the audit. The five-feature question is such a bundle.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that would be a counterfactual removal experiment across domains with a non-moving anchor. None exists in the material read.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8).

The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are frame reports about feature salience. They can corroborate that no universal list has been found. They cannot import a new primitive into the figure set, because they do not pass a source gate against the question "which feature is necessary in all complex systems". Their agreement with the shape is corroboration, not a name.

## 4. Why a feature list is the specific forbidden error here

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*

A feature list records ingredients and not arrangement. Fig 12's isomer case (Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*) is the exact reason: two systems can share every named feature and differ in behavior because the bond differs. So a list cannot be a verdict even in principle. It is a filter for which system to study first.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 to §4

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any of the five candidates at all. That overstated it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Here, network is already a held member of the six (Fig 13 :24), and feedback is already a held bond (Fig 12 :121). Naming them as *figure categories* is a binding onto held structure, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is naming an *empirical* winner, because that needs the ground. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that "feedback is key" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim, and a refuter.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of which features are necessary and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
