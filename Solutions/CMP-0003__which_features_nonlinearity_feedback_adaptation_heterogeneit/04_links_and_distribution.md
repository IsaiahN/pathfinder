## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (joined across the two wrapped lines). Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essay.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The public venues in `03` are places to try the claim, not places to collect endorsements.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. No theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious after recording a verdict, offer the three source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A method may cross; an answer may not (Fig 4 :22).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As written in `01`, it is a shape claim with named strains, not an empirical necessity verdict. Keep that wording. Do not upgrade it to "the answer" in a cover note.
