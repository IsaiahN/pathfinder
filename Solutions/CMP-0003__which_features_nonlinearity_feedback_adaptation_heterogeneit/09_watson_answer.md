## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical necessity verdict does not exist in this material. The five candidates are not one kind of thing: network structure is the only primitive member; nonlinearity as an amplifying remainder is a derivative; feedback is the loop; adaptation is an operation of that loop; heterogeneity is a residual-relative relation. They sit in a dependency order, not an importance ranking. The framework returns this shape, not a name, and the question of which feature is necessary in all complex systems is a `gap` awaiting a ground. This is `general` / `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, names two strains and records four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an empirical-sounding feature list instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The structural classification is removable without touching the loop derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already strip any empirical winner. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A counterfactual removal experiment establishing one feature as necessary and sufficient across domains would break the union verdict; a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a seventh term, would break the substrate; a demonstration that the candidates are independent conditions would break F1; a lower-overlap frame with a sourced import would move the wall.
