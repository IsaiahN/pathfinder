## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 13 :46-47 (directedness), Fig 10 :24-28 (channel), Fig 11 :43-53 (habitat and cascade), Fig 2 :31 and :42 (independence and cost).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`, that an early-warning set is per-slot, directed, anchored, thresholded, channel-maintained, habitat-enumerated, independent, and instrumented at the objective link.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete monitoring proposal, for example "monitor lag-1 autocorrelation of subpolar gyre SST, compare to a 95 percent confidence boundary, and act when it crosses."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **status of the directed statistic**. Fig 13 licenses directedness; it does not license variance or autocorrelation by name. The concrete proposal imports those from Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen 2023, with a source gate and a shadow test. The shorthand hides that import.
- the **confidence condition**. Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen warn that an EWS trend within a limited window can be a random fluctuation. The shorthand *"compare to a boundary"* silently assumes a statistical confidence model that the figures do not supply.
- the **selection-bias condition**. Boettiger and Hastings show elevated false positives when systems are selected after observing their transition. The shorthand *"act when it crosses"* has no term for how the system set was chosen, which is a D4 convention the monitor must author and declare.

One thing survives cleanly: a global mean can hide a live slot signal, and the channel can fail silently while internal instruments report green. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the full figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap.
