## The question restated as an effect

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s. What is being decided is not *which number to publish* but *which observable readings o′, sampled at what resolution and compared to what boundary, make the model-to-ground gap visible while an action a can still change the outcome*.

- Option A, "monitor one global index": leads to the prediction that a mean over the whole system will surface a change in time. Fig 1 :22 says the opposite: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- Option B, "indicators are per-slot, directed, anchored, thresholded, channel-maintained, and enumerated from the contact habitat": leads to the prediction that early detection is a **union** of conditions with its own loop and its own ground, not one metric. Verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **shape** with eight constraints, per-slot indexing, a directed component, a ground anchor, a decision threshold with lead time, a reachable channel, habitat coverage including cascades, independence across frames, and an instrument on the broken middle link. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it names the final indicator list, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of earth-system monitoring.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
