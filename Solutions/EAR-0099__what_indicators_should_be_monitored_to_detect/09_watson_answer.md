## QUESTION

What indicators should be monitored to detect changes early enough for practical action? Restated as an effect: which observable readings o′, sampled at what resolution and compared to what boundary, make R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| visible per slot while an action a can still change the ground outcome? Option A, one global index: leads to a live slot signal hidden in the mean and a channel that can fail silently. Option B, a union of eight conditions: leads to a checkable shape, six written refuters, and an open list until a ground pays. Verdict: B, kept per domain and never averaged.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale. Arity: multi-place and indexed by component, region and time; prediction versus observation plus a comparison to a decision boundary. Symmetry: broken, change has direction, time is ordered, and "early enough" is one-way. Scale: local readings to global aggregates, fast transients to slow trends, lossy between scales (Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point.").

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives with eight conjunctive conditions: per-slot, directed, anchored, thresholded, channel-instrumented, habitat-enumerated to the cascade, independent in evidence, and with a measured instrument on the objective/vocabulary link. They are never averaged: a system can satisfy seven and fail on the channel, in which case it reports green while failing. The specific indicator names and thresholds are a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. The variance/autocorrelation binding is `specified`, ungrounded, and provenance-labelled. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The single most actionable figure-grounded point is the silent channel: a monitoring system can lose touch while every internal instrument reports green.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected adaptation. A programme that fails P5 will buy more sensors and still not know when the channel died; one that fails P6 will monitor the sprayed field and miss the top predator; one that fails P4 will collect readings nobody can act on. The cost is bounded and locatable because each condition has a named failure mode and a written refuter, but in a high-cost tipping case it is not trivially reversible. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes for the shape. A refutation removes a condition or demotes a binding; nothing has to be deleted from the record. Fig 6 :42 states that the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted."* A published indicator list can be superseded. A missed action window cannot.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A held-out case in which acting at the warning time changes the outcome relative to baseline settles the shape. Any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 removes a condition. A source-discriminating instrument that separates critical slowing down from an observation-density artifact would name a slot. A figure re-census that directly governs a specific observable would move the `gap`. Prestige or number of endorsements is not such an instrument.
