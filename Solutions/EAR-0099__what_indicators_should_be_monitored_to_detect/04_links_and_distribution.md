## What the wider literature bears on the question

The world sources are corroboration only. They never overrule a figure line.

1. **Generic signals exist, across fields.** Scheffer et al. 2009 establishes that early-warning signals are generic across ecosystems, finance and climate. Predicts that one shape can transfer across domains; corroborates the figure-level claim that the required properties are structural (Fig 1, Fig 13).
2. **The directed quantities are specific, and their confidence must be established.** Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen 2023 names variance and lag-1 autocorrelation and warns that *"a trend in the EWSs within a limited period of observation could be a random fluctuation within steady-state statistics."* Predicts that a rise in a statistic is not yet a warning; corroborates F2, F5 and the channel requirement F3.
3. **Selection bias manufactures false alarms.** Boettiger and Hastings 2012 *conflicts* with a naive reading of the generic-signal claim. After re-census the conflict sits in the decision/evaluation domain (D4), not in the observation domain (D1), so it corroborates unauthored pools and replicate designs rather than flipping the verdict (F8).
4. **Interdependence is about processes, independence about observers.** The Stockholm Resilience Centre says boundaries cannot be considered in isolation and that early warning signs are emerging. Predicts a union, not a list; corroborates the union reading in `06_isomers_and_order.md` and Fig 9 :55.
5. **Low-overlap field, human neurology.** Litt et al. 2001 found quantitative EEG changes hours before seizure onset in five patients. Predicts that a transition can have a long pre-clinical window measurable by directed statistics; corroborates the shape (lead time plus directed component) without sharing the earth-system evidence pool.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a full indicator list before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (with leading clause *"disqualifying for a verdict:"*). A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Tipping-point errors are high cost, so independent checking venues are worth the extra contact cost.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the eight-condition shape and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Do not mention the framework's interiors.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three verified links (verbatim figure text, canonical usage rule, Scheffer 2009 or Ditlevsen 2023 as the field entry point). Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Formula, "Direction"), and a method's own output is never a measure of success (Fig 5 :48 *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."*).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading. Do not present it as settled.
