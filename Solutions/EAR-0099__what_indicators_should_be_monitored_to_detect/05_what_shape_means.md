## 1. A shape is a constraining description, not a filled list

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and at :135 it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the method: use *"the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

So for this question a **shape** is the set of constraints that any usable early-warning indicator set must satisfy, statable before the specific indicators and thresholds are named. The eight conditions P1 to P8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are a shape. A finished list of named variables with numeric thresholds is a name.

| shape (this answer) | name (not supplied) |
|---|---|
| indexed per slot, never aggregated | which exact components, at what resolution |
| has a directed component that can be spent | which statistic: variance, autocorrelation, skewness |
| ranged against a ground measurement | which instrument is the anchor for this variable |
| comparison to a decision boundary with lead time | what the number for that boundary is |
| channel instrumented for staleness, drops, saturation, pooling | which sensor and what limit |
| habitat enumerated outward to the cascade | which actors and relations are in this cascade |
| independent frames, not repeated versions | which combination of satellite, in situ, archive, local knowledge |
| instrument on the broken middle link | who owns the threshold decision |

The right-hand column is a `gap` at figure level (F9). It belongs to the world frame, with provenance.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a bootstrapping convergence process."* For EAR-0099 the bootstrapping terms came from the figures (residual, slot, anchor, gradient, comparison, channel, habitat, instrument) and were then ranged against the question.
- Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** The residual was described in effect terms in `01` section 0 before Scheffer, Ditlevsen, the Stockholm Resilience Centre or Boettiger and Hastings were read. Otherwise any of them would have seemed to fit.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The outreach email in `02` describes the effect (a live slot hidden by a mean), not the framework's cause.
- Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** A shape is only useful if the constraints in it can be checked. Each of P1 to P8 has a refuter.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For an indicator, that means the warning pays in a held-out case where action taken at the warning time changes the outcome.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen's SST fingerprint for AMOC strength is an import of this kind: it is a description of what to measure, arrived at in a different frame, with its own record. It does not become a figure primitive.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the difference. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can produce one reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The instrument that resolved the case was a spectrometer, *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* For indicators, a rising variance reading does not by itself name the mechanism approaching. It may be critical slowing down, a change in observation density, or a trend artifact. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 4. Why "more indicators" is not the same as "better shape"

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Two people reading one report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. A panel of ten correlated retrievals is one witness wearing ten names. Fig 13 :35-39 applies the same test to the six substrate terms: if two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information and one would be redundant.

This is a shape constraint, not a count. The world source on planetary boundaries adds a separate fact: the processes are interdependent, so they cannot be considered in isolation. Interdependence of the measured system and independence of the measuring frames are different properties on different objects. Confusing them is a category error recorded in `01` F5 and its retraction log.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. In every stable system, the two are separated. The thing that earns trust is not the same as the thing that gets to act."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Also: *"If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."*

For this folder: a well-known author or a large monitoring institution gives an indicator set a budget to be tried. It does not make the set early, independent or correct. The outreach material in `02` therefore asks for a refutation, never an endorsement, and records the verdict before any theory is shown.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of whether any indicator actually detects change early. They must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on. From the same essay: *"An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

## 7. Where the shape stops and the world begins

The honest boundary is this: the figures constrain the *form* of a monitoring answer, the world literature supplies *candidates* whose fit must be paid for at the ground, and no agreement between them is a second witness when they share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40). The shape is general. The bindings are `specified`. The final list is a `gap`. Nothing here is `grounded`.
