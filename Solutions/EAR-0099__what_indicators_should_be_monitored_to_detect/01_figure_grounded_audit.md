## 0. Residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Symbols table: `R` is the residual, `s` is one object, region or entity being tracked).

- **Γ** is the model or forecast the monitoring system holds.
- **b** is the current belief about earth-system state, built from past readings and inherited priors.
- **a** is the monitoring choice: which quantities to sample, at what resolution, against what boundary.
- **o′** is what the earth system actually returns: the indicator readings.
- **s** is a component or region (atmosphere, ocean, cryosphere, land, biosphere, or a subregion).

The question asks: which o′ make R become visible at a time t such that an action a taken at t can still change the ground outcome. "Early enough for practical action" is not a property of an indicator alone. It is a **comparison** (Fig 12 :85-87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*) between a detected trend and a boundary tied to a decision goal (Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*).

**The sought-for shape, bound before the run** (Formula, "Direction"): an indicator set has eight conjunctive properties, P1 to P8 below. Until these are written down and bound as an ordinary term, they are a preference, not a term, and no check inside the loop can see them.

**Options and what each leads to:**

| option | leads to |
|---|---|
| A: one global index suffices | a live slot signal disappears into the mean (Fig 1 :22); the channel can saturate silently (Fig 10 :25-26) |
| B: per-slot, directed, anchored, thresholded, channel-maintained, habitat-enumerated, independent, with an instrument on the broken link | a checkable union with separate loops and a stated cost if wrong |

Verdict: B, `specified`. The specific names of indicators and thresholds remain a `gap` (F9).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: multi-place and indexed by slot (component, region, time); the relation is prediction versus observation plus a comparison to a decision boundary, so it is not a one-place quantity. Symmetry: broken. Change has direction, time is ordered, and "early enough" is a one-way threshold, not a symmetric join. Scale: local readings to global aggregates, fast transients to slow trends, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor.** A domain needs a nameable anchor; without one it is not yet a domain.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Observation channel | sensors, proxies, satellites, in situ networks, archives, local knowledge | readings, contact with the system | the measured earth system (Fig 2 :29 *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*) | admitted |
| D2 Model / forecast frame | models, Γ, priors, forecasts | prediction, residual R | the same ground, reached through a different closure | admitted |
| D3 Earth-system habitat | atmosphere, ocean, ice, land, ecosystems, human land use; conditions and relations | contact and cascades | the ground: what survives is what the ground kept (Fig 11 :14-15) | admitted |
| D4 Decision / action frame | decision makers, thresholds, lead times, goals | comparisons, cost of being wrong | consequences of action (an outcome that pays or does not) | admitted |
| X1 "the single best indicator" | one metric | convenience | none nameable that is not already D1 plus D4 | not a domain; absorbed into the D1/D4 compound |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Early detection requires P1 AND P2 AND P3 AND P4 AND P5 AND P6 AND P7 AND P8. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Unbundle; each condition has its own loop and its own ground.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** perception (D1: can we read it at all) → vocabulary (D3: what is in contact with the residual) → objective (D4: what would count as progress, a threshold) → planning (action under incomplete information) → learn and carry. The middle link, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, is where chains break because it has the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :25-27).

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - An indicator must be indexed per slot; averaging is how the signal dies (P1)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 1 :20: *"a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert"* one.

A global mean over an earth system can be near zero while one component or region carries the live signal. Support is satisfied when some slot carries positive mass; a global summary can hide that entirely. The per-slot requirement is therefore not a preference for detail, it is the condition under which the signal exists at all.

- **Mode:** `general` (structural) for the per-slot rule, `specified` for the earth-system application.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 1 :19-22 and Fig 13 :35-39, where six distinct members overlapping would be fewer members wearing six names, low overlap between a control-loop figure and a substrate figure).
- **Refuter:** a global aggregate that gives equal or better warning, at equal false-alarm cost, across a held-out set of regional transitions. If that holds, P1 falls.

### F2 - The level is a spectrum; early warning needs directedness (P2)

Fig 13 :20: **"ordering, so more and less can be said"** (spectrum).
Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"** (gradient).
Fig 13 :46-47: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."**
Fig 13 :75-76: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

A reading that only orders (this value is higher than last) tells you where things stand. It does not tell you which way the system is moving or how much headroom remains before action is no longer possible. A directed component (a trend, a rate, a change in variance or autocorrelation) is what makes a reading spendable as a warning. The figures distinguish a gradient over space from one over time, so a spatial pattern and a time trend are different terms and both may be needed.

**Important binding caveat:** the world literature calls the directed quantities *variance*, *lag-1 autocorrelation* and *critical slowing down* (Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen 2023: *"Predictions based on observations rely on detecting early-warning signals, primarily an increase in variance (loss of resilience) and increased autocorrelation (critical slowing down)"*). Calling those the figure's Gradient is a **binding from the world frame onto a held structural atom**, not a derivation from the six terms. The figures license "a directed component is required"; they do not name variance or autocorrelation. Mode `specified`, ungrounded for the binding.

- **Status:** `triangulated` for the requirement (Fig 13 plus the Operators table comparison rule plus an independent world field); `single-source` for the specific variance/autocorrelation binding.
- **Refuter:** a level-only indicator with no directed component and no comparison to a boundary that yields equal lead time in held-out cases. If it does, the gradient requirement fails.

### F3 - An indicator is only as good as its channel to the ground (P5)

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :25-26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is"* / **"you have lost touch"**
Seat Map :285: *"Every instrument points inward unless one is built to point out. Wiring, consumption and behaviour are all read from inside; whether the record that was supposed to reach the outside arrived is read by nothing. That channel can fail silently for weeks with every internal instrument reporting green."*

The named failure modes are exactly monitoring failure modes: a sensor goes stale, a transient is dropped between samples, a metric saturates at its range limit, two populations are pooled after a change. In all four the internal instruments stay green. The system has lost touch and reports a verdict instead. Keeping the channel reachable is therefore part of the indicator design, not an operations afterthought. It requires an instrument that points outward, which is the Ground Maintainer's office (Fig 10 :29 *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*).

- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 10 and Seat Map, two different texts with distinct roles).
- **Refuter:** an indicator set whose channel diagnostics show no staleness, no dropped transients, no saturation and no pooling, yet which fails to warn while the underlying change crosses a threshold. If that is common, P5 is not load-bearing.

### F4 - Enumerate the habitat and the cascades; no standing list survives a residual change (P6)

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
Fig 11 :52-53: **"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**
Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

The figure's own example is an earth-system early-warning failure: DDT biomagnified through the food chain to eggshell thinning in eagles, three steps out from the direct target. An indicator panel scoped to direct contact misses the residual that mattered. The list is read off the world, not invented, and a fixed list is invalidated when the residual changes. This is also why a "core indicator set" cannot be declared once and left standing.

- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 11 and Seat Map :215-221, which repeats enumeration, valence and cascades as seat rules; also corroborated by Earth System Dynamics 2026 on indirect effects and aggregation loss).
- **Refuter:** an indicator panel assembled without enumerating second or third order cascades that still detects a cascading change in time across held-out cases. If that holds, P6 falls.

### F5 - Independence between measuring frames, not more names of one frame (P7)

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**
Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**
Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Two instruments pointed at the same thing are two frames; two people reading the same report are one. For earth-system indicators, satellite retrieval, in situ measurement, historical archives and local knowledge are low-overlap frames only if they truly draw on different evidence. Ten versions of one retrieval are not ten witnesses. Because tipping points are high-cost errors, the cost of being wrong justify buying independence. A null result from one frame is not proof that no warning exists.

The Stockholm Resilience Centre page corroborates the structural point from inside the field: *"Planetary boundaries are interdependent. This means we cannot consider planetary boundaries in isolation in any decision-making on sustainability."* Interdependence of processes is a fact about the habitat; independence of measuring frames is a separate requirement about the observers. They must not be confused.

- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 8, distinct arguments for independence; world source is institutional corroboration only).
- **Refuter:** two indicators that draw on the same underlying measurement and are treated as independent corroboration where the verdict depends on that independence. If the dependence cannot be removed, P7 is violated.

### F6 - Seek a missing indicator in this order: SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD

Formula step 7 asks three distinct questions when no term in the closure closes R. Asked first, cheapest, and assumed by the other two: **SEARCHED**, is it in the panel and unfound? Then **INWARD**: Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34 *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Then **OUTWARD**: import from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test, described in effect terms first (Fig 9 :27-28 **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**).

For this question, a new indicator is not minted from a verbal specification. It is found inside the existing panel, or it is a sharpening of a proxy already returning something (an SST fingerprint for AMOC strength is a real example from Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen 2023), or it is imported from a different discipline with provenance. Out of reach at this budget (no proto-instrument anywhere) and underivable in principle are different facts with different instructions; importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

- **Status:** `general` (method), `specified` for the indicator application. Triangulated with Fig 8 and the canonical usage rule.
- **Refuter:** an indicator designed from a pure description with no proto-instrument already returning something, which then works. If such cases are the norm, the INWARD constraint as stated is wrong.

### F7 - The middle link has no instrument (P8)

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
Fig 3 :29-30: **"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."**
Fig 3 :25-26: the link between having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress *"has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."*

Monitoring programmes are good at perception (sensors exist and are counted) and at planning (response protocols exist). The link that is least instrumented is the vocabulary/objective link: does the system have a term for what the reading affords, and can it say what would count as progress? An indicator without a decision threshold is a reading, not an early warning. Measuring more sensors cannot rescue a missing threshold.

- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 3 plus the Operators table goal-is-a-comparison rule, distinct figures).
- **Refuter:** an indicator with no stated decision threshold that nevertheless changes action in time across documented cases. If so, the threshold requirement is not necessary.

### F8 - False alarms are a cost to price, not a reason to abandon indicators

Boettiger and Hastings 2012 (*Proceedings of the Royal Society B*, read 2026-11-22) report, from simulated systems that crossed a threshold by chance, *"an elevated rate of false-positives in common warning signal statistics."* They attribute it to *"conditionally selecting systems known to experience a transition of some sort and failing to account for the bias this introduces--a statistical error often known as the prosecutor's fallacy."*

This **conflicts** with a naive reading of F2 and F5. The conflict was returned to the census, not used to flip the verdict. Re-census places it in D4 (how indicators are selected and evaluated), not in D1 or D3. It then corroborates two existing requirements: the pool of systems must be unauthored first and examined second (Seat Map :213), and replicates with independent experience avoid the pitfall entirely. The figures already price this: Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Boettiger and Hastings add that the cost includes false alarms from selection bias; they do not refute the shape.

- **Status:** `single-source` for the specific bias result (one study, ecology/statistics); corroborates after re-census.
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that false-alarm rates are independent of how the system pool is selected and of frame independence. If so, this finding does not bear.

### F9 - Naming the indicators is a `gap`

After a genuine re-census across all figure lines, no line directly names an earth-system observable or a numeric threshold. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The figures return the shape any useful indicator set must have. They cannot return the list. The world literature supplies candidates (variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness, spatial patterns, SST fingerprints for the AMOC, nine planetary-boundary variables), but each is a binding from a world frame with its own provenance and its own ground, not a figure derivation. Declaring a final list would be a name where only a shape is licensed.

- **Status:** `gap`, plainly stated. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.
- **Refuter for the gap:** any figure line found that directly governs a specific observable and threshold. None was found after the census in section 2.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A global aggregate gives equal or better warning than per-slot indicators at equal false-alarm cost (breaks F1).
2. A level-only indicator with no directed component and no decision boundary gives equal lead time (breaks F2 and F7).
3. Channel diagnostics stay green while warnings fail systematically across regimes (breaks F3).
4. A fixed panel without cascade enumeration detects third-order effects reliably (breaks F4).
5. Frames sharing one evidence source are shown to add independent corroboration weight (breaks F5).
6. A missing indicator is routinely designed successfully from a pure description with no proto-instrument (breaks F6).

If any fires, that condition is removed from the shape and the relevant claim is retracted. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and its refuter, not with a list of indicators (Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
2. State the mode on every claim (F10). Never present a binding as a derivation.
3. Instrument the channel itself: staleness, dropped transients, saturation, pooling (F3). This is the cheapest high-value addition.
4. Enumerate the habitat outward to the cascade, and re-enumerate when the residual changes (F4).
5. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, and price false alarms from selection bias (F5, F8).
6. Send the generator, not a recording: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* (Fig 4 :25).

### Strain check after re-census

One live strain is named, not hidden: the world frame's "loss of resilience" (rising variance) is a statistical equilibrium concept that assumes quasi-stationary dynamics, while the figures' gradient is a substrate structure. They rhyme at the level of directedness but are not identical objects. The binding remains `specified`, ungrounded. No figure line was found to strain against another after re-census.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: per-slot, directed, anchored, thresholded, channel-maintained, habitat-enumerated, independent, with an instrument on the broken middle link. The specific list and thresholds do not: they are bindings from world frames and a declared `gap` at figure level. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single point for practical use is F3: the channel fails silently while internal instruments report green, and that failure is instrumentable.
