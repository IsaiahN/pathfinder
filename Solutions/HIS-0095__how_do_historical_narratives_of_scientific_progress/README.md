## The question restated as an effect

The question asks what a historical narrative *does*. In effect terms: a community holds expectations about research and innovation (which problems are tractable, how fast results should arrive, what counts as progress, which risks are acceptable, where money goes) that it did not read off the present ground directly. Those expectations were generated by a frame that inherited a picture of how science advanced. The residual is the gap, per research domain, between the expectation a community holds and what the present ground would return if it were ranged against the expectation without the story in the loop.

Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, treat the narrative as a transparent report of how science works.** Then an expectation is taken to be a direct reading of the world, the story is invisible, and no instrument is owed. This is the frame-internal error Fig 1 :24-27 names: credibility accrued is a quantity the frame produces and cannot score itself with.
- **Option B, treat the narrative as a frame with a closure, a currency, and a bond-typed account of progress.** Then the expectation is a prediction of that frame, its reach is bounded by what the story holds, and the ground must be ranged against it separately per slot. This is the reading derived here.

What an answer would look like was written down and bound as an ordinary term before the loops were run, per the formula's "Direction" rule: a **separator** that says which conditions must hold for a narrative to shape an expectation, which condition turns agreement into a mirror rather than a verdict, and which quantity no figure line returns. The sought-for shape was thereby made refusable.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim to name the narrative that dominates in any particular institution, and no numerical effect size. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32; canonical figures README), and this is recorded as a `gap`, not filled with a guess.

Figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

Legally: publishing personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That practical failure sits on top of the structural one.
