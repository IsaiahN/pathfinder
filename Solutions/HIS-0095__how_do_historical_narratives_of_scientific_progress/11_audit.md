Arity: three conditions in contact, a frame that holds the story, a decision that forms the expectation, and a ground that should settle it. Symmetry: ordered, story then expectation, so `→` or `⇒`, not a symmetric `+`. Union, not partition. A lever on one condition leaves the intersections standing. Order by dependency: register the residual per slot; supply vocabulary and bonds; state the objective; carry the story; pick a differing room; range against the ground. The break is at vocabulary to objective.

Two things survive cleanly: a story bounds the reach of expectations by the atoms and bonds it holds, and a reference that updates on the story turns triangulation into coherent drift.
