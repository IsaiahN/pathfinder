# The Tether's answer: how historical narratives shape present expectations

**Mode: `specified` on the four-condition separator and on each domain verdict, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the dominant narrative and the size of the effect, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

A community's expectation about research and innovation, what is tractable, how fast the return should come, what counts as progress, which risks are acceptable, is not read directly off the evidence. It is generated by a frame that inherited a story about how science advanced. The residual is the per-slot gap between that expectation and what the present ground would return if the story were not mediating the reading. It is indexed by research domain; a single average would hide the live signal (Fig 1 :19, :22).

The question is a **union of four conditions**, not one lever with a single answer (Fig 9 :55, :57). A change to one leaves the intersections standing because the other conditions still bind.

## 2. One loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

### D1 - The narrative as frame - `general` structural, `specified` at a slot, triangulated

A narrative is a closure. Its atoms are the events it holds; its bonds are the order and relation it imposes on them. Expectations generated inside that closure cannot reach an atom the story does not contain. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* The practical consequence: "communicate the success story better" searches harder inside the same closure and cannot change the direction of expectation. An actual result, instrument, failed replication or alien discipline would be an import and would need provenance and a shadow test.

### D2 - Transmission - `specified`, triangulated

A story travels as a recording of one occasion: Fig 4 :25, *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Each handoff to a textbook, panel or rubric carries that recording, and the rooms converge on each other: Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A chain of agreeing rooms is one long room, and the world never gets a vote. The condition matters: a chain is a selection between rooms only when the next room genuinely differs and matches the residual the last one surfaced. So the verdict is not "transmission is always corrupting"; it is "transmission without a differing room and a non-updating ground is a mirror".

### D3 - Expectation formation - `specified` on the anchor and convention claims, `general` on the broken link; single-source on Fig 3 for the break

The decisive failure is a reference that updates on what the frame thinks. Citations, prestige and panel consensus are not anchors; they move when pushed, and the resulting drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices (Fig 2 :29, :33-34; Fig 10 :24). The chain breaks at the vocabulary-to-objective link: a community can say what its story affords and cannot say what would count as progress at the present slot, because no instrument measures that bond (Fig 3 :25, :27). And a progress story that fixes an allocation has stopped being a convention and has become a verdict (Fig 10 :47; Fig 2 :41, :43).

### D4 - The research habitat - `general` on the contact claim, `specified` on the direction; triangulated on contact, single-source on the habitat enumeration

Capability is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 11 :39-40). A narrative shapes expectations partly by changing the habitat: which instruments get built, which problems get staffed, which failures become visible. The habitat is enumerated from the residual, never composed (Fig 11 :43-44), and its valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51). A story that was a benefactor to one expectation can be a malfactor to another.

## 3. The separator, in one list

A historical narrative shapes a present expectation when all four conditions hold:

1. **Closure condition.** It supplies the atoms and bonds from which the expectation is composed at that slot.
2. **Bond condition.** The bond it uses to encode progress selects the direction of the forecast: sequence and subtraction give steady accumulation, comparison against the present gives inevitability, comparison plus refusal with a preservation constraint gives discontinuous return.
3. **Channel condition.** It travels through rooms that compound agreement without evidence, unless the next room differs and matches the surfaced residual.
4. **Habitat condition.** It changes the community's contact, not merely its belief.

The **failure mode** is condition on the reference: if the test of the expectation is itself a product of the story, agreement is coherent drift rather than triangulation. The **blind spot** is which narrative dominates and by how much; no figure line returns that.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any progress narrative is false. The question is about what a story does to an expectation, not about the truth of the story.
- Not that the linear model, Whig history or the revolutionary picture dominates anywhere. That is a `gap`, not a silence, and naming one without provenance would be a metamerism error.
- Not that transmission is always corrupting. A room that differs and tests the surfaced residual is a selection between rooms, not a mirror.
- Not that prestige or citation counts settle anything. They are channel readings, not anchors.
- Not a numerical effect size, not a survey result, not a ground reading. `specified` and `gap` only.
- Not a seventh concept of progress. The set is closed; only the arrangements vary (Fig 13 :115-116).

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

The missing instrument is a **story-variation design**, not another argument:

1. Choose a decision room (a funding panel, a review committee, a curriculum board) with a fixed evidence packet.
2. Randomly vary which progress story the room reads before deciding: accumulation, Whig, or revolutionary.
3. Record the expectation formed: time horizon, problem choice, risk tolerance, allocation.
4. Range the resulting decisions against outcomes that do **not** update on the story: replication results, patient outcomes, instruments that register something, a market, a measured failure.
5. Check per slot, never as an average (Fig 1 :19). Keep every cut ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :40-41).

Standing falsifiers, written in advance in `01` section 4: an expectation with no supporting atom in the inherited story; two stories with the same bond producing opposite directions; a chain whose agreement tracks the ground at every handoff; a ground that updates on the story yet still corrects; an instrument measuring the bond at the slot; or a figure line that returns a dominant narrative or a number. Any of these changes the verdict.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are structural: they say what a compositional system requires for a story to shape an expectation, not what any particular community exhibits. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :133). The canonical figures README makes the choice of primitives visible as *"a human's careful guess"* rather than a discovery, and the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* That is what this file does. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which story about scientific progress is true, and it does not name a culprit. It says that a story shapes a present expectation by supplying the atoms and the bond from which the expectation is composed, by travelling through rooms that compound agreement unless the next room genuinely differs, and by changing what a research community is in contact with. The decisive failure is when the reference used to test the expectation is itself a product of the story, because then everyone converges coherently and nobody notices. The chain usually breaks where a community can name what its story affords but has no instrument for whether its progress claim holds now. The framework returns a four-condition separator, six refuters written in advance, and the design of the instrument that would close the one thing it cannot say: which narrative dominates in a given institution, and by how much. That slot stays open until a ground settles it.
