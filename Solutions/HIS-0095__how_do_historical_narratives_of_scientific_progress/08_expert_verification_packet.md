# Expert verification packet - the Tether on narratives of scientific progress

**What this is:** a claim in a form an historian of science, an STS scholar, or a cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the dominant narrative and the magnitude, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. Nothing here was measured against a research community.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of four conditions, not one lever. A historical narrative shapes a present expectation about research and innovation when it supplies the atoms and the bond from which the expectation is composed, when that bond selects the direction of the forecast, when the story travels through rooms that compound agreement without evidence unless the next room genuinely differs and matches the surfaced residual, and when it changes the habitat to which the community has contact rather than only its belief. The decisive failure is a reference that updates on the story itself, producing coherent drift that looks like triangulation. The chain typically breaks at the vocabulary-to-objective link, where no instrument measures the bond between a story about the past and a forecast about the future. The framework returns the separator, six refuters written in advance, and a story-variation design that would close the one thing it cannot say: which narrative dominates in a given institution, and by how much.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 6

- **Statement:** "Progress" is a bond, not a stock. Over a fixed set of historical events, different bonds give different forecasts; the stories are isomers, and all share a functional group, past events arranged into a route to a present.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 - *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit two progress stories over a fixed atom set with the same bond chain but opposite expected directions; or show that one story requires an atom the other does not while yielding the same expectation. Either breaks the isomer-to-expectation step. The fixed-atom condition is a live caveat, recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1.

### C2 - historiography-facing; checkable against a real transmission chain

- **Statement:** A story handed down through textbooks, panels and rubrics is a recording of one occasion, not a reapplicable method. Rooms that are selected to resemble the last one compound agreement without adding evidence. The chain becomes a selection between rooms only when the next room differs and matches the residual the last one surfaced.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :25 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 4 :26 - *"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."* Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* Fig 7 :35 - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :36 - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
- **What to check:** take one concrete chain, for example the linear-model story moving from historiography into grant rubrics. At each handoff, ask whether the recipient room differed from the sender and whether it was selected against a residual the sender surfaced, or whether it was selected for agreement. Then ask what independent, non-updating outcome was consulted at each step.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a chain whose agreement tracks an independent, non-updating ground at every handoff. That makes it triangulation, not a mirror, and breaks this sub-claim.

### C3 - decision-facing; checkable by a funder, reviewer or cognitive scientist

- **Statement:** The necessary condition for correction is an anchor that does not update on what the frames think. When citations, prestige or panel consensus occupy that seat, the result is coherent drift. The broken link is the vocabulary-to-objective link, which has no instrument. A story that decides an allocation has become a verdict, not a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :34 - *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* Fig 3 :27 - *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 10 :47 - *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
- **Refuter:** show a reference that updates on the story yet still corrects expectations; or name an instrument that measures the story-to-expectation bond at the slot; or show a story used as a convention that fixes no allocation and can be violated freely. The third does not break C3, which concerns the outcome-fixing case; it is the control condition.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any progress narrative is false, and not that one dominates. The question is about effect, not truth or prevalence.
- Not a numerical effect size, survey result, or measured change in funding behaviour.
- Not that all transmission corrupts. A room that differs and matches the surfaced residual is a selection between rooms.
- Not that citations or prestige are useless. They are efficient for a convention about shared meaning (Fig 2 :41); they are disqualified as a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a seventh concept of progress and not a new primitive. The set is closed; arrangements are not (Fig 13 :115-116).
- Not `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

A story-variation design, not a further argument. Hold the evidence packet constant; vary which progress story the decision room reads; record the expectation; range the decision against outcomes that do not update on the story, per slot.

1. Which bond changes the forecast direction, if any? That settles C1.
2. Does a chain with a genuinely differing room behave differently from a mirror chain? That settles C2.
3. Does removing prestige and citation readings from the reference seat restore correction? And can an instrument for the story-to-expectation bond be built from a worse instrument already returning something? That settles C3 and the broken link.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :112, :117, :123 and Fig 7 :35-36. Four short passages.
2. Pick one real chain you know (a textbook tradition, a funding rubric, a review standard) and say whether the next room differed and whether it matched a surfaced residual.
3. Name the anchor in that chain and say whether it moves when the story is pushed. If it moves, point to the drift.
4. Name one instrument that would measure the story-to-expectation bond at the slot, or say why none is available.
5. Send the failure. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record your verdict before reading the framework; later agreement is not pooled as corroboration.
