# The Tether's answer on measuring AI's impact

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a protocol and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A business books a local win: minutes saved on the task the AI touched. Then the win disappears into the system. Review queues lengthen, expectations of turnaround rise, rework appears, or new tasks arrive that did not exist before. The firm-wide number stays flat, so the report says "no measurable impact", while something is plainly still moving.

R is `|Γ(b,a) - o′|` per slot s. Γ(b,a) is the local prediction (time saved on task X); o′ is the whole-system return; s is the task, the downstream station, the reviewer, the customer. The mis-indexing is the question: the measurement sits on one slot while the residual lives on others. Fig 9 :54-57 makes this a **union compound**, not one hard problem: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Index the residual per slot, never net it into a headline.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Report the saving and the new work in separate columns with separate units. A single "net productivity" figure is the flat formula of Fig 12, not a fix.
2. **A flat aggregate with one live slot is a legal state.** Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. So "our overall number did not move" is not evidence of zero impact. It is evidence that the aggregate cannot hold what the slots carry.
3. **Enumerate the habitat outward.** Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The new work is a second or third-order effect, not a first-order one; Fig 11 :52-53 gives the DDT/eagles case. Measure the slots at the distance where the residual stops mattering, not just the instrumented task.
4. **Impact is a property of the AI-and-workflow, not the tool.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A tool benchmark or a local timer is an agent-only reading.
5. **The ground is the only metric.** Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :29 names the anchor: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* For a business that means realized customer outcome, quality-adjusted throughput, revenue. Time saved is an instrument ranged against the ground, and is disqualified as the verdict.
6. **Keep the channel reachable.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. When the time-saved KPI saturates or starts setting bonuses, say the channel lost touch, not that the ground changed.
7. **Buy independence deliberately.** Fig 2 :31: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Pair the internal dashboard with an instrument that does not share its evidence.
8. **Describe the effect before choosing the metric.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Write down what you would expect to see if time were being re-absorbed, before the dashboard is specified.
9. **Report the round-trip gap and the mode.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Where nothing registers, Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Report `unreachable at this budget`, not zero.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that AI saves no time. The local saving is a real reading; it just is not a verdict about where the time went.
- Not that new work is always created. That is an empirical question for a controlled, per-slot measurement, not a conclusion of this derivation.
- Not a single scorecard, weighting or KPI. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that time saved should be discarded. It is demoted from verdict to instrument.
- Not that the economics of Jevons, Parkinson or Goodhart settle this case. They are corroborations from other pools, retrodictions, not results this derivation produced.
- Not `grounded`. No business measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. The same reading, "20 minutes saved", is compatible with capacity released, work shifted to a reviewer, quality quietly lowered, or a task redone elsewhere. What would close it:

- **A per-slot instrument that registers the receiving slot before the local metric moves.** If it exists and returns a stable reading across deployments, it names the route rather than guessing at it.
- **A controlled before/after at the ground:** quality-adjusted output and customer outcome, measured independently of the time dashboard, with the habitat enumerated to the distance where the cascade stops.
- **A discriminating instrument for the sign of the loop** (Fig 12 :121: *"The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*): does the saved time regenerate as a method, or is it spent on one review?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** a case where a single aggregate locates the responsible condition; an agent-only improvement that moves an unchanged ground; low-overlap instruments agreeing with each other while both miss the ground; a measured round-trip gap of zero.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The habit of measuring minutes is *"a human's careful guess"* at what matters, a visible choice, not nature's own list (*They're Scanning Books*, opened this session). Another measurement frame need not begin with minutes. This derivation stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish the source of a local saving, and records the route so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Where a firm has no instrument for the other slots, the correct report is `unreachable at this budget`, not `zero impact`. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Do not ask whether AI saved time on the task you instrumented. Ask where the residual went. Measure per slot, not in aggregate: a flat firm-wide number with one live reviewer queue is a legal state, not evidence of no impact. Enumerate the contact cascade outward to the third order, because the new work appears where the timer is not looking. Keep the local saving as an instrument and pin the verdict to an external ground such as customer outcome or quality-adjusted output, since a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. Report hours and new work in separate units; a net figure adds things that do not add. Watch for a saturated metric, pooled readings and a KPI that has become a target, and pair the dashboard with an instrument that does not share its evidence. Describe the effect you expect before choosing the metric, report what your headline drops, and say "unreachable at this budget" rather than "zero" when nothing registers. That is a shape any candidate measurement protocol must have, with refuters written in advance.
