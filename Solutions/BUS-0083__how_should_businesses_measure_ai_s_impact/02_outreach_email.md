**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the analogue caveat in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 (strain S2) is respected in the text. The claim must not present Fig 12's bit/action currencies as a direct law of business accounting.

**Subject:** Check one measurement claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when a team saves 20 minutes on an AI-augmented task, the useful unit of measurement is not those 20 minutes and not a firm-wide net of minutes saved minus work created. It is the residual indexed per slot: the saving on the task, the changed load on downstream slots, and the result at an external ground such as customer outcome or quality-adjusted output.
>
> Three things follow from the framework's own rules. First, a flat firm-wide number with one live reviewer queue is a legal state, not evidence of zero impact; aggregation is how a live signal disappears into an average. Second, the new work is a contact cascade, usually second or third order, so the measurement habitat has to be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering. Third, the local timer belongs to the frame that deployed the tool, so it is an instrument, not a verdict: a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that AI saves no time; that new work always appears; that there is a single scorecard or weighting that settles this; that hours and new work can be added after a convenient conversion; or that economic analogues such as Jevons, Parkinson or Goodhart settle a business case. Those are readings from other fields, not measurements of your firm. If no instrument registers the new work, the honest report is "unreachable at this budget", not "zero impact".
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a controlled case where one aggregate number correctly predicts where new work appears, with no slot carrying a live residual while the aggregate is near zero;
> 2. an agent-only AI improvement, with the workflow and its contacts unchanged, that moves the external ground;
> 3. a reporting scheme whose headline-to-slot round trip is measured lossless;
> 4. two genuinely low-overlap instruments that agree with each other while both diverge from the ground for the same unrecorded reason.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, that part of the protocol is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): available on request as `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The figures and formula as published: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - The architecture and citation list: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
