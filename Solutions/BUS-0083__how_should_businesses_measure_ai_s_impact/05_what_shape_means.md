## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its contents. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0083, "measure per slot, enumerate the contact cascade, pin to an external ground" is a shape. "Adopt this specific AI-impact scorecard and this specific weighting of time saved versus new work" is a name, and a premature one.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- The same essay on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Applied here: before choosing a metric, describe what one would expect to see if saved time were being re-absorbed into new work elsewhere.

The effect description for this question is deliberately metric-neutral: *if a saving on task X is being re-absorbed, then the load on some other slot should rise, the queue should lengthen, the quality floor should shift, or the customer outcome should fail to move, in a way that the task-X timer cannot see.* Any business can check that against the world without first accepting the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

A measurement dashboard is a declared set of primitives. Its choice of "minutes" and "headcount" is visible only if it is written down. A scorecard that silently treats hours and tasks as commensurable has made an unrecorded choice. Naming a specific KPI as *the* measure of AI impact turns that visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the source question matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, opened this session. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The local time saving is exactly such a reading. The same "20 minutes saved" is compatible with genuine capacity released, with work shifted to a reviewer, with quality quietly lowered, with a task dropped that someone else now redoes. The reading does not determine the source. *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the same point in its video-frame example: the frame and the photograph are *"indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."*

What is owed is a discriminating instrument, as in the spectrometer case, not a name for the source and not a cleverer argument about the reading. This is why F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` demotes rather than discards the time-saved metric: it is a genuine projection that cannot identify its own source.

## 5. What the eight essays jointly contribute

| essay | contribution to this question |
|---|---|
| *How Intelligence Actually Works* | projection versus source; provenance loss; "indiscernibility is not identity"; contact as distinction resolver |
| *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* | "Prestige is not action"; the output is only as good as the validator; LLM output as simulacra |
| *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* | effect descriptions travel when contact does not; drift when frames only check each other |
| *How a Society of Agents Would Work* | one ground under many frames; stay the rate per kind of situation, not the pooled average; the arrangement matters more than the members |
| *The Library of Babel* | shape before name; proximity bootstrapping; bundling stop condition |
| *They're Scanning Books* | outputs are not generators; imports add a dimension, not a term; declared primitives are visible choices |
| *The Hard Problem is About Source* | metamerism; a reading cannot comment on its source; a better instrument settles it |
| *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* | the figures and formula; the "three mappings offered and declined" precedent; the self-validation refusal |

Two of these bear directly and are easy to over-read:

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* says *"Use the success rate per try, not the total. Whatever gets tried most piles up votes."* That is the per-slot rule at a different stratum, and it is why a firm-wide hours-saved total is a poor verdict even when every local number is honest.
- *Arrival of the Fittest* records *"Three mappings offered and declined"*, declining a mapping that resembles the framework without aligning with it. It also says the triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* So the world sources that corroborate this derivation, including the economics and management literature, are retrodictions at best, not results the framework produced.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. In every stable system, the two are separated. The thing that earns trust is not the same as the thing that gets to act."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A survey of well-known AI thought leaders agreeing that AI raises productivity is the named collapse: the high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige, and the ground is never consulted. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the measurement protocol a budget to be tried, and let the ground (customer outcome, realized value, a controlled before/after) settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture article adds that generated solutions *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So this folder's derivation is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any business claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
