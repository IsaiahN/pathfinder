# Expert verification packet - the Tether on measuring AI impact

**What this is:** a claim in a form an AI-impact or management researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No business measurement is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the business question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When AI saves time on an instrumented task, the correct unit of measurement is not the local saving and not a firm-wide net of saving minus new work. It is the residual indexed per slot: the saving on the task, the changed load on the downstream slots, and the result at an external ground such as customer outcome or quality-adjusted output. A flat aggregate with one live slot is a legal state, so "our overall number did not move" is not evidence of zero impact. The new work is a second or third-order contact cascade, so the measurement habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering. Hours and new work are different units and must be held apart. The local timer is an instrument, not the verdict, because a frame cannot score itself with what it produces. The protocol also requires an independent instrument, a described-before-choice effect statement, a measured round-trip gap and a mode label, with `unreachable` reported where nothing registers.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 11

- **Statement:** the residual must be indexed per slot; the habitat must be enumerated outward; a near-zero aggregate with one live slot is a legal state.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*; Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*; Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*; Fig 11 :52-53 *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled case in which one aggregate number correctly predicts where new work appears, with no slot carrying positive residual while the aggregate is near zero; or a deployment where every material change is confined to first-order contact and nothing registers further out after the cascade window. If either holds, C1 is false.

### C2 - measurement-facing; checkable by a researcher against their own impact study

- **Statement:** time saved is a frame-internal instrument and is disqualified as the verdict; the ground is the only metric; hours and new work cannot be added.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :23 *"The ground enters as the only metric."*; Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*; Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*; Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* and Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."*
- **What to check:** is the "time saved" number produced by the same frame that reports the impact? If so, Fig 1 :27 disqualifies it as a verdict. Can the two units be added without a stated conversion? If a conversion is stated, what anchors it?
- **Analogue caveat, stated before the check:** Fig 12's two currencies are bits and actions in composition cost. Applying it to hours and tasks is an extrapolation from composition pricing to business measurement, not a governing law. C2 stands on Fig 1 :23/:27 and Fig 2 :29; the Fig 12 reading is corroboration from a different domain. A refutation of the analogue does not by itself refute C2.
- **Refuter:** a measurement system that settles business impact with its own time-saved metric alone, where the anchor does not move when the metric's designers push on it; or a demonstrated unit conversion that is grounded rather than stipulated.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about the reporting channel

- **Statement:** the channel decays rather than the ground; a metric that becomes a target stops being a convention; independence must be bought; the round trip has a gap; a null reading is `unreachable`, not `zero`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; Fig 2 :31 *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."*; Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*; Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** a channel whose readings remain faithful after its metric saturates and is used to decide outcomes, checked against an independent instrument; or a reporting scheme whose headline-to-slot round trip is measured lossless. Then C3's prescriptions are unnecessary.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that AI saves no time; the local reading is real.
- Not that new work always appears; that is for a controlled per-slot measurement.
- Not a scorecard, a weighting, or a named KPI. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that time saved should be discarded; it is demoted from verdict to instrument.
- Not that Jevons, Parkinson, Goodhart, the productivity paradox or any citation settles a business case. They are corroborations from other pools and are retrodictions, not results produced by this derivation.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a business measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating measurement at the ground, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A per-slot instrument that registers the receiving slot before the local metric moves.
2. A controlled before/after of quality-adjusted output and customer outcome, independent of the time dashboard, with the habitat enumerated to the distance where the cascade stops.
3. A test of the sign of the loop: does the saved time regenerate as a method or is it spent on one review?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :13/:19/:22/:23/:27 and Fig 11 :44, :52-53. Six short passages.
2. Look at a study you know: is its impact number produced by the same frame that reports the saving? If so, does Fig 1 :27 disqualify it as a verdict?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate "capacity released" from "work shifted to another slot" in your domain, or say why none is available. That is the INWARD question.
4. Name one frame whose closure already predicts the shape of the re-absorbed work, and describe the residual in effect terms before pointing at it. That is the OUTWARD question.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
