## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13/:19/:22/:23/:27 (per-slot indexing, metric rule), Fig 11 :39/:44/:52 (habitat and cascade), Fig 2 :29/:31 (anchor and independence), Fig 4 :30 (round trip) and Fig 8 :39 (no proof of absence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: measure per slot, enumerate the habitat, pin to ground, hold the currencies apart, buy independence, report the gap, say unreachable.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete proposal to a business, for example a per-task timer plus a reviewer-queue counter plus a customer-outcome measure, reported as "AI impact, specified".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **distance at which the cascade stops mattering** (Fig 11 :44). The shorthand says "enumerate outward" but the concrete instrument must choose a cutoff, and that cutoff is a decision, not a reading of the figure;
- the **independence price** (Fig 2 :42). "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" becomes "use two instruments" unless the cost is stated, and the two are not the same instruction;
- the **convention/verdict boundary** (Fig 10 :47). A concrete KPI tends to become a target immediately, so the shorthand's "keep the channel reachable" understates the maintenance work;
- the **analogue status of the two-currency reading** (S2). The shorthand "hold the currencies apart" can be read as a direct law of business accounting, which Fig 12 does not give. The concrete report must carry the caveat explicitly or it drifts.

One thing survives cleanly: a saved minute on one slot and a new load on another are different readings, and a firm-wide net discards the only signal that says where to look. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no business ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
