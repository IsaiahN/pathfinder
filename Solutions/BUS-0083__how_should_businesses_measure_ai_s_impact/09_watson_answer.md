**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This WATSON run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. No business measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about measuring AI impact when a local time saving may be re-absorbed as new work elsewhere. Option A: net the saving against the new work into one headline and report it as the impact. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, index the residual per slot, enumerate the habitat, pin the verdict to an external ground and report what the instruments cannot see. What each leads to: A produces a single number in incommensurable units, lets a live slot disappear into an average, and lets the reporting frame score itself with a quantity it also produces. B produces a checkable protocol, written refuters, separate currencies and an honest `unreachable` where no instrument registers the new work.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the local prediction "time saved on task X" and the whole-system return "what work now exists and what the customer gets". Arity: four-place (firm Γ, action a, system return o′, slot s), ordered in time. Symmetry: asymmetric; work that appears is not removed by reversing the deployment. Scale: first order at the instrumented task, second and third order at downstream slots, firm-level at the market return. Fig 9 :57 names it a union compound: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (paraphrase of the line's governing clause; the verbatim line is quoted in TETHER below).
- **Domain 1, the instrumented task.** Actors: worker, AI tool, task X. Currency: minutes per task, local throughput. Anchor: observable task output at a stated quality floor. Question type: slot-local, one agent at one scale.
- **Domain 2, the workflow habitat.** Actors: downstream tasks, reviewers, coordinators, new demand, quality checks. Currency: work volume and load per slot. Anchor: per-slot work counts checked against realized output. Question type: the "elsewhere" of the question; a contact cascade, enumerated and never composed.
- **Domain 3, the business ground.** Actors: customers, revenue, competitors. Currency: realized value. Anchor: customer outcome and market return; Fig 2 :29 names "a market". Question type: the ground for any impact verdict.
- **Domain 4, the measurement channel.** Actors: dashboards, time tracking, KPIs, review protocols. Currency: readings. Anchor: the ground the readings should track; contact is testable there. Question type: the failure site, because the ground does not decay while the channel does.
- **X1, "AI productivity" as a property of the tool.** No anchor independent of a habitat; a benchmark is frame-internal. Excluded before any figure line is applied.
- **Partition or union.** D1/D2 overlap where saved time re-enters as work, and D2/D3 overlap where output meets the market. The whole is a union and must be unbundled before searching (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** 1 perception of other slots, 2 vocabulary for re-absorbed work and progress, 3 ground-level objective, 4 planning, 5 learn and carry (Fig 3 :20, :24, :27). The break is at 2 to 3, where no instrument exists.

Strain test applied: an early pass applied Fig 13's six physical terms to work tasks and Fig 12's bit/action currencies directly to hours/tasks. Re-census resolved the first by limiting Fig 13 to the honest-stop role, and demoted the second to an analogue (strain S2 in `01` section 6). F1 stands on Fig 1 alone. No figure was smoothed to fit.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a single net metric does not. Measure the residual per slot and never net hours against new work into one headline. Treat a flat aggregate with one live slot as a legal state, not as evidence of zero impact. Enumerate the contact cascade outward until it stops mattering, because the new work is second or third order. Demote the local time saving from verdict to instrument and pin the verdict to an external ground such as customer outcome or quality-adjusted output. Keep the channel reachable against saturated metrics, pooled readings and a KPI that has become a target; buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong; describe the expected effect before choosing the metric; report the round trip gap and the mode; and say `unreachable at this budget` where nothing registers. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. Cost if wrong, undoable and reversed by are stated below.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The protocol states its mode, its refuters and its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of the people checking it by sending a recording (one quarter's dashboard) instead of a generator (the measurement protocol and its refuter). Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective moves are removable without touching the derivation: stop netting the two units, stop calling the local timer a verdict, stop reading a null as zero. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A published protocol can be superseded by a corrected one, and no instrument has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument that registers the receiving slot before the local metric moves; a controlled before/after of quality-adjusted output and customer outcome independent of the time dashboard; a demonstrated unit conversion grounded rather than stipulated; an agent-only improvement that moves an unchanged ground; or a measured round-trip gap of zero. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
