## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what preserves a practice's reliability across a generation boundary. Option A: faithful copying of a record (recipe, rule, text). Option B: a reapplicable generator and a head start cross the boundary, each new practitioner re-runs the loop against an outcome that does not update, the habitat is maintained, and variants are selected. What each leads to: A gives a room chain of mirrors, agreement that compounds without evidence, and systems that repeat a past success; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters, and a lineage in which an error can be located.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A generation boundary across which a practice must carry the ability to produce a *new* correct outcome. Arity: two necessary conditions, a carrier and a selector, so a union compound. Symmetry: directed, because a method crosses up and a head start crosses down (Fig 4 :22), and the transform is lossy. Scale: first order within a career, cross-scale at the membrane.
- **Domain 1, the practice loop.** Actors: practitioner, patient / field / sea / wall, instruments. Currency: per-slot residuals and corrections. Anchor: the outcome, which does not move because anyone agrees. A partition boundary question at the kind of composition.
- **Domain 2, the membrane.** Actors: master, apprentice, community of practice. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: whether the apprentice generates a new correct outcome, not whether they reproduce the record.
- **Domain 3, the habitat.** Actors: soil, season, swell, star, materials, neighbours. Currency: contact, with introduction available and exclusion unavailable. Anchor: the habitat updates, the ground does not. Capability is agent-and-habitat.
- **Domain 4, the chain.** Actors: successive cohorts and variants. Currency: residuals surfaced per handoff; agreement and evidence are different currencies. Anchor: the ground selects between rooms, and is not one of them.
- **Order by dependency.** Habitat enumerated (D3) then method settled (D1) then method carried (D2) then variants selected (D4). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test: an early reading made Fig 4 :11 forbid all writing. Re-census showed that a head start may be a written effect-description; what is forbidden is using a recording as a generator. No figure line was softened.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B. Practices preserve knowledge by carrying a reapplicable generator and a head start across the generation boundary, keeping each slot's residual ranged against an outcome that does not update, building the unstated bond through two-way correction, maintaining the habitat, and surviving as a chain selected between variants. A copied record is a head start at best; it is not preservation, and treating it as preservation produces systems that repeat a past success and cannot produce a new one. The specific content of any practice is a name, not a shape, and is left for a ground reading or a provenanced import. This is `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the shape, `candidate` until an instrument settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent as written, because mode, refuters and lineage are stated before the conclusion. The real cost is misdirecting preservation work: funding digitisation as if it were preservation, producing a repeating system while believing the practice is safe. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. Recording and digitisation programmes are additive and can be reframed as head starts without withdrawing anything. The claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive must be given up. A sent claim is recoverable because the lineage is public.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A transplant test showing a place-bound practice fully reliable in a substituted habitat would break Fig 11 :40 and :67. A record-alone test yielding novel correct outcomes would break Fig 4 :23 and Fig 12 :47. A fidelity measurement showing that exact copying suffices under changed conditions would break Fig 7 :35. A figure re-census admitting a recording as a generator would break Fig 4 :22. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments (Fig 2 :35).
