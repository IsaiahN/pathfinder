# The Tether's answer: how practices preserve knowledge across generations

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

A healer, farmer, navigator or builder produces a correct outcome across many generations, often without written theory or formal institution. What is carried across the boundary? Option A: a faithfully copied record. Option B: a reapplicable generator and a head start, re-run against an outcome and selected between variants. The contact failure is a **union compound** (Fig 9), because the carrier and the selector are both necessary and neither is the other.

## 2. The shape

1. **A method crosses up; a head start comes down.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A record of one success is a head start at best. Replayed as a method it produces repetition, not new correct outcomes (Fig 4 :25-26). Writing is not banned; it is demoted from generator to head start.

2. **The ground does not update.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Patient, harvest, landfall, standing wall. Practitioner agreement is not the anchor: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

3. **The residual is per slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Each patient, field, bearing and joint is tracked separately. A single "knowledge retained" score throws away the signal that says where to look.

4. **The bond is transmitted by contact, not by the ingredient list.** Fig 12 :47 "A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself." The twist of the wrist, the reading of a swell, the feel of soil: these are bonds, not operands, and they are built by trying, being corrected and trying again.

5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The practice includes the soil, season, star field, material supply and neighbours. Moving it substitutes a habitat, and you inherit actors you did not choose (Fig 11 :67).

6. **Preservation is selection, not copying.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36 "The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." A chain of identical copies is one long room: motion, no progress.

7. **The lineage is the knowledge.** Fig 13 :130 "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." Fig 13 :142 "The seed was always the seedline."

8. **The framework names no recipe.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Which plant, which star, which gesture needs a ground reading or a provenanced import.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that tradition is superior to formal science, and not that formal science overlooks nothing. The question is the mechanism of preservation, not a ranking of knowledge systems.
- Not that any particular community fits this shape. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that writing is useless. A written effect-description is a head start and can be checked by anyone.
- Not that agreement among practitioners is evidence. If they share an evidence pool, their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that "culture" or "tradition" is a mechanism. Those are names for a pool, not an anchor.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

The ground is a measurement, not a further argument. Concrete tests:

- **Fidelity test:** measure transmission fidelity across generations (the cultural-learning literature already distinguishes mechanisms by *"the fidelity with which behavioural forms are transmitted"*) and ask whether high fidelity alone predicts new correct outcomes under changed conditions. If it does, F1 and F3 fail.
- **Transplant test:** move a place-bound practice to a new habitat, reproducing or not reproducing its original actors, and measure outcome reliability. This is the sharpest test of Fig 11 :40 and :67.
- **Record-alone test:** give a new practitioner only the written record, no contact and no correction, and ask for a novel correct outcome. This tests Fig 4 :23 and Fig 12 :47.
- **Per-slot test:** track residuals per patient, field, route and structure rather than as one score, and see whether the localized signal predicts where transmission fails. This tests Fig 1 :19.
- Standing falsifiers: any of the four in `01` §4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments: no transplant test, fidelity measurement or record-alone trial was run in this session. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. We can state the shape any preserving transmission must have; we cannot say which specific practice in which specific place has it. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank traditional knowledge against science and does not claim to know any particular recipe. It says that a practice survives a generation boundary only if a reapplicable method crosses and a head start comes down, never the recording of one success pretending to be the method; that each patient, field, bearing and wall is ranged against an outcome that does not move because anyone agrees; that the bond, the twist and the feel of it, is built by two-way correction rather than read off an ingredient list; that the practice includes its habitat, which is permanent work and does not travel with the agent; and that the chain persists by selection between variants, not by fidelity of copying. What is offered is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The specific content stays open until the ground settles it.
