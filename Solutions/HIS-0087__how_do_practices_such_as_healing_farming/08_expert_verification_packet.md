# Expert verification packet: the Tether on traditional knowledge transmission

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ethnobiologist, cognitive scientist, historian of technology or practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is an AI-produced simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures, and it cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A practice does not preserve its knowledge by copying a record faithfully. It preserves knowledge by carrying a reapplicable generator and a head start across the generation boundary, while each new practitioner ranges each slot's residual against an outcome that does not update, builds the unstated bond by two-way correction, maintains the habitat the practice depends on, and survives as a chain selected between variants rather than copied between rooms. The framework does not name the specific content of any practice; it returns a shape. Four refuters are written below, before any theory is shown.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 12
- **Statement:** a recording is not a generator. What crosses up is a method that can be reapplied; what comes down is a head start. The unstated arrangement is not recoverable from the ingredient list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Also Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Also Fig 12 :47 "A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."
- **Refuter:** a written record that, with no practice, no correction and no ground check, yields a correct *novel* outcome in a new practitioner; or a demonstration that the decisive bond is recoverable from the operand list alone.

### C2 — World-facing; checkable against the transmission literature and the crafts
- **Statement:** capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, and preservation is selection between variants rather than fidelity of copying. The TEK and vernacular-architecture literature describes knowledge as adaptive, place-based and learned by trial and error over generations; cultural learning treats transmission fidelity as a measurable variable.
- **What to check:** is it true that place-bound practices lose reliability when the habitat is substituted? Is high fidelity of copying sufficient for new correct outcomes under changed conditions? Does transmission between practitioners show variation selected by outcomes, or only reproduction?
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Also Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Also Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **Refuter:** a place-bound practice transplanted into a different habitat retaining full reliability without reproducing the original actors and conditions; or a chain of exact copies whose reliability holds or rises under changed conditions with no ground-driven selection.

### C3 — Framework-internal derivation of the chain
- **Statement:** the lineage is part of the knowledge, not documentation of it; and the residual must be measured per slot, not aggregated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :130 "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." Also Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Also Fig 13 :142 "The seed was always the seedline."
- **Refuter:** a practice whose reliable transmission is fully carried by the artifact with no lineage and where a later reader can still locate and repair an error from the artifact alone; or a demonstration that aggregating per-slot residuals preserves the information needed to locate a failing transmission.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Priorities:

1. **Transplant test:** move a place-bound practice to a new habitat, with and without reproduction of its original actors, and measure outcome reliability.
2. **Fidelity test:** measure transmission mechanism and fidelity across cohorts (cultural learning provides the vocabulary) and ask whether fidelity alone predicts novel correct outcomes.
3. **Record-alone test:** written record only, no contact, no correction, novel outcome demanded.
4. **Per-slot test:** track residuals separately per patient, field, route and structure.
5. Any of the four standing falsifiers in `01` §4.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes
