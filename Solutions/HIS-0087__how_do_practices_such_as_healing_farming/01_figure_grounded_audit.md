## 0. The residual, in effect terms

Observed: a healer cures, a farmer harvests, a navigator makes landfall, a builder raises a wall that stands. They do this repeatedly, across many generations, often with no written theory and no formal institution. The residual is the difference between a transmission that keeps this reliability and one that loses it.

Stated as an effect, with the sought-for shape bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"):

> **R:** a generation boundary across which a practice must carry something that lets the next generation produce a *new* correct outcome, not merely repeat an old one. Bound shape: **a generator plus a head start, ranged against a ground, in a maintained habitat, with selection between variants.**

- **Option A (copying):** preservation equals fidelity of reproduction. Success is the record surviving unchanged.
- **Option B (generating):** preservation equals continued ability to re-derive. Success is a new correct outcome under changed conditions.

The two options are not versions of one problem. They issue different instructions: A funds recording, B funds contact and selection. Fig 9 :5 warns that disagreements which do not shrink with effort are several well-formed questions, not one hard one. This one splits at the carrier, so it is a **union compound** with its own loop.

## 2. Domain census

### 2.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** two necessary conditions, a carrier and a selector. One residual of transmission, not four residuals of four crafts (healing, farming, navigation, building are instances, not domains).
- **Symmetry:** directed, not symmetric. A method crosses up, a head start crosses down (Fig 4 :22). Reversing the direction does not restore the skill, because the transform is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).
- **Scale:** first order within one career (practitioner and ground), cross-scale at the membrane (one practitioner to a population across generations).

### 2.2 One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

**D1, the practice loop (one practitioner and their ground).**
Actors: practitioner, patient / field / sea / wall, instruments (hand, eye, star compass, soil).
Currency: per-slot residuals and the corrections they produce.
Anchor: the outcome, which does not update because the practitioner wants it to. A sick patient either recovers.
No nameable anchor would make this a non-domain; here the anchor is named and public.

**D2, the membrane across the generation boundary.**
Actors: master, apprentice, community of practice.
Currency: methods carried up (verified and compressed) and head starts sent down.
Anchor: whether the apprentice can generate a new correct outcome, not whether they reproduce the master's record.

**D3, the habitat.**
Actors: soil, seed, season, swell, star, materials, neighbours, predators.
Currency: contact. Introduction is available, exclusion is not.
Anchor: the habitat updates; the ground still decides. Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat.

**D4, the chain of generations.**
Actors: successive cohorts and the variants of the practice they carry.
Currency: residuals surfaced per handoff; agreement and evidence are different currencies here.
Anchor: the ground selects between variants. The anchor is not one of the rooms.

### 2.3 Partition or union

D1 and D3 are a **union compound**: capability lives in agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), so a "practice" that excludes soil, season or materials is not the practice. D2 and D4 are a **union**: transmission needs both a carrier across the membrane and a selector between variants. Removing either leaves a chain of copies or a lone genius with no successor. The overlaps have their own loops, so they are not averaged into one verdict.

### 2.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

D3 (habitat enumerated) → D1 (method settled against the ground) → D2 (method carried, not the recording) → D4 (selection between variants). Effort spent on D4 before D1 lands on a step whose input has not arrived. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

### 2.5 Strain test applied

An early reading tried to make Fig 4 :11 ("a head start, never a recording") forbid all written material. That strains: the tacit-knowledge essay opened this session says text carries context well and subtext partly, so a written effect-description can be a head start. Re-census: the false reading was assigning a recording to D2 as the generator. What Fig 4 forbids is a recording carried upward *as knowledge* and replayed downward *as a method*. Writing as a head start is permitted. Strain dissolved by re-census, not by softening the quote.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 — The carrier is a method, not a recording (decisive for D2)

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
Fig 4 :23: **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**
Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**

A recipe that survives unchanged is a recording of one occasion. It can be a head start, which is permitted and useful. It cannot be the thing that preserves the practice, because replayed downward it *"produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one"* (Fig 4 :26). The tacit-knowledge essay states the same limit in transmission terms: what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself.

**Refuter:** show a written record that, with no apprenticeship, no practice and no ground check, reliably yields a correct *novel* outcome in a new practitioner. If that exists, D2 fails.

### F2 — The ground is the anchor, agreement is not (D1 and D4)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

For healing the anchor is the patient outcome; for farming the harvest; for navigation the landfall; for building the standing wall. None moves because a guild or a community agrees. The tacit-knowledge essay gives the failure mode: two people who only check against each other drift together, and *"the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"* (Fig 2 :34, quoted in that essay).

**Refuter:** show a tradition that kept reliable outcomes for many generations while checking only against its own members' agreement and never against an outcome. If that exists, F2 fails.

### F3 — Preservation is selection, not copying (D4)

Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
Fig 7 :36: **"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**

A chain of generations with correlation near one is one long room: motion without progress. Traditional navigation gives the concrete case: the knowledge was *"passed by oral tradition from master to apprentice, often in the form of song"*, and it was *"widely lost after contact with and colonization by Europeans"* (Polynesian navigation, Wikipedia). What was lost was not the song alone but the selection loop that kept the song honest.

**Refuter:** show a chain in which each generation copied the previous exactly, with no variation and no ground-driven selection, and reliability nevertheless increased or held under changed conditions.

### F4 — The bond is carried by practice, not by the ingredient list (D2)

Fig 12 :47: **"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."**
Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

The "twisting stretch" of the master baker, recorded in the tacit-knowledge essay, is exactly this: the ingredients and even the visible stretching were nameable; the twist was a bond visible only to someone acting and being corrected. The essay's sentence is precise: *"An apprentice isn't receiving messages; they're slowly building the history that makes the messages make sense."*

**Refuter:** show that the bond is recoverable from the operand list alone in a craft case, or that a state-only vocabulary suffices to transmit a route. Fig 12's six tests would then need re-census.

### F5 — Capability is agent-and-habitat (D3)

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :63: *"a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."*
Fig 11 :67: **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required"**

Traditional ecological knowledge is described as *"a family of culturally situated rules and practices rooted in specific places and ecological worldviews"* (TEK, Wikipedia). Vernacular building is *"learned by trial and error over generations of building construction, often existing long before the scientific theories which explain why they work"* (Vernacular architecture, Wikipedia). Both are habitats maintained by work. Moving a practice to a new clinic, field, sea or climate substitutes a habitat, loses what you failed to reproduce and introduces actors you did not choose.

**Refuter:** show a place-bound practice transplanted into a different habitat retaining full reliability without reproducing the original actors and conditions. That would break Fig 11 :40 and :67.

### F6 — The residual is per slot, never aggregated (D1)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Each patient, field, bearing and joint has its own residual. A programme that measures "traditional knowledge retained" as a single score aggregates across slots and throws away the only signal that says where to look. The middle links of Fig 3's chain matter most here: having terms for what a situation affords, and being able to say what would count as progress, are exactly the links with the fewest instruments in craft transmission.

**Refuter:** show that aggregating per-practice residuals into one score preserves the information needed to locate a failing transmission. That contradicts Fig 1 :22.

### F7 — The lineage is part of the knowledge (all domains)

Fig 13 :127: *"the record is what makes it verifiable"*
Fig 13 :130: **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."**
Fig 13 :142: **"The seed was always the seedline."**
Fig 10 :62: **"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."**

Apprenticeship is not only skill transfer; it is the transmission of a seeded lineage. The record of what was tried, failed and corrected lets a later reader locate an error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :63-64). Digitising an artifact without its seedline is the flat-form collapse of Fig 12 :6-17.

**Refuter:** show a practice whose reliable transmission is fully carried by the artifact with no lineage, and where a later reader can still locate and repair an error from the artifact alone.

### F8 — The answer is a shape, not a name (limit)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

The figures licence the claim that *some* generator must cross and *some* ground must settle. They do not licence naming, for a given community, which plant, star or gesture carries the method. That requires a provenanced import with a shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13) or a ground reading. This is a limit on the derivation, not a hole to fill with a plausible story.

**Refuter:** derive a specific traditional recipe from the figure set alone, with its source gate and shadow test recorded. If that is possible, this limit is wrong.

### F9 — Instruments and imports are how the wall moves (D1 and cross-frame)

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**

Better diagnostics in healing, better reading of swell in navigation, better soil reading in farming: each is a worse instrument already returning something, improved. Cross-cultural contact (the outrigger and the stick chart, the sweet potato, Tupaia's chart) is import, which needs a source gate and a shadow test and is debited against independence. Agreement between two practitioners who learned from the same master is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not corroboration.

**Refuter:** show an instrument specified from a description alone, with no prior worse instrument returning a reading; or an import that adds no atom yet moves the wall. Either breaks Fig 6.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A practice keeps reliable outcomes across generations with verbatim copying only, no ground re-check and no habitat maintenance (breaks F2 and F3).
2. A written record alone, with no practice or correction, produces a correct novel outcome in a new practitioner (breaks F1 and F4).
3. A place-bound practice transplanted into a different habitat keeps full reliability without reproducing the original actors and conditions (breaks F5).
4. A figure re-census admits a recording crossing upward as a generator (breaks Fig 4 :23 and :25).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter, not with the framework (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
2. Treat recording and digitising as **head starts**, never as preservation itself. Fund contact, correction time, and time alone at the bench alongside the master.
3. Measure per slot: each patient, field, route and structure gets its own residual (Fig 1 :19). Refuse a single aggregated retention score.
4. Maintain the habitat. Introduction is available, exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). A managed habitat is permanent work.
5. Keep the seedline. A recording without its lineage is the flat-form collapse of Fig 12.
6. Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60): venue tiers decide what gets tried, never whether the claim is true.

## 7. Per-domain verdicts (kept separate, never averaged)

- **D1, practice loop:** `triangulated` across Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and Fig 2 :29 (anchor that does not update), with TEK (ecology) and vernacular architecture (engineering) as low-overlap world corroboration. Verdict: reliability lives in continued contact with an outcome, not in internal coherence.
- **D2, membrane:** `triangulated` across Fig 4 :22-25 and Fig 12 :47, with situated learning (cognition) and tacit knowledge (cognition) as low-overlap corroboration. Verdict: the carrier is a generator plus a head start, and the bond is transmitted by two-way correction.
- **D3, habitat:** `triangulated` across Fig 11 :40, :43, :63, :67 and the TEK world source. Verdict: capability is agent-and-habitat; place-specific knowledge fails under substitution.
- **D4, chain:** `triangulated` across Fig 7 :13, :35, :36 and cultural learning (animal behaviour). Verdict: preservation is selection between variants, not fidelity of copying.

**Overall verdict from the figures:** Option B. Practices preserve knowledge by carrying a reapplicable method and a head start across the generation boundary, keeping each slot's residual ranged against a ground that does not update, maintaining the habitat, and selecting between variants. The specific content of any one practice is a name, not a shape, and is left open. Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the four domains. Not `grounded`.
