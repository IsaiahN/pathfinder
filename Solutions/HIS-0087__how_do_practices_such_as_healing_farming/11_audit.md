## Independent domain census (written from the residual line first)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a generation boundary across which a practice must carry the ability to produce a new correct outcome. Arity: two necessary conditions, a carrier and a selector, so a **union compound** (Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either). Symmetry: directed, because a method crosses up and a head start crosses down; the transform is lossy, so reversal does not restore the skill. Scale: first order within one career, cross-scale at the membrane.
- **Domain 1, the practice loop.** Actors: practitioner, patient / field / sea / wall, instruments. Currency: per-slot residuals and corrections. Anchor: the outcome. This is a partition question at the boundary of what composes across the two kinds.
- **Domain 2, the membrane.** Actors: master, apprentice, community. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: generation of a new correct outcome, not reproduction of a record.
- **Domain 3, the habitat.** Actors: soil, season, swell, star, materials, neighbours. Currency: contact; introduction available, exclusion not. Anchor: the habitat updates, the ground does not.
- **Domain 4, the chain.** Actors: cohorts and variants. Currency: residuals per handoff. Anchor: the ground selects between rooms.

Order by dependency: habitat, then settled method, then membrane transfer, then selection. Describe R before choosing a frame.
