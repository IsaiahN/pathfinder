## The question in effect terms

What is being decided is the mechanism of preservation, not the value of the practices.

- **Option A:** a practice preserves knowledge by copying a record faithfully (recipe, rule, text, fixed procedure). If A holds, the measure of success is fidelity of reproduction, and digitising or writing things down is itself preservation.
- **Option B:** a practice preserves knowledge by carrying a reapplicable generator and a head start across the generation boundary, while each new practitioner re-runs the loop against a ground that does not update, and the community selects between variants. If B holds, fidelity of copying is not the measure; continued contact is.

What each leads to: A produces a room chain of mirrors, agreement that compounds without evidence, and systems that repeat a past success and cannot produce a new one. B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and a lineage that locates errors.

## What an answer would look like

A **shape**, bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: a directed membrane (a method crosses up, a head start crosses down), an anchor that neither generation can move, a habitat that is enumerated and maintained rather than composed, and a chain that is selected rather than copied. No particular recipe, plant, star, or joint detail is named by the framework.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list selected for receptiveness is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies agreement.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes the agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally, opt-in institutional channels also avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that scraping personal addresses would create.
