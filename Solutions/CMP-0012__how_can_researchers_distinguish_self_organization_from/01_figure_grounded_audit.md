## 0. The residual in effect terms

R: a researcher observes one stable large-scale order O in a system S and must attribute it. Three candidate causes are live at once:

- **A, self-organization:** O arises from local interactions among components with no external template or central controller.
- **B, imposed order:** O arises from an external constraint, boundary condition, design, or maintainer.
- **C, hidden coordination:** the components agree because of a hidden shared source or common cause; they never coordinated locally.

Each option leads to a different intervention. If A, removing a suspected maintainer leaves O intact and the order tracks what the ground kept. If B, removing the constraint collapses O, and the maintainer leaves an asymmetry (it can introduce but not subtract). If C, breaking the shared source dissolves the agreement even though no component ever communicated.

The residual is a **union, not a partition**: A, B and C can co-occur (an external energy flux drives every self-organizing system; a stigmergic trace is a shared environmental source that is not an external designer). Fig 9 :54-55: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single averaged verdict over the three would be a category error.

Mode of the shape itself: `general`, because it is structural. Mode of each diagnostic below: `specified`, because each predicts what an instrument would return. None is `grounded`.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

R by arity, symmetry and scale. Arity: three hypotheses over one observation. Symmetry: O is a lossy projection, the same reading under A, B and C; the observation does not carry the source. Scale: micro (local interaction) to macro (order), with the causal attribution crossing the membrane between scales (Fig 4).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2. Maintained habitat (imposed order) | maintainer, instruments, introduced actors, boundary conditions, design | introduction (asymmetric), convention | the ground, but a seat acts | yes, Fig 11 :58-61 |

It is a link nobody has looked at."), then describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28), then run interventions. Partition or union: union (above). The four domains are kept separate below; their verdicts are never averaged.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1. The pattern is a projection and cannot comment on its own source (decisive)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

The essay *How Intelligence Actually Works* (read 2026-11-22) states the exact case: "The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." It calls this provenance loss: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."

Therefore the observable order O is not a verdict about A versus B versus C. It is a reading with lossy information. A researcher who attributes the cause from O alone has committed the error *The Hard Problem is About Source* names: an instrument "speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Mode: `general`, structural.

**Refuter:** exhibit an invariant of O alone, measurable without any intervention, that reliably separates A, B and C across multiple systems. If one exists, F1 is false and attribution becomes a lookup.

### F2. Self-organization and imposed order are two modes of one loop, distinguished by the seat

Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :58-59: "Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there."

The RSI essay makes the same point in plain words: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector," and "The same code runs when there is a Ground Maintainer. It runs in cultivation, when humans select which plants to breed."

So the axis is **not** "orderly versus chaotic" and **not** "complex versus simple." It is "is there a maintainer that authors the template?" The diagnostic is a removal test: take the seat away (remove the constraint, the field, the designer) and see whether O persists. Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

**Refuter:** a case where O persists intact after the maintainer is demonstrably removed and the template is shown to have been authored by that maintainer. That breaks the removal test and the seat axis with it.

### F3. Hidden coordination is a named collapse, detected by independence and the anchor

Fig 2, panel "collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)": "they never talk, and still agree so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other". Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

The distinguishing test is not whether the components talk. It is whether their agreement survives when the shared source is cut and each frame is ranged separately against an anchor. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update."

World corroboration from a genuinely different field: *Confounding* (Wikipedia, read 2026-11-22) states "Confounding is a causal concept rather than a purely statistical one, and therefore cannot be fully described by correlations or associations alone," and gives the intervention operator `P(y|do(x))` distinct from the observational `P(y|x)`. That is the same move: observation of agreement is not intervention on the source. Triangulated (Fig 2 + Fig 8 + causal inference), low overlap between the figure's alignment panels and the statistics field.

**Refuter:** a case where component agreement persists unchanged after the common source is removed and each frame is ranged against an independently pinned anchor, with no residual shared evidence. Then C is not distinguishable by this test.

### F4. The distinction needs a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument

Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

The essays give the worked analogue. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: mined versus lab-grown diamonds were indistinguishable until isotope ratios, a real trace, became readable; "The tool only works because a trace existed, and not because a better tool was created." Color metamerism is the opposite case: there is no trace in the reading, only provenance. The same essay: "A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin," and "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."

World corroboration: *Metamerism (color)* (Wikipedia): "metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions" and "Material colors that match under one source will often appear different under the other." Change the illuminant and the match fails. That is the anchor test in optical form. Triangulated, low overlap (color science).

So the honest answer has two branches. If a trace exists, an instrument can close the case; if none exists, only provenance can. Until an instrument returns a difference, the verdict is `candidate`.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a source-discriminating trace for the order in question that no current instrument reads, then show it is unreadable in principle, not merely at this budget. That would convert "unreached" to "unreachable" and change the instruction from instrument-building to provenance-only.

### F5. Read the residual per slot; an aggregate throws away the signal

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A hidden coordinator often shows up in one slot only (one region locks to an external clock, one subpopulation shares a signal). A global order parameter can look smooth while one slot carries the entire attribution signal. This is the per-slot rule applied to causal attribution.

**Refuter:** a case where the discriminating signal is provably uniform across all slots and no per-slot reading carries more information than the aggregate.

### F6. A constraint that decides the outcome has stopped being a neutral background

Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one". Fig 10 :19: "→ install what can be violated".

A boundary condition that selects which pattern forms is not part of the scaffold; it is a cause. World corroboration: *Dissipative system* (Wikipedia): "This reproducible steady state may be reached by natural evolution of the system, by artifice, or by a combination of these two." That is exactly the A / B / union split, and it shows the energy flux itself is not the discriminator: flux is present in both. Triangulated, low overlap (thermodynamics).

**Refuter:** a case where a constraint demonstrably selects the outcome yet the resulting order is indistinguishable from the unmaintained case by every intervention in this audit. Then the convention test is not a discriminator.

### F7. Stigmergy strains the axis, and the re-census resolves it

A genuine difficulty: stigmergy is "a mechanism of indirect coordination, through the environment, between agents or actions" (*Stigmergy*, Wikipedia), and it "produces complex, seemingly intelligent structures, without need for any planning, control, or even direct communication between the agents." This looks like hidden coordination (C) but is classified as self-organization (A). The same source notes its use in building design, which ties directly to the design-research field of this question.

Re-census: the axis is not "direct versus indirect communication" and not "does the environment carry a trace." It is "does a seat author the template?" A stigmergic trace is written by the components themselves inside their own habitat; an external designer's template is authored outside it. By Fig 11 :64, "maintained the seat adds to a habitat it still did not author," and in the unmaintained mode "nothing composes it." Strain resolved by re-census, not by softening either category. Recorded so the correction is visible.

**Refuter:** a case of indirect coordination through an environment where the trace is authored by an outside seat and the components merely read it. That would force stigmergy into B and break the re-census.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The verdict of this audit is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An invariant of the observable order alone, requiring no intervention, reliably separates self-organization, imposed order and hidden coordination across multiple systems (kills F1).
2. A case shows order persisting intact after the maintainer is removed while the template is shown to have been authored by that maintainer (kills F2).
3. Component agreement survives cutting the shared source and ranging each frame against a pinned independent anchor, with no residual shared evidence (kills F3).
4. A source-discriminating trace exists and is readable, and still fails to separate the causes (kills F4).

Until one is run, no attribution in this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1: the pattern is a projection, so nobody should attribute a cause from the order alone (Fig 9 :32).
2. Run three interventions, not one debate: remove the seat, cut the shared source, range against a fixed anchor (F2, F3).
3. Read per slot, never as one global order parameter (F5).
4. Look for a trace before looking for a better argument; if there is no trace, record provenance and stop honestly (F4).
5. Do not average the three domains into one verdict; unbundle the union (Fig 9 :54-55).
6. Offer this to outside frames as a residual and a refuter, never as a theory to endorse (Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
