## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about distinguishing three causes of one observable order. Option A: attribute the cause from the pattern's appearance. Option B: keep the shape the figures license and refuse the attribution until an intervention returns a difference. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode verdict that confuses a projection with its source and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable set of discriminating interventions with refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where no instrument can discriminate.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** One stable large-scale order O with three candidate causes: local interaction with no template, an external constraint or designer, a hidden shared source. Arity: three hypotheses over one observation. Symmetry: O is a lossy projection, the same reading under all three; the observation does not carry the source. Scale: micro to macro, with attribution crossing the membrane between scales (Fig 4). This is a union compound, not one hard question: every self-organizing system has external energy and contact, and stigmergic traces are shared sources inside self-organized processes.
- **Domain 1, unmaintained habitat.** Actors: components, local interactions, environment. Currency: contact and survival, what the ground kept. Anchor: the ground, with no seat. Union member, not a partition cell.
- **Domain 2, maintained habitat.** Actors: maintainer, instruments, introduced actors, boundary conditions, design. Currency: introduction (asymmetric) and convention. Anchor: the ground, but a seat acts. Union member.
- **Domain 3, common cause.** Actors: components plus a hidden shared source. Currency: correlation and shared evidence. Anchor: an anchor that does not update, and independence between frames. Union member.
- **Domain 4, researcher's observation frame.** Actors: instruments, analyst's frame. Currency: per-slot residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all frames draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero and agreement carries no corroborating weight.
- **Order by dependency.** Secure the instrument first, then describe the residual before choosing a frame, then run the seat removal, then the source cut, then read any trace. A link below a missing instrument cannot be diagnosed (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test applied: stigmergy appears to blur self-organization and hidden coordination. Re-census resolves it by changing the axis from communication (direct or indirect) to authorship of the template (seat or no seat). Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the attribution does not. Self-organization, imposed order and hidden coordination can produce the same observable order and are Fig 12 isomers over a shared component set: same visible substance, different causal bond. The figures decide three things. First, the order cannot name its cause, so appearance-based attribution is refused. Second, the seat axis separates unmaintained from maintained order by a removal test. Third, independence plus a fixed anchor separates hidden coordination from genuine local coordination. The three are a union, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. Where no source-discriminating trace exists, only provenance can settle the case, and the framework reports that stop rather than inventing a name. This is `specified` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished attribution instead of a test, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoided by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The attribution tests are separable; one can be refuted without withdrawing the others. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can replace a sent one. The one thing not undone is a reader's independence once spent by showing them the framework before their verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A seat-removal experiment, a source-cut experiment with a fixed anchor, a per-slot scan, or a source-discriminating instrument would move a domain to `grounded`. An order-only invariant separating the causes would kill the projection verdict. A case of agreement surviving the source cut would kill the independence verdict. A figure re-census that makes the three causes non-isomeric would change the structural claim. Prestige or expert consensus is not such a reading. Until one fires, each domain stands as `candidate`.
