## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the form of a discriminating test that can be stated before the cause is known. "Remove a maintainer, cut a shared source, range against a fixed anchor" is a shape. "The order is caused by [named mechanism]" is a name. The shape constrains which answers are admissible; it does not fill the slot.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the words you already have that are closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place"; the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (three causes, one observable) before any figure line was chosen, and the sought-for shape (a ranked list of discriminating interventions, each with a refuter) was bound as an ordinary term in the README before the derivation ran.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming the cause of an observed order is licensed only when an intervention returns a difference (a seat removal, a source cut, a fixed anchor, a discriminating instrument) or when provenance is supplied. An elegant story about the mechanism is not a license.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six" and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The three-cause census in `01` is a visible guess about causal axes, not nature's own list. This is why the census is published rather than hidden.

## 4. Why "it looks self-organized" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered: the reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," that resolved it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the exact attribution case: "The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." A researcher who reads the cause off the order commits this error. What is owed is a discriminator or provenance, not a finer description of the pattern.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A simulated attribution cannot validate itself.

## 5. The specific shape for this question

The canonical shape has three parts, all fillable before the cause is known:

1. **Seat axis.** Is there a maintainer that authors the template? Operationally: remove it and observe whether the order persists. Source: Fig 11 :56 and :58-59; essay 2's "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."
2. **Independence axis.** Do the parts agree because they draw on the same hidden source? Operationally: cut the source and range each part separately against an anchor that does not update. Source: Fig 2 panel 2 and Fig 2 :29, :31-32.
3. **Trace axis.** Does the observable carry a source-discriminating trace? If yes, build the instrument; if no, record provenance and stop. Source: *How Intelligence Actually Works* gemstone versus color cases; Fig 6 :34-35; Fig 8 :39.

The shape does not say which cause holds in any particular system. It says what any answer must survive.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Independence is a measured quantity, not a virtue.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different. Figure 2 defines independence as 'how little they draw on the same evidence.' Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." This is the outreach rule for hidden coordination: a room of people who all learned the same evidence cannot check a claim about whether the order was caused by a shared source.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of experts chosen because they already agree with an attribution is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that LLM outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency. They cannot settle any attribution they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own §4

I initially wrote that the essays forbid naming a cause from the pattern. Narrowed: what they forbid is naming a cause from a **projection with no discriminating trace**, which is the metamerism case. Where a real trace exists (the gemstone case), naming after an instrument reads it is licensed. The honest rule is trace first, provenance second, never "the pattern looks like X." Both statements are kept so the correction stays visible.
