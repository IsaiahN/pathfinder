## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The source file places this question immediately after Q35 (what the CMB patterns say about the earliest expansion) and Q36 (whether inflation generated the small density differences that grew into galaxies). "Those patterns" therefore means the whole observed pattern class:

1. near-perfect isotropy and homogeneity on large scales (the horizon puzzle),
2. spatial flatness to about 0.4 percent (Planck plus BAO),
3. a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, statistically Gaussian scalar spectrum with a slight red tilt, n_s about 0.965,
4. a acoustic peak structure in the CMB angular power spectrum,
5. a small upper bound on primordial tensors, r_(0.002) less than 0.056 at 95 percent confidence with BK15,
6. an upper bound on isocurvature and on primary non-Gaussianity.

In effect terms the residual is: **the observed pattern P is a projection of an unobserved early-universe process, and the question asks whether P determines that process uniquely or only constrains the shape of any process that could produce it.**

- Option A: P is a one-to-one signature. Then no alternative could reproduce P, and the pattern itself names inflation.
- Option B: P is many-to-one. Then the pattern fixes the shape any candidate must have, does not name the route, and the route must be settled by an instrument that reads a quantity on which the routes differ.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): a statement of the shape P, a statement of whether the map from process to P is one-to-one or many-to-one, a list of discriminating quantities, and a refusal condition. The sought-for shape is bound so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The pattern is a many-to-one projection (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*
Fig 13 :123-124: *"Two chains built from different links may compose functionally identical terms"*
Fig 13 :124: *"the same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance."*
Fig 13 :128-129: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

P is the artefact. An artefact carries the atoms and the quantity it reports; it does not carry the route that made it. So "those patterns" do not identify inflation as their source. This is the figure-grounded core of the answer. It is triangulated: Fig 13 gives the many-route law, Fig 12 :117 gives the isomer law at a fixed atom set, and the two are low-overlap in the sense that one is a law about routes and the other a law about composition.

**Mode:** `specified` that P does not discriminate by itself; `general` that artefacts never encode routes.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated one-to-one map from the full observed pattern class to a unique early-universe process, with every other closure excluded by the same pattern. If that exists, Fig 13 :121 and :128-129 are wrong as applied here.

### F2 - The pattern fixes a shape, not a name (triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 9 :30-31: *"describe the effect rather than the cause"*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

Any viable route must reproduce P: near scale invariance, adiabaticity, Gaussianity to current bounds, a red tilt near 0.965, flatness near 0.4 percent, acoustic peaks, low tensors, low isocurvature. That is a shape. No figure line selects inflation, a bounce, string gas, varying light speed or defects. The identity of a working alternative is a `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget rather than as silence.

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a specific early-universe process, or a ground measurement that excludes every non-inflationary closure while agreeing with inflation.

### F3 - n_s and r are filters on model space, never witnesses for a verdict (single figure, world corroborated)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

Planck measures n_s = 0.9649 plus or minus 0.0042 at 68 percent confidence and r_(0.002) less than 0.056 at 95 percent confidence with BK15. Those numbers cut models. They do not vote for a source, because the Planck paper states its model conclusions *"In the framework of single-field inflationary models with Einstein gravity"*. A cut performed inside one closure cannot certify that closure. Seat Map :281: *"An unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."*

**Refuter:** show that a single measured number is sufficient to identify a generative process independently of the model class in which it is interpreted.

### F4 - The instruments are partly one evidence pool (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*.

CMB temperature, polarization, lensing and BAO are different instruments, but they are projections of one realization of one primordial field. Cosmic variance sets an irremovable floor (Kamionkowski and Loeb 1997, opened this session). Their mutual agreement is partial corroboration, not four witnesses to the same source claim. This is why a source-discriminating observable, not more precision on the same projection, is owed.

**Refuter:** a second, genuinely independent realization of the primordial field, or an instrument that reads a different quantity rather than a finer reading of the same one.

### F5 - Working harder inside one paradigm cannot manufacture an alternative (triangulated)

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

An alternative is an import from a frame with a different closure, not a better fit within inflation's model family. Conversely, the historical failure of alternatives to match the data is not a proof that none can. The correct reading is Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

**Refuter:** a proof that a single closure contains every possible process compatible with P. No such proof is in the sources opened.

### F6 - A working alternative is a ground question, not a figure question (gap)

Brandenberger and Peter (2016) write: *"The successful predictions which inflation has made for the spectrum of cosmological perturbations are not specific to inflation."* Brandenberger (2008) writes of *"competing scenarios that also agree with observation."* So the structural possibility of a non-inflationary route is corroborated in the world. But which route, if any, actually matches the full pattern class is settled by data, not by figures. The sources opened report real obstacles: blue tilts, large non-Gaussianity, anisotropy, singularity at the bounce. Fig 13 :134 governs the honest stop.

**Refuter:** a peer-reviewed, source-discriminating measurement that selects one non-inflationary route over inflation, or excludes all non-inflationary routes. The verdict here is `specified` on possibility and `gap` on identity; it is not a claim that any named model works.

### F7 - The route question is second order (single-source on the figure, with the recursion corroborated)

Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*

First order: a process produces P. Second order: two first-order results, each settled against the ground, are compared as molecules now serving as atoms. "Which process" composes over "what pattern". This is why the question cannot be answered by measuring P more precisely: the second-order question needs a different operand, a quantity where the routes differ.

**Refuter:** show that a settled first-order result cannot serve as an atom for the source question, or that no second-order composition exists over the pattern class.

### F8 - The outreach cannot corroborate the claim (single-source on the figure)

Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

A campaign that gathers cosmologists who already favour alternatives, teaches them the framework, and counts their agreement is the named collapse. Stage 1 must carry P and its refuter only. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Does P name its source?** No. Fig 13 :121 and :128-129. Artefact does not carry route.
2. **What shape must any route have?** Near scale invariance, adiabaticity, Gaussianity, red tilt, flatness, acoustic peaks, low tensors and isocurvature. Fig 9 :32. No name follows.
3. **Can search inside inflation find the answer?** No. Closure is idempotent (Fig 8 :32). A different closure is an import (Fig 6 :23).
4. **Do n_s and r decide?** No. Filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
5. **Are the observations independent witnesses?** Partly. They share one realization and one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40; Kamionkowski and Loeb 1997).
6. **What closes the route question?** A source-discriminating instrument on a quantity where routes differ, improved from a worse instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32). Metamerism is the canonical analogue.
7. **Status?** `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the name, ungrounded, candidate. The honest stop is recorded, not hidden.
