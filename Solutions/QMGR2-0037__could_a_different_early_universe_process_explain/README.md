## What the question decides, in effect terms

"Those patterns" are the CMB patterns of Q35 and the density differences of Q36: near-perfect isotropy, spatial flatness, a nearly scale-invariant adiabatic scalar spectrum with a slight red tilt, acoustic peaks, and a small tensor upper limit. The question asks whether that pattern class is a one-to-one signature of inflation, or a many-to-one projection that several early-universe routes could produce.

Option A: the pattern names its source, so the absence of a working alternative is evidence for inflation. Option B: the pattern fixes the shape any source must have but does not identify the source, so a different process is not excluded by the patterns and must be checked on a quantity where the routes differ.

The figures decide B at the level of shape. Fig 13 :121 says *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 13 :128-129 says *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* The pattern is the artefact. It does not carry its route.
