## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether the observed CMB and large-scale-structure patterns identify inflation as their source, or only constrain the shape of any process that could produce them. "Those patterns" are the near-isotropy, flatness, near scale-invariant adiabatic scalar spectrum with a red tilt near 0.965, acoustic peaks, tensor upper limit and isocurvature bounds of Q35 and Q36. Option A: the pattern names its source, so no alternative could reproduce it and the absence of a working alternative counts for inflation. Option B: the pattern is a many-to-one projection, so it fixes a shape but does not name a process, and the process must be checked on a quantity where routes differ. What each leads to: A turns a filter into a verdict and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Yes in the precise sense the figures decide, and not in the stronger sense the question might suggest.** The patterns do not identify their process. Fig 13 :121 and :128-129 give the many-to-one projection; Fig 9 :32 gives the shape-not-a-name limit. Any viable route must reproduce the observed pattern class; inflation and the non-inflationary alternatives are different closures whose two-point predictions can coincide, standing as isomers over the predicted quantity set (Fig 12 :117). The route question is second order (Fig 12 :104) and is closed by a source-discriminating instrument (Fig 6 :32), subject to a cosmic-variance floor that is a budget limit, not underivability in principle (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134). Which alternative, if any, actually works is a `gap`, not a finding. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged: D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` on possibility and `gap` on identity, D3 `general` triangulated. This is ungrounded and remains `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a list of people selected for agreement. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The stronger overstatement, that this establishes a working alternative, is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no cosmological claim is staked.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A one-to-one pattern-to-process map, a higher-order fingerprint encoding the route in the artefact, or a source-discriminating measurement excluding every non-inflationary route while agreeing with inflation would reverse the shape claim. A measurement selecting a named non-inflationary route would move the `gap` from unreached to settled without touching the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
