## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and the next line adds that it *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for QMGR2-0037 a **shape** is the set of properties any early-universe process must have if it is to produce the observed patterns, stated before we can name which process did it. The shape here is: nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, statistically Gaussian, slightly red-tilted, spatially flat, with acoustic peaks, low tensors and low isocurvature. "Some process with those properties" is a shape. "The inflaton with a concave potential" is a name. "A matter bounce with an ekpyrotic phase" is also a name.

Fig 13 :121 is the governing line: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* The substance is the pattern class. The routes are the processes. The shape constrains the substance; it does not select a route.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The pattern is the effect; the process is the cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it"*.

Applied here: describe P in effect terms (isotropy, flatness, tilt, peaks, tensors), do not begin by naming inflation, and let the description itself narrow the field of admissible routes. This is Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles it, *"Verified only by what pays."* A source-discriminating measurement on a quantity where the routes differ.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, the measured tilt n_s about 0.965 is not such a settlement. It is a cut inside a model class. Planck's own conclusion is conditional: *"In the framework of single-field inflationary models with Einstein gravity"*, the slow-roll models with concave potentials are favoured. A cut inside one closure does not name a source (Fig 9 :60).

## 4. Why "the pattern identifies the process" is the metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The cross-field corroboration opened this session is exact. The University of East Anglia Colour Group defines metamerism: *"Metamerism is the phenomenon whereby surfaces which, though their underlying physical properties are different, are perceived by an observer (for example, the human visual system or a colour camera) to be the same colour."* And: *"it is not possible for us to uniquely identify the physical properties of that surface but rather, there exist many surfaces consistent with the response."* RP Photonics adds the instrument half: the eye has three receptor types while a spectrometer has hundreds or thousands of channels, so a richer instrument can separate sources that a trichromatic reading merges.

Here the CMB power spectrum is the trichromatic reading. It reports a few numbers (tilt, amplitude, tensor bound, isocurvature bound). Inflation and several non-inflationary routes can produce the same numbers. Naming the source from those numbers is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument, exactly as Fig 6 :32 says: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Fig 13 :128-129 is the same claim in the framework's own language: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

## 5. Q: Does the framework name the process that produced the patterns?

**Answered: no.** The framework narrows the shape and refuses the name. A specific process is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The status is `gap`, which is a verdict (Fig 13 :134), not silence and not an invitation to invent a winner.

### 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4, kept visible

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket ban on naming any specific structure. The canonical phrase is about a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an atom already held is a binding from another frame and is provenance work, not the invention of a new primitive.

For this question the correction does **not** change the answer. The route (inflation versus bounce versus string gas) is not held in the six-term library, so the limit applies to it exactly: naming a route from the pattern is a name where only a shape is available. But the correction matters for `06`: writing a cosmology in the vocabulary of space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network is a binding, not a derivation, and it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous cosmologists to agree that alternatives are plausible is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let an instrument settle the route.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the cosmology and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
