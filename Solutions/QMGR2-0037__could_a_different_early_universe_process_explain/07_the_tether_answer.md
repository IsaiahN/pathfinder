# The Tether's answer: could a different early-universe process explain those patterns without inflation?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which process actually did it. NOT `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Those patterns" are the CMB patterns of Q35 and the density seeds of Q36: near-isotropy, flatness to about 0.4 percent, a nearly scale-invariant adiabatic scalar spectrum with a red tilt near n_s = 0.965, acoustic peaks, a tensor upper limit r_(0.002) below 0.056 with BK15, and bounds on isocurvature and non-Gaussianity. The question asks whether this pattern class is a unique signature of inflation, or a projection that several processes could produce.

The contact is a **union of observables**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). It has its own ground and its own loop. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a single "tension" score.

## 2. The shape

1. **The patterns do not name their source.** Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 13 :128-129: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* The pattern is the artefact. It carries the measured quantities, not the history that made them.
2. **Any viable route must reproduce the same shape.** Near scale invariance, adiabaticity, Gaussianity to current bounds, a slight red tilt, flatness, acoustic peaks, low tensors, low isocurvature. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The framework returns this shape and refuses to select a route.
3. **Inflation and the alternatives are different closures, not one mechanism with parameter settings.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Searching harder inside inflation's model family cannot manufacture an alternative (Fig 8 :32). The absence of a found alternative is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
4. **Over the predicted quantity set they are isomers in prediction space.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Expansion plus slow roll, or contraction plus bounce, are different bonds delivering the same two-point numbers. The difference shows up in higher-order and tensor observables. In the framework's strict six-term atom set they are not isomers at all, but different closures whose projections coincide. Both readings are recorded, not merged.
5. **The source question is second order.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. The pattern is settled at first order; "which process" composes over it and needs a second operand.
6. **The gap is closed by an instrument, not by an argument.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Discriminators named in the opened literature: tensor amplitude (ekpyrotic predicts very low tensors), tensor and scalar tilt (string gas predicts a blue scalar tilt), non-Gaussianity amplitude and bispectrum shape, isocurvature. Fig 9 :60: n_s and r are *"filters for the budget"* until they are read as source-discriminating measurements between two stated routes.
7. **There is an instrument floor.** CMB and large-scale structure are partly one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40); cosmic variance limits what one realization can say. That is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that inflation is wrong, and not that inflation is confirmed. Both are verdicts the figures do not issue.
- Not that a bounce, ekpyrotic, cyclic, pre-big-bang, string-gas, varying-light-speed or defect model has been shown to work. The world sources report both candidate mechanisms and live obstacles (blue tilts, non-Gaussianity, anisotropy, the bounce singularity).
- Not that the six terms classify early-universe models. Fig 13 governs the minimum set any composed system needs. Binding a model onto an atom is provenance, not derivation (Fig 6 :21).
- Not a derivation of one theory from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No cosmological measurement was taken in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*. The cross-field corroboration opened this session is metamerism: physically different spectra produce the same trichromatic response, and a spectrometer with more channels separates them.

Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Tensor test:** does a primordial tensor background exist at a level that excludes the low-tensor alternatives, or is the bound tightened further toward zero?
- **Tilt test:** is the scalar tilt red at the measured value in a way that excludes blue-tilt routes such as simple string gas, and is the tensor tilt measured?
- **Bispectrum test:** does the non-Gaussianity amplitude and shape match slow-roll or the order-unity shape predicted by bounce models?
- **Adiabaticity test:** do isocurvature constraints exclude routes that require entropy modes?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a one-to-one pattern-to-process map; a higher-order fingerprint that encodes the route in the artefact; a discriminator that excludes all non-inflationary routes while agreeing with inflation.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops where the instruments stop separating sources, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical figures README). A different process *could* explain those patterns, in the precise sense that the patterns do not exclude one; this is not the same as saying one has been shown to do so. The specific identity of a working alternative is a `gap` reported as a verdict, not a silence and not a placeholder for a name.
