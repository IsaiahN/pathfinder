## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the pattern, the many-to-one claim and the refuter. The local figure paths come later.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A mass send selected for people who already favour alternatives produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer; sending a recording of one conclusion does.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) the question, what would you count as a failure and which discriminator would you reach for; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the local figure paths, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
