## DERIVATION

### Q1: What is the structure of alpha?

- Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**
- Fig 13 :76: **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**
- In the physics frame alpha is e-squared over h-bar c: a coupling crossed with the quantum of action and the invariant speed. In the framework's vocabulary this is a **threshold relation** between quantities that already carry states: the Operators table defines ⋛ as **"a threshold relation between two quantities."** Fig 12 :86-89 gives the identifying test: *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*
- So alpha is a ⋛ composition over quantities at a level, not a new member of the six. This is the same structural form as the running coupling read at two energy scales: NIST reports 1/137 at zero energy and about 1/128 at the W mass, which is a **gradient over the energy state**, not a change in the primitive set.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** show that alpha cannot be written as a comparison of quantities at any level, or that it requires a state-state or structure-structure bond with no intervening opposite-kind term. Then the composition reading fails.

### Q2: Are the couplings isomers or a functional group?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- As currently written, alpha (e, h-bar, c), the strong coupling alpha_s (g3, h-bar, c), and the gravitational coupling alpha_G (G, h-bar, c) do **not** share all atoms: they differ in the coupling operand. By Fig 12's definition they are **not isomers**; they are members of the same **functional group**, "dimensionless coupling = a cross-kind ratio of a coupling to the quantum-relativistic scale", with different operands in the slot.
- The stronger version: if all couplings are written over the **full shared atom set** permitted by a unification frame, differing only in which structure mediates which state pair, then they **are** isomers, same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. Uzan 2011 states the physics version: the couplings may be related to **"a unique coupling constant alpha_U and an energy scale of unification M_U"** through running, which is exactly a shared atom set with different arrangement. Barontini et al. 2022 add that in grand unified models **"shifts in, e.g., the fine structure constant alpha and the coupling constant of quantum chromodynamics alpha_s are related."**
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if any coupling cannot be written with the same atom set as alpha (for example, if it needs an atom alpha does not have), the isomer claim fails and only the functional-group claim stands. This is the same test the exemplar used for GR and QM.

### Q3: What is the order of the union?

- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**
- The measured value of alpha is settled against the ground by two independent instruments (NIST). It can therefore serve as an atom at the next level. The **running** of alpha is a second-order composition over that settled atom: it is legal *because* the low-energy value settled. This is the recursion Fig 12 names, not an import that adds a primitive.
- The possible **drift** in time is a different order again: it compares two settled readings separated by a cosmological baseline, so it is a ⋛ relation over second-order molecules-now-atoms. Fig 13 :76 makes the two distinct: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Running (over energy) and drifting (over cosmic time) must never be read as one effect. That is the F9 round-trip strain in `01`.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** settle the value first, then compose the running, then ask about drift. A drift search that does not hold the value fixed cannot interpret its own reading.

## CONCLUSION

The measured alpha is a ⋛ comparison over quantities at a level, not a primitive and not a seventh kind. The dimensionless couplings as currently written are a **functional group**, not isomers: they share the form, not all the atoms. The stronger isomer claim (all couplings over a shared unification atom set, arranged differently) is real and has a stated falsifier. The running of alpha is a second-order composition over a settled value; a possible drift is a different second-order composition over cosmic time, and the two must stay separate. Nothing here derives 1/137.036, and nothing here is `grounded`.
