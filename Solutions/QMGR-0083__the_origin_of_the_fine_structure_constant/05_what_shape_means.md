## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a dimensionless coupling is a derived remainder left by a composition across the two kinds, at the intersection of independently introduced scales. Its value is supplied by the ground.
- **Not a shape:** the number 1/137.036. That is a reading, not a constraint.
- **Also not a shape:** "alpha varies" as a bare verdict. The shape of the constancy question is a role question, anchor or channel, settled by an instrument that does not move when frames push on it.

The distinction is echoed in *How Intelligence Actually Works*: a replicator is *"a tool that provides the shape of something that you described"*, and the recursion converges until the result is indiscernible, but *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape that fits every reading is not the reading's source.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrapping by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The vocabulary used for alpha was dimensionless ratio, running coupling, boundary condition, and threshold. Each narrows without supplying the number.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* We describe the effect of a coupling (it sets the splitting of spectral lines and the probability of photon emission) before picking a frame to explain it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For R2 this is the operational form: what would a drift look like in a ratio of two clock frequencies with different sensitivity coefficients?
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, and *"by the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For alpha, the honest stop is earlier: a shape is reached, the number is not.

## 3. What licenses a name or a value

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* NIST's two independent determinations settle the *value* at the current precision. Nothing in the six-term set settles it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Sticker 2026 is a candidate import of a shape, not of a value, and it is recorded as such in the ledger.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"Outputs do not contain their generator."* The measured 1/137 is an output of a theory; it does not contain the generator of that number. And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the six *"does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the mapping of alpha onto cross-kind composition is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why one reading does not give the source: metamerism

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: the reading 1/137.036 is compatible with a low-energy boundary condition of a unified theory, a moduli expectation value, an anthropic selection, or a numerological coincidence (the sources are listed in Barontini et al. 2022 and Buchanan 2010). The reading does not discriminate among them. Uzan 2011 adds that when a dimensionless change is detected, *"it is impossible to determine, which dimensional constant is varying."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. Why constancy is a ground question, not a consensus question

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* The SI second is defined by the caesium hyperfine interval, so most clock frequencies are already mutually referenced. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* restates the test: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*, so a clock's ability to detect drift is a property of the clock and its reference habitat, not of the clock in isolation.

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it"* is the essay's own quotation of Fig 2 :45. Two teams that never talk but calibrate to the same caesium standard are panel 2 of Fig 2, not two witnesses.

So the shape of R2 is: an unmoving reference against which differently-sensitised instruments range separately. A drift result is credible only if the instruments differ in their alpha sensitivity and neither can move the other's reference.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous names agreeing that alpha is constant is the named collapse, not corroboration. A clock ratio is the budget to try; the ground settles it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture article warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids stating any structure or number at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Here the value is not a missing primitive; it is a measurement. What the rule forbids is generating the number by argument from the six terms. What it permits is stating a shape (a derived cross-kind ratio) and recording the number as a ground reading with provenance. Both readings are kept visible: the narrow rule is applied in F2 and F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, and the overstatement is retracted here rather than silently erased.
