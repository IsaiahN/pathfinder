## VERDICT (from the figures)

The two sub-questions keep separate verdicts and are never averaged. **Origin:** the framework returns a shape, a dimensionless remainder of a cross-kind composition at the intersection of independent scales. It does not return 1/137.036; the value is a `gap` owed to a ground measurement or a provenanced import. **Constancy:** the framework's ground/channel rule makes this a role question. Current instruments give stable at instrument resolution, candidate, not grounded. Agreement among correlated clocks is one witness. The dimensionless couplings are a functional group as written, with a real isomer claim over a shared atom set, and the running of alpha and a possible cosmic drift are different second-order compositions that must stay separate. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states both residuals, the refuter and the mode before any theory, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is collapsing the two loops, or spending readers' independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, both of which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The number is never asserted, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. A drift detection would be recorded as new ground evidence and would relocate alpha from anchor to channel; a first-principles derivation would close the `gap` and upgrade the value verdict. A superseding packet can replace this one without retraction of the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A derivation of 1/137.036 from the six-term set alone with no fitted boundary condition would close the value gap; a confirmed drift in two instruments with different alpha sensitivities would break the stable-at-resolution verdict; a proof that variation is impossible in principle would close the ground; a source-discriminating instrument would name the source of the value; a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the substrate. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
