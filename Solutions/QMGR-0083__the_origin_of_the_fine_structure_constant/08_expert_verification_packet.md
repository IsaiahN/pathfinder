# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the fine-structure constant

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or metrologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the Planck scale or in a clock comparison is claimed by this agent. The numeric value and any drift are ground questions.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Alpha is not a primitive of the six-term substrate. It is a dimensionless quantity left by a composition across the two kinds, at the intersection of independently introduced scales; the framework gives that shape and does not produce 1/137.036. The dimensionless couplings as written are a functional group rather than isomers, with a real isomer claim if they are written over a shared atom set. The running of alpha with energy and any drift in cosmic time are different second-order compositions and must not be conflated. Constancy is an anchor or channel role question: current instruments give stable at resolution, candidate, not grounded. Agreement among clocks calibrated to one standard is one witness unless their alpha sensitivities differ.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 9

- **Statement:** a dimensionless coupling is a derived cross-kind remainder, not a primitive; the framework supplies a shape for its origin, not the number.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :43-44 *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- **What to check:** does the six-term set contain any dimensionless pure number as a primitive? Does any rule in the figure text produce a numeric value for a coupling? The answer predicted here is no to both.
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five, or derive 1/137.036 from the six alone with no fitted boundary condition and no imported atom, or show the figure text does produce the number. Any one fires C1.

### C2: physics-facing; checkable by a physicist or metrologist

- **Statement:** the running of alpha with energy is not the same quantity as a possible drift in time, and the dimensionless couplings are a functional group, not isomers as currently written.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :76 *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Fig 12 :124 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** NIST reports alpha at zero energy approximately 1/137 and at the W mass approximately 1/128. Is that a different effect from a change in the low-energy value over cosmological time? Do alpha and the strong coupling share all their atoms, or only the functional form? The claim predicts: different effects, and a shared form with different operands.
- **Refuter:** show the two effects are one quantity (which collapses the order distinction), or show the couplings share a full atom set in the current theoretical frame (which upgrades the functional group to isomers and changes the structural verdict).

### C3: verification-facing; checkable against any review of constant-variation bounds

- **Statement:** constancy is an anchor or channel role question, and agreement among correlated clocks is one witness, not several.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 2 :31-32 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **What to check:** are the clock constraints on alpha derived from frequency ratios with different sensitivity coefficients, or are they all referenced to the same caesium hyperfine interval? Do the quoted limits (order 10^-17 per year from clocks, order 10^-7 over two billion years from Oklo, disputed quasar results) support "stable at resolution" rather than "constant in principle"?
- **Refuter:** a confirmed, systematics-free drift seen by two instruments with different sensitivities (which shows alpha was a channel), or a proof that variation is impossible in principle (which closes the ground), or a demonstration that two supposedly independent constraints have identical alpha sensitivity and a shared calibration chain (which reduces their agreement to one pool).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a derivation of 1/137.036, and not a numerological fit to 137.
- Not a proof that alpha is constant. A null search is not a proof of absence.
- Not a claim that the framework names the source of the value. It returns a shape, not a name.
- Not a claim that this agent took a measurement. All numbers here are quoted, with provenance below.
- Not `grounded`. The verdicts are `specified` or `general` and remain `candidate` until a ground settles them.
- Not a claim that agreement among physicists corroborates the shape. Correlated agreement is one witness.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement:

1. A derivation of the low-energy value from the six-term set alone, with no fitted boundary condition.
2. A source-discriminating instrument that separates a boundary condition from a moduli value, an anthropic selection, and chance.
3. A systematics-free drift of alpha in two instruments with different sensitivity coefficients.
4. A proof that variation is impossible in principle.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :74 and :43-44, Fig 9 :32, Fig 10 :24 and Fig 8 :39. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against the NIST article: is the running from 1/137 to about 1/128 a different effect from a cosmological drift?
3. Check the clock references in Barontini et al. 2022 or Uzan 2011: which constraints actually use different alpha sensitivities?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate the competing sources of the value, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
