# The Tether's answer on the fine-structure constant

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two residuals, kept in separate loops because they have different grounds (Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*):

- **Origin:** why does a pure number take the value 1/137.036, and is that value derivable or only measurable?
- **Constancy:** does the number drift in time or vary in space, and what would count as a real detection?

Each is answered below. Neither answer uses the other as evidence.

## 2. The shape

1. **Alpha is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* A dimensionless coupling is not one of the six states or structures. It is a quantity left by a composition across the kinds, at the intersection of independently introduced scales.
2. **The framework gives the shape of the origin, not the number.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"a shape rather than a name."* So the answer to "why this value" is: a derived cross-kind ratio whose value is supplied by the ground or by a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs. The exact number is a `gap` here. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."
3. **The couplings are a functional group, with a real isomer claim behind it.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* As written, alpha and the strong coupling share the form but not all the atoms, so they are members of a **functional group** (Fig 12 :124: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*). If they are written over a shared unification atom set, differing only in arrangement, they become Fig 12's isomers. That is a checkable claim with a stated falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
4. **Running and drifting are different orders and must stay separate.** NIST gives 1/137 at zero energy and about 1/128 at the W mass; that is a gradient over the energy state. A cosmological change, if any, is a comparison of settled readings across a time baseline. Fig 13 :76: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Conflating the two is the standard error in this field.
5. **Constancy is an anchor or channel role question, not a consensus question.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* If alpha drifts, it was a channel, not an anchor. The current verdict is **stable at instrument resolution, candidate, not grounded**: clock bounds near 10^-17 per year, Oklo near 10^-7 over two billion years, quasar results disputed.
6. **Agreement is not the ground.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Ten clocks on one caesium reference are one witness. Independence comes from different alpha sensitivities, not different laboratory names.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a derivation of 1/137.036. No number is produced here.
- Not a proof that alpha is constant. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not a claim that a numerological fit to 137 explains anything. Such a fit predicts no other quantity.
- Not a claim that the framework names the source of the value. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken by this agent; the cited measurements belong to their authors.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

1. **The value:** a derivation from the six-term set alone with no fitted boundary condition, or a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test. A numerological fit does not fire this.
2. **The source of the value:** a source-discriminating instrument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved that case. Here the competing sources are a low-energy boundary condition, a moduli expectation value, an anthropic selection, and chance.
3. **The drift:** a confirmed, systematics-free variation of alpha seen by two instruments with different sensitivity coefficients, neither of which can move the other's reference. A single disputed quasar dataset does not fire it.
4. **Standing falsifiers:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a seventh primitive to hold alpha; prove variation impossible in principle; show one instrument settles both sub-residuals.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** From *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the six makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape that fits every reading is still not the source of the reading.

The round-trip gap `R_T` from condensing the derivation into a slogan is nonzero and is enumerated in `11_audit.md`. Treating "alpha is a derived cross-kind ratio" as identical to the full derivation would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not derive the fine-structure constant and does not claim alpha is constant in principle. It says two things about it, in separate loops. First, alpha is not a primitive: it is a dimensionless remainder left by a composition across two kinds of term, at the intersection of independently introduced scales, and the framework returns that shape rather than the number 1/137.036. The number is a ground reading or a provenanced import, not something the six terms generate. Second, constancy is a role question. An anchor does not move when frames push on it; a channel decays. Current clocks, the Oklo reactor and quasar spectra show alpha stable at their resolution, not constant in principle, and agreement among clocks calibrated to one caesium reference is one witness, not several. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The value's source stays open until the ground settles it.
