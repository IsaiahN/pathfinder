## 0. The residual in effect terms

The measured quantity is a pure number, alpha approximately 1/137.036. It sets the strength of the electromagnetic coupling. Two things are owed, and they have different grounds:

- **R1, origin.** Why this value? From what composition does a dimensionless coupling arise, and is the value derivable or only measurable?
- **R2, constancy.** Does the number stay fixed in time and space, or does it drift? What would count as a genuine detection rather than a systematic?

In effect terms the two sub-residuals are:

> R1: a pure number is in the library but its value is not explained by any term held in the library.
> R2: a reading used as a reference may itself be a channel that changes, and nothing in the reading tells us which role it plays.

R1 and R2 are **a union, not a single hard question**. Fig 9 :54-55: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again. A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The Library of Babel essay asks the same question directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* R1 needs a derivation or an import from a more fundamental theory. R2 needs a chronometer or a spectrometer. A lever that clears one leaves the other standing.

This unbundling is the first finding, and it is recorded before any figure is chosen as a verdict.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** The quantity alpha is one scalar; the question carries two sub-residuals (R1, R2). One scalar, two conditions, so a union compound with two loops.
- **Symmetry.** Alpha is dimensionless. It is invariant under change of unit system, which is exactly why a variation in it can be measured at all. Uzan 2011: *"This implies that only the variation of dimensionless constants can be measured and in case such a variation is detected, it is impossible to determine, which dimensional constant is varying."* The value is not symmetric under change of energy scale: it *runs* (NIST; Jentschura and Nandori 2014). Unit invariance and scale dependence are different symmetries, so they are recorded separately.
- **Scale.** The number first appears at first order, as Sommerfeld's relativistic correction to the Bohr atom. Its possible drift is read at three instrument scales: laboratory clocks (years), geochemical and meteoritic records (billions of years), and quasar absorption spectra (cosmological redshift). These are different instruments, not three readings of one instrument.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The question is a union of two residuals with different grounds (structural, `general`)

Fig 9 :54-55: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The Library of Babel essay: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

R1 (origin of the value) and R2 (constancy over time) do not share an instrument. A successful first-principles derivation would not tell us whether alpha drifted; a null clock result would not give the value. Searching them as one question produces exactly the "one hard problem" illusion Fig 9 warns about.

**Refuter:** show a single instrument or derivation that settles both sub-residuals at once, so that a result on one fixes the other. Until then, keep two loops and two verdicts.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 union rule plus the essay's malformed-question test; low functional overlap). This is a shape claim, not a world claim.

### F2: Alpha is not a primitive; a cross-kind composition is required (decisive for D1, `specified`)

Fig 13 :41: **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**

The canonical article's substrate section adds: *"the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."*

Alpha is a pure dimensionless ratio of a coupling to the quantum-relativistic scale. It is not one of the six states or structures, and it fails the indivisibility test: it is composed of e, h-bar and c in the physics frame. So the framework does not need an eighth primitive to hold it. What the framework can give is the **shape** of its origin: a derived quantity left by a composition across kinds, at the intersection of independently introduced scales. This is independently stated in the world literature by Sticker 2026: *"alpha is a scaled quantity that arises only where the structural scales contributed by classical electromagnetism (e), quantum mechanics (h-bar), and special relativity (c) intersect. None of these theories, taken individually, supplies the independent scales required to define alpha."*

**Refuter:** derive alpha as a seventh member of the six-term set (breaks Fig 13 indivisibility), or show the six-term set alone yields the exact low-energy value 1/137.036 without any measured boundary condition (closes the shape claim and the value gap at once).

**Status:** `triangulated` across two figure lines with low functional overlap (substrate minimality and derived-remainder rule). The world statement from Sticker is a separate author and a separate field, but it is a single source and it is corroboration only, never the verdict.

### F5: Constancy is an anchor question, and the ground does not decay (`specified` for D3)

Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**
Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does."**

R2 asks whether alpha can play the anchor role. That is a legitimate anchor question, because the question is constitutively about a reference that does not move. The framework's rule separates two possibilities:

- If alpha is part of the ground, it does not change because frames think about it. A measured drift would relocate it to the channel, which decays.
- If alpha is a channel, a drift is expected and the constancy verdict was an artefact of short baselines.

The world literature makes the same structural point in different vocabulary. Uzan 2011: *"Any constant varying in space and/or time would reflect the existence of an almost massless field that couples to matter."* Barontini et al. 2022: *"In these models all fundamental constants are assumed to be immutable in space and time and to have had the same value throughout the history of the universe. Challenging this central assumption could be the key to solving the dark matter and dark energy enigmas."*

Current measurements: clock comparisons constrain present-day drift at the 10^-17 per year level; Oklo constrains change over two billion years at roughly 10^-7; quasar results are disputed. So the verdict is **stable within current instrument resolution, not proven constant in principle**. That is `candidate`, not `grounded`.

**Refuter:** a confirmed, systematics-free variation of alpha in at least two independent instrument types, or a proof that variation is impossible in principle. Either would break the "open ground" verdict. A single disputed quasar dataset does not fire it.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 anchor rule and Fig 10 channel rule; low overlap). World agreement is corroboration only.

### F7: The dependency chain breaks at the objective, not at perception (`specified`)

**Refuter:** exhibit an instrument whose explicit objective is to derive the value, returning a number that discriminates among candidate derivations. If no such instrument can be specified, the break stands.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the exemplar's Seat Map note: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* Written here before the conclusion of this audit:

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

If any fires, the affected claim is withdrawn and the correction is recorded here without erasing the earlier reading. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 8. Verdict

R1: the framework gives a **shape** for the origin of alpha, a dimensionless remainder of a cross-kind composition at the intersection of independently introduced scales. It does **not** give the number. The value is a `gap` here, owed to a ground measurement or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs. That is a verdict, not a silence.

Current instruments give **stable at resolution, candidate, not grounded**: clock drift bounds near 10^-17 per year, Oklo near 10^-7 over two billion years, quasar results disputed. Agreement among correlated clocks is one witness, not two.

Both verdicts are `specified` or `general`, never `grounded`. The derivation is simulated, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
