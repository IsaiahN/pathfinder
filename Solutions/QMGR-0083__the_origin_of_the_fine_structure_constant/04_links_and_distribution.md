## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Two independent determinations of the same number.** NIST reports the electron magnetic moment anomaly and the quantum Hall effect as independent routes that corroborate the value. Under Fig 2 :31-32 these are two instruments pointed at the same thing, so their agreement is real, but it is still a measurement of the value, not a derivation of it.
2. **The running coupling is not the same as a drift in time.** NIST and Uzan both distinguish the energy dependence of alpha (1/137 at zero energy, about 1/128 at the W or Z mass) from a possible cosmological change. Conflating the two is the most common error in this field and is why F9 records a nonzero round-trip gap.
3. **Constraint hierarchy.** Uzan 2011 and Barontini et al. 2022 agree on the shape of the evidence: laboratory clock ratios give the tightest present-day bounds (order 10^-17 per year), Oklo gives roughly 10^-7 over two billion years, and quasar absorption results are disputed. These are not three measurements of one quantity; they are three instruments with different baselines and different systematics.
4. **The source of the value is still open.** Jentschura and Nandori review a century of first-principles attempts; Sticker proposes that alpha is a scaled quantity arising only where three independent structural scales intersect. Sticker is a single recent source and is treated as corroboration of the shape, never as the verdict.
5. **Numerological fits are not derivations.** Buchanan 2010 catalogues formulae close to 1/137. The Library of Babel essay's warning applies: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* A fit that predicts no other number has cut the search short the wrong way.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52:** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two sub-residuals and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter, and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their stage-1 verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. The stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` states exactly what would break it. A failure reported at stage 1 is more useful than an endorsement at stage 3.
