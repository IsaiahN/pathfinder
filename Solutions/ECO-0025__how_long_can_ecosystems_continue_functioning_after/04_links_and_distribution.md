## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a review of extinction debt before anyone has described their own residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would expect to see if the claim were true before showing the figure text.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Do not select readers for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* A long list of journals is a distribution channel, not a verdict machine.
- **Fig 10 :59** — declare the author's stake; an undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as the crossing moment and what instrument would report it at the time; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the figure text and the two open journal links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the figure text and the two open articles. Their agreement after reading the theory is never added to the stage-1 verdict (Fig 2 :40). There is no request for trust, no "true answer" claim and no bulk follow-up.

**Stage 3, if asked.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse (Fig 4 :22), with the instruction that each user states their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and labels the mode of what they get. The Formula's "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; a written refuter is the discipline that makes the preference refusable.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a shape and a `gap`, not a duration. If a recipient wants a number, say plainly that none is returned and that inventing one would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
