## What is deliberately not here

- No personal email addresses of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan and no request for trust or endorsement.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not give you a ground.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). A list of readers chosen because they already accept an extinction-debt framing is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Most of the literature on this question cites the same small set of founding papers. Agreement inside that pool is one witness, not many.
3. **Sending the theory first spends the reader's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: harvested personal emails and unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR / CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and would get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The one-line result, for orientation only

The figures do not return a number of years. They return a shape: the lag is a
**channel delay** measured per species-and-function slot, gated by a threshold,
carried by the interaction bonds, and sitting in a union of overlapping
conditions. The universal "how long" is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict
and not a silence (Fig 13 :134). Read `07_the_tether_answer.md` for the
argument and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` for the checkable form.
