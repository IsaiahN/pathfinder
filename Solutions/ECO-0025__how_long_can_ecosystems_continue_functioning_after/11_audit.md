## 4. Round trip gap R_T

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

The shorthand says "channel"; the concrete case must distinguish four sources that give one reading.
- the **bond type** between redundancy and function. The shorthand says "carried by bonds"; the concrete case must decide ∥ versus +, which is a different term with a different crossing time.
- the **cascade endpoint**, the last function to cross rather than the first species to disappear. The shorthand says "union"; the concrete case must enumerate until the cascade stops mattering.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must be checked per slot, and a threshold must be named before a duration is reported. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
