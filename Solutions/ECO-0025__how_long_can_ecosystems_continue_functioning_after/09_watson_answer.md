## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a requested duration: how long an ecosystem keeps functioning after a key species falls below the abundance at which it performs its role reliably. Option A: report a universal relaxation time as a property of the ecosystem. Option B: report a per-slot shape, a union of conditions and a declared `gap` on the number. What each leads to: A gives a portable number that hides the live slot signal and invites a deadline no instrument supports; B gives separable, checkable verdicts, seven refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at the instrument limit.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what current species abundances predict for a function and what the function reading still shows. Arity: at least two conditions, actor demography and a separately measured function, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: threshold comparison ⋛, ordered decline and recovery, not a symmetric sum. Scale: individuals and populations, interaction network, function reading, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4 :27).
- **D1, population and life-stage frame.** Actors: species, populations, individuals, dormant stages, propagules. Currency: abundance, demographic rates, generation time. Anchor: a field count that does not change because a model predicted it. Domain admitted: yes, anchor nameable.
- **D2, function and threshold frame.** Actors: pollination, decomposition, nutrient retention, primary production, the ⋛ comparison. Currency: function rate versus required level. Anchor: a direct function assay, not a species-presence proxy. Domain admitted: yes.
- **D3, network and bond frame.** Actors: species as atoms, interactions as typed bonds, guilds, modules. Currency: arrangement, redundancy, cascade length. Anchor: an observed interaction record. Domain admitted: yes.
- **D4, channel and observation frame.** Actors: indicators, monitors, stale readings, pooled samples. Currency: lag between contact change and reading change. Anchor: the ground, which does not move (Fig 10 :24). Domain admitted: yes.
- **D5, maintainer and management frame.** Actors: managers, restorers, reintroducers. Currency: introductions, restoration, monitoring. Anchor: the same function ground. Domain admitted: yes.
- **Partition or union:** union. The five conditions overlap, so one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** non-identical abundance and function readings first; terms for what a species affords a function second; a threshold objective third, where the chain breaks (Fig 3 :27); intervention fourth; carry a verified method fifth (Fig 4 :22).

## WORLD (secondary, corroboration only)

- **Theoretical ecology.** Tilman, May, Lehman and Nowak 1994, Nature 371:65-66. Quote: *"Because such extinctions occur generations after fragmentation, they represent a debt"*. Predicts a delayed, deterministic loss driven by competition and colonization. Corroborates the channel-lag shape at the most general level. Overlap: the root of nearly every other source, so one witness.
- **Macroecology / model comparison.** Halley et al. 2016, Nature Communications 7:12283. Quote: *"The timescales for this to happen have been reported to range from a few years to thousands of years."* Also power-law decay with *"extinctions are distributed more evenly across timescales rather than dominated by a single timescale."* Predicts no single clock; half-life rises with area while first extinction can occur in under ten years. Corroborates the per-slot and union verdicts. Overlap: cites Tilman and Kuussaari, shared pool.
- **Whole-ecosystem experiments.** Haddad et al. 2015, Science Advances 1:e1500052, open PMC full text. Quote: *"Loss of function amounted to 30% after 1 year, rising to 80% after a decade in small and isolated fragments"*. Predicts a function debt distinct from extinction debt and immigration lag, with strong size and isolation dependence. Corroborates F1, F3 and F10. Overlap: independently designed experiments, one of the two genuinely low-overlap witnesses.
- **Field comparison, multiple trophic levels.** Krauss et al. 2010, Ecology Letters 13:597-605, open PMC full text. Quote: *"short-lived butterfly specialists showed no evidence for an extinction debt at a time scale of c. 40 years"*. Predicts different debts for long-lived plants and short-lived butterflies at the same sites. Corroborates the per-slot verdict and refutes any single duration. Overlap: different design from Haddad, the other low-overlap witness.
- **Functional extinction and cascade.** Anderson et al. 2011, Science 331:1068-1071. Quote: *"Mainland sites have similar adult densities, but 55% fewer juvenile plants per adult"*. Predicts threshold crossing while adults persist. Corroborates the threshold verdict and the catalyst-like reading. Overlap: shared with the next row.
- **Perspective on the same case.** Sekercioglu 2011, Science 331:1019-1020. Quote: *"hundreds of species now contribute negligibly to ecosystem function and are 'functionally extinct'"*. Predicts that functional extinction is a threshold on contribution, not on presence. Corroborates F2. Overlap: one case with Anderson, not counted twice.
- **Network ecology.** Blanchard and Munoz 2023, Oikos 2023:e09435 (abstract only, full text paywalled). Quote: *"the structure of interaction networks and the contrasting tempo of extinction in species, function and interaction can determine co-extinction debts scenarios."* Predicts cascading delayed co-extinction across networks. Corroborates C3. Overlap: reduced weight, read at abstract level.
- **Scales and mechanisms review.** Figueiredo et al. 2019, Ecography 42:1973-1990 (abstract and Box 1 via public index). Quote: *"Our review demonstrates an increasing effort to understand the mechanisms involved in extinction debts across systems and scales."* Predicts hierarchical response by organizational level. Corroborates the union verdict. Overlap: shared Kuussaari/Tilman pool, reduced weight.
- **Detection methods.** Ridding et al. 2021, Ecography 44:33-43 (open PDF excerpt). Corroborates the instrument claim. Overlap: reduced weight.
- **Mechanism review.** Hylander and Ehrlén 2013, Trends in Ecology & Evolution 28:341-346 (abstract via public index). Corroborates the union. Overlap: shared pool, reduced weight.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

There is no single duration. The figure-derived answer is a shape with five domain verdicts kept separate: the residual is per species-slot and per function-slot; functioning is a ⋛ threshold on an independently measured function; the observed lag is channel decay, not a change in the ground; roles are residual-relative and no standing key-species list survives a new residual; the typed bond arrangement makes same-list sites isomers with different possible lags; contact cascades outward to the last function to cross; and management is permanent, asymmetric work. The universal number is a declared `gap`, a verdict and not a silence. Unreachable here means unreachable from a figure set that returns shapes and from presence-only monitoring that cannot discriminate sources, not unreachable in principle. Mode `specified` on the domain claims, `general` on the threshold form, `gap` on the number, never `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its refuters and its lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is giving a potential outside checker a number instead of a discriminator, which Fig 4 :30 prices as dead reckoning and Fig 2 :42 prices as an ungrounded purchase of independence. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the seven refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal-number claim is withdrawn rather than repaired, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. Each domain claim can be dropped independently when its refuter fires; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already contain the corrected form. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A prospective, non-updating threshold assay that returns one duration across two functions, two habitats and two perturbations would break the per-slot verdict; a linear function-abundance relation with no threshold would break the ⋛ verdict; a function reading that updates on a model's prediction would break the anchor; a figure line with a verbatim `Fig N :line` citation that returns a duration would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` with a declared `gap`.
