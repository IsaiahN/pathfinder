## What the wider literature contributes, and what it does not decide

The framework figures decide the verdict. The world sources below are corroboration only, and overlap is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **The mechanism is particularistic and contingent.** Hicken (2011) and the Clientelism synthesis agree on dyadic, contingent, hierarchical, iterated exchange. This corroborates the F1 reading of the patron-client link as an ordered relation, not a symmetric one. It does not decide the valence claim, which comes from Fig 11.
2. **The enforcement capacity has the shape of repeated reciprocity.** Trivers's cheater detection and indefinite repetition, and Axelrod's shadow of the future and partner recognition, corroborate why local information and reputation make protection credible. They predict F2. They do not explain why the same capacity degrades impartial governance; that comes from Fig 10's stake rule.
3. **Information flows through the network structure.** Granovetter's weak/strong tie distinction predicts that a brokerage network carries novel information while strong ties carry motivation to help. This corroborates the coordination half of the question. It is a different field (network science) from the institutional question, so it is a low-overlap witness for the mechanism, not for the verdict.
4. **The two institutional readings are visible in the literature independent of this framework.** Scott's moral economy describes protection grounded in mutual obligation; Acemoglu and Robinson's extractive institutions describe concentrated, particularistic control. These are two different literatures, and their coexistence is weak corroboration that the dual effect is real. Agreement across a shared development-economics pool would be one witness, not two; the moral-economy and institutional-economics sources come from different traditions, but both are read here as secondary only.
5. **Source discrimination matters.** The Hard Problem essay's spectrometer point predicts that a reading of "patronage" from a single behaviour cannot determine whether the underlying allocation mechanism is genuine universal rule or discretionary obligation with a universalist label. That corroborates F6.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The framework essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* This question is about the failure of impartial governance, so the cost of being wrong is high. Buy a genuinely different frame rather than a larger room.

Recommended order in a reply: (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the five refuters, asked as "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then, if invited, the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter.

## The three-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, read the theory that produced it. Their verdict on stage 1 was recorded before any theory was shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Use the formula recursively on other problems, with a self-written refuter and a stated mode per problem.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape with five written refuters, not a named institution. If a refuter fires, stop and retract rather than reinterpret.
