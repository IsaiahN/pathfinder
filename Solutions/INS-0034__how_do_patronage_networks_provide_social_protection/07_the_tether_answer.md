# The Tether's answer on patronage, protection and impartial governance

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Where formal insurance, enforcement and dispute resolution do not reach every household, a personalistic network supplies material help, information and coordination. The same network routes offices, benefits and judgements to insiders. The residual is the co-occurrence, not either effect alone. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The question is answered only if one mechanism explains both effects and the mechanism's failure mode is structural rather than incidental.

## 2. The shape

1. **One actor, two valences.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* The patron is beneficial against the household-insurance counter and harmful against the impartial-application counter. Verdicts stay separate, never averaged (Fig 1 :19).
2. **The protection is credible because enforcement is local, information-rich and repeated.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*; :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* The anchor here is a material counter, per Fig 2 :29-30: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
3. **The same enforcement capacity disqualifies the patron as an impartial anchor.** Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* The discretion that makes a promise credible to a client is the discretion that decides who gets the office. It cannot be both a verdict and a convention.
4. **Internal reform cannot add the missing anchor.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Only an import moves the wall, with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* makes this definitional.
5. **The lever is a shape at the membrane, not a named policy.** Keep the protection counter visible per household; move selection and sanction to a frame whose anchor neither patron nor client can move; measure the round trip (Fig 4 :30). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Any specific institutional design is a binding from the development-policy frame and must be recorded as such.
6. **Provenance is the discriminator.** Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* A universalist rule written by an interested network is not the same object as an imported impartial rule, even if the two produce identical observed allocations.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that patronage is everywhere, universal, or always harmful. This is a structural claim about the cases where the two effects co-occur.
- Not that every network provides protection, or that the state is absent. Fig 9 :54 governs the compound only.
- Not a trade-off on one welfare scale, and not a net-welfare score (Fig 1 :22).
- Not a derivation of any anti-corruption programme, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not that the framework's six-term substrate forbids or requires any institutional form. That would be a mode error (retraction R5 in `01`).
- Not that adding formal rules automatically helps. F6 records the provenance strain.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"it cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the discriminators are the five pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4, restated as measurable questions:

- Can the same relation be scored beneficial on the insurance counter and harmful on the impartiality counter at the same slot and the same time? If yes, F1 fails.
- Can an internal, impartial allocation anchor be built with no import? If yes, F5 fails.
- Does external impartial allocation displace protection one-for-one across every locality, with no residual insurance loss? If yes, F7 fails.
- Can the patron's stake in the value returned be removed while protection is unchanged? If yes, F3 fails.
- Does one lever improve both counters monotonically in every slot, with no intersection residue? If yes, F4 fails.

To these add the standing rule: Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A checker who shares the evidence pool is not a second witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not declare patronage good or bad and does not offer a reform design. It says that the protective effect and the governance-weakening effect are two residuals measured on the same actor: the patron is a benefactor against the household-insurance counter and a malfactor against the impartial-application counter. The capacity that makes the relation credible as insurance, local information, reputation and discretionary sanction, is exactly what disqualifies the patron as an impartial anchor, because an anchor must not update on what the parties think. The compound is a union, not a trade-off, so no single lever clears both conditions; a rule added inside the network may simply carry the network's interest under a universalist name. What the framework returns is a shape: keep the protection counter visible per household, move selection and sanction to a frame whose anchor neither patron nor client can move, and measure what the move costs. It writes down five refuters before the run and stops where the instruments stop. That is all this claims.
