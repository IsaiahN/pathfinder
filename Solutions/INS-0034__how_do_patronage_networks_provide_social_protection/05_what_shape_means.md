## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **A shape:** "one enforcement capacity read against two different anchors, with the lever at the membrane between strata."
- **A name:** "abolish machine politics and replace it with a professional civil service," or "adopt conditional cash transfers." Both are bindings from the development-policy frame, not derivations.

The shape also says what an answer must *not* look like: it cannot be a single net-welfare score, and it cannot be a moral verdict that skips the mechanism.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual in `01` section 0 is written as "did the household eat, was the office routed to an insider," not as "clientelism," "patrimonialism" or "state capture." Those words arrive after the effect is described.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-23): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email leads with observable counters.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a name would be licensed by a measurement in which the protection counter and the impartiality counter are both tracked per slot across an intervention, with an anchor neither patron nor client can move. No such measurement is in this folder. The derivation stops at the shape.

## 4. Why the source question matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: observing that "a public benefit went to a network member" does not determine the source. It could be universal rule applied to an eligible recipient who happens to be a network member (convergent derivation), or discretionary allocation under a universalist label (adopted import). Fig 8 :50-52 says the contents look identical. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The five refuters in `01` section 4 are written as candidate discriminators.

## 5. Correction to my own reading

I initially wrote that the framework's "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming *any* institutional mechanism in this answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in Γ. patronage, insurance, reputation and sanction are not framework primitives at all; they are terms from the institutional frame. Naming them is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new framework atom.

What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* a particular anti-corruption design, or that a particular institutional form is the only one compatible with the six-term set. Those are provenance and closure errors, not a ban on using ordinary words. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous development economists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an audit, a counter, a measured outcome, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape for INS-0034, stated once

> An answer to this question must treat the protective effect and the governance-weakening effect as two residuals measured on one actor, keep their verdicts separate, recognise the compound as a union rather than a trade-off, and locate the lever at the membrane where an anchor neither patron nor client can move is kept reachable. It must not name a specific institution, must not average the two counters, and must state what instrument would settle it. That is a shape. Everything beyond it is a binding from a world frame and must be provenanced as such.
