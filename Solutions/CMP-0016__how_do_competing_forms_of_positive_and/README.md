## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among people who share the same evidence pool is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
3. **Sending the full theory before the verdict spends the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and submission channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

This package is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." It cannot settle the question it reports on.
