## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule is about a missing thing: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". So a shape narrows the form an answer must have; it does not fill the slot.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The essay adds the bundle rule that matters here: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

For CMP-0016 the shape is therefore not "positive feedback makes things grow and negative feedback makes them stable". That is a name and, as F2 in `01` shows, a mislabelling. The shape is: a signed cycle is required; the labels are valences relative to a tracked residual; and the three fates are a bundle to be split before search.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: proximity bootstrapping. Use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The question is the tether: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So describe what amplifying and damping do to a deviation, not what they are called in a given field.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the exact reason the outreach material leads with a checkable effect and a refuter.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question we are not done. The per-system regime remains unsearched at the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a pattern's fate, that means a time series or an intervention, not a modeller's preference.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A regime label borrowed from control theory is an import and must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books* was not reopened, so I make no quoted claim from it here. The general point stands from the canonical rule: declaring a vocabulary does not make it neutral; it makes the choice visible.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism that most constrains this question. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source". Several sources can produce one reading: yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet found. The essay's conclusion is "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing that a pattern grew does not tell you which loop caused the growth. Amplification, redistributed coupling, a boundary change and an external drive can all produce the same time series. Naming "positive feedback" as the source of a growth reading is the metamerism error unless an instrument discriminates among sources. This is why F3 is `specified` and the per-system answer is `gap`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of respected feedback researchers who agree with the shape gives it a budget to be tried; it does not settle it.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What would license saying which feedback regime a pattern enters?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns the shape: a signed cycle, residual-relative valences, three regimes to be split, a ground that decides. A particular regime for a particular system is licensed only by an instrument that discriminates among sources, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** Not if it stays in shape form. Saying "growth is positive feedback" would be two errors at once: it names a source from a projection (metamerism) and it treats a residual-relative valence as an intrinsic kind (Fig 11 :49). `01` and `07` keep the labels relational and mark the per-system outcome `gap`.

## 5b. Correction to my own first reading

I initially read "shape, not a name" as meaning the framework cannot talk about feedback signs at all, only about some unnamed dynamical quantity. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Cycle, comparison and threshold are all already in the figure set (Fig 12 :121, Fig 12 :87, Operators table), so describing a signed cycle is not inventing a seventh term. What the framework cannot do is name which loop dominates in a given system or call that dominance a settled verdict. I retract the blanket ban and keep the narrower one: the per-system regime is `gap`, and the global labels are valences relative to a residual.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly". The ask in `02_outreach_email.md` gives the claim a budget to be tried and requests a refutation. It does not ask anyone to endorse it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Effect descriptions travel when contact does not.** From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." A feedback regime claim stated in a recipient's own field terms is the part that travels. A framework vocabulary does not. This is a second, independent reason to lead with the residual and the refuter and offer the links only after a verdict is recorded.
