## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-gain answer does not. A feedback process is a signed cycle, and a chain cannot close. Positive and negative are valences relative to a tracked residual, not intrinsic kinds. Growth is a compounding remainder, stability is a correction ranged against an anchor, and collapse is loss of the contact that sustained the pattern. These three fates form a union compound that must be split before search. A new regime needs an import, not more search on the same components. Which regime a particular system enters is `gap` here, settled only by a ground reading that discriminates among sources. This is a `general` and `specified` result, not a `grounded` one.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, splits the union, writes five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a checkable output. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-gain framing and any claim that the framework picks a regime are removable without touching the structural derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive must be withdrawn, and the open ⋛ operand strain is recorded rather than hidden.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An intervention that varies amplification and damping independently and records the three regimes would settle the shape at the ground. An instrument that distinguishes a returning cycle from an external drive producing the same growth curve would name the source. A result showing one monotone gain determines all three regimes without splitting, or a residual-invariant valence, or a new regime manufactured by search inside a fixed closure, would break the shape. A figure re-census that settles ⋛ operands as quantities at loop level would clear the remaining strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
