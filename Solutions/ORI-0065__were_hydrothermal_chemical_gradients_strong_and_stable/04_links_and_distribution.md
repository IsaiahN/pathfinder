## The live conflict, kept rather than smoothed

The sources divide, and the division is informative:

- **Pro magnitude and persistence:** Martin and Russell 2003 and 2007 supply the original vent-gradient model; Schwander et al. 2023 report roughly three pH units and field lifetimes over 30,000 years; Keil et al. 2017 show heat flow sustaining gradients in the laboratory.
- **Against magnitude at the working site:** Jackson 2016 calculates about 0.004 pH units across a machine in a thick inorganic membrane and finds no evidence for thin membranes holding sharp gradients at Lost City.
- **Against the ancient geology:** PNAS 2026 argues ancient serpentinizing systems had shorter-lived, less focused venting and no strong pH gradients at hydrothermal temperatures.
- **Against the necessity of the gradient:** Schwander et al. 2023 and Brunk and Marshall 2021 both report CO2 reduction without a gradient, with catalysts and a maintained H2 supply doing the work.

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The conflict is a union of at least three conditions: magnitude at the working site, geological persistence, and whether the gradient is the operative energy source at all. A single lever cannot clear all three. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Several of these papers cite the same Russell and Martin lineage, so the pro-side agreement is one witness until an independent instrument returns a value.
