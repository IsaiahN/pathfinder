## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks whether a particular class of setting (a hydrothermal system) supplied chemical gradients whose **magnitude** and **persistence** were sufficient to drive early chemistry. Stated as effects:

- **"strong"** = a difference that can be spent, with enough directedness at the place where work is done to couple to a reaction. Not a bulk number. Fig 13 :46: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."
- **"stable"** = that spendable difference is maintained over a time long enough for work to compound into networks. This is a source-and-channel property, not staticness. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- **"drive early chemistry"** = the difference composes with energy into a flow, and the flow composes into relations, networks, and cycles that fix carbon and build polymers. Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow."

The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard question. "Strong" and "stable" are two conditions with different instruments and different failure modes. A vent can be strong and short lived, or stable and too diffuse to focus. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound, so it must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** A shape claim: *a setting drives early chemistry if and only if it holds a spendable difference with (i) directedness at the working site, (ii) a maintained source that outlasts the chemistry it drives, and (iii) a coupling from difference to energy to flow to network.* This is bound now, before the derivation, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

**R (arity / symmetry / scale).**

- Symmetry: a gradient is directed, not symmetric. Fig 13 :46: "neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." The comparison "enough" is a threshold relation, Operators table :49: "a threshold relation between two quantities".

That is the dependency break to test first, before judging whether any vent was "strong and stable enough".

## 2. Findings, with verbatim figure lines and refuters

### F1. A gradient is a spendable, directed difference, not a static distribution (triangulated)

Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"**
Symbols table :97: **"A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."**
Fig 13 :46: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."**

The question's word "strong" must be read as spendability with direction, not as a large static difference. A large pH difference that cannot be coupled to work is a distribution, and by the figure's own definition is not the thing being asked about. Two distinct figure passages (Fig 13 and the Symbols table) carry the same definition with different wording, and they draw on different parts of the figure set, so this is triangulated within the framework. The world source Keil et al. 2017 independently demonstrates that heat flow "forms and sustains stable pH gradients", which is low-overlap corroboration of the spendable-and-sustained form, not of the framework itself.

**Refuter:** show a static distribution, with no consumable directedness, doing net chemical work that compounds. That breaks Fig 13 :46 and the Symbols definition at the ground, and the finding is withdrawn.

### F2. Energy alone is directionless; the gradient supplies the direction, and the two compose a flow (triangulated)

Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (the line for energy)
Fig 13 :76: **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."**
Fig 13 :71: **"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"** (what is lost without gradient)

These three lines are load-bearing for the word "drive" in the question. Remove the gradient and the thermodynamics lose both the direction of flow and the remainder that the next step composes against. Fig 13 :71 is the law-table row, Fig 13 :76 is the composition table, and Fig 13 :14 is the state definition, so this is triangulated across three distinct passages.

The geochemical frame agrees on the shape: Schwander et al. 2023 write that the continuous far-from-equilibrium state of serpentinizing systems "produces pH-, temperature- and redox-gradients, which create a steady supply of chemical energy sources". Altair et al. 2021 describe the same model as requiring "entropy conversion and gradient dissipation mechanisms". Agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, so this corroborates the shape, not the framework's authority.

**Refuter:** demonstrate net chemical work driven by energy at equilibrium with no directed difference anywhere in the coupling. That breaks Fig 13 :71 and :76.

### F3. "Strong" is a threshold at the working site, not a bulk property (single-source for the figure rule; corroborated at the ground by a conflict)

Operators table :49: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**
Fig 9 :27: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**
Fig 9 :31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**

The figure rule says "enough" is a comparison between two quantities, and it must be described in effect terms before a frame is chosen. The geochemical frame supplies the operands, and they do not agree. Schwander et al. 2023 report "a natural proton gradient of roughly three pH units, a far greater gradient than the roughly one pH unit or less that modern cells require". Jackson 2016 reports that in a thick inorganic membrane the gradient across the machine can be "only about 0.004 units", an "insignificant 24 J mol−1 protons transferred across the machine". Jackson's point is a scale mismatch: bulk ΔpH of 4 and working-site ΔpH of 0.004 are different quantities. Fig 9 :31 requires the effect description, so "strong" must be indexed to the site, not to the vent bulk.

This is single-source as a figure rule (the Operators table and Fig 9 are distinct passages but one framework), and grounded as a measurement claim only in the world sources. The world sources conflict, and per the WATSON rule the conflict sends us back to the census, not to a flipped verdict. Re-census: the two camps are measuring different quantities at different scales, so the union must be unbundled (F6), not averaged.

**Refuter:** state the threshold in units at the working site and show the bulk number is the wrong operand, or show one measurement that legitimately serves as both. If the field cannot name the operands, the question stays a shape and cannot be moved to `grounded`.

### F4. "Stable" is a maintained channel, not a static difference (re-census, not a strain)

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**
Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**

The apparent contradiction between "gradient" (spendable, directed) and "stable" (persisting) is dissolved by assigning persistence to the source-to-channel contact. A sustained vent is maintained by continuous serpentinization. Schwander et al. 2023: two fluids "continuously mix, generating far from equilibrium conditions over the lifespan of the vent, which in the case of Lost City can be over 30,000 years". Brunk and Marshall 2021 list as a criterion "permeable containment in an environment with pervasive and steady energy and material flows", and separately "cradle of life was long-lived and/or geographically extensive". These are two operands again, not one. Fig 11 :46: **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"**; the habitat is read off the world, not derived from the six terms.

**Refuter:** show a spendable difference persisting with no maintaining source and no contact cascade. That breaks Fig 10 :24 and Fig 11 :39, and the re-census fails.

### F5. The objective link has no instrument in several proposals (dependency finding)

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."**
Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

The middle link, "what would count as enough", has no standard instrument in the natural-vent proposals. Jackson 2016 states the gap directly: "A direct demonstration that a model, laboratory-synthesized, non-protein, molecular machine can utilize a ΔpH established across a laboratory-synthesized, inorganic membrane is needed." Altair et al. 2021 describe reactors, micro fluidics, chemical gardens, and electrochemistry as the experimental tools, which is an instrument inventory already returning something. So this is not unreachable in principle; it is unreached at this budget, and Fig 6 :32 says the next instrument is built from the worse one, not from a description.

**Refuter:** name an instrument that measures ΔpH and persistence at the working site, show it returning a value, and the link moves from assumed to measured. The finding is then discharged, not preserved.

### F6. The question is a union compound and must be unbundled before search (structural result)

Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**
Fig 9 :55: **"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"** (opening of the passage)
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

"Strong" and "stable" are not two versions of one difficulty. A single lever that raises bulk gradient magnitude leaves the persistence and focusing conditions standing. The field's own disagreement is the evidence: PNAS 2026 argues ancient serpentinizing systems gave "shorter-lived and less focused venting" and that "neither modern nor ancient serpentinizing systems are characterized by strong pH gradients at hydrothermal temperatures", while Schwander et al. 2023 argue the gradients were "of sufficient magnitude to support chemiosmotic ATP synthesis". These are different conditions, not a contradiction to average.

**Refuter:** show that strength and stability are one condition, or that one alone is sufficient for net chemical work. If so, the union reading fails and the compound collapses to a single-conditions question.

### F7. Several sources do not require the gradient at all, which relocates the residual

Schwander et al. 2023: "The key to organic synthesis in serpentinizing systems are not gradients but catalysts", and Preiner et al. obtained formate and acetate "in free solution with no gradients or compartments". Brunk and Marshall 2021: "with a steady source of H2 from the underlying serpentinization, it does not seem that the proton gradient is needed as an energy source for the scenario to work".

This is not a conflict with the figures. It relocates the residual from "was the gradient enough?" to "which operand does the work: gradient, catalyst, or both?" Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."** Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** A catalyst is a part that survives use; a vent with a maintained H2 source is a cycle, not a chain. The figure has the vocabulary, but which structure occupies which role is a ground question.

**Refuter:** show that all net synthesis in the relevant setting requires the gradient, with the gradient removed as the controlled variable. Then the relocation fails and the original wording stands.

### F8. The empirical verdict is a `gap` from the figures, unreachable rather than silence

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**
Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

The question is constitutively about a past physical system. The legitimate anchor is measurement. The figure closure can return the shape of a sufficient gradient and the dependency order of the tests, but it cannot return a number for a Hadean vent. This is a `gap` for the empirical domain, not a failure of the derivation and not a license to answer from preference. Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."**

**Refuter:** produce a figure line that returns a Hadean pH, flux, or duration without an imported measurement. None exists in the verbatim extract. If one is produced, this finding is withdrawn.

### F9. Agreement among the origins community is one witness, not several

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**
Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

The pro-gradient sources (Martin and Russell 2003, Schwander et al. 2023, Brunk and Marshall 2021) and the anti-gradient sources (Jackson 2016, PNAS 2026) partly cite the same Lost City literature and the same Russell/Martin lineage. Their disagreement is informative precisely because it is not a vote. The agreement among the pro sources is weaker than it looks: shared pool, one witness. Independence must be bought with separate instruments and separate experience, Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

**Refuter:** exhibit two genuinely independent measurements with disjoint evidence pools and different instruments that agree or conflict at the working site. That would be a new witness, and would move the empirical domain toward the ground.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before outreach: a falsifier written in advance is a design instrument. The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds.

1. A static distribution, with no consumable directedness, produces net compounding chemistry. (breaks F1, F2)
2. Energy drives net chemical work with no directed difference anywhere in the coupling. (breaks Fig 13 :71)
3. A spendable difference persists with no maintaining source and no contact cascade. (breaks Fig 10 :24, Fig 11 :39)
4. "Strong" and "stable" are shown to be one condition, or one alone suffices. (breaks Fig 9 :57)
5. A figure line is produced that returns a Hadean measurement without an import. (breaks F8)
6. The threshold operand at the working site is shown to be the bulk gradient, not the focused difference. (breaks F3)

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or repaired in public. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape, not with a verdict about vents. Say what a sufficient gradient must be; do not say that one existed.
2. Unbundle "strong" and "stable" into separate sub-claims with separate instruments (F3, F4, F6).
3. Name the operand and the scale before citing any number. A bulk pH unit and a working-site pH unit are different quantities (F3).
4. Carry the conflict, do not resolve it by vote. Jackson 2016, Schwander et al. 2023, and PNAS 2026 are three witnesses with partly shared pools (F9).
5. Ask what would refute the shape claim, then record the answer before offering any source (Fig 10 :52).

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is a gradient a state or a structure?** A structure, one of the six (Fig 13 :21 to :22). It is not a seventh primitive; importing one would be a wrong atom (Fig 6).
2. **What does "drive" compose to?** Energy (state) plus gradient (structure) compose a flow (Fig 13 :76). The flow composes onward to network and cycle (Fig 13 :83 to :85).
3. **Does "strong" name one quantity?** No. It is a threshold comparison (Operators :49) between a focused difference and a reaction's demand, at a stated scale (Fig 9 :30 to :31).
4. **Does "stable" name the same quantity?** No. It names the persistence of the maintaining contact (Fig 10 :24), a different operand with a different instrument.
5. **Can the closure return a Hadean number?** No. `gap` (Fig 13 :134), not silence and not a refusal of the question.
6. **Where is the instrument?** Inward, at the working site (Fig 6 :32; Jackson 2016 names the missing demonstration). Outward, in geochemistry and reactor experiments (Altair et al. 2021).
7. **What would reverse the result?** A measurement at the working site, a demonstration that the bulk operand is the right one, or a static distribution doing net work. All are ground readings (Fig 2 :45).

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: a sufficient gradient is a directed, spendable difference, focused at the working site, maintained by a persistent source, and coupled through flow into network and cycle. The empirical claim does not survive as a figure verdict; it is a `gap` reserved to measurement, and the world literature is genuinely in conflict about it. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**. Two live open items are named rather than smoothed: which operand (gradient or catalyst) carries the work, and whether the bulk or focused difference is the correct "strong" operand.
