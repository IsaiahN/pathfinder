# The Tether's answer on hydrothermal chemical gradients

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural result, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether hydrothermal chemical gradients were strong and stable enough to drive early chemistry. In effect terms it asks three things at once: is there a spendable difference, is it focused where work is done, and is it maintained long enough for work to compound? These are two conditions plus a consequence, a union compound in Fig 9's sense, not one hard question. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

## 2. The shape

1. **A gradient is a spendable, directed difference, not a static distribution.** Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent". Symbols table :97: "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness." So "strong" cannot mean "large and unchanging"; it must mean "directed and consumable at the working site".
2. **Energy plus gradient compose a flow.** Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." Fig 13 :71, on what is lost without the gradient: "no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step." The word "drive" in the question is a composition claim, not a metaphor.
3. **"Strong" is a threshold with operands that must be named.** Operators table :49: "a threshold relation between two quantities." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." A bulk pH unit and the difference focused across a barrier are different quantities. Jackson 2016 and Schwander et al. 2023 report different numbers because they measure different operands.
4. **"Stable" is a maintained source-to-channel contact.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A gradient that merely stays put is a distribution. Persistence belongs to the source, not to the difference's being spendable.
5. **The composition runs state to structure to flow to network, and only a closed cycle is chemistry that persists.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." A single reaction is a flow; a metabolism is a cycle.
6. **A catalyst can occupy the driving role instead of the gradient.** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Schwander et al. 2023 and Brunk and Marshall 2021 report carbon fixation with no gradient, driven by catalysts and maintained H2. The figure has the vocabulary; which structure occupies it is a ground question.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any Hadean vent *was* strong and stable enough. That is a ground verdict, and this solution has no measurement (Fig 2 :36).
- Not that alkaline vents are the cradle of life, and not that pH gradients were necessary. The sources conflict and two of them explicitly say the gradient was not needed.
- Not a derivation of one theory from another, and no new primitive. Gradient and network are already among the six; importing a seventh to explain "enoughness" would be a wrong atom (Fig 6).
- Not `grounded`. `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."
- Not a claim that the figure closure can return a pH value or an age. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the discriminating questions are:

- **Operand test:** is the right "strong" quantity the bulk ΔpH or the focused ΔpH at the working site? Jackson 2016 calculates about 0.004 pH units across a machine in a thick membrane; Schwander et al. 2023 report roughly three pH units at the vent-ocean interface. Both can be correct at their own scale.
- **Machine test:** does a laboratory non-protein machine utilize a ΔpH established across an inorganic membrane? Jackson 2016: "A direct demonstration ... is needed." That is the missing instrument, and Fig 6 :32 says it is built from a worse one already returning something.
- **Persistence test:** does the source maintain the focused difference for longer than the chemistry it drives? Field lifetimes over 30,000 years are reported for Lost City (Schwander et al. 2023); PNAS 2026 argues ancient serpentinizing systems were shorter-lived and less focused.
- **Substitution test:** remove the gradient and keep the catalysts and H2. If carbon fixation is unchanged, the gradient was not the operative energy source (Schwander et al. 2023; Brunk and Marshall 2021).
- **Standing falsifiers from section 3 of `01`:** a static distribution doing net work; energy doing net work with no directed difference; a difference persisting with no source; strength and stability collapsing to one condition.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The framework does not say whether Hadean hydrothermal vents were strong and stable enough. It says what a sufficient gradient would have to be: a spendable, directed difference, focused at the place where work is done, maintained by a source that outlasts the chemistry, and coupled through a flow into a network that closes as a cycle. "Strong" is a threshold at the working site, not a bulk pH number; "stable" is a maintained channel, not a static difference. A catalyst or a maintained H2 flux can occupy the driving role instead, and which one actually did so is a measurement question, not a figure verdict. The sources disagree: some report several pH units and vents lasting tens of thousands of years, others calculate a tiny difference across the working machine and argue the ancient vents were short-lived. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the names of the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
