## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical verdict does not exist at this closure. A sufficient gradient is a spendable, directed difference, focused at the working site, maintained by a source that outlasts the chemistry, and coupled through a flow into a network that closes as a cycle. "Strong" is a threshold with named operands at a stated scale; "stable" is a maintained channel rather than staticness. A catalyst or a maintained H2 flux can occupy the driving role, and which one did so in the Hadean is a ground question. The question of whether ancient hydrothermal gradients actually met those constraints is a `gap`: unreachable from the figure closure, reserved to measurement, and the world literature is in live conflict about it. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `general` structural spine.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, names its operands, and records its refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost would be answering yes or no from agreement: that spends the independence of outside readers (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong") and averages a union compound (Fig 9 :57). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing in the framework is withdrawn if a measurement says ancient vents were weak, short-lived, or irrelevant. The shape claim is independent of the empirical answer, and the substitution finding is already recorded rather than hidden. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and no primitive is retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement of the focused ΔpH and its persistence at the working site would settle "strong" and "stable"; a demonstration that the bulk operand is the legitimate one would break F3; a static distribution doing net work, or energy doing net work with no directed difference, would break the shape; a controlled removal of the gradient with unchanged carbon fixation would relocate the residual to catalysts and H2. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` with the empirical domain marked `gap`.
