## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Symbols table :97, Fig 13 :46 and :76, Fig 10 :24, Fig 12 :119 and :121, Operators table :49.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: a spendable difference, focused at the working site, maintained by a source, coupled through flow into network and cycle.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the empirical question as the field would ask it: were Hadean vent gradients strong and stable enough?

**R_T is nonzero and is the main finding of this audit.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **operand of "strong"**: the abstraction says "focused at the working site", but the literature supplies a bulk pH number and a focused number that differ by orders of magnitude (Jackson 2016, Schwander et al. 2023). The abstraction cannot decide which to report.
- the **unit of "stable"**: the abstraction says "maintained by a source", but field lifetimes over 30,000 years (Schwander et al. 2023) and PNAS 2026's "shorter-lived and less focused venting" are different measurements, neither of which the abstraction names.
- the **role of the gradient**: the abstraction says it is coupled into a network, but Schwander et al. 2023 and Brunk and Marshall 2021 report the work done without it. The condensation silent-selects the gradient as the operative structure.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a gradient is a directed, spendable difference, and energy plus gradient compose a flow. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
