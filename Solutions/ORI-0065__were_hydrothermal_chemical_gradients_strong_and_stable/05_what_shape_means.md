## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The figures README: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

*The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. For ORI-0065:

- A shape: *a sufficient gradient is a spendable difference, focused at the working site, maintained by a source, coupled through flow into network and cycle.*
- A name or a date: *Lost City-type vents at 4.0 Ga were strong and stable enough.* That is a ground verdict, not a shape.

The distinction decides what this solution may say. It may constrain; it may not report a Hadean measurement.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the nearest known vocabulary is *difference*, *directedness*, *source*, *coupling*.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So we describe what a sufficient gradient *does* (spendable, focused, maintained, coupled), not which geochemical story caused it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what they would expect to see if the shape were false.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question that means the shape is complete when the terms stop producing new distinctions between a gradient that drives chemistry and one that does not. The shape is not complete when a favourite vent hypothesis is named.

## 4. The source error, and why it governs this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "It's possible that the source might be one green light. But the 'source' could also be a mix of one Yellow light and one Blue light." The instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

This maps directly onto ORI-0065. A measured bulk pH difference is a reading. The source of the chemical work is underdetermined by that reading: it could be the gradient, a catalyst surface, a maintained H2 flux, or a combination. Schwander et al. 2023 report formate and acetate in free solution with no gradients or compartments, so the reading "steep gradient present" does not determine the source "gradient drove the chemistry". What is owed is a discriminating instrument at the working site, not another inference from the bulk reading.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a ground

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that vents were sufficient is the named collapse, not corroboration.

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Most sources in this folder cite the same Russell and Martin lineage, so their agreement is one pool talking. Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Independence is bought with separate instruments, not with separate opinions.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay is explicit that the theory "cannot validate itself" and that "An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figure text. They are **not** tests of whether Hadean gradients were strong and stable. They cannot settle the claim they report on. The only thing they can honestly return is the shape a sufficient answer must have, the dependency order of the measurements, and the refuters written in advance.

## 7. What "shape" means here, in one sentence

A shape is the set of constraints a sufficient hydrothermal gradient must satisfy, stated before any particular vent, pH value, or epoch is named, so that an outside reader can say what would count as a failure without first accepting the framework.
