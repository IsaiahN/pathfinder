**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive mechanisms, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement of a real institution's learning was taken in this run.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pipeline, not by this author).

## What is being decided, in effect terms

An institution holds a library of policies, models and beliefs. It acts, the world returns an
outcome, and there is a gap between what the library predicted and what happened. Three next
states are possible:

1. **Learn.** The gap arrives at a seat that can change what the institution is able to compose,
   so the next prediction differs.
2. **Conceal.** The channel carrying the gap to that seat is degraded, so the gap stops arriving.
   The institution still agrees with itself, and the drift is invisible from inside.
3. **Repeat.** The failure, or a past success, is stored as a recording and replayed as a rule,
   so the institution produces a familiar result and cannot produce a new one.

An answer is worth having if it states what must be present for branch 1 and absent for branches
2 and 3, in terms that a checker outside the institution could test. Bind that description as an
ordinary term before the run, per the formula's "Direction" section, so it can accrue a residual
and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this analysis.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that any named real institution has or has not learned. The question is answered at
  the level of the shape of the mechanisms, and the figures return a shape, not a name
  (Fig 9 :32).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not this derivation.
3. **The keeper of the measurement must be outside the slice being graded.** Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."*

Legally: public institutional submission channels and opt-in reporting venues avoid
GDPR and CAN-SPAM style problems that unsolicited personal outreach creates.

## Honest standing of this folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a
ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, which was fetched and read this run:
LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* while *"the truly testable output would
need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This folder
cannot settle the question it reports on. It can only state the shape a checkable answer must
have and the refuters that would break it.
