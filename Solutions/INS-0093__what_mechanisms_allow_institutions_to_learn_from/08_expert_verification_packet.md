# Expert verification packet: the Tether on institutional learning from failure

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher of institutions, organisations or safety can check.
Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for
and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No real institution was measured. This is a shape and a
route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Concealment and repetition are two different failures, not two degrees of one failure. Concealment
is a dead channel between the residual and the seat that could act on it; repetition is a working
channel whose correction crosses as a replayed recording instead of a method. They are isomers over
the same institutional atom set (same atoms, different bonds, different behaviour), and a compound
case with both conditions survives a lever that clears only one. For concealment, the outcome
measure must be something the institution cannot move, the reading must be held outside the slice
being graded with any unremovable stake declared, and the residual must stay indexed to the
specific policy, place and population. For repetition, the institution must have an operator for
refusal, ordered before it mints a new initiative, and it must carry the method of correction
rather than the conclusion, measuring what the crossing dropped. Reviewers must differ from one
another and match the residual the last one surfaced. No final institutional design is claimed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 12

- **Statement:** concealment and repetition are a union of two conditions and are isomers over the
  same atom set. A single lever does not clear the compound case.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **Refuter:** show that the same set of repairs clears both failure modes in every case, including
  the compound case where both conditions bind. That would collapse the union and break the isomer
  reading.

### C2 - world-facing; checkable against the literature on reporting and oversight

- **Statement:** the party holding the outcome reading must be outside the slice being graded, and
  a reputation to protect in the result disqualifies it as a ground. Declaring an unremovable stake
  is the permitted substitute; more information inside the same frame is not.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 10 :86 - *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."*
  - Fig 10 :93 - *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."*
  - Fig 10 :60 - *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*
  - Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **What to check:** Reason's BMJ 2000 paper argues that a reporting culture requires a just culture
  and that trust is its key element. Vaughan's archival study of Challenger found that signals were
  reinterpreted as acceptable from inside the frame. Do those cases support, or contradict, the
  claim that the keeper's stake is structural rather than merely motivational?
- **Refuter:** an institution whose measurement keeper is the measured party and has a reputation
  to protect in the result, producing ground-paid corrections with no independent seat anywhere in
  the chain. That breaks Fig 10 :86 and :93 together.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading the Operators table and Figure 4

- **Statement:** a vocabulary that can adjust and report but cannot refuse can be corrected and
  cannot carry the correction, so a correction carried as a recording produces repetition. The
  refusal must precede mint.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Operators table :58 - *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*
  - Operators table :74 - *"can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."*
  - Fig 4 :26 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*
  - Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- **Refuter:** an institution with no mechanism for declaring a held premise false, only adjustment
  and addition, shown to prevent recurrence at the ground while keeping that premise composable
  into new policy; or an institution carrying only conclusions between cycles, with no method and
  no round-trip gap, shown to produce a genuinely new correct prediction.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that transparency alone produces learning. A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict
  (Fig 7 :35), and a mirror cannot correct you (Fig 2 :35).
- Not that any named real institution has or lacks these properties. No institution was measured.
- Not that there is a single right committee, law or reporting system. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind
  of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- Not that a refusal deletes history. Nothing leaves the library; the reach falls because something
  was added (formula step 3).
- Not that learning from failure is always beneficial. The Lehman and Ramanujam 2022 result is a
  recorded conflict, and it was returned to the census rather than used to soften a verdict.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. Six tests are listed in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §4. The
three decisive ones:

1. **Anchor test:** can the outcome measure be moved by what the institution thinks about it?
   A measure that moves is not an anchor (Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not
   an anchor."*).
2. **Keeper test:** is the reading held by the measured party, with a reputation to protect
   (Fig 10 :93)?
3. **Refusal and round-trip test:** is there a formal "this premise is false" step before new
   initiatives, and did the last correction cross as a method or a recording (Fig 4 :25-30)?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54 and :57, Fig 12 :117, Fig 10 :86 and :93, and Operators :58 and :74. Six short
   passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know: who held the outcome reading, and were they inside the
   slice being graded?
3. Check C3 against one after-action or lessons-learned process you know: did it carry a method or
   a conclusion, and could it declare a held premise false?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish a working channel from a broken one in that case,
   or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
