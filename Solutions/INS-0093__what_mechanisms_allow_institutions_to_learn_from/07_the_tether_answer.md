# The Tether's answer: mechanisms for learning from policy failure

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a set of refuters, not a result.
Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading of any real institution.

## 1. The residual it answers

An institution predicts, acts and observes. The gap `R` between prediction and outcome is the only
signal that says where to look, and it is indexed per policy, place and population (Fig 1 :19,
Symbols :42-44). The question is why that signal sometimes changes the institution's reach and
sometimes does not. The figures say this is a **union** of two conditions (Fig 9 :54), so the answer
is per domain and is never averaged:

- **Condition C, concealment:** the channel carrying `R` to the seat that could act on it dies.
- **Condition R, repetition:** the channel works, but the correction crosses as a recording rather
  than a method, so the library replays and the reach does not move.

Under Fig 12 :117 the two are isomers: same atoms, different bonds, different behaviour.

## 2. The mechanisms, by domain

### D1 and D2, the policy frame and its ground: keep the signal alive and unaveraged

1. **The outcome measure must be an anchor, not a frame.** Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push
   on it, it is not an anchor."* A measure whose answer changes with what the institution thinks
   about it cannot settle anything. It is a mirror (Fig 2 :35: *"A mirror cannot correct you."*).
2. **The four channel killers must be named and watched.** Fig 10 :25-26: *"stale percepts,
   dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. The honest output when they are
   active is not a verdict about the policy but *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :27-28).
3. **The residual stays indexed.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal
   disappears into an average."* Programme-wide success rates are the form concealment usually
   takes in an institution that is not lying.

**Verdict for D1/D2: `triangulated`** across Fig 1, Fig 2 and the Symbols table. Single claim,
three figures, low overlap in content. Ungrounded.

### D3, the verification seat: put the keeper outside the graded slice

4. **Whoever is measured cannot own the reading.** Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded.
   Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to
   protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* A frame
   cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27).
5. **Declare the stake that cannot be removed.** Fig 10 :59-60: *"an interest in the outcome is
   unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* and *"Where a stake cannot be
   removed, declare it."* A seat with no stake at all does not exist (Fig 10 :58).
6. **Select between rooms, not within one.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a
   verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* The next room must differ from the
   last and match the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36). Independence is bought in proportion to the
   cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42), and two people reading the same report are one room wearing two
   names (Fig 2 :31-32).

**Verdict for D3: `triangulated`** across Fig 10, Fig 1, Fig 2 and Fig 7. This is the strongest
figure-supported part of the answer. Ungrounded.

### D4, the transmission frame: carry a method, refuse a premise, bind the direction

7. **Install negation, ordered before mint.** Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation,
   which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Operators table :74: a vocabulary
   with subtraction but not negation *"can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say
   that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."* Formula step 2 routes REFUTED
   before mint. A refusal does not delete the old policy from the record; it removes its standing
   to be composed over (Fig 5 :55: *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*).
8. **Carry methods, not recordings, and measure the gap.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only
   head starts come down."* A recording replayed downward *"produces a system that repeats a past
   success and cannot produce a new one"* (Fig 4 :26). Take the round trip and measure `R_T`
   (Fig 4 :30).
9. **Put provenance on imports.** Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look
   identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. A best
   practice with no source record cannot be counted as new reach.
10. **Bind the sought-for shape before the run.** Fig 5 :65: *"The remedy is a discipline rather
    than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* Otherwise the institution holds
    "this policy works" as a preference that accrues no residual, and aims its refusal at the
    unwanted outcome rather than at the belief it holds (Fig 5 :62-64).

**Verdict for D4: `triangulated`** across Fig 5, Fig 12, the Operators table, Fig 4 and Fig 8.
Ungrounded.

### No final design

Fig 7 :6: **"the limit: no last room"**. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
itself"**. The framework returns the shape of the mechanism, not the name of the agency that
implements it. Any real institution is a room in a chain that must keep differing; booking a room
like the last one is how a chain stops travelling (Fig 7 :42).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that transparency alone produces learning. More agreement inside a frame is one evidence
  pool talking (Fig 2 :40), and a mirror cannot correct you (Fig 2 :35).
- Not that any named real institution has or lacks these properties. No institution was measured.
- Not that a policy failure is always a failure of the institution. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence
  is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The habitat must be enumerated, not
  composed (Fig 11 :43), and the relevant signal may be several steps out (Fig 11 :52-53).
- Not that a refusal deletes history. Nothing is ever deleted; the reach falls because something
  was added (formula step 3).
- Not that learning is always good. Lehman and Ramanujam (Organization Science, 2022, world source
  that conflicts with the simple story) find that attention to one failure class can increase
  another. Fig 1 :19 and Fig 12 :125-127 keep those slots separate.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any institution was taken in this run.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (world analogue,
second-hand quote from the exemplar): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the
source"*; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

1. **Anchor test:** can the outcome measure be moved by what the institution thinks about it?
   A measure that moves is not an anchor (Fig 2 :30).
2. **Keeper test:** is the party holding the reading inside the slice being graded, with a
   reputation to protect in the result (Fig 10 :86, :93)?
3. **Slot test:** does the signal survive at the disaggregated level, and disappear only in the
   average (Fig 1 :22)?
4. **Refusal test:** can the institution declare a held premise false, and does that refusal come
   before any new initiative (Operators :58, formula step 2)?
5. **Round-trip test:** what was carried from the last correction into this one, a method or a
   recording, and what did the coarser description drop (Fig 4 :25-30)?
6. **Standing refuters from `01` §4:** if any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure lines close the shape: the mechanisms above are what a
learning institution must have, given the figures. What they do not give is the relative cost of
each mechanism in a real institution, or which arrangement pays at the ground. Fig 12 :125-127
prices a composition in two currencies, bits and actions, that do not add, and this simulated run
has no budget for either. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it
improves."* The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the right committee or the right reporting law, and it does not claim
> that transparency alone makes institutions learn. It says that concealment and repetition are two
> different failures: the first is a dead channel to a seat that could act, the second is a working
> channel whose correction crosses as a replayed recording. For the first, the outcome measure must
> be something the institution cannot move, the reading must be held by someone outside the slice
> being graded, and the failure must stay indexed to the policy, place and population that
> produced it. For the second, the institution needs an operator for refusal, ordered before it
> mints a new initiative, and it must carry the method of correction rather than the conclusion,
> measuring what the crossing dropped. Reviewers must differ from one another and match the
> residual the last one surfaced, because a room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. The
> desired shape has to be written down before the run so it can be refused like anything else. No
> final design is claimed: there is no last room. What is offered is the shape any checkable answer
> must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ground
> decides, not agreement.
