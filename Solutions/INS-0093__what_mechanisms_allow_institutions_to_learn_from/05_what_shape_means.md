## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape,
not a name)"* (figures README, local copy). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched this run): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name
the institution that implements it. "A keeper outside the slice being graded" is a shape. "The
Inspector General's office of country X" is a name. "An operator for refusal ordered before mint"
is a shape. "A quarterly ethics training module" is a name. The framework gives the first column.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

*The Library of Babel* (fetched this run): *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you
will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to
describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the
space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

The derive-first run did exactly this. Starting from the ordinary words "conceal" and "repeat", the
closest figure vocabulary was *channel*, *anchor*, *slot*, *refusal* and *method*. Each term
narrowed the question:

- *channel* turned "conceal" from a moral claim into an instrument failure (Fig 10 :24-28).
- *anchor* turned "more information" into "does the outcome measure move when pushed" (Fig 2 :30).
- *slot* turned "programme failure" into "which policy, which place, which population" (Fig 1 :19).
- *refusal* turned "learn" into "can a held premise be declared false" (Operators :58).
- *method* turned "institutional memory" into "does the correction cross as a generator" (Fig 4 :22).

The same essay also supplies the union warning that governs F1: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle
or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those
problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*
Corroboration only. The verdict came from Fig 9 :54.

Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
in effect terms."* Hence the residual was stated in terms of a prediction gap and a channel before
any field was named.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays.
   A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow
   test (Fig 6 :23-24, Fig 8 :51).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (second-hand quote from the exemplar): *"The real import
is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the
primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: naming a real agency as the mechanism would be a binding from the world frame onto a
figure-shaped slot. It is allowed only with provenance, and it adds no atom to the framework's
closure (Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*). This is why
`03_public_contact_points.csv` lists venues and not people, and why `07` does not name a country.

## 4. Why "more transparency" is not the shape

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (second-hand quote from the exemplar) gives the mechanism of
metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several different
sources can produce the same reading, so the reading does not determine the source. *"If several
paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The
spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

The same error appears here. "We published the failure report" is a reading taken off an
institutional instrument. It cannot comment on the source: the report could come from a working
channel with a disqualified keeper, or from an internal room that agreed with itself. These are
different sources with the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator (an anchor the frame
cannot move, a keeper outside the graded slice), not more readings of the same kind.

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched this run), the instrument-side statement:
*"If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken
channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."*

## 5. Q: what is the shape for INS-0093?

**Answer:** two conditions, four required properties, two ordering constraints, and no named final
design.

- **Conditions (union, F1):** the channel condition (does the residual arrive?) and the
  transmission condition (does the correction cross as a method?). A compound case has both.
- **Required properties:** an anchor that does not update (F2); a keeper outside the graded slice
  with declared stakes (F3); a residual kept per slot (F4); an operator for refusal (F5); low
  overlap between reviewer rooms (F7).
- **Ordering constraints:** refuse before mint (F5); carry the method and measure the round trip
  rather than replay a recording (F6); bind the desired shape before the run (F9).
- **No final design:** Fig 7 :6, *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing,
  never the answer itself"*.

The framework cannot say which real institution has these properties. It can say what any
institution must have for the claim to survive, and what would break it. That is the shape.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (scope of "shape, not a name")

I initially wrote the limit as if it forbade naming any mechanism. That overstates it. The README's
phrase governs *what a missing thing would have to look like*. The properties in §5 are not missing
primitives; they are constraints already stated in figures this run holds (Fig 2, Fig 4, Fig 5,
Fig 10, Operators). Naming them is deriving from held figures, not inventing a seventh term. What
the limit still forbids is naming the *real-world instance* as if the framework selected it. Both
statements are kept: deriving the properties is allowed; selecting the agency is not. This matches
the exemplar's own correction in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1, where naming a held atom was
distinguished from inventing a new one.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched this run): *"Prestige
must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can
give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it.
The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A research campaign that gathers prestigious
names to agree with this analysis commits the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite:
give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (fetched this run): LLMs *"can
quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*
while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working
the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against
the figures. It is not a test of any institution and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle
anything it reports on.
