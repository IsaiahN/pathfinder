## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Let an institution hold a library Γ of policies, models and standing beliefs, act on the world
with action `a`, and receive observation `o'`. The prediction is `Γ(b,a)` and the residual is
`R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`, indexed per slot `s` (Symbols table :36-40, Fig 1 :11).

The question asks what makes the next step go to branch L rather than branches C or R:

- **L (learn):** `R` arrives at a controller that can change the reach, so the next prediction
  differs in a way the ground pays for.
- **C (conceal):** the channel carrying `R` to the controller is degraded, so `R` stops arriving.
  Fig 10 :24 names the failure exactly: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*.
- **R (repeat):** the past policy is stored as a recording and replayed. Fig 4 :25-26 names the
  result: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion.
  Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new
  one."*

The residual is therefore **not** "how do institutions get more information about failures." More
information inside a closed frame is more spelling, not more evidence (Fig 6 :49, Fig 8 :32).
The residual is: what keeps the channel open, what gives a seat the power to refuse a held term,
and what makes a correction cross between cycles as a method rather than a replay.

What an answer would look like, bound now as an ordinary term so it can be refused (formula
"Direction"): **a set of figure-constrained properties that any institutional arrangement must
have for a policy failure to change the reach, stated separately for the concealment condition and
the repetition condition, with a ground test for each.**

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

### 1.1 The residual by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** at least two conditions. Concealment is a failure of the channel to a seat;
  repetition is a failure of transmission across cycles. A case in which a concealed failure is
  also ritualised into a procedure is a specific compound of the two, not a harder case of either
  (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*).
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric across slots. A failure is indexed to the policy, place and
  population that produced it. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not
  aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an
  average."*
- **Scale:** first order inside one policy cycle (perceive, act, observe); second order across
  cycles, where one settled correction becomes a term for the next; third order at the seat that
  checks the checker. The loop is recursive and the level decides what is irreducible (Fig 12 :103).

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable anchor? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the policy frame (institution as agent) | officials, belief `b`, library Γ of policies | prediction `Γ(b,a)` against observation `o'`; residual per slot | the outcome of the policy, if the frame ranges against it; the frame's own credibility if it does not | yes, the outcome, which can be moved by what the frame thinks only if the frame owns the measurement |
| D2, the ground of the policy | affected population, counter, measurement, market, patient outcome, experiment | `o'`, what actually happened | the outcome itself; Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* | yes, and it is the only one that settles |
| D3, the verification seat (audit, court, open data, peer frame, reporting channel) | checker separated from the measured; convention author | residuals surfaced; conventions authored, never verdicts | keeps the ground reachable; must not author an atom or a verdict (Fig 10 :41-44) | yes, the reachability of the anchor |
| D4, the transmission frame (institutional memory across cycles and generations) | methods, head starts, recordings; the membrane | what crosses the membrane: reapplicable method or one-occasion recording | the round-trip gap `R_T` (Symbols :75-78, Fig 4 :30) | yes, `R_T`, measurable qualitatively as a list of dropped constraints |

### 1.3 Partition or union

This is a **union**, not a partition. Concealment (D1 to D3 channel failure) and repetition (D4
transmission failure) overlap, and a single lever aimed at one leaves the intersection standing
(Fig 9 :54-56). The honest move is to unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54) and keep verdicts per
domain. No averaging across domains; Fig 1 :22 forbids averaging the very signal in question.

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. **Perception (D1 to D2):** is the residual arriving at all? Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no
   instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
2. **Refusal (D3):** can a held term be refused once the residual arrives? Formula step 2 puts
   REFUTED before mint; a refusal is owed, not an explanation.
3. **Carry (D4):** does the correction cross as a method? Fig 3 :15: *"name what worked, verify it,
   and carry it to the next problem."*

Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* An
institution with excellent reporting templates but no independent seat has optimised a link above
the break.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - The question is a union, so there is no single mechanism (decisive for the shape)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

*The Library of Babel* (fetched this run, Isaiah N, 2026-10-02) says the same in its own field:
*"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You
will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them
in a satisfactory way."* This is world corroboration, not the source of the verdict.

Consequence: any answer of the form "institutions learn when they are transparent" is a filter
offered as a witness. Transparency can open a channel (C condition) without installing an operator
that can refuse (R condition), and can do both without making the correction cross as a method
(D4). Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 9 plus the essay, low overlap between a metalaw and a search
essay).

**Refuter:** show that concealment and repetition are one condition, so that a single lever clears
both the single-condition regions and the intersection. Until then, per-domain verdicts stand.

### F2 - Anti-concealment mechanism 1: keep the anchor reachable, and instrument the channel

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :25-26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, / saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 10 :27-28: **"the output is not a verdict, it is / you have lost touch"**
Fig 10 :29: **"→ keep the anchor reachable"**

Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."**
Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Concealment is a channel failure, not an ignorance failure. The four named ways a channel dies
are stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings. In institutional
terms: reports about last year's failure that no one reads; near misses never recorded;
indicators pinned at their ceiling; outcomes from different programmes pooled so the live slot
disappears. The honest output of a working channel monitor is not "the policy failed" but "you
have lost touch", which is a claim about the instrument, not the world.

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (world, corroboration only): *"If the maintainer is
swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a
working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."*

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 10 and Fig 2, low overlap between the seat-maintenance panel and
the triangulation geometry).

**Refuter:** a real institution in which the outcome measure changes its answer because of what
the institution thinks about it, and which nevertheless corrects its predictions against that
measure, would break this finding. Also broken if more agreement inside one frame were shown to
be sufficient for correction.

### F3 - Anti-capture mechanism 2: the keeper of the measurement must be disqualified from owning it

Fig 10 :86: **"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."**
Fig 10 :93: **"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."**
Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."**
Fig 10 :60: **"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."**

Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**
Fig 9 :51: **"what the verifier may see: evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"**

This is the structural core of the answer to "conceal." An institution that grades its own
policies with a metric it also produces (number of reviews completed, number of reports filed,
public satisfaction with its own communications) is executing the named output-layer defect. The
measure is not thereby false; it is disqualified as a verdict. The mechanism is to put the
reading in a seat outside the graded slice, and where that cannot be done, to declare the stake in
the record. Fig 10 :58: *"A seat with no stake at all does not exist."* The rule is not
stake-free; it is stake-declared and reading-independent.

Fig 10 :65: **"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."**
This blocks the easy substitute: a perfectly documented internal review is still a frame judging
itself if its keeper has a reputation to protect.

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 10, Fig 1, Fig 9; three figures, low overlap between seat rules,
metric rules and verifier-admissibility rules).

**Refuter:** an institution whose measurement keeper is the measured party and has a reputation
to protect in the result, shown to produce corrections that the ground pays for, with the keeper's
own credibility as the only witness. That would break Fig 10 :93 and Fig 1 :27 together.

### F4 - Anti-concealment mechanism 3: keep the residual indexed, never averaged

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 1 :28: **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."**
Symbols table :42-44: **"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."**

A national programme success rate is the aggregate that hides the live slot. Concealment in
institutions is often not a lie; it is an average. The mechanism is to publish outcomes at the
slot level (which policy, which place, which population, which quarter), so that the residual can
say where to look. Fig 10 :25-26 names "pooled readings" as one of the four channel killers;
F4 is the positive form of that same rule.

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 1 and the Symbols table, two distinct figures with low overlap).

**Refuter:** a case where the aggregate alone surfaced the live failure and the disaggregated
readings did not, with the slot-level data available and ignored. That would break the per-slot
rule in this domain.

### F5 - Anti-repetition mechanism 4: install negation, not just correction

Operators table :58: **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."**
Operators table :74: **"can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."**
Fig 5 :53: **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**
Fig 5 :55: **"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."**

Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**

An institution can have a rich vocabulary of adjustment: reallocate, retarget, retrain, revise
the implementation guidance. Those are subtraction and sequence. They can say what is missing
from the arrangement. They cannot say that the arrangement is wrong. Without the unary operator
of negation, the institution can be corrected and cannot carry the correction: the wrong premise
stays in the library and remains available as a base for the next policy. The mechanism is a
formal refusal: a way to declare a held policy false that pays the same bargain as a new term
(cost of the refusal plus leftover residual less than the original confusion), with the same three
guards. Formula step 3: *"NOTHING IS EVER DELETED, and the reach falls because something was ADDED."*
The old policy is not struck from the record; it loses its standing to be composed over.

Order matters. Formula step 2 routes REFUTED before mint: *"a term held is false; what is owed is a
refusal, not an explanation."* An institution that responds to a failure by minting a new
initiative before refusing the premise has reversed the loop.

World analogue, corroboration only: *double-loop learning* (Argyris and Schön, introduced 1978;
definition quoted in Chatti, Jarke and Schroeder, Springer, 2012) is *"learning that results in a
change in the values of theory-in-use, as well as in its strategies and assumptions."* This
rhymes with refusing a held term. It is not the same claim: the figure requires a named operator
and an ordering (refuse before mint), which the analogue does not supply. Recorded as
corroboration, never as derivation.

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 5 and the Operators table and Fig 12, low overlap between the mint
pipeline, the operator arity table and the bond analysis).

**Refuter:** an institution with no mechanism for declaring a held premise false, only adjustment
and addition, shown to prevent recurrence at the ground while keeping that premise composable.
That would break Operators :74 and Fig 5 :53.

### F6 - Anti-repetition mechanism 5: carry methods, measure the round trip

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
Fig 4 :23: **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**
Fig 4 :24: **"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."**
Fig 4 :25-26: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."**
Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

Formula step 6: *"PROMOTE generators cross up, playback never does."* Symbols :75-78 defines
`R_T` as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down"*.

"Lessons learned" that record the conclusion (what worked in 2009) rather than the method (how the
institution found out, what refuter it used, what residual it was answering) are recordings. They
produce exactly the behaviour named in Fig 4 :26: a system that repeats a past success and cannot
produce a new one. The mechanism is to carry the generator, and to bracket the crossing with a
round trip: send up the method, bring it down into the next policy cycle, and measure what the
coarser description could not hold.

World corroboration: Weick and Sutcliffe, *Managing the Unexpected* (Ross School summary, fetched
this run), on why success is the enemy of learning: *"Too often, success narrows perceptions,
breeds overconfidence in current practices and squelches opposing viewpoints."* And James Reason,
BMJ 2000 (fetched this run): *"High reliability organisations... Instead of isolating failures,
they generalise them. Instead of making local repairs, they look for system reforms."*

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 4, formula step 6, Symbols table; three low-overlap statements of
the same crossing rule).

**Refuter:** an institution that carries only conclusions across cycles, with no method and no
round-trip measurement, shown to generate a genuinely new correct prediction at the ground. That
would break Fig 4 :22-26.

### F7 - Anti-capture mechanism 6: select between rooms, and buy independence

Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**
Fig 2 :31-32: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."**
Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**
Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**

An internal consensus conference is one room. Its agreement is variation inside a closure, not a
verdict. The mechanism is selection between rooms whose closures differ, with the next room chosen
against the residual the last one surfaced. Note that this is not "more reviewers." Fig 8 :29
requires *"low overlap: B does not draw on A's evidence"*, and Fig 2 :40 disqualifies agreement
from shared pools.

Fig 7 :6 names the limit that prevents any of this becoming a final design: **"the limit: no last room"**.
Fig 7 :30: **"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"**.
So no institutional arrangement is the answer; each is a room in a chain that must keep differing.

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 7, Fig 2, Fig 8; three figures with low overlap between chain
selection, anchor geometry and union surplus).

**Refuter:** a chain of agreeing rooms sharing one evidence pool (a mirror chain, Fig 7 :12-13)
shown to correct a prediction against the ground. That would break Fig 7 :35 and Fig 2 :35.

### F8 - Mechanism 7: provenance on imports

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
Fig 6 :24: **"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."**
Fig 8 :51: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**
Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."**

Institutions import "best practice" constantly. Without a provenance record, an adopted import
and a convergence reached independently look the same in the contents, so an institution cannot
tell whether it has gained a new term or merely renamed one it held. The mechanism is a source
gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does it explain a gap), applied before the import is counted
as learning. Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."**

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 6 and Fig 8, low overlap between closure geometry and union
provenance).

**Refuter:** an import with no source record shown to add a genuinely new term to the institution's
reach, with the reach measured before and after. That would break Fig 8 :51.

### F9 - Mechanism 8: make the desired outcome an ordinary term before the run

Fig 5 :61: **"BUT THE TERM MOST WORTH REFUSING MAY NOT BE IN THE LIBRARY AT ALL."**
Fig 5 :62: **"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no"**
Fig 5 :65: **"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."**

An institution committed to "this policy works" holds the desired outcome as a preference, not as
a term. It accrues no residual, so the loop cannot check it; the institution can refuse the right
kind of thing and still aim the refusal at the outcome it did not want rather than at the belief it
holds (Fig 5 :63-64). The mechanism is the pre-registered refuter: write down what an answer would
look like before the run, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else. This is also
why the outreach packet leads with the refuter. Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the
direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

Fig 3 :29-30: **"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."**

Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 5 and the formula's Direction section, low overlap between pipeline
limitation and loop discipline).

**Refuter:** an institution that never writes down what success would look like before a policy,
shown to refuse a held belief because of a failure rather than to re-aim the refusal at an
unwanted outcome. That would break Fig 5 :62-65.

### F10 - What the figures do not give, and the honest stop

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

No figure line names a real institutional design (a particular committee, a freedom-of-information
statute, an inspector general, a reporting hotline) as the mechanism. The figures constrain the
shape of any design; they do not select among designs. Naming one would be a binding from the
world frame, provenanced as such, and not a derivation.

Also: whether a given arrangement *actually* learns is a ground question. Fig 11 :40:
**"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :73: **"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."**
An institution can install any subset of F2 to F9 and still fail if the change does not alter
contact with outcomes it cannot move.

Verdict: **single-source for the limit** (Fig 9 and Fig 13, same framework pool, low overlap in
content but shared origin; declared, not hidden).

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a specific institutional body as necessary and sufficient
for learning from failure. None was found after the census. If one is produced, F10 is retracted.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat-map discipline, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape
claim in `07_the_tether_answer.md` is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A real institution corrects its predictions against an outcome measure that changes its answer
   when the institution pushes on it, with the institution's own credibility as the only witness.
   (Breaks F2, F3; Fig 2 :30, Fig 10 :93.)
2. An institution whose measurement keeper is the measured party, with a reputation to protect in
   the outcome, produces ground-paid corrections with no independent seat anywhere in the chain.
   (Breaks F3; Fig 10 :86, :93, Fig 1 :27.)
3. An institution with no operator for refusal, only adjustment and addition, prevents recurrence
   at the ground while keeping the failed premise composable into new policy. (Breaks F5;
   Operators :74.)
4. An institution carrying only recordings of past successes, with no method and no round-trip
   gap, generates a genuinely new correct prediction at the ground. (Breaks F6; Fig 4 :25-26.)
5. A mirror chain, rooms sharing one evidence pool, corrects a prediction against the ground.
   (Breaks F7; Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :35.)
6. An import with no provenance record adds a genuinely new term to the reach, measured before
   and after. (Breaks F8; Fig 8 :51.)

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn, the domain census is redone, and the verdict is
rerun. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Treat the question as a union and report per-domain verdicts (F1). Do not average.
2. Lead with the channel and the keeper (F2, F3). Transparency without an independent keeper is
   more information inside a frame, which is not more evidence.
3. Publish failures at the slot level (F4), not as programme averages.
4. Install an operator for refusal, and put it before mint (F5).
5. Carry methods and measure `R_T` (F6); require provenance on imports (F8).
6. Bind the desired shape as an ordinary term before the run (F9).
7. Do not assert a final institutional design (F7, F10). Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward
   looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, not the
   conclusion.
