## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and
scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by
dependency. I did not use the primary's four domain names as a starting point.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an institution's predicted and
  observed policy outcome. Arity: I read it as **two conditions** because "we never saw the
  failure" and "we saw it and repeated the action" have different repairs, including in the
  compound case. Scale: one cycle for arrival, several cycles for transmission, one level up for
  the checker. Symmetry: asymmetric across slots; the failure belongs to a specific policy and
  population, and the programme average is precisely the object that hides it (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain A, the acting frame.** Actors: decision makers, the standing belief, the policy library.
  Currency: predictions and outcomes. Anchor: the outcome, unless the frame captures the measure.
- **Domain B, the outcome.** Actors: the affected world and its counters. Currency: what actually
  happened. Anchor: by definition; it fails the anchor test if the frame can move it (Fig 2 :30).
- **Domain C, the checking position.** Actors: whoever holds the reading and authors conventions.
  Currency: surfaced residuals. Anchor: reachability of the ground. This is where I differ slightly
  from the primary's emphasis: I read Fig 10 :93 (*"A ground with a reputation to protect..."*) as
  the load-bearing line of the whole solution, not merely one finding among eight.
- **Domain D, the crossing between cycles.** Actors: methods and recordings. Currency: what
  crosses. Anchor: the round-trip gap `R_T`.

Order by dependency: arrival before refusal, refusal before mint, method before storage of a
conclusion. The direction discipline crosses all of them.

**Comparison with the primary census.** Same four domains, reached by different starting figures:
I began with Fig 2 :30 and Fig 10 :93 (anchor and keeper), while the primary began with Fig 1 :19
and Fig 9 :54 (slot and union). Low overlap between routes, same boundaries. That is internal
consistency under a different starting point, not corroboration, because both routes are in one
evidence pool.

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran
the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure constraints, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :30, Fig 4 :22-26,
   Fig 5 :55, Fig 9 :54, Fig 10 :86 and :93, Fig 12 :117, and Operators :58 and :74.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: institutions learn when the residual reaches a
   seat that can refuse, the keeper is outside the graded slice, and the correction crosses as a
   method rather than a recording.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete design someone might build from that shorthand: publish
   disaggregated incident reports, appoint an independent reviewer, require an after-action review
   before the next policy cycle.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down unless
they are separately designed in:

- the **no-updating-anchor** requirement, which a published incident report does not supply if the
  measure itself is institution-controlled;
- the **keeper disqualification**, which an "independent reviewer" does not supply if the reviewer
  is inside the graded slice or has a reputation to protect in the result (Fig 10 :93);
- the **refusal operator**, which an after-action review does not supply if it can only adjust and
  add (Operators :74);
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**, which is breached the moment the concrete design is asserted as
  the right one (Fig 9 :32).

One thing survives cleanly: a residual must stay indexed to the slot, and a correction must cross
as a method rather than a replay. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured
numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set
of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set `R_T` to zero
and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

world source with genuinely low overlap, Vaughan's archival study, corroborates the coherent-drift

settlement of this solution.

non-updating anchor; a keeper outside the graded slice with declared stakes; a per-slot residual;
