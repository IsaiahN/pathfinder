## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0010 the shape is: a union split per slot, a threshold on duration and depth, a signed persistence cycle, a refusal operator for a regime change, and a declared gap where a real-time selector would go. The name of the selector, and the name of the winning school, are not in the shape.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also gives the exact move this question needs: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is Fig 9 :4 in plain language.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question: what you would expect to see if persistence were a signed cycle is a per-slot residual that stops trending down after a regime change, not merely a longer series of the same deviations.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place". The paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- The anchor is the question itself: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether".

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming "debt deflation" as the persistence mechanism is such a binding from the economics frame onto a held structural slot. It is legitimate only when its origin is recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So declaring "shock", "rigidity", "debt" and "regime" as the macro atoms makes a choice visible; it does not discover nature's own list.

## 4. The metamerism warning, retained

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". Several paths can create the same behavior, so "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Apply this to ECN-0010: an aggregate unemployment path or a national GDP series cannot comment on the source. Debt deflation, a liquidity trap, hysteresis and an adverse policy regime can produce similar aggregate readings. Naming one of them from the projection alone repeats the metamerism error. What is owed is a per-slot, source-discriminating instrument. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." That is why the shape insists on per-slot residuals and a declared gap for the selector.

## 5. Correction to my own first pass: "shape, not a name" is narrower than I first wrote

**Earlier reading:** the framework forbids naming a regime mechanism, so "debt deflation" and "liquidity trap" must not appear.

**Corrected reading:** the README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Debt overhang, a zero lower bound, hysteresis and a policy-regime switch are not missing primitives in the economics closure; they are atoms already available from the world frame. Naming one is a **binding**, not an invention, and the binding must be recorded with its provenance. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework derives which one, or that the binding adds a new atom to the closure (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" is about the wall, and a binding onto a held slot adds none).

**What is not retracted:** the metamerism objection in section 4. Naming the source of a single aggregate reading remains an error until a discriminating instrument exists. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous macroeconomists who agree that "one theory can explain both" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim against the data, with a written refuter.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too"; however, "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Seven-question check for this question

1. **Is the claim a shape?** Yes: union split plus signed cycle plus refusal plus gap, not a named winner.
2. **Is the shape obtained from the residual or from a preferred school?** From the residual (Fig 9 :27-28, describe before looking). The schools enter only as secondary corroboration in `09`.
3. **What would license a name?** A ground-settled measurement or a provenanced binding. Neither has occurred for the selector.
4. **Does the aggregate reading discriminate sources?** No. That is the metamerism warning, and it is the reason for per-slot indexing.
5. **Is "one theory" a final room?** Only under the strong reading, which is refused (Fig 7 :6). Under the generative reading it is permitted.
6. **Are the macro atoms nature's list?** No. They are a visible choice (They're Scanning Books).
7. **Can this document validate itself?** No. It is a simulation, and the framework cannot certify its own completeness.
