## 3. Three findings from the world that bear directly on the claim

1. **A real candidate unification exists, and its shape is exactly the conditional one.** The JEL review unifies three landmark downturns through negative natural rates, a zero lower bound, and a policy-regime switch. It is not a one-parameter covering law: it explicitly says the effects depend on the monetary-fiscal regime and central bank credibility, and that regime changes reversed deep slumps by shifting expectations. This corroborates F1, F2 and F7 of `01`. It is one evidence pool with the other macro sources, so its agreement is not a second witness.
2. **The category boundary is conventional; the mechanism is not.** The economic-depression source shows that the same chronology yields different "depression" spans under two definitions, and that NBER dates recessions but not depressions. That is direct world-side support for F3 and for the refusal to let a label decide a mechanism.
3. **The owed discriminator has a known shape but no settled instrument.** Regime-shift and early-warning sources show generic warning signals, hysteresis and differing flip and flip-back thresholds, while warning that detection is retrospective, data-hungry and not yet reliable. The output-gap source says potential output is not observable. Together they corroborate the *shape* of F9's gap without filling it.

A conflict was returned to the census rather than hidden: the secular-stagnation record that post-recession unification warnings have repeatedly been wrong conflicts with any strong claim that a compact general theory is already in hand. The census response was F11's minimum-description-length constraint. The figure verdict did not flip.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A unifying result sent before anyone has described a residual is a recording of one occasion, not a generator. Lead with the union split and the refuter.
- **Fig 9 :30** "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect". Describe the duration-depth pattern in effect terms before naming debt deflation, liquidity trap or hysteresis as its source.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong general theory applied to a live downturn, so independent checking is worth real effort.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and what instrument would settle the region assignment; (3) record that answer; (4) only then, if they are curious, the source links, with the note that the figures and the data, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer, and its precondition

Stage 1 checks the output against the ground. Stage 2 offers the theory, opt in only. Stage 3 offers the loop as a method, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode.

The precondition is that stage 1 survives. It does so only as a shape: a union split, a signed persistence cycle, a refusal operator and a declared gap. It does not survive as a claim about which named school is right, nor as a claim that a final theory has been found. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room."
