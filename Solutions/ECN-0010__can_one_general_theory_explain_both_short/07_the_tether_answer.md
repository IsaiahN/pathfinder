# The Tether's answer: can one general theory explain both short recessions and prolonged depressions?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape and on the refusal of a final frame, `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `gap` on the discriminator and on empirical adequacy. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We want a theory whose application to a pre-episode state predicts both the short-recession class and the prolonged-depression class. The residual is that any single-mechanism theory fits one region and misses the other, and that depression-specific mechanisms are added after the fact. Fig 9 :4: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The residual is per slot (households, firms, banks, policy regime, data vintage), not a national average (Fig 1 :19, :22).

## 2. The three verdicts, kept separate and never averaged

### Verdict 1: the strong reading of "one general theory" is refused (`general`, triangulated)

If "general" means a terminal frame that explains all downturns with one fixed parameter set and nothing further to ask, the framework forbids it. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room". Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." The canonical figures README devotes a section to why this is not a theory of everything. This is a structural refusal, not a prediction about which empirical model will win.

### Verdict 2: the generative reading is permitted, but only with a specific closure (`specified`, triangulated)

A theory can span both regions in the sense of a generator that composes per episode and per slot, provided its closure contains:

1. **A per-slot residual**, so that a live signal is not averaged away (Fig 1 :19).
2. **A declared threshold comparison `⋛`** on duration and depth. Operators :51: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one." The cut is a convention for the label and a filter for the budget, never a witness for the mechanism (Fig 9 :60).
3. **A persistence cycle with the right sign.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Debt deflation, liquidity traps and hysteresis are bindings onto structural slots, not names the framework derives.
4. **A refusal operator for a regime change.** Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A vocabulary that can only add mechanisms cannot record the 1933-style reversal of a held convention.
5. **A union map of the habitat**, not a list of causes. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
6. **An import with a source gate** for every persistence atom, because closure is idempotent (Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom").

Under a fixed atom set, a short episode and a depression are Fig 12 :117 isomers: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." If one requires an atom the other does not, only the weaker functional-group claim survives (Fig 12 :123). The fixed-atom condition is a live constraint with a written falsifier.

### Verdict 3: the real-time region selector is a `gap` (`specified` on its shape, `gap` on its identity)

No figure line names an instrument that assigns a live episode to the persistence region before the outcome. The owed instrument must be per-slot, directional (flip-in and flip-back points differ), regime-invariant under the Lucas critique, and built from a series already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34: "the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."). Low-overlap analogues from critical transitions and ecological regime shifts constrain that shape but do not supply it. Fig 13 :134: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." So this is **unreached at this budget**, not underivable in principle, and it is reported as a verdict and not as silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Keynes, Friedman, Minsky or any single school has won. World sources are secondary corroboration only.
- Not that a depression is a bigger recession, or that one parameter set fits both classes. That is the union error (Fig 9 :55-57).
- Not that the duration cut is a natural kind. It is a declared convention until a ground settles it (Fig 10 :18), and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that the framework names the persistence mechanism. It returns a shape; the name is a provenanced binding from the economics frame.
- Not that the 2025 JEL review settles the question. It is a candidate that corroborates the shape of a possible unified closure.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, no episode was dated, and no selector was run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

1. **Single-condition test:** do short and long episodes share one condition observed at two horizons, and does one lever clear both regions with a per-slot residual that trends down across independent data vintages? If yes, Verdict 2 collapses into a simpler theory.
2. **Cycle-sign test:** measure the sign of the re-entrant loop in a depression-class episode. A damped loop with depression-class depth and duration fires refuter F4.
3. **Refusal test:** identify a depth-to-recovery transition and ask whether it required refusing a held policy convention or only adding a propagation term. The former supports F7; the latter fires it.
4. **Selector test:** a real-time, non-updating, low-overlap region selector that survives a data revision. This is the gap in Verdict 3. The *Hard Problem is About Source* essay's spectrometer is the right analogue: the case was closed by an instrument, not an argument.
5. **MDL test:** state the compact closure and the episodic alternative under a declared code and compare `|φ| + left(R,φ)` with `|R|` (Fig 5 :43). A theory with a special term per episode fails.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The atoms named here are "a human's careful guess" at the macro compression axes; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another evidence pool might cut the regions differently. The framework returns the shape any adequate answer must have and the refuters written in advance, then stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The `⋛` operand type at the second-order, cross-episode level remains a live strain, named rather than hidden.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not pick a winner among schools of macroeconomics. They answer a prior question. "Short recession" and "prolonged depression" are threshold cuts on one measured trajectory, so they form a union of overlapping conditions, not two natural kinds and not one hard problem. A single lever clears the short-only region and leaves the overlap standing. A theory can be general as a generator that composes per episode, but only if it holds a per-slot residual, a declared duration cut, a persistence loop with the right sign, a refusal operator for a regime change, and the habitat conditions that select between regions. It cannot be general as a final covering frame, because there is no last room and no sealed room. The real-time selector that would assign a live episode to the persistence region before the outcome is a declared gap: the framework returns its shape, not its name. What is offered is that shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. Nothing here is settled by measurement.
