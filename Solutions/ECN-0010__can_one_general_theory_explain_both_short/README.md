## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe an answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, stated in the figures' own terms:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list chosen for likely assent manufactures the correlation that would make its assent worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence." Adding economists raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
3. **A verifier that holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03` are public institutional or opt-in routes.

## The short answer, for orientation only

The figures do not answer yes or no to the literal question. They answer a meta-question first. "Short recession" and "prolonged depression" are threshold cuts on one measured trajectory, so they form a **union of overlapping conditions**, not two natural kinds and not one hard problem (Fig 9 :4, :55-57). A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. A theory can be **general in the generative sense**, one closure that composes per episode and per slot and contains a threshold, a persistence cycle with the right sign, a refusal operator for a regime change, and the habitat conditions that select between regions. It cannot be **general in the final-room sense**: there is no last room (Fig 7 :6) and no sealed frame (Fig 9 :44). Which real-time instrument assigns a live episode to the persistence region before the outcome is a declared `gap`, reported as a verdict and not as silence (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134). Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged into a single percentage.
