# Expert verification packet: the Tether on short recessions and prolonged depressions

**What this is:** a claim in a form a macroeconomic theorist or economic historian can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape and the refusal of a final frame, `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `gap` on the region selector. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Short recession" and "prolonged depression" are threshold cuts on one measured trajectory, so they form a union of overlapping conditions rather than two natural kinds or one hard problem. A single fixed-parameter mechanism clears the short-only region and leaves the overlap standing. A theory can be general as a generator that composes per episode and per slot, but only if its closure holds a per-slot residual, a declared duration and depth comparison, a persistence cycle whose sign decides whether it closes, a refusal operator for a policy-regime change, and the habitat conditions that select between regions. It cannot be general as a final covering frame: there is no last room and no sealed room. Over a fixed atom set the two classes are isomers, with a functional-group fallback if the atom set is not shared. The real-time selector that assigns a live episode to a region before the outcome is a declared `gap`, reported as a verdict and not filled with a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 9, 12 and 7

- **Statement:** the two classes are a union, not a partition; one lever leaves the intersection standing; persistence is a signed cycle; and a terminal all-explaining frame is refused.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room".
- **Refuter:** show that short and long episodes are one condition at two horizons, so a single lever clears both exclusive and intersection regions with a residual that trends down per slot; or show a depression-class episode whose persistence is an open chain with no re-entrant output; or amend the figures to admit a terminal frame.

### C2: economics-facing, checkable against the data and the two literatures

- **Statement:** propagation mechanisms fit the short region, persistence mechanisms fit the long region and the overlap, and the 2025 JEL review's unification works by holding a negative-natural-rate condition plus a policy-regime switch, not by one parameter set. Over a fixed atom set the classes are isomers (Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."); otherwise they are functional-group variants (Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member.").
- **What to check:** is the union reading faithful to the episode record? Does a model with no persistence atom predict depression duration out of sample? Does the JEL review's framework actually keep the regime switch as an independent condition, or does it reduce persistence to propagation?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a model with no persistence atom in its primitives that predicts depression duration out of sample with a per-slot residual that trends down across independent data vintages; or exhibit an episode pair where one class requires an atom absent from the other's closure, which kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim.

### C3: framework-internal derivation with a live type strain

- **Statement:** each episode settled against the data becomes an atom at the cross-episode level, so a general theory composes over settled episodes without a seventh primitive; a regime reversal is a refusal, not another composition; and the `⋛` comparison at that level has an unsettled operand type.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 5 :59 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at". The Operand type is strained: the Operators table defines `⋛` as "a threshold relation between two quantities", and whether a settled molecule-now-atom counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument.
- **Refuter:** show a depth-to-recovery transition fully explained by adding a propagation term with no refusal of any held convention and with a residual that trends down; or prove settled molecules cannot enter a `⋛` relation at the higher level. This is the one type question still open; it is named, not hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict on Keynes versus Friedman versus Minsky, and not a claim that any school has won.
- Not a final theory of everything. The union centre stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not that a depression is a scaling up of an ordinary recession. The cut is a convention (Fig 10 :18) and the mechanism is a separate question.
- Not that the framework names debt deflation, liquidity trap or hysteresis as *the* persistence mechanism. Those are provenanced bindings from the economics frame.
- Not that the 2025 JEL review settles the question. It is a world-side candidate that corroborates the shape only.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, episode date or selector result is asserted here.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Specifically:

1. A single-condition result with a per-slot residual that trends down across independent data vintages (fires C1 and C2).
2. A cycle-sign measurement in a depression-class episode (fires or supports C1).
3. A depth-to-recovery transition with or without a refused convention (fires or supports C3).
4. A real-time region selector with a non-updating anchor and low-overlap instruments that survives a data revision (closes the declared gap).
5. An operand-type instrument for `⋛` at the second-order level (closes the live strain).
