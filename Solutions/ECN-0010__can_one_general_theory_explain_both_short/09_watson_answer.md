## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that one general theory can explain both short recessions and prolonged depressions. Option A: one fixed closure and parameter set predicts both outcome classes and is the final word. Option B: one generative closure spans both regions only if it holds a per-slot residual, a declared threshold, a signed persistence cycle, a regime refusal and a habitat map, while the real-time region selector stays a `gap` and the final-room reading is refused. What each leads to: A ships a covering-law claim with no region selector and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B ships a checkable shape, written refuters, a live type strain and an honest stop. The figures decide; world sources corroborate only.

Arity: two conditions plus their intersection, so a union compound. Symmetry: the short/long cut is a threshold comparison on duration and depth, not a symmetric join of two kinds. - **D1 Measured episodes.** Actors: output, employment, prices, bank balance sheets, data vintages. Currency: realised per-slot outcomes and their time path. Anchor: the realised path plus a counterfactual that does not move on announcement. Union of two regions on one trajectory. The category boundary is conventional, the regime difference is not.
- **D2 Propagation theory.** Actors: shocks, nominal rigidities, financial accelerator, expectations. Currency: fit to ordinary fluctuations. Anchor: ordinary recession data. Fits the short region only.
- **D3 Persistence theory.** Actors: debt overhang, zero lower bound, hysteresis, policy regime, credibility. Currency: fit to deep and long episodes. Anchor: depression episodes. Fits the long region and the intersection.
- **D4 Unifying claim.** Actors: natural rate, policy regime, selector condition, closure boundary. Currency: cross-episode prediction of region and duration. Anchor: out-of-sample episode assignment, currently unavailable. Union handled in one closure if the closure holds the selector.

The chain breaks at the discriminator: Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The strong reading is refused; the generative reading is permitted but conditional; the selector is a `gap`. "Short recession" and "prolonged depression" are threshold cuts on one measured trajectory, so they form a union of overlapping conditions, not two natural kinds and not one hard problem. A single lever clears the short-only region and leaves the intersection standing. A theory can span both regions only as a generator that composes per episode and per slot and holds a per-slot residual, a declared threshold comparison, a signed persistence cycle, a refusal operator for a regime change, and the habitat map that selects between regions. Over a fixed atom set the two classes are isomers; without a shared atom set only the functional-group claim survives. Cross-episode composition is legal because settled episodes become atoms, but the `⋛` operand type at that level is a live strain. A final all-explaining frame is structurally refused. The real-time region selector is unreached at this budget, reported as a verdict and not as silence. This result is `specified` where the figures govern and `gap` where they stop. It is not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three checkable sub-claims, a pre-registered refuter and the live type strain, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a covering-law conclusion instead of a generator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The strong-reading claim, the named-mechanism claim and the category-as-natural-kind claim are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no macro atom has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A single-condition model with a per-slot residual that trends down across independent vintages would collapse the union into one mechanism; a cycle-sign measurement in a depression-class episode would fire or clear F4; a depth-to-recovery transition with no refused convention would fire F7; a real-time non-updating low-overlap selector that survives a data revision would close the `gap`; an operand-type instrument at the second-order level would settle the `⋛` strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
