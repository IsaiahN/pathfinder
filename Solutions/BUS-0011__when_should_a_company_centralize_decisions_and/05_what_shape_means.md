## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "a shape, not a name." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The README adds "Indiscernibility is not identity."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form of a decision-rights rule that can be stated before any particular org chart is named. It constrains: an answer must say where the residual is measured, where the ground is reachable, and what may cross between levels. It does not fill: it cannot say "centralize 60 percent" or "adopt a matrix structure." "Authority follows the slot structure of the residual and the location of reachable ground contact" is a shape. "Give pricing to regional VPs" is a name, and a company-specific one at that.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already available that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with "authority", "ground", "slot", "method" and let the figure lines refine them.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the rule is written in terms of observable residuals and ground readings, not in terms of "empowerment" or "alignment", which are already conclusions in someone's vocabulary.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach material leads with what a reader would expect to see if the slot-and-ground rule were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question the stop is when a decision class can be assigned a level and a refuter without importing a new term.
- The same essay names the exact structural error this question makes when treated as one lever: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things license moving from a shape to a name, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework stops "at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For a company, a ground reading on the slot would license a specific decision-rights assignment.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A colleague's spreadsheet factor is not such an import unless it is attributed and shown to explain a gap the local closure could not.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the second point. An instrument that reads one value "cannot comment on the source." "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Applied to organization design: a high margin in a region is a reading. It does not say whether the local decision was right, because several arrangements could have produced the same margin. Naming the source (good local judgment versus a favorable territory) from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a new org chart.

## 4. Why the question's usual framing is the forbidden error

The usual framing asks for a single degree of centralization. That has the shape of a comparison on one quantity. Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." A scalar "degree of centralization" can name a state and cannot name a route to one (Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."). The decision-rights question needs the bond: which evidence, at which level, crossed to which decision.

The JPART study put it in the field's own vocabulary: "moderation matters more than the polar ends" and the findings "challenge the conventional thinking that the choice between centralization or decentralization is binary." That is F5 restated by an independent field, and it is corroboration only.

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular company's decision-rights assignment?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular assignment is licensed only by a ground reading on the slots, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the assignment is the designer's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the declared `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10, and it is of the shape-not-a-name kind, not the unreachable kind.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming any level at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive. "Authority", "ground", "slot" and "method" are all held terms in the derivation, so assigning a decision class to a level is a binding from the company frame onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework itself names a universal org chart. Both readings are kept here so the correction is visible rather than quietly applied.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A submission to a prestigious journal gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not make the claim true. A list of well-known people who like the answer is the named collapse.

The same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." That is Fig 2 in prose, and it is why the outreach packet asks for a refutation rather than an endorsement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. Contact, not persuasion

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* explains why the first contact must carry only the residual and its refuter. Tacit knowledge "carries the context well and the subtext partly, and it almost never carries the pretext", so a compressed framework explanation unpacks only for someone who already shares the history. The essay quotes Polanyi: "we can know more than we can tell." What travels is a description of effects. It also quotes the figure rule "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down" (Fig 4 :22), which is the practical reason stage 3 gives the method away while stage 1 withholds the framework.

And the drift warning: two people who only check against each other drift together without noticing. "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it" (Fig 2 :45). A conversation about whether centralization feels right is not a check.
