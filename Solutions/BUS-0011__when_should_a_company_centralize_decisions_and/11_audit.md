## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an operational decision-rights assignment for a particular company, the table in `07` §3.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an added measurement:

- the **union structure**, which forbids treating the rows of the table as points on one centralization scale;

One thing survives cleanly: if a decision's residual is live per slot and its ground is independently reachable there, delegating it keeps the signal that a company-wide average deletes. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no company ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "centralize what is common, decentralize what is local" as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
