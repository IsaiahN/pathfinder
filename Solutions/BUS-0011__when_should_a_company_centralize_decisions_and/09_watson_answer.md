# WATSON answer: when should a company centralize decisions, and when give authority to local teams?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No company was measured.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about allocating decision authority between a central frame and local teams. Option A: centralize one decision class for all slots. Option B: delegate it to the slots. What each leads to: A keeps a rule uniform and can supply an atom no local closure holds, but it averages away a slot-level residual (Fig 1 :22) and can turn the center into a mirror (Fig 2 :35). B keeps the per-slot signal and local ground contact, but it cannot create an atom the local closure lacks (Fig 6 :21) and can drift into an informational cascade if locals only copy one another. Restated: R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), with the anchor the customer or market outcome that does not update on frames (Fig 2 :29).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: N slots, N greater than one. Symmetry: slots are not interchangeable, an actor's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Scale: first order at the slot, second order at the center, third order at the portfolio. Union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57).
- **D1 Slot decision.** Actors: local team, customer, local conditions. Currency: per-slot residual R_s. Anchor: the slot's ground return. Admitted.
- **D2 Center among frames.** Actors: central decision makers and local teams. Currency: agreement versus independence. Anchor: the same non-updating ground. Admitted.
- **D3 Membrane.** Actors: center and locals. Currency: methods versus recordings. Anchor: whether what crosses is a reapplicable method. Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat.** Actors: local actors, conditions, relations. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **D5 Import and closure.** Actors: local closure, peer closures, specialist frames. Currency: atoms added to a closure. Anchor: provenance and shadow test. Admitted.
- **X1 The right org chart.** Actors: none nameable. Currency: prestige. Anchor: none. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 One best degree of centralization.** Actors: the CEO. Currency: influence. Anchor: already inside D2. Absorbed.

**Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The decision classes overlap, so one lever leaves the intersections standing. **Order by dependency:** perceive the slot residual, then have terms for what the slot affords, then state progress, then plan and delegate (Fig 3 :20). Strain test applied: Fig 2 governs updating and independence; Fig 11 governs contact and habitat. A center may route and rule without authoring a verdict. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains, a convention that fixes an outcome (D3/D2), recorded in F8 of `01` and not smoothed.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated | single-source | gap)

Cited against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Quotes are verbatim from the SVGs.

**D1 Slot decision, status triangulated (Fig 1 per-slot residual and Fig 11 habitat contact, low overlap).**

- Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." -> authority belongs where the slot's signal can be read.
- Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." -> central pooling deletes the local signal.
- Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> improving the center in isolation does not help the slot.
- Reading: delegate slot-specific judgments with independently reachable ground. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a slot-live decision decided better centrally on a ground measure.

**D2 Center among frames, status triangulated (Fig 2 anchor and Fig 7 room chain, different quantities).**

- Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." -> market or customer outcome is the anchor; an internal dashboard is not.
- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." -> agreement between rooms is not a check.
- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." -> centralize by selecting between differing rooms.
- Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" -> an escalation chain is motion, not progress.
- Reading: the center ranges against a ground it cannot move; it does not become a party to the disagreement. Mode `general`. Refuter: a ground moved by internal updating with no independent reading detecting it.

**D3 Membrane, status single-source (Fig 4) with low-overlap engineering corroboration.**

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." -> centralize method, not the particular decision.
- Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." -> a price fixed centrally is a recording, not a method.
- Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" -> a standard that fixes a value is a verdict.
- Reading: method up, head start down, recording never. Mode `general` for the rule, `specified` for the standard split. Refuter: a replayed recording that improves independent ground readings, or an outcome-fixing convention that stays checkable by a non-author.

**D4 Habitat, status triangulated (Fig 11 and Fig 1, contact versus measurement).**

- Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." -> authority follows contact with the relevant habitat.
- Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." -> list contacts; do not assume direct contact is enough.
- Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." -> no standing list of decisions that belong at the top.
- Reading: a central decision substitutes a habitat, with silent failures (Fig 11 :68-69). Mode `general`. Refuter: an isolated central improvement that measurably helps local ground outcomes.

**D5 Import and closure, status triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 8, wall expansion versus idempotence).**

- Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." -> a local team cannot derive what it cannot represent.
- Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." -> central provision of a missing atom is legitimate.
- Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." -> more local search does not create the missing atom.
- Fig 6 :24 "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." -> import is priced; default centralization is an unpriced atom.
- Reading: centralize a genuinely missing atom with provenance and a shadow test. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a local team with the atom absent locally reaching the same ground without contact with a differing closure.

**Method, status triangulated (Fig 9 union and Fig 3 dependency order).**

- Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." -> unbundle the decision portfolio.
- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." -> size and geography are filters, not licenses.
- Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." -> objective before plan.

**The numerical allocation and the universal org chart, status `gap`.** No figure line returns a percentage or a chart. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." This gap is of the shape-not-a-name kind: a company's own ground readings would settle it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not return centralize or decentralize. They return a shape: authority follows the slot structure of the residual and the location of reachable, non-updating ground contact. Centralize methods, shared meanings, instruments and atoms genuinely absent from local closure. Do not centralize a slot-specific judgment whose ground the local team can read, do not replay a recording of one success down the hierarchy, and do not escalate through rooms that share a dashboard. Treat a standard that fixes an outcome as a decision, not a convention. The question is a union, so verdicts are kept per decision class and never averaged into a single percentage. The numerical allocation for any particular company is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Low-overlap world sources corroborate the mechanism shape; none sets the allocation, and none was allowed to overrule a figure.

- **cost if wrong:** misallocated decision rights that look correct from inside. A slot-live decision averaged into a company number hides the local failure (Fig 1 :22), while a missing atom imported without provenance costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24). The cost is bounded and locatable because the shape states which test failed, but it is paid in real customer and safety outcomes, not only in diagrams. Buying independence only when the cost of being wrong is high is Fig 2 :42's instruction, not an afterthought.
- **undoable:** yes. No decision right is deleted if a class fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). A reallocation can supersede the earlier one without withdrawing the method layer.
- **reversed by:** any of the pre-registered falsifiers, namely (1) a slot-live decision decided better centrally on a ground measure across independent slots, (2) a pooled reading retaining a local signal the per-slot reading missed, (3) a ground moved by two internal frames updating on each other with no independent reading catching it, (4) an isolated improvement to the central team that improves local outcomes with no change in contact, or (5) one lever clearing the whole portfolio including the intersections, measured rather than assumed. A company-specific ground reading that assigns every decision class would close the `gap`. Prestige, agreement and a well-known framework are not such readings.
