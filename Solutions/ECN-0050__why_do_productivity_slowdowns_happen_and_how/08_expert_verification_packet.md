# Expert verification packet: the Tether on productivity slowdowns

**What this is:** a claim in a form a productivity economist or economic measurement specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the procedure, not `grounded`.** No economic statistic was re-estimated. This is a shape and a check list, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A measured fall in output per hour is a reading, not automatically a fact about productive capability. The ground can hold still while the channel degrades through stale price indices, new goods booked as inflation, unmeasured intangible investment, and sector mixes averaged into one rate. So "genuine versus measured" is a ground-versus-channel discrimination: the first instrument owed is a set of independent readings ranged separately at the same ground, with agreement from a shared data pool counted as one witness. The residual must be read per sector, because a near-trend national average is compatible with live slot-level slowdowns. On the generative side, a genuine rise in productive reach needs an import and time to compose around it, so more research inside a fixed technique set grows the library without growing the reach. The framework names no historical cause and offers the shape, the refuters and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** a measured fall may be a channel event, not a ground event; the statistic is not automatically an anchor for "genuine productivity."
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25-26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **Refuter:** show that for the question asked the measured rate is the ground by constitution (Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it"), so no channel distinction applies; or show a measure that moves only when the ground moves.
- **Economics check:** is the official output-per-hour series a reading whose value changes with conventions? BLS Q2 2026 nonfarm business productivity was revised up 0.7 percentage point, which the receipt itself documents.

### C2. Economics-facing, checkable against published industry and adjustment data

- **Statement:** the national rate hides live slot-level residuals, and alternative adjustments change the reading materially.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"; Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **Refuter:** a slowdown uniform across every sector and firm, with no dispersion and no revision, surviving every alternative measurement. That would make Fig 1 :22 inapplicable to the case.
- **Economics check:** BLS 2025 data show total factor productivity fell in 70 of 86 four-digit manufacturing industries while 15 of 30 service industries rose; the J-Curve paper reports a TFP level "15.9 percent higher than official measures by the end of 2017"; Feldstein (2017) says "The official measures provide at best a lower bound on the true real growth rate with no indication of the size of the underestimation." Do these readings share the same underlying national accounts, or do they discriminate sources? If they share it, they are one witness (Fig 2 :40).

### C3. Framework-internal derivation about generative reach

- **Statement:** more composition inside a fixed technique set expands the library, not the productive reach; a genuine rise needs an import.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"; Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall"; Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not".
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a case where more composition within a fixed technique set raises productive reach without bound and no import is required.
- **Economics check:** is the post-2000 slowdown better described as exhausted technique (no new atom) or as a slow absorption of a new general-purpose technology with complementary intangible investment? The J-Curve paper supports the second reading but does not settle the historical question. This is the one sub-claim where the figure rule is `general` and the economic mapping is `specified` and unmeasured.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a name for any historical cause. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32).
- Not "mismeasurement explains it all." Adjustment size is a filter for budget, not a witness for verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not "the slowdown is entirely real." "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
- Not a derivation of economic growth from the six physical substrate terms. Only rules migrate, not atoms (Fig 13 :74).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this run.
- Not endorsed by any named economist. Prestige is not action: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

## 5. What would settle it

1. Does the fall survive revision and every alternative data vintage?
2. Does it appear in every sector, or is it concentrated in live slots the average hides?
3. Do hedonic, intangible-adjusted, sectoral and physical readings fall together *without sharing source data*?
4. Does the rate move when the measurement convention is pushed (new-good treatment, quality adjustment, classification)?
5. Does a new general-purpose technology and its complementary investment arrive, with a reach expansion after the J-curve lag?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :24-28, Fig 2 :29-30 and Fig 1 :13-22, three short passages.
2. Check C2 against a BLS industry release or the J-Curve abstract: do the slot-level readings exist, and do the alternative measures share source data?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a fall in capability from a fall in the channel at current resolution, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
