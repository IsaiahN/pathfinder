## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a fall in measured productivity growth below trend. Option A: treat the rate as a fact about productive capability and explain the fall causally. Option B: treat the rate as a reading, first discriminate a change in the ground from a change in the channel, then explain whatever genuine component survives. What each leads to: A risks explaining a measurement event and choosing the wrong policy lever; B demands independent instruments, per-slot residuals and a stated refuter before any cause is named. The question is a union of two conditions (genuine fall, measurement artifact), not one hard question (Fig 9).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no historical cause does. A measured fall is a reading, not automatically a ground event, so the first task is a ground-versus-channel discrimination using independent instruments ranged at the same ground, with agreement from a shared data pool counted as one witness. The residual must be read per sector, because a near-trend average is compatible with live slot-level slowdowns. A genuine expansion of reach needs an import and time to compose around it; more research inside a fixed technique set grows the library without growing the reach. The genuine and measurement readings are isomers over the same ingredients, differing in the bond between reading and source, which is invisible in the headline rate and decisive in the policy response. The constitutional-anchor reading remains a strain, the substrate analogue remains limited to rules rather than atoms, and this is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is choosing the wrong policy lever: stimulating an economy whose slowdown is a measurement event, or re-measuring one whose capability genuinely fell. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" prices the remedy; the cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no statistic is asserted as settled. The discrimination procedure can be revised when an instrument discriminates sources, and a superseded shape can be sent without retracting the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A revision-stable, slot-uniform fall that survives every alternative measurement would pay for the genuine-ground claim; a measure shown to be the ground by constitution (Fig 2 :36) would dissolve the channel distinction; a demonstration that composition inside a fixed technique set raises reach without bound would break Fig 6 :21; an instrument that names the source of a productivity reading directly would retire the metamerism limit. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
