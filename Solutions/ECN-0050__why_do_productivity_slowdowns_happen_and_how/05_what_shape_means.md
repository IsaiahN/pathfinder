## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the cause

The canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."

So for this question a **shape** is the discriminating structure we can state before we can say which historical episode had which cause:

- a measured rate is a reading, not automatically the ground (Fig 10 :24);
- the residual must be read per slot, not only as a national average (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- ground change and channel change are two conditions of a union that must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54-57);
- a genuine rise in reach needs an import, not just more composition (Fig 6 :14, :21);
- the verdict comes from independent instruments ranged at the ground, not from agreement (Fig 2 :35, :42).

That is a shape. "The 1973 slowdown was caused by oil shocks" or "the post-2000 slowdown was entirely mismeasurement" are names, and the framework does not supply them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. The searcher narrows by proximity. "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-23): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." And "a measurement with no question is noise, and a reading with no encounter is projection."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction to my own first reading

Two things only, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

**Correction to §1 and §2 as first drafted.** I initially wrote that the framework can never name a cause in this domain, citing "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. Here the candidate causes (oil shocks, intangibles, sector mix, GPT absorption lag) are terms already held in the economics frame; selecting one is a **binding** from that frame onto a slot, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is presenting the binding as derived from the figures, or claiming it moves the wall. Fig 13 :134-135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The correction narrows the rule, it does not remove it: no historical cause is named in this folder.

## 4. Why the metamerism point is the core of this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What resolved it was not an argument but a better instrument: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

A productivity rate is the same kind of projection. A real fall in productive capability, a quality change booked as inflation, an unmeasured intangible investment, and a shift of weight toward sectors whose measured productivity is near zero by construction can all produce the same falling rate. The reading does not determine the source. The honest response is to ask what instrument would discriminate among sources, not to pick one from the reading. The relevant figure lines are Fig 13 :16 "it names what is being measured" and Fig 13 :26 "it decides what the measurement would mean": a measurement requires both a declared object and a declared interpretation.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question that means a well-known economist's endorsement of "the slowdown is real" or "it is all measurement" has zero verdict weight. It can get the shape claim a hearing (a budget to be tried). It cannot settle whether the ground moved. The outreach material in `02_outreach_email.md` therefore asks for a refutation, never an endorsement.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this folder's other derivations are simulated, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of any claim about productivity and cannot settle the question they report on. That limitation is stated on every substantive file.

## 7. The shape, in one paragraph

The shape of an answer to "why do slowdowns happen, and how do we tell genuine from measured?" is a discrimination procedure plus a generative constraint: read the residual per slot rather than as a single average; separate a change in the ground from a change in the channel and treat the measured rate as an instrument until an anchor test says otherwise; unbundle the two conditions before searching; range independent instruments at the same ground and refuse to count agreement from a shared data pool; and expect a genuine expansion of reach to require an import and time to compose around it. The shape names none of the historical causes. Those remain bindings from the economics frame, owed provenance and a ground reading before they can be anything more.
