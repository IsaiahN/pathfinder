# Outreach draft: check the discrimination claim before the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the recipient can see the shape claim and its refuter without holding any framework interior. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

---

**Subject:** One claim about telling a real productivity slowdown from a measurement artifact

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to read any theory first. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The shape claim (`general`, not `grounded`):** a measured fall in output per hour is a reading, not automatically a fact about productive capability. The thing being measured can hold still while the channel degrades: stale price indices, new goods counted as inflation rather than output, unmeasured intangible investment, and sector mixes averaged into one rate. So "is this slowdown genuine?" is a ground-versus-channel discrimination, and the first instrument owed is a set of independent readings ranged separately at the same ground, not a stronger causal story. A second structural point: more research inside a fixed technique set adds to the library of methods but does not by itself expand what is reachable; a genuinely new productive reach needs an import, which in this field means a general-purpose technology and its complementary investments.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular decade's slowdown was an artifact, that mismeasurement explains all or none of it, that the size of a statistical adjustment settles the question, or that a six-term physical substrate derives economic growth. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am not asking you to accept the framework.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written down before I sent this):**
> 1. For the question asked, the measured rate is the ground by constitution, so no ground-versus-channel distinction applies.
> 2. A genuine slowdown is shown in which every sector and firm moves together, no dispersion exists, and the result survives every alternative measurement and every data revision.
> 3. More composition inside a fixed technique set is shown to raise productive reach without bound, with no import.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Bureau of Labor Statistics, Office of Productivity and Technology, methods and latest data: https://www.bls.gov/productivity/
> - NBER Economic Measurement Research Institute: https://www.nber.org/emri
> - Figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** write down what an answer would look like, bind it as an ordinary claim, and state what would refute it before you run anything. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and its three refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the sources. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they hold the frame.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Hard precondition: stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives contact. This is a `general` shape and a `specified` procedure, not a ground reading, and the email says so.
