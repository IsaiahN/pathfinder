# The Tether's answer on productivity slowdowns

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the discrimination procedure, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No economic measurement was re-estimated.

## 1. The residual it answers

Measured productivity growth falls below trend. The question bundles two conditions: did productive capability actually fall, and did the instrument stop registering output that moved? Fig 9 :54-57: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again", because "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The two conditions have different grounds and different remedies, so they need separate loops and separate verdicts. They are never averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, generative.** A genuine slowdown is best read as a stall in the productive reach or a lag in absorbing a new general-purpose technology, not a shortage of effort. Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"; Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall". More papers, patents and process tweaks can grow the library while the reach stays put (Fig 7 :13, "agreement compounds, evidence does not"). The world analogue is the J-curve: intangible complementarities are "often intangible and poorly measured in national accounts" and produce early understatement followed by later overstatement (Brynjolfsson, Rock and Syverson 2021). The framework cannot name which general-purpose technology or which decade; it returns a shape.

**D2, measurement.** A measured fall is a reading, not automatically a fact about the ground. Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does", with the named channel failures "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25-26). An anchor is "whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29-30). If the rate moves when the measuring convention is pushed, it is not an anchor for "genuine productivity." The framework's metamerism essay states the underlying limit: a projection "cannot comment on the source."

**D3, aggregation.** The national rate is an average over slots. Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"; Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." BLS 2025 industry data show why: total factor productivity fell in 70 of 86 four-digit manufacturing industries while 15 of 30 service industries rose, and headline numbers are revised materially between vintages.

**D4, verification.** Telling genuine from measured requires triangulation with independence, not agreement. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Rival estimates that all read the same national accounts are one witness (Fig 2 :40). Find no artifact and you have not proved there is none (Fig 8 :39).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a name for any historical cause. No oil shock, no intangible investment, no sector shift is asserted as *the* cause. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32).
- Not "mismeasurement explains the slowdown." The size of an adjustment is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not "the slowdown is entirely real." Failure to find a measurement artifact is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
- Not a derivation of economics from the six physical substrate terms. Only the rules migrate; the atoms do not (Fig 13 :74; `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q4).
- Not a settled statistic. Fig 5 :69 "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The question is not settled by another argument. It is settled by an instrument that discriminates among the sources of a reading. From the metamerism essay: a spectrometer, not a debate, resolved the green-light case. Here the concrete tests are:

1. **Revision test.** Does the fall survive the next data vintage and every alternative vintage? BLS Q2 2026 was revised up 0.7 percentage point, so the reading is not the ground.
2. **Slot test.** Does the fall appear in every sector and firm, or is it concentrated in live slots that the average hides (Fig 1 :13)?
3. **Independent-instrument test.** Do hedonic-adjusted prices, intangible-adjusted TFP, sectoral series and physical indicators all fall together *without sharing source data*? If yes, that is triangulation; if they share the same accounts, it is one witness.
4. **Border test.** Does the measured rate move when the measurement convention is pushed (new-good treatment, quality adjustment, sector classification)? If yes, it fails the anchor test of Fig 2 :29-30.
5. **Import test.** Does a genuinely new general-purpose technology and its complementary investment arrive, and does the reach expand after the J-curve lag? A rise in the count of methods alone does not settle this.

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4: the rate is the ground by constitution; a uniform slowdown survives all alternative measurement and revision; composition inside a fixed technique set raises reach without bound; a fully independent instrument discriminates source at current resolution.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape and a refuter, then stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. The canonical README says "Indiscernibility is not identity." Two strains remain named: whether the question is constitutively about the measured rate (Fig 2 :36), and the limit of migrating physical figure rules to economic atoms. Neither was smoothed. This folder's second seat shares the same evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the cause of any historical productivity slowdown. It gives the shape an answer must have. A measured fall in output per hour is a reading, not automatically a fact about productive capability: the ground can hold still while the channel degrades through stale prices, new goods booked as inflation, unmeasured intangible investment, and sectors averaged into one rate. So the first task is a ground-versus-channel discrimination using independent instruments ranged at the ground, not agreement inside one data pool. The national rate must be read per sector, because a near-trend average is compatible with live slot-level slowdowns. And a genuine expansion of productive reach needs an import, a new general-purpose technology and time to compose around it, so more research inside a fixed technique set grows the library without growing the reach. What is offered is that shape, three refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. No cause is named until the ground settles it.
