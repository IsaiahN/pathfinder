## Wider literature that bears on the question but was not fully read

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the shape and the refuter.
- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so offering it as a method is permitted; sending a recording is not.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Validate the output first.** Send the residual and the three refuters. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure extract and the economic sources. The framework interior stays closed until stage 1 is recorded.
3. **Reusable method.** Offer the formula with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode, and names their own ground.

No bulk send, no personal emails, no receptiveness ranking, no request for trust. Public institutional venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` only.
