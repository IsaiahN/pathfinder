## Residual in one line

Measured output per hour falls below its prior trend: how much of that gap is a change in the thing being measured (the ground), how much is a change in the measuring channel, and what generative mechanism produces a genuine fall? Arity two, a union compound (Fig 9), not one hard question.

## What survives
