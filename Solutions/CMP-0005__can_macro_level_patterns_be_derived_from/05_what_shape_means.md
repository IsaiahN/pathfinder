## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0005 the shape is not a list of derivable macro patterns. It is the round trip with a measured gap: `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, a declared split of what survives and what is given up, and a refuter that would kill the claim.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and, in the same passage, "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19) states the scale point directly: "On every level of a system, data is lost. Context changes based on the environment." This is the essay-level form of Fig 4 :27.

For this question the "closest vocabulary" is coarse-graining, entropy, effective complexity and the renormalization group from statistical mechanics. Those are borrowed words. Fig 9 :30 says to describe the effect, so the answer is stated as a gap and a survivor set, not as a commitment to any one formalisation.

## 3. What licenses a verdict, and what licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (2026): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Consequence here: saying "macro patterns are derivable" or "macro patterns are irreducible" as a universal is a name where only a shape is available. What is available is the shape of the measurement that would decide it for a stated system.

## 4. The source-discrimination point, applied

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". Metamerism: the reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". What resolved the colour case was the spectrometer, not an argument.

Applied to CMP-0005: a macro reading (temperature, density, a pattern statistic) is compatible with many micro states. Naming one micro derivation as *the* source of the macro pattern is the metamerism error. The correct instrument is the one that discriminates sources, which here is a measured round trip under a declared macro map. This is a second, independent reason for `R_T`: not only is the trip lossy (Fig 4), the reading cannot identify its own source (essay). The two claims are kept separate rather than merged.

## 5. The specific shape for CMP-0005

The framework's answer to the literal question is a refusal of the premise plus a route:

- Refusal: "without losing essential information" is not a property of a cross-scale transform. Fig 4 :27 and :28-29.
- Route: measure `R_T`; split "essential" into the kernel reading (survives) and the detail reading (does not cross; reappears as noise, Fig 13 :31-32); describe the residual before choosing a field (Fig 9 :27-28).
- Limit: a shape, not a catalogue. Fig 9 :32.
- Status: `specified`, ungrounded. No source-discriminating instrument was run.

That is the whole deliverable. It is intentionally not a yes or a no standing alone, because a bare verdict would be a shape the framework says it cannot return.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." In this field that means a list of well-known complex-systems researchers agreeing that macro patterns are "really" derivable is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the round trip and let a ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
