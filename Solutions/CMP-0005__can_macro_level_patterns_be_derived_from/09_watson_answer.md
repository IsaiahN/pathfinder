## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that macro-level patterns can be derived from micro-level rules without losing essential information. Option A: the crossing is information preserving, `R_T = 0`, so the macro level is shorthand and micro optimisation is the whole task. Option B: the crossing is lossy, `R_T` is measurable, the kernel crosses while the detail does not, and the discarded detail reappears as noise. What each leads to: A licenses dropping the macro level and optimising the micro solver; B licenses measuring the gap, keeping the macro frame with its own ground, and asking what survived and where the loss went.

The sought shape was bound before the run: "essential information" is split into the kernel reading and the detail reading, and the deliverable is a round trip with a measured gap plus a refuter, not a bare yes or no.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The answer is not a bare yes or no. On the literal detail reading, **no**: the transform between scales loses information by construction, and `R_T` is the measurable report of what was given up. On the kernel reading, **yes**: the reapplicable method crosses, though the level it lands on remakes it. The discarded detail does not vanish; it reappears as noise above the crossing. Aggregation is one concrete mechanism of the loss. Irreducibility is level-relative, and a settled macro pattern becomes an atom for the next composition. Underivability from a closure is different from unreachability at a budget, so the honest deliverable is a shape and a route: measure `R_T` under a declared macro map, name the survivor and the loss, and keep the verification frame anchored to a ground rather than to agreement. All of this is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded, from a simulated derivation. The strongest single figure-grounded point is Fig 4 :27, that the loss is the point, not an error.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuter before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework instead of a characterised residual. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The two readings of "essential" are stated separately and can be revised independently; no primitive must be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. Prestige or agreement cannot substitute for the ground (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it.").
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured `R_T = 0` over a declared domain would break the decisive verdict. Detail shown to vanish without reappearing as noise would break Fig 13 :31-32. A macro pattern derived from inside one closure while constituting a genuine new level would collapse the union and force re-census. A derivation of one of the six from the other five would break level-relative irreducibility. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
