# The Tether's answer: macro patterns from micro rules

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A micro-rule set is held, and a macro pattern is asserted to be derivable from it without losing essential information. The residual is the asserted absence of a gap. In effect terms the question is whether the round trip `x -> (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)` is the identity. Fig 9 treats a residual as the selecting instrument: describe it before choosing a frame. Here the description yields one decisive domain (the membrane between scales) and three supporting ones.

## 2. The shape, per domain (never averaged)

**D1, the micro-rule frame.** Compositions inside `closure(Γ)` are derivable from its atoms; nothing outside that closure is reached by search. Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Verdict: `general`, triangulated with Fig 6 :23.

**D2, the macro-pattern frame.** The coarser description holds only what the gap leaves. It is reapplicable, not a replay. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Verdict: `general`, triangulated with Fig 4 and the Symbols table.

**D3, the membrane (decisive).** No, not without loss. Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :28-29: "Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought back down will not match what was sent." The loss is measurable: Fig 4 :31-32: "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." What survives is the kernel or method (Fig 4 :19-20). What does not survive is the recording and the per-slot detail (Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."). The discarded detail reappears above the crossing as noise (Fig 13 :31-32). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 4, Fig 13 and Fig 1, which are low-overlap figures from different parts of the set.

**D4, verification.** The anchor is the ground, never agreement. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." In this simulated run the second seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency only. Verdict: `general`, triangulated with Fig 10.

### The disambiguation that makes the answer checkable

"Essential information" is split, not decided by fiat:

| reading of "essential" | verdict | figure |
|---|---|---|
| the reapplicable method or kernel | survives the crossing, but is remade by the level it lands on | Fig 4 :19-20, :34-35 |
| the particular per-slot detail and the recording | does not cross; the loss is `R_T`; the detail reappears as noise | Fig 4 :27-29, :25; Fig 13 :31-32; Fig 1 :22 |

So the answer is **no, not on the detail reading; yes on the kernel reading**, and those are separate verdicts for separate domains. Averaging them would destroy the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :19).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that macro patterns are unreal, illusory or mere shorthand.
- Not that everything is reducible in principle from the current micro vocabulary. Composition never leaves the closure it started in (Fig 6 :23).
- Not that information is destroyed by coarse-graining. It becomes unavailable at the macro level and reappears as noise (Fig 13 :31-32).
- Not that strong emergence is established, or that Anderson 1972 proves ontic novelty. It is a world-field corroboration only.
- Not that irreducibility is absolute. It is a fact about a level (Fig 12 :104).
- Not a catalogue of which patterns are derivable in which systems. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. No instrument or simulation was run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. For a declared system and a declared macro map, what is the measured `R_T`? It must be a number with an error bar, not an assertion.
2. Does the detail discarded at the crossing reappear as noise at the macro level, and with the predicted statistics?
3. Does the reapplicable kernel survive the crossing, or is only a recording being replayed?
4. **Falsifier, written before the run:** a transform with `R_T = 0` over a declared domain, checked against a ground, kills the decisive verdict. So does detail that vanishes without reappearing, or a crossing that carries a recording as a method.
5. Standing structural falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5: a composition inside a closed set that adds an atom; a derivation of one of the six from the other five.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The question as posed asks for a universal yes or no across all systems and all levels. That is a uniqueness claim over a census nobody has run, and no frame is a sealed room (Fig 9 :44). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The world literature supplies corroboration from statistical mechanics, complex systems and philosophy of science, but the figures decide, and this run is a simulation.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether macro patterns can be derived from micro rules without losing essential information, because the phrase hides two different questions. Across a scale boundary the transform is lossy by construction: going up throws away detail, the trip back down does not reproduce what was sent, and the gap between them is the honest report of what was given up. What does cross is the reapplicable method, not the recording; what does not cross reappears above the boundary as noise. If "essential" means the kernel, it survives, though the new level remakes it. If it means the particular detail, it does not survive, and the framework measures the loss rather than hiding it. A settled macro pattern can later become an atom for a higher composition, so irreducibility is a fact about a level, not an eternal badge. The deliverable is this shape, the round-trip measurement that would close it, and a refuter written in advance. It is a `specified` result from a simulated derivation, not a ground reading, and the ground is a source-discriminating instrument, not agreement.
