**Status:** done

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before the run, the sought shape is made refusable. Here "derived without losing essential information" is bound to a checkable quantity: the round-trip gap `R_T` between a micro description sent up to a macro description and that macro description brought back down. `R_T = 0` means lossless derivation. `R_T > 0` means information was given up, and `R_T` is the honest report of how much. Writing it down this way lets the figures check the direction of the question rather than only its mechanism (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-66).

## The one-line answer carried through this folder

No, not on the literal reading: the transform between scales loses information by construction, and the loss is the measurable quantity `R_T`, not a defect to be hidden. But the framework does not stop at a bare "no". It returns the shape of the answer (a round trip with a measured gap), separates what survives the crossing (the reapplicable method or kernel) from what does not (the recording and the per-slot detail), and records where the discarded detail goes (it reappears above the crossing as noise and chaos).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan and no receptiveness column used as evidence.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room is not an anchor. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
3. Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

## Boundary and honest standing of this work

This folder contains a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The framework's own rule applies: LLM output is "simulacra", and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026).
