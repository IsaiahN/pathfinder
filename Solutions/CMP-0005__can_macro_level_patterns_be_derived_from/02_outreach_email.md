**Subject:** Check one output about scale-crossing before you decide whether the claim is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question in the foundations of complex systems: can a macro-level pattern be derived from micro-level rules without losing essential information? I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a scale transform is lossy by construction. Going up throws away detail, the trip back down does not reproduce what was sent, and the gap between the two is the measurable quantity that reports what the coarser description cannot hold. In notation, `R_T = gap(x, (T_E after T_A)(x))`. The output is not a new theory of emergence. It is the claim that the loss is not an error to be eliminated but the honest measurement of the coarse description's limit.
>
> The word "essential" does two different jobs here and I keep them apart. If it means the reapplicable method or kernel, that survives the crossing. If it means the particular per-slot detail, that does not; it reappears above the crossing as noise, and when the chain amplifies, as chaos. Those are two verdicts, not an average.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that macro patterns are unreal, that everything is reducible in principle from the current micro vocabulary, that information is destroyed by coarse-graining, or that this settles the philosophical debate between reductionism and emergentism. The framework returns a shape and a route, not a name and not a list of which patterns happen to be derivable in which systems. No instrument was run here, so none of this is `grounded`.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this run):**
> 1. a scale transform with `R_T = 0` over a declared domain, checked against a ground rather than assumed;
> 2. discarded detail that vanishes entirely, without reappearing as noise or re-entering as a residual, while the higher level is still a distinct frame;
> 3. a kernel that does not survive the crossing, or a recording that crosses and is reapplicable as a method;
> 4. a macro pattern derived from inside one closure that is nevertheless a genuine new level, which would collapse the union I assume into a partition.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would count as a check:** name a ground for "essential information is preserved" that does not depend on a declared macro map; or, for a system you know, say what you would measure to get `R_T` and which quantity you would treat as the survivor. I am asking what you would expect to see, before showing you any source.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures and formula: `local: FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and `local: FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` (hosted link to be supplied by the owner)
> - Statistical mechanics and coarse-graining, Gell-Mann and Lloyd 1996: https://exa.ai/library/publication/xcwt5kw0pq7
> - Emergence as a many-to-one map, Carroll and Parola 2024: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15468
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run the round trip on a system you know well. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the residual as an effect, the shape claim and its refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure and what they would measure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three source links, noting that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the round-trip procedure as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
