## What is being decided, in effect terms

We hold three things that do not yet compose into an answer: a mapping from nucleotide triplets to amino acids (the code), a machine that implements the mapping (ribosome, tRNA, aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases), and the proteins of which that machine is made. Each seems to require the others. The question asks what intermediate state closed that loop and what ground could settle the claim. An answer would look like a self-sustaining arrangement in which ingredients, mapping and catalyst are mutually regenerating, with the historical slot left open until an instrument bears on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that agreement from a list of sympathetic readers is evidence.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Room is not anchor. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness is a mirror.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
