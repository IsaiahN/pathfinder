## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," so a stop with the slot open is an admissible output, not a failure to answer.

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is therefore: *a self-sustaining catalytic cycle that joins a supply of operands to a convention-like mapping, with the identity of the first molecule and the historical route left open*. That shape constrains candidate answers. It does not fill the slot with "RNA world", "peptide world" or "thioester world". Those are names; the shape is the requirement any of them must satisfy.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the unknown, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is: a mapping must be implemented, the implementer must be regenerated, and the operands must be supplied.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly F1 of the audit: the origin of translation is a union of conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." In this case the stop is not a full answer; it is a narrowed shape plus a recorded unreachability.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A lab reconstruction that closes the loop and is checked against a ground would name a candidate mechanism.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8). Importing "autocatalytic set" from complexity science into origin-of-life chemistry is such an import; it must be recorded as an import, not presented as a derivation from the figures.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the limit. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Here, observing a code table or a ribosome structure does not identify the historical path that produced it, because several paths can yield the same endpoint. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 4. Why the historical slot stays open

Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom", and Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 records the admissible verdict. The local figures README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest" and that "Indiscernibility is not identity."

Applied here: the three live code-origin stories (random freeze, stereochemical affinity, error-minimisation optimality) can all fit the same endpoint. Without an instrument that discriminates among them, choosing one is a convention, not a discovery. The shape does not pick a winner. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be checked by someone positioned to refute it, and let the ground settle it.
