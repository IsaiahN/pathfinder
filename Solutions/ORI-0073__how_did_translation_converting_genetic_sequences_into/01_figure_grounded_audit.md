## 0. The residual, in effect terms

We observe a working mapping: a sequence of three nucleotides specifies one amino acid, and a ribonucleoprotein machine builds the corresponding polypeptide. We do not hold the route from prebiotic chemistry to that closed arrangement. The residual R is the gap between:

- the operands: nucleotides, amino acids, short oligos, ions and surfaces;
- the bond: a convention-like mapping from triplets to amino acids;
- the catalyst: a translating apparatus made partly of the product it makes.

What is being decided: which intermediate arrangement joined those three, and what instrument could ever bear on the claim. A candidate answer must be a shape (what the missing thing would have to look like), not a name of a particular historical scenario.

**Arity.** At least three co-dependent conditions (supply, mapping, catalyst). More than one mechanism is live for each. This is a union compound, not one hard question.

**Symmetry.** The mapping itself behaves like identity (a conventional pairing, symmetric in assignment), while the act of translation behaves like production (ordered, the product absent before the step fires). The two must not be conflated. Fig 12 separates them: the bond table lists identity as symmetric and production as not.

**Scale.** Molecular (monomers, triplets, peptide bonds) up to system level (a self-maintaining protocell). Any claim that crosses that boundary is a membrane crossing and owes a round-trip gap (Fig 4).

## 3. Findings

### F1: The residual is a union compound, not one hard question (`general`, decidable from the figures)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

The supply of ingredients, the mapping, and the catalyst are separate conditions that overlap. A single lever that solves supply leaves the mapping bound; a lever that solves the mapping leaves the catalyst bound. Treating "the origin of translation" as one question is the bundling error. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 9 with the D1 to D4 census; low overlap between a figure rule and the domain descriptions).

### F2: Ingredients are not the bond (`specified`, structural)

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

Fig 12 :46-47: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands. A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."

Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

Prebiotic chemistry (Miller-type synthesis, the Sutherland routes to pyrimidine ribonucleotides, meteoritic delivery) supplies operands. It does not supply the triplet-to-amino-acid relation. Knowing that amino acids and nucleotides form is knowing the ingredients; it is not knowing how they were joined. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 12 bond definition and Fig 13 composition rule, low overlap). The world sources corroborate; they do not decide.

### F3: Translation is production, and the product is absent before it fires (`specified`)

Operators table :36-40 defines the production bond: "A produces B; B did not exist before" and the test "different from sequence: the second operand is not waiting, it is absent until the first fires". Fig 12 :77 carries the same line.

Applied here: the protein is not waiting in the prebiotic soup for the code to pick it up. It does not exist until translation fires. A model that assumes ready-made functional proteins available to build the first ribosome has silently used production as if it were sequence. Verdict: `single-source` for the bond typing (Fig 12 and Operators are one source family), corroborated by the structural-biology fact that the PTC is RNA and no protein lies within 18 angstroms of the active site.

### F4: The closure of a catalytic cycle is the shape of the origin (`specified`, the central result)

Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."

Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."

The product (peptide) re-enters as a participant in the thing that made it, by stabilizing or helping to replicate the RNA that carries the mapping. A chain of prebiotic steps cannot close by construction; a catalytic cycle can. At the origin there is no maintainer seat, so "nothing chooses": the sign of the loop, and the ground, decide what persists. This is the self-sustaining arrangement a candidate answer must have. It does not say which molecule closed the loop. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 12 cycle and catalyst with Fig 11 unmaintained habitat, low overlap). World corroboration is strong but from a shared chemistry pool: autocatalysis, collectively autocatalytic sets, and the RNA/RNA-peptide world literature.

### F5: The code began as a convention and froze when it decided the outcome (`specified`)

Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."

Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"

A mapping is a convention while alternatives are live. Once it decides which protein is built, it is a constraint: the frozen code. Fig 12 :46-47 also says the bond is not recoverable from the operands, which is why the particular assignment is not derivable from the chemistry of amino acids and triplets alone. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 10, low overlap). World sources: Crick's "frozen accident" (1968), stereochemical affinity, and optimality/error-minimization are three live candidates for how the convention got fixed. The figures do not choose among them; they only say that a convention which decides an outcome has stopped being a convention and that the assignment is not recoverable from operands.

### F6: The historical route is unreachable, and that is a verdict, not a silence (`gap` for the route)

Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom"

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

Fig 6 :32-33: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

A direct observation of the origin event is not an instrument that can be sharpened from a worse one already returning something, because nothing at any resolution is currently returning that event. Comparative genomics and lab reconstructions are instruments already returning something, so they are legitimate and worth improving. They narrow the shape; they do not deliver a time machine. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 8, Fig 13 and Fig 6, low overlap). Mode `gap` for the specific route; `specified` for the claim that present components cannot derive it.

### F7: Residual first, frame second; describe the effect, not the cause (`general`, method)

Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms."

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

If we pick a favourite origin scenario first (RNA world, peptide world, thioester world), any of them will seem to fit. The search must start from the described residual: a mapping is needed, a catalyst is needed, and the catalyst must be regenerated. Only then do we ask which frame predicts that shape. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 8, Fig 6, Fig 9).

### F8: A strain, named: the six-term substrate does not name molecular parts (`general`)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."

Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

These govern the six primitives of the substrate. They do not license saying "tRNA is a structure" or "the code is a network" inside this derivation. What survives is the structural constraint at the right level: a direct state-state or structure-structure bond is not composable, so any candidate account must put a cross-kind term where two same-kind terms would otherwise touch. No figure line governs the molecular naming, so that part is a `gap`, not a hidden derivation.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. Fig 5 :66-67: "Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A linear, non-autocatalytic prebiotic route is demonstrated that yields a functional coded translation system with no component regenerated by its own products (breaks Fig 12 :121, "a chain cannot close by construction").
2. The codon-to-amino-acid mapping is shown to be uniquely determined by stereochemical or physicochemical affinity, so that it is recoverable from the operands and was never a live convention (breaks Fig 12 :46-47 and Fig 2 :41 with Fig 10 :47).
3. An instrument is built that returns a direct observational record of the origin sequence, where currently nothing at any resolution returns it (reverses F6; breaks Fig 6 :32-33 and Fig 8 :39).
4. A translation system is demonstrated that works with no part surviving its own use, that is, with no catalyst regenerated through the loop (breaks Fig 12 :119).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. A failure sent by a reader is more useful than an endorsement.
