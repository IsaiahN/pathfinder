## Relevant wider literature, sorted by bearing

1. **The mapping.** Crick (1968), *Journal of Molecular Biology* 38:367 to 379, "The origin of the genetic code"; Koonin and Novozhilov (2009), *IUBMB Life* 61:99 to 111, "Origin and evolution of the genetic code: the universal enigma". Both reached as references on the Genetic code page and read there as citations, not as independently fetched full texts, so they are corroboration at one remove and are not listed as read sources in the ledger.
2. **The catalyst.** Tirumalai et al. (2021), fetched in full. The PTC is RNA, the oldest part of the ribosome, accreted in phases, and survived largely unchanged since pre-LUCA times.
3. **The loop.** Autocatalysis and collectively autocatalytic sets (Kauffman, Jain and Krishna), reached through the Autocatalysis page. This is the low-overlap field: it predicts that closure, not mere supply, is the milestone.
4. **The instruments.** In-vitro selected RNA polymerase ribozymes, proto-ribosome dimer constructs, and minihelix aminoacylation, all reported on the RNA world page and the PTC review. These are instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32-33), so they can be sharpened.

Note the overlap: the genetic-code, translation, RNA-world and PTC pages share much of the same literature pool. They are witnesses to a consensus, not independent corroborations of it. The autocatalysis source is the lowest-overlap witness in the set.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 4 :22-25: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full framework before a recipient has described the residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the derivation.
- Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Later agreement from a receptive list is not a second witness.

**The two-stage offer.** Stage 1: send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer before showing any framework material. Stage 2, opt in only: if they are curious, offer the three local links, with the note that the figures and not this summary are the authority. Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes a refuter and states a mode before running it.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As written in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` it is a shape, not a historical claim; do not inflate it into a named origin scenario when sending it.
