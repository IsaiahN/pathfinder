# The Tether's answer: how did translation begin?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the loop, `gap` on the historical route. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold a mapping from nucleotide triplets to amino acids, a machine that implements it, and proteins from which the machine is built. We do not hold the route that joined them. The residual is a union compound (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."), with three co-dependent conditions: supply, mapping and catalyst. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, prebiotic supply.** Ingredients are necessary and insufficient. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Prebiotic synthesis delivers operands, not the triplet-to-amino-acid relation. Mode `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 with Fig 13).

**D2, the mapping.** Translation is production: Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before". The protein is absent until the mapping fires, so a model that assumes ready-made functional proteins available to build the first machine has used sequence where production applies. The code began as a convention and froze when it decided outcomes (Fig 2 :41, Fig 10 :47). The particular assignment is not recoverable from operands (Fig 12 :46-47). Mode `specified`, triangulated for the freeze, single-source and live-debated for non-recoverability.

**D3, the catalyst and the loop.** This is the central result. Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." An origin account must be a self-sustaining catalytic loop whose product helps regenerate the carrier of the mapping. At that stage there is no maintainer seat. Mode `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 with Fig 11). The identity of the first molecule is not given.

**D4, the historical route.** Unreachable at this budget, as a verdict. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :32-33: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Lab reconstructions are returning something and can be sharpened; a direct observation of the origin event cannot be built from a description. Mode `gap`, triangulated (Fig 8, Fig 13, Fig 6).

**D5, the verification frame.** Agreement is not an anchor. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." In this simulated run the seats share one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a named scenario. The framework returns a shape, not a name. No claim that RNA first, peptides first, or thioesters first.
- Not a derivation of the genetic code table from chemistry. That table is a convention that froze; the figures say the bond is not recoverable from the operands.
- Not a uniqueness claim over the three code-origin hypotheses. They are figure-isomers over the same atoms, and no discriminator has been applied (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- Not "selection did it". Fig 11 :36 describes the mode at the origin; it does not name the missing bond.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles any historical claim.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

1. **Closure test:** build a minimal system in which a peptide product helps regenerate the RNA carrier of a triplet mapping. If it closes and pays against the ground, D3 moves toward `grounded`.
2. **Convention test:** show whether the triplet-to-amino-acid assignment can be derived from stereochemical or physicochemical affinity alone. If it can, the "live convention that froze" claim is refuted.
3. **Order test:** an instrument that discriminates the three code-origin isomers, or a lineage in which synthetase function demonstrably precedes any adaptor.
4. **Standing refuters:** a linear non-autocatalytic route to coded translation; a determination of the mapping from operands; a direct observational record of the origin sequence; a translation system with no part surviving its own use. Any one fires against the shape.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the first translating molecule, and it does not claim to have replayed history. It says that the origin of translation is a union of at least three conditions: a supply of prebiotic ingredients, a mapping from nucleotide triplets to amino acids, and a catalyst that implements the mapping; that ingredients alone never supply the bond, because a recipe naming only its ingredients has recorded half of itself; that translation is production, so the protein is absent before the mapping fires and a linear chain cannot close; and that the origin must therefore have been the closure of a self-sustaining catalytic cycle in which the peptide product helped regenerate the carrier of the mapping, with no maintainer present and selection running. The code is treated as a convention that froze once it decided which protein got built, not as a table derived from chemistry. The identity of the first molecule and the historical route are recorded as unreachable at this budget, which is a verdict and not a silence. What is offered is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
