## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, with partition or union and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between prebiotic operands and a working triple (mapping, implementer, product). Arity: at least three co-dependent conditions, so a **union compound**; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either, and overlapping conditions must be unbundled before search. Symmetry: the mapping behaves like identity (a conventional pairing), while the act of translation behaves like production (ordered, product absent before the step). These must not be conflated. Scale: molecular monomers up to a self-maintaining protocell; any crossing is a membrane crossing with a round-trip gap.
- **Domain 1, prebiotic supply.** Actors: amino acids, nucleotides, sugars, lipids, metal ions, mineral surfaces. Currency: bond formation, activation, replication. Anchor: laboratory prebiotic synthesis and meteorite analysis. Necessary, not sufficient.
- **Domain 2, the mapping.** Actors: codons, anticodons, amino acids, adaptor RNAs, synthetases. Currency: correspondence between triplet and amino acid. Anchor: the genetic code table itself (64 entries, near universal, degenerate, error-structured). The table is a fact; its origin is not settled by it.
- **Domain 3, the catalyst and cycle.** Actors: proto-ribosome, PTC rRNA, tRNA, peptides, aaRS. Currency: catalysis and fidelity, survival of the part through its use. Anchor: structural biology and comparative genomics (PTC is RNA, accreted, pre-LUCA).
- **Domain 4, the historical route.** Actors: lineages, selection, protocells, LUCA. Currency: differential persistence. Anchor: phylogenetic reconstruction. The specific event has no returning instrument.
- **Domain 5, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers and public venues. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Union, not partition.** D2 is implemented by D3, D3 is built from D1, and D1 alone never yields D2. Order by dependency: a carrier before a mapping can be implemented, a mapping settled before it can be composed. The full dependency graph is a cycle, which is a finding, not a defect.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the names do not. Translation began, in the sense the framework can state, as the closure of a self-sustaining catalytic cycle that joined prebiotic operands to a mapping which started as a live convention and froze once it decided which protein was built. The protein is absent before the mapping fires, so a linear chain cannot close. At that stage there is no maintainer and selection ran. Which molecule first closed the loop, and the sequence of events, are `gap`: unreachable at this budget, not silence. The three code-origin hypotheses are figure-isomers over the same atoms until a discriminator is applied. This is a `specified` and ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending a recorded scenario instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A named scenario can be withheld or withdrawn without touching the derivation. Nothing in the figure claims depends on RNA first or peptide first. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A lab system closing the loop with a peptide product regenerating an RNA carrier would move D3 toward `grounded`; a demonstration that the triplet assignment is uniquely fixed by stereochemical affinity would refute the convention claim; a direct observational record of the origin sequence would end the `gap`; a linear non-autocatalytic route to coded translation would break the cycle claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
