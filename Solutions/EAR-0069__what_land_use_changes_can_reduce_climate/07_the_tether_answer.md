# The Tether's answer on land use, climate risk, food security and ecosystems

**Mode: `specified` on the applied claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No land-use intervention was tested.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which land-use changes reduce climate risk while protecting food security and ecosystems. In effect terms the residual is the gap, in a named place and season, between a predicted and a measured state on three axes with three different currencies. An intervention that lowers emissions while damaging household food access or pollinator relations has not closed the residual; it has moved it between slots. Fig 9 names this a union compound: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."** It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The three axes do not add.** Climate is a global accumulating stock with local fluxes; food security is household-level and four-dimensional; ecosystem integrity is local and relational. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A national net-zero land sector is a legal state alongside a live food-access slot in one district.
2. **There is no single lever.** Fig 9 :55-56: **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** The parcel where all three conditions overlap is the object that needs its own loop.
3. **Enumerate the habitat before naming an intervention.** Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."** The list is read off the world, not invented from a vocabulary of interventions. Fig 11 :46-47 gives the categories: **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"** and **"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"**.
4. **No actor is good by kind.** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** A forest, a crop or a pollinator is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another.
5. **Management is introduction and permanent work.** Fig 11 :61-62: **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."** Fig 11 :63-64: **"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."** Write the intervention as a cycle, not a one-off chain.
6. **Displacement is substitution, not removal.** Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :68-69: **"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"** and **"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"**. A mitigation that shifts production to another region inherits actors in the receiving habitat that were not chosen.
7. **The bottleneck is the objective link.** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Fig 3 :29-30: **"Most improvement effort in practice lands above the break, because that is where the measurements already exist."** Carbon accounting works; a joint statement of progress across three currencies does not yet have a comparable instrument.
8. **The ground, not agreement, decides.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Cost per tonne is a filter. Measured emissions, household food access and biodiversity state are witnesses.
9. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. The deliverable is the five-part class: slot, currency, enumerated habitat, cascade, and instrument. Selecting a named land use is a binding from the management frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation.
10. **Import when closure fails.** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** A carbon-accounting frame cannot derive household access or pollination from its own atoms. Agronomy, ecology, remote sensing and local knowledge are imported with a source gate and a shadow test.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranked list of named interventions. No figure line selects afforestation, intensification, rewilding or any other named option for this residual.
- Not a claim that carbon, food and biodiversity can be added into one net-benefit scalar. The figures forbid it and supply no weights. That gap is declared, not filled.
- Not that land sparing beats land sharing, or the reverse. As usually written they share a functional group, not an atom set; the world literature conflicts; the conflict is evidence of the union finding, not a verdict.
- Not that protected areas are impossible. "Exclusion is not" concerns the habitat (contact, conditions, relations), not the governance act of removing one actor from a parcel.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles any sub-claim.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A ground reading in a named place, not a further argument. The instruments owed are:

- **Per-slot measurement:** emissions and land-surface state at the parcel and landscape scale; household-level availability, access, utilisation and stability; species abundance and habitat state. All four food dimensions at once, per the FAO definition.
- **Cascade measurement:** an enumeration outward until the cascade stops mattering, with second- and third-order effects recorded. The DDT case in Fig 11 :52-53 is the standing example.
- **Displacement measurement:** the receiving region's residuals over a full cascade, including actors that were not chosen.
- **Objective instrument:** a statement of what counts as progress across the three currencies that can be checked against those measurements, rather than a preference smuggled into a score.

**Standing falsifiers** (pre-registered in `01` section 5): one intervention clearing all three conditions in every slot; a lossless scalar combining the three axes; a displacement leaving all residuals non-positive in the receiving habitat; a figure line naming a land use without ground or provenance.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README calls this *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A better instrument, not a better argument, would name the slot. Fig 8 :39: **"A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** This is a candidate result. Prestige or agreement cannot move it: Fig 2 :42 **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning land use and does not claim a single net-benefit score. It says that climate risk, food security and ecosystem integrity are a union of three conditions with three currencies that do not add, so there is no single lever and the residual must be read per place and per household. It says to enumerate the habitat (actors, conditions, relations) and the cascade before choosing an intervention; that no actor is good by kind; that management is continuing introduction rather than a solved state; and that moving production elsewhere substitutes one habitat for another rather than removing the residual. The real bottleneck is not a shortage of carbon data but the absence of an instrument that states progress across all three currencies at once. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the measurements that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
