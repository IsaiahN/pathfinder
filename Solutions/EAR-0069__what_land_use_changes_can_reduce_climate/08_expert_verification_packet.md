# Expert verification packet - the Tether on land use, climate risk, food security and ecosystems

**What this is:** a claim in a form a land-use, climate-mitigation, food-security or ecosystem researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the applied claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No land-use measurement is claimed to have settled any part of this. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it comes from a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The three objectives in the question are a union compound with three currencies that do not add, so there is no single land-use lever that clears all three. The residual must be read per slot (place, household and habitat), the habitat must be enumerated before any intervention is named, and no actor is good by kind. Management is continuing introduction rather than a solved state, and displacement of production is substitution of one habitat for another rather than removal of the residual. The bottleneck is not a shortage of carbon data but the absence of an instrument that states progress across all three currencies at once. The framework returns this five-part shape, not a named land use, and leaves the slot open until a ground reading settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 11

- **Statement:** this is a union compound, so a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :55-56 - **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** Fig 11 :49 - **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :51 - **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**
- **What to check:** whether the three axes share a currency and an instrument. If they do, the union finding is wrong.
- **Refuter:** exhibit one intervention that clears all three conditions in every slot and season, with no positive residual left in any one of them.

### C2 - land-science facing; checkable against cases you know

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot, and displacement is habitat substitution, not removal. Effects are owed two or three steps out.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22 - **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 11 :66 - **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :67 - **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."** Fig 11 :52-53 - **"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**
- **What to check:** do you know a case where a national or global average hid a live household or watershed slot? Do you know an intervention whose displacement was measured only to first order? The IPCC WGIII SPM C.9 states that *"trade-offs may result from the impacts of climate change, competing demands on land, conflicts with food security and livelihoods"*; Meyfroidt et al. (2020) describe *"the displacement of land-uses to near or remote sites"*.
- **Refuter:** show a displacement that leaves all three slot residuals non-positive in the receiving region over a full cascade, with the receiving habitat's unchosen actors explicitly accounted for; or show that a global carbon total plus a national food-availability mean plus a biodiversity index predicts all three slot outcomes without losing a live slot.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** the dependency break is at the objective link, and a cost or carbon figure cannot be allowed to hand the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :27 - **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Fig 3 :29-30 - **"Most improvement effort in practice lands above the break, because that is where the measurements already exist."** Fig 9 :60 - **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**
- **What to check:** is there an instrument in your field that states progress across climate, food and ecosystem outcomes simultaneously and is actually used at the same effort level as carbon accounting? If yes, the break is already repaired.
- **Refuter:** exhibit that instrument with its measured results, and show that it is used at comparable effort.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranked list of named land uses. No figure line selects afforestation, intensification, rewilding or any other named option for this residual.
- Not that the three currencies can be added into one net-benefit scalar. The figures forbid it and supply no weights. That gap is declared in `01` section 4.
- Not that land sparing beats land sharing or the reverse. They are a functional group as usually written; the world literature conflicts, and the conflict is evidence of the union finding.
- Not that protected areas are impossible. "Exclusion is not" concerns the habitat, not the governance act of removing an actor from a parcel.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. The instruments owed are:

1. Per-slot measurement: emissions and land-surface state at parcel and landscape scale; all four FAO food-security dimensions at household level; species abundance and habitat state.
2. Cascade measurement: enumeration outward until the cascade stops mattering, with second- and third-order effects recorded.
3. Displacement measurement: the receiving region's residuals over a full cascade, including unchosen actors.
4. Objective instrument: a checkable statement of progress across all three currencies, not a preference inside a score.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55-56, Fig 11 :49, :51, :66-69 and Fig 3 :27, :29-30. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know: was the residual aggregated, and was displacement measured beyond first order?
3. Name one instrument that states progress across all three currencies, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
