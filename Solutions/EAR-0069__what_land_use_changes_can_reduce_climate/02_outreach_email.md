# Outreach draft - check the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 0 and 5 are intact and you have checked them yourself. The offer collapses on contact if the first stage does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about land-use mitigation, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I work on land use and climate mitigation research. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "What land-use changes reduce climate risk while protecting food security and ecosystems?" is not one question with three footnotes. It is a union of three overlapping conditions in the same place. Climate risk is an accumulating stock with local fluxes; food security is a household-level, four-dimensional state; ecosystem integrity is local and relational. They have different currencies and do not add. From that it follows that there is no single lever: an intervention that clears only the region where one condition holds leaves the intersections standing.
>
> Three further consequences follow without any new data:
>
> 1. The residual has to be read per slot. A national net-zero land sector is a legal state alongside a live food-access slot in one district and a live biodiversity slot in one watershed. A global average cannot see those slots.
> 2. The habitat is enumerated, not composed. Before naming any intervention, list what acts on the residual in this place, the conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), the relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency), and then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering.
> 3. Displacement is substitution of habitat, not removal of the residual. Moving production to another region to spare land here inherits actors in the receiving habitat that were not chosen, and what was failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this identifies a named land use as the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am not claiming a cost per tonne ranks the options, that two models using the same land-cover and emission-factor data are independent witnesses, or that any of this is settled by measurement. It is a shape any candidate answer must have, plus the instruments that would close it.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. One intervention clears all three conditions in every slot and season, with no positive residual left in any one of them.
> 2. A global carbon total, a national food-availability mean and a biodiversity index form a scalar that predicts all three slot outcomes without losing a live slot.
> 3. A displaced production system leaves all three slot residuals non-positive in the receiving region over a full cascade, with its unchosen actors accounted for.
> 4. A figure line is exhibited that selects a named land use for this residual without a ground reading.
>
> If any holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - IPCC AR6 WGIII Summary for Policymakers headline statements: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/resources/spm-headline-statements
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

**Two-stage discipline.** Stage 1 is the claim and its refuter, checked by the recipient without any framework interior. Their verdict is recorded before stage 2. Stage 2, opt-in only, offers the links. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method to reuse. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
