## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks which land-use changes lead to three things at once: lower climate risk, maintained or improved food security, and maintained or improved ecosystem integrity. Each option in answer space is a land-use intervention. What each leads to is measured in three different currencies that cannot be added: tonnes of greenhouse gas avoided or removed, household-level food access and stability, and the state of species, habitats and relations. An answer that lowers one currency while raising another has not resolved the question; it has moved the residual between slots. This is the effect form used throughout the folder.

An answer would look like a class of land-use interventions with stated properties: the slot (where), the currency (what is being reduced), the affected habitat (which actors, conditions and relations are in contact), the cascade (what is two or three steps out), and the instrument that would show whether the intervention paid. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 so it can accrue a residual like anything else, per the formula's Direction rule.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the independence the recipient could have contributed.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach runs against GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The contact venues used here are public institutional or opt-in submission routes.
