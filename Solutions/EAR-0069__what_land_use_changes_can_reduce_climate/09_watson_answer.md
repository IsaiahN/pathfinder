## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap, in a named place and season, between predicted and measured state on three axes. Arity: three conditions, so a union compound. Symmetry: not symmetric; climate is a global accumulating stock with local fluxes, food security is household-level and four-dimensional, ecosystem integrity is local and relational; the currencies do not add. Scale: field, landscape, global, with a lossy transform between. Fig 9 names a case in two conditions a specific compound, not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, biophysical mitigation.** Actors: atmospheric carbon, albedo, evapotranspiration, soil carbon, sinks, fluxes. Currency: tCO2e and land-surface state. Anchor: flux towers, concentration records, satellite land-surface measurement. Anchor exists, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, food and agriculture.** Actors: crops, livestock, soils, water, pollinators, pests, households, markets. Currency: availability, access, utilisation, stability. Anchor: harvest measurement and household food-security surveys. Anchor exists.
- **Domain 3, ecosystem integrity.** Actors: species, habitats, and relations of pollination, predation, competition and dependency; conditions of light, water, temperature, season, terrain. Currency: abundance and habitat state. Anchor: field survey and long-term monitoring plots. Anchor exists.
- **Domain 4, decision and verification.** Actors: planners, landholders, markets, subsidies, reviewers. Currency: agreement, cost, policy. Anchor: measurements in domains 1 to 3, never agreement. This domain authors conventions but cannot author the ground.
- **Partition or union:** union. The three axes overlap on the same parcel; their intersections are a compound with its own loop and ground.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The break is at the objective link, where no joint instrument exists.

Re-census resolves it: the figure governs the habitat (contact, conditions, relations), not the governance act of removing one actor from a parcel. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; named prescriptions and a single net-benefit score do not. This is a union compound of three conditions with three currencies that do not add. Read the residual per slot, enumerate the habitat and its cascade before naming an intervention, treat management as continuing introduction, and treat displacement as substitution of habitat. The bottleneck is the unmeasured objective link, and cost is a filter, not a witness. What is owed is the five-part shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the slot. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Verdict mode: `specified` for the applied findings, `general` for the structural shape, all `candidate`, none `grounded`. The gap is declared: no figure supplies weights for adding the currencies, and the figures forbid inventing them.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not trivial. If the union finding is wrong and the axes really do share a currency, this solution sends readers toward a needlessly complex assessment and away from a workable scalar. The more likely cost is the opposite: a single net-benefit score hides a live household or watershed slot, and the hidden slot is discovered only after the intervention is deployed. Fig 2 :42 prices the check: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is reduced by asking for the per-slot residual first and by keeping cost curves as filters.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim is structural and carries no named intervention, so no practice has to be withdrawn if it fails. A ground reading adds a term that refuses a slot or confirms it; the record grows and the reach is recomputed. The outreach packet can be superseded by a corrected one without retracting any land-use practice.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument showing that all three currencies collapse to one measurable quantity would break the union finding; a case where one intervention clears all three conditions in every slot would break the single-lever refusal; a displacement leaving all residuals non-positive in the receiving habitat over a full cascade would break the substitution verdict; an instrument stating progress across all three currencies and used at the effort level of carbon accounting would close the declared gap; and a figure re-census licensing a lossless scalar would clear the forbidden-addition finding. Prestige, authority and reviewer agreement are not such instruments.
