## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-56 (union compound), Fig 1 :19, :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :43-69 (habitat, asymmetry, substitution, cascade), Fig 3 :27, :29-30 (objective break), Fig 9 :60 (filter versus witness).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: union compound; read per slot; enumerate habitat; introduction not exclusion; displacement is substitution; objective is the bottleneck; shape not a name.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete recommendation in land-use terms, for example "restore a watershed at scale and count the carbon".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot requirement** collapses if the concrete report gives only a national carbon total and a national biodiversity mean;
- the **cascade requirement** collapses if the report enumerates only first-order neighbours;
- the **substitution requirement** collapses if displaced production is outside the project boundary;
- the **filter/witness distinction** collapses if a cost-per-tonne rank is presented as evidence of success.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the union character of the three conditions, and the obligation to enumerate the habitat before naming an intervention. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
