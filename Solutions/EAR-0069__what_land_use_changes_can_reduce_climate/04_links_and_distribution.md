## Distribution rules - offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Send the five-part shape and its refuter first.
- **Fig 2 :45** - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* - describe the residual in effect terms before selecting a venue or a reader.
- **Fig 7 :13** - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* - a mass send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - if two respondents use the same emission factors and land-cover dataset, they are one witness.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the five-part shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure in this place; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if curious, the figure text, the canonical README and the IPCC headline statements; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1: validate the output against the ground. Stage 2, opt-in only: read the framework that produced it. Stage 3: reuse the formula on another problem with its own ground and refuter.

Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). Stage 2 is opt-in by construction, because a recipient who holds the interiors can no longer be an outside frame. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Stage 3 is permitted because a method crosses up, not a recording: Fig 4 :22.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The stage-1 output here is a shape, not a named land use. If a reader points to a named intervention and says the shape claim predicted it, that is a failure of stage 1, not a success.
