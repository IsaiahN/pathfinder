# The Tether's answer: oral tradition and written record as carriers of memory

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the empirical winner. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A remembered past is a reconstruction indexed per slot, not a store (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The question is not which medium holds more items; it is what the carrier changes in the loop that turns a prediction about the past into a checkable memory. It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: perception, carrier, chain and record are overlapping conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :54-57). One lever (the invention of writing) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape

1. **The reconstruction loop is the same for both carriers.** Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* What differs is the per-slot input, not the loop.

2. **The carrier is a membrane, and the variable is what crosses it.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* A re-applicable oral formula carries a method; a written archive entry carries a one-occasion recording. But a written manual is a method and a verbatim recitation is a recording, so the physical medium is not the governing quantity (F7 of `01`). The governing quantity is the bond that crosses: method or recording.

3. **Repetition inside a chain compounds agreement, not evidence.** Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* An oral tradition whose only check is the previous teller is a chain of mirrors (Fig 7 :36); it drifts coherently, which is why nobody notices (Fig 2 :34-35). Writing does not by itself fix this, but it supplies a stable object against which the drift can be measured.

4. **Writing makes the round trip measurable, and adds closure as a failure mode.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The Symbols table defines R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). A fixed text can be abstracted and re-applied and the gap compared; an oral chain cannot treat its own last performance as a fixed object. Ong's "finalized... state of completion" names the new defect: the fixed record is mistaken for the ground (Fig 10 :47).

5. **A record carries provenance, never truth.** Fig 10 :62: *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."* Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that."* Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* A written chronicle fixes who said what; that is provenance, not a guarantee of what happened.

6. **"Organize the past" is a bond question.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Chronology is sequence →; causation is production ⇒; comparing eras is ⋛; refusing a date is ¬ (Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*). Over a fixed atom set, a chronological and a causal account of the same episodes are isomers (Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*). The mnemonic formula is a catalyst that survives its use (Fig 12 :119).

7. **The empirical winner is a `gap`.** No figure line and no instrument run here returns which medium yields more accurate memory in a given society. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* This is unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that writing improves memory, or that orality is a deficient form of it.
- Not that the carrier determines cognitive content. The loop is shared (D1).
- Not that a written record is true because it is fixed; provenance never settles truth (Fig 10 :65).
- Not that agreement between tellers or between experts is corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the figure set can name which medium wins. It returns a shape (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator and an instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Method/recording test:** with the carrier held constant and crossing varied, measure per-slot R after one transmission. If crossing makes no difference, F1/F4 fail.
- **Ground-tracking chain:** follow an oral chain and an independent ground record; if the chain's agreement tracks the ground better than a single account, F3 fails (Fig 7 :13).
- **Round-trip measurement:** measure R_T between oral retellings and between written recensions. If equal at the same precision, F4 fails.
- **Bond test:** find a memory practice whose organizing relation fails all seven bond tests. That is a new claim, not a missing operator (Fig 12 :90).
- Standing refuters from `01` section 4: provenance becoming truth, carrier acting at fixed crossing, channel surviving a break in the line of tellers.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say writing is better than orality. It says the two are carriers, not content dispensers: what changes is the bond that crosses between minds, a re-applicable method or a one-occasion recording. An oral tradition that is re-composed each telling carries a method, but a chain whose only check is the previous teller compounds agreement rather than evidence, so it can drift coherently with no anchor. A written record fixes provenance and lets you take a round trip and measure the gap, but a fixed record can also be mistaken for the ground. Organizing the past is a bond question, not an ingredient question: chronology is sequence, causation is production, and a refused date is a negation. Over the same remembered atoms, different bond arrangements are isomers. The framework returns that shape and a set of refuters written in advance. It does not name a winner between media; that is an open question requiring an instrument, not an argument.
