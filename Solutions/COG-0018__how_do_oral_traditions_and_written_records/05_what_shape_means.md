## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the winner. For COG-0018 the shape is the four-field per-slot form: (carrier, crossing as method or recording, anchor, round-trip gap). That shape constrains any answer; it does not tell us that writing beats orality, or which society remembers better.

The same essay gives the second reason the question must be split: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :54 in plain words, and it is why D1 to D4 are kept separate.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"You should then be able to connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual is stated as a change in crossing and anchor, not as a change in "civilization" or "literacy."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

The answer reached here is a shape because it stops at what the figures and the opened sources can distinguish. The empirical comparison of media is not reached; it is reported as a `gap`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Legare 2019 and Dehaene 2009 are the low-overlap frames offered here; neither is used to overrule a figure.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar's mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate and the seven bonds are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming "sequence" as the bond for chronology is a binding onto an already-held term, not a discovery.

## 4. Why "a copy of a copy" is the decisive image for this question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session, describes LLM training data as "a copy of a copy of a copy. You can only do that so many times and retain fidelity." It then adds the ground rule: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

That is Fig 7 :13 (*"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*) and Fig 2 :35 (*"A mirror cannot correct you"*) stated for a modern transmission chain. An oral tradition whose only check is the previous teller is the same structure as a set of agreeing frames with no anchor: the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices. Writing's contribution is to make a stable copy available so the drift can be detected; its defect is that a stable copy can be mistaken for the ground (Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried from the exemplar's mapping): an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source; the spectrometer resolved it. Here, a remembered account does not determine whether it came from a lived event, a respected teller, or a fixed text. That is a discriminator problem (an instrument), not an argument problem, and it is why the empirical verdict is withheld.

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED: what licenses "writing lets you measure the gap"

Nothing in the framework licenses "writing is better." What the framework licenses is narrower and checkable: a fixed recording makes a round trip possible, and Fig 4 :30 requires the gap to be measured rather than assumed. A name (which medium wins) is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is reported in F4 and F9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 6. Correction to my own §3-§5 (shape, not a name, narrowed)

I initially wrote as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any bond or carrier in this answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Sequence (→), production (⇒), comparison (⋛) and negation (¬) are already in the operator set; the carrier (oral or written) is an observable, not a missing primitive. So naming them is a **binding** from the world frame onto terms already held, which is provenance, not an invented primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

What remains genuinely forbidden: inventing an eighth bond to explain a new cultural memory practice, or claiming the framework itself names which medium wins. Both claims are retracted if they appear anywhere in this folder. The correction is kept visible here and in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1, so nothing is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. This matters acutely in oral-tradition studies, where the authority of a respected teller is itself part of the mechanism being studied.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
