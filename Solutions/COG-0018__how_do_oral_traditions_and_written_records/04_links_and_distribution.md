## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The chain law has a laboratory analogue.** Bartlett's serial reproduction shows a strange narrative being shortened, conventionalized, and fitted to the rememberer's own schema across retellings. This is Fig 7 :13 (*"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*) and Fig 2 :35 in cognitive psychology rather than in the framework's own vocabulary. It corroborates F3, but it is one witness, not a second frame that independently derives the figure.
2. **Each retelling is an interpretation of its predecessor.** Legare's review carries Sperber's line that cultural content is "distributions of representations in a human population, ecological patterns of psychological things," and that traditions form "causal chains" in which "each link of such a chain is an interpretation of its predecessor." That is D3 described from developmental psychology, a genuinely low-overlap field.
3. **Orality is a method, not a deficit.** Ong's report of Lord's finding that literacy "disables the oral poet" because it substitutes a controlling text for "the remembrance of songs sung" is the strongest world corroboration of F1 and F7: the distinction is the re-applicability of the method, not the presence or absence of a script.
4. **Writing fixes provenance and can close the record.** Ong's "finalized... state of completion" and Assmann's institutionalised cultural memory corroborate F4 and F5. The failure mode is treating the fixed recording as the ground (Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*).
5. **Literacy changes the substrate.** Dehaene's neuronal recycling is lower overlap and corroborates the claim that the carrier is not merely an external container; but it operates at a different stratum (neural development, not the social chain), so it is not used to settle F1 to F9.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The essays and the canonical repository share the framework author's evidence pool completely, so their agreement is expected and carries no independent witness weight.
