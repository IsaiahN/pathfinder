## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that current systems reason.
- No bulk-send plan, no scraped contact list.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Gathering researchers who already agree cannot turn a source claim into an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list selected for likely agreement is a list selected to share a pool.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03` are public institutional submission routes or opt-in channels.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `02_outreach_email.md` to a public venue in `03`. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record any verdict before showing the framework. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
