## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line in the question alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between what a system's library predicts a novel problem requires and what it demands. Arity: two-indexed `(system, problem-slot)`, with a hidden frame index that decides what counts as unfamiliar. So the source question is **two conditions in a union**, not one variable with opposite values (Fig 9 :54). Symmetry: asymmetric, because novelty is relative to a library and a generator crosses levels while a playback does not. Scale: one inference within a scale, versus capability acquired across problems at the membrane.
- **Domain 1, model-internal composition.** Actors: atoms, bonds, candidate molecules, slots. Currency: residual per slot, cost of a candidate against cost of the unexplained, closure and reach. Anchor: figures as metalaws; ground for settlement. Question type: partition inside the closure.
- **Domain 2, corpus, priors and habitat.** Actors: examples, inherited priors, verifiable rewards, a maintaining seat. Currency: contact, imported atoms, enumerated habitat. Anchor: an outcome that does not update on a model's report. Question type: union of library contents and wall crossings.
- **Domain 3, evaluation.** Actors: test designers, held-out sets, scores. Currency: measured residual, contamination. Anchor: the problem's correct answer. Question type: union of novelty of form and availability of a discriminator.
- **Domain 4, other substrates.** Actors: people, corvids, pigeons, experimenters. Currency: novel-sample transfer, relational mapping. Anchor: controlled experiment. Question type: union of fluid and crystallized conditions; operational discriminators exist here.
- **Domain 5, verification of the package.** Actors: readers, figures, refuter. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence in this simulated run: zero.

Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The break is at vocabulary/objective, because "what would count as progress on the unfamiliar" has no common instrument across Domain 1 and Domain 3. Search inside the closure before composing, compose before importing, settle last.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the union rule, the three cases S1/S2/S3, novelty relative to Γ, the source-underdetermination claim, the habitat claim.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`, "the binary is a union, scores cannot see sources, capability needs contact."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete test on a named system and held-out problem.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** (a system may be S2 on one task and S1 on another; the shorthand sounds system-level);
- the **provenance requirement** (a trace is a projection, not a source, so a score plus a trace still cannot separate the cases);
- the **dependency order** (S3 is a later remedy owed only after S1 and S2 fail, not a superior S2);
- the **missing-atom versus unreachable-at-budget distinction** (the shorthand collapses two different instructions into one).

One thing survives cleanly: a new molecule can be composed from held atoms, so a novel success is not by itself evidence of a mechanism beyond the examples. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
