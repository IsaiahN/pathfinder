# Expert verification packet — the Tether on reasoning versus pattern extension

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in AI reasoning or cognitive science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` per domain, `gap` on empirical attribution. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result on any named system. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "do AI systems reason through unfamiliar problems or only extend learned patterns?" is a union of conditions, not a partition. Three cases must be separated: search inside an unchanged closure; a new molecule composed from atoms already held; and an import of an atom from a frame whose closure differs. The second case is novel as an arrangement and ordinary as a reuse of the same atoms, which is why the offered either/or cannot describe it. Novelty is relative to a particular system's library and indexed per problem slot. A benchmark score cannot identify which case occurred, because contents do not carry provenance. Capability belongs to a system and its habitat, never to a model in isolation. The framework returns the shape of a discriminator, not a verdict on any model.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9, 6 and 13

- **Statement:** the binary is a union; new combinations do not require new atoms; only import moves the wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :22 *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."* Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- **Refuter:** a clean non-overlapping partition of reuse and novel composition across three fields; or a composition from an atom absent from `atoms(Γ)` with no differing frame and provenance to show it (R1, R2).

### C2 — evaluation-facing; checkable by a benchmarking researcher

- **Statement:** an accuracy figure, however high, cannot identify the source of a success; a fluent reasoning trace is another projection. What is needed is a probe that varies novelty while holding structure fixed, plus provenance for any import.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*; Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*; Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** do the perturbational benchmarks actually hold structure fixed while changing surface form? Lake and Baroni's SCAN *dax* split and Mirzadeh et al.'s GSM-Symbolic are attempts. Does the evaluation anchor move when the model's own report changes? If it does, it is not an anchor.
- **Refuter:** a behavioural reading that identifies derivation versus import across two low-overlap frames without provenance or an instrument change (R3).

### C3 — capability-facing; checkable against agent-and-habitat evidence

- **Statement:** capability is a property of a system and its habitat; a synthetic harness solve proves wiring, not capability; the unit of attribution is per system and per problem slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** an agent-only improvement with no contact change that transfers reliably to a genuinely novel habitat (R4). This is the falsifier Fig 11 itself records.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named system reasons, and not that any named system only pattern-matches. Empirical attribution is `gap`, not a verdict.
- Not that reasoning and pattern extension are identical. They overlap in S2 and differ categorically at S3.
- Not a theory of consciousness, qualia or understanding. The contact question replaces the substrate question.
- Not that benchmarks are useless. They are instruments for the residual, not witnesses for a source (Fig 9 :60).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of a system was taken by this derivation. Status: `candidate`.
- Not that import is always owed. It is last by dependency, after search and composition; importing early adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :24).

## 6. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and it was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved it.

1. **Discriminator Test:** on a fixed system and held-out problem, vary novelty while holding structure fixed and show S1 versus S2 versus S3, replicated by a frame that does not share the training corpus.
2. **Provenance Test:** show where each atom in a successful composition came from, so convergent derivation and adopted import separate by route.
3. **Import Test:** show R still open after exhaustive S1 and S2 search, with a missing atom characterised in effect terms and a supplying frame named under a source gate.
4. **Habitat Test:** an agent-only contact-free improvement that transfers to a novel habitat would break C3.
