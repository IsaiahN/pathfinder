## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, the shape is: **a discriminator that separates search inside a closure from a new composition over held atoms from an import of a new atom, plus an anchor that does not move when a model's score changes.** The shape does not say "GPT-4 reasons" or "LLMs merely pattern-match." Those are names, not shapes.

A second shape, from *How Intelligence Actually Works*, is the author's own definition: *"a system can answer questions to problems it's never seen before and in environments it has no training data for."* That is a shape of an answer to "what makes a system capable", and it is habitat-inclusive, consistent with Fig 11 :39.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for recurring new terms that correlate. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the success in effect terms (a residual closed on a problem the examples did not contain), not in mechanism words ("attention", "reasoning", "retrieval") borrowed from one camp.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks the reader what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A source-discriminating instrument that pays on a specific system and slot would name that case.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the benchmark's own priors are a visible choice. A model trained on a human corpus is compressed along human-language axes; the absence of a term in that corpus is not evidence that the term is unthinkable.

## 4. Why a score cannot settle the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the metamerism argument: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can give the same reading, and *"an instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved the case.

Applied here: a benchmark score is the green reading. "Reasoned" and "replayed" are two possible sources. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* puts the AI version directly: *"Training on it reproduces 'the traces the thought left, not the thought', with enough structure to rebuild the appearance of reasoning"*, and *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."*

This is why F5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` refuses to infer a mechanism from a score. What is owed is a better instrument, not a louder claim.

## 5. Union horizon and the honest stop

*How Intelligence Actually Works* defines a *"distinction horizon"* for one observer and a *"union horizon"* for a group with all its instruments: *"A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."* And crucially: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

*They're Scanning Books* adds the representation limit: *"every reasoner, biological, artificial, or composed, can only be surprised along axes its primitives can already represent"*, and *"the recognizer can only register an anomaly it has the primitives to represent."* The frontier is *"a mechanism for enlarging the window from inside"*, and the essay says this is *"unfired in every system now operating."*

For this question the union horizon is the honest stop: no instrument opened this session discriminates the three cases S1, S2 and S3 on a named system. That is a report about available instruments, not a proof that the distinction is unreal. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. The capability claim and the unit of attribution

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Fig 11: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* It adds that a group is worth having only while its members stay different, and that agreement is never the verdict. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the practical version: the ARC games are *"novel"*, have *"no instructions"*, and their rules *"must be discovered purely through interaction"*, with RLVR as the ground: *"You either solve the level or you don't. That is the ground."*

So "AI systems reason" is a claim about a system and a habitat, indexed per problem. The essay's own agent is entered in a public competition rather than announced as a reasoner, which is the right posture for a `candidate`.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest*: outputs produced by prompting the framework *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any AI system and cannot settle the question they report on. This must be stated on every substantive claim.

## 8. Correction to my own §3 and §4

**First reading (wrong):** "shape, not a name" forbids saying anything about the mechanism of a success, so the only honest answer is silence about reasoning.

**Correction:** the README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like."* It restricts naming a primitive not held in Γ. Here the three cases S1, S2 and S3 are distinctions among arrangements and contacts over primitives already named in the figures. Specifying those arrangements is a shape, not an illicit name. What remains forbidden is naming a particular system as *the* reasoner or naming a missing primitive that would explain it.

**Second reading (wrong):** a high score plus a fluent trace is a name, because the trace shows the reasoning.

**Correction:** the trace is another projection. Without provenance it cannot distinguish convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :50), and metamerism applies to it exactly as it applies to the colour reading in §4. The trace is evidence to be ranged against the ground, not a verdict.

Both first readings are preserved here rather than erased. Neither changes the verdict in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`; both sharpen the non-claims in `07_the_tether_answer.md`.
