## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* and **Fig 2 :45** *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Stage 1 goes out first; the recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior.
- **Fig 8 :50** *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*: the reader's verdict is recorded before the links are sent. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*: ask what the reader would count as a failure before offering any theory.
- **Fig 7 :35** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*: no bulk send, no list selected for likely agreement.
- **Fig 4 :25** *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*: the generator (claim plus refuter) is sent; the architecture is offered only after.
