## Independent domain census

Arity: each candidate mechanism is a three-term relation (read the firm, report, consequence), so an ordered `→` bond, with a `⋛` comparison at the point where a reported value crosses a threshold the firm must not set. Symmetry: asymmetric, the mechanism and the firm are not interchangeable. Scale: the named decision slot, the habitat around the firm, the reporting channel across cycles. Fig 1 :19 indexes per slot.
- **Domain 1, the decision loop.** Actors: managers, routines, Γ. Currency: actions and residual reduction. Anchor: the realized outcome on the slot. Question type: what moves `a` so R shrinks.
- **Domain 2, the accountability habitat.** Actors: regulators, investors, employees, buyers, standard setters, NGOs, media. Currency: sanctions, capital, wages, purchases, attention. Anchor: a counter, a market, a verified outcome (Fig 2 :29). Question type: which actor ranges against which ground.
- **Domain 3, the verification arrangement.** Actors: boards, auditors, raters, reporting systems, complaint channels. Currency: agreement, provenance, independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: reconstructible or not, live channel or dead.
- **Domain 4, the temporal channel.** Actors: metrics, disclosures, ratings, warnings. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground does not decay, the channel does (Fig 10 :24).
- **Excluded.** Self-grounding culture: no independent anchor. Reputation as ground: it updates when pushed, so it belongs inside D3/D4 as an instrument.
- **Union.** D2 overlaps D3 (oversight actors are habitat actors) and D3 overlaps D4 (the channel is the verifier's instrument), so this is a union that must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Dependency order.** Anchor first (C1), then non-reconstructible check (C2), then live channel with consequence (C3), then import from a differing closure (C4). Fig 3 :20.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its two exclusions and its union reading. Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a mechanism whose reading moves when pushed is not an anchor, and a check the firm can produce is not a check. That residue is worth sending.
