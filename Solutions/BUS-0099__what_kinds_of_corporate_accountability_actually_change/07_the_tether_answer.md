# The Tether's answer on corporate accountability

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The five candidates all reliably produce a change in what a firm **says** about itself: a policy, a report, a rating, a pledge, a campaign. They do not reliably produce a change in what the firm **does** against a ground. The residual is the gap between those two, per decision slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question as posed treats this as a five-way contest with a winner. It is a union of four conditions, and the answer cannot be a ranking (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **The mechanism must range against a ground that does not move when the firm pushes on it.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A statute with an inspector and a court can do this; a self-scored index cannot. Law is an anchor only when enforcement is real. An unenforced statute is a convention, and *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47).
2. **The check must be one the firm cannot produce.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Investor oversight works only when the overseer holds a reading and a ground the firm cannot manufacture; a rating paid for by the issuer fails. Employee voice is the lowest-correlation reading of the five when it is protected, and a dead channel when it is not.
3. **The channel must deliver a per-slot signal to someone who can impose a consequence.** *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24); *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26). Public transparency and consumer pressure change decisions only when the reading reaches a decision point with a consequence attached, at the level of the named decision rather than a firm-wide average.
4. **The mechanism must bring a closure that differs.** *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (Fig 6 :14); *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23). A mechanism that only re-processes what the firm already knew adds no atom.
5. **There is no universal winner.** Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-50). The same mechanism that forces a safety recall can reward a misleading label elsewhere. Which condition fails, and which actor can repair it, is a per-slot question.

These four conditions are a **dependency chain**, not a scorecard: C1 before C2 before C3 before C4 (Fig 3 :20). Mandating more disclosure cannot rescue a mechanism whose reading the firm can set (Fig 3 :19).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a league table of the five mechanisms. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that law always wins, or that transparency never matters. Valence is relative to the residual.
- Not that more disclosure, more ratings or more policies change behaviour. That is library growth, not reach growth (Fig 5 :72).
- Not that reputation, culture or stakeholder language are grounds. They are instruments inside the firm's or the verifier's frame.
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)` for any mechanism.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Non-updating test:** does the mechanism's reading change when the firm is given reason to expect a different answer, changes scope, or shops the metric? If yes, C1 fails.
- **Non-reconstructibility test:** can the firm author and grade the check itself? If yes, C2 fails.
- **Dead-channel test:** does a named decision-maker with power to impose a consequence actually read the per-slot signal? If not, C3 fails.
- **Import test:** did any atom enter the firm's reach from a closure that differs? If not, C4 fails.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** a mechanism that moves when pushed still changing decisions; a reconstructible check being as effective; a dead report changing action; a universal ranking holding across habitat substitutions. Any one of these breaks the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five mechanisms are a human catalogue of habitat actors, not nature's own list. Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral (*They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*). The answer stops where the available instruments can no longer discriminate `Δcontact` from `Δdescription`, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* so the missing per-mechanism ranking is reported as a `gap`, not hidden.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank law, investor oversight, employee voice, public transparency and consumer pressure. It says the question is a union, not a contest: an accountability mechanism changes a decision only when its reading does not move when the firm pushes on it, when the firm cannot produce the check, when the reading reaches a decision-maker who can impose a consequence at the level of the named decision, and when it brings in something the firm's own library did not contain. Law is the strongest anchor only when enforcement is real; voice is the most independent reading only when it is protected; disclosure and pressure change contact only when tied to a point of consequence. The five are habitat actors whose value depends on the decision at issue, so there is no standing winner. What the framework offers is the shape any effective mechanism must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap between a changed description and a changed decision. The ranking stays open until the ground settles it.
