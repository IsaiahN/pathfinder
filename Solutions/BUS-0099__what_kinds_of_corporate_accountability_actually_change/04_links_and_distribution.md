## What the wider literature bears on the question

The literature is used as a **budget filter and one witness**, never as the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Four observations matter:

1. **Disclosure can change the description only.** The CSR page's "lip service" and "window dressing" lines, and the ESG page's greenwashing line, are the field's own names for `Δdescription` without `Δcontact`. They corroborate F7 but they do not prove that no disclosure ever changes a decision.
2. **Consumer response is conditional on the signal being anchored where the decision happens.** Houde and Myers find responsiveness to local, salient energy costs. That is C3 at the purchase point, not a general claim that consumer pressure works.
3. **Voice needs protection to be a check.** The whistleblowing source shows retaliation as the default and confidentiality as the enabling condition. That is the framework's stake rule (Fig 10 :59) observed in an independent field.
4. **Oversight has its own stake.** The shareholder-activism source says large holders can self-deal against minorities, and the corporate-governance source demands independent verification. Those are the Seat Map's "interest in the reading is disqualifying" and the Fig 10 :42 non-reconstructibility rule in the finance field.

**Overlap warning.** The four Wikipedia pages are one evidence pool (one encyclopedia, one editorial community), so they count as **one witness**, not four (Fig 2 :40). The NBER paper is an independent pool. The framework essays all share an author with the figures, so they corroborate the method, not the verdict. No source here was allowed to flip a figure verdict; the one tension (ESG activity as evidence of change) was returned to the census and re-mapped as `Δdescription` in `01` section 6.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four conditions and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Seat Map :285** - *"Every instrument points inward unless one is built to point out."* This is the substantive point of the question, so the first contact must itself point outward: ask for a failure, not an endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-condition shape and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which condition they think is not necessary; (3) then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

The proposition is: **(1)** validate the output against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on other problems.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the four conditions without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a shape with four conditions and a `gap` on the ranking. That is honest, but it is not a published empirical result. Do not present it as one.
