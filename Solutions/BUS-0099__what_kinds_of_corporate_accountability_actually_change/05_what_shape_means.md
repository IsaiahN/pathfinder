## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* followed by the limit that it *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0099, the shape is "an accountability mechanism that satisfies C1 to C4." A rank order such as "law beats transparency beats consumer pressure" is a name, and it is not licensed by the derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* That is why the residual in `01` section 0 is stated as `Δcontact` versus `Δdescription`, not as "accountability" versus "no accountability."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly the move that turned the five-way ranking question into a union of four conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, the five named mechanisms (law, investor oversight, employee voice, public transparency, consumer pressure) are names supplied by the business field. They are candidate bindings onto habitat actors in D2, not terms the framework returned. Their use is legitimate only as provenanced bindings, never as a derivation. Declaring "law is the anchor" turns a visible choice into an invisible one unless the condition "its reading does not move when pushed" is kept beside it.

## 4. Why a reading does not determine the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a firm publishes a sustainability report, incidents fall, and investors re-rate it. The reading "improved accountability" does not discriminate among harm actually reduced, scope redefined, a supplier dropped, or a better PR budget. The world source on CSR calls the last of these *"window-dressing"*. What is owed is a discriminator that separates the sources, not a name for the reading. This is the reason C1 (non-updating ground) and C2 (non-reconstructible check) are in the shape at all.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A self-scored accountability index is a frame validating itself.

## 5. Q1 - Does the framework return a winner? No

**What licenses a particular mechanism as *the* effective one?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a claim about a named slot, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** Not if it keeps the four conditions in front and the five names behind. `01` section 6 records the retraction of the first draft, which tried to rank the five as if they were five atoms. That is the error this section forbids.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that "law works" or "transparency works" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground (a verified outcome, a court, a purchase, a capital allocation) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the accountability question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

I initially wrote that naming any of the five mechanisms is forbidden by "shape, not a name." That was too broad. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Law, oversight, voice, transparency and pressure are already in the habitat (D2); naming them is a **binding from the business frame** onto actors already enumerated, not the invention of a new kind. What remains wrong is claiming that such a binding *derives* which one is effective, or that naming one moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a reading of "improved accountability" still cannot comment on its source without a discriminator. The reason is the missing instrument, not a ban on naming held actors. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. What "shape" means for BUS-0099, in one sentence

A shape here is the four-condition constraint (non-updating ground, non-reconstructible check, live per-slot channel with consequence, differing closure) that any accountable mechanism must satisfy to change a decision, stated before and instead of a ranking of the five candidates that the figures do not license.
