## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the identity does not. The question is a union of three loops that must be unbundled and ranged separately against the ground. The residual is indexed per slot, so a single global w is not an adequate statement of it. A directed expansion needs a structure between energy and a state, and the candidates disagree about time-translation symmetry and which term carries it. They are a functional group of first-order molecules as written, with a conditional isomer claim over the shared atom set, and any new term must pay its description-length bargain. No figure line selects a constant, an evolving field, or modified gravity, so the identity is reported as `gap` while the shape is reported as constrained. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest world corroboration is not agreement but the DESI collaboration's own statement that the nature of dark energy remains unknown and that measurable differences are the target of current instruments.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a candidate name before the shape has been checked; Fig 2 :42 prices that ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."), and here the cost is high because the answer changes the inferred fate of the universe. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate bindings are withheld rather than asserted, so there is nothing physical to withdraw. A later ground measurement can fill the open slot without touching the shape; a refutation of any of C1 to C3 would withdraw the shape claim, and the correction is recorded in `11_audit.md` with provenance. No primitive has to be abandoned.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A decisive source-discriminating measurement (expansion versus growth, redshift evolution of w at high significance, or an early/late H0 resolution) would move the identity from `gap` to `grounded`; a derivation of the small vacuum value would close the upstream loop; a figure line that picks a candidate, an unrefusable direct state-state bond, energy alone yielding directed expansion, or a demonstrated partition of the three candidates would fire against the shape. Until one of these occurs, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
