In the framework's minimum set, energy is "conserved, finite, directionless alone", while the gradient is "a difference that can be spent". A direction therefore requires a structure bonded to a state, and the constant/evolving argument is really about whether that structure is fixed or changing and which state it is bonded to.

## Stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send only the output, the mode and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A Stage 2 or Stage 3 agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). A Stage 1 refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source.
