# The Tether's answer on the origin of dark energy

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the figure-derived constraints, `gap` on the identity of the physical cause. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Observations indicate accelerated expansion, while the standard model cannot say whether the driver is a fixed vacuum energy, a time-varying field, or a large-scale failure of gravity. The QFT vacuum prediction overshoots the observed value by about 120 orders of magnitude (question 3 in the same source, upstream). A 2025 DESI combination mildly prefers an evolving density at about 3.2 to 3.4 sigma, below discovery significance, and DESI alone remains consistent with ΛCDM. The residual is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*), not one question about a single parameter.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before searching.** Three loops, each with its own ground: the vacuum-energy discrepancy, the expansion-history shape, and the large-scale gravity question. No average across them is legitimate (F2). Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**
2. **The residual is per slot.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A global w near -1 is compatible with a live residual in growth of structure, the distance ladder, or the vacuum slot.
3. **A directed expansion needs a structure.** Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (energy); Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"** (gradient). Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."** A bare energy term cannot supply a direction. The constant-versus-evolving dispute is about whether the mediating structure is fixed or changing.
4. **The symmetry axis is time translation.** Fig 13 :65: **"no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder"**. "Constant" keeps that symmetry, "evolving" breaks it, "artifact" relocates it to geometry. That is what the candidates disagree about.
5. **The candidates are not yet isomers; they are a functional group.** Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** As written they share too few atoms to be isomers; expressed over the full six-term set they would be, with a stated falsifier (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
6. **A new term must pay.** Fig 5 :43: **"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."** The cosmological constant is the cheapest candidate, one number, which is a structural reason for its standard role, not a measurement of nature.
7. **The question is not foreclosed.** Fig 6 :34: **"It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."** DESI and other probes are already returning such readings, so this is hard at the current budget, not underivable.
8. **The ground decides; agreement does not.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**. A vote of cosmologists cannot move the anchor.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "dark energy is a cosmological constant". That is a name where the framework returns a shape, and a source read off a projection without a discriminating instrument.
- Not "dark energy is quintessence" and not "gravity must be modified". Same reason.
- Not that DESI has discovered evolving dark energy. The hint is about 3.2 to 3.4 sigma, below 5 sigma, and DESI alone is consistent with ΛCDM.
- Not that the framework derives the value of the cosmological constant, or cancels the QFT vacuum energy. That is question 3, a separate loop.
- Not a theory of everything, and not a claim that this shape is the only possible one. A frame cannot certify its own completeness.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R, φ)` for any of the three candidates.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the discriminators are:

- **Expansion versus growth:** does the same component fit both the distance history and the growth of structure? A modification of gravity generally changes the relation between them; a dark-energy component does not in the same way.
- **Redshift evolution:** is w measurably different from -1 at any redshift, at decisive significance? That would favour an evolving field and break the catalyst reading of a constant term.
- **Early versus late H0:** does the Hubble tension track a change in the dark-energy sector, or a local systematic, or a large-scale inhomogeneity?
- **The vacuum value:** a derivation of the small non-zero value from particle physics would close the upstream loop. Absent that, the anthropic and landscape arguments remain a preference, not a settlement.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a figure line that picks a candidate; a direct state-state or structure-structure bond the framework cannot refuse; energy alone yielding directed expansion; or a demonstrated partition of the three candidates. Any one fires against the shape claim, not for a candidate.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The figure text says **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** (Fig 13 :134) and **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** (Fig 8 :39). So the identity is reported as `gap` while the shape is reported as constrained. The stop is at the limit of what current instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.
