**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the constraints, `gap` on the physical identity. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the required significance is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is dark energy constant, evolving, or an artifact of gravity at large scales" is a union of three loops, not one question: the vacuum-energy discrepancy, the expansion-history shape, and the large-scale gravity question. Each has its own ground and no average across them is legitimate. The residual is indexed by object slot, so a single global equation-of-state parameter can hide a live signal in structure growth, the distance ladder or the vacuum slot. Under the six-term substrate, energy is directionless alone and a gradient is a spendable difference, so a directed expansion requires a structure between energy and a state; the constant/evolving dispute is about whether that structure is fixed or changing and which symmetry it carries. The three candidates are a functional group of first-order molecules, not isomers as the field currently writes them, and any new term must pay its description-length bargain. No figure line selects a candidate, so the identity is reported as `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument decides it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that dark energy is a cosmological constant, quintessence, or modified gravity. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that DESI has discovered evolving dark energy. The significance is below the conventional 5 sigma and DESI alone is consistent with ΛCDM.
- Not a derivation of the cosmological constant's value and not a solution of the 120-order discrepancy (that is question 3, a separate loop).
- Not that the three candidates are already isomers, and not that the catalyst or cycle analogies are physical laws. They are `specified` structural readings with stated falsifiers.
- Not a theory of everything, not a uniqueness claim, and not `grounded`.
- Not a claim that this simulated second seat is an independent witness. It shares the primary's evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Expansion versus growth:** does one component fit both the distance history and structure growth, or does the relation between them change, as modified gravity predicts?
2. **Redshift evolution:** is w measurably different from -1 at decisive significance at any redshift? That would favour an evolving field and break the catalyst reading of a constant term.
3. **Early versus late H0:** does the Hubble tension track the dark-energy sector, a local systematic, or large-scale inhomogeneity?
4. **The vacuum value:** a derivation of the small non-zero value from particle physics would close the upstream loop.
5. **Against the shape claim:** a figure line that picks a candidate, an unrefusable direct state-state bond, energy alone yielding directed expansion, or a demonstrated partition of the three candidates.
