## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are the three candidates isomers?

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

As the field writes them, the three candidates occupy different slots: a constant Λ is a term in the geometric equation with the effect of a uniform energy density; quintessence is a scalar field (a structure with a potential, evolving over Time); modified gravity alters the geometric side. As the record currently stands they share almost no atoms in common, so under Fig 12's definition they are **not isomers**; they are different arrangements with different atom inventories.

The stronger version: if all three are written over the **full shared atom set** `{Space, Time, Energy, Spectrum, Gradient, Network}`, differing only in which structure mediates which state pair and which bond holds, then they **are** isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance, invisible in a flat ingredient list and decisive in the behaviour. That is the structural claim worth testing. It also explains why the observational degeneracy is hard: an isomer difference is exactly the difference a formula that records only ingredients cannot show.

### Q2 - Are they a functional group instead?

Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

The weaker and safer reading is that each candidate is a `state → structure → state` first-order molecule. The group says "cross-kind expansion driver"; the qualifier says which member. Under that reading the disagreement is about the qualifier, not the kind. This matches F3: the substrate requires a structure but does not select among the three structures it holds.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if one candidate can be written without an atom that another requires (for example, modified gravity with no Energy term, or quintessence with no Space), the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands instead. This is checkable by inspecting the three compositions, not by argument.

### Q3 - The catalyst test: does the term survive its own use?

Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

The source review states that vacuum energy is not diluted as space expands, so a constant vacuum term behaves like a **catalyst**: it survives its own use across expansion. An evolving scalar field does not; its density changes with time, so it is a spent-and-regenerating part. A modification of gravity is neither, because it changes the rule rather than supplying a part.

This is a genuine figure-grounded discriminator, but it is a **structural analogy, not a measurement**. Its mode is `specified`. It predicts what to look for: a term whose density is constant in expansion has catalyst-like standing, and a term whose density evolves does not. The world-side fact that the density *"may be slowly decreasing with time"* (DESI hint) is therefore evidence against the catalyst reading, at low significance. Recorded as `specified`, unmeasured.

### Q4 - The cycle test: what is the sign of the loop?

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

The cosmological-constant literature notes that the equilibrium of Einstein's static universe is unstable: if the universe expands slightly, the expansion releases vacuum energy, which causes yet more expansion. That is a cycle whose output re-enters as input with a **positive** sign, so it closes into runaway expansion. The sign is the thing to check, not the mere presence of a loop. A quintessence model with tracker behaviour can be read as a cycle of the opposite or regulated sign, because the field's density tracks the background rather than amplifying every perturbation.

**Falsifier:** if a candidate's composition cannot be written as a cycle with a determinate sign, the cycle reading does not apply to it and must be withdrawn for that candidate. This is recorded rather than hidden.

### Q5 - What order is the union?

Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**

Dependency order of the three loops from F2:

1. **Perception (settled):** the acceleration and the 120-order vacuum discrepancy are both observed. This link works.
2. **Vocabulary (mostly settled):** terms exist for all three candidates. The middle link of Fig 3, having terms for what the situation affords, exists here, unlike the exemplar case.
3. **Objective (live):** what would count as progress? A measurement discriminating the candidates at decisive significance, plus a derivation of the small vacuum value. This is where DESI operates.
4. **Planning (live):** which instrument and which cross-check (growth versus expansion, local versus early-universe H0).
5. **Learn and carry (not reached):** name what worked and carry it to the next problem.

Effort spent above a break arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :21). Spending more effort on perception (more of the same distance measurements) cannot rescue a break at the objective-to-planning link, which needs a discriminating cross-check rather than more precision on the same observable.

## CONCLUSION

The three candidates are **not isomers as the field writes them** (different atom inventories); they are isomers only in the stronger form where both are expressed over the full shared six-term set, with a stated falsifier. The safer reading is a **functional group** of first-order `state → structure → state` molecules. Two secondary structural tests separate them further: a constant term is **catalyst-like** (survives its own use), an evolving term is not; and the constant case contains a **positive-sign cycle** (expansion releases vacuum energy which drives more expansion), while tracker quintessence is a regulated cycle. None of this picks a winner. The union's dependency chain currently stops at the objective-to-planning link, not at perception. The order is Fig 3's, not an importance ranking.
