## What is decided, in effect terms

The residual is the observed accelerated expansion of the universe, plus the failure of the standard model to say whether the thing driving it is a fixed vacuum energy, a time-varying field, or a large-scale failure of gravity. Each option leads to a different instrument programme and a different theoretical commitment. A `constant` answer commits to time-translation symmetry of the vacuum energy. An `evolving` answer breaks that symmetry. An `artifact of gravity` answer relocates the cause to the geometric side. The figures constrain the shape any answer must have and say which questions must be split and ranged against the ground; they do not contain dark-energy physics, so the identity question ends in `gap`, plainly stated.
