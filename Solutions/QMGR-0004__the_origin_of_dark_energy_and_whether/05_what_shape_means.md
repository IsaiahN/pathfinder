## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**.

From *The Library of Babel* (essay 5, opened 2026-11-23): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the set of constraints any answer about dark energy must satisfy before we can name the physical cause:

- the residual is a union of loops, not one scalar question (F2);
- it is indexed by slot, not by one global parameter (F1);
- a directed expansion needs a structure between energy and a state (F3);
- the candidates disagree about a symmetry that the substrate treats as load-bearing (F4);
- the ground, not agreement, decides (F5);
- the identity is a `gap` until an instrument discriminates (F6).

A **name** would be "dark energy is a cosmological constant", "dark energy is quintessence", or "dark energy is modified gravity". Those are the fillers the shape refuses. `SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's analogue; the analogue here is `ENERGY -(structure)- EXPANSION` with the structure and its binding left open.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual is described as "accelerated expansion plus a 120-order vacuum discrepancy plus a 3-sigma evolution hint", not as "quintessence".
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the shape plus the named ground, not a candidate.
- Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."** The habitat is surveys, instruments and data, enumerated, not composed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For dark energy that means a measurement discriminating the candidates at decisive significance, plus, for the vacuum discrepancy, a derivation of the small value.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay 7, opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the source.

Here the reading is the measured expansion history. Several sources could produce it: a constant vacuum term, a rolling field, or a modification of gravity. Naming one from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is why the DESI hint, however interesting, is not a licence to name: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

## 4. Why the global equation-of-state parameter is the specific forbidden error

The question's traditional framing reduces the residual to one number, w, and asks whether it equals -1. Under Fig 1 :19 (**"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**) and :22 (**"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**), this is exactly how a live signal vanishes. The slots here are H(z), growth of structure, CMB, the local distance ladder, and the vacuum-energy discrepancy. A single global w can be consistent with -1 while one slot carries the residual that will eventually break the model.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this: a reading that does not discriminate among sources is a projection with lossy information. Averaging slots is building a coarser projection and then reading a source off it. The shape therefore forbids treating "is w = -1?" as the whole question.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid discussing candidates at all?

**Answered: no.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Λ, a scalar field and a modified action are not among the six substrate terms, so naming any of them as *the* answer is still unlicensed, but the reason is that it is a binding from the physics frame onto an unheld primitive, not that the framework imposes a blanket ban on the word. Discussing them as candidates, with provenance, is allowed and necessary for the WORLD section.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay 2, opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous cosmologists who agree with a shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground, plus a refuter, one to one.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"The output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figure text. It is **not** a test of dark energy and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on. The essay's own disclaimer, *"I am not claiming that the Tether is a self-validating axiom system"*, applies with full force here: a framework that returned the identity of dark energy without a source-discriminating instrument would be certifying itself beyond its ground.
