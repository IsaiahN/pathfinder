## Three findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The identity is openly unresolved.** DESI's own cosmology page says the essential nature of dark energy *"remains unknown"* and that *"Other ideas involve new forces of nature, modifications of the theory of gravity, or novel effects of quantum gravity. We don't know."* This is the world-side corroboration of the `gap` verdict in F6. It is a secondary source, not the ground.
2. **The candidates are distinguishable in principle.** The same page says *"some of these theories predict measurable differences in cosmological observables, and it is these differences that DESI will attempt to detect."* This corroborates F7: an instrument already returns a failing reading, so the question is hard, not foreclosed.
3. **The current hint is not a settlement.** The DESI Wikipedia page reports 3.2 to 3.4 sigma, below the conventional 5 sigma, with DESI alone consistent with ΛCDM. This corroborates F5: the ground has not yet voted decisively, and agreement among data combinations is not the same as a measurement that discriminates.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer sources after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them any framework material.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Here the cost is high, so the frame you ask must be genuinely outside this evidence pool.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim, its mode and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only if they are curious, the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape, mode and pre-registered refuter. No theory. Record the verdict before anything else is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the three source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The essay's own warning applies: an output *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

## Hosting

Nothing is sent by this agent. Stage the packet at a public, opt-in page you host (a repository release, a personal site with a decline option, or a forum thread after a public preprint exists). Prefer a link a recipient can decline over an attachment pushed to a list. Public venue details are in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
