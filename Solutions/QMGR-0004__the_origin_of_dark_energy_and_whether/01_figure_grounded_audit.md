## 0. The question under audit, stated as an effect

> "The origin of dark energy and whether it is constant, evolving, or an artifact of gravity at large scales" (QM & GR Questions.txt line 8)

Stated in effect terms: multiple independent observations (Type Ia supernovae, CMB, BAO, weak lensing, Lyman-alpha forest) indicate that the expansion of the universe is accelerating. The standard model (GR plus a cosmological constant plus cold dark matter) fits a wide range of data, but two things fail to resolve. First, the vacuum energy inferred from quantum field theory exceeds the observed value by roughly 120 orders of magnitude, the cosmological constant problem, which is question 3 in the same source and is upstream of this one. Second, combined DESI data from 2024 to 2025 mildly prefer a dark-energy density that weakens with time, at about 3.2 to 3.4 sigma, below discovery significance and not confirmed by DESI alone.

The three candidates are not three answers to one question yet. They are three commitments:

| candidate | what it commits to | what would decide it |
|---|---|---|
| constant (Λ) | time-translation symmetry of the vacuum energy density; one number | a measurement of w consistent with -1 at all redshifts, plus a derivation of the value |
| evolving (quintessence, dynamical dark energy) | a time-varying scalar field with a potential; a new term | a detected redshift evolution of w or of the density |
| artifact of gravity at large scales | modification of the geometric side, or breakdown of FLRW/inhomogeneity | a growth-versus-expansion discrepancy that no energy component fits |

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula step "Direction").** Before the run, the sought-for shape is committed: *a figure-grounded account of the constraints any answer must satisfy, a separation of the compound into its loops, and a named ground that decides each loop, with the identity of the physical cause reported as `gap` unless a figure line actually governs it.* This binding makes the preference refusable (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** The residual has three candidate causes plus one upstream discrepancy (QFT vacuum versus observed Λ). That is a union of conditions, not a single scalar question. - **Symmetry.** "Constant" asserts time-translation symmetry of the energy density. "Evolving" denies it. "Artifact" relocates the symmetry question to the geometric side. Fig 13 :65 (*"no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder"*) makes time load-bearing for any conservation derivation, so the symmetry disagreement is structural, not cosmetic.
- **Scale.** The question says "at large scales". Fig 4 :19-20 (*"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*) says the kernel does not change with size. A large-scale modification is therefore a change of actors, currency or anchor at a stratum, not a new kernel. Fig 12 :104 makes irreducibility level-relative.

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

1. **Observational cosmology (cosmographic).** Actors: supernova surveys, CMB, BAO, weak lensing, Lyman-alpha forest, distance ladder. Currency: measured expansion history H(z), distances, growth of structure. Anchor: instruments and the sky. Legitimate domain; the ground decides.
2. **Model-building (theory).** Actors: metric, stress-energy tensor, scalar field, modified action, vacuum energy. Currency: predictions plus the description cost of a new term. Anchor: observation and the six-term substrate's composition rules (Fig 13). This is where the three candidates live.
3. **Framework-internal composition (the Tether).** Actors: six terms (Space, Time, Energy states; Spectrum, Gradient, Network structures), seven bonds, negation, closure, import. Currency: composition and residual. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. This domain constrains shape; it does not select among physical models.
4. **Verification and outreach.** Actors: cosmologists, referees, readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so its internal agreement has zero corroborating weight (Fig 2 :40).

### 1.3 Partition or union

The three candidates form a **union**, not a partition. A single lever that rules out evolution does not rule out a modification of gravity, because the two can be degenerate in the same observables. Fig 9 :54 (*"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*) and :57 (*"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). The upstream vacuum-energy discrepancy (question 3) is a separate loop with its own ground. The overall residual is therefore a compound of at least three loops: (a) why the QFT vacuum prediction is so wrong, (b) whether the expansion history requires a new component at all, (c) whether gravity is the thing that fails at large scales. Each gets its own loop and its own verdict; they are never averaged.

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Perception: do we see acceleration? Yes, long settled. Vocabulary: do we have terms for what the residual affords? Yes, Λ, scalar fields, modified gravity. Objective: what would count as progress? A measurement discriminating the three at sufficient significance. Planning: which instrument? DESI, Euclid, Rubin/LSST, CMB-S4 class experiments. Learn and carry: name what worked and carry it. The chain currently stops at the objective to planning link: the hint exists but is not yet decisive. Fig 3 :27 (*"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*) does not apply here, because an instrument exists and is returning a failing reading; the link is being worked, not ignored.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9, each with verbatim quotes and a refuter

### F1 - The residual is per object slot, not one global parameter

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

**Refuter:** show that one aggregated parameter closes every slot residual with no live slot left. If that holds, this finding fails. **Status: single-source** (Fig 1), corroborated in shape by Fig 9 :4-5 (split, do not average).

The observed acceleration is a directedness. By the substrate, energy alone carries no direction; a gradient is the structure whose defining property is that it can be spent. Under the six-term set, a directed expansion therefore requires a structure bonded to a state, not a bare energy term. "Constant versus evolving" is then a question about whether that structure is fixed or changing, and "artifact of gravity" is a question about which state the structure is bonded to. This is a shape constraint, not a physics measurement.

**Refuter:** exhibit, inside the six-term set, a composition in which energy alone yields directed expansion with no structure, or show the framework must admit a direct state-state bond it cannot refuse. **Status: single-source** for the substrate reading (Fig 13), triangulated with the Formula substrate section (low overlap: compact loop versus figure text).
