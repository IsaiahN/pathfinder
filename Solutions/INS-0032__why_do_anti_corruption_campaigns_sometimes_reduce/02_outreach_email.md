# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with a declared instrument gap, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about why anti-corruption campaigns split before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about anti-corruption campaigns that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** an anti-corruption campaign is not an instrument whose effect lives inside the campaign. Its effect is a relation between the campaign and the habitat it is ranged against. Two conditions have to hold at once for it to land on the reduction side:
>
> 1. the measure of success is a ground that does not move when the regime pushes on it, meaning the reported number is not generated and filtered by the campaign's own machinery;
> 2. the campaign's investigative reach is renewed by contact with situations and institutions whose closure differs from the regime's own, meaning evidence and a refusal seat can enter from outside the chain of command.
>
> The claim is that these are two different failure modes with two different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. A campaign whose success is measured by its own arrest count drifts toward counting enemies; a campaign whose investigators all sit inside the regime's own closure can only rearrange what the regime already holds. The further claim is that the same agency can be the thing that reduces corruption under one residual and the thing that removes rivals under another, so the benefactor and the malfactor can be the same object.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can tell you which named campaign is a purge, that any particular country is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, that perception indices measure actual corruption, or that this is the only account of corruption and cleanup. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name. The instrument that would discriminate a genuine reduction from a factional removal under the same headline number is not in my hands, and I say so.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a campaign's sustained reduction in corruption is unchanged after its success metric is replaced by an independently gathered record that does not update on what the campaign thinks;
> 2. a campaign renews its reach entirely by internal composition, with nothing entering from a differing closure, and still passes that independent test across a change of habitat;
> 3. an anti-corruption agency's measured value stays the same across a documented change in what the ruling elite needs;
> 4. two campaigns with identical laws, courts, investigators and budgets diverge in outcome with no difference in ground contact or imported evidence;
> 5. one intervention restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35) |
| Two failure modes kept separate | "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57); a blended effectiveness score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Own count disqualified as the anchor | "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." (Fig 1 :27) |
| No name for which campaign is a purge | "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32) |
| Stake declared | "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." (Fig 10 :59) |
| Pre-registered refuter | "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device" |
| Mode stated on the claim | "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
