## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a three-place relation among authority belief, campaign action and outcome, indexed by slot and ordered by time; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "reduces" is a threshold relative to a baseline, and removal is not reversible by undoing the action. Scale: one campaign loop, a population of agencies and factions, and a time channel across campaigns; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** an anti-corruption campaign lands on the reduction side when its success measure is ranged against a ground that does not update on what the campaign thinks and when its reach is renewed by contact with closures that differ from the regime's own; it lands on the purge side when the metric is self-produced or the reach stays inside the regime's closure, and the same instrument changes valence when the residual it is ranged against changes. The two conditions are kept separate per domain and never averaged. The same formal atoms under different bonds are isomers, so a statute-book comparison cannot settle the case. Which named campaign is a purge is a declared `gap`: the source-discriminating instrument is characterized but not held. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The political-science pool corroborates the split but cannot settle it; the low-overlap fields corroborate the measurement and closure mechanisms, not the campaign-level fact.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A campaign failing C2 would be sold a better metric, or a campaign failing C1 would be given more independent investigators, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 4 :25); buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a sustained reduction unchanged after the metric is replaced by an independent record, (2) a campaign renewing its reach purely internally yet passing that test across a habitat change, (3) invariant agency valence across a change of residual, or (4) one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. The arrival of a source-discriminating instrument that separates reduction from removal under the same reading would close the `gap`. A world source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-campaign outcomes are fully predicted by a single measure would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
