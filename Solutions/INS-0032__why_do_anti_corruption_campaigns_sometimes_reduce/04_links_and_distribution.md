## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** The campaign-effect studies on China (Li and Manion 2023, Duben 2018, Tsai 2025, Cheng Li 2014, Keyu Jin 2023, Thomas and Wang 2026, Manion's institutionalization reading, Alonso et al. 2022) all draw on the same case, the same language of party discipline and the same narrow set of observable readings. Per Fig 2 :31, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Their disagreement is a split inside one pool, which is exactly why Fig 9 :4-5 requires splitting the question rather than averaging the estimates.

- Gillespie and Okruhlik's 1991 title, *"The Political Dimensions of Corruption Cleanups: A Framework for Analysis"*, is the earliest bibliographic marker that corruption cleanups are analysed as political acts, not merely administrative ones. Only the record was opened, so no substantive quote is used.
- The Wikipedia Purge article records that governments *"want to obscure that they are doing so, may often justify their actions as dealing with security threats or corruption"*, and that purges are especially likely *"when the power of competing elites is temporarily low"*. This is the valence claim at the regime stratum, from a political-science pool.
- The Economist 2014 line, *"it is hard not to see corruption allegations as the latter-day weapon of choice in the winner-takes-all power struggles that the party has always suffered"*, is the clearest statement of the purge reading from inside that pool.
- Cheng Li 2014 and Keyu Jin 2023 state the reduction reading from the same pool. The conflict was returned to the census (F1, re-census R1) and not used to flip the verdict.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each read directly:

1. **Economics of measurement and systems theory.** Goodhart's law and Campbell's law both state that a measure used for control stops measuring the thing it was built to measure. Campbell's phrase, *"the more subject it will be to corruption pressures"*, is almost literal: a campaign success metric becomes corrupt under the pressure of being used as a target. Corroborates C1's channel mechanism, not the campaign fact.
2. **Survey measurement and source discrimination.** The CPI article states that perception may differ from actual levels and that the index exists *"due to the difficulty of measuring absolute levels of corruption"*. IACA's programme states that existing measures cannot distinguish state capture from petty corruption. These corroborate the declared `gap`: the missing thing is an instrument that discriminates sources.
3. **Anti-corruption practice and implementation research.** U4's Ukraine review reports that reform durability depends on relationships across authorities, civil society and business, that international norms are reshaped by domestic actors, and that there are *"no universal laws in anti-corruption"*. This corroborates the habitat and closure-difference conditions from a practitioner pool, not from political science.
4. **Country-level institutional documentation.** U4's Benin Helpdesk Answer records agencies placed under the executive, described as *"susceptible to political influence and capture"*, alongside a gap between reported perception change and reported personal experience. It corroborates the same closure condition at a different site, but it is still anti-corruption research, so it is not counted as an independent field.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** "any frame you pick will seem to fit" if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.
- **Fig 10 :47:** "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A campaign's own definition of corruption that decides who is removed is no longer a neutral convention; the packet never asks a recipient to accept one.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
