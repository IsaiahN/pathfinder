### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among authority belief b, campaign action a and outcome o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. It has at least three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (campaign, outcome) would lose the prediction and the authority's belief, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. An independent record of corrupt acts does not update on the campaign's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Reduces" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is a quantity compared against a baseline, not merely conjoined with another.
- **Scale:** the slot is a campaign, but the ranking of "reduces corruption" exists only against a population of other campaigns and baselines (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between one campaign's step loop and the population's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *anti-corruption works while its measure stays independent and its reach stays open to the outside.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific campaign situation. Result: *use independent monitors and let outsiders investigate.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into two checkboxes and loses Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Coming back down, it tells a campaign with a self-authored metric to add outside monitors, which is effort aimed at the wrong failure.
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Independent" does not specify that the record must not update on what the campaign thinks (Fig 2 :29-30). Coming back down, it permits a campaign to measure its own success with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the valence flip. "Let outsiders investigate" has no place for the possibility that the outside body becomes the instrument of a different residual, or that the same agency changes sign (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
4. It loses the source-discriminator requirement and the type question about a real refusal seat versus the unary negation operator. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a reduction from a removal under the same reading, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
