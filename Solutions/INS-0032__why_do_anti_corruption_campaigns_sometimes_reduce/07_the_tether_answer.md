# The Tether's answer on anti-corruption campaigns and purges

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No independent corruption measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a campaign's institutional library predicts and what the campaign actually returns splits into a reduction regime and a purge regime, indexed per campaign and per target. R is indexed per campaign, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about the **bifurcation of one action class**, not about the average effectiveness of enforcement.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the campaign's measure of success is a ground that does not update on what the campaign thinks.
- **C2:** the campaign's investigative reach is renewed by contact with a closure that differs from the regime's own.

## 2. The shape

1. **The effect is not in the campaign alone.** Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." More laws and more investigators are an agent-only improvement if the habitat stays captured.
2. **The campaign's own count is not the anchor.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Arrests, conviction rates and approval ratings are readings from inside the campaign's own machinery.
3. **When the metric and the regime update on each other, the definition drifts coherently.** Fig 2 :13-15: "no anchor; each updates on the others" and "is whoever had the most influence." Fig 2 :34: "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." This is the purge regime at the level of the definition: "corruption" comes to name whoever the incumbent needs removed.
4. **When the reach stays inside the regime's closure, it cannot add an atom.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal inspection adds an evidence source. This is the purge regime at the level of the instrument: the agency can only rearrange what the regime already holds.
5. **The same agency can be benefactor or malfactor.** Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual and "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and "nothing records which a term is." Under a residual of eroding governance the agency reduces corruption; under a residual of threatened power it removes rivals. Nothing in its charter records the switch.
6. **Two campaigns with identical formal atoms can be isomers.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Same law, court, agency and budget; one bonded to an independent evidence source, the other bonded to the appointing executive. A statute-book comparison cannot tell them apart.
7. **The readings do not settle the source.** Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" / "rather than the cause." A fall in arrests is consistent with genuine reduction, a definitional change, a purge, or a reporting shift. Fig 13 :129: "It is recoverable from the lineage." What is owed is a record of who defined the charge, who gathered the evidence and who could refuse the verdict.
8. **The break is usually in the middle of the chain.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary/objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. Campaigns can count arrests and pass laws; they rarely have a term for "what would count as corruption reduction here".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No independent corruption record pays for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which campaign, which regime or which leader is a purge. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not an average. It makes no claim about what percentage of campaigns work. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that laws, investigators or leaders are irrelevant. They are Γ. The claim is that Γ does not settle the outcome on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not a standing list of good institutions. Independence is a relation to the residual, not a property carried by a charter (Fig 11 :51).
- Not corroboration by agreement. The campaign studies share one case pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The measurement and practitioner literatures corroborate the mechanisms from lower-overlap fields, not the campaign-level fact.
- Not a theory of everything about institutions. It stops where the instrument stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are arrests, approval, corruption-perception scores and connected-firm profits; the candidate sources are genuine reduction, a changed definition, factional removal and a reporting shift.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. sustained reduction unchanged after the campaign's metric is replaced by an independently gathered record that does not update on what the campaign thinks;
2. reach renewed purely by internal composition with no atom from a differing closure, still passing that independent test across a habitat change;
3. agency valence invariant across a documented change of the ruling residual;
4. two campaigns with identical atoms and identical bonds diverging with no difference in contact or imported evidence;
5. one intervention restoring both conditions with nothing categorically different left over.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per campaign, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the campaign flipped would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is owed. Naming a campaign from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The eight conditions in section 2 are a shape; the ninth thing, which named campaign is a purge, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank regimes or name the campaign that is a purge. It says that an anti-corruption campaign's effect is a relation, not a property of the campaign: the campaign has to keep its measure of success ranged against a ground that does not move when the regime pushes on it, and it has to keep moving its investigative reach outward by contact with evidence and refusal seats that differ from the chain of command. Those are two conditions with two different failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. When the campaign counts its own arrests, the definition of corruption drifts toward whoever had the most influence; when all its investigators sit inside the regime's closure, it can only rearrange what the regime already holds. The agency that cleaned up governance can become the agency that removes rivals, because its valence follows the residual and nothing in its charter records the switch. Two campaigns with the same law, court and budget are isomers if the bonds differ. What is offered is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which named campaign is a purge stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
