# Expert verification packet - the Tether on anti-corruption campaigns and purges

**What this is:** a claim in a form a political scientist, institutional economist or anti-corruption practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No independent corruption measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An anti-corruption campaign's effect is a relation between the campaign and the habitat it is ranged against, not a property the campaign owns. Two conditions have to hold at once for it to land on the reduction side: the campaign's measure of success is a ground that does not move when the regime pushes on it, meaning the number is not generated and filtered by the campaign's own machinery; and its investigative reach is renewed by contact with institutions and evidence whose closure differs from the regime's own, so a refusal seat and an independent evidence source can enter from outside the chain of command. These are two different failure modes with two different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. When the metric is self-produced, the definition of corruption drifts toward whoever has most influence; when the reach stays inside the regime's closure, the campaign can only rearrange what the regime already holds. The same agency can be the thing that reduces corruption under one residual and the thing that removes rivals under another. Two campaigns with the same law, court, agency and budget can be isomers if the bonds to their evidence sources differ. The claim is a shape; it names no campaign and offers no standalone effectiveness number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 10

- **Statement:** a campaign's own count cannot be the anchor; a measure that decides an outcome stops being a convention; when the measurer and the measured update on each other, they converge on whoever had the most influence.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :27 - "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
  - Fig 2 :29 - "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
  - Fig 2 :14 - "they converge, and what they converge on"; Fig 2 :15 - "is whoever had the most influence"
  - Fig 2 :34 - "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."
  - Fig 10 :47 - "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** in the Chinese campaign the conviction rate for those investigated in the first half of 2014 was around 99 percent and evidence was gathered covertly before an announcement. The Economist wrote in 2014 that *"it is hard not to see corruption allegations as the latter-day weapon of choice in the winner-takes-all power struggles that the party has always suffered"*. Does a near-unity conviction rate and that reading not indicate a metric that has become a target, rather than a measure of corruption incidence? A "no" to both questions is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a campaign whose sustained reduction in corruption is unchanged after its success metric is replaced by an independently gathered record that does not update on what the campaign thinks; or a campaign in which the investigatory definition is set by a body that does not update on regime preferences and whose targets are drawn independently of political position, with that independence measured. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one institutional-facing check

- **Statement:** capability is campaign-and-habitat; composition inside a closure cannot add a new evidence source; renewal of reach requires contact with a closure that differs.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :39 - "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
  - Fig 11 :40 - "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
  - Fig 6 :21 - "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
  - Fig 6 :23 - "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
  - Fig 8 :32 - "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** the Chinese campaign was carried out by party disciplinary bodies, and *"Investigations by the party's disciplinary bodies are not part of the state judicial system"*; the sociologist Hu Xingdou speculated a second stage that would include *"the establishment of independent anti-corruption organs"*. U4 reports that in Benin anti-corruption authorities are *"placed under the authority of the executive branch"* and *"susceptible to political influence and capture"*. Are these not exactly a closure that cannot add an atom and an import that has not yet happened? A documented case of an internal-only campaign that passes an independent corruption test across a habitat change would refute the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a campaign that renews its reach purely by internal composition, with no new atom from a differing closure, and that still passes the independent corruption test across a change of habitat. That breaks idempotence and C2 with it.

### C3 - Structure, valence and the source gap

- **Statement:** the union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; two campaigns with identical atoms but different bonds are isomers; the agency's valence flips with the residual; the reading does not determine the source.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
  - Fig 12 :117 - "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
  - Fig 11 :50 - "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
  - Fig 11 :51 - "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
  - Fig 13 :129 - "It is recoverable from the lineage."
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** the same campaign is read as a purge by Li and Manion 2023 and as genuine reform by Cheng Li 2014 and Keyu Jin 2023, while approval rose and an external perception rank fell. Are these not different readings of one campaign whose source is not determined by any of them? A source-discriminating instrument that reliably separates reduction from removal under the same reading would close the gap and is the single most valuable next step.
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves no categorically different residue; or two campaigns with identical atoms and identical bonds whose outcomes diverge with no difference in contact or imported evidence, which would move the explanation out of composition and into habitat alone.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No independent corruption measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a theory of everything about institutions, and no final or unique theory claim.
- Not an average. No claim about the percentage of campaigns that work or the share of variance attributable to politics, law or leadership.
- Not that laws, investigators or leaders are irrelevant. They are Γ, the library operand; the claim is that Γ alone does not settle the outcome.
- Not a standing list of good institutions. Independence is a relation to the residual, provenanced as such, not a property carried by a charter.
- Not a name for any specific campaign or regime. That is a `gap` at the instrument boundary, not a hidden answer.
- Not that the political-science studies independently corroborate this. They share one case pool. Goodhart's law, Campbell's law, the CPI measurement critique and the U4 practitioner reviews corroborate mechanisms from lower-overlap fields, not the campaign-level fact.
- Not that this packet, produced with an LLM, is submittable or publishable as authored work. See the caveat in section 6.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-campaign study that measures C1 and C2 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the campaign flipped. Concretely:

1. **Anchor test:** does an independently gathered corruption-incidence record move differently from the campaign's own count, and does the campaign's count move when the campaign pushes on it? (Fig 2 :29-30, Fig 1 :27)
2. **Convention test:** did the definition of what counts as corrupt get set by a body that does not update on regime preferences, and could any seat refuse the charge? (Fig 10 :47, Fig 10 :42)
3. **Import test:** did any new evidence source or refusal seat enter from a closure that differs, before the campaign's outcome was observed? If not, C2 is refuted. (Fig 6 :23)
4. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study tell a genuine reduction from a factional removal when both produce the same headline number? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.

## 7. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :29-30 and :34, Fig 6 :21 and :23, Fig 8 :32, Fig 11 :39-40 and :50-51, Fig 10 :47, Fig 12 :117 and Fig 9 :57. Twelve short passages, about eight minutes.
2. Check C1 and C2 against your own knowledge of a campaign you know: is there an independent corruption record, and did a differing closure enter? About five minutes.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a genuine reduction from a factional removal under the same arrest count, or say why none exists. About five minutes.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
