## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name is a gap. A measurement singles out an observable through coupling to an external frame, not by reading the state. Basis equivalence is identity over descriptions, not physical preference, and the false inference to "no observable selected" is the term to refuse. What is selected is per system and per coupling, stable and redundantly recorded, and the outcome is the only anchor. The bundle must be split: whether any stable basis, which observable, and why one outcome need separate loops. The figure set returns the kind of thing, not the observable's name. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Decoherence selects a stable set but does not, by itself, deliver a definite outcome; that live strain is recorded, not smoothed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework interior before their stage-1 verdict. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. A defective name, if one is proposed, is removable without touching the derivation; the shape claim can be narrowed to "depends on coupling" without loss. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument discriminating candidate pointer observables and their redundancy, a refutation 1 or 2 firing, or a figure re-census that permits naming a basis from the substrate alone would reverse the verdict. Until one fires, it stands as `candidate`.
