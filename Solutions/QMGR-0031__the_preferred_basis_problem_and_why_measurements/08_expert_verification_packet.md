# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the preferred basis problem

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement here names a preferred observable. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The observable a measurement singles out is not readable off the quantum state. It is a bond-level fact supplied by coupling to an external frame, selected from a frame whose closure differs from the closed unitary formalism, and settled only by the recorded outcome. Bases are equivalent as descriptions of one state, which is identity (two names, one referent), not physical preference, so that equivalence cannot be used to deny that a measurement selects an observable. What is selected is per system and per coupling: stable under the interaction, robust, and redundantly recorded. The figure set returns that shape, not the name of the observable. The name is a gap that awaits a ground reading.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 6

- **Statement:** the basis is a bond-level, contact-selected fact, not an ingredient of the state.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a rule that recovers the measurement basis from the state vector alone, with no coupling, apparatus or environment. If that succeeds, C1 falls.
- **Mode:** `general` structural, `specified` in application.

### C2. Framework-internal; checkable by reading the Operators table and Figure 1

- **Statement:** basis equivalence is identity over descriptions, not a production relation over observables, and the selection is per system, not global.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :25 "two names, one referent"; Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Refuter:** show that two basis descriptions of one state yield different physical predictions under an identical coupling (then they are isomers, not identities), or exhibit a single basis preferred for all systems under all couplings.
- **Mode:** `general` for the identity rule, `specified` for its application here.

### C3. Framework-internal plus physics-facing; checkable against the measurement chain

- **Statement:** the decisive dependency link is the move from suppressed interference to a definite recorded outcome; that link is under-instrumented, so starting the answer at decoherence is working above the break. The only anchor is the outcome.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check against the physics:** the SEP decoherence entry states that decoherence explains why we do not observe superpositions of outcomes but "does not explain why we do observe measurement results in the first place." Is that still the consensus? If a within-formalism account of definite outcomes now exists with no added interpretation, collapse or record, this sub-claim fails.
- **Refuter:** an instrument at that link resolving outcomes without added structure, or a derivation from unitary dynamics alone yielding unique outcomes.
- **Mode:** `specified`; the figure rules used are `general`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that position, or any observable, is *the* preferred basis by framework decree. A name is an import with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8).
- Not that decoherence alone solves the measurement problem.
- Not a new primitive, not a seventh term, not a derivation of one basis from the Hamiltonian alone.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` here.
- Not a verdict from agreement. Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (before the conclusion)

1. One system, identical preparation and coupling, returning definite outcomes in two incompatible bases.
2. A derivation inside unitary quantum mechanics alone, with no environment, apparatus, cut or added record, yielding a unique preferred basis and definite outcomes.
3. A rule recovering the measurement basis from the state vector alone, with no external contact.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed and the retraction is logged in `01` section 7.

## 6. What would settle it

An instrument that discriminates among candidate pointer observables and their redundancy in the environment, plus the recorded outcome. Not a further interpretive argument. The three refuters in section 4 double as the ground tests.
