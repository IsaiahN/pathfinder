## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

*The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0032 the shape is not a molecule name. It is this: whatever formed had to be a bonded arrangement rather than an ingredient list, had to be reachable from plausible feedstocks, had to have its order supplied by a habitat rather than an author, and had to survive long enough to be composed over. `SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's shape; the analogue here is `FEEDSTOCKS → (bonded intermediates) → ACTIVATED NUCLEOTIDE, with the arrow supplied by the habitat`. Naming the Sutherland route, the Becker route, or the formamide route as *the* answer fills a slot the framework leaves open.

A shape constrains. It also refuses. The flat-formula reading is refused before any chemistry is cited (Fig 12 :5).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the target, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* And: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."*

Applied here, the starting vocabulary is deliberately plain: sugar, base, phosphate, bond, order, setting, survival. The new terms acquired from the world sources are amino-oxazole, anhydronucleoside, 2′,3′-cyclic phosphate, wet-dry cycling, autocatalytic formose, environmental filtering. Each narrows the search. The question-as-tether is the residual of §0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, which is why the chemical frames were named only after the six slots were enumerated.

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the same rule in effect terms: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

*The Library of Babel* also names the union diagnosis directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :55 applied to this question. The bundle is reach plus contact plus the historical fact.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things only, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For ORI-0032 that means an experiment that runs the geochemical sequence without lab-only aids, or a discriminating signature in the rock record.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are imports of this kind: each has a URL, an author, a venue, a year and a quote, so the record of where it came from is preserved.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discrimination rule. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* Several routes reach ribonucleotides. Reading the product and naming one route as its source is the same metamerism error. The remedy is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a better argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This does not forbid naming a route as a candidate. It forbids naming a route as the ground's verdict.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

For ORI-0032 the anchor is Fig 2 :29's *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* A consensus statement about the RNA world is not one of these. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

The source problem has a specific form here. The thing being explained, a Hadean reaction network, is not available to inspection. What is available are projections: laboratory products, isotopic readings, meteoritic organics, mineral associations. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* So the honest report is that the historical route is `unreached`, with the instrument gap named, not that it was or was not the Sutherland route.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does a satisfactory answer to ORI-0032 have?**

1. It is per slot, not one averaged verdict (Fig 1 :19).
2. It treats the nucleotide as a bonded arrangement, so the bond and the anomer and the phosphorylation site are part of the answer, not footnotes (Fig 12 :5, :94).
3. It keeps reach and habitat contact as two conditions with two grounds (Fig 9 :55, :57).
4. It treats the geochemical setting as an enumerated habitat whose cycling supplies the order (Fig 11 :43, :46, :48).
5. It refuses to convert a laboratory success into a historical claim (Fig 11 :70).
6. It treats the absence of a found route as non-proof (Fig 8 :39) and the missing historical ground as a declared verdict (Fig 13 :134).
7. It records provenance for every imported world source, because convergence and adoption look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :51).

**Does the record commit a named error?** A naive answer does: it answers a `ground` question from a `general` shape and calls the result yes. The correct answer keeps the modes apart. The shape is `general` and `specified`; the historical fact is `candidate` and, with present instruments, `unreached`.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A famous reviewer's agreement about the RNA world is a budget, not a verdict. Contact should be sought with ground-visible people working in a different closure, through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any prebiotic chemistry claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Effect terms travel; interiors do not.** Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The outreach material therefore leads with the residual and the refuter, in the plain vocabulary of slots and conditions, and offers the figures only after a verdict is recorded.
