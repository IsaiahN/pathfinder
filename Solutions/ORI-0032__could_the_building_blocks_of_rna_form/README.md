## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that RNA building blocks formed naturally.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not become an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 2 :29 names the legitimate anchor: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Showing the interior before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public journal submission portals, society contact forms and grant solicitations are opt-in routes and are used instead.

## The honest stop in one sentence

The figures decide what shape an answer must have and what would settle it, not whether a specific Hadean reaction network ran: the "building blocks" framing is the flat-formula collapse named in Fig 12, the lab-to-habitat gap is a union of two conditions, and the historical verdict stays `candidate` until an instrument returns a reading from the ground.
