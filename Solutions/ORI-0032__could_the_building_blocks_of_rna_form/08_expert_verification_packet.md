# Expert verification packet: the Tether on RNA's building blocks

**What this is:** a claim in a form a prebiotic chemist or origin-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the composition rule, NOT `grounded`.** No Hadean measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. ## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "could RNA's building blocks form naturally under realistic early-Earth conditions?" is a bundle of six slots and a union of two conditions: reach from plausible feedstocks, and contact with a habitat that supplies concentration and cycling. It is not one hard question. The phrase "building blocks" is the flat-formula reading: a nucleotide is not ribose plus a base plus phosphate, but a bonded arrangement in which the glycosidic bond, the anomer and the cyclic phosphate carry the identity. Laboratory routes succeed by composing the molecule through bonded intermediates and bypassing free ribose and the free nucleobases, but they prove wiring, not contact with the ground. On the early Earth the assembly order would have to be supplied by cycling conditions, not by an author. The framework returns the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not say whether any of this actually happened.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 9

- **Statement:** the ingredient-list picture of a nucleotide is the flat-formula collapse; the bond carries the identity; and the reach/contact question is a union requiring separate loops.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **What to check:** do those four lines say what the packet claims they say? Is the binding to a nucleotide a fair application, or a strained one?
- **Refuter:** show that the sugar-base conjunction is symmetric under the figure bond set, so that ingredient order carries no information; or show that reach and habitat contact are the same variable, so the union diagnosis fails.

### C2 — Chemistry-facing, checkable by a prebiotic chemist against the published record

- **Statement:** the successful laboratory routes reach activated pyrimidine ribonucleotides by bypassing free ribose and the free nucleobases, while the direct ribosylation of pyrimidines does not work under prebiotically plausible conditions; and a laboratory route does not by itself establish geochemical plausibility.
- **What to check:** Powner, Gerland and Sutherland, Nature 2009: the abstract states *"it is far from obvious how such ribonucleotides could have formed from their constituent parts (ribose and nucleobases)"* and that the route *"bypasses free ribose and the nucleobases"*. Sutherland 2010: *"it is premature to conclude that the newly discovered self-assembly route to activated pyrimidine nucleotides (Powner et al. 2009) is geochemically plausible, the signs are looking good!"* and the route as operated has different conditions at different steps with purification between them. Becker et al. 2019 report concurrent purine and pyrimidine nucleoside formation under wet-dry cycles; Yadav, Kumar and Krishnamurthy 2020 caution that *"there are-and will continue to be-many more questions than answers"*. Are those characterisations faithful? Has the field moved past them?
- **Refuter:** a single-set-of-conditions, no-purification one-pot experiment yielding all four canonical activated ribonucleotides at polymerisation-ready concentration and purity under independently established early-Earth conditions. This would collapse the second condition of the union and move the historical verdict toward `grounded`.

### C3 — Framework-internal derivation about the habitat, checkable against Fig 11 and the geochemical record

- **Statement:** a laboratory synthesis is a substituted habitat and proves wiring, not capability; the geochemical setting is an enumerated habitat whose cycling supplies the assembly order; and the formose cycle's sign decides whether carbon reaches a productive intermediate or an inert tar.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :46 *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."*
- **What to check:** does the habitat enumeration in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` read conditions off the world rather than compose them? Is the phosphate catalyst-to-constituent role transition (Powner et al. 2009: phosphate incorporated late but *"its presence from the start is essential"*) recorded rather than smoothed over?
- **Refuter:** evidence that self-assembly is invariant across habitat substitution, so C2 is not a separate condition; or a demonstration that productive and tar-forming branches of formose chemistry have the same loop sign, so cycling does not discriminate.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **One-pot geochemical test:** all four activated ribonucleotides, one set of conditions, no purification, polymerisation-ready concentration and purity, conditions independently established as early-Earth plausible.
2. **Habitat-substitution test:** product identity tracks pH, wet-dry and UV regimes, or demonstrably does not.
3. **Source-discriminating ground test:** a mineral or isotopic signature diagnostic of a specific proposed intermediate, or its demonstrable absence where it should be detectable.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** symmetric sugar-base conjunction; collapse of the union; habitat-invariance of self-assembly; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground.

Until one of these fires, the verdict is `candidate` and the specific Hadean route is a declared `gap`.
