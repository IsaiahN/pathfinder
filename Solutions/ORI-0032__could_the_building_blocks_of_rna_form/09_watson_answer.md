## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a yes or no chemistry verdict is refused. A nucleotide is a bonded arrangement, not an ingredient multiset, so the "building blocks" framing is the flat-formula collapse named in Fig 12. The question is a union of reach and habitat contact, and the two conditions have separate grounds; laboratory success proves wiring, not contact. The habitat is enumerated from the world and supplies the order through cycling, and the formose cycle's sign decides whether carbon reaches a productive intermediate or an inert tar. No route found is not proof of no route, and the specific Hadean route stays a declared `gap` until an instrument or a geochemical sequence returns a reading. Convergent lab routes are corroboration only with provenance. Mode `specified` on these claims, `general` on the composition rule, nothing `grounded`. The per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged: D3 triangulated, D1 triangulated and candidate, D2 triangulated and candidate, D4 `gap`/candidate, convergence triangulated for shape and single-source for independence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded but real. A wrong yes sends research effort toward a route that never ran; a wrong no closes a live line. The figure-grounded cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* This is avoidable by leading with the shape and its four refuters (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the six slots and the refuters are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A one-pot geochemical sequence yielding all four activated ribonucleotides under independently established early-Earth conditions would collapse the second condition and move D1/D4 toward `grounded`; a habitat-substitution experiment showing invariance would dissolve the union; a source-discriminating mineral or isotopic signature would name or exclude a route; a symmetric sugar-base conjunction under the figure bond set would break the flat-formula finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
