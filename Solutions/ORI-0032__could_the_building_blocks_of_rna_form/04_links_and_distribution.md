## What in the wider literature bears on the residual

1. **The flat-formula failure is a finding, not a metaphor.** Sutherland 2010 writes that ribonucleotides *"undoubtedly consist of these three building blocks"* to the eye, and that decades of work assumed assembly from ribose, nucleobases and phosphate. The one step that fails is the joining of ribose to the nucleobase; for pyrimidines the direct reaction *"does not work at all."* This is Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information"* in the voice of the chemist.
2. **The lab-to-habitat gap is named explicitly.** Sutherland 2010: different conditions at different steps, purification between steps, and *"clearly, these issues need to be addressed before the synthesis can be seen as geochemically plausible."* This is Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat"* without the framework vocabulary.
3. **The union is visible from inside the field.** Hud and Fialho: separate purine and pyrimidine syntheses existed, but *"no single geochemical scenario has generated both."* Reach alone was not contact.
4. **Messiness is both obstacle and opportunity.** Vázquez-Salazar 2026 lists side reactions, inert tars and low yields, but also redundant routes, environmental filters and byproducts that re-enter productive cycles. This is the enumerated habitat of Fig 11, with valence relative to the residual.
5. **Convergence needs provenance.** The 2009 and 2019 routes reach related products by different chemistry; the 2023 formose work ties one route to an autocatalytic sugar network. Fig 8 :51 says convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents, so the record of where each came from is the whole issue.
6. **Low-overlap corroboration of the shape only.** Polger 2024 and Sober 1999 (philosophy of mind and philosophy of science) describe the same shape as Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* They corroborate the shape, not the chemistry, and carry no weight for which Hadean route ran.
