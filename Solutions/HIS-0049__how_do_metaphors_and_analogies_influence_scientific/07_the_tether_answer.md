# The Tether's answer: how metaphors and analogies influence scientific classification and explanation

**Mode: `general` on the operation, `specified` on the domain predictions, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Scientists visibly re-sort kinds and change explanations when they carry a structure from a familiar domain onto an unfamiliar one. The operative question is what the influence is and what settles it. Kept as four domains with separate verdicts, never averaged: D1 the import operation, D2 classification, D3 explanation, D4 the social record. D1, D2 and D3 are union compounds with their own loops; D4 has no anchor of its own (Fig 9 :54-58, Fig 2 :29-30).

## 2. The verdicts, per domain

**D1, the operation (`general`).** A metaphor or analogy is an import: contact with a frame whose closure differs. Fig 6 :11-14: "IMPORT", "source gate: provenance", "shadow test: explains a gap", "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Therefore an analogy cannot change a science by rearranging what that science already holds; it changes what is reachable. And the order is fixed: Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"; Fig 8 :27-28, "1: describe R, then find the frame that already predicts its shape". Pick the frame first and the fit is manufactured. **Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 9, low overlap between the closure argument, the union-surplus argument and the leave/arrive argument).

**D2, classification (`specified`).** The influence is on the bond, not the ingredient list. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." A metaphor imports a way of seeing the bond, which splits one apparent kind into two or merges two into one. The clean witness is literal: Fig 12 :97-98, "This is not an analogy. Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced structure into the notation." **Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 12 + Fig 13 + independent isomer history; all three agree the arrangement, not the label, decided the kind).

**D3, explanation (`specified`).** What transfers is a system of relations, not a list of attributes. Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." The figures license the import and the shape limit (Fig 8 :27-28, Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself") but do **not** contain a relations-versus-attributes claim; that comes from the world source (Gentner 1983) and is marked secondary. Two guards govern this domain. Fig 11 :38: "That is not a metaphor for intelligence. It is a description of the practice." Read globally this would forbid metaphor, which contradicts D1; by the self-consistency rule it governs the habitat description only, so an explanation earns standing by describing the practice that contacts the ground, not by being vivid. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A metaphor that only improves retellability improves pedagogy, a different domain with a different currency. **Verdict: triangulated for the import and shape limit; single-source (world) for relations-not-attributes.**

**D4, the social record (no verdict).** Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." A memorable metaphor repeated by a community is contact, not evidence. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." The effect is real; its verdict weight is zero. **Verdict: no anchor, so no verdict.** This is a finding, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a metaphor is the mechanism. An import adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :28).
- Not that any particular metaphor is thereby true. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that Fig 11 :38 bans metaphor, or that Fig 12 :97 bans analogy. Both global readings were retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6; each governs a specific object in another domain.
- Not that the psychological mechanism (why relations map more easily than attributes) is figure-derived. It is a declared gap, filled only by a secondary source.
- Not grounded. No measurement in this derivation settles any of the four verdicts.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

The pre-registered refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, written before this conclusion. The corresponding ground tests are:

1. A case where an import adds a primitive to the world, or moves a wall without contact with a different frame. Breaks D1.
2. A case where a community re-sorts a kind with unchanged bond and unchanged behaviour, only a new label, and the ground pays for it. Breaks D2. This is the instrument the colour-metamerism argument calls for: an observation that discriminates arrangement from label the way the spectrometer discriminated source from reading.
3. A case where an analogy's explanatory influence survives with zero shared relational structure, only attribute overlap, and the ground settles it. Breaks D3 and the Gentner corroboration.
4. A case where agreement about a metaphor, with no new measurement and no new source gate, changes the ground's answer to a classification question. Breaks D4 and the anchor definition at Fig 2 :29.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Fig 3 :24-27 names the weak link: the place where a frame has terms for what a situation affords has no instrument of its own here. "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The derivation stops there and records the lineage. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that metaphor is the mechanism of scientific change, and it does not claim any particular analogy is true. It says that an analogy is an import from a frame whose closure differs, licensed only by provenance and by a shadow test that asks whether it explains the gap it was brought in for, and that the residual must be described before the frame is chosen or any frame will seem to fit. On classification, the influence runs through the bond rather than the ingredient list: same atoms, different arrangement, different kind, which is what the 1820s isomer cases actually settled. On explanation, what transfers is a system of relations rather than surface attributes, and a comparison that changes no contact has improved pedagogy rather than explanation. On the social side, a memorable metaphor that a community repeats is agreement, not evidence, and agreement has no anchor. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer. What it offers is the shape any candidate account must have, four refuters written in advance, and the cases that would close it. The question stays open until the ground settles it.
