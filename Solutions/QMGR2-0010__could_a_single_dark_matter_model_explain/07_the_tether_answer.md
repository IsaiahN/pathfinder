# The Tether's answer to "could a single dark-matter model explain four observational slots?"

**Mode: `specified` on the per-slot verdicts and the shape, `general` on the structural rule, `gap` on which specific model is correct, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles four measurements into one predicate. Each has its own anchor that does not move when a model proposes an answer: rotation curves (galaxies), cluster masses from kinematics and X-ray and SZ (clusters), lensing convergence and image geometry (lensing), and CMB acoustic peaks plus BBN abundances (early universe). The framework's rule is that "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19) So the real residual is four indexed residuals over a dependency chain, a union rather than a partition (Fig 9 :54). The single-model ask is a reachability question about one closure Γ covering that union (Fig 6 :21, :23), not a request for more data.

## 2. The shape

1. **Four slots, not one.** Galaxies, clusters, lensing and the early universe must be stated as R_galaxy, R_cluster, R_lensing and R_early. An averaged "dark matter works / fails" throws away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.").
2. **Ordered by dependency.** The chain runs early universe first, then clusters and lensing, then galaxies (Fig 3 :20). The CMB fixes Ω_c h² and the initial perturbations; a galaxy-scale tune cannot rescue a CMB mismatch (Fig 3 :19).
3. **One closure, reach reported per slot.** Whether a single model reaches all four is a reachability question. "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." (Fig 6 :21) Where a slot fails, the remedy is an import from a frame whose closure differs, gated by provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14, Fig 8). More search at the same reach is a different question.
4. **No last room.** The unconditional "one final model explains everything" is refused. Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room"; Fig 13 :116-118 says "a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach." This is a structural limit, not an empirical denial. "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." (Fig 8 :39)
5. **A shape, not a name.** "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). No figure line selects ΛCDM, warm, self-interacting or unified-fluid dark matter. Any candidate must be stated as a closure with four named anchors, an import rule for the failing slot, and a refuter per slot. Which model is correct is a `gap`, recorded as a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134).
6. **Independent anchors, never agreement.** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). The Bullet Cluster lensing map and the CMB acoustic peaks are different instruments; agreement among models fit to the same dataset is one pool talking.
7. **Competing candidates are conditional isomers.** Over a shared atom set, cold, warm, self-interacting and unified-fluid models are Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." If no shared atom set can be written, the weaker functional-group claim stands (Fig 12 :123). The CMB molecule settles first and becomes an atom for structure formation (Fig 12 :104), with a lossy round trip that is owed, not assumed zero (Fig 4 :30).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that dark matter does not exist, or that no single model could ever work. The figures refuse a terminal all-explaining frame; they do not assert an empirical negative.
- Not that a specific particle, fluid or equation of state is selected. That would be a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that the small-scale tensions by themselves falsify a model. Baryonic physics is in contact with the galaxy residual, so that slot is a union of conditions (Fig 9 :55-56).
- Not that the four slots can be averaged into one verdict or one percentage of explanatory success.
- Not grounded. No measurement at any slot was taken here; every substantive claim is candidate.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at the failing slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. **Per-slot closure test:** at each slot, does the candidate's closure predict the observation against an anchor that does not update on the model? A residual that persists and does not trend down at one slot is where the refusal belongs (Formula step 2, opposite sign of the shrinking test).
2. **Upper-chain test:** does the candidate reproduce Ω_c h² and the acoustic peak structure (PDG: Ω_c h² = 0.1200 ± 0.0012; the acoustic scale is "the most precisely measured observable") before being tuned at galaxy scales? If not, Fig 3's break is at the upstream link.
3. **Single-fluid discriminator:** does a unified dark fluid produce outer galaxy and cluster profiles consistent with flat or falling rotation curves and NFW-like lensing profiles? El-Zant 2015 gives the current answer for a broad class of equations of state: it does not without fine-tuning the sound speed to extreme values.
4. **Cross-slot consistency:** do CMB secondary anisotropies and late-universe structure pull the model's clustered fraction the same way? The 2025 unified-sector study finds they do not, which is the dependency inversion made empirical.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** a closure settling all four slots at once; a slot with no anchor; a partition rather than a union; a figure line naming a model; a galaxies-first dependency order. Any one of these moves or refutes the verdict.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The candidate primitives (cold, collisionless, pressure-supported, self-interacting) are a visible guess from the physics frame, and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible" (*They're Scanning Books*, carried). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." One live strain remains: the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim (`06` Q1, `01` S2). It is named, not hidden.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a dark-matter particle and does not answer yes or no to a bundled question. It says the four phenomena are four separate residuals with four separate anchors, ordered from the early universe down to galaxies, so a single closure's reach has to be reported slot by slot rather than averaged. It says that whether one model reaches all four is a reachability question: where a slot fails, something must be imported from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test. It refuses the unconditional all-explaining model as a last room, while refusing also to turn a null result into a universal "no." What it returns is the shape any candidate must have, a set of refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each slot. Which model occupies the shape stays open until the ground settles it.
