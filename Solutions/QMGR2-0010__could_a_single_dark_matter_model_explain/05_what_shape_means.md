## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "one closure that reaches four named slots with four named anchors and a refuter per slot" is a shape. "The answer is a 100 GeV WIMP with a velocity-independent cross section" or "the answer is a generalized Chaplygin gas with α = 0.0001" is a name.

The distinction is not academic here. El-Zant 2015 shows that a single unified fluid's equation of state is constrained to α ≤ 0.0001 or α ≥ 2 by galaxy and cluster profiles, and even tighter by other requirements. That is a name with a number, purchased by observation. The figures do not produce it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: describe the residual at each slot in effect terms first (a velocity that does not fall as expected, a lensing mass displaced from the gas, a third acoustic peak height, a light-element abundance). Only then look for the frame whose closure predicts that shape. Fig 9 :53: "Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description." If you pick CDM or a Chaplygin gas first and then map the slots onto it, any slot will seem to fit, which is the apophenia the figure warns against.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For dark matter this means the candidate's primitives (cold, collisionless, pressure-supported, self-interacting) are a visible guess from the physics frame, not a discovery of the framework's six-term set. Naming one as *the* answer to the single-model slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. The 2025 unified-sector result that CMB and large-scale structure pull the clustered fraction in opposite directions is a ground reading that discriminates; the framework itself does not.

## 4. Why a single name from a single reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The Bullet Cluster is the analogue in this question's own field. A lensing convergence map reads mass, and mass alone does not say dark matter versus modified gravity versus a modified fluid. The Clowe et al. 2006 separation of lensing mass from X-ray gas is powerful precisely because it is a source-discriminating reading: it rules out a large class of "mass follows the gas" alternatives. But it does not by itself name which particle or fluid. The 2014 MOND incommensurability literature (referenced in the search results) argues that modified-gravity analyses can reproduce aspects of the offset, which is a dispute about what the reading discriminates, not a defeat of the anchor. What is owed at any slot is a better discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Also, directly: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any candidate at all, including candidates whose atoms are already in the physics frame's library. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. ## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that one model works is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried at each slot, and let the instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any dark-matter model and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
