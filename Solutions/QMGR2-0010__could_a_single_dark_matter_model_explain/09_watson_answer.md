## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: four slots (galaxies, clusters, lensing, early universe), so a union of four conditions, not one residual. Symmetry: asymmetric across slots, since the anchors are different instruments and the single-model ask is an unlicensed symmetry demand across scale. Scale: roughly kpc to Gpc, redshift 0 to 1100, with the early universe upstream. Fig 1 indexes the residual per slot and Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling a union.
- **D1 galaxies.** Actors: stars, gas, dark halo. Currency: rotation velocity and inner density slope. Anchor: measured rotation curves. Per-slot residual, union with baryon physics.
- **D2 clusters.** Actors: galaxies, intracluster medium, dark halo. Currency: mass, velocity dispersion, X-ray and Sunyaev-Zel'dovich signal. Anchor: cluster mass measurements. Per-slot residual.
- **D3 lensing.** Actors: source galaxies, lens mass distribution. Currency: convergence, shear, image positions. Anchor: observed lensing maps, for example the Bullet Cluster mass-gas separation. Geometry-direct, cross-cutting anchor.
- **D4 early universe.** Actors: photons, baryons, dark matter, neutrinos. Currency: acoustic peak locations and heights, Ω_c h², light abundances. Anchor: Planck CMB spectrum and BBN abundances. Upstream slot.
- **D5 the model closure.** Actors: the model's primitives and bonds, a gravitating component with equation of state, sound speed, interaction and phase-space distribution. Currency: predictions of structure across scale. Anchor: the four observations above. This is where the single-model claim lives.
- **D6 verification and outreach.** Actors: external checkers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Partition or union: union. D1 and D2 overlap through cluster galaxy populations and baryon physics; D3 cross-cuts D2 as an independent mass anchor; D4 sets the density inherited by all downstream slots. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Order by dependency: D4, then D2 and D3, then D1, then D5 and D6. A break at the upstream link cannot be rescued by tuning the downstream end (Fig 3 :19).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the question's aggregation and the strong terminal-model reading do not. Four indexed residuals on a dependency chain running early universe first and galaxies last, each with a non-updating anchor, reach reported per slot, an import with provenance and a shadow test where a slot fails, and refuters written in advance. The unconditional one-final-model answer is refused as a last room, while a null at one slot is not converted into a universal no. Which specific dark-matter model is correct is a `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name and unreachable-at-this-budget kind. Competing candidates are conditional isomers over a shared atom set, with a functional-group fallback and one live strain. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the four-slot indexing rule, because it is the repair the question itself requires.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The corrected packet states its mode, four slots, dependency order and per-slot refuters before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an averaged verdict or a named model instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregation is removable without touching the derivation: `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The gap on which model stays open rather than being filled with a guess.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A closure settling all four per-slot residuals at once against independent anchors with no unaccounted import would break the union and reachability verdict. A figure line naming a specific model would retract the gap. A demonstrated galaxies-first dependency order would reverse the chain direction. A slot found without a nameable anchor would invalidate that domain. An outside refutation with provenance is copied into `11_audit.md` and re-evaluated by the auditor seat like any other source. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
