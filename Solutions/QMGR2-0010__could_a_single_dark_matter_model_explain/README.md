## The question restated as an effect

A "single dark-matter model" is a claim that one frame, one library Γ with its atoms and bonds, settles the residual at four observational slots at once: galaxies, clusters, gravitational lensing, and the early universe (CMB and BBN). Each slot has a different instrument and a different non-updating anchor. The question asks whether one closure reaches all four. Saying yes commits to a frame whose closure covers a union of four differently anchored slots across a dependency chain running from the early universe down to galaxies. Saying no commits to per-slot verdicts and, where one slot fails, an import from a frame whose closure differs. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a shape with four named slots, four named anchors, a dependency order, a refuter per slot, and an explicit record of which slot is unreached, rather than a name of a particle or a fluid.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures name a dark-matter particle, a fluid, or a model. They return a shape.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42) The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
