## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a discrimination between two hypotheses over one observed act. Arity: two hypotheses, one act. Symmetry: the map from latent source to act is many-to-one, so from the act the two are interchangeable. Scale: individual signal and belief -> network of observation ties -> aggregate adoption record. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* This makes it a slot-indexed discrimination, not a population statistic.
- **Domain 1, the act and its record.** Actors: individuals as slots, acts, timestamps, public signals. Currency: observed choice. Anchor: the record, legitimate for *what happened* and an instrument for *why*. Question type: observation of an artifact.
- **Domain 2, the private source.** Actors: private signal, belief, preference. Currency: conviction. Anchor: private, stake-reduced self-report, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people. Question type: source attribution.
- **Domain 3, the diffusion network.** Actors: early movers, followers, observation ties. Currency: social information. Anchor: an intervention that severs the tie. Question type: contact cascade, habitat to be enumerated.
- **Domain 4, the checking arrangement.** Actors: researcher and outside reader. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Union, not partition.** Three bundled conditions: identification in principle, field measurement, definition of genuine. One lever cannot clear all three.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> instrument -> carry. The suspected break is at instrument in field settings.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 8 and Fig 13, I derived from Fig 1's slot rule and Fig 9's union rule.
