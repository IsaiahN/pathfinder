# The Tether's answer: cascades versus genuine belief change

**Mode: `specified` on the two-part verdict, `general` on the structural claim that a source cannot be read off an artifact. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and are never averaged into a single "partly."

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is a union: identification in principle, field measurement, and what counts as "genuine" (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Stated in effect terms: for one person at one decision, the observed act is the same under "I followed the private signal after it changed" and under "I ignored my private signal and copied." The live residual is in the unread source. Per slot, not per crowd: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

## 2. The two-part verdict

**Part one: NO, not from the act or the aggregate alone.**

1. A cascade and a genuine change are identical in the observed content. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*; Fig 13 :127-128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*
2. Crowd agreement is not evidence of truth when the crowd shares an evidence pool, which a cascade does by definition. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
3. Aggregate series hide the per-person signal. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
4. In the field, the chain breaks at the instrument link. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**Part two: YES, where a source record or instrument exists.**

1. The anchor for "genuine belief" is the person, because the question is constitutively about people. Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37-38: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
2. The record of provenance is the separator. Fig 8 :51 again, and Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
3. The laboratory has a working proto-instrument: belief elicitation plus controlled private signals (Celen and Kariv 2004). Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
4. The two hypotheses are isomers of the act, so the difference is real and behaviorally decisive even when the notation cannot show it. Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a cascade and a genuine belief are the same state. Indiscernibility is not identity. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
- Not that any named statistic (fragility, unanimity, serial correlation) is the discriminator. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that the laboratory result settles the field question. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not that self-report is a clean anchor under an audience. Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
- Not `grounded`. No field discriminator was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not more argument. Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."* Concretely:

1. **Signal-randomisation test:** vary the private signal while holding the public history fixed. A source instrument must move with the private signal under H_g and not under H_c.
2. **Private, stake-reduced elicitation:** record what the person believed before and after, with the audience removed and the incentive declared. Kill criterion: elicited belief fails to track the manipulated private signal.
3. **Convergence-at-independence test:** show whether the apparent unanimity came from independent private signals (then it is corroboration, Fig 8 :50) or from a shared pool.
4. **Habitat enumeration:** list actors, conditions and relations outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :43-44), including second-order contact.
5. **Standing refuters** from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* The laboratory reaches the distinction in a substituted habitat; the field version is unmeasured here. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a flat yes or no. It says that an information cascade and a genuine change of belief are identical in the observed act, because a cascade is defined by an act that ignores the private signal and a genuine change by one that follows it, and both produce the same choice. So from behavior or an adoption curve alone you cannot tell them apart, and a crowd's agreement is not evidence when the crowd drew on the same pool. You can tell them apart where you hold the source: a private, stake-reduced record of what the person believed, or an intervention that randomises the private signal while holding the public history fixed. The laboratory already does this for belief elicitation, which proves the mechanism is distinguishable in a substituted habitat, not that it is measured in the field. What the derivation offers is the shape of that discriminator, the three questions the bundle must be split into, and refuters written in advance. The center stays open until a ground settles it.
