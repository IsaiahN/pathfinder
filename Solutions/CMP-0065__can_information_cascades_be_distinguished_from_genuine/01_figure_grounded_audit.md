## 0. The question in effect terms

> Can information cascades be distinguished from genuine changes in people's beliefs or preferences?

Stated as a residual in effect terms:

- **Option A:** Yes, from the observed sequence of acts and aggregate patterns alone. A cascade leaves a signature (fragility, excess unanimity, serial correlation) that a genuine belief change does not.
- **Option B:** No, not from the act or the aggregate alone. The two hypotheses map to the same observable act; a discriminator requires access to the **source** of the act (private signal versus imitation) or an intervention that breaks the shared evidence pool. Where neither is available, the honest report is *unreached / unreachable at this budget*, not a verdict of identity and not silence.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula step "Direction"): a **source-discriminating instrument**, defined as a procedure whose output differs under the two hypotheses at fixed observed act, together with the conditions under which it can be deployed. If that term cannot be bound, the residual stays open and is reported as such. The preference for a yes/no answer is held as a preference, not as a term, and is therefore not allowed to decide (Fig 5 :62: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."*).

**R, stated as an effect.** For a slot s (one person at one decision), let o' be the observed act. Under H_c (cascade) the act does not depend on that person's private signal; under H_g (genuine change) the act does depend on a changed private belief or preference. Both produce the same o'. Hence `R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` is near zero under both, and the live residual is in the unread source, not in the act. This is exactly the legal state named in Fig 1 :13, *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** two hypotheses over one observed act. The question asks for a discrimination, so the residual is a relation, not a single quantity.
- **Symmetry:** the map from latent source to observed act is many-to-one. From the act, H_c and H_g are symmetric (interchangeable). This is a structural property of the observation, not a lack of data. In Fig 2's mirror case both frames range against an anchor that does not update on either; the relevant point here is that no reading of the act alone separates the two sources.
- **Scale:** individual (private signal, belief, preference) -> network (who observes whom, in what order) -> aggregate (market, crowd, vote, adoption curve). Each scale has a different currency and needs its own loop.

### 1.2 One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the act and its record | individuals as slots, acts, timestamps, public signals | observed choice | the record of the act (a market, count, vote, adoption curve) | observation; anchor legitimate for *what happened*, not for *why* |
| D2, the private source | private signal, belief, preference of each person | conviction | the person's own private, stake-reduced judgment | constitutively about people, so the person is the legitimate anchor (Fig 2 :36) |
| D3, the diffusion network | early movers, followers, observation ties, timing | social information / imitation | an intervention that severs the tie (randomised information, serial private elicitation) | contact cascade; habitat to be enumerated, not composed |
| D4, the checking arrangement | researcher, experiment, outside reader | independence | the ground, never agreement | triangulation; verdict from a manipulated anchor, not from a crowd |

### 1.3 Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** The question bundles at least three conditions: (a) identification in principle, (b) measurement from existing field data, and (c) what is to count as "genuine" (a private belief, a stable preference, or merely a different behavioral disposition). Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A single lever that clears observational data will leave the measurement and definition conditions standing. Each needs its own loop, and the union needs its own loop too.

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. **Perception:** can we read the act, its timing, and the observation order? Usually yes in digital traces.
2. **Vocabulary:** do we have terms for private signal, imitation, belief change, source? Yes, the field has them (BHW 1992; Celen and Kariv 2004).
3. **Objective:** can we say what would count as progress here, in our own terms? Yes: a statistic or instrument that fires differently under H_c and H_g at fixed act.
4. **Planning:** can we design a route to it under incomplete information? In the laboratory, yes. In the field, often no.
5. **Learn and carry:** name, verify, and carry the discriminator to the next setting.

Fig 3 :25-26 names where such chains usually break: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The suspected break here is between 3 and 4 in field settings: the vocabulary and the objective exist, the deployable source-instrument usually does not.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The two hypotheses are identical in the observed content (decisive for D1)

Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*

Fig 13 :127-128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

A cascade and a genuine change of belief produce the same ingredients: the same person, the same act, the same time. What differs is the bond to the source, and that bond is exactly the half the record omits. BHW 1992 define a cascade by *"an individual's action does not depend on his private information signal"*; a genuine change is defined by the opposite dependence. Both definitions are about the signal, not about the act. So no function of the act alone can separate them without an extra premise about the signal.

**Refuter:** exhibit an act-level statistic S whose distribution under H_c and under H_g is provably disjoint at fixed observed act sequence, for all admissible private signals. If such an S exists, F1 fails and the distinction is available from behavior alone. Candidate signatures such as fragility after a shock are not counterexamples: they read a second observation (a later reversal), not the original act.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 8 and Fig 13, low overlap: one is about provenance between frames, the other about route recovery from an artifact).

### F2 - The legitimate anchor for "genuine belief" is the person, not the crowd (D2)

Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

Fig 2 :37-38: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

The question is constitutively about people's beliefs and preferences. So the person's own private judgment is the legitimate anchor for "genuine." A market, a vote count, or an adoption curve is only an **instrument** for this question, and instruments are wrong on axes they do not measure. They measure behavior; they do not measure the source of behavior. This is why "the crowd adopted it" cannot settle whether beliefs changed.

But the instrument that is legitimate in principle must be protected from the stake that comes with being observed. Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."* So the anchor must be elicited privately, without an audience, with the incentive and the declaration recorded.

**Refuter:** show that private elicitation fails to track the latent belief under experimental control (for example, elicited belief does not move when the private signal is manipulated, or it moves when only the public signal is manipulated). Then the person is not an anchor for this question and D2 must be re-censused.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 anchor-legitimacy rule and Fig 10 stake rule, different quantities).

### F3 - Cascade agreement carries no evidence about truth (D1 and D4)

Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."*

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

A cascade is defined by followers drawing on predecessors' acts. That is a shared evidence pool by construction. So large, fast, unanimous adoption is exactly the pattern that carries the least information about whether the underlying belief is correct or genuinely held. This does not mean unanimity is always a cascade: it means unanimity cannot be used as the discriminator.

**Refuter:** demonstrate convergent derivation at maximum independence, two or more people reaching the same act from private signals that were never exchanged (Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*). Then agreement is evidence.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 independence and Fig 7 room chain).

### F4 - The chain breaks at the source-instrument link in field settings (D3 and D4)

Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

The field has the vocabulary and the objective (section 1.4, links 2 and 3), but in most observational settings nothing is already returning a reading of the private source. There is no edge to extend from. In the laboratory there is: Celen and Kariv (2004) elicit beliefs and control private signals, which is precisely a proto-instrument already returning something. That is why the lab can distinguish what the field cannot.

Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* The laboratory result proves the mechanism can be made to fire. It does not prove that the same instrument works in the wild, because the habitat was substituted.

**Refuter:** a field-deployable instrument that reads private source without changing the act, shown to fire correctly under randomised information. If one exists and is validated, the break is repaired and the verdict moves.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 3 dependency rule and Fig 6 instrument rule; lab limit single-source from Fig 11).

### F5 - Where no discriminator exists, the report is unreached, not identity and not silence (all domains)

Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

From the inside of the behavioral frame, a cascade and a genuine belief change look the same. That is a statement about reach, not about the world: it does not say the two states are identical, only that this frame cannot tell them apart with what it holds. The correct output is a shape (what a discriminator would have to look like) plus the record of what is missing in effect terms. Silence and a forced yes/no are both failures here.

**Refuter:** any working discriminator, or a proof of observational equivalence strong enough to move the verdict from *unreached* to *underivable in principle*. These are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-41: *"underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts"*).

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 6, Fig 9, Fig 13).

### F6 - Same act, different bond: a real isomer claim (supports D1)

Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

Hold the act A fixed. The two molecules are `A joined by private signal` and `A joined by imitation`. Identical ingredient set, different bond, and the difference shows up downstream: one is robust to new information about the state of the world and the other is fragile, because a cascade once started lasts even if it is wrong (BHW 1992). This is the cleanest expression of why the question is not idle: the two are different substances even though the notation of observed behavior cannot tell them apart. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the full derivation and its falsifier.

**Refuter:** show that downstream behavior is provably identical under the two bonds for all states of the world. Then the bond carries nothing and the isomer claim is idle.

**Status:** `single-source` for the figure definition (Fig 12), with the behavioral difference corroborated by BHW 1992 and Celen and Kariv 2004 (low overlap with the figure text).

### F7 - The question must be unbundled before it is searched (all domains)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The three bundled conditions are identification in principle, field measurement, and the definition of "genuine." A lab result clears the first two under controlled conditions and leaves the third and the field version standing. This is why a flat yes/no answer misdescribes the state of the question.

**Refuter:** a re-census showing the three conditions are one condition, so a single loop closes them. Until then, keep them separate and never average their verdicts.

**Status:** `single-source` (Fig 9), corroborated by the Celen and Kariv (2004) result that the two cases need different apparatus.

### F8 - Aggregate data hides the per-person residual (D1)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*

A population adoption curve can be almost perfectly explained while individual slots carry live residuals: some people changed their beliefs, others copied, and the average washes the difference out. Any claimed discrimination based only on aggregate time series is measuring the wrong slot. The unit of the residual is the person at the decision, and the observation order must be preserved.

**Refuter:** an aggregate statistic that is a sufficient statistic for the source under both hypotheses. None is known; if one is proven, F8 fails.

**Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 1 slot rule and Fig 2 warning about pooled readings).

### F9 - The cascade's habitat must be enumerated (D3)

Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*

The habitat of a cascade is not just immediate neighbors: it is early movers, followers, visibility of acts, timing, and second-order contact. Enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering. Do not compose a list of "influencers" from a theory, because valence is relative to the residual: the same account is a benefactor for one question and a malfactor for another.

**Refuter:** a cascade whose spread is fully accounted for by direct ties, with no second-order contact effect. That would collapse the habitat to first order and simplify the instrument.

**Status:** `single-source` (Fig 11), with the animal-behaviour literature (McCormick et al. 2024) corroborating that transmission mechanism matters.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim in `07_the_tether_answer.md` is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An act-level statistic exists whose distributions under cascade and genuine change are provably disjoint at fixed act sequence (kills F1).
2. A field instrument reads private source without changing the act and validates against randomised information (repairs the break in F4).
3. The apparent unanimity is shown to be convergent derivation from independent private signals, so the agreement is corroboration rather than a shared pool (kills F3 for that case).
4. A re-census shows the question is a partition rather than a union, so one loop closes it (kills F7).
5. Downstream behavior is proven identical under the two bonds for all states, so the isomer distinction carries nothing (kills F6).
6. Private, stake-reduced elicitation fails to track the latent belief under experimental control (kills F2).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn in a visible correction, not silently softened. Until one is run, nothing above is labelled `grounded`.

1. Answer in two parts: **No** from the act or the aggregate alone (F1, F3, F8), and **Yes** where a source-discriminating instrument or provenance is available (F2, F4). Keep the verdicts per domain; never average them into a single "partly."
2. Ship the shape of the discriminator, not a claim that it is already built. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

4. Cite the lab result as a proof that the mechanism is distinguishable in a substituted habitat, and say plainly that the field version is unmeasured (F4, Fig 11 :70).

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the residual in the act?** No. Both hypotheses predict the same act (Fig 8 :51, F1).
2. **What is the anchor?** The person's private judgment, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-38); protected from stake (Fig 10 :59).
3. **Is crowd agreement evidence?** No when the crowd shares a pool; yes only under convergent derivation at independence (Fig 2 :31-32, Fig 7 :13).
4. **Which link breaks?** The source-instrument link in field settings (Fig 3 :27); the lab has a proto-instrument (Fig 6 :32).
5. **Does the lab result transfer?** The method does (Fig 4 :22); the result does not, because the habitat was substituted (Fig 11 :70). Measure R_T.
6. **Is the question one question?** No, a union of three conditions (Fig 9 :54).
7. **What is the stop?** A shape and a refuter, with *unreached* recorded rather than a forced verdict (Fig 13 :134-135).

### Updated verdict

The answer survives in two parts and stays `specified`. From the act and the aggregate alone the two hypotheses cannot be distinguished (F1, F3, F8). With a provenanced source or a source-discriminating instrument they can be distinguished, and the laboratory has already done so in a substituted habitat (F2, F4, F6). The question is a union that must be unbundled (F7). Nothing is `grounded`: no field discriminator was run in this derivation. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; the one live limitation, laboratory-to-field transfer (R_T), is named in `11_audit.md`.
