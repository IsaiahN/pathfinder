## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From The Library of Babel: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on the recursive narrowing: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For this question the shape is **the specification of a source-discriminating instrument**, not a named statistic and not a yes/no verdict. It says: any discriminator must output differently under "the act did not depend on the private signal" and "the act did depend on a changed private belief," at fixed observed act; it must not be derived from the act alone (Fig 8 :51); it must put the person in the anchor seat because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-38); and it must hold the two frames at low overlap so their agreement means something (Fig 2 :31-32). That is a shape. Naming "serial correlation" or "fragility" as *the* discriminator would be a name, and the essays forbid filling the slot from the searcher's preferred vocabulary.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: describe the effect, then find the frame

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, correlate it with recurring new terms, recurse. The question itself is the anchor: *"If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Decoding Tacit Knowledge: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* And the concrete instruction: *"what you would see, what would happen."*

Applied here: describe the discriminator in effect terms, "a reading that changes when the private signal is randomised and does not change when only the public signal is randomised," before naming any laboratory procedure. The Celen and Kariv apparatus then arrives as the frame that already predicts that shape, not as a frame picked first and mapped afterwards (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. Why the bundle must be split before the search

The Library of Babel essay names the exact failure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Fig 9 :54 says the same: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

This question bundles identification in principle, field measurement, and the definition of "genuine." A flat yes/no search over the bundle will keep landing on whichever subset has the instruments. Shape-first means the three are separated and given their own loops before any literature is picked.

## 4. The metamerism point is the core of this question

The Hard Problem is About Source: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The essay's resolution is instrumental, not argumentative: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

This maps directly. A green reading can come from one green light, from yellow plus blue, from RGB, from CMYK. An adopting act can come from a private belief that changed or from a person who ignored their private signal. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is the spectrometer equivalent for belief source, not a more elaborate theory of the act. The independent color-science source in `04_links_and_distribution.md` (Kerr, Stanford) confirms that many spectra give one color, so the analogy is not framework-internal.

**Overlap warning:** this essay is by the framework's author and shares its evidence pool with the figures. It is high-overlap corroboration, not an independent witness. The color-science source carries the independent witness.

## 5. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A source-discriminating instrument that fires correctly under randomised information would settle the field version.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The laboratory's belief-elicitation apparatus is such an import, and its provenance (Celen and Kariv 2004) is part of the claim, not decoration.

Until one of those, the name of a specific statistic is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 6. Why agreement and drift are the wrong instruments here

Decoding Tacit Knowledge: *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* Fig 2 :33-34: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

A cascade is almost the definition of two people checking against each other. So the more strongly a group converges, the less their convergence tells you about the source. This is why the answer cannot be "look at how many people agreed." The legitimate check is each frame corrected separately by an anchor that does not move when they push on it (Fig 2 :46), and private elicitation is the only available anchor for a question about belief.

## 7. Correction to my own earlier reading

An earlier draft of this file said "the framework returns a shape, not a name, so no statistical signature may ever be proposed." That overstates the rule. The canonical wording governs *"a missing thing"*: a primitive not held in the library. Here the relevant terms (private signal, belief, imitation, observation order) are already held by the field. What the framework forbids is filling an unmeasured slot with a name and calling it settled. It does not forbid proposing a candidate discriminator, provided the candidate is bound as an ordinary term with a residual and a refuter. This correction is kept visible; the earlier sentence is not silently erased.

## 8. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous cascade researchers assembled to agree with the shape claim would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a controlled ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
