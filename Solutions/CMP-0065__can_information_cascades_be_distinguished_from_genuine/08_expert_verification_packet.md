# Expert verification packet - the Tether on cascades versus genuine belief change

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in information cascades and social diffusion can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No field measurement is claimed. This is a shape of a discriminator and a two-part verdict, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of three conditions: identification in principle, field measurement, and what counts as "genuine." From the observed act and the aggregate adoption curve alone, a cascade and a genuine change of belief cannot be distinguished: both produce the same act, and what differs is the unobserved bond to a private signal. They can be distinguished where the source is recorded or an instrument reads it, which the laboratory does by eliciting beliefs under controlled private signals. So the answer is **no** from behavior alone and **yes** where a source record or instrument exists. The two are isomers of the act: same ingredients, different bond, decisive downstream behavior, invisible in the act itself. What is offered is the shape of the discriminator, the split of the bundle, and refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - observational equivalence at the act (checkable by reading Figures 8 and 13)

- **Statement:** from the observed act and its sequence, the two hypotheses are not separable. The separator is the record of provenance, not any statistic of the act.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 13 :127-128 - *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit an act-level statistic whose distributions under cascade and genuine change are provably disjoint at fixed observed act sequence, for all admissible private signals. Fragility after a later shock is not such a statistic, because it reads a second observation.

### C2 - the person is the anchor, and crowd agreement is not (checkable by reading Figure 2 and the cascade definition)

- **Statement:** because the question is constitutively about people's beliefs, the legitimate anchor is private, stake-reduced judgment. Agreement among people who draw on the same pool is disqualified as a verdict. A cascade is precisely a shared pool.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :36 - *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37-38 - *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 10 :59 - *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
- **What to check:** does the standard definition of an informational cascade indeed make the act independent of the private signal? BHW 1992: *"An informational cascade occurs if an individual's action does not depend on his private information signal."* If yes, followers share a pool by construction.
- **Refuter:** private elicitation that does not track the latent belief under experimental control, or a demonstration that the apparent agreement came from independent private signals (then it is corroboration at maximum independence, Fig 8 :50).

### C3 - the lab/field gap is a round trip with a measurable cost (checkable by reading Figures 6, 11 and 12)

- **Statement:** the laboratory has a proto-instrument, belief elicitation plus controlled signals, so the distinction is achievable in a substituted habitat. That does not establish the field case. The two hypotheses are isomers of the act, which is why the distinction is not idle.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 - *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** Celen and Kariv (2004) state *"By using techniques only available in the laboratory, we elicit subjects' beliefs. This allows us to distinguish informational cascades from herd behavior."* Does that apparatus transfer to field data without changing the act? This is the open R_T question, recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`.
- **Refuter:** a field-deployable source instrument validated against randomised information, or a proof that downstream behavior is identical under the two bonds for all states (which kills the isomer claim).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a flat yes or no; the bundle is split into three conditions with separate verdicts.
- Not that a named statistic is the discriminator. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that indiscernibility is identity. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
- Not that the laboratory result settles the field question.
- Not that self-report under an audience is a clean anchor.
- Not a derivation of any economic or psychological model from the figures. The figures govern the shape of the discriminator and the status of the evidence; the ground decides the fact.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Signal-randomisation test:** private signal varied, public history held fixed; does the candidate reading move as the belief account predicts?
2. **Private, stake-reduced elicitation:** record pre- and post-act belief without an audience, with incentive and stake declared.
3. **Convergence-at-independence test:** did the unanimity come from independent signals or a shared pool?
4. **Habitat enumeration:** list actors, conditions and relations outward to second-order contact.
5. Any of the six standing refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :51, Fig 13 :127-128, Fig 2 :36-38 and Fig 11 :70. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against the cascade definition you use: is the act independent of the private signal? If not, the main verdict needs a re-census.
3. Name one field instrument that would discriminate source at fixed act, or say why none is possible. A "none is possible" answer would move the verdict from *unreached* to *underivable in principle*, which is a different and important result.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record the verdict before reading any source, so the check stays independent.
