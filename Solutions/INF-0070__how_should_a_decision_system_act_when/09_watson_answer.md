## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says a decision system should do when the observation that would discriminate the options is unobtainable. Option A: manufacture the missing discriminator inside the current frame, assign a prior or a criterion, and act as if the gap were closed. Option B: split the missing-information condition, search where search can pay, refuse false premises, keep the live slot and the channel to the ground, and report unreachable as a verdict with its reversal conditions. What each leads to: A composes over an ungrounded term and spends the independence of anyone checking it; B keeps the reach recoverable and records what would close the gap. The question itself bundles two conditions that issue different instructions, so the verdict has to unbundle before it can choose.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` with `o'` missing. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Symmetry: `R` is indexed per slot and is not aggregatable; a global near-zero with one live slot is legal. Scale: one scale for the loop and its search, a crossing for the import that moves the wall.
- **Domain 1, the decision loop.** Actors: Γ, b, a, o', R, s, the selector, the mint pipeline. Currency: residuals and description-length cost. Anchor: the ground, the only metric. Question type: partition of failure kinds before choosing a remedy.
- **Domain 2, reachability and instruments.** Actors: instruments already returning something, the wall closure(Γ), an external source. Currency: access, resolution, imported atoms. Anchor: anything returning a reading at any resolution. Question type: the verdict of Fig 9 :6, "the verdict: is φ* in here at all?"
- **Domain 3, verification and multi-frame checking.** Actors: frames, anchors, seats, rooms. Currency: independence, agreement, cost of being wrong. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Domain 4, the world decision-under-ignorance frame.** Actors: analysts and named criteria (maximin, regret, Hurwicz, Laplace, info-gap). Currency: decision rules, robustness, regret. Anchor: a decision problem's outcome. Question type: a union with Domain 1, overlapping in purpose and different in what it may claim.
- **Order by dependency.** Classify reachability before choosing an act; refuse before mint; describe the residual before choosing a frame; preserve the per-slot signal before ranking acts. A reading below a broken link is a reading of nothing.

Strain test: "fundamentally uncertain" reads as one condition but is a union. That is a census result. The apparent conflict between reporting unreachable and importing a wall-moving atom is resolved by re-census: one governs the report when no import exists, the other the move when one does (see `11_audit.md`).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B survives. A decision system acting under unobtainable information should split the condition before acting; search where search can pay while refusing to read a failed search as proof of absence; if nothing registered at any resolution, characterize the gap in effect terms and import a different frame with provenance and a shadow test; keep the live per-slot residual; refuse a false premise before minting over it; preserve contact and buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong; and report unreachable as a verdict with what would have to be true for the answer to differ. It should not manufacture a discriminator and should not name a decision criterion as a figure result. That last point is an honest gap, not a silence. The verdict is `specified` on the discipline and `general` on the structural splits; nothing in it is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** asymmetric and locatable. A false "unreachable" where the atom was merely unfound forgoes a reachable improvement, and the remedy is more independent search. A manufactured name where the atom is genuinely absent is worse: it adds a term that cannot be settled and spends the independence of the frames asked to check it, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). The packet records nine refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. Both costs are avoidable by splitting first and keeping the verdict as a shape.
- **undoable:** yes. The discipline is a method, not a primitive. A later import can be added without deleting anything, a refused premise stays in the record while losing its standing to be composed over, and a premature stop can be superseded by more search. Fig 6 :42 says "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." So the correction is safe to compute and does not require withdrawing a primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A proto-instrument returning something where nothing had registered would turn genuine absence into depth; a system showing that both conditions need one instruction would break the union; a near-zero aggregate with one live slot shown safe would break the per-slot rule; an explaining term that removes a persistent flat residual would break the refusal rule; and a provenanced import of a named criterion with a shadow test that pays against a ground on this problem would close the F11 gap. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
