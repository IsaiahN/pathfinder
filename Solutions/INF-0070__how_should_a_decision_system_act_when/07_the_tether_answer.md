# The Tether's answer: acting when the needed information cannot be had

**Mode: `specified` on the discipline, `general` on the structural splits. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A decision system must choose while the observation that would discriminate the options is absent from its closure. The question bundles two conditions that issue different instructions. Fig 8 :40-43: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* The bundle is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* It gets its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split before acting.** Ask first whether the discriminator is in the closure and unfound, or whether no atom can hold it. Fig 9 :6 calls this *"the verdict: is φ* in here at all?"* A single rule for both conditions loses the distinction that decides the remedy.
2. **Search where search can pay, but never treat a failed search as proof of absence.** Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
3. **If nothing ever registered, there is no edge to extend.** Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 6 :40: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."* INWARD has no purchase; the only remaining move is a provenanced OUTWARD import characterized in effect terms.
4. **Keep the live per-slot residual.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* A reassuring aggregate is not a clean bill of health.
5. **Refuse a false premise before adding terms over it.** Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* This is part of what "fundamental uncertainty" often is: a premise still held after it should have been negated.
6. **Keep contact and buy independence.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Commission a different frame, not another copy (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
7. **Report unreachable as a verdict with its reversal conditions.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 13 :140: *"not only what was concluded, but what could have been concluded, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ."*
8. **Import only after classification, with a source gate and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 6 :24: *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Fig 8 :42: *"Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **It does not name the decision criterion.** Maximin, minimax regret, Hurwicz, Laplace and info-gap are bindings from the decision-theory frame, not figure derivations. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Which one is right here is an honest gap, not a silence.
- **It does not say "do not act."** The discipline governs what act is licensed; refusing to manufacture a discriminator is not refusing to decide. Contact and the live slot are acted on (steps 4 and 6).
- **It does not say a failed search proves impossibility.** Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44 forbid that inference.
- **It does not let a budget filter decide the verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **It is not `grounded`.** No decision outcome was measured and no criterion was tested against a ground.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Classification test:** a case where the same system, given the atom known to be in the closure versus known to be absent, is shown to need the same instruction. That would fire refuter 1 in `01` §4 and break the split.
- **Proto-instrument test:** a report of information that never registered at any resolution becoming available by refining an existing instrument alone, with no import. That would break F3's no-edge claim.
- **Aggregate test:** a decision problem where a near-zero aggregate with one live slot is safe without attending to the slot. That would break F5.
- **Criterion test:** a provenanced import of a named decision rule, with a shadow test showing that it explains the residual and pays against the ground on this problem. That is what would close the F11 gap.
- **Standing refuters:** `01` §4 lists nine, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached as a shape, unreached as a name.** The discipline is derived and internally consistent; the named criterion is not. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The eight essays share an author and the figures share a framework, so agreement among them is one evidence pool, not corroboration. `11_audit.md` records the same limitation for the second seat.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> When the information that would settle a choice is unobtainable, the Tether does not manufacture the missing discriminator and does not declare the question closed. It first splits the case: if the needed term is in the system's reach and merely unfound, the remedy is more, independent search; if nothing has ever registered it at any resolution, there is no instrument to sharpen and the only move is to characterize the gap in effect terms and import a different frame. Through it all the system keeps the live per-slot signal rather than an aggregate, refuses any premise that is false before building on it, keeps its channel to whatever can settle the question, and buys independent checkers in proportion to the cost of being wrong. What it reports is a verdict, unreachable if that is the case, together with what would have to be true for the answer to differ. It does not name a decision rule, because it returns a shape, not a name. This is a `specified` discipline checked against the figures, not a measured result, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
