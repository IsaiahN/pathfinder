## Wider literature that bears on this question

Each entry below was opened. Full provenance and quotes are in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Economics, Knight 1921.** The unmeasurable case is categorically different from measurable risk: no group of instances can be formed. Predicts that "unobtainable" must be split before an act is chosen. Corroborates F1. Independent of the framework.
2. **Philosophy, SEP on Rawls and maximin.** Names a criterion for choice without probabilities. Predicts that the world frame supplies a *name* where the figures supply a *shape*. Corroborates the need to act under ignorance, and at the same time illustrates the gap in F11: the framework cannot select among the named rules.
3. **Economics, Hurwicz criterion.** A coefficient of pessimism balances best and worst outcomes. Another named binding, provenance recorded, not a figure derivation.
4. **Economics, Ellsberg 1961 via the 2024 retrospective.** Ambiguity is a quality of one's information about relative likelihoods, and reasonable people violate Savage's axioms under it. Corroborates F5 (an aggregate reading loses the live signal) and the union reading in F1.
5. **Philosophy, Hansson 1994.** Reducing ignorance to risk by the principle of insufficient reason is an explicit classical move. Corroborates the warning in F9 against adding an atom that was not needed.
6. **Engineering / risk, info-gap.** A robustness framing for severe uncertainty, with an internal criticism that a local model around a poor estimate is unsuited to severe uncertainty. Corroborates the import discipline in F9 and, in its criticism, the cost of importing a frame whose shadow was not tested.
7. **Risk analysis, SRA calls for papers.** The field itself treats extreme uncertainty and incomplete information as live research objects. Corroborates that the residual is nameable and ground-visible without any framework.

No world source was read as a verdict source. The figures decide; all of the above corroborate or illustrate, and none overrules. Where a world source names a criterion, that is recorded as a binding, not promoted into a figure line.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :23**: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35**: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Choose venues and, where a venue permits it, diverse frames; do not choose recipients for receptiveness.
- **Fig 9 :60**: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A journal's scope or a conference's deadline is a budget filter, never a reason the claim is right.

**Order in a reply:** (1) residual and shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, and its honest precondition

**Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and refuter. The recipient checks it against their own field without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the sources in `04`. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes down what would refute their own result and states their own mode, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (THE_FORMULA, Direction).

**Precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. In this simulated derivation it is internally consistent with the verbatim figures (`01` §7, verified again in `11_audit.md`). That is not a ground reading. If a real expert fires any refuter, the claim is redrawn before stage 2 is offered.

**What this agent cannot do.** Nothing here is sent. Hosting and sending are yours. Prefer an opt-in download page or a public submission portal over an attachment pushed to a list, so a recipient can decline contact without friction.
