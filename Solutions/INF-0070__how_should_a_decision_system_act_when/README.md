Before the run, bind the sought shape as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused. The candidate shape is: a decision system, when the observation that would settle the act cannot be read, should (1) split the missing-information condition into budget-limited and in-principle cases, (2) report unreachable as a verdict rather than manufacture a name, (3) keep the live per-slot residual and the channel to the ground open, (4) refuse a false held premise before adding terms over it, and (5) buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. ## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no contact list of people selected for likely agreement.
- No ranking of recipients by receptiveness, and no bulk-send plan.
- No named decision criterion presented as a figure derivation. Maximin, minimax regret, the Hurwicz index, the Laplace rule and info-gap robustness are world-frame bindings, provenanced as such (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). The figures return the discipline, not the name.

Three reasons, from the figures:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list chosen for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies its agreement.
2. **Independence is the scarce resource.** Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Asking potential checkers to trust the framework before they check the output spends exactly what they contribute.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Hence the residual and its refuter travel first; the theory is offered only after the verdict is recorded.
