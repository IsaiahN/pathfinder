# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the setting of life's emergence

**What this is:** a claim in a form a geologist, geochemist or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the union-compound finding, `gap` on the setting name. Not `grounded`.** No Hadean measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The "where did life begin" question is a union of overlapping candidate conditions, not one hard multiple-choice problem, so the settings must be enumerated from the world and kept as separate slots with an open "other" slot. Any candidate must supply three separate necessary conditions: a gradient that can be spent, a reaction network, and a cycle whose output re-enters as input. The settings are isomers over a shared condition set, and different routes can reach the same metabolic outcome, so one LUCA does not force one site. The figures do not name a setting; that name is owed to geological and experimental evidence. What is offered is the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the discriminating instrument question.

### C2 - framework-internal plus chemistry-facing; checkable by a prebiotic chemist

- **Statement:** a viable setting must independently supply a spendable gradient, a reaction network and a closing cycle. These are three necessary conditions, not a score.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 13 :24 *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*; Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **What to check:** does each proposed setting actually supply all three, and is the cycle genuinely closing rather than a chain ending in a stable product? The open objections are dilution in open submarine vents and UV plus impact destruction on land (Omran and Pasek 2020; Mulkidjanian et al. 2012).
- **Refuter:** show a setting that lacks one of the three yet yields a self-maintaining evolving system; or show that one of the three is sufficient on its own, which would collapse the three conditions into one lever.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a decision between vents, ponds, volcanic land and ice. No setting is named, and none is excluded.
- Not a claim that life arose once. Different routes can reach the same substance; LUCA is a descent bottleneck, not a count of origins.
- Not an order among settings. Convergence is not sequence.
- Not a theory of everything. The specific historical event stays a compound with its own ground.
- Not `grounded`. No geological measurement or prebiotic synthesis was run by this agent.
- Not a claim that the electrostatic proton gradient case is settled. The 2026 PNAS item named in `04_links_and_distribution.md` is a live counter-argument, recorded but not used as a cited source because full text could not be fetched.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not another review.

1. Which measurement on Hadean-relevant material returns a different value under two candidate settings? Isotope fractionation, dated microfossils, mineral textures and reactor yields are instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :34-35).
2. Does a candidate supply a spendable gradient sustained for the required duration? Westall et al. (2023) make stability and longevity explicit constraints.
3. Do products feed further reactions, and does any pathway close on itself (Fig 12 :121)?
4. Do any of the four refuters in C1 to C3 fire?
