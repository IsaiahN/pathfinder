## 0. The claim under audit

The question looks like a multiple-choice request for one historical location. Stated in effect terms, it is this:

> Given a set of candidate settings, each a different bundle of conditions (energy source, concentration mechanism, mineral catalyst, cycling, UV shielding, duration), which bundle crossed from geochemistry to a self-maintaining, evolving lineage, and what would discriminate among them?

Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A: name one setting.** Deep-sea vents, or ponds, or volcanic land, or ice. This delivers a `general`-mode verdict about a particular Hadean event with no anchor, spends the independence of the people asked to check it, and hides the fact that several settings may have produced the same outcome. Fig 9 :27-28 warns: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
- **Option B: give the shape and hand the name to the ground.** State the necessary conditions any candidate must satisfy, keep the candidates as separate slots, name the discriminating instruments, and mark the specific setting as owed. This is checkable from outside without the checker joining the frame.

The residual R is the gap between what any proposed setting predicts and what the geological and experimental record actually returns, **per setting**, never averaged. It is indexed by candidate slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

### Residual described before any figure line is chosen

- **Arity.** Four named candidates plus "another setting" (an open slot), so at least five. Arity is greater than one; this is not a single hard problem. Fig 9 :4-5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*
- **Symmetry.** Candidates are not interchangeable. The same ingredients (water, CHNOPS, energy) under a different arrangement (submarine vent versus evaporating pond versus ice) are Fig 12's isomers: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Yet different arrangements can reach the same outcome (Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*). So symmetry is partial: non-interchangeable as routes, possibly equivalent as outcomes.
- **Scale.** Molecular (monomer formation), microenvironmental (pores, films, eutectic brines), planetary (Hadean crust and ocean), and temporal (lab hours versus millions of years). The figure set is domain-agnostic across scale (Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*).

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union compound, not one hard problem (decisive for the form of the answer)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :57-58:

> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**

Applied here: "deep-sea vent or pond or volcanic land or ice" is not one difficult multiple-choice question. Each candidate is a different compound of conditions. Worse, some candidates are themselves compounds: a shallow alkaline vent is a vent *and* a wet-dry cycling setting (Barge and Price 2022, read 2026-11-23). Fig 9 :54 is explicit: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Consequence: the setting list must be enumerated and kept as separate slots. This is a `specified` finding about the question's logical form, triangulated between Fig 9 (union/partition) and Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual). It says nothing about which setting actually hosted life.

**Refuter:** show that the candidates reduce to a partition under one mechanism that predicts all and only the observed constraints; if one condition is necessary and present in exactly one setting, the union reading fails and a single slot is licensed.

### F2 - The figures cannot name the setting; the name is owed to the ground

Fig 2 :29-30:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :138-139:

> **"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."**

No figure line selects a Hadean location. A claim "life began at alkaline vents" is a claim about a 4 Gyr old event; the only things that can settle it are dated rocks, isotopic fractionation, microfossils, and reproducible prebiotic syntheses. Those are D1 anchors. The figures govern what a candidate must look like and how to keep the candidates honest. Handing over a name would be a `general`-mode verdict with no anchor, the named output-layer defect.

This is a `gap` for the specific setting, in the technical sense: after a genuine re-census no figure line governs the name. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* It is not an evasion. It is the report the framework is built to produce.

**Refuter:** exhibit a verbatim figure line whose predicates select one of the settings and exclude the others; or produce a ground measurement that settles the name (in which case the answer moves from `gap` to `grounded` on that measurement, not by figure derivation).

### F3 - The habitat must be enumerated from the world, not composed from a mechanism

Fig 11 :43:

> **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**

Fig 11 :46:

> **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"**

Fig 11 :48:

> **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

The candidate list (vents, ponds, volcanic fields, ice, other) is only legitimate if it is read off Hadean-relevant geology and experiment. The world literature does exactly this: Westall et al. (2023) review the stability and longevity of the proposed locations; Barge and Price (2022) add shallow alkaline vents as an overlooked candidate; Mulkidjanian et al. (2012) argue from cell ion composition for inland geothermal fields. A list composed by starting with a favourite mechanism and then finding a venue for it inverts the rule and is the apophenia Fig 9 :27-28 names.

Note the "other setting" slot. Fig 11 :48 makes the unmeasured part of the habitat the residual itself, so an open slot is required, not optional.

**Refuter:** show that the candidate list is closed and derived from the six substrate terms alone, so that enumeration from the world adds nothing. No figure line supports this; the habitat is explicitly read from the world.

### F4 - Any viable setting must supply a spendable gradient, a network and a closing cycle

Fig 13 :22 (gradient):

> **"a difference that can be spent"**

Fig 13 :24 (network):

> **"that entities relate, and influence travels"**

Fig 12 :121 (cycle):

> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

This is the shape. A setting counts as a candidate only if it offers a gradient that can be spent (redox, pH, thermal, light, wet-dry chemical potential), a network in which products feed further reactions, and a cycle whose output re-enters as input (autocatalysis, self-maintenance). These are three separate necessary conditions, not a score to be summed. Fig 1 :19 forbids aggregating them into one number.

The world literature corroborates each condition independently: vents are described as having "steep energy gradients" (Omran and Pasek 2020); the hot-spring case is described as needing wet-dry cycling and mineral surfaces; submarine alkaline vent models are built around proton gradients across Fe(Ni)S barriers (Barge and Price 2022; Mulkidjanian et al. 2012, which argues the marine case fails a different constraint). Corroboration is from genuinely independent authors, not from the framework author. It still cannot decide the verdict; the figures decide.

**Refuter:** exhibit a claimed origin-of-life setting that lacks a spendable gradient, a reaction network, or a closing cycle yet produces a self-maintaining evolving system. That would break the shape claim.

### F5 - Different routes can reach the same substance, so "only one setting" is not licensed

Fig 13 :121:

> **"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."**

Fig 8 :50:

> **"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."**

Omran and Pasek (2020) put the field version plainly: *"If life originated only once, then we must choose between these two environments; however, there is no reason to assume life emerged only once."* Their proposed "yes and" paradigm is the world-level counterpart of the figure claim. LUCA is a bottleneck in *descent*, not proof of a single *site* of abiogenesis; horizontal gene transfer among progenote populations makes the one-site inference uncertain (Omran and Pasek 2020, section 2.2).

Consequence: a verdict of "deep-sea vents" is not forced even if life arose once. The figure claim is about the shape of the inference, not a geological finding.

**Refuter:** show that convergent abiogenesis is impossible in principle, or that LUCA's uniqueness entails a unique originating location. Either would collapse the union to one slot.

### F6 - Absence of evidence in one setting is not evidence of absence

Fig 8 :39:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 9 :44:

> **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

The Hadean rock record is fragmentary. Westall et al. (2023) state that what is still incomplete is constrained information about the Hadean environment and "the stability and longevity of the various environments suggested as locations"; Mulkidjanian et al. (2012) note that because of heavy bombardment, "finding any geological traces of the first life forms is unlikely" if life originated in the Hadean. Fig 8 :40-43 distinguishes underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-this-budget with different instructions. Here the failure is budget and preservation, not a sealed room.

**Refuter:** exhibit a sealed, complete record for one setting in which the relevant chemistry could not have occurred; then absence in that record would be evidence of absence.

### F7 - A discriminating instrument is owed; more argument inside a frame is not

Fig 6 :34-35:

> **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

Fig 6 :49-50:

> **"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."**

Formula step 7 asks SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, and keeps them distinct. Applied here: (1) SEARCHED: the geological and experimental literature has not swept all candidate settings to the end (Westall et al. 2023; Omran and Pasek 2020 both call for more experiments); (2) INWARD: sharpen the instruments already returning something, isotope fractionation, microfossil morphology, reactor yields, mineral surface RNA extension experiments; (3) OUTWARD: import a frame that already predicts the shape, for example planetary analogues (icy moons) where wet-dry cycling is unavailable and vents remain the only candidate (Omran and Pasek 2020, section 3). Never read INWARD against OUTWARD.

**Refuter:** show either that the candidate set has been searched to closure with independent instruments (then the remedy is OUTWARD, not more SEARCHED), or that no instrument anywhere is already returning a discriminating reading (then even INWARD is unavailable and the honest status is `unreachable` at this budget).

### F8 - Agreement among reviewers cannot settle the name

Fig 2 :35:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Fig 2 :40:

> **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 2 :42:

> **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

A panel of origin-of-life researchers who all read the same reviews is one evidence pool speaking. The legitimate ask is for each reader to range separately against the ground: what would they expect to see if a given setting were the site, and what would refute the shape claim. Per the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`, the verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Refuter:** show that a panel's agreement tracked an independent measurement rather than a shared review; that would be triangulation, not negotiation, and would carry weight.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A verbatim figure line is exhibited that selects one of the candidate settings and excludes the others by its own predicates (breaks F2, the `gap` finding).
2. A claimed setting lacking a spendable gradient, a reaction network, or a closing cycle is shown to produce a self-maintaining evolving system (breaks F4).
3. The candidates are shown to reduce to a partition under one mechanism with a single necessary condition (breaks F1 and F5).
4. The Hadean record for one setting is shown to be sealed and complete with independent instruments, so absence there is evidence of absence (breaks F6).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. The setting name is not protected by any refuter because it is not claimed; it is marked `gap`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Answer with the shape, not a name. Keep four plus one slots separate; do not average them into "life began in hydrothermal settings" (F1, F2).
2. Require each candidate to be justified by an enumerated habitat condition, and keep the "other" slot open (F3).
3. Test each slot on gradient, network and cycle as three separate necessary conditions (F4).
4. Say plainly that multiple settings may have reached the same outcome, and that the record's silence is not a verdict (F5, F6).
5. Ask outside readers for the discriminating instrument and for what would refute the shape, not for agreement (F8).
6. Carry methods, not recordings: send the residual, the refuter and the five tests, then offer the figures by opt-in (Fig 4 :22-25).
