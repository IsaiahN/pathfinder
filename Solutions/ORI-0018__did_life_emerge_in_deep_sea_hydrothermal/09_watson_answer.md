## QUESTION

Option A: name one setting as the site. Option B: give the shape any candidate must satisfy, keep the candidates as separate slots, and report the name as `gap` because it is owed to the ground. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and is triangulated: the setting question is a union compound whose candidates must be enumerated from the world and kept as separate per-slot residuals; any viable candidate must supply, as three separate necessary conditions, a spendable gradient, a reaction network and a closing cycle; the candidates are isomers over a shared condition set with at least two internal compounds; and different settings can converge on the same metabolic outcome. The specific setting name does not survive as a figure-derived claim. No figure line selects Hadean vents, ponds, volcanic land or ice, and the anchor that would do so is a measurement or experiment. The status of the name is therefore `gap`, reported plainly. This is a `general` and `specified` result, not a `grounded` one, and it cannot settle the historical question it reports on.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim states three separable necessary conditions and four written refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name before they have described a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape, the refuters and the instrument question.
- **undoable:** yes. The name is not claimed, so no geological finding forces a retraction of the shape; a discriminating measurement simply fills the open slot. If the union finding is refuted, it can be narrowed to a partition without touching the three necessary conditions. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement on Hadean-relevant material that returns a different value under two candidate settings would name the slot; a sealed complete record for one setting would make absence informative; a setting lacking gradient, network or cycle yet yielding a self-maintaining evolving system would break the shape; a verbatim figure line selecting one setting would end the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` with the name marked `gap`.
