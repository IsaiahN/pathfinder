# The Tether's answer on the setting of life's emergence

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the union-compound finding, `gap` on the setting name. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We want to know where life began. In effect terms the question is which bundle of conditions crossed from geochemistry to a self-maintaining evolving lineage, and what would discriminate among the bundles. Four candidates are named and one slot is open. They are not one hard multiple-choice problem but several well-formed ones (Fig 9 :4-5), and some are compounds of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :57-58). The residual is indexed by candidate, never averaged (Fig 1 :19).

## 2. The shape

1. **Enumerate the habitat; do not compose it.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The candidate list is legitimate only if read from Hadean-relevant geology and experiment, and the "other" slot stays open because the unmeasured part of the habitat is the residual.

2. **Keep the candidates as separate slots.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Four plus one is a union of conditions with at least two internal compounds (shallow vents; ice plus mineral surfaces). One lever clears only the region where a single condition holds (Fig 9 :56). Unbundle before searching again (Fig 9 :54).

3. **Three necessary conditions, held apart.** A candidate counts only if it supplies a gradient that can be spent (Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"*), a reaction network (Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*), and a cycle whose output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*). These are not a score to be summed.

4. **The settings are isomers, not instances of one thing.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Same conditions, different arrangement, different refuters. The safer weaker reading is that they are functional-group variants (Fig 12 :124: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"*) over the group "gradient plus network plus cycle."

5. **Convergence is licensed; a single site is not.** Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* A single LUCA is a bottleneck in descent, not a count of abiogenesis sites. Omran and Pasek (2020), read 2026-11-23: *"If life originated only once, then we must choose between these two environments; however, there is no reason to assume life emerged only once."*

6. **Silence is not a verdict.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* The Hadean record is fragmentary (Westall et al. 2023; Mulkidjanian et al. 2012), so absence in one setting cannot eliminate it.

7. **The name is `gap`.** No figure line selects a Hadean location. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 2 :29: the anchor that settles a name is *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The verdict is not that no setting existed; it is that the figures cannot say which, and that the answer is owed to the ground.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "life began in hydrothermal vents." That is a name where the framework returns a shape, and it was retracted from this folder (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).
- Not "any of the named settings is ruled out." Ice, ponds, volcanic land and vents all remain live slots until the ground discriminates.
- Not "one origin." Different routes can reach the same substance.
- Not an order among settings. Convergence is not sequence (Fig 12 :49). Only the investigation order is licensed.
- Not `grounded`. No geological measurement was taken here and no end-to-end prebiotic synthesis was run.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The questions to put to the ground:

- **Setting-discrimination test:** which measurement on Hadean-relevant material would return a different value under two candidate settings? Isotope fractionation, microfossil morphology in dated rocks, mineral textures, and reactor yields are instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :34-35). A new one must be improved from one of these, not specified from a description.
- **Gradient test:** does the candidate supply a gradient that can be spent, and is it sustained for the required duration (Westall et al. 2023 on stability and longevity)?
- **Network and cycle tests:** do products feed further reactions, and does any pathway close on itself rather than ending in a stable chain (Fig 12 :121)? The open objection for submarine vents is dilution; the open objection for ponds is UV and impact destruction (Omran and Pasek 2020; Mulkidjanian et al. 2012).
- **Standing refuters:** a figure line that selects one setting; a setting lacking gradient, network or cycle that still yields life; a single necessary condition that partitions the candidates; a sealed complete record making absence informative.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The missing thing is an instrument that already registers a discriminating difference in Hadean-relevant material. Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Westall et al. (2023) say the geological scene is still incompletely constrained and call for merging geology with chemistry; Omran and Pasek (2020) call for experiments that could be run in both settings. So there is an edge to extend from. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Cost of the stop: we do not get a name. Benefit: we do not manufacture one from a shared evidence pool and then ask others to endorse it (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner between deep-sea vents, ponds, volcanic land and ice, and it does not claim the final answer. It says the question is a union of overlapping conditions rather than one hard multiple-choice problem, so the settings must be kept as separate slots; that any candidate must supply three separate things, a gradient that can be spent, a reaction network, and a cycle whose output re-enters as input; and that different settings can reach the same outcome, so one LUCA does not force one site. It refuses to name the setting, because the anchor that settles a Hadean name is a measurement, not a derivation. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the question of which instrument would discriminate between settings. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
