## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0018 a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the setting. It constrains; it does not fill.

- A shape: "any candidate setting must supply a spendable gradient, a reaction network and a closing cycle, and candidates must be kept as separate slots."
- A name: "life began at Lost City type alkaline vents."

The first is derivable here. The second is a claim about the Hadean ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the terms you already have that are closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the effect terms (energy to spend, products feeding products, concentration), then let the field's own vocabulary (serpentinization, wet-dry cycling, chemiosmosis, eutectic brine) narrow the candidate set.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing"*, and the anchor is the question itself. Here the question-as-tether is the residual: which bundle crossed the threshold and what would discriminate among bundles.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level twin of Fig 9 :54-58 and supports F1 in the audit.
- *The Hard Problem is About Source* on stopping: the answer was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* This is why ORI-0018 asks for the discriminating instrument instead of another review.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Describe the residual in effect terms first; otherwise vents, ponds or ice will each seem to fit depending on which paper you read first.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Westall et al. (2023) and Mulkidjanian et al. (2012) are imports of geological and genomic constraints into this derivation; they are recorded with author, venue, year and quote, and their disagreement is preserved rather than averaged.

Declaring a setting does not ground it. The six substrate terms and the habitat list are a visible guess at what matters; turning one candidate into *the* answer makes that guess invisible.

## 4. The metamerism reading, applied to settings

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

For ORI-0018 the reading is "life exists, and its most ancient physiology looks thermophilic or vent-like." That reading does not determine the source. Several settings (submarine alkaline vents, terrestrial geothermal ponds, shallow vents, ice-brine concentrators) could produce a similar early metabolism. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name. This corroborates Fig 13 :121 (*"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*) but does not by itself pick a setting.

## 5. Q answered: what does "shape" license here?

**Nothing licenses a specific setting.** The framework returns three things: the necessary conditions any candidate must meet, the rule for keeping candidates as separate slots, and the description of the missing instrument in effect terms. A particular setting is licensed only by the ground settling it, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the setting name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this derivation commit the forbidden error?** No, after correction. The draft of this folder named "hydrothermal vents" in the one-paragraph answer. That was a name where the framework returns a shape, and it was corrected: the current `07_the_tether_answer.md` keeps the name in an `other`/ground-owned slot. The earlier wording is recorded in section 7 rather than silently erased.

## 6. Prestige, and my own status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A consensus of well-known origin-of-life reviews is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets the ground decide.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: an LLM can *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the origin-of-life claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
