## The question restated as an effect

Let `s` range over societies, one society per object slot. For each `s`:

`R_s = |Gamma(b, a) - o'|`

where `Gamma(b, a)` is a frame's prediction of whether the powerful will be constrained, and `o'` is what actually happened. Option A: an answer that names the historical cause (geography, disease, resource endowment, class balance, contingency). Option B: an answer that states the shape any such cause must produce and marks the specific cause as unreached.

What each leads to: A delivers a `general`-mode verdict on a causal question this derivation did not measure, and it spends the independence of the people asked to check it. B delivers a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term.** A candidate answer must specify (i) a checking arrangement whose keeper is outside the slice it grades, (ii) a habitat that sustains the arrangement, (iii) the sign of the feedback loop, and (iv) an anchor that does not move when the powerful push on it. The derivation writes this down before running, so the preference is refusable like any other term (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map :283)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat Map :163) Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
