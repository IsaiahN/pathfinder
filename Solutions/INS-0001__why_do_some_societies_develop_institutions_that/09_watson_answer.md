## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that some societies develop institutions constraining powerful actors while others consolidate power. For society `s`, `R_s = |Gamma(b, a) - o'|`, the gap between a predicted institutional trajectory and the observed one. Option A: name the historical cause, geography, disease, endowment, class balance or contingency. Option B: state the shape any such cause must produce and mark the specific cause as unreached. What each leads to: A gives a `general`-mode causal verdict with no measurement and spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per-society gap between predicted and observed constraint. Arity: two conditions bundled (establishment of a check, resistance to capture), so a union compound. Symmetry: asymmetric, introduction of a check is available and subtraction of the habitat is not. Scale: actor, arrangement, society outcome. Fig 9 :4 says to split a union rather than search it as one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the checking arrangement.** Actors: powerful actors, checking bodies, the seat that keeps the record reachable. Currency: conventions, verdicts, compliance. Anchor: the constraint record that holds in crisis. Question type: partition boundary between the checked and the checker.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: elites, citizens, classes, geography, disease environment, resource endowment, neighbouring societies, trade. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the world as read off and enumerated. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 3, the explanation frame.** Actors: competing theories and expert readers. Currency: evidence and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is low and agreement is not corroboration.
- **Domain 4, the specific historical cause.** No nameable anchor available at this budget. Held as unreached, not silently filled.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle the check before its cascade (D1 before D2); keep the ground reachable throughout (D3).

Strain test: Fig 10's seat is defined by epistemic access, not by constitutional office. Re-census with political bodies as actors, conventions as currency and the constraint record as anchor removes the strain. The binding is declared `specified`, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the causal name does not. A constraint on power is a checking arrangement whose keeper is outside what it grades and gains nothing from a particular reading (D1, triangulated). Its capacity sits in the arrangement and habitat, so a design change that does not change contact changes nothing (D2, single-source figure with world corroboration). Agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool is variation, not a verdict, so independence is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (D3, triangulated). The question bundles establishment and capture resistance, and the loop sign decides whether a check survives its own use. Which historical factor produced a given society is unreached at this budget, reported as a gap rather than filled by an unprovenanced import (D4). This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a causal story instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The causal imports are withheld, so nothing has to be withdrawn from a claim about a named country. The shape can be refined by re-census, and D4 can be opened by a gated import without touching D1 to D3.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A crisis record in which the constraint holds while the keeper's reputation is at stake would settle the anchor test; a charter change with no habitat contact that nevertheless moves the record would break D2; two societies with identical institutions and independence bonds but opposite outcomes would break the isomer check; a gated causal claim with provenance and a shadow test would move D4 from unreached to grounded for that case. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
