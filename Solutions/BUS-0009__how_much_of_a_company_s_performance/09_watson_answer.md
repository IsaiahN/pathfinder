## QUESTION

How much of a company's performance comes from management choices versus industry structure and broader economic conditions?

Restated as an effect: why does the share of performance attributed to three named sources keep moving with the frame that measures it, and what object would a valid attribution have to index? R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per firm-slot and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Option A: the split is a findable universal number. Option B: the split is a union-level filter, the residual stays per slot, and a universal number is a `gap`. Each leads to a different ask: A asks for a bigger dataset, B asks for an instrument and a set of refuters.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: a four-place relation (firm Γ, action a, market return o′, time t), indexed by slot and ordered by time. It carries an ordered bond (`→`, sequence) and a comparison bond (`⋛`, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, Fig 12 :87). Symmetry: "performance" is a threshold relative to competitors, not an absolute quantity; firm and market are not interchangeable. Scale: the single firm loop, the industry population, and the economy-wide time channel; the membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4).
- **D1 Management choices.** Actors: decision makers, routines, the firm's Γ. Currency: actions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized market return on the action (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D2 Industry structure.** Actors: firms, customers, entrants, suppliers, regulators. Currency: survival, share, profit. Anchor: customer choice and willingness to pay; the market as ground (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D3 Economic conditions.** Actors: habitat conditions, instruments, metrics. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24), with conditions named at Fig 11 :46. Admitted.
- **X1 Managerial genius as free-floating cause.** No anchor independent of the market verdict. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 / X3 The industry percentage and the macro percentage as separate causes.** Absorbed into D2 and D3; the three named sources are conditions around one ground, not three grounds.

Union, not partition: D1/D2, D2/D3 and D1/D3 overlap, so the three sources are overlapping conditions with an unassignable shared fraction (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The "how much" question is posed at the objective link, which has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's physical six terms to industry and economy only fit by calling industry a kind of space. That is a strain, so Fig 13 governs only the honest-stop role here. Fig 1 governs the agent's residual signal; a population variance share is an observer's statistic, a different object. No strain survives re-census (see `01` section 7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The universal percentage is a `gap`; the shape survives. Index the residual per firm-slot and per time, because aggregating across slots removes the steering signal. Keep management, industry and economy as a union of overlapping conditions with an unassignable shared fraction, not as additive parts. Treat any variance share as a filter for the budget, never as a witness for a cause. Locate management in the lever that changes contact, with valence that flips as the residual changes; locate industry and macro in the habitat and time channel around one market anchor. Because "performance" is a threshold, any attribution must say which side of the comparison moved. The literature's shifting effect sizes, including the firm effect moving from 43.9% to 11.5% when a neighborhood condition is added, are the empirical signature of this verdict. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **Cost if wrong:** a misdirected diagnosis and a wasted budget. A firm failing contact would be sold an import, or a firm needing a new atom would be sold an instrument, and a single global lever would be applied to a union that needs per-slot treatment. Fig 2 :42 - "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is real but bounded, and it is largely reversible if the filter is labelled as a filter.
- **Undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than deleted (Fig 6). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error. A published percentage can be superseded by a corrected per-slot attribution without withdrawing any primitive.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the five pre-registered falsifiers would reverse it: a frame-invariant share that survives a fourth condition and two datasets; an aggregate that outperforms the per-slot reading on an intervention; a source-discriminating instrument with two independent frames agreeing against the market anchor; a management contribution invariant across a change of residual; or a variance share that predicts the outcome of intervening on its named source. A genuinely different evidence pool establishing that per-firm attribution is fully captured by a single additive share would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
