# Outreach draft - check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the pre-registered refuter below must stay attached to the shape claim. If it is removed, the offer collapses on contact, because a claim without its failure condition asks the reader to join a frame rather than check a residual.

---

**Subject:** A failure condition for the "how much of performance is management?" question

> Hello,
>
> I am working on a small, checkable claim about the question of how much of a company's performance comes from management choices, industry structure and broader economic conditions. I would like you to check one output against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question as posed assumes that performance is one aggregable quantity and that the three sources are separable parts that sum to a share. The figures I am working from say both assumptions fail. The residual that steers a firm is indexed per firm-slot; averaging across slots removes the signal that says where to look. And the three named sources are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition, so a case in two conditions is categorically different from a case in either one. A variance decomposition is a filter for deciding where to spend attention, not a witness for what caused the result. Its own practitioners say it does not identify the causal driver.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that there is a universal split such as "management 40 percent, industry 10 percent, economy the rest." I am not claiming that management skill is a free-floating property, that industry is a second anchor alongside the market, or that a published effect size settles a cause. The framework returns a shape, not a name: index per slot, keep the union unbundled, attach the frame to any percentage you report, and specify the instrument that would close the attribution.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a stable percentage split that survives adding a new overlapping condition and replicates across two datasets with different methods, against an anchor that does not update;
> 2. a demonstration that the global percentage predicts a named firm's response to an intervention better than the per-firm reading does;
> 3. a source-discriminating instrument that identifies, for a named firm, which condition changed the market's return, with two independent frames agreeing while ranging separately against the market;
> 4. a management contribution invariant across a documented change of residual and habitat;
> 5. a variance share that, with no extra instrument, predicts the outcome of intervening on the source it names.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures and formula (public repository): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Variance decomposition of firm performance, a review: https://www.emerald.com/mrr/article/44/6/867/311687/Variance-decomposition-of-firm-performance-past
> - Under the spotlight: contextualizing the firm vs. industry debate, Vedula et al., open access: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10490-025-10009-5
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> With thanks,

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No universal percentage asserted | Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* |
| Variance share called a filter | Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* |
| Three sources called a union | Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* |
| No request for agreement or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | A frame cannot check the direction of its own question; the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. |
