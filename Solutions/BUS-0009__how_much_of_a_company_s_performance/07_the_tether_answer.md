# The Tether's answer on management versus industry versus the economy

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a share: how much of a company's performance comes from management choices, industry structure, and broader economic conditions. In effect terms, performance is the market's return o′ against the firm's prediction Γ(b,a), and R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per firm-slot and per time (Fig 1 :19). The requested share aggregates across slots and divides the result among three named causes. The figure-derived answer is that this object is not the one that steers, and the three causes are not separable parts.

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57): a case in management and industry conditions is categorically different from a case in either one. It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The residual must stay indexed.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global percentage discards the signal that says where to look. Per-firm, per-time verdicts, never averaged.
2. **The three sources form a union, not a partition.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* There is a shared fraction that cannot be assigned. The empirical signature: adding a neighborhood condition moves the reported "firm effect" from 43.9% to 11.5% (Vedula et al 2026).
3. **A variance share is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The method's own practitioners say it *"does not pinpoint which underlying aspect of an effect class influences firm performance"* and is *"not examining causal relationships of specific drivers"*. The word "effect" means variance share, not cause.
4. **Management choices contribute only where they change contact.** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* And valence is relative to the residual: Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* There is no universal management percentage; there is a per-slot judgement.
5. **Industry and macro conditions are habitat and time channel, not extra anchors.** Fig 11 :46 names conditions as habitat elements; Fig 10 :24 says *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. The market is the one anchor that settles all three (Fig 2 :29).
6. **"Performance" is a threshold.** Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. An attribution must say which side of the comparison moved, because a comparison can change when either operand changes. A single ROA share does not.
7. **The universal number is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms, plus independent frames ranging against the market anchor.

## 3. Verdicts per domain, never averaged

| domain | verdict | mode |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Management choices | A lever, not a stock. Contributes where it changes contact; valence flips with the residual. No universal share. | `specified` |
| D2 Industry structure | A habitat condition. Its measured share moves when the frame or the bond set changes. No standing share. | `specified` |
| D3 Economic conditions | The time channel and the ground's conditions. The ground does not decay; the channel does. A condition, not a lever. | `general` for the channel, `specified` for firm application |
| Union of all three | Unbundled, indexed per slot, with an unassignable shared fraction. | `specified` |
| Universal percentage | `gap`, not a hidden answer. | `general` |

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "management matters X percent". No universal number is asserted at any granularity coarser than a named firm-slot.
- Not "industry does not matter" or "the economy is irrelevant". Both are habitat conditions; the union verdict says they cannot be cleanly separated, not that they are empty.
- Not that variance decomposition is useless. It is a filter for the budget, which is a legitimate use (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a causal identification. The method's own practitioners disclaim it, and the figures forbid promoting a filter to a witness.
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating instrument has been run.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further aggregate. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- For a named firm-slot, which condition changed the market's return: a choice, a habitat condition, or a time-channel state? An instrument that discriminates these would name the source.
- Does the per-slot attribution survive a change of frame with a different closure, with both frames ranging separately against the market anchor (Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*)?
- Does the attribution predict the outcome of intervening on the named source? If not, it remains a filter.

Standing falsifiers from section 4 of `01`: a frame-invariant share; an aggregate that outperforms the per-slot reading; a discriminating instrument with two independent frames; an invariant management contribution; a variance share that predicts an intervention.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The habitat enumeration is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (framework-internal essay). Declaring the three sources makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

A note on kinds: unreachable-at-this-budget and underivable-in-principle are different facts (Formula step 7). The universal percentage is not a metaframe theorem; it is an aggregate over a union, so the remedy is a change of object and an instrument, not an import of a new effect class. Importing a fourth class against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed and would be priced above starting from primitives.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not report a percentage split of performance into management, industry and the economy. It says the question asks for the wrong object: the steering signal is indexed per firm and per time, and averaging across firms removes the signal that says where to look. The three named sources are a union of overlapping conditions, not additive parts, so a shared fraction cannot be assigned; that is why adding a neighborhood condition to a standard decomposition moves the reported firm effect from about 44 percent to about 12 percent. A variance share is a filter for deciding where to attend, not a witness for what caused the result, and its own practitioners say it is not causal. Management choices contribute where they change contact, and their sign flips when the residual changes, so there is no standing management percentage. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close the attribution. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
