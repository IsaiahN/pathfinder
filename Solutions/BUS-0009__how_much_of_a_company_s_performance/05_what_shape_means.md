## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0009 the shape is: index the residual per firm-slot, keep the three named sources as a union, treat any percentage as a filter, and specify the instrument that would close the attribution. The shape is **not** "management 40, industry 10, economy 50". That is a name with a number attached.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the audit describes the question as "a global share that aggregates across slots" instead of "industry effect" or "CEO effect": the latter are the discipline's borrowed words, and describing the effect first avoids the frame fitting itself to the question.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the checkable expectation is: if a new overlapping effect class is added to a decomposition, the reported share will move.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the moment the universal percentage is recognised as the wrong object; after that, the per-slot attribution is a new question with its own ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For BUS-0009, the instrument would have to discriminate, for a named firm-slot, which condition changed the market's return.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The ecology variation-partitioning result about the unassignable shared fraction is such an import from a genuinely different field; it is recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`, with field and overlap, and it corroborates the shape rather than supplying the answer.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Declaring the three sources (management, industry, economy) does not make them neutral either. It makes a choice visible: the choice to carve the habitat into three named classes. That choice is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such, not presented as a finding.

## 4. Why an aggregate percentage is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

A variance decomposition reads the green: total variance in ROA. It does not comment on the source: which condition actually changed the market's return. Several different arrangements of conditions produce the same share (the 43.9% and the 11.5% in Vedula et al 2026 are both readings of the same underlying data under different bonds). Naming "management" as the source of a share is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading, where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A variance decomposition cannot validate its own interpretation; that is why its practitioners disclaim causation (see `04_links_and_distribution.md`).

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED: what licenses a particular attribution?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular attribution for a named firm-slot is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the literature commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, when an effect share is read as a cause. Rumelt (1991) is careful: he speaks of *"industry factors (whatever their nature)"* and of *"effects"* that are variance shares. The error appears downstream, when a share is quoted as "how much management matters". That is naming a source from a reading without a discriminator. The correct form is the per-slot attribution with its instrument stated, or an honest `gap`.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming management, industry or economy at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Management choices, industry structure and economic conditions are not missing primitives; they are conditions already enumerated in the habitat (Fig 11 :46 names conditions, Fig 11 :45 names actors, Fig 11 :47 names relations). Naming them is a **binding from the business frame** onto habitat elements, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong is treating the binding as if it settled a cause. Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"* - naming a habitat condition adds no atom. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reading still cannot discriminate among sources. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 11 + Fig 6).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous strategy scholars to agree with a share is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground - an instrument, a market, an intervention - settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the business claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The stop condition for this question

The framework returns a shape and stops. For BUS-0009 the stop is not "we could not find the right percentage". It is "the object the question asks for is an aggregate over a union, and the instrument that would settle a per-slot attribution is not held". Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate where the stop happened and what would move it.
