## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions. WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** A conclusion C held in common while interpretations of C remain disputed. Arity: two or more frames, one term, an interpretation relation per frame. Symmetry: the frame-to-C bond is not recoverable from C. Scale: belief update, room, chain, institution. Unit: per slot, never per field average.
- **Domain 1, intra-frame update.** Actors: a frame with b and w. Currency: posterior revision. Anchor: the frame's own residual. Verdict: belief revision, not agreement.
- **Domain 2, inter-frame convergence.** Actors: frames and rooms. Currency: influence and agreement. Anchor: the ground only if pinned. Question type: union of three bonds. Verdict: per-case compound.
- **Domain 3, semantic layer.** Actors: conventions and terms. Currency: shared meaning. Anchor: none, since a convention makes no claim about the world. Verdict: convention while interpretation is unresolved; changes kind when it decides an outcome.
- **Domain 4, evidence layer.** Actors: instruments and pools. Currency: evidence and independence. Anchor: the ground that does not update. Verdict: independence and a non-updating anchor are the only converts from agreement to evidence.

Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, frame selection, carry. I derived this from Fig 3, whereas the primary derived the same order from Fig 3 plus the field's own measurement practice. Low overlap between routes, one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 panels and valence rules, Fig 7 selection between rooms, Fig 9 union call and filter rule, Fig 10 convention rule, Fig 1 slot rule.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the three-bond diagnostic, the conditional convention reading, and the instruction to score per case.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical assignment, for example "this consensus was pool-driven and C was a convention until it was used in policy."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19). A field-wide label "the consensus is X" discards the live slot that made the claim checkable.
- the **dependency order** (Fig 3 :20-23). The shorthand lists three scores in parallel; it does not enforce perception before vocabulary before objective.
- the **non-updating condition on the anchor** (Fig 2 :33). The shorthand says "anchor score"; if the anchor moved, the case is panel 1 and the score is meaningless.
- the **provenance requirement** (Fig 8 :50-52). Two communities with the same C and different bonds are indistinguishable in the finished assignment unless the lineage was recorded.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: agreement under a shared pool is not corroboration, and a convention makes no claim about the world. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the diagnostic is the figures in fewer words" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
