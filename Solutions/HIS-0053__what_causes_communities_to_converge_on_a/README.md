## Restating the question as an effect

A community holds conclusion C in common while its members continue to disagree about what C means, which mechanism it implies, or how it should be applied. Two readings are being decided:

- **Option A:** this is a single phenomenon with one cause, so a general mechanism of consensus formation explains it.
- **Option B:** this is a union of overlapping conditions with a diagnostic that separates them, and the shared conclusion functions as a convention until a non-updating ground settles it.

What each leads to: A invites a single-cause story and a receptiveness-ranked list of people who already agree, which manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40). B yields a checkable shape, per-case verdicts that are never averaged, and refuters written in advance.

The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: **convergence with interpretive disagreement** means a term C held by frames whose closures differ, where the bond between a frame and C is not recoverable from C alone. Binding it makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else (formula, step 2, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no scraped contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that the framework has discovered the cause of any particular historical consensus.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Room is not anchor.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list selected for agreement cannot corroborate.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures that shared pool.
3. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: public opt-in submission portals avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that follow from unsolicited bulk mail. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
