## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0084 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about decision rights that can be stated before any particular department, role or decision is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *autonomy is licensed per decision slot by a non-updating anchor, a legitimate anchor type, a verdict-or-convention classification and a non-reconstructing checker.*
- Name, withheld: *customer service should use AI for recommendations, fraud detection should decide autonomously, HR should never automate.*

The distinction matters here because the AI-adoption literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Vendor maturity models name stages; risk frameworks name high-risk domains; organizational designs name centres of excellence. Those are bindings from the management frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

The *How Intelligence Actually Works* essay sharpens the stop: the recursion *"converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* A plausible department list can be indiscernible from a correct one at the current instrument boundary, and that is exactly why it must not be presented as the answer.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The Library of Babel essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "human in the loop", "high-risk AI", "algorithm aversion", "automation bias", "decision rights" and "algorithmic management". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (duration of a low-residual slot under a given decision-rights arrangement, indexed per slot) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0084 as a union of four conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question and not as a binary partition.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the four conditions are stated, an instrument is named for each, and the nine falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0084 neither has happened for the specific allocation. There is no prospective per-slot study that pays for "this decision right should be autonomous", and the management-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F13 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

They're Scanning Books adds the deeper version: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* What is owed for BUS-0084 is a new axis of measurement, a source-discriminating per-slot instrument, not another policy sentence.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error an AI-governance dashboard makes when it reads a good outcome and names its source. A low error rate, a high adoption score or a clean audit is a reading. It is consistent with a genuinely anchoring ground, with a pooled metric that the AI itself shapes, with a favourable habitat, or with simple luck. The reading does not determine the source. The governance literature's habit of naming the source from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

The same essay's spectrometer case is the positive counterpart: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The honest stop in F11 is therefore not a refusal to answer. It is a specification of the instrument that would answer.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to AI adoption

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness. It is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that companies behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous AI researchers or celebrated chief executives who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 6. Contact, not agreement, is the mechanism

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, read directly:

> *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

The essay's account of two people drifting together is the human-scale version of Fig 2 :34: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Its three safeguards are the ones used in F5: a ground both frames are corrected by separately, separate experience so independence is renewed, and claims in effect terms so a reader can check them without the private shorthand.

> *"An AI and the people overseeing it are the same: they line up by each checking against a reference point neither of them controls, not by persuading each other."*

That sentence is the plain-language form of the oversight condition C4. It is framework-internal, so it corroborates the reading of Fig 2 and Fig 10 rather than supplying independent evidence.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the organizational scale: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*, and the group is only worth having *"while its members stay different"*. Ten reviewers reading the same dashboard are one opinion said ten times. That is F4 and F5 at the population scale.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular decision slot or department is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a decision mechanism is already in a company's library, naming it is a binding from the management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be.

The correction does **not** convert the `gap` of F11 into a nameable answer. The gap is at the instrument boundary: no source-discriminating per-slot measurement is held here. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 8. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about companies. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

The lead essay adds a caveat that applies directly to this folder: LLM simulation produces *"simulacra"*, and outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* The pre-registered refuter is the discipline that answers that caveat. It is written before the conclusion and is refusable by the same test as everything else.

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0084 the canonical shape is: **a four-condition conjunction over decision slots with four distinct failure modes, a verdict-or-convention valve that changes what agreement means, and a bond difference between recommendation and autonomy that a resource inventory cannot record.** It is not a department list, not a maturity stage, not a risk-tier label, and not a ranking of functions by AI readiness. The names that appear in the management literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded allocation are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
