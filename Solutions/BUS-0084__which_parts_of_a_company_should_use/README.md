## The question restated as an effect

The offered choice partitions an organisation chart into recommendation units and autonomous units. Stated as an effect, R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed by **decision slot**, never by department (Fig 1 :19). The slot is a recurring decision at a specific point where the company's library Γ predicts an outcome and the world returns one. A department is a bundle of such slots, hence a union rather than a unit (Fig 9 :54). The answer would be a set of conditions on a slot that decide whether Γ may select the action `a` directly (autonomy) or must hand a candidate to a person (recommendation). Until that shape is written down and bound as an ordinary term, it predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0.

## What an answer would look like

A checkable answer would be: (1) a named anchor for each slot that does not move when the AI's belief pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30); (2) a classification of the slot as constitutively about people or constitutively about something an instrument can settle (Fig 2 :36-38); (3) a classification of the decision as verdict or convention (Fig 2 :39-43); (4) a per-slot instrument that discriminates a failed channel from a failed ground. Conditions (1) to (3) are derived below. Condition (4) is not held here, so the specific named allocation is a declared `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not upgrade the label: the Seat Map field guide says agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence, so adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor; the same rule is Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Second, selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their verdict worthless (Fig 2 :39-41). Third, showing the framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
