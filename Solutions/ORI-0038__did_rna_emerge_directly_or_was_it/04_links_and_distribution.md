## Wider literature that bears on the question

Robertson and Joyce 2012 is the governing review: it separates the evidence that an RNA World existed from the weaker claim that life began with RNA, and it explicitly frames the RNA-later alternative. The ASU News piece reports the XNA transfer result, but a laboratory demonstration is not a proof that the crossing happened on Earth. The Wikipedia TNA entry supplies the candidate-specific facts (four-carbon backbone, base pairing with RNA) and the Wikipedia error-threshold entry supplies the low-overlap mathematical condition. These three chemical and mathematical sources are distinct fields, but the ASU piece and the Robertson and Joyce review draw on the same XNA/TNA literature, so their agreement is one witness, not two. LabXchange is a teaching summary and carries no independent weight. All framework material is the primary authority for the shape and is kept separate from the world sources.

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a full theory before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if a first polymer had the claimed shape before naming PNA, TNA or GNA.
- **Fig 7 :12-13** *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 1 :19** *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Ask about each condition separately; do not ask for a yes or no on the whole theory.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six failure conditions; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the local figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only if asked, offer the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.
