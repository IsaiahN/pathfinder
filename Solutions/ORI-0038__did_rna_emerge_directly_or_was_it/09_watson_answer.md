## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "Did RNA emerge directly, or was it preceded by a simpler genetic polymer?" Option A: RNA satisfied all conditions first. Option B: a simpler polymer satisfied them first and transferred information to RNA. Both options require RNA to appear, so the either/or is a union with an ordering, not a partition.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is refused as written. The two options form a union of overlapping conditions with an ordering bond, not a partition, so per-condition verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The figures give the shape a first genetic polymer must have: a cross-kind composition settled against the prebiotic ground, which becomes an atom for what composes over it, with an ordered, sequence-preserving transfer to RNA. Same bases with a different backbone is isomerism, so a precursor and RNA are different members of one functional group. "Precedes" is ordered and "simpler" is a threshold comparison on a quantity. No figure line names a molecule, so the identity of the first polymer is a `gap`, reported as unreached and not as silence. A failed direct synthesis is a residual, not a verdict for the precursor. The result is `specified` on the shape, `general` on the split, `gap` on the name, and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries six pre-registered failure conditions in `01` section 5, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording (a favored polymer story) instead of a generator (the shape and its refuter). Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No ground claim about a molecule was made, so nothing about a polymer must be withdrawn. The union framing, the shape, the isomer reading and the ordering all stand without a name. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one if a measurement fires one of the six refuters. The naming gap can be closed by a source-discriminating instrument without changing the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured prebiotic synthesis route plus a copying fidelity above the error threshold for one candidate under a stated habitat, with a demonstrated sequence-preserving transfer to RNA, would settle option B. A direct RNA route satisfying all four conditions in the same habitat with no transfer bond would settle option A. A clean partition of the four conditions would break the union verdict. "Simpler" as a symmetric intrinsic property would break the comparison reading. A base-set change rather than a backbone change would narrow the isomer claim to the functional group. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
