# WATSON answer: what tests distinguish general capability from narrow familiar-task competence?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census.

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This WATSON run synthesizes `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*) it cannot settle the claim it reports on. No system was tested.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what observations would separate two hypotheses that currently return the same reading: H_general, a system that can acquire competence in an unprepared habitat; H_narrow, a system competent only inside a familiar closure. Option A: use a broad benchmark score as the discriminator. Option B: require a differential instrument whose outcome would differ between the two. What each leads to: A spends budget on a frame-internal coverage quantity and cannot separate the hypotheses; B yields a shape, six test designs, six pre-registered refuters and an honest stop at the limit of the available instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two hypotheses, one score, no discriminator. Arity: at least two conditions (familiar and substituted habitat) plus an ordered comparison between them, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57). Symmetry: the comparison is ordered, so → and ⋛ govern it, not +. Scale: one task loop, one family, the lossy membrane from training closure to substituted habitat.
- **D1 Test instrument.** Actors: items, generator, slots, scoring rule. Currency: per-slot residual and its trend. Anchor: the ground reading the item approximates, never the score. Status: admitted; partition boundary question, which items lie inside the closure.
- **D2 System under test.** Actors: Γ, atoms, bonds, belief b, reach. Currency: prediction error per slot, cost of a term. Anchor: none in isolation. Status: admitted only as agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39).
- **D3 Substituted habitat.** Actors: novel tasks, conditions, consequences, other agents. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Status: admitted.
- **D4 Provenance ledger.** Actors: training corpus, release dates, item lineage. Currency: exposure, independence, shadow. Anchor: a dated public record. Status: admitted.
- **D5 Verification seat.** Actors: outside checker, instrument, refuter. Currency: residual, not agreement. Anchor: the ground; agreement disqualified. Status: admitted.
- **X1 General intelligence as a property of the agent alone.** Actors: the agent. Currency: talent. Anchor: none nameable. Status: not a domain yet; dissolved by Fig 11 :39.
- **X2 Benchmark score as verdict.** Absorbed into D1 as a filter; never a witness.
- **Order by dependency.** perception, vocabulary and objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The middle link is uninstrumented and must be built before transfer is scored.

Strain test applied: the psychometric fluid/crystallized distinction governs persons under a test; Fig 11 :39 governs agent-and-habitat at any stratum. Different objects, so after re-census there is no strain, only a parallel from a low-overlap field.

- Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." -> a harness solve is not proof of generality.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The answer is a shape, not a score. A discriminating test must vary contact (Fig 11 :39-40, :70), report per-slot residual and trend (Fig 1 :19-22), carry a dated exposure record (Fig 8 :50-52), use independent anchors that cannot be reconstructed (Fig 10 :42-43, Fig 2 :31-35), separate depth from absence (Fig 6 :27, Fig 8 :40-41), and instrument the vocabulary/objective link before scoring transfer (Fig 3 :25-27). The six verdicts stay separate and are never averaged. More items from the same family are a filter for the budget, never a witness (Fig 9 :60). A generality quotient for an agent in isolation is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified` on the designs, `general` on the shape, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** a team builds a novel-task suite, watches a score rise, and announces general capability when it has measured wiring in a substituted habitat. The cheaper error is the reverse: declaring absence from one failed family and importing a primitive that was not owed. Both are bounded because the packet carries pre-registered refuters and a provenance trail, so the error is locatable. The avoidable part is spending an expert's independence by showing them the derivation before their verdict (Fig 10 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. A refuted test design is added as a term that refuses; nothing is deleted and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The six refuters and the figure lines are recorded in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, so a later reader can locate which property failed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An agent-only change that moves the ground across substituted habitats; a demonstration that averaging loses no information; repeated search in a closed set that adds a recorded new atom; an audited ledger showing exposure changes nothing; a reconstructing verifier agreeing with a non-reconstructing one; or a system at ceiling on substituted habitats with no vocabulary link. Any one of these fires a pre-registered refuter. Agreement among evaluators who share the same evidence pool is not such a reading (Fig 2 :40).
