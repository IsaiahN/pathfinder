The framework's own canonical usage rule is stricter than any essay: the figures return *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 puts it as *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a shape is **the narrowed form of a discriminating observation that can be stated before any system has been tested**. It constrains the design of the test. It does not supply the test's result, a score, or the name of a capability. `H_general` versus `H_narrow` is a shape claim: two hypotheses, one current reading, and a requirement on what a separating observation must look like. A number such as "85% on a novel-task suite" is not a shape; it is a reading that both hypotheses can produce.

The bounded ordinary term from `README.md` is itself such a shape: *a differential instrument whose outcome would differ between a general capability and a narrow competence*. It is written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused, rather than remaining a preference invisible to the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest vocabulary was drawn from psychometrics (fluid versus crystallized), animal cognition (transfer to never-seen stimuli), and benchmark engineering (contamination, dynamic generation).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the form of a shape claim: an expectation stated so anyone can check it.
- Fig 6 :30, quoted in that essay: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* So the claim is stated as contact, exposure and anchor, not as a Tether vocabulary lesson.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In this solution the finish line is the six test properties in `07`, which are the residual restated in operational form.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A test battery that returns a separating result on a recorded, substituted habitat would license a verdict about a particular system.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Borrowing "fluid intelligence" from psychometrics is such an import and is recorded as one, with its source and date.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also says *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's list. Naming one of its atoms as *the* mechanism of general capability would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The essay that most directly governs this question

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* draws the exact distinction the question asks for:

> **"General intelligence is not reliable success on the novel, nobody has that. It is the right kind of failure: making progress through search-and-adaptation rather than confabulation. The bar is whether failure accumulates traction, not whether the answer arrives."**

That is a shape, and it is operationalisable. A discriminating test should measure the *trend* of the residual under search, not just whether a novel item was solved. It also supplies a refuter: if both H_general and H_narrow systems show identical failure curves, the proposed discriminator fails.

The same essay's definition of the generator is relevant: discovery is *"a random walk plus a model that can be surprised"*, and a system can only be surprised along axes its primitives can represent. Therefore a test that never presents an axis the system cannot represent cannot discriminate general capability; it only re-tests the existing representation. Fig 6 :27 says the same more shortly: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

## 5. Fig 11 supplies the replacement question and the built-in refuter

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes the figure directly: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* It then asks three test questions of its own agent design: whether an agent born into a shared store reaches its first level in fewer moves than one starting alone, whether that holds on kinds of game the store has never seen, and whether adding agents with different experience helps more than adding copies. Those are differential instruments in exactly the sense developed here, and they are recorded as a corroborating parallel, not as evidence for the verdict.

Fig 11 :70, *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability,"* is the reason a pure harness score cannot be the answer. Fig 11 :72 supplies the honest failure condition: *"an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."*

## 6. Why a source-discriminating instrument is owed

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Two systems with the same benchmark score are the metamerism case. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name for the source. That discriminator, in this question, is the dated exposure audit plus the substituted habitat.

## 7. Correction to my own reading

I initially wanted to write that the framework forbids naming any capability at all. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Naming a term that is already held, such as "transfer" or "search", is a binding from another frame and is legitimate when its provenance is recorded. What remains forbidden is (a) inventing a seventh primitive to explain a test result, (b) claiming that binding a psychometric term moves the wall, and (c) presenting the shape as a ground verdict. Both the initial reading and this correction are kept visible.

## 8. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A test design cited because a famous lab uses it has a budget to be tried; it does not thereby become a witness.

The same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay adds that LLM outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any system and cannot settle the question they report on.
