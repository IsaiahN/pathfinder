# The Tether's answer: what tests could distinguish general capability from narrow competence

**Mode: `specified` on six test designs, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading. No system was tested.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system that can acquire competence in an unprepared habitat and a system competent only inside a familiar closure can return the same score. The missing object is a **separating observation**, not a better theory of intelligence. Capability is not a property of an agent in isolation. Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

## 2. The shape of a discriminating test

A test discriminates only if it changes what the system is in contact with while keeping the system fixed, and reports an anchor the system cannot move. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* The shape is a → chain ending in an ordered ⋛ comparison, not a symmetric sum of subscores (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3-Q4). Its output is a curve per slot, not a number.

## 3. Six test designs

Each design keeps its own verdict. They are never averaged into a generality index (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

### T1 Habitat substitution, not item proliferation

Hold the system, the task format and the scoring rule fixed. Change the habitat: tasks whose generating rule was unavailable during training, evidenced by a post-cutoff date or a rule-generated lineage. Compare the per-slot residual in the familiar and substituted habitats. Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :68-69: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."* A specialist loses contact here; a general system's residual stays small or falls with few examples. **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground reading across substituted habitats (Fig 11 :72).

### T2 Per-slot residual curves

Report R per task family and per object, with its trend across attempts, not a mean. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :20: *"a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The discriminant is not passing versus failing; it is whether a residual persists and does not trend down after search. **Refuter:** per-slot residuals are a deterministic function of the aggregate for all systems tested.

### T3 Dated exposure and provenance audit

Before scoring, record whether each item was in the training corpus, generated after the training cutoff, or rule-generated with a recorded seed. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* **Refuter:** a complete, independently audited ledger showing exposure status never changes the residual for any system tested.

### T4 Independent anchors, not a panel of agreeing readers

Use at least two test frames with low evidence overlap, each ranged against the ground separately. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Human approval is the right anchor for "is this a good answer for a person" and only an instrument for "is this code correct" (Fig 2 :37-38). **Refuter:** both frames derive their items from one generator or one corpus, so their agreement is one pool talking.

### T5 Depth versus absence

A failure on a novel family is not yet a capability verdict. Distribute independent searchers at the same reach and watch the trend. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* If residual trends down with more independent search, the remedy was search, not a new primitive. **Refuter:** independent search at the same reach reduces a previously failed family to zero with no import, showing the absence verdict was a budget error.

### T6 The middle link: instrument vocabulary and objective

Before scoring transfer, ask whether the system can state what the new situation affords and what would count as progress there. Fig 3 :25-26: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 3 :22-23 warns that *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can"*: a transfer score taken below a broken vocabulary link is a reading of nothing. **Refuter:** a system at ceiling on substituted habitats with no vocabulary/objective link on multiple probes.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named system is or is not generally capable. No system was tested.
- Not that a score, however broad, measures capability. Breadth is a frame-internal coverage quantity (Fig 1 :24-27).
- Not that adding more benchmark items from the same family adds evidence. Closure is idempotent (Fig 8 :32).
- Not that the framework can name the primitive a system lacks. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- Not that human agreement is the ground. It is a filter for where to look, never a witness.
- Not a theory of everything. The residual stays a union compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, that *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and that *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the discriminator is the dated exposure audit plus the substituted habitat plus an independent anchor. Run T1 through T6 on a named system with the refuters pre-registered, and the verdict becomes a ground reading. Until then it remains `candidate`.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape, not a name: a constraint on what a separating observation must look like. The six designs are not validated instruments; they are specifications with refuters. The essays themselves warn that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* This folder is such a simulated output. It records the lineage so a reader can locate the error. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A broad benchmark score cannot tell a generally capable system from a specialist trained on familiar tasks, because both can produce the same reading. The Tether's answer is a shape for a discriminator, not a number: hold the system fixed and substitute its habitat; report the prediction error per slot and its trend rather than an average; date every item's exposure so an import cannot pass as a derivation; use independent anchors instead of an agreeing panel; separate "not found at this budget" from "not reachable"; and instrument whether the system can say what progress would even mean in the new situation. Each of those six tests carries a failure condition written in advance. If an agent-only change moves the ground, or if averaging loses no information, or if exposure changes nothing, the shape is refuted. What is offered is the constraint any separating test must satisfy, not a verdict about any system, and not a claim that the framework can name the missing primitive. The stop is honest until a ground reading runs the battery.
