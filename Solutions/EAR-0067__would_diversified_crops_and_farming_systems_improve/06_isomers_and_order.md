# Isomers, order, and the shape of the comparison

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the analogue. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

Two supporting questions remain: (1) are two diversified packages with the same list of practices different substances or the same substance under two names, and (2) what order and bond does the comparison have, and what happens to it when the horizon changes?

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are two diversified packages isomers?

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Package A: a maize-soybean-wheat rotation with a red clover cover crop under reduced tillage. Package B: the same four species and the same tillage, but wheat underseeded with clover in a different position in the sequence, so the clover occupies a different temporal niche. Same atoms, different order. Under the ordered bond `→` these are different terms: Operators :30, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*, with the test at Operators :33, *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."* Gaudin et al. 2015, opened this session, report that the *"effects on corn were mostly associated with the temporal niche provided by small grains for underseeded red clover or alfalfa"*, which is exactly an arrangement effect invisible in a species list.

So the answer is: yes, under Fig 12 :117, provided the practitioner records the arrangement. Both packages are members of the same **functional group**, "diverse rotation with a legume", where *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* A study that records only species richness has recorded the ingredients and not the molecule, which is Fig 12 :5.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if two packages with identical atom sets and different bond arrangements behave identically across a second shock distribution, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands alone.

### Q2 — What order and symmetry does "offset" have?

Operators table :49: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**

Operators table :51: **"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"**

Operators table :52: **"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"**

`⋛` is one of the four ordered bonds. The test is whether swapping operands changes the meaning. "Resilience gain offsets yield loss" and "yield loss offsets resilience gain" are not the same claim: the first is a claim about a practice worth adopting, the second is a claim about a practice to avoid. So the comparison is ordered, and a net score that treats the two directions as interchangeable is a notation error.

Comparison is also a **goal**, not an ingredient. A vocabulary with only practice names can say what a farm contains and cannot state what would count as getting somewhere. This is the missing middle link of Fig 3, named at Fig 12 :115: *"having terms for what a situation affords was taken for a dictionary and it is a grammar."* The question supplies a goal in ordinary language, "offset the trade-offs", but has not bound an instrument to it.

### Q3 — Where does time enter?

Fig 12 :89, the comparison test: **"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"** The threshold itself is a relation between two quantities at one level. Time is not a new bond. It is a parameter on the ordered bonds: `→` fixes what must happen before what, and `⋛` compares at a stated horizon.

Operators :68: **"A relation that fails all seven is not an operator you are missing; it is a claim that has not been made yet."**

So "short-term yield penalty, long-term profitability gain" is not an eighth bond and not a new primitive. It is two comparisons at different horizons. Raveloaritiana and Wanger 2026, opened this session, report win-win yield-service outcomes *"during the first 40 years"* and then lose-yield outcomes as most likely. Declaring the horizon is part of stating the operands; omitting it is the flat-form error Fig 12 :14 names: *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not."*

### Q4 — Can a multi-year package become an atom for a regional claim?

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**

A rotation that has been measured over a 31-year trial (Gaudin et al. 2015) is settled at the field level and can serve as an operand for a regional comparison. But the transform is lossy: Fig 4 :30, *"take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The round trip gap R_T recorded in `01` §6 is the qualitative measure of what a regional headline cannot hold. A settled field result licenses a regional claim only after the gap is stated.

### Q5 — Does the union have a bond?

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

The three conditions (shock response, yield, cost) overlap. The intersection where a practice raises a service and lowers profit is a specific compound, not a harder case of either. It needs its own loop. The bond that joins the conditions is not a symmetric `+`: it is the ordered `⋛` applied within each currency, plus a sequence `→` in time. A single `+` over all three would be the flat-form collapse.

## CONCLUSION

Two diversified packages with the same ingredients in a different order are **isomers** under Fig 12 :117, and members of one **functional group** under Fig 12 :123. "Offset" is an ordered threshold comparison over two quantities, not a symmetric join and not an addition of currencies. Time is a parameter on the ordered bonds, not a new operator. A multi-year field result can become an atom for a regional claim, but only with the round trip gap written down. The union of conditions has no single bond that clears it; each intersection needs its own measurement.

This framing rescues what is checkable in the ordinary question without accepting its net figure: the arrangement of a package matters, the direction of the comparison matters, and the horizon matters. None of those requires a new primitive or a summable net score.
