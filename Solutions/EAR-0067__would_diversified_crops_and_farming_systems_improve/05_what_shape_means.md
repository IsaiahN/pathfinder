## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0067 the shape is not a percentage and not a recommended practice. It is four constraints:

1. the residual is per slot (crop, field, season, region, shock), never a global average;
2. a change in diversity changes contact between a package and an enumerated habitat, not a property carried by the package;
3. the currencies do not add, so "offset" needs a declared currency and horizon before it has the right operand type;
4. the three conditions form a union that must be unbundled before any search.

`SPACE(???)TIME` is a shape in the exemplar. Here the corresponding shape is `Δshock(s) ⋛ [Δyield(s) + Δcost(s)]` with the bracket explicitly marked as not composable until bound. Writing the bracket is binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, as the formula's Direction rule requires.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Also the bundle warning: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is F5 in `01`.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is what the outreach email in `02` does.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Here the stop is a declared `gap`, not a finished yes or no.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."*

For this question, saying "intercropping" or "agroforestry" is not inventing a new primitive. Those practices are already in the agriculture frame. Naming one as *the* answer would be a binding onto a held atom, and it needs provenance. What the figures refuse is the standing superlative, not the mention of a practice.

## 4. Why a net number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Yield variance is that kind of reading. A diversified and a simplified package can show similar yield variance for different reasons: true absorption of a shock, simple portfolio averaging, a favourable season, or a practice that merely moved the measurement window. Gaudin et al. 2015, opened this session, say stability and resilience are *"often used interchangeably to describe fluctuations in final crop yields after perturbance"* and explicitly decline to establish the dynamics of resilience. Handayani et al. 2026 say soil health, biodiversity, yield, income, or diet improvements *"do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks."*

What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a net number. Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

## 5. Q and A, answered

**What licenses a universal diversification percentage?** Nothing in the framework. The figures return a shape and a refuter. A percentage is licensed only by a ground settling a minted term at named slots, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the percentage is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The world sources report 20 to 38 percent gains (Sridhar et al. 2026), a neutral mean effect (Tamburini et al. 2020), and long-term profitability gains with no significant yield effect (Raveloaritiana and Wanger 2026). Those are filters for where to spend a trial, not witnesses for a standing number (Fig 9 :60).

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, if "yes, diversification pays" is delivered as a verdict. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and a number is a name at a slot. The correct form keeps the slot, the shock, the horizon, and the currency explicit, and records the net verdict as `gap` until an instrument closes it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any practice. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Crop species, rotations, intercropping, and tillage are already among the atoms of the agriculture frame. Naming one is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in a naive reading is calling that binding an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 :22 says *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*, and this binding adds none. The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted: the reason a yield reading cannot name the resilience source is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected names to agree that diversification works is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, and lets an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What shape means for this specific question, in one paragraph

A shape here is the set of constraints any defensible answer must satisfy: the comparison must be indexed per crop, field, shock, and season; "resilience" must be tied to a shock-discriminating measurement and not to yield variance alone; yield, money, and labour must stay in separate currencies until a declared price and horizon bind them; and the three conditions must be unbundled with their intersections given their own loop. The shape does not say which practice to adopt, does not give a percentage, and does not return a global yes or no. It says what a checkable answer would have to look like, and it names the instrument whose absence makes the literal question a `gap` at this budget.
