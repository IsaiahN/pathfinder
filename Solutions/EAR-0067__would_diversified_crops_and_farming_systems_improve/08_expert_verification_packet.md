# Expert verification packet — the Tether on diversification and resilience

**What this is:** a claim in a form an agronomist, ecologist, or agricultural economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the literal net yes or no. Not `grounded`.** No field measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A net claim that diversified crops and farming systems improve resilience *enough to offset* yield or cost trade-offs is not composable as written. The comparison operator is a threshold relation between two quantities, while a shock-response trajectory, a mass of grain, and a sum of money are different currencies that do not add. The defensible residue is a shape: diversification is a maintained change of contact, indexed per crop, field, season, region, and shock, whose contribution is relative to a residual; the farming seat can introduce actors and cannot subtract the habitat; yield variance is a proxy whose source is undiscriminated; and the three conditions form a union that must be unbundled before search. The literal net verdict is a `gap`, unreached at this budget, with three distinct routes named for closing it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal, checkable by reading the figure text

- **Statement:** cross-currency netting is the named notation error, and the comparison bond is ordered.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125, *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :130, *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Operators :49, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Operators :52, *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*.
- **Check:** does the figure set define comparison over two quantities and forbid summing currencies, as quoted?
- **Refuter:** produce a verbatim figure line that licenses adding the three currencies, or a measurement convention that binds them in one currency and holds across a second environment-type distribution. If one exists, this sub-claim fails.

### C2 — field-facing, checkable against the trial and review literature

- **Statement:** yield variance does not establish shock resilience, and the sign of a diversification effect is per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :27, *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What to check:** Gaudin et al. 2015, *PLOS One* 10(2):e0113261, write that *"stability and resilience are often used interchangeably to describe fluctuations in final crop yields after perturbance"* and state *"Our study does not investigate systems response to stress during crop development..."* They also report that *"soybean and corn had opposite responses to tillage in hot and dry years."* Handayani et al. 2026, *AIMS Agriculture and Food* 11(4):947-973, state that *"improvements in soil health, biodiversity, yield, income, or diet do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks."* Are those characterisations faithful?
- **Refuter:** a direct shock-response measure, independent of yield proxies, showing that diversification moves absorption or recovery at the same slots, with rank invariance under a second shock type. If it exists and preserves ranks, the per-slot-plus-gap reading is too weak and should be replaced by a standing yes.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation about the object of the claim

- **Statement:** the three conditions form a union that must be unbundled, and the effect flips with horizon.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** Tamburini et al. 2020 report neutral mean yield, *"lnRR = 0.01; 95% confidence interval (CI) = −0.12 to 0.14"*, with positive ecosystem services. Sridhar et al. 2026 report *"average yields that are 20-38% better than monocropping"* with context moderation. Raveloaritiana and Wanger 2026 report *"no significant effects on crop yield, pest control, and climate regulation"* with long-term profitability gains, and win-win outcomes *"during the first 40 years"* followed by lose-yield outcomes. Are these different estimands and horizons rather than three measurements of one quantity?
- **Refuter:** a single genuinely low-overlap source that measures shock response, yield, and cost in one currency at one horizon and finds a partition with a single binding lever. This would break the union verdict.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a yes or no in the abstract and not a universal diversification percentage.
- Not a ranking of practices that are most resilient. Ranks flip with crop, shock, horizon, and environment type.
- Not that diversification fails. The world sources report positive services and gains under stated conditions.
- Not that yield variance is resilience, and not that soil health, income, or diet improvements demonstrate shock capacity.
- Not a derivation of agriculture from the six-term physical substrate. That mapping was retracted and kept only as a `general` analogue.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken in this derivation, and this packet cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating shock instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does the diversified package change loss magnitude and recovery time for a defined shock, independently of yield level?
2. **Threshold test:** at a declared horizon and in a declared currency, does the shock-response gain exceed the yield and cost loss at the same slots?
3. **Rank-invariance test:** does the ordering hold under a second environment-type distribution and a second shock type?
4. **Permanence test:** does the gain persist without continued maintenance?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :125 and :130, Operators :49 and :52, Fig 3 :27, Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54/:55/:60. Nine short passages.
2. Check C2 against the Gaudin trial and the Handayani review: are the characterisations faithful?
3. Name one instrument that would separate absorption, recovery, and portfolio averaging for a defined shock, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
