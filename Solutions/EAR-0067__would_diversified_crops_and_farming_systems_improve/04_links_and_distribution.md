## How the world sources bear on the question

1. **The three estimands conflict at the level of a pooled number, not at the level of a verdict.** Tamburini reports a neutral mean yield effect with positive services; Sridhar reports 20 to 38 percent gains with context moderation; Raveloaritiana reports long-term profitability gains with no significant yield effect. These are different estimands, service sets, and horizons. They are recorded as a re-census input (F5), not averaged into a verdict.
2. **The objective link is the weak point.** Handayani et al. 2026 and Gaudin et al. 2015 both say, from different fields and designs, that intermediate outcomes such as yield variance, soil health, and biodiversity do not by themselves demonstrate a shock-response capacity. This is the lowest-overlap corroboration of Fig 3 :27.
3. **Timescale is a parameter on the comparison.** Raveloaritiana and Wanger 2026 show a win-win yield-service region followed by a lose-yield region. A "yes" or "no" that omits the horizon is underdescribed.
4. **Per-slot signs differ.** Gaudin et al. 2015 find opposite corn and soybean responses to tillage under hot and dry conditions. A global percentage hides this, which is Fig 1 :22 in a real field trial.
