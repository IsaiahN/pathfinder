# The Tether's answer: would diversification pay for itself in resilience?

**Mode: `general` on the bare shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the literal net yes or no. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A grower compares a diversified package with a simplified one and asks whether the resilience gain is large enough to pay for yield and cost losses. There are three per-slot gaps, not one: shock response, yield, and money and labour. Each is indexed by crop, field, season, region, and shock type. The three conditions overlap, so this is a union compound: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own instrument.

## 2. The shape

1. **The net figure is refused as written.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* The comparison bond is *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators :49). A recovery trajectory, a mass of grain, and a sum of money are not one quantity. A net score needs a declared currency and horizon before it has the right operand type.
2. **Success is per-slot maintained contact, not a carried property.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* No crop or package wins in general.
3. **Diversification is introduction, not subtraction, and it is permanent work.** Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :63: *"a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state"* The seat can add a crop or a habitat strip and cannot subtract the habitat. The added actors bring cascades the seat did not author, so a practice must carry a refusal condition.
4. **The objective link is broken.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Yield variance is a proxy for resilience, not a measure of it. Handayani et al. 2026 and Gaudin et al. 2015 say, from different fields, that intermediate outcomes do not establish the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to a defined shock. A source-discriminating instrument is owed.
5. **Unbundle before searching.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Shock response, yield, and cost each get their own loop; their intersections get their own loop. Effect sizes choose where to spend work and are never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
6. **The literal verdict is a `gap`, unreached at this budget.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* SEARCHED, INWARD, and OUTWARD remain distinct: search the existing direct-evidence literature; sharpen the yield-stability instrument already returning a reading; import a frame whose closure predicts the shape, with provenance and a shadow test.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a yes or a no in the abstract, and not a universal diversification percentage.
- Not a ranking of crops or practices that are "most resilient". Ranks flip with environment type, crop, shock, and horizon.
- Not that diversification fails. The world sources report positive services, neutral or positive yield in different estimands, and long-term profitability gains (Tamburini et al. 2020, Sridhar et al. 2026, Raveloaritiana and Wanger 2026). The figures withhold a verdict, not a recommendation.
- Not that yield variance is resilience. It is a reading whose source is undiscriminated.
- Not a derivation of agriculture from the six-term physical substrate. That mapping was retracted as identity in `01` §8 and kept only as a `general` recursion analogue.
- Not grounded. No field measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A direct shock discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved what argument could not. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the diversified package change loss magnitude and recovery time for a defined shock, independently of yield level? An instrument that separates absorption, recovery, and adaptation would name the resilience term.
- **Threshold test:** at a declared horizon and in a declared currency, does the shock-response gain exceed the yield and cost loss, at the same slots?
- **Rank-invariance test:** does the ordering of packages hold under a second environment-type distribution and a second shock type? If it flips, the standing claim fails and the per-slot claim stands.
- **Permanence test:** does the practice keep its gain without continued maintenance? If it must be re-installed every season, it is work, not a capital improvement.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §3: a figure line licensing cross-currency addition; a direct instrument settling the comparison; rank invariance; a removal that persists without maintenance; a partition source; an agent-only change that moves the ground.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The relevant six-term set and the diversification vocabulary are visible guesses, not nature's own list. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README warns that indiscernibility is not identity. A global yes or no would be a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether diversification is worth it in general. It says that the question as asked cannot be answered yet, because resilience, yield, and cost are different currencies and "offset" asks for a sum the figures forbid. Diversification is a maintained change of contact: a farming seat can introduce species and practices and cannot subtract the habitat, so adaptation is permanent work, and its payoff is per crop, per shock, per season, not a standing winner. Yield variance is a proxy whose source a yield reading cannot discriminate, so a direct shock instrument is owed before anyone claims a resilience gain. What the framework offers is the shape any checkable answer must have, the six refuters written in advance, and the three routes, SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD, that could close the gap. The net verdict stops as unreached at this budget, and that stop is a verdict, not a silence.
