## QUESTION

Should countries restrict foreign investment or technology transfers in industries tied to national security? Restated as an effect: for sector-slot `s`, does action `a` (admit, condition, refuse) reduce `R_s = |Γ_s(b,a) - o'_s|`, the gap between predicted and observed delivery under adversarial pressure, with `R` indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19)? Option A, refuse, leads to a smaller domestic closure and a substituted habitat. Option B, admit with conditions, leads to an imported atom and a dependency channel. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The yes or no question is refused as a bundled union. The figure-derived shape is: capability is sector-and-habitat property, so contact changes must be measured; import is the only operator that moves a closure, so a sealed sector cannot manufacture a missing atom, while an import can be the wrong atom when it creates a capturable channel; the boundary is a filter with a direction, not a wall; isolation substitutes a habitat and a synthetic solve proves wiring, not capability; and a security designation cannot be its own anchor. The directional default is conditional admission where the import closes `R_s`, refusal where the dependency moves the anchor, both revisable at a measured ground. D1, D2 and D3 are triangulated; D4 is single-source and untested forward; the qualifying sectors and the net empirical sign are a declared `gap`. Mode `general` and `specified`, never `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, names the domains and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than hidden. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers with a categorical policy slogan instead of a shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the union and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No atom is deleted when a finding fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded with provenance (Fig 10 :62-64), so a later reader can locate the error. A sent claim can be superseded without withdrawing any primitive.
- **reversed by:** the pre-registered falsifiers, in particular a sector whose measured capability rises under strictly reduced contact; a new capability atom from purely internal composition; a designation whose anchor moves with the designator; one lever clearing all three union conditions; or a figure re-census that names a qualifying sector and closes `R_s` at the ground. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes capability from an untested dependency would move the empirical gap into a grounded verdict. Agreement, prestige and low rejection rates are not such instruments.
