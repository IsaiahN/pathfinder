## What the wider literature actually bears on

Separated by evidence pool so that one pool is not counted as several witnesses.

**Pool A, investment screening economics and accounting (one witness).** Godsell 2026 measures a real cost channel: post-FINSA investment inefficiency rises in national security industries, and the author's own interpretation is that *"regulators misidentify politically motivated investors and exclude financially motivated investors."* Chen, Naranjo and Tang 2024 (SSRN abstract read via search extract, full text not opened) reports *"detrimental productivity, operating efficiency, and innovation costs on U.S. critical industry firms."* These two agree, but they are the same policy-economics pool, so they count as one corroboration of the wrong-atom rule (Fig 6 :24), not two.

**Pool B, public audit (a separate witness).** GAO 2024 documents the channel failure: agreements quadrupled, enforcement decisions undocumented, no documented process for periodic relevance review. This corroborates Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*

**Pool C, international relations and security studies (low overlap, mechanism witnesses).** Farrell and Newman 2019 supplies the chokepoint and panopticon effects: an import can create a channel the foreign frame can close. Pham and Scholten 2026 supplies the isolation result: reshoring and broad controls dismantle the interdependencies that make aggression costly. Both corroborate the mechanism shape, not the policy verdict. Their agreement with the figures is not a vote.

**Pool D, law (a separate witness for the designation problem).** Wang, Liu and Dong 2025 documents expanded scope and unpredictable review time, and reciprocal Chinese restrictions. That is the contact cascade of Fig 11 :52, *"And contact cascades,"* observed in a different field.

**Policy-descriptive fact, not a witness.** UNCTAD reports that roughly 40 percent of restrictive measures in 2025 were national security screening and that only about 1.6 percent of screened projects were prohibited. This tells us what states do. By Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict,"* it is a budget and prevalence filter, not evidence for or against the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52:** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the union shape and its refuters. Send the links only after a verdict on the first stage is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :35:** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask a screening community to confirm its own prior.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is high, so include at least one low-overlap frame (security studies or accounting), not only foreign investment specialists.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the framework.
- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, not an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union shape and the four refuters; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record that answer; (4) only then, if invited, the three core links, with the note that the figures and the sources, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual and the pre-registered refuter, ask what would count as a failure. No theory, no links to the framework.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule, and the three world sources. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded, so later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes a refuter and states a mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a shape, not a yes or no. If a recipient reads it as "the framework says do not restrict," the round trip has failed and the gap R_T in `11_audit.md` has been ignored. That is the dead-reckoning error of Fig 4 :30.
