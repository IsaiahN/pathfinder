# The Tether's answer on ORI-0096

**Mode: `specified` on the three per-slot verdicts, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the specific alien implementations. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three slots: genetic molecules, cellular boundaries, energy systems. Restated as an effect, each slot asks what a lineage needs to keep contact with a ground across generations, and what can be said about the identity of the filler before a second, independent sample of life has been read. It is a union compound, not one question with a yes or no answer. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Three verdicts are owed, kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Heredity: the function is required, the polymer is not.** A hereditary slot needs variation that survives selection. Fig 11 :36: "Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." The chemistry of the medium is an implementation. The framework returns a kind of thing, not a name: Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

2. **Boundary: a maintained difference, not necessarily a lipid bilayer.** Two regions cannot bond directly, so a structure must sit between them. Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Fatty-acid vesicles and membraneless condensates already show more than one filler on Earth, so the bilayer is one implementation among several.

3. **Energy: a spendable gradient, not sunlight specifically.** Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent." Energy alone is directionless; a gradient supplies directedness. Chemosynthesis shows that even on Earth the most visible implementation (sunlight) is not the only one.

4. **Different has three regimes.** Same atoms, different arrangement is an isomer (Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."). Different atoms is an import, which moves the wall only on contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). A different implementation of the same function with the same chemistry is a functional-group variant. Only the first regime is reachable inside our closure; the other two need either a second sample or an Earth-side example. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

5. **The specific fillers are a declared gap.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." A search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."). The gap is of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not the underivable-in-principle kind.

6. **Cost has two currencies.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." A catalogue of hypothetical biochemistries is cheap in bits and expensive in actions to settle; a shape claim is compact but still needs an instrument to close.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that alien life must be silicon-based, arsenic-based, ammonia-based or use XNA. Those are candidate fillers, not outputs of the framework.
- Not that the six-term substrate (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network) is nature's own list. It is a visible guess; another sensorium's list need not be those six.
- Not that a biosignature reading names its molecular source. An instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; the discriminator is missing, and naming the source would be a metamerism error.
- Not that a seventh primitive is owed. The three slots compose from the six already held; none requires a new atom.
- Not that Earth-only agreement corroborates the answer. All world sources read here are Earth-based or framework-based, one pool under different names.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing in this answer has been settled by a second sample or a source-discriminating instrument.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A second, independent sample of life is the only ground that can fill the three slots. Failing that, a source-discriminating instrument would move the verdict on individual slots.

- A biosignature instrument that distinguishes which molecular implementation produced a reading would close the source problem on the hereditary slot without naming the source from the reading alone.
- A reproduced boundary maintained under selection in a second sample would settle the compartment slot.
- A flow that pays, measured in an independent biochemistry, would settle the energy slot.
- Standing falsifiers from the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5: derive a seventh term; show a lineage persists with no heritable variation, no maintained boundary difference or no spendable gradient; show the three slots collapse to one lever; recover a molecular source from a reading without a second sample; show a different-atom biochemistry composes inside our closure without import.

Agreement is not any of these. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The framework narrows the shape of any candidate answer and stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish. It records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The specific alien molecules stay a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind until a second closure is read. Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides." This is a simulation, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name an alien genetic molecule, membrane or metabolism, and it does not claim Earth's chemistry is necessary. It says the question is three conditions in one system, not one question with a yes or no answer. What can be said before a second sample is read is a shape: a hereditary slot needs variation that survives selection but the polymer is not named; a boundary slot needs a maintained difference but the lipid bilayer is one implementation; an energy slot needs a difference that can be spent but sunlight is one implementation. A different chemistry with the same atoms in a different arrangement is an isomer and needs no new primitive; a different chemistry with different atoms is an import and needs a second, differing closure we have not read. The specific alien fillers are a declared gap of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, which is a verdict rather than a silence. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop until the ground, a second independent sample or a source-discriminating instrument, settles it.
