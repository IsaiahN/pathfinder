## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not supply an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
2. **Verifier non-access is structural.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict on the residual converts a potential outside frame into a frame insider.
3. **Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay, 2026-11-19): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." Prestige can give a claim a budget to be tried. It cannot settle the claim.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public and opt-in.

## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks whether three slots can be filled by other things than Earth uses: a hereditary medium, a compartment boundary, and an energy coupling. Stated as an effect: what does a lineage need in each slot to keep contact with a ground across generations, and what can be said about the identity of the filler before any alien ground is read? Option A says Earth's molecules are necessary. Option B says the functions are required and the specific molecules are bindings settled only by a ground. A sends a verdict in `general` mode with no second sample; B sends a shape, a refuter, and an honest stop. The ordinary term bound for the loop is "a hereditary function, a maintained difference, and a spendable gradient, each settled against a ground." It accrues a residual like any other term.
