# Outreach draft - check the shape first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 has been read and accepted as written. A two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about "alien biochemistry", before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The question "could alien life use different genetic molecules, cellular boundaries, or energy systems?" is three conditions in one system, not one question with a yes or no answer. What the framework can say before any alien sample is read is a shape, not a name:
>
> 1. A hereditary slot needs variation that survives selection, but the framework does not name the polymer. The specific polymer is a binding from Earth's one sample, not a requirement.
> 2. A boundary slot needs a maintained difference between inside and outside, but the bilayer is one implementation. Fatty-acid vesicles and membraneless condensates already show more than one filler on Earth.
> 3. An energy slot needs a difference that can be spent. Sunlight is one implementation; chemosynthesis shows another already exists here.
>
> The specific alien molecules are a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. That is a verdict, not a silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that alien life must be silicon-based, or arsenic-based, or use XNA. Those are candidate fillers, not outputs. The framework's own limit is that it gives "a kind of thing, never the answer itself." I also am not claiming that the six-term substrate (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network) is nature's own list. It is a visible guess, and another sensory system's list need not be those six.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. deriving any one of the three required functions away, so a lineage can persist with no heritable variation, no maintained boundary, or no spendable gradient;
> 2. deriving a necessary molecular primitive from the six-term set (a seventh term);
> 3. showing the three slots are governed by one lever, so the union collapses;
> 4. showing a biosignature instrument can recover the molecular source without a second sample;
> 5. showing a different-atom biochemistry composes inside our closure without an import from a differing frame.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source material is below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Figure SVGs and formula: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - NASA Astrobiology public Q&A on "life as we don't know it": https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/ask-an-astrobiologist/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - Isaiah
