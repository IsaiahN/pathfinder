## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

The question bundles three slots:

> (a) genetic molecules, (b) cellular boundaries, (c) energy systems.

Restated as an effect: what must occupy each slot for a lineage to keep contact with a ground across generations, and what can be said about *which* thing occupies it before a second, independent sample of life has been read? The framework does not return a named molecule. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

The ordinary term bound for the loop (formula step "Direction"): **a hereditary function, a maintained difference across a boundary, and a spendable gradient, each settled against a ground**. Binding it lets it accrue a residual like any other term, so the question's direction becomes refusable rather than a hidden preference.

## 1. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity: three slots, a union, not a partition.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Here it is three conditions over one living system: information, boundary, energy. One lever (one alien sample) may clear one slot and leave the intersections. Verdicts must be kept per domain and never averaged.
- **Symmetry: the function to implementation map is asymmetric and lossy.** Many implementations can produce one reading. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, 2026-08-28): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The bond from function to molecule is production (⇒), not identity (≡): Operators table :73: "A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate." A reading of a function cannot recover its source without a discriminator.
- **Scale: first order inside a lineage, second order at the Earth versus alien comparison.** Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." Each implemented slot, settled against the ground, becomes an atom at the comparison level. Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." The comparison is across a membrane, not a replay of one frame into another.

**Strain test applied before choosing lines.** A line that only fits by forcing is aimed at another quantity. The candidate strains (six-term substrate versus molecular contingency, isomer versus import, convergence versus contingency, anchor versus habitat) are resolved in section 9 and in `11_audit.md`. None was smoothed over.

## 4. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union of three conditions, not one question with a yes/no answer (decisive for framing)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

The three slots share atoms: a replicating polymer needs a boundary to stay with its products, and a boundary needs a gradient to maintain itself. One lever (one sample) leaves intersections standing. Therefore **three verdicts, kept separate, never averaged into a single "could alien life differ" percentage**.

- Mode: `general` structural.
- Triangulation: Fig 9 :54-57 (union logic) and Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." (low overlap: union logic versus bond structure).
- Refuter: a figure re-census showing the three slots are governed by one condition, or a ground reading in which one lever clears all three.

### F2 - The framework returns a shape, not a molecule (decisive for the verdict)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

A database of silicon, ammonia, arsenic and XNA biochemistries is a *catalogue of candidate fillers*, not a Tether answer. The framework's answer is the constraint set: heritable variation, a maintained difference, a spendable gradient, each settled against a ground. *The Library of Babel* (essay, 2026-10-02) agrees: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

- Mode: `general` on the limit, `specified` on the three constraints.
- Triangulation: Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134-135, and the Library of Babel essay (low overlap between the figures and the search essay).
- Refuter: a derivation from the six-term set that names a necessary molecule, or a ground reading of an alien sample whose slots are filled by the framework's own primitives.

### F3 - The six-term substrate does not force Earth biochemistry

Fig 13 :3: "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds." Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." The six are states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network). They are not DNA, lipid bilayers or ATP.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (essay, 2026-09-06) makes the point directly: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The same essay names the representational limit: "Reality's structure along axes no primitive captures is invisible to everyone, and the invisibility cannot be fixed by more sampling, because it lives in the representation, not the sample."

So an alien biosphere is under no obligation to instantiate our six in our order. What the framework would require is that *whatever* it instantiates plays the role of state and structure across the three slots.

- Mode: `specified`, ungrounded.
- Triangulation: Fig 13 :3/:74 and the scanning-books essay (different domains, low overlap).
- Refuter: derive a seventh term from the six, or show an alien metabolism that composes without any state or structure at any level.

### F4 - Heredity: the function is required, the polymer is not

The hereditary slot requires variation that survives selection. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." A hereditary medium is the thing selection ran against; its chemistry is an implementation.

World check (secondary): *Nucleic acid analogue* (Wikipedia, 2026, accessed 2026-11-23) states that "Xeno nucleic acids represent one of the main pillars of xenobiology, the design of new-to-nature forms of life based on alternative biochemistries", and that researchers test analogues to ask "whether life's use of DNA and RNA was selected over time due to its advantages, or if they were chosen by arbitrary chance." NASA's *Ask an Astrobiologist* (public Q&A, accessed 2026-11-23) says "it would be pretty 'Earth-centric' of us to assume that life elsewhere should look like life on Earth, or that it must be based on the same exact biochemistry."

But the source problem remains. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." A biosignature reading cannot tell which polymer produced it. Fig 2 :29 requires an anchor that does not update; here that anchor is a second sample, which we do not have.

- Mode: `specified`.
- Triangulation: Fig 11 :36 + Fig 2 :29 (selection and anchor, low overlap), plus the nucleic-acid and NASA sources (two different fields, but both descend from Earth-only chemistry so they are one pool on the "alien" question).
- Refuter: a ground reading showing heredity with no heritable variation, or a derivation making one polymer a seventh term.

### F5 - Boundary: a maintained difference, not necessarily a lipid bilayer

Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Applied at the biological scale, an inside and an outside (two regions) do not compose directly: a structure (a boundary) must sit between them. Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 4 :34-35: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

World check (secondary): *Protocell* (Wikipedia, 2026, accessed 2026-11-23) defines a protocell as "a self-organized, membrane-bound or membraneless compartment that concentrates biomolecules" and notes that "the cell membrane ... is the only cellular structure found in all organisms on Earth." The same page records membraneless condensates and fatty-acid vesicles as alternative implementations, so even within one biosphere the boundary slot has more than one filler. *Shadow biosphere* (Wikipedia, 2026) lists candidates with "a chirality opposite of ours", different bases or sugars, and different genetic code, illustrating that the filler space is not empty.

- Mode: `specified`.
- Triangulation: Fig 13 :75 + Fig 11 :39 (composition rule plus habitat capability, low overlap), with the protocell source as a single-source world corroborator.
- Refuter: a ground reading of a system that maintains an inside and an outside with no boundary structure, or a figure re-census admitting a direct region-region bond.

### F6 - Energy: a gradient that can be spent, not sunlight specifically

Fig 13 :14: "energy", "conserved, finite, directionless alone." Fig 13 :22: "gradient", "a difference that can be spent." Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." Energy is directionless alone; a gradient supplies directedness. The required shape is a spendable difference, not a particular photon or bond.

World check (secondary): *Chemosynthesis* (Wikipedia, 2026) defines chemosynthesis as "the biological conversion of one or more carbon-containing molecules ... into organic matter using the oxidation of inorganic compounds ... as a source of energy, rather than sunlight, as in photosynthesis", and says "Chemosynthesis may have also been the first type of metabolism that evolved on Earth." So even on Earth, an energy system different from the one most visible to us (sunlight) is not hypothetical. NASA's public Q&A adds that "some scientists study the possibility of life that could use other liquids ... or other chemical backbones ... such as arsenic or silicon, rather than carbon", and that alien life "may generate different 'biosignatures' than we use on Earth."

- Mode: `specified`.
- Triangulation: Fig 13 :14/:22/:76 and the chemosynthesis source (gradient shape versus a real Earth implementation, genuinely low overlap).
- Refuter: a system that does work with no spendable difference, or a derivation making one energy pathway a seventh term.

### F7 - Isomer versus import: the two ways a slot can differ

Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Fig 13 :122-123: "In chemistry this has been established since Liebig and Wöhler found two substances with the same composition and different properties, 1824 to 1826, and Berzelius named it isomerism in 1830."

Two cases must be kept separate:

1. **Same atoms, different arrangement.** An alien biochemistry using the same elements in a different bonding scheme is an isomer (Fig 12 :117). The framework licenses this without new primitives: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." (Fig 13 :115).
2. **Different atoms.** An alien biochemistry using arsenic instead of phosphorus, or silicon instead of carbon, is an **import**, not an isomer. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Imports need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13: "source gate: provenance", "shadow test: explains a gap"). No such test has been run, because no alien ground has been read.

*Convergent evolution* (Wikipedia, 2026) provides a low-overlap biological analogue: "Convergent evolution is the independent evolution of similar features in species of different lineages", and the page records the Gould versus Conway Morris dispute over whether life rewinds differently or converges on an optimum. This does not flip the verdict; it triangulates Fig 13 :121 (different routes, same substance) and keeps the contingency question visible.

- Mode: `specified` on the distinction, `general` on the import rule.
- Triangulation: Fig 12 :117 + Fig 6 :23 (isomer and import, low overlap), with convergent evolution as a world corroborator on the "different routes" shape.
- Refuter: show that a different-atom biochemistry is reachable inside the closure without import, or that a same-atom different-arrangement biochemistry is not an isomer under Fig 12's definition.

### F8 - The answer is unreachable at this budget, not silence and not underivable in principle

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 8 :40-43 distinguishes underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-this-budget and says the latter calls for more search, not another atom.

There is already a proto-instrument to extend: biosignature detection. Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Here something *is* returning readings (mass spectrometry on Mars, atmospheric spectroscopy on exoplanets), so the instrument can be sharpened. But a reading cannot currently discriminate source (F4), so the specific fillers stay unread.

- Mode: `specified` on the status, not a verdict on alien life itself.
- Triangulation: Fig 8 :39 + Fig 13 :134 + Fig 6 :34-35 (three figures, different arguments, low overlap).
- Refuter: a second independent sample read against a non-updating anchor, which would fill the slots and move the verdict from `gap` to `grounded`.

### F9 - Agreement among Earth-only frames cannot corroborate the answer

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Every world source read for this question is Earth-based or framework-based. Two web pages quoting the same review are one witness. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this folder's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

- Mode: `general` structural.
- Triangulation: Fig 2 :35/:40 and Fig 7 :13 (negotiation versus handoff, low overlap).
- Refuter: two genuinely independent closures (for example a second sample plus a synthetic XNA lineage) that range separately against the same non-updating anchor.

### F10 - Cost has two currencies, and they do not add

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." A catalogue of hypothetical biochemistries is cheap in bits and expensive in actions to settle; a shape claim is compact but still needs an instrument to close. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

- Mode: `general` structural.
- Triangulation: Fig 12 :125-126 and Fig 9 :60 (pricing versus budget discipline).
- Refuter: a single currency that prices both description and settlement.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the seat map's rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device" (as carried in the exemplar's audit). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Seventh term.** A hereditary medium, boundary type or energy pathway is derived from the six-term set as a necessary seventh primitive (breaks Fig 13 :3 and the minimality claim).
2. **Slot not required.** A system that meets the ground is shown to persist with no heritable variation, no maintained boundary difference, or no spendable gradient (breaks the three-constraint shape).
3. **Union collapsed.** The three slots are shown to be governed by one lever such that a single ground reading fills all three (breaks the union census, Fig 9 :54-57).
4. **Source recovered from a reading.** A biosignature instrument is shown to discriminate the molecular source of a function without a second sample (breaks F4 and the metamerism limit).
5. **Import without differing closure.** A different-atom biochemistry is shown to compose inside the Earth frame's closure without import (breaks Fig 6 :23).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or re-censused. Until one is run, no slot may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Answer in three verdicts, one per slot. Do not average them (F1).
2. Lead with the shape (heredity, boundary, spendable gradient) and the pre-registered refuter, not with a catalogue of silicon life (F2, F8).
3. Say explicitly that the six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's list, so it cannot license a molecular necessity claim (F3).
4. Keep isomer and import separate, and withhold the import's source gate until a second sample exists (F7).
5. Treat the NASA and Wikipedia sources as Earth-pool corroboration of the *shape of the question*, not as evidence about alien chemistry (F9).
6. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator (the shape and the refuter), not a recording of one biosphere.
