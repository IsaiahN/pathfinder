## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "could alien life use different genetic molecules, cellular boundaries, or energy systems?" is a union of three conditions in one system, not one question with a yes or no answer. Before any alien sample is read, the framework can say only a shape: a hereditary slot needs variation that survives selection but does not name the polymer; a boundary slot needs a maintained difference between inside and outside but the lipid bilayer is one implementation; an energy slot needs a difference that can be spent but sunlight is one implementation. A changed chemistry over the same atoms in a different arrangement is an isomer and needs no new primitive. A changed set of atoms is an import and needs contact with a closure that differs from ours, which we do not have. The specific alien fillers are a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, a verdict rather than a silence. What is offered is the shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop.

### C2: Isomer and import are different verdicts

- **Statement:** a same-atom, different-arrangement biochemistry is an isomer, reachable inside our closure by rearrangement. A different-atom biochemistry is an import, which requires a source gate, a shadow test and a frame whose closure differs. The two may not appear in one undifferentiated list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 8 :9-13: "source gate: provenance"; "shadow test: explains a gap."
- **Refuter:** show a different-atom biochemistry composing inside our closure without an import; or show a same-atom, different-arrangement system is not an isomer under the figure's definition. The arsenic-DNA claim (retracted 2025) is a useful caution here: a source gate was not passed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that alien life must be silicon-based, arsenic-based, ammonia-based, or use XNA. Those are candidate fillers, not outputs.
- Not that Earth's DNA, lipid bilayer or ATP sunlight pathway are necessary. Their status is one implementation, not a requirement.
- Not that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess; another sensorium's list need not be those six.
- Not that a reading names its source. A biosignature reading that cannot discriminate implementations demonstrates its own inadequacy, not a property of the source.
- Not that a seventh primitive is owed, and not an import of one. The three slots compose from atoms already held.
- Not that agreement among Earth-based sources corroborates the claim. All world sources read here are one pool.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by a ground.
