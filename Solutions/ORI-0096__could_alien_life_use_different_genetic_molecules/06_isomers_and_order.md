## RESIDUAL

The question asks whether three slots (hereditary medium, compartment boundary, energy system) can be filled by things other than Earth uses. The word "different" is doing two jobs that must be separated before any verdict:

1. Same atoms, different arrangement (an isomer under the bond rule).
2. Different atoms (an import from a closure that differs from ours).

A third job is often smuggled in: a different *implementation* of the same function using the same chemistry (a fatty-acid vesicle instead of a phospholipid bilayer, chemosynthesis instead of photosynthesis). That is a functional-group variant, not a new kind of thing. Collapsing all three into one list of "alternative biochemistries" is what the union logic forbids.

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Is a different genetic molecule an isomer or an import?

Two cases, kept separate.

**Case A, same atoms, different bond.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." An alien polymer built from the same elements in a different bonding scheme is reachable inside our closure by rearrangement. It needs no import and no new term. Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." The historical case is given in the figure itself: Fig 13 :122-123: "In chemistry this has been established since Liebig and Wöhler found two substances with the same composition and different properties, 1824 to 1826, and Berzelius named it isomerism in 1830."

**Case B, different atoms.** An alien polymer using arsenic instead of phosphorus, or silicon instead of carbon, is not an isomer. It is an import. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Imports carry a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap"). No alien closure has been read, so the gate cannot be passed. The case stays `gap`, not `denied`: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).

The world literature mixes these two cases in one list. *Hypothetical types of biochemistry* (Wikipedia, 2026, accessed 2026-11-23) states that "Several forms of biochemistry are agreed to be scientifically viable, but are not proven to exist at this time." That sentence spans both cases without distinguishing them. The framework requires the split before a verdict. Mode: `specified` on the distinction, `general` on the import rule.

### Q2. What is the order of the three slots?

Order by dependency, not by importance. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

There are two orders, and they must not be confused.

**Order of settlement (what an observer can close first).** Biosignature instruments already return readings (atmospheric spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, chiral excess). Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Something is returning readings, so the edge exists. The chain for each slot runs perception (a reading exists) before vocabulary (a chemistry-free term for the function) before objective (what would count as a filler) before planning. The break is at vocabulary. Fig 3 :25-26: "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." We have good instruments for DNA sequences and poor ones for a hereditary function realised by an unknown chemistry.

**Order of composition inside a lineage (what builds on what).** A spendable difference (Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent") composes with energy into a flow (Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow."). A flow maintains a boundary difference: two regions cannot bond directly, so a structure must sit between them (Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."). A maintained boundary lets a hereditary system keep its products near itself, so selection has something to run against: "Nothing was intelligent; selection ran" (Fig 11 :36). This is a dependency order of composition, not a claim that one slot is more important.

**Strain test.** An earlier reading put heredity first because DNA is what we sequence most easily. That confuses the order of our instruments with the order of composition. Re-census resolves it: perception order is about observers, composition order is about the system. Different objects, no strain after re-census (see `11_audit.md`).

### Q3. Are the three slots isomers of each other?

No. Isomerism requires a shared atom set under different bonds (Fig 12 :117). The three slots do not share an atom set; they are three different functions in one organism, so they form a union (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Within a single slot, different implementations can be isomers (same elements, different arrangement) or functional-group variants (Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."). The three functions together are a functional group only in the loose sense that each must be serviced; they are not isomers of one another.

## CONCLUSION

"Different" has three regimes. Same atoms, different arrangement is an isomer and is reachable inside our closure. Different atoms is an import and requires a second, differing closure that we have not read. A different implementation of the same function with the same chemistry is a functional-group variant, already visible on Earth (fatty-acid vesicles, chemosynthesis, XNA). The three slots form a union whose verdicts stay separate, and their composition order runs gradient, boundary, heredity while our instruments close them in the opposite order. The specific alien filler of any slot is unreachable at this budget, a verdict rather than a silence.
