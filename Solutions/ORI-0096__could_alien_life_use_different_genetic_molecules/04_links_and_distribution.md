## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **Shadow biosphere literature.** The Wikipedia page cites Cleland 2007 "Epistemological issues in the study of microbial life: alternative biospheres" (Studies in the History and Philosophy of Biological and Biomedical Sciences 38:847-861), Cleland and Copley 2005 "The possibility of alternative microbial life on Earth" (International Journal of Astrobiology 4(4):165-173), and Davies et al. 2009 "Signatures of a Shadow Biosphere" (Astrobiology 9(2):241-249). These were reached as citations inside the opened page; **the papers themselves were not opened**, so they are not quoted as read in the ledger. This is a philosophy-of-biology and astrobiology pool, low overlap with the framework essays.
2. **Synthetic alternative genetics.** The nucleic-acid analogue page cites Malyshev et al. 2014 (Nature) on an expanded genetic alphabet in *E. coli*, and Hirao's Ds-Px work at RIKEN. Reached as citations, not opened. Same caveat.
3. **Origin-of-life environments.** The protocell page cites Work-Ljungdahl pathway work at alkaline hydrothermal vents, hot-spring versus vent debates (including Jackson's criticisms), and wet-dry cycling. Reached as citations, not opened.
4. **Convergence versus contingency.** The convergent-evolution page gives the genuine low-overlap biological frame: the same function can be reached independently by different substrates. This triangulates Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance" without naming a molecule.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A catalogue of hypothetical biochemistries is a recording of one closure. Send the shape and the refuter first.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict on the residual has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the recipient would expect to see if an alien hereditary system used a different polymer, before giving them the framework.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." All sources here are Earth-based. A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13:** "agreement compounds, evidence does not." More Earth-only opinions do not move the answer toward a ground.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three shape claims and the five failure conditions; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing any framework; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A recipient who absorbs the theory stops being an outside frame, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. As written, stage 1 is a shape plus a declared gap, not an assertion that alien life did or did not differ. If a recipient reads it as a claim about a particular chemistry, the refuter has not been communicated clearly enough; repair the message before resending.

## Fetch failures and access limits, recorded honestly

- **Web search unavailable.** The search integration returned "No search results found" for every query tried on 2026-11-23, including "shadow biosphere" and "alternative genetic polymer XNA origin of life." Sources were therefore reached by direct fetch to known URLs. No source was cited from a search snippet alone.
- **PubMed blocked.** `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18053938/` (Cleland 2007) returned an HHS vulnerability-disclosure page rather than an abstract. It is not cited as read anywhere in this folder.
- **astrobiology.nasa.gov migration.** The legacy home and Ask-an-Astrobiologist URLs returned a migration notice pointing to science.nasa.gov/astrobiology. The notice itself was read and is recorded above.
- **Citation-only sources.** The Cleland, Davies, Malyshev and Jackson papers appear in the reference lists of pages that were opened. They are mentioned in this file as wider literature but are not quoted as read and are absent from `10_source_ledger.csv`.
