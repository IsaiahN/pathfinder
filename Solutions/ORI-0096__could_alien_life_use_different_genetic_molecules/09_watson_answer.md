## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question whether alien life could use different genetic molecules, cellular boundaries or energy systems. Option A: assert that Earth's molecules are necessary (or that a list of silicon, ammonia and XNA biochemistries answers the question). Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, refuse the specific molecular bindings, and declare a gap of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode verdict with no second sample, a catalogue that fails settlement, and a list that spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends three checkable per-domain verdicts, written refuters, and open slots until a ground settles them. The residual is the three unfilled slots, stated in effect terms as a hereditary function, a maintained difference across a boundary, and a spendable gradient, each settled against a ground.

- **Specific alien implementations.** No nameable anchor has been read. Union residue across D1 to D3. Status: `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, declared, not a domain awaiting a verdict by averaging.

- **Order by dependency.** Settle gradient, then boundary, then heredity in composition order (Fig 13 :76, Fig 13 :75, Fig 11 :36); observers close them in the reverse order because perception of a biosignature precedes vocabulary for a chemistry-free function (Fig 3 :20). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific answer is unreached. The question is a union of three conditions whose verdicts stay separate. A hereditary function, a maintained boundary difference and a spendable gradient are required in effect terms; DNA, the lipid bilayer and sunlight are implementations, not necessities. Same-atom different-arrangement alternatives are isomers reachable inside our closure; different-atom alternatives are imports that need a differing closure we have not read. The identity of any alien filler is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, a verdict rather than a silence. Nothing here is `grounded`. The result is `specified` on the three slot verdicts, `general` on the union structure and `gap` on the alien implementations.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states mode and refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a catalogue of candidate fillers instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices directly. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its five refuters. A second cost is mistaking an Earth-pool agreement for a second witness, which turns a convention into a fake verdict.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate-filler lists are removable without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them from the claim. A sent packet can be superseded by the corrected version, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The gap status is explicit, so a later second sample fills slots rather than overturning a settled verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A second independent sample read against a non-updating anchor would fill the three slots and move the verdict to `grounded`. A source-discriminating instrument for molecular implementations would close the source problem on a slot. A derivation of a seventh term, a system persisting with no heritable variation, no maintained boundary or no spendable gradient, a single lever filling all three slots, or a different-atom biochemistry composing inside our closure without import would each break the shape claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
