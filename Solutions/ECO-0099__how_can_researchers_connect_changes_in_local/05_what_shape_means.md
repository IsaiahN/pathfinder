# What "shape" means for ECO-0099

**Sources actually opened 2026-11-23:** the canonical local figures README (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`), `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, and four essays by Isaiah N at adventuresinml.substack.com: *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context, Subtext, & Pretext Matter*. The other four essays listed in the exemplar's `05` were not re-opened this session and are not quoted here. Every quote below comes from a source actually read.

---

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The local extract of Fig 13 adds: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the form of the bridge you can state before you can name it. It constrains any acceptable answer: the bridge must cross a directional membrane, carry a method rather than a site transcript, publish a measured round-trip gap, and refuse to name the local source from the global reading. It does not supply a scaling exponent, an index name, or a universal law. `R_T` per slot is a shape. "Biodiversity Intactness Index" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing and recurse by proximity. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the closest local vocabulary is interaction, contact, cascade, habitat; the closest global vocabulary is pattern, biome, realm, indicator.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A stage-1 packet should therefore describe what would be observed if the shape claim were true, not assert the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line becomes the starting place once the vocabulary is sufficient to compose the answer. That is the honest place to stop and record the lineage.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applied here: neither metabolic scaling theory nor the species-area relationship nor any intactness index is licensed by the figures. Each is a world candidate with its own evidence, and each must be recorded as a binding. The Nyström et al. 2026 measurement licenses a narrower claim: a particular class of averaged global models has a measured, nonzero transfer gap. That is a ground reading of a model class, not a derivation from the figures.

## 4. Metamerism: why the global reading cannot name its local source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". An instrument that detects a color "cannot comment on the source". The reading does not determine which physical arrangement produced it; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

The ecology parallel is direct. A global diversity or intactness reading is a coarse instrument over many local histories. Several different interaction changes can produce the same reading. The species-area literature in `04_links_and_distribution.md` already lists several candidate mechanisms for one pattern, and disputes whether the pattern is mechanistic or a sampling artifact. The low-overlap color-science source in `10_source_ledger.csv` states the same structure independently: different spectral power distributions yield an equivalent receptor response. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name read off the projection.

## 5. Binding the sought shape as an ordinary term

`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is a preference rather than a term that predicts. The remedy is a discipline: write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual.

For ECO-0099 the sought shape is written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 as an ordinary term: a method-carrying crossing with a measured R_T and two separately ranged frames. Because it is bound and predicting, it is refutable by the three pre-registered tests in section 4 of that file. A universal scaling law was *not* bound as the sought shape, precisely because no figure line governs one; that would be a preference smuggled in as a term.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous ecologists who agree with a scaling claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried against held-out sites.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
