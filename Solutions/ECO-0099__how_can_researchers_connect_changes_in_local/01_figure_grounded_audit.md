## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

R is a transform failure between two scales, not a shortage of observations.

- A local interaction change is measurable: a pollinator lost, a predator removed, a competitor introduced, a mutualism broken. Its currency is interaction strength, local abundance and vital rates at a site.
- A global pattern is measurable: an intactness map, a species-area curve, a latitudinal diversity gradient, a decline in ecosystem function. Its currency is a statistical regularity over many sites.
- What fails is the bridge. The local causal account does not carry to the pattern without losing the specificity that made it causal, and the pattern does not read back to a unique local cause.

**Arity:** at least two scales (local site, regional pool, global pattern), so the residual is over a crossing, not over one quantity. **Symmetry:** asymmetric. Local change produces global pattern (production, `⇒` in the Operators table); the global reading is a lossy projection that cannot name which local source produced it. **Scale:** first order at the site, second order at the metacommunity or biome, third order at the biosphere. **Slot indexing:** the residual lives per taxon, per interaction and per site, not in a global mean. "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19). "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." (Fig 1 :22).

What an answer would look like (direction bound as an ordinary term, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction"): a rule for carrying a *method* of local interaction measurement across the scale boundary, a measured round-trip gap for what the coarse description drops, and two low-overlap frames ranged separately against a ground that does not move. That is a shape. It is not a universal scaling exponent and not a named index.

## 3. Findings, each with verbatim lines and a refuter

### F1 - The crossing is a membrane with a direction, not a magnification of the same thing (decisive)

Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."
Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."

Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it."

A global pattern is not a larger local interaction. Its actors are biomes and taxa rather than a focal pair; its currency is statistical order rather than interaction strength; its ground is a global assessment rather than a field manipulation. So what connects them is a *generator*: a reapplicable method for how a local change propagates under changed context. A global indicator that averages study-level effects is a recording carried upward. Fig 4 :25 warns what that becomes: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 4 membrane rule and Fig 10 level rule are two figures with low conceptual overlap: one is about what crosses a boundary, the other about what a level is).
- **Refuter:** show a local interaction measurement that is itself the global pattern at another size, with the same actors, currency and ground and no transform between them. If that exists, F1 fails.

### F2 - Enumerate the contact habitat and follow the cascade outward (decisive on the local side)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :47: "relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"
Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

The global signature of a local interaction change is usually not one edge away. The figure's own ecological case is DDT reaching eagles through biomagnification, with the material residual three steps out. So a study that measures only the focal pair and the immediately adjacent species has scoped the habitat too tightly. The enumeration is read off the world, not composed from a list of "important" species. Fig 11 :50 adds the reason a fixed list fails: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 enumeration rule plus the world source on trophic cascades, which independently reports multi-step indirect effects and the openness of real systems).
- **Refuter:** a comprehensive set of cases in which the effect of a local interaction change stops at first-order contacts, with no measurable second or third-order cascade. That would break the contact-cascade claim.

### F3 - Keep the residual per slot; the global mean is where the live signal dies

Fig 1 :19 and :22 as quoted in section 0.
Fig 11 :39: "And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Ecosystem function is a property of the community and its habitat, not of a species in isolation (Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."). The slot is the place where contact is registered. A global indicator that reports one number per biome has already performed the aggregation that Fig 1 forbids for the purpose of locating the live residual. It may still be useful as a pattern; it cannot tell a researcher which interaction to fix.

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 slot rule and Fig 11 capability rule).
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that the failing slot cannot be located at finer resolution than a global average in any system, so per-slot reporting carries no information the mean lacks.

### F4 - The transform is asymmetric; a global reading cannot name its local source

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

The local-to-global direction is production: the local change produces a pattern that did not exist before at that scale. The reverse direction is a projection with loss. In color science this is metamerism: different spectral power distributions give the same receptor response, so the reading cannot comment on the source (*Metamerism (color)*, opened 2026-11-23: "Metamerism occurs because each type of cone responds to the cumulative energy from a broad range of wavelengths, so that different combinations of light across all wavelengths can produce an equivalent receptor response and the same tristimulus values or color sensation."). The species-area literature shows the same shape: one pattern, many candidate mechanisms, including a genuine dispute over whether the pattern is mechanistic or a sampling artifact.

Consequence for the question: researchers cannot infer the local mechanism from the global map. They must carry a method up and test it down. Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible." Once a local result is settled, Fig 12 :104 lets it become an atom for the next level, but the lineage, not the artifact, records which route produced it.

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 shape limit, Fig 12 ordered composition, plus the low-overlap color-science source).
- **Refuter:** show a global pattern that determines a unique local interaction mechanism at a given site, so the projection is invertible and the reading does name its source.

### F5 - The round trip must be measured; the world already supplies a measured instance of a nonzero gap

Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."
Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
Fig 4 :31: "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 6 names the quantity: `R_T = gap( x , (T_E ∘ T_A)(x) )`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Nyström et al. (2026, *Nature Ecology & Evolution*, opened 2026-11-23) provide an empirical instance. Their abstract reports "a challenging gap between effect size inference and local prediction"; models relying on "highly averaged fixed effects" have low predictive accuracy; ecologically structured models improve interpolation but "performance when extending predictions to other contexts remains low." In the results, the biome-realm model's Spearman correlation falls from 0.42 in sampled contexts to 0.13 under cross-study validation, with R² going negative. That is a measured R_T, not an argument about one.

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 4 round-trip law plus an independent peer-reviewed measurement; the two do not share an evidence pool).
- **Refuter:** a local interaction method carried to global scale and back with the same predictions recovered at both scales and R_T indistinguishable from zero across several biomes.

### F6 - The chain breaks at the objective, not at the data

Fig 3 :27 as quoted in section 1. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

The local frame already perceives interactions and the global frame already perceives patterns. What is missing is an instrument for "what would count as progress in carrying this local change to that pattern." Nyström et al. supply the diagnosis from inside ecology: validation has focused on model fit and parameter robustness, which is "important but not sufficient for assessing generality." A better-fitted global index is effort spent above the break.

- **Status:** single-source (Fig 3 is the only figure that names the dependency order; the world source corroborates the symptom but is not a figure line).
- **Refuter:** show an instrument that already states a local-to-global progress criterion and is in routine use, with the criterion measured rather than assumed.

### F7 - This is a union compound, and unbundling comes before searching

Fig 9 :54 and :55 as quoted in section 1. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Three conditions are being bundled in the question: (i) measuring changes in local interactions, (ii) predicting a global biodiversity pattern, (iii) predicting ecosystem function. They overlap but are not the same. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Tier scores, data availability and journal prestige are filters for what gets attempted; they are never witnesses for what is true.

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union/partition rule and Fig 9 filter/witness rule are one figure but two distinct operations; the world sources on ecosystem function versus diversity pattern supply the ecological unbundling).
- **Refuter:** show that the three conditions are one quantity under one instrument, so the overlap is total and union handling is unnecessary.

### F8 - Triangulate, do not consolidate, and range the frames separately against a ground

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."
Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."
Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

A field manipulation of a local interaction and an independent global compilation are two frames. Their agreement means something only if they draw on different evidence, and each is corrected separately by something neither can move: a measured local response, a held-out site, a counted outcome. Merging them into one consensus model, or selecting the global frame for agreeing with the local one, is the collapse Fig 2 :40 names: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 anchor/independence and Fig 8 union surplus).
- **Refuter:** show a single instrument that measures both the local mechanism and the global pattern without loss and without a shared evidence pool with any confirmer. If it exists, the need for separate ranging falls.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A global biodiversity or function pattern is shown to determine a unique local interaction mechanism at a given site, that is, the local-to-global projection is invertible and the reading names its source (breaks F4).
2. A local interaction method is carried to global scale and back with R_T indistinguishable from zero across several biomes, with the same predictions recovered at both scales (breaks F5 and Fig 4 :27-31).
3. Contact enumeration stops at first-order effects in a comprehensive set of cases, with no measurable second or third-order cascade (breaks F2 and Fig 11 :52).

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State the residual per slot before fitting any global indicator (F3).
2. Enumerate the habitat to two or more steps of contact around the changed interaction (F2).
3. Carry up a method, not a site transcript, and measure R_T by bringing it down at a held-out site (F1, F5). Fig 5 :26 names the bargain: "does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?" A new universal law must pay for itself.
4. Treat the question as a union compound and state the local-to-global objective as an ordinary term before searching (F7, F6).
5. Range a field frame and a global frame separately against a ground neither can move, and never pool their later agreement as corroboration (F8).
6. Keep the refuter in front of the links in any outreach (Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").

### Round-trip note

The condensation used in this folder is: enumerate per slot, carry the method up, measure R_T, triangulate against a fixed anchor. Sending that down to a concrete claim produces: a named index, a stated exponent or a universal law. Those are the three things the round trip drops, and they are recorded here rather than silently assumed. R_T was not measured numerically by this derivation; an empirical instance of a nonzero gap is cited in F5. Treating the condensation as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives. Local and global are separated by a directional membrane with a lossy transform; the local residual must be enumerated per slot through contact cascades; the bridge is a method whose round-trip gap is measured; and the frames are ranged separately against a ground that does not update. A specific universal law is a `gap` after census, not a silence. Strains after re-census: one literal Fig 13 mapping, named and refused in F9; no hidden strain remains.
