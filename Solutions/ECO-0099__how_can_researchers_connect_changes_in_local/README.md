## Effect statement of the question

The residual is not missing data. It is a transform problem. A researcher can measure a local interaction change (a pollinator lost, a predator removed, a competitor introduced) at one site, and can measure a global pattern (an intactness map, a diversity gradient, a decline in ecosystem function) at another scale. What is missing is a rule that carries the local mechanism up to the pattern and brings a prediction back down to a new site without pretending the two readings are the same thing. Option A: treat the global pattern as a scaled-up local interaction and search for one universal law. Option B: treat the crossing as a membrane with a direction, carry a method up, measure what the transform loses, and triangulate against an anchor that does not update. The figures decide for B.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside frame contributes (Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").
