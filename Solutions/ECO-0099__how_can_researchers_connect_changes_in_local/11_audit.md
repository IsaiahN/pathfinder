## Independent domain census (redone from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the transform failure between a changed local interaction and a coarse biodiversity or function pattern. Arity: two or more scales, so a crossing, not one quantity. Symmetry: asymmetric. Local change produces a condition that did not exist before (production `⇒`); the pattern is a lossy projection that cannot invert to a unique local source. Scale: site, biome or region, biosphere. The residual is per slot; a global mean is a different object from a live local failure.
- **Domain 1, local interaction frame.** Actors: species, relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency), conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain). Currency: interaction strength and local response. Anchor: field manipulation and measurement. Ground-visible.
- **Domain 2, scaling / membrane frame.** Actors: methods, models, indices. Currency: what crosses up and what is lost, R_T. Anchor: a round trip tested at a held-out site. Ground-visible only through the round trip.
- **Domain 3, global pattern frame.** Actors: biomes, realms, taxa, indicators. Currency: statistical order. Anchor: global observation and assessment, which does not move when a model prefers it. Ground-visible, different ground from Domain 1.
- **Domain 4, verification frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: name the per-slot residual before fitting a global indicator; state what counts as progress at the wider scale before planning the crossing; carry the method, not the recording (Fig 4 :22).

**My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading.** Where the primary derived the membrane from Fig 4 and Fig 10, I derived the same boundary from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 2's anchor test. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is low overlap between *routes* within one framework, but still one evidence pool, so it is internal consistency rather than corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :21-30 (directional membrane, methods cross, measured round trip), Fig 10 :68 (different actors, currency and ground) and Fig 11 :43-52 (enumerated habitat, multi-step cascade).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: enumerate per slot, carry the method up, measure R_T, triangulate two low-overlap frames against a fixed anchor, refuse to name the source.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete ecological answer would name an exponent, an index, a particular cascade depth or a universal law, and would predict a specific site's biodiversity response.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the bridge**: the shorthand permits any provenanced method, while a concrete answer wants a named law, and the figures forbid naming one (Fig 9 :32);
- the **cascade depth**: the shorthand says "two or more steps", while a concrete site prediction needs the actual species and the actual number of steps, which must be enumerated from that world (Fig 11 :48);
- the **independence of the frames**: the shorthand says "two low-overlap frames", while a concrete pair of datasets may share repositories and thus lose the independence the triangulation claim needs (Fig 2 :31).

One thing survives cleanly: the global frame is not a larger local frame, and the transform is lossy and asymmetric. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically by this audit; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. A numerical instance from the world literature is cited in F5 of the primary audit. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
