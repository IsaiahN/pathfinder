# Outreach draft: check one output before deciding whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are checked against verbatim figure lines. The output must survive its own check or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about scaling local interactions to global biodiversity

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the checks):** a change in a local species interaction is measurable at a site, and a global biodiversity or ecosystem-function pattern is measurable at another scale. What is not established is the transform between them. The claim is that this is a membrane crossing, not a magnification: the local and global frames have different actors, different currency and different ground, and what carries up is a reapplicable method, not the record of one site. The global reading is a lossy projection, so it cannot name which local mechanism produced it. That is a shape, not a law. I am not claiming a universal scaling exponent or that any particular biodiversity index is the answer.
>
> **Three things I would count as failure, written before this message:**
> 1. A global pattern is shown to determine a unique local mechanism at a site, so the projection is invertible and the reading names its source.
> 2. A local interaction method is carried to global scale and back with the round-trip gap indistinguishable from zero across several biomes.
> 3. Contact enumeration stops at first-order effects in a comprehensive set of cases, with no measurable second or third-order cascade.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not asking:** I am not asking you to trust the framework, to endorse it, or to say whether you like it. I am asking what you would expect to see if the shape claim were true, and what you would count as a refutation. Your verdict on this stage is recorded before any framework is shown, and I will not pool later agreement as corroboration.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula (local canonical extract): path available on request, citation form `Fig N :line`.
> - Two-stage method note and distribution rules: `04_links_and_distribution.md`.
> - Three open public contact and submission venues for ecological scaling work: `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run the loop on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether what you got is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage sequence

1. Stage 1: send the residual, the shape claim and the three refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. Stage 2, opt-in only: if they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

No agreement, trust or endorsement is requested at any stage. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
