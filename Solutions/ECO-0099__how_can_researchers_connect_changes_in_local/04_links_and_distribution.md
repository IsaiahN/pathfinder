## What in the wider literature bears directly on the claim

1. **A measured R_T.** Nyström et al. 2026 is the single strongest corroboration: effect-size inference does not transfer to local prediction, and structured models improve within-context interpolation but remain weak out of context. This is exactly the round-trip gap Fig 4 :30 requires to be measured rather than assumed.
2. **The local frame is a network of relations, not a list of species.** The ecological-network source defines the local object as nodes plus links, and connects network structure to system properties. That matches the Fig 11 census of actors, conditions and relations.
3. **The cascade is multi-step and context-open.** The trophic-cascade source gives indirect effects across trophic levels and warns that a popular single-cause story is too simple for an open system. That matches Fig 11 :52 and the habitat-substitution rule.
4. **One global pattern, several local mechanisms.** The species-area source lists immigration/extinction balance, disturbance, predator-prey dynamics and spatial clustering as candidate determinants, with a dispute over whether the pattern is mechanistic or a sampling artifact. That is metamerism at the pattern level and supports the asymmetry claim.
5. **A candidate crossing exists but is contested.** Metabolic scaling theory is a genuine attempt to carry a mechanism from cells to the biosphere, and its own page records controversy over the exponent and the diversity pattern. It is a provenanced world binding, not something the figures derive.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only if they are curious, the links, with the note that the figures rather than the summary are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (stage 3 is opt-in)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** follows Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** An outside frame that has absorbed the theory stops being an independent check. The stage-1 verdict is recorded first and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."
