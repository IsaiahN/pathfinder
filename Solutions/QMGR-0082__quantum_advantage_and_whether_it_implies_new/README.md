# Solution QMGR-0082: quantum advantage and new physical principles

**Question:** Quantum advantage and whether it implies new physical principles beyond quantum mechanics

**Source:** `Questions/QM & GR Questions.txt` line 104 (question 82)

**Section:** Computation, Information, and Complexity

**Mode:** `specified` on the figure-derived shape, `general` on the framework topology, not `grounded`.
No experiment was run by this derivation. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal
consistency against the figures, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**One-line answer, stated in effect terms:** a demonstrated quantum advantage is a new *arrangement*
inside the closure of quantum mechanics, not a new physical atom. The claim that it entails new
principles is a `gap` under the figures: no figure line licenses that implication, and several
figure lines positively forbid inferring an import from a speedup. A new principle would be owed
only by a measured deviation from quantum predictions, not by the existence of an advantage.
