## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's conclusions, following
the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency /
anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** One observed separation between device output and classical cost,
  contested as to whether a new physical element is owed. Arity: three terms, two frames sharing one
  event, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-58), not one hard question. Symmetry: the two frames are
  not interchangeable; the classical half is an absence-of-algorithm claim, the quantum half a
  positive prediction with a measured distribution. Scale: circuit step, device run, asymptotic class,
  physical theory. R changes between these scales, so a verdict at one cannot be pasted onto another.
- **Domain 1, the device experiment.** Actors: qubits, gates, noise, measurement, verification.
  Currency: samples, fidelity, depth. Anchor: the output distribution and its agreement with QM
  predictions. This anchor is movable by an instrument, so it is a genuine anchor.
- **Domain 2, the physics frame.** Actors: state, Hamiltonian or channel, measurement, the six-term
  substrate reading. Currency: predicted probabilities. Anchor: experiment.
- **Domain 3, the complexity frame.** Actors: algorithms, simulators, BPP/BQP/PH, conjectures.
  Currency: runtime and asymptotic resources. Anchor: proofs and reductions, not a laboratory
  measurement. This is why no finite device run decides the asymptotic claim.
- **Domain 4, the metatheory frame.** Actors: Γ, atoms, bonds, closure, R, seats. Currency: residuals
  and imports. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for measurement claims.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, referees, venues. Currency:
  agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence here is zero in a
  simulated run because all seats use one evidence pool.

Order by dependency: verify the output before pricing the barrier; price the barrier before asking
whether a physics residual remains; ask that before deciding import (Fig 3 :20-23). The union is
D2 intersected with D3, and it has its own loop because each half has a different anchor.

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from
Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 13's closed-set rule, I started from Fig 3's dependency order
and Fig 2's anchor definition. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between
routes within the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the figure level. It is

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and
what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip
on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (`x`):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-58 (union), Fig 6 :21 and Fig 8 :32 (closure
   and idempotence), Fig 5 :53 (REFUTED gate) and Fig 13 :134-135 (shape, not a name).
2. **Abstraction (`T_A`):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, "an advantage is a
   new arrangement inside the quantum closure, not a new physical atom; a deviation would license an
   import".
3. **Concretisation (`T_E`):** the question as posed, "does quantum advantage imply new physical
   principles beyond quantum mechanics?", and the stronger asserted answer "quantum mechanics is
   incomplete at the complexity frontier".

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure** of F1. The shorthand says "advantage" as one term; the question as posed
  splits into a physics-anchored half and a complexity-anchored half, and the stronger asserted answer
  pastes them together;
- the **level-relative irreducibility** of F7. The shorthand says "arrangement inside the closure";
  the asserted answer reads that barrier as an incompleteness of physics;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit** of F6. The shorthand offers a constraint set; the asserted answer
  asks for a named new principle.

Conversely, what survives cleanly is the core: no import is owed by a speedup alone, and a measured
deviation would be the licensing event. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground
reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating
the formula as an identical condensation of the figures would set `R_T` to zero and would be the
dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap,
which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
