## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The contrast between computational and physics-backed advantages.** Huang et al. state that
   computational advantages rest on unproven conjectures (BPP not equal to BQP, non-collapse of PH),
   while learning and sensing advantages rest on physical principles. That is exactly the union
   compound of F1: one half is proof-shaped, the other measurement-shaped. Do not let a result from
   one half be reported as a result about the other.
2. **Verification is a test of the theory as well as the device.** The same authors write that a
   deviation from quantum predictions at the complexity frontier could signal new physics, while
   noting that verification becomes harder there. Slagle's echo protocol and Aharonov and Vazirani's
   interactive experiments are the concrete forms this takes. This is what would reverse the verdict;
   it is not evidence already in hand.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** :*"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward
  looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before
  anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and its
  refuter; offer links only after.
- **Fig 10 :42** :*"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has
  absorbed the whole framework before answering stage 1 has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** :*"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
  Ask what a new principle would have to predict before giving them any theory.
- **Fig 2 :40** :*"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence
  pool"*. A send selected for people who already believe quantum advantage implies new physics
  produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** :*"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* Prestige, venue name
  and number of recipients are contact-cost filters, never evidence.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and three refuters; (2) ask what they would count
as a failure, and record it; (3) only then, if they are curious, the links, with the note that the
figures and the cited papers, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and
   Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient
   checks the residual without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before any
   theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement
   stops being evidence. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts
   come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user
   must write down what would refute their own result and state their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If the recipient uses
"advantage" to mean a failure of quantum predictions rather than a classical barrier, stop and
re-census before continuing (finding F11 in `01`).
