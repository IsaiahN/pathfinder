# The Tether's answer: does quantum advantage imply new physical principles?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the framework topology, not `grounded`.** This is a
shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground
reading. No experiment was run.

## 1. The residual it answers

A device completes a task in resources that the best known classical procedure cannot match. The
event is settled at the device level; what is unsettled is whether the event owes a new element to
the physical inventory. The claim "quantum advantage implies new physical principles beyond quantum
mechanics" treats a resource separation as a failed physical prediction. The residual is a **union
compound** of a physics half (does the device match QM?) and a complexity half (is there no efficient
classical account?), each with its own anchor (Fig 9 :54-58). Those halves are never averaged.

Dependency order: verify the output, then price the classical barrier, then ask whether any physics
residual remains, then decide import. Effort spent at the import step before the earlier steps arrive
is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :20-23).

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

**D1, the device.** The anchor is the observed output distribution and its agreement with quantum
predictions, not the agreement among theorists (Fig 2 :29-30, :33). A verified match closes the
physics residual. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** at the level of Fig 2 and Fig 5, single-source
for the specific anchor line. No new principle is owed by a match.

**D2, the physics frame.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however
long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 13 :115: "The set is
closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." A deeper circuit or a new sampling
task is an arrangement of the quantum closure. If the output matches QM, the advantage is a reached
composition, not a new physical kind. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** across three figures with
low overlap. Not `grounded`, because this derivation did not read the experiment.

**D3, the complexity frame.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching
and finding nothing proves nothing." The classical barrier is a failure to find plus conjectures; it
is not a ground reading that physics failed. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for
the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Qubit counts and runtime ratios are budget
filters. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 8 plus Fig 9), with the asymptotic-versus-budget
distinction carried from Fig 8 :40-43.

**D4, the import decision.** A new physical principle is an import, the only operator that moves the
wall (Fig 6 :14), and an import adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25). It requires a source gate and a
shadow test (Fig 8 :12-13), and the residual that licenses it is the REFUTED bin, not the NOVEL bin
(Fig 5 :53). Advantage is a NOVEL-bin residual. Verdict: `general` on the topology of imports,
`specified` that advantage does not fire the REFUTED gate. **Gap** for the affirmative proposition:
after a genuine re-census no figure line governs "advantage implies new principles". The figure lines
govern its negation. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

**D5, the verification frame.** Agreement is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35); a list selected
for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40). The first contact carries the
residual and its refuter, not the source (Fig 10 :52). Verdict: `general`, triangulated with Fig 2 and
Fig 7.

No verdict is averaged with another. The overall answer is the conjunction of D1 to D5, each with its
own mode.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that quantum mechanics is complete or final. Fig 7 names "the limit: no last room"; Fig 13
  says the arrangements do not run out. The framework is a shape generator, not a final theory.
- Not that QM cannot break at high complexity. Fig 6 :37-39: *"It is not a rule about theory. theory
  routinely precedes its instrument"*. The right statement is that the test is a different residual.
- Not that no classical simulator will ever be found. Fig 8 :39 forbids converting "not found" into
  "absent". Conjectures are filters on the budget, not witnesses for the verdict.
- Not a name for a replacement principle. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing,
  because naming it would mean already holding it."* Only a shape is offered.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement or proof was produced by this derivation.
- Not that a speedup is physically unreal. It is real at the device level; the dispute is about what
  kind of explanation it owes.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

1. **Device-level deviation.** Slagle's 2021 circuit-inverse echo protocol: run a random non-Clifford
   circuit and its inverse; standard QM predicts fidelity decays exponentially with depth from noise,
   while emergent-quantum-mechanics predicts a markedly faster decay past a depth threshold. A
   deviation with noise accounted for fires the pre-registered refuter and owes a new principle.
2. **Verification at the frontier.** Huang, Choi, McClean and Preskill (2025/2026): verification
   protocols can be stringent tests of QM in regimes not previously explored; *"any deviation from
   quantum predictions in these regimes could signal the discovery of new physics"*, while noting
   that verification becomes harder there. This is the same ground as (1), read from a different
   field with low overlap.
3. **Unconditional separation.** An unrelativized proof that the observed behaviour lies outside BQP
   would turn a budget fact into a closure fact. Aaronson and Chen (2016) stress that the ECT is
   asymptotic and no finite experiment can decide it; that is why this is a proof-shaped ground.
4. **Instrument rule.** Any proposed test must be an instrument improved from one already returning
   something (Fig 6 :32-35). More qubits alone do not change contact (Fig 11 :40).

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. The shape is
stated, the refuters are written down, and the residual stays open at the boundary where QM has not
been tested at high complexity. This is `candidate`, not settled. Agreement among readers is one
evidence pool (Fig 2 :40) and cannot move it to `grounded`. Indiscernibility is not identity: a
speedup that looks like new physics is not thereby new physics.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Quantum advantage does not, by itself, imply new physical principles. It is a new arrangement
> inside the quantum account, and the figures are explicit that composition inside a closure can
> never add an atom: the set is closed, the arrangements are not. The hard part of the claim lives in
> the classical half, where "no efficient simulator is known" is a search result and a conjecture, not
> a failed prediction of quantum mechanics; the figures say a search that finds nothing is never a
> proof of absence. A genuinely new principle would be an import, and imports are licensed by a
> measured deviation from quantum predictions, not by a speedup. So the honest answer has two parts:
> no import is owed on the evidence of an advantage, and the question whether quantum mechanics
> breaks at the complexity frontier is open, with a stated instrument, an echo test that compares
> fidelity decay against the noise model. The shape of the missing principle is recorded, its refuter
> was written before this run, and nothing here claims to have settled the experiment.
