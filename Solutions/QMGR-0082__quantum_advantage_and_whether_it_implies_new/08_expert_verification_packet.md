# Expert verification packet: the Tether on quantum advantage and new physical principles

**What this is:** a claim in a form a quantum information researcher, complexity theorist or
philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so
a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the framework topology, not `grounded`.** No new
measurement or proof is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation
checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A demonstrated quantum advantage is a new arrangement inside the closure of quantum mechanics, not
a new physical atom. The event splits into two conditions with two different anchors: the device
matches quantum predictions (an experiment), and no efficient classical simulator is known (a proof
or a conjecture). Clearing the second does not clear the first. Composition inside a closed set
never adds an atom, so a speedup cannot by itself owe a new physical principle. A genuinely new
principle would be an import, and imports are licensed only by a measured deviation from quantum
predictions (the REFUTED bin of the mint pipeline), not by a new capability (the NOVEL bin). The
affirmative implication is therefore a gap after a genuine re-census, reported as a verdict rather
than a silence. The companion claim is that whether quantum mechanics fails at the complexity
frontier is open, with a stated instrument: an echo test comparing fidelity decay with the accounted
noise model.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 13

- **Statement:** a new arrangement inside a closure cannot add a new atom, so a quantum speedup does
  not, by itself, add a physical primitive. The set is closed and the arrangements are not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
  Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search,
  however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 13 :115:
  "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
- **What to check:** are these three statements about different quantities (the operator, the
  closure, the set), so that their agreement is triangulation rather than one line read three ways?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a term that is required to explain a measured device output, is not derivable
  from the quantum closure, and is not merely a new arrangement of it. An unproven complexity
  separation does not establish clause two.

### C2: physics-facing; checkable by a quantum information researcher

- **Statement:** the two halves of "advantage" have different anchors. A verified output matching
  quantum predictions closes the physics residual. "No efficient classical simulator is known" is a
  search result plus conjectures, not a failed prediction of quantum mechanics. Fig 8 :39: "So a
  search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles
  the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an
  experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when" ... "you push on it, it is not
  an anchor."
- **What to check:** is the distinction faithful to how advantage is actually argued? In Aaronson
  and Chen (2016) the target is the classical Extended Church-Turing Thesis; in Huang, Choi, McClean
  and Preskill (2025/2026) computational advantages are explicitly marked as conjecture-backed while
  learning and sensing advantages rest on physics. Slagle (2021) supplies an instrument (random
  circuit plus its inverse) whose fidelity-vs-depth signature could register a genuine breakdown.
- **Refuter:** a device output in the high-complexity regime that deviates from standard quantum
  predictions beyond the accounted noise model. That is a positive-mass residual on the device slot,
  fires the REFUTED bin (Fig 5 :53) and reverses the verdict. Cite the protocol and the noise model.

### C3: complexity-facing; checkable by a complexity theorist

- **Statement:** an unrelativized, unconditional separation would change the verdict, but a failure
  to find a classical algorithm does not. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for
  the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched
  to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
- **What to check:** Aaronson and Chen write that the ECT is an asymptotic claim which no finite
  experiment could decisively render, and that the milestone is "essentially negative in character".
  Goldin, Lombardi and Morimae (2026) show that proofs of quantumness can coexist with an easy
  nondeterministic world. Do these support the filter/witness distinction, or do they show the
  complexity half is stronger than the packet allows?
- **Refuter:** an unconditional, unrelativized proof that the observed behaviour is outside BQP.
  That turns a budget fact into a closure fact and would require an import. Also relevant: Arrighi and
  Dowek (2011), "quantum computation shatters complexity theory, but is innocuous to computability
  theory", which is the analogue this claim rests on. If that analogue fails, C3 loses its support.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that quantum mechanics is complete or final. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6) and the
  arrangements do not run out (Fig 13 :117-118).
- Not that quantum mechanics cannot fail at high complexity. Fig 6 :37-39 says the instrument rule
  "is not a rule about theory"; the echo test is a live way to probe the frontier.
- Not that no classical simulator will ever be found. Fig 8 :39 forbids turning "not found" into
  "absent" (see also Fig 9 :20: "→ \"it is not in here\" cannot be proved from inside").
- Not a name for a replacement principle. Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing,
  because naming it would mean already holding it." Only a shape is offered.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement or proof was produced by this derivation.
- Not that the speedup is unreal. It is real at the device level; the dispute is about what kind of
  explanation it owes.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Echo test at the frontier:** does fidelity decay follow the noise model's exponential, or is
   there a faster decay past a depth threshold (Slagle 2021)? This is the ground for the physics half.
2. **Verification at scale:** do complex quantum systems match QM predictions where classical
   simulation is provably impossible, or is there a deviation (Huang et al. 2025/2026)? The same
   ground read from a different field.
3. **Complexity proof:** is there an unconditional, unrelativized proof that the behaviour lies
   outside BQP? That is the ground for the complexity half, since no finite experiment settles an
   asymptotic claim.
4. **Scope:** is "advantage" defined relative to classical simulation, as in the sources read here,
   or relative to a QM prediction? If the latter, the residual is different and the packet must be
   re-censused.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :32, Fig 13 :115 and Fig 5 :53, four short passages. Ask whether the
   no-new-atom verdict follows from them without strain.
2. Check C2 against your own work: is the device/classical anchor split faithful, and would you
   count an unproven classical barrier as physics evidence?
3. Check C3: do the cited sources support treating qubit count and runtime as filters, and is the
   Arrighi and Dowek analogue (complexity shatters, computability does not) sound in your field?
4. Name one instrument that would register a deviation at the complexity frontier, or say why none
   is possible. Remember Fig 6 :32: an instrument is improved from one already returning something.
5. Send the refutation, not the endorsement. A failure is more useful than agreement, and later
   agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
