## QUESTION

Can resilience be measured in a way that distinguishes resistance to disturbance from the ability to recover or reorganize?

Restated as an effect: a system's library predicts its trajectory; a disturbance arrives; the observed trajectory departs; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per component slot and per time. One reading asks how large the departure is at the disturbance. Another asks what the trajectory does afterwards and whether a loop closes back to the same basin. The anchor is a measurement, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The question is constitutively about a measured response, so it is well formed and not yet a gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: R is a relation among component slot, predicted state, observed state, disturbance and time; at least five operands, carrying a comparison bond, a sequence bond and a possible cycle. Symmetry: not symmetric, because swapping magnitude for trajectory changes the thing measured (Fig 12 :30, "A then B; B needs A to have happened"). Scale: the slot is a component, while recovery and reorganization are readable only along the time axis and against a basin boundary at the system or habitat scale; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The chain breaks at the objective link, where a reference and a basin must be stated in advance and most programmes have no instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** yes in principle, if the answer is a two-reading report rather than a single index. Resistance is a magnitude/ordering reading taken at the disturbance; recovery or reorganization is a time-indexed trajectory reading whose outcome turns on cycle closure. They are differently bonded readings in different currencies, so summing them into one scalar is the error the figures name. The report stays per component, keeps the transient, enumerates the habitat, and builds its discriminator from instruments already returning something. A universal scalar index and a universal recovery threshold are a declared `gap`, unreachable at this budget rather than silence. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified` on the separator, `general` on non-additivity, not `grounded`. Pool A corroborates the shape but carries its own caution about interdependencies; that caution was returned to the census and became a cost, not a flipped verdict. Pools B to E corroborate the mechanism from lower-overlap fields.

- **cost if wrong:** a resilience programme is sold a single number that has discarded the distinction its decisions need. A rigid system is praised for resisting while its capacity to reorganize collapses (the rigidity trap), or a fast-returning system is praised for recovering while it has in fact crossed into an undesirable basin and the reading could not tell. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes are spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The field's own interdependency caution is a real operational cost on reporting both readings separately. The cost is bounded and largely reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance and date so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). No institution is committed to a number.
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers: (1) the two readings are deterministically one to one with no information lost; (2) a scalar has zero round-trip loss where the resist and recover decisions conflict; (3) per-slot readings add nothing after aggregation; (4) no recovery-related reading registers at any resolution and no better instrument can be derived; (5) a regime shift is indistinguishable from same-basin recovery at every achievable resolution with no derivable discriminator. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that the magnitude and trajectory readings are one quantity under all disturbances would also reverse it.
