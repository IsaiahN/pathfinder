## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* cited here as a substring of the line.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0047 the shape is not a resilience number. It is a two-axis report: magnitude taken at disturbance, trajectory taken through the transient, per component, with a discriminator between same-basin return and basin change. `resilience = 0.73` is a name (and an unjustified one). `magnitude here, trajectory there, held apart` is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* bootstraps vocabulary by proximity: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here, the nearest available terms are already in the field: resistance, return time, recovery rate, lag-1 autocorrelation, robustness, graceful extensibility. Recursing on those narrows the question from "measure resilience" to "keep two readings apart."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the first contact describes what a disturbance does to a reading, not what resilience "is."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with failure conditions rather than with theory.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* The resilience question is exactly such a bundle, and Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling before searching again.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: by the time you have the terms, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The stop is a shape, not a revelation.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Applied here: an instrument is licensed when it discriminates the two readings against an observed trajectory, not when it is fashionable or widely cited.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test. Applied here: importing "resilience ratio" or "return time" from another field is legitimate only if the provenance is recorded and the gap it explains is named.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The chosen reference state and basin boundary are visible guesses, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* reference turns a visible choice into an invisible one, which is why the answer keeps the reference explicit.

## 4. Why a single reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes create identical readings. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* An instrument that *"can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here, a peak-deviation reading cannot say whether the system returned to the same basin or crossed into another. Several trajectories give the same peak. Naming the outcome from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a bigger index. The instrument that resolves it, not an argument, will settle which outcome occurred.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction-horizon language. There is a *"distinction horizon"* for each observer and a *"union horizon"* for all instruments any group can build. A case below the union horizon is *"never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* This is exactly the status assigned in `01` F6 and F7: the regime-shift discriminator is live, and the universal scalar is reported unreachable at this budget, not proven impossible. The essay also says *"indiscernibility is not identity,"* which is why the lineage is kept even when two trajectories read alike.

## 5. The practitioner supplies the measurement, not the framework

This is the most important limit for CMP-0047. *How Intelligence Actually Works* describes a replicator: each recursive pass adds content, not just constraint, because *"if a practitioner traverses the framework and supplies measurements at each level, then the output will eventually converge toward a perceptibility that is indiscernible."* But it immediately warns: *"The content result is theirs, not the framework's."*

So the Tether cannot return a calibrated recovery time for a lake, a forest or a supply network. It returns the shape of the separator and the failure conditions. The numeric content must be supplied by the practitioner and provenanced as theirs. This is the ground-level reason the answer in `07` and `09` ends at a shape plus a gap rather than at a number.

*Arrival of the Fittest* states the same limit for the framework as a whole: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* And on LLM use: outputs generated this way *"must be seen as simulacra."* The citations list in that essay also includes Goodhart's law on proxies, which is the practical danger of promoting a resilience score into a target: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention"* (Fig 10 :47).

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous resilience researchers to agree with the two-reading shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to test it against a trajectory.

The same essay supplies the simulation status: LLMs can *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula,"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` through `11` are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a test of the resilience question, and cannot settle it.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds an operator-level check that matters here: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A room of agreeable readers is a single frame wearing many names (Fig 8 :48-49). An outside frame with a different evidence pool is what is needed, and that is what the expert packet in `08` is for.

## 7. Correction to my own reading, kept visible

I first wrote §3 as if the figures return a shape in the sense of a placeholder to be filled later, so naming resistance and recovery would be premature. That overstates the limit. The README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Resistance and recovery are already held terms in the field's vocabulary; distinguishing them is a binding and a bond assignment, not the invention of a new primitive. The correction does not restore the universal index: no figure line selects a number, and that remains a declared `gap` in `01` F7.

A second correction: I initially treated the resilience question as answerable by choosing the better of two existing metrics. *The Hard Problem is About Source* refuses that. Two metrics that give the same reading on a basin crossing are one witness with two labels. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which is why F6 keeps an open refuter rather than a preferred metric.

Both corrections are recorded here rather than erased, consistent with the standing instruction that corrections and retractions stay visible.
