## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among component slot s, predicted state Γ(b,a), observed state o′, disturbance d and time t. At least five operands. A two-place reading (system, score) is rejected because it loses the disturbance, the prediction and the ordering.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor does not update on the system's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Separable" is itself a threshold claim about whether the information carried by one reading is carried by the other, not a symmetric conjunction.
- **Scale:** the slot is a component, but the outcome (same regime or different regime) is a property of a stability landscape at the system or habitat scale, and the measurement crosses the membrane of Fig 4. So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and a single-scale instrument will lose one of the two readings.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's separator to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a measurement should report both how far a system was knocked and what it did afterwards.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific disturbance. Result: *record the peak deviation and the time to return to normal.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It loses the bond typing. "How far" and "what happened afterwards" become two columns in one table, which is close to the summing error the primary refuses (Fig 12 :130). Coming back down, it invites a resilience index.
2. It loses per-slot indexing. "A system" invites an average across components, and the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
3. It loses the channel failures. "Time to return to normal" silently assumes the transient was recorded, the peak did not saturate and the readings were not pooled (Fig 10 :26).
4. It loses the basin question. "Return to normal" presupposes that normal is knowable and unique; it has no place for multiple basins, reorganization or a regime shift (Fig 12 :121).
5. It loses the gap itself. The abstraction has no way to say that no instrument currently discriminates the outcomes, so it reads as a solved measurement problem rather than an owed instrument (Fig 9 :32).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-paragraph version. The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
