## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about combining satellite remote sensing, in-situ field measurements, historical and documentary records, and Indigenous and local knowledge responsibly. Option A: treat them as four columns of one merged product and let the product serve as the reference. Option B: treat them as four frames ranging separately against a ground that does not update, keep the residual per slot, unbundle the union, require provenance and a shadow test for carried atoms, and make responsibility a property of the checking arrangement. What each leads to: A produces a fast, smooth product that cannot be refused, in which shared evidence is counted twice and the extractive channel is invisible. B produces a checkable shape with a written refuter, separate per-domain verdicts, and a declared `gap` where the framework returns no name.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The part of an earth-system state or change that a frame fails to register, indexed per place, variable, time and affected group. Arity: four frames plus a ground. Symmetry: the four are not interchangeable; their currencies, closures, scales and time-depths differ, so the arrangement is ordered and the bond carries information the ingredient list does not. Scale: global and frequent (satellite), local and direct (field), decadal to centennial (historical records), place-based and multi-generational (ILK). Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, observation frames.** Actors: sensors, field instruments, archives and documents, knowledge holders and their communities. Currency: registered observations and the residuals they leave. Anchor: the earth-system state at the slot, which does not change because a frame thinks otherwise. Question type: union, overlap to be measured.
- **Domain 2, integration and fusion.** Actors: data managers, assimilation schemes, provenance records, uncertainty models. Currency: combined estimates, corrections, uncertainty and lineage. Anchor: the same ground, reached through a channel that can decay while the ground does not. Question type: union compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 3, responsibility and decision.** Actors: affected communities, resource managers, governance bodies, reviewers. Currency: consequences, consent, benefit, harm and the standing to refuse. Anchor: twofold and scope-dependent, the earth system for physical-state questions and human judgment for questions constitutively about people. Question type: union with a scope condition that must be stated, not smoothed.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The middle link has no instrument for incommensurable currencies, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.
- **Strain test applied.** Fig 8 union surplus and Fig 2 agreement worthlessness are not in conflict: Fig 8 :47 says *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* One parameter governs both; using them as two witnesses would be one pool talking.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the operational dream does not. A responsible combination is a per-slot, unbundled, residual-first triangulation around a non-updating ground, with measured independence, a provenance gate and shadow test on every carried atom, declared stakes, a verifier that cannot reconstruct what it checks, a two-anchor scope stated per sub-question, and a habitat enumerated out to cascades. The merged product is a frame and must never become the anchor. Indigenous and local knowledge is a differing closure contacted two-way, not a data layer to be ingested. Verdicts for D1, D2 and D3 are `specified`, triangulated, and kept separate; the recipe is a declared `gap`. This is a candidate, not a `grounded` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent as written. The claim states its mode, its three refuters and its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is extractive contact with knowledge holders, which the world sources say causes intergenerational harm and broken trust.
- **undoable:** yes. The operational recipe was never asserted as figure-derived, so nothing needs to be withdrawn from the shape claim to change a fusion method. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and no primitive has to be deleted. What is not undoable is an extractive relationship once trust is broken; that is why the two-way gate belongs in the claim rather than in an appendix.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A held-out test showing that a residual-first, unbundled combination does not beat the best single frame; a measured overlap near one that still yields a genuine surplus; an instrument that names the source of a product reading; a governance arrangement that produces a refused-in-advance harm verdict while its verifier can reconstruct the fusion; a first-order habitat boundary that predicts a third-order harm. Any of these sends the relevant domain back to census. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
