## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Parallel knowledge systems corroborate the triangulation shape, with low overlap.** Tengö et al. (2014) is the strongest world-side witness: validation should occur within each knowledge system, and contributions should form an enriched picture for triangulation, not be subordinated to one system's validation protocol. This is Fig 2's triangulation rule arrived at from ecosystem governance, not from the framework. It is not a second copy of the figures: the authors, venue and vocabulary differ.
2. **Two-eyed seeing corroborates keeping both frames with their integrity intact.** Leonard et al. (2022) supply both the positive claim (sensors and millennia of observation are both invaluable) and the failure mode (extractive research without consent and leadership causes intergenerational harm). The extraction warning maps onto the figure requirement for a source gate and shadow test, and onto Fig 12's rule that an ingredient list without the bond records only half the thing.
3. **Metrology and data assimilation corroborate per-slot structure.** Merchant et al. (2017) and ECMWF (2025) are independent fields arriving at per-datum uncertainty and at the incompleteness of any single observing system. Merchant's correlated-errors line is especially close to Fig 2's common-cause collapse: frames can agree without confirming each other.
4. **Provenance literature corroborates the source gate.** Pasquier et al. (2017) and ARDC (2024) give the operational form of provenance: a record of entities, activities and agents, with a W3C standard. The 140 percent figure is a concrete cost of losing the bond between data and pipeline.
5. **A conflict that was returned to census, not used to flip a verdict.** Tengö et al. describe "integration" as sometimes respectful and empowering in a specific case, which could be read as supporting a single merged product. Re-census with Fig 8 :5 and :7 resolves it: the figures forbid the *blend that produces no atom*, not every act of translation between frames. The world source is therefore read as a scope note on integration, not as a contradiction. The verdict is unchanged.

Overlap declaration: Tengö et al. and Leonard et al. are in the same broad knowledge-systems literature and share case studies, so they count as one evidence pool on the ILK point, not two independent witnesses. Merchant et al., ECMWF, Pasquier et al. and ARDC are in different fields (metrology, operational meteorology, data science, research-data infrastructure) and are genuinely low overlap. They corroborate the *mechanism shape*, not the earth-systems fact. No world source was taken as deciding a verdict; the figures decide.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A recipient who receives the full framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The checker must be unable to produce what they are checking.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :35**: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement, and do not gather endorsements as evidence.
- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator (the residual, the shape claim and the refuter), not a recording of one successful combination.
- **Fig 7 :13**: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A chain of agreeing recipients is one long room.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (adapted from the exemplar)

**(1)** Validate the shape claim against the ground. **(2)** Only if the recipient is curious, offer the sources that produced it. **(3)** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A Track A recipient's verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** under Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula, Direction: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. The refuter in `01` section 5 is what makes that checkable, and `08` is the packet to send.

## Submission caution for simulated work

This folder is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading. Several of the journals and societies listed require either a real data product or a checkable claim, and some have policies on AI-generated text. Before any submission, check the current author policy of the venue. Do not present a simulated derivation as a measurement or as a settled result. If a venue requires original data, offer the shape claim through a public discussion or abstract route first, and state the mode plainly.
