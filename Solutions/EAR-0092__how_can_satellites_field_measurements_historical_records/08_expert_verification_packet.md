# Expert verification packet: four observation frames combined responsibly

**What this is:** a claim in a form a data-integration or earth-system researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims and the shape, `general` on the structural rule, `gap` on the operational recipe. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Satellites, field measurements, historical records and Indigenous and local knowledge are four frames, not four columns of one table. They form a union of overlapping conditions with a per-slot residual, so the union is unbundled and verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. The earth system, not the merged product, is the anchor that does not update when frames push on it. Agreement is worth the measured independence behind it, and a blend that pools vocabulary without a residual adds nothing. Anything carried between frames needs a provenance gate and a shadow test; the Indigenous and local knowledge channel runs two ways, because a knowledge holder is a differing frame, not a data layer. "Responsible" is a property of the checking arrangement: stakes are declared, a convention cannot decide an outcome, the verifier cannot reconstruct the fusion it checks, community judgment is the anchor for questions constitutively about that community and only an instrument for physical-state questions, and the habitat is enumerated out to cascades. The framework withholds the fusion algorithm, the weights and the roster; it returns a shape and a refuter written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 8, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the four fail as a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition; the residual stays indexed per slot; a flattened average loses the signal and the bond.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 12 :47 *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*
- **What to check:** does the figure text say what is claimed, and does the union reading survive re-census? A case in two conditions is a specific compound, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57-58).
- **Refuter:** show that the four failure conditions are disjoint, or exhibit a flattened average that retains per-slot residuals, the typed relations between inputs, and the standing to be refused when the ground turns. By construction an average retains none of these.

### C2. Data-integration-facing, checkable by someone who has built a fusion or assimilation system

- **Statement:** frames must range against a non-updating ground; agreement is worth the independence behind it; a blend produces no surplus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 8 :5 *"→ vocabulary, never an atom"* and Fig 8 :7 *"→ interdisciplinarity, no mint"*
- **What to check:** in a real system, is the reference a measured quantity that the inputs cannot move, or is it the previous analysis product being tuned by the same streams it corrects? Can the overlap of the four evidence pools be measured, and do two of them in fact share an upstream source? Merchant et al. (2017) report that in climate data records *"different errors may be correlated across a wide range of timescales and space scales,"* which is the same collapse in metrological language. ECMWF (2025) states that *"Observations are unevenly distributed and come with errors. They cannot provide a complete and accurate picture of the state of the Earth system across the globe at a given point in time."*
- **Refuter:** a combination whose reference changes when the inputs push on it yet still predicts a held-out measurement; or four streams with measured overlap near one that still produce a ground-checked surplus on a held-out slot.

### C3. Responsibility-facing, checkable by a community-engaged researcher or governance reviewer

- **Statement:** responsibility is a checking-arrangement constraint, not a data layer. It requires declared stakes, a verifier that cannot reconstruct the fusion, a two-anchor scope stated per sub-question, a two-way channel for knowledge holders, and a habitat enumerated to cascades.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
- **What to check:** can the consent or benefit rule be violated and detected, or is it a declared constant with no instrument behind it? Does the review seat hold the interiors of the fusion? Is the anchor appropriate to the sub-question, physical state versus consequence to people? Are third-order harms inside the listed habitat, or does the enumeration stop at direct users? Leonard et al. (2022) warn that research without *"Indigenous consent and leadership will lead to intergenerational harm and broken trust."*
- **Refuter:** a governance arrangement that yields a checkable, refused-in-advance verdict on harm while its verifier can reconstruct and revise the fusion it checks; or a first-order habitat boundary that predicts a third-order harm on a held-out case; or a demonstration that community judgment is irrelevant to a question constitutively about harm to that community.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a fusion algorithm, weighting scheme, assimilation configuration or governance roster. No figure line governs these. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not that all four frames are needed in every sub-question. A union is unbundled before searching.
- Not that four sources are four witnesses. Overlap is measured; shared upstream evidence makes one witness.
- Not that ILK is a data layer to be ingested. It is a differing closure, contacted two-way with a source gate and a shadow test.
- Not that community judgment settles physical-state questions. Fig 2 :37 says it is *"only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
- Not that prestige, institutional reputation or agreement from many recipients confirms the claim. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not `grounded`. No held-out measurement or harm assessment was used in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A held-out measurement and a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source,"* and the case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

1. On held-out slots, does the residual-first, unbundled combination beat the best single frame and a flattened average, with per-slot residuals reported?
2. Does the surplus survive replacing the two highest-overlap streams with one witness?
3. Can an instrument identify which frame produced a given product reading, or can several combinations yield the same reading with no discriminator?
4. Does the review seat have standing to refuse, without holding the fusion's interiors?
5. Does the habitat enumeration include third-order harms, checked against a case where the product reached only second-order users?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read four short passages: Fig 9 :54, Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :29 and :40, Fig 10 :52 and :42. That is the claim's spine.
2. Check C2 against one system you know: name the anchor, and say whether the inputs can move it. If they can, the system is in Fig 2's panel 1.
3. Estimate the overlap of two of the four streams you work with. If they share an upstream source, count them once.
4. Name one third-order harm of a data product in your field, or say why none exists.
5. Send the refutation or the missing failure condition. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no reply is needed if you would rather not engage.
