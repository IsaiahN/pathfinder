# Outreach draft: validate the shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01` section 5 and the 20-minute check in `08` are intact. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 claim cannot survive without the theory.

**Mode of what is offered:** `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rule, `gap` on the recipe. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about combining earth observations before reading any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know about data integration and earth system research. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** satellites, field measurements, historical records and Indigenous and local knowledge are four frames, not four columns in one table. Each has a different closure, currency, anchor, spatial and temporal scale. The earth system is the thing being measured and it is the anchor that must not change its answer when any frame pushes on it. From that it follows that a responsible combination has a particular shape:
>
> - the gap between observation and system state stays indexed per place, variable, time and affected group, not averaged into one agreement score;
> - the four are a union of overlapping conditions, so the union is unbundled before any search, and verdicts stay separate per domain;
> - agreement is worth exactly the independence behind it, so two streams sharing an upstream source count as one witness;
> - nothing is carried between frames without provenance and a test of which gap it explains, and the Indigenous and local knowledge channel runs two ways;
> - "responsible" is a property of the checking arrangement, not a fifth data layer, so stakes are declared and the checker cannot reconstruct the fusion it is checking.
>
> That is a shape and a set of tests. It is not a fusion algorithm, a weighting scheme, or a governance roster. The framework does not return those, and I am not claiming it does.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a particular assimilation system implements this, that a particular number of independent streams is required, or that community judgment settles a physical-state question. For questions about physical state, community judgment is an instrument and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. For questions about whether an outcome is good for a person or community, it is the legitimate anchor. Which one applies has to be stated per sub-question.
>
> **What would refute the claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a combination whose reference changes when the inputs push on it, yet whose agreement still predicts a held-out measurement;
> 2. four streams with measured overlap near one that still produce a genuine ground-checked surplus on a held-out slot;
> 3. a flattened average that retains per-slot residuals, full provenance and the standing to be refused when the ground turns;
> 4. community judgment shown to be irrelevant to a question constitutively about harm to that community;
> 5. a first-order habitat boundary that predicts a third-order harm on a held-out case.
>
> If any one holds, I will withdraw the finding it kills. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Before you answer, one thing I would value:** what would you count as a failure of the shape claim that I have not listed? I will record your answer before showing you any theory, and I will not treat a later agreement as corroboration.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The eight-step formula and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Multiple Evidence Base, Tengö et al. 2014, Ambio: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4132468
> - Two-eyed seeing, Leonard et al. 2022, PLOS Biology: https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3001876
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the local file. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Claim before theory | Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Record the verdict before showing theory | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient absorbs the theory, agreement stops being evidence. |
| Mode on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`, mode rule: state `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. |
| No claim that a particular system implements the shape | Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; the recipe is a declared `gap`. |
| Independence measured, not assumed | Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 8 :47, *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* |
| Two-way knowledge channel | Fig 6 :12-13, source gate and shadow test; the Leonard et al. quote on extractive research. |
| Stakes declared | Fig 10 :59-60, *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* and *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask the recipient for their own failure condition | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so make the sought-for shape an ordinary term and let it accrue a residual. |

## What is deliberately absent from the email

- No request for trust, endorsement or a public statement of support.
- No personal emails, no scraped list, no bulk send.
- No claim that agreement from several recipients would confirm the shape. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
