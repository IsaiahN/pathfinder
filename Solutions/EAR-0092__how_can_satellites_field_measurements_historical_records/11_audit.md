## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I did this before adopting the primary's conclusions.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between what an observation frame returns and what the earth system does. Arity: four frames plus a ground, so a union rather than a partition (Fig 9 :54). Symmetry: the four are not interchangeable operands. Their closures, currencies, anchors, spatial and temporal resolutions and time-depths differ, so the bond is ordered and the arrangement carries information the ingredient list does not (Fig 12 :47). Scale: global and frequent, local and direct, decadal to centennial, place-based and multi-generational; the crosses between them are lossy (Fig 4 :27).
- **Domain 1, observation frames.** Actors: sensors, field instruments, archives and documents, knowledge holders and communities. Currency: registered observations and residuals. Anchor: the earth-system state at the slot. This is a union whose overlap must be measured, not a set of four witnesses already known to be independent.
- **Domain 2, integration and fusion.** Actors: data managers, assimilation schemes, provenance records, uncertainty models. Currency: combined estimates, corrections and lineage. Anchor: the same ground through a channel that can decay while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24). Union compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 3, responsibility and decision.** Actors: affected communities, managers, governance bodies, reviewers. Currency: consequences, consent, benefit, harm, standing to refuse. Anchor: twofold and scope-dependent. For questions about physical state the earth system; for questions constitutively about people, human judgment. I regard this domain as properly admitted only because both anchors can be named; if neither could be, it would not be a domain yet.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The middle link is the one without an instrument, because there is no common currency for "what would count as progress" across a radiance, a soil sample, a document and a relational observation. Separately, discovery order (residual first, frame second) and settlement order (refuse before mint) are two tracks and must not be read against each other (Fig 6 :31).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. I arrived there by a different route: I started from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency chain, whereas the primary started from Fig 1's per-slot residual and Fig 2's anchor rule. Both routes land on the same domain boundaries with low overlap between their starting figures, so the internal agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 and :40 (anchor and independence), Fig 6 :12-13 and :23 (source gate, shadow test, import), Fig 8 :5, :7, :45 and :53 (blend, surplus, residual first), Fig 9 :54 (unbundling), Fig 10 :42 and :52 (verifier and residual), Fig 11 :44 (cascade), Fig 12 :47 and :125 (bond, two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried through the folder: triangulate independent frames with provenance, keep the residual per slot, make responsibility a checking arrangement.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an operational data-assimilation pipeline with a governance layer, as a reader would actually build it.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **exact per-slot indexing**, which becomes a gridded product with a single uncertainty field unless a per-datum discipline is enforced;
- the **typed bond** between an observation and its producer, which a provenance list can name but cannot reconstruct when several sources yield the same reading (the metamerism case from `05`);
- the **two-currency split**, which a quality score quietly sums (Fig 12 :130);
- the **habitat cascade**, which a fusion pipeline's boundary normally cuts at direct users;
- the **scope condition on human judgment**, which a generic "stakeholder engagement" box erases by treating community judgment as a ground for physical state.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must hold the frames apart around a non-updating ground, and the residual must remain locatable. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
