# The Tether's answer: combining four observation frames responsibly

**Mode: `specified` on the three domain verdicts and the shape, `general` on the structural rule that any combination needs a non-updating ground, `gap` on the operational fusion algorithm and governance roster. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An earth system does something at a place and time. Satellite remote sensing, field measurement, documentary records and Indigenous and local knowledge each register part of it and miss part of it. The gap between what a frame returns and what the system does is R, indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The question asks for a combination that reduces R responsibly. The word "responsibly" is not a fifth data stream. It is a constraint on the checking arrangement: who may author what, what may serve as an anchor, what must be declared, and how far the habitat must be enumerated.

The four frames form a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition (Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. So verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The earth system is the anchor; the merged product is not.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* If the product becomes the reference that inputs are corrected against, the loop is Fig 2's mutual update: frames converge on whoever had the most influence, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Agreement is worth the independence behind it.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 13 :38-39: *"independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence, and it is what makes their agreement mean anything. Six members that overlapped would be fewer members wearing six names."* Four sources are four witnesses only to the degree of low overlap.
3. **Triangulation, not a blend.** Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* The two named collapses are the blend, *"→ vocabulary, never an atom"* (Fig 8 :5), and the undirected union, *"→ interdisciplinarity, no mint"* (Fig 8 :7). The union is searched from contact: *"Residual first, frame second."* (Fig 8 :53).
4. **Per-slot residuals, full provenance, two currencies.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 12 :47: *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."* Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* A single quality score mixes description cost with settlement cost and loses the bond.
5. **Carried atoms need a source gate and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :12-13 names the two gates: *"source gate: provenance"* and *"shadow test: explains a gap"* For ILK, the gate includes consent and benefit on the holder's own terms. Extraction is not import: the atom moves but the bond does not, and the holder is treated as a resource rather than a differing frame.
6. **Responsibility is a checking arrangement.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 10 :59-60: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* and *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
7. **The anchor scope is twofold and must be stated per sub-question.** Fig 2 :36-37: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Human judgment is the right anchor for a question about consequences to people, and *"only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* So community judgment settles "is this outcome good for this community," not "what is the soil moisture at this pixel."
8. **The habitat is enumerated to its cascades.** Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :29-30 names the two silent failures: *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you."* A product checked only in a harness proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :70).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a fusion algorithm, a weighting scheme, a data-assimilation configuration, or a universal governance roster. No figure line governs those. The figures return *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that all four frames must be used in every sub-question. A union is unbundled; some sub-questions are single-condition and need only the relevant frame.
- Not that four sources are four witnesses. Overlap must be measured. Two streams with a common upstream source are one witness.
- Not that ILK is a data layer to be ingested. It is a differing closure contacted two-way, and extraction fails the source gate.
- Not that community judgment settles physical-state questions. The scope rule in Fig 2 :36-38 is explicit.
- Not that agreement from many recipients confirms the shape. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not `grounded`. No held-out measurement, covariance estimate or harm assessment was used in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a held-out test, not further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the form: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Here the corresponding checks are:

1. **Per-slot surplus.** On held-out places and variables, does a residual-first, unbundled combination predict better than the best single frame and better than a flattened average, with the per-slot residuals reported rather than a global score?
2. **Independence test.** Measure the overlap of the four evidence pools. Does the surplus survive when the two highest-overlap pairs are replaced by a single witness? If it does not, the agreement was the pool talking.
3. **Source-discrimination test.** Can an instrument distinguish which frame produced a given product reading? If several combinations yield the same product and no discriminator exists, the source is named from a projection and the provenance record is incomplete.
4. **Refusal test.** Does the arrangement contain a seat that can refuse a fusion when the ground turns against it, without reconstructing the fusion it is checking?
5. **Cascade test.** Does a habitat boundary at first-order contact still predict a third-order harm on a held-out case? If not, enumeration must go further out.
6. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5:** a changing reference that still predicts; four high-overlap frames with a genuine surplus; a flattened average retaining per-slot residuals and provenance; community judgment irrelevant to a question about community harm.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is stated and checkable. The operational recipe is not figure-derived and is recorded as `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*; Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*). The choice of the four frames is a visible choice, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The derivation stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a fusion algorithm for satellites, field measurements, historical records and Indigenous and local knowledge. It offers the shape any responsible combination must have. The earth system, not the merged product, is the anchor that does not update when frames push on it. The four are a union of overlapping conditions, so the union is unbundled, the residual stays indexed per place, variable, time and group, and verdicts are never averaged. Agreement counts for exactly the measured independence behind it, and a blend that only pools vocabulary adds nothing. Anything carried between frames needs a provenance gate and a shadow test, and the Indigenous and local knowledge channel runs two ways because a knowledge holder is a differing frame, not a data layer. "Responsibly" is a property of the checking arrangement, not another dataset: stakes are declared, a convention cannot decide an outcome, and the checker cannot reconstruct what it checks. Community judgment is the anchor for questions about consequences to that community and only an instrument for physical-state questions. The habitat is enumerated out to cascades. What the framework withholds is the algorithm, the weights and the roster. It returns a shape and a refuter written in advance, and it stops there until a held-out measurement and a source-discriminating instrument close the gap.
