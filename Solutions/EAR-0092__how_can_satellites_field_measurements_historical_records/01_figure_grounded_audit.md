## 0. The claim under audit, restated as an effect

The question names four things to be combined: satellite remote sensing, in-situ field measurements, historical and documentary records, and Indigenous and local knowledge (ILK). It adds one constraint, "responsibly." The derive-first move is to state what is being decided in effect terms before choosing any figure line.

**What is being decided.** Whether to treat the four as four partial readings of one ground that can be brought into relation, or as ingredients of one merged product. Each option leads somewhere different:

- **Merge and flatten.** Average, bias-correct and fuse the four into a single best estimate, then use that product as the reference. This is fast and convenient. It also removes the very differences that made the four informative, and it installs the product as a new frame that then decides what counts as a correct reading.
- **Triangulate and keep the frames separate.** Range each frame separately against the earth system, keep the residual per place, variable and time, allow a surplus only where the closures genuinely differ, require provenance and a shadow test for anything carried between frames, and keep human judgment as the anchor for questions that are constitutively about people. This is slower. It preserves the independence that makes agreement mean something and makes a failure locatable.

**What an answer would look like (Direction).** An answer must return the *shape* that any responsible combination has, the checks that would show the shape is wrong, and the instruments that would close it. It must not return a named algorithm, a weighting formula, or a universal governance roster, because no figure line governs those. Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term is the discipline the formula requires: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"* (Formula, "Direction"), so the preference can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else.

**The residual, stated in effect terms.** The earth system does something at a place and time. Each frame predicts or registers something. The gap between what a frame returns and what the system does is R, and it is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). The four frames differ in what they can even represent, so each is a partial transform, not a defective copy of one true record. The residual after combining is not a single scalar. It is a vector over places, variables, times and consequences, and it is the aim of the loop as well as its error signal.

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 The residual by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** four frames plus a ground. The combination is therefore a union of overlapping conditions, not a two-item partition and not one dataset with four columns. Fig 9 :54 states the rule: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **Symmetry:** the four frames are not interchangeable operands. Satellite sensing is remote, global, frequent and indirect; field measurement is in-situ, local, direct and sparse; historical records are documentary or early-instrumental, decadal to centennial, and filtered by what institutions chose to keep; ILK is place-based, multi-generational, relational and held by people with a stake in the consequences. Under a symmetric bond an ordering would be free; here the ordering, the scale and the anchor differ, so the bond is ordered and the arrangement carries information the ingredient list does not (Fig 12 :47, *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*).
- **Scale:** they operate at different spatial and temporal resolutions, and they cross scale boundaries only through a lossy transform (Fig 4 :27, *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*). A global satellite pixel and a single observer's seasonal observation do not stack into one another without remainder.

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

A domain is admitted only if it has a nameable anchor. If no anchor can be named, it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Observation frames | sensors, field instruments, archives and documents, knowledge holders and their communities | registered observations and the residuals they leave | **yes:** the earth-system state and change at the slot, which does not change because a frame thinks otherwise (Fig 2 :29) | union: four partial readings, overlap to be measured |
| D2 Integration and fusion | data managers, assimilation schemes, provenance records, uncertainty models | combined estimates, bias corrections, uncertainty, and the lineage of each transform | **yes:** the same ground, reached through a channel that can decay while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24) | union compound, with a compound loop of its own |
| D3 Responsibility and decision | affected communities, resource managers, governance bodies, reviewers | consequences, consent, benefit and harm, and the standing to refuse a reading | **yes, and twofold:** for questions about physical state the earth system; for questions constitutively about people, human judgment (Fig 2 :36-37) | union with a scope condition that must be stated, not smoothed |

D1 is admitted because the earth system is an anchor that does not update on what any frame thinks. D2 is admitted because it has the same anchor, approached through a channel whose decay is measurable. D3 is admitted because the question "responsible for whom" is constitutively about people, and Fig 2 :37 gives the legitimate anchor for that question: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Note the scope condition in the same line: it is *"only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Community judgment is not a ground for a physical-state question. That is a scope rule, not a strain, and it is kept visible.

### 1.3 Partition or union

This is a **union**, not a partition. Combining four frames fails in different ways for different reasons: a sensor drifts, a field site is moved, an archive has a gap, a knowledge holder is not asked or is asked extractively. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). A case that is both badly sampled by satellites and misread in the archive is not a harder case of either; it is a specific compound with its own loop. So the correct move is to unbundle before searching, keep verdicts per domain, and never average them into a single confidence score.

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 orders the chain by dependency, not importance: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Applied here:

1. **Perception:** can each frame tell what is in front of it? Field instruments calibrate; satellite radiances are inter-calibrated; archives are dated; ILK is registered with the people who hold it. This link has obvious tests.
2. **Vocabulary:** are there terms for what an observation affords before there are terms for fusing it? This is the middle link that usually breaks. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Data integration has abundant instruments for perception and for computation, and few for saying what counts as progress when the currencies are incommensurable.
3. **Objective:** can each frame say what would count as progress in its own terms, before a shared objective is declared? A declared shared metric is a convention, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).
4. **Planning:** a route to combination that keeps the union unbundled and the imports provenanced.
5. **Learn and carry:** name what worked, verify it, and carry the *method*, not the recording of one successful merge (Fig 4 :22).

Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it. Most integration effort currently lands at the compute layer, which is above the vocabulary/objective break. That is a diagnostic, not a verdict about any particular project.

## 3. Findings

### F1. The earth system, not any of the four frames, is the anchor (decisive for D1)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*

If the merged product is made the reference, then the four frames are tuned to the product and the product is tuned to the frames. That is Fig 2's collapse 1, mutual update. Fig 2 :14-15: *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"*; :34: the drift *"is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* If two of the four draw on the same upstream evidence, for example a historical record and a satellite product both traceable to the same station network or the same reanalysis, that is collapse 2, common cause. Fig 2 :20-21: *"so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other."*

The operational consequence is a negative one with a positive form: do not let the fusion product become a frame that the inputs are then corrected against. Keep the reference outside the loop, and check it with an instrument the frames cannot move. Verdict for D1: **triangulation required**, `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 11 (low overlap between the ground/anchor rule and the habitat enumeration rule). Fig 8 and Fig 2 are *not* two independent witnesses here, because Fig 8 :47 explicitly says *"Independence is one quantity read twice."*

**Refuter:** show a four-frame combination whose reference changes its answer when the frames push on it, yet whose agreement still predicts a held-out measurement at the slot. If that holds, the anchor rule is wrong for this domain.

### F2. Agreement is worth exactly the independence behind it (D1, D2)

Fig 2 :32: *"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 13 :38-39: *"independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence, and it is what makes their agreement mean anything. Six members that overlapped would be fewer members wearing six names."*

Four sources are not four witnesses. They are four witnesses only to the degree that they draw on different evidence. The world literature corroborates this structurally: Tengö et al. (2014) define the Multiple Evidence Base as an approach in which *"evaluation of knowledge occurs primarily within rather than across knowledge systems,"* and Merchant et al. (2017) warn that in climate data records *"different errors may be correlated across a wide range of timescales and space scales."* The second is the common-cause collapse in metrological language. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Refuter:** demonstrate a four-way agreement with measured overlap near one that still delivers a genuine, ground-checked surplus on a held-out slot. That would break the independence law.

### F3. A blend is not a combination (decisive for D2)

Fig 8 names two collapses directly. Collapse 4 is the blend: *"→ vocabulary, never an atom"* (Fig 8 :5). Collapse 5 is the undirected union: *"→ interdisciplinarity, no mint"* (Fig 8 :7). The grounded alternative is triangulation: *"→ the wall moves"* (Fig 8 :9). A merged product that merely pools vocabulary and averages values adds no atom to the frame and produces no surplus. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

The positive requirement is that the union be searched from contact. Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Start from the per-slot residual in effect terms, then find the frame whose closure predicts its shape. Starting from a preferred fusion technique and mapping the four sources onto it is the inversion the figures forbid.

Verdict for D2: **the combination must be a residual-directed triangulation, not a blend**, `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 (import, source gate) and Fig 12 (bond and two currencies), which have low overlap.

**Refuter:** exhibit a flattened average of all four streams that retains the per-slot residual, the provenance of each contribution, and the ability to be refused when the ground turns against it. By construction an average keeps none of these, so a successful demonstration would falsify the finding.

### F4. Import needs a source gate and a shadow test, and extraction is not import (D2, D3)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The operators on the import path are named at Fig 6 :12-13: *"source gate: provenance"* and *"shadow test: explains a gap."* Fig 6 :24: *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*

Applying this to ILK requires care. If a scientific frame takes an ILK observation, strips its context, and inserts it as a value in a dataset, that is not a clean import with a source gate. It is extraction: the atom moves, the bond that gave it meaning does not, and the knowledge holder is not a frame that the importer has contacted. Fig 12 :47 (the ingredient list is half the record) and Fig 4 :25 (*"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*) both bear here. The world source is blunt: Leonard et al. (2022) write that western science often *"extracts" Indigenous knowledge as if it is a resource for the taking*, and that *"Ocean research not grounded in community and which lacks Indigenous consent and leadership will lead to intergenerational harm and broken trust."*

A responsible import therefore has three tests: provenance that names the holder and the conditions of the gift, a shadow test that shows which gap the import explains, and a two-way channel so the scientific frame is also a source for the holder's own purposes. Tengö et al. (2014) give the world-side counterpart: knowledge systems should *"let each knowledge system speak for itself,"* and the MEB approach *"emphasizes that evaluation of knowledge occurs primarily within rather than across knowledge systems."*

**Refuter:** show an import from a knowledge holder into a dataset that has full provenance, explains a named gap, benefits the holder on their own terms, and is refused when the holder withdraws consent. If that is impossible, the source gate is not a gate but a slogan, and this finding fails.

### F5. The residual must stay indexed per slot (D1, D2)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A national or global "agreement score" across the four streams is exactly the aggregation the figure forbids. The residual is per place, per variable, per time and per affected group. Merchant et al. (2017) give the metrological form: *"total standard uncertainty should be quantified per datum in a CDR, meaning that uncertainty estimates should clearly discriminate more and less certain data."* ECMWF (2025) gives the observational form: *"Observations are unevenly distributed and come with errors. They cannot provide a complete and accurate picture of the state of the Earth system across the globe at a given point in time."* These are two independent fields arriving at per-datum structure.

**Refuter:** a single scalar uncertainty or agreement statistic that guides a decision as well as any per-slot vector, on held-out slots. That would dissolve the per-slot requirement.

### F6. Responsibility is a property of the checking arrangement, not a fifth data stream (decisive for D3)

"Responsibly" cannot be added as another layer of metadata. It is a constraint on who may author what, what may be used as an anchor, and what must be declared. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* A consent protocol that is declared but cannot be violated is a constant the author stamped, not a ground.

Fig 2 :36-38 supplies the two-anchor scope: for a question about physical state, human judgment is *"only an instrument,"* and instruments can be *"wrong on axes they do not measure."* For a question about whether an outcome is good for a person or a community, human judgment is the legitimate anchor. Most real "combine responsibly" questions are compounds of both, so the scope must be stated per sub-question, not chosen once.

Fig 10 :59-60: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* and *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* Knowledge holders and data producers both have stakes. The remedy is declaration and a refusal seat, not a pretence of neutrality. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The checker must not hold the interiors of the fusion.

**Refuter:** a governance arrangement that produces a checkable, refused-in-advance verdict on harm while its verifier can reconstruct and revise the fusion it is checking. That would break the verifier-non-access requirement.

### F7. The habitat must be enumerated out to the cascade (D3)

Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* and *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52-53 gives the canonical caution: *"contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

A responsible combination has to enumerate who and what is in contact with the data product, then what is in contact with those. A satellite-derived fire-risk map does not end at the map. It reaches evacuation orders, insurance, grazing access, ceremonial sites and language transmission. The silent failures are named at Fig 11 :29-30: *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you."* A fusion product validated only in a test harness proves wiring, not capability: Fig 11 :70, *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

Valence is residual-relative: Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A knowledge holder is not a permanent benefactor or a permanent obstacle; the relation changes with the residual. This is why a standing roster of "stakeholders" is not a domain model.

**Refuter:** an enumerated habitat whose boundary is set at first-order contact and still predicts a third-order harm on a held-out case. If it does, cascade enumeration is unnecessary for that domain.

### F8. Two currencies, not one score (D2, D3)

Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

A data product is cheap to describe and expensive to settle in the field, or the reverse. A confidence number that mixes description cost with verification cost is the named error. Fig 12 :111 adds the structural point: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Listing the four sources tells you what is present; it does not tell you the route from observation to decision. The route is carried by the typed relations, and those relations are what a responsible combination must record.

**Refuter:** a single-number cost that predicts both the description length and the settlement actions across held-out products with no loss. That would dissolve the two-currency rule.

## 4. Step 7: three questions, kept distinct

No figure line in `closure(Γ)` returns the operational recipe. The formula requires that SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD be asked separately, in that order.

- **SEARCHED, asked first.** Is the recipe already in the closure and merely unfound? The relevant closure is the set of figure-derived shapes plus the assimilating literature already read. ECMWF (2025) and the NOAA EMC review describe mature data-assimilation machinery, including coupled assimilation and ensembles. What is *not* in that closure is a rule for keeping ILK as an independent frame rather than an ingested observation. A search of the framework itself does not produce one. Result: not found at this budget.
- **INWARD.** Extend the instrument from one already returning something. The instrument here is the per-slot residual with uncertainty (Merchant et al. 2017) and the provenance record (Pasquier et al. 2017; ARDC 2024). Sharpening it means adding per-slot error covariance, correlation structure and lineage. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* INWARD can improve the residual's resolution; it cannot decide the governance question.
- **OUTWARD.** Characterize R in effect terms and find the frame whose closure predicts its shape. We did: the residual is a union of four partial readings with a two-anchor scope, and the frame that predicts that shape is the Multiple Evidence Base literature (Tengö et al. 2014) together with two-eyed seeing (Leonard et al. 2022). Fig 6 :30 gives the travel rule: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The import is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24) and these sources share no author with the figures, so they are genuine low-overlap witnesses.

INWARD and OUTWARD were not read against each other. OUTWARD supplies the shape of the governance arrangement; INWARD supplies the resolution of the residual. Importing a governance protocol against a budget limit would add an atom that is not needed (Fig 8 :42).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, as Seat Map :287 requires: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A four-frame combination is demonstrated whose reference frame changes its answer when the inputs push on it, yet whose agreement still predicts a held-out measurement (breaks F1, Fig 2 :29-34).
2. Four frames with measured overlap near one are shown to produce a genuine, ground-checked surplus at a held-out slot (breaks F2, Fig 2 :40, Fig 8 :47).
3. A flattened average of all four streams retains per-slot residuals, full provenance and the standing to be refused when the ground turns (breaks F3 and F5, Fig 8 :5-7, Fig 1 :19).
4. An import from a knowledge holder into a dataset has full provenance, explains a named gap, benefits the holder on their own terms, and stops when consent is withdrawn (breaks F4 as a practical obstacle).
5. Community judgment is shown to be irrelevant as an anchor for questions constitutively about harm to that community (breaks F6, Fig 2 :36-37).
6. A first-order habitat boundary predicts a third-order harm on a held-out case (breaks F7, Fig 11 :52-53).
7. A single-number cost predicts both description length and settlement actions across held-out products without loss (breaks F8, Fig 12 :125-130).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn and the shape re-censused. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Start from the per-slot residual in effect terms, not from a fusion technique (F3, F5). Describe what failed to resolve at which place, variable, time and group before choosing any method.
2. Keep the earth system as the anchor. Never let the merged product become the reference the inputs are corrected against (F1).
3. Measure overlap between frames before counting agreement. If two streams share an upstream source, they are one witness (F2).
4. Treat the four as a union of overlapping conditions. Unbundle, keep verdicts per domain, and do not average (F3, section 1.3).
5. Require a source gate and a shadow test for anything carried between frames, and make the ILK channel two-way (F4).
6. State the anchor scope per sub-question: ground for physical state, affected people for consequences to people (F6).
7. Enumerate the habitat out to cascades and declare stakes; a consent protocol that cannot be violated is not a ground (F6, F7).
8. Keep description cost and settlement action cost in separate columns (F8).
9. Offer the shape and the refuter first, the sources only after a recorded verdict (Fig 10 :52, Fig 4 :22).

### Eight-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Are the four frames or anchors?** Frames. The earth system is the anchor (Fig 2 :29).
2. **Is their agreement evidence?** Only where overlap is low (Fig 2 :40, Fig 13 :38-39).
3. **Is the union a partition?** No. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57).
4. **What blocks the middle link?** The vocabulary/objective terms for incommensurable currencies; no instrument, so nobody has looked (Fig 3 :27, Fig 12 :125).
5. **Can the four be averaged?** No. R is per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22); the bond is lost (Fig 12 :47).
6. **What about ILK?** A differing closure contacted two-way, with source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, :23-24).
7. **What does "responsibly" govern?** The checking arrangement: no updating reference, declared stakes, a verifier that cannot reconstruct the fusion, and a scope condition on human judgment (Fig 10, Fig 2 :36-38).
8. **What is unreachable?** The specific algorithm, weights and roster. The figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).

### Updated verdict

The shape survives and is stronger than the operational dream. A responsible combination is a per-slot, unbundled, residual-first triangulation around a non-updating ground, with measured independence, provenance and a shadow test on every carried atom, declared stakes, a two-anchor scope, and a habitat enumerated out to cascades. The specific fusion recipe does not survive as a figure-derived claim; it is a declared `gap`. After re-census, strains are named, not hidden: one live scope note on "introduce, never subtract" applied to weights, and one resolved overlap note on Fig 2 versus Fig 8.
