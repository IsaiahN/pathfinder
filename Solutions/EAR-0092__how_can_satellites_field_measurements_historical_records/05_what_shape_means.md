## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* From the Library of Babel essay: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **shape:** a per-slot, unbundled, residual-first triangulation around a non-updating ground, with measured independence, provenance on carried atoms, declared stakes, a two-anchor scope and a habitat enumerated to its cascades.
- **name:** a particular assimilation algorithm, a particular uncertainty weighting, a particular consent form, a named list of stakeholder institutions. None of these is figure-derived.

The distinction matters most at the point where the question is most tempting. "Combine responsibly" invites a checklist. The figures return a check, not a checklist.

A correction carried from the exemplar and confirmed here: the phrase "shape, not a name" governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. It does not forbid naming a binding between things already held. In this question, the four frames are already here; naming a specific fusion method is a binding from the data-integration frame onto held structure, and may be stated as a provenanced candidate. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as a figure-derived primitive or as an atom that moves the wall (Fig 6 :22, *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*). The earlier overstatement is preserved in section 7.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the outreach packet in `08` leads with a refuter stated in effects, not with the framework's vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on the tether: the anchor is the question itself, and the books returned must be *"tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* In this folder, the anchor is the per-slot residual, not the list of four sources.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* For this solution, the stop is reached when only the operational recipe remains open and it is declared `gap`, not when a fusion product has been designed.

Applied here, the shape was obtained by describing the residual first (four partial readings of one ground, per slot), then letting that description select the figures: Fig 1 for per-slot indexing, Fig 2 for anchor and independence, Fig 8 and Fig 9 for union and unbundling, Fig 10 for the checking arrangement, Fig 11 for the habitat. No frame was chosen before the residual was described. That order is the one Fig 9 :27-28 requires.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world sources in this folder are such imports: ecosystem governance (Tengö et al.), ocean observation (Leonard et al.), metrology (Merchant et al.), operational meteorology (ECMWF), data science (Pasquier et al.), research-data infrastructure (ARDC). Each supplies a shape that the figures already constrain, and each is recorded with its provenance and overlap.

The carried-from-exemplar essay *They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The four frames chosen for this question are a visible choice, not nature's own list; treating ILK as a dimension rather than a data column is the move this essay names.

What this means practically: a named fusion algorithm is licensed only by a held-out measurement showing that the shape claim survives with it, or by an import with a source gate and a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs this question most directly. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all yield the same reading. The essay's statement is: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay adds that an instrument unable to distinguish these cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Here is the application. A merged data product reads "the ecosystem is healthy" or "fire risk is high." That reading does not determine its source. Satellite reflectance, a field plot, a documentary proxy and a knowledge holder's seasonal observation could all have contributed, and several different combinations could yield the same product. A provenance record that lists the four inputs without recording the bonds between them is the ingredient-list error of Fig 12 :47: *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."* What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name assigned from the projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A fusion product cannot be its own validator. That is the figure-grounded reason the merged product must not become the anchor (F1 in `01`). It also gives the prestige rule in the same essay: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A well-known data centre or a respected knowledge institution gives a claim a budget to be tried; it does not make the claim true.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the contact requirement that makes the ILK case specific. It notes Polanyi's *"we can know more than we can tell"* and explains that *"writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* It concludes: *"You can't send someone your history in words that only make sense once they already share it."* This is why a two-way channel is not a courtesy in a responsible combination. It is the only way the pretext travels, and the only way a correction can land. The essay is explicit that *"The loop only closes if the learner acts; ... the highlighted step is where the correction arrives, unstated"* and that *"One-way subtext can't correct anything, because the teacher never sees how it was understood."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of a responsible combination?**

1. **Per-slot, never averaged.** The residual is indexed by object slot (Fig 1 :19). One agreement score for the whole product discards the only signal that says where to look.
2. **Union, not partition; unbundled before searching** (Fig 9 :54-58). Four frames overlap in conditions. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
3. **The ground does not update** (Fig 2 :29, :33). The earth system is the anchor for physical-state questions. The merged product is a frame and must never be promoted to the anchor.
4. **Independence measured, not assumed** (Fig 2 :40; Fig 13 :38-39). Shared upstream evidence collapses four witnesses into one.
5. **Provenance and shadow test on every carried atom** (Fig 6 :12-13), two-way contact where knowledge holders are involved, because pretext does not travel in a spreadsheet and an extraction is not an import.
6. **Responsibility is a checking arrangement, not a data layer** (Fig 10 :47, :52, :59-60). A consent rule that cannot be violated is a constant the author stamped. Stakes are declared; the verifier cannot reconstruct the fusion.
7. **Two-anchor scope, stated per sub-question** (Fig 2 :36-38). Affected people are the legitimate anchor for questions about consequences to them and only an instrument for physical-state questions.
8. **Habitat enumerated to cascades** (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53). The residual that matters may be three steps out.
9. **Two currencies held apart** (Fig 12 :125-130). Bits price the description; actions price finding out whether it holds.
10. **A declared `gap` where the framework returns no name** (Fig 9 :32). The algorithm, the weights and the roster are not figure-derived.

That is the whole shape. No item names a technique.

## 6. Consequences for contact

Two consequences follow for how this result may be offered to anyone else.

**First, the claim goes before the theory.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* warns that two frames which only check against each other drift together and neither notices: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* A recipient who reads the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame. Fig 10 :42 is the hard form: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So `02` and `08` lead with the per-slot residual, the shape and the refuter, and the sources come only after the verdict is recorded.

**Second, this derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* It adds that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* and calls simulated output *"simulacra."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of whether four-frame combination works in any basin, catchment or community, and they must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction retained visibly

An earlier reading of the "shape, not a name" rule was too broad: it was taken to forbid naming any particular frame or method. The canonical wording governs a **missing** primitive, not the selection among things already held. In this question, all four frames are already present; naming a specific fusion binding is provenance, not invention, and it may be stated as a `specified`, ungrounded candidate. What remains wrong is calling such a binding a figure-derived atom or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :22). Both the overstatement and its correction are kept here so neither is quietly lost.
