## The residual in one paragraph

Each of the four named ways of observing an earth system is a frame with its own closure, currency, anchor, scale and time-depth. No one of them registers the whole state or change. The residual is the part of the earth-system state that a frame fails to register, indexed per place, variable and time, and it is the aim as well as the error signal. "Responsible combination" asks for the shape a combination must have so that it reduces that residual without destroying the independence that makes the four frames informative, and without harming the people whose knowledge and whose lives are bound to the ground. The figures return that shape. They do not return a fusion algorithm, a weighting scheme, or a governance protocol, and that is a declared `gap`, not silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not the anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Four frames tuned to agree with one another are a mirror chain, not a measurement.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 8 :47 says *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* A recipient set chosen because it already agrees spends the independence that would make its agreement mean anything.
3. **Sending the source before the residual spends the reader's independence.** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52) and *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission venues avoid that failure and the structural one together.
