# What "shape" means for this question

Sources read for this file: the eight essays by Isaiah N, 2026, of which two were opened directly this session (The Library of Babel, Oct 2 2026, https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-library-of-babel-locating-the; The Hard Problem is About Source, Aug 28 2026, https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-hard-problem-is-about-source) and six are carried from the completed exemplar's canonical mapping, marked as such rather than pretended to be re-opened: Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, Decoding Tacit Knowledge, How a Society of Agents Would Work, They're Scanning Books When They Should, How Intelligence Actually Works, Arrival of the Fittest. Also the canonical `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` and `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, both opened this session.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From The Library of Babel: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a union of seed mass and growth with a signed feedback cycle and an owed discriminator" is a shape. "The earliest supermassive black holes were direct-collapse black holes" is a name. The figures licence the first and withhold the second.

The Library of Babel essay also gives the failure mode for this exact question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is the essay-level version of Fig 9 :55, and it is why §0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` splits the residual into four slots before any channel is considered.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Proximity and recursion.** Library of Babel: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary is mass, redshift, cooling, accretion and feedback; the accumulated terms narrow the answer to a continuum over those axes.
- **Effect terms.** Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Describe "a mass too large too early for the standard growth equation," not "a DCBH."
- **Tacit knowledge.** Decoding Tacit Knowledge (exemplar mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question the plain description is: if channel X dominates, an instrument of type Y should see Z at z above 10. That is checkable without accepting the framework.
- **Stopping.** Library of Babel: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For the channel question we are not done: the instrument is not yet there, so the stop is `unreached`, not completion.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." A 21 cm depth that fixes the direct-collapse abundance, or a gravitational-wave background that fixes the merger fraction, would be such a ground reading.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Mapping the astronomical vocabulary onto the six-term substrate is such an import; it needs provenance and it adds no primitive.

They're Scanning Books (exemplar mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. None of the seed channels is one of the six, and none needs to be.

## 4. Why a channel name from a mass reading is the forbidden error

The Hard Problem is About Source, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all produce the reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Here the reading is M_BH versus M_star at z about 10. Direct collapse, a heavy Pop III remnant with fast accretion, a cluster runaway, and a primordial black hole seed can all project to the same point, as the opened papers show. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Volonteri 2025 makes the same move in the field's own vocabulary: model results are "this is what models find", not "this is how the Universe behaves".

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming anything the framework did not derive. The exemplar's correction applies here too. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. Naming an already-held binding, such as a gradient or a network, is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in any claim that names a seed channel from the figures is not that a name was used, but that the name was presented as figure-derived when no figure line governs it. Both readings are recorded here; neither is silently erased.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (exemplar mapping): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names agreeing that direct collapse is the answer is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the formation question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. One-paragraph canonical reading

For this question, "shape" means the constraint that survives before a channel is named: the formation question is a union of overlapping conditions over a seed-mass continuum, the growth loop must have a sign that lets it close before feedback cuts the fuel, the observation anchors the effect but not the source, and an instrument with low overlap is owed to discriminate among channels that currently project to the same mass and redshift. The framework returns this kind of thing, never the answer itself. The name of the channel is licensed only by a ground reading or a provenanced import with a shadow test, and neither is in hand.
