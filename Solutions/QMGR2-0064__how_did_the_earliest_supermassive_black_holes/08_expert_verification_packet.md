**What this is:** a claim in a form an astronomer can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the four domains, not `grounded`.** No measurement at high redshift is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. ## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How did the earliest supermassive black holes form" is a union compound of overlapping conditions, not one question with competing channel names. The residual indexes per slot: seed mass at birth, growth loop and duty cycle, the observation that anchors the effect, and model agreement. The measured mass and redshift settle that a massive object existed early, but several seed channels project to that same reading, so the source is underdetermined until a low-overlap instrument discriminates it. Growth is a signed cycle: accretion output re-enters as fuel supply, and the channel works when the loop closes before feedback cuts the fuel. Starting heavy buys time; fast or super-Eddington accretion is the other way to close it. The channels lie on a continuum between those extremes. The framework does not select a channel. It returns the shape, the falsifiers and the instrument owed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the question must be split into a union of overlapping conditions, and the residual indexed per slot, never averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** do the four slots actually overlap? A heavy seed changes the required duty cycle; merger efficiency changes the fit; a mass-estimate revision changes the residual without changing either. If they were independent, the union would collapse to a partition.
- **Refuter:** one condition alone shown sufficient with the other three at zero, or a demonstration that the four slots are independent.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by an astronomer against the growth literature

- **Statement:** growth is a signed cycle in which the black hole persists while the gas is consumed, and the sign is set by environment. Heavy seeds and fast accretion are the two ways to close it. This is a shape constraint, not a channel verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Also Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." The low-overlap corroborator is control theory: negative feedback "tends to promote a settling to equilibrium", while positive feedback "tends to instability via exponential growth, oscillation or chaotic behavior" (Wikipedia, Negative feedback, opened 2026-11-23).
- **What to check:** in the models you know, does accretion output actually re-enter as fuel supply through heating, outflow and inflow? Does a heavy seed at 70,000 solar masses growing at about half the Eddington rate (Latif et al 2026) and a light seed requiring super-Eddington accretion (Jeon et al 2025) represent two signs of one loop rather than two different mechanisms?
- **Refuter:** a growth history in which accretion output never re-enters as input yet the mass is reached; or a demonstration that the loop sign is irrelevant to whether growth closes.

### C3 — observation-facing; checkable against the measurement chain

- **Statement:** the high-z mass measurement anchors the effect, not the source; several channels project to the same reading; the discriminator is an instrument with low overlap, not more model fitting.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **What to check:** Volonteri 2025 writes that model results are "this is what models find" rather than "this is how the Universe behaves" and that many high-z results are still "green". Jeon et al 2025 report that both pathways reproduce observations at z about 5 to 6 but diverge at z about 10. A&A 2025 finds the preference flips with the assumed merger efficiency. Are these not the signature of an underdetermined source?
- **Refuter:** an instrument reading that separates channels at low overlap and selects one, or a mass-estimate revision that removes the timing residual entirely.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A 21 cm absorption depth at cosmic dawn that fixes the direct-collapse abundance at low overlap with UV-based models.
2. An ultradeep or lensed JWST field reaching about 10^6 solar masses at z 10 to 15 with a channel-resolved signature.
3. A gravitational-wave background or individual massive-binary detections that fix the merger contribution.
4. A mass-estimate reanalysis that removes the timing residual, which would dissolve the question as an artifact.

1. Read Fig 9 :55, :57 and Fig 1 :19 (three short passages) and say whether the four slots overlap.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: does accretion feedback re-enter as fuel supply, and is the sign of that loop the variable that decides whether growth closes?
3. Name one instrument that would separate seed channels at z above 10, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
