# The Tether's answer on the earliest supermassive black holes

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the four per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question aggregates four object slots. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The slots are: seed mass at birth; the growth loop and its duty cycle; the observation that anchors the effect; and the agreement between models. Under ordinary Eddington-limited accretion from a stellar-mass seed, the measured masses at z about 6 to 10 arrive too early. That is the residual. It is a vector of four gaps, not an average.

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union compound, not a contest between two names.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Heavy seeds reduce the required duty cycle; fast accretion compensates for light seeds; merger efficiency and selection effects move the fit. A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). Verdicts below are kept separate and never averaged.
2. **The observation is an anchor for the effect, not for the channel.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A redshift and a line width settle that a massive object existed early. They do not settle its source, because several sources project to the same reading. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
3. **Growth is a signed cycle.** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Starting heavy buys time; accreting at or above the Eddington limit is the other way to close the loop. Which one operated is not given by the loop itself.
4. **The channels form a continuum, not a dichotomy.** Inayoshi, Visbal and Haiman 2020 and Volonteri 2025 both describe a continuum of seed masses and growth histories. Under Fig 12 :124 they are members of a functional group with different qualifiers, not isomers over a fixed atom set as currently described.
5. **The discriminator is an instrument, not another argument.** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The chain already returns broad lines, host masses and upper limits; ultradeep JWST fields, 21 cm absorption, X-ray limits and a future gravitational-wave background are the sharper instruments built on those residuals.
6. **Model agreement is not corroboration.** Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Semi-analytic and simulation results share the same observed mass function and much of the same sub-grid physics.
7. **The channel name is a gap.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (the line continues with a comma). No figure line selects DCBH, Pop III remnant, cluster runaway or PBH. The slot stays open until a ground reading or a provenanced import fills it.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "direct collapse is how they formed." No figure line names a channel.
- Not "light seeds are ruled out." Both pathways reproduce z about 5 to 6 in Jeon et al 2025; they diverge at higher z where the instrument is still being aimed.
- Not "the mass estimates are the ground." They are model-bearing readings (Volonteri 2025); the photons are the ground.
- Not "simulations agreeing proves the union." Their agreement is one evidence pool.
- Not a theory of everything. A union compound keeps its own loop and its own ground.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From The Hard Problem is About Source: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a 21 cm absorption depth at cosmic dawn fix the abundance of direct-collapse seeds at low overlap with the UV-based models? (Kimura et al 2025; the DCBH/21 cm paper at arXiv:2503.22130, abstract obtained via search integration and not counted as a read source.)
- Does ultradeep blank-field or lensed JWST imaging reach a million solar masses at z about 10 to 15 and reveal a seed population with a channel-specific signature?
- Does a gravitational-wave background fix the merger contribution, or does a LISA detection resolve individual massive binary mergers?
- Do the high-z mass estimates survive a reanalysis that removes the selection bias Volonteri and others warn about? If the timing residual disappears, the question changes rather than being answered.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter

Written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 before this conclusion. The shape claim is refuted by: (1) a low-overlap instrument that separates channels and selects one; (2) one condition sufficient with the others at zero; (3) a growth history with no loop; (4) a mass-estimate revision that removes the residual; (5) a re-census finding a verbatim figure line that governs channel selection. Until one fires, the verdict is `candidate`.

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The available instruments return something that fails to resolve the source. That is depth, not proven absence: Fig 6 :27 "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The answer stops at the limit of what the instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a formation channel for the earliest supermassive black holes, and it does not claim the question has one answer. It says the question is a union of overlapping conditions: the seed mass, the growth loop, the observation that anchors the effect, and the agreement between models. The measurement settles that massive black holes existed very early, not which channel made them, because several channels project to the same mass and redshift. Growth must be a signed cycle, and the channels form a continuum between starting heavy and accreting fast. What the framework offers is that shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the name of the instrument owed to discriminate: a 21 cm depth, an ultradeep lensed field, or a gravitational-wave background. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
