# Continuum, cycle and dependency order in the formation question

**Mode: `specified` on the derivations, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The literature presents seed channels as alternatives: Pop III remnants, direct-collapse black holes, runaway stellar mergers in clusters, primordial black holes. Fig 9 :57 refuses the either/or frame: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Three questions are owed. (1) Are the channels a partition (isomers over a fixed atom set) or a continuum of arrangements? (2) What is the order of the dependency chain from environment to seed to growth to observation? (3) What is the sign of the growth loop, and does the figure's cycle structure apply?

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Partition or continuum?

- Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- Taken strictly, light and heavy seeds do **not** share an atom set: a Pop III remnant and a direct-collapse seed differ in the atoms required (a stellar remnant versus a supermassive star plus suppressed H2 cooling). So they are not isomers over the full set. They are members of a **functional group**, the shared substructure "seed + accretion + host halo", with different qualifiers on the seed atom.
- Volonteri 2025 and Inayoshi, Visbal and Haiman 2020 both describe a continuum, with lightest and heaviest seeds as extremes. That is a claim about the *values of a parameter* over a fixed functional group, not a new kind. It is therefore a composition inside the closure (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"), not a wall-moving import.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if observations show two disjoint populations with no intermediates after selection corrections, the continuum claim fails and the partition (isomer over a narrow shared set) stands.

### Q2 — What is the order?

- Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- The chain here is: environment (radiation background, metallicity, streaming velocity) → cooling and fragmentation behaviour → seed mass → accretion and merger history → observable mass at redshift → instrument reading.
- Fig 3 :24-26 names the middle link as the one that usually breaks: having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress. In this question the middle link is the mapping from an environment to a seed mass. There are models for it, but the discriminator that would test it is the weakest link (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.").
- Fig 12 :73 on the ordered bond →: "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." Reversing the order, fitting an environment after choosing a channel, is exactly the inversion the figures warn against (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second.").
- **Falsifier:** a demonstration that environment and seed mass are independent so the arrow can be swapped without changing the prediction.

### Q3 — Is growth a cycle, and what is its sign?

- Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- Accretion output (radiation) changes the input (cold gas supply). If the loop suppresses the fuel faster than the seed grows, growth stalls; if the seed grows fast enough or starts heavy enough, the loop closes on a massive object. A chain without the return edge cannot close at all, which is why a one-off seed with no sustained fuel cannot produce the observed masses.
- Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." The black hole itself is the part that persists through the loop and grows, unlike the gas that is consumed. Latif et al 2026 model a seed that survives and accretes for hundreds of Myr; Kimura et al 2025 model X-rays that suppress the fuel and flip the effective sign. Both are the same cycle read with different environmental parameters.
- The genuinely low-overlap corroborator is control theory (Wikipedia, Negative feedback, opened 2026-11-23): negative feedback "tends to promote a settling to equilibrium", while positive feedback "tends to instability via exponential growth, oscillation or chaotic behavior". The sign of the loop is the variable, exactly as Fig 12 :121 says.
- **Falsifier:** a growth history in which accretion output never re-enters as an input to the gas supply, so no loop exists, yet the observed mass is reached. That would remove the cycle claim without touching the union claim.

### Q4 — What is settled and what can compose over it?

- Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"
- Each seed channel, if it were settled by a ground reading, could serve as an atom for the next level (the observable population). Since no channel is settled, the next level is currently composed over an unsettled term. That is the structural reason the model agreement cannot be promoted to a verdict: a library that grows is not a reach that grows.
- Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Bits price the description of a channel; actions price finding out whether it holds at z above 10. A model can be cheap to state and take a whole observing campaign to settle. Summing the two into one score is the error Fig 12 :125 names.
- **Falsifier:** a channel whose description cost and settling cost are shown to be the same quantity, which would collapse the two-currency claim.

### Q5 — An analogue from outside the field, with its limit

The Library of Babel essay (opened 2026-11-23) gives the search analogue: the searcher narrows a finite set of books by accumulating vocabulary, and "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That maps onto the four-slot union, but only as a *search* analogue. It does not predict any astrophysical fact. Fig 8 :56-57 lists the field's own cases where a triangulation claim "fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward"; the analogue here is likewise a retrodiction of the method, not a result the framework produced. Recorded as corroboration of shape only.

## CONCLUSION

The seed channels are not isomers over a fixed atom set as currently described; they are members of a functional group over a continuum of seed mass, with environment setting the qualifier. The dependency order runs environment → seed → growth → observation, and the middle link lacks a channel-discriminating instrument. Growth is a signed cycle in which the black hole is the catalyst-like part that survives, and the sign is set by environment. All four claims are `specified`, ungrounded, and each has a written falsifier. What survives is the shape: union, order, signed cycle, owed instrument. No channel name survives.
