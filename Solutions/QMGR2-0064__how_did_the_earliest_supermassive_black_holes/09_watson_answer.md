## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** A mass and redshift that the standard growth equation cannot reach from a stellar-mass seed with ordinary accretion duty cycle. Arity: four overlapping conditions (seed mass, growth loop, observation, model agreement), so a union compound. Symmetry: seed mass and growth rate are ordered contributors to a mass at a redshift, not a symmetric join; swapping them changes the object. Scale: halo (10^7 to 10^8 solar masses), seed (10 to 10^6 solar masses), horizon and 21 cm, and instrument scale.
- **Domain 1, seeding frame.** Actors: pristine and metal-poor halos, H2 and HD cooling, Lyman-Werner and X-ray backgrounds, Pop III stars, supermassive stars, dense clusters, PBHs. Currency: seed mass at birth and number density. Anchor: indirect only (abundances, 21 cm depth, CMB, GW background). Union over a continuum. Dependency first.
- **Domain 2, growth frame.** Actors: gas supply, Eddington and super-Eddington accretion, AGN feedback, mergers, halo potential. Currency: mass growth rate and duty cycle. Anchor: measured M_BH and M_star, which can be revised. Union with D1 through shared terms. Dependency second.
- **Domain 3, observation frame.** Actors: JWST NIRCam/NIRSpec, ALMA, Chandra, HERA 21 cm, future LISA. Currency: spectra, luminosities, line widths, redshifts. Anchor: flux and redshift, which do not update on what a model thinks. Instrument, not a competing theory frame. Dependency third.
- **Domain 4, model and verification frame.** Actors: semi-analytic codes, cosmological simulations, reviewers. Currency: fit to the observed mass function. Anchor: the ground, never the fit. Overlaps D1 and D2 through shared initial conditions and sub-grid physics. Dependency fourth.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound of four overlapping conditions, and the earliest supermassive black holes formed through some arrangement of a seed-mass continuum and a growth loop whose sign decides whether accretion closes before feedback cuts the fuel. The observations anchor the effect, not the source: several channels project to the same mass and redshift, so the channel is underdetermined at current instrument resolution. Channels lie on a continuum between starting heavy and accreting fast, not in a dichotomy. Model agreement is one evidence pool, not corroboration. No figure line selects DCBH, Pop III remnants, cluster runaways or PBHs, so the channel name is a declared `gap`. Status is `unreached`, not `unreachable`. Mode `general` on shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`; the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a channel name instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the four-slot residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The channel slot is open, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. A discriminating instrument can fill it without touching the union or cycle shape; if one condition is shown sufficient, the union narrows to a partition and the files can be superseded. A sent claim can be replaced by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A 21 cm absorption depth that fixes the direct-collapse abundance, an ultradeep lensed JWST field that resolves a seed population, or a gravitational-wave background that fixes the merger fraction would select a channel; a mass-estimate revision that removes the timing residual would dissolve the question; a re-census finding a verbatim figure line that governs channel selection would replace the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
