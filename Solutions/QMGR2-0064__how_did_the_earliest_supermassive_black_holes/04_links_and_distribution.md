## 3. Two things in the literature that bear directly on the pitch

1. **"Both pathways can reproduce observations."** Jeon et al 2025 and the A&A 2025 model both say that light and heavy channels can fit the data, with the preference flipping under an assumption about merger efficiency. That is exactly the union verdict, and it is the reason a single-channel claim is not settled. Use it as the evidence for splitting the question, not as evidence for either channel.
2. **The mass reading is model-bearing.** Volonteri 2025 and the thesan-zoom paper (search-index abstract) both caution that overmassiveness can arise from selection effects or from a fundamental BH-dynamical-mass relation. So the observation anchors the effect while its interpretation remains a frame. This is the metamerism point: the reading does not determine the source.
