## What is being decided, in effect terms

The residual is the gap between what standard light-seed growth predicts and what is observed: roughly solar-mass seeds at high redshift cannot reach the measured 10^9 solar-mass quasars by z about 6, or the overmassive systems now found at z about 10, under ordinary Eddington-limited accretion. The options are not mutually exclusive names to be guessed. They are overlapping conditions: the initial seed mass, the accretion duty cycle and whether it can exceed the Eddington limit, the merger efficiency, and the possibility that the mass estimates themselves are biased. Each option leads to a different instrument that would settle it. The figures do not name a channel. They fix the shape the answer must have and the discriminator that is owed.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words. First, a room is not an anchor: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). Second, selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Third, a checker must not hold the interiors: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). Several published simulations of this question share initial conditions, sub-grid physics and the same observed mass function, so their agreement is one pool talking, not independent witnesses.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
