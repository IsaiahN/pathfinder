## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Adding more agreeing historians raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
