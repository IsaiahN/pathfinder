## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "colonial knowledge practice moved recordings upward and recorded the items without the bonds" is a shape. "The 1901 Census invented caste" is a name, and a ground-level historical claim. The framework cannot produce the second.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This governs the "what would refute it" section of `02_outreach_email.md`: a plain description of the failure is checkable by a historian without their adopting the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it". For this question, the search stops when the historical frame and the figure frame predict the same shape, which is why the world sources are imported with provenance and then the loop stops rather than trying to settle history.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here, the ground is archival and comparative evidence, not a measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The colonial history of knowledge frame supplies exactly such an import. Its findings are used as corroboration with provenance recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not as figure-derived verdicts.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The last sentence bears directly on this question. Treating categories as neutral is a choice to make an invisible choice appear natural. The figures call for the choice to be made visible.

## 4. Why the record's answer would be the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own adequacy" is not the point; the point is "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a finished classification table is a reading. It cannot say which route produced it, a convergent local derivation or an imported colonial one. By Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Naming a source from the table alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better record, not a name. This is F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Answer to the question "which empire, which category?"

**Nothing in the framework licenses it.** A particular historical instantiation is licensed only by the ground settling a claim, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is F7.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No. It names structural constraints (membrane direction, missing bond, unrecorded provenance), all of which are figure terms or direct compositions of them. It does not name a census, a category, or a polity as the answer. The world sources name those, and they are recorded as external imports, not as figure output.

## 5b. Correction to an overstatement (kept visible)

An earlier reading of "shape, not a name" could be taken to forbid naming *any* concrete structure in the slot. The exemplar corrected this: the README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Naming an atom already held is a binding from another frame, which is permitted if provenanced. The correction is kept here so it is not silently lost.

Applied to this question: it is legitimate to say that a census entry is a composition of held atoms with unrecorded bonds, or that a specimen is an imported atom with a stripped source gate. It is not legitimate to say the framework names which indigenous category was erased. That would be a name where only a shape is available.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40 agrees: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape in one sentence, for this question

Empire inserts a directional membrane that carries recordings instead of methods, imports atoms without their source gate, drops the bond information from what it classifies, manufactures agreement from a shared evidence pool, and leaves artefacts whose routes survive only in damaged provenance records, while an empty archive registers as reachability rather than absence.
