## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Cohn's "investigative modality" is the world analogue of the collection-plus-classification chain.** It names one process that defines a body of information, gathers it, orders and classifies it, then converts it into reports, returns, gazetteers, codes, and encyclopedias. That is exactly the D1 to D2 dependency in the census (Fig 3 :20). It corroborates the chain, but it does not by itself establish the figure verdict; the figure line governs.
2. **Bowker and Star and Trouillot independently converge on the record point.** Each standard and category "valorizes some point of view and silences another" (Bowker and Star); every narrative is "a particular bundle of silences" (Trouillot). These are Fig 8 :51 ("Only the record of where each came from separates them") and Fig 13 :128 ("Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it") arriving from information science and historiography. Because these authors are external to the framework, this is real corroboration. They do share a partial critical pool (Foucault, Said), so it is not two fully independent witnesses.
3. **Herr is a conflict, honestly reported.** Her 2025 paper argues that Frickerian epistemic justice cannot promote decoloniality, because the colonized at conquest are not members of the same epistemic community as the colonizers. This conflicts with a naive "apply epistemic injustice straight to empire" move, not with the figure verdict. Per the conflict rule it sends us back to the census: it strengthens the decision to keep D1 (contact between closures that differ) separate from D2 (classification inside a frame). It is recorded as a conflict in `09_watson_answer.md` and the ledger.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :60** "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." Say plainly that the archive is both the evidence and a product of the process under study.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, restated

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter.

## Hard precondition

Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It is offered as `specified`, with refuters written in advance, and it stops at a shape (Fig 9 :32). It is not presented as a historical finding. If a recipient fires one of the six refuters, that part is retracted before any theory is shown.
