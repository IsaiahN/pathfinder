# The Tether's answer on colonial knowledge collection and classification

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on its instantiation in colonial knowledge practice, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Under empire, what is gathered, how it is named, and what record survives of its route all change in a systematic way. The residual is measured per slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"), so it is not one global claim about "colonial knowledge". It splits into three dependent operations (collection, classification, record) whose evidence comes from three different literatures (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape (one loop per domain)

**D1, collection.** Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What empire carried from the periphery to the centre was recordings of particular occasions, specimens, counts, observations, rather than re-applicable methods. The receiving frame could move its wall only by contact with a closure that differed (Fig 6 :23), and such a contact is an import that requires a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12, :13). The structural finding is that the import ran with a weakened source gate. Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 × Fig 6).

**D2, classification.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Classifications recorded the items but not the relations that made them mean something in the source frame. The result is the flat-formula collapse: same ingredients, different arrangements, nothing downstream able to tell them apart (Fig 12 :95). Fig 1 :27 disqualifies the classifier's own categories as evidence for the classifier: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 × Fig 1).

**D3, the record.** Fig 13 :128: "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 13 :130: "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." The finished artefact cannot disclose its own route; the provenance record carries it, and empire damaged that record. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". An empty place in the archive is therefore a reachability verdict, not proof that the knowledge never existed. Verdict `general`, triangulated (Fig 13 × Fig 8).

**Across domains, agreement.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Metropolitan consensus on a scheme is efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :41), not corroboration of it.

**The limit.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework does not name a census, a category, or an empire as the answer. That is a ground-level historical claim.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that knowledge under empire was *only* extraction. Local actors collaborated, adapted, and resisted; the PolSci survey records this and it is imported as corroboration, not denied.
- Not that any specific category was "invented" by empire. That is a name, not a shape (F7). The debate over how far caste was constructed is a ground-level dispute recorded in the world source.
- Not that the archive is worthless or that silence equals absence. Fig 8 :39 says the opposite about what a failed search can establish.
- Not a verdict on moral culpability. The framework describes structure; it does not assign blame.
- Not `grounded`. No ground reading was taken. No archive was opened by this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating record, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- For a given slot, was the bond information (the local relations) recorded alongside the items? A dated catalogue or survey notebook that shows this would fire refuter 2.
- Did what crossed the membrane include a re-applicable method with intact provenance and a shadow test? A dated correspondence or manual would fire refuter 1.
- Did two frames with genuinely low evidence overlap independently settle the same classification? That would fire refuter 3.
- Does an artefact disclose its own route without an external provenance record? That would fire refuter 4.
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4: derive a figure term from the others, show the flat formula preserves arrangement information, or find a figure line that names a historical institution as the answer.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The colonial history of knowledge frame already holds the concrete cases; importing them with provenance is permitted (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). What remains outside is the ground: the archives themselves. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Where the provenance record is damaged, the honest report is `Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence`, not a guess about what must have happened.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which census or category empire invented, and it does not settle a historical argument. It says that empire inserts a directional membrane: what travels from the periphery to the centre is recordings of particular occasions rather than re-applicable methods, so what arrives looks like knowledge while being a description of one occasion. At the centre, the items are named and arranged, but the local relations that gave them meaning are not recorded with them, so two arrangements sharing the same items become indistinguishable downstream. Agreement among central institutions then compounds without adding evidence, because those institutions draw on one evidence pool. The finished artefact cannot disclose its own route; that route lives only in a provenance record, which the same process damaged. So an empty place in the archive is a verdict about reachability, not proof that the knowledge was absent. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of record that would close it. The ground, not agreement, decides.
