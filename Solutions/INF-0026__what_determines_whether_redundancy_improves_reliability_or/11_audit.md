## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's finding titles. The residual line I worked from was: *per-slot gap between predicted and observed reliability gain when a redundant copy is added under a finite capacity budget.*

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: two conditions, separate draws versus one shared cause. Symmetry: under separate draws the copies are interchangeable operands, so order carries nothing; under a shared cause they are k spellings of one state. Scale: bit, component, committee. The per-slot indexing is required because averaging would hide the live residual.
- **Domain S1, the physical channel.** Actors: signal, noise, receiver, capacity budget. Currency: bits and error probability. Anchor: measured error rate. Question: does the noise repeat or vary across copies?
- **Domain S2, the artifact.** Actors: component, failure cause, maintainer. Currency: failure probability. Anchor: failures in service. Question: is the failure coupled?
- **Domain S3, the observers.** Actors: judges, shared evidence, the state of the world. Currency: correctness probability. Anchor: the state of the world. Question: are the errors independent of one another given the truth?
- **Domain S4, the claim-checking arrangement.** Actors: claim, frames, ground, seat. Currency: agreement versus evidence, bits versus actions. Anchor: the ground. Question: is this a convention or a verdict?

Order by dependency: describe the residual, discriminate the source of the copies, price the bargain, settle against the ground. A cost paid before the source is known is spent above the break.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed zero.

1. **Sent up (x):** Fig 8 :47-49 (independence decides surplus), Fig 5 :43 (the bargain), Fig 12 :125-126 (two currencies), Fig 9 :54-56 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** "redundancy helps when the copies are independent and the cost pays."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete engineering claim, for example "three copies at rho = 0.3 give three independent votes and three times the reliability."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip down:

- the effective count is bounded by 1/rho, not by N (the world sources give the formula, the figures do not);
- the coupled term Q_C and the coherence cost kappa can reverse the gain even when copies differ;
- the two currencies (bits and actions) cannot be added, so "three times the reliability" is not a costed claim.

One thing survives cleanly: copies drawing on one evidence pool carry one observation stated several times. That is the residue worth sending. R_T is recorded qualitatively as the set of dropped constraints, not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## Strains and re-census

- **Fig 13 :37 versus engineering redundancy.** The first reading made the "redundant rather than minimal" line govern the whole question. Re-census: that line governs a generating set; a receiver combining two noisy observations is a different object. The governing line is Fig 8 :47-49. Recorded as a retraction in `01` §5, not erased.
- **Wisdom-of-crowds "horse race".** Larrick, Mannes and Soll report that discussion can transmit knowledge as well as error. Read as "sharing is always bad" this conflicts with Fig 8. Re-census resolves it: sharing that brings new contact with the ground is Fig 8's import; sharing that recycles one pool is Fig 7's mirror chain. The figures predict both cases, so the world source refines rather than flips the verdict.
- **Two currencies versus the question's single "capacity".** The question frames the cost as one scarce resource. Fig 12 :125-126 says there are two. This is not a strain inside the figures; it is a limit in the question's framing, and the solution reports it as such.
