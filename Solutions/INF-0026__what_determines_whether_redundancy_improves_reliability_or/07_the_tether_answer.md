# The Tether's answer: what decides the sign of redundancy

**Mode: `specified` on the deciding relation, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A finite capacity budget, and a choice to repeat. The observed gain from a repetition is sometimes positive, sometimes zero, sometimes negative once coordination cost is counted. R is the gap between predicted and observed gain, per repetition slot. Fig 1 :19 says the residual is indexed per slot, and Fig 1 :22 says "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## 2. The shape

1. **Independence of the repeats decides whether the agreement carries information.** Fig 8 :47-49: "Independence is one quantity read twice. How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 13 :36-37: "If two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information, and one of them would be redundant rather than minimal."

2. **The sign is continuous in overlap, not a single threshold given by the figures.** Fig 6 :53-54: "Independence decides whether more of them buy more search or only more spelling, which makes it the parameter rather than a caveat." Copies whose correlation is fixed at origin cannot be refreshed by more use (Fig 10 :80-81); replacement with a different closure is the refresh.

3. **A bargain licenses the extra capacity.** Fig 5 :43: "A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before." Redundancy is licensed only when the capacity spent plus the error it leaves is less than the error before it. Fig 5 :45 connects this to Shannon coding as borrowed machinery.

4. **Two currencies, not one.** Fig 12 :125-126: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Capacity is bits; verification is actions. A one-number optimization is blind to one of them.

5. **Convention and verdict are different jobs.** Fig 2 :39-43: correlation is "disqualifying for a verdict" and "efficient for a convention". A repeated acknowledgement, a shared protocol word or a duplicated label wants agreement, and there high overlap is the product. A claim about the world wants independent contact, and there the same overlap is the collapse. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.

6. **The order is source first, bargain second, settlement third.** Fig 3 :20 orders links by dependency. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a code recommendation. No figure line names a repetition code, a parity check, an error-correcting code or a redundancy percentage.
- Not a critical correlation or a channel capacity. Those are imports, with source gates and shadow tests, never figure derivations.
- Not "redundancy is always good" and not "redundancy is always waste". The sign depends on overlap and on whether the setting is a convention or a verdict.
- Not that more independent judges always win. Fig 9 :60 says filters are for the budget, witnesses for the verdict; adding judges does not replace a ground.
- Not `grounded`. No error rate, failure rate or majority accuracy was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

An instrument that discriminates sources, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a green reading "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

- **Channel test:** vary the correlation of the noise draws while holding rate and length fixed, and measure the error curve. If independent draws lower the error and shared draws do not, the independence claim is supported.
- **Component test:** separate Q_I from Q_C in a common-cause audit (ECSS handbook basic parameter model). If adding copies reduces the independent term but leaves the coupled term fixed, the coupling claim is supported.
- **Committee test:** range each judge separately against the same fixed ground and measure the effective sample size, rather than counting heads.
- **Bargain test:** measure bits spent and actions spent separately, and check the MDL inequality. If the inequality fails, the design is wasteful even when it is independent.
- **Standing falsifiers:** an independent scheme that never improves at any cost; a fully correlated scheme that improves a verdict without new contact; a scheme with no new information that lowers both currencies; a re-census showing Fig 8 :47-49 governs conventions only. All are in §3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The figures decide the shape; they do not decide the number. The number has to travel from a frame whose closure differs, cast in effect terms first (Fig 9 :30-31), with provenance recorded (Fig 8 :50-52). The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` supply candidate numbers as secondary corroboration only. They do not upgrade this answer to `grounded`, and agreement among them that shares an evidence pool is one witness, not two.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a code or a threshold. It says that what decides whether redundancy helps is how much the repeated signals draw on the same evidence. If they share one cause or one evidence pool, their agreement is one opinion said several times: agreement compounds while evidence does not, and the extra capacity buys a convention, not a verdict. If they are independent draws, each copy can carry a new observation and the union has a surplus neither had alone. Even then the design must pass a bargain: the bits spent plus the error left behind must be less than the error before, and the actions needed to check the copy are a second currency that does not add to the bits. For a shared protocol word, high overlap is the product; for a claim about the world it is the failure. The framework returns that shape and the refuters written in advance. The number comes from an experiment, not from the figures.
