# Expert verification packet: the Tether on "before the Big Bang"

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of cosmology can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the barrier shape, `specified` on the reach claim. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the boundary is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the astronomical question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A terminal hot Big Bang would be a last room, and the figures forbid a last room (Fig 7 :6), a top (Fig 4 :33) and a bottom (Fig 12 :104). So any answer to the question of what came before must have an outside room, marked `OUTSIDE(???)`. The framework does not name that room: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). A single bounce and a cyclic universe are different structures, a directional membrane crossing (Fig 4 :21) versus a closing cycle (Fig 12 :121), and are isomers over a shared atom set under Fig 12 :117. Whether any of them exists physically is a ground question; current readings, the BICEP/Keck tensor limit and Planck's non-Gaussianity constraints, do not discriminate, so the verdict is **unreached at this budget**, not yes and not no. The application of a frame-level rule to physical time is declared as an analogue, not a measurement.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 7, 4, 12 and 9

- **Statement:** a terminal first frame is not a composable term; an outside is structurally required; the outside is not nameable by the framework.
- **Sources, verbatim:**
  - Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room"
  - Fig 4 :33 "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."
  - Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"

- **Refuter:** re-census the figures and admit a composable last room or bottom; or derive one specific prior phase from the six-term set alone, with no import and no measurement, excluding all rivals.
- **Scope caveat:** this is a claim about frames. Its application to a physical temporal boundary is an analogue (see C3).

### C2, physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the current literature

- **Statement:** the instruments are already returning readings, but none discriminates among bounce, ekpyrotic/cyclic and quantum-gravity prior frames. Therefore the ground verdict is "unreached", not "no".
- **Sources read:**
  - BICEP/Keck Collaboration, Phys.Rev.Lett.127,151301 (2021): "r_0.05<0.036 at 95% confidence" and "These are the strongest constraints to date on primordial gravitational waves." https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00483
  - Planck Collaboration, 2019: f_NL^local = -0.9 +/- 5.1 and "we also place tight constraints but do not detect any signal" in the additional cases. https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.05697
  - Novello and Perez Bergliaffa, Phys.Rept.463:127-213 (2008): "We review the general features of nonsingular universes (i.e. those that go from an era of accelerated collapse to an expanding era without displaying a singularity) as well as cyclic universes." https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1634
  - Khoury, Ovrut, Steinhardt and Turok, Phys.Rev.D64:123522 (2001): "A prediction that distinguishes this scenario from standard inflationary cosmology is a strongly blue gravitational wave spectrum." https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0103239
- **What to check:** is the BICEP/Keck limit correctly described as a limit and not a detection? Does the ekpyrotic blue-tensor prediction mean that the r limit does not by itself rule out that scenario? Are the Planck non-Gaussianity numbers consistent with zero as stated?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument already returning a reading that uniquely selects one prior frame and excludes the others. That would close the slot and move C2 toward `grounded`. Alternatively, show that the cited readings do discriminate, which would refute the "unreached" claim.

### C3, cross-domain; the declared strain, checkable by a philosopher of cosmology

- **Statement:** the frame-level "no last room" rule bears on the physical question only as an analogue. The singularity theorems establish a boundary inside classical general relativity, which is a frame, so they corroborate the need for an outside frame rather than establishing a frame-independent first cause.
- **Sources, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
  - Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
  - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Philosophy of Cosmology" (2017): "the singularity theorems (Hawking & Ellis 1973) ... establish that the existence of an initial singularity is robust" in models satisfying physically plausible assumptions; and "Cosmological physics extending beyond the physics horizon faces an underdetermination threat due to the lack of independent lines of relevant evidence." https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cosmology/
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that the frame-level rule has no bearing at all on a physical temporal boundary. This removes the application but not the metatheory claim; the packet is narrowed accordingly. Or demonstrate that the re-census is wrong and the singularity theorem does establish a frame-independent last room.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a bounce occurred, or that any particular prior model is correct.
- Not that there definitely was a physically prior universe. The structural requirement of an outside *frame* is not a measurement of a prior *universe*.
- Not that there was nothing before. Non-detection is not a negative result (Fig 8 :39).
- Not "the singularity theorems prove a beginning" in a frame-independent sense.
- Not a unified or final theory. No last room applies to the framework itself.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an instrument.
- Not a derivation of bounce or cyclic models from the six-term set. They are imports with provenance and a shadow test.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A tensor measurement selecting one prior-frame prediction and excluding the others.
