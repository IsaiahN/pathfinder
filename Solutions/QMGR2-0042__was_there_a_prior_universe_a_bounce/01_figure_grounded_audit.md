## 0. The claim under audit, stated as a residual in effect terms

The hot Big Bang model is a frame that retrodicts the universe from a hot, dense early state forward. It fits a great deal of data and is silent about its own boundary: it cannot say what, if anything, preceded that state, or whether the state is the whole story. Stated in effect terms, the residual R is:

> **R:** the primordial boundary condition is measured (perturbation amplitude, tilt, statistics, tensor content) but not explained by the hot-Big-Bang frame; the frame has no term for its own origin, and no instrument currently returns a reading that discriminates among the candidate frames that claim to supply one.

What each answer leads to:

- **Option A, "nothing before, the hot Big Bang is absolute":** treats the frame as a terminal room. In the figures' vocabulary this is a last room, a bottom, a top. It stops the search by declaration rather than by the ground.
- **Option B, "a bounce":** imports a prior contracting phase and a crossing. It supplies an outside room and a mechanism, but names a specific atom (a bounce) where the figures return only a shape.
- **Option C, "another phase" (cyclic, ekpyrotic, eternal inflation, a quantum-gravity phase):** also imports an outside frame, with a different atom. B and C are not one answer; they are a union of overlapping conditions.

The sought-for shape is made an ordinary term before the run, as the method requires. Bind it now:

> **SHAPE (bound before derivation):** an answer to this question must (i) refuse a terminal first room, (ii) name a prior frame only by a ground reading or a provenanced import, and (iii) report "unreached" rather than "no" when no instrument discriminates. Falsifiers for this shape are written in §3, before the conclusion.

## 1. Domain census before any figure line is chosen

R by arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Where no anchor can be named, it is not yet a domain.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: one frame's boundary condition, but three candidate replies (terminal, bounce, other phase), so a union of conditions rather than one question. Symmetry: "before" is an ordered relation, not a symmetric join. The six-term set defines time as "the ordered succession of differences" (Fig 13 :12), so reading "before" as interchangeable with "after" strains. Scale: the hot Big Bang is one room; a prior phase is a different room, reached by a crossing (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, observational cosmology (the ground candidate).** Actors: CMB temperature and polarization maps, primordial non-Gaussianity, B-mode polarization, large-scale structure, light-element abundances. Currency: measured observables (r, f_NL, n_s, etc.). Anchor: instruments and data, which do not change because a theory thinks otherwise. This anchor exists.
- **Domain 2, theoretical early-universe frames.** Actors: hot Big Bang/ΛCDM, inflation, ekpyrotic and cyclic scenarios, loop quantum cosmology, generic bounce models. Currency: predicted primordial spectra and their statistics. Anchor: the readings of Domain 1. Multiple low-overlap frames currently fit the same readings, so this is a union, not a partition.
- **Domain 3, the metatheory (the Tether figures).** Actors: rooms, closures, imports, the six terms and the bonds. Currency: composition and residual. Anchor: the figures as metalaws for design questions. This is not the astronomical ground and must not be presented as one.
- **Domain 4, verification / outreach.** Actors: cosmologists acting as outside frames. Currency: residuals, never agreement. Anchor: the readings of Domain 1. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so agreement here is one witness, not corroboration.
- **Partition or union.** Domain 2 is a union of overlapping conditions, so it must be unbundled before searching (Fig 9 :54). Domain 1 against Domain 2 is a partition for the purpose of this audit: the instrument settles, the frame predicts, and the two are not the same office.
- **Dependency order (Fig 3).** Perception (which observables register a prior phase?) before vocabulary (do we have terms for a pre-hot-Big-Bang phase?) before objective (what would count as a discriminating signal?) before planning before carrying. Fig 3 :27 names the failure mode: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." For this question the link that is weak is objective: we have data, but no agreed observable that uniquely selects a prior frame.

Habitat enumeration (Fig 11 :48): "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." The habitat here is the set of things in contact with the boundary condition: CMB polarization, non-Gaussianity, large-scale structure, gravitational-wave backgrounds, quantum-gravity proposals, brane models. It is enumerated, not composed.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8, each with verbatim lines and a refuter

### F1: A terminal hot Big Bang is the forbidden last room (structural, `general`)

Fig 7 :6 names **"the limit: no last room"**. Fig 7 :30-31: "every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives". Fig 4 :33: "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress." Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom".

Option A declares the hot Big Bang to be the final room, the bottom and the top, the one frame after which nothing can be asked. The figures forbid that room categorically, not because a telescope saw past it, but because a terminal frame would require both a bottom and a top, which the theory rules out definitionally. The canonical usage rule makes the same point: the framework is a generator of the shape of an answer, not a final answer.

**Mode:** `general` and structural. This is a claim about frames, not a measurement of physical time. The cross-domain strain is named in §7 and is not hidden.

**Refuter:** re-census the figures and admit a composable last room or a bottom (for example, derive a seventh primitive, or show that composition has a terminal element). If such a room is legal under the figures, this finding is withdrawn.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 7 (room chain), Fig 4 (membrane) and Fig 12 (recursion), three low-overlap routes.

### F2: The figures return a shape, not a name (`general`)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom".

The figures can say that an outside room is required (F1). They cannot say whether that room is a bounce, a cyclic phase, a brane collision, eternal inflation or a quantum-gravity regime. That is a `gap` for the "which model" sub-question, and it is reported as a gap, not filled by preference. Each candidate is a binding from Domain 2, provenanced as such. The canonical figures README states the limit directly: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)".

**Refuter:** derive a specific prior phase from the six-term set alone, with no import and no ground reading, such that all competing prior frames are excluded. If that succeeds, the slot is nameable by the framework and this finding fails.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 and the canonical figures README.

### F3: SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD (`specified`)

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 separates three questions. Fig 6 :36-37: "It tells a question that nothing could ever answer apart from one that is merely hard. It is not a rule about theory." Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."

Applied in order:

- **SEARCHED.** Is the discriminating reading already in our closure and unfound? The BICEP/Keck limit r_0.05 < 0.036 and the Planck non-Gaussianity constraints are readings that already exist. More analysis of existing data is SEARCHED, and is the cheapest move.
- **INWARD.** Sharpen the instrument we already have. BICEP/Keck and Planck are instruments already returning something (a limit, not a detection), so there is an edge to extend from. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **OUTWARD.** Import a frame whose closure predicts the boundary shape: bounce models (Novello and Perez Bergliaffa 2008), the ekpyrotic scenario (Khoury and colleagues 2001), loop quantum cosmology (Ashtekar and Singh 2011). Each import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :11-13).

A claim that nothing could ever answer this question would be a rule about a sensor for something that has never registered. That is not what the figures say: theory may precede its instrument. The honest statement is that the question is currently unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle. Fig 8 :40-43 keeps those facts distinct.

**Refuter:** exhibit a proto-instrument already returning a reading that uniquely selects one prior frame, or show that no reading at any resolution can bear on the boundary. The first moves the verdict to `grounded`; the second moves it to underivable. Either refutes the "unreached at this budget" sub-claim.

**Status:** `specified`, single-source for the instrument rule (Fig 6), corroborated by the world sources in §9 of `09_watson_answer.md` as a separate evidence pool.

### F4: Absence of a signal is not proof of no prior phase (`general`)

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

No detection of a primordial B-mode signal, or no detection of non-Gaussianity, does not yield "there was no prior universe". It yields "the current instruments do not discriminate". Reporting silence as a negative result is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 warns about.

**Refuter:** seal the room, that is, exhibit a frame with no outside from which a complete search is possible, and complete the search. If no frame can be sealed, the refuter cannot fire and the verdict stands as unreached.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 8, Fig 9 and Fig 13.

### F5: The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (`general`)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

"Was there a prior universe, a bounce, or another phase?" bundles three conditions: (i) is the hot Big Bang terminal, (ii) if not, which frame supplies the prior phase, and (iii) can any instrument tell them apart? A single lever will not clear all three. Answering (i) affirmatively does not answer (ii); answering (ii) theoretically does not answer (iii). Each gets its own loop and its own ground.

**Refuter:** show that the three conditions are composable into one term with one ground, such that a single measurement settles all three. If so, the union reading is wrong.

**Status:** triangulated for the unbundling rule across Fig 9 and the domain census.

### F6: The anchor is an instrument, not a consensus (`general`)

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A show of hands among cosmologists, or a count of papers citing bounce models, is not an anchor. It moves when the field's fashion moves. The anchor here is a measurement that would be the same regardless of which theory anyone prefers. Note also the cost asymmetry: this question is cheap to be wrong about in the sense that no engineering decision hangs on the answer, but expensive to be wrong about in the sense that a falsely named prior phase would misdirect a generation of instrument proposals. Independence is bought in proportion to that cost.

**Refuter:** exhibit a consensus mechanism whose output is invariant under changes in what the participants believe about the boundary. If one exists, consensus can serve as an anchor here.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 lines 29, 35 and 42.

### F7: An imported prior phase needs a source gate and a shadow test (`specified`)

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".

Every concrete prior-phase model imports at least one atom the hot-Big-Bang frame does not hold: a contracting branch, a brane collision, a discrete quantum geometry, an inflaton history. Two things follow. First, the import is legitimate only if it explains a gap the original frame cannot (a shadow test), and its provenance is recorded. Second, importing does not add a primitive to the world; it gives this frame access to one another frame holds. A bounce is not made true by being imported.

**Refuter:** exhibit a prior-phase model that adds no atom to the hot-Big-Bang closure and is still not derivable from it. If such a thing exists, the import analysis is wrong.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8.

### F8: Filters rank the budget, they do not decide the verdict (`general`)

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Ranking candidate bounce models by tractability, popularity, or how easily they fit current data is a budget filter. It decides which model gets checked first. It does not decide which model is true. Nor should the residuals for different observables (tensor modes, non-Gaussianity, spatial curvature, large-scale structure) be averaged into one global "bounce score"; Fig 1 :19 requires the residual to be indexed per slot.

**Refuter:** exhibit a ranking whose order is identical to the ground's verdict across all candidate frames. Until such a ranking is demonstrated, a filter is a filter.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 1.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any conclusion or outreach: a falsifier written in advance is a design instrument (the seat-map rule invoked in the exemplar at Seat Map :287). The bound shape claim is:

> **SHAPE:** a terminal hot Big Bang is not composable; an outside room is required; the identity of that room is not nameable by the figures; the physical question is currently unreached, not settled.

It is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **Terminal-room refuter.** A figure re-census admits a last room or a bottom as a legal composition (breaks F1).
2. **Nameability refuter.** A specific prior phase is derived from the six-term set alone, with no import and no ground reading, excluding all rivals (breaks F2).
3. **Instrument refuter.** An instrument already returns a reading that uniquely selects one prior frame and excludes the others; this does not refute the existence of an outside, but it refutes the "slot stays open / unreached" part of the shape and moves it toward `grounded` (breaks F3 and F4).
4. **Bundle refuter.** The three bundled conditions are shown to have one ground, so the union reading is false (breaks F5).
5. **Cross-domain refuter.** A cosmologist shows that the figures' "no last room" is a claim about argumentative frames only and has no bearing whatever on a physical temporal boundary. This would not refute the shape as a metatheory statement, but it would remove its bearing on this question, which is the application claimed here.

If any fires, the claim is withdrawn or narrowed, and the withdrawal is recorded here rather than silently edited away.
