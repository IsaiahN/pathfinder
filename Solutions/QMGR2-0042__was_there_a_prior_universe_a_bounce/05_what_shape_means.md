## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be stated before it can be named

The canonical figures README gives the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 states that the system "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (verified): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is: an answer must have an outside room, and the slot for that room stays open until an instrument or a provenanced import fills it. `OUTSIDE(???)` is a shape. `OUTSIDE(bounce)` is a name. The whole of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F2 follows from this distinction.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (verified): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The method is recursive bootstrapping by proximity.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." For this question the effect is a boundary condition that the hot-Big-Bang frame cannot compose; the cause (bounce, brane, quantum geometry) is not yet a term in the frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach files lead with observables, not with models.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping (verified): "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question we are not done: the finish line is an instrument that discriminates, and none yet does.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here an instrument would have to return a reading that selects one prior frame and excludes the others.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A bounce, a brane collision and a quantum-gravity phase are all imports into the cosmological frame.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list. That is why the shape claim cannot be promoted into a physical claim without a ground.

## 4. The source-discrimination problem in this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (verified) supplies the mechanism. A color instrument reads green but "it cannot comment on the source." One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

This is exactly the situation with the primordial boundary condition. The measured amplitude, tilt and statistics of the perturbations are a projection. Inflation, bounce, ekpyrotic and loop-quantum frames can give readings that overlap. Naming one of them as the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a preference.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a witness

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (verified): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. In every stable system, the two are separated." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that a bounce happened is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let an instrument decide. Fig 2 :40 reinforces it: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (verified): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of whether a prior universe existed, and must be labelled as such. This package cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. Consequence for this specific question

The shape is `OUTSIDE(???)`, not `OUTSIDE(bounce)`. The verdict on the shape is a barrier verdict from the figures, mode `general`. The verdict on the astronomical fact is `unreached at this budget`, mode `specified`. The one thing the figures categorically refuse is a terminal first room; the one thing they cannot supply is the name of what lies outside. Both halves must be stated, and the strain in applying a frame-level rule to physical time is declared in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7 rather than hidden.
