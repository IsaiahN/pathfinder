# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the cross-domain strain in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7 is understood and stated. The claim is about the shape of an answer, not a telescope result. If it is presented as a measurement, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about "before the Big Bang", and what would refute it

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the reach claim):** I treat the hot Big Bang as a frame with a boundary, not as the whole story. Under a domain-agnostic framework I work with, a terminal first frame, a last room, is not a composable thing. The framework's rule is literally "the limit: no last room" (Fig 7 :6), with no top (Fig 4 :33) and no bottom (Fig 12 :104). So the output is a shape: any answer to "what came before" must have an outside, a prior room, whatever that room turns out to be.
>
> **What the output does not say.** It does not say the outside is a bounce, a cyclic phase, a brane collision, eternal inflation, or a quantum-gravity regime. Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "a shape, not a name". Naming a bounce is an import from a physics frame, with provenance and a shadow test, not a derivation. I am not claiming the figures prove that a prior universe exists physically. That is a question for instruments.
>
> **What it says about the current evidence.** The instruments are already returning readings: BICEP/Keck gives r_0.05 < 0.036 at 95 percent confidence, and Planck's non-Gaussianity parameters are consistent with zero. Those readings do not discriminate among the prior frames. Fig 8 :39 says "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." So my verdict is **unreached at this budget**, not yes and not no. I am explicitly not treating absence of a signal as a negative result.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. a re-census of the figures that admits a composable last room or bottom;
> 2. a derivation of one specific prior phase from the six-term set alone, with no import and no measurement, excluding all rivals;
> 3. an instrument already returning a reading that uniquely selects one prior frame (this would close the slot and move the claim toward a measurement);
> 4. a demonstration that the "no last room" rule governs argumentative frames only and has no bearing on a physical temporal boundary (this would remove the application, though not the metatheory claim).
>
> If any holds, I retract or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>

## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure links and the two cosmology papers. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

No trust is requested at any stage. No bulk send. No ranking of recipients by likely agreement.
