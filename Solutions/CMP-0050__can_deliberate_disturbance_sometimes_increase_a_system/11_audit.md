## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among library Γ, action a and habitat return o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time. A two-place reading (system, resilience score) would lose the action and the prediction, so it is rejected. The comparison "increase" needs a threshold bond; the time index needs an ordered bond.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor does not update on the disturbance's opinion (Fig 2 :25), and reversing the perturbation does not restore the prior state. "More resilient" is a threshold against a control, not an absolute quantity (Fig 12 :87).
- **Scale:** the slot is a patch, but "long-term" is only visible across a chain of handoffs, and the cascade crosses scales. The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the membrane transform is lossy (Fig 4 :30).

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *disturbance helps when it reconnects a system to what settles it, and when the gain is measured from outside.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific patch. Result: *burn the patch and monitor recovery,* or *import a new practice and keep measuring the same score.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a system failing C2 merely to reconnect harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Reconnects" does not specify that the settling reading must not move when pushed (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a manager to measure success with a quantity the intervention produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the cascade. "Reconnect" has no place for second- and third-order effects (Fig 11 :52). Coming back down, the same move becomes kudzu.
4. It loses the component split. "The gain" presumes one quantity; Walker et al. 2004 show four that can move in opposite directions.
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would separate a genuine gain from a changed reading, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
