## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for CMP-0050 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about disturbance and resilience that can be stated before any particular disturbance, ecosystem, metric or management regime is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a deliberate disturbance helps only when it changes contact, its cascade is enumerated outward, repetition is replaced by contact with a differing closure, and the gain is measured by a source-discriminating instrument against a control.*
- Name, withheld: *prescribed burning every five years, a particular grazing regime, a particular flood pulse, the Resilience Alliance assessment protocol, or a particular recovery-time metric.*

The distinction matters here because resilience practice is almost entirely in the naming business. Walker et al. name latitude, resistance, precariousness and panarchy; the fire literature names prescribed burning and fire severity; the assessment literature names response diversity and connectivity. Those are bindings from their frames onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "resilience", "disturbance regime", "adaptive cycle", "rigidity trap", "response diversity", "precariousness" and "natural experiment". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."* The effect description (duration of a low-residual slot under a deliberate perturbation, indexed per patch) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating CMP-0050 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question. It is reinforced by Walker et al. 2004, who say different management interventions are required for each of the four components of resilience.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions C1 and C2 are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the seven falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32 *"→ one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For CMP-0050, neither has happened for the general question. There is no controlled study that pays for "this disturbance increased long-term resilience in this patch for this reason" across enough slots to settle the general claim, and the fire and resilience names were adopted without a source gate at the framework level. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

Hamilton and Burton 2023 come closest to a real instrument: they treated a wildfire as a natural experiment and measured before and after. That settles a case, not the general shape, and the framework's own rule says a case is a recording until its method is carried up and re-applied (Fig 4 :22-25).

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
> *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*

This is the exact error a resilience explanation makes when it reads a shorter recovery time and names its source. A faster return can come from a genuine increase in resilience, from a changed reading, from a shift into a different basin, from reduced disturbance severity, or from luck. The reading does not determine the source. Aslan et al. 2024 sharpen the point: they distinguish variables that are *"significantly predictive of resilience"* from those that are *"significant indicators of resilience"*. An indicator is a reading; a predictor is closer to a source. Treating the indicator as the source is the metamerism error, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to resilience research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about ecosystems.

For outreach this means: a list of famous resilience researchers who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular disturbance is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. A disturbance such as a low-severity fire regime is a **binding** from the ecology frame onto atoms already in the habitat, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall by itself (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) and calling it a derivation rather than an import. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

A second narrowing: I initially treated the *"resilience is not always a good thing"* line in Walker et al. 2004 as a refutation of the question. It is not. The question is constitutively about a desired configuration; the line is a warning that the desired configuration must be named before "increase" has a referent. That warning is now part of the shape (which basin, which component), not a rival verdict.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about disturbance and resilience. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For CMP-0050 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction (contact-changing introduction with an enumerated cascade, and reach renewed through a differing closure) whose gain is split into distinct components and measured by a source-discriminating instrument with a control.** It is not a list of disturbances, not a name of a management regime, and not a ranking of interventions. The names that appear in the resilience literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
