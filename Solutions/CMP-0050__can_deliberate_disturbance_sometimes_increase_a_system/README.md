## The question restated as an effect

The loop defines R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`). Here:

- **b** is the system's belief about its habitat, formed from its own history and inherited priors.
- **a** is the deliberate disturbance, an action applied by a seat or manager.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the system's library predicts will happen under that disturbance.
- **o′** is what the habitat actually returns: state after the perturbation, recovery time, survival, identity retained.
- **s** is the system or patch. R is indexed per slot and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

The phenomenon to explain is not whether a disturbance moves R at all. It is why the same class of intervention leaves R small, or leaves the ground reachable, for a long run in some slots and not in others.

- Option A, "disturbance is intrinsically resilience-building": predicts a monotonic benefit and a single lever.
- Option B, "a disturbance increases resilience only when it changes contact, enumerates its cascade, and meets a differing closure, and the increase is a union of distinct components": predicts a conditional, non-monotonic benefit with two different instruments (Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 9 :54-57).

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a conjunction of conditions under which introduction changes contact and moves the reach outward, plus a source-discriminating instrument with a control that separates a genuine resilience gain from a changed reading. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it. It is now bound, so the pre-registered refuter in `01` can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the resilience question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of resilience.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* and Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
