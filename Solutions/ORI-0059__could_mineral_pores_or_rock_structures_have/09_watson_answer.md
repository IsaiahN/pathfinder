## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a boundary that the chemistry did not make can do the work a boundary must do. Option A: mineral pores or rock structures were the first compartments. Option B: only a boundary the chemistry makes itself counts as a compartment. What each leads to: A sends the search to concentrated chemistry in ancient pore networks and commits to a date no figure governs; B commits to a definition of compartment the figures do not state. The residual is the coupling of replication, metabolism and boundary: products must stay near producers while the network remains in contact with a flow that can be spent. The shape an answer would look like is written down before the run: a directed filter, a spatial containment bonded through a structure to a gradient, with the pore-to-membrane handoff a habitat substitution.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the historical priority claim does not become an answer. A compartment is a directed filter, a spatial containment bonded through a structure to a spendable gradient, and the pore to membrane handoff is a habitat substitution with inherited conditions and unchosen actors. The framework does not name the carrier and does not date the handoff; "before free-floating membranes evolved" is `gap`, which is a verdict rather than silence. Mineral walls fit the catalyst analogue, and only a re-entering output closes a lineage. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, and this run is a simulated derivation rather than a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its refuter and its re-census, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a favourite mineral and a date) instead of a generator (the shape and its refuter), which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The carrier name and the priority claim can be dropped without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already drop them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A filter test with a well-mixed control, a gradient removal test, or a cycle test showing product re-entry would move the shape toward `grounded`. Only a geological or experimental sequencing of the two boundary kinds moves the `gap` on "before". A figure re-census that produces a verbatim line governing chronological priority, or a derivation of containment from the other five terms, would reverse the figure verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
