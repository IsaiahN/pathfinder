## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 states it can say the answer is out of reach *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0059 a **shape** is the narrowed form of "compartment" that can be stated before one can name the carrier. The shape returned is:

> a directed filter: a spatial containment bonded through a structure to a spendable gradient, with the pore to membrane handoff a substitution of habitats.

`SPACE(contained) + structure + gradient` is a shape. `iron sulfide pore` or `fatty acid vesicle` is a name. The question asks for a name and a date; the figures can supply only the shape and the method. That mismatch is the central honesty point of this solution.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` is stated as retention plus contact, not as "pore geometry".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The first contact in `02_outreach_email.md` is therefore an effect description.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For this question the stopping place is the filter shape plus the enumerated habitat, not a geological period.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applying this to ORI-0059: calling the compartment "a rock pore" is a binding from the geology frame onto the held atom space (contained). It is not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall. Calling it "the first compartment" additionally claims a historical date, which no figure line governs. Both the name and the "before" need an instrument: a pore experiment with a well-mixed control, or a geological reading that sequences the boundary types. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` report such experiments but do not settle the sequence.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: an instrument that cannot distinguish these cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question the projection is the observation "chemistry was concentrated in a mineral setting". Several sources could produce it: thermal diffusion in a plugged pore (Baaske et al. 2007), adsorption and templating on layered hydroxides (Erastova et al. 2017), wet-dry cycling in a hot spring pool (Damer and Deamer 2020), or a lipid boundary trapping a coacervate. The reading "concentrated chemistry" does not name the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a favourite mineral.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular carrier in the compartment slot? Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A carrier is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the carrier is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question commit the forbidden error?** Yes, if it is answered as written. "Mineral pores were the first compartments" names a carrier and a date where the framework returns a shape and records a `gap` on chronology. The correct form is: a boundary with direction is required, the carrier slot stays open, and the priority claim is unreached. This is not a refusal to engage with the empirical literature; it is a refusal to let a figure-derived shape carry a name and a date it does not license.

**A correction to my own §3.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Space (contained), gradient and network are all already among the six (Fig 13), so naming one of them as the kind of thing a compartment is made of is not inventing a seventh. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world"*) and ranking one world carrier as the figure's answer. Both claims are recorded so neither is silently lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous origin-of-life authors who agree that pores came first is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is for a refuter: what would show the shape wrong?

**The simulator warning.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This audit is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Effect terms carry across a seam.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: a message without shared pretext only travels if it is a plain description of what one would expect to see. That is the ordering rule in `02_outreach_email.md`: residual, expected observation, refuter, then links.
