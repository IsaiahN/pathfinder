# The Tether's answer: Could mineral pores or rock structures have acted as compartments before free-floating membranes evolved?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the substitution claim, `general` on the structural rule, `gap` on historical priority, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The source section states the coupling: a replicating molecule alone is not a cell, and a simple compartment alone does not reproduce information. The residual R is the coupling of replication, metabolism and boundary. In effect terms, something must keep reaction products near their producers while the network stays in contact with a flow that can be spent. Without it, products diffuse away, no slot carries a live residual (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), and no lineage settles.

The question bundles four conditions: a bounded region exists; it retains products; it stays in contact with a gradient; it precedes self-made boundaries. Fig 9 :4-5 *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* This is a union compound with its own loop, not one hard question.

## 2. The shape

1. **A compartment is a filter with a direction, not a wall.** Fig 4 :21 *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* A boundary that retains everything breaks contact with the flow the residual needs. (Re-census: this governs the rule form, not a lipid bilayer; see `01` §6 R1.)
2. **Containment is a state, and a structure must mediate to energy.** Fig 13 :10 *"near, far, between, contained"*; Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75 *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* A pore with no spendable gradient is a fragment, not a first-order compartment.
3. **The habitat must be enumerated.** Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Pore walls, thermal gradient, pH contrast, wet-dry cycles, UV, retained molecules and later lipids are read off the world, not invented from a story about what compartments must be.
4. **The handoff is a substitution of habitats.** Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67 *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* "Pores then membranes" is not replacement; it is relocation with inherited conditions and unchosen actors.
5. **The carrier and the date are not returned by the framework.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* A mineral pore is a binding from the geology frame onto the held atom space (contained); a lipid vesicle is a binding from the chemistry frame. Neither is derived. Historical priority is governed by no figure line. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*
6. **Mineral walls can be catalysts, and only a cycle closes a lineage.** Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Concentration alone is a sink, not a lineage.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that mineral pores *were* the first compartments. That is a name and a date, and the figures return a shape and a `gap`.
- Not that membranes were first either. The same refusal applies to the rival option.
- Not that the framework prefers one setting. Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* forbids selecting the setting before the residual is described.
- Not that the pore is an import that moves the wall. All its atoms are held; Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- Not a derivation of one carrier from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Filter test:** does a mineral pore retain products while permitting a directed exchange of matter and energy, measured against a well-mixed control? A wall that retains everything breaks the shape claim.
- **Gradient test:** with no maintained temperature or pH gradient, does the bounded region still accumulate products against the control? If yes, the cross-kind requirement in F2 breaks.
- **Cycle test:** does the product re-enter as input, with the closing sign, or is the pore only a concentrator? The world conflict in W2 turns on exactly this: Deamer and Damer argue pore products *"would become trapped within these pore systems upon polymerization and would not undergo combinatorial selection"*.
- **Substitution test:** after swapping pore for self-made boundary, is contact and composition preserved? If the swap changes nothing, the habitat made no difference.
- **Sequencing test:** a geological or experimental reading that orders the two boundary kinds. This is the only thing that can move the `gap` on "before". Agreement among reviews cannot.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that mineral pores were the first compartments, and it does not say membranes were. It says that any compartment must be a filter with a direction rather than a wall, that containment is a spatial state which cannot bond directly to energy but must be mediated by a spendable gradient, that the habitat around the residual has to be enumerated rather than composed from a story, and that moving a chemistry from a rock pore to a membrane is a substitution of habitats, with the goal's conditions reproduced and new actors inherited, not a replacement of one by the other. It refuses to name the carrier, because the framework returns a shape, and it reports the historical "before" claim as `gap`, a verdict rather than a silence. What it offers is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The slot stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
