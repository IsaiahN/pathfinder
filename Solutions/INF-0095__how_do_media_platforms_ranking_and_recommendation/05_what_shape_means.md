## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit in the figures README is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the extract adds that it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it or measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: "the encounter has its own ground, the trust has its own ground, and the two must not be scored by the same quantity."
- Not a shape: "ranking reduces trust by 12 percent."
- Also not a shape: "the algorithm is a gradient." That is a name bound onto a software artifact, not a figure result.

How Intelligence Actually Works adds the operational definition: the loop *"stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it. That's operational, and it's the only version that doesn't require knowing the answer in advance."* And the standing caveat: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A feed that feels complete from inside is not a feed that is complete.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the outreach packet leads with an anchor test anyone can run.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: a problem may be *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one."* This is exactly the question as asked, which joins "encounter" and "trust" in one sentence. The shape procedure starts by splitting it (Fig 9 :5).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line *"will also be the starting place,"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :48 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, "the engagement metric measures trust" is an attempted name. It is licensed by neither path: no instrument discriminates among the sources of a high engagement reading, and the binding has no source gate. It must be recorded as the searcher's contribution, not as a figure result.

## 4. Why that attempted name is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

How Intelligence Actually Works uses the same structure for provenance: a video frame extracted from a video and a photograph *"are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* The distinguishing information is not in the artifact; it is in the lineage.

An engagement reading from a recommender has the same shape. Agreement, outrage, amusement, habit and misperception can all produce a click. The click artifact does not record which. Naming "trust" from it is reading a source off a projection. What is owed is a discriminator (an independently checked human judgment, or an instrument that separates the sources), not a name.

Fig 2 :37 supplies the principle: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." The same line warns that judgment is only an instrument for other questions and "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." So we must not swap one bad proxy for an unexamined survey: the trust instrument itself has to be checked.

## 5. What shape means here, answered

**The answerable question is not "how much do algorithms change trust?" It is "what would have to be true for a claim about encounter or trust to be checkable at all?"**

The shape the figures give is:

1. Two anchors, kept separate: a slate log for encounter, independent human judgment for trust (Fig 2 :29, :37).
2. A disqualification: a metric produced by the ranking frame cannot be the ground for the ranking frame (Fig 1 :27).
3. A mechanism: selection toward predicted engagement contracts exposure toward the closure the history already defines, because "No frame escapes its own closure" (Fig 6 :23).
4. A symptom: in-feed agreement is the pool talking until independence is shown (Fig 2 :18 to :21, :40).
5. A price: cross-closure contact is an import, debited against independence, with a sign not given in advance (Fig 6 :24, Fig 11 :49).
6. A stop: no magnitude from the figures. The instrument for the magnitude is an experiment, and the one existing experiment returned a three-month null on attitudes while confirming a large effect on exposure. That is `gap` plus a recorded partial refuter, not silence and not a flipped verdict.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I first wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids using the word "ranking" or "feedback loop" in the derivation. That was an overstatement, and it is visible here rather than quietly deleted.

The README's exact phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Ranking is not a missing primitive: the comparison bond ⋛ and the ordering structure (spectrum) are already in the figures' vocabulary (Fig 12 :85 to :89; Fig 13 :20). Naming them is a **binding** of a held term onto a software artifact. That is permitted as `specified`, and it must be provenanced as a binding, exactly as the QMGR exemplar narrows "Gradient" to a binding onto a held atom.

What remains wrong in the attempted "engagement equals trust" move is not that it names a held atom. It is two things: (1) the source is named from a lossy projection without a discriminator (the metamerism point, not retracted); and (2) the binding is asserted as if the figure derived it, when it is an import with no source gate (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52). Both claims are now recorded so neither is lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A packet sent to famous names to gather endorsements is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it. The public submission venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are used because they are opt-in and ground-visible, not because they are prestigious.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Therefore `01` through `09` are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. *They're Scanning Books* adds the reason this matters: *"A model trained on text... is learning the compressed traces of many minds' outputs"*, and a reader who has not walked the ground cannot tell a generator from a fluent output. The lineage and the refuter are the only things that make this checkable.
