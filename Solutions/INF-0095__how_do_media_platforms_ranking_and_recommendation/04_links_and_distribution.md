## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons for the order:

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the anchor test and the refuters; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would count as the ground for encounter and for trust before giving them the figures.
- **Fig 2 :40**: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool. A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Seat 4 discipline:** an outside frame sees the residual and the shape claim only, not the framework's interiors, records or stake.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split and the anchor test; (2) the three refuters; (3) ask what they would count as ground and what would count as a failure; (4) record that verdict; (5) only then offer the framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (6) offer the formula as a method and ask them to write their own refuter.

## The two-stage offer, and the third stage

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user must state what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.
