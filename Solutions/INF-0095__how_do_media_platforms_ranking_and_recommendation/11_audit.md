## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :27, Fig 2 :29 to :40, Fig 6 :23 to :24, Fig 8 :48, Fig 9 :5 and :32, Fig 11 :40, Fig 12 :121.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a researcher would act on, that a specific platform's engagement-optimised ranking caused a specific drop in trust.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19). The shorthand says "exposure contracts"; the concrete claim needs a per-item distribution against a control. An aggregate CTR average silently discards it.
- **The anchor distinction** (Fig 2 :29 to :30). The shorthand says "the metric cannot anchor"; a concrete audit must name which instrument is the anchor for encounter and which for trust, separately.
- **The sign of introduced contact** (Fig 6 :24, Fig 11 :49). The shorthand says "import is priced"; a concrete intervention must register its direction in advance or the result is uninterpretable.
- **The world null** (Guess 2023). The shorthand's mechanism shape can read as "ranking alters trust," which the three-month experiment does not support. The condensation must carry the partial refutation with it or it misleads.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a metric produced by the frame cannot anchor the frame, and the two conditions must have separate grounds. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this folder; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
