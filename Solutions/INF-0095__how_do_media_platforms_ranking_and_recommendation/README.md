## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 says "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The metric the platform produces cannot anchor the platform.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
3. **Sending the interior before the verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: public opt-in submission venues avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that attach to unsolicited bulk email.
