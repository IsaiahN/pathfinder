## Residual in effect terms

The question asks for the referential status of one term. Classical general relativity, through the Penrose and Hawking singularity theorems, returns a term "singularity": a maximal spacetime containing an inextendible path of finite generalized affine length, usually also a divergent curvature invariant. The question asks whether that term names something in the world or something in the classical frame's own closure.

The either/or is not one question with two answers. It bundles at least three: what the classical frame licenses, whether the ground registers such a thing, and whether an imported quantum frame would remove the term. Those have different anchors and cannot be averaged (Fig 9 :54). One lever does not clear all three. The verdict is therefore recorded per domain, and the literal reality verdict is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). A list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The anchor cannot update on frames.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). For this question the anchor is an instrument reading, not a census of opinions about whether singularities are real.
3. **Sending the interior before asking for a check spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42), and *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52).

Legally, scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All contact points in `03` are institutional submission routes or public contact pages.
