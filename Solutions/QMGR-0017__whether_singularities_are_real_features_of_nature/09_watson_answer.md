## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **Domain 1, classical GR frame.** Actors: manifolds, geodesics, trapped surfaces, energy conditions, Raychaudhuri focusing. Currency: geodesic incompleteness, curvature divergence, theorem-derived boundary. Anchor: the singularity theorems as proofs, legitimate because Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Closure-internal domain.
- **Domain 2, ground and observation.** Actors: telescopes, gravitational-wave detectors, horizon-scale imaging, local curvature probes, infalling observers. Currency: photons, gravitational waves, local watch and accelerometer readings. Anchor would be an instrument reading that registers SIG. None exists at any resolution. Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it." Domain with an unreached anchor, not silence.
- **Domain 3, quantum or replacement frames.** Actors: loop quantizations, strings and fuzzballs, regular black hole models, semiclassical gravity. Currency: resolved curvature, finite Dirac observables, bounce or remnant. No settled ground anchor: no quantum gravitational effect near a black hole has been experimentally observed, and Wall's results give reasons the removal hope faces serious problems. Candidate import habitat, not a domain yet.
- **Domain 4, description and invariance.** Actors: coordinate charts, diffeomorphisms, curvature scalars, boundary constructions. Currency: invariant versus coordinate-dependent infinity, completeness versus inextendibility. Anchor: diffeomorphism invariance, legitimate because the sub-question is constitutively about which divergences survive chart changes. Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- **Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The either/or silently treats four domains as one variable.
- **Order by dependency.** D4 first, D1 second, D2 third, D3 last. The chain breaks at the objective link because there is no instrument for existence. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Strain test applied at census: D3 was first given an experimental anchor because quantum gravity is a physical theory and physics has experiments. The line resisted, since no quantum gravitational effect near a black hole has been observed. D3 was recensused as a candidate import habitat with no anchor. A line that fits only by pretending an experiment exists is a strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; both binary names are refused. The question is a union of at least three conditions with different anchors. Classical GR licenses SIG inside its closure, which settles what the theory says and not what the world contains. No instrument at any resolution registers SIG, and even the horizon that hides it is locally unverifiable, so the literal reality verdict is `gap` of the unreached kind, reported rather than hidden. Artifact splits into a chart-relative structure removable by re-description and a theory-limit structure that requires an import. The adjective irreducible is level-relative and is refused as a claim about nature. A replacement, if one arrives, will be an import with a described effect, a source gate and a shadow test, not a re-description and not more search inside classical GR. Per-domain verdicts are D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `gap` unreached single-source, D3 `gap` no anchor, D4 `specified` triangulated. This is `specified`, `general` and `gap` as declared above, never `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is publishing a yes or no about nature that no instrument supports, and spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion before a check. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused binary names are replaced by a declared gap and per-domain verdicts without withdrawing any primitive or deleting any record. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. The wrong adjective can be dropped while the structure it governed stays.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. An instrument reading at any resolution registering a divergent curvature invariant or unextendible local path closes D2 directly. A local measurement that registers the horizon reopens INWARD. A candidate replacement whose closure predicts the described residual shape, with provenance and shadow test recorded, settled by an instrument, gives D3 an anchor. A derivation showing the classical term obtainable in another frame's closure without an import defeats the closure bound. A single anchor and instrument shown to govern all three conditions defeats the union split. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
