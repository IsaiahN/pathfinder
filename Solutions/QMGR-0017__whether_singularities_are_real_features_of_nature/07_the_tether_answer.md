## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the referential status of one term. Classical GR, through the Penrose and Hawking singularity theorems, returns a maximal spacetime with an inextendible path of finite generalized affine length, usually accompanied by a divergent curvature invariant. The question asks whether that term names something the ground returns or something produced inside the classical frame's closure.

The either/or is not one question with two answers. It bundles at least three conditions with different anchors: what classical GR licenses, whether the ground registers such a thing, and whether an imported frame would remove the term. ## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that singularities are unreal. That is a verdict the ground has not returned.
- Not that singularities are real. The theorems are a closure result, not a measurement.
- Not that the curvature singularity is a coordinate artifact. The invariant divergence survives every chart.
- Not that any particular quantum gravity programme is right. Naming loop quantization, fuzzballs or regular black holes is a binding from the physics literature, provenanced as such.
- Not that the horizon is the ground anchor. It is globally defined and locally unverifiable, so the D2 anchor would have to be a curvature-reading instrument, which does not exist.
- Not grounded. No observation or instrument was consulted.
- Not a claim that agreement among physicists settles anything. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :35: "A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The Hard Problem is About Source gives the form: an instrument that reads a projection cannot comment on the source, and what resolved the colour case was a spectrometer, not an argument. For this question the required discriminations are:

1. Any instrument reading, at any resolution, that registers a divergent curvature invariant or an unextendible local path. That would close D2 directly.
2. A local measurement that registers the horizon. The SEP entry says this is impossible in principle with current global definitions, so this would require a revision of the definition, not a better detector alone.
3. A candidate replacement whose closure predicts the described residual shape, with a recorded source gate, shadow test and provenance, and whose prediction has been settled by an instrument. That would give D3 a nameable anchor and close the gap.
4. A derivation showing that the classical term is obtainable in another frame's closure without an import. That would defeat the closure bound used in section 2, item 1.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The literature records a live disagreement that does not shrink with effort: the SEP entry lists physicists who resist the reality of singularities, physicists who read them as the limit of GR's domain of applicability, and physicists who take the theory at its word. Wikipedia records that some believe singularities do not exist while others hold that some likely still exist despite quantum gravity. Per Fig 9 :4-5, a disagreement like this is split rather than searched. The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Nothing in the figures rules out a future instrument that registers Planck-scale curvature, so the report is `unreached` at this budget, not a claim about what nature forbids.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer whether singularities are real, and it does not pretend the question is one binary variable. It says the classical singularity theorems are a proof inside general relativity's closure, so they settle what the theory licenses and not what the world contains. It says no instrument, at any resolution, returns a reading of a singularity, and even the horizon that hides one is globally defined and locally unverifiable, so the reality verdict is a reported gap rather than a yes or a no. It separates a coordinate artifact, which a chart change removes, from an invariant curvature divergence, which survives every chart, and it refuses the phrase irreducible feature of nature because irreducibility is a fact about a level. If a replacement ever arrives it will be an import with a described effect, introduced with provenance, not a re-description and not more search inside classical general relativity. What the framework offers is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The centre stays open until the ground settles it.
