## 0. The residual in effect terms

> **Question as posed:** are singularities real features of nature, or artifacts of classical theory?

Stated as an effect, not a label:

- The classical GR frame, given its premises, returns a term: a maximal spacetime containing an inextendible path of finite generalized affine length, often accompanied by a divergent curvature invariant. Call this term **SIG**.
- The question asks for the standing of SIG: does it name something the ground returns (a real feature), or is it produced inside the classical frame's closure and therefore removable by a better frame (an artifact)?

**Arity.** Three conditions are bundled, not two: (C1) what the classical closure licenses; (C2) what the ground registers; (C3) what an imported frame would do to SIG. The question's grammar offers one binary variable ("real" versus "artifact"), but the residual has three slots, so this is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54). A single lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Symmetry.** "Real" and "artifact" are not opposite values of one quantity. "Real" is a claim about the ground. "Artifact" is a claim about a frame's closure. Those have different anchors, so they cannot be averaged or traded off (Fig 9 :4-5: a disagreement that does not shrink with effort, where each rule works well on a different subset, is not one hard question but several well-formed ones; split it rather than search).

**Scale.** C1 is intra-closure at the classical level. C2 crosses from theory to instrument at horizon and Planck scales. C3 is a level crossing, from the classical frame to a quantum frame whose closure differs.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula "Direction").** An answer would be one of: (a) a ground reading that registers SIG at some resolution; (b) a provenanced import whose closure predicts a finite replacement for SIG and has passed a shadow test against a described residual; or (c) a demonstrated limit at which the question cannot be settled by any available instrument, recorded as `unreached` rather than silence. I commit to this list **before** the derivation, so the shape I am looking for can be refused by the same test as everything else (Fig 5 :65).

## 3. Findings

### F1  -  The either/or is a union compound, not a partition (decisive for framing)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :56-57:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

The world literature confirms the non-shrinkage rather than resolving it. Curiel's Stanford Encyclopedia entry reports three live attitudes: some resist the reality of singularities, some read them as the limit of GR's domain of applicability, and some *"are inclined to take general relativity at its word, however, and simply accept its prediction of singularities as a surprising but perfectly consistent account."* Wikipedia's entry likewise records that *"Some physicists believe that singularities do not exist"* while others hold that some likely still exist despite quantum gravity. When a debate does not shrink with effort and each side works well on a different subset (theorem versus instrument versus replacement), Fig 9 says split, not search.

**Verdict:** union. Verdicts kept separate per domain, never averaged. **Mode: `general`** for the split. **Refuter:** show that all four conditions share one anchor and one instrument, so that one lever does clear every intersection. Until then, any single "yes" or "no" answers a different question from the one asked.

### F2  -  Classical GR licenses a theorem inside its closure, not a verdict about the world

Fig 2 :36:

> **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**

Fig 6 :21:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 2 :29:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

The singularity theorems are proofs: given energy conditions, a trapped surface, and suitable global structure, an inextendible finite path follows. That is a legitimate anchor **for C1**, because C1 is constitutively about what classical GR says. Wikipedia's Penrose-Hawking entry: *"The singularity theorems prove that this cannot happen, and that a singularity will always form once an event horizon forms."* But the theorem is an element of closure(Γ_classical). By Fig 6 :21 it cannot add an atom to the world. Using it to answer C2 would move a closure result across the boundary without an import, which Fig 6 :23 forbids.

This is the exact point where the question's phrasing misleads. "Artifact of classical theory" is not a diminished kind of reality; it is a correct description of the term's provenance. The theorem settles the shape of what the classical frame must return. It does not settle reference.

**Verdict for D1: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 2 :36 (anchor legitimacy for frame-internal questions), Fig 6 :21 (closure bound) and the theorem record itself (low overlap: a usage rule, a closure law, and a physics proof). **Refuter:** produce a ground reading that settles C1 without the theorem, or show the theorem does not follow from its premises. Neither is available here; the proof stands as a closure result and no more.

### F3  -  No instrument returns a reading of SIG at any resolution; the inward path is closed

Fig 6 :34-35:

> **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

Fig 6 :40:

> **"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."**

Fig 3 :27:

> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

For D2, the ground anchor is an instrument that registers SIG. There is none. A black hole singularity is hidden behind a horizon and has never registered in any observation; Wikipedia states plainly: *"it is unknown whether these singularities truly exist in real black holes."* Curiel's entry is stronger on the epistemic point: *"in the absence of any conceivable observational data about the interior of black holes, the only means of approaching these issues are mathematical methods."* It also notes that locating the horizon locally is *"impossible in principle, no matter how ingeniously the instruments are arranged"* and that the horizon's presence is *"a strictly unverifiable hypothesis."* So even the boundary that hides SIG is not locally measurable. There is no proto-instrument returning a failing resolution of the interior that could be sharpened.

Two qualifications, both required for honesty:

1. **This is not a ban on theory.** Fig 6 :37-38: *"theory routinely precedes its instrument, and is welcome to. Neptune was calculated before it was seen, and the prediction is what told anyone where to point."* Predicting SIG from classical GR is legitimate. The limit is on the instrument path, not on prediction.
2. **This is not underivable in principle.** Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* The correct report is `unreached` with the current instruments, not "nature forbids an answer". A new instrument that registers Planck-scale curvature would move this domain, and nothing in the figures rules one out.

**Verdict for D2: `gap` of the unreached kind, single-source on the figure side** (Fig 6 governs the instrument condition; D2's physics is corroborated by two world sources that partly share a review pool, so the figure verdict is not upgraded). **Refuter:** exhibit any existing instrument reading, at any resolution, whose residual fails to resolve because of SIG, or show a local measurement that registers the horizon. Either would reopen INWARD. Until one appears, the chain's objective link has no instrument.

### F4  -  "Irreducible feature of nature" strains; irreducibility is level-relative

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

Fig 4 :33:

> **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

A common realist reading says a singularity is an irreducible, fundamental feature of the world. Fig 12 :104 governs irreducibility, and it says irreducibility is a fact about a level, never about the world, with no bottom. So if SIG is irreducible inside classical GR, that is a statement about the classical level. Promoting it to "irreducible in nature" is a level error, not a discovery.

**Strain test and re-census.** I initially tried to use Fig 12 :104 to conclude the claim that singularities are not real. That is a strain. Fig 12 :104 governs irreducibility, not existence; a thing can be real and composite, or real and only provisionally irreducible. Using it as an existence verdict would make Fig 12 contradict Fig 2 (the ground decides reality), and the canonical rule says a contradiction means a misidentified domain (FIGURES_README_canonical.md). Re-census: Fig 12 :104 applies to D1 and D3 (level structure), Fig 2 :29 applies to D2 (reality). Different quantities, no contradiction. The finding is narrowed to: **the realist's "irreducible" adjective is refused; the realist's "real" adjective is not decided by this line.**

**Verdict for the adjective: `specified`, single-source on the figure side** (Fig 12 :104 alone governs irreducibility; the no-bottom corroboration in Fig 4 :33 is a different figure about levels, so the pair is weak triangulation). **Refuter:** derive a level at which composition stops and irreducibility becomes absolute. The figures forbid it definitionally, so this refuter is a test of the framework, not of physics, and is recorded as such.

### F5  -  Chart artifact and theory-limit artifact are different domains

Fig 12 :5:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 8 :32:

> **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

The word "artifact" covers two different things, and D4 exists to keep them apart.

- **Chart artifact.** The apparent divergence at the Schwarzschild radius disappears in Kruskal coordinates; Wikipedia's coordinate-singularity entry calls such a discontinuity *"only apparent; it is an artifact of the coordinate system chosen."* Re-description inside the same closure removes it. No import is owed.
- **Curvature or theory-limit artifact.** At the centre of a non-rotating black hole the Kretschmann scalar diverges, and that is diffeomorphism invariant; changing charts does not remove it (Wikipedia "Gravitational singularity"). Removing it requires a new closure, because Fig 8 :32 says searching inside GR never adds an atom.

This distinction is why the question cannot be answered by a glib "yes, it is just a coordinate artifact". The invariant curvature divergence survives every chart. The "artifact" claim, if it is to mean anything, must be the stronger claim that GR's representational capacities end there. Curiel's entry states that view exactly: it *"denies that singularities are real features of the actual world, and rather asserts that they are merely artifacts of our current, inevitably limited, physical theories, marking the regime where the representational capacities of the theory at issue breaks down."* That is a claim about a level, and it is the honest form of the artifact side.

**Verdict for D4: `triangulated`** across Fig 12 :5 (bond carries what ingredients do not), Fig 8 :32 (closure bound) and the invariant-versus-chart distinction in the world sources, which come from mathematics and philosophy of physics with low overlap. **Refuter:** exhibit a chart change that removes a divergent curvature scalar, or show that a theory-limit artifact is removable by re-description alone. Neither is available; if one appeared, D4 and D1 would collapse into one domain and the census would have to be redrawn.

### F6  -  The import is owed, but no source gate or shadow test is satisfied

Fig 6 :14:

> **"the only operator that moves the wall"**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 6 :24:

> **"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame’s independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."**

Fig 8 :51:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

After SEARCHED (SIG is found in the classical closure, so this path closes immediately) and INWARD (F3, closed), Formula step 7 sends us OUTWARD: characterise the residual in effect terms, then find a frame whose closure predicts its shape. The effect-term description is: a replacement for SIG must (i) keep the classical predictions away from the extreme regime, (ii) return finite curvature where GR returns divergence, and (iii) keep geodesic extendibility or explain its failure without a boundary.

Candidate frames exist. Gambini, Olmedo and Pullin report that in loop quantum gravity *"one could complete the quantization the model and that the singularity inside black holes is resolved."* Borde, Guth and Vilenkin show that for cosmology *"inflationary models require physics other than inflation to describe the past boundary of the inflating region of spacetime."* The journal *Classical and Quantum Gravity* currently lists a "Focus on Regular Black Holes" collection, so the nonsingular programme is a live research area, not a dead speculation.

But none of these is a settled import. First, no source gate and shadow test have been passed for this residual: the candidate frames were named in the literature, not selected by a described residual, and Fig 9 :27-28 requires the opposite order (*"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*). Second, the candidates are mutually incompatible, so choosing one is a choice with a cost above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24). Third, Curiel's entry records a live counterweight: *"Recent important work by Wall (2013a, 2013b) shows that these hopes face serious problems."* A conflict in world sources sends the derivation back to the census (it does not flip a verdict); here it returns D3 to `candidate import habitat` with no anchor.

**Verdict for D3: `gap`, no anchor, single-source per candidate frame, none triangulated at the ground.** **Refuter:** show a candidate frame whose closure predicts the described residual shape, whose provenance and shadow test are recorded, and whose prediction has been settled by an instrument. Then D3 becomes a domain with a nameable anchor and the gap closes.

The literature contains genuine disagreement, and the sources partly overlap. They are one broad evidence pool with different emphases, not three independent witnesses. Even if they all agreed that singularities are artifacts, that agreement would be worthless as a verdict about the world, because it is agreement about what a frame returns rather than a reading from an anchor. Fig 7 :35 says selection between rooms is where the ground votes, and no room here has an instrument that registers SIG.

**Verdict: `general` structural, triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 7 (two figures with different topologies, ground and room chain). **Refuter:** an independent instrument reading that all rooms would have to range against. That is the same refuter as F3, which is the point: every road back to reality goes through the ground.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing discipline, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim in this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Ground firing.** Any instrument reading, at any resolution, registers SIG as a divergent curvature invariant or an unextendible local path. This settles the reality verdict directly and closes F3.
2. **Closure collapse.** A derivation shows that classical GR's SIG term is obtainable in a quantum frame's closure without an import, so Fig 6 :21 and :23 do not bind the way F2 and F6 claim.
3. **Chart collapse.** A coordinate change removes a divergent curvature scalar, collapsing D4 into D1 and defeating F5.
4. **Level refutation.** A level is exhibited at which composition stops and irreducibility becomes absolute, defeating Fig 12 :104 as used in F4.
5. **Union refutation.** A single anchor and a single instrument are shown to govern C1, C2 and C3, so the union split in F1 was wrong.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded here rather than erased. Until one is run, no part of this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1 (union split) and F3 (no instrument), not with an ontology verdict. The question's grammar invites a yes/no answer; the figures refuse to supply one.
2. Report D2 as `unreached`, never as "the answer is no" and never as silence (Fig 13 :134). Distinguish underivable in principle from unreached at this budget (Fig 8 :40-41).
3. Keep F4 narrow. Refuse the adjective "irreducible" applied to nature; do not pretend Fig 12 :104 decides existence.
4. Record any import with a source gate, a shadow test and a provenance stamp (Fig 8 :51). Naming LQG or fuzzballs is a binding from the physics frame, not a derivation.
5. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the derivation and the refuter, not a conclusion about what nature contains.
