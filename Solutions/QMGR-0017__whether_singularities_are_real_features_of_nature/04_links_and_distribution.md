## 2. What the wider literature contributes

Grouped by the domain each bears on. These are secondary corroboration only. The figures decide the verdict.

- **D1, classical closure.** The Penrose-Hawking page states: "The singularity theorems prove that this cannot happen, and that a singularity will always form once an event horizon forms." That is a proof inside the classical closure. It settles what GR licenses, not what the world contains. The SEP entry gives the semi-official definition and notes there is no commonly accepted strict definition of singularity, which is a reason to keep the term indexed per domain rather than average it (Fig 1 :19).
- **D2, ground and observation.** Wikipedia states: "it is unknown whether these singularities truly exist in real black holes." The SEP entry is stronger on the epistemic point: "in the absence of any conceivable observational data about the interior of black holes, the only means of approaching these issues are mathematical methods and conceptual analysis." It also says locating the horizon locally is "impossible in principle, no matter how ingeniously the instruments are arranged" and the horizon's presence is "a strictly unverifiable hypothesis." No proto-instrument is returning a failing resolution of the interior, so INWARD is closed (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **D3, replacement frames.** The Gambini abstract says "one could complete the quantization the model and that the singularity inside black holes is resolved." The Penrose-Hawking page reports Borde, Guth and Vilenkin: inflationary cosmologies "are still past-incomplete, and thus require physics other than inflation to describe the past boundary of the inflating region of spacetime." The CQG journal lists a Focus on Regular Black Holes collection. These make the import candidate live. They do not gate it: the SEP entry records the counterweight that "Recent important work by Wall (2013a, 2013b) shows that these hopes face serious problems." A conflict sends the derivation back to the census, and here it returns D3 to candidate habitat with no anchor.
- **D4, description and invariance.** Wikipedia's curvature section says "a change of coordinate systems will not cause these infinities to go away" for diffeomorphism invariant scalars, while the event horizon divergence is removable in Kruskal coordinates. That is the chart artifact versus theory-limit artifact split the figures require (Fig 12 :5).

**Overlap warning.** The SEP entry and the two Wikipedia pages draw on the same standard GR review pool (Curiel, Hawking and Ellis, Wald, Joshi). They are one broad evidence pool with different emphases, not independent witnesses. The three Substack essays share an author with the figures. Fig 2 :40 warns that "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Their agreement is recorded as corroboration of shape, never as a verdict about nature.
