## RESIDUAL

The question offers one binary variable, real or artifact. The term "artifact" covers at least two different structures, and the term "irreducible" is used as if it were absolute. Three questions are owed before any verdict: (1) is "artifact" one kind or two, (2) is "irreducible feature of nature" a well-formed claim, (3) if a replacement frame arrives, does it subtract the singularity or introduce something else. The falsifier for the whole supporting derivation is written at the end, before the conclusion.

## DERIVATION

### Q1: Is "artifact" one kind or two?

- Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." The same ingredient name can carry different structures, and the structure is what decides the behaviour.
- **Chart artifact.** The divergence of the Schwarzschild metric at the radius of the horizon disappears in Kruskal coordinates. Wikipedia states that the regularity "becomes evident when changing to another coordinate system (such as the Kruskal coordinates), where the metric is perfectly smooth." This is removal by re-description inside the same closure. No import is owed.
- **Theory-limit artifact.** At the centre of a non-rotating black hole the Kretschmann scalar diverges and is diffeomorphism invariant. Wikipedia states that "a change of coordinate systems will not cause these infinities to go away" for such scalars. Fig 8 :32 applies: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Removal requires a frame whose closure differs.
- The honest artifact claim is therefore the stronger one, and the SEP entry states it: general relativity's singularities "are merely artifacts of our current, inevitably limited, physical theories, marking the regime where the representational capacities of the theory at issue breaks down." That is a claim about a level, not about a chart, and not about the world directly.
- ⇒ Two domains, D4 and D1, not one. Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 :5 and Fig 8 :32 with the invariant-versus-chart distinction from the world sources (mathematics and philosophy of physics, low overlap with the figures).

### Q2: Is "irreducible feature of nature" well formed?

- Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"
- Fig 4 :33: "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."
- If SIG is irreducible inside classical GR, that is a statement about the classical level. Promoting it to "irreducible in nature" is a level error. Fig 4 :33 supplies the other end, so there is no privileged stratum in either direction.
- **Strain test and re-census.** I first tried to use Fig 12 :104 to conclude that singularities are not real. That is a strain: the line governs irreducibility, not existence. A thing can be real and composite, or real and only provisionally irreducible. Making it an existence verdict would make Fig 12 contradict Fig 2, and the canonical README says a reading that makes figures contradict each other has misidentified its domain. Re-census: Fig 12 :104 applies to level structure (D1, D3), Fig 2 :29 applies to reality (D2). Different quantities, no contradiction.
- ⇒ The adjective "irreducible" is refused; the adjective "real" is not decided by this line. Verdict `specified`, single-source on the figure side with weak corroboration from Fig 4 :33.

### Q3: Does a replacement subtract the singularity or introduce something?

- Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"
- Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
- Fig 11 :32: "introduce, never subtract"
- Fig 6 :42: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."
- A replacement closure is introduced. SIG is not deleted from the record; what falls is its standing as something the new closure must compose over. This is why the artifact verdict is a negation of SIG's standing, not its removal from the library. The Operators table defines negation as the refusal of a claim, distinct from subtraction, which is the absence of an operand. Both are needed to say precisely what happens: the new frame adds an atom (import), and the classical term loses reach (refusal of its standing).
- **Analogue, used for structure only.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Classical GR and a regular-black-hole model can share most observational atoms away from the extreme regime and differ in the arrangement at the core. They are isomers in the figure's sense only if the atom sets can be made to match; as currently formulated they share the classical limit but not a full atom set, so the weaker functional-group reading is the honest one. Fig 12 :124: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." This analogue is a structural claim about two closures, not a physics result.
- Verdict `specified` for the introduction/refusal structure (triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 11), `gap` for whether any particular replacement is the one nature uses.

### Q4: What is the dependency order?

Fig 3 :18: "The break is drawn between three and four as an illustration. Where a given chain stops is a reading, never a property of the chain." Applied here:

1. D4 first, because chart artifacts must be separated from invariant ones before asking about reference.
2. D1 second, because the classical term must be characterised before asking whether it refers.
3. D2 third, because the anchor question presupposes a characterised residual (Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
4. D3 last, because import is only owed after SEARCHED and INWARD have been run.

The chain breaks at the objective link: there is no instrument for existence. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Effort spent on links above the break cannot repair it. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## CONCLUSION

"Artifact" is two structures (chart-relative and theory-limit), not one. "Irreducible in nature" is not well formed, because irreducibility is level-relative. A replacement introduces a new closure and refuses the classical term's standing; it does not subtract an atom from the world. The dependency order puts description before reference and reference before import, and the chain stops at the missing instrument. These are structural findings. None of them returns a yes or no to the original question.
