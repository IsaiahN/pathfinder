# The Tether's answer: can ecological tipping points be predicted reliably in time?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement in this folder settles the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

A monitoring system reads an ecosystem and must decide whether a crossing is approaching and whether action is still possible. What failed to resolve: an aggregate reading can be flat while a single patch carries the live signal (Fig 1 :13), and the actor governing the crossing may be three contact steps away from what is watched (Fig 11 :52-53). The residual is the gap between what the indicator returns and the state that governs reversal. The sought-for answer would be a prospective, pre-registered statement of false-alarm and miss rates for a named system against the ground. Until that exists, any yes or no is a name where only a shape is available.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**D1, the habitat. Verdict: reliability cannot be certified from inside the monitoring frame.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." The list is read off the world, not composed, and the residual that mattered in the DDT case "was three steps out" (Fig 11 :52-53). A frame cannot certify from inside that it holds every actor capable of moving the crossing. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 11 plus Fig 13 :31).

**D2, the instrument. Verdict: reachable in bounded cases, unreached where nothing registers.** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Where a statistic is already returning a signal that fails to resolve, the inward route is open. Where nothing at any resolution registers, there is no edge to extend, and the report is unreached, not safe. Fig 8 :40-43 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Status: `triangulated` (Fig 6 plus Fig 8 plus Fig 9 :44).

**D3, the reading. Verdict: a pooled all-clear is not a verdict.** Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The signal that matters is per patch. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 1 plus Fig 10 :25 "pooled readings").

**D4, the decision and reversal. Verdict: "in time" is a separate, usually unmeasured link.** Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Reversal difficulty is a property of the driver-response loop, not of the predictor: Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." If the driver can only be changed slowly, action is needed well before the indicator rises. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 3 plus Fig 12 plus Fig 2 :42).

**Cross-cutting: absence of warning is not safety.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." And the statistic-to-state map has a gap that must be reported: Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

**No verdict is averaged across domains.** The four verdicts are different in kind and stay separate.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a yes. The figures do not license "tipping points can be predicted reliably" as a blanket claim.
- Not a no. They do not license "tipping points cannot be predicted" either. Bounded reachability is a different fact from unreachability in principle.
- Not that any named statistic is reliable. Variance, lag-1 autocorrelation and skewness are bindings from the ecology frame, provenanced as such, not names the framework derives.
- Not that a flat reading means safety. A flat reading can be a saturated metric or a pooled average (Fig 10 :25).
- Not that reversibility is a property of the ecosystem alone. It is a property of the loop between driver and response plus the rate at which the driver can be changed.
- Not `grounded`. No false-alarm or miss rate is asserted for any real ecosystem.
- Not a final answer. Fig 13 :90 "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."

## 4. What would settle it, and the ground

A ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a prospective, pre-registered test on systems **not** selected conditional on having tipped (Boettiger and Hastings 2012), reporting false-alarm and miss rates, with per-slot residuals, a measured round trip gap R_T between the statistic and the observed state, a channel-health test distinguishing quiet from dead, and the driver-manipulation rate recorded. Four discriminating checks follow from the essays:

1. **Source discrimination.** From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." A flat variance reading has several possible sources. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates them, not a name assigned to the reading.
2. **False-positive control.** Select systems without conditioning on the crossing, then measure. Otherwise "an elevated rate of false-positives" is built into the sample.
3. **Lead time against reversal rate.** Record both the indicator's lead time and the time required to change the driver. A lead time without a reversal rate does not answer "in time."
4. **The five refuters.** Run the checks in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 before claiming the shape survives.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The available instruments have not settled the claim, and the framework's own rule is to report that rather than produce an answer anyway. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides". The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: what was unbundled, what was imported from the ecology frame, what refuters were written in advance, and what would reverse the verdict. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether ecological tipping points can be predicted reliably. It says the question is four questions bundled together: whether the actor that governs a crossing is in contact with the monitor, whether any instrument is already returning a resolvable signal, whether the reading is per patch or pooled into a regional average, and whether the driver can be changed fast enough for a warning to be usable. Reliability is not a property of an indicator. It is a property of the monitor plus its habitat, earned per system against the ground. A pooled average can be flat while one patch is about to cross; no warning found is not the same as no warning owed; and a warning that arrives after the driver can no longer be changed is not a warning in time. What the framework offers is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The answer stops there until a prospective ground reading settles it.
