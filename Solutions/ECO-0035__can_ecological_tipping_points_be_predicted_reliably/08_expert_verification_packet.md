# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ecological tipping-point prediction

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist or earth-systems scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the parts, not `grounded`.** No false-alarm or miss rate for a real ecosystem is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Can ecological tipping points be predicted reliably in time to act?" is not one question. It bundles four overlapping conditions with their own loops: the habitat census, the instrument, the reading, and the decision and reversal link. Reliability is not a property an indicator carries; it is a property of a monitoring system together with the habitat it ranges against, earned per system against the ground with a measured round trip gap and per-slot residuals. A pooled regional average can be flat while one patch carries the live signal. No warning found is not proof of safety. A warning that arrives after the driver can no longer be changed is not a warning in time. The framework returns that shape, not a numeric verdict.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal and ecology-facing; checkable by reading Figure 1 and Figure 11

- **Statement:** a pooled reading cannot be treated as a verdict when one slot carries the live residual, and the actor governing a crossing may be several contact steps outside what is monitored.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a real ecosystem where a pooled statistic carries the same decision-relevant information as per-slot residuals, and where every actor capable of moving the crossing is in contact with the instrument, both verified against the ground.

### C2 : ecology-facing; checkable against the early-warning literature

- **Statement:** absence of a warning does not establish absence of an approaching crossing, and apparent warning reliability is inflated when systems are selected because they tipped.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
- **What to check:** Boettiger and Hastings 2012 report "an elevated rate of false-positives in common warning signal statistics" under conditional selection; Dakos et al. 2012 state that "opinions on their practical use differ widely, and there is no comparative study that tests the limitations". Do these support the shape rather than a blanket no?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a closed, enumerated frame for an ecosystem, verified against the ground, so that finding nothing licenses absence. Or show that conditional selection does not inflate the hit rate for the statistic in question.

### C3 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 4 and Figure 12

- **Statement:** an early-warning statistic is a lossy map of system state with a measurable round trip gap R_T, and the "in time" condition depends on the rate at which the driver can be changed, not only on the indicator's lead time.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** Biggs et al. 2009 find "Large increases in the indicators only occur once a regime shift is initiated, often too late for management to avert a shift" when drivers change only gradually. Layritz et al. 2025 show that under non-Gaussian noise the classical indicators produce "spurious, false-positive results". Both bear on the gap and on the assumption set the indicator does not measure.
- **Refuter:** show a statistic whose abstraction and concretisation are identity on the relevant states so R_T is zero, or show that time available for management is independent of driver manipulation rate.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a yes or a no to the bundled question. Per-domain verdicts only, never averaged.
- Not that any named statistic (variance, autocorrelation, skewness) is reliable or unreliable across the board. Those are bindings from the ecology frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that a flat reading means safety. A flat reading can be a saturated metric or a pooled average (Fig 10 :25).
- Not that early-warning signals never work. Bounded reachability is a different fact from unreachability in principle.
- Not `grounded`. No numeric false-alarm or miss rate is asserted for any ecosystem.
- Not a final answer. Fig 13 :90 "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective ground reading, not agreement. A pre-registered test on systems not selected conditional on having tipped, reporting false-alarm and miss rates, with per-slot residuals, a measured R_T, a channel-health test, and the driver-manipulation rate recorded. The five refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :13, :22 and Fig 11 :52-53 (three short passages). Is the pooled-versus-per-slot distinction real in your system?
2. Read Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44. Do you know of a sealed frame for which finding nothing licenses absence? If not, absence is silence.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a safe flat reading from a saturated or pooled flat reading, or say why none exists.
4. Name the rate at which the driver in your system can actually be changed. Compare it with the lead time of your best indicator.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
