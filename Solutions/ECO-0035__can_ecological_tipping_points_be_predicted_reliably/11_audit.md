## Independent domain census (written before reading the primary's conclusions)

I redid the census from the residual line in the question and from the figure extract alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between a monitoring reading and the state that governs crossing and reversal. Arity: two conditions in the question itself, a signal appearing and a signal discriminating in time to act, so a **union compound** rather than one hard question. Symmetry: not symmetric; the system's return path differs from its outbound path, which is a cycle rather than a chain. Scale: one patch to a regional aggregate, one crossing to a cascade of coupled crossings.
- **D1, habitat.** Actors: species, stressors, conditions, relations. Currency: contact cascading outward. Anchor: the measured physical trajectory. Question type: enumeration, never composition.
- **D2, instrument.** Actors: monitoring statistics, time series, models. Currency: detection, false alarms, misses. Anchor: the crossing, retrospective. Question type: reachability of a sensor.
- **D3, reading.** Actors: pooled versus per-slot readings. Currency: residual per slot versus averaged residual. Anchor: the local transition at the slot that crossed. Question type: disaggregation.
- **D4, decision and reversal.** Actors: managers, policy rate, driver manipulation rate. Currency: actions under uncertainty. Anchor: reversibility. Question type: dependency link with no instrument in most cases.
- **Order by dependency:** enumerate habitat, aim instrument, read per slot, decide in time. A link with no instrument is not a link that works.
- **Union, not partition:** unbundle before searching; keep verdicts separate.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. I derived the union from the two conditions stated in the question and the cycle from the asymmetry of reversal, whereas the primary derived them from Fig 9 and Fig 12. Different starting points, same domain boundaries. Because we share the figure text and the world sources, this agreement is weak but not circular; it is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-53 (habitat enumeration and contact cascade), Fig 1 :13 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 9 :44 (no sealed room), Fig 12 :121 (cycle).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: reliability is a property of monitor plus habitat; pooled readings hide live slots; absence of warning is not safety; the gap must be measured; "in time" depends on driver manipulation rate.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete form an ecologist would want: a numeric false-alarm rate and miss rate for a named ecosystem, with a named indicator and a named lead time.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the governing actor** in a real habitat. The shorthand says "the habitat must be enumerated"; the concrete claim requires naming the actor, and the framework returns a shape, not a name;
- the **numeric reliability values**. The shorthand says "measure the gap"; the concrete claim requires a number, and no figure line supplies one;
- the **retrospective anchor**. The shorthand says "the ground settles it"; in practice the ground is the crossing, observable only after the event, so prospective settlement is not available from the figure set.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the four domains are distinct, a pooled reading is not a verdict, and no warning found is not safety. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
