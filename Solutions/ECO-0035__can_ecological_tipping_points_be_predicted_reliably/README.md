## Restating the question as an effect

- Option A: a tipping point can be predicted reliably in time to act, so an early-warning indicator may be treated as an anchor for management.
- Option B: reliability is not a property an indicator carries. It is a property of a monitoring system together with the habitat it ranges against, earned per system against the ground, with a measured round-trip gap and per-slot residuals. Absence of a warning is never an all-clear.

What an answer would look like if bound as an ordinary term (formula step, Direction): a statement of the form "for this system, given this habitat census and this instrument, the false-alarm and miss rates are bounded at these values, measured prospectively against the ground." That is the sought-for shape written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the agreement that would be worthless as a verdict.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Two reviewers reading the same review are one witness.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
