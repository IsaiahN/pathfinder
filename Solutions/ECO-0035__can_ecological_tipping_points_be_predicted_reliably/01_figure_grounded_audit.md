## 0. The claim under audit, stated as a residual in effect terms

**Option A:** ecological tipping points can be predicted reliably in advance of the crossing, early enough that management can still act. Leads to: treating a rising variance or lag-1 autocorrelation reading as an anchor for intervention; treating the absence of such a reading as an all-clear.

**Option B:** "reliable" is not a property carried by an indicator. It is a property of a monitoring system plus the habitat it ranges against, and it is earned per system against the ground with a measured round-trip gap and per-slot residuals. Leads to: prospective, pre-registered tests that report false-alarm and miss rates; keeping the habitat under enumeration; treating absence of warning as silence, not safety.

**R in effect terms.** What failed to resolve, before any theory was chosen: an ecosystem can look stable in aggregate while a single patch is about to cross (Fig 1 :13), and the actor that governs the crossing may be several contact steps away from what is monitored (Fig 11 :52-53). The residual is the gap between the reading the indicator returns and the state of the system that actually governs reversal.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term (formula step, Direction).** The sought-for answer would look like this: "For this system, with this habitat census and this instrument, the false-alarm and miss rates are bounded at these values, measured prospectively against the ground before the crossing." Written down, it can accrue a residual and be refused. Without writing it down, the preference for a yes or a no is invisible to the loop (Fig 5 :62-63).

**Scale and ground.** The anchor is the actual crossing and its reversibility, a state of the world that does not change because a model thinks otherwise (Fig 2 :29). Crucially, it is observable only after the event it is meant to predict. This asymmetry is the source of most of the difficulty below.

## 1b. Domain census (derive first, before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**
- Arity: at least two conditions. (i) A signal appears before the crossing. (ii) The signal discriminates, with bounded false alarms and misses, in time to act. These are not one hard question. Fig 9 :55 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." A single lever, more data for instance, clears where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing.
- Symmetry: not symmetric. The crossing is a threshold relation between a driver and a response, and the return path differs from the outbound path. Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- Scale: local slot to regional aggregate; one crossing versus a cascade of coupled crossings. Fig 11 :52-53 gives the DDT case, where "the residual that mattered was three steps out."

**Partition or union: UNION.** "Reliable prediction" bundles at least four overlapping conditions, each with its own loop: the habitat census, the instrument, the reading, and the decision to act before reversal becomes difficult. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is decided before any figure line is chosen to govern a verdict.

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor. A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.**

- **D1, the habitat and cascade.** Actors: species, stressors (temperature, nutrients, harvesting), conditions (light, water, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact and cascade, first order out to third. Anchor: the measured trajectory of the physical system, which moves but does not consult the model. Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
- **D2, the monitoring instrument.** Actors: early-warning statistics (variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness, recovery rate), time series, models. Currency: signal detection, false alarms, misses. Anchor: the actual crossing event, observable only retrospectively. Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **D3, the reading frame.** Actors: global versus per-slot readings, pooled indicators. Currency: residual per slot versus averaged residual. Anchor: the local state transition at the slot that actually crossed. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D4, the decision and reversal frame.** Actors: managers, policy processes, the rate at which a driver can be changed. Currency: actions taken under uncertainty; cost of being wrong. Anchor: reversibility itself, whether the system returns when the driver is restored. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Without this anchor D4 would not be a domain, and the phrase "before changes become difficult to reverse" would be decorative.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Enumerate the habitat (D1) before aiming the instrument (D2); read per slot (D3) before pooling; settle the perception-to-objective link (D4) before asking whether the system is good at predicting. Fig 3 :29 "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed." Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19-20).

## 2. Findings

### F1 : The question is a union compound, not one hard question

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :55 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

"Signal appears" and "signal discriminates reliably in time to act" are two conditions. A better detector clears the first while leaving the second binding; a faster policy process clears the second while leaving the first. Bundled, they invite a single yes or no that neither domain supports. The honest answer is four per-domain verdicts kept separate and never averaged.

**Refuter:** show that the four domains draw on the same evidence such that a single lever moves all four at once. That would collapse the union to a partition and remove the need to unbundle.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 plus the dependency order of Fig 3, two figures with different subject matter).

### F2 : The habitat is enumerated, never composed, and the governing contact may be three steps out (decisive for D1)

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

A monitoring scheme that tracks the obvious direct drivers can be correct at first order and still miss the actor that governs the crossing. The list is read off the world, not composed from a theory, so no frame can certify from inside that it is complete. This is why a claim to reliability cannot be certified in advance by the monitoring frame itself.

**Refuter:** a closed, enumerated habitat for a real ecosystem in which every actor capable of moving the crossing is shown to be in contact with the instrument, with the closure demonstrated against the ground. Until such a closure is shown, the census stays open.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 enumeration and cascade, Fig 13 :31 chaos/noise as a different mechanism limiting resolution).

### F3 : Reliability is a property of agent and habitat, not of a statistic

Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"
Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

A better early-warning algorithm, trained on the same data and pointed at the same variables, "changes nothing" in the sense that matters if the actor governing the crossing is still outside contact. This is the figure's capability claim applied to monitoring. It forbids answering the question with a ranking of indicators independent of where they are deployed.

**Refuter:** demonstrate an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, that is, a statistic that reduces miss and false-alarm rates on a new ecosystem without any change in what the observing system is in contact with. Fig 11 :71-72 names this as the standing refuter of the capability claim.

**Status:** single-source for the framework claim (Fig 11), corroborated weakly by world sources that show indicator performance depends on the model and the observation conditions (Zhang, Kuehn and Hallerberg 2015, `10_source_ledger.csv`). Different evidence pools, so the corroboration is not circular, but the figures decide.

### F4 : A pooled all-clear is a legal state with a live local residual

Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"
Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

This governs false negatives directly. An ecosystem-wide mean can be flat while one patch is about to cross. The signal that matters is per slot, and the framework disqualifies the aggregate as a verdict: a global statistic cannot say where to look. In ecological terms, a regional-average indicator can read quiet while a local tipping event is underway.

**Refuter:** a case where a pooled statistic carries the same decision-relevant information as the per-slot residual for a system with known spatial heterogeneity, demonstrated against the ground. That would show aggregation does not lose the signal here.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 10 :25 "pooled readings", two separate figures).

### F5 : Instrument reach is bounded, and the two instruments are opposed

Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 6 :34-35 "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."
Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

For systems where a CSD-type statistic is already returning a failing signal, the inward route is open: sharpen the instrument from that signal. For systems where nothing at any resolution has registered, there is no edge to extend, and the honest report is unverified, not "safe." Fig 8 :40-43 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Confusing them yields either false confidence or needless imports.

**Refuter:** exhibit a proto-instrument for a system where nothing currently registers, built without being improved from a worse instrument already returning something, and show it works against the ground. That would break the figure's instrument rule.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 instrument construction, Fig 8 proof of absence, Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing.").

### F6 : The indicator is a lossy map; the round trip gap must be measured

Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
Fig 4 :31 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"

An early-warning statistic is an abstraction from a high-dimensional trajectory to one or two numbers. Carrying it back down to a claim about the system state is a concretisation that will not match what was sent up. The gap R_T is not noise to be explained away; it is the measurement of what the statistic cannot hold. A report of reliability that does not report this gap is dead reckoning.

**Refuter:** a statistic whose abstraction and concretisation compose to identity on the relevant states, so R_T is zero, verified against the ground. No such result is claimed here.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 4 round trip, Fig 10 :24 channel decay as a separate failure mode).

### F7 : Absence of warning is not evidence of safety

Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

An ecosystem is not a sealed room. A monitoring programme that has searched and found no warning has established only that its search did not register. To report "no tipping point ahead" from that silence is to treat a closure as sealed. The figures' instruction is to report unreached, not safe.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a sealed frame for the ecosystem in question, meaning a closure proven exhaustive against the ground, so that finding nothing genuinely licenses absence. This is the same refuter as F2 with the stricter requirement.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 9 and Fig 13, three figures with distinct roles).

### F8 : A flat reading may be a dead channel, not a safe system

Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"
Fig 10 :27 "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"

The ground keeper's failure mode maps exactly onto monitoring. A saturated metric, a pooled reading, or a dropped transient produces the same output as a genuinely quiet system: the indicator is flat. The output of that state "is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch." So a claim to reliability must include a test that the channel is still ranging against the ground, not just a test that the reading is flat.

**Refuter:** an instrument for which channel death is distinguishable from system quiet by construction, with both states mapped to different readings and confirmed on the ground.

**Status:** single-source for the figure list (Fig 10), corroborated by Layritz et al. 2025, who show that the standard indicators fail under non-Gaussian noise and can yield spurious results. Different field and evidence pool.

### F9 : "Before it becomes difficult to reverse" is a separate dependency link

Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

Reversal difficulty is not a property of the predictor. It is a property of the loop between the driver and the response: if the driver can only be changed slowly, action is needed well before the indicator rises; if it can be changed quickly, action can wait until the shift is underway (Biggs, Carpenter and Brock 2009, `10_source_ledger.csv`). The positive feedback that closes the tipping loop is Fig 12's cycle, and the return path is a different composition. A predictor that does not measure the rate at which the driver can be changed has not answered the "in time" part of the question.

**Refuter:** show that the time available for management is determined entirely by the indicator's lead time, independent of how fast the driver can be manipulated. That would collapse D4 into D2.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 3 dependency order plus Fig 12 cycle plus Fig 2 cost of being wrong).

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Required before outreach: Seat Map rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." Written here before the world search and before the verdict.

The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A closed, enumerated habitat is demonstrated for a real ecosystem in which every actor capable of moving the crossing is in contact with the instrument, verified against the ground (breaks F2, F5, F7).
2. A pooled statistic is shown to carry the same decision-relevant information as per-slot residuals in a system with known spatial heterogeneity, verified against the ground (breaks F4).
3. An indicator is shown to have a zero round trip gap between its abstraction and its concretisation on the relevant states (breaks F6).
4. Channel death is shown to be distinguishable from system quiet by construction in a deployed monitor (breaks F8).
5. The time available for management is shown to be independent of how fast the driver can be changed (breaks F9).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the verdict is re-censused, not softened.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Do not answer yes or no to the bundled question. Answer the four domains separately (F1).
2. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim, not with an indicator ranking (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
3. Require any reliability claim to carry a habitat census, a per-slot residual, a measured R_T, a channel-health test, and a driver-manipulation rate (F2, F4, F6, F8, F9).
4. Report absence of warning as unreached, never as safe (F7).
5. Use public opt-in submission venues and ask for a refutation, not agreement (Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Strains and open items

- **Fig 6 inward versus outward.** Named, not hidden. The inward route (sharpen an instrument already returning something) and the outward route (find another frame holding the atom) are opposed and must not be read against each other. Both are run; neither decides the other.
- **Fig 11 habitat enumeration is never complete.** This is a structural open item in the framework itself, not a defect found here. It is the reason F7's refuter requires a demonstrated closure.
- **The ground is retrospective.** The anchor that settles a tipping prediction is the crossing, which is only observable after the event. The figures give no rule for settling a prediction before the event except the pre-registered refuter and the measured gap. Recorded as an honest limit, not smoothed.
- **World source overlap.** Scheffer 2009, Dakos 2012 and Lenton 2008 share authors and an evidence pool. Their agreement is one witness, not three. Layritz 2025, Zhang 2015, Hurlstone 2025 and Boettiger 2012 are lower overlap but are corroboration only; the verdict comes from the figures.

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives: the question is a union compound; reliability is a relation between a monitor and its habitat; pooled readings hide live slots; absence of warning is not safety; the statistic-to-state map has a measurable round trip gap; and "in time" depends on how fast the driver can be changed. Every specific claim is `specified` and ungrounded. No figure line was forced to carry a quantity it does not govern. Strains after re-census: three named, none hidden.
