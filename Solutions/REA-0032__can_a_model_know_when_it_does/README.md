## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a simulated derivation settles the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label." Adding agreeing readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that voids their agreement.
3. A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03` avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
