**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **refusal operator**: calibration tunes a score and does not install negation; the shorthand "knows when it does not know" silently absorbs an operator the figures leave open;

- the **independence requirement**: a deployment that checks a model against its own samples has dropped the Fig 2 low-overlap condition;
- the **direction limit**: a deployment that never wrote its sought-for shape down cannot check whether it answered the question asked.

Conversely, what survives cleanly: a frame cannot certify its own completeness, and an external anchor is required for a verdict. That is the residue worth sending.
