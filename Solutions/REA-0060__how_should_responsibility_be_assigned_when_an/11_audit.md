## Independent domain census

Symmetry: the decisive question is whether swapping two hands changes the harm and the refusal set. If it does not, the hands are one term under a symmetric bond (Fig 12 :92); if it does, the bond carries the difference. Scale: individual, organization, deployed system, with the membrane between sizes changing only actors, currency, and anchor (Fig 4 :20).

- **Domain 2, habitat.** Actors: enumerated contacts and their contacts outward. Currency: contact with the residual. Anchor: the harm. Enumeration, not composition. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 3, assignment.** Actors: checker, parties, forum. Currency: evidence versus agreement. Anchor: the ground, never negotiation. Nameable: yes, but strained when the ground is an institution.

Refuse before mint; describe residual before choosing a frame. The middle link has no instrument (Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. - **Partition or union.** Hand level: partition. Question-type level: union of attribution, capability, assignment (Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*).

My census matches the primary's three domains and both levels of the partition/union reading.
