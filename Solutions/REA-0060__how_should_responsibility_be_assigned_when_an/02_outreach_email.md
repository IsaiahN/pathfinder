**Subject:** One checkable claim about assigning responsibility with many hands

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output to put in front of you. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when a harm comes from many designers, operators, and institutions, responsibility should be indexed per contributor and per institution, never averaged into one global verdict. Each contributor is a separate slot. For each slot, three questions are asked and recorded: what did this hand hold (data, authority, instructions), was the harmful route reachable from what it held, and did it have a refusal available. The decision record, not the roster of names, is the evidence. A court or board that assigns the verdict should sit outside the slice it grades, declare any stake it cannot remove, and leave a record of the residual answered, the reasoning, and the date. The harm itself is the fixed point; it does not change because the parties reinterpret it.
>
> This is a **shape and a procedure**, not a name. It does not say that any particular engineer or firm is liable in any actual case. That needs an investigation or a court, which I am not claiming to have.
>
> **What would refute it, written before I wrote the rest:**
> 1. A case where averaging across the hands preserves, for every hand, the signal that shows which hand's change would have altered the outcome.
> 2. A hand held responsible at the ground for whom the harmful route was unreachable in what it actually held.
> 3. A decision process whose fixed point changes when the parties push on it and still settles responsibility correctly later.
>
> If any of these holds, I withdraw the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figure text is the authority, not my summary of it.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` (local extract of the canonical SVG set)
> - Eight-step formula and mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]
