# What "shape" means for this question

**Mode: `general` on the reading, `specified` where it binds a claim.**

Sources read 2026-11-23: the canonical figures README (local copy of the GitHub `figures/README.md`), `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, and the eight framework essays as mapped by the completed exemplar at `Solutions/QMGR-0037__spacetime_continuum_or_emergent/05_what_shape_means.md`. The essays are by Isaiah N, 2026-08 to 2026-11.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134-135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

For this question, a shape is the form a correct responsibility assignment must take before anyone can name a liable party. It constrains: per-slot indexing, enumerated habitat, recorded bonds, decidability per slot, a non-updating ground, a seat outside the slice, and a provenanced ruling. It does not fill: it does not say which engineer, operator, or firm is answerable in a real case.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The six-part procedure is that narrowed set.

**Concrete contrast.** "The system was responsible" is a name without a shape: it skips indexing and lands on the artefact. "Engineer X was negligent" is a name without provenance: it skips the bond and the decidability test. "Enumerate the hands, index each, test reachability, read the lineage, fix the ground, seat the checker outside" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- The figures README says to name actors, currency, and anchor, then apply the loop. That is the domain census in `01` section 1.
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** So the residual is described as "a harm with many causal contributors", not as "a negligence claim under statute X", until the frame is chosen.
- Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** The effect is the harm and the distribution of contributions; the cause is a legal characterization that has not yet been earned.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends a checkable procedure, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because the answer was composed through the locating process. For responsibility, the stopping point is a recorded lineage whose route can be read; that is both the method and the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question the ground is a checkable record, an investigation, or a court, applied to a specific harm. Until one pays, status is `candidate`.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A legal doctrine imported into the procedure must say where it came from and explain a gap the procedure already had.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring "the responsible party" does not make the assignment neutral; it makes the choice visible and therefore refusable.

## 4. Why the roster commits the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the behavior artifact is the reading. Many different routes through the hands can produce the same harm. Naming a responsible hand from the artifact is exactly the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a better instrument, which in this domain is the decision lineage kept at the time (Fig 13 :127, :130), not a better argument after the fact.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is why the assigning seat must be outside the slice it grades (Fig 10 :86) and why this derivation cannot certify itself.

## 5. Prestige and agreement are not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A list of famous experts who agree with a responsibility shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground (a real case, a record, an investigation) settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional venues, not personal addresses of prominent authors.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction recorded rather than erased

An earlier reading of this file treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any responsible role. That overstates it, the same correction the exemplar records for its own reading. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Here the roles (designer, operator, institution, checker) are already in the habitat; they are not missing primitives. What the framework forbids is inventing a standing role that predicts nothing, and naming a liable party without a ground. The narrowed form is kept, and the earlier overstatement is preserved here so a reader can see the correction rather than only the corrected claim.
