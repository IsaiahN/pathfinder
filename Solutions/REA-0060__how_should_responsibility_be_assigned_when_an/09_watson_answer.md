## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms how answerability should be assigned when an autonomous system's behavior results from many designers, operators, and institutions. Option A: diffuse responsibility across all hands, or assign it to no one because each hand held only a partial role. Option B: enumerate the hands, index each as a slot, record the bonds, test reachability and refusal per slot, fix a non-updating harm ground, and assign from a seat outside the slice graded. What each leads to: A sends the live signal into an average (Fig 1 :22), lets the anchor drift into a frame (Fig 2 :34), and moves the question along a mirror chain where agreement compounds and evidence does not (Fig 7 :13). B sends a checkable procedure, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles the specific party.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A harm with many causal contributors, and no settled answer to who is answerable. Arity: k hands plus the harm. Symmetry: hands are not interchangeable unless swapping them leaves the harm and the refusal set unchanged (Fig 12 :92); if they are, the roster suffices. Scale: individual, team or organization, deployed system (Fig 4 :20). Fig 9 :57 names the question-type level a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, attribution (framework internal).** Actors: hands as slots, typed bonds between contributions, the residual harm. Currency: residual mass per slot, cost of a term or refusal. Anchor: the recorded harm, which does not move when parties reinterpret it. Nameable: yes. Question type: partition across hands.
- **Domain 2, the socio-technical habitat.** Actors: designers, operators, deployers, data, institutions, conditions, relations. Currency: contact with the residual, causal contribution. Anchor: the harm and the enumerated habitat. Nameable: yes. Question type: enumeration, never composition (Fig 11 :43).
- **Domain 3, the assigning seat.** Actors: the checker, the parties, the forum. Currency: evidence versus agreement, provenance. Anchor: the ground, never negotiation. Nameable: yes, but strained when the ground is an institution; see the strain note below.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the harm; acquire terms for what each hand affords; say what counts as progress; plan the assignment; carry the ruling with provenance (Fig 3 :20; Fig 10 :62). Refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
- **Strain test.** The six-term substrate of Fig 13 governs physical composition, not attribution; mapping it onto responsibility types was attempted and dropped as a strain. The live strain is the institutional ground: a court can be moved by framing, so the anchor is narrowed to the recorded harm and the sanction is a convention (Fig 10 :46).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no name does. Responsibility should be indexed per hand and per institution, never averaged; the bonds between hands must be recorded, because same roster with different bonds is a different substance; each hand must be tested for what it held, reachability, and refusal; the harm record is the fixed point; the assigning seat is outside the slice graded, declares unremovable stakes, and issues a dated provenanced ruling. The many-hands case is a partition across hands and a union across attribution, capability, and assignment, so it must be unbundled before a search. Buck-passing is a mirror chain. The framework returns this procedure and its refuter, not a liable party. Status `specified`, `candidate`, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the procedure is sent with its refuter. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A wrong indexing rule would hide a live slot residual inside an average, which is exactly the failure Fig 1 :22 names. That cost is avoidable by keeping the per-slot record.
- **undoable:** yes. The procedure can be revised slot by slot without withdrawing the enumeration rule. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no part of the habitat has to be deleted. If a hand drops out on the reachability test, the record keeps it rather than erasing it, consistent with the framework's refusal to delete.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A recorded decision lineage that shows which route produced the harm; a per-slot reachability measurement; a procedure whose fixed point is shown to survive pressure; a figure re-census that admits a standing responsible role surviving changes of residual; or a documented case where averaging preserves the discriminating signal for every hand. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
