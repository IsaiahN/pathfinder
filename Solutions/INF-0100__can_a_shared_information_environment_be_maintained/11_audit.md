## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set actually used, chiefly Fig 2 :39-47 (valence and anchor), Fig 7 :13, :35-36 (handoff), Fig 8 :47-49 (independence), Fig 10 :46-49 (conventions), Fig 11 :61-66 (habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: two answers for two objects; convention yes, verdict no; independence as budget; refusal required; introduction not exclusion; permanent work.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete question with three disputed slots and real communities.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor for relevant context**. "Non-updating anchor" is clean at the abstract level; for S3 the only candidate anchor may be the affected community, which is a party (Fig 2 :36 against :47). The shorthand drops the constitutive question.
- the **measurement of overlap**. "Low overlap" is a structural term; at the ground it needs measured shared-source overlap and an intervention that breaks the pool. The shorthand does not say how, and Fig 10 :83 warns that the selection test has not been tested forward.
- the **cost of a refusal**. Operators table :58 says a vocabulary without negation can only accumulate; what a refusal costs in actions, and who is authorised to issue one without becoming a party, is not carried by the shorthand.

One thing survives cleanly: a convention and a verdict are different objects with different refuters, and agreement has opposite valence for each. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
