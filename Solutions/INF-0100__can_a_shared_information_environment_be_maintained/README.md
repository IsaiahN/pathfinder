# Solution INF-0100: can a shared information environment be maintained?

**Question:** Can a shared information environment be maintained when communities disagree about sources, standards of evidence, and what counts as relevant context?

**Source:** Information Theory.txt line 121.

**Section:** 10. Social information, reliability, and misinformation.

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement of an actual information environment was taken.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pass, not by this folder).

**Standing of this derivation.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading and it cannot settle the question it reports on. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6 and `11_audit.md`.

## The residual in effect terms

The question asks whether a condition can be maintained. The residual is not "do the communities agree" (agreement is frame-internal, Fig 1 :27). The residual is the failure of a claim to range against a common anchor when the parties dispute which sources count, which evidence standards count, and which context is relevant. Those are three live slots, not one global score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

The answer turns on a distinction the figures force: a shared information environment can be a **convention** (shared meaning) or a **verdict** (a claim about the world). Correlation has valence: it is "disqualifying for a verdict" and "efficient for a convention" (Fig 2 :39-41). The two halves must be kept separate and never averaged.

## Short answer

Yes for the convention, no for a single verdict, and only under conditions. A shared environment is maintainable when (1) the shared object is an authored convention that makes disagreement statable and never decides an outcome (Fig 10 :16-17, :46-49), (2) each frame ranges separately against an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29-30, :33, :45-46), (3) independence between frames is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42, Fig 8 :47-49), and (4) the environment can carry a refusal, because a vocabulary without negation can only accumulate (Operators table :58). It is not maintainable as one agreed body of facts, because "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13) and a chain of agreeing rooms is one long room (Fig 7 :36).
