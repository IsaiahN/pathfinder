## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when formalized hierarchy, rule and procedure protect a company from error and when they suppress initiative and adaptation. Option A: treat bureaucracy as one thing with a magnitude, so the answer is a right amount. Option B: treat the question as a union of conditions with residual-relative valence, so the answer is a shape with per-domain verdicts and no universal number. What each leads to: A returns a number no figure holds and averages away the per-slot signal; B returns a checkable shape, six refuters written in advance, and a declared gap where an instrument is owed.

Arity: two conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"); Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". - **Domain 1, error detection (the guard).** Actors: the slot, the check, the counter, the frontline expert. Currency: per-slot residual R_s. Anchor: an external outcome that does not change when the organization thinks about it (Fig 2 :29). Question type: partition boundary (is a channel to that anchor reachable and is the signal indexed?).
- **Domain 2, adaptation (the reach).** Actors: the method, the head start, the import, the differing room, the habitat. Currency: atoms crossing a closure wall and contact. Anchor: the same ground read fresh, plus the requirement that the room change with the question (Fig 10 :72 "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom."). Question type: a binding onto held arrangements, not a new atom.
- **Domain 3, control and verification.** Actors: the standard, the KPI, the approver, the seat maintaining the anchor. Currency: agreement versus residual, convention versus verdict. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). Question type: is the rule a checkable convention or a convention that decided an outcome?
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); keep the anchor reachable (Fig 10 :29); settle the first-order guard before composing the chain (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Bureaucracy protects where a rule keeps an unmoved ground reachable at the slot, keeps the residual indexed, remains a reapplicable method and can be refused. It suppresses where the rule becomes its own outcome, the channel saturates, the approval rooms share one evidence pool, and a spent recording is replayed into a changed habitat. The two are a union with a categorically different intersection; per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged, and no universal amount is returned. The specific labels "enabling" and "coercive" are provenanced bindings from another closure, not figure-derived primitives and not imports that move a wall. Mode `specified` on the domains, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`; the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its six refuters and its provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is returning a number the framework does not hold, which would be a frame certifying more than it can settle (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A superseded packet can replace this one, the per-domain verdicts can be narrowed independently, and the bindings to organizational sociology can be corrected without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the main temptations (a universal amount, a compliance score as safety, a claim that the labels were derived).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates a self-updating reference from an unmoved one at the slot would settle Domain 1. A source-discriminating instrument showing whether a rule regenerates each use would settle Domain 2. A demonstration that a one-pool approval chain adds evidence at the ground, or that a ruleset without ¬ can refuse a false rule without loss of reach, would break Domain 3. Showing that protection and suppression are one measurable function with one optimum would dissolve the union. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
