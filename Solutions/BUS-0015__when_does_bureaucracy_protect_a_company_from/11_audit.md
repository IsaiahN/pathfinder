Question type: is the rule a checkable convention or a convention that decided an outcome? Here too the independence is zero, because this simulated seat reads the same files as the primary.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29/:33-35, Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 4 :22-26, Fig 10 :24/:47/:72, Fig 7 :35-36, Fig 11 :49-51 and Fig 12 :117.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete management recommendation, for example "adopt enabling bureaucracy," "cut two approval layers," "cap formalization at 30 percent."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **the source of the anchor.** The shorthand says "unmoved ground." A real firm has many candidate anchors (market share, patient outcome, regulator, board), and a source-discriminating instrument is needed to tell which one is actually not updating. "Adopt enabling bureaucracy" carries no such instrument.
- **the method/recording distinction.** Fig 4 :22-26 requires the rule to be reapplicable. A handbook entry does not record whether it was regenerated against the ground this shift. The shorthand's word "method" is a claim the concrete artefact cannot support without a fresh reading (Fig 12 :119 "nothing records which a term is").
- **the refusal operator.** Fig 10 :42 requires a verifier that cannot reconstruct the claim, and the Operators table makes negation unary. A committee with authority is not obviously a unary operator on a term. C3 in `08` states the claim; the instrument to test it is not supplied. This is the live type question.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a self-updating reference is the drift failure, and an averaged compliance score has thrown away the per-slot signal. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
