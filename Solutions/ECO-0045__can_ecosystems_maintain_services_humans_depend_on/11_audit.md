## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's conclusions, in the
WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor;
partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a vector over five services and over species
  slots. Arity: multi-place, so a union compound rather than one scalar question. Symmetry: the
  services are not symmetric terms; the isomer and functional-group structure of Fig 12 applies, so
  permuting actors or relations can change the service. Scale: local contact, out to a cascade three
  steps beyond direct contact, and across time to a delayed failure.
- **Domain 1, habitat and ecological contact.** Actors: species, conditions, relations. Currency:
  contact and the flows it sustains. Anchor: what the ground kept in the unmaintained mode. Question
  type: enumeration of a habitat, never composition of one.
- **Domain 2, service and function.** Actors: functional groups, catalysts, cycles. Currency:
  per-service flow. Anchor: the measured flow for that service. Question type: bond classification
  and settlement.
- **Domain 3, management and assessment seat.** Actors: managers, societies, assessors, instruments.
  Currency: introduced actors, conventions, evidence. Anchor: the same ground, which does not update.
  Question type: asymmetric addition with a declared stake.
- **X1, biodiversity as a free-floating guarantee.** No nameable anchor distinct from D1 and D2.
  Absorbed.
- **X2, habitat as ecological niche.** This is the ecologist's term, not the framework's. The
  canonical article declines the mapping. Recorded as a refused mapping, not a domain.

Order by dependency: describe R per service; enumerate habitat and cascade; settle each service;
declare the seat and its stake; then compose cross-service trade-offs.

## Round trip gap R_T

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :40, :52-53, :61, :63; Fig 12 :117, :123; Fig 1 :19.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` §8 and `07` §2, namely "services depend on habitat structure, functional groups and cascades, not on count."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a claim that a particular service survives a particular percentage loss in a particular place.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the identity of the functional groups and bonds for the particular service, which the shorthand leaves as a variable;
- the cascade depth and the conditions under which the contact enumeration stops mattering;

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a species count cannot decide a service, and redundancy is a bond question. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
