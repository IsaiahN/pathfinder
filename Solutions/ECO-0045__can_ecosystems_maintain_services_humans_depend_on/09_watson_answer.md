## QUESTION

Can ecosystems maintain services humans depend on while losing much of their biodiversity? Restated as an effect: for each service s (pollination, soil formation, nutrient cycling, water regulation, carbon storage), can the flow stay above the level the ground pays for while the species count falls substantially? Option A: yes, services are buffered by redundancy, so manage for delivery with fewer species. Option B: no, services depend on particular actors, relations and cascades, so loss degrades them, often with a delay and sometimes past a threshold. R is indexed per service and per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The sought-for shape was written down and bound before the run, as the Formula's "Direction" rule requires.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Services can be maintained under biodiversity loss only conditionally, never by virtue of the count. The figure-derived shape is: services stay up if and only if the functional groups, bonds and contact cascades that generate each service survive, and each service is settled separately against its own measured ground. Interchangeability is within a functional group under the same bond; different bonds give different services even with the same species list. Failure can be third-order and delayed. Maintenance by humans is permanent work, with addition available and exclusion unavailable, and requires a declared seat. Verdicts stay per service and are never averaged. The safe loss percentage is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and wasted checks. A service failing because of a severed third-order cascade would be sold more species, and a count-driven index would be treated as a witness. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is bounded because the packet carries its refuters and lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out. The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A count-invariance result across several habitats and cascades; a bond-invariance result; a service crossing its threshold with every enumerated contact intact; a demonstrated exclusion of an unwanted actor; or a single lever restoring all services with no categorically different residue. Any of these fires a pre-registered refuter in `01` §5. Agreement among assessors sharing an evidence pool is not such a reading.
