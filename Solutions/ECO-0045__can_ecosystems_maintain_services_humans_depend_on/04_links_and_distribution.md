## Relevant wider literature, grouped by what it bears on

1. **Species identity and per-service residuals.** Cardinale et al. 2012 and Hooper et al. 2012 both
   report that species identity and which species are lost change the outcome. These bear directly on
   Fig 11 :49-51 and Fig 12 :117. They share authors and the NCEAS working group, so they are one
   evidence pool, not two independent witnesses.
2. **Assessment and unbundling.** IPBES 2019 and the Millennium Ecosystem Assessment 2005 both
   organise the problem per service and per human-well-being outcome. They corroborate the
   unbundling rule (Fig 9 :54) and the ground rule (Fig 1 :23), as institutional framing.
3. **Habitat engineering and cascades.** The two Wikipedia entries corroborate Fig 11 :65 and :52-53.
   Their authority is secondary. The "Ecosystem engineer" entry draws on the same Jones et al. 1994
   source the figure cites, so overlap on that atom is high; Fig 8 :50-51 says convergent derivation
   and adopted import look identical in the contents, and only the record separates them.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The one tension, Cardinale et al.'s "maintain a
satisfactory level of ecosystem services" read as an unqualified yes, was returned to the census in
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and downgraded to a `specified` claim awaiting its ground. The
conflict rule was not triggered.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter.
Record the recipient's verdict before any framework material is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never
pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter,
names their own ground and states their own mode (Formula "Direction" and "Mode").

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. Nothing in this folder
claims it has been checked against a ground; it is a candidate, `specified` and ungrounded.
