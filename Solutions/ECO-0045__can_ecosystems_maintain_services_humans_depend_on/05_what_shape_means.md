## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13
says it can report that the answer is out of reach and characterise what is missing in effect terms,
"which is the description that travels to a frame that may hold it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For ECO-0045, the
shape is: services stay up if the functional groups, bonds and contact cascades that generate each
service survive, and the ground settles each service separately. A number, such as "up to 30 percent
species loss is safe," is a name for a threshold, not a shape. The framework cannot give it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for
  recurring new terms that overlap with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and
  deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the
  process of locating it."

For this question, the effect description is deliberately not "biodiversity supports services." It
is the more awkward but checkable statement about functional groups, bonds, cascades and per-service
ground. The awkwardness is the point: a smooth slogan is a projection that cannot be discriminated.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."
   The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can
   distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test
   (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the
existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a
human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be
those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: declaring "the habitat" or "the functional group" does not make the choice neutral. It
makes it visible. Naming a particular species or a particular loss percentage as *the* answer turns
a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source problem: why a service index is not enough

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and
"cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme
not yet discovered, all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then
behavior is a projection with lossy information." "The answer is unreached until a better instrument
exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point with the video frame and the photograph: the
two artifacts are indistinguishable and "nothing in the image records which." Its warning applies
directly to an ecosystem-service index: two habitats with the same index reading can have different
sources, and the reading cannot say which. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a
better slogan.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or
the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. The mapping this question is most likely to get wrong

The canonical article *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* has a section "Three mappings
offered and declined." One of them is this question's trap:

> "The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its
> environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the
> same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and
> the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than
> borrowing the ecological term outright."

So Fig 11's "habitat" is not the ecologist's "habitat" or "niche." It is residual-relative and it
updates when the question changes. Because ECO-0045 is an ecology question, the word looks native
when it is not. This is the exact near-miss the article records, and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4
records the retraction of the earlier "home ground, low strain" reading.

The same article's citation list is also relevant: it states that several claims carry no citation
because none was found, including "an actor's valence is relative to the residual rather than a
property it carries" and "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
Those are framework claims, not borrowed ecological findings. World sources may corroborate the
shape, but they do not establish the framework claim itself.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be
allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget
to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer
must be blind to prestige." A list of famous assessors who agree that services will be maintained is
the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and
lets measured service flows settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the
real architecture or working the formula yourself." The article's demo section adds that outputs
"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01`
is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test
of whether ecosystems maintain services under biodiversity loss, and it must be labelled as such. It
cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. ANSWERED: what "shape" means here

Nothing in the framework licenses the threshold number, the species identity, or the order in which
services fail. What it licenses is the conditional structure: functional groups and bonds decide
interchangeability; contact cascades decide delayed failure; the seat can add and cannot subtract;
each service is settled by its own ground. A particular number or species is licensed only by a
ground measurement or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, it is the author's own
contribution and must be recorded as such.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Fig 11 directly: "capability is a property of
agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." For ECO-0045 that is the structural heart of the
answer, with the §5 caveat firmly attached: it is a metalaw about residual-relative habitat, not a
restatement of the ecological niche, and it is not a measurement of any real ecosystem.
