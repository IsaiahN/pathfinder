# Outreach draft: check the residual before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the measure in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4 is in place. If the first contact uses a verbal report as the sole measure of reasoning, the offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about concepts without words, and what would refute it

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** Take a concept-slot s that a person's language does not routinely name. The question is whether the person's prediction error on that slot, R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|, can be driven down by acting, or whether a word for s is a precondition. The framework measures R per slot, never by counting how many slots have words. So "unnamed in the language" is not the same claim as "no term in the person's library".
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A name is neither necessary nor sufficient for effective reasoning. What does the work is (1) contact with a ground through perception, action and correction, and (2) whether the concept is reachable from atoms the person already holds. That gives two tracks, and they are opposed: search inside the current reach first, because it is cheapest; if nothing closes R_s, import from a frame whose closure differs (another language, another practice, an instrument). The two tracks must be run separately and never read against each other.
>
> **What this does not claim.** It does not claim that any particular language traps its speakers. It does not claim that words never help; a word can be a cheap way to state a term the person already holds. It does not claim that the six-term substrate is a language's word list. It does not claim to know which unnamed concepts are tractable, because that needs an instrument, and this derivation has none. Strong linguistic determinism is refused on structural grounds: a language with no outside would be a sealed room, and the framework's own rule says no frame is a sealed room.
>
> **What would refute it (written before this message):**
> 1. A concept-slot with no term in any modality, with perception, objective and planning links independently intact, where R_s does not trend down across repeated acts.
> 2. Adding only a word, with contact and everything else held fixed, produces a maintained and replicated improvement on s across two independent groups using different measures.
> 3. A new atom appears from composition strictly inside the current reach, or from a frame with an identical closure.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Worked architecture essay: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [Your name]

## Contact discipline

- One recipient at a time. No distribution list, no bulk send, no automation.
- The verdict on stage 1 is recorded **before** any framework material is shown. Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").
- A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not").
- If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have it re-evaluated as if it came from any other source. The author of this solution has a declared stake in the reading and may not be the one who decides it.
