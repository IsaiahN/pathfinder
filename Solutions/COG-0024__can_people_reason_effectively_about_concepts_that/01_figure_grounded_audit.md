## 0. The question under audit, as a residual

> Can people reason effectively about concepts that their language does not routinely name?

Restated in effect terms: R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′| on a concept-slot s for which the ambient language L supplies no routine label. Two options and what each leads to:

- **A (determinism).** No label means link 2 of the dependency chain has no term, so no objective can be stated, no plan can be formed, and R_s cannot be driven down. Language is a sealed room.
- **B (contact and reach).** A label is one public convention sitting on top of Γ. Γ also holds percepts, motor schemas, images, affordances and dispositions. R_s can fall through contact and composition even when L is silent. A name may compress the reasoning, but is not a precondition for it.

**Bound shape before the run** (Formula step "Direction", Seat Map :287): an answer is a *per-slot condition* of the form "R_s falls iff the slot is reachable in closure(Γ) or contact with a differing closure supplies the missing axis; the linguistic label is neither necessary nor sufficient." I write this down now so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term.

**Arity / symmetry / scale of R.** Arity three: agent library Γ, concept-slot s, ground return o'. Symmetry: none that matters. The relevant bonds are ordered and thresholded, not symmetric (Fig 12 :72 "A then B; B needs A to have happened"; Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"). Scale: one person's loop, a language community's transmitted vocabulary, and the lossy membrane between them (Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"). R is per slot, never averaged across people or concepts (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").

## 3. Findings

### F1 — A missing name is not a missing slot; R is indexed per concept, never per word

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symbols table :43: "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global" (line wraps to "number"). Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

The slot is the concept, tracked by its effects. A word is a public label that may or may not be bound to that slot. The question's own phrasing, "does not routinely name", is a statement about L's conventions, not about the existence of the slot or the presence of a term in Γ. The measure of effective reasoning is whether R_s trends down under action and is paid by the ground, not whether L has a lexeme. A global count of "concepts without names" is exactly the aggregation Fig 1 :19 disallows.

**Refuter:** show a concept-slot s for which R_s does not exist or cannot be registered in any modality until L supplies a word, with an instrument that registers slots independently of vocabulary.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 1 per-slot residual and Fig 2 anchor rule, low overlap: one is loop mechanics, the other is what settles). **Mode: general** (structural).

### F2 — The vocabulary link is a grammar of affordances, and it can be filled without words

Fig 3 :7: "it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked". Fig 12 :115: "having terms for what a situation affords was taken for a dictionary and it is a grammar." Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

The determinist reading needs link 2 of Fig 3 to mean *word*. That is a strain. The figure says terms for what things afford, and the citation record in *Arrival of the Fittest* names Gibson's affordance and Spelke and Kinzler's core knowledge as the load-bearing imports for that link, not a lexicon. Terms at link 2 can be perceptual, motor, or social. So "L does not name s" does not entail "link 2 has no term for s". A genuine break requires showing that no term in any modality occupies the slot. Fig 3 :22-23: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing." That warning cuts both ways: a study that measures only verbal access is reading below a break it has not located.

**Refuter:** a task where perception, objective and planning links independently test intact, no term in any modality is available for s, and R_s nevertheless does not trend down across repeated acts.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 3 dependency order and Fig 12 grammar reading, different figures). **Mode: specified** (the application to human concept-slots).

### F3 — Capability lives in contact, not in the possession of a label

Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 4 :20: "it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

Nonlinguistic experience is contact with the habitat. A person can acquire a term for a concept through observation, imitation, and correction without any routine word for it. Adding a word while holding contact fixed is exactly the "improvement in isolation" Fig 11 :40 refuses. The kernel is invariant across size: the same loop runs for one person and for a community; only actors, currency and anchor change.

**Refuter:** a controlled intervention that adds only the name to L, with contact, Γ and habitat held fixed, and produces a maintained step change in R_s across two frames with independent instruments and different evidence pools.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 11 capability and Fig 4 kernel, different scales). **Mode: specified**.

### F4 — A verbal report is an instrument with one axis; it cannot measure nonlinguistic reasoning

Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Fig 2 :37-38 continues: "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings".

There are two anchors here and they must not be merged. Whether an answer is good *for a person* is anchored in human judgment. Whether a person reasoned effectively about s is anchored in the task's ground: the outcome that pays. A verbal report is an instrument on the naming axis. When the report is used as the sole measure of reasoning, a saturated metric (Fig 10 :26) has been mistaken for a verdict. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong answer about human cognition, so the independence is owed.

**Refuter:** an instrument that registers both naming access and reasoning success on the same slot in the same person, and shows they diverge on the naming axis while agreeing on the reasoning axis; or a demonstration that the verbal report is sensitive to the very concept it is asked to measure.

**Status: single-source** for the anchor distinction (Fig 2), corroborated structurally by Fig 10 channel failure. **Mode: specified**.

### F5 — Two cases after a genuine re-census: the unnamed concept is in closure(Γ), or it is not

Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 13 :113: "The array is unbounded and the reach is not. More compositions never add a member, and no chain reaches outside what the six and their bonds already span." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

- **Case 1, in closure.** The concept is composable from atoms the person already holds. It is unnamed in L but reachable. Search is the right instrument (Formula step 7 SEARCHED). A word may be a cheap mint under Fig 5 :26 "and then: does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?" but it is not a new atom and does not move the wall.
- **Case 2, out of closure.** No composition of held atoms reaches s. Then the remedy is OUTWARD: a provenanced import from a differing closure (another language, another sensorium, an instrument, a different practice). Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The import must carry a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13), debited against independence.

The two instruments are opposed and must not be read against each other (Fig 6 :31: "Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."). Both may be needed for the same question, which is why the domain map is a union.

**Refuter:** show closure expanding by internal composition alone (breaks idempotence), or an import from a frame with identical closure adding an atom, or no difference in outcome between the SEARCHED and OUTWARD tracks where the census says there is one.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 6 and Fig 8, two distinct laws). **Mode: general** for the two-case shape, `specified` for applying it to languages.

### F6 — Strong linguistic determinism is a frame certifying its own limit; refused

Fig 8 :37: "A frame cannot certify its own limit. Chaitin: beyond a fixed constant, no system proves that any object exceeds its own complexity." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one".

"Language determines what can be thought" makes the language a final room: a frame with no outside left to import from. The figures forbid that room. The claim is also a convention that has been promoted to decide an outcome, which is the named ground-maintainer failure. And from inside a language you cannot prove that the concept is absent; you can only report that your frame did not find it. This is a refusal, not a softer reading: Formula step 2, REFUTED owes a "refusal, not an explanation".

**Refuter:** produce a frame that proves its own completeness from inside, or an anchor that moves when a language changes and is still an anchor.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 8 limit theorems and Fig 10 convention rule). **Mode: general**.

### F7 — The specific empirical question is a `gap`, not a hidden answer

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," followed by Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 9 :27-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". Fig 6 :34-35: "the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

The figures can deliver the *shape of the condition*: per-slot, contact-dependent, two-track, union. They cannot deliver a list of which concepts, in which languages, for which people, because that requires the ground. Nothing here registers which atoms a given speaker holds, and no instrument in this simulation returns it. To ask for the list is to ask the loop to check its own direction, which Formula step "Direction" declines. This is recorded as `gap`, with the missing thing characterised in effect terms: an instrument that registers a concept-slot independently of the subject's verbal report and independently of the experimenter's language.

**Refuter:** a figure line that, after re-census, predicts the specific distribution of reasonable unnamed concepts across languages without an instrument. None was found.

**Status: single-source** for the limit (Fig 13 plus Formula "Direction", same framework, so one pool). **Mode: general**.

### F8 — World agreement must be bought at low overlap; the conflict returns to the census

Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

The world sources actually read (ledger in `10_source_ledger.csv`) fall into clusters. Human-cognition syntheses about language and thought (linguistic relativity, approximate number system, object permanence) are one broad pool and agree with weak relativity; that is one witness, not three. Comparative work (animal cognition, number sense in animals) is a genuinely low-overlap witness for the mechanism: concept use and numerosity without any language at all. Mathematics and engineering history (infinitesimals; Polanyi's tacit knowledge) are further low-overlap witnesses for "reasoned before named". The Pirahã literature returns a **conflict** (Gordon vs Everett on the same field site), which by the method goes back to the census and does not flip the verdict: it shows the measure matters, not that one side won. All sources were opened 2026-11-22; none is used as a verdict.

**Refuter:** a genuinely independent frame with no shared evidence pool that conflicts with the two-case shape after the residual is restated in its own effect terms.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 2 independence and Fig 7 room chain). **Mode: general**.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

Two readings strained and were sent back:

1. **"Fig 3's vocabulary link means words."** Forced only with a strain. Re-census: the figure says *terms for what things afford*, and its own citation record names affordance and core knowledge. It governs sensorimotor and perceptual terms, not lexemes. Re-read as any modality; strain dissolved. This is the load-bearing correction of the whole solution.
2. **"Fig 13's six-term set is the vocabulary of a language."** Forced. Re-census: Fig 13 governs the substrate of any composed system at any level, not a word list. Applied to a language's lexicon it would claim that no language has more than six words, which is not what it says. Re-read as Γ's atomic kinds; strain dissolved.

One strain is **live and named, not hidden**: Fig 2 makes human judgment the right anchor for "good answer for a person" while the mechanism question is anchored by the task outcome. Those are two anchors on two axes. Keeping them separate is required; merging them is the failure Fig 2 :34 names ("everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"). No further re-census is owed, because the census keeps them in D3 and D1 respectively.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument (Seat Map :287). The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. **Slot without any term.** An instrument that registers concept-slots independently of vocabulary shows a slot s for which no term exists in any modality, perception/objective/planning links test intact, and R_s does not trend down across repeated acts. This kills F1 and F2.
2. **Name is sufficient.** Adding only a label to L, with contact, Γ and habitat held fixed, produces a maintained, replicated reduction in R_s across two independent frames with different evidence pools. This kills F3's contact condition and forces a rewrite in which the label is an atom.
3. **Closure moves from inside.** A composition strictly inside closure(Γ) adds a new atom, or an import from a frame with identical closure adds one. This kills Fig 6/Fig 8 idempotence and the two-track shape in F5.
4. **The two anchors collapse.** A measurement shows that human-judgment quality and task-ground success on s are the same quantity on this axis, so the report instrument is not saturated. This narrows F4.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
