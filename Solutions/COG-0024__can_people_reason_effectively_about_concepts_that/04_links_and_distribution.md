## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong answer about human cognition, which is high enough to pay for one-to-one contact and independent measures.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the two-track shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) if they ask, offer the formula as a method to reuse, with a fresh refuter required for their own problem.

## 5. The two-stage offer (three stages with the method)

1. **Stage 1** validates the output against a ground the recipient chooses, without showing the framework. Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in.** If the recipient is curious, offer the sources. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded, and any later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not").
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). Each user states their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and labels the mode of what they get.
