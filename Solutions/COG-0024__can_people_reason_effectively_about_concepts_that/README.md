## Residual in effect terms

R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′| for a concept-slot s that the ambient language L does not routinely name. The question asks whether R_s can be driven down by acting when L has no routine label for s, or whether a linguistic label is a precondition for reducing R_s. Option A (linguistic determinism) says no label means no objective and no planning, so the slot cannot be reasoned about. Option B says a label is one possible occupant of a slot in Γ, not the slot itself, so reasoning can proceed through perceptual, motor, and social contact. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a **per-slot condition** stating which closures and which contacts make R_s fall without a name, and which make it fail. That is a shape, not a list of concepts.

## What is deliberately not here

No personal emails of named individuals. No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. No bulk-send plan. No claim that this simulated derivation settles the empirical question.

The figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list of believers is one evidence pool spelled many ways.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the framework before the verdict spends the only independence an outside frame contributes.
4. Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." Hence the refuter is in `01` and in the first contact, not in a footnote.
