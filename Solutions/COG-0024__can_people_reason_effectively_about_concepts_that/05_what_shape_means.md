# What "shape" means for COG-0024

**Mode: `general`.** This is a canonical reading of a term, not a measurement. All eight essays below were fetched and read on 2026-11-22. They are linked in `04_links_and_distribution.md`, recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`, and used only for the canonical reading: they share an author with the figures and carry no independent corroboration weight.

## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the occupant

The canonical usage rule (local copy `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) says the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (Oct 02 2026): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And, closer to this question than anything else in the corpus: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."

For COG-0024, a **shape** is therefore the per-slot condition an answer must satisfy: the slot is registered independently of vocabulary; capability tracks contact and reach rather than label possession; there are two opposed tracks and a union of compounds. A **name** would be a list of the concepts for which this is true. The framework returns the first, not the second.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, not translation

*The Library of Babel* gives the method: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".

Applied here: the residual cannot be stated as "people can/cannot think without words", because both sides of that sentence borrow the experimenter's words. It is stated as an effect: does R_s fall under action when L is silent? That description can be checked by anyone with a slot-and-ground instrument, whatever language they speak.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (Sep 16 2026) adds the stopping rule: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." The shape stops when nothing available registers a difference. It does not become the answer.

## 3. What licenses filling a slot: two doors, both outside the shape

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (Sep 06 2026): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." So the two doors are:

1. the ground settles a minted term (Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground.");
2. a provenanced import from a differing closure, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).

For a concept L does not name, door 1 is an instrument that registers the slot, and door 2 is contact with a person, language, practice or sensorium whose closure already predicts the slot's shape. Neither door is a dictionary.

## 4. Why verbal report is a metamerism error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Aug 28 2026): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The green-light example: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source".

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point for artefacts: a single video frame and a photograph "are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants"; "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it".

A failure to name is a reading on one instrument. It cannot comment on the source (the person's reasoning). Several different sources can produce the same silent reading: no term held, a term held but not accessible verbally, a term held but the task gives no route to express it, or an instrument saturated on the naming axis. Concluding "no reasoning" from "no name" is exactly the projection-for-source substitution these essays refuse. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument.

## 5. The essay that is almost a direct answer to COG-0024

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context, Subtext, & Pretext Matter* (Nov 10 2026) opens: "Tacit knowledge can be defined as 'knowledge that is difficult to extract or articulate': the things you know but can't easily put into words." Its apprentice learns "not only through language but also by observation, imitation, and practice". And: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

Polanyi is quoted there as "we can know more than we can tell". Collins's collective kind is "knowledge we draw that is the property of society, such as the rules for language" and is acquired "only by living among them". So the framework's own corpus already contains the distinction this solution derives from the figures: knowing a concept and having a public word for it are different quantities, and the bridge between them is contact, not translation.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (Oct 22 2026) adds the transmission rule, quoting Fig 4: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What a community passes on is a method for rebuilding the concept, not the concept as a recording. A word is a head start, not a transported competence.

## 6. Prestige and simulation, the two standing limits

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Nov 19 2026): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

This solution is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is a simulacrum in that essay's sense. It cannot settle COG-0024. A famous name agreeing with it would fund it a hearing and would settle nothing. *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* (Aug 19 2026) is blunter: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." Its own caveat says outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula", which is why `01` binds the sought-for shape before the run and writes a refuter before the conclusion.

## 7. Correction to my own §3

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming anything the framework has not produced. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the question is about a concept that may already be reachable in the person's closure under a different modality. Naming such a concept as a binding from the person's frame is not inventing a seventh term; it is recording provenance.

What remains forbidden is the stronger move: taking the community's silence in L as proof that the slot is empty, or naming an occupant from a projection without an instrument that discriminates sources. Both claims in `01` F5 and F7 survive, but the reason is now precise: the limit is on inventing primitives and on naming sources from lossy readings, not on using a word for a term already held. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. Eight essays, one convergent discipline

Across all eight, the recurring move is the same: state the residual in effect terms, bootstrap vocabulary by proximity, keep the lineage, refuse a source named from a projection, and stop when the instrument stops. For COG-0024 that converges on a single instruction: do not ask whether the language has a word. Ask what the person's ground returns on the slot, and what would have to be imported if it does not.
