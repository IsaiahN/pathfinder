## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Reasoning about a concept is assessed per concept-slot, not by counting the words a language supplies for its slots. A public name is a production bond onto a term already held in the person's library; where the term is not held, the word is a bond to a missing operand and carries nothing. What drives the per-slot residual down is contact with a ground and reachability in the person's closure, not label possession. Two opposed tracks follow: search the current reach first, and only if nothing closes the residual import from a frame whose closure differs. A verbal report is an instrument on the naming axis, so a silent report is not by itself a failed reasoning trial. Strong linguistic determinism is refused as a self-certifying sealed room. Which specific unnamed concepts are tractable is a declared `gap`, owed to an instrument rather than another opinion.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1 and Figure 11

- **Statement:** reasoning must be scored per concept-slot; a missing linguistic label is not evidence of a missing slot, and contact, not label possession, is what changes capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** does the claim depend on a global average over named versus unnamed concepts, or on a word count? If so, it is measuring the wrong quantity under Fig 1 :19. Does the proposed mechanism change contact, or only the label?
- **Refuter:** a slot with no term in any modality, with perception, objective and planning links independently intact, where the residual does not trend down across repeated acts; or a maintained improvement from adding only the word while contact is held fixed, replicated across two frames with different evidence pools.

### C2 — Two-track claim; checkable by reading Figure 6 and Figure 8

- **Statement:** there are two cases, and the instruments are opposed. If the unnamed concept is composable from atoms already held, search closes the residual without a new word and without moving the closure wall. If it is not composable, the only remedy is a provenanced import from a differing closure. Search first; never read the two tracks against one another.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :31 "Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."
- **What to check:** is the proposed explanation an internal search explanation or an import explanation? Can a single study test both at once? If it conflates them, it cannot distinguish "the concept was there and unfound" from "the concept needed a new axis".
- **Refuter:** a composition strictly inside closure(Γ) that adds a new atom, or an import from a frame with identical closure that adds one. Either breaks idempotence and the two-track shape.

### C3 — Verification claim; checkable by reading Figure 2 and Figure 10

- **Statement:** the verbal report is an instrument on the naming axis, not the ground for "reasoned effectively". Human judgment anchors "good answer for a person"; the task outcome anchors "effective reasoning". These are different axes and must not be merged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
- **What to check:** does the study's measure of "reasoning" reduce to a verbal answer? If so, what independent registration shows that the slot was tracked when the word was absent? Is the person asked to endorse the framework before giving the verdict? If so, their later agreement is not evidence.
- **Refuter:** an instrument that registers naming access and reasoning success on the same slot in the same person and shows the report is not saturated on the naming axis, or a measurement showing the two anchors are the same quantity on this axis.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that words are useless, or that teaching a word never changes anything. The claim is that a word is a production bond onto a held term, so its effect depends on the term being there.
- Not that any named language or culture traps its speakers. Determinism is refused structurally, not replaced with a softer version.
- Not that the six-term substrate is a word list, or that every person holds the same atoms.
- Not a list of concepts that people can reason about without words. That is a `gap`. Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
- Not `grounded`. No human participant was run in this derivation.
- Not a claim that the eight essays independently corroborate the verdict. They share an author with the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A name-independent registration of the concept-slot, with per-slot residual trending under action (tests C1).
2. A name-only intervention with contact held fixed, replicated across independent frames with different measures (tests C1 and the isomer claim in `06`).
3. A test that separates SEARCHED from OUTWARD, with source gate and shadow test for any import (tests C2).
4. A report-versus-ground round trip with the gap measured, not assumed zero (tests C3).
5. Any of the four falsifiers in `01` §5.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

3. Name one instrument that could register a concept-slot without using the subject's vocabulary or your own. If none exists, say so: that is the `gap`, recorded as a verdict.
