## QUESTION

Can people reason effectively about concepts that their language does not routinely name? Restated as an effect: can R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′| be driven down on a concept-slot s for which L has no routine label? Option A says no: without a word, link 2 of the dependency chain has no term, so no objective or plan can be formed. Option B says yes in a bounded way: a word is one public convention on Γ, and contact plus closure reach do the work. What each leads to: A makes L a sealed room and refuses an import that the figures require; B yields a checkable per-slot shape, a pre-registered refuter, and an honest `gap` for the specific enumeration.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, then partition or union and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three-place: agent library Γ, concept-slot s, ground return o'. No useful symmetry: the relevant bonds are ordered (sequence, production) and thresholded (comparison), not symmetric. Scale: one person's loop, a community's transmitted vocabulary, and the lossy membrane between. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated across people or concepts (Fig 1 :19).
- **D1 Perceptual and motor reasoning.** Actors: perceptual and motor systems, the object or event in s. Currency: R reduced through acting. Anchor: the task ground; for "good answer for a person", human judgment on a separate axis. Admitted.
- **D2 Linguistic and cultural composition.** Actors: speakers, words, grammatical bonds. Currency: what composes in closure(Γ); cost(φ) + left(R,φ). Anchor: the ground for truth, the convention for meaning only. Admitted.
- **D3 Report and verification.** Actors: experimenter, task, verbal report, instrument. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat of acquisition.** Actors: caregivers, peers, practice, other languages. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground. Admitted.
- **X1 Language as sealed room (strong determinism).** Actors: the language alone. Currency: words. Anchor: none nameable, because the claim certifies its own limit. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D2/D3 as a refusable term.

**Union, not partition.** D1+D4 form a contact compound; D2+D3 form a composition-and-report compound. They overlap on the same slot, so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54). **Order by dependency:** perception → vocabulary of affordances → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20), with refusal before mint (Formula step 2) and SEARCHED before OUTWARD (Formula step 7).

**Strain test.** Two readings strained and were re-censed: "Fig 3's vocabulary link means words" (rejected; the figure governs affordance terms in any modality) and "Fig 13's six terms are a language's word list" (rejected; the figure governs substrate kinds). One live strain remains, named: the human-judgment anchor and the task-ground anchor are different axes and must not be merged.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict, kept per domain and never averaged: reasoning about a concept is possible without a routine linguistic name whenever the slot is reachable in the person's closure or a differing contact supplies the missing axis. A name is a production bond onto a held term, not the term and not the measure. Two opposed tracks, SEARCHED then OUTWARD, with import only against a genuine closure difference. A verbal report is an instrument on the naming axis, so silence there is not failure there. Strong determinism is refused as a sealed room. The specific enumeration of unnamed concepts is a `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. No world source overruled the figures; the one internal conflict was returned to the census.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside frame's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out (Formula step 3). The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the four pre-registered falsifiers in `01` §5: (1) a slot with no term in any modality whose residual does not trend down with links 1/3/4 intact; (2) a name-only intervention with contact fixed that produces a maintained, replicated improvement; (3) internal composition adding an atom or import from an identical closure adding one; (4) a demonstration that the two anchors are one quantity on this axis. A genuinely independent frame with a different evidence pool reaching the opposite shape would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
