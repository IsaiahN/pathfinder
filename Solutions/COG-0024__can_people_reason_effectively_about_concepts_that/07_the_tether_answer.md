# The Tether's answer: reasoning about concepts a language does not name

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive verdict, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′| on a concept-slot s for which the ambient language L has no routine label. Option A: a label is a precondition, so the slot cannot be reasoned about. Option B: a label is one public convention on Γ, so the slot can be reasoned about through contact and composition. This is a union of two compounds, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, perceptual and motor reasoning: yes, and the name is irrelevant to the loop.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The person's library is not the language. Percepts, motor schemas and correction histories occupy the slot; R_s falls through acting. Status **triangulated** (Fig 1 per-slot residual and Fig 11 contact, low overlap). Mode `specified`. Refuter: a slot with no term in any modality, links 1, 3 and 4 intact, where R_s does not trend down.

**D2, compositional reasoning and the linguistic layer: two tracks, one order.** Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." If the unnamed concept is reachable from held atoms, it is in closure and search finds it; a word may compress it (Fig 5 :26 "and then: does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?") but adds no atom. If it is out of closure, the remedy is an import from a differing closure (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). Status **triangulated** (Fig 6 and Fig 8). Mode `general` for the two tracks, `specified` for languages. Refuter: closure expanding by internal composition, or an atom imported from an identical closure.

**D3, report and verification: a verbal answer is one axis, not the verdict.** Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Whether an answer is good for a person is anchored in human judgment; whether a person reasoned effectively is anchored in the task ground. The verbal report is an instrument on the naming axis, and a silent report is not a failed reasoning trial. Status **single-source** for the anchor distinction (Fig 2, corroborated structurally by Fig 10 channel failure). Mode `specified`. Refuter: an instrument that shows report and reasoning agree on the same slot while the report is demonstrated to be naming-sensitive.

**D4, habitat of acquisition: contact installs terms without words.** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Observation, imitation, correction and practice can occupy the slot. The essays' apprentice and Polanyi's "we can know more than we can tell" are corroboration, not the derivation. Status **triangulated** (Fig 11 and Fig 4 :20 "it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"). Mode `specified`.

**X1, strong determinism: refused, not absorbed as a weaker claim.** Fig 8 :37 "A frame cannot certify its own limit. Chaitin: beyond a fixed constant, no system proves that any object exceeds its own complexity." Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." A language with no outside would be the terminal room the figures forbid, and a convention promoted to decide the outcome has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"). Status **triangulated**. Mode `general`.

**The specific enumeration: `gap`.** Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The figures give the shape of the condition, not which concepts, languages or people satisfy it. Missing thing, in effect terms: an instrument that registers a concept-slot independently of verbal report and independently of the experimenter's language. Status **single-source** (Fig 13 plus Formula "Direction", one pool). Mode `general`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that words are useless. A name is a cheap production bond onto a held term and can make a concept shareable; it just is not the precondition or the measure.
- Not that any particular language traps its speakers. That claim has no anchor here and is refused as a sealed room.
- Not that the six-term substrate is a word list, or that every person holds the same atoms.
- Not a list of unnamed concepts that people can reason about. That is a `gap`, not hidden knowledge.
- Not `grounded`. No participant, task or measurement was run.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not more agreement

1. Register a concept-slot without using the subject's or the experimenter's vocabulary, and check that R_s falls under action while links 1, 3 and 4 test intact.
2. Add only a public name with contact and Γ held fixed, using two independent groups and two different measures. A maintained effect would force the name from a cheap bond to an atom and rewrite F3.
3. Show that the named and unnamed modes are isomers over the same atom set, or show that adding the name adds an atom, which kills the isomer claim in `06`.
4. Measure the round trip between a practical, nonlinguistic task and a verbal report on the same slot. A nonzero gap is expected under Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
5. Run the pre-registered refuter in `01` §5. A failure is more informative than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

Reached for the shape, unreached for the list. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost of a wrong answer about human cognition is high, so the independence is owed before the verdict is trusted. Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". The world sources read here are split across fields but share a host and a broad literature, so they corroborate the mechanism, not the verdict. The figures decide; the instrument decides the rest. This derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that language traps thought, and it does not say that words are irrelevant. It says that reasoning is measured per concept-slot, not by counting the words a language has for its slots. A public name is a production bond onto a term the person already holds; when the term is not held, the word is a bond to nothing. What actually drives the error down is contact with a ground and whether the concept is reachable from atoms already in the person's library. That gives two opposed tracks: search the current reach first, and only if nothing closes the gap import from a frame whose closure differs. A verbal report is an instrument on the naming axis, so silence on that instrument is not evidence of a failure to reason. Strong linguistic determinism is refused because it would make a language a sealed room, and the framework's rule is that no frame is a sealed room. What the figures return is the shape of the condition, not a list of concepts; the list is owed to an instrument, and until one exists the honest status is `gap`.
