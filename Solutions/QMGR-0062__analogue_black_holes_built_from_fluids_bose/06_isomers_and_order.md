### Q1 - Are the realisations isomers? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST PASS IN `01`

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

The isomer line therefore **strains** when applied to them. Per the canonical rule, a straining line governs another quantity, so re-census.

### Q2 - Are they a functional group?

### Q3 - What is the order of the two compounds?

## CONCLUSION

The realisations are not isomers under Fig 12 :117, because they do not share atoms. They share a functional group (Fig 12 :123), and if their independence is real they **converge** on the same shape (Fig 8 :50, Fig 13 :121), which is corroboration at maximum independence for the shape only. The order is harness first, gated import second, and no gravitational verdict from arrangement alone. Second-order composition is legal inside the harness frame; extending it to D3 needs an instrument that is not held.
