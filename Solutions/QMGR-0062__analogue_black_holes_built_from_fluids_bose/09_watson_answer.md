## QUESTION

Analogue black holes built from fluids, Bose-Einstein condensates, and other laboratory systems. Restated as an effect: given a bench system whose perturbations see an effective horizon and whose measured correlation spectrum matches Hawking's thermal prediction, what does that match license about an astrophysical black hole, and what remains owed? R is indexed per realisation and per quantity, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** the analogue question is a union. R_lab is settled in its own ground for the wiring and for temperature-consistent spectra in several media; R_grav is a `gap` from the bench. An analogue is a substituted habitat that proves wiring, not gravitational capability. Independent realisations corroborate the shape of the mechanism at maximum independence. The transfer to real black holes is a gated import, with provenance and a shadow test, carrying zero verdict weight for D3 until a source-discriminating instrument or external validation exists. Thermality cannot name its source. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. The world conflict about confirmation was returned to the census: the figures condition the transfer on exactly the independence and provenance that the philosophers dispute, so the conflict refines the verdict rather than flipping it.

- **cost if wrong:** over-claiming tells people the information paradox is experimentally settled and spends the independence of potential checkers by asking for agreement before a verdict (Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."). Under-claiming discards a genuine cross-frame check of the Hawking mechanism's universality, and would leave the trans-Planckian problem untested from the only direction currently available. Both costs are real; the over-claim is the larger and less reversible one in outreach terms.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."; Fig 6 :43: "A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted."). The shape, the gates and the six refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom.").
- **reversed by:** (1) a bench measurement that directly settles a gravitational quantity; (2) a demonstration that thermality is medium-specific and the shared-structure provenance is false; (3) a horizon realisation failing mode conversion under a correct effective description; (4) collapse of independence to one evidence pool; (5) external validation that upgrades an analogue to a conventional experiment for a stated quantity, reversing the gap for that quantity; (6) derivation of a seventh substrate term or of one of the six from the other five. A world source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that cross-realisation convergence is an artefact of shared design would also reverse the shape claim. Agreement and prestige are not such instruments.
