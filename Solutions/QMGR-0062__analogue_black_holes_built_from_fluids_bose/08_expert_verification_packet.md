## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A question about analogue black holes is a union of two questions with different anchors. The bench question, does a prepared medium with an effective horizon produce the predicted mode conversion and thermal correlation, has a ground in the instrument and has been substantially settled in published realisations. The gravitational question, does the same mechanism operate in an astrophysical black hole and settle unitarity, has a named anchor that is not reachable from the bench. An analogue is a substituted habitat: it tests the wiring, not the capability of the gravitational system. Independent realisations can corroborate the shape of the mechanism, but the transfer to real black holes is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, carrying zero verdict weight until a source-discriminating instrument or an externally validated equivalence exists. The framework offers the shape, the gates and pre-registered refuters, not a gravitational verdict.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and physics-facing; checkable by reading Figure 11 and the experimental record

- **Statement:** an analogue experiments on a substituted habitat. It establishes the horizon-and-field mechanism within the bench ground, and does not by itself establish the same mechanism in an astrophysical black hole.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Also Fig 11 :66 - *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
- **What to check:** is the physics literature's own scope language consistent with this? The standard review says *"Of course analogy is not identity"*; the 2019 Nature letter reports a spectrum *"agrees well with a thermal spectrum"* in the analogue and a temperature set by the analogue surface gravity. Both stop at the analogue.
- **Refuter:** a laboratory measurement that directly determines a gravitational quantity, for example an astronomical Hawking temperature, would break this sub-claim.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 8 and Figure 6

- **Statement:** different media can corroborate the shape only at maximum independence, and the transfer to the gravitational frame is an import that needs provenance and a shadow test. Publication volume is a budget filter, not a witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50 - *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :51 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.
- **What to check:** do the cited realisations (water waves, BEC, optical) actually draw on independent evidence and independent theory groups, or on one shared design and reduction? Is there a stated shadow test the import explains, for example the trans-Planckian sensitivity?
- **Refuter:** collapse of independence to one evidence pool; or a demonstration that the thermal signal is medium-specific and the shared-structure provenance is false; or a genuine new closure that adds an atom and moves the wall, recorded with gates.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** the two compounds are ordered harness first, import second, and no gravitational verdict follows from arrangement alone. The correct structural relation among realisations is convergence, not isomerism.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 13 :121 - *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. - **Refuter:** a composition inside the harness closure that adds an atom to the gravitational frame; or evidence that the realisations share a functional group but not convergence; or a derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any tabletop system has detected Hawking radiation from a real black hole, or measured an astrophysical Hawking temperature.
- Not that the information paradox or unitarity is settled in the lab.
- Not that thermality in one medium entails thermality in gravity. Isoard and Pavloff report *"no such general principle is expected to hold for analogue systems"*.
- Not that the analogue is the same type of system as its target. The review's wording is *"analogy is not identity"*.
- Not that publication count, reputation or group size supplies a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not `grounded` for the gravitational target. This packet cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Source Test:** an instrument that discriminates horizon-universal from medium-specific contributions to the correlation spectrum.
2. **External Validation Test:** independent validation of the source-target equivalence for a stated quantity, in the sense of Evans and Thebault's *"inductive triangulation"* and *"one or more distinct and independent modes of inductive reasoning"*.
3. **Convergence Test:** a documented independence check across realisations with genuinely different microphysics.
4. **Falsifiers:** the six pre-registered items in `01` section 4, including the reverse case where external validation upgrades an analogue to a conventional experiment for one quantity.
