## 3. The shape that survives

1. **The two questions are a union and must be unbundled** (Fig 9 :55-57). This is the decisive structural result.
2. **An analogue tests wiring, not capability** (Fig 11 :66, :70). It is a substituted habitat.
3. **Independent realisations corroborate a shape, not a name** (Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*; Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*). The shape is: horizon plus field yields robust mode conversion with temperature set by surface gravity.
4. **The transfer to D3 is an import with gates** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). Without provenance and a shadow test it carries zero verdict weight.
5. **The thermal reading cannot name its source** (Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*). An instrument that discriminates horizon-universal from medium-specific contributions is owed.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a tabletop system has detected Hawking radiation from an astrophysical black hole.
- Not that the information paradox is resolved, or that unitarity is settled, in a BEC or water channel.
- Not that thermality in a medium entails thermality in gravity; the field itself reports medium-specific departures.
- Not that the analogue is the same type of system as the target. The standard review's own words: *"analogy is not identity"*.
- Not that many papers or famous groups corroborate the result. Independence is the parameter.
- Not `grounded` anywhere in D3.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Source Test:** which part of the measured correlation spectrum is fixed by the horizon condition alone, and which by the medium's dispersion and preparation? An instrument that separates these would name the source.
- **External Validation Test:** can the source-target equivalence be validated independently for a stated quantity (Evans and Thebault's inductive triangulation), so that the bench becomes an anchor for that quantity?
- **Convergence Test:** do realisations with genuinely different microphysics and independent origin reach the same shape, under an explicit independence check (Fig 2 :31)?
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: a direct bench measurement of a gravitational quantity; a medium-specific explanation of thermality with false provenance; a horizon realisation failing mode conversion; collapsed independence; externally validated equivalence (which would reverse the `gap` for that quantity).
