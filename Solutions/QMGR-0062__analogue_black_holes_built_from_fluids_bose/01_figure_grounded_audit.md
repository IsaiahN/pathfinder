## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The source question is a topic label, not a yes/no proposition. Read as an effect, it becomes:

> Given a tabletop system prepared so that a field's modes encounter an effective horizon (fluid flow, BEC, optical or polariton medium), and given that the measured correlation spectrum matches Hawking's thermal prediction, what does that match license about real gravitational black holes, and what remains owed?

Write `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot (Symbols table :38, Fig 1 :19). The slots are not "the analogue gravity result"; they are individual realisations and individual quantities. For each realisation, `Γ` is the effective-field description, `b` the prepared state, `a` the drive and measurement, `o'` the observed spectrum or correlation function.

Two residuals are being conflated by the topic label:

- **R_lab.** Does this prepared medium produce the predicted horizon mode conversion and thermal correlation? This is closeable in the bench ground (Fig 2 :29: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*).
- **R_grav.** Does the same mechanism operate in an astrophysical black hole, and does the result settle unitarity or the information paradox? The anchor for R_grav is named but is not reachable from the bench. No figure line lets a lab instrument settle it (Fig 2 :37-38: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*).

Because the two residuals have different anchors, they are a **union**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :55-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* and *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :60). This is the first derivation, and it governs everything below.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - An analogue is a substituted habitat: it proves wiring, not capability (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

The fluid or BEC replaces the gravitational habitat with one in which the same mode-conversion wiring can be driven and read out. This is a legitimate and powerful test of the wiring: does a horizon plus a quantum field with the relevant dispersion produce paired, entangled, approximately thermal quanta? It is not a test of the capability of an astrophysical black hole to do it. The two panels come from different figures with low overlap (habitat substitution vs closure/import), so this is **triangulated**. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter.** A bench measurement that directly determines a quantity of D3 (for example an astronomical Hawking temperature) would break the substituted-habitat reading.

### F2 - The bench ground settles only the lab question (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :37-38: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Fig 11 :73: *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*

The prepared flow is a genuine anchor for R_lab. Relative to R_grav it is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure: the trans-Planckian regime, quantum-gravity microphysics, and backreaction are exactly such axes. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* **Triangulated** (Fig 2 with Fig 11). Mode `general` for the anchor/instrument distinction, `specified` for the analogue case.

**Refuter.** Externally validate the source-target equivalence (Evans and Thebault's term) for the relevant quantity: then the instrument has become an anchor for that quantity and this finding is reversed for it.

### F4 - Import needs a source gate and a shadow test (triangulated)

Fig 6 :12: *"source gate: provenance"*. Fig 6 :13: *"shadow test: explains a gap"*. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.

"The universal Hawking mechanism" crosses from the analogue frame into the gravitational frame. It must carry provenance (which structural features are actually shared, which are medium-specific) and a shadow test (does it explain a gap that the gravitational frame already registers, for example the trans-Planckian sensitivity?). Without both, it carries zero verdict weight for D3. Barcelo, Liberati and Visser state the same gate in physics language: *"Of course analogy is not identity, and we are in no way claiming that the analogue models we consider are completely equivalent to general relativity - merely that the analogue model (in order to be interesting) should capture and accurately reflect a sufficient number of important features of general relativity"*. The philosophy-of-science literature agrees the gate is the crux: Crowther, Linnemann and Wuthrich argue the target-modelling assumption is smuggled in and the argument *"beg the question"*. **Triangulated** (Fig 6 with Fig 8).

**Refuter.** A demonstration that the shared structure is convergent derivation rather than adopted import, with the record showing independent arrival (Fig 8 :50), would strengthen the transfer; a demonstration that the thermal signal is medium-specific and the provenance claim is false would break it.

### F5 - Convergence across media is the witness; "isomer" is a strained reading (triangulated, with a re-census)

Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*

Fluid, BEC and optical realisations are different closures with different microphysics. If they independently reach the same mode-conversion shape, that is corroboration at maximum independence **for the shape**. First attempt: call them **isomers** (Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."*). Strain test fails: the media do not share the same atoms, so the isomer line governs a different quantity. Re-census to convergence (Fig 8 :50, Fig 13 :121), which fits without strain. The strain is kept visible here rather than erased. **Triangulated** after re-census.

**Refuter.** Two "independent" realisations drawing on the same design, theory group or data reduction are one witness wearing two names (Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*); if independence is low, the corroboration collapses to one source.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted or reversed if any of the following holds:

1. A laboratory measurement directly settles a quantity of D3, for example an astronomical Hawking temperature or a unitarity outcome. (Breaks F1, F2, F3.)
2. The thermal signal is shown to depend on a microphysical feature with no gravitational counterpart, and the claimed shared-structure provenance is false. (Breaks F4, F5.)
3. Even within the lab, a realisation with a bona fide horizon fails to produce the predicted mode conversion after the effective description is applied correctly. (Breaks F1's wiring claim.)
4. Two supposed independent witnesses draw on the same evidence pool, leaving one witness for the shape. (Breaks F5.)
5. An externally validated source-target equivalence removes the inaccessibility for a stated quantity, upgrading that analogue from analogue experiment to conventional experiment (Evans and Thebault). (Reverses the `gap` for that quantity only.)
6. A seventh substrate term is derived or a sixth is derived from the other five. (Breaks the framework itself; Seat Map :186.)

Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Unbundle R_lab from R_grav before any claim is written (Fig 9 :55-57). Report the former as measured in its own domain, the latter as a `specified` import.

5. Lead with the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
