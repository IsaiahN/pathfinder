## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says it can report an answer as "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," but "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its content. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `three slots, separately anchored, never averaged` is a shape.
- `0.4 percent per year` is a name, and it belongs to one slot only (erosion-driven global yield loss), not to "soil degradation" in general.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (OPENED): "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The method is proximity: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then recurse on the new terms that correlate with it.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (OPENED): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason `02_outreach_email.md` asks which slot an instrument reads instead of asserting a number.
- *Library of Babel* (OPENED) on the termination of search: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question the rate is licensed only by soil-science measurement. The four literature rates in `01` §4 F7 are named values for particular slots and instruments; they arrive as imports with provenance (author, venue, year, quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`), not as figure derivations.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (OPENED) sharpens the instrument point: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)," and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The essay's own resolution was instrumental, not argumentative: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Applied here: the disagreement among the four rate numbers calls for an instrument that distinguishes the slots, not a verdict about which paper is right.

## 4. The bundle question is a shape question, not a measurement question

*Library of Babel* (OPENED) names the exact inversion this question invites: "If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed. Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

That is Fig 9 :54 in prose: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The three outputs of EAR-0052 (productivity, water retention, resilience) are such a bundle. The shape claim is that they must be unbundled before any rate is reported.

## 5. Q — Does "shape, not a name" forbid reporting any rate from the figures?

**Answered: yes from the figures, no as a general ban on rates.** The phrase governs a primitive not held in the library; a rate is not a primitive, it is a measurement. What the figures forbid is *deriving the numeric rate from the figure frame*, which holds no soil physics. The correct form is: the figures give the shape (three slots, union, ground-anchored, cascade, permanence), and the soil-science ground gives the numbers, each with its own anchor and mode. Note both claims so neither is quietly lost:

- Claim A (kept): the figures return a shape, not a rate. A "speed of soil degradation" derived from the figures is forbidden.
- Claim B (kept): naming an atom already held is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. Reporting a measured rate for a specific slot is allowed, provided it is provenanced as an import and not presented as a figure output.

**Correction to my own §1 (kept visible).** I first wrote "a rate is a name, full stop." That overstates the rule. A rate is a ground measurement, which is a different category from naming a missing primitive. I narrowed it above. The narrowed form is what `01`, `07` and `09` use.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous soil scientists who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim against the ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (OPENED): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
