## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The figures README gives the canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 gives the short form: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 adds that a system with a closure can answer decidability before it searches and *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For EAR-0088 the shape is not a switching date or a probability. It is the four-condition separator stated in `README.md`: substitution plus reproduction of goal-required conditions, no defeating cascade from inherited actors, no shifted risk onto the receiving area, and a per-slot outcome comparison against a non-updating anchor. That is a constraint on any acceptable answer. It is not an answer.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

*Library of Babel* prescribes proximity and recursion: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, notice new terms that overlap or correlate, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here, the starting vocabulary was hazard, exposure, defence, rebuild, buyout, relocation. The terms that narrowed it were habitat, substitution, reproduction, cascade and valence, read off Fig 11. That is why the final shape is about habitat operations rather than about comparing two hazard maps.

*Library of Babel* also names the failure mode this question is prone to: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union finding F6. A single "when" is the conflation; four overlapping conditions are the unbundled result.

Fig 9 :30-31 requires describing effects rather than causes: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**. Saying "move is safer" borrows the word safe from a frame. Saying "the destination reproduced the household's income, school access and kin contact, and no inherited actor defeated them, measured per slot after arrival" is an effect description that anyone can check.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the field version: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email in `02` leads with observable conditions and not with the framework's vocabulary.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what is refused here

Two things license a name in the framework: the ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Neither has happened for a universal switching number in this question.

There is a correction to keep visible. The "shape, not a name" rule governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming an atom already held, such as "habitat" or "cascade", is a binding from another frame onto an existing atom, which is permitted if its provenance is recorded. It is not the invention of a seventh term and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). This corrects an overstatement I would otherwise make: it is not forbidden to say "substitution"; it is forbidden to claim the framework returns a numeric threshold it does not hold.

What is refused here: a universal probability, cost ratio, return period or date at which moving becomes safer. No figure line yields one, and none of the world sources supplies a figure-derived name for it. The gap is recorded as `unreachable at this budget`, which is a verdict, not silence (Fig 8 :39).

## 4. Metamerism: one "safe" reading, several possible sources

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here: a county-level drop in expected flood loss after a buyout programme is a reading. Its source might be genuine risk reduction, or displacement of exposure to a poorer neighbourhood, or a change in the hazard map, or an interval of quiet years. The reading cannot comment on the source. Mach et al. 2019 is the exact case: buyouts were more likely in richer counties while the bought-out properties sat in more vulnerable neighbourhoods inside them, with documented relocations to "areas with equal flood risk and greater social vulnerability". One aggregate reading, several incompatible sources. What is owed is a discriminator, per slot, not a nicer name for the reading.

The same essay's point about the instrument applies to the two silent failures in F2. Until an instrument registers a missing condition before the goal fails, the planner's suitability model is the green-reading instrument and the source stays unknown.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question, a well-known advocate for managed retreat, or a well-known opponent, can get a study funded. Neither can settle whether a particular move was safer. The settlement is per-slot outcomes against an anchor that neither advocate can move. The same essay states the self-validation limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." That is why this derivation is labelled a simulation and why its second seat in `11_audit.md` disclaims corroboration.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim, they took no outcome measurements, and they cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Consequences for the two-stage offer

1. **Lead with the shape and the refuter.** A checker's first job is to say what would count as a failure. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, because a reader who has absorbed the framework has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).
2. **Describe effects, not causes.** Ask what one would observe if the four-condition claim were true, in the researcher's own vocabulary. This is *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*'s checkable description and Fig 9 :30-31's effect language.
3. **Do not ask for agreement.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list of people chosen because they favour relocation would manufacture the exact correlation that disqualifies their agreement as a verdict.
4. **Offer the method as a head start, never a recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each researcher writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
5. **Keep the gap visible.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The absence of a universal switching number is a finding about what the figures can compose, not an apology.
