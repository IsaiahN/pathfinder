## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when moving away from a high-risk location (M) becomes safer than continued protection and reconstruction in place (P). Option A: assert a universal switching threshold on a hazard, cost or probability variable. Option B: return the shape and conditions that any correct site-specific answer must satisfy, with a declared gap for the threshold. What each leads to: A gives a number the figures do not compose and invites a decision whose anchor moves with the forecast; B gives a per-slot separator, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would settle a particular site.

The ordinary term was bound before the run (README): M is safer than P only if, per slot and against a non-updating anchor, the substituted habitat reproduces what the goal required, inherits no actors whose cascade defeats the goal, shifts no risk to the receiving community, and holds a lower residual than P can without perpetual work. That term is refusable by the same test as anything else.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap, per household and subgroup and outcome slot, between what P or M predicts and what actually happens over a horizon long enough for second-order effects to register. Arity: many slots crossed with many outcome kinds, so no scalar. Symmetry: ordered comparison, since P and M are not interchangeable and "safer than" is ⋛, not a conjunction. Scale: first order is direct hazard and direct outcome; second order is the cascade (Fig 11 :52, :53). Fig 9 :4-5 and :57 require a union reading, not one hard question.
- **D1, origin habitat under protection and reconstruction.** Actors: hazard, defence, infrastructure, local institutions, households, insurers, ecosystems. Currency: realised outcomes and repeated work. Anchor: measured origin outcomes, which do not update on a model revision. Nameable anchor, so D1 is a domain.
- **D2, destination habitat after the move.** Actors: host community, tenure, labour market, services, governance, destination ecology and hazard, the relocated households. Currency: realised outcomes after arrival. Anchor: post-move outcomes, unreadable before the move because the two failure modes are silent. Nameable anchor in principle, so D2 is a domain.
- **D3, decision and forecast frame.** Actors: planners, models, projection suites, benefit-cost ratios. Currency: coherence and agreement among forecasts. Anchor: none of its own; it must range against the ground (Fig 2 :45). The frame is a domain because its anchor is nameable outside it, but it has no standing to supply the verdict.
- **D4, receiving area and neighbouring communities.** Actors: host residents, their services and infrastructure, employers, new arrivals. Currency: their outcomes. Anchor: their measured outcomes. Nameable anchor, so D4 is a domain. D2 and D4 overlap as a compound with its own loop.
- **Union, not partition.** Four overlapping conditions, each able to bind alone: hazard outruns sustainable protection; goal-required conditions reproducible at destination; inherited actors do not defeat the goal; no shifted risk to D4. One lever clears only the region where one condition holds (Fig 9 :54-58).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe R in effect terms; enumerate habitat and cascade; bind "safer" as an ordinary term; settle P and M per slot; compare; let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42).

Strain test applied: applying Fig 11's "isolation" to human relocation was checked. The law is about habitat substitution, not laboratory practice; it holds for a test harness (Fig 11 :70) and a town with the actor set changed and the operation fixed. Resolved by re-census, recorded in `11_audit.md` as S1.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a universal threshold does not. Both levers are habitat operations. Moving substitutes a habitat and must reproduce goal-required conditions while inheriting actors it did not choose; protection is permanent work that matters only when it changes contact. The comparison is a second-order ⋛ over per-slot outcomes settled against a non-updating anchor, not an aggregate expected-loss scalar. The four conditions form a union, so one lever leaves the intersections standing. Under a generous atom binding, stay and move are isomers; under the strict binding they are a functional group, and the correction is visible in `06`. Costs come in two currencies that do not add. The universal switching number is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** a wrong site decision can expose a household to the hazard it fled, strand a household in a habitat whose required conditions were not reproduced, or harm a receiving community whose outcomes were never in the scorecard. The figure-side cost of getting the *derivation* wrong is small and locatable, because the packet states modes, refuters and provenance before the conclusion. The larger avoidable cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. A site verdict can be superseded when post-move and stay-put outcome instruments report; a failed protection term can be refused without withdrawing the habitat law; the shape claim can be narrowed as each refuter fires. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no number has to be retracted, because none was asserted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. The reversal triggers are: a relocation with no inherited actors and no reproduction requirement on per-slot outcomes; an instrument that registers a silent failure before it acts; a defence that permanently removes hazard contact without renewed work or new cascades; one variable monotonically ordering all cases; a verbatim figure line that returns a universal switching quantity; a standing safe-destination list that keeps its sign across changed residuals; or a scalar calibrated to both cost currencies. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
