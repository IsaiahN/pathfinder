## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are the two options isomers? (CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING)

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Written over the full shared atom set {L, H, S, Y, V, N, G, C, B}, the two options are:

```
P (stay and defend)  = L + H(gradient) + S + Y + V + N + G + C + B,
                       bonded so that S and V are held against H at L
M (move and rebuild) = L' + H'(gradient) + S' + Y' + V' + N' + G' + C' + B',
                       bonded so that the goal atoms are reproduced at L'
```

- Under a **generous binding** (L' is a new value of the same location atom, B' the same neighbour atom at a new site, and so on), the atom sets are the same and the arrangement differs. They are isomers. The bond decides the outcome, which is exactly why two options with identical ingredient lists can have opposite per-slot residuals.
- Under a **strict reading**, M introduces a receiving population whose role has no counterpart in P, and P introduces a defence structure whose role has no counterpart in M. Then they are not isomers but members of a **functional group**: Fig 12 :123, **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Both are "defend the goal against a hazard"; the qualifier says whether the work happens at L or at L'.
- **I narrowed my own first reading.** I initially wrote that the two are straightforwardly isomers. That depended on an unstated binding of L' and B' onto the same atoms. The strict reading is the safer one. Both readings are recorded so the correction is visible rather than erased.
- **Falsifier, written before the conclusion:** if one option demonstrably requires an atom the other cannot bind at any level, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group claim stands. If the two also fail to share a functional role, both claims fail and the union reading of `01` is the only surviving structure.

### Q2 — The comparison is second order, and the order is not optional

- Operators table on ⋛: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one". The question "safer than" is a comparison of two quantities, not a conjunction.
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."** Each option has to be settled against the ground before it can serve as an operand of the comparison.
- Fig 5 :62-63 says a frame searching for an answer of a given shape holds that shape as a preference, not a term, so "nothing here can see it". A comparison made before either path is settled is a preference, not a verdict.
- Order: (1) describe R per slot; (2) settle P's per-slot outcomes against origin outcomes; (3) settle M's per-slot outcomes against destination outcomes; (4) only then compare with ⋛ per slot; (5) let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42).
- **Falsifier:** a comparison that ranges against the ground before either path is settled, with the same verdict after both are settled. If that holds, the order claim is wrong and the comparison can be made early. Triangulated on the order rule (Fig 12 :104 plus Formula step 5); single-source on the preference limit (Fig 5).

### Q3 — Refusal before minting

- `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 routes a residual whose term "persists and does not trend down" to a refusal. Fig 5 :58-60 gives the same test: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at".
- Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate".
- A community that rebuilds the same structure after the same flood is accumulating a term that persists and does not trend down. The owed move is ¬ on "defence at L is sufficient", not another mint of the same defence. Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**
- This is not an argument against protection in general. It is an argument against a specific term whose residual is flat. Where the residual trends down, no refusal is owed.
- **Falsifier:** a series of rebuilds whose per-slot residual trends down monotonically against a non-updating anchor, with no new cascade. Then the refusal is not owed and the accumulation is legitimate.

### Q4 — The round trip is nonzero and the two currencies do not add

- Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
- Send up: a relocation plan abstracted to a suitability score (hazard, housing, jobs, services). Come back down: an actual household in an actual neighbourhood. What the abstraction drops is precisely F2's content, the goal-required pretext and the inherited actor, plus the cascade of F4. The gap R_T is nonzero; it cannot be assumed away by declaring the model validated.
- Fig 12 :125-127 gives the second reading: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Bits price the description of the plan; actions price finding out whether the plan holds. A short, cheap-to-state suitability score can take a whole budget to settle. A long, expensive defence chain can be refuted by one event. Neither figure predicts the other.
- **Falsifier:** a suitability abstraction whose round-trip gap is zero on held-out relocations, measured per slot, and one scalar that calibrates both currencies. Either would close this finding.

### Q5 — Two low-overlap analogues

- **Conservation biology (assisted migration).** The moved species may "perform better (e.g., become invasive) or worse (e.g., fail to establish) in a new habitat than in its current range if the community of competitor, predator, and mutualist species is different there". That is Fig 11 :67 stated in ecology, from a closure with no contact with the Tether.
- **Flood engineering (levees).** "Artificial levees can lead to an elevation of the natural riverbed over time" and "The increased channelization and overall reduction in floodplain area amplifies the flood magnitudes in the highest impact storms, requiring even more engineering to compensate." That is Fig 11 :63 stated in geomorphology.
- These analogues corroborate the *shape* of the findings. They do not supply figure lines and they do not settle any site. They are not additional witnesses for the framework claim, only independent fields whose structures rhyme.

## CONCLUSION

The two options are isomers under a generous binding of atoms across origin and destination, and members of one functional group under the strict reading. The generous reading carries the stronger claim, so it is recorded with its binding condition and its falsifier rather than asserted flatly. The comparison is a second-order ⋛ between two settled molecules, and a refusal (¬) is owed before another defence is minted when a flat residual persists. The round trip from plan to lived habitat is lossy, and R_T must be measured rather than set to zero. Costs come in two currencies that do not add.

This rescues what is checkable in the ordinary framing without pretending a single lever or a single number decides the case: stay and move are different arrangements of the same kind of thing, and the bond, not the ingredient list, decides the outcome.
