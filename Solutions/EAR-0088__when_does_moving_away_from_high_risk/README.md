## The question restated as an effect

A community sits in a location whose hazard contact is rising or persists. Two levers are on the table. Lever P is continued protection plus reconstruction in place. Lever M is moving away, that is relocation, resettlement or managed retreat. The word "safer" names the thing to be settled. It is not a property of a lever. It is the ground: realised outcomes for actual people over time, measured per household and per subgroup, across hazard exposure, housing, livelihood, services, health, and social and cultural continuity.

What an answer would look like has to be written down and bound as an ordinary term before the search, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :65). The ordinary term bound here is:

> A move is safer than staying if and only if, for the same residual described in effect terms and measured per slot, the substituted habitat reproduces what the community's goal required, does not inherit or introduce actors whose cascade defeats that goal, and leaves a lower residual on the same outcome slots than protection and reconstruction can hold, with the comparison settled by outcomes that do not move when a planning frame revises its forecast.

That term is a **shape with conditions**, not a number. No figure line returns a probability, a dollar threshold or a date at which M becomes universally better than P. The absence of such a line is reported as `gap`, and "unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134 style claim; see `01`, F7).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that moving is better.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal relocation threshold presented as a figure-derived result.

Reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not supply an anchor. "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35)
2. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." (Fig 9 :60)
3. Showing the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence. "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42)

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03` are public and opt-in.
