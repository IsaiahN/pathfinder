## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the difference between predicted and realised outcomes for a community choosing between defending in place and moving away. Arity: many slots, one per subgroup crossed with one per outcome kind (safety, housing, livelihood, services, social and cultural continuity, and receiving-area outcomes). Symmetry: the question is ordered, "safer than" is a threshold comparison, and the two levers are not interchangeable because one keeps a habitat and the other substitutes it. Scale: first order is direct contact and direct outcome; second order is the cascade. So R is a union compound, not one hard scalar question.
- **Domain 1, origin habitat.** Actors: hazard, defences, infrastructure, institutions, households, insurers, ecosystems. Currency: realised outcomes and repeated work. Anchor: measured origin outcomes that do not move with a planner's forecast. Nameable anchor exists.
- **Domain 2, destination habitat.** Actors: host community, land tenure, labour market, services, governance, new hazard field, relocated households. Currency: realised outcomes after arrival. Anchor: post-move outcomes, which cannot be read pre-move because the two failure modes are silent. Nameable anchor exists in principle.
- **Domain 3, decision frame.** Actors: planners, models, ratios. Currency: forecast coherence. Anchor: none of its own; it must range against D1 and D2 outcomes. The frame is a domain whose anchor is outside it.
- **Domain 4, receiving area.** Actors: host residents, services, employers, arrivals. Currency: their outcomes. Anchor: their measured outcomes. Nameable anchor exists. D2 and D4 overlap as a compound.

Union, not partition: each of the four conditions can bind alone and the intersections are categorically different from the single conditions. Dependency order: describe R, enumerate habitat and cascade, bind the ordinary term "safer", settle D1 and D2 per slot, then D4, then compare, then let the ground decide.

My census matches the primary's four domains, union reading and dependency order. Where the primary derived from Fig 11's substitution law and Fig 9's union/partition split, I derived from Fig 2's anchor test and Fig 3's dependency chain. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes inside the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the level of the census. It is still one evidence pool, so no independent weight follows.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :66-69 (substitution, reproduction, inherited actors, silent failures), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 2 :29 (non-updating anchor), Fig 9 :42 (ground decides).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: both levers are habitat operations; moving substitutes and must reproduce; protection is permanent work; compare per slot after settlement; no universal number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the ordinary decision problem: a county choosing between a levee programme and a buyout and relocation programme, with a benefit-cost score.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot identity** of the losers, which a county-level expected-loss figure pools away;
- the **pretext** of the destination, meaning the kin and service history that a suitability score cannot carry (this is the tacit-knowledge point from the essay);
- the **inherited actor** at the destination, which no origin-side hazard map contains;
- the **order of refusal before mint**, which the practical budgeting cycle reverses when it funds another rebuild;
- the **two cost currencies**, which the benefit-cost score collapses into one number.

Conversely, three things survive cleanly: habitat substitution as the operation, the per-slot measuring rule, and the requirement that the anchor not update. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no outcome reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the full figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
