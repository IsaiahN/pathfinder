## Wider literature that bears on the question

Two clusters, with overlap declared. The managed-retreat cluster (Hino et al. 2017, Mach et al. 2019, Siders et al. 2019 as cited in the Wikipedia article, and the PDD and Climigration practitioner pages) is one evidence pool, not several independent witnesses. The two genuinely low-overlap corroborators are conservation translocation (assisted migration) and flood engineering (levees): both independently produce the same structural warning, that moving an entity into a new habitat inherits a community of actors you did not choose, and that defending in place can grow the residual it was meant to remove. The essays share the framework author's pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.

Three things in the world sources bear directly on the shape claim:

1. **Mach et al. 2019** is the strongest ground-visible check on F2 and F4. Relocations to "areas with equal flood risk and greater social vulnerability" are the inherited-actor failure, and the county versus neighbourhood split is the per-slot signal that an average hides. Bring this source to a checker first.
2. **IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 17** is the strongest check on F5 and F11. Its statement that success depends on who evaluates and at what time, plus its emphasis on monitoring and evaluation, is the ground-and-channel distinction in the assessment literature's own words.
3. **Assisted migration** is the cleanest low-overlap analogue for Fig 11 :67. The niche-model failure, that a species "may perform better (e.g., become invasive) or worse (e.g., fail to establish) in a new habitat" because "the community of competitor, predator, and mutualist species is different there", is the substitution law stated in ecology.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** says "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending this whole derivation before a reader has described their own residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** says "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** says "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before showing them the packet.
- **Fig 9 :60** says "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A list sorted by likely agreement is a budget filter misused as a witness.
- **Fig 2 :45** says "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." No follow-up sequence should try to talk a checker into agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the researcher would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the four-condition shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure of this claim? Do not ask for agreement and do not ask for trust. Record the answer before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the researcher is curious after stage 1, offer the three links listed in `02_outreach_email.md`. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, per Fig 2 :40.

**Stage 3, optional and also opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, which is permitted because a generator can be given away without giving away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write their own refuter before running it and must state the mode of what they get, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The current output is a set of conditions with a declared gap, not a universal threshold. If a checker shows a figure line that returns such a threshold, or fires any item in the pre-registered refuter, the packet is rewritten before any further contact.
