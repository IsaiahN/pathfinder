# The Tether's answer on machine memory

**Mode: `specified` on the three-operation policy, `general` on the structural rules. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

Per object slot s, the library predicts Γ(b,a) and the slot returns o′, so R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′| (Fig 1 :19; Symbols table :38-40). A memory policy decides which terms stay composable, which lose that standing, and which become portable methods. The question also asks which seat's criterion decides. This is a **union compound**, not one lever: the record and the reach are two quantities, *"one monotone, one not"* (Fig 6 :43), and a single memory-quality score would erase the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22).

## 2. The shape

1. **Remember as provenanced record.** Fig 12 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* The machine keeps the lineage, not just the artifact. Origin and seq are part of what is kept (Formula step 4, local).

2. **Forget as added refusal, never deletion.** Fig 5 :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Fig 6 :46: *"A store that deleted instead would have neither property, and could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up."* The term to refuse is the bound term whose residual persists and does not trend down (Fig 5 :58; Formula step 2, local).

3. **Compress methods, not recordings, and measure the gap.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The metric "compression achieved" is disqualified (Fig 1 :25-27).

4. **The ground decides, and interests are declared.** Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* The model's own utilisation score is frame-internal; the designer's preference is an interest in the reading; a user preference can be an anchor only when the question is constitutively about that user, and then it is an instrument that can be wrong on other axes (Fig 2 :36-37). The verifier must not author the policy (Fig 10 :42).

5. **Order matters.** Refusal comes before compression, because compressing a term that should be refused would make a wrong predictor portable (`06` Q1). The remaining instrument gap is declared, not filled.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a retention schedule, a pruning threshold, a replay-buffer size or a compression ratio. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not "machines must never overwrite parameters." Physical overwriting is channel behaviour: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). Privacy law can require erasure; that is a declared stake, not a figure verdict.
- Not that any of the three operations has been settled by measurement. No ground reading was used, so the status is `candidate`, not `accepted` (`THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule, local).
- Not that agreement among memory researchers would settle the policy. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- Not a derivation of continual-learning or unlearning algorithms from the figures. Those are bindings from the machine-learning frame, provenanced as such.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (via `05`): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. Can an instrument distinguish a retained method from a recording of one occasion at a stated resolution? That would close the F6 gap.
2. **Deletion test:** can a store that deletes still distinguish never-held from held-and-given-up and compute a safe fall of its reach? If yes, F2 is refuted.
3. **Self-score test:** can a policy score itself by "compression achieved" without that score being produced by the frame it grades? If yes, F3 is refuted.
4. **Anchor test:** can an anchor's answer change because a frame thought differently? If yes, F4 is refuted.
5. **Refusal-ranking test:** does a policy refuse a shrinking-residual term while leaving a persistent flat one composable, with no ground reason? If yes, F5 is refuted.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4 are written before the run. Until one is tested, no part of this answer is `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is an instrument that discriminates method from recording; it is characterized in effect terms but not held. The six-term vocabulary and the memory vocabulary are visible choices, not nature's own list. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *Indiscernibility is not identity* (FIGURES_README_canonical.md).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a pruning schedule or a compression ratio. It says a machine's memory has three asymmetric operations: remember as a provenanced record, because the lineage is the term; forget by adding a refusal that removes a term's standing to be composed over while the record keeps it, so the reach falls because something was added and not because anything was deleted; and compress a method, never the transcript of one success, bracketing the loss with a measured round trip. Refusal comes before compression, or a wrong predictor becomes portable. The decision is settled by the ground the system is ranged against, not by the machine's own memory-utilisation score, and any seat with an interest in the reading must declare it. What is still owed is an instrument that can tell a method from a recording. Until one exists, the answer is a shape with pre-registered refuters, not a settled policy.

## 7. Cost if wrong, undoability, reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** a machine team would receive a three-part constraint that is structurally tidy but operationally silent, and might spend a budget measuring the wrong thing. The cost is bounded: no term is deleted from the record if the claim fails.
- **Undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :43). The lineage is kept so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five tests in §4, in particular a deleting store that keeps the two properties in Fig 6 :46, or a source-discriminating instrument that shows a retained recording composes as well as a settled method.
