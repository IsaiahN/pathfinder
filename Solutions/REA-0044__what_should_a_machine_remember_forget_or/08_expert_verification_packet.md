# Expert verification packet: the Tether on machine memory

**What this is:** a claim in a form a memory or continual-learning researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three-operation policy, `general` on the structural rules. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of a deployed system is claimed. This is a shape and a route.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle REA-0044.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Per object slot, remember as a provenanced record; forget by adding a refusal that removes a term's standing to be composed over while the record keeps the term; compress a settled method, never a recording of one occasion, with a measured round-trip gap; refuse before compressing. The criterion is the ground the system is ranged against, not the machine's own memory-utilisation or compression score, and any seat with an interest in the reading must declare it. A final instrument gap is declared: no available figure line names the specific retention schedule or the detail a compressed method must keep.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 5, 6 and 12

- **Statement:** "forget" is not deletion. It is the addition of a refusal, keeping the record and letting the reach fall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :55, *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Fig 6 :46, *"A store that deleted instead would have neither property, and could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up."* Fig 12 :112, *"A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*
- **What to check:** are those three lines exact substrings of the figures, and does the conclusion follow from them rather than from a metaphor about forgetting?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a store that deletes entries yet still distinguishes never-held from held-and-given-up and computes a safe fall of its reach. That breaks Fig 6 :46 and the sub-claim is withdrawn.

### C2 Engineering-facing, checkable against known memory systems

- **Statement:** the thing that must cross a scale boundary is a method that can be reapplied, not the recording of one success; the compression must be bracketed by a measured round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25, *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 4 :30, *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Also Fig 1 :25, *"coverage, and compression achieved"*, and Fig 1 :27, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **What to check:** does event sourcing (Fowler 2005) or selective retention (Kirkpatrick et al. 2017) actually instantiate an additive record and a ground-paid importance criterion, or is the resemblance only a shared vocabulary? Is "compression achieved" genuinely a quantity the grading frame produces?
- **Refuter:** show a coarser description that crosses the boundary and returns an exact match with no loss measured, or a self-score that is not produced by the frame it grades.

### C3 Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** the decision seat is the ground, not the model's own accounting; frame-internal scores are disqualified and interests in the reading must be declared.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :23, *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 10 :59, *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :86, *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."*
- **What to check:** does the seat rule exclude the designer's preference and the model's utilisation score without excluding a legitimate ground? Is the user-preference exception in Fig 2 :36-37 applied correctly (an instrument, not a verdict)?
- **Refuter:** an anchor whose answer changes because a frame thought differently, or a verifier that authored the policy and still verifies it (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Deletion test:** can a deleting store keep the two properties in Fig 6 :46?
2. **Source-discriminator test:** can an instrument distinguish a method from a recording of one occasion at a stated resolution?
3. **Self-score test:** can a frame avoid producing the metric it grades itself by?
4. **Anchor test:** can an anchor move because a frame thought differently?
5. **Order test:** does compressing before refusing ever improve the next ground reading as much as refusing first?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 5 :55, Fig 6 :46, Fig 4 :22-30, Fig 1 :25-27, Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :59 and :86. Eight short passages.
2. Check C1 against their own knowledge of parameter overwriting and unlearning. Does the record/channel distinction survive?
3. Check C2 against one system they know: is the retained object a method or a transcript, and was a round-trip gap measured?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish method from recording, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
