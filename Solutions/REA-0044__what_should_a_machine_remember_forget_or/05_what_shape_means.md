## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops at the limit of what instruments can distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is honest. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: a system with a closure, *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

*The Library of Babel* gives the positive version: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the three-part constraint on a memory policy:

1. remember as a provenanced record (the bond is part of the term, Fig 12 :130);
2. forget as an added refusal, not a deletion (Fig 5 :55, Fig 6 :42-43);
3. compress as a method with a measured round trip (Fig 4 :22-31);

with the decision seat fixed by the ground and declared stakes (Fig 1 :23, Fig 10 :59-60).

A specific retention schedule ("prune weights below 1e-4 after 30 days") is a **name**, not a shape. The figures do not yield it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the residual per slot, then add the terms record, reach, refusal, method, gap, stake.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was therefore stated as a prediction-versus-return gap per slot, not as "memory pressure".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* This is why the outreach artifact uses "refusal that removes composability" rather than framework jargon as the first sentence.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (via exemplar mapping): when contact is not possible, the best that can be sent is a plain description of what one would expect to see, because anyone can check it against the world. Hence the four falsifiers in `02` are observational, not doctrinal.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by the time the exact search terms are known, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the search terminates at a declared instrument gap (F6), not at a specific policy.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 (local): *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a memory policy, that means an instrument or benchmark that pays for the retention rule, not a memory-utilisation counter.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (via exemplar mapping) sharpens this: the real import is a new dimension of the space, not a new term inside it, and declaring primitives does not make them neutral, it makes the choice visible. The six-term substrate and the memory vocabulary are visible choices, not nature's own list. Turning one into "the answer" would be the forbidden move.

Consequence for REA-0044: selecting a specific pruning threshold, replay buffer or unlearning algorithm is a binding from the machine-learning frame onto a slot the shape leaves open. It is allowed as a hypothesis with provenance; it is not a figure result.

## 4. Why the "whose interests" clause is a shape question too

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (via exemplar mapping) gives the metamerism argument: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and several physically different sources can yield the same reading. The reading does not determine the source; only a better instrument does. Applied here, a memory-utilisation reading cannot tell whether the system kept a method or a recording of one occasion. Naming "the user's interest" or "the model's interest" from that reading would repeat the error.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* supplies the interest rule directly: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The shape therefore includes a seat discipline: declare the stake, do not let it author the verdict, and do not select checkers for likely agreement (Fig 2 :40).

One unusual primary detail from that essay bears directly on this question: the author writes that in the Tether agent *"I could decide the pattern of how and when data degrades"* and could load information into the library without pretraining. This confirms that the degradation schedule is a design choice inside the architecture, not a figure-derived constant. It is recorded as framework-internal provenance, with maximal overlap with the figures and zero independent witness weight.

## 5. Q: does the framework name what to forget? ANSWERED, partly

**What licenses pointing a refusal at a term?** Fig 5 :58 (local figure text) says every bound, predicting term is a candidate, and Formula step 2 ranks them by a residual that persists and does not trend down. That is a figure-grounded rule for a **bound** term.

**What licenses retiring a preference the machine never bound?** Nothing mechanisable. Fig 5 :62-63 says the sought-for shape is held as a *"PREFERENCE rather than as a term"*, it predicts nothing and accrues no residual, and the remedy is a discipline: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* That is a limit, not a branch. F5 in `01` keeps both halves and does not pretend the second is solved.

## 6. Simulation status (required, not decorative)

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The same essay says *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

Therefore `01` through `09` in this folder are a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. This file cannot settle REA-0044. It can only report the shape a candidate answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that is still owed.

## 7. Consequences for outreach

1. Lead with the shape and the four refuters, not with a recommended pruning schedule (`02`).
2. Never ask a recipient to trust the framework. Prestige can fund a trial, not settle a claim.
3. Ask what they would count as a failure *before* showing the sources, so their stage-1 verdict is not manufactured by the frame (Fig 10 :52, :42).
4. State the limitation that the framework returns a shape, so a request for a concrete memory budget is answered honestly as a gap plus a provenanced binding, not as a figure result.
