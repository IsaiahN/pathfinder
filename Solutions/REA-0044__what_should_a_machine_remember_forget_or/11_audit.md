## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among a candidate term, its description cost, its leftover residual and a slot index, ordered in time. At least four places. A two-place reading (model, memory footprint) loses the criterion and the slot, so it is rejected. The policy also uses a unary refusal operator, so arity is mixed, a point the primary made in `01` §1 and `06` Q4.
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. Introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). The record and the reach are two quantities, one monotone and one not (Fig 6 :43). A symmetric add/remove metaphor is strained and rejected.
- **Scale:** the slot is inside one machine loop; the decision seat exists at the checking arrangement; compression crosses the membrane between one agent and a population with a shared library (Fig 4 :5, :19-20). Intrinsically cross-scale, so one loop at one scale cannot close it.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's four-domain shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a memory policy keeps a provenanced record, changes reach by refusal, carries methods with a measured loss, and is settled by an outside ground.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific machine situation. Result: *keep logs with metadata, add "do not use" flags instead of deleting, compress the training rule, and evaluate on a held-out ground.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union of record and reach into "keep a record", losing Fig 6 :43, *"one monotone, one not"*. Coming back down, it licenses "keep more logs" as if that improved memory, which is exactly the frame-internal error Fig 1 :27 names.
2. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. "Compress the training rule" does not say how to tell a rule from a transcript of one occasion, which is the actual F6 gap.
3. It loses the refusal-ranking rule (Fig 5 :58). "Add a do-not-use flag" has no criterion for which term gets one, so a shrinking-residual term could be flagged while a persistent flat one stays composable.
4. It loses the seat discipline. "Evaluate on a held-out ground" could be read as the designer choosing the ground, which Fig 10 :93 disqualifies when the ground has a reputation to protect.
5. It loses the declared-stake rule. The abstraction has no place for the GDPR erasure duty, which is a real conflict returned to census in `01` S1.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version. The paragraph in `07` §6 is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
