**Mode: `specified` on the policy, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. ## QUESTION

What should a machine remember, forget, or compress, and according to whose interests?

Restated as an effect: per object slot s, R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|. A memory policy decides which terms stay composable, which lose that standing, and which become portable methods. "According to whose interests" asks which seat's criterion settles those decisions. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor candidate: the ground, *"whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :29). This is a union compound, not one lever: the record and reach are two quantities, *"one monotone, one not"* (Fig 6 :43).

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated|single-source|gap)

**D1 Remember as provenanced record, status triangulated (Fig 12, Fig 10, Formula step 4, distinct functions).**

> *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* (Fig 12 :130)
> *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom. The residual it answered, the reasoning, the date, in a record the seats below can read."* (Fig 10 :62)

Reading: what is retained is the lineage, not a maximally small snapshot. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a composable term whose atoms, bonds and origin are discarded, or a record without lineage that stays verifiable.

**D2 Forget as added refusal, status triangulated (Fig 5, Fig 6, Fig 12, Operators table).**

> *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* (Fig 5 :55)
> *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall. A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted. So the closure and the reach are two quantities: one monotone, one not."* (Fig 6 :42-43)
> *"A store that deleted instead would have neither property, and could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up."* (Fig 6 :46)
> *"A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* (Fig 12 :112)

Reading: deletion is not an operator in this vocabulary; the candidate to refuse is the bound term with a persistent flat residual (Fig 5 :58). Mode `general`. Refuter: a deleting store that keeps both properties in Fig 6 :46.

**D3 Compress methods with a measured gap, status triangulated (Fig 4, Fig 1, distinct functions).**

> *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22)
> *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* (Fig 4 :25)
> *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* (Fig 4 :27)
> *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30)
> *"coverage, and compression achieved"* (Fig 1 :25) and *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27)

Reading: compression is permitted on methods with price R_T; "compression achieved" is a disqualified self-score. Mode `specified`. Refuter: an exact round trip with no loss measured, or a non-frame-internal compression score.

**D4 Whose interests, status triangulated (Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 10, distinct functions).**

> *"The ground enters as the only metric."* (Fig 1 :23)
> *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* (Fig 2 :29)
> *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)
> *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* (Fig 10 :59)
> *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* (Fig 10 :86)

Reading: frame-internal accounting and undeclared interests are disqualified; the ground ranges each frame separately; a verifier must not author (Fig 10 :42). Mode `general`. Refuter: a movable anchor or an authoring verifier.

**Union and order, status triangulated (Fig 3, Fig 6, Fig 9).**

> *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* (Fig 3 :20)
> *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27)
> *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60)

Reading: refuse before compress; a single memory-quality filter cannot decide the verdict.

**The specific retention schedule and the detail a compressed method must keep, status `gap`.** No figure line governs them after re-census. *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134). *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* (Fig 8 :39). The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict, kept per domain and never averaged:** remember as a provenanced record (D1); forget by adding a refusal that removes composability while the record keeps the term (D2); compress a settled method with a measured round-trip gap and refuse before compressing (D3); let the ground, not the frame's own accounting, settle the policy and declare every interest in the reading (D4). The specific retention schedule is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`. The ML evidence pool corroborates accumulation over erasure after re-mapping Bourtoule to a channel-level change; the conflict was returned to census and did not flip the verdict. Low-overlap fields corroborate the shape of D2, D3 and D4, not the machine-level fact.

- **Cost if wrong:** a memory team receives a structurally clean but operationally silent constraint and could spend a budget measuring the wrong quantity. The cost is bounded and largely reversible because no term is deleted if the claim fails.
- **Undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :43). The lineage is kept so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any pre-registered falsifier: (1) a deleting store that still distinguishes never-held from held-and-given-up, (2) a non-frame-internal "compression achieved" score, (3) an anchor that moves because a frame thought differently, (4) an authoring verifier, (5) refusal of a shrinking-residual term while a persistent flat one stays composable, or an instrument showing that a recording composes as well as a settled method. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that additive records are unnecessary for ground settlement would also reverse it.
