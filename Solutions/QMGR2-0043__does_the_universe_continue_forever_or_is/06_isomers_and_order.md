# Unbundling, order, and analogues for the finite/infinite question

**Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the order claims, `gap` on the physical value.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The wording "continues forever, or total extent finite" packs a temporal question, a spatial question, and an observability question into one sentence. The figures require a union to be unbundled before it is searched. Two things are owed: (1) what structure relates the two candidate values, and (2) in what order the split, the search and the stop must be run.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — What bond joins "finite" and "infinite"?

- Fig 12 :46: **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**
- The operators table defines ⋛ (comparison) as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, with the test *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"*
- The two candidate values are rival global hypotheses, not quantities that compose into a third substance. No bond in the seven joins them. So the correct relation is a **comparison of hypotheses against a reading**, not a molecule built from "finite" and "infinite".
- Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** The arrangement here is the relation between a bounded reading and a global claim, and that is what the answer must describe.
- **Falsifier, written before this run:** if a bond is found whose typed relation composes "finite" and "infinite" into a settled term at the same level, this derivation fails and a global value might be mintable from the pair.

### Q2 — What is the required order?

1. **Split before search.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The Library of Babel essay says the same in effect: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*
2. **Describe before choosing a frame.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. In effect terms: what would a finite compact universe produce that an infinite one would not? Repeated images; a matched circle-in-the-sky pattern; a curvature scale. Those are effects, ready to check.
3. **Choose the cheapest question first.** Formula step 7 asks SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD. Fig 9 :43: **"The verdict tells you to leave. The lookup tells you where to go. Elimination is what you do after you arrive."**
4. **Keep the two instruments separate.** Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."* Sharpening an existing instrument (INWARD) and describing the residual so a different frame can predict its shape (OUTWARD) are opposed. A flatness bound refined forever does not settle topology.
5. **Refuse before mint.** If "infinite" is being held as a preferred answer, that preference is not a term and accrues no residual. Formula step 2 says every check runs on the mechanism, not on the direction of the question. Write the sought shape as an ordinary term and let it be refusable.

**Falsifier for the order claim:** if a single instrument settles both D1 and D2 without the split, the dependency order is wrong. If sharpening a CMB instrument and importing a topology frame turn out to be the same operation, step 4 is wrong.

### Q4 — The local to global transform has a nonzero round-trip gap

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

- **Sent up (x):** a local reading (flat to within bounds, no detected edge, measured expansion history).
- **Abstraction (T_A):** "the data fit a flat, accelerating model."
- **Concretisation (T_E):** "the universe is spatially infinite and lasts forever."

**R_T is nonzero.** What the shorthand holds but the global claim drops: the distinction between geometry and topology; the possibility of a compact flat fundamental domain beyond the horizon; the difference between a measured expansion history and all future history. Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the local reading is real and reproduces the data. That is the residue worth sending. R_T is recorded qualitatively, not numerically, because no ground reading decides the global value. Treating the condensation as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The second seat measures this in `11_audit.md`.

## CONCLUSION

The two candidate values are not composable; they are rival hypotheses to be compared against a reading. The required order is split, describe in effect terms, SEARCHED first, then keep INWARD and OUTWARD separate, then stop at the horizon with the lineage recorded. The analogues from perception and instrument discrimination corroborate the method only. The physical value remains a `gap`, and the round trip from local data to global claim has a nonzero, enumerated gap R_T.
