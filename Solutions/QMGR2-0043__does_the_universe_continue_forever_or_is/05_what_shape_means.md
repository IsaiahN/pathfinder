## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: a system *"can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before the value is available:

- the question is a bundle and must be split before it is searched;
- the answer distinguishes a horizon-limited reading from a global claim;
- the answer names the instrument that would discriminate the two;
- the answer records where the frame stops.

It is not the value "finite" or "infinite". Those are names, and the framework does not hold them for this question.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on the value of confusion: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay's own instruction for a union residual like this one. It follows with: you need to search the problems individually and then combine the answers.
- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the known terms closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30, quoted in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the effect description is: a topology signature, a curvature scale, or an expansion-history discriminator that a reader could look for.

## 3. The distinction horizon is the shape's stopping rule

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the term this question needs. There is a **distinction horizon** for each observer, and a **union horizon** across all observers and instruments. A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin. Crucially: *"Being “below the union horizon” is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

The same essay splits the "no trace" case from the "trace exists but is not yet detectable" case. In the first, *"You can only ever say nothing has been found yet."* In the second, better instruments eventually resolve it, as isotope ratios resolved mined versus lab-grown diamonds. This is the essay-level version of Fig 6 :27 (*"some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same"*) and of Fig 8 :40-41 (*"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions"*).

So a shape for this question must keep two cases open and must not report "no trace" as if it had been confirmed. *The Hard Problem is About Source* names the error: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* For global extent, the flat CMB reading is the projection; the source (finite compact or infinite simply connected) is not determined by it.

## 4. Why "no infinite regress" cannot fill this shape

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* derives the six terms and five rules. It calls the product *"infinite variety of composition"* and immediately says: *"This is also why there is no infinite regress, or hierarchy in the conventional sense: these chains do not loop around on themselves, but merely spread out, diverging further as they are connected (which we colloquially call branching)."*

The "infinite" here is a property of the arrangement space, and "no infinite regress" is a property of the checking hierarchy. Neither is a statement about the metric volume or future duration of the physical universe. Fig 4 :33 (*"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*) and Fig 13 :90 (*"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*) are the figure-level versions of the same point. Using them to answer QMGR2-0043 is a category error, recorded as a strain in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4.

## 5. What licenses a name, and the correction to my own §1

Only two things license a name in this framework: the ground settling a term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test.

**Correction to §1 of this file.** I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids the framework from making *any* claim about global extent. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive or value not held in Γ. What I state here is different in kind: it is a claim about the **reach of a frame**, which the figures do govern (Fig 6 :27, Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44, Fig 13 :134). So the framework *can* say "a bounded frame cannot decide this value"; it cannot say "the value is finite" or "the value is infinite". Both readings are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 9; the essays agree but share the author's evidence pool.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* warns what happens when a declared set is treated as nature's own list: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* And on imports: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Neither sentence licenses a cosmological value.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Asking a prestigious cosmologist to endorse the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to test the claim, and let an instrument, not a reputation, settle the value.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself"*; LLM output is *"simulacra"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle QMGR2-0043. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* A panel of agreeable readers would be a room, not an anchor (Fig 7 :35).
