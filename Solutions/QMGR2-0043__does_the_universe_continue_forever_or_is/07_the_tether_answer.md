# The Tether's answer on universe extent

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the governance claims, `gap` on the finite/infinite value. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles a temporal fate question and a spatial extent question, plus an observability question. Observation returns a flat, accelerating universe with no detected edge or repeated image, and that reading is consistent with both an infinite simply connected space and several finite compact ones (Luminet 2016; Souradeep 2006; Reboucas and Gomero 2004). So the residual is a per-slot mismatch between a horizon-bounded reading and a global claim. Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*.

## 2. Per-domain verdicts (kept separate, never averaged)

**D1, spatial extent. Verdict: `gap` on the value; `specified` on the governance.** No verbatim figure line governs the global spatial extent of the physical universe. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* A null topology search therefore licenses "nothing found yet", not "infinite". A finite compact model whose fundamental domain lies inside the horizon is reachable at this budget; a global value with no signature at any resolution is underivable in principle. Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."*

**D2, temporal fate. Verdict: `gap` on the value; `specified` on the governance.** Same structure, different anchor. A measured expansion history constrains models; it does not deliver all future time. The "no top / no last route" lines are refused here as a physical answer: they govern composition levels (Fig 4 :33, Fig 12 :103, Fig 13 :90). Using them would be a strain, and the strain is recorded, not hidden.

**D3, framework-internal reach. Verdict: `general`, triangulated within the framework.** A bounded frame cannot certify its own global limit: Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (about the frame). Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

**D4, verification. Verdict: `general`, single evidence pool in this simulation.** The anchor is an instrument, never agreement. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## 3. The shape

1. **Split the union first.** Temporal duration and spatial extent have different anchors. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
2. **Describe effects, not causes.** Ask what a finite compact space would produce inside the horizon (matched circles, repeated images, a curvature scale), and look for that. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
3. **Keep depth and absence distinct.** From inside, an unreachable signature because of the horizon and a genuinely absent signature look the same (Fig 6 :27). Do not collapse them.
4. **Keep underivable and unreachable-at-budget distinct.** Different facts, different instructions (Fig 8 :40-41). More search is the remedy in one case, a different frame in the other.
5. **Report the stop honestly.** Fig 13 :138: *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"*. Say `gap` or `unreachable`; do not manufacture a value.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the universe is infinite" and not "the universe is finite". Both are names the framework does not hold here.
- Not "the framework's no infinite regress settles cosmology". That line is about levels of composition, not physical extent (F4).
- Not that flatness implies infinity. Geometry constrains but does not dictate topology (Luminet 2016; Souradeep 2006).
- Not that the question is meaningless. It is a union compound with its own ground, exactly the kind of question the figures say to split rather than dismiss.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument in this derivation discriminates the global values.
- Not a claim that every null search is equally informative. Some topologies are detectable at this budget; the shape says which question to ask first.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

1. **Topology detection:** matched circle pairs in the CMB, repeated images, or a detected compact fundamental domain. That moves D1 from `gap` to `grounded`.
2. **Curvature detection:** a positive curvature large enough to close the space, measured independently of the flatness prior.
3. **Fate discrimination:** an expansion-history measurement that separates eternal expansion from recollapse or a future singularity, moving D2 to `grounded`.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a figure line about physical global extent; a proof that D1 and D2 share one anchor; a demonstration that the "no top" lines are about the metric; or a demonstration that a frame inside the horizon can certify its own limit.

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The existing instruments (CMB maps, expansion probes) already return something; they are the edge to extend from.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not proven unreachable.** *How Intelligence Actually Works* says a horizon report is *"never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet"*, and in the no-trace case *"You can only ever say nothing has been found yet."* The framework's own six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the distinction horizon and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say the universe is infinite, and it does not say it is finite. It says the question is two questions bundled into one: a spatial extent question and a temporal fate question, with different instruments that could settle each. It says that after a genuine census no figure line gives the global value, so that value is a `gap`, and that the framework's "no infinite regress" language is about levels of composition, not about physical space, a reading we refuse and record. What the figures do say is checkable: a horizon-bounded frame cannot promote a null search to a global verdict, because depth and genuine absence look the same from inside; underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts with different remedies; and the anchor is a source-discriminating instrument, never agreement. What is offered is the split, the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the exact instruments that would close it. The value stays open until the ground settles it.
