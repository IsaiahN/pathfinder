## Restating the question as an effect

Two options are offered. Option A says the universe continues forever; option B says its total extent is finite. Stated in effect terms, the question asks what a bounded observer would have to see in order to choose between a horizon-bounded sample and a global claim. The two options do not differ in what they predict inside the horizon on the present evidence, which is the residual. The answer we bind is: *a global finite/infinite value is not decidable from a frame whose reach is bounded, and a shape can still be stated for what any candidate answer must look like*.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A list chosen for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* For this question the ground is an instrument, not a vote.
3. **Do not hand over the interiors before the verdict.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM failures that come with harvested personal addresses, as well as the structural failure above.
