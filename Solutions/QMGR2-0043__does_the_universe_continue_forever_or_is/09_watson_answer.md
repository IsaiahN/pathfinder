## VERDICT (from the figures)

The physical finite/infinite value is a `gap`, not a figure-governed verdict. The shape survives and is triangulated within the framework: unbundle the union before searching; no null search inside a horizon can be promoted to a global absence or infinity claim; depth and genuine absence look the same from inside; underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts; the anchor is a source-discriminating instrument, never agreement. The "no infinite regress" and "no last route" lines are refused as physical answers, and that strain is recorded. D1 and D2 keep separate verdicts, never averaged. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `gap` at the centre.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, refuters and the refused strain, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a value claim instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The `gap` is reported, not asserted as a settled value, so no physical claim has to be withdrawn. If a topology or curvature detection fires, D1 moves to `grounded` by adding a settled term; nothing in the record is deleted. The refused strain stays visible so later readers can see why it was not used.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A compact-topology or curvature detection would settle D1; an expansion-history discriminator would settle D2; a verbatim figure line about global physical extent, or a proof that D1 and D2 share one anchor, would refute the shape claim; a demonstration that a frame inside the horizon can certify its own limit would break C3. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, with the value explicitly `gap`.
