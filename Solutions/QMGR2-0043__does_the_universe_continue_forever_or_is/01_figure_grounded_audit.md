## 0. The claim under audit

> Option A: the universe continues forever (no global end in time, or no finite spatial bound).
> Option B: its total extent is finite (a global bound that an instrument could in principle register).
>
> Proposed shape: the finite/infinite value is a `gap` under the figures; what the figures govern is the *form* of an admissible answer. The question must first be unbundled, a bounded frame cannot promote a horizon-limited reading to a global value, and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).

Nothing in this audit asserts that the universe is finite, or that it is infinite. That is the point of the finding.

## 1. The residual, stated in effect terms

What failed to resolve? Two observations that are both real and both consistent with more than one global hypothesis:

1. Precision cosmology fits a spatially flat model (Planck Collaboration 2018/2020, read this session). Flatness does not settle topology: a flat universe may be infinite and simply connected, or finite and multiply connected (Luminet 2016; Reboucas and Gomero 2004).
2. No repeating pattern, edge, or positive curvature signature has been detected inside the horizon. Under the figures, a search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39). So the observation does not decide the residual.

Fig 1 :13 is exactly the legal state this produces: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. A global fit near zero (flatness, no detected edge) coexists with one live slot (global extent) that has not resolved. Fig 1 :19 adds: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Aggregating the fits into one number *"is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22).

So R is not "how big is the universe" as one scalar. R is a per-slot mismatch between what a horizon-bounded frame can represent and what a global claim would require.

## 2. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

### 2.1 R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** At least two conditions are bundled. The wording "continues forever" can mean temporal duration or unbounded spatial extent; "total extent finite" is spatial. A third condition is whether either can be settled from inside a horizon. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :55-57). Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **Symmetry.** Finite and infinite are not related by a symmetric bond; they are rival global values, so a single measurement that discriminates them would be a comparison (⋛) between hypotheses, not a composition of them. No bond in the operators table joins "finite" to "infinite" into a third substance.
- **Scale.** The measurement slot is local (CMB multipoles, standard rulers, expansion history). The claim slot is global (the whole spatial section, or all future time). The transform between them is lossy and not invertible from inside. That is the membrane question (Fig 4), not a unit conversion.

### 2.2 One line per domain, with actors, currency, anchor

A domain is admitted only if it has a nameable anchor (Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | admitted? |

No fifth domain is admitted. "What lies outside the universe" has no nameable anchor: there is no instrument whose reading changes with it, so it is not a domain yet. This is recorded rather than silently dropped.

### 2.3 Partition or union

Union across D1 and D2 (two conditions bundled in one question). D3 governs the shape of any answer in both. D4 checks them. Because the split is a union, D1 and D2 get **separate verdicts**, never an average (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

### 2.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54). This is link 2, vocabulary: you cannot say what would count as progress until the conditions are separate.
2. For each unbundled condition, describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*).
3. Separate depth from genuine absence (Fig 6 :27) and underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-budget (Fig 8 :40-41). These are link-3 questions about what would count as an answer.
4. Only then run any search or instrument (Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*). Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 4. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union and must be split (general, decisive for method)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :55-57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**, and :59: **"The residue is finally describable."**

Temporal duration (D2) and spatial extent (D1) do not share an anchor. A measurement of Ω_k constrains D1; a measurement of the equation-of-state parameter constrains D2. Answering one does not answer the other. Joining them into one "forever versus finite" question produces a bundle whose two conditions bind separately, so a single lever (one number) clears neither intersection.

**Refuter:** show that one instrument discriminates both the global spatial value and the temporal fate at once. The census was wrong if a single anchor settles both. Mark `general` for the method claim; the world sources are secondary and agree that geometry, topology and fate are distinct observables.

### F2 — No figure line governs the physical finite/infinite value: `gap` (general)

After the census of §3, no figure line states or implies a value for the global spatial extent or the total duration of the universe. The lines that appear to do so are about frames, closures and composition levels, not about physical space (F4). The honest verdict is therefore `gap` on the physical quantity.

Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. That verdict governs the frame's reach, not the universe. It means a frame may be unable to derive a value and must say so. Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.

**Refuter:** produce a verbatim figure line whose subject is the global spatial extent of the physical universe rather than the reach of a frame. Until that is done, any finite/infinite verdict from the figures alone is an import, not a derivation.

### F3 — A bounded frame cannot promote "no edge seen" to "infinite" (specified, triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."** Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

These are three distinct figure mechanisms (closure idempotence; the sealed-room condition; the two instruments for reachability) with low overlap, so the governance verdict is **triangulated within the framework**. It still rests on one evidence pool, so it is internal consistency, not independent corroboration.

Consequence: the observation "no edge, no repeated image, flat to high precision" is a reading inside the horizon. It licenses *nothing has been found yet*, not *there is no outside*. World sources corroborate: Luminet 2016 reports that Planck data fit an infinite flat model yet remain consistent with finite compact shapes; Mota, Makler and Reboucas 2006 note that repetitions may be unobservable because of the finite distance to the last scattering surface.

**Refuter:** a detection of a specific compact topology, or a measurement of positive curvature large enough to close the space. That would settle D1 at the ground and flip the verdict from `gap` to `grounded` for that condition.

### F4 — The "no infinite regress" lines do NOT decide physical extent: strain named and refused (general)

A tempting reading of Fig 4 :33 (*"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*), Fig 12 :103 (*"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."*) and Fig 13 :90 (*"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*) is: the framework forbids infinity, therefore the universe is finite. This reading is **refused**.

Fig 4 :19-20 says the invariant across sizes is *"the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked... Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Fig 4 :33 follows a discussion of the lossy transform between scales. Fig 13 :113 says **"So the array is unbounded and the reach is not."** The "space" with no last route is the space of compositions, and "no top" is a claim about the hierarchy of frames. *How Intelligence Actually Works* confirms it: *"there is no infinite regress, or hierarchy in the conventional sense: these chains do not loop around on themselves, but merely spread out, diverging further as they are connected."*

Applying these lines to physical spatial extent is a **strain**, and the rule is: a line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so re-census (figures README, canonical usage). Re-census puts these lines in D3, where they govern the reach of a frame. They cannot be used as evidence about the universe's size.

**Refuter:** a figure passage whose "no top / no bottom" claim is explicitly about a physical metric rather than about levels of composition. None was found. This strain is retained in the log, not erased.

### F5 — Underivable in principle and unreachable at budget are different facts (specified, single-source)

Fig 8 :40-41: **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."** Fig 6 :34-35: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."** Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

For D1, the global value may be underivable in principle for a horizon-bounded observer if no signature exists at any resolution (the "no trace" case in *How Intelligence Actually Works*). For a specific compact model with a fundamental domain smaller than the horizon, the value is reachable at this budget: the remedy is more search over CMB maps, not a new primitive. These two cases must not be merged. Importing a new frame against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 8 :42-43).

This finding is **single-source** for the figure distinction (Fig 8), corroborated in method by the essays but not independently measured here.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that the two instruments converge, that is, that an instrument extension designed from a description reaches a value that no search could find. The figures say they are opposed; a demonstration otherwise breaks this finding.

### F6 — The anchor for this question is an instrument, not a consensus (general, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

The anchor here is a source-discriminating observation (a topology signature, a curvature measurement, an expansion-history discriminator). Agreement among cosmologists, however numerous, is a room's variation: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict"* (Fig 7 :35). Fig 7 :35 continues: *"Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

**Refuter:** a ruling body whose decision changes the global extent. None exists; if one were found, Fig 2 :36 would have to be re-censused for a question constitutively about a convention rather than a world.

### F7 — Filters price the budget, witnesses decide the verdict (general)

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 9 :43: **"The verdict tells you to leave. The lookup tells you where to go. Elimination is what you do after you arrive."**

The priority of a venue, the size of a dataset, and the precision of a fit are filters. They decide what gets tried first and how much of the space is swept. They do not decide finite versus infinite. A flatness bound of 0.4 percent (NASA WMAP) is a filter result, not a verdict on topology.

**Refuter:** show a case where a precision bound alone, with no discriminating instrument, settles a global value. The figure rule was written to forbid exactly that inference.

### F8 — Recorded habitat and stopping rule (general)

The habitat of this residual (Fig 11 :43-44, enumerate everything in contact) includes CMB maps, large-scale structure, standard rulers, expansion-history probes, and the instruments that produced them. It is enumerated, not composed. Fig 11 :48: you do not invent the list, you read it off the world.

Stop where the instrument can no longer distinguish (the distinction horizon in *How Intelligence Actually Works*), record the lineage, and say `unreachable` or `gap` rather than manufacture a value. Fig 13 :138: **"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"**. Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

**Refuter:** a new instrument that registers a difference inside the current horizon. Then the stop was premature, and the lineage says exactly where to extend.
