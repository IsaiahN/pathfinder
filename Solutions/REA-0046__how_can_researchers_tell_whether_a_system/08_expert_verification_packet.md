# Expert verification packet: the Tether on REA-0046

**What this is:** a claim in a form a representation-learning researcher, interpretability researcher, or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No claim is made that any particular representation has been shown to correspond to any particular world structure. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Does an internal representation correspond to a meaningful structure in the world?" is a union of three independently failing conditions: information (a decodable signal about the structure), causal role (intervening on the representation changes world-tracking behaviour with specificity, and intervening on the world changes the representation as predicted), and structure (the representation preserves the relation, not only the feature list). The world is the anchor, so a declared label is not grounded; agreement among frames is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. Independent frames converging by separate, recorded routes are the strongest available corroboration. The framework returns this shape, with refuters written in advance, and stops at the limit of what the available instruments discriminate.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** correspondence is a union compound and must be unbundled; the world, not agreement or the frame, is the anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
- **Refuter:** show that the three conditions reduce to one measurable quantity with a single lever, or show a representation whose declared correspondence survives removal of the world structure, or show a case where changing the researcher's description changes the world's verdict.

### C2: field-facing; checkable against the interpretability literature

- **Statement:** a decodable signal is necessary but not sufficient, and the world-side test is an intervention with specificity. The three conditions must keep separate verdicts.
- **What to check:** does control-task selectivity (Hewitt and Liang, EMNLP 2019) discriminate a representation-borne signal from a probe that learned the task? Does causal intervention with specificity (Meng et al., NeurIPS 2022) discriminate a decisive activation from a merely correlated one? Does superposition (Elhage et al., 2022) make a single-unit feature list the wrong granularity? Are any of these results being read as sufficient for correspondence when they only settle one condition?
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **Refuter:** a representation that passes the selectivity test, the causal-intervention test, the bond test and the independence test, and then fails under a new intervention on the world. This refutes the shape itself, not one condition. A cheaper partial refuter: a demonstration that decoding W from X is sufficient for correspondence with no causal or structural condition needed.

### C3: framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** two representations with the same feature set but different relational organization are Fig 12 isomers; their difference is invisible in a feature list and decisive in behaviour. A settled correspondence claim becomes an atom for second-order claims, with no new primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
- **Refuter:** identical behaviour under every intervention designed to distinguish the two organizations, with no instrument improvable from a returning reading to separate them. Then the bond carries no information and the isomer claim fails for that system.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named unit or vector is a named world structure. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that high probe accuracy establishes meaning, or that fluency is evidence of grounding.
- Not that agreement among researchers, reviewers, or models settles correspondence. A room is not an anchor; a mirror cannot correct you.
- Not that a failed search proves absence. Status is `unreached`, not a verdict of no correspondence.
- Not that passing the available tests makes a representation identical to the world structure. Indiscernibility is not identity.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Selectivity Test:** high task accuracy with low control-task accuracy.
2. **Threshold Test:** intervention on X changes W-tracking behaviour with specificity, and intervention on W changes X as predicted.
3. **Bond Test:** two feature-identical states with different organization behave differently under discriminating interventions.
4. **Independence Test:** separate frames with recorded provenance converge on the same atom; shared evidence pools are declared and withdrawn as witnesses.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54, :57, :60 and Fig 2 :29 (four short passages). Do you accept that the question is a union with the world as anchor?
2. Check C2 against your own field: are selectivity, causal intervention and superposition being treated as sufficient for correspondence anywhere, or only as readings that fail to resolve?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate the bond (C3) between two feature-identical representations, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
