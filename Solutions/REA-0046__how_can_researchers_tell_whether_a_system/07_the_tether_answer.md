# The Tether's answer on REA-0046

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A researcher holds an internal state X of a system and a claimed world structure W, and wants to decide "X corresponds to W." The system's own fluent output, internal coherence, and decodable activation patterns cannot settle this, because they are frame-internal quantities. The residual is the gap between what the frame reports about itself and what the world returns under intervention. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."

## 2. The shape

1. **This is a union of three conditions, not one question.** Information (X carries a decodable signal about W), causal role (intervening on X changes world-tracking behaviour with specificity, and intervening on W changes X as predicted), and structure (X preserves the relation, not only the feature list). Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Verdicts are kept per condition and never averaged.

2. **The world is the anchor; a declared label is not.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Calling a unit "the W unit" is a binding, not an anchor. The anchor pays only when pushing on the world changes the reading as predicted, and pushing on the frame does not change the world.

3. **Frame-internal scores and output are disqualified as verdicts, but a probe is a valid worse instrument.** Fig 1 :27 disqualifies self-scores; Fig 6 :32 permits refinement: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." A control-task-selective probe is the reading that already fails to resolve; intervention is the sharper instrument built on it. Fig 6 :35: "the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

4. **Independent convergence is the strongest witness, but only with a record.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". Ten seeds on one corpus are one frame wearing ten names. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

5. **The arrangement is the substance.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Superposition makes single-unit readings unreliable, so the unit list is the wrong granularity for a correspondence claim.

6. **Agreement is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Venue prestige and researcher consensus buy a trial for the claim; they do not settle it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named activation is any named world structure. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that high probe accuracy establishes meaning. A probe can learn the task itself.
- Not that many agreeing researchers establish correspondence. A room is not an anchor.
- Not that a search that finds no correspondence proves none exists. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not that a representation passing the available tests is identical to the world structure. Indiscernibility is not identity; the lineage must be kept.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles any specific representation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Selectivity test:** does a probe achieve high task accuracy with low control-task accuracy, so the signal is in the representation rather than in the probe's capacity?
- **Threshold Test:** does intervening on X change the system's W-tracking behaviour with specificity, and does intervening on W change X as predicted? This is the anchor test applied to representations (world source: Meng et al., 2022).
- **Bond Test:** do two states with the same feature set but different relational organization behave differently under interventions designed to separate them? If not, the structure condition collapses into the feature list.
- **Independence Test:** do genuinely separate frames (different seeds, architectures, or laboratories, with a recorded provenance) converge on the same representation atom? If their evidence pools overlap, the convergence witness is withdrawn.
- **Standing refuter from `01`:** a representation that passes all four tests and then fails under a new intervention on the world. This is the refuter that targets the shape itself.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The probe suite available at this budget discriminates some sources (control tasks, causal tracing, cross-seed convergence) and not others (structure along axes no current primitive can represent). Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The stop is checkable because the three conditions and their refuters were written down before the run. No numerical R_T is claimed: no ground reading exists.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a particular neuron or vector "is" a particular thing in the world, and it does not claim that a crowd of agreeing researchers settles the question. It says that "does an internal representation correspond to a meaningful structure" is a union of three questions that must be kept separate: can we read a signal about the structure from the representation, does intervening on the representation change the system's world-tracking behaviour with specificity, and does the representation preserve the relation rather than only the feature list. The world, not the frame, is the anchor: a declared label is not grounded, and a mirror cannot correct you. Independent models that converge by separate, recorded routes are the strongest available corroboration; agreement from a shared corpus is one opinion said many times. What is offered is the shape any checkable test must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The answer stops at the limit of what current instruments discriminate, with the lineage recorded.
