## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :27 (self-scores disqualified), Fig 2 :29 (anchor test), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 12 :5 and :117 (bond and isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: "three conditions, the world is the anchor, independence needs a record, agreement is a filter."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete experimental reading: a selective probe, a causal intervention with specificity, a bond-discriminating design, and cross-seed convergence with provenance.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the requirement that the instrument already return a reading (Fig 6 :32), which the shorthand compresses into "build a probe";
- the requirement that the record separate convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :51), which the shorthand compresses into "independent";
- the requirement that the union be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54), which the shorthand compresses into "three conditions".

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the world is the anchor and a declared label is not grounded. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
