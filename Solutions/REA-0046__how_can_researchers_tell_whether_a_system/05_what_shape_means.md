## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the answer. It constrains; it does not fill. It is also what steers the search, because the question itself acts as the anchor that says "are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"

For REA-0046 the shape is NOT "this neuron means dog." It is the three-condition correspondence test bound in `README.md` and section 0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:

> a correspondence claim is settled only when an anchor that does not update pays for information, causal role and structure, and when independent frames converge with a recorded lineage.

That statement is small and descriptive enough to be checked before any particular representation is named. It is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary bootstrapping: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer." Start with the nearest vocabulary you have (probe, intervention, decodability) and let the residual sharpen it.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the shape is written in effect terms: what would change contact, not what "meaning" is in the abstract.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach email in `02` sends a description of the test, not the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here that means: the test's refuter and its shape are the same object, and that is the signal to stop.
- Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The shorthand "anchor, independence, structure" is a lossy condensation; the round-trip gap R_T is enumerated in `11_audit.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." From *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16): "Contact Is the Distinction Resolver" and "the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted." Applied here: a representation claim becomes nameable when an intervention resolves the distinction.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06) sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And it warns that the choice of primitives is visible but not neutral: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question that means: calling a decoded feature "the representation of W" is a binding from the system's frame (or the researcher's frame) onto an observed regularity. It is not a derivation, and declaring it does not ground it.

## 4. The metamerism problem, applied to representations

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument that detects colour reads green but "it cannot comment on the source." The same green can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The instrument "demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Apply this to REA-0046. A probe reads "W is decodable from X." That reading cannot comment on the source. The same decodability can arise from the representation encoding W, from the probe memorizing the task (Hewitt and Liang's result), from a shared training corpus, or from superposition (Elhage et al.). Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (causal intervention, control tasks, independent seeds with a recorded lineage), not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the crucial limit: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." So even a representation that passes every available test is not proven identical to the world structure; the record of the route is what keeps the result checkable.

## 5. Q2 of this question: what counts as a meaningful structure?

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (2026-10-22) states the framework's own division of labour: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" and "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." It also says "Independent discoveries count most."

For REA-0046 this means "meaningful" cannot be a property of the internal pattern alone. It is a property of the representation-and-habitat relation, where the habitat is the world structure the system is in contact with. Ten seeds trained on the same corpus are one opinion said ten times; independent discovery with a recorded provenance is the stronger witness. This answers the "meaningful" half of the question in the same terms as the "correspond" half: contact, not consensus.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

Consequently the outreach artifacts never gather prestigious names to endorse the shape, never rank researchers by likelihood of agreement, and never treat a venue's status as evidence. A submission venue gives the claim a budget to be tried. The ground, an intervention, settles it.

## 7. What licenses the three conditions, and what does not

| condition | licensed by | not licensed by |
|---|---|---|
| information | a selective probe with a control task that returns a reading | raw probe accuracy; the system's own output |
| causal role | intervention with specificity, checked against the world | correlation; activation magnitude; fluent explanation |
| structure | a relational test showing the bond survives across rearrangements | a feature list; superposition-compressed unit readings |
| all three together | an anchor that does not update, plus independent frames with a record | agreement, prestige, or a single frame's self-report |

The table is a shape, not a recipe. It says what would have to be true of a check; it does not say which representation passes.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And from *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* (2026-08-19): "because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself"; outputs "must be seen as simulacra"; and a reported fit "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any representation, and they cannot settle REA-0046. The honest verdict is the shape, the refuters, and the instruments that would close it.

## 9. Corrections to my own earlier reading

| earlier reading | status |
|---|---|
| "shape, not a name" forbids naming any decoded feature | **Narrowed.** The canonical phrase governs what a *missing primitive* would look like. Here the shape governs the criterion (the test), not a ban on discussing features already observed. Naming a measured feature is a binding and must be recorded as such. |
| "a probe is frame-internal, therefore useless" | **Narrowed.** A self-score cannot be a verdict (Fig 1 :27), but a selective probe is a worse instrument already returning a reading (Fig 6 :32). It is an input to refinement, not a disqualified quantity. |

| "indiscernible after all tests means the representation is the structure" | **Refused.** "Indiscernibility is not identity." The lineage is kept precisely when the copy is perfect. |
