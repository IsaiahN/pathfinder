## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim "internal representation X corresponds to world structure W." Option A: treat a decodable signal, a fluent output, or a consensus of researchers as sufficient. Option B: treat correspondence as a three-condition union settled by an anchor that does not update, with agreement as a budget filter and independent convergence as a record-dependent witness. What each leads to: A confuses a frame-internal score with a verdict and manufactures the disqualifying correlation; B yields a checkable shape, written refuters, and an honest stop at the instrument limit.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The claim relating X to W. Arity: binary relation in form, triadic in operation (X, W, the instrument linking them). Symmetry: aboutness is directional and not recoverable from content, because convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Scale: X at the system's internal level, W at the environment level, the instrument spans them. Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the system's internal frame.** Actors: features, activations, circuits, the library Γ, the weighting w. Currency: prediction residual, description length, activation magnitude, probe accuracy. Anchor: none available from inside. It is a frame, not a domain with a nameable anchor; its self-scores are disqualified.
- **Domain 2, the world or ground.** Actors: world structures, the environment, interventions. Currency: measurements, counterfactual changes, behavioural consequences. Anchor: the world, which does not update on what any frame thinks. The only domain with a genuine anchor.
- **Domain 3, the researcher or verification frame.** Actors: researchers, probes, independent frames (seeds, architectures, labs), instruments. Currency: agreement and residual across frames. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run the seats draw on partly overlapping evidence pools, so their agreement is limited.
- **Partition or union:** union of information, causal role and structure; unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Within the conditions: information first, then causal role, then structure, with the record kept throughout.

Strain test applied: a reading that Fig 1 disqualifies self-scores while Fig 6 permits a probe as a refined instrument is not strained. Fig 1 concerns verdict weight; Fig 6 concerns instrument input. Different quantities, so no re-census is owed. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Correspondence is a three-condition union settled by an anchor that does not update. The world is the anchor, a declared label is not grounded, and a mirror cannot correct you. A selective probe is a valid worse instrument that fails to resolve; causal intervention is the sharper instrument built on it; the bond is load-bearing and a feature list records only half of the claim. Independent convergence is the strongest witness only when a record separates it from adoption, and agreement is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. The framework returns this shape with pre-registered refuters and stops at the limit of what the instruments discriminate. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three checkable sub-claims and their refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three-condition shape is a protocol, not a primitive. A failed correspondence can be recorded without withdrawing the method, and a claimed correspondence that fails a new intervention can be refused by adding a term rather than deleting one (Fig 6 amendment on the record only growing).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that settles whether a representation's decodable signal is representation-borne, causally decisive and relation-preserving would move a specific claim toward `grounded`; a new intervention that breaks a representation which passed all four tests would refute the shape itself; a figure re-census showing that the three conditions reduce to one lever would collapse the union reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
