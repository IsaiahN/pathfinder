# The Tether's answer: what evidence is needed to trace a claim from observation to policy

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain evidence classes. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No particular claim's trajectory was traced.

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold an endpoint (a theory, an institutional position, a public policy) and want the route a claim took to reach it. The artifact does not carry the route: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* *"It is recoverable from the lineage."* (Fig 13 :128-129). The question is therefore not "which documents exist" but "what form must the evidence have to discriminate among routes?" That is a shape question.

## 2. The shape of the evidence needed

1. **Per-item, not aggregated.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Each reading, term, review and ruling is tracked separately. A period consensus is frame-internal and cannot score itself.
2. **Bonded and ordered, not an ingredient list.** Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* What must be recorded is which bond held (sequence, production, subtraction, comparison, negation), with dates. Citation adjacency is not a bond.
3. **Provenance at every import.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. So notebooks, correspondence, grant records and dated drafts are required, not just the published argument.
4. **Refusals included.** Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Rejected hypotheses, failed replications and withdrawn standards are part of the evidence, with dates. Nothing is deleted; the reach falls because something was added.
5. **Selection between rooms, not a roster of endorsements.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* For the institutional stage, record which room differed, which residual it surfaced, and what the ground returned.
6. **Ruling provenance and the convention crossing.** Fig 10 :62-63: *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom. The residual it answered, the reasoning, the date, in a record the seats below can read."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. For policy, record both the decision's lineage and the moment a neutral convention became an outcome-decider.
7. **Habitat enumerated to far orders.** Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The effect that matters may be three steps out, as in the DDT example (Fig 11 :52).
8. **Independent low-overlap frames and measured gaps.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Ten archives from one press release are one witness. Each crossing carries an R_T that must be measured, not assumed zero.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a named archive. The framework returns a shape, not a name. No specific repository is asserted to be necessary or sufficient.
- Not a narrative for any actual claim. No historical case was traced here.
- Not that agreement or prestige establishes a route. Fig 7 :35 and Fig 2 :35 (*"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*) forbid it.
- Not that a complete record is possible. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Where the record is silent, the output is a described residual.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an archive, a measurement, or an outcome.

## 4. What would settle it

The ground, not more argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not a further debate, resolved the case. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Attach a real case** and attempt the trace with the five evidence layers. Does the lineage discriminate among the competing routes, or do the routes remain indistinguishable?
2. **Measure R_T** at one membrane crossing (observation to theory, or institution to policy). Is the gap nonzero and enumerated, or is the transform lossless?
3. **Run the refuter.** Exhibit an artifact with an injective route mapping, a lossless crossing, a provenance-free settled trajectory, or a successes-only correct trace. Any one narrows or withdraws the claim.
4. **Check independence.** Are the archives low-overlap, or do they all descend from one report?

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Here the five evidence layers are a shape, not a discovery about any particular archive. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (local figures README). An incomplete trace is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not reconstruct any particular claim's history, and it does not name a required archive. It says that an endpoint cannot carry its own route, so tracing a claim from observation to theory to institution to policy needs lineage rather than documents alone: per-item readings, the bonds and order between them, provenance of imports, refusals with dates, selection between independent rooms, provenance of policy rulings, and the moment a convention began to decide an outcome. It requires the habitat to be enumerated out to the order where the effect actually lands, independent low-overlap frames, and a measured loss at each crossing, and it treats silence in the record as a described residual rather than an invented continuity. What it offers is the shape the evidence must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the crossings where the ground must be consulted. The case stays open until a ground settles it.
