## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what evidence would let a historian trace a claim's movement across four slots: observation, theory, institution, public policy. Option A: the endpoint artifact and its surrounding documents are sufficient. Option B: the artifact cannot carry its route, so lineage evidence is required, and the framework returns only the shape of that evidence. What each leads to: A yields a retrospective narrative that cannot discriminate among isomeric routes and spends the independence of the people checking it; B yields a checkable set of evidence classes, written refuters, and an honest stop where the record is silent.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between an endpoint we hold and the route we want. Arity: four slots, so a dependency chain, not one question. Symmetry: a route is ordered (`→ sequence`, not `+ conjunction`); the artifact-to-route map is many-to-one and lossy, so distinct routes can be isomers (Fig 13 :121). Scale: first order inside a stage, second order at each membrane crossing.
- **Domain 1, observation / ground contact.** Actors: instruments, readings, the ground. Currency: per-slot residuals. Anchor: the ground, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). Question type: is there a live reading and a stable anchor.
- **Domain 2, theory / composition.** Actors: frames, Γ, candidate terms, bonds, imports. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ) and settlement. Anchor: the ground via settlement. Question type: which terms were minted, imported or refused, and in what order.
- **Domain 3, institution / room chain.** Actors: institutions, peer frames, editors, prestige. Currency: agreement and selection between rooms. Anchor: should be the ground, often collapses into agreement. Question type: did the next room differ and get selected against the residual.
- **Domain 4, policy / convention / habitat.** Actors: policymakers, seats, public, habitat actors. Currency: conventions, decisions, consequences. Anchor: the ground (outcomes); *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47). Question type: when did a convention become an outcome-decider, and what was the far cascade.
- **Domain 5, historian's verification frame.** Actors: archivists, source critics, independent frames. Currency: credibility and independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run every seat draws on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle observation before theory before institution before policy (Fig 3 :20). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Measure the round-trip gap at each crossing (Fig 4 :30).

Strain test applied: Fig 3 (ordered chain) and Fig 9 (union/unbundle) govern different objects, a dependency chain and an overlapping compound, so there is no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The evidence needed is lineage evidence, and the framework returns its shape, not a named archive. At observation: per-item readings and a nameable, non-updating anchor. At theory: bonds and order, provenance of imports with source gate and shadow test, and refusals with dates. At institution: selection between independent rooms, not a roster of endorsements. At policy: ruling provenance plus the moment a convention became an outcome-decider, with the habitat enumerated to the order where the effect lands. Across all layers: low-overlap frames, chain of custody, measured round-trip gaps, and described residuals where the record is silent. Option A fails because the endpoint cannot carry its route and because many routes produce the same endpoint. This is a `general` shape with `specified` per-domain classes, and it is ungrounded. No particular historical case was traced.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a retrospective narrative instead of a lineage and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The five evidence layers are removable or revisable without touching the figure text; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state the non-claims. A published account can be superseded by a better-sourced trace, and no primitive is withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An artifact from which the route is uniquely recoverable by inspection; a lossless transform at a membrane crossing; a trajectory settled without provenance or independent frames; a correct trace from successes alone with every refusal omitted; or a re-census that shows the four stages are exchangeable. Any of these fires a pre-registered refuter. Until one does, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
