## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described the residual sends the source instead of the characterisation. Lead with the residual and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* What may cross to an outside reader is a reusable method with its own refuter, not a finished narrative.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would expect to see in a genuine trace before giving them the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35**: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* A Society or journal accepting the piece is selection into a room, not a verdict on the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing the theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figures link and the local extracts. Their later agreement is a conversation, never corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if a real case is attached and survives the refuter. As currently drafted, this package carries an output shape, not a traced case. Attach a case before sending.
