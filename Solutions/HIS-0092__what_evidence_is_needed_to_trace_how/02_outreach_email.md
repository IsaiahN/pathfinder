# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding about the account

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until a real case has been attached to the residual below. The shape claim alone is a method, not a result. If the case does not survive the refuter, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** What would you count as missing if a claim's route from observation to policy were reconstructed?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about how a claim travels. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output fails, there is nothing further to send.
>
> **The residual (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the classes):** an endpoint is in hand, a theory, an institutional position, or a public policy. We want the route a claim took to get there. The artifact cannot carry that route. As the figure text puts it, *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* *"It is recoverable from the lineage."* So the evidence a trace needs is lineage: per-item readings, the bonds and order between them, provenance of imports, the refusals with dates, the selection events between institutions, the provenance of policy rulings, and the round-trip gap at each crossing. That is a shape of evidence, not a named archive.
>
> **What this does not claim:** that a particular claim followed any particular route; that a citation trail is the same as a lineage; that institutional endorsement establishes truth; that a complete record is possible. Where the record is silent, the honest output is a described residual, not a filled gap.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim** (written before this check):
> 1. an endpoint artifact from which the route is uniquely recoverable by inspection, so the lineage adds nothing;
> 2. a transform between two adjacent stages shown to be lossless, so no round-trip gap need be measured;
> 3. a trajectory settled against the ground with no provenance records and no independent frames, and not later overturned;
> 4. a correct trajectory recovered from successes alone with every refusal omitted.
>
> I wrote those down before sending this. If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the material is below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, canonical public repo: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Figures text with line numbers (local extract used for all citations): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`

> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a historical case you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
