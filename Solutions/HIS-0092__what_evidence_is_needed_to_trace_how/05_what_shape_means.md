## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be instantiated. For HIS-0092 the shape is the form of the evidence a trace must have, not a list of named archives and not a reconstructed narrative for any particular claim. "Lineage evidence" is a shape. "The Oxford archives" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for *"new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms."* The searcher *"must become a better searcher."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 5 :66: *"Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working."* The pre-registered refuter in `01` §3 is the shape made bindable.

Applied here: the residual was stated in effect terms (endpoint artifact versus route) before any field or source was chosen. The five evidence layers were then derived, not looked up.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a historical trace, the ground is the measurement, the archive, the dated record, the outcome. A plausible narrative is not a settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test.

The local README adds the stopping rule: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Naming a specific document as *the* required evidence would convert a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the endpoint cannot name its own source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. An instrument that detects green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or *"a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

A public policy is such a reading. From the policy text alone you cannot comment on whether it came from a measurement, a theoretical commitment, an institutional selection, or a political decision. Several routes create the same endpoint. What is owed is a discriminator, that is, lineage evidence, not a name assigned from the projection.

The same essay gives the honest stop: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* An instrument that *"can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 5. What the shape is for this question

**The evidence needed has this shape:** per-item and unaggregated; bonded and ordered, not an ingredient list; provenanced at each import; inclusive of refusals and retractions; selected between independent rooms rather than endorsed by a crowd; carrying ruling provenance and the convention-to-outcome crossing; enumerating the habitat to the order where the effect lands; and measured for loss at every membrane crossing. Where any of this is absent, the output is a described residual, not an invented continuity.

This is a `general` shape. Turning it into a case-specific checklist is a `specified` step and requires a ground, which this package does not supply.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of prestigious historians who agree with the account is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
