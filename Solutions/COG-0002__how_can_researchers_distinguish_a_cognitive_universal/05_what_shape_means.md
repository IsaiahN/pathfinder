## 1. A shape is a constraint on the test, not a list of universals

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is **the form of a discriminator**, not a roster of shared traits. It says: any acceptable test must establish independence of the common cause, equivalence of the instrument, per-slot reporting, habitat variation, and a provenance record that separates convergence from copying. It does not say that color terms, face recognition or any other candidate is a universal. That would be a name where only a shape is available.

## 2. Why the observation cannot settle it: the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the exact mechanism:

> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"
> "the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."
> "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
> "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

This is the question in the framework's own vocabulary. A cross-cultural survey reads green: a pattern is present here, there and everywhere. It cannot say whether the source is a shared architecture (one green light), a common practical problem (yellow plus blue), a shared institution (RGB), or a copying history (CMYK). Arguing about the reading cannot settle the source. *It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved metamerism.* Here the corresponding spectrometer is a design that decorrelates the causes, plus an instrument shown to measure the same construct in each group.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (inherited from the exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So declaring "cognitive universal" as the primitive does not make it one; it makes the choice visible and therefore refusable.

## 3. How a shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Stop when the narrowed form is checkable, not when a name is found. *Library of Babel*: "the finish line will also be the starting place."

Applied here: describe P as an effect (under task T, group G responds W) before choosing societies. Then borrow the vocabulary of the discriminator from low-overlap fields: independent contrasts from evolutionary biology, measurement invariance from psychometrics, habitat substitution from ecology. Do not borrow a label for the universal from the same evidence pool that produced the observation.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here: a decorrelated design and a mechanism-discriminating instrument return a stable answer.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here: phylogenetic comparative methods and invariance testing are imports; they must be shown to explain the specific residual (non-independence, construct non-equivalence) before they are used.

Until one of those, naming a particular pattern as universal is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 5. Why the framework cannot certify its own list

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (inherited) supplies the room-per-problem image: a commons of methods, ground under all. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." So:

- a cross-cultural literature that has not checked dependence has not searched;
- a literature that has checked and found no environmental correlate has returned `unreached at this budget`, not `universal`;
- the framework cannot manufacture a universal by aggregating more agreeing cases (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and mode

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous societies or famous researchers who agree with a universality claim is the named collapse. What the claim needs is a budget to be tried against a decorrelated ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. It is not a test of any real pattern and must be labelled as such.

## 7. Correction to my own §1 and §4

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any candidate universal. That overstates it. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame. Candidates such as face recognition or color categorization are not missing primitives of the framework; they are contents of the cross-cultural frame. What is forbidden is declaring them universal on the strength of a reading that cannot discriminate sources, and treating the declaration as a derivation. The corrected claim is: the framework returns the shape of the discriminator; which pattern occupies the universal slot is decided by the ground or by a provenanced import, never by prevalence. Both the earlier and the corrected readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
