## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three conditions compose one observation. A mechanism-side cause U, a habitat-side cause E, and an instrument M. Symmetry: the observation is symmetric under U <-> E; swapping them changes nothing in the data. Only provenance breaks the symmetry. Scale: individual slot to society frame to cross-cultural chain, with a lossy transform. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57), and it must be unbundled before search.
- **Domain 1, the comparison frame.** Actors: societies, samples, lineages, instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: behavior under varied conditions, never agreement. Question type: union until split into a U loop and an E loop.
- **Domain 2, the measurement chain.** Actors: items, tasks, translations, raters. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: whether the instrument measures the same construct. Question type: partition by link. This is the middle link that has no obvious instrument and therefore attracts the least attention (Fig 3 :24-27).
- **Domain 3, the habitat.** Actors: environmental and institutional conditions, migrants, natural experiments, test harnesses. Currency: contact. Anchor: which actor changes the residual. Question type: union of agent-side and habitat-side contributions.

Order by dependency: describe the effect before choosing frames (Fig 9 :27-28); instrument before verdict (Fig 3 :27); decorrelate before calling a universal; habitat substitution last, and reported as substitution (Fig 11 :66).

**My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading.** I reached it from a different starting figure, Fig 9's union/partition split plus Fig 3's dependency order, whereas the primary began with Fig 2's collapse 2 and Fig 8's identical-contents rule. Different starting points, same boundaries, low overlap between routes within the framework. It is still one evidence pool, so the agreement is expected and carries no independent weight.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :19-21 and :31-32 (common cause and independence), Fig 8 :50-52 (identical contents, provenance), Fig 3 :27 (instrument link), Fig 11 :39 and :66 (agent and habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: "prevalence is not evidence; buy independence; establish invariance; report per slot; vary the habitat".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete study protocol: sample societies, compile a connection matrix, run multi-group CFA, apply phylogenetic contrasts, report item-level residuals, run a migrant or natural-experiment comparison.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **unit of independence**. The shorthand says "independent societies"; the figures require frames whose closures differ and, in the world literature, independent *events of change*. Related societies can be many frames in name and one in evidence. The protocol must specify the connection matrix and the contrast method or the shorthand is dead reckoning.
- the **precondition order**. The shorthand lists invariance as one item in a sequence. Fig 3 :19 says a link above the break cannot rescue one below it: if scalar invariance fails, the decorrelation step has nothing to operate on.
- the ** substitution caveat**. The shorthand says "vary the habitat". Fig 11 :66 says isolation substitutes a habitat and does not remove it. The protocol must state what was failed to reproduce and what was unintentionally introduced.
- the **source-discriminating instrument**. The shorthand implies decorrelation settles the case. It cannot. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says the reading cannot comment on the source and that the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists. The protocol can return `unreached`, and must be allowed to.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: agreement under a shared cause is not evidence. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
