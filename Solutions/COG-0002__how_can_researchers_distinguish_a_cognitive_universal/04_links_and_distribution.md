## Wider literature that bears directly on the question

1. **Galton's problem is the exact structural analogue of E.** Similarity between societies may be inheritance, borrowing or independent development. Only the last is confirmation in the law-seeking sense. The statistical fixes (effective sample size, spatial autocorrelation, phylogenetic comparative methods) are literal implementations of "buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" (Fig 2 :42). Cite this first when explaining why a count of societies is not evidence.
2. **Phylogenetic comparative methods outperform raw regression when cases are non-independent.** Currie, Greenhill and Mace 2010 show that non-phylogenetic analysis regularly returns spurious correlations where a PCM correctly reports independence. This is world corroboration for Fig 2 :31-32 at maximum independence. It is not a derivation of the figure; the figure decides, the study corroborates.
3. **Measurement invariance is the instrument for the lower link.** Fischer and Karl 2019 separate configural, metric and scalar invariance and explicitly permit the conclusion that groups cannot be compared. That is Fig 3 :27 made operational: a link with no instrument is not a link that works.
4. **Similarity does not prove innateness.** The cultural-universal reference states the non-nativist explanation directly. This is a world statement of the residual, not a figure line, and is recorded as such.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the residual and the refuter. The links come after the recipient has recorded a verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the packet.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Do not send to a long list to make the claim look well supported. One careful reader is a witness; a thousand agreeing readers are a convention.
- **Fig 4 :22-23 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Stage 3 offers a reusable method, not a conclusion.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) the verdict, recorded; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
