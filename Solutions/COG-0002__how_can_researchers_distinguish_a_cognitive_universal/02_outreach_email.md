# Outreach draft: check the discriminator before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you can state the residual and the refuter in your own words. The offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not survive.

---

**Subject:** Would this test separate a cognitive universal from a shared environment?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one methodological claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to trust anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when a pattern is found in many societies, the observation is identical under two explanations. One says the pattern comes from a cognitive architecture shared by all humans. The other says it comes from similar environments or institutions, so the societies that show it are independent copies of the same cause. Counting societies cannot tell these apart. Several causes create the same reading. What is owed is a test that discriminates sources.
>
> **The shape claim:** agreement is evidence for a universal only to the degree that the societies whose agreement is cited are independent of the common cause. Two societies with a shared language, media system, curriculum or colonial history are one witness, not two. The test I am proposing is: (1) write the provenance record of connections among the societies first, (2) establish that the instrument measures the same construct in each group, (3) decorrelate the environmental and institutional conditions by independent contrasts, migrants, natural experiments or independently assembled samples, (4) report the residual per task and subgroup, never only as a cross-cultural average, and (5) check whether the pattern survives when the habitat changes.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular pattern is or is not a universal. That a larger cross-cultural sample settles anything. That a failure to find an environmental explanation proves innateness. That a lab result is a pure test of the person rather than of the person in a substituted habitat. That I have measured anything; this is a method shape, not a result.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. An instrument that reads the cognitive mechanism directly, shows invariance across varied habitats, and does not depend on independence of societies.
> 2. A pattern that survives documented decorrelation of the common cause *and* converges independently in a second sample with no contact, with both provenance records shown.
> 3. Scalar invariance rejected while the universality claim is maintained anyway.
> 4. Slot-level residuals that still covary with the environmental variable after decorrelation.
> 5. A pattern that disappears under habitat substitution and returns only in the original habitat.
>
> If any of these holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text, verbatim with line numbers: [link you host]
> - Verification packet with the three checkable sub-claims: [link you host]
> - Public methods literature on non-independence and measurement invariance: [link you host]
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get (`general`, `specified` or `grounded`). A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage (in practice three-stage) offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. **Record the verdict before any theory is shown.**
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, opt in only:** offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A later expression of agreement is never pooled as corroboration. By Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", and by Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
