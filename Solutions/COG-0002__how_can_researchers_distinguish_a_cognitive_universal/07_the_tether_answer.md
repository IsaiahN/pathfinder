# The Tether's answer: how to distinguish a universal from a shared environment

**Mode: `general` on the shape of the discriminator, `specified` on the prediction that decorrelating the environment preserves a true universal and dissolves an environmentally caused pattern. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A pattern P found in many societies is compatible with a shared architecture (U), a shared environment or institution (E), and an instrument artifact (M). The reading is the same under all three. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The residual is the source question, and it is a **union compound**: U, E and M are three conditions in one observation, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Prevalence is not evidence.** Two societies that never talk and still agree because they share a condition are one witness. Fig 2 :19 "they never talk, and still agree" and Fig 2 :20-21 "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." The number of societies is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60), never a witness for the verdict.

2. **Independence is the discriminator.** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." And Fig 2 :31-32 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." A universal is supported only by agreement that survives decorrelation of the common cause. Concretely: compile the provenance record of connections; use independent contrasts or phylogenetic comparative methods for historical data; use migrants, natural experiments and independently assembled samples for contemporary data; count independent events of change, not societies carrying a trait. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

3. **Convergence and copying look the same; only the record separates them.** Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." So the provenance record is not documentation. It is the evidence. Without it the case is undecidable from the contents.

4. **Fix the instrument before the verdict.** Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Establish configural, metric and scalar invariance and emic equivalence before interpreting a cross-cultural difference or similarity. Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Define P as an effect before choosing the societies, because Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

5. **Report per slot, never only as an average.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." And Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Here a slot is a task, a domain, a subgroup or an individual. Slot-level residuals that track the environment are the signature of E hiding inside an apparent universal.

6. **Vary the habitat, and admit that substitution is not removal.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." And Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Natural variation and controlled experiment are two different instruments; neither alone settles the source.

7. **Anchor on the ground, not on agreement.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement is the thing to be explained; the anchor is behavior under varied conditions.

8. **Honest stop.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No environmental correlate found at this budget returns `unreached`, not `universal`. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 3. The test in one sequence

1. State P as an effect, with task and population, before sampling.
2. Compile the provenance record: contact, descent, shared institutions, shared instruments, convenience sampling.
3. Establish invariance per item and per group. If scalar invariance fails, stop; there is no common construct to compare.
4. Model the dependence: effective sample size, spatial autocorrelation, phylogenetic contrasts, or independent replication.
5. Re-estimate P after decorrelation. Does its magnitude shrink, vanish, or persist?
6. Vary the habitat: migrants, natural experiments, controlled substitution.
7. Report per slot, with the refuter and the mode attached.
8. Return one of: `candidate universal` (survives all of the above with recorded independence), `environmental` (tracks the cause after decorrelation), `instrument artifact` (invariance fails), or `unreached at this budget` (no discriminating instrument available).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular cognitive capacity is universal. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that a large and diverse sample is sufficient. Independence is not the same as variety; a diverse sample of related societies is still one witness.
- Not that a failed search for an environmental cause proves innateness. Fig 8 :39.
- Not that a laboratory result isolates the person from the habitat. Fig 11 :66.
- Not that agreement among experts settles anything. Fig 2 :35.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A ground reading, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the spectrometer resolved metamerism: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here that means:

- a mechanism-discriminating instrument that reads the cognitive source rather than the behavioral projection, with invariance across habitats;
- a natural experiment in which the supposed common cause is absent and the pattern persists, with provenance showing independence;
- a decorrelated sample in which the pattern covaries with an environmental variable and disappears when that variable is removed;
- failure of scalar invariance, which settles M and ends the comparison.

Standing refuters from `01` §4: a direct mechanism instrument; shared-cause agreement surviving documented decorrelation and independent convergence; invariance rejected while universality is maintained; structured slot residuals after decorrelation; reversal under habitat substitution.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The discriminator can be specified, but this derivation has no data and took no measurement. It returns the shape a settling test must have, the order of its links, and the refuters written in advance. It stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical figures README). A verdict of `candidate` is the strongest honest output here.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Prevalence is not proof of a universal. When a pattern is found in many societies, the observation looks the same whether it comes from a shared human architecture or from similar environments and institutions; several paths create one reading. The Tether's answer is a shape for a discriminator: write the provenance record of how the societies are connected; show that the instrument measures the same construct in each group; decorrelate the environmental and institutional causes by independent contrasts, migrants, natural experiments or independently assembled samples; report the residual per task and subgroup instead of hiding it in an average; and check whether the pattern survives a change of habitat. Only agreement that survives those steps counts, because convergent derivation and copied agreement look identical in the data and only the record separates them. A failure to find an environmental cause is reported as `unreached at this budget`, never converted into a universal. The refuters are written in advance, and nothing here is a measurement or a claim about any particular cognitive capacity.
