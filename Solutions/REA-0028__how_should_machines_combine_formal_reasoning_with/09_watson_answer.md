## QUESTION

How should machines combine formal reasoning with intuitive, approximate, or context-sensitive judgments?

Restated as an effect: a formal library Γ holds symbols, atoms and a checker. At slot `s` the prediction Γ(b,a) misses the observation o′, and the term φ* that would close the gap is outside closure(Γ). A second frame with a differing closure registers structure at `s` that Γ cannot name. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The question is how to couple the two frames so the result is settled by the ground rather than by their agreement. Anchor check: a nameable anchor exists, the task outcome or verifiable reward, listed as an anchor kind in Fig 2 :29. So this is a well-formed question and not yet a `gap`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among formal frame Γ, approximate frame, and ground, carrying a residual indexed by slot. At least three operands plus an error signal. Symmetry: the two machine frames are symmetric in rank and asymmetric in role (Fig 13 :4-5); independence is a property of the pair (Fig 2 :31). Scale: the single inference loop, a population of frames or rooms, and the lossy membrane between them (Fig 4). The residual is cross-scale by construction.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated or single-source or gap)

**D1 Formal frame, status triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 6 and Fig 10, distinct roles).**

> *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* (Fig 8 :32)
> *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* (Fig 6 :21)
> *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42)

Reading: the formal frame cannot manufacture the missing atom, and its verifier must not author what it checks. Mode `general` for idempotence and verifier non-access. Refuter: a new atom produced by internal composition alone, or a verifier that reconstructs its own witness and the claim still settles.

**D2 Approximate or context frame, status triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 11, import source vs habitat).**

> *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23)
> *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* (Fig 6 :24)
> *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* (Fig 11 :40)

Reading: the fast frame is valuable only insofar as its closure differs and its contact changes. Mode `specified` for the machine application, `general` for the capability claim. Refuter: a fast output that is provably a rebinding of existing atoms yet expands the reach.

**D3 Combination seat or membrane, status triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 4 and Fig 8, low overlap).**

> *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)
> *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22)
> *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30)
> *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* (Fig 8 :45)

Reading: the combination is two independent frames ranged against one ground, coupled by a directional lossy membrane with a measured round trip, not a merge and not a vote. Mode `general`. Refuter: two frames with high overlap whose agreement settles a verdict, or a copy across the membrane claimed as transfer with no gap measured.

**D4 The ground, status triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10, anchor rule vs channel decay).**

> *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29)
> *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* (Fig 2 :38)
> *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24)

Reading: the ground is the task outcome, not model agreement and not human approval for a correctness question. Mode `specified` for the machine case. Refuter: a restatement of the question that is constitutively about human judgment, which would make human judgment the anchor.

**Method order, status triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 9 and Fig 5).**

> *"Residual first, frame second."* (Fig 8 :53)
> *"describe it before you go looking, or"* (Fig 9 :27) *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :28)
> *"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* (Fig 5 :66)
> *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60)

Reading: characterize the residual before naming any architecture, bind the sought-for shape as a refutable term before the run, and never let a ranking hand you a verdict.

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** machines should couple the two as independent frames ranged separately against one immovable ground, with the fast frame proposing terms only as provenanced imports that name the gap they explain, the slow frame verifying what it did not produce and refusing what it holds, the coupling measured by a round trip, and the sought-for shape bound as a refutable term before the run. The blend, the vote, and the self-certifying architecture are refused. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `general` on the barriers, `specified` on the coupling shape. The world sources corroborate the shape from five low-overlap fields; the neuro-symbolic pool does not settle the architecture, and that silence is recorded as F12 rather than repaired by a name. One world/figure conflict was returned to census: the neuro-symbolic literature's framing of "combination" as integration was re-mapped from conjunction to a compound of production, sequence and refusal, and the verdict was not flipped. Which concrete architecture settles on the ground is a declared `gap`.

- **Cost if wrong:** a machine shipped with a blend will treat agreement as verification and fail exactly where the ground is unforgiving and novel; a machine shipped with pure formalism cannot reach φ* at all. Worse, a self-certifying version will report high confidence while the channel to the ground is broken, and a broken channel looks like a working one until the drift is large. The cost is real but bounded and reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered refuters, in particular (1) a new atom produced inside Γ by internal composition alone, (2) high-overlap frames whose agreement settles a verdict the ground pays, (3) a checker that reconstructs its own claim and the claim still settles, (4) an import with no source gate or shadow test that still expands reach, or (5) a specific architecture derived from the figures that settles on a ground. A source from a genuinely different evidence pool showing that a single blended frame reaches a ground-settled new atom with no import would also reverse it.
