# The Tether's answer: how should machines combine formal reasoning with intuitive, approximate, or context-sensitive judgments?

**Mode: `general` on the barrier findings, `specified` on the coupling shape, `gap` on which specific architecture settles on the ground. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A machine holds a formal library Γ. At a slot `s` its prediction misses what the world returns, and `φ*` is outside the reach: Fig 6 :8, *"not composable: REACHABILITY fails."* A second, approximate frame registers structure at `s` that Γ cannot name. The residual is the gap between what the formal frame can compose and what the ground actually requires, indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). It is a union compound, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## 2. The shape

1. **Search inside the formal frame cannot close it.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* This is why "add more rules" is not an answer. `general`.
2. **A blend is not a combination.** Fig 8 :5: *"→ vocabulary, never an atom"*; Fig 8 :14: *"their union adds nothing."* Feeding the fast output as a soft prior into Γ adds vocabulary, not a primitive. `general`.
3. **The two frames must be independent and ranged separately against one immovable ground.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* If they share an evidence pool their agreement is one witness (Fig 2 :40). `general`.
4. **The fast frame proposes; the slow frame verifies and refuses.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* Every import carries a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13); refusal is a unary operator, not a subtraction. `specified` for the machine application.
5. **The coupling is a measured round trip, not a copy.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* `general`.
6. **The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary refutable term before the run.** Fig 5 :66: *"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* The five refuters below are that binding. `general`.
7. **Which concrete architecture settles on the ground is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument that tells a genuine new atom from a fast binding of an atom already held. Characterized in effect terms, not named.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a blend, a vote, or a self-certifying architecture.
- Not that human approval is the anchor for a correctness question. Fig 2 :38: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
- Not a claim that fast judgment is always bias and slow reasoning is always right. Dual-process research itself refuses that shortcut (see `09`, WORLD).
- Not a named neural symbolic design, not a system 1 / system 2 split, not a routing rule, not a training schedule. Those are bindings with zero verdict weight until provenanced.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the general architecture claim.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and the case was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

- **Discriminator test:** can an instrument distinguish a genuinely composed new atom from a fast rebinding of atoms already in Γ? Until it can, F12 stays open.
- **Independence test:** measure evidence-pool overlap between the two frames. If it is high, agreement is worthless as a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- **Non-access test:** can the checker reconstruct the claim it checks? If yes, it is not a checker (Fig 10 :42).
- **Round-trip test:** carry a method up, bring a head start down, measure `R_T`. If no gap is measured, the transfer is a recording, not a method (Fig 4 :31).
- **Standing pre-registered refuters:** the five in `01` section 4. If any fires, the shape is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Shape reached, instrument unreached.** The six-term physical substrate is a human's guess, and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The framework says *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"* (Fig 9 :42) and *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a hybrid architecture and does not claim the question is settled. It says a formal library cannot manufacture what its closure lacks, so a second frame with a different closure is needed; that merging the two as a soft prior adds vocabulary and not a new primitive; that the two frames must stay independent and be corrected separately by one ground they cannot move, so their agreement is never a verdict; that the fast frame may propose terms only with provenance and a stated gap, while the slow frame verifies what it did not produce and can refuse what it holds; and that the coupling is a measured round trip, because only methods cross up and only head starts come down. The sought-for shape is written down as a refutable term before the run, with five falsifiers in advance. Which concrete architecture actually settles on the ground is an open `gap`: what is owed is an instrument that can tell a genuine new atom from a fast rebinding of an old one. The shape is checkable; the name is not claimed.
