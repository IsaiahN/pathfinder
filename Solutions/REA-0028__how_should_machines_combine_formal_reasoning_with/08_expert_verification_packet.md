## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A formal library cannot manufacture what its closure lacks, so a second frame with a different closure is needed. Merging the two as a soft prior adds vocabulary rather than a primitive. The two frames must stay independent and be corrected separately by one ground they cannot move, so their agreement is never a verdict. The fast frame may propose candidate terms only with provenance and a stated gap, while the slow frame verifies what it did not produce and can refuse what it holds. The coupling is a measured round trip: only methods cross up, only head starts come down. The sought-for shape is written down as a refutable term before the run, with five falsifiers. Which concrete architecture settles on the ground is an open `gap`, characterized in effect terms as the need for a source-discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal plus engineering-facing; checkable by reading Figure 8 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** a blend of the two frames into one soft-prior system adds vocabulary, not a new atom; two frames that share an evidence pool are one witness, not two.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :5 — *"→ vocabulary, never an atom"*; Fig 8 :14 — *"their union adds nothing"*; Fig 2 :31 — *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40 — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- **Refuter:** a blended single frame that adds a genuinely new primitive to the formal closure, passes a source gate and a shadow test, and whose reach gain survives an independent check; or two frames with measured high overlap whose agreement is used as a verdict and the ground pays.

### C2 — formal-methods-facing; checkable against verification practice

- **Statement:** the verifying frame must be unable to produce what it checks, and must be able to refuse held terms; a checker that can reconstruct its own witness is not a checker.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :42 — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :43 — *"Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking."* Fig 5 :53 — *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."*
- **What to check:** in your own system, can the component that accepts or reject a candidate also generate that candidate? If so, the arrangement is the collapse this figure names. Separately, does the system have a way to withdraw a standing term, or can it only accumulate?
- **World corroboration, secondary:** Barbosa et al., CACM 2023, separate proof finding from proof checking so the checker need not be trusted. seL4, SOSP 2009, demonstrates machine-checked end-to-end verification. Alrawajfeh, 2026, *"machine checking establishes the correctness of a proof with respect to an encoded statement, while the correspondence between that statement and the intended theorem remains outside that guarantee"* — the formal checker does not reach the intended, context-sensitive claim, which is exactly why a second frame is needed.
- **Refuter:** a verifier that generates its own witness, checks it, and the claim still settles against an independent ground; or a system with no refusal operation whose reach nevertheless falls correctly after a refutation.

### C3 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 4 and Figure 6

- **Statement:** the coupling is a directional, lossy membrane with a measured round trip, and imports require provenance and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30 — *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 6 :14 — *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 6 :24 — *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*
- **Refuter:** a combination that copies outputs between frames with no round trip and claims transfer; or an import that crosses with no source gate and no shadow test and still expands reach.

### The declared `gap` (not a sub-claim, so it cannot be scored as one)

Fig 13 :135 — *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* No figure line names a hybrid architecture. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates a genuine new atom from a fast rebinding of an atom already held. If you know of such an instrument with a different evidence pool from the framework, that is the most useful failure you could report.
