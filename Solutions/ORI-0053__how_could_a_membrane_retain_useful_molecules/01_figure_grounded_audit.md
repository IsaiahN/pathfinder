## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks for a boundary that does two things that pull against each other:

- **Retain.** Keep polymers, catalysts and genetic molecules concentrated inside, so that a composition can persist and be composable over (Fig 12 :106 *"Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."*).
- **Exchange.** Let nutrient building blocks in and inhibitory side-products out. Mansy 2010 states the failure plainly: *"the permeability barrier would inhibit chemical exchange with the environment. Such an exchange is important not only for acquiring nutrient substrates for primitive metabolic processes, but also for the release of inhibitory side-products."*

Stated as an effect, R is the gap between a boundary that merely **separates** and a boundary that **discriminates**. A wall that retains everything starves; an open edge that passes everything cannot hold a composition. R is two-sided, directional and selective. The question is not "which molecule forms the boundary" but "what rule governs what crosses it."

**Direction, made an ordinary term before the run.** The sought-for shape is written down and bound here, as the formula's "Direction" requires: an answer would look like *a boundary with a typed, directional crossing rule that keeps what composes inside and imports what the inside cannot make*. Binding it now lets it accrue a residual and be refusable by the same test as everything else (Fig 5 :65-66 *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*).

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** two operands, interior and exterior, with a typed filter between them. It is also a **union compound**, not one hard problem: retention and exchange are two conditions that must both hold, and they share the membrane as their common site (Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*; Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). It has its own loop and its own ground.
- **Symmetry:** crossing is **not symmetric**. What passes inward differs from what is retained, and the same molecule changes role with the state of the compartment. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A nucleotide is nutrient when the interior can use it and loss when it leaks out; it carries no "useful" label in itself.
- **Scale:** two scales at once. Fig 4 :2 names *"The Membrane Between Scales"*; Fig 4 :3: *"The same machine at two sizes, and the rule about what may pass between them."* The other scale is the compartment itself, where space gives containment (Fig 13 :10 *"near, far, between, contained"*) and a gradient gives directedness (Fig 13 :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 13 :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*).

**Partition or union:** D2 is a union compound, so it is unbundled into retention and exchange, then recombined as a compound with its own loop. D1 and D3 are distinct domains and are not averaged into one verdict.

### Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* The chain runs:

1. A boundary rule exists and discriminates (D1). Without this there is no compartment to discuss.
2. The interior can compose behind it (D2 retention).
3. Import and export sustain that composition (D2 exchange). Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*.
4. Growth, competition and division become possible (D3, measured).

The permeability link is **not** Fig 3's invisible middle link: it has instruments (permeability coefficients, partitioning assays), so a reading of it is a reading of something, not a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).

### Strain test, applied before concluding

Fig 4 is titled "The Membrane Between Scales" and its prose is about one agent versus a population, methods crossing up and head starts crossing down. Read literally as a claim about lipid bilayers, *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction"* only fits with a strain: lipids do not carry methods. Strain test says a line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so re-census.

**Re-census.** Fig 4 :19-20 says *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"* while the kernel does not. The figure is a general boundary rule at any stratum. In D3 the actors change to amphiphiles and solutes and the currency changes to permeability and gradient; the rule (a typed, directional filter, not a wall) does not. No strain after re-census. The same reasoning applies to Fig 12's isomer line when applied to mechanisms rather than molecules: it is a level-relative shape, and the discriminator is an instrument (see `06`, where the strain is named rather than hidden).

A second apparent strain: Fig 4 :22 says *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Nutrients are neither methods nor head starts. Re-census resolves it: :22 governs the **vertical, cross-scale** membrane, whereas this question is about the **horizontal, same-scale** membrane between a compartment and its environment. The shared shape is "typed, directional passage governed by a rule"; the cast differs. I use :21 and :19-20 for the shape and mark :22 as governing another quantity, not as evidence for fatty-acid transport. This is recorded as a narrowing, not a silent discard.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

Mode is stated on each. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

### F1 - The boundary is a filter with a direction, not a wall (D1, D2)

Fig 4 :21: *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 4 :3: *"The same machine at two sizes, and the rule about what may pass between them."*

The shape an answer must have is therefore not "a membrane" (everything has a boundary) but "a boundary with a typed crossing rule." Retention and passage are not two extremes of one dial; they are two outputs of a filter. This is `general` as a structural rule (it holds at any stratum) and `specified` as applied to a protocell (it predicts that a working boundary discriminates rather than seals or leaks).

World corroboration, secondary: Mansy 2010 *"What seems to have been necessary was the appearance of a simple membrane system capable of retaining and releasing specific molecules. In short, a protocell needed to be selectively permeable."* Britannica *"A semipermeable membrane is a barrier that allows certain molecules or ions to pass through by diffusion, but blocks others."*

**Triangulated?** Yes across figures: Fig 4 (boundary shape) and Fig 6/8 (a closed set cannot sustain composition, so passage must exist) are low-overlap figures. World sources overlap heavily with each other (origins field), so they count as one witness.

**Refuter:** a compartment with a boundary that passes everything or nothing, yet sustains an composing, growing interior, breaks F1.

### F2 - A sealed compartment cannot sustain composition (D2)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*.

Nutrients are **imports**: atoms the interior cannot compose from what it holds. Waste is what must leave for the closure to remain workable. A vesicle that retained everything would be a sealed room, and Fig 8 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room"* plus Fig 9 :44 about searching and finding nothing apply: the interior cannot manufacture new kinds by rearranging what it already has. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded at this scale.

**Triangulated?** Yes: Fig 6 and Fig 8 make the same closure claim from different figures (reachability and idempotence), with Fig 11 adding the contact condition. Low overlap between figures.

**Refuter:** a vesicle sustaining growth and genome replication with zero net import across its boundary would break F2. Note the world literature supplies the positive case rather than the refutation: Mansy 2010 reports template copying inside fatty-acid vesicles when activated nucleotides are supplied externally, and *"The same conditions did not result in template copying inside of diacyl phospholipid vesicles because of the impermeability of the membrane."*

### F3 - The membrane is where a gradient is spent (D2, D3)

Fig 13 :10 *"near, far, between, contained"*; :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*; :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*

Containment (space, a state) and a concentration difference (gradient, a structure) compose a flow across the boundary. The figures do not say which ion or solute; they require that a spendable difference exists and that crossing is a flow. Ma and Yu 2025 report the modelling consequence: reduced permeability selects for *"membrane transporters, a hallmark of modern cells, would have emerged as a means for overcoming low membrane permeability"*. Mode: `general` for the composition of containment and gradient, `specified` for the protocell instance.

**Triangulated?** Fig 13 alone supplies the composition rule; the spendable-gradient claim is corroborated by the world literature (osmotic pressure, pH gradients) rather than by a second figure. Marked **single-source** for the figure verdict, world-corroborated.

**Refuter:** sustained directed exchange with zero difference of any kind across the boundary breaks F3.

### F4 - "Useful" and "waste" are roles, not labels carried by molecules (D2)

Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

This dissolves the apparent paradox in the question. The membrane does not recognise "useful"; it discriminates by physical criteria (size, charge, hydrophobicity, partition coefficient). The category "nutrient" or "waste" is assigned by the compartment's state, which changes. Mansy 2010 gives the mechanism-level version: *"Even subtle differences in partition coefficients can lead to large permeability differences that could potentially be exploited for early metabolic processes."* Mode: `general` (role relativity), `specified` (that a physical discriminant, not a semantic one, does the work).

**Triangulated?** Fig 11 plus the chemistry sources (which show selectivity arising from partition coefficients and size) are low-overlap. Figure verdict single-source (Fig 11), world corroboration distinct in kind.

**Refuter:** a boundary that selects by an intrinsic "usefulness" tag, with no physical discriminant, breaks F4.

### F5 - The framework returns a shape, not a name (D1 versus D3)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Fig 6 :12-13: *"source gate: provenance"*, *"shadow test: explains a gap"*. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.

No figure line names a fatty acid, a phospholipid, a pore or a partition coefficient. Naming any of them is a binding from the chemistry frame onto the shape, an import that must carry a source gate and a shadow test. If the binding is presented as a figure derivation, it is the exact apophenia Fig 9 :27-28 warns about: the word "membrane" appears in both the figure and the question, so any frame picked will seem to fit. Mode: `general` (a limit of the method), and it governs every substantive claim in this folder.

**Triangulated?** Yes: Fig 9 and the canonical figures README (*"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*) are two low-overlap statements of the same limit.

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a specific molecule as the retained/passed agent would break F5. Searching the extract for such a line returns none.

### F6 - The permeability reading does not determine the mechanism (D3, world/essay layer)

This is not a figure finding; it is the *Hard Problem is About Source* pattern applied to the world layer. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Mansy 2010 lists five mechanisms that can produce permeability: solubility-diffusion, transient pore, packing defect, head-group gated and lipid carrier. Chen and Walde 2010 add the K+ permeability contrast between single-chain and double-chain membranes. Several causes give the same reading ("large polymer retained, small nutrient passed"); the reading cannot identify the source. Mode: `specified` about the world literature, not a figure verdict.

**Refuter:** a measurement that discriminates the mechanism from the permeability reading alone would resolve F6 and **name the slot**; it would not break the shape claim.

### F7 - Retention and exchange are a union compound with its own loop (D2)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 9 :55-56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*

A single lever, for example "make the membrane tighter," improves retention and worsens exchange. The residue after that lever is smaller and categorically different: it is the compound, where both conditions bind at once. Ma and Yu 2025 model exactly this trade-off: phospholipid content stabilises the membrane but *"the permeability of the membrane declines in proportion to the rise in phospholipid content"*, driving selection for transport functions. Mode: `specified` for the compound, `general` for the union rule.

**Triangulated?** Fig 9 supplies the union rule; the world literature supplies the trade-off. Figure verdict single-source, world corroboration independent in method (simulation and experiment).

**Refuter:** a single lever that optimises retention and exchange with no residual compound would break F7.

### F8 - The condensation carries a measurable round trip gap (method layer)

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Symbols table, R_T: *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold."*

Sending up "filter with a direction" and bringing it back down as "fatty acid vesicles with partition-coefficient selectivity" loses at least three things that the figure shape holds: the typed direction (methods up versus head starts down, which has no chemical counterpart), the union structure of retention plus exchange, and the role-relativity of "useful." These are enumerated in `11_audit.md` as R_T. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. Mode: `general`.

**Refuter:** showing the shape maps losslessly onto the chemical description would break F8. It does not.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, as Seat Map :287 requires: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A boundary with no selective rule, passing everything or nothing, still sustains a composing and growing interior (breaks F1 and F2).
2. A compartment grows and replicates its genome with zero net import across its boundary (breaks F2).
3. A figure line names a specific molecule or pore as the answer (breaks F5).
4. The union of retention and exchange is shown to be one condition, with a single lever that optimises both and leaves no compound residue (breaks F7).
5. The round trip from the figure shape to the chemical description is shown to be lossless (breaks F8).

A fifth kind of result would **settle the slot without breaking the shape**: an instrument that discriminates which of the candidate mechanisms actually operated (F6). That would name the slot, which the framework says it cannot do from inside; it is owed to the ground, not to a further argument.

Until one of 1 to 5 fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
