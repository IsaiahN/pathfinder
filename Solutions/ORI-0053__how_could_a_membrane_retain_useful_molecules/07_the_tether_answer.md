# The Tether's answer: how a membrane can retain and still exchange

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive shape, `general` on the structural boundary rule, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 5), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A boundary that retains everything starves and accumulates inhibitors; a boundary that passes everything loses the concentrated interior that makes a protocell a candidate at all. The residual is the gap between a boundary that merely **separates** and one that **discriminates**. It is a union compound: retention and exchange are two conditions sharing the membrane, and a single lever (tighten the membrane) clears only the region where one condition holds (Fig 9 :54-57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **A boundary is a filter with a direction, not a wall.** Fig 4 :21: *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Crossing is typed and asymmetric. The answer must specify what crosses, in which direction, under what rule. A mere "semi-permeable barrier" is the functional group, not the answer.
2. **A closed interior cannot keep composing.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Nutrients are imports, the only operator that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14). Waste is the efflux that keeps the closure workable. Retention without exchange caps the composition; exchange without retention removes the thing composed.
3. **Containment and a spendable gradient compose the flow.** Fig 13 :10 *"near, far, between, contained"*; :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*; :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Influx and efflux are not mere presence on two sides; they require a difference that can be spent. Membrane growth *produces* such a gradient in the fatty-acid case (Chen and Walde 2010), so the bond is production (`⇒`), not mere sequence.
4. **"Useful" is a role, not a label carried by a molecule.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The membrane discriminates by physical criteria (size, charge, hydrophobicity, partition coefficient); the categories nutrient and waste are assigned by the compartment's state and change with it. Mansy 2010: *"Even subtle differences in partition coefficients can lead to large permeability differences."*
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* No figure line names a fatty acid, a phospholipid, a pore or a partition coefficient. Those are bindings from the chemistry frame, admitted only with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52). The five candidate mechanisms in Mansy 2010 are a metamer set: the permeability reading does not identify which one operated.
6. **The condensation carries a round trip gap.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The gap between "filter with a direction" and "fatty-acid vesicle with partition-coefficient selectivity" is enumerated in `11_audit.md`; it is not assumed zero.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ground reading. No experiment in this folder settles which mechanism operated.
- Not that the framework names fatty acids, phospholipids, transient pores or carriers. That is a chemistry-frame binding, provenanced as such, and still unproven as the historical mechanism.
- Not that permeability alone identifies the mechanism. Several mechanisms give the same reading (F6).
- Not that retention and exchange are one dial. They are a union compound with its own loop.
- Not a claim that modern protein transporters are excluded. The shape is about the rule a boundary must implement, not about which molecular hardware implements it at a given time.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument that discriminates the mechanism, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour meter *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

- **Mechanism Test:** a measurement that separates solubility-diffusion from transient-pore, packing-defect, head-group-gated and carrier transport under the same permeability reading. That would name the member of the functional group.
- **Import Test:** grow a vesicle with zero net import across its boundary and check whether the interior can still replicate its genome. A positive result breaks F2.
- **Role Test:** find a boundary that selects by an intrinsic "usefulness" tag with no physical discriminant. That breaks F4.
- **Union Test:** find a single lever that optimises retention and exchange with no compound residue. That breaks F7.
- **Standing refuters** from `01` section 4: a non-selective boundary sustaining growth; a figure line naming a molecule; a lossless round trip.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreached for the name.** The figures decide the shape (typed directional filter, closure requiring import, containment plus gradient composing flow, role-relative valence). They do not decide the molecular mechanism. The framework stops at the limit of what its instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). A discriminating instrument would move the name from `candidate` to `grounded`; agreement about the shape would not.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the first membrane molecule. It says what rule any working boundary must implement. A membrane that retains everything starves and one that passes everything loses the concentrated interior that makes a protocell possible, so the boundary must be a filter with a direction, not a wall. What is kept is what can be composed over; what crosses inward is an import, because a closed compartment cannot manufacture new kinds of building block from what it already holds. Nutrient and waste are not labels carried by molecules but roles assigned by the compartment's changing state, so the discrimination is physical, by size, charge and partitioning. Retention and exchange are two conditions in one compound, not two ends of one dial. The framework returns this shape and stops; which of the candidate mechanisms actually operated is owed to a discriminating experiment, not to a further argument. The refuters are written down in advance, and the slot stays open until the ground settles it.
