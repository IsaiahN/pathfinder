## What the wider literature changes, and what it does not

1. **It corroborates the shape, it does not supply it.** Mansy 2010 and Britannica independently describe selective permeability, retain-large/pass-small behaviour, and nutrient-in/waste-out. These are world witnesses for the shape the figures already decide. They share an origins/membranes evidence pool with each other, so their agreement is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
2. **It names candidates, not the answer.** The five mechanisms in Mansy 2010 are a metamer set: several sources give the same permeability reading. The reading does not identify the source; a discriminating instrument is owed (*The Hard Problem is About Source*).
3. **It supplies the ground for the mechanism name.** The permeability coefficients in Chen and Walde 2010 and the simulation results in Ma and Yu 2025 are measurements and models, which is the proper anchor for D3 (Fig 2 :29-30). No figure line competes with them.
4. **It does not authorise a prestige shortcut.** The field's famous names (Szostak, Deamer, Chen, Mansy) appear as provenance for claims, never as reasons to believe them. Seat Map :171 *"an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."*

## Two-stage offer, stated explicitly

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). What they contributed at stage 1 stands or falls on its own.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a generator, not an answer. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get, because the formula's "Direction" note says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"*.
