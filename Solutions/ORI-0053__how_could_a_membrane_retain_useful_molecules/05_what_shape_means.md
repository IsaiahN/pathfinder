## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 (WATSON 60 quotes it): *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer we can state without naming the molecule. The shape is: *a typed, directional filter that retains what composes and imports what it cannot make*. "Fatty acid vesicle", "transient pore" and "partition coefficient" are names, not shapes. The distinction is the whole of F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you have that is closest to the thing, then use proximity. *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."* Here the closest held words are "boundary", "filter", "gradient", "contained", "import".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual is stated as retention and exchange, not as lipid chemistry.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* The world sources were tested against the residual, not used to pick a frame first.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the shape, not the mechanism name.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Here: a permeability assay, a partition coefficient measurement, an instrument that discriminates among the candidate transport mechanisms.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming fatty acids is exactly this: an import from the chemistry frame. It must be recorded as such, not smuggled in as a figure result.

WATSON 60 sharpens the second point: *"the figures are the ground for design questions; Isaiah is the anchor for what the figures genuinely cannot settle."* So no human ruling was requested on whether a lipid bilayer is a filter; the figures decide the shape, and the experiment decides the name.

## 4. Why "retaining useful molecules" is the metamerism trap

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism of the error. An instrument that detects green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*. The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, or CMYK. And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*

Applied here: the observation "the vesicle retained the polymer and passed the nucleotide" is a reading. Mansy 2010 lists at least five mechanisms that can produce it (solubility-diffusion, transient pore, packing defect, head-group gated, lipid carrier). Naming one as *the* mechanism from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a more confident name. The essay's own resolution: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

This is not a figure verdict; it is the source-discipline applied to the world layer (F6 in `01`). It corroborates F5 but does not govern the shape.

## 5. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The famous names in the origins literature are provenance for claims, never a reason the shape is true. Outreach gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask for endorsement (Seat Map :163 *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. None of them can settle anything they report on.

## 6. Answer to the only substantive question here

**What licenses a particular molecular mechanism in the answer slot?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement (an instrument that discriminates among the metamer set), or by a provenanced import with a shadow test, recorded as an import. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.
