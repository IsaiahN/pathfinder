# Expert verification packet: the Tether on protocell membrane permeability

**What this is:** a claim in a form a membrane chemist or origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural boundary rule, not `grounded`.** No measurement in this packet settles the molecular mechanism. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A working boundary is a typed, directional filter, not a wall. It retains what can be composed over and imports what the interior cannot make, because a closed compartment cannot gain new kinds of building block by rearranging what it holds. Containment and a spendable gradient compose influx and efflux; "useful" and "waste" are roles relative to the compartment's state, not labels carried by molecules; and retention and exchange form a union compound that a single lever cannot clear. The framework returns this shape and stops. It does not name fatty acids, pores or partition coefficients as the answer; those are chemistry-frame bindings awaiting a discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 4, Figure 6 and Figure 8

- **Statement:** a boundary must be a filter with a direction; a fully closed interior cannot sustain composition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :21 *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
- **Refuter:** a compartment with a boundary that passes everything or nothing still sustaining a composing, growing interior; or a compartment growing and replicating its genome with zero net import.

### C2 - world-facing; checkable against vesicle permeability data

- **Statement:** fatty-acid membranes realise the shape: they retain large and highly charged solutes while passing smaller, less polar ones, and "useful" versus "waste" is a role set by the compartment rather than an intrinsic property.
- **What to check:** Mansy 2010 (open access, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2908771) states that *"Vesicles composed of fatty acids are able to retain large polymers and highly charged molecules, while simultaneously allowing for the diffusion of smaller, less polar solutes"* and that *"Even subtle differences in partition coefficients can lead to large permeability differences that could potentially be exploited for early metabolic processes."* Chen and Walde 2010 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2890201) give the K+ permeability contrast between phospholipid and fatty-acid membranes. Britannica's general reference entry states the same retain-large/pass-small rule outside the origins field.
- **Refuter:** a boundary that selects by an intrinsic usefulness tag with no physical discriminant; or a measured permeability difference that cannot be accounted for by any physical property in the cited frames.

### C3 - mechanism-level; checkable against the metamer problem

- **Statement:** the permeability reading does not identify the mechanism. Several candidate routes give the same observation, so naming one is a binding, not a derivation.
- **What to check:** Mansy 2010 lists solubility-diffusion, transient pore, packing defect, head-group gated and lipid carrier mechanisms. *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the discrimination rule: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and the historic case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **Refuter:** a measurement that separates the candidate mechanisms under the same permeability reading. This would **name the slot** and clear F6 without breaking the shape. It is the result that would move the mechanism from `candidate` to `grounded`.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

1. **Mechanism Test:** a measurement that discriminates solubility-diffusion from transient-pore, packing-defect, head-group-gated and carrier transport under the same permeability reading (clears F6, names the slot).
2. **Import Test:** a vesicle sustaining growth and genome replication with zero net import across its boundary (breaks F2 if positive).
3. **Union Test:** a single lever that optimises retention and exchange with no residual compound (breaks F7 if found).
4. **Lossless Test:** a demonstration that the shape maps without loss onto the chemical description (breaks F8 if shown).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 4 :21, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :32, Fig 9 :32 and Fig 11 :49, five short passages.
