# Solution ORI-0053

**Question:** How could a membrane retain useful molecules while still allowing nutrients and waste to pass?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 74 (question 53, section 6)

**Section:** 6. Membranes, compartments, and protocells

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive shape claims, `general` on the structural boundary rule. Nothing here is `grounded`: no experiment in this folder settles the molecular mechanism. The whole package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pass, not by this author)

## What this folder decides, in effect terms

A boundary that retains everything starves and accumulates inhibitors; a boundary that passes everything loses the concentrated interior that makes a protocell a candidate at all. The residual is not "find a membrane" but "find the rule that lets a boundary discriminate what crosses." The figures decide the shape of that rule: a filter with a direction, not a wall; a closure that must import to stay alive; a gradient that can be spent. They do not name fatty acids or pores. Figure 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The molecular name is a provenanced import from the chemistry frame, settled by experiment, not a figure verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*; Seat Map :163 *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*; Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and asking them to trust the framework before they check the output spends the only independence they bring.
