## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the name is withheld. A working boundary is a typed, directional filter, not a wall; it retains what can be composed over and imports what the interior cannot make, because a closed compartment cannot manufacture new kinds of building block. Containment and a spendable gradient compose influx and efflux; "useful" and "waste" are roles relative to the compartment's state, so discrimination is physical, by size, charge and partitioning; retention and exchange are a union compound that one lever cannot clear. The framework returns this shape and stops. It does not name fatty acids, pores or partition coefficients: those are chemistry-frame bindings with zero evidentiary weight until provenance and a shadow test are shown, and the five candidate mechanisms in Mansy 2010 are a metamer set whose common reading does not identify the source. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest outreach point is that the slot stays open until a discriminating instrument names it.
