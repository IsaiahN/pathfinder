## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that statistical theories are falsifiable.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words.

1. **Agreement is not a witness.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list of methodologists selected for their agreement with this answer would manufacture exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A verifier who can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Showing an outside frame the full derivation before asking for its verdict spends the independence it was approached for.
3. **Prestige cannot settle a test.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." The ask is for a refutation of a checkable shape, not an endorsement of a framework.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Every venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is institutional, public and opt-in.
