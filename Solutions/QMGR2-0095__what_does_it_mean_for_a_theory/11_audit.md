## Independent domain census

I redid the census before comparing my conclusions with the primary's, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the attribution gap between a staked statistical prediction and a reading. Arity: four conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"), not one hard question with a yes/no answer. Symmetry: a distribution-to-observation comparison is ordered; the tail probability is an ordering and a threshold relation, not a symmetric bond (Fig 12 :87; Operators table :47-52). Scale: first order within an experiment, second order across replications, with a lossy membrane between a reading and a distribution over readings (Fig 4 :27-32).
- **D1, the statistical model frame.** Actors: parameters, priors, likelihoods, Γ, rejection region, sufficient statistic. Currency: probability mass and description length. Anchor: the pre-committed test procedure. Question type: partition boundary over the sample space. It ceases to be a domain if the test is chosen after the data, because the anchor then moves into D2.
- **D2, the measurement frame.** Actors: instruments, datasets, calibrations, systematics, pooled readings. Currency: observations o'. Anchor: the data-generating process, which does not update on the theory. Question type: union of signal, channel decay and auxiliaries.
- **D3, the verification frame.** Actors: protocol, replications, reviewers, conventions. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: union with an unquantified shared fraction. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: bind the refusal rule before the run (Fig 5 :66); declare the stake (Fig 10 :60); refuse before minting an adjustment (Formula step 2); range against a non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :29); settle before composing (Fig 12 :104). My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim separator, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 (non-updating anchor), Fig 5 :53/:66 (negation and pre-registration), Fig 8 :39 (non-refusal), Fig 9 :60 (filter not witness).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` §2: stake a refusal region, keep the anchor fixed, use independent frames, report non-refusal as unreached, treat the threshold as a convention.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete statistical test: rejection region R_q, significance level alpha = 0.05, p-value, Neyman-Pearson likelihood ratio, false-discovery rate or Bayes factor, with a specific dataset and instrument.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source of the mismatch**. The p-value cannot discriminate a wrong theory from a decayed channel, a contingent analysis path, an unmodelled systematic or luck; the figure set requires a source-discriminating instrument, which the test does not supply;
- the **per-slot indexing**, which a single global fit statistic discards;
- the **union structure**, which flattens into a single "falsifiable or not" verdict if the three domains are not kept separate.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the requirement to state a refusal before the run and to keep the reading independent of the prediction. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
