## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form any workable answer must take, statable before the number is named: *a pre-committed per-slot refusal region, read through a channel to a non-updating ground, checked by independent frames, with non-refusal reported as unreached and the threshold recorded as a budget convention.* That is a shape. "Use alpha = 0.005", "use a Bayes factor above 10", "frequentism is correct" are names. The distinction is load-bearing because the question is often asked as if a single number were the answer.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the target, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Recurse until the space is small and descriptive.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the shape claim is written as a refusal rule that anyone can inspect, not as a thesis about a framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the four conditions of `01` were obtained by narrowing from the ordinary phrase "can come back wrong" to (1) a refusal region, (2) a non-updating anchor, (3) independent frames, (4) honest non-refusal. Each narrowing came from a figure line, not from a preferred statistical school.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

From *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral.

For this question, a specific alpha would be licensed by the ground only if a measurement showed that this error rate, in this field, produces a better refusal record than the alternatives. Absent that, choosing one is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the declared `gap` in `01` F10.

## 4. Why a single p-value cannot be the answer

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument detects green and "cannot comment on the source" (it might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The resolution came from the spectrometer, not an argument: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

A p-value is a reading of one projection, data versus model. It cannot comment on the source of the mismatch: a wrong theory, a decayed channel, an unmodelled systematic, a contingent analysis choice, or a genuinely improbable stroke of luck. The world literature agrees. The ASA statement's second principle: "P-values do not measure the probability that the studied hypothesis is true, or the probability that the data were produced by random chance alone." And its conclusion: "No single index should substitute for scientific reasoning."

What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument about the number. That is the honest stop in `07` §5.

## 5. Q - Is "falsifiable" a binary property of a statistical theory?

**ANSWERED with a refusal of the framing.** Binary verdicts are the wrong object. The framework's own output-layer failure genus is "a mode stated as a verdict" (Seat Map :281), and a yes/no label on "falsifiable" hides the four conditions that can separately fail. Vignero and Wenmackers, opened this session, reach the same structural conclusion from a different pool: falsifiability is gradable, depends on informativeness, and "co-depends on the severity of available experiments". They also show that a probabilistic hypothesis can be outright falsifiable relative to a fixed experimental partition, so Answer A ("never falsifiable") is too strong.

**Does the answer commit the error the README forbids?** No, provided the specific number stays a gap. Naming a per-slot refusal region is a *shape*, not a name: it says what the region must do, not where its cutoff lies. Naming "p < 0.05" as the answer would be a name where the framework returns a shape, and it would be a name read off one projection (the p-value) as if it settled the source. Both are refused.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially read the "shape, not a name" rule as forbidding any concrete test specification. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. A declared rejection region, a stated alpha and a named instrument are not missing primitives; they are bindings from the statistics frame onto terms already held (comparison at a threshold, a non-updating anchor, per-slot residuals). Naming them is provenance, not a forbidden invention, and it is not an import that adds an atom (Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom" - this adds none).

What remains wrong in a naive account is not the naming but the *source* attributed to the name. If a single p-value is read as telling us whether the theory is false, that is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous statisticians who agree with this answer is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a ground, plus a refuter written in advance.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What would move the shape to a name

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument, not an argument. Here that means:

- an instrument or design that discriminates among the sources of a mismatch (wrong theory, decayed channel, contingent analysis, luck) at the slot where the residual is live;
- an independent dataset from a different evidence pool that refuses or fails to refuse the same staked region;
- a field-specific cost of error imported with provenance and a shadow test, which would license a particular threshold without pretending the figures produced it.

Until one of these fires, the answer stays a shape and the number stays a gap. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README), and the record of the route is what keeps the result checkable.
