# The Tether's answer: statistical falsifiability

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts, and `gap` on the literal threshold. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a refusal rule, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No statistical test was run to produce it.

## 1. The residual it answers

The ordinary question asks whether a theory is "falsifiable" when its predictions are distributions. Stated in effect terms, the residual is: *what has to be in place for such a claim to be capable of coming back wrong, and for the wrongness to be attributable to the claim rather than to the test, the instrument or an auxiliary?* Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The answer must therefore be per-slot and per-domain, never a single label on a theory.

The two tempting answers both fail. Strict Popperianism applied naively says a distribution is compatible with every finite sample, so it cannot be falsified, only disconfirmed. Naive significance testing says any improbable outcome refutes it, which promotes a decision convention to a truth verdict. The figures route between them.

## 2. The shape: a four-condition separator

1. **A pre-committed, per-slot refusal region.** The theory stakes which outcomes at which observables would count as a refusal, written down before the run. Fig 5 :66: "Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single global p-value performs the prohibited aggregation.
2. **A non-updating anchor with a reachable channel.** The data-generating process must not move when the theory is revised. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" - so most practical failures are channel failures (saturated metrics, pooled readings, cued observers), not changes in the world.
3. **Independent frames.** A refusal counts when the reading was not produced by the same evidence pool as the prediction. Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Ten analyses of one dataset are one frame wearing ten names.
4. **Honest non-refusal.** Not being refused is reported as unreached at this budget, with the checked slots and the instrument that would change the report. It is never reported as verification. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The significance threshold is a filter for the decision budget, not a witness for the verdict: Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**Falsification itself is a negation, not a fit.** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". A theory that only ever reports "consistent with the data" has no operator for being wrong.

## 3. Verdicts per domain, kept separate and never averaged

- **D1, the statistical model frame - `specified`, triangulated.** A falsifiable statistical theory is informative enough to stake a low-probability region and carries a refusal rule bound before the run. A theory with enough free parameters to accommodate any outcome is the flat formula (Fig 12 :6: "collapse 6: the flat formula"). Falsifiability is graded by informativeness and by the severity of available experiments, not binary.
- **D2, the measurement frame - `specified`, triangulated.** The anchor is the process that produces the data; the channel is what fails. Independence decides whether two readings are two witnesses or one pool talking. The world analogues are the early-time versus late-time H0 determinations (two instruments) and the Clever Hans effect (a cued channel is not a reading of the subject).
- **D3, the verification frame - `specified`, triangulated, with a `gap`.** Non-refusal is unreached; the threshold is a convention that allocates the decision budget; outside frames must not hold the interiors before giving a verdict. The specific alpha, prior or Bayes factor is a `gap`: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

A theory can pass D1 and fail D2, or pass D1 and D2 and fail D3. The conditions form a union of overlapping requirements, not a partition into falsifiable and unfalsifiable camps (Fig 9 :55). No overall percentage or single yes/no label is returned.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular p-value threshold, confidence level or Bayes factor is the correct one. That is the declared gap, and choosing one is provenance from the statistics frame, not a derivation.
- Not that frequentist methods are right and Bayesian methods wrong. They are different anchors and currencies; the figures decide neither framing.
- Not that a non-significant result supports the theory. It reports a budget and a set of checked slots.
- Not that a statistical theory can never be refused. Vignero and Wenmackers show outright falsification is possible relative to a fixed experimental context; the figures require the context and refusal rule to be declared.
- Not that agreement among experts corroborates the answer. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not grounded. Nothing here was settled by running a statistical test.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): an instrument that detects green "cannot comment on the source"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Discriminator.** What instrument or design would distinguish a wrong theory from a decayed channel, a contingent analysis choice, an unmodelled systematic, or luck, at the slot where the residual is live? Gelman and Loken show how easily the analysis path masquerades as a fixed test.
- **Independent replication.** Does a dataset from a different evidence pool refuse or fail to refuse the same staked region? Fig 8 :50 allows convergent derivation at maximum independence as genuine corroboration, and only the record of where each came from separates it from an adopted import.
- **Persistent residual.** Does a term's residual keep being wrong and not trend down across new data (Fig 5 :59)? The H0 tension is the live cosmological instance; a resolution that merely increases the uncertainty through extra degrees of freedom is a fit, not a refusal.
- **Cost of error.** Which threshold is right requires a field-specific cost of being wrong, imported with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :24). That would close the gap for that field without pretending the figures produced the number.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: no refusal region needed; aggregation lossless; independence dispensable; non-refusal is verification; the threshold is a world fact. Any one firing narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The separator says what a test must look like and what would have to be true for the verdict to differ. It does not deliver the number a working statistician wants, and it does not claim the figure set is nature's own list of conditions. "Indiscernibility is not identity"; the lineage is what makes the stop checkable. The canonical README's limit applies: the framework returns a shape, stops at the limit of the available instruments, and records the route.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> For a statistical theory, "falsifiable" does not mean "some possible outcome has zero probability" and it does not mean "p crossed 0.05." It means the theory has staked, before the run, a low-probability refusal region on each observable it claims to predict; that the reading comes from a ground the theory cannot move, through a channel that has not decayed; that the refusal is checked by instruments which do not share the prediction's evidence pool; and that failure to refuse is reported as unreached at this budget rather than as confirmation. Falsification is a negation, so a vocabulary that only reports "consistent with the data" cannot carry it. The significance threshold is a convention that allocates the decision budget, never a verdict about the world, and no figure returns a correct alpha, prior or test. What survives is a checkable four-condition separator, a pre-registered refuter, and the names of the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
