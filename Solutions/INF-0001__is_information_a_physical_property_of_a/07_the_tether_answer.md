# The Tether's answer: property, relation, or both?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the single definition. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The either/or fails because the word "information" is doing two jobs with different arities and different currencies. A property reading prices a state description; a relation reading names a typed bond; an observer reading makes meaning depend on a frame's residual. Fig 9 :4-5 says a disagreement that does not shrink with effort while each rule works on a different subset is *"not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The bond carries information the operands do not.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :46: *"which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* A complete physical inventory is half a record. This is the figure vocabulary's own use of the word, not an analogy.
2. **There is also a physical, bits-reading, and it is a different currency.** Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* So a bit-cost is real and payable, but it cannot be added to the bond's typed relation.
3. **"Both" is right only as an unbundled union.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Verdicts are indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19) and kept separate. Averaging them is the named error.
4. **The observer is a frame, not a seat.** Symbols :111: *"A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* What a signal means depends on what the frame can read (Symbols :38-40), but the frame must range against a ground that does not update (Fig 2 :29). Without that, drift is coherent and invisible (Fig 2 :34).
5. **The relation is not mere subjectivity.** Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Independence is *"how little they draw on the same evidence"* (Fig 2 :31), so independent frames can carry the same content.
6. **Correlation is not yet information on a channel.** The relevant bond is ordered (Operators table :59-62); a symmetric correlation undercharges the relation. Fig 13 :36: *"an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."*
7. **The single definition is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* No figure returns one univocal notion covering carrier cost, typed bond and frame-relative meaning.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new definition of information. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that information is "just correlations." Correlations are symmetric and the bond is ordered.
- Not that information is "in the eye of the beholder." The frame must range against a non-updating anchor, and independent frames can converge.
- Not that the physical reading is illusory. Bits price the description, and the world has an erasure experiment (Bérut et al. 2012) to show it.
- Not that the two currencies can be summed (Fig 12 :130).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the relevant discrimination point was taken.
- Not that "observer" is a seat. That was my own first-pass error and it is retracted in `05` section 5.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried from the exemplar mapping): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a given measured bit-pattern arise from a physical channel, from a shared evidence pool, or from both in an inseparable compound? A discriminator between channel and pool is owed. Fig 2 :31: *"two instruments pointed at the same thing are"* independent; two people reading the same report are one.
- Can the bit-price of a description and the cost of settling the typed bond be varied independently in one experiment? If they cannot, the two-currency claim is in trouble (Fig 12 :125-128).
- **Threshold test:** does an ordered bond hold between the source type and the receiver type, or is the observed relation symmetric? Operators table :63: *"remove one. Does it still work? Yes."* is the disjunction test, not the channel test; the channel test is whether removing the source breaks the receiver's state.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a bond recoverable from operands alone; one additive currency; no convergence at independence; a univocal figure definition; a ground reading showing bit-cost and bond-typing are one quantity.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The missing thing is an instrument at the bond-typing to ground-ranging link. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The figures can characterise what that instrument must discriminate (a shape), not name it. Fig 13 :135-136: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not define information, and it does not split the question into two exclusive answers. It says that the bond carries information the operands do not, so a bare inventory of a system is half a record; that there is also a physical bit-price, and the two are currencies in different units that cannot be added; that "both" is therefore a union of overlapping conditions to be checked separately, not an average; that the observer is a frame whose reading depends on its residual but must be ranged against a ground that does not update; and that low-overlap frames can nevertheless converge on the same term, so the relation is not whim. Correlation alone is not enough, because the channel bond is ordered. What the framework cannot return is one definition covering all of these. That is a declared gap, and the instrument that would close it is named as a shape. The answer stops there, with its refuters written in advance.
