## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried from the exemplar mapping): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For INF-0001 the shape is:

> two conditions, two currencies, one union, per-slot verdicts, one declared gap.

That is not a definition of information. It is a constraint any candidate answer must satisfy if the figures govern the question. The single definition is the `gap`.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question, "describe the effect rather than the cause" is decisive. Instead of asking "what is information in itself?", the residual asks: what fails to resolve if information is treated as a unary property; what fails if it is treated as a pure relation; and what instrument would distinguish the two. Those are checkable effects.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

**Application here.** Calling the carrier cost "bits" and the typed relation "the bond" is a binding onto quantities the framework already holds (Fig 12 :125-126, :46). It is not the invention of new primitives, and it is not a wall-moving import. Calling a particular physical quantity "the information" would be a name where the framework returns a constraint, and would need a source-discriminating instrument.

## 4. Why "property or relation" is the wrong form of the question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, the "reading" is the bit-count or the measured correlation. The "source" is whichever of the two conditions (carrier description or typed bond) produced it. A bit-count cannot comment on the source. A correlation cannot distinguish a physical channel from a shared evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). So the question as posed asks an instrument a question it cannot answer, and the honest move is to name the discriminator that is owed.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried, and returned in this session's search results): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q2 of this folder: what licenses calling the observer a "frame"?

Nothing in the framework licenses calling it a *seat*. Symbols :111: *"A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."* Fig 10 :43-44: a seat *"transmits verdicts and issues none."* The observer in the question is the thing for which the signal makes a difference, so it is a frame. If it were a seat, it would have to be blind to the claim it checks (Fig 10 :42, :85-86), which is not what "observer" means in this question.

**Correction to my own first pass, kept visible.** I initially mapped "observer" to the seat. That was wrong: it would have made the observer a checker rather than a reader. Retracted. The frame reading is triangulated (Symbols :111 plus Fig 11 :49 plus Fig 9 :30-31, three different objects), so the correction is not a downgrade of the answer.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of philosophers selected for agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape verdict for INF-0001

- The framework can say: the bond carries information the operands do not (Fig 12 :5, :46); bits and actions are two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125-126); meaning is frame-relative but must be ranged against a ground (Fig 11 :49; Fig 2 :29); low-overlap frames can converge (Fig 8 :50).
- The framework cannot say: which single definition of information is the right one across all domains. That is a `gap`, and it is reported as unreachable, not as silence (Fig 13 :134).
- What would close it: a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.
