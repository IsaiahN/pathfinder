**Subject:** One checkable claim about "is information physical or relational"

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`):** the "is it a property, a relation, or both?" question does not split into two exclusive answers. It splits into two conditions with different currencies that overlap in a specific compound. In the figure vocabulary I am using:
>
> 1. The bond carries information the operands do not. Fig 12 :5 reads: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A complete physical list of a system is, on this reading, half a record.
> 2. There is also a physical, bits-reading, and it is a different currency. Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
> 3. So "both" is right structurally, but only if the two conditions are unbundled and checked separately. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing."*
>
> The observer in this account is a frame, meaning a bounded system with a library and a residual, not a seat in a checking arrangement. What a signal means to a frame depends on what it can currently read. That is not subjectivity: two low-overlap frames can reach the same term separately, which Fig 8 :50 calls *"corroboration at maximum independence."*
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can define information in one univocal sense across physics, biology, cognition and society. No figure line does that, and I record it as a `gap`, not a silence. I am also not claiming this is settled by experiment. Nothing here is `grounded`.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. a case where the typed bond is recoverable from the operands alone, so an ingredient list is a complete record;
> 2. a case where description cost and settlement cost are one additive currency;
> 3. two genuinely low-overlap frames that cannot converge on the same term;
> 4. a single measure that preserves bit-cost, bond type and semantic truth at once.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract, path on request): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Information: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/information/
> - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Semantic Conceptions of Information: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/information-semantic
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, so a reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` — *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| The observer is a frame, not a seat | Symbols :111 defines frame; Fig 10 :43-44 says a seat *"transmits verdicts and issues none."* |
| "Both" is unbundled, not averaged | Fig 9 :55-57; Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* |
| Single definition declared a gap | Fig 9 :32 — *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 — *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* |
| Links after the verdict, never before | Fig 10 :42 — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige *"can fund an action... but it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the method, not a conclusion | Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* |

**Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked, so their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
