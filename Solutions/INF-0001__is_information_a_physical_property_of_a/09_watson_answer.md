## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what the Tether says about three options: A, information is a physical property of a system; B, information is a relation between a system and an observer; C, both. Arity: A is unary on a slot, B is binary or ternary, C is a union. Symmetry: a physical correlation is symmetric, a channel is ordered. Scale: first order for a carrier reading, composed for meaning. What each leads to: A preserves the bit-price but drops the typed bond; B preserves the bond but drops the description cost; C is structurally right but becomes an error if the two conditions are averaged instead of unbundled and checked per slot. The ordinary word "information" is bound here as an expectation of one univocal definition; that expectation is the residual and is made refusable before the run.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** No category assignment is settling because one word is used for two quantities with different currencies. Arity unary, binary and union. Symmetry mixed: correlation is symmetric, channel is ordered. Scale: first order carrier, composed frame. Fig 9 :4-5 says such a disagreement is "not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
- **D1, carrier and description.** Actors: states, degrees of freedom, signals, memories, distributions. Currency: bits, the log of distinguishable alternatives. Anchor: an instrument reading and a physical cost that does not move with a frame's opinion. Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."
- **D2, bond and channel.** Actors: operands, typed bonds, situations, sources, receivers, constraints. Currency: the relation, which carries what the operands do not. Anchor: the ground plus a reliable non-random regularity. Fig 12 :5: "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- **D3, frame and interpretation.** Actors: frames (bounded systems with libraries), residuals, seats. Currency: meaning, salience and valence, all residual-relative. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Symbols :111: "A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."
- **D4, verification and settlement.** Actors: frames, seats, anchors, instruments. Currency: verdicts versus agreement, independent readings versus pooled readings. Anchor: a thing that does not update. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **Partition or union.** Union. D1 and D2 overlap in the channel's carrier, D2 and D3 overlap in the reading frame, D4 ranges over all three. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Order by dependency.** Read the slot state, then type the bond, then range the frame against the ground, then settle. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

The apparent conflict between "information is physical" in the world and the relation-primary figure vocabulary dissolved because they price different quantities; Fig 12 :125 says the currencies do not add. ## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources corroborate only. Overlap is declared rather than hidden. Each gives field, author / venue / year, quote, prediction, and status.

- **Mathematics, engineering and communication theory.** Claude Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication," Bell System Technical Journal, 1948: "These semantic aspects of communication are irrelevant to the engineering problem." Predicts that the bit-measure is a separate currency from meaning. Corroborates D1 and the two-currency split. Overlap: with Landauer and Bérut, one physics-and-engineering pool.
- **Physics / thermodynamics of computation.** Rolf Landauer, "Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process," IBM Journal of Research and Development, 1961: "information processing is inevitably accompanied by a certain minimum amount of heat generation." Predicts a physical cost for the bit. Corroborates D1 at the ground. Overlap: same pool as Shannon and Bérut.
- **Physics, experimental.** Antoine Bérut, Artak Arakelyan, Artyom Petrosyan, Sergio Ciliberto, Raoul Dillenschneider and Eric Lutz, "Experimental verification of Landauer's principle linking information and thermodynamics," Nature, 2012: "This result demonstrates the intimate link between information theory and thermodynamics." Predicts that the bit-price is measurable. Corroborates D1.
- **Philosophy of computation / philosophy of physics.** James Ladyman, "What Does it Mean to Say a Physical System Implements a Computation?," Theoretical Computer Science, 2009: "a hybrid physical-logical entity that combines a physical device, a specification of which physical states of that device correspond to various logical states." Predicts that a physical device alone is not enough; a state-to-logic binding is required. Corroborates the union claim. Overlap: low, genuinely independent frame.
- **Biology / cybernetics.** Gregory Bateson, *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*, 1972: "any difference which makes a difference in some later event." Predicts that information is a relation with a downstream effect for a receiver. Corroborates D2 and D3. Overlap: low, genuinely independent frame.
- **Philosophy of information, reference work.** Pieter Adriaans and Johan van Benthem (eds.), "Information," Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2012, revised 2023: "Take three influential proposals and their definiens for information (Shannon-probability; Kolmogorov-computation; Floridi-truth) and observe that they have next to nothing in common." Predicts plural measures rather than one definition. Corroborates the `gap`. Overlap: same philosophical pool as the next source.
- **Philosophy of information, reference work.** Luciano Floridi et al., "Semantic Conceptions of Information," Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2005, revised 2022: "with a channel being a relation allowing information the flow from one state to another." Predicts that information flow is a relation. Corroborates D2. Also reports Shannon's "It is hardly to be expected that a single concept of information would satisfactorily account for the numerous possible applications of the general field," corroborating the `gap`.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict after re-census. The conflict rule was not triggered. The philosophy sources share an evidence pool and count as one witness; the physics sources share a pool and count as one witness; Ladyman 2009 and Bateson 1972 are the two genuinely low-overlap witnesses.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The "or" is a union, not a partition and not an average. The bond carries information the operands do not, so the property-only reading is incomplete. Bits price the description and actions price settlement in currencies that do not add, so the relation-only reading is incomplete. The observer is a frame whose reading is residual-relative but must be ranged against a non-updating anchor; low-overlap frames can still converge on the same term, so the relation is not whim. The channel bond is ordered, so correlation alone is not information on a channel. A single univocal definition across carrier, bond and frame is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. This is `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished definition instead of a separator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The one-sided readings are removable without touching the census; `01` section 6 records both retractions. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing that a given bit-pattern comes from a physical channel, a shared pool, or an inseparable compound would move D1 and D4; an experiment showing bit-price and bond-settlement vary together in one additive accounting would break the two-currency claim; a figure line returning a single definition would close the `gap`; a demonstration that low-overlap frames cannot converge would break D4. Prestige, consensus and a simpler story are not such instruments. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
