## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks for a category assignment: is "information" (A) a unary property of one system, (B) a binary relation between a system and an observer, or (C) both?

Stated as an effect, the three options lead to different checks:

| option | what it asserts | what it would take to check it | what it hides |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, property | information is carried by the state of one system, independent of who reads it | a reading of the state, a cost for changing it | the typed relation between source and receiver, and the frame that makes the reading mean something |
| B, relation | information exists only where a system and an observer stand in a relation | a channel, a constraint, a receiver for which the signal makes a difference | the description cost of the carrier, which is paid whether or not anyone interprets it |
| C, both | both of the above hold | an unbundled check of each condition separately | if treated as one averaged property, it hides which condition is doing the work in which case |

**Residual R:** the category assignment is not settling because the ordinary word "information" is being used for at least two different quantities with different currencies and different anchors. The residual is not "which label is correct." It is "which quantity is being asked about, and what would settle it." Fig 9 :4-5: disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

Arity, symmetry, scale:

- **Arity.** A is unary on a slot (the state of one system at a moment). B is binary or ternary (source, channel, receiver; or two situations and the channel between them). C is a union of both arities.
- **Symmetry.** A physical correlation is symmetric under swapping its relata. A channel is directed: a constraint from a source type to a receiver type is not the constraint reversed (Fig 13 :35-36: *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction"*). So "relation" is not one symmetry class either.
- **Scale.** The physical reading is first-order over a state description. The semantic and pragmatic readings are composed: meaning depends on a frame whose library and residual are themselves composed terms.

**What an answer would look like.** Bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"): a figure-returned shape of the form "two conditions, two currencies, one union, per-domain verdicts kept separate," plus a named gap for the single definition. We write this down before the run so the preference is refusable.

## 1. Domain census

One line per domain with actors / currency / anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

**D1. Carrier and description (the physical, bits-reading).**
Actors: states of a slot, degrees of freedom, signals, memories, probability distributions.
Currency: bits, the log of the number of distinguishable alternatives, paid as a description cost.
Anchor: an instrument reading and a physical cost that does not move when a frame changes its mind (erasure heat at a temperature is the world's instance; the figures' instance is the ground). Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 13 :16: states *"names what is being measured."*

**D2. Bond and channel (the relational reading).**
Actors: operands, typed bonds, situations, sources, receivers, constraints.
Currency: the relation itself, which carries information the operands do not.
Anchor: the ground plus a reliable non-random regularity between relata. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :24: the network is *"that entities relate, and influence travels."*

**D3. Frame and interpretation (the observer-reading).**
Actors: frames (agents, disciplines, models, sensoria), libraries Γ, residuals R, seats.
Currency: meaning, salience, and valence, all relative to the frame's current residual.
Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Symbols table :38-40: R *"is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

**D4. Verification and settlement (cross-cutting).**
Actors: frames, seats, anchors, instruments.
Currency: verdicts versus agreement; independent readings versus pooled readings.
Anchor: a thing that does not update. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1 and D2 overlap in the channel (a channel has a physical carrier and a typed relation), D2 and D3 overlap in the frame (a channel is read by a frame), and D4 ranges over all three. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* So answering "A" or "B" alone clears one region and leaves the intersections.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle D1 before D2 (you cannot type a bond between states you cannot read); settle D2 before D3 (a frame can only mean something by a channel it has); settle D3 before D4 (a verdict needs a frame that can be corrected). Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

## 3. Findings

### F1 — In the figures, "information" is the bond, not the ingredients (decisive for the relation half)

Fig 12 :5:
> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 12 :46-47:
> **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**
> **"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."**

Fig 12 :94:
> **"The arrangement is the substance."**

Fig 13 :24 and :26:
> **"that entities relate, and influence travels"**
> **"it decides what the measurement would mean"**

This is the single most direct figure bearing on the question, and it is not an analogy: the framework's own word "information" denotes the typed relation that the operands do not carry. A list of the ingredients of a system, even a complete physical list, has *"recorded half of itself."* So the property-only reading (A) fails against the primary vocabulary of the framework. The relation half is supported by two figures with low overlap: Fig 12 derives it from the cost of an arrangement, Fig 13 derives it from the network structure and the meaning of a measurement. **Triangulated.**

Refuter: a figure line in which the bond's information is recoverable from the operands alone, or in which a single state in isolation is said to carry the full information of a composed term.

### F2 — The physical, bits-reading is also figure-primary, but it is a different currency

Fig 12 :125-126:
> **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
> **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**

Fig 4 :27:
> **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

Fig 13 :16:
> **"it names what is being measured"**

The figures use "information" a second way: as that which a description costs and a lossy transform drops. This is a unary price on a state description, and it is paid whether or not any observer interprets the result. It corresponds to the physical side of the question. But Fig 12 :125 says explicitly that bits and actions *"do not add."* The bit-price and the bond-relation are two readings, not two amounts of one stuff. World corroboration (secondary): Shannon 1948 restricts the engineering measure to selection from a set and explicitly sets semantics aside; Landauer 1961 and Bérut et al. 2012 give the bit-price a ground in heat dissipation. **Triangulated** across Fig 12 and Fig 4, with world corroboration from physics.

Refuter: a figure line that prices description and settlement in the same additive currency, or an experiment showing that the bit-cost of a state and the typed relation between operands are the same quantity.

### F3 — "Or both?" is a union, and the union must be unbundled before it is searched

Fig 9 :54:
> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :55-57:
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

Fig 9 :60:
> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Fig 1 :19:
> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

So option C is structurally right and also dangerous. It is right because D1, D2 and D3 overlap. It is dangerous if "both" is used to average the readings into one property: that is the aggregation Fig 1 :19 forbids and Fig 9 :60 disqualifies. The correct form is C-unbundled: per-slot, per-condition verdicts, with the intersections named as compounds. **Triangulated** across Fig 9 (union structure) and Fig 1 (per-slot indexing), low overlap.

Refuter: a demonstration that the physical and relational conditions form a clean partition with no overlapping case, or that their verdicts can be averaged without loss.

### F4 — "Relation to an observer" is frame-relativity, not subjectivity

Symbols table :111:
> **"A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."**

Fig 11 :49-50:
> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
> **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**

Symbols table :38-40:
> **"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."**

Fig 9 :30-31:
> **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**

The "observer" in the question maps onto a frame, not onto a seat (a correction recorded in section 6). A frame has a library and a residual, so what a signal means to it depends on what it can currently read. But this is a disciplined relativity: the frame's reading must be described in effect terms and ranged against a ground. Without that, "relative to an observer" collapses into Fig 2 :34: *"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* **Single-source on the figure side for the frame definition (Symbols table), triangulated with Fig 11 valence and Fig 9 effect-language across different objects.**

Refuter: a figure line in which meaning is a property of the signal alone with no frame, or a demonstration that drift among frames is a valid settlement.

### F5 — The relation has an objective spine: convergence at maximum independence

Fig 8 :50:
> **"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."**

Fig 8 :51-52:
> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

Fig 2 :31:
> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**

Fig 2 :35:
> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

So "relation" does not mean "whatever the observer says." Two low-overlap frames can reach the same term separately, and that is the strongest form of corroboration the framework has. What the figures forbid is treating a shared evidence pool as two witnesses: Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* World corroboration (secondary): the Barwise and Perry information-channel tradition and the Fodor, Evans and Dretske informational-semantics tradition both make the same information transmissible over different channels; both are surveyed in the SEP entry on semantic conceptions. **Triangulated** across Fig 8 and Fig 2, low overlap (surplus structure versus anchor structure).

Refuter: a demonstration that two genuinely low-overlap frames cannot converge on the same atom, or that all apparently convergent readings share an evidence pool.

### F6 — The single definition is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than as silence

Fig 9 :32:
> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134:
> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

Fig 13 :115:
> **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."**

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* No figure line returns one definition of information that covers the carrier cost, the typed bond and the frame-relative meaning. The world agrees: the SEP entry on information reports that Shannon-probability, Kolmogorov-computation and Floridi-truth *"have next to nothing in common,"* and quotes Shannon's own *"It is hardly to be expected that a single concept of information would satisfactorily account for the numerous possible applications of the general field."* This is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not a failure to search. It is `general` on the shape and `gap` on the name.

Refuter: a figure line that defines information once, univocally, over all three domains; or a world result that supplies one measure preserving bit-cost, bond type and semantic truth under one currency.

### F7 — "A link with no instrument" is where this question actually sits

Fig 3 :25-27:
> **"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Most of the dispute about "what information is" is conducted at the word level (D1 vocabulary and D3 meaning) while the middle link (what would count as progress for a given information claim) has no instrument. Fig 3 :29: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."* This is why a defender of A and a defender of B can both keep working on their own subset without the disagreement shrinking (Fig 9 :4-5). **Single-source on the figure side (Fig 3), corroborated by the world's plurality of measures.**

Refuter: an instrument that returns a progress signal for an information claim in a specific system, which would move the question from `gap` to `specified` at the ground.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

**S1. Fig 12 :46 versus the physical reading.** Read literally, *"which one holds is not recoverable from the operands"* looks like a ban on any physical account of information. Strain. Re-census: Fig 12 :46 governs bond typing inside a composition, not the description cost of a carrier. Fig 12 :125-126 governs the two currencies and explicitly preserves the bits-reading. Different quantities. After re-census: not strained. This is the README's rule that a reading making figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

**S2. Fig 4 :27 uses "information" in the lossy-transform sense.** This looks like a third sense that would break the two-currency separator. Re-census: Fig 4 :27 is the bits-price of a description across a scale membrane, so it belongs to D1's currency. Not a third quantity. Not strained.

**S3. Fig 11 :40 says capability is *"a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*** Tempting to read this as the whole answer to the question. Narrowed: it governs capability, not information. It is an analogue for the union claim, not a figure line that decides it. Recorded as an analogue with reduced weight, not as a witness.

**S4. The world's "information is physical" (Landauer) versus the figure's relation-primary vocabulary.** Read as a conflict, this would flip the verdict. Re-census: Landauer prices erasure in heat, which is D1's bit-currency; the figures' bond is D2. Fig 12 :125 says the two currencies do not add, so there is no conflict to resolve. The conflict rule was not triggered; a conflict would have sent us back to the census, never flipped a verdict.

**After re-census: four strains named, none hidden, none left as a contradiction.**

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line makes the typed bond recoverable from its operands alone, so that an ingredient list is a complete record.
2. A figure line prices description and settlement in one additive currency.
3. Two genuinely low-overlap frames are shown to be incapable of converging on the same atom.
4. A figure line returns a single univocal definition of information across carrier, bond and frame.
5. A ground instrument shows that bit-cost and bond-typing are the same measurable quantity.
6. The union claim fails: the conditions form a clean partition with no overlapping case.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the answer is re-derived from the census. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
