## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions. R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failure of "property, relation, or both" to settle. Arity: unary (a state), binary or ternary (source, receiver, channel), and a declared union. Symmetry: mixed, because a correlation is swap-invariant while a channel constraint is ordered. Scale: a first-order carrier reading and a composed frame reading. This is a union compound under Fig 9 :55, not one hard question with three answers.
- **D1, carrier and description.** Actors: state slots, degrees of freedom, signals, memories. Currency: bits. Anchor: a reading and a cost that does not move when a frame changes its mind. Type: a partition boundary question about what counts as a state description.
- **D2, bond and channel.** Actors: operands, typed bonds, situations, constraints. Currency: the typed relation. Anchor: the ground plus a reliable regularity. Type: a binding question, not a new primitive.
- **D3, frame and interpretation.** Actors: frames with libraries, residuals, seats. Currency: meaning and valence, residual-relative. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Type: an agent-and-contact question, with low independence in this simulated run.
- **D4, verification.** Actors: frames and seats. Currency: verdicts versus agreement. Anchor: a non-updating thing. Type: triangulation, not negotiation.
- **Order by dependency.** Read the slot, type the bond, range the frame, settle. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Refuse the one-sided readings before minting (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived D2 from Fig 12's pricing of an arrangement and Fig 13's network structure, I derived it from Fig 9's union/partition split and the Operators table's symmetric-versus-ordered distinction. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular inside the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5, :46, :125-126 (bond is information; two currencies), Fig 9 :54-57 (union, not partition), Fig 2 :29 (anchor) and Fig 13 :134 (unreachable is a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: information is the bond, bits are a separate currency, "both" is an unbundled union, the observer is a frame, the single definition is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ordinary answer to the original question, for example "information is physical structure, or the pattern that survives across observers."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **arities**: the unary bit-price and the binary typed bond collapse into one "pattern" in the ordinary answer;
- the **currency separation** of Fig 12 :125, which the ordinary answer silently sums;
- the **ordered bond** of the Operators table, which the ordinary "pattern" reading treats as a symmetric correlation;
- the **anchor requirement** of Fig 2 :29, which the ordinary answer replaces with consensus or with "what observers agree on."

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a bare inventory is not a complete record, and a relation needs a ground. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
