## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public, opt-in venues in `03`. Ask what would count as a failure. Record the verdict on stage 1 **before** any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
