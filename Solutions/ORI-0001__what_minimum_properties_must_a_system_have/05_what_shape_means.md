## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence. It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (as catalogued in the exemplar): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question it is not a list of properties. It is the form of the contact answer: a spendable gradient, a cycle, a catalyst-like part that survives use, a network that carries influence, and a boundary. Those constrain any candidate; they do not fill the slot with a definition.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- The SEP *Life* entry supplies the field's own version: the move called "Specify: divide the concept into distinct types and label them". That is the operation Fig 9 :4-5 requires, performed inside philosophy.

The residual is described in effect terms first: not "is a virus alive" but "each rule works well on a different subset and the disagreement does not shrink with effort". Only after that description is a frame chosen. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that would be a source-discriminating instrument or a repeatable laboratory construction that the field accepts, not a new definition.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So the six-term substrate is a visible guess. Treating it as nature's own definition of life would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. This is the correction applied in `01` F4 and recorded in its retraction log.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or an undiscovered scheme can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: observing that a system maintains itself, reacts, and changes over generations is a reading from which several different sources could have produced it (a genetic record, a chemical gradient maintained by geology, a catalytic surface, an external controller). Naming one source as "life" from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a sharper list.

World corroboration from a low-overlap field: NASA's *Life on Other Planets* (2023) reports Laurie Barge's framing of the search as *"The challenge is deciding what is life - when to say, 'I found it,'"* and describes a proposed 1-to-7 confidence scale for biosignatures. A confidence scale is an instrument-facing discriminator, not a definition. Corroborates the need for an instrument; it does not decide it.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So this `05` cannot validate its own shape claim.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "what minimum properties must a system have to count as alive?"**

It is a two-part answer, and neither part is a list:

1. **Convention part.** For a stated purpose, choose an operational definition and declare it a convention. NASA's is the best-known: a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution. Its own authors call it non-binding and note that nobody voted on it. That is a legitimate convention (Fig 2 :41). It is not a verdict (Fig 10 :47).
2. **Contact part.** The answerable question is what makes a system capable of self-maintenance, heritable change and adaptation in a habitat. Its shape is the five structural requirements in §1, held as a functional group rather than a sufficient condition (Fig 12 :123: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"*). The boundary of "alive" is left open until an instrument settles it.

**Does this commit the error the figures forbid?** No, provided the five structures are not promoted to a seventh primitive or to a necessary-and-sufficient list. The README's *"shape, not a name"* governs a missing primitive; here the structures are already in the six, so naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new one. The figure that matters is Fig 9 :32: the result is a kind of thing, not the answer itself.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to endorse a definition is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
