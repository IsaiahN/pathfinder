## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question"*

So for INS-0012 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about building reach without harm that can be stated before any particular agency, oversight body, statute or staffing rule is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a conjunction of a maintained case-level channel to a settling ground and a checking seat that cannot author what it checks, with the tools' valence left open to the residual.*
- Name, withheld: *create an independent anti-corruption commission*, *separate the registry from the inspectorate*, *require Parliament to approve data sharing*, *use merit-based civil service recruitment*.

The distinction matters here because the governance literature is almost entirely in the naming business. The state-capacity synthesis names fiscal centralization and institutional impartiality; Scott names legibility; the WGI names six governance dimensions; the ICTD article names six dimensions of data governance. Those are bindings from the governance frame onto slots, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "state capacity", "administrative legibility", "government effectiveness", "institutional impartiality", "separation of powers", "data governance" and "independent oversight". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (which case-level residual is shrinking, indexed per citizen) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating INS-0012 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question. The two conditions C1 (channel maintained) and C2 (use checked) are not two degrees of "good governance".

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions are stated, an instrument for each is named, and the eight falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For INS-0012 neither has happened within this derivation. There is no case-level measurement paying for "this specific oversight design prevented that specific harm", and the governance-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error an administrative explanation makes when it reads "service delivered, no scandal" and names its source. That reading is consistent with honest implementation, with a lucky absence of opportunity for misuse, with a suppressed complaint, with an oversight body that cannot see its own agency, or with a patronage network that has not yet been audited. The reading does not determine the source. The ICTD article supplies a field-level instance: Robodebt's debt figures were a reading produced by an algorithm combining two datasets, and the Royal Commission found the scheme *"neither fair nor legal"*. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not another name. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to governance research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about governments.

For outreach this means: a list of famous governance scholars or respected agencies who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. Public opt-in venues are used for that reason.

## 6. Corrections retained as evidence

**Correction 1, to my own F10 wording.** I initially wrote that "naming any specific oversight institution is forbidden by shape, not a name". That is too broad and is withdrawn. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. An oversight body already present in a polity's institutional vocabulary is a binding from the governance frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible.

**Correction 2, to my own use of Scott.** I initially treated *Seeing Like a State* as direct evidence that centralization always fails. That is not what the book claims, and the review record notes both that Scott is not necessarily anti-state and that his evidence selection has been criticised. It is now narrowed to mechanism-shape corroboration for F4 (differing closures are needed) only. The claim that centralization never works is not made here.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, opened directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about administration. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For INS-0012 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes and a tool whose valence can change sign.** It is not a list of administrative attributes, not a name of an oversight body, and not a ranking of countries. The names that appear in the governance literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
