# The Tether's answer on administrative capacity without repression or patronage

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No institutional measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question assumes one lever, "administrative capacity", with a good effect and a bad one. The figures refuse the framing: R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per case, never aggregated.

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

The same data link that closes the residual "the benefit did not arrive" can open the residual "a person was targeted or a favour was dispensed". Those are different slots with different anchors. The question is therefore a **union compound**, not one hard problem: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Capacity is a relation, not a stock.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Adding staff, records or powers is an agent-only improvement unless it changes contact with the people and conditions that settle the case (Fig 11 :40).

2. **Condition C1: a maintained channel to a ground that settles each case.** The anchor is the independently verified case outcome, not agency self-report and not expert consensus. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Reach is renewed only by contact with a closure that differs (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*).

3. **Condition C2: a checking seat that cannot author what it checks.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :43: *"Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking."* This is a position defined by what it cannot see, not by the reputation of whoever occupies it.

4. **The tools have no fixed valence.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A register is not "good" or "bad"; it is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another. Under Fig 12 :117 the two uses are *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."*

5. **The power to refuse is structurally different from the power to act.** Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*; Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* A state that only adds tools has no term for "this use must stop".

6. **The two conditions are unbundled and ordered.** C1 is settled first, because a checker cannot range an outcome it cannot see; C2 composes over the settled visibility, since *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it"* (Fig 12 :104). A single lever clears one region and leaves the intersection (Fig 9 :55).

7. **The specific institution is not named.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework returns a shape. Which oversight body, which statute, which staffing rule fills the slot is a `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named agency, commission, statute or merit system is the answer. Those are bindings from the governance frame, provenance owed.
- Not that centralization is always wrong or always right. The figure-grounded claim is about contact and closure, not about a preferred constitutional form.
- Not that one lever can achieve both conditions. The question is a union, not a partition.
- Not that a single national capacity or governance score can settle which citizen is safe. Such scores are budget filters, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not `grounded`. No case-level measurement pays for the shape here.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only account of state capacity. That would be a frame certifying its own limit.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a result *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Per-case outcome test:** for a named citizen and a named administrative act, was the benefit delivered and the right kept, verified by a source the agency cannot move?
- **Valence test:** does the same tool's measured sign flip when the residual changes from "benefit failed" to "person targeted"? An invariant sign would refute the valence claim.
- **Checker non-access test:** can the oversight body author or reverse the decision it reviews? If it can and detection does not degrade, the checking-seat condition is wrong.
- **Two-condition intervention test:** does one lever restore both C1 and C2 with no categorically different residue left over? If so, the union reading collapses.
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** the eight listed in `01` section 4, in particular an aggregate index that prospectively predicts per-citizen harm, and a state that renews reach with no import from a differing closure.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The state-capacity vocabulary used here is a public, contestable frame, not nature's own list. The answer stops where the available instruments can no longer distinguish honest implementation from suppression, patronage or a blind checker, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the agency or the statute that solves this. It says that administrative capacity is not one stock but a set of case-level residuals, so the move that makes a state see citizens better is also the move that can give it new ways to target them. The answer is therefore a conjunction of two kept-separate conditions: a maintained channel to a ground that settles each case from outside the agency, and a checking seat that cannot author what it checks and can refuse a use. The tools themselves have no fixed valence, they change sign with the residual, so no list of "good" administrative instruments is an answer, and a single lever clears only one of the two conditions. What the framework offers is that shape, two distinct failure modes, eight falsifiers written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would close it. Which specific institution fills the slot stays open until the ground settles it.
