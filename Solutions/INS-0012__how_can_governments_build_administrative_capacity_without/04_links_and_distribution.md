## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The per-slot split is already visible in the field.** The state-capacity synthesis distinguishes capacity from "the tactics that states use to gain compliance from society". That is the distinction the figures enforce between the service slot and the harm slot. It is corroboration, not a verdict, and it comes from the same broad governance pool as the other field sources (Fig 2 :40).

2. **The measurement instruments are themselves part of the problem.** The WGI critique shows why a single "capacity and integrity" score cannot serve as the anchor: the indexes share an evidence pool, are perception-based, and are weak on actionability. Fig 1 :27 says a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. The practical form: a government's own e-governance score is not the ground, and a cross-country aggregate is a filter for budget, never a witness for which citizen is safe.

3. **The dual-use claim has a real, documented instance.** The ICTD article on linked tax data is the clearest field-level statement: data integration improves administration and simultaneously requires rules on purpose, access, correction and oversight. Robodebt is the valence switch observed, with a named failure and a public commission finding. This is the strongest world corroboration in this folder, and it is one witness.

4. **Legitimacy is a relation that can be checked at the case level.** Weigand's interactive dignity, as reported on the political legitimacy page, says people expect procedures to be fair and practices respectful, with a serving rather than extractive attitude. That is a case-level, checkable expectation, which fits Fig 2 :29's anchor requirement better than a global trust score. It is a low-overlap frame relative to tax administration.

5. **The refusal capacity has an economics analogue.** Principal-agent theory reports that the problem worsens when the principal lacks the means to punish the agent, and that the multiple-principal problem is especially serious in the public sector. That corroborates the need for a negation operator (Operators table :58) from a different field, with no shared evidence pool with the governance sources.

6. **The framework essays are internal, not independent witnesses.** Their agreement with the figures is expected. They are used only for the canonical reading of "shape", for the simulation status, and for the source-discrimination limit. They carry no independent corroboration weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the two conditions and the eight refuters; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the two-condition shape were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A mass send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two conditions and their refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer before any theory is shown; (3) only then the three local framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This simulated derivation is `specified` and ungrounded; it is offered as a shape with refuters, not as a finding. It must be checked before it is circulated.
