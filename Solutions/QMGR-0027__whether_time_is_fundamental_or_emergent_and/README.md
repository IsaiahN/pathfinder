## Question

Whether time is fundamental or emergent, and how quantum evolution relates to it.

**Source:** `QM & GR Questions.txt` line 37. **Section:** Quantum Foundations and Measurement.

Restated as an effect: a physics claim uses time in two roles. In quantum mechanics the state evolves in a parameter t generated by the Hamiltonian, so time is an input. In emergent-time programs time is not primitive and must be recovered from correlations, thermal states, causal order, or update steps. The residual is the mismatch between the two roles. Option A (fundamental) leaves the ordered succession of differences unanalysed. Option B (emergent) owes a derivation that does not already presuppose ordering, succession, or difference. What an answer would look like: a statement of which role the figures license, at which level, with a stated refuter, rather than a verdict on nature.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." People chosen for receptiveness are chosen to agree, which manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."
3. **Do not hand the checker the interior.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 sees the residual and the shape claim only.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

This folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-19) warns that LLM output simulates an approximate answer and that "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." It cannot settle the question it reports on. The verdict below is what the figures license at the level of shape, not a claim about which answer nature has realised.
