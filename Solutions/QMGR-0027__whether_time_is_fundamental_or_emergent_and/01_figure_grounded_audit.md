## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question packs two roles of time into one phrase.

- **Role 1, the input role.** In textbook quantum evolution the state is parameterised by t: the Hamiltonian generates change in a background time. Time is consumed, not produced.
- **Role 2, the output role.** In emergent-time programs (thermal time, relational clocks, entanglement-based clocks, discrete update steps) time is not in the primitive furniture. It is recovered from a state, a correlation, an algebra, or a rule.

**What fails to resolve.** If time is primitive, quantum evolution merely rides on it and owes no account of its origin, but the primitive is left unanalysed. If time is emergent, the derivation must produce "the ordered succession of differences" without already smuggling in order, succession, or difference. A clock built out of correlations can re-describe change, but whether it *is* time or only a time-representation is exactly the unresolved point.

**Arity / symmetry / scale of R.** Two roles, so this is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54-57: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again. A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Symmetry: the two roles are not symmetric; one consumes a parameter, the other derives one. Scale: the substrate claim is level-relative (Fig 12 :104), so a verdict at one level does not transfer to another without a round trip (Fig 4 :30).

**What an answer would look like (Direction).** Before searching, bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term: a statement of (a) whether the time slot is a substrate member or a composition, (b) which figure line governs that slot, (c) what level the claim is made at, and (d) what would refute it. Formula "Direction": write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual like anything else. This paragraph is that binding.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — Time occupies a substrate slot and is indivisible within the six-term set (governing line)

Fig 13 :3: **"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."**

Fig 13 :9-12: **"space" / "near, far, between, contained" / "time" / "the ordered succession of differences."** The Symbols table :86-88 repeats time as a state: "The ordered succession of differences."

Fig 13 :41: **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**

Fig 13 :42: **"Not hard to derive. There is no route. Ordering does not produce adjacency; duration does not produce conservation; a quantity does not produce"**

Fig 13 :63-65: **"time" / "first, third" / "no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder"**

**Verdict, D1 only: `specified`, `triangulated`.** Fig 13 and the Symbols table are two low-overlap routes within the framework (the six-term derivation versus the notation table) to the same slot: time is a state and one of six indivisible members. "Fundamental" in this framework means *substrate member of the minimal set*, a structural fact about a level, not an assertion about nature's own furniture.

**Refuter.** Derive any one of the six from the other five (Fig 13 :143-144 makes this the standing refutation), or show a load-bearing law that survives removing the term the minimality table says it needs. If either fires, F1 falls.

**Mode note.** This is figure-internal. Fig 13 :143-144: "Indivisibility and minimality are claims and both are refutable: derive a member from the other five, or derive one of the established laws without a member the table says it needs." So F1 is a candidate, not a settlement.

### F2 — Adjacency is not duration; spacetime is a derivative, not a substrate object

Fig 13 :35-36: **"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network. Each answers a question the other two"** / **"cannot: adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."**

Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Fig 13 :43-44: **"the fact that things relate at all. A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or"** / **"smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

**Verdict, D1 to D3 boundary: `specified`, `triangulated` (Fig 13 :74-75 and :43-44 are separate passages with different objects: cross-kind composition and minimality).** Space and time are distinct states; the 4D spacetime continuum and the metric are compositions, at the framework level, not members of the six. This is exactly the line the exemplar `QMGR-0037` used, and it transfers here because the subject is the same slot.

**Refuter.** Show the framework must admit a direct state-state bond (space-time with no structure in the slot), or that the continuum is a seventh member. Both are refused by the quoted lines, so a refusal would mean the six-term set is wrong, not that the reading is strained.

### F3 — Quantum evolution composes over time; it does not generate the time state

Fig 13 :76: **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

Fig 13 :63-65 (minimality table): time covers "first, third"; without it "no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder."

Fig 13 :28-32: **"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."** and **"And because each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next, it is also where the randomness comes from"**

**Verdict, D2: `specified`, `triangulated` (Fig 13 :76 and the minimality table).** Hamiltonian evolution is a composition of energy (a state) over time (a state) through a structure, which the figure explicitly licenses as a gradient-over-time. Time-translation symmetry is listed as a consequence of having the time term. So quantum evolution *presupposes* the time state in the framework: it is a consumer of the slot, not a mint of it. A clock built from a subsystem (Page-Wootters style) is a second-order composition that serves as a time-*representation*; it does not add a seventh member and does not derive the substrate time.

**Refuter.** Exhibit a derivation inside closure(Γ) of the ordered succession of differences from energy, spectrum, and network alone, with no time term and no hidden ordering in the initial conditions. If that succeeds, F3 falls and "time is emergent" wins at D2.

**World check, secondary only.** Chua (2024) argues the thermal time hypothesis "requires dynamics -- and hence time -- to get off the ground, thereby running into worries of circularity." That is the same shape from a different field. Moreva et al. (2014) demonstrate a clock-correlation experiment where "an 'internal' observer that becomes correlated with the clock photon sees the other system evolve, while an 'external' observer that only observes global properties of the two photons can prove it is static." That is a *clock construction*, not a derivation of the substrate state; it corroborates F3's distinction rather than refuting it.

### F4 — The barrier: "emergent" as derivation of the substrate state is unreachable inside the closure

Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."**

Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

Fig 13 :116-118: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever"** / **"rearranges the kinds already held. Meanwhile the arrangements over those six do not run out. So a frame can search without end and"** / **"still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach."**

Fig 13 :134: **"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

**Verdict, D5 and D1: `specified`, `triangulated` (Fig 8, Fig 9, Fig 13, three different figures with different objects).** If "emergent" means *derived by composition from the other five*, the answer is unreachable-in-principle inside this closure, by the definition of closure. If it means *a new kind of thing*, that requires an import (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.") and is therefore not a derivation from within. If it means *a metric, an arrow, a clock reading, a continuum*, it is an ordinary composition and is allowed. The word "emergent" is doing three different jobs; the figures sort them rather than choosing a side.

**Refuter.** Show a composition inside the six that yields a seventh member, or show that the closure is not idempotent. Neither is available without breaking Figures 8 and 13; a break would be a framework failure, not a reading error.

### F5 — "Fundamental" is level-relative; the dichotomy dissolves across levels

Fig 12 :103-104: **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."** / **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

Fig 10 :68: **"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does"**

Fig 4 :33: **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

**Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 12, Fig 10, Fig 4).** At the substrate level, time is an atom and "fundamental" is the right word *within the framework*. At a higher level a settled clock-composition becomes an atom and can be treated as fundamental *for that level*. The two answers are not contradictory because they are answers to different level-questions. This is the single most important finding for the question as worded: "fundamental or emergent" is not one question once levels are distinguished.

**Refuter.** Exhibit an absolute irreducible (a bottom) or show that a settled molecule cannot serve as an atom. Fig 12 :104 and Fig 4 :33 forbid both, so a successful demonstration would rewrite the framework.

**Round trip warning.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Carrying "time is fundamental" from Fig 13 up to a physics claim about nature loses the level qualifier. `11_audit.md` measures this R_T.

### F6 — The continuum is a derivative, so emergent-time programs are legitimate compositions

Fig 13 :43-44 (quoted in F2): the continuum was removed as a candidate seventh member because it is "a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time." Fig 13 :124-125: **"holds too: the same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance. Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a"** / **"particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

**Verdict, D3: `specified`, single-source (Fig 13 plus the Substrate section of `THE_FORMULA.md`, which share a framework author).** Programs that recover a continuum, a metric, a thermal flow, or a clock from more basic structure are doing exactly what the framework says composition does: they are deriving a *derivative*, not a member. So the framework does not refute emergent-time research. It refuses to call the result a new primitive. This is the bridge that keeps the answer honest toward the world literature.

**Refuter.** Show that a recovered continuum is a seventh substrate member rather than a composition, or that smoothness is a member rather than "what a particular composition leaves behind."

### F8 — World bindings are imports in provenance terms, not derivations

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

**Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure rule, with world corroboration secondary.** Martinetti and Rovelli (2003) state the thermal time hypothesis as "time is the physical quantity determined by the flow defined by a state over an observable algebra." Their agreement with F3 is expected because they are the source of the physical framing; it is not independent corroboration of the framework.

**Refuter.** Show a world program that derives the ordered succession of differences with no time-like primitive and no circularity, and F3/F4 must be re-censused. Chua (2024) is the standing counter-source, so a genuine derivation would also refute his critique.

**Refuter.** Another sensorium, or an instrument that registers an axis these six cannot represent, would break the claim that the set is minimal for all observers. The essay itself names this as open.

## 8. Conclusion (after refuter and canonical check)

The figures do not return "fundamental" or "emergent" as a single verdict. They return a **level-indexed shape**: at the substrate level time is one of six indivisible states, "the ordered succession of differences," and nothing composed from the other five yields it (F1, F2). Quantum evolution is a composition *over* that state, generated by energy and parameterised by time, so it consumes the slot rather than producing it (F3). What emergent-time programs derive, successfully, is a derivative: a clock, a continuum, a thermal flow, an arrow (F6). Calling any of those the time state itself would be a category error at this level, and deriving a seventh member is unreachable inside the closure (F4). Because irreducibility is a fact about a level and not about the world (F5), the dichotomy is a union of two level-questions that must be answered separately, and neither is settled at the ground (F7, F9).

**Verdict: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.** Triangulated where two figures with different objects agree (F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F7); single-source where only one figure passage or the framework author's essays bear (F6, F8, F9). No domain verdict was averaged with another. Gaps: the ground question (R4) is unreached, and whether a settled clock-composition is the time state at a higher level is open. Per Fig 8 :39, the search finding nothing is not a proof of absence; per Fig 13 :134, unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.
