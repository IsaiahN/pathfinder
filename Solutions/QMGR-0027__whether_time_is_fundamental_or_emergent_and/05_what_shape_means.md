## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape, allowed:** time is a substrate state; quantum evolution composes over it; derivatives such as clocks and the continuum are compositions; "fundamental" and "emergent" are level-indexed answers to different questions.
- **Name, not returned by the framework:** which particular physical object in nature realises the time slot, whether nature's own list has six members, and which fundamental theory (if any) settles the ground question.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, then steering

*Library of Babel* on vocabulary: search with the words you already have that are closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The search is steered by the question as tether: "you can ask: Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"

Applied here, the bootstrapping was:

1. Start with the two role descriptions (time as input to evolution; time as output of a derivation).
2. Find the closest framework vocabulary: state versus structure, member versus composition, level versus substrate.
3. Recurse until the residual stops registering: the mismatch resolves into a level distinction, not a single choice.

*Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here that means the answer to "fundamental or emergent" is composed in the census: once levels are enumerated, the dichotomy no longer names one question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens why a reading is not a source: metamerism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several causes produce one reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was "not by argument" that the case was proven wrong, but "by the arrival of a better instrument."

For this question: observing a clock evolve does not tell you whether time is a substrate state or an internal construction. The observation is a projection; the source is underdetermined. That is exactly why R4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` asks for an instrument, not another argument.

## 4. Correction to my own §3 ("shape, not a name" applied too broadly)

I initially wrote the rule as if it forbade naming *any* slot in the six-term set. It does not. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.

Time **is** held in Γ: Fig 13 :11-12 names it "the ordered succession of differences," and the Symbols table lists it as a state. Naming the time slot is therefore not forbidden; it is reading the framework's own atom. What remains forbidden is:

- promoting a derivative (clock, continuum, arrow) to a seventh member;
- claiming the framework returns a name for nature's primitive list;
- treating a binding from the physics frame (thermal flow, entanglement clock) as a derivation inside the closure.

This correction is recorded here rather than silently applied. It changes F9 of `01` from a prohibition into a scope limit.

## 5. Why "fundamental or emergent" is a shape question, not a name question

*They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

So when the framework says time is a substrate state, it is reporting a visible choice with a stated refuter (Fig 13 :143-144), not claiming to have read nature's own list. The shape survives even if the particular six are later replaced: what remains is that a time-like slot must be held or imported, and that a derivative cannot do the work of a primitive without a new frame.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous physicists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The ask is for a check against a ground, not for endorsement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle whether time is fundamental or emergent in nature.

## 7. One-paragraph version

A shape is a narrowed answer you can state before you can name it, and the framework returns shapes, not names. Here the shape is level-indexed: time is a substrate state in the six-term set, quantum evolution composes over it rather than deriving it, and clocks, continua and thermal flows are legitimate second-order compositions that must not be promoted to primitives. Naming the time slot is allowed because the figure already holds it; claiming the framework has read nature's own list is not, because the six are a visible, revisable guess. An instrument, not agreement and not another argument, is what would move this from `specified` to `grounded`.
