## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The phrase "time is fundamental or emergent" packs two level-questions. At the substrate level of the six-term set, time is one of six indivisible states, defined as "the ordered succession of differences," and no arrangement of the other five yields it. Quantum evolution is a composition over that state: the Hamiltonian generates change in the ordering, so evolution consumes the time slot rather than producing it. What emergent-time programs derive, successfully, is a derivative: a clock constructed from correlations, a continuum, a thermal flow, an arrow. Those are legal second-order compositions, and promoting any of them to a seventh substrate member would be a category error. Because irreducibility is a fact about a level and not about the world, "fundamental" and "emergent" are both true at different levels and are never averaged. The framework returns this shape, five refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. It does not claim nature has realised these six terms.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** time is a substrate state and is indivisible at that level; a continuum is explicitly not a member.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :3 "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds." Fig 13 :41 "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it." Fig 13 :43-44 "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."
- **What to check:** is the citation accurate, and does the minimality table at Fig 13 :63-65 really list time as load-bearing for the first and third laws?
- **Refuter:** derive any one of the six from the other five; derive a law the table says needs time without time; or show a seventh primitive is required. Any one fires R1 and the substrate claim falls.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable against quantum evolution

- **Statement:** quantum evolution composes over time rather than generating the time state; an internal clock is a second-order composition, not a substrate derivation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"
- **What to check:** is the characterisation faithful? In the Schrodinger picture the Hamiltonian generates change in a background parameter t; in Page-Wootters type constructions an internal subsystem serves as a clock and an external description is static (Moreva et al. 2014). Does the construction derive the ordering, or presuppose it in the correlations it reads?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a derivation inside the closure of the ordered succession of differences from non-temporal primitives with no ordering in the premises (R3). Chua (2024) argues exactly this circularity for thermal time; a successful derivation would refute both him and this claim.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a physics-facing edge

- **Statement:** emergent-as-derivation-of-the-substrate is unreachable inside the closure; emergent-as-clock is reachable and legitimate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
- **What to check:** is the two-meaning split of "emergent" forced by these lines, or imported? Check whether the thermal-time and entanglement-clock programs in fact derive a primitive or a clock.
- **Refuter:** show a composition inside the six that yields a seventh member, or show the closure is not idempotent. Also fire R4: an instrument that distinguishes a background time parameter from an internally generated clock in a regime where they predict differently.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and no last room (Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room").
- Not a refutation of emergent-time research. Clocks, continua, thermal flows, relational time and discrete update steps are compositions and are allowed.
- Not "quantum mechanics generates time," and not "gravity is not curvature." Settled physics is not overwritten.
- Not that nature's own minimal set has six members. The six are a visible, revisable choice.
- Not that a clock is the time state. It is a molecule that becomes an atom at the next level.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by experiment.
