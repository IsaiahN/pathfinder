## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict"* (Fig 7 :35). Adding participants raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for people likely to agree is one pool wearing many names.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries only the residual and its refuter.

Legally: scraping private contact details for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The useful audience is people with a public, checkable record of contact with this residual, reached through a channel they opted into, and positioned to refute it.
