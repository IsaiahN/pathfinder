## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question:

- **not a shape:** "build three cooling centres and a seawall".
- **a shape:** "refuse the past-as-guide term on the live slots; unbundle the compound; acquire vocabulary before objective and objective before plan; enumerate the habitat and its cascade; keep cuts reversible; let the ground settle".

The first is a name and a recording. The second constrains any admissible plan without selecting one.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."
- The same essay names the failure this question is prone to: "If your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is Fig 9 :54-58's union compound, arrived at from a different field.
- Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." That is Fig 2 :45, frames range against the ground.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the ground is observed conditions and outcomes after the novel condition arrives, not stakeholder agreement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A community that borrows an adaptive-pathways method from a water utility in another basin has imported a method with provenance; a community that copies its plan because a consultancy recommends it has adopted a recording.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the difference: an instrument that reads green "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And the resolution: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." A plan that worked in the past is a reading, not a demonstration that the same source (the same climate regime) will persist.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Applied here: a community observes damage, heat illness, or a flooded substation and calls it "climate change". The reading does not determine the source. It could be a shifting gradient, a network failure, a maintenance failure, a governance failure, or a combination with no historical precedent. Naming the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a confident attribution.

This is also why the question's phrase "unlike anything in their historical experience" is itself a residual description, not a diagnosis. It says what failed to resolve (the past-as-guide prediction), not which structure sits in the slot.

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A well-known adaptation consultancy or a heavily cited framework gives a plan a budget to be tried. It does not make the plan correct.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" mean for this question?** It means the constrained form a community plan must take under genuine novelty, stated before any particular measure is named:

1. a per-slot residual, never an aggregate score (Fig 1 :19-22);
2. a refusal of the false held term before any new plan (Fig 5 :53, Formula step 2);
3. an unbundled union, each compound with its own ground (Fig 9 :54-58);
4. inward and outward run as two tracks (Fig 6 :29-31);
5. vocabulary, then objective, then plan (Fig 3 :25-27);
6. an enumerated habitat with cascades, not a composed wish list (Fig 11 :43-48);
7. ranked and reversible cuts, the ground as witness (Fig 9 :41, :60);
8. methods transferred across scales, not recordings (Fig 4 :22-25);
9. an honest stop at what the instruments can distinguish (README, Fig 8 :39).

**Does the framework name which measure a community should adopt?** No. That requires a ground reading in that community's habitat, or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, naming a measure is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Correction to my own §3-§4.** The "shape, not a name" rule governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Adaptation measures are not missing primitives; they are bindings onto atoms the community already holds (shelter, network, gradient, comparison). Naming a candidate measure is therefore not forbidden. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* the measure, or that an adopted binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**). My §3 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reason a measure cannot be licensed from a damage reading alone is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both readings are kept visible.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any community's planning and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Two practical consequences.**

1. The outreach asks a specialist to check the shape, not to endorse it. The refuter is written first (§3 of `01`, §2 of `08`). A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
2. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). If three planners agree after reading the framework, that is one evidence pool talking, not three witnesses. The only witness that matters is the ground: what happened in the habitat after the condition arrived.
