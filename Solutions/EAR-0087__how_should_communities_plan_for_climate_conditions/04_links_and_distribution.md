## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The residual is a real, mappable object, not a metaphor.** Williams et al. (2007) quantify no-analogue climates and show that *"standard conservation solutions... may be insufficient"* in the affected regions. This is why the figure line "refuted: a term we hold is wrong" is not being read as a rhetorical flourish. It also supplies the honest limit: *"the lack of current analogs for future climates limits our ability to validate ecological model predictions."*
2. **Decision science reaches the same shape from a different closure.** The Toolkit's DMDU lesson says "predict-then-act" breaks down under deep uncertainty and replaces confidence in a model with confidence in a decision, tested by stress tests over many futures. That is the world-level echo of Fig 8 :32 (search inside a closure cannot add an atom) and Fig 9 :41 (keep cuts ranked and reversible).
3. **Ecology has already faced the historical-reference failure.** Perring et al. (2013) and the BES explainer (2024) both say history stops being a usable target once thresholds are crossed. They corroborate the refusal in F1 from a low-overlap field. Their agreement is still one evidence pool for the novelty concept, so it is recorded as corroboration, not as a second witness for the figures.
4. **Institutions supply enabling conditions, not the ground.** IPCC AR6 WGII C.3 and D.2 say governance, finance and inclusive processes enable adaptation. This matters because the derivation refuses consensus as an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30): inclusive governance is an enabling condition in the world literature, which is exactly the role the figures give it.
5. **One overlap to watch.** Williams et al. (2007) is cited inside the Perring et al. reference list, and both use the no-analogue concept. They are two publications but not two independent witnesses on the existence of novelty. The ledger marks this.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a finished plan before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before naming any framework.
- **Fig 2 :42** — *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Irreversible adaptation decisions are high cost, so select reviewers from different closures, not from a list of likely supporters.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what instrument would show it; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third stage)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* — and Fig 2 :45 — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 are live, and any one of them firing removes the corresponding claim before it is sent.
