## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 says "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: per household slot, ground-anchored, union-unbundled, triangulated across low-overlap instrument frames, reversible. "Focus on cash" or "focus on assets" is a name, not this shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." The binding condition is described before the instrument is chosen.

Applied here: describe the household's material shortfall in effect terms (what exactly is missing, for whom, under what conditions) before naming transfer, service, wage or asset as the response.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question that means a measured material outcome, not an input metric.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A result from wage-setting research can be imported into the policy shape only if it explains the per-slot residual and carries its provenance.

*They're Scanning Books* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the policy categories does not make them neutral; it makes the choice visible.

## 4. Why the "focus on one instrument" answer is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (as recorded in the exemplar) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a rise in household consumption is a reading; its source could be a transfer, a service, a wage, an asset drawdown, or a combination. Naming "cash" as the source from the consumption reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The J-PAL framing that questions remain about "for whom, and under what conditions" is the demand for that discriminator.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A policy frame cannot validate its own instrument with its own delivered-input metric.

## 5. Q2 — ANSWERED: what licenses a particular instrument?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular instrument is licensed only by the ground settling a choice for a stated binding condition, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the single-focus answer commit the error the README forbids?** Yes. It names an instrument where the framework returns a shape, and (in the common policy argument) it names the source of a welfare reading without a source-discriminating instrument. The correct form stays: the residual is described per slot, the union is unbundled, and the slot stays open until the ground settles it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Cash transfers, public services, wage-setting institutions and asset ownership are **all already held frames** in the policy library. Naming one is a **binding from the policy frame**, not the invention of a new member. What remains wrong in the single-focus answer is calling that binding a derivation that settles the whole question, and treating a consumption reading as a source discriminator. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted; the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held frames. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with a single instrument is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** an evaluation of any policy and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
