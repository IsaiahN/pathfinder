## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: should policy focus on cash transfers, public services, wage-setting institutions, or asset ownership to reduce poverty? Option A: pick one instrument as the focus and defend it. Option B: refuse the single-focus framing, give the shape any answer must have, and leave the empirical ranking open to measurement. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a household's realized material condition and what any single lever delivers. Arity: four candidate conditions that overlap, so a union compound rather than a partition of one cause. Symmetry: not symmetric, because a lever's effect depends on which condition binds (workless, working-poor and assetless households are not interchangeable operands). Scale: first order at the household slot, second order at the population aggregate, where aggregation discards the where-to-look signal. Fig 9 names a case in two conditions a categorically different compound, not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, household material condition (the ground).** Actors: household members, their time, health, skills, cash, assets, services received. Currency: realized consumption, security, capability. Anchor: the household's actual material outcome, which does not move because a policy frame re-describes it. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 2, policy instrument frames.** Actors: transfer programs, service providers, wage floors, asset accounts, administrators. Currency: delivered inputs (dollars, service units, wage rates, account balances), which are frame-internal. Anchor: none of its own; it must borrow Domain 1's anchor. Question type: four low-overlap frames, not a list of costs.
- **Domain 3, evaluation and verification.** Actors: researchers, agencies, budget offices. Currency: measured indicators, agreement, cost-effectiveness. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on overlapping evidence pools, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Domain 4, intersections (compound).** Actors: households in two or more conditions simultaneously. Currency: compound residual. Anchor: the same Domain 1 ground. Question type: union, whose intersections need their own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Name the material conditions and household slots, state what counts as progress for that slot, then choose the instrument; refuse the single-focus framing before minting an instrument choice; keep every cut ranked and reversible.

Strain test applied: Fig 12's functional group (instruments as members of one kind) and Fig 9's union (conditions as overlapping) govern different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains, concerning how "contact" is measured in a social habitat; it is named in `01` section 6, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as written is refused: the four options are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition served by one lever. The figure-grounded shape survives at `general` / `specified`, ungrounded: read the residual per household slot, anchor on the realized material outcome rather than the delivered input, treat agent-only change as insufficient to change contact, combine the four instruments as low-overlap frames selected by residual, and keep every cut ranked and reversible. The single-focus answers (cash, services, wages or assets alone) are not licensed. The empirical ranking of the four for a stated binding condition is a `gap` that only a measured outcome can close. One strain is live: how contact with a social habitat is measured.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name where a shape is owed, and by scoring a program with the input it produces. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is household welfare, so independence is worth buying.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-focus framing can be withdrawn without touching the shape; the ranking slot stays open rather than asserting a winner. No instrument is refused and no primitive is withdrawn, so a corrected packet can supersede a sent one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A partition result showing disjoint conditions; a delivered-input metric that tracks the material outcome identically in every slot; an agent-only change shown to move contact with no habitat change; a figure re-census admitting a line that names one instrument as the unique focus; or a measured outcome ranking one instrument for a stated condition, which would close the ranking gap rather than refute the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate, not grounded.
