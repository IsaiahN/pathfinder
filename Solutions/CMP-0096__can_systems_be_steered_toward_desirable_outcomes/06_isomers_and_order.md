## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are the two modes isomers? (correction to my own §5b of `05`)

- Fig 12 :117 **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 11 :55 *"Two modes, and only one of them has a seat."*
- Write the unmaintained mode as `habitat(actors, conditions, relations) with ground selecting`, and the maintained mode as `habitat(same atoms) with seat → introduction + instruments, ground still selecting`. The atom set is shared. What differs is the bond to the seat: absent in one, present as introduction and instrument-building in the other.
- By Fig 12 :117 they are therefore **isomers of one habitat**: same atoms, different arrangement, different behaviour (arrival waited for versus arrival worked on). This is not an analogy; it is the figure's own definition applied to the two modes.
- **Correction to `05` §5b:** naming a mode is a binding onto held atoms, which is allowed. What is not allowed is claiming that binding adds a new atom. Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Triangulated (Fig 12 plus Fig 11).
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if the presence of a seat makes no difference to contact (so the two modes can be written with the same bond), the isomer claim fails and the two are one mode with a label change.

### Q2 - What is the dependency order of a steering intervention?

- Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
- Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
- Fig 10 :29 *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*; Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."*
- Formula step 2: refuse before mint (¬ is not a bond).
- Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*: settle the first-order intervention before composing the next one.

So the order is: describe R in effect terms → enumerate the habitat → keep the anchor reachable → refuse the held term that is wrong → introduce an actor or instrument → settle against the ground → compose at the next level. Effort spent above a break cannot rescue the broken link (Fig 3 :19 *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

**Falsifier:** if an intervention that introduces actors before enumerating contact still produces a checkable residual against a fixed ground, the order claim is weakened to a preference and is recorded as such.

### Q3 - Which structures appear in a steering system?

- **Catalyst.** Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A steering institution that survives its own use is a catalyst; one that is consumed by it is not. The difference is not recorded in a policy description unless deliberately tracked.
- **Cycle.** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Feedback in governance is a cycle, and its sign (correcting or reinforcing) decides whether it closes. A one-way command chain cannot close by construction.
- **Comparison.** Operators table :51 *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* Without ⋛ a steering system cannot state the desirable outcome it is aimed at.
- **Negation.** Operators table :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* A governance vocabulary with rules but no way to refuse a held rule can only accumulate.

### Q4 - What order is the union of the two modes?

- The question bundles two conditions into one residual, Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific"* (continues :58 *"compound."*).
- Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* So the maintained and unmaintained modes must not be averaged into a single "degree of steering" score. They are a partition of modes within a union compound, each with its own ground contact.
- Polycentric arrangements compose the modes at the next level: Fig 7 :33 *"→ variation inside, selection between"*; Fig 7 :35 *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

## ANALOGUES (corroboration only, never verdicts)

- **Guided self-organization** (world source, 2008 to 2009): regulation of a self-organizing process *"by restricting local interactions between the system components, rather than following an explicit control mechanism or a global design blueprint."* This is the world-field version of introduction and local constraint. Corroborates Fig 11 :61. Overlap with the framework: low.
- **Ecosystem engineering** (named inside Fig 11 :65): *"Introducing an actor for its cascade is ecosystem engineering, after Jones, Lawton and Shachak, 1994. Cane toads and kudzu are the same move with the cascade unenumerated."* Corroborates the asymmetry; the failure case is the cascade not enumerated.
- **Nested common-pool institutions** (Ostrom, via the world source read this session): *"Nested enterprises"* organized in multiple layers. Corroborates Fig 7 :33 selection between rooms. Overlap: low.
- **NOT an analogue:** autopoiesis. Maturana refused *"self-organization"* as *"Operationally it is impossible."* It narrows the vocabulary; it is not evidence for the habitat reading.

## CONCLUSION

The maintained and unmaintained modes are **isomers of one habitat**: same atoms, different bond to the seat, different substance. The dependency order is describe → enumerate → keep anchor reachable → refuse → introduce → settle → compose. A steering system additionally needs a catalyst, a correctly signed cycle, ⋛ for the goal, and ¬ for refusal. The union compound must be unbundled rather than averaged. This rescues what is defensible in the intuition that steering and self-organization conflict (they are different modes with different bonds) without importing a new primitive or treating consensus as a ground.
