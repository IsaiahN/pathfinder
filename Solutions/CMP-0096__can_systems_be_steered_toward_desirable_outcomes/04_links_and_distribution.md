## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Guided self-organization is the field's own version of the question.** The self-organization overview says regulation can work *"by restricting local interactions between the system components, rather than following an explicit control mechanism or a global design blueprint."* That is introductions and constraints on local contact, which is what Fig 11 :61 calls *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* High corroboration with the figure verdict, low overlap with the framework's author.
2. **Leverage points rank self-organization near the top.** Meadows places *"The power to add, change, evolve, or self-organize system structure"* at number 4 of 12, above goals and paradigms only in the sense that those are higher. She also warns that *"Encouraging variability and experimentation and diversity means 'losing control.'"* This corroborates the figure verdict that steering which keeps self-organization must work by changing contact, and that the attempt to eliminate variation is the named failure.
3. **Requisite variety sets a floor on the steerer.** Ashby's *"only variety can destroy variety"* predicts that a governor with less variety than the governed system cannot regulate it. This corroborates Fig 11 :37 *"enumerate the habitat, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, read the ground"*, which is a procedure for acquiring variety, not for suppressing it.
4. **Polycentric governance matches selection between rooms.** Ostrom's design principles include *"Minimal recognition of rights to organize"* and *"Nested enterprises"*. These corroborate Fig 7 :33 *"→ variation inside, selection between"* and Fig 7 :35 *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
5. **Autopoiesis is a narrowing, not a synonym.** Maturana's refusal of *"self-organization"* as *"Operationally it is impossible"* means the framework's habitat usage must not be silently identified with autopoiesis. This is a partial conflict with loose usage in the field, and it sent the derivation back to the census (F9 strain 1), not to a softer reading.

No world source overrides a figure verdict. Where a source conflicts, the conflict is recorded and the census is revisited.
