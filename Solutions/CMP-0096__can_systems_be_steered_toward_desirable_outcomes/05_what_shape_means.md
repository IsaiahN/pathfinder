## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, as mapped in the exemplar: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is a set of constraints on any steering arrangement that preserves self-organization, not a named institution, policy, or desirable outcome.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 11 :47 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The habitat is enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*).

For this question, describing the effect means saying what you would observe if a steering arrangement were preserving self-organization: the ground still decides; contact changes; new actors can be introduced and old contact cannot be deleted; residuals stay per slot; agreement among like institutions is not treated as a verdict.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles a term, verified only by what pays. The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So naming a particular institution (a polycentric board, a specific regulator, a given incentive scheme) as *the* answer to a steering slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. The framework returns the shape of a steering arrangement; which concrete arrangement occupies the slot is a binding provenanced by whoever makes it.

## 4. Why one global score is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, as mapped in the exemplar, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A single governance index is the same error. It reads one value for a system whose residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), then names a source (the system is well steered) from a projection that several distinct underlying states could produce. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. The shape for THIS question

The shape any answer must have is four constraints:

1. **A seat may act, and the ground must remain the ground.** Fig 11 :22 *"the ground still decides"*; Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."*
2. **The seat's moves are introductions and instruments, not subtraction of contact.** Fig 11 :61 *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
3. **The work is permanent, not a solved state.** Fig 11 :63 *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."*
4. **"Desirable" is bound as an ordinary term before it can steer.** Fig 5 :65-66 requires writing down what an answer would look like, binding it, and letting it accrue a residual; Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*

That is a shape. Naming a specific governance regime, KPI set, or policy mix as the answer exceeds it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule when I first drafted §3. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. For this question the relevant atoms (habitat, ground, introduction, selection) are already in the framework's vocabulary, so naming a steering mode is a **binding onto held atoms**, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is claiming the binding adds a new atom or moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. A single global score still names a source from a lossy reading. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, as mapped in the exemplar: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with a steering claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
