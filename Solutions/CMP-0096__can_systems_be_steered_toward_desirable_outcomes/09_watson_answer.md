## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a seat can steer a system toward a desirable outcome without destroying its capacity for self-organization. Option A: assert that steering is compatible with self-organization without conditions, treating goals and feedback as sufficient. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, which says steering is one of two modes of a habitat and is compatible only when the seat acts on contact while the ground still decides. What each leads to: A turns the success metric into a movable anchor and produces coherent drift that nobody notices; B yields a checkable shape, named forbidden moves, refuters written in advance, and an open slot for "desirable" until an operator binds it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, conditionally. Steering preserves self-organization when it is ground-maintenance: enumerate contact, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, keep the anchor fixed, and leave the ground to decide. Steering destroys self-organization when it moves the anchor, promotes a convention to an outcome, or substitutes a model for the habitat. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so improving the center in isolation is inert. Governance should be polycentric selection with independent rooms, reversible cuts, and instruments kept as filters rather than verdicts. "Desirable" is not a framework output; it is an operator-supplied binding that must be written down, made refusable, and checked per slot. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result: a shape and a route, not a measured institutional finding.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines, and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a convention that decides an outcome rather than a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three forbidden moves are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already refuse them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A contact test showing a seat that changes outcomes without changing contact; an anchor that updates with the frames and still settles; a convention that decides an outcome and remains a convention; a synthetic pilot that proves capability; or a re-census that writes the two habitat modes with the same bond would each break a stated claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
