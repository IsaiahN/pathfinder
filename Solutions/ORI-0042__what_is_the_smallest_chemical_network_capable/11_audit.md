## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`: the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x).** The verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union compound), Fig 13 :14, :22, :74-76 (gradient, flow, cross-kind rule), Fig 12 :119, :121 (catalyst, cycle), Fig 11 :39, :70 (habitat), Fig 8 :32, :39 (idempotence and no proof of absence) and Fig 12 :125-126 (two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A).** The shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: split the two conditions; harvest by coupling a spendable gradient through a structure; maintain with a catalyst and a closing cycle; locate capability in network plus habitat; price bits and actions separately; record the number as `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E).** A concrete lab claim a reader might try to build from that shorthand: a two or three reaction pyruvate/glyoxylate sequence is the smallest self-maintaining network.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down.

- **The cycle is lost.** Stubbs et al. report an analog sequence whose own introduction says ancestral pathways *"likely did not complete a metabolic cycle."* A chain is Fig 12 :48 *"the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching"* and cannot close by construction (Fig 12 :121). The concrete claim drops the load-bearing structure.
- **The habitat is lost.** A reactor supplies food, temperature and buffering, so Fig 11 :70 applies: a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability. The shorthand's word capability does not travel into the flask.
- **The currency is lost.** Reaction count is bits. The shorthand also demands an experimental cost in actions. A concrete network that is smallest by molecule count can be the most expensive to settle. Fig 12 :125-126 forbids adding the two.
- **The verdict is lost.** Fig 8 :39 forbids reading a failed search as absence, and Fig 13 :134 forbids silence. A concrete claim of uniqueness turns a `gap` into a name.

One thing survives cleanly across the trip: a directed gradient coupled through a structure to a catalytic, cyclic network, with persistence assessed against a habitat. That residue is the part worth sending to an expert. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## 5. Strains and re-census

- **Smallest RAF is NP-hard.** Huson, Xavier and Steel (2024) show that determining the size of a smallest irreducible RAF is NP-hard. Re-census: this is a computational cost inside a given catalytic reaction system. It does not bear on whether a unique smallest network exists in nature, and it is not a framework closure limit. Named as a strain in F8 and `07`, not hidden and not used as a verdict.
- **Bits versus actions.** Fig 12 :125-126 keeps two currencies apart. A literature that equates smallest with fewest reactions is using one. A ground that asks how many experiments settle persistence is using the other. Neither predicts the other, so both are read or the choice is made blind. Named, not summed.
- **No bottom.** Fig 12 :104 and Fig 4 :33 were tried as a direct answer to network size and refused by re-census. They govern levels of composition, where irreducibility is level-relative; cardinality inside a level is a different quantity. Recorded as a rejected strain in F9.
- **The number is a preference.** Fig 5 :62 and the Direction paragraph of `THE_FORMULA.md` say a frame searching for a shape holds it as a preference, not a term. The primary binds it as an ordinary term in section 0 with four refuters. I find that discipline correctly applied and refusable.
- **Unanimity of the essays.** All eight essays share the framework's author. Their agreement with the figures is one evidence pool talking. Declared, not counted as corroboration.
- **After re-census: three strains, all named, none hidden.** No figure contradiction was found. Where two figures seemed to strain, the actors or the quantity were different, so the README's rule (a reading that makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain) was applied rather than a figure being overruled.
