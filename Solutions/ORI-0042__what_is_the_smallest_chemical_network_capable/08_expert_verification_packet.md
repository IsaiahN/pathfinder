# Expert verification packet : the Tether on the smallest self-maintaining network

**What this is:** a claim in a form a chemist or origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the number, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle a minimum.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question bundles two conditions with different grounds and must be split before search. Harvesting energy means coupling to a spendable gradient, because energy is conserved, finite and directionless on its own; a structure must mediate between the energy state and the reacting metabolites. Maintaining oneself requires a catalyst that survives its own use and a cycle in which output re-enters as input, so an acyclic chain cannot qualify however short it is. Capability is a property of the network plus its habitat, so a lab reactor proves wiring rather than capability. The smallest number is not settled at this reach: reaction count and experimental cost are two currencies that do not add. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : catalyst and cycle; checkable by reading Figure 12 and by chemistry
- **Statement:** a self-maintaining network needs a part that survives its own use and a cycle. A linear chain cannot close, however short it is.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 : *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121 : *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Also Fig 12 :48 : *"A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching."*
- **What to check:** is there any demonstrated case of a purely acyclic reaction set persisting across many turnovers under a fixed food set? Does the catalyst in your candidate network regenerate, or is it consumed?
- **Refuter:** exhibit such an acyclic self-maintaining set, or show a candidate network whose catalyst is consumed in every turnover and which nevertheless persists indefinitely. Either breaks the claim.

### C2 : energy is a state; harvesting means spending a gradient through a structure; checkable in Figure 13
- **Statement:** energy alone is directionless; a gradient is the spendable structure, and energy plus a gradient composes a flow. Two states never bond directly, so a structure must mediate between the energy source and the reacting metabolites.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14 : *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*; Fig 13 :22 : *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 13 :75-76 : *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*; Fig 13 :74 : *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
- **What to check:** does your candidate network couple to an external disequilibrium (a gradient), or does the claim treat energy as a substance the network produces? Does the mediating structure remain unnamed in the framework, as claimed?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a self-maintaining network with no external gradient or disequilibrium, in which a directionless conserved quantity alone drives persistence. That breaks the claim.

### C3 : capability is network plus habitat; checkable in Figure 11 and against the lab record
- **Statement:** a laboratory reactor is a substituted habitat. Demonstrating that a small reaction set runs in one proves the wiring, not the capability of the network in an unmaintained habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 : *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 : *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :70 : *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **What to check:** in the alpha-ketoacid pathway (Stubbs et al., *Nature Chemistry*, 2020) or the mineral H2/CO2 system (Preiner et al., *Nature Ecology & Evolution*, 2020), is a cycle closed and a catalyst regenerated, or are products accumulating in a one-pass reaction? Is the habitat supplied by the experiment?
- **Refuter:** show the same reaction set persisting, with catalysts regenerated, in an unmaintained natural habitat with no experimental subsidy. That would move the result from wiring to capability.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named smallest network, a reaction count, or a unique minimum.
- Not that the acetyl-CoA pathway, the reverse TCA cycle, or the alpha-ketoacid analogue is self-maintaining. Those are bindings from the chemistry frame onto held structure.
- Not that "no bottom" (Fig 12 :104) proves anything about network size. It governs levels of composition.
- Not a single ordering of "smallest." Bits (description length) and actions (experimental cost) do not add (Fig 12 :125-126).
- Not `grounded`. The number is recorded as `gap` at this reach.
- Not a second witness. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :119 and :121, and Fig 13 :14, :22, :74-76. Five short passages.
2. For one network you know well, say whether its catalyst regenerates and whether its graph contains a cycle.
3. Say whether its energy source is an external gradient or is being treated as a produced substance.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish catalyst regeneration from one-pass product accumulation. If none exists, say so.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
