## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the number does not. Split the two conditions. Harvesting energy is coupling to a spendable gradient through a structure, because energy alone is directionless and two states never bond directly. Maintaining oneself requires a catalyst that survives its use and a cycle that closes, so an acyclic chain cannot qualify. Capability is network plus habitat, so lab reaction sequences prove wiring, not capability. The specific smallest network is recorded as `gap` at this reach: closure is idempotent, no search adds an atom, and searching without finding is not a proof of absence. Bits and actions are separate currencies, so "smallest" without a declared currency is not a well-formed objective. No named pathway is asserted. This is `specified` on the shape and `gap` on the number, with no part `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states shape, mode, provenance and four refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named pathway instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A named smallest network can be withdrawn without touching the shape derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold it. A later ground reading can fill the slot without withdrawing the cycle, catalyst, gradient or habitat claims.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A sustained experiment that distinguishes catalyst regeneration from one-pass accumulation, a self-maintaining acyclic chain, a network persisting with no external disequilibrium, a unique minimum whose ordering is the same in bits and in actions, or a derivation of one of the six from the other five would each reverse a stated part of the verdict. A figure re-census that admits a direct energy-to-metabolite bond would reverse the cross-kind claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
