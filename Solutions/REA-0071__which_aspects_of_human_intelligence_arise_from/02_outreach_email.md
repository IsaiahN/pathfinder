**Subject:** Check one output on nature versus nurture before you decide about the theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "which aspects of human intelligence come from biology, and which from culture, education and social learning?" asks for a two-column attribution. The derivation says that split is malformed. Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone. Biological constraints set what a substrate can represent and compose at all (the reach). Culture and education are head starts and imports that move a frame's reach without adding any new primitive to the world. An "aspect" then sits in the overlap of the two conditions, not in one column. The per-aspect split stays open until an instrument settles it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework's six terms are a list of biological constraints. They are a declared guess at compression axes, claimed universally. Calling them biological is my binding, not a derivation. I am also not claiming that a heritability percentage assigns a cause to an individual aspect, or that a cross-cultural universal settles the question. Those are population statistics and transmission facts, not verdicts about a slot.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a ground-visible instrument that assigns an aspect to biology alone or culture alone, and the assignment survives removing the other term;
> 2. a capability improvement that changes no contact and still increases capability;
> 3. a case of cultural transmission that adds a new atom to the world rather than a head start to a frame;
> 4. a clean partition of the two conditions with an empty intersection, decided by an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - How Intelligence Actually Works (worked narrative and the six-term derivation): https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture (all 13 figures, formula, tables): https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
