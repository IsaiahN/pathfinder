## VERDICT (from the figures)

The partition is refused; the shape survives. Capability is agent-and-habitat, the two named sources form a union compound with a dependency order, biological constraints set the reach, and culture and education are head starts and imports that add no atom to the world. Germline and cultural transmission are a functional group of head-start carriers, not isomers. A heritability percentage is a budget filter, not a verdict about an aspect. The six-term set is a declared, universal guess; calling it the biological list is a binding, not a derivation. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The refusal of the partition is `general` and structural. The per-aspect attribution is `unreachable` with the held instruments, not `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by asking them to endorse a two-column list instead of testing contact. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The two-column attribution is withdrawn without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already replace it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the functional-group and contact claims remain intact.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument returning different readings for germline-constrained and socially transmitted contributions to one aspect would reopen the split; a capability that survives removal of one term would break the contact claim; an anchor that assigns an aspect to exactly one condition and does not move when a frame pushes on it would reinstate the partition. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
