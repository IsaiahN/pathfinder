## 0. The residual in effect terms

R is the failure of a requested partition. The question asks for a rule that assigns every aspect of human intelligence to biology (A) or to culture, education and social learning (B), as if a ground could decide each assignment.

- **Arity:** two candidate sources are named. But Fig 11 :44 requires the habitat to be enumerated, not assumed from a two-slot prior: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."** Two named sources are not a census.
- **Symmetry:** the question presents the two sources as symmetric (which from A, which from B). They are not the same kind of thing. Biology is a substrate that closes a reach; culture is a membrane that hands a head start to a new frame. Treating them as two mixable ingredients is the flat-formula error of Fig 12.
- **Scale:** selection works over generations, education over a lifetime, culture over intergenerational handoffs. Fig 4 :21 names the membrane as "a filter with a direction" between sizes. A single two-column answer cannot be a single-scale claim.

So R is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a partition. Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**.

The sought-for shape was bound before the run (Formula "Direction"): "a two-column, ground-settleable attribution of each named aspect to A or B." That bound term accrues a residual and is refused below.

## 3. Findings F1..F7

### F1: The either/or framing is refused (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**

Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

The question asks which column an aspect belongs in. Under Fig 11, an "aspect of human intelligence" is not an object that can be assigned to a single column: it is a capability at the contact of an agent and a habitat. Removing either term does not leave a smaller copy of the capability; it leaves no capability. The partition is therefore not a hard version of the question, it is a malformed one.

- Mode: `general` (structural refusal), triangulated across Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 9 :54-57 and the essay *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (read 2026-11-23), which states the same Fig 11 line in the context of a newborn agent made capable by its surroundings. Low overlap between a habitat figure, a split figure and a multi-agent essay.
- Refuter: show a capability that survives removal of one term (an isolated agent retaining the capability, or a habitat-only capability with no agent), or show the two conditions are a partition rather than a union.

### F2: It is a union compound (triangulated)

Fig 9 :54: **"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

The two named sources overlap continuously in development: genes shape the environments children seek and receive (gene-environment correlation), and cultural practices alter selection pressures (gene-culture coevolution). World corroboration (secondary only): Wikipedia *Nature versus nurture* (read 2026-11-23) reports that both factors contribute "often in an inextricable manner" and records Hebb's response to the which-contributes-more question: "Which contributes more to the area of a rectangle, its length or its width?".

- Mode: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9 split rule plus a low-overlap world field: developmental psychology).
- Refuter: exhibit a clean discriminant that assigns an aspect to exactly one condition and leaves the intersection empty.

### F3: Biological constraints govern the reach, not a list of aspects (single-source at figure level, triangulated with D1 lines)

Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"** refers to import; by contrast composition cannot leave the closure. Fig 13 :116: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."**

Biology fixes the closure: what the substrate can represent and compose at all. Fig 3 :19 (above) makes earlier links prerequisites. Fig 11 :36 makes selection, not a label, the source of the lineage's fit. But the framework does **not** license naming the six terms as "the biological constraints." The essay *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (read 2026-11-23) calls the six *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six"* and adds *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Applying them to biology is a binding from the biology frame onto atoms already held, provenanced as such, not a derivation of a biological list.

- Mode: `specified`, single-source for the figure rule (Fig 6/13/3/11 are one framework evidence pool), with the scope caveat recorded below. A world source from behavioural genetics (Wikipedia *Heritability of IQ*, read 2026-11-23) corroborates that heritability is about variation between people, not a per-aspect cause: "Heritability measures the proportion of variation in a trait that can be attributed to genes, and not the proportion of a trait caused by genes."
- Refuter: derive one of the six from the other five, or show a heritability estimate that licenses a per-individual per-aspect causal attribution.

### F4: Culture and education are head starts and imports (triangulated)

Fig 4 :11-12: **"a head start, never a recording"**

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

Fig 4 :24: **"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."**

Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."**

So education does not inject a new substance that mixes with biology. It gives a new frame access to a closure that already differs (Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**). The essay *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read 2026-11-23) states the cross-generational form directly: *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself."* World corroboration from a different field: Wikipedia *Dual inheritance theory* (read 2026-11-23) reports that genes and culture "continually interact in a feedback loop" and that "Genes constrain the brain's structure and, hence, the ability of the brain to acquire and store culture."

- Mode: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 4, Fig 6, a cognition essay and an evolutionary-anthropology world source.
- Refuter: demonstrate cultural transmission adding a new atom to the world, or a head start that needs no contact and no further testing by the receiver.

### F5: Handing a tradition along cannot settle the attribution (single-source)

Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

A cross-cultural universal, or a long tradition of schooling, is evidence of transmission, not evidence of which column an aspect belongs in. World corroboration from animal behaviour (Wikipedia *Animal culture*, read 2026-11-23): Whiten et al. found 39 of 65 behaviour categories habitual in some chimpanzee communities and absent in others, with the behaviour "passed from chimpanzee to chimpanzee within the group through a complex mix of imitation and social learning."

- Mode: `specified`, single-source for the figure rule.
- Refuter: show an agreement between two frames that share no evidence pool and that directly settles one aspect's attribution.

### F6: A link with no instrument is not a working link (triangulated)

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

A percentage (for example "50% genetic") is a filter for the research budget, not a witness for a verdict about an individual or an aspect. Wikipedia *Heritability of IQ* (read 2026-11-23) contrasts the "two buckets" picture with a "homogenous mudpie" picture and notes that vocabulary size is substantially heritable although "every word in an individual's vocabulary is learned."

- Mode: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 3, Fig 9, behavioural-genetics world source).
- Refuter: name an instrument that returns a per-aspect attribution and show it does not change its answer when the frame's weighting changes.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the framework's own discipline, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A ground-visible instrument assigns a given aspect to biology alone or culture alone, and the assignment survives removing the other term (breaks Fig 11 :39-40).
2. A capability improvement is demonstrated that changes no contact and still increases capability (breaks Fig 11 :40).
3. Cultural transmission is shown to add a new atom to the world rather than a head start to a frame (breaks Fig 6 :25 and Fig 4 :11-12).
4. The two conditions turn out to be a partition with an empty intersection, settled by an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it (breaks Fig 9 :54-57 and Fig 2 :29).

Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Stop asking for the two-column list. Ask instead: what is in contact with this aspect, and what would change if one term were removed? (Fig 11 :43-44).
2. State the mode on every claim (Formula mode rule).
3. Price at three scales: selection history, individual development, intergenerational handoff (Fig 4 membrane). Do not average them.
4. Put the derivation before any mapping to particular aspects, with provenance, so import and convergent derivation are distinguishable (Fig 8 principle, corroborated by the canonical README).
5. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator (the refuter and the shape), not a conclusion list.

### Step-7 triage (kept distinct)

- **SEARCHED, asked first:** is the two-column attribution already in here and unfound? No. The instruments held (twin and adoption designs, cross-cultural comparison, heritability estimates) return population variances and transmission patterns, not per-aspect source assignments. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space visited.
- **INWARD:** extend the instrument from one already returning something. Candidate: a design that removes one term while holding the other fixed and still reads the aspect. This is sharpening in our own terms, and it is where the refuter lives.
- **OUTWARD:** import a frame whose closure predicts the shape. Candidate: dual inheritance theory or the extended-mind thesis (world sources, read 2026-11-23). Source gate and shadow test: do they explain the union shape without adding an atom that was not needed? Declared honestly as corroboration, never as the verdict.

INWARD and OUTWARD are not read against each other. Importing against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed.
