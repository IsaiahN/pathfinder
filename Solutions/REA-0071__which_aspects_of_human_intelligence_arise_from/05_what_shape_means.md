## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 (essay reading): *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, the sought-for shape was written down before the run: "a two-column, ground-settleable attribution of each named aspect to biology or to culture." That shape is what the derivation tests, and it is refused. What survives in its place is a different shape: a union compound whose capability sits at the contact of agent and habitat. "Two columns" is a filler shape; "union at contact" is a constraint shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here, the effect terms are "reach", "head start", "contact" and "union". They were substituted for the borrowed cause terms "gene" and "culture" precisely so that a reader in any of the five low-overlap fields can check them against the world.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning them into "the biological constraints on intelligence" converts a visible choice into an invisible one. That is why `01` records it as a binding, not a derivation.

## 4. Why the two-column request is the named error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The question reads a projection (an observed competent behaviour) and asks for its source (biology or culture). Several sources produce the same reading. Twin studies, adoption studies and cross-cultural comparison are the current instruments; they return variances and transmission patterns, not source names for an aspect. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a sharper argument. The world source on heritability makes the same point in a different vocabulary: the "two buckets" picture is a misreading of a population statistic.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q answered: what licenses calling the six "biological constraints"?

**Nothing in the framework.** The six are declared, universal, and a guess. Naming them as biology's contribution is a binding from the biology frame onto held atoms, provenanced as such. It is not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** Not as written, because the record never claims the six are biological. The error would be introduced by answering the question with "these three aspects are the six terms, therefore biological." That answer is recorded as refused in `01` F3 and F7.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4 (see `06` Q1)

I initially wrote that the metamerism argument forbids *any* naming of the sources of an aspect. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The sources here (genes, neural substrate, teachers, language, institutions) are already in the world; the question is which of them the reading discriminates. The correct claim is narrower: naming a source from a projection that does not discriminate among sources is the metamerism error, and the remedy is a better instrument. Both the original claim and the narrowed form are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: I initially read Fig 6 (*"the only operator that moves the wall"*) as conflicting with Fig 11 :40 (*"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing"*). Re-census dissolves it: an import is contact with a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), so education changes contact. No strain remains.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names agreeing that nature matters is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
