## What the wider literature contributes (corroboration only)

1. **The dichotomy is a compound, not a partition.** *Nature versus nurture* gives Hebb's rectangle analogy, the exact shape the figures refuse to split. Fig 9 :54-57 governs; the article corroborates.
2. **Heritability is a population statistic, not a per-aspect cause.** *Heritability of IQ* supplies the two-buckets versus mudpie contrast, which is the flat-formula error of Fig 12 in a different vocabulary, and the vocabulary example, which is Fig 11 :39-40 in a different field.
3. **Culture is a head start, not a new primitive.** *Animal culture* and *Dual inheritance theory* supply the transmission and feedback-loop evidence that Fig 4 :11-12 and Fig 6 :25 describe structurally.
4. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** *Extended mind thesis* supplies a philosophical corroboration from a field with no stake in the figures. Fig 11 :39 governs; the thesis corroborates.

All five world sources are Wikipedia overview articles, so they share a venue. Their substantive citations are to different primary literatures (behavioural genetics, comparative primatology, gene-culture coevolution, philosophy of mind), which is why they are treated as different fields, but they are not independent experiments run by this agent. Overlap is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
