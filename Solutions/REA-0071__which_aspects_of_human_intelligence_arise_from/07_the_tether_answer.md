# The Tether's answer: biology, culture and the aspects of human intelligence

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive shape, `general` on the structural refusal of the partition, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question requests a two-column attribution: which aspects come from biology, which from culture, education and social learning. No anchor is named that could decide a column. Under Fig 9 this is a union compound with its own loop, not one hard problem and not a partition. The answer below returns the shape any responsible answer must have and leaves the per-aspect split open.

## 2. The shape

1. **The either/or is refused.** Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**. An aspect of intelligence is not an object that can be assigned to one column. Remove either the agent or the habitat and the capability does not shrink, it ceases.
2. **The two conditions overlap.** Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Development is the overlap: genes shape the environments sought and received, and cultural practices alter selection pressures. The world source on gene-culture coevolution corroborates the feedback loop, but the figure governs.
3. **Biological constraints set the reach.** Fig 13 :116: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."** Fig 6 gives import as **"the only operator that moves the wall"**, so composition inside a substrate cannot leave what that substrate can represent. Biological constraints bound what can be composed at all. The framework does **not** identify the six terms as a biological list; that naming is a binding, provenanced as such.
4. **Culture and education are head starts and imports.** Fig 4 :11-12: **"a head start, never a recording"**. Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** Education gives a new frame access to a closure that already differs; it does not add a new primitive to the world. A method survives its use (Fig 12 catalyst), so each generation need not re-pay what an earlier one settled.
5. **The order is dependency, not rank.** Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** Biological prerequisites are links 1-4; "learn and carry" is link 5. More education cannot rescue a broken earlier link (Fig 3 :19). Refusal of the partition comes before any aspect assignment (Formula step 2).
6. **A percentage is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A heritability estimate is a population variance. It cannot say that 50 percent of one person's navigation is genetic and 50 percent learned.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a two-column list of aspects. That shape was bound before the run and refused.
- Not "the six terms are the biological constraints." They are a declared, universal guess at compression axes, and calling them biological is the searcher's binding.
- Not that culture adds new intelligence. It gives access; it adds no atom to the world.
- Not that genes are destiny or that the blank slate is true. Both are the collapsed positions the union compound excludes.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument returns a per-aspect source attribution.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Removal test:** hold one term fixed and remove the other. If the aspect survives intact in a single term, the capability-at-contact claim fails.
- **Contact test:** does a capability improvement that changes no contact still increase capability? Under Fig 11 :40 the answer must be no. A yes refutes the shape.
- **Discriminating instrument:** a design that returns different readings for germline-constrained and socially transmitted contributions to the same aspect, where current designs return the same reading for both. This is the INWARD question (extend an instrument already returning something).
- **Partition test:** an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it and that assigns an aspect to exactly one condition (Fig 2 :29). None is currently named.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unanswerable in principle.** The instruments held return population variances and transmission patterns, not per-aspect source names. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A search that returned no two-column answer is not proof that none could exist; it is a report about the current union horizon. The answer stops where the instruments stop discriminating and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The essays warn that *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not split human intelligence into a biology column and a culture column. It says that capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone, so an "aspect" sits in the overlap and does not survive removal of one term. Biological constraints set what a substrate can represent and compose at all; culture and education are head starts and imports that move a frame's reach without adding any new primitive to the world. Their order is dependency: perceptual and conceptual prerequisites first, "learn and carry" second, capability at contact. A heritability percentage is a filter for the research budget, not a witness for a verdict about an individual. What the framework returns is a shape, four written refuters and the instruments that would close it. The per-aspect split stays open until a ground settles it.
