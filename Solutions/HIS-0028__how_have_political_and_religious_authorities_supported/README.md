## The question restated as an effect

The question is not asking for a chronicle. It is asking for the shape of a mechanism that produces two opposite effects from the same class of actor. In effect terms, per Formula step 1 and the Symbols table:

- **b** is an inquiring community's belief about what the world will return, formed from its own history and inherited priors.
- **a** is the act of inquiry: build an instrument, run an experiment, publish a result, train a novice, compute a calendar.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the community's library predicts will come back.
- **o′** is what the ground actually returns: a measurement, a harvest, an eclipse, a patient outcome.
- **s** is the inquiry slot. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed per slot, never averaged into a verdict about "science" as a block (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

Authorities are not outside this loop. They are actors in the habitat in contact with R (Fig 11 :45-48). The residual to explain is: *why does the same actor class appear as benefactor to one inquiry and malfactor to another, and why do support and limit often arrive together from the same institution in the same period?*

- Option A, "an authority is good or bad by nature": leads to the prediction that valence is a property carried by the actor.
- Option B, "an authority's valence is relative to the residual and to the operation it performs": leads to the prediction that support and limit are two relations, not two kinds, and that which one appears depends on whether the actor changes contact with a ground or substitutes itself for the ground.

The shape bound before the run (Formula, "Direction"): the answer would name the two operations and an instrument that discriminates a contact-changing intervention from a ground-substituting one. Until bound, that shape is a preference and no check in the loop can see it. It is now bound as an ordinary term, so the refuter in `01` section 5 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B, in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the historical question, and no claim that the framework is the only account of science and authority.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
