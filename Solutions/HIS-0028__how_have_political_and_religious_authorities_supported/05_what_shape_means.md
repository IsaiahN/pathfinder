## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill the slots. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *an authority changes contact with a ground, or substitutes itself for the ground, and the two are different operations whose signs can flip with the residual.*
- A name: *the Medici, the Jesuits, the CPSU, the Vatican Observatory.* Those are archive bindings, not shapes.

The distinction matters because the question asks "how", not "which". A name would answer a different question and would invite the reader to rank institutions by moral sign, which Fig 11 :51 forbids: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "changed contact" and "substituted ground", not "enlightenment" and "obscurantism". The question-as-tether is the residual described in section 0 of `01`; the loop is steered by asking, of each candidate case, whether a new reading became possible after the intervention.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, the institution names in the world sources are imports from the archive frame. They are allowed as illustrations only when their provenance is recorded. They are not licensed as the answer, because no instrument here has discriminated one institution's dominant operation from another's across cases.

## 3b. Correction to the usual reading of "shape, not a name"

The README's phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. An authority is not a missing primitive under this reading. It is an actor in the habitat, which the habitat section of `THE_FORMULA.md` says to *"enumerate, never compose"*: *"Everything in contact with R, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering."* So naming an authority as an actor is a census step, not the forbidden naming of a slot. What remains forbidden is turning that actor into a standing moral kind (Fig 11 :51) or claiming the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21). Both this correction and the original "shape, not a name" rule are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 11 :45-51).

## 4. The source/provenance trap in this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Apply that to the archive. A chronicle that says "the Church supported astronomy and also condemned it" is a reading of a projection. It does not disclose the source: was the support an introduction that changed contact, or a prestige transfer that changed no reading, or an installation of the institution as the thing that settled the question? Several paths give the same chronicle. What is owed is a discriminator, not a verdict. That is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks for three checkable sub-claims rather than a narrative.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the same limit in another register: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."*

## 5. Two consequences for the outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question, the temptation is to gather famous historians who agree that "it was complicated". That is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried against a case the reader knows, and let the archive and the ground settle it.

**A society of readers is a habitat, not a verdict.** From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*, and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* A panel of agreeable specialists raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Seat Map :283).

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture page adds that without a verifiable white-box run the outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and that results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The honest stop for this question

The shape stops at two operations and four refuters. It does not stop at a list of good and bad centuries, and it does not stop at a claim about which institution was decisive. *Arrival of the Fittest* says the framework is *"recursive, domain-agnostic, barrier theorem"* and that *"because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* The lineage is the deliverable: a residual, a census, verbatim figure lines, refuters written in advance, and a record of what could not be reached. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
