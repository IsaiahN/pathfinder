# The Tether's answer: authorities supporting and limiting scientific inquiry

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical verdict. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No archive was adjudicated in this run.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question's "while also limiting it" is a valence inversion, not a puzzle about institutional character. The same authority, in the same century, can increase what an inquiring community can read and then remove the instrument that would correct it. Stated as R: an authority's action sometimes reduces the gap between what a community predicts and what its ground returns, and sometimes replaces that ground with the authority's own decree, so the gap can no longer be measured. This is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*), not one hard question about whether authority is good or bad. It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Valence is a relation, not a property.** Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* No standing list of good and bad authorities survives a change of the question. The answer cannot be a ranking of institutions.
2. **Support is introduction that changes contact.** Fig 11 :37 names the operation: *"enumerate the habitat, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, read the ground."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* An authority supports inquiry when it adds an instrument, a method imported from a closure that differs (Fig 6 :23), or a head start (Fig 4 :22) that makes the next reading cheaper. Money without changed contact is a prestige transfer, not support.
3. **Limit is the substitution of the authority for the ground.** Fig 2 :33-34: *"The anchor must not update."* If it does, *"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* A decree that settles a dispute has taken the anchor chair. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
4. **The two operations are isomers under a fixed atom core.** `06_isomers_and_order.md` derives them as different bond arrangements of authority, inquirers and ground: production plus sequence with an importable mediator, versus a declared identity that collapses the mediating slot. Same atoms, different bond, different behaviour (Fig 12 :117). The atom-counting fork has a stated falsifier.
5. **A support can turn into a limit when the residual changes.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* A body that once imported methods can become a room handing itself the same closure (Fig 7 :36). Nothing in its charter records which it has become.
6. **The framework does not name the guilty institution.** It returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). Institutional names in the archive are bindings, provenanced as such, not derivations.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named authority was wholly good or wholly bad. That is the excluded domain X1 in `01` §1.2.
- Not that "science and religion" are two fixed blocks at war. That is X2, a union reported as a partition, re-censused in `01` §6 R1.
- Not that a single funder causes bad science. The relevant quantity is independence of evidence (Fig 2 :31), not the count of patrons.
- Not that the conflict thesis is false, and not that it is true. The figures govern the mechanism; the archive would have to settle the historical distribution.
- Not grounded. No new archival measurement was taken and no existing one was adjudicated here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not an argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Contact test:** after an authority's intervention, did a new reading become available that was not available before, with a new instrument or an import from a differing closure? If no, the "support" was a prestige transfer.
- **Anchor test:** after a decree, was the ground still able to reverse the decree through the institution's ordinary procedure? If no, the authority took the anchor chair.
- **Catalyst test:** did the institution keep generating new readings after its original question changed, or did it start reproducing its own past success? (Fig 12 :119)
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** invariant valence across a documented change of residual; support with no contact change; a decree that is routinely reversed by the ground; inseparable operations; persistence of a spent method across two generations.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The world sources were opened and quoted, but they share pools with each other and with the archive. The Lysenko sources are one pool; the Galileo and Catholic Church sources are another. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The six-term physical substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring the habitat makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the instruments can no longer discriminate an introduction from a substitution and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that political or religious authorities are either the friends or the enemies of science. It says their sign depends on the operation, not on their identity. An authority supports inquiry when it changes an inquiring community's contact with what can actually settle its questions: it introduces an instrument, imports a method from somewhere else, or funds a head start. The same authority limits inquiry when its decree takes the place of that ground, so the measurement can no longer correct the frame. The two operations are different arrangements of the same three things, authority, inquirers and ground, and a support can quietly turn into a limit when the question changes. What the framework offers is the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which institution, in which century, performed which operation is left to the archive, and is a `gap` here, not a verdict.
