## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :40, :49-51, Fig 2 :33-34 and Fig 10 :47, :93.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in the README and `07`: an authority supports by changing contact and limits by taking the anchor chair; valence is relative to the residual.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete historical claim a reader might form: "the Church funded astronomy but persecuted Galileo, so religious authority was both a help and a hindrance."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **operation discriminator**. The shorthand distinguishes introduction from substitution, but the concrete sentence collapses both into "help and hindrance", which is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26). Without a new reading, no import and a reversed decree, the sentence cannot say which operation occurred.
- the **isomer fork**. The shorthand says the two operations are the same atoms differently bonded; the concrete sentence needs a fourth atom (the mediator or the decree) before it can describe a case. Whether the fork resolves as isomer or functional group is not carried down.
- the **reputation condition**. Fig 10 :93 only fires when the certifier has a standing to protect. The concrete sentence does not record the stake, so the condition silently disappears.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a single actor can have opposite signs on different slots, and support must change contact if it is to count as support. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
