# Expert verification packet : the Tether on persistent controversy

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian of science, STS scholar or misinformation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`.** No measurement of a named controversy is claimed. This is a separator and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A controversy that survives the settling of its factual slots was never one question. The factual slot settles, but the disagreement is a union of conditions with different anchors: what each frame can compose from what it holds, whether the frames draw on one evidence pool, whether standing earned on the old dispute survives the settlement, whether the channel still carries a recording of the old disagreement rather than the current settlement, and whether the live residual is that a held term is false. Where the residual is REFUTED, what is owed is a refusal, not another explanation, and no amount of same-kind fact reaches it. Over a fixed set of settled atoms, two controversies with different bonds are isomers. The framework returns this shape and five refuters written in advance. It returns no dominant cause, no magnitude and no name for the live slot in any particular case.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 9

- **Statement:** the residual is indexed per slot; a global near-zero with one live slot is legal; and a REFUTED residual is not reachable by the other four route kinds.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*; Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 5 :53 *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*; Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a controversy whose per-slot residuals all fall to zero when the fact is settled; or close a false held term with more same-kind facts and no refusal term. Either breaks the claim.

### C2 : world-facing, checkable by a historian or STS scholar against the literature

- **Statement:** continued disagreement after settlement is overdetermined by several mechanisms with different anchors, so no single one can be selected from the outcome alone.
- **What to check:** the opened sources report (a) ambiguous evidence can widen rather than narrow a disagreement when each side reads it through its held theory, Lord, Ross and Lepper 1979 as reported on Wikipedia; (b) the experimenter's regress, Collins 1981 and 1985, where the right result is needed to know the experiment worked; (c) manufactured doubt used to *"keep the controversy alive"* long after consensus, Oreskes and Conway 2010 as reported on Wikipedia; (d) false balance *"creating doubt about the scientific state of research"*, as reported on Wikipedia. Are these four different mechanisms or four descriptions of one?
- **Refuter:** show that one of these mechanisms subsumes the other three at the ground, with a discriminator that range-tests them separately. Then the union collapses to one condition in that domain.
- **Overlap declaration:** (c) is drawn from one history-of-science evidence pool with agnotology and is counted as one witness; (a), (b) and (d) are genuinely lower overlap. None overrules a figure line.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation about order and bond

- **Statement:** description precedes frame choice, refusal precedes mint, settling precedes composition; and same settled atoms under a different bond are a different controversy.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :27 to :28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where composition over an unsettled term succeeds and the ground pays; or two controversies with identical atoms and identical bonds but different persistence. Either breaks the order or the isomer claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot discriminator, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried, maximal overlap with the figures): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Here that means:

1. Enumerate the live slots for a named controversy and measure which carry positive residual after the fact settles.
2. Range-test independence: do the two sides draw on separate evidence and a non-updating standard of settlement?
3. Test the channel: is the current settlement reaching the same audience as the old dispute (Fig 10 :24)?
4. Test the refusal: name the held term whose residual persists and does not trend down, and ask whether a refusal has standing (Fig 5 :58 to :60).
