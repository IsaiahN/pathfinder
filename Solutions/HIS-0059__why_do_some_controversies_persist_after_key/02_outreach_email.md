**Subject:** Check one separation before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about persistent controversies. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the structure, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`):** when the key factual questions in a controversy are settled, the disagreement can continue because it was never one question. The factual slot is one slot among several. The other live slots have different anchors: what each side can compose from what it holds; whether the two sides draw on one shared evidence pool; whether standing earned on the old dispute survives the settlement; whether the channel still carries the current settlement or a recording of the old dispute; and whether the live residual is that some held term is false, in which case what is owed is a refusal rather than another explanation. I am not claiming a number, a dominant cause, or a name for the slot in any particular case.
>
> **What would refute it (written before this message):**
> 1. A controversy whose per-slot residuals all go to zero when the key fact is settled, with no live slot left.
> 2. Two frames in a persisting controversy shown to use genuinely independent evidence and a standard of settlement that does not move when either pushes on it, with the disagreement unchanged.
> 3. A false held term closed by more same-kind facts with no refusal term admitted.
> 4. A persisting controversy in which no actor's standing survives the settlement and no convention has acquired verdict power.
> 5. A sequence of low-overlap rooms under high ground pressure that leaves the residual flat.
>
> If any holds, the corresponding part of the claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the parties are irrational, that manufactured doubt explains the whole phenomenon, that the facts did not matter, or that this diagnoses any named historical case. Those are bindings onto particular domains, offered with provenance only after your verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula for direct download, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a controversy you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
