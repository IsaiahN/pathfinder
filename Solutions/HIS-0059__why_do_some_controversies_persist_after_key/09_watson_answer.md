## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, why a disagreement continues after the factual slots inside it have been settled at the ground. Option A: treat it as one hard question and look for the cause of continued irrationality or manufactured doubt. Option B: treat it as a union of conditions with different anchors, settle each slot separately, and report a shape plus refuters. What each leads to: A aggregates slots into a person or a single cause, scores the frame with a quantity the frame produces, and cannot say which slot is live; B keeps verdicts per domain, never averaged, returns no dominant cause and no number, and states what instrument would close the remaining gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Continued disagreement after the fact settles. Arity: at least two conditions, a settled one and a still-live one, so a union compound and not one hard question (Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."). Symmetry: the relation between settling and persisting is ordered, not symmetric; settling is necessary and not sufficient. Scale: individual belief, community or discipline, and public or institutional field. Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1, factual slot.** Actors: measurements, datasets, experiments. Currency: settlement, R toward zero on one slot. Anchor: the ground, a measurement that does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."). Settled by the question's premise.
- **D2, frame-closure slot.** Actors: the contending communities, their libraries and weightings. Currency: composition inside a closure, import across a differing one. Anchor: a closure that differs, reached with provenance and a shadow test. Underivable inside the frame is not the same as absent (Fig 6 :27 "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same.").
- **D3, evidence-pool slot.** Actors: the citing community, shared reviews and datasets. Currency: agreement. Anchor: independence, how little the two frames draw on the same evidence. One pool is one witness wearing many names.
- **D4, stake and standing slot.** Actors: institutions, funders, careers, identities. Currency: interest and standing. Anchor: a convention that does not decide an outcome, and a ground with no reputation to protect. Standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on (Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on.").
- **D5, channel and habitat slot.** Actors: journals, platforms, education, media norms. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground that does not decay (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"). Isolation substitutes a habitat rather than removing one.
- **D6, false-held-term slot.** Actors: a regularity held by a side that is false. Currency: negation. Anchor: the ground paying for a refusal. Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". REFUTED is not reachable by the other route kinds.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D2 to D6 overlap, so overlaps are compounds with their own loops, not harder cases of one condition. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame; write the refusal before minting more terms; settle before composing over a term. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between the non-updating anchor of Fig 2 and the residual-relative valence of Fig 11 is resolved by re-census. Fig 2 governs what may anchor a verdict; Fig 11 governs an actor's valence relative to a residual. Fig 10 :93 disqualifies a ground with a reputation to protect. Different objects, no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The premise is legal: a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state. A controversy that persists after its factual slots settle was never one question. It is a union of a settled condition and still-live conditions with different anchors. D2 through D6 each have their own verdict, kept separate and never averaged: closure difference, shared evidence pool, surviving standing, a decayed or recording channel, and a possible false held term that owes a refusal. Over a fixed set of settled atoms, two such controversies are isomers, differing in bond rather than ingredient. A chain of agreeing reviews is one long room, motion without progress. What is returned is a shape, five pre-registered refuters and the instruments that would tell which slot is live in a named case. No dominant cause, no magnitude and no name for the missing thing is returned. This is `general` on structure and `specified` per domain, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a pooled cause as if it were a verdict; Fig 2 :42 puts a price on that ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Each domain verdict is removable without touching the others, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold the bindings that would name a dominant slot. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected separator.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot discriminator showing all residuals fall when the fact settles would break the union; independent frames with a non-updating anchor still disagreeing at the same magnitude would break D2 and D3; a false held term closed without a refusal would break D6; a low-overlap, high-ground-pressure room chain that leaves the residual flat would break the mirror-chain claim. A figure re-census admitting a usable social refusal operator would clear the one live strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
