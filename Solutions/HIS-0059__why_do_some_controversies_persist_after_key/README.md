## The verdict in one line

A controversy that survives the settling of its factual slots was never one question. It is a union of conditions with different anchors. The factual slot settled; the frame-closure, evidence-pool, stake, channel and refusal slots did not. Where the residual is that a held term is false, more facts cannot reach it: what is owed is a refusal, not another explanation. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. There is no number, no named dominant cause and no ground reading here.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
