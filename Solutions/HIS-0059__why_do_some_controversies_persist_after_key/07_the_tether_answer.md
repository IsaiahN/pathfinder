# The Tether's answer : why controversies persist after the facts are settled

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a separator, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observation is that a disagreement continues after the factual slots inside it have been settled at the ground. Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. So the premise is not paradoxical. The question was asking for one cause of an aggregate, while the residual is indexed per slot. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

The answer is a **union separator**, not a list of causes. The controversy persists because settling one condition does not settle the others, and the other conditions have different anchors.

## 2. The per-domain verdicts (kept separate, never averaged)

1. **D1, factual slot : settled, by the question's own premise.** This link already works. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* More facts land above the break.
2. **D2, frame closure : `specified`, triangulated.** A fact settled at the ground can be unreachable from what the other frame holds. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Depth and genuine absence look the same from inside (Fig 6 :27).
3. **D3, evidence pool : `specified`, triangulated.** If the parties draw on one pool, agreement or disagreement is the pool talking. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Independence is the parameter (Fig 8 :47).
4. **D4, stake and standing : `specified`, triangulated.** Standing survives the outcome it was earned on (Fig 10 :61). A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47). A ground with a reputation to protect is disqualified (Fig 10 :93). World corroboration: agnotology and manufactured doubt, one evidence pool.
5. **D5, channel and habitat : `specified`, triangulated.** The ground does not decay, the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Stale percepts, saturated metrics and pooled readings sever contact while the claim stays the same (Fig 10 :25 to :28). Isolation substitutes a habitat (Fig 11 :66). World corroboration: false balance.
6. **D6, false held term : `specified`, single-source plus formula, with a live type strain.** Where the residual is that a regularity held is false, what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation (Fig 5 :53). More facts cannot reach it (Fig 8 :32). Whether a real community has a usable ¬ operator is not given by the figures (Operators table :77).
7. **The dominant slot in any named case : `gap`, reported as unreached.** No figure line selects which slot is live, and no figure returns a magnitude. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

Two cross-cutting shapes:

- **Same facts, different bond.** Over a fixed set of settled atoms, two controversies can be isomers. Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **A chain of agreeing rooms travels nowhere.** Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* This is why repeating the facts, another review, or another panel does not close a live non-factual slot.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the parties are irrational, and not that the facts did not matter. D1 is settled and load bearing.
- Not that manufactured doubt, identity, interest or polarisation is "the" cause. Each is a binding onto one domain with its own provenance.
- Not that a controversy is one question with a weighted mix of causes. It is a union; verdicts are not averaged.
- Not a diagnosis of any named historical case. No case measurement was taken.
- Not a number, a dominant slot, or a name for the missing thing.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would reverse it

**Settling needs a discriminator, not another argument.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The corresponding instruments here are:

1. A per-slot census for a named controversy: which slots carry positive residual after the fact is settled, and what anchor would settle each one.
2. An independence instrument: do the two sides' readings draw on separate evidence, or one pool?
3. A channel instrument: is the current settlement reaching the public at the same rate as the old dispute (Fig 10 :24 to :28)?
4. A refusal test: name the held term whose residual persists and does not trend down, and ask whether a refusal has standing to remove it (Fig 5 :58 to :60).

**Reversal conditions:** a ground reading that shows all per-slot residuals falling with the fact (breaks the union); independent frames with a non-updating anchor still disagreeing at the same magnitude (breaks D2/D3); a false term closed without a refusal (breaks D6); a low-overlap, high-ground-pressure room chain that does not move (breaks F7). Any of these sends the census back to the figures, never to a softer reading.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a list of reasons why people keep arguing after the facts are in. It says the question is bundled. The factual slot does settle, but a controversy is a union of other slots with different anchors: what each side can derive from what it holds, whether the two sides share one evidence pool, whether standing from the old fight survives the settlement, whether the channel still carries the dispute rather than the settlement, and whether the live residual is that something held is false. If it is false, what is owed is a refusal, not another explanation, and no amount of additional fact closes it. Over the same settled atoms, two such controversies are isomers: the bonds differ, not the facts. And a chain of agreeing reviews is one long room, motion without progress. What the framework returns is this shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would tell you which slot is live in a particular case. It does not name a dominant cause, and it is not a ground reading.
