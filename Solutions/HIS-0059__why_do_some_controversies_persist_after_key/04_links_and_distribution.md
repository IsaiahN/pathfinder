## 6. Distribution : offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** : *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the slot separator and its refuter first; the framework comes later and only if asked for.
- **Fig 2 :45** : *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the reader would count as a failure, not whether they agree.
- **Fig 4 :22** : *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A recording of one occasion is not a method. Do not send a success story as if it were evidence.
- **Fig 8 :47** : independence is *"one quantity read twice"*. A list of people chosen because they already agree is one evidence pool wearing many names.
- **Fig 9 :60** : *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Venue prestige and contact cost choose where to send, never whether the claim holds.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record that answer; (3) only then the figure text link, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method with each reader's own ground and refuter.
