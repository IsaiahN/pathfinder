## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks why a disagreement state continues after the factual slots inside it have returned a settled answer from the ground. In effect terms, the observation to be explained is: a global residual near zero on the factual slots, with at least one live slot residual still registering. Fig 1 :13 permits exactly that state: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. Fig 1 :19 adds *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 warns *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

So the naive form of the question, "how can a controversy survive when the facts are in?", already aggregates. It treats the controversy as one object whose residual should fall when the factual object is settled. The figures say R is a slice, never a total. Symbols table :38: *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it."* Symbols :39: *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

Residual R, characterised by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity.** At least two conditions. The factual slot and the persistence slot are not the same object. Fig 9 :4 to :5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The Library of Babel essay says the same in plain words: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*
- **Symmetry.** The relation between "fact settled" and "disagreement continues" is ordered, not symmetric. Fig 12 :71 gives the sequence test: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*. Settling is a step that can precede, but does not force, the next step. Fig 5 :69: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."* Fig 5 :72: *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*
- **Scale.** The same loop runs at three scales: the individual belief, the community or discipline, and the public or institutional field. Fig 4 :19: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size"* and :20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Verdicts at one scale are not averaged into verdicts at another.

The question is therefore a **union compound**, not one hard problem and not a two-list partition. One lever, settling the fact, clears only the region where the factual condition holds alone and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55).

## 3. Findings

### F1 : The factual slot and the controversy slot are different objects (decisive for the framing)

Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

A settled fact drives one slot to zero. It does not follow that another slot has moved. The continued disagreement is evidence that R is still live somewhere else, not that the fact failed to settle. Asking "why does the controversy persist after the fact is settled?" and answering "because people are irrational" silently aggregates the slots into a person and throws away the index.

*Refuter for F1:* show a controversy whose per-slot residuals all fall to zero when the key fact is settled, with no live slot remaining. That would collapse the union back into one question.

**Strain test.** Applied to a single two-person dispute about a number, the union reading would be strained: one slot might really be all there is. That is why the verdict is `specified` per domain and the union claim is about the class of persistent controversies, not about every dispute. Marked, not hidden.

### F2 : A settled fact is not a settled verdict when the anchor updates or the pool is shared

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 2 :47: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."*

When a community's standard of what counts as settled is itself a party to the dispute, the disagreement can converge coherently and still be wrong. Fig 2 :13 gives the collapse: *"no anchor; each updates on the others"*. Fig 2 :14 to :15: *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"* (the separate line :15 reads *"is whoever had the most influence"*). Conversely, Fig 2 :20: *"they never talk, and still agree"* because of :18 *"one evidence pool"*, and :21 to :22 *"so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"*.

The world literature agrees from a low-overlap field. Oreskes and Conway 2010 (as summarised on the opened Wikipedia page) describe a strategy to *"keep the controversy alive"* by *"continuing to spread doubt and confusion long after a scientific consensus has been reached"*. That is a channel and pool finding, not a discovery that the facts were unsettled.

*Refuter for F2:* show two frames in a persisting controversy whose agreement or disagreement draws on demonstrably independent evidence and whose standard of settlement does not move when either frame pushes on it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

### F3 : REFUTED residuals cannot be closed by more facts; a refusal is owed

This is the part of the answer that the ordinary word "controversy" hides. Fig 5 :50: *"the gap has a fifth kind, and the pipeline has no branch for it."* Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 5 :58 records the ranking test: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. The source line continues with wording this folder deliberately does not reproduce, because the no-em-dash rule would break the verbatim string; the shorter substring is exact. Formula step 2 states: *"REFUTED a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation"* and *"no arrangement of the other four reaches it"*.

So if the live residual is that a regularity held by one side is false, adding more facts of the same kind is the wrong operator. Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* The Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. This is why a persisted controversy can look like a failure of evidence when it is actually a missing refusal.

The experimenter's regress literature gives the mechanism from sociology of science. Collins (Wikipedia page opened 2026-11-23): *"the scientist has to get the right answer in order to know that the experiment is working, or know that the experiment is working to get the right answer."* Two frames with different tacit standards can each be internally correct and still fail to range against a shared ground.

*Refuter for F3:* show a persisting controversy in which the live residual is not that any held term is false, or show a false held term closed by additional same-kind facts with no refusal term admitted. Either would narrow D6 out of that case.

### F4 : What is settled in one closure may be underivable in another

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

A fact can be settled at the ground and remain unreachable from the vocabulary the other side holds. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The remedy is import with a source gate and shadow test, not more of the same evidence. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34: *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

Attitude-polarisation research supplies the low-overlap cognitive mechanism. Lord, Ross and Lepper 1979 (as reported on the opened Wikipedia page): *"When people encounter ambiguous evidence, this bias can potentially result in each of them interpreting it as in support of their existing attitudes, widening rather than narrowing the disagreement between them."* The same page records that *"When people had read both the research that supported their views and the research that did not, they tended to hold their original attitudes more strongly than before they received that information."* It also records methodological criticism of that study, which is noted here rather than hidden. This corroborates the mechanism shape, not the specific effect size.

*Refuter for F4:* exhibit a case where the settled fact is expressible in both closures and both sides can derive its consequences, and the disagreement still persists. That would remove closure difference as a live condition in that case.

### F5 : Standing and stake survive the outcome they were earned on

Fig 10 :61: *"And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

Once a factual question is settled, the actors who earned standing on the earlier dispute keep it. Their valence is relative to the residual, not carried: Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* What counted as a credible defender before settlement can count as an obstacle after it, with no change in the actor.

Agnotology gives this from history of science. Schiebinger 2004, quoted on the opened Wikipedia page: *"Ignorance is often not merely the absence of knowledge but an outcome of cultural and political struggle."* The same page defines the field as the study of *"deliberate, culturally cultivated ignorance or doubt, typically to sell a product, influence opinion, or win favour"*.

*Refuter for F5:* show a persisting controversy in which no actor's standing survives the settlement and no convention has acquired verdict power. Then D4 is not a condition of that case.

### F6 : The ground does not decay; the channel does

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :25 to :26 name the failure modes: *"stale percepts, dropped transients,"* and *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 10 :27 to :28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. The figures require *"keep the anchor reachable"* (Fig 10 :29).

A public can keep receiving a dispute long after the ground has paid, because the channel carries a recording of the old disagreement rather than the new settlement. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Isolation is not removal: Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*

False balance is the low-overlap communication finding. The opened Wikipedia page: *"It creates a public perception that some issues are scientifically contentious, although in reality they are not, therefore creating doubt about the scientific state of research."* The same page quotes Liz Spayd: *"The problem with false balance doctrine is that it masquerades as rational thinking."* This is a channel defect, not an epistemic defect in the facts.

*Refuter for F6:* show that the channel in a given controversy carries the current settlement at the same rate it carries the dispute, with no stale percepts or pooled readings. Then channel decay is not doing the work there.

### F7 : A chain of agreeing rooms is one long room

Fig 7 :12 to :13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 7 :42: *"the next room you book is the direction you travel, and booking a room like the last one is how a chain stops travelling at all."*

This explains the common observation that repeating the facts, publishing another review, or convening another panel does not end a persistent controversy. If the next room matches the last, Fig 7 :32 returns *"one long room: motion, no progress"*. Progress per handoff has two factors, *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* and *"the ground's pressure on the room"* (Fig 7 :37 to :39); if either is zero the chain runs in place.

*Refuter for F7:* show a sequence of rooms in a persisting controversy with low overlap between them and high ground pressure, and no movement. That would break the selection-between-rooms claim.

### F8 : Same settled facts, different bond, different controversy

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

Two controversies can share the same settled facts and differ entirely in the bond that joins them (values, identity, interests, jurisdiction). Over a fixed atom set they are isomers: the difference is not in the facts. A catalyst part, such as an institution or a prestigious name, survives its own use, which is Fig 12 :119 and Fig 10 :61 seen from two angles. Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* So description cost and action cost must be read separately; pooling them is Fig 12 :130 *"the error this names, not the fix for it."*

*Refuter for F8:* exhibit two controversies that share every settled atom and the same bond arrangement yet behave differently. That would break the isomer reading, leaving only an unmeasured difference.

### F9 : The refusal operator may not be available in the social domain (live strain)

Fig 5 :56: *"Same mint operator, same three guards, same bargain. Only the direction of the residual differs. Requires an operator for negation, which the seven bonds do not contain."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. The figures give ¬ as a unary operator but leave its price open (Operators table :77: *"What a unary operator costs is open: an atom slot, nothing, or the choice of what to negate."*).

Whether a real community possesses a usable refusal with standing to remove a held term's composability is **not** given by the figures. It is named as a live type question, not solved by an analogy. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* This is the most honest place to stop on D6.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion, 2026-11-23)

Required before any outreach: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map field guide). The shape claim in this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A controversy is exhibited whose per-slot residuals all go to zero when the key factual question is settled, with no live slot remaining (breaks F1).
2. Two frames in a persisting controversy are shown to draw on genuinely independent evidence and to hold a standard of settlement that does not move when either pushes on it, and the disagreement still persists at the same magnitude (breaks F2 and F8).
3. A false held term in a persisting controversy is closed by additional same-kind facts with no refusal term admitted (breaks F3).
4. A case is shown where no actor's standing survives settlement and no convention has acquired verdict power, yet the controversy persists unchanged (breaks F5).
5. A sequence of rooms with low overlap and high ground pressure is shown to leave the residual flat (breaks F7).

If any fires, the corresponding domain verdict is withdrawn, not softened. Until one is run, no part of this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union split and the per-slot residual, not with a list of "reasons people disagree" (F1). An averaged cause is a frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*).
2. Ask, for each alleged persistent controversy, which slot still carries positive residual and what its anchor is. If no anchor can be named, it is not yet a domain and the census is incomplete.
3. Test D6 first with a refusal written in advance: name the held term that would have to be false. Formula step 2 routes REFUTED before mint.
4. Treat independence as the parameter, not a caveat (Fig 8 :47). Two studies quoting the same review are one witness.
5. Send the generator, not a recording. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
