## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the source line 93 question. Option A: sunlight was the early source. Option B: chemical reactions were. Option C: multiple sources were. What each leads to: A commits to a photic habitat and very early pigment reaction centres; B commits to a redox couple, natural gradients and a vent or crustal setting; C commits to a union of overlapping conditions with its own loop. The figure-derived move is to refuse the forced choice, state the shape any source must have, and defer the historical attribution to the ground.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: at least binary and conjunctive, because light and chemical redox overlap in photothioautotrophy and vent geothermal light; R is a union compound (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: the options are not peers, because energy is directionless alone and must be bonded to a gradient (Fig 13 :76). Scale: prebiotic synthesis, first self-maintaining system, LUCA, and first phototroph are distinct strata (Fig 12 :103).

- **Domain 1, geochemical source habitat.** Actors: H2, CO2, Fe2+/Fe3+, serpentinisation, pH/redox/temperature gradients, inorganic microcompartments. Currency: redox free energy and proton motive force. Anchor: modern vent chemistry and lab CO2-reduction experiments. Partition or union: overlaps D2.
- **Domain 2, photic source habitat.** Actors: photons (sunlight and low-intensity geothermal light), Zn-tetrapyrroles, chlorophylls, reaction centres. Currency: excited-state electrons and ferredoxin reduction. Anchor: isotopes, stromatolites, microfossils, molecular clocks (disputed but nameable). Overlaps D1.
- **Domain 3, earliest-metabolism reconstruction.** Actors: phylogenetic gene families, Wood–Ljungdahl pathway, ATP synthase. Currency: phylogenetic reconciliation and presence probabilities. Anchor: genomic datasets and fossil/isotope calibrations. Composes over D1 and D2.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on overlapping evidence pools, so independence is low and agreement is not corroboration.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Gradient availability first, then coupling mechanism, then temporal order, then ground settlement. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced choice is refused. Any energy source for early metabolism must supply a spendable gradient; the source is one enumerated habitat condition among several; and the three options form a union of overlapping conditions, so "multiple sources" is a structural re-description and not a third peer. The figures do not name which source operated first, so that attribution is a `gap` at the figure level and a `specified`, ungrounded world reading: geochemical redox gradients appear to precede biological phototrophy, with light entering as a habitat condition and later as a reaction centre, and photochemistry feeding H2 and HCN back into surface ecosystems. The claim that chemical reactions alone or sunlight alone won is refused as written.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries its refuter before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a settled historical narrative instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The `specified` historical reading is removable without touching the figure derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` already keeps them on separate lines. No figure term has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating isotope or petrographic instrument naming the producer behind an ancient fractionation; a direct relative date of the first light-driven reaction centre against the first acetyl-CoA-type coupling; a clean-partition demonstration (refutes the union); a gradient-free self-maintaining system (refutes the shape); or a figure re-census that entailed a specific geochemical setting (refutes the shape-not-a-name limit). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
