## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question is *"Did early metabolism rely on sunlight, chemical reactions, or multiple sources?"* (source line 93). Stated as an effect:

> R = the gap between what we can read off the earliest energy-conversion machinery and what we can say about the free-energy source it coupled to.

Per-slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The slots are: (s1) the gradient available in a habitat, (s2) the coupling to that gradient without modern enzymes or membranes, (s3) the temporal order of source adoption, (s4) the instrument that can discriminate among sources from the rock and genome record.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: *a shape stating what any source must provide to be spendable, a union/partition judgement on the three options, a per-source verdict, and a named ground that would decide the historical order.* This is the formula's "Direction" discipline applied to this question. It makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** the question presents three options, but arity is not settled by the grammar. Fig 9 :55 supplies the test: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Light and chemical redox overlap in photothioautotrophy and in vent geothermal light, so R is at least binary and conjunctive. Treat it as a **union compound**, not a three-way partition.
- **Symmetry:** the options are not symmetric. Energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :13-14) and becomes usable only when bonded to a gradient (Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*). Light and H2/CO2 differ in whether and how they deliver a spendable gradient, so a symmetric vote among options is invalid.
- **Scale:** prebiotic geochemistry, first self-maintaining chemical systems, LUCA, and later phototrophy are distinct strata. Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* An answer at one stratum becomes an atom at the next.

**One line per domain. A domain needs actors, currency, and a nameable anchor.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Geochemical source habitat | H2, CO2, Fe2+/Fe3+, serpentinisation, pH/redox/temperature gradients, inorganic microcompartments | redox free energy, proton motive force | yes: modern vent chemistry (Lost City) and lab CO2-reduction experiments |
| D2 Photic source habitat | photons (sunlight and low-intensity geothermal light), Zn-tetrapyrroles, chlorophylls, reaction centres | excited-state electrons, ferredoxin reduction | yes: stromatolites, microfossils, carbon isotopes, molecular clocks (disputed but nameable) |
| D3 Reconstruction of the earliest metabolism | phylogenetic gene families, Wood–Ljungdahl pathway, ATP synthase presence probabilities | phylogenetic reconciliation and presence probabilities | yes: genomic datasets plus fossil/isotope calibrations |
| D4 Verification and outreach | expert readers | agreement versus residual | yes: the ground (experiment, measurement), never agreement |

All four have anchors, so all four are domains. D1 and D2 overlap (photothioautotrophy, vent geothermal light); D3 composes over both; D4 ranges against the ground.

**Partition or union:** union. The overlap between D1 and D2 has its own loop, so it is a compound, not a harder member of either. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Settle which gradients are available and stable (D1) before asking how energy is captured without modern enzymes (s2). Establish the coupling shape before dating the adoption of phototrophy (D2/D3). Range all of it against the ground (D4). Effort spent above the break cannot rescue a link below it (Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

## 3. Findings (verbatim quote first, then conclusion)

### F1 - The three options form a union, not a partition (decisive for the question's form)

Fig 9 :54-58, quoted verbatim:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**

World check: photothioautotrophs use light *and* a chemical electron donor (H2S). Martin, Bryant and Beatty (2018) propose that *"photosynthesis probably arose at hydrothermal vents under low-intensity, long-wavelength geothermal light"*, which places the first light-driven metabolism inside a chemical-gradient habitat. Moody et al. (2024) find that photochemistry in the atmosphere recycled H2 back into the surface ecosystem. Light and chemical redox are therefore overlapping conditions.

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** "multiple sources" is not a third peer of sunlight and chemical reactions. It is the re-description of the question as a compound with its own loop. The forced choice is refused by the figures. **Refuter:** show a clean partition, that is, a census in which every earliest metabolism uses exactly one source and no system ever couples two, with no overlap anywhere. If that holds, F1 fails and the three options can be treated as a list.

### F2 - Energy is directionless alone; a gradient is what becomes spendable

Fig 13 :13-14 and :76, quoted verbatim:

> **"energy / conserved, finite, directionless alone"**
> **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."**

Symbols table: energy is *"Conserved, finite, and directionless on its own."* Fig 13 :46: *"a difference that can be spent"* defines a gradient. Fig 13 :76 continues: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** the shape any energy source must have is *a spendable gradient across a boundary*, not merely the presence of energy. This governs the form of the answer, not the historical winner: sunlight can generate a gradient in a pigment just as serpentinisation generates a pH gradient across a vent wall. **Refuter:** demonstrate a self-maintaining chemical system that spends energy with no gradient of any kind, or show from the figures that energy can be spent without a gradient. Either breaks F2.

### F3 - The energy source is a habitat condition to be enumerated, not ranked

Fig 11 :43-48, quoted verbatim:

> **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
> **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
> **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"**
> **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** light is one item in an enumerated list of conditions. The list has no rank order. Any claim that sunlight is *the* source, or that chemical redox is *the* source, promotes one condition above the others without a figure warrant. **Refuter:** exhibit a figure line that ranks habitat conditions by energetic contribution to self-maintenance, or show that the enumeration is actually a composition with a dominant term. Neither exists in the figure set. If one were found, F3 would be withdrawn.

### F4 - Capability belongs to the agent-and-habitat pair, not to the source alone

Fig 11 :39-40, quoted verbatim:

> **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**
> **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

The same redox couple supports different outcomes depending on catalysts, compartments, and concentration. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A source is not intrinsically "the energy source for life"; its valence is relative to the system that couples to it.

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** asking which source early metabolism "relied on" treats a relation as a property. The answerable form is: which source was coupled to which system, under which conditions, at which stratum. **Refuter:** show a source whose capacity to drive self-maintenance is independent of habitat and coupling. That would break the habitat-relative reading.

### F5 - The historical order is a ground question; agreement cannot settle it

Fig 2 :29, :35 and :40, quoted verbatim:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
> **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

The world sources conflict on timing, and the conflict is informative:

- Blankenship (2010): *"photosynthesis began early in Earth's history, but was probably not one of the earliest metabolisms and that the earliest forms of photosynthesis were anoxygenic, with oxygenic forms arising significantly later."*
- Cardona (2019): *"The chief unproven assumption in the evolution of photosynthesis is that the origin of anoxygenic photosynthesis pre-dates the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis."*
- Martin, Bryant and Beatty (2018): *"There is broad consensus that H2-based chemosynthesis predated chlorophototrophy in microbial evolution and that anoxygenic photosynthesis predated the oxygenic form. There is, however, no consensus concerning the physiological processes that mediated either transition."*
- Moody et al. (2024): *"We found no evidence that LUCA was photosynthetic, with low PPs for almost all components of oxygenic and anoxygenic photosystems."*

These are not four independent votes. Sousa (2013), Martin et al. (2018), Schwander et al. (2023) and Weiss et al. (2016) share an author group and much of the same vent/H2 evidence pool. Cardona and Blankenship work on photosystem evolution from a different direction. Moody et al. use a different method (probabilistic reconciliation). Agreement among them is one pool talking where they overlap, and genuinely low-overlap corroboration where they do not.

**Conclusion (mode `general` on the rule, `specified` on the reading):** no figure line names the historical winner. The figures govern how the question must be checked, not what the answer is. The figure verdict on the historical attribution is a `gap`, deferred to the ground. **Refuter:** produce a figure line that states which source operated first, or a ground measurement that directly dates the first coupling to light versus redox. Until one fires, any historical claim stays `candidate`.

### F6 - The figures return a shape, not a name

Fig 9 :32, quoted verbatim:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** "H2/CO2 redox at alkaline vents" and "sunlight in the photic zone" are names supplied by the world frame. They are bindings, not figure derivatives. Recording them as figure outputs would be the named error of naming from a reading without a source-discriminating instrument. **Refuter:** show that the six-term set plus its bonds entails a specific geochemical setting. It does not; the set is domain-agnostic (Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*).

### F7 - Strata must not be flattened

Fig 12 :103-104, quoted verbatim:

> **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**
> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Prebiotic synthesis, the first self-maintaining system, LUCA, and the first phototroph are different levels. A result about LUCA (a reconstructed, already complex cell) does not settle the pre-LUCA origin question. Moody et al. (2024) put LUCA at ~4.2 Ga and call it *"a prokaryote-grade anaerobic acetogen"* that was *"part of an established ecological system"*, which is a level above the origin itself.

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** keep the per-domain verdicts separate and never average them across strata. **Refuter:** demonstrate that a LUCA-level reconstruction settles a pre-LUCA coupling question. That would collapse the level distinction and break F7.

### F8 - Self-maintenance is a cycle, so the source must be continuous

Fig 12 :121, quoted verbatim:

> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Sousa et al. (2013) describe the vent case in these terms: *"The chemical disequilibrium at vents arises from the contact between their reducing, H2-containing effluent with CO2-containing ocean water, which is more oxidized."* Serpentinisation is continuous over geological time, which is what makes the loop close rather than run down.

**Conclusion (mode `specified`, unmeasured here):** continuity of flux, not just presence of energy, is part of the shape. **Refuter:** exhibit a self-maintaining system driven by a one-off energy pulse whose output re-enters as input indefinitely. That would break the cycle requirement.

### F9 - The instrument rule: whether a source *could* work is the wrong question

Fig 6 :34-35, quoted verbatim:

> **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**

The rock record already returns readings: carbon isotopes, stromatolites, sulfur isotope fractionation. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The absence of a direct readout of the first metabolism is not evidence that no source operated; it is a statement about resolution.

**Conclusion (mode `general`):** the residual is an instrument problem as much as a chemistry problem. **Refuter:** show an instrument at any resolution that already discriminates the first light-coupled system from the first redox-coupled system. That would move this question toward `grounded`.

## 5. SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD (formula step 7, kept distinct)

No figure line closes the historical attribution after the census above. Apply the three questions in order.

1. **SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest).** Is the answer already in the closure, unfound? The habitat list contains light and chemical conditions (Fig 11 :46), and the gradient rule is in Fig 13. More searching within the six-term set will not produce a date or a geochemical name. The terms are present; the historical fact is not. So more search inside Γ is not the remedy.
2. **INWARD (extend an instrument already returning something).** Carbon isotopes, stromatolites, sulfur fractionation, and molecular clocks already return readings that fail to resolve the first coupling. Sharpen those: source-discriminating isotopes, better early-rock petrography, tighter clock calibrations. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
3. **OUTWARD (import from a frame whose closure differs).** The world frames used here are geochemistry (serpentinisation, vent gradients), phylogenomics (LUCA reconstruction), and photosystem evolution. Each has a source gate (provenance: the paper and its data) and a shadow test (does it explain a gap in R?). Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and were not read against each other. Importing a name (for example, "serpentinisation won") does not move the figure wall; it adds a world atom to the description, and is priced as such (Fig 6 :24).

## 6. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat-map requirement that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A clean partition is demonstrated: earliest metabolisms use exactly one source, no system ever couples two, and no overlap exists between the light and chemical conditions (breaks F1).
2. A self-maintaining chemical system is shown that spends free energy with no gradient of any kind, or a figure line permits this (breaks F2).
3. A figure line is found that ranks habitat conditions by energetic contribution (breaks F3).
4. Capability to drive self-maintenance is shown to be a property of a source alone, independent of habitat and coupling (breaks F4).
5. A source-discriminating instrument dates the first light coupling before the first redox coupling at the ground (breaks the `specified` historical reading in F5).
6. A figure re-census shows that the six-term set entails a specific geochemical setting (breaks F6).

If any fires, the claim is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
