# Expert verification packet - the Tether on early energy sources

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in early energy sources can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the historical reading, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Any energy source for the earliest self-maintaining chemical systems must present a spendable gradient; energy alone is directionless. The energy source is a habitat condition, enumerated rather than ranked, and light is one item in that enumeration. The three options in the question (sunlight, chemical reactions, multiple sources) are a union of overlapping conditions, not a symmetric list, so "multiple sources" is a structural re-description rather than a third peer. The figures do not name which source came first; that is a ground question. The world literature, read second, favours H2/CO2 redox and natural pH gradients as the earliest coupling, with phototrophy later and possibly first under vent geothermal light.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 11
- **Statement:** no energy source is spendable without a gradient, and habitat conditions are enumerated, not ranked.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :76 - *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Also Fig 13 :46 - *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*; Fig 11 :46 - *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"*.
- **Refuter:** show a self-maintaining system that spends free energy with no gradient of any kind, or exhibit a figure line that ranks habitat conditions by energetic contribution. Either breaks C1.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9
- **Statement:** the three options form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition; the forced choice is malformed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Also Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*; Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **Refuter:** show a clean partition in which earliest metabolisms each use exactly one source, no system couples two, and no shared gradient substructure exists. That breaks C2 and restores the list reading.

### C3 - world-facing; checkable against the published literature
- **Statement:** the best-supported historical reading is chemical redox first, phototrophy later, with both entering the same early ecosystems.
- **What to check:** Sousa et al. (2013) on H2 as electron donor and vent disequilibrium; Weiss et al. (2016) and Moody et al. (2024) on a H2-dependent, non-photosynthetic LUCA; Blankenship (2010) and Cardona (2019) on the disputed timing of early phototrophy; Martin, Bryant & Beatty (2018) on vent geothermal light; Schwander et al. (2023) on the absence of sunlight in crustal primary production.
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating measurement dating the first light coupling before the first redox coupling, or a robust reconstruction showing a phototrophic LUCA. That breaks the `specified` reading in C3. Note also that the vent/H2 papers share an author group and evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness, not several.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the figures proved chemical reactions won. They return a shape, not a name.
- Not that sunlight was absent or unimportant. It is a habitat condition and later a biological converter.
- Not that "multiple sources" means an even mixture. It is a union with a still-open order.
- Not that LUCA was the first life. It is a later stratum.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that agreement among reviews settles the timing. Several reviews share one evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

1. An isotope or petrographic instrument that ties a fractionation signal specifically to phototrophic or chemolithoautotrophic primary production.
2. A direct relative date for the first light-driven reaction centre and the first acetyl-CoA-type coupling.
3. A clean partition demonstration (refutes C2).
4. A gradient-free self-maintaining system (refutes C1).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :46 and :76, Fig 11 :43 and :46, Fig 9 :32, :55 and :57. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: does any known early metabolism couple light and a chemical donor at once? (Photothioautotrophy is one case.)
3. Check C3 against one paper they know well, and say whether the evidence pool is independent of the vent/H2 group.
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a phototrophic from a chemolithoautotrophic signature in the oldest rocks, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
