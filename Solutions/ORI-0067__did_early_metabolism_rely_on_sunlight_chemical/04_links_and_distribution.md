## What the wider literature actually says (secondary only)

1. **The forced choice is not how the field is structured.** The reviews separate *source of reductant* (H2 from serpentinisation versus photochemistry) from *source of energy* (redox disequilibrium versus photons) from *time of adoption*. Schwander et al. (2023) explicitly frame two categories of solution, surface and subsurface reduction, rather than a single choice. This corroborates the figure reading that the problem is a union.
2. **Chemical-first is the majority reading, not a figure verdict.** Sousa et al. (2013), Martin, Bryant & Beatty (2018), Weiss et al. (2016), Schwander et al. (2023), and Moody et al. (2024) all place H2/CO2 redox and natural gradients before biological phototrophy. But the first four share an author/evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness. Moody et al. use a different method and still reach a chemical-first reconstruction. Blankenship (2010) and Cardona (2019) add that the *timing* of early photosynthesis remains unresolved even if phototrophy is ancient.
3. **Light is not absent from the chemical-first picture.** Martin, Bryant & Beatty (2018) propose vent geothermal light as the setting for the first chlorophyll-based phototrophy. Moody et al. (2024) model photochemical regeneration of H2 and HCN in the surface ecosystem. This is why "multiple sources" is a union and not a tie to be split.

These are corroborations of a `specified` historical reading. They are not figure outputs and they do not move the figure wall.
