## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* and Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described the residual sends a recording. Lead with the separator and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would count as a failure before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; and Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A journal's impact factor or a reviewer's prestige is a filter for where to try, never a reason the separator is true.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim, and the five refuters in `02_outreach_email.md`. Ask what they would count as a failure and which instrument in their field discriminates the two causes. Record their verdict *before* showing sources.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three framework links. The figures, not this summary, are the authority. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a generator. Ask each user to write their own refuter, state their mode, and keep inward and outward tracks separate. A failure report is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 separator survives. The pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are the test. If one fires, revise before sending.
