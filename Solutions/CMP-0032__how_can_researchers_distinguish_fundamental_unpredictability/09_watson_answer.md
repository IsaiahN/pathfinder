## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how a researcher distinguishes two causes of a failed forecast. Option A: the target is fundamentally unpredictable, that is no composition in the current closure reaches it and more data or better models inside the frame will not. Option B: the uncertainty comes from incomplete data or imperfect models, that is the term is already reachable and a longer record, a better instrument or a corrected model would close it. What each leads to: treating B as A mints an atom that was not owed and debits independence; treating A as B spends budget on more search inside a closure that cannot reach the target. The figures decide neither by a number nor from inside the frame. They supply a separator, a prohibition, a protocol and a declared gap.

- **Domain 2, instrument and data channel.** Actors: sensor, resolution, record length, noise, surrogate data. Currency: `o′`, action cost. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay; the channel does. Question type: inward. Order: before D3.
- **Domain 3, ground / experiment.** Actors: measurement, experiment, counter, outcome. Currency: payment of terms. Anchor: itself, pinned. Question type: verdict. Order: after D2, before D4.
- **Domain 4, independent peer frames and public record.** Actors: other disciplines, other instruments, differing closures. Currency: imports with provenance, triangulation. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: outward and union. Order: last.
- **Union, not partition.** Both conditions can hold at once. One lever clears the contingent region and leaves the intersection (Fig 9 :56).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception (does anything register?) then vocabulary (what shape would the missing thing have?) then objective (what closes this slot?) then planning (two separate tracks) then learn and carry (provenance).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures answer the question with a separator and a protocol, not a decision number. Underivable in principle and unreachable at budget are different facts with different remedies. Failure to predict proves neither, because no frame is a sealed room and absence cannot be certified from inside. From inside the two look the same, so the inward track (instrument, channel, record, observed variables) and the outward track (a frame with a differing closure and non-shared evidence) must be run apart and never read against each other. "Fundamental" is irreducibility at a level relative to a declared closure, never an absolute bottom. Chaos is a compounding remainder and noise is discarded detail reappearing above a crossing; neither is a new primitive. Verdicts are per slot and per domain, never averaged. The universal numeric cutoff is a declared `gap`, a verdict and not a silence, with an instrument owed. Mode `specified` on the distinction, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the threshold, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the protocol is followed. The separator, its refuters and the per-slot rule are written down first, so an error is findable. The real cost is minting an unneeded atom by calling a budget-limited case fundamental, which Fig 8 :43 prices above starting from primitives, and spending the independence of outside readers by asking for agreement before a verdict. Both are avoidable by leading with the refuter and recording the stage-1 verdict before showing sources.
- **undoable:** yes. The numeric gap can be closed by an instrument without changing the separator. A wrongly named fundamental case can be reclassified by inward repair. A wrongly named contingent case can be re-examined with an outside witness. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the record keeps both readings visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A proved barrier relative to a declared closure (Fig 8 :34, :37) would license a positive fundamental claim; a residual that trends down under inward repair (Fig 6 :29, Fig 10 :24) would reclassify a case as contingent; an instrument that discriminates compounding remainder from discarded detail at the same level would firm the chaos/noise shape; a figure line returning a universal threshold would overturn the gap; deriving one of the six from the other five would break the level discipline. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
