## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unexplained response at a species after a direct-link prediction. Arity: three or more species, because A must act through B to reach C; a binary edge cannot carry it. Symmetry: directed and sign-bearing, with the sign relative to the species tracked, so the same intermediary flips valence when the residual changes. Scale: first order direct, second order one relay, third order and beyond cascade. The figure's own instance sits three steps out.
- **Domain 1, food web as habitat.** Actors: species and the relations predation, competition, mutualism, parasitism, dependency. Currency: abundance per species and contact distance. Anchor: an intervention with a measured response. Without that anchor this is not a domain yet, only a candidate list.
- **Domain 2, compositional structure.** Actors: the six substrate terms, with network and gradient load-bearing, and the ordered bonds. Currency: composition and flow. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. Question type: partition of what may compose.
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: the manipulator, path models, strength estimates. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement.

Order by dependency: enumerate habitat and direct contacts first; then bind the per-slot residual and its step index as the objective; then choose instruments; then let the ground settle. I reached the same three domains and the same union reading as the primary, but from different starting figures. I began with Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's bond typing; the primary began with Fig 11's habitat and Fig 1's slot indexing. Different routes, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within one framework, still one evidence pool.

One point where I differed before reading the primary: I initially wanted to admit a fourth domain, "the literature on indirect effects", with citations as its currency. Re-census removed it. Citations are not an anchor, and Fig 7 :13 says *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* The ecology literature belongs inside Domain 3 as a pool of frames, not as a separate ground.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43 to :54 (habitat enumeration, contact cascade, DDT case), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (slot indexing), Fig 12 :121 and :125 (loop sign, two currencies), and Fig 9 :54 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: propagate by contact outward, index per species and step, dominance is a scoping condition, sign is relative to the residual, flow and impact do not add.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a proposed ecological study design: perturb A, measure every species at every step, fix the dominance step in advance, permute links for the isomer test.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the anchor**, which the shorthand leaves as "a ground" but which must be a specific intervention with a measured response before any dominance comparison is admissible;
- the **sign multiplication rule**, which is our binding onto Fig 12 :121 and is not itself a figure line, so it can fail at the ground (the sign refuter in `01` section 4 exists for exactly this reason);
- the **currencies**, which the shorthand keeps apart but which a single "importance" column in a real dataset silently recombines.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must be enumerated outward from the residual, and the residual must be indexed per slot. That is the residue worth sending to an outside checker. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
