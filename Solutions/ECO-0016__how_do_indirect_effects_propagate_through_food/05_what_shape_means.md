## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (V): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0016 the shape is therefore:

> a per-slot, signed residual vector over contact distance, carried by enumerated contact outward through the habitat, with a dominance condition stated as a scoping relation between a first-order enumeration and a ground-visible residual further out.

This is a shape because it constrains any acceptable answer without supplying the magnitude, the species, or the universal threshold. A specific claim such as "indirect effects account for 40 percent of change" (Menge 1995) is a **measured instance in one system**, not the shape and not a law. "The dominant residual was three steps out" (Fig 11 :52) is the figure's own instance, likewise not a universal number.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (V): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* (V) on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is precisely the direct-plus-indirect union (Fig 9 :54). It must be split before searching.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (V): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` is built on this: it sends the expected residual pattern, not the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* We describe a per-slot residual and a contact distance, not "trophic importance".
- *Library of Babel* (V) on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For ECO-0016 the stop is when further enumeration stops changing the per-slot residual, which is exactly Fig 11 :44's *"outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* For this question the anchor is an intervention with a measured response, as in the DDT case (Fig 11 :54).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Watts 2002 from network science is such a low-overlap frame, but it corroborates only the cascade shape; it does not license an ecological magnitude.

*They're Scanning Books* (C) sharpens the import rule: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* No new dimension is imported here. Network is already one of the six structures (Fig 13 :24), so the ecology-to-framework mapping adds no atom.

## 4. The specific forbidden error for this question

The temptation is to name a single winner, "indirect effects", and attach a universal magnitude. Three framework rules refuse it:

- Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A valence-free ranking of "important interactors" is the flat reading.
- Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* A single importance score mixes flow and impact.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (C) gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads a projection *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can give the same reading. A web-level total abundance is a reading that cannot comment on which slot or which relay produced it. What is owed is a better, per-slot instrument, not a name.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (V): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

Applied here: a well-cited review, a famous ecologist, or a high-impact journal does not settle whether the dominance condition holds in a given web. Those things can give the claim a budget to be tried. The intervention is what settles it. The contact CSV in `03` deliberately lists venues and opt-in channels, not prestige ranks.

## 6. Agreement is not corroboration

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (V): *"You may all agree and be wrong."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The ecology sources read for this solution mostly cite one another (Wootton, Menge, Paine, Novak, Zhao). They are one witness. The network-science source (Watts) is low overlap and can corroborate the cascade shape only. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*

## 8. Retraction and correction carried into this reading

- The instinct to treat "indirect effect" as a fifth interaction type is retracted. It is a union condition, not a kind (Fig 9 :54; Fig 11 :50).
- The instinct to read "roughly 40 percent" (Menge 1995) as a general constant is retracted. It is a result in rocky intertidal webs for one residual definition, and Wootton 1994 explicitly reports strong target-specific cases where indirect exceeds direct. Two readings, not an average (Fig 12 :130).
- The instinct to call network a new primitive for ecology is retracted. It is one of the six (Fig 13 :24).
- The claim that this framework can rank species by importance is refused. Fig 9 :28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
