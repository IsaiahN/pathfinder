## The question restated as an effect

Two options are being decided. (A) The response of a species to a perturbation is explained by its direct links alone, so a sufficiently precise direct-link model closes the residual. (B) A residual persists at species several contact steps from the perturbation, and under a stated scoping condition that residual dominates the direct component. Option A leads to enumerating pairwise links and stopping. Option B leads to enumerating the habitat outward until the cascade stops mattering, measuring the response per species and per step, and naming a ground that can settle the magnitude comparison. What an answer would look like is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 1 before any figure line is chosen: a per-slot residual vector indexed by contact distance, with a sign rule and a dominance condition.
