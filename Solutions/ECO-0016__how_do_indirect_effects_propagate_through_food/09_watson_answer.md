## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about two things: what carries a response from species A to species C when no direct A to C edge exists, and under what condition the indirect contribution exceeds the direct one. Option A: the residual is a web-wide scalar and a sufficiently precise direct-link model closes it. Option B: the residual is indexed per species and per contact step, carried by contact outward through an enumerated habitat, and dominance is a scoping condition between a first-order enumeration and a ground-visible residual further out. What each leads to: A hides the signal in an average and offers no testable dominance condition; B yields a checkable shape, six written refuters, and a per-slot perturbation experiment that would close the magnitude question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the direct-link prediction and the observed response at a species. Arity: at least three species, A, an intermediary B, and the recipient C; a two-species edge cannot express it. Symmetry: directed and sign-bearing, with the sign of a length-k chain the product of link signs relative to the tracked residual. Scale: first order direct, second order one intermediary, third order and beyond a cascade; the figure's own case is three steps out.
- **Domain 1, the food web as habitat.** Actors: species and the relations predation, competition, mutualism, parasitism, dependency. Currency: abundance and biomass per species, and contact distance in steps. Anchor: an intervention or measured response, the ground. This is a habitat enumerated never composed; with no named measurement it is not yet a domain.
- **Domain 2, the compositional structure.** Actors: the six substrate terms, especially network and gradient, and the bonds sequence, production, cycle, comparison. Currency: composition inside closure(Gamma) and flows over the network. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. Question type: a partition of what may compose, and which bond carries the effect.
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: the manipulating ecologist, path analysis or structural equation models, interaction-strength estimates. Currency: the residual per species and per step. Anchor: the ground, never agreement among frames.
- **Partition or union.** A union. Direct and indirect effects can co-occur for the same species pair; the indirect case lives in the intersection of two conditions and needs its own loop. Unbundle before searching.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate habitat and direct contacts, state the per-slot residual and its step index, choose instruments and manipulations, then let the ground settle. A centrality score without a measured per-slot response is a reading below the break.

Strain test applied: the phrase "the web is a network" first looked like a seventh primitive. Re-census showed network is one of the six structures, so no atom is added and no import is owed. No other strain survived re-census.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources are corroboration only. Each entry gives field, author / venue / year, a quote, its prediction here, and whether it corroborates or conflicts.

- **Community ecology, definition and routes.** J. Timothy Wootton, Annual Review of Ecology and Systematics, 1994: "Indirect effects occur when the impact of one species on another requires the presence of a third species." Also: "there are many examples where the direct effects of a manipulated species on a particular target species are much weaker than the indirect effects involved." Predicts a ternary-plus effect and target-specific dominance. Corroborates the arity and the dominance condition.
- **Community ecology, review and methods.** J. Timothy Wootton, Journal of Sea Research, 2002: "Indirect effects arise when direct interactions mediated by changes in density share a species, when species change the interaction between individuals of other species, or both." Predicts two members of the union. Corroborates the unbundling verdict.
- **Community ecology, timing.** Bruce A. Menge, The American Naturalist, 1997: "indirect effects appeared either simultaneously with direct effects or shortly after direct effects were evident." Predicts that indirect effects are not necessarily slow. Corroborates with a limit on any "indirect means delayed" reading.
- **Community ecology, magnitude.** Bruce A. Menge, Ecological Monographs, 1995: indirect effects accounted for roughly 40 percent of change in community structure from manipulations, range 24 to 61 percent, roughly constant with web richness. Predicts a large but not dominant average fraction. Corroborates the refusal of a universal dominance law and the target-specific reading.
- **Community ecology, predictability.** Mark Novak, J. Timothy Wootton, Daniel F. Doak, Mark Emmerson, James A. Estes and M. Corroborates the need for a named slot and anchor.
- **Community ecology, node importance.** Zhao et al., Ecology Letters, 2016: "controlled removal studies have demonstrated empirically that material flow does not always translate directly to the impacts that interaction strength purports to capture." Predicts that flow and impact rank differently. Corroborates the two-currency verdict.
- **Community ecology, foundational.** R. T. Paine, Journal of Animal Ecology, 1980: "A consumer will be a strong interactor if, in its absence, pronounced changes ensue." Predicts an intervention definition of strength. Corroborates the ground requirement.
- **Education reference, trophic cascades.** Dafeng Hui, Nature Education Knowledge, 2012: "Indirect interaction occurs when two species do not interact with each other directly, but influenced by a third species." ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the universal magnitude law does not. Indirect effects propagate by contact outward through a habitat enumerated until the cascade stops mattering, with the residual indexed per species and per step and signed relative to the tracked species. Dominance of indirect over direct is a scoping condition: it holds when a first-order enumeration misses a ground-visible residual that sits further out and the relays do not damp it. Direct and indirect form a union compound, flow and impact are two currencies, and two webs over the same species set with different links are isomers. The universal form, "indirect effects always outweigh direct interactions", is a `gap` after a genuine re-census, reported as a verdict. Every substantive claim here is `general` or `specified` and ungrounded; this run is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three checkable sub-claims and six refuters before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the framework before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is ecological: treating flow as impact could mis-rank a conservation target.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal claim is not made; the scoped claim is a shape, and the isomer and sign bindings are removable without touching the habitat derivation. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The experiment owed is a per-slot perturbation, which is itself reversible in the sense that a wrong step index can be re-fixed before the data are read.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot perturbation experiment that fixes the dominance step in advance and finds first-order dominance in every species under every perturbation would break the dominance condition; a sign product that fails independently measured links would break the sign binding; identical per-slot residuals from two webs with permuted links would break the isomer claim; a scalar that collapses flow and impact would break the two-currency verdict. More reviews, more citations, or a larger list of agreeing ecologists would not reverse any of it (Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not").
