# The Tether's answer: What forms of memory support planning and learning across long periods?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the falsifiable role predictions. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question can be read as two options. Option A: there is one memory faculty and what supports planning and learning over long periods is simply more of it, a bigger or longer store. Option B: there are non-interchangeable forms with distinct roles, and which forms are required is decidable from what each must do in the loop. Option A cannot explain why a stored episode does not by itself yield a novel plan. Option B gives a shape with separate roles and separate refuters. The figures return Option B's shape, but they do not return a list of named systems. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

## 2. The shape

Four role constraints follow from the figures. Each has its own mode and status.

1. **The record and the reach are two different memory quantities (`general`, triangulated).** Fig 6 :42: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." Fig 6 :43: "So the closure and the reach are two quantities: one monotone, one not." A bigger store is not the same thing as more memory being available now. This is why a single-faculty answer fails at the structural level.

2. **Across long periods, what carries forward is a method or a head start, not a recording (`specified`, single-source with low-overlap world corroboration).** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Fig 4 :25-26: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." The temporal qualifier is answered by this membrane rule, not by inventing a new memory kind.

3. **Planning memory must carry bonds, not just contents (`general` for the bond claim, `specified` for its application, single-source with world corroboration).** Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." The form that supports planning is the form that retains typed relations, not the form that holds the most items.

4. **Long horizons bind on channel maintenance, not on store size (`specified`, triangulated).** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch." A memory system that is never re-checked against its ground drifts, and the drift is coherent (Fig 2 :34: "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."). Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

These four are the shape any answer must have. The named forms from cognitive science, episodic, semantic, procedural, working and prospective memory, are bindings onto these roles arrived at from a world frame. They are imports with provenance, recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`, and they carry no figure-derived authority. "Across long periods" is not a seventh memory primitive: time is already a state (Fig 13 :11-12: "time / the ordered succession of differences"), and the continuum precedent (Fig 13 :43-44) rules out adding what the held terms already produce.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a derivation of the textbook taxonomy from the figures. The names are world imports.
- Not that one named form is the method form or the route form. That binding is unproven and is left open.
- Not that "long-term memory" is a new primitive. Duration is a scale, not a kind.
- Not that an episode can never help planning. Flexible recombination of episodes under bonds is a composition (role 3), not the replay-only case role 2 refuses. This corrects an earlier reading recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, lesion, ablation or behavioural result was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground reading, not a consensus. The pre-registered refuters, written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, are the things that would break the shape claim:

1. a functioning system with one monotone memory quantity and no separate fallible reach;
2. a novel successful plan produced from verbatim replayed occasion-specific recordings, with no method compression and no head start;
3. genuine planning from unconnected atoms with no sequence, comparison or subtraction structure;
4. a long-horizon system whose ground channel needs no maintenance, or whose growing store guarantees continued contact;
5. a cognitive-science memory form derived from the figures alone, with no world import;
6. a duration-only, indivisible memory form not derivable from the held terms and bonds.

Any one of these moving the claim to `grounded` requires an instrument or experiment, not agreement among researchers (Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). A list selected for receptiveness would manufacture the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
