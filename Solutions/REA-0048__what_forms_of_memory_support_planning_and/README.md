## What the answer says in one line

The figures do not return a list of named memory systems. They constrain the **shape** such a list must have: a monotone record kept distinct from a fallible reach, methods and head starts that cross scales rather than recordings that only replay, a bond vocabulary that can name a route and not just a state, and a maintained channel because the ground does not decay while the channel does. The named memory forms from cognitive science are secondary bindings onto those roles, each with its own refuter.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
2. **Do not hand over the interiors before asking for the verdict.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
3. **Agreement is not corroboration when the pool is shared.** This package's second seat in `11_audit.md` reads the same pool as the primary, so its agreement is internal consistency, not a second witness.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public, opt-in venues in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
