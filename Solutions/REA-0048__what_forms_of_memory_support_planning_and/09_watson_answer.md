## QUESTION

Option A: one memory faculty, where more and longer storage is what matters. Option B: non-interchangeable forms with distinct roles, decidable from what each must do in the loop. What each leads to: A collapses the record into the reach and the method into the recording, and cannot explain why a stored episode does not by itself yield a novel plan; B gives a shape with separate roles, separate refuters and an answer to the temporal qualifier that needs no new memory kind.

## VERDICT

The shape survives; the named bindings do not. The figures license four roles: two distinct memory quantities (one monotone record, one fallible reach); a membrane across which methods and head starts pass and replayed recordings do not; a route vocabulary carrying sequence, comparison and subtraction; and a maintained channel because the ground does not decay while the channel does. "Across long periods" is a scale on a held time axis, not a seventh primitive. The named cognitive-science forms are imports with declared overlap, kept as bindings onto the roles and never promoted to figure-derived names. Option A is refused as a `specified` structural result, not a ground verdict. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; no measurement was taken.

- cost if wrong: bounded and locatable. The packet carries mode, verbatim lines and refuters written before the run, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- undoable: yes. The named bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already hold them as imports rather than figure names. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A functioning system with one monotone memory quantity and no fallible reach; a novel plan reliably produced from verbatim replay with no method compression and no head start; genuine planning from unconnected atoms; a long-horizon system whose ground channel needs no maintenance or whose growing store guarantees contact; a figure-derived cognitive-science form; or a duration-only indivisible form. Any one of these would break the relevant role. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
