## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The episodic/semantic boundary is itself under revision.** The Memory & Cognition journal page lists a call for papers on *"Rethinking the Distinction between Episodic and Semantic Memory"*, noting that *"numerous findings have put in doubt the clear-cut nature of this distinction"* (journal page, fetched 2026-11-23). This is a live ground-level dispute about whether the memory forms are distinct kinds or one system at different timescales. It does not settle the framework's shape claim; it is exactly the kind of evidence a refuter would use.
2. **Animal planning has a live alternative explanation.** The Bischof-Kohler criticism in the episodic-like memory literature says future-oriented behaviour may be *"either fixed action patterns or cued by their current motivational state"* rather than planning (Episodic-like memory, fetched 2026-11-23). That is the "recording replayed" rival to the method/head-start reading, named from an independent field. It is a useful question to put to an expert at stage 1.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full essay set before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four role constraints and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :41, *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four role constraints and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** the residual in effect terms, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. The reader checks it against their own knowledge without holding the framework's interior. Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if the reader is curious, the three local framework sources and the wider literature links. Their stage 1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3:** the formula offered as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes a refuter and states a mode before running it. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" explains why a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage 1 claim survives. The claim is stated with mode and refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. If the refuter fires, stop and retract rather than send.
