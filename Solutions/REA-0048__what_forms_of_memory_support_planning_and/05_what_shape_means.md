## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again. It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (read this run): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the method: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed, checkable form of an answer you can state before you can name its occupants. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, *"a method or head start must cross the scale boundary, not a replayed recording"* is a shape. *"Procedural memory is that method"* is a name, and the figures do not supply it.

The essay also gives the union warning directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9's union split applied to the question as asked: "memory supporting planning" and "memory supporting learning across long periods" are two conditions, not one.

## 2. How a shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the refuter in `01` is written as things one would observe (a monotone reach, a novel plan from replay, planning from atoms), not as a dispute over labels.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* This run stops at the four role constraints, not at a finished taxonomy.

## 3. What licenses a name, and a correction to my own first reading

My first reading of "shape, not a name" was too broad. I initially wrote that the figures forbid naming episodic, semantic or procedural memory as occupants of the roles. That is wrong, and the correction is recorded here rather than erased.

**Correction.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in `Γ`. Here the cognitive-science forms are not candidate new primitives of the six-term substrate (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network). They come from a **different frame** and arrive as imports. By Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs"*, and by Fig 8 :50-52, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* So:

- naming a world memory form is **allowed as a provenanced binding** onto a role;
- it is **not** a figure derivation;
- it does **not** move the wall, because it adds no atom to the framework's substrate;
- it carries zero verdict weight until its source gate and shadow test are shown.

What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh memory primitive defined only by duration. Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Time is already a state (Fig 13 :11-12: *"the ordered succession of differences"*), so "long-term" is a scale, not a kind. Both readings are kept visible: the narrowed naming rule and the anti-seventh rule.

## 4. Why the "one faculty, more of it" reading is the named error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism with metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several underlying sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or an undiscovered scheme) can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: observing that a store grew, or that a system remembered more after longer training, is a **reading**, not a discrimination among the sources of that improvement. A bigger record, a better reach, a compressed method and a maintained channel can all produce the same behavioural reading of "remembered more". Without an instrument that discriminates them, naming "more memory" as the cause is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better discrimination, which is exactly what the refuter list in `01` tries to provide.

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* So the honest stop is "shape reached, names not derived", not "no answer".

## 5. Two consequences for this answer

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous memory researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a ground that decides between the roles.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question.

## 6. One more thing the essays add to the temporal qualifier

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the long-period transfer rule in almost exactly the framework's words: *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself. 'Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down' (Fig 4 :22)."* And the cost of dropping the record across a boundary: *"where the record isn't carried, 'the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again' ... Each new learner pays part of the bill again, and contact is how they pay it."* This is an independent essay grounding for treating "across long periods" as a membrane and contact question rather than a storage-duration question. It corroborates F2 and F4 of `01`, but because it shares an author with the figures, it is one evidence pool, not a second witness.
