# Expert verification packet: the Tether on concentrated ownership and the rules of competition and labor

**What this is:** a claim in a form a competition or labor expert can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No rule capture, wage effect, or threshold is asserted as measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When a few owners control a large share of a market, competition rules and labor rules do not merely bend; a rule that decides an outcome changes kind, from an impartial convention into a constant authored by whoever holds the rule-making seat. A market or wage benchmark that moves when a concentrated actor pushes on it has stopped being an anchor. Ownership reaches labor only through a structure, with the slot left unnamed, and the rule change is second-order recursion whose loop sign decides whether it closes. Provenance and not wording decides whether a rule was captured, and the signal is per market and per labor segment, never an aggregate. The framework returns that shape, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal and rule-facing, checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** a rule that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention, and an anchor that moves when pushed is not an anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47 - "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 10 :48 - "a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority". Fig 2 :43 - "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 2 :30 - "you push on it, it is not an anchor."
- **What to check:** take one rule you know well. Is it checkable without consulting the body that authored it, and does it still function as a shared meaning rather than a determinant of outcomes? Did its benchmark move when a concentrated actor pushed?
- **Refuter:** show a rule that decides an outcome and still works as a two-names-one-referent convention checkable by anyone; or show a benchmark that did not move under a documented push, with a before and after record. Either breaks C1.

### C2. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 13 and Figure 12

- **Statement:** ownership reaches labor through a structure, never directly, and the rule change is second order with a sign.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75 - "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Fig 12 :104 - "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom" Fig 12 :121 - "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** is there any institutional path from an ownership position to a wage or entry outcome with no intervening contract, rule, licensing regime, or bargaining institution? Does the rule change reuse the settled firm as an actor at the rule level, and does its sign increase or reduce the concentration that produced it?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a direct composition with no mediator in a domain where cross-kind composition is claimed; or show a rule change that is demonstrably first order with no settled firm becoming an atom at the rule level. Either breaks C2.

### C3. Framework-internal and evidence-facing, checkable by reading Figure 8 and Figure 1, plus a source check

- **Statement:** provenance and not wording decides capture, and the residual is per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 - "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures" Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 - "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** can you distinguish a captured rule from an independently derived one by wording alone? Does a national concentration number preserve every live local labor slot? The public sources on regulatory capture, common ownership, monopsony, and occupational licensing are secondary corroboration only, with overlap declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- **Refuter:** recover a rule's source purely from its text; or exhibit an aggregate statistic that preserves every live per-slot signal. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a concentration threshold, a named captured rule, or a list of guilty firms.
- Not that concentration is always harmful. Fig 11 :49 - "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
- Not an average across competition rules and labor rules. They are a union of three loops with their own compound.
- Not a claim that any real institution was captured. That requires an authorship record and an enforcement record.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was used.
- Not a final theory. Fig 7 :35 - "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

## 5. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :47-48, Fig 2 :30, Fig 13 :75, Fig 12 :104 and :121, Fig 8 :51, and Fig 1 :19 and :22. Nine short passages.
2. Pick one competition or labor rule you know. Apply C1 and C3 to it. Does wording alone tell you its provenance? Did its benchmark move under a push?
3. Try to name one direct ownership-to-labor path with no intervening rule or contract. If you cannot, say so; if you can, that is the refuter for C2.
4. Name one instrument that would measure a rule's loop sign, or say why none is available.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
