| Monopsony | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopsony | Theory from Joan Robinson (1933) and dynamic monopsony from Alan Manning (2003). States that monopsony power lets a buyer set wages below competitive levels, and that empirical evidence is limited, with no observable monopsony power in low-skilled US labor markets outside specialised fields. Used for the per-slot residual and the shape-not-a-name limit. |

Used for the structure-mediated, second-order link. |
| Ideal free distribution | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_free_distribution | Ecology and animal behaviour model (Fretwell and Lucas, 1969; Fretwell, 1972). Individuals distribute by resource quality, but dominant individuals can keep weaker ones from the ideal patch, and unequal competitors change the match. Low-overlap analogue for contact, habitat, and relative valence. |
| Occupational licensing | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupational_licensing | Licensing as a barrier to entry and a rent-seeking institution; Kleiner and Krueger wage premium estimates; a 2017 study finding 36 percent lower between-state migration in licensed occupations. ## Wider literature that bears on the question

These were not all opened individually and are not in the ledger as read sources. They are recorded here as pointers for a checker who wants the primary literature behind the summaries above.

- George Stigler, The Theory of Economic Regulation, Bell Journal of Economics, 1971. The source of the capture hypothesis quoted in the Regulatory capture page.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full derivation before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot residual and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 9 :60** - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A concentration score says where to spend attention, not what is true.
- **Fig 7 :35** - "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) the question, what would you count as a failure; (3) only after their verdict is recorded, the six links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, 'Bring a characterised residual, never the source', and Fig 2 :45, 'Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.' The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it searches for is not a term it holds. Each reader must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

## Hosting and sending

This agent can prepare the files in this folder but cannot host them or send them to anyone. If you host them, prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. That is closer to the figure rule than a bulk attachment, and it avoids the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failure that comes with scraping personal addresses.
