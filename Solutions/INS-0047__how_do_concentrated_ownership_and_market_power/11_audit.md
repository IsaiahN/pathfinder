Arity: two conditions plus their intersection inside the firm, which is seller in product markets and buyer in labor markets, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55-57), not one hard question and not a list of two. - **Domain 2, product-market competition.** Actors: firms, consumers, entrants. Currency: prices, output, share. Anchor: nameable, a market and its prices and output. Question type: first order.
- **Domain 3, labor.** Actors: workers, employers, unions, licensing bodies. Currency: wages, job access, mobility. Anchor: nameable, wage and employment records. Question type: first order, overlapping Domain 2 inside the firm.
- **Domain 4, rule-making and institutions.** Actors: regulators, legislators, courts, standard setters. Currency: rules, enforcement, interpretation. Anchor: nameable, rule text plus enforcement record and provenance. Question type: second order.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, journals, consultation portals. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all five domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Union and order.** Three loops owed, read in dependency order: ownership condition, first-order product and labor outcomes, then the second-order rule change, then feedback. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
