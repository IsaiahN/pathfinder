## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the reshape of competition rules and labor rules under concentrated ownership and market power. Option A: assert that concentration simply captures rules and produce one verdict about the powerful. Option B: describe the residual first, keep the figure-derived shape, and refuse the specific names. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode verdict with a named culprit the framework says it cannot name, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, six written refuters, and open slots until a ground settles them.

The residual in effect terms: when a few owners control a large share of a market, rules that present themselves as impartial and open come to operate differently. The residual is the gap between the rule as presented and the rule as operating under concentration, indexed per market and per labor segment.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two conditions plus their intersection inside the firm, where the owner is seller in product markets and buyer in labor markets. Symmetry: asymmetric, since introduction of actors and rules is available and exclusion of the habitat is not. Scale: first order inside a market, second order at the rule level. - **Domain 1, ownership and concentration.** Actors: owners, holding companies, boards, merger filers. Currency: control rights, ownership stakes, market share. Anchor: nameable, ownership registries, merger filings, concentration ratios. Status: a condition until anchored, then a full domain.
- **Domain 2, product-market competition.** Actors: firms, consumers, entrants. Currency: prices, output, market share. Anchor: nameable, a market and its prices and output (Fig 2 :29 names a market as an anchor). Question type: first order.
- **Domain 3, labor.** Actors: workers, employers, unions, licensing bodies. Currency: wages, job access, bargaining power, mobility. Anchor: nameable, wage and employment records. Question type: first order, overlapping Domain 2 inside the firm.
- **Domain 4, rule-making and institutions.** Actors: regulators, legislators, courts, standard-setting bodies. Currency: rules, enforcement, legal interpretation. Anchor: nameable, rule text plus enforcement record and provenance. Question type: second order, the seat that authors conventions.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, journals, consultation portals. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Three loops are owed: competition rules, labor rules, and the compound at their intersection (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Establish the ownership condition, read first-order product and labor outcomes, then read the second-order rule change, then close feedback onto ownership (Fig 3 :20). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the names do not. A rule under concentration changes kind from convention to authored constant; a market or wage benchmark that moves when pushed is not an anchor; ownership reaches labor through an unnamed structure; the change is second-order recursion whose loop sign decides closure; provenance and not wording decides capture; the residual is per slot; and concentration's valence is relative to the residual. What is refused: any concentration threshold, any named captured rule, and any global verdict that concentration is bad. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, `candidate` until a ground reading fires one of the six tests in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 4.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, writes six refuters before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as follows: 'Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.' That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is presenting a contested empirical mechanism as settled, which invites a refutation that lands on the whole package rather than one slot.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific namings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. Six tests are stated so a failed slot can be dropped while the remaining shape stands.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A before and after enforcement record showing a benchmark did not move when pushed would break the anchor finding; an authorship record showing independent derivation would break the capture reading; a wage series showing no live residual in a slot would break the per-slot finding; a demonstrated direct ownership-to-labor composition with no mediator would break Fig 13 :75 as applied; a measurement of a rule's loop sign would settle the recursion claim; and a figure re-census that treats settled rule systems as quantities at second order would clear the operand-type strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
