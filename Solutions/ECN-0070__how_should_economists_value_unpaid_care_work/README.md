## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the size of the monetary estimate but the shape of the rule that reduces a measurement residual without destroying the diagnostic. In effect terms: economists' measurement frame Γ (the System of National Accounts, GDP, labour force statistics) predicts Γ(b,a), what counts as output and employment. The observation o' is the full set of productive activity actually performed in households. R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| is measured per slot s (activity type, household, sex, country), never as one global number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "assign one monetary equivalent and add it to GDP": leads to a single aggregate that pools the very slots whose signal is needed, and lets a convention decide an outcome (Fig 1 :22; Fig 10 :47).
- Option B, "keep the residual per slot, separate the convention from the verdict, and treat wage proxies as blind instruments": leads to a shape with stated falsifiers, per-slot time and distribution first, money second (Fig 4 :22; Fig 2 :39-41).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a rule that (i) keeps R indexed by slot, (ii) unbundles the convention from the factual claim, (iii) names the axes on which each valuation instrument is blind, and (iv) carries a measured round-trip gap when transferred across contexts. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` section 4 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. The single "correct" number is a `gap`: the framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the economics question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of care valuation.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence, and this question is partly a convention. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40) but *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* (Fig 2 :41). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names on the factual part, and is doing legitimate work only on the unit-of-account part.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
