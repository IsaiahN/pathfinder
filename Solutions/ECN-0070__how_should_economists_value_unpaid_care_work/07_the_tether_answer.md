# The Tether's answer on valuing unpaid care work

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The gap between what the national accounts count and what households actually produce persists after decades of valuation work. The field answers with a single ratio (X per cent of GDP) computed from a wage proxy. That answer pools the slots whose signal is needed, treats a convention as a measurement, and uses an instrument that is blind to the very undervaluation it is meant to correct. The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep the residual per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Publish time and distribution by household, activity and sex before money. The X per cent figure is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
2. **Unbundle the union.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Separate the factual verdict (what households produce and who does it), the convention (unit of account and production boundary), and the instrument question (which proxy, blind on which axes). Fig 2 :39-41: agreement is disqualifying for the verdict and efficient for the convention.
3. **The accounts cannot score themselves.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Zero-by-boundary and X-per-cent-of-GDP are both self-scores. The ground is time-use measurement and recipient outcomes, *"a measurement"* and *"a patient outcome"* named in Fig 2 :29.
4. **Wage proxies are instruments, not anchors.** Fig 2 :30: *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :37-38: instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Replacement cost reproduces feminized low pay; opportunity cost varies with caregiver socioeconomic status; sex-disaggregated mean wages lower the estimate because women do more care at lower wages. Name those axes next to every number.
5. **Care is agent-and-habitat and ordered.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* An hour's value depends on the relation and its place in a sequence, not on the hour alone.
6. **Measure the round trip.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Moving a wage rate or conversion across countries and survey rounds loses information; report R_T, do not assume it is zero.
7. **Stop at the shape.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* No single monetary number is derived here. That is a `gap`, not an omission.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that unpaid care has no value, and not that its exclusion from GDP is correct. The framework describes the measurement contact; it does not rank the moral worth of care.
- Not that there is one correct valuation method. The methods are isomers (same atoms, different bond, different substance, `06_isomers_and_order.md`), not dialectical variants of one number.
- Not that care should simply be added to GDP. Adding it by convention is the move that lets a convention decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that the 2025 SNA satellite-account guidance is a derivation. It is a provenanced convention from the statistics frame, recorded as such.
- Not grounded. No time-use or outcome measurement settles `left(R,φ)` in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A better instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Anchor test:** does a wage proxy stay invariant across a documented change in the gender pay gap and occupational segregation? If yes, it is an anchor on that axis; if no, it is blind (F4).
- **Visibility test:** can an instrument record passive, on-call and relational care, so that what was residual becomes readable without a new residual appearing elsewhere (F5)?
- **Outcome test:** do measured recipient outcomes co-vary with the time and quality of care in a way that discriminates among the competing wage bonds? That is the "patient outcome" anchor of Fig 2 :29.
- **Round-trip test:** what is R_T when a method moves between two countries or survey rounds (F7)?
- **Standing framework refuters:** derive one of the six substrate terms from the other five; admit a state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused; show closure is not idempotent.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The time-use categories and the production boundary are *"a human's careful guess"* at what to compress, not nature's own list; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another instrumentarium could carve the day differently. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a single "correct" value for unpaid care work, and it does not claim the accounts are wrong for a moral reason. It says the measurement gap must be kept per household and activity, because an average throws away the signal that says where to look; that the factual verdict about what households produce must be separated from the convention about the unit of account, because agreement is the product for one and disqualifying for the other; that wage proxies are instruments blind to the gender pay gap they are meant to correct, not anchors; that care is a relation in a habitat and an ordered sequence, not a stock of hours; and that any transfer of a method across countries must report its round-trip gap. What it offers is the shape any acceptable valuation must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
