# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four properties in the shape claim below are checked for internal consistency. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one measurement claim before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question in economic measurement, and I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** unpaid care work and household production leave a gap between what the national accounts count and what households actually produce. That gap should be kept indexed by household, activity type and sex, rather than folded into one GDP-equivalent ratio. The reason is structural: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (a figure line I will quote in full at stage 2). The usual answer, "unpaid work is X per cent of GDP", is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what the answer is.
>
> The output has four parts. (1) Keep the residual per slot. (2) Separate the factual claim about what households produce from the unit-of-account convention, because agreement is the product for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict. (3) Name the axes on which each valuation instrument is blind: replacement cost tends to reproduce the low pay of feminized occupations, opportunity cost varies with the caregiver's socioeconomic status, and neither sees on-call or relational care. (4) Measure the loss when a valuation method is moved from one country or survey round to another, instead of assuming the transfer is lossless.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that there is one correct monetary number; that unpaid care should simply be added to GDP; that the framework names which wage rate is right; or that this derivation is `grounded`. No measurement settles it here. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute the claim:**
> 1. a wage proxy whose output is unchanged across a documented change in the gender pay gap and occupational segregation;
> 2. a single aggregate statistic that predicts, prospectively and per named household or activity, which one will lose contact with the ground;
> 3. one lever that resolves the factual question, the convention and the instrument question with no categorically different residue left over;
> 4. a valuation settled by the accounts' own metric with no external time-use or outcome measurement;
> 5. a transfer of a method between two contexts whose round-trip gap is measured and zero.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the sources, with the figure text as the authority rather than my summary of it.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the eight-step loop is public. Run it on a measurement problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 - frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | the design-instrument rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Convention and verdict separated | Fig 2 :39-41: correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention. |
| No single number asserted | Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. |
| Blind axes named | Fig 2 :37-38 - instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. |
| No request for agreement or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the method as a generator, not a conclusion | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Each user writes their own refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the sought-for shape is not a term it holds. |

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** send this email with the shape claim and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three source links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground, mode and refuter.
