## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about valuing unpaid care work and household production in employment and output statistics. Option A: publish one monetary equivalent and fold it into GDP. Option B: keep the measurement residual per slot, separate the factual verdict from the unit-of-account convention, treat wage proxies as instruments with named blind axes, and measure the round-trip gap on transfer. What each leads to: A produces a single aggregate that pools the signal, lets a convention decide an outcome, and prices care with the pay gap it is meant to correct; B produces a checkable shape with five written refuters and an open number until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the accounts count and what households actually produce, which persists under decades of valuation effort. Arity: three, an activity, a measurement frame and a unit of account, indexed by household, activity, sex, country and survey round. Symmetry: asymmetric; caregiver and recipient, market and household are not interchangeable, and the observed division of labour is strongly gendered. Scale: the household care sequence, the national accounts with *"something that ranks what they produce"* (Fig 4 :5), and the channel across rounds and over a life course. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the measurement frame.** Actors: statistical offices, the SNA production boundary, GDP, labour force surveys. Currency: monetary aggregates and employment counts. Anchor: yes, time-use measurement, *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"* (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **Domain 2, the household care habitat.** Actors: caregivers, recipients, activities, conditions including "on call" time, distance to water, illness. Currency: time, effort, use-value, recipient outcomes. Anchor: yes, measured minutes and a patient outcome (Fig 2 :29). Admitted. The habitat is *"enumerated, never composed"* (Fig 11 :43).
- **Domain 3, the valuation instruments.** Actors: replacement wage, opportunity wage, output price, productivity adjustment. Currency: money. Anchor: no independent anchor. A market wage moves when pushed (Fig 2 :30) and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :37-38). Not a domain; an instrument inside Domain 1.
- **Domain 4, the convention frame.** Actors: UN SNA revisions, SDG 5.4.1 reporting, agencies, ministries. Currency: agreed meaning, budget, decisions. Anchor: consequences in the world, but the object is partly a convention (Fig 2 :41). Admitted as a convention domain; its verdicts are not pooled with Domains 1 and 2.
- **Excluded X1, intrinsic worth as a free-floating property.** No anchor independent of the valuer's frame (Fig 11 :48). Not a domain yet. **Excluded X2, women's natural duty to care.** No anchor independent of measured behaviour; fails independence (Fig 2 :31).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (can the survey see the activity?) to vocabulary (does the classification have terms for what a household affords?) to objective (state the convention) to planning (choose a method) to learn and carry. The break is usually at vocabulary/objective (Fig 3 :24); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's physical six-term set to factors of production only fit by renaming time as energy and a relation as a gradient. Re-census sent Fig 13 to the honest-stop role only. No strain remains after re-census (see `11_audit.md`).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single number does not. Economists should keep the residual indexed by household, activity and sex; unbundle the factual verdict from the unit-of-account convention from the instrument question; treat time-use measurement and recipient outcomes as the anchors and wage proxies as blind instruments; enumerate the care habitat before pricing an hour; and measure the round-trip gap on every cross-context transfer. The methods are isomers over the same atoms, not rival spellings of one value (Fig 12 :117). The "correct" number is a `gap`: the framework returns a shape and the convention part has no ground to settle it. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The corrected packet is mode-labeled, states five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single persuasive ratio instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective moves are removable without touching the derivation: the aggregate ratio is replaced by per-slot time and distribution, the wage proxy is labeled an instrument with named blind axes, and the convention is declared. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already do this. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a published shape can be superseded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A wage proxy invariant across a documented change in the gender pay gap (F4), an instrument that reads passive and relational care with no new residual (F5), recipient outcomes that discriminate among the wage bonds (outcome test), or a cross-context transfer whose R_T is measured and zero (F7) would move specific findings. A derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or a failure of idempotence, would break the framework itself. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
