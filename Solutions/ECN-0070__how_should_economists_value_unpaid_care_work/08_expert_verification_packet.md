# Expert verification packet - the Tether on valuing unpaid care work

**What this is:** a claim in a form a statistician, care economist or feminist economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to have paid for the shape. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The measurement gap between what national accounts count and what households produce should be kept indexed by household, activity and sex, never folded into one GDP-equivalent ratio. The question is a union of three things that need different tests: a factual verdict about productive activity, a unit-of-account convention, and an instrument question about wage proxies. Agreement is the product for the convention and disqualifying for the verdict. Wage proxies are instruments, not anchors: replacement cost tends to reproduce the low pay of feminized occupations, opportunity cost varies with the caregiver's socioeconomic status, and neither records on-call or relational care. Care value is agent-and-habitat and ordered, and any transfer of a valuation method across contexts carries a round-trip gap that must be measured. The framework returns this shape and five refuters; it does not return a single number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot; a single aggregate is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **Refuter:** produce a single aggregate statistic that predicts, prospectively and per named household or activity, which slot will lose contact with the ground. If it exists, C1 is false.

### C2 - statistics-facing, checkable against the anchor rule and the valuation literature

- **Statement:** a market wage is an instrument with named blind axes, not an anchor; the anchor is time-use measurement and recipient outcomes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :30 - *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :37-38 - *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they"* / *"do not measure."*
- **What to check:** Jones, Ruiz and Undurraga (2026, *Oxford Review of Economic Policy*) write that *"replacement cost risks replicating the low pay typical of feminized occupations, while opportunity cost varies by the socioeconomic status of caregivers."* Budlender (2004) shows that sex-disaggregated mean wages *"lowers the overall estimated value of unpaid work"* because women do more of it at lower average wages. Does the recommended wage proxy move on that axis?
- **Refuter:** a wage proxy demonstrated invariant across a documented change in the gender pay gap and occupational segregation while still tracking the care activity. If one exists, C2 is false.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a measurement consequence

- **Statement:** the union must be unbundled, and a convention must not decide the outcome; cross-context transfer has a measurable gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 10 :47 - *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; Fig 4 :30 - *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **What to check:** the 2025 SNA revision *"encourages satellite (or thematic) accounts on unpaid work... without altering the core production boundary"* (OECD 2025). Is that a visible convention or a hidden decision? And when a wage rate is exported from one country to another, is the loss measured?
- **Refuter:** one lever that resolves the factual verdict, the convention and the instrument question with no categorically different residue; or a transfer whose round-trip gap is measured and zero. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a monetary number, and not "add X per cent to GDP." That would let a convention decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that care lacks worth, and not that its exclusion from GDP is morally correct.
- Not that one valuation method is the right one. The methods are isomers: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"* (Fig 12 :117).
- Not a derivation of the SNA boundary or of the 2025 revision. Those are provenanced conventions from the statistics frame.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that this simulated derivation can settle the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Anchor test:** does a wage proxy stay invariant across a documented change in the gender pay gap and occupational segregation?
2. **Visibility test:** can an instrument record passive, on-call and relational care, with no new residual appearing elsewhere?
3. **Outcome test:** do measured recipient outcomes discriminate among the competing wage bonds?
4. **Round-trip test:** what is R_T when a method moves between two countries or survey rounds?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 2 :29, :30, :37-38; Fig 9 :57, :60; Fig 10 :47; Fig 4 :30 - eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: does the wage proxy they use move with the pay gap it is meant to correct?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate among the competing sources of a wage reading, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
