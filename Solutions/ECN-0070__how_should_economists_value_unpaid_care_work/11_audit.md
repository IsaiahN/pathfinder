## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 2 :29, :30, :37-38 (anchor and blind instruments), Fig 9 :54-57, :60 (union and filters), Fig 11 :39, :48, :49 (habitat and unseen residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: keep R per slot, split convention from verdict, treat wages as blind instruments, measure the transfer gap, stop at the shape.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the field's concrete claim: unpaid care is X per cent of GDP, value it at a replacement or opportunity wage, add it to a satellite account.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** collapses into one headline percentage (Fig 1 :22);
- the **convention/verdict split** collapses when the wage convention silently sets the factual magnitude (Fig 2 :41 read against Fig 2 :40; Fig 10 :47);
- the **blind-axis disclosure** disappears when the ratio is quoted without its wage source (Fig 2 :37-38);
- the **unseen residual** is treated as zero when only active care is recorded (Fig 11 :48).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the need for a non-moving anchor and a per-slot measurement rather than an aggregate self-score. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
