## The residual in effect terms

A sequence distinction held in a hypothetical pre-RNA polymer P becomes a sequence distinction in RNA, and RNA behaviour tracks it. What must be true of P for that handoff to be possible and to matter? Option A names a polymer (PNA, TNA, GNA) and asserts priority. Option B derives the necessary property set, keeps verdicts per domain, refuses to crown a molecule, and marks priority as a gap. This folder takes B. A leads to a name that no available instrument settles and to a standing list the figures refuse; B leads to a checkable condition set plus a discriminator that would close the gap.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes them; they are deliberately not reproduced into this folder.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that a pre-RNA polymer existed.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that PNA, TNA, GNA or any other named polymer was the actual first genetic polymer. Candidate names enter as provenanced bindings only, never as a figure output.

Three figure reasons, stated plainly:

1. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The figures return a shape.
2. Fig 11 :51 says *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A condition set is allowed; a standing list of winning molecules is not.
3. Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for agreement would manufacture the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
