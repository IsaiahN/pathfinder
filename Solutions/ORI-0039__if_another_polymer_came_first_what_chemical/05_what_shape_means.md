## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0039 a **shape** is the narrowed set of conditions a pre-RNA polymer would have to satisfy, statable before anyone can name the polymer. Seven conditions (C1 to C7 in `01`) are a shape. "PNA" is a name. "TNA came first" is a historical claim, which is a third thing again and is a gap here.

The distinction matters because the question's own wording, "what chemical properties would it need", sounds like a request for a list of properties and is easily converted into a request for a molecule. The figures permit the first and refuse the second.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the ordinary terms "carry", "copy", "read", "persist" and narrow until the figure vocabulary of bond, cycle, catalyst, functional group, closure, import and settlement is available. The narrowing is what produced C1 to C7.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So C1 is stated as "a typed linkage with distinguishable states", not as "a 2-prime to 5-prime phosphodiester bond", until chemistry supplies the latter as a provenanced binding.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks for a discriminator before it offers the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the shape stops not when a polymer is found but when the condition set generates its own refuters.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A chemistry paper can supply the name "TNA" as a candidate occupant of a role. It cannot supply the figure's permission to call TNA the first polymer.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A candidate polymer selected because it fits C1 to C7 is a choice made visible, not a discovery forced by the figures.

## 4. Why a name from a lab reading is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a laboratory transfer from PNA to RNA is a reading, not a source attribution. Several histories could produce the same reading: a real pre-RNA handoff, an engineered polymerase doing work nature never had, or a convergent RNA-only route. Calling the reader "PNA world" names a source off a projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 9 :32 again: a kind of thing, never the answer itself.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This folder's output cannot validate itself either.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid the chemistry entirely? ANSWERED with a correction

**No.** The exact phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in the library.

The correct reading for this question:

- The conditions (bond, cycle, catalyst, functional group, closure, import, settlement) are figure-held structures. Naming them is legitimate derivation.
- Chemistry names such as PNA, TNA, GNA, homo-DNA and 4-prime-thioDNA are bindings from the chemistry frame onto those roles. Naming one as a *candidate occupant* is provenance and is allowed, provided the habitat and the source are recorded. It does not move the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- Naming one as *the actual first polymer* requires a ground reading (chronology or an equivalent discriminator) that does not exist here. That claim is a `gap`, not a derivation and not a forbidden name.

**Correction to my own first pass, kept visible.** I initially read the rule as "the framework may never name a molecule" and nearly removed every chemistry name from the folder. That was an overstatement. The narrowed form above is now used in `01` section 7, `06` and `09`. The metamerism objection from section 4 is **not** retracted: the reason we do not crown a polymer is the lack of a source-discriminating instrument, not a ban on naming held roles.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous origins-of-life researchers who agree that a pre-RNA world existed is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is for a budget to try the discriminator and for a verdict on the condition set.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a chemistry experiment and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
