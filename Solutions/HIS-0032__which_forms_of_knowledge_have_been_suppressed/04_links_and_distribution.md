## Relevant wider literature

The six world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` span philosophy of science, social theory, history of science, bioethics, cognitive science and mathematics. They are deliberately low-overlap: no two of them quote the same review or the same dataset. Their agreement with the figure verdict is corroboration only, never the verdict itself. The four framework essays share an author with the figures, so their overlap is declared as high and their agreement carries little independent weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the shape claim and the refuter. The figures and the architecture come after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; **Fig 7 :35** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 offers the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer (the honesty constraint)

Stage 1 is the claim plus refuter. Stage 2 is opt-in sources. Stage 3 is the method. This is machine-simulated work, as the RSI essay makes explicit: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Therefore:

- Do not ask anyone to trust the framework. Ask them to refute the shape claim.
- Do not present the named historical cases as figure-derived. They are world illustrations with zero verdict weight.
- Do not paste this derivation into a journal submission that prohibits AI-produced content. Feminist Studies is one such venue; check each venue's policy.
- If a reader returns a real refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
