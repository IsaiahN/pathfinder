## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a roster

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the set of constraints that any correct answer about suppressed knowledge must satisfy, stated before the names are available. It is a typology by mechanism: union of three conditions, per-slot residuals, provenance as the discriminator, closure difference as the source of exclusion, contact as the carrier of methods. A roster of named traditions would be a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* The list of forms of suppressed knowledge is read off the habitat, not composed from a theory.

This is why the residual in `01` is stated in effect terms before any figure line is chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question that means archival, legal, oral or experimental evidence that settles a particular slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* An official history that records a result cannot thereby tell you who produced it. It was a spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the analogous case in that essay. The second essay's own provenance practice is the same rule: *"I could prove I had not copied her because my paper trail was different, and my vocabulary was different."*

## 4. Why the roster is the specific forbidden error for this question

Naming a set of suppressed traditions as "the answer" commits a metamerism error. Several different histories (convergent independent derivation, adopted import with credit, adopted import without credit) can produce the same artifact. The artifact does not determine the source. What is owed is a better discriminator (provenance), not a name read off a projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds the social version: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."* A consensus history is a clock sync, not a bearing on the ground. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

An earlier draft of this file read "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any contributor or tradition. That overstates the rule.

**Correction:** the canonical phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. The excluded knowledge in this question is not a missing primitive; it is held in a different frame (Fig 6 :23) and was never imported with its provenance. So naming Franklin, Lacks or ethnomathematics is not forbidden by the shape rule. What is forbidden is presenting those names as figure-derived and giving them verdict weight. They are secondary illustrations, and the figures decide the mechanism.

Both claims are now recorded: the metamerism point in §3 stands (the artifact does not determine its source), and the scope of the shape rule is narrowed to missing primitives. Neither is silently erased.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of prestigious historians who agree that knowledge was suppressed is the named collapse, not corroboration.

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
