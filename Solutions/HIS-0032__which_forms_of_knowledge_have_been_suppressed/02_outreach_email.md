> 2. Appropriation is a provenance failure, not a content failure. A derived result and a taken result look identical in the artifact. What is missing is the record of the route, not a new fact.
> 3. Exclusion is a closure effect. The knowledge was not absent from the world; the institutional frame had no contact with a frame that held it.
> 4. Tacit methods, routes and correction loops do not survive when only the product is archived. Adding a name is not the same as recovering a method.
>
