# Expert verification packet: the Tether on suppressed, appropriated and excluded knowledge

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian of science, archivist or epistemologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The three verbs in the question are not three rosters. They are overlapping conditions over a union, each needing its own loop. An official history can be globally coherent while single contributor slots stay live; the global aggregate hides the signal. Appropriation is a provenance failure rather than a content failure, because a derived result and a taken result look identical in the artifact and only the lineage separates them. Exclusion is a closure effect: the knowledge was in the world, but the archive had no contact with the frame that held it, and importing it adds access rather than new atoms. Tacit methods and routes do not travel as recordings. The framework returns this shape, not a roster of names; specific cases are secondary illustrations with zero verdict weight.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9
- **Statement:** the live residual is per slot, and the three conditions form a union whose intersections need their own loops; aggregating or bundling loses the signal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :56 *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*
- **Refuter:** show that the intersections are empty so one lever suffices, or show a case where the slot information is fully recoverable from the global aggregate with no loss.

### C2 - history-facing; checkable by a historian against a known case
- **Statement:** in an appropriation case, the artifact's contents do not determine its source; the discriminator is provenance. Adding a credit line clears the provenance condition but not necessarily the exclusion condition, because the two sit in different conditions of the union.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."* Fig 13 :128 *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* Fig 10 :62 *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."*
- **What to check:** take one case you know well. Did the contents alone settle who produced the result, or did that require a separate record (lab notebook, letter, permission, dated draft)? Did restoring credit also restore the contributor's standing to count as a knower, or only add a footnote?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the provenance record survives intact yet the contents still look identical, so lineage was not the discriminator. Or show that adding credit fully restored standing with no further mechanism.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation
- **Statement:** exclusion is a closure effect, and the remedy is an import with a source gate from a frame whose closure differs; an import gives the archive access to an atom, it does not add one to the world.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."*
- **Refuter:** show a frame composing knowledge outside its closure by composition alone, with no contact with a differing frame and no import. Or show that the import added a new primitive to the world rather than access to one already held elsewhere.

## 5. What would settle it

A provenance-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*

1. **Slot test:** does an archive show a near-zero global residual with live per-slot residuals, with slot information lost in aggregation?
2. **Provenance test:** for a claimed appropriation, does the artifact fail to determine the route while a separate record settles it?
3. **Closure test:** is the knowledge reachable only through contact with a differing frame, with a source gate and a shadow test?
4. **Contact test:** does the knowledge fail to travel as text but travel as a method through practice?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

2. Pick one case you know. Apply the slot test and the provenance test to it.

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
