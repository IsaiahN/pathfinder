## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` §11 and `07`: three overlapping conditions; suppression as averaging; appropriation as provenance loss; exclusion as closure effect; methods not recordings; shape not roster.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a proposed list of suppressed traditions, or a claim that adding names repairs the canon.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **closure requirement**: adding a name to the archive is not import from a differing closure and adds no access to the frame that held the knowledge (Fig 6 :23 and :25).

One thing survives cleanly: an official history can be globally coherent while individual slots stay live, and the signal is lost by aggregation. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
