## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks which forms of knowledge have been suppressed, appropriated, or excluded from official histories of science. Stated as an effect rather than a topic:

> The official record predicts a nearly complete account of science, so its global residual looks near zero. Yet particular slots stay live: a named person's data used without credit, a craft that worked but never entered the archive, a body of knowledge held in a closure the archive never contacted, a correction that could be stated but never entered the canon. Which forms occupy those live slots?

This is already an effect description, so no frame is named before the census. The global near-zero with live slots is a legal state under Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* The failure mode is treating the smooth global narrative as if it settled the slots. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

**R by arity, symmetry, scale:**

- **Arity:** three conditions (suppressed, appropriated, excluded). They are a **union**, not a partition. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single historical case can satisfy two or all three at once.
- **Symmetry:** ordered, not symmetric. Suppression blocks entry, appropriation removes provenance while keeping contents, exclusion changes what counts as knowledge. These are different operators. Refusal is involved (negation), and Operators table :58 says *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* A canon with no ¬ can add names and cannot carry the correction.
- **Scale:** recursive. Individual contributor slot, institutional closure (journal, society, citation index), civilizational archive. Fig 13 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Each slot at one level becomes an atom at the next.

## 3. Findings

Each finding states mode, verbatim figure line, a refuter, and a triangulation status. `triangulated` means two figures with low overlap, not two web pages.

### F1 - The three verbs are a union, not three lists (`general`)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
Fig 9 :56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*

A case can be suppressed (kept out), appropriated (taken without provenance) and excluded (framed as not counting) at once. Treating them as three independent rosters is the bundled-problem error: a single lever such as "add more names to the canon" clears only the suppression-only region and leaves the intersections standing.

- **Refuter:** show that every historical case falls in exactly one of the three conditions, so the intersections are empty and no second-order loop is owed.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union rule and Fig 1 per-slot rule are different figures with low overlap: both refuse to merge distinct conditions into one aggregate). World corroboration: the SEP entry on feminist epistemology lists six distinct mechanisms of disadvantage, which are not mutually exclusive.

### F2 - Suppression: the global near-zero with live slots (`specified`)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*

An official history can be globally coherent and still be wrong at particular slots. The archive reports R near zero because it averages across contributors, traditions and practices. The live residual is the individual slot: the uncredited data, the unrecorded craft, the tradition held outside the archive. This is why "the canon is complete" and "this contributor was erased" are not contradictory.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the slot information is fully recoverable from the global aggregate with no loss, so aggregation did not hide a live signal.
- **Status:** single-source (Fig 1). World corroboration: SEP reports that in some contexts women are *"not allowed to speak, or their questions, comments, and challenges are ignored, interrupted, and systematically distorted, or they aren't accepted as experts."* Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).

### F3 - Appropriation is provenance erasure, not content transfer (`specified`)

Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*
Fig 13 :127: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."*
Fig 13 :128: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*
Fig 13 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."*
Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*

This is the central finding of the audit. When knowledge is appropriated, the artifact travels and the lineage does not. Because the contents are identical under convergent derivation and adopted import (Fig 8 :50), a reader of the official history cannot tell from the artifact alone who produced it. The missing thing is not a new fact; it is the record of the route. Fig 10 :62: *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."* Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately."*

The framework author's own provenance practice is an illustration: *"I could prove I had not copied her because my paper trail was different, and my vocabulary was different."* That is the same test applied to a claimed independent derivation.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a case of appropriation in which the provenance record survives intact and the contents still look identical, so the lineage was not the discriminating thing. Or show a composed term whose route is recoverable from the artifact alone.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 8 surplus/provenance and Fig 13 lineage-as-term are different figures with low overlap). World corroboration: Rosalind Franklin's Photo 51 was shown to Watson without her permission, and the 1953 Nature model carried only a footnote acknowledging *"having been stimulated by a general knowledge of Franklin and Wilkins' 'unpublished' contribution."*

### F4 - Exclusion is a closure effect (`specified`)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (import).
Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*
Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* and :26 *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*

The excluded knowledge was not absent from the world. The institutional frame lacked contact with a frame that held it. Import into the archive adds no atom to the world; it gives the archive access to one. This is why the right verb for the remedy is not "discover" but "import with provenance." Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

- **Refuter:** show a frame composing an atom that is outside its closure by composition alone, with no contact with a differing frame and no import (breaks Fig 6 :23).
- **Status:** single-source (Fig 6). World corroboration: ethnomathematics *"mainly focuses on addressing Eurocentrism by countering the common belief that most worthwhile mathematics known and used today was developed in the Western world."* This is a frame-closure claim, not a claim that the mathematics was newly created.

### F5 - The archive is not the anchor; it updates on its own frames (`specified`)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
Fig 2 :47: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."*
Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."*
Fig 10 :85: *"Who holds the seat is not chosen. The position selects the occupant."*

Official histories are written by the frames whose work they grade. The published record therefore moves when those frames move, so it is not an anchor. The ground is what actually happened and what worked, which does not consult the archive. The institutional actors are not villains; they occupy a position that cannot own the reading by Fig 10 :86.

- **Refuter:** exhibit an official record whose entries do not change when the editors, reviewers and citation practices that authored it change, so the record is genuinely pinned.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 anchor/updating and Fig 10 seat position are different figures with low overlap). World corroboration: standpoint theory holds that the views of groups with more social power are validated more than those in marginalized groups; this corroborates the direction of the drift but is not a figure verdict.

### F6 - Exclusion happens by narrow habitat scoping (`specified`)

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
Fig 11 :52 to :53: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

An archive that lists only direct contributors misses the cascade: the technicians, translators, funders, instruments, materials and communities two or three steps out. Valence is relative to the residual, so a person or practice that is a benefactor to one history is a malfactor to another; there is no standing list of "important contributors" that survives a change of question.

- **Refuter:** show a complete account of a historical episode obtainable from first-order direct contacts only, with no second- or third-order actor changing any slot.
- **Status:** single-source (Fig 11). World corroboration: the Lacks family learned that HeLa material was still in use only in 1975 through a chance dinner-party conversation, decades after the research, which is exactly a third-order effect beyond direct contact.

### F7 - Tacit knowledge cannot be carried as a recording (`specified`)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

A form of knowledge that lives in a route, an apprenticeship or a correction loop is lost when only its product is archived. Adding a carbon copy of the artifact to the canon does not recover the method. This is one reason "include more names" is not sufficient for the forms of knowledge in question.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where a tacit method was fully recovered from a written artifact alone, with no contact with a practitioner and no method carried across.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 bond/route and Fig 4 method/scale membrane are different figures with low overlap). World corroboration: Polanyi *"we can know more than we can tell,"* and the tacit-knowledge literature notes that *"Explicit knowledge can be aggregated at a single location, stored in objective forms, and appropriated without the participation of the knowing subject."*

### F8 - Consensus cannot corroborate a history (`general`)

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 7 :12 to :13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

A chain of official histories that cite one another is a mirror chain. More agreement inside it is motion, not progress. Corroboration requires a frame whose closure differs, ranged separately against the ground (Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).

- **Refuter:** exhibit two histories that reach the same slot claim while drawing on genuinely non-overlapping evidence and each ranged against an independent ground, so their agreement is not the pool talking.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 alignment and Fig 7 room chain are different figures with low overlap). World corroboration: *"You may all agree and be wrong,"* from the RSI essay.

### F9 - The answer is a shape, not a roster of names (`general`)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*
Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The figures govern the mechanisms that produce live slots. They do not name specific suppressed traditions, and they cannot: naming a primitive would mean already holding it. The honest answer to "which forms" is therefore a typology by mechanism, with the ground-level instances supplied by historical research as illustrations. Absence from the archive is not proof that nothing was there.

- **Refuter:** produce a figure line that names a specific historical tradition or contributor as suppressed, so the roster is figure-derived rather than ground-researched.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 kind-not-answer and Fig 13 unreachable-as-verdict are different figures with low overlap). Note: this is a limit finding, not a silence.

### F10 - Adding names is not the same as refusing the exclusion (`specified`)

Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*

A canon that only adds entries can be corrected in content and cannot carry the correction. Refusing the mechanism that produced the live slot is a negation (¬), not a new name. Fig 1 :19 still applies: the refusal must be indexed to the slot, not averaged into a general statement that the canon has biases.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a canon that fully refuses an exclusion mechanism by adding names alone, with no term that says the earlier standing-to-compose was wrong.
- **Status:** triangulated (Operators table unary ¬ and Fig 5 fifth gap are different figures/tables with low overlap).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The typology above is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Every historical case falls in exactly one of suppression, appropriation and exclusion, so the intersections are empty and Fig 9 :54 does not apply.
2. A case exists in which slot information is fully recoverable from a global aggregate with no loss (breaks Fig 1 :19 and :22).
3. A case of appropriation exists in which the provenance record survives intact yet the contents still look identical, so lineage is not the discriminator (breaks Fig 8 :50 and Fig 13 :127).
4. A frame composes an atom outside its closure without import or contact with a differing frame (breaks Fig 6 :23).
5. Two official histories reach the same slot claim from genuinely non-overlapping evidence, each ranged against an independent ground (breaks F8; would not refute the figure line but would change its application here).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn and the census redone. Until one is run, none of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a list of wrongs (Fig 9 :27 to :28).
2. Treat "suppressed", "appropriated" and "excluded" as three loops over a union, and search the intersections separately (Fig 9 :54 to :58).
3. For any claimed recovery, require provenance: where did the atom come from, what gap does it explain, and what is the record of the route (Fig 6 source gate, Fig 8 :50, Fig 10 :62).
4. Range the recovered claim against a ground outside the archive, not against more archival agreement (Fig 2 :35, :45).
5. Carry methods and head starts, not recordings (Fig 4 :22 to :25). A token mention of an uncredited contributor is a recording.
6. Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). A tier or a journal's prestige is a budget filter, never a witness that the history is correct.
