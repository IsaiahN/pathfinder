## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that certain forms of knowledge have been suppressed, appropriated or excluded from official histories of science. Option A: treat the three verbs as three rosters of names and answer by listing cases. Option B: treat them as a union of overlapping conditions and answer with a shape and a typology by mechanism, with named cases as zero-weight illustrations. What each leads to: A produces a list that looks decisive but cannot discriminate a derived result from a taken one, and it spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B produces checkable mechanisms, pre-registered refuters, and an open roster until a ground settles each slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the official record (global residual near zero) and the ground-level record of what was actually known and done (live contributor slots). Arity: three overlapping conditions. Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric; suppression blocks entry, appropriation removes provenance, exclusion changes what counts. Scale: contributor slot, institutional closure, civilizational archive; recursive under Fig 13 :103.
- **Domain 1, the institutional archive.** Actors: editors, historians, reviewers, citation indexes, societies. Currency: entries, citations, credibility. Anchor: claimed anchor is the published record, but it is authored by the frames it grades, so it is defective; Fig 2 :47 *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."* This is a frame.
- **Domain 2, the ground of practice.** Actors: practitioners, instruments, experiments, materials. Currency: settlement, does it work. Anchor: a real anchor that does not update on what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). This is the ground.
- **Domain 3, excluded frames.** Actors: craftspeople, indigenous and colonized knowledge holders, uncredited researchers, oral and apprentice traditions. Currency: tacit methods, provenance, routes. Anchor: their own ground contact; a closure that differs from Domain 1 (Fig 6 :23). Import source.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: this derivation, outside readers, second seat. Currency: residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Partition or union:** union. The three conditions overlap (Fig 9 :54) and Domain 4 overlays the others as a checking arrangement. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency:** perceive the slots (Fig 1), get vocabulary by enumerating the habitat (Fig 11), state the objective (settled slot plus recovered route), plan the import (Fig 6), carry the method (Fig 4). Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

Strain test applied: Fig 6 (horizontal frame-to-frame import) and Fig 4 (vertical scale crossing) are different axes, so no strain; see `01` §7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a roster does not. The three verbs are overlapping conditions of one union. Suppression is per-slot information lost to aggregation; appropriation is the loss of a route whose contents are indistinguishable from an independently derived result; exclusion is a frame lacking contact with another frame that holds the knowledge; tacit methods are lost when only products are archived. The archive is not the anchor, and consensus inside it cannot settle a slot. The framework returns kinds of things and discriminating tests, not names. Named cases are world illustrations with zero verdict weight. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result from a simulated derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a roster and a theory before the shape claim, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism claims can be corrected without withdrawing the whole typology, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already strip the zero-weight names from the claim. No primitive must be withdrawn, and a sent packet can be superseded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An archival provenance record that settles a route despite identical contents; a case with empty intersections between the three conditions; a slot fully recoverable from the global aggregate; a frame composing knowledge outside its closure without import; a tacit method recovered from text alone with no contact. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
