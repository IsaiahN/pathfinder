## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that knowledge was suppressed.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No list of suppressed traditions presented as a figure verdict. Figures return a shape, not a name.

Reason, from the figures: Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
