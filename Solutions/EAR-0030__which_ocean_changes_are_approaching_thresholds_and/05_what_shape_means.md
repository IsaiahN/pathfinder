## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

*The Library of Babel* states it in the searcher's terms: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The same essay warns that "the problem is you", meaning the searcher's vocabulary, not the library, is the obstacle. It also names a failure mode that is exactly this question's framing: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."

For EAR-0030 a **shape** is therefore:

> A per-slot test that distinguishes a comparison against a critical value plus a closing feedback from a gradient spent over time, and that states what instrument would move the slot.

It is not: a ranked list of ocean changes. "Which ocean change is closest" is a name-level question. The framework gives the form the answer must have, then stops.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: begin with the ordinary words "threshold" and "trend", then refine them into "comparison bond", "closing cycle", "gradient over time", "flat residual".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the shape is described as *what you would observe*: the error stops shrinking, variance rises, the loop closes. Not as a term only this framework can read.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email in `02` leads with observable expectations.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." For this question the finish line is the point where the per-slot instrument returns a distance to threshold. Until then the loop stops honestly at `candidate`.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The shape test is such a guess, made visible. It does not become nature's own classification by being stated.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the precise error to avoid: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An observed weakening trend can have several sources, natural variability, gradual forcing, or an approaching bifurcation. A reading of the trend "cannot comment on the source." The essay continues: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." For this question, the better instrument is the per-slot, physics-based observable, not another argument about whether AMOC is "close".

## 4. Why the provisional world map is not a name

The map in `01` section F11 names AMOC, ice sheets, reefs and others. That is legitimate only because it is explicitly a `specified` reading of world sources, not a framework output. The distinction matters:

- The framework returns the shape: comparison plus feedback, versus gradient over time, with a flat-residual diagnostic.
- The world literature returns provisional slot assignments, contested, with overlapping evidence pools.
- Conflating the two is the metamerism error: naming a source from a projection that several sources could produce.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A map produced by this simulated seat cannot validate its own slot assignments.

## 5. Correction to my own reading

First draft of this section treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding any mention of a named ocean change in the solution. That was too strong. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing: what an absent primitive would have to look like. Here the classes are not missing primitives; they are bond patterns the figures already define. Naming an ocean change as a `candidate` slot is a binding from the world frame onto those patterns, provenanced as such. What remains forbidden is presenting that binding as a figure-grounded verdict or as `grounded`. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the corresponding structural correction.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A panel of famous names saying AMOC is close to tipping gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the classification and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
