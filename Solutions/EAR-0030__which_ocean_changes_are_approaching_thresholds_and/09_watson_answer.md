## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the naming is owed to the ground. Classify per slot, never by a global ocean mean. A threshold is a comparison between a control value and a critical value plus a closing feedback; a continuing trend is a gradient spent over time whose residual shrinks under observation. The two classes form a union, so the change that is both weakening and approaching a threshold is a specific compound with its own loop. The diagnostic is a residual that persists and does not trend down. What is not settled is which named ocean change sits where; the provisional map is a contested world reading, `specified` and `single-source` per row, never a figure result. This is an ungrounded `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named ranking instead of a shape. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot rule and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The provisional map can be revised slot by slot without touching the shape. A sent ranking can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument showing that the prediction error shrinks for a named change moves it toward the trend class; one showing a flat error plus an identified signed feedback past a critical value moves it toward the threshold class. A global mean shown to predict a basin's regime shift better than the basin's own residual would break the per-slot rule. A demonstration that the two conditions are mutually exclusive for every ocean element would break the union reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
