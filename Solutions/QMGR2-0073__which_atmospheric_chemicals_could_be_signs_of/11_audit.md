I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Symmetry: the feature is unchanged when the source is swapped, so the source is not in its contents. Scale: molecular line, planetary context, temporal replenishment.
- **Domain 1, observation.** Actors: telescope, spectrograph, retrieval. Currency: spectral depth. Anchor: the measured spectrum. This is perception and vocabulary for composition, not for provenance.
- **Domain 2, abiotic chemistry.** Actors: photolysis, volcanism, impacts, water-rock reactions, crust redox, stellar UV. Currency: fluxes and sinks. Anchor: laboratory rates and validated models. Question: can X be made here without life?

- **Domain 5, outside analogue.** Actors: screening test, confirmatory test, gold standard. Currency: likelihood ratios. Anchor: biopsy. Question: does a positive reading settle a cause?

Order by dependency: detect the feature, enumerate the routes, state what would count as excluding one, assemble independent witnesses, attempt settlement.
