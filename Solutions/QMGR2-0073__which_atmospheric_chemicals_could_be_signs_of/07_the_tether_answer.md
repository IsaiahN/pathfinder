# The Tether's answer on atmospheric biosignature gases

**Mode: `specified` on the verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A spectrum returns a molecular feature. The prediction library contains at least two sources for almost every candidate feature: a biological metabolism and an abiotic pathway. `R_s` is the unresolved source on that slot. Asked "which gas means life?", the frame tries to answer a source question with a composition instrument. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The answer must be per gas, per context, per planet.

The question is a **union compound**, not a list. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. It must be unbundled into a chemistry loop (can X arise without life here?) and a source loop (was X made by life here?). Those two loops have different grounds and must never be averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **No molecule alone is a biosignature.** A reading symmetric under source substitution carries no information about its source. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*; Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Mode `general`.
2. **Every candidate gas has some abiotic route in some context.** Des Marais et al. 2002, Segura et al. 2007, Harman et al. 2014 and Schwieterman et al. 2018 jointly establish this for O2, O3, CH4 and the other standard candidates. Within the chemistry frame, more searching does not add a source discriminator: Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Mode `specified`, world-corroborated within one literature pool.
3. **A biosignature is a bonded conjunction.** `(detected feature) + (context excluding the abiotic routes for that feature under this star and this planet) + (a witness from a closure that differs from the one that produced the reading)`. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Two gases from one spectrum are one frame with two slots, not two witnesses. Mode `specified`.
4. **Disequilibrium is the strongest known conjunctive shape, not a name.** O2 plus CH4 in thermodynamic disequilibrium (Hitchcock and Lovelock 1967), with liquid water, a benign stellar UV field, and the named false-positive mechanisms excluded (Meadows 2017), is the best current shape. It remains `specified`: Harman et al. 2014 find *"simultaneous detection of O3 and CH4 by a UV-visible mission is not a strong biosignature without proper contextual information"*.
5. **The same gas changes valence with the habitat.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Abundant O2 with water and an active hydrological cycle is hard to make abiotically (Segura et al. 2007); abundant O2 on a desiccated M-dwarf planet can be made by CO2 photolysis. Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Mode `specified`.
6. **The source verdict is `unreachable`, not `gap` and not `silence`.** The anchor for "was it life?" is the actual biota on that planet. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Schwieterman et al. 2018 agree that *"ground truth verification of the biogenicity of any remotely detected signature will be unachievable for the determinable future"*. NASA's Confidence of Life Detection scale is the right practical output: a graded confidence with a recorded lineage.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that O2, O3, CH4, N2O, CH3Cl, DMS or any other named gas *is* a biosignature. A name is a binding from the terrestrial-biology frame, not a figure output.
- Not that O2 plus CH4 is sufficient. It is the strongest conjunctive shape, and it is still context-dependent.
- Not that a non-detection means no life. Cryptic and anoxic biospheres are a named false-negative category (Schwieterman et al. 2018, section 8).
- Not that the framework can certify its own completeness. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*
- Not `grounded`. No remote measurement settles biological origin here.

## 4. What would settle it, and what is actually owed

The ground is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*, and the colour case was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

What is owed now, in dependency order (Fig 3):

1. **Per-slot enumeration.** For each feature, list the abiotic pathways and the conditions under which each operates. Do not average across gases (Fig 1 :22).
2. **Independent context imports.** Surface water, host-star UV spectral energy distribution, crust redox state, and time variability, each with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23).
3. **Explicit negation.** Record which false-positive mechanisms have been excluded and which remain live. *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."* (Fig 12 amendment).
4. **Graded reporting.** Report a confidence and a lineage, as the CoLD scale does, and keep every cut ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41: *"so keep every cut ranked and reversible"*).
5. **Standing falsifiers from section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.** A gas with an empty abiotic closure; a remote instrument that comments on source; statistical independence of two gases from one retrieval; or an instrument that decides origin from composition alone.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, and `unreachable` at this budget.** The framework can narrow the shape and name the missing thing in effect terms: a source-discriminating instrument. It cannot say which primitive would fill the slot, because naming it would mean already holding it. Fig 13, near :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Each settled gas can become an operand for the next comparison; the source remains a separate, ungrounded level.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> There is no molecule that means life by itself. A spectral line tells you what is in an atmosphere, not how it got there, and CO2 photolysis, volcanism, impacts and water-rock reactions can make the same gases that biology makes. What the figures license is a shape, not a name: a candidate biosignature is a detected feature bonded to a planetary context that excludes the known abiotic routes for that feature, plus a witness from a different kind of measurement. Two gases in one spectrum are one witness, not two. O2 plus methane in thermodynamic disequilibrium, with surface water and the stellar UV field checked, is the strongest version of that shape we have, and it is still a `specified` candidate, not a detection of life. For a remote world the source ground is unreachable, so the honest output is a graded confidence with the false-positive mechanisms recorded, not a yes or no. A result that fails any of the four refuters written in advance would change this. A reported failure is more useful to me than agreement.
