## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README (local copy read this session) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13, near :134: *"can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway."*

From *The Library of Babel* (read this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For QMGR2-0073, the shape is:

> a candidate biosignature is a three-part molecule of (detected feature) + (context that excludes the known abiotic routes for that feature) + (a witness from a closure different from the one that produced the reading).

`"O2"` is a name. `"O2 plus excluded CO2-photolysis conditions plus confirmed surface water plus an independent stellar-UV constraint"` is a shape. The first is what the question asks for; the second is what the framework can licence.

## 2. How the shape is obtained, and why the question as asked is malformed

*Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* And on a failed exhaustive search: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

That is exactly this question. "Which gases are signs of life, and which arise without life?" bundles two loops:

- a chemistry loop: can gas X be produced here without life? This has a ground in photochemical models, laboratory rates and Solar System spectra.
- a source loop: was the gas on this particular planet produced by life? Its ground is the actual biota, unreachable remotely.

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The Library of Babel essay says the same in different words: *"You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Low overlap between a 1941-library metaphor and a 2026 metatheory figure, same instruction. This is the one place in this solution where a genuine two-frame triangulation occurs.

## 3. What licenses naming a particular gas

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a remote exoplanet, that ground is unavailable. Schwieterman et al. 2018 say *"ground truth verification of the biogenicity of any remotely detected signature will be unachievable for the determinable future"*. NASA's response, the Confidence of Life Detection scale, is to communicate a gradient, which is the practical form of an honest `unreachable` verdict.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Stellar UV, surface water and crust redox state are imports from other frames. Each needs a source gate and a shadow test: does it explain a specific false-positive pathway?

Naming a gas as *the* biosignature is a binding from the terrestrial-biology frame onto a molecule. It is allowed only as `specified` world content, provenanced as such. It is not what the framework returns.

## 4. Why "the molecule tells you the source" is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. What resolved the colour case was a spectrometer, not an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Apply it here. A spectrograph reads an absorption feature. It reports composition. It cannot report which process made the molecule. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument about the molecule. The clinical analogue in section 5 is the low-overlap witness for this claim, because it reaches the same rule without sharing the framework's author or evidence pool.

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* In this question the "bond" is the *relation between the gas and its context*. A candidate list records the ingredients and drops the bond, which is exactly the flat-formula failure the figure names.

## 5. A low-overlap witness: clinical testing

Merck Manual, "Understanding Medical Tests and Test Results" (opened this session): *"a test result does not provide a definitive diagnosis but only estimates the probability of a disease being present or absent"*. And: *"This method is limited by the important assumption that each of the tests is conditionally independent of each other."* A screening review (PMC4389712, opened this session) puts it more starkly: *"With few exceptions, screening tests do not diagnose the illness. Rather subjects who test positive typically require further evaluation with subsequent diagnostic tests or procedures."*

This is the same shape as the gas problem: a positive reading reweights a prior; it does not name a cause. Confidence accumulates only through conditionally independent tests, and the gold standard is a different kind of thing from the screening test. No author or evidence pool is shared with the astrobiology sources. It corroborates Fig 2 :31 (independence) and Fig 2 :35 (triangulation is not negotiation) from a genuinely different field.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous astrobiologists to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground, an instrument or an independent refuter settle it.

Same essay on the standing of this document: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* However, *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not tests of the biosignature question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
