## Relevant wider literature, not all of it read in full

- Des Marais et al. 2002, "Remote Sensing of Planetary Properties and Biosignatures on Extrasolar Terrestrial Planets", Astrobiology 2(2): the seminal review that gives O2/O3 highest priority and notes CH4 has crustal sources. Text extract opened at https://www.saraseager.com/_files/ugd/92999e_f8bb8f9a09c04fc4804b8737878ddd02.pdf
- NExSS Biosignatures Workshop, "O2 Lessons": false-positive mechanisms and the rule that each one ruled out increases robustness. The direct PDF fetch at https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180007564/downloads/20180007564.pdf returned an unsupported content type; the quote in this folder was taken from the search-result text extract of that URL and is marked as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`. This is a recorded limitation, not a silent success.
- Sagan et al. 1993, "A search for life on Earth from the Galileo spacecraft", Nature 365: the Earth control case, O2 plus CH4 plus a red edge plus narrow-band radio. Abstraction seen in search results; the full paper was not opened, so it is not used as a witness in the verdict.
- Green et al. 2023, "Moving toward a framework for communicating the confidence of life detection", Nature Astronomy: listed on the NASA resources page; not opened, cited only as a pointer.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape and the refuter first. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the review articles.
- **Fig 2 :31**, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* One-to-one contact only. A bulk send to a list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that voids the agreement.
- **Fig 2 :42**, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A false life claim is costly, so several genuinely independent checkers are worth the effort.
- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula may be given away as a method. The result of this run may not be presented as a finding about any particular planet.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what false-positive pathway they would exclude first and how; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures and the papers, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, restated

- **Stage 1:** the output, its mode and its refuters. No theory, no request for trust, no request for agreement.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** sources and figures, after the stage-1 verdict is recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** the formula as a reusable method, with each user declaring their own ground and writing their own refuter before running it.

**Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent.** Hosting and sending are yours to do. Prefer an opt-in download or submission page to an attachment pushed to a list.
