# Expert verification packet: atmospheric biosignature gases

**What this is:** a claim in a form an atmospheric chemist, exoplanet observer or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at an exoplanet is claimed to settle biological origin.

**Status: a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

No atmospheric chemical is a biosignature by itself. A spectral feature reports composition and cannot report the process that made the molecule; biology and abiotic chemistry can terminate in the same gas. What the figures license is a shape: a candidate biosignature is a detected feature bonded to a planetary context that excludes the known abiotic routes for that feature under this star and this planet, plus at least one witness drawn from a closure different from the one that produced the reading. O2 with CH4 in thermodynamic disequilibrium, with surface water, a checked stellar UV field and the named false-positive mechanisms excluded, is the strongest current instance of that shape, and it is still a `specified` candidate. On a remote world the source ground is unreachable, so the honest output is a graded confidence with the exclusions recorded, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. The reading does not determine the source (framework-internal and chemistry-facing)

- **Statement:** a molecular detection is symmetric under substitution of its production pathway. O2 from oxygenic photosynthesis and O2 from water or CO2 photolysis produce the same absorption feature.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*; Fig 13 :121, *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 9 :30-31, *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- **World check:** Des Marais et al. 2002 and Harman et al. 2014, read this session. Harman: *"simultaneous detection of O3 and CH4 by a UV-visible mission is not a strong biosignature without proper contextual information."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a molecule with exactly one physically possible production route, and that route requires a living agent.

### C2. Independence must come from a different closure, not from a second gas in the same spectrum

- **Statement:** O2 and O3 are one slot observed at two depths, not two witnesses. Two co-detected gases from one retrieval are one frame with two slots. A genuinely independent witness comes from surface water, the stellar UV spectral energy distribution, crust redox state or time variability, each measured separately.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 12 :5, *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- **World check:** Hitchcock and Lovelock 1967 make the disequilibrium case; Segura et al. 2007 and Meadows 2017 show that its force depends on water and on which false-positive mechanisms are excluded. A clinical analogue read this session states the same rule independently: *"This method is limited by the important assumption that each of the tests is conditionally independent of each other."* (Merck Manual, 2026)
- **Refuter:** show that two gases from a single retrieval are statistically independent evidence about biological origin, or that one gas with no context crosses a stated confidence threshold.

### C3. The source verdict is unreachable remotely, so the correct output is a graded confidence

- **Statement:** the anchor for "was this gas made by life?" is the actual biota on that planet. It is not in the spectrum, not in the photochemical model and not in the terrestrial-analogue library. A null detection is not evidence of no life.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **World check:** Schwieterman et al. 2018: *"ground truth verification of the biogenicity of any remotely detected signature will be unachievable for the determinable future"*. NASA's Life Detection Forum and Confidence of Life Detection scale are the field's own move toward graded confidence.
- **Refuter:** a remote instrument whose output changes with the biological-origin hypothesis rather than only with composition.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named gas is *the* biosignature. That is a binding from the terrestrial-biology frame, not a figure output.
- Not that O2 plus CH4 is sufficient. Harman et al. 2014 show the conjunction is still context-dependent.
- Not that a non-detection means no life. Cryptic and anoxic biospheres are a named false-negative category.
- Not that the framework certifies its own completeness. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an exoplanet measurement.
- Not a claim about any particular planet. The shape is planet-relative; the habitat must be enumerated.

## 5. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read C1 and decide whether you know of a gas with an abiotic production set of size zero. If you do, the claim fails.
2. Read C2 and check the O2/O3 relationship: is O3 really a downstream spelling of O2 in your retrieval, or can it be produced independently in the atmosphere at detectable levels?
3. Read C3 and name any existing remote measurement that comments on source rather than composition. If one exists, the `unreachable` verdict is wrong.
4. Name the false-positive pathway you would exclude first for a candidate gas, and say what observation excludes it. That is the shadow test the shape requires.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Stage rule:** this packet is stage 1. The figures and the wider literature are offered only after your verdict on these three sub-claims is recorded. Agreement expressed after seeing the framework is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
