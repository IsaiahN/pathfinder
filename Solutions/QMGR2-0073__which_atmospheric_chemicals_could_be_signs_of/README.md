## What this solution decides, in effect terms

The question asks for a list split into two piles: gases that mean life and gases that do not. The derivation shows that the split cannot be made at the level of the molecule. A spectral line is a reading, and a reading does not comment on its source. Under the figures, the honest answer is a **shape**: a gas is a candidate biosignature only as a bonded conjunction of (i) a detected molecular feature, (ii) an environmental context that closes the known abiotic pathways, and (iii) at least one witness drawn from a closure that differs from the one that produced the reading. The molecule alone returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). The question also splits into two loops that must not be averaged: "can this gas be made without life here?" is a chemistry question with a ground in models and laboratory rates, while "was this gas made by life here?" is a source question whose ground is unreachable for a remote exoplanet at this budget. `Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence` (Fig 13 :134).

An answer would look like a rule that maps a gas plus a characterised context to a confidence about biological origin. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, so it can accrue a residual and be refused, rather than remaining an invisible preference.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the framework's own words. First, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Second, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40): selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that voids their agreement. Third, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42): the outside frame sees the residual and its refuter, never the framework interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.
