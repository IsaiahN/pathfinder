## 3. Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Craig (2019)** is the most useful single source to quote back to a physicist. It concedes the force of the hierarchy problem while refusing the idea that naturalness dictates the answer. Use it to justify F1, F3 and F6.
- **Grinbaum (2012)** is the low-overlap corroboration from philosophy of science. It independently says naturalness cannot be objective when comparing models. It corroborates Fig 9 :60 without sharing the framework's evidence pool.
- **Wikipedia (Little hierarchy problem, 2026)** supplies the empirical content of "the bargain is unpaid": SUSY cancels the divergences to all orders, but the observed 125 GeV Higgs and the null sparticle searches push the superpartners up and reintroduce a small tuning. This is the world-side version of `left(R,φ) > 0`.
- **Wikipedia (Hierarchy problem, 2026)** lists the actual alternatives: conformal Standard Model, extra dimensions, braneworld models, UV/IR mixing. This is what makes D2 a union rather than a list with one member.

No world source was found that says the figures decide which mechanism is real. That is expected: the figures are a shape generator, not a collider. Agreement among these sources does not overturn the figure verdict; it corroborates the narrower claim that naturalness is a heuristic and the name awaits the ground.
