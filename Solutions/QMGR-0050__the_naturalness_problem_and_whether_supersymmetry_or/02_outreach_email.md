# Outreach draft - check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until F1, F6 and F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are reflected in the wording. The output must survive a check or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Stage rule:** record the recipient's verdict on Stage 1 *before* showing any theory. A reply that begins "this fails because..." is a successful outcome. A reply that begins "I agree" after seeing the framework is worthless as evidence (Fig 2 :40).

**Subject:** Check one claim about naturalness before deciding whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the physics, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. The naturalness residual is one live slot, the Higgs mass parameter. Its correction grows as the cutoff squared, so with a Planck cutoff the cancellation is about one part in 10³⁴. That is real mass, not an aesthetic preference.
> 2. No amount of calculation inside the Standard Model alone can close it. Composition inside a closed set never adds a new degree of freedom. Any resolution is therefore an import of new atoms from a frame whose closure differs. Supersymmetry, compositeness, extra dimensions and relaxion dynamics are competing imports, not variations of one thing.
> 3. "Naturalness" itself is a preference, not a witness. It filters which theory gets tried first. It cannot decide which theory is true. The verdict belongs to an instrument: a measurement, an exclusion limit, a coupling deviation.
> 4. Consequently the sentence "supersymmetry resolves naturalness" is a verdict stated without a ground. The honest form is: supersymmetry is a candidate import whose cancellation is real at the level of the loop integrals, but whose bargain is unpaid, because the superpartners have not been seen at the scale that would make the residual small. Whether it is *the* resolution is `gap` at this budget. That is a verdict, not a silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the figures pick supersymmetry; that naturalness is wrong; that the LHC has refuted supersymmetry; or that a nicer theory is more likely to be true. I am not claiming this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a Standard Model only calculation that drives the Higgs mass residual to zero with no new degrees of freedom and no change of closure;
> 2. an operational naturalness measure whose value is fixed by a measurement rather than chosen after the fact;
> 3. a ground result that settles the name, either a distinctive atom of one mechanism detected with its predicted couplings, or all candidates of a class excluded by a measurable signature;
> 4. a demonstration that the candidate mechanisms share no common substructure that fixes the cancellation outcome.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will narrow or retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. Please tell me your Stage 1 verdict before opening them. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
