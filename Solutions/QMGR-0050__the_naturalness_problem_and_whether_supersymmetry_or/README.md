## Restatement as an effect

The question asks which of two things is being decided. Option A: the Higgs mass parameter is unnatural, so a specific new mechanism (historically supersymmetry) resolves the discrepancy. Option B: the residual is a real cross-scale prediction failure whose *shape* of resolution the figures can characterise, but the *name* of the mechanism awaits a ground reading. What each leads to: A attaches a `general`-mode verdict to an empirical question and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B sends a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter, and an honest open slot until experiment settles it.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a resolution is a candidate term φ such that (i) it adds at least one atom to the closure of the Standard Model effective field theory frame, (ii) it cancels or softens the quadratic sensitivity of the Higgs mass to the cutoff, (iii) it pays the bargain `cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R)`, and (iv) it is settled by experiment. This binding is written down in `01` §2 before any verdict, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that supersymmetry is correct.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle an empirical collider question.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A thousand theorists agreeing that a mechanism is elegant adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients by receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must not hold the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR or CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in venues avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (essay, 2026-11-19): LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the naturalness question it reports on. It can only report the shape a resolution would have, what would refute that shape, and where the stop is honest.
