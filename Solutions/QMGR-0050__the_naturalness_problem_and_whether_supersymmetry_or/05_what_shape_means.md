## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133 *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form a resolution must have, statable before we can name the mechanism:

> any resolution imports at least one new atom into the EFT closure, couples it to the Higgs so as to cancel or soften quadratic sensitivity to the cutoff, pays the mint bargain, and is settled by an instrument.

That is a shape. "The MSSM with tan beta near 50 and a 2 TeV gluino" is a name. The figures deliver the first, not the second.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the target, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual in `01` is described as a mass discrepancy on a slot, not as "a crisis of naturalness."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place."* The paradox: you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here, writing down the bound shape in `01` §0 is already the steering device.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means a detector result, a coupling deviation, or a precision exclusion.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8). Importing "supersymmetry" from the physics frame into the derivation is legitimate only if the record says it came from there and shows that it explains the gap it claims.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Using it to rank mechanisms does not make the ranking a measurement.

## 4. Why "naturalness" is the exact forbidden invisible preference

Fig 5 :62-63: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."* Formula step 2: *"Every check here runs on the MECHANISM and none on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION."*

Naturalness is precisely such a preference. Craig (CERN Courier, 2019) says the acceptable amount of fine-tuning *"has changed over time"* and invokes the Potter Stewart measure, *"I know it when I see it."* Grinbaum (Foundations of Physics, 2012) calls it *"essentially a formalized aesthetic criterion"* and says it *"cannot be defined objectively"* when comparing models. Wikipedia (Naturalness, 2026) calls it *"a particular, aesthetically motivated construction principle."*

So a derivation that ranks SUSY above composite Higgs because it is "more natural" is checking a preference while believing it checked a mechanism. The discipline, per Fig 5 :65, is to *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* That is what §1 of `05` and §0 of `01` do.

## 5. Metamerism: why a source cannot be named from the reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: a measured Higgs mass of 125 GeV does not comment on which mechanism produced it. SUSY, compositeness, extra dimensions and relaxion dynamics can in principle yield the same reading. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a verdict. This is the world-side content of Fig 9 :32 "a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any specific mechanism in this solution. That was too broad.

- The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive **not held** in Γ.
- The mechanisms here are not missing primitives of the six-term set. They are imports from the physics frame onto held structure. Naming one as a *candidate* is a binding, not the invention of a seventh term (Fig 6 :26 *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).
- What remains forbidden is asserting that the framework *derives* the name, or that a name from a reading settles the source (§5 metamerism).
- **Retracted:** the blanket "no naming." **Retained:** the metamerism point and the provenance requirement. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 6 + the source essay).

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names agreeing that supersymmetry is natural is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, then lets an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of supersymmetry or of naturalness and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**The framework does not validate itself.** *Arrival of the Fittest* (2026-08-19): *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* That is why the verdict is offered with a refuter and a mode, not as a conclusion.
