## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The naturalness residual is one live slot, the Higgs mass parameter, whose radiative correction grows as the cutoff squared. No composition inside the Standard Model closure can close it, so any resolution is an import of new atoms from a frame whose closure differs. The candidate mechanisms (supersymmetry, composite Higgs, extra dimensions, relaxion dynamics) form a functional group sharing the substructure "new weak-scale degrees of freedom that soften the quadratic divergence," not an isomer set as written. "Naturalness" is a preference and a budget filter, never a witness; the verdict belongs to experiment. Supersymmetry's cancellation is a real first-order structure, but its bargain `cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R)` is unpaid at current mass limits, so which mechanism resolves the residual is `gap` at this budget. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a physicist against the two theories

- **Statement:** SUSY's cancellation is real at the level of the loop integrals, but with sparticles above roughly 1-2 TeV the residual tuning is nonzero, so the figure bargain is not yet shown to pay. The mechanisms are a functional group, not isomers as written.
