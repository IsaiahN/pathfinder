# The Tether's answer on naturalness and supersymmetry

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The Higgs mass parameter is sensitive to the cutoff as δm² ~ Λ². With a Planck cutoff the needed cancellation is about one part in 10³⁴ (Wikipedia, Hierarchy problem, 2026). This is a real residual on one slot: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* It is not "naturalness." Naturalness is a preference about the residual, and a preference has no anchor (Fig 5 :62).

The question bundles three domains: the EFT calculation, the family of proposed mechanisms, and the experimental ground. Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Split before searching.

## 2. The shape

1. **No Standard Model only search closes it.** Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* A resolution is an import that adds at least one new atom to the EFT frame's closure. Wikipedia (Hierarchy problem, 2026) agrees in physics language: the problem cannot be formulated strictly inside the Standard Model because the Higgs mass is not calculable there.

2. **A resolution is a mint that must pay a bargain.** Formula step 3: `accept iff cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R)`; Fig 5 :26 *"and then: does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"* Supersymmetry is a real first-order cancellation structure, but with superpartners pushed above roughly 1-2 TeV the leftover tuning is nonzero (Wikipedia, Little hierarchy problem, 2026). The bargain is therefore `specified` and unpaid, not settled.

3. **The mechanisms form a functional group, not an isomer set.** Fig 12 :124 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* SUSY, composite Higgs, extra dimensions and relaxion dynamics share the substructure "new weak-scale degrees of freedom that soften the quadratic divergence" but do not share an atom set, so they fail Fig 12 :117's isomer test as written (`06`).

4. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Choosing SUSY over its alternatives is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation. Fig 13 :133 *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

5. **The verdict belongs to the ground.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Null LHC results disfavour specific models, not the idea as such: Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Craig (CERN Courier, 2019): *"The LHC null results teach us only about specific (and historically popular) models that were inspired by naturalness."*

6. **The specific name is `gap` at this budget, and that is a verdict.** Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 8 :41 *"different facts, and they issue different instructions."* Do not import an atom against a budget limit; more search and better instruments are the instruction for unreachable-at-budget.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that supersymmetry is false. Null results are not proofs of absence.
- Not that supersymmetry is true, elegant, or "the" resolution. No ground reading settles the name.
- Not that naturalness is a law. It is an aesthetically motivated heuristic (Grinbaum, Foundations of Physics, 2012) and a filter for the budget, never a witness.
- Not a derivation of one mechanism from the six-term substrate. That substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (They're Scanning Books, 2026).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the relevant scale settles `left(R,φ)` for any candidate.
- Not that the cancellation is a figure bond. The bond identification was withdrawn under strain (`06` Q5, `01` §7).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* The spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. **Name Test:** does any detector result distinguish the mechanism that produced the weak scale? A sparticle with the predicted couplings, a compositeness signature in Higgs couplings, a Kaluza-Klein resonance, or a relaxion-specific signature would each name a member.
2. **Bargain Test:** does a candidate drive `left(R,φ)` below the declared threshold, measured against a predeclared tuning criterion, not one chosen after the data?
3. **Closure Test:** is there a Standard Model only calculation that closes the residual with no new atoms? If yes, F2 is refuted and the import requirement falls.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §5:** an operational naturalness measure fixed by a ground reading; a demonstration that no common substructure fixes the cancellation; a proof that the bargain cannot pay for any assignment.
