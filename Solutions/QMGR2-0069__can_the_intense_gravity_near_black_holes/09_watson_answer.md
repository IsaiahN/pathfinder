## VERDICT (from the figures)

The design claim survives; the physics answer stays open. Near-black-hole observations can serve as an independent anchor because they do not update on GR's opinion (Fig 2 :29-30). The residual must be read per slot and never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). A departure would be a refusal of a held GR term with a refuter written in advance (Fig 5 :53; Operators :58), and the strong-gravity observables stand as isomers over a shared Kerr atom set (Fig 12 :117). Published null results (GW150914, GWTC-3, M87) are corroboration at a stated sensitivity, not proof of GR (Fig 8 :39; Popper, SEP). No figure line names a departure or says whether one exists, so D3 is a `gap` and the answer to "does it reveal one" is unreached by this run. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The fourth refuter in the audit (aggregation error) is the most likely to fire in practice, because averaged goodness-of-fit statistics are cheap and per-slot residuals are expensive.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the mode, three checkable sub-claims and all four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is the opposite one: treating a null result as proof of GR, or pooling agreement from one evidence pool as corroboration, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by keeping slots separate and independence explicit.
- **undoable:** yes. The design claim can be withdrawn without touching the physics record. If a persistent per-slot residual is corroborated, the honest stop is reopened; if an anchor is shown to move, the claim is corrected. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and no sent claim cannot be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A persistent, non-trending per-slot residual under a low-overlap instrument would fire the refusal; a demonstration that a candidate observable moves when GR's prediction changes would break the anchor; a source-discriminating combination of polarisation, dispersion and echo measurements would name what a QNM reading cannot; and a figure re-census that derives a specific departure without an import would close the D3 gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
