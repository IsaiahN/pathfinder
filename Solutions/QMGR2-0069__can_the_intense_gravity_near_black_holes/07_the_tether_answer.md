# The Tether's answer on strong-gravity tests of GR

**Mode: `specified` on the design claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

GR predicts observables for strong-field systems; instruments near black holes return observations. The residual is the gap, measured **per observable slot**: inspiral phasing, ringdown modes, shadow geometry, polarisation, propagation speed. Two questions are bundled here and must be kept separate (Fig 9 :54-58): can the configuration reveal a departure (a design question), and does it actually reveal one (a ground question).

## 2. The shape

1. **The near-black-hole measurement can be an anchor.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A strong-field observation is constitutively about the strong field (Fig 2 :36), so it is the right kind of anchor. GR is a frame that ranges against it (Fig 2 :45), not the other way round.

2. **The residual is per slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global "GR passes" is not a finding; a per-slot residual that persists and does not trend down is.

3. **A departure is a refusal, not another parameter.** Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. The refuter is written before the run; the refusal precedes any mint (Formula step 2).

4. **The instrument edge already exists.** Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."* Strong gravity is already returning readings from GW detectors, horizon-scale imaging and X-ray instruments. The inward track has an edge to extend from.

5. **A null result is corroboration, not proof.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* GW150914 and GWTC-3 report "no evidence for violations" and "no evidence in support of physics beyond GR"; that is the correct form.

6. **Which departure is right is a gap.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The figures return the shape of a valid detection, not a theory name. A specific modified gravity is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that GR is correct. That is a verdict only the ground can give, and no ground reading is claimed here.
- Not that GR is wrong, and not that a departure has been detected. No slot residual was measured by this agent.
- Not that a null result proves GR. Fig 8 :39 and the Popper entry (SEP) agree on that.
- Not that any named modified-gravity theory is the answer. That would be a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that all strong-gravity observables are the same test. They are isomers over a shared Kerr atom set (Fig 12 :117), with a falsifier stated in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3.
- Not grounded. Every substantive claim above is `specified` or `general`; the historical claim that instruments return readings is `grounded` in the opened world sources, used as secondary corroboration.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a single slot return a persistent, non-trending residual under an independent instrument? If yes, a refusal is owed and the honest stop in section 2.5 must be reopened.
- Can two observables that share the Kerr atom set be shown to respond differently to the same departure? If yes, the isomer claim of `06_isomers_and_order.md` is settled at the test level.
- Can a combination of polarisation, dispersion and echo measurements break the degeneracy among theories that give the same QNM reading? That is the analogue of the spectrometer.
- Standing refuters from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3: anchor failure, persistent slot residual, false gap, aggregation error.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give the shape of a valid test and stop at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The canonical README: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The round trip gap `R_T` between the figure set and any slogan drawn from it is nonzero and is recorded in `11_audit.md`. Agreement among people who share the GWTC evidence pool is one witness, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The lineage is the proof, so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you whether general relativity breaks near black holes, and it does not name a replacement. It says the strong-field observation can be an anchor because it does not move when GR's prediction changes; that the prediction-observation gap must be read per observable slot, never averaged, or one live slot disappears into the mean; and that a departure would be a refusal of a held term, with the refuter written before the run, not another fitted parameter. Published null results (GW150914, GWTC-3, the M87 shadow) are corroboration at a given sensitivity, not proof of GR: a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence. Which departure is right is a genuine gap, settled only by an instrument that discriminates among the theories giving the same reading. What the framework offers is the shape any valid strong-gravity test must have, the refuters in advance, and the honest stop at the instruments' limit.
