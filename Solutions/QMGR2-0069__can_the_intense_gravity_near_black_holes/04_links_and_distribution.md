## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Weak field versus strong field is a categorical difference, not a degree.** Berti et al (2015) say most tests probe the weak-field regime and that there are theoretical and experimental reasons to expect modification when fields are strong. They name black holes and neutron stars as the best laboratories. This is world corroboration for treating the residual as per-slot and scale-dependent, and it is not a figure quote.
2. **Null results are careful, not proofs.** Both GW150914 and GWTC-3 phrase their findings as "no evidence for violations" and "no evidence in support of physics beyond GR," and GWTC-3 additionally states that residual power after subtracting the best-fit waveform is consistent with detector noise. Fig 8 :39 (*"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*) governs the reading of those statements; the papers do not conflict with it.
3. **Independent instruments exist.** GW detectors, EHT imaging and X-ray timing are operationally different. Under Fig 2 :31-32 they are different frames, so agreement between them can mean something. Agreement between two papers using the same GWTC data cannot.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52,** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the design claim and its refuter. Do not send the framework first.
- **Fig 2 :45,** *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the reader would count as a failure before offering any theory.
- **Fig 9 :27-28,** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Describe the residual in effect terms first.
- **Fig 2 :40,** agreement from a shared evidence pool is worthless as a verdict. Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement Trap,** prestige funds an action but cannot settle it. A famous name on an endorsement is a budget, not a witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the design claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a departure and which instrument would discriminate it; (3) only then the local figure files, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual and the pre-registered refuter. Record the reader's verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2 (opt-in).** If they are curious, offer the three local sources. Their later agreement is never added to the evidence for the claim.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Fig 4 :22-23: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away gives away a method, not an answer. Fig 5 :62-63: a sought-for shape held as a preference is invisible to its frame, which is why the refuter must be written down as an ordinary term before the run.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. If a reader fires refuter 1, 2, 3 or 4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3, stop and correct the claim before any further distribution.
