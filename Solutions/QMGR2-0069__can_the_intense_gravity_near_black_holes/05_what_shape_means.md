## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is:

> a refusal of a held GR term, registered as a persistent per-slot residual against an anchor that does not move when GR's prediction changes, written with its refuter in advance, stopping at the instruments' limit.

That is a shape. "The graviton has mass m", "dynamical Chern-Simons gravity with coupling a", or "Lorentz violation at scale E" are names. The shape does not select among them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: use the terms you already hold that are *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the held terms are residual, slot, anchor, instrument, refusal, ground.
- *Library of Babel* on the question as tether: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."* The residual, described in effect terms, selects the frame; picking a modified-gravity theory first and then looking for a place to put it inverts that order.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach draft sends the effect description first.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :9-14). A specific modified-gravity model is an import from the physics frame into the figure vocabulary. It adds an atom to our frame, not to the world (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

For this question, neither condition has been met by this agent. Therefore no departure is named.

## 4. Why "no detection" is not a name either

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was *"the arrival of a better instrument"* that resolved the case, not an argument.

Applied here: a measured ringdown frequency or shadow diameter is a reading. Several underlying theories can produce the same reading. A null result is a reading of "no deviation at this resolution," not a name for GR's correctness, and a positive deviation would still be a reading whose source needs a discriminating instrument. This is world corroboration for F5 and F8 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, not a figure quote.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a departure?

**No, and the distinction matters.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. The shape here is the form of a term we do not yet hold: a specific departure is not among our atoms. So the limit applies directly: we may state that a refusal is owed at a slot, and that it must survive an independent anchor, but we may not fill the slot with a theory name and call it a derivation. A named modified gravity is a provenanced import from the physics frame, and must be recorded as such.

What remains wrong would be the reverse error: treating the shape as if it were already the answer. *Library of Babel* warns that by the time you have the exact search terms, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it"*; that is a statement about the searcher's vocabulary, not a licence to skip the ground.

## 6. Prestige, agreement and the status of this document

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read this session:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

So a list of respected names agreeing that GR survives strong-field tests is a convention, not a verdict. Fig 2 :40: agreement is worthless *"when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

**This document is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Section 1 through 5 above are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle whether intense gravity reveals departures from GR. Only the ground can.
