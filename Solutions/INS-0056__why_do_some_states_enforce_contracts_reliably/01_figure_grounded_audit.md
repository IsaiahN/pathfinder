## 0. The residual in effect terms

A state is observed to do two things at once:

1. It enforces private contracts predictably. A clearly named breach produces a court order, and the order can be executed against identifiable assets. The outcome is visible to an instrument: a recovery rate, a time to judgment, a payment or a seizure.
2. It fails to protect persons from coercion or discriminatory treatment. A person with an formally equal right has no effective remedy when the coercer is an employer, a landlord, a police officer, or the state itself. The harm is often invisible to the official instruments that would have to register it, and the only "measurement" available is the state's own declaration that the right exists.

Stated as an effect, R is **the differential between a live contract slot and a dead personal-protection slot inside the same apparatus**. The surprise is not that one fails; it is that the same courts, police, statutes and budget produce reliability in one and not the other. A theory that predicts they should move together is refuted by the observation. A theory that says the two are unrelated is also incomplete, because they share the state's coercive apparatus.

`R` by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** at least three terms, two private parties plus the state as checker in the contract case; a person plus a coercer plus the state in the protection case. The two cases overlap where the contracting party is also a person needing protection (employment, tenancy, credit, immigration status). By Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not two cleanly separate ones.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The contract case has an anchor that does not move when a frame thinks about it (payment, seizure, a market price for enforcement reliability). The protection case, as formally constituted, has only a declared meaning. Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one".
- **Scale:** first order inside a single dispute (two parties, one checker), second order when the checker is the state itself and must be checked (Fig 12 :104, settled molecule becomes an atom at the next level).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

One line per candidate domain: actors / currency / anchor. A nameable anchor is required; where none exists it is recorded as not yet a domain.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Contract enforcement | two private parties, court, enforcement officer, market participants | enforceable claim, damages, recovery, time to judgment | yes: payment or seizure, and the market that prices reliability | genuine domain |
| D2 Personal protection against coercion or discrimination | person, coercer (private or state agent), formal rights regime, inspectorate, press | rights on paper versus effective remedy | **no** if the anchor offered is the state's own rights declaration; **yes** if an external check is named (independent court with subpoena, inspectorate, free press, measured outcome) | not a domain until the external check is named |
| D3 Verification and measurement | observers, courts, regulators, journalists, international monitors, index builders | agreement versus residual | the ground: actual outcomes, not index scores | genuine domain |

**Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a union, with a real intersection (the worker, tenant, debtor or migrant who is both a contracting party and a person needing protection). Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." D3 ranges over both, so improving D3 without changing D1 or D2 changes contact only for the measurement, not for the case.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).**

- Link 1, perception: can the instrument see the harm? A missed payment is visible. Coercion behind a closed door and discrimination with no complaint channel are not. Fig 3 :5: "the system can tell what is in front of it, and tell it apart from what was there before".
- Link 2, vocabulary: does the regime have terms for what the situation affords? Contract law has breach, damages, execution. Personal protection may have the words on paper but no usable term in the local court.
- Link 3, objective: what counts as progress? For D1, a measured recovery rate. For D2 as formally constituted, "rights protected", a convention rather than an anchor. This is the break. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- Links 4 and 5, planning and carry: cannot be diagnosed from below the break. Fig 3 :23: "It is a reading of nothing."

**Strain test applied before concluding.** An early reading said "contract enforcement has an anchor, rights protection has none." That strains Fig 11 :39, which says capability is a property of the agent-and-habitat. Re-census: the anchor exists for rights when the habitat includes an external checker; what fails is not the possibility of an anchor but the state's willingness to install one. The anchor is not missing from the world, it is missing from the frame. This correction is kept in §6.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The differential is per slot, and aggregation hides it (decisive for the framing)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"

A country can score well on an aggregate "rule of law" or "enforcing contracts" measure while the personal-protection slot is dead. That is not a paradox inside the framework; it is the legal state Fig 1 names. The aggregate is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). World source: the World Bank's B-READY site publishes a "Dispute Resolution" topic alongside separate "Labor" and "Financial Services" topics, which is exactly this per-topic disaggregation in practice.

**Refuter:** show a dataset in which contract-enforcement performance and personal-protection outcomes move together so tightly that the per-slot residual is always zero whenever the global score is high, with no live exceptions. Then the differential is an artefact of aggregation and this finding fails.

### F2 - The anchor test separates the two cases

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."
Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."

In D1 the anchor is payment or seizure, which does not consult the court's opinion. In D2 as formally constituted the proposed anchor is a written guarantee, which changes answer whenever the state reinterprets or suspends it. The Nobel press release on Douglass North (1993) states: "The lack of opportunity of entering binding contracts and other institutional arrangements is a cause of economic stagnation", while North's lecture concedes that "Measuring and enforcing agreements in political markets is far more difficult". The asymmetry is in the anchor, not in the amount of law.

Critical correction: D2 is not anchorless in principle. An independent court, an inspectorate with subpoena power, a free press or a measured outcome are nameable anchors. What is refused is the state's own declaration as its own anchor. By Fig 2 :36 an anchor is "legitimate when the question is constitutively about it", so human judgment is the right anchor for a question about harm to persons, but only when the judging office does not move when the state pushes on it.

**Refuter:** exhibit a rights regime whose sole check is its own legislature or executive and show, by a record of reversed outcomes under political pressure, that this check still satisfies Fig 2 :29. If it does, the anchor test does not separate the cases.

### F3 - The checker position, not the quantity of law, is the load-bearing difference

Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
Fig 10 :43: "Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking. Never a player, never a proxy, never the author of a verdict or an atom."

In D1 the state stands **outside** two private parties. It is not a player, so it can verify. In D2 the state is a potential party, and the internal affairs unit, the ministerial inspectorate or the commercially oriented court is often authored by the same apparatus. It can reconstruct the claim because it wrote the claim. This is Fig 2 :13 to :15 written large: "no anchor; each updates on the others", "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence". Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

World source: the Wikipedia entry on judicial independence (citing Hamilton, *Federalist No. 78*) gives "The complete independence of the courts of justice is particularly essential in a limited constitution", and its lead defines the principle as courts "should not be subject to improper influence from those branches or from private or partisan interests". That is the checker-position condition arrived at in political science and law, with no reliance on the framework.

**Refuter:** show a case where the state is a party to the dispute and its own internal checker produces verdicts against it at the same rate as an external checker, under comparable caseloads. Then non-access is not necessary and F3 fails.

### F4 - The dependency chain breaks at the objective link in D2

Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"

D1 has an objective with an instrument: recovery, delay, cost. D2's formal objective, "equal protection" written into a statute, is a declared meaning until something can be violated and measured. The middle link of Fig 3 (having terms for what a situation affords and being able to say what counts as progress) is where chains usually break, because it has neither obvious failure nor obvious test. Spending reform effort on court buildings or sentencing guidelines is effort above the break.

**Refuter:** name a rights regime that has an instrumented objective (for example a published, independently audited rate of substantiated coercion complaints with a fixed denominator) and still fails to protect persons at the same rate as a regime without one. Then the objective link is not the break.

### F5 - The vocabulary can accumulate rights and cannot carry a refusal

Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"
Fig 12 :140 (amendment): "it cannot write that any of those is false"
Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"

The seventh bond set can write breach → judgment → execution, can write damages as subtraction, can write a threshold comparison between two claims. It cannot write "the state's own act in this case is false" unless a unary negation is available. The fifth gap in Fig 5 is exactly this: a term is owed that refuses another rather than explaining a gap. In a regime where the checker is the author of the verdict, the refusal cannot be carried, so the only available move is to add another right, another guideline, another commission. Fig 5 :55: "What it removes is not a term but a term’s standing to be composed over." Nothing is deleted; the reach falls.

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." A checker that can refuse the state needs an instrument that is already returning something, such as complaint data, inspection records or a free press. Adding a statute does not build one.

**Refuter:** show a legal vocabulary that, without any external checker, successfully carries a refusal of its own state's act and records it as a standing term (not a one-off reversal). Then the missing unary operator is not the constraint.

### F6 - The two conditions are a union, and the intersection is the hard part

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."
Fig 9 :58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"

Commercial courts, digital registries and secured-transaction reform clear the contract-only region. They leave standing the case of the employee who signed a contract under threat, the tenant who cannot complain because the landlord is the local official, the debtor who is also a target of discrimination. That is not a contract case with a rights add-on; it is a specific compound with its own loop. *The Library of Babel* essay (2026-10-02) names the corresponding error: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

**Refuter:** show that the two conditions form a partition, that is, no agent in the observed state is simultaneously a contracting party and a person exposed to coercion or discrimination. Then the union diagnosis is wrong and one lever may clear the whole region.

### F7 - Where nothing registers, there is no edge to extend

Fig 6 :34 to :35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

A state with no complaint channel, no independent press and no labor inspection returns no discrimination cases. That is not evidence of no discrimination; it is a closed channel. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" and :28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch". The search question (formula step 7) is SEARCHED first, INWARD second (sharpen the complaint instrument), OUTWARD third (import a frame that already holds the measurement). They are distinct: more searching of official records is SEARCHED and adds nothing if the channel is closed.

**Refuter:** exhibit a state with no independent complaint channel whose official records nevertheless register and trend coercion cases accurately against an external gold-standard dataset. Then the no-proto-instrument claim is wrong.

### F8 - Capability lives in the habitat, so reforming the agent alone does nothing

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"
Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
Fig 11 :52 to :53: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

Training more judges, or writing a better commercial code, improves the agent in isolation. The coercion residual is three steps out: an employment contract leads to economic dependence, which leads to housing and health exposure, which leads to the harm that the contract court never sees. The habitat must be enumerated (actors, conditions, relations), not composed from the statute book. Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

**Refuter:** show an agent-only intervention (judicial training alone, with no change in complaint channels, press, inspection or standing) that measurably reduces coercion and discrimination in a state that previously enforced contracts reliably. Then the habitat claim fails for this residual.

### F9 - Agreement among internal reviewers is variation, not a verdict

Fig 7 :35: "Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."

The cost of being wrong in D2 is a person's liberty or life, which is higher than in D1. By Fig 2 :42 the required independence should therefore be higher exactly where it is usually lower. Adding more internal review panels buys vocabulary, not evidence. The parallel RSI Trap essay is explicit: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

**Refuter:** show that adding internal reviewers moved a measured rights outcome in a way that survives an external audit, with no change in the reviewers' independence or instruments. Then the room critique does not bite here.

### F10 - Are they isomers? Same apparatus, different position of the checker

Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
Fig 12 :124: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

Both regimes use the same atoms: a rule, a coercive apparatus, a checker, an enforceable outcome. They differ in the relation. In D1 the checker is external to both parties; in D2 the checker must be external to the state, which is itself a party. Under Fig 12 :117 that is an isomer reading, with the bond carrying the difference. Under the union reading of F6 the two are also overlapping conditions. These are different objects (the mechanism versus the question), so no strain. The falsifier is in `06_isomers_and_order.md`: if rights protection requires an atom with no analogue in commercial enforcement, the isomer claim fails and only the functional-group reading stands.

**Refuter:** show that the rights case uses an atom absent from the contract case (for example an external monitor with no counterpart in commercial courts), so the atom sets differ. Then it is not an isomer and the bond is not the whole difference.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A state's own internal checker, with no external check, produces verdicts against the state at a rate statistically indistinguishable from an external checker, under comparable caseloads (breaks F2 and F3).
2. A legal vocabulary that lacks an external refusal seat carries a standing refusal of its own state's act (breaks F5).
3. Contract-enforcement and personal-protection outcomes form a partition with no overlapping cases, and one lever clears both (breaks F6).
4. An agent-only reform, with no change to complaint channels, press, inspection or standing, moves the measured coercion residual (breaks F8).
5. The per-slot residual collapses into the aggregate, so a high global rule-of-law score always implies a live personal-protection slot (breaks F1).

If any fires, the checker-position shape is wrong and the claim is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the differential and its refuter, not with a theory of institutions. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. Refuse the aggregate as a witness. Use it only to decide where to look (Fig 9 :60).
3. Describe the union before choosing any foreign frame; otherwise every institutional import will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27 to :28).
4. Treat external checker independence as the load-bearing design variable, and price it against the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
5. Do not send a commercial-court manual as the remedy. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator: the residual, the shape and the refuter.
6. Record the lineage and the stop. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32); the particular institution is an import that needs provenance and a shadow test.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: the differential is per slot, the two conditions are a union, the load-bearing variable is the position of the checker relative to the case, and the rights regime needs a refusal operation the contract regime does not. What does not survive is any claim that a particular court, commission or statute is the answer. Strains after re-census: none hidden; one live gap, the unnamed institution.
