## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks for the cause of a **differential inside one state**: reliable enforcement of private agreements (a creditor can recover) alongside unreliable protection of persons (a worker, tenant, minority or detainee has no effective remedy against coercion or discriminatory treatment). Option A treats this as one institution that works in one place and fails in another, so more of the same law should fix both. Option B treats it as two domains with different anchors and different checker positions, so a lever that improves contract enforcement need not move the personal-protection residual at all. The answer is bound as the ordinary term "checker position": where the checker stands relative to the case it must decide. What an answer would look like is a shape, namely a condition on that position that separates the two outcomes, plus a stated refuter. Anything less is a label, not an answer.
