# What "shape" means for this question

Sources read 2026-11-23. The eight framework essays are by Isaiah N and are listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`; the three actually quoted here were opened this session. The canonical figure files are local.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 to :135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0056 a **shape** is therefore the condition any answer must satisfy, stated before an institution is named. The shape is: *reliable personal protection requires a checker positioned outside the case it decides, with an instrument that registers harm and a vocabulary that can refuse the state's own act.* A named ombudsperson, a particular court, a specific inspection regime: those are names. They are not licensed by the figures.

A crucial scoping correction, kept visible: the README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Here the atoms (courts, statutes, coercive apparatus, checkers) are already held. What is missing is their **arrangement and position**, which Fig 12 :117 treats as decisive: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." So the shape is not a claim about a missing primitive; it is a claim about the bond. My earlier draft said "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any institution in every context. That overstatement is narrowed here and in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, note the new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- The same essay names the bundle failure directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is why the census in `01` begins with a union, not with a search for a single cause.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The effect terms here are "checker position" and "registered harm", not "rule of law" or "state capacity", both of which already smuggle in a frame.
- Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (listed and read for the exemplar, quoted there): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question: if the shape is true, a state with strong commercial enforcement but no independent complaint channel will show a live contract slot and a silent personal-protection slot, even after rights statutes are added.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means a measured outcome, not an index and not a legal declaration.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. If an institutional design is proposed, its provenance and the gap it explains must be recorded; otherwise "convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents" and only the record separates them.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) supplies the instrument rule: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A state can publish a rights statute, a complaint form and an annual report, all of which read as "protection", from sources as different as a genuine independent checker and a ministry press office. The reading does not determine the source. "What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." So the next move is an instrument that discriminates sources, not another argument about which label is correct.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Two specific meterisms in this question:

- **Low complaint volume.** It can mean low coercion or a closed channel. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 10 :28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch."
- **High rule-of-law score.** It can mean genuinely reliable enforcement across slots, or a high contract slot averaged over a dead rights slot. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Both are source questions. Both need an instrument that can discriminate, which is why F7 and F8 in `01` are about instruments and habitat, not about better arguments.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What would an answer look like before it is named?** A statement of the necessary position of the checker relative to the case, plus the conditions under which that position can be occupied (an instrument that registers harm; a refusal operation; independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong). That is what `07_the_tether_answer.md` gives.

**Does the framework license naming the checking institution?** No. It licenses the constraint, not the design. Fig 9 :32 again: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The particular court, commission or inspectorate is an import. Until it has a source gate, a shadow test and a ground reading, it carries zero evidentiary weight.

**Is this a gap?** For the institution, yes, and it is stated plainly rather than hidden: no figure line names it. For the shape, no. Six figures bear directly on the checker-position constraint (Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 3, Fig 5, Fig 9, Fig 10) with low overlap between their routes, so the shape is triangulated within the framework. Within-framework agreement is not world corroboration; see `11_audit.md`.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known constitutional scholars who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question they report on.
