## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusion, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** The differential between a live contract slot and a dead personal-protection slot. Arity: two conditions plus a shared state apparatus; the two overlap wherever a contracting party is also a person exposed to coercion. Symmetry: asymmetric, because one case has an anchor that does not update on opinion and the other has a declared meaning. Scale: first order inside a case, second order where the checker must itself be checked. Union, not partition.
- **Domain 1, commercial enforcement.** Actors: two parties, court, enforcement office, market. Currency: claims and recoveries. Anchor: payment or seizure, nameable.
- **Domain 2, personal protection.** Actors: person, coercer, rights regime, inspectorate, press. Currency: formal right versus effective remedy. Anchor: absent if offered as the state's own declaration, present if an external check is named. Not a domain until that check is named.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: observers, index builders, journalists. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.

Order by dependency: register harm, bind a travelling term, instrument the objective (break), plan with an external checker, settle and carry. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and Fig 10 (verifier non-access), I derived from Fig 3's dependency chain and Fig 9's union split. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap within the framework, but still one evidence pool. Agreement here is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 (anchor invariance), Fig 9 :56 (union), Fig 10 :42 and :43 (checker non-access), Fig 12 :117 (bond difference).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: the differential is per slot; the checker position is load-bearing; the two conditions are a union; the institution is not named.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an attempt to name a concrete institutional answer, for example "install an external oversight office with subpoena power and published complaint rates".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Anchor invariance.** A named office can be captured, and then it moves when the state pushes on it, so it ceases to be an anchor under Fig 2 :29. The shorthand's "external checker" is a position, not a guaranteed person.
- **Instrument-from-worse-instrument.** Fig 6 :32 forbids specifying the instrument from a description; the office must grow from something already returning a signal.
- **Operationalisation of the slot signal.** The per-slot residual needs a denominator and independent audit before it can register; without them the aggregate swallows it again.
- **Provenance and shadow test.** The named office is an import. Without its source gate and the gap it explains, "convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents" (Fig 8 :50 to :52).

One thing survives cleanly: the constraint that a checker must be outside the case it decides, and that the two regimes share atoms under a different bond. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
