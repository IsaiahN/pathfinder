## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The bound is conditional on logical irreversibility, not on information as such.** Landauer's
   original abstract already separates *"logical functions that do not have a single-valued inverse"*
   from computing in general, and the 2021 retrospective reports Bennett's resolution: the demon is
   saved by *"the erasure of the information in the memory"*, not by the measurement. This is the
   world-side corroboration of the figure-derived partition in `01` F1. Overlap warning: these sources
   all sit in the same thermodynamics-of-information pool, so this is one witness, not several.
2. **The bound is a limit approached, not a value measured exactly.** The 2012 Nature letter says the
   heat *"saturates at the Landauer bound in the limit of long erasure cycles"*, and the 2025 review
   says *"the practical erasure processes dissipate much more heat than the LB."* A claim that a real
   device dissipates exactly kT ln2 would be a misreading. The honest statement is a lower bound
   approached quasistatically.
