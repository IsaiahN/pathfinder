## Effect statement of the question

The four verbs are not one question. Create, store, transmit and erase differ in logical symmetry: erase is many-to-one (logically irreversible) while the other three can be one-to-one and reversible in principle. The residual is the gap between information as an abstract measure (Shannon bits) and the physical carrier that must pay for each operation. Option A: all four operations carry an unconditional energy-per-bit price. Option B: only erase has an unconditional thermodynamic floor; the other three can be reversible, with costs arising from noise, finite time and maintenance. What each leads to: A cannot accommodate logical reversibility and is contradicted by the shaded compute literature; B yields a checkable per-operation resource ledger and a stated refuter. The Tether derives B as a shape, and refuses to name the numeric floor from the figures.
