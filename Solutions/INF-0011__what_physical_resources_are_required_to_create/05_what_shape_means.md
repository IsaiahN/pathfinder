## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books* (Isaiah N, Adventures in ML / Substack,
2026-10-02, opened 2026-11-23):

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive
> enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A shape narrows the class of acceptable answers without filling the slot. For INF-0011 the shape is:
four operations, asymmetric, only one with an unconditional price, that price paid from a spent
gradient and leaving a remainder. The number kT ln2 is not part of the shape; it is a name supplied by
the literature.

The same essay gives the stopping rule and the union warning:

> *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

The second sentence is the direct warrant for splitting create / store / transmit / erase in `01`

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what
   the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* The
   erasure bound is a name licensed this way (Berut 2012, read 2026-11-23), and its provenance is
   recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. It is not credited to the figures.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. Binding Shannon's H onto the figure term Spectrum is a binding of a
   held atom, not an import that adds one: Fig 6 :26 *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame
   gains access."*

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, Adventures in ML / Substack, 2026-08-28, opened
2026-11-23) gives the instrument rule:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*

Applied here: a calorimeter reading of heat dissipated does not by itself name the source of the
cost. Several physical mechanisms could produce the same reading. Reporting the figure term Gradient
as the source of a measured heat flow would be the metamerism error. The instrument that would
discriminate is the one that tracks the free-energy gradient, not merely the joules.

## 5. Q - Does the framework return the number of joules?

**No.** The six terms are space, time, energy (states) and spectrum, gradient, network (structures).
Energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14); Gradient is *"a difference that
can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22); together they compose a flow (Fig 13 :76). That composition yields the
form of the price, not its magnitude. The magnitude requires Boltzmann's constant, a temperature and
an experiment, none of which is a figure term. Asking the framework for joules is asking a shape
machine for a name. The correct answer is a gap plus a direction to the ground.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried from the exemplar's recorded reading, not
re-fetched this run): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can
fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the
ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known names
who agree with the ledger is the named collapse, not corroboration. The right ask is a budget to try
the shape against an experiment.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable
output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM
output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal
consistency against the figure text. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as
such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The only ground reading cited is the published
erasure experiment, and it is cited as world corroboration with provenance, never as a figure ruling.
