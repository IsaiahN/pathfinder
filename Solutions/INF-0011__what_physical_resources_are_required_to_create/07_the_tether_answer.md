# The Tether's answer: physical resources for information operations

**Mode: `specified` on the per-operation ledger, `general` on the structural shape, nothing `grounded`
on the Tether side.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against
the figure text (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The four verbs are not one question. Create, store, transmit and erase differ in logical symmetry:
erase is many-to-one, the others can be one-to-one. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union
rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by
object slot, not aggregated."* Option A (one price for all four) cannot accommodate logical
reversibility. Option B (only erase has an unconditional floor) yields a checkable ledger. The Tether
derives B as a shape and leaves the numbers to the ground.

## 2. The shape: a per-operation ledger

**1. Create.** A new record is production of a state in a bounded region. Fig 12 :76: *"A produces B;
B did not exist before"* (⇒ production). Fig 13 :62: *"no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region
to make isolated mean anything."* Resource: a distinguishable state in a bounded region, and work to
set it **if the prior state is unknown**. If the prior state is already the target state, setting it
costs nothing in the reversible limit. There is no logical kT ln2 floor for create alone.

**2. Store.** Storage needs a state that persists across time and a barrier that keeps it distinct
against thermal noise. Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a
structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can
be spent"* (the Gradient used as a held barrier over space). Fig 13 :68: *"finiteness in a finite region
is what makes the counting terminate."* Resource: a bounded region, a barrier large against kT, and
maintenance actions in a real finite-temperature memory. In the ideal static case there is no ongoing
power supply. Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
The bit count and the maintenance actions do not add.

**3. Transmit.** Influence travels between regions. Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence
travels"* (Network). Resource: a carrier, a channel, and energy to launch or regenerate a signal in a
noisy channel. **The figures do not set an energy-per-bit floor here.** That sub-question is a `gap`
(Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*). Shannon's coding theorems
describe how many bits a channel carries, not what energy that costs; binding Shannon H onto Spectrum
adds no atom (Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*).

**4. Erase.** The only logically irreversible operation: a many-to-one reset. Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and
a gradient compose a flow."* Fig 13 :14: *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Energy has no
direction by itself). Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"* (Gradient supplies direction).
Fig 13 :71: *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step."* Resource: a
free-energy gradient to spend, a state to reset, and a bath to take the remainder as heat. Fig 13 :65:
*"no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder."* This is the sole
operation with an unconditional floor, and that floor is a shape, not a number from the figures.

**5. The cycle.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides
whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* A demon that measures but never erases
has an open loop. Erase closes it, and the bath is the part that survives the cycle: Fig 12 :119:
*"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* (Analogy limit: the bath is only restored ideally.)

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Shannon entropy and thermodynamic entropy are the same quantity. At most a binding.
- Not that the figures produce kT ln2. That is a world result with provenance (Georgescu 2021; Berut
  2012), reported as secondary.
- Not that create, store or transmit have an erasure-like floor. Their costs are apparatus costs.
- Not a number for the energy per bit of reversible transmission. That is a `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles the ledger.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A calorimeter that tracks the free-energy gradient, not just joules, would name the source of the
erasure cost (Hard Problem is About Source, opened 2026-11-23: *"The answer is unreached until a better
instrument exists."*). Concretely:

- **Erasure floor test:** does reset of a one-bit memory approach the bound only in the quasistatic
  limit? (World sources already report yes; this is a check of the figure-derived shape.)
- **Symmetry test:** can create, store and transmit be demonstrated with vanishing dissipation in the
  reversible limit? A nonzero floor for any of them breaks the partition.
- **Transmission gap:** does a reversible channel have a measurable energy-per-bit floor at the
  appropriate limit? If so, the gap in D2 closes; if not, the gap stands.
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive Energy or Gradient from the other five; show bits and actions are one
  currency; exhibit a closed memory cycle with no reset term.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give one "cost of information" and does not claim the figures set kT ln2. It
> splits the question into four asymmetric operations. Only erase is logically many-to-one, so only
> erase carries an unconditional floor: it spends a free-energy gradient and leaves the remainder as
> heat in a bath. Create is production of a state in a bounded region, store is a held barrier above
> the thermal noise, and transmit is a network effect whose energy price the figures do not set; those
> three can be reversible in principle, with costs coming from noise, finite time and maintenance.
> Four sub-questions are declared gaps, and the kT ln2 number is reported as a published result with
> provenance, not as a figure output. What the answer offers is the shape any candidate must have,
> falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gaps. The four slots stay
> open until the ground settles them.
