## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to

condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 13 :14, :22, :62, :65, :71, :74-76 and

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: four asymmetric slots; erase alone priced from a

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete operational reading: a one-bit memory in a bath, the Landauer
   reset protocol, a barrier above kT, a noisy channel, the kT ln2 number.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **numeric floor**, kT ln2, which is a world name carried by experiment, not a figure term;
- the **gap status** of reversible transmission, which the shorthand keeps open but which a concrete
  protocol would be tempted to fill by analogy from erase;
- the **catalyst analogy**, where the bath is restored only ideally at cycle end, not in the strong
  chemical sense.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the four slots and the asymmetry of erase, with the gradient
spent and the remainder dumped. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured
numerically, because no ground reading exists for the whole ledger; it is recorded qualitatively as
the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the condensed formula as identical to the figure
set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
