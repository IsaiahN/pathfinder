## VERDICT (from the figures)

The four operations are four slots and only erase has an unconditional thermodynamic floor. It is
paid by spending a Gradient composed with Energy into a flow, leaving a remainder as heat in a bath.
Creation is production of a state in a bounded region; storage is a held barrier above the thermal
noise; transmission is a Network effect whose energy price the figures do not set. Those three can be
reversible in principle, with costs from apparatus, noise and maintenance. The cycle is second-order
and closes only when the memory is reset. Four sub-questions are `gap` (the numeric floor, the
transmission energy per bit, real memory maintenance power, and whether Shannon and thermodynamic
entropy are identical). The kT ln2 value is reported as a published world result with provenance, not
as a figure output. Mode `specified` on the ledger, `general` on the shape, nothing `grounded` on the
Tether side.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the slot split, the figure lines and the
  refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence
  of outside readers by sending a single-number conclusion instead of a four-slot generator. Fig 2
  :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by
  leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four slots can be re-priced separately without touching the six-term set. A
  gap can be closed by an instrument without withdrawing the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by
  a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A calorimeter that tracks the free-energy gradient
  rather than bare joules would name the source of the erasure cost; a demonstration that create,
  store or transmit carry the same reversible-limit floor would break the partition; a derivation of
  Energy or Gradient from the other five terms would break the substrate; a figure re-census that
  prices bits in joules would close the D2 gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as
  `candidate`.
