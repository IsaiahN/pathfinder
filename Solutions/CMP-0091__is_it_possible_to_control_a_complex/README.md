## The question restated as an effect

The residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|`, measured per slot `s` and never averaged (Fig 1 :19). The question asks whether a controller whose library `Γ` does not contain the full internal structure of the system (all atoms, all typed bonds, the full closure) can still drive `o'` toward a target at the ground.

Two readings are being decided:

- **Reading A (full understanding required):** control is possible only when the controller holds a complete internal model. What it leads to: control is deferred until a complete model is built, which for an open complex system is typically unreachable in principle or at budget.
- **Reading B (contact and lever suffice):** control is a property of controller-and-habitat contact. What it leads to: an anchor that fixes the target, an enumerated habitat on which a lever can be introduced, and a channel that keeps `R` per slot measured. The internal model is one instrument among several, not a prerequisite.

An answer would look like: a set of conditions each of which is necessary and jointly sufficient for control, stated in effect terms, with a refuter that would break each condition. That shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and is allowed to accrue a residual like anything else, per the formula's "Direction" rule.

## Verdict in one line

**Yes, conditionally.** Full internal understanding is not necessary. Control is possible when a ground anchor fixes the target, the habitat is enumerable enough to find a lever, and the channel keeps per-slot residuals honest. Where no anchor or no lever exists, the honest verdict is `gap` or `unreachable`, not a universal yes. Mode `specified`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label: Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Gathering more agreeable readers adds vocabulary, not a ground.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict: selection for likely agreement manufactures the shared evidence pool that Fig 2 :40 calls *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence: Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
