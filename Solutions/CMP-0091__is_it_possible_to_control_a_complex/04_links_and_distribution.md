## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Structural controllability is topology-first, not full-dynamics-first.** Liu, Slotine and Barabasi (2011) identify driver nodes from network structure and report that the number of driver nodes is determined mainly by the degree distribution. That is the world analogue of F6: you can say a great deal about control from the arrangement without holding every internal dynamic. It does not name which lever for a given system, which is why the figure-side verdict stays a shape.
2. **Control without dynamics is an active engineering result, with stated conditions.** Baggio, Bassett and Pasqualetti (2021) construct optimal controls from input-output data without knowledge of the network dynamics, while explicitly noting that reconstructing the full network matrix from partial measurements is impossible and that the data must be rich enough. That is a precise version of the figure claim: contact and independent experiments replace a complete internal model, but they do not remove the need for a ground check.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a target and a lever sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - later agreement from the same reader is a conversation, never a second witness.
- **Fig 7 :13** - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* - no mass send.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim, (2) the three verbatim figure lines, (3) the pre-registered refuter, (4) ask what they would count as a failure, (5) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## Stage 3, opt-in only

If the reader asks for the method, offer the formula and require that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode before running it. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that a simulated output *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"*; the remedy is the pre-registered falsifier, not more trust.
