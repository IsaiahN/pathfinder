## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the verdict; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method for them to reuse, with their own ground and their own refuter.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Send the characterisation of the residual, the union claim and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Only if they are curious and only after their stage-1 verdict is recorded, offer the figure links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they have now seen the source.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it, and labels the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The pre-registered refuter in `01` is the test. If it fires, the offer stops and the claim is retracted.
