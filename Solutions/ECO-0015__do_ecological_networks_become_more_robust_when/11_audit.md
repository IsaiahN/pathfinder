## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what an interaction structure predicts and what a perturbation returns. Arity: three-place (web structure, perturbation, outcome), indexed by slot and ordered in time, so it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond. Symmetry: "robust" is a threshold relative to a perturbation regime, not an absolute magnitude, so the comparison is relative. Scale: loop, local neighbourhood and web sequence; the transform between scales is lossy.
- **Domain 1, interaction habitat.** Actors: species, links, consumers, resources, conditions. Currency: interaction strength and energy flow. Anchor: measured persistence and extinction in a perturbation experiment. Question type: a habitat enumerated from the world, not composed (Fig 11).
- **Domain 2, loop composition.** Actors: links, signs, loops, biomass pyramids. Currency: loop gain, sign, arrangement. Anchor: measured return time and damping. Question type: composition inside a closure, with isomers under rearrangement.
- **Domain 3, measurement channel.** Actors: field strength estimates, metrics, instruments. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Question type: channel maintenance.
- **Domain 4, novelty.** Actors: invaders, new perturbations, differing closures. Currency: reach under change. Anchor: measured persistence after the novel perturbation. Question type: import.
- **X1, strength as intrinsic valence.** Actors: a magnitude. Currency: none independent of the residual. Anchor: none nameable. Not a domain yet.
- **X2, complexity as sole cause.** Actors: species count and connectance. Currency: topological indices. Already inside domains 1 and 2, so absorbed.

Order by dependency: measure links, name what a link affords, state what counts as progress for a slot, choose the perturbation, carry the result. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :27 (per-slot residual, frame-internal quantities), Fig 9 :4-5, :54-57 (split, union), Fig 11 :49-51 (valence relative to residual), Fig 12 :101, :121 (flat case, cycle sign) and Fig 13 :48 (load-bearing).

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a literal answer to the question as posed, "networks are robust when interactions are weak" or "strong interactions are necessary for stability".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union must be split rather than averaged**, which the binary question erases (Fig 9 :55);
- **strength has no valence independent of the residual**, which the words "weak" and "strong" as predicates of robustness erase (Fig 11 :49);
- the **anchor is a per-slot perturbation experiment**, which the widespread practice of scoring stability with an assumed matrix erases (Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :29);
- the **specific rule is a gap**, which the literature's appetite for a universal threshold erases (Fig 9 :32).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a few strong couplings can be load-bearing and many weak links damp oscillations and lower loop weight, with the sign of the loop deciding closure. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
