## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form of an answer we can state before we can name the essential species. It is the per-function, habitat-enumerated, removal-tested separator with its cascade and failure modes. It constrains any acceptable answer: an answer that names a species without a function, a removal and a habitat has not met the shape. "Pisaster is essential" is a name; "for intertidal diversity, removal of the top predator collapses the space-holder set, measured against a control, with the effect cascading through mussel dominance" is the shape filled by a provenanced result.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the function failure in effect terms (a measured drop below a threshold), not "loss of charisma" or "loss of iconic value."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what they would expect to see if the separator were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." In ecology this is the point at which enumerating the habitat and testing removals is the same activity as answering the question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." In ecology this is the removal or exclusion result plus a function measurement that does not update on reviewer opinion.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A species name borrowed from a removal experiment in another habitat is an import; it needs provenance and a shadow test (does it explain a gap here?) before it travels.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six substrate terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming a species as *the* essential one turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a removal test is the right discriminator

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own adequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The analogue here: observing that a function persists with a species present cannot tell you whether that species is the source of the function or merely correlated with it. Several community compositions can give the same function reading (the insurance hypothesis makes this precise). What is owed is a discriminator, a removal or exclusion that separates sources, not a name read off a projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Sections 1 to 4 answered, with the correction kept visible

**Q: Does the framework name the essential species?** No. It returns a shape. A particular species is licensed only by a ground-settled removal result, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Q: Does treating "essential" as a removal test commit a forbidden error?** No, but the first-pass wording did. I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name" any species, full stop. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. A species in an enumerated habitat is not a missing primitive; it is a candidate binding from the ecology frame onto a held atom (the network atom plus the enumerated habitat field). Naming it is provenance, not forbidden invention. What remains wrong is claiming that the *framework* derives the name; it licenses the removal result, not the species identity.

Both the original and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the exemplar's §5b correction.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The conservation-bias literature (Guénard 2025, Colléony 2017, both in `10_source_ledger.csv`) is the world's demonstration of this: charisma funds attention, but funding was not associated with population recovery. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that a species is essential is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Falsifier for this file

If a reader can show that the framework's "shape, not a name" rule governs held bindings, not only missing primitives, then §5's correction is wrong and the stricter reading is restored: no species may be named at all from this derivation. That is a live, checkable condition, recorded rather than hidden.
