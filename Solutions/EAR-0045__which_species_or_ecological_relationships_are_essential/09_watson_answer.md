## QUESTION

Option A: return a standing list of essential species and relationships. Option B: return a per-function, habitat-enumerated, removal-tested separator and declare the literal list a gap. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The failure of "essential" to stabilise across functions and habitats; different removal rules work on different subsets, so disagreement does not shrink with effort. Arity: plural function slots. Symmetry: asymmetric, since a species can be a benefactor to one function and a malfactor to another; a presence-function correlation is symmetric and cannot carry "essential." Scale: local habitat with cascades several steps out, and across the scale membrane. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the function slot.** Actors: primary production, nutrient cycling, pollination, decomposition, carbon storage, resilience. Currency: a measured function level. Anchor: an independent measurement that does not update on reviewer opinion (Fig 2 :29). This is a per-slot partition boundary; the anchor is nameable per slot, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: species, relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency), conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain). Currency: contact and effect on the function. Anchor: the world, enumerated not composed (Fig 11 :42, :46, :47). A union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), not a partition.
- **Domain 3, the cascade.** Actors: second- and third-order effects. Currency: influence travelling through relations. Anchor: the same per-slot ground reached indirectly. Dependency chain outward (Fig 3).
- **Domain 4, the intervention.** Actors: managers, restorers, engineers. Currency: introduction, not exclusion. Anchor: the ground still decides (Fig 11 :20-22). Asymmetric seat (Fig 11 :61-62).
- **Order by dependency.** Name the function and its anchor before enumerating the habitat (Fig 9 :27-28); enumerate before tracing the cascade (Fig 11 :43); trace the cascade before choosing an intervention (Fig 3 :19). The objective link is often unmeasured (Fig 3 :27), so work lands above the break.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :51 (no standing list of good actors) and Fig 12 :123 (a functional group fixes the outcome) govern different objects, actor valence relative to a residual versus arrangement kind; re-census dissolves the apparent contradiction. The habitat is not a seventh substrate term (Fig 11 :73). No figure line selects a species name; that is a gap, not a strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The literal standing list is refused and declared a `gap`; the separator survives. Verdicts per domain are separate: per function slot, `specified` and triangulated; habitat enumeration, `specified` and triangulated; valence and no-standing-list, `specified` and triangulated; cascade, `specified` and triangulated; removal-plus-threshold test, `specified` and single-source on the figure side; intervention and silent failures, `specified` and triangulated. Structural frame: union, not partition; split before searching. The framework returns a shape, not a name. A species name is a provenanced binding from the ecology frame, licensed by a removal result in a named habitat against a non-updating function measurement, and is never derived by the figures. This package is simulated and ungrounded, candidate until a removal result pays at the ground.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, keeps verdicts per slot, records refuters before the conclusion and keeps the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a charismatic species list instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A wrong species binding is removable without touching the separator; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already refuse the binding. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no part of the habitat or the held atom set has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A removal or exclusion with a non-updating function measurement would name a slot; an invariant essential list across functions would break the union and valence verdicts; a symmetric correlation shown to determine essentialness with no removal would break the ordered-test verdict; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground would break the capability claim; a subtractable habitat or a fully enumerable cascade in a novel habitat would break the intervention verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` and the literal list stays a declared `gap`.
