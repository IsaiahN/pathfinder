## 3. Wider literature that actually bears on the claim

Each of these was opened and is recorded with author, venue, year and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They are secondary corroboration only; the figures decide.

1. **Paine 1966 and 1969, keystone removal experiment.** Paine removed the starfish *Pisaster ochraceus* from rocky intertidal plots and left a control. Species diversity fell from about 15 to 8 after one year and to a near monoculture after five. Paine's own wording (Paine 1969, full text at the Berkeley PDF returned by the search): "These individual populations are the keystone of the community's structure, and the integrity of the community and its unaltered persistence through time, that is, stability, are determined by their activities and abundances. They may be unimportant as energy transformers." This is the world's clearest instance of the subtraction test: "essential" is established by removal, and a species can be structurally decisive while being an unimportant energy transformer. Corroborates F6 and Fig 11 :47-51.
2. **Jones, Lawton and Shachak 1994, "Organisms as ecosystem engineers", Oikos 69:373-386.** Full abstract opened: "Ecosystem engineers are organisms that directly or indirectly modulate the availability of resources to other species, by causing physical state changes in biotic or abiotic materials. In so doing they modify, maintain and create habitats." This is the paper Fig 11 :65 cites by name. It corroborates the claim that the essential actor is often a habitat modifier, not a trophic link, and that the relation is a process, not a kind. It also warns that the same move with the cascade unenumerated gives cane toads and kudzu (Fig 11 :65).
3. **Yachi and Loreau 1999, "Biodiversity and ecosystem productivity in a fluctuating environment: the insurance hypothesis", PNAS 96:1463-1468.** Open access via PMC: "biodiversity insures ecosystems against declines in their functioning because many species provide greater guarantees that some will maintain functioning even if others fail." This is a genuine low-overlap corroborator for the union and per-slot verdict: redundancy means no single species is essential for the variance function, while a dominant competitor can be essential for the mean. It also explains why "essential" flips with the function (F1, F3).
4. **Loreau 2021, "Biodiversity as insurance: from concept to measurement and application", Biological Reviews.** Opened: the theory separates a "buffering effect" (reduced temporal variability) from a "performance-enhancing effect" (increased temporal mean), and notes "exciting future challenges include linking the effects of biodiversity on ecosystem functioning and stability, incorporating multiple functions and feedbacks." Corroborates the per-function split (F1, F9) from within ecology.
5. **May 1972, "Will a large complex system be stable?", Nature 238:413-414.** Opened via the review literature and a PDF: complexity tends to destabilize community dynamics, with the stability condition often written as a threshold on species number, connectance and interaction strength. Corroborates the network and threshold reading (F6) from mathematics, and warns that an undirected union has no surplus (Fig 8 :19-21) without a described residual.
6. **Guénard et al. 2025, "Limited and biased global conservation funding means most threatened species remain unsupported", PNAS.** Opened: "The focus on charismatic species to generate funding and public support has led to inadequate support for a large majority of 'less-charismatic' but threatened species" and "Charismatic species are often poor indicators for range-restricted or more specialist taxa." A low-overlap corroborator from conservation behaviour for "no standing list" (Fig 11 :51) and "describe the residual before choosing a frame" (Fig 9 :27-28).
7. **Colléony et al. 2017, "Human preferences for species conservation: Animal charisma trumps endangered status", Biological Conservation 206:263-269.** Opened: "People did not donate more to the more endangered species in a conservation program" and "Charismatic species were more likely to be chosen." A second, genuinely low-overlap human-cognition witness for the same structural point; it is a different study from Guénard et al., not a restatement of it.
8. **Wikipedia, "Single point of failure" (engineering).** Opened: "A single point of failure (SPOF) is a part of a system that would stop the entire system from working if it were to fail." Low-overlap engineering analogue of the removal-and-threshold test (F6), and of the insurance result that redundancy removes single points of failure. An analogue only; an ecosystem is not a designed circuit and the figure verdict does not depend on it.

Overlap declaration: Paine via Nature Scitable, JSTOR Daily, the Estes 2016 obituary, the Berkeley PDF and HHMI poster is one evidence pool (one Paine experiment), not five witnesses. Jones 1994 and Jones 1997 plus the Lawton book chapter are one author stream, counted as one witness. Yachi and Loreau 1999, Loreau 2013, Loreau 2021 and Leary 2009 are one insurance-hypothesis pool. Guénard 2025, Colléony 2017 and the Mongabay/Bioscience commentaries are one conservation-bias pool with two genuinely different primary studies inside it. May 1972 and the engineering SPOF page are the two low-overlap frames. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons for the order:

- **Fig 4 :22-25 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending a full species list before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter; offer the sources after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which function they would anchor to; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against a function they can measure, **(2)** only if curious, read the framework that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on other functions. Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because a recipient who has absorbed the theory stops being an outside frame. Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22), but each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter of their own.
