## What is deliberately not here

- No personal email addresses of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors of the claim.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
