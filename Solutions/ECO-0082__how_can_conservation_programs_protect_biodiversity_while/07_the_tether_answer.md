# The Tether's answer on conservation, rights and local knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the named arrangement.** This is a shape and a route, not a site result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A conservation program must make a biodiversity ground register improvement while a rights-and-knowledge ground registers that people were not overridden. These are two conditions with different anchors, so the question is a union compound, not one dial with a right setting (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). A lever that clears a species count while displacing people leaves the intersection standing.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

1. **Enumerate the habitat with people in it.** Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The list includes actors, conditions and relations, read off the world, not off a categories template. Local people are in it. Conservation capability is a property of the program and the habitat together, not of the program alone (Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*).

2. **Keep the residual per site slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A regional compliance average is a live signal hidden in a global number near zero. Verdicts stay per site and per condition, never averaged into one score.

3. **Two anchors, one per condition.** The biodiversity half is settled by an outcome that does not change when the program changes its report. The rights half is settled by human judgment because that half of the question is about people: Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* A species count cannot settle a consent claim; a satisfaction survey cannot settle a population decline.

4. **Two independent knowledge frames.** Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Formal conservation science and local ecological knowledge have a surplus only where their evidence pools differ (Fig 8 :48: *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."*). Merging them into a single project report creates one frame with two names. Respecting knowledge therefore means preserving its independence and its provenance, so that agreement, when it comes, is triangulation rather than a shared pool talking (Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).

5. **Consent is a refusal term, prior by sequence.** Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. A participation score is a convention; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47). The refusal must be admitted before the prescription, because Formula step 2 routes REFUTED before MINT. "Prior" is a real `→` constraint (Fig 12 :71: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*), not an adjective.

6. **Exclusion is substitution, not removal.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* You can add an actor; you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact does not consult you (Fig 11 :61-62). The two silent failures are what the goal needed that is no longer reproduced and what you brought in that you did not choose (Fig 11 :68-69). Contact cascades, so a first-order boundary count can miss the material effect three steps out (Fig 11 :52).

7. **No standing friend or enemy list.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A community is neither intrinsically a threat nor intrinsically a guardian. Which it is depends on the residual and the site, and must be re-read when the residual changes.

8. **Transfer the method, never the recipe.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A governance form copied from one park is a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :25). What crosses is the diagnostic: enumerate, keep frames separate, refuse before mint, register both grounds per slot, measure the round-trip gap.

9. **The specific arrangement is a declared gap.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. No figure line chooses co-management, an ICCA, an FPIC protocol, a boundary or a benefit split. Those are world-frame bindings, recorded as such. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that community management always works, and not that every protected area is invalid. Both universal claims are refused by Fig 11 :49-51.
- Not that participation equals respect. A program-authored participation score is a convention that decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that all knowledge should be merged into one indicator. That destroys the surplus (Fig 8 :48).
- Not that a single institution, percentage or boundary follows from the figures. That is the F11 gap.
- Not that livelihood payments are compensation after exclusion. They are contact-changing levers (Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*).
- Not `grounded`. No site measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case, not the debate. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Independent biodiversity reading at each slot**, one that does not update on the program's own reports, so that the biodiversity ground is reachable (Fig 2 :29).
- **Independent rights judgment at each slot**, with a refusal seat the program did not author, so that the human anchor is not moved by the program's stake (Fig 10 :59-60).
- **Provenance for each knowledge frame**, recording which prediction came from which frame, so that convergence can be told apart from adoption (Fig 8 :52).
- **A source-discriminating reading of participation**, able to separate consent from co-option, elite capture and a program-authored report. Without it the instrument demonstrates its own inadequacy.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: a single lever clearing both conditions; an exclusion program with no substitution failures; two overlapping frames with the same surplus; post-hoc consent with equal refusal power; a biodiversity instrument settling a rights claim; a figure line naming an arrangement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The arrangement at a site depends on habitat enumeration and contact that have to be read off the world. The framework stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable (canonical README: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*). This derivation is a simulation, and the simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency and not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The verdict stays `candidate` until a ground pays for it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning conservation model. It says the question is two conditions with two different grounds, not one dial: a biodiversity outcome that does not move when the program rewrites its report, and a human judgment about rights that is legitimate because the question is about people. It says the habitat must be enumerated with local people in it, that their valence is relative to the site rather than fixed, and that excluding them substitutes a new habitat whose failures are invisible until the goal breaks. It says formal science and local knowledge are two frames whose extra reach exists only while they stay independent, and that consent is a refusal term that must come before the prescription, not a score recorded after it. What it offers is that shape, a set of refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The choice of arrangement stays open until a ground settles it.
