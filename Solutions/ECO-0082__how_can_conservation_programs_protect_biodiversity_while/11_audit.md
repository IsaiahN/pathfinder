- **Domain 2, livelihoods and rights.** Actors: communities, customary institutions, knowledge holders, staff, courts, consent procedures. Currency: rights, consent, benefits, trust, displacement. Anchor: human judgment, legitimate by Fig 2 :36 because the question is about people. Type: union, binds in the intersection with D1.

- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: enumerate before lever (Fig 11 :44); describe residual before frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle per slot before composition (Fig 12 :104).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-69 (habitat enumeration, valence, asymmetry, isolation), Fig 2 :29-42 (anchors, independence, drift), Fig 8 :45-53 (surplus, provenance) and Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: enumerate people into the habitat, two grounds, two independent frames, refusal before prescription, per-slot verdicts, no named arrangement.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete site program, for example a community co-management board with an independent biodiversity survey and a prior-consent protocol.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **legal versus contact distinction**, because a fence is legally available and the shorthand's "exclusion is unavailable" refers only to contact and habitat;
- the **independence measurement**, because "two frames" can be satisfied on paper by one staff team collecting both datasets, which loses the surplus;
- the **provenance test**, because convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the delivered report;
- the **specific arrangement**, which the shorthand refuses to name and which a real program must name at some point.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a structure must be enumerated off the world with people in it, and the two conditions have different anchors and must be judged separately. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no site reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
