## 5. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A full framework sent before a recipient has described a residual is a recording, not a generator.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before showing any of these links.
- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A journal's or a society's prestige is a budget to be tried, never a witness.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" means a later agreement from someone who has read the whole framework is not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the links and the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 6. The two-stage offer, restated

1. Validate the output against the ground. The ground here is an experiment on a named mineral slot, and the world sources already read are examples of such ground.
2. Only if curious, read the theory that produced the shape.
3. Use the loop on another problem, writing a fresh refuter and stating a fresh mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 2 and 3 only work if stage 1 survives. If a refuter in `02_outreach_email.md` fires, stop and retract for that slot.
