## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether an environmental non-encoded structure can persist through the reactions it accelerates, so that those reactions close into a network driven by geochemical energy before encoded enzymes exist. Option A: affirm that iron, sulfur, or other minerals could do this. Option B: refuse the claim as too bundled to answer. What each leads to: A gives a checkable three-part shape and a per-slot empirical verdict from the ground; B gives a split of the question into catalyst persistence, cycle closure and energy coupling, each with its own test. This run takes A at the world level and B at the figure level, and states where the ground, not the figures, supplied the yes.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide the shape and the tests, not the chemical fact. The figure verdict is: a positive answer must show a part that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119), a cycle whose output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121), and a spendable difference coupled to the network (Fig 13 :22); verdicts are per slot (Fig 1 :19); and the chemical fact is settled by the ground (Fig 2 :29). The world sources read here report exactly that shape for FeS, NiS, mackinawite and greigite in several settings, so the empirical answer is yes for those slots under those conditions, `grounded` by published experiments and not by this simulation. The general case across all minerals remains open; the complete reverse Krebs version is explicitly disputed. This is not a figure verdict about iron, and it is not a pooled verdict about minerals.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error can be found. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the full framework before their verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoided by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A slot-specific retraction removes only that slot's claim; no primitive has to be withdrawn and no other domain is touched. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already separate the figure shape from the world yes, so the two can be corrected independently.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled early-Earth experiment showing that the mineral is consumed stoichiometrically, that the reactions cannot close a cycle, or that no spendable difference is coupled, would reverse the verdict for that slot. Results superseding the FeS and NiS reports would reverse the empirical yes. Agreement among readers who have seen the framework is not such a result (Fig 2 :40).
