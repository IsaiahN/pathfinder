## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local canonical `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From "The Library of Babel" (fetched 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the answer itself is named. It constrains; it does not fill.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- Residual first: the effect statement in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 names persistence, closure and coupling before any mineral is discussed.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" is why the shape was written before the world search. Without it, any mineral would have seemed to fit after the fact.
- "The Library of Babel" describes the bootstrapping: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The shape is written as "persists through use, closes a cycle, couples to a spendable difference", not as a list of element symbols.
- The stop condition is the narrowed set, not a final proof. "The Library of Babel": "the finish line will also be the starting place".

## 3. The shape for ORI-0043

The framework's answer to "could minerals catalyze early metabolic reactions?" is not a name. It is the conjunction of three figure constraints:

| constraint | figure | verbatim line |
|---|---|---|
| survives its own use | Fig 12 :119 | "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." |
| closes a loop | Fig 12 :121 | "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." |
| rides a spendable difference | Fig 13 :22 | "a difference that can be spent" |

Anything satisfying these three is a candidate positive answer. Anything failing one is a specific negative, not a vague failure.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." In this question the anchor exists and has spoken: the FeS and NiS experiments in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming FeS is a binding from the geochemistry frame onto a slot the framework leaves open.

So "FeS and NiS" is not the Tether naming the answer. It is the chemistry frame reporting what its instruments returned. The distinction is recorded so a reader can tell convergent derivation from adopted import, which "look identical in the contents" and are separated only by the record.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I first read the canonical phrase as forbidding the naming of any particular mineral. That was an overreach.

- The phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in the library.
- The six-term substrate holds space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network (Fig 13). Iron and sulfur are not members of that set and are not "missing primitives" of it; they belong to the chemistry frame.
- Therefore naming FeS is a **binding from the world frame**, licensed by the ground if the experiments pay. It is not the framework inventing a seventh term, and it is not a forbidden name.

**Retracted as applied to mineral names.** Retained: the framework itself returns only the shape (survive use, close cycle, couple to gradient), and the specific mineral is the world's contribution. Both statements are kept visible so the correction can be audited.

## 6. Why this audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap" (fetched 2026-11-23): "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

This file, and every other file in this folder, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the chemical claim. The empirical yes is reported from published experiments that were read, not run, here.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From the same essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree with this shape would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` and let an experiment settle the slot.
