## The short answer

The figures do not decide whether iron or sulfur catalyzed early metabolism. That is an empirical fact settled by the anchor, which Fig 2 :29 calls "a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." The figures decide what a yes must look like: a part that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119), a reaction set that closes a cycle (Fig 12 :121), and a spendable difference that drives it (Fig 13 :22). The world sources read in this run report exactly that shape, so the empirical verdict is yes, `grounded` by those published experiments and not by this simulated derivation.
