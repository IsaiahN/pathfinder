## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's findings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three-place plus slot index: report, action, outcome, in a time order. Not symmetric; swapping management and outcome changes the object. Scale: one decision, the board and its committees, the firm across periods. The time order carries an ordered bond; "adequate" carries a threshold comparison. **Union**, because two different failures (moved anchor, authored decision) share symptoms but not bonds.
- **Domain 1, the maker.** Actors: management and the board pack. Currency: reports, forecasts, credibility. Anchor: none outside the maker's claims. This is the frame under check, not a witness domain.
- **Domain 2, the ground.** Actors: customers, cash, operations, regulators. Currency: realized outcomes. Anchor: outcomes that resist being pushed.
- **Domain 3, the checking seat.** Actors: the board and its committees. Currency: independence, conventions, rulings. Anchor: the ground kept reachable, with the seat's stake declared. The board is Seat 3 in the seat map, not a generic monitor.
- **Domain 4, witness frames.** Actors: external and internal audit, regulators, customer data, protected reporting. Currency: low-overlap evidence. Anchor: readings not drawn from management's pool.
- **Excluded:** board intuition about the CEO (no anchor outside the board's acquired model), and a single composite governance score (pooled readings, aggregation across slots).

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame; install a reading of the ground; range the report against it; rule with provenance; refresh the frame. The middle link (terms for what the situation affords, and what counts as progress) has no instrument.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and the seat map's four-seat dependency; the primary derived from Fig 2's anchor test and Fig 10's seat rules. Different starting figures, same boundaries, low topical overlap within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-34 (unmoved anchor, independence), Fig 10 :42-51 (verifier non-access, seat rule), Fig 9 :51, :54 (admissibility, union) and Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: keep an unmoved outcome reading, use low-overlap instruments, stay outside what you grade, author conventions, keep R per slot.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete board blueprint of the familiar kind: a stated number of independent directors, an audit committee, a monthly pack, a composite governance score.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down into that blueprint:

- the **unmoved-anchor test** is replaced by a headcount proxy;
- the **low-overlap condition** is replaced by "independent" as a label, which can mean merely not employed by the firm;
- the **admissibility rule** (maker evidence denied) is silently dropped when the pack grades reputation and tenure;
- the **declared-stake rule** disappears into a claim of objectivity;
- the **per-slot indexing** is lost when the blueprint reports one composite score.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a checker should not author what it grades, and a convergence of reports is not evidence when the reporters share a pool. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
