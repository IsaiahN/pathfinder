## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For BUS-0053, a **shape** is the narrowed form of a checking arrangement you can state before you can specify its implementation. It constrains: the anchor must not move; the checker must stay outside what it grades; the readings must be low-overlap; evidence about the maker is inadmissible. It does not fill: "60 per cent independent directors", "two committees", "monthly dashboards" are names, not shapes.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For boards this means describing the failure in effects (which report diverged from which outcome, in which slot, for how long) before naming a governance mechanism.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For boards, a name (a specific structure) is licensed only when a ground reading shows that this structure kept the channel open and the seat outside in a measurable case.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. FRC guidance and ISA 500 are such imports into this derivation, and they are recorded as imports with their provenance, not as figure-derived board rules.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Naming a board structure as *the* answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. The FRC's "comply or explain" model is instructive here: it deliberately refuses a single required structure and asks for an explanation instead (see `04_links_and_distribution.md`, section 1). That is closer to the figure's shape rule than a checklist is.

## 4. Why "trust the board" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it (Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*).

In board terms: a clean board pack, an unqualified audit opinion and a stable share price can all be produced by more than one underlying condition. Reading the source off the projection (for example, "a clean report means the board is monitoring well") is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, an instrument that distinguishes "management is right" from "management's report is merely consistent", not a reassurance.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A board that validates its own monitoring quality with its own monitoring metrics is the named failure.

## 5. Q2: What would license a particular monitoring instrument?

**Answered, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework licenses a specific instrument. The framework returns a shape. A particular instrument (external audit, a whistleblower line, customer-level churn data, site visits) is licensed only by a ground reading that shows it changed contact with the outcome, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The same limit applies to the board's own fringe benefits of the framework: it cannot name which director should hold which seat. Seat Map :200: *"The seat is not chosen; the position selects the occupant."* That is a structural rule about who may occupy a checking position, not an appointment recommendation.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming *any* concrete instrument in this solution. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The board's instruments (audit, independent readings, declared stakes) are not missing primitives; they are bindings from the auditing and governance frames onto the checking arrangement. What remains wrong is calling those bindings figure-derived, or claiming they move the wall in the sense of Fig 6. They are imports with provenance, recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Both the limit and its narrowing are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering well-known governance experts to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a ground to be tried against, as Fig 9 :51 permits evidence about the residual and denies evidence about its maker.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any board and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
