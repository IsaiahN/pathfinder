## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about board monitoring. Option A: treat the answer as a governance checklist (more independent directors, more committees, more reporting) and ask management for the data to run it. Option B: treat the two named failures as a union compound and derive the conditions under which a checker can range a report against an unmoved ground without becoming a player in the frame it grades. What each leads to: A spends independence on more readers of the same pack and can destroy the voluntary information flow the board needs; B yields a checkable shape, written refuters and an explicit stop where the specific instrument is not held. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot s: Γ is management's report, a is management's action, o' is the realized outcome, s is the decision area and period.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least three-place (report, action, outcome), indexed by slot and ordered in time, carrying a sequence bond and a threshold comparison. Symmetry: asymmetric; management and the ground are not interchangeable. Scale: single decision, board and committees, firm across periods. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the maker under check.** Actors: CEO, CFO, management, internal reporting, board pack. Currency: reported figures, forecasts, narrative, credibility. Anchor: none independent of the maker's claims; admitted only as the frame being checked, never as a witness.
- **Domain 2, the ground of outcomes.** Actors: customers, cash, operations, employees, regulators, markets. Currency: realized outcomes. Anchor: outcomes that do not move on being pushed (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Domain 3, the board's checking seat.** Actors: directors, audit committee, chair. Currency: independence, conventions, rulings, allocation. Anchor: the ground kept reachable, with the seat's stake declared (Fig 10 :59).
- **Domain 4, independent witness frames.** Actors: external audit, internal audit with a direct line, regulators, customer and supplier data, protected reporting channels. Currency: low-overlap evidence. Anchor: instruments that do not draw on management's evidence pool (Fig 2 :31).
- **X1 "board intuition about the CEO" and X2 "one composite governance score"** fail the anchor test (Fig 10 :51; Fig 1 :22) and are excluded as witnesses. X2 is kept as a budget filter only.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual (Fig 9 :27); install a reading of the ground; range the report against it; rule with provenance; refresh the checking frame (Seat Map :97). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). The middle link, vocabulary and objective, is where boards break and has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any specific board blueprint is a `gap`. A board can monitor without micromanaging or being captured only if it keeps an unmoved reading of realized outcomes, buys that reading from low-overlap frames, stays outside the decisions it grades, authors conventions rather than verdicts, keeps R per decision area, admits evidence about the residual and denies maker-level evidence, declares its stakes and records rulings with provenance. Anchor-reachability and seat-independence are two conditions of a union, not one score. Which structure implements them is not held here. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a full framework before a verdict is recorded, or prescribing a structure that intensifies monitoring of the same management channel and loses the information the board needed. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn; the shape claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and a board can change its instruments without changing the conditions those instruments must satisfy. A sent claim can be replaced, though a lost outside view cannot be restored by more of the same checking.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An anchor that moves when management pushes; two shared-dataset channels that still yield independent outcome information; a board that routinely grades decisions it authored without losing the outside view; a threshold that stays informative after becoming a target; a composite score with real per-slot predictive power; or a figure re-census that admits one lever restoring both conditions. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
