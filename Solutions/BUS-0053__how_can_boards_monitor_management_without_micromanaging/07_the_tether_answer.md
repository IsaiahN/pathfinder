# The Tether's answer: how a board can monitor without micromanaging or being captured

**Mode: `specified` on the board-design claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a checking arrangement, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A board must decide whether management's reported Γ(b,a) matches what actually happened (o′), per decision area and per period (slot s). It must do this without taking over the action and without treating the party's own report as the thing that settles the question. The two failures are a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54): a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Each needs its own instrument.

## 2. The shape

1. **Name an anchor that does not move.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The board pack is Γ, not the anchor. The anchor is the realized outcome. If the reading changes when management disputes it, the channel is broken and the board's first verdict is "contact has been lost", not a judgement about the world (Fig 10 :28-29).

2. **Buy independence from low-overlap sources, not from headcount.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :32: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* External audit, regulators, customer-level data, site observation and protected internal reporting are different closures; more directors reading the same pack are one frame with more spellings.

3. **Stay outside what the board grades.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :43: *"Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking. Never a player, never a proxy, never the author of a verdict or an atom."* Micromanagement is not merely slow; it forfeits the board's capacity to check. Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."*

4. **Author conventions, not verdicts.** Fig 10 :45: *"It does author conventions, because nothing else can see across a seam."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. The board may define metrics, thresholds and evidence requirements. Once a threshold becomes the target, it stops carrying news. Goodhart's law and ISA 500 corroborate from different fields.

5. **Keep the residual per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A single blended governance score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) and destroys the signal that says where to look. Use filters for the budget, witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

6. **Admit only evidence about the residual.** Fig 9 :51: *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. Reputation, tenure and track record are stakes, not measurements of a report's truth. Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* Fig 10 :61: *"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on"*.

7. **Record rulings with provenance.** The residual answered, the reasoning, the date, in a record a later reader can use to locate an error (Fig 10 :62-66). Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately; the ground shows whether it was right.

8. **Describe the residual before choosing the instrument.** Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The framework does not name the board structure, the number of independent directors or the reporting cadence. It gives the shape those choices must satisfy.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a governance code, not a compliance checklist, not legal advice.
- Not "independent directors are the answer" or "more monitoring is always better". The latter can destroy the information flow the board needs (F10).
- Not that management is untrustworthy. Management is Seat 1, the maker, and a maker who knows the criterion cannot be checked by it (Seat Map :31).
- Not that the board should never decide. It should author conventions and rulings; it should not author the decision it later grades.
- Not `grounded`. No board, company or dataset was measured, and none of the pre-registered refuters has been run.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating reading, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Anchor test:** can management move the outcome the board calls its anchor by disputing the report? If yes, the anchor is not an anchor (Fig 2 :30).
- **Independence test:** do the board's channels draw on the same underlying evidence? If yes, agreement among them is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- **Seat test:** can the board produce an independent ground-checked grade of a decision it authored, with no loss of outside view? If routinely yes, Fig 10 :42-43 weakens.
- **Threshold test:** does an escalation metric still carry news after it becomes a target? If not, the convention has become a verdict (Fig 10 :47).
- **Slot test:** does one composite score predict, prospectively and per decision area, which area will fail next? If yes, F6's methodological consequence is false.
- **Standing falsifiers:** any of the nine conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework can characterize the missing instrument in effect terms (low overlap, unmoved anchor, outside seat); it cannot name it without already holding it. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Indiscernibility is not identity, and the record of the route is what keeps the stop checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a board structure or a governance checklist. It says that a board can monitor management only if two conditions hold separately: the reading of what actually happened must not move when management disputes it, and the board must not author the decision it later grades. Independence comes from instruments with a different evidence base, not from more people reading the same board pack; evidence about a manager's reputation is inadmissible, while evidence about the report's match to reality is admissible. The board's legitimate authorship is conventions (metric definitions, thresholds, escalation rules) rather than verdicts, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. The residual must be tracked per decision area, never as one blended score, and each ruling should carry provenance so a later reader can locate the error. What the framework returns is the shape a checking arrangement must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The specific structure stays open until a ground settles it.
