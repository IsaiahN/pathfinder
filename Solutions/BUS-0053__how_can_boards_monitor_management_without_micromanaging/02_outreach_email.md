# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the recipient must have a real ground in view (a board, an audit committee, a dataset, a case) against which the shape claim can fail. Without that, there is nothing to check and the note becomes an argument about governance theory.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about board monitoring, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about boards. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a board can monitor management without micromanaging or being captured by management's reporting only if two conditions hold separately.
>
> 1. **An anchor the board cannot move.** The reading of what actually happened must not change because management disputes it. Management's board pack is a report, not an anchor. Two directors reading the same pack are one reader with two names; an independent reading comes from a source with a different evidence base.
> 2. **A seat that stays outside what it grades.** Once the board authors a decision, it can no longer independently grade that decision. The board's legitimate authorship is conventions (metric definitions, thresholds, evidence requirements, escalation routes), not verdicts.
>
> The framework also predicts that information about the maker (reputation, tenure, standing, track record) is inadmissible when deciding whether a report matches reality, and that a single blended governance score destroys the signal that says which decision area is losing contact. Stakes are structural, so declare them rather than pretending they are absent.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular board structure, number of independent directors, committee design or reporting cadence is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming this is a theory of everything, that it is the only true framework, or that any board has been measured here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. the outcome the board calls its anchor changes when management pushes on it and the change is accepted as legitimate;
> 2. two readers of the same management report still produce independent information about the realized outcome;
> 3. a board that authors decisions routinely produces independent ground-checked grades of them with no loss of outside view;
> 4. a single composite score predicts, prospectively and per decision area, which area will fail next.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture essay: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a board or governance problem you can check against a real outcome. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`, *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Independent reading defined by low overlap, not headcount | Fig 2 :31-32. More people reading the same pack do not add an anchor (Seat Map :283). |
| Maker-level evidence withheld | Fig 9 :51: evidence about the maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it. |
| No named board structure | Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* |
| Stakes declared, not denied | Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| The verdict is recorded before theory | Stage 2 is opt-in by construction; a recipient who has absorbed the framework has joined the frame being checked, so later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). |

## Distribution constraint for this field

The Academy of Management, a major venue for this research area, states at `https://www.aom.org/publications/journals/publishing-with-aom/`: *"Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* This package is a simulated derivation. Any submission to an AOM venue must therefore cite its provenance explicitly and must not present the derivation as human-authored empirical work. Treat that as a constraint on where and how this note may be sent, not as a reason to disguise it.
