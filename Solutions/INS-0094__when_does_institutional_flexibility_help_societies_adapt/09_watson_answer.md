## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A society's institutional library Γ predicts an outcome per slot; the environment returns `o'`; `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Arity: at least four, rebind, mint, import, and the seat property, overlapping. Symmetry: "helps adapt" is symmetric while "creates capture" is ordered in time and influence, so a pooled scalar cannot choose the arrow (Operators table). Scale: first order inside one rule-domain, second order at the interface between domains. Fig 9 :4-5 names this a union compound: a disagreement that does not shrink with effort and where each rule works on a different subset is several well-formed questions, not one hard one.
- **Domain 1, the rule library.** Actors: rule-makers, firms, citizens, rules. Currency: residual per slot and settled terms. Anchor: the outcome the rule predicts, which must not update on what the rule-makers think. Question type: can Γ close R_s with what it holds, or is an import owed?
- **Domain 2, the channel.** Actors: metrics, monitors, reports, the reference in use. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Question type: is the reading at s still a reading of the world, or of the frame's last report?
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: regulators, concentrated interests, the outside checker. Currency: verdicts and conventions. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: did a convention decide an outcome, and does the deciding seat hold an undeclared interest in the reading?
- **Domain 4, the arrangement.** Actors: institutional configurations over a fixed atom set. Currency: arrangement rather than ingredients. Anchor: behaviour at the ground. Question type: are flexible and rigid different amounts of one thing, or different bonds over the same atoms?
- **Union not partition.** The domains overlap: an import can sever a channel and install a decision-making convention at once (Fig 9 :54-57). One lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive R_s, have terms for what a rule affords, state what would count as progress, plan, then name what worked and carry it (Fig 3). The break is at the objective link, which has no instrument (Fig 3 :27). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53). Settle each operator's verdict before composing the whole (Fig 12 :104).

Strain test: Fig 11 :51 (no standing list) versus the practical wish to recommend institutions is not strained after re-census; Fig 11 governs valence relative to a residual and a recommendation bound to a named slot is a candidate binding. Fig 8 :45 (two frames reach more) versus Fig 2 :40 (agreement worthless when pooled) is not strained: same independence parameter, two quantities. The one live strain is the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim (Domain 4), kept visible in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The dial is refused. The question is a union of four overlapping conditions, and the verdicts are kept separate. Flexibility helps adaptation where a rule change alters contact with a non-updating ground, where the residual stays indexed per slot, and where any genuinely new capacity arrives as a provenanced import with a shadow test debited against the checkers' independence. It creates uncertainty where the reference updates on the frame's own reports, so the channel dies while the ground does not and everyone drifts together without noticing. It creates opportunities for capture where a convention decides an outcome, or where a seat's interest in the reading is undeclared. Over a fixed atom set, flexible and rigid forms are isomers, same atoms under a different bond; the variable is which bond may change, not how many rules change, and no standing list of good flexible institutions survives a change of residual. No figure line returns a numerical amount of flexibility, so that is a declared gap, reported as unreached rather than silence. Mode: `specified` on the separators, `general` on the union shape, `gap` on the amount, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separators and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending a design catalogue or a flexibility score instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter, and by never pooling later agreement as corroboration.
- **undoable:** yes. The separators and bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already strip the numerical claim and the design catalogue. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The five framework essays and the six world sources remain as provenance.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot residual measured before and after a specified rule change with a named slot and an instrument that would detect reference movement would settle Domain 1 and Domain 2. An anchor test that shows the reference moving when pushed would flip Domain 2 to panel 1. A case where a convention remains a convention after deciding an outcome, with a refusal path exercised and no interest in the reading, would break Domain 3. A demonstration that two arrangements sharing every atom differ only by a scalar with identical bonds and behaviour would break Domain 4. A figure re-census that exhibits a line governing the numerical amount would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
