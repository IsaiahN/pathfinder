## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :40 (contact), Fig 2 :33-34 (anchor), Fig 10 :47/:59 (convention and stake), Fig 12 :117 (isomer), Fig 9 :32 (shape).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: flexibility helps where contact changes, hurts where the reference updates or a convention decides, flexible and rigid are isomers, the number is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual institutional recommendation, for example "devolve permitting to local boards with a flexible 'reasonable' standard and an annual performance index".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The concrete recommendation is usually stated nationally; the per-slot residual is averaged away, which is exactly the failure Fig 1 :22 names.
- **The unmovable anchor requirement.** The "annual performance index" in the concrete form is typically produced by the same boards it grades, so the anchor has become another frame (Fig 2 :34).
- **The refusal seat.** A flexible standard with no seat able to say the report is wrong has no negation operator; a vocabulary without ¬ can only accumulate (Operators table).
- **The gap itself.** The concrete recommendation smuggles in a number, the annual index, that the framework says it cannot select (Fig 9 :32). The shorthand said "gap"; the concretisation filled it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a rule change that does not alter contact changes nothing, and imported capacity needs a source and a shadow test. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
