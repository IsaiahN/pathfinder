## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is the set of conditions that must hold for sustained autonomous operation. A checklist of internal parts (memory, planner, world model) is a name-level answer. The shape is: contact maintained, reach renewed, dependency chain unbroken, channel to the ground kept reachable, chain of differing rooms.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the residual is described in effect terms first (R stays small over a horizon), then the figure lines are read, then the world frames (AI engineering, embodied cognition, biology, foraging) are brought in as bindings. The order is *"Residual first, frame second"* (Fig 8 :53).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So a claim such as "episodic memory is required for autonomy" is a binding from the AI frame. It can be true or false, but it is not a figure derivation, and its provenance must be recorded as a binding.

## 4. Why the capacity-list form is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

For this question, the observed fact is "this system kept acting autonomously for a long time." Several underlying conditions can produce that same reading: a maintained channel, a renewed reach, an unbroken chain, or some combination. The reading does not determine which. Naming one capacity (for example, memory) from the single reading of "it kept going" is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the operational form: *"The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence."* And the stop condition: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A system that produces the same output as an autonomous agent is not shown to be one; the lineage has to be kept.

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular mechanism in the shape?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the "capacities" form commit the error the README forbids?** Yes. It asks for names where the framework returns a shape, and it invites reading the source off a projection (the system kept going, therefore it must have had capacity X). The correct form is the union of conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F11, with the mechanism slot left open.

## 5b. Correction to my own §1-§3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially treated the "shape, not a name" rule as forbidding any architecture talk. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Gamma. Several of the conditions named here (chain, channel, habitat, reach) are already figure terms; naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - it adds none) or that the framework names a specific implemented module. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The observed reading (sustained operation) still does not determine which condition produced it. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
