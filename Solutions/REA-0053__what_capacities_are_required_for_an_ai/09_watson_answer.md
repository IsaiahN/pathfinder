## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that a fixed list of internal capacities is what an AI system needs to act autonomously over extended periods. Option A: autonomy is a stock of capacities the model owns, so sustained operation can be improved by improving the agent in isolation. Option B: autonomy is a relation maintained between agent and habitat, and sustained operation fails when contact, reach, channel, or dependency chain fails. What each leads to: A produces a capacity inventory that cannot state a route, that ignores the habitat, and that invites selecting checkers who agree; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and open mechanism slots until a ground settles them.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read; field, author, venue, year, quote, prediction, corroborates|conflicts)

World sources are corroboration only. The verdict comes from the figures. Overlap is declared rather than hidden.

**Pool A, AI engineering (one witness, not three).**

- **AI, lifelong learning.** Guanzhi Wang, Yuqi Xie, Yunfan Jiang et al., *Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models*, arXiv:2305.16291, 2023, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: *"an ever-growing skill library of executable code for storing and retrieving complex behaviors"*; the skills are *"temporally extended, interpretable, and compositional"* and *"alleviates catastrophic forgetting."* Prediction: a long-lived agent needs a growing, compositional skill store. **Corroborates** D4 (reach renewal as learn and carry) at the mechanism level; does not establish agent-and-habitat.
- **AI, reinforcement learning.** Noah Shinn, Federico Cassano, Edward Berman et al., *Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning*, arXiv:2303.11366, 2023, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: agents *"verbally reflect on task feedback signals, then maintain their own reflective text in an episodic memory buffer to induce better decision-making in subsequent trials."* Prediction: feedback become text becomes memory closes the learn-and-carry link. **Corroborates** D1 and D2 mechanism shape.
- **AI, survey.** Zhiheng Xi, Wenxiang Chen, Xin Guo et al., *The Rise and Potential of Large Language Model Based Agents: A Survey*, arXiv:2309.07864, 2023, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: *"AI agents are artificial entities that sense their environment, make decisions, and take actions."* Prediction: a brain/perception/action decomposition. **Corroborates** D1's chain decomposition at the mechanism level.
- Overlap: all three are from the same field with overlapping citation pools, so they are one witness. Their agreement cannot count as three confirmations (Fig 2 :40).

**Pool B, philosophy of mind and cognitive science (low overlap with Pool A).**

- **Philosophy of mind / cognitive science.** *Embodied Cognition*, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, first published 2021, substantive revision 2026, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: Brooks's robots *"use the world as its own model"* and Gibson's *"affordances"* are *"opportunities for action that are revealed through the interaction of organisms with features of their environment."* Prediction: cognitive capacity is not located in the agent alone. **Corroborates** C1 (Fig 11 :39-40) from a frame that does not share the figures' vocabulary. Low overlap, so a genuinely separate witness for the shape. The article also records that embodied theses are contested, which is a caution against treating affordances as a settled mechanism.

**Pool C, biology and systems theory (low overlap with Pools A and B).**

- **Physiology.** *Allostasis* (concept from Sterling and Eyer, 1988), Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: *"maintaining stability through change"* (Wingfield); *"Allostasis is essential in order to maintain internal viability amid changing conditions."* Prediction: viability under change needs anticipatory regulation plus feedback correction. **Corroborates** D2's channel-maintenance condition from living systems. Separate witness; partial analogy because allostasis is not the figures' import rule.
- **Systems theory / biology of cognition.** *Autopoiesis* (Maturana and Varela, 1972), Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: *"a system capable of producing and maintaining itself by creating its own parts"* and an autopoietic machine *"continuously regenerate[s] and realize[s] the network of processes (relations) that produced [it]."* Prediction: autonomy is maintenance of an organization, not possession of a part. **Corroborates** the maintenance reading of F1; partial, since operational closure differs from the figures' requirement that only import moves the wall.

**Pool D, cognitive science and animal behaviour (separate witnesses).**

- **Cognitive science / neuropsychology.** *Executive functions*, Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: cognitive control processes that *"support goal-directed behavior, by regulating thoughts and actions through cognitive control, selecting and successfully monitoring actions that facilitate the attainment of chosen objectives."* Prediction: goal maintenance, inhibition, working memory and flexibility support extended goal-directed behaviour. **Corroborates** D1 at the human-cognition analogue. Same broad pool as Pool B for cognition, so counted with it as one witness for the mechanism analogy, not a new independent verdict source.
- **Animal behaviour / behavioural ecology.** *Optimal foraging theory*, Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23. Quote: an animal adopts a strategy that *"provides the most benefit (energy) for the lowest cost"*; the marginal value theorem describes *"a situation in which an organism searching for food in a patch must decide when it is economically favorable to leave."* Prediction: a leave/arrive decision under diminishing returns and travel cost. **Corroborates** D6 and the two-currency price (Fig 12 :126) from a field with almost no overlap with the others. Does not corroborate any specific AI architecture.

**Framework-internal essays, used for the canonical reading only, not as world witnesses.** Six essays by Isaiah N opened 2026-11-23 and quoted in `05_what_shape_means.md`: *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02), *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19), *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28), *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10), *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (2026-09-06), *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the question's own form does not. Sustained autonomous operation requires a maintained union of an unbroken, measured dependency chain, a live channel to a ground that does not update, capability located in agent-and-habitat, and reach renewed by contact with differing closures, iterated as a chain of differing rooms with two nonzero handoff factors. The request for a list of internal capacities is refused: Fig 11 :40 denies agent-only improvement and Fig 12 :111 says an inventory cannot state a route. The specific mechanisms (episodic memory, skill libraries, executive control, allostasis) are bindings from other frames, not derivations, and are recorded as such. Which mechanism fills each condition is a declared `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; no verdict here is corroborated by agreement among the AI sources, which share one evidence pool.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not zero. The cost of the shape being wrong is that a designer could be sent to maintain a channel when the real failure is a dead reach, or to import a new closure when the real failure is a broken middle link in the dependency chain. The packet states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. A second cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a capacity checklist instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 prices it, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the seven refuters.
- **Undoable:** yes. No condition is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A superseding packet can replace this one without withdrawing any primitive.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A channel instrument discriminating a live reading from a saturated metric; a long-horizon measurement showing atoms(Gamma) growing through a differing closure; separate measurements of bracket fidelity and ground pressure showing a nonzero product with pressure zero; a source-discriminating instrument showing which single condition produced sustained operation; or any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers. A low-overlap source showing that sustained operation is fully predicted by an agent-only property with contact held fixed would also reverse it. Prestige or agreement is not such a source.
