# The Tether's answer: capacities for extended autonomous operation

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An AI system bets, acts and observes. R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is indexed per slot and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The question asks what must hold for R to stay small across a long horizon. Under the figures, this is not one hard question: it is a **union compound** of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55-57), with its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Autonomy is a property of agent-and-habitat, not a stock the model owns.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 adds that an improvement which does not change contact changes nothing. So the required things include relations, not just internal parts.
2. **An unbroken, measured dependency chain is required.** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The links are perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :5, :7, :10, :13, :15). The relevant capacity is not the presence of modules but the absence of an undiagnosed break, since a reading taken below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).
3. **A maintained channel to a ground that does not update is required.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* The ground is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). A system can report success while the channel is dead, because its internal instruments read through the channel.
4. **Reach must be renewed by contact with a differing closure.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Without this, a long horizon is *"infinite effort inside a finite reach"* (Fig 13 :118). The curiosity drive helps only in a narrow sense: Fig 5 :48 says *"Its own output is never a measure of success."*
5. **Extended operation is a chain of differing rooms, with two nonzero factors.** Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 7 :37-39 names bracket fidelity and ground pressure as the two factors; zero either and the chain runs in place.
6. **The conditions form an ordered union, not an inventory.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever clears only the region where one condition holds. And an inventory cannot state a route: Fig 12 :111, *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
7. **The specific architecture is withheld.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework returns the shape any candidate must have, not the implementation. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* so this is a declared stop, not an omission.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of internal capacities a model owns. That is the Option A reading refused by Fig 11 :40.
- Not a claim that episodic memory, a skill library, a world model, executive control, or any named mechanism is *required*. Those are bindings from the AI and cognitive-science frames, provenanced as such (`04_links_and_distribution.md`).
- Not a claim that consciousness or desire is a prerequisite. Neither has a nameable anchor or an instrument here, so neither is admitted as a domain (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` census, X1 and X2).
- Not a claim that capability is independent of the habitat, or that a synthetic solve proves capability. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not grounded. No measurement of R over a long horizon has been taken here.
- Not a final account of agency. Fig 7 names no last room.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Channel test:** does an independent reading show that the channel to the ground is live, or is the system reading a saturated metric and reporting success? An instrument that discriminates these would locate a failure of condition 3.
- **Reach test:** over a long horizon, did atoms(Gamma) grow through contact with a frame whose closure differs, or did the system only compose inside the same wall? This locates a failure of condition 4.
- **Handoff test:** measure bracket fidelity and ground pressure separately across successive rooms. If either is zero, the product is zero regardless of how long the system runs (Fig 7 :37-39).
- **Source discrimination:** given two systems with the same output (they kept operating), which of the conditions actually produced it? This is the metamerism problem of `05_what_shape_means.md` section 4.
- Standing falsifiers from the audit: an agent-only improvement that changes contact; one intervention restoring all conditions; a broken link with continued operation; stale channel with small R; reach renewal with no import; same-closure chain with positive progress; a figure line naming an implementation.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*), and declaring its primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. Autonomy research may use different axes. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The mechanism question is a declared `gap`: Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a checklist of internal capacities for long-running autonomy. It says that capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of the agent alone, and that sustained operation therefore requires a union of conditions held at once: an unbroken and measured dependency chain from perception through planning to learning, a live channel to a ground that does not change when the system pushes on it, a habitat whose actors are enumerated rather than assumed, and a reach renewed by contact with frames whose closures differ. Over time this is a chain of differing rooms, and progress per handoff is a product of bracket fidelity and ground pressure, so either at zero stops it. The framework refuses to name the architecture that fills these conditions: it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would tell which condition failed. The mechanism slot stays open until the ground settles it.
