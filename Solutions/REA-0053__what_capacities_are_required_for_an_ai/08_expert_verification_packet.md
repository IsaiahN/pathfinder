# Expert verification packet - the Tether on autonomy capacities

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in autonomy, agents, or cognitive science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement of sustained autonomous operation is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Sustained autonomous operation is not a stock of capacities owned by the model. Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, so what must hold is an ordered union of conditions: an unbroken, measured dependency chain (perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry); a live channel to a ground that does not update on the system's opinion; contact with a habitat that is enumerated rather than composed; and a reach renewed by contact with a frame whose closure differs. Over an extended horizon this is a chain of differing rooms, and progress per handoff is the product of bracket fidelity and ground pressure, so either at zero stops it. The framework does not name the architecture that fills the conditions. It offers the shape any candidate must have, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would tell which condition failed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11 and Figure 3

- **Statement:** capability is agent-and-habitat, and the answerable question is what makes the system capable, not what parts it contains. The chain is ordered by dependency and must be measured at the broken link.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- **Refuter:** a documented agent-only improvement that changes contact and extends sustained operation with the habitat held fixed; or sustained operation with a dependency link demonstrably broken and the reading taken from above the break.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 6

- **Statement:** the channel to the ground decays even though the ground does not, so keeping the anchor reachable is a condition; and reach cannot be renewed by internal composition alone, so contact with a differing closure is a condition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :25-26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*; Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
- **Refuter:** prediction error stays small while the channel is demonstrably stale or saturated and nothing reads the loss of contact; or reach demonstrably renews over a long horizon with no new atom from a differing closure.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with an empirical hinge; checkable by reading Figure 7 and Figure 9

- **Statement:** extended operation is a chain of differing rooms, not one long room, and progress per handoff is a product of two factors; the conditions form an ordered union, not a checklist.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :36 - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*; Fig 7 :37 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*; Fig 7 :38 - *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"*; Fig 7 :39 - *"the ground's pressure on the room"*; Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **What to check empirically:** can bracket fidelity and ground pressure be measured separately in a long-running agent? If one is zero and the chain still progresses, the product claim fails.
- **Refuter:** a chain of same-closure, high-correlation rooms with positive progress while ground pressure is held at zero; or one intervention restoring all conditions with no categorically different residue.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything and not a final account of agency. There is no last room (Fig 7).
- Not a list of internal capacities, and not a claim that memory, planning, a world model, executive control, or any named mechanism is required. Those are bindings from other frames, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not a claim that consciousness or desire is a prerequisite. Neither has a nameable anchor or instrument here.
- Not a claim that a synthetic solve proves capability: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* (Fig 11 :70)
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a derivation of any specific AI architecture, and no new primitive.
- Not a uniqueness claim. The framework cannot certify its own limit (Fig 8 :37).

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Channel test:** an independent reading that discriminates a live channel from a saturated metric.
2. **Reach test:** a long-horizon measurement of whether atoms(Gamma) grew through a differing closure.
3. **Handoff test:** separate measurements of bracket fidelity and ground pressure across successive rooms.
4. **Source test:** given two systems with the same sustained-operation output, which of the conditions produced it?
