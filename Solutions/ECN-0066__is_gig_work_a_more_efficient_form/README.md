# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** Is gig work a more efficient form of flexible employment, or does it transfer costs and insecurity onto workers?

**Source:** `Questions/economics questions.txt` line 88 (question 66), section 7 "Labor markets and employment".

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape (a union compound, unbundle per slot), `specified` on every substantive claim about gig work. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement was taken by this run.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

**What an answer would look like (the bound shape).** A checkable answer names, for each affected party (worker, platform firm, consumer, regulator), the quantity at stake, the anchor that settles it, and the net position on that anchor. It does not force one global verdict across parties. It records whether "efficiency" and "cost transfer" are the same quantity measured in the same units or two different quantities that can both move. This is written down before derivation so the sought-for shape is an ordinary term and can be refused (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list chosen for receptiveness is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Sending the interior before asking for a verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*; Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Verdict in one line

`general`: the question is a **union compound** and an exclusive-or is the wrong form, so it must be unbundled and answered per slot; `specified`: the efficiency claim and the cost-transfer claim can both hold at once and are measured against different anchors; `grounded`: unreached, because only a ground reading (net worker outcome, matching data, statute) settles each slot, and this run took no measurement.
