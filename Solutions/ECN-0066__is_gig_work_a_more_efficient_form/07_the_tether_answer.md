# The Tether's answer on gig work

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on every domain claim, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether gig work is a more efficient form of flexible employment or transfers costs and insecurity onto workers. In effect terms the residual is measured per party: worker, platform firm, consumer, regulator. "Efficiency" registers against matching data; "cost and insecurity" register against worker net outcome and against statute. The question aggregates these into one global either/or. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The first correction is therefore to the form of the question, not to its content.

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union compound, not a partition.** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* and Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Both effects can register at once. Choose one side and the answer loses the other slot.
2. **One loop per party, with its own anchor.** Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* D1 matching: measured fill rates and finding rates. D2 worker: net pay after costs, unpaid time, risk. D3 firm: costs avoided and liability. D4 law: statute and ruling. D5 consumer: price and convenience. No verdict is averaged across them.
3. **Two currencies, held apart.** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* "More efficient" is a comparison; "transfers costs" is a movement of an operand between parties. Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* No "net efficiency" score is licensed.
4. **The same classification has opposite valences in different domains.** Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Contractor status lowers firm cost in D3 and raises worker risk in D2. This is why the either/or cannot be settled by inspecting the arrangement.
5. **The objective link must be stated before measuring.** Fig 3 :29 *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."* Whose efficiency, whose cost: until that is said, more data lands above the break.
6. **No final room, no last verdict.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* The compound stays open at the limit of the instruments.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that gig work is efficient, and not that it is exploitative. Neither side is affirmed.
- Not that one study or one jurisdiction settles any domain. Johnston et al. (2024) move two quantities at once in one platform; Habans (2015) prices a cost shift in four sectors; Kanayama and Otani (2026) measure matching in one Japanese platform. Each is a witness for one slot, not a verdict for the question.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this run.
- Not a new theory of labour markets. No new primitive is minted; the vocabulary was all already in the library.
- Not a net score. Adding the two currencies is the named error.

## 4. What would settle it, domain by domain (the ground)

| domain | ground reading that would settle it |
|---|---|
| D1 matching | a measured finding rate and search-time series for the platform against a public comparator, with the maker of the measurement outside the platform frame |
| D2 worker cost | net income after vehicle, insurance, equipment and unpaid waiting, by worker, with the same instrument applied to a standard-employment comparator |
| D3 firm | audited labour cost avoided and liability retained, with classification status held fixed |
| D4 legal | the applicable statute and ruling, which settle status without consulting anyone's opinion about flexibility |
| D5 consumer | observed prices and service quality, kept as a fifth slot rather than a tiebreaker |

Standing refuters (written before the run, in `01` §4): a common measure that makes the two quantities one; a measured necessary tradeoff between them; a demonstration that the firm's own metric is constitutively the ground; an actor with fixed valence; or one ground reading that settles every domain at once. Any of these fires against this answer.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The instruments exist (matching data, worker surveys, statutes, accounts); this simulated run did not operate them. Fig 6 :34 *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Here many things are already returning readings, and the job is to keep them separate, not to manufacture a new theory. Indifference between the two slogans is not identity of the two quantities.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the gig-work argument. It says the question is malformed as an exclusive-or: "efficiency" is measured against matching data, "cost and insecurity" against worker net outcome and statute, and both can move at once because the same independent-contractor classification lowers cost for the firm and raises risk for the worker. That makes it a union compound, so it must be answered once per party, in two columns, with no net score and no final room. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, three refuters written in advance, and the ground readings that would settle each party. The question stays open until those readings are made.
