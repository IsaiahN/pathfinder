## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is: a union compound with one loop per party, each loop carrying its own anchor and its own currency, with no aggregated verdict and no final room. It is not the claim that gig work wins or loses. `A or B` is a filler; `partition or union, per slot` is a constraint.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The terms gained were slot, anchor, union, comparison, transfer, status.
- The same essay gives the decisive move for this question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54 in narrative form.
- Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and :31 *"rather than the cause"*: describe "costs land on the worker" (an effect) rather than "precarity" (a cause with many definitions).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*. Here the finish line is the unbundling; from that point each party's question can be listed and handed to its anchor.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, as quoted in the exemplar's `05`: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends a refuter, not a theory.

## 3. What would license a name (a verdict on a slot)

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For D1 that is measured matching data; for D2 net worker outcome; for D4 a statute or ruling.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Johnston et al.'s natural experiment is an import from labour relations into the matching question; it is recorded with author, venue, year and an open URL, so its provenance is visible.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, as quoted in the exemplar's `05`: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The union reading adds a dimension (the party slot), not a new adjective.

## 4. The source error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, as quoted in the exemplar's `05`: metamerism, where an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, so *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The analogue here: a platform's fill-rate reading cannot comment on who bought the vehicle or who holds the insurance. Naming "efficiency" from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (net outcome by party), not a name.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous economists who already agree with either side is the named collapse. Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle each slot.

## 7. Correction to my own first reading

I first treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding any naming of the five domains, and nearly wrote the answer as an unlabelled "compound". That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, something not held in the library. Worker, firm, consumer, regulator and matching data are all already nameable and have anchors, so naming them is Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed"*, not minting a seventh kind. The correction is kept visible: the shape claim is about the form of the answer (union, per slot, no net score), not about refusing to name the parties.

## 8. Consequence for this answer

The Tether's output here is a shape plus a refuter, not a side. It says the question must be split before it is searched, that each split has its own anchor, and that two currencies must be held apart. It refuses to say which side wins until a ground reading pays. That is the honest stop.
