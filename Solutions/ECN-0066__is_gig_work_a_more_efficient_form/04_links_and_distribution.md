## Wider literature that bears on the union shape

These were seen in search results and are listed for the recipient's own checking, not quoted as evidence in the verdict:

- Andrei Hagiu and Julian Wright, "The status of workers and platforms in the sharing economy" (2019): a formal model in which *"The effect of worker misclassification on worker welfare can go in either direction in our model."* The PDF could not be fetched (unsupported file type), so it is marked accordingly in the ledger and carries less weight than the opened sources.
- Amedeo De Cesari, Marie Dutordoir, Lingxiao Lynn Liu and Joao Quariguasi Frota Neto, "Is the gig up? The impact of worker-status reclassification regulation on shareholder value" (2024): event study on WSRR announcements, corroborating the firm-domain anchor.
- Liya Palagashvili and coauthors, "Assessing the Impact of Worker Reclassification: Employment Outcomes Post-California AB5" (Mercatus, 2024): reports no significant increase in traditional employment after AB5, a caution against treating statute as an automatic ground for D2.
- Zoe Cullen and Chiara Farronato, "Outsourcing Tasks Online" (TaskRabbit, AEA 2016): estimates matching efficiency and gains from trade in a spot marketplace, a second efficiency anchor independent of the Japanese platform.
