## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The structural shape survives; no microscopic theory is named.** No held term is terminal, there is no top and no bottom, and GR is a settled term that can be an atom for a deeper composition (`general` on the rule, `specified` on GR). The GR-to-deeper relation is a lossy transform with nonzero R_T, so the effective theory does not determine the microscopic one. The identity of the deeper theory is a declared `gap`, unreached rather than unreachable, and a search that has found no unique source is not a proof of absence. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged: D1 is decided by the figures, D2 is open at the ground, D3 is a verification frame whose agreement carries no verdict weight. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **Cost if wrong:** moderate and located. The most expensive error would be to present a candidate completion as the figure's answer, spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). The next most expensive would be to treat the `gap` as a proof of absence and stop looking. Both are avoidable: lead with the shape and its four refuters, and name the missing instrument rather than a theory.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."*). The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **Reversed by:** any of the pre-registered refuters, in particular (1) a terminal level or a bottom admitted by the framework, or one of the six base terms derived from the other five, (2) a round trip with zero gap showing the effective theory invertible, (3) a source-discriminating measurement naming a unique microscopic source, (4) a demonstration that GR cannot serve as an atom for a deeper composition, or (5) a re-census showing the two conditions are a partition. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Agreement or prestige is not such a reversal.
