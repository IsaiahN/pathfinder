## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, and later, *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *"GR is an effective description of a deeper microscopic theory"* is a **shape** in any meaningful sense only after the structural constraints are stated: no terminal primitive, a lossy transform, a nonzero round-trip gap, an unnamed deeper primitive.
- *"The completion is asymptotic safety"* or *"the completion is string theory"* is a **name**, and a name of something not held in Γ.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The search is steered by the question: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly what the stage-1 email sends: what you would expect to see if the shape were true.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then *"you have composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the shape is obtained by describing the residual in effect terms (a settled term whose standing is in question), not by picking a favourite UV completion and mapping GR onto it. Picking the frame first is the inversion named in F6 of the audit: any frame will then seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here the ground would be an instrument or proof that discriminates among candidate microscopic sources.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A candidate UV theory qualifies only if its provenance and the gap it explains are recorded.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second route: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And the decisive caveat: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the six-term set is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Saying "GR is effective" inside this framework is a claim about the framework's composition rules, not a measurement of nature. That is why the mode is `general`/`specified`, never `grounded` here.

## 4. Why naming a completion from the effective reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*. The same reading could come from several underlying arrangements: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: GR's low-energy readings, including the quantum corrections Donoghue computes, are a projection. Several microscopic theories could give the same projection. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument at the relevant scale, characterised in effect terms but not held. *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the matching limit: *"A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin"*, and *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* So the correct word is **unreached**, not **unreachable** or **unknowable**.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds the self-validation limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This derivation is therefore not a validator of itself.

## 5. ANSWERED: what licenses a particular microscopic theory?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular microscopic theory is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it keeps three things separate: (1) the structural claim that no term is terminal (Fig 12 :103-104, Fig 4 :33), (2) the applied claim that GR can be composed over (Fig 12 :104), and (3) the `gap` on which microscopic theory does the composing. The audit `01` keeps them separate in F1 to F3 and F10. Naming asymptotic safety or string theory as *the* answer would be the metamerism error described in section 4.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A famous advocate of a candidate completion gives that candidate a budget to be tried. It does not make the candidate true. This governs the contact list: public opt-in venues, no personal emails, no ranking by likely agreement.

**Independence is spent by showing the theory too early.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* So stage 1 carries the residual, the shape and the refuter; the theory comes only after a verdict is recorded.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay as section 6: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And in *Arrival of the Fittest*: LLM outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and the solutions *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and this WATSON folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
