# Expert verification packet - is general relativity an effective description?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a gravitational physicist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the level rule, `specified` on the application to GR, `gap` on the identity of the deeper theory. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the Planck scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Irreducibility is a fact about a level, not about the world. A settled term can serve as an atom for whatever composes over it, and there is no bottom, so GR cannot be a terminal primitive in this framing: it is a settled term available to a deeper composition. The relation between GR and any deeper description is a lossy transform with a nonzero round-trip gap, so the deeper theory is not recoverable from the effective reading alone. The framework cannot name that theory, so the identity of the deeper level stays a declared `gap`, with a source-discriminating instrument characterised as what is missing. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close the question.

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 4

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a physicist against the EFT literature

- **Statement:** the GR-to-deeper relation is a lossy, non-invertible transform with nonzero R_T. This matches the standard effective-field-theory treatment, in which low-energy quantum effects are separated from unknown high-energy contributions, and the coarse graining is form-preserving with flowing parameters.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :27 - *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :31-32 - *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."*
- **What to check:** is the EFT characterisation faithful? Does the effective description lose information about the UV in the sense the figures describe, and is the round trip genuinely non-invertible? Donoghue 1994 is the reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9405057. The renormalisation parallel is Wilson 1975 as characterised in the canonical article, not read directly here.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a round trip with zero gap at the relevant scale, i.e. an invertible effective-to-microscopic map.

### C3 - limit; checkable by reading Figures 9, 13 and 8

- **Statement:** the framework returns a shape, not a name. It can characterise the missing deeper primitive in effect terms but cannot select among candidate completions. This is a `gap`, a verdict of unreached, not silence and not proof of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :135 - *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **What to check:** is there any figure line that licenses naming a specific microscopic theory from within the six-term set? If not, candidate completions are world imports needing a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Refuter:** name the deeper primitive by derivation from the six-term set, or show that a source-discriminating measurement at the relevant scale uniquely fixes it. The second would close the `gap` at the ground and is the most informative failure.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that GR is wrong in its tested regime. Its residual is small there.
- Not that string theory, asymptotic safety, or loop gravity is the completion, and no ranking of candidates.
- Not that the effective description is invertible, or that the microscopic theory is uniquely recoverable from the GR projection.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no new primitive or import that moves the wall.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that this simulated derivation can validate itself. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: the output *"cannot validate itself"*, and an external ground is always needed.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument or a proof, not a further argument. Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

1. **Invertibility test:** round trip between a candidate deeper description and GR-scale predictions with zero gap would refute C2.
2. **Source test:** an instrument that discriminates among candidate microscopic sources giving the same GR projection would close or refute C3 at the ground.
3. **Fundamental-level test:** a demonstration admitting a terminal level, or a derivation of one of the six base terms from the other five, would refute C1.
4. **Composition test:** a demonstration that GR cannot serve as an atom for a deeper composition would refute the `specified` application.
