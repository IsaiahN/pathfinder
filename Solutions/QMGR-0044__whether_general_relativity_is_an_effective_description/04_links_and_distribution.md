## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The EFT framing is standard, not speculative.** Donoghue 1994 separates known low-energy quantum effects from unknown high-energy contributions. That is the physics-language version of Fig 4 :27 (*"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*). It corroborates the shape but does not decide which completion is right. The word "unknown" is doing exactly the work the `gap` does in the solution.
2. **A candidate completion is not the completion.** Niedermaier and Reuter's asymptotic safety scenario is a concrete UV candidate, with evidence surveyed from several independent lines. It is a world import with its own provenance. Fig 6 :23 (*"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*) explains why holding one candidate does not close the question. Source gate and shadow test are still owed.
3. **Low-overlap fields return the same shape.** The Stanford Encyclopedia entries on scientific reduction and reduction in biology, and the entry on the computational theory of mind, all separate "more basic level" from "same level, different arrangement" and all insist that a higher-level description can be explanatory without being eliminable. They corroborate the level shape from philosophy of science, biology and cognitive science, with almost no overlap with the quantum-gravity evidence pool. They are one cluster of witnesses (Fig 8 :48-50), not several.
4. **The classic renormalisation parallel is already in the canon.** The canonical article's citations tag Wilson 1975 (Rev. Mod. Phys. 47, 773) as *a parallel* for Fig 4: the transform is *"lossy, non-invertible, form-preserving with flowing parameters."* This was read in the article, not in Wilson's paper; it is recorded as a characterisation here, not as a direct source.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before showing the derivation.
- **Fig 7 :13** - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; no mass send selected for people who already agree.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A wrong account of what GR is costs enough to justify seeking readers outside the usual closure.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against the ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the theory that produced it. **(3)** Use the formula recursively on other problems.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape claim with four refuters, not a candidate theory, and it must be checked as such. If a reader shows that the round trip is invertible or that a terminal level is admitted, the shape is withdrawn.
