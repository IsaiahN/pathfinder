## 0. Residual in effect terms

GR is a term held in the physics library Γ that predicts observations. Its residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is small in every regime we can test. The question does not ask whether that is so. It asks what **kind of term** GR is: a terminal primitive, or a lossy composition over a deeper microscopic level.

Restated on the Symbols table definition, R is the aim and is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The live slot here is not a measurement failure but a **standing question**: the term GR predicts, yet nothing in the library says whether it is irreducible.

- **Option A, GR is fundamental.** Leads to a terminal primitive, a last theory, a bottom of the chain.
- **Option B, GR is an effective description.** Leads to a level-relative composition, a lossy transform between scales, and a shape for the deeper theory that the framework cannot fill in.

**The sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula, "Direction"): *a level-relative term whose irreducibility is a fact about its level, reached from a deeper level by a lossy transform with a nonzero round-trip gap, with the deeper primitive unnamed until a ground or a provenanced import supplies it.* Binding it makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else. **Prediction committed before the run:** if the figures licence any terminal primitive or any bottom level, this shape is false. That prediction is the pre-registered refuter in section 3.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — Irreducibility is a fact about a level, and there is no bottom (decisive, `general`)

Fig 12 :103:

> **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Fig 4 :33:

> **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

The framework refuses a terminal primitive categorically. A term that has settled at one level becomes an operand at the next, so nothing in the library is irreducible "in the world", only at a level. Applied to GR (mode `specified`): GR is a settled term, so it can serve as an atom for whatever composes over it. "GR is fundamental" is not a position the framework can hold. This is a **structural** result, not a measurement of nature. The same figure that refuses a bottom also refuses a top, so the "deeper microscopic theory" cannot be a last theory either. That is the limit named in the census, not a strain.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated across Fig 12 (composition recursion) and Fig 4 (membrane, no top), two figures with different roles and low overlap. Refuter: exhibit a terminal level admitted by the framework, or derive one of the six terms from the other five (breaks minimality, Fig 13 :41).

### F2 — The relation is a lossy transform, not an identity (triangulated)

Fig 4 :27:

> **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

Fig 4 :31-32:

> **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."**

Fig 4 :30:

> **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

An effective description in the framework's own terms is an abstraction: detail is thrown away, which is what makes the result reapplicable, and the round trip does not reconstruct what was dropped. So "GR is an effective description" means more than "GR is approximate". It means the relation between GR and a deeper level is non-invertible and carries a measurable gap R_T. Claiming the deeper theory is recoverable from GR alone is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

World corroboration, secondary: Donoghue's treatment of gravity as a quantum effective field theory separates known low-energy effects from unknown high-energy contributions, which is the same split in physics language (Donoghue 1994, ledger row 1). The formal statement that a coarse graining is lossy and non-invertible is the renormalisation-group parallel the canonical article draws for Fig 4 (Nwukor, Arrival of the Fittest, citations section: Wilson 1975 tagged *a parallel*).

**Triangulation status:** triangulated (Fig 4 plus the physics EFT literature, low overlap). Refuter: show a round trip with zero gap at the relevant levels, i.e. the deeper theory fully recoverable from the effective one.

### F4 — Underivable and unreached are different facts (method)

Fig 8 :40-41:

> **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."**

Fig 6 :26-27:

> **"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."**
> **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

The three routes must be kept distinct (Formula step 7). **SEARCHED:** is the deeper term already in the physics library and unfound? Asked first, cheapest; the major candidate theories already exist as public literature, so this is a literature search, not a new atom. **INWARD:** extend the instrument from one already returning something, for example precision tests of GR at shorter scales or stronger fields. **OUTWARD:** import a characterisation from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test. Inward and outward are opposed and never read against one another. Importing a candidate UV theory against a mere budget limit would add an atom that was not needed, and Fig 6 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated (Fig 8 theorem, Fig 6 instrument rule). Refuter: demonstrate that the missing item is in closure(Γ) and was merely unfound, in which case the remedy is more search, not import.

### F6 — Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error

This is the question-specific form of F3. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23) makes the mechanism explicit: an instrument reads green *"it cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; the resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: GR's readings (the metric, the interval, low-energy quantum corrections) are a projection. Several microscopic sources could yield the same effective reading. Naming one of them from the reading alone would be a source named from a projection, which is exactly the error the essay identifies. This is a core reason the verdict stays a shape. Note the limit of the analogy: the colour case is resolved by a spectrometer; there is no Planck-scale spectrometer here. The correct status is `gap` with a characterised missing instrument, not a claim that the case is unresolvable.

**Triangulation status:** single-source for the mechanism (the essay, same author as the figures), corroborating F3 rather than deciding it. Refuter: a source-discriminating instrument at the relevant scale.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the seat map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The framework admits a terminal level or a bottom, or one of the six terms is derived from the other five (breaks Fig 12 :104, Fig 4 :33, Fig 13 :41).
2. A round trip between GR and a deeper description is demonstrated with zero gap, i.e. the effective description is invertible (breaks Fig 4 :27, :31-32).
3. A source-discriminating measurement at the relevant scale names a unique microscopic source for the GR reading (breaks F3 and F6, converts the `gap` to `grounded` and would also test whether the shape was right).
4. A measurement shows GR bonding directly to a microscopic level with no lossy transform, or that GR cannot serve as an atom for any deeper composition (breaks F2 and F1 applied).
5. A re-census shows the two conditions in F7 are a partition, so one loop settles both.

If any fires, the relevant claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
