## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :103-104 (level-relative irreducibility, no bottom), Fig 4 :27-32 (lossy transform, round trip, gap as measurement), Fig 9 :32 (shape, not a name), Fig 13 :134-135 (unreachable is a verdict, cannot name a missing primitive).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: no term is terminal; GR is a settled atom for a deeper composition; the relation is lossy with nonzero R_T; the deeper primitive is a `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete physics claim, for example "asymptotic safety is the UV completion of GR", or "GR is recoverable from the deeper theory".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the deeper primitive**, which the shorthand refuses to name but a concrete completion names; that name is added by the physicist, not derived from the six terms;
- the **non-invertibility of the transform**, which a concrete claim can quietly drop by treating the effective theory as a direct projection of the UV;
- the **`general`/`specified`/`gap` mode separation**, which a concrete claim collapses into a single verdict.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a settled term can be composed over, and there is no bottom. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the figures are the same as the formula" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
