# The Tether's answer: is general relativity an effective description?

**Mode: `general` on the level rule, `specified` on the application to GR, `gap` on the identity of the deeper theory. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

GR predicts well in every regime we can test; its residual is already small. The question is not about accuracy but about the **standing of the term**: terminal primitive, or lossy composition over a deeper microscopic level? R is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), and the live slot is a standing structural question, not an average over measurements. The question is a **union** of two conditions (Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*): a structural one the figures decide, and a physics one only a ground can decide. They are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **No held term is terminal.** Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"* Fig 4 :33: *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."* Applied to GR at `specified` mode: GR is a settled term, so it cannot be a terminal primitive in this framing. It can be an atom for whatever composes over it.
2. **No final microscopic theory either.** The same figures forbid a bottom. So "deeper microscopic theory" is legitimate as a direction of composition, not as a last theory. The word "deeper" does not smuggle in a terminus; a terminus is exactly what is refused.
3. **The relation is lossy and non-invertible.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :31-32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."* The round trip carries a nonzero gap R_T. Do not claim the microscopic theory is recoverable from GR alone: that is the dead-reckoning error of Fig 4 :30. World corroboration, secondary: Donoghue 1994 separates *"the (known) low energy quantum effects from the (unknown) high energy contributions."*
4. **The deeper primitive cannot be named by the framework.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* String theory, asymptotic safety and loop gravity are world imports with their own provenance, not figure outputs. Their standing is a `gap` until a ground settles one or a provenanced import with a shadow test supplies one.
5. **`gap` is a verdict, not silence.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held. Unreached, not unreachable in principle, and certainly not unknowable.
6. **Method, kept distinct.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* SEARCHED first (is the candidate already in the literature and unfound?), then INWARD (sharpen an instrument already returning something), then OUTWARD (import a characterisation with a source gate). INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and never read against each other (Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."*).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that GR is wrong in its tested regime. Its residual is small there.
- Not that any candidate UV completion is correct, and not a ranking of candidates. Prestige funds a trial; it does not settle it (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."*).
- Not that the effective theory is invertible, and not that the deeper theory is uniquely recoverable from the effective reading. Several sources can give one projection (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"it cannot comment on the source"*).
- Not a derivation of a microscopic theory from the six-term set. No new primitive is minted here.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the identity of the deeper level here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a proof, not further agreement. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Concretely:

- **Invertibility test:** does a round trip between a candidate deeper description and GR-scale predictions show zero gap? If yes, the lossy-transform claim fails.
- **Source test:** does any instrument at the relevant scale discriminate among candidate microscopic sources that give the same GR projection? If yes, the `gap` closes and the shape is either confirmed or refuted at the ground.
- **Standing test:** does a demonstration admit a terminal level, or derive one of the six base terms from the other five? If yes, the no-bottom claim fails.
- **Composition test:** can GR serve as an atom for a deeper composition at all? If not, the `specified` application fails while the `general` level rule may stand.
- **Census test:** are the two conditions in section 1 a partition, so one loop settles both? If yes, the union reading is wrong.

These are the pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* No part of this is a ground reading, and this derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a microscopic theory behind general relativity, and it does not claim the final answer. It says that irreducibility is a fact about a level, so no settled term, GR included, is a terminal primitive, and there is no bottom for a final theory to occupy. It says the relation between GR and any deeper description is a lossy transform with a measurable round-trip gap, so the deeper theory is not recoverable from the effective reading alone. It refuses to name that theory, because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the question. The identity of the deeper theory stays open until the ground settles it.
