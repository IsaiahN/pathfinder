## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether GR is mathematically correct in its tested regime. Its residual is already small there. What is being decided is the **standing of the term GR** in the library: is it a terminal primitive, or is it a lossy composition over a deeper microscopic level?

- Option A, "GR is fundamental": leads to a terminal primitive, a last theory, a bottom of the compositional chain.
- Option B, "GR is an effective description": leads to a level-relative composition, a lossy transform between scales, and a shape for the deeper theory without a name for it.
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* stating (1) no held term, GR included, is irreducible in an absolute sense, (2) the relation between GR and any deeper theory is a lossy transform with a nonzero round-trip gap, and (3) the framework cannot name the deeper primitive, so the name must come from a ground or a provenanced import. Until that shape is bound it is a preference, predicts nothing, and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` keeps the domains separate: the figures decide the structural shape (Option B in `general` mode as applied to levels, `specified` for GR), while the identity of the deeper theory is a declared `gap`, unreached rather than unreachable.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles whether GR has a microscopic completion, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of levels.

Four reasons:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
