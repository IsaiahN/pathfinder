## QUESTION

When do coalitions between business, labor, and government support broad development, and when do they exclude outsiders? Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small for every group-slot under some coalitions and grow in one excluded slot under others, and is that difference a cast effect or a contact effect? R is indexed per slot and never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Anchor: the separately measured outcome of each group, which does not move when the coalition calls it development (Fig 2 :29). Option A: the right cast produces development, predicting that adding partners is sufficient. Option B: development is a property of coalition-and-habitat conditional on two things, predicting that the same cast can produce either result depending on contact and measurement (Fig 11 :39, Fig 11 :40).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: a three-place coalition plus a fourth class it does not bind (outsiders), and more slots once contact cascades. Symmetry: insider versus outsider is a threshold and an absence, carried by ⋛ and −, not a symmetric +. Scale: bargaining table, sector or nation, and population-wide outcomes over a decade, with a lossy membrane between them (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 The bargaining coalition.** Actors: employer associations, unions, state agencies. Currency: concessions, wage pacts, policy commitments. Anchor: realized population-wide outcomes that do not move with the communique. Admitted.
- **D2 The outsider habitat.** Actors: informal and non-union workers, excluded firms, consumers, future entrants. Currency: access, opportunity, costs borne. Anchor: their realized outcomes, measured separately. Admitted.
- **D3 The closure mechanism.** Actors: Γ, the seven bonds, the imported atom, the wall. Currency: reach and imports. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **D4 The verification and research frame.** Actors: development scholars, institutes, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted, but in this simulated run it draws on the same evidence pool as the coalition literature, so its independence is low.
- **X1 "coalition quality" as a free-floating property.** No anchor nameable apart from self-report. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D1 and D3.
- **X2 "strong unions cause development" as a standing law.** Valence flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Absorbed, not a domain.

**Union, not partition** (Fig 9 :54-57). C1 open closure and C2 population-wide channel can fail separately, so the whole is a union of overlapping conditions, not a scale. **Order by dependency:** perceive who is in and out, build a vocabulary of what the coalition affords, bind "broad development" as a comparison, plan, learn and carry (Fig 3). The break is at the third link, where no instrument usually exists.

Strain test: Fig 11 :61 appears to conflict with the ordinary observation that coalitions exclude. Re-census resolves it. Exclusion as substitution is available; subtraction of a contact from the world is not. No strain remains after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Broad development is not a property of the coalition's cast. It is a property of coalition-and-habitat requiring both an open wall (C1) and a per-group channel (C2). Exclusion follows from either failing and is a substituted habitat, never a clean subtraction, with two silent costs. The actor classes have no standing valence; the same business, labor or government actor changes sign when the residual changes. Which specific design satisfies both conditions in a given place and decade is `gap`, returned as a shape rather than a name. The verdict is kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The Nobel 2024 and 2009 accounts corroborate the institutional and governance surface from two different evidence pools; the resilience literature corroborates the sealed-closure branch from a third. None overrules the figures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape states which condition failed, so a wrong diagnosis is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an expert's twenty minutes refuting a cast-only story that was never the right shape, and spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework before the residual. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing, so the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the refuters and the lineage are recorded in `01`, `07` and `08`, so a later reader can locate the error. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-group outcomes that stay broad while the wall is demonstrably sealed and the metric reads only members; a clean subtraction with no substituted habitat or silent cost; a standing actor list that predicts across changes of residual; or one lever clearing both conditions without a categorically different residue. Any of these would reverse the verdict. A well-designed institution named by a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test would fill the `gap`, but would not by itself settle the shape.
