# The Tether's answer on business, labor and government coalitions

**Mode: `specified` on the two-condition shape and the failure modes, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A coalition of business, labor and government predicts that its bargains produce development. The ground returns outcomes per person and per group. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| must be read per slot, never as a national average (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The live slot is usually one the coalition does not count: uncovered workers, informal firms, consumers, future entrants, or a group three steps out in the cascade (Fig 11 :52). The naive question asks which actors sit at the table. The residual asks what contact the table keeps.

## 2. The shape

1. **The question is a union, not an inclusiveness scale.** Two conditions can fail separately: an open wall and a population-wide channel. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
2. **Condition C1, open closure.** Broad development requires contact with a frame whose closure differs from the coalition's. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Without it, more bargaining is more spelling: Fig 8 :32, *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* The coalition's agreement then compounds while its evidence does not (Fig 7 :13).
3. **Condition C2, a population-wide channel.** "Broad development" must be an ordinary term with a per-group instrument. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 names *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. An aggregate growth figure is a reading that cannot comment on its source, so it cannot distinguish broad gains from concentrated gains plus an excluded periphery.
4. **Both, not either.** C1 without C2 is an open coalition measuring only its members. C2 without C1 is an accurate scoreboard behind a sealed wall. The two failures are categorically different and take different levers (Fig 9 :57).
5. **Exclusion is substitution, not subtraction.** Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* What is called exclusion is an enclave, a formal-sector gate, or a gated benefit, with two silent costs: what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69).
6. **The cast does not carry the verdict.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Business, labor and government are roles whose valence flips with the question. Adding labour or business to the table is a binding from the political-economy frame, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular coalition form (corporatism, social partnership, a growth coalition) is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32). Which design works where is `gap`.
- Not that actor identity is irrelevant. It says identity alone cannot close the residual, because valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not that aggregate statistics are useless. It says an aggregate cannot discriminate a source, so it cannot settle the question of breadth (source essay, metamerism).
- Not that "exclusion" is impossible. Substitution is available and common. What is unavailable is a clean subtraction of a contact from the world.
- Not grounded. No per-group development outcome was measured in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator that reads each slot separately and can survive a change of residual, not a further argument.

- **Per-slot measure:** outcomes for members, uncovered workers, informal firms, consumers and second-order groups, reported separately. This is the instrument that was already returning something, so it can be sharpened rather than invented (Fig 6 :32-34).
- **Wall test:** does a new actor from a differing closure change the coalition's predictions? If the wall moves only on contact, a real change should appear when contact appears (Fig 6 :23).
- **Substitution ledger:** for every "excluded" group, name the substituted habitat, what was not reproduced, and what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :66-69).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** broad per-group gains with a sealed wall and a members-only metric; a clean subtraction with no substituted habitat; a standing actor list that predicts across residual changes; one lever clearing both conditions without leaving a categorically different residue.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The two conditions are a shape derived from the figures; the specific institution is a `gap`. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Declaring the gap is the compliant move. The six-term substrate and the figure rules are a visible guess about what to measure, not nature's own list. From *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The answer stops where the per-source instrument stops, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the winning coalition. It says the question is a union of two conditions that fail separately: the coalition's wall must be reachable by a frame that differs from it, and "broad development" must be read per group rather than as an average. When the wall seals, bargaining only re-arranges what the coalition already holds and its agreement compounds while its evidence stands still. When the channel fails, a healthy national figure can sit on top of one excluded group, and the reading cannot tell you which source produced it. Exclusion, in this reading, is never a clean removal but a substituted habitat, with costs that stay invisible until the goal fails or the introduced actor acts. The actor list itself carries no fixed verdict, because the same business, labor or government actor changes sign when the question changes. What is offered is the shape, the instrument that would discriminate the two failures, four refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where the specific institution would be. The slot stays open until a ground reading closes it.
