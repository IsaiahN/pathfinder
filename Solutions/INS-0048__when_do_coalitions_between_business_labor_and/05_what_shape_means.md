## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is **two conditions plus a discriminator**: open closure and a population-wide channel, with an instrument that tells which one failed. "A coalition with the right actors" is a name; "a coalition whose wall can be moved by a differing frame and whose metric reads each group separately" is a shape. A shape narrows the search. It does not supply the country, the policy, or the year.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it; "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the searcher "leave[s] as the author". Here that means the two-condition shape is what remains after the field vocabulary (corporatism, clientelism, inclusion, capture) is stripped back to effects.

The residual was described in effect terms before any frame was chosen, per Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Otherwise any development theory would seem to fit a coalition story.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question that means a measured, per-group change in outcomes that survives a change of residual.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A political-economy concept such as "enclave" or "insider wage floor" can be imported, but the import must be recorded as coming from that frame and must explain the residual.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The two conditions are a visible choice of what to measure, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why a source cannot be read off a single development reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a rise in national income is a reading. It does not comment on whether the gains were broadly shared, captured by coalition members, or produced by an enclave that shifted costs to outsiders three steps away. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a per-group instrument, not a better slogan.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the matching limit: "indiscernibility is not identity". Two coalitions whose published outcomes look identical can have reached them by different routes, and the route is not recoverable from the outcome. The lineage is the proof.

## 5. Q1 - Does the framework name the winning coalition form?

**No.** The framework returns a shape. A particular institutional form is licensed only by a ground reading (per-group outcomes that survive a change of residual) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is why F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is `gap` rather than a suppressed answer.

## 5b. Q2 - Does "shape, not a name" forbid describing C1 and C2 at all?

**No, and the distinction matters.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. C1 and C2 are not new primitives; they are compositions of already-held quantities (contact, closure, import, anchor, channel, slot). Describing them is allowed. What is forbidden is presenting them as a newly discovered institutional species, or claiming the framework can name the specific design that satisfies them. Both claims appear in F8 and are refused.

I record this narrowing so the earlier, broader reading is not silently erased: "the framework never names anything" would be an overstatement, and it would also make this entire audit impossible.

## 6. Why the research frame cannot validate the shape

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Fig 2 :40 says the same for correlated frames: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." A development-coalitions research community that draws on the same datasets, funders and assumptions as the coalitions it studies is one frame wearing several names. Its endorsement of the shape on stage 1 would be a convention, not a witness.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A coalition with prestigious signatories, or a claim endorsed by prestigious scholars, has been given a budget to be tried. It has not been settled. The correct ask of an expert is a refutation of the two-condition shape, not an endorsement of the framework.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that LLM outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the coalition question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The four pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 are what would let a real ground begin to settle it.
