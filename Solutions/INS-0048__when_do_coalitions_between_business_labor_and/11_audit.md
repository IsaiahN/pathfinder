## Independent domain census

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-group gap between what a coalition predicts and what outsiders actually receive. Arity: three coalition roles plus at least one class they do not bind, and more once contact cascades. Symmetry: the insider/outsider boundary is a threshold, not a symmetric join; exclusion carries a subtraction and a negation (a refusal of membership), not a conjunction. Scale: table, sector or nation, and population over a decade, with a lossy transform between them.
- **Domain 1, the bargaining frame.** Actors: employer associations, unions, state agencies. Currency: concessions and commitments. Anchor: independently measured population outcomes. Question type: a frame that ranges against a ground it cannot move.
- **Domain 2, the outsider habitat.** Actors: uncovered workers, informal firms, consumers, future entrants. Currency: access and costs. Anchor: their own outcomes, separately reported. Question type: enumerated, never composed; contact cascades two or three steps out.
- **Domain 3, the closure mechanism.** Actors: library, bonds, wall, imported atom. Currency: reach. Anchor: settlement. Question type: partition of what composes, but a union of the two conditions that govern it.
- **Domain 4, the research frame.** Actors: scholars and reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this run its independence is near zero because it shares the coalition literature's evidence pool.
- **Rejected candidate:** "coalition quality" as a property of the signatories alone. No anchor nameable without the coalition's own self-report, so not a domain. Matches the primary's X1.

Order by dependency: perceive who is in and out, find terms for what the coalition affords, bind the objective with a per-group measure, then plan, then carry only what paid. The break is at the objective link; effort above it cannot be used below it.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :32 (closure), Fig 11 :61 and :66 (substitution).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: open wall plus per-group metric, both required, exclusion is substitution, cast has no fixed valence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual country case, for example a national bargain that widens training while an informal-sector group pays higher service prices three steps out, or a reputed "inclusive institution" attached to a coalition that has never been tested for contact.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which of the two conditions actually failed in a given case, because the shorthand names both but does not discriminate them;
- which slot carried the residual, because a case study tends to report the coalition's members and the national average;
- the substituted habitat and its two silent costs, because a policy description names the instrument and not what it displaced or introduced;
- the `gap` status of the winning institution, because the natural reading of "open wall plus good metric" is a demand for a named policy design, which the figures refuse.

One thing survives cleanly: a coalition's internal agreement cannot settle whether outsiders benefited, and a national average cannot discriminate the source of a gain. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "inclusive institutions good, extractive institutions bad" as identical to the figure-derived shape would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
