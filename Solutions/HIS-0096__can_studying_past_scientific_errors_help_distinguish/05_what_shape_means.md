## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For HIS-0096 the shape is therefore **not** a list of episodes labelled mistake or limit. It is a constraint on any adequate discriminator:

- it must state the residual of the episode in effect terms before choosing a frame;
- it must route the residual into the five bins by what is owed;
- it must keep the three reachability questions separate;
- it must range each past frame against its own ground;
- it must record the lineage and the refuter, and stop where the instruments stop.

That is a shape. "Ptolemy's epicycles were a mere mistake and the ether was a permanent limit" is a name, and the framework does not produce it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Bootstrapping by proximity, not by authority.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" / "rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." So the shape can become checkable without becoming the answer.

Applied here: describe the residual as "a prediction that failed on this slot against this measurement", not as "an error committed by a pre-modern thinker". The first can be ranged against a ground by anyone; the second is a present-centred narrative (see section 5).

## 3. The bundle is part of the shape

*The Library of Babel* names a failure that governs this question directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Fig 9 :54 says the same in figure form: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

So an adequate answer must first refuse the question's own binary framing. A "mistake versus limit" split is a union of at least two conditions (a wrong held term, and a reach that may lack the replacement), and a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds. This is a structural constraint on the shape, not a rhetorical preference.

## 4. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :19-28), recorded as an import rather than a derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator in plain terms: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and the case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Applied here: a long-unresolved episode is not named "limit" until an instrument shows that nothing at any resolution is registering the thing that would resolve it (Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution.").

## 5. Why the archive's own story is part of the residual, not the verdict

*Historiography of science* (Wikipedia, read this run) describes narratives "selecting only those events that seem important from the present point of view". Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the operational rule: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." A reading circle that all studied the same survey of the history of science has one evidence pool wearing many names. The historian's own frame must therefore be included in the census, with the ground as its anchor and agreement explicitly disqualified.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this field the prestige proxy is the famous case: the famous failed theory, the famous critic, the famous revolution. Citing these to settle whether a class of errors is correctable would let reputation stand in for proof. They are useful only as a budget to try a discriminator against primary sources.

## 7. The simulated-status limit

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* is blunter: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and it cannot settle the question it reports on. Every substantive claim in this folder is labelled `general`, `specified`, or `grounded` accordingly; none is `grounded`.

## 8. Correction to my own section 4

I first wrote section 4 as if the shape/not-a-name rule forbade naming *any* slot in an answer. The canonical README qualifies it: the rule governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like", that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an episode as a limit is not naming a missing primitive; it is a **binding** from the historian's frame onto a conclusion, and it must carry provenance like any other import.

The correction is recorded rather than silently applied. What remains wrong in an unqualified "error study names limits" claim is not that it names something, but that it names from a reading without a discriminator: the metamerism point in section 4 stands. Both readings are kept.

## 9. What shape means, in one sentence

For HIS-0096, a shape is the checkable form of a discriminator (state the residual, route it, run the opposed reachability instruments separately, range each frame against its ground, keep the lineage and the refuter), stated before any particular episode is labelled, and stopping where the instruments stop.
