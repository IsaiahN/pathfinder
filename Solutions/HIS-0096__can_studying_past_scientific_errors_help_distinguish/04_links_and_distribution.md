## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending a taxonomy of famous errors before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the residual and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :42:** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is misclassification of real episodes, so primary sources and instruments are worth more than agreeable commentaries.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim, and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
