# Expert verification packet - the Tether on past scientific errors

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on applications, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result about any named episode.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can studying past scientific errors distinguish correctable mistakes from enduring limits?" bundles two different questions. A past error is a residual of an earlier frame against its ground, and the figures route a residual in five ways: nothing owed, extend the probe, refit what you have, mint a new term, or refuse a term that was false. A limit is a different object, a wall of the reach that needs an import or a new instrument rather than a better argument. Because a term never found and an atom never available look the same from inside the record, the archive cannot by itself carry the label. What error study can return is a checkable procedure: state the residual in effect terms, route it, refuse before mint, run the opposed reachability questions separately, range each frame against its own ground, and keep the lineage and the refuter. Study helps iff it changes which ground-reading action is owed next.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 5 and 9

- **Statement:** the binary split is a union compound, and the five route bins are not the same thing as the two labels.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 - "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 5 :13 - "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent." Fig 5 :53 - "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Fig 5 :14 - "mechanism broken: a new term is owed."
- **What to check:** are the bins mutually exclusive as stated, and does the fifth (refusal) require an operator the first four do not? Fig 12 :112 - "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."
- **Refuter:** show that every entry in a representative archive falls into exactly one of the two requested labels and never changes bin when its ground is consulted, so the five-bin sort does no work. Or show that the bins are not exhaustive.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8

- **Statement:** an enduring limit is a property of the reach, not a wrong term, and the record cannot certify which kind of limit it is.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32 - "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 8 :39 - "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :27 - "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."
- **What to check:** does the figure really split unreachable-at-budget from underivable-in-principle, and are the instruments opposed (Fig 6 :31 "Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks")?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a wall removed by refusing a held term with no import and no new instrument. Or show an instrument that registers nothing at any resolution and is still licensed to name the missing atom directly.

### C3 - verification-frame claim; checkable against the historiography literature

- **Statement:** agreement among readers of the same secondary literature cannot corroborate a classification, so the study must range each past frame against its own ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 7 :13 - "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 2 :45 - "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- **What to check:** is the shared-pool condition actually met in the proposed study? If the readers used disjoint primary sources and disjoint instruments, and recorded their verdicts before reading each other, Fig 2 :31 ("Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence") would allow their agreement to mean something.
- **Refuter:** produce two classifications that agree, from disjoint primary evidence, with the agreement recorded before contact. That is a genuine second witness, not a pool talking.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Bin-change test:** route a sample of episodes from primary sources, then consult the ground. Does any episode change bin? A yes makes the sort informative; a universal no fires refuter C1.
2. **Inward test:** is anything at any resolution already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34)? If so, more search, not an import.
3. **Outward test:** does another frame's closure predict the residual's shape (Fig 8 :27-28)? If so, an import with source gate and shadow test is owed.
4. **Provenance test:** does the narrative record the residual, the ground, and the date (Fig 10 :62)? If not, it is a recording, not a checkable classification.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54, Fig 5 :13 and :53, Fig 6 :27 and :39-42 of Fig 8. Six short passages.
2. Check C1 against a handful of episodes you know: do they change route bin when the primary record is consulted?
3. Check C3 against the way your field actually cites history: are its "errors" read from primary sources or from the same survey literature?
4. Name one instrument or source frame that would discriminate depth from absence in a case you know, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
