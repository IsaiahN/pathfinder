## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that automatic stabilizers
(unemployment benefits, progressive taxes) are more reliable than new discretionary
stimulus. Option A: accept the comparison as a single dial and name a winner. Option B:
unbundle the question into per-domain property tests, give a shape verdict on the channel
and bond properties, and record a `gap` on magnitude. What each leads to: A delivers a
verdict no figure line supports and invites a pool of agreement as corroboration; B
delivers a checkable shape, a set of refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at
the limit of the instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and
anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A shock opens a gap between actual and potential employment and
  output, indexed per household and firm. Arity: two corrector conditions plus one shock,
  so a union compound, not a partition. Symmetry: asymmetric. The standing rule is a
  closed cycle whose sign reverses with the gap; the new decision is an ordered sequence
  with a seat at its head. Scale: slot level up to the national aggregate, with per-slot
  residuals hidden by averaging.
- **Domain 1, the standing-rule domain.** Actors: households, firms, the tax schedule,
  the benefit rules, the payment system. Currency: disposable income and aggregate
  demand. Anchor: realised income and employment, a counter and a market outcome that
  does not update on what any frame thinks. Question type: cycle.
- **Domain 2, the seat-authored domain.** Actors: legislature, executive, agencies,
  committees. Currency: authorised outlays and votes. Anchor: a model estimate of the
  output gap and a political objective, both frames that can move when the frame's own
  reports change. Question type: dependency chain with breakable middle links.
- **Domain 3, the verification domain.** Actors: budget offices, forecasters,
  econometricians. Currency: estimates and counterfactual residuals. Anchor: the realised
  outcome, readable only after the event. Independence is low because most estimates draw
  on the same national accounts and model families.
- **Order by dependency.** Counter reachable first, then objective, then plan, then
  outlay. Refuse the single-dial framing before minting a winner. Describe the residual
  before choosing a frame. Effort spent above a broken link cannot rescue it.

Strain test applied. The first pass mapped "automatic" onto one of the six substrate
terms. Re-census moved it to the bond and the decision rule, since the six terms are
physical states and structures, not policy properties. The apparent conflict dissolves.
A second strain remains live but narrowed: a control loop's set point and a fiscal
objective are not the same kind of thing, so the control-theory source supports only the
lag and sign claims, not a fiscal magnitude.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The comparison is a union, so there is no single winner. On the channel-and-bond
properties the verdict is per domain and is not averaged. The standing rule has the
reliability shape: a closed cycle bonded to a non-updating counter, firing per slot,
scaling without a new decision, and reversing sign with the gap. The seat-authored route
does not have that shape until its objective is re-grounded, its channel shortened and
its lags controlled. The two differ in bond type, cycle versus sequence, not in amount.
Magnitude, multiplier and net effect are a declared `gap`: no figure line returns them,
and the empirical literature on them is contested. Mode `specified` on the shape,
`general` on the union, `gap` on magnitude, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries six property tests,
  five pre-registered refuters and a mode label, so an error is findable rather than
  invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a
  conclusion instead of a test. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of
  being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the tests and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape verdict can be redrawn without withdrawing the union
  framing, and the magnitude gap is an explicit open field rather than a hidden premise.
  The domain boundary in Domain 2 is reversible if a counter-bonded, pre-committed
  discretionary process is exhibited; that is F3's refuter. Nothing in the record has to
  be silently erased.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that separates a
  counter-driven response from an estimate-driven response in the same episode would name
  the anchor; a measured amplification by the automatic loop, a discretionary process
  that is counter-bonded and lag-free, or a standing rule that needs a fresh decision
  when the shock moves would break the shape claim; an identified aggregate estimate
  would close the magnitude gap; a figure line directly ranking two instruments would
  remove the gap. Prestige or later endorsement is not such an instrument.
