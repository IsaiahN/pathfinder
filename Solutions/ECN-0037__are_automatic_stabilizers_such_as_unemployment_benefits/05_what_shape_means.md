## 2. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing
thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of
thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with
the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it
would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of
books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the
answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it.
It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0037 the shape is:

> a corrector whose channel reaches a reference that does not move when the frame thinks,
> which fires per slot without a new decision and with a sign that reverses with the gap,
> as against a corrector that must rebuild the reference and authorise a new route each
> time.

That shape does **not** fill in a multiplier, a stabilisation percentage, or a named
winner. Those are magnitudes, and the figures return none.

- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can

  the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can

  frame second."*). That is why the first move in `01` was a six-property effect test,
  not a search for a favourite economics paper.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by
   what pays."* For ECN-0037 that means a budget-office estimate, an identified empirical
   study, or an observed episode, not a further argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51),
   with a source gate and a shadow test. The control-theory and game-theory sources
   qualify as low-overlap frames. They license the *shape* vocabulary of channel, phase,
   commitment and set point; they do not license a fiscal magnitude.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing
space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not
make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-property test in `01` is a
visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list of reliability criteria.

## 5. Why "which is more reliable" is the wrong form for a name

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly: an instrument that reads green
*"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths can create the same reading; *"If several
paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*
The reading does not determine the source, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish
these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument,
that resolved the case.

Applied here: a measured drop in unemployment after a package cannot by itself tell us
whether the correction came from the standing counter or from the new decision, because
both were active in the same episode. The reading is a projection with lossy information.
What is owed is a discriminator (an exogenous variation, a staggered rollout, a
budget-office counterfactual), not a name attached to the reading. This is the reason the
magnitude and net-effect question is a `gap` rather than an imported verdict: the world
literature itself is contested, and the instrument that would separate the sources is
not supplied by the figures.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed
to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a
budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The
Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers well-known economists to endorse the verdict is the named
collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget
to be tried through a public venue, and let a ground, an estimate or an observed episode
settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains only institutional and
opt-in channels, and why no recipient is ranked by likely agreement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula
found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that
you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to
be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the output
is *"simulacra."*

So `01` and the rest of this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal
consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the fiscal claim and must be
labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range
against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

## 8. Correction to my own §4

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any of the six reliability
properties as the answer. That overstates the rule. As the exemplar's correction records,
the phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming a
property that is already available in the frame, such as "closed loop," "counter" or
"commitment," is a binding from another frame onto an available term. What remains
wrong is claiming that such a binding settles the fiscal question. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT
composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* A binding adds no atom, and no
binding moves the wall. Both the earlier overstatement and its narrowing are recorded
here so neither is quietly lost.

## 9. Consequence for this folder

The answer in `07_the_tether_answer.md` therefore has three layers, kept separate:

1. `general`: the question is a union of two conditions with different anchors, and no
   single number ranks them.
2. `specified`: on the channel-and-bond properties, the standing rule has the reliability
   shape and the seat-authored route does not until it is re-grounded.
3. `gap`: the aggregate magnitude and net effect in a given recession are not returned by
   any figure line and remain contested in the empirical literature.

That layering is what "shape" means when applied to ECN-0037: a constraint on the form
of a defensible answer, not the answer itself.
