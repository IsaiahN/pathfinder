# The Tether's answer: reliable but difficult to reproduce evidence

Mode: `specified` on the procedure, `general` on the metalaw statements, not `grounded`. This is a shape and a route, not a historical finding. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A community holds a reading that has paid against an anchor but cannot be re-derived inside its frame. In effect terms this is a three-place gap, (reading, ground, frame), and it is a union compound: two conditions that overlap, not one hard question and not two separable ones (Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."). Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## 2. The shape

1. Reliability and reproducibility are different quantities. Fig 5 :69: "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient." Fig 5 :70: "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it." So a reliable, hard-to-reproduce reading is a candidate, not a false claim and not a settled member of the community's usable library.
2. More of the same closure cannot repair it. Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The wall moves only on import from a differing frame.
3. What must travel is the method. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 13 :130: "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." A bare published result sends a recording, not a generator.
4. A replication is a substituted habitat. Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails." This is single-source and flagged by the figure itself as untested forward (Fig 11 :71); it is not upgraded by correlated historical cases.
5. Agreement is not the ground. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
6. Non-reproduction is not refutation. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Formula step 7 keeps SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD distinct and orders them.
7. A field rate is a filter, not a witness. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The verdict is per slot, against the ground.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that every reliable but hard-to-reproduce reading is true. "Candidate" is a status, not a verdict.
- Not that consensus or institutional reputation settles a disputed reading. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- Not that an aggregate replication rate decides a particular case.
- Not that the channels of transmission are immutable. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- Not `grounded`. No historical case was measured here; the world sources are secondary corroboration only.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating range, not a further argument. From The Hard Problem is About Source: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The operational questions for a community are:

- Which specific slot failed: instrument, calibration, material batch, ambient condition, practitioner skill, or a genuine null?
- Does an instrument already returning something allow INWARD sharpening, or is the thing unseen at every resolution so that only OUTWARD import can reach it (Fig 6 :34)?
- Does a second frame with a genuinely different closure, using a differing instrument or arrangement, range against the same anchor and return a reading (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :45)?
- Was the method carried as a head start, with the lineage recorded so an error can be located (Fig 4 :30, Fig 10 :62-65)?

Standing falsifiers from `01` §3: a settled reading automatically reproducible in every frame; repeated same-closure replication adding an atom; agreement moving the anchor; the ground changing while the channel stayed intact.

## 5. The honest stop

Unreached, not unreachable. The isolation claim in F4 is single-source, and Fig 11 :71 says it "fit cases already known and have not been tested forward." The Collins/Franklin conflict in the historical literature is a world-world conflict, not a figure conflict, and is recorded rather than pooled. The eight essays are all by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight. This answer stops at the limit of the instruments, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." (figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say a hard-to-reproduce result is false, and it does not say it is proven. It says reliability and reproducibility are two different things: a reading can have paid against the ground while the reproducing frame lacks the atom needed to compose over it. Repeating the same protocol in the same frame cannot supply that atom, because closure is idempotent; the wall moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs. What has to travel is the method, with its instrument, calibration, material conditions and skill, not the published result, and a replication is a substituted habitat whose omissions stay invisible until the goal fails. So the community should keep the claim as a candidate, name the exact slot that failed, describe the missing condition in effect terms, then ask in order whether it is unfound in the closure, whether an existing instrument can be sharpened, or whether another frame already holds it. It should choose a next room that differs from the last and range it against the ground separately, never pool agreement as corroboration, and record the lineage. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The case stays open until the ground settles it.
