## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what shape any answer to "how have scientific communities handled evidence that is reliable but difficult to reproduce?" must have. Option A: treat non-reproduction as failure of the evidence and let consensus close the case. Option B: treat the case as a union compound, keep the claim as candidate, describe the missing condition in effect terms, import from a differing frame, carry methods rather than recordings, and stop when the ground settles. What each leads to: A produces mirror-chain agreement, mistakes prestige for proof and can declare a settled reading false without a ground reading; B produces a checkable procedure, written refuters and an open slot until a discriminator arrives. Figures are primary and decide; world sources are secondary corroboration only.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- R (the residual). A reading that has paid against an anchor but cannot be re-derived in the reproducing frame. Arity: three places (reading, ground, frame), two independent conditions. Symmetry: asymmetric; reliability is ground contact, reproducibility is within-frame reach, and they do not trade. Scale: per object slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.") and per handoff along a chain of communities. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." So this is a union compound with its own loop.
- Domain 1, ground contact. Actors: phenomenon, instrument, observation. Currency: the residual R. Anchor: the ground, which does not update (Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."). Question type: partition boundary, what counts as a reading at all.
- Domain 2, reproducing frame. Actors: lab, protocol, calibration, practitioner, tacit habit. Currency: reproducibility inside closure(Γ). Anchor: none inside the frame; the ground is reachable only by contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). Question type: part of the union; the frame is not an anchor.
- Domain 3, verification arrangement. Actors: peers, reviewers, replication teams, editors, funders. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). Question type: part of the union.
- Domain 4, historical transmission. Actors: records, protocols, apprentices, instruments handed down. Currency: methods versus recordings crossing the membrane. Anchor: the ground persists, the channel decays (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does."). Question type: part of the union.
- Order by dependency. Perception, then vocabulary for the instrument and procedure, then objective per slot, then planning which differing frame, then learn and carry. The likely break is at vocabulary/objective (Fig 3 :26), a link with no obvious instrument (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). SEARCHED before INWARD before OUTWARD (Formula step 7).

Strain test applied before findings: Fig 8 :32 idempotence and Fig 6 :23 import govern different quantities (composition inside a wall versus what moves the wall); Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not" governs which room is next, not whether a differing room can be chosen. No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any verdict about a particular historical case does not. Reliable but hard-to-reproduce evidence is a settled term that a reproducing frame cannot compose over. The community's legitimate moves are to name the slot, keep the claim as candidate, describe the missing condition in effect terms, run SEARCHED then INWARD then OUTWARD, carry the method rather than the recording, choose a next frame that differs from the last, range it separately against the ground, and record the lineage. It must not call non-reproduction false, must not let consensus or prestige settle the case, and must not let a field rate hand down a verdict. The isolation claim stays `single-source`; everything else here is `specified` or `general`. This is a procedure, not a historical finding.

- cost if wrong: bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- undoable: yes. A case-level application can be superseded without touching the metalaws; no primitive has to be withdrawn, and a refused reading is never deleted from the record. The ordering of SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD can be reversed only if a new atom is honestly imported, which is growth, not erasure.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that identifies which condition was missing would name the slot; a documented case of a settled reading automatically reproducible in every frame would break the two-conditions verdict; a case of repeated same-closure replication adding an atom would break Fig 8 :32; a case of agreement moving the anchor would break Fig 2 :35. A figure re-census that admits the isolation law as tested forward would lift the `single-source` label on F4. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
