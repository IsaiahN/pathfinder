## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "*Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.*"; Fig 2 :40 "*agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool*"; Fig 4 :22 "*Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.*" A list ranked for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless, and a recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` to the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record the answer before offering any theory. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
