## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "*what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name).*" Fig 9 :32: "*you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself.*" Fig 13 :134: "*Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.*" Fig 6 :26: "*What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution.*"

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "*you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question.*"

For this question a **shape** is the procedure a community must follow, stated before it can name the missing instrument: keep the claim as candidate, locate the slot, describe the missing condition in effect terms, choose SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD, carry a method rather than a recording, pick a differing next room, record the lineage. It constrains any answer; it does not fill any particular case with a verdict.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. "*With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary.*" Applied here: start from the vocabulary a lab already has (calibration, batch, protocol, ambient condition), then find which of those recurs in the failed replications.
- Fig 6 :30: "*The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms.*" Fig 9 :30-31: "*your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause.*"
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): "*When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world.*" That is the outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "*You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place*"; the paradox is that by then "*you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it.*" So the shape converges on the procedure, not on a historical verdict.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what is silently carried

Only two things license naming the missing condition, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "*the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays.*" The README: the framework "*stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest.*"
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Here the second route dominates historical practice: the missing thing is usually a held atom (a calibration routine, a palladium batch, a capacitor lead length) imported from a frame that already had it, not a new primitive. *They're Scanning Books* (opened in the exemplar's reading): "*The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space.*"

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the direct mechanism for F3 and F4. It relays Polanyi's "*we can know more than we can tell*" and Collins's three kinds of tacit knowledge: relational, somatic and collective. It says writing "*carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part.*" And: "*Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish*" (Fig 12 :107). That is exactly why a published result is not a reproducible method: the cost of establishing the arrangement was paid once and is not in the description.

## 4. Source discrimination, not more argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): "*If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information.*" The instrument reads green "*cannot comment on the source*"; "*An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy.*" It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: two failed replications of the same reading can have different sources (wrong protocol, wrong batch, missing skill, genuine null). A vote among them cannot discriminate the source; only an instrument or a differing frame can. This is Fig 2 :35 (triangulation, not negotiation) and Fig 9 :32 (a kind of thing, never the answer itself) in cognitive terms.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "*the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself.*" And "*You may all agree and be wrong.*" A field that validates its own reproducibility rate is doing exactly that.

## 5. Answer to the operative question for this case

**What licenses a community to stop calling a reading difficult to reproduce?** Either a source-discriminating range against the ground, or a provenanced import of the missing condition from a differing frame. Nothing else: not a count of replications, not an agreement among readers of the same report, not the reputation of the original laboratory.

**Does the framework license naming the missing thing here?** Often yes, but only as a binding with provenance. See §5b.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I first wrote that "*shape, not a name*" forbids naming the missing condition at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "*what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)*." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Here the missing thing is usually an atom the framework and the community already hold (a calibration, a protocol, a material batch, a skill), so naming it is a **binding** onto a held atom, not the invention of a new term. What remains forbidden is pretending the binding is a derivation from the six-term set, or claiming it moves the wall when it adds no new atom (Fig 6 :22: "*IMPORT adds an atom*"). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a reading still does not determine its source without a discriminator. Both readings are kept visible.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "*Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it.*" In the cold fusion case, Collins reports that debunking required agreeing that the authors "*were not the right kind of scientists to be doing the work*" ( Physics Today, 2021). That is a prestige proxy doing verdict work, the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim and an anchor to range it against.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "*LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output*" and "*the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself.*" So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and cannot settle the question it reports on.
