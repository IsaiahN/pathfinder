## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of an explanation you can state before you can name the mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape derived in `01` is: accept the ground reading, make |Omega minus 1| small at the observed epoch without paying the same fine-tuning twice, and state a refuter. "OMEGA(???)" is a shape. "OMEGA(INFLATON)" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For flatness, the effect is a comparison outcome near a threshold, not a cause named "inflaton".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the CMB angular-scale test: the shape predicts what a curved universe would look like, so a reader can check it without the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* and the paradox is that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In this question, the search for the measure becomes the search for a ground that could decide the measure.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For flatness, the measured value is settled; the cause is not.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Inflation has both: a literature trail (Guth 1981, Starobinsky, Linde) and a shadow test (it explains flatness, horizon and monopoles). It may therefore be named as an import with provenance. It may not be named as the final answer.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list (Fig 13 :143-144: indivisibility and minimality are refutable claims).

## 4. Why the two standard answers commit the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Both popular answers to flatness read a source off a projection:

- **"Inflation explains flatness"** reads the cause from the effect (near-flatness), when several mechanisms (inflation, bounce, varying-c, anthropic selection, Einstein-Cartan torsion) can produce the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, such as a primordial tensor-mode measurement or a non-Gaussianity signature, not a name.
- **"Anthropic selection explains flatness"** reads the cause from being an observer, when Hossenfelder's question applies: *"What's the probability distribution and where does it come from?"* Without a measure, the reading does not discriminate among sources.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A cosmological model cannot certify that its own initial conditions are typical. That is a seat's office, not the loop's.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the mechanism? ANSWERED with a correction

**Narrow answer:** no. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Inflation and a measure ensemble are imports from other frames, not missing members of the six. Naming them is a binding with provenance, not an invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding is a derivation from the six (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).

**Correction to my own §3 and §4.** I initially wrote that the framework bans naming the mechanism and that this was the specific forbidden error. That overstates the rule. The genuine error is naming a source from a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Both claims are now recorded: the narrowing of the rule, and the metamerism point that survives it. This matches the correction made in the exemplar's `05` §5b.

**Q2: is the measure open?** Yes. Fig 9 :32 again: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework can say that a measure is owed (a selection rule needs a distribution) and what it would have to do (make near-flatness typical and distinguish a different value). It cannot say which measure is real. That needs a ground.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous cosmologists who agree that inflation is settled is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the flatness problem and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
