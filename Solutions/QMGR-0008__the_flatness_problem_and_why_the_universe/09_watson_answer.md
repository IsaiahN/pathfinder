## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the flatness problem and the claim that the universe's spatial geometry is close to flat. Option A: near-flatness is a brute initial condition and there is nothing to explain. Option B: near-flatness is a reachability failure of the standard frame that an import (inflation) or a selection rule with a measure addresses, neither of which is final. What each leads to: A stops at a datum and refuses the union; B moves the wall, keeps the measure open, and states refuters. The shape bound before the run: an admissible answer must accept the ground reading, make |Omega minus 1| small at the observed epoch without paying the same fine-tuning twice, and state what would refute it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the standard slogans do not. Near-flatness is an anchor, and inside the Friedmann / GR closure it is a reachability failure, so an import is owed. Inflation is the dominant ground-supported import with provenance and a shadow test, but it relocates the fine-tuning to its own initial conditions and potential, so there is no last room. Anthropic selection is a legitimate operator that needs an enumerated ensemble and a measure; without one it is a convention deciding an outcome, and the measure is an open instrument rather than a result. Spatial flatness is a derived leftover, not a seventh primitive. Omega is an ordered threshold comparison. The framework returns the shape any answer must have, with refuters written in advance, not a name for the cause. One strain stays live: whether the ensemble is a ground or a convention. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; only the measured near-flatness is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a settled "inflation solves it") instead of the generator (the shape and its refuters). Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The two standard slogans are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The measure strain can be cleared by a grounded measure without altering the rest of the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument (primordial tensor modes or non-Gaussianity) that names the mechanism; a grounded measure under which near-flatness is typical and a different value distinguishable; a derivation of near-flatness inside the closure with no new atom; a seventh primitive or a derivation of one of the six from the other five; or a figure re-census that admits flatness as a member. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
