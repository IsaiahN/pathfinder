# The Tether's answer on the flatness problem

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, `grounded` only on the measured value of Omega.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The ground returns a near-flat spatial geometry (Omega within about one percent of 1). The standard Friedmann / GR frame returns Omega = 1 as an unstable fixed point, so a generic initial condition does not reproduce the reading. The contact is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-58): it bundles an instability fact, a measure or prior fact, and a semantic fact about what "likely" means. It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The reading is an anchor.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The CMB angular scale, BAO and supernova distances settle that Omega is near 1. Theory does not negotiate with the reading.
2. **Near-flatness is a reachability failure, not a search failure.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* No computation inside FRW plus GR plus known matter produces a mechanism. Fig 6 :27: *"some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*
3. **Import is the only operator that moves the wall.** Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall."* The dominant import is inflation: a field with a potential and an exit, with provenance (Guth 1981 and successors) and a shadow test (it explains flatness, horizon and monopoles). It is an import, not a mint from the closure.
4. **No import is a final room.** Fig 7 :30: *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives."* Inflation relocates the fine-tuning to its own initial conditions and potential (Wikipedia "Flatness problem"; Hossenfelder 2017). Helbig (2021, note 20) records that the instability returns after inflation if Lambda = 0. The residual is handed forward, not closed.
5. **Selection is a legitimate operator with obligations.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Anthropic conditionalisation is selection, but Fig 11 :43 says *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* It needs an ensemble and a measure that could have returned otherwise. Fig 2 :43: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* The measure is an **open instrument**, not a result.
6. **Flatness is a leftover, not a member.** Fig 13 :43: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this."* Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Spatial flatness is a derived property of an arrangement.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The shape: accept the ground reading, make |Omega minus 1| small at the observed epoch without paying the same fine-tuning twice, state what would refute it. Naming inflation or a particular measure is a binding with provenance, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the flatness problem is unreal. That minority position is recorded, not endorsed, and the number is still an anchor.
- Not that inflation is settled, unique, or the final cause. It is the ground-supported import, and it relocates the residual.
- Not that anthropic reasoning is verified. The measure is open; a convention cannot decide the outcome.
- Not that the six-term substrate explains the early universe. It is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* and its indivisibility is a refutable claim (Fig 13 :41, :143-144).
- Not `grounded` on any cause. No source-discriminating instrument for the mechanism was read in this run.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Mechanism discriminator:** a primordial tensor-mode or non-Gaussianity signature that distinguishes single-field slow-roll inflation from a bounce, a varying-c model, Einstein-Cartan torsion, or a no-inflation initial condition. The reading does not determine the source; an instrument must.
- **Measure test:** a grounded measure over the ensemble under which near-flatness is typical and a different value would be distinguishable. This is the F6 strain; until it is run, selection is a convention with obligations, not a verdict.
- **Premise check:** a consistent CMB+BAO+SNe analysis showing Omega far from 1 (refuter R6).
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive near-flatness inside FRW with no new atom; show inflation flattens for all initial values with no new field; derive a seventh primitive or one of the six from the other five.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). One strain stays live: whether the anthropic ensemble is a ground or a convention (Fig 2 :36, :43). It is named in `11_audit.md` and not smoothed over.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not solve the flatness problem and does not name its cause. It says that the near-flatness reading is an anchor that theory must range against; that within the standard Friedmann frame Omega = 1 is an unstable fixed point, so near-flatness is unreachable by search alone and an import is owed; that inflation is the dominant ground-supported import but relocates the fine-tuning to its own initial conditions, so there is no last room; and that anthropic selection is a legitimate operator that still owes an enumerated ensemble and a measure. Spatial flatness is a derived leftover, not a primitive. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The cause stays open until the ground settles it.
