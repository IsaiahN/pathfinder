## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The ground returns a reading: the universe's spatial geometry is close to flat, with the total density parameter Omega within roughly one percent of 1 from CMB anisotropies combined with large-scale structure and Type Ia supernovae (Wikipedia, "Flatness problem", 2026; Fraser Cain, phys.org, 2017-06-07). Running the standard Friedmann / GR frame backward, Omega = 1 is an unstable fixed point, so the early value must have been of order 10^-62 or smaller (Wikipedia, "Flatness problem"; Helbig 2021). The residual R is the gap between the reading and what the frame predicts from a generic initial condition.

- **Arity.** Two conditions, not one: (1) a dynamical instability, why Omega is still near 1 after 13.8 Gyr; (2) a measure or prior question, why the initial value was so close to 1 at all. These are a **union compound** under Fig 9 :54-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A third, semantic condition rides on the second: what "likely" means before a probability distribution is named.
- **Symmetry.** Omega is a **comparison** of two quantities, actual density against critical density. Operators table :49: *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* The comparison is ordered, not symmetric: Omega greater than 1 leads to collapse, Omega less than 1 leads to empty expansion. Fig 12 :74: *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*
- **Scale.** First order inside the Friedmann frame (instability of a fixed point). Second order at the union of dynamics plus a measure, mechanism, or ensemble. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Each first-order theory settles before the second-order comparison is attempted.

**Bound sought-for shape, written before the search (Formula "Direction"):** a candidate answer must (i) accept the ground reading, (ii) make |Omega minus 1| small at the observed epoch without paying the same fine-tuning again, and (iii) state what would refute it. A name (inflation, anthropic selection) is not the shape; the shape is the set of constraints.

## 3. Findings

### F1:The ground settles the reading, not a theory

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

The near-flatness reading is an anchor: the angular scale of the first acoustic peak in the CMB, combined with BAO and supernova distances, constrains Omega to about 1 within one percent (Wikipedia, "Flatness problem", 2026; Cain 2017). It does not move because inflation or anthropic reasoning is preferred. All frames range against it separately (Fig 2 :45).

**Mode:** `grounded` for the number (a measurement), `specified` for its interpretation. **Status:** triangulated across low-overlap instruments (CMB angular scale versus supernova distance-redshift). Caveat: Wikipedia and phys.org/Cain share a general-audience evidence pool, so they are one witness for the prose; the underlying WMAP and Planck measurements are the real anchors, and they are not the same instrument.

**Refuter:** a future measurement showing a curvature beyond the current error bars in a way no combination of CMB, BAO and supernova data supports would remove the premise. The framework would still hold; this particular residual would change.

### F2:Inside the Friedmann closure, near-flatness is unreachable

Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

Einstein's equations plus FRW plus standard matter and radiation form a closure. Within it, Omega = 1 is an unstable fixed point and nothing in the equations forces the initial value to be near 1 (Wikipedia, "Flatness problem"; Helbig 2021, abstract: *"no fine-tuning in the early Universe is needed in order to explain the fact that there is no strong departure from flatness"* is the dissenting claim, not the standard one). Composing further inside the closure cannot add a mechanism. This is a **reachability failure**, not a search failure.

Fig 6 :27 matters here: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* From inside FRW, "the answer is buried in initial conditions" and "there is no answer in this closure" look identical. The discriminator is an import, not more arithmetic.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 13 (low overlap between closure mathematics and the substrate census).

**Refuter:** derive near-flatness from FRW plus GR with a generic initial measure and no new atom. If that succeeds, F2 is false and no import is owed.

### F3:"Why flat" is a union and must be unbundled before search

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

Three conditions are bundled: (a) the instability fact, (b) the fine-tuning / measure fact, and (c) the semantic fact that "likely" is undefined until a probability distribution is specified. Hossenfelder (2017, Backreaction, fetched 2026-11-23) states (c) directly: *"whenever someone talks about likelihood or probability you should ask 'What's the probability distribution and where does it come from?'"* Helbig (2021) separates (a) from (b) explicitly in his note 25: the flatness problem *"consists of the instability problem ... and the fine-tuning problem."*

A single lever that clears (a) leaves (b) and (c) standing. This is why a claim such as "inflation solves the flatness problem" is unstable as stated: it answers one condition and appears to answer all three.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated across three low-overlap fields: framework closure algebra (Fig 9), critical physics (Hossenfelder 2017), peer-reviewed history of science (Helbig 2021 EPJ H).

**Refuter:** show that the three conditions share a single measure and a single dynamics such that resolving one resolves the others. Then the union reading collapses to a partition and F3 is withdrawn.

### F4:Inflation is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, not a mint

Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*

Inflation imports a new atom into the cosmology frame: a scalar field with a potential and an exit (reheating). Wikipedia ("Cosmic inflation", 2026): *"The detailed particle physics mechanism responsible for inflation is unknown."* Hossenfelder (2017): *"You have to introduce a new field and a potential for that field and then a way to get rid of this field once it's done its duty."* The **source gate** is provenance (Guth 1981, Starobinsky, Linde; Kavli Prize 2014 as recorded by Wikipedia). The **shadow test** is that it explains a gap: why Omega is near 1, why the horizon problem is solved, why monopoles are absent.

Crucially, this import adds no seventh primitive to the six-term substrate. It binds an external mechanism onto arrangements already reachable. Calling it a new primitive would breach Fig 13 :41: *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."*

**Mode:** `specified` (structural reading of the import), `grounded` only on the observation that near-flatness is measured. **Status:** single-source for the figure rule (Fig 6); triangulated for the physics across Wikipedia, Hossenfelder and Helbig, all low overlap with the framework.

**Refuter:** show that the mechanism is derivable inside FRW plus known Standard Model fields with no new degree of freedom. Then it is a mint, not an import, and F4 changes.

### F5:Inflation does not close the loop; it hands the residual to the next room

Fig 7 :30: *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives."* Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room."* Fig 13 :90: *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*

The inflationary room is ground-visible and successful on the fluctuation spectrum, but it relocates the "why" to its own initial conditions and its potential. Wikipedia ("Flatness problem"): *"some physicists deny that inflationary theory resolves the flatness problem, arguing that it merely moves the fine-tuning from the probability distribution to the potential of a field."* Hossenfelder (2017) goes further: *"if you start with some very large value ... the result will still be incompatible with data,"* so the initial-value assumption survives the exponential factor. Helbig (2021, note 20) observes that *"the flatness problem will appear again in the future, at least if Lambda = 0,"* because post-inflationary FRW evolution resumes the instability.

This is the central verdict of the audit: **inflation is the dominant, ground-supported import and still not a final explanation.** It moves the wall outward and owes an account of its own ground. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 7 plus three physics sources with no shared author with the figures). No source was found that reverses the no-last-room reading; a source that did would be a conflict and would send the census back to the start, not flip the verdict.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that inflation drives |Omega minus 1| to zero for *all* initial values with no new field, so that the fine-tuning is genuinely removed rather than relocated. Then F5 falls.

### F6:Anthropic selection is a legitimate operator, but it needs a habitat and a measure

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*

Selection is not foreign to this framework; it is what the loop looks like when no seat maintains the ground. So the anthropic move is not dismissed as unscientific by fiat. It is admitted as an operator with two obligations:

1. **Habitat.** The ensemble of universes or patches must be enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43). Collins and Hawking (1973, as cited by Wikipedia "Flatness problem") and the weak-patch version both require an ensemble. If the ensemble is only a mathematical possibility, it has not met the ground.
2. **Measure.** A conditionalisation needs a prior. Wikipedia ("Anthropic principle", 2026): *"Critics of the weak anthropic principle point out that its lack of falsifiability entails that it is non-scientific."* Schmidhuber, quoted there: *"To gain more predictive power, additional assumptions on the prior distribution of alternative universes are necessary."* Hossenfelder (2017) makes the same demand in plain terms.

Fig 2 :43: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* An anthropic argument that simply declares the observed value typical, without a measure that could have returned otherwise, is a convention deciding an outcome. The GHS measure mentioned in Helbig's note 27 is a real attempt to supply one; whether it is grounded is an instrument question, not an argument.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** single-source for the figure rule (Fig 11, plus Fig 2 :43, same framework), triangulated for the physics across Wikipedia "Anthropic principle", Hossenfelder 2017 and Helbig 2021.

**Strain, named not hidden:** whether the ensemble is a ground or a convention is not settled by the figures. Fig 2 :36 says an anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it; the measure question is constitutively about the ensemble, but the ensemble has not been observed. This is a live strain, recorded in `11_audit.md`.

**Refuter:** supply a measure over the ensemble that is itself grounded (not stipulated), under which Omega near 1 is typical and a different value would be distinguishable. That clears the strain and upgrades F6.

### F7:Spatial flatness is a derived leftover, not a seventh primitive

Fig 13 :43: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this."* Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*

Zero spatial curvature is a property of a composition's leftover, like smoothness. It is not a primitive the loop assumes, and it is not the continuum that was struck from the set. The substrate question "why is space flat?" is therefore a question about an arrangement, not about a member.

**Terminological caution, kept visible.** Fig 12 :101 says *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* That "flat case" means a free bond, not a flat universe. I initially read it as bearing on spatial flatness; that reading is retracted below. Conflating the two would create a strain where none exists.

**Mode:** `general` (structural). **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 plus Fig 12), both framework primary but different figures with low conceptual overlap (substrate membership versus bond pricing).

**Refuter:** derive spatial flatness as a seventh primitive not reducible to spectrum, gradient and network over space, time and energy. That would break Fig 13 :41 and F7 with it.

### F8:Omega is an ordered threshold comparison, which is why "near 1" is the whole question

Operators table :49: *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."*

Omega = rho / rho_critical is a comparison. The question is not the magnitude of rho but where the ratio falls relative to a threshold. The two sides of the threshold lead to different universes (collapse versus empty expansion), which is why the ordered comparison bond, not a symmetric conjunction, is the right notation. Fig 12 :74: *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** single-source (Operators table and Fig 12 are the same framework), so low independent weight. It is included because it governs the *form* of the residual, not because it decides the physics.

**Refuter:** show that Omega enters the Friedmann equation as a bare magnitude with no threshold semantics. Then F8 is a notation preference only.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. From the Seat Map (field guide, local): *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **R1:** a derivation of |Omega minus 1| near zero from FRW plus GR with a generic initial measure and no new atom (breaks F2 and the import verdict);
2. **R2:** a demonstration that inflation flattens the universe for *all* initial values with no new field or potential (breaks F4 and F5, the no-last-room finding);
3. **R3:** a measure over the ensemble that is grounded in observation rather than stipulated, under which near-flatness is typical and a different value would be distinguishable (clears the F6 strain);
4. **R4:** a seventh primitive derived or observed, or one of the six derived from the other five (breaks Fig 13 :41 and F7);
5. **R5:** a figure re-census that admits spatial flatness as a substrate member rather than a leftover (breaks F7 directly);
6. **R6:** a ground measurement showing Omega far from 1 within a consistent CMB+BAO+SNe analysis (removes the residual itself).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the packet is reissued. Until one is run, no substantive claim here may be labelled `grounded` except the measured value itself.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and its refuter, not with "inflation is the answer" or "the flatness problem is not a problem." Both are verdicts stated before the ground has settled them (Fig 5 :62-64).
2. Unbundle the conditions before search (F3). Ask which of instability, measure, and semantics a proposed solution actually clears.
3. Treat inflation as an import with provenance and a shadow test (F4), and say plainly that it relocates the residual (F5). Do not present it as a closed explanation.
4. Admit anthropic selection as an operator with obligations (F6), and name the measure as the open instrument. A convention that decides the outcome is disallowed (Fig 2 :43).
5. Send the generator, not the recording. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
