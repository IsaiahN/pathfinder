## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The relationship between information erasure and thermodynamic cost is not a universal equality delivering one constant. It is a priced bond between two currencies that do not add: bits of description discarded and actions (heat or work) dissipated. It is universal only inside a substrate that provides configurations to count, a finite region in which the counting terminates, and a direction that can be spent. In real, noisy systems the cost is a per-slot quantity whose mean approaches a floor in the reversible limit while individual realisations fluctuate around it, with the discarded detail reappearing as heat. The question "how universal" is a union of overlapping conditions (speed, bath size, coupling, symmetry, coherence), which must be unbundled before it is searched; its domains stand to one another as isomers under Fig 12, same atoms arranged differently, different behaviour. Numeric constants, finite-time corrections and quantum-specific bounds are gaps for the figures and enter only as provenanced imports.

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13

- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 - *"Bits price the description. - **Refuter:** derive the numeric erasure constant from the six-term set alone; or exhibit a frame that certifies its own universality without an outside witness; or demonstrate the relationship where there is nothing to count, no finite region and no direction to spend.

### C2 - noise and per-slot structure; checkable against stochastic thermodynamics

- **Statement:** in noise the cost is a per-realisation residual. Averaging hides the live signal; the mean approaches the floor only under the reversible limit, and discarded detail reappears as heat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :32 - *"the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* Fig 5 :55 - *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** does the fluctuation structure of a real erasure ensemble behave as a per-trajectory quantity around a mean floor, and does the mean saturation occur only as protocol duration grows? The Berut experiment (Nature 483, 187, 2012) reports: *"the mean dissipated heat saturates at the Landauer bound in the limit of long erasure cycles."* That is corroboration, not a figure line.
- **Refuter:** show that averaging per-realisation residuals preserves rather than hides the live signal; or show a reproducible sub-floor mean for a symmetric quasi-static bit in a thermal bath.

### C3 - union and order; checkable as a structural claim

- **Statement:** the universality question is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question; its domains are isomers under Fig 12 and must be settled in dependency order. A single universality score is the aggregation error.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- **Refuter:** show the conditions are independent, so the residual is a partition; or show two domains share every atom and the same dominant bond, so they collapse to one; or show a domain is assessable before the one above it is settled.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a new constant, not a numeric universality score, not `kT ln2` from the figures.
- Not an identity between bits and joules; the currencies do not add.
- Not a claim that noisy systems escape the floor. They exceed it; the floor is a bound.
- Not a quantum result. Coherence, entanglement and strong coupling are `gap` for the figures, handled only as imported world sources with provenance.
- Not one question with one answer. It is a union to be unbundled.
- Not a final theory and not a unique framework. No last room.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this derivation.
