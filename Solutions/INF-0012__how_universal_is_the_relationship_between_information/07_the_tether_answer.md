# The Tether's answer: how universal is the erasure-cost relationship?

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a domain map, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The familiar statement is that erasing one bit costs at least `kT ln2` of heat. The question asks how universal this is in real, noisy systems. In effect, the relationship was established in a frame with named conditions (symmetric bit, quasistatic protocol, thermalising bath, weak coupling, equilibrium start) and is being asked to survive frames that break those conditions. The residual is the gap between the statement and its domain of validity. It is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4, :54). Verdicts below are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, with a verdict per domain

**D1 - The ideal theorem.** Fig 13 :49 *"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard."* The figures do not contain the bound; they contain a test for whether a law is load-bearing. Erasure needs configurations to count (Fig 13 :58 *"no configurations to count"*), a finite region so the counting terminates (Fig 13 :68 *"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"*) and a direction to spend (Fig 13 :71 *"no direction to drive the flow"*). **Verdict: `general` structural, `specified` applied. Triangulated (Fig 13, figures README, metalaws note). The numeric constant is a `gap` for the figures.**

**D2 - Real, noisy mesoscopic systems.** Fig 13 :31-32: the turn leaves a remainder and *"the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* Fig 5 :55: *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach"* Erasure moves information into the environment rather than deleting it, so a price is owed. In noise the quantity is per realisation: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* and :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 and Fig 1, low overlap). The floor is approached in the mean in the reversible limit; individual trajectories fluctuate around it. The fluctuation law itself is `gap` for the figures.**

**D3 - Quantum and coherent systems.** Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The figures license the shape (a priced bond whose cost depends on what is discarded), but say nothing about coherence, entanglement or non-Markovian coupling. **Verdict: `gap` for the figures on the specific quantum bound. Any quantum statement here is a provenanced import, not a derivation. Single-source where the figures speak, absent where they do not.**

**D4 - Asymmetric and imperfect erasure.** Fig 12 :128 *"The two readings come apart in both directions."* An asymmetric bit changes the price because the description-length side changes; an imperfect erasure changes what is discarded. Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* **Verdict: `specified` on the direction of the change (the price moves with the symmetry and the success probability); `gap` on its magnitude. World sources supply the formulas as secondary corroboration.**

**D5 - The relation itself is a price, not an equality.** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* A single number cannot be an identity between bits and joules. The relationship is a floor, a rate of exchange licensed under conditions. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12, Fig 5).**

**The cross-domain verdict: "universality" is a claim about a frame's reach.** Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."* A frame cannot certify that its bound holds in all frames. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 8, Fig 7, Fig 9).**

These six verdicts are not components of an average. Adding them would reproduce the error named in Fig 1 :22.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new physical constant. The figures supply no `kT ln2` and no numeric universality score.
- Not an equality between information and energy. They are two currencies that do not add.
- Not a claim that the relationship fails in noisy systems. It is a floor approached under the reversible limit and exceeded, not abolished, under finite-time and noisy conditions.
- Not a settled quantum statement. Coherence and strong coupling are `gap` for the figures.
- Not a single question with one answer. It is a union to be unbundled.
- Not a theory of everything, and not a final frame. No last room (Fig 7).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not supply a universal constant for information erasure, and it does not claim the final frame. It says that erasure cost is a priced bond between two currencies that do not add, so it is a floor rather than an equality; that the floor is universal only inside a substrate with configurations to count, a finite region and a direction to spend; that in real noise the cost is a per-slot quantity whose average approaches the floor only under the reversible limit, with discarded detail reappearing as heat; and that "how universal" is a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched, its domains standing to one another as isomers, same atoms arranged differently. Numeric constants, finite-time corrections and quantum-specific bounds are gaps for the figures and enter only as provenanced imports. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the per-domain verdicts kept separate, and the falsifiers written in advance. The center stays open until the ground settles it.

## 7. Cost if wrong, undoability, reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending a reader's independence by sending a number to agree with instead of a shape to check; Fig 2 :42 prices that - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes. The numeric imports are removable without touching the shape, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A reproducible sub-floor measurement for a symmetric quasi-static bit, a demonstration of the relationship where there is nothing to count, a refutation of the per-slot claim, or a proof that the conditions form a partition would each break the shape. A source-discriminating quantum experiment would close D3. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
