## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (`local: FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the answer itself can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "The relationship is a priced floor between two currencies that do not add" is a **shape**.
- "`kT ln2`" is a **name**, and a numeric one at that. It is not returned by the framework.
- "The relationship is universal only inside a substrate with configurations to count, a finite region and a direction to spend" is a **shape**.
- "The relationship is 97 percent universal" is neither; it is a filter result masquerading as a verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* The starting vocabulary here was supplied by the question itself: erasure, cost, noisy, universal.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect is: configurations are reduced, and something is paid. The cause (why the constant has the value it has) is not in the figures.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The effect terms used in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are "configurations to count", "finiteness in a finite region", "direction to spend", "per-slot residual". Any of these can be checked by a physicist against an experiment.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The stop here is recorded when the residual stops arriving against the ground, not when a satisfying constant is found.

## 3. What licenses a name (and why the constant is withheld)

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The Berut experiment settles the saturation limit; it does not derive it from the figures.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The finite-time and quantum bounds enter this way, recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv` with author, venue, year and a quote.

*They're Scanning Books* is mapped in the exemplar as making the point that *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* That essay was not opened this session, so the point is noted from the exemplar's mapping and not used as a quote. The figure text alone is sufficient: Fig 6 :18 adds *"the imported primitive"* and :19 *"mint can now reach φ*"*. The constant `kT ln2` is an imported primitive of the physics frame, not a term minted inside the figures.

**Consequence for this question.** The framework returns the conditions under which a floor exists; the numeric value of the floor is the physics frame's contribution. Asking the Tether for the constant is a category error, and supplying one from memory would be exactly the metamerism error the exemplar warns about: reading a source off a projection.

## 4. Why "universal" is the specific forbidden move

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*

A claim that the erasure-cost relationship is universal over all physical implementations requires a census of all implementations. No such census exists, and the figures rule out the frame that could certify its own completeness. What is available is narrower and checkable: the relationship holds inside the named substrate and under the named conditions, and outside them the verdict is a documented modification or an honest gap.

The word "universal" therefore does not name a property of the world discovered by the derivation. It names a verdict about a frame's reach, and the figures decide that verdict: no last room, no final frame.

## 5. Correction to my own section 3 (added before conclusion)

I initially wrote that the framework *cannot name any constant*, full stop. That is overbroad. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The subject is a primitive **not held in Γ**. `kT ln2` is not a missing primitive of the framework at all; it is a term of the physics frame that was never a candidate for minting inside the figures. So the correct claim is:

> The figures neither supply nor forbid the numeric constant; they are silent on it. Silence is a `gap`, not a prohibition. The constant is an import, and imports need a source gate and a shadow test, not a ban.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The practical consequence is unchanged: the outreach material withholds the constant until stage 2, because a verifier asked to check a shape should not be handed a number to agree with.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this field, a paper's citation count, a journal's impact factor or an author's reputation is a contact-cost estimate, at best a way to get the shape a budget to be tried. It is not a witness. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are chosen for public opt-in routes and ground-visible work, not for prestige. A response from a highly cited author is a conversation, never corroboration.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the Landauer question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

Also from the same essay, on self-validation: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* The second seat in `11_audit.md` is internal to this session's evidence pool and cannot supply that external validator.
