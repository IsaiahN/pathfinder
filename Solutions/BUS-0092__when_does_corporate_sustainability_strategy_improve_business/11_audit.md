## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a firm's concrete claim, "our sustainability strategy improved performance by 12 percent and cut emissions 20 percent".

**R_T is nonzero.** At least four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot index** is collapsed into a headline percentage, so the supplier-region residual that rose is invisible;
- the **anchor condition** is dropped, so it is unclear whether the market moved because impact fell or because the report improved;
- the **two currencies** are merged into one ROI figure, which Fig 12 :130 names as the error;
- the **channel decay** is assumed away, so a one-year read is treated as a ground reading.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the requirement that a per-slot residual fall against an anchor that does not update on the firm's own report. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no firm-level ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
