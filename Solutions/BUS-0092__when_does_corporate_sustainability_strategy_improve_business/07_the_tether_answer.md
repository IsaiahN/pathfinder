# The Tether's answer on sustainability strategy and business performance

**Mode: `specified` on the per-firm and per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a boundary shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question as posed asks for one answer to a union. Restated in effect terms: under what conditions does a sustainability action lower `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` for a firm-slot against a ground that pays, and under what conditions does it leave a positive residual in some slot that persists and does not trend down? The residual is indexed per firm and per impact slot, never as a single score: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19). *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22).

The union is not a partition: a paid-contact regime and an unpaid-residual regime overlap in the priced-impact region, which has its own loop (Fig 9 :55-57). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape: a boundary with two regimes and four domains

1. **It improves performance where the action changes contact with a paying ground.** The anchor is *"whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* (Fig 2 :29). If the market anchor moves when the firm's own report changes, it is not an anchor. This is D1+D2.
2. **It involves a real trade-off where a positive residual persists in an unpaid slot.** The operational signature is Formula step 2: *"the residual that PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN."* Unpriced harms sit in D3, and contact cascades beyond direct suppliers and regions (Fig 11 :52). A single score aggregates this signal away.
3. **Disclosure is a channel, not the ground.** *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* (Fig 11 :40). A report can lower cost of capital where investors genuinely pay on information, but that is a D2 effect and must be settled by a moving-capital anchor, not taken as proof that D3 changed.
4. **The contested boundary is a broken objective link.** *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27). Scope, horizon and offsets belong to the objective link; reporting belongs to a later link. *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* (Fig 3 :19).
5. **Where the wall must move, internal optimisation is the wrong remedy.** *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* (Fig 8 :32). If the harm is unpriced, only an import from a differing closure (regulation, a price, a covenant, a physical limit, a community claim) moves it, with a source gate and a shadow test.
6. **Strategy forms are isomers and the two currencies do not add.** *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* (Fig 12 :117). *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* (Fig 12 :125). Integrated and bolt-on strategies are arrangements, not amounts.
7. **No figure line names the winning practice or the numerical threshold.** *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). Declared `gap`, not silence and not a forced fit.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that sustainability never pays. The paid-contact regime is real and figure-derived.
- Not that every trade-off is a failure. A persistent unpaid residual is a **description** of where the ground is not being met, not a moral verdict. Actor valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49).
- Not that disclosure is worthless. It is a channel with a decay rate; where a payer pays on it, it has a D2 effect that must be measured.
- Not a single ROI number. The two currencies are held apart.
- Not a named practice or a universal percentage. The framework returns a shape.
- Not `grounded`. No firm-level measurement was taken; the whole folder is a simulation.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

- **A per-slot instrument that discriminates sources** (the source essay's spectrometer): one that separates genuine reduction from boundary shift, relocation, offset and reclassification. An ESG score cannot comment on the source (Fig 10 :26, *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*).
- **A non-updating market anchor:** did capital actually move to lower measured impact, not merely to a better report?
- **A cascade horizon:** measure own operations, suppliers, regions and later dates, not just the first-order slot (Fig 11 :52).
- **An objective-link instrument:** a boundary and horizon both disputing parties accept before optimization begins.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` §5: disclosure-only ground improvement; a trade-off with no persistent positive residual; a market anchor moved by a report; independent frames converging from disjoint evidence; a commensurable currency or a figure line that names the practice.

## 6. The honest stop

**Shape reached, name and number unreachable.** The boundary condition is stated in effect terms; the specific practice and the universal threshold are not. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement among raters reading the same disclosure cannot settle which slot is real. The result stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Two live strains are named in `01` §7 and carried into `11_audit.md`: the cycle bond versus the thermodynamic limit, and the social ground versus the anchor rule.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "does sustainability pay" with a yes or a no. It says the question is a union of two regimes. A sustainability action improves performance where it changes contact with a ground that pays, a customer, an investor, a regulator, and the per-slot residuals fall against an anchor that does not update on the firm's own report. It involves a real trade-off where a positive residual persists and does not trend down in a slot the market does not price, often a supplier, a region, or a later date, which a single composite score aggregates away. Disclosure is a decaying channel, not the ground; the boundary dispute (scope, horizon, offsets) is a broken objective link that more reporting cannot rescue; and two strategies with the same atoms and different bonds are isomers, not amounts of the same virtue. What it refuses to give is the winning practice or a universal percentage: that is a shape, not a name. What it offers is the boundary condition, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Nothing here is a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.
