## What this folder answers, in effect terms

The question is a conditional with two halves: under what conditions does a sustainability action lower a firm's residual R against a paying ground, and under what conditions does it leave a positive residual that persists and does not trend down in some slot (a real trade-off). R is indexed per firm-slot and per impact-slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The two halves are not opposite answers to one question: they are a **union of overlapping conditions** (Fig 9 :54-57) with different anchors, so verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that sustainability pays.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons: a room of agreeing readers is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35); selecting for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40, Fig 2 :42); and a verifier who has absorbed the full frame before judging loses the independence that makes the judgement worth anything (Fig 10 :42).
