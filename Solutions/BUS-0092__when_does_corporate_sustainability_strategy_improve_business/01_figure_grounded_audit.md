## 0. The claim under audit, restated as an effect

The question is not *whether* sustainability strategy pays. It is a two-part conditional:

- **Part A (improvement):** under what conditions does a sustainability action `φ` lower `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` for a firm-slot against a ground that actually pays?
- **Part B (trade-off):** under what conditions does `φ` leave a positive residual in some slot that **persists and does not trend down**, which Formula step 2 names as the signature of a term that is wrong rather than merely missing?

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term per the "Direction" discipline in `THE_FORMULA.md`: a **shape of the boundary condition** that separates the two regimes, plus a named anchor per regime, plus a refuter that would break it. The sought-for shape is written down here before the run so it can accrue a residual like any other term.

R is a slice, not a total: *"R is the residual"*, *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."* (Symbols table :37-40). So **"business performance" must not be read as one scalar**. A single composite score is the named error: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22).

## 1. Residual, arity, symmetry, scale

- **R (the residual).** A firm's sustainability action changes firm state; the market, a regulator, a supplier, a community, or a physical flow returns an observation `o′`. The residual is the gap per slot.
- **Arity:** a three-place relation (firm Γ, action a, ground observation o′), indexed by firm and by impact slot and ordered in time. It carries an ordered bond (`→`, sequence) and a comparison bond (`⋛`), not symmetric ones: the action precedes the reading, and "improves" is a threshold.
- **Symmetry:** "improve" is a comparison, not a conjunction. Operators table :52: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* Progress is a subtraction: Operators table :46: *"progress is a subtraction, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot say how far along it is."*
- **Scale:** single firm loop, population/market ranking, supply-chain and temporal cascade. The membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

It is a link nobody has looked at."* Most sustainability effort lands on measurement (D4), which is *above* the objective link in Fig 3's ordering, so it cannot rescue a broken objective. They do not negotiate with it."* and does not say there is only one ground for a union. No strain after re-census. It is recorded in §9 anyway so the route is visible.

## 4. Findings

### F1 - Improvement is conditional on a paying ground; the trade-off is a persistent positive residual in another slot (decisive)

Fig 2 :29, verbatim:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

Formula step 2, verbatim (the shrinking test read with the opposite sign):

> **"the residual that PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN."**

Reading: a sustainability action improves business performance where the ground that registers the improvement is the ground that pays (D1+D2). It involves a **real trade-off** where the improvement in the paid slot is accompanied by a positive residual in an unpaid slot, D3, that persists and does not trend down. The two cases are distinguished by per-slot residuals, not by a net present value. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a strategy that lowers the paid residual and leaves every impact-slot residual trending down as well, measured over a horizon long enough for a cascade, with no slot carrying persistent positive mass. If such a case is documented, the trade-off half of this finding is false for that case.

### F2 - Disclosure moves the channel, not the ground

Fig 10 :24 and :26, verbatim:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
> **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**

Fig 11 :40, verbatim:

> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Reading: an ESG rating or report is a reading of the channel. A strategy that improves the report without changing physical or social contact may genuinely lower cost of capital where investors pay on the report, but that is a D2 effect and must be settled by a D2 anchor (did capital actually move to lower impact?). It is not evidence that D3 changed. This is the exact structure the greenwashing literature names (world source, Greenwashing Wikipedia): a firm may spend more on the green image than on environmentally sound practice, and one of the seven "sins" is literally *"Hidden Trade-off"*. Mode `specified`, triangulated (Fig 10 + Fig 11, low overlap between the channel rule and the capability rule). **Refuter:** a disclosure-only change that is followed by a ground-verified, persistent fall in physical impact with no operational change. That would break the channel/ground distinction as applied here.

### F3 - A single ESG score aggregates away the live trade-off slot

Fig 1 :22, verbatim:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 7 :13, verbatim:

> **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

Reading: a composite rating, and a set of raters who read the same disclosure, are one witness. The world source *Aggregate Confusion* tradition (recorded here via the Greenwashing page's ESG-ratings section, and the reader is directed to the underlying paper rather than this summary) shows ratings can disagree sharply because they measure different things. Disagreement among raters is not a defect to be averaged out; it is evidence that the slots differ. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** two ratings built on disjoint primary data that converge on the same per-slot verdict, with provenance showing no shared pool. Our "one witness" claim would then be too strong for that pair.

### F4 - Contested scope is a broken middle link, not a measurement problem

Fig 3 :27, verbatim:

> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Fig 3 :29-30, verbatim:

> **"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."**

Reading: the objective link in sustainability strategy is "what counts as progress": Scope 1 versus 2 versus 3, location-based versus market-based, offsets versus reductions, 2030 versus 2050, tonnes versus intensity. A chain that reports beautifully on an unchosen boundary has a strong measurement link above a broken objective link. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The world source *Externality* records the same difficulty in pricing: *"since regulators do not always have all the information on the externality, it can be difficult to impose the right tax."* That is the objective link failing in the economics frame. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a documented case where a boundary dispute was settled by an instrument that both sides accept as non-updating, and performance then improved without a further boundary change.

### F5 - Where the wall does not move, optimisation is the wrong remedy

Fig 8 :32 and Fig 6 :23, verbatim:

> **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."**
> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Reading: if the residual comes from an unpriced harm (D3), then optimizing the existing business model inside D1 cannot remove it. The remedy is either an INWARD change of instrument (measure the harm) or an OUTWARD import from a frame whose closure differs: regulation, a consumer price, an investor covenant, a physical limit, a community claim. This is why the world source on the *Porter hypothesis* distinguishes the weak version (regulation stimulates innovation) from the strong version (it improves business performance) and reports *mixed evidence* for the strong version: the import sometimes pays, sometimes not.

Formula step 7 is explicit that these are two tracks: *"INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed; never read one against the other."* Mode `general` for idempotence, `specified` for firms. **Refuter:** a firm that renews reach and removes a persistent unpriced residual purely by internal optimisation with no new atom and no changed contact. That would falsify the closure claim as applied here.

### F6 - Strategy forms are isomers, not versions of one thing

Fig 12 :117, verbatim:

> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Reading: two firms holding the same atoms (capital, staff, technology, suppliers, a budget) but bonding them differently (sustainability integrated into the operating loop versus a bolt-on report and offset programme) are **isomers**, not different amounts of the same good. The composite score records the ingredients and not the bond, which is Fig 12's *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not."* This is developed in `06_isomers_and_order.md`, including catalyst, cycle, functional group, and the two-currencies rule. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a case where rearranging the same atoms produces no behavioural difference on any ground, or where the two configurations can be distinguished by the ingredient list alone.

### F7 - The two currencies do not add

Fig 12 :125-126 and :130, verbatim:

> **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
> **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**
> **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."**

Reading: a sustainability report is cheap in actions and expensive in bits; a supply-chain redesign is cheap to state and expensive to settle. A single "ROI of sustainability" number sums two readings the figure forbids adding. The world source on *Circular economy* makes the same point empirically: firms adopting secondary raw materials *"can face significantly higher perceived production costs"* while achieving *"an average turnover growth premium of 10%"*, and the source adds that sectoral effects differ. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a demonstrated common unit in which the bit-cost of a disclosure and the action-cost of a physical change are commensurable without loss. None was found; the search is recorded, not hidden (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

### F8 - No figure line picks the winning practice (declared gap)

Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134, verbatim:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

Reading: after a genuine re-census, no figure line selects which specific practice pays in which firm, or the numerical threshold at which an improvement counts as enough. The framework returns the **shape** of the boundary condition, not the name of the practice. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Naming "Scope 3 supplier engagement" or "a 20 percent intensity cut" as *the* answer would be a preference smuggled in as a term. Record as `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not silence and not a forced fit. **Refuter:** a figure line, or a source with a genuinely different evidence pool, that governs the specific practice and the threshold. Until one appears, the honest verdict is `gap`.

### F9 - Prestige is not the verdict

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay read 2026-11-22, verbatim):

> **"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."**

Reading: an award, a ranking, a well-known certifier, or a famous adopter gives a sustainability strategy a budget to be tried. None of them settles whether it changed contact. Mode `general`. Refuter: a case where a scheme's reputation alone, with no ground reading, correctly predicted persistent impact reduction. Reputation is a contact-cost estimate in outreach, never an evidence column.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the discipline recorded in the exemplar and by *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* applies to the source case; the operative design rule is that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The boundary-shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A strategy that changes no physical or social contact, only disclosure, yields a ground-verified persistent fall in realized impact **and** in realized return over a cascade horizon. (Breaks F2 and Fig 11 :40.)
2. A firm lowers its paid residual while own-operation emissions fall and supplier or regional emissions rise by the same or a greater amount, **yet** no slot carries a positive residual that persists and does not trend down. (Breaks F1 and Formula step 2.)
3. The market anchor moves when the firm's own report changes with no change in cash flows or physical impact. (Breaks Fig 2 :29 and Fig 2 :33, *"The anchor must not update."*)
4. Two frames with genuinely disjoint evidence converge on the same per-slot verdict and the convergence is traceable to independent grounds, not a shared pool. (Breaks F3's "one witness" reading.)
5. A figure re-census admits a composition that makes the bit-cost and action-cost commensurable, or a new primitive that selects the winning practice. (Breaks F7 or F8.)

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the census is redone. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Publish the per-slot table before the verdict. A single score is the error (F1, F3).
2. Ask which anchor pays in each domain before asking whether the strategy pays at all (F1).
3. Treat disclosure as a channel with a decay rate, and measure the round-trip gap R_T from impact to report and back to decision (F2, Fig 4 :30).
4. Settle the objective link (scope, boundary, horizon) with an instrument before optimizing the measurement link (F4).
5. Where the harm is unpriced, do not expect internal optimisation to move the wall; name the import and its source gate (F5).
6. Compare strategy forms as isomers, not as quantities of virtue (F6), and keep the two currencies apart (F7).
7. Keep the gap open and visible (F8). "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## 8. Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **One score or many slots?** Many. Fig 1 :19, :22.
2. **What pays?** A ground in D2 that does not update on the report; Fig 2 :29.
3. **What is a trade-off?** A persistent, non-trending-down positive residual in a slot; Formula step 2.
4. **Is the gap measurement or objective?** Objective: scope, boundary, horizon; Fig 3 :19, :27.
5. **Can internal optimisation close it?** Not where the harm is unpriced; Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23.
6. **Are strategy forms quantities?** No, isomers; Fig 12 :117, and two currencies; Fig 12 :125.
7. **Which practice wins?** Unreachable at this budget. Shape only; Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134.

## 9. Updated verdict

The shape survives: a sustainability strategy improves performance where its action changes contact with a ground that pays and the per-slot residuals fall; it involves a real trade-off where a positive residual persists and does not trend down in a slot the market does not price, most often reached by a cascade beyond direct contact. Disclosure is a channel with a decay rate, not the ground. The boundary dispute is a broken objective link. Strategy forms are isomers over two currencies that cannot be added.

Everything specific is withheld: which practice, which threshold, which firm. The two live strains are named. After re-census, no further figure line governs, so the honest stop is `general` shape, `specified` per-domain verdicts, and a declared `gap` on the name and the number. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
