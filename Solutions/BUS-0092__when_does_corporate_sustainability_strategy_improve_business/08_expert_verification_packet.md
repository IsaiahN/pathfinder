# Expert verification packet - the Tether on sustainability strategy and performance

**What this is:** a claim in a form a sustainability-strategy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No firm-level measurement is claimed. This is a boundary shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of two regimes, not one question with opposite answers. A sustainability action improves performance where it changes contact with a ground that pays and the per-slot residuals fall against an anchor that does not update on the firm's own report. It involves a real trade-off where a positive residual persists and does not trend down in a slot the market does not price, often beyond first-order contact. Disclosure is a decaying channel rather than the ground; the contested boundary (scope, horizon, offsets) is a broken objective link; and two strategies with the same atoms but different bonds are isomers, not quantities of virtue. The framework withholds the winning practice and the universal threshold. What it offers is the boundary condition, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus business-facing; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and the Formula

- **Statement:** residuals must be indexed per firm and per impact slot; a real trade-off is a positive residual that persists and does not trend down; "improve" is a threshold against a non-updating anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
  - `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 - *"the residual that PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN."*
- **Refuter:** a strategy that lowers the paid residual while own-operation impact falls and supplier or regional impact rises, with no slot carrying a positive residual that persists; or a market anchor that moves when the report changes but cash flows and impact do not.

### C2 - business-facing; checkable by a sustainability-strategy researcher against published data

- **Statement:** disclosure moves a decaying channel, not the ground, and the contested boundary is an objective-link failure that cannot be repaired by more reporting.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*; Fig 3 :27 - *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **What to check:** find a case where a disclosure-only change, with no operational change, is followed by a ground-verified persistent fall in realized impact over a cascade horizon; or a case where a boundary dispute was settled by an instrument both sides accept and performance then improved without further boundary change. Either would break the claim as stated.
- **Refuter:** either of those cases, documented with primary data.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** strategy forms are isomers over a shared atom set and the bit-cost and action-cost cannot be added; where the harm is unpriced, internal optimisation cannot move the wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
- **Refuter:** a case where an integrated and a bolt-on configuration of the same atoms rank identically on every ground, or where the bit-cost of a disclosure and the action-cost of a physical change are shown commensurable without loss, or where a firm removes a persistent unpriced residual by internal optimisation with no new atom and no changed contact.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that sustainability never pays. The paid-contact regime is figure-derived.
- Not that every trade-off is a failure. A persistent unpaid residual is a description of where the ground is not met, not a moral verdict (Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*).
- Not that disclosure is worthless. It has a real D2 effect where a payer pays on information, but that needs a moving-capital anchor.
- Not a single ROI number, not a named winning practice, and not a universal percentage.
- Not `grounded`. No firm-level measurement is claimed; this is a simulation.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating instrument that separates genuine reduction from boundary shift, relocation, offset purchase and reclassification. A composite score *"cannot comment on the source"* (*The Hard Problem is About Source*, read 2026-11-22).
2. A non-updating market anchor: did capital move to lower measured impact, not merely to a better report?
3. A cascade horizon covering own operations, suppliers, regions and later dates (Fig 11 :52).
4. An objective-link instrument both sides accept before optimization begins.
5. The standing refuters in `01` §5.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 2 :29; Fig 10 :24; Fig 3 :27; Fig 12 :117, :125 - six short passages.
2. Check C1 and C2 against one published case you know: is there a slot with a persistent positive residual that the headline score hides?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate genuine reduction from boundary shift, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no later agreement will be pooled as corroboration.
