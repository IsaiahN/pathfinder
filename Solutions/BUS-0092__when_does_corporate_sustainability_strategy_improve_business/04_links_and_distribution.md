## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :25** - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole frame before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would count as a persistent trade-off slot before showing the essay.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* No mass send.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot boundary claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer before any theory is shown; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if they ask, offer the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"*; the architecture essay warns that solutions may be *"contrived to fit the formula."* Each user writes their own refuter before the run.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
