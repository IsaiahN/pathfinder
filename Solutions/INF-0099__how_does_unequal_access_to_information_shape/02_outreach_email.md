**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01` has been checked against the verbatim figure lines by the recipient. The offer collapses if the stage-1 output does not survive.

**Subject:** Check one structural claim about information access before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output
> does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** Unequal access to information is a differential between
> frames in what they can represent and reach. I want to know what shape that differential gives
> to three things people actually care about: power, coordination, and social inequality. I am
> not asking for a measure or a policy yet. I am asking whether the structural claim below is
> even the right shape.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the social binding, not `grounded`).**
> Three separate claims, not one average:
>
> 1. Access is reach, not new information in the world. A frame gains reach only by contact with
>    a frame whose closure differs; composition inside its own reach never adds an atom. The
>    figures put it plainly: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access"* (Fig 6
>    :26).
> 2. Power is position in the update graph and control of the shared evidence pool, not simple
>    possession of information. When frames update on one another instead of ranging against an
>    anchor that does not move, *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most
>    influence"* (Fig 2 :14-15). Coordination that works is triangulation against that unmoved
>    anchor: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"* (Fig 2
>    :35). High overlap between frames is *"disqualifying for a verdict"* and only *"efficient for
>    a convention"* (Fig 2 :40-41).
> 3. Social inequality is differential capability in an agent-and-habitat pair, not a property of
>    the isolated agent: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however
>    much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40). A seat can introduce a method or a head start but cannot
>    subtract a habitat (Fig 11 :61-62), and contact cascades several steps out.
>
> I wrote these down before asking anyone. I am keeping the three verdicts separate rather than
> averaging them into one inequality score.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That more information automatically reduces inequality. That a
> specific ministry, platform, or policy is *the* cause. That a single number can rank all three
> effects. The framework returns a kind of thing, not the answer itself (Fig 9 :32), so the
> institutional name is a stated gap, not a hidden conclusion. Nothing here is `grounded`: this
> is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a measurement.
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these before the run:
> 1. a frame gains a term by pure composition with no import from a differing closure;
> 2. two frames with almost completely overlapping evidence still produce a surplus that survives
>    an independent ground reading;
> 3. two frames sharing a pool produce agreement that survives an independent ground reading;
> 4. an access intervention that leaves habitat and contact unchanged moves the ground-settled
>    inequality outcome;
> 5. one scalar predicts all three effects better than separate slot readings on held-out data.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is
> more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Please record your verdict on the claim above before reading anything else I have written.**
> That matters structurally, not politely: once you hold the full framework interior, your
> agreement is no longer an independent check (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the
> claim cannot verify it"*).
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Eight-step loop and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Public field venues for information inequality research: `03_public_contact_points.csv` in this packet
>
> I cannot host or send the packet for you; that part is yours.
