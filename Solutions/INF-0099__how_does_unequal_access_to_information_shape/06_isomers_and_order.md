## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Which operator states the differential?

- Operators table :48-49: `⋛` is *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, test: *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*
- Operators table :15: `+` conjunction is symmetric, test *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*
- The access differential is ordered (who has more reach), so `+` is wrong and `⋛` is correct. Negation is not a bond: *"Negation is not one: it takes a term and yields a term, and joins nothing"* (Fig 12 :135-136). So a refusal of a claim uses `¬`, not `⋛`.
- **Falsifier:** if the differential turns out to be non-ordered (a pure difference with no "more than"), `⋛` is mistyped and the symmetric `+` or the subtraction bond `−` governs. Written before the result.

### Q2 — What is the dependency order?

- Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- Link 1 perception: what each frame can access. Link 2 vocabulary: terms for what access affords. Link 3 objective: what would count as reduced inequality *in the frame's own terms*. Link 4 planning: a route under incomplete information. Link 5 learn and carry.
- Fig 3 :24-27: *"Why the middle link is where chains usually break. Perception has obvious failures and obvious tests. Planning has obvious failures and obvious tests. The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither."* And :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- ⇒ For information inequality, devices and bandwidth (link 1) are measured; the objective (link 3) is not. A program that optimizes link 1 cannot rescue link 3. This is the structural reason a more-connected world can still be more unequal in capability.
- **Falsifier:** an instrument that states the objective link for inequality and shows it has been working all along would dissolve the break. Triangulated (Fig 3 plus Fig 9 :54-58 on unbundling); `specified`.

### Q3 — Are power, coordination, and inequality isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- As the question writes them, the three effects do **not** share an atom set: power is built from frames and an unpinned anchor (Fig 2), coordination from frames plus a pinned ground (Fig 2 :35), inequality from agent-and-habitat (Fig 11). They are therefore **not isomers** under Fig 12's definition.
- They are better read as a **functional group** across three different substrates: each is a "differential reach composed against a ground", with the qualifier (which ground, which frame set) deciding the member. The same substructure, different atom sets.
- The stronger isomer claim would require writing all three over one shared atom set: `{frame, closure, ground, habitat, import}` with differences only in which bond is charged. That is a real checkable claim, but the figure does not license the shared set without an import, so it is `specified` and ungrounded.
- **Falsifier:** exhibit a composition for any one effect that uses an atom the other two do not require, under the shared-set version. That kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group reading standing. Written before the run.

### Q4 — What order is the union?

- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**
- The three effects are not settled molecules here (no ground reading), so none can serve as an atom for a higher composition yet. Their union is therefore **not** second-order in the sense the exemplar used for settled GR/QM molecules. It is a first-order compound of three unsettled loops, and the compound gets its own loop (Fig 9 :54-58).
- Fig 13 :114: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."** So adding a new arrangement of the three effects does not add a kind. The institutional name, if it comes, must be an import with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13), not a new arrangement.
- Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** The condensation from the figures into "differential reach, independence, habitat" carries a round-trip gap `R_T` that is measured in `11_audit.md`, not assumed zero.

### Q5 — Is a single inequality scalar a valid composition?

- Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**
- A scalar that sums power, coordination, and inequality mixes a description price with an action price and uses one unit for both. Fig 12 :129: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*
- ⇒ The three slots must be held apart. This is a second, independent route to F5 in `01`, reached from the bond-pricing figure rather than the slot figure. `specified`, triangulated internally (Fig 12, Fig 1).
- **Falsifier:** a ground reading in which bits and actions trade at a fixed rate for this question. That would license the sum.

## CONCLUSION

The differential itself is a `⋛` comparison, not a conjunction. The dependency order is access, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry, with the objective link the likely break and the least instrumented. The three named effects are not isomers as written; they are a functional group over different atom sets, and the stronger isomer claim needs a shared atom set that has to be imported. Their union is a first-order compound of unsettled loops, not a second-order composition over settled molecules. A single inequality scalar is refused because it adds two currencies that do not add.

Everything here is `specified` or `general` and ungrounded. The falsifiers were written before the conclusions.
