## Question restated in effect terms

There is a differential in what different frames can represent and reach (their libraries Γ, their instruments, their contact with the ground). We observe three named effects: power asymmetries, coordination patterns (alignment versus coherent drift), and persistent social inequality. The residual is the gap between the differential and each effect. What is being decided is the shape of that relation, not a magnitude and not a policy.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term (formula step, Direction).** The sought-for shape is written down here before the run: *unequal access is differential reach; its effects run through independence, the anchor, and the habitat, not through the volume of information held.* Binding it lets it accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as any other claim. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Fig 5 :62-64), so this binding is deliberate and recorded.

An answer would look like: one loop per named effect, with actors, currency, anchor, a verbatim figure line that governs it, a triangulation status, a refuter, and an honest statement of what the figures do not return (a kind of thing, never a name, Fig 9 :32).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03` are public or opt-in institutional routes.
