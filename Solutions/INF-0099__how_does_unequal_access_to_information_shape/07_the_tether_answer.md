# The Tether's answer: how unequal access to information shapes power, coordination, and social inequality

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the binding to these three effects, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Frames hold different libraries Γ and different reach. The question bundles three effects of that differential: power asymmetries, coordination patterns, and social inequality. It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-58): *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. It has three slots, and each slot gets its own loop. The residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), and aggregating across slots throws away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22).

## 2. The shape

1. **Access is reach, not stock.** A frame gains reach only on contact with a frame whose closure differs; composition inside its own closure never adds an atom. *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access"* (Fig 6 :26). Differential access is therefore a comparison `⋛` between reaches, ordered, not a sum.

2. **Power is influence over the pool, not possession.** Where frames update on one another instead of ranging against an anchor that does not move, *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"* (Fig 2 :14-15). With a common evidence pool, *"they never talk, and still agree"* (Fig 2 :19). The figure-grounded shape of power is a position in the update graph and control of what enters the shared pool. Correlation has valence: *"disqualifying for a verdict"*, *"efficient for a convention"* (Fig 2 :40-41).

3. **Coordination is triangulation or drift.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"* (Fig 2 :35). Working coordination requires independent frames ranging separately against an unmoved ground; a chain of agreeing frames is one long room in which *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). Differential access helps coordination only when the frames are independent; high overlap gives *"two names for one frame"* (Fig 8 :13).

4. **Social inequality is differential capability in agent-and-habitat.** *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* (Fig 11 :39). *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40). The seat's power over the habitat is asymmetric: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not"* (Fig 11 :61), so managing inequality is permanent work, not a solved state. Contact cascades several steps out (Fig 11 :52-53), so first-order enumeration of devices misses the effect that matters.

5. **The three effects are a functional group, not isomers as written, and not one number.** They share a substructure (differential reach composed against a ground) over different atom sets, which is Fig 12 :124's functional group. Fig 12 :125-126 adds that bits price the description and actions price finding out whether it holds, in two currencies that do not add. So a single averaged inequality score is refused (Fig 1 :22).

6. **The institutional name is a gap.** *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). The missing thing must have the shape of a differing closure, an unmoved anchor, or a changed contact. The figures do not name which institution or policy. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134), so the gap is recorded rather than filled by guesswork.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that more information automatically reduces inequality. Fig 11 :40 forbids that inference.
- Not that any specific ministry, platform, law, or curriculum is *the* cause. That is a name where the framework returns a shape (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that the three effects are one measurable quantity. They are three slots (Fig 1 :19), held apart (Fig 12 :125-129).
- Not that agreement among experts settles anything. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation"* (Fig 2 :35); *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that adding frames to a room adds evidence. *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this run settled `left(R,φ)` for any slot. All three verdicts are `candidate`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The candidate readings are:

- **Access test:** does adding a term to a frame's reach require an import from a differing closure, or can it be done by pure composition? An instrument that distinguishes these settles F1.
- **Overlap test:** do two frames with almost completely overlapping evidence still produce a union surplus that survives an independent ground reading? Settles F2.
- **Anchor test:** does convergence follow influence when no anchor is pinned, and does agreement survive an independent ground reading when frames share a pool? Settles F3.
- **Habitat test:** does an intervention that raises access without changing habitat or contact move the ground-settled inequality outcome? This is the key test, and it settles F4. It is the one most likely to reverse the verdict.
- **Slot test:** does one scalar predict all three effects better than separate slot readings on held-out data? Settles F5.
- **Name test:** a provenanced import with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13) that names a specific institution and survives a ground reading. This would close the recorded gap.

Standing refuters from `01` §3 are unchanged. Any one firing withdraws its slot.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreachable for the name at this budget.** The six-term substrate is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list; the canonical README calls it *"a human's careful guess"*. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The stop is at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this run's evidence pool, so by Fig 2 :40 its agreement is the pool talking, not a second witness. Prestige is not action: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it"* (essay, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched 2026-11-23).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single inequality number and does not name a culprit. It says that unequal access is differential reach: a frame gains reach only by contact with a differing closure, because composition inside its own closure never adds an atom. It then returns three separate shapes. Power is influence over the shared evidence pool when no anchor is pinned. Coordination is triangulation against an unmoved ground, and a chain of agreeing frames is a mirror that compounds agreement without adding evidence. Social inequality is differential capability in an agent-and-habitat pair, maintained by asymmetric introduction power and compounding through contact cascades several steps out. The three effects are a functional group over different atom sets, not one scalar, because description bits and settling actions are two currencies that do not add. The framework returns the kind of thing that is missing, not the institution that causes it, so the name is a stated gap. What it offers is the shape per slot, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each one. The gaps stay open until the ground pays.
