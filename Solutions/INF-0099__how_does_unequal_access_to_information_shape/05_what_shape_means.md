## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the searcher's obstacle: *"Your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers, not the vastness of the library."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains and does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: unequal access is differential reach; its effects run through independence, the anchor, and the habitat.
- A name: a specific ministry, platform, tax, or curriculum is the cause. The shape is licensed by the figures; the name is not (§3).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, correlate it with recurring new terms, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is exactly the situation in INF-0099: power, coordination, and inequality arrive as one question but are three conditions (Fig 9 :54-58).
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* (Fig 9 :30-31). Hence "differential reach" instead of "digital divide": the first is an effect term, the second is a borrowed name that already presupposes a field.
- On stopping: *Library of Babel* says the end comes when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this run the stop is the recorded gap at the institutional name, not a final answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. An import adds no atom to the world, it gives a frame access (Fig 6 :25-26).

For this question, the world sources in `09` are exactly such imports: economics, communication studies, network science, cognitive science. Their agreement with the shape is corroboration from different closures, not a name provided by the figures. The institutional name still needs a ground reading (an instrument that shows which intervention moves the outcome), and none was taken here. So the name stays a stated gap.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

A reading does not determine its source. This is the metamerism point of *The Hard Problem is About Source* (not re-fetched this run, so cited only through the local README's *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* and not quoted as an essay line). Concretely here: a survey showing that more broadband correlates with less inequality reads one thing from several possible sources. It could be differential reach, a changed habitat, an anchor that started moving, or a measurement channel that saturated (Fig 10 :25-28). An instrument that discriminates those sources is owed, not another argument.

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. For information inequality the channel is the survey instrument, the platform metric, the administrative record. A channel can stay green while losing contact with the effect it was built to measure (Seat Map :285: *"Every instrument points inward unless one is built to point out"*).

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does unequal access have?** A threefold constraint, not a cause and not a number:

1. It is a differential in reach, available only through contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23, :26). It is not a stock of atoms.
2. Its value is set by overlap, not volume (Fig 8 :48-49). Low overlap yields a union surplus; high overlap yields two names for one frame (Fig 8 :13).
3. Its effects are slot-specific. Power follows influence in the update graph (Fig 2 :14-15); coordination is triangulation or drift (Fig 2 :35, Fig 7 :13); inequality is differential capability in agent-and-habitat with asymmetric introduction power (Fig 11 :39-40, :61-62).

**What licenses a particular institution as the answer?** Nothing in the framework. A particular institution is licensed only by a ground reading or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The figures give the kind of thing: a differing closure, an unmoved anchor, a changed contact. They do not give the name.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous information-inequality advocates gathered to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an outside frame refute it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. Mode on the whole folder: `general` / `specified`, never `grounded`.

**Declare the stake.** Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* The author benefits from the framework being found useful. That interest in the outcome is unavoidable and recorded; an interest in a particular reading is disqualifying and refused. Seat Map :171: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
