## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :21, :23, :26 (access is reach), Fig 8 :48-49 (overlap), Fig 2 :14-15, :35, :40 (power and coordination), Fig 11 :39-40, :61-62 (habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: unequal access is differential reach; its effects run through independence, the anchor, and the habitat; three slots, never averaged.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about information inequality, for example: unequal broadband access gives one group power over the evidence pool, breaks coordination, and causes social inequality, so the remedy is to distribute devices.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The overlap condition.** The concrete claim assumes more reach always helps. Fig 8 :48-49 says the value depends on overlap; :13 says high-overlap union adds nothing.
- **The anchor condition.** The concrete claim treats coordination failure as a information deficit. Fig 2 :35 says it is a failure of triangulation, which needs an unmoved anchor, not more data.
- **The contact condition.** The concrete claim makes device distribution the lever. Fig 11 :40 says an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing.
- **The slot separation.** The concrete claim averages three effects into one inequality outcome. Fig 1 :19-22 and Fig 12 :125-126 refuse the average.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: access is reach won by contact with a differing closure, and the three effects are different compositions against a ground. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
