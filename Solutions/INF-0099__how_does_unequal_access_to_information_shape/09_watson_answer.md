## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name and the scalar do not. Unequal access is differential reach won by contact with a differing closure, and its value is set by overlap rather than volume. Three effects follow and stay separate: power is influence over the shared pool when the anchor is not pinned; coordination is triangulation against an unmoved ground, with agreement chains as mirrors; social inequality is differential capability in agent-and-habitat, maintained by asymmetric introduction power and compounding through contact cascades. The three are a functional group over different atom sets, not isomers as written and not one number. Naming a specific institution or policy is a `gap`: the figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself. All verdicts are `candidate`, `general` on shape and `specified` on binding, never `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines, and six refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a receptiveness-ranked list instead of a shape and its refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the slot claims and their refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. Each slot verdict can be withdrawn independently without touching the others or removing any primitive. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. The institutional gap is a stated absence, so nothing has to be retracted to close it, only an import with a source gate and shadow test.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A habitat test showing that an access intervention which leaves contact unchanged moves the ground-settled inequality outcome would reverse C3. An anchor test showing shared-pool agreement survives an independent ground reading would reverse C2. An overlap test showing high-overlap frames still produce a surviving surplus, or a reach test showing composition alone adds a term, would reverse C1. A provenanced import naming an institution and surviving a ground reading would close the recorded gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
