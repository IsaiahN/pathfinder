## RESIDUAL

The question asks for "combinations" as though the four mechanisms were ingredients in a recipe. Two supporting questions are owed: (1) is a four-item list a molecule, or only a list of atoms; (2) if there is an order, what is it, and can it be derived without importing a country-specific theory of reform?

## DOMAIN CENSUS (summary; full census in `01` section 1)

R is a union compound of opportunity, discretion, motive and weak constraint (Fig 9 :55, :57). Domains D1 oversight, D2 transparency, D3 enforcement, D4 participation have anchors that are nameable only at case level: a behaviour record, a disclosure that is read, a consequence that occurs, a correction an insider could not produce about itself. ## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Is "oversight + transparency + enforcement + participation" a molecule?

- Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**
- Fig 12 :111-112: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one. A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."**
- Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*.

**Reading.** The four names are atoms in the sense of being states of an institution. The bonds between them are: a record that feeds a check (D1 to D2), a check that feeds a consequence (D1 or D2 to D3), an outside frame that supplies a correction the insiders could not produce (D4 to D1), and a ground that corrects each frame separately. Without those bonds the list records ingredients and not a route. This is a notation finding, not an empirical one. **Mode `general`. Triangulated** across Fig 12 and the Operators table, which differ in what they enumerate.

**Falsifier.** A programme specified only as a four-item checklist that nonetheless states its dependency order, its losing condition and its progress measure, and is checked at the ground.

### Q2 - What is the dependency order?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**
- Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
- Fig 3 :4-16 gives the links: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry.

**Reading.** Applied to accountability:

| link in Fig 3 | accountability reading | can it be assessed? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 perception | can anyone read the behaviour at all? | yes, a transaction log or audit record |
| 2 vocabulary | are there terms for what the situation affords, what can be checked, blocked or sanctioned? | often **assumed**, which is Fig 3's middle break |
| 3 objective | can we say what would count as progress against this residual? | often a perception index, which is an instrument, not an objective |
| 4 planning | is there a route from a finding to a consequence? | yes, if enforcement is installed |
| 5 learn and carry | is the method carried up and replayed as a head start, with a measured R_T? | rarely |

So the order is: make the ground reachable, name the objective in case terms, secure independence of the checking frames, install a violable rule with a consequence, then compose and carry. Starting with enforcement before a reachable anchor and a checkable objective spends budget above the break. **Mode `specified`. Triangulated** across Fig 3 and Fig 10 (the channel must be reachable).

**Falsifier.** A reform sequence that starts with sanctions before any reachably recorded behaviour or checkable objective, and is shown at the ground to reduce the residual without producing the other three conditions.

### Q3 - Union or partition, and what is unbundled?

- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- Fig 9 :55-57 (quoted in `01` F1).
- Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.

**Reading.** The overlap regions are compounds: captured oversight (D1 fails, D2 and D3 present), unread transparency (D2 fails, D1 present), toothless enforcement (D3 fails, D1 and D2 present), authored participation (D4 fails). A programme that adds a mechanism to all four regions clears the single-condition regions and leaves the compounds. Unbundling means naming the compound before searching for a lever. This is the structural reason the question's "most reliably" cannot be read off a pooled cross-country regression. **Mode `general` for the union rule, `specified` for the four compounds.**

**Falsifier.** Show the four failure regions behaving additively, so that one extra mechanism reduces the residual equally in each and no categorically different residue remains.

### Q4 - Why "more monitors" is not the same as more independence

- Fig 8 :48-49: **"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."**
- Fig 2 :44 (the effective-sample-size warning, quoted in `05` section 4).
- Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**

**Reading from low-overlap fields.** The society-of-agents essay, opened 2026-11-23, reports Kish's correction as `N / (1 + (N-1)rho)` and gives the same scaling limits for groups: *"however many join, the group never counts as more than 1/rho, and ten clones count as one."* It also cites the Universal Scalability Law, where a coherence cost that grows as N(N-1) makes *"past a peak... adding more makes the group slower."* In accountability terms, adding monitors drawn from the same ministry, briefed from the same file and graded on the same indicator raises ρ and the coherence cost. The number of monitors grows; the effective number of independent checks does not. **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 8, Fig 7 and the statistics/engineering literature (low overlap with the corruption field).

**Falsifier.** Ten checking bodies that share one source and one criterion producing ten independent corroborations at the ground.

### Q5 - Is there a standing "best combination" across cases?

- Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
- Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**
- Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

**Reading.** No. Which condition is missing changes the compound. A country where the auditor is captured needs a different lever from one where findings are never acted on, even though both score the same on a composite index. The framework can enumerate the habitats and tell you which condition binds; it cannot fix a global ranking without a case-level instrument. The society-of-agents essay records Ostrom's polycentric governance as a parallel, explicitly noting that the structures *"rhymed"* while the vocabularies and paper trails differed, which is Fig 8's convergent derivation, not an adopted import. **Mode `specified`. Status `single-source`** for the figure governance with an outside parallel that carries no verdict weight.

**Falsifier.** A stable ranking of the four conditions across a sample of cases in which the binding condition is shown to be the same in every case, with ground readings in each.

## CONCLUSION

The four mechanisms are atoms, not a molecule; the bonds are the record-to-check, check-to-consequence, outside-correction and ground-correction relations. The dependency order runs from a reachable anchor to a nameable objective to independent frames to an installed violation to composition and carry. The four conditions form a union whose overlaps are categorically different compounds, so scaling monitors is not the same as scaling independence, and no standing global ranking follows from the figures. What survives is the ordered shape and its falsifiers. The claim that one combination is "most reliable" remains a `gap` until case-level ground measurements close it.
