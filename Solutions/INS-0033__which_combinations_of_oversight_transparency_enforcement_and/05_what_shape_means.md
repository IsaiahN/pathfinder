# What "shape" means for INS-0033 - canonical reading

**Sources.** The eight essays by Isaiah N are the canonical grounding for the word "shape." Six were opened directly on 2026-11-23: *The Hard Problem is About Source*, *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, *The Library of Babel*, *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, and the Medium architecture article *Arrival of the Fittest*. Two (*How Intelligence Actually Works*, *They're Scanning Books When They Should*) were not opened in this session and are not quoted here as first-hand readings. The canonical usage rule is the local `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, also opened this session. Figure quotes come from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* It adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it, and before you can rank its instances. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Not a shape:** "oversight plus transparency plus enforcement plus participation is the best combination."
- **A shape:** "any working combination must have a non-updating anchor, independent checking frames, an installed violation with a consequence, and an unauthored outside pool; which subset wins in a given case is a ground question."

The ranking is not a small omission at the end. It is a different kind of object, a measurement over cases rather than a constraint over shapes.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "more oversight," notice the new term "capture," then "independence," then "anchor," and the space narrows from a headcount to a relation.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So say "a monitor whose finding moves when the monitored objects," not "weak institutional capacity."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet leads with failure conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is earlier: the shape is complete, the ranking is not, and that distinction is the honest place to stop.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why a ranking is further away still

Only two things license a particular name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters for anti-corruption indices. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK could all give that reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that settled the source: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A perception index reads a projection, an aggregate of expert assessments, and names a source: "this country is corrupt." Several paths can give the same score. Naming "the best mechanism combination" from that score is the same metamerism error one level up: a ranking read off a reading that does not discriminate among the sources that produced it. What is owed is a discriminator, not an ordering.

## 4. Independence is a form of not-knowing, and it is the scarce input

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the property in terms a corruption researcher will recognise: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* And: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* It lists the two collapses: agents update on each other until they converge on *"whoever had the most influence"*, or they never talk but share one source, so *"the agreement is the pool talking."* It also states the contribution of an outside frame in this question's own vocabulary: *"The arrangement mattered more than the members, which is Figure 12's 'The arrangement is the substance.'"*

For unauthored selection the essay gives the mechanisms: *"a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed."* For the check of independence by contact it says frames must be corrected separately against something that cannot be moved, and that long close contact *"slowly uses up that extra reach."* An oversight body that has worked with the same auditee for years is more calibrated and less independent, which is the shelf-life rule, not a personnel failing.

Fig 2 :44 names the arithmetic: *"The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."* The essay's society-of-agents example makes it concrete: N samples that resemble each other by an amount ρ count as only `N / (1 + (N-1)rho)` independent ones, so ten clones count as one.

## 5. The combination question is an arrangement question

Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :111-112: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one. A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."** The Medium article's citation list marks Goodhart, Campbell and Strathern as instances of *"what happens when a measure becomes a target"*, which is exactly the failure of a published indicator that becomes the thing being maximised. A four-item checklist is a state description; it has no bond, no order, no comparison and no progress measure. The shape supplies those without pretending to know the ranking.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The same essay: *"someone let prestige stand in for proof... Someone let reputation substitute for result."* A packet addressed to famous anti-corruption names in the hope that they agree is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets a ground, a case record or an instrument, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The Medium article is blunter: LLM output with the formula attached produces *"plausible simulated output"* and must be seen as *"simulacra"* because *"the library is unknown to us (black-box training data) which is not Framework Compliant."* It also says the outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Answer to the one question this file owes

**Does "shape" license saying which combination is most reliable?** No. The shape licenses the four conditions and the failure modes. A reliability ordering over subsets is a comparison across cases, and the framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). The honest stop is: four conditions stated, refuters registered, ranking declared `gap` until a case-level ground measures it. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).
