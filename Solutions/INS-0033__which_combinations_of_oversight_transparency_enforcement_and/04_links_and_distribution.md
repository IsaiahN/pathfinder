## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The field's own formula is a heuristic, not a law.** The World Bank version of Klitgaard's *Corrupt Cities* calls the arithmetic *"metaphorical in many senses, not least in the notion of addition and subtraction"* (seen in the search result for the IMF article read 2026-11-23). This corroborates F1: the four mechanisms cannot be summed into a ranking by inspection.
2. **Enforcement is a conditional, not a main effect.** The IMF article pairs transparency with *"the probability of being caught, as well as the penalties for corruption"*; the EU toolbox says irregularities *"need to bear consequences"*. Both are field-level corroboration of Fig 10's violable convention.
3. **The measurement ground is contested inside the field.** UNODC distinguishes direct experience data from perception indexes; Transparency International explains what its index omits. These are two first-party sources agreeing on the limitation, but they share the same measurement discourse, so this is one witness with two voices, not independent corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
4. **A low-overlap witness for independence exists.** Design effect and effective sample size are standard survey statistics, and the figure text itself cites them (Fig 2 :44). The agreement between the figure and the statistics page is structural: correlation reduces effective sample size. It corroborates the mechanism, not the corruption ranking.
5. **A critique from outside the reform community is useful.** Stephenson's 2014 critique is recorded because an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59). It does not by itself settle anything.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four conditions and the refuter; offer links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a method each user runs against their own ground with their own refuter.
