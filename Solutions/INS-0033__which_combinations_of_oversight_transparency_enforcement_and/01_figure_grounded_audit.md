## 0. The question under audit

> Which combinations of oversight, transparency, enforcement, and public participation most reliably constrain corruption?

Restated as an effect before any figure line was chosen:

- **Actors and move.** A principal (citizens, a ministry, a board) expects an agent (official, contractor, office) to act on a public good. The agent has discretion. Corruption is the residual between the public-serving action predicted and the action observed, measured at an institutional slot, not at a country.
- **Option A (the common reading):** the four mechanisms are four ingredients; more ingredients reduce corruption monotonically; a cross-country index can rank the subsets.
- **Option B (the derive-first reading):** the four are overlapping conditions in a union; what decides is the arrangement, namely whether checking frames are independent, whether an anchor exists that does not move when frames are pushed, whether rules can be violated and consequences occur, and whether the pool of outside frames was authored by someone with a stake. The reliability ranking is then a ground question, not a figure question.
- **Direction term, bound in advance:** a *shape*, meaning (i) the necessary structural conditions of a working combination, (ii) an instrument that discriminates which condition fails in a case, and (iii) an honest `gap` for the empirical ordering. Until this shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check inside the loop can see it (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-68).

## 1. Domain census (done before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 The residual by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** multiple. Corruption is a compound of opportunity, discretion, motive and weak constraint. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. The principal/agent and monitor/monitored relations are ordered; swapping the operands changes the thing. Oversight of A by B is not oversight of B by A.
- **Scale:** three levels, transaction to organization to system, with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4). What stops corruption at a procurement desk is not a scaled copy of what stops it in a national integrity system.

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Oversight / monitoring** | an overseer frame and an overseen frame | readings, verdicts, access to records | a behaviour record that does not move when either frame is asked. Nameable: an audit finding, a transaction log, a court record | admitted |
| **D2 Transparency / visibility** | multiple reader frames plus a published record | observability, independence, reach of the publication | the same unmoving behaviour record, seen by frames that do not share one evidence pool. Nameable: disclosure that is actually read and used | admitted |
| **D3 Enforcement / consequences** | a rule, an enforcer, a sanction, the sanctioned | cost of violation; probability of being caught | a violable convention with a consequence that occurs. Nameable: a prosecution, a dismissal, a recovered payment | admitted |
| **D4 Public participation / outside frames** | citizens, media, civil society, outside experts | variation between checking rooms; independence | the ground selecting between rooms, not the room's internal agreement. Nameable: a finding a participant could not have produced about itself | admitted |
| **X1 "National integrity score" as the anchor** | index producers and index consumers | reputation, perception | **not nameable as an anchor.** It moves when the experts who compose it are asked, and it is constitutively about opinion rather than about the behaviour it estimates | not a domain yet |
| **X2 "More oversight" as a stand-alone cause** | the overseer alone | headcount of monitors | already inside D1; adding monitors without independence adds spellings, not a frame | absorbed into D1 |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. X1 is therefore excluded as an anchor and admitted only as an *instrument*, which can be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38: *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"*).

### 1.3 Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** D1 to D4 overlap: oversight without visibility is a monitor reading a sealed record; visibility without outside frames is one frame wearing many names; enforcement without an independent finding punishes the wrong person; participation without a reachable ground is noise. A single lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). This is why "which combination is best" cannot be answered by adding four main effects.

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. **Perception and a reachable anchor** (can anyone read the behaviour at all?),
2. **Vocabulary / objective** (can we say what would count as progress against this residual?),
3. **Independence of checking frames**,
4. **A violable convention with a consequence**,
5. **Composition into a combination**, then learning and carrying.

Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* In accountability work the middle link, naming what would count as progress, is the one most often assumed rather than measured (Fig 3 :24-26).

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The four mechanisms form a union, not an additive stack (decisive for the form of the answer)

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

World-side, Klitgaard's formula is explicitly a heuristic, not an arithmetic law: the original notes call it *"metaphorical in many senses, not least in the notion of addition and subtraction"* (World Bank, *Corrupt Cities*, footnote 5, read 2026-11-23). So even the field's own most famous stack disclaims additivity.

**Reading.** The question's phrase "combinations ... most reliably" invites a ranking of subsets. The figures say the object is a compound with its own loop. A single lever, for example publishing procurement data, clears the region where only visibility was missing and leaves the intersection where the monitor was captured or no sanction followed. **Mode `general` for the union shape; `specified` for the four-domain application.**

**Refuter.** Show a case family in which the four conditions are independent, each lever's effect is additive, and the intersection behaves as a harder instance of one condition rather than a categorically different compound.

### F2 - Oversight constrains only while the monitor does not update on the monitored and the anchor does not move

Fig 2 :13: **"no anchor; each updates on the others"**; :14-15: **"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"**; :25: **"pinned: it does not update on A or B"**; :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**; :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**.

**Reading.** An anti-corruption agency whose findings are negotiated with the ministry it watches, or an auditor whose budget is set by the auditee, is the first collapse: the monitor has become a frame that updates on the monitored. The remedy is not a larger oversight budget but a channel to a record neither can move. Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** The 2026 OECD work on supreme audit institution independence (seen in search, fetch returned 403, not used as an opened source) and the opened Wikipedia entry on SAIs both describe independence as the operative property, not headcount. **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 2 (anchor rule) and Fig 10 (channel rule), two figures with low overlap in their starting point.

**Refuter.** Produce a working oversight arrangement in which the checking frame is corrected by the checked frame's opinion of it and the record still settles disputes. Under the figures this cannot happen; if it does, F2 is wrong.

### F3 - Transparency buys nothing without low-overlap frames reading it

Fig 8 :48-49: **"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."** Fig 2 :31-32: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** and **"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."** Fig 2 :44: **"The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."**

**Reading.** Publishing a dataset to a thousand readers who all learned from the same manual, the same leaked file and the same lead is one reader wearing a thousand names. The European Commission's own practitioner toolbox, opened 2026-11-23, says the same thing in field language: *"the existence of open data itself does not automatically translate into lower corruption"* and the data must reach users *"both willing and able to use information for fighting corruption."* Fig 8 :47: **"Independence is one quantity read twice."** **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 8 and Fig 2, with the statistics literature on design effect (opened 2026-11-23) as a low-overlap corroboration of the arithmetic, not of the corruption claim.

**Refuter.** Show independent corroboration of a corruption finding where every corroborating reader draws on the same disclosure and the same evidence pool.

### F4 - Enforcement is a violable convention with a consequence, not a published rule

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**; :19: **"install what can be violated"**; :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**; :53: **"And a prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong."**; :54: **"A rule no real occupant could meet describes an idealisation, not a position."** Seat Map :271 names the silent failure *"Produced, recorded, and not read"* and :273 the sharpest form, a switch never thrown. Fig 2 :43: **"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**

**Reading.** A code of conduct that no official can comply with, or a disclosure form nobody reads, is a declared meaning with no violation installed. Enforcement is the part of the combination that makes the ground bite; without it the other three mechanisms only produce records. The EU toolbox, opened 2026-11-23, states the dependency in field terms: *"observed irregularities need to bear consequences, meaning that law enforcement should be able to act on uncovered risks."* **Mode `specified`. Status `single-source`** for the figure governance (Fig 10 plus Seat Map, same framework pool) with strong practitioner corroboration that carries no independent verdict weight.

**Refuter.** Show an arrangement where a rule has no possible violation and no consequence, yet behaviour changes in the way the rule prescribes, with a ground reading to prove it.

### F5 - Public participation supplies unauthored outside rooms, not a headcount

Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**; :41: **"And the pool it is drawn from must be unauthored. See Figures 10 and 11."** Fig 10 :73-74: **"A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second."** Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

**Reading.** A consultation whose participants are hand-picked by the agency under investigation is an authored room. Citizen monitoring is valuable not because it adds bodies but because it can carry a different closure, different failure modes, and therefore a correction on shape that insiders read past. Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** The Open Government Partnership page, opened 2026-11-23, describes its Independent Reporting Mechanism as *"independent, evidence-based, and objective reporting to hold OGP members accountable"*, which is the same office in field language. **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 7 and Fig 10, with Fig 11 adding the habitat rule.

**Refuter.** Show a participation exercise whose participant set was selected by the body being checked, whose agreement is treated as a verdict, and which nonetheless refutes an insider claim at the ground.

### F6 - The framework returns the shape, not the ranking (the empirical part is a gap)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :141: **"Which is what a hard decision needs and rarely gets: not only what was concluded, but what could have been concluded, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ."**

**Reading.** After a genuine re-census, no figure line ranks oversight plus transparency plus enforcement plus participation above any other subset by reliability. That is not a failure of the search; it is the correct output for a question whose answer depends on which condition fails in which case. The figures can say what a working combination must contain. They cannot say which of sixteen subsets wins in a given country without case-level ground readings. **Mode `general` for the limit. Status `gap` for the ranking.** "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

**Refuter.** Derive a reliability ordering of the sixteen subsets from figure lines alone, with no case-level measurement, and show it survives the strain test. Until then the ranking is not claimed.

### F7 - The evidence pool warning applies directly to anti-corruption indices

Fig 2 :39: **"Correlation has valence."** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**; :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

**Reading.** Transparency International's CPI explainer, opened 2026-11-23, says a country's score is *"a combination of at least 3 data sources drawn from 13 different corruption surveys and assessments."* The UNODC anti-corruption teaching module, opened 2026-11-23, distinguishes direct experience-based measures from indirect perception measures and says experience-based methods are *"far more precise than indirect or composite methods."* A critique of the CPI opened via ScienceDirect's abstract states that *"The CPI's data sources are closely related to the World Bank's Control of Corruption."* These are not independent witnesses to a ground; they overlap heavily. An index may be a useful filter for where to look. It is not a witness for which mechanism combination works. **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 2 and Fig 9.

**Refuter.** Show that two anti-corruption indices rank mechanisms identically while drawing on non-overlapping primary measurements, with provenance for both.

### F8 - The four-item list is ingredients, not a route

Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :111-112: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one. A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."** Operators table :51: **"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"**.

**Reading.** "Oversight, transparency, enforcement and participation" names four states of an institution. It does not say which comes first, what each consumes, or how a failure of one is detected. Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** The derived answer must supply the order and the comparison, otherwise it is a flat formula, which Fig 12 warns records the ingredients and not the arrangement. **Mode `general` for the notation point, `specified` for this question.**

**Refuter.** Show an anti-corruption programme specified only as a four-item checklist that nonetheless states its own dependency order, failure condition and progress measure.

### F9 - Stakes must be declared, including the standing of the reformer

Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"**; :60: **"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."** Fig 11 :49 (quoted in F5) makes the same point for actors: valence is relative to the residual.

**Reading.** A reformer who benefits from a finding that the reform worked cannot be the frame that checks the finding. The 2014 critique by Matthew Stephenson of Klitgaard's formula, opened 2026-11-23, is a useful outside frame precisely because it does not share the reform community's stake in the formula: *"framing this point as a 'formula' - which implies that it is an immutable law - is inaccurate and misleading."* **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 10 and Fig 11.

**Refuter.** Show a reform evaluation in which the evaluating body's standing from a previous success is declared and demonstrably does not affect which value the ground returns.

### F10 - No scaling without a measured round-trip gap

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

**Reading.** A mechanism that works in one procurement agency cannot be carried upward as a recording and replayed downward into a national programme. What crosses is the method: enumerate the residual, name the anchor, keep the checking frames independent, install a violable rule, draw the outside pool unauthored. The difference between the case-level shape and the programme-level claim is R_T and must be measured, not assumed zero. **Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated`** across Fig 4 and the formula's step 6.

**Refuter.** Show a national anti-corruption rollout whose case-level mechanisms transfer with zero loss, with a ground reading at both levels and a measured gap of zero.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A working arrangement is demonstrated in which the checking frame updates on the checked frame and the record moves with opinion (breaks F2).
2. Transparency is shown to produce independent corroboration when all readers draw on one evidence pool (breaks F3).
3. A rule with no possible violation and no consequence is shown to change behaviour at the ground (breaks F4).
4. A participant set authored by the body under check is shown to refute an insider claim at the ground and its agreement is then treated as a verdict (breaks F5).
5. A reliability ordering of the sixteen subsets is derived from figure lines alone, with no case-level measurement (breaks F6) or, conversely, a case-level ground measurement settles the ordering, in which case the `gap` is closed by data rather than by argument.
6. Two anti-corruption indices with non-overlapping primary measurements are shown to rank mechanisms identically (weakens F7).
7. The round trip between a case-level mechanism and a national programme is measured with R_T = 0 (breaks F10).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the verdict in `07` and `09` is revised. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

**Distinction kept explicit.** SEARCHED (is the ranking already in the figures and unfound?), INWARD (extend the case-level instrument), OUTWARD (import a ground measurement from a frame whose closure differs). This audit answers SEARCHED: no ranking is present. The empirical ranking needs INWARD and OUTWARD work, not more composition.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Stop answering the question as asked. The phrase "most reliably combin[ation]" presupposes a ranking the figures do not yield. Answer the shape question and declare the ranking a `gap` (F6).
2. Lead with the union finding and the anchor rule (F1, F2). Those are the two strongest figure-derived points and they are cheap to check.
3. Treat every published index as a filter for where to look, never as a witness for the verdict (F7, Fig 9 :60).
4. Ask for a refutation of the four conditions, not agreement with them. Route through the opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
5. Carry the generator, not the conclusion: the residual, the conditions, the refuter. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives: oversight needs a non-updating anchor; transparency needs low-overlap readers; enforcement needs an installed violation and a consequence; participation needs an unauthored pool selected against the residual; and the four form a union whose arrangement, not headcount, decides what is cleared. The empirical claim does not survive: no figure line ranks the subsets by reliability, and the perception indices in the field are instruments sharing an evidence pool, not anchors. Strains after re-census: none hidden; two live boundaries recorded, the convention/enforcement distinction in F4 and the case-to-system round trip in F10. `Mode: specified`. `Status: candidate`. `Empirical ranking: gap`.
