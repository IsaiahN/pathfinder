# The Tether's answer on mechanism combinations and corruption

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Corruption is not one hard problem and the four mechanisms are not four ingredients. The contact failure is a **union compound** of opportunity, discretion, motive and weak constraint (Fig 9 :55-57): *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It has its own loop and its own ground. The residual is measured per institutional slot, never averaged into a score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

## 2. The shape

1. **Oversight requires a non-updating anchor.** A monitor that is corrected by the monitored is another frame in the first collapse, and the two converge on whoever had the most influence (Fig 2 :13-15). The record must be something neither can move. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
2. **Transparency requires low-overlap readers.** Independence is *"how little they draw on the same evidence"* (Fig 2 :31-32). One disclosure read by a hundred people who share a source is one reader wearing a hundred names (Fig 8 :48-49). Open data alone does not lower corruption in the field's own practitioner guidance.
3. **Enforcement requires an installed violation and a consequence.** *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one → install what can be violated"* (Fig 10 :18-19). A rule no real occupant can satisfy, or a report nobody reads, is a convention that never bites. *"observed irregularities need to bear consequences"* (EU toolbox, opened 2026-11-23).
4. **Public participation requires an unauthored pool.** *"A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second"* (Fig 10 :73-74). The selection is against the residual, not against a standing list of good actors (Fig 11 :49-51).
5. **The four are a union, not a stack.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The arrangement, not the headcount, carries the information (Fig 12 :94).
6. **The order is dependency, not importance.** Reach a record, name what counts as progress, keep frames independent, install the consequence, then compose and carry with a measured round-trip gap (Fig 3 :20, Fig 4 :30).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranking of the sixteen subsets. No figure line governs that comparison and no case-level measurement was taken. This is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- Not that a perception index is the anchor. An index is an instrument and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :37-38). It is a filter for where to look, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that more oversight, more disclosure, more rules or more participants is monotonically better. Correlated monitors and a coherence cost can make a larger group worse (Fig 8 :48-49 and the scaling analogues in `06` Q4).
- Not that the framework names a universally best reform sequence. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not `grounded`. No case-level reading of corruption was used.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating case instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Anchor test:** does the behaviour record remain unchanged when the monitor and the monitored are asked to revise it? An instrument that shows it moving disqualifies it as an anchor.
- **Independence test:** what is the effective number of checking frames after the correlation correction, `N / (1 + (N-1)rho)`? If it stays near one as N grows, transparency is not producing corroboration.
- **Violation test:** can a real occupant violate the rule, and does a consequence follow? A rule with no possible violation is *"a prohibition stated wrong"* (Fig 10 :53).
- **Authorship test:** was the participant pool assembled before the question existed, by lottery, from everyone who qualifies, or from the habitat enumerated? If a participant chose it, the room is authored.
- **Compound test:** in a case where two conditions fail, does clearing one leave a categorically different residue as Fig 9 :57 predicts?
- **Ranking test:** a case-level, pre-registered comparison of the subsets against a direct experience-based measure, with provenance for each measurement. This is the instrument that closes the `gap`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Shape reached, ranking unreached at this budget.** The distinction matters: underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget issue different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). The ranking is reachable in principle with case-level measurements; it is not in the closure of the figures, and more composition of figure lines will not produce it. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134), and *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39). The six-term substrate and the arrangement rules are a visible guess, not nature's own list; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning combination of oversight, transparency, enforcement and participation, and it does not claim a ranking. It says the four are overlapping conditions in a union, not ingredients in a stack. A working combination needs a record neither the monitor nor the monitored can move, readers who do not share one evidence pool, a rule that can actually be violated and a consequence that follows, and an outside pool that was not hand-picked by the body being checked. Those conditions have an order: reach the ground first, say what would count as progress, keep the checking frames independent, install the violation, then compose and carry. The framework returns that shape, the instruments that would test each condition, and refuters written in advance. Which subset wins in a given country is a case-level measurement, and the answer stops there until one is taken.
