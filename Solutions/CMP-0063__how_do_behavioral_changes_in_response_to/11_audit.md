## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40 (contact), Fig 12 :119, :121 (catalyst, cycle), Fig 9 :57 (union compound), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: a coupled compound whose output re-enters through contact; sign and delay decide damping or waves; perceived risk per slot; waves reopen via a persisting part.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete epidemic statement: "fading protective behavior caused this wave, so reduce the reporting delay."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **refusal to name the sign**; the concrete statement names fading awareness as the cause from a curve alone, which is the metamerism error;
- the **distinction between sign and delay**; the concrete statement collapses them into a reporting-delay fix;
- the **union structure**; the concrete statement treats the wave as a disease-only problem and ignores the behavioral intersection.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: behavior acts only through contact, and the coupling is a cycle. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
