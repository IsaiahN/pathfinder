## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

Applied here, a **shape** is the form any answer about behavioral feedback must have before we can name the sign, the delay, or the magnitude. "A coupled compound whose output re-enters as input through contact, with a sign and a delay" is a shape. "A 40 percent contact reduction at day 12 with a 6-day reporting lag" is a name, and it belongs to a particular population and a particular pathogen. The shape constrains; it does not fill.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the nearest vocabulary is contact, feedback, perceived risk, and threshold.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." That is Fig 2 in prose: frames range against the ground.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." So the outreach packet states what would be observed if the loop were real, not what theory produced it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, the concepts "awareness", "risk perception" and "contact matrix" are bindings from the epidemiology frame onto terms the framework already holds (belief, actor, network). They are not new primitives and they do not move the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom", and this adds none). What would move the wall is a genuinely new axis of representation, for example an instrument that distinguishes two different sources of the same observed case curve.

## 4. Why the source question is the binding question here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is the live failure mode in epidemic modelling. A single observed case curve is compatible with many source structures: a fixed-transmission epidemic with external noise, a behavior-damped epidemic, a behavior-amplified epidemic with fading awareness, or a reporting artefact. Naming the mechanism from the curve alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, for example a contact survey paired with a behavioral time series, not a better story.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the warning about *simulacra*: where "in that case of simulacra, perhaps one composition of green was all you ever knew, while the others remained unprovided, which went unnoticed until you could discern or recreate the other kinds." A modelling group that has only ever fitted one behavioral specification will not notice that another specification fits the same curve.

## 5. Consequence for this question

**What licenses "the loop is negative here"?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a cycle and a sign rule; it does not return which sign the outbreak actually has. A sign is licensed only by an anchor that can discriminate sources: a measured contact reduction coincident with a case rise, a serological wave that rules out a reporting artefact, an intervention that reverses the sign and changes the outcome. Until one of those, the sign is the modeller's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the packet commit the forbidden error?** Not if it keeps the four refuters in front of the interpretation. The moment it asserts "this wave was caused by fading protective behavior" from a case curve alone, it names a source from a projection and commits the metamerism error. Section 5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records the re-census that keeps the binding honest.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous epidemiologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming anything at all in this answer. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Contact, network, belief and risk are all held or are bindings onto held terms, so naming them is permitted. What remains forbidden is naming the **sign, delay or magnitude** for a real outbreak from a projection, because that would name the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates sources. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
