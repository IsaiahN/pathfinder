# The Tether's answer on cooperation without direct reward

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism-shape and on each domain verdict, `general` on the structural invariant. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An actor cooperates where a direct-reward-and-punishment frame predicts defection. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The slot is the single no-incentive act. R is the excess cooperation over the direct-frame prediction.

The failing object is the **frame**, not the actor. The frame models one isolated actor with one immediate payoff. Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Stripped of the habitat, the actor cannot be predicted to cooperate, but that is a property of the model, not of the person.

## 2. The shape, in five separate verdicts

1. **The "no reward" condition is a scoping error.** Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The direct frame measured only first-order material contact. The return arrives in currencies the frame cannot carry.

2. **Cooperation is a relation, not a person-property.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same act is beneficial to one residual and harmful to another (Fig 11 :50). The right question is what relation makes this act cooperative.

3. **The return is a contact cascade beyond first order.** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades." Reputation, future reciprocation and group survival are second and third order effects of a first-order act. Scoping to direct contact is how the residual that matters gets missed.

4. **The bond can close without immediacy.** Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input." Trust and reputation survive the act; a repeated encounter returns the output as input. A one-shot chain "cannot close by construction", so it cannot represent this return.

5. **Selection between rooms and transmitted methods carry the rest.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 11 :36 "Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Groups with more cooperators can be selected; norms travel as methods, not recordings.

These are five domains of one union compound (Fig 9 :54-58). They are **never averaged** into one mechanism.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- It does not name a mechanism. The canonical rule returns a shape, not a name. Kin selection, direct reciprocity, indirect reciprocity, network reciprocity, group selection, strong reciprocity and internalized norms are provenanced imports from Nowak, Axelrod, Fehr, Ostrom and Tomasello, not figure derivations.
- It does not claim a hidden immediate material payoff. Fehr, de Waal and Tomasello show cooperation and helping with no such payoff, and material rewards can undermine helping.
- It does not reduce the proximate motive to "selection". Empathy and internalized norms are real proximate mechanisms in their own domain; Fig 11 :36 governs the unmaintained-habitat reading, not the motive.
- It is not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation discriminates among the mechanisms.
- It does not say agreement among cooperators is evidence. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 4. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Observing cooperation does not tell you which mechanism produced it. What would close the residual:

1. An instrument that discriminates the currency of the return for a specific no-incentive act (repeated encounter, reputation, group differential, internalized norm, or none).
2. The isolation test: a cooperative act with the actor cut off from all habitat contact still cooperating systematically. That breaks the shape claim.
3. The hidden-payoff test: a complete immediate material payoff found in every case, so R returns to zero.
4. The cascade test: the selective consequence shown strictly first order. That breaks the cascade claim.
5. The loop test: a repeated setting where the output never re-enters and cooperation is unchanged.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §6 (six in total) are the pre-registered set. Until one fires, the verdict is `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set can say the act must contact something beyond the immediate payoff and that the contact is a relation in a habitat. It cannot say which mechanism fills the slot. Another sensorium's vocabulary might cut the mechanism space differently. The derivation stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a single new reason why people cooperate. It says the question has been framed too narrowly. A direct-reward frame models one isolated actor with one immediate payoff, and the figures say capability is a property of the agent and its habitat together, never of the agent alone. So "no direct reward" does not mean "no return"; it means the frame cannot carry the currency in which the return arrives. That return can be a repeated encounter whose output re-enters as input, a reputation that survives its own use, a group that out-competes other groups, or a norm transmitted as a method rather than a recording. These are five different shapes of contact, and the derivation keeps them separate rather than averaging them. It does not name which one fills a given case: the figures return a shape, not a name, and several mechanisms produce the same cooperative act. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would discriminate among the mechanisms. The slot stays open until a ground settles it.
