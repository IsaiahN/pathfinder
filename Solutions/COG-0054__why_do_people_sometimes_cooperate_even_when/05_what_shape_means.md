## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0054 this means the derivation can say: the cooperative act must contact something beyond the immediate payoff, in a currency the direct frame cannot carry. It cannot say *which* mechanism fills that slot.

**SHAPE for this question:** a no-direct-reward cooperative act is an act embedded in a habitat, where the return arrives through a relation (kin, repeated encounter, reputation, group, norm) rather than through the immediate payoff. The slot marked `???` is the currency of the return, not the name of a mechanism.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Use the vocabulary closest to the thing, find recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Here the effect description is the residual itself: an actor cooperates where the direct frame predicts defection. The search is for what that act contacts. That is what makes the shape checkable without first accepting the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45-52).

So the names "kin selection", "indirect reciprocity", "group selection", "strong reciprocity" and "internalized norm" are imports from Nowak, Axelrod, Fehr and Tomasello. They are real and evidence-backed, but they are not figure-derived. The figures select a shape; the world supplies names.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator rule. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; several paths can create the same reading, and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Here, observing cooperation cannot tell you which mechanism produced it, because several mechanisms produce the same cooperative act. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name read off the behaviour. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

## 4. Why the record's shape claim is not a mechanism claim

The record in `01` deliberately marks the return-currency slot as `???` and keeps five domains separate. That is the canonical shape form. If it named one mechanism as *the* answer, it would commit the metamerism error of section 3: reading the source off a projection that several sources can produce.

A related warning from *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: two people who only check against each other drift together, and "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices". This is why the outreach material never asks for agreement and never pools it. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not ground

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this question, the prestigious-name route would be to collect endorsements from famous cooperation researchers. That buys a hearing, not a verdict. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let a ground (an experiment, a dataset, a discriminating instrument) settle it. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". The essay also states "Metatheories cannot validate themselves."

So `01` through `09` are a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the cooperation question. They cannot settle it. The world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are the empirical material, and even they corroborate the existence of mechanisms, not the figure mapping.

## 7. Consequences for this question

1. Say "a return in another currency and at another scale", not "the return is reputation" or "the return is genes".
2. Keep the five domains separate; they are five different shapes of contact, not five spellings of one mechanism.
3. Name mechanisms only with provenance, and state the overlap of the sources that supply them.
4. State the refuter before the conclusion. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.
