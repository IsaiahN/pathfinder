## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **Shared evidence pools disqualify a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (full line begins "disqualifying for a verdict:").
3. **Do not hand a verifier the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
