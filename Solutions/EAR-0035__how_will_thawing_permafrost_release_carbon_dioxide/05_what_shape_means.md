## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 text: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0035 a **shape** is: a per-place index, an enumerated habitat, a union-compounded pair of pathways, a production step, and a ground reading per slot. That constrains what a complete answer must contain. It does not supply the flux. "One global number plus a regional breakdown" is not the shape; it is a number with a decoration.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest held terms are per-slot residual (Fig 1), habitat (Fig 11), union (Fig 9) and production (Fig 12).
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as "the gap between forecast and measured flux, per place", not as "a carbon-climate feedback parameter".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what they would expect at a site before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the ground reading, not more framework text.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means chamber and tower fluxes, boreholes, active-layer probes and isotopic source signatures.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 provenance and shadow test), with a source gate and a shadow test. The IPCC range and the NSIDC redox mechanism are imports of this kind. They corroborate; they do not become figure results.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Mapping a thermal gradient onto the six-term atom "gradient" is a visible choice, not a discovery about soil physics.

## 4. Why the published number is not the Tether's answer

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green but *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK could give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A permafrost flux is the same kind of reading. A net CO2 flux at a site can come from several sources: aerobic decomposition of old carbon, root respiration, fire legacy, lateral transport. The reading does not name the source. Isotopic and flux-discriminating instruments are the analogue of the spectrometer. Until one is applied at a place, naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error.

So the 3 to 41 PgC range is not refused because it is wrong. It is withheld as a figure result because the figure set has no instrument for magnitude, and because the source of the reading is underdetermined without place-specific discriminators. F7 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records this as `gap`.

## 5. What this question can legitimately say

The framework returns:

- a per-place index, never a global scalar (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- an enumerated habitat with relative valence (Fig 11 :43, :44, :49);
- a union compound that must be unbundled (Fig 9 :55-57);
- a dependency order, thaw before substrate before gas before verification (Fig 3 :20);
- a production bond for the gas (Fig 12 :77);
- a stated `gap` for magnitude and timing (Fig 9 :32, Fig 8 :39).

It does not return which place dominates, how large the flux is, or when the threshold is crossed. Those are answers, and the ground supplies them.

## 6. Prestige and committee agreement are not the ground

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of well-known institutions that endorse the shape would fund a hearing, not settle the per-place residual. The legitimate ask is a ground reading at a site (Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it"*).

## 7. Correction to my own earlier framing

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any concrete quantity in this answer. That was overstated. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive or an unreachable answer, not the use of an already-held atom as a binding, and not the honest quotation of a world measurement. What remains forbidden is presenting the world range as if the figure set produced it, or as if a global number answered the "by place" half. Both claims are now recorded separately: F8 records the binding, F7 records the `gap`, and section 4 above explains why the range is corroboration only.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. It cannot settle the permafrost carbon question. It is labelled `specified` for that reason.
