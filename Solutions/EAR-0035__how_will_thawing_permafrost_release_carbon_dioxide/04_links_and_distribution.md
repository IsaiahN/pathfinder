## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-place claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see at a place if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-place shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a site they know; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. Send the residual, the shape and the refuter. No framework, no links to framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the three local framework links and the two public science links. Their stage-1 verdict is already on record, so later agreement cannot be pooled as evidence (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction"; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator"*).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are written down and unanswered. That is the honest state, not a passing result.
