## 0. The claim under audit and the residual

The question, in effect terms: given warming, what will the frozen carbon pool return as gas, and how will that return differ from one place to another?

**R (arity / symmetry / scale).**

- **Arity:** at least four indexed slots: place, gas (CO2 / CH4), pathway (oxic / anoxic, gradual / abrupt), time (season to century). Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Symmetry:** CO2 and CH4 are not symmetric operands. Oxic microbial respiration makes CO2; anoxic conditions make CH4 (NSIDC, *Why Frozen Ground Matters*, opened 2026-11-22). Methane is *"a much more potent greenhouse gas than carbon dioxide, although it does not remain in Earth's atmosphere as long"* (same source). A symmetric two-item list misstates the physics.
- **Scale:** microbe, soil profile, landscape (thermokarst), region, globe. The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30), so a global number cannot be pushed back down to a place without a measured round trip.

**The residual itself:** the gap between a forecast of release and the flux the ground actually returns. The live part of that gap is per place. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

Every quoted figure string below was checked against `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` this session. Triangulation means two figures with low overlap, never two pages sharing a source.

### F1 - The "vary by place" half is per-slot and must not be averaged (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**

These two figures have low overlap. Fig 1 is about measurement indexing, Fig 11 is about contact topology. Together they say that "vary by place" is not a modifier on a global answer; it is the answer's index. A single global PgC number is a legal frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :13 allows a global R near zero with one live slot), but it is the wrong quantity for this question.

**Refuter:** a forecast in which one global flux closes the residual and every per-place residual is near zero. If live per-place mass does not exist, this finding fails.

### F2 - The habitat must be enumerated outward, and valence is relative to the residual (single-source)

Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :52: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**

For this residual the enumeration starts with soil temperature, ice content, water saturation and carbon stock, then reaches topography, vegetation, snow cover and drainage, then fire and coastal erosion. No condition carries a fixed sign: the same water saturation that suppresses CO2 by going anoxic can raise CH4. This is why a standing list of "high emitters" is not a substitute for enumeration.

**Refuter:** measured per-place fluxes statistically independent of the enumerated contact conditions, or a place where a condition flips valence with no change in residual. The world source NSIDC *Why Frozen Ground Matters* corroborates the oxygen/no-oxygen switch (secondary, not a figure).

### F3 - The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance (single-source)

Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**

A better emission factor does not rescue a missing per-place measurement. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The middle link here is the place-specific vocabulary of conditions (what a site affords to thaw and to gas production). It has instruments (boreholes, chambers, active-layer probes) but they are not uniformly distributed.

**Refuter:** a case where gas is released without a thaw step and without newly available substrate, for example a pre-existing free-gas reservoir dominating the flux. That would be a different residual and must be split off (Fig 9 :54).

### F4 - Gas release is a production bond, not a sequence (single-source for bond type)

Fig 12 :77 (Operators table :37): **"A produces B; B did not exist before"**

The CO2 and CH4 in question are new atmospheric molecules made from old soil carbon by microbial action. That is production (⇒), not sequence (→): before the process fires, the B is absent, not waiting. NSIDC *Why Frozen Ground Matters* (opened 2026-11-22) states: *"If there is oxygen available, the microbes make carbon dioxide. But if there is no oxygen available, they make methane."* The bond type comes from the figure; the redox split comes from the ground.

**Refuter:** a site where the released gas is demonstrably already free gas before thaw, so no production event fires. Then ⇒ is the wrong bond and the finding is withdrawn.

### F5 - CO2 and CH4 are a union compound, not a symmetric two-item list (single-source)

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

Oxic and anoxic microsites coexist in one soil; gradual and abrupt thaw coexist in one landscape. A single lever (for example, a single temperature-driven emission factor) clears the oxic-only region and leaves the anoxic intersection standing. The compound has its own loop and its own ground and must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54).

**Refuter:** evidence that one redox condition governs both gases at a site with no overlap, or that gradual and abrupt thaw never co-occur. The union claim then collapses to a partition.

### F6 - The anchor is the ground, not agreement among models (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** Fig 9 :42: **"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"**

Fig 2 is about anchoring and independence; Fig 9 is about what happens after arrival. Low overlap, same verdict. IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch5 (opened 2026-11-22) states: *"Thawing terrestrial permafrost will lead to carbon release (high confidence), but there is low confidence in the timing, magnitude and the relative roles of CO2 versus CH4 as feedback processes."* An ensemble of models sharing calibration data and process assumptions is one evidence pool, not independent witnesses. Its agreement is a budget filter, never a verdict witness (Fig 9 :60).

**Refuter:** a model ensemble whose members update on disjoint measurements and whose agreement still fails to track withheld flux observations. Until such a test is run, ensemble agreement stays frame-internal.

### F7 - Magnitude and timing are a `gap` at this budget (verdict, not silence)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

The figures license the shape and the dependency order. They do not contain a carbon-stock number, a thaw-rate constant, a methane yield or a spatial pattern. IPCC AR6 gives a range, *"CO2 release from permafrost is projected to be 3–41 PgC per 1°C of global warming by 2100, based on an ensemble of models"*, and immediately notes that *"incomplete representation of important processes such as abrupt thaw, combined with weak observational constraints, only allow low confidence"* in that estimate. The honest report is `gap` for quantities: unreachable from the figures at this budget, reachable only through ground readings. This is a verdict (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*), not an omission.

**Refuter:** this finding cannot be refuted from inside the figure set. It would be closed by a ground reading that returns the per-place fluxes, or by a figure line that actually governs a flux magnitude. No such line was found after the census above.

### F8 - Heat entering the ground is a flow composed from energy and a gradient (single-source figure, with a recorded binding)

Fig 13 :76: **"and a gradient compose a flow"** (the preceding line ends with the state "Energy"). Fig 13 :77: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

A thermal gradient over space drives heat into the ground; the same gradient over time is a different term. This is why active-layer deepening is not interchangeable with a warming air-temperature trend. Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"** defines the gradient as directedness, which a temperature difference has.

**Binding recorded, not a derivation:** naming the physical thermal gradient as the figure's held atom "gradient" is a binding from the physics frame onto an atom already in the six. Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** This binding adds no atom and moves no wall; it needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 import conditions) before it is used as anything but an analogy.

**Refuter:** a thaw process in which no spatial gradient can be identified, or one where the gradient over space is indistinguishable from the one over time. Then the flow composition fails.

### F9 - Agreement among overlapping frames compounds, evidence does not (single-source)

Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"** Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

This governs how the output should be offered, not what it says. A review panel or a list of endorsing institutions cannot close the per-place residual. The next "room" must differ from the last and match the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36). The practical consequence is the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: stage-1 verdict recorded before any theory is shown.

**Refuter:** a case where two frames sharing an evidence pool produce a genuinely novel, independently checked per-place prediction. That would be convergence at maximum independence, which Fig 8 :50 allows, but it would need the provenance record to distinguish it from an adopted import.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach. Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* These were written before the conclusion below.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Per-slot refuter.** A single global flux closes the residual and all per-place residuals are near zero (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. **Union refuter.** At a given place, CO2 and CH4 release are governed by one condition with one redox state, or gradual and abrupt thaw never co-occur (Fig 9 :55-57).
3. **Habitat refuter.** Measured per-place flux is independent of the enumerated contact conditions, or a condition's valence does not change with the residual (Fig 11 :44, :49).
4. **Production refuter.** The gas released is already free before thaw, so no production event fires (Fig 12 :77).
5. **Bond/order refuter.** A gas-release pathway is shown to run without the dependency order thaw -> substrate -> production -> verification (Fig 3 :20).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and `07` and `08` are revised. Until one is run, no part of this is `grounded`.
