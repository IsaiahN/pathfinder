# The Tether's answer on permafrost carbon release

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8). It cannot settle the question it reports on, and it does not produce a flux number.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is the gap between a forecast of CO2 and CH4 release from thawing ground and the flux the ground actually returns, indexed by place, gas, pathway and time. The live part of that gap is per place. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**D1, ground thermal state and carbon stock.** A place has a thermal state and a finite frozen carbon stock. Heat and a spatial gradient compose a flow into the ground: Fig 13 :77: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."** This is a `general` binding of the physics frame onto held atoms, not a derivation of a thaw rate (F8). Verdict: shape licensed, magnitude `gap`. Single-source figure.

**D2, thaw pathway.** Gradual active-layer deepening and abrupt thermokarst are overlapping conditions in one landscape. Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** Verdict: union compound, unbundle before searching. Single-source figure, world-corroborated.

**D3, microbial gas production.** Gas is produced, not merely released: Fig 12 :77: **"A produces B; B did not exist before"**. Oxic conditions make CO2, anoxic conditions make CH4. The production motif is a functional group across places, and the feedback closes as a positive-sign cycle. Verdict: bond type licensed by the figure; the redox split is ground-decided. Single-source figure.

**D4, spatial variation.** The habitat must be enumerated outward, with valence relative to the residual: Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Verdict: triangulated across Fig 1 (per-slot indexing) and Fig 11 (contact topology), two figures with low overlap. This is the strongest verdict in the package because it directly answers the "by place" half.

**D5, forecast verification.** The anchor is the measurement, never the agreement of models that share an evidence pool. Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** Verdict: triangulated across Fig 2 (anchoring) and Fig 9 (ground decides after narrowing). Low overlap, same result.

**Order by dependency.** Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."** Place-level perception and vocabulary come before a place-level objective; a global emission factor placed above a broken per-place measurement cannot rescue it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a global PgC number. The published range (IPCC AR6: 3 to 41 PgC per degree of warming by 2100, low confidence in timing and in the CO2 versus CH4 split) is world corroboration with a source gate, not a figure result.
- Not a claim that any particular place dominates, or that any particular condition has a fixed sign. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not a claim that a model ensemble is an anchor (F6), or that agreement among reviewers settles a place (Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).
- Not a derivation of a thaw rate, a methane yield, or a threshold date. Those are `gap` at this budget (F7).
- Not `grounded`. No chamber, borehole, tower or isotopic reading was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at a place, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a spectrometer reads green but *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Here the questions are:

1. **Per-place flux.** Does a chamber or tower return a flux at each place that carries positive residual against the forecast, or does one global number close it? (F1 refuter)
2. **Redox discrimination.** Do oxic and anoxic production overlap at a site, and can isotopic signatures separate old-carbon methane from other sources? (F5 refuter)
3. **Pathway discrimination.** Do gradual and abrupt thaw co-occur in one landscape, and do they share the same atom set? (Q2 and Q4 in `06_isomers_and_order.md`)
4. **Habitat test.** Is the per-place flux independent of the enumerated contact conditions, or does a condition's valence flip with the residual? (F2 refuter)
5. **Cycle sign.** Does released carbon actually re-enter as ground warming at the site, closing the loop with a positive sign? (Q4 in `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are all five, written before this answer.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 text: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The figure set narrows the shape of a complete answer and stops. The numbers are reachable through instruments, not through more composition. *They're Scanning Books* adds the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The binding of a thermal gradient onto the held atom "gradient" is a visible choice, recorded in F8, not a discovery about soil physics.

Magnitude and timing remain `gap`. The derivation records the route, the refuters and the instruments that would close it. That is what a checkable stop looks like.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not forecast how much carbon thawing permafrost will release, and it does not claim the final number. It says that the residual is indexed by place, so a single global flux is the wrong answer to the "by place" half of the question; that the habitat of each place must be enumerated, with no condition assigned a fixed sign in advance; that gradual and abrupt thaw, and oxic and anoxic gas production, are overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before they are searched; and that the gas is produced by microbes, not merely released, so the feedback closes as a positive-sign cycle. The anchor is the per-place measurement, not agreement among models that share an evidence pool. What the framework offers is the shape any complete answer must have, a dependency order, five falsifiers written in advance, and the ground readings that would close the remaining `gap` in magnitude and timing.
