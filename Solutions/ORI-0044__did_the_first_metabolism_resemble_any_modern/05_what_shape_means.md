## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be stated before the name

The canonical README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the shape is: **a genuine answer must say what kind of relation holds between the origin network and a modern pathway, and must not merely answer yes or no.** Functional resemblance, compositional identity and descent are three different relations. That is a constraint on any acceptable answer, stated before we know which way the empirical evidence will go.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, narrowing, and splitting bundles

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to what you seek and look for *"new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms."* With each new term *"you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on a failed search: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level source of F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms"* (Fig 6 :30, quoted in that essay). So the residual is described as effects (does the same job, shares the same bonds, has a recoverable lineage), not as a preferred label.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here the ground is geochemical experiment and comparative genomics, not the figures.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming the acetyl-CoA pathway as "the first metabolism" would be an import from the geochemistry frame with a source gate and a shadow test still owed.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why the artefact is not enough: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Applied here: a modern pathway's pattern is a projection, and several ancestral chemistries could give the same pattern. The essay states the general rule: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 4. Why the original question is a source-naming problem

The question asks whether the first metabolism "resembled a modern pathway." That treats the modern pathway as a reading of an ancient source. Under the metamerism argument, the reading does not determine the source:

- the same overall chemistry (CO2 reduction with H2, thioester energy currency) can be catalysed by bare metals or by a hundred-plus enzymes, which is the same reading from different sources;
- the same ingredients under different bonds are a different substance (Fig 13 :124), so "resembled" at the ingredient level is not identity at the molecule level;
- the lineage is required because *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact"* (Fig 13 :129).

So the honest move is to refuse the binary as stated and ask for the instrument that would discriminate sources. That is the shape.

## 5. What the shape is NOT

- Not a date for the first metabolism.
- Not a name for the first pathway. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not "acetyl-CoA was first." That is a hypothesis from the geochemistry frame, `specified` and ungrounded here, with a source gate and shadow test owed.
- Not "the first metabolism was completely different." Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not a verdict by agreement. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A packet that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let an experiment, a reconstruction, or an instrument settle it.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first read *"a shape, not a name"* as forbidding the mention of any specific chemistry, including metal-sulfide catalysis and thioesters. That overstates it. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame. Metal centres, redox gradients and thioester energy carriers are already ordinary terms in the geochemistry frame; naming them is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the figures derive that binding, or that it moves the wall (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this binding adds none). Both the overreach and the narrowed form are recorded so neither is silently lost.

## 8. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The author's demo note adds that such outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any origins-of-life claim and cannot settle the question they report on.
