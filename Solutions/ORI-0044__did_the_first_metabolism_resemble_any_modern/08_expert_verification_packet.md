# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the first metabolism question

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origin-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the split and per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the historical fact. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "did the first metabolism resemble a modern pathway, or was it fundamentally different?" is a union of three different questions and must be split before it is searched: functional resemblance, compositional identity, and lineage continuity. The figures constrain the shape of any defensible answer. A seed does not contain the tree, so continuity, if any, is a reusable method or a functional group, not a miniature modern pathway. The same function can be reached by different routes, and the same ingredients under different bonds are a different substance, so a yes at the function level and a no at the chemistry level can both be true. Descent cannot be read off the modern artefact; it needs a lineage. Neither offered option is settled by the figures, and absence of a known analogue is not proof of fundamental difference. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument or a lineage record.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9, 1 and 4

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound and must be split; a seed does not contain the scaled-down tree.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 4 :34 *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*
- **Refuter:** show that functional resemblance and compositional identity are one partition rather than two conditions, or show that a seed does contain the scaled-down structure of what grows from it.

### C2 - chemistry-facing; checkable by an origin-of-life researcher

- **Statement:** same function does not imply same substance; what may be shared is a functional group (metal-centred redox carbon fixation), with different catalysts.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :124 *"the same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance."* Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 *"A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **What to check:** Martin and Russell (2006) say carbon *"might have entered early metabolism via reactions hardly different from those in the modern Wood-Ljungdahl pathway"*; Mrnjavac et al. (2024) report metals that *"replace the function of over 120 enzymes"* for the H2 plus CO2 to pyruvate conversion. Are those claims about the same overall function under different catalysts, rather than about identical molecules? If they are claims about identical molecules, the functional-group reading fails.
- **Refuter:** show an identical full atom set and bond arrangement, with a continuous series, between the ancestral network and a modern pathway. That upgrades the functional-group claim to strict identity or isomerism and breaks this sub-claim.

### C3 - framework-internal and historical; checkable against the record

- **Statement:** descent cannot be read off the modern artefact; a lineage is required, and the historical fact is a gap from the figures.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :129 *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that the ancestral route is recoverable from the modern artefact alone with no lineage record, or supply a geochemical or phylogenetic measurement that settles the fact. The second would move the fact from `gap` to `grounded` and is the intended way to retire this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the acetyl-CoA or Wood-Ljungdahl pathway was the first metabolism. That is an ungrounded import from the geochemistry frame.
- Not that the first metabolism was fundamentally different in a strong sense. Absence of an analogue is not proof.
- Not a date, a name, or a reconstruction of the ancestral network. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. No early-Earth measurement is claimed.
- Not that the vent literature is independent corroboration. Most of it shares one evidence pool.
- Not that parsimony is an anchor. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

## 5. What would settle it

1. **R1:** a controlled geochemical experiment showing the metal-centred carbon-fixation functional group cannot arise under plausible early-Earth conditions. Breaks the shared-function claim.
2. **R2:** a lineage reconstruction showing identical full atom set and bond arrangement with a continuous recoverable series. Breaks the compositional-difference claim.
3. **R3:** a demonstration that the ancestral route is recoverable from the modern artefact alone. Breaks the lineage-required claim.
4. **R4:** a figure re-census collapsing the three conditions into one partition. Collapses the split itself.
