## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework can say *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (figures README). Fig 9 :32: *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134/:135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* / *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `the 120 orders is an aggregate over mode slots` is a shape.
- `a new particle X cancels the vacuum energy` is a name.
- `the mechanism is an area-counted closure` is a shape (a kind of counting rule).
- `the mechanism is the strings of a specific theory` is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

From *The Library of Babel*: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Fig 9 :26 to :28 governs the ordering: *"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*" / *"describe it before you go looking, or"* / *"any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual was described by arity, symmetry and scale in `01` before any frame was chosen for the import. The vocabulary bootstrapped was: slot, aggregate, anchor, harness, union, reach, bond, import. Each is a figure term, not a physics name.

The stop condition from *The Library of Babel*: *"The anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."* Here the question is not answered by a mechanism, so the loop stops at a shape and a budget verdict rather than at a name.

## 3. The bundle warning is the governing essay result for this question

*The Library of Babel* states the exact failure mode found in the census:

> "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

Fig 9 :4 to :5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset,"* / *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

This is why the answer returned in `07` is a split and not a value. The three unbundled conditions are: the ultraviolet mode sum, the coupling of the absolute level to gravity, and the observed magnitude. A shape that addresses only one is not an answer to the question as asked.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Fig 9 :42: *"→ narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The area-counting candidate in F10 is an import of the second kind, offered as a candidate. It explains the volume overcount (its shadow) and it carries provenance ('t Hooft, 1993). It does not name the mechanism that fixes the observed magnitude, and it is not promoted to a verdict.

## 5. The source-discriminating instrument is what is actually owed

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism of the honest stop. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources produce the same reading, so *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The case was resolved not by an argument but by *"the arrival of a better instrument"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Applied here: the field-theory calculation and the cosmological observation both return an energy density with the same Lorentz-invariant form. The reading cannot comment on the source. A counterterm named from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources, not a name for one. Fig 10 :27/:28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is"* / *"you have lost touch."* The 120 orders is a saturated reading of that kind.

## 6. Simulation disclaimer and the prestige rule

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So this whole package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, cannot settle the cosmological constant problem, and must be labelled as such everywhere.

The same essay gives the outreach rule: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that collects famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a check against the ground.

## 7. The shape returned for this question

Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, as formula step "Direction" requires, it has five parts:

1. **Slot indexing.** The residual is indexed per field mode, never reported only as an aggregate (Fig 1 :19/:22).
2. **A non-updating anchor.** The comparison is against a measured expansion history that does not move when a theorist changes the cutoff (Fig 2 :29/:30/:33).
3. **A bargain that fits more than one occasion.** The candidate explains the per-mode distribution and the observed magnitude together, not the observed value alone (Fig 5 :43/:44).
4. **Union split before search.** The three conditions are stated separately and a proposed lever is marked against each (Fig 9 :54/:58).
5. **Provenance for anything imported.** A frame from outside the closure arrives with a source gate and a shadow test, and is reported as a candidate (Fig 6 :23/:24, Fig 8 :56/:57).

This shape is checkable without knowing the answer. It is also refusable: `01` §5 wrote six refuters before the conclusion. The framework returns the shape and the budget verdict, then stops.

## 8. Honest stop

From *The Library of Babel*, the paradox at the end: *"By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* That is not a claim that this derivation found the answer. It is a warning that narrowing vocabulary can feel like progress. The audit keeps the two quantities apart: the library of terms grew; the reach did not move (formula step 8: *"IT IS THE REACH THAT DRIVES THE LOOP, NOT THE LIBRARY."*). The residual is reported as `gap` for the mechanism, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The two equal-looking densities are not one term.
