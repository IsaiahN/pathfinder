## 2. Independent re-census, written before comparison

**Arity.** The residual compares two quantities, so the comparison bond ⋛ is the outer form. The predicted quantity is itself a sum over a very large set of terms, so a unary aggregation sits inside one operand. Two operators at different depths, not one.

**Symmetry.** Both quantities are Lorentz-invariant energy densities. The two frames that produce them are not interchangeable: one has an internal scale it can vary (the cutoff), the other has a measurement that does not depend on that variation. The apparent symmetry of the written equation hides an asymmetry of authority.

**Scale.** The discrepancy is between an ultraviolet scale and an infrared scale. Any figure line about a small or large value must be checked for which scale it governs before use.

**Domains, one line each:**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Field modes and their energies | oscillators in momentum space, measured transition energies, Casimir plates | mode energy, measured differences | mode differences are anchored by experiment | domain, partial authority |
| B. Regulator and counterterm | cutoff, box, subtraction scheme, bare term | where the sum stops; tuning precision | none, the cutoff is a convention | **not a domain**, substituted habitat |

| D. Boundary counting | horizon area, boundary bits, black-hole entropy | area rather than volume | black-hole entropy is a theoretical deduction, not a measurement of Λ | **not a domain yet** under strict test |

**Partition or union.** Union. The overlap is energy: operand A uses energy as a state, operand C uses it as a density bonded to space. Three conditions must be separated: the size of the mode sum, the coupling of the absolute level, the observed magnitude.

**Dependency order.** Measurement of the expansion is reached. Terms for cutoff and counterterm are reached. A criterion for what would count as progress on the absolute level is **not** reached. Mechanism proposals sit above that break.

**Independent verdict from this seat.** The residual is a union compound whose live signal is per mode; the regulator stage is a harness with no anchor; the observation is the only full anchor available; the mechanism is unreachable at this budget; nothing in the held closure names a primitive that would close it. A boundary-counting frame is the only candidate import, but under the strict anchor test it is **not yet a domain** and therefore cannot be used as a witness, only as a prospective import to be gated.
