## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The discrepancy is real and is a statement about frames.** Weinberg gives both the magnitude and the cure's cost: a cancellation *"accurate to 120 decimal places."* Carroll shows why the number scales as the cutoff to the fourth power. Padmanabhan gives the same bound in Planck units. None of them derives the observed value. They are witnesses to the residual, not to a mechanism.
2. **The sum is over slots.** Carroll describes a field as *"a collection of an infinite number of harmonic oscillators in momentum space."* This is the physical counterpart of Fig 1 :19 and :22. The aggregate hides which mode carries the mass.
3. **The two written forms are not the same claim.** Padmanabhan: *"The nature of this problem and its theoretical backdrop acquires different shades of meaning depending which of the two forms of equations in (3) is used."* This is the isomer point in `06_isomers_and_order.md`: same ingredients, different bond, different substance.
4. **Area counting is a live alternative frame.** 't Hooft's two-dimensional lattice is an import candidate with a provenance gate. It is not evidence that Λ is holographically determined; it is a witness that the volume counting is not the only closure available. Fig 8 :56/:57 lists Bekenstein and holography as a known triangulation case with the limit that the claim has not been tested forward.
5. **The objective is contested, not the data.** Koberinski, Falck and Smeenk classify six explanations of Λ and conclude that naturalness and strong anthropic appeals are not similarly justifiable. Friederich records that the discrepancy ranges from 10^50 to 10^123 depending on assumptions. That spread is itself evidence that the number is a frame-dependent report.
6. **The problem has a history of reinstatement for convenience.** O'Raifeartaigh et al. show that the term was never truly banished and that its return was gradual. This corroborates the Fig 3 finding that the break is at the objective link rather than at a single decisive measurement.

No source above overrules a figure line. Where a source and a figure strain, the census is re-run; the verdict stays with the figures. Agreement among these sources on the magnitude is one evidence pool, not two witnesses, because several of them cite the same review chain (Fig 2 :40).

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict before any source is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three framework links. State that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method. The user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a shape and a budget verdict, not a number; say that plainly. Do not let a recipient think they are being asked to endorse a solution to the cosmological constant problem.
