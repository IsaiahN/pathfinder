## DERIVATION

### Q1 : Are the two written forms isomers?

- Fig 12 :95 : "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is"
- Fig 12 :96 : "carried entirely by the bond."
- Fig 12 :117 : "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Both forms contain the same ingredient: a constant term with the dimensions of energy density and a Lorentz-invariant stress tensor. They differ in the bond. In Padmanabhan's first form the term sits in the matter Lagrangian, so the bond is from the matter side to the geometry through gravitation. In the second form the term sits in the gravitational Lagrangian, so it is a constant of the field. Carroll: *"The effect of an energy-momentum tensor of the form (12) is equivalent to that of a cosmological constant, as can be seen by moving the Λgμν term in (7) to the right-hand side."*

The equivalence is a **change of bond, not of ingredients**. Under Fig 12's definition they are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the written value and decisive in the behaviour (one form suggests a subtractive cancellation; the other suggests a constant of nature to be measured). The chemistry precedent is Fig 12 :97/:98: *"This is not an analogy. Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced"* / *"structure into the notation. Liebig and Wöhler, 1824 to 1826; named isomerism by Berzelius, 1830."*

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if the two forms are connected by an identity that preserves all physical content and produces the same predictions under every operation (so the ≡ bond, *"two names, one referent"*, Operators table :24 to :27, is the correct bond), the isomer claim fails and they are one term. A second falsifier: exhibit a measurable quantity that differs between the two forms, in which case they are not the same ingredient set.

### Q2 : Can space and energy bond directly?

- Fig 13 :74 : "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
- Fig 13 :75 : "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."
- Fig 13 :76 : "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."

A "vacuum energy density of space" reads as a direct bond between two states, Energy and Space. Under Fig 13 :75 that is not composable. A structure must sit between them. Which structure is not given by the six-term set; the framework returns a shape, not a name. **Falsifier:** show that the framework must admit a direct state-state bond between Energy and Space, or a measurement in which energy density is a property of space with no structural intermediary. Either defeats this.

### Q3 : At what order does the comparison sit?

- Fig 5 :69 : "AMENDMENT · settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."
- Fig 5 :70 : "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."
- Fig 12 :103 : "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."
- Fig 12 :104 : "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"

The observed Λ is settled: the ground pays for it through the expansion history. It can serve as an atom at the next level. The regulated zero-point sum is not settled: its value changes with the regulator and no ground pays for a particular cutoff. It cannot yet be composed over.

So the comparison ⋛ between them is not a first-order composition of two settled terms. It is a **second-order composition whose left operand is not settled**. Fig 12 :106: *"Which is why a system that cannot settle has one level however large its library. Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."* This is the structural reason the 120-order residual persists: at the level where the comparison is attempted, one operand has not been admitted as a part.

**Falsifier:** show that the regulated sum is settled by an instrument that selects the cutoff without reference to the observed value. Then both operands are settled and the comparison is an ordinary second-order composition; the level argument falls away.

### Q4 : Contact cascade and the third-order effect

### Q5 : Two currencies, not one score

- Fig 12 :125 : "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."

## CONCLUSION

The regulated vacuum term and the observed cosmological term are **isomers** under Fig 12: identical ingredient set, different bond, different consequence. They are not one term, and the written equality of their units is not a licence to identify them (Fig 12 :95/:96). A direct Energy-Space bond is not composable under Fig 13 :75, so the phrase "energy density of space" is a shape with a structure still missing. The comparison between the two sits at second order, with one operand unsettled (Fig 5 :69/:70), which is why the residual does not close. The contact cascade makes a single cutoff lever a third-order intervention at best.

This framing preserves what is defensible without naming a mechanism: the isomer claim is checkable by reading Fig 12, the level claim is checkable by reading Fig 5, and both carry falsifiers above. No new primitive is introduced and no import is smuggled in.
