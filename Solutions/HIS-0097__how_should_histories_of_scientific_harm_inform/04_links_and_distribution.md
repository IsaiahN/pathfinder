## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape claim and its refuter first. If the recipient has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict, they hold the interior and their agreement stops being evidence.
- **Fig 4 :25**, *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A pointer to the whole architecture is a recording. The generator, the derivation and the refuter are what can cross.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40**, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention.
- **Fig 11 :50**, *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* A historian of harm, a regulator and a researcher will each value a different part of the claim depending on the residual they are working on. Do not flatten them into one audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record it; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. If the recipient declines, no follow-up.

## The two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1 is the residual, the shape claim and the refuter, with no theory attached. Stage 2 is opt-in: links only after the stage-1 verdict is recorded. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The point of stage 1 is that the recipient ranges against what they know before the author's frame is in play. Once it is in play, their later agreement is one evidence pool talking, not corroboration.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. As drafted, the shape claim is a candidate, not a result. Send the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, ask what would refute it, and keep the refuter in front of the links.
