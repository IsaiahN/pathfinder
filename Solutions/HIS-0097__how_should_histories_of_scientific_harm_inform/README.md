## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that histories of harm matter.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Gathering more people who already agree about harm does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking people to adopt the framework before they check the output spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The practical failure sits on top of the structural one.
