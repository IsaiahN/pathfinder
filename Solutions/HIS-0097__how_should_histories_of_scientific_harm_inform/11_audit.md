## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an institution's prediction about its own research and what a historical record says happened. Arity: many slots, because the harm attaches to a protocol, a funder, an institution and an affected group rather than to "science" as one object. Symmetry: asymmetric, because a research act is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :50), so there is no symmetric trust bit to flip. Scale: first order at a claim, second order at a policy. The question bundles four conditions, so it is a **union** (Fig 9 :55).
- **D1, the record domain.** Actors: historians, archives, harmed communities, documents. Currency: evidence and provenance. Anchor: what occurred, a record or patient outcome. Question type: a scoped refusal with provenance, not a general verdict.
- **D2, the decision domain.** Actors: researchers, boards, funders, journals, regulators. Currency: protocols, predictions, funding. Anchor: the ground for the claim. Question type: per-slot ranging, with a different ground for acceptability than for correctness.
- **D3, the verification domain.** Actors: decision-makers, expert checkers, affected communities. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run the independence of this domain is zero, because all seats draw on one evidence pool.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the false term before minting the checking method (Formula step 2); settle each first-order slot before the second-order policy (Fig 12 :104).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the record must stay indexed by slot, and the harm history must name what it refuses. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
