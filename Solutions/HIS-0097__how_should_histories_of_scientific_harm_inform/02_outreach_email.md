# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive your own check. The output must stand on its own or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how harm histories should be used

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a record of scientific harm is a refusal of a particular claim, not a verdict on research as such. It should stay indexed to the slot it concerns: which protocol, which institution, which funding relation, which group bore the consequence. Three rules follow from that.
>
> 1. Do not average the signal into a global trust score. The framework's own rule is that the residual "is indexed by object slot, not aggregated," and that "aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A blanket verdict of "all research is untrustworthy" is that aggregation error.
> 2. Buy independent checking in proportion to the cost of being wrong, at the joints where the harm record shows independence was corrupted. The harm is evidence about where to look, not a licence to stop looking.
> 3. Unbundle the question. "Is the result correct," "was the process acceptable to those it affected," "were benefits and burdens distributed fairly," and "was the checking independent" are four conditions, not one. A single lever clears the easy region and leaves the intersections standing.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that research is inherently untrustworthy, that a harmed community's judgment is unreliable, that institutions can judge their own safety, or that the framework names a specific policy such as mandatory registration. The framework returns a shape, not a name. A record of harm is a refusal of a term, not a negation of the whole library. Where a stake cannot be removed, it should be declared rather than denied.
>
> **What would refute the claim:** (1) a figure rule that lets a per-slot residual be averaged into a global verdict without losing the signal; (2) evidence that a research act carries trustworthiness independent of the decision and of who bears the consequence; (3) a controlled case where adding independent checking at a corroded joint made the decision worse at equal cost; (4) a harm record that cannot be expressed as a refusal of one claim without refusing every claim alongside it.
>
> I wrote these down before writing the claim. If any one holds, I withdraw the part it touches. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Stage 2, only if you are curious and only after you have recorded your verdict on the above:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - Decoding Tacit Knowledge, on why an effect description travels where a recording does not: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/decoding-tacit-knowledge-why-context
>
> **Stage 3, if you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the architecture link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The architecture post warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for your time,
> [name]

**Record before theory:** if the recipient gives a verdict on stage 1, write it down before sending any stage 2 material. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once the framework's interior has been shown, the frame and the author share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
