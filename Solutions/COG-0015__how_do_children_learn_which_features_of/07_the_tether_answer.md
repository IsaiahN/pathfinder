# The Tether's answer: how children learn which features are worth attending to

**Mode: `specified` on the four per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal feature list. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6). No child was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A child has a limited attentional budget and an environment with many potentially attendable features. The question asks how a feature comes to be "worth attending to". Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The residual is per slot: Symbols table, "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." The answer is therefore not a list of features. It is the loop by which a slot acquires live mass and the conditions under which that mass settles into a habit. The question is a union of four overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."), so the four verdicts below are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. One loop per domain

**D1, the internal loop.** Actors: the child's library, slots, bonds. Currency: bits and actions, residual per slot. Anchor: the ground. *Verdict (`specified`, triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 5 and the predictive-coding world source):* attention is allocated by a boundary diff that sorts the prediction gap into held, new, mis-attached and mechanism-broken (Fig 5 :10). "where to look: broken vs novel" (Fig 1 :15). The curiosity drive "seeks the gap that is large and compressible" (Fig 5 :18). A global average hides the live slot (Fig 1 :22). Refuter: a global statistic that outperforms per-slot residual without importing the slot structure.

**D2, the habitat.** Actors: caregivers, objects, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground. *Verdict (`general` structural, triangulated with Gibson's affordance):* capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the child alone (Fig 11 :39). The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43), and "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual" (Fig 11 :51). Contact cascades, so the feature that matters can be three steps out. Refuter: an improvement to the child in isolation that changes contact-free capability at the ground.

**D3, the social import.** Actors: adults and peers as differing closures, gaze, pointing, speech. Currency: imported pointers with provenance. Anchor: the ground. *Verdict (`specified`, triangulated with the joint-attention source):* another person's attention is an import from a closure that differs, and it crosses only with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :45). Gaze is a cue to where rewarding events occur, not a command; the child ranges it against its own residual (Fig 2 :45). Refuter: zero surplus from following a pointer even where the other closure demonstrably differs, or adoption of a pointer that decides an outcome and has become a verdict.

**D4, the measurement frame.** Actors: experimenters and instruments. Currency: looking time and other readings. Anchor: the ground, which must not update on the reading. *Verdict (`general`, triangulated with the habituation and predictive-coding instrument caveats):* the broken link is the middle one, terms for affordances plus what counts as progress, which has the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24); saturated metrics and pooled readings sever the channel. Refuter: an instrument that reads per-slot residual without a non-updating ground and still agrees with the ground rather than with the experimenter's report.

## 3. The shape in one list

1. Attention is per slot; the live slot is the one whose residual persists and carries positive mass.
2. Valence flips with the residual, so no standing feature list is licensed.
3. The child inherits a head start (Fig 4 :22) and imports pointers from differing closures with provenance.
4. "Worth" is the large and compressible gap (Fig 5 :18), not noise and not the fully familiar.
5. The ground settles which commitments pay; the instrument must keep that ground reachable.
6. Two regimes over the same feature set are isomers; the arrangement, not the ingredient list, carries the difference (Fig 12 :117).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a feature list, an age schedule or a ranking of stimuli. That is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that novelty alone, salience alone or reward alone is the variable. Each is one condition in a union.
- Not that the looking-time reading identifies the source of attention. It is green, and several sources can produce green (metamerism, `05` §4).
- Not that social pointing is instruction. It is a cue that must be ranged against the child's residual.
- Not a claim that infants are little Bayesians or that predictive coding is the literal mechanism. Those are world analogues and are secondary to the figures.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument was run and no child was observed.

## 5. What would settle it, and the honest stop

A discriminator, not another argument. The figures name the required shape of the ground: an anchor that does not update on the reader's report (Fig 2 :29), a per-slot reading rather than a pooled average, an instrument improved from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32), and a model that can refuse a term whose residual persists and does not trend down.

Concretely: a within-child, per-feature model whose predicted looking is compared with observed looking slot by slot, with a social-pointer condition and a control for low-level salience, and with a pre-registered set of failure conditions from `01` section 4. If the per-slot residual outperforms the global average, if the large-and-compressible gap beats both noise and the familiar, and if the pointer adds a predictive feature only where the adult's closure differs, the shape gains ground. If any of the refuters fires, it is withdrawn.

The stop is **unreached, not unreachable**: the framework can fix the shape of the answer and the instrument that would close it, and it cannot name the feature list. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a list of features children should attend to. It says the question has the wrong shape for a list: a feature's valence is relative to the child's current residual and habitat, so no standing list survives a change of what the child is trying to do. What survives is a four-condition separator. Attention is allocated per slot by a boundary diff on the gap between prediction and observation; the selected gap is large and compressible, not noise and not the fully familiar; the child inherits a head start and imports pointers from other people's differing frames with provenance; and a ground that does not update on the researcher's own reading settles which commitments pay. Two attentional regimes over the same features are isomers, so the arrangement carries the difference a feature list cannot see. The framework returns that shape, six refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The feature list stays open until the ground settles it.
