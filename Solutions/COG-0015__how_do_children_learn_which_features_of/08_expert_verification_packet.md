# Expert verification packet: the Tether on attention in development

**What this is:** a claim in a form a developmental psychologist or attention researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the checkable sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal feature list. Not `grounded`.** No child was measured. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Which features are worth attending to" does not license a feature list. A feature's valence is relative to the child's current residual and habitat, so no standing list survives a change of residual. What survives is a four-condition separator: attention is allocated per slot by a boundary diff on the gap between prediction and observation; the selected gap is large and compressible; the child inherits a head start and imports pointers from other people's differing closures with provenance and a shadow test; and a ground that does not update on the researcher's report settles which commitments pay. Two regimes over the same feature set are isomers, so the arrangement carries the difference. The framework returns that shape, refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The feature list itself is a declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal and falsifiable; checkable against figures 11, 1 and 5

**Statement:** a standing list of worth-attending features is structurally refused; attention is per slot and the selected discrepancy is intermediate, not maximal and not zero.

**Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 5 :18 "seeks the gap that is large and compressible".

**Refuter:** (a) a feature that stays worth attending to across every change of residual and habitat with the ground paying in each case; (b) a global attention statistic that predicts the next look better than per-slot residual without importing the slot structure; (c) a systematic preference for noise or for the fully familiar over the intermediate compressible discrepancy, with the ground paying for it.

### C2. World-facing and checkable against the developmental attention literature

**Statement:** the orienting response behaves as a comparator (a model of the expected stimulus is compared with input, a match inhibits, a change releases looking), and social pointers act as cues that are ranged against the child's own residual rather than as commands.

**What to check against your own knowledge:** does the habituation and dishabituation evidence support a mismatch comparator over a pure fatigue account? Does gaze-following behave as an expectation-based orienting cue, and is the imported pointer attached to what the child is already attending to? Is the claim that "worth" is intermediate (Berlyne's collative variables and optimal-arousal theory) consistent with the infant looking-time data you know?

**Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :11 "held: the model was right, nothing owed"; Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

**Refuter:** dishabituation with no change in prediction model and no residual, explained by fatigue or sensory adaptation; or zero surplus from following another's gaze where the adult's closure demonstrably differs; or a pointer adopted without being ranged against the child's residual, which would make it a verdict rather than a convention (Fig 10 :47).

### C3. Measurement-facing and checkable against the instrument literature

**Statement:** the broken link is the middle one, terms for what a situation affords plus what counts as progress; a pooled looking-time average can hide the live slot, and the channel decays while the ground does not.

**Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 2 :37-38 "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

**Refuter:** an instrument that reads per-slot residual without a ground that updates on the experimenter's own report, and whose readings agree with the ground rather than with expectation. Also refuted if an effect-size average can replace the per-slot reading without losing the "where to look" signal.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a feature list, an age schedule, or a named winner among novelty, salience and reward.
- Not that infants are literal Bayesian or predictive-coding machines. Those are world analogues, secondary to the figures.
- Not that looking time reads the source of attention. One reading can have several sources (metamerism).
- Not that social pointing instructs. It is a cue needing provenance and a shadow test.
- Not `grounded` and not a claim that this document settled anything.
- Not a theory of everything. No final room; the feature slot stays open.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A within-child, per-feature model whose predicted looking is compared with observed looking slot by slot, with a social-pointer condition and a low-level-salience control.
2. A demonstration that the large-and-compressible discrepancy beats both uncompressed noise and the fully familiar at the ground.
3. A demonstration that following a pointer adds a predictive feature only where the other person's closure differs, with the source and the explained gap recorded.
4. The six refuters in `01` section 4, any one of which withdraws the claim if it fires.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :49 and :51, Fig 1 :19, and Fig 5 :18. Four short passages.
2. Say whether the no-standing-list claim is a category error or an empirical generalization, and which one the figure actually makes.
3. Name the instrument that would distinguish a mismatch comparator from fatigue in a looking-time paradigm, or say why none exists.
4. Name one thing a caregiver's gaze adds that the child's own closure could not reach, or say why the surplus is zero.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
