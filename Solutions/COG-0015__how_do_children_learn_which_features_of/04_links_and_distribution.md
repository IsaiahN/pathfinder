## Overlap assessment

- Five of the seven substantive world sources are encyclopedia pages. They are one evidence pool in the provenance sense (same host and editorial system), even though the literatures they summarize (Gibsonian ecology, Friston's computational neuroscience, habituation and attention research, joint attention developmental psychology, curiosity motivation research) are genuinely low-overlap fields. They count as **one weak witness for the mechanism shape**, not five.
- Friston 2010 and the free-energy encyclopedia page overlap in author and subject; they are one witness, not two.
- The habituation and predictive-coding syntheses both discuss prediction error and infant orienting. They share some underlying literature, so their agreement about the boundary-diff shape is corroboration at moderate, not maximum, independence.
- All world sources are secondary to the figures. None was allowed to flip a verdict. The two world tensions (Dark-Room critique, FEP unfalsifiability) were returned to the census and scoped in `01` section 6.
