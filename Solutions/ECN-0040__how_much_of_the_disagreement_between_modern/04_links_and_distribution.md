## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Mankiw's split sentence is the cleanest confirmation that the two parts are different.** He grants *"some kernels of truth"* to MMT while saying its *"most novel policy prescriptions do not follow cogently from its premises."* That is a mechanism concession paired with a policy verdict, exactly the two-part structure the figures require. It is corroboration, not authority; the verdict comes from Fig 9 and Fig 2.
2. **Palley gives a mechanism dispute with a possible ground.** *"MMT lacks a plausible theory of inflation"* is a claim about the world that data and model comparison can answer. The Liu, Minford and Ou (2024) simulation is an instance of such a test.
3. **Caplan and Miller are the honest conflict.** Their claim that many disagreements *"described as value judgments are simply disagreements about facts"* means the D1/D2 boundary must be drawn empirically for each dispute. It does not produce a proportion. Recorded as a live strain in `01` F8.
4. **Carnevali and Fontana supply the union diagnostic.** That *"much of the debate over MMT has taken place outside conventional scholarly settings"* fits Fig 9 :4-5's non-shrinking disagreement, but it is a diagnostic, not a measurement.
5. **Risk perception supplies the D3 split.** Starr's finding that people accept risks far greater when voluntary, and Slovic's work on dread and affect, show that judged severity is not the same as statistical probability. That is why risk judgments cannot have one anchor.
6. **The fact-value literature is an outside analogue with low overlap.** The Wikipedia synthesis distinguishes positive economics (description, falsifiable predictions) from normative economics (prescriptions). This is a corroborating analogue for the Fig 2 anchor rule, not a substitute for it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the split method and the refuter first, not the conclusion of either school.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the split claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Seat Map :163:** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not a check.
- **Fig 9 :60:** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A contact list is a budget filter, never a witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask which condition a named dispute belongs to and what would settle it; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the sources above. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the eight-step loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator; the *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* requires an external validator for any output it produces.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. The strongest objection is F8 in `01`: if every apparently normative dispute can be reduced to a fact dispute, D2 loses its separate anchor. Ask that question explicitly before offering the theory.
