## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0040 the shape is: a split into separately anchored parts with a verdict per part, and no single proportion. A percentage is a filled-in answer, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing and look for recurring terms that overlap it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Describe the residual in effect terms: a disagreement that does not shrink with effort and where each side works on a different subset.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the direct ancestor of the F1 split in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name (or a number)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.

For this question: naming a proportion would require a ground that measures both an empirical residual and a value choice in one currency. No such instrument is named in the sources read. The Caplan and Miller claim that some value judgments are fact disputes in disguise (section 4 below) is a candidate for moving particular disputes, not for measuring the whole.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37-38: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*

This is the source problem for ECN-0040. A mechanism dispute reads the world; a policy dispute reads a preference. One instrument cannot serve both, and an instrument that serves one will be wrong on the axes of the other. The Wikipedia article on positive and normative economics makes the same point in the discipline's own vocabulary: positive statements are *"potentially falsifiable and may be contradicted by evidence"*, while a normative statement *"reflects value judgments"*. Its report of Caplan and Miller, that *"many policy disagreements often described as value judgments are simply disagreements about facts"*, is a genuine conflict. Per the canonical rule it sends us back to the census, not to a softer reading. The re-census in `01` F8 strengthens the split: each disputed point must be classified, not assumed.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* on the validator: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."* A poll of economists is that clock, not a ground.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape must a correct answer to ECN-0040 have?** It must be a decomposition, not an apportionment:

1. For each concrete point of disagreement, say which condition it lives in (world claim, people claim, or a mixed risk claim that needs its own split).
2. Name the anchor that would settle it: empirical ground for world claims, human judgment for value claims.
3. Give a verdict per part and keep the verdicts separate.
4. Do not add them. Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*

**Why not a percentage?** Because the question asks for a ratio between things measured in different units, and Fig 12 :130 names that sum as the error. The framework returns a shape and a route; it does not return the fraction. The honest stop is `unreached`, not `unreachable`: a census of the actual disputes is possible empirical work, but it was not done here.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists who agree with the split is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a measurement of the literature and cannot settle anything it reports on. That is why its mode is `specified`, not `grounded`.
