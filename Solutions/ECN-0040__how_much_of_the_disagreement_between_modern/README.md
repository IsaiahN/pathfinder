**Mode:** `specified` on the structural claim (the disagreement is a union compound that must be split, and each part has its own anchor), `general` on the shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement of the actual disagreement was taken, and no proportion is asserted.

**Status:** done

## The residual stated as an effect

The question asks for a proportion: split the MMT versus mainstream disagreement into a mechanisms part and a policy-priorities-and-risk-judgments part, and say how much of each there is. Option A treats this as one measurable disagreement that can be apportioned, like dividing a sum. Option B asks first whether the disagreement is one question or several, and gives a verdict per part with a named ground for each. Option A yields a single number that mixes two currencies and cannot be settled. Option B yields a decomposed map that a specialist can check and refute.

An answer would look like this: for each concrete point of disagreement, a statement of which condition it lives in, which anchor would settle it, and a verdict that is not averaged with the verdicts of other points. Bind that description as an ordinary term before the run, so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Formula step "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No percentage answer to the question. The framework returns a shape and per-domain verdicts, not an apportionment of incommensurable quantities.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* Gathering agreement among economists does not measure the disagreement.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Send the residual and the refuter, not the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
