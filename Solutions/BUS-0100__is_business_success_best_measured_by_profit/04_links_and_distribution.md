## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**Two pools, counted carefully.** The management and performance-measurement material (balanced
scorecard and the AOM/Journal of Business Ethics venue pages) is one witness, not several. The
measurement-gaming material (Goodhart and Campbell) is another closely related pool, so those two
corroborations count as one witness between them. Arrow's theorem and the bioindicator literature are
two genuinely low-overlap fields.

- **Balanced scorecard.** It is a set of financial and non-financial measures in four perspectives,
  explicitly *"not meant to be a replacement for traditional financial or operational reports but a
  succinct summary"*. The literature's own design question, *"Of all the measures you could have
  chosen, why did you choose these?"*, is the frame asking itself to justify its score, which Fig 1
  :27 disqualifies as self-scoring. The recorded criticism of *"lack of an overall score"* (Jensen
  2001) corroborates Fig 12 :130 from within the business pool. Its documented shift away from the
  original US-commercial four perspectives when applied to public and nonprofit organizations
  corroborates Fig 11 :51: no standing list of good measures survives a change of residual. Business
  pool, one witness.
- **Goodhart and Campbell.** *"When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure"* and
  *"The more any quantitative social indicator is used for social decision-making, the more subject it
  will be to corruption pressures..."* are the same rule as Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47 arrived at in
  monetary policy and social-science evaluation. Corroboration at a different stratum; one shared
  measurement-gaming pool.
- **Arrow's theorem (mathematics, social choice).** *"no social welfare function f satisfies U, SO,
  WP, D, and I"* with more than two alternatives. The impossibility of aggregating individual
  rankings into a single social ordering is a precise, low-overlap corroboration of F4 and F7: there is
  no neutral procedure that turns several incommensurable measures or several stakeholders' judgments
  into one ranking without importing a value choice. Arrow's own line, *"it seems to make no sense to
  add the utility of one individual... with the utility of another individual,"* is the mathematical
  version of Fig 12 :125. The theorem decides nothing about business; it corroborates the shape.
- **Bioindicator (ecology).** A single species can reveal environmental status, but indicators *"have
  been reported as inaccurate when applied to geographically and environmentally diverse regions"*
  and each index must be shown relevant to the conditions it monitors. That is Fig 11 :49-51 and the
  source essay's metamerism argument at a different stratum: a reading is not transferable to a new
  habitat without re-validation. Low overlap with management.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in
`05_what_shape_means.md` only. Their author wrote the figures, so they carry no independent
corroboration weight. The ledger records that overlap explicitly.

**Venue pages opened, no verdict bearing (silent on the question).** AOM homepage, Publishing with

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the
  residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and
  is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has
  absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask
  what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the
  framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is
  disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**Two hard practical constraints.**

1. The AOM Publishing page states: *"Artificial Intelligence: Work submitted to AOM must be created by
   the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research
   question and properly cited."* The Journal of Business Ethics submission guidelines add: *"Large
   Language Models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria."* This
   whole solution is an LLM-assisted simulated derivation and is labelled as such everywhere. It must
   **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a
   private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check,
   and let that human author whatever they conclude, with disclosure under the applicable policy.
2. The Registered Reports route is the one venue type whose design matches the pre-registered refuter
   discipline, because it evaluates introduction, methods, measurement plan and analysis without the
   results. A human researcher could own a Registered Report built from the stage-1 refuter. The LLM
   output itself is not the submission.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would
you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the
note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with
the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the
three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No
provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1,
offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later
agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter
before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an
endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an
opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an
attachment pushed to a list.
