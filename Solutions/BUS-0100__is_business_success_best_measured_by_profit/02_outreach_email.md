# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must
have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is
shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a
refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition
they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about how business success should be measured before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about performance measurement that I would like you to check against what you
> already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with
> anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "best measured by profit, growth, resilience,
> innovation, stakeholder outcomes, or some combination" is not a question with one metric as its
> answer. A measure is legitimate only when it is ranged against a ground that the question itself
> selects. Three different grounds are in play: the market, for realized return and survival; the
> habitat across time, for continued contact and renewal; and human judgment, for questions that are
> constitutively about people. Two further things follow. First, the measures are in different
> currencies, so a single blended score is not a deeper measure, it is the error; they have to be read
> separately. Second, several measures that read the same underlying fact are one witness, not
> several. On the "who decides" half: the residual selects the ground rather than a person choosing
> it, and whoever is measured by the reading, or benefits from a particular value of it, is out of the
> deciding seat.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that profit, growth or any other listed metric is useless; that a
> composite index is never convenient; that I can name which single measure wins; that any particular
> firm or rating is a case in point; that this is settled by evidence; or that it is the only account
> of business success. I am offering a shape that any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is
> not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a measure whose value is invariant under every reframing and timing choice available to the
>    measured party while the ground is held fixed, and which still settles the question;
> 2. a composite with commensurable units and a declared exchange rate that pays at the ground, so
>    that summing the components is not the error;
> 3. a people-question settled correctly on every axis the people care about by a non-human metric
>    alone, with no appeal to human judgment;
> 4. a negotiated convention that settles a factual world question with no external ground and is
>    later confirmed by an independent instrument;
> 5. a metric whose validity and valence stay the same across a documented change of residual that
>    reverses what the decision is for;
> 6. one instrument that settles both conditions in both failure types and leaves no categorically
>    different residue behind.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful
> to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my
> summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers: the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether`
>   repository.
> - The essays on shape, source discrimination and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and
> can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and
> state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Grounds kept separate, not blended | *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* (Fig 12 :130) |
| "Who decides" answered as a seat, not a name | *"Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* (Fig 10 :86); *"Who holds the seat is not chosen. The position selects the occupant."* (Fig 10 :85) |
| Human judgment placed as an anchor where appropriate | *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* (Fig 2 :37) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning measure | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check what it is looking for; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
- This packet was produced with LLM assistance. Do not submit it as authored work. See the AI-policy note in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` section 6 and `04_links_and_distribution.md` section 3.
