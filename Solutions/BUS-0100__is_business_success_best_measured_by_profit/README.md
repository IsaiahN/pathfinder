## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not which number is largest. It is which reading is allowed to settle the
residual between what a firm's library predicts and what the ground actually returns, and who is
entitled to select that reading. In effect terms:

- **b** is the frame's belief about what "success" would look like.
- **a** is the action taken on that belief: investing, reporting, disclosing, rewarding, regulating.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the frame's library of measures predicts will follow.
- **o'** is what the ground actually returns: realized market outcome, survival through a shock, or
  the judgments of the people the question is about.
- **s** is the firm or the decision unit. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed per slot, never aggregated
  into a single corporate "success score" (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

Each candidate in the question is a proposed anchor, not just a number:

- Option A, "one metric is best, and a decider picks it": leads to a frame scoring itself with a
  quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27) and to a convention that decides an outcome, which has stopped
  being a convention (Fig 2 :43).
- Option B, "the residual selects the ground per domain, several incommensurable grounds are held
  apart, no composite is summed, and the seat is the position rather than the person": leads to a
  checkable shape with written refuters and an open instrument gap.

**What an answer would look like**, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run
(Formula, "Direction"): (i) a rule that maps the residual described in effect terms to the
appropriate ground, (ii) a discriminator that tells whether two candidate measures are independent
witnesses or one evidence pool wearing two names, and (iii) an account of the deciding seat that
excludes anyone measured by the reading or benefiting from a particular value of it. Until bound,
that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in section 4
of `01` can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and
never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the business question, and no
  claim that the framework is the only true account of business success.

Four figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms
   is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A
   mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the
   correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless
   when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Profit, growth and share price
   often read the same market fact; their agreement is one witness.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify
   it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the
   framework.
4. Whoever is measured cannot own the reading. *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured
   by a reading cannot own it."* (Fig 10 :86). This is the figure-grounded answer to the "who decides"
   half of the question, stated here because it governs the outreach design too.
5. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many
   jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as
   well as the structural one.
