## 1. The residual line, taken alone

> Which proposed reading o' is entitled to settle the gap between what a frame's measures predict and
> what the ground returns for a firm or decision unit, and who may select that reading?

R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot. The object of explanation is the **authority to choose o'**, not the level
of any score. Nothing in this line says "profit," "growth," "resilience," "innovation," "stakeholder,"
"index," "board" or "agency." Any answer that begins with one of those words has named a candidate
before describing the residual, against Fig 9 :27-28 (*"describe it before you go looking, or any frame
you pick will seem to fit"*) and Fig 8 :53 (*"Residual first, frame second"*).

**Anchor check.** A nameable anchor exists in general: Fig 2 :29 lists *"a market"* and *"a patient
outcome"* as anchors. But the residual asks which anchor is legitimate for a given question, so the
answer must be a selection rule, not the assertion that one anchor always wins. This is well formed
and not yet a `gap`.

## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among frame Γ, action a, proposed reading M and selecting seat D, indexed by
  slot. At least four operands, with an ordering (measure before decision) and a threshold comparison
  ("best"). A two-place reading (firm, score) would lose the authority half of the question, so it is
  rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor does not update on the frame (Fig 2 :25, :33). "Best" is a
  threshold comparison between two quantities (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is compared with another,
  not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a firm or decision unit, but "best" exists only at the population scale
  (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between a firm's measurement loop and the
  population's selection loop (Fig 4). The stakeholder ground crosses into a different kind of anchor
  entirely (Fig 2 :36). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale and cross-kind.

**Divergence from the primary census, recorded.** The primary called its first two domains D1 (market
ground) and D3 (human ground). This seat at first proposed splitting the market reading into two
domains: "reported performance" (profit, growth as accounting facts) and "market verdict" (return and
survival as ground). On re-census they share one anchor, the realized market outcome, and their overlap
is a compound, so they are one domain A. This seat also tested merging C (people) and A (market), since
customers and investors are people; it declined, because the anchors differ in kind: A is settled by
realized return, C by the judgment of the people the question is about, which Fig 2 :36 makes legitimate

**Partition or union:** union. The two named conditions are ground selection (A, B, C) and seat
legitimacy (D). Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).

**Dependency order:** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3). This
seat agrees with the primary ordering and adds that the residual line itself only supports the first
three links; planning and carrying are candidate answers introduced by the frame, not forced by the
residual.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead
reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *success is a
  relational loop between a firm and the ground that settles its residual, and the loop picks its own
  observer.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a concrete measurement decision. Result: *pick a
  balanced set of KPIs, have the board approve them, and review them each quarter.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two
   conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it gives the board one approval step
   for two conditions that need different instruments.
2. It loses the no-addition rule. "Balanced set of KPIs" invites exactly the sum Fig 12 :130 forbids.
3. It loses the anchor rule. "The ground that settles its residual" does not say that the market, the
   habitat and human judgment are different in kind (Fig 2 :29, :36). Coming back down, it permits a
   people-question to be settled by a financial instrument wrong on the cared-about axes (Fig 2 :37-38).
4. It loses the seat rule. "Picks its own observer" sounds like self-selection; it drops the exclusion
   of whoever is measured or benefits (Fig 10 :86, :93) and the prohibition on a convention deciding
   an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
5. It loses the independence test and the source-discriminator gap. The abstraction cannot say why five
   KPIs may be one witness (Fig 2 :31), nor what instrument would separate value creation from luck.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot
hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version
(Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as
such.

   Business Ethics authorship rule quoted in `10_source_ledger.csv` constrain whether it can be
