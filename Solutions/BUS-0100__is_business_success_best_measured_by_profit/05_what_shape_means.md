## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing
thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer
distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive
is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it
> provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0100 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about measurement that can be stated
before any particular metric, index, board or agency is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *success is a comparison against a ground selected by the residual, held in separate
  incommensurable currencies, with the deciding seat occupied by whoever is outside the slice being
  graded and gains nothing from a particular reading.*
- Name, withheld: *the best measure is profit, or revenue growth, or return on equity, or the balanced
  scorecard, or an ESG rating, or a triple-bottom-line index.*

The distinction matters here because performance-measurement practice is almost entirely in the naming
business. The balanced scorecard names four perspectives; rating agencies name composite scores;
standard setters name reportable metrics. Those are bindings from the management frame onto
quantities already in Γ, not outputs of the loop. The framework's own limit applies: a shape is what
any candidate answer must have; the candidate itself is not supplied.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do
> know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your
> vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "balanced scorecard," "key performance indicator,"
"shareholder value," "stakeholder theory," "triple bottom line," "Goodhart's law" and "registered
report." None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual,
per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (which reading is
entitled to settle the residual, and on what authority) came first; the frames were selected only
after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will
> need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a
> satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0100 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question.
Here the bundle is especially easy to miss because both halves share the word "measured." "Which
measure" and "who decides" are different loops with different instruments.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is
> nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the ground-selection rule, the no-addition rule, the
independence test and the seat rule are stated, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that
only renames the candidate metrics is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no
progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A
   gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source
   gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the
   same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0100, neither has happened for a single winning metric. There is no prospective measurement
that pays for "profit is the best measure of business success" across the habitat and stakeholder
grounds, and the management-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F11
of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the
> definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy
> information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a measurement system makes when it reads a profit or ESG score and names its
source. One high score is a reading. It is consistent with genuine value creation, a favourable
habitat, an accounting choice, a temporary information asymmetry, monopoly granted by regulation, or
simple luck. The reading does not determine the source. Naming the source from the reading is
metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. Fig 2 :37-38 adds the instrument
version: *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* A profit instrument can be right on
return and silent on harm. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description
travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy,
it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the
expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

The ecology witness corroborates this at a different stratum without sharing the framework's evidence
pool. The bioindicator literature records that indicators *"have been reported as inaccurate when
applied to geographically and environmentally diverse regions"* and that each index must be shown
relevant to the conditions it monitors. A reading is not transferable to a new habitat without
re-validation. That is Fig 11 :51 (*"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual"*)
in a field that never heard of the Tether.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to "who decides"

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an
> action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige
> agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is
one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the
author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded
here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about boards or rating agencies.

For BUS-0100 this is the direct answer to a lazy reading of "who gets to decide." A famous investor, a
prestigious standard setter or a widely cited rating is not entitled to settle the measurement
question by reputation. Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone
benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* Prestige can fund a measurement system, which
is exactly what a budget is for, but it cannot make the system right.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular metric is forbidden by "shape, not a name." That statement
was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held
in Γ. If a measure is already in a firm's or a field's library, naming it is a binding from the
management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding
moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) or that the
framework itself selected it. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a
category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the
withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory,
> cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of
> the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture
> post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too.
> However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the
> formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the
figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about business measurement. It is not a
test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its
refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0100 the canonical shape is: **a ground-selection rule, a no-addition rule, an independence
test and a seat rule, with the winning metric left unstated.** It is not a list of KPIs, not a ranking
of indices, and not a claim that profit is wrong or that stakeholder outcomes are soft. The names that
appear in the measurement literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would
convert the shape into a grounded answer, in particular one that discriminates value creation from
rent extraction from luck when all produce the same score, are not held here, so the answer stops at
`specified` and the stop is recorded.
