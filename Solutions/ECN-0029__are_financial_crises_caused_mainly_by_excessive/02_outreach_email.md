# Outreach draft - validate the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been re-checked against the verbatim figure lines. The stage-1 offer collapses on contact if the output does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about "the main cause of financial crises"

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a small derivation about how causal questions get framed, and I have one output I would like you to check against what you already know about financial crises. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "is it mainly risk-taking, credit growth, bad regulation, or financial structure?" is not one question with four answers. It is a union of overlapping conditions presented as a partition. From the figures: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* And: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* So an intervention on rules can clear the cases where only rules failed while leaving the intersection with a credit boom intact.
>
> The second part is about measurement, not economics: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single "main cause" number is an aggregation across crisis episodes. It can look clean while one live slot still carries the signal.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that structural features are the main cause, that credit growth is always the strongest predictor, that regulation is good or bad, or that this derivation settles anything empirical. The figures give a dependency and contact order, not a causal share. Excessive risk-taking is the directly visible actor; credit and balance-sheet structure sit further out in the cascade. That is an ordering claim, and it is `specified`, not measured. I am also not asking you to trust a source I have not shown you.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. The four conditions form a partition: each crisis falls under exactly one, and removing the dominant one drives the residual to near zero on held-out episodes.
> 2. A single aggregate "main cause" statistic predicts held-out crises as well as the per-case residuals, with no live slot left behind.
> 3. A model truncated at direct actors predicts held-out crises as well as one that includes balance-sheet connectivity, so the cascade adds nothing.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the union claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would like from you:** just your verdict on stage 1, before any theory is shown. What would you count as a failure of the union claim? Which of your own results would make you reject it?
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the source material. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
>
> - [name]

## Stage 2 and stage 3, opt-in only

**Stage 2** (only if the recipient asks): send the three verified source links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`, with the note that the figures themselves are the authority. Record the stage-1 verdict before sending anything else, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Stage 3** (only if they want the method): offer the formula as a generator to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter, names their own ground, and states the mode of what they get. From the RSI Trap essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*
