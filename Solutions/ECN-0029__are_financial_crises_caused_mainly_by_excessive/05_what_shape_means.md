## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is in the figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. For this question the shape is: a union of overlapping conditions, per-domain verdicts with separate anchors, no legitimate global "mainly" statistic, and a contact order running from the visible actor out to structural fragility. The shape does **not** include a name for the dominant factor. That is the part owed to the ground.

Concretely: *"crises arise under a union of interacting conditions"* is a shape. *"crises are mainly caused by credit growth"* is a name, and the figures do not return it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap and correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So "unstable credit growth" is admitted as an effect description (a growth rate), not as a cause named from a theory.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For this question we are not done: the dominant share is still owed, which is why the verdict is `specified` with a stated gap.

## 3. The question itself may be malformed, and that is a finding

*The Library of Babel* (opened this session) names exactly this case:

> *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed."*
>
> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

Fig 9 :4-5 says the same in the figure vocabulary: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The four-cause "or" question is the bundle. Unbundling it is not a refusal to answer; it is the answer at the level the figures govern.

## 4. Reading a cause off a crisis outcome is a source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green but *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several underlying sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered) create the same reading. And *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*

Apply this to the residual: a crisis reading (a crash, a default wave, a recession) is a projection. Risk-taking, a credit boom, a captured rule, and a fragile balance sheet can all produce the same reading. Naming one as "the source" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. The essay's remedy: *"This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source"*, and it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* So what is owed is a discriminator (a counterfactual, a natural experiment, a cross-country panel), not a further argument about which narrative is more compelling.

## 5. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a name (a dominant factor) is licensed only when an anchor discriminates among the four conditions on held-out episodes. No such reading was taken in this derivation, so none of the four is named as dominant.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding any mention of a candidate cause. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in the library. Here all four candidates are already nameable domains with their own actors and anchors (see `01` §2). Naming a domain is not inventing a seventh term. What the figure actually forbids is asserting a **dominant causal share** the framework does not return. The correct form is: name the four domains, keep their verdicts separate, leave the ranking open. Both the original reading and this narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session):

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous crisis economists asked to endorse the union claim would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone who can refute it, and lets the ground (data, counterfactuals, a board) settle it.

The same essay supplies the honest standing of this whole folder:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09`, and `11` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not ground readings and cannot settle the question they report on. Mode is `specified` throughout, with the structural part `general`.

## 7. One more limit from the essays

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (opened this session): *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* This is why `01` F10 refused to import Figure 13's physics six-term set into finance, and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The four domains used here are a declared choice, recorded as such, not a discovery of nature's own list of crisis causes.
