## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described the residual sends a recording rather than a generator. Lead with the union claim and its three refuters.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the union claim were true before showing the figure text.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 7 :13** - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Do not treat a pile of positive replies as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a reusable method, not an answer.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the union claim and its refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then, if invited, the links above with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer (summary of `02_outreach_email.md`)

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the union claim, and the three refuters. Ask for a verdict on that alone. No framework material.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the source links. Their stage-1 verdict was already recorded, and any later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, method only.** Offer the formula as a generator to reuse, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives checking. If a checker fires one of the refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, the union claim is withdrawn and the packet is not sent further.
