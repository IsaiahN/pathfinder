## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5 and :55-57 (union), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 11 :52 (contact cascade).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`, namely "union not partition; mainly aggregates; four anchors; structure further out; regulation can become an outcome variable; no winner named."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete four-cause question and the stage-1 email in `02`, which asks a researcher to test the claim against crisis episodes.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **scope of the union verdict**: the figures license "this framing is a union", not "every crisis is a union of exactly these four conditions", and the shorthand in `02` can read stronger than the figure allows;
- the **relative valence of actors** (Fig 11 :49): the shorthand says "structure is further out", which a reader can easily hear as "structure is more important", the exact confusion the figure refuses;
- the **anchor test for domain C** (Fig 2 :29-30): the shorthand says "four anchors" without carrying that the regulatory anchor partially updates, so a reader may treat all four as equally firm.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a non-shrinking four-way disagreement must be split before it is searched, and a global "mainly" statistic discards the live per-episode signal. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
