# The Tether's answer on universal mind versus learned form

**Mode: `general` on the structural separator, `specified` on each method domain, `gap` for the feature list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a cognitive pattern, a population and an observed form, a researcher wants to say which source produced the reading: shared machinery, exposure-dependent learning, historical diffusion, or an institution-fixed convention. All four can produce the same behavior. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." The residual is three-indexed (feature, population, candidate source), not two-indexed, and it is not symmetric: the source cannot be read back off the form (Operators :29 to :33 contrast ordered and symmetric bonds).

## 2. The shape

1. **Split the question; do not pool it.** The classes overlap. A form can be universal in machinery and learned in its triggering conditions. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A pooled universal percentage is the named failure in Fig 1 :22.
2. **Ask what is invariant and what is bound differently.** Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Over a fixed atom set the variants are isomers (Fig 12 :117): "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." Where a new institution appears, the relation is composition plus import, not isomerism.
3. **Discriminate among sources; do not accumulate replications.** The same reading has several sources. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." A cross-population replication is another reading of green, not a spectrometer. The separator uses low-overlap frames: cross-species presence (phylogeny), developmental exposure (ontogeny), documented transmission lineage (provenance), and an intervention that moves the candidate cause. Fig 8 :50: "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."
4. **Require a non-updating anchor.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :31: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A construct whose reading moves when researchers reinterpret it is demoted to a frame.
5. **Fix the causal arrow with a change, not a correlation.** Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Correlation is symmetric; "culture causes form" is ordered. An intervention, a natural experiment, or a provenanced transmission history is owed, plus a pre-registered refusal (Operators :58).
6. **Establish comparability before comparison.** The chain breaks at vocabulary and objective (Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). A pseudoetic construct measures the instrument, not the population. Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip gap to be measured.
7. **Name no feature.** Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The separator returns a shape and an instrument requirement. The feature list is a `gap`, and it is a gap that a source-discriminating instrument would close, not a gap that more argument can fill.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of universal cognitive features. That list is `ground`-owed and unreached here.
- Not that any particular feature is innate, or that any particular feature is purely learned.
- Not that cultural variation is noise, error, or a thinner version of a universal. It is a different binding, and sometimes a different atom set.
- Not that cross-cultural psychology's results are wrong. The WEIRD critique is a sampling and comparability finding, and Fig 2 :40 makes its agreement one evidence pool, not a refutation.
- Not a new operator mapping culture onto mind. Operators :68 refuses an eighth bond without a derived test.
- Not `grounded`. No comparative measurement, intervention or source-discriminating instrument was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would settle the feature list

Two different questions, kept separate.

**To settle the separator (the shape):** a working source-discriminating observation that separates U, L, H and I for a named pattern, with a non-updating anchor, two genuinely low-overlap frames, and a pre-registered refusal that has been tested. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." The proto-instruments already returning something are cross-species comparison, developmental exposure studies, transmission-history reconstruction and quasi-experimental variation.

**To settle a feature slot:** a ground reading that pays for a source assignment for that slot. *The Hard Problem is About Source* names the standard: it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the metamer. Here the corresponding discriminations are:

- Does the form track cross-species presence with no transmission link (U), exposure and developmental timing (L), a documented lineage (H), or an outcome-fixing local convention (I)?
- Does the assignment survive when the cause is moved by an intervention or an exogenous shock, with an outcome that does not update on the researchers' reading?
- Does the construct survive the round trip into another language and back, with the gap measured (Fig 4 :30)?

**Standing falsifiers (pre-registered in `01` section 4):** clean partition; new cognitive primitive; pooled assignment reproducing every per-slot assignment; source classes always distinguishable without provenance; a moving anchor or a collapsed independence claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached for the list, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The figures give the shape any candidate separator must have, the order in which its instruments must be applied, and the refuters written in advance. They stop at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 11 :39: capability, and therefore universality, is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which cognitive features are universal, and it does not average a percentage. It says that the universal and the learned overlap, so the verdict belongs to each feature and population separately; that the invariant item is a functional group whose members and bindings vary; and that the same observed form can be produced by shared machinery, by learning, by diffusion, or by an institution-fixed category. Repetition in the same kind of sample cannot tell these apart, because they are metamers of one reading. What can tell them apart is a source-discriminating observation built from low-overlap frames, a documented provenance, an intervention that moves the cause, and an anchor that does not move when the researchers reinterpret it. The separator returns a shape, a dependency order and five refuters written in advance. It refuses to name a feature until the ground pays for the assignment. The list stays open until then, and that is a verdict, not a silence.
