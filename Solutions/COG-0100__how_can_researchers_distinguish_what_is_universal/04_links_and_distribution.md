## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

Recommended order in a reply: (1) the separator and its five refuters, (2) ask what they would count as a failure, (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
