## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is a separator: split the union, keep the verdict per slot, identify the invariant functional group and the variable binding, and discriminate among sources. The shape is not a list of cognitive features. "Shared machinery with variable binding" is a shape; "number, color, and face recognition are universal" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* (carried) on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: describe the residual as "several sources give one reading" before choosing a comparison frame. Otherwise any field picked will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27 to :28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12, :13).

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning one into *the* universal feature turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source problem: why repetition is not discrimination

This section is the one *The Hard Problem is About Source* governs, and it is the reason this question has a methods answer at all. Opened 2026-11-22. The essay states:

- "You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."
- "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."
- "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."
- "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
- "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

The essay's color case is a metamer: one green reading from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The full set of ways to produce a reading "is not known to be closed or determined." The spectrometer resolved it, not an argument.

The cross-cultural analogue is exact. One observed form can come from shared machinery, exposure-dependent learning, diffusion from a common historical source, or an institution-fixed convention. A cross-cultural replication is a second reading of green. It is not a spectrometer. Fig 8 :51 gives the framework form: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."

**Consequence for this question:** the separator's decisive step is source discrimination, and the thing owed is an instrument that comments on source, not further readings of the same form. This is a `general` shape. The specific instrument is a `gap` (D7 in `01`), constrained by Fig 6 :32 to be improved from an instrument already returning something.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): "Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"; "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"; "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous cross-cultural psychologists to agree with a separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a comparative measurement, an intervention, a source-discriminating instrument, settle it.

## 6. Q: what shape does this question get, and what does it not get?

**Gets:** a `general` structural shape and `specified` per-domain methods:

1. union, not partition (Fig 9 :54),
2. per-slot verdicts (Fig 1 :19),
3. invariant functional group plus variable binding (Fig 12 :123, :117),
4. source discrimination via low-overlap frames, provenance and a non-updating anchor (Fig 8 :50 to :51, Fig 2 :29, :31),
5. an intervention or instrument for the causal arrow (Fig 12 :111 to :112),
6. a pre-registered refusal (Operators :58).

**Does not get:** the names of which cognitive features are universal. That is a `gap`, with two distinct reasons. First, the figures cannot name primitives (Fig 13 :135). Second, and more important for a methods question, the names are a matter for the ground: they require the source-discriminating instrument that does not yet exist generically. Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Where a proto-instrument exists (the spectrometer case, cross-species comparison, developmental exposure, documented transmission), the corresponding slot can be promoted from `gap` to `specified` by a ground reading, not by this derivation.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any member of a slot. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. The invariant substructures invoked here (categorising, perceiving, evaluating) are not claimed as new primitives of the six-term set; they are bindings from the cognitive-science frame onto structures already held, and naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term. COG-0001 section 5b and the exemplar's `05` section 5b make the same correction.

What remains wrong in any record that names a specific feature as *the* universal is not the naming of a held atom, but (a) treating a binding as a derivation and (b) reading a source off a projection without a discriminator, which is the metamer error of section 4. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"; "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay calls such output "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the separator and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
