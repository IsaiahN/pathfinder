## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the attribution of an observed cognitive form to an origin. Arity: three-indexed, `(feature, population, candidate source)`. A two-indexed residual can register a difference but cannot attribute it, which is what the question asks for. Symmetry: not symmetric. The ordered bonds in the Operators table settle this: sequence and production are "no" for symmetry, conjunction is "yes." Reading a source back off a form is the inverse direction and is not licensed by the observation. Scale: from one laboratory to cross-species and cross-historical comparison; large scale makes pooling tempting.
- **Candidate frames in contact with R (before naming domains):** cross-population comparison, cross-species comparison, developmental exposure, emic/etic comparison, documentary transmission, experimental intervention, and instruments that distinguish sources. I found nameable anchors for all seven, so all seven qualify. I found no eighth.

- **Partition or union:** union. Presence and form are separate axes; a form can be shared in machinery and learned in triggering conditions and diffused in history. M1 to M7 overlap, so one lever leaves intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The break is at vocabulary and objective. M7, the source discriminator, is the instrument owed at that break. An intervention applied to a construct whose meaning did not travel is effort above the break.

My census matches the primary's seven domains and union reading.
