## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about quantifying the value of a message before its
content is known. Option A: assert that a single principled ex-ante scalar exists and use it as the
value. Option B: keep the per-slot ex-ante rule and refuse the settled scalar. What each leads to: A
sums two currencies, hides the live slot, promotes a budget filter to a verdict, and cannot be ranged
against a ground that has not paid; B returns a shape, a two-currency price, a written refuter, and an
honest `gap` on the number, with the instrument that would close it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

`R` by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor.
Partition or union, and order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).

- **R (the residual).** The unread slot: a frame must choose to seek, buy, design or skip a message
  while `o'` is unavailable and `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` cannot be evaluated at that slot. Arity: two
  conditions in contact, the channel's possible content and the frame's decision rule. Union, not one
  hard question (Fig 9 :54-57): a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry:
  ordered and threshold-like, not a symmetric join; `→` and `⋛` are ordered bonds. Scale: first order
  inside one decision frame, second order when the message is an import. Symbols table: *"what cannot
  be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*
- **D1, decision frame.** Actors: belief `b`, action `a`, observation `o'`, slot `s`, candidate term
  `φ`. Currency: per-slot residual mass, description cost, leftover, actions to settle. Anchor: the
  ground, what pays. Partition boundary: which slots carry live mass.
- **D2, message-as-import.** Actors: channel, external source, imported atom. Currency: an atom
  crossing the wall and bits of description. Anchor: the ground plus provenance and shadow test.
  Union: in-closure depth versus genuine absence.
- **D3, verification / settlement.** Actors: expert frames, maintainer seat, ground. Currency:
  agreement versus residual and actions to test. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition:
  whose reading ranges against the non-updating anchor.
- **D4, notation and currency.** Actors: atoms, bonds, eight operators. Currency: bits and actions in
  different units. Anchor: the priced bond under a declared code. Union of two readings held apart.
- **Order by dependency.** Support before search; SEARCHED before INWARD or OUTWARD; refuse before
  mint; settle before composing over. Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by
  importance."*

Strain test applied before choosing figure lines: Shannon bits versus decision value was re-censused
as two currencies (Fig 12 :125-130), and the field's non-additivity was kept as a distinct claim with
a different reason. No figure line was made to strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of two questions with different anchors. **Yes**, there is a principled
ex-ante quantification: a per-slot rule that finds live residual mass, seeks a gap that is large and
compressible, runs the MDL bargain, and prices the result in two currencies held apart (Fig 1 :19,
Fig 5 :18/:21/:43-44, Fig 12 :125-130). **No**, there is not a principled settled scalar before
content: the content has not arrived, depth and absence are indiscernible (Fig 6 :27), the ground has
not paid (Formula step 5), and the missing thing cannot be named before it is held (Fig 13 :135).
The ex-ante number is a filter for the budget and never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Verdicts
are kept per domain and never averaged. This is `specified` on D1 and D4, `general` on the structural
reading of D3, and a declared `gap` on the concrete number and the message identity. Nothing is
`grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, non-claims, three checkable
  sub-claims and six refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible.
  The real cost is presenting a filter as a verdict and thereby deciding which experiment to run
  without a ground, which Fig 9 :60 names as the error. Buying independence in proportion to the cost
  of being wrong (Fig 2 :42) is the mitigation; the number itself carries no verdict weight until a
  ground pays.
- **undoable:** yes. The settled-scalar reading is removed without touching the per-slot rule. The
  corrected files `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it.
  No primitive has to be withdrawn and the shape survives intact.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that measures
  description cost and action cost separately and shows whether or not they add would settle the
  currency question; an instrument that distinguishes in-closure depth from genuine absence before
  content would dissolve the Fig 6 :27 indiscernibility; a measurement showing the ground pays for the
  slot the rule ranks highest would test the anchor; a scalar that sums the two currencies
  invariantly, an ex-ante ranking independent of per-slot residual, or a number that settles a
  decision with no ground would fire the refuter. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as
  `candidate` and the number remains a `gap`.
