# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the frame

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`
are checked against the verbatim figure text. The output must survive validation or the two-stage
offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about valuing information before you know what it says

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking
> you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not
> survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** before a message arrives, a frame can still apply a
> principled rule. It can ask which object slots carry positive residual mass, whether the gap is
> large and compressible, and whether stating a candidate term costs less than the confusion it
> removes. The framework's own words are that the curiosity drive *"seeks the gap that is large and
> compressible"* and that the bargain is *"stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it,
> costs less than what was unexplained before."* That is a ranking over slots. It is not the value
> of a particular message.
>
> **What the output does not do:** it does not return one settled number. The framework prices a
> composition in two currencies: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it
> holds."* *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Your own
> field has the same split: Shannon counts bits and says the semantic aspects are irrelevant to the
> engineering problem; Howard's information value theory says a theory of probabilities without
> consequences cannot describe the importance of uncertainty to a decision maker. So an ex-ante
> value is a shape and a filter for the budget, not a witness for what the world will say.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a single scalar that sums bits and actions and is invariant under re-description of the coding
>    and of the experiment;
> 2. an ex-ante ranking independent of the per-slot residual distribution, or a slot with no live
>    mass selected that the ground still pays for;
> 3. a classification of the missing quantity as in-closure versus needs-import before any content
>    arrives, with no instrument and no ground;
> 4. a demonstration that the ex-ante number settles a decision without a ground reading.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no
> reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Figure text extract with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The loop and mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a
> reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | The standing rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Two currencies held apart | Fig 12 :125-130 - bits and actions do not add. This is the substantive check a decision theorist can make in five minutes. |
| Per-slot, never aggregate | Fig 1 :19, :22 - aggregating is how the live signal disappears. |
| No stable list of valuable sources | Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-23): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| The framework returns a shape | Canonical README: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The email never names which message will arrive. |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 10 :42 - a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. |
| Offer the formula as method | Fig 4 :22-25 - send the generator, not a recording of one success. |

**Correction applied before shipping:** an earlier draft left a stray non-English token in the

**Note on the two-stage ask:** stage 1 asks only for a check of the shape claim and its refuter. Stage
2, opt-in only, offers the figure links. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method with the instruction
that each user writes their own refuter. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2
:40), because a recipient who has absorbed the framework has joined the frame being checked.
