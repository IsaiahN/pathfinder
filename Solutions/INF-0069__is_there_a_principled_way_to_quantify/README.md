## The question restated as an effect

A frame holds a belief `b`, a library `Γ`, and an action `a`. A channel may deliver a message whose
content (the observation `o'`) is not yet available. The decision to seek, buy, or design that
message has to be made now, while the residual it would resolve is `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot `s`
and `o'` has not arrived. Two options follow:

- **Option A:** assert that a principled ex-ante scalar value exists (entropy, expected value of
  perfect information, buying price), and use it as the value of the information. This produces a
  single number before content, but it sums two currencies, hides the per-slot signal, and promotes
  a budget filter to a verdict.
- **Option B:** the principled ex-ante object is a **shape and a budget filter**: a per-slot ranking
  of gaps that are large and compressible, with bits and actions priced separately, plus a
  pre-registered refusal. The settled value requires the content and a ground that pays. This
  produces a checkable stop and names the instrument that is owed.

The derivation below lands on **B with a qualified A**: yes, a principled ex-ante quantification
exists, but it is a shape, not the settled value; per slot, not aggregated; two currencies held
apart, not one scalar; a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles the question it reports on.

Three figure-grounded reasons, and one practical one:

1. **A room does not add an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Agreement from one pool is one witness.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). The decision-analysis, health-economics and rational-inattention sources used here partly share one expected-utility pool, and the essays share the framework author with the figures.
3. **Sending the interior spends the reader's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). Stage 1 carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
4. **Legal.** Scraped personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and get the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
