# The Tether's answer on quantifying the value of information before its content is known

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the concrete
number and on which message will arrive. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal
consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A frame must decide whether to seek, buy, design, or skip a message whose content has not arrived.
The error signal it would use, `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`, is indexed per slot, and `o'` is unavailable. The
field asks for a single ex-ante "value of information". Fig 9 :54-57 says this is a **union compound**:
the channel's possible content and the frame's decision rule are two overlapping conditions with an
unassignable shared fraction, not one dial. Each condition has its own anchor and its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **A principled ex-ante rule exists, and it is per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by
   object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 5 :18: the curiosity target *"seeks the gap that is large and
   compressible"*. Fig 5 :21 requires *"the gap has real mass somewhere"*. Formula step 3 gates the
   mint with `SUPPORT |R⁺_s| > 0 for some s`. So before content, a frame can rank slots by live mass
   and compressibility and can run the MDL bargain: Fig 5 :43-44, *"stating it, plus what remains
   unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."*
2. **It is a shape, not a settled value.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
   itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would
   mean already holding it."* The message's identity and its settled value are missing things; the
   rule over held slots is not.
3. **Two currencies, never one scalar.** Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies,
   and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
   Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Shannon's
   bits and Howard's expected utility are the two readings; a single number that sums them is the
   flat-formula collapse of Fig 12 :6-17.
4. **Depth and absence are indiscernible before content.** Fig 6 :27: *"some unreachability is depth
   and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :40-43 issues
   different instructions for the two. Formula step 7 puts SEARCHED first because it is cheapest. The
   field's expected information gain is an expectation over outcomes (Ivanova 2021; Lindley 1956),
   which is the shape; it does not tell you which kind of thing will arrive.
5. **The number is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters
   for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 1 :27:
   *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A VoI
   number becomes a metric only when ranged against an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29).
6. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49, :51: *"An actor's valence is relative to the
   residual, not a property it carries"*; *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of
   residual."* The same message is valuable to one residual and worthless to another. The ranking
   must be re-run each loop, not tabulated once.
7. **Only the ground settles.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays.
   A gate is not the ground."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror
   cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Shannon entropy is the value. Entropy prices the description, one currency only.
- Not that EVPI or EVSI is a settled verdict. They are expectations over outcomes used to choose an
  experiment; they are filters for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that any particular message is valuable. No content has been read and Fig 13 :135 forbids
  naming the missing primitive.
- Not that a standing table of high-value information sources can be published. Fig 11 :51 refuses it.
- Not that agreement among experts settles the number. A shared evidence pool makes agreement the
  pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no source-discriminating instrument was run.
- Not a theory of everything. The canonical README's own section says the framework is not one, and
  Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*
(opened 2026-11-23): a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was *"the spectrometer,
not an argument"*, that resolved it. The corresponding questions here:

- **Currency test:** can the description cost (bits) and the action cost (experiment plus opportunity
  cost) be measured separately for the same slot and shown not to add? ISPOR's ENBS and Howard's
  non-additivity are partial precedents, not a settlement.
- **Discriminator:** can an instrument show whether the missing quantity is in the current closure
  (remedy: independent SEARCHED) or requires an import (remedy: OUTWARD with provenance and shadow
  test)? Until it can, both are live (Fig 6 :27).
- **Per-slot payment:** does the ground actually pay for the slot the ex-ante rule ranks highest, and
  does the ranking fail where the ground does not pay? That is the anchor test (Fig 2 :29).
- **Standing refuters:** a scalar that sums the two currencies invariantly; an ex-ante ranking
  independent of the per-slot residual; a classification of depth versus absence with no instrument;
  an ex-ante number that settles a decision without a ground reading. Any one fires the refuter in
  `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape and a route, then stops at the limit of
what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage (canonical README). Fig 13
:134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is
never a proof of absence."* The concrete numeric value for a concrete decision is a `gap`, reported
as such. What is owed is a discriminator, not a new assumption. The essays all share the framework
author with the figures, so their agreement is one evidence pool, not a witness, and this whole
folder is a simulation, not a ground reading. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single number for the value of information before the message arrives,
> and it does not say no either. It says there is a principled ex-ante rule: rank object slots by live
> residual mass and compressibility, ask whether the missing thing is already in the closure before
> importing anything, and price the answer in two currencies that must not be added, bits for the
> description and actions for finding out whether it holds. That rule is a shape and a filter for
> which experiment to try, not a verdict about what the world will say. The value of a particular
> message stays unnamed until its content arrives and a ground that does not update on our opinion
> pays for it. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in
> advance, and the instrument that would close the gap. The number stays open until the ground
> settles it.
