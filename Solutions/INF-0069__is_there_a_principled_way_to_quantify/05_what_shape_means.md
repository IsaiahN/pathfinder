## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13
:134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding
it."*

*The Library of Babel* (opened directly) gives the operational version:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that
> it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INF-0069 the shape is therefore:

> a per-slot ranking of gaps that carry positive residual mass and are compressible, priced in two
> currencies held apart, with a written refusal and an instrument owed.

It is **not**:

> "the value of this particular message is 7.4 utility units", nor "entropy is the value of
> information".

The shape constrains what any acceptable answer must look like. It does not fill the slot with a
message we have not seen.

## 2. How the shape is obtained before content is known

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you
  narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the closest held vocabulary is
  residual, slot, support, bargain, and two currencies, all available before any message arrives.
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the
  effect rather than the cause."* So do not ask "what is information worth" (borrowed words). Ask
  "what would change in the slot, and what would it cost to find out", which can be answered with
  held terms.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a
  plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
  That is the two-stage offer's stage 1: describe what would count as a failure, not the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the
  starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating
  it."* Here that means: when our vocabulary for the slot is precise enough to state the discriminator,
  the search has composed the shape. That is a stop, not a measurement.

## 3. What licenses a name (and why the message stays unnamed)

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."*
   The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can
   distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A message's settled value is named
   only after its content has arrived and something external has paid for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. A report that a particular instrument has high value in a decision
   can be imported, but only with its provenance and only if it explains a described gap.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new
term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives
does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* An ex-ante value scale is a declared
choice of axes, not nature's own list. Naming a number as *the* value turns a visible choice into an
invisible one.

## 4. The source-discrimination error is the specific failure here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads
green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK,
or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a
projection with lossy information."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"*, that resolved it.
An instrument that cannot distinguish sources *"demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied to INF-0069: a single ex-ante number reads like a measurement of "value", but several
different sources can produce the same reading: bits of description, expected utility, cost of the
experiment, opportunity cost of delay, and prestige of the source. The number cannot comment on
which source produced it. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (the two-currency price
and the ground reading), not a better argument about which number to use. This is Fig 5 :43-44's
bargain applied to the measurement itself.

## 5. Corrections to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

The exemplar and several completed solutions record the same narrowing, and the canonical check here
confirms it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not
a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`.

For this question:

- The ex-ante **rule** (support, large-and-compressible, two currencies, SEARCHED first) is built from
  terms already held: `R`, slot, `φ`, cost, bonds. It is a bound term that accrues a residual, which
  the Formula "Direction" section explicitly permits and requires. The Direction discipline says to
  make the sought-for shape an *"ORDINARY TERM"*, write down what an answer would look like, and let
  it be refusable. That is what the pre-registered refuter does.
- The **identity** of the message that will arrive, and the settled value once it does, are not held.
  Those are the missing things the limit governs. Naming them before content would be naming a
  primitive we do not yet hold (Fig 13 :135).
- The earlier reading "shape, not a name forbids any ex-ante number" is therefore **retracted as
  applied to the ranking rule** and **retained as applied to the message identity and the settled
  value**. Both clauses are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): *"Prestige must
never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action
a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground
Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous decision theorists who agree with the shape
is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim and a
ground that can refuse it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**The value ranking must be re-run per residual.** *How Intelligence Actually Works* (carried) and
Fig 11 :49-51 agree: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"*
and *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A table of high-value
information sources is a snapshot of one residual, not a result. This is why the outreach packet
asks the expert to check the per-slot rule rather than endorse a list.

## 8. One-paragraph canonical reading

For this question a shape is the narrowed form of a value statement that can be made before any
message arrives: which slots carry live residual mass, whether the gap is large and compressible,
what it costs in bits to describe and in actions to settle, and what would refuse the claim. The
framework returns this, not a settled number and not the name of the message, because a missing thing
can only be characterised in effect terms until a ground pays for it. The spectrometer, not an
argument, is what would discriminate among the several sources that can produce the same reading.
The essays agree with the figures because they share an author, so their agreement is one pool, not a
witness. The result is `specified` on the shape and `gap` on the concrete value, and it is a
simulation, not a ground reading.
