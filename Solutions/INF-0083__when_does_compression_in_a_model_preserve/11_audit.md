- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The erasure condition and the preservation condition overlap; a case in both is a compound.
- **Order by dependency.** Per-slot residual first, then the objective, then bond type, then the measured round trip, then ground settlement. Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13/:19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :28-32 (lossy transform with measured gap), Fig 12 :5/:94/:117 (bond as structure), Fig 10 :24-26 (channel decay).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, the method versus recording split, bonds kept, load-bearing terms kept, round trip measured, ratio demoted to a filter.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete compressed model with an aggregate accuracy report and a named rare slot.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an instrument:

- the **per-slot leftover** left(R,phi). Fig 5 writes the bargain over one R, while Fig 1 requires per-slot indexing. The shorthand assumes the per-slot reading carries through; no measurement here confirms it.
- the **bond discriminator**. "The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour" (Fig 12 :117) means the isomer claim needs a behaviour test at a slot. No such test is in the folder.
- the **ground-anchored score**. Fig 1 :27 bars self-scoring, but the folder cannot exhibit an alternative metric that does not move when the model's own scoring changes.

One thing survives cleanly: a lossy compression must report what it gave up by a round trip at named slots, and a pooled accuracy number cannot certify the rare slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no compressed model was read; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
