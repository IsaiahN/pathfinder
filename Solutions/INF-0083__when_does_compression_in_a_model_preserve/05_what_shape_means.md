## 2. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

*The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

Applied here: a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name a safe ratio. "A separator with a measured round trip gap and per-slot live mass" is a shape. "Compress to 8 bits and keep 90 percent of accuracy" is a name, and the framework cannot produce it.

## 3. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." So the residual (a rare slot lost after a transform) is described before the field of model compression is chosen as the place to look.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

For this question the bootstrapping vocabulary is: slot, residual mass, transform, gap, bond, method, anchor. Each narrows the space without supplying the number.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A measured per-slot eval in a particular model is an import from the evaluation frame; it is licensed by provenance, not derived from the figures.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discriminator point. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information", and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." An aggregate accuracy reading cannot comment on which slot was lost. Several compression schemes give the same aggregate reading. The instrument owed is a per-slot ground discriminator, not a better argument about the ratio.

## 5. The specific forbidden error for this question

Naming a safe compression ratio, or a fixed list of "rare" examples, from the framework alone. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A rare case is rare relative to a residual and a ground; when the question changes, the set changes. The figures return the condition under which a case becomes invisible, not the identity of the case.

A second forbidden error is treating the compression ratio as the verdict. Fig 1 :25-27: "coverage, and compression achieved" and "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." The ratio is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60), not a witness for preservation.

## 6. Correction to my own overstatement, kept visible

An earlier pass of this derivation read "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any concrete mechanism in the answer. That was too strong. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It governs a primitive not held in the library. Naming an already-available binding, such as the round trip gap R_T or a typed bond, is a binding of held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework returns the numerical ratio or the identity of a rare case. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This matches the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 7. Consequence for the answer

The Tether answer is a separator with four per-domain verdicts and a declared gap, not a threshold. It says what must be measured and what would refute the claim. It stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."
