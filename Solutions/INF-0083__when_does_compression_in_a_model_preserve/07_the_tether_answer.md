# The Tether's answer: when compression preserves and when it erases

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical ratio. Not `grounded`. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.**

## 1. The residual it answers

A model is compressed and a description is kept. The question asks what decides whether the kept description still carries the structure that matters, and what decides whether the loss fell on the rare case the ground would have paid for. This is a **union compound**, not one dial: at least two conditions with different anchors overlap, and a case in both is a specific compound, not a harder common case (Fig 9 :55, :57).

## 2. The separator, per domain

### D1, the compression transform

Compression is a lossy cross-scale transform. Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." The honest report of the loss is the measured round trip gap R_T, not the ratio. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 and Symbols table: R_T).

### D2, the representation

Structure is the bonded arrangement, not the ingredient list. Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." A compression preserves structure when it keeps the typed bonds and collapses only spellings that a symmetric bond makes equivalent (Fig 12 :92-93). It erases when it flattens an ordered bond, drops the bond, or removes a load-bearing primitive (Fig 13 :48: "And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."). Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 + Fig 13 + Operators table), with the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim recorded as live.

### D3, the residual and the channel

A rare case is a live per-slot residual. Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients," and "saturated metrics, pooled readings". Erasure happens when the channel loses the transient and the metric pools it, while the ground stays exactly where it was. Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 + Fig 10), with the world source on quantization as low-overlap corroboration of shape.

### D4, the score

The compression ratio is not a witness. Fig 1 :25-27: "coverage, and compression achieved" and "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The ratio can decide what gets tried; it cannot decide whether the rare case survived. Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 + Fig 9).

### The union, not an average

Preservation and erasure are kept as separate verdicts per domain. They are not averaged into a quality score, because their anchors differ: bits for the description, a still-running derivation for the representation, a live slot for the residual, and ground payment for the score. Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a safe compression percentage, layer count, or rarity threshold. That is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). Unreachable is a verdict, not silence.
- Not that compression is bad. Pruning and quantization can leave a trainable subnetwork or an aggregate-equivalent model (Frankle and Carbin 2019; Han et al. 2016). What those reports certify is limited by the pooling that produced them.
- Not that a particular example is "the rare case". Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51), so the set changes with the question.
- Not that an aggregate accuracy drop is required for erasure. The channel can do the damage with unchanged aggregate accuracy (Fig 10 :24-28).
- Not grounded. No compressed model was measured and no slot was read.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A per-slot ground reading, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Owed here:

1. **Round trip instrument.** Measure R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)) at named slots, not as a scalar over the whole model. If R_T is zero at every slot of interest, the round trip requirement is satisfied.
2. **Per-slot live mass.** Measure the residual at the rare slot before and after compression. A global near-zero with one live slot is a legal state, so the aggregate cannot substitute.
3. **Bond discriminator.** Show whether the typed arrangement survived, by a behaviour test at the slot that distinguishes two isomers over the same retained atoms. Behaviour change at that slot means the bond was load-bearing.
4. **Derivation test.** Check that an established derivation still goes through after the primitive set shrinks. If it does not, a load-bearing term was removed.
5. **Standing falsifiers:** a measured method-crossing compression that still erases a paid-for rare case; a ground-anchored compression metric immune to the model's own scoring; a single scalar that prices both currencies without loss; a figure line naming a universal ratio.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The per-slot instrument and the bond discriminator do not exist in the material read for this solution. The framework returns the shape of the separator and the refuters, then stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a safe compression ratio. It gives a separator. Compression preserves useful structure when what crosses the scale is a reapplicable method rather than a recording of one occasion, when the typed bonds survive rather than just the parameter list, when load-bearing terms still carry an established derivation, and when the round trip gap is measured at named slots. It erases rare cases when the residual is aggregated across slots, when readings are pooled or transients dropped, when a metric saturates, and when the compressed model scores itself with the ratio it produced. The compression ratio is a filter for the budget, not a witness for preservation. Preservation and erasure are two conditions with different anchors, so their verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The framework returns that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the per-slot instrument that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
