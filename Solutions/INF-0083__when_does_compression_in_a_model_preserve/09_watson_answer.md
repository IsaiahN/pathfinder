## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a model compression. Option A: there is a safe compression ratio that preserves useful structure and only unusually bad compression erases rare cases. Option B: preservation and erasure are two conditions with different anchors, and what decides them is per-slot live mass, the typed arrangement that crosses the scale, a still-running derivation, and a measured round trip gap. What each leads to: A promotes a frame-internal quantity to a verdict and hides the rare slot inside an average; B yields a separator, per-slot verdicts kept apart, five written refuters, and a declared gap where no figure returns a number.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the compressed description predicts at a slot and what the ground returns there. Arity: slot-indexed, not global. Symmetry: the transform is asymmetric and non-invertible by inspection, so the loss must be measured by a round trip. Scale: cross-scale, between a finer and a coarser description. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 4 :28 "Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought back down will not match what was sent."
- **Domain 1, the compression transform.** Actors: finer model x, coarser T_A(x), concretisation T_E, named slots. Currency: round trip gap R_T in bits and settlement actions. Anchor: the ground at the slot. Question type: cross-scale transform, what the coarser description cannot hold.
- **Domain 2, the representation.** Actors: retained primitives, the seven bonds, negation. Currency: description length in bits. Anchor: an established derivation that still goes through after pruning (Fig 13 :49 "Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard. Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none."). Question type: partition of what is kept, atoms, bonds, or both.
- **Domain 3, the residual and the channel.** Actors: object slots, live mass at a slot, rare cases. Currency: per-slot residual mass. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Question type: union of conditions, with the rare case as a live slot rather than a fixed example class.
- **Domain 4, the score.** Actors: the evaluator of the compression, the compressed model. Currency: compression achieved, frame-internal. Anchor: borrowed from domains 1 and 3 in this simulated run, declared rather than hidden. Question type: verdict versus budget filter.
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." A rare case that is also structurally decisive is a compound with its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the residual per slot; name what would count as progress; identify the bond type held; run the transform and measure R_T; settle at the ground per slot. Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Strain test applied: Fig 4 governs the cross-scale loss while Fig 12 governs what a term must carry to be itself. Different objects, so no contradiction after re-census. One live price question remains: left(R,phi) must be scored per slot although Fig 5 writes the bargain over one R. Recorded, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator survives; a universal safe ratio does not. Compression preserves structure when a reapplicable method crosses, the typed bonds survive, load-bearing terms keep an established derivation running, and the round trip gap is measured at named slots with the leftover paid there. It erases rare cases when the residual is aggregated, readings are pooled or transients dropped, a metric saturates, and the compressed model scores itself with the ratio it produced. The ratio is a budget filter, never a witness. Preservation and erasure are two conditions with different anchors, so the verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. No figure returns a ratio, a layer count, or a rarity threshold; that is a declared `gap`. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single figure point is Fig 1 :27: a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an aggregate success story instead of a per-slot generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregate framing and the self-score are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no framework primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument showing R_T exactly zero at every slot of interest and a behaviour test showing the bond survived would move the preservation half toward `grounded`; a ground reading of a rare slot unchanged after pooling, or a ground-anchored metric immune to the model's own scoring, would break the erasure half; a figure re-census that returns a universal ratio would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
