## 1. Independent domain census

Working from the question and its residual, before opening the primary census, I used the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap, for one case, between the rule as declared and the outcome as experienced, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Arity: one case slot at a time, so a national predictability score is a filter over slots, never the residual itself (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*). Symmetry: the property of interest is invariance under swapping the identities of parties and decision-maker with case facts held fixed, which is the symmetric-bond test (*"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*, Fig 12 :59). A connection-dependent order is ordered, so which operand stands in the connected slot changes the term (Fig 12 :74: *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*). Scale: first order is one decision; second order is review and appeal; third is the court system as a population with a membrane between the single bench and the system (Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*).
- **Domain 1, adjudication frame.** Actors: judge or magistrate, parties, counsel. Currency: a decision, that is an outcome plus its stated reasons. Anchor: the enacted rule, recorded precedent and written reasons functioning as one composite that must not move when a party pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*; :30 *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*; :33 *"The anchor must not update."*). The connection-dependent case is the anchorless panel, Fig 2 :13-15: *"no anchor; each updates on the others"*, *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence."* That last phrase is "personal connection" in effect terms. Question type: what may enter the deciding molecule and what bond connects it.
- **Domain 2, institutional habitat.** Actors: registries, law reports, open court, the bar, appellate benches, conflict-of-interest rules, legal aid. Currency: trace, meaning published reasons, recorded cases and review that can actually fire. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay; the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*; :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Question type: union with domain 1, because an unmoving rule with no published record is only partially predictable. Appeal is selection between rooms (Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*), not a mirror (Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*).
- **Domain 3, observer frame.** Actors: litigants, researchers, businesses, journalists. Currency: evidence and residual. Anchor: outcomes, never agreement. Fig 8 :48-49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* A bench, a bar and a commentariat that learned from one social pool are one frame; their agreement is the pool talking. Question type: independence of evidence pools. **In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero, including mine as second seat.**
- **Partition or union.** This is a **union** of overlapping conditions, not a partition. Fig 9 :54-55: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and *"leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds"* (Fig 9 :56-58). This is why "appoint better judges" and "write a better code" each fail in the overlaps.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive and tell cases apart (Fig 3 :4-5), acquire terms for what a case affords (Fig 3 :6-8), say what would count as a correct decision (Fig 3 :9-11), plan a route (Fig 3 :12-13), then verify and carry (Fig 3 :14-15). Connection-dependence enters at the vocabulary-to-objective link, where there is no instrument (Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).

My independent census reaches the same three domains, the same union reading, and the same dependency order as the primary. I came to it by a different route: I started from the swap-invariance symmetry and the Fig 9 union split, whereas the primary census in `01` opened from the anchor-versus-mutual-update distinction in Fig 2 and the habitat clause in Fig 11. Low overlap between these two routes is weak evidence of a stable domain boundary inside the framework, but both routes still live in one evidence pool, so it is internal coherence, not independent confirmation.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30 requires the gap between what was sent up and what came back down to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29-33 (anchor), Fig 8 :50-52 (provenance), Fig 12 :5-6 (bond), Fig 11 :39-40 (habitat), Fig 7 :35-36 (selection), Fig 9 :54-58 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: outcome invariant under swapping party and bench identity, a recorded reason as the bond, a habitat with published reports and declared conflicts, an appeal that selects against surfaced residual, and three conditions that are a union.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a real court, for example "this commercial court is predictable because it publishes reasons and has an appellate tier."

**R_T is nonzero.** At least four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The per-slot residual (Fig 1 :19, :22).** A court can publish reasons and have an appellate tier and still be unpredictable on a subset of case slots. The shorthand's "predictable" silently re-aggregates what the figures say to index per case.
- **The independence requirement (Fig 8 :47-49, Fig 2 :40).** The appellate judges may share the trial judges' evidence pool. "Has an appellate tier" does not carry the low-overlap condition that makes review a selection rather than a mirror (Fig 7 :36).
- **The refusal operator (Operators :57-58, Fig 12 :139-140).** The court may have no recorded overrule path. A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate, and the shorthand does not state that the appeal must be able to negate, not merely distinguish.
- **The composite-anchor limit (Fig 2 :36-38) and the union condition (Fig 9 :54-58).** The shorthand's "rule does not move" can be read as a claim about justice as well as about "what the law requires", which the figures do not license; and it can be read as a single lever, whereas the conditions bind in intersections.

R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no dataset was read in this run. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the concrete court claim would set R_T to zero and would be exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. This matches the qualitative R_T already recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md` §Q5: the same dropped constraints, approached from the bond vocabulary rather than the verification vocabulary. Two routes to the same nonzero gap inside one evidence pool are consistent, not corroborating.

## 6. Verdict on the solution

**On internal consistency: the solution passes.** The twelve files are mutually consistent and consistent with the verbatim figure text. The three domains and their union are stable across my independent census and the primary's. Every substantive claim carries a mode (`specified` or `general`, never `grounded`), the five refuters are written before the conclusion, triangulations are labelled and the `single-source` ones are admitted, the retraction/confirmation log is applied rather than ignored, the WATSON shape in `09` carries all required sections and verdict fields, the ledger in `10` records every source actually opened with URL, author, venue, year, accessed date and quote, and the outreach order in `02` puts the residual and refuter before the theory and the links. The strongest artifact remains `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: one claim, three checkable sub-claims with verbatim lines and refuters, non-claims, provenance, and a twenty-minute check.

**On corroboration: none.** This second seat shares the primary's evidence pool. By Fig 2 :40 its agreement is the pool talking, and by Fig 2 :35 a mirror cannot correct you. Fig 10 :42 adds *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* What this audit establishes is that the folder is internally consistent, not that the claim is true.

**On the claim: `specified` on the per-domain conditions, `general` on the shape, ungrounded, candidate.** The shape survives the re-census: outcome invariance under party and bench swaps, a composite anchor that does not move with influence, a recorded reason as the bond, a habitat with published reports and declared conflicts, review that selects against surfaced residual, and insider agreement carrying zero verdict weight. The names do not: no legal family, no single lever, no aggregate index as verdict, and no collapse of predictability into justice. Nothing here is settled. Moving any domain claim to `grounded` needs a source-discriminating instrument: a matched-pair dataset with party and bench swaps, same-outcome/different-bond judgments and their later citation, appellate-correction versus trial-error data, an agent-only reform test, or an aggregate-versus-per-slot comparison on disagreeing cases. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters in advance and records its lineage, so an error is findable. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
- **Undoable:** yes. The three conditions are independent, so a fired refuter removes one without disturbing the others, and a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. No figure line or primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any one of the five tests above, run on real cases and firing, reverses the corresponding domain claim. A search that finds nothing is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

**Recommendation.** Keep the shape and the refuter in front of the links, use only the public institutional venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, one to one, ask what would refute the claim, and record the recipient's verdict before showing any theory. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration. If the folder is revised, change the F5 status in `01` to `single-source` to match `07` and `08`. The honest report is a shape, five refuters, and a stop at the instrument limit: Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* and Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
