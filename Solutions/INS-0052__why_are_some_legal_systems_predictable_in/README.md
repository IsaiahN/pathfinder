## What is being decided, in effect terms

Two options are compared. Option A treats predictability as a property of a legal family, a constitution, or the virtue of individual judges. Option B treats predictability as a shape that three separable conditions must jointly satisfy: a decision anchored to something that does not move with influence, a habitat that keeps that anchor reachable through recorded reasons and independent review, and an observer who does not mistake agreement inside one social pool for evidence. Each option leads somewhere different. A spends the independence of the people asked to check the claim. B offers a per-case shape, refuters written in advance, and a clear statement of what the figures do not decide (in particular, justice).

The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's "Direction": an answer would name the condition that makes an outcome invariant to who decides and whom they know, while leaving room for reasoned judgment. That binding is stated in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 and is refusable by the pre-registered refuter in section 4.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no receptiveness score used as evidence.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. Asking for trust before checking spends independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The only channels listed here are public institutional ones, and every contact is one to one.
