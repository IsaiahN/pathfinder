# What "shape" means for legal predictability: canonical reading from the eight essays

All eight essays were opened on 2026-11-23. They are by Isaiah N and are listed with URLs in `04_links_and_distribution.md` and with quotes in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The figures remain primary; the essays are the philosophical grounding for the word "shape".

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form an answer must have, stateable before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `outcome invariant under swapping party and bench identity` is a shape.
- `common law` is a name.
- `a composite anchor of rule, record and reasons` is still a shape, because it specifies the role and the invariance, not a particular code or court.
- `the World Justice Project score above 0.8` is a threshold on a filter, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here, the effect vocabulary was gathered before any figure line was chosen: invariance under swaps, reasons as a checkable trace, review that fires, independence of evidence pools. Only then were figure lines quoted. Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A matched-pair study on real cases would settle whether outcomes are invariant; the derivation cannot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "judicial independence" as the cause would be an import from legal institutional theory, and would need its provenance recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the warning: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The three conditions derived in `01` are a visible choice of where to look, not nature's own list of what makes courts predictable.

## 4. Metamerism: why the outcome cannot tell you the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is the exact error the derivation refuses in F3. A ruling that followed the rule and a ruling that rewarded a connection can present the same outcome. Reading the source off the outcome is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, namely the written reasons, not a name assigned from the projection.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the same point as provenance loss: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* An outcome is the frame. The reason is the record of which.

## 5. Agreement is not a check, and prestige is not action

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* Applied here, a bench, a bar and a commentariat that all learned from the same pool are one frame. Their consensus on what "everyone knows" about a court is not evidence of predictability.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A well known judge endorsing the shape claim gives it a budget to be tried. It does not make the claim true. The same essay states the limit of this whole folder: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 6. Q1: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a legal family?

No, and the reason is precise. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in the frame. "Common law" and "civil law" are already held in the legal frame. Choosing one is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is claiming the derivation produced the choice, or treating the binding as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world"*; Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). The name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such. Triangulated: README plus Fig 9 :32 plus Fig 13 :134.

## 7. Q2: Is my own audit a ground reading?

No. Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The output is *"simulacra"*. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of anything in a court, and it cannot settle the question it reports on. State `specified` or `general` on every claim and never `grounded`.

## 8. Correction to my own section 4

I initially wrote that published reasons *discriminate the source* of a decision. That is too strong. Reasons are themselves a projection and can be post hoc, as the legal realists noted and the "Legal realism" page records. The defensible claim is the weaker one now in F3: reasons are the **bond that is recorded**, and without a recorded bond the judgment cannot be used as a precedent whose route is known. Whether a recorded reason is truthful is a separate question with a separate ground. Both statements are kept here so neither is quietly lost.
