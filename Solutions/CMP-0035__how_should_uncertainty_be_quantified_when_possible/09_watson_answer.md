## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about quantifying uncertainty when outcomes depend on a poorly known mechanism as well as random variation. Option A: assign one number (a variance, an entropy, a single interval) to the total spread and recompose the parts additively. Option B: unbundle the compound and report a structured object with separate fields, a structural refusal, an independence audit and a ground score. What each leads to: A gives the consumer a number that cannot distinguish "collect more data" from "change the model class" from "hedge," and restores the conflation after a cosmetic decomposition; B gives the consumer a discriminating report, five refuters written in advance, and an honest marker where the ground has not paid.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any single scalar, additive recombination and completeness claim do not. The question is a union compound, so it must be unbundled before it is searched. An acceptable report is per slot; separates reducible spread from irreducible spread; marks structural absence with a refusal rather than a wider interval; distinguishes amplified residual (chaos) from discarded detail (noise); audits the independence of the evidence behind candidates; and is scored against materialised outcomes with mode and cost if wrong. The fields are not summed, because bits and actions are different units. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with one single-source figure line (chaos/noise) and one single-source application (isomer reading), both marked. The specific estimator is a gap, recorded plainly: the framework returns a shape, not a name. Unreachable here is a verdict, not a silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the refuters are used. The packet states the mode, the five refuters and the provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is operational: if the structural marker is added to the other fields, the consumer is told to hedge when they should change the model class, which is the original defect restored. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The conclusion is a report form, not a primitive. No term has to be withdrawn to replace a bad estimator, and the pre-registered refuters are removable independently. A published report can be superseded by a corrected form. If the flat-residual diagnostic fires the saturation refuter, the remedy is INWARD (repair the instrument), not a new atom, so the change is local.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A held-out, properly scored forecast comparison showing that the five-field report does not beat the best single predictive distribution would fire refuter 2; a demonstration that the self-reported uncertainty is sufficient for its own calibration error would fire refuter 4; an instrument that separates parameter ignorance from model-class absence would settle the structural field; a pre-registered exchange rate between bits and actions that survives ground scoring would narrow the no-summing verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
