# What "shape" means for CMP-0035, from the eight essays

All eight essays by Isaiah N were opened directly on 2026-11-22 and are listed with quotes in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The canonical usage rule is `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, which says the figures outrank every summary and return "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."

## 1. A shape is a constraint before it is a filler

The framework returns a narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the specific estimator is named. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." The figures README puts the limit plainly: a shape is "a shape, not a name."

From *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is the five-field report in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0: per slot, two separated spreads, a structural marker, an independence audit, and a ground score with a mode. The specific estimator that fills field 1 or field 2 is a name. The figures do not supply it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: the near vocabulary is the UQ field's own aleatoric/epistemic language. It is a starting term, not the answer, because it misses the chaos/noise split and the structural-absence case.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." We described the residual by its effect (a number that cannot tell the reader which action is owed), not by a cause ("wrong prior").
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is earlier: the shape is stated, the refuters are pre-registered, and the specific estimator is explicitly owed rather than invented.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For UQ, that means a pre-registered, held-out, properly scored forecast comparison. Until such a test fires, "this estimator is the right one" is a candidate, not an accepted term.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). An estimator borrowed from, say, reliability engineering must carry its source gate and shadow test. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* warns that outputs are not generators: "The corpus contains the problems evolution already solved and someone wrote down. It does not contain the capacity to solve new ones, because that capacity lives in the living process, the mind generating, not in the text it generated." So importing a published estimator is inheriting an output. The generator is the derivation and its refuter.

The same essay adds the representation limit: "A random walk finds the unconceived only if the unconceived is expressible in the primitives the walker happens to hold." A mechanism outside the modeller's primitives is not just poorly estimated; it is unrepresentable until a new axis is imported. That is the deepest version of F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. Why self-reported uncertainty is not a ground reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Metamerism: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." One wide interval can be produced by random variation, by parameter ignorance, or by a wrong mechanism class. The reading does not determine the source. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

For UQ, the corresponding instrument is a discriminating experiment or a held-out score that separates the sources. Until one exists, naming the source of a wide interval from the interval alone is the metamerism error. This is the world-side reason behind Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "You may all agree and be wrong." That is why the packet asks for a refutation first.

## 5. Two consequences for this question

### 5a. The shape is not the aleatoric/epistemic split

The near-miss matters. A reader will expect the answer "report aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty separately." That is a good start but not the figure-derived shape. It misses:

- per-slot indexing (Fig 1 :19, Symbols table :43);
- the two currencies (Fig 12 :125-130), so a decomposition that is summed back into one number fails;
- chaos versus noise inside "aleatoric" (Fig 13 :31);
- the structural-absence case, which is not a distribution at all (Fig 6 :27, Fig 8 :32);
- the independence audit (Fig 2 :31);
- ground scoring and stated mode (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 and mode rule).

So the shape is a *structured report form*, not a two-column table.

### 5b. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially wrote that the figures forbid naming any source of uncertainty because the framework "returns a shape, not a name," and that any named component would be an illegitimate import. **That was overstated and is retracted.**

The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Names for quantities already in the frame's library are bindings, not new primitives. Aleatoric variation, model error and parameter uncertainty are all already in the UQ library. What the figures forbid is (a) inventing a new primitive to plug a slot, (b) claiming a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here), and (c) naming the source of a reading where several sources would give the same reading (the metamerism error in section 4).

My metamerism point stands: a wide interval does not determine its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held quantities. Both the retraction and the standing claim are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figure text. They are **not** a test of any uncertainty quantification method and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From the same essay: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous UQ researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the report form against a ground, and a recorded refutation or survival. That is what `02_outreach_email.md` does.
