## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Carroll 2014 moves the fine tuning into the measure, not the horizon or flatness.** His claim that *"the fraction of cosmological histories that were smooth at early times is incredibly tiny"* is exactly the comparison class that F3 says has no anchor. This corroborates the `gap` in effect terms, and it also warns that a "measure" is being used as if it were an instrument when it is currently a choice. Cite this when explaining why the framework refuses to name the comparison.
2. **Carroll and Chen 2004 is a real differing room, so it must not be dismissed as a restatement.** Their de Sitter mechanism is *"statistically time-symmetric on ultra-large scales"*, which means the local arrow is bought from a vacuum regime rather than posited as a boundary. That is progress in Fig 7's sense, because the room differs and matches the residual. It is not a last room, because the vacuum regime is prior and its own status is unregistered. This is the honest reason F2 says "no last room" and does not say "no mechanism."

The physics sources in this section and in `09_watson_answer.md` are secondary. They corroborate or conflict in effect terms; they never overrule a figure verdict. A conflict sends the derivation back to the census (F5 is the recorded case).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the check has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people already sympathetic to fine tuning produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 6 :29-30** - *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."* versus *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The first message must be in effect terms.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what they would count as an anchor for the measure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
