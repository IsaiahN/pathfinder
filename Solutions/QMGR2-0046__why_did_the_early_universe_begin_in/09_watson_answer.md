## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: entropy is a derivative of the six, so the low entropy initial state is a particular per-mode arrangement at the earliest registered turn, not a primitive and not one averaged cosmic number. The "why" chain is a sequence of differing rooms with no last room; inflation, a bounce and a de Sitter fluctuation are candidates to be checked, not terminations. The comparison "unusually" needs a measure over histories that no instrument currently returns, so that sub-question is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Candidate mechanisms form a functional group, not an isomer relation. The framework returns a shape and does not name the mechanism. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for its application, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode labeled, states four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter. If the derivative claim is wrong, the cost is a misdirected search for a measure when the actual defect is in the substrate table.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. Fig 6 :42 records that the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted."* The shape and its four falsifiers are in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). A superseding packet can narrow the claim without withdrawing any primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An observation that discriminates between measures over histories would give X1 an anchor and convert the `gap` into a domain; a model that yields our low entropy past from a measure-generic state with no special boundary and no further unexplained prior regime would break the no-last-room verdict; a derivation of entropy as a seventh primitive, or a second law in a system with none of the six, would break the derivative claim; a per-mode residual that averages to zero while the global claim stays well posed would break the slot indexing.
