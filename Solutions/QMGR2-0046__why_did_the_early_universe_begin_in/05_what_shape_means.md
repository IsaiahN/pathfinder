## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"a generator of the shape of an answer at any level"* and can say *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish. Fig 9 :32: *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a per-slot derivative of the six at the earliest registered turn, embedded in a chain of differing rooms with an open first slot, plus a declared `gap` for the measure that makes "unusually" meaningful.
- **Name:** "inflation did it", "a bounce did it", "a de Sitter fluctuation did it". Each is a binding from the cosmology frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for *"new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms"*. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the effect description is: whichever arrangement occupies the first slot must be low entropy per mode, must be reachable from a prior regime that does not itself require a further special condition, and must be ranked by a measure that some instrument can return. That is a shape with three checkable joints. It is not a cosmological model.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 names settling as the ground's work: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming a cosmological mechanism as the answer to the first slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the measure problem is a source-discrimination failure, not an argument failure

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, whether one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The passage states: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Here the reading is "the early universe was smooth", and the possible sources are inflation, a bounce, a fluctuation, or something not yet conceived. The CMB reading does not discriminate among them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument or signature, not a further argument about which measure is elegant.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So the simulated derivation in `01` cannot validate its own shape claim.

## 5. Q1 - Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming inflation? ANSWERED

**What licenses a particular mechanism in the first slot?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement signature that discriminates among sources, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the framework forbid the word "entropy"?** No. See the correction in section 5b. Entropy is not a missing primitive; it is a derivative of the six. The limit applies to the mechanism that filled the first slot, not to the derivative that describes the state.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**No last room.** *How Intelligence Actually Works* states: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* The same essay shows why an explanatory chain has no conventional infinite regress: *"This is also why there is no infinite regress, or hierarchy in the conventional sense: these chains do not loop around on themselves, but merely spread out, diverging further as they are connected."* Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room."* The answer shape therefore cannot be a final mechanism. It is a route that approaches while the ground keeps refusing.

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous cosmologists who agree that fine tuning is real is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try a source-discriminating check.
