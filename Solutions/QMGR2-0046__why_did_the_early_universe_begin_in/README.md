## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that the early universe was fine-tuned.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A consensus that the past hypothesis is needed is a shared reading of the same CMB data, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7.
