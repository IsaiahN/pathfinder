**Subject:** One checkable output on the early universe's low entropy, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation of the early universe's low entropy. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` structural, `specified` in application, not `grounded`):** entropy is not a primitive in this framework. It is a derivative of six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), which compose only across kinds. Counting configurations needs the network term, finite counting needs energy, a one-way flow needs a gradient, and a remainder after each step needs time. So the low entropy initial state is not an extra substance that arrived; it is a particular arrangement of those six at the earliest turn we can register, indexed by mode and region rather than collapsed into one global number. The derivation marks that arrangement as open. It does not name a mechanism.
>
> **What this does not say:** it does not say inflation is false, it does not say a bounce is impossible, and it does not say the CMB result is in doubt. Concrete mechanisms are treated as different rooms in a chain, each of which must differ from the room before it and match the residual it was summoned by. The framework's own rule is that there is no last room: a chain of explanations approaches without arriving. So the question "why low entropy" is offered as a chain with an open first slot, not as a final mechanism.
>
> **What is still owed:** the word "unusually" compares the early state against a measure over cosmological histories. No instrument currently returns that measure. I record that as a `gap`, not an answer, and I would like to know what you would count as an anchor for it.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. deriving a seventh primitive that is entropy itself, or showing a system with none of the six still has a second law;
> 2. a model that yields our low entropy past from a measure-generic initial state, with no special boundary and no further unexplained prior regime;
> 3. an observation that discriminates between measures over histories, which would give the comparison an anchor;
> 4. a per-mode entropy residual that averages to zero while the global claim stays unchanged.
>
> I wrote these down before running the derivation. If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
