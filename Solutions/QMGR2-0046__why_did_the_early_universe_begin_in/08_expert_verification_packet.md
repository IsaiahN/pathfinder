The "why" chain is a sequence of differing rooms with no last room, so inflation, a bounce and a de Sitter fluctuation are candidates to be checked rather than terminations. Competing mechanisms share a functional group, not an isomer relation. The framework does not name the mechanism that filled the first slot: it returns a shape, not a name.
