## What the wider literature bears on the question

Three independent findings converge on the figure-derived shape, without overruling it:

1. **Bonds, not boxes.** IPCC Chapter 9 and the Critical Zone measurement catalogue both locate the limiting uncertainty in feedbacks and interface fluxes. This is F1 in world terms.
2. **Instruments already returning something.** Pritchard shows new snowfall and ice-thickness methods built on biased pluviometers and existing surveys. This is the INWARD branch of F5. It is a direct instance of Fig 6 :32, not a merely similar story.
3. **Third-order residuals.** The DDT material (Stanford, US EPA, and Fig 11 itself) shows that the observation that mattered was several steps from the first-framed cause and involved a change of mechanism, not a finer reading of the same one. This is F4.

Overlap is declared rather than hidden: GCOS and WMO are one programme family, the three essays share an author with the figures, and the IPCC Chapter 9 material is an assessment that cites GCOS. Agreement among them is corroboration of the shape at low cost, not a ground reading of this derivation.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 -** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 -** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 -** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40 -** agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Seat Map :163 -** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
