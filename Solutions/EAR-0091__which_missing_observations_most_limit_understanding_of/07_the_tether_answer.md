# The Tether's answer: which missing observations most limit understanding of Earth-system interactions?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which missing observations most limit understanding of interactions among atmosphere, ocean, ice, land and ecosystems. Stated in effect terms: an observing system predicts the state of a coupled slot, the world returns an observation, and R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is measured per slot. The question is a request to characterise R where it has live mass. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

## 2. The shape

1. **The limiting observations are at the bonds, not inside the five boxes.** Fig 12 :5: **"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** An inventory of atmosphere, ocean, ice, land and ecosystems records the ingredients and not the couplings. Air-sea fluxes, ice-ocean freshwater, land-atmosphere water and energy, and ecosystem state transitions are the observations that bear on interactions.

2. **The residual is a union compound, not five separate gaps.** Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Observing each compartment in isolation clears the single-condition regions and leaves the intersections standing. What remains is categorically different: a specific compound at the interface.

3. **Enumerate the habitat by contact cascade.** Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."** Fig 11 :52-53: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** The limiting observation may be second or third order in contact distance.

4. **Triage each slot by three distinct reachability questions.** SEARCHED is asked first (Formula step 7), because it is cheapest and the other two assume its answer. INWARD asks whether a proto-instrument is already returning something that fails to resolve: Fig 6 :34, **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."** OUTWARD asks whether another frame holds the observation, with a source gate and a shadow test. The two are opposed and never read against one another (Fig 6 :31).

5. **Rank by persistent, flat, live residual mass, and treat the rank as a budget filter.** Fig 5 :18: **"seeks the gap that is large and compressible"** (the curiosity drive). Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** "Most limiting" means large live mass at that slot, persistent and not trending down, and tractable by an instrument already returning something. The rank decides what is tried first. It does not decide what is true.

6. **Buy independence across genuinely different instruments.** Fig 2 :31: **"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."** Satellite, in-situ, archive and local-knowledge frames count separately only when their evidence pools differ. Two satellite products sharing a retrieval are one witness.

7. **The framework does not name the single winner.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** The output is a ranked set of slots with reachability classes and closing instruments. Candidate slots from the literature (ocean interior and sub-canopy variables, snowfall water content, glacier ice volume, interface fluxes, geographical gaps) are provenanced imports, not derived names.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single "most important observation." The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that more satellite resolution closes the gap. Independence requires frames different from the satellite closure.
- Not that model disagreement identifies the priority. Model spread is a residual; an instrument that resolves the coupling is the anchor (Fig 2 :29).
- Not that a search that finds nothing proves absence. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation closed a slot.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A measurement programme at the interfaces, not another argument. Concretely:

- An instrument that measures snowfall water content and glacier ice volume at representative sites (Pritchard, 2021 describes methods already returning something), so the Greenland surface-mass-balance spread can be culled at the ground.
- In-situ observations beneath the ocean surface and within canopies, where WMO iClimateAction says satellites cannot adequately capture the needed variables (2026).
- Interface flux measurements across bedrock-soil, soil-atmosphere, soil-vegetation and vegetation-atmosphere (CZO common measurements).
- Re-analysis of held archives and digitisation of unprocessed records before buying new instruments (SEARCHED first).
- Local and Indigenous knowledge treated as a frame with a different closure, imported with provenance and a shadow test.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5: a compartment-only schedule closes the interaction residual; a global scalar preserves a live slot averaging would erase; a sensor for something never registered is built from a description alone; agreement in a shared evidence pool settles the rank; the framework names the winner without an import.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The priority list is a filter for the budget; the verdict waits for instruments. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."** The record stops at the limit of what current instruments distinguish, and the lineage of that stop is recorded so it is checkable. Candidate slots are marked as imports. What the framework cannot do is rank them numerically without a ground reading. That gap is reported, not filled with a plausible name.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single most important missing observation, and it does not claim to settle Earth-system observation priorities. It says that the observations limiting understanding of interactions are at the interfaces between atmosphere, ocean, ice, land and ecosystems, not inside those five boxes; that the residual must be ranked per slot rather than averaged into one global score; that the habitat of a limiting observation must be enumerated outward through the contact cascade, because the effect that matters can be three steps from the first-framed cause; and that each slot owes three different questions, already-held-and-unfound, instrument-too-coarse, or held-in-another-frame. The resulting priority order is a filter for the budget, not a verdict; only an instrument that resolves the coupling settles it. The framework offers the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ranking stays open until the ground pays.
