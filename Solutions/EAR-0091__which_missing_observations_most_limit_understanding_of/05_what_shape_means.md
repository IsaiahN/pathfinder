## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a ranked set of interface slots with their reachability classes and the instruments that would close them" is a shape. "Ocean heat content below 2000 m is the single most limiting observation" is a name. The first is derivable; the second is an import that must be provenanced.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay's own version of Fig 9's union/partition split and it governs this question directly: five compartments plus their interactions are a bundle until unbundled.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email gives a checkable shape, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: an instrument that resolves an interface flux, closing a slot's residual.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. GCOS, Pritchard, IPCC and the CZO measurement catalogue are such frames. Their candidate slots enter the answer as imports with provenance, not as derivations.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The five compartments in the question are a declared choice of vocabulary, not nature's own list. Treating atmosphere, ocean, ice, land and ecosystems as the atoms of the problem turns a visible choice into an invisible one. Fig 12 :5 is the same point: *"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

## 4. The source/provenance rule for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a wide model spread over Greenland surface mass balance is a reading. It cannot say which source (snowfall input, albedo, melt physics, boundary conditions) produced it. Naming snowfall water content as the culprit from the spread alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that measures snowfall water content, which is exactly what Pritchard (2021) describes. The same essay's distinction horizon and union horizon are relevant: a slot can be *"below the union horizon"* for the current community, and that is a statement about the current state of instruments, not a settled verdict.

## 5. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let an instrument settle it, and record failures. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the same move operational: votes come from outcomes, not popularity, and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22) is its own rule for keeping per-slot rates.

**A room does not upgrade the label.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: a group is only worth having while its members stay different; ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times. This is the reason `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains submission venues across remote sensing, geosciences, critical-zone observation and Earth-system modelling rather than a list of like-minded recipients, and why no receptiveness ranking exists anywhere in this folder.

## 6. Capability is agent-and-habitat, not instrument alone

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Contact is Distinction Resolver"* and *"the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted."* From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, quoting Fig 11: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

For this question, an observing system is not made more capable by a better sensor in isolation. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Adding resolution to a satellite that cannot see beneath a canopy (WMO iClimateAction, 2026) improves the instrument without changing contact with the sub-canopy residual. The remedy is an additional frame with a different closure: in-situ, field, archive or local knowledge. That is F7 in essay terms.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum. *Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter: the outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The five refuters in section 5 of `01` exist to make that limitation checkable by someone outside this frame.

## 8. Shape reading for EAR-0091, stated in one place

- **Shape:** a per-slot ranking of interface couplings, with live residual mass, reachability class and a named instrument that would close each slot.
- **Not a name:** no single "most limiting observation" can be derived.
- **How obtained:** describe R in effect terms (bonds and cascades), enumerate the habitat, triage SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD.
- **What licenses a name:** an instrument that resolves the coupling, or a provenanced import with a shadow test.
- **What would settle it:** the measurement programme itself, not more agreement.
