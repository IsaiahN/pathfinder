## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific named winner is not derivable. The observations that most limit understanding of interactions are at the interfaces between the five compartments, ranked per slot by live residual that persists and does not trend down, enumerated outward through the contact cascade, and triaged by SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD. The priority order is a budget filter; the verdict waits for an instrument that resolves the coupling. Independence is bought across genuinely different instruments, not across reports about the same retrieval. Candidate slots from the literature are provenanced imports, not figure-derived names. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a reported `gap`: the framework cannot rank slots numerically without a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory instead of a checkable shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is misallocating observation budgets to a global average that hides a live interface.
- **undoable:** yes. A priority rank can be superseded when an instrument resolves a slot. No primitive is withdrawn and no observation programme must be deleted. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the named-winner reading. A sent claim can be corrected; the record keeps the earlier reading so the correction is checkable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that measures snowfall water content or interface fluxes and closes a slot would name that slot; a demonstration that a compartment-only schedule closes the interaction residual would break the bond-first verdict; a scalar score that preserves a live slot averaging would erase would break the per-slot rule; a sensor for something never registered built from a description alone would break INWARD; a case where frames sharing an evidence pool settled the rank would break the independence rule; a case where the framework named the winner without an import would break the shape limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
