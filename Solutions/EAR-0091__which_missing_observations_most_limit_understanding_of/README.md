## The question restated as an effect

The question is not asking for a longer inventory of gaps. It is asking where the residual has live mass. In effect terms, an observing system holds a library Gamma; it predicts the state of a coupled slot, Gamma(b,a); the world returns o-prime; R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is measured per slot, never as a single global number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). "Missing observations that most limit understanding" are the slots where R persists and does not trend down, at the interfaces between the five named components rather than inside them.

- Option A, "produce a ranked list of the most important missing variables": leads to a single global priority score, which Fig 1 :22 says is how a live signal disappears into an average, and to a list of compartments, which Fig 12 :5 says records the ingredients and not the bond.
- Option B, "characterise the shape of the limiting residual and the triage that finds it": leads to a per-slot, bond-first, cascade-enumerated ranking, split by SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD, with the verdict left to an instrument that resolves the coupling.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a function from a described residual to a ranked set of slots, each tagged with its reachability class and the instrument that would close it. That shape is the sought-for term. Until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. A `gap` is recorded honestly: the framework returns a shape, not the name of the single most limiting observation.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it names the single most limiting observation, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of Earth-system observation.

Four reasons, three from the figures and one practical:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) and *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). A list of researchers selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Two satellite products built from the same retrieval are one witness wearing two names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
