## Wider literature that bears on this question

- **Psychometrics and the criterion problem.** Torrance's scoring categories treat *"originality, the statistical rarity of the responses"*, which is a library-relative frequency reading, not a ground reading (via the opened Wikipedia Creativity page, §Personal assessment). This is why F2 distinguishes the guard from the rarity descriptor.
- **Quantitative creativity research.** Simonton's 2026 abstract makes personal creativity *"the multiplicative product of originality, utility, and surprise"*, independently of this framework. His 2012 title supplies a three-criterion definition from the patent system. Two different quantitative frames reach products and multiple criteria rather than a single additive scale.
- **Cross-cultural psychology.** The opened Wikipedia Creativity page records that Westerners view creativity in individual attributes while Chinese respondents emphasise social influence. That is the habitat-relative valence of F5 stated in another vocabulary, low overlap with the figures.
- **Computational creativity.** Generation and evaluation are separate phases; novelty is filtered against what the system already knows; usefulness can be tested by playing a level (ANGELINA). This is Fig 5 and Formula step 5 in an engineering frame, and the SPECS 14 components are a concrete example of why an ingredient list is not a verdict.
- **Sociology of science and cultural markets.** The Matthew effect and MUSICLAB are independent demonstrations that visible popularity biases the reading. Acclaim is an updating channel, which is what Fig 2 forbids as an anchor.
- **Goodhart, Campbell and Strathern** are cited in the opened architecture article under *"Proxies, and what happens when a measure becomes a target."* I did not open those primary works; they are named here as the framework's own cited provenance for F8, not as sources I read this session.

Overlap declaration: the two Simonton papers are one author and one research programme, so they are one witness, not two. The Wikipedia pages and the Crossref records are reference sources, not independent empirical witnesses. The essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely with the figures and carry no independent corroboration weight. The genuinely low-overlap witnesses are the cross-cultural finding, the computational-creativity engineering frame, and the MUSICLAB/Matthew-effect material.
