# Outreach draft - check the output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used anywhere in this folder.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim has been checked against Fig 1 :22, Fig 5 :33 and Fig 12 :130. If those three lines do not survive, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One output to check about evaluating creative work when the criteria disagree

> Hello,
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about evaluating creative work. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** "Original," "useful," and "culturally significant" are not three weights on one scale. They have different actors, currencies and anchors:
>
> - Originality is a gate relative to a declared prior-art set. It is not a weight that can be compensated by high usefulness. Under the minimum-set rules I am using, the guards are a product, so a zero at one stops the term.
> - Usefulness is a settlement with something outside the judging frame, an instrument, a use test, an outcome. A committee's impression of usefulness is scored by the same frame that produces it.
> - Cultural significance is a relation between the work and a community's residual, including second and third order effects. It is not a property the work carries, and acclaim is a measure of attention rather than a measure of that relation.
>
> So when the three conflict, the right output is three separate verdicts per work, with the arrangement priced and the decision owned, not one composite score. Bits, actions and contacts are different units, and summing them produces a ranking with no referent.
>
> **What this does not claim:** that a universal set of weights is impossible in principle, that community judgment is never a legitimate anchor, or that a rubric is useless. A shared definition makes a disagreement statable. A rubric that decides the outcome has stopped being a convention. I also do not claim a ground reading: no measurement here has settled this.
>
> **What would refute the output, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. Show that the three criteria form a partition over one variable with a shared anchor and currency, so a case in two conditions is just a harder case of one.
> 2. Show a common unit that already measures novelty, settled use and habitat cascade before weighting, and that pays for its own conversion.
> 3. Show an acclaim measure that does not move when judges' attention moves and that settles a disputed significance claim.
> 4. Produce a figure line or a ground measurement that returns universal coefficients across fields.
>
> A one-line "this fails because ..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figures with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Eight essays on the architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** take the formula, run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground, and write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time.

## Distribution constraints

- One recipient at a time. No mailing list, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Record the stage-1 verdict before sending any theory link. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- Public venue pages and opt-in channels only. A Track B style reply would be a conversation with `verdict_weight = 0`, never a witness.
- If a recipient returns a real refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have it re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
