## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan and no receptiveness column.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A panel assembled for likely agreement is a mirror chain (Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A creativity panel whose members share a training pool, a journal circuit and a prize circuit returns one witness wearing many names (Fig 2 :40).
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's record.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## The answer in one line, before the derivation

The three criteria are not three weights on one scale. They are three differently anchored slots with different currencies: originality is a library-relative gate, usefulness is a settlement with a ground that does not update on the evaluator, and cultural significance is a habitat-relative valence whose acclaim measure is a mirror. Keep the verdicts separate and never average them, price the arrangement and not just the ingredient list, and use independent frames with a pre-registered refuter. Universal weights are a declared `gap`, a shape not a name.
