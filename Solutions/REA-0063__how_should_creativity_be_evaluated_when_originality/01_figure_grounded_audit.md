## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

A committee must decide whether a creative work is "creative." It has three readings: originality (how new it is), usefulness (how well it works), cultural significance (what it means to a community). They can come apart. A work can be original and useless, useful and derivative, or culturally loaded and neither.

State the options by what each leads to:

- **Option A, weighted sum.** Assign weights (for example 0.4 originality, 0.3 usefulness, 0.3 significance), score each criterion, add. Leads to a single rank order. Costs: the three criteria use different units, the weights are authored by someone with a stake, and the rubric that assigns them becomes the thing judged.
- **Option B, per-slot verdicts.** Treat the three criteria as three domains with different actors, currencies and anchors, settle each separately, keep the results unaveraged, and record what could not be settled. Leads to a structured judgment with a stated refuter and a named gap. Costs: no single winner falls out for free, so a decision still has to be made and owned.

The residual R is the conflict between these options. It is not "which weight is correct." That restatement already assumes Option A. In effect terms R is: **a set of per-work residuals arriving from three differently anchored readings, plus the demand that they be collapsed into one number.** Arity: three conditions plus the collapsing operation. Symmetry: not symmetric; the three criteria are different operators, not three views of one quantity. Scale: per work (slot), per library (originality), per habitat (significance), and per ground (usefulness).

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: *a protocol that (a) refuses the collapse where the units differ, (b) gives each criterion a verdict with its own anchor, (c) states the failure condition for each, and (d) reports the residue as a gap where no instrument settles it.* I wrote this down before selecting figure lines, per Formula step "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

## 2. Domain census (written before choosing figure lines)

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** three conditions (originality, usefulness, cultural significance) plus a fourth operation, the collapse into one score. Three is not a fixed count of a natural kind; it is the count the question hands us. A re-census may unbundle any of the three (Fig 9 :4-5).
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. Originality is a guard with a zero, usefulness is a settlement with a ground, significance is a relation to a habitat. Their currencies do not commute or add.
- **Scale:** originality is measured against a library Γ at the work slot; usefulness against an instrument at the use slot; significance against a habitat over a cascade. Different scales, so a cross-scale transform is lossy and must be bracketed (Fig 4 :30).

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

**D1, originality (library-relative novelty).** Actors: the work φ, the prior-art corpus, the library Γ and its atoms, the searcher. Currency: novelty, a pass/fail guard, `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`. Anchor: the census of prior art, a closure (a patent corpus, a literature set), not the world and not the ground. If this anchor moves when the committee praises the work, it is not an anchor. Verdict type: partition question at the guard, union question when prior art is contested.

**D2, usefulness (ground settlement).** Actors: the work, the user or instrument, the residual R at the use slot, the evaluator. Currency: description bits and action cost (Fig 12 :125-126), what pays (Formula step 5). Anchor: an experiment, a market, a patient outcome, a working prototype, a measurable behaviour that does not change because a judge admires the work. Verdict type: settlement, not score.

**D3, cultural significance (habitat-relative valence).** Actors: the work as actor, the community, institutions, critics, prizes, audiences, adjacent works, conditions and relations. Currency: contact and cascade, second and third order effects; survival through selection. Anchor: the ground acting on the habitat over time (Fig 11 :36, :56). For the constitutively-about-people part of the question, structured community judgment is a legitimate anchor (Fig 2 :36-37), provided its reading is not allowed to update on its own prestige. Acclaim and prize counts are instruments at best. Verdict type: union, enumerated, not composed.

**D4, the evaluation arrangement (seats).** Actors: maker, interior auditor, ground maintainer, outside frame. Currency: independence, agreement, residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45). Verdict type: arrangement question, with a convention/verdict distinction and a declared stake.

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. All four have one: D1 prior art, D2 instrument, D3 ground-in-habitat, D4 ground.

### Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** The three criteria can hold at once on the same work, and a conflict case satisfies at least two conditions. Fig 9 :54 says *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57-58 says *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* A work that is original and culturally significant but useless is not a hard case of originality; it is a compound with its own loop. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (Fig 1 :19).

### Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. Refuse the collapse first (Fig 9 :54-58). You cannot diagnose a score before you have unbundled what it bundles.
2. Settle D1 originality as a guard (Fig 5 :33, :41), before any judgment of quality, because a zero at the guard stops the mint.
3. Settle D2 usefulness against an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-34), before composing a significance claim on top of it.
4. Enumerate D3 habitat and cascade (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53), after the work's first-order effects are settled.
5. Set the arrangement D4 last: which frames check which readings, and what the verifier may see (Fig 10 :42, :52).

The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance (Fig 3 :20). Effort spent designing a weighted rubric (step 1 in most committees) arrives at a step that cannot use it if the middle link, "what would count as progress here," has no instrument (Fig 3 :25-27: *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither"*).

## 3. Findings F1 to F12

### F1 - The three criteria are a union, not one scale (governing)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54 and :57-58:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

Fig 1 :19 and :22:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

A single weight vector across originality, usefulness and significance aggregates across slots. The work that is original but useless has a live residual in D2 that disappears into the average with high D1 and D3 scores. The right object is three verdicts per work, plus a record of the compound.

**Refuter:** show that the three criteria are a partition over a common variable with a shared anchor and a shared currency, so that a case in two conditions really is one harder case rather than a different compound. If that demonstration is made, the union verdict fails.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 1 (low overlap: union logic versus per-slot residual). `specified`.

### F2 - Originality is a product guard, not a weight

Fig 5 :24-25 and :33 and :41:

> **"is it actually new?"**
> **"it is not already in the library"**
> **"The three guards are a product, not a checklist."**
> **"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."**

Fig 6 :22:

> **"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 states the same product: SUPPORT, REACHABILITY, NOVELTY, *"gated by a product; any factor at zero forces inertness."*

Originality is relative to a declared library, not to the world. A work can be novel to one committee's prior art and unoriginal relative to a wider corpus; the disagreement is about which Γ was enumerated, not about the work. And because the guards are a product, a zero in novelty cannot be compensated by high usefulness or high significance. The question's "conflict" is therefore partly a category mistake: one of the three is a gate, not a weight.

**Strain, named:** a committee can still grade degrees of novelty above the zero (a patent examiner does). The figure governs a mint decision, not a fine-grained rating. Re-census assigns "is it new at all" to the guard and "how unusual within the prior-art distribution" to a separate descriptor with its own instrument. The product claim is not a ban on grading; it is a ban on compensation across the zero. Kept visible.

**Refuter:** demonstrate at the ground that a work failing the novelty guard against a declared corpus still settles and composes as the same term, with the same anchor and no import. Then novelty is a weight after all.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 5 and Fig 6 plus Formula step 3 (three documents, different loci). `specified`. This is the one finding with a direct independent corroborator from outside the framework: Simonton 2026 defines personal creativity as *"the multiplicative product of originality, utility, and surprise"* (Crossref abstract, opened 2026-11-23), a field arriving at a product rather than a sum from a different direction.

### F3 - Usefulness is a settlement with a non-updating ground

Fig 1 :27:

> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Formula step 5, cited from `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*

Fig 2 :29 and :36:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
> **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**

Fig 5 :69 and :72:

> **"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one."**
> **"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."**

A "usefulness score" produced by the same committee that wants the work to be creative is frame-internal. It cannot score the work on a quantity the committee also manufactures. Usefulness needs a reading from something outside the judging frame: a use test, a patient outcome, a market, a working device, a reproduction attempt, a behaviour change. Fig 2 :31 defines independence as *"how little they draw on the same evidence"*, so a second judge reading the same citation list is not a second instrument.

**Refuter:** produce a ground reading of usefulness that moves when the evaluator's opinion changes and still settles the question. By Fig 2 :30 that object is a frame, not an anchor, and the claim stands rather than falls; the genuine refuter is an instrument whose reading both changes with opinion and is accepted as the settlement.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 5 and Formula step 5. `specified`.

### F4 - The two currencies do not add, so a weighted sum is the named error

Fig 12 :125-126 and :130:

> **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
> **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units"**
> **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."**

Novelty is priced in description bits against a library. Usefulness is priced in actions against a ground. Significance is priced in contacts and cascades in a habitat. A rubric that converts all three to 1 to 10 and adds them is doing exactly what Fig 12 :130 calls the error rather than the fix. Summing numbers that share a numeral system but not a unit produces a ranking with no referent.

This is the most decisive figure line against Option A, and it is structural, not a preference. It does not say weighted sums are unpopular; it says the arithmetic is a category error when the terms are in different units.

**Refuter:** exhibit a common unit in which novelty, settled use and habitat cascade are already measured before weighting, with a conversion that does not consume the residual. If such a unit exists and pays for itself under the MDL bargain, the ban on summing softens to a ban on naïve summing.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 1. `specified`. Simonton 2026's multiplicative product is again a low-overlap corroborator for "do not add."

### F5 - Cultural significance is habitat-relative valence and cascade, not a carried property

Fig 11 :43-44 and :48-51:

> **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
> **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
> **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**
> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
> **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

Fig 11 :52-53 and :56:

> **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**
> **"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."**

"Culturally significant" cannot be read off the work. It is a relation between the work and a residual in a habitat, including effects three steps out; the same work is a benefactor to one community's residual and a malfactor to another's (Fig 11 :50). Significance measured by immediate praise is a first-order enumeration with the cascade unseen, which is the DDT failure. What survives longer term is what the ground kept, a selection statement, not an acclaim statement.

**Refuter:** show a cultural-significance reading that is a property carried by the work across a change of residual, with the same valence in two habitats whose grounds differ. Fig 11 :51 predicts this cannot happen; a demonstrated case refutes the valence claim.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 2 (different figures, different mechanisms). `specified`. World corroboration: the Matthew effect page records the MUSICLAB experiment, where *"the group that saw which songs were the most popular and were downloaded the most were then biased to choose those songs as well"* (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23), an independent demonstration that visible acclaim biases the reading rather than reporting the ground.

### F6 - Acclaim and prestige are a mirror, not an anchor

Fig 2 :35 and :39-40:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
> **"Correlation has valence."**
> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Seat Map :171: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Seat Map :205: *"An anchor someone benefits from is one whose keeper is disqualified."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

Prize counts, citation counts and panel acclaim are a popularity reading of D3. They can fund a closer look at the work; they cannot settle whether the work matters. They also update on the evaluator: a prize raises the perceived significance of the work, which raises the prize's apparent validity. That is panel 1 of Fig 2 (mutual update), not panel 3 (anchor).

**Refuter:** exhibit an acclaim measure that does not move when the judges' attention moves and that settles a disputed significance claim at the ground. Until then acclaim has `verdict_weight = 0` in this derivation, the same rule the project applies to direct-outreach replies.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and the Seat Map (two documents) with an essay corroborator. `specified`.

### F7 - Human judgment is the legitimate anchor only for the people-question

Fig 2 :36-38:

> **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
> **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."**
> **"It is only an instrument for is this code correct"**

This is the strain that required a re-census. The question "is this significant for us?" is constitutively about people, so structured community judgment is the right anchor there. The question "is this novel against the corpus?" and "does this work in use?" are not constitutively about the committee; for those, human judgment is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. A single "human judgment is the anchor" sentence applied to all three criteria is the error. Applied per domain it is the rule.

Fig 11 :36 records the other half: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Cultural selection can run unmaintained. A maintained committee can steer introduction, but it cannot subtract the habitat (Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*).

**Refuter:** show a committee reading of novelty or usefulness that is itself the ground for those questions, not an instrument, with a non-updating property demonstrated rather than asserted.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 11. `specified`. Strain resolved by domain assignment, not by softening either line.

### F8 - A rubric is a convention until it decides an outcome

Fig 10 :47 and :49:

> **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
> **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**

Seat Map :167: *"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and a piece of content both do."* Seat Map :168: *"A convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority."*

A shared definition of "original" is useful: it makes the disagreement statable (Fig 10 :16: *the point that a convention makes a bug statable rather than fixing it*). A scoring rule that turns that definition into a funding or award decision is no longer a convention; it has become a verdict authored by the seat that should only maintain the channel. This is why the protocol in §5 records the rubric as a shared meaning and routes the actual decision through readings that can refuse it.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a scoring convention whose author has no gain from any particular reading and whose output cannot be moved by the scored party, and which is nonetheless accepted as a verdict by all frames. Then the collapse is not automatic and the claim narrows.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 10 and the Seat Map. `specified`.

### F9 - The checking arrangement must buy independence, not agreement

Fig 2 :31, :35, :42 and :45:

> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**
> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
> **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**
> **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."**

Fig 10 :42 and :43:

> **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."**
> **"Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking. Never a player, never a proxy, never the author of a verdict or an atom. It transmits verdicts and issues none."**

Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

A jury of peers who share a field, a journal circuit and a prize circuit is one evidence pool wearing many names. Adding more of them raises the vocabulary, not the anchor (Seat Map :283). The arrangement needs at least one frame whose closure differs: a user who never read the critics, an instrument that measures use, an outside discipline that holds a different vocabulary. Seat Map :213 sets the selection rule: *"a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed"*, never a panel chosen by a participant.

**Refuter:** show that two frames drawing on one evidence pool can still correct each other on the same claim without importing a third reading. Fig 2 :31 says no; a demonstrated case would refute the independence requirement.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 10. `specified`.

### F10 - The broken link is the objective, not the scoring

Fig 3 :25-27 and :29:

> **"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
> **"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."**

Most evaluation debates start at scoring (planning) with an assumed objective. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* The missing middle link is "what would count as progress for this criterion in this domain," and it has no instrument in most creativity assessment. A reading below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

**Refuter:** exhibit an instrumented objective for each of the three criteria that is already in routine use and whose readings are not pooled with the evaluator's own preference. Then the break is elsewhere and this finding narrows to the institutions that still lack it.

**Status:** single-source on the figure side (Fig 3), with independent world corroboration from the psychometric literature's criterion problem and the computational-creativity generation/evaluation split. `specified`.

### F11 - The arrangement must be priced, not just the ingredients

Fig 12 :5 and :46-47 and :94 and :104:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
> **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**
> **"The arrangement is the substance."**
> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

A checklist of novelty, utility and impact records ingredients. Two works with the same three scores can differ entirely in how the three are bonded: a work whose originality produces its usefulness (`⇒` production) is a different substance from one whose originality merely coexists with usefulness (`+` conjunction), and one whose significance is a threshold crossed by the first two (`⋛` comparison) is different again. Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

A settled verdict at one level becomes an atom at the next, which is how a field can build on a previous judgment without re-running the whole evaluation. But Fig 5 :72 warns: *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* More past decisions do not by themselves make the next decision reachable.

**Refuter:** show two works with identical ingredient scores and identical bonds under the seven-bond test whose evaluations still differ at the ground with no new atom imported. Then the bond is not carrying the information the figure claims.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 5. `specified`.

### F12 - No figure returns universal weights, a single score, or a named rubric (the gap)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

After a genuine census, no figure line governs "what weights should originality, usefulness and cultural significance have?" The figures govern the shape of a defensible evaluation, not its field-specific coefficients. A universal weight vector would be a `general`-mode verdict with no ground (Seat Map :281: *"an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict"*).

Declare this a `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, not underivable in principle. A field-specific instrument (a validated protocol, a use dataset, a cascade study, a discriminating measure) could settle coefficients for a domain. That is INWARD work (Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*) plus OUTWARD work (Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*) with a source gate and a shadow test. **Unreached is a verdict, not a silence.**

**Refuter:** a figure line that directly licenses a universal weight vector, or a ground measurement that settles coefficients across domains with one non-updating anchor. Either fills the gap and moves the verdict to `grounded`.

**Status:** `gap`, declared after census. Not a failure to find an answer, a finding about what the framework can return.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Union refuter.** The three criteria are shown to be a partition over one variable with a shared anchor and currency, so a case in two conditions is just a harder case of one. (Fails F1.)
2. **Guard refuter.** A work failing the novelty guard against a declared prior-art corpus still settles and composes as the same term, with no import and no change of anchor. (Fails F2.)
3. **Currency refuter.** A common unit is exhibited that already measures novelty, settled use and habitat cascade before weighting, and that pays for its own conversion under the MDL bargain. (Fails F4.)
4. **Mirror refuter.** An acclaim or prize reading is shown to be non-updating on evaluator attention and to settle a disputed significance claim at the ground. (Fails F6.)
5. **Gap filler.** A figure line or a ground measurement returns universal coefficients for all three criteria. (Fills F12 and changes the mode.)

Until one fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation: the shape a defensible evaluation must have

1. **Unbundle before scoring.** Record three verdicts per work, one per domain, plus an explicit note on which conditions hold together (F1). Never publish just the average (Fig 1 :22).
2. **Run novelty as a gate against a declared corpus.** State Γ before the search. A zero at novelty stops the mint; it is not traded against usefulness (F2). Grade degrees of rarity separately with a named instrument, and record that grading as a binding, not as the guard itself.
3. **Settle usefulness against a non-updating instrument.** Use, reproduction, behaviour, outcome. A committee's impression of usefulness is frame-internal (F3).
4. **Keep currencies apart.** Bits, actions and contacts are three readings (F4, Fig 12 :130). If a decision must be single, make it as an owned decision with a named decision-maker and a declared stake, not as a sum pretending to be a measurement.
5. **Enumerate the habitat for significance.** List actors, conditions and relations outward until the cascade stops mattering; index valence to a residual; record which community's residual (F5). Treat acclaim as a funding signal, not a verdict (F6).
6. **Make structured community judgment the anchor only for the people-question**, and keep it blind to prize and prestige (F7).
7. **Treat the rubric as a convention.** It makes disagreements statable. It does not decide the outcome (F8).
8. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.** At least one frame from a different closure and at least one instrument that does not read the critics' reports (F9). Pool members by an unauthored rule, never by likely agreement (Seat Map :213).
9. **Price the bonds.** State whether originality produces usefulness, coexists with it, or is compared against it. Same ingredients, different bond, different work (F11).
10. **Declare the gap.** Universal weights are not returned. Record what an instrument would have to look like and stop there (F12).

Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the protocol and its refuter, not a list of past decisions.
