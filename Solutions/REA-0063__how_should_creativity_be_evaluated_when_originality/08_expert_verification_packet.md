## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Originality, usefulness and cultural significance are not three weights on one scale. They have different actors, currencies and anchors, so their verdicts must be kept separate and never averaged. Originality is a gate relative to a declared prior-art set and cannot be compensated by high usefulness. Usefulness is a settlement with something outside the judging frame, a use test, a reproduction, an outcome, because a committee's impression of usefulness is scored by the frame that also wants the work to be creative. Cultural significance is a relation between the work and a community's residual, including second and third order effects, not a property the work carries; acclaim and prize counts measure attention, which updates on the judges. The currencies are bits, actions and contacts, and adding them after conversion produces a rank with no referent. What the framework returns is a shape: three separately anchored verdicts per work, the bond between them priced, independent frames, an owned decision, and a declared gap on universal weights. Falsifiers were written before the run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal and prior-art facing: originality is a gate, not a weight

- **Statement:** novel means novel relative to a declared library, and the guards are a product, so a zero at novelty cannot be traded against usefulness or significance. Graded rarity above zero is a separate descriptor with its own instrument.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :22, "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Fig 5 :33, "The three guards are a product, not a checklist." Fig 5 :41, "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."
- **What to check:** does the prior-art practice you know treat a failed novelty check as a gate or as a discount? If novelty is a discount in your field, say so with an instrument that pays for it; the claim then narrows to the frames where it is a gate. Note the honest limit: Fig 5's product runs over the three novelty-side guards, so cross-criterion non-compensation is carried as an analogue from the gate structure, not as a direct figure dictum (`06` Q1).
- **Refuter:** a work that fails the novelty guard against a declared prior-art corpus still settles and composes as the same term, with no import and no change of anchor. Then novelty is a weight after all.

### C2, framework-internal and methodological: the currencies do not add, and usefulness needs a non-updating ground

- **Statement:** novelty is priced in description bits against a library, usefulness in actions against a ground, significance in contacts in a habitat. Converting all three to a common score and summing is the structural error the figure names. A usefulness reading from the same frame that wants the work to be creative is frame-internal and cannot settle usefulness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :130, "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." Fig 1 :27, "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check:** is there a common unit, already measured before weighting, in which these three readings already live, with a conversion that pays for itself? In evaluation practice, does a use test or reproduction attempt return a reading that moves when the evaluator's opinion moves and still get accepted as the settlement? If so, the claim narrows.
- **Refuter:** exhibit that common unit and conversion, or exhibit a usefulness instrument whose reading both changes with opinion and is accepted as the ground. The first softens the ban on summing to a ban on naive summing; the second breaks the non-updating requirement.

### C3, field facing: significance is habitat-relative valence, and acclaim is a mirror

- **Statement:** cultural significance is a relation between the work and a residual in an enumerated habitat, including effects two and three steps out. The same work can be a benefactor to one community's residual and a malfactor to another's. Acclaim, prizes and citation counts are instruments of attention, not anchors; they can fund a closer look and cannot settle the claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43, "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :50, "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :52-53, "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- **What to check:** in a case you know, did the cultural effect that mattered appear only at second or third order, and did the same work carry opposite valence in two communities? Does the acclaim measure move when attention moves? The MUSICLAB experiment quoted in `04` is one low-overlap demonstration of the mirror effect; an independent case from your field would be a better test.
- **Refuter:** show a significance reading that is a property carried by the work across a change of residual with the same valence in two habitats whose grounds differ, or show an acclaim measure that is non-updating on attention and settles a disputed significance claim at the ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal rubric, a weight vector, a single winner or a field-wide verdict.
- Not that originality cannot be graded, only that the gate decides zero and grading needs its own instrument.
- Not that community judgment is never a legitimate anchor. It is legitimate for the constitutively-about-people question and an instrument for novelty and usefulness.
- Not that a rubric is useless. It makes a disagreement statable; when it decides the outcome it has stopped being a convention.
- Not that acclaim has zero practical use. It can fund a closer look; `verdict_weight = 0` here means it does not settle a verdict.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing in this packet was settled by a measurement.
