# The Tether's answer: evaluating creativity when three criteria conflict

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Per-domain verdicts are stated separately and never averaged. No field-wide coefficient has been settled by a ground.

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This answer synthesizes `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md` and `06_isomers_and_order.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A committee has three readings of one work: originality, usefulness and cultural significance. They can come apart. The demand is to collapse them into one number. The residual is not which weight is correct, because that restatement already assumes a weighted sum. In effect terms it is a set of per-work residuals arriving from three differently anchored readings, plus a demand for collapse. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The work that is original but useless has a live residual in the usefulness slot that disappears into an average with high originality and high significance. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

What an answer would look like was bound before the run in `01` §0: a protocol that refuses the collapse where units differ, gives each criterion a verdict with its own anchor, states the failure condition for each, and reports the residue as a gap where no instrument settles it.

## 2. The shape: one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

### D1, originality: a gate against a declared library

Originality is relative to a prior-art closure Γ that the committee must state before it searches. It is a pass/fail guard at zero, not a weight that usefulness can compensate. Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist." Fig 5 :41 "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting." Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."

A work can be novel to one committee's corpus and unoriginal against a wider one. That disagreement is about which Γ was enumerated, not about the work. Degrees of rarity above the gate are a separate descriptor with its own instrument; the guard produces a zero, not a rating. Strain kept visible in `01` F2 and `06` Q1.

### D2, usefulness: a settlement with a ground that does not update on the judge

A usefulness score written by the same committee that wants the work to be creative is frame-internal. Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Usefulness needs a reading from outside the judging frame: a use test, a reproduction attempt, a behaviour change, a patient outcome, a market. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 5 :69 "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one."

Settlement is not the same as composability. A library of past favourable decisions is not a reach that grows.

### D3, cultural significance: habitat-relative valence, cascade included

Significance cannot be read off the work. It is a relation between the work and a community's residual, including effects second and third order out. Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :50 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." List actors, conditions and relations outward until the cascade stops mattering; index valence to a named community's residual; record which residual, because valence changes with it.

For the constitutively-about-people part of the question, structured community judgment is the legitimate anchor. Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." For novelty and usefulness it is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Prize counts and acclaim are a funding signal at best: they move when attention moves, so they cannot anchor a verdict.

### D4, the checking arrangement: buy independence, not agreement

A jury drawn from one field, one journal circuit and one prize circuit is one evidence pool wearing many names. Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The arrangement needs at least one frame from a different closure and at least one instrument that does not read the critics' reports. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Send the characterized residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior. Pool members by an unauthored rule, never by likely agreement.

### Cross-domain: the union and the non-addable currencies

The three criteria are a union, not a partition. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A work that is original and significant but useless is not a harder case of originality; it is a compound with its own loop. Record three verdicts per work plus the bond between them, and own the final decision rather than extracting it from a sum.

The currencies do not add. Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." Originality is priced in bits against a library, usefulness in actions against a ground, significance in contacts in a habitat. Adding them after conversion produces a ranking with no referent. Two works with the same three scores can be isomers, different substances whose difference is carried by the bond and invisible in the scoreboard (`06` Q2).

### The gap on universal weights

No figure line returns a universal weight vector, a single score or a named rubric. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." A field-specific instrument could settle coefficients for one domain; nothing here settles them across domains. This is declared as a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not as silence and not as underivable in principle. Unreached is a verdict, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a universal weight vector is impossible in principle. It is unsettled here, which is a different claim.
- Not that originality cannot be graded. The gate decides zero; grading above zero needs a separate instrument with its own anchor.
- Not that community judgment is never a legitimate anchor. It is legitimate for the people-question and an instrument elsewhere.
- Not that a rubric is useless. A shared definition makes a disagreement statable; a rubric that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- Not that acclaim measures significance. It measures attention, which is an updating channel.
- Not that this analysis is a ground reading. No measurement settled any of it.

## 4. What would settle it, and the pre-registered falsifiers

A ground reading, not agreement. The falsifiers were written in `01` §4 and `06` before this conclusion:

1. Show the three criteria are a partition over one variable with a shared anchor and currency, so a case in two conditions is just a harder case of one.
2. Exhibit a common unit that already measures novelty bits, settled-use actions and habitat cascade before weighting, and that pays for its own conversion.
3. Exhibit an acclaim measure that does not move when judges' attention moves and that settles a disputed significance claim at the ground.
4. Produce a figure line or a ground measurement that returns universal coefficients across fields.
5. Show a work failing the novelty guard against a declared corpus still settling and composing as the same term with no import.

Until one fires, no part of this is labelled `grounded`. What a real settlement would look like: a validated protocol in a declared domain, a discriminating use dataset, a cascade study, or a measure whose reading refuses the committee's preference.

## 5. The honest stop

The three criteria are vocabulary the question hands us, already bound in the evaluators' frame. Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." A per-work verdict carried to a field policy is a lossy transform with an R_T that must be measured, not assumed zero. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a weighting for originality, usefulness and cultural significance, because the three are not three weights on one scale. Originality is a gate relative to a declared prior-art set and cannot be compensated by usefulness. Usefulness is a settlement with something outside the judging frame, a use test or an outcome, not the committee's impression. Cultural significance is a relation between the work and a community's residual, including effects three steps out, and acclaim measures attention rather than that relation. The currencies are bits, actions and contacts, and summing them produces a rank with no referent. What survives is a shape: three separately anchored verdicts per work, the bond between them priced, at least one checking frame from a different closure, an owned decision, and a declared gap on universal weights. The refuters were written before the run. Nothing here is a ground reading, and a reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
