## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *three separately anchored verdicts, a novelty gate, an instrument for use, a habitat reading for significance, priced bonds, independent frames, and a declared gap on weights.* A universal weight vector (0.4 / 0.3 / 0.3) is a name where the framework can only give a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

For this residual the bootstrapping started in effect terms: what does each criterion touch, what pays for it, what would make its reading move? The figure lines were selected after that census, not before (`01` §2). That order is the difference between derivation and echo.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, opened this session: *"You cannot accumulate your way from the category of outputs to the category of generators; they are different kinds of thing, and no quantity of the first sums to the second."* A pile of past evaluation decisions is an output, not the generator of a decision. Aggregating them into a rubric does not create a ground.

The same essay: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The three criteria the question hands us are likewise a visible choice from one community's vocabulary, not nature's list. Naming them as *the* three axes of creativity would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a universal weight vector is the specific forbidden error

*They're Scanning Books*: *"Put a hundred geniuses in a room. The output is not super-genius. Aggregation buys breadth (more fields, more perspectives) and parallelism (more problems at once); it does not buy depth on a single hard novel problem."* And: *"Often it is worse there, because a committee regresses toward consensus, and consensus is the average, which is duller than the best member on the thing the best member is best at."*

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*, opened this session, states the operational version: *"Votes come from outcomes, not popularity."* And: *"Use the success rate per try, not the total. Whatever gets tried most piles up votes."* A composite score that rewards the criterion with the most visible signals is a popularity reading wearing a measurement costume.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Three committee scores can look identical for works with different sources; the reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a finer average.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A rubric authored by the evaluators and used to validate their evaluations is that failure in one sentence.

## 5. Q2 - what licenses a particular weight? ANSWERED as a gap

**What licenses a particular weight for originality, usefulness or cultural significance?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A weight is licensed only by the ground settling a measured term in a declared domain, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the weight is the committee's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Is a universal rubric a forbidden act?** Yes, in two ways: it names where the framework gives a shape, and it is a `general`-mode verdict with no ground. Seat Map :281: *"an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."*

**What survives instead?** The four-condition separator in `01` §5. A committee can still own a decision; it just cannot claim the decision was extracted from the measurement.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I nearly overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any of the three criteria. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Originality, usefulness and cultural significance are ordinary vocabulary from the question, already held as bindings in the evaluators' frame. Naming them is not inventing a primitive; it is declaring which bindings are in play, which is provenance.

What remains forbidden is naming a seventh criterion as if the framework derived it, and treating a declared binding as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 6 :22 on what IMPORT adds). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The gap is about weights, not about the vocabulary of the three criteria.

A second correction: my §4 first read *"the best member"* as licensing an appeal to a single expert. It does not. That sentence is a warning about averaging, not an appointment of an authority. The framework's answer to whose reading counts is structural: a frame with a different closure, ranged against a non-updating ground (Fig 2 :31, :35). No person is named.

## 6. Culture, drift and the people-question

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session, is the governing essay for D3: *"Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge. Its members can hold very different mindsets; what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each other against."* And: *"It also drifts. Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice (Fig 2 :34)."*

The same essay names the three safeguards: *"Science's independent replication is separate experience plus something real to check against; Apprenticeship is two-way contact with correction; Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."* For cultural significance, the first and third are the relevant ones: separate experience across communities, and an anchor that neither the work nor the committee can move.

Fig 2 :36-37 makes the scope explicit: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* So structured community judgment is legitimate for "is this significant for us" and an instrument for "is this novel" and "does this work." See `01` F7 and the retraction table.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Diversity is a natural hedge."* And on rooms that only check each other: *"the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on)."* A diverse panel is useful only while it keeps ranging against something it cannot move.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A prize or a citation count can get a work examined. It cannot settle whether the work is creative, and the person holding the prize has a standing stake that survives the outcome (Fig 10 :61: *"And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."*).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture article: LLM outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01`, this file and `07` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. The stopping place

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"you get to decide your stopping place if you only need it to cover a part of a field of study or only apply it to a certain specific issue."* And: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

This solution stops at a shape and a falsifier. It does not return a rubric, a weighting, a winner or a field-wide verdict. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
