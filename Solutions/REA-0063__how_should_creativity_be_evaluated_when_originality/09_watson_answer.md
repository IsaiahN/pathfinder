## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when a committee must evaluate a creative work whose originality, usefulness and cultural significance readings come apart. Option A assigns weights, scores each criterion and adds them into one rank. Option B keeps three separately anchored verdicts, refuses the collapse where currencies differ, prices the bond between the readings, buys independent frames, owns the final decision and declares a gap on universal weights. What each leads to: A sends a rank whose numbers share a numeral system but not a unit, authored by a frame that scores itself, with the live per-work residual averaged away; B sends a checkable shape, three falsifiers written before the run and an honest open slot where no instrument settles the coefficients.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. Partition or union. Order by dependency (Fig 3).

- R (the residual). Three readings arriving from differently anchored slots plus a demand that they collapse into one number. Arity: three conditions plus a fourth operation, the collapse. Symmetry: not symmetric; the readings are different operators, not three views of one quantity. Scale: per work, per library for originality, per instrument for usefulness and per habitat over a cascade for significance. Fig 9 :54 is the governing union line; a case in two conditions is a compound with its own loop, not a harder case of either.
- D1 originality, library-relative novelty. Actors: the work, the prior-art corpus, the library and its atoms, the searcher. Currency: description bits against a declared closure. Anchor: the enumerated prior-art set, which does not move when a judge praises the work. Question type: partition at the zero guard, union when prior art is contested.
- D2 usefulness, ground settlement. Actors: the work, the user or instrument, the residual at the use slot, the evaluator. Currency: actions, what pays. Anchor: a use test, reproduction, behaviour or outcome that does not change because the evaluator admires the work. Question type: settlement, not score.
- D3 cultural significance, habitat-relative valence. Actors: the work as actor, community, institutions, critics, prizes, audiences, adjacent works, conditions and relations. Currency: contact and cascade, second and third order effects. Anchor: the ground acting on the habitat over time, plus structured community judgment for the constitutively-about-people part. Question type: union, enumerated, never composed.
- D4 the checking arrangement, seats. Actors: maker, interior auditor, ground maintainer, outside frame. Currency: independence, agreement, residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: arrangement, with a convention and verdict distinction and a declared stake. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- Order by dependency. Unbundle the union before scoring; bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run; settle the novelty gate against a declared library before judging quality; settle usefulness against an instrument before composing a significance claim; enumerate the habitat after first-order effects are settled; set the arrangement last; measure the round trip when carrying a per-work verdict to a field policy.

Strain test applied: a guard decides zero while field practice grades degrees of rarity. Re-census assigns them to two quantities with two anchors, so no figure strains after reassignment. The Fig 5 product runs over novelty-side guards, so cross-criterion non-compensation is carried as an analogue with a stated limit.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the premise that the conflict is a weighting problem does not. The three criteria are a union of differently anchored slots, not three weights on one scale. Originality is a gate relative to a declared prior-art closure, usefulness is a settlement with a ground that does not update on the evaluator, and cultural significance is habitat-relative valence whose acclaim measure is a mirror. Keep the three verdicts per work and never average them, price the bond between the readings, buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, own the final decision with a declared stake, and declare universal weights a gap. Nothing here is grounded: no instrument returned a measurement at the time of writing. The strongest outward artifact is `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, which leads with the claim and its refuters.

- cost if wrong: bounded and locatable in the packet, but real if the collapse is accepted. A single composite score averages away the live per-work residual (Fig 1 :22) and spends the independence of outside readers by sending a recorded rubric instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. The avoidable part is leading with the shape and its refuter rather than with a weighting scheme.
- undoable: yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no coefficient is committed. The decision stays owned and can be superseded by a corrected packet; the three verdicts remain separable, so a failed sub-claim can be dropped without disturbing the others.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A validated protocol in a declared domain returning stable coefficients, a discriminating use instrument whose reading refuses the evaluator's preference, a cascade study showing a carried property with unchanged valence across two habitats, or a figure re-census that returns universal weights would each change the verdict. A panel agreeing with this run would not; agreement from one evidence pool is one witness, and a mirror cannot correct you.
