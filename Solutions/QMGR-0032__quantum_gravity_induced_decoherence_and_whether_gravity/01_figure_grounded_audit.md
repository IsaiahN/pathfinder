## 0. The question under audit, restated in effect terms

> Quantum gravity-induced decoherence, and whether gravity itself causes wavefunction collapse.

The sentence joins two claims. Stated as options with consequences:

- **Claim A, gravity induces decoherence.** A system loses coherence because gravitational degrees of freedom (time dilation, metric fluctuations, a graviton bath, internal energy superpositions sourcing a field) carry away phase information. Consequence: the reduced state diagonalises, interference visibility falls, but no branch is selected by a physical collapse. The global state stays pure in the larger system.
- **Claim B, gravity itself causes collapse.** A physical, non-unitary reduction occurs, sourced by gravity (for example the Diósi-Penrose class of models). Consequence: the dynamics itself is modified; a new term must be admitted; the ground of the theory changes.

A third option exists and must not be smuggled in: **A and B are the same thing**. That is the conflation this audit tests.

**What an answer would look like (binding the sought shape as an ordinary term).** An answer has three parts: (1) a statement of which quantity decays (the channel or the ground), (2) a partition of the two claims so each can face its own instrument, (3) a named instrument that would fire if the answer were wrong. Until all three are present, the question is a preference, not a term. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

R described by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity.** Two claims joined by "and". This is at least a union, not a single question. Claim A concerns a reduced dynamics; claim B concerns a modified dynamics.
- **Symmetry.** Decoherence is a one-way loss of visibility under tracing out; collapse is a stochastic selection of one outcome. They are not interchangeable under any symmetry the figures recognise. Fig 12's symmetric bonds (`+ ∥ ≡`) would treat their conjunction as one term; the ordered bonds would not. Nothing in the audit licenses the symmetric reading.
- **Scale.** Claim A is first order: a system coupled to an unobserved field. Claim B is a change at the level of the dynamics itself, so second order relative to A.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union compound; unbundle before searching (decisive at the level of the question)

Fig 9 :54 verbatim:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :5 verbatim:

> **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

The word "and" in the source question joins a channel claim (A) to a ground claim (B). Fig 9 :54-59 names the failure: a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. If we search "does gravity collapse the wavefunction" as one question, any decoherence measurement can be read as an answer to both, which is exactly the conflation to avoid.

Verdict: **union**, `general` structural, triangulated across Fig 9 :5 and Fig 9 :54 (two passages, different quantities: splitting a non-shrinking fact versus unbundling a union). Refuter: show that A and B share a single dynamics such that a measurement of A alone is a measurement of B. The world literature says the opposite: Matsui 2026 states *"decoherence alone cannot certify that gravity is quantized"* (secondary corroboration, not the verdict).

### F2 - Decoherence is channel decay; collapse is a ground claim (decisive at the level of the figures)

Fig 10 :24 verbatim:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Fig 2 :33 verbatim:

> **"The anchor must not update."**

In D2 the thing that decays is the channel: the experimental access to coherence, measured as visibility or as a heating rate. Pikovski et al. 2015 derive decoherence of position *"even without any external environment"*, which is a statement about correlations with internal degrees of freedom, not about a change in the dynamics. Angeli, Gundhi and Bassi 2026 derive a master equation whose *"noisy contributions suppress coherences between states with different mass quadrupole"*. Both are channel descriptions in the figure's sense.

In D3 the claim is that the dynamics itself changed. That is a claim about the ground of the theory. Fig 10 :24 says the ground does not decay, and Fig 2 :33 says the anchor does not update. So the framework cannot derive a collapse term from a decoherence term: doing so would turn the channel into the ground, which is the collapse named in Fig 2 :34 (*"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together"*).

Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** (Fig 10 :24 and Fig 2 :33 are two figures with low overlap: channel decay versus anchor stability). This is the strongest single figure-grounded point in the folder. Refuter: a figure re-census that produces a line in which the ground decays, or an experiment in which visibility loss is accompanied by a physical selection of one branch with no unobserved degree of freedom carrying the phase.

### F3 - A collapse term is a mint with an import, not a sorted residual

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 verbatim:

> SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0 for some s` before the search
> REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)` during
> NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)` after
> **"gated by a product; any factor at zero forces inertness"**

Fig 6 :25 verbatim:

> **"An import adds no atom to the world."**

For claim B:

- **SUPPORT** is present. A residual exists: definite outcomes are not delivered by unitary evolution alone. Bassi et al. 2013 open their review with exactly this gap (superposition versus the observation that *"macroscopic objects are never found in a linear superposition of position states"*).
- **REACHABILITY** fails inside the framework. A stochastic nonlinear modification is not composed from the six terms plus the seven bonds. It needs an atom from the physics frame.
- **NOVELTY** is satisfied relative to `Γ` but not relative to physics: collapse models already exist in the world literature. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*
- **SETTLEMENT** is absent here. Bassi et al. 2013 conclude that ongoing experiments *"can verify or rule out the proposed stochastic modification of quantum theory"*, that is, the ground has not yet paid.

Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the framework gate (Formula step 3), triangulated with Fig 6 :25 for the "no atom added to the world" clause. Refuter: derive a collapse operator from the six terms and seven bonds without importing a stochastic term.

### F4 - Instruments already exist, so this is unreached, not unreachable

Fig 6 :35 verbatim:

> **"extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

Fig 6 :34 verbatim (first clause):

> **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**

Fig 8 :39 verbatim:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Matter-wave interferometry returns a visibility; mechanical oscillators return a noise spectrum; both already fail to resolve the relevant gravity-induced rate at current sensitivity. Donadi, Lobo and Fadel 2026 report that displacement and occupation measurements *"can already constrain the fluctuation time of the first model below the Planck time"* and propose improved bounds. So there is an edge to extend from. The honest status is **unreached**, not `unreachable`, and not `grounded`.

Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 :34-35 and Fig 8 :39 (instrument availability versus absence-of-proof). Refuter: show that no existing instrument returns any gravity-correlated coherence signal at any resolution, so there is no edge to extend.

### F5 - The magnitude of decoherence is not a figure verdict; it is a `gap` for the figures

No figure line governs a decoherence rate, a heating rate, or a localisation length. Fig 13 :118 says *"infinite effort inside a finite reach"*, which is about composition inside a closure, not about whether a graviton bath decoheres a quadrupole at a given rate.

Buza and Toroš 2026 derive Lindblad equations for graviton baths and conclude that *"the effect remains small for realistic parameters"*. That is a physics result with an instrument behind it, not a figure result. Per the method: **if no figure line governs after a genuine re-census, say `gap` plainly.** Mark this quantity `gap` at the figure level and hand it to the ground.

Verdict: `gap` at figure level, world-corroborated but not figure-decided. Refuter: a figure re-census that produces a line pricing a decoherence rate.

### F6 - Naming Diósi-Penrose would be an import with no source gate

Fig 6 :23 verbatim:

"Gravity causes collapse because of Diósi-Penrose" names a specific term from the physics frame and binds it onto a framework slot. That is permitted as a provenanced binding, but it is not a derivation and it carries zero verdict weight until its source gate and shadow test are shown. The framework returns a shape: a ground-level claim is needed if collapse is real. It does not return the name of the model.

Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the import rule (Fig 6 :23), corroborated on provenance by Fig 8 :51-52. Refuter: derive the Diósi-Penrose parameters from the six terms alone.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per the standing rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An experiment shows a gravity-induced loss of coherence with no unobserved degree of freedom carrying the phase, so the decay is in the ground and not the channel (breaks F2).
2. A figure re-census produces a line in which the anchor decays or the ground updates (breaks F2 at the framework level).
3. A collapse operator is derived from the six terms and seven bonds with no imported stochastic atom (breaks F3).
4. Decoherence alone is demonstrated to certify that gravity is quantised, with a source-discriminating instrument reported (breaks F1 and the secondary corroboration from Matsui 2026).
5. No existing instrument returns any gravity-correlated coherence signal at any resolution, so the question is genuinely unreachable rather than unreached (breaks F4).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **One question or two?** Union, unbundle (Fig 9 :54).
2. **Which quantity decays?** The channel, not the ground (Fig 10 :24). The slot is not filled by a figure.
3. **Is a new term owed?** Yes for collapse, no for decoherence (Formula step 3; Fig 6 :25).
4. **Is it in `closure(Γ)`?** No. A stochastic nonlinear term must be imported (Fig 6 :23).
5. **Is there a source gate?** Physics papers supply the source; the shadow test is whether the term explains the definite-outcome residual. Provenance recorded, verdict weight zero until the ground pays.
6. **Settlement?** None here. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Status `candidate`.
7. **Route?** Refuse the conflation before minting a mechanism (Formula step 2). Negation is unary (Operators table :70), so the refusal is a term, not a subtraction.
