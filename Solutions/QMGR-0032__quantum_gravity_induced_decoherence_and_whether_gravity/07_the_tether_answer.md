# The Tether's answer on gravitational decoherence and collapse

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The source question asks whether gravity induces decoherence and whether gravity itself causes wavefunction collapse. Taken as one question it cannot be searched: a visibility loss can be read as an answer to both halves. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The residual is a **union compound** with two different instruments, not one hard problem.

## 2. The shape

1. **Two claims, two quantities.** Decoherence is a claim about a channel: phase information goes into degrees of freedom not tracked by the observer, so the reduced state diagonalises. Collapse is a claim about a ground: the dynamics itself is modified so one outcome is physically selected. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, triangulated by Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* These verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
2. **The channel claim is figure-supported in shape, not in magnitude.** A system coupled to an unobserved gravitational degree of freedom loses coherence. Pikovski et al. 2015 derive this *"even without any external environment"*; Angeli et al. 2026 derive a master equation whose noisy terms suppress coherences. No figure line prices a rate, so the magnitude is `gap` at figure level and is handed to the instrument.
3. **The collapse claim is a mint needing an import.** Formula step 3 gates SUPPORT, REACHABILITY and NOVELTY. Support exists (definite outcomes are unexplained by unitary evolution alone); reachability inside the framework fails (a stochastic nonlinear term is not composed from the six terms and seven bonds); the term is a provenanced binding from the physics frame, not a derivation. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
4. **The two claims are not isomers as written, but are a testable isomer claim in reduced-state form.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Written over the same reduced-state atoms with different coupling bonds, they become checkable rather than verbal. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
5. **The order is fixed.** Refuse the conflation before minting a mechanism (Formula step 2). Settle the channel claim against an instrument before asserting a change of ground (Fig 3 :19-21). Negation is unary and is not subtraction (Operators table :73).
6. **Instruments already exist.** Fig 6 :35: *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Matter-wave interferometry and mechanical noise measurements already return unresolved gravity-correlated signals. The status is **unreached**, not `unreachable`, and certainly not `grounded`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that gravity has been shown to cause collapse. No experiment reported here pays for that term.
- Not that a particular collapse model (Diósi-Penrose, GRW, CSL) is the answer. The framework returns a shape; the name is a provenanced binding from physics, with zero verdict weight until the ground settles it.
- Not that gravity-induced decoherence has a particular rate. That quantity is `gap` at figure level.
- Not that decoherence settles whether gravity is quantised. A world source states the opposite: *"decoherence alone cannot certify that gravity is quantized"* (Matsui 2026), and this is corroboration only.
- Not a derivation of one claim from the other. Their compound has its own loop and its own ground.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*; every room closes some of the gap and no finite number arrives.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Source test.** Which coupling produces the observed visibility loss: internal time dilation, a graviton bath, stochastic metric fluctuations, or a genuine collapse term? An instrument that distinguishes these names the source.
- **Ground test.** Is there a spontaneous localisation event beyond standard quantum predictions, with gravity as its source? Bassi et al. 2013 conclude that ongoing experiments *"can verify or rule out the proposed stochastic modification of quantum theory"*.
- **Non-interferometric test.** Donadi et al. 2026 show heating and continuous position measurements constrain decoherence models. A tighter bound that fires would settle the channel half.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4:** ground decay without an unobserved carrier; a figure re-census in which the anchor updates; a collapse operator derived with no imported atom; decoherence shown to certify quantisation; no instrument returning any signal at any resolution.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :34-35 distinguishes a proto-instrument that is already returning something from one that has never registered anywhere. Here instruments are already returning signals that fail to resolve the relevant rate. The six-term substrate is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that gravity has been shown to collapse the wavefunction. It says the question is two claims in one sentence, and they must be unbundled. Decoherence is a channel effect: phase information goes into degrees of freedom you did not track, so visibility falls while the ground of the dynamics is untouched (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Collapse is a claim that the ground itself changed, which is a new atom that must be imported from physics with provenance, not derived from the six-term set. Written over the same reduced-state atoms, the two are a testable isomer claim: same atoms, different coupling, different substance. The framework refuses to name a collapse model; it returns a shape, and the name comes from an instrument. Instruments already exist, so this is unreached rather than unreachable. What is offered is the split, the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The source stays open until the ground settles it.
