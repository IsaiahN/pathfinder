## Four things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Decoherence does not decide quantisation.** Matsui 2026: *"decoherence alone cannot certify that gravity is quantized."* This is the world-side reason to unbundle F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. It is corroboration only; the figure verdict comes from Fig 9 :54 and Fig 10 :24.
2. **Channel decay without an external bath.** Pikovski et al. 2015 derive decoherence *"even without any external environment"* through coupling between internal degrees of freedom and the centre of mass. This is a channel effect in the figure's sense: the phase goes into degrees of freedom you did not track.
3. **Collapse is a separate, falsifiable dynamics.** Bassi et al. 2013 review gravity induced collapse as a stochastic nonlinear modification and conclude the experiments *"can verify or rule out"* it. This supports F3: a new atom is owed, and the ground has not paid.
4. **The effect may be small.** Buza and Toroš 2026 report that graviton-induced decoherence *"remains small for realistic parameters"*, while Donadi et al. 2026 show non-interferometric probes can already constrain models below the Planck time. These are instrument facts, not figure verdicts; the magnitude is `gap` at figure level (F5).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described the unbundled residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split, the channel claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 9 :27** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if gravity-induced decoherence were a channel effect before showing where the claim came from.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Record a stage-1 verdict before showing any theory; never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, which is allowed, because a generator is not an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the unbundled residual and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure for each half; (3) record that answer; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the split (channel claim versus ground claim), the two separate instruments, and the pre-registered refuter. No theory, no links, no request for agreement or trust.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links: the local figure extract, the local formula, and the Bassi et al. review. Their stage-1 verdict is already on record, so their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter *before* running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently drafted it does: F1 and F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are repaired in the split and the channel/ground distinction is stated with its refuter. If a reader fires any item in §4 of `01`, the packet is withdrawn or narrowed before stage 2 is offered.
