## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what changes when the risks that diversification is meant to average away become correlated across financial, ecological or infrastructure networks. Option A: usefulness is a function of the number of named parts, so adding parts always helps and correlation is a second-order detail. Option B: usefulness is a function of the number of independent parts, so correlation erodes the very thing being bought, the erasure has a valence by purpose, and the source that correlates the parts stays open until an instrument names it. What each leads to: A keeps the common practice of counting holdings, species or links and treats a stress failure as an exception; B sends a checkable shape, four refuters, and a clear list of what a ground reading would have to measure.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per-slot gap between predicted and realised loss. Arity: `k >= 2` slots, so a union of slots inside any one network. Symmetry: slots are interchangeable for counting under a symmetric conjunction, but their correlation is a relation of shared provenance, not a property carried by one slot. Scale: first order inside a network, second and third order at the shared source and its cascade. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" govern the metric: a single aggregate variance is the average in which the localising signal is lost.
- **Domain 1, financial networks.** Actors: holdings, investors, leveraged intermediaries, a common factor. Currency: capital and realised return. Anchor: a market and its counters, realised losses (Fig 2 :29 names "A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"). Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 2, ecological networks.** Actors: species, functional groups, a shared stressor. Currency: biomass, persistence, ecosystem function. Anchor: measured survival and function under perturbation. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 3, infrastructure networks.** Actors: nodes, links, common-mode sources such as shared supply or control software. Currency: service continuity and flow. Anchor: measured uptime and service delivered. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 4, framework-internal independence law.** Actors: frames, evidence pools, anchors. Currency: evidence, never agreement. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Nameable anchor: yes. This domain supplies the governing lines and is not averaged into the applied verdicts.
- **Partition or union.** Across domains, a partition by actors / currency / anchor; only the cast changes (Fig 4 :19-20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"). Inside each domain, diversification is a union of slots, and heavy overlap turns it into one frame with several names, so it must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again").
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive slots before aggregating; acquire terms for overlap and common cause before setting the objective; count effective bets before buying independence; carry the method with a measured round trip. The correlation link is the middle link of the dependency chain with no instrument.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :40 (correlation disqualifying for a verdict) and the fetched systemic-risk source (connectivity is a shock-absorber up to a tipping point) do not conflict after re-census. The figure governs a valence rule about a verdict; the world source describes a quantitative budget with a turning point. Different objects. One live strain remains, reported in VERDICT: correlation is not one of the seven typed bonds, so the isomer claim in file 06 is held at the functional-group level.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the quantities do not exist in the figures and are not claimed. Usefulness of diversification falls as overlap rises because a union of heavily overlapping frames is one frame with several names, so the effective bet count, not the name count, is the currency. Correlated risk is the hidden-common-cause collapse in which parts that never talk fail together. The effect is conditional on purpose: disqualifying for a verdict about independent exposure, productive for a convention about shared meaning, so the unqualified sentence "correlation always destroys diversification" is refused and recorded as a retracted earlier reading in file 01. Independence is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong, as a budget filter rather than a verdict. The shared source in a given system stays `???` until an instrument names it. This is a `general` shape applied at `specified` level to three domains, ungrounded, and candidate. Per-domain verdicts: D1 triangulated, D2 triangulated, D3 triangulated, D4 triangulated, numeric quantities gap.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoided by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The numeric gap can be filled by a measurement without withdrawing the shape; a domain finding that fails its refuter is removed without touching the other three; no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating stress measurement naming the common cause would close the `???` slot; a collection whose loss reduction fails to fall with instrumented overlap would break F1 and F3; a convention-function made worse by agreement or a verdict-function made better by perfect correlation would break F4; a first-order correlation audit that predicts joint losses as well as full enumeration would break F6. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
