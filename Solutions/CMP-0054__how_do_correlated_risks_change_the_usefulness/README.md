## The question restated in effect terms

The question asks what happens to a specific property, the usefulness of diversification, when a specific relation changes, the correlation among the risks that diversification is meant to average away. The three named networks (financial, ecological, infrastructure) are not three different questions. They are three instances of one shape: many slots, each carrying a loss residual, combined so that a loss in one slot is offset by a non-loss in another.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a relation between the **overlap** among slots (how much they draw on one hidden source) and the **effective number of independent bets** they deliver. The answer must say which way the relation runs, why it has a valence (it can be productive for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict), and what would settle it at the ground. Each option leads somewhere different: if usefulness tracks the count of slots, then more holdings always help and correlation is a detail; if usefulness tracks independent slots, then correlation erodes the very thing being bought, and it erodes it fastest where the cost of being wrong is highest.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Correlation is the failure being studied.** Fig 2 :39: "Correlation has valence." A campaign that selects for agreement would instantiate, inside the outreach, the same common-cause collapse the solution warns about (Fig 2 :6, :18-21).
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." So the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
