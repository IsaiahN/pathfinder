## Wider literature that bears on the question

These were encountered inside the fetched pages and are recorded as leads, not as sources read this session. They are named so a checker can locate the relevant ground quickly.

- Harry Markowitz, "Portfolio Selection", Journal of Finance, 1952, the origin of the covariance-based account of diversification.
- Longin and Solnik, 1995, on time-varying international equity correlations, and the broader literature that correlations rise in market stress.

- Judea Pearl's back-door / do-calculus framework, for the common-cause structure behind confounding.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source.
- Fig 2 :39: "Correlation has valence." A mass send selected for people who already agree manufactures the common-cause collapse the solution warns about.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :42 (a verifier who can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it) and Fig 2 :35 ("Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40), so the stage-1 verdict is recorded first and never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 says only methods cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** A frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` are written so that the check can be done without the theory. Nothing here was sent by this agent; hosting and sending are the owner's to do.
