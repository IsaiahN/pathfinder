## What this folder answers, in effect terms

The question decides between two bindings of the same settled observations (horizon entropy proportional to area, a Hawking temperature, the equivalence principle, the Einstein field equations). Option A binds them as: gravity is fundamental, and thermodynamics is a coarse-grained description derived from it. Option B binds them as: gravity is a composed term, and thermodynamics supplies the atoms. A correct answer must say what each option leads to, what shape any candidate must have, and what instrument would settle the choice. It must not silently pick a side by asserting that the framework has settled a world question.
