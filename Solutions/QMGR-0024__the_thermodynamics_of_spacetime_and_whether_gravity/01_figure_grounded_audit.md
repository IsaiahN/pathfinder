## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

The settled observations in this domain are: horizon entropy proportional to area (Bekenstein, Hawking); a horizon temperature; the equivalence principle; and the Einstein field equations. The unresolved question is how to bind them.

- **Option A (fundamental gravity).** The metric and its dynamics are primitive. Thermodynamic language is a coarse-grained, derivative description of a fundamentally geometric entity. Everything thermodynamic is composed out of gravitational atoms.
- **Option B (emergent gravity).** Entropy, temperature and information bearing are the atoms. Spacetime and the Einstein equation are composed terms. Gravity is a derivative, not a member of the fundamental furniture.
- **What each leads to.** A: the search target is a quantization of the metric; the wall is the lack of a microscopic atom for the metric. B: the search target is the closure in which thermodynamic atoms already sit; the wall is whether the Einstein equation is reachable from them, and which atom, if any, must be imported.
- **Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction).** Write down what an answer would look like so it can accrue a residual: an answer is a specified composition within `closure(Γ)` whose operands are held atoms and whose currency is a finite, ordered, spendable structure; it names no seventh primitive; and it is settled only by an instrument that discriminates fundamental from composed gravity. Without that binding, the preference "I want gravity to be emergent" is unbound, predicts nothing and cannot be refuted (Fig 5 :62-63).

## 2. Findings

### F1 : Spacetime is not a primitive; "emergence" is the framework's default shape (decisive in D1)

Fig 13 :75:

> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Fig 13 :74:

> **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

The question "is gravity emergent from thermodynamics?" is therefore partly dissolved. Under the six-term set, a spacetime-like term is already a derivative: a state, a structure, a state. It is not a member of the minimum set. What the framework can say is that *if* a spacetime term composes at all, a structure must sit between the states; it cannot say which structure. This is a `general` structural claim, not a measurement.

- **Mode:** general for the shape; specified for the prediction that no direct state-state bond occurs.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 13 :74-75 (substrate composition rule) and Fig 12 :103-104 (recursion and level-relativity), two figures with low overlap of subject matter.
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five, or measure a direct state-state bond with no intervening structure. Either breaks the substrate and the finding is withdrawn.

### F2 : The continuum was removed as a seventh member, and thermodynamics is the test of the set (decisive in D1)

Fig 13 :44-45:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

Fig 13 :49-50:

> **"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard. Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none."**

This is the framework's most direct statement on the question. A smooth spacetime continuum is precisely the thing the substrate refuses to admit as a primitive, and the four laws of thermodynamics are the load-bearing test that the six-term set is built to pass. So thermodynamic behaviour is not an add-on pasted onto a geometric substrate; it is what the substrate is answerable to. This is figure-grounded support for the *shape* of Option B (spacetime as derivative), while leaving the world question (does nature do this?) open.

- **Mode:** general (structural), not grounded.
- **Status:** single-source (Fig 13 only) for the removal of the continuum; triangulated for the test claim by Fig 13 :49-50 and the Fig 13 :54-71 removal table, which name which law would fail without which member.
- **Refuter:** derive a seventh member from the six, or exhibit a law of thermodynamics for which every member of the six is idle. The second is the falsifier the figure itself offers (Fig 13 :143-144).

### F3 : Emergence is level-relative, not a property of the world's furniture (D1, D2)

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Fig 4 :33:

> **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

So "gravity is emergent" cannot mean "gravity is not real" or "there is a bottom beneath which nothing exists." It means that a term settled at one level becomes an atom for the next. The reciprocity is the point: a fundamental graviton at one level and a composed metric at another are different claims about composition, not different claims about existence. The philosophic distinction between weak and strong emergence is relevant here and is treated in `06_isomers_and_order.md` with a low-overlap source (IEP).

- **Mode:** general for the level rule; specified when applied to gravity.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 4 (cross-scale recursion and no-top rule, independent figure texts).
- **Refuter:** show a molecule that cannot serve as an atom for any composition, or a level at which the transform is invertible so that "irreducible" becomes absolute. That would break the recursion, not just this application.

### F4 : Reachability is the real question, and more search cannot answer it (D2)

Fig 6 :21:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 8 :32:

> **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

Two sub-cases follow, and they are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). If entropy, temperature and area are atoms already in the held set, then the Einstein equation may be in `closure(Γ)`: Jacobson 1995 shows that it composes given `δQ = TdS` and entropy proportional to area. If a microscopic substrate (holographic bits, entanglement structure) is required, that is an atom from a frame whose closure differs, and the import must pass the source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 import panel). Publishing another entropic-gravity paper that assumes equipartition and a holographic screen adds search inside one closure, not an atom.

- **Mode:** specified (a reachability claim about a named closure, not a measurement).
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8 (what moves the wall, and why nothing else can). The physics-facing sub-case is single-source (Jacobson) and unmeasured here.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a derivation of the field equations from thermodynamic atoms inside the closure with no imported microscopic atom (then the import is unnecessary), or exhibit a term that provably cannot compose from the held atoms and whose absence is demonstrated rather than merely unfound (Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*).

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: Seat Map :287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or a legitimate seventh member is exhibited (breaks Fig 13 :41-44).
2. A spacetime term is measured to bond directly, with no intervening structure (breaks Fig 13 :75).
3. A law of thermodynamics is found for which no member of the six is load-bearing (breaks Fig 13 :49-50).
4. The field equations are shown to require an atom that is neither held in the thermodynamic closure nor supplied by a provenanced import (breaks the reachability claim in F4; the term is then genuinely unreachable, and importing against that limit would add an unneeded atom, Fig 8 :41-43).
5. A source-discriminating instrument settles the world question decisively against composition (Option A at the ground). This does not refute the shape; it ends the question as a world claim and is recorded as such.

If 1 to 4 fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of the world claim may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Strain test and re-census

A figure line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so re-census rather than soften the reading.

- **Strong versus weak emergence (live strain, named).** "Emergent gravity" in the literature can mean weak emergence (derivable from a fine-grained base but novel at the macro level) or strong emergence (irreducible with novel causal powers, IEP). The figures' recursion rule (Fig 12 :104) and no-top rule (Fig 4 :33) govern the weak reading. They are silent on strong emergence. Re-census: the framework's claim is about which level composes over which, not about ontological novelty. This is a scope limit, not a correction of any figure.
- **Which atom is entropy (live strain, named).** Fig 13 :20 defines spectrum as *"ordering, so more and less can be said"* and Fig 13 :22 defines gradient as *"a difference that can be spent"*. Entropy increase is ordering-like and temperature is gradient-like, but the mapping is a binding from the physics frame. Naming it as a figure verdict would convert a visible choice into an invisible one. Held as a hypothesis with provenance, not smoothed over.
- **Fig 13 versus F4 (resolved by re-census).** Fig 13 :74-75 governs composition of the six primitives; F4 governs reachability of a physics-frame term inside a closure. Different objects, so no contradiction.
- **After re-census: two strains, both named, none hidden.**
