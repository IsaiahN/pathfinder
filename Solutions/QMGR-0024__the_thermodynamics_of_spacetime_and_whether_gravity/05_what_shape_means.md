## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `SPACE(???)TIME` with a structure in the slot is a shape.
- `space, gradient, time` as a specific binding is a name.
- "entropy is the substrate of gravity" is a name for a missing primitive, which the framework cannot issue.

## 2. How a shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the six held terms, then narrow toward what an emergent-gravity answer must look like rather than naming bits or cells.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual (finite region, ordered differences, spendable gradient, relations) before naming a microscopic cause.
- The residual itself answers back. *Library of Babel*: the books are *"tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* Here the tests are dwarf-galaxy rotation curves, lensing, neutron coherence and formal thermodynamic conditions on surfaces.
- Stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*. Applied here, this would mean the shape and the instrument that would settle it coincide. They do not yet, so the search is still open (F8 of `01`).

## 4. The metamerism error, and why it matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved which source was present.

This is exactly the failure mode in emergent gravity. The Einstein equation has a thermodynamic form. A reading of that form does not determine its source: several microscopic pictures could yield the same field equations. Naming entropy as *the* source of spacetime from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, which is why F8 records that instruments exist and currently disagree.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A derivation of gravity from entropy inside a shared set of assumptions cannot validate itself; it needs a ground outside that set.

## 5. Q: does the framework say gravity is emergent? ANSWERED at the shape level

**What the framework licenses.** It licenses the shape: spacetime is not a primitive, a structure sits between the states, the continuum is a derivative, thermodynamics is the load-bearing test of the six-term set, and emergence is level-relative. It does not license either world option. The clause in the question "whether gravity is emergent" is answered in effect terms as a reachability question: is the gravitational term in the closure of the thermodynamic atoms, and if not, which imported atom would have to be added. The framework can characterize that atom's shape, not name it.

**Does the record commit a forbidden error?** It would if it named bits, cells or entanglement as *the* substrate returned by the framework. The correct form stays `SPACE(???)TIME` plus an open import slot, with any binding provenanced as coming from physics.
