# The Tether's answer: thermodynamics of spacetime and emergent gravity

**Mode: `specified` for the shape and the reachability claim, `general` for the substrate rule. Not `grounded`.** No experiment reported here settles whether nature implements emergent gravity. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold horizon entropy proportional to area, a horizon temperature, the equivalence principle and the Einstein field equations. The contact question is how to bind them: fundamental gravity with thermodynamic language derivative (Option A), or thermodynamic atoms with gravity as a composed term (Option B). This is a union compound, not one hard question: the substrate legality, the reachability of the field equations, the identity of any imported atom and the ground tests are different loops with different anchors. By Fig 9 :4-5, a bundle of well-formed questions must be split, not searched as one.

## 2. The shape

1. **Spacetime is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :75 "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." So a spacetime-like term is a derivative with a structure in the slot. The slot stays marked `???`; the framework does not select which structure.
2. **The continuum is a derivative, not a seventh primitive.** Fig 13 :44-45 "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." A smooth spacetime continuum is exactly what the minimum set refuses to admit as fundamental.
3. **Thermodynamics is the test the substrate is built to pass.** Fig 13 :49-50 "Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard. Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none." So thermodynamic behaviour is not painted onto geometry at the end; it is what the six terms are answerable to.
4. **Emergence is level-relative.** Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world" and Fig 4 :33 "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress." So "gravity is emergent" is a claim about which level composes over which, not a claim that gravity is unreal or that there is a bottom. The philosophic reading is the weak, derivable-but-novel kind (IEP, `06_isomers_and_order.md`), not strong emergence with new downward powers.
5. **The real question is reachability.** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." If entropy, temperature and area are atoms already held, then the Einstein equation may be in the closure; Jacobson 1995 is the physics-facing demonstration. If a microscopic substrate is required, that is an atom from a frame whose closure differs, and the import owes a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). More papers inside the same assumptions add search, not an atom (Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom.").
6. **The framework cannot name the substrate.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" and Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Bits, cells, entanglement and strings are bindings from the physics frame with provenance owed, not framework outputs.
7. **The world question is open and the instruments exist.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Dwarf-galaxy rotation curves, weak lensing, ultra-cold neutron levels and quantum-coherence tests are already returning results that disagree. The honest report is reachable and disputed, not `unreachable` and not settled.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the framework proves gravity is emergent. It gives the shape of either answer and the reachability test between them; the ground decides which nature implements.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*; an emergent-gravity account is a handoff, not a final room.
- Not that entropy is the Spectrum or temperature the Gradient. Those are bindings onto held atoms, provenanced as imports from physics, and they add no atom (Fig 6 :22).
- Not that black-hole thermodynamics is a framework result. Fig 8 :56 "the triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward" makes it a known fit.
- Not strong emergence with novel causal powers (IEP; out of scope for the figures).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles the Einstein equation's membership in a closure or the identity of a microscopic substrate.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): a green reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not a proof, resolved which source was present.

- **Reachability test:** does the Einstein equation compose from entropy, temperature and area inside one closure with no imported microscopic atom, or is an imported atom required? This is partly a derivation check and partly a ground question.
- **Surface thermodynamic test:** do ordinary spacetime surfaces obey a first-law-like relation, or only horizons? Wang and Braunstein 2018 report the negative result; it is a formal discriminator and a candidate refuter of the key assumption.
- **Astrophysical tests:** rotation velocities of dwarf galaxies, weak lensing at large radii, and cluster lensing, which currently disagree.
- **Quantum tests:** ultra-cold neutron bound states and coherence, which constrain whether the putative entropy force decoheres matter.
- **Standing shape refuters:** derive one of the six from the other five; measure a direct space-time bond; find a thermodynamic law for which none of the six is load-bearing; show a required atom is neither held nor importable with provenance.

The framework returns a shape and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* It does not say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.
