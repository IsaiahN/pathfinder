## Three things in the world literature that bear directly on the question

1. **The composition limit is already a live result.** Padmanabhan 2004 proves, inside a specific theoretical closure, that iterating the free graviton action cannot produce the full Einstein-Hilbert action. Mapped to the framework, this is a reachability failure, not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*). It supports F4 without deciding Option A versus Option B.
2. **There are discriminating instruments, and they disagree.** The encyclopedic synthesis records positive lensing results and negative dwarf-galaxy and formal results. So this is not an unreachable-at-budget question (F8). The right report is disputed and reachable.
3. **"Emergence" is ambiguous.** IEP separates weak from strong emergence. The figures' recursion rule (Fig 12 :104) governs the weak reading only. This is a scope limit to declare, not a strain to hide (§6 of `01`).
