## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between what each of four parties predicts from the legal order and what the ground returns. Arity: a distribution over parties and a separate distribution over pairwise relations, ordered in time. Symmetry: not symmetric; "distribution" is a threshold comparison (`⋛`), not a conserved subtraction (`−`). Scale: four scales (employment relation, firm, sector, polity) separated by a lossy membrane. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57).
- **Domain 1, the rule-making frame.** Actors: legislatures, regulators. Currency: the description in bits. Anchor: enacted text. Question type: convention, not verdict.
- **Domain 2, adjudication and inspection.** Actors: courts, inspectorates, arbitrators. Currency: actions, rulings, sanctions. Anchor: decision record. Question type: where the two currencies meet.
- **Domain 3, the industrial-relations habitat.** Actors: employers, workers, unions. Currency: wages, conditions, bargaining power. Anchor: realized outcomes. Question type: enumerated habitat.
- **Domain 4, the civic frame.** Actors: citizens and movements. Currency: voice and mobilisation. Anchor: elections and enacted policy. Question type: a differing closure, the source of imports.
- **Domain 5, the cross-scale channel.** Actors: instruments and metrics. Currency: contact and R_T. Anchor: the measured round trip. Compound of D1 and D2.
- **Exclusions.** "Power" as a free-floating substance has no anchor independent of outcomes, so it is not a domain yet. "Legal institutions" as one actor dissolves into D1 and D2, which have different closures and grounds.

Order by dependency: perceive the legal signal and the workplace, acquire terms for the affordances, state what counts as progress (a goal is a comparison), plan, then learn and carry. The middle links have no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

My census matches the primary's five domains, two exclusions and union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 per-slot indexing and Fig 11 habitat, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's two-currency rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :39-40/:61-62 (habitat, introduction), Fig 10 :46-47 (convention versus verdict), Fig 12 :125-130 (two currencies), Fig 9 :32 (shape not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: law changes the habitat, not the actors; rights and enforcement are two currencies; law is grammar not verdict; the checker is a shape, not a name.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional claim, for example "a labour inspectorate with power to sanction moves worker power".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor invariance of any nameable office** (Fig 2 :29-30; an inspectorate that moves when a minister pushes on it is not an anchor);
- the **instrument-from-worse-instrument rule** (Fig 6 :32, *"An instrument is not built from a description"*), which the shorthand "add enforcement" skips;
- the **operationalised slot denominator** (Fig 1 :19/:22), without which the "power" measurement silently becomes an average;
- the **import provenance and shadow test** (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52), which a named office would owe and which the shorthand omits.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the checker must stand outside the case it decides, and a right on paper is not the same reading as an enforced outcome. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
