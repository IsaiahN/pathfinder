## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about how legal institutions shape the distribution of power between the state, employers, workers and citizens. Option A: power is a stock that law divides among four parties, so a reform is successful when the text grants more to one party. Option B: legal institutions set the bonds and the habitat in which the four parties act, and a change of text moves outcomes only when it changes contact with the ground, with the four party-slots and their pairwise relations kept separate. What each leads to: A produces a scalar rank and conflates a right on paper with its enforcement; B produces a per-slot shape, two currencies held apart, a provenance requirement, and an open slot for the checker that no figure names.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot gap between what a party predicts from the legal order and what the ground returns: R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|, indexed by party and by pairwise relation. Arity: a four-place distribution plus a distribution over relations, ordered in time. Symmetry: not symmetric, since the state and a worker are not interchangeable, and "distribution" is a threshold comparison rather than a conserved subtraction. Scale: the employment relation, the firm, the sector and the polity, with a lossy membrane between them. Fig 9 calls it a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the rule-making frame.** Actors: legislatures, regulators, standard setters. Currency: the description in bits (statutes, rights, procedures, definitions). Anchor: the enacted text, an official counter. Question type: a convention, not a verdict.
- **Domain 2, the adjudicative and inspection frame.** Actors: courts, inspectorates, agencies, arbitrators. Currency: the actions, rulings, sanctions, remedies. Anchor: the decision record. Question type: a binding from the text onto held atoms, and the place where the two currencies meet.
- **Domain 3, the industrial-relations habitat.** Actors: employers, workers, unions, managers, contractors. Currency: contracts, wages, conditions, bargaining power. Anchor: realized wages, conditions, agreements. Question type: a habitat enumerated from contact, not composed.
- **Domain 4, the civic and political frame.** Actors: citizens, movements, parties. Currency: voice, vote, mobilisation, agenda change. Anchor: elections, enacted policy, recorded mobilisation. Question type: a differing closure from which legal imports arrive.
- **Domain 5, the cross-scale channel.** Actors: instruments and metrics. Currency: contact with the ground, and the round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: the measured round trip. This is the compound of D1 and D2 across the membrane.
- **Excluded before figure lines.** "Power" as a free-floating substance has no anchor independent of the outcomes, so it is not a domain yet. "Legal institutions" as one monolithic actor dissolves into D1 and D2, which have different closures and different grounds.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 overlap (legislation supplies the grammar courts apply), D2 and D3 overlap (enforcement happens in the workplace), D3 and D4 overlap (outcomes feed mobilisation). The whole question is a union of overlapping conditions, so it must be unbundled into C1 description, C2 actions and C3 habitat before search (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the legal signal and the workplace, acquire terms for what the situation affords, state what would count as progress (a goal is a comparison), plan (organise, draft, litigate, inspect), then learn and carry (precedent). The middle links, vocabulary and objective, have no instrument and break first (Fig 3 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the aggregate ranking does not. Power must be indexed per party and per relation. Legal institutions act by changing the habitat, introducing actors, conditions and relations, and by keeping the channel from text to outcome reachable. Law is a grammar that makes disputes statable, not a verdict about who is right. A right on paper and its enforcement are two currencies that do not add. The four parties are atoms and legal bonds decide the substance; regimes with the same parties and different bonds are isomers in the strong reading and share a functional group in the cautious one. New legal categories arrive by import from a differing closure, with provenance separating convergence from borrowing. Precedent is a room chain whose progress needs the ground voting. No standing list of powerful actors survives a change of residual. Which named office closes a given slot is a `gap`, reported as unreached. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim figure lines and refuters before the conclusion, and it records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and recording the reader's verdict before any theory is shown.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregate score, the conflation of rights with enforcement, the convention-as-verdict reading and any unprovenanced named checker are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no atom has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A currency-discriminating instrument showing that rights and enforcement can be validly summed; a legal change that moves outcomes with every actor, condition, relation and instrument held fixed; a frame expanding its reach by internal composition alone across a habitat change; a precedent chain with ground-verified novelty and unchanged closure; or a residual-independent ranking of the four parties that predicts per-slot outcomes across regimes. Any of these fires a pre-registered refuter. Until one does, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
