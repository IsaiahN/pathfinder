## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "an outside checker standing in a closure that differs from both parties, ranging against a record neither can move" is a shape. "An ombudsperson", "a labour inspectorate" or "the courts" are names. The first is checkable; the last three are imports that still owe a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).

This is why the question's four parties must stay as slots. "State, employers, workers, citizens" are names in the question, and they have to be bound as ordinary terms to be checkable. "Power" is the sought-for shape and is not bindable until it is stated in effect terms as a per-slot residual (Fig 1 :19).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :54-58 in the essay's own words and is the reason `01` splits the question into C1 (description), C2 (actions) and C3 (habitat).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: choosing to measure "wage outcomes per relation" is a visible choice of compression axis, not a discovery of what power really is. The choice must be declared so a later reader can revise it. This is why `01` reports the anchor per slot instead of claiming one true power index.

## 4. The source-provenance error in this domain

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

For this question the analogue is precise. A statute reading and a court ruling can both be cited as "worker protection". One is a description paid for in bits, the other an action paid for at the ground (Fig 12 :125-130). A count of the first cannot tell you which produced the second. Naming "worker power" from the statute count is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* records the same point as provenance loss: a video frame and a photograph are *"indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* A legal right won by convergent derivation and one borrowed by import are indistinguishable in the text; only the lineage separates them (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 5. Q  -  What does "shape the distribution" mean, canonically?

**Answered: it means changing the arrangement of typed bonds in the habitat, not transferring a quantity between four containers.**

- The four parties are atoms; legal institutions supply the bonds (rights, duties, procedures, contracts, franchises). Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- The change has to reach contact to count. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Fig 11 :39 and :40 and adds that *"a newborn agent is made capable by the surroundings it is born into"*. The same is the institutional claim: changing a party's label without changing what it can perceive and do does not move R.
- The effect is per relation. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* records *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times"* and Fig 1's warning that *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A national "power balance" is the aggregate that loses the live slot.
- The institution that carries a rule from text to outcome is a channel, and the channel can fail while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24). The measurable object is the round-trip gap R_T (Fig 4 :30), not the count of laws.

**Does the folder commit the error the README forbids?** No, provided the checker slot stays open. `01` R4 declares a `gap` where a name would be convenient, and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` states the shape of an outside checker without appointing one. If a later revision names an office without a source gate and shadow test, it would breach the shape-not-a-name rule and must be retracted.

## 6. Two consequences for the answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* A survey that gathers famous legal scholars to agree that a reform empowered workers is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an inspectorate record, a court decision, a wage series, settle it. The same essay: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

**Conventions are the product, agreement is not the verdict.** Fig 2 :41 makes agreement about shared meaning the product in a convention-heavy domain like law. This is why lawyers must agree on what "employee" means before any case can be heard. But Fig 2 :40 and Seat Map :163 make the same agreement worthless as evidence about world-facing power when the frames share an evidence pool. The distinction is carried into every file: the two-stage offer records a verdict before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the institutional claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I first wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any of the four parties or any legal bond. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"a **missing** thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. The parties and their bonds are already in the domain's library; naming them is a binding from the labour-law frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is naming an *outside checker* for a specific residual without provenance, because that atom is not held. The correction is recorded rather than quietly made: the parties and bonds may be named and declared as choices; the checker stays a `gap`. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 13 :134-135).
