## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how schools and universities shape what a society regards as basic, authoritative or useful knowledge. Option A: schooling is a neutral conduit that delivers knowledge whose standing is fixed elsewhere. Option B: schooling is a membrane, a convention-authoring seat and a habitat substitute that confer standing in three different ways. What each leads to: A leaves the standing gap unexplained and treats a canon as a recording; B gives three separate mechanisms, four written refuters and an open strain about real institutions. The three predicates are a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), not one question and not three independent ones.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The standing-conferral gap between knowledge without formal schooling and knowledge with it. Arity: three overlapping predicates, so a union with compounds at the intersections. Symmetry: none; each is a directed conferral from institution to knowledge to society. Scale: two, the single learner's step-by-step loop and the population-level library with a ranking rule. Fig 4 is the two-scale machine.
- **Domain 1, transmission.** Actors: one learner, the population, the curriculum as library, the teacher as membrane keeper. Currency: head starts versus recordings, measured as search cost for the next agent. Anchor: reapplicability in a new habitat, plus the ground for any settlement. Question type: partition of what crosses up (methods) from what crosses down (head starts).
- **Domain 2, conferral.** Actors: the institution as seat, credentials, canons, peer frames, examiners. Currency: authority and credibility, which are frame-internal. Anchor: the ground, which does not move when the institution pushes. Question type: partition of convention from verdict.
- **Domain 3, selection.** Actors: ideas, methods and instruments introduced; conditions; relations; the ambient habitat. Currency: arrival, survival and cascades. Anchor: the ground, which decides what survives contact. Question type: union of a substituted habitat over an unauthored one.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the conduit reading before minting a conferral mechanism (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2); settle transmission before conferral and conferral before usefulness; measure the round trip at each handoff (Fig 4 :30).
- **Partition or union verdict.** The three predicates are a union. Where a credential also lowers search cost, that overlap is a compound with its own loop, not an average of two verdicts.

Strain test applied: Fig 10 :47 (a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one) strains against real institutions, which must decide outcomes. Re-census does not dissolve it: Fig 10 :53-54 says a prohibition nothing can satisfy is stated wrong. The strain is carried forward, not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives in three separate verdicts. D1: schooling controls what makes a next search cheaper, so "basic" is a head start and "useful" is knowledge plus habitat. D2: "authoritative" is a convention that can become a verdict when it decides an outcome without a reachable anchor, and frame-produced credibility cannot ground it. D3: the classroom substitutes a habitat, introduction is available and exclusion is not, and an exam proves wiring rather than capability. Curricular selection is a filter for the budget and must not become a witness. The three conditions form a union with compounds at the intersections. The conduit reading, the final canon and the promotion of consensus into corroboration are refused. All of this is `specified` and ungrounded. One strain remains live: the absolute form of Fig 10 :47 describes nearly every real university, so the open question is whether a credential can decide outcomes with the ground still reachable.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and the live strain before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three domain verdicts can be corrected independently; none depends on withdrawing a primitive. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. The strain can be answered by an institutional design without changing the figure reading.
- **reversed by:** a discriminator, not agreement. A controlled comparison of head-start versus recording curricula in an unseen habitat would settle D1. A multi-institution credential study against an unmoving anchor would settle D2. A transfer study with enumerated introduced actors would settle D3. A search that finds a genuinely final canon, or a derivation of one of the six figure primitives from the other five, would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
