# The Tether's answer: how schooling shapes what counts as knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No historical measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The gap between the standing knowledge would have without formal schooling and the standing it has once schools select, credential and transmit it. The three predicates in the question form a **union of overlapping conditions** (Fig 9 :54-57), not three independent questions and not one hard question. Each overlap is a compound with its own loop, so the verdicts below are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, basic knowledge: a head start, not a recording.** Fig 4 :11-12: "a head start, never a recording" and "it makes the next agent's search cheaper." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." So "basic" names the knowledge that has been compressed far enough to lower the search cost of the next learner. A curriculum made of recordings produces a system that can repeat a past success and cannot produce a new one. Fig 4 :34-35: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." Usefulness is therefore a property of the knowledge *and the habitat*, never of the item alone. **Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 4 and Fig 11 :39), low overlap within the framework; still not independent corroboration.

**D2, authoritative knowledge: a convention that can silently become a verdict.** Fig 10 :45-47: the seat "may author what has no truth value and nothing that does", but "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A grading scale, a canon, a definition of a field: these make disagreement statable. The failure is a convention that starts deciding who is employable or which claim counts, while remaining unchecked against anything it did not author. Credibility is frame-internal and cannot score the frame (Fig 1 :26-27: "predicates minted, and credibility accrued" and "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."). Agreement inside a canon is efficient for a convention, not a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"). **Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 10, Fig 1, Fig 2), with one live strain named in §4.

**D3, useful knowledge: a cascade in a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." A school introduces ideas, methods and instruments; it cannot exclude the ambient habitat. Usefulness is a cascade property of the combined habitat, including what was unintentionally introduced. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." An exam proves the wiring of the harness, not capability in the unmaintained habitat. **Verdict: single-source** for the habitat law (Fig 11), with outcome-side corroboration from the transfer literature.

**Cross-cutting: selection is a filter, never a witness.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." No society can teach everything, so selection is permitted and unavoidable. Promotion of exclusion into non-knowledge is the failure. Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." A canon handed down unchanged compounds agreement, not evidence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that schooling is a neutral conduit. That reading cannot account for the standing gap and is refused before minting (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2).
- Not that any one predicate explains the other two. They have different anchors and are not averaged.
- Not that a credential is an import that adds new knowledge. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
- Not that canonical consensus is corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not that a canon is final. Fig 7 names "the limit: no last room", and Fig 13 :118 "infinite effort inside a finite reach."
- Not `grounded`. No historical measurement settles `left(R,φ)` here.

## 4. The live strain

Fig 10 :47 says a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one. Read literally, that describes nearly every real university, which must decide admission, graduation and hiring. Fig 10 :53-54 warns: "a prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong" and asks what would have to be true for a real occupant to meet the absolute. The honest form is a question, not a rule: can a credential decide an outcome while the ground remains reachable? This strain is not dissolved in this derivation. It is sent to `11_audit.md` and stated in the outreach packet.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the model: a spectrometer resolved what an argument about green could not, because "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." Here:

1. **Basic:** a controlled comparison of head-start curricula against recording curricula, measuring novel capability in a habitat the learner has not seen. This fires refuter 1.
2. **Authoritative:** a multi-institution study of credentials whose authority is checked against an anchor that does not move when the institution pushes. A change of institution with authority unchanged, against an unmoving anchor, fires refuter 2.
3. **Useful:** a transfer study measuring capability loss when an item moves to a novel habitat, with the introduced actors enumerated. Zero loss with no unenumerated actor fires refuter 3.
4. **Closure:** any final canon that successfully closes inquiry, or a curriculum that grows while its reach grows with it, fires refuter 4.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure vocabulary is a visible guess at the compression axes of this domain, not nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books* says: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the available instruments can no longer distinguish a convention from a verdict, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (`05_what_shape_means.md` §2). "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that schools merely deliver knowledge whose standing is already fixed. It says three different things about three overlapping conditions. "Basic" names the knowledge compressed into a head start that makes a next learner's search cheaper, not a recording of a past success. "Authoritative" names a convention authored by an institution that can silently become a verdict about the world once it decides an outcome while remaining unchecked against anything the institution did not author. "Useful" names a cascade in a habitat the school substituted, not a property of the knowledge item alone, so an exam proves wiring and never capability. Curricular selection is a budget filter and must not be promoted into a witness. The framework returns a shape with four written refuters, not a history and not a verdict. The live strain is that real universities must decide outcomes, so the question is whether a credential can do that with the ground still reachable. Until a discriminator answers it, the claim stays `specified` and ungrounded.
