# Expert verification packet: the Tether on schooling and the standing of knowledge

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian of education can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No historical measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Schooling does not merely deliver knowledge whose standing is fixed elsewhere. For three overlapping conditions it does three different things. "Basic" names the knowledge compressed into a head start that makes a next learner's search cheaper, not a recording of a past success. "Authoritative" names a convention authored by an institution that can silently become a verdict about the world once it decides an outcome while remaining unchecked against anything the institution did not author. "Useful" names a cascade in a habitat the school substituted, not a property of the knowledge item alone, so an exam proves wiring and never capability. Curricular selection is a budget filter and must not be promoted into a witness. The three conditions form a union, not a partition, and the framework returns a shape with four written refuters, not a history and not a verdict. The live strain is that real universities must decide outcomes, so the operative question is whether a credential can do that with the ground still reachable.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and pedagogy-facing; checkable by reading Figure 4 and the transfer literature

- **Statement:** what crosses a school-to-learner boundary should be a head start that lowers the cost of a next search, not a recording of a past success. Usefulness is a property of knowledge and habitat together.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :11-12: "a head start, never a recording" and "it makes the next agent's search cheaper." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 4 :34-35: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."
- **What to check:** does the curriculum being examined transmit a reapplicable method or a replay of one occasion? Does its usefulness survive being moved to a habitat the learner has not seen? The transfer literature calls the pedagogical counterpart "bridging"; its own admission that transfer's prevalence and existence are contested is consistent with the figure's warning, not a conflict with it.
- **Refuter:** knowledge attains "basic" standing in a society with no formal transmission institution and the same search cost as in a schooled society; or an item transfers to a novel habitat with no loss of capability and no unenumerated introduced actor.

### C2 - framework-internal and institutional; checkable by reading Figures 10, 1 and 2

- **Statement:** an institution legitimately authors conventions, but a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one; credibility produced by the frame cannot score the frame; agreement inside a canon builds shared meaning, not a verdict about the world.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :46: "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."
- **What to check:** does the credentialing practice being examined keep a reachable check against something it did not author, or has its own certificate become the measure of the knowledge? Fig 10 :53-54 warns: "a prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong." So the check is not whether an institution decides outcomes, which it must, but whether the ground stays reachable while it does.
- **Refuter:** a credential that keeps its authority across a change of institution while being continuously checked against an anchor that demonstrably does not move when the institution pushes.

### C3 - framework-internal and ecological; checkable by reading Figure 11 and Figure 9

- **Statement:** a classroom is a substituted habitat in which introduction is available and exclusion is not; an exam is a harness that proves wiring, never capability; curricular selection is a budget filter that must not hand a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** which ambient actors remain in contact with the learner despite the institution, which actors were introduced with unenumerated cascades, and whether a harness result is being treated as capability. The NDEA is a documented instance of a filter with a stated goal: "Latin and Greek programs are not funded under this title, on the grounds that they are not modern foreign languages, and thus do not support defense needs."
- **Refuter:** an examination result that transfers to capability in a novel habitat with no residue loss; or a society in which curricular exclusion leaves excluded knowledge's public standing unchanged with a separate witness channel functioning.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that schooling is a neutral conduit, and not that any single predicate explains the others.
- Not that a credential is an import that adds new knowledge. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
- Not that canonical consensus is corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not that a canon is final. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that the figure set is nature's own list of axes.
- Not that Fig 10 :47 is satisfied by any real institution. It is a live strain, stated in §1 and offered for testing.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument:

1. A controlled comparison of head-start curricula against recording curricula, measuring novel capability in an unseen habitat.
2. A multi-institution study of credentials against an anchor that does not move when the institution pushes.
3. A transfer study enumerating introduced actors and measuring capability loss across habitats.
4. A search for a final canon that actually closes inquiry, or for a curriculum whose reach grows with its size.
