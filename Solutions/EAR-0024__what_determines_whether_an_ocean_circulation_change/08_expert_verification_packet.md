# Expert verification packet: the Tether on gradual, abrupt and reversible ocean circulation change

**What this is:** a claim in a form an oceanographer or dynamical-systems scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the relevant resolution is claimed. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Gradual, abrupt, reversible" is a union of conditions, not one dial. Continuity versus abruptness is set by the sign of the dominant feedback cycle against a threshold; reversibility is set by basin structure (one basin returns, two basins need a reverse threshold); the rate of forcing is a third determinant because fast forcing can tip without crossing a critical level and fast reversal can tip on the way back. Verdicts are per circulation cell and depth, never a basin-wide average. The framework returns this shape, not a numerical threshold, a critical rate, or a yes/no on today's AMOC. Only sustained observation can settle those.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - The union framing (framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9)

- **Statement:** the three adjectives form a union of at least three overlapping conditions with different anchors, not a partition with one governing variable; verdicts are kept per condition and never averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* and Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Per-slot: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** can a single circulation change be simultaneously abrupt and irreversible, or gradual yet irreversible on a human timescale? If yes, one variable cannot order all three adjectives.
- **Refuter:** all observed changes lie on one continuous axis with a single governing parameter and no intersections requiring a second condition.

### C2 - Continuity set by feedback cycle sign against a threshold (physics-facing)

- **Statement:** a stabilising (negative) feedback makes the response track the forcing; an amplifying (positive) feedback compounds a remainder so that crossing a threshold produces a jump. The salt-advection loop is the named positive loop, given as a binding from ocean science.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 13 :32 *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos"* and the comparison test Fig 12 :87 *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"*
- **World check:** Stommel 1961 two stable regimes; Rahmstorf 2005 *"all models ... show a clear hysteresis response"* driven by *"a basin-scale positive feedback"*; Lenton 2021 *"reinforcing (positive) feedbacks ... take over from stabilising (negative) feedbacks."*
- **Refuter:** an abrupt change with a stabilising dominant feedback, no threshold crossing and no rate effect.

### C3 - Reversibility set by basin structure; reversal is wall-moving (dynamical-systems facing)

- **Statement:** one basin means the original state is reachable and the system returns; two basins mean the current closure cannot reach it internally, so reversal requires a forcing that crosses a reverse threshold. "Import" is used as a reachability shape, not as a physical substance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"* and Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
- **World check:** Stommel 1961, the jumped state *"would then stay there even when A was restored to its original value"*; Lenton et al. 2008 report collapse reversible in some models and irreversible in others; Rahmstorf 2005 hysteresis in all tested models. Rate caveat: Ritchie et al. 2023 and Chapman et al. 2024.
- **Refuter:** a two-basin model that returns to the original basin on forcing removal without crossing a reverse threshold, or a critical forcing level invariant across slow and fast ramps.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a freshwater threshold, critical rate, collapse date, or verdict on whether today's AMOC has tipped.
- Not that variance and lag-1 autocorrelation are reliable early-warning signals; van Westen et al. 2024 find them window-sensitive and Ben-Yami et al. 2024 find tipping-time extrapolation unreliable.
- Not that all models agree; they share an evidence pool, which is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency. It cannot settle the question.
- Not that "salt-advection feedback" or "saddle-node" is a new primitive from the framework. Both are bindings from other frames onto held atoms, recorded as such.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument (Hard Problem is About Source, canonical mapping):

1. A sustained observing record long enough to see whether the circulation returns to its prior basin after a perturbation (RAPID 26°N, SAMBA 34°S, Argo, paleo proxies). van Westen et al. 2024 state current reanalyses are too short.
2. A physics-based observable of feedback strength, e.g. FovS at 34°S, checked against an independent product.
3. A controlled reversal experiment across multiple forcing rates measuring the reverse threshold and critical rate in a model with a genuinely different closure from the CMIP pool.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55/:57, Fig 12 :121, Fig 6 :14/:23, Fig 1 :19 (five short passages).
2. Check C2 and C3 against their own knowledge: is cycle sign the determinant of continuity, and basin structure the determinant of reversibility, in the models they know?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a gradual weakening from a rate-induced or noise-induced jump in the present ocean, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
