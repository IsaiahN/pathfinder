# What "shape" means for this question

**Canonical reading from the eight essays.** The eight essays are by Isaiah N: *How Intelligence Actually Works*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, *The Library of Babel: Locating the*, *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, and *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether*. ## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried from the canonical mapping): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0024 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the specific threshold, rate and reversibility are known. It constrains: the answer must treat the three adjectives as a union, must be indexed per slot, must tie continuity to feedback sign against a threshold, reversibility to basin structure, and rate to an independent axis, and must name observation as the anchor. It does not fill in any of those quantities. "Union of three conditions with a per-slot verdict and an owed instrument" is a shape. "The AMOC collapses at +4 C and cannot recover" is a name, and is refused.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The derivation therefore described gradual, abrupt and reversible as effects (response shape, jump, return path) before naming salt-advection or saddle-node.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the stage-1 offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: describe what would count as a failure, in observable terms.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, having been *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the ground is sustained observation (RAPID, SAMBA, Argo, paleo proxies), not a model intercomparison.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test. The name "salt-advection feedback" comes from physical oceanography, a different closure from the figures, and is recorded as a binding with provenance.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Naming the salt-advection feedback as the positive loop in a specific model is a visible choice, not a derivation of nature's list.

## 4. Why a reading from a single source cannot settle source questions

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK and unknown schemes all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the analogue is precise. An observed weakening of the overturning can be produced by at least three distinct sources: gradual forcing tracking a single stable branch, rate-induced tipping without crossing a threshold, or noise-induced escape from a basin. The time series alone cannot comment on the source. Ben-Yami et al. 2024 (*"the uncertainties are too large to reliably estimate tipping times by extrapolating historical information"*) is the field's own statement of this lossy projection, and van Westen et al. 2024 supply the analogous spectrometer in the form of a physics-based observable (FovS at 34°S) plus a demand for sustained section measurement. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Correction to my own reading of the shape-not-a-name rule

I initially treated "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any concrete mechanism in this question. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Gamma. Gradient, cycle and threshold are already among the held structures and operators (Fig 13, Fig 12, Operators table). Naming "salt-advection feedback" as the loop that instantiates the positive cycle, or "saddle-node" as the bifurcation, is a **binding** from the ocean and dynamical-systems frames onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive and not a wall-moving import (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; these bindings add none).

What remains forbidden is the stronger move: asserting that a specific threshold value or a specific yes/no on reversibility follows from the framework. That is a name where the framework returns a shape, and it is the metamerism error of section 4. Both the narrowed rule and the original limit are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 13 + Fig 9 :32).

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous modellers who agree about a threshold is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against observations.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Why "shape" matters specifically for EAR-0024

The question is phrased as a forced choice among three adjectives. The shape discipline is the tool that refuses the forced choice: it shows that the three adjectives occupy different conditions with different anchors and determinants, and that the refused object ("the tipping point" as one number) has no nameable anchor independent of the instruments and experiments in D1 and D2. The answer is the union and the determinants, written before any threshold is known, with a refuter and an owed instrument. That is the shape, and it is checkable without settling the question.
