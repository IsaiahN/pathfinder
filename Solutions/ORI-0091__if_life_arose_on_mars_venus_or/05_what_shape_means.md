## 1. A shape is a constraint on what would count, not a filler for what is missing

Figure 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The figures README: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the class of evidence that would discriminate shared ancestry from independent origin, stated before we have that evidence. It is a constraint on a future reading. It is not: a list of candidate molecules, a percentage similarity, or a verdict about any particular biota.

For ORI-0091 the shape is: a character class in which H_A and H_B predict different readings, where the difference is not forced by chemistry or physics, and where an instrument already returning something can be sharpened to resolve it. That is Fig 8 :51 plus Fig 6 :32, :34-35 compressed into one sentence.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively; *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Applied here: describe *arbitrary historical record* in effect terms before deciding that the genetic code is the decisive test. Otherwise any molecule we already know will seem to fit.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* In effect terms, the discriminator is not "DNA"; it is "a mapping whose particular assignments were not forced, so a second origin need not reproduce them."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the outreach rule in `02`: send the expectation, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question that means the shape is stable only when the character class can be stated independently of whichever biota is eventually found.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measured arbitrary-record match in a confirmed sample would settle H_A; it has not happened here.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the mechanism that governs this whole question. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A biota's molecular contents are the green reading. Ancestry is the source. No amount of staring at the reading decides the source; a source-discriminating instrument is required.

**Correction to a common overstatement (kept visible).** The rule "shape, not a name" governs a primitive *not held in Γ*. Codon mappings, handedness and nucleic acids are already held atoms of the biology frame. So naming them as candidate discriminators is a **binding** from the biology frame onto held atoms, with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is promoting such a binding to a verdict without a source-discriminating instrument. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same narrowing the exemplar made in its own section 5b.

## 4. Provenance is the whole answer, and the record is the provenance

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the case with the video-frame and diamond examples. A frame extracted from a video and a photograph of the same scene look identical as artifacts, and *"nothing in the image records which."* Lab-grown and mined diamonds were indistinguishable until isotope ratios became readable: *"provenance is recoverable: isotope ratios genuinely differ, so if you have a tool that can perceive the trace, you regain distinction. The tool only works because a trace existed, and not because a better tool was created."*

That is exactly the ancestry question. Two biota with identical contents may still differ in provenance, and only a trace of the lineage decides. The essay's two horizons matter:

- **Distinction horizon**: the point at which one observer cannot tell two generation methods apart.
- **Union horizon**: the point at which no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate origin. *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

This is the canonical backing for audit F8: a null is `unreached at this instrument and budget`, not `independent origin` and not silence. The note that a union horizon moves with instruments is the reason to keep the lineage rather than trust depth.

The same essay's Rule 4 is the figure-congruent statement of F1: *"you can find instances where similar paths of varying derivatives could create similar or potentially identical derivations. Only their provenance chains/lineage would be able to distinguish them, and those get lost through transformation over time."* And: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. The two-stage offer follows from tacit knowledge

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* explains why sending the framework before the verdict spends the only thing an outside reader contributes. Writing carries context and part of subtext but *"almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* Two frames that only check against each other *"drift together, so neither notices."* The safeguards named there are exactly the three in the figures: a ground both are corrected by separately, separate experience, and claims in effect terms.

For ORI-0091 this means the first contact carries the residual, the shape and the refuter (Fig 10 :52), not the architecture. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree that DNA implies relatedness is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator and let the sample settle it.

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture's own demo note calls such outputs *"simulacra"* and adds that they *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*
