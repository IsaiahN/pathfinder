## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The ancestry question is explicitly live in the transfer literature.** Worth et al. do not leave it as a rhetorical aside: they state that any life found on the outer moons "cannot be assumed to be of independent origin" and that searches must determine whether it "arose independently or descended from common ancestors to Earth life." That is a world-source corroboration of the audit's union reading (Fig 9 :54-58), from a planetary-dynamics frame with low overlap with the figure text.

2. **Transfer feasibility is not transfer evidence.** The Frontiers/Deinococcus result strengthens H_A's plausibility but says nothing about whether a transfer actually happened. This is the figure rule that a filter for the budget is not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). It is recorded as a candidate filter, not as corroboration of shared ancestry.

3. **The convergence frame is the strongest independent corroboration of F1.** The convergent evolution entry shows that similar form and function arise without common ancestry, and that this happens at the molecular level, not only in body plans. That is the world-side counterpart of Fig 8 :51 and Fig 13 :121. It is a separate field from the figure text and from the origin-of-life papers, so it is the lowest-overlap witness in the set.

4. **The chirality frame strains a naive reading.** The homochirality entry gives a non-ancestral route (deterministic seeding from extraterrestrial material) to the same handedness. This does not conflict with the figure verdict; it sends the chirality slot back to the census and narrows it to an ambiguous binding (Fig 8 :51, Fig 9 :32). No verdict flips.

5. **The code frame supplies the record concept with a caveat.** The frozen-accident idea is exactly an arbitrary historical record, so a shared non-optimal codon quirk is the right shape of discriminator. The convergent evolution entry warns that *functional* molecular identity is not record identity. Both are held at once; neither is smoothed away.

6. **Contamination is a structural failure, not an inconvenience.** The planetary protection entry shows that the sample-return chain is designed precisely because a substituted habitat can confuse sensitive life-detection tests. This corroborates Fig 11 :70 from a mission-operations frame.

**Overlap declaration.** The Wikipedia pages share one encyclopedia pool and are counted as one witness, not four. The two transfer sources (Worth 2013 and the Frontiers news item) share the planetary-science community and partly overlap in the studies they cite. The four framework essays share the author of the figures completely and carry little independent weight; they are used for the canonical reading of "shape," not as outside corroboration. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered. One websearch integration returned no results on several queries; all source discovery here was by direct fetch of known URLs.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the discriminator shape and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict."* A consensus statement is not an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the discriminator shape and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual in effect terms (a biological signature whose lineage source is undecided), the shape claim (contents do not decide; the arbitrary record does), and the pre-registered refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because after reading the framework they are no longer an outside frame (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. The essays' own warning stands: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. If the shape claim can be shown to fail on any of the five refuters, stop and retract before sending anything further.
