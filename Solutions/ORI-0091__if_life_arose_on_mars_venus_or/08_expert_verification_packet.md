# Expert verification packet, the Tether on shared ancestry with Earth life

**What this is:** a claim in a form a comparative origin-of-life researcher or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the discriminator shape and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural constraints, not `grounded`.** No measurement of an alien biota is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A biological signature whose lineage source is undecided cannot be assigned to shared ancestry or independent origin from its molecular contents. Convergence and adopted ancestry look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. The discriminator is therefore a character class that is arbitrary, historically contingent, and not forced by chemistry or physics, read by an instrument that can already resolve something, with contamination enumerated as part of the habitat of the residual. Each character is ranged separately against the sample, per slot, never averaged, and a null is reported as unreached at the current instrument and budget rather than as a second genesis. The pre-registered refuters are stated in section 2 before any conclusion is drawn.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 8 and 13

- **Statement:** identical contents do not discriminate shared ancestry from independent origin. The lineage record does.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Also Fig 8 :50: "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." And Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."
- **What to check:** does the figure actually say the contents are insufficient, or only that two frames can share an atom? Read the line about the record, not just the line about convergence.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where two independently derived biota reliably share an arbitrary, non-functionally-forced marker with high probability, so contents discriminate after all. Note that Fig 8 :50 and Fig 13 :121 share the same chemistry example (Liebig, Wöhler, Berzelius), so they are one evidence pool, not two witnesses.

### C2, biology-facing, checkable against the molecular evidence

- **Statement:** the discriminating record is an arbitrary assignment or a non-functionally-constrained sequence, not a catalytic site, not a shared ingredient, and not a similarity scalar. Homochirality and DNA identity are candidate bindings with zero standalone verdict weight.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** is a shared non-optimal codon quirk more likely under shared ancestry than under a second origin? Is sequence homology in weakly constrained regions a better record-level discriminator than catalytic-site identity? Does the homochirality literature supply a non-ancestral route (deterministic seeding) to the same handedness?
- **World sources to check against:** convergent evolution (independent catalytic-triad origin more than 20 times), genetic code (frozen accident plus known variants), homochirality (extraterrestrial seeding of L-amino acid and D-sugar bias). All were fetched 2026-11-23 and are in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- **Refuter:** a quantified result showing the observed codon map is the unique physical optimum, or that handedness is fixed by a universal deterministic field. Either removes the arbitrary record from the discriminator set. Also refuted if function-level identity turns out to be reliably record-level identity.

### C3, mission-facing, checkable against the sample chain and instrument reach

- **Statement:** contamination is a substituted-habitat failure that must be enumerated before any shared sequence is interpreted, and a null is unreached rather than absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :69: "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **What to check:** has the contact chain (spacecraft bioburden, laboratory reagents, sequencing adapters, human handlers, return capsule) been enumerated and excluded? Has the search been run at a stated resolution, and is the null reported as budget-limited or depth-limited rather than as evidence of a second origin?
- **Refuter:** a documented Earth-derived contamination route that fully accounts for the shared markers (the claim is withdrawn as an artifact), or a demonstration of a sealed, fully swept search space at a stated resolution, which would permit a genuine absence claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that DNA, RNA, or any particular polymer implies relatedness.
- Not that left-handed amino acids or right-handed sugars decide ancestry.
- Not that panspermia happened. Transfer feasibility (Worth et al. 2013; Frontiers 2020) is a plausibility filter with zero verdict weight (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that a similarity percentage, a shared gene or a shared enzyme is a verdict.
- Not that an unreadable record is an absent record.
- Not `grounded`, and not a verdict on any actual sample.
- Not an answer to sibling question ORI-0087 (is the signature biological?). That is granted here.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only true account of biological relatedness.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; the spectrometer resolved that case. Here:

1. **Record test:** do the alien codon assignments share non-optimal, arbitrary quirks with Earth life beyond functional prediction?
2. **Non-functional homology test:** do weakly constrained sequences align beyond chance after contamination control?
3. **Mechanism discriminator:** is the shared handedness forced by a universal field or contingent on a local accident?
4. **Three-way null discipline:** exclude budget failure (SEARCHED), then depth (INWARD), before considering genuine absence (Formula step 7).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :50-51, Fig 13 :121, Fig 11 :70 and Fig 8 :39, four short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own field: is an arbitrary non-functional record a better ancestry discriminator than function-level identity? Give one counterexample if not.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a shared frozen accident from convergence at a stated resolution, or say why none is possible.
4. Point to a contamination route in a real sample chain that the shape does not enumerate.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
