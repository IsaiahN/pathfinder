## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether a signature is biological (that is sibling question ORI-0087) but the **source of the lineage** behind a biological signature. In effect terms: a found alien biota carries a library of molecular forms. The prediction Γ(b,a) is what that library would look like if it descended from Earth life; the observation o′ is what a mission actually measures; R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed per character slot (genetic polymer, handedness, codon table, energy currency, membrane lipids, isotopic fractionation), never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). 

- Option A, "shared ancestry": the alien biota is a branch of the Earth tree, transported by natural transfer (lithopanspermia) or otherwise descended from the same lineage. Predicted: the *arbitrary* historical record (codon-table quirks, non-functional sequence homologies, shared molecular workarounds) should match, beyond what function forces.
- Option B, "independent origin": a second genesis, built from a different closure. Predicted: functional solutions may converge, but the arbitrary record should not match.
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **shape for a discriminator**, namely a character class in which the two hypotheses predict different readings on an instrument that can actually read it, plus a pre-registered refuter. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it (Fig 5 :62-63).

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is that the two hypotheses are **indiscernible by contents alone** and are separated only by the *record* of arbitrary historical choices (Fig 8 :51), in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. This is a route and a shape, not a laboratory result.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the ancestry question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of biological relatedness.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. Independence decides whether agreement means anything. *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"* (Fig 8 :45), but only where their closures differ; a list selected for receptiveness is one frame wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
