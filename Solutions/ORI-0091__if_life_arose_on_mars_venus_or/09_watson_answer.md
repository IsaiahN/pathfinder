## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how scientists could determine whether a biota found on Mars, Venus, or an icy moon shares ancestry with Earth life. Option A: shared ancestry, a branch of the Earth tree transported to a new habitat. Option B: a second independent origin. What each leads to: A predicts a shared arbitrary historical record; B predicts functional convergence without that record. Contents alone are predicted to look the same under both, so the practical question is whether any character class can discriminate them at a resolution an instrument already returns something at.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Biological relatedness is a property of a lineage record, not of a molecular ingredient list. Shared ancestry and independent origin are indiscernible in contents, so the discriminator must be an arbitrary, non-functionally-forced character read by an instrument already returning something, with contamination enumerated as part of the habitat of the residual. DNA identity, homochirality, transfer feasibility and similarity scalars are bindings or filters with zero standalone verdict weight. Every character gets its own verdict, never averaged. A null is unreached at this instrument and budget, not a second genesis. The held term must be refusable. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, status `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, shape and five refuters before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is promoting a candidate binding (DNA, chirality, transfer) to a verdict and spending the independence of outside readers with a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no atom has to be withdrawn from the biology library.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement showing shared arbitrary codon quirks and non-functional homology in a confirmed sample would settle shared ancestry. A systematically different arbitrary record after contamination exclusion and adequate reach would settle independent origin. A quantified result making the codon map a unique optimum would remove the record discriminator. A demonstrated universal chiral field would remove the chirality slot. A derivation showing contents do discriminate after all would break the central finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
