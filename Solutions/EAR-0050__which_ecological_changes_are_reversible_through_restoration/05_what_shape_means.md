## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The framework can say that a target is out of reach with the library as it stands, and characterise what is missing in effect terms, but "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (2026, fetched this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be filled. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0050 the shape is:

> reversibility is a **per-slot reachability property of a seat acting on a habitat**: name the actors, the currency and the anchor for each change; enumerate the cascade; ask which atom is missing and which donor frame holds it; measure the round trip gap; then decide. The target is maintained, not finished.

That is a shape. A statement such as "wetland type X at site Y will recover in 12 years" is a name plus a measurement, and it is not in this folder.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on the recursion converging to indiscernibility: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." This is the reason a recovered-looking ecosystem is not thereby *the* historical ecosystem.

The residual here was described in effect terms before any figure line was chosen (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0), so the figures were used to test the shape rather than to supply it. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question: monitoring per slot against an adjusted reference model, with survival, recruitment, function and external exchanges measured, not inferred from appearance.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question: a donor population or seed source whose provenance is recorded and whose cascade was enumerated before introduction.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The reference model chosen for a site is such a declared choice: visible is not the same as neutral.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came from the spectrometer, not an argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a site that *looks* like a reference forest can have several histories. Planted even-aged natives after soil enrichment; natural regeneration after herbivore removal; a substituted novel assemblage that mimics structure without function. Each can project the same canopy reading. Naming the cause from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, per slot: soil profile, age structure, recruitment, mycorrhizal association, trophic occupancy, not a better-looking photograph.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point with provenance loss: a single video frame and a photograph can be indistinguishable as artifacts and carry different warrants, and "nothing in the image records which." The historical reference and the reconstructed analogue are the frame and the photograph.

## 5. Q for this question: what does "shape" license us to say?

**Answered.** The shape licenses four claims and no fifth:

1. Verdicts are per slot, never an aggregate recovery score (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. Reversibility is bounded by closure and import (Fig 6 :21, :23), by lossy cross-scale transforms (Fig 4 :28-29), and by an enumerable cascade (Fig 11 :43-44).
3. Unreachable has two kinds with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43).
4. Recovery is maintained work (Fig 11 :63).

It does **not** license a site-specific species list, a recovery timetable, or a declaration that a named ecosystem type is categorically irreversible. Those are names and measurements, and they require the ground.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and to an easy mapping

I first wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any particular organism. That overstates it. The README phrase governs *"a **missing** thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. A donor species or a reference attribute is an atom held by another frame; selecting it is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is pretending the framework named it, or claiming the binding moved the wall (Fig 6: "An import adds no atom to the world"). This correction is kept visible rather than silently applied to sections 1 to 4.

A second correction concerns the word **habitat**. The architecture essay lists "The habitat as an ecological niche" under "Three mappings offered and declined": "A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align." So the framework's habitat must not be quietly replaced with the ecological-niche concept, even in a document about restoration. The same essay lists Carson 1962, Jones, Lawton and Shachak 1994, and Elton 1958 as **instances** that Figure 11 describes, arrived at independently. That is useful provenance, and it is not a derivation of the figure from ecology.

A third correction: *How Intelligence Actually Works* says "The Slide Rule Principle applies to many things, be it backyard gardens, factory farming, urban planning, landscape engineering or ecological restoration." I initially took this as the framework citing restoration as evidence for itself. It is the author applying a derived principle to a domain, which is a prediction offered for testing, not a ground reading of any restoration project.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And from the architecture essay's own caveat: "due to the black-box nature could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, and every other file in this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of which ecological changes are reversible, and they cannot settle the question they report on. The five pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 are the instrument a real reader would use.
