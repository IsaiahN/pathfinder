## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set governing this question, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22, Fig 4 :28-32, Fig 6 :21-25, Fig 8 :39-43, Fig 9 :55-57, Fig 11 :43-44 and :61-67.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, "per-slot reachability, add not subtract, import from a donor frame, measure the loss, maintain the result."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete prescription of the form "plant species X, reintroduce predator Y, reconnect the river, expect reference condition in ten years."

**R_T is nonzero.** The following survive or drop as listed:

| constraint in x | survives T_E? | note |
|---|---|---|
| per-slot indexing | frequently dropped | the concrete prescription reports one completion date for the whole site |
| addition available, subtraction of habitat not | partly | reintroduction survives; "remove the stressor and wait" silently assumes a subtractive move when the stressor is climate or land use |
| donor frame with source gate | often dropped | the prescription names a species, not the provenance of the stock |
| two unreachable kinds with different instructions | dropped | failure is read as "not ready yet" or "impossible" without a donor census |
| no proof of absence from one failed search | dropped | one failed attempt is reported as irreversibility |
| union compound with ongoing change | dropped | the historical target is retained without adjustment |
| maintenance as the product | dropped | completion is treated as the end of the project |
| lossy transform and measured R_T | dropped | fidelity is asserted from canopy appearance |

What survives cleanly is the head start: a site should be described per slot, the habitat enumerated, the donor frame found, and the gap measured. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no site exists in this folder; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
