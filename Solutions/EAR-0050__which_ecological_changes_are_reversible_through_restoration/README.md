## The residual in effect terms

An ecosystem is in a changed state C and a target state T is specified (a reference model adjusted for changed and predicted conditions, not a frozen historical snapshot). Restoration is a seat's work on the habitat: it can introduce actors, conditions and processes, and it cannot subtract the habitat or the contact that defines it (Fig 11 :61-62). The decision is per slot: for each change (species composition, structure, soil, hydrology, function, external exchanges), is T_s reachable from C_s with the atoms and bonds available to the restoration frame, with any missing atom importable from a frame whose closure differs, before a lossy cross-scale transform has discarded what the return would need? Reversible means reachable and maintainable. Unreachable splits into two different facts: the atom is gone or a threshold was crossed (different expectations or adaptation), or the atom exists elsewhere and only budget, knowledge or logistics are missing (more search and more resources, not a new atom). Fig 8 :40-43 separates these, and Fig 13 calls the first a verdict: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this analysis.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-22): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional submission and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
