# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cross-level information flow

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher of organizational communication and information flow can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No organization was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Accurate information transfer across organizational levels is not a matter of sending more or clearer messages. It requires three conditions, and each distortion is the failure of one. Transfer fidelity: what crosses a level boundary must be a re-applicable method going up and a head start going down, with the loss measured; a recording of one occasion produces repetition. Chain independence: each handoff must reach a genuinely different receiver still under the pressure of the same non-updating ground; a chain of mirrors compounds agreement without adding evidence. Verification: the receiver must check its picture against a ground that does not move when pushed, using evidence independent of the source; agreement from one shared pool is one witness. The residual stays per decision slot, never averaged. The framework does not name a best medium, a layer count or a universal percentage.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Transfer fidelity, checkable by reading Figures 4 and 10

- **Statement:** a cross-level summary is a lossy transform. What should cross upward is a re-applicable method, not a recording of one success; the round trip gap must be measured.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25-26 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."
- **What to check:** in a case you know, did the artifact that crossed the boundary name a method that could be reapplied in a different situation, or did it narrate one occasion? Was the gap between what went up and what came back down measured, or was the summary treated as the situation?
- **Refuter:** a one-occasion recording replayed downward still generates a valid new decision with the ground paying, and the round trip gap is demonstrably zero across all decision-relevant slots.

### C2. Chain independence, checkable by reading Figures 7 and 3

- **Statement:** a handoff that hands the same closure along is a mirror. Progress needs a differing receiver, fidelity of the bracket, and ground pressure; the chain is ordered by dependency and usually breaks at the uninstrumented middle link.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 7 :38 "the bracket's fidelity across the handoff" and Fig 7 :39 "the ground's pressure on the room". Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **What to check:** does the receiving level bring independent evidence, or does it only re-summarize the previous level? Does the surfaced residual decide which room is consulted next? Is there an instrument for the receiver's affordance vocabulary and progress criterion, or only for message delivery and decision output?
- **Refuter:** an instrument reads the middle link and the chain works unchanged whether it is present or absent, or a single lever clears fidelity, independence and verification at once with ground contact measured.

### C3. Verification and anchor, checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** accuracy requires a non-updating anchor and independence between frames; distortion is coherent drift, a shared evidence pool, a decayed channel, or a convention promoted to ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" and Fig 10 :26 "saturated metrics, pooled readings". Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- **What to check:** for a specific report, name the ground and test whether it moves when the reporting relationship pushes on it. Are the agreeing frames using different instruments or the same dashboard? Can the metric saturate, and is a shared definition still violable by an observation?
- **Refuter:** two genuinely low-overlap instruments agree against an unmoved ground, and the same reporting metric still discriminates across the full operating range with no pooling. In that case the distortion mechanism named here did not fire.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a best medium, an org chart, a layer count, or a universal accuracy percentage. Those are a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that shared meanings are a corruption. Agreement about a shared meaning is efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that distortion is mostly intentional. Serial-reproduction research shows it is usually reconstructive.
- Not that fewer layers or more reporting is better. An ordered bond means direction and content decide, not volume (Fig 4 :22).
- Not that a global communication score is useful. Residuals are per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not `grounded`. No organization was measured and no falsifier has been run.

## 5. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 4 :22 and :25-26, Fig 7 :12-13 and :36, and Fig 2 :29, :34 and :40. Seven short passages.
2. Pick one cross-level report you know. Name the method or recording, the receiver's independence, and the ground. Say which of the three conditions failed, if any.
3. Name one instrument that would measure the round trip gap per decision slot, or say why none is possible for that case.
4. Try to break one of C1 to C3 with a counter-case. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
