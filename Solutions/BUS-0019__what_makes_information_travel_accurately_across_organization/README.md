## Restating the question as an effect

An item of decision-relevant information is raised at one level about a situation and must arrive at another level in a form that lets the receiver make the decision the ground would have supported. Accurate travel means the arriving picture preserves what is decision-relevant and can still be ranged against the same non-updating ground. Distortion means the arriving picture diverges systematically from what would let the receiver do that. What is being decided is which conditions let a signal cross a level boundary with its decision content intact, and which conditions degrade it. An answer is worth having if it names those conditions in effect terms, keeps the per-decision residual visible, and states what would refute each condition. Bind that object as the ordinary term **R**, the residual between a receiver's prediction and the observation the ground returns, indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Symbols table :36).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35) Gathering agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree; agreement about a shared meaning is efficient for a convention, not for a verdict (Fig 2 :41).
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42)

A Track B direct-outreach list is out of scope for this solution. Any later contact must use the public opt-in venues in `03` and must record the verdict on stage 1 before showing any theory.
