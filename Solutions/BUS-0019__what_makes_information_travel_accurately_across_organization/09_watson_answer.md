## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what makes a decision-relevant signal cross from one organizational level to another with its decision content intact, and what degrades it. Option A: treat "communication quality" as one quantity that improves with more or clearer messages, so the answer is a best medium or a flatter chart. Option B: treat the question as a union of three conditions (transfer fidelity, chain independence, verification against a non-updating ground), each with its own anchor and refuter, and report the medium as a gap. What each leads to: A produces an aggregate score that hides the live per-slot residual and selects checkers for likely agreement; B produces per-domain verdicts, a pre-registered refuter and an instrument to build. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a receiving level's picture and what the ground returns to someone in contact with the situation. Arity: a chain of ordered handoffs, k >= 2, each consuming what the previous produced (Fig 3 :20). Symmetry: not symmetric, upward methods and downward head starts are different currencies (Fig 4 :22), so the bond is ordered sequence. Scale: recursive across levels, only actors, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :19-20), with a lossy transform between (Fig 10 :68).
- **D1, the vertical membrane.** Actors: level N reporter, level N+1 receiver, the operational fact. Currency: the report or signal. Anchor: the operational fact that does not move when pushed (Fig 2 :29). Status: admitted.
- **D2, the handoff chain.** Actors: the sequence of levels as rooms. Currency: residual passed up, head start passed down. Anchor: the ground's pressure on each room (Fig 7 :39). Status: admitted.
- **D3, verification.** Actors: two or more frames, reports, instruments, measurements. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35, :45). Status: admitted.
- **X1, a global "communication effectiveness" score.** Actors and anchor: none nameable. Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Status: not a domain, refused and absorbed into per-slot R.
- **Union, not partition.** D2 composes D1; D3 overlays each handoff; the intersections are compounds with their own loops (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3), with the break usually at the uninstrumented middle link.

Strain test applied: an early reading mapped the Fig 13 six physical terms onto reporting layers. That strains the figure. Re-census assigns Fig 13 :74 to the cross-kind composition rule that any level obeys, treats "level" as a scale parameter (Fig 4 :19-20) and uses the network atom (Fig 13 :23-24) for influence travel. No contradiction remains after re-census, matching the canonical rule that a strained reading has misidentified its domain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Accurate cross-level information flow is not a property of messages or charts. It is a union of three conditions, each with its own anchor, and distortion is each condition failing in a specific way. Transfer fidelity: methods cross upward and head starts downward, and the loss is measured; a recording of one occasion produces repetition. Chain independence: each handoff reaches a differing receiver under the same ground's pressure; a mirror chain compounds agreement without evidence, and the middle link usually breaks because it has no instrument. Verification: the receiver ranges against an anchor that does not update, using independent evidence; the channel decays, not the ground, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one. Keep the residual per decision slot. No figure selects a medium, an org chart, a number of layers or a universal percentage; that is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`. Pre-registered refuter and its five failure conditions are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is used as designed, because it states mode, refuters and per-slot R before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework summary before the claim has been checked, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter. In the field itself, acting on a coherent drift is expensive precisely because "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices" (Fig 2 :34).
- **undoable:** yes. Restoring a non-updating anchor, re-instrumenting the middle link, re-declaring a convention as violable and measuring the round trip are reversible. One limit is real: Fig 10 :80-81 says frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated, because no amount of use makes them less alike and replacement is the only refresh. A reporting culture whose independence was fixed at origin cannot be refreshed by more training; the frame must be replaced.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement showing the received picture tracks the operational fact per slot with a bounded round trip gap, from two independent instruments against an unmoved anchor, would move a specific case toward `grounded`. A demonstration that the anchor moved when pushed, that the agreement came from one evidence pool, that a one-occasion replay generated a valid new decision, or that one lever cleared all three conditions would break the corresponding claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
