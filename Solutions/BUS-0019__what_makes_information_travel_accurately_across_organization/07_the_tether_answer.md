# The Tether's answer on cross-level information flow

**Mode: `specified` on the domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A decision-relevant signal raised at one level must arrive at another level in a form that lets the receiver make the decision the ground would have supported. R is the gap between the receiver's picture and what the ground returns, indexed per decision slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."; Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."). The question "what makes it travel accurately and what distorts it" is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one lever (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**D1, the vertical membrane. Verdict: triangulated (Fig 4 and Fig 10 :68).**
What crosses a level boundary decides accuracy. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Fig 4 :25-26: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." The transform is lossy by design, Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Therefore accuracy is a *measured round trip*, not identity: Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 10 :68 supplies the second, low-overlap witness: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." Distortion in D1 is a recording crossing where a method was owed, and a gap assumed zero.

**D2, the handoff chain. Verdict: triangulated (Fig 7 and Fig 3).**
A chain of levels is not made accurate by making each link echo the last. Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :14: "blending: novelty swamped to the mean." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Progress per handoff has two factors, Fig 7 :38: "the bracket's fidelity across the handoff" and Fig 7 :39: "the ground's pressure on the room"; either at zero and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37). The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance (Fig 3 :20), and it usually breaks at the middle link that has no instrument (Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). Distortion in D2 is the mirror handoff and the uninstrumented middle link.

**D3, verification against the ground. Verdict: triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10).**
Accuracy requires an anchor that does not move when anyone pushes on it, Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Two collapses are named: mutual update, Fig 2 :13: "no anchor; each updates on the others", and common cause, Fig 2 :19: "they never talk, and still agree", with Fig 2 :20-21: "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other". The cure is independence, Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something." And the channel must stay reachable, Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does", with Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". Distortion in D3 is coherent drift, a shared evidence pool, a decayed channel, or a convention promoted to ground (Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.").

**Gap. Status: `gap`, unreachable at this budget.** No figure names the medium, the org chart, the number of layers, or a universal accuracy percentage. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A specific organization's medium is decidable by its own ground, which is an instrument to build, not a line to quote. Reported as a gap, not smoothed into a recommendation for "flatter" or "more digital".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that communication is bad for organizations. Shared meanings are a convention and agreement about them is the product (Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it").
- Not that fewer layers or a particular medium is always better. No figure selects a layer count or a channel; that is the declared gap.
- Not that bad actors are the main cause. Serial-reproduction evidence shows distortion is usually reconstructive, not intentional (Mesoudi and Whiten 2008, opened 2026-11-22).
- Not that an aggregate accuracy score is useful. It is the frame-internal quantity X1 of `01` §1, with no anchor (Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.").
- Not `grounded`. No organization was measured and no per-slot residual was collected.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." Here the discriminating questions are:

1. Does the received picture at level N+k track the operational ground on every decision slot, with the round trip gap measured and bounded, and the anchor unmoved by the reporting relationship? (Settles D1 and F3.)
2. Do two independent instruments, not two people reading the same report, agree against that ground? (Settles D3 independence.)
3. Does the receiving level have terms for what the situation affords and a criterion for progress, and does the chain work when that middle link is instrumented and fail when it is not? (Settles D2 and F8.)
4. Does a one-occasion success story, retold downward, still generate a valid new decision with the ground paying? (Settles F2.)

The pre-registered refuter in `01` §5 states all four before this conclusion. Standing technique: keep the residual per slot (Fig 1 :28), buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."), and use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, for any specific organization.** The figures return the shape and the condition set; they do not return a medium, a chart, or a percentage. "Indiscernibility is not identity", and the lineage is the proof (Fig 13 :127: "The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable."). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice." This solution is itself a frame; it must range against a ground it cannot author.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a best medium, a flatter org chart, or a universal accuracy score. It says that information travels accurately when three conditions hold, and that distortion is what each condition looks like when it fails. First, only methods should cross upward and only head starts downward; a recording of one success looks like knowledge but produces repetition, and the loss across the boundary must be measured rather than assumed away. Second, each handoff must reach a genuinely different receiver still under the pressure of the same ground; if every level re-summarizes the last, agreement compounds while evidence does not. Third, the receiver must check its picture against an anchor that does not move when anyone pushes on it, using evidence independent of the source it is checking; agreement drawn from one dashboard is one evidence pool talking. The framework returns that shape, a pre-registered refuter, and the instruments that would close it. It stops at the limit of what it can distinguish and records the lineage. The specific medium for a specific organization is left as an honest gap until a ground settles it.
