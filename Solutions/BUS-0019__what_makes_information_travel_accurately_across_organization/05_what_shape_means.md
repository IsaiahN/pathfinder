## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence", and the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it" (Fig 13 :135).

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0019 the shape is: accuracy is a measured round trip under a non-updating anchor with independent frames, and distortion is a recording crossing instead of a method, a mirror chain, a decayed channel, an averaged-away live slot, or a convention promoted to ground. A named best medium, a chosen org chart, or a universal percentage is a name, not this shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the operational meaning of effect terms for information flow: describe the observation a correct transfer would produce, so any receiving level can check it.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Residual first, frame second. The residual here was censused in `01` §1 before any figure line was chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So saying "the ground is an anchor that must not update" is not inventing a new primitive; it is applying the held anchor rule (Fig 2 :29) at this level. Naming *which* metric in *which* firm is a binding that needs provenance and a ground check.

## 4. Why the ordinary term "communication accuracy" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case.

Applied here: a dashboard reading (on-time delivery, engagement, error rate) is a projection. Several underlying situations can produce the same reading. Naming the situation from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a better-worded report. This is also why "the numbers look good" is not evidence that information travelled accurately.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the second limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A level's own report that it communicated well is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27).

## 5. Contact, drift and the three drift-stoppers

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the essay closest to BUS-0019. Its claims, all read directly:

- "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." The compressed message cannot unpack without the receiver's history, which is built by contact.
- "Between two people, who reshape each other's understanding as they talk, and who drift away from the truth together if they only ever check against each other." This is Fig 2's panel 1 at the interpersonal scale.
- "Nobody in Bartlett's study lied: details that didn't fit the listener's schema were simply 'omitted from the recollection, or transformed into more familiar forms.'" Distortion is reconstructive, not usually deceptive. This corroborates the serial-reproduction world source in `04`.
- "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."
- "If it moves, 'everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices' (Fig 2 :33-34)."
- "Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources (Fig 2 :40-41)."
- "Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice (Fig 2 :34)." This is the organizational-level claim of BUS-0019 in the framework's own words.
- The three drift-stoppers: "A ground that both are corrected by, separately", "Separate experience", and "Claims in effect terms". These map exactly onto the three conditions in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- "what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself. 'Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down' (Fig 4 :22)."
- The round trip: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked (Fig 4 :30)." And: "An understanding that's never checked feels exactly as certain as one that has been." This is why distortion is invisible from inside.

These are framework-essay statements, high overlap with the figures, so they corroborate the derivation but do not add an independent witness.

## 6. Prestige is not accuracy

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." And: "If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."

For outreach this means: do not gather senior titles or friendly readers to endorse the shape. Give the claim a budget to be tried against a ground. Also: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

## 7. Correction to my own §3 and §4, kept visible

**Overstatement retracted.** I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any concrete mechanism in the answer. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. A non-updating anchor, an independent instrument and a measured round trip are already held figures (Fig 2 :29, Fig 2 :31-32, Fig 4 :30), so naming them is a **binding** of held rules to this level, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is naming *which* specific metric, medium, or layer count occupies a slot without provenance and a ground check. Both the narrower rule and my original overstatement are recorded here so neither is silently erased.

**Metamerism point not retracted.** §4 still stands: a dashboard reading does not determine the situation that produced it. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. This is the honest stop in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §5.
