## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule is in the local figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The README adds: "Indiscernibility is not identity."

"How Intelligence Actually Works" gives the same limit in its own vocabulary: "indiscernibility is not identity" and "The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."

Applied here: a **shape** is what any acceptable answer to "rules, discretion, or both" must look like before the institutional details are named. It constrains: the anchor must not update, the rule must be violable, the channel must stay live, the residual must be read per slot, the two loops must be unbundled. It does not fill: it does not say which Taylor coefficients, which target rate, which escape clause, or which committee design.

- `RULES ONLY` is a name, not a shape.
- `DISCRETION ONLY` is a name, not a shape.
- `CONSTRAINED DISCRETION WITH A 2 PERCENT TARGET AND A 0.5/0.5 TAYLOR COEFFICIENT` is a bundle of names.
- The shape is: *a violable convention plus a live, non-updating ground, unbundled into a coordination loop and an adaptation loop, with the residual read slot by slot.*

## 2. How the shape is obtained

From the framework's search discipline (Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"):

- Describe R in effect terms before choosing a literature: the error is a three-slot vector, not a scalar; the decision and the outcome sit at different scales; the map is lossy. That description selects the habitat before any frame is picked.
- From "How Intelligence Actually Works": "we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered." The parallel here is deliberate: the framework does not say which loop composed first, or whether the coordination loop must precede the adaptation loop. Order is introduced at the level of composition, not at the level of the kinds. That is why the answer stops at two loops and does not rank them.
- The same essay describes the stopping condition: "It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it. That's operational, and it's the only version that doesn't require knowing the answer in advance." For this question, the stop is when a proposed arrangement no longer registers a difference against the five refuters in `01` section 3.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For monetary policy the ground is the outcome data and the institutional record, not agreement among economists.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

So a numerical target is licensed by a decision and an outcome record, not by the figure derivation. Calling it figure-derived would be naming a thing the framework returns only as a shape. Fig 9 :32 again: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

"How Intelligence Actually Works" sharpened this for me. The replicator thought experiment: "the content result is theirs, not the framework's." If a practitioner supplies the domain measurements at each level, the convergence toward an answer is the practitioner's contribution. The same applies here: the monetary content comes from the economics frame; the framework supplies the shape.

## 4. Why "rule-compliance" is the specific forbidden error

From "How Intelligence Actually Works": the ground is "immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are. The ground doesn't update on anything that the frames do and only outputs data that will show if something is the right answer or not for a problem."

A bank that scores itself by how closely it followed its rule scores itself with a quantity it also produces. Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 1 :24-26 disqualifies "coverage", "predicates minted, and credibility accrued" as "frame-internal." Rule-compliance is the same genus of quantity. Goodhart's law (opened this session) names the world instance: "Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes."

The correct form is: report the slot residuals against the outcome data, and record the rule as the convention that was held, not as the evidence that the policy was right.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap" (opened this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this field the prestige leak is acute. A rules-versus-discretion claim attached to a famous central banker or a Nobel prize gets a budget to be heard, which is legitimate. It does not get a verdict. The same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." That is Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you" stated in prose.

## 6. The simulation caveat

From "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap": "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So this solution is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.** It is not a ground reading. It cannot settle whether rules, discretion or a blend is the better regime. It can only state the shape a settled answer would have, the refuters that would break it, and what would have to be imported to move it to `grounded`.

## 7. Consequences for this question

1. The outreach claim is the shape and its refuter, never a policy prescription.
2. The two loops are kept separate; their agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. Numerical parameters, if offered at all, are labelled bindings from the economics frame, with provenance and a shadow test.
4. The stop is honest: no last answer, only a lineage. "Indiscernibility is not identity."
