# The Tether's answer: rules, discretion, or both?

**Mode: `specified` on the four clauses, `general` on the two-loop structure. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6). No measurement settles the regime here.

## 1. The residual it answers

A central bank's error is a vector over at least three slots: inflation, real activity or employment, and financial stability. It is not a scalar. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The question "rules, discretion, or both" is a **union**, not a partition: coordination and adaptation can both bind and overlap. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Pick-one and average-both are both single levers that leave the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape

1. **Read R per slot.** A headline target is a projection. Where a live residual sits on a slot the aggregate does not show, the aggregate has thrown away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :19/:22).
2. **The outcome data are anchor-like.** Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." If the target moves when the bank pushes on it, it becomes another frame: Fig 2 :47 "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame." The bank cannot score itself with rule-compliance, because "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces" (Fig 1 :27).
3. **The rule is a violable convention.** Its value is coordination of expectations, which Fig 2 :41 calls "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." But Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one," and Fig 10 :48: "a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority." So exceptions, review and refusal are conditions of the rule remaining a convention. Fig 10 :19: "install what can be violated." Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one."
4. **Discretion is channel maintenance and gated import.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does," with "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25-26) leading to "you have lost touch" (Fig 10 :28) and "keep the anchor reachable" (Fig 10 :29). Novelty needs an import; a fixed rule cannot make one, because "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom" (Fig 6 :21) and "No frame escapes its own closure" (Fig 6 :23). The import carries "provenance" and a "shadow test" (Fig 10 :34-35), and "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives" (Fig 6 :24).
5. **Two loops, not a blend.** Coordination (D3) and adaptation (D2/D4) are separate loops with separate verdicts, neither senior. Fig 8 :4: "collapse 4: the blend"; Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Their agreement is never pooled as corroboration because they draw on different evidence. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "rules win" and not "discretion wins." The question is misposed if it requires one winner; the two loops do different work.
- Not "combine them by averaging." The averaged hybrid is a blend collapse (Fig 8 :4-5), not the figure synthesis.
- Not a numerical rule, target or escape clause. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). Any parameter is a binding from the economics frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that a rule is bad. A rule is the institutional form of the convention that coordinates expectations; its defect is only when it becomes unviolable or is treated as the verdict.
- Not that discretion is freedom from the anchor. It is the office that keeps the anchor reachable.
- Not `grounded`. No outcome record in this derivation settled which arrangement performs better.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A ground reading, not agreement. Concretely:

1. **Slot-resolved outcomes.** A record showing a live residual on one slot while the aggregate target is met, and showing whether the unbundled loops detect it before a blended rule does. This would settle F1 and F6.
2. **Novelty tests.** A shock whose description was absent from the committee's model at the time; compare the actions reached by the fixed rule and by the gated import. This would settle F2 and F7.
3. **Violability record.** Published exceptions and their outcomes: does a rule with escape clauses retain its coordinating value while a rule without them becomes an authored constant? This would settle F4.
4. **Anchor test.** A case where the target moved because the bank wanted it to move, and the outcome series failed to detect the move. This would settle F5.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 3.** Seven numbered conditions, written before the run. Any one fires the retraction of the corresponding clause.

An instrument, dataset or institutional record is required. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session.)

## 5. The honest stop

Unreached, not unreachable. The atom list used in `06` is a binding from the economics frame, not a discovery of the framework's primitives. "How Intelligence Actually Works" (opened this session): "the content result is theirs, not the framework's." The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

One strain stays live: the bank partly produces the data it reads through expectations, so the anchor is never perfectly outside the frame. Independence institutions mitigate it; no figure says it vanishes. Recorded in `01` F8 and `11_audit.md`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not choose between rules and discretion, and it does not average them. It says the policy error must be read slot by slot, because an aggregate target hides a live signal; that the outcome data must not move when the bank changes its mind; that a rule is a convention, so it must be violable and checkable or it becomes a constant with authority; and that discretion is the narrow office of keeping the channel to the ground live and importing new information under a source gate. Coordination and adaptation are two loops, not one hybrid, and neither is senior. What the framework returns is that shape plus seven written refuters, not a Taylor rule, not a target number, and not a verdict on which regime works better. The centre stays open until an outcome record, not an agreement, settles it.
