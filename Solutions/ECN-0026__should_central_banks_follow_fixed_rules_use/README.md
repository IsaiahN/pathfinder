## Restatement in effect terms

The question asks what a monetary policy committee should bind its future self to. Option A, a fixed rule, buys a stable shared meaning for expectations and blocks drift in the bank's own reaction function, at the cost of a bounded reach when a shock lies outside the model the rule was built from. Option B, discretion, keeps the channel to the ground live and permits imports of new information, at the cost of coherent drift if the target itself becomes movable. Option C, combine both, is ambiguous: it can mean an unbundled pair of loops, or a single blended hybrid. The figures license the first reading and refuse the second.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a specification of which commitments are anchor-like and must not update, which readings are per slot, which operator maintains contact when novelty arrives, and what would refute the arrangement. That bound shape is stated in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 and tested by the pre-registered refuter in section 3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No numerical rule (no Taylor coefficient set, no inflation target number) presented as figure-derived. The figures return a shape, not a name.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35) A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that disqualifies agreement.
2. **A public channel is for a convention, not for corroboration.** "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it" (Fig 2 :41); but "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Trust before checking spends independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42) The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure and the structural one.
