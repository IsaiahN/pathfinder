## 0. The claim under audit and the bound answer-shape

The question is not a three-way menu. It is a question about **which things a controller may bind, and what maintains its contact with the ground.**

Before the run, the sought-for shape is written down as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else"). The bound shape is:

> A settlement in which (i) the outcome data the bank ranges against are anchor-like and do not update on the bank's beliefs; (ii) the rule is a violable convention that coordinates expectations and can be refused; (iii) discretion is the operator that maintains the channel to the ground and imports new information under a source gate; (iv) residuals are read per mandate slot, not aggregated; (v) coordination and adaptation are kept as separate loops, not averaged into one hybrid.

If any clause fails its refuter in section 3, this shape is withdrawn.

### 0.1 Residual, in effect terms

R is the gap between what the bank's frame predicts will follow from its chosen policy action and what the economy actually delivers. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symbols table: R is "always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."

- **Slots:** inflation, real activity or employment, financial stability.
- **Arity:** three, not one. A single headline loss is a projection of three slots.
- **Symmetry:** none. A one percentage point miss in inflation is not a one percentage point miss in employment. There is no permutation that makes the slots interchangeable.
- **Scale:** the reaction function lives at the decision scale; the outcome lives at the economy scale; the map between them is lossy. Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."

### 0.2 Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. A line with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition / union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 The bank's controller | monetary policy committee, model Γ, belief b, instrument a | prediction Γ(b,a), residual R_s, the policy rate | measured outcomes, which must not move with the bank's belief | boundary of the control loop |
| D2 The economy as ground | households, firms, banks, prices, credit | observed inflation, output, financial conditions | the data series themselves; a measurement that does not take instructions | ground, not a frame |
| D3 The expectations convention | households, firms, markets forming expectations of the bank's rule | shared meaning, coordination, credibility | the convention's own checkability, not a verdict about the world | convention, not evidence |
| D4 Novelty and regime change | shocks, new instruments, balance sheets | residuals the current Γ cannot compose | a frame whose closure differs, contacted by import | the import boundary |

**Order by dependency (Fig 3), not by importance.** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The chain for this question is perception (do we see the shock?) -> vocabulary (do we have a term for financial stability?) -> objective (what counts as progress?) -> planning (the reaction function). Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Rule design sits at the planning link. If the break is at vocabulary, a better rule arrives at a step that cannot use it.

**Partition or union.** The rules-versus-discretion split is a **union**, not a partition. Coordination and adaptation are conditions that can both bind and overlap. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single lever (pick one, or average them) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. Findings

### F1 - Residuals must be read per slot (figure-derived, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."

A single target or a single loss number is a projection of the inflation, activity and financial-stability slots. If the live residual is on financial stability while inflation is on target, averaging reports a small global R and the signal vanishes. This is not an analogy imported from economics; it is the figure's own indexing rule applied to a three-slot mandate. The world literature corroborates it (Taylor rule Wikipedia: "critics argue that its simplified formula focusing primarily on inflation and output may not adequately capture important factors such as financial stability, exchange rates, or structural changes in the economy"), but the verdict comes from Fig 1.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 1 :19/:22 (controller indexing) and the FEDS working paper on risk reading two separate traditions, scenario analysis and distributional forecasting (low overlap: one is a framework indexing rule, the other is central-bank risk practice). The agreement is not from one pool.
- **Refuter:** show that a single aggregate target preserves every live slot residual, that is, that no slot can carry positive mass while the aggregate is near zero.

### F2 - A fixed rule has bounded reach and cannot manufacture novelty (figure-derived, triangulated)

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

A reaction function is a controller policy composed from the bank's model. It reaches every action inside closure(Γ) and no action outside it. A shock whose description is not in Γ (a new asset class, a pandemic, a balance-sheet regime) is an atom the rule cannot compose. No amount of searching the existing rule adds it. Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." A rule running on data it cannot interpret cannot tell a regime it has never seen from one that does not exist.

Corroboration: the Taylor rule page reports "the effective lower bound creates an asymmetric policy space that a fixed rule cannot accommodate; the unobservable inputs (potential output, the natural rate) enter linearly, amplifying real-time measurement error." Orphanides (2003) is reported there as showing the rule "can mislead policymakers who face real-time data." These are secondary and do not decide the verdict.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 6 :21-23 (closure) and Fig 8 :32 (idempotence) are different figures with different constructions; Fig 3 :19 adds the dependency-chain reason why effort on the rule cannot rescue an earlier break. Low overlap between the three routes.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a fixed rule that demonstrably reaches the correct action for a shock whose description is absent from its model, with no import from any frame of differing closure.

### F3 - Rule design is the wrong lever when the break is upstream (figure-derived, single-source plus corroboration)

Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

The chain runs perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning. A rule is a plan. If the bank has no term for an emerging condition, perfecting the reaction function spends effort above the break. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The 2008 financial-stability case is the world instance; the figure supplies the structure.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Triangulation status:** `single-source` within the figures (Fig 3), corroborated by the FEDS paper "Beyond Financial Conditions: Measuring Structural Vulnerabilities in the U.S. Financial System" which notes financial condition indices "measure current credit market conditions and spike during periods of financial turmoil" while structural vulnerabilities build gradually. Different instruments, same shape.
- **Refuter:** show a case where improving the planning link alone, with perception and vocabulary unchanged, closed a residual that originated at perception or vocabulary.

### F4 - The rule is a convention and must remain violable (figure-derived, triangulated)

Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"
Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
Fig 10 :19: "install what can be violated"
Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
Fig 10 :48: "a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority"

This is the figure answer to why a rule is useful at all. Its value is in the expectations domain (D3): a shared reaction function coordinates private expectations, and that coordination is a convention whose product is agreement. But a convention that decides the outcome regardless of the ground has stopped being a convention and has become an authored constant. Hence escape clauses, review clauses and published exceptions are not concessions to discretion; they are the conditions for the rule to remain a convention rather than a constant. A declared target does not ground itself.

World corroboration is unusually strong and came from sources opened this session:

- Svensson (NBER w9421, 2003): "Targeting rules allow the use of judgment and extra-model information, are more robust and easier to verify than optimal instrument rules." This is a commitment to the target, not to the instrument.
- Taylor (1993), as quoted on the Taylor rule page: "There will be episodes where monetary policy will need to be adjusted to deal with special factors." The rule's own author makes it violable.
- Goodhart (1975), via Wikipedia: "Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes." A rigidity that becomes a target ceases to function as a measure.
- Commitment device literature (Wikipedia): "a mechanism or arrangement that implements precommitment by deliberately restricting one's own future options in order to make a commitment credible," with a distinction between "immutable" devices whose consequences are irreversible and "mutable" ones "which allow for reversal," and a finding that "commitment contracts can also backfire."

- **Mode:** `general` on the convention structure, `specified` on the claim that an unviolable rule stops being a convention.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 10 (seat and convention structure) and Fig 2 (anchor and correlation valence) are low-overlap figure routes; the world sources are independent fields (monetary economics, behavioral economics, metatheory) and do not share the figures' evidence pool.
- **Refuter:** show a rule that cannot be violated, refused or checked, and that nevertheless remains a convention rather than an authored constant carrying authority.

### F5 - Discretion is maintenance of the channel, not freedom from the ground (figure-derived, triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"
Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is / you have lost touch"
Fig 10 :29: "keep the anchor reachable"
Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update."

Discretion has a precise figure role: it is the seat work that keeps the anchor reachable when the channel decays. It is not licence to move the target. If the target moves when the bank pushes on it, the anchor has become a frame: Fig 2 :47 "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame." The world literature names the same failure as dynamic inconsistency. Wikipedia (Dynamic inconsistency): "Monetary policy makers suffer from dynamic inconsistency with inflation expectations, as politicians are best off promising lower inflation in the future. But once tomorrow comes lowering inflation may have negative effects, such as increasing unemployment, so they do not make much effort to lower it." Commitment device literature, same page family: "Odysseus limits his future agency and binds himself to a commitment device (i.e., the mast) to survive this perilous example of dynamic inconsistency." The rule is the mast; discretion is knowing when the mast is no longer in the water.

Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A bank that reports success as "we followed the rule" scores itself with a frame-internal quantity. Fig 1 :24-26 disqualifies "credibility accrued" and similar measures explicitly.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 10 (channel maintenance) and Fig 2 (anchor non-updating) plus Fig 1 :27 (no self-scoring) are three different construction routes; world corroboration from dynamic inconsistency and Goodhart.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a bank that updates its own anchor by its own beliefs and nevertheless remains anchored to the ground, or that scores itself by rule-compliance without losing contact.

### F6 - "Combine both" as a blend is a collapse, not a synthesis (figure-derived, triangulated)

Fig 8 :4: "collapse 4: the blend"
Fig 8 :5: "→ vocabulary, never an atom"
Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not"

If "combine" means a single hybrid reaction function that averages rule-like and discretion-like coefficients, the figures name it a blend: a change of vocabulary that adds no atom and swallows novelty to the mean. The union of two conditions (coordination and adaptation) must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). The figure-consistent combination keeps two loops with separate verdicts: a violable convention in D3 and a live ground channel in D2/D4. Their agreement is never pooled as corroboration because they draw on different evidence.

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A rule is a filter for the budget of attention. It cannot hand you the policy verdict; the ground does.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 8 (blend collapse) and Fig 7 (room chain, blending to the mean) are separate figures; Fig 9 :54 supplies the unbundling instruction.
- **Refuter:** show a single averaged hybrid that tracks every slot residual as well as the unbundled pair, including under a shock absent from its model, with no loss of convention checkability.

### F7 - Imports need a source gate, so discretion is not unbounded (figure-derived, single-source)

Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."
Fig 10 :34-35: "provenance" / "shadow test"
Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

When D4 injects a new atom (a new indicator, a new instrument, a new model of financial cascades), that atom is an import. Import carries provenance and a shadow test: where did it come from, and does it explain a gap? A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives. So the figure verdict on unconstrained discretion is not "freedom"; it is "import under gate." The description must travel in effect terms, or it is sharpened into terms only the importing bank can read and the check cannot travel with it.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Triangulation status:** `single-source` within the figures (Fig 6 plus Fig 10's provenance/shadow test), corroborated by the FEDS Taylor-rule estimation paper's finding that "Evidence of 'changing perceptions' of monetary policy via economically meaningful time-varying response coefficients is weak overall," which warns against importing a change of regime merely because coefficients move.
- **Refuter:** show an import with no provenance and no shadow test that nevertheless expanded the reach without debiting independence or admitting a wrong atom.

### F8 - Strains recorded, not hidden

1. **The bank partly produces the data it reads.** Expectations mean the outcome series are not wholly outside the bank. Fig 2 :47 "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame" names this as a standing risk. It is a strain on D2's anchor status, not a refutation: central bank independence is the institutional device for keeping the anchor from updating, and the dynamic-inconsistency literature corroborates why it matters. Recorded as live.
2. **Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom" versus the committee's real capacity to commission new data.** Re-census resolves it: commissioning a new series is an import from a frame with a different closure (the statistical agency, the market), not a mint inside the bank's model. Different objects, no strain after re-census.
3. **A wrong-atom cost is stated but not priced.** Fig 6 :24 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives but gives no number. Unmeasured, named, carried forward.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before the WATSON run, per the standing rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim in section 0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single aggregate target demonstrably preserves every live slot residual (breaks F1, Fig 1 :19/:22).
2. A fixed rule demonstrably reaches the correct action for a shock absent from its model with no import from a frame of differing closure (breaks F2, Fig 6 :21-23, Fig 8 :32).
3. Improving the planning link alone closes a residual that originated at perception or vocabulary (breaks F3, Fig 3 :19).
4. A rule that cannot be violated, refused or checked remains a convention rather than an authored constant (breaks F4, Fig 10 :47/:48).
5. A bank that updates its own anchor by its own beliefs nevertheless remains anchored, or a bank scores itself by rule-compliance without losing contact (breaks F5, Fig 2 :33, Fig 1 :27).
6. A single averaged hybrid tracks every slot residual as well as the unbundled pair under novelty, without loss of convention checkability (breaks F6, Fig 8 :4-5, Fig 9 :54).
7. An import with no provenance and no shadow test expands the reach without debiting independence (breaks F7, Fig 6 :24).

If any fires, the corresponding clause is withdrawn. Until one is run, no clause may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the refuter. Describe R per slot before naming any rules literature: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" (Fig 9 :27-28).
2. State the mode on every substantive claim. An unmeasured structural consequence is a specification of what to measure, not a verdict.
3. Keep the two loops separate in any write-up. Do not present a blended hybrid as the figure result.
4. Treat numerical parameters as bindings from the economics frame, with provenance and a shadow test, never as outputs of the derivation.
5. Send the generator, not the recording. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
6. Use the public institutional venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask what would refute the shape, not whether the reader agrees.

Sent up: the verbatim figure set (Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 2 :33/:41, Fig 6 :21-24, Fig 8 :4-5, Fig 9 :54, Fig 10 :18/:47).
