## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about changing institutional incentives without triggering resistance that restores the status quo. Option A: treat resistance as a conservative force to be overcome with stronger communication and a better written rule. Option B: treat it as the convergence of unanchored frames and change the conditions under which the rule is grounded. A leads to coherent drift toward whoever has most influence, with an averaged satisfaction score that cannot say where to look. B leads to a checkable shape, refuters written in advance, and a ground that closes the question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per affected group, the gap between the incentive the reformer intends and what actually pays after the change. Arity: two conditions, a rule change plus a restoration, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54-57). Symmetry: ordered, arriving after the reform as a negative feedback, not a symmetric relation. Scale: individual behaviour, institution rules, field norms, with a membrane between scales.
- **Domain 1, institution as maintained habitat.** Actors: reformer seat, members, formal rules, informal norms, monitors, instruments. Currency: what actually pays, compliance, waste. Anchor: the measured outcome a counter, market, board or patient outcome returns. Maintained, not unmaintained. Union with D2.
- **Domain 2, members' frame and the resistance.** Actors: members updating on each other, incumbents holding influence. Currency: agreement and influence. Anchor: none inside the frame, so it must be supplied from D1. Collapse 1, coherent drift.
- **Domain 3, the reformer's seat.** Actors: reformer, ground, members. Currency: conventions versus verdicts and atoms, and the stake. Anchor: the ground, which must not update. Seat, not frame.
- **Domain 4, reform content.** Actors: the institution's closure of expressible incentives. Currency: atoms versus arrangements. Anchor: source gate (provenance) and shadow test. Closure question.
- **Domain 5, pace and measurement.** Actors: the five dependency links, affected groups as slots. Currency: per-slot residuals and round trips. Anchor: an instrument at each link, ground pressure. Ordered chain.
- **Order by dependency.** Fix the anchor (D1/D3), describe the residual, find the broken link (D5), then choose a frame (D4). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe before looking (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :32 "introduce, never subtract" governs the seat's power over a habitat; negation of a written rule is a different operator (Operators :58). Different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains and is stated in the TETHER section below.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no named reform does. Resistance is the convergence of unanchored frames toward whoever holds influence, and it is coherent, so it is invisible from inside. The failure is a union compound with two loops. A reform strategy has the shape: anchor to a measure incumbents cannot move; range each group against it separately, not by negotiation; change what pays, not only what is written; introduce monitors, instruments and comparison sites rather than relying on subtraction; declare the reformer's stake; find the broken link before spending effort; report per-group residuals; select differing pilots. The framework returns no policy name. The live strain is whether a world-frame measure can serve as an anchor without moving when pushed. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three refuters and the strain before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a finished policy list, instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The binding from world frames onto atoms (which measure, which monitor) is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold it. A circulated claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An Anchor Test showing that every candidate measure moves with incumbent influence would break the strategy at its first term; two groups whose agreement predicts which changes the ground pays for would break Fig 2 :40; a first loop that fails to settle yet composes successfully into a second institution would break Fig 12 :104; an instrument at the objective link with the failure unchanged would move the break to another link; and a derivation of a new incentive primitive inside the old closure would break Fig 6 :23. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
