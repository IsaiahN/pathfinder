Order by dependency: describe the residual, fix an anchor, instrument the objective link, import if needed, then iterate by selection. Union between D1 and D2, not a partition.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :13-15 and :29-35 (anchor, drift, triangulation), Fig 10 :47-60 (convention, stake), Fig 11 :40-61 (contact, introduction), Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` §2: anchor, contact, introduce, declare, per-slot, differing pilots.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional reform: publish a new metric, add a monitor, run two differing pilots, report by group.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **Anchor Test**: the figures require the anchor not to move when pushed (Fig 2 :30), but a published metric can be captured by the coalition that reports it. The shorthand says "anchor" as if the test were already passed;
- the **union split**: Fig 9 :54 requires two loops, while a concrete programme tends to run one change-management loop over both conditions;
- the **declared stake**: Fig 10 :59 disqualifies an interest in the reading, but a programme led by the reformer's office usually ties that office's standing to a favourable verdict.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: without an anchor, members converge on influence, and changing what is written is not changing what pays. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
