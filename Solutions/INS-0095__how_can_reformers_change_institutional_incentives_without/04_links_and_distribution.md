## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole frame before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source.
- **Fig 7 :35** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A send selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The higher the cost of a wrong reform, the more genuinely different the checking venues should be.
- Prestige rule from the validation essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action." A venue's prestige is a contact-cost estimate, never an evidence score.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as failure; (3) record that verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. No framework. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure here?
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three public links. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled as later corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user names their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and states the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The live strain in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7 must be carried in the version sent. Do not polish it away.
