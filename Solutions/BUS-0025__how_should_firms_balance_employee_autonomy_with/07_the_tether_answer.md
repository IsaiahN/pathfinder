# The Tether's answer on autonomy versus monitoring and control

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading. No per-slot measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what management's library predicts an autonomy/monitoring/standardization arrangement will return and what a work slot actually pays stays small in some slots and periods and grows in others. R is indexed per employee-task slot, never pooled (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about the **shape of the arrangement**, not about a level of autonomy or a quantity of monitoring.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is four conditions with two anchors, not one dial:

- **C1:** the slot's channel to what pays stays reachable.
- **C2:** what crosses as a standard is a re-applicable method or head start, not a replay or a fixed verdict.
- **C3:** the checking arrangement holds frames that differ at the evidence level.
- **C4:** whether the arrangement is good for the worker is ranged against human judgment.

## 2. The shape

1. **The paying outcome is the anchor for production; human judgment is the anchor for the person question.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a market, a patient outcome"*; :36-37 says *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Engagement scores, sentiment and manager ratings are instruments that can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Never score the arrangement with a quantity the arrangement produces (Fig 1 :27).
2. **Monitoring is a channel, not the enemy.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"keep the anchor reachable"*. A slot loses its reading when percepts stale, metrics saturate or readings pool. Fig 7 :37-39 gives the two-factor form: bracket fidelity times ground pressure, zero either stops the chain.
3. **Standardization is a head start, not a replay or a verdict.** Fig 4 :22-24: methods cross up, head starts come down, never a recording of one occasion or a replay of what worked once. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* A fully scripted role is a substituted habitat whose silent failures appear only when the goal fails (Fig 11 :66-69).
4. **Autonomy supplies differing closures.** Fig 7 :35-36: *"Selection between rooms is where the ground votes"* and *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 2 :31 defines independence; Fig 8 :48-49 makes it one quantity read twice. If every employee holds the same closure and the same evidence, the firm is one long room. Autonomy is the structural supply of different rooms, not a benefit that accrues regardless of contact.
5. **Control is a seat, not an atom.** Fig 11 :37: the seat enumerates the habitat, ranks by cascade, introduces actors, builds instruments, reads the ground. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Authority added without contact change does not change capability.
6. **Renewal needs a differing closure.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent. Internal recombination of the same standards never adds an atom.
7. **The good practice can change sign.** Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual and no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and *"nothing records which a term is"*. Operators :58: a vocabulary without negation can only accumulate, so a control regime needs a refusal term, not just more standards.
8. **The break is usually at the middle link.** Fig 3 :25-27 and :29: the firm can count keystrokes but has no terms for what changed work affords or what progress would count as. More monitoring budget lands above the break.
9. **Two teams with the same ingredients can still differ.** Under Fig 12 :117 they are isomers if the bonds differ: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance."* An inventory of metrics and standards cannot predict which arrangement pays.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No per-slot prospective measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a dial setting. It returns no autonomy percentage, monitoring ratio, standard count or optimal span. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not a standing list of good practices. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not that autonomy is intrinsically good or monitoring intrinsically bad. Both change valence with the residual; the refusal is of saturated and pooled readings, not of contact.
- Not that managers, culture, standards or trust are irrelevant. They are Γ, the library operand and the seat. The claim is that Γ and the seat do not settle the outcome on their own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage of variance attributable to autonomy, monitoring or leadership. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not corroboration by agreement. The work-design studies partly share one survey evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The cybernetics and animal-behaviour sources corroborate the mechanism shape at a different scale, not the organizational fact.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are output, quality, satisfaction and turnover; the candidate sources are lost ground contact, channel decay, closure exhaustion, habitat change, a valence flip and a person-ground failure.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08` and `09`:

1. one intervention restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue;
2. a monitoring metric that is a sufficient statistic for the paid outcome across habitat changes;
3. output declining while readings are current, unsaturated and unpooled, with the ground paying comparable slots;
4. identical closures and evidence yet continuing ground-verified new readings across changes;
5. a replay with no re-application producing continuing ground-verified readings;
6. control authority rising with unchanged contact and still moving the paid outcome;
7. a practice's valence invariant across a documented change of residual.

A prospective per-slot study that measures C1 to C4 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, uses two anchors, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources for a falling return, plus an anchor-faithful reading for the person question. Naming a winner from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The four conditions are a shape; the fifth thing, which specific allocation pays in this firm, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return an autonomy percentage or name the winning practice. It says that "balancing autonomy with monitoring" is a union of four conditions, not one dial. The slot's channel to what pays has to stay reachable, so monitoring is legitimate as contact and fails when readings go stale, saturate or get pooled into an average. What crosses as a standard has to be a re-applicable method or head start, not a replay of one success or a convention that fixes an outcome. The checking arrangement has to hold frames that differ at the evidence level, which is what autonomy structurally supplies; a single shared closure is one long room where agreement compounds and evidence does not. And whether the arrangement is good for the worker is a separate question with its own anchor, the worker's own judgment, not an output reading. A practice that helped can change sign when the work changes, so there is no standing list. What is offered is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which allocation pays in a given firm stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
