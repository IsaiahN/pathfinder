## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among the library Γ, the arrangement a and the paid outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time. A two-place reading (autonomy, performance) loses the arrangement and the prediction, so it is rejected. "Better" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), not a conjunction.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The paying ground does not update on management's opinion (Fig 2 :25), and the employee is not interchangeable with the manager. Autonomy and control are not opposite values of one quantity, because they bind to different operands: autonomy to the employee's own closure, control to the seat and the channel.
- **Scale:** the slot is an employee-task pair, but "good" is assessed at the team or firm population and across time. The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).

The R_T for that transform is exactly what the primary audit must measure, and section 4 does.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's four-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *good work design keeps the worker in contact with what pays and keeps the arrangement fresh.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific team. Result: *listen to customers and introduce new practices.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a team failing C3 merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor split. "Contact with what pays" does not say that human judgment anchors the person question (Fig 2 :36-37). Coming back down, it permits satisfaction to be used as if it were the paid outcome (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the method-versus-recording distinction. "Introduce new practices" does not distinguish a head start from a replay (Fig 4 :23-24) or a convention from a verdict (Fig 10 :47).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep the arrangement fresh" has no place for a formerly good practice changing sign when the residual changes (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would separate lost ground contact from channel decay from closure exhaustion, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
