## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0025 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about a work arrangement that can be stated before any percentage, metric, practice or named theory is chosen.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *an arrangement pays while its channel to what pays stays reachable, what crosses as a standard is a method rather than a replay, its checking frames differ at the evidence level, and the person question is ranged against human judgment.*
- Name, withheld: *give 80% autonomy, track keystrokes, use this standard, adopt self-determination theory, adopt the job characteristics model, ban surveillance.*

The distinction matters here because the management literature is almost entirely in the naming business. SDT names autonomy support, competence and relatedness; the job-characteristics tradition names skill variety, task identity, significance, autonomy and feedback; the monitoring literature names reactance, trust and privacy calculus. Those are bindings from the organizational frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "employee autonomy", "electronic performance monitoring", "standardization", "managerial control", "job design", "empowerment", "trust" and "algorithmic management". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (a per-slot gap between what management predicts an arrangement will return and what the work pays) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0025 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57) rather than one hard question with a middle setting.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the four conditions C1 to C4 are stated, the instrument for each is named, the valence flip is stated, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0025 neither has happened for a specific allocation. There is no prospective, per-slot measurement that pays for "this monitoring density caused this outcome", and the work-design names were adopted without a shadow test at the firm scale. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a management explanation makes when it reads a drop in output and names its source. A falling return is a reading. It is consistent with lost ground contact, saturated or pooled metrics, an exhausted closure, a changed habitat, a practice that changed valence, or a person-ground failure. The reading does not determine the source. The literature's habit of naming the source from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory. The cybernetics witness is interesting here: Beer's average-temperature example is a low-overlap instance of the same rule, where an average reading misses the one signal that matters (Fig 1 :22).

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to work-design research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that firms behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous management thinkers or celebrated companies that agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular standard or practice is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a routine, metric or standard is already available in the firm's library, naming it is a binding from the management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. Two anchors, one correction to §1

In §1 I wrote as though the answer were anchored only by what the work pays. That is incomplete. Fig 2 :36-37 says an anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it, and that human judgment is the right anchor for *"is this a good answer for a person"*. The question "should firms do X" silently asks both whether X pays and whether X is good for the worker. Those are two questions with two anchors, and the verdicts must be kept separate. The correction does not change the shape; it adds a fourth condition C4 and forbids averaging the person verdict into the production verdict. Both formulations are kept.

## 8. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about work arrangements. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0025 the canonical shape is: **a four-condition conjunction with four distinct failure modes, a valve that can change sign, and two anchors.** It is not a dial setting, not a monitoring ratio, not a practice list, and not a ranking of management styles. The names that appear in the work-design literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are a per-slot channel check, a method-versus-recording check, an independence check, a person-ground reading, and a source discriminator for failure attribution. They are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
