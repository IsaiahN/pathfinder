# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about autonomy and monitoring before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about work design that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "balancing autonomy with monitoring and control" is not one dial with a right setting. It is four conditions with four different failure modes and four different instruments.
>
> 1. **The channel condition.** The employee's route to what the work actually pays, a customer, an artifact, a patient outcome, has to stay reachable. Monitoring is legitimate as that channel. It fails when readings go stale, saturate (everyone is green) or get pooled into a team average. The market keeps paying someone; the firm stops hearing who.
> 2. **The method condition.** What crosses to the employee has to be a re-applicable method or a head start that makes the next search cheaper. Standardization fails when it is a recording of one past success replayed downward, or when a convention becomes an outcome-fixing verdict.
> 3. **The independence condition.** The checking arrangement needs frames that differ at the evidence level. If every employee gets the same routine, the same review and the same evidence, internal agreement compounds while evidence does not. Autonomy is the structural source of those differing frames, not a mood.
> 4. **The person condition.** Whether an arrangement is good for the worker is a separate question with its own anchor, the worker's own judgment. An output reading cannot settle it.
>
> The further claim is that a practice which helped can change sign when the work changes, so a standing list of good practices is not an answer.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name the right percentage, which metrics to use, or which tasks to standardize. That is a gap at the instrument boundary, and I am reporting it as a gap rather than filling it. I am not claiming autonomy is intrinsically good, that monitoring is the enemy, that any particular firm is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of work design. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. one intervention restores all four conditions in all failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind;
> 2. a monitoring metric is a sufficient statistic for what the work pays across habitat changes, so the metric and the ground never diverge;
> 3. a team's output falls while its readings are current, unsaturated and unpooled, and the ground keeps paying comparable teams;
> 4. a team where everyone shares one closure and one evidence base keeps producing new, ground-verified readings across changes in the work;
> 5. a replay of one past success, with no re-application, keeps producing new, ground-verified readings across habitat changes;
> 6. control authority increases with unchanged contact and nevertheless moves the paid outcome per slot;
> 7. a practice's measured value stays the same across a documented change in what the work rewards.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers: the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Four conditions kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Two anchors, not one | *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* (Fig 2 :37) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the right practice | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) and *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* (Fig 11 :51) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* (Fig 10 :59)
