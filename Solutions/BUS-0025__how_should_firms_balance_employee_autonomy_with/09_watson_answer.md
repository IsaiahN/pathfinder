# WATSON answer, BUS-0025

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

How should firms balance employee autonomy with monitoring, standardization, and managerial control?

Restated as an effect: what shape must an arrangement a have so that R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stays small per employee-task slot s as the slot and its habitat change? R is indexed per slot, never pooled (Fig 1 :19). There are two anchors: the paying outcome for the production question and human judgment for the person question (Fig 2 :29, :36-37). The answer would be a conjunction of conditions with distinct instruments, not a dial setting.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among management's library Γ, the arrangement a and the paid outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "good" is a threshold relative to a slot's own ground and to alternatives (Fig 12 :87). Scale: employee loop, team/firm population, and the time channel; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** a work arrangement keeps its slot residual small while four conditions hold, and each is a separate verdict never averaged with the others. C1: the channel to what pays stays reachable, so monitoring is contact and fails as stale, saturated or pooled readings. C2: what crosses as a standard is a method or head start, not a replay or an outcome-fixing convention. C3: the checking arrangement holds frames that differ at the evidence level, which autonomy structurally supplies; one shared closure is one long room. C4: whether the arrangement is good for the worker is anchored in human judgment, not the output reading. A practice that helped can change sign when the residual changes, so there is no standing list. Mode `specified` on these claims, `general` on the union and structure, `gap` on the allocation. Control is a seat acting on a habitat, not a free-floating good; reach renews only through a differing closure. The work-design literature corroborates the shape only after scale and anchor are fixed; the cybernetics and animal-behaviour witnesses corroborate the mechanism shape, not the organizational fact. Which allocation pays is a declared gap, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A team failing C2 would be sold more monitoring, a team failing C3 would be given another standard, and a person-ground failure would be treated as a productivity problem. A single lever would be applied to a union that needs four. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, and Fig 2 :42 prices the guard: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) one intervention restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue, (2) a monitoring metric that is a sufficient statistic for the paid outcome across habitat changes, (3) output falling with current, unsaturated and unpooled readings while comparable slots keep being paid, (4) identical closures and evidence yet continuing ground-verified readings, (5) a replay producing continuing ground-verified readings, (6) control authority rising with unchanged contact and moving the outcome, or (7) invariant practice valence across a change of residual. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot outcomes are fully predicted by a single autonomy or monitoring variable would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
