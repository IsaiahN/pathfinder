## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not a point on a dial between two nouns. It is the shape of the control arrangement that keeps a work slot's prediction error small while the slot and its habitat change. In effect terms (Formula step 1, Symbols table `R`):

- **s** is the employee-task slot. R is measured per slot, not pooled across employees (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).
- **b** is management's belief about what the employee will do, formed from its own history and inherited priors.
- **a** is the arrangement installed: autonomy granted, monitoring density, standardization of routine, managerial approval and direction.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the firm's library of routines and instruments predicts that arrangement will produce.
- **o′** is what the slot actually returns to the paying ground: a customer outcome, an artifact, a patient outcome, a quality or safety result (Fig 2 :29 names these as anchors).

- Option A, "more autonomy is the answer": leads to the prediction that removing monitoring and standardization reduces R per slot.
- Option B, "more control is the answer": leads to the prediction that adding monitoring, standards and approval reduces R per slot.
- Option C, "take a middle point on a single autonomy-control dial": leads to the prediction that the two are one quantity read in opposite directions, so a compromise score is an answer.

The derivation returns none of these as written. It returns a **union of conditions with distinct failure modes and distinct instruments**: a reachable channel to what pays (monitoring as contact), methods that make the next search cheaper rather than replay a success (standardization as head start), and frames that differ at the evidence level so selection between them is possible (autonomy as the supply of differing closures). Whether an arrangement is good for the person is a fourth question with its own anchor, human judgment (Fig 2 :36-37). Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula, "Direction"): an arrangement is a four-condition union, not a dial setting. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` section 4 can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the management question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of work design.
- No universal autonomy percentage, no monitoring ratio, and no standing list of good practices.

Four figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. There is no standing list of good actors. *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* (Fig 11 :51). A generic "autonomy is good" or "monitoring is bad" rule is exactly the list the figure refuses.
5. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
