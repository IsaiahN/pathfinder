# The Tether's answer: which institutional arrangements share shock costs without undermining long-term investment?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, `gap` on the unique named arrangement. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a measurement.

## 1. The residual it answers

A shock is a residual indexed by slot: a region, sector, household cohort or balance sheet takes the hit, not a homogeneous population. Two conditions are wanted at once: spread the loss so no slot collapses, and keep the directed difference that turns current resources into future productive use. This is a **union compound** with an overlapping intersection, not one hard optimisation problem. Fig 9 :54 says "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again", and Fig 9 :56 says "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". An arrangement that sits in both conditions, such as a guarantee that pools risk and also changes who takes risk, needs its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Read the shock per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A national mean tells you the size of the shock, not where to aim the sharing.
2. **Pooling is a network, investment is a gradient, and they are not the same thing.** Fig 13 :24: "that entities relate, and influence travels." Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent." Fig 13 :46: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." Full equalisation produces a distribution; a distribution has no directedness, so it cannot carry investment. Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." The two compose through a credible state, not by being summed into one score.
3. **Coverage can be introduced; exposure cannot be removed.** Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." So a sharing arrangement is permanent work, not a solved state. A viable one is a replenishment cycle rather than a one-off (Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input.").
4. **A neutral rule that starts picking winners has left its office.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." This is the figure-grounded form of the commitment problem: the arrangement must not become the grader of the investment it is meant to protect.
5. **The ground is measured outcomes.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A panel of agreeing economists is a room, not an anchor. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
6. **The framework returns a shape, not a named winner.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Unemployment insurance, deposit insurance, index insurance, stabilisation funds and mutual-aid networks are bindings imported from economics and anthropology with provenance; no figure line selects among them. The honest verdict on the unique name is `gap`: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named institution is the answer. Names are imports, not derivations.
- Not that full consumption smoothing is desirable without qualification. Full equalisation removes the gradient that the question asks to preserve.
- Not that sharing and investment can be traded off on one scale. They are two structures, priced and settled separately.
- Not that professional agreement settles anything. Agreement among frames sharing an evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here decides which arrangement works in a given society.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the questions are:

- **Per-slot test:** do local shortfall readings predict transfer needs better than a national aggregate? If not, finding 2 falls.
- **Gradient test:** does measured long-term investment survive high coverage, and at what coverage does it begin to fall? This is the empirical form of Fig 13 :46.
- **Scale test:** does the network's reach match the risk's reach (correlated regional shocks defeat a narrow pool)? World corroboration: Cronk and Aktipis (2021) list network scale as design principle 7.
- **Seam test:** does the rule start determining who survives a shock, so that it has become a verdict rather than a convention (Fig 10 :47)?
- **Standing refuters:** full equalisation leaves measured investment unchanged or higher; network and gradient prove to be one structure; a national aggregate predicts local shortfalls as well as local readings; a figure line names an institution.

## 5. The honest stop

The shape stops where the instruments stop. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). What is reached is a kind of arrangement, with four constraints and written refuters. What is not reached is the unique name. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62). Agreement from this folder's own sources is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not pick a winning institution. It says the question is a union of two conditions and must be unbundled first. A shock arrives per slot, so a national average hides where to aim; pooling is a network in which influence travels, while investment is a spendable gradient with direction, and a flat distribution is not a gradient. Any viable arrangement must therefore read the loss locally, spread it through a network whose scale matches the risk, keep that gradient alive, and avoid becoming the rule that decides who survives. Which named institution actually does this is settled by measured outcomes in the target society, not by expert agreement. What the framework offers is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
