## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 says "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a shape is the narrowed form of an institutional answer that can be stated before a specific institution is selected. It constrains: the arrangement must read the shock per slot, spread it through a matching network, preserve a spendable gradient toward future use, and be settled by outcomes. It does not fill the slot with "unemployment insurance" or "a sovereign fund". Those are names.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary closest to the residual, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is a loss arriving unevenly and a future investment that must survive it. The cause, whether a slump, a pandemic, a bank run or a drought, is not named by the figures.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place; the answer is "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Concretely for this question: the effect vocabulary is per-slot loss, spread through relations, directed future use. The institutional vocabulary (tax, transfer, premium, fund, guarantee) is borrowed from economics and must be declared as an import.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical rule: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that means measured consumption smoothing and measured long-term investment, in the same society and period, with the residual described first.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Transferred here: an observed fall in investment volatility does not tell you *which* institution produced it. Several institutional paths give the same reading. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error; what is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So this simulated derivation cannot certify its own answer.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework say an answer to this question would look like?** A shape with four constraints, stated before any institution is named:

1. The shock is registered per slot (household, sector, region), not folded into a national average.
2. The loss is spread through a network whose scale matches the risk's scale; coverage can be introduced but exposure cannot be removed, so this is permanent maintenance.
3. A spendable gradient toward future productive use survives; full equalisation is a distribution, not a gradient, and so cannot be the whole answer.
4. Which arrangement actually works is settled by measured outcomes in the target society, with the provenance of each imported institution recorded.

**Does the framework name the winner?** No. Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." The unique name is a `gap` verdict, not a silence.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially applied the rule as if it forbade naming any institution. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in the library. Here the relevant structures, network and gradient, are already among the six (Fig 13). The question asks for an *arrangement over held structures*, and the framework's own text says the arrangements do not run out even though the set is closed (Fig 13 :115-118).

So the correct statement is: naming a specific institution is **not** forbidden, but a name is a **binding imported from the economics or anthropology frame**, not a figure derivation. What is forbidden is claiming the figure produced the name, or claiming a unique best name without a ground reading. The metamerism point in section 4 stands, and for the same reason: the reading does not determine the source. This correction is kept visible rather than quietly replacing the earlier reading.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of distinguished economists who endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets measured outcomes settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any institution and must be labelled as such.
