## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Shock sharing and investment preservation are a union of two conditions, not one quantity, and the overlap is a compound with its own ground. A shock is registered per slot, not as a national mean. Risk pooling is a network, influence travelling between participants; investment is a spendable gradient with direction. Those are different structures, so full equalisation removes the gradient the question asks to preserve. Any viable arrangement must read the loss locally, spread it through a network whose scale matches the risk, keep the gradient alive, and avoid becoming the rule that decides who survives. The figures return that shape and explicitly do not name a winning institution; named institutions are imports from other frames, to be settled by measured outcomes.

### C2: Structural, checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** pooling (network) and investment (gradient) are distinct structures; a flat distribution is not a gradient; they compose across kinds, never within one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels"; Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent"; Fig 13 :46 "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness"; Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
- **What to check:** is "influence travels" genuinely different from "can be spent and has direction"? Does the world evidence support a tradeoff, for example Einav and Finkelstein (2018): "the presence of moral hazard leads optimal insurance contracts to be incomplete, striking a balance between reducing risk and maintaining incentives"?
- **Refuter:** derive directedness and spendability from the network relation alone, or show that full equalisation leaves measured long-term investment unchanged or higher. Either breaks the shape.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking of unemployment insurance, deposit insurance, index insurance, sovereign funds or mutual aid. Each is a binding imported from another frame, with provenance.
- Not that full consumption smoothing is good without qualification. The question itself asks to preserve the investment gradient.
- Not that sharing and investment can be summed into one welfare score. They are two structures with different anchors.
- Not that agreement among specialists settles the question. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"; Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not `grounded`. No measured outcome in this packet decides the case.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

1. **Per-slot test:** do local readings predict shortfalls better than a national aggregate?
2. **Gradient test:** at what coverage does measured long-term investment begin to fall?
3. **Scale test:** does the pool's reach match the correlation scale of the shock?
4. **Seam test:** has the rule begun to decide which firms or households survive, turning a convention into a verdict (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one")?
