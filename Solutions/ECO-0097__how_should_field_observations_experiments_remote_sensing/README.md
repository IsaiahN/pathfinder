## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Adding more ecologists who agree cannot substitute for a ground that settles.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends the only thing they contribute.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*; Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
