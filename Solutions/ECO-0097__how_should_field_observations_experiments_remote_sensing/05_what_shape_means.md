## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* while *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ECO-0097 a **shape** is the form of a combination you can state before you can name the winner. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape is: a union of conditions kept separate, residuals indexed per slot, a ground nobody can move, independence bought and recorded, imports with provenance, and a measured round trip gap. "A fixed optimal weighting over five inputs" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the bootstrapped terms are residual, slot, anchor, evidence pool, closure, import, and round trip.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is forecast error against an arriving observation, not the vocabulary of any one method.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the packet asks an ecologist what they would expect to see if the union claim were true, before giving them the figures.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The eight essays are the process that produced the shape; they are not an external check on it.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the exact warrant for F1 in `01`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means a forecast scored against observations that arrived after it was issued, with uncertainty stated in advance.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A harvester's seasonal indicator can be imported as a constraint on a model, but only with a record of where it came from and an account of which gap it explains.

An earlier pass of this file overstated the rule as a ban on naming any method. Corrected: *"shape, not a name"* governs a missing primitive. Field observations, satellites, experiments, Indigenous knowledge and models are all already held frames. Selecting among them is a binding, to be provenanced, not an invented seventh way of knowing. What remains forbidden is naming the winner as if the framework derived it.

## 4. Why the blend is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can all give the reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

An ensemble score that fits the same field observations is a projection. Several mechanisms (or several evidence chains) can give the same fit, so the fit cannot name the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation: a new measurement, a manipulation, or an independent pattern that only one mechanism predicts. This is the metamerism point applied to equifinality, and it corroborates F5 and F6 from the human-cognition of measurement rather than from ecology.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A combination whose score is computed from the same data used to tune it is a frame scoring itself, which Fig 1 :27 disqualifies.

## 5. Q: does "shape" forbid a combination at all?

**Answered, no further question needed.** No. It forbids presenting the combination as the derived winner. It permits, and requires, stating the form the combination must have. The form is in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 2. The moment someone asks "which of the five is best", they have turned a shape into a name and left what the framework can license.

**Does this folder commit that error?** No, provided F9 stays a `gap`. If a later reader finds the winner named in `07` or `09` without a ground reading, that is the metamerism error and should be retracted.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous ecologists who endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tested, then lets an arriving observation settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
