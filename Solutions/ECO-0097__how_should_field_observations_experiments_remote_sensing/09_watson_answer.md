## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about combining five ways of knowing to improve ecological prediction. Option A: treat the question as a contest among five methods and answer with a single optimal weighting or a best method. Option B: treat it as a union of conditions and answer with a shape that keeps per-slot residuals, frame independence, an immovable ground and a provenance record, while declaring the winner a `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a flat formula that records ingredients and not the bond, spends the independence of its frames, and offers an average that hides the failing slot. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Forecast error per ecological slot (population, site, season, indicator, decision). Arity: a union of at least three conditions, state updating, mechanism discrimination and validation governance (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: not symmetric. The bond between two frames' forecasts is a threshold comparison, and the forecast-analysis cycle is ordered. Scale: leaf to continent, event to living memory, individual to food web; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 Field observations.** Actors: observers, plots, sensors, specimens. Currency: in situ measurement, temporal frequency, representativeness error. Anchor: the repeatable measurement at a named site. Admitted.
- **D2 Experiments.** Actors: manipulations, treatments, controls, natural experiments. Currency: intervention-induced change. Anchor: the manipulated outcome, or a pattern rival mechanisms cannot produce. Admitted.
- **D3 Remote sensing.** Actors: satellites, drones, observation operators. Currency: spatially continuous repeated radiance with bias and gaps. Anchor: the calibrated reading with stated uncertainty. Admitted.
- **D4 Indigenous knowledge.** Actors: harvesters, Elders, rangers, governance bodies. Currency: long time depth, qualitative indicators, local causal hypotheses. Anchor: validation within that system's own protocols. Admitted.
- **D5 Models.** Actors: parameters, states, priors, ensembles. Currency: probabilistic prediction with uncertainty decomposition. Anchor: out-of-sample forecast verification. Admitted.
- **D6 Forecast-analysis cycle.** Actors: forecast, analysis, residual, provenance record. Currency: uncertainty-weighted updates. Anchor: the observation that arrives later. Admitted; this is the compound where the "how" lives.
- **X1 A single best method.** No nameable anchor outside the frame that prefers it. Not a domain; absorbed as the blend collapse.
- **X2 Translate everything into one currency.** Assumes one frame's validator may validate another. Refused; see the re-census note below.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D3 and D2/D4 form overlapping compounds; D5 draws on all four. Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (per-slot residual) → vocabulary (what each frame can express, with uncertainty) → objective (a forecast scored against a later observation) → planning (the forecast-analysis cycle) → learn and carry (provenance and R_T). Fig 3 :27, *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**Strain test applied.** The figures speak of one ground; the world supplies several validation protocols. Re-census resolves the apparent conflict: the ground settles forecasts; protocols are conventions until one decides an outcome (Fig 10 :46-47). Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single combination does not. Split the union before search and keep the three conditions separate. Score R per slot and refuse an averaged skill number as a verdict. Name the arriving observation as the anchor and let nobody move it. Buy independence by checking evidence pools, and keep convergence distinct from adoption with a provenance record. Treat Indigenous knowledge as a frame with its own validation and governance, imported with a source gate and a shadow test. Ship an ordered forecast-analysis cycle with an enumerated habitat and a measured R_T. The winner for a given ecosystem and slot is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the winner, nothing `grounded`. The one world conflict (one-currency integration) was returned to the census and X2 was refused.

- **Cost if wrong:** a combination could be adopted that scores well in aggregate while the slot driving a decision keeps failing; the independence of field, satellite and Indigenous frames could be spent by treating them as corroboration from one pool; and an indigenous knowledge holder could be made a validator of a model rather than a partner with their own authority. Fig 2 :42 prices this: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is real and partly irreversible in the governance case, because trust once spent is not recovered by a later correction.
- **Undoable:** mostly. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded with provenance (Fig 10 :62), so a later reader can locate the error. The governance cost is only partly undoable, which is why Fig 10 :59 requires the stake to be declared before the work starts.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the seven pre-registered refuters: (1) one fixed weighted combination beating every separate stream on held-out slots across several ecosystems with no residue; (2) pooling residuals improving the decision; (3) Indigenous criteria fully reducible to model criteria; (4) all five sharing one evidence pool yet agreement still tracking the ground; (5) equifinal models discriminated with no new observation; (6) a figure line directly governing the winner; (7) R_T measured at zero. A source-discriminating observation, an independently calibrated satellite product, or a governance arrangement in which knowledge holders control the use of their evidence would be ground readings, not endorsements.
