## 1. Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census before using the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is forecast error indexed by ecological slot: population, site, season, indicator, decision. Arity: three coupled conditions, state updating, mechanism discrimination and validation governance, so a **union** rather than a single hard question (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: not symmetric. The relation between two forecasts is a threshold comparison, and the forecast-analysis sequence is ordered. Scale: nested, from a leaf and plot to a catchment and continent, from an event to living memory; the transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 Field observations.** Actors: observers, plots, sensors, specimens. Currency: in situ measurement with representativeness error. Anchor: the repeatable measurement at a named site. Admitted.
- **D2 Experiments.** Actors: manipulations, treatments, controls. Currency: intervention-induced change. Anchor: the manipulated outcome, or a pattern rival mechanisms cannot produce. Admitted.
- **D3 Remote sensing.** Actors: satellites, drones, observation operators. Currency: spatially continuous radiance with bias and gaps. Anchor: the calibrated reading with uncertainty. Admitted.
- **D4 Indigenous knowledge.** Actors: harvesters, Elders, rangers, governance bodies. Currency: long time depth, qualitative indicators, local causal hypotheses. Anchor: validation within its own protocols. Admitted.
- **D5 Models.** Actors: parameters, states, priors, ensembles. Currency: probabilistic prediction. Anchor: out-of-sample verification. Admitted.
- **D6 Forecast-analysis cycle.** Actors: forecast, analysis, residual, provenance record. Currency: uncertainty-weighted update. Anchor: the observation that arrives later. Admitted as the compound where the "how" lives.
- **X1 A single best method.** No anchor outside the preferring frame. Not a domain.
- **X2 One common currency for all five.** Makes one frame's validator sovereign over another. Refused on re-census.

Union, not partition. Order by dependency: perception (per-slot residual) → vocabulary (what each frame can express and with what uncertainty) → objective (a forecast scored against a later observation) → planning (the cycle) → learn and carry (provenance and R_T). I refuse before mint (Formula step 2) and describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's five evidence lines, the compound loop and the two refused candidates. I reached the same boundaries from different starting figures: I began with Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's bond typology, where the primary began with Fig 1's per-slot residual and Fig 2's anchor rule. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :31, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :21, Fig 9 :54, Fig 11 :43, Fig 5 :72.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** split the union; score per slot; name the anchor; buy independence; import with provenance; measure the gap; stop honestly.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field protocol naming which stream constrains which slot, at which scale, with which uncertainty, and which observation settles it.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down unless explicitly restored:

- per-slot indexing collapses into an overall skill score;
- evidence-pool independence collapses into a count of methods used;
- provenance collapses into a citation list;
- the stop at the instrument limit collapses into a recommendation to collect more data.

One thing survives cleanly: a union must be unbundled and each frame ranged against a ground it cannot move. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

**On internal consistency: the solution passes.** The twelve files agree with each other and with the verbatim figure text. **On corroboration: none from this seat.** The second seat shares the primary's evidence pool. By Fig 2 :40 its agreement is the pool talking, and by Fig 7 :13 a mirror cannot add evidence. What this audit establishes is that the folder is internally consistent, not that the ecological claim is true.

**On the claim: `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the winner, nothing `grounded`.** The shape survives: split the union, score per slot, keep the arriving observation as an anchor, buy and record independence, import with a source gate, ship an ordered cycle with a measured R_T, stop at the instrument limit. The single combination does not survive, and neither does the claim that agreement among the five is corroboration. Nothing here is settled. A forecast scored after issuance on held-out slots, a source-discriminating observation, or a governance arrangement in which knowledge holders control the use of their evidence would be required to move it to `grounded`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

**Recommendation.** Send the packet with its mode and refuters attached, ask what would refute it, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration. Keep the shape claim and the refuter in front of the links. If refuter 1 or 2 fires, retract the shape before any further distribution.
