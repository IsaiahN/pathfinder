# Expert verification packet - the Tether on combining ecological evidence

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecological methodologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the barrier rules, `gap` on the winner. Not `grounded`.** No field dataset was analysed and no measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the ecological question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The five ways of knowing do not combine into one optimal weight. The question is a union of state updating, mechanism discrimination and validation governance, and the union must be unbundled before search. Forecast residual R is indexed per population, site, season and indicator, never pooled into a single skill score. The ground that settles a forecast is the observation arriving after it, and no frame may move it. Field, satellite, experimental, Indigenous and model evidence are separate frames only when their evidence pools differ, and their agreement counts only with a provenance record. Indigenous knowledge validates within its own protocols and governance; importing it requires a source gate and a shadow test, not translation into model variables. The arrangement is an ordered forecast-analysis cycle with a measured round trip gap. Which combination wins for a given ecosystem and slot is a `gap` that only a ground reading can close.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 9

- **Statement:** score R per slot; treat the combination question as a union to unbundle; let the arriving observation, not agreement, settle it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **What to check:** is the per-slot versus pooled distinction real for the decision in question, or is an overall skill score sufficient? Does the claim that the question is a union survive a case where one intervention clears all three conditions?
- **Refuter:** a decision that improves under pooling with per-slot data still available; or one lever that restores all three conditions with no categorically different residue.

### C2 - ecology-facing; checkable against forecast practice

- **Statement:** independence, not method count, decides whether two streams corroborate; new observations of the right kind reduce equifinality, while more of the same enlarges the record without growing the reach.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :50, *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 8 :51-52, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 5 :72, *"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*
- **What to check:** in a system you know, do the field and satellite streams share an evidence chain (for example, a product tuned entirely against the same plots)? If they do, is their agreement carrying any information the ground did not already supply? Worden et al. 2025 report the equifinality trade-off; Grimm and Railsback 2012 require multiple patterns across levels.
- **Refuter:** two streams sharing their whole evidence base still carry independent information on held-out slots; or a weighting of fully correlated frames outperforms independent ranging on forecasts scored after issuance.

### C3 - knowledge-systems facing; checkable against governance practice

- **Statement:** Indigenous knowledge is a frame with its own anchor, and importing it needs provenance and a shadow test; it is not a covariate to be validated by the receiving model.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :30, *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."* Fig 10 :59, *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What to check:** Tengö et al. 2014, *"MEB emphasizes that evaluation of knowledge occurs primarily within rather than across knowledge systems."* Robinson et al. 2022 show Indigenous-led governance deciding how evidence is used, with oral and quantitative evidence given equal exposure. Does the proposed combination preserve that governance, or does it silently make the model the external validator?
- **Refuter:** Indigenous validation criteria fully reducible to the receiving model's criteria with no loss and no governance question left over.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a best method, a best weighting, or a named data-assimilation framework.
- Not that Indigenous knowledge must be translated into scientific variables before it counts.
- Not that all five are independent by construction, or that their agreement is corroboration.
- Not that more data resolve equifinality; new observations of the right kind can, more of the same cannot.
- Not that a forecast scored on the data used to tune it is a test.
- Not that any field dataset was analysed here. Nothing is `grounded`.

## 5. What would settle it

The ground, not agreement. Seven refuters were written before the derivation (`01` section 3). The load-bearing ones for a field ecologist:

1. Does one fixed weighted combination beat every separate stream on held-out slots across several ecosystems, with no residue needing a different closure?
2. Does pooling across slots improve the decision the per-slot signal was meant to drive?
3. Do the streams that agree share an evidence pool?
4. Is there an observation that discriminates among the equifinal candidates, or is none available at any resolution?
5. Did the knowledge holders whose protocols are in play decide how their evidence was used?
6. What is the measured round trip gap R_T between the figure set and your field protocol?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 2 :29, :31, :45, five short passages.
2. Check C2 against a forecast system you know: name one pair of streams and say whether their agreement is independent or one pool talking.
3. Check C3 against a monitoring programme you know: who currently holds the authority to decide how Indigenous evidence is used?
4. Name one observation that would discriminate among equifinal candidate models, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
