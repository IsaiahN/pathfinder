# Isomers, order and the round trip: supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified` on the structure, `general` on the bond rules, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

The main audit (F1, F5) says the five ways of knowing form a union, not a single blend, and that the arrangement carries the information. Three questions are owed: (1) are the five **isomers** under Fig 12's definition, or a **functional group**; (2) what is the **order** of the composition, and does the order matter; (3) what does the **round trip** from figures to field protocol lose?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: five evidence lines (D1 field, D2 experiment, D3 remote sensing, D4 Indigenous knowledge, D5 model) plus the compound loop D6. Bonds available: the seven typed bonds and negation. No new atom is introduced. Currencies: measurement, causal discrimination, radiance, protocol-governed indicator, probability. These are not one unit, so a flat `+` over them is a notation error.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are the five isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."**
- Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- As usually written, the five lines share no common atom pool. A plot, a satellite pixel, a treatment, an indicator and a parameter vector have almost nothing in common as objects. By Fig 12's definition they are **not isomers**.
- The stronger form: if all five are written as members of one class, *constraints on a per-slot residual*, differing in which slot they constrain, at which scale, under which validation protocol, then they **are** isomers: same atoms (residual, slot, ground, provenance), different bonds, different substances. That is the checkable structural claim.
- The weaker form is that they share a **functional group**: each is a way of making a residual visible. The group says what kind (a way of seeing R); the qualifier says which member (field, experiment, satellite, knowledge holder, model).
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if one of the five can be written without the shared atom set, for example a model prediction with no residual or no ground, the isomer claim fails and only the functional-group claim stands.

### Q2 - What order is the composition?

- Fig 12 :49: *"The seven bonds, and whether the order of the operands matters."* The Operators table gives → as **sequence** and ⋛ as **comparison**: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*.
- The forecast-analysis cycle is ordered: describe the residual, then range the frames, then import from a differing closure, then update, then carry provenance. Reversing it (import first, describe the residual later) is the inversion Fig 9 :27-28 forbids: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
- Refusal precedes mint (Formula step 2). If "combine the five" is held as a preferred shape rather than a bound term, it is invisible to the mechanism, so it must be written down and allowed to accrue a residual (Fig 5 :65-66).
- The bond between two frames' forecasts is **comparison**, not symmetric conjunction: the question is which one the ground pays, not whether both are present. That is why an unweighted average is a notation error: it uses `+` where ⋛ is required.
- **Falsifier:** if swapping the order of residual description and frame selection makes no difference to the forecast that the ground pays, the ordered-bond reading is strained and the union should be re-censused as symmetric.

### Q3 - Where does recursion enter?

- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**.
- A forecast that has been settled against an arriving observation becomes an atom at the next level: a component of a decision, a management rule, or another model's prior. It does not need a new way of knowing.
- Two forecasts, each settled, can be compared at the next level: `(settled field-constrained forecast) ⋛ (settled knowledge-constrained forecast)`. This is second-order composition, legal because both first-order molecules settled. It is the recursion Fig 12 names, not an import.
- **Type caveat, still open.** The Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. At second order the operands are settled molecules now acting as atoms. Fig 12 :104 permits the demotion to atom, but whether an atom counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument, not an argument. Named, not hidden.

### Q4 - What does the round trip lose? (R_T)

- Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :31 (independence), Fig 6 :23 (contact with a differing closure), Fig 8 :21 (the mute union), Fig 9 :54 (unbundle the union), Fig 11 :43 (enumerate the habitat), Fig 5 :72 (*"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*).
- **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand used in `07` and `08`: split the union, score per slot, name the ground, buy independence, import with provenance, measure the gap, stop honestly.
- **Concretisation (T_E):** a field protocol naming which stream constrains which slot, at which scale, with which uncertainty, and which observation will settle it.
- **R_T is nonzero.** Four constraints do not survive the trip down unless explicitly restored: (i) per-slot indexing collapses into an overall skill score; (ii) evidence-pool independence collapses into a count of methods used; (iii) provenance collapses into a citation list; (iv) the stop at the instrument limit collapses into a recommendation to collect more data. Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a union must be unbundled and each frame ranged against a ground it cannot move. That is the residue worth sending.
- R_T is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints, not measured numerically against a ground reading. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## CONCLUSION

The five ways of knowing are a **functional group** as commonly written and a genuine **isomer claim** when written over the shared atom set (residual, slot, ground, provenance) with different bonds. The composition is **ordered**, because the residual must be described before a frame is chosen and refusal precedes mint, and the bond between settled forecasts is **comparison**, not addition. Recursion enters when a settled forecast becomes an atom at the next level. The round trip from figures to field protocol is lossy in four named places, so R_T must be carried explicitly. This framing rescues what is defensible in "combine the five" without naming a winner or treating agreement as a verdict.
