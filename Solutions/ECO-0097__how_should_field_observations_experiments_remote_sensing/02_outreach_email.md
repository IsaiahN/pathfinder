# Outreach draft - check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 are stated and the shape claim in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 2 is labelled `specified`. The output must survive the check or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about combining ecological evidence, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about ecological prediction. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question of how field observations, experiments, remote sensing, Indigenous knowledge, and models should be combined is not one question with five candidate answers. It is a union of at least three conditions that have to be kept separate: maintaining a channel to a ground that settles a forecast (future observations), identifying mechanism with frames whose evidence does not overlap, and governing whose validation rules count. A single weighted average over all five treats them as commensurable ingredients and deletes exactly the information that makes them useful together. The figure I am checking this against says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* So what I am offering is the shape of a combination, not a recipe and not a named winner.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. One fixed weighted combination that beats every separate evidence stream on held-out slots across several ecosystems, with no residue that needs a frame holding a different closure.
> 2. Averaging across slots, sites and seasons improves the decision that the per-slot signal was supposed to drive.
> 3. Indigenous validation protocols are shown to be a special case of scientific validation with no distinct criteria, so there is no second closure to import from.
> 4. All five streams are shown to draw on one evidence pool, yet agreement among them still tracks the ground.
> 5. Equifinal models are shown to be distinguishable with no new observation, so no source-discriminating instrument is owed.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Dietze et al. 2018, *Iterative near-term ecological forecasting*, PNAS: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5816139
> - Tengö et al. 2014, *Connecting diverse knowledge systems*, Ambio: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4132468
> - Grimm and Railsback 2012, *Pattern-oriented modelling*, Philosophical Transactions B: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3223804
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and its author warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, framework second | Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Formula step "Direction": write down what an answer would look like, bind it, let it accrue a residual. "A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument." |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which. Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate."* |
| Union kept separate, never averaged | Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A recording sent before a residual is described cannot be checked. |
| Ask each recipient to state their own ground and refuter | Fig 5 :65-66, *"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* |
| Indigenous knowledge offered as a frame with its own anchor, not as data to be ingested | Fig 2 :29, the anchor is whatever settles a question and does not change because of what a frame thinks. Tengö et al. 2014: evaluation occurs primarily within, not across, knowledge systems. |
