# The Tether's answer to ECO-0097

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the barrier rules, `gap` on the winner. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Ecological prediction fails per slot: a population, a site, a season, an indicator, a decision. R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is indexed by that slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question of how the five should be combined is a **union of conditions**, not one lever: maintain a channel to a ground that settles a forecast, identify mechanism with frames that do not share an evidence pool, and keep governance of whose validation counts. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split the union before searching.** State updating, mechanism discrimination and validation governance are three conditions with their own loops (Fig 9 :55, :54). A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
2. **Score per slot, not in aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." An overall skill number is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 1 :22; Fig 9 :60). A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. **Name the anchor and refuse to let a frame move it.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." The ground is the observation that arrives after the forecast (Fig 2 :45). A forecast redefined after the observation arrives has become a frame (Fig 2 :33-34). Saturated metrics, pooled readings and dropped transients are loss of contact, not stability (Fig 10 :24, :26).
4. **Buy independence and record provenance.** Two streams are two frames only if they draw on different evidence (Fig 2 :31). Convergence is worth more than adoption, and only the record separates them (Fig 8 :50-52, :51-52). A room of agreeing ecologists is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).
5. **Use experiments where they discriminate, and another instrument where they cannot.** An instrument is sharpened from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32). Where nothing has ever registered at any resolution, the honest report is unreachable at this budget, not a proof of absence (Fig 6 :34-35; Fig 8 :39).
6. **Treat Indigenous knowledge as a frame with its own gate.** Evaluation occurs within a knowledge system first (Tengö et al. 2014); the Kakadu case let Indigenous governance decide how evidence could be used (Robinson et al. 2022). Import with a source gate and a shadow test, never as a covariate stripped of its protocol (Fig 6 :23).
7. **Ship a loop, not a blend.** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Enumerate the habitat to the cascade horizon (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); accept a term only if it beats the MDL bargain (Fig 5 :42); update with stated uncertainty; carry provenance (Fig 10 :62).
8. **Stop where the instruments stop.** Which particular combination wins for this ecosystem and slot is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134). Name the instrument that would close it. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one optimal weighting, one best method, or one data-assimilation framework named as the answer.
- Not that Indigenous knowledge must be translated into scientific variables before it counts. That is unidirectional validation.
- Not that more data resolve equifinality. More of the same enlarges the record; new observations of the right kind can reduce equifinality.
- Not that agreement among the five is corroboration. Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness.
- Not that remote sensing and field observations are automatically two independent frames.
- Not a claim that any field dataset was analysed here. No measurement was taken.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing in this answer is asserted as settled by an arriving observation.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A forecast scored against observations that arrived after it was issued, with uncertainty stated in advance and the residual reported per slot. The concrete questions an instrument can answer:

- **Per-slot test:** does the combination reduce R on the slot that drives the decision, with the per-slot signal still visible? A pooled improvement does not count.
- **Independence test:** do the streams that agree draw on different evidence? If they share a pool, their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
- **Source test:** does a new observation discriminate among equifinal mechanisms, as a spectrometer discriminates among sources that all read green (*The Hard Problem is About Source*)?
- **Governance test:** did the knowledge holders whose protocols are in play decide how their evidence could be used (Fig 10 :59)?
- **Round trip test:** what R_T was lost when the figure set became a field protocol, and was the loss enumerated (Fig 4 :30)?
- Standing refuters from `01` section 3: a blend wins; aggregation helps; no second closure; zero independence; no instrument owed; a governing figure line is found; R_T is zero.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set returns a shape: a union kept separate, per-slot residuals, an immovable ground, bought independence, provenanced imports, an instrument-shaped stop. It cannot name the winner. Another evidence base may already hold the atom that would close the gap, and OUTWARD is the track to look for it; INWARD, a better observation operator, is the opposed track and must never be read against it (Formula step 7). The record of the route is what keeps the stop checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a best method for ecological prediction and does not average the five ways of knowing into one score. It says the question is a union of three conditions, state updating, mechanism discrimination and validation governance, that must be split before search. Forecast error has to be scored per population, site, season and indicator, because a pooled average hides the live slot. The ground is the observation that arrives after the forecast, and no method gets to redraw it. Field plots, satellites, experiments, Indigenous knowledge and models count as separate frames only when their evidence does not overlap, and their agreement is evidence only when the record shows where each contribution came from. Indigenous knowledge keeps its own validation and governance; importing it needs a source gate, not a translation into model variables. What the framework returns is the shape any combination must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap. Which combination actually wins stays open until the ground settles it.
