# Expert verification packet - the Tether on culturally specific categories

**What this is:** a claim in a form a developmental psychologist or categorization researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "how do children learn culturally specific categories" bundles at least four slots (personhood, kinship, ownership, responsibility), each a compound of overlapping conditions, so it is a union of loops and must not be averaged into one mechanism. The figure-derived shape has four parts: two children with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their learnable weighting between inherited prior and private experience diverge; culture hands the child a head start that makes its own search cheaper, not a finished boundary copied verbatim; caregivers bind what would count as an instance so the child can accrue residual against it; and cultural specificity is carried mostly by the arrangement, the bonds between shared atoms, so the same atoms under a different bond give a different substance. The framework does not name which specific bond fills which slot; that awaits a ground reading. The shape comes with refuters written in advance and an honest stop.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus method-facing; checkable by reading Figures 1, 6 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot; the question is a union; transmission is head-start import from a differing closure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Symbols table :23-24 (in `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`) - *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."*
- **Refuter:** matched histories, matched w and matched habitat still yield different boundaries on one slot; or a boundary is transmitted as a verbatim replay that produces the same category in a different habitat with no re-derivation; or a child closes a slot entirely inside its own Γ with no import and the ground pays.

### C2 - psychology-facing; checkable against developmental and cross-cultural evidence

- **Statement:** the local habitat and the child-to-caregiver transmission channel are load-bearing; specificity is at the bond and qualifier level rather than at a different atom set. This is the part an expert can refute with evidence.
- **What to check:** do the split-second norm-enforcement and conformity findings (children enforce conventional norms for ingroup but not outgroup; children go with the majority over their own experience), folk-taxonomy granularity varying with cultural significance, and context/goal sensitivity of category representations, fit a bond-level, group-relative account better than a different-primitives account? Or do they fit equally well under a domain-general learner with no imported structure?
- **Source, verbatim (world, secondary):** Tomasello 2014, *European Journal of Social Psychology* 44(3):187-194: *"children enforced moral norms on both ingroup and outgroup individuals equally, but they enforced conventional norms on ingroup members only"*; *"the human children went with the majority over their own experience"*. Folk taxonomy (Wikipedia): *"All parts of the world have their own systems of naming local plants and animals."*
- **Refuter:** two cultures whose category systems use disjoint atom sets with no shared substrate at any level; or a domain-general model with no imported structure that closes all four slots with equal fit and no residual that persists and fails to trend down.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the four categories are a union, and over shared atoms they stand as isomers or functional-group members. The specific bond assignment is not derived.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :56 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*; Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 12 :124 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*; Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **Refuter:** one mechanism that closes all four slots with no slot-specific term and no compound-specific condition; or a figure re-census admitting a line that names the specific category in a slot.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not one mechanism of category learning. Four slots, four loops.

- Not that the specific bond for a category is known. The draft's atom mappings are retracted in `01` §6.
