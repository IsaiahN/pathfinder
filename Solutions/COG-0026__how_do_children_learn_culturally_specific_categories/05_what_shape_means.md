## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its fillers. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: one loop per slot, a union of compounds, head-start imports from differing closures, a learnable weighting between inherited prior and private history, and bond-level variation over shared atoms. The name of the specific bond that fills a specific slot in a specific culture is not in the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for *"commonly used terms that correlate with the shape of what you have been describing"*. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* (fetched) on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the direct essay warrant for unbundling the four categories (F1).
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* (fetched) on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox: by the time you have the exact search terms, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45-46), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (as recorded in the exemplar) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. That is why mapping a named cultural category onto a named substrate atom without a ground reading is an import with no source gate. It was retracted in `01` §6.

## 4. Why the atom-mapping was the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (as recorded in the exemplar) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, or CMYK all read the same. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The draft read a category's behavioral surface and named its source atom (kinship equals network, ownership equals gradient). That is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any held atom. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Binding onto an atom already held is a provenance act, not the invention of a missing primitive (compare the exemplar's `05` §5b and `06` Q1).

So the correct reading for this question is: naming which bond fills which slot in which culture is **not forbidden in principle**, but it is a binding from the developmental-psychology frame and must carry provenance and a shadow test. I make no such bindings in this solution. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reason the bindings are refused here is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of agreeable developmental psychologists gathered to endorse the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an experiment, a dataset or a cross-cultural replication, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (fetched): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
