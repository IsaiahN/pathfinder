## 0. The residual in effect terms

A child acts: it bets that a boundary falls here (this person is kin, this object is mine, this act makes me responsible). Its library Γ predicts an outcome. The cultural ground returns an observation o'. The residual is R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| per slot s.

- **Arity:** at least four slots in the question as asked (personhood, kinship, ownership, responsibility), and each of those is itself a compound of overlapping conditions, so the true arity is larger.
- **Symmetry:** category boundaries are threshold relations and ordered bonds, not symmetric conjunctions. A boundary is a comparison (⋛) and a cut (−), and obligations are sequences (→). Symmetry is not assumed.
- **Scale:** two. First order is the individual child closing R against the local ground. Second order is the intergenerational membrane, where methods cross up and head starts come down (Fig 4).

**What is being decided.** Option A: the boundaries are innate, culture only supplies labels. Option B: the boundaries are read off the world by a domain-general learner with no inherited structure. Option C: the figures' answer, that the child is a frame composing over a held substrate, the cultural ground supplies imports and binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, and cultural specificity lives in the weighting w and the bonds, not in a different set of primitives.

**What an answer would look like (bound before the run, per the Formula's "Direction"): a mechanism that (i) closes R on each slot, (ii) explains why two children with identical architecture can land on different boundaries, (iii) says which part of a category is shared and which part is local, and (iv) names an instrument that would settle it.**

Kinship is a different compound. Each slot gets its own loop; searching for one mechanism is searching a bundle.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - R is per slot and the question is a union (decisive)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 9 :56: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**

A single theory of "how children learn categories" averages four live signals into one. The figure rule says the signal that says where to look is lost in exactly that average. Personhood, kinship, ownership and responsibility must be uncoupled before a mechanism is sought. Each is a compound with its own ground contact.

- **Mode:** `general` (structural).
- **Verdict:** **triangulated**, Fig 1 (measurement indexing) and Fig 9 (union/partition) with low overlap in what they measure.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single mechanism M such that M closes R on all four slots with no slot-specific term and no compound-specific condition, and show M is not an average over slots.

### F2 - The weighting w is the figure handle on cultural specificity

Symbols table, `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` :22-24: **"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior."** and **"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."** (w is "learnable from the outcomes of past weightings", and w_A + w_B = 1.)

This is the closest thing the figures give to a direct answer. Two children with identical architecture land on different boundaries because their histories and their w diverge. The inherited prior is the cultural side; private history is the child's own contact; w is learnable, so it is not a fixed gene/culture switch. Fig 4 :34-35 triangulates: **"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."** Fig 11 :39 triangulates again: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Verdict:** **triangulated** (Symbols table + Fig 4 + Fig 11, three different measured quantities), but all three are one framework, so this is internal consistency, not independent corroboration.
- **Refuter:** two children with matched histories, matched w and matched habitat still converge on different boundaries for one slot; or w is shown to be unlearnable (not updated by outcomes). Either breaks this claim.

### F3 - Culture sends head starts, not recordings (the "how")

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
Fig 4 :24: **"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."**
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

What passes from caregivers and peers to the child is not a finished boundary copied verbatim. It is a head start: something that makes the child's own search cheaper, and that the child's habitat remakes. The wall moves only on contact with a closure that differs, which is why a child alone cannot generate a cultural category from inside its own Γ. Fig 4 :34-35 (above) supplies the seed/conditions rule.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Verdict:** **triangulated** (Fig 4 membrane + Fig 6 import, low overlap: one governs what crosses a scale, the other what moves a closure).
- **Refuter:** a boundary transmitted as a verbatim replay that produces the same category in a different habitat with no re-derivation and is admitted by the framework; or a child acquiring a cultural boundary with zero contact with a differing closure.

### F4 - The caregiver binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term

Formula, step "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*
Fig 5 :61-65 (amendment): a frame searching for an answer of a given shape holds that shape as a *"PREFERENCE rather than as a term"*, so *"nothing here can see it"*, and the remedy is to *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*.

The loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A child cannot derive, from inside, what would count as being kin or being responsible. Caregiver language, scaffolding and norm talk do exactly what the Formula prescribes: they bind the sought-for shape as a term the child can accrue residual against. World corroboration: Vygotsky's zone of proximal development and scaffolding (Wikipedia, read 2026-11-22), and Tomasello's intention-reading and pattern-finding account of language acquisition (Wikipedia and PNAS profile, read 2026-11-22).

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Verdict:** **single-source** within the figures (Formula Direction and Fig 5 amendment are one figure family), with world corroboration that is secondary.
- **Refuter:** a child closing a cultural category slot with no external binding of what would count as an instance, where the child's own Γ alone generates the objective and the ground pays for it.

### F5 - Cultural specificity lives in the bonds, not in different atoms

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**

Two cultures can hold the same atoms and get different categories because the bond between the atoms differs. That is the isomer reading. Where the atoms differ in kind but the substructure is shared, the categories are members of one functional group with different qualifiers. This is why a universal substrate and culturally specific categories are not contradictory: the substrate is the atom set; the culture supplies much of the arrangement. Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."**

The specific binding of a named category to a named substrate atom (for example "kinship is the network structure") is **refused**. It is an import with no source gate and no shadow test (Fig 6 :14, Fig 8). The framework returns a shape, not the name (F8).

- **Mode:** `specified` for the isomer/functional-group reading; the specific atom bindings are a **gap**.
- **Verdict:** **triangulated** (Fig 12 isomer + functional group + Fig 13 closed-set/open-arrangements, low overlap).
- **Refuter:** two cultures whose category systems use disjoint atom sets with no shared substrate at any level, so neither isomer nor functional-group description applies.

### F6 - Where the chain breaks, and why it is missed

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

The middle link (having terms for what a situation affords, and being able to say what would count as progress) has neither an obvious failure nor an obvious test, so it attracts the least attention. This is exactly where the cultural ground does its work: it supplies the objective ("this is what being a good kin/owner/person looks like"). Effort aimed at perception or at explicit instruction misses the break. World corroboration: category representations vary with the learner's goals and context (Cognitive categorization, Wikipedia, read 2026-11-22).

- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Verdict:** **single-source** (Fig 3), world corroboration secondary.
- **Refuter:** a demonstrated instrument that measures the affordance-to-objective link directly in children across two low-overlap cultures, together with evidence that the link is not a dependency break.

### F7 - The ground, not agreement, settles cross-cultural claims

Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

A cross-cultural finding counts as a witness only when the cultures are low-overlap frames. Two laboratories reading the same review are one pool talking. A classroom intervention that changes how a child talks about categories without changing contact with the ground has not changed the capability. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Verdict:** **triangulated** (Fig 2 + Fig 11).
- **Refuter:** a result settled by unanimous expert agreement across a shared evidence pool with no independent ground contact.

### F8 - The framework returns a shape, not the specific category

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**
Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

So the honest output is: a per-slot mechanism shape, a union of compounds, head-start imports, a learnable w, bond-level variation, and a ground-settled stop. Which specific bond occupies which slot in which culture is not given by the figures. From *The Library of Babel* (Isaiah N, Oct 2 2026, read 2026-11-22): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Verdict:** **triangulated** (Fig 9 + Fig 13).
- **Refuter:** a figure re-census that admits a line naming which specific category occupies which slot without a ground reading.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach. Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **F2/F3 break:** two children with matched histories, matched w and matched habitat still converge on different boundaries on one slot.
2. **F3 break:** a cultural boundary is transmitted as a verbatim replay that yields the same category in a different habitat with no re-derivation, and the framework admits it as a permitted crossing.
3. **F5 break:** two cultures' category systems use disjoint atom sets with no shared substrate at any level.
4. **F3/F6 break:** the child's category objective is shown to be derivable inside the child's own Γ with no import from a differing closure, and the ground pays for it.
5. **F8 break:** a figure re-census admits a line that directly names the specific category occupying a slot.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
