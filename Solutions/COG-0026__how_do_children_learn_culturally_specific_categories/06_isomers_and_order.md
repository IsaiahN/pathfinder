# Are the four categories isomers, and what order are they in?

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The question bundles four terms. Two derivations are owed: (1) is the bundle a partition or a union, and (2) if the four categories are compositions over shared atoms, are they isomers or members of a functional group, and in what dependency order are they learned.

Atoms held: the six substrate terms (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures) plus sensory and social particulars. ## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :56: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**
- Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- Fig 9 :55-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*
- Ownership, for example, binds at least four conditions: a holder (state), a claim (network), a comparison of mine and yours (⋛), and a negation (not-yours). Kinship binds network plus time plus comparison. Personhood binds agency plus time plus recognition. Responsibility binds sequence (→) plus comparison (⋛) plus negation (the act not done).
- ⇒ The four are a **union** of overlapping conditions, not a partition. A single mechanism clears the single-condition regions and leaves the intersections standing. Each slot needs its own loop.
- **Falsifier:** exhibit one mechanism that closes R on all four slots with no slot-specific term and no compound-specific condition, and that is not an average over slots.
- **Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 1 per-slot indexing + Fig 9 union rule), `general`.

### Q2 - Are they isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**
- Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."**
- If two cultures compose the same atoms under different bonds, they produce different category substances that are invisible in an ingredient-only description. That is the isomer reading, and it is exactly where cultural specificity can live without a different substrate.
- **Falsifier:** find two cultures whose category systems use disjoint atom sets with no shared substrate at any level, so neither the isomer nor the functional-group description applies.
- **Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 12 + Fig 13), `specified`, unmeasured.

### Q3 - Or a functional group?

- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Where the atoms differ but a substructure is shared, the four categories are members of one functional group with different qualifiers. The group fixes the kind (a cross-kind composition: state → structure → state); the qualifier fixes which member (which state, which bond).
- Under this reading the shared substructure is the universal part and the qualifiers are the cultural part. That is the structural form of the nativism/empiricism truce the figures permit: not a compromise average, but a separation of levels.
- **Falsifier:** show that no shared substructure exists across the four slots even after re-census, so the group claim fails and only the union claim stands.
- **Verdict:** single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), `specified`.

### Q4 - What order are they in?

- Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
- Fig 3 :22-23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."*
- Dependency order: perception (tell persons from things, and now from before) → vocabulary (terms for what things afford: movable, claimable, owe-able) → objective (what counts as progress toward being kin, owner, person, responsible) → planning → learn and carry.
- Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** The middle link (affordance to objective) is where the chain usually breaks, because that link has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests. Cultural scaffolding acts at exactly this link (F4 of `01`).
- **Falsifier:** demonstrate an instrument that measures the affordance-to-objective link directly in two low-overlap cultures, and show the link is not the dependency break.
- **Verdict:** single-source (Fig 3), `general`.

### Q5 - What crosses between individual and culture?

- Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
- Fig 4 :24: **"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."**
- Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
- A method that a child settles can cross upward to the shared library. What comes down is a head start, not a recording. The order is: settle at the individual scale, then cross up; receive a head start, then let the local conditions remake it (Fig 4 :34-35).
- **Falsifier:** a verbatim replay that produces the same boundary in a different habitat with no re-derivation and is admitted by the framework.
- **Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 4 + Fig 6), `specified`.

## CONCLUSION

The four categories are a **union** of overlapping conditions, so four loops, not one. Over shared atoms they are either **isomers** (same atoms, different bond, different substance) or members of a **functional group** (shared substructure, different qualifier), depending on whether the atoms or the arrangement is held constant. Cultural specificity is carried at the bond and qualifier level; the specific assignment of bond to slot is a provenanced binding, not a figure derivation. Dependency order is perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry, and the break-prone link is affordance to objective, which is where cultural scaffolding acts. This framing rescues what is defensible (shared substrate, local arrangement, head-start transmission) without the strained atom mappings retracted in `01` §6.
