# The Tether's answer: which settings lasted long enough?

**Mode: `specified` on the four per-domain conditions, `general` on the union shape, `gap` on the literal list of named settings, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a list of early-Earth settings that persisted long enough for complicated chemistry to accumulate. Under Fig 9 that question is a **union compound**, not one question with one answer:

- a setting can supply contact and persistence and still dilute its building blocks (the vent case in the world sources);
- a setting can concentrate and cycle and still have uncertain persistence (the pond and hot-spring cases);
- a setting can concentrate and persist and still lack a spendable gradient;
- a setting can have a gradient and concentrate and still fail because its cycle does not re-enter.

Fig 9 :4-5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The answer is therefore four verdicts, kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape (four verdicts)

1. **Contact and persistence are necessary, not sufficient.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 13 :12 names time as *"the ordered succession of differences."* A setting whose contact is severed before products compound fails, no matter how favorable its chemistry. Mode `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 13 + geological ground).

2. **Concentration must beat dilution.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Composition inside a dilute closure cannot concentrate it. The world sources are direct: the Abiogenesis page says vents *"do not concentrate prebiotic materials, due to strong dilution by seawater"* and leave *"little residence time to accumulate"*; Campbell et al. 2019 call overdilution *"a well-known problem in the origin of life."* Mode `specified`, triangulated.

3. **A spendable gradient must be present.** Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent."* A uniform warm environment has a distribution, not directedness. The Hydrothermal vent page: *"Hydrothermal plumes embody physical and chemical gradients that promote several types of chemical reactions."* Deamer & Weber 2010 tie energy flow to increasing complexity. Mode `specified`, triangulated.

4. **Cycling must re-enter, with the right sign.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Wet/dry, freeze/thaw, and diurnal loops can close; one-pass flows cannot. The lab contrast in Campbell et al. (regulated rehydration 16.0 percent versus heavy rain 1.9 percent) shows the sign deciding the outcome. Mode `specified`, triangulated.

Over the fixed condition set the candidate settings are Fig 12 :117 isomers: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* At minimum they share the four-condition functional group (Fig 12 :123). See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. The gap: the list itself

No figure line names a vent, a pond, ice, a hot spring, or the crust. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

So the literal answer to "which settings" is a declared `gap` at the figure layer, with a discriminator owed to the ground:

- rock and dating establish which settings persisted (Fig 2 :29);
- concentration assays and lab reconstruction establish which settings concentrated and cycled with the right sign;
- a source-discriminating instrument, not an argument, decides which setting actually hosted accumulation (the metamerism point in `05` §4).

This is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`: the four conditions are measurable and the geology frame already holds candidate settings. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranked winner. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A ranking manufactured from the four conditions is a frame-internal product.
- Not that all four conditions are independently sufficient. They are a union; each is necessary and none alone selects a setting.
- Not that vents fail or ponds win. The world sources give vent dilution and pond concentration as two readings of the same four-condition shape, not as a verdict.
- Not that the six-term set names nature's own primitives. *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*
- Not `grounded`. No geological measurement, date, or experiment was taken by this agent.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

The anchor is rock and experiment (Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*). Specifically:

1. **Duration discriminator:** dating that shows whether a candidate setting maintained contact across the required number of cycles. The Hydrothermal vent page's persistence claim and the Abiogenesis page's *"little residence time"* claim are competing readings to be settled, not reconciled by agreement.
2. **Concentration discriminator:** measured concentration factors in a setting, including whether closed-basin retention (Last Chance Lake) or deliquescent self-regulation (Campbell et al.) beats open dilution.
3. **Gradient discriminator:** an instrument that distinguishes a spendable gradient from a mere distribution at the setting's scale.
4. **Cycle-sign discriminator:** a measurement of whether a setting's cycling spends or hydrolyzes its products across multiple re-entries.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §5:** no-gradient accumulation, no-cycle accumulation, dilution-counterexample, persistence-counterexample, union collapse. Any one fires against the shape.

## 6. The honest stop

The answer stops where the instruments stop resolving. The lab-to-geology transform is lossy (Fig 4 :30: *"take the round trip and measure the gap"*), so a humidity-chamber result is a method carried up, not a replay of a Hadean setting. The round trip gap `R_T` is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`. Nothing here maintains the ground: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). Prestige or agreement is not a discriminator.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning setting for the origin of life. It says the question is a union of four conditions, not a contest: a setting must keep contact with its chemistry long enough, concentrate building blocks faster than dilution removes them, supply a gradient that can actually be spent, and run a cycle whose output re-enters as input with the right sign. Vents, ponds, ice, hot springs, and crustal pores are different arrangements of those same four conditions, so they stand to each other as isomers, not as right and wrong answers. The framework returns that shape and a set of refuters written in advance; it does not return the list, because which setting actually lasted long enough is a question for rock, dating, concentration assays, and experiment. Until such a discriminator fires, the honest verdict is a declared gap, not a silence and not a guess.

## 8. Verdict fields (for the WATSON packet)

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is mode-labeled, lists its refuters, and records its lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named winner instead of a shape and refuter (Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable.
- **undoable:** yes. The binding of the four conditions to any particular setting is removable without touching the shape. A ground result can supersede the gap; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A duration or concentration discriminator that selects a setting, a direct measurement of accumulation after contact is severed, a demonstration of accumulation with no spendable gradient or no re-entrant cycle, or a re-census that dissolves the union into one lever. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
