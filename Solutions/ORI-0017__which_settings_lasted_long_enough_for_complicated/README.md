## Question restated as an effect

Something is being decided: which early-Earth environments had enough temporal persistence and enough accumulation capacity for multi-step prebiotic chemistry to compound rather than be diluted, destroyed, or reset. Option A is a standing list of named settings (vents, ponds, ice, hot springs, crust). Option B is the shape any such setting must have, with the naming left to geological and experimental ground and a refuter written in advance. A leads to a `general`-mode list that the figures cannot license and that spends the independence of the people checking it. B leads to a checkable separator, per-domain verdicts, and an open slot until the ground settles which actual settings qualify.

What an answer would look like is bound as an ordinary term before the run: a setting qualifies when its conditions stay in contact with the chemistry long enough for a spendable gradient and re-entrant cycling to build products faster than degradation removes them, with concentration exceeding dilution, and with that whole arrangement settled by rock and experiment rather than by agreement. This is the "Direction" discipline from `THE_FORMULA.md`: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29, :35).
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. **Sending the source before the residual spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

## Honest standing

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figure text. No geological measurement, laboratory run, or field observation was taken by this agent. It cannot settle which settings actually lasted long enough. It can only say what a setting would have to be like, and what would refute the claim. The world sources were opened and quoted, but they are secondary corroboration and share evidence pools; they are not a ground reading performed here.
