**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the gradient** (thermal, pH/redox, photo, or electrochemical) is not specified by "a difference that can be spent"; the world sources disagree about which setting supplies it;

- the **cycle sign** is not fixed by "re-entrant"; the lab data show the same cycle can hydrolyze rather than condense when water is added;
- the **lab-to-geology scale transform** is lossy: 24-hour humidity cycles are not a Hadean season, and the Hadean exposure of continental crust is unknown.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a setting must maintain contact and concentrate while a spendable gradient and re-entrant cycling operate. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
