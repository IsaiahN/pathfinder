## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0017, the shape is:

```
a setting whose conditions stay in contact long enough,
with a spendable gradient and re-entrant cycles,
that concentrates building blocks faster than degradation removes them,
settled by rock and experiment
```

That is a shape. "Deep-sea alkaline hydrothermal vents at 40 to 90 degrees C" is a name. The figures return the first, not the second.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the near vocabulary is residence time, concentration, gradient, and cycling, all of which the world sources supply.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Cast the four conditions in effect terms before importing a setting from geology.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Say "stay in contact long enough," not "Hadean pond," until the ground pays.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the outreach posture in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): the finish line is where *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is reached when the four conditions are stated and the gap on the list is declared.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the ground is a rock, a date, a concentration assay, or a lab reconstruction.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a setting is an import from the geology frame. It adds no atom to the six-term set; it binds a held atom (time, gradient, network) to a geological object.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list; binding a setting to them is provenance, not discovery.

## 4. Why the naive "which setting" reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several different sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing that a vent has a gradient and persists does not tell you that a vent is where accumulation happened. Several settings can supply the same four-condition reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (a geological or experimental discriminator), not a name chosen from a reading. This is the mechanism behind F7 and the declared gap in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular setting in the shape?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular setting is licensed only by ground settlement of a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question's own section commit the error?** The section "Early Earth: where and when?" asks an enumeration question, which Fig 11 :43-44 licenses (*"The habitat is enumerated, never composed"*). The error would be to convert the enumeration into a ranked winner without a ground discriminator. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

## 5b. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule, as the exemplar's author did before me. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Time, gradient, network, and spectrum are all already among the six (Fig 13). Naming a geological setting that binds those atoms is a **binding from the world frame**, not the invention of a missing primitive.

So the honest statement is: the figures cannot synthesize the name of a setting because that would require a new primitive or a ground reading; but a provenanced binding of the four conditions to a named setting is permitted and is exactly what the geology frame supplies. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework derived the setting, or that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not retracted**. The reason the list stays open is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous advocates for vents or for ponds is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried against rock and experiment.

However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
