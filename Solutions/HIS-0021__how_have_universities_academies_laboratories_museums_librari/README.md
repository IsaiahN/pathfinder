## The question restated as an effect (formula step "Direction")

The ordinary term "shaped what could be known" is bound as an ordinary term before the run. In effect terms it means: for a researcher or community holding a library Γ and a closure(Γ), an institution changes at least one of (a) what instruments can represent at all, (b) which atoms and bonds are in Γ, (c) whether the wall of the closure is moved by contact with a differing closure, (d) what crosses the scale membrane between one generation and the next, (e) whether the anchor that settles claims stays reachable and non-updating, (f) whether a seat able to refuse a verdict exists, (g) which actors are in contact with the residual, and (h) whether the next room in a chain differs from the last and matches the residual it surfaced. An answer would look like a per-institution, per-domain statement of which of these channels changed, with a refuter. That is the shape bound here; which named institution produced which named historical result is not returned (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
