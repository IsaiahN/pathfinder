Option A: treat the six names as a ranking of causes, so the answer is a list of the most influential institutions and the periods in which they determined belief. What each leads to: A turns agreement into a verdict and an updating reference into an anchor, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns checkable mechanisms, a pre-registered refuter, and an open historical attribution until a dated discriminator is supplied.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the ranking does not. The question is a union of eight channels with different anchors. Institutions changed what instruments could register, what libraries could compose, whether differing closures could be contacted, what methods crossed to the next generation, whether a non-updating reference stayed reachable, whether a refusal seat survived, which actors were in contact with the residual, and whether the next room differed from the last. They changed reach and contact, not the ground. A growing library is not a growing reach; a convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention; a chain of mirrors is motion without progress; and institutional valence flips with the residual. Which named institution produced which dated result is a `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate; it is not a historical finding.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separates per-domain verdicts and prints refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking or a recording instead of a shape and a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A channel claim can be withdrawn without touching the others, because verdicts are per domain and never averaged. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` remains open rather than being filled by a guess.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A dated archive source showing a change in contact rather than belief for a named institution would settle a channel; a demonstration that an institution's decree moved the anchor itself would break Fig 2; a case of composition adding a new atom with no differing closure would break Fig 6; a replay producing a new result with the method discarded would break Fig 4; a figure re-census locating a line that names a historical cause would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
