# The Tether's answer: how institutions shaped what could be known

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain mechanism claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on historical attribution. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how six named institution types "shaped what could be known." In effect terms the residual is the change in a knowing frame's reach and contact, indexed per slot, not a global "state of knowledge" (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It is a **union** of eight channels with different anchors (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."), so verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, with per-domain verdicts

1. **D1 Representation (`specified`, triangulated).** Instruments and instrument habitats extended what could be represented at all, because an instrument is built from a worse instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."). Laboratories, observatories and collections are the sites.
2. **D2 Library (`specified`, triangulated).** Libraries, archives, catalogues and collections enlarged Γ, the set of composable terms. This changes the reach only when a settled term becomes an atom for the next composition (Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."). A library that grows is not a reach that grows (Fig 5 :72 "a library that grows is not a reach that grows.").
3. **D3 Import (`specified`, triangulated).** The wall of a closure moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). Academies, universities, correspondence networks and museums were contact zones. The import needs provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12, :13) and is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24).
4. **D4 Membrane (`specified`, triangulated).** Across generations and sites, only methods cross up and only head starts come down (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."). A recording of one occasion looks like knowledge and is not. The lossy transform carries a round-trip gap that must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30).
5. **D5 Ground (`specified`, triangulated).** Laboratories and experimental societies could keep an anchor reachable, where "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29-30). When an institution lets its reference update on its own reports, everything drifts together coherently (Fig 2 :34). A convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one").
6. **D6 Seat (`single-source` on the operator form, triangulated on the seat form).** A seat able to refuse a claim was a precondition for correction. "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The operators table names the missing unary operator: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate" (Operators table :58). Whether a real review body implements this as an explicit negation is a live type question, not a figure conflict.
7. **D7 Habitat (`specified`, triangulated).** Institutional effects are properties of the researcher-and-habitat pair. "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." (Fig 11 :40). Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."), so no standing list of good or bad institutions survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51). Contact cascades beyond direct contact.
8. **D8 Room chain (`specified`, triangulated).** A succession of like-minded institutions is one long room. "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35). The next room must differ from the last and match the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36).

## 3. The single most important correction

Institutions shaped **reach and contact, not truth**. The tempting answer, that institutions decided what was true, promotes a frame to the anchor, which is the collapse Fig 2 names. The figure-governed answer is narrower and checkable: institutions changed what could be registered, composed, imported, carried, settled, refused and contacted; the ground still decided which claims paid (Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). Shared standards and conventions are good where they coordinate and bad where they decide outcomes; those are two different verdicts, not one dial.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- No ranking of the six institution types, no influence percentage, no "most important institution." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- No claim that a decree changed what the ground returned. A decree changes publication or funding, which are D2 and D5.
- No claim that agreement among historians corroborates the shape. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- No theory of everything and no last room. Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room".
- No derivation of one institution from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No archive measurement, instrument or dated provenance chain was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried via the exemplar): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Per-slot discriminator:** for a named institution and a named period, does a dated source show a change in contact (D7) rather than a change in belief only? An archive citation is the instrument.
- **Anchor test:** was the reference non-updating for the claim in question (Fig 2 :29-30, :33)? Pushing the institution's rating must not move the ground.
- **Method-versus-recording test:** what crossed the membrane, a re-applicable derivation or a replay (Fig 4 :23-25)? A student trained in the method is the discriminator.
- **Import gate:** for each claimed cross-institutional effect, is there provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12-13), or is the surplus an undifferentiated union (Fig 8 :19-21)?
- **Standing falsifiers:** the eight conditions in `01` §5 and `08` §2. If one fires, the corresponding channel claim is withdrawn.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not disproved.** The channels are a visible guess at the compression axes of this question, not nature's own list. The framework can narrow the shape and state refuters, and it can report which attribution is out of reach with the library as it stands (Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"). Another archive or another sensorium's minimum set need not yield these eight channels. The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). A search that finds no governing line is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank universities, academies, laboratories, museums, libraries and government agencies, and it does not say institutions decided what was true. It says the question is a union of channels with different anchors, not one lever. Institutions changed what instruments could register, what a library could compose, whether a differing closure could be contacted, what method crossed to the next generation, whether a non-updating reference stayed reachable, whether a seat able to refuse survived, which actors were in contact with the residual, and whether the next room differed from the last. A growing library is not a growing reach; a chain of like-minded rooms is motion, not progress; and the same institution changes valence when the question changes. Institutions changed reach and contact. The ground still settled which claims paid. Which named institution produced which historical result stays open until a dated source and a discriminator are supplied. That is a verdict, not silence.
