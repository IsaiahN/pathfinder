## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135 "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before a historian can name which institution caused which result. "Eight channels with different anchors, union not partition, reach and contact changed but the ground not changed" is a shape. "The Royal Society caused the scientific revolution by 15 percent" is a name with a fake number. The first constrains; the second fills.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

The order in this solution follows that: the residual was described in effect terms (what changes in reach and contact) before the history-of-science frame was opened. Had the frame been opened first, "universities, academies, laboratories..." would have seemed to fit any theory (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"). This ordering is recorded, not assumed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." (`THE_FORMULA.md`, local file.) The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming a specific institution's effect is a **provenanced binding** from the historical record, not a derivation from the figures. It is allowed only with a dated source and a described residual. It is not a `gap` being filled by assertion.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The eight mechanism channels in `01` are therefore a visible guess at the compression axes of this question, not nature's own list. Turning one into *the* cause of a historical change would hide that choice.

## 4. Why a ranking of institutions is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An instrument that cannot distinguish these cases "demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A historian who observes that a period had more recorded discoveries and more academies has a reading, not a source attribution. Several institutional paths can produce the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator (an archive, an instrument, a dated provenance chain), not a ranking. Fig 6 :27 "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially wrote that the rule forbids naming any institution. That was an overstatement. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The institutions named in this question are not missing primitives; they are habitat actors supplied by the historical frame. Naming one as a binding is provenance, not an invented primitive.

What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* which institution mattered, or that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."). My §1 to §4 metamerism point is not retracted: the reason a ranking fails is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held actors. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous historians selected to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried, by asking what would refute it.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-paragraph version of the shape

> The figures do not rank universities, academies, laboratories, museums, libraries and government agencies. They return the shape any account of institutional effect must have: the question is a union of channels with different anchors, not a single lever; institutions changed what instruments could register, what a library could compose, whether a differing closure could be contacted, what method crossed to the next generation, whether a non-updating reference stayed reachable, and whether a seat able to refuse survived; the same institution changes valence when the residual changes; and a chain of like-minded rooms is motion, not progress. Institutions changed reach and contact. The ground still settled which claims paid. Which named institution produced which historical result stays open until a dated source and a discriminator are supplied. That stop is a verdict, not silence.
