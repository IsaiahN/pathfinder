## What the answer is, in brief

The figures do not name a worst-represented process. They return the shape of the answer: rank process slots by a residual that persists and does not trend down, against an instrument that does not move when modellers push on it; keep absent, mis-attached, unsettled and hidden-by-compensation conditions separate; treat the cross-process comparison as second-order; and leave the top slot open until the ground settles it. Cloud microphysics and aerosol-cloud interaction, marine ice-sheet dynamics, and permafrost carbon feedback are recorded as `specified` witnesses with no ground order among them. The first-pass answer, "clouds, because an assessment calls cloud feedback the largest uncertainty", is retracted visibly in `01` section 6 and `05` section 5.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that any single Earth-system process is the settled worst.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. **A frame cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :27, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A multimodel spread is a filter for where to aim an instrument, not a witness.
3. **Do not show the interior before the verdict.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Stage 1 carries the residual and its refuter; the framework is offered only after that verdict is recorded.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Pre-registered refuter

Written before the candidate ranking was fixed, in `01` section 3. The shape claim is withdrawn if a per-process instrument comparison puts the worst slot above the broken link, if a consensus ranking changes what an instrument returns, if one candidate residual trends down while unlisted slots persist, if one protocol collapses all four conditions, or if a figure re-census ranks two processes by name.
