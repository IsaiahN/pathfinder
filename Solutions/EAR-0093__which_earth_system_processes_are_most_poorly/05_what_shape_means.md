## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the Library of Babel essay: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the form of the answer you can state before you can rank the candidates. It constrains; it does not name. For this question:

- "rank by per-slot residual that persists and does not trend down" is a shape;
- "cloud microphysics is the worst represented process" is a name;
- "a process at an unmeasured middle link, whose term is absent or mis-attached or unsettled" is a shape;
- "add a cloud microphysics scheme" is a prescription, not a shape.

The distinction matters because a name smuggles in a frame choice. The Society of Agents essay puts the ground rule: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* Picking clouds first and then finding agreement makes the agreement worthless before it is counted.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- **Library of Babel:** use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: the term already held is "parameterization", and the recurring neighbours are "structural uncertainty", "process rate", "compensating error", "equifinality". The vocabulary narrows toward a *kind of slot* before it narrows toward a process.
- **Fig 6 :30:** *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- **Decoding Tacit Knowledge:** *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet describes what a refuter would look like rather than asserting a process name.
- **Library of Babel on bundling:** *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* That is the four-condition split in `01` F4, arrived at from the essay side.
- **Library of Babel on stopping:** you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the loop stops when no opened source supplies a per-slot instrument comparison, not when a name feels obvious.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And on the status of declared primitives: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, a process name is licensed only by an instrument that returns its process-level residual and does not move when modellers push on it. Adding a process because a paper describes it is an import without a source gate.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that most matters here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This maps directly onto the present question. Wang et al. (W2) show six models getting cloud optical depth approximately right through compensating errors. The optical depth is the green reading. Its source, which process is actually misrepresented, is not determined by that reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, such as the cloud-susceptibility constraint from a volcanic eruption, not another argument about which scheme looks more physical.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the same point in its provenance cases: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. And nothing in the image records which."* And: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A model that reproduces the global mean is a frame, not a ground verdict.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A ranking produced by the same models it ranks is not a validator.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape must an answer to EAR-0093 have?**

> A per-slot ranking of Earth-system processes by a residual that persists and does not trend down, measured against an anchor that does not move when models push on it, with the four conditions (absent, mis-attached, unsettled, hidden by compensation) kept separate, and the top slot left open until a process-level instrument settles it.

That is the whole of what the figures and essays license. No process is named by them.

**Does the draft audit commit the forbidden error?** It flirted with it. The first pass drafted "the answer is clouds, because the assessment says clouds are the largest uncertainty in climate sensitivity." That is a name from a projection, and it is recorded and withdrawn in `01` section 6. The corrected form is the three-candidate table in `01` F5, each row marked `specified`, with no ground order. See `11_audit.md` for the independent check.

### Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the shape-not-a-name rule in the first pass. The README phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, that is a primitive not held in `Γ`. Cloud microphysics, ice-sheet dynamics and permafrost carbon are **not** missing primitives of the Earth-system modelling frame. They are processes held in the literature and, in varying degrees, in models. Naming one as a candidate is a **binding from the world frame**, not an invention of a new primitive and not a claim that the framework names the slot.

What remains wrong is treating the candidate ranking as settled. The reason is the missing source-discriminating instrument, not a ban on naming processes. Both readings are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction the exemplar made for Gradient and Spectrum in its own `05` section 5b, reached here independently.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous climatologists who agree that clouds matter is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets a process-level instrument settle it.

**Separate frames, one ground.** From the Society of Agents essay: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* Six models tuned to the same satellite products are six copies for the purposes of this question. The audit's F6 follows from this directly.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; the outputs are *"simulacra."* The architecture post adds: *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency. It is **not** a test of the ranking and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
