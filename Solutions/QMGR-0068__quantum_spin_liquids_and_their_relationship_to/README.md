## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room does not add an anchor. "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. A checker must not hold the interiors. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
