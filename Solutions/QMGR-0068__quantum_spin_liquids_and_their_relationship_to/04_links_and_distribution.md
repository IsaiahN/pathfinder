## 3. What the wider literature does to the derivation

1. **It confirms the derivation-versus-import split in effect terms.** Kitaev, Tarabunga, Cheng and Wiese all describe a gauge structure obtained *from* a spin or blockade model. None of them claims a new fundamental field. This is Fig 6 :21 ("MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom") read in the world, at low overlap with the framework's author pool.
2. **It supplies two genuine ground readings, both synthetic.** Semeghini (string order in a 219-atom simulator) and Karch (pinch points and interferometric coherence over about 100 sites) settle gauge wiring in their own habitats. Fig 11 :70 confines them there: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
3. **It names the remaining ground.** Yang, Shannon and Lee discuss Higgs transitions "from deconfined to confined phases of a lattice gauge theory" and daughter phases of a U(1) QSL; Linsel et al. supply percolation-inspired order parameters "suitable as a probe for quantum spin liquids." These are candidate instruments, not settlements for a named material. They keep F11's `gap` open and give it a route.
4. **Wen 2001 is the low-overlap corroborator for the isomer reading.** "we construct hundreds of symmetric spin liquids, which have SU(2), U(1) or Z2 gauge structures at low energies" is a world statement that many members of one functional group exist, which is what Fig 12 :123 predicts at the figure level. The agreement is one pool talking if both are read through the framework author; Wen is independent of that pool, so this one carries some weight.

No world source conflicts with a figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Where a source might seem to conflict (Wiese's "underpin our understanding" of both particle physics and materials), the re-census in `01` section 6 resolves it: same atoms, two closures, no new atom in the world.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before a reader has described their residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :40 - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the four links, with the note that the verbatim figures, not the summary, are the authority.
