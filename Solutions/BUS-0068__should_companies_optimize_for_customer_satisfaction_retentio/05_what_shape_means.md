## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill in the company-specific numbers. It constrains; it does not fill. The four-condition separator in `01` section 0 is a shape. "Optimize 60% satisfaction, 30% profit, 10% trust" is a name with no derivation behind it.

The same essay names the failure mode this question is prone to: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is Fig 9 :54-57 in narrative form, and it is the reason the question is treated as a union rather than a dial.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The essay calls the steering device the question itself: "This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."
- Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The question's four candidates are all borrowed words from the business frame, so the census describes their effects (what each reading does when optimized) before naming any figure line.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach draft asks what a failure would look like before offering any theory.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

## 3. What licenses a name (and what this question may name)

Only two things license a name beyond a shape: the ground settling a term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52).

For this question, three names are already available as bindings from the business frame and are not forbidden inventions:
- **profit per customer**, a money reading with a market anchor (Fig 2 :29);
- **retention**, a repeat-purchase recording with no nameable anchor of its own;
- **trust**, re-grounded as a channel-to-ground plus a refusal seat (Fig 10 :24, :42; Fig 11 :39-40).

What is *not* available is a universal weight, horizon, or discount rate. No figure line returns one, so those remain a gap (Fig 13 :134-135). The distinction matters because the canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, not the selection of terms already held by the census. My earlier broader reading of the rule is corrected in section 5.

## 4. The source-discrimination requirement

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) supplies the instrument rule that governs the retention and trust findings:

> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"
>
> the instrument "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"
>
> "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
>
> "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

A repeat purchase is a reading of behavior. It cannot distinguish delight, habit, inertia and switching barriers. Keiningham et al. (2008) name those sources and find the repeat count predicts different futures in different subgroups. A trust survey is a reading of a judgment. It cannot distinguish a kept promise from a well-managed report. Ranaweera and Prabhu (2003) supply the discriminating case: "Even a 'satisfying' service recovery process might be inadequate to prevent loss of trust." What is owed in both cases is a discriminator, not a better-argued name.

## 5. Correction to my own §3: "shape, not a name" was overstated

I first wrote that the framework forbids naming profit per customer, retention or trust. That was wrong. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. Profit per customer and the channel-to-ground condition are bindings from the business frame onto terms the census already holds. Naming a binding is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive.

Two errors remain and are *not* retracted:
1. Claiming a universal weight or horizon would be a name where no primitive is missing and no ground has settled it. That is a gap, not a binding.
2. Calling the repeat count a source label is the metamerism error. The count is a projection with lossy information; the source is still unread.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened):

> "Prestige is not equal to actions."
>
> "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."
>
> "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of well-known customer-metrics authorities selected for likely agreement is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a firm's own per-customer data, settle it. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement)." And: "However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also says "Metatheories cannot validate themselves."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, and every other analytic file in this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the question. They cannot settle it. What could settle it is a longitudinal, per-customer dataset with margin, channel state and defection after a defection opportunity, read by independent frames that do not share the firm's report as an evidence pool.

## 8. What shape means for this specific answer

Putting the sections together, "the shape of the answer" for BUS-0068 is:

> a per-slot comparison against a paying market that does not update on the firm's own report, with instruments whose blind spots are declared, a channel that is kept reachable and can be refused, and a refusal of any universal weight until a source-discriminating instrument is supplied.

That is a kind of thing, not a number and not a named winner among the four candidates. The honest stop is at the limit of the instruments: satisfaction can be read, retention can be read, profit can be read, but trust has no discriminating instrument in this folder, and no universal allocation has been imported with a source gate. Indiscernibility is not identity; the record of the route is what keeps the result checkable.
