## What is being decided, in effect terms

Four candidate objectives are offered as if they were four values of one dial. They are not. Each is a different quantity, read by a different instrument, against a different anchor, on a different timescale, and at a different slot of the customer population. The residual is the unchosen objective: what does the firm actually settle against when it says it is optimizing one of these, and which of the four stays in contact with a market that does not move when the firm's own report moves? An answer would be a separator, stated in effect terms and written as an ordinary term, that tells a firm which quantity it may safely promote into an objective and which quantities must remain instruments. That sought-for shape is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 and allowed to accrue a residual like anything else, per the formula's Direction rule.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal percentage, weighting formula, or "right" objective. Fig 9 :32 gives "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself", and Fig 13 :134 gives "something has to be imported before it is asked again". A number would be an import with no source gate and no shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).

Three figure-grounded reasons for the omissions:

1. **Agreement is not the ground.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness is a mirror.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting checkers for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
3. **Send a generator, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :42).
