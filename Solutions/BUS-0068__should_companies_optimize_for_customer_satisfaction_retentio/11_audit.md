## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29/:36-38 (anchor and instrument), Fig 4 :25 (recording), Fig 10 :24/:42 (channel and seat), Fig 9 :55-56 (union), Fig 13 :134 (gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: keep profit as a per-slot comparison over a horizon; satisfaction is an instrument; retention is a recording; trust is a channel; no universal weight.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a firm's actual dashboard, a customer-health KPI with four sub-indices and a single target.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** is lost when the four readings are averaged into one KPI, so the live account disappears;
- the **source discrimination** on retention is lost when the repeat count is treated as a source label, so barrier-retained and trust-retained customers are scored identically;
- the **refusal seat** on trust is lost when a trust survey becomes an outcome-fixing convention, so the channel can be severed while the score rises.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the paying market as a non-updating anchor and the per-customer margin comparison over a horizon. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no firm reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap.
