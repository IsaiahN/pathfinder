# WATSON answer, COG-0093

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal ratio, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

How should researchers combine laboratory experiments, ethnography, historical evidence, and population-level data?

Restated as an effect: for one case at a time, each method's prediction Γ(b,a) misses part of what the phenomenon returns o′, giving R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| indexed per slot and ordered in time. The question is what makes a second, third, or fourth frame add reach rather than add agreement. Each method is a bounded frame with different actors, currency and anchor (Fig 4 :20). The sought-for shape, bound before the run, is a separator with checkable conditions plus a declared limit, not a mixing recipe.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: at least three operands, frame prediction, observation, slot, ordered in time. Symmetry: not symmetric; the ground does not update on the frame (Fig 2 :25), and "better" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87). Scale: the slot is a case, while population-level data lives at a different stratum, so R crosses a lossy membrane (Fig 4).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Laboratory experiment | experimenter, participant, controlled task | predicted versus observed response under manipulation | produced outcome under control | admitted |
| D2 Ethnography | participant-observer, community members | thick description, emic categories, observed practice | what people do and say in context | admitted |
| D3 Historical evidence | archives, artifacts, dated records, historian | documented sequence and provenance | the dated record | admitted |
| D4 Population-level data | populations, survey and administrative records, analyst | frequencies, distributions, covariation | the measured count or distribution | admitted |
| X1 Consensus among methods | the four frames | agreement | none independent, each updates on the others | not a domain yet, mirror collapse |
| X2 Culture as a free cause | a reified entity | influence | none separate from the four readings | absorbed into the compound |

Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-57). The four analytic interests overlap at the contact; a case in two conditions is categorically different from a case in either. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-16). D1 through D4 are not a partition of one method; they are a compound with its own loop.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain)

**D1 Laboratory experiment, status `triangulated` (Fig 1 per-slot residual and Fig 2 anchor rule, distinct roles).**

> *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19)
> *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22)
> *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29)

Reading: a lab result is a per-case reading against a produced outcome, not a universal law, and its anchor must survive a push. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a pooled lab composite that predicts a non-updating ground better than per-case readings.

**D2 Ethnography, status `triangulated` (Fig 4 cast rule and Fig 6 provenance).**

> *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* (Fig 4 :20)
> *"source gate: provenance"* (Fig 6 :12) *"shadow test: explains a gap"* (Fig 6 :13)
> *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (Fig 6 :14)

Reading: ethnography differs from the lab by cast, not by being less rigorous. An emic category moved into another method is an import and needs a source gate and a shadow test. Mode `specified`. Refuter: an ungated category that earns verdict weight, or a reading whose anchor moves with the ethnographer's interpretation.

**D3 Historical evidence, status `triangulated` (Fig 4 method versus recording and Fig 8 provenance).**

> *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22)
> *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* (Fig 4 :25)
> *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* (Fig 8 :50-52)

Reading: the dated record supplies provenance, which is the one thing the other frames cannot manufacture from their outputs. What transfers is the dating procedure and its lineage, not the finished category. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a replayed historical category that adds the same reach as a transferable method with provenance held constant.

**D4 Population-level data, status `triangulated` (Fig 10 channel and Fig 11 valence).**

> *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24)
> *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26)
> *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* (Fig 11 :49)
> *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* (Fig 11 :51)

Reading: population readings can pool away the live slot, so they are a channel that decays, not the ground, and their usefulness flips with the residual. Mode `specified`. Refuter: an invariant method ranking across two documented changes of residual.

**Union and selection, status `triangulated` (Fig 8 and Fig 9).**

> *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* (Fig 8 :45)
> *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* (Fig 8 :47)
> *"Residual first, frame second."* (Fig 8 :53)
> *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* (Fig 9 :55)
> *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* (Fig 9 :57)
> *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60)

Reading: independence decides both whether agreement means anything and whether the union has surplus. The residual selects the frame. A single lever leaves the intersections standing. Effect sizes and sample sizes are budget filters.

**Order and structure, status `triangulated` (Fig 3 and Fig 12).**

> *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* (Fig 3 :20)
> *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27)
> *"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* (Fig 12 :5)
> *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* (Fig 12 :125)

Reading: the licensed order is dependency, with the break at vocabulary or objective; the bond between methods is part of the result and the two prices do not add.

**Sequence of rooms, status `triangulated` (Fig 7).**

> *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13)
> *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35)
> *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* (Fig 7 :36)

Reading: adding a fifth study in the same closure is motion, not progress.

**Universal mixing ratio, method order, or sample allocation, status `gap`.** No figure line governs it after a genuine re-census. Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held. This is a shape-not-a-name gap, not an unreachable-in-principle theorem.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read)

**W1. Animal behaviour.** Niko Tinbergen, "On Aims and Methods in Ethology," *Zeitschrift für Tierpsychologie* 20:410-433, 1963; read via the Wikipedia entry 2026-11-22. Quote: *"Tinbergen's four questions ... are complementary categories of explanations for animal behaviour."* Prediction: four frames answer four different questions; an integrative understanding keeps all four, and the schema highlights gaps. **Corroborates** the union and per-domain verdict shape. Low overlap. Analogue only, not a figure line.

**W2. Social-science methodology.** R. Bogdan and S. Biklen / P. Rothbauer and Denzin as reported in the Wikipedia "Triangulation (social science)" entry, read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"triangulation refers to the application and combination of several research methods in the study of the same phenomenon,"* borrowed from *"navigational and land surveying techniques that determine a single point in space with the convergence of measurements taken from two other distinct points."* Prediction: triangulation as two distinct bearings on a fixed point. **Corroborates** Fig 2 :35 in that sense. **Conflicts** in the "search for regularities in the research data" sense: that is the shared-pool collapse of Fig 2 :40. The conflict was returned to the census and resolved by separating two quantities, not by flipping the verdict.

**W3. Research methods.** Johnson, Onwuegbuzie and Turner 2007 and the multimethodology literature via Wikipedia "Multimethodology", read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"The multiple approaches being taken need not agree or converge with one another. Instead, the researcher using dialectical pluralism in the conduct of a mixed-method study may tack back and forth between models and perspectives in order to develop insight."* The entry also assigns each approach a distinct analytic interest. Prediction: method-specific analytic interests and complementarity without convergence. **Corroborates** the domain census and Fig 2 :40. Same broad methods pool as W2, so W2 and W3 are one witness.

**W4. Anthropology.** Kenneth Pike 1954 / Mostowlansky and Rota 2020 via Wikipedia "Emic and etic", read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"The emic approach adopts an insider's perspective"* and *"The etic approach adopts an outsider's perspective"*; *"the complementarity of emic and etic approaches to anthropological research has been widely recognized."* Prediction: two frames with different closures; their combination yields a richer view without requiring convergence. **Corroborates** Fig 8 :45 and Fig 4 :20. Low overlap with physics, shared social-science pool with W2 and W3.

**W5. Cultural psychology (the target field).** Wikipedia "Cultural psychology", read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"there has been repeated failure to replicate Western psychology laboratory findings in non-Western settings"* and *"Findings from psychology research utilizing primarily W.E.I.R.D. populations are often labeled as universal theories and are inaccurately applied to other cultures."* The Methods section reports Nisbett and Cohen's combination of a laboratory aggression measure with crime-rate and demographic statistics. Prediction: a lab frame naming itself universal is certifying its own limit, and a failed transport is a residual owed to a differing closure. **Corroborates** Fig 8 :34 and Fig 10 :72. Shares the social-science pool; used for field grounding and the residual, not as an independent verdict witness.

**W6. Physics.** Wikipedia "Proton radius puzzle", read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"Historically the proton charge radius was measured by two independent methods, which converged to a value of about 0.877 fm. This value was challenged by a 2010 experiment using a third method, which produced a radius about 4% smaller."* Prediction: two methods that appear independent can share a hidden pool and agree while wrong; a third, differing method surfaces the residual; the ground settles it. **Corroborates** Fig 2 :18-21, :40 and Fig 7 :35. Genuinely low overlap with all social-science sources.

**Framework essays, secondary and internal.** All eight essays by Isaiah N, opened 2026-11-22. Used for the canonical reading of shape and the simulation-status declaration only. Maximal overlap with the figures; zero independent witness weight.

**Venue pages opened 2026-11-22.** MMIRA homepage, contact, JMMR and conference pages; IACCP homepage, journal, contact and conference pages; ORPC home and author guidelines. These establish public opt-in routes only and are **silent** on the question. No verdict bearing.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: combine the four methods by triangulation against a non-updating ground, keeping verdicts per case and separate, buying independence in proportion to the cost of error, describing the residual before selecting a method, unbundling the union, respecting the dependency chain, transferring methods with provenance rather than recordings, pricing bond and actions separately, and choosing the next method against the residual the last one surfaced. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. World sources corroborate the shape in low-overlap fields (W1, W6) and in the target field (W5); W2's convergence sense conflicted and was resolved by re-census without flipping the verdict. The universal ratio is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, `general` on structure, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** a research programme spends its budget on the wrong lever. If the union is treated as a partition, one method is added when the overlapping conditions need unbundling; if agreement is treated as a verdict, a shared-pool composite is declared robust and a live slot is averaged away; if a finished category is imported without a source gate, a binding from one frame silently becomes a universal. The cost is misdirected fieldwork, wasted archival or survey budget, and a false sense of corroboration. It is real but largely bounded and reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the separator fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The residual, the census, the verbatim lines and the refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 3, in particular (1) a pooled composite that beats per-case verdicts against a ground none can move, (2) two shared-pool methods that still contribute independent weight, (3) an ungated import that earns weight, (4) a moving anchor, (5) a figure-derived universal ratio, (6) one lever restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue, or (7) an invariant method ranking across residual changes. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that agreement across a shared pool adds verdict weight would also reverse it.
