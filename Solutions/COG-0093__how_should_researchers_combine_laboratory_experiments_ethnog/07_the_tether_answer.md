# The Tether's answer on combining four methods

**Mode: `specified` on the methodological claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

One method predicts something about a case and the case returns something else. That gap is indexed per case and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The four named methods are four frames with different actors, currencies, and anchors (Fig 4 :20). The question is what makes a second, third, or fourth frame add reach rather than just add agreement. It is a union of overlapping conditions, not one lever aimed at one problem (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Name the ground and keep it still.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A measurement, an outcome, a dated record, a count. If the reference moves when a method pushes on it, it is a frame, not a ground (Fig 2 :33-34).

2. **Range each method against the ground separately. Do not negotiate.** Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Four verdicts, not one pooled score.

3. **Keep the verdicts per case.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A global near-zero with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). Aggregating across cases is how the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).

4. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost of error.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* If the lab stimulus, the interview guide, the archive code, and the survey item all come from the same theory, they are one witness wearing four names (Fig 8 :48-49). Independence has to be renewed with separate experience (Fig 10 :80).

5. **Describe the residual before selecting the method.** Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (lines 27-28). An undirected union of four methods, with no described residual, is mute (Fig 8 :19-21).

6. **Unbundle the union.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* One lever clears only the region where one condition holds.

7. **Respect the dependency order, and instrument the middle link.** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The break is usually at vocabulary or objective (Fig 3 :25); a link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27). Do not add a method until you can say what the case affords and what would count as progress.

8. **Transfer methods and head starts, never recordings.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A category borrowed from the field into the lab needs provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14), because convergence and adopted import look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :50-52).

9. **Treat the combination as a molecule with a priced bond.** Fig 12 :5: *"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Sequence, production, and comparison are different arrangements of the same four methods. Description bits and settling actions are two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125).

10. **Select the next method against the residual the last one surfaced.** Fig 7 :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Agreement compounds, evidence does not (Fig 7 :13). More studies in the same closure are motion, not progress.

11. **No ratio is returned.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* A universal mixing percentage, method order, or sample allocation is a declared `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the four methods agree more when combined. Agreement from a shared pool is the pool talking (Fig 2 :18-21, :40).
- Not that a pooled effect size, a meta-analytic average, or a composite score is the answer. Those are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that there is a standard sequence such as ethnography, then lab, then archive, then survey. Only the dependency chain is licensed, and it names links, not methods (`06` Q3).
- Not that "culture" is a variable with its own anchor. It is the compound/habitat, and the habitat updates while the ground settles (Fig 11 :73).
- Not that one method is best. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not that the framework has settled any of this against a ground. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

Fig 2 :44 notes that the effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis. The mixed-methods version is an instrument question, not a further argument:

1. **Independence test.** Can we exhibit, for each pair of methods, the evidence each draws on that the other does not? If no such separation can be stated, their agreement carries zero verdict weight.
2. **Anchor test.** For each claim, name the reading that would change if the claim were false and that no method's internal vote can move. If none exists, the "ground" is a frame.
3. **Per-slot transport test.** Does the combined reading still predict when the case changes and one method's slot goes live? A pooled score that fails here is the aggregation failure of Fig 1 :22.
4. **Middle-link instrument.** Can we measure what the case affords and what each method counts as progress before the run? If not, the chain breaks at vocabulary or objective (Fig 3 :25).
5. **Provenance test.** For every category moved between methods, is there a source gate and a shadow test naming the gap it explains? (Fig 6 :12-14).
6. **Ground test for the remaining `gap`.** An instrument or design that returns a unique allocation of effort across the four methods, validated against an outcome none can move, would close the gap. Without one, the honest report is `unreachable`.

Standing refuters from `01` section 3 apply: a pooled composite that beats per-slot verdicts; two shared-pool methods that still contribute independent weight; an ungated import that earns weight; a moving anchor; a figure-derived universal ratio; one lever restoring all four conditions; an invariant method ranking across residual changes.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figures narrow the shape of a defensible combination; they do not supply the cultural theory, the population, the archive, or the discriminating instrument for a particular study. The separation of method from ground is not given by the World sources either: the social-science literature uses "triangulation" in two senses, and the figures decide between them, not the literature. *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The four methods named in this question are likewise a visible choice, not nature's list. *Indiscernibility is not identity*: a combination whose outputs match the phenomenon is still a projection until the lineage is recorded.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a recipe for combining laboratory experiments, ethnography, historical evidence, and population data, and it does not claim that four methods are better because they agree. It says that each method is a separate frame with its own actors, currency, and anchor, so each must be ranged separately against a ground that does not change when any method pushes on it; that the four verdicts stay indexed per case and are never averaged into one score; that independence between methods is the thing to buy, because two methods drawing on the same evidence are one witness; that the residual must be described before a method is chosen, and the four conditions unbundled because they overlap; that what travels between methods is a method and its lineage, not a recording of one occasion; and that the next method should be selected against the residual the last one surfaced. No figure line returns a universal mixing ratio or method order. That is reported as a gap, and the refuters are written in advance. The shape any defensible combination must have is returned; the particular combination for a particular cultural phenomenon is owed to a ground and a practitioner, not to the framework.

**Use of the one-paragraph version:** it is a deliberately lossy artefact. `11_audit.md` measures what it drops. It is suitable only as an invitation to check the full packet, never as a substitute for it.
