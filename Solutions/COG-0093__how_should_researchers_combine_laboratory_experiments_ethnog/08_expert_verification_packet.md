# Expert verification packet - the Tether on combining four methods

**What this is:** a methodological claim in a form a mixed-methods or cultural-psychology researcher can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement made here settles which combination is best. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The four methods are four frames with different actors, currencies, and anchors. Combine them by ranging each separately against a ground that does not update on any frame's opinion, keeping the verdicts indexed per case and never averaged, buying independence between methods in proportion to the cost of being wrong, describing the residual before selecting a method, and treating the four as a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled. What transfers between methods is a method and its lineage, not a recording of one occasion. No mixing ratio or fixed method order is returned by the figures; that is a declared `gap`. What is offered is the shape any defensible combination must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the rest.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 2

- **Statement:** each method is a separate frame, each yields a verdict per case, and those verdicts must be ranged against a non-updating ground rather than pooled into a consensus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Check in your own terms:** name the ground for one of your mixed-methods claims. Does its answer change when a team's interpretation changes? If yes, it is a frame. Can you state a case where one method's slot goes live while the pooled score stays near zero? If yes, the pooling has thrown away the signal.
- **Refuter:** a pooled composite that predicts a non-updating ground better than per-case separate verdicts, across a change of residual. If that is demonstrated, C1 falls.

### C2 - framework-internal and design-facing; checkable by reading Figures 2, 8, and 9

- **Statement:** independence is the design variable, and a union of methods has a surplus only when a residual is described before a frame is selected. Two methods drawing on the same evidence pool are one witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 8 :21 - *"nothing was aimed; the union is mute"*; Fig 8 :53 - *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Check in your own terms:** for one published mixed-methods study you know, list the evidence source of each method's reading. If the lab stimulus, the interview schedule, and the survey item were all written from the same theory, how much independent verdict weight should their agreement carry? Then ask whether the study described its residual before or after choosing its methods.
- **Refuter:** two methods with demonstrably shared evidence that still contribute independent verdict weight against a ground neither can move, or a method selected with no residual description that reaches the same result with no provenance difference. If either is shown, C2 falls.

### C3 - structural, checkable by reading Figures 3, 4, 6, and 12

- **Statement:** the only licensed order is dependency, not a fixed method sequence; the chain breaks at vocabulary or objective; what crosses between methods is a generator with provenance, not a recording; and the combination has a bond structure with two non-additive prices.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 3 :27 - *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 6 :12 - *"source gate: provenance"* and :13 - *"shadow test: explains a gap"*. Fig 12 :5 - *"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
- **Check in your own terms:** in a study you know, can you state what the case affords and what counted as progress before the methods were chosen? Can you name the bond between the field category and the lab measure (sequence, production, or comparison)? Can you state the source of any category moved across methods and the gap it explains?
- **Refuter:** a project that fails at planning while both the affordance vocabulary and the objective comparison measure high; an ungated imported category that earns verdict weight; or a demonstration that description bits and settling actions are convertible at a fixed rate. If any holds, C3 falls.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that four methods agree more when combined, or that agreement itself is validity.
- Not that a pooled effect size, meta-analytic average, or composite score is the verdict.
- Not that there is a standard method sequence. The dependency chain names links, not methods.
- Not that "culture" is an anchorable variable; it is the compound and habitat.
- Not that any method is best in itself. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not that a universal mixing ratio, order, or sample allocation is available. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement, and this packet is LLM-assisted.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument or design, not a further argument:

1. An independence test that exhibits, method pair by method pair, the evidence each draws on that the other does not.
2. An anchor test naming the reading that would change if the claim were false and that no method's internal vote can move.
3. A per-slot transport test across a change of case, where a pooled score fails and separate verdicts hold.
4. A middle-link instrument measuring affordance vocabulary and each method's progress criterion before the run.
5. A provenance test with a source gate and a shadow test for every cross-method category.
6. For the `gap`: a validated design that returns a unique allocation of effort across the four methods against an outcome none can move.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19/:22 and Fig 2 :29/:31/:35/:45, six short passages.
2. Check C1 against one of your own studies: can you name the ground, and do you keep per-case verdicts?
3. Check C2: list the evidence sources of your methods. How much of their agreement is independent evidence? Was the residual described first?
4. Check C3: state the bond between two of your methods and the source of any category borrowed between them.
5. Try one refuter. A failure is more useful to me than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
