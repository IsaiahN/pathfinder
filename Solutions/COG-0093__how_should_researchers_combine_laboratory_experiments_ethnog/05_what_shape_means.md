## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel* gives the operational version: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The anchoring device is the question itself: *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."*

For COG-0093 a **shape** is therefore the separator that any acceptable combination of the four methods must satisfy, stated before a particular design is chosen. The separator is: per-slot verdicts kept apart, anchors that do not move, independence bought, residual described before frame selected, union not partition, dependency order observed, methods rather than recordings transferred, imports gated, and a declared `gap` where no ratio is available. That is a shape. A fixed "40 percent lab, 30 percent field, 20 percent archive, 10 percent survey" recipe is a name, and the framework does not return it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then recurse as *"with each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel*, steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* (lines 30-31).
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

Applied here: describe the combination problem as an effect (what residual survives one method, which frame's closure predicts it), not as a list of method names. That is what `01` section 0 and `09` QUESTION do.

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction to my own first reading

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

**Correction, retained visibly.** My first pass applied "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any of the four methods. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Lab experiments, ethnography, historical records, and population data are all already available bindings from the research frame. Naming one as the answer to a slot is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the binding is derived when it is borrowed, (b) claiming it moves the wall, when Fig 6 :21 says *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom,"* and (c) inventing a universal ratio the figures do not return. Both the first reading and this correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Outputs are not generators

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The corpus contains the problems evolution already solved and someone wrote down. It does not contain the capacity to solve new ones, because that capacity lives in the living process, the mind generating, not in the text it generated."* And: *"You cannot accumulate your way from the category of outputs to the category of generators; they are different kinds of thing, and no quantity of the first sums to the second."*

This is the deepest reason a pile of findings from four methods is not a mixed-methods answer. Four outputs do not contain the generator that produced any of them, and aggregating them does not create one. What crosses between methods must be the procedure, the instrument, and the refuter, which is Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A combined dataset is an output; a transferable way of asking and checking is a generator.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the same point at the group scale: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict"*, and *"a group is only worth having while its members stay different."* Ten teams using the same coding scheme are one team wearing ten names.

## 5. Q1: Does the shape forbid saying which method to add?

No, with provenance. Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* That line is about the physical substrate and does not name research methods; applying it directly to methods was strain S1 in `01` and was re-censused away. Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* governs here.

So: if the residual is a lab effect that does not travel, the frame whose closure predicts that shape is population-level data, and naming it is a binding from the field, not a derivation. Record it as such. What the figures do forbid is a standing list of which method wins: Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

**Falsifier for Q1:** a method selected with no residual description that produces the same reach as one selected after description, with provenance held constant. That would make Fig 8 :53 idle and the correction in this section would be withdrawn.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A methods campaign that gathers famous names to endorse the combination is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the combination a budget to be tried against a ground.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* records the caveat directly: outputs produced by an LLM prompted with the figures *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the methodological claim and cannot settle it.

## 7. Why this matters for mixed-methods cultural psychology specifically

The field's own record supplies the residual that makes the shape non-vacuous. *Cultural psychology* reports Shweder's observation of *"repeated failure to replicate Western psychology laboratory findings in non-Western settings"* and the W.E.I.R.D. overrepresentation, where *"Findings from psychology research utilizing primarily W.E.I.R.D. populations are often labeled as universal theories and are inaccurately applied to other cultures."* A lab frame naming itself universal is a frame certifying its own limit, which Fig 8 :34 forbids. The correction is an import from a differing closure, with provenance and a shadow test, not a call to average the lab and the field into one score.

That is the honest scope of "shape" here: the figures narrow what a defensible combination must look like. They do not supply the cultural theory, the population, the archive, or the instrument that would settle a particular case. Those are the practitioner's contribution and must be recorded as such.
