## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a three-place relation among prediction Γ(b,a), observation o′ and slot s, ordered by time. It carries at least one ordered bond and one comparison bond. A two-place reading (method, result) would lose the action and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The reference point does not update on the frame (Fig 2 :25); "adds reach" is a threshold against a fixed reference, not a conjunction. This seat initially tried a symmetric four-way `+` and rejected it because the cast changes across methods (Fig 4 :20).
- **Scale:** the slot is a case, while a population reading lives at another stratum. The residual crosses a lossy membrane (Fig 4), so any account that treats the four ways as same-size peers is already wrong.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's separator to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *keep verdicts separate and keep the reference fixed while frames stay different.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific study. Result: *run four methods, report four readings per case, and check that they do not all come from the same theory.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It loses per-slot indexing. "Keep verdicts separate" does not say separate by what, and coming back down it permits a pooled score with four labels (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. It loses the source gate. The abstraction cannot say that an emic category moved into the lab needs provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14).
3. It loses the union structure. "Frames stay different" sounds like a partition of four independent conditions; coming back down it licenses one lever where the intersections need unbundling (Fig 9 :55-57).
4. It loses the dependency order and the middle link. The abstraction has no place for an instrument measuring what the case affords or what counts as progress (Fig 3 :25-27).
5. It loses the two currencies. "Run four methods" hides the fact that a cheap description can be unaffordable to settle (Fig 12 :125-126).
6. It erases the `gap`. The one-sentence form sounds like a complete recipe, while the declaration that no ratio is available is the honest stop (Fig 13 :134).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator and not the one-paragraph version, and why `07` labels that paragraph deliberately lossy.
