## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 2 :40-41 (valence), Fig 6 :42 (record grows, reach falls), Fig 9 :4 and :57 (split), Fig 10 :24-28 (channel decay).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: keep the residual per claim, classify agreement, carry a refusal, report lost contact, split the bundle.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a proposed five-state claim record (pending, settled, refused, lost-contact, unreached) with provenance and source-relative trust.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing** is lost if the five-state record is attached to a corpus-level score rather than to each claim slot. Fig 1 :22 names the loss.
- **Valence of agreement** is lost if the implementation uses one consensus rule for both world-claims and conventions. Fig 2 :40-41 separate them; Fig 10 :47 guards the convention.
- **Anchor-must-not-update** is lost if the system lets the flow of opinions revise what it treats as settled. Fig 2 :34 names the coherent drift that follows.
- **The open contradiction at Fig 6 :47** has no branch in the five-state record. A term and its refusal can both be settled, and the report must say that the kind is undecidable, not silently pick one.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a structure of per-claim reporting, and the requirement that a refusal be carried rather than deleted. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
