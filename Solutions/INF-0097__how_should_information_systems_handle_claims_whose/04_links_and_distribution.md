## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Trust is modulated, not default.** Sperber et al. describe a suite of mechanisms that guard against being misinformed. This is the world-side counterpart of "range frames against the ground" (Fig 2 :45), and it is a genuinely different field and author, so it is an independent witness for the *shape*, not for the framework's authority.
2. **Disconfirmation beats confirmation for contested claims.** The CIA tradecraft primer's ACH method evaluates evidence that will disconfirm hypotheses. That matches Fig 9 :27-28 (*"describe it before you go looking"*) and Fig 2 :35 on triangulation rather than negotiation. It is a practice from intelligence analysis, a low-overlap field.
3. **A correction does not erase the old term.** The continued influence effect matches Fig 6 :42: the record grows, and what is reachable falls because a refusal was added, not because anything was deleted. Ecker et al. are social psychologists, a second low-overlap witness.
4. **Future claims need scored contact over time.** Tetlock's calibration and discrimination scores are the world-side analogue of keeping the channel reachable (Fig 10 :29). The fox result is about revising with the unexpected, not about one grand theory. This corroborates D3, with the honest caveat that the book itself was not opened.
5. **A measure used as a target stops measuring.** Goodhart/Strathern corroborates Fig 1 :27: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Engagement and internal credibility scores are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The three essays share an author with the figures and are used for the canonical reading only, never as independent witnesses.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. If a refuter fires, send the retraction instead of the offer.
