## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Two failures to resolve, not one:

- **R1 (classification).** The measured Higgs mass near 125 GeV and top mass near 173 GeV, evolved with the renormalisation group, drive the Higgs quartic coupling λ(μ) negative at high scales. The electroweak vacuum then fails to be the global minimum. Effect: a threshold comparison between the measured couplings and the critical surface λ = 0 has returned a value on the "local minimum" side, and the framework must say what kind of thing that answer is.
- **R2 (rate and consequence).** Given a false vacuum, quantum fluctuations can nucleate a bubble of true vacuum. Effect: a comparison between the barrier's tunnelling action and the ambient energy (temperature or H), integrated over a spacetime volume, decides whether any bubble appears; a second comparison between surface cost and volume gain decides whether a bubble that appears grows or shrinks.

Per Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The relevant slot is one Hubble patch, and the expected event count is a sum over patches, not an average that hides a live slot (Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*).

The question as posed bundles R1 and R2. Fig 9 :4-5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

## 4. Search discipline: three questions kept separate

Following formula step 7 and the derive-first procedure:

1. **SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest).** Is the answer in the current closure and merely unfound? The figure set plus the measured couplings does contain the shape (threshold, rate, cycle sign). What is not in the closure is the numerical value of the critical surface and the exponent. More searching of the figures cannot produce a number. Result: searched out, not a budget failure for the shape, a genuine `gap` for the number.
2. **INWARD.** Extend the instrument from one already returning something. Precision top-mass and α_s measurements improve the critical surface; this is a real instrument with a returning reading. Result: this can move D1, but it does not create a proto-instrument for D3.
3. **OUTWARD.** Import from a frame whose closure differs: Coleman's bounce (statistical nucleation theory carried into field theory) is exactly such an import, with provenance (1977) and a shadow test (it explains the barrier shape that the threshold leaves open). Result: accepted as a secondary witness and recorded with its source gate, not treated as a figure line.

INWARD and OUTWARD were not read against each other. A precision mass measurement is not the same thing as an imported tunnelling theory (Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before outreach: a falsifier written now is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A measurement of m_h, m_t or α_s, or an improved calculation, places the SM in the absolutely stable region (λ(μ) positive for all μ below the Planck scale). This refutes the classification for our world.
2. A computation or observation gives a nucleation rate large enough that the expected bubble count in our past light cone is of order unity or greater while the electroweak vacuum is still observed. This refutes the rate reading or the `unreachable` verdict.
3. No radius exists at which the volume-energy gain exceeds the wall's surface cost, for the actual potential. This refutes the cycle-and-threshold reading of catastrophe.
4. New physics below the instability scale changes the sign of λ. This refutes the extrapolation, not the figure shape.
5. Any instrument at any resolution returns something that fails to resolve vacuum decay. This refutes the "no accessible ground" reading of D3.

If any fires, the specific claim is withdrawn and the reason is recorded. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
