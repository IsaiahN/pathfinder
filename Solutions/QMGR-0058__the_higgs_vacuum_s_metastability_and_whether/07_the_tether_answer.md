- Not a lifetime, not the instability scale (~10^9 to 10^11 GeV is a world result, not a figure line), and not the name of a Planck-scale operator. These are `gap` from the figures (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135).

- **D1:** a source-discriminating top-mass determination (pole versus running mass scheme) plus confirmed m_h and α_s that places the SM inside or outside the metastable region. The 2014 Degrassi paper shows the top-mass scheme is exactly the reading/source problem from *The Hard Problem is About Source*.
- **D2:** a bounce calculation that fixes the action for the measured potential, and a demonstration of whether the critical bubble's wall converts false to true at its boundary (the cycle check).
- **D3:** only a new instrument returning a failed resolution of decay. None exists. Without one, `unreachable` is the honest stop.
- **Standing refuters** (pre-registered in `01` section 5): stable-region measurement; order-one bubble count in our past light cone; no radius where volume gain exceeds surface cost; new physics below the instability scale; any decay-resolving instrument.
