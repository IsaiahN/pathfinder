**What this is:** a claim in a form a particle physicist or cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the two domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the relevant scale is claimed by this derivation.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is the Higgs vacuum metastable and could it decay catastrophically" is a union of two conditions with different grounds, not one hard question. Classification is a threshold comparison: the measured (m_h, m_t, α_s) are ranged against a critical surface where the quartic coupling λ changes sign; the ground is the measured masses. Decay is a rate per unit spacetime volume, indexed per Hubble patch, and a nucleated true-vacuum bubble is a cycle whose output re-enters as input, with the sign of the loop (surface cost versus volume gain) deciding expansion or collapse. The figures return that shape, not the instability scale and not a lifetime. Observable catastrophe has no accessible ground at the quoted lifetimes, so its verdict is `unreachable at this budget`, not `imminent` and not `safe`. Four refuters were written before the answer.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not the instability scale (~10^9 to 10^11 GeV), not a lifetime (~10^100 to 10^655 times the age of the universe), and not the name of a Planck-scale operator. These are world results or gaps, not figure outputs.
- Not that the true vacuum is a seventh primitive or a new kind of thing. It is a rearrangement of the same field (Fig 13 :116: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."*).
- Not that a long lifetime makes decay impossible. *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that the vacuum is safe, and not that it is doomed. No final room (Fig 7 :6).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment in this derivation.
- Not a claim that agreement among published papers corroborates the verdict twice. They share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
