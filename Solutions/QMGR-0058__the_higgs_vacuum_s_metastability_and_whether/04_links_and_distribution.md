## Relevant wider literature, read through abstracts or full text

These are recorded because they bear directly on the two domains. They are secondary. Their agreement about the Coleman bounce is one evidence pool, not independent corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

- **Particle physics, NNLO.** Degrassi, Di Vita, Elias-Miró, Espinosa, Giudice, Isidori and Strumia, *Higgs mass and vacuum stability in the Standard Model at NNLO*, JHEP 08 (2012) 098: *"absolute stability of the Higgs potential is excluded at 98 % C.L. for M h < 126 GeV"*. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP08(2012)098 (abstract read).
- **Particle physics.** Isidori, Ridolfi and Strumia, *Higgs mass implications on the stability of the electroweak vacuum*, arXiv:1112.3022 (2011): *"For a Higgs mass in the range 124–126 GeV, and for the current central values of the top mass and strong coupling constant, the Higgs potential develops an instability around 10^11 GeV, with a lifetime much longer than the age of the Universe."* Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.3022 (abstract and search extract read).
- **Quantum field theory.** Coleman, *Fate of the false vacuum: Semiclassical theory*, 1977: *"the state of higher energy density becomes unstable through barrier penetration; it is a false vacuum."* Also the full paper text via https://www2.physik.uni-muenchen.de/lehre/vorlesungen/wise_21_22/Neutrino-Mass-and-Grand-Unification/Material/coleman_tunneling.pdf, which states that the wall expands and converts false vacuum to true, and that the relevant parameter is the product of the rate and the four-volume of the past light cone.
- **Quantum field theory.** Callan and Coleman, *Fate of the false vacuum. II. First quantum corrections*, Phys Rev D 16, 1762 (1977): the *"decay probability per unit time per unit volume"* is the quantity studied. Source: https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevd.16.1762 (abstract read).
- **Statistical physics, low overlap.** Maris, *Nucleation in liquids and liquid helium*, Comptes Rendus Physique (2006): *"the total energy at first increases with increasing radius before reaching a maximum value at a critical radius Rc, and then decreasing"*. Source: https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/physique/item/10.1016/j.crhy.2006.10.019.pdf (full-page text read).
- **Statistical physics, low overlap.** *Comparison of two methods for calculating nucleation rates*, cond-mat/0002278: *"Our present understanding of the phenomenon of nucleation is based largely on the work of Langer"*. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0002278 (abstract and opening text read).
- **Cosmology.** Markkanen, Rajantie and Stopyra (2019) already listed; also *"High Hubble rates during inflation and high temperatures afterwards could have potentially increased the rate significantly."*

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. Conflict would send the derivation back to the domain census, not flip the verdict. The low-overlap statistical-mechanics sources corroborate the threshold-and-sign shape; the particle-physics sources corroborate D1's classification. They do not corroborate each other independently on the mechanism, because the field-theory treatment imports the statistical picture.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a recorded lifetime before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union split, the threshold and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing any paper.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-condition split and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual (classification plus rate-and-consequence), the three checkable sub-claims and the four refuters from `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the architecture article and the ledger of sources. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method only.** Offer the split-and-refute procedure for reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Each new problem needs its own ground (the Medium article's warning about outputs *"contrived to fit the formula"*).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If a reader shows that the bubble cycle is not a sign-of-loop, or that D3 has a reachable ground, the packet is withdrawn and the reason is recorded in `11_audit.md`.
