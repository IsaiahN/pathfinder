## DERIVATION

### Q1: Are the false and true vacua isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 13 :121: **"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."**
- Fig 13 :122-123: **"In chemistry this has been established since Liebig and Wöhler found two substances with the same composition and different properties, 1824 to"** / **"1826, and Berzelius named it isomerism in 1830."**
- The two vacua are minima of one potential, built from the same field and the same couplings, arranged differently (different field value and different curvature at the minimum). By Fig 12's definition they are **isomers in the structural sense**: same ingredients, different arrangement, different behaviour (one decays into the other).
- This is a **binding from the physics frame**, not a derivation from the figures. It is `specified`, and it is what the figures would call a shape rather than a name: the framework says "same atoms, different arrangement", not "this particular field value".
- **Triangulated** across Fig 12 (bond-based definition) and Fig 13 (chemical precedent), two figures with low overlap in their evidence. The world literature's two-minimum potential corroborates the arrangement but shares the physics evidence pool with the claim, so it is not an independent witness.

### Q2: Is a new primitive owed for the true vacuum?

- Fig 13 :116: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."**
- Fig 13 :41: **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**
- The true vacuum is an arrangement, not a new member. No mint is owed.
- Fig 5 :21: **"the gap has real mass somewhere"**, Fig 5 :23: **"it composes from what we already have"**, Fig 5 :25: **"it is not already in the library"**. The three guards are a product. A candidate "true-vacuum primitive" fails the third guard (it is not novel relative to the field already held) and the second (it composes from what is held).
- **I earlier asked which primitive was missing. That was wrong.** The canonical README's *"shape, not a name"* rule and Fig 13 :116 together refuse the question. Retracted in `01` section 7; kept visible here so the correction is not silently erased.

### Q3: What order do the conditions bind in?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step"** / :21 **"that cannot use it."**
- Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
- Dependency chain for this question:
  1. **Perception:** measured m_h, m_t, α_s return a reading.
  2. **Vocabulary:** a critical surface λ = 0 exists and can be compared against the reading.
  3. **Objective:** classification (D1) says stable or false.
  4. **Planning:** only if false, compute the bounce and rate (D2).
  5. **Learn and carry:** only with a rate, ask about catastrophe and observability (D3), then choose a checker (D4).
- A paper that quotes a lifetime before fixing the top-mass scheme is working above the break: the lifetime consumes the classification, not the other way round.
- Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** D3 is precisely a link with no instrument. It is not a working link that returns "safe".
- **Triangulated** for the ordering rule across Fig 3 and Fig 9 :4-5 (split before search). Single-source for the specific five-link mapping onto this question.

### Q4: What does the sign of the loop govern?

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- Fig 12 :87: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**
- A nucleated wall converts false vacuum to true at its boundary; the new true vacuum is the input to the next slice of wall. That is the cycle. The comparison is between the surface cost (wall tension, scaling with area) and the volume gain (energy drop, scaling with volume). Above the critical radius, the loop grows; below it, the loop closes.
- Fig 1 :10: **"one bet per object slot, every action"** applies per patch: the nucleation bet is placed in each patch independently; aggregation is the error Fig 1 :22 names.
- **Single-source** for the cycle mapping (Fig 12). World sources (Coleman 1977; Sethna's teaching page; Maris 2006) corroborate the threshold-and-sign structure from a different field, but they are secondary and the field-theoretic treatment imports the statistical one, so they are not an independent witness for the mechanism.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if the converting region does not re-enter as input to the wall (a coasting bubble with no false-to-true conversion at the boundary), or if no radius makes volume gain exceed surface cost, the cycle-and-threshold reading fails. The remaining honest claim would be only "a threshold exists somewhere in the rate", with no catastrophe structure.

### Q5: Two currencies, not one

- Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**
- Fig 12 :127: **"They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"**
- Fig 12 :129-130: **"A long chain of cheap parts is expensive to describe and may cost one action to refute. A short arrangement"** / **"over a costly operand is cheap to state and may take a whole budget to settle."**
- Describing the threshold and the cycle is cheap in bits. Settling D1 is an action (precision mass measurements); settling D2 is another action (higher-loop bounce calculations); settling D3 would need a new instrument that does not exist. Summing these into a single "confidence" number is the error Fig 12 names, not the fix.
- **Mode `general`. Single-source** (Fig 12's two-currency rule), with the world's precision-versus-exponent history as illustration only.

## CONCLUSION

The false and true vacua are isomer-like arrangements of one field, not two kinds of thing. No new primitive is owed, and the earlier claim that one was missing is retracted. The conditions bind in dependency order D1 → D2 → D3 → D4, and effort spent quoting a lifetime before fixing the classification is effort above the break. Catastrophe is the sign of a cycle at a threshold, a structural claim that remains `single-source` and physics-facing checkable, with a stated falsifier. The two currencies (bits to describe, actions to settle) are held apart; the D3 action has no instrument, which is why its verdict is `unreachable at this budget` rather than a probability.
