## The question restated as an effect

Two things are being asked and they do not share one ground:

1. **Classification.** Given the measured Higgs and top masses, is the electroweak vacuum the global minimum of the effective potential, or only a local one? What each option leads to: if it is global, the decay question never arises; if it is local, a second question becomes live.
2. **Decay and consequence.** If it is local, what is the nucleation rate of true-vacuum bubbles per unit spacetime volume, and would a nucleated bubble expand (catastrophe) or collapse (harmless)? What each option leads to: a finite barrier plus a tiny rate gives a long-lived false vacuum; a rate large compared with the cosmological 4-volume would have ended our vacuum already.

Fig 9 :4-5 says *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The package therefore answers two domains on two grounds and refuses to average them.
