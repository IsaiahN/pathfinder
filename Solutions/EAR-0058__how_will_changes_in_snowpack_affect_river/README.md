## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies agreement (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
2. **The ground, not agreement, settles it.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* For this question the anchor is a SWE measurement, a stream gauge, a reservoir record, or an isotope source partition.
3. **Send the residual, not the framework.** Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A hydrologist who has absorbed the metatheory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
