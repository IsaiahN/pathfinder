## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and
what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip
on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 12 :23 (symmetric
   bond), Fig 4 :21 and :30 (membrane and round trip), Fig 9 :32 (shape not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` §2: two conditions, lossy bridge, symmetric
   bond, shape not value, ground decides.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete SIDM claim of the kind found in the literature, for example
   a velocity-dependent cross-section producing cores in dwarfs and core collapse in lower-mass
   halos, constrained by cluster-merger offsets.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **coupling law**: Fig 12 gives the bond's symmetry and type, not the continuous
  cross-section function that the halo models actually use;
- the **baryonic dependence**: the coarse phrase "lossy bridge" does not recover which galaxy
  formation parameters enter an inner profile, a point the Bullock and Boylan-Kolchin review states
  explicitly;
- the **probe disagreement**: the shorthand says "ground decides" but does not carry the fact that
  the 2025 satellite-kinematics preference and the 2025 velocity-dependent simulation results point
  in different directions;
- the **regime condition on refusal**: the formula's REFUTED branch applies where the
  collisionless-only residual persists and does not trend down, not globally.

One thing survives cleanly: the two conditions must be separated, a structure must carry the
influence between particles, and a cross-section cannot be read directly off a density profile.
That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading
was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating
"the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would
be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a
measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
