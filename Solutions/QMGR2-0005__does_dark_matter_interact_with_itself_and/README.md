**Question:** Does dark matter interact with itself, and could that explain the structures seen in
galaxies?

**Source:** `QM & GR Questions.txt` line 141. Section A. Dark matter.

**Field for public contact points:** self-interacting dark matter research.

## Restatement in effect terms

The question is a compound of two conditions that can come apart. Condition A is a microphysical
existence claim: dark matter particles scatter off one another with some cross-section per unit
mass, `sigma/m`. Condition B is an explanatory-sufficiency claim: that microphysics closes the
gap between collisionless cold dark matter predictions and the structure observed in galaxies.
A can be true while B is false (a self-interaction too weak, or with the wrong velocity
dependence, to move a halo). B can be partly true while A is undecided (baryonic feedback moves
the inner profile without any non-gravitational dark matter physics). What an answer would look
like, bound as an ordinary term before the run, is: a separated verdict for A and for B, a
characterisation of the bridge between them, and a named ground that would settle each. A single
yes or no is not an admissible answer shape.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection
   between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35)
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"High correlation is
   disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* is the Seat Map rule, and the figure
   form is Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the
   agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier
   that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 sees the residual and the shape claim,
   never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style
rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical
failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing of the whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is
not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the truly testable output would need
to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle
whether dark matter self-interacts, and it does not claim to.
