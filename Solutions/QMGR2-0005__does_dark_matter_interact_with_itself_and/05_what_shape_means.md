## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would
have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and the next lines note
that the system cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already
holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It
constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: two conditions kept apart, a lossy
bridge between them, a symmetric self-interaction bond whose strength is not derivable from the
six-term set, and a ground reading that must settle the strength. A number such as
`sigma/m = 0.5 cm²/g` is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you narrow
  the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Apply that here by starting from terms
  already held, cross-section, halo, inner slope, baryon, and letting the field's own vocabulary
  narrow the search, rather than starting from a preferred SIDM model.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast
  it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather
  than the cause"*. The effect here is a density profile and a cross-section bound, not a
  particle identity.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
  This is the whole reason the outreach email in `02` leads with a checkable shape.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several
  problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*.
  That is the essay-level corroboration of Fig 9 :54-57 and finding F1 in `01`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by
   what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a measurement: a
   cluster-merger lensing offset, a rotation curve, a dwarf kinematic sample.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. A particle-physics model of a light mediator is an import from
   the physics frame; it is licensed by its own ground record, not by the six-term set.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new
dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at
reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also:
*"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming a dark matter particle
or a mediator as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that detects green *"is
speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*. The same reading can come
from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same
behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an
argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here, a cored inner density profile can come from dark matter self-interaction or from bursty
stellar feedback. The rotation curve reads the mass distribution; it does not, by itself, name the
source of the core. Naming self-interaction as the source from the profile alone is the
metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator: velocity dependence across halo masses, or a
merger-origin offset, not a name.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What can the framework say about "does dark matter interact with itself, and could that explain
galactic structure"?** Only this:

1. The question is a union of two conditions that must be unbundled; the intersection needs both
   grounds and is not settled by either alone.
2. A self-interaction between identical particles is a symmetric bond, so its two orderings are
   one term.
3. Influence travelling between particles is the network structure under the six-term set, not a
   seventh primitive.
4. The particle-to-halo move crosses a membrane and loses information; the round trip gap `R_T`
   must be measured rather than assumed zero.
5. The framework cannot return a value of `sigma/m`, cannot name a mediator, and cannot rank SIDM
   against baryonic feedback. Those are ground questions.

**What licenses a particular value or model?** A measurement or a provenanced import, never the
shape itself. Until one of those, the choice is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as
such.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it never writes a cross-section as
a framework output. `01` F4 and `07` §3 police this. The one place a number appears, in `04` §3,
it is explicitly attributed to a published measurement or simulation result, not derived here.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be
allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a
budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of
well-known SIDM proponents who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not
corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a refutation if it fails.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as
the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And:
*"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the
formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal
consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the physics and cannot settle anything they
report on. This status is stated on every substantive file.

**The record is the checkable thing.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: a compressed message carries
context and some subtext but almost never the receiver's history. What travels is an effect
description and the lineage, not the experience. That is why the packet in `08` carries verbatim
figure lines and provenance rather than a summary of the framework.
