# The Tether's answer: which limits to AI progress are technical?

**Mode: `specified` on the discriminator and each domain verdict, `general` on the four-way structure. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for one list split into technical and non-technical. That request is itself a candidate wrong term. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* A real brake is usually a union of conditions with different anchors, so the residual is per slot, not per list. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. The answer

1. **The technical/non-technical line is a reach verdict, not a list of topics.** Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* A limit is **technical** when the term that would close it is outside `closure(Γ)` and no instrument already returning something can be sharpened into it (Fig 6 :23, :32). Its remedy is import or a new instrument, never more of the same search. A limit is **economic in the relevant sense** when the term is already in the closure and was not found (Fig 8 :42). Its remedy is more search, distributed among independent searchers (Fig 6 :50).
2. **Actions and bits are two currencies, not one budget.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* A larger compute budget cannot supply a term the frame cannot compose, and a cleverer description cannot pay for tests nobody funded. Fig 13 :118: *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."*
3. **Regulation and social choice are habitat and convention changes, not closure changes.** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 10 :46: *"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."* A statute changes what may be deployed, who may check, and what contact the system has. Those are real effects on the channel and the habitat, but they add no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).
4. **Agreement and prestige cannot settle any of this.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Many labs agreeing that "data is the limit" is one evidence pool, not a witness.
5. **What the framework returns is the discriminator, not the classification.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. It returns: for each candidate limit, the slot, the governing domain, the anchor, the currency, and what would move it. It does not name which current limit falls where, because that needs a ground reading. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* (Fig 13 :135)

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that compute, data, or any named statute is technical or non-technical. Those are names; the framework returns a shape.
- Not that the four domains are a final partition of reality. F1 of `01` records them as a working census of a union compound.
- Not that more money never helps. It helps in the unreachable-at-budget branch and does not help in the underivable branch. The question is which branch a given limit is in.
- Not that regulation is unreal or merely rhetorical. It changes contact and the checking channel; Fig 10 :43 requires the checker to be unable to produce what it checks.
- Not that this derivation is `grounded`. No measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it, slot by slot

The ground is *"whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :29). For this question:

- **Reach slots:** an instrument that discriminates whether the closing term is composable from what is held. If a term outside the closure is produced by search alone, the technical verdict breaks.
- **Budget slots:** an observation that more actions change what is composable. If it exists, underivable and unreachable-at-budget are one fact and the main discriminator dies.
- **Habitat/convention slots:** an observation that a statute or convention added a primitive to a frame's closure, or that a convention decided a ground verdict while remaining a convention.
- **Metric slots:** a frame that scores itself with its own output and nevertheless settles a verdict.

A source-discriminating instrument is what is owed, not more argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-24): *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; it was *"the arrival of a better instrument"* that resolved the color case.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The four-domain census is a visible choice of frames, not nature's own list; *They're Scanning Books When They Should* warns that *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The answer stops where the available instruments cannot discriminate source from reading, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that some limits on AI progress are technical and others are economic, social or regulatory, as if these were entries on one list. It says there are two different reach facts that the word "limit" hides. If the term needed to close a brake is outside what the frame can compose and no instrument reaches into it, the brake is technical: more search cannot add it, and an import is owed. If the term is already inside the closure and was not found, the brake is economic in the relevant sense: more search, kept independent, is the remedy. Actions and bits are two currencies that do not add, so one cannot buy what the other prices. Regulation and social choice change the habitat or install a convention, which changes contact and who may check, not what the frame can compose. What the framework returns is the test you would apply to each candidate limit, its anchor and its reversal condition, not a name for today's bottleneck. The named limits stay open until a ground settles them.
