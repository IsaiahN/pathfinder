Symmetry: not symmetric, spending actions cannot change what is in the closure, and a convention cannot add a primitive. Scale: first order inside a frame's closure, second order at the habitat and checking levels. Fig 9 :57 names the union reading: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, technical reach.** Actors: Γ, atoms(Γ), closure(Γ), instruments, the ground. Currency: composition and settlement. Anchor: the ground. Partition boundary question, what composes across kinds.
- **Domain 2, economic budget.** Actors: the composer, actions, the action budget. Currency: actions, not bits. Anchor: the reach verdict about how much of the space is searched (a decision rule, not a Fig 2 anchor). Reach-within-closure question.
- **Domain 3, habitat and social choice.** Actors: habitat actors, conditions, relations, the seat, conventions, regulators. Currency: contact and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Union compound of habitat plus convention.
- **Domain 4, verification and public checking.** Actors: outside frames, expert readers, the ground maintainer. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Selection between rooms, not internal agreement.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The two-bin partition is refused. A brake is technical when its closing term is outside `closure(Γ)` with no instrument reaching into it, and its remedy is import or a new instrument. A brake is economic in the relevant sense when the term is inside the closure and unfound, and its remedy is more independent search. Actions and bits are two currencies that do not add. Regulation and social choice change contact and conventions, hence the channel and habitat, not the closure. The framework returns the discriminator with reversal conditions, not a name for today's bottleneck. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; the named limits stay open.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a ranked list instead of a discriminator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the discriminator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No named limit has been classified, so no classification must be withdrawn. The four domains are a working census; if a fifth governing anchor is found, it is added and the mapping is rewritten without touching the figures.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument showing that more actions change what is composable would merge the two reach facts and kill C1. A demonstration that bits and actions are commensurable would kill C2. A statute that adds a primitive to a closure, or an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, would kill C3. A frame scoring itself with its own output and settling a verdict would kill domain 4. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
