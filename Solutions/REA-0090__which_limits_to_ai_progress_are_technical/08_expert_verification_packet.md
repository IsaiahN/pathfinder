# Expert verification packet: the Tether on limits to AI progress

**What this is:** a claim in a form an outside frame can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the discriminator and domain verdicts, `general` on the four-way structure. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle any named limit.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Technical versus economic/social/regulatory" is not one list split two ways. It is a reach verdict plus two different kinds of non-closure change. A brake is technical when the term that would close it is outside what the frame can compose and no instrument already returning something can be sharpened into it; its remedy is import or a new instrument. A brake is economic in the relevant sense when the term is already in the closure and was not found; its remedy is more search, kept independent. Actions and bits are two currencies that do not add. Regulation and social choice change habitat or convention, hence contact and who may check, not what the frame can compose. The framework returns that discriminator with a reversal condition per slot, not a name for today's bottleneck.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Reach verdict (checkable by reading Figures 8, 6, 2)
- **Statement:** underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget are different facts with different remedies; this is the figure-grounded answer to the literal question.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* Fig 8 :42: *"Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where a larger action budget changes the membership of the frame's closure, or a term outside the closure is produced by search alone with no import and no instrument extension.

### C2: Two currencies (checkable by reading Figure 12)
- **Statement:** the cost of a composition has two non-additive readings; an action budget cannot pay for a description-side absence, and a description cannot pay for unfunded tests.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a single accounting in which the two are commensurable, so that the shortfall Fig 12 names cannot occur.

### C3: Habitat and convention (checkable by reading Figures 11 and 10)
- **Statement:** regulation and social choice alter contact or install conventions. They do not add primitives. Their legitimate effect is on the channel through which the ground is reached, including who may check.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 10 :46: *"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a regulation that adds a primitive to a frame's closure, or a convention that decides a ground verdict while remaining a convention. Also relevant against the claim: an agent-only improvement that demonstrably moves the ground.
