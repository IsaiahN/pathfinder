## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched 2026-11-24): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *"for each candidate limit, name the governing domain, its anchor, and the observation that would move it to another domain."*
- A name: *"compute is the technical limit; the AI Act is the regulatory limit; data is both."*

The name is the thing the framework refuses without a ground reading. The shape is what it can return.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the ordinary terms "hard", "expensive", "forbidden", "unverifiable", and find the figure predicates that discriminate them (reach, budget, habitat, channel).
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quoted in exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends the discriminator and the refuter, not the architecture.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-24) gives the discriminator: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. Applied here: "progress has slowed" is a reading with many possible sources (a reach wall, an action shortfall, a habitat change, a saturated metric). The reading does not determine the source. An instrument that discriminates source is what is owed, not a name assigned from the reading.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (quoted in exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The four-domain census is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

The question invites us to name real limits and file them. That is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-24): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

There is a narrower error too. I initially treated "budget" as an anchor, then retracted: a budget is a currency (Fig 12 :126), and an anchor must not change its answer because of what a frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). Naming the wrong kind of thing as an anchor is worse than naming no anchor, because it looks like a settled domain. The retraction is visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## 5. Q answered: what shape means here

**What is the shape of an answer to "which limits are technical"?** A four-way discriminator with a reversal condition per slot:

| reading of the brake | governing domain | anchor | what would reverse it |
|---|---|---|---|
| The needed term is outside `closure(Γ)` and no instrument extends into it | technical reach | the ground | a term composed from the closure alone, or a sharpened instrument |
| The term is in the closure and unfound | economic budget | the reach verdict | evidence that more actions change the closure |
| The contact surface or the permitted conventions changed | habitat / social choice | the ground, never agreement | a statute or convention that adds a primitive |
| The metric is produced by the frame being scored | verification hygiene | the ground | a frame scoring itself validly with its own output |

This is a **kind of thing** (a decision procedure), not an answer (a list). It is `specified`, because each row has a refuter, and `general` at the level of the four-way structure. It is not `grounded`, because no row has been settled against ground in this run.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-24): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well known names to agree with the classification is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator, with the ground settling it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, fetched: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, however *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the classification and is labelled as such everywhere. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
