# What "shape" means for this question: canonical reading from the eight essays

All eight essays by Isaiah N at `adventuresinml.substack.com` were opened and read on 2026-11-23. URLs and a representative quote for each are in `10_source_ledger.csv`. These are world sources and share the author's evidence pool with the figures, so they corroborate; they do not decide.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form of a ground-settled answer that can be stated before the internal name is licensed. It says: per-slot, model-and-habitat, contact-anchored. It does not say which feature or circuit occupies the slot. "Some internal state must be settled against the ground before it is called a representation" is a shape. "Feature 4096 in layer 12 is the object representation" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap with "the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of," then look for new terms that "overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably" and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applying this: the effect is per-slot correctness under contact; the cause-word is "representation." Describe the effect first. The shape stops when it constrains what any candidate answer must look like, not when a feature is found.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming an internal feature as *the* representation turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The specific error this question is prone to

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all yield the reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A probe that decodes "object" from activations is a reading of green. Naming the source (the model represents the object) from that reading is the metamerism error when several internal configurations could yield the same decode. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name. This is the essay-level form of F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction horizon: "A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach." And: "indiscernibility is not identity." A flat probe result and a genuinely absent representation look the same from inside the frame.

## 5. The recursion and the contact answer

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments." "The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence." And: "Contact Is the Distinction Resolver."

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*, Figure 11 (quoted in the essay): "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Figure 1: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." And Figure 9: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Together these give the shape for REA-0045: reasoning is the mapping against an unforgiving environment, slots are kept separate, and a benchmark score is a filter for where to spend effort, not a verdict about representation.

## 6. What the author says specifically about large models

Three passages are direct enough to quote verbatim:

- *They're Scanning Books*: "When a model trains on text, it is not learning to think; it is learning the compressed traces of many minds' outputs and reconstructing the distribution that generated them."
- *They're Scanning Books*: "The human fails by searching; the decompressor fails by guessing."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Training on it reproduces 'the traces the thought left, not the thought', with enough structure to rebuild the appearance of reasoning." And: "Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."

These are the framework author's own words, not figure lines. They predict exactly the figure-derived shape: internal structure can exist and carry useful traces, while the "of the world" and "supports reliable reasoning" claims remain ground questions. They corroborate F1, F2 and F7 of the audit. Because they share the figures' author and pool, they are one witness, not several.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous labs or famous interpretability papers gathered to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground settle it.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is simulacra. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 9. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape not a name" forbids naming any internal feature. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Naming an already-held internal feature is a binding from the ML frame onto something the frame already has, which is a provenance question, not a prohibition. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding settles C2 without a ground. Both the narrowed and the original claim are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :30-31).
