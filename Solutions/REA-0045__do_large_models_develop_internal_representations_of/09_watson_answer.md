## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "Do large models develop internal representations of objects, people, and events that support reliable reasoning?" The question bundles C1, a state claim about model-internal geometry, and C2, a capability claim about ground contact. Option A: answer yes or no at the level of the whole model, treating internal structure as the thing that produces reliability. Option B: unbundle the two conditions, refuse the internal name until a ground settles it, and answer C2 as a shape about model-and-habitat. What each leads to: A scores the frame with a quantity it also produces and turns a benchmark average into a verdict; B yields a per-slot ground claim, three falsifiers written in advance, and an open binding.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency. This census was written before the figure lines in the TETHER section were chosen.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either), not one hard question. Symmetry: "representation of" is an identity bond and "supports" is a production bond, so the causal claim is ordered, not symmetric. Scale: internal activations are within-run and micro; reliable reasoning is across-run and habitat-level, with a lossy transform `R_T` between.
- **Domain 1, model-internal structure.** Actors: activations, weight geometry, features, circuits, linear directions. Currency: probe accuracy, compression achieved, description length. Anchor: none nameable; Fig 1 disqualifies the currency as frame-internal. This is not yet a deciding domain for C1.
- **Domain 2, behaviour and ground contact.** Actors: the model acting on tasks, objects/people/events as world slots, instruments and environments. Currency: per-slot residual, verifiable outcome. Anchor: the ground, a measurement or environment that refuses. This is the domain that decides C2.
- **Domain 3, verification and instruments.** Actors: probing researchers, interpretability practitioners, expert readers, seats. Currency: agreement versus residual, evidence-pool independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. It decides instrument adequacy, not the causal claim on its own.
- **Domain 4, habitat and corpus.** Actors: the training corpus, selection, the world that produced the traces. Currency: contact, introduction, cascade. Anchor: the ground that produced the corpus and can refuse the model. It decides whether an internal "of" relation can form at all.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D2, D3 and D4 overlap on the ground, and the causal claim lives at the intersection of D1 and D2, which needs its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle the per-slot ground before the route; compose the route before naming the state; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the per-slot rule governs the unit of the residual; the benchmark average governs a budget filter. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. The terminological strain between representation as state and representation as grounded correspondence is bound to two separate terms, not smoothed.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources are corroboration only. Overlap is declared rather than hidden. The conflict rule was not triggered; where a source seemed to pull the other way (the linear representation hypothesis looks like a yes to C1), re-census showed it governs internal structure, not the ground-settled "of the world" claim.

- **Field: philosophy of mind, low overlap.** Extended mind thesis (Wikipedia entry, reporting Clark and Chalmers 1998; accessed 2026-11-23): "the mind does not exclusively reside in the brain or even the body but extends into the physical world." Clark: "It is the way the information is poised to guide reasoning ... and behavior that counts." Predicts that an internal-only account of capability fails. Corroborates Fig 11 :39.
- **Field: cognitive science and anthropology, low overlap.** Distributed cognition (Wikipedia entry, reporting Hutchins 1995; accessed 2026-11-23): "Nervous systems do not form representations of the world, they can only form representations of interactions with the world." Predicts that "representation of objects" is the wrong unit; the unit is contact. Corroborates Fig 9 :30-31 and Fig 11.
- **Field: ecological psychology, low overlap.** Affordance (Wikipedia entry, reporting Gibson 1979; accessed 2026-11-23): "The affordances of the environment are what it offers the animal, what it provides or furnishes, either for good or ill. ... It implies the complementarity of the animal and the environment." Predicts that capability is animal-and-environment. Corroborates Fig 3 :7 and Fig 11 :39.
- **Field: philosophy of mind, low overlap.** Chinese room (Wikipedia entry, reporting Searle 1980 and Goldstein and Levinstein 2024; accessed 2026-11-23): LLMs show "robust internal representations of the world" while "the evidence for LLMs having action dispositions necessary for belief-desire psychology remains inconclusive." Predicts exactly the C1/C2 split: internal structure may be present while the action and contact claim stays open. Corroborates the unbundling.
- **Field: direct field, probing, high overlap with the claim.** John Hewitt and Percy Liang, "Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks", EMNLP 2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03368: "does this mean that the representations encode linguistic structure or just that the probe has learned the linguistic task?" and "popular probes on ELMo representations are not selective." Predicts that a probe can manufacture the reading it reports. Corroborates Fig 10 :49 and Fig 6 :34.
- **Field: direct field, interpretability, high overlap.** Mechanistic interpretability (Wikipedia entry; accessed 2026-11-23): "high-level concepts are represented as linear directions in the activation space of neural networks ... although it does not hold up universally." Predicts internal structure exists but not universally; weakly corroborates the existence of structure, and conflicts with no figure verdict because existence is not the claim under audit.
- **Field: direct field, metrics, high overlap.** Rylan Schaeffer, Brando Miranda and Sanmi Koyejo, "Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?", NeurIPS 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.15004: "emergent abilities appear due to the researcher's choice of metric rather than due to fundamental changes in model behavior with scale." - **Field: direct field, reasoning and acting, high overlap.** Shunyu Yao et al., "ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models", ICLR 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629: "actions allow it to interface with external sources, such as knowledge bases or environments, to gather additional information" and the method "overcomes issues of hallucination and error propagation prevalent in chain-of-thought reasoning by interacting with a simple Wikipedia API." - **Field: framework essays, high overlap.** Isaiah N, "Decoding Tacit Knowledge", 2026: "Training on it reproduces 'the traces the thought left, not the thought', with enough structure to rebuild the appearance of reasoning." Predicts fluency without grounding. Corroborates Fig 9 :30-31.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as written receives no yes or no. The figure-governed answer is a shape. For C2, capability is a property of model-and-habitat, measured per object slot against an unmovable ground; an internal change that does not change contact changes nothing. For C1, the literal state claim is a `gap`: its currency is frame-internal and no anchor is nameable, so the figures refuse to score the frame with its own output. "Representation" is a borrowed cause-word until a ground settles it; a feature names a state, while reasoning is a route with ordered bonds. Interpretation is an INWARD instrument that can sharpen but cannot ground, and a null result is not absence. Whole answer mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, unbundles the two claims and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict where a shape is owed. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is high because reliability claims affect deployed systems, so independent per-slot checks are worth buying.
- **undoable:** yes. The internal name is withheld rather than asserted, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. A later grounded measurement can bind a feature to a world slot without changing the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A selective probe plus causal intervention demonstrated necessary for per-slot correctness on novel slots under an independent anchor would close C1 to C2 and refute the `gap`. Invariance of reliable reasoning under removal of contact would break Fig 11 :40. Admission of a frame-internal quantity as an anchor would break Fig 1 :27. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
