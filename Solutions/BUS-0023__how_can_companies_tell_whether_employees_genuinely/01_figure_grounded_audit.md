## 1. The residual in effect terms

The loop defines `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot `s` (Formula step 1). Here:

- **b** is what the employee privately holds: values, commitments, the reasons they would actually act when no one is checking.
- **a** is the observable agreement behaviour: survey answers, use of the values vocabulary, participation in culture rituals, staying, praising the stated values in public.
- **Γ(b,a)** is the company's reading instrument: the engagement or culture survey, the manager's "culture fit" judgement, the promotion decision. It predicts what agreement behaviour will look like.
- **o'** is what the company wants to know: whether `b` would guide a costly, unobserved choice in the direction the stated values require.
- **s** is the employee or team slot. R is indexed by slot, never aggregated into one company-wide "culture score".

> Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
> Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

State the residual as an effect: **why does the same agreement reading recur in slots where the private state that would produce it is absent, and what instrument would discriminate the sources of that reading?** This names no theory, no "culture", no "disengagement", no "bad hire". Those are candidate names and are withheld until the census.

The structural fact that makes this a hard question is the **arity** of the mapping: several latent sources converge on one observable reading. Kelman 1958 names three of them and notes that "the underlying processes in which an individual engages when he adopts induced behavior may be different, even though the resulting overt behavior may appear the same" (Harvard PDF, read 2026-11-22). The company's instrument reads the overt behaviour. It does not read the process.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** before the run (Formula, "Direction"): an instrument specification that separates sources of one reading, with a refuter written in advance and an anchor that does not move when pushed. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing. It is now bound, so section 5's refuter can fire on it.

**Anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :30: "you push on it, it is not an anchor." A culture survey moves when you push on it: change the anonymity promise, change who administers it, tell respondents what answer is wanted, and the score moves. It is an instrument, not an anchor. Candidate anchors are things that do not change because of what the employee thinks at the moment of measurement: a costly choice made when unobserved, an exit, a whistleblowing report, a decision that goes against short-term interest, a behaviour that a third party can check. Fig 2 :37-38 adds a necessary nuance: human judgment is the right anchor for a question constitutively about people, "because the question is about people", but "it is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Self-report is human judgment pointed at an axis it does not measure: the source of the reading.

## 2. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale of R

- **Arity.** R is a many-to-one relation: sources (latent states) to reading (agreement behaviour) to consequence (a choice under the ground). The figure vocabulary says a reading of one state under one set of conditions can be produced by another state under another set. Fig 12 :13: "three different changes, one written formula"; Fig 12 :14: "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"; Fig 12 :15: "nothing downstream can tell them apart,". The bond from source to reading is **production** ("A produces B; B did not exist before", Fig 12 :77), not identity ("two names, one referent", Fig 12 :68). Reading the bond as identity is the error the company makes when it says the score *is* the commitment.
- **Symmetry.** The mapping is asymmetric. Many sources give one reading; the reading does not uniquely recover a source. The comparison "genuine" is a threshold relative to a norm (Fig 12 :87), not an absolute quantity. A "genuine" employee is not the one with the highest score; it is the one whose value state would still produce value-consistent behaviour when agreement costs something.
- **Scale.** Three scales are in view: the individual employee's step loop, the team as a population with a shared vocabulary and a ranking of what counts, and the organisation across time. The membrane between them is "Not a wall: a filter with a direction" (Fig 4 :21), and "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22). A method that works on one employee must be re-anchored, not copied, at team scale: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." (Fig 4 :20). The transform is lossy, so the round-trip gap R_T must be measured (Fig 4 :30).

### 2.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

A domain is admitted only if an anchor can be named. Where none can be named, it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor, nameable? | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Employee loop** (one slot, one scale) | the employee, their latent value state, their choices | agreement behaviour and its residual | yes: a costly or unobserved choice that pays or does not pay at the ground | admitted |
| **D2 Survey / instrument frame** | HR, item writers, respondents, managers | self-report scores, "culture fit" ratings | yes, but as an instrument: a source-discriminating reading, not the score itself (Fig 2 :37-38) | admitted as instrument, not anchor |
| **D3 Social habitat** (team, peers, manager) | peers, norms, rewards, sanctions | reward and sanction for agreement | yes: the valence of agreement changes when the reward changes (Fig 11 :49-51) | admitted |
| **D4 Temporal channel** (repeat measurement cycles) | the survey series, metrics, percepts | contact with the ground | yes: the ground does not decay, "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24) | admitted |
| **X1 "Culture strength" as a free-floating property** | the culture | "strength" | **no** anchor independent of what respondents and managers say | not a domain yet; absorbed into D2 and D3 |
| **X2 "Personality" as the sole cause** | the person's disposition | trait level | measurable, but this is already the employee's Γ inside D1 | absorbed into D1 |

X1 fails the anchor test and is excluded before any figure line is applied to it. This is a structural exclusion, not a claim that shared values never matter.

### 2.3 Partition or union

D1 and D2 overlap: the reading is produced inside the loop, so the overlap is a compound with its own loop. D2 and D3 overlap: the survey is administered inside a social habitat that rewards certain answers, so that overlap is a second compound. D3 and D4 overlap: norms persist through repeated measurement cycles. The whole question is a **union**, not a partition.

> Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
> Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
> Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The unbundled sources are at least three: **C1 internalization** (the content is rewarding in itself and congruent with the person's values), **C2 compliance** (the behaviour is adopted to gain a reward or avoid a punishment), **C3 identification** (the behaviour is adopted to maintain a self-defining relationship to a person or group). These are Kelman's three processes, read 2026-11-22. Compliance: "hopes to achieve a favorable reaction from another person or group". Identification: "wants to establish or maintain a satisfying self-defining relationship to another person or a group". Internalization: "He adopts the induced behavior because it is congruent with his value system." They overlap: a person can comply and identify, identify and internalize, or all three. A single lever, for example "make the survey anonymous" or "add a values question", clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. C1 failure and C2 failure are not degrees of one failure; they need different instruments.

Allen and Meyer 1990, read 2026-11-22, supply a second business-pool description of the same union at the commitment level: "Employees with strong affective commitment remain because they want to, those with strong continuance commitment because they need to, and those with strong normative commitment because they feel they ought to do so." That is three sources of one observable (staying), which is the same arity. Both Kelman and Allen and Meyer share the organizational-behaviour evidence pool, so they count as one witness, not two (Fig 2 :31).

### 2.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

> Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."
> Fig 3 :25-26 "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."
> Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Order for this question: 1 perception (can the company read value-relevant behaviour at all?) → 2 vocabulary (does it have terms for what a stated value affords in a concrete situation?) → 3 objective (can it say what would count as genuine, before the measurement?) → 4 planning → 5 learn and carry. The middle link is where this question breaks. Companies have rich terms for engagement, satisfaction and belonging, and no instrument for "what would count as a value-consistent choice when agreement is not rewarded". Most diagnosis effort lands above the break, where the survey instruments already exist (Fig 3 :29).

## 4. Findings

Mode tag on each: `general` if structural, `specified` if a falsifiable prediction about companies, `grounded` only if settled by a measurement. None is `grounded`.

### F1 - One agreement reading has several sources; the reading cannot discriminate them

**Mode: `specified` for the company application, `general` for the projection claim. Domains D1, D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 12 bond notation and the vision-science metamerism result, low topical overlap: composition theory versus colour psychophysics).**

> Fig 12 :13 "three different changes, one written formula"
> Fig 12 :14 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"
> Fig 12 :15 "nothing downstream can tell them apart,"
> Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

Three people can return the same "I live these values" answer. One produces it from internalization, one from compliance with a reward, one from identification with the team. The written formula is the same; the bond that produced it is not recorded, and "nothing downstream can tell them apart". Fig 8 :51 gives the provenance version of the same fact: the contents are identical and only the record of origin separates them.

The framework's own source essay states the mechanism in effect terms. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, reopened 2026-11-22: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." A culture survey is the green-reading instrument. What is owed is a discriminator, not another item on the same scale.

Low-overlap corroboration: Funt and Roshan 2021, *Journal of Vision*, read 2026-11-22: "the greater the degree of metamer mismatching for a color, the wider the range of spectral reflectances that could have led to it". In vision science, one cone-response triple is produced by many spectral reflectances; the reading does not determine the source. This is the same arity as the agreement reading, arrived at in a different field with different instruments, so it is a genuine second witness for the shape (Fig 2 :31).

**Refuter:** an instrument demonstrated to map one agreement reading uniquely to one latent source, with published discrimination error rates and a proof or strong test of injectivity over the relevant conditions. If such an instrument exists and survives a change of conditions, F1 is false. Until then, a high score is a reading with an unrecorded source.

### F2 - The survey is an instrument, not an anchor

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 2 anchor rule and Fig 10 channel-decay rule, low overlap: triangulation versus ground maintenance).**

> Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
> Fig 2 :30 "you push on it, it is not an anchor."
> Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
> Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"

A score that rises when the CEO announces it matters, falls when anonymity is withdrawn and rises when respondents believe a good answer helps them is moving when pushed. It is a frame, not an anchor. Fig 10 :26 names the decay modes of the channel that carries the reading: saturated metrics, pooled readings. An annual engagement score that has been stable at 82 percent for six years is more likely to be a saturated instrument than a settled ground.

World corroboration, same business pool, so one witness: Fisher, Rawal, Hochstein and Plouffe 2024, *Personality and Individual Differences*, read 2026-11-22, developed the SDR-O because "Organizational respondents often provide misleading or patently false information on job applications, personality tests, and other self-reports", and their scale "reflects inauthentic impression management rather than self-deceptive enhancement." Ried, Eckerd and Kaufmann 2022, *Journal of Purchasing and Supply Management*, read 2026-11-22, note that "source triangulation can help in reducing social desirability bias." Both are methodological corroboration of F2, not independent grounds for a verdict about any particular employee.

**Refuter:** a self-report measure whose score is invariant to pushes (anonymity, administrator, stated stakes, known-answer conditions) and that still pays at the ground in prospective prediction. If it does not move when pushed and still predicts, it behaves like an anchor and F2 is wrong for that measure.

### F3 - Buy independence: two instruments, not two agreeable raters

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1, D2, D4. Status: triangulated (Fig 2 independence rule, Fig 8 overlap rule and Fig 9 describe-first rule, three figures with distinct roles).**

> Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
> Fig 2 :44 "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."
> Fig 8 :29 "low overlap: B does not draw on A’s evidence"
> Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"
> Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A manager rating and an employee self-report of the same week are not two frames; they are one evidence pool read twice. Two instruments pointed at the same thing are, which is why Fig 2 :31 uses that exact image. The company should pick the second frame *after* describing the residual in effect terms, not before, or any frame will seem to fit. Podsakoff, MacKenzie, Lee and Podsakoff 2003, *Journal of Applied Psychology*, read 2026-11-22: "common method biases pose a rival explanation for the correlation observed between the measures." That is Fig 2 :44's collapse stated in the survey-methods literature: correlated observations are one witness, and the effective-sample-size correction is a warning, not a licence to count them twice.

**Refuter:** a single frame that predicts the ground as well as the best low-overlap pair, with no gain from adding the second frame, measured prospectively per slot. If so, the independence requirement is not buying anything and F3 weakens to a budget rule.

### F4 - Per-slot verdicts, never a blended culture score

**Mode: `general` with a `specified` consequence for method. Domains all. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 per-slot rule and Fig 2 survey-statistics warning, low overlap).**

> Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric."
> Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
> Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

An overall "values alignment: 82 percent" figure is produced by the same instrument it is meant to validate. It is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27). It also throws away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22). The specified consequence: any report that answers BUS-0023 with an average has already destroyed the diagnostic. Report per team and per slot, keep the three sources separate, and never collapse compliance and internalization into a single "alignment" number.

**Refuter:** an averaged organisation-level statistic that prospectively identifies which named slot will defect from a stated value under a costly choice, with better accuracy than per-slot readings. If one exists, F4's methodological consequence is false.

### F5 - The sources are a union and must be unbundled before a lever is chosen

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1, D3. Status: triangulated (Fig 9 union rule and Kelman's three processes from a different field, so one framework witness plus one business-pool description of the structure).**

Fig 9 :54, :55 and :57 (quoted in section 2.3) govern. The three sources have different instruments:

- **Internalization** shows up when the value-content payoffs for the person, when agreement is unrewarded and when the behaviour is costly (Kelman: "He adopts the induced behavior because it is congruent with his value system.").
- **Compliance** shows up when the agreement track tracks the reward schedule: the score rises when praise is attached and falls when it is not (Kelman: "hopes to achieve a favorable reaction from another person or group").
- **Identification** shows up when the agreement track tracks the relationship, not the content: the person defends the group's phrase while unable to say what it would require in a concrete case (Kelman: "wants to establish or maintain a satisfying self-defining relationship").

A single lever, for example "increase recognition for living the values", clears the region where compliance alone holds and leaves the internalization and identification intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). This is why the verdicts in `09_watson_answer.md` are kept separate and never averaged.

**Refuter:** a single intervention that removes all three sources' contributions, leaves no categorically different residue, and still pays at the ground. That would collapse the union into a partition.

### F6 - A survey is a substituted habitat: it proves echoing, never living

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1, D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 isolation law and Fig 6 instrument rule, low overlap: habitat substitution versus instrument descent).**

> Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
> Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
> Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

A survey creates a habitat in which the only action available is to produce a reading. It proves that the person can echo the values vocabulary in that habitat. It does not prove that the person would choose accordingly in the original habitat, where a value may cost a promotion, a friendship or a weekend. "Isolation substitutes a habitat, it does not remove one" (Fig 11 :66). What was failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails. The honest move is to bracket the round trip: carry the survey reading up, bring the behavioural reading back down, and measure R_T rather than assume it is zero (Fig 4 :30).

**Refuter:** a survey-based measure whose round-trip gap to a ground-verified behaviour is measured, not assumed, and shown to be zero across two habitat changes. If it survives, the survey is not a substituted habitat in the relevant sense and F6 is false.

### F7 - Sharpen an instrument that is already returning something

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 instrument rule and the vision-science illuminant result, low overlap).**

> Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall"
> Fig 6 :34 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
> Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

The instrument that would discriminate sources is not specified from a description and then deployed. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something. In the vision analogue, the proto-instrument is the second illuminant: the metameric pair matches under one light and mismatches under another, so changing the illuminant is a readymade discriminator (Funt and Roshan 2021: "when the light is changed, their cone responses may no longer be identical to one another"). In the company, the proto-instruments already returning something are: exit and stay interviews, decisions made when the manager is absent, reports through a channel that does not reward the reporter, promotion and turnover after a value conflict, and items that ask what the person would expect to see if the value were real. Sharpen these. Do not design a new "culture score" from a specification. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A declared `gap` after this search is a verdict, not a silence.

**Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument specified from first principles, with no prior reading to sharpen, that works on first deployment across two populations. That would break the instrument-descent rule and F7 with it.

### F8 - Repeating the same survey is motion, not progress

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D4. Status: triangulated (Fig 7 room chain and Fig 10 channel decay, low overlap: selection between rooms versus ground maintenance).**

> Fig 7 :12 "same closure, handed along;"
> Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"
> Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."
> Fig 7 :37 "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"
> Fig 7 :38 "the bracket’s fidelity across the handoff"
> Fig 7 :39 "the ground’s pressure on the room"

An annual survey with the same items, the same respondents and the same administration is one long room. Agreement inside it compounds while evidence does not. Progress per handoff needs both the bracket's fidelity and the ground's pressure. If either goes to zero, the series runs in place. This is the figure-grounded reading of "survey fatigue": not that people are tired, but that the next room was not selected against the residual the last one surfaced.

**Refuter:** an unchanged annual instrument whose repeated readings increase both bracket fidelity and ground pressure, measured rather than assumed, over two or more cycles. If so, F8's mirror claim is false for that series.

### F9 - The break is at vocabulary and objective

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D1. Status: single-source (Fig 3, with Fig 12 :111-113 as a corroborating line from a different figure but the same framework, so one witness).**

> Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
> Fig 3 :29 "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."
> Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

Companies can name a state: engaged, aligned, committed. They cannot name the route from a stated value to a concrete choice under cost, because the bond is not in the vocabulary (Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."). The highest-value diagnostic question is therefore not "do our employees share our values?" but "at which link does our chain currently stop, and was that measured?"

**Refuter:** a documented team where perception, vocabulary and objective links all measure as working and the failure is located at planning. That would falsify the "usually at the middle" claim, though not the dependency ordering.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the design-instrument rule: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following is observed:

1. An agreement reading that uniquely determines the latent source, with demonstrated injectivity and published discrimination error across a change of conditions (breaks F1).
2. A self-report measure invariant to pushes (anonymity, administrator, stakes, known-answer) that still pays at the ground prospectively (breaks F2).
3. A single frame that predicts costly, unobserved, value-consistent behaviour as well as the best low-overlap pair, with no gain from independence (breaks F3).
4. An averaged organisation-level score that prospectively identifies per-slot defection better than per-slot readings (breaks F4).
5. One intervention that clears all three union conditions with no categorically different residue (breaks F5).
6. A survey measure with a measured, zero round-trip gap to behaviour across two habitat changes (breaks F6 and F7).
7. An unchanged annual instrument whose repeated readings increase both bracket fidelity and ground pressure (breaks F8).
8. Figure-level: a derivation of one of the bond types from the others, or a failure of closure idempotence inside a closed set (breaks the figures, not just this reading).

No part of this solution may be upgraded to `grounded` until at least one of these has been run and has failed to fire, and until a ground measurement has paid for the shape.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the refuter, not with a theory of culture (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
2. Replace the question "do employees share our values?" with "what would we expect to see if they did, and what would we expect to see if they did not?" (Fig 9 :27-28). The first question can always be answered yes; the second must be answered before the instrument is chosen.
3. Keep three verdicts per slot: compliance, identification, internalization. A blended "alignment score" is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) and destroys the diagnostic.
4. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42, :44). Low-overlap frames: self-report, an unobserved costly choice, a third-party or exit record. Range each against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45).
5. Sharpen proto-instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32, :34): exit interviews, off-hours decisions, safe reporting channels, post-conflict promotion and turnover, items about what the value would require. Change the illuminant, not the wording of the same question.
6. Bracket the round trip (Fig 4 :30; Fig 11 :70). Measure the gap between what the survey predicts and what behaviour shows. Do not assume it is zero.
7. Stop at the shape (Fig 9 :32). Do not name an employee as genuine or performing. Declare `gap` where the instrument is not held.
8. Offer the formula as a method after a verdict is recorded (Fig 4 :22). Each team writes its own refuter and states its own mode.

## 8. Recursive derivation summary

1. **Is this one question?** No. Fig 9 :54, :55, :57: it is a union of at least three sources of one reading, each with its own consequence and instrument.

## 9. Updated verdict

The shape survives: **a single agreement reading cannot discriminate genuine sharing from performed agreement, because the same reading is produced by several sources whose arrangement the reading does not record. A company can only specify the shape of a discriminator, keep the sources' verdicts separate per slot, buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, and range each frame against an anchor that does not move when pushed. Which particular employee is genuine is a declared `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument exists.** Mode `specified`, with `general` structure and no `grounded` claim. No world source flipped a verdict; the one genuine conflict (costly signalling) was returned to the census and re-mapped. No unresolved strain remains after re-census.
