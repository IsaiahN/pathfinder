# Expert verification packet: the Tether on genuine sharing versus performed agreement

**What this is:** a claim in a form an organizational-behaviour researcher, commitment scholar or survey methodologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No company-level measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A single agreement reading cannot discriminate genuine sharing from performed agreement, because the same reading is produced by several sources whose arrangement the reading does not record. The sources are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different consequent history. A self-report score is an instrument, not an anchor, because it moves when pushed. A company can only specify the shape of a discriminator, keep the sources' verdicts separate per slot, buy low-overlap frames pointed at the same residual, and range each against an anchor that does not move when employees push on it. Which particular employee is genuine is a declared gap until a source-discriminating instrument exists. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 8

- **Statement:** one reading has several sources; contents do not record which bond produced them. The three sources are isomers, not variants of one state.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :13 "three different changes, one written formula"; Fig 12 :14 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"; Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."; Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".
- **Business-facing check:** Kelman 1958, "the underlying processes in which an individual engages when he adopts induced behavior may be different, even though the resulting overt behavior may appear the same", and "Responses adopted through different processes will be performed under different conditions, will be changed and extinguished under different conditions, and will have different properties."
- **Refuter:** an instrument demonstrated to map one agreement reading uniquely to one latent source, with published discrimination error that holds across a change of conditions. Or evidence that the three processes have no distinguishable consequent history under any change of reward, relationship or content, which would collapse the isomer claim.

### C2 - Survey-method facing; checkable by a methodologist against the measurement literature

- **Statement:** a self-report agreement score is an instrument, not an anchor; two readings from the same source at the same moment are one witness, and effective sample size must be corrected for their correlation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."; Fig 2 :30 "you push on it, it is not an anchor."; Fig 2 :44 "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."; Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings".
- **What to check:** does an agreement score move under changes of anonymity, administrator and stated stakes? If it does, it is a frame. Does a second reading from the same respondent add independent information? Podsakoff et al. 2003: "common method biases pose a rival explanation for the correlation observed between the measures." Fisher et al. 2024: "Organizational respondents often provide misleading or patently false information on job applications, personality tests, and other self-reports."
- **Refuter:** a self-report measure invariant to those pushes that still pays prospectively at the ground; or a single frame that predicts costly, unobserved, value-consistent behaviour as well as the best low-overlap pair, so independence buys nothing.

### C3 - Framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 6, 10 and 4

- **Statement:** the discriminator descends from an instrument already returning something, and the survey's habitat substitution must be bracketed by a measured round-trip gap R_T.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."; Fig 6 :34 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."; Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."; Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- **What to check:** is there an existing reading to sharpen, for example an exit interview, an off-hours decision, a safe reporting channel, a promotion outcome after a value conflict? Has the gap between the survey prediction and behaviour been measured rather than assumed?
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument specified from first principles, with no prior reading to sharpen, that works on first deployment across two populations; or a survey measure whose R_T to behaviour is measured and shown to be zero across two habitat changes.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that surveys are useless, or that agreeing employees are lying. Surveys are instruments and useful ones.
- Not that any named employee, team or manager is genuine or performing. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that Kelman's three processes or Allen and Meyer's three components are figure-derived primitives. They are business-pool bindings onto the union's structure; the import adds no atom (Fig 6 :25).
- Not that a value-consistent behaviour is a seventh primitive, or that the six-term physical substrate governs the content of values. Fig 13 was tried on that content in `01` and sent to the honest-stop role by re-census.
- Not that the proposed dispositions of "catalyst" and "cycle" in `06` are measurements. They are `specified` analogues.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Injectivity test:** does an instrument map a reading uniquely to a source, with error rates that survive a change of conditions?
2. **Push test:** does a self-report survive anonymity, administrator and stake manipulations while predicting a costly choice?
3. **Independence test:** does a low-overlap pair beat either frame alone at prospective per-slot prediction?
4. **Round-trip test:** is R_T measured and does it shrink when the illuminant changes?
5. **Union test:** does one lever clear all three sources with no categorically different residue?
