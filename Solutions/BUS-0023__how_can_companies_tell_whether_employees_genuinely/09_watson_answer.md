## QUESTION

How can a company tell whether employees genuinely share stated values or simply perform agreement? Restated as an effect: why does the same agreement reading recur in slots where the private state that would produce it is absent, and what instrument would discriminate the sources of that reading? R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot s (Formula step 1), where b is the private value state, a is the agreement behaviour, Γ is the company's reading instrument, and o' is a costly or unobserved value-consistent choice that pays at the ground. Option A: treat a high score as evidence of genuine sharing. Option B: keep three per-slot verdicts separate, buy low-overlap frames against an anchor that does not move when pushed, and measure the round-trip gap. A leads to a verdict with no anchor and spends the independence of the people asked to confirm it. B leads to a checkable discriminator shape with falsifiers written in advance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: many-to-one, several latent sources converge on one agreement reading, which then bears on a costly choice. Symmetry: asymmetric, source produces reading; reading does not recover source. "Genuine" is a threshold relative to a norm (Fig 12 :87), not an absolute quantity. Scale: individual employee loop, team population with a shared vocabulary and a ranking, organisation across repeated measurement cycles; the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :30).
- **D1 Employee loop.** Actors: the employee, their latent value state, their choices. Currency: agreement behaviour and its residual. Anchor: a costly or unobserved choice that pays at the ground. Admitted.
- **D2 Survey / instrument frame.** Actors: HR, item writers, respondents, managers. Currency: self-report scores and culture-fit ratings. Anchor: a source-discriminating reading, not the score itself; Fig 2 :37-38 makes human judgment legitimate for questions about people but an instrument on axes it does not measure. Admitted as instrument, not anchor.
- **D3 Social habitat.** Actors: peers, norms, rewards, sanctions. Currency: reward and sanction for agreement. Anchor: the valence of agreement changes when the reward changes (Fig 11 :49-51). Admitted.
- **D4 Temporal channel.** Actors: survey series, metrics, percepts. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Culture strength as a free-floating property.** Actors: the culture. Currency: strength. Anchor: none independent of what respondents say. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D2 and D3.
- **X2 Personality as sole cause.** Actors: disposition. Currency: trait level. Anchor: already inside D1's Γ. Absorbed.

Union, not partition: D1/D2, D2/D3 and D3/D4 overlap, so each overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57). Unbundled conditions: C1 internalization, C2 compliance, C3 identification. Order by dependency: perception of value-relevant behaviour, vocabulary for what a value affords, objective (what would count as genuine), then instrument and planning (Fig 3 :20, :25-27).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's six-term physical substrate to the content of values only fit by calling a value a kind of energy. Re-census sent Fig 13 to the honest-stop role only (strain S1, resolved). No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a verdict about a person does not. A single agreement reading cannot discriminate genuine sharing from performed agreement, because several sources produce one reading and the bond that produced it is not recoverable from the operands. The sources are isomers and must be kept separate per slot: internalization, compliance and identification. Self-report is an instrument, not an anchor; independence is bought with low-overlap frames ranged separately against a ground that does not move when pushed; discriminators descend from instruments already returning something; and the round-trip gap between survey prediction and behaviour must be measured. Which employee is genuine is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`, with `general` structure and no `grounded` claim.

- **cost if wrong:** a company spends its measurement budget sharpening the wrong instrument. If the three sources were one state, a better survey would suffice; treating them as a union would buy unnecessary frames and delay a decision. Conversely, if they are a union and the company treats them as one, it applies a single lever to overlapping conditions, clears only the region where one condition holds, and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a reading and calling it a source. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, because "NOTHING IS EVER DELETED, and the reach falls because something was ADDED" (Formula step 3). The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62). The business-pool bindings are labelled as such and can be removed without touching the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument shown to map a reading uniquely to a source with error rates that survive a change of conditions breaks F1; a self-report invariant to pushes that still predicts a costly choice breaks F2; a single frame matching the low-overlap pair breaks F3; an averaged score outperforming per-slot readings breaks F4; one lever clearing all three conditions breaks F5; a measured zero R_T breaks F6; an unchanged survey raising both handoff factors breaks F8; deriving a bond from the others or failing closure idempotence breaks the figures. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
