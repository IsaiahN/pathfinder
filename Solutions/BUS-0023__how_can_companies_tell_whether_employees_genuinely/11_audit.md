## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the recurrence of one agreement reading in slots where the private state that would produce it is absent. Arity: many-to-one, sources to reading to costly consequence, so this is a source-discrimination problem, not a measure-the-source problem. Symmetry: asymmetric, source produces reading (Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before"), not identity (Fig 12 :68 "two names, one referent"). Scale: individual loop, team population with a shared vocabulary, organisation across repeated cycles; the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :30).
- **D1 Employee loop.** Actors: employee, private value state, choices. Currency: agreement behaviour and its residual. Anchor: a costly or unobserved choice that pays at the ground. Admitted.
- **D2 Instrument frame.** Actors: item writers, respondents, managers. Currency: self-report scores. Anchor: a source-discriminating reading, not the score. Admitted as instrument, not anchor; Fig 2 :37-38 makes human judgment legitimate for questions about people while limiting it on axes it does not measure.
- **D3 Social habitat.** Actors: peers, norms, rewards, sanctions. Currency: reward and sanction for agreement. Anchor: the changing valence of agreement when the reward changes (Fig 11 :49-51). Admitted.
- **D4 Temporal channel.** Actors: survey series, metrics, percepts. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Culture strength as a free-floating property.** No anchor independent of what respondents say. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D2 and D3.
- **X2 Personality as sole cause.** Already inside D1's Γ. Absorbed.

Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-57): D1/D2, D2/D3 and D3/D4 overlap, and each overlap is a compound. Unbundled conditions: internalization, compliance, identification. Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, then instrument and planning (Fig 3 :20, :25-27).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 12's bond definition and Kelman's consequent-history test, I derived from Fig 6's instrument-descent rule and Fig 2's anchor rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low topical overlap between routes, so the agreement is not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The formula defines R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :13-16 and :117 (same formula, different bond; isomer), Fig 2 :29-30 and :44 (anchor, push test, correlated observations), Fig 6 :32 (instrument descent), Fig 11 :70 (substituted habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: a reading is not a source, keep three verdicts separate, buy low-overlap frames, sharpen proto-instruments, measure R_T.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the company-level method: three separate per-slot readings, a low-overlap pair pointed at unobserved costly choices, a measured gap between survey prediction and behaviour, and a declared `gap` on naming individuals.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **consequent-history test** that makes the three sources distinguishable (Kelman's "performed under different conditions, will be changed and extinguished under different conditions"); a company that simply adds three survey items has not implemented the discriminator;
- the **anchor's immovability**, which is easy to assert and hard to secure, because every company instrument can be pushed by someone with an interest in the answer (Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying.");
- the **cost-of-deviation nuance** from the biology source. The shorthand "costly signals are honest" does not survive; Számadó et al. 2023 refute it. What survives is the narrower claim about the cost of faking, which requires a discriminator, not a bigger signal.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: one reading has several sources whose arrangement it does not record, so a single score cannot settle the question. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
