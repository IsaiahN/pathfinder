## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The same README adds two exact lines: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0023 the shape is:

> an instrument specification that separates the sources of one agreement reading, with its refuter written in advance, ranged separately against an anchor that does not move when pushed, reported per slot and never averaged.

That is a shape. "Employee 41 is genuinely committed" is a name. "Our culture alignment is 82 percent" is a reading with an unrecorded source, not even a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect", Fig 9 :31 "rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- The stop condition from *Library of Babel*: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: describe the residual before choosing an instrument. Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." If a company starts with "we will run an engagement survey", any result will seem to answer the question. If it starts with "we need to know whether a value would still guide a choice when agreement is unrewarded and unobserved", the instrument has to be selected against that description.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For BUS-0023, the company's listed values are a declared vocabulary, not a neutral instrument. Declaring them makes the choice visible; it does not make them a ground. Naming one employee as "genuine" from a reading turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the survey reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, reopened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument detects green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

A culture survey reads the value-consistent answer. It cannot say whether the source was internalization, compliance or identification. Kelman 1958 (world source, read 2026-11-22) supplies the three sources empirically: "the underlying processes in which an individual engages when he adopts induced behavior may be different, even though the resulting overt behavior may appear the same." Funt and Roshan 2021 (vision science, read 2026-11-22) supply the physics: "the greater the degree of metamer mismatching for a color, the wider the range of spectral reflectances that could have led to it."

The error is therefore not "surveys are bad". It is **naming a source from a projection**. What is owed is a discriminator, as the essay says: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The spectrometer resolved the green reading, not an argument about what green really is.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A survey validated by another question in the same survey is a frame validating itself.

## 5. Q: what would license a verdict about an employee? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A verdict about a particular person is licensed only by the ground settling a source-discriminating instrument, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No. `01` section 2.3 binds three conditions from Kelman and marks them as a business-pool description of the union, not as a figure-derived name for the employee's private state. `07` and `08` keep the shape claim and the gap explicit. The one place a name appears is the hypothetical reported answer ("Employee 41 is genuinely committed"), which is refused, not asserted.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, reopened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers respected HR leaders to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator, with the ground settling it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this evidence pool and is therefore internal consistency, not a witness.

## 7. Narrowing note (kept visible)

An earlier reading of the "shape, not a name" rule treated it as a ban on using any named psychological construct. That was too broad. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing". Kelman's three processes and Allen and Meyer's three components are already available in the business frame; using them to describe the union's conditions is a binding from that frame onto a structure the figures also describe (Fig 9 :54). What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world.") or that the construct *is* the private state rather than a reading of it. Both claims are refused in `01` section 7. This narrowing is recorded rather than silently applied.
