# The Tether's answer on genuine sharing versus performed agreement

**Mode: `specified` on the company claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No employee was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A company states values and reads agreement: survey answers, use of the values vocabulary, public praise, staying. It wants to know whether a private value state would still guide a costly, unobserved choice. The same reading is produced by several sources whose arrangement the reading does not record. Fig 12 :13 "three different changes, one written formula"; Fig 12 :14 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not". This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54), because internalization, compliance and identification overlap in the same person.

## 2. The shape

1. **Agreement is a reading, not a source.** Fig 12 :46 "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." The bond from a private state to an agreement answer is production (⇒), not identity (≡). Fig 12 :68 "two names, one referent" is the identity the company incorrectly assumes. The green-reading instrument cannot comment on the source.

2. **A self-report score is an instrument, not an anchor.** Fig 2 :30 "you push on it, it is not an anchor." Change the anonymity, the administrator or the stated stakes and the score moves. Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" names the ways the channel decays. The anchor is a costly or unobserved choice that pays at the ground: an exit, a report through a channel that does not reward the reporter, a decision made against short-term interest, a third-party check. Range each frame against it separately (Fig 2 :45).

3. **The three sources are isomers and must be kept separate.** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Kelman 1958 records the distinguishing consequence: "Responses adopted through different processes will be performed under different conditions, will be changed and extinguished under different conditions, and will have different properties." A blended "alignment score" is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) that erases exactly the difference that matters.

4. **Independence is bought, not assumed.** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :44 "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning." An employee self-report and a manager rating in the same week are one witness wearing two names. Pick the second frame after describing the residual in effect terms, or any frame will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).

5. **Sharpen an instrument already returning something.** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The proto-instruments are exit and stay interviews, off-hours decisions, safe reporting channels, promotion and turnover after value conflicts, and items about what a value would require in a concrete case. The vision analogue: a metameric pair matches under one light and mismatches under another, so changing the illuminant is a readymade discriminator. Do not design a new culture score from a specification.

6. **Bracket the round trip.** Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A survey proves that a person can echo the values vocabulary in a habitat built for echoing. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Measure R_T between what the survey predicts and what behaviour shows.

7. **Repeating the same survey is motion, not progress.** Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Progress per handoff needs both bracket fidelity and ground pressure (Fig 7 :37-39). The next instrument should be summoned by the residual the last one surfaced.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that engagement surveys are useless, or that people who agree are lying. They are instruments and useful ones; they are simply not the source.
- Not that a single anonymous item, a stronger culture campaign or a larger sample would settle it. Those change the reading or the pool, not the source discrimination.
- Not a verdict about any named employee, team or manager. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that Kelman's or Allen and Meyer's labels are figure-derived primitives. They are business-pool bindings onto the union's structure, recorded as such; the import adds no atom (Fig 6 :25).
- Not `grounded`. No ground measurement discriminated sources in the making of this answer.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. The corresponding questions here:

- **Injectivity test:** does an instrument map one agreement reading uniquely to one latent source, with published discrimination error that holds across a change of conditions? If yes, F1 breaks and the shape narrows.
- **Push test:** does the self-report remain unchanged under anonymity, administrator and stake manipulations while still predicting a costly choice? If yes, it behaves like an anchor and F2 breaks.
- **Independence test:** does a low-overlap pair (self-report plus unobserved choice, or self-report plus exit record) predict the ground better than either alone? If no independence gain exists, F3 weakens to a budget rule.
- **Round-trip test:** is the gap between survey prediction and behaviour measured, not assumed, and does it shrink when the illuminant changes? Fig 4 :30 requires the measurement.
- **Union test:** does one lever clear all three sources with no categorically different residue? If yes, F5 breaks.
- Standing figure-level falsifiers: derive one of the bond types from the others, or show closure idempotence failing inside a closed set.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The company's value vocabulary is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. What is missing is characterized in effect terms: a source-discriminating instrument that returns something before it is sharpened, with a shadow test against a ground. It is not held here. The answer stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which employees are genuine. It says that a survey reading is a projection, not a source: the same "I live these values" answer can be produced by content that is genuinely held, by a wish for reward, or by a wish to belong, and the reading does not record which bond produced it. So a high score cannot settle the question. What can be offered is the shape any valid method must have: keep the three verdicts separate per person; treat self-report as an instrument, not an anchor; buy a second frame with low overlap and range each against a ground that does not move when pushed; sharpen instruments already returning something, such as unobserved choices and exit records; and measure the round-trip gap between what the survey predicts and what behaviour shows. The framework names no employee as genuine, because it returns a shape, not a name. The center stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists and pays at the ground.
