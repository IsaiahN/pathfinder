# The Tether's answer on financial sustainability and social-mission accountability

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the named instrument, legal form, certification or threshold. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question forces two o′ readings on one model: what the market returns and what beneficiaries' lives return. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed by slot and by time, never blended (Fig 1 :19, :22). The phenomenon to explain is the duration for which both residuals stay small, and the mechanism by which one small residual comes to stand for a large one on the other slot. The question is a union of conditions, not one hard dial (Fig 9 :54-58). It has its own loop and its own ground on each slot.

## 2. The shape

1. **Two slots, two anchors, never one score.** Financial return is settled by the market; mission outcome is settled by an external measurement of a person's life. Fig 2 :29 "A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." names both kinds of anchor. Both must be things that do not move when the firm reports success (Fig 2 :30). A self-authored impact rating is an instrument, not an anchor.
2. **The trade-off is a unit error.** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Money and beneficiary outcome cannot be summed. Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." `⋛` is a threshold relation between two quantities (Fig 12 :87); it can say a slot clears a bar, not convert one bar into the other. Any single blended score has already performed the forbidden sum.
3. **Accountability is model-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39-40: capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, and an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. A mission clause, a certification or a board promise is Γ; its reach must be tested against the ground.
4. **Mission drift is channel failure.** The ground does not decay; the channel does (Fig 10 :24). The named decay modes are stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings (Fig 10 :26). A decade of accountability is a chain of handoffs with two factors, fidelity of the bracket and pressure of the ground; zero either and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39).
5. **Reach must be renewed from a differing closure.** MINT composes inside the closure and never adds an atom (Fig 6 :21). To keep the mission slot honest when the residual changes, the model needs contact with frames that do not draw on its evidence: beneficiaries who are not customers, regulators, independent researchers, affected communities outside the value chain (Fig 6 :23). Contact cascades, so enumeration must go beyond direct stakeholders (Fig 11 :52).
6. **A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one** (Fig 10 :47). A metric becomes a constant the firm authored when it sets pay, certification or public ranking. The low-overlap witness is Goodhart's law: *"When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure."*
7. **A reading cannot name its source without a discriminator.** An impact score is a projection; several generators can produce the same reading. The source essay's metamerism argument says an instrument that cannot distinguish them *"demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What is owed is a better instrument, not a better narrative.
8. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** It cannot say which legal form, certification, metric or threshold occupies the mission slot. That is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single dial, not an optimum on a profit-versus-mission curve, and not a blended "double bottom line" score.
- Not that any certification proves accountability, that shared value resolves the tension, or that a mission lock changes contact.
- Not that the financial ground is the only real ground, and not that the mission ground is whatever the firm says it is.
- Not a derivation of one slot from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No model was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a paired contact test, not a further argument.

- **Mission discriminator:** an outcome measure that changes when the model changes and does not change when only the report changes, with at least two alternative generators of the same reading explicitly separated. This is the spectrometer step of the source essay.
- **Financial contact:** willingness to pay and survival measured per model, not pooled into an industry average (Fig 1 :22).
- **Two-factor audit:** bracket fidelity and ground pressure measured independently at each handoff (Fig 7 :37-39).
- **Habitat substitution:** a documented case in which the habitat changes and both residuals are re-measured. A model that keeps both small is evidence for the shape; one that loses only the mission slot identifies which condition failed.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a non-pooled prospective score that predicts which slot fails next; a self-authored rating shown to be immovable; a commensurable unit between money and outcome; a model that renews reach with no import; a metric that stays a convention after deciding outcomes. All are written before the run in `01` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The candidate atoms here are bindings from a business frame, not nature's own list. The available instruments separate a reading from a source only in a few special cases; elsewhere the alternative generators are not closed. The answer stops where the instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. Nothing here settles whether any particular business model actually achieves both; that is for the two grounds.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "yes" or "no" on a single profit-versus-mission dial, and it does not claim to have measured any firm. It says the question is two conditions with two different grounds: the market for financial return, and an external outcome in beneficiaries' lives for the mission. Those two currencies do not add, so the familiar trade-off framing makes a unit error before it starts. Both residuals can stay small only when contact with each ground is maintained separately, when the mission is measured by something the firm cannot move, when reach is renewed through contact with frames that differ from the firm's own, and when reporting metrics never become the outcome they were meant to describe. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. It does not say which certification, legal form or metric fills the mission slot. That stays open until the ground settles it.
