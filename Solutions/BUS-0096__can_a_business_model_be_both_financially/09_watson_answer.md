## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a business model that is claimed to be both financially sustainable and genuinely accountable to a social mission. Option A: treat it as one dial, a trade-off between financial return and mission, with an optimum where the two balance. Option B: treat it as a union of two conditions with different anchors and instruments, where a small residual on one slot can mask a large one on the other. What each leads to: A sends the search into a blended score that sums two currencies and cannot say which slot failed; B sends the search toward two anchors, a source-discriminating instrument for the mission slot, and separate refuters that can fire independently. The sought-for shape, bound before the run, is a pair of conditions whose conjunction keeps both residuals small plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of financial contact from a failure of mission contact.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a model's library Γ predicts and what two grounds return: the market for financial return and beneficiaries' lives for mission outcome. Arity: four-place, Γ, action a, two o′ readings, indexed by slot and time. Symmetry: not symmetric; customer and beneficiary are not interchangeable even when the same person. Scale: the single model loop, the population with a shared library, and the time channel across years. Fig 1 :19 indexes R per slot; Fig 9 :57 makes the two-condition union categorically different from either condition alone.
- **Domain 1, the business-model loop.** Actors: decision makers, routines, promises, metrics. Currency: residual reduction on both slots. Anchor: realized market return and realized beneficiary outcome. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, the market habitat.** Actors: customers, competitors, entrants, suppliers, regulators. Currency: willingness to pay, share, survival. Anchor: the market, named in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, the mission habitat.** Actors: beneficiaries, communities, workers, regulators, independent auditors, affected non-customers beyond direct contact. Currency: the outcome in people's lives. Anchor: an external outcome measure, the figure's "a patient outcome" (Fig 2 :29), not the firm's own report. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, the reporting channel.** Actors: impact metrics, audits, certifications, disclosures. Currency: contact with the ground versus readings of contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **Excluded, mission as brand narrative.** No anchor independent of the firm's own claim, so not a domain yet (Fig 1 :27).
- **Partition or union.** A union, because D2 and D3 have different anchors and different units; unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-58). D1 and D4 overlap both conditions, so the overlap is a compound with its own loop, not a separate average.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle each first-order slot before composing the union (Fig 12 :104); refuse the blended-score premise before minting a metric (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to treat the two slots as one `⋛` comparison only fit by treating money and outcome as commensurable; Fig 12 :125-130 forbids the sum, so the reading was re-censed into the two-currency finding F3. An early attempt to apply Fig 13's six-term physical substrate to business-model components was retracted as strain S1.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-dial framing does not. Financial return and mission outcome are two slots with two external anchors and two currencies that do not add, so a blended score performs a forbidden sum and hides which slot failed. A business model can keep both residuals small only under conditions that must be maintained: separate contact with each ground, an external mission instrument the firm cannot move, reach renewed through frames whose closures differ, and reporting conventions that never decide the outcome. Drift is channel failure and reach failure, not a fading of intent. Which legal form, certification, metric or threshold occupies the mission slot is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. Mode `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the name. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, records provenance and writes the refuter before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named answer instead of the two-slot shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is the unit error itself: a firm that adopts a blended score because this derivation said "both" has adopted a metric that cannot say which ground failed.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-dial premise is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can replace a sent one. A firm that adopts a bad metric can drop it once a discriminating instrument is available.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating mission instrument that survives reversing the reporting incentive, paired with per-model market measurement, would settle the two-slot shape for a given model. A non-pooled prospective score that predicts which slot fails next, a commensurable unit between money and beneficiary outcome, a model that renews reach with no import from a differing closure, or a metric that remains a non-deciding convention after setting pay, would each break the shape as written. A figure re-census that admits a single currency across the two grounds would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
