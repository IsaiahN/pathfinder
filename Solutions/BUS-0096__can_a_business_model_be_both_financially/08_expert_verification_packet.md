# Expert verification packet: the Tether on BUS-0096

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher or practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the named instrument. Not `grounded`.** No business model was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can a business model be both financially sustainable and genuinely accountable to a social mission?" is a union of two conditions with different anchors, not one dial. Financial return and beneficiary outcome are different grounds in different units, so the "trade-off" framing performs a forbidden sum. A model can keep both residuals small only when it maintains contact with each ground separately, measures the mission slot with an instrument the firm cannot move, renews its reach through frames whose closures differ from its own, and keeps its reporting conventions from deciding the outcome. Which certification, legal form, metric or threshold occupies the mission slot is not returned; that is a declared gap. What is offered is a checkable shape, refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal and methodological; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the question must be unbundled into two slots with two anchors; money and mission outcome do not add, so a blended score is a unit error, not a synthesis.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."*
- **Refuter:** produce a single prospective per-model score that does not pool the slots, predicts which one will fail next, and survives habitat substitution; or demonstrate a commensurable unit in which a marginal euro and a marginal beneficiary outcome are the same quantity for the decision at hand.

### C2: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** the mission anchor must be external. A self-authored impact rating is a reading that moves when the reporting incentive moves, so it cannot be the anchor; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :30 *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* Fig 10 :59 *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
- **What to check:** can the recipient name, for a model they know, an outcome measure that changes when the model changes and does not change when only the report changes? If not, the mission slot has no anchor and the question is not yet answerable for that model.
- **Refuter:** show a self-authored rating that remains immovable when the reporting incentive is reversed, or a metric that stays a non-deciding convention after it has been made the basis of pay or certification.

### C3: figure-internal with a structural prediction; checkable by reading Figures 6, 7, 10 and 11

- **Statement:** mission drift is channel decay and reach failure, not a fading of intent; accountability is model-and-habitat, and a chain of reporting handoffs needs both bracket fidelity and ground pressure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* Fig 7 :37 *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** a model that loses mission accountability while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining; or a model that renews mission reach with no import from a differing closure and still passes an external outcome test across a habitat change.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a single dial, not an optimum on a profit-versus-mission curve, not a blended "double bottom line" score.
- Not that any certification, legal form, constitution clause or impact score proves accountability. These are candidate bindings from the business frame, provenance required, and their contact is untested here.
- Not that shared value resolves the tension. Porter and Kramer's joint-value claim and Schwartz's accountability critique are recorded at different strata in `01` F9; neither is promoted to the verdict.
- Not that the financial ground is the only real ground, and not that the mission ground is whatever the firm says it is.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement of any firm.
- Not a claim that this derivation settles the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument and paired contact measurements, not a further argument.

1. **Mission discriminator:** an outcome measure that changes when the model changes and not when only the report changes, with alternative generators of the same reading separated. This is the spectrometer step in *The Hard Problem is About Source*.
2. **Financial contact:** willingness to pay and survival measured per model, not pooled into an industry average (Fig 1 :22).
3. **Two-factor audit:** bracket fidelity and ground pressure measured independently at each reporting handoff (Fig 7 :37-39).
4. **Habitat substitution:** both residuals re-measured after a documented habitat change.
