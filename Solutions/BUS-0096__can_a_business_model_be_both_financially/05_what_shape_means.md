## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *two low residuals against two external anchors, with an instrument that discriminates a failure of one from a failure of the other.*
- A name: *B Corp certification*, *benefit corporation legal form*, *shared value*, *a particular impact score*.

The difference matters because a name can be adopted without changing contact. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the effect first (two slots, two anchors, a channel that can decay), then look for frames. If instead we start with "B Corp" or "shared value" and map the residual onto them, Fig 9 :27-28 applies: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: an external outcome instrument that changes when the model changes, and a market that keeps paying.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and shadow test. A certification scheme is an import from the measurement-and-governance frame into the business frame. It moves the wall only if it genuinely adds an atom, and its record must show where it came from.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The practical consequence: a questionnaire that lists "mission" as an item has declared a primitive. It has not shown that the mission ground pays.

## 4. Why a reading is not a name: the metamerism rule

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a social-impact score is a reading. Genuine delivery, selective attribution, easy-case selection and plain relabeling can all produce the same score. Fig 9 :30-31 says *"describe the effect rather than the cause."* What is owed is a discriminator, not a better story. The framework returns a shape.

The same essay adds a limit on inference: *"The full set of ways to produce a reading is not known to be closed or determined."* So an unclosed set of alternative generators cannot be dismissed by a single successful audit.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a legal form or certification?

**No, and this is a correction to an earlier reading of mine.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Legal forms, certifications and impact metrics are not primitives; they are candidate bindings from the business and measurement frames onto the mission slot. Naming one is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong is calling such a binding a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall without a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The correct sentence is: *the framework returns the two-slot shape; a specific form, badge or metric is a provenanced binding whose contact must be tested against an external anchor.*

## 6. Why the union reading is a shape claim and not a verdict

The question asks whether the two can coexist. The figures do not answer yes or no about a particular firm. They narrow what any yes or no would have to look like:

- two residuals, indexed separately (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- two anchors, neither of which updates on the firm's report (Fig 2 :29, :30);
- two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125, :130);
- a union that must be unbundled before a lever is chosen (Fig 9 :54, :55);
- a channel that decays even when the ground does not (Fig 10 :24);
- an instrument that discriminates source, not only reading (section 4).

That is a shape. Any particular business model that claims to satisfy it is a name, and its claim is settled only by the two grounds. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* And the operative sentence: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this field the prestige proxy is the well-known certification, the famous founder, the highly cited framework or the award-winning case study. Each can give a model a budget to be tried. None can settle whether beneficiaries' outcomes changed. The outreach packet therefore asks for a refutation of the shape, not an endorsement by a recognised name.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, fetched this session: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And on self-validation: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any business model and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 9. Two corrections kept visible

1. **Forced choice refused.** The question's "both or not?" was initially treated as a single decision. Fig 9 :4-5 and :54-57 require splitting a union first. The answer is a two-condition shape, not a point on a dial.
2. **"Shape, not a name" narrowed.** Initially read as a ban on naming any certification or form. It governs missing primitives (section 5). Naming a held candidate binding is allowed, provided its provenance is recorded and its contact is tested.
