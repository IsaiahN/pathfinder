## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that cultural differences in perception persist when people perform the same task under the same conditions. Option A: answer yes, the differences are in the observer and survive. Option B: answer no, the differences were in the conditions and wash out. What each leads to: A treats an intervention on the immediate setting as if it squared the observer's history, weighting and library, and predicts persistence without measuring the carrier. B treats the controlled setting as if it removed history, and mistakes a harness result for an agent-and-habitat result. The figures return neither A nor B globally. They return a per-slot split of a union, a named carrier, and a refuter.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

**R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the between-frame difference in measured percepts for one task. Arity: two observer frames with one shared stimulus operand. Symmetry: the identity constraints are symmetric (same stimulus, same instruction, same apparatus, `≡`), the comparison of outcomes is ordered (`⋛`), and the perceptual judgment itself is ordered (`→`). The difference therefore lives in the bond or arrangement, not in the atoms. Scale: first order inside each observer, second order at the between-group comparison, third order when the difference becomes an atom in the social frame.

**Domain 1, the perceptual frame.** Actors: observer, object slot `s`, stimulus, library Γ, belief `b`, weighting `w`, action `a`, observation `o'`. Currency: per-slot residual `R_s` and description-length cost. Anchor: a measurement of the percept that does not move because a frame thinks it should. Question type: partition inside one closure (which compositions are legal).

**Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: built environment, language, daily practice, experimenter, apparatus. Currency: contact and introduction, never subtraction. Anchor: the environment, enumerated from the world. Question type: union with D3, because the stimulus carries one habitat into the controlled setting.

**Domain 3, the method and verification frame.** Actors: experimenters, stimuli, instructions, statistics, replications. Currency: independence between frames and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: union with D2.

**Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." One lever, holding conditions fixed, clears the region where only the conditions differ and leaves the intersection standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception before vocabulary before objective before planning before learn and carry. Culture enters at learn and carry and feeds back to perception. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The vocabulary and objective links are the unmeasured ones.

Strain test applied: Fig 13 is declined as a governing line for perceptual content; re-census assigns it to substrate primitives only. See section 9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is a union, not a yes or no. The intervention fixes the action and the immediate habitat but leaves history, weighting and library untouched, so a difference carried in those terms persists (D1), while a difference carried in the conditions or the response measure washes out. The controlled setting is a substituted habitat, so convergence in a harness proves wiring and not absence of the difference (D2). Task equivalence and the anchor are measurements most studies do not make, and between-group agreement is not a witness when the frames share one stimulus pool (D3). The answer must be given per perceptual slot. Over a shared atom set the observers are isomers; the flat formula cannot record the bond. The named culture, the named slot, the direction and the percentage are a declared `gap`. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain and per-slot claims, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a global verdict instead of a split residual. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The global verdict is withdrawn without touching the domain census. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already contain the corrected form. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and no empirical finding about a culture is staked.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A registered multi-site protocol with validated task equivalence finding all per-slot residuals at zero would collapse the union; a source-discriminating instrument showing identical belief, weighting and library across histories would remove the carrier; a re-census showing the weighting and habitat lines do not govern perception would leave the derivation with no figure; evidence that the re-entrant loop has the same sign everywhere would license a general answer. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
