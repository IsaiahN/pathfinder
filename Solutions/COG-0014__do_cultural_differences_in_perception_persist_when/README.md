**Source:** `Questions/cognitive science.txt` line 19

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## The question restated as an effect

Two observers whose histories differ perform task T under instrumentally identical conditions C. One reading of the question predicts that the difference in their percepts washes out, because C is now the same. The other predicts that it persists, because a difference carried in history does not disappear when only the immediate conditions are squared. The effect to be decided is the per-slot residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` for each observer, and the between-frame comparison of those residuals. `b` is the belief formed from private history and inherited prior, `a` is the action the task demands, `o'` is the measured percept.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a statement of which variables in `R_s` the intervention "same task, same conditions" actually touches, which it leaves alone, and therefore in which cases the difference must persist, must vanish, or is undecidable without a ground reading. A single global "yes, they persist" or "no, they wash out" is refused as a falsely bound term. The figures return a shape and a route, never a global empirical verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle an empirical question about any particular culture, stimulus or task.
- No global percentage of how much of perception is cultural.

Three figure-grounded reasons for the exclusions:

1. Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement selected for is not a verdict.
2. Fig 2 :31, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." A list chosen for receptiveness is one evidence pool wearing many names.
3. Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the framework before asking for a verdict on the residual spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.

Legally: public opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that come with unsolicited personal-mail campaigns, as well as the structural failure above.
