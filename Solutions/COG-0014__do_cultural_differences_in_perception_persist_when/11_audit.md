## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before reading the primary's conclusions. WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the between-frame difference in a measured percept under a fixed action and fixed immediate conditions. Arity: two observer frames with one shared stimulus operand. Symmetry: the identity constraints are symmetric (`≡`), the comparison of outcomes is ordered (`⋛`), and the perceptual judgment is ordered (`→`). The difference lives in the bond, not in the atoms. Scale: first order inside one observer, second order at the group comparison, third order at the social framing of the difference.
- **Domain 1, perceptual frame.** Actors: slot `s`, stimulus, Γ, `b`, `w`, `a`, `o'`. Currency: per-slot residual and description length. Anchor: a measurement of the percept. Question type: partition (which compositions are legal inside a closure).
- **Domain 2, habitat.** Actors: environment, language, practice, experimenter, apparatus. Currency: contact and introduction. Anchor: the environment, enumerated. Question type: union with D3.
- **Domain 3, method.** Actors: protocols, stimuli, instructions, statistics, replications. Currency: independence and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: union with D2.

Order by dependency: perception before vocabulary before objective before planning before learn and carry; history re-enters perception, so the order is a cycle, not a chain (Fig 3 :15, Fig 12 :121).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: "same conditions does not touch history or weighting; answer per slot; harness proves wiring; union of conditions and carrier".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a reader might take away, "culture X sees Y differently under the same task, therefore the difference is in the observer".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor split**: shorthand "percept" collapses into both the anchor for "what" and an instrument for "why"; Fig 2 :36 and :37 separate them;
- the **task-equivalence measurement**: shorthand "same task" becomes "same printed instruction"; Fig 3 :7 and :10 require measured vocabulary and objective links;
- the **isomer condition**: shorthand "different histories" becomes a claim about different atoms rather than the same atoms under a different bond; Fig 12 :117 requires the atom set to be fixed;
- the **loop sign**: shorthand "persists" becomes a universal direction; Fig 12 :121 makes the sign a ground question.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: fixing action and conditions does not fix history or weighting, so a history-borne difference is not removed by that intervention. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
