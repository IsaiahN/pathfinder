## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README, opened this session, states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134, "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape, stated:** the answer is a per-slot split of a union, with the carrier named in terms of `b`, `w`, Γ and habitat, the sign of the re-entrant history loop left to measurement, and a source-discriminating instrument owed. The intervention "same conditions" is a substituted habitat, not a removal of history.
- **Not a shape:** "culture X sees Y differently", "30 percent of perception is cultural", "the Müller-Lyer illusion is cultural", "it is universal". Each of those fills a slot the figures leave open.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30, "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30, "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

Applied here: describe R in effect terms before choosing a frame. The residual is "a between-group difference in a measured percept that survives or fails to survive an intervention that fixes the action and the immediate conditions". That description selects the frames (perceptual loop, habitat, method) before any culture is named. Amir and Firestone's re-description of the Müller-Lyer residual, which asked whether the effect appears in animals, other modalities and the blind, is an instance of this order whatever one thinks of their conclusion.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, "culture" is not a seventh primitive. It is a name for a binding onto `b`, `w`, Γ and habitat, all of which are already in the loop's vocabulary. Naming a particular culture as the occupant of a slot is a provenance-bearing binding from the cross-cultural frame, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall.

## 4. Why "the reading does not determine the source" matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same reading is visible in the opened world sources. A measured magnitude of illusion is a projection: the same magnitude can be produced by shared scene statistics, by a learned rectilinear-environment prior, by a response criterion, or by a combination. Naming "culture" from the magnitude alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a larger meta-analysis.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is why the four refuters in `01` and `08` are not decoration.

## 5. The answer to the specific question

**What does "shape" mean here?** It means the following four constraints, and nothing more:

1. **The answer is indexed.** One verdict per perceptual slot and task, never a group mean (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. **The carrier is named in loop terms.** Persistence is expected when the difference lives in `b`, `w` or Γ, which the intervention does not touch (Symbols table :25-26). Washout is expected when it lives in the conditions or the response measure.
3. **The intervention is re-described.** "Same conditions" is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66, :70), and "same task" needs a measured vocabulary and objective link (Fig 3 :7, :10, :27).
4. **The remainder is owed to an instrument.** The sign of the re-entrant history loop (Fig 12 :121) and the source of the percept are ground questions. Until a discriminator runs, the verdict is `candidate`, `specified` on the structural split and `gap` on the global answer.

No figure line returns a named culture, a named stimulus, a universal direction, or a percentage (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any of the terms in the loop. That is an overstatement. The canonical README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. `b`, `w`, Γ and habitat are already held by the loop. Naming them is not invention, it is using the vocabulary. What remains forbidden is naming a seventh primitive, or naming a specific culture or percentage as if the framework derived it.

My metamerism point is **not** retracted. The measured magnitude still does not determine its source, and an instrument that cannot distinguish sources is inadequate for the "why" question. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held terms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
