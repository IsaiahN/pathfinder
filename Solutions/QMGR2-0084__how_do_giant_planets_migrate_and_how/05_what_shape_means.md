## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "migration is an ordered flow over a spendable gradient, the channels form a union, the routes are isomers, and the moved planet substitutes a habitat" is a shape. "Channel X acted in system Y" is a name, and the final orbit does not carry the term that would license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (OPENED): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also warns that a problem may be "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one," which is exactly F1's split of R1 and R2.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (CARRIED): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (OPENED): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (CARRIED): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral.

For this question the names "Type II disk migration", "planet-planet scattering", "Kozai plus tides" and "in situ" are bindings from the astrophysics frame onto held terms (flow, cycle, gradient, substitution). They are legitimate imports with provenance. What is not licensed is promoting any one of them to a figure-derived verdict for a given system.

## 4. Why the route is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (OPENED) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The final architecture of a planetary system is a reading of exactly this kind. Two systems with the same hot Jupiter, same period and same mass can be reached by a smooth disk flow or by scattering plus tidal circularization. The figure text states the structural version: Fig 13 :128-129 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." What is owed is a discriminator, not a name guessed from the projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: what would license the per-system route?

**Answer:** nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular channel is licensed only by a measurement that discriminates among channels with a non-updating anchor, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test that applies to that system. Until one of those, the channel name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the `gap` of F4.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any mechanism. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Disk torque, scattering and tides are bindings onto terms the framework already holds (flow, cycle, gradient, network), not new primitives. Naming them is provenance, not invention. What remains wrong in a naive reading is treating the binding as a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the final orbit is a projection with lossy information about its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held bindings.

## 6. Two consequences for verification and outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." In astrophysics this means citation counts of a migration model, or the fame of its authors, cannot decide whether the shape claim holds. A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (OPENED): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Canonical limit

The figures README says the framework is "not a Theory of Everything" and "a generator of the shape of an answer at any level, applied recursively until the residual stops registering against the ground." It returns "a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32) and stops where "Indiscernibility is not identity." This solution stops at exactly that line: the migration union, the flow shape, the isomer reading and the cascade are stated; the per-system route is left open with a named instrument owed.
