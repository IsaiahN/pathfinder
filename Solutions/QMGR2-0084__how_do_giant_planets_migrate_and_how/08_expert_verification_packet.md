## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How do giant planets migrate" is a union of overlapping conditions, not one mechanism with one name. A giant's radius can change through disk-driven flow, through scattering or secular excitation followed by tidal circularization, or possibly through in-situ formation. These are bindings from the astrophysics frame onto held structural terms, not figure-derived names. The framework contributes a shape: migration is an ordered `→` flow over a spendable gradient, so the sign of the movement matters; alternative routes to the same final orbit are isomers whose difference lives in the record, not in the final artefact; resonance capture is a cycle and a catalyst; and the reshaping is habitat substitution with cascading, residual-relative valence. Which channel acted in a given system is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget rather than as silence. The result is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, with falsifiers written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  - union and direction (checkable by reading Fig 9 and Fig 13, then checking against migration theory)

- **Statement:** the channels form a union, not a partition, and the direction of migration is a distinct term (inward, outward, reversal).
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Fig 12 :70-72 defines `→` as "A then B; B needs A to have happened" with the test "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."
- **What to check:** do the known channels actually overlap in the conditions they require, and is there a real system (the Grand Tack is the cited case) in which the sign of migration reverses? A world anchor is Walsh et al. 2011: the inward migration of Jupiter to 1.5 AU and its subsequent outward migration truncate the planetesimal disk at 1 AU.
- **Refuter:** show that a single mechanism covers all observed cases with no overlap (kills the union), or that migration direction is a parameter and not a distinct term in any structure that matters here (kills the sign claim).

### C2  - isomerism and the route gap (checkable by reading Fig 12 and Fig 13)

- **Statement:** disk-driven and scattering-driven hot Jupiters are isomers over a shared atom set, and the final orbit does not record which route produced it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 13 :128 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 13 :129 "the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- **What to check:** is there an observable that is uniquely produced by one channel and by no other? Obliquity, eccentricity and distant companions are informative, but each channel can mimic them. Dawson and Johnson 2018 report "there is no consensus on their predominant origin channel. Three classes of hot Jupiter creation hypotheses have been proposed: in situ formation, disk migration, and high eccentricity tidal migration."
- **Refuter:** exhibit such a discriminating observable with a non-updating anchor (closes the `gap`), or show that the channels require disjoint atom sets so the isomer claim fails and only the functional-group claim stands (Fig 12 :123).

### C3  - reshaping as substitution and cascade (checkable by reading Fig 11, then checking against Solar System and exoplanet architecture)

- **Statement:** a giant's movement substitutes a habitat and triggers second and third order effects; the valence of the effect is relative to the residual, not carried by the giant.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails."
- **What to check:** does the cited case list actual third-order effects (Mars mass, asteroid belt composition gradation, possible loss of an early super-Earth generation, water delivery), and are the benefit and harm claims indexed to different residuals rather than averaged?
- **Refuter:** show that a giant's migration leaves every other orbital parameter statistically unchanged (kills the cascade), or produce a standing list of migration effects with invariant valence across residuals (kills the valence claim).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a new migration mechanism, and not a universal migration rate, ranking or percentage.
- Not a unique channel for any given system. The union is not collapsed.

- Not that an ecology analogue derives the astrophysics. It is a shadow-only import with a source gate.
