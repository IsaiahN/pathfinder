## The verdict in one line

The figures give a shape, not a name: an institution is regarded as legitimate when it occupies the anchor seat for the question of who has standing to decide, so its standing does not move when a person disagrees with a decision (Fig 2 :29, :33), and when its shared meaning stays a convention rather than being used to decide an outcome (Fig 2 :41, :43). The specific empirical mechanism among the competing accounts in the literature is a `gap` after re-census, not an omission to be papered over.
