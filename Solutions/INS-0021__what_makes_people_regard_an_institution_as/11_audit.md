Symmetry: asymmetric, since a decision residual and an institution's standing do not cancel. Scale: first order at the decision, second order at the standing.
- **Domain 1, person's decision frame.** Actors: person, belief, action, observation. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: the person's own outcomes. Question type: partition of which slot is live.
- **Domain 2, institution's standing.** Actors: institution, procedure, record. Currency: standing and provenance. Anchor: whatever settles who has standing and does not move when a frame pushes. Question type: anchor and convention.

Order by dependency: register the disagreement per slot, anchor the standing, then read agreement with its valence (Fig 3 :19). Refuse self-scoring before minting a mechanism (Formula step 2).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-33, :36-43, Fig 10 :61, :65, :93, Fig 1 :19, :22, :27.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: standing is an anchor that does not update on decision content; legitimacy is a convention; standing survives the outcome; the ground is human judgment.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the world literature claim that fair procedures build legitimacy which produces compliance even with unpopular decisions.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor-not-update rule** is lost when "fair procedure" is silently re-read as "responsiveness to people's views on the decision," which is the opposite move (Fig 2 :30);
- the **convention versus verdict distinction** is lost when legitimacy is used as evidence that a particular decision is correct (Fig 2 :43, Fig 10 :47);
- the **per-slot residual** is lost in aggregate approval statistics (Fig 1 :19, :22).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: standing survives the outcome it was earned on, and provenance is not correctness. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
