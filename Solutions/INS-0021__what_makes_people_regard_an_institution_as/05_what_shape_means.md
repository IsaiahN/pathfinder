## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local rule, `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the form an answer to the legitimacy question must have before the mechanism is known. It constrains: standing lives in an anchor slot that does not update on decision content; legitimacy is a convention, not a verdict; the ground is human judgment because the question is about people. It does not fill: it does not name procedural justice, diffuse support, obligation, or any particular institution. Those are names, and names require a ground settlement or a provenanced import (§3).

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the thing and bootstrap. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the bootstrapped terms were anchor, standing, convention, slot and provenance, all taken from the figures.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was therefore stated as an effect (standing persists despite disagreement), not as a cause (fair procedure causes compliance).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the rule behind `02_outreach_email.md`: describe the failure condition, not the doctrine.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"ride that recursion, steering the selection process until the shape of the answers sinks beneath the union horizon and becomes indistinguishable from the answers themselves."* That convergence is not claimed here. F9 stays open.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question the ground is human judgment (Fig 2 :37), so a mechanism would be named only by a measurement of people's regard that discriminates among mechanisms.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The procedural-justice literature is such a frame, but its studies share survey pools, so it is one witness and cannot name the mechanism by agreement.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."* The figure vocabulary is a visible choice. Calling any one empirical mechanism *the* answer would turn that visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the question's "even when" is a source question, not an argument question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism of the error: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: an observed pattern of compliance despite disagreement is a projection. Fair procedure, diffuse support, normative obligation and content-independent reasons can each produce it. An aggregate approval reading cannot discriminate among them, so naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a preferred name. This is the substantive reason F9 is recorded as `gap`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A mechanism that fits every observation is still not thereby the source.

## 5. Why the standing is not the institution's own score

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* The same essay gives the positive rule: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

An institution's own accrued credibility is prestige in this sense. It can fund a hearing; it cannot settle whether standing exists. This is the essay-level corroboration of Fig 1 :27 and Fig 10 :93 (F5).

## 6. Why agreement is not a witness here

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds that two people who only check against each other *"drift together, and they drift together, so neither notices"*; and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"*.

The legitimacy literature is a room of frames that share survey programs and a theoretical lineage. Its internal agreement is the product of a shared meaning (a convention, legitimate in its own lane), but it cannot corroborate a claim about the world without independent contact. *They're Scanning Books*: *"outputs do not contain their generator."* A consensus of outputs is not a generator of a check.

## 7. Correction to my own §3

I initially drafted §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any empirical mechanism whatsoever, full stop. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. Here the thing missing is not a primitive of the six-term set; it is a causal mechanism in a social domain, which must be **imported** from a world frame. So naming it is not forbidden in principle; it is forbidden **without** a source gate, a shadow test, and a discriminating ground. The claim is about evidence, not about vocabulary. Both the narrower form and the error are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

This correction is triangulated: the canonical README sets the subject of the rule, and Fig 8 :50-52 sets the provenance requirement. `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 records the same move for held atoms.

## 8. Two consequences for this solution

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such.

**The answer must carry its gap.** *Arrival of the Fittest*: the framework *"cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* The honest output is a shape with a written refuter and an open mechanism slot, not a completed theory of legitimacy. Indiscernibility, where it appears, is not identity.
