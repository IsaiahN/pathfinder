## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how much the features shared by modern organisms license about the earliest life. Option A: shared features license a direct reading, so a trait universal today can be named in the first life and a portrait can be asserted. Option B: shared features constrain the shape of what the first life had to be capable of, the descendant-to-ancestor transform is lossy and non-invertible, and the specific slot stays open until an independent ground settles it. A sends a portrait that overstates the evidence and spends the independence of the people checking it. B sends a narrowed shape, a boundary, written refuters and an honest stop. The derivation reaches B, with the reach bounded rather than denied.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** Arity: many extant observations bear on one unobservable target. Symmetry: extant lineages are largely interchangeable as witnesses for a shared trait, so two bacteria reading an inherited genome are one witness repeated, not two instruments. Scale: step (one character), lineage (descent with modification over 4 billion years), root (the deepest reconstructable node, below which lies a different question). The transform between scales is lossy and non-invertible.
- **Domain 1, comparative reconstruction.** Actors: extant genomes, traits, trees, reconciliation models. Currency: probabilistic ancestral-state estimates. Anchor: the true ancestral state, which is unobserved. No direct anchor.
- **Domain 2, the shared-feature pool.** Actors: lineages, genes, ribosomes, the genetic code, horizontal transfer. Currency: inheritance and transfer of traits. Anchor: common descent as the common cause. A nameable anchor exists, but it is one pool.
- **Domain 3, independent ground frames.** Actors: fossils, isotopes, geochemistry, prebiotic experiments, laboratory evolution. Currency: physical traces and reproducible outcomes. Anchor: rock, experiment, measurement. A real ground, weakly connected to the deepest reconstructable node.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, journals, societies. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is the scarce quantity.
- **Partition or union.** A union, not a partition. Domain 1 runs on the pool of Domain 2; Domain 1 and Domain 3 overlap at calibration points where fossils date the molecular clock, forming a compound with its own loop. A single lever (more genomes) clears the region where only Domain 1 binds and leaves the compound standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Establish the transmission mechanism, then reconstruct on a tree, then calibrate against an independent frame, then decide what a verification community counts as a failure. Refuse the common-cause explanation before naming an ancestral state (Formula step 2); describe R in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27); introduce the habitat rather than subtracting modern complexity (Fig 11 :32).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the portrait is held as a candidate and the name is refused. Shared modern features license an inference to the shape of the earliest life (a common ancestral population with heritable molecular machinery, bounded by the mechanisms that produced the sharing) and establish a boundary (the deepest node reconstructable from genomes is not the origin of life). They do not license a unique named state, because the transform is non-invertible and the evidence is one pool rather than independent frames. The specific acetogen portrait is a world-model result held as `specified` and ungrounded; the first life's chemistry is out of reach of this method. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. This is a report of shape and refuters, not a measurement of LUCA.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The answer is mode-labeled, states its three refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a portrait before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The portrait is removable without touching the shape derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive or term has to be withdrawn. The refusal adds a term and removes nothing, so the record stays intact.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An invertibility demonstration (a known ancestor recovered uniquely from its descendants), a demonstration that the lineages acquired the trait independently with no common cause, or a fossil, isotopic or experimental reading that settles a specific ancestral trait excluded by the shape would each break the corresponding claim. A separate instrument that reaches behind the deepest reconstructable node would move the boundary. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`; agreement from more genomes or a selected audience does not fire any of them.
