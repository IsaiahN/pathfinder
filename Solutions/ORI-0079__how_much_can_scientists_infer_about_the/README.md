## What is being decided, in effect terms

Option A: shared features of modern organisms license a direct reading of the earliest life, so that a trait present everywhere today was present in the first life and can be named. Option B: shared features constrain the shape of what the earliest life had to be capable of, but the transform from descendant to ancestor is lossy and non-invertible, so the slot stays open until an independent ground settles it. A sends a named portrait and overstates the evidence; B sends a narrowed shape, a stated refuter, and an honest stop. The derivation below reaches B, with the reach of the inference bounded rather than denied.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." More genomes are a bigger room, not a new anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 "must not hold the interiors" (Seat Map, Seat 4). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public, institutional, and opt-in.
