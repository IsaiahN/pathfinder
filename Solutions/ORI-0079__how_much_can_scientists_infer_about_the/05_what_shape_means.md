## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."

So for ORI-0079 a **shape** is the narrowed form of a claim about the earliest life that can be stated before the ancestral state can be named. "A common ancestral population with heritable molecular machinery, bounded by loss, transfer and convergence" is a shape. "A complex anaerobic acetogen at 4.2 Ga with 19 CRISPR gene families" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* uses proximity: use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, then look for "new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms." With each term gained "you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the known vocabulary is the shared molecular machinery; the new terms are transmission, loss, transfer, calibration.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 says "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the effect: a shared trait constrains what the ancestor could produce, rather than asserting the cause directly.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, when contact is impossible: "the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A refuter written in effect terms is exactly such a description.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" because by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." For LUCA the equivalent stop is the boundary at the deepest reconstructable node, not the origin of life.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." Here that means a fossil, isotopic or experimental reading of an ancestral trait. The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test. Rocks and experiments are such frames; more genomes of the same domains are not.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So using the six-term substrate to talk about LUCA is a visible choice of vocabulary, not nature's own list; importing a fossil frame is the move that adds a dimension.

## 4. Why the portrait is the specific forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a modern shared trait is the reading. "LUCA was an acetogen with genome X" is a source named from that reading. Gene loss, horizontal transfer and convergence are the several paths that can create the same reading, which the 2024 Nature paper and the Quanta article both document. The ancestor's source is underdetermined by the modern projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating frame, not a more confident portrait.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the same in its own thought experiment: "The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it." And it gives the honest stop: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." Also: "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." That is exactly F3's status: unreached, not unknowable.

## 5. Q for this question: does "shape, not a name" forbid saying LUCA had a genetic code?

**No.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. The genetic code, the ribosome and the shared chirality are not missing from modern life; they are observed. Saying the last universal common ancestor had a genetic code is a well-supported inference from universal presence plus vertical transmission, recorded as such by Moody et al. (2024) and by the LUCA reference article.

What remains forbidden is reading the **source** off the **projection**. The specific portrait (acetogen, 4.2 Ga, ~2600 proteins, an early Cas system) is a name assigned to one of several sources that could give the same modern reading. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." So the portrait is held as a `specified` candidate from the phylogenetics frame, provenanced as such, not as a figure derivation. This is the same correction the exemplar recorded in its own §5b, and it is applied here rather than inherited blindly: I re-derived it from the canonical wording.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous labs who agree with the portrait is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a refutation against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle whether the reach of inference is what they say it is.

## 7. What the essays do not give us

No essay supplies the specific shape of LUCA. They supply the rule for how far a shape can travel and where it must stop: *They're Scanning Books* on outputs not being generators ("a pile of human reasoning's outputs is not an engine"), *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* on underivable not being unknowable (Fig 8 :54), and *The Library of Babel* on the search being steered by the question.
