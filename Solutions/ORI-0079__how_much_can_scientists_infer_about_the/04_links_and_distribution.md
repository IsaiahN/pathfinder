## Wider literature bearing on the verdict

Two points in the sources deserve to be separated from the rest, because they are the ones that would move the derivation if reversed.

1. **The common-pool point.** Moody et al. model transfer explicitly because most gene families have been transferred since LUCA. Quanta's Goldman quote says the same thing in plain words. If transfer and loss were negligible, F1's reach would be larger: universality would be a much stronger signal. It is not negligible, so the inference is bounded by mechanism.
2. **The known-ancestor check.** The T7 phage experiment in the ancestral-reconstruction article is the closest thing in the world literature to a ground reading of an inference method. It found a failure of parsimony that a fossilized sample repaired. That is exactly the "independent frame imported" move the figures call outward (Fig 6 :30), and it is the strongest practical argument for spending budget on rocks and experiments rather than more genomes.
