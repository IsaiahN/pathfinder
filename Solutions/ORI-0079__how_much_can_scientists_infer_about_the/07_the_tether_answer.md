# The Tether's answer: how much can be inferred about the earliest life from shared modern features

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive verdict, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement of a fossil, cell, genome or experiment was taken here. By the framework's own rule it cannot settle the question it reports on; it reports the shape of the answer and the refuters.

## 1. The residual it answers

Thousands of living organisms share a small set of molecular features: a genetic code, ribosomes, ATP as energy currency, shared chirality, core metabolism. The tempting inference is that whatever is universal today was present in the earliest life, and that the earliest life can be named from the shared set (a complex, prokaryote-grade, acetogenic, membrane-bounded cell at about 4.2 Ga). The residual is the gap between what is measured now (traits in living organisms) and what is asserted about the target (the ancestral state). Gene loss, horizontal transfer, convergence and the erasure of 4 billion years all act between the two, so "shared now" does not transfer to "present then" without a mechanism.

Option A: shared features license a direct reading, so a universal trait can be named in the first life. Option B: shared features constrain the shape of what the first life had to be capable of, the transform is lossy and non-invertible, and the slot stays open until an independent ground settles it. The derivation reaches B, with the reach of inference bounded rather than denied.

## 2. The shape

1. **A shared feature constrains a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 10 :70 "The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside." The earliest life had to be capable of producing the shared feature under some transmission history. The specific state, date, metabolism and genome size are not delivered. Common ancestry with heritable molecular machinery is supported; the named portrait is not.

2. **The agreement of modern genomes is one pool read many times, not independent corroboration.** Fig 2 :19 "they never talk, and still agree so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other". Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Universality is strong evidence for ancestry when transmission is vertical and the trait is not readily lost or transferred. Its strength is set by that mechanism, not by the number of organisms sharing it.

3. **The deepest reconstructable node is a boundary, not the origin of life.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution." phylogenomics reaches LUCA, the oldest node it can reconstruct, and reaches neither LUCA's contemporaries nor abiogenesis. This is unreached *by this method at this reach*, not a denial that a different instrument could move it (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").

4. **Probabilities are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A posterior of 0.75 for a gene family in LUCA decides which candidates enter the metabolic reconstruction. It is not the ground settling that the gene was there.

5. **An inventory is not an organism.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Even a perfect gene list would not deliver the portrait without the bonds between genes, pathways and regulation, which are exactly what loss and transfer erase.

6. **More of the same genomes refine the estimate; they do not add a new kind of evidence.** Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The legitimate outward move is fossils, isotopes, prebiotic chemistry, or experimental evolution with a known ancestor.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say (non-claims)

- Not that the earliest life was a complex acetogen at 4.2 Ga with ~2600 proteins and an early CRISPR system. That is a candidate model from phylogenetics, held as `specified` and ungrounded, not a figure result.
- Not that saying the ancestor had a genetic code, a ribosome or shared chirality is forbidden. The shape-not-a-name limit governs a *missing* primitive; these ingredients are observed, and their inference to ancestry is licensed under vertical transmission (`05` §5).
- Not that the earliest life is unknowable. It is unreached by shared features at this reach, which is a verdict about the method, not about science.
- Not that the first life's chemistry is reachable from shared modern features alone. That is a `gap` for this method, not for chemistry.
- Not `grounded`, and not a measurement. No part of this answer has been settled by a ground reading.
- Not a claim that more genomes are useless. They improve the estimate inward (Fig 6 :29); they simply do not buy the independence that an outward frame buys.

## 4. What would settle it, and the falsifiers written before the conclusion

A source-discriminating ground reading, not further agreement. Three pre-registered refuters from `01` §4:

1. **Invertibility.** Show that the modern distribution of a shared trait uniquely determines its ancestral state with no multiplicity left under loss, transfer and convergence (for example, a simulated known ancestor recovered uniquely). That breaks the shape limit.
2. **Independence.** Show that the lineages carrying the trait acquired it independently, with no common cause and no transfer, so their agreement is genuine triangulation. That breaks the one-pool claim.
3. **Ground.** Produce a fossil, isotopic or experimental reading that directly settles a specific ancestral trait in a way the shape inference excluded. That moves that trait from `candidate` toward `grounded` and bounds the "unreached" verdict.

A report that the genomes agree, or that a probability is high, fires none of these. If one fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn and the answer rewritten. The cost of being wrong is proportionate to what is at stake in the claim (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."), so the check is worth buying from a different closure.

## 5. The honest stop

The framework can report "unreachable" and characterise what is missing in effect terms. Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." What it can do is narrow: split the question where it lands on a union, describe what is missing in terms that travel to a frame that may hold it, and record the stop so it is checkable. Fig 13 :140 "what could have been concluded, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ." So the answer to "how much" is: a shape and a boundary, the specific portrait held as a `specified` candidate, and the first life's chemistry explicitly out of reach of this method. This is not a silence and not a denial of progress.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a portrait of the earliest life, and it does not say the earliest life is unknowable. It says that the features shared by modern organisms are one evidence pool read many times, so their agreement is strong evidence for common ancestry but not independent corroboration, and that the step from descendant to ancestor is a lossy, non-invertible transform. What shared features license is a shape: an ancestral population with heritable molecular machinery, bounded by loss, transfer and convergence. What they do not license is a name, a date or a specific genome, and they reach only the deepest node reconstructable from genomes, not the origin of life. Probabilities filter the search; they do not settle the verdict. The answer offers that shape, the three written refuters, and the independent frames (rocks, isotopes, experiments with known ancestors) that would move the boundary. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
