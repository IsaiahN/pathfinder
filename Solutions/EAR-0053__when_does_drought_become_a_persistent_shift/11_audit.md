## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the event-versus-shift boundary for a water store at a place. Arity: two, a forcing and a store state, plus a comparison of two return trajectories. Symmetry: broken, since a reversed forcing does not reverse the state, so sequence and comparison are the candidate bonds. Scale: cross-scale, since a fast state couples through a structure to a slow state. Union, not partition: four overlapping drought conditions.
- **Domain 1, detection and measurement.** Actors: gauges, wells, flux towers, satellites, proxies, indices. Currency: readings and variance. Anchor: the measured record at a place. Type: partition inside each slot, union across the four types.
- **Domain 2, land-surface mechanism.** Actors: soil, vegetation, groundwater, evapotranspiration, infiltration, disturbance. Currency: fluxes and storage. Anchor: conservation laws and field experiments. Type: union compound.
- **Domain 3, baseline and reference.** Actors: normals, reference periods, paleo records. Currency: the reference state. Anchor: the measured record, not the chosen normal. Type: imported frame with a shadow test.
- **Domain 4, decision.** Actors: managers, irrigators, ecosystems. Currency: cost and reversibility. Anchor: actual availability plus judgment where people are the subject. Type: comparison.

Order by dependency: baseline, detection, mechanism, decision. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The likely break is the definition of "new state," not the climate model.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 12 :121 (cycle), Fig 12 :112 (comparison), Fig 13 :74 (cross-kind), Fig 2 :29 (anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: read per slot, compare recovery with return interval, require a closed loop, anchor in independent readings, withhold the number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field decision such as "declare a hydrological persistent shift for aquifer X after Y months below threshold Z."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**: a regional or administrative decision reintroduces aggregation and can hide the live slot;
- the **loop-closure test**: a duration rule satisfies the comparison but never tests whether the loop closed, so an open chain can be mislabelled a shift;
- the **independence of anchors**: a practical rule often accepts one index and one agency reading, which is one evidence pool;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**: Y and Z are exactly the number the framework withholds, and writing them into a figure-derived verdict would set R_T to zero dishonestly.

One thing survives cleanly: a state must be compared with a reference at a named place, and persistence needs a closed loop. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for this derivation. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
