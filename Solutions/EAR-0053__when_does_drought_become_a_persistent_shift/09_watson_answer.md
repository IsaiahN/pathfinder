## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the boundary between a temporary drought event and a persistent shift in water availability. Option A: declare persistence when a chosen index crosses a fixed threshold for a fixed duration, making the index the anchor. Option B: declare persistence when the recovery relation at a named slot changes, a feedback has closed across a fast and a slow variable, and independent ground readings agree, making the measurement the anchor and the number a thing owed from the ground. Option A is portable and fragile. Option B is checkable and withholds the number.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least two, a forcing and a store state evaluated across time, plus a comparison of a returning and a non-returning trajectory. Symmetry: broken, since reversing the forcing does not reverse the state (hysteresis); the governing bonds are sequence and comparison. Scale: cross-scale and cross-kind, since fast states couple through structures to slow states. Fig 9 names the bundled form a union, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, detection and measurement.** Actors: gauges, wells, stream gauges, flux towers, satellites, tree-ring proxies, indices SPI, SPEI, PDSI, USDM. Currency: readings of water availability and variance. Anchor: the measured record at a place, calibrated by the paleo record. Type: partition of event versus shift, and a union of four drought types.
- **Domain 2, land-surface and ecohydrological mechanism.** Actors: soil, roots, vegetation, groundwater, evapotranspiration, runoff, infiltration, dust, disturbance. Currency: water and energy fluxes and storage. Anchor: conservation of mass and energy plus field experiments. Type: union compound with interacting conditions.
- **Domain 3, baseline and reference.** Actors: climate normals, reference periods, paleo reconstructions. Currency: the reference state. Anchor: the measured record, not the chosen normal period. Type: a frame with a differing closure, imported with a shadow test.
- **Domain 4, decision and management.** Actors: water managers, irrigators, cities, ecosystems. Currency: cost, reversibility, options. Anchor: actual water availability plus human judgment where the question is about people. Type: comparison of transform versus restore.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Baseline before detection; detection before mechanism; mechanism before management. The likely break is the definition of "new state," the under-instrumented middle link.

Strain test applied: Fig 13's cross-kind rule governs the six primitives; the drought transition is state to structure to state and is legal. Reading Fig 13 as forbidding a fast-to-slow transition was a domain error and was retracted in `01` §6. The live strain is abrupt versus continuous transition type, recorded rather than smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number does not exist yet. Persistence is a per-slot comparison of recovery time with a forcing's return interval, maintained by a closed positive feedback across a fast and a slow variable, and confirmed by independent ground readings. Meteorological, hydrological, ecological and socioeconomic persistence are a union and must be split. The boundary is a comparison and a cycle, not an index value. The NDMC and NIDIS framing of short-term versus long-term drought and the multi-driver hydrology literature support the split, but the verdict comes from the figures. This is `specified` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** high and asymmetric. Calling a persistent shift a temporary event spends the recovery interval waiting for a return that will not come, and land degradation, once established, is permanent work (Fig 11 :63-64). Calling an event a shift triggers unnecessary transformation. The honest cost is bounded because the packet states mode, refuters and per-slot scope, so an error is locatable; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong," which here means buying separate ground anchors, not more agreeing indices.
- **undoable:** the classification is undoable, the physical state may not be. A label can be superseded when recovery curves arrive; a mined aquifer or a crossed vegetation threshold cannot be relabelled back. Fig 11 :61: introduction is available, exclusion is not. This is why the per-slot anchor matters before action.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Recovery curves showing return within the forcing interval at every slot; a documented persistent state whose persistence survives removal of the candidate feedback; a single rule classifying all four drought types and their intersections; or a figure re-census that derives a universal duration from the verbatim lines. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
