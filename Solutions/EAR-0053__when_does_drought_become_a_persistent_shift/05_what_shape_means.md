## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "a shape, not a name." Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, the shape is the three-test boundary: per-slot, ground-anchored, comparison-based and cycle-maintained. "SPEI below minus two for twenty-four months" is a name. The shape constrains any acceptable name without supplying one.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- The residual was described in effect terms first (failure to recover), then the vocabulary was borrowed: recovery time, return interval, feedback closure, ground anchor. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*
- *Library of Babel* also names the bundle failure directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is finding F6 in `01`.
- Stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the stop is not an answer but a well-formed shape whose threshold is visibly owed elsewhere.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A duration becomes `grounded` when recovery curves and storage records at named slots pay for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. Bringing "alternative stable states" from ecology into drought hydrology is an import of that kind, and the source must be recorded (Scheffer 2001, Rietkerk 2004 in `10_source_ledger.csv`).

*They're Scanning Books* is listed in the canonical set but was not re-fetched, so no line from it is quoted here.

## 4. The metamerism error in drought classification

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it was opened this session: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." A green reading can come from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."

The drought analogue is exact. A single index reading of "severe drought" can come from a rainfall deficit that will reverse, a groundwater store that has been mined, a vegetation state that has crossed a fire or beetle threshold, or a demand increase that has changed what "available" means. The index cannot comment on which. Naming the source (persistent shift) from the reading (severe index) is the metamerism error. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The essay's resolution was instrumental, not rhetorical: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the better instrument is the independent set of ground readings in test 3, not a new index definition.

## 5. Q: what licenses a numeric threshold? ANSWERED

Nothing in the framework. The comparison bond and the cycle relation are already held atoms, so the three-test shape is a legal composition, not an invented primitive. What is missing is the numeric boundary, and *The Library of Babel* says what that means: "If you don't have the answer to it's because you are lacking certain things in the library (instruments, information) that would help, or perhaps you are asking the wrong question altogether."

So the honest form is: the shape is available now at `specified`; the number is unreached pending ground readings. It is not unreachable in principle. Fig 8 :40-43 keeps those two facts apart.

**Correction to my own earlier framing.** I initially wrote as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any specific quantity in this answer. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Recovery time, return interval, threshold comparison and cycle closure are all already in the library, so naming them is composition, not the forbidden act. What remains forbidden is promoting a number to `grounded` without a measurement. Both statements are kept so neither is silently lost, matching the exemplar's own §5b correction.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of distinguished drought scientists selected for likely agreement with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets gauges, wells, flux towers and proxies settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"; "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle EAR-0053.
