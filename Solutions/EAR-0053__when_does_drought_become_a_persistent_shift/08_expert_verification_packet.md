# Expert verification packet: the Tether on drought persistence

**What this is:** a claim in a form a drought, hydrology or land-degradation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the limit. Not `grounded`.** No measurement here settles a universal threshold. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A drought becomes a persistent shift, as opposed to a temporary event, when three tests hold at a named place. First, recovery time from a perturbation exceeds the return interval of the forcing at that slot. Second, the dry state is maintained by a closed positive feedback crossing a fast and a slow variable, where a temporary event is only an open chain with negative return. Third, independent ground readings, meaning streamflow, storage, vegetation and past-drought context, agree that the state has held beyond the forcing. The boundary is a comparison and a cycle, not a named index value. The numeric threshold is not supplied by the framework and is owed from measurement.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Framework-internal and observational; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 2

- **Statement:** persistence must be assessed per slot and anchored in measurements, not in a regional index or in agreement among indices.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
- **Refuter:** an aggregated index that classifies local persistence perfectly at every slot with no live residual, or two indices on disjoint evidence whose agreement tracks the ground and whose disagreement tracks known failures.

### C2: Mechanism-facing; checkable against watershed and vegetation evidence

- **Statement:** a temporary event is an open chain with negative return; a persistent shift requires a closed positive loop across a fast and a slow variable. The two are isomers over a nearly shared atom set (Fig 12 :117), and the extra relation is the cycle.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 11 :52-53 *"contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** in a catchment you know, does the persistent dry state survive removal of the candidate feedback? Is the relevant variable three steps out, for example groundwater recharge affected by vegetation loss rather than rainfall alone?
- **Refuter:** a documented persistent dry state with no closed positive feedback where removing the loop leaves persistence unchanged; or event and shift separable by atom set alone without reference to loop sign.

### C3: Definition-facing; checkable against the recovery record

- **Statement:** the boundary is a comparison of recovery time with the forcing's return interval, and a sequence of states, not a state predicate. Describing the failure to recover must precede choosing an index or a baseline.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Operators table :51 *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"* Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
- **What to check:** does a recovery curve, rather than an index crossing, actually discriminate the cases in your record? Does changing the reference period change the classification while the ground readings do not change?
- **Refuter:** a state predicate with no comparative or temporal operator that discriminates all known event and shift cases.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal duration or index threshold. The framework returns a shape, not a number.
- Not that every persistent shift is abrupt; abrupt and smooth transitions both satisfy the comparison.
- Not that the four drought types are one problem; they are a union and must be split.
- Not that index consensus is evidence; these indices share inputs.
- Not that climate models alone settle the question; the under-instrumented link is the definition of "new state."
- Not a derivation of any number from the figures, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. As *The Hard Problem is About Source* puts it, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." Here a severe index reading cannot say whether the source is a rainfall deficit, a mined aquifer, a vegetation threshold or a demand shift. Needed:

1. recovery curves at named slots, including critical slowing down (van Nes and Scheffer 2007);
2. a loop-closure test, such as a watershed or manipulation study that removes the candidate feedback;
3. storage and regeneration status of the buffer (Fig 12 :119, catalyst);
4. agreement across streamflow, well levels and paleo context (Cook et al. 2015);
5. any of the five pre-registered falsifiers in `01` §4.
