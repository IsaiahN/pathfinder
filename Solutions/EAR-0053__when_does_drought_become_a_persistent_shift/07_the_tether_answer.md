# The Tether's answer: when does drought become a persistent shift?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the cross-kind composition rule. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a water store at a place, when does a drying excursion stop being a transient deviation and become a regime that does not return? A fixed index threshold answers cheaply but makes the index the anchor. The figures require the measurement to be the anchor and return a shape with three tests, leaving the number to be measured.

## 2. The shape

1. **Read per slot, never as an average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A regional mean can be near zero while one aquifer is in a live shift. Drought is split into meteorological, hydrological, ecological and socioeconomic conditions before search (Fig 9 :4-5), because they overlap as a union rather than a partition.
2. **The boundary is a comparison plus a sequence, not a state name.** Fig 12 :111-112: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Compare recovery time with the return interval of the forcing, and order the states so the second depends on the first. Describe the failure to recover before choosing an index or a baseline (Fig 9 :27-28).
3. **The mechanism is a closed loop, not a chain.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* A temporary event is an open chain with negative return. A persistent shift has a positive loop across a fast and a slow variable. The chain of contact runs three steps out (Fig 11 :52-53), so scoping to rainfall misses the store that matters.
4. **The transition crosses kinds and scales.** Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* A fast state couples through a structure to a slow state. A precipitation index refined forever fails by refining an axis that cannot carry the result (Fig 6 :29).
5. **The verdict is anchored in measurement, not agreement among indices.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Independent readings of streamflow, storage, vegetation and paleo-context must agree. Indices that share precipitation inputs are one evidence pool, not two witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
6. **Management follows the verdict, and is asymmetric.** Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Once the new attractor exists, the response is introduction and permanent work, not waiting for a return (Fig 11 :63-64).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal duration, not "twenty-four months," not a named index value as a boundary. The framework returns a shape, not a number.
- Not that every persistent shift is abrupt. The comparison and cycle rules cover abrupt and smooth transitions; which occurs remains open (Deb et al. 2024).
- Not that a consensus of drought indices is evidence. They largely share inputs.
- Not that "drought" is one question. It is a union of at least four conditions and must be unbundled.
- Not that climate models alone will settle it. The bottleneck is the definition link, below which measurement effort must land.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles any slot.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating set of readings, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." Here the question is whether "severe drought" came from a rainfall deficit, a mined store, a vegetation threshold or a demand shift. The discriminators are:

1. **Recovery curves at named slots.** Does recovery time exceed the forcing's return interval, and is recovery slowing (van Nes and Scheffer 2007, critical slowing down)?
2. **Loop-closure tests.** Does removing the candidate feedback remove the persistence? A watershed or vegetation manipulation, not an argument.
3. **Storage and catalyst status.** Is the groundwater buffer spent and regenerating, or mined (Fig 12 :119)?
4. **Independent anchors.** Streamflow, well levels, flux towers, tree-ring megadrought context (Cook et al. 2015) must agree before the label is used.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4.** Any of the five fires and the shape is withdrawn.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set gives the tests but no number. The number is owed from instruments that do not yet exist everywhere at the needed resolution, and a search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*). Another sensorium's drought categories need not be these four. The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a universal month count for when drought becomes persistent. It gives the shape any such boundary must have. Read each catchment, aquifer or stand separately, because an average hides the live signal. State the boundary as a comparison of recovery time with the forcing's return interval, not as a named index value. Require a closed positive feedback across a fast and a slow variable, since a linear chain cannot close and therefore cannot produce persistence. Anchor the verdict in independent readings of streamflow, storage, vegetation and past drought, not in agreement among indices that share the same rainfall input. Then, and only then, treat the state as a new regime and manage it as permanent work, because you can add water or vegetation but you cannot subtract the climate and land use that are already there. What is withheld is the number. It is owed from measurement, and a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument, is what would settle it.
