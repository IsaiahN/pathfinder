## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not settle anything.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* An audience selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **Independence is the witness, not the headcount.** Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
3. **Do not hand over the interiors before the verdict.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: harvested personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
