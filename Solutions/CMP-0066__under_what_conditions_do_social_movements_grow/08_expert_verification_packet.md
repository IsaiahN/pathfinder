# Expert verification packet - the Tether on local recruitment vs structural shifts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a social movement or collective dynamics researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the domain conditions, not `grounded`.** No dataset is claimed to have been opened for this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result about a named movement.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The growth question is two conditions in a union, not one dial. Local recruitment is composition inside a movement's existing closure and adds no atom; a structural shift is the operator that can add an atom or change contact, and so moves the wall. Which binds is decided by whether the atoms growth requires are already reachable through the movement's tie arrangement. Arrangement, not tie count, is what carries recruitment. A stalled drive is a question, not a verdict, because a route that is in the closure and unfound and a route that is absent look the same from inside. Seeds are head starts whose fate is decided by conditions they did not author. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8
- **Statement:** local recruitment composes inside a closure and cannot add an atom; import is the only operator that can move the wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 - *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*. Fig 6 :14 - *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **Refuter:** a documented growth in which every required atom (new participant class, new resource, new institution, new framing) was absent from the closure and no import or habitat change occurred; or a condition change adding no atom and changing no contact that reliably produces the spurt in independent data.

### C2 - framework-internal with an empirical test; checkable by reading Figure 12 and by inspecting network data
- **Statement:** tie arrangement, not tie count, decides which local compositions are reachable. Same members under different bonds are isomers; movements sharing only a core substructure are a functional group.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 13 :24 - *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*.
- **What to check:** hold member set and tie count constant and vary clustering or bridging. Does recruitment differ? If it does not, the isomer claim is idle for that quantity.
- **Refuter:** statistically indistinguishable recruitment trajectories across networks with identical tie counts but different clustering or bridging, across the full measured range.

### C3 - method claim; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 6
- **Statement:** this is a union compound, so it must be unbundled before search; and a stalled local drive cannot be diagnosed from inside as depth versus absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 6 :27 - *"from inside the frame the two look the same"*. Fig 8 :39 - *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** one measured variable predicting both branches and the intersection with no interaction term and no change of anchor; or an instrument that discriminates depth from absence for the same movement without consulting a second frame.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Closure test:** enumerate actors, conditions and relations in contact with the residual, outward until the cascade stops mattering. Was the required atom already in the closure?
2. **Bond test:** same members, same tie count, different arrangement, different trajectory? This is the Fig 12 test.
3. **Shift test:** did a condition change add an atom or change contact, measured independently of the movement's own reports?
4. **Ground:** a before/after record with an anchor neither frame can move. Agreement between studies sharing the same cases and reviewers is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).
