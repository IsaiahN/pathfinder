## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the case

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a shape constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *growth binds on whichever of two reachability relations fails first: the needed atoms are already in the closure but unfound, or they are not in the closure at all.*
- A name: *the 1960s civil rights movement grew because of X tipping point on tie strength Y.*

The shape transfers across movements; the name belongs to a ground reading of one movement, which was not taken here.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then recurse on recurring new terms. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *The Library of Babel* also names the exact failure this question invites: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is F1 in `01`. The question as posed is two conditions in a union, not one dial.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* And: *"the history itself can't be sent, but a description of effects can: what you would see, what would happen."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the shape is written in terms of atoms, closure, contact and bonds, not in framework jargon sent to a sociologist.

## 3. What licenses a name for a specific movement

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the green reading.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Here that would be a dataset or field instrument that genuinely predicts which condition binds for a named movement.

From *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The atoms we enumerate for a movement are a visible choice, not nature's list.

## 4. Why "arrangement" is part of the shape, not a detail

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* reports the general result that in human groups *"the arrangement mattered more than the members, which is Figure 12's 'The arrangement is the substance.'"* It also warns that *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22), and recommends *"Use the success rate per try, not the total."*

For this question: a count of organisers or ties is the flat formula. Clustering, bridging and sequence are the bonds (Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*). Two movements with the same member list under a different bond are isomers, not variants: Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

## 5. Why the stalled-drive case cannot be decided from inside

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Applied here: a plateau in recruitment is a reading. It is consistent with a route that is in the closure but unfound, and with a route that requires an import. The reading does not select between them.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the deeper limit: *"indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* And from the same essay, the practical instruction: *"Traversing outward requires selection; traversing inward requires elimination."*

So the honest shape includes a stop: we can say what a resolution would have to look like (a second frame or a new instrument), not which condition actually binds for a movement we have not measured.

## 6. Why this whole document is a simulation, not a test

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any claim about real movements. From *Arrival of the Fittest*, the same caution: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* The pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4 is the design instrument that keeps this honest.

## 7. Prestige is not action (governs the outreach, not the verdict)

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of well-cited movement scholars who agree with the shape is a prestige loop, not a check. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the output against their own data, in one-to-one contact, with the refuter in front of the links.

## 8. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote that naming a condition for a named movement is "forbidden" by the shape-not-a-name rule. That overstates it. The figures README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in Γ. A participant class or institution is not a framework primitive; it is an atom in a specific movement's closure, which is a different object. The correct statement is narrower: naming which condition binds for a specific movement requires a ground reading or a provenanced import, which this run does not have. The ban is on inventing the resolution from inside the frame, not on ever naming a condition. Both the earlier and the corrected readings are recorded here so the change is visible.
