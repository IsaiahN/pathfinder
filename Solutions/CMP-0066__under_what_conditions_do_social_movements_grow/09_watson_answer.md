## VERDICT (from the figures)

Local recruitment and structural shifts are not two strengths on one dial. Local recruitment composes inside a closure and adds no atom; a structural shift is an import or habitat substitution that can add an atom or change contact, and so moves the wall. Tie arrangement, not tie count, decides which local compositions are reachable. Seeds are head starts whose fate is decided by conditions they did not author. A stalled drive is a question, not a verdict, because depth and absence are indiscernible from inside one frame. Which condition binds for a named movement stays `candidate` until a ground settles it. This is a `general` shape with `specified` domain conditions, not a `grounded` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the refuters are sent before the theory. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by handing them the framework before they have recorded a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A second cost is a movement funder applying the wrong lever: seeding a network whose required atoms are absent, or chasing a structural change when the route is merely unfound.
- **undoable:** yes. No intervention is authorized by this derivation, no atom is imported, and no primitive is withdrawn. The four public refuters in `01` allow the shape to be retracted at a named finding without touching the other findings. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A closure test showing the required atom was present and reachable (or absent) for a named movement; a bond test holding tie count constant and showing indistinguishable trajectories; a before/after shift measurement that adds no atom and changes no contact yet precedes growth; or an instrument that discriminates depth from absence without a second frame. Prestige, citation count or number of agreeing scholars is not such an instrument.
