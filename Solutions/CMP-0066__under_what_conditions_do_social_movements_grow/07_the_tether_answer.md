# The Tether's answer: local recruitment versus structural shifts

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the conditions, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result about any named movement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a defined movement and period, observed growth is not predicted by one mechanism. Two descriptions compete: growth as composition inside the network and habitat the movement already has, and growth as dependent on something the local closure does not hold. These form a union compound, not one hard question: `Fig 9 :57`: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Verdicts stay per movement.

## 2. The shape

1. **Local recruitment is composition inside a closure, and it adds no atom.** `Fig 6 :21`: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* `Fig 8 :32`: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*. So local recruitment can reach every arrangement the movement can already compose; it cannot create a new participant class, resource or institution. When the required atoms are already in the closure, the lever is seeds and the arrangement of ties.

2. **A structural shift is the operator that can add an atom or change contact.** `Fig 6 :14`: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. `Fig 11 :40`: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* `Fig 11 :39`: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* It is not a bigger cascade. It is a different operation, with a different anchor (before/after growth measured independently of the movement's own reports).

3. **Arrangement, not tie count, decides which local compositions are reachable.** `Fig 12 :111`: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* `Fig 12 :117`: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Same members under different bonds are isomers, not variants. `Fig 13 :24`: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"* names the network as a structure, not a count.

4. **Seeds are head starts, and conditions decide what grows.** `Fig 4 :34-35`: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* `Fig 4 :22`: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Replaying a past winning tactic is a recording, not a generator.

5. **A stalled drive is a question, not a verdict.** `Fig 6 :27`: *"from inside the frame the two look the same"*. The needed route may be in the closure and unfound (SEARCHED, then INWARD), or absent (OUTWARD, import). `Fig 8 :39`: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

6. **No new tactics need not mean weak recruitment.** `Fig 5 :41`: *"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."*

7. **The research frame must range against the ground, not against agreement.** `Fig 2 :35`: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* `Fig 9 :60`: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one threshold, not a local/structural dial. Two loops plus an overlap, each with its own ground.
- Not that any named movement grew by one route. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that "structural shift" means "large local cascade". It adds an atom or changes contact.
- Not that tie count is irrelevant. It is a flat description; the bond is the missing half (Fig 12 :5).
- Not that a plateau proves local recruitment failed. Depth and absence are indiscernible from inside.
- Not that the framework names the condition for a specific movement. It returns a kind of thing, never the answer (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` for any movement here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a before/after record, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Closure test:** enumerate actors, conditions and relations in contact with the residual, then outward until the cascade stops mattering (`Fig 11 :44`: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*). Was the atom growth required in the closure at the start?
2. **Bond test:** do the same members under different tie arrangements produce different trajectories? A test of Fig 12's isomer claim (F5), with tie count held constant.
3. **Shift test:** did a condition change add an atom or change contact, measured independently of the movement's own reports? A change that does neither cannot be the cause under F3.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** a single-variable predictor with no interaction; local composition adding an atom; a no-atom no-contact shift producing the spurt; identical recruitment across arrangements.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single tipping point for social movements. It says the growth question is two conditions in a union, not one dial. Local recruitment is composition inside a movement's existing closure, so it can reach every arrangement it can already compose but cannot add a new participant class, resource or institution. A structural shift is the different operation that can add such an atom or change what contact means, so it moves the wall. Which one binds for a given movement depends on whether the things growth requires are already reachable through its tie arrangement, and arrangement, not tie count, is what carries recruitment. A stalled drive is therefore a question, not a verdict: the route may be in the closure and unfound, or absent, and from inside the movement those look the same. Seeds are head starts whose fate is decided by conditions they did not author. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The case stays open until a ground settles it.
