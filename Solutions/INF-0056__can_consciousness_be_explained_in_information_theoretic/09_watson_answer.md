## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the question is a union and must be unbundled. On reachability, the answer is `unreached from inside`: an information-theoretic closure cannot certify its own adequacy, and what the framework returns is a shape (per-slot, cross-level, directed term, source-discriminating instrument), not the name of a measure; Φ is a binding from the neuroscience frame with provenance owed, not a figure result. On discrimination, a valid observation must be an anchor whose reading does not move with the interpreter, with predictions before data, theory-neutral acquisition and a result that can fail both leading accounts; no-report readouts, perturbational complexity and adversarial comparisons are proto-instruments, not verdicts. The "what is consciousness" form is replaced by a contact question. The specific measure that is correct is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Verdicts are kept separate per domain, never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before its conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a theory before the discriminating output, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 amendments). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded with provenance (seat map: a ruling carries provenance like an atom), so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a declared information-theoretic closure that derives the phenomenon from its own primitives or proves its absence under a declared code; one observation that collapses the union with a single criterion; a figure derivation that uniquely names the measure; a discriminator whose outcome is invariant under theory-neutral acquisition and that passes for one account and fails for the others; or an instrument that discriminates among the sources that currently produce the same reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
