## 0. The claim under audit, restated as an effect

The question as written bundles two different asks:

> (a) Can consciousness be explained in information-theoretic terms?
> (b) What observations would discriminate competing accounts?

Stated in effect terms, **R** is the gap between what a subject can report or a instrument can read on a given phenomenon-slot and what a candidate account predicts for that same slot. R is indexed per slot, never averaged into one "hard problem":

| slot | what fails to resolve | competing predictions |
|---|---|---|
| level | why awake differs from dreamless sleep, sedation, coma | integrated information (Φ) versus global broadcasting/ignition versus recurrent or higher-order accounts |
| content | why this experience has this quality, and why content decodes here rather than there | posterior "hot zone" versus prefrontal workspace |
| access/report | what a verbal report adds beyond the experience | no-report paradigms claim report inflates the neural correlate |
| substrate | which physical arrangement carries the phenomenon | causal structure versus long-range ignition versus quantum proposals |

An answer that would close R would have to do two things: say what shape any adequate account must have, and name at least one observation on which two accounts predict different readings for the same slot. The first is a **reachability** question. The second is an **anchor** question. They are not the same question, and the census below shows why they must not be searched together.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: at least three-place (system state, observed reading, account prediction), with multiple slots as tabulated above. Symmetry: many information measures are symmetric under relabeling of states (Shannon entropy H, mutual information MI); integrated information is defined through directed effective information, EI(A→B), which Tononi states "in general are not symmetric." Symmetry is therefore itself a discriminator between candidate measures, not a decorative property. Scale: micro (cells, synapses), meso (circuits), macro (whole-brain signals and subject report), with a lossy membrane between them (Fig 4).

One line per candidate domain, with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| id | domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | first-person / phenomenon | experiences, subjects | what-it-is-like, discriminable report | a reading that does not move with the theory: no-report neural readout, patient outcome, measured response | admitted, anchor contested |
| D2 | information-theoretic formalism | H, MI, EI, Φ, compression | bits, description length | the measurements the formalism predicts (for example a PCI value); the formalism itself is a frame, not a ground | admitted |
| D3 | neural implementation and instrumentation | neurons, circuits, TMS, EEG/MEG/fMRI/iEEG | neural signals, complexity indices | the instrument reading | admitted |
| D4 | adjudication / verification | rival proponents, theory-neutral teams, preregistration | confirmed or refuted predictions | the ground (experiment), never agreement | admitted |
| X1 | "the hard problem" as one monolithic explanatory target | none nameable beyond the phrase | none | none nameable | not a domain yet, must be split |
| X2 | "information" as an observer-relative semantic quantity | interpreter plus signal | meaning | depends on D1's anchor | absorbed into D1/D2, not a separate ground |
| X3 | "consciousness as a free-floating property" | the property alone | none | none | refused, Fig 11 disallows agent-only explanations |

**Partition or union.** This is a **union**, not a partition. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The two asks (a) and (b) overlap but are categorically different: one concerns what composes inside a closure, the other concerns which external reading settles a disagreement.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (characterize R per slot) → vocabulary (bind what "information-theoretic" means: Shannon coding, intrinsic/integrated information, compression) → objective (state what counts as an explanation and what counts as a discriminator) → planning (choose an instrument) → learn and carry (record the lineage). ## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - The question is a union and must be unbundled (decisive for method, `general`)

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

"Can it be explained" asks what is reachable from a closure. "What observations would discriminate" asks what external reading settles rival frames. Searching them as one question lets a theory's explanatory success stand in for its testability. The world literature shows the same split: Seth and Bayne report that "it is unclear how current theories relate to each other, or whether they can be empirically distinguished," and the construct-first literature complains of "dozens of theories and no noncontentious way of deciding between them." Mode `general` for the union rule, `specified` for its application here. Status **triangulated** (Fig 9 union rule plus Fig 3 dependency order, low overlap: one governs problem shape, one governs chain order). Refuter: a single observation that simultaneously establishes adequacy and discriminates all rival accounts, with the same criterion, no unbundling required.

### F2 - Reachability cannot be settled from inside one information-theoretic closure (`general` structural, `specified` application)

Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

So the honest answer to (a), stated in the framework's terms, is not "yes" and not "no". It is: from inside a fixed information-theoretic vocabulary you can enumerate arrangements without end, but you cannot derive whether the phenomenon is in the reach without a term that was not in the primitives. A search that fails does not prove absence, and a search that succeeds only shows what that closure already held. The specific candidate Φ is an import/binding from the neural frame, not a composition the framework derives. Status **triangulated** (Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 13, different roles: wall, idempotence, set-versus-arrangements). Refuter: a derivation of consciousness inside a declared information-theoretic closure from its own primitives with no new atom, or a proof that no such composition exists under a declared code. Either would close the reachability question from inside.

### F3 - The framework returns a shape, not the name of a measure (`general`)

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :133 "what it returns is a shape rather than a name"

The canonical figures README states the same limit in its own words: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Tononi's 2004 paper proposes a specific name: "According to the theory, consciousness corresponds to the capacity of a system to integrate information." That is a candidate binding from the neuroscience frame. It is not licensed by a figure line, and under Fig 8 :50 convergent derivation and adopted import "look identical in the contents" and are separated only by the record. So the deliverable on part (a) is the shape: any adequate account must be a cross-level, per-slot composition with a directed/intrinsic term and a discriminator. Which measure occupies the term stays open. Status **triangulated** (Fig 9 plus Fig 13 plus canonical README, low overlap between figure limit and usage rule). Refuter: a figure-grounded derivation that uniquely selects one information measure as the substrate term without an import, or a demonstration that the shape constraint is empty (that every candidate measure satisfies it trivially).

### F4 - A discriminator must be an anchor, and agreement is not evidence (`general`)

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

An observation discriminates only if its reading is invariant under who interprets it. A result whose significance depends on whether a proponent of IIT or of GNW analyses it fails this test. The field's own response is instructive: the preregistered adversarial protocol puts "six theory-impartial laboratories" behind three instruments, and the 2025 result reports findings that "align with some predictions of IIT and GNWT, while substantially challenging key tenets of both theories." That is the shape of a discriminator: theory-neutral acquisition, predictions written before data, and a result that can fail both sides. Status **triangulated** (Fig 2 anchor rule plus Fig 7 room-chain rule, plus Fig 10 non-access). Refuter: a proposed discriminator whose outcome tracks the theoretical commitment of the team, or which is validated solely by agreement within one evidence pool.

### F5 - The discriminator must be a source-discriminating instrument, not a finer report (`specified`)

Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 6 :35 "the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

The world side supplies three working proto-instruments, each already returning something that fails to resolve: (1) no-report paradigms, where Tsuchiya et al. write that "the requirement of reports has biased the search for the neural correlates of consciousness over the past decades"; (2) the perturbational complexity index, where Casali et al. report that "PCI reliably discriminated the level of consciousness in single individuals during wakefulness, sleep, and anesthesia"; (3) preregistered adversarial comparisons with theory-neutral labs. These are not proofs of a theory. They are instruments improved from worse ones, and they are the only honest route from the residual to a ground. Status **triangulated** for the method shape (Fig 6 plus Fig 3 plus three low-overlap world fields: methodology, clinical measurement, human cognition). Refuter: a claimed discriminator that is only a refinement of verbal report with no independent readout anywhere at any resolution, in which case Fig 6 :35 says the search has no edge to extend from and the claim must be withdrawn as `unreachable-at-this-budget`.

### F6 - Symmetry is a structural discriminator between candidate information measures (`specified`)

Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

Fig 12's symmetric/ordered bond tests separate operators by whether swapping operands changes the term. The same test, applied to information measures, separates candidates that "contain the same ingredients": Shannon MI is symmetric in its operands; Tononi's effective information is explicitly asymmetric, since "EI(A→B) and EI(B→A) in general are not symmetric." A theory claiming an intrinsic, directed perspective cannot be built from a symmetric measure without losing the bond that carries the claim. This is structural, not numerological. Status **single-source** for the figure test applied to measures (Fig 12 only); the measure mathematics is world-sourced and not figure-derived. Refuter: an information measure that is symmetric under the relevant relabeling yet still supports the intrinsic/extrinsic distinction the theory claims, or a demonstration that Fig 12's symmetry test does not transfer to measures at all (in which case this finding is withdrawn and the census re-run).

### F7 - Settling is necessary but not sufficient; fitting all data does not make a theory discriminating (`specified`)

Fig 5 :41 "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."

THE_FORMULA.md step 5 adds "SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground," and Fig 5's amendment states "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient." A theory that accommodates every observation by adjusting auxiliary assumptions has a growing library, not a growing reach. The world record supplies the critique: Searle argues that "we cannot use information theory to explain consciousness because the information in question is only information relative to a consciousness," and Krohn and Ostwald conclude that "integrated information does not appear to be sufficient for consciousness." The 2025 adversarial result challenges "key tenets of both theories," which is what a real ground does. Status **triangulated** (Fig 5 plus THE_FORMULA plus Fig 6 :42-43 on record versus reach). Refuter: a theory for which successful settlement of current data also guarantees composability and unique discriminating power, with no auxiliary adjustments available.

### F8 - "What is consciousness?" is the wrong question form (`general`)

Fig 11 :34 "What is intelligence?"
Fig 11 :35 "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."
Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

The figure's move transfers directly, with the anchor re-named per Fig 4 :20 ("Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"). Asking what consciousness is invites a substrate noun and a final room. Asking which contact conditions make a system support a discriminable first-person reading is an answerable question, and it keeps the habitat (instrument, task, report condition, state) inside the account. Status **single-source** for the figure transfer (Fig 11 alone), corroborated in effect by the field's own "construct-first" turn, which asks what a theory must explain rather than what consciousness is. Refuter: a capability-and-contact account that leaves all first-person facts and all discriminator outcomes unchanged, in which case the reframe is cosmetic and is withdrawn.

### F9 - The field is broken at the middle dependency link, not short of cleverness (`specified`)

Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break."
Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Perception has obvious tests; planning has obvious tests. The link between having terms for what a neural state affords and saying what would count as progress has neither. That is exactly the published diagnosis: theories "have developed in parallel, with little to no cross-talk," support "is often highly dependent on methodological choices," and the field has "dozens of theories and no noncontentious way of deciding between them." Fig 3 :25-26 names the symptom: the link "attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." The remedy is not another theory; it is an instrument at that link, which is F5. Status **triangulated** (Fig 3 plus Fig 9 :60, with world corroboration from methodology reviews, low overlap with the IIT-specific sources). Refuter: a demonstration that the field's non-convergence survives a battery of theory-neutral, preregistered, instrument-level discriminations with predicted outcomes recorded in advance.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: the seat map field guide says "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single observation establishes both adequacy of an information-theoretic account and discrimination among rivals, with one criterion and no unbundling (kills F1).
2. A declared information-theoretic closure derives consciousness from its own primitives with no new atom, or proves its absence under a declared code (kills F2).
3. A figure-grounded derivation uniquely names which information measure occupies the substrate term without an import, or the shape constraint is shown empty (kills F3).
4. A proposed discriminator's outcome moves with the theoretical commitment of the team, or is validated only by agreement within one evidence pool (kills F4).
5. A genuinely discriminating observation is shown to be only a refinement of verbal report, with no independent readout anywhere at any resolution (kills F5).
6. A theory's success at fitting current data is shown to guarantee both composability and unique discriminating power, with no auxiliary adjustments (kills F7).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the census re-run. Until one is tested, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Unbundle before searching (F1). Do not answer "can it be explained" and "what would discriminate" in one paragraph.
2. Answer (a) as a reachability verdict and a shape, not as a yes/no and not as a name (F2, F3).
3. Answer (b) as a protocol: per-slot predictions written before data, theory-neutral acquisition, an anchor that does not update, and low overlap between teams (F4, F7).
4. Prefer instruments already returning something: no-report readouts, perturbational complexity, adversarial comparisons with preregistered pass/fail criteria (F5).
5. Use symmetry and directedness as structural tests when comparing measures (F6), and reframe the substrate question as a contact question (F8).
6. Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The number of theories, the impact factor of the venue and the size of a consensus are budget filters, never witnesses.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **One question or two?** Two, a union (Fig 9 :54-57). Split before search.
2. **Is information theory excluded?** No. Shannon coding is already inside the MDL bargain (Fig 5 :45). The wall concerns the substrate term, not the vocabulary as such.
3. **Is the answer reachable from inside?** Undecidable from inside (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :39). Report as a reachability verdict, not as silence.
4. **Can the framework name the measure?** No (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :133). Φ is a binding, provenance owed.
5. **What would discriminate?** An anchor: theory-neutral acquisition, predictions before data, a result that can fail both sides (Fig 2 :29, :35, :45). Existing proto-instruments: PCI, no-report, adversarial comparison.
6. **What carries the intrinsic/directed claim?** The bond, not the ingredient list (Fig 12 :5). Symmetry of the measure is a live test.
7. **What is the honest stop?** `Unreached, not unreachable`: instruments exist and are improving, but no source-discriminating reading yet closes the level/content/slot set. Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :40-42. Keep the union unbundled and the lineage recorded.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: the question is a union whose reachability half is a closure verdict and whose discrimination half is an anchor-and-instrument protocol. The specific claim that information theory already explains consciousness, or that Φ is the framework's answer, does not. One live strain is recorded (symmetry transfer from bonds to measures, F6); it is named, not hidden. No part is `grounded`.
