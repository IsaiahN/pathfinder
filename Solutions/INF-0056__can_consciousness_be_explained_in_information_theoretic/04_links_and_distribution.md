## What these sources do and do not bear on

1. **They do not decide the question.** Tononi's Φ, PCI, no-report paradigms and adversarial collaborations are world facts. The figures decide what counts as a discriminator (an anchor that does not update) and what the reachability verdict is (unreachable from inside, not silence). Agreement among these sources is not a second witness where they share authors or evidence pools; the ledger records this.
2. **Two of them are genuinely low-overlap witnesses for the method shape.** Tsuchiya et al. (human cognition methodology) and Searle (philosophy of mind) do not draw on the figure framework and disagree with each other about the promise of information-theoretic explanation. Their convergence on "the vocabulary must be bound before the search" is corroboration of F1, not of a theory.
3. **The 2025 adversarial result is the clearest existing instance of the required protocol**, not evidence that either theory is true. Its value here is structural: preregistered divergent predictions, theory-neutral teams, and a result that challenged both leading accounts.
