# Isomers, symmetry and order in the consciousness question

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The level/content/report/substrate slots in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` share an ingredient vocabulary (entropy, mutual information, effective information, compression, measurement) but yield incompatible predictions. Two questions are owed: (1) are the rival information measures **isomers** under Fig 12, and (2) in what **order** must the unbundled parts be searched so that a refusal is not out of order?

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are the candidate measures isomers?

Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

Shannon entropy, mutual information and integrated information all quantify a repertoire of states, so they share a **functional group** (a state count with a logarithm). They are not the same substance. Tononi explicitly contrasts a camera of one million photodiodes (huge repertoire, causally independent parts) with an integrated complex, and introduces directed effective information to make the distinction. Under Fig 12 :117, two measures with the same ingredients but different causal bonds are isomers: the difference is invisible if you record only the ingredient list, and decisive in which predictions the theory makes.

- **Weaker, safe claim:** the measures are members of one functional group (repertoire quantification) with different qualifiers. Mode `general` for the figure definition, `specified` for the application.
- **Stronger, testable claim:** they are isomers over a shared atom set, distinguished by the ordered/directed bond. Mode `specified`, unmeasured.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a candidate measure can be written without an atom another measure requires (for example a complexity index with no causal/perturbational term, or Φ with no repertoire term), the isomer claim fails and the functional-group claim stands instead.

### Q2 - Symmetry as the bond test

Fig 12 :59 "swap the operands. Does the meaning change?"
Fig 12 :73 "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."

Fig 12 separates symmetric bonds from ordered ones by asking whether swapping operands changes the term. Applied to measures:

- Shannon MI is symmetric under exchange of its operands. Tononi's paper defines MI(A;B) = H(A) + H(B) - H(AB) and notes "MI(A:B) is symmetric and positive."
- Effective information is not. The same paper states "EI(A→B) and EI(B→A) in general are not symmetric."

So a theory whose central claim is an intrinsic, directed perspective cannot be built from a symmetric measure alone; the directedness lives in the bond, not in the operands. Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

Status `single-source` for the figure test applied to measures (Fig 12 only); the measure mathematics comes from the world source and is not figure-derived. **Falsifier:** a symmetric measure that still supports the intrinsic/extrinsic distinction the theory claims, or a demonstration that the symmetry test does not transfer from bonds to measures. Either withdraws this finding and sends the census back to the measure domain.

### Q3 - What order does the union have?

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
THE_FORMULA.md step 2: the fifth bin is REFUTED, "a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."

The correct order is therefore:

1. **Characterize R per slot** (perception). Do not average level, content and report.
2. **Bind "information"** (vocabulary). Shannon coding, intrinsic/integrated information and compression are different terms; Searle's objection is that the word is used equivocally. This is the middle link of Fig 3, the one with no instrument.
3. **Refuse before mint** (objective). "Information is observer-relative, so the naive information-theoretic explanation is false" is a negation (¬), not a new measure. THE_FORMULA.md step 2 requires it before any mint; Fig 12's amendment notes "A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."
4. **Mint candidates against the owed bin** only after the refusal: Φ, global ignition, recurrent accounts, each with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).
5. **Choose the instrument** (planning): theory-neutral acquisition, predictions before data, an anchor that does not update.

Reversing steps 3 and 4 is the observed field failure: new measures are minted while the disputed term is left standing, which is why the literature has a growing library and no shrinking reach. **Falsifier:** a case where minting a new measure before refusing the old term reduces the residual on all four slots without any auxiliary adjustment.

### Q4 - Analogues that bear, and one that does not

- **Cycle** (Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input."): the vocabulary bootstrapping in *The Library of Babel* is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes. With a source-discriminating instrument it closes; without one it drifts, which is Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not."
- **Functional group** (Q1): shared repertoire quantification; the qualifier decides which measure.
- **Catalyst** (Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use.") does **not** transfer cleanly. An information measure is not a part that survives its own use in any sense the figures define, and forcing it would be a strain. Recorded and refused rather than smoothed.

## CONCLUSION

The rival information measures are best read as a functional group with an isomer claim still open: same repertoire atoms, different causal bond, different predictions. Symmetry is the sharpest available figure-grounded test, because the intrinsic/directed claim lives in the bond and a symmetric operand set cannot carry it. The union's order is refuse, then mint, then instrument; the field's habit of minting before refusing is a dependency-chain error at the middle link. One analogue (cycle) transfers, one (catalyst) is refused, and the isomer claim carries a stated falsifier.
