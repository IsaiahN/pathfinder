# Expert verification packet - the Tether on consciousness and information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a consciousness researcher, philosopher of mind or information theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the application, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** No first-person or neural ground reading is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of two categorically different asks and must be unbundled. On the reachability half, a fixed information-theoretic closure cannot certify from inside whether a first-person phenomenon is reachable; the honest verdict is `unreached from inside`, not "yes", not "no", and not silence, and the framework returns the shape any adequate account must have (per-slot, cross-level, with a directed term and a source-discriminating instrument) rather than the name of a measure. On the discrimination half, a valid discriminator must be an anchor whose reading does not move with who interprets it, with per-slot predictions written before data, theory-neutral acquisition and a result that can fail both leading accounts. Working proto-instruments exist (no-report readouts, perturbational complexity, preregistered adversarial comparisons) but are instruments, not verdicts. The hard problem is a source-discrimination problem.

### C3 - adjudication protocol; checkable against the published record

- **Statement:** a discriminator must be an anchor that does not update, with theory-neutral acquisition and preregistered divergent predictions; agreement among theory proponents is disqualifying for this purpose.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. Is that a real discriminator or just a well-run study whose interpretation still depends on the theory?
- **Refuter:** a proposed discriminator whose outcome tracks the theoretical commitment of the analysing team, or which is validated only by agreement within one evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-11-23): "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

1. A perturbation readout that separates wake, sleep, anesthesia and disorders of consciousness under theory-neutral acquisition, or a result showing it does not.
2. Preregistered divergent predictions on content location and temporal dynamics that pass for one theory and fail for another, or fail for both (the 2025 result did the latter).
3. An independent readout of conscious content that survives removal of report, attention and task demands, or evidence that every correlate collapses into report.
4. A test of measure directedness that discriminates symmetric from ordered candidates.
5. Any of the six pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4.
