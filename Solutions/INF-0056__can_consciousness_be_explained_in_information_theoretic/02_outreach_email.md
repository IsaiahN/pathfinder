# Outreach draft - validate the discriminator first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the union split and the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are intact. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one discriminator before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about a narrow output, not about a theory. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The question "can consciousness be explained in information-theoretic terms, and what observations would discriminate competing accounts?" is a union of two categorically different questions. Fig 9 says to unbundle a union before searching it. The first half is a reachability question: from inside a fixed information-theoretic vocabulary you can enumerate arrangements without end, but you cannot decide from inside whether the phenomenon is in the reach. That is a verdict of `unreachable from inside`, not a silence and not a "no". The framework returns a shape, not a name, so it does not pick Φ or any other measure as the answer. The second half is an anchor question: a discriminator must be a reading that does not move with who interprets it, with predictions written down before data and theory-neutral acquisition, and with a result that can fail both leading accounts.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that integrated information theory is established (the 2025 preregistered adversarial result challenged key tenets of both IIT and global neuronal workspace). Not that consciousness is "just information". Not that no-report or perturbational-complexity measurements have settled the hard problem. Not `grounded`: no first-person or neural ground reading was taken here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. one observation that establishes both adequacy and discrimination with a single criterion and no unbundling;
> 2. a declared information-theoretic closure that derives consciousness from its own primitives with no new atom, or proves its absence under a declared code;
> 3. a figure-grounded derivation that uniquely names the information measure, or a demonstration that the shape constraint is empty;
> 4. a proposed discriminator whose outcome tracks the theoretical commitment of the team, or that is validated only by agreement inside one evidence pool.
>
> I wrote these before sending this message. If any holds, I withdraw the finding and re-run the census. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. I have a stake in this working, so I am declaring it rather than asking you to take it on trust.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Canonical figures and line-numbered text (local framework files, branch `general-agent`): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
