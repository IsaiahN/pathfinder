## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133 "what it returns is a shape rather than a name".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a per-slot, cross-level composition with a directed term and a discriminator that does not move with the interpreter. "Φ is consciousness" is a name. "An information measure that fails to distinguish sources is inadequate to the claim" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained, and how a bundle is detected

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Bind the vocabulary closest to the residual (level, content, report, substrate, entropy, mutual information, integrated information) and recurse.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, written before this derivation and therefore not fitted to it: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level corroboration of F1's union split.
- *Library of Babel* on anchoring: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality". Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Do not ask "is consciousness computational?" Ask "which reading fails to resolve on which slot?"

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means a source-discriminating observation with predictions recorded before data.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). Naming Φ is exactly this kind of binding from the neuroscience frame, and until its provenance is shown it carries zero framework authority.

*The Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". The paradox is that by the time the search terms are exact, the answer has been composed through locating it. Here that would mean: when the discriminator is precise enough to run, the theory it tests no longer needs the framework to describe it. That is a stop condition, not a claim of arrival.

## 4. The hard problem is about source, and this is the central essay for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened 2026-11-23) reframes Chalmers' problem as an instrument problem. Its claims, in its own words:

- "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."
- "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."
- "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
- "This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source."
- "Any theory claiming to have explained experience is conflating projection for the real thing."

The metamerism case is decisive here. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that discriminated the sources. Applied to this question: a report, a complexity index, or a decoding accuracy is a reading. It does not by itself name the source (integrated information, global ignition, recurrent processing, or something not yet held). Multiple theories can fit the same reading, which is exactly why the field has "dozens of theories and no noncontentious way of deciding between them." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which is F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

This essay is framework-internal and shares an evidence pool with the figures, so it corroborates the shape but is not a second witness. It does, however, state explicitly that the hard problem is a measurement problem, which makes the second half of the user's question the framework-native half.

## 5. Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming Φ? Answered, with a correction to §3

**Answer.** Not categorically. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Entropy, mutual information and encoding are already in the framework's vocabulary (Fig 5 :45 makes Shannon coding a component of the MDL bargain), and Φ is a candidate measure proposed from the neuroscience frame. Naming a measure as a candidate is therefore a **binding**, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as a figure-derived result or as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom").

**Correction to my own §3.** In §3 I wrote that naming Φ carries "zero framework authority until provenance is shown." That is too strong in one respect and too weak in another. Too strong: a provenance-less binding is still a legitimate candidate from the world frame; the framework does not forbid stating it. Too weak: the defect is not merely missing paperwork, it is that the binding would be presented as the framework's answer when the framework returns only a shape. Keep both statements: the candidate is admissible as a world claim, and it is not a figure result. Status `specified`, refuter: a figure derivation that uniquely selects the measure.

§4 is **not** retracted. The source-discrimination point stands independently of whether naming held atoms is allowed: an instrument that reads a complexity value cannot comment on which theory generated it.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous proponents who agree with a consciousness theory is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator, and let a theory-neutral instrument settle it. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any consciousness theory and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-paragraph canonical reading

For this question, "shape" means the form any adequate answer must take before it can name a measure: per-slot rather than averaged, cross-level rather than substrate-only, with a directed term that survives the symmetry test and a discriminator that does not move with the interpreter. The framework returns that form, not "Φ" and not " consciousness is information." A name becomes licensed only when a source-discriminating instrument settles it or a provenanced import from a differing closure supplies it. The hard problem, read canonically, is a measurement problem: several sources can produce one reading, and what is owed is a better instrument, not another argument.
