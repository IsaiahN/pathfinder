# The Tether's answer on consciousness and information

**Mode: `specified` on the application, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two conditions. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

- **(a) Reachability.** Can a first-person phenomenon be composed from an information-theoretic vocabulary? This is not a yes/no; it is a question about what a closure can reach.
- **(b) Discrimination.** Which observation would make two rival accounts predict different readings on the same slot? This is an anchor question.

They are kept separate and never averaged. A theory's explanatory elegance on (a) cannot count as evidence on (b).

## 2. The answer, per domain

**On (a), the reachability verdict: unreached from inside, not silence and not a "no."**

Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

From inside a fixed information-theoretic vocabulary, you can enumerate arrangements without end; you cannot decide from inside whether the phenomenon is in the reach. A failed search is not a proof of absence, and a successful arrangement shows only what the closure already held. What would have to arrive is a term that is not in the primitives, characterized in effect terms. The framework returns that shape, not the name: Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

**On (a), the shape any adequate account must have:** per-slot (level, content, report, substrate, never averaged); cross-level (a membrane with a measured round trip, Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."); with a directed or intrinsic term that survives the symmetry test (Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."); and with a source-discriminating instrument, since a reading does not name its source.

**On (b), the discriminator verdict: an anchor, not an agreement.**

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

A discriminating observation must be readable without depending on which theory's proponents interpret it. Its protocol has four features: per-slot predictions written before data; theory-neutral acquisition; an anchor that does not update on the theories; and a result that can fail both leading accounts. Working proto-instruments already exist: no-report paradigms remove the report confound (Tsuchiya et al. 2015), the perturbational complexity index gives a state-independent level reading (Casali et al. 2013), and preregistered adversarial comparisons put theory-neutral labs behind divergent predictions (Melloni et al. 2023; Cogitate Consortium 2025). These are instruments, not verdicts.

**On the question form itself.**

Fig 11 :35 "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."
Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Transferred with the anchor re-named (Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"): the answerable question is not "what is consciousness" but "under which contact conditions does a system support a discriminable first-person reading, and which measurement would change if those conditions changed?" That keeps the habitat (instrument, task, report condition, state) inside the account.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "yes, consciousness is information." The framework returns a shape, not a name, and the word "information" is equivocal between Shannon coding and intrinsic integrated information (Searle 2013; Koch and Tononi 2013).
- Not "no, information theory cannot touch it." Fig 5 :45 makes Shannon coding a component of the framework's own MDL rule. The barrier concerns the substrate term and import, not the vocabulary as such.
- Not that Φ, global ignition, recurrent processing or any other current theory is correct. The 2025 adversarial result "substantially challeng[ed] key tenets of both theories."
- Not that PCI or no-report paradigms have solved the hard problem. They are instruments improved from worse ones (Fig 6 :32), and an instrument that reads a value "cannot comment on the source."
- Not `grounded`. No first-person or neural ground reading was taken here.
- Not a final theory. The figures README forbids a last room, and Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room."

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-11-23) states the principle: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Concrete tests, each tied to a slot:

1. **Level:** a perturbation-based readout whose value separates wake, sleep, anesthesia and disorders of consciousness on single individuals under theory-neutral acquisition. PCI is the existing prototype; a result that fails to separate these states would break the level claim.
2. **Content:** divergent location and temporal predictions (posterior sustained activity versus prefrontal ignition) tested with preregistered pass/fail criteria. The 2025 result challenged both, which is the ground working.
3. **Report:** an independent readout that survives removal of report, attention and task demands. No-report paradigms are the prototype; if every "conscious" correlate vanishes without report, the access slot collapses into the report slot.
4. **Substrate / symmetry:** a measure whose directedness is testable, not stipulated. If a symmetric measure can carry the intrinsic claim, or if no instrument can distinguish the candidate sources, the directed-bond requirement is withdrawn.
5. **Standing falsifiers:** those in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, including a closure-internal derivation of the phenomenon or a single observation that collapses the union.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Proto-instruments exist and are improving (Fig 6 :35 "the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there"), but no source-discriminating reading yet closes all four slots. The stop is at the limit of what current instruments distinguish, with the lineage recorded so the stop is checkable. The canonical README adds "Indiscernibility is not identity": a resolved reading would not be the same as an identified source. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument: *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that consciousness is information, and it does not say information theory can never reach it. It says the question is a union of two categorically different asks that must be unbundled. On the first, a fixed information-theoretic closure cannot certify from inside whether the phenomenon is reachable; that is a reachability verdict, not silence and not a "no", and what the framework returns is a shape: per-slot, cross-level, with a directed term and a source-discriminating instrument, not the name of a measure. On the second, a discriminator must be an anchor whose reading does not move with who interprets it, with predictions written before data and theory-neutral acquisition, and with a result that can fail both leading accounts. No-report readouts, perturbational complexity and preregistered adversarial comparisons are working proto-instruments, not verdicts. The hard problem, read canonically, is a measurement problem: several sources can produce one reading, and what is owed is a better instrument, not another argument. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
