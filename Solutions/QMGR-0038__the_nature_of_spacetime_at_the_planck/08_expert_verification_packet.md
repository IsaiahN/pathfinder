## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Spacetime" is not a primitive: two states never bond directly, so if space and time compose at all
a structure sits between them, and the slot stays open. Continuity and discreteness are not members
of the six-term set either; both are what particular compositions leave behind, so the question
"continuous or discrete at the Planck scale?" asks for a name where the framework returns a residue.
There is no privileged bottom level and no last room, but that is a statement about levels, not a
measurement of a minimum length at 10^-35 m. The specific microstructure is unreachable with the
current library: the set is closed, the arrangements are not, and importing a candidate needs a
source gate and a shadow test. What is offered is the shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the
instrument that would be required to name the slot.

an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "the answer is unreached until a
better instrument exists." Here:
