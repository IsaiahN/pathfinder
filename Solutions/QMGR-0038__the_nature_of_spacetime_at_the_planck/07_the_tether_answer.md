# The Tether's answer: spacetime at the Planck length

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** Simulated
derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a
ground reading. No instrument at 10^-35 m was used.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles at least three conditions: what composes to make "spacetime", whether the
continuum or its denial is primitive, and whether there is a bottom. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two
conditions is not a harder case of either." This is a union compound, not one hard problem, and each
condition keeps its own loop and its own verdict. The three are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **"Spacetime" is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not
   space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." If space
   and time compose at all, a structure sits between them. The slot stays marked `???`.
2. **Neither continuity nor discreteness is a primitive.** Fig 13 :43-44: "A continuum was the
   seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness
   over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." Fig 13
   :124-125 adds that discreteness is likewise "what a particular composition leaves behind, and
   none is a seventh member." So the familiar question, continuous or discrete at the Planck scale,
   asks for a name where the framework can only return a residue.
3. **There is no privileged bottom level, but that is not a measured minimum length.** Fig 13 :104:
   "irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"; Fig 7 :6:
   "the limit: no last room". The strain against a physical minimum length is re-censused in F3 of
   `01`: levels versus lengths are different quantities. The physical floor stays `gap`.
4. **The six cannot select the structure in the slot.** Fig 13 :76 licenses more than one first-order
   molecule. Selecting Gradient, Network or Spectrum is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced
   as such, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall.
5. **A search among candidate microstructures cannot settle the name.** Fig 13 :115: "The set is
   closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :118: "infinite effort
   inside a finite reach." LQG spin networks, causal sets, strings and foam are frames with a
   different closure; importing any of them needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8).
6. **The honest stop is a gap, not a silence.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a
   silence," and Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework narrows
   the answer to a shape and stops where the instruments stop distinguishing.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that spacetime is discrete at the Planck scale, and not that it is continuous at the Planck
  scale. Both are residues.
- Not that loop quantum gravity, causal sets, string theory, asymptotic safety or spacetime foam is
  correct. Those are bindings from a physics frame with no source gate here.
- Not that there is no physical minimum length. "No bottom" governs levels, not a measured length.
- Not that space and time bond directly, and not that "spacetime" is a member of the six.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 forbids the last room.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at 10^-35 m settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a
colour instrument reads green but "it cannot comment on the source"; "the answer is unreached until a
better instrument exists." Here:

- **Source test:** does the Planck-scale behaviour of the metric look like ordering (spectrum-like),
  relation (network-like) or directedness (gradient-like)? An instrument that discriminates these
  would name the slot. None currently reaches 10^-35 m.
- **Residue test:** does a given continuum or discrete behaviour survive when the bond arrangement is
  changed? If a behaviour is invariant across arrangements it was misclassified as a member; if it
  changes it is a residue.
- **Floor test:** does any observation establish a physical minimum length at 10^-35 m? The existing
  Chandra, Fermi and VERITAS bounds show smoothness only down to about 1,000 times smaller than a
  hydrogen nucleus (NASA, 2015), which is many orders above the Planck length.
- **Standing falsifiers from the pre-registration in `01`:** primitive continuity or discreteness; a
  direct space-space-time bond; a seventh irreducible primitive; an absolute bottom of levels.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The six are "a human's careful guess at reality's
compression axes"; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum
set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the
lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The verdict fields are in
`09_watson_answer.md`: cost if wrong, undoable, and reversed by a ground reading rather than
agreement.
