## Restatement in effect terms

Decide what "spacetime" is at the smallest scale currently given a name. Option A: it is
fundamentally continuous, so the continuum survives to the bottom. Option B: it is fundamentally
discrete, so a lattice, a spin network, a causal set or a foam is the ground floor. Option C: it is
a derivative with no bottom at all, and the continuum/discrete pair is something a composition
leaves behind. Each option leads somewhere different: A and B predict different behaviour of light
over cosmic distances and different ultraviolet completions; C predicts that the question as asked
is a bundle that must be split before it is searched. What an answer would look like is a claim
about which of the six substrate terms compose at that scale, plus a statement of what would
discriminate the options. Bound as an ordinary term ("Direction" in `THE_FORMULA.md`): the sought
shape is "a structure sits in the slot; continuity and discreteness are residues, not members".

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list
   selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Ten agreeing readers are one evidence pool,
   not ten witnesses.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the architecture
   before the verdict spends the reader's independence.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules
in many jurisdictions and blacklists the sending domain. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as
well as the structural one.
