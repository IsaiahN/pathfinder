## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. "Spacetime" is a derivative with a structure in the slot and the slot stays open. Continuity and discreteness are composition residues, not primitives, so the usual Planck-scale dichotomy is mis-posed as a primitive question. There is no privileged bottom level and no last room, but this is a statement about levels and must not be converted into a claim about a measured minimum length at 10^-35 m. The specific microstructure is unreachable with the current library: a closed set with unbounded arrangements, so search cannot manufacture a new kind and any candidate would have to be imported with a source gate and a shadow test. This is a `general` shape with `specified` structural claims, ungrounded, and the per-domain verdicts are kept separate rather than averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name or a recording instead of a shape and a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. There is no seventh primitive to withdraw and no physical claim to retract beyond the bindings, which were never asserted. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. The strained reading of "no bottom" was narrowed by re-census without touching the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing spectrum-like, network-like or gradient-like behaviour at the Planck scale would name the slot; a measurement of a direct space-space-time bond, a demonstration that continuity or discreteness is primitive, a seventh irreducible primitive, or an absolute bottom of levels would break the shape claim; a measurement establishing a physical minimum length at 10^-35 m would not by itself break the shape, because levels and lengths are different quantities, but it would force the re-census to be rewritten. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
