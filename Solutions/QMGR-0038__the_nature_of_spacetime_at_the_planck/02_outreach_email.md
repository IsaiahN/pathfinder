**Subject:** One checkable output about spacetime at the Planck scale, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking
> you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not
> survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the structure, not `grounded`):** a minimum
> viable set of six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum,
> gradient, network), composes only across kinds. Two states never bond directly, so "spacetime" is
> not a primitive: if space and time compose at all, a structure sits between them, and the slot is
> left open. The continuum was explicitly removed from the set, because it is a spectrum without
> gaps or smoothness over space and time and a generating set must not contain what its own members
> produce. Discreteness is in the same position: it is listed as something a composition leaves
> behind, not as a member. So the usual question, is spacetime continuous or discrete at the Planck
> scale, asks for a name where the framework can only return a shape.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that spacetime is definitely discrete at 10^-35 m, that loop quantum
> gravity or causal sets or string theory has been confirmed, that there is no physical minimum
> length, or that the framework settles the question. "No bottom" in the framework means no
> privileged level of irreducibility, not a statement about a measured length. I am also not claiming
> "spacetime is real" or "spacetime is emergent" as a verdict; those are bindings from a physics
> frame and they carry provenance, not derivation.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. an instrument showing that continuity or discreteness is a primitive property of spacetime, not
>    a derivative of a composition;
> 2. a measurement of a direct space-space-time bond with no structural intermediary;
> 3. a candidate microstructure that requires a seventh irreducible primitive and cannot be imported
>    with a stated source and a shadow test;
> 4. evidence establishing an absolute bottom of levels at the Planck scale in the framework's sense.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no
> reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule (figures README): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
