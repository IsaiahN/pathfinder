## 0. The claim under audit and its residual

Question QMGR-0038 asks for the nature of spacetime at roughly 10^-35 m. Stated as an effect: a
decision is wanted about what composes at the smallest named scale, and about whether the continuum
or its denial is a primitive there. Three readings are on the table:

- **A, continuum to the bottom:** smoothness survives without limit. Leads to a picture in which
  the metric is defined at arbitrarily small scales and the ultraviolet divergences of perturbative
  gravity are a technical defect, not a structural one.
- **B, discrete floor:** a lattice, a spin network, a causal set or a foam is the ground floor.
  Leads to a minimum length, a cut-off on ultraviolet modes, and in some models to energy-dependent
  propagation of light.
- **C, derivative with no bottom:** there is no primitive spacetime at any scale; a structure sits
  between space and time, and continuity and discreteness are what particular compositions leave
  behind. Leads to the claim that the question as asked is a bundle that must be split, and that the
  specific floor is unreachable until a source-discriminating instrument exists.

The residual **R** is the gap between the effect described and any answer held in the current frame.
Fig 9 :27 describes the discipline: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will
seem to fit". So the residual is written before any frame (loop quantum gravity, causal sets, string
theory, asymptotic safety) is named.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** at least three conditions, not one. (i) What composes to make "spacetime" at all;
  (ii) whether continuity or discreteness is primitive or derivative; (iii) whether there is a
  bottom. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." This is a union
  compound, not a harder version of one question.
- **Symmetry:** the continuum/discrete pair is a comparison of two derivative properties, not a
  symmetric join of two primitives at the same level. No `+`, `∥` or `≡` bond applies to them as
  members, because they are not members.
- **Scale:** the question is explicitly cross-scale, so Fig 4's membrane and round-trip gap govern

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The continuum was explicitly removed from the primitive set (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."

Fig 13 :41: "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it." Fig 13 :124-125:

> "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."

Reading A ("continuum to the bottom") is therefore not an option inside this framework. The
continuum is a derivative of spectrum plus space plus time, and it was removed precisely because a
generating set must not contain what its own members produce. Equally, **discreteness is not a
primitive either**: option B cannot be obtained by promoting "discrete" to a seventh member. Both
horns of the usual debate are residues. This is triangulated across Fig 13 :43-44 and Fig 13 :124-125
plus the substrate section of `THE_FORMULA.md` (low overlap between the indivisibility argument and
the derivatives list). It does **not** say which composition nature actually uses; that is a ground
question.

### F2 — "Spacetime" is a derivative with a structure in the slot (triangulated)

Fig 13 :75:

> "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." So the
question "what is spacetime at the Planck scale" reduces to "which structure occupies the slot at
that scale". Fig 13 itself shows the slot is not selected by the six: Fig 13 :76 "A gradient over
space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it
is." The framework returns a kind of thing, not the thing: Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never
the answer itself". **Verdict:** the shape is settled, the name is not.

### F3 — There is no bottom of levels, and the Planck length is not a framework floor (strain, re-censused)

Fig 13 :104:

> "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"

Fig 13 :90: "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route." Fig 4 :33: "Which is
why there is no top and no infinite regress." Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room".

**Strain test:** applying "no bottom" directly to 10^-35 m strains the line. "No bottom" governs
irreducibility of levels, not a physical length. Re-census: the framework forbids a privileged
stratum; whether 10^-35 m is a physical minimum length is a claim about the physics frame (D2), not
about the closure of D1. After re-census there is **no strain**: Fig 13 :104 governs levels, the
minimum-length question governs instruments. Two different quantities, so no contradiction. The
framework's shape is "no final level"; it does not adjudicate the measured floor. Marked
`triangulated` for the no-privileged-level claim (Fig 13 :90, Fig 4 :33, Fig 7 :6) and `gap` for the
physical-floor claim.

### F4 — The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (triangulated)

Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 adds:
"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The
three conditions in D0 (composition, continuum/discreteness, bottom) each have their own loop and
their own ground. Searching them as one question is the failure the figure names. *The Library of
Babel* (2026-10-02, read 2026-11-23) makes the same point from search: "Perhaps your problem was a
bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those
problems individually". **Verdict:** unbundle first; keep the three verdicts separate, never average.

### F5 — A finite reach cannot decide among candidate microstructures (triangulated)

Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13
:118: "infinite effort inside a finite reach." Fig 13 :116-117: six members and no amount of
arranging produces a seventh. So enumerating lattice, spin network, causal set, string and foam
inside the current closure cannot settle which one nature uses. Each candidate that needs a new
kind would have to be imported (Fig 6: only import moves the wall) with a source gate and a shadow
test (Fig 8). **Verdict:** the search for a name is a budget question; the verdict is a gap until a
ground speaks.

### F6 — Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (triangulated)

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which
primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed
room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing
proves nothing." The canonical figures README adds the limit: the framework returns "a shape, not a
name" and stops at what the instruments distinguish. So the correct answer to "what is spacetime at
the Planck scale" includes an explicit `gap` on the specific microstructure, not an embarrassed
silence and not a guessed name.

### F7 — The ground, not agreement, decides among the physics programs (single-source per program, union pooled)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45:
"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The world literature disagrees
with itself: loop quantum gravity reports a granular structure of space at the Planck length

discrete (*Causal sets*), causal dynamical triangulation reports a continuum phase in 2D (cited in
that page), and Chandra/Fermi/VERITAS constrain foam models without finding them (*Quantum foam*;

information. It is **not** evidence that the framework's shape is right; most of them share a
physics evidence pool, so by Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing
frames share an evidence pool". Verdict per program stays separate; none is averaged into the Tether
verdict.

### F8 — The cross-scale round trip is lossy (triangulated)

Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :30:

> "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the seat map: "a falsifier written before the run is a design
instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An instrument demonstrates continuity or discreteness as a **primitive** property of spacetime,
   not a derivative of a composition (breaks Fig 13 :43-44 and :124-125).
2. A direct space space-time bond is measured with no structural intermediary (breaks Fig 13 :75).
3. A candidate microstructure is shown to require a seventh primitive irreducible to the six and
   cannot be imported with a source gate and shadow test (breaks Fig 13 :41, :116-117).
4. Evidence establishes an absolute bottom of levels in the framework's sense at 10^-35 m (breaks
   Fig 13 :104, Fig 4 :33, Fig 7 :6).

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is `grounded`.
