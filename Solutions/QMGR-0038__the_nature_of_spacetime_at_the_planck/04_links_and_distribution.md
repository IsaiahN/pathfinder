## What the wider literature actually says (secondary only)

The world sources do not deliver a verdict; they show a live disagreement that the shape claim must
survive rather than resolve:

1. **Discrete side.** Loop quantum gravity reports a granular structure of space at the Planck
   length; causal sets take spacetime as fundamentally discrete and recover geometry from order plus
   counting. Both are frames with a different closure from the six-term set.
2. **Continuum side.** Causal dynamical triangulation reports evidence for a continuum phase in 2D
   (as cited in the causal-set page). Asymptotic safety seeks a continuum ultraviolet fixed point
   (as described in the quantum-gravity page). These are not the same claim as "the continuum is a
   primitive"; they are continuum-like behaviours arising from a dynamics, which is closer to the
   framework's residue reading.
3. **Instrument side.** Chandra, Fermi and VERITAS ruled out two foam models and found spacetime
   smooth down to about 1,000 times smaller than a hydrogen nucleus, which is still vastly larger
   than 10^-35 m. This bounds models; it does not decide the floor. Vasileiou et al. 2015 is cited
   within the quantum-foam page and was not opened directly, so it is not used as a standalone quote.
4. **Cognition side (low overlap).** The phi phenomenon shows a continuous percept composed from
   discrete stimuli, with the intermediate positions never presented. It is an analogue for "a
   continuum can be a derivative of discrete composition", not evidence about Planck-scale physics.
   Its overlap with the physics sources is low, which is why it is worth including; it corroborates
   the *shape* of the residue claim, not its physical application.

No source conflicts with the figure verdict. Where sources disagree with each other, that is kept as
information about the ground and is never averaged into the Tether verdict (Fig 2 :35).
