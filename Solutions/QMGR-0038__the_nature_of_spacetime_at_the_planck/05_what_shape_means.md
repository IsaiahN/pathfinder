## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13
:135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and
descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the
 Planck-scale microstructure can be named. `SPACE(???)TIME` with a marked slot is a shape.
"A discrete causal set at 10^-35 m" is a name. The shape constrains: it says a structure must sit
between space and time, and that continuity and discreteness are residues. It does not fill the slot.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is "closest to the words that you wish to

  by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here, the held vocabulary is the six terms and
  their bonds; the unresolved vocabulary is "floor", "foam", "granularity".
- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". The
  residual was written in `01` before LQG, causal sets, strings and foam were named.
- Fig 6 :30 (via the exemplar's local extract): "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened
  into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." And Fig 9 :30: describe the effect rather
  than the cause. So "foam" is not used as an explanation; it is treated as an effect to be described.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". Here that means
  the answer stops where the instruments stop distinguishing.

## 3. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any structure in the slot. That overstates
the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Gradient, Network
and Spectrum are **already among the six** (Fig 13). Selecting one of them for the slot is a
**binding** from the physics frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a seventh. What is still
forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom") or that the
framework itself selects the atom. Both the overstatement and the correction are kept visible here,
as the exemplar keeps its own F1 retraction visible.

For this question the distinction matters twice:

1. Naming Gradient, Network or Spectrum in the slot is a binding, allowed with provenance, and still
   unproven: nothing at 10^-35 m discriminates among them.
2. Naming LQG spin networks, causal sets, strings or foam as the physical floor is a different move.
   Those are candidates from a frame whose closure differs from D1. Under Fig 6 they would have to
   be **imported**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8). Until that is done they are names
   without standing.

## 4. Why the specific name is not licensed: source discrimination

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green but "it
cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme
not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with
lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "the answer is unreached until a better instrument
exists", and it was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a smooth macroscopic metric and a bound on foam are readings that several underlying
microstructures could produce. Naming one source from that reading is the metamerism error. What is
owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. The NASA, Chandra, Fermi and
VERITAS results are such instruments at scales far above 10^-35 m; they bound models without naming
the source. This is why the verdict on the physical floor is `gap`, not a quiet guess.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular Planck-scale microstructure?

**Nothing in the framework.** A particular microstructure is licensed only by the ground settling a
minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the
searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The framework returns the shape: a
structure between the states, continuity and discreteness as residues, no final level.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation cannot validate itself

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"
and "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an
action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground
can settle it." A list of famous supporters would be a budget, not a verdict.

Same essay: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool,
or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Also: "LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be
derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` through `11` are a
simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test
of the claim and cannot settle it. This is stated on every substantive file.
