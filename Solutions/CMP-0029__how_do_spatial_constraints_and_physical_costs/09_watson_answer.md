## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, every numeric consequence is ungrounded. An abstract topological closure cannot generate distance, capacity or budget; embedding is a wall-moving import from the physical frame. Once space and cost are present, links must compose across kinds, so free equal-cost long-range links are not composable. The prediction error is indexed by link and region rather than by the network mean, and topological hops and route distance are two currencies separated by a measurable round-trip gap. Geometry and cost are a union compound requiring two loops and a third, not one blended parameter. The framework returns that shape and its refuters, not a revised universal constant. This is a `general` and `specified`, ungrounded result, `candidate` until a ground pays for it.

- **undoable:** yes. The specific bindings, such as which held atom carries cost, are removable without touching the structural derivation. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
