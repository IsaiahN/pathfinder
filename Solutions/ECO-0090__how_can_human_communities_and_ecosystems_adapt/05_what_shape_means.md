## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The
local reading adds: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands ... It
cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."*

For ECO-0090 a shape is therefore:

> a per-slot enumeration of the habitat, including the receiving habitat and the cascade beyond it;
> a separate ground per affected group that the benefiting frame cannot move; a union unbundled
> before search; two currencies held apart; a method transferred rather than a case replayed.

It is **not** a named culprit, a named community, a named technology, or a single net score. In the
exemplar's notation, `SPACE(???)TIME` is a shape and `SPACE(Gradient)TIME` is a name. The analogue
here is `ACTOR(???)BURDEN`: the slot where the cost lands is marked and stays open until a ground
settles it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap from the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new
  terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen
  your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Applied here: describe the residual first (a burden that reappears in another group's habitat),
  then let that description select the frame. Do not pick "resilience", "environmental justice" or
  "green gentrification" first and then map the question onto them; that is the apophenia the
  exemplar names as echo without shadow.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays."* Here that means a per-slot measurement in the receiving habitat, taken by a frame
   independent of the benefiting institution. Until then, "who bears the cost" stays a slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. A life-cycle assessment or an EJScreen burden index can be such an
   import only if its provenance, the gap it explains, and the independence of its evidence pool are
   stated.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It
is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It
makes the choice visible."*

## 4. The metamerism warning, applied

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot
comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths
create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the
spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question the analogue is sharp. A community reports a successful adaptation (a green
reading), but that reading cannot distinguish (a) the burden genuinely eliminated, (b) the burden
moved downstream, (c) the burden moved to a future generation, or (d) the burden moved into a
non-human actor's habitat. What is owed is a per-slot instrument, not a name applied to a projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or
the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Correction to my own §1 and §4

I initially read *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding any naming of actors in this answer. That is too
strong. As the exemplar's §5b correction records, the phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not
held in Γ. Here the atoms (communities, habitats, conditions, relations) are already held; what is
missing is the **arrangement** of the cost-bearing relation and the instrument that resolves it.
So:

- Naming a habitat or an actor already in the census is allowed, as a binding from the world frame,
  provenanced as such.
- What remains forbidden is (a) inventing a new primitive to absorb the residual, (b) claiming the
  binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*), and (c) naming the
  source of a reading that several sources could produce. My §4 metamerism point stands, but its
  reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on names.

Both readings are kept here so the correction is visible.

## 6. Q2 answered

**What licenses naming the group that bears the shifted cost?** Nothing in the framework alone. A
per-slot ground reading does, or a provenanced import with a shadow test does. Until one of those,
the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No at shape level: `01` F9 records the specific
distribution as `gap`. It would commit the error if it asserted that a particular community is the
bearer without a per-slot reading. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`
state this as a non-claim.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed
to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be
tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be
blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that the adaptation is just is
the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried, with the ground per slot deciding.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the
real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a
simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any
real adaptation and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
