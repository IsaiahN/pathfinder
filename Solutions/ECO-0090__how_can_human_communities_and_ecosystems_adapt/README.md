## Restatement as an effect

A community holds a residual R, the gap between the conditions it needs and the conditions its
habitat now supplies. An adaptation reduces R for the acting community. The same adaptation may
land the burden in another habitat, another group, another species, or a later generation. The
residual is not resolved; it is relocated. Because the acting community has no instrument in the
receiving habitat, the relocation is invisible there and the adaptation looks settled. What an
answer would look like: a shape that keeps R per slot visible across the whole contact cascade,
keeps each affected group's ground as an anchor that does not move when the powerful frame changes
its account, and refuses a single net score as a verdict. That shape is bound as an ordinary term
in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `06_isomers_and_order.md` so it can accrue a residual and be
refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that adaptation can be just.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list ranked by receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
Public opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one at the same time.
