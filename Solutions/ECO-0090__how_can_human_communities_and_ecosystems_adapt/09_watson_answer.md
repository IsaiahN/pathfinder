## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a community adaptation that lowers its own
environmental burden while the same burden appears in a less powerful group's habitat. Option A:
accept a net "adaptation succeeded" score as the answer. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and
refuse the net score until the per-slot burden is measured. What each leads to: A reports success
while the live slot that carries the cost stays invisible, and it spends the independence of the
people asked to check it; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a
ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the conditions a community needs and the conditions its
  habitat supplies, indexed per affected group and per actor, not aggregated. Arity: at least three
  systems (acting community, habitat, receiving habitat) plus the cascade beyond. Symmetry: broken,
  the action is ordered and the seat's power is asymmetric. Scale: local habitat through distant,
  telecoupled systems; cross-scale and lossy between scales. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not
  one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1 Community adaptation seat.** Actors: residents, planners, engineers, managers. Currency:
  reduced local burden. Anchor: measured local conditions that do not move when the seat wants them
  to. Admitted.
- **D2 Habitat and ecosystem.** Actors: species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season,
  terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: survival,
  reproduction, resource flows. Anchor: what the ground keeps, "nothing selects; what survives is
  what the ground kept". Admitted.
- **D3 Displaced habitat, the less powerful group.** Actors: that group and its habitat actors,
  including unchosen ones. Currency: the burden that lands there. Anchor: that group's ground,
  independently measured, not the benefiting institution's account. Admitted.
- **D4 Verification and channel.** Actors: instruments, per-slot metrics, outside frames. Currency:
  residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement; independence between frames.
  Admitted.
- **X1 Community resilience as a property of the community alone.** Actors: the community. Currency:
  none distinct from D1/D2. Anchor: none nameable apart from D1's local reading. Not a domain yet;
  absorbed by D1+D2.

Union, not partition: D1/D2/D3 overlap through the substituted habitat and the spillover, and the
overlap has its own loop and ground. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning,
learn and carry (Fig 3). Strain test: "exclusion is not" governs the habitat, not policy tools;
Fig 2 :40 governs agreement about the world, not deliberation as a convention. Both first readings
were narrowed in `01` §5 rather than smoothed over.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a net score does not. Adaptation is a change in contact, so a community-only gain is not adaptation. The habitat must be enumerated to the cascade horizon; a relocated burden is a substitution of habitats, not a removal; each affected group needs a ground that the benefiting frame cannot move; a union must be unbundled; and description cost and action cost must be read separately. Two programmes with the same actors can be isomers whose ingredient list hides the decisive cost-bearing bond. The specific distribution of the burden is a declared `gap`, owed to a per-slot instrument, not a name. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **Cost if wrong:** a real cost shift would be reported as success, and the check would be aimed at the programme design rather than at the receiving habitat. The avoidable part is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, that is a net score and a success story, instead of the shape and its refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its four falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** a per-slot instrument in the receiving habitat that registers the omitted slot; a demonstration that a single net score preserves every per-slot residual; a relocation leaving the receiving habitat's actor set and goal conditions unchanged with no unchosen inherited actors; a community-only improvement that measurably changes habitat contact with no enumeration; a manipulation in which the benefiting frame's account changes while physical conditions are held fixed and the burden reading does not move. Any of these fires the pre-registered refuter in `01` §3. Agreement or prestige is not such an instrument.
