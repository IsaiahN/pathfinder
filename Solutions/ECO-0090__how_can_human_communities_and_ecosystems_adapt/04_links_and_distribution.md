## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework
  before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape
  claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has
  absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
  Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the
  audit.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the
  agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a
  verdict"*: a list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :60** *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* Declare that this derivation was
  produced by a simulated seat with no ground measurement, and declare the interest in the outcome.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the
recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three local links, with
the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only then the formula as a method
to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Residual and shape claim, with refuters and mode. No theory, no request for trust or
   agreement. The verdict is recorded here.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** The audit and verbatim figure text. Later agreement is never pooled as
   corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** The formula as a reusable generator, with a fresh refuter and a declared
   ground per problem.

Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent. You host and you send. An opt-in download or a public
submission portal is closer to the figures' rule than an attachment pushed to a list. `03_public_contact_points.csv`
