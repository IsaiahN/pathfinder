# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and §3 are stated plainly. The offer collapses on contact if the
first-stage output cannot survive a check.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about adaptation without cost shifting

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the
> output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** an adaptation that reduces a community's burden
> while the same burden lands on another group has not resolved the problem; it has relocated it.
> Three consequences follow, and each is checkable:
>
> 1. A net "benefit" score cannot be the verdict. The burden is indexed per affected group and per
>    actor, and averaging across those slots is how a live signal disappears. This is a shape, not
>    a measurement.
> 2. Moving a burden to a different habitat is substitution, not removal. The receiving habitat
>    brings actors nobody chose, and both what was left behind and what was carried in stay
>    invisible until they act.
> 3. The reading of the burden on the less powerful group must come from a ground that the
>    benefiting institution cannot move. Otherwise the measurement drifts with the account of the
>    institution that benefits.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I know which group bears the cost in any particular case, or by
>    how much. The framework returns a shape, not a name. The specific distribution is an open gap,
>    and I have written it down as an owed instrument rather than filling it with a culprit.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a relocation shown to leave the receiving habitat with the same actor set, all goal conditions
>    reproduced, no unchosen inherited actors, and no new invisible failure;
> 2. a single score shown to preserve every per-slot residual and predict the displaced burden
>    without separate per-slot readings;
> 3. an instrument showing the receiving group's ground unchanged across substitution while the
>    benefiting institution's account changes;
> 4. a community-only improvement shown to change habitat contact per slot with no habitat
>    enumeration.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it.
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not
> engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them. Please record your verdict on the four points above before reading them, so
> your independence is not spent.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The worked audit this came from: `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the eight-step loop is in `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`.
> Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute
> your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more
> useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]

## Two-stage offer, explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a
   failure. Record their verdict **before** showing theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local links. The figures, not the
   summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40
   *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames
   share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each
   user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Nothing is sent by this agent. You host and you send. Prefer an opt-in download or submission route
over an attachment pushed to a list.
