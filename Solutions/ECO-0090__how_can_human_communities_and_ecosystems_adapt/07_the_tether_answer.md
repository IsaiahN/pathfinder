# The Tether's answer on adaptation without shifting costs

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the barrier rules, not `grounded`.**
This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A community reduces its burden R while the same burden reappears in another group's habitat, a later
generation, or a non-human actor. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated: *"The residual is indexed
by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19). A net success score has deleted the
signal that says where to look. The problem is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: the acting
community, the ecosystem and the receiving group are overlapping conditions with their own loops.

## 2. The shape

1. **Adaptation is a change in contact, not a change in the community alone.** Fig 11 :40:
   *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that
   does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A community-only gain is not
   adaptation to its ecosystem.
2. **Enumerate the habitat outward until the cascade stops mattering.** Fig 11 :44: *"List everything
   in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade
   stops mattering."* Fig 11 :53: contact scoping is how DDT reached the eagles, three steps out.
   Direct-contact assessment is the failure mode, not a neutral simplification.
3. **A relocated burden is substitution, not removal.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the
   habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you
   must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you
   did not choose."* The less powerful group inherits actors it did not choose, and both what was
   left behind and what was carried in are invisible until they act (Fig 11 :68-69).
4. **Each affected group needs an anchor the benefiting frame cannot move.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is
   whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
   Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its
   keeper is thereby disqualified."* The burden reading for the receiving group cannot be authored by
   the institution that benefits from the adaptation.
5. **Unbundle the union; a net index is a filter, not a verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the
   budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Per-slot residuals
   stay visible; one score cannot decide whether a cost was shifted.
6. **Two currencies stay separate.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they
   do not add."* The reported benefit (description) and the field audit (action) are in different
   units. Adding them is how a programme becomes uncheckable.
7. **Transfer methods, not cases.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
   A successful project copied into a new habitat is a seed, and *"the conditions it lands in decide
   what it becomes"* (Fig 4 :35). Measure the round-trip gap rather than assuming the copy is the
   original (Fig 4 :30).
8. **The chain breaks at vocabulary and objective.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a
   link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Better design above the break cannot rescue
   a receiving habitat nobody measures.
9. **Participation is a convention, not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not
   a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Deliberate to build the shared
   meaning, then range each room against the ground separately.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not which group bears a cost, or by how much. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
  itself."* The distribution is a declared `gap` (F9), owed to a per-slot instrument.
- Not that community participation is useless. It is a convention (Fig 2 :41); it is disqualified
  only when it decides an outcome (Fig 2 :43).
- Not that "exclusion is not available" means a manager may never exclude an actor from a plan.
  Fig 11 :62 governs the habitat: contact is a fact that does not consult the manager. That first
  reading was retracted in `01` §5.
- Not that any named technology, index or programme is the answer. A binding is provenance, not a
  derivation (Fig 8 :50-52).
- Not grounded. No per-slot measurement of a displaced burden was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A per-slot instrument already returning something, refined inward, and an import from a frame with a
different closure, with a source gate and a shadow test. The specific questions:

- Does a burden index for the receiving habitat register the same slot that the acting community's
  report omits? If it returns nothing at every resolution, this is a proto-instrument search, not a
  sharpening (Fig 6 :34-35).
- Does the reading move when the benefiting institution changes its account while physical
  conditions are held fixed? If yes, the reading is a frame, not an anchor.
- Do two programmes with the same atoms and a different cost-bearing bond produce different per-slot
  distributions? That is the isomer test in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1.
- Are the persisting monitoring parts actually regenerating, or spent and reported as live (Fig 12
  :119)?

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8
:39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape can be stated, and the
missing instrument can be characterised in effect terms, but it cannot be named from inside the
frame. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a culprit and does not offer a net score. It says that adaptation is a
> change in contact, so a community improved in isolation has not adapted; that the habitat must be
> enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering, because that is how a cost reaches a group
> three steps away; and that moving a burden is substitution rather than removal, so the receiving
> group inherits actors it did not choose and failures it cannot yet see. The reading of that
> burden must be an anchor the benefiting institution cannot move, and it must be kept separate from
> the programme's reported benefit, because description cost and ground-settling action cost do not
> add. Two programmes with the same actors can still be isomers whose ingredient list hides the
> decisive difference. What is offered is the shape any just adaptation must have, falsifiers written
> in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The specific distribution stays open
> until a ground settles it.
