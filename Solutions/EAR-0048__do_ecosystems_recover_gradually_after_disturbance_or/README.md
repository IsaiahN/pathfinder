## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the agreement it would then cite.
2. **Shared evidence pools disqualify verdicts.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **Do not hand the interior to the verifier.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
