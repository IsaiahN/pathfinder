## QUESTION

Do ecosystems recover gradually after disturbance, or can they settle into a different stable state?

Restated as an effect: after a perturbation displaces an ecosystem from S0, does per-slot R(t) = |Γ(b,a) - o'| decline monotonically to zero at S0, or settle at S1 not equal to S0 with a threshold and a history-dependent return path? Option A (gradual) leads to a single-attractor management rule. Option B (different state) leads to a threshold and hysteresis rule. The figures decide a third thing first: the options form a union of conditions, not a partition of ecosystems.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The binary question is refused as posed and answered as a union. Gradual recovery is composition inside one basin; settlement in a different stable state is a change of basin requiring an import, and reversing the driver is not the same as crossing back. The regime is selected by loop sign, closure change, enumerated habitat conditions, and channel fidelity. Same species under different bonds are isomers. The framework states this decision rule and the hysteresis test that discriminates the cases, and declares a gap on which basin a given ecosystem occupies. Mode `specified` with `general` barrier claims, ungrounded, candidate. Per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** asymmetric and partly irreversible. Assuming gradual return when the system has crossed into a different basin means small perturbations are applied to a locked state and recovery is mis-measured by a saturated aggregate index. Assuming a shift when the response is gradual invites an import, and Fig 11 :61-62 says an introduced actor cannot be subtracted. Fig 6 :24 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives. The cost is bounded but real, and is largest for management decisions.
- **undoable:** only partly. A false inference can be corrected, because a refutation is added and nothing is deleted, so the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 amendment). But an actual regime shift with introduced actors is permanent work rather than a solved state (Fig 11 :63). The shape claim itself is fully undoable: withdraw it on any pre-registered falsifier and keep the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A return-path measurement under increasing then decreasing driver; a feedback-sign measurement that fails to predict regime; a documented basin crossing by internal composition alone with no import; a per-slot measurement showing the aggregate suffices; a figure line quoted verbatim that names the settlement state for a named habitat. Any one of these fires a pre-registered refuter. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
