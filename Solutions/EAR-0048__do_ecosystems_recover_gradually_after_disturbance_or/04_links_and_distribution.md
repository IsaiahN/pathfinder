## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on the question

1. **The sign of the loop and the source of the driver split the cases.** Dent, Cumming and Carpenter report that internally reinforced states are explained by nonlinear feedback models, while linear models "may be adequate, particularly if ecosystem behaviour is primarily controlled by external processes." So the answer to "gradual or different state" depends on whether the controlling processes are internal or external. This is a union, not a binary. It corroborates Fig 9 :54 without overruling it.
2. **The mechanism transfers across fields, the vocabulary does not.** The synthetic-biology chapter derives two stable states from positive feedback plus ultrasensitivity, and hysteresis as memory of a transient input; Wikipedia's dynamical-systems account requires an unstable equilibrium between the two minima. Both are the same shape Fig 12 :121 names ("The sign of the loop decides whether it closes"), reached in a field that does not draw on ecology. That is a low-overlap witness for the mechanism, not for any ecosystem fact.
