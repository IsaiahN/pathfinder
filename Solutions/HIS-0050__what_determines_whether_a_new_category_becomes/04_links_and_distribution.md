## Relevant wider literature, grouped by field

- **History and philosophy of science.** Fleck's thought collective and thought style; Kuhn's paradigms, exemplars and taxonomic incommensurability; Hacking's historical ontology and looping kinds. The Fleck and Kuhn material is the strongest low-overlap corroboration for the claim that uptake is selection between different closures, not repetition inside one.
- **Science and technology studies.** Star and Griesemer's boundary objects and Bowker and Star's *Sorting Things Out* explain how a classification stays common enough to travel while being interpreted differently at each site. This is the habitat clause of the shape, in another vocabulary.
- **Cognitive science.** Rosch's basic-level categories and prototype theory: categories that win cognitively are those that maximise within-category information and minimise between-category overlap. This is the description-length bargain, in a different currency.
- **Philosophy of convention.** Lewis, Hume and the SEP entry: a convention is a self-perpetuating regularity sustained by common knowledge, and for many philosophers the point is exactly how conventions create facts. This corroborates the separation of convention from verdict without settling it.
- **Framework essays (high overlap, framework author).** Library of Babel for shape, Hard Problem for source discrimination, Recursive Self-Improvement Trap for simulation status and prestige. Agreement with the figures here is expected and carries little independent weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the shape claim and its refuter. The essay on recursive self-improvement adds that LLM output is a simulation and cannot validate itself, so the receiver must check the output before being shown the generator.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the figures.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Never select recipients by likelihood of agreement, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not".** A mailing list of people in one closure is one long room.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** the recipient checks the shape claim against their own knowledge of classificatory practice, without the framework's interiors. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, they read the figures and essays. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they now hold the source (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, on request:** the formula is offered as a reusable generator. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user must name their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because the loop cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. As written, the shape claim is `specified` and the naming part is a declared `gap`. If a reader fires any of the five refuters, the claim is narrowed or withdrawn before stage 2 is offered.
