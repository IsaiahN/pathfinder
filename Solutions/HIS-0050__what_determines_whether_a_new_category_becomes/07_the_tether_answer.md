# The Tether's answer: what determines whether a new category becomes widely accepted and used

**Mode: `specified` on the four-part shape, `general` on the verdict/convention split, `gap` on naming a winner. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No historical measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A proposed category either becomes a working coin of a community or disappears. The question asks what closes that difference. The figures say it is not one question: wide uptake is a **convention** (Fig 2 :41), while whether the category carves nature at a joint is a **verdict** (Fig 2 :40). The two have different anchors and different verdicts, and they are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Wide uptake is a convention, and agreement is its product.** Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". But that same agreement is worthless as evidence of truth when the agreeing frames share a pool (Fig 2 :40). So the first determinant of "widely accepted and used" is not truth; it is the formation of a shared meaning.
2. **The category must pay its description-length bargain.** Fig 5 :43: "A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before." Fig 5 :41: "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely." A category that costs more than the confusion it removes will not survive a next generation's budget, however loudly it is launched.
3. **It must change contact in a habitat.** Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A category is introduced (Fig 11 :32 "introduce, never subtract") into instruments, counts, predictions and teaching. Agent plus habitat is the unit of capability (Fig 11 :39), not the category alone.
4. **It must be selected between rooms, not mirrored.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A chain of identical rooms is one long room (Fig 7 :36); agreement compounds while evidence does not (Fig 7 :13). The category that wins is the one each new room selects against the residual the last one surfaced.
5. **Its route must be recorded.** Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". Usage alone cannot tell a derived category from an imported one.
6. **A category that decides an outcome stops being a convention.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Once a diagnostic, administrative or legal consequence attaches, popularity is no longer a check and an anchor is owed.
7. **The framework does not name the winner.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." The naming determinant is a `gap`: unreachable here, not silent.

The dependency order is fixed (Fig 3 :20): describe the residual, refuse what is false, mint and pay, introduce into a habitat, select between rooms, settle at the ground, record the route. Skipping a link is a break, not a weaker chain. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the full order and its falsifiers.

## 3. Verdicts per domain, kept separate

- **Domain 1, term worthiness: `triangulated`.** Fig 5 :43 plus Kuhn's five criteria and Rosch's maximally informative basic-level categories. Three different frames, low overlap, same bargain.
- **Domain 2, community uptake: `triangulated`.** Fig 2 :41, Fig 7 :35 and Fig 11 :39-40, corroborated independently by Fleck's next-generation condition, Star and Griesemer's boundary objects, and the Convention entry's account of self-perpetuating regularities.
- **Domain 3, verification: `single-source` inside the framework, `gap` at the naming level.** This audit and the second seat share one evidence pool, so their agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. No figure line selects a specific winning category.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that wide use makes a category true. Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one".
- Not that this settles any historical case. No archive, instrument or measurement was used.
- Not that prestige or authority is a determinant of truth. Prestige can fund a trial; it cannot settle one (framework essay, `05_what_shape_means.md` §4).
- Not a theory of everything. Each room closes some of the gap and no finite number arrives (Fig 7 :30-31).
- Not grounded. Every substantive claim here is `specified` or `general`.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a checkable historical measure, not more agreement. Following *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Bargain test:** does the category reduce the community's description length or residual once introduced, against a documented baseline?
- **Contact test:** which instrument, count, prediction or training record changed when it was introduced, and can that be dated?
- **Room test:** did each adopting community differ in closure from the last and select against a surfaced residual, or was the chain a mirror (Fig 7 :36)?
- **Boundary test:** does the category now decide an outcome, and if so what anchor and check were installed (Fig 10 :47-48)?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a category that stays used without paying its bargain; one whose use changes no contact; wide agreement settling a fact with no ground; a figure line that names a winner; a route readable without a record.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** A category is a visible guess at a community's compression axes, not nature's own list. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fleck's thought styles are often incommensurable, and the SEP entry notes that what is a fact in one collective may not exist in another. The answer stops where the available instruments can no longer discriminate, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which scientific category will win, and it does not mistake popularity for truth. It says that "widely accepted and used" is a convention, so agreement about the shared meaning is the product, not the evidence; that any category that stays useful must pay a description-length bargain, must change contact in a habitat of instruments and teaching, must be selected between different communities rather than copied down a chain of identical ones, and must arrive with a recorded lineage, because a derived term and an imported term look the same in the contents. Once a category starts deciding outcomes, its popularity stops being a check and an anchor is owed. What the framework offers is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
