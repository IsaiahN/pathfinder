## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what determines the spread differential of a new category: it either becomes a working coin of a community (papers, instruments, textbooks, codes, a next generation) or disappears. Option A: treat wide acceptance as evidence that the category is right. Option B: treat wide acceptance as a convention, keep the truth verdict separate, and give the shape any durable category must have while refusing to name a winner. What each leads to: A mistakes agreement for an anchor and lets a category that decides outcomes go unchecked; B sends a checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The spread differential of a proposed category. Arity: two conditions bound (a term worth having, a community that takes it up), so a **union compound**, not one hard problem: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :55-57). Symmetry: uptake is directed (proposer to community) and threshold-like, closer to `→` sequence and `⋛` comparison than to a symmetric `+`. Scale: first order inside a room, second order across rooms into a habitat.
- **Domain 1, the mint (term worthiness).** Actors: candidate category φ, library Γ, three guards, the MDL acceptance test. Currency: description length in bits and leftover residual. Anchor: the ground, "whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). Question type: partition, whether the term is licensed.
- **Domain 2, the convention (community uptake).** Actors: communities, rooms, habitats, instruments, textbooks, next generation. Currency: shared meaning and usage. Anchor: the ground for truth; agreement itself is the product for convention. Question type: convention, not verdict.
- **Domain 3, verification / outreach.** Actors: historians, expert readers, this simulated second seat. Currency: records and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (formula step 2); pay the bargain before spread (Fig 5, formula steps 3 and 5); select between rooms after a room has surfaced a residual (Fig 7 :35); record the route last (Fig 8 :50-52).

Kuhn's consensus account and Fig 7 :35 likewise agree after re-census: his consensus follows puzzle-solving power, which is selection, not mirroring.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Wide acceptance is a convention, not a verdict. Agreement is the product for a shared meaning and disqualified as evidence of truth when the frames share a pool. Durable uptake needs a term that pays its description-length bargain, a habitat whose contact it changes, selection between rooms rather than mirror handoff, and a recorded route. A category that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention and owes an anchor. No figure line names the winner, so the naming determinant is a `gap`: unreachable here, not silence. All claims are `specified` or `general`; none is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is treating a category's popularity as a ground and letting an outcome-deciding convention go unchecked. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is reduced by leading with the shape and its refuters and by demanding an anchor when an outcome attaches.
- **undoable:** yes. The naming claim is not made, so nothing must be withdrawn to correct it. The four constraints are independently refutable; a failed constraint can be narrowed without touching the others. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be removed, only refused, which lowers the reach without deleting the record (Fig 6 :42-43).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A dated measure showing that a category reduced description length or residual on introduction would settle the bargain test; a dated instrument or count would settle the contact test; a reconstructed chain of genuinely different rooms would settle the selection test; a discriminating instrument would name the winner and close the `gap`. A demonstration that wide agreement alone settled a fact, or that a category deciding outcomes stayed checkable by popularity, would break the split. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
