# The Tether's answer on RNA parasites

**Mode: `general` on the relational laws, `specified` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical mechanism. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An RNA replicase copies templates *in trans*. The property that gets a sequence copied (template attractiveness) comes apart from the property that keeps the system going (catalytic contribution). The mismatch is R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| per molecular slot, not aggregated: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: information maintenance, selection direction and habitat arrangement all bind at once (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape

1. **Parasitism is a relation, not a kind.** Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* A sequence is parasitic relative to the residual "catalytic function maintained under copying". There is no standing list of bad molecules. Mode `general`.
2. **The fix must change contact, not just the agent.** Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Higher fidelity without a changed selection regime is an isolated-agent improvement. What must change is what a sequence has to do to be copied. Mode `specified`.
3. **Errors cannot be excluded; an actor or regime can be introduced.** Fig 11 :61 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* A compartment, a spatial structure or a cooperative loop can be added. Error generation itself cannot be subtracted. So this is permanent work, not a solved state. Mode `general`.
4. **The working unit is a closed catalytic loop.** Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* The catalytic motif is the functional group (Fig 12 :123). The defect is the **absence** of the returning contribution (`−`), not a refusal (`¬`); see the retraction in `01` R1. Mode `specified`.
5. **Selection must be between rooms, and the signal per slot.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Inside one mixed pool, *"blending: novelty swamped to the mean"* (Fig 7 :14). Compartments or spatial waves create the low-overlap rooms the ground can vote between. The anchor is a function assay, not a copy count. Mode `specified`.
6. **The error threshold is a filter, not a verdict.** Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The World agrees from population genetics: Tejero et al. 2011 show *"entry into error catastrophe is the selection of the mutant phenotype because of the selective advantage provided by its greater mutational robustness."* The system is not overwhelmed by an absence of selection; selection changed target. Mode `general`.
7. **The historical mechanism is a declared `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework returns the four constraints above, not the name of the arrangement that actually operated. Unreached, not unreachable.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a particular molecule or vesicle was *the* origin-of-life answer. That is a name where a shape is available.
- Not that defective RNAs can be eliminated. Chemistry keeps generating them (Pathak and Nagy 2009: DI-RNAs *"are created spontaneously during the replication of the viral genome"*).
- Not that error catastrophe is a failure of selection. Selection changes level and target (Tejero et al. 2011).
- Not that copy abundance is a ground. It is a reading that cannot comment on its source (*The Hard Problem is About Source*, verified 2026-11-23).
- Not `grounded`. No function assay was run for this derivation and no simulation was executed by this agent.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at the molecular level, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not a debate, resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Function assay:** does a sequence contribute catalytic work to the loop, or is it copied only? An assay that separates these names the slot.
- **Per-slot test:** does the persistence of function track per-sequence or per-compartment residuals rather than total copy number? If an aggregate suffices, Fig 1 :19-22 fails here.
- **Between-room test:** do compartments or spatial patches with different internal compositions survive differently? If a well-mixed system persists indefinitely under the same mutation pressure, the habitat shape fails.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` §4: invariant valence across a changed residual; isolated-agent fix works; all errors eliminated; aggregate copy count sufficient; a single intervention clears the whole union.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is fully stated and checkable, but the historical arrangement is not. The instruments that would name it are not held; Fig 6 :34 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* The world literature supplies candidates (hypercycles, stochastic correctors, spatial waves, DI-RNA dynamics) with provenance as models, and Szostak et al. 2016 state plainly that hypercycles have not been demonstrated experimentally. This derivation stops there and records the lineage. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the molecule or vesicle that saved the first replicators, and it does not claim the question is settled. It says that a "parasite" is not a kind of molecule but a relation to what the system is keeping, so the fix cannot be a better polymerase alone: contact has to change. Errors cannot be excluded from a noisy chemistry, so the work is permanent, and what can be added is a compartment, a spatial wave or a closed catalytic loop in which a sequence is copied only as its catalytic contribution is registered. Selection has to act between compartments rather than only inside one mixed pool, and the signal has to be read per sequence or per compartment, because an average copy count hides it. What looks like being overwhelmed is selection changing target, not selection stopping. The framework returns that shape, the refuters written in advance and the kind of instrument that would close it. The historical mechanism stays open until the ground settles it.
