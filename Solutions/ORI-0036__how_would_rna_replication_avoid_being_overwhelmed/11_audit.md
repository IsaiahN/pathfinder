## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: parasite status is relational; change contact; errors cannot be excluded; closed loop; between-room selection; per-slot reading; threshold is a filter; mechanism is a `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete origin-of-life claim of the form "vesicles plus a catalytic loop plus a function assay solve the parasite problem".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **sign of the catalytic loop** (Fig 12 :121) is a bond-vocabulary binding; a chemical rate constant is not a sign without an instrument that decides what counts as closing;
- the **identity of the slot** in "per slot" (Fig 1 :19) changes with scale: a molecular species slot is not a compartment slot, and the lossy membrane (Fig 4) is where they part company;
- the **function assay** that anchors D4 is named but not built, so concretisation cannot pay for it (Fig 6 :34: no edge to extend from where nothing registers).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: parasitic status is relative to the residual and cannot be a standing molecular kind, so an isolated-agent fix cannot be the whole answer. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
