## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (verified this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "change the habitat so catalytic valence is registered under copying" is a shape. "The origin of life used lipid vesicles with a particular replicase" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (verified): if you do not know the right terms, use the vocabulary you do have that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* In this derivation the bootstrap went from "defective molecule" to "relation with relative valence" to "selection between rooms" to "closed catalytic loop".
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar extract): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly what the three refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` do.
- *Library of Babel* (verified) on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it"*.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (exemplar extract): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Here the six are not at issue. What is at issue is which *arrangement* over chemical kinds is licensed. Naming vesicles, thermal gradients or a particular ribozyme network as *the* answer would be a name where only a shape is available. The world literature supplies names as candidates, but with provenance: Szostak et al. 2016 and Colizzi and Hogeweg 2016 are explicitly models, not ground readings of the historical origin.

## 4. Why the instrument gap is a source problem, not an argument problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (verified this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a count of RNA copies is a reading that cannot comment on the source. The same abundance profile can arise from a functional catalytic community or from a parasitic bloom. What is owed is a source-discriminating assay that separates a catalytic motif from a copied-but-inert variant, not another theoretical argument. That assay is the missing anchor of D4 in `01`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (verified): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q answered: what does "shape" mean for this question?

**It means the set of constraints any answer must satisfy, stated before the mechanism is named.** For ORI-0036 the shape has four parts:

1. parasitic status is a relation to a residual, not a standing molecular kind;
2. contact must change (habitat or selection regime), because an isolated-agent fix cannot change contact;
3. error generation cannot be excluded, so the work is permanent;
4. selection must be between rooms and signalled per slot, with the anchor a function assay rather than a copy count.

The framework does **not** return which chemical arrangement satisfied these constraints. That slot stays `gap`.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming *any* mechanism. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Compartment, spatial structure and catalytic closure are not missing primitives in the sense the README means; they are candidate arrangements described in the world frame. What is forbidden is claiming the framework derives or names the historical winner, and claiming an import that adds no atom moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*). My §4 source-discrimination point is **not** retracted: the missing thing there is a discriminator, and no amount of argument is a spectrometer. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (verified): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a function assay or a controlled experiment, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (verified): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
