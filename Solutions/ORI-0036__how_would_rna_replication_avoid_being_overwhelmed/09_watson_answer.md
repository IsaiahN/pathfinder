## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that RNA replication can be overwhelmed by defective or parasitic sequences. Restate the effect: the property that gets a sequence copied (template attractiveness) comes apart from the property that keeps the system going (catalytic contribution). R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| is indexed per molecular slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Option A: treat defects as a standing class to be excluded, fix the replicator in isolation. Option B: treat parasitic status as a relation to the residual and change the habitat or selection regime so the functional signal is registered. What each leads to: A leaves contact unchanged and treats permanent error generation as a removable intruder; B keeps the errors but changes what a sequence must do to be copied, and declares the historical mechanism a `gap`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the naive framing does not. Parasitic status is a relation to a residual, so the answer is not a standing class of molecules to eliminate. An isolated-agent fix such as higher fidelity clears one condition of a union but does not change contact. Errors are intrinsic to a finite noisy chemistry and can be rearranged but not excluded, so the work is permanent. Any working solution is a closed catalytic loop whose catalytic motif is a functional group, with defect as absence (`−`) rather than refusal (`¬`), selected between compartments or spatial patches and read per slot against a function assay rather than a copy count. What looks like being overwhelmed is selection changing target, not selection stopping. The specific historical arrangement is a declared `gap`: unreached, not unreachable. This is a `general` / `specified` / `gap` result, nowhere `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a theory before a characterised residual (Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*), and the cost of applying a single lever to a union that needs three conditions. Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out (Fig 6 amendment line 42). The `gap` marker is explicit, so a later ground reading can fill it without withdrawing the shape. The corrected packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carries the mode and refuters.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A function assay showing that copy number fully predicts function persistence would break the per-slot rule; a well-mixed system maintaining function indefinitely at high mutation would break the habitat claim; an invariant valence across a changed residual would break the relational law; a mechanism shown necessary and sufficient under plausible prebiotic conditions would convert the `gap` to a candidate and then, only if it pays, to `grounded`. Prestige or expert agreement is not such an instrument.
