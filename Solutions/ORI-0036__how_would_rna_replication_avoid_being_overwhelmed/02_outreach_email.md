# Outreach draft: check the shape first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive a colleague's read. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold up.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about RNA parasites: what would refute it?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about the RNA-world parasite problem that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` as a shape, not `grounded`):** a parasitic sequence is not a molecular kind with a fixed badness. Its valence is relative to what the system is trying to keep, here the catalytic function that survives copying. The framework's habitat figure states this directly: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds" (Fig 11 :50). From that it follows that a fix cannot be an agent-only improvement such as a more accurate polymerase while the selection regime stays the same. What has to change is contact: a compartment, a spatial structure, or a catalytic arrangement in which a sequence is copied only as its catalytic contribution is registered. Errors cannot be excluded from a noisy chemistry, so this is permanent work, not a solved state.
>
> In structural terms the working arrangement is a closed catalytic loop, because "the output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes" (Fig 12 :121), and selection has to act between compartments or spatial patches rather than only inside one mixed pool, because "a room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes" (Fig 7 :35).
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework names which arrangement actually operated at the origin of life. It returns a shape, not a name. Compartmentalisation, spatial self-organisation and catalytic cooperation are kinds of arrangement, not a historical winner. I am also not claiming that an error threshold is a catastrophic failure of selection: it is a budget filter, and the ground decides. I am not claiming this is settled by experiment.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. show that a sequence's valence toward catalytic function is invariant across a documented change of resource or host (breaks Fig 11 :49-51);
> 2. demonstrate a well-mixed, uncompartmentalised replicator system that keeps catalytic function and information indefinitely at high mutation, with no between-room selection and no catalytic closure (breaks the habitat claim);
> 3. show that a total copy count fully predicts persistence of function, leaving no per-sequence or per-compartment signal (breaks Fig 1 :19-22).
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Signed, [name]

## Two-stage offer, explicit

- **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and its three refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not this summary, are the authority.
- **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A stage 2 reply is a conversation, never corroboration. Later agreement is never pooled as a witness (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
