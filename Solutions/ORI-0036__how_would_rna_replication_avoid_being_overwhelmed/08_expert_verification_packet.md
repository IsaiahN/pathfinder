# Expert verification packet: the Tether on RNA parasites

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in RNA parasite resistance can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the relational law, `specified` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical mechanism. Not `grounded`.** No function assay was run for this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A defective or parasitic RNA sequence is not a standing molecular kind with fixed badness. Its valence is relative to the residual, here "catalytic function maintained under copying". It follows that an isolated-agent fix such as higher fidelity cannot solve the problem while the selection regime is unchanged. Contact must change: what is added is a compartment, a spatial structure or a closed catalytic loop in which a sequence is copied only as its catalytic contribution is registered. Error generation cannot be excluded, so the work is permanent. Selection must act between rooms, and the signal must be read per slot, because an aggregate copy count hides which molecules carry function. What is called being "overwhelmed" is selection changing target, not selection stopping. The framework does not name which arrangement historically did it; that slot is a declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** parasitism is a relation to a residual, not an intrinsic property of a molecular kind; an isolated-agent improvement cannot fix it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Also Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* And Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **Refuter:** show a molecule whose valence toward catalytic function is invariant across a documented change of resource or host; or demonstrate a well-mixed system that keeps function under high mutation through a fidelity increase alone, with no habitat or regime change.

### C2. Structural; checkable in Figure 12 and in the replicator literature

- **Statement:** the working unit is a catalyst in a closed cycle; the catalytic motif is a functional group; the defect is the absence of the returning contribution, not the refusal of a claim. Replicator and parasite are isomers over the shared atom set in the strong reading, members of a functional group in the weak reading.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Operators table: *"A with B removed or absent, which is the absence of an operand. Negation is the refusal of a claim."*
- **What to check:** does a linear catalytic cascade ever match a closed loop under the same copying error rate? Does an actively interfering RNA require `¬` rather than `−`? Is the isomer reading too strong when parasites are modelled as a separate class (Colizzi and Hogeweg 2016)?
- **Refuter:** a linear cascade that maintains function as well as a closed loop; or a parasite written without an atom the replicator requires (kills the isomer claim, leaves the functional-group claim); or evidence that absence alone never suffices and active refusal is always required.

### C3. Population and habitat; checkable against experiments and models

- **Statement:** selection must act between compartments or spatial patches, and the signal must be indexed per slot; the error threshold is a budget filter, not the verdict. The historical mechanism is a `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
- **What to check:** do controlled compartment or spatial experiments show differential survival by internal composition? Does a total copy count ever fully predict persistence of function? Is there an experiment that names one mechanism as necessary and sufficient in a plausible prebiotic setting?
- **Refuter:** a well-mixed system maintaining function and information indefinitely at high mutation with no between-room selection; or an aggregate statistic that leaves no per-slot residual; or a ground-reading experiment that names the historical arrangement (which would convert the `gap` to a candidate).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a particular historical mechanism. Vesicles, thermal gradients, hypercycles and spatial waves are candidate kinds of arrangement, not a name returned by the framework.
- Not that defective RNAs can be eliminated. They are generated by the copying process itself (Pathak and Nagy 2009: DI-RNAs *"are created spontaneously during the replication of the viral genome"*).
- Not that error catastrophe is a failure of selection. It is selection changing target (Tejero et al. 2011).
- Not that copy abundance is a ground. It is a lossy reading of source.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement in this run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (verified 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Function assay:** separate a sequence that contributes catalytic work from one that is merely copied. That names the slot.
2. **Per-slot test:** show that function persistence tracks per-sequence or per-compartment residuals, not total copy number.
3. **Between-room test:** show differential survival of compartments or spatial patches by internal composition, or show that a well-mixed system does not need it.
4. **Mechanism test:** identify one arrangement as necessary and sufficient under plausible prebiotic conditions. That converts the `gap`.
