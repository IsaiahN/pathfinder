## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage 1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage 1 shape survives.
