## 0. The claim under audit, stated as an effect

**The residual R.** In a system where an RNA catalyst replicates other RNA molecules *in trans*, the observation is that copies appear which carry the template features that attract the replicase but do not return catalytic help. The prediction carried by the replication frame is that what gets copied is what carries function. The mismatch is R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| per molecular slot. It is indexed per sequence species and per compartment, never aggregated: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Counting total copies is exactly the aggregation that hides the live signal.

**Two options and what each leads to.**

- **Option A, improve the replicator in isolation.** Assume the fix is a better polymerase or higher fidelity. Leads to: errors are treated as a standing class of bad molecules to be excluded; the habitat, the selection regime and the population structure are left unchanged. Fig 11 :40 says an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. The world literature also says a fully error-free prebiotic replicase is not available (Eigen's paradox, read via Szostak, Wasik and Blazewicz 2016).
- **Option B, change the habitat.** Treat the defective sequence as a relation whose valence depends on the residual, and reattach the copy signal to catalytic contribution by adding an actor or a selection regime (compartment, spatial structure, catalytic closure). Leads to: errors keep being generated, but the system's contact with the functional signal is maintained. This is permanent work, not a solved state, because exclusion is not available (Fig 11 :61).

**What an answer would look like (bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, Formula step "Direction").** A checkable answer must state: (i) what is counted as a slot, (ii) what anchor discriminates function from mere abundance, (iii) what actor or regime is introduced, and (iv) what observation would reverse the claim. Anything that only names a molecule class fails (i) and (ii). I bind this before the run so it can accrue a residual like any other term.

**Mode declaration.** `general` for the claim that parasitism is relational; `specified` for the claim that any working solution must change contact through habitat or selection regime; `gap` for which historical arrangement did it. Mode is stated on every substantive claim below.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** A three-place relation (catalytic molecule, template, shared resource) plus a population term and a habitat term. This is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* and Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Symmetry.** Asymmetric. Catalysis is directed (A catalyses B), and the "parasite" designation is a threshold comparison between what a sequence takes and what it gives, not a symmetric join. The relevant bonds are ordered (production `⇒`, sequence `→`, comparison `⋛`), not `+` or `≡`.
- **Scale.** Molecular (one sequence), population (quasispecies or replicator community), habitat (compartment, surface, spatial wave). The transform between scales is lossy: Fig 4 :20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*

### 1.3 Partition or union, and dependency order

**Union, not partition.** Fidelity (D1/D4), selection direction (D2) and habitat regime (D3) all bind at once. A single lever that clears one condition leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). So "avoid being overwhelming" must be unbundled into: keep the information, keep selection pointing at function, keep the arrangement that does both.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** 1 perception: can a discriminator tell functional from copied-only (D4 instrument, missing); 2 vocabulary: terms for catalyst, template, compartment (D1); 3 objective: progress is maintenance of catalytic function, not abundance of copies (D2); 4 planning: the arrangement (D3); 5 learn and carry: settled molecules become atoms at the next level (Fig 12 :104). The chain classically breaks between 2 and 3: replication can be perceived, but the instrument that discriminates the source of function is absent. Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Effort spent above the break (for example more fidelity) arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19 *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1. Parasites are relations, not kinds (decisive, `general`)

Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

The question's grammar invites a standing class: defective sequences and parasitic sequences. The figures forbid that class. A sequence is parasitic relative to the residual, which here is "catalytic function maintained under copying". If the residual changes (a different resource, a different host, a different catalytic requirement), the same sequence can change valence. The world record agrees from a different field: Pathak and Nagy (2009, *Viruses*) report that defective interfering RNAs *"can attenuate the symptoms caused by the helper virus"*, that is, a sequence defined as parasitic can improve the system-level outcome.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a molecular kind whose valence toward a maintained catalytic function is invariant across a documented change of resource, host or residual. That breaks Fig 11 :49-51 and dissolves this finding.

### F2. The fix cannot be an agent-only improvement (`specified`)

Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Raising replication fidelity alone is an agent-only improvement if it leaves the selection regime and the habitat unchanged. The structural claim is that contact must change: what must be true is that a sequence cannot be copied without the system's functional signal being registered. Mode `specified`, ungrounded. Szostak, Wasik and Blazewicz (2016) report the standing estimate that enzyme-free replication is limited to about 100 bases, insufficient to encode a high-fidelity replicase, which is the world-level reason the isolated-agent route stalls.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated system in which a fidelity increase alone maintains catalytic function indefinitely under high mutation, with no habitat or regime change and no catalytic closure. Mode then reverses.

### F3. Introduction is available; exclusion is not (`general`)

Fig 11 :61 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
Fig 13 :118 *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."*

Error generation is intrinsic to a finite, noisy chemistry, not an intruder that can be subtracted. What can be added is an actor or arrangement: a compartment, a spatial gradient, a cooperative catalytic loop. The practical consequence is that "avoid being overwhelmed" is permanent work rather than a solved state. The world record agrees: Pathak and Nagy write that DI-RNAs *"are created spontaneously during the replication of the viral genome"*, so the generating process is not removable by excluding the products.

**Refuter:** a chemically demonstrated mechanism that eliminates error generation itself rather than rearranging its consequences, with no permanent management. That would break the asymmetry claim as applied here.

### F4. The working structure must be a closed loop with the right sign (`specified`)

Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

A cooperative catalytic network is the figure's catalyst plus cycle: each part survives its own use, and the output re-enters as input. A defective sequence is the local flip of the loop's sign; it is copied but does not regenerate the part that copied it. The catalytic motif is the functional group that fixes the outcome. This is why the fix is a structural arrangement, not a name. Szostak et al. (2016) state that *"Parasitic branches are species coupled to a cycle that do not provide any advantage to the reproduction of a cycle, which, in turn, makes them useless and decreases the selective value of the system."*

**Refuter:** a demonstration that a purely linear (chain) catalytic arrangement, with no re-entry and no surviving part, maintains function as well as a closed loop under the same copying error rate. That breaks the cycle finding.

### F5. Selection must be between rooms, not only inside one (`specified`)

Fig 7 :14 *"blending: novelty swamped to the mean"*
Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

In one well-mixed room, local agreement about which sequences are abundant is variation, not a verdict. Compartments and spatial waves create low-overlap rooms selected against the residual. World corroboration from three independent fields: Colizzi and Hogeweg (2016, *PLoS Comput Biol*) show that *"parasites may not be considered a problem for evolution in a prebiotic system, but a degree of freedom that can be exploited by evolution to enhance the evolvability of replicators, by means of emergent levels of selection"*; Szostak et al. (2016) record that compartmentalised models and the stochastic corrector are robust against parasites; Hardin (1968, *Science*) gives the ecology of a shared commons in which *"Freedom in a commons brings ruin to all."*

**Refuter:** a well-mixed, uncompartmentalised system maintaining catalytic function and information indefinitely at high mutation, with no between-room selection and no catalytic closure. That breaks this finding and F2 together.

### F6. The signal is per slot; abundance is the wrong anchor (`specified`)

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

The anchor for this question is a function assay, not a copy count. A count of total RNA says nothing about which molecules carry the catalytic motif. Tejero, Marín and Montero (2011, *BMC Evol Biol*) show that what looks like collapse is a selective transition: *"entry into error catastrophe is the selection of the mutant phenotype because of the selective advantage provided by its greater mutational robustness."* The "master sequence lost" statistic is an aggregate; the figure requires the per-slot reading that explains it.

**Refuter:** a system where an aggregate copy-number statistic fully predicts persistence of function with no residual left at the per-sequence or per-compartment level. That breaks Fig 1 :19-22 here.

### F7. The error threshold is a filter, not a verdict (`general`)

Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

The mutation rate and the error threshold price the budget. They do not decide which sequence wins; the ground does. Tejero et al. (2011) corroborate from population genetics: error catastrophe is selection changing targets, not natural selection ceasing. So the phrase "overwhelmed" should be rewritten as "the selection target moved to a more robust but less functional class". That is a claim about which anchor is being ranged against, not about a catastrophic failure of selection.

**Refuter:** a controlled system in which crossing a mutation-rate threshold destroys function while all selection coefficients and per-slot valences remain unchanged. That restores the naive "overwhelmed" reading.

### F8. This is a union and must be unbundled before search (`general`)

Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

Three conditions conjoin: maintain information (D1/D4), keep selection aimed at function (D2), supply an arrangement that links them (D3). Hypercycles, compartments and spatial structure are not rival magic bullets; they are different answers to different conjuncts. Szostak et al. (2016) record the object lesson: compartmentalised package models are robust to parasites yet *"package models do not solve the error threshold problem that originally motivated the hypercycle."* A single lever therefore clears one condition and leaves the intersections.

**Refuter:** a single intervention that restores all three conditions and leaves no categorically different residue (Fig 9 :57). Until then the union reading stands.

### F9. The specific historical mechanism is a `gap`, not a hidden answer

Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 6 :34 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*
Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

The figures can return the shape: relational valence, changed contact, closed loop, between-room selection. They cannot return the name of the arrangement that actually operated at the origin of life. That requires a source-discriminating instrument at the molecular level (Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). We report `gap` for that slot. Unreached, not unreachable.

**Refuter for the gap declaration:** a ground-reading experiment that identifies one mechanism as necessary and sufficient in a plausible prebiotic setting. That converts the slot from `gap` to `candidate` and then to `grounded` only if it pays.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A molecular kind is shown to have invariant valence toward catalytic function across a documented change of residual, breaking Fig 11 :49-51.
2. A well-mixed, uncompartmentalised replicator system maintains catalytic function and information indefinitely at high mutation with no between-room selection and no catalytic closure, breaking F2 and F5.
3. An aggregate copy-number statistic fully predicts persistence of function, leaving no per-slot residual, breaking Fig 1 :19-22.
4. Error generation itself is eliminated (not merely rearranged) so that management is no longer permanent work, breaking F3.
5. A source-discriminating instrument at the molecular level shows that the sequences labelled defective carry the catalytic function, inverting the valence at the ground.
6. A single intervention restores information, selection direction and arrangement with no categorically different residue, breaking F8.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
