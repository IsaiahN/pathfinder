# Expert verification packet - the Tether on informal authority and the state

**What this is:** a claim in a form an institutional scholar, legal pluralist or empirically minded public official can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No prospective behavioral measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Trust is a relation between a citizen, their habitat and the forum they actually use, not a stock an authority owns and not a national mood. Two conditions have to hold at once for a citizen to keep relying on a forum in a given domain: the forum's channel to what actually resolves that citizen's matter stays reachable, meaning its readings are not stale, saturated or pooled into an average; and an alternative closure in the citizen's habitat holds a composing term for that matter, so the citizen's reach is larger through that route than through the state one. These are two different failure modes with two different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. The forum that produced the reliance can change sign when the matter changes, so a trusted institution and an obstacle can be the same object. The anchor is behavioral reliance plus independently measured resolution, not a survey score. The claim is a shape that any candidate answer must have; it does not name which forum wins in a named community.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 10 and 2

- **Statement:** capability is a property of citizen-and-habitat, so reliance cannot be explained by the authority alone; quick loss of state reliance is a failure of the channel, not a decay of the governed ground; the anchor is behavioral reliance plus resolution, not a self-reported trust number.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
  - Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
  - Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same line names *"a market, a patient outcome"* as instances; Fig 2 :37 names human judgment as the right anchor for a question about people.
  - Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **Refuter:** a citizen whose reliance on an informal authority is unchanged across a habitat substitution that removes the state channel's failure; or a citizen whose reliance shifts while both bracket fidelity and ground pressure measure high and non-declining; or a national trust score that predicts per citizen and prospectively which forum will be used, with no behavioral measurement. Any of these fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one institutional-facing check

- **Statement:** internal agreement is not evidence, and a forum that keeps the same closure stops progressing; availability of a differing closure explains the smaller residual because composition inside a closure cannot add an atom.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :36 - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** the legal-pluralism literature reports that *"individuals would choose to bring their legal claims under the system that they thought would offer them the best advantage"*, and the polycentric-law literature describes providers that *"compete or overlap in a given jurisdiction"*. Do those two claims not already name both the choice rule and the availability condition described in Fig 6 :23? A "no" to both is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a citizen whose reach is enlarged by an imported atom from a differing closure, who still routes the same domain to the state with a larger measured residual across repeated trials; or a forum whose measured valence is invariant across a documented change of matter and habitat. Either breaks idempotence or the valence rule and C2 with it.

### C3 - Structure, convention and competing explanations

- **Statement:** the union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; two forums with identical atoms but different bonds are isomers, so a forum inventory alone cannot predict reliance; a legitimacy convention becomes a verdict claim as soon as it decides where a matter goes; agreement from one evidence pool is not corroboration.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 2 :41 - *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 2 :43 - *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
  - Fig 8 :50 - *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*
  - Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves no categorically different residue; or two forums with identical atoms and identical bonds whose served slots diverge with no difference in contact or imported atoms, which would move the explanation out of composition and into habitat alone.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a theory of everything about institutions, and no final or unique theory claim.
- Not a national average. No claim about the percentage of variance attributable to tradition, performance, identity or participation.
- Not that informal authorities are inherently legitimate, or that the state is inherently illegitimate. Both are candidate libraries and channels; neither settles reliance alone.
- Not that any specific forum is the answer. That is a `gap` at the instrument boundary, not a hidden answer.
- Not that the institutional literature independently corroborates this. Legal pluralism, procedural justice, Weber types, polycentric law and everyday resistance share one broad evidence pool. The trust literature from social psychology is lower overlap but still not a measurement of these citizens.
- Not that this packet, produced with an LLM, is submittable or publishable as authored work. See the AI-policy note in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-citizen-slot study that measures C1 and C2 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the switch. Concretely:

1. **Channel test:** does the state's reading of the citizen's matter degrade while other forums keep resolving matters? Indicators: stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings (Fig 10 :25-26).
2. **Pressure and fidelity test:** are bracket fidelity and ground pressure both nonzero at each handoff? Fig 7 :37-39 says zero either stops the chain.
3. **Import test:** did any new atom enter the citizen's reach from a closure that differs, before the reliance switch? If not, C2 is refuted.
4. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish a failed state channel from an available alternative closure from a convention about standing from coercion, when all four produce the same routing reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 10 :24 and :26, Fig 2 :29 and :43, Fig 7 :13 and :36, Fig 8 :32 and :50, Fig 6 :23, Fig 9 :57 and Fig 12 :117 and :119. Eleven short passages, about eight minutes.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of forum choice: is there a documented case of a larger reach through a differing closure with no state-channel failure and no better measured resolution? About five minutes.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish the four sources of the same routing reading using observable behavioral data, or say why none exists. About five minutes.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
