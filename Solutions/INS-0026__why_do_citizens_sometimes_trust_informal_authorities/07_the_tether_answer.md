# The Tether's answer on informal authority and the state

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No behavioral, source-discriminating measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a citizen expects from an authority and what that authority actually delivers stays smaller when the citizen relies on a chief, elder, religious judge, mediator or informal provider in some slots and domains, and smaller when they rely on the state in others. R is indexed per citizen and per domain, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **which forum keeps the residual small**, not about the level of trust in institutions.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the forum's channel to what actually resolves this citizen's matter stays reachable.
- **C2:** an alternative closure in the citizen's habitat already holds a composing term for this matter, so the citizen's reach is larger through that route.

A third element, the valence of the forum relative to the matter (Fig 11 :49-51), is carried inside both conditions rather than treated as a separate lever.

## 2. The shape

1. **Trust is not a stock an authority owns.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A list of institutional attributes is Γ, a candidate library whose reach must be tested against the ground, not the answer.
2. **Quick loss of state reliance is channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. The state stops hearing which citizen is being failed, usually by pooling the very readings that would say where to look. Fig 7 :37-39 gives the two-factor form: bracket fidelity times ground pressure, and zero either stops the chain.
3. **Long reliance is a chain of differing rooms.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* A service that keeps handing itself the same review keeps agreeing with itself and stops travelling.
4. **The alternative forum need not be better; it must compose differently.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal procedural refinement adds the local atom. Kinship, known history and a customary rule the parties already accept are not distractions from the real work; they are the operators that move the wall.
5. **The strength and the obstacle are the same object.** Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual and no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and *"nothing records which a term is"*. The chief who settles a land dispute cheaply can be the person who pressures a complainant into silence in the same community.
6. **Behavioral reliance, not survey mood, is the anchor.** Fig 2 :29 names a *"market, a patient outcome"* among anchors; :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A trust survey is a frame to be ranged against the ground separately (Fig 1 :27). Fig 2 :37 makes human judgment the right anchor for a question constitutively about people.
7. **The convention becomes a verdict when it routes a dispute.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; :43: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. A belief in standing is fine as shared meaning; the moment it decides where a matter goes, it is a claim about the world and must be checked.
8. **The break is usually in the middle of the chain.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary/objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. More reform budget lands above the break.
9. **Two forums with the same atom can still differ.** Under Fig 12 :117 they are isomers if the bonds differ: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance."* This is why a forum inventory alone cannot predict reliance.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective behavioral measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which forum, tradition or reform wins in a named community. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not a national score. It makes no claim about the percentage of trust attributable to tradition, performance, identity or participation. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that informal authorities are inherently legitimate, or that the state is inherently illegitimate. They are Γ and a channel respectively; neither settles reliance on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not corroboration by agreement. Legal pluralism, procedural justice, Weber types, polycentric law and everyday resistance share one broad evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40).
- Not a mechanism for routing under coercion. That is a source question, and it is a `gap`, not a hidden answer.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the reading is which forum the citizen used; the candidate sources are state-channel failure, alternative-closure reach, convention about standing, and coercion or dependency.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. reliance on an informal authority unchanged when the state channel measures reachable and paying;
2. reliance on the state when no alternative forum holds any term for the matter and the state channel measures failing;
3. reliance invariant across a documented change of matter and habitat;
4. one intervention restoring both conditions with nothing categorically different left over;
5. a source-discriminating instrument showing one source, for example coercion alone, in all measured cases;
6. a national trust score predicting per citizen and prospectively with no behavioral measurement;
7. idempotence failing inside a closed set, or a derived sixth term in Fig 13.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per citizen-slot, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the switch would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is owed. Naming a winner from a routing reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The conditions in section 2 are a shape; the source of a given switch is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank institutions or name the forum that wins. It says that trust is a relation, not a stock: a citizen keeps relying on the forum whose channel to what actually resolves their matter stays reachable and whose way of composing the matter already holds a cheaper term, and that same forum can change sign when the matter changes, so the trusted chief and the obstacle can be the same person. The two conditions have two different failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. Quick loss of state reliance is usually the channel going stale, saturated or pooled into a district average; durable informal reliance is a chain of forums that each differ from the last and are selected against the residual the last one surfaced. Behavioral reliance plus resolution, not a survey mood, is the anchor. What is offered is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which forum occupies the slot in a named community stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
