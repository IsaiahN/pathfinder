# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no behavioral measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about why people use informal authorities before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about institutional trust that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** trust is not a stock that an authority owns, and it is not a national mood. It is a relation between a citizen, the habitat they live in and the forum they actually use, and it has to be maintained. Two conditions have to hold at once for a citizen to keep relying on a forum in a given domain:
>
> 1. the forum's channel to what actually resolves that citizen's matter stays reachable, meaning its readings are not stale, saturated or pooled into a district average;
> 2. an alternative forum in the citizen's habitat holds a composing term for that matter, so the citizen's reach is larger through that route than through the state one.
>
> The claim is that these are two different failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. A citizen whose state channel has gone stale needs a different instrument from a citizen who has no alternative forum. The further claim is that the forum which produced the trust can change sign when the matter changes, so the trusted institution and the obstacle can be the same object.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which forum wins in a named community, that customary or religious authorities are inherently legitimate, that the state is inherently illegitimate, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of institutional trust. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name. I am also not using a survey score as the anchor; the anchor is behavioral reliance plus independently measured resolution of the matter.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a citizen's reliance on an informal authority is unchanged when the state's channel is independently measured as reachable and paying;
> 2. a citizen relies on the state even when no alternative forum holds any term for their matter and the state channel is measured as failing;
> 3. reliance is invariant across a documented change of the matter and the habitat;
> 4. one intervention restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind;
> 5. a source-discriminating instrument shows the same reliance reading comes from exactly one source, for example coercion alone, in all measured cases;
> 6. a national trust score predicts prospectively and per citizen where they will route the next matter, with no behavioral or outcome measurement, and survives reframing of the survey question.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Two failure modes kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended trust gap is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Behavioral anchor named, survey demoted | *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"* (Fig 1 :27); *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :29) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning forum | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Valence flip stated, not hidden | *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"* (Fig 11 :49) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
