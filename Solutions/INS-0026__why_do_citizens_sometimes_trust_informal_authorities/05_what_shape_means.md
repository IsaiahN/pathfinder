## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for INS-0026 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about institutional reliance that can be stated before any particular authority, tradition, institution or reform is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *reliance stays on the forum whose channel to the citizen's ground is reachable and whose closure already holds a cheaper term for the matter, and the forum's valence changes with the matter.*
- Name, withheld: *the chief, the sharia court, the elders' council, the neighborhood mediator, the informal provider, or the state agency wins here because...*

The distinction matters here because the institutional literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Weber names traditional, charismatic and rational-legal authority; legal pluralism names customary and religious forums; procedural justice names voice, neutrality and trust. Those are bindings from their frames onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "legal pluralism", "forum shopping", "procedural justice", "everyday resistance", "infrapolitics", "traditional authority" and "polycentric governance". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (which forum reduces which citizen's residual in which domain) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating INS-0026 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with one explanation.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when C1 and C2 are stated, the instrument for each is named, the valence rule is stated, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For INS-0026, neither has happened for the identity of a winning forum. There is no prospective, source-discriminating measurement that says "in this community, this matter, this forum won because C1 failed rather than because C2 obtained". The institutional names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error an institutional explanation makes when it reads observed reliance and names its source. A citizen taking a land dispute to a chief is a reading. It is consistent with a state court that has lost touch, with a customary forum that already holds the parties' history, with a convention that the chief has standing, with pressure from a landlord, or with simple convenience. The reading does not determine the source. The literature's habit of naming the source from the reading (tradition, legitimacy, state failure) is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to institutional research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

For outreach this means: a list of famous legal pluralists or renowned institutional economists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. The last line is the author's own agent-system instance of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular authority is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a forum is already in a citizen's habitat, naming it is a binding from the institutional frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) and claiming that the name itself is the answer. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about citizens and forums. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For INS-0026 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes, a valence rule that can flip a trusted forum into an obstacle, and an instrument boundary where the source of the reading is not discriminated.** It is not a list of authority types, not a name of a winning forum, and not a ranking of institutions by legitimacy. The names that appear in the institutional literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
