# WATSON answer, INS-0026

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Why do citizens sometimes trust informal authorities more than the state?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay smaller in some citizen-slots when the citizen relies on a chief, elder, religious judge, mediator or informal provider, and smaller in others when they rely on the state? R is indexed per citizen and per domain, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: behavioral reliance plus independently measured resolution of the matter, not a survey score (Fig 2 :29; Fig 1 :27). Option A says informal authorities possess a legitimacy the state lacks. Option B says the smaller residual is a maintained relation among citizen, habitat and forum. The verdict below is B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** citizens route their reliance to the forum whose residual is smaller, and that residual is smaller when the forum's channel to what actually resolves the citizen's matter stays reachable and when a differing closure already holds a composing term for that matter; the forum's valence changes with the matter, so the trusted institution and the obstacle can be the same object. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`, with `general` on the structural claims. Legal pluralism and the trust literature corroborate the shape after re-mapping the traditional-authority conflict; the conflict did not flip the verdict. Which forum wins in a named community is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and a wasted check. A community failing C1 would be offered alternative forums it does not need, or a community failing C2 would be told to improve state service delivery, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62).
- **Reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) reliance unchanged when the state channel measures reachable and paying, (2) reliance on the state when no alternative forum holds any term for the matter and the state channel is failing, (3) invariance across a change of matter and habitat, (5) a source-discriminating instrument showing a single source such as coercion in all cases, or (6) a survey score predicting per-citizen routing with no behavioral measurement. A source from a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot switching is fully predicted by national trust mood would also reverse it.
