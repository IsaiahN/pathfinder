## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *sustained reliance requires maintaining a relational channel between the citizen and the forum that resolves their matter.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific citizen situation. Result: *listen to local complaints and bring in more local forums.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into two parallel good practices. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a community failing C2 merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Relational channel" does not specify that behavioral reliance plus resolution, not a survey mood, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :27). Coming back down, it permits a service to measure its own success with a quantity it produces.
3. It loses the valence flip. "Bring in more local forums" has no place for the possibility that the old trusted forum changed sign (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
4. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a failed state channel from an available alternative closure from a convention about standing from coercion, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.
5. It loses the convention-to-verdict transition (Fig 2 :41, :43). "Trust" in the abstraction sounds like shared meaning, so the coming-down version never asks whether the belief has started routing disputes.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
