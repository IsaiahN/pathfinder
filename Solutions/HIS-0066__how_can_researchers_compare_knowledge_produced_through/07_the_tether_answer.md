# The Tether's answer on comparing experimental and observational or practice knowledge

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A researcher holds two bodies of knowledge. One comes from intervention, control and random assignment. The other comes from unmanipulated observation over long time and from skill learned by doing. Each works in its own setting. The residual is the comparison: what a claim from one body means for the other.

The failure modes are specific. A pooled score averages away the live difference. A prestige ranking lets one frame score the comparison with a quantity it produces itself. "Incommensurable" stops the search before SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD have been run. Fig 1 :22 says *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :4 says *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

## 2. The shape

1. **Compare by triangulation against a shared anchor, not by averaging or ranking.** Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :29 says *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The two frames range against the anchor separately. Their agreement counts only when they drew on different evidence, since Fig 2 :40 says agreement is *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

2. **Describe the residual before naming the winning method.** Fig 8 :53 says *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 9 :27 says *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Otherwise every result confirms the method chosen in advance.

3. **Transport carries a measurable loss.** Fig 4 :22 says *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30 says *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* A result moved from a controlled setting into a field setting must be reported with its round-trip gap R_T. The gap is not an error to hide; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold.

4. **A controlled experiment is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :66 says *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :70 says *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* An experiment therefore gives real evidence about wiring, and only partial evidence about capability in the field. Fig 11 :68 and :69 name what is invisible: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*.

5. **Where each rule wins on a different subset, split into domains of validity.** Two frames can be locally correct with no shared reading. The honest output is a scope map: experiment as authority for short-horizon counterfactual questions whose habitat was reproduced, observation and practice as authority for long-horizon unmanipulated questions and for the skill of noticing what to measure. This is a union split into its conditions, not a compromise.

6. **Skill is carried by contact, not by a full record.** Fig 4 :25 says *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Both the bench scientist and the field practitioner hold unarticulated skill. A comparison that records only the written method has recorded half of itself.

7. **The two bodies stand as isomers over a declared atom set.** Fig 12 :117 says *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Experiment holds subtraction, production and threshold comparison; practice holds long sequence, co-occurrence and a skilled functional group. Over a shared phenomenon they differ by bond. Their union is second-order, licensed by Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*.

8. **The comparison must be checkable by someone who cannot reconstruct it.** Fig 10 :42 says *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Send the residual and the refuter before the theory. Record the reader's verdict before showing the framework, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that experiments are higher knowledge than observation, or the reverse. The figures rank nothing by prestige; Fig 1 :27 says *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- Not that one body can be translated into the other without loss. That is exactly what the round trip measures.
- Not that all disagreement is one question. Unions split.
- Not that the comparison is `grounded`. No measurement was taken.
- Not that a method name is returned. The framework returns a shape; selecting randomised trials or ethnography is a binding from the methods frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that the whole question is a `gap`. A shared, nameable anchor exists. `gap` remains available per claim, after the three questions in Formula step 7.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating comparison, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding checks are:

- **Blend test:** a pooled estimate that preserves the live experiment/field difference signal and is settled by an anchor neither frame authored.
- **Transport test:** a finding carried into the field with its round-trip gap measured at the ground, not assumed zero.
- **Habitat test:** an experiment that reproduces the full field habitat and preserves capability, or an agent-only improvement that changes contact.
- **Single-rule test:** one method that dominates at the ground across all subsets where the other currently works.
- **Codification test:** a novice who reproduces expert practice from the written record alone with no residual.
- **Standing falsifiers:** the seven listed in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, written before the findings.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** One strain remains: the operand type of `⋛` at second order. The Operators table restricts comparison to *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, while the operands here are settled bodies now serving as atoms. Fig 12 :104 permits the demotion; whether an atom counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument. It is named rather than smoothed.

The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish between a transported experimental finding and a field capability. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank controlled experiments above long-term observation or practice, and it does not offer a conversion formula between them. It says that a comparison must be triangulation of two frames that draw on different evidence against one anchor that neither frame can move; that the residual has to be described before a method is chosen; that carrying a result from the lab into the field loses information and the loss must be measured; that a controlled experiment is a substituted habitat which proves wiring and never field capability; that where each method wins on a different subset the honest result is a map of scopes, not a winner; and that skill travels by contact, not by a complete written record. What it offers is the shape any checkable comparison must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The verdict on any particular comparison stays open until the ground settles it.
