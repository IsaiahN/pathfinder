## Round trip gap R_T

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The measured gap.** A weight is not a round-trip measurement. Fig 4 :30 asks for the loss, not a compromise number.
- **The scope split.** A single recommendation erases the domains of validity that Fig 9 :4 requires.

One thing survives cleanly: two frames must be kept separate and ranged against an anchor that neither can move. That is the residue worth sending.
