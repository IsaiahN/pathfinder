# The Tether's answer on biodiversity loss, resilience, carbon and recovery

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape and separator, `general` on the union framing, `gap` on a universal sign or magnitude. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A decision maker wants to know what is removed when diversity declines and what follows for three outcomes that are measured differently. The verb "affect" is symmetric; each causal claim is ordered (Operators table :15 versus :33; Fig 2 :39). The three outcomes are a union, not one question: Fig 9 :4-5 says to "Split it rather than search", and Fig 9 :55 says a bundled problem is "a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones". Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **Diversity is the habitat's actor set, not a stock.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :47: "relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency." Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Loss is a change of contact. It is not subtraction from a substrate: Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another", and Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

2. **Resilience is selection between rooms.** Fig 7 :33: "→ variation inside, selection between". Fig 7 :36: "The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." A disturbance surfaces a changed condition; the system keeps a function only if some remaining response type is selected against it. The active parameter is independence of responses, not head count: Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus." Many species with the same response are one frame wearing many names.

3. **Carbon storage is a composed stock, with a stock-specific verdict.** Fig 13 :14 names energy as "conserved, finite, directionless alone"; a carbon inventory has that shape until a gradient supplies direction. Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Multiple species can compose the same carbon-accumulating function, so losing one route need not lose the substance until the routes fail. But no figure returns a sign or magnitude. Soil accrual, aboveground biomass stock and blue carbon are different objects with different verdicts, as Chen et al. 2023, Sullivan et al. 2017 and Moffett et al. 2026 show. This branch is `specified` on shape and `gap` on number.

4. **Recovery is habitat substitution with two silent failures.** Fig 11 :68-69: "what you failed to reproduce," / "and what you brought with you". Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 7 :36 supplies the selection rule, and Fig 12 :123 identifies the unit: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." A recovered system is a different room selected against the residual, not the old one replayed. Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."

5. **The currency problem is structural.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." A diversity index and a carbon stock are in different currencies; an "effect size" that divides one by the other is a filter for the budget, never a witness (Fig 9 :60).

6. **The arrow is not carried by the correlation.** Fig 2 :39: "Correlation has valence." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." An ordered claim needs an imported disturbance or intervention with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13), not a pooled association.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one percentage, not a universal sign, and not a ranking of species by "goodness". Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
- Not that more species always means more resilience. Independence and response diversity are the parameters (Fig 8 :48), and a chain of mirrors adds names, not rooms (Fig 7 :36).
- Not that a fast carbon sink is a resilient ecosystem. The outcomes have different anchors and can diverge.
- Not that restoration returns a previous state. It substitutes a habitat and inherits actors you did not choose (Fig 11 :67).
- Not that a semantic index is the ground. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does".
- Not `grounded`. No function, inventory or trajectory was measured in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator instrument, not a further pooling of studies. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a spectrometer, not an argument, distinguishes sources that produce the same reading. Here:

- **Room test.** Measure the variance of a function through a perturbation and enumerate which response types are selected against which surfaced residual. Does function persist when richness falls but response diversity and independence are held? (F1 of the refuter list.)
- **Carbon test.** Declare the pool, scale and interval: soil accrual, aboveground stock, or blue carbon. An inventory with declared pools settles the stock; no cross-pool average does.
- **Recovery test.** Record the trajectory against a declared reference and enumerate both what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69).
- **Anchor test.** Can the metric be moved by the management decision it grades? If yes, it has become another frame and Fig 2 :34 predicts coherent drift.
- **Standing falsifiers:** the six conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The derivation stops at the separator: which rooms must exist, which must differ, which anchors must stay fixed, and which instrument settles each branch. It does not name a species, a percentage or a policy. The record of what was dropped along the way is in `11_audit.md` as R_T, so the stop is checkable rather than asserted. Agreement from readers who share our evidence pool is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not corroboration.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a single percentage for the effect of biodiversity loss. It splits the question into three outcomes with different currencies and anchors, then gives the shape each answer must have. Diversity is the set of rooms available for selection; resilience is whether a remaining room with a different response is selected against the residual a disturbance surfaces, so independence of responses matters more than head count. Carbon is a composed stock: several species can arrive at the same substance, and the sign and size of the effect are specific to the pool and scale measured. Recovery is habitat substitution, with two silent failures, what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced. The framework returns that shape plus refuters written in advance, not a species list or a number. A measured function, inventory and trajectory are what close each branch; agreement is not.
