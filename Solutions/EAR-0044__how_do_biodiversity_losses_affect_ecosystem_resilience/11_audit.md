## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5 and :55 (union split), Fig 1 :19 (slot indexing), Fig 7 :33 and :36 (selection between rooms), Fig 8 :48 (independence), Fig 11 :43 and :66 (habitat and substitution), Fig 12 :123 and :125 (group and two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in the folder: the three outcomes are a union; diversity is the room set; resilience is selection; carbon is a pool-specific stock; recovery is substitution; the active parameter is independence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a policy-facing sentence someone might actually write: "biodiversity loss reduces ecosystem function, so protect diversity to keep carbon and resilience."

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure**, flattened into one "ecosystem function";
- the **per-slot indexing**, collapsed into a plot or national average;
- the **independence requirement**, replaced by a head count;
- the **pool and scale declaration** on carbon, without which the Chen and Sullivan results look like a contradiction;
- the **two silent failures** of substitution, which a "protect diversity" slogan cannot express.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a disturbance surfaces a residual and the system needs different rooms to meet it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
