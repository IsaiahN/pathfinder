## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question bundles three outcomes with different currencies and anchors into one verb, "affect". It is a union, not one effect size. Diversity is the habitat's actor set; its loss is a change of contact, not a subtraction of items. Resilience is selection between rooms: a disturbance surfaces a residual, and function persists only if some remaining response type differs and is selected against it, so independence of responses matters more than a species count. Carbon is a composed stock: several routes can arrive at the same substance, but the sign and magnitude are specific to the pool and scale. Recovery is habitat substitution, with two silent failures, what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced. The framework returns that shape and its refuters, not a species list or a percentage.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The split is required; the three outcomes are a union with different anchors

- **Statement:** resilience, carbon storage and recovery must be answered separately. A single "effect of biodiversity loss" is a budget filter, not a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Also Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." And Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **World check:** Ives and Carpenter 2007 (*Science*) write that "different types of stability describing different properties of ecosystems lead to multiple diversity-stability relationships." Moffett et al. 2026 (*Nature Ecology & Evolution*) find oceanic blue carbon "exceptionally sensitive" while hazard regulation is "relatively insensitive to biodiversity".
- **Refuter:** demonstrate one lever that clears all three intersections with no residual in any branch, so the union is actually a partition.

### C2: Resilience runs on independence of responses, not on head count

- **Statement:** many species with the same response are one room wearing many names. The insurance effect requires rooms that differ and are selected against the surfaced residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :33 "→ variation inside, selection between". Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus." Fig 8 :49 "completely are one frame wearing two names."
- **World check:** Yachi and Loreau 1999 (*PNAS*) name "the degree of asynchronicity of these responses" as one of three determinants of insurance. Mori et al. 2013 (*Biological Reviews*) say "Traditional diversity measures (e.g. 'species richness') may not adequately capture the aspects of biodiversity most relevant to ecosystem stability and functionality." Hong and Page 2004 (*PNAS*, mathematics, low overlap) find that greater individual ability "is more than offset by their lack of problem-solving diversity."
- **Refuter:** hold response diversity and independence constant and show that richness loss still changes a function with no change in room availability.

### C3: Carbon is pool- and scale-specific, and recovery is substitution with two silent failures

- **Statement:** a carbon verdict cannot be averaged across soil accrual, aboveground stock and blue carbon; recovery is not a replay of the previous system but a substituted habitat whose inherited actors were not chosen.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68-69 "what you failed to reproduce," / "and what you brought with you". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
- **World check:** Chen et al. 2023 (*Nature*) find "greater tree diversity is associated with higher soil carbon and nitrogen accumulation", while Sullivan et al. 2017 (*Scientific Reports*) find "Diversity-carbon relationships among all plots at 1 ha scale across the tropics are absent". These are different pools and scales, not one refuted result.
- **Refuter:** show that a carbon stock composes within one kind with no cross-kind structure, or that restoration removes a habitat without substituting one and without inheriting unchosen actors.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal percentage, sign or species ranking. Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
- Not that more species always means more resilience; independence and response diversity are the parameters.
- Not that a fast carbon sink is a resilient ecosystem. The outcomes can diverge.
- Not that restoration returns a previous state.
- Not that a diversity index is the ground. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does".
- Not that the pooled literature is independent corroboration; most of it shares plot networks and response variables.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument that discriminates sources, not more agreement. Specifically:

1. A perturbation experiment reporting response diversity and per-slot function, with the rooms selected named. Does function persist when richness falls but independence is held?
2. A carbon inventory with declared pool, scale and interval, for soil accrual, aboveground biomass or blue carbon separately.
3. A recovery trajectory against a declared reference, enumerating both what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced.
4. An anchor test: can the metric be moved by the decision it grades? If yes, Fig 2 :34 predicts coherent drift.
