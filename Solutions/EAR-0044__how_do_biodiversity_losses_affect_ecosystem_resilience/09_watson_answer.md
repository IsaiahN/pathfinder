## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of three outcomes with different currencies and anchors, not one effect size. Diversity loss changes the actor set and the independence structure of a habitat. Resilience is selection between rooms against a surfaced residual, so response diversity and asynchronicity carry the insurance effect and head count does not. Carbon storage is a composed stock whose sign and magnitude are specific to pool and scale, so no universal number is licensed. Recovery is habitat substitution with two silent failures and a functional-group structure, not a replay of a previous state. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. This is `specified` on shape, `general` on the union framing and `gap` on a universal sign or magnitude. Nothing is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the two-stage form is kept. The packet carries mode, three checkable sub-claims, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is publishing a single percentage that averages live slot residuals and then spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." This question bears on carbon policy and restoration spending, so that cost is high and the avoidance is cheap: lead with the split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The composite effect size was never asserted; the per-domain verdicts can be corrected without withdrawing the union structure or the habitat reading. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The unresolved carbon branch is a declared gap, not a committed number.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A perturbation experiment that holds response diversity and independence constant while richness falls would test D2; an inventory with declared pool, scale and interval would settle D3; a trajectory with enumerated failures would settle D4. A demonstration that the three outcomes form a partition, or that a carbon stock composes within one kind with no cross-kind structure, would break the shape claim. A figure re-census admitting a single currency for description and action would clear the Fig 12 strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
