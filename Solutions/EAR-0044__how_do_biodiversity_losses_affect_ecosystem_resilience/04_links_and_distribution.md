## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split and the refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." This question bears on carbon policy and restoration spending, so the cost of being wrong is high and independence is worth paying for.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three-way split and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure for each of the three outcomes; (3) record that verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before anything else.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the claim were true, and what would count as a failure. Record that verdict first.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three framework links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interior their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the eight-step loop as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits a method to cross. Each user must state their own ground, write their own refuter before the run, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a separator with refuters; it is not a single effect size. If a reviewer shows that the three outcomes form a partition, or that independence does not carry the insurance effect, the split must be withdrawn before any theory is offered.
