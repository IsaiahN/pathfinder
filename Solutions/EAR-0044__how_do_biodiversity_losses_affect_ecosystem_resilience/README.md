**Question:** How do biodiversity losses affect ecosystem resilience, carbon storage, and recovery after disturbance?

**Source:** earth systems.txt line 60

**Section:** 5. Forests, ecosystems, and biodiversity

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain shape and separator claims, `general` on the union framing, `gap` on any universal sign or magnitude. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement was taken in this derivation. The whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." An outside frame that has absorbed the interior has joined the frame being checked.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The residual in one line

The question bundles three outcomes with different currencies and anchors into one verb, "affect". Fig 9 :4-5 says that disagreements which do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working on a different subset, are "not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The derivation splits before it concludes.
