## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** Fig 4 :19-22 (membrane, kernel, method rule), Fig 11 :39/66-69 (habitat), Fig 2 :29/33/40 (anchor, correlation), Fig 9 :54 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand "kernel invariant; anchor must not update; methods cross; shared evidence is correlation; unbundle the union."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional design, for example "install independent monitoring, graduated sanctions and nested conflict resolution in the large institution."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which actor in the new habitat maintains the non-updating anchor, since the incoming actors are unchosen (Fig 11 :67);
- whether the monitor is itself accountable, which is a separate design condition (Ostrom design principle 4, world corroboration only);
- whether the "method" was re-derived rather than copied as a recipe (Fig 4 :23-25).

One thing survives cleanly: the invariant kernel and the requirement that the substitute anchor not move with opinion. R_T was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
