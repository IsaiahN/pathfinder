## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what changes when people move from a small community into a large anonymous institution. Option A: the person switches strategy while the internal machinery is unchanged. Option B: the machinery is invariant and the habitat changes around it, so what changes is the cast, the currency and the anchor that settles cooperation. What each leads to: A sends research toward a universal cooperation module and individual-level training; B sends research toward institutional design, anchor replacement, independent verification and method transfer, and records the empirical roster of strategies as a gap.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the vocabulary of cooperation predicts in a repeated face-to-face setting and what resolves in a large anonymous institution. Arity: two conditions in transit plus four sub-mechanisms (repeated contact, reputation, sanction, formal authority), so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54), not one hard question. Symmetry: directional and lossy, a substitution of habitat (Fig 11 :66), with one-way power (Fig 11 :61). Scale: individual, group, institution, population (Fig 4 membrane).
- **Domain 1, the kernel.** Actors: individual agents. Currency: cooperate/defect decisions, payoffs, reputation effects. Anchor: outcomes that settle whether a strategy paid; Fig 2 :29 "A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Legitimate domain.
- **Domain 2, the small-community habitat.** Actors: known others, repeated interaction, reputation network. Currency: reciprocal obligation, informal sanction, trust from repeated contact. Anchor: direct repeated interaction and known reputation. Legitimate domain, though the candidate anchor fails the non-updating test.
- **Domain 3, the large anonymous institutional habitat.** Actors: strangers, formal authorities, monitors, rules. Currency: formal sanctions, enforceable rules, institutional trust. Anchor: formal measurement, records, courts, markets, enforcement. Legitimate domain.
- **Domain 4, the membrane between scales.** Actors: methods crossing up, head starts coming down, the population that ranks output. Currency: method versus recording. Anchor: the round-trip gap R_T measured between scales (Fig 4 :30). Legitimate domain.
- **Domain 5, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The kernel is invariant; what changes across scale is the cast, the currency and the anchor. Cooperation is agent-and-habitat, so the move to a large anonymous institution is a substitution of habitat in which what the community supplied must be reproduced or replaced and unchosen actors are inherited. Reputation moves with opinion and therefore fails the anchor test; a non-updating anchor (rule, record, audit, court, market) is the structural substitute. Shared-evidence trust is correlation, a convention not corroboration, so independence must be bought. Only methods cross the membrane, and standing is a stake that must be declared. Which specific strategies change, and by how much, is a `gap`: the figures return a shape and the instruments that would close it, not a name. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is sending a psychological story instead of a shape and spending a reader's independence by asking for agreement before a check. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical gaps and the unproven bindings are removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An anchor-disabling field experiment in a large institution, an isolated-disposition study that moves the ground, an independence comparison, or a transplant test (all in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 4) would move the content to `grounded` or refute the relevant finding.
