## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework asks for "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is honest: "Indiscernibility is not identity."

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0060 a **shape** is therefore: the set of constraints any answer about cooperation across scale must satisfy, stated before we can name which specific strategy changes. "The kernel is invariant; the anchor must not update; only methods cross; shared evidence is correlation" is a shape. "Tit for tat becomes generalized trust" is a name, and the essays do not license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing and bootstrap. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a bootstrapping convergence process."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." We described the residual as a change of anchor, currency and cast, not as a change of "human nature."
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". An instrument that reads the same output from several sources cannot name the source. Applied here: observing that cooperation is high in both a village and a firm does not tell us the mechanism is the same; it may be a projection.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means an experiment, field dataset or institutional outcome that changes when the proposed mechanism changes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The world sources on Ostrom and on public goods games supply content, but they do not license naming a figure slot; they corroborate the shape.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the independence case directly, and it is unusually close to this question: "The paper I derived and hers were not a carbon copy. They rhymed. I had arrived at the same conclusions from bacteria and biology and game agents. She had arrived at them from committees and fisheries and forests." Two frames reaching the same structure from different evidence is corroboration at maximum independence, not one pool talking. This is the one part of the world search that genuinely raises confidence in the shape, though it still cannot settle it.

## 4. Why "which strategy" is a gap, not a silence

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

The figures cannot name the empirical strategy because the question's empirical content is not a term held in the framework's library. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The honest report is `gap` for content and `specified` for shape, with a recorded list of instruments that would close it (see `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 4).

## 5. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A well-known institutional designer agreeing with the shape gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not make the shape true. The outreach material therefore asks for a refutation and never asks for an endorsement.
