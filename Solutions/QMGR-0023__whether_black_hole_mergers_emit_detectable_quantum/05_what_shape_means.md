## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :137: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form that any detectable quantum signature would have to take, stated before we can name the mechanism that produces it. It is a set of constraints on a residual:

1. It is indexed per slot (event, detector, time-frequency bin), because Fig 1 :19 says *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A pooled catalog excess is not a shape-conforming answer.
2. It is above a noise floor that does not update on the theory. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
3. It survives the source-to-detector scale transform. Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*
4. It is attributable to a source by a discriminator, not merely present as a deviation. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*

`A positive residual somewhere in the data` is not a shape. `A per-slot residual above a non-updating anchor, surviving a measured round trip, and attributed by a discriminator` is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest held terms are residual, anchor, slot, membrane, import and discriminator.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: describe the effect rather than the cause. So we describe a signature in terms of what an instrument would return, not in terms of a particular theory's ontology.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the situation in D2, where there is no contact with a quantum-gravity ground.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question that would mean the detector reading and the quantum mechanism coincide in one discriminating measurement. They do not, which is why the honest stop is `unreachable`.
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions (opened): if the final book does not answer the question, either the question was unanswerable or *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level precedent for F1's union reading.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a per-event residual attributed by an independent instrument to a quantum mechanism would settle a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. A quantum-gravity theory with its own settled anchor and an in-band prediction made before the search would qualify.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Neither applies yet, because no quantum-gravity frame has passed a source gate for this question.

## 4. Why naming a mechanism from a residual is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism directly. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The essay's conclusion is that *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and that the case was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a post-merger residual can come from a glitch, template mismatch, environmental coupling, detector quantum noise or a candidate quantum source. The waveform instrument reads the residual and cannot comment on which source produced it. Naming "quantum signature" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

This is reinforced by the detector characterization literature (world source W5): in the first part of O4, 29 percent of GW candidates had overlapping or nearby glitches in one or more detectors. Multiple paths really do produce similar readings in this instrument.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A template that has been tuned to absorb a possible quantum deviation cannot then certify that the deviation is absent. The null result is only as good as the validator that produced it.

## 5. The correction I owe my own section 3

The phrase *"shape, not a name"* is easily overextended. The figures README says *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. For QMGR-0037 the correction was that Gradient and Spectrum are both held, so naming one is a binding, not an invention.

For QMGR-0023 the situation is different and the stricter reading holds. No quantum-gravity primitive is held with an anchor in the library that this derivation can use. The classical six-term substrate (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network) describes the classical waveform and its detector, but no anchor returns a value for a quantum-gravity emission mechanism. So:

- Saying "some quantum mechanism is required for a quantum signature" is a shape claim and is licensed.
- Saying "the mechanism is a horizon reflection surface at a specific frequency" is a name and is **not** licensed by the framework. It is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such, and still unproven (world source W7 even finds the predicted frequency incompatible with the claimed signal).
- Saying "there is no such mechanism" is also a name-shaped verdict and is not licensed. The gap and the unreachable stay open.

Both errors are visible here so neither is quietly lost: the mechanism is not named, and its absence is not asserted.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of well known advocates for quantum black hole echoes is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, let the detector ground settle it, and record the refuter before the run.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim, cannot reduce detector data, and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Consequence for this question's shape statement

The one-paragraph shape that survives all of the above:

> A detectable quantum signature from a black hole merger would have to be a per-slot residual in released detector data, above a non-updating noise anchor, surviving a measured lossy transform from the source scale to the detector band, and attributed to a quantum source by an instrument that discriminates among glitches, template mismatch, environment, detector quantum noise and the candidate mechanism. No such reading has registered at any resolution, so detection is unreachable at this budget. The framework returns that shape and cannot name the mechanism, and a null search is not a proof of absence.
