## Wider literature that bears on the question

These were opened and are recorded with full provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **GWTC-4.0: Tests of General Relativity. III. Tests of the Remnants** (LIGO Scientific Collaboration, Virgo Collaboration and KAGRA Collaboration, arXiv:2603.19021, 2026-03-19, ApJL focus issue). Four of seven remnant tests explicitly allow post-merger echoes. The abstract says: *"We find no evidence for post-merger echoes in the events that were analyzed."* It also reports a hierarchical result where the GR prediction lies at the boundary of a 98.6 percent credible region, with the significance decreasing when more events are included, and concludes *"there is no strong evidence for a GR deviation."* This is the most direct anchor for the detection domain and it is recent.
2. **Tests of general relativity with GW150914** (LIGO and Virgo, arXiv:1602.03841, PRL 116, 221101, 2016). *"In conclusion, within our statistical uncertainties, we find no evidence for violations of general relativity in the genuinely strong-field regime of gravity."* It also constrains the graviton Compton wavelength to a 90 percent lower bound of 10^13 km. A propagation-side bound from the first event.
3. **GW170817 and GRB 170817A** (LIGO, Virgo, Fermi GBM, INTEGRAL, arXiv:1710.05834, ApJL 848:L13, 2017). Constrains the difference between the speed of gravity and the speed of light to between -3×10^-15 and +7×10^-16 times c and places bounds on Lorentz invariance violation. The genuinely independent instrument in the propagation domain.
4. **Tests of General Relativity with GW170817** (LIGO and Virgo, arXiv:1811.00364, PRL 123, 011102, 2019). Bounds dipole radiation, post-Newtonian deviations, modified dispersion and large extra dimensions; *"The results of all tests performed here show good agreement with GR."*
5. **Observation of Gravitational Waves from a Binary Black Hole Merger** (LIGO and Virgo, arXiv:1602.03837, PRL 116, 061102, 2016). Gives the detector reading that anchors the whole question: a peak strain of 1.0×10^-21, a sweep from 35 to 250 Hz, SNR 24, and a waveform that *"matches the waveform predicted by general relativity for the inspiral and merger of a pair of black holes and the ringdown of the resulting single black hole."*
6. **Hawking radiation** (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23). A secondary reference, used only for the magnitude and non-detection claims: the radiation is *"predicted to be extremely faint and is many orders of magnitude below the current best telescopes' detecting ability"*, *"Such radiation bursts have not yet been detected"*, and for a stellar-mass black hole the temperature is about 10^-7 K against a 2.7 K background so *"no stellar black holes can evaporate: they are colder than outer space."*
7. **Gravitational wave echoes from strange stars** (Mannarelli and Tonelli, arXiv:1805.02278, PRD 97, 123010, 2018). Notes that for a black hole *"a second internal reflection surface, associated to quantum effects near the black hole horizon, must be present"* for echoes, and finds that strange-star echoes would appear at tens of kHz and are *"not compatible with the 72 Hz signal."* This is a candidate mechanism, not a detection.
8. **Black hole spectroscopy: from theory to experiment** (Berti et al., Class. Quantum Grav. 43, 123001, 2026), seen on the CQG journal page. Reviews using quasinormal modes to *"search for physics beyond the Standard Model or new gravitational degrees of freedom"* and reviews LVK ringdown observations. Useful for locating the mainstream formulation of the search, not used as a quoted authority for the verdict.

Two framework essays were opened for the canonical shape reading: *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* and *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*, plus *The Hard Problem is About Source* for the source-discrimination point. All three share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries no independent witness weight.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the claim "no detectable quantum signatures" does not survive if it is stated as a fact rather than a budget verdict (F2 in `01`). Send the budget verdict and the refuter, not the fact.
