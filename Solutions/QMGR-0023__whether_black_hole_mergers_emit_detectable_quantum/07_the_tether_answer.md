# The Tether's answer: QMGR-0023

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the naming of a mechanism, `unreachable` on detectability at this budget. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No detector data was reduced.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question "do black hole mergers emit detectable quantum signatures in gravitational-wave data?" bundles four conditions with different anchors: emission, propagation, detection and source attribution. The residual is the per-slot gap between the classical numerical-relativity prediction and the observed strain, indexed by event, detector and time-frequency bin. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Reading A (yes) would require a new settled term, a named mechanism and a measurable deviation. Reading B (union) keeps the four verdicts separate, returns a shape, and records where the search stops. This answer is Reading B.

## 2. The answer, domain by domain

Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

- **D1, detector / measurement. `specified`, triangulated.** The instrument returns classical strains and has never returned a quantum-gravity signature at any resolution. Fig 6 :32-33 says an instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something, and Fig 6 :40: **"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."** The LVK GWTC-4.0 remnant analysis finds *"no evidence for post-merger echoes in the events that were analyzed"* (W1), and the GW150914 tests find *"no evidence for violations of general relativity in the genuinely strong-field regime of gravity"* (W2). Detectability is therefore **unreachable at this budget**. It is not proven impossible: Fig 8 :39, **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
- **D2, emission / source. `gap` for the mechanism, triangulated for the import claim.** No quantum-gravity mechanism has a nameable anchor here. Fig 13 :137: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** The figures return the shape any emission signature must have and cannot name the mechanism. Hawking radiation from a stellar-mass remnant is about 10^-7 K against a 2.7 K background (W3), so the quantum emission is present in theory and produces no reading. The set is closed and the arrangements are not (Fig 13 :115), so an import would be required before the mechanism could be named with standing.
- **D3, source attribution. `specified`, triangulated.** A residual is a filter, not a witness. Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** The metamerism argument (E3) shows that an instrument reading a projection cannot comment on the source; *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* In O4a, 29 percent of candidates had overlapping or nearby glitches (W5), so multiple causes really do produce similar readings. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**
- **D4, propagation. `specified`, triangulated.** This is the one domain with genuine independence. GW170817 and GRB 170817A are two instruments pointed at the same event (Fig 2 :31-32). The 1.74 s delay constrains the gravity-light speed difference to between -3×10^-15 and +7×10^-16 c (W6), and the dedicated tests report *"The results of all tests performed here show good agreement with GR"* (W4). Dispersive quantum-gravity propagation signatures are bounded on a channel independent of waveform morphology.

Order by dependency (Fig 3): emission, propagation, detection, attribution. The break sits between emission and detection. Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."** A more sensitive interferometer optimizes a working link; it does not create the missing observable.

## 3. The shape

Any candidate answer must have all of these properties:

1. A positive live residual in at least one slot (event, detector, time-frequency bin), not a pooled catalog average.
2. Above a noise anchor that does not update on the theory (Fig 2 :29-30).
3. Surviving a measured, lossy transform from the source scale to the detector band (Fig 4 :27, :30).
4. Surviving glitch, calibration and template-systematic checks.
5. Attributed by a source-discriminating instrument to a quantum mechanism, not merely present as a deviation (Fig 9 :30-31).
6. Accompanied by a mechanism with its own settled anchor and a prediction made before the search, if a name is to be licensed (Fig 8 source gate and shadow test).

Over the held atom set, the quantum-corrected waveform is an isomer of the classical waveform: same atoms, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :117). But it is an unsettled candidate, so it cannot yet be composed over (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "black holes do not emit quantum signatures". That would be a name-shaped assertion about a gap. The emission domain is `gap`, not `no`.
- Not "quantum gravity is wrong" or "quantum gravity is confirmed". No quantum-gravity frame passed a source gate in this derivation.
- Not "the null echo searches prove absence". Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44 forbid that upgrade.
- Not that any specific mechanism (firewall, fuzzball, horizon reflection surface) is the explanation. W7 finds the predicted frequency of one such model incompatible with the claimed signal.
- Not that detector quantum noise is the same thing as a source quantum signature. Detector noise is a habitat condition that registers; the source signature is a residual that has never registered (`01` F9).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no claim is settled by experiment here.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. This is the spectrometer point from E3: the color case was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

1. A per-event residual in released GW data (GWOSC) that survives glitch vetoes, calibration and template-systematic checks, and is attributed by an independent instrument to a quantum mechanism at high confidence.
2. A quantum-gravity theory with its own settled anchor predicting a specific in-band shape in advance, with provenance and a shadow test, and that shape being found.
3. Hawking-like radiation from a stellar-mass merger remnant detected above the background and above detector noise.
4. A measurement showing the source-to-detector transform is not lossy for this quantity.
5. A multi-messenger event with an energy-dependent arrival delay that survives instrumental and source-emission explanations.

Standing structural refuters: a demonstration that the four conditions share one anchor; a figure re-census that admits the quantum-gravity atom into the closure with a source gate; a residual that is attributed but turns out to be a glitch after discrimination. The pre-registered list is in `01` section 4 and is repeated in `08` and `09`.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 6 :40 says a sensor for what has never registered anywhere is unavailable, and Fig 8 :40-41 distinguishes underivable in principle from unreachable at this budget. We are in the second case with the additional complication that the source half has no anchor at all. The six-term substrate is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list; another instrument or another frame could change the reach. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."**

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether black hole mergers emit detectable quantum signatures, because the question is four conditions with different anchors. The detector has returned classical strains and has never returned a quantum-gravity signature at any resolution, so detection is unreachable at this budget, which is a verdict about our instruments and not a proof of absence. Independent multi-messenger timing bounds the propagation channel, and a residual by itself is a filter for where to search, not a witness for a quantum source. What is offered is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
