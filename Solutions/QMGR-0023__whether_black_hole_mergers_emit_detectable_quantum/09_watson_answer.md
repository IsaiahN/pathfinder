## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of four conditions, and the figures decide two of them while refusing to decide the others. Detection is **unreachable at this budget**: no quantum-gravity signature has registered in gravitational-wave data at any resolution, so a sensor for it cannot be grown from an instrument already returning one, and the published null searches are budget results and not proofs of absence. Which mechanism emits is a `gap`: the framework returns the shape a signature must have, a per-slot residual above a non-updating noise anchor, surviving a measured lossy scale transform and attributed by a discriminator, and cannot name the mechanism. Attribution is a separate condition that requires a source-discriminating instrument, because a residual can be a glitch, a template mismatch, detector quantum noise or a candidate quantum source. Propagation is separately and independently bounded by multi-messenger timing. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified` on those verdicts, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is misusing a null result as a proof of absence or a residual as a source, which would close a question the ground has not settled. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter and by recording the outside verdict before showing theory.
- **undoable:** yes. The budget verdict is revisable the moment something registers, without withdrawing the figure derivation. The gap is closable by a sourced import with provenance and a shadow test. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no figure line has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-event residual in released data that survives systematic checks and is attributed by an independent instrument to a quantum mechanism; a quantum-gravity theory with its own anchor predicting a specific in-band shape in advance and that shape found; Hawking-like radiation from a stellar-mass remnant above the background; a measurement showing the source-to-detector transform is lossless for this quantity; or a multi-messenger event with an energy-dependent arrival delay surviving instrumental and source explanations. Prestige or a consensus of papers is not such an instrument.
