## 3. Two results in the wider literature that bear directly on the question

1. **The upper gap result is a two-instrument result, not a bigger catalog.** Tong et al. (2026) locate the gap in the secondary-mass distribution and align it with a spin transition; Antonini et al. (2026) use spin orientation to separate a low-spin first-generation population from an isotropic high-spin population and independently constrain the same nuclear rate. These are different readings aimed at the same residual. By Fig 8 :50 they can be corroboration at maximum independence; by Fig 2 :40, if they actually share the same GWTC-4 posteriors they are partly one pool. The ledger records the partial overlap. Neither result is a figure derivation.
2. **The lower gap classification is prior-sensitive in a way that matters.** Mali and Essick (2026) show that pairing preferences and spin tilts move P(NS) for GW230529's primary across most of the unit interval while the mass reading stays fixed. This is the metamerism point in the astrophysical literature: the reading does not determine the source. It is the single strongest world corroborator for the figure shape.

## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the union split, the three verdicts and the refuter. Send the theory links only after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :35** — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement and do not rank recipients by likely agreement.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing any of the framework.
- **Fig 7 :13** — *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Later agreement from people who read the same packet and the same figures is one pool, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the loop as a reusable generator, never the answer.
- **Fig 9 :60** — *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* Journal prestige, citation counts and catalog size are filters. They never enter the verdict.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the three public links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the method with an instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the effect statement of the residual, the three per-domain verdicts and the pre-registered refuter. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure? Record the answer before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the LIGO summary, the Nature paper and the ARA&A review. Their agreement after reading the framework is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, on request.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
