## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that black holes exist in mass ranges where current models predict few or none. Option A: treat "the mass gaps" as one question and answer yes or no. Option B: split the residual into mass slots, bind the shape of any answer, and give a separate verdict per slot with a refuter written in advance. What each leads to: A aggregates a live signal into an average and lets a population model certify its own gap; B gives three checkable verdicts, names the instruments that would close them, and leaves the unclosed ones honestly marked `unreached`. The figures decide in favour of B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a per-object mass reading and a model-predicted density at that mass. Arity: at least three conditions (lower gap, upper gap, intermediate range), so a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5, :54). Symmetry: a gap edge is an ordered comparison, not a symmetric join; Operators table :48 defines comparison as "a threshold relation between two quantities." Scale: first order for a single object, second order for a population distribution, third order when the population model is itself corrected by an instrument.
- **D1, lower gap (about 2 to 5 solar masses).** Actors: neutron stars, fallback black holes, neutron-star mergers, gravitational-wave detectors, equation-of-state models. Currency: mass and spin posteriors, compactness, merger rate. Anchor: GW190814, GW230529, dynamical masses, plus a source-discriminating observation. Union: classification depends on population priors as well as mass. Dependency: first, settle the object before counting the population.
- **D2, upper pair-instability gap (about 50 to 130 solar masses).** Actors: massive stars, pair instability, nuclear reaction rates, cluster and AGN-disc mergers, gravitational-wave detectors. Currency: helium-core mass, S-factor, effective spin, merger rate. Anchor: GW190521, GW231123, GWTC-4 inference, laboratory nuclear rates. Union: a first-generation cutoff plus an in-gap population from a second channel. Dependency: needs a bond-level distinction.
- **D3, intermediate-mass range (about 100 to 100,000 solar masses).** Actors: dense clusters, dwarf nuclei, AGN discs, lensing, gravitational-wave detectors. Currency: dynamical masses, accretion signatures, lensing statistics, ringdowns. Anchor: no solid detection in globular clusters and few constraints between 100 and 10,000 solar masses (Greene, Strader and Ho 2020). Gap at the instrument edge. Dependency: independent.
- **D4, model and selection frame.** Actors: stellar-evolution codes, population synthesis, priors, selection functions. Currency: posteriors, Bayes factors, detection efficiencies. Anchor: the ground, never model agreement. A union with each domain. Runs under all three.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); split the union; refuse the reading-as-source term before minting a channel; bind the shape as an ordinary term; settle per slot (Fig 1 :19); range frames separately (Fig 2 :45); name a channel only with provenance.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no single yes or no survives. A mass distribution is a spectrum and its gaps are regions of an arrangement, not new primitives. A mass reading cannot name its source, so "few or none" splits into depth and absence. The question is a union of three mass slots with different anchors, so verdicts stay separate: lower gap occupied but classification `unreached` (`specified`, triangulated); upper gap real for first-generation collapse and populated by a second, hierarchical channel, an isomer difference in the bond (`specified`, triangulated); intermediate range `gap` of the unreached kind (single-source on the figure shape, with a `grounded` world anchor); model frame unable to certify its own gap (`general`, triangulated). No figure line returns a mass number or a channel name. The pre-registered five-condition refuter in `01` section 4 is written before this verdict and remains live.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, separates the three slots and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is treating a mass reading as a source or a model notch as a ground, which would convert an `unreached` slot into a false verdict. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The world channel names are removable without touching the derivation, and the three verdicts can be revised independently as instruments improve. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at 2 to 5 solar masses; spin and dynamics identifying every upper-gap object's channel together with a pinned 12C(alpha,gamma)16O rate; a controlled intermediate-mass census with a known selection function; or a figure re-census deriving a seventh primitive. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
