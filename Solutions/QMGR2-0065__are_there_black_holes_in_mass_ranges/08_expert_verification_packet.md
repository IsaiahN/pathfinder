# Expert verification packet: the Tether on black hole mass gaps

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astrophysicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed by the derivation. World measurements are cited as world sources with provenance.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Are there black holes in mass ranges where current models predict few or none?" is a union of at least three conditions with different anchors, not one gap. A mass distribution is a spectrum and a gap is an empty region of an arrangement, not a new primitive. A mass reading is a projection that cannot name its source, so "few or none" must be split into instrument depth and genuine absence. Applied to the public record: lower-gap objects exist but their classification is `unreached`; the upper pair-instability gap is a real first-generation cutoff populated by a second, merger channel, an isomer difference in the bond; the intermediate-mass range is `unreached` at current resolution. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The framework returns that shape plus five refuters, not a mass number or a channel name derived from the figures.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13, 9 and 1

- **Statement:** a mass distribution is a spectrum, a gap is an empty region of it, and the one-question reading of the mass gaps fails because the question is a union of at least three conditions whose verdicts must not be averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :20, *"ordering, so more and less can be said."* Fig 13 :43-44, *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** one mechanism with one boundary and one instrument accounts for all three mass ranges; or a seventh primitive is derived from the six; or a direct state-state or structure-structure composition is admitted that cannot be refused.

### C2. Physics-facing; checkable against event data and population inference

- **Statement:** for the upper gap, the objects forbidden to first-generation collapse are supplied by a second channel whose behavioural signature is spin orientation; same atom set, different bond, different substance. For the lower gap, mass alone does not label the source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :121, *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** does the 2026 two-population spin result (low spin below the gap, isotropic high spin across it) survive a reanalysis with different waveform models and selection functions? Does the 2021 low-spin exclusion region remain empty of hierarchical explanations? Does the lower-gap classification stay prior-sensitive under a pinned equation of state?
- **Refuter:** in-gap objects above 50 solar masses with low spin and no hierarchical history, while the 12C(alpha,gamma)16O rate is pinned high enough to forbid single-star remnants there; or a complete volume-limited lower-gap sample in which every object is a neutron star.

### C3. The limit on what can be concluded; checkable by reading Figures 8, 6 and 13

- **Statement:** the intermediate-mass range is `unreached`, not shown empty, and no population model can certify its own gap. A failed search is not a proof of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :34-35, *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :34, *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled intermediate-mass census with known selection function returning zero where instruments would have detected them; or a demonstration that a model frame proves its own completeness without importing an anchor.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a mass number derived from the figures. The 44, 50, 85 and 100-to-100,000 numbers are world measurements, not figure outputs.
- Not a claim that any event's object is a black hole. GW190814 and GW230529 classifications are open.
- Not a derivation of fallback supernovae, pair instability, hierarchical merger, primordial black holes or any other channel. These are world imports with provenance, not figure results.
- Not that the intermediate-mass range is empty. It is unreached.
- Not a theory of everything, not a final room, and not the only framework. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing in the derivation was settled by a measurement taken here.
- Not that agreement among papers or models counts as corroboration. Shared evidence pools are one witness (Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).

2. A complete volume-limited sample with pinned equation of state shows every lower-gap object is a neutron star and no black hole exists between 2 and 5 solar masses.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :20 and :43-44, Fig 9 :54, Fig 1 :19, Fig 12 :117 and :121, Fig 8 :39 and :34. Eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against the event data: are GW190814 and GW230529 classifications still open? Is the two-population spin result robust to waveform and prior choices?
3. Name one instrument that would separate neutron stars from black holes at 3.6 solar masses, or one that would return a source-discriminating mass in the 100 to 10,000 range, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
