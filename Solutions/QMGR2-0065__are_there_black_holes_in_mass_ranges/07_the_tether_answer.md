# The Tether's answer: are there black holes in predicted mass gaps?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05` section 6), not a ground reading. The world measurements quoted below are `grounded` in their own field and are recorded as world sources; the mapping onto the figure vocabulary is not.

## 1. The residual it answers

An observer has a mass reading and a model predicts few or none at that mass. The residual is indexed per object and per mass slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). It is a union of at least three conditions, so it must be unbundled before any lever is applied (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). "Few or none" conflates depth with genuine absence, and from inside one frame they look the same (Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **A mass distribution is a spectrum; a gap is a region of it with no members.** Fig 13 :20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said."* Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* No seventh primitive is needed.
2. **A mass reading is a projection that cannot name its source.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A source label requires a source-discriminating instrument. Without one, the answer is `unreached`, not `none`.
3. **Each slot gets its own verdict, never an average.** The lower gap, the pair-instability gap and the intermediate-mass range have different actors and different anchors. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 3. Verdicts per domain (kept separate, never averaged)

### D1, lower gap (about 2 to 5 solar masses). `specified`, triangulated

Objects are present. GW190814's secondary is 2.50 to 2.67 solar masses and is *"either the lightest black hole or the heaviest neutron star ever discovered"* (LIGO DCC, 2020). GW230529's primary is about 3.6 solar masses and is the first binary candidate with a primary in the gap (LIGO science summary, 2024). Whether each object is a black hole is still `unreached`: Mali and Essick (2026) find the neutron-star probability sweeping from 1 to 67 percent under different population priors while the mass reading is unchanged. The model prediction of "few" is weakened; a prediction of "none" does not survive for the mass range.

### D2, upper pair-instability gap (about 50 to 130 solar masses). `specified`, triangulated

The gap is real as a cutoff for **first-generation** collapse and is populated by a **second** channel. Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* GW190521's primary at 85 solar masses lies *"within the gap produced by (pulsational) pair-instability supernova processes"* (Abbott et al. 2020). Two independent 2026 analyses find a lower edge near 44 solar masses and a high-spin isotropic population occupying the gap, interpreted as hierarchical mergers, and constrain the 12C(alpha,gamma)16O rate. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The first-generation gap and the in-gap population are not a contradiction.

### D3, intermediate-mass range (about 100 to 100,000 solar masses). `gap` of the unreached kind, single-source on the figure shape

Greene, Strader and Ho (2020) report *"no solid detections of black holes in globular clusters"* and *"few observational constraints on black holes in any environment"* between 100 and 10,000 solar masses. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The verdict is that the current instrument set cannot return a source-discriminating reading here. It is not a claim that none exist. Lensing statistics, dynamics and ringdowns are instruments already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35).

### D4, the model and selection frame. `general`, triangulated

A frame cannot certify its own gap (Fig 8 :34). Population fits that assume a notch return a notch; the 2021 "Poking Holes" result finds upper-gap evidence under one mass model and none under another. Model agreement is a budget filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- That any specific object is a black hole. The lower-gap classification is open.
- That the figures return a mass number. They return a shape; the numbers come from instruments.
- That the framework derives pair instability, fallback supernovae, hierarchical mergers or primordial black holes. Those are world imports with provenance, not figure results.
- That the intermediate-mass range is empty. It is unreached.
- That agreement among papers, reviews or models settles any slot. Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness (Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).
- That this is `grounded`. No measurement in this folder was taken by the derivation.

## 5. What would settle it, and what would refute the shape

The pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4 is reproduced in full in `08`. The ground readings that would close the three slots are:

1. **D1:** a tidal deformability, counterpart or equation-of-state determination that labels the lower-gap objects, plus a complete volume-limited census.
2. **D2:** spin orientations and cluster dynamics that identify every in-gap object's channel, plus a pinned 12C(alpha,gamma)16O rate. The low-spin exclusion test of the 2021 ApJ paper is a concrete version.
3. **D3:** a dynamically confirmed intermediate-mass black hole, a controlled lensing inference, or a ringdown component mass in the range.

The five refuter conditions are: one mechanism for all three ranges; no black holes at all in the lower gap in a complete sample; in-gap upper objects with low spin and no hierarchical history plus a pinned nuclear rate; a controlled census returning zero intermediate-mass black holes where instruments would have seen them; or a seventh primitive derived from the six. If any fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 forbids reading a failed search as proof of absence, and Fig 13 :134 makes unreachability a verdict rather than a silence. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README's warning applies: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The simulator cannot certify its own direction, so the shape term was bound and refuters were written before the run.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a black-hole mass number and does not name a formation channel from a mass reading. It says that a mass distribution is a spectrum and a gap is an empty region of it, not a new kind of object; that a mass reading cannot comment on its source, so "few or none" must be split into instrument depth and genuine absence; and that the question is a union of three mass slots with different anchors, so their verdicts must never be averaged. At the lower gap the objects are present but their classification is still open. At the upper pair-instability gap the gap is real for first-generation collapse and populated by a second, merger channel, which is an isomer difference in the bond rather than a contradiction. At intermediate masses the honest verdict is unreached, not empty. What the framework offers is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would close each slot. The slots stay open until the ground settles them.
