- **Domain 4, maintenance / channel seat.** Actors: the apparatus that resets the receptor and keeps the channel reachable. Currency: channel integrity. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: keeping the anchor reachable.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the mechanism is not named. A receptor reading cannot name its source, so detection must be resolved per slot, must range against a ground that does not move with the cell's model, must use time or an independent second channel, and must pay in two currencies that do not add. Noise is the discarded detail of a lossy crossing, so signal and noise can be isomers over the same atoms. The question is a union compound, so the answer is a family of discriminators, not one pathway. D1, D2 and D3 are `specified` and triangulated; D4 is `specified` and single-source; the molecular mechanism is `gap` at figure level. Nothing here is `grounded`. The strongest single figure-grounded point is Fig 13 :32, corroborated by the source essay and by the measurement literature: noise is what a crossing discards.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states refuters before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a mechanism name instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism slot is empty, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct this solution. A ground reading can name a pathway for a particular cell without touching the shape. A re-census can add a coupled habitat domain if the cell moves its own ground. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment showing single-reading discrimination with no time comparison, no second channel and no penalising ground would break F1 and F3; a demonstration that signal and noise are intrinsic molecular properties would break F2; a demonstration of zero-delay, zero-dissipation, zero-extra-description specificity would break F5; a proof that a cell can certify its own complete receptor set would break F8. Agreement among receptive readers is not such an instrument.
