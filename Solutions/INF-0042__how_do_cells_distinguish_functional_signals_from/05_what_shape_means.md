## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

Fig 13 in the local extract: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that you can state before you can name the thing that fills it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INF-0042 the shape is the four-part discriminator in `01` F1 to F5. The sentence "the cell uses kinetic proofreading" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the exact reason the outreach email leads with effects, not mechanism names.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the residual (a reading cannot name its source), then let the figure lines narrow the space to four constraints, then stop and record the stop.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, an experiment that shows which discriminator a particular cell actually uses, and that it pays at the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Hopfield's kinetic proofreading, Berg and Purcell's receptor counting, and Mangan and Alon's feed-forward loops are imports from the biology frame. They are recorded with author, venue, year, and quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`. That provenance lets them be used as candidate mechanisms without pretending the figures derived them.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible choice, not nature's own list, so a cell's sensorium need not be described in those six terms. That is a standing limitation on how far the shape can travel.

## 4. Why this question is especially exposed to the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism directly. An instrument that reads green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The essay's general claim: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The resolution was not an argument but *"the arrival of a better instrument."*

A receptor binding event is the same shape. The reading does not say whether the binding came from a real environmental change, from a near-cognate molecule, or from a thermal fluctuation. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. Fig 13 :32, quoted in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* itself: *"the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* Noise is precisely the discarded detail of the lossy molecular-to-decision crossing. That is why the answer cannot be "the molecule with the right shape wins": the right shape is relative to the residual and the level.

## 5. Q: Does the framework name the discriminator? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework names a molecular mechanism for signal versus noise.** The framework returns the constraints: per-slot resolution, an anchor not moved by the frame, independent channels or time comparison, two prices, refusal, and the union split. A specific pathway is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the pathway is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the folder commit the forbidden error?** It would if it asserted that kinetic proofreading *is* the figure-derived discriminator. It does not: `01` F10 records the mechanism as a `gap` at figure level, and `07` lists the candidate mechanisms under "what it explicitly does not say". The one place where a figure-derived term could be misnamed is the word **noise**. Here it is used in the figure's sense (discarded detail of a crossing), not as a name for a class of molecules.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the mention of any mechanism at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Kinetic proofreading, receptor arrays, and feed-forward loops are not missing primitives of the framework; they are terms held in the biology frame. Naming them is a provenanced import, not an invention of a seventh primitive.

What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the figures named them, (b) claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; a candidate mechanism adds no atom to the figures), and (c) treating a reading of a pathway as proof of its source. All three are recorded so none is quietly lost. This is the same narrowing the validated exemplar applied to "the framework returns a shape, not a name" in its §5b.

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous signalling labs whose names are used to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an experiment settle it. Contact venues in `03` are public and institutional for exactly this reason.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09`, and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the biological claim. Each says so in its mode line. This folder cannot settle how cells distinguish signals from noise; it can only state the shape any answer must have and the refuters that would break it.
