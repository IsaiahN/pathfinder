## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (verified 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** long-term habitability is a maintained, cross-scale contact property of a coupled star-interior-surface-biosphere system, governed by recycling loops and spendable gradients, with the ground being the persistence of life.
- **Name:** "the carbonate-silicate thermostat controls it," or "plate tectonics is the master lever." These are specific bindings read off the world, not returned by the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the nearest held terms are cycle (Fig 12 :121), gradient (Fig 13 :22), network (Fig 13 :24) and habitat (Fig 11).
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* So describe "maintained contact property" rather than "Gaia," and let the world frame supply its own name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (via exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email leads with a checkable failure condition.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stop is honest: the figures give the shape and stop; the process list is owed from another frame.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For habitability, the ground is the persistence of life, which the exoplanet literature explicitly distinguishes from modelling habitability.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test. Carbon, water, silicate weathering and tectonics all enter this way.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (via exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The four domains in `01` are a visible guess at the habitat's compression axes, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why naming a master process is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (via exemplar) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For habitability, a retrieved atmospheric spectrum or a measured mass and radius reads a **projection**. Several histories (different interior states, different biospheres, different impact histories) can yield the same reading. Naming one process as "the controller" from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (verified 2026-11-23): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q — what licenses a particular process in the slot?

**Answer: nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular process is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the error?** No. `07_the_tether_answer.md` keeps the process list in the WORLD section with provenance and overlap declared, and the TETHER section returns only the shape. The one place a name appears in the shape claim is "persistence of life as the anchor," which is not a process name but the definition of the ground (Fig 2 :29-30).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (verified): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign gathering famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (verified): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
