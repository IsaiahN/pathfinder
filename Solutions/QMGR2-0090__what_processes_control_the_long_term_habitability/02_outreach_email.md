# Outreach draft — check the output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and §4 are stated cleanly. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about what keeps a planet habitable

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` as a shape, not `grounded`):** long-term habitability is not a property of a planet alone. It is a maintained property of the contact between the star, the interior, the surface and any biosphere, and the ground that settles it is whether life actually persists. Under the framework I am using, capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone, and an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. The processes that control habitability are therefore the ones that maintain the flows and recycling loops across those strata, not a fixed list of conditions. The framework returns a shape; it does not itself name carbon, water or plate tectonics. Those are read off the world.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I have derived the carbonate-silicate cycle from the framework, or that the framework is a theory of everything, or that any particular process is proven necessary. Specific processes are imports from planetary science and Earth system science, and until their provenance and shadow tests are shown they carry zero evidentiary weight. I am also not claiming that a famous reviewer or a large number of agreeing papers settles anything: agreement among frames that share an evidence pool is one witness, not many.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a world whose long-term habitability is fully determined by intrinsic planetary properties, with no contribution from stellar evolution or a biosphere, measured against the persistence-of-life ground;
> 2. a case where the governing conditions form an independent list with no coupling, no feedback and no cascade;
> 3. a case where the anchor is model agreement rather than an outcome that does not move when frames change;
> 4. a specific planetary process derived from the framework's own primitives alone that predicts a measured boundary.
>
> If any holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the three public links, with the note that the figures, not my summary of them, are the authority. If you want to use the machinery, the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground, and write down what would refute your result before you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]
