## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40, :44-48, :52, :56; Fig 12 :121; Fig 13 :14, :22, :24; Fig 2 :29-30.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: habitability is a maintained, cross-scale contact property governed by loops and gradients, grounded in persistence of life, with named processes as imports.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claim: carbonate-silicate thermostat, plate tectonics, magnetic shielding and biological feedback control habitability, with Venus as the broken-loop case.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **provenance of the named processes**. The shorthand says "imports with provenance"; the concrete claim must show which gap each import closes and where it came from. Dropping that record turns a generator into a recording.
- the **asymmetry of introduction and exclusion**. Fig 11 :61 is preserved in the shape (unmaintained, no authoring seat) but lost in the concrete language of "feedback regulation," which can smuggle in a designer (the teleology Lenton et al. 2020 removes).
- the **anchor definition**. The shorthand says "persistence of life"; the concrete literature often substitutes a spectral proxy or a modelled habitability index, which is a frame and not the anchor (Fig 2 :29-30).

One thing survives cleanly: the claim that habitability is a maintained contact property of a coupled system, governed by recycling loops and spendable gradients, with the sign of the loop deciding persistence. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
