# Expert verification packet — the Tether on long-term planetary habitability

**What this is:** a claim in a form a planetary scientist, astrobiologist or Earth-system scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` for the shape, `general` for the structural claims, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle the process list. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Long-term habitability is not a property of a planet alone. It is a maintained property of the contact between star, interior, surface and biosphere, and the ground that settles it is whether life actually persists. The conditions form a coupled union whose effects cascade across scales; what keeps a world habitable is the recycling loops and spendable gradients across those strata, not an ingredient list. The framework returns that shape and stops. Specific processes (carbon cycling, liquid water, tectonics, magnetic shielding, biological feedback) are provenanced imports from the world literature, not figure derivations. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the discriminators that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 2

- **Statement:** habitability is a capability of the agent-and-habitat pair, not of the planet; the anchor that settles it is an outcome that does not update with frames.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 — *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 — *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :29-30 — *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **Refuter:** a world whose long-term habitability is measured to be fully intrinsic, with no contribution from stellar evolution or a biosphere, against the persistence-of-life ground; or an anchor that moves when frames change.

### C2 — structure; checkable by reading Figures 12, 9 and 11

- **Statement:** the controlling conditions form a union compound with cascading contact and recycling loops; the sign of the loop decides whether the state persists.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 — *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 11 :52 — *"And contact cascades."* Fig 12 :121 — *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **Refuter:** a measured case where the conditions are independent, with no feedback and no cross-scale cascade; or a long-lived habitable world whose state is time-invariant in all governing fluxes.

### C3 — provenance; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 9

- **Statement:** the figures cannot name the specific processes; the process list is an import and its provenance is the whole issue.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 — *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 — *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*; Fig 6 :23 — *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** derive a specific planetary process from the six terms alone, with no world reading, and show it predicts a measured boundary. Then the shape-not-a-name limit is wrong for this question.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named master process. Carbon, water, weathering, tectonics and magnetic shielding are imports with provenance, not derivations.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The centre stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not that habitability is a state or a yes/no property. It is a maintained trajectory.
- Not that agreement among papers or famous reviewers settles anything. Shared-pool agreement is disqualified for a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that this packet settles the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. The standing refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. The concrete discriminators are:

1. A world whose long-term habitability is measured to be fully intrinsic, with no stellar or biospheric contribution.
2. A measured case with independent conditions and no feedback or cascade.
3. An anchor that is a model proxy and changes when frames change.
4. A planetary process derived from the figures alone that predicts a measured boundary.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, :52, :56 and Fig 2 :29-30, :40. Five short passages.
