## What the wider literature actually shows (D2, secondary only)

1. **The question is live and unsettled in physics itself.** The Wikipedia background says it is *"impossible to be certain what spacetime looks like at small scales"* and that *"there is no definitive reason that spacetime needs to be fundamentally smooth."* That corroborates F1 (smoothness is not guaranteed, it is a derivative) without giving an existence verdict.
2. **The experimental picture is bounds, not detections.** Chandra/Fermi/VERITAS excluded two diffusion-style foam models; Vasileiou et al. constrained stochastic photon-speed fuzziness beyond the Planck scale; the Holometer excluded a specific holographic-noise model. These are per-slot constraints (Fig 1 :19), not one averaged verdict, and their null results are exactly the "search that finds nothing" that Fig 8 :39 says is not a proof of absence.
3. **Named microstructures are bindings.** Spin foam is *"a version of quantum foam"* in one research programme. It is a candidate name for the small-scale slot, which is why Fig 9 :27-28 requires the residual be described before the frame is chosen.

No world source was read that confirms foam exists. None was read that proves it absent at all scales. The sources agree on a shared evidence approach (astrophysical propagation and interferometric noise), so their agreement is a pool talking, not several independent witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
