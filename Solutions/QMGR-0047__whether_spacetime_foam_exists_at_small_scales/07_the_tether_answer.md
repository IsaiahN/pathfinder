## 2. The shape (from the figures)

1. **The continuum is not a primitive; smoothness is a remainder.** Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"*; Fig 13 :124: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* So a smooth regime and a foam regime are what different compositions leave at different levels, not two substances.
2. **There is no smallest scale.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*; Fig 4 :33: *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."* "Foam at the smallest scale" is refused as written because there is no terminal floor. Foam, if it exists, is a level, not a bottom.
3. **The two regimes are arrangements of one six-term set.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Over the full shared atom set they are isomers; as currently describable they are at least a functional group (Fig 12 :124).
4. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Spin foam, causal sets and noncommutative geometry are bindings from physics frames onto the small-scale slot (Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). None is derived uniquely by the six.
5. **Existence is a gap, not a verdict.** No figure line says foam exists or does not. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :34-35 gives the instrument rule: *"It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Current null results are bounds, not a decision.
