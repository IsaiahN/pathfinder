## 0. The residual in effect terms

- **R1, existence (a ground question).** Does the smooth continuum description of spacetime fail at sufficiently small scales and give way to a fluctuating or discrete regime? - **R2, shape (a figure question).** If such a regime exists, what must it look like, given what the figures say about smoothness, levels and composition? What is owed is a constraint, a shape, not a name.

R by arity, symmetry, scale:
- Arity: two conditions (existence, shape), so a union compound with its own loops, not one hard question.
- Symmetry: the figures grant no privileged scale. There is no smallest and no largest level (Fig 12 :104, Fig 4 :33).

Fig 13 :124: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* So smoothness and discreteness are **derivatives**, not primitives. That single line reframes the question: "does smoothness persist to arbitrarily small scale" asks whether a particular composition keeps leaving the same remainder at every level, not whether a primitive is present.

## 3. Findings F1 to Fn (quote before concluding)

### F1 - Smoothness is a derivative, not a primitive (governs R2; triangulated)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"**
> **"A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

Fig 13 :124:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` substrate section: *"the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."*

So the continuum is excluded from the minimum set, and smoothness is explicitly a leftover of a turn. This is the decisive figure fact for R2. It gives a shape with no name: a smooth regime is what some composition leaves behind at a level; a foam regime is what a different composition leaves behind at another level. Neither requires a new primitive. Both are arrangements of the six.

**Mode: `general` (structural).** Triangulated across Fig 13 :43-44, :124 and the formula substrate section. Refuter: derive smoothness as a seventh irreducible member from the other five, or show a composition leaves no remainder anywhere.

### F2 - There is no bottom, so no terminal small scale (governs R2; triangulated)

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

Fig 4 :33:

> **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

Fig 13 :90:

> **"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."**

The phrase "foam at the *smallest* scales" carries a hidden terminal-bottom assumption. The figures refuse it: whatever scale is reached is a level whose composition can become an atom for a further level, and there is no bottom and no last route. So a candidate answer that says "at scale L spacetime is foam and that is the end" fails the figure. A candidate that says "at scales below L the composition leaving smoothness stops leaving it, and that regime is itself composable further" is figure-licensed.

**Mode: `general` (structural).** Triangulated across Fig 12 :104, Fig 4 :33, Fig 13 :90. Refuter: exhibit a terminal bottom at which composition cannot continue.

### F3 - The union of smoothness and foam is level-relative, not a new kind (governs R2; single-source for the recursion)

Fig 12 :103:

> **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**

Fig 12 :106 (as cited in the canonical reading): *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it"* (same clause as :104). Fig 13 :75:

> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Spacetime is not a primitive in this framework; it is a derivative with a structure in the slot. Foam, likewise, would be a derivative of the six at a level where the structure-in-the-slot arrangement changes. A discrete or network-like regime uses the network and gradient structures already held; it does not add a seventh member. The foam/smooth pair is one of level-relative irreducibility, not two substances.

**Mode: `specified`.** Single-source for the recursion claim (Fig 12), corroborated at low overlap by Fig 13 :75 on derivative structure. Refuter: show foam requires a primitive outside the six.
