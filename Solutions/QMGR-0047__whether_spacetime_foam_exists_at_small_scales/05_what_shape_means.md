## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on method: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a small-scale regime made of the six terms, in which the composition that leaves smoothness at larger scales no longer leaves it, and in which no scale is a terminal bottom. The name, spin foam or causal set or something not yet proposed, is not supplied.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. That is the proximity trick the essay calls *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach artifact describes effects, not microstructure.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument that bears directly here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several underlying causes create the same reading. The essay says *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it,"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: an interferometric or astrophysical null result reads "no foam signal at this sensitivity" and cannot comment on the source. Naming a microstructure from that reading would be the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. Why existence is a gap, not a silence

The essays and the figures agree that unreached and absent are different states. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

So section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records R1 (existence) as `gap`, not as "no" and not as "yes." That is the honest stop. It is a verdict about what the current instruments and figures can settle, not a statement about the world.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that foam exists, or that it does not, is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, let a discriminating instrument settle it, and never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction on "shape, not a name" (carried from the exemplar, applied here)

The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Gradient, spectrum and network are already among the six (Fig 13), and smoothness and discreteness are derivatives of them, as the figure text states. So the canonical shape-not-a-name limit here does not forbid discussing which held atoms compose a foam-like regime. It forbids claiming the six derive a unique named program. A binding such as "spin foam uses the network structure" is a provenanced mapping from the physics frame, not a derivation of a seventh term. This correction is recorded rather than silently applied.
