# Expert verification packet: the Tether on spacetime foam at small scales

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on shape, `general` on structure, `gap` on existence. Not `grounded`.** No small-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Smoothness is not a primitive of the framework; it is what a cross-kind composition leaves behind at a level (Fig 13 :124). The continuum was removed from the minimum set for failing the generating-set test (Fig 13 :43-44). Therefore a smooth regime and a foam regime are two arrangements of the same six-term set, not two substances; over the full shared atom set they are isomers (Fig 12 :117). There is no smallest scale, so foam cannot be a terminal floor (Fig 12 :104, Fig 4 :33). The framework returns this shape, not a name, and it does not decide existence, which is a `gap` to be settled only by an instrument that already fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35, Fig 8 :39).

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** smoothness and discreteness are derivatives, not primitives; the continuum is not a member of the minimum set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43-44 - *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"* and *"A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Fig 13 :124 - *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*
- **Refuter:** derive smoothness as a seventh irreducible member from the other five, or show a composition leaves no remainder anywhere. If either holds, C1 falls.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 4

- **Statement:** there is no terminal small scale; foam cannot be the bottom of spacetime.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* (also Fig 4 :33 - *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*). Fig 12 :117 - *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a level at which composition stops for lack of a further level, or show a foam regime requires an atom the smooth regime does not (which kills the isomer claim and leaves a functional-group claim standing).

### C3 - physics-facing; checkable against what instruments have actually returned

- **Statement:** existence is a `gap`; existing null results constrain models but do not settle the question, and the framework supplies only an instrument criterion.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :34-35 - *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
- **What to check:** do the cited results (Perlman et al. 2015; Vasileiou et al. 2015; the Fermilab Holometer results) in fact report bounds and model exclusions rather than a detection or a proof of absence? An honest "yes, bounds only" corroborates C3. A verified detection, or a proof that no instrument could ever resolve, would change it.
- **Refuter:** a ground measurement that settles foam present or absent. That closes the gap and upgrades the mode to `grounded`; this packet must then be rewritten.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that foam exists, and not that it does not. Existence is `gap`.
- Not a named microstructure. Spin foam, causal sets and noncommutative geometry are bindings from physics frames, provenanced as such, not derivations from the six.
- Not a terminal scale, a bottom of spacetime, or a last room.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that current null experiments refute foam. They constrain specific models.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; the answer is *"unreached until a better instrument exists."* Concretely:

1. Is any instrument already returning a small-scale reading that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35)?
2. Do photon propagation or interferometer correlations show a cross-kind remainder exactly where the smoothness-leaving composition should leave smoothness?
3. Standing falsifiers: derive one of the six from the other five; show smoothness is a primitive; exhibit a terminal bottom; get a ground measurement that names a unique microstructure or settles foam present or absent.
