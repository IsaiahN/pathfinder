## What is being decided, in effect terms

Two classes are offered: a system that pursues goals (call it A) and a system that follows instructions (call it B). The residual is the ambiguity: the same outward behaviour can be produced by both, so an observer cannot classify the system from behaviour alone. A says the difference is an intrinsic possession (a goal inside). B says the difference is that the aim comes from outside. The shape derived below refuses both as essences. It asks which link in the dependency chain the system can close in its own terms and whether its channel to the ground survives removal of the instructor. What each option leads to: an essence answer gives a name and cannot be checked; a contact answer gives a shape, a test, and a refuter.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The LLM was not the intelligence. The LLM was a tool." The same caution applies to this file.

Three figure-grounded reasons for the refusals:

1. A room is not an anchor. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
2. Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. A checker who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
