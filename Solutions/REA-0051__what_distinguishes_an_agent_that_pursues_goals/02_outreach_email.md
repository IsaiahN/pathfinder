# Outreach draft: validate the discriminator first, then decide about the framing

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two strains in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6 are understood and the channel-cut test is stated exactly as written there. The offer collapses on contact if the discriminator is oversold as a measurement.

---

**Subject:** One check for telling a goal-pursuing system from an instruction-following one

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific discriminator I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the discriminator does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** The same sequence of percepts and actions can be described as an agent pursuing a goal or as a tool following instructions. I want a checkable difference, not a definition of the word "agent." In one research field an agent is a delegate acting on a principal's behalf; in another a thermostat is an agent. The name cannot decide the class.
>
> **The shape claim (`general`, structural, not a measurement).** The difference is not an interior possession. It is a contact fact about the system and its habitat. Two things have to hold: the system can say what would count as progress in its own terms, rather than borrowing that comparison from the instruction stream; and its channel to the ground survives removal of the instructor. A tool's contact is supplied from outside and ends with the channel. An agent's aim remains in its own vocabulary when the channel is cut.
>
> **The operational form (`specified`, falsifiable).** Remove the external instruction stream and watch a single object slot, not an average. If a live residual persists on that slot and steers the next bet, the aim was held. If the aim collapses with the channel, the system was a tool. Aggregating across slots hides exactly this case, so the check must be per slot.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That the system has desires, intentions, consciousness, or human-like goals. That "agent" names a natural kind. That a panel of experts can settle this by agreement. That this test has been run on a real system by me. This is a shape and a proposed check, not a result.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. a system that pursues a goal while holding no comparison term in its own vocabulary, with the comparison demonstrably external;
> 2. an isolated interior improvement that changes contact with the ground, with no habitat change;
> 3. a single stable cross-field definition of "agent" that makes the label itself the classifier;
> 4. a pure tool, independently classified, that keeps an aim-like live slot after the instruction stream is cut;
> 5. a classification settled by expert vote with no ground reading.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three source links. The figure text, not my summary of it, is the authority.
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** it is public in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two rules for sending

1. Record the recipient's stage 1 verdict before any link is sent. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
2. Send to one person at a time through a public or opt-in channel. No list, no automation, no opt-out omitted. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 9 :60).
