## Restating the question as an effect

The ordinary term "fiscal capacity" bundles at least three different effects: (1) the ability to make payment in the currency the government itself issues, (2) the ability to command real labour, materials and energy without setting off inflation, and (3) the ability to meet obligations denominated in a currency the government does not issue. What fails to resolve is the apparent contradiction between the first, which cannot be denied for a floating-rate issuer, and the second and third, which plainly can bind. An answer would look like a separator: which quantity is the binding one, at which slot, and what anchor would settle it. That separator is bound here as an ordinary term and treated as the sought-for shape, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction".

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. No scraped contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: a room does not supply an anchor (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*); selecting for agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*); and showing a recipient the framework before taking their verdict spends the independence they contribute (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).
