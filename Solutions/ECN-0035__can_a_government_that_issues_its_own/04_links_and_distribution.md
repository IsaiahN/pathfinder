## What in the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The proponent side already splits the question.** MMN concedes price-stability constraints; Wray names inflation, not default, as the real problem. The "cannot run out" slogan is a statement about the nominal closure, and its own authors say so when writing carefully. Use this to dissolve the apparent contradiction rather than to pick a side.
2. **The external constraint is a separate literature.** Original sin and currency mismatch are not MMT criticisms; they come from international finance. They describe atoms the domestic issuer does not hold. Low overlap, so it is the strongest world witness.
3. **The per-slot problem appears independently in ecology.** Carrying capacity is measured for a specific environment and a specific species with specific resources; it varies by region and time. It is a shape analogue, not an economic result, and is labelled as such.
4. **Agreement is not a ground.** The reported 2019 survey result is evidence about a room, not about the world. By Fig 9 :60 it can filter which venue gets tried first; it cannot decide the question.
5. **A convention that decides an outcome stops being one.** Fig 10 :47. A government declaring itself capable does not make itself capable, just as an ecosystem does not gain carrying capacity by declaring its population sustainable.
