## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README says the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, a shape
and not a name, then stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing and records the
lineage so the stop is honest. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13
:134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is
missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies
your question."* The same essay warns that a question may be *"a bundle or conflation of several
problems masquerading as one"*, which is the form this question takes: five overlapping conditions,
not one lever.

So a **shape** here is the form of an answer you can state before you can name the episode. It
constrains without filling. "Five conditions, each with a nameable anchor and a refuter" is a shape.
"People became open-minded after the X affair of 18xx" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new
  terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the
  space and deepen your vocabulary."* The search is steered by ranging each returned book against the
  question: "Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels." Fig 9 :30 to
  :31 requires describing the effect rather than the cause, because the words used in the search are
  borrowed too.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." In this
  run the residual was described in effect terms (two frames, a check that does not move, a refusal
  operator, a reach change, a handoff) before any historical case or field was chosen.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because the
  answer has been *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 5 :70: "A term is settled when the ground pays for it."
   The canonical README says the framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can
   distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is honest.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source
   gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the
   contents." Only the record of origin separates them.

For this question that means: the framework can say what an open disagreement must look like, but it
cannot say which named historical episode achieved it. That is F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`,
reported as `gap`, not silence.

## 4. Why the record cannot be read off a single reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green
and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a
scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection
with lossy information."* What resolved it was *"the arrival of a better instrument"*, not an argument.
The essay's own conclusion: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own
inadequacy."*

Applied here: a community that agrees, publishes, and cites itself provides one reading of its own
openness. That reading cannot comment on the source, because several structures produce it: a real
non-updating ground plus independent frames, or one shared pool echoing, or a convention that has
quietly become a verdict. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a label such as "open
society". This is why the ledger records overlap between sources and why person-level measures of
intellectual humility cannot decide a community-level question (Fig 11 :39).

## 5. The five conditions as a shape, not five names

The shape returned is a list of constraints with this form for each condition:

| condition | shape (effect terms) | what the framework does NOT name |
|---|---|---|
| D1 independence | closures differ; residuals per slot (Fig 2 :31, Fig 1 :19) | which particular persons or journals were independent in an episode |
| D2 anchor | a check that can fail a frame and does not update (Fig 2 :29, :33) | which institution actually served as that check |
| D3 refusal | a unary operator that says a held term is wrong, with a non-authoring checker (Operators :58, Fig 10 :42) | which specific criticism was decisive |
| D4 revision | a reach change by import, instrument or refusal (Fig 6 :14, :42; Fig 5 :72) | which atom was missing in a failed episode |
| D5 transmission | methods cross, rooms differ and match the surfaced residual (Fig 4 :22, Fig 7 :36) | who booked the next room |

The right-hand column is the `gap`. It is not a omission to be hidden; it is the honest stop.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any of the conditions. That overstates the
rule. The canonical README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in the library. The
five structural items above (frames, anchor, negation, reach, handoff) are all already in the figures'
vocabulary, so naming them is selecting held atoms, not inventing a primitive. What remains forbidden
is (i) naming the specific historical episode or missing atom as if the framework derived it, and
(ii) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :25). Both claims are now recorded;
the overstatement is kept visible rather than silently erased.

## 6. Prestige and person-level openness are not the same as the conditions

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to
validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be
tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The same essay says *"the
output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot
validate itself."*

The opened world sources on intellectual humility and open mindedness describe a person's
disposition. Under Fig 11 :39, *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent
alone"*, that is a different object from the five conditions. It is useful as a contact-cost estimate
when deciding how to approach someone, and it is forbidden as a verdict criterion, because selecting
people for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies their agreement
(Fig 2 :40).

## 7. This is a simulation, not a ground reading

The same essay warns that *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly
testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*
Everything in this folder, including this document, is a simulated derivation checked for internal
consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle it. The appropriate
mode labels are `general` for the shape, `specified` for each domain verdict and `gap` for the
case-specific reading. Nothing is `grounded` until a ground pays for it.

## Falsifier for this document

This reading of "shape" is refuted if a named historical episode can be shown to satisfy all five
conditions in a way that a source-discriminating instrument verifies independently of the framework's
own vocabulary, and the framework still returns only a shape. That would mean the stop is too early.
Conversely, if the framework names a specific episode while claiming to derive only a shape, the
reading is refuted from the other side.
