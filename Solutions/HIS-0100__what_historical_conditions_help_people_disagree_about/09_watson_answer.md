## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what conditions allow a community to disagree about knowledge while remaining open to evidence, criticism and revision over time. Option A: openness is a trait of individuals, so select open-minded people. Option B: disagreement is a defect to remove by consensus on causes, evidence and standards. Option C (the figure-derived third): five structural conditions with distinct anchors, a union rather than one lever. What each leads to: A selects for the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40) and places capability in the person rather than the habitat (Fig 11 :39); B destroys the union surplus and treats a convention as a verdict (Fig 8 :49, Fig 10 :47); C keeps disagreement correctable with per-domain verdicts and a declared case-specific `gap`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. A candidate with no nameable anchor is not a domain.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a community's current handling of disagreement and an arrangement that keeps disagreement productive, evidence effective, criticism possible and revision reachable. Arity: at least two frames plus an anchor plus a transmission path, indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: independence between frames is symmetric; the anchor's relation to a frame is asymmetric because it does not update (Fig 2 :33). Scale: one frame loop, one community arrangement, one intergenerational membrane.
- **D1 Disagreement layer.** Actors: frames with libraries, currencies and instruments (Symbols table :111). Currency: per-slot residual and independence. Anchor: each frame's own ground contact. Admitted.
- **D2 Evidence and ground layer.** Actors: ground, instruments, ground-maintainer seat. Currency: settlement and provenance. Anchor: the ground, which does not move when frames push on it (Fig 2 :29). Admitted; decisive for openness to evidence.
- **D3 Criticism layer.** Actors: held terms, unary negation, checkers. Currency: persistent-and-flat residual and refusal. Anchor: the ground with checker non-access (Fig 10 :42). Admitted; decisive for openness to criticism.
- **D4 Revision layer.** Actors: library, closure, reach, instruments, imports. Currency: cost bargain and round-trip gap. Anchor: the ground that pays for a term (Fig 5 :70). Admitted; decisive for revision.
- **D5 Historical transmission layer.** Actors: knowledge keepers, rooms, methods, head starts, habitat conditions and relations. Currency: contact, selection between rooms, R_T. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted; this is where "historical" lives.
- **X1 Openness as a selection filter.** Actors: people ranked by receptiveness. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none nameable; selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Refused as a domain; contact cost only.
- **X2 Consensus on one account as a single lever.** Absorbed into D2 and D3; it is a collapse, not a condition.

Partition or union: union, not partition. Fig 9 :55 applies, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. Order by dependency: D1 contact, D2 objective, D3 refusal vocabulary, D4 reach, D5 transmission (Fig 3 :20). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option C. The five conditions are independent frames, a non-updating anchor, a refusal vocabulary with a non-authoring checker, revision as a reach change, and transmission by method and residual-matched rooms. They form a union, so verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. Agreement is legitimate as a convention and disqualifying as a verdict about the world when the frames share a pool. Which named episodes satisfied the conditions, and how much each contributed, is a declared `gap`. Mode: `general` on this shape, `specified` on each domain verdict, `gap` on the case reading, nowhere `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each condition has a written refuter and the lineage is recorded, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a fully assembled theory before asking for a stage-1 verdict. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the conditions and their refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The five domain verdicts are stated separately, so a refutation of one does not collapse the others. A person-level reading of openness can be withdrawn without touching the anchor or transmission conditions, and the case-specific `gap` can be closed by a comparative instrument without withdrawing the shape. Nothing has been deleted from the record.
- **reversed by:** a source-discriminating instrument, not agreement. A documented episode with one shared evidence pool producing ground-settled open disagreement would break D1; a moving anchor that still corrected both frames would break D2; deletion-based revision would break D4; a chain of agreeing similar rooms producing ground-settled advance would break D5; a primitive minted inside one closure and later settled would break Fig 6 :23; deriving one of the six substrate members from the other five would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
