**Hard precondition:** do not send until the reading in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` has survived a

**Subject:** Five conditions for disagreement that stays correctable, and what would break each one

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the
> framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is
> nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on each condition, `gap` on the case reading):** a
> community can disagree about knowledge and stay open to evidence, criticism and revision only when
> five conditions hold at once. Each has a different anchor, so they cannot be merged into one score.
>
> 1. **Independent frames.** The disagreeing parties must draw on different evidence. If both read
>    the same report, they are one frame with two names, and their agreement is the pool talking.
>    The figures put it as "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same
>    evidence" (Fig 2 :31) and "Independence is what makes agreement mean something" (Fig 2 :32).
> 2. **An anchor that does not update.** Evidence bites only if the check can fail a frame and cannot
>    be moved by what the frame believes. "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not
>    change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29), and "The anchor must not update"
>    (Fig 2 :33). A standard that a committee revises in response to the claim is not an anchor.
> 3. **A vocabulary that can refuse.** Criticism needs a term that says a held term is wrong. "A
>    refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate" (Operators
>    table :58). The checker must also be unable to author the claim: "A verifier that can reconstruct
>    the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42).
> 4. **Revision as a change of reach.** Collecting more commentary inside one closure cannot revise
>    anything, because "no search, however long, adds an atom" (Fig 8 :32). What changes the reach is
>    a provenanced import, a sharpened instrument, or a refusal: "a library that grows is not a reach
>    that grows" (Fig 5 :72).
> 5. **Transmission that selects rooms against the residual.** A tradition that agrees with its
>    predecessor is "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13).
>    "The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced" (Fig 7 :36). What
>    crosses is a method, not a recording.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that these conditions are a personality trait, that a community should
> first agree on causes, or that the framework tells us which historical episodes satisfied them.
> The last of those is an honest `gap`, not a silence. Anything that decides a knowledge outcome by
> agreement has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47).
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a case where one shared evidence pool was enough to produce a ground-settled, independently
>    checkable result while disagreement stayed open;
> 2. a case where a supposed anchor moved in response to a frame and still corrected both frames;
> 3. a case where a term genuinely disappeared from the record rather than being refused by addition;
> 4. a case where criticism worked although the checker could reconstruct the claim from the interior;
> 5. a chain of agreeing, similar rooms that produced ground-settled advance with no new instrument,
>    import or residual-matched room;
> 6. a primitive minted inside one closure with no import, later settled by the ground;
> 7. one of the six substrate members derived from the other five.
>
> If any holds, that condition is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me
> than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them. I will send them only after your verdict on the separator above is recorded.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, framework second | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Refuter written in advance | The framework's own discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule: general, specified, grounded, state which. |
| No receptiveness ranking | Fig 2 :40, agreement about the world is worthless when the frames share a pool. Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. |
| The case reading is a `gap` | Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". |
| Agreement is scoped to conventions | Fig 2 :41 and Fig 10 :47, agreement about shared meaning is the product until it decides an outcome. |
| One-to-one, opt-in venues only | Fig 7 :35, a room's agreement is not a verdict; a bulk list selected for belief is one long room. |
| Links only after stage 1 | Fig 10 :42, a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
