## Wider literature that bears on this question

Three clusters were read deliberately across low-overlap fields:

1. **Human cognition and judgement.** Intellectual humility, open-mindedness and the critical-rationalist account all put fallibility at the centre, but they locate it in the person. Fig 11 :39 locates capability in agent-and-habitat, so these sources corroborate the need for a fallibility vocabulary while conflicting with a person-only reading. The conflict was returned to the census and resolved as a domain difference, not used to flip the verdict.
2. **The social organisation of disagreement.** Scientific controversy, scientific dissent, deliberative democracy and adversarial collaboration describe institutions that make disagreement statable and fair. They are one science-studies and social-epistemology pool, so their agreement is one witness under Fig 2 :40, not four.
3. **Research practice and historical infrastructure.** Open science, the replication crisis, Registered Reports and the Royal Society motto describe ways of keeping the check external and the record public. The motto is a genuinely distinct historical witness; the rest share the metascience pool.

The most useful points for a checker are these: adversarial collaboration requires both sides to accept the test ex ante (a convention that makes the disagreement statable without deciding it); Tetlock and Mitchell warn it is least feasible where falsification criteria are absent (the broken objective link, Fig 3 :27); deliberative democracy's second-generation standards aim at both consensus and clarifying conflict (agreement as convention, not verdict); and failed replication is described as elimination of unsupported hypotheses (revision as reach change, not library growth).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the five conditions and their refuters first. Do not open with the framework's history.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if each condition were absent before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A mass send chosen for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Spend contact effort on people whose public work touches the residual from a different closure.

### The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the five conditions and the seven refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict *before* any framework material is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three framework links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits method transfer; Fig 4 :25 forbids sending a recording as if it were knowledge.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 are written down and none has fired in this simulated run, but no ground check has been performed.

## Legal and contact hygiene

No personal emails of named individuals appear anywhere in this folder. No list was purchased or
scraped, and there is no bulk-send plan. Only institutional and opt-in channels are used in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many
jurisdictions; an opt-in venue avoids that failure as well as the structural one.
