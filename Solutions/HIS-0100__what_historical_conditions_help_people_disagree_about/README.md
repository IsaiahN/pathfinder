## What an answer would look like

Stated as an effect before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction": a set of necessary
structural conditions, each with a nameable anchor and a refuter, such that an episode of sustained
disagreement remains correctable by the ground rather than collapsing into mutual update, into a shared
evidence pool, or into a convention that silently decides the outcome. The sought-for shape was bound
as an ordinary term and allowed to accrue a residual rather than treated as a preferred answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no purchased or scraped contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree, and no receptiveness column.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles the question it reports on.

Three reasons in the figures' own words: a room's agreement is not a verdict (Fig 7 :35), agreement
about a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict (Fig 2 :40), and a verifier that can
reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). The aim of being least overwhelmed is usable
only as a contact cost estimate; being most receptive is forbidden as a selection criterion because
it manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
