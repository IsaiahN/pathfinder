## Three things in the world literature that bear directly on this residual

1. **Contact, not introspection, is the driver.** Ward and Szabó define acculturation as change following *"Intercultural contact"*, and Kreienkamp et al. make *"culture and contact"* the prerequisites of the psychological experience. Both match Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* They are one evidence pool, so one witness, not two.
2. **The three conditions overlap in the measurement literature.** Hu and Cheung find that *"the usefulness of analyzing different aspects of social identity on social integration is challenged."* Kreienkamp et al. report *"a crucial disconnect between theory and practice"* and a framework in which affect, behavior, cognition, and desire are *"not experienced as distinct entities."* This is the union reading of Fig 9 :55 from a different pool, and it is why the solution refuses a single blended adaptation score.
3. **The low-overlap witness.** Larue et al. find that dispersing caribou repeat the habitat selection tactics of their natal range. That is Fig 11 :66-67 (*"substitution of one habitat for another"*) and Fig 11 :69 (*"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*) observed in a system with no shared evidence pool with migration psychology. It corroborates the mechanism shape, not the human content.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. If a source had conflicted, the derivation would return to the census, not soften the reading.

- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 4 :30** — *"So take the round trip and measure the gap."* Treat `09_watson_answer.md` as a lossy condensation of the figures, not as identical to them; the gap is the thing to check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim, and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the verdict; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground, per person and per condition, with the refuter in view.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The output in `02_outreach_email.md` is `specified` and ungrounded by design; it is offered for checking, not for belief.
