## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its content. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *identity, belonging, and emotional life are person-and-habitat, altered by a change of contact and a substitution of habitat, with two conditions kept separate.* "Integration," "assimilation," "bicultural identity," and "resilience" are names, not shapes.

## 2. How this shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then find recurring new terms that overlap it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31: *"rather than the cause"* and Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email leads with a checkable residual, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the union finding in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1.3.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The shape is what remains after the search, not a quote from an authority.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this question withholds names

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For COG-0050 neither is held. No source-discriminating instrument names the atom a given person needs, and no import has a shadow test. So "which practice should this person adopt" is recorded as `gap`, not answered.

**Correction to my own §1 and §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1).** I initially read "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any term in the answer. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the relevant atoms (self-concepts, cultural schemas, emotion concepts, the weighting w) are already in the person's library. Naming one of them is a binding from the social-science frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What stays forbidden is claiming that the binding is framework-derived, or that it moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*; the binding adds none, so it adds no evidentiary weight until provenanced). Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second, sharper correction: an earlier draft tried to cast physical primitives onto identity, calling a routine a kind of energy and an emotion concept a kind of gradient. That was a strain (S1 in `01` section 6). *They're Scanning Books* warns: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Fig 13 therefore governs the honest stop and the lineage, not the content of identity.

## 4. The source problem is the reason the stop is honest

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can produce one reading. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* And: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a person's self-report of "I feel I belong" is a reading. Its source could be genuine recognition, shared history, a display rule, a response to the interviewer, or a narrative the person has learned to tell. The framework cannot name the source from the reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* And: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

This is also why the person's verdict is an anchor for "is this a good answer for this person" (Fig 2 :37) but only an instrument for cross-cultural comparison. Fig 2 :37: instruments *"can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*

## 5. Why the answer is a shape of a union, not a single claim

*Library of Babel* gives the bundle test. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The three things the question asks about (identity, belonging, emotional life) overlap: each is a compound with its own loop, so a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. The shape therefore has the form of **two conditions plus a discriminator**, not one factor and not an average.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the level distinction: *"The group shares methods, never recordings."* Personal identity and collective identity share a kernel, but only methods cross between them (Fig 4 :22). A person's migration story is a recording; what can travel to the group is the method by which the story was checked.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this package is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A package of famous names agreeing with this shape would be a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The correct ask is a refuter, not an endorsement.

Same essay, on standing: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` through `11` are a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They cannot settle the question they report on. *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* And its own warning: solutions *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* The pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4 exists to make that failure detectable rather than invisible.

## 7. Consequence for this solution

- The shape survives: two conditions, per-person indexing, human anchor for a question about people, a union that must be unbundled, and an honest `gap` for the atom.
- No discipline-level name (integration, biculturalism, identity style) is promoted to an answer.
- No claim of `grounded` status anywhere.
- The correction in §3 is kept visible rather than erased, as is the S1 strain in `01` section 6.
