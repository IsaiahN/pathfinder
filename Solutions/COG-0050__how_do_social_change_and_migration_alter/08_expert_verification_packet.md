## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Identity, belonging, and emotional life are a property of person-and-habitat, never of the person alone. Social change and migration alter them by changing contact: migration substitutes a habitat, so the person must reproduce what their identity goals required and inherits actors they did not choose. What moves a person's reach is contact with a frame whose closure differs, so the alteration is an import rather than more introspection. Two people from one origin diverge because the weighting of private history against inherited priors differs. A practice that once produced belonging can change valence when the residual changes. Personal and collective identity share a kernel, but only methods cross between them. The three conditions form a union that must be unbundled and measured per person, never averaged. Which specific atom a person needs remains unnamed until a source-discriminating instrument names it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 6

- **Statement:** capability, and by extension identity and belonging, is a property of person-and-habitat; the bound moves only on contact with a differing closure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled habitat substitution that removes social payment for what a person does, with identity and belonging unchanged; or an identity change with no atom entering from a differing closure that still passes the ground test.

### C2 - field-facing; checkable against the person-level measurement literature

- **Statement:** the phenomenon is a union of overlapping conditions, not a single factor, and must be measured per person rather than as a group average.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** does any existing instrument discriminate a failure of contact maintenance from a failure of reach renewal? Hu and Cheung 2024 report that *"the usefulness of analyzing different aspects of social identity on social integration is challenged"*; Kreienkamp et al. 2024 report *"a crucial disconnect between theory and practice."* Are those the same failure the figure names, or a different one?
- **Refuter:** a single intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types with no categorically different residue; or an averaged statistic that predicts prospectively and per person which named individual will lose belonging next.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation, with a field-facing tail

- **Statement:** personal and collective identity are two levels sharing a kernel; only methods cross, and the round trip has a measured gap. Emotion concepts are atoms with typed bonds, so a culture that orders them differently forces an import.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information"* and Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- **What to check:** Mesquita et al. write that *"the emotional constituents are assembled in culture specific ways that are meaningful and predictable"* and Heine describes *"display rules are the culturally specific rules that govern when, how intensely, and what facial expressions are appropriate in a given situation."* Do these describe an import of new atoms, or only a rebonding of atoms already held? The framework's answer is the latter unless a genuinely new category enters.
- **Refuter:** an emotion category that maps one to one across cultures in frequency, intensity, appraisal, and display rule with no residue; or a personal identity recording carried to the collective level and back with zero R_T.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that integration, assimilation, separation, or marginalization is the answer. These are discipline-level bindings with no source gate here.
- Not that migration damages identity, or that belonging is a stock recoverable by reversing the move.
- Not that a stable self is a fixed inner essence that migration uncovers. It failed the anchor test and was excluded.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The union keeps its own loop.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument and person-level longitudinal measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Here:
