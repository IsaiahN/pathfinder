## The residual in effect terms

For a person s, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|`: **b** is the person's belief about who they are and where they belong, mixed by the weighting **w** from private history and inherited cultural priors; **a** is the act of moving, acculturating, or adapting to changed norms; **Γ(b,a)** is what the person's library of self-concepts, cultural schemas, and emotion concepts predicts will happen; **o'** is what the social environment actually returns, namely whether belonging is granted, whether emotion terms map, and whether the self-narrative still fits. R is indexed by person, never averaged into "the migrant experience." Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Bound the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): the answer would be a pair of conditions whose conjunction keeps a person's contact with a paying ground reachable and their reach moving outward through a differing closure, plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of one condition from the other. That shape is now bound, so the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of receptive readers produces a convention, not corroboration.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: harvested personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest stop

The shape survives; the names do not. Identity, belonging, and emotional life are properties of person-and-habitat, altered by a change of contact and by habitat substitution, not a fixed inner essence that migration merely uncovers. Which specific atom a given person must import is a `gap`: the framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). This package cannot settle the question it reports on.
