# The Tether's answer: how social change and migration alter identity, belonging, and emotional life

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a person s, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|`: the gap between what a person's self-and-belonging library predicts a setting will return and what that setting actually returns. When the setting changes through social change or migration, the return changes faster than the library, and the residual spikes. The question asks how the person's identity, belonging, and emotional life are altered by that spike. It is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not one hard problem: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). Social change and migration are kept as separate operations: one alters the habitat around a person who stays, the other substitutes the habitat for a person who moves (Fig 11 :66).

## 2. The shape

1. **Identity and belonging are person-and-habitat, not person alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* The alteration is therefore a change of relation, not a discovery of a hidden inner core. The "fixed inner essence" reading failed the anchor test and was excluded at the census.
2. **Migration is habitat substitution with two silent failures.** Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* What fails is what you could not reproduce (Fig 11 :68) and what you carried in unnoticed (Fig 11 :69). Social change does the same to a stationary person by changing the field around them.
3. **The mechanism is contact with a differing closure.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Import is *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (Fig 6 :14). More introspection inside the same library adds no atom (Fig 8 :32).
4. **Two people from one origin diverge by the weighting w.** Symbols table: *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."* Over two identical cultures of origin, siblings with different private histories lean differently on inherited priors, so the same new setting returns different residuals.
5. **A carried practice changes valence when the residual changes.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* A practice that produced belonging can become a liability without itself changing.
6. **Emotional life changes when emotion concepts must cross a closure.** Emotion categories are atoms and their relations are bonds (Fig 12 :5, :46). A culture that orders them differently forces an import or leaves a term with no matching source. The person's own verdict is the anchor for "is this a good answer for this person" (Fig 2 :37), but a cross-cultural comparison uses that verdict as an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they don't measure.
7. **The personal and collective levels share a kernel.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The round trip has a gap R_T that must be measured (Fig 4 :30). A personal story is a recording; the method by which it was checked can travel.
8. **Which specific atom a person needs is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Naming a discipline's strategy as the answer would be a binding with no source gate or shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any acculturation strategy (integration, assimilation, separation, marginalization) is *the* answer. Those are names from one evidence pool, not framework outputs.
- Not that migration damages identity, or that belonging is a fixed stock that can be lost and recovered by reversing the move.
- Not that a stable self is a fixed inner essence that migration merely uncovers. That fails the anchor test and is excluded.
- Not a derivation of identity from culture, and no new physical primitive (S1 strain resolved by re-census, `01` section 6).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the person level settles `left(R,φ)` here.
- Not that researcher agreement or prestige settles any of it. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and person-level longitudinal contact, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **C1 instrument:** does contact with what pays stay reachable? Measured as the bracket between expected and actual social response per person, not as a group average (Fig 1 :19; Fig 10 :26).
- **C2 instrument:** does reach move outward through a differing closure? Measured as an atom entering from a frame the person did not author, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52).
- **Source discriminator:** which of several sources produced a "I belong" reading? Until an instrument discriminates these, the atom stays unnamed (F10).
- **Standing falsifiers:** a habitat substitution that leaves identity unchanged; identity change with no import; belonging lost while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high; two identical histories and w diverging; a practice whose valence is invariant across a change of residual; an emotion category mapping one to one in all respects; a single lever restoring both conditions with no residue; a zero R_T across the scale membrane. All are written before the run in `01` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six physical terms in Fig 13 are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; they govern the physical substrate, not the content of a person's identity, and were not carried onto it. The answer stops where instruments can no longer discriminate sources and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a strategy for migrants and does not claim the question is settled. It says that identity, belonging, and emotional life are a property of person-and-habitat, so social change and migration alter them by changing contact: migration substitutes a habitat, and the person must reproduce what their identity required while inheriting actors they did not choose. What moves a person's reach is contact with a frame whose closure differs, so the alteration is an import rather than more introspection. Two people from one origin diverge because their weighting of private history against inherited priors differs, and a practice that once gave belonging can change valence when the setting changes. Personal and collective identity share a kernel, but only methods cross between them. The three conditions form a union that must be unbundled and measured per person, never averaged. What the framework offers is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which specific atom a person needs stays open until a source-discriminating instrument names it.
