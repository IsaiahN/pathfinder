## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the names do not. Identity, belonging, and emotional life are properties of person-and-habitat, altered by a change of contact and a substitution of habitat. The person must reproduce what their identity required, inherits actors they did not choose, and moves their reach only through contact with a differing closure. Two conditions (contact maintained, reach renewed) form a union, kept separate and measured per person, never averaged. Two people from one origin diverge by w; a carried practice can change valence; a personal and a collective identity share a kernel with a measured round-trip gap. Which specific atom a person needs is a `gap`: the framework returns a shape, not a name. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

A source-discriminating instrument that names the needed atom for a named person; a habitat substitution that leaves identity unchanged; an identity change with no import from a differing closure; belonging lost while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high; two identical histories and w diverging; a practice whose valence is invariant across a change of residual; a zero R_T across the scale membrane.
