## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39, :66-67, :49-51 (habitat, substitution, valence), Fig 6 :14, :23 (import), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 4 :22, :30 (membrane, round trip).

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-person indexing**, which the discipline-level reading converts into a group mean (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **union rule**, which the discipline-level reading turns into four mutually exclusive strategy types, a partition rather than a union (Fig 9 :55);
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**, which the discipline-level reading breaches by naming a strategy as the framework's answer (Fig 9 :32; Fig 8 :50-52, where convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a structure must sit in the relation between person and setting, and the alteration runs through a change of contact. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
