## Wider literature that bears on the derivation

1. **The anchor is a measurement, not a consensus.** Fixsen 2009 gives 2.72548 ± 0.00057 K. This is the non-updating anchor for the *fact* of uniformity (Fig 2 :29). It does not by itself name the source (F8 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).
2. **A genuine alternative exists.** Lehners 2009 states that in ekpyrotic/cyclic models *"the big bang is described as a collision of branes - and thus the big bang is not the beginning of time"* and that the contracting phase yields *"a nearly scale-invariant spectrum of cosmological perturbations containing a significant non-gaussian component."* So the uniform reading is a projection with several possible sources; the discriminator is non-Gaussianity and tensors, not the reading itself.
3. **The mechanism is not settled at the level of initial conditions.** The opened Cosmic inflation article records Earman and Mosterín's 1999 conclusion that there were *"no good grounds for admitting any of the models of inflation into the standard core of cosmology"* and Penrose's argument that inflation *"requires extremely specific initial conditions of its own."* These are low-overlap voices (philosophy of science and mathematical physics) inside a cosmology source, and they corroborate the "no last room" finding rather than the rejection of the shape.
4. **Observational confirmation is real but non-discriminating.** The same article records n_s = 0.968 ± 0.006 and r below about 0.06 at 95 percent confidence, "consistent with the null hypothesis, but still also consistent with many remaining models of inflation." Corroboration of predictions is not discrimination among sources.
