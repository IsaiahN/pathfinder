# The Tether's answer on the cosmic horizon problem

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** The CMB uniformity is a grounded measurement; this derivation is a simulated check against the figures, not a ground reading (`05` section 6).

## 1. The residual it answers

The CMB is near 2.725 K and uniform to about one part in 100,000 (Fixsen 2009; Planck via the opened Cosmic inflation article). In a hot Big Bang without an early accelerated epoch, patches more than about 2 degrees apart on the last-scattering surface were outside one another's particle horizons, so no subluminal signal could equalize them. The residual is the gap between the prediction of uncorrelated temperatures and the reading of a shared one. It is indexed per patch, not averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). It is a union compound of contact, thermalization and later stretching, bundled with flatness and monopoles (`06` Q1).

## 2. The shape

1. **Uniformity is unreachable inside the standard non-inflationary closure.** Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* More searching in that closure cannot manufacture a causal link its atoms forbid. This is a closure failure, not a hard puzzle (F1).
2. **Only an import moves the wall.** Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The missing atom, an early accelerated epoch, came from the particle-physics frame (Guth 1980, Starobinsky, Linde). It needs a source gate and a shadow test (F2, F3).
3. **The shape any answer must have.** Now-disconnected patches were earlier in causal contact and near thermal equilibrium; a structure mediated between the connected beginning and the smooth state; expansion then stretched the equalized patch beyond the horizon. Any proposal that does not change causal contact is out (Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*). Inflation, varying c, ekpyrotic/cyclic and big bounce all pass this filter, so the filter is not a verdict (F5).
4. **The framework returns a shape, not the name.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Naming inflation is a binding from the physics frame onto the shape, provenance recorded; it is not a figure derivation and it does not add an atom to the world (F4).
5. **The closing instrument is still being built.** The uniform, nearly scale-invariant reading does not determine its source. Non-Gaussianity, the tensor-to-scalar ratio and the perturbation spectrum are the discriminators. Current data (n_s = 0.968 ± 0.006, r below about 0.06) are consistent with inflation but also with other models (F8).
6. **No last room.** Fig 7 :6 *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 13 *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."* Inflation pushes the initial-conditions and measure questions back rather than terminating them (F7).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the framework proves inflation is correct, selects a model, or returns a spectral index or e-fold count.
- Not that the horizon problem is merged with flatness and monopoles into one verdict. It is a union; verdicts stay separate.
- Not that the temperature uniformity is disputed. The fact is grounded by instruments; only the source is open.
- Not grounded for the derivation. No source-discriminating measurement was taken or cited as settling the mechanism.
- Not that the word "inflation" is a figure output. It is a provenanced binding.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

- **Non-Gaussianity test:** Lehners 2009 predicts a *"significant non-gaussian component"* for ekpyrotic models. A measured level would favour or disfavour that source.
- **Tensor test:** the tensor-to-scalar ratio r; current data put r below about 0.06 at 95 percent confidence, still consistent with many models.
- **Fact test:** a measurement showing large, causally structured temperature differences beyond 2 degrees would remove the residual itself.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: contact-unchanged mechanism; derivation of uniformity inside the standard closure; an anchor that updates on the frame; a discriminator that excludes every contact-plus-stretch mechanism.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is triangulated across Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :14/:23, Fig 11 :40 and Fig 9 :32. The mechanism binding is `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`, with strong but non-discriminating world corroboration. The literal question *which mechanism did it* is a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*). The framework stops where the instruments stop discriminating and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not prove inflation and does not name a mechanism. It says the observed uniformity of the cosmic microwave background cannot be reached from inside the standard hot Big Bang closure, because a closed set cannot compose its way to a causal contact its atoms forbid. What closes the gap is an import from a different frame, and the framework returns only the shape that import must have: the regions now called disconnected were once in contact and near equilibrium, a structure mediated between that beginning and the smooth state, and expansion then stretched the equalized patch beyond the horizon. Every serious alternative, including varying c, ekpyrotic/cyclic and a big bounce, is a contact-changing lever of that shape, so the shape is not a verdict for any one of them. Inflation is a provenanced binding onto the shape, supported by measurements of the perturbation spectrum but not yet separated from its rivals by a discriminating instrument. The center stays open until that instrument exists, and the refuters that would break the shape were written before this answer.
