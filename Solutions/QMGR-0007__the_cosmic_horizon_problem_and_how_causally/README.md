## The question restated as an effect

The observable universe is nearly the same temperature in every direction, about 2.725 K, uniform to roughly one part in 100,000 (Fixsen 2009; Planck). Yet in a hot Big Bang without an early accelerated epoch, regions of the last-scattering surface more than about 2 degrees apart had no time to exchange a light signal, so the prediction "no causal contact, no shared temperature" fails against the reading "near-perfect shared temperature." What is being decided is what kind of thing could close that gap, and what each candidate answer commits to. An answer would look like a shape: a constraint any acceptable mechanism must satisfy, written down as an ordinary term with its own residual and its own refuter, not a final name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that inflation is right.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. A room does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29, Fig 7 :35). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40). Asking people to trust the framework before they check the residual spends the only thing they contribute (Fig 10 :42, :52). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.
