## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and is triangulated: uniformity is unreachable inside the standard closure, so a contact-changing import is owed; that import must make earlier-connected patches, mediate their equilibration, and stretch them apart; the framework can characterise this but cannot name the mechanism. Naming inflation is a provenanced binding with strong but non-discriminating world corroboration, not a figure output and not `grounded`. The literal mechanism question is a `gap` of the unreached kind. Inflation also does not close initial conditions, so there is no last room.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states five refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The binding to inflation is removable without touching the shape derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminant (non-Gaussianity level or tensor amplitude) that excludes every contact-plus-stretch mechanism would reverse the binding and break the shape; a measurement of large causally structured temperature differences beyond 2 degrees, or a derivation of uniformity inside the standard closure, would break the shape itself; a figure re-census admitting a terminal room would break the no-last-room finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
