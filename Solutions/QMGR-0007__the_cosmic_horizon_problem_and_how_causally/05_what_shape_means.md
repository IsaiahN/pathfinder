## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *now-disconnected patches were once in contact, were equalized, then stretched apart by expansion.*
- A name: *the mechanism is slow-roll inflation with a quadratic potential.*

The shape survives a change of favourite mechanism. The name does not.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* We describe *uniformity across patches*, not *inflaton potential*, before choosing a frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The 10^-5 uniformity is exactly such a plain description.
- *Library of Babel* (carried) on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, the paradox being that the search composes the answer it locates. Here the shape becomes an ordinary term in `01` section 0, so it can accrue a residual and be refuted.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here the ground has settled the *fact* of uniformity (Fixsen 2009), but has not settled the *source* (F8 of `01`).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Inflation is such an import, from particle physics into cosmology. Its provenance is recorded and it passes the shadow test; it is not a figure output.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So even the six-term lattice used in `01` is a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why naming the source from the reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (**opened this session**) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The full set of ways to produce a reading is not closed. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*, proved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The essay adds that an instrument that cannot distinguish these cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: the reading *uniform, nearly scale-invariant CMB* does not determine whether the source was inflation, varying c, an ekpyrotic contracting phase or a bounce. Lehners 2009 gives a concrete second source with a *"significant non-gaussian component."* Naming inflation from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (non-Gaussianity, tensors), not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (**opened this session**): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* This solution is such an unvalidated output; section 6 says so.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming inflation? ANSWERED, with a correction retained

**No.** The rule governs a *missing primitive*, one not held in Γ. Inflation is not being proposed as a seventh member of the six-term substrate; it is an imported arrangement from a different frame, with provenance. Calling it by name is a binding, not the invention of a primitive, and not a derivation. What the figures forbid is presenting the binding as a figure output or as a wall-moving import that adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

**Correction to my own first pass, kept visible.** In an earlier reading I overstated the rule as a blanket ban on naming a candidate. That was wrong for the same reason the exemplar corrected itself: the phrase says *missing* thing. The corrected form is: the framework returns a shape; a specific mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling an imported term or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such. Both the overstatement and its retraction are preserved here; neither is quietly erased.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** The world literature does not: it reports measurements and competing mechanisms with provenance. A presentation that said "the Tether proves inflation" would commit it, which is why `02_outreach_email.md` withholds that claim.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (**opened this session**): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that inflation is right is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle the source.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (**opened this session**): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay also says *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this folder are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the horizon problem and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
