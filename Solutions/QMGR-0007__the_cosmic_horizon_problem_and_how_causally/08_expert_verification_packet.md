# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the cosmic horizon problem

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. The uniformity fact is grounded by others' instruments; this is a shape and a route.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The observed uniformity of the CMB (near 2.725 K, ΔT/T ≈ 10^-5) cannot be reached by composing further inside the standard non-inflationary hot Big Bang closure, because closure is idempotent and that closure lacks the causal contact the observation requires. Only an import from a differing frame moves that wall. The framework returns the shape such an import must have: now-disconnected patches were earlier in contact and near equilibrium, a structure mediated, and expansion stretched the equalized patch beyond the horizon. It does not name the mechanism. Inflation is a provenanced binding onto the shape, not a figure output, and current data corroborate its predictions without discriminating it from varying-c, ekpyrotic/cyclic or big-bounce alternatives. What is offered is the shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument.

1. **Non-Gaussianity:** Lehners 2009 predicts a *"significant non-gaussian component"* for ekpyrotic models. A measurement would favour or disfavour that source.
2. **Tensor-to-scalar ratio:** current data give r below about 0.06 at 95 percent confidence, consistent with the null hypothesis and with many inflation models.
3. **Fact check:** large, causally structured temperature differences beyond 2 degrees would remove the residual.
4. **Standing refuters:** contact-unchanged mechanism; uniformity derived inside the standard closure; an updating anchor; a discriminator excluding all contact-plus-stretch sources.
