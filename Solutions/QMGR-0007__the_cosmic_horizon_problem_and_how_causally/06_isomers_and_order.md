## DERIVATION

### Q1: Partition or union? DECISIVE FOR METHOD

- Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- The horizon question bundles at least three conditions: prior causal contact, thermalization, and later stretching that separates the regions. It is historically bundled further with flatness and the magnetic-monopole problem, all addressed by one accelerated epoch (Wikipedia, Cosmic inflation, Observational status).
- ⇒ A single lever can clear the bundle, which explains the appeal of inflation, but the verdicts are not one verdict. A measurement that settles flatness does not settle uniformity.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if it were shown that uniformity, flatness and monopole absence all follow from one and the same condition with no separable residual (a true partition), the unbundling instruction would be withdrawn and the three verdicts could be merged. Kept open.

### Q3: Are the mechanisms isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- Consider the candidate mechanisms at the level of the shape rather than of field content:

| mechanism | prior-contact structure | stretch/separation structure | bond arrangement |
|---|---|---|---|
| inflation | pre-inflationary connected patch in near equilibrium | quasi-exponential expansion, then reheating | contact ⇒ stretch, ⇒ production |
| ekpyrotic/cyclic | contracting pre-bang phase (Lehners 2009) | bounce, then expansion | contact → bounce ⇒ stretch |
| varying c | enlarged early causal radius | normal expansion thereafter | c-change ⇒ contact, then stretch |
| big bounce (ECSCI Kibble) | pre-bounce contracting universe | bounce at finite minimum scale factor | contraction → bounce ⇒ stretch |

- Over the shared atom set {contact, equilibrium, expansion, mediating field, horizon, stretch} the mechanisms are **isomers**: same atoms, different bond order, different observable behaviour (non-Gaussianity, tensor amplitude, sign of the perturbation spectrum). Over a narrower atom set they are merely members of the same **functional group** (a contact-changing lever with a later stretch), which is the weaker claim.
- The isomer claim is **triangulated** for its figure definition (Fig 12) and **single-source** for the physics of each alternative (one opened source per alternative; inflation from the Cosmic inflation article, ekpyrotic/cyclic from the Lehners 2009 abstract opened this session, varying-c and big bounce from the Horizon problem and Cosmic inflation articles).
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if one mechanism requires an atom none of the others needs (for example a brane collision with no counterpart in inflation), the isomer claim fails and the functional-group claim stands instead. This could already be the case for ekpyrotic brane collision; recorded as a live strain in `11_audit.md`.

### Q4: What order is the import?

- Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*
- The CMB measurement is settled at the level of the fact (Fixsen 2009). Each candidate mechanism is a settled or candidate atom at the level of prediction. Their comparison is a second-order composition: the observed uniformity ⋛ the predicted spectrum from each mechanism. Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Re-census: uniformity and prediction are not two raw states bonded directly; the comparison runs through the instrument (a structure reading each), so the rule holds. No strain after re-census.
- Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* At second order the operands are spectra, which are quantities at that level, so the type is permitted. This is stricter than the exemplar's lingering ⋛ strain and is dissolved here by choosing quantity-typed operands.

## CONCLUSION

The question is a union compound, so per-condition verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The dependency order puts the instrument at the objective link, which is where the work still is. Over a shared atom set the mechanisms are a real isomer claim with a stated falsifier; if that fails, the weaker functional-group claim stands. Neither reading adds a primitive, and neither turns the binding to inflation into a figure output.
