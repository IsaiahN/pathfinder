## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question as given: *how did causally disconnected regions come to share the same temperature?*

Restated as a failure of prediction against observation:

- **Observation (the ground).** The CMB has a thermal blackbody spectrum at 2.72548 ± 0.00057 K (Fixsen 2009, ApJ 707:916, arXiv:0911.1955), and is isotropic to roughly one part in 25,000, with temperature coordinated across the sky to ΔT/T ≈ 10^-5 (Wikipedia, Horizon problem; Cosmic microwave background, opened 2026-11-23).
- **Prediction of the standard non-inflationary hot Big Bang.** Regions of the last-scattering surface separated by more than roughly 2 degrees lay outside one another's particle horizons. No signal travelling at or below c could have passed between them before decoupling, so there is no mechanism in that closure to equalize their temperatures. The expected reading is uncorrelated temperatures, not a shared one.
- **The residual R.** The gap between that prediction and that observation is the effect to be explained. It is not a single global number. It is indexed per sky patch and per patch pair. Described by arity, symmetry and scale:
  - **Arity:** two or more object slots (patches). Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Averaging the sky into one temperature would destroy the signal that says where to look.
  - **Symmetry:** the observed relation *same temperature* is symmetric, but the process that could produce it (thermal contact takes time, A then B) is ordered, and the question *could a signal traverse before decoupling?* is a threshold comparison of two scales. The explanation must carry an arrow that the observation does not.
  - **Scale:** the mismatch is between a horizon angular scale of about 2 degrees and a correlation scale of the whole sky, and between microphysical thermalization and cosmic structure. It is a cross-stratum residual, so a single-level answer will be the wrong shape (Fig 4, Fig 12 :103-105).

**What an answer would look like (binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term).** An acceptable answer must state (a) a mechanism that changes causal contact, (b) a reason the equalized patch was later stretched beyond the horizon, and (c) a discriminator that would distinguish that mechanism from another that yields the same uniform reading. Until (c) exists the answer stays a shape. This is the "Direction" discipline of `THE_FORMULA.md`: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, let it accrue a residual.

## 3. Findings

### F1: Inside the standard hot Big Bang closure, uniformity is unreachable (decisive, `general`)

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

The non-inflationary Big Bang closure holds known matter and radiation plus GR. Its particle horizon at recombination was about 2 degrees. Composing further inside that closure rearranges the same atoms and cannot produce a causal link the atoms forbid. So the horizon problem is not a hard puzzle to be searched harder; it is a closure failure. "More search" is the wrong instrument. This reading is `general` over any closed frame, and `specified` when applied to this cosmology.

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* That warning cuts the other way too: F1 does not prove no mechanism exists, only that none is reachable from this closure. That is exactly why the import in F2 is owed.

### F2: The only wall-moving operator is an import from a differing closure (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (IMPORT). Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

The missing atom is an early epoch of accelerated expansion driven by a scalar field. Historically it was imported from the particle-physics frame: Guth proposed it in 1979/1980 while investigating the magnetic-monopole problem, and Starobinsky and Linde developed it; the Wikipedia article records the 2014 Kavli Prize "for pioneering the theory of cosmic inflation" and the distinct particle-physics motivation. The import crossed from a frame whose closure contains scalar fields, false vacua and phase transitions into one that lacked them. This is not a rearrangement inside cosmology; it moves the wall. Triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and the recorded provenance (low overlap between the figure rule and the historical record).

### F3: The import needs a source gate and a shadow test, and it has them (`specified`)

Fig 8 names the two gates: *"source gate: provenance"* and *"shadow test: explains a gap."* Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

- **Shadow test:** the imported epoch explains the uniformity gap and predicts a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic spectrum of perturbations with two free parameters. Wikipedia, Cosmic inflation: the Planck analysis finds the universe *"homogeneous and isotropic to one part in 100,000"* and *"n_s=0.968 ± 0.006"*, in the range the simplest models predict. This is a shadow that explains the described gap.
- **Source gate:** provenance is explicit and dated (Guth 1980; Starobinsky; Linde; the 1982 Nuffield workshop where four groups derived the fluctuation spectrum separately). The independent routes are a genuine convergent derivation of the perturbation prediction, which is stronger than an adopted import, though still inside one broad physics evidence pool.

Caveat, kept visible: an import that matches data is not thereby confirmed. Fig 8 :32 and :50-52 warn that agreement without provenance is worthless as a verdict. Here provenance exists; confirmation remains an instrument question (F8).

### F4: The framework returns a shape, not the name "inflation" (`general` limit; `specified` binding)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The canonical figures README states the limit as *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 13 :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* And Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

The figure-derived shape is:

> The patches now called disconnected were earlier in causal contact and near thermal equilibrium; a structure mediated between an initial connected state and the later smooth state; expansion then stretched the equilibrated patch beyond the horizon, so the equalized regions now appear disconnected.

That is a kind of thing, not a theory name. The framework does not return the word *inflation*. Naming inflation is a **binding** from the physics frame onto an already-held shape, recorded as provenance (as the exemplar binds Gradient, with the same correction: the "shape, not a name" rule governs a *missing* primitive; inflation is an imported arrangement, not a new member of the six). Verdict on the shape: `triangulated` across Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :14/:23, Fig 11 :40 and Fig 9 :32. Verdict on the specific binding: `single-source` on the figure side, world-corroborated by n_s, `candidate`, not `grounded`.

### F5: Any acceptable mechanism must change contact (decisive discriminator)

Fig 13 network: *"that entities relate, and influence travels."* A horizon is a network boundary, a limit on which nodes can influence which. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Apply this as a filter over candidates. Each genuine proposal is a contact-changing lever:

| candidate | what it changes | contact changes? |
|---|---|---|
| inflation | the early expansion history, so the horizon was much larger before the stretch | yes: prior contact then separation |
| varying speed of light | the value of c in the early universe, enlarging the particle horizon | yes: larger causal radius at decoupling |
| ekpyrotic / cyclic | adds an expanding epoch before the Big Bang (Lehners 2009) | yes: contact before the bounce |
| big bounce (Einstein Cartan Sciama Kibble) | replaces the singularity with contraction plus bounce | yes: pre-bounce contracting phase |

A proposal that merely re-describes the same temperature reading without moving a causal boundary fails this filter. This is a filter for the shape, not a witness for any one mechanism, per Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

### F6: It is a union compound, so verdicts are kept separate (decisive for method)

Horizon, flatness and monopoles are historically bundled into one inflationary solution, and within the horizon question the conditions *prior contact*, *thermalization* and *later stretching* overlap. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

Consequence: the fact that one lever (accelerated expansion) clears the whole bundle explains the theory's appeal, but it does not license averaging the verdicts. The uniformity verdict, the flatness verdict and the monopole verdict each have their own ground. This file reports the uniformity verdict only.

### F7: No last room, so inflation is a generator, not an answer (canonical confirmation)

Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* Fig 13 :90: *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*

The world record agrees in a different voice. Wikipedia, Cosmic inflation, Criticism: Earman and Mosterín (1999) concluded *"we do not think that there are, as yet, good grounds for admitting any of the models of inflation into the standard core of cosmology."* Penrose is quoted: *"There is something fundamentally misconceived about trying to explain the uniformity of the early universe as resulting from a thermalization process."* And the eternal-inflation section records that *"Scientists disagree about how to assign a probability distribution to this hypothetical anthropic landscape."* These are not reasons to reject the shape; they are the expected signature of a theory that pushes initial conditions back rather than terminating them. The measure problem page fetch returned 404 on 2026-11-23 and is not cited (recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv` notes in `04_links_and_distribution.md`).

So the honest stop is **unreached, not unreachable**: the shape is stable, the mechanism is a candidate, and the question of what set up the initial conditions remains a live room after inflation.

### F8: The source of a uniform reading needs a discriminator, not an argument (`specified`)

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*, proved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The metamerism case: a spectrometer resolved green-from-mixtures where the eye could not.

Here the reading *uniform, nearly scale-invariant CMB* could be produced by several sources: inflation, varying c, ekpyrotic/cyclic, big bounce. The reading does not name its source. What would discriminate: primordial non-Gaussianity (Lehners 2009 predicts a *"significant non-gaussian component"* in ekpyrotic models), the tensor-to-scalar ratio r, and the detailed perturbation spectrum. Wikipedia records that BICEP2's 2014 claim lost confidence and that *"By 2018, additional data suggested, with 95% confidence, that r is 0.06 or lower: Consistent with the null hypothesis, but still also consistent with many remaining models of inflation."* So the discriminator is still being built. F8 is a live strain, named not hidden.

### F9: Declared gap, which mechanism is the true one (`gap`)

No figure line returns the word inflation, a spectral index, a number of e-foldings, or a choice among inflation, VSL, ekpyrotic and big bounce. Those are world facts arrived at by instruments and derivations, not figure output. Fig 9 :32 gives only a kind of thing; Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* So the literal question *which mechanism actually did it* is a `gap` of the **unreached, not unreachable in principle** kind, because a ground exists (CMB and large-scale-structure instruments) even though it has not yet discriminated. The gap is recorded rather than filled. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the framework: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim of F4 and F5 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The fact fails: a measurement shows patches separated by more than 2 degrees at last scattering with temperature differences that are large and track causal structure, so the uniformity residual R is not a fact.
2. The contact condition fails: a mechanism is demonstrated that produces the observed uniformity while leaving causal contact unchanged (breaks Fig 11 :40).
3. The closure reading fails: a derivation shows the standard non-inflationary Big Bang closure reaches the uniformity from its own atoms (breaks Fig 8 :32).
4. The anchor fails: the uniformity is shown to be an observer-selection or convention artifact with no non-updating ground (breaks Fig 2 :29).
5. The source is discriminated against the shape: an instrument excludes every prior-contact-plus-stretching mechanism and settles a non-contact mechanism. The binding reverses; the shape falls.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
