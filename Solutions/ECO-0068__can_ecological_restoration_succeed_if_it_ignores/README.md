## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A ground settles, a room does not.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 11 :73: "the habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."
3. **Sending the interior spends the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
