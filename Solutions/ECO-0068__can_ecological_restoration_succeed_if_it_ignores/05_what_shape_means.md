## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 says the system can report that it lacks something rather than produce an answer anyway, and that naming the missing primitive would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECO-0068 the shape is: *a restoration that persists under change must keep the generator loop among organism, habitat and ground intact, with the adaptation term instrumented and the anchor independent of the plan.* The name of the winning seed mix or genotype is not the shape. `REFERENCE_SITE(???)` is a shape; `SOURCE_A + SOURCE_B at ratio 70/30` is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Here that means describing the residual as "a planted population that cannot change when conditions change", not as "a provenance policy failure".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (via exemplar mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A restorer can check whether planted populations retain variation and whether the site still functions after a shift.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The paradox is that by the time the shape is precise enough to state, the searcher has effectively built the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, a multi-generation monitoring record that pays or does not pay settles which provenancing rule works at a site.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing "evolutionary rescue" terminology from experimental evolution is legitimate only if the provenance is recorded and the term explains a gap this frame could not close.

*They're Scanning Books* (via exemplar mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list; that does not stop it from governing the composition rules.

## 4. Why the flat reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (via exemplar mapping) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

For ECO-0068 the analogous error is reading "the site looks restored" (a projection: cover, species presence, structure) and naming its source as "the restoration succeeded". Several paths give that reading: a genuinely self-sustaining community, a temporarily planted one, and one held together by continuing intervention. The reading does not discriminate among them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (survival and function across a shift), not a name.

Fig 11 :68-69 name the two invisible failures directly: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Ignoring the adaptation term guarantees the instrument cannot discriminate.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming adaptive capacity?

No. Adaptive capacity is not a missing primitive. It is a property of an admitted entity (D2 in `01`, the introduced organisms as generators). The figures' phrase governs a primitive not held in Γ, as the exemplar's own correction (section 5b of its `05`) makes explicit. Naming adaptive capacity is a binding from the restoration frame onto terms already in the loop (variation, contact, change over time); it is not the invention of a seventh substrate member and it is not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here).

What remains forbidden is turning that binding into a name for the answer. "Keep the generator loop intact" is shape. "Use composite provenancing at 70/30" is a name, and it is licensed only by a ground measurement at a specific site. This claim and the correction are both recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous restoration ecologists agreeing that adaptation matters is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried: one venue, one refuter, one recorded verdict.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle ECO-0068.

## 7. What this question's shape actually is

Pulling the eight essays together:

- **Ladder of vocabulary** (Babel): describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame, so any provenancing scheme does not automatically seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).
- **Commons of methods** (Society of Agents): send the refuter and the formula, not a conclusion, so a checker keeps its own ground.
- **Source, not reading** (Hard Problem is About Source): distinguish "site looks restored" from "site persists under change".
- **Tacit knowledge** (Decoding Tacit Knowledge): a plain description of what you would expect to see is checkable by anyone with a site and a monitoring record.
- **Outputs are not generators** (Arrival of the Fittest / Recursive Trap): planted material is a seed, not a small tree; what it becomes depends on the conditions and on its capacity to change.
- **Non-equilibrium** (Babel, Chapter 9 framing in the exemplar): the target is a trajectory, not a snapshot, so "unmaintained" and "maintained" modes must be kept distinct (Fig 11 :56-59).

The resulting shape is one sentence: **successful restoration under change requires a maintained, instrumented generator loop in which the organisms can change, the habitat is enumerated rather than composed, and the ground, not the declared reference, settles the verdict.** Everything specific beyond that (which species, which provenance, which mix) is a `gap` closed only by measurement.
