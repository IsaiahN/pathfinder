## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are the sources the derivation actually leaned on, grouped by evidence pool. Within a pool, agreement is one witness, not several (Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

- **Restoration and conservation practice pool (one witness).** Broadhurst et al. 2008, Sgrò et al. 2011, Aitken and Bemmels 2016, Aitken and Whitlock 2013, Stockwell et al. 2006/2017, Gann et al. 2026. They converge on the point that adaptive potential is treated as a design variable and that managers have historically ignored it. They share the same literature base, so they are recorded as one pool.
- **Experimental evolution and evolutionary rescue pool (a distinct witness for the mechanism, not for the restoration fact).** Bell 2009 in yeast: rescue "may occur within 25 generations". This is a different field and a different instrument from restoration practice. It corroborates that the change term can be fast enough to matter, not that any particular restoration fails.
- **Ecosystem resilience pool (partial overlap with the first pool).** Angeler et al. 2019 operationalize "adaptive capacity as the latent potential of an ecosystem to alter resilience in response to change." Useful vocabulary, but it draws on the same ecological literature, so it is not a fully independent witness.

No source was used to decide the verdict. The figures decide; these sources corroborate or, where noted, are silent.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 9 :60** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A tier, a journal's prestige or an author's reputation is a filter for where to send, never a reason to accept the claim.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Do not pool later agreement from the same field as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only if they are curious, the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) only if they ask, the formula as a method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.
