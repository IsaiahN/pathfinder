## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** A relation among restoration frame Γ, intervention *a* and observed trajectory o′, indexed by slot *s* and ordered by time *t*. At least three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (site, success) loses the intervention and the prediction, so it is rejected. It carries a comparison bond because "success" is a threshold (Fig 12 :89).
- **Symmetry.** Not symmetric. The ground does not update on the practitioner's opinion (Fig 2 :25). This seat rejects any reading that treats the reference community as a co-equalframe that negotiates the outcome.
- **Scale.** The slot is a population or function; the "success" ranking exists at community scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between an organism's step loop and community assembly (Fig 4). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

**Divergence from the primary census, recorded.** The primary admitted D1 to D4 with names (practitioner seat, generators, habitat, channel). On re-census they share one anchor, contact-driven survival and reproduction under change, and their overlap is a compound, so they are one domain B. The primary kept them separate for the same reason without stating the test explicitly. Both censuses arrive at four admitted domains.

**Partition or union:** union. The two named conditions are contact maintenance (A and D) and generator renewal (B in C). Their overlap is a compound with its own cyclic loop (Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 12 :121).

**Dependency order:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). This seat agrees with the primary ordering, and adds that the residual line itself forces only the first three links; planning and carrying are candidate answers introduced by the frame, not forced by the residual. The break occurs at vocabulary because that is where the term for change would live, and the frame that drops it has no term to put there.

### 2.3 Where this seat disagrees with the primary's emphasis

The primary elevates Fig 4 (seed versus recording) as the decisive finding. This seat would elevate Fig 2 plus Fig 10 (anchor versus convention) as the decisive finding, because the literal question is about **success**, and success is a verdict predicate before it is a generator question. You cannot ask whether the generator was present until you have said what would settle the claim. Fig 9 :6: "1 · the verdict: is φ* in here at all?" comes before the lookup and the narrowing.

This is a difference of emphasis, not a conflict. The primary's F4 covers the anchor rule; this seat makes it lead. Both verdicts are the same two senses of success, kept separate.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-sense verdict to one sentence. Result: *restoration success is a ground-settled property of the organism-habitat loop, not a match to a declared snapshot.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific restoration decision. Result: *measure variation and persistence, and ignore the reference list.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The abstraction cannot hold four things:

1. **The reference list is not useless.** It is a convention and can be efficient for coordination (Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"). Coming back down, "ignore the reference list" would discard a legitimate coordination tool. The one-sentence form loses the distinction between a convention used for coordination and a convention deciding an outcome.
2. **The union collapses.** "Measure variation and persistence" reads as one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Coming back down, a site failing contact would be told to add variation.
3. **The two invisible failure classes vanish.** Fig 11 :68-69 name what was failed to reproduce and what was unintentionally introduced. The abstraction has no slot for either.
4. **The gap is lost.** The abstraction cannot say which instrument would discriminate a self-sustaining community from one held together by intervention. That is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator and the refuter, never the one-paragraph version alone. That paragraph in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
