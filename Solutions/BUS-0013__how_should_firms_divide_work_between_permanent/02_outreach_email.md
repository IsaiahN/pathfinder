**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about dividing work, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about staffing, contracting or operations. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The problem, in effect terms.** A firm has to assign each unit of work to a governance mode: keep it inside the firm, import it from a contractor or supplier, or hand it to an automated system. The interesting quantity is not which mode is cheapest on average. It is the gap, per work slot, between what the firm's staffing predicts it can deliver and what the market actually pays for. That gap is indexed per slot. A firm-wide percentage mix throws away the only signal that says which slot is failing.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** four conditions that behave differently, so they cannot be averaged.
>
> 1. Work whose value comes from composing inside the firm's own library stays inside it. Composition inside a closure cannot add a new primitive, so internal work alone cannot renew reach.
> 2. Work whose value comes from something the firm does not hold is imported from a closure that differs. That is the only operator that moves the wall. The contractor and the supplier are different bonds: one brings a head start that composes inside you, the other brings a finished molecule you compose over. Showing where the imported capability came from is part of the claim, not an afterthought.
> 3. Automated work is a method or a recording. A method can be reapplied and changes contact. A recording replays one success and proves only that the harness was wired correctly.
> 4. What decays is the channel to the market, not the market. Stale percepts, saturated metrics and pooled readings across slots are how a work division loses value while the market keeps paying someone else.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. A firm renews its reach across a habitat change with no import from any differing closure and still passes the market test.
> 2. An automated system passes a source-discriminating ground test that the same slot failed before automation, with the channel independently measured as unchanged.
> 3. A work division loses value while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining.
> 4. A single lever restores all four conditions and leaves no categorically different residue.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** a fixed percentage mix, a named vendor or tool, or that "automation replaces people" as a general verdict. The framework returns a shape, not a name. It does not tell you which specific contractor or system wins. Any agreement with me is not evidence; I am asking you to check the output against the ground you know, not to join a frame.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Public figures and formula (local extract used for this derivation): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` in the Project Pathfinder workspace.
> - Figures repository, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a staffing decision you know well and can check against a market. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage rule for the reply

1. **Stage 1:** send only the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A later agreement with the framework is never pooled as corroboration. By Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", once someone has absorbed the interior, their agreement is the pool talking.
