# The Tether's answer on dividing work

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A firm assigns each unit of work to a governance mode: inside the firm, imported from a contractor or supplier, or delegated to an automated system. The residual is the per-slot gap between what its staffing library predicts it can deliver and what the market actually pays for. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A firm-wide percentage mix is not an answer; it is an average across the very slots that carry the signal (Fig 1 :22). The question is a **union of four conditions**, not one lever (Fig 9 :54-57), so the verdicts below are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep inside the closure the work whose value comes from composing inside the library.** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The kernel, the tacit knowledge that does not travel in a specification, is what is stable across size (Fig 4 :20). But internal work alone cannot renew reach; closure is idempotent (Fig 8 :32).

2. **Import from a differing closure the work whose value is an atom the firm does not hold.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." This is the function of contractors and suppliers, not cheapness. Imports need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13), because a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24). The contractor and the supplier differ by **bond**: one brings a head start that composes inside you (Fig 4 :22), the other brings a settled molecule that becomes an atom for what you compose over it (Fig 12 :104). Same deliverable, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :5).

3. **Admit automated work only as a generator that changes contact, never on a harness result.** Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." The test is Fig 11 :40: an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. Bainbridge 1983 and Dekker and Woods 2002 corroborate at low overlap: automation moves the hard residual into the human and transforms practice, so a fixed "who does what" list is not a stable answer.

4. **Keep the channel to the ground reachable; the market does not decay.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." The named decay modes are stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings (Fig 10 :26). A work division loses value when the bracket no longer matches the residual or the ground's pressure on the room goes to zero (Fig 7 :37-39), not because the market stopped paying.

5. **Valence flips when the residual changes.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A governance mode that is a benefactor when the residual is "we lack an atom" can be a malfactor when the residual is "protect the channel". No standing list of good modes survives the change (Fig 11 :51). Gordon 2015 corroborates: robust allocation is a live distributed response to current conditions, not a standing assignment of types.

6. **Two currencies, held apart.** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Bits price the description of the arrangement; actions price finding out whether it holds (Fig 12 :126). A staffing decision priced only in money proposes arrangements it cannot afford to test.

7. **The framework does not name the winner.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Which specific vendor, tool or ratio occupies a slot is a `gap`, not a silence. The instrument that would name the source is not held here.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a fixed mix such as "70/20/10". That is an aggregate (Fig 1 :19, :22) and a frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :27).
- Not that contractors are cheaper or that automation is inherently better. Price is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60), never a witness.
- Not a standing classification of work into core and non-core. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not a derivation of one firm's answer from another's. A binding onto an atom already available is provenance, not a new primitive (Fig 6 :21).
- Not `grounded`. No market measurement settles the per-slot allocation of `left(R,φ)` here.
- Not that people are obsolete. Automation is judged by contact, and the human takeover skill is part of the channel that decays when unused (Bainbridge 1983).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Import test:** does a new external arrangement put an atom into the firm's closure that was demonstrably not derivable there, and does the market pay for it? A provenance record plus a shadow test, not a vendor claim.
- **Generator test:** does the automated slot pass a ground test it previously failed, with the channel independently measured as unchanged? A harness pass is not this test (Fig 11 :70).
- **Channel test:** does per-slot fidelity stay high while the market's payment for the slot changes? If fidelity and ground pressure both stay high while R grows, the mechanism is elsewhere.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a firm renewing reach with no import across a habitat change; a valence-invariant governance mode; a single lever restoring all four conditions; a derivation of one of the figures' core laws from fewer terms.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are a shape, not a staffing plan. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states why the missing part cannot be shipped in a specification: the history that makes a head start usable *"does not travel"*, and what can be sent is a description of effects anyone can check. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." ## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a fixed percentage mix of employees, contractors, suppliers and automated systems. It says that work should be divided per unit, not by a global ratio, and that the four governance labels are bindings onto four different structural facts. Work whose value comes from composing inside the firm's own library stays inside, because internal composition cannot add a new primitive. Work whose value is an atom the firm does not hold is imported from a closure that differs, with provenance and a shadow test, and the head start a contractor brings is a different bond from the settled molecule a supplier delivers. Automated work is admitted only as a generator that changes contact, never on a harness solve. What decays is the channel to the market, not the market, and a mode that helped under one residual can harm under another. The framework returns a shape, a set of falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which vendor or tool wins stays open until the ground settles it.
