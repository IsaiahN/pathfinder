## The residual, in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Formula step 1). Here s is a **unit of work**, not a department or a headcount:

- **b** is the firm's belief about what the market will pay for, formed from its own history and inherited priors.
- **a** is the allocation decision itself: which work slot is performed inside the firm's closure, which is imported from a differing closure, and which is delegated to a generator that is not a person.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the firm's current staffing library predicts it can deliver, at what cost, quality and lead time.
- **o′** is what the market actually returns: delivered work, defects, rework, lead time, and renewal of the capability that produced it.

The question asks for a rule that keeps R small per work slot while the firm's reach keeps moving. The four named categories (employee, contractor, supplier, automation) are treated as **bindings from the management frame**, not as figure-given primitives. They are ranged against the residual only after the residual is described.
