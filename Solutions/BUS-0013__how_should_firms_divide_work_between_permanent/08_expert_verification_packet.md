# Expert verification packet - the Tether on dividing work

**What this is:** a claim in a form a staffing researcher, contracting scholar or operations researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No market measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Divide work per unit, not by a global mix ratio. Keep inside the firm's closure the work whose value comes from composing inside its own library, because internal composition cannot add an atom. Import from a differing closure the work whose value is an atom the firm does not hold, with provenance and a shadow test; the contractor's head start and the supplier's settled molecule are different bonds, not different prices. Admit automated work only as a generator that changes contact, never on a harness result. Keep the channel to the ground reachable, because the ground does not decay and the channel does, and expect a governance mode's valence to flip when the residual changes. The two currencies, price of stating the arrangement and price of testing it, do not add. Which vendor or tool wins is a `gap`, not a verdict.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8
- **Statement:** internal work composes inside a closure and cannot add a primitive; external work is the only import that moves the wall. A wrong import costs more than starting from primitives, so provenance and a shadow test are part of the claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :52 "Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."
- **What to check:** is the internal/external distinction being used as a function (reach versus closure), not as a cost label? Can the firm name the atom an external arrangement supplied and where it came from?
- **Refuter:** a firm that renews its reach across a habitat change solely by composing inside its own closure, with no atom from a differing closure, and still passes the market test. That breaks idempotence and C1.

### C2 - automation-facing, checkable by an engineer or human-factors researcher
- **Statement:** automation is a generator or a recording. A generator can be reapplied and changes contact; a recording replays one success and proves only that the harness was wired correctly. The human takeover skill is part of the channel and decays when unused.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** Bainbridge 1983, *Ironies of automation*, Automatica 19(6):775 to 779: *"The second irony is that the designer who tries to eliminate the operator still leaves the operator to do the tasks which the designer cannot think how to automate."* Dekker and Woods 2002, *Cognition, Technology & Work* 4(4):240 to 244, abstract: *"Quantitative 'who does what' allocation does not work because the real effects of automation are qualitative: it transforms human practice and forces people to adapt their skills and routines."* Does the proposed automated slot change the ground, or only the test?
- **Refuter:** an automated system that passes a source-discriminating ground test the slot previously failed, with channel fidelity independently measured as unchanged. That shows capability and breaks the recording half of C2. A harness-only pass confirms it.

### C3 - measurement-facing, checkable by an operations researcher
- **Statement:** the arrangement has two non-additive currencies, and per-slot R is never aggregated into a mix score. Channel decay, not ground decay, explains quick loss.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- **What to check:** Foote and Folta 2002, *Human Resource Management Review* 12(4):579 to 597: *"real options theory offers managers the ability to consider irreversibility and to make workforce investment decisions under conditions of minimum uncertainty and maximum flexibility."* The CONFIRM 2009 queueing model concludes that *"if future demands are volatile, managers would prefer recruiting experienced workers to apprentices, and if the firm has to pay a higher cost for hiring experienced workers, managers would prefer training apprentices to experienced workers."* Both price finding out; neither prices the market's payment. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **Refuter:** a single metric that prices description and testing in the same units without loss, or a firm that loses a work division's value while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a fixed percentage mix, not a vendor, not a tool, not a standing core/non-core list.
- Not that contractors are cheaper or that automation is inherently better. Price is a filter, not a witness.
- Not that the market stops paying. The market is the anchor; the channel decays (Fig 10 :24).
- Not `grounded`. No prospective market measurement is supplied here.
- Not that this derivation settles the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
- Not that agreement with the author counts. Once a reader holds the framework's interior, their agreement is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement:

1. **Import test:** the atom supplied by an external arrangement is named, its provenance recorded, and the market pays for it. A vendor claim is not this.
2. **Generator test:** the automated slot passes a ground test the same slot failed before, with the channel independently measured as unchanged.
3. **Channel test:** per-slot fidelity stays high while the market's payment changes, and the value loss tracks the channel, not the ground.
4. **Reversal:** a firm renewing reach with no import across a habitat change, a valence-invariant governance mode, or a single lever restoring all four conditions would reverse the shape.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21 and :23, Fig 8 :45 and :52, Fig 11 :40 and :70, Fig 12 :125 and :126 (eight short passages).
2. Check C2 against your own experience of automation: did the system change the ground, or only the test? Was the takeover skill maintained?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish an imported atom from an internally derived one, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
