## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0013 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an allocation answer that can be stated before any particular vendor, tool, percentage or governance label is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *work whose value comes from composing inside the firm's closure stays inside; work needing an atom the firm does not hold is imported with provenance; delegated work is admitted only as a generator that changes contact; the channel to the ground, not the mix ratio, is what decays.*
- Name, withheld: *use 60 percent employees, 25 percent contractors, 10 percent suppliers and 5 percent automation; pick vendor X; move customer support to tool Y.*

The distinction matters because the staffing literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Coase names the cost of using the price mechanism; Foote and Folta name real options; the queueing literature names a threshold R. Those are bindings from management and OR onto structural conditions, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "make or buy", "core versus non-core", "contingent work", "real options", "task allocation", "human in the loop" and "automation irony". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." The effect description (per work slot, gap between predicted and realized delivery, indexed over time) came first; the frames were selected after it was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0013 as a union of four conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with one lever.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Here the stop is reached when the four conditions are stated, an instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, "one long room: motion, no progress").

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."

For BUS-0013 neither has happened at the firm level. No prospective measurement in this folder pays for "this specific vendor should own this specific slot", and the management/OR names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a staffing explanation makes when it reads a good quarter and names its governance source. A high return per worker is a reading. It is consistent with a protected internal routine, a favourable habitat, a temporary information asymmetry, a supplier who absorbed the risk, or an accounting choice. The reading does not determine the source. Naming "the contractor model" or "automation" as the cause from that reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among managers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30 "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to staffing opinion

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 ("A chain of mirrors is one long room") and Fig 10 :24 ("the ground does not decay, the channel does"). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness. It is recorded as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that firms behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous management scholars who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Contact, pretext and why a specification is not the skill

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, read directly:

> *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

The essay's three terms explain why an internal routine survives inside the closure and a contractor's specification does not carry the same thing. Writing carries context and some subtext but almost never pretext, the history and assumptions the listener brings. That history is rebuilt only through contact: trying, being corrected and trying again. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

This gives the hiring analogue a precise limit. Bringing in an external expert imports an atom; it does not import the pretext that makes the atom usable. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A head start still has to be grown in the receiving habitat, and the firm inherits actors it did not choose (Fig 11 :66-69).

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular governance mode is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a routine, vendor class or tool is already available to the firm, naming it is a binding from the management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 8. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about firms. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0013 the canonical shape is: **a per-slot rule with four distinct conditions, two non-additive currencies, a valve that changes sign when the residual changes, and a generator/recording test for delegated work.** It is not a percentage mix, not a vendor list, not a ranking of governance modes. The names that appear in the staffing literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
