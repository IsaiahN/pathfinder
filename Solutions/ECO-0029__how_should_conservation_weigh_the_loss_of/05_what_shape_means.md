## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From The Library of Babel, opened directly: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on the method: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECO-0029, the shape is a per-slot decision procedure with the two losses held apart, a union boundary, and a declared vacancy for the scalar. A number such as `w = 0.3` would be a name, not a shape, and no figure line licenses one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- The Library of Babel gives the vocabulary-by-proximity method: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Here the closest vocabulary is functional group, atom, bond, threshold and residual.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as function-not-performed and atom-removed, not as "biodiversity loss," which is a borrowed aggregate.
- Decoding Tacit Knowledge, carried from the exemplar mapping: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach asks what a failure would look like before showing any theory.
- The Library of Babel on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the finish line is a per-slot measurement that closes `R_s`.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the weight is not one

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

For this question, calling a species functionally unique is a **binding** from the ecology frame onto held atoms, licensed by an enumeration of the habitat and a functional-group measurement. It is not a new primitive and it does not move the wall. Calling a species "worth 0.3 of a widespread decline" would be a name without either license, so it is a `gap`. The canonical usage rule reports a shape, not a name.

They're Scanning Books, carried from the exemplar mapping: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A priority index that puts atom loss and mass decline in one number is a declared choice, not a discovery of a natural unit.

## 4. The source error: why present counts do not name future loss

The Hard Problem is About Source, opened directly, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The same error appears here when a present abundance count is used to name the future loss. The Extinction debt page states that present species counts may not measure the habitat's ability to support species in the future. A count is a reading; the commitment to extinction is the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, a pre-declared viability threshold, not a name read off the count.

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap adds the validator limit: *"The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A prioritization model cannot certify that its own currency is the right one.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid identifying a functionally unique species?

**Answer: no.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Functional groups, atoms, bonds, comparison and subtraction are all already held. Identifying a functionally unique species is a provenanced binding from the ecology frame onto held atoms, established by enumerating the habitat (Fig 11 :43-44). What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh kind of primitive to carry the value;
- calling the binding an import that moves the wall, when Fig 6 says "IMPORT adds an atom" and this adds none;
- reading a species name off an aggregate projection, which is the metamerism error of §4.

**Correction to my own earlier reading.** An earlier draft of this audit treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any particular species in a slot. That overstates the rule. It governs missing primitives, not the selection of atoms already in the library. The corrected form is kept here rather than silently erased, matching the exemplar's own §5b correction. The genuine name-shaped vacancy in this question is the universal scalar weight `w`, which is exactly a thing not held in Γ and therefore a `gap`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* It also states: *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture"* and *"Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers distinguished conservationists to endorse a weight is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a pre-declared measurement settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the conservation claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-paragraph shape

> For this question, a shape is the form a checkable conservation decision must take before anyone is entitled to name a weight: index the residual per function slot, keep atom subtraction and mass decline in separate columns, treat uniqueness as a relation established by enumerating the habitat, unbundle the threshold cases where decline becomes loss, put the heavier burden of proof on the irreversible option, and mark the universal scalar as unreachable until a measurement supplies a common currency. The framework returns that form and its refuters. It does not return the number.
