# The Tether's answer: unique species versus many widespread declines

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain mechanisms, `gap` on any universal scalar weight, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A finite conservation budget must be committed. One option removes one species with no functional equivalent; the other reduces abundance across many widespread species. The question asks for a single weight `w`. The residual is `R_s = |r_s - o_s|` per function slot `s`, indexed by slot and never by community total (Fig 1 :19, :22). The two candidates overlap where a widespread decline crosses a viability threshold and becomes an atom loss, so the case is a union compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape

1. **The two losses are different operators, so the requested scalar is not licensed.** Extinction subtracts an atom from the held set; no composition over what remains can rebuild it (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). The widespread declines reduce mass in atoms still held, so recovery there is rearrangement inside the closure. Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." By the same structure, atom subtraction and mass decline are two readings held apart.
2. **A weight is a budget filter, never a verdict.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A prioritization index decides what gets tried first. When it decides what counts as the loss, it has become an authored verdict (Fig 2 :43). The budget must still be set, but it does not settle the case.
3. **Uniqueness is a relation, not a label.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." A species is unique for a function slot where no other held atom composes that function. A rare species may be functionally redundant; a common species may be functionally unique. No standing list transfers between slots (Fig 11 :51).
4. **Index per function slot, never by aggregate.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A large decline total can hide an unperformed unique-species function, and one charismatic species can hide many failed slots. Both losses stay in separate columns.
5. **Decline and loss form a union with a threshold.** Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities." A decline crossing pre-declared viability is an atom loss. There are three regions: pure unique loss, pure mass decline above threshold, and the intersection. The intersection is unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54).
6. **Irreversibility raises the burden of proof, not the score.** Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A reintroduction is an import into a substituted habitat and is permanent work (Fig 11 :63). So the irreversible option owes more independent checking before commitment. It does not acquire a bigger number.
7. **The universal weight is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." No figure line ranks atom subtraction against distributed mass reduction in one currency. What is owed is a measurement that supplies a common currency and does not move when a frame disagrees (Fig 2 :29-30). Until then the vacancy is reported as a verdict, not filled.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a decision. No budget is allocated, no species is selected, and no ethical weighting is endorsed. This is a shape a checkable decision must have.
- Not "the unique species always wins." Irreversibility is a burden of proof, not a lexical priority, because a widespread decline that crosses viability is itself an atom loss.
- Not "the many declines win by numbers." Fig 1 :22 names that aggregation error.
- Not that Red List categories, phylogenetic distinctiveness scores or population indices are the ground. They are frames; the ground is a pre-declared per-slot measurement (Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :73).
- Not `grounded`. No field measurement was taken and no common currency was demonstrated.
- Not a claim that the framework settles an ethical question. The figures are metatheory about composition and grounding, not a value theory.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A pre-declared per-slot measurement, not a further argument (Fig 9 :42). From The Hard Problem is About Source: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot measurement:** for each function slot, what is `r_s`, what is `o_s`, and which held atoms can compose `f`? This establishes whether the species is functionally unique for that slot.
- **Threshold test:** what is the pre-declared viability comparison for each widespread species, and has it been crossed? Present abundance is a projection, not the source (Extinction debt).
- **Common-currency test:** is there a measurement that expresses atom loss and mass decline in one unit and does not move when an analyst disagrees? If yes, F1 fails and the weight becomes legitimate. If no, the gap stands.
- **Standing refuters:** a rank-preserving unique-species list across slots; an aggregate that settles a slot; a clean partition with no threshold; a recovery restoring the original habitat with no maintenance.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The held atom set is a visible choice, not nature's own list; the canonical README says the framework reports a shape, not a name, and stops at the limit of the instruments. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." More prioritization models are SEARCHED at the same reach; a slot-resolved instrument is INWARD; reliability engineering and clinical triage are OUTWARD imports with their own source gates. The two tracks are not read against each other.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a weight for trading one unique species against many widespread declines. It says the two losses are different operators: extinction removes an atom that cannot be rebuilt from what remains, while the declines reduce mass in atoms still held, and the figures forbid summing incommensurable readings. A priority score may choose what gets tried first, but it must not decide what counts as the loss. Uniqueness is a relation to a function slot, established by reading the habitat outward, not a standing label; decline and loss overlap at a viability threshold and must be unbundled; and irreversibility raises the burden of proof before action rather than supplying a number. What is offered is the shape a checkable decision must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close the remaining gap. The weight stays open until a measurement supplies one currency that does not move when a frame disagrees.
