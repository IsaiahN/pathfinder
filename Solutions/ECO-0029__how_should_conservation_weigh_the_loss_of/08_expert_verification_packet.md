# Expert verification packet: the Tether on unique species versus many declines

**What this is:** a claim in a form a conservation scientist or prioritization researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the mechanisms, `gap` on the universal scalar weight, not `grounded`.** No conservation decision is asserted as settled by measurement. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question asks for a single weight trading the loss of one functionally unique species against the decline of many widespread species. The figures do not license that number. The two losses are different operators: extinction subtracts an atom that no composition over the remaining atoms can rebuild, while the widespread declines reduce mass in atoms still held and are recoverable by rearrangement. Fig 12 :125 says "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add," and Fig 12 :130 says "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." A prioritization index is therefore a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Uniqueness is a functional-group relation to a function slot, established by enumerating the habitat outward, not a standing label; decline and loss overlap at a viability threshold and must be unbundled; and irreversibility raises the burden of proof before action rather than supplying a score. The universal weight is a declared `gap`, owed to a measurement that supplies one currency and does not move when a frame disagrees.

### C2: ecology-facing, checkable by a conservation scientist against real systems

- **Statement:** uniqueness is a relation to a function slot, not a standing property; and the decline cases overlap with loss at a viability threshold. A species is unique where no other held species composes the same function, which is Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." The same actor changes valence with the residual (Fig 11 :49). The threshold is a comparison, Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities."
- **What to check:** is functional uniqueness in your system slot-specific and context-dependent? Does the common-species depletion paradox hold in your taxon, so that a decline in many abundant species removes large amounts of function without any extinction? Is there a pre-declared viability threshold that creates a third, mixed case? Isaac et al. (2007) measure evolutionary distinctness as a relational property and report that EDGE species often miss existing protection; the Extinction debt literature reports present counts understating future loss.
- **Refuter:** a standing list of unique species that keeps its rank across function slots and residuals; an aggregate community metric that demonstrably settles a live slot; or a clean partition between decline and loss with no threshold and no shared actor.

## 5. What would settle it

A pre-declared per-slot measurement, not agreement (Fig 9 :42). Three concrete tests:

1. **Slot test:** for each function slot, name `r_s`, `o_s` and the held atoms that compose `f`. This decides whether the species is functionally unique for that slot.
2. **Threshold test:** declare the viability comparison before measuring, then test whether any widespread decline has crossed it. Present abundance is a projection, not the source.
3. **Currency test:** find a measure of atom loss and mass decline in one unit that does not move when an analyst disagrees. If it exists, F1 fails; if none exists after a genuine SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD pass, the gap stands as a verdict.
