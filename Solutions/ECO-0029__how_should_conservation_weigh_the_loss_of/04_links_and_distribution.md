## What bears on the derivation from the wider literature

1. **Gaston and Fuller via the Common species page** is the closest field-level statement of the question: the trade-off between species extinctions and population depletion. It corroborates the union framing rather than a single currency.
2. **Isaac et al. 2007** shows that evolutionary distinctness is a measurable relational property, but also that EDGE species often miss existing protection. It corroborates F3 and warns against turning the score into the verdict.
3. **Myers et al. 2000 versus Wiedenfeld et al. 2021** is a real conflict about the budget premise. It is assigned to Domain 3 as a convention dispute, not used to flip the figure verdict.
4. **Extinction debt** corroborates the F5 threshold: present counts understate future loss, so present abundance is not the anchor.
5. **Triage and single point of failure** are the low-overlap analogues. They corroborate the shape (scarcity forces a ranking; one unique atom has a different failure signature) while their design stance conflicts with Fig 11 :61 on authored habitats. That conflict is assigned to designed systems, not used to change a figure reading.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
