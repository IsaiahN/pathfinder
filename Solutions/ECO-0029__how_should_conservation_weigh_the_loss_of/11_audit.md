- **Domain 2, habitat, contact and boundary.** Actors: remaining species, conditions, relations, viability thresholds. Currency: contact with the residual. Anchor: a pre-declared per-slot measurement. Question type: enumeration, never composition, with a comparison bond at the boundary.
- **Domain 3, settlement and budget.** Actors: experiments, datasets, instruments, review frames, budget convention. Currency: residual versus agreement. Anchor: a measurement that does not move when a frame disagrees. Question type: triangulation; the budget is a convention and may not decide an outcome.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat; bind the function and its group; declare the viability comparison before measuring; index the slot residual; settle and carry. Effort above a broken middle link cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).

- **the bond information**, since a final species list plus total abundance cannot tell which losses were unique and which declines crossed a threshold (Fig 13 :129).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the decision must be reported per slot with the two losses in separate columns and the refuters attached. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
