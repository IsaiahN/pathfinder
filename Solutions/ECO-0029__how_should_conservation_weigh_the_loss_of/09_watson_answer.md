## QUESTION

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not return a weight. They return the shape a checkable verdict must have. Atom subtraction and distributed mass reduction are different operators, and Fig 12 :125-130 forbids summing incommensurable readings; a priority score is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. Uniqueness is a functional-group relation to a function slot, established by enumerating the habitat outward, not a standing label. Decline and loss overlap at a pre-declared viability threshold and must be unbundled, with the mixed case given its own loop. Irreversibility raises the burden of proof before action, not the score. Every verdict is per slot, and the universal scalar is a declared `gap` owed to a measurement that supplies one currency and does not move when a frame disagrees. The result is `general` on shape, `specified` on mechanisms, `gap` on the scalar, and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** a decision could be settled by an aggregate that hides a live slot, or by a standing rarity list whose valence flips across functions, or by treating a decline below viability as recoverable when the atom is already lost. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is a real management decision, so independent checking is worth buying. It is bounded because the packet states its refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The avoidable part is spending readers' independence by sending a conclusion instead of a generator.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim and its refuters can be superseded without withdrawing any figure line. A slot measurement can overturn a verdict for one community without changing the structure, and the union can be re-bundled if a clean partition is demonstrated. No primitive is committed and no species is selected. A sent packet can be corrected.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement supplying one common currency for atom loss and mass decline that passes the anchor test of Fig 2 :29-30 would close the F1 gap and license the weight; a standing unique-species list retaining rank across slots would break Fig 11 :49-51; an aggregate shown to settle a live slot would break Fig 1 :19/:22; a clean partition with no viability threshold would break the union reading; a recovery restoring the original habitat with no maintenance would break Fig 11 :61/:66. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
