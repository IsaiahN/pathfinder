## What the wider literature contributes, and its limits

1. **The object is split, not singular.** Haidt (2007) and Haidt, Koller and Dias (1993) show that what counts as a moral violation varies with culture and with the kind of act, and that affect often predicts the judgment better than harm appraisal. This corroborates the figure instruction to index the residual per slot (Fig 1 :19), but it is a different discipline with its own evidence, so it is corroboration at moderate independence, not proof.
2. **The person is not the whole mechanism.** Bandura's modeling page and Richerson and Boyd's definition both put the learning inside social transmission. This corroborates Fig 11 :39-40 (capability is of agent and habitat). Whiten et al. show a conformity bias in chimpanzees, a genuinely low-overlap animal-behaviour source: the habitat mechanism is not uniquely human. Fig 2 :31 reminds us that the value of this agreement depends on how little the frames share.
3. **A highly relevant essay is also a warning.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says directly of the developmental case: "The cognitive development I was thinking about was not actually a blank slate. Children had parents who taught them, nurtured them, corrected them. The blank slate was a half-truth. The child is a lineage." This is the framework author writing about the same subject, so it has high overlap with the figures. It corroborates the habitat claim but cannot independently witness it. It also supplies the honesty rule that the output is "simulacra" unless derived from the real architecture.

No source read conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The essays are by the framework's author, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight; the four external research sources carry more.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** — "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essays.
- **Fig 2 :40** — "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** — "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted; it does not send an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** validate the shape claim against the ground, without the theory. The recipient checks the per-slot claim, the habitat claim and the union split using their own knowledge. Their verdict is recorded before any framework is shown.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the four source links above. Because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors, any later agreement is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. It is currently a `specified` shape with a recorded `gap` for mechanism, so it is offerable as a shape and refuter, never as a result.

## Hosting

Create the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` as a single file and host it on a page that lets a recipient decline contact. An opt-in download page is closer to the figure rule than an attachment pushed to a list. The figures are the authority; the linked essays are context, not proof.
