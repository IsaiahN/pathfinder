# The Tether's answer on learning what is acceptable or admirable

**Mode: `specified` on the shape claims, `general` on the bare structural rule, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A person must acquire a vocabulary of which desires and ambitions are acceptable or admirable. The residual R is the gap, per kind of desire, between the evaluator the person currently holds and what the social ground rewards, sanctions, models and ignores. It is a union compound: "acceptable" is a threshold and "admirable" is an ordering. A global "well adjusted" score is not an answer to it (Fig 9 :55-58, derivation in `06_isomers_and_order.md`).

## 2. The shape

1. **Learning is indexed per desire-kind, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19 **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** and Fig 1 :28 **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."** The object slot here is a kind of desire or ambition. Mode `specified`.

2. **The learner is not the whole mechanism.** Fig 11 :39 **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** and Fig 11 :40 **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** The habitat is enumerated outward (Fig 11 :43-44), and an actor's valence is relative to the residual, not fixed (Fig 11 :49-51). Mode `specified`.

3. **The two predicates unbundle.** "Acceptable" is a comparison threshold (Operators table :47-52); "admirable" is an ordering (Fig 13 :19-20). Fig 9 :58 **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."** They share a functional group (Fig 12 :124), and across cultures with the same desire-kind set they can be isomers (Fig 12 :117). Mode `specified`.

4. **The binding failure is the vocabulary link.** The learning chain runs perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Fig 3 :25-26 **"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."** A child may read approval accurately and still lack a term for the ambition being approved. Mode `specified`, unmeasured.

5. **What crosses generations is a method, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22 **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :34 **"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."** Mode `general` for the rule, `specified` for its application here.

6. **The figures return a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** They cannot say which desire, which weight, which age. That is a recorded `gap`, not an oversight. Mode `general`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a developmental mechanism. No age, no stage sequence, no causal weight for any actor is asserted.
- Not "parents determine ambitions." The habitat is enumerated, not reduced to one actor; valence is relative to the residual.
- Not that "admirable" and "acceptable" are the same thing, and not that they form a single scale.
- Not that the six physical primitives of Fig 13 govern this social question. That was a retracted strain; the governing lines are Fig 11 and Fig 4.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, no per-desire instrument, no independent frames were run. This is a simulated derivation.
- Not a claim that human judgment settles the mechanism. Fig 2 :37 makes it the right anchor only for a question constitutively about people; for the mechanism it is an instrument that can be wrong on unmeasured axes.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The corresponding checks are:

- **Per-slot measurement:** does evaluative learning show a per-desire residual structure, or is a single global scalar sufficient? (tests claim 1).
- **Contact manipulation:** does an isolated change in the learner with no change in habitat contact move the ground? Fig 11 :40 predicts no (tests claim 2).
- **Union test:** is there an intersection case where "acceptable" and "admirable" come apart, so two conditions bind? (tests claim 3).
- **Vocabulary instrument:** can a term-for-desire measure be built from a worse one already returning something, per Fig 6 :32 **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** (tests claim 4).
- **Method versus recording:** can a transferred practice be shown to be reapplicable rather than a replay? (tests claim 5, the live strain).
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5: a global-scalar result, an isolated improvement that moves the ground, a single-scalar collapse of the two predicates, a recording-only culture, or a figure line that names a specific desire.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :134 **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"** and Fig 13 :115 **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."** The shape stops where the instruments stop: the vocabulary link is a link nobody has looked at (Fig 3 :27). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which desires are admirable in a given culture, and it does not claim a developmental mechanism. It says that the learning is indexed per kind of desire rather than averaged into one score; that the learner and the habitat form one system, so improving the person in isolation changes nothing; that "acceptable" (a threshold) and "admirable" (an ordering) are two overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before they are studied; that the likely break in the chain is the missing vocabulary for a rewarded desire, a link with no instrument; and that what passes between generations is a method and a head start, not a recording of one success. It refuses to name the desire because it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the gap. The center stays open until a ground settles it.
