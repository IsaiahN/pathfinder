## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: two overlapping conditions (acceptable = threshold, admirable = ordering), so a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-58). Symmetry: the two predicates are ordered, not symmetric; learner and ground are asymmetric. Scale: individual learning at one scale, enculturation across generations at another (Fig 4 :19-25). R is a slice, never a total.
- **Domain 1, the learner's frame.** Actors: the person, belief `b`, weighting `w`, library Γ of desire-kind terms. Currency: per-slot residual, bets, observations. Anchor: the ground for "what is in fact rewarded"; human judgment is legitimate only for the constitutively-about-people sub-question (Fig 2 :36-38). Question type: partition of which desire-compositions are legal.
- **Domain 2, the social habitat.** Actors: caregivers, peers, institutions, media, models. Currency: approval, sanction, imitation, status, correction. Anchor: the ground, which does not update in the unmaintained case; capability is of agent and habitat (Fig 11 :39-40). Question type: union of overlapping contact conditions.
- **Domain 3, the culture / population scale.** Actors: many agents, a shared library, something that ranks what they produce. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground and the conditions the seed lands in (Fig 4 :34). Question type: partition of what may cross the membrane.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: moral-development researchers, expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-17). The middle link is the likely break. Refuse the Fig 13 direct-composition reading before minting (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a research frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific content does not. Learning is per desire-kind, agent-and-habitat, limited by an under-instrumented vocabulary link, with methods and head starts rather than recordings crossing generations; "acceptable" and "admirable" are a threshold and an ordering that unbundle as a union compound. The figures return a shape, not a name, so which desire is learned and by what causal weight is a recorded `gap`. A first reading that used Fig 13's physical composition rule for this social question was retracted as a strain. Everything here is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded, `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The retracted Fig 13 reading and the over-broad "shape, not a name" reading are recorded, not erased; the specific bindings are removable without touching the derivation. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-desire instrument showing whether the residual is indexed per slot; a contact manipulation showing whether an isolated learner change moves the ground; an intersection-case test for the union split; a method-versus-recording discriminator for transmitted practices; or a figure re-census that legitimately governs social desire-learning without strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
