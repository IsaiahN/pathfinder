## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are figure-grounded, not just legal. A set of readers chosen for likely agreement is a set selected to share an evidence pool, and "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Alignment is not produced by negotiation: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). And a reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42).

Legally, unsolicited bulk outreach harvested from personal pages breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

This folder was produced by a language model simulating a derivation. By the framework's own rule the output is only as good as its validator and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself" (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, 2026-11-19, opened 2026-11-22). A reader who wants to settle this question must take the packet in `08` to a ground: a developmental study with per-desire measures and frames that do not share an evidence pool. Until then the verdict stands as `candidate`.
