## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran it on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:28 (per-slot residual), Fig 3 :25-27 (under-instrumented middle link), Fig 4 :22-23 (methods up, head starts down), Fig 9 :54-58 (union unbundling) and Fig 11 :39-44 (habitat and capability).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: learning is per desire-kind, agent-and-habitat, split into threshold and ordering, limited by a vocabulary link, transmitted as methods; the framework returns a shape.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a would-be concrete study claim, for example "Bandura's modeling is the Fig 11 introduction operator" or "conformity bias is the ⋛ bond."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the requirement to **enumerate the habitat outward** (Fig 11 :44) is usually replaced in a study by listing the parents only, a first-order enumeration;
- the **asymmetry** of Fig 11 :61-62 (introduction available, exclusion not) is lost when a study speaks of "removing" an influence;
- the **anchor split** of Fig 2 :36-38 collapses when human judgment is used as a single ground for both the factual and the mechanism questions.

A fourth constraint is at risk: the **per-slot indexing** of Fig 1 :19 is easily averaged away into a single "moral development score," which is exactly the error the figure names.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a structure must be learned in contact with a habitat, and what crosses generations is a method and a head start. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
