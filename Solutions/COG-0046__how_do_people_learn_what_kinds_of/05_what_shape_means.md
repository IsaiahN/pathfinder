## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**, because naming the primitive would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "Learning is indexed per desire-kind" is a shape. "Children learn ambition X at age Y from parent Z" is a name.
- "Capability belongs to agent and habitat" is a shape. "The mother is the primary transmitter in all cultures" is a name.
- "A threshold and an ordering unbundle into two conditions" is a shape. "The threshold is Kohlberg's stage 3" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then let recurring new terms narrow the space. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." This is the method for the under-instrumented middle link of Fig 3.
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: describe the effect rather than the cause, because "your words are borrowed too".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach packet asks what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the searcher leaves as the author. Also: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That sentence is the exact justification for the union unbundling in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The external research on moral learning is such an import candidate, but it has not been used to name a primitive. It corroborates the shape only. Fig 8 :50-52 warns that "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." The ledger in `10_source_ledger.csv` is that record.

## 4. Why this particular question is exposed to the source error

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the mechanism. A projection of green "can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it." Indiscernibility is not identity: "the projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."

Applied here: an observed pattern of admiration is a projection. Reading "the peer group caused this ambition" off that projection is a source claim, and several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a confident name. Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* sharpens this for the developmental case. "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." A list of admired traits carries almost none of the history that made them admired. That is the tractable residual for a study: measure the correction, not just the trait.

## 5. The question is explicitly about children, and the essay corpus knows it

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* is the fourth essay read this session and it addresses this question's own subject:

> "The cognitive development I was thinking about was not actually a blank slate. Children had parents who taught them, nurtured them, corrected them. The blank slate was a half-truth. The child is a lineage."

This is direct corroboration for the habitat finding F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. It is also a caution: the same essay says an LLM can only simulate the derivation, and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

**Correction to an earlier reading in this folder.** I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket prohibition on naming any actor in the learning story. It is not. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Caregivers, peers and institutions are habitat actors read off the world (Fig 11 :43-48), not primitives invented to fill a slot. Naming them is enumeration, not a forbidden name. What remains forbidden is naming a specific desire or weight as if the framework derived it. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A set of readers gathered for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground to answer.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
