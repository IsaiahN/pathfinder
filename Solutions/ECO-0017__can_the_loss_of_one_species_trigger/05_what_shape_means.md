## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the form of the answer we can state before we can measure it: *a set of contact paths, typed, ordered outward from the primary loss, with a stop condition and a per-slot residual*. It is not a cascade count, not an R50, and not a named "top keystone species."

Concretely:

- shape: "enumerate contacts, then contacts of contacts, until the residual stops mattering" (Fig 11 :44).
- name or number: "the loss of species X will cause exactly 7 secondary extinctions in this web."

The first is figure-licensed. The second is a ground reading that this folder does not have.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For ecology, that is exactly what a refuter in effect terms does: state the removal, the resolved contact distance, and what would count as no cascade.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time." The same essay warns: "indiscernibility is not identity." A shape that fits every dataset is not thereby the cause of the cascade.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here that means the enumeration stops when no further contact slot carries residual, not when a preferred number is reached.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So importing a network-science threshold from physics is allowed only with provenance and a shadow test over this residual; it does not become an ecological primitive.

## 4. Why a single cascade number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; several physical compositions produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," the spectrometer.

The same structure appears in *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "the frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." That is provenance loss.

Applied here: "7 secondary extinctions" is a reading, not a source. A coextinction path, a trophic cascade, and a shared-abrupt-environmental-driver can produce the same count. A count cannot comment on which. What is owed is a better instrument that discriminates paths (interaction strengths, typed links, removal experiments), not a more confident number.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Our simulated census is in exactly that position.

## 5. Q: what does "shape" mean for this question? ANSWERED

For ECO-0017 the shape is:

1. **A typed contact enumeration**, starting from the removed species and extending outward, step by step, to the point where the residual stops mattering (Fig 11 :43-44, :47).
2. **A per-slot report**, not an aggregate: which species is lost, at which order out, through which relation (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. **A residual-relative role assignment**: which actors are live buffers and which are live transmitters for the residual being tracked (Fig 11 :49-51).
4. **A stop condition** that is measured, not assumed: the enumeration ends when no further slot carries positive residual, and the instrument must be able to resolve that absence.
5. **A refusal of the number**: a threshold or count is a filter for budgeting effort (Fig 9 :60), never a witness.

The shape is checkable by a coextinction researcher in under twenty minutes because each part is an effect description: remove this species, resolve contacts at these typed links, report the losses at each order, name what would refute it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

Two overreadings are recorded rather than silently erased.

**Correction 1: "shape, not a name" governs a missing primitive, not a measured relation.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. The contact types here (pollination, predation, competition, dependency, Fig 11 :47) are not missing primitives; they are read off the world. Recording a measured typed contact is a binding from the ecological frame, provenanced as such. It is not the invention of a seventh primitive. My §4 metamerism point still stands: a *cause* inferred from a bare count is undetermined by that count. What is wrong is the inference, not the naming of a measured link.

**Correction 2: the Tether's "habitat" is NOT the ecological "niche."** This is a direct warning in *Arrival of the Fittest*, section "Three mappings offered and declined": "The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright." I initially treated Fig 11's habitat as if it were the standard ecological habitat concept. It is not. It is a residual-indexed field of contact, and it changes when the question changes. Therefore:

- the Fig 11 DDT example is an **instance** the framework describes, arrived at independently (the source article tags Carson 1962 as *an instance*, a retrodiction, not a derivation);
- using "habitat" as a synonym for "niche" would be the declined mapping, and is refused here;
- the correct phrase is "field of contact with R," enumerated from the residual outward.

Both corrections are carried into `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 and `11_audit.md`. Neither erases the earlier reading.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous coextinction researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a real community.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The article's own demo section adds that untested outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
