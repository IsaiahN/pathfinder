# The Tether's answer on cascading extinctions

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the domain mechanisms, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

A primary extinction is observed; a one-step prediction says only its direct partners should be affected; the observation sometimes shows additional losses two or three steps out, and sometimes none. R is indexed per species slot, not pooled into a biodiversity number (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is a union compound (possibility plus scale), not one hard question (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape, per domain

**Domain 1, the ecological field of contact (figure-governed).**

1. **Yes, conditionally.** Fig 11 :52-53: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." A loss propagates where the contact slot carries live residual. The formula's SUPPORT guard, |R⁺_s| > 0 for some s, applies: a species with no live dependents has no cascade. So the possibility claim is `general`, not universal.
2. **Scale is a path, not a count.** Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." The stop condition is the scale. Reporting a single number is a filter for effort, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
3. **Role is residual-relative.** Fig 11 :49-51: an actor's valence is relative to the residual, not carried; the same actor is benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another; no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. This is why "which species matter" cannot be answered once and for all.
4. **Bond type is decisive.** Fig 11 :47 names the typed relations; Fig 12 :117 makes same-atoms-different-bond a different substance. Mutualistic, trophic and competitive arrangements of the same species set are isomers, not variants. Functional group (Fig 12 :123) explains redundancy; catalyst (Fig 12 :119) explains why generalists survive their own use; cycle (Fig 12 :121) explains why feedback cannot be represented by a chain.
5. **The framework refuses the name and the number.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It returns the enumeration rule, not the cascade magnitude and not a keystone species name.

**Domain 2, network topology (`specified`, corroborated but not decided by world sources).** Robustness, R50, connectance, nestedness and modularity are frame-internal scores. They are useful for budgeting search (Fig 9 :60) and they corroborate the structural shape: world sources report that functional redundancy and modularity matter (Kehoe et al. 2021; Sanders et al. 2018), that no single common index reliably guides management (McDonald-Madden et al. 2016), and that connectance delays a rivet-like threshold (Dunne et al. 2002, read via the review literature). None of them is the ground for this derivation; the ground is a removal record.

**Domain 3, network physics and infrastructure (low-overlap corroboration, `specified`).** Dependency among components creates vulnerability; targeted failure of a high-connectivity node triggers a cascade; islanding can halt propagation (Valdez et al. 2020). Modularity limits contagion across financial networks (Haldane and May 2011). These are different closures, so their agreement with the shape means something; their numerical thresholds do not transfer. Islanding is habitat substitution, not exclusion (Fig 11 :61, :66; strain S5 in `06_isomers_and_order.md`).

**Domain 4, verification (`general`).** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so their agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40 says why.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a cascade magnitude, threshold value, or extinction count.
- Not a named "most important species." Roles are residual-relative.
- Not that contact with the ecological niche concept is licensed. The Tether habitat is a residual-indexed field of contact; the niche mapping was explicitly declined in *Arrival of the Fittest* (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5b).
- Not that the DDT case was derived by the framework. It is tagged an *instance*, a case arrived at independently (Carson 1962, in the source's citation list).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles a magnitude.
- Not that every species loss propagates. SUPPORT requires a live slot.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color reading "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it by arriving as a better instrument. Here:

1. **Path test:** remove a species with measured live typed dependents and resolve secondary losses at first, second and third order. If the residual truly stops at order two, the enumeration is confirmed; if it keeps arriving, the stop condition was wrong.
2. **Isomer test:** hold species set and interaction strengths fixed, rearrange interaction types, and compare cascade distributions. A difference confirms the bond claim (Fig 12 :117).
3. **Aggregation test:** compare per-slot contact enumeration against a pooled richness or R50 predictor out of sample. If the aggregate wins, the per-slot rule is refuted (Fig 1 :22).
4. **Standing falsifiers:** the six pre-registered conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

The dependency chain is ordered: the missing instrument is the objective link, what counts as scale (Fig 3 :19, :27). "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at this budget, not underivable.** The instruments exist in incomplete form (network datasets, interaction strengths, removal experiments), so the residual is sharpenable from something already returning a reading (Fig 6 :32). Formula step 7 keeps the three questions apart: SEARCHED (robustness metrics already in the closure, cheapest, tried first), INWARD (resolve indirect contacts and define scale), OUTWARD (network physics as a low-overlap frame, already opened). INWARD and OUTWARD are never read against each other. "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39). The answer stops at the instrument limit and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for an extinction cascade, and it does not name a keystone species. It says that a species loss can propagate through typed contact, that first-order scoping undercounts because the residual can be three steps out, and that the scale is the length and structure of the live contact paths: which species, through which relation, at which order, and where propagation stops. Roles are relative to the residual being tracked, so no standing list of important species transfers between questions. The same species set with different interaction types is a different substance, not a variant, and redundancy is a functional-group property rather than a headcount. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ground settles the magnitude, and nothing here has claimed that settlement.
