## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: cascade scale is set by the typed contact structure enumerated outward, roles are residual-relative, report per slot, refuse the number.

- **The ground as anchor.** A simulation output is not a removal record. Fig 2 :29: an anchor does not change its answer because of what a frame thinks.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be enumerated outward from the loss, and the stop condition is where the residual stops arriving. That is the residue worth sending.
