## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Patel et al. 2015** directly tests the "one subsystem must have preceded the others" assumption. They show precursors of ribonucleotides, amino acids and lipids from common feedstocks (HCN and derivatives, UV light, H2S reductant, Cu(I)/Cu(II) photoredox). This is the strongest world corroboration of the cycle/co-emergence shape. It is a laboratory result, not a historical demonstration.

2. **Sutherland 2010** states the issue as an open, chemistry-addressable question, and explicitly allows that *"Metabolism, or the roots of metabolism, could also potentially have coevolved with RNA"* given a suitable geochemical setting. It also notes that if synergies are found with lipid and peptide chemistry, the purist RNA-world concept might have to be loosened. Corroborates the union finding.

3. **The iron-sulfur world frame** (Wachtershauser, as summarised in the Wikipedia extract) is the metabolism-first position: a primitive autocatalytic surface metabolism, a metabolic cycle, and later cellularization. It supplies the network side of the cycle, and its own account ends in coevolution of cellular organisation, genetic machinery and enzymatisation of metabolism, which converges with the co-emergence shape from a low-overlap frame.

4. **The dissipative-systems frame** (Prigogine and followers, as summarised) supplies the physical grammar: open systems far from equilibrium, autocatalytic reactions, spontaneous symmetry breaking and limit cycles. This is a low-overlap physics corroboration of Fig 12's cycle and Fig 13's gradient (a difference that can be spent).

5. **Conflict rule.** No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The chemistry and geochemistry frames are genuinely independent of the framework (different authors, different evidence pools), so their convergence with the figure shape is stronger corroboration than the framework author's own essays would be. It is still corroboration only, never a ground reading.

**Stage 1.** Send the residual (the bundled either/or), the split, the cycle shape and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
