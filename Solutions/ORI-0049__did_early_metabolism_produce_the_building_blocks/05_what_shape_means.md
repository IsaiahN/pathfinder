## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and :135 adds *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the answer itself. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0049 the shape is **a dependency that closes a cycle, crossing from a structure (network/gradient) through a state (energy or time) into another structure (ordering/sequence), with a catalyst that survives its own use**. The name the question wants ("metabolism first" or "RNA first") is exactly what the shape withholds.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on method: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Then: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- **This question's own essay warning.** *The Library of Babel*: *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed. Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is F1 in the audit: the either/or is a bundle, and the search cannot be run to an end until it is split.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground is the synthesis experiment, the replication experiment, and the geological record. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :26-28). The chemistry and geochemistry frames are low-overlap imports; they carry provenance and corroborate the shape, but they do not name the historical route.

To call the origin "metabolism-first" or "RNA-first" is to name a route. The framework returns a shape. Until a ground reading discriminates the routes, the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The *source* problem: a reading of an artifact does not determine its history. The exemplar's `05` records this from *The Hard Problem is About Source* (metamerism: a spectrometer reads green and cannot say whether the source was one light or a mixture). I did not re-fetch that essay this session, so I cite it here only as recorded in the exemplar and do not rely on it for the verdict.

What matters for ORI-0049 is the same structural point stated by the figures: Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* The chemistry frame (Patel 2015, Sutherland 2010) and the geochemistry frame (iron-sulfur world) reach a co-emergence-like conclusion from different evidence pools. That is why their agreement is worth more than agreement inside one camp, and still not a ground reading.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework say the answer must look like?** Not "which came first", but a closed loop: a network that supplies monomers (energy, gradient, network), monomers that compose into a polymer (ordering), and a catalyst that survives its use and returns the polymer's catalytic function to the network. The loop passes through a state because Fig 13 :75 says two structures join only through a state, and no kind is senior to the other (Fig 13 :4). The historical route is `gap`, to be closed by the ground.

**Does the question commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, in its framing. It asks for a name ("A produced B" or "B produced A") where the framework can deliver only a shape, and it treats a union as a partition. The corrected form is: *a cycle crossing kinds through a state, with the historical order left open*, stated `specified` with four written refuters.

## 6. Consequences for contact and for my own status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous names who pick a camp is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be tried against the ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the origin question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
