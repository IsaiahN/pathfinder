## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54 and :57 (union split), Fig 12 :121 and :119 (cycle and catalyst), Fig 13 :75 and :4 (cross-kind medium, no rank).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: the either/or is a union, the dependency is a cycle, the historical order is `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete answer of the form "metabolism and RNA co-emerged", or a camp's "metabolism first".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **cross-kind medium**, that the two structures join only through a state (Fig 13 :75); a flat "co-emergence" drops it;
- the **catalyst condition**, a part that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119), which is what actually closes the loop;
- the **no-rank rule** (Fig 13 :4), which a historical "first" would breach by ranking one kind above the other.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a union must be split, and the dependency closes a cycle. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
