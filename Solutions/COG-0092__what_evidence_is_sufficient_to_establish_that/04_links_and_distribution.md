## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Metamerism and identifiability are the same shape in two fields.** Color science shows that a three-value reading cannot identify its spectral source; statistics shows that observationally equivalent parameterizations cannot be separated by data alone. Both predict that a between-group score cannot name its own cause. Both are genuinely low-overlap with the framework and with each other. This is the strongest world corroboration of F1.
2. **The field's own standards are conventions under active dispute.** Putnick and Bornstein 2016 document a ladder of nested constraints; Avvisati et al. 2019 document the limits of standard cutoffs and the failure of preventive measures to guarantee comparability; Funder and Gardiner 2024 and Fischer et al. 2025 dispute prohibitionist uses of invariance. Read together they corroborate the figure rule that a cutoff is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. They do not decide the figure verdict: Fig 9 :60 does.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the articles.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here includes discarding a genuine cultural difference because a gate failed.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before any framework material; (4) only then the links, with the note that the verbatim figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a recipient shows that a reading determines its source with no discriminator, or that a cutoff has become the verdict, stop and repair before sending further material.
