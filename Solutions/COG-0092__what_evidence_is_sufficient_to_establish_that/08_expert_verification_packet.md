## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A between-group score difference does not determine its source. Culture, translation, response style and sampling can all produce the same reading, so a passed invariance test is a gate and not a verdict. Sufficient evidence is structural, not a single statistic: describe the difference in effect terms before choosing a method; split the overlapping sources and name the compound that survives each lever; measure the translation round trip; read each item within each group; enumerate who was in contact with the claim; keep an anchor that does not move when the frame is pushed; obtain a second method drawing on genuinely different evidence; use thresholds as budget filters, never as verdict witnesses; record the lineage; and report `unreached` where instruments stop discriminating. Over a fixed item-response set, two groups with the same score but different item-meaning bonds are isomers, so the score records ingredients and not arrangement. No figure line names a sufficient test, so method choice is an import whose provenance and shadow test must be shown.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — A reading does not determine its source; a gate is not the ground

- **Statement:** `Δ` underdetermines G versus M versus P. Naming the source from the score alone is the metamerism error. Passing invariance is necessary for many comparisons but sufficient for none.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :95 *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."* Fig 10 :18 *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*. Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a reading from which the source follows deductively at the available resolution with no discriminating procedure; or show that every item-by-group residual is flat while the pooled mean still shifts, so indexing was unnecessary; or show a ground reading in which a gate paid for the verdict.

### C2 — Independence and a non-updating anchor are what make agreement count

- **Statement:** many studies that share a scale, translation, sample frame and disciplinary priors are one witness. What is needed is low-overlap methods each ranged separately against an anchor that does not update on the hypothesis. For a question constitutively about people, blind informed judgment about outcomes is a legitimate anchor; the questionnaire is an instrument that can be wrong on axes it does not measure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :38 *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
- **What to check:** are the two proposed methods actually independent at the evidence level, not merely differently named? Can the proposed anchor be moved by what the measuring frame thinks? If the judge knows the hypothesis, it is a frame, not an anchor.
- **Refuter:** show that two methods thought independent draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement collapses to one witness; or show that the anchor updates on the frame's own report, so panel 1 drift returns; or exhibit low-overlap methods that diverge with no resolver.

### C3 — The sufficient test is a gap; method choice is an imported discriminator with provenance

- **Statement:** no verbatim figure line names a sufficient statistical test with a fixed threshold. The named methods in the wider literature (round trips, cognitive interviews, MTMM designs, anchoring vignettes, invariance models, phylogenetic independence) are imports from other frames, not figure verdicts. They are admissible only with a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** a figure re-census that finds a governing line naming a sufficient test; or a demonstration that a statistical procedure determines source with no round-trip gap; or an import used here without provenance or shadow test. Any fires and this sub-claim is withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a passed invariance test proves a real difference, and not that a failed one proves an artifact.
- Not that any named statistic, cutoff, method or sample size is sufficient by itself.
- Not that many agreeing studies establish a source. Agreement in one evidence pool is one witness.
- Not that translation equivalence is impossible. A measured round trip can clear a specific item; what is refused is declaring equivalence without measuring it.
- Not that this packet establishes any particular cross-cultural claim. No dataset was read or measured.
- Not `grounded`. Every claim above is `general` or `specified` and remains a candidate.
- No personal emails, no bulk-send plan, no request for trust or agreement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Concretely: a check installed before the comparison that could have failed and did not, on an axis outside the channel; a second low-overlap method returning the same source assignment; an enumerated habitat; per-item, per-group readings; and an anchor that did not move. Fig 9 :42 *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*. If instruments stop discriminating, the honest answer is `unreached` with the dropped constraints enumerated.

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
