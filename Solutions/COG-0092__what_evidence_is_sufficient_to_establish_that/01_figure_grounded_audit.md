## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A cross-cultural comparison returns a reading: two groups differ by `Δ` on a score, a response distribution or an observed behaviour. The prediction *"same construct, same instrument, same contact"* fails to explain `Δ`. The residual `R` is: **the source of `Δ` is unassigned at the resolution of the instrument.** At least three candidate sources predict the same `Δ`:

- **G (world):** the difference is contact with a real difference in what the question is about, the construct, practice or experience.
- **M (measurement channel):** the difference is produced by translation, item meaning, response style, scale use, mode or instrument drift.
- **P (contact habitat):** the difference is produced by who was sampled, where, when and under what conditions.

The candidates are not interchangeable. Swapping G for M or P changes the remedy, so this is not one question with three labels. It is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57): overlapping conditions, not a partition, so a lever that clears M leaves the G-and-P intersections standing.

**The word "sufficient" is itself the sought-for shape.** The formula's Direction section warns that a frame cannot check the direction of its own question because the shape it seeks is held as a preference rather than a bound term (Fig 5 :62-66). If that phrase is left as a preference, no check can refute it. So bind it now, before any method is chosen, as an ordinary term:

> **Bound term, S:** evidence for "real" is sufficient when it (i) discriminates G from M and P at the resolution the claim needs, (ii) comes from low-overlap frames each ranged separately against an anchor that does not update on the reading, (iii) survives a check outside the channel that could have failed, (iv) is read per item and per group, (v) enumerates the habitat, and (vi) is recorded with a lineage that lets a later reader locate the error. When no such discrimination is possible, the honest output is `unreached`, not `real` and not `artifact`.

This binding is a `specified` prediction about what a study would have to do. It is not a figure line and is not claimed as `grounded`.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — A score difference does not determine its source (decisive premise)

Color metamerism is the clean instance: three cone classes reduce a spectrum to three tristimulus values, so *"different combinations of light across all wavelengths can produce an equivalent receptor response and the same tristimulus values or color sensation"* (Wikipedia, *Metamerism (color)*, 2026, opened 2026-11-22). Statistics names the same structure: a model is non-identifiable when *"two or more parametrizations are observationally equivalent"* (Wikipedia, *Identifiability*, 2026, opened 2026-11-22). The framework says it in Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*, and Fig 12 :95: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

So `Δ` is a projection. G, M and P can produce the same reading. A study that reports `Δ` and then names G has named a source from a reading, which is the metamerism error in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. The essay's own resolution is the model for this question: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*. Declaring that the scale means the same thing in both groups does not make it so.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 8, Fig 12, Fig 10, plus low-overlap world sources in mathematics and color science). **Refuter:** exhibit a reading `Δ` from which the source follows deductively at the instrument's resolution, with no discriminating procedure required. If that exists, F1 fails and no source-discriminating instrument is owed.

### F2 — Translation is a lossy transform; measure the round trip, do not assert equivalence

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31-32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."* A translation that is never round-tripped feels exactly as equivalent as one that was.

Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* An item that is sharp only in the source language fails to travel. Fig 12 :95 (above): two items with identical wording after translation can be different instruments whose notation cannot show the difference.

World corroboration: Avvisati, Le Donné and Paccagnella 2019 (*Measurement Instruments for the Social Sciences*, open access, full article opened) report that *"no statistical procedure can remedy what a scale, by design, cannot deliver"* and that even rigorous preventive measures *"cannot guarantee the full comparability of measurement instruments"*. Putnick and Bornstein 2016 (*Developmental Review*, PMC, full text opened): *"different groups can apply different meanings to the same cognition or behavior"*. Gary King's anchoring-vignettes page (Harvard, opened) proposes to *"measure directly, and then subtract off, the incomparable portion"*, but Avvisati et al. record that vignette equivalence is often hard to meet, so the anchor is not automatic.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 4, Fig 6, Fig 12, Fig 10, low overlap between a membrane rule, a description rule, a bond rule and a grounding rule). **Refuter:** a documented round trip with a measured gap below the preset resolution, plus a cognitive-interview result in which the target-culture respondents use the item in the source-intended way. If both hold for the specific item, the translation seam for that item is cleared, not assumed.

### F3 - Channel failure is not world failure; the anchor must not update

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :25-28 name the mechanisms: *"stale percepts, dropped transients,"* *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*, and *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. A rejected invariance test is a diagnosis of the channel, not a proof that no real difference exists. Conversely, a passed invariance test is a gate, not the ground: `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 says *"SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*

Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :33-34: if the reference moves, *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*

Fig 2 :37-38 is decisive for which anchor is legitimate here: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* The same lines warn that a scale *"is only an instrument"* for other questions and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"*. A cross-cultural claim about experience is constitutively about people, so blind, informed human judgment about outcomes is a legitimate anchor; the questionnaire is an instrument and can be wrong on axes it does not measure (translation, response style). If the judge knows the hypothesis, the anchor becomes another frame (Fig 2 :34).

Fig 2 :45-46: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Each frame is corrected separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move the anchor.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 10 and Fig 2, with `THE_FORMULA` step 5). **Refuter:** show that the proposed external criterion updates on what the measuring frame thinks, or that the supposed channel failure is actually a change in the ground. Either breaks the distinction and the verdict with it.

### F4 — Read per item and per group; the pooled mean is where the signal dies

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global mean difference across a 20-item scale can be near zero while one item carries a live between-group residual, and Fig 1 :20 confirms that is *"a legal state and not an inert one"*. The decision-relevant unit is the item-by-group slot: differential item functioning, threshold shifts and intercept shifts are per item and per group.

World corroboration: Putnick and Bornstein 2016 show that when an item such as crying or weight gain indicates depression in one group but not another, *"mean differences on the scale may mislead"*. Avvisati et al. 2019 note that global fit tests *"provide little information about the specific restrictions (groups and item parameters) that are responsible for the rejection"*.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 1, plus psychometrics). **Refuter:** demonstrate that every item-by-group residual is flat while the pooled mean still shifts. Then aggregation was not hiding a live signal, and F4 does not apply to that comparison.

### F5 — Independence is the parameter; same-instrument replication is one witness

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* :32: *"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."* :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 8 :47-49: independence is *"one quantity read twice"*, deciding both whether agreement means anything and whether a union has surplus; *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*

Applying two self-report questionnaires in two languages to the same respondents under the same mode is one method wearing two names. The MTMM literature states the same rule operationally: *"delivering two self-report measures are not truly different measures; whereas using an interview scale or a psychosomatic reading would be"* (Wikipedia, *Multitrait-multimethod matrix*, 2025, opened). Campbell and Fiske's design requires multiple traits and genuinely different methods, because *"Scores could be correlated because they measure similar traits, or because they are based on similar methods, or both."*

Fig 10 :80-81: *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained; frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated, because no amount of use makes them less alike and replacement is the only refresh."* A second lab using the same translated scale is a rotation, not new evidence about source.

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 8, Fig 10, with MTMM as low-overlap corroboration). **Refuter:** show that two methods thought to be independent actually draw on one evidence pool (their agreement then collapses to one witness), or show that a genuinely low-overlap method returned a different source assignment and no discriminator resolves it. The first strengthens the warning; the second breaks any claim that agreement has already settled the case.

### F6 — Split the union before searching; one lever leaves the intersections standing

Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* The remainder *"stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound"* (Fig 9 :57-58).

Concretely: back-translation fixes some M, a larger sample fixes some P, and neither fixes the compound where a translated item is also answered under a different response norm. Each compound needs its own loop and its own check. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 9, Fig 3). **Refuter:** find a single lever that clears every G/M/P intersection and whose failure condition can be stated. Then the union reading is wrong and the question can be searched as one problem.

### F7 — The sample is a habitat; enumerate it, never compose it

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Adding more respondents from the same convenient pool does not fix contact with the population the claim names.

Fig 11 :66-70: isolation substitutes a habitat; *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A laboratory panel, a student sample, or a convenience online panel proves that the instrument works there. Fig 11 :73-74: *"A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second."*

The phylogenetic-comparative-methods literature is a low-overlap formal analogue: *"the fact that closely related lineages share many traits and trait combinations as a result of the process of descent with modification means that lineages are not independent"* (Wikipedia, *Phylogenetic comparative methods*, 2026, opened). Shared origin, not shared conclusion, is what removes independence. Cultures and samples can be correlated at origin the same way.

**Verdict: single-source on the figure side** (Fig 11 governs habitat enumeration and substitution; the anchor test in Fig 2 corroborates generally but does not govern sampling). World corroboration is from evolutionary biology. **Refuter:** enumerate a habitat and show that omitting an enumerated condition never flips a source assignment. Then contact enumeration was decorative for that comparison.

### F8 — No cutoff is sufficient by itself: the direct answer to the question

The field's standard answer is a threshold: pass configural, metric and scalar invariance (Fig-free world material: Putnick and Bornstein 2016; Avvisati et al. 2019). The figures refuse to let a threshold decide a verdict. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 9 :60: filters are for the budget, witnesses are for the verdict. A cutoff is a filter: it decides where to spend the next hour of checking, not whether the difference is real.

Fig 10 :53: *"A prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong."* An invariance gate that no real dataset can pass is an idealisation, not a requirement. Funder and Gardiner 2024 (*European Journal of Personality*, opened via search extraction with reduced weight) make the same methodological point in the field's own vocabulary: *"the prohibitionist tone of discussions surrounding MI is unhelpful unscientific and discouraging"*. Fischer, Karl, Luczak-Roesch and Hartle 2025 (*Cross-Cultural Research*, opened via search extraction) ask for *"a careful integration of both theoretical arguments and empirical evidence"*. These are classified as corroboration of the filter rule, not as conflicts with the figures: the dispute is about a convention, while the figure verdict is about structure.

What is sufficient is the whole discriminator in the bound term S (section 0): source discrimination, low-overlap frames, a non-updating anchor, a check that can fail, per-slot readings, an enumerated habitat, and a lineage. No single component is sufficient. This is Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* A statistical fit and an external discriminating action are different currencies; summing them into one "sufficiency score" is the error Fig 12 names.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 10, Fig 9, Fig 12). **Refuter:** exhibit a verbatim figure line that names a specific sufficient statistical test with a fixed threshold, or demonstrate a case where a cutoff alone determines the source of `Δ` with no external check. That would close the gap in F9 and change the answer.

### F9 — No figure line names the sufficient test: `gap`, recorded as unreached, not silence

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*

So the honest output of this derivation is a shape plus a refuter and a list of instruments owed, not a named test. The concrete methods named in this folder (round trips, cognitive interviewing, MTMM, anchoring vignettes, invariance models, phylogenetic independence) are **imports from world frames**, each with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*; Fig 8 :50-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). They are not figure verdicts. They are borrowed because they return something at the resolution of the residual.

**Verdict: `gap` on the named test; triangulated that the gap is real** (Fig 9, Fig 8, Fig 13). **Refuter:** a figure re-census that finds a governing line naming a sufficient test, or a world source that shows a measurement procedure which determines source with no round-trip gap. Until one fires, method choice remains an import with provenance.

### F10 — The checker must not author what it checks; keep the lineage

Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* :62-64: *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom. The residual it answered, the reasoning, the date, in a record the seats below can read."* :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that"*. Fig 13 :126-130: *"The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable."* *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."*

For outreach this means the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework. Fig 9 :51: *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*. The author's stake in this answer is declared here and in `09_watson_answer.md`.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 10, Fig 13, Fig 9). **Refuter:** a verification arrangement in which the checker authors the claim and still returns a ground verdict. Then the non-access rule fails and the packet must be redesigned.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim in section 0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A reading `Δ` is shown to determine its source deductively at the available resolution, with no source-discriminating procedure (breaks F1).
2. Two genuinely low-overlap methods that could each have failed return different source assignments for the same `Δ`, and no further instrument discriminates them, or they converge but are shown to share an evidence pool after all (breaks F5 and the anchor requirement).
3. A check installed outside the channel is shown to be unable to fail, or a convention is shown to decide the verdict (breaks F3 and F8).
4. An enumerated habitat condition, when added, flips a source assignment that had been reported as settled (breaks F7 and the union split in F6).
5. A figure line is found that actually names a sufficient test with a fixed threshold (closes the F9 gap and changes the answer).
6. For a specific comparison, every item-by-group residual is flat while the pooled mean still shifts (F4 does not apply there), or conversely a live per-slot signal is shown to survive intact in the pooled mean so indexing was unnecessary.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed accordingly. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation: the shape of sufficient evidence

1. **Bind "sufficient" before choosing a method** (formula Direction; F1). State what would count as a failure and what an answer would look like, so the preference becomes refusable.
2. **Split the union** (F6). Treat translation, response channel and contact habitat as overlapping conditions with their own loops. Name the compound that survives each lever.
3. **Measure the round trip at the translation seam** (F2), with a gap that could have been large and a preset resolution.
4. **Read per item and per group** (F4) and enumerate the habitat before trusting the sample (F7).
5. **Install a check outside the channel that could have failed** (F3, F8), and keep an anchor that does not update on what any frame thinks. For a question constitutively about people, blind informed human judgment about outcomes is a legitimate anchor; the instrument is not.
6. **Buy independence** (F5) with methods drawing on different evidence, and record the provenance of each import so a convergent result is distinguishable from an adopted one.
7. **Use thresholds as filters, never as verdict witnesses** (F8). Do not let a cutoff hand you the answer.
8. **Record the lineage and stop honestly** (F9, F10). Where instruments no longer discriminate, report `unreached` with the enumerated dropped constraints, not a confident source name.
