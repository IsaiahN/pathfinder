## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; the next line adds that the system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For COG-0092 the shape is **not** a named statistic, a named invariance level or a named cutoff. It is the discriminator's form: a difference is established as real only when its source is discriminated by low-overlap checks ranged against an anchor that does not update, read per item and per group, with the habitat enumerated and the lineage recorded. "Pass scalar invariance" is a name where the framework returns a shape. `Δ CFI <= .01` is a name where the framework returns a shape. Both may be useful filters; neither is the answer.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the unknown, then bootstrap: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest held vocabulary is "anchor", "channel", "independence", "round trip" and "slot", not "Cronbach's alpha".
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Describe the residual (an unassigned source of a reading) before choosing a method, or any method will seem to fit.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So say "a check that could have failed and did not", not "a multigroup confirmatory factor analysis with scalar invariance".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet in `08` asks the recipient what they would count as a failure before offering any framework material.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it"*. Closing a source-discrimination question means having built the discriminator, not having quoted a guideline.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question a name (this difference is real, this item is biased, this sample is the problem) is licensed only when a discriminating check could have failed, did not fail, and returned the same name at the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52) with a source gate and a shadow test. The methods in this folder are such imports from psychometrics, survey methodology, statistics, metrology and evolutionary biology. Their provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`; they are not figure verdicts.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A source-discriminating instrument is precisely a new axis: it lets the frame perceive a difference between G, M and P that the bare score could not represent. And the same essay warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Saying "we used the same scale" is a declaration, not a measurement.

## 4. The governing error for this question: source named from a reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the single most relevant essay here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes can create the same reading: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The essay's conclusion is the operational rule: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and the historical resolution was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"* (the spectrometer).

Applied to COG-0092: a between-group score difference is the green reading. "Culture", "translation", "response style" and "sampling" are the candidate light mixes. A study that names the source from the reading alone commits the metamerism error; what is owed is a discriminator, not a stronger assertion. Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*. Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

The independent mathematical statement is identifiability (Wikipedia, *Identifiability*, 2026, opened): a model is non-identifiable when *"two or more parametrizations are observationally equivalent"*. The cross-cultural question is an identifiability question about the source of a reading.

## 5. Independence, agreement and the one-pool trap

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: two people who only check against each other *"drift, and they drift together, so neither notices"*; shared pretext *"is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* The essay names the three safeguards: a ground both are corrected by separately, separate experience, and claims in effect terms. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* restates the arithmetic: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."*

Fig 2 :35 (quoted in the essay): *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 8 :48-49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*

This is why "many studies found the same difference" is not sufficient evidence. If they share a scale, a translation, a sample frame and a discipline's priors, they are one witness spelled many times. The same point appears in *Arrival of the Fittest*'s citation list under Kish 1965 and Shumailov et al. 2024: correlated observations and recycled outputs do not compound evidence.

## 6. Two correction records, kept visible

### Correction A: "shape, not a name" was overstated in an earlier reading

I initially treated the rule as forbidding any concrete method name. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in the library. Saying "use a round trip" is a provenanced import of a method from another frame, not the invention of a missing primitive. What remains forbidden is declaring that the framework itself names the sufficient test. So the gap in `01` F9 is a gap about a governing figure line, not a ban on borrowing methods with their provenance shown. The earlier overstatement is retracted here, not erased; compare `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and the exemplar's analogous `05` section 5b.

### Correction B: the invariance gate is a filter, not a verdict

I initially wrote that scalar invariance "establishes" comparability. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*, and Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The corrected reading: an invariance cutoff allocates checking effort and can rule out some comparisons; it cannot decide that a difference is real. *Arrival of the Fittest*'s citation list marks Goodhart 1975 as an instance of *"what happens when a measure becomes a target"*, which is the named failure when a cutoff becomes the verdict.

**Falsifier for these corrections:** a canonical figure line that names a specific sufficient statistical test for cross-cultural comparison, or a demonstration that a figure's "shape, not a name" rule governs already-available methods rather than missing primitives. If found, both corrections are withdrawn and F9 in `01` is closed.

## 7. Why this folder is a simulation and cannot settle the question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; the outputs are *"simulacra"*. *Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter: the framework *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"*. The same essay says the theory *"cannot validate itself"* and *"you need an external validator"*.

So this folder, including this file, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot establish that any particular cultural difference is real. It can only return the shape that such an establishment would have to take, the refuter written in advance, and the honest stop where instruments no longer discriminate. *Library of Babel*: if the final book does not answer, the possibilities are that the question *"was unanswerable, or malformed"*, or that it was *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*. Both are live here, and both are reported as verdicts rather than silences (Fig 13 :134).

## 8. Prestige is not action (for the outreach consequence)

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous cross-cultural psychologists who agree with this packet would be a prestige loop, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, ask what would refute it, and let a discriminating check settle it. That is the rule behind `02_outreach_email.md` and `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
