# Outreach draft: check the discriminator before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in section "What would refute this" is stated in your own words and you can name what you would count as a failure. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about cross-cultural difference before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the cross-cultural application, not `grounded`):** a between-group score difference does not tell you where it came from. Culture, translation, response style and sampling can all produce the same reading. So a passed measurement-invariance test is a gate, not a verdict. What the figures require is structural: describe the difference in effect terms before choosing a method; split the overlapping sources and name the compound that survives each lever; measure the translation round trip; read each item within each group; enumerate who was actually in contact with the claim; keep an anchor that does not move when the frame is pushed; get a second method drawing on genuinely different evidence; and use thresholds as budget filters, never as verdict witnesses. Where instruments stop discriminating, the honest output is `unreached`, not a confident source name.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named statistic, cutoff or method is sufficient by itself; that a failed invariance test proves a difference is an artifact; that many agreeing studies establish a source when they share a scale and a sample frame; or that this analysis establishes that any particular cultural difference is real. I read no dataset for this. The methods named in the wider literature are imports from other fields, offered with their provenance, not verdicts of the framework. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a reading from which the source follows deductively at the available resolution, with no source-discriminating procedure;
> 2. two genuinely low-overlap methods that could each have failed returning different source assignments for the same difference, with no instrument that resolves them;
> 3. a check that cannot fail, or a cutoff that decides the verdict;
> 4. an enumerated habitat condition that flips a source assignment already reported as settled;
> 5. a figure line that actually names a sufficient test with a fixed threshold.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The verbatim figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Eight essays by Isaiah N, 2026: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-hard-problem-is-about-source and https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-recursive-self-improvement-trap
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the local `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`. Run it on a comparison you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]

| the move | figure-grounded reason |

| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately. |

| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
