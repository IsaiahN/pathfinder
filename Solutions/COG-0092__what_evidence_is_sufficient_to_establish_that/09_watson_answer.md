## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what evidence would establish that a between-group difference is real rather than an artifact of measurement. Option A: a passed invariance test plus a significant group difference is sufficient. Option B: any observed difference is potentially an artifact, so nothing is sufficient. Option C: a reading does not determine its source, so sufficiency is structural, a discriminator with low-overlap frames, a non-updating anchor, per-slot readings, an enumerated habitat, thresholds as filters and a recorded lineage, ending in `unreached` where instruments stop discriminating. What each leads to: A names a gate as a verdict; B refuses all verdicts including the true one; C returns a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop. The figures decide C.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** One reading `Δ` whose source is unassigned among G (world), M (measurement channel) and P (contact habitat). Arity: one reading, at least three candidate sources. Symmetry: many-to-one, so the mapping from source to reading is not invertible and swapping sources changes the remedy. Scale: instrument and habitat seam, recursive at each subscale. Fig 9 :54-57 makes it a union compound, not a partition: a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds.
- **Domain 1, translation and meaning seam.** Actors: source item, target item, translators, adjudicators, bilingual reviewers. Currency: survival of meaning across a crossing, the semantic round-trip gap. Anchor: a documented procedure that can fail. Seam, upstream.
- **Domain 2, response channel and instrument.** Actors: items, scales, response styles, mode, administrator. Currency: the construct-to-score mapping: loadings, thresholds and intercepts, residuals, differential item functioning. Anchor: an external criterion the channel does not author, plus a constraint that can be rejected. Nested partition internally, compound across sources.
- **Domain 3, contact habitat and sample.** Actors: who, where, when, under what conditions; the population the claim names. Currency: contact with that population. Anchor: an enumerated habitat and the target population, not the convenient panel. Habitat, not a partition.
- **Domain 4, substantive claim frame.** Actors: the two groups, the construct, the theory. Currency: predictions about the world. Anchor: the ground for the substantive question, blind informed human judgment about outcomes for a question constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-37). This is where "real" is decided.
- **Domain 5, verification frame.** Actors: independent methods, replications, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Bind the sought shape first (formula Direction), then translation and habitat, then channel invariance, then the substantive comparison, with verification alongside. A comparison read before the upstream checks is a reading of nothing with respect to source.

Strain test: an earlier attempt to use Fig 13 "spectrum = ordering" to require scalar invariance governed a different quantity and was retracted (`01` section 6, `05` section 6). After re-census, one live design strain remains: a cutoff used as a verdict is forbidden (Fig 10 :47) while a cutoff used as a budget filter is permitted (Fig 9 :60). It is kept visible as an operational distinction, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A between-group difference underdetermines its source at the instrument's resolution. No single test is sufficient. What is sufficient is structural: bind the sought shape before choosing a method; split the union of translation, response and habitat conditions and name the compound that survives each lever; measure the round trip across the translation seam; read per item and per group; enumerate the habitat; install a check outside the channel that could have failed; keep an anchor that does not update on what any frame thinks; buy independence with genuinely low-overlap methods; use thresholds as filters and never as verdict witnesses; record the lineage; and report `unreached` where instruments stop discriminating. Over a fixed item-response set, two groups with the same score but different item-meaning bonds are isomers (Fig 12 :95, :117), so the score is the flat formula that records ingredients and not arrangement. The named sufficient test is a `gap`, with method choice an import whose provenance and shadow test are recorded. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the cross-cultural application, ungrounded, still a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or discarding a genuine difference because a cutoff failed. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by treating cutoffs as filters.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. A source assignment can be superseded when a discriminating instrument returns a different result; the method imports are recorded with URLs and dates and can be replaced; a compound can be re-split. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the claims that failed the figure check.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating check that could have failed and did not, on an axis outside the channel; a single lever that clears every G/M/P intersection (would break the union verdict); a figure re-census naming a sufficient test (would close the gap); a demonstration that a score difference determines its source with no round-trip gap; or evidence that repeated administration and translation never shift the construct-to-score mapping. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
