## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is therefore: the narrowed form of the answer you can state before you can settle it. It says *the identity claim dissolves into a contact claim, the contact claim is unreached, an unfalsifiable version has no anchor, and any final law is forbidden*. It does **not** say which computational structure, if any, occupies the fundamental slot.

`UNIVERSE(???)COMPUTES` is a shape. `UNIVERSE(QUANTUM_COMPUTATION)COMPUTES` is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox that you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it".

Here the effect description is: *I observe law-governed, mathematically describable evolution and want to know whether a source-discriminating instrument can tell literal computation from mere computability.* That is a checkable request. "Is the universe a computer?" is not, until a level and a failure are named.

## 3. The essay that is almost this question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* contains a direct treatment of the neighbouring simulation question, and it is the single most relevant canonical passage for QMGR-0074:

> "the question of whether we are in a simulation is below the union horizon, and we have no improved or derived tools that can ascertain the presence of traces of provenance of origin, and no like kind to distinguish it against."

> "'No trace exists' can never be confirmed by better instruments. A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach. You can only ever say nothing has been found yet."

> "The split between these two cases is not symmetrical: one case can close, while the other can only stay open."

This is the framework's Fig 6 :34-35 proto-instrument rule and Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" applied to a cosmos-level identity claim. It licenses exactly the audit's `unreached, not refuted` verdict and forbids upgrading silence to a "no". The essay also says: "The existence of provenance, or its traces, within a projection is what salvages the ability to measure it." That is what the pre-registered refuter in `01` §5 asks for.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming "computation" as *the* fundamental account turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 5. Why the audit's §F6 is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The world literature supplies a matching case. Copeland, Shagrir and Sprevak (2017) distinguish Zuse's literal computer from the merely computable universe; Lloyd (2013) offers "regarded as"; Floridi (2017) says any system can be presented as computational at some level. Several sources, one reading. Naming "computer" from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 6. Correction to my own §4 and §5 (narrowing "shape, not a name")

I first wrote the rule as "the framework cannot name computation". That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Computation is **not** missing: it is a composition already reachable from the six-term set (Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."). So:

- Naming a computational description is **not** forbidden. It is a binding from the physics or computation frame onto arrangements the six already span.
- What **is** forbidden is (a) calling that binding a derivation of a seventh primitive, (b) claiming it moves the closure wall when Fig 6 :25 says "An import adds no atom to the world", and (c) treating it as a terminal, self-certifying law (Fig 7 :6, Fig 8 :37).
- My §5 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held arrangements. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second narrowing: Fig 11 :41 says "The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not." I initially read this as "the question is meaningless". It is not. The contact question ("what generates the observed regularity, and can a discriminator be built?") is fully answerable in principle and is the surviving content.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that the universe computes is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." "However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
