## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about "whether the universe is a quantum computer and what that would mean for physical law". Option A: assert the literal identity and derive consequences for law. Option B: split the union, keep the checkable contact question, and refuse the parts that have no ground. What each leads to: A sends an identity claim with no discriminator, no level of abstraction and a terminal law, spending the independence of the people checking it; B sends a shape with separate per-domain verdicts, written falsifiers, and an open slot until an instrument fires.

R is stated in effect terms: an observation of law-governed, mathematically describable evolution is being used to name a source (computation), where several sources yield the same reading. Arity: three conditions. Symmetry: identity (`≡`) versus sequence (`→`), a bond type mismatch. Scale: first order inside physics, second order at the metaframe, where the Godel/Tarski/Chaitin barriers apply (Fig 8 :34, :37).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed does not admit a single yes/no verdict. It is a union of three conditions with separate grounds. The figures decide the shape: "computer" is a level-relative composition, not a primitive; the literal identity claim is unreached rather than refuted, because no instrument discriminates literal computation from mere computability; the unqualified "everything computes" version has no anchor and therefore no verdict; and a terminal "physical law is source code, finished" conclusion is refused as a last room. What survives is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. Which specific computational structure, if any, is fundamental is a declared `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the union split from Fig 9, with the substrate-dissolution move from Fig 11 as its partner.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a finished "the universe is a computer" story) instead of a generator (the shape plus its refuter). Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :41-43). The defective bindings (identity, programmer, final law) are removable without touching the derivation. `07` and `08` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the Planck scale returning a determinate answer would settle C1; an operational definition with a level and a failure condition would give the claim an anchor and reverse the no-anchor finding; a source-gated seventh primitive that moves the wall would reverse C1 of the packet; a complete self-validating law would break the no-last-room rule; or a derivation showing no computational description composes from the six would break the binding claim. Agreement from a shared evidence pool, or prestige, is not such an instrument.
