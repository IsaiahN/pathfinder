# Outreach draft — check one output before deciding whether the frame is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four domain verdicts in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 8 are read as `specified` and the inventory is read as `gap`. The offer collapses on contact if the shape is presented as a settled historical verdict.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how knowledge survives displacement

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** For any item of knowledge in a contact zone, preservation and dismissal are decided at that item's slot, not for a whole culture. Three conditions overlap there rather than partitioning it. In each, one thing has to hold:
>
> 1. **Where authority to certify is unequal:** the certifying reference must not update on the certifying institution's own standing. If it does, the record drifts coherently toward whoever has the most influence, and the institution's agreement with itself is not evidence that the knowledge still works.
> 2. **Where people move:** a habitat is substituted, not removed. Knowledge survives to the degree the receiving habitat reproduces what the knowledge's goal required. The two failures are silent: what you failed to reproduce, and what you unintentionally brought with you.
> 3. **Where a boundary is crossed:** what carries knowledge is a reapplicable method and a head start, not a recording of one occasion. A recorded result without its arrangement travels as a description and must be re-earned by each generation.
>
> Dismissal, on this reading, is a refusal of a held classification, not the mere absence of an item. A record that holds only ingredients and subtraction can say what is missing and cannot say that its own claim about the knowledge is false.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular practice or language was preserved or destroyed by this mechanism, that colonial contact produced no knowledge, that an archive's silence proves absence, or that a clean provenance makes a claim true. The framework returns a shape, not an inventory of names. Which forms survived at a named place is a `gap` here, and I am treating unreached as a verdict rather than filling it with a guess.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a knowledge item preserved or dismissed with the ground paying for the verdict while all three conditions are absent;
> 2. a certifying frame that updates on the powerful and still keeps a non-updating anchor reachable at that slot;
> 3. knowledge surviving a substituted habitat with none of its goal's conditions reproduced and no imported head start;
> 4. a recording crossing a boundary with its typed arrangement intact, requiring no re-derivation downstream;
> 5. a certifying institution whose reading is corrected by an anchor it cannot move, so its convention never became a verdict;
> 6. a source-discriminating reading at a named slot that settles the inventory against the shape.
>
> If any one holds, the claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures and formula are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula for direct download: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a case you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]

## Two-stage offer, stated plainly

**Stage 1.** Send the four-condition separator and the six refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure at a slot they know. Record their verdict *before* showing the framework.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figures and formula. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: if they have read the framework, they share the evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A method may cross; a recording of this particular answer may not (Fig 4 :23-25).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If any of the six refuters fires, stop and send the correction instead. Nothing here is sent by this agent; the user hosts and sends.
