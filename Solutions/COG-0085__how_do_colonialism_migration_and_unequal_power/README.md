## Question restated as an effect

The question asks what each of three conditions leads to at a knowledge slot. Under a coercive contact between frames, under a movement of people and language into a substituted habitat, and under an unequal distribution of the authority to certify, some items of knowledge keep a working contact with what they are about, while others become inert inscriptions or leave the chain of transmission altogether. An answer would name the mechanism that distinguishes those two outcomes per slot, and it would state what instrument would show the distinction had been drawn correctly. The term that binds the sought-for shape is "a channel to a ground that does not update on the certifying frame, plus a method rather than a recording, in a habitat whose required conditions were reproduced." That term is written down here so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other (Formula, Direction).
