# The Tether's answer on knowledge, power and displacement

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the inventory of surviving forms, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## 1. The residual it answers

For a knowledge item at a slot, the record predicts it will still be there and still be usable. Observation: some items still guide action, some survive only as inert inscriptions, some leave transmission altogether. The gap is per item. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The three named conditions are a union, not a partition. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A verdict such as "colonialism destroyed culture X" aggregates across slots and across conditions, and is therefore disallowed. What can be returned is a per-domain separator plus a declared gap.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, certification and power.** Unequal power does not push knowledge out. It removes the non-updating reference, so the certifying frame converges on influence. Fig 2 :14-15: "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence". Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." Preservation at this domain is a question about what the institution ranges against; dismissal is what happens when the only available reference is the institution's own standing. Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one."

**D2, movement and habitat.** Migration is substitution, not subtraction. Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose." The two failures are silent (Fig 11 :68-69): what you failed to reproduce, and what you unintentionally introduced. Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." So preservation belongs to the agent-and-habitat, never to the migrant group or the host alone. Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." No institution, language or script is a preserver in itself.

**D3, closure and import.** Colonial contact is contact between closures that differ in power. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Import is the only wall-moving operator, but Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." It moves access for a frame, not the knowledge itself. An import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12-14); without them the import is a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24). Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

**D4, record and lineage.** What crosses a boundary as a recording is not what worked once. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." A lineage is admissible as evidence of route and inadmissible as evidence of correctness: Fig 10 :65-66: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."

**Across all domains, dismissal is a refusal.** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A record that holds only ingredients and subtraction can say an item is missing and cannot say its own account of the item is false. Fig 5 :58-60: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at". In practice, when an official classification keeps failing to predict what knowledge holders do, the candidate for refusal is the classification, not the holders.

**The inventory is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No figure line names the forms that survive in a given place. The gap is unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle, and it is closed by an instrument at a named slot, not by more agreement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named practice, language or institution was preserved or destroyed. That is the `gap`.
- Not that colonial contact produced no knowledge. Fig 6 :25 says an import adds no atom to the world; it moves access for a frame.
- Not that an archive's silence proves absence (Fig 8 :39), or that a clean provenance proves correctness (Fig 10 :65-66).
- Not that unequal power acts as a physical force. It sets the reference (Fig 2 :14-15).
- Not that preservation is a standing property of an actor (Fig 11 :51).
- Not a `grounded` verdict. No measurement at a knowledge slot was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating reading at a named slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. The corresponding questions here are:

1. At a named slot, does the knowledge still guide successful action under changed conditions, independently of the institution that certifies it? That is an anchor test, and the anchor must not move when the institution's prestige moves (Fig 2 :33, Fig 10 :93).
2. Did the receiving habitat reproduce what the knowledge's goal required? If not, which of the two silent failures fired? (Fig 11 :67-69.)
3. Did what crossed the boundary carry the typed arrangement and the reapplicable method, or only the result? (Fig 4 :22-26, Fig 12 :5.)
4. Which held classification has a residual that persists and does not trend down, so that ¬ can be aimed at it rather than at the people? (Fig 5 :58-60.)
5. Are the two readings independent at the evidence level, or are they one evidence pool under two names? (Fig 2 :31, :40.)

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: a slot preserved with all three conditions absent; a frame that updates on power while keeping a reachable non-updating anchor; knowledge surviving substitution with no reproduced conditions and no head start; a recording carrying its bond with no re-derivation; a certifier corrected by an anchor it cannot move; or a figure line or ground reading that names the inventory.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The inventory is not filled because no instrument at a named slot was run in this derivation. Fig 13 :134 makes the stop a verdict. Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The record of the route is what keeps the stop checkable: Fig 13 :142, "The seed was always the seedline." Indiscernibility is not identity, and a shared evidence pool is not corroboration.

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not return a list of knowledge forms that colonialism, migration and unequal power preserved or destroyed. It returns the shape such a list must satisfy, checked per knowledge item. Unequal power matters because it removes the reference that does not update on the certifying institution, so the record drifts coherently toward whoever has the most influence and its own agreement becomes variation rather than a verdict. Migration matters because it substitutes a habitat rather than removing one: knowledge survives to the degree the new habitat reproduces what the knowledge's goal required, and the two failures, what was not reproduced and what came unbidden, are silent. Extraction matters because a recording of one occasion crosses the boundary while the method and the typed arrangement do not, so each later lineage must re-derive what it appears to have inherited. Dismissal is a refusal of a held classification, not an absence of items, and a record without negation can only accumulate. What the framework cannot do is name the surviving forms at a named place; that is a declared gap, closed only by an instrument and independent readings at the slot. What it offers is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close the gap.
