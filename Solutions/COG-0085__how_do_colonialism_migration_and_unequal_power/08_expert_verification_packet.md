# Expert verification packet — the Tether on knowledge, power and displacement

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in postcolonial or cultural psychology can check. Mode, derivation and refuters come before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the inventory of surviving forms, not `grounded`.** No measurement at a named knowledge slot is claimed.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Knowledge preservation and dismissal are decided per knowledge item, not for a whole culture. Three conditions form a union rather than a partition. Where authority to certify is unequal, the certifying reference stops being independent and converges on influence, so the institution's consensus is variation rather than a verdict. Where people move, the habitat is substituted rather than removed, and what survives depends on whether the receiving conditions the knowledge's goal required were reproduced; the two failures are silent. Where a record crosses a boundary, a method and a head start cross, not a recording of one occasion, and without a typed arrangement the result must be re-derived by each generation. Dismissal is a refusal of a held classification, which a record without negation cannot carry. Which specific forms survived at a named place is a declared `gap`, not an omission.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — certification domain; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7 and 10

- **Statement:** unequal power enters by removing a non-updating reference, so the record converges on whoever has the most influence; agreement inside such a record is not evidence that the knowledge still works.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :14-15: "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence". Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
- **What to check:** at a case you know, did the certifying institution's reading of what counts as knowledge change with its own standing, or was it corrected by something it could not move? Is the "consensus" in the archive several independent readings or one evidence pool under several names (Fig 2 :40)?
- **Refuter:** show a frame that updates on the powerful and still keeps a non-updating anchor reachable at that slot, with independent readings that would detect divergence; or show the convergence target tracking ground contact rather than influence under a demonstrable power asymmetry.

### C2 — movement and record domains; checkable by reading Figures 11, 4 and 12

- **Statement:** migration substitutes a habitat; preservation is reproduction of the conditions the knowledge's goal required, and what crosses a boundary is a method rather than a recording.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68-69: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- **What to check:** in a displacement case you know, which of the two silent failures occurred? Did the list of preserved items travel with the arrangement that made them work, or only the result? Does a later lineage still have to re-derive what it appears to have inherited (Fig 12 :107-110)?
- **Refuter:** show knowledge surviving a substituted habitat with none of its goal's conditions reproduced and no imported head start; or show a recording crossing a boundary with its typed arrangement intact and no re-derivation downstream.

### C3 — dismissal and inventory; checkable by reading Figure 5, the Operators table, Figures 9 and 13

- **Statement:** dismissal is a negation of a held classification, not an absence of items, and the inventory of surviving forms is a `gap` that only a ground reading closes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."
- **What to check:** can the archive you know say that its own classification of a knowledge form is false, or only that the form is absent or was removed? Is the absence of an inventory here a consequence of Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134, or a failure of search that a named instrument could repair?
- **Refuter:** produce a verbatim figure line that names a surviving form under all three conditions, or a ground reading at a named slot that settles the inventory. Show a record that carries a genuine negation while remaining a convention that decides no outcome.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named language, practice or institution was preserved or destroyed. That is the `gap`.
- Not that colonial contact produced no knowledge; an import adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25).
- Not that an archive's silence proves absence (Fig 8 :39), or that a clean provenance proves correctness (Fig 10 :65-66).
- Not that unequal power is a physical push, or that any standing actor is a preserver (Fig 11 :51).
- Not that the framework licenses a cultural verdict, a policy allocation or a percentage.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement at a slot.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating reading at a named slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here:

1. Does the knowledge still guide successful action under changed conditions, independently of the certifying institution? Anchor test (Fig 2 :33, Fig 10 :93).
2. Did the receiving habitat reproduce what the goal required, and which silent failure fired? (Fig 11 :67-69.)
3. Did what crossed carry the typed arrangement and the reapplicable method, or only the result? (Fig 4 :22-26, Fig 12 :5.)
4. Which held classification has a residual that persists and does not trend down, so ¬ can be aimed at it? (Fig 5 :58-60.)
5. Are the readings independent at the evidence level, or one pool under two names? (Fig 2 :40.)

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :14-15, :34 and :40, Fig 7 :35 and Fig 10 :93. Decide whether C1 describes a case you know, and name what the institution ranged against.
2. Read Fig 11 :66-69 and Fig 4 :25. Pick one displacement case and say which silent failure is visible and whether a method or a recording crossed.
3. Read Fig 5 :53 and Operators :58. Look at one archive record and say whether it can refuse its own classification.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish preservation from inert survival at that slot, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and later agreement after reading this packet is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
