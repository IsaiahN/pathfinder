## 2. Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The epistemicide frame.** Santos's account predicts that dismissal is an active production of absence, not a passive failure to copy. That corroborates F5 (dismissal is a refusal) and F1 (inventory verdicts must be per slot). It shares a pool with Mignolo and the Ngugi piece and counts as one witness with them.
2. **Language as the carrier.** Ngugi's line, reported by Mukoma wa Ngugi, predicts that severing the medium severs the arrangement, not merely the labels. It corroborates F4. The 2017 classroom observation that vernacular speaking was prohibited is a first-order contact reading of a convention deciding an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
3. **Who controls the frame.** Mignolo's coloniality thesis predicts that the authority to define reason is itself the mechanism of loss. It corroborates F2 and F6. His polycentric claim also gives a low-overlap analogue to the framework's "no last room": no single centre should tell every field what counts.
4. **Migration as loss of conditions.** Bhugra and Becker predict that relocation removes language, values, social structures and support networks, and that the host's diagnostic frame can misread the resulting distress. That corroborates F3 and F6 at the clinical slot.
5. **Perceived macro support.** Wang 2026 predicts that the *perceived* policy environment, not the policy text, is the strongest statistical correlate of transmission intention. Corroborates the D1 claim that the frame's reference governs what it preserves, with the limit that an intention is a frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :24-27). A filter, not a witness.
6. **Memory without containers.** Coman et al. 2009 predict that memories travel and are multidirectional and that nations and ethnicities are often not the containers of collective memory. That corroborates F3 against a container model of culture.
7. **Psychology's own sample problem.** Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan 2010 predict that a discipline can mistake a local sample for the species. Corroborates F2 and F6: the certifying frame's reference can be its own population. Low overlap, genuinely independent of the postcolonial theory pool.
8. **Culture as a survival-relevant transmission system.** The CMS material predicts that cultural knowledge is lost when habitats fragment and knowledge-holders disappear, before demographic collapse is visible. That corroborates F3 and Fig 11's two silent failures in a non-human field, with no shared evidence pool with the social sources.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule (return to the census, never flip the verdict) was not triggered. One source was narrowed rather than rejected: Wang 2026 measures stated intention, not settlement at the ground.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four-condition separator and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see at a named slot before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement.
- **Fig 10 :59** declare the stake. The author's interest in the framework's standing is structural and is declared in `01` and `08`.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a slot they know; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if they opt in, the three links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

Stage 1 is the residual and refuter first. Stage 2 is opt-in sources. Stage 3 offers the loop as a reusable method with the instruction that each user write a fresh refuter and state a mode. A method may cross a boundary; a recording of this answer may not (Fig 4 :23-25).

**Hard precondition:** this agent hosts nothing and sends nothing. The user hosts and sends, one to one, through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. SAGE's authorship and AI-disclosure policy is recorded in that CSV and must be read before any journal submission, because a simulated derivation cannot be presented as empirical work.
