## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks for a mechanism, not a list. For a particular knowledge item `k` at a particular slot (a language, a practice, a diagnostic rule, a migration route, a crop calendar), the record predicts that `k` will still be there and still be usable. The observation is that some items still guide action and some survive only as inert inscriptions, or vanish from the chain of transmission. The residual `R` is the gap at that slot.

Arity: at least three conditions in play (coercive contact, movement into a changed habitat, unequal authority to certify), and they overlap. This is a **union compound**, not three independent causes to be averaged. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

Symmetry: preservation and dismissal are not mirror processes. Preservation is continued settlement against what the knowledge is about. Dismissal is a refusal of a claim, which is not the same as the absence of a term. Operators :73-74: "A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate." A record that can only accumulate cannot carry the correction that a held term is false.

Scale: first order inside a knowledge community, where a term is used and settles against a ground; second order when a record about the knowledge crosses an institutional boundary (archive, canon, curriculum, database). The two orders have different anchors and must not be averaged.

Per-slot indexing is mandatory. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A verdict of the form "colonialism destroyed culture X" is exactly the aggregation the figure disallows. Nothing in this folder returns such a verdict.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term).** A shape: knowledge is preserved where a channel to a ground that does not update on the certifying frame stays reachable at its slot, where what crosses a boundary is a reapplicable method and a head start rather than a recording of one occasion, where the receiving habitat reproduces the conditions the knowledge's goal required, and where the record can carry a refusal. Dismissal is what remains when one of those conditions is absent. This term is written down before the run so it can be refused by the same test as everything else (Formula, Direction; Fig 5 :62-65).

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union, and the verdict is per knowledge slot

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The three named conditions do not partition the world into colonised, migrated and unequal-power cases. A displaced speaker in a former colony is in all three at once. The shape therefore returns a separator, not an inventory: for each slot, which condition is actually binding, and which anchor is reachable there.

**Refuter.** Produce a case in which a knowledge item is preserved or dismissed at its slot with the ground paying for the verdict while all three conditions are absent, or show that the three conditions are separable into disjoint populations with no overlap. Either breaks the union claim.

**Mode: `specified`. Single-source for the per-slot rule (Fig 1) and triangulated for the union rule (Fig 1 + Fig 9, different figures and different objects).**

### F2 — Unequal power is a missing anchor, so the record converges on whoever had the most influence

Fig 2 :13-15: "no anchor; each updates on the others" and "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence". Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

When the institution that certifies knowledge takes its reference from the same field it certifies, the two become a mirror chain. The result is not a conspiracy and does not require anyone to intend it. The drift is coherent precisely because it looks like agreement. Unequal power enters the mechanism here: it decides whose update counts, so the convergence target tracks influence rather than what the knowledge is about.

Two further figure lines close the domain. Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." A canon whose authority is its own prestige is a room chain with no ground vote. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." This governs the interpretation of any consensus about which knowledge is valuable: if the agreeing institutions read the same canon, that agreement is one pool talking.

**Refuter.** Show a frame whose reference updates on the powerful frame while a non-updating anchor remains reachable at the slot, with independent readings that would detect the divergence. That breaks the missing-anchor reading. Or show the convergence target tracking ground contact rather than influence in a contact zone with demonstrable power asymmetry.

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 10 (low overlap: ground geometry, room chain, seat rules).**

### F3 — Migration is habitat substitution, and the loss is failure to reproduce the conditions

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose." Fig 11 :68-69: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Migration does not subtract a culture. It substitutes a habitat and inherits actors no one chose. The two failure modes are silent: what was not reproduced, and what came along unbidden. Preservation is therefore not a property of the migrant group or of the host institution. It is a property of the agent-and-habitat at that slot. This is the figure's capability claim applied to a knowledge item rather than to a person.

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A language, a ritual or an institution is not a preserver or destroyer in itself. Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." The school that preserves a script in one slot can erase an oral practice in another. Verdicts stay per slot.

**Refuter.** Show knowledge surviving a substituted habitat with none of its goal's required conditions reproduced and no imported head start from another frame. Or name a standing actor (a language, an institution) that preserves knowledge across residuals without relation to a specified residual. Either breaks F3.

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated across Fig 11 (habitat), Fig 4 (what crosses) and Fig 12 (what carries the route).**

### F4 — Extraction moves a recording, not a method

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 4 :26: "Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."

A colonial archive, a specimen catalogue or a transcribed oral text can carry the artifact and drop the arrangement that made it work. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." With the bond gone, a later reader sees ingredients and cannot tell which knowledge it is.

Fig 12 :107-110: "Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again." A community whose knowledge survives only as a result, not as a derivation, must re-earn the understanding at each generation. That is the mechanism behind the observation that a practice can be listed in a curriculum and still be lost.

Fig 4 :34-35: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." Importing a method into a different habitat does not replay it. The receiving conditions decide what it becomes, which is why the imported form and the original can diverge without either being corrupted.

**Refuter.** Show a recording crossing a boundary that carries the typed bond and the reapplicable method with it, with no head start supplied and no re-derivation required downstream. Or show a lineage that re-derives the arrangement from ingredients alone where the arrangement was never recorded. Either breaks F4.

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 4 membrane + Fig 12 bond).**

### F5 — Dismissal is a refusal, and a record without negation can only accumulate

Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." The fifth kind of gap sorts by what is wrong, not by what is missing; the other four cannot reach it. Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Operators :73-74: "Subtraction is the nearest bond and is not it." A record can say that a practice is absent from the canon; it cannot say that the canon's own claim about the practice is false unless it has ¬.

Fig 5 :58-60: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at, which is the same shrinking test the selector already runs, read with the opposite sign." The practical consequence is important for this question. If an official classification of a knowledge form keeps failing to predict what its holders do, and the residual does not shrink with more cataloguing, the candidate for refusal is the classification, not the knowledge. A framework that points the refusal at the people rather than at its own held term has aimed the negation at the outcome it did not want.

Fig 5 :62-65: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it." This is why a search for "the knowledge that was lost" can run without end: the sought shape is not a term in the library. The remedy is the discipline already applied in section 0, writing the shape down as an ordinary term.

**Refuter.** Show an official record that refuses a held classification (a genuine ¬, not an absence or a subtraction) while remaining a convention that decides no outcome. Or show that the persistent-flat residual test selects the knowledge rather than the classification when both are held. Either breaks F5.

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 5 fifth kind + Operators table).**

### F6 — Legitimacy is a declared meaning, not a grounded one

Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A canon, a language policy or a credentialling rule is a convention for coordinating meaning. When it decides which knowledge counts at a slot, it has become a verdict, and a verdict needs a ground that it cannot move.

Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged." Fig 10 :61: "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." The institution's prestige, accumulated on earlier verdicts, is a stake that survives those verdicts and quietly weights the next one. This is the exact structure *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* names as prestige standing in for proof: prestige can fund an action, it cannot settle one.

Fig 10 :72: "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." A curriculum designed for an earlier population that remains fixed after migration is a room that stayed fixed while the question moved.

**Refuter.** Show a certifying institution that decides knowledge outcomes at a slot while its reading is corrected by an anchor that does not move when the institution's prestige changes, with the correction recorded. That would mean the convention had not become a verdict, and F6 would be narrowed.

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 10 seat rules + Fig 2 anchor + Fig 7 room selection).**

### F7 — Provenance is the whole issue when two records hold the same contents

Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Two communities can arrive at the same diagnostic rule separately, or one can take it from the other. The artefact cannot tell you which. The lineage does.

Fig 10 :62: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom." Fig 10 :65-66: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record." Fig 13 :126: "WHY THE LINEAGE IS THE PROOF." Fig 13 :129: "It is recoverable from the lineage." Fig 13 :142: "The seed was always the seedline."

For colonial knowledge histories this is decisive and also dangerous. A perfectly provenanced catalogue of an extracted practice is an unimpeachable record of an extraction, not evidence that the practice was preserved. Conversely, a practice with no written provenance is not therefore unfounded; its lineage may live in oral transmission that the archive cannot read. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." The world did not gain or lose the knowledge when a record crossed; one frame gained access and another lost its copy.

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :12-14: import carries a "source gate: provenance" and a "shadow test: explains a gap". Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." An extraction that imports the labelled item without the shadow test imports a wrong atom at a cost above starting from the holders' own account.

**Refuter.** Produce two records with identical contents whose origins are provably the same while their lineages differ, or show a provenanced import that adds an atom to the world rather than access for a frame. Either breaks F7.

**Mode: `specified`. Single-source for the closure law (Fig 6), triangulated for the provenance requirement (Fig 6 + Fig 8 + Fig 10 + Fig 13).**

### F8 — The framework returns a shape, not a name: the identity of the surviving forms is a `gap`

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

No figure line names a form of knowledge and says it survives colonialism, migration and unequal power in all cases. Fig 11 :51 refuses the standing list. What the figures can return is the shape stated in section 0 and the per-domain verdicts below. The specific inventory in a particular place is owed to a ground: archival work, application tests, independent readers, and a source-discriminating instrument where the records conflict. Until that work is done, the honest word is `gap`, of the unreached-at-this-budget kind rather than the underivable-in-principle kind (Fig 9 :40-43). This is a verdict, not a silence.

**Refuter.** Produce a verbatim figure line that names a specific knowledge form preserved or dismissed under all three conditions, or a ground reading at a named slot that settles the inventory. The first would close the gap from inside the framework; the second would close it at the ground. Until one appears the gap stands.

**Mode: `general` on the limit, `gap` on the inventory. Triangulated (Fig 9 + Fig 13 + Fig 8).**

### F9 — Independence is the scarce resource, and self-reported agreement is not a witness

Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."

Most of the literature reviewed here is produced inside the same academic evidence pool, and three of the world sources (Santos, Mignolo, the Ngugi piece) share an anti-colonial theoretical pool. They count as one witness, not three. The genuinely lower-overlap witnesses are the cultural-psychiatry paper, the animal-culture conservation material, the collective-memory work and the cross-cultural psychology of science. Even these corroborate the *mechanism shape*, not the historical outcome at a named slot. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A strong effect size in a language-policy survey is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what was preserved.

**Refuter.** Show two putatively independent witnesses drawing on demonstrably separate evidence pools who range against the ground at the same named slot and disagree in a way the ground then settles. That would be a real triangulation, and it would either strengthen or break the per-domain verdicts; report which.

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 2 + Fig 8 + Fig 7).**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A knowledge item is preserved or dismissed at its slot with the ground paying for that verdict while all three domain conditions are absent, breaking F1.
2. A frame whose reference demonstrably updates on the powerful frame is shown to keep a non-updating anchor reachable at that slot, breaking F2.
3. Knowledge survives a substituted habitat with none of its goal's required conditions reproduced and no imported head start from a differing closure, breaking F3.
4. A recording crosses a boundary carrying the typed bond and the reapplicable method with it, with no re-derivation downstream, breaking F4.
5. A certifying institution is shown to decide knowledge outcomes while its reading is corrected by an anchor it cannot move, narrowing F6.
6. A verbatim figure line names a specific knowledge form preserved or dismissed under all three conditions, closing F8 from inside the framework.
7. A ground reading at a named slot settles the inventory against the shape, closing F8 at the ground.

Condition 6 and 7 are not symmetric: 6 would mean this audit misread the figures, 7 would mean the gap has been filled by an instrument. Both are recorded as failures of the present claim, not as adjustments to it. Until one fires, no part of this folder may be labelled `grounded`. The discipline requirement is from Fig 5 :65: "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the per-slot residual and the union reading; drop any culture-wide "destroyed" or "preserved" verdict (F1).
2. Ask, for each knowledge item, what the certifying frame ranges against. If the answer is its own prestige, the frame is a mirror and its consensus is not evidence (F2, F6, F9).
3. Ask what conditions the knowledge's goal required, and whether the receiving habitat reproduced them. Treat the two silent failures as the things to look for, not as metaphors (F3).
4. Distinguish method from recording at the boundary. A listed item is not a carried term (F4, F7).
5. Write the refused term explicitly. Ask which held classification has a residual that persists and does not trend down (F5).
6. Declare `gap` for the inventory and name the instrument that would close it (F8).
7. Offer the two-stage packet in `02_outreach_email.md` through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask what would refute the shape, never for agreement.

Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not a conclusion.

## 7. Strains and re-census

- **S1, Fig 13 physical substrate versus social knowledge terms.** Initial reading forced knowledge types onto the six-term set. Re-census assigns Fig 13 to the physical substrate and Fig 6/8 to library contents inside any frame. Different objects, no contradiction. Kept visible.
- **S2, Fig 11 valence-relative actors versus Fig 2 non-updating anchor.** A school can be a preserver (D2) and a mirror (D1) at the same time. Re-census: Fig 11 governs valence relative to a residual; Fig 2 governs whether the reference can be moved. Two different quantities, both live. Kept as a named tension, not hidden.
- **S3, Fig 12 isomer claim.** Two knowledge systems over a fixed set of items with different bonds would be Fig 12 :117 isomers. The fixed-set condition is a live strain: migration adds and drops actors, so the atom set is usually not fixed. `06_isomers_and_order.md` states the falsifier. Named, not smoothed.
- **S4, self-reported intention versus settlement.** The Wang 2026 result is a strong predictor of *intention* (a frame-internal quantity). Fig 1 :24-27 disqualifies quantities a frame produces as a score for itself. The source is used as evidence about which lever a policy frame perceives, not as evidence that knowledge was preserved. Re-census narrows its weight; no conflict with the figure verdict.
- **S5, the archive as record versus as authority.** Fig 10 :62 gives a ruling provenance; Fig 10 :93 disqualifies a keeper with a reputation. Re-census: the archive is admissible as a record of a route and inadmissible as a self-certifying ground. Kept as a live design constraint.

**After re-census: five strains, all named, none hidden; one gap (F8).**
