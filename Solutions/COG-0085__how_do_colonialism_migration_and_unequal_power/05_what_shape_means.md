## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."

A shape here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the surviving items. For this question the shape is the four-condition separator: per-slot indexing, a non-updating reference, a reproduced habitat, and a carried method with a refusal operator. It constrains any answer about knowledge survival; it does not fill the historical inventory. The bound ordinary term in `README.md` is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring terms that overlap with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: the field's existing terms are "archive", "canon", "habitual knowledge", "transmission". The recurring neighbours that narrow the search are "anchor", "method versus recording", "habitat substitution", "bond", "negation". Each one cuts the space.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect is: the knowledge still guides action at its slot, or it does not.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (mapping carried from the exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A shape must be checkable by someone who does not hold the framework. That is why the outreach packet leads with refuters.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question the stop is not the completed inventory. It is the point at which further source-reading only returns the same four conditions under new names, which is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." At a knowledge slot the ground would be an application test, an independent reading, or an instrument that distinguishes sources that a single record conflates.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test. A reported practice in an archive is a candidate import. Until its route and its gap are recorded, it is a labelled item, not a settled term.

*Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The spectrometer resolved the green reading not by argument but because "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Applied here: an archive's reading of a practice cannot comment on its source. Several lineages could produce the same inscription. Naming the source from the inscription is the metamerism error, and what is owed is a discriminator.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Declaring which knowledge counts does not make the canon neutral; it makes the choice visible.

## 4. Why the inventory is a `gap` rather than an omission

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A practice that preserves knowledge in one settlement can be a marker of exclusion in another. Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing, not the answer. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

So the honest statement is: the figures license the shape and the per-domain verdicts; they do not license a list of surviving knowledge forms, and the absence of such a list in this folder is a recorded `gap`, not a failure to find something that might be there. What would close it is named in `01` section 4, condition 7.

## 5. The question this section answers

**What licenses the claim that a particular form of knowledge was preserved?** Nothing in the framework alone. The form must be bound by the ground at its slot, or imported with a provenanced route and a shadow test, and the binding must be recorded as the searcher's contribution. A clean archive trail shows the ruling was legitimate (Fig 10 :65-66), never that the knowledge survived.

**Does the literature reviewed here commit the metamerism error?** Some of it risks it. A survey that measures stated transmission intention reads an artifact of a frame and names a cause (policy perception) without an instrument that discriminates among the several sources that could produce the same intention. `01` S4 narrows its weight accordingly. This is not a retraction of the source; it is a record of the boundary of what it can witness.

## 6. Correction to my own §3: "shape, not a name" is narrower than I first wrote

I initially treated the limit as a ban on naming any knowledge form. The canonical wording is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 13 :135 gives the reason: you cannot name a missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it. Knowledge forms in a frame's library are not missing primitives. Naming a form that is already held is a binding from that frame, to be provenanced, not an invented seventh. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves a wall (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world."), or inventing a new kind where only arrangements are available (Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."). Both the earlier and the corrected readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A canon's authority gives a knowledge claim a budget to be tried. It cannot settle the claim. The prestige loop "looks like agreement, and agreement feels like truth", which is Fig 2 :40 and Fig 7 :35 in the essay's own vocabulary. This is why the outreach material asks for a refutation and never for endorsement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"; "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." From *How Intelligence Actually Works* (carried from the exemplar): outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim, it cannot settle anything it reports on, and it must be labelled as such wherever it travels.
