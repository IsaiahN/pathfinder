# The Tether's answer on restoring connectivity

**Mode: `specified` for the substantive claims, `general` for the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A manager adds a link to a fragmented habitat. The sentence asks for two things at once: target movement, gene flow and persistence restored, and established local communities left intact. These are two slot residuals on one habitat, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Their intersection is categorically different from either condition: Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

`R` is indexed per slot, never averaged: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single landscape connectivity index cannot decide this question.

## 2. The shape

1. **The habitat comes first, and it is enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Actors, conditions and relations are read off the landscape; what instruments cannot see is part of `R`.

2. **Restoration is introduction, and the power is asymmetric.** Fig 11 :59 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."* Fig 11 :63 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* So this is not the undoing of a subtraction. The manager adds a connector to a habitat they did not author, and cannot remove the residents to make room. Fig 11 :61 *"adds to a habitat it still did not author."*

3. **The link's sign is relative to each residual.** Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The corridor is benefactor to the target, and can be malfactor to the residents. Both descriptions are true under different residuals.

4. **The decisive effect can be several steps out.** Fig 11 :52 *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* First order is target movement; second order is pollination, predation and competition; third order is the pathogen, invader or prey decline that decides non-disruption. Counting target crossings is a reading of the wrong link if the question is about the residents.

5. **This is permanent work, settled only at the ground.** Fig 11 :60 *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 11 :70 *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."* So "reconnected without disruption" is not a state to be achieved once and banked. It is a maintained channel with detection, reversal and a lineage.

6. **"Without disruption" must be bound before it can be checked.** Fig 5 :62 *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."* Unbound, it predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so no check in the loop can see it. Bound as a slot residual with a threshold, it becomes refusable.

7. **Which connector, for which species, at which site stays a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures can characterise the missing thing in effect terms, a site-specific instrument that discriminates target movement from antagonist spread; they cannot name it. That is a verdict, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that corridors are always harmful. The 2014 review found negative mean effect sizes indistinguishable from zero, and the figure rule is that valence is relative to the residual, not carried.
- Not that corridors are always benign. The 2025 comment says costs are under-measured, and the Elwha case shows one link delivering both fish recovery and invasive plant spread.
- Not a species, a width, a location or a monitoring threshold. Those are imports, provenanced as such, or gaps.
- Not a single "connectivity score". Per-slot residuals only.
- Not grounded. No measurement at a named site settles the binding in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at paired sites, not another argument. The questions the world must answer:

- Does the rate of target movement change together with, or independently of, resident community structure and pathogen or invader detection at the same sites? If they always move together, the union collapses.
- What is the second- and third-order cascade: which predator, competitor, pathogen or fire regime changes, and at what distance from the link? A first-only answer would refute the cascade claim.
- What threshold would trigger reversal, and was it stated before the link was built? A goal is a comparison; a comparison without a pre-registered threshold is a preference.
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5: union refuted, exclusion demonstrated, invariant valence, first-order-only cascades, once-and-for-all settlement, or a figure line that selects a connector for a named species.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The facts we cannot yet distinguish are not absent; they are below the current instrument horizon. Fig 6 :35 *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* The answer stops where occupancy counts, genetic measures and pathogen detection already register something that fails to resolve the intersection. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

The one live strain is the alternate reading of "communities" as human communities. Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* If people are the subject, human judgment becomes the right anchor and D5 must be given its own verdict. It is kept separate, not averaged into the ecological reading.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that corridors are good or bad, and it does not name a species, a width or a site. It says this is a union of two conditions on one habitat, so reconnection and non-disruption must be measured separately. It says restoring connectivity is an introduction into a habitat nobody authored: you can add a link, you cannot subtract the residents, and isolation was already a substitution rather than a removal. It says the same link is a benefactor to the target and a possible malfactor to the residents, because valence is relative to the residual, and the effect that decides the outcome can be two or three steps out. It says a managed ecosystem is permanent work, not a solved state, and that only a measurement at a named site settles the claim. What it offers is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and a declared gap where the missing instrument should be.
