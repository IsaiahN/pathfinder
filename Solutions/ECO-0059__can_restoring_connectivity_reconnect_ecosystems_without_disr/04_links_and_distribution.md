## What the wider literature adds, and what it does not

1. **It corroborates the first condition strongly.** Two independent study lines (large-scale experiments and a 78-experiment meta-analysis) support increased movement. They share authors and evidence pools, so they are one witness about movement, not two.
2. **It does not settle the second condition.** The 2014 review found mean negative effect sizes indistinguishable from zero, and the 2025 comment says the costs are under-measured. This is an instrument gap, not a verdict either way. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
3. **The disagreement is the union, not a contradiction to be averaged.** The same link is measured by movement instruments in one pool and by pathogen and invader instruments in another. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*, and conversely low-overlap instruments can disagree because they range against different residuals.
4. **The Elwha example is the cleanest case.** Dam removal revived native fish and increased invasive plants through the same new connection. That is Fig 11 :50, not a failed corridor: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*

## Two-stage offer, restated

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement. No theory attached.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule and the formula. Their agreement after this point is not evidence (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the loop as a reusable method. Each user writes their own refuter and states `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so the discipline is external to the loop.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives a twenty-minute check. `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the file to send.
