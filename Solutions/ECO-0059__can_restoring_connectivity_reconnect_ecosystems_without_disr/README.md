## Restating the question as an effect

A manager (a seat) adds a connector to a fragmented habitat. Option A: the connector restores movement for the target biota and the established local communities remain intact. Option B: the connector restores movement but the same link also carries antagonists, pathogens, competitors or disturbance into the resident community, so the residual is displaced rather than closed. An answer would look like: a stated connector, a stated target residual, a stated resident residual, the second- and third-order cascade each one predicts, and a measurement that would show the claim false. Bind that shape as an ordinary term before the run, so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Fig 5 :62, `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

## What the figures decide, in one line

This is a **union**, not one hard question: the reconnection residual and the non-disruption residual are two conditions on one habitat, and a single lever clears the region where only one binds while leaving the intersection standing (Fig 9 :54, :55, :57). Restoration is an **introduction** into a habitat the manager did not author; introduction is available, exclusion is not (Fig 11 :57, :59, :60). The same connector is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :49, :50). So "reconnect without disrupting" is not a state that can be settled once and banked: *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* (Fig 11 :60).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Send the residual, not the interiors.** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
3. **Declare the stake.** *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."* (Fig 10 :60). Anyone who manages a corridor has an interest in the outcome; only the ground settles the reading.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
