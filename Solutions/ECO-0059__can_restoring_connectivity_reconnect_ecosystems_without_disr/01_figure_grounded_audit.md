## 0. The question under audit

> Can restoring connectivity reconnect ecosystems without disrupting established local communities?

Read as an effect: a seat adds a link to a habitat. Two residuals are named in one sentence. One wants movement, gene flow and persistence restored for the target biota. The other wants the resident community and its relations left intact. The two conditions share the same habitat and the same actors, so this is a **union compound**, not one hard question. It needs its own loop and its own ground, and it must be unbundled before any frame is chosen (Fig 9 :54).

**Sought-for shape, bound before the run.** An answer would look like: a named connector, a named target residual per slot, a named resident residual per slot, the second- and third-order cascade each predicts, and a measurement that would fire a refuter. This is written down now so the preference can accrue a residual like any other term: Fig 5 :62 *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."*; Fig 5 :65 *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."*

## 1. Residual in effect terms

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot `s` (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 1; Symbols table).

- **Slots.** Target species/patch occupancy and gene flow; resident species abundance and interaction structure; pathogen and invader detection; human stakeholders where "communities" is read as people. No slot is averaged into a single landscape score.
- **Arity.** Two conditions on one habitat. The connector is a binary relation between patches; its effects are indexed by the object each slot tracks.
- **Symmetry.** The link itself is spatially symmetric: what carries a target species outward also carries an antagonist inward. The seat's power over it is **asymmetric**: introduction is available, exclusion is not (Fig 11 :59). The relation is not a symmetric join of two independent goods.
- **Scale.** First order: movement between two patches. Second order: changes in predation, competition, pollination and seed dispersal. Third order: the residual that matters can be several steps out, as in the DDT cascade (Fig 11 :52).
- **Anchor.** A demographic or genetic measurement at a named site, which does not move because a manager or a reviewer thinks the corridor worked (Fig 2 :29). If "communities" is read as human communities, Fig 2 :36 makes human judgment the right anchor; that alternate reading is kept separate in the census and is not silently merged with the ecological one.

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A landscape-level "connectivity improved by 50 percent" is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

**Partition or union: union.** D1 and D2 overlap because the same species can be a target in one slot and an antagonist in another (Fig 11 :50). A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing, because the second condition still binds (Fig 9 :55). The compound is categorically different from either condition: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* (Fig 9 :57).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Enumerate the habitat (perception) → have terms for what a connector affords (vocabulary) → state what counts as reconnection and what counts as disruption (objective) → choose a connector (planning) → read the ground and carry the result (learn). The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance, and effort spent above a break arrives at a step that cannot use it. The middle link, terms for what a situation offers and what would count as progress, is the one with the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :25, :27): *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**Strain test applied during census.** Reading "restore" as subtracting a barrier strains Fig 11 :59 and the formula's habitat line, because exclusion is unavailable. That is a sign the wrong operator was bound, so X1 is refused and the term is re-censused as introduction. No strain remains after that re-census.

## 4. Findings

### F1 : The question is a union, not one hard question (decisive)

Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

"Reconnect" and "without disrupting" share actors and habitat. A response that reports only total species richness, or only target-species movement, has answered the region where one condition holds and left the intersection unexamined. Mode `general` for the union rule, `specified` for this question. **Refuter:** one intervention that closes both residuals with no categorically different residue would show it was a partition after all.

### F2 : Restoration is introduction into a habitat nobody authored

Fig 11 :57 *"Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there."* Fig 11 :59 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."* Fig 11 :60 *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* Fig 11 :61 *"adds to a habitat it still did not author."*

A corridor is an added actor in an existing contact network. There is no operator for removing the resident community, and the ground does not consult the manager. So the manager can add, cannot subtract, and must keep reading. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a demonstrated operator that removes an incumbent actor from the habitat without substituting another habitat would break the asymmetry claim.

### F3 : Isolation is substitution, not removal

Fig 11 :63 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :64 *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* `THE_FORMULA.md`:86 *"Isolation substitutes a habitat, it does not remove one."*

So "restoring connectivity" is not undoing a subtraction. Fragmentation replaced one habitat with several plus a matrix; reconnection substitutes again, and inherits whatever the new habitat brings. Two silent failure modes are named: Fig 11 :65 *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and Fig 11 :66 *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*. Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a landscape where reconnection restores the original contact set exactly, with no inherited actors and no missing conditions, would falsify the substitution claim for that case.

### F4 : Valence is relative to each residual, not carried by the connector

Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

The corridor is not "good" or "bad". Against the target persistence residual it can be a benefactor; against the resident integrity residual the same structure can be a malfactor, by admitting a predator, a pathogen or a competitor. This is why "walkable corridor" and "disease pathway" are both true descriptions of the same link under different residuals. Mode `general` for the rule, `specified` for the corridor application. **Refuter:** an invariant valence for a connector across documented changes of residual (beneficial to every species and every community at every site) would break the rule.

### F5 : Contact cascades; first-order enumeration misses the effect

Fig 11 :52 *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 11 :53 *"Carson, Silent Spring, 1962. The mechanism, biomagnification through the food chain to eggshell thinning, was established over the following decade."*

Counting the target species on each side of a corridor is first-order enumeration. The residual that decides "without disruption" can be a predator increase, a nest predation rate, a pathogen, or an invasive plant three steps out. Fig 11 :47 names the relations to enumerate: *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"*. Mode `general` for the cascade rule. **Refuter:** a documented restoration where the decisive residual was always first-order and no second- or third-order effect changed any verdict would weaken the rule into a caution.

### F6 : The ground settles; agreement cannot; this is permanent work

Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 11 :70 *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*

A consensus that corridors are good is a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :41 would make agreement about shared meaning the product, but here the claim is about the world). Because the habitat updates and the ground does not, a settled design is not a permanent solution: Fig 11 :60 already says managed ecosystems are permanent work. Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a corridor whose outcome is settled once by a measurement that stays valid while the habitat, climate and community change would break the permanence claim.

### F7 : Enumerate the habitat, never compose it

Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* `THE_FORMULA.md`:86 *"Habitat. Enumerate, never compose."*

The list of actors, conditions and relations is read off the landscape, not generated from a design vocabulary. What the instruments cannot yet see is part of `R`, not evidence of its absence. Fig 6 :35 *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a habitat list derived entirely from an instrumentation catalogue that nevertheless included every operative actor and condition would challenge the enumeration rule.

### F8 : "No disruption" is a preference until it is bound as a term

Fig 5 :62 *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."* Fig 5 :65 *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".*

"Without disruption" is the shape the search is looking for. Unbound, it predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so no check in the loop can see it. Writing it down as a slot residual (resident abundance, interaction structure, pathogen detection, stakeholder outcome) is what makes it refusable. Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a design process that treats non-disruption as a measurable bound term from the outset and never shifts its criterion would show the discipline was already in place; the claim then reduces to a method note, not a finding.

### F9 : Which connector, for which species, at which site: `gap`

After genuine re-census, no figure line names a species, a corridor width, a location or a monitoring threshold. Fig 9 :28 *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 11 :69 *"can still come back wrong: an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."*

This is a `gap`, stated plainly, not a hidden answer. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The figures can say what a missing thing would have to look like (a site-specific, source-discriminating instrument that separates target movement from antagonist spread), not which thing it is. Mode `general` for the limit. **Refuter:** a figure line that actually selects a corridor type for a named species would close this gap; none was found after re-census.

### F10 : The world literature disagrees in the way the union predicts

Haddad et al. 2015 report that fragmentation reduces biodiversity and call for restoration to improve connectivity; Gilbert-Norton et al. 2010 find corridors increase movement by about 50 percent; Haddad et al. 2014 find no overarching negative effects but admit the negative-effect studies are few; Firth et al. 2025 argue the costs are under-measured. These are not four independent votes. The first three share authors and evidence pools, and a conflict sends the derivation back to the census, never flips the verdict. The pattern is exactly the union: strong evidence for C1, unsettled evidence for C2, and an under-instrumented intersection. Mode `specified` for the reading, world status `corroborates` for C1 and `silent` for the specific intersection. **Refuter:** a single well-powered study that measures both residuals at the same sites and shows they always move together would collapse the union.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Union refuted:** one lever demonstrably closes the reconnection residual and the resident-integrity residual at the same sites, with the same actors, and the residue is not categorically different from either condition alone.
2. **Asymmetry refuted:** an operator is demonstrated that removes an incumbent actor from a habitat without substituting another habitat, so exclusion is available after all.
3. **Valence refuted:** a connector keeps the same sign for every residual across documented changes of target species and community, so valence is carried rather than relative.
4. **Cascade refuted:** across restoration cases the decisive residual for non-disruption is always first-order, and no second- or third-order effect ever changes a verdict.
5. **Ground refuted:** a corridor outcome is settled once by a measurement that remains valid while the habitat, climate and community measurably change, so management is a solved state rather than permanent work.
6. **Gap closed:** a figure line is found that selects a connector type for a named species at a named site, so the `gap` was a search failure, not a genuine absence.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing above is `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Unbundle before doing anything else: keep target movement and resident integrity as separate slot residuals, never one "connectivity score" (F1, F9).
2. Enumerate the habitat to second and third order before choosing a connector: actors, conditions, relations, and what the instruments cannot yet see (F5, F7).
3. State the seat's asymmetry in the plan: what will be added, what cannot be removed, what will be monitored, and what would be reversed (F2, F3).
4. Bind "no disruption" as an ordinary term with its own refuter, then let the ground settle it (F6, F8).
5. Treat any chosen connector as a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs, never as a derivation from the six-term set (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50).
6. Send the generator (the residual, the shape and the refuters), not the conclusion (Fig 10 :52; Fig 4 :22-25).

## 8. Seven-question recursive derivation

Fig 9 asks SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD; the three are kept distinct.

1. **SEARCHED, is it in here and unfound?** No figure line selects a connector for a named species. The closure does not change: same atoms and wall, more of the space visited. Fig 8 :39 says finding nothing proves nothing, so this is a `gap` after a genuine search, not a proof of absence.
2. **INWARD, extend the instrument from one already returning something.** Yes: occupancy, genetic and pathogen detection instruments already return readings that fail to resolve the intersection. Sharpening those, adding detection of second-order predators and competitors, is INWARD. Fig 6 :32: an instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something.
3. **OUTWARD, import.** Characterize the residual in effect terms (a link that carries targets out and antagonists in), then find the frame whose closure predicts that shape: landscape genetics, disease ecology, network robustness. Source gate, shadow test, debited against independence (Fig 6 :12-13; Fig 8 :50-52).
4. **Is the question one question?** No. It is a union of C1 and C2 (Fig 9 :54). Unbundle before the next search.
5. **Which term is wrong?** Candidate held terms: "connector equals benefit" and "no disruption equals a settled endpoint". Both accrue persistent, non-declining residuals and are refused rather than repaired (Fig 5 :58-60). Negation is not a bond; nothing is deleted, the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42-43).
6. **What does the ground decide?** A measurement at a named site, not a conference consensus. Fig 2 :45; Fig 11 :70.
7. **What is the round trip?** The abstraction from the figures to "corridors are good" drops the union, the asymmetry, the valence rule and the cascade. `R_T` is nonzero; see `11_audit.md`.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: this is a union managed by introduction under asymmetric power, with relative valence and cascading contact, settled only at the ground and never as a solved state. The specific connector is a declared `gap`. Strains after re-census: **one named and live**, the alternate human-community reading (D5) changes the anchor; it is not smoothed over but kept as a separate domain with its own verdict.
