## QUESTION

Can restoring connectivity reconnect ecosystems without disrupting established local communities? Restated as an effect: a seat adds a link to a fragmented habitat. Option A closes both the target-movement residual and the resident-integrity residual. Option B closes the first and displaces the second, because the same link carries antagonists, pathogens, competitors or disturbance into the community. The union has to be unbundled before any frame is picked (Fig 9 :54). R is indexed per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: a demographic or genetic measurement at a named site (Fig 2 :29).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** Arity: two conditions on one habitat. Symmetry: the link is spatially symmetric, carrying targets out and whatever else inward, while the seat's power over it is asymmetric (Fig 11 :59). Scale: first order movement, second order relations, third order decisive residual, fourth order management feedback. Union, not partition.
- **D1 Target connectivity.** Actors: target plants and animals, patches, corridor, manager. Currency: movement, gene flow, occupancy, persistence. Anchor: counts and genetic measurements at a site. Admitted.
- **D2 Resident community.** Actors: incumbent species, competitors, predators, mutualists, microclimate. Currency: abundance, richness, interaction structure. Anchor: before/after community survey and demographic fate. Admitted.
- **D3 Antagonist cascade.** Actors: invasive species, pathogens, fire, hitchhiking predators. Currency: spread rate, infection, establishment. Anchor: detection and containment records. Admitted.
- **D4 Management seat.** Actors: agencies, landowners, monitors. Currency: interventions, reversibility. Anchor: the ground, never internal agreement. Admitted.
- **D5 Human local community, alternate reading.** Actors: residents, stakeholders, custodians. Currency: livelihood, access, trust. Anchor: human judgment, legitimate when the question is about people (Fig 2 :36). Admitted as an alternate, not merged with D2.
- **X1 "Restore" as subtracting a barrier.** No nameable anchor: exclusion is unavailable and isolation is substitution (Fig 11 :63). Refused, not a domain.
- **X2 "No disruption" as a settled endpoint.** A preference, not a bound term (Fig 5 :62). Not a domain until bound as a slot residual.

Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat, acquire terms for what the link affords, state the objective, choose the link, read the ground and carry the result (Fig 3 :20). The middle link, what counts as progress, has the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :27). Strain test: reading restoration as subtraction strains Fig 11 :59, so X1 is refused and the term is re-censused as introduction. No other strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any specific connector does not. This is a union of two slot residuals on one habitat, so they must be measured separately and the intersection has its own loop. Restoration is introduction under asymmetric power: a link can be added, the residents cannot be subtracted, and isolation was already a substitution. Valence is relative to the residual, so the same link can serve the target and harm the residents, and the decisive effect can be three steps out. The ground settles the claim at a named site; management is permanent work, not a solved state. "Without disruption" must be bound as a threshold before it can be checked. Which connector works where is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The D5 human-community reading is a live strain, kept separate.

- **cost if wrong:** a single lever is applied to a union. A manager optimises target movement with an instrument that cannot see pathogen or invader spread, reports success, and the resident residual grows three steps out. The cost is real and should be priced with independence before the work (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). The cost is bounded and undoable only if the threshold was written down before construction.
- **undoable:** yes. No actor or term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reachable design space falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42). Design cuts must be kept ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41). A link that can be closed during an outbreak is a cheaper error than one that cannot.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: paired measurements showing the two residuals always move together; a demonstrated operator that removes an incumbent without substituting a habitat; a connector with invariant sign across changes of residual; restoration cases where every decisive effect is first-order; an outcome settled once and still valid after the habitat changes; a figure line selecting a connector for a named species; or, for D5, evidence that the question is constitutively about people so human judgment becomes the anchor. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
