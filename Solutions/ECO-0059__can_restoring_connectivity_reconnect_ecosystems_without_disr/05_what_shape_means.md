## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The essay *The Library of Babel* puts the operational version: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form any answer must take before a species, a corridor width or a site is named. It is: two slot residuals kept separate; an introduction into an unauthored habitat; valence relative to each residual; a cascade enumerated outward; a ground that settles and a seat that keeps reading. It is not "build a wildlife corridor". That is a name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Starting vocabulary: links, patches, movement. Recurring new terms that overlap: corridor, gene flow, edge effect, pathogen spillover, invasion pathway, extinction debt.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* and Fig 9 :31 *"rather than the cause"*: the residual was described as "a link that carries targets out and whatever else is in the matrix in", not as "fragmentation" or "restoration", because those names would have picked a frame first (Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."*).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the outreach email asks what the recipient would expect to see, rather than explaining the architecture.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling, directly on point: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level source for the union finding F1.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means a demographic or genetic measurement at a named site that does not move because a manager or reviewer thinks the corridor worked (Fig 2 :29).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Choosing a particular corridor design from landscape genetics is such an import, and must be recorded as an import rather than a derivation.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the distinction: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Adding "pathogen surveillance" to a corridor plan is a new term; adding the ability to detect a pathogen along the link is a new instrument, which changes what can be represented at all. For this question the missing thing is mostly instrumental, not lexical.

The same essay adds a limit worth keeping visible: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the figures' habitat rules are a visible choice of axes, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why "corridor equals benefit" is the specific named error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: "movement increased" is a projection. It is consistent with successful reconnection of the target species and with a new pathway for a pathogen or an invader. The same tracking data cannot discriminate the two sources. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (pathogen detection, predator and competitor monitoring, community structure before and after), not a name for the corridor.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the ecological case almost verbatim: *"The slide rule principle applies to many things, be it backyard gardens, factory farming, urban planning, landscape engineering or ecological restoration."* and *"when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."* That is Fig 11 :52 in narrative form.

## 5. The habitat is not an ecological niche

*Arrival of the Fittest*, section "Three mappings offered and declined", refuses this exact mapping: *"A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes."*

This matters for ECO-0059. If we import the niche concept, we picture a fixed container with a fixed set of good and bad species. The figures say the opposite: the list is read off the world for *this* residual, and it changes when the residual changes (Fig 11 :44, :49-51). The same essay notes that the habitat claims carry no borrowed citation, and applies its own rule: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* So the enumeration rule is a rule the framework imposes on itself, not a settled fact about nature.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A well-known reviewer, a well-funded corridor programme or a famous reserve is a budget, not a verdict. The legitimate ask in outreach is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let a measurement settle it. The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Capability is agent-and-habitat.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* A corridor does not work "by itself"; it works in a landscape with particular matrix, species and instruments. The same essay records the asymmetry that governs F2: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* and warns, in its own words, that cane toads and kudzu are *"the same move with the cascade unenumerated."*

**Do not average the residuals.** The same essay states the operational rule directly: *"A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them."* That is Fig 1 :22 applied to management: a single "connectivity score" across species and sites is how the live signal disappears.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*.

*Arrival of the Fittest* adds the caveat on its demo: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, cannot settle it, and must be labelled as such everywhere.

## 9. Correction carried forward, not erased

An earlier reading of "shape, not a name" can be over-generalised into "never name any component". The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. For this question the missing thing is a site-specific source-discriminating instrument, and the framework cannot name it. Naming a *held* figure element, such as the habitat introduction asymmetry or the cascade rule, is permitted; it is a reading of what the figures already contain. What is not permitted is naming the missing instrument or the winning corridor as if the framework had derived it. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
