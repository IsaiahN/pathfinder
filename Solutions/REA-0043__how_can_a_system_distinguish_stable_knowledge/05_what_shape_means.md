## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question it is the set of constraints any stability discriminator must satisfy, not the discriminator itself and not a threshold. Concretely:

- a shape is **per slot**, not a global flag (Fig 1 :19, :22),
- a shape has **four instruments with different anchors**, not one score (F1),
- a shape includes a **measured gap**, not an assumed zero (Fig 4 :30),
- a shape ends in a **refuter**, not a verdict (Formula, "Direction").

A number like "stable for 30 days across 5 contexts" is a *name*, not a shape. It is the author's convention dressed as a result, and F12 records its absence as a `gap`.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel* bootstraps vocabulary by proximity: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start from the terms the question already supplies (stable, temporary, context-specific, fact, assumption), then let the figures supply the discriminating terms (slot, ground, settlement, method, recording, round trip, closure, provenance, refusal). Each new term narrows what an answer could be.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the shape is stated as effects: what would you observe if a term were stable rather than a recording?
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the stage-1 offer in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the four instruments plus the declared `gap`; further searching inside the figures adds no atom (Fig 8 :32 *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*).

## 3. Why the three classes cannot be read off the artifact

This is the central lesson of the essays for this question.

- *How Intelligence Actually Works*, on a video frame versus a photograph: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* And the general statement: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* This is Fig 12 :5 and Fig 8 :51 in narrative form.
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* Four different physical sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from a better instrument, the spectrometer, *"not by argument."* For this question: a stability flag read from a term's content is the green reading; the source (ground-settled, once-paid, or closure-bound) is invisible to it. What is owed is a discriminator, not a better label.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* introduces the **distinction horizon**: *"There exists a 'distinction horizon' for every individual observer, the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another."* And the **union horizon**: a projection sinks below it *"when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."* Crucially: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* This is the honest standing of the `gap` in F12.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on provenance: *"some processes leave traces and depth recovers them; some produce identical output and no depth helps. ... that's why the lineage has to be recorded rather than reconstructed."* This is the engineering case for F6.

## 4. Context is part of the term's warrant, not decoration

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is directly on the "context-specific assumptions" half of the question. It defines pretext as *"the history you bring to the words"* and says: *"Context, subtext and pretext decide what a message means to you. None of them decides whether it is true. Only the ground does that."* This separates two things this solution keeps separate:

- what a term *means* in a frame (context/pretext, D3, closure), and
- whether the term *is* (settlement by the ground, D1).

The essay also gives the failure mode of conflating them: two people who only check against each other *"drift, and they drift together, so neither notices,"* because *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* A memory system that validates its own terms against its own stored terms is doing exactly this. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

The essay's measurement proposal is the instrument for F4: *"The size of a correction tells you how much of the knowledge is still tacit for that learner, and it should shrink as their experience builds. That makes tacit transfer measurable: watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* A shrinking correction trend is the same quantity Fig 5 :59 uses to rank a term for refusal: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN."*

## 5. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. A fact imported from a corpus is a context-specific assumption of that corpus until it is re-settled.

Everything else is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is why the public-contact venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are checked for ground contact, not for agreement.

## 6. Why this whole audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

This folder is the approximate output. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures and against five essays and six independent world sources. It is **not** a test of a memory system and it cannot settle the question it reports on. *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

## 7. Prestige is not action

From the same essay: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question the practical consequence is that a well-cited memory architecture is not thereby a correct one. Its reputation can buy it a test (a budget to be tried); it cannot substitute for the test. The outreach artifacts ask for a refutation of the shape, never an endorsement of the source.

## 8. Correction to my own §3 reading (preserved, not erased)

I first read the distinction-horizon passages as saying that provenance alone is sufficient to classify a term. That overstates it. A provenance record can be forged or incomplete, and Fig 10 :65 says plainly: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that."* The corrected reading is: provenance is a **necessary** discriminator for the class "context-specific assumption", not a sufficient verdict on the term's truth. Both the earlier and corrected readings are kept here so the change is visible. Triangulated: Fig 8 :51, Fig 10 :65, and *How Intelligence Actually Works* on traces versus recorded lineage.
