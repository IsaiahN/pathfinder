# Expert verification packet - the Tether on stable knowledge versus temporary facts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a memory researcher, knowledge-representation engineer, or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No memory system was built and no measurement was taken. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A system cannot read stability off a stored term, because a record of one occasion looks like knowledge once it is stored and a convergent derivation looks like an adopted assumption in its contents. The Tether therefore requires four instruments with different anchors, run in dependency order and kept per slot: keep the ground channel reachable; check that the ground settled the term *and* that something can compose over it (two conditions); carry the term up to a rule and back down to a case and measure the round-trip gap `R_T`; and record the term's provenance and closure. Temporary facts are marked with a refusal rather than deleted. Verdicts are never averaged into one score, and the numerical cutoff is declared unreachable rather than invented.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 5

- **Statement:** a stable term must satisfy two independent conditions, settlement by the ground and usability for composition. Storage growth is not reach growth. A method survives re-application; a recording does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :70 *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one."* Fig 5 :72 *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a term that was never settled by a ground and is nevertheless composable over and later produces a novel success in a new situation; or exhibit a system whose reach grows exactly as fast as its library with no refusals and no change of composition rules.

### C2 - measurement-facing; checkable by anyone who builds a memory system

- **Statement:** the round trip is the discriminator, and its gap must be measured, not assumed zero. Verdicts are per slot, not averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*
- **What to check:** for terms independently labelled methods and recordings, does the trend in round-trip correction size separate the two? Does a global score near zero hide a live slot residual?
- **Refuter:** a recording of one occasion that survives the round trip with zero gap and is later shown to have lost information in the abstraction; or a system whose per-slot residuals are all zero for a term that fails on a previously unseen slot with no novelty channel.

### C3 - engineering-facing; checkable against provenance and channel design

- **Statement:** a context-specific assumption is identified by its provenance and closure, not by its content; a stale channel is an alarm, not a knowledge classification; refused terms are marked, not deleted.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*; Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :28 *"you have lost touch"*; Fig 6 :46 *"A store that deleted instead would have neither property, and could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up."*
- **Refuter:** two propositions with identical content, identical provenance and identical closure, one stable and one context-specific; or a stability certificate that changes under a frame swap while the ground measurement is identical; or a system that deletes a refused term and still distinguishes never-held from held-and-given-up without an external record.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- No numerical stability threshold, time window, or context count. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. This is a declared `gap`.
- Not a completed memory architecture, benchmark, or implementation.
- Not a confidence ranking. A confidence score aggregates across slots and hands a filter a verdict (Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not "delete the temporary facts". Refusal, not deletion, is the licensed operation (Fig 6 :46).
- Not that provenance settles truth. Fig 10 :65 *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that."*
- Not grounded, not a ToE, not the only true account of memory. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

1. Build the discriminator and measure `R_T` per slot for terms with independent ground labels. The gap must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30).
2. Swap the frame holding the ground fixed; a certificate that moves was an opinion, not an anchor (Fig 2 :29).
3. Break the channel deliberately and check that "you have lost touch" is reported as an alarm (Fig 10 :24-28).
4. Cross two frames with disjoint evidence; agreement from one corpus is one witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 5 :70 and :72, Fig 4 :22 and :25, Fig 4 :30, Fig 1 :22, Fig 8 :51, Fig 10 :24 and :28, Fig 6 :46. Nine short passages.
2. Check C2 against any memory or retrieval system you know: is the round trip measurable, and does a global score hide live slots?
3. Check C3 against truth maintenance or bitemporal databases: is provenance the discriminator in practice, and is refusal implemented as marking rather than deletion?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a method from a recording without an appeal to agreement, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
