# The Tether's answer on stable knowledge versus temporary facts

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not an implemented memory system. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The system holds terms. It must decide, per term and per slot, whether the term is stable knowledge (a re-applicable generator), a temporary fact (a record of one occasion), or a context-specific assumption (true only in one closure). The three are indiscernible from the term's content: Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* So the question is not *what is stored* but *what the system is entitled to do with what it stores*. That is an instrument question, not a storage question.

## 2. The shape: four instruments, four verdicts, never averaged

1. **Keep the anchor reachable.** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :28 *"you have lost touch"*. A stale reading is an operational alarm, not a knowledge classification. Without this first link every later test reads a dead ground (Fig 3 :23 *"It is a reading of nothing."*).

2. **Settlement is necessary but not sufficient.** Fig 5 :70 *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one."* A memory that grows is not a reach that grows: Fig 5 :72 *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* A system can hold a paid-for term it cannot compose over.

3. **Method or recording, measured by a round trip.** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* A stable abstraction survives concretisation with a small, measured, non-zero `R_T`. A recording fails on return. Fig 5 :44 rejects *"a term that explains one occasion perfectly."*

4. **Provenance and closure:** a convergent derivation is not an adopted assumption. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 10 :62 *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."* But provenance is legitimacy, not truth: Fig 10 :65 *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that."*

Additional structural requirements:

- **Per slot, never aggregated.** Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single global stability flag is the named failure.
- **Refuse, do not delete.** Fig 6 :46 a system that deletes *"could not tell never-held from held-and-given-up."* A temporary fact stays in the record but loses its standing to be composed over. The record grows; the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42).
- **Independence, not agreement.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Two checkers from one corpus are one witness.
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* "The world changed", "the channel decayed", and "it was a recording" are three overlapping conditions, not one.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- No numerical threshold. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Any number of days or contexts would be a convention, not a figure-derived result. This is a declared `gap`.
- Not a completed memory architecture. No implementation, no benchmark, no measurement.
- Not a ranking of facts by confidence. A confidence score is an aggregation across slots and a filter handed a verdict (Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not "forget the temporary facts". Deletion is refused by Fig 6 :46; refusal is the licensed operation.
- Not a claim that provenance settles truth. It settles only the class "assumption" (Fig 10 :65).
- Not grounded, not a ToE, not a claim that this is the only true account of memory.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator and a measurement, not more agreement. From the RSI trap essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Concretely:

- Build a memory system with per-slot verdicts, a non-updating ground channel, a promotion gate, and provenance tags. Measure `R_T` for terms known to be methods and terms known to be recordings; the trend in correction size should separate them (Decoding Tacit Knowledge: *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given"*).
- Swap the frame while holding the ground fixed. A certificate that moves was a frame opinion, not an anchor (Fig 2 :29).
- Break the channel deliberately and check that "you have lost touch" is reported as an alarm, not reclassified as "these facts were temporary" (Fig 10 :24-28).
- Cross two frames with disjoint evidence. Agreement under a shared corpus is one witness (Fig 2 :31).

## 5. The honest stop

**The shape is reachable; the cutoff is not.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* What remains outside is a seat's office, not the loop's: someone must supply the ground for a particular memory system and decide what would refute the classification before running it. *How Intelligence Actually Works* warns that *"indiscernibility is not identity"* and that a projection below the union horizon is still a projection. Indiscernible is not identical, and the record of the route is what keeps the result checkable. All four domain verdicts here are `specified` candidates. No measurement settled any of them.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell a machine what to forget. It says that a single "is this stable?" flag is the wrong object, because a record of one occasion looks exactly like knowledge once it is stored. What is needed is four separate instruments with different anchors: keep the channel to the ground reachable; check that the ground actually paid for the term and that something can compose over it (these are two conditions, not one); carry the term up to a rule and back down to a case and measure the gap, because assuming the gap is zero is dead reckoning; and record where the term came from and which frame derived it, because a convergent derivation and an adopted assumption are indistinguishable in their contents. Verdicts stay per item and per slot and are never averaged. Temporary facts are marked with a refusal, not deleted, since a store that deletes cannot tell never-held from held-and-given-up. The framework returns this shape, not a numerical cutoff. The cutoff is reported as unreachable, and the shape is offered with its refuters written in advance so a failure is more useful than an endorsement.
