## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about discriminating stable knowledge from temporary facts and context-specific assumptions. Option A: stability is a property readable from the stored term, so one score or flag suffices. Option B: stability is a relation between a term, a slot, a ground, a scale and a closure, maintained by a channel, so four instruments with different anchors are required and their verdicts stay separate. What each leads to: A produces an aggregated flag that hides live slot residuals, mistakes a recording for knowledge and an assumption for a derivation, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a per-slot shape with a measured gap, a provenance record, a refusal discipline and a pre-registered refuter, and declares the numerical cutoff unreachable.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The discrimination problem itself. Arity: three classes (stable / temporary / context-bound), indexed per slot. Symmetry: not symmetric; each class is a different directed relation between a term and the ground. Scale: within one Γ and across the membrane, with a round trip measured by `R_T`. Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"* applies: the persistence axis and the closure axis have different anchors.
- **Domain 1, term life and settlement.** Actors: terms, atoms, bonds, Γ, slots. Currency: per-slot residual, settlement, composition. Anchor: the ground (Fig 5 :70). Union or partition: a partition question about what composes across the two kinds.
- **Domain 2, membrane and scale transform.** Actors: one agent, population, methods, recordings, head starts, replays. Currency: reapplicability and round-trip gap. Anchor: the measured round trip (Fig 4 :30). Question type: a partition with a lossy transform.
- **Domain 3, frame and closure.** Actors: frames, closures, imports, provenance records. Currency: independence and surplus. Anchor: a differing closure plus the ground (Fig 6 :23). Question type: union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 4, channel and maintenance.** Actors: anchor, channel, seats, percepts, transients, pooled readings. Currency: anchor reachability. Anchor: keep the anchor reachable (Fig 10 :29). Question type: a partition between a verdict and a loss of touch.
- **Order by dependency.** D4 anchor reachable, then D1 settlement, then D2 round trip, then D3 closure provenance (Fig 3 :20). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Stability is not a property of a stored term. It is a four-part relation that must be re-established per slot: a reachable ground channel, ground settlement plus composability, a measured round trip that distinguishes a method from a recording, and a provenance and closure record that distinguishes a derivation from an assumption. Verdicts stay separate by domain and are never averaged. Temporary facts are marked with a refusal rather than deleted, since a store that deletes cannot tell never-held from held-and-given-up. Two checkers sharing a corpus are one witness. The framework returns this shape and refuses to name a numerical cutoff, which is reported as `gap` of the unreachable kind. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, and keeps the record of refusals, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished architecture before they have described a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four instruments can be added to a memory system without withdrawing any stored term, because refusal marks rather than deletes. The shape can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Building the discriminator and measuring `R_T` per slot for independently labelled methods and recordings; swapping the frame while holding the ground fixed; breaking the channel deliberately and confirming that loss of touch is reported as an alarm; or crossing two frames with disjoint evidence. A figure line that selects a numerical cutoff would replace the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
