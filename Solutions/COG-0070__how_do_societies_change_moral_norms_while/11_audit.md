## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Two reports co-arrive: norms changed, and "we are still us." Arity: at least two, and four after the mechanism is censused. Symmetry: change is a refusal plus a recomposition; continuity is an identity-style claim across time, so the two are related as `¬` and `≡` rather than as two values of one variable. Scale: person, institution, intergenerational. That makes it a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."), not one hard question with a volume knob.
- **Domain A, warrant.** Actors: a person judging conduct, the person affected, enforcers. Currency: residual about harm and welfare. Anchor: judgment of conduct that does not move merely because a frame argues, and which is indexed per case. I reached this from Fig 2 :36-37 and Fig 1 :19 rather than from the primary's world sources.
- **Domain B, identity.** Actors: members and narrators. Currency: shared backdrop and agreement. Anchor: none in the world verdict sense, because agreement is the product for a convention (Fig 2 :41). This is a legitimate domain but not a verdict domain.
- **Domain C, transmission.** Actors: teachers, learners, media. Currency: methods versus recordings. Anchor: whether contact changes (Fig 11 :40). I reached this from Fig 4 rather than from the primary's Fig 4 and Fig 11 pairing.
- **Domain D, versioning.** Actors: reformers and courts. Currency: refusal and standing. Anchor: the record (Fig 6 :42). Without a unary refusal, a system can be corrected but cannot carry the correction.
- **Order by dependency.** Split A from B first; this is the uninstrumented middle link. Then refuse, then transmit with a measured gap, then re-range against the ground. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the split from the valence of correlation (Fig 2 :39-41), I derived it from the arity of the residual (Fig 9 :54-57) and the operator difference between `¬` and `≡`. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes inside one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the level of derivation. It is still one pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the four-condition shape, the add-not-delete rule, the methods-through-the-membrane rule, and the per-domain verdict separation.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: split verdict from convention; refuse without deleting; carry methods; keep the record; leave the specific norm open.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete question as a practitioner would ask it: what should this society do about this norm, and how do we justify calling the result continuous?

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The shorthand says "judgment per case"; the concrete ask turns it into a survey average, which Fig 1 :22 says throws away the signal.
- **The non-updating anchor.** The shorthand says "a ground that does not move on opinion"; the concrete ask substitutes majority sentiment, which turns panel 3 of Fig 2 into panel 1.
- **The convention/verdict split.** The shorthand keeps agreement's two valences apart; the concrete ask uses "what the community feels" as evidence for what is right.
- **Add-not-delete.** The shorthand keeps the refused norm in the record; the concrete ask reaches for replacement, erasure or a clean slate.

One thing survives cleanly: continuity is the recoverable lineage and the carried method, not the unchanged norm list. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
