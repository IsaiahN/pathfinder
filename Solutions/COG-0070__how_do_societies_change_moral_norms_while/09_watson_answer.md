## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what shape an answer must have to the question of how societies change moral norms while keeping continuity. Option A: one mechanism, with the society's own sense of continuity as evidence that the change is right. Option B: a union of four differently anchored conditions, with verdicts kept separate and the specific norm left as a gap. What each leads to: A averages a convention into a verdict and manufactures the correlation that makes the agreement worthless; B gives a checkable shape with seven refuters written in advance and an open slot until a discriminator exists.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two reports arrive together: norms changed, and "we are still us." Arity: four conditions once censused, so a union compound rather than one hard question. Symmetry: change and continuity are not symmetric; continuity is an identity claim about a referent across time (`≡`), while change is a refusal plus a new composition. Scale: person, institution and intergenerational transmission. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **D1, warrant verdict.** Actors: the person judging conduct, the person harmed, enforcing institutions. Currency: residual about harm and welfare, sanctions. Anchor: structured human judgment per case, not an instrument and not the majority report (Fig 2 :36-37, Fig 1 :19).
- **D2, continuity convention.** Actors: members, narrators, ritual and institutional custodians. Currency: shared meaning, agreement, narrative. Anchor: agreement is the product, legitimate until it decides an outcome (Fig 2 :41, Fig 10 :47).
- **D3, transmission.** Actors: teachers, apprentices, media, newcomers. Currency: methods and head starts versus recordings (Fig 4 :22). Anchor: whether contact changes (Fig 11 :40).
- **D4, versioning.** Actors: reformers, norm entrepreneurs, courts, dissenters. Currency: refusal `¬`, standing to be composed over. Anchor: the record, which only grows (Fig 6 :42).
- **Union, not partition.** A case where the continuity narrative is also being used as the verdict is a specific compound, not a harder case of either loop (Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.").
- **Order by dependency.** Split verdict from convention (the uninstrumented middle link, Fig 3 :24-27), then aim the refusal, then carry the method with a measured round trip, then re-range against the ground. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

Strain test: an early reading made "anchor must not update" forbid moral change. Re-census assigned continuity to D2 and D4 and dissolved the strain. A second strain, the Fig 13 substrate applied to moral foundations in `06`, is an analogy and is named as such. No figure-to-figure strain remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union, not one mechanism. Warrant is a verdict anchored in per-person judgment of conduct; continuity is a convention about shared meaning that becomes illegitimate when it decides an outcome. Continuity itself is lineage and arrangement, not identity of the norm list. Change is a refusal that adds without deleting, so the record grows while the reach changes. Generations carry methods and seeds, not frozen norm lists. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Which specific norm should change, and which specific narrative is legitimate rather than a veto, are declared gaps of the unreached kind. This result is `specified` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its refuters and its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a full theory before the shape claim is checked, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The four-condition shape is a recombination of held operators and atoms, not a new primitive. If R1 to R5 or R7 fire, the relevant finding is withdrawn without disturbing the others, and no primitive has to be abandoned. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates the sources of a felt continuity reading (recorded lineage versus coherent drift versus recently invented tradition) would name the slot; independent instruments agreeing per case on the consequences of a disputed norm would move D1 toward `grounded`; a method that produces a novel correct norm in a new habitat with a measured round-trip gap would confirm D3; pure erasure retaining the same lineage evidence would fire R1 and break D4. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
