## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A society holds a set of moral norms N(t). At t+1 it holds N(t+1), different from N(t). At the same time its members report continuity: "we are still us, the same community, our past still binds us." The residual R is the gap between:

- the prediction of a frame that treats identity as persistence of content: if the norms change, the community's identity and authority should break, so one of the two reports must be false; and
- the observation: change and continuity arrive together, often without anyone noticing a break.

Arity: at least two conditions, possibly four once the mechanism is censused. Symmetry: change and continuity are not symmetric. Continuity is a claim that two things at different times are the same referent, which is the identity bond `≡` in Fig 12 :65-69; change is a claim that a term has been refused and a different one composed. Scale: the individual (a person revises a judgment), the societal (institutions enforce and legitimate), and the intergenerational (something crosses between people who never met).

**The sought-for shape is bound here as an ordinary term before the search**, per THE_FORMULA.md "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* The candidate shape is: **four conditions with different anchors, a refusal that adds without deleting, a membrane that carries methods rather than norm lists, and a lineage that is recoverable rather than an identity that is preserved.** This shape is now refusable by the same test as everything else. If it fails any refuter in section 3, it is withdrawn.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 verdict: is a change warranted? | the person judging conduct, the person harmed or helped, enforcing institutions | residual about harm and welfare, sanctions | structured judgment of conduct by persons, per person, not aggregated (Fig 2 :36-37, Fig 1 :19) | separate loop, ground-anchored |
| D2 convention: who are we? | members, narrators, institutions, ritual specialists | shared meaning, agreement, narrative | agreement itself is the product (Fig 2 :41), legitimate until it decides an outcome (Fig 2 :43) | separate loop, convention-anchored |
| D3 transmission: what crosses generations? | teachers, apprentices, media, newcomers | methods and head starts versus recordings (Fig 4 :22-25) | whether contact changes (Fig 11 :40) | condition on both loops |

**Partition or union?** D1 and D2 have different anchors and different failure conditions: a disagreement in D1 shrinks when both sides range against a record; a disagreement in D2 is not supposed to shrink, because agreement about shared meaning is the product. They cannot be averaged. D3 and D4 are not new anchors but conditions that must hold for a change to be both real and continuous. This is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition of one cause. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A norm change in which the continuity narrative is also being used as the verdict is a specific compound, not a harder case of either loop.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." The order is:

1. Split verdict from convention (D1/D2). This is the middle link Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break." It has no instrument (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.") because both sides call their claim "moral" and "traditional".
2. Aim the refusal at the right held term (D4). Formula step 2: refuse before mint.
3. Carry the method or seed across the membrane and measure the gap (D3).
4. Re-range the new norm against the ground (D1 again).

Strain test applied during the census: an early reading assigned "continuity" to D1 and concluded that the figures forbid moral change because an anchor must not update. That strains Fig 2 :33 against the phenomenon itself, since human values demonstrably move. Re-census: continuity is not a verdict about the world, it is a convention about shared meaning (D2), plus a lineage claim (D3/D4). Different quantities, so no strain remains. The correction is kept in section 6.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

Each finding gives the figure line first, verbatim, then the derivation, then its refuter.

### F1 - The question is a union of differently anchored loops, not one question about moral change (decisive at census level)

Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence."

Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"

A claim that a norm change is warranted is a verdict about the world and needs a ground that does not move on opinion. A claim that "we are still us" is a convention about shared meaning where agreement is the product. Asking one question with one mechanism conflates them. The common move, treating the society's own sense of continuity as evidence that the change is right, is Fig 2 :43 "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."

- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the continuity report and the warrant claim share one anchor and one failure condition, so that they shrink or fail together under the same instrument. Then the split is wrong and the two loops should be merged.
- **Mode:** `general` (structural).

### F2 - The anchor for a warrant verdict is per-person judgment of conduct, not majority agreement and not an external instrument

Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."

Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Because the question is constitutively about people, the legitimate anchor is human judgment of conduct, not a measuring device that can be wrong on axes it does not measure (the second half of Fig 2 :37). But "human judgment" here is the structured judgment of each person, indexed per case, not the pooled opinion of the society. A majority report of continuity is an average over slots and is exactly what Fig 1 :22 says destroys the signal. World corroboration, at reduced weight because it is a review: SEP *The Moral/Conventional Distinction* (Machery and Stich, 2022) records that Turiel's criterion judgments are *"authority independent"* and concern *"harm, injustice, or the violation of rights"*, and that Haidt found *"the effect of SES was much larger than the effect of location"*, so a society-level average is not the person-level reading.

- **Refuter:** a measurement of harm and welfare which both sides accept, which does not move when either argues, and which yields the same verdict per person as the aggregated social consensus. That would collapse D1's per-slot requirement into D2's agreement.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F3 - Continuity is not identity `≡` of the norm content; it is recoverability of the lineage

The identity bond `≡` is defined in Fig 12 :65 *"two names, one referent"* with the test in Fig 12 :67 *"substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No."* A real norm change loses something, so continuity cannot be identity of content under `≡`.

Fig 13 :128 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."

Fig 13 :128-129 "The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."

Fig 13 :132 "The first is provenance: how did this come to be, and from what."

Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."

Continuity is the lineage: the present rule can show the route by which it was produced, including the rule it refused. The community persists as the arrangement that carries the record, not as an unchanged list. World corroboration: Wikipedia *Invented tradition* reports Hobsbawm's claim that many "traditions" which "appear or claim to be old are often quite recent in origin and sometimes invented," and Beiner's preferred term "reinvention of tradition", signifying "a creative process involving renewal, reinterpretation and revision". Continuity is a property of the transmission line, not of the age of the content. The framework essay *How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the analogue directly: *"The Tether places identity in the lineage, not on a single timeline"* and treats the ship of Theseus as two real lineages rather than one paradox.

- **Refuter:** a society that changes a norm, loses every record of the earlier norm and of the route, and reports the same strength of continuity under a test that distinguishes "we kept the record" from "we only feel the same now". That would make continuity a present-tense convention alone and fold F3 into F1.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F4 - Change requires a refusal that adds without deleting, and that is exactly what preserves continuity

Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"

Fig 5 :55 "Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."

Fig 6 :42 "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."

Fig 6 :43 "A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted."

Fig 12 :140 "A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."

Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"

Operators table :73 "Subtraction is the nearest bond and is not it."

A moral change is not an overwrite. It is a new entry that refuses the standing of an old norm to be composed over, while the old norm stays in the record. Continuity is the record half of this operation; change is the reach half. They are not in tension because they are different quantities. A society with subtraction but no negation can remove an operand and say what is missing, and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong (Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."). World corroboration: Wikipedia *Moral foundations theory* records that *"The theory's proponents are open to adding, subtracting, and otherwise modifying the content of theory's foundations."* The framework essay *How a Society of Agents Would Work* describes the same engineering: *"Things fade, so they can be tried again... The record is never deleted."*

- **Refuter:** a norm change implemented by pure erasure, with no refusing term added to the record, which nevertheless preserves the same lineage evidence as an add-only change. That breaks the add-not-delete shape.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F5 - What crosses generations is a method or a seed, not a recording of the norm list

Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."

Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

Fig 4 :34 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

A society maintains continuity by transmitting the generator, the way to deliberate and correct under new conditions, not by freezing the list of conclusions. Freezing the list is a recording replayed downward: Fig 4 :25-26 "Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." World corroboration from four low-overlap fields: the essay *They're Scanning Books When They Should* states *"outputs do not contain their generator"*; *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself"*; the Cultural Evolution Society's *What is cultural evolution?* notes that *"cultural traits are often transformed and reconstructed as they are passed from person to person"* and lists conformity and prestige bias as transmission biases; Rendell et al. 2010 (Science, full text opened) found that *"Social learning proved advantageous because individuals frequently demonstrated the highest-payoff behavior in their repertoire, inadvertently filtering information for copiers"* and that the winner *"weighted information according to the time since acquisition"*, which is a method of updating, not a stored answer.

- **Refuter:** a society that transmits only a fixed norm list, with no method for revising it, and that still generates a novel correct norm under conditions the list never covered. That breaks the seed requirement.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F6 - A felt sense of continuity is a shared backdrop: good for coordination, blind to truth, and slow to drift

Fig 2 :19-21 "they never talk, and still agree so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"

Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."

Fig 10 :80 "Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained; frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated"

The phrase "a sense of continuity" is doing two jobs. As a convention it is the product: shared backdrop makes coordinated life possible. As evidence for the warrant claim it is worthless, because members who share one pool agree without checking anything. World corroboration: *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says *"Culture is a shared backdrop... It also drifts. Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice"* and lists the three safeguards that stop it: independent replication, two-way correction, and courts that rule on evidence neither side can move. Wikipedia *Collective memory* reports that *"talking with one another about the past results in memory changes that increase the similarity between the interactional partners' memories"*, which is the mirror chain of Fig 7 :36 at the scale of conversation.

- **Refuter:** two frames inside the society with genuinely separate contact whose agreement on continuity is no stronger than that of frames sharing one backdrop, on a test where the shared backdrop has been falsified. That would mean the agreement carries information beyond the pool.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F7 - "Tradition as veto" is the named collapse: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one

Fig 10 :46 "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."

Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"

Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."

The legitimate use of precedent is as provenance, a source-gated import into a verdict (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.") and Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". The illegitimate use is to let "this is our tradition" decide which norm may be changed, because then a meaning with no truth value has been given an outcome. World corroboration: Wikipedia *Invented tradition* reports that Hobsbawm's invented traditions serve to *"foster social cohesion, legitimize institutions and authority structures, and solidify value systems and beliefs"*; Wikipedia *Social norm* reports Sandholtz's finding that change agents are more likely to succeed when they *"can reference existing foundational meta-norms, and can reference precedents"*. Precedent is a legitimate import into a warrant claim; it is not the warrant itself.

- **Refuter:** a tradition used to veto a change while making no claim about the world and remaining a checkable coordination convention, that is, one whose adherents would accept its replacement by an equivalent coordination device without loss. If they would, F7 was too strong.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F8 - The ground for a change must be source-discriminating, not a count or a prestige signal

Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Persuasion, reframing and majority counts can give a change a budget; they cannot decide whether it pays. World corroboration: Wikipedia *Moral foundations theory* records a 2026 study that *"found no evidence that reframing political positions and issues to moral foundations shifted support among conservatives and liberals for those political issues"*, and Wikipedia *Collective memory* quotes Beiner that *"the construction of a completely collective memory is at best an aspiration of politicians, which is never entirely fulfilled and is always subject to contestations."* Rendell et al. 2010 supplies the low-overlap warning in the other direction: the pure copying strategies won the tournament while *"may result in a population with lower mean fitness"*, which is what an agreement-only channel looks like when there is no ground vote between rooms.

- **Refuter:** a norm change settled by the number or prestige of its proponents alone, which then survives independent contact with the same force as one settled by a discriminating measurement.
- **Mode:** `specified`.

### F9 - No single mechanism is nameable and no final norm set is reachable; the specific case is a declared gap

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room"

Fig 13 :118 "infinite effort inside a finite reach."

Fig 13 :134 "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

World corroboration: the SEP entry records that the decades-long project to define morality *"failed to reach anything close to consensus"*; Wikipedia *Invented tradition* quotes Burke asking *"Given that all traditions change, is it possible or useful to attempt to discriminate the 'genuine' antiques from the fakes?"* Which specific norm should change in a given society, and which specific continuity narrative is legitimate rather than a veto, are therefore **gaps of the unreached kind**, not silences. A source-discriminating instrument for the consequences of a norm and independent frames that do not share the society's backdrop would be owed before either is named.

- **Refuter:** an instrument that discriminates among the sources of an observed norm outcome (a spectrometer for this case) and that settles a specific disputed change without importing a new primitive.
- **Mode:** `general` on the limit, `gap` on the specific case.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Written before the recommendation and the WATSON run, per Fig 10's rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim of section 0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

- **R1.** A norm change occurs by pure erasure from the record, with no refusing term added, and the same lineage evidence survives (breaks F4).
- **R2.** Continuity reports are unchanged when the shared backdrop is replaced with an independent one and the original backdrop is shown false (breaks F6).
- **R3.** A tradition used to decide an outcome is shown to make no claim about the world and to remain a checkable coordination convention (breaks F7).
- **R4.** A norm change is settled by headcount or prestige alone and survives independent contact with equal force (breaks F8).
- **R5.** Two frames with identical evidence pools agree on a warrant verdict and that agreement is shown to carry information beyond the pool (breaks F1 and F2).
- **R6.** A source-discriminating measurement of harm and welfare yields the same verdict per person and across independent instruments for a disputed change (moves a gap toward `grounded`, does not break the shape).
- **R7.** One mechanism with a single anchor is found to cover both the change verdict and the continuity convention, so the union split is unnecessary (breaks F1).

If R1, R2, R3, R4, R5 or R7 fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until R6 fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Split the question before searching. State which claim is a warrant verdict and which is a convention about shared meaning (F1). Do not average them.
2. Anchor warrant claims in per-person judgment of conduct, not in the society's reported sense of continuity (F2).
3. Treat continuity as lineage and arrangement, not as identity of content (F3).
4. Require a refusal operator: add a term that refuses, keep the old one in the record, and let the reach change (F4).
5. Carry methods and seeds across generations and measure the round-trip gap rather than assuming the method carried intact (F5, and Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").
6. Use precedent as a provenanced import, never as a veto (F7).
7. Keep the refuters in front of the links in any outreach, and report the specific-norm slot as a gap (Fig 9 :32).

## 7. Round-trip gap R_T, in brief

Sent up: the four-condition shape. Abstraction: "split verdict from convention; refuse without deleting; carry methods; keep the record; report a gap." Brought back down: the concrete demand "name the mechanism by which societies change norms while staying continuous." Three constraints do not survive the trip and are enumerated rather than smoothed: the per-slot anchoring of the verdict (it becomes an average), the prohibition on a convention deciding an outcome (it becomes tradition as veto), and the add-not-delete rule (it becomes replacement or erasure). One thing survives cleanly: continuity is the lineage and the method, not the unchanged norm list. R_T is recorded qualitatively, not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. The full round trip is in `11_audit.md`.
