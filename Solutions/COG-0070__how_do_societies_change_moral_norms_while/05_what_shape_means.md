## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *"Change happens while continuity is kept"* is a description of the phenomenon, not a shape.
- *"Four conditions with different anchors; refusal adds without deleting; the membrane carries methods; continuity is lineage"* is a shape. It says what any answer must look like without naming which norm changes in a given society.
- *"The norm that must change is X"* is a name, and is outside what the framework can return.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The same essay says to split a *"bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*, which is the move made in `01` §1.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" rather than the cause. The residual in `01` §0 is stated in effect terms: two reports arrive together, and a frame predicts they cannot.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question that means a source-discriminating reading of the consequences of a norm, not a recount of how many people endorse it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). Precedent and metanorms can be imported into a warrant claim this way (Wikipedia *Social norm*: Sandholtz on referencing *"existing foundational meta-norms, and... precedents"*), but the import must be recorded. Once it decides the outcome on its own it has become a convention that decides an outcome, which Fig 10 :47 forbids.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the distinction: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* A norm list is an output. The capacity to revise norms under new conditions is the generator. A society that transmits the first and loses the second has a recording, not a continuity of method.

## 4. Why "we are still us" is a source error in miniature

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The parallel here is exact in shape. A society reports "we are still us." That reading does not determine its source. The source could be an unbroken record of refused and re-settled norms (F3 and F4), or a shared backdrop that has drifted coherently so that nobody notices (F6), or a tradition constructed recently and presented as ancient (Wikipedia *Invented tradition*: *"appear or claim to be old are often quite recent in origin and sometimes invented"*). The felt continuity is one reading with several possible sources. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

The same essay's warning applies to this whole folder: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Until a discriminator exists, the specific case stays a gap (F9).

## 5. Q: what shape does this question need? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses the four-condition shape?** Nothing in the framework names a specific norm or a specific legitimate narrative. The shape is licensed by bindings onto operators and atoms already held: Fig 9 :54 on unbundling a union, Fig 6 :43 on refusal that adds without deleting, Fig 4 :22 on methods crossing a membrane, Fig 13 :128 on lineage, and Fig 2 :39 on the two valences of agreement. These are recombinations of held terms, not imports of new primitives.

**Does the folder commit the error the README forbids?** Not if it stays with the shape. If `07` or `08` were to name which norm a society ought to change, that would be a name where the framework returns a shape, and it would be the metamerism error of §4: a source named from a reading. The folder therefore keeps that slot open as a declared `gap`.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any mechanism at all. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Refusal, the membrane, the record and the anchor are already in the framework's operator and figure vocabulary. Naming them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is naming the **instance**, the specific norm or narrative, as if the framework had settled it.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason it stands is the missing discriminator among sources of the same felt reading, not a blanket ban on naming held operators. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected names to agree that a norm change is just is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that the outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any claim about any society, and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
