## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** The recipient checks the shape claim and the refuter against their own knowledge, without holding the framework's interiors. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, they may read the figures and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, never a witness. This is not a hedge: it follows from Fig 2 :40 and Fig 10 :42.
