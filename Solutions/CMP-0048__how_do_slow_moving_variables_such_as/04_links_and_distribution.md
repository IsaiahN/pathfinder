## What the wider literature changes, and what it does not

The world sources are secondary. They do not decide the verdict; the figures do.

1. **They name what the figures leave open.** The figures give a shape (accumulated remainder, loop sign, spendable difference, cascade, invisibility from inside). The world gives the name: saddle-node/fold bifurcation, critical slowing down, τ → ∞, breaking time and point of no return. This is a provenanced import, not a derivation. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
2. **They supply the only truly independent witness.** The ecology, mathematics, Earth-system and neuroscience sources do not share the framework author's evidence pool, so they carry more independent weight than the essays do. Their convergence with the shape is corroboration at low overlap; their internal conflict (Jiruska 2018 versus Wilkat 2019 versus Freestone 2020; Kéfi 2013 on specificity) is kept visible.
3. **They locate the break in the dependency chain.** The whole-ecosystem experiment (Carpenter 2011) shows indicators appearing more than a year before the shift, so the perception/objective link can work in some systems. The 2019 null result shows it fails in others. Both are D2 (instrument) findings and neither touches D1 or D3.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three source links. Their verdict on stage 1 must already be recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, optional:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 lists the five tests it must pass. Repair or withdraw before sending.
