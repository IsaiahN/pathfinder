## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not anchor a claim.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list of people chosen for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Agreement among readers who all learned the same tipping-point literature is one pool talking, not two witnesses.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing the outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
