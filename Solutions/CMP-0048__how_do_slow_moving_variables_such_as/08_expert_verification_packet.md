# Expert verification packet - the Tether on slow variables and sudden change

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecosystem-dynamics or complex-systems researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the flip is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A slow variable is a stock whose per-turn change is small but whose remainder is carried into the next turn, so the stock drifts while every individual step looks negligible. The abrupt flip comes when the loop that holds the current regime changes sign or spends its stored difference at a threshold. The flip is system-wide because influence cascades through the network, often several links from the slot that first crossed. Before the flip, an observer inside the frame cannot tell a robust regime from one about to fail, so detection has to be per slot and a null warning is not proof of safety. The figures give this shape and its ordered route; they do not name the specific bifurcation, and they do not license any particular early-warning statistic as universal.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and ecology-facing; checkable against a named system

- **Statement:** the slow stock is a remainder carried across turns, and the runaway form is a cycle whose loop sign decides whether it closes. Stress (cycle bond) and a regenerating resource (catalyst bond) are isomers: same atoms, different bond, decisive difference in behaviour.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :88-89 "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** in a system you know, does the stock's output re-enter as input, or is the part regenerated by its own use? Is each pass beginning from the prior remainder? Does the loop sign change at the flip?
- **Refuter:** a slow stock that changes with no remainder carried forward, or an abrupt flip with no loop-sign change and no stored difference spent. Either breaks C1. Refuter 1 and 2 in `01` §5.

### C2 - detection-facing; checkable against time series or an experiment

- **Statement:** detection must be per slot. A global average can stay near zero while one slot carries the live signal. Before the flip, depth and genuine approach look the same from inside, and a null warning result is not a refutation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 6 :27 "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** do per-slot indicators anticipate the shift better than an ecosystem-wide mean, scored before the shift? Does any statistic separate depth from approach without being calibrated on the shift? Note the published conflict: Jiruska et al. 2018 found progressive loss of resilience before seizures; Wilkat et al. 2019 found "no evidence for critical slowing down prior to 105 epileptic seizures"; Freestone et al. 2020 found a reliable indicator in 14 patients. The framework's verdict does not depend on which wins, because all three are D2 instrument claims.
- **Refuter:** a measurement in which the aggregate tracks the flip as well as or better than per-slot residuals, consistently before the flip; or an instrument that reliably separates depth from approach before the shift without using the shift to calibrate it. Refuter 4 and 5 in `01` §5.

### C3 - propagation-facing; checkable against spatially resolved observations

- **Statement:** the system-wide extent is a cascade, not a simultaneous event. A local crossing becomes a second-order effect, and the residual that matters can be several links out; each settled local flip becomes an atom for the next neighbour.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."
- **What to check:** in a recorded regime shift, which slot crossed first, and in what order did the rest follow? Is there a measurable influence path between them, or were the crossings independent and simultaneous?
- **Refuter:** a genuine simultaneous independent crossing in every slot with no network path. That breaks C3 and the "system-wide" part of the claim. Refuter 3 in `01` §5.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a derivation of a saddle-node, fold or Hopf bifurcation. That is a provenanced world import (Scheffer et al. 2009; Lenton et al. 2008; Li et al. 2019), not a figure result.
- Not that critical slowing down, variance, lag-1 autocorrelation or spatial correlation is a universal early-warning signal. The sources conflict and the figures do not decide statistics.
- Not a single global resilience number. Fig 1 :19, :22 require per-slot residuals.
- Not that all slow variables are the same kind. Cycle and catalyst are isomers under Fig 12 :117.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a final theory: no last route, no last room.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a green reading cannot name its source; the spectrometer did. Here:

1. Per-slot stock measurement before the flip, testing whether the remainder is carried forward.
2. Loop-sign and stored-difference measurement at the crossing.
3. Spatially resolved crossing order, with an influence path between slots.
4. Prospective score of aggregate versus per-slot indicators against the realised shift.
5. A depth-versus-approach discriminator calibrated without the later shift.

Partial ground already exists: Carpenter et al. 2011 (Science) found signals more than a year before a whole-ecosystem shift; Li et al. 2019 (Physica D) derived a salvage window between breaking time and point of no return. Neither settles the shape in general.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :88-89, Fig 12 :121, :117, Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 11 :52-53. Six short passages.
2. Pick one system you know. Name the stock, the loop sign and the first slot to cross.
3. Say which of C1, C2 or C3 would be easiest to refute there, and how.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
