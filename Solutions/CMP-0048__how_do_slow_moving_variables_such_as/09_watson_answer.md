## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a proportional prediction (smooth response to gradual forcing) and the observed abrupt, system-wide flip. Arity: at least two timescales, a slow stock and a fast response, plus a threshold; multiple slow stocks make it a union. Symmetry: asymmetric; the stock accumulates or depletes, the flip is one-way over the window. Scale: first order locally (one slot's remainder), second order globally (network cascade). Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Domain 1, the physical system.** Actors: slow stock, fast response variables, feedback loop, intervening structure. Currency: the accumulated quantity and its spendable difference. Anchor: a measured stock and an observed flip. Question type: union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 2, the observer / modeller frame.** Actors: model, monitor, early-warning statistics. Currency: residual R. Anchor: the realised shift, never the statistic. From inside, depth and genuine approach are indiscernible (Fig 6 :27). This is where the published early-warning conflict lives.
- **Domain 3, the propagation frame.** Actors: neighbouring slots, network links, cascade paths. Currency: influence travelling. Anchor: which slots flipped and in what order.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the slow drift before building statistics; refuse the catalyst reading before minting; settle each local flip before composing the cascade (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
- **Partition or union.** Union. A single lever clearing stress leaves the stress-plus-depletion intersection standing (Fig 9 :55). Verdicts below are kept per domain and never averaged.

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :31-32 says a compounding remainder "is chaos," but a critical transition is often a bifurcation. Re-census: the line governs compounding generally, not the specific post-threshold regime. The name is open (Fig 9 :32). No figure-to-figure contradiction remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the names and statistics do not survive as figure results. A slow variable is a remainder carried into each new turn, so small per-step changes accumulate. The flip occurs when the loop that holds the regime changes sign or spends its stored difference at a threshold, and it is system-wide because influence cascades through the network several links out. The approach is invisible from inside, so detection is per slot and a null warning is not proof of safety. Cycle and catalyst stocks are isomers, not synonyms. The specific bifurcation, the fold, the Hopf or the noise-induced transition, is a gap the figures declare rather than a name they supply (Fig 9 :32). The early-warning statistics are world imports with a live conflict inside D2; that conflict does not touch D1 or D3. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest checkable artifact is the refuter list in `01` §5 and `08` §2.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, derivation and five falsifiers before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a polished conclusion instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.") prices that. It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The imported names and statistics are removable without touching the D1 or D3 shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot stock measurement before the flip, a loop-sign or stored-difference measurement at the crossing, a spatially resolved crossing order, a prospective score of aggregate versus per-slot indicators, or a depth-versus-approach discriminator calibrated without the later shift, any of these could move the claim toward `grounded` or fire one of the five refuters. Agreement among readers, or a prestigious endorsement, is not such an instrument. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" prevents a null result from being mistaken for a reversal.
