# The Tether's answer: how slow variables create sudden system-wide change

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system appears stable while a stock drifts slowly, then flips abruptly and system-wide. A proportional model predicts smooth response; observation gives a discontinuous one. The gap is `R`, indexed by slot, not by global average (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It is a union compound, not one hard problem: an accumulating stock, a loop whose sign changes, a threshold, and a network that propagates are overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").

## 2. The shape, per domain

Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. An average would destroy the very signal in question (Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.").

### D1, the physical system - `general` structure, `specified` mechanism, triangulated

1. **The slow variable is a carried remainder.** Fig 13 :88-89: "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place." Each pass begins where the last ended, so a small per-turn change accumulates. Fig 12 :121 corroborates from a different figure: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
2. **The flip is a sign change or a spending of a stored difference.** Fig 13 :46-47: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." The stock is ordering-like (Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"); when the loop can no longer close or the stored difference is spent, the regime moves to a contrasting one. The specific name, fold, Hopf, or noise-induced, is **not** given by the figures (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"). It is a world import with provenance.
3. **The runaway and the regenerating stock are isomers.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Stress typically has the cycle bond; a replenished resource has the catalyst bond (Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."). Same ingredients, different behaviour.

### D2, the observer frame - `specified`, triangulated for structure, single-source and contested for instruments

4. **The approach is invisible from inside.** Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Before the flip, a robust regime and one about to fail look identical to an observer inside it. Fig 10 :24 adds the mechanism: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; what fails is the monitor ("stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings", Fig 10 :25-26), not the system.
5. **Detection must be per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global average can stay near zero while one slot carries the live signal. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." So a null early-warning result does not refute the shape, and a positive one does not fix the name; the published conflict (Jiruska 2018 against Wilkat 2019, with Freestone 2020 in between) is inside D2 and recorded.

### D3, the propagation frame - `specified`, triangulated

6. **System-wide means cascade.** Fig 11 :52-53: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 13 :24 corroborates: "that entities relate, and influence travels." A local crossing becomes a second-order effect, and the residual that matters can be several links out. The order is sequence then production then cascade (Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one.").

**Never averaged.** D1's accumulation, D2's detection and D3's cascade have different anchors and different currencies. Folding them into one "resilience index" is Fig 1 :22's error.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures derive a saddle-node bifurcation. That is a world import.
- Not that critical slowing down, variance, or autocorrelation is a universal warning signal. The sources conflict and the figures do not adjudicate statistics.
- Not that a single global score measures resilience. Per-slot residual, never an aggregate.
- Not that the slow variable is always the same kind. Stress (cycle) and depleting resource (subtraction plus slow regeneration) are isomers, not synonyms.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,φ)` for any particular system.
- Not a final theory. Fig 13 :90: "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route." The answer stops at the instrument limit and records the lineage.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a spectrometer, not a debate, resolved which mixture produced a green reading. Here the corresponding tests are:

- Measure the stock per slot, before the flip, and test whether each slot's remainder is carried into the next turn (refuter 1).
- Identify the loop sign and the stored difference in a named system, and test whether they change at the crossing (refuter 2).
- Trace which slots flipped and in what order, to test the cascade directly (refuter 3).
- Compare aggregate and per-slot indicators measured before the flip and scored against the realised shift (refuter 4).
- Try to separate depth from genuine approach before the shift, without calibrating on the shift (refuter 5).

The world already supplies partial ground: Carpenter et al. 2011 found warning signals more than a year before a whole-ecosystem shift; Li et al. 2019 derived a salvage window between breaking time and point of no return. Neither settles the shape in general.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a new bifurcation and does not claim a universal early-warning test. It says that a slow variable is a remainder carried into the next turn, so a tiny per-step change accumulates; that the sudden flip comes when the loop that holds the regime changes sign or spends its stored difference; and that the flip is system-wide because influence cascades through the network, often several links from the slot that first crossed. Before the flip, an observer inside the system cannot tell a robust regime from one about to fail, so detection has to be per slot and a null warning is not a proof of safety. The framework gives the shape and the ordered route, refuses to name the specific transition, and records five refuters written in advance. The mathematical names and the early-warning statistics are imports from the literature with their conflicts intact, not figure results. The claim stays `specified` until an instrument, not an argument, settles it.
