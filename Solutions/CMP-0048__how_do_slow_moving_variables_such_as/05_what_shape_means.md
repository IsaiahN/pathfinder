## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this pass): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Filled name:** "the system undergoes a saddle-node bifurcation at phosphorus concentration X."
- **Shape:** "a stock accumulates across turns, a loop changes sign or spends a stored difference at a threshold, and the local change cascades through the network; the approach is invisible from inside until it fires."

The second is what the figures license. The first is a world import with a provenance record.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"; then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The recursive bootstrapping is the method, not the answer.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this pass): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly the outreach ordering in `02`: describe the effect (rising per-slot variance, slowing return, cascade order), let the recipient check it, and withhold the source until their verdict is recorded.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And the paradox: "By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (gradual forcing, abrupt system-wide flip, invisible approach) before any frame was chosen. Only then were the saddle-node and early-warning literatures imported. That order is the discipline; reversing it would make any field seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question the ground is a measured threshold and an observed flip, plus a test of whether an indicator anticipated it before the fact.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The fold bifurcation and the critical-slowing statistics are such imports. They are recorded with author, venue, year and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`; they are not presented as figure derivations.

*They're Scanning Books* (mapped, not opened this pass) sharpens this in the exemplar: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." That line is cited here from the exemplar's record only, not as a quote I verified this session.

## 4. Source discrimination: why the name is owed to an instrument, not an argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this pass) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes create identical readings: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," the spectrometer.

For this question the same error appears when someone reads a rising variance or a slowing return and names its source as "an approaching fold." Several paths produce that reading: an approaching catastrophic bifurcation, a non-catastrophic loss of stability (Kéfi 2013), a spatially heterogeneous pressure, or a change in the observation channel itself (Fig 10 :25-26). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" is the matching limit on null results.

## 5. What "shape" means for this question specifically

| level | statement | mode |
|---|---|---|

## 6. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming the mathematical transition. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in the library. A fold bifurcation is not a missing primitive of the six-term substrate; it is a term from the world frame. So the correct statement is:

> The framework does not derive the name, and it does not forbid naming it. It requires that a name arrive either by a ground settlement or as a provenanced import, and it forbids presenting an import as a derivation (Fig 8 :50-52).

Both readings are recorded so the correction is visible. This is the same correction the exemplar made in `05` §5b about Gradient, applied to a different object.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this pass): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuters.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle it. Its agreement with the world literature is corroboration only, and where the essays share the author with the figures the overlap is high and the corroboration weak.
