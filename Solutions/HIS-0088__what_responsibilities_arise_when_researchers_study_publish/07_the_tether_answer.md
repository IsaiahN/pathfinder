# The Tether's answer: responsibilities to community-held knowledge

**Mode: `specified` for the six obligations, `general` for the structural rules. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A research frame acts on knowledge that lives in another closure. It predicts data with no anchor and no provenance obligation; the ground returns harm, decontextualization, broken reciprocity and loss of control. R is indexed per community and per knowledge item, never aggregated into a net-benefit ledger: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19)

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **The community is the anchor, not an instrument.** "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." (Fig 2 :36) "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." (Fig 2 :37) The community's verdict must not move when the researcher's prestige or framing changes; otherwise it is a frame, not an anchor.

2. **The habitat must be enumerated before acting.** "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." (Fig 11 :43) "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." (Fig 11 :44) "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." (Fig 11 :39) The item, its holders, its place, its season, its transmission relations and its protocols are one term. Dropping them is first-order enumeration that misses the third-order residual.

3. **Provenance is the term, not a footnote.** "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." (Fig 13 :130) Holder, place, protocol, prior informed consent and mutually agreed terms travel with the knowledge or the knowledge does not travel. "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right" (Fig 10 :65), so provenance is necessary but the community ground still decides.

4. **Refusal must be real.** "Three panels, one move: arrange for refusal." (Fig 10 :82) "A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate." (Fig 12 :140) Consent that cannot register a no, or cannot be withdrawn, is a process that can be corrected but cannot carry the correction.

5. **Stakes declared, prestige excluded.** "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged." (Fig 10 :60) "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." (Fig 1 :27) Prestige, citation count and committee size can fund a trial; they cannot settle responsibility.

6. **Study, publish and commercialize are a union of three loops.** "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55) One lever clears only where one condition holds; the intersections are specific compounds with their own loops. The two accounts stand as isomers over the full shared atom set, or as a functional group as currently written (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named protocol (CARE, OCAP, Nagoya, FPIC) is the answer. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). Naming one is a provenanced binding from a world frame, not a derivation.
- Not that publication credit or attribution discharges the obligation. Attribution without habitat, refusal and benefit is a recording carried upward, not a generator.
- Not that the community is an outside consultant to be informed after the design is fixed. It is the anchor for a question constitutively about it.
- Not that absence of a figure rule means absence of an obligation. "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." (Fig 8 :39)
- Not `grounded`. No community adjudication, measurement or benefit-sharing record was examined here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground reading, not more agreement. Concretely: a community's own adjudication of harm and benefit; an instrument that discriminates whether a published account carries the holder, protocol and relation, or only the atom; a provenance record that survives the move from habitat to product; a functioning withdrawal. "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." (Fig 2 :45)

## 5. Standing falsifiers

The six in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, written before the run: (1) a community anchor that moves with the researcher's prestige; (2) a knowledge claim invariant under removal of habitat; (3) control unaffected by erasing provenance; (4) a sound result with no way to refuse; (5) lossless rearrangement into a paper or patent; (6) a figure line selecting one protocol. Any one fires and the corresponding obligation is withdrawn.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns the shape and stops where the instrument runs out. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134) and "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." (Fig 4 :30) The round-trip gap for this condensation is enumerated in `11_audit.md`. It is not set to zero. Which specific protocol would satisfy a given community is left to that community's ground.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a governance protocol and does not claim to settle any ethics dispute. It says that when researchers touch community-held knowledge, the community is the anchor for any question about responsibilities to it; that the knowledge is a property of holders and habitat, not of the extracted item; that provenance is part of the term itself, so holder, place, protocol and agreed benefit travel with it; that consent must be able to register a real refusal; that prestige and publication metrics can fund a trial but cannot settle responsibility; and that study, publish and commercialize are three overlapping loops over one habitat, not one action with one rule. What it offers is the shape any acceptable answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would close it. The specific form is left open until the community ground settles it.
