## 0. The residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Fig 1 :19). A research frame Γ_r holds academic categories, methods, publication norms and patent law. It acts on knowledge that lives in a different closure: the community's habitat of holders, practitioners, place, season, transmission relations and protocols. The frame predicts the knowledge is extractable data with no anchor and no provenance obligation (b). The action is study, publish, or commercialize (a). The observation at the community slots is harm, decontextualization, broken reciprocity and loss of control (o′). R persists and does not trend down when the frame keeps producing the same mismatch: "we documented it and credited it, so the obligation is discharged," while the ground keeps returning the same loss.

- R is indexed per slot, never aggregated. "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19) "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." (Fig 1 :22) A net-benefit ledger that sums publication gain against community loss throws away the only signal that says where to look.
- Frame-internal quantities are disqualified as ground. "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." (Fig 1 :27) Citation count, impact factor and number of "beneficiaries" are produced by the research frame, so they cannot settle whether the research was responsible.
- The question is what the loop owes when R persists. Fig 5 :53 to :56 names a fifth kind of gap: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* The held term here is "knowledge as free-floating, decontextualized data." What is owed first is a refusal, not a new method.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction").** An answer is a set of constraints any legitimate contact between a research frame and community knowledge must satisfy, statable before naming any specific protocol, and refusable by the same test as anything else. I write that shape down here so it can accrue a residual rather than hiding as a preference.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: a multi-place relation (researcher, knowledge item, community habitat, action, ground outcome). The actions are ordered, not interchangeable: study → publish → commercialize is a sequence/production chain, and at each step a new artifact appears (Fig 12 :75 to :79, production ⇒). Symmetry: the test of "responsible" is a threshold comparison ⋛ against community ground, not a symmetric agreement ≡ between researcher and community (Fig 12 :85 to :89). Treating consent as equivalence is a bond error. Scale: the individual researcher loop, the community habitat, and the institutional or commercial stratum; the membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4 :27 to :30).

**One line per domain. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Research frame | researchers, Γ_r, methods, institutions, peer review | publications, credibility, citations | empirical ground (measurement, experiment) | admitted; credibility is frame-internal and excluded from verdict weight |
| D2 Community knowledge habitat | knowledge holders, practitioners, place, season, relations of transmission, protocols | survival and transmission of the practice, collective benefit | the community's ground: the reality the knowledge tracks and the people it serves | admitted; legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36) |
| D3 Verification and consent arrangement | ethics boards, community review, researchers | agreement versus residual | the ground, never agreement | admitted; failure mode is a board that negotiates |
| D4 Publication and commercialization channel | journals, publishers, funders, firms, patents | reach, precedence, profit | the ground: provenance intact, community benefits | admitted; channel decays (Fig 10 :24) |
| X1 "Knowledge as free-floating commodity" | a patent claim with no holder, no place, no protocol | legal title | none nameable: the claim's "ground" is the institution that grants it, which moves when pushed | not a domain yet; refused |
| X2 "Impact as the sole measure of responsibility" | metric, index | citation count | already inside D1 and frame-internal | absorbed, not admitted as a separate anchor |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Study, publish and commercialize each contact D2, and they overlap; "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55) A single lever (one consent form) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds (Fig 9 :56 to :57). Each of the three needs its own loop sharing D2.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The chain is "ordered by dependency, not by importance." (Fig 3 :20) Perception (recognize community knowledge as knowledge, not as data) → vocabulary (terms for what a situation offers) → objective (what counts as progress, and for whom) → planning (consent, benefit sharing, governance, authorship) → learn and carry (provenance in the record). The likely break is the objective link: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." (Fig 3 :27) "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." (Fig 3 :19)

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1. The community is a legitimate anchor, not an instrument (decisive for D3)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
Fig 2 :37: **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."**
Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."**

The question "what responsibilities arise to communities" is constitutively about people, so community judgment is the right anchor, not merely an instrument. An ethics board that negotiates a comfortable agreement between researcher and community has replaced triangulation with panel 1 of Fig 2, where "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence." The anchor must not update: if the community's answer changes when the researcher's prestige or summary changes, it is a frame, not an anchor.

- Mode: `specified` for the application, `general` for the anchor rule.
- Triangulation: **triangulated** across the anchor definition (Fig 2 :29) and the constitutive-legitimacy clause (Fig 2 :36 to :37), with low internal overlap, plus the ranging rule (Fig 2 :45).
- Refuter: a case where the community's verdict on harm or benefit tracks the researcher's reputation rather than the community's own ground, so the supposed anchor moves when pushed. If it fires, D3 has no anchor and the consent verdict is void.

### F2. Knowledge is a property of holder-and-habitat, never of the extracted item (decisive for D2)

Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
Fig 11 :52 to :53: **"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**

A medicinal use, a crop variety or a navigation route is an item. Its validity and bearing live in the habitat: the practitioners, the harvesting rules, the season, the ecosystem relations, the restrictions on who may use it. Publishing the item while dropping the habitat is first-order enumeration that misses the residual three steps out. The world source on traditional ecological knowledge corroborates this at low overlap: TEK is *"a cumulative body of knowledge, practice, and belief, evolving by adaptive processes and handed down through generations by cultural transmission"* and is *"a family of culturally situated rules and practices rooted in specific places and ecological worldviews"* (Wikipedia, drawing on Berkes, opened 2026-11-23).

- Mode: `general` for the habitat rule, `specified` for community knowledge.
- Triangulation: **triangulated** across Fig 11 (habitat enumeration) and Fig 4 (the membrane, where "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change" (Fig 4 :20)); the ecology source corroborates the shape but shares no evidence pool with the figures.
- Refuter: a knowledge claim whose validity and bearing are invariant under removal of place, practitioners and transmission relations. If it fires, the habitat obligation is empty.

### F3. Isolation substitutes a habitat; a lab result proves wiring, not capability

Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**
Fig 11 :67: **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."**
Fig 11 :68: **"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"**
Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Moving community knowledge into a journal, a database, a laboratory assay or a product is a habitat substitution, not a neutral relocation. "What you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" (Fig 11 :68). A validated compound in a lab establishes that the molecule works in that harness; it does not establish that the knowledge system which selected, prepared and governed it carries over. The community-of-practice source puts the same point in cognition and knowledge-management terms at low overlap: *"Tacit knowledge is considered to be valuable context-based experiences that cannot easily be captured, codified and stored"* (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23).

- Mode: `specified`. Triangulation: **triangulated** (Fig 11 :66 to :70 with the Fig 4 lossy membrane); the cognition source is an independent witness for the mechanism shape.
- Refuter: a demonstration that the lab or publication result reproduces the full habitat relation, including the actors the new habitat did not choose, so that nothing was lost at the boundary.

### F4. Provenance is the term, not documentation attached to it (decisive for commercialization)

Fig 13 :130: **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."**
Fig 13 :127: **"The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable."**
Fig 10 :62: **"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."**
Fig 10 :65: **"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right"**

Stripping provenance (filing a patent as if the knowledge were newly discovered, publishing without holders or protocol) does not merely misattribute. It removes the bonds that make the item the knowledge it is. Fig 6 names the legitimate route: **"source gate: provenance"** (Fig 6 :12) and **"shadow test: explains a gap"** (Fig 6 :13), with "the only operator that moves the wall" (Fig 6 :14) being import. The Nagoya Protocol's "prior informed consent and mutually agreed terms" and "fair and equitable sharing of benefits arising from the utilization of genetic resources" (CBD, opened 2026-11-23) are the world-frame form of this source gate. Fig 12 :107 states the cost asymmetry: **"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."** The community paid the establishing cost over generations; a lineage that drops the record does not re-pay it, it simply does not carry the derivation.

- Mode: `general` for the lineage rule, `specified` for benefit sharing.
- Triangulation: **triangulated** across Fig 13, Fig 10 and Fig 6, three distinct offices; Nagoya corroborates at low overlap.
- Refuter: a case where a knowledge claim's validity and rightful control are unaffected by erasing its origin, holder and protocol. If it fires, the provenance obligation collapses.

### F5. Refusal must be structurally possible (decisive for "consent")

Fig 10 :82: **"Three panels, one move: arrange for refusal."**
Fig 12 :140: **"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."**
Fig 5 :56 (amendment): *"Requires an operator for negation, which the seven bonds do not contain."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*

Free, prior and informed consent is structurally a negation operator: the community must be able to refuse a term being composed over its knowledge. A process that treats silence as consent, or consent as a one-time signature with no withdrawal, is a frame with only the accumulating bonds. It can be corrected ("we will add a benefit-sharing clause") but it cannot carry the correction, because it cannot say no. The Nagoya Protocol's "prior informed consent" (CBD, opened 2026-11-23) and OCAP's statement that "First Nations alone have control over data collection processes in their communities, and that they own and control how this information can be stored, interpreted, used, or shared" (FNIGC, opened 2026-11-23) are world-frame instances of this refusal standing.

- Mode: `general` for the operator requirement, `specified` for consent processes.
- Triangulation: **triangulated** across Fig 10 (seat design), Fig 12 (bond algebra) and the Operators table (negation), which are low-overlap routes.
- Refuter: a research protocol that reaches a ground-settled result while offering the community no mechanism to refuse or withdraw. If it fires, this obligation is decorative.

### F6. Same observations, different bond: the arrangement is the substance (D1 versus D2)

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**
Fig 12 :95: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing."**
Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

A scientific paper and a community account may contain the same observations (the atoms) while differing entirely in bonds: proprietary versus communal, experiment versus apprenticeship, publication versus oral transmission, individual author versus collective holder. Under Fig 12 they are different substances, and the notation (a data table, a patent claim) cannot show what was lost. Flattening community knowledge into variables is the "flat formula" collapse of Fig 12 :6 to :17: *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"* and *"nothing signals that anything was lost."* The TEK source records the same hazard at low overlap: *"the same methods that could resolve the issue of documentation to meet legal requirements may compromise the very nature of traditional knowledge"* (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23).

- Mode: `specified`. Triangulation: **single-source** for the figure definition (Fig 12), with the ecology source as an independent witness for the hazard.
- Refuter: a demonstration that for this knowledge the bond carries no information beyond the atoms, so rearrangement is lossless. If it fires, the isomer reading fails.

### F7. Prestige and agreement cannot settle a responsibility dispute

Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**
Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."**
Fig 10 :60: **"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

The essay opened this session is explicit: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A prestigious journal, a famous reviewer or a large ethics committee gives a claim a budget to be tried; it does not settle whether the research was responsible. Where a commercial stake cannot be removed it must be declared, because an undeclared interest is modelled anyway. Independence must be bought in proportion to a cost borne by communities, which is high.

- Mode: `general` for the rule, `specified` for research settings. Triangulation: **triangulated** across Fig 1, Fig 10 and Fig 2; the essay is framework-internal and adds no independent weight.
- Refuter: a case where citation count, institutional prestige or committee size settles an ethics dispute at the ground without any community adjudication.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds. Written before the run as a design instrument (seat map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"*).

1. A community anchor is shown to move with the researcher's prestige or framing, so it is a frame rather than an anchor (breaks F1).
2. A knowledge claim is shown to retain its full validity and bearing with place, holders and transmission relations removed (breaks F2 and F3).
3. A knowledge claim's rightful control is shown to be unaffected by erasing origin, holder and protocol (breaks F4).
4. A research process reaches a ground-settled result while providing no way for the community to refuse or withdraw (breaks F5).
5. Re-arranging community observations into a publication or patent is shown to be lossless in the arrangement (breaks F6).
6. A figure line is found that directly selects one governance protocol after re-census (breaks F8, and the gap becomes a named term).

If any fires, the corresponding obligation is withdrawn and the audit records the retraction rather than softening the reading.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Treat the community as the anchor for questions constitutively about the community, and keep the anchor from updating (F1).
2. Enumerate the habitat outward before acting, including cascades three steps out, not just the extractable item (F2, F3).
3. Put provenance inside the term: holder, place, protocol, consent, mutually agreed terms, benefit sharing. Do not treat attribution as a footnote (F4).
4. Install a real negation: refusal and withdrawal must be possible and effective (F5).
5. Declare commercial and career stakes; do not let prestige or committee size stand in for the ground (F7).
6. Describe the residual before selecting a governance frame; keep study, publish and commercialize as three loops over one shared habitat (F6, F8).
7. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Send the generator (the shape and its refuters), not a recording of a conclusion.
