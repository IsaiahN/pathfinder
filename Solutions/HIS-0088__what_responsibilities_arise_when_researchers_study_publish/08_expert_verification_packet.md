# Expert verification packet — responsibilities to community-held knowledge

**What this is:** a claim in a form a research ethicist, Indigenous data-governance practitioner or historian of knowledge can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` for the six obligations, `general` for the structural rules. It is not `grounded`.** No measurement or community adjudication is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Any legitimate contact between a research frame and community-held knowledge must satisfy six constraints that can be stated before naming a governance protocol. The community is the anchor for questions constitutively about it. The knowledge is a property of holders and habitat, not of the extracted item. Provenance (holder, place, protocol, prior informed consent, agreed benefit) is part of the term itself. Refusal must be possible and effective. Stakes must be declared and prestige excluded from the verdict. Study, publish and commercialize are three overlapping loops over one habitat, not one action with one rule. The framework returns a shape, not a name; which protocol satisfies it is left to the community ground.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 2

- **Statement:** the community is a legitimate anchor here, not an instrument, so its answer must not move with the researcher's framing or prestige.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Also Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." And Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- **Refuter:** show a community verdict on harm or benefit that tracks the researcher's reputation rather than the community's own ground, so the supposed anchor moves when pushed. If it fires, the consent arrangement has no anchor and F1 is withdrawn.

### C2 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 13

- **Statement:** knowledge is a property of holder-and-habitat, and provenance is the term itself, so extracting the item while dropping place, holders, transmission relations and protocol produces a different substance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 13 :130 "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."
- **Refuter:** show a knowledge claim that keeps its full validity and bearing with habitat removed, or whose rightful control is unaffected by erasing origin, holder and protocol. If it fires, the habitat and provenance obligations are withdrawn.

### C3 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 10, Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** refusal is structural. Consent that cannot register a no and cannot be withdrawn is a process that can be corrected but cannot carry the correction; and prestige cannot settle the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :82 "Three panels, one move: arrange for refusal." Fig 12 :140 "A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate." Fig 10 :60 "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."
- **Refuter:** show a research process that reaches a ground-settled result while offering the community no way to refuse or withdraw, or a case where citation count or institutional prestige settles an ethics dispute at the ground without community adjudication. If it fires, F5 and F7 are withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named protocol (CARE, OCAP, Nagoya, FPIC) is the answer. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Naming one is a binding from a world frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that publication credit, an ethics approval number or attribution discharges the obligation. These are recordings of one occasion, not generators.
- Not that the community is a stakeholder to be consulted after the design is fixed. For this question it is the anchor.
- Not that a literature search finding no rule means no obligation. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that this derivation can validate itself.
- Not a ranking of communities, disciplines or reviewers by likely agreement. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat map :163)

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. In particular: community adjudication of harm and benefit; an instrument that discriminates whether a published account carries holder, protocol and relation or only the atom; a provenance record that survives the move from habitat to product; a functioning withdrawal. The essay "The Hard Problem is About Source" states the general form: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :35 to :37, Fig 11 :39 to :44, Fig 13 :130, Fig 10 :82 and Fig 12 :140. Six short passages.
2. Check C1 against your own ethics-review practice: is there a ground that does not move when the researcher reframes the question?
3. Check C2 against one case you know: did the published account carry the habitat and the protocol, or only the observation? Name what was lost.
4. Name one instrument or record that would let the community register refusal after publication or commercialization has begun, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
