## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of communities or reviewers by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a particular governance protocol (CARE, OCAP, Nagoya, FPIC) is the one the figures select. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not the anchor.** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35) Selecting people for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must stay outside.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42) Sending the whole framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.
3. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.** Fig 2 :42. Here the cost is borne by communities, so the check must come from frames that do not share the researcher's evidence pool.
