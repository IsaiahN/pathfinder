## QUESTION

What responsibilities arise when researchers study, publish, or commercialize knowledge held by communities? Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| persist at the community slots when a research frame Γ_r treats community knowledge as extractable data with no anchor and no provenance obligation? R is indexed per community and per knowledge item, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Option A: say the obligations are discharged by attribution, ethics approval and publication credit. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse that claim. A sends a `general`-mode verdict with frame-internal quantities mistaken for ground; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an open slot until a community ground settles the specific protocol.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: multi-place (researcher, knowledge item, community habitat, action, ground outcome); actions are ordered, study → publish ⇒ commercialize, each producing a new artifact. Symmetry: the test of responsibility is a threshold comparison ⋛, not a symmetric agreement ≡ (Fig 12 :85 to :89). Scale: individual researcher loop, community habitat, institutional or commercial stratum, with a lossy membrane and a round-trip gap R_T (Fig 4).
- **D1 Research frame.** Actors: researchers, Γ_r, methods, peer review. Currency: publications, credibility, citations. Anchor: empirical ground. Credibility is frame-internal and excluded from verdict weight.
- **D2 Community knowledge habitat.** Actors: holders, practitioners, place, season, transmission relations, protocols. Currency: survival and transmission of the practice, collective benefit. Anchor: the community's ground, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36 to :37).
- **D3 Verification and consent arrangement.** Actors: ethics boards, community review, researchers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Failure mode: a board that negotiates.
- **D4 Publication and commercialization channel.** Actors: journals, publishers, funders, firms, patents. Currency: reach, precedence, profit. Anchor: the ground, provenance intact and community benefit. The channel decays: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24).
- **X1 Knowledge as free-floating commodity.** A patent claim with no holder, place or protocol has no nameable anchor, its "ground" being the institution that grants it. Not a domain yet; refused.
- **X2 Impact as sole measure.** Absorbed into D1; frame-internal and disqualified.

Union, not partition: study, publish and commercialize each contact D2 and overlap (Fig 9 :55 to :57). Dependency order: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3), with the objective link (what counts as progress, for whom) the unmeasured break (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test applied: Fig 1 disqualifying frame-internal metrics and Fig 11 locating capability in the habitat govern different quantities, so no strain after re-census. Fig 9's shape-not-a-name limit and the world-frame naming of CARE, OCAP and Nagoya also do not strain: one governs what the framework returns, the other is a provenanced binding from a different closure (Fig 8 :51).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific protocol is undetermined. A legitimate contact between a research frame and community knowledge must treat the community as the anchor for questions about it, enumerate the habitat rather than extract the item, carry provenance inside the term, make refusal effective, declare stakes and exclude prestige, and handle study, publish and commercialize as three overlapping loops. The two accounts stand as isomers over the full shared atom set, or as a functional group as written. Which named protocol satisfies these constraints is a `gap` left to a community ground reading. Mode `specified` for the obligations, `general` for the structural rules. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a finished protocol endorsement) instead of a generator (the shape and its refuters). Fig 2 :42 prices this: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Here the cost falls on communities, so it is high and avoidable by leading with the shape claim.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a refuter fires; the refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42 to :43, "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."). The six obligations are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62 to :64).
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Community adjudication showing the anchor moves with researcher prestige; an instrument showing the knowledge is invariant under habitat removal; a case where control is unaffected by erased provenance; a sound result with no way to refuse; a lossless rearrangement into a paper or patent; or a figure line selecting one protocol after re-census. Any of the six pre-registered falsifiers reverses the corresponding obligation.
