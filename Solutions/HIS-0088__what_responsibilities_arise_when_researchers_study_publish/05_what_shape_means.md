## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And from *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the six obligations in `01`: community as anchor, habitat enumerated, provenance in the term, effective refusal, declared stakes, and the union of three loops. "Adopt CARE" is a name. "The holder and protocol travel with the term" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet leads with refuters stated in effect terms rather than with the framework's vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, and by the time you have the exact terms you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The same essay warns that a problem may be *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"* and must be searched individually. That is the union reading of study, publish and commercialize in `06`.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the community is not a "source" to be read off a projection

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape: the ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism of the error this question is prone to: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay's conclusion is: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Applying that to community knowledge: reading a publication or a patent and naming the community's relation to the knowledge from that reading is the metamerism error. A better instrument (community adjudication, provenance, a benefit-sharing record) is owed, not a name inferred from the artifact.

Fig 13 :130: **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."** The lineage is the knowledge.

## 4. Tacit knowledge and why the habitat cannot be dropped

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* It quotes Polanyi: *"we can know more than we can tell."* A community's knowledge is exactly this: much of it is context, subtext and pretext that a paper cannot carry. Fig 11 :40 says **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* restates the habitat rule: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* And: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A published description is a recording, not a method the receiver can reapply without the habitat.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* In this question the ground is the community and the reality its knowledge tracks. A prestigious journal, a famous reviewer or a large committee gives a claim a budget to be tried; it does not settle responsibility. Where a commercial stake cannot be removed it must be declared.

The same essay records the second half of the canonical reading: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* Agreement within a research frame, or between a researcher and a community that has only seen the researcher's frame, is not the check.

## 6. The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Applied here: the six obligations in `01` are a figure-derived shape, not a description of how every community actually governs knowledge. "Declaring them does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." If a community's own protocol differs, that is a binding from a different frame, to be provenanced, not an error to be smoothed away.

The same essay names the legitimate way in: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Community knowledge can import a new axis into a research frame. It cannot be reduced to a new variable inside the frame's existing axes.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the responsibilities, and it cannot settle them. *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the caveat directly: an LLM demo *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* This is why the packet asks for a refutation written before the theory is shown, and why no part of this folder is labelled `grounded`.
