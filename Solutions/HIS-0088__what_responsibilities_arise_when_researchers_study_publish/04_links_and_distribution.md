## What these sources add, and where they overlap

- **One evidence pool, not several.** CARE, OCAP, Nagoya, UNDRIP and the GIDA page all sit in the Indigenous-rights and data-governance pool. They agree strongly, but by Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* They count as one witness for the shape, not five.
- **Two genuinely low-overlap witnesses.** Traditional ecological knowledge (ecology and science studies) and community of practice (cognition, education and knowledge management) arrive from different fields. They corroborate the mechanism shape: knowledge is situated in a habitat and a community, and tacit, contextual knowledge is not captured by codification. Neither is a measurement of the figure verdict.
- **A live tension in the world pool, not a conflict with the figures.** CARE promotes open data sharing under "Be FAIR and CARE," while the TEK page notes that *"the same methods that could resolve the issue of documentation to meet legal requirements may compromise the very nature of traditional knowledge."* By the figures this is a lossy transform question, not a flip of the verdict: the record that travels upward is a fixed-up compressed description, and Fig 4 :30 requires its round-trip gap to be measured rather than assumed zero. The conflict rule was not triggered; the tension was returned to the census and recorded.
- **No source conflicts with the figure verdict.** Where a source seemed to strain (open data versus protection), re-census showed it governs the channel, not the anchor.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the six constraints and the refuter; offer the links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :42** — *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is borne by communities, so the check must come from frames that do not share the researcher's evidence pool. One-to-one contact only, never a bulk send.
- **Seat map :163** — *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the six constraints and their refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a verdict on the shape. Do not ask for trust, do not ask for agreement. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure text and the four world sources above. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. If a refuter fires, the corresponding obligation is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` before anything is sent.
