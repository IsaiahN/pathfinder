## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about measuring resilience so that the measurement works in forests, oceans, grasslands and other systems. Option A: one scalar index whose number is comparable across systems. Option B: one transferable protocol that reads a per-slot residual against a system-specific ground and reports the round-trip gap. What each leads to: A gives a convention that can rank systems but hides the live failing slot and spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives separate checkable verdicts, an honest account of what was lost in transfer, and four falsifiers written in advance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a resilience measure predicts about recovery and what the ground returns after a real disturbance. Arity: indexed by slot (system, function at risk, disturbance regime, scale), not one number. Symmetry: asymmetric, because a reading is relative to a function and a disturbance and an actor's valence changes with the residual. Scale: first order within one system at one scale, second order across scales, because recovery at a focal scale depends on states above and below it.
- **Domain 1, measurement and instrumentation.** Actors: index, instrument, slots, disturbance. Currency: residuals, bits of description, actions spent in the field. Anchor: the actual recovery outcome after a disturbance. Question type: partition within a system across slots, union across systems.
- **Domain 2, the ecological habitat.** Actors: species, functions, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition), and a manager where one exists. Currency: survival and recovery of the function at risk. Anchor: what recovery actually happened. Question type: union of overlapping systems, enumerated never composed.
- **Domain 3, cross-system transfer and comparison.** Actors: forest, ocean, grassland, and their intersections. Currency: comparability of readings and what is lost to make them comparable. Anchor: none shared; each system has its own ground. Question type: union, so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the recovery trajectory, acquire terms for what the system affords under the disturbance, state what counts as progress, then choose a route to a metric (Fig 3). Refuse the scalar before minting a new index (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Scheffer et al.'s generic early-warning signals are a pattern read within one system's time series, not a scalar compared across systems. Re-census resolves the apparent conflict. The world source governs a different quantity, so the figure verdict does not flip (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures refuse Option A and license Option B as a shape, not a measurement result. A single cross-system scalar is the aggregation that destroys the live slot (Fig 1 :19, :22, :28) and is a convention rather than a verdict (Fig 2 :40-41). What transfers is the kernel, not a recorded value (Fig 4 :19-22). Forest, ocean and grassland form a union, so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54). The anchor is a system-specific recovery ground that must not update (Fig 2 :29-33, Fig 11 :73). Indicators have no standing valence (Fig 11 :50-51). Cross-system claims owe a measured round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30-32). Resilience is a remainder, not a seventh primitive (Fig 13 :124-125). All of this is `general` structural and `specified` falsifiable, ungrounded, candidate. The four refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a table of scores) instead of a generator (the protocol and refuter), which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar claim is removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A published claim can be superseded by a corrected protocol, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that reads recovery across systems without hiding the live slot; a demonstration that a scalar is itself a slot-level anchor that settles recovery without retuning; a proof that the three systems partition; or a transfer with R_T = 0 would each break the shape claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
