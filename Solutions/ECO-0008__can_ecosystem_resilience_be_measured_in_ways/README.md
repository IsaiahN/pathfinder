# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can ecosystem resilience be measured in ways that work across forests, oceans, grasslands, and other systems?

**Source:** ecology.txt line 11

**Section:** 1. Biodiversity, stability, and resilience

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the falsifiable predictions. Nothing here is `grounded`: this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What this package decides, in effect terms

Two options. Option A: one scalar resilience index whose number is comparable across a forest, an ocean and a grassland. Option B: a transferable protocol that measures a residual per slot against a ground each system defines for itself, with the round-trip gap recorded. A leads to a convention that can rank but cannot settle, and to an average in which the one failing system disappears. B leads to separate, checkable verdicts and an honest account of what did not survive the transfer.

The figures decide B. What travels is the kernel, not the recording. No universal scalar is licensed.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

A reply from any venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is a correction on shape, not corroboration. It is never pooled as a second witness.

## Honest standing

The second seat in `11_audit.md` is simulated and reads the same evidence pool. Its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. No instrument was run, no recovery trajectory was measured, and no expert has replied. The verdict is a candidate, not a settlement.
