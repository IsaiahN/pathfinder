# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the microstates

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you can state the residual and its refuter without opening the framework. If the stage-1 claim cannot survive on its own, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about black hole entropy, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [your name]. I have one specific claim about black hole entropy that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual.** Bekenstein and Hawking gave us a macro reading: entropy proportional to horizon area, `S = A/4`. That reading is not in question here. What is in question is its microscopic source, the configurations being counted. The area reading is a projection. It does not tell us which source produced it.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Any microscopic account must supply three things, and the six-term substrate says each is load-bearing for the second law: a finite count that terminates, configurations that can be related to each other, and an ordering that makes a thermal equivalence possible. It must also bind those structures to a bounded region. What I am claiming is only that the area reading leaves the choice among accounts open. I am **not** claiming that string theory, loop quantum gravity, holography, or any other program has been selected or refuted by this work.
>
> Several physically different calculations reproduce the area law. They agree on the reading and disagree on what is being counted. That is convergent derivation of the reading, not corroboration of any one source. Their agreement is what one evidence target predicts, so it carries no verdict weight for the source question.
>
> **What would refute the claim.** I wrote these down before running the derivation:
> 1. A measurement or theorem showing black hole entropy is not proportional to horizon area in a regime where the semiclassical derivation is otherwise accepted.
> 2. An instrument at or near the horizon that distinguishes the competing microscopic accounts. That would name the source and end the open question.
> 3. One derivation that reproduces `A/4` for every black hole including Schwarzschild with no free parameter, while every other program demonstrably fails.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the public sources. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - The figures and formula, public repo: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The architecture article, with the retrodiction caveat and the citation list: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. The framework's own article warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

## Contact rules

- One to one. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Public institutional or opt-in channels only. No personal emails of named individuals.
- Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 *before* any framework material is shown. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- A reply is a conversation, never a witness. If a refutation arrives, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and let the auditor seat evaluate it as it would any other source.
