## 0. The residual in effect terms

Bekenstein and Hawking tied black hole entropy to horizon area. The macro reading is `S_BH = A/4` in Planck units. The question asks for the **microscopic origin**: what is being counted.

Stated as a residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot:

- **Prediction Γ(b,a):** the thermodynamic frame predicts that entropy is the logarithm of a number of microstates, and that the number scales as `exp(A/4)`.
- **Observation o′:** the area law is obtained, and obtained by several physically different programs. The observation does not say which microstates produced it.
- **Residual R:** the gap between a macro reading (area) and a micro source (which configurations). It is an inverse problem: the forward map from microstates to the area reading is many to one.

**Why this is not one hard question.** Fig 9 :56–57 names it: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The bundle contains at least three conditions: (1) what configurations are counted, (2) how the counting is tied to the first law and the dynamics of the horizon, (3) what the thermodynamic system *is*. Carlip's review names the same bundle from inside physics: the remaining puzzles include *"the nature of the quantum microstates, the problem of universality, and the information loss paradox."* A single lever that answers (1) for extremal black holes leaves (2) and (3) standing. Per Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

**Bound the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"). The answer being searched for is written down here before the run so it can accrue a residual and be refused:

> **BOUND SHAPE (candidate, `specified`):** any microscopic account of black hole entropy must supply a finite, discrete set of distinguishable configurations in a bounded region, with an ordering that defines thermal equivalence, whose logarithm reproduces the area law for the black holes the account can treat; and the area reading does not, by itself, select among such accounts.

That shape is a preference until it is bound, and a bound term is refusable. Its refuters are in §3, written before the findings are concluded.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the findings)

Required by the Seat Map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The BOUND SHAPE in §0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Area reading fails.** A measurement or theorem shows that the entropy of a black hole is not proportional to horizon area in a regime where the semiclassical derivation is otherwise accepted. This breaks the macro anchor the shape is built on.
2. **A source-discriminating instrument fires.** An instrument at or near the horizon distinguishes the competing microscopic accounts (D-brane degeneracy versus horizon geometry versus lattice bits). That would name the slot, and would falsify the verdict that the reading underdetermines the source.
3. **Universality breaks in one direction.** A derivation inside one closure reproduces `A/4` for all black holes including Schwarzschild with no free parameter, while every competing program demonstrably fails. That would move the question from underdetermined to settled for one program.
4. **Substrate fails.** One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or a composition of two states or two structures with no intervening opposite-kind term is admitted and cannot be refused. That breaks the counting shape in §1.
5. **Census fails.** A re-census shows the question is a partition, not a union. That breaks the unbundling step and the per-domain verdict structure.

Until one of these is run, no part of this solution may be labelled `grounded`. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 4. Findings

### F1 : The six-term set makes entropy counting finite, discrete, and cross-kind (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 13's minimality table is load-bearing for the second law. Quoted verbatim:

> Fig 13 :68 **"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"** (energy, second law)
> Fig 13 :58 **"no relation between systems, and no configurations to count"** (network, zeroth and second)
> Fig 13 :56 **"no ordering, so no equivalence over thermal state, so no transitivity"** (spectrum, zeroth)
> Fig 13 :62 **"no region to make isolated mean anything"** (space, first and second)
> Fig 13 :74 **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

Derivation:

- A finite entropy requires a count that **terminates**. Termination is supplied by energy in a finite region, not by adding more states. Fig 13 explicitly says the continuum was removed for failing this exact test: a continuum is *"a spectrum without gaps"* and a generating set must not contain what its members produce. So the count must be discrete at the relevant scale.
- A count requires **configurations**. Those are supplied by network: relation between systems, configurations to count.
- An entropy requires a **thermal equivalence**. That is supplied by spectrum: ordering, so more and less can be said, so equivalence over thermal state and transitivity.
- A bounded system requires **space**: a region to make isolated mean anything.
- The composition rule requires that these cross kinds: a state (the bounded region) with a structure (the ordered, related configurations). A micro account that lists only configurations and never bonds them to a region cannot name an entropy.

This is a `general` structural result. It says what any microscopic account must supply. It does not name the account. Triangulated across five separate rows of Fig 13 plus the composition rule (low overlap inside the figure: different thermodynamic laws are used as the test for different members). Corroborated from outside physics by the inverse-problem frame's insistence that the model space and the observation map be stated before a solution is claimed.

**Refuter:** refuter 4 in §3. Also: a demonstration that the count can terminate without finiteness in a finite region (e.g. a rigorously finite entropy from an unbounded continuum) would break this finding without breaking the rest of the solution.

### F2 : The area reading underdetermines the source (`specified`, triangulated)

This is the central finding. Fig 13 :134–135:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**
> **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

The argument:

1. Bekenstein and Hawking give a macro reading, `S = A/4`. This is the projection, the observed data `d_obs`.
2. At least four low-overlap programs produce that reading: string-theoretic BPS counting (Strominger and Vafa, 1996, for five-dimensional extremal black holes), loop quantum gravity horizon geometry (Rovelli, 1996, for Schwarzschild), 't Hooft's Boolean variables on a two-dimensional lattice (1993), and Susskind's one discrete degree of freedom per Planck area (1995).
3. The programs use different atoms and different bonds. They are not one program wearing four names. Their agreement is therefore not the common-cause collapse of Fig 2, panel 2, provided their provenance is kept separate (Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*).
4. But they all predict the same **reading**. By Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*, their agreement corroborates the area law, not any one micro picture. The shared evidence pool is the area reading itself.
5. Therefore the area law is a projection with lossy information about its source, exactly the metamerism case in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the instrument *"cannot comment on the source."*

Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across two figure lines (Fig 13 and Fig 9, low overlap: one concerns the reach of primitive naming, the other concerns what lookup returns) plus the independent inverse-problem frame and the source-discrimination essay. Not `grounded`: no instrument discriminates sources.

**Refuter:** refuter 2 in §3. If a source-discriminating instrument fires, this finding is withdrawn and the name is settled by the ground.

### F3 : The programs are isomers over a shared reading, not rival names for one thing (`specified`, single-source for the figure definition)

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

At the macro level the string count, the loop count and the holographic bit count all yield `S ∝ A`. They are the same *substance* at that level (Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance"*). At the micro level they differ in what is bonded to what. Fig 12's isomer test applies: same atoms at the level of the reading, different arrangement at the level of the account. The difference is invisible in `S = A/4` and decisive in the behaviour off extremality, in subleading corrections, and in the answer to the information question.

This is single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12). The physics-facing half is corroborated by Carlip's review, which counts *"the many independent ways of obtaining the thermodynamic and (perhaps) statistical mechanical properties of black holes"* and names *"the problem of universality"* as a remaining puzzle. Universality is exactly the isomer point: many routes, one macro substance, no selection among routes by the macro reading.

**Refuter:** if two programs are shown to be the same account under a change of vocabulary (identity bond, Fig 12 :65: *"two names, one referent"*), the isomer claim between them fails. If all programs reduce to one, F2's underdetermination also fails.

### F4 : Ask SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD (structural, triangulated)

THE_FORMULA step 7 separates three questions. Applied in order:

1. **SEARCHED, is it in here and unfound?** Yes, partly. Within existing closures the microstate question is answered for restricted classes: Strominger and Vafa derived the area law for a class of five-dimensional extremal black holes by counting BPS bound states; Rovelli obtained entropy proportional to area for Schwarzschild from loop quantum gravity. The Wikipedia record states that after 1995 *"the result always agreed with the Bekenstein–Hawking formula"* for many extremal and near-extremal cases, but that *"for the Schwarzschild black hole ... the relationship between micro- and macrostates has not been characterized."* So the residual persists and does not trend down on the general question. Fig 6 :49: *"More search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change."* More counting of the same extremal cases buys spelling, not a general answer.
2. **INWARD, is the representation adequate?** Here there is a proto-instrument already returning something: gravitational-wave observations of black hole area increase, and analogue black hole platforms that realize a state-independent Hawking temperature. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** These instruments currently resolve the macro reading, not the source. They are the thing to sharpen, not a reason to declare the question closed.
3. **OUTWARD, does another frame already hold it?** Yes. The inverse-problem frame already holds the shape: *"An inverse problem in science is the process of calculating from a set of observations the causal factors that produced them"*, and when the forward map is rank deficient *"the solution of the inverse problem will be undetermined. This is a first difficulty."* That frame predicts the shape of R without drawing on the Tether. Import it with a source gate and a shadow test, and debit it against independence (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :10–13).

**Do not import a new primitive to cover a budget gap.** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** The fact that we cannot currently discriminate sources is a budget-and-instrument statement, not an underivability theorem. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

**Refuter:** a demonstration that an instrument already discriminates sources (refuter 2) would move INWARD from a plan to a result. A demonstration that the inverse-problem frame draws on the same evidence pool as the framework would remove the OUTWARD witness, though not the figure verdict.

### F5 : The dependency chain breaks at vocabulary, not at counting (`general`, single-source for the figure line)

Fig 3 :25–27, quoted in §1, names the link. The chain here:

**Refuter:** name an instrument that returns a source-discriminating reading for a horizon. That installs the missing link and the chain proceeds.

### F9 : The retrodiction caveat

Presenting this solution as a derivation of black hole entropy would invert the record.
