## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any name does not. A microscopic account must supply a finite, discrete, related and ordered set of configurations in a bounded region with a finite energy bound, and its logarithm reproduces the area law. The area reading underdetermines the source: the low-overlap programs converge on the reading and stand as isomers over the shared macro substance, and no instrument in this derivation discriminates them. The question is a union that must be unbundled, and the dependency chain breaks at the vocabulary link where a source-discriminating instrument would sit. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; the source verdict is a `gap` at this budget, not a silence. The area law itself is not in doubt and is a retrodiction for the framework, not a framework result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is stated in effect terms with five refuters written before the run, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The name claims are withheld rather than withdrawn, so no primitive has to be removed and no published calculation has to be retracted. A source-discriminating result supersedes the shape cleanly, and each program keeps its provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The outreach packet can be replaced without touching the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An observable that distinguishes D-brane counts from horizon geometries from lattice bits would name the slot and falsify the underdetermination verdict; a measurement in which entropy is not proportional to area where the semiclassical derivation otherwise holds would break the macro anchor; a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a theorem that the area reading is injective over the relevant microstate space, would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
