## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a finite, discrete, cross-kind count in a bounded region with a thermal ordering, whose logarithm reproduces the area law.
- **Name:** D-brane bound states, or horizon spin networks, or Planck-area bits, as *the* origin.

The shape is what `01` binds. The name is what the framework refuses to supply.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30–31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect is *an area reading that requires a finite count*. We describe the effect, not the cause (D-branes versus loops). The programs are then examined as candidate frames, not chosen first and mapped backwards.

## 3. Why the source is not recoverable from the reading

This is the essay that governs the question most directly. *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Metamerism: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states the general case:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*

and the resolution:

> *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

It was, in the color case, *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction and union horizons. A projection falls below an observer's **distinction horizon** when that observer cannot tell two generation methods apart. It falls below the **union horizon** when *"no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."* Crucially: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* And: *"the projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* Also: *"recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

For black hole entropy, the area reading is the projection and the microstates are the source. Several sources yield the same reading. Until an instrument discriminates, the honest report is a shape plus a lineage, not a name.

That essay also states the question's own difficulty in one line: *"There have been many theories surrounding how entropy works, but few have been concrete or verifiable about its origins."* The framework's contribution here is the shape of what a concrete account must supply, not a new origin story.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50–52), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books* sharpens what an import can be: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all. For this question the needed axis is a source-discriminating measurement, not another term inside an existing microstate program.

The same essay warns: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the six-term reading of the entropy question is a visible guess applied honestly, not nature's own list. That is why the solution stays `specified`.

## 5. Bundling and the shape of a split

*The Library of Babel* names the same move Fig 9 makes: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

The black hole entropy question bundles the count, the first-law tie, and the identity of the thermodynamic system. A single answer to "what is being counted" does not close the others. The canonical reading of "shape" therefore includes the **split shape**: say which conditions are binding and which have been cleared. That is the first deliverable of `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous programs that agree on the area law is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be tried: ask what would refute it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and such outputs *"must be seen as simulacra."* The architecture article adds: the results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the framework's "shape, not a name" limit forbids saying anything about microstates at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs what is **missing** from Γ. In this question the six terms are already held; what is missing is a discriminating instrument, not a primitive. The correct claim is the one in §3: the framework returns a kind of account, and names only when the ground pays or a provenanced import crosses. The earlier reading is retained here rather than silently erased.
