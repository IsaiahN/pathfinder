## Three findings from the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Convergence on the reading, disagreement on the source.** Strominger-Vafa (string, extremal 5D), Rovelli (loop, Schwarzschild), 't Hooft (Boolean lattice) and Susskind (Planck-area bits) use different closures and all arrive at `S ∝ A`. By Fig 2 :40 their agreement is *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* for the source question. It corroborates the reading, not a picture. Carlip's phrase *"the problem of universality"* is the physicist's name for the same shape.
2. **The source question is an inverse problem.** The independent inverse-problem frame starts from the effects and calculates the causes, and warns that the solution can be undetermined when the forward map is not injective. This is the mathematical counterpart of the essay's metamerism point. It is a low-overlap corroboration of Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
3. **The retrodiction caveat is in the source.** The architecture article tags Bekenstein and Hawking as an instance and says the triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* Black hole thermodynamics is therefore a case the framework describes, not a result it produced. Do not present it otherwise.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the residual, the shape and the refuter first. Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the verdict on stage 1 has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27–28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the links.
- **Seat Map :163**, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list chosen for people likely to agree produces a convention, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter, and the question of what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, the links, with the note that the figures and the papers, not this summary, are the authority.

1. **Stage 1:** the area reading is settled; the microscopic source is not. Claim: any source must supply finite discrete configurations in a bounded region with a thermal ordering, and the reading does not select among sources. Ask for a failure, not agreement.
2. **Stage 2, opt in:** the three source links (repository figures, canonical README, architecture article), plus the physics papers named above. No verdict from stage 2 is pooled as corroboration.
