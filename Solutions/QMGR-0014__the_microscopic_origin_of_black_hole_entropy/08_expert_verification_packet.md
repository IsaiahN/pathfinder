# Expert verification packet: the Tether on black hole entropy

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints. Not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Bekenstein and Hawking gave a settled macro reading, `S ∝ A`. The microscopic source is not settled. The Tether's six-term set requires that any microscopic account supply a finite, discrete set of related and ordered configurations in a bounded region with a finite energy bound, so that the count terminates and a thermal equivalence can be defined; the logarithm of that count is what the area law reads. The area reading is a projection that does not select among sources. Low-overlap programs (string D-brane counts, loop horizon geometries, lattice bits) converge on the reading and differ as isomers over the shared macro substance. The framework returns the shape and the refuters, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** entropy counting is load-bearing on spectrum, network, space, energy and gradient. A finite count requires a bounded region and a terminating rule; it requires configurations and an ordering for thermal equivalence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :68 *"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"*; Fig 13 :58 *"no relation between systems, and no configurations to count"*; Fig 13 :56 *"no ordering, so no equivalence over thermal state, so no transitivity"*; Fig 13 :62 *"no region to make isolated mean anything"*; Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five, or derive an established thermodynamic law without the term the table says it needs, or admit a state-state or structure-structure composition the framework cannot refuse.

### C2 : the source question; checkable against the published calculations

- **Statement:** the area reading underdetermines the source. Several low-overlap closures reproduce `S ∝ A` while disagreeing on what is counted: Strominger and Vafa derived it *"for a class of five-dimensional extremal black holes in string theory by counting the degeneracy of BPS soliton bound states"*; Rovelli obtained *"a black hole entropy proportional to the area"* from loop quantum gravity; 't Hooft described *"Boolean variables defined on a two-dimensional lattice"*; Susskind required *"one discrete degree of freedom per Planck area"*. Their agreement corroborates the reading, not any one source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
- **What to check:** is the convergence claim fair? Do the four calculations count the same class of black holes, and would a source-discriminating observable distinguish them? Carlip's review names *"the nature of the quantum microstates"* and *"the problem of universality"* as open.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a theorem that the area reading is injective over the relevant microstate space, or an instrument that distinguishes the accounts. Either fires refuter 2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and ends the open question.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 9

- **Statement:** the programs are isomers over the shared macro substance, and the question is a union that must be unbundled before a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Refuter:** show that two of the programs are related by a definitional identity (*"two names, one referent"*, Fig 12 :65), that all reduce to one, or that the question is a partition rather than a union.

## 6. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating observable at or near the horizon. If it distinguishes D-brane counts from horizon geometries from lattice bits, the name is settled and the underdetermination verdict is withdrawn.
2. A demonstration that the first-law tie holds with the same subleading corrections in only one program. That breaks universality in one direction.
3. A measurement in which entropy is not proportional to area where the semiclassical derivation is otherwise accepted. Refuter 1 fires.
4. An instrument already returning a macro reading that can be sharpened to resolve the source. That is the INWARD route of Formula step 7 and it is the cheapest next step.
