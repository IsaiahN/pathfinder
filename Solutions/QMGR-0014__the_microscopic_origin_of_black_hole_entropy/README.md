## The question restated as an effect

Bekenstein and Hawking established a macro reading: entropy proportional to horizon area, `S_BH = A/4`. The question asks for the **source** of that reading, the microstates being counted. The two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, name a source** (a particular microstate program is the origin). This delivers a name where the available instruments cannot discriminate among names, and it spends the independence of the people who would check it.
- **Option B, derive the shape** (state the constraints any microscopic account must satisfy, and record that the reading underdetermines the source). This delivers a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

An answer would look like: a finite, discrete set of distinguishable configurations in a bounded region, ordered so a thermal equivalence can be defined, whose logarithm reproduces the area law for the black holes the account can treat. That sentence is bound as an ordinary term in `01` so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that string theory, loop quantum gravity, holography, or any other program has been selected or refuted by this derivation.
- No claim that the framework settles a question that physics has left open.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words and the seat map's:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make the agreement worthless (Seat Map :163).
2. **A verifier that holds the interior cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's source.
3. **The question is about the ground, not about prestige.** Seat Map: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying. Prestige may fund a check; it cannot decide one.
