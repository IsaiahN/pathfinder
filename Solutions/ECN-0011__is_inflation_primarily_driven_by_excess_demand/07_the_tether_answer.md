# The Tether's answer on the drivers of inflation

**Mode: `general` for the shape, `specified` for the per-domain predictions, `grounded` nowhere.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. It cannot settle which driver of inflation is primary.

## 1. The residual it answers

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot, where a slot is a price sub-index in a country in an episode. The question asks which candidate driver, or which combination, minimizes `R` across those slots. The four candidates are overlapping conditions in a union, not exclusive boxes (Fig 9 :55). The headline rate is an aggregate over the vector, and the aggregate is not the thing to be explained.

## 2. The shape

1. **The residual is per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." So the right question is which driver closes the residual in food, in energy, in shelter, in services, in a given episode. The Federal Reserve already works this way: it examines subcategories to decide whether a rise is temporary or unique. `primarily` is the wrong operator for a vector.

2. **The four candidates are a union, and "combination" is a restatement, not an answer.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." They share a functional group, `state → structure → state`, with different atoms in the slots (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1). Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).

3. **No causal frame may score itself.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A school's variance share is a self-score. Each frame must range separately against an anchor that does not move when the school changes its mind (Fig 2 :29, :45).

4. **Expectations are a cycle, not a fourth linear driver.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Whether expectations amplify or damp is a measured question about sign, not a matter of school.

5. **Agreement cannot decide.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A survey of economists agreeing on money growth is one evidence pool, not a witness (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :12-13).

6. **Which driver dominates is a `gap` from the figures.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," The figures are a domain-agnostic metatheory. They do not name a macro driver and cannot rank these four. Reporting a name would breach the canonical limit: the framework returns *"a shape, not a name."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that demand is irrelevant, that money never matters, that supply shocks are imaginary, or that expectations are "only psychology."
- Not that inflation is always and everywhere monetary, or always a supply phenomenon. The valence of a driver is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), not a property it carries.
- Not that "some combination" is a finding. It is the union restated.
- Not that a survey, a prestige ranking, or a citation count is evidence. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not grounded. No inflation series, natural experiment or decomposition was run here.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would settle the shape

**To settle the shape (the thing claimed here):**

- Show that one aggregate driver predicts every sub-index residual as well as the per-slot decomposition, against a non-updating anchor.
- Show the four conditions are mutually exclusive, so a single lever clears every overlap.
- Show the expectations feedback sign is negative at the measured scale.
- Show causal attribution is invariant to index, window and subcategory.

**To settle the empirical name (the thing claimed as a gap):**

A source-discriminating instrument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* is explicit: one inflation reading can be produced by several sources, and the reading "cannot comment on the source"; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Concretely: a decomposition that separates demand and supply contributions per sub-index, plus a natural experiment that isolates one condition while holding the others, plus replication. The 2021 to 2023 literature is actively doing this, and it still records controversy about the role of profits, supply chains and stimulus. That is the expected state of an open ground, not a failure of the figures.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures say the ground decides and the figures do not hold the name. The world sources show real instruments being built: the Fed's subcategory procedure, median CPI and trimmed mean PCE as differently constructed readings of the same ground, and a live dispute about sources. The answer stops at the limit of what those instruments currently distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* is the canonical caution. A gap is a verdict here, not a silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a primary driver of inflation, and it does not say "it is complicated" and stop. It says the question is made in the wrong unit. Inflation is a vector over sub-index, country and episode, and the word "primarily" averages that vector into one number, which is exactly how a live signal disappears. Demand, supply, money and expectations are not four exclusive boxes; they are overlapping conditions sharing one grammatical shape, and the expectations term is a feedback loop whose sign decides whether a shock amplifies or dies. No school may score itself with the quantity it produces, and agreement among schools is one evidence pool, not a witness. Which driver actually dominates in a given episode is left as a gap, because the figures are a metatheory and do not hold that name. What they give is the shape any candidate answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the requirement for an instrument that can discriminate among sources. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
