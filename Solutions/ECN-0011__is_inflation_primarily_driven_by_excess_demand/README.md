## The question restated as an effect

The word `primarily` asks for a single dominant cause. Four candidates are offered: excess demand, supply disruption, money and credit, expectations, plus a catch-all `some combination`. Each option leads somewhere different. If demand dominates, the lever is aggregate demand management. If supply dominates, the lever is supply repair and the demand lever is misdirected. If money and credit dominate, the lever is monetary aggregates and credit conditions. If expectations dominate, the lever is credibility and communication. If it is a combination, no single lever clears the residual and the levers interact.

What an answer would look like: a statement of which candidate, or which arrangement of candidates, predicts the observed inflation path per price sub-index and per episode better than the alternatives, with an anchor that does not move when an economist changes school.

Binding that sought-for shape as an ordinary term: `the driver that closes R per slot against a non-updating anchor`. That binding is refusable by the same test as any other term, which is what `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` does before concluding.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a school of thought.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness to one causal story would manufacture the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not become a ground.** *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :12-13). More readers of the same review do not add evidence.
3. **Hand over the residual, not the interiors.** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The first contact carries the shape claim and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional submission and contact pages avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
