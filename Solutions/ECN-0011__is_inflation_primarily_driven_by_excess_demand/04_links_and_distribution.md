## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on the pitch

1. **The measurement institution already splits the residual.** The Fed FAQ does not read one headline number. It averages over time, examines subcategories to separate temporary from unique events, and checks core measures. That is an existing, checkable instance of Fig 1's rule, and it is the strongest bridge between the framework and the question. Lead with it.
2. **The hard part is source, not correlation.** The quantity theory page records both an overwhelming correlation between money and prices and Friedman's own caveat that the statistical connection says nothing about direction of influence; it also records the failure of money targeting. The 2021 to 2023 page records a live dispute about profits, supply and demand. Together these are an instance of the metamerism problem: one price reading, several possible sources, and no instrument that has settled which source was active. That is exactly the `gap` the audit reports.
