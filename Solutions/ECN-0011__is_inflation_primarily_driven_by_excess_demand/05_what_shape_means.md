## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at *"the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form an answer must have before the name of the dominant driver is available. It constrains: per slot, unbundled union, outside anchor, reflexive expectations, ground decides. It does not fill: it does not say demand, supply, money or expectations.

`headline rate = one number` is a name. `residual indexed by sub-index, country and episode` is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on the starting vocabulary: use what you already know that is closest, then let "new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably" narrow the space. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". Applied here: describe the failure of a single lever to clear a sub-index residual before naming a school.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the canonical-language version of Fig 9 :54-57.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." That is Fig 2 :45 in search vocabulary: frames range against the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A source-discriminating instrument, a natural experiment, a reproducible decomposition that survives replication.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). In economics this would be a term imported from a different discipline with a stated provenance and a check that it explains a gap the receiving frame could not.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Several underlying causes produce the same inflation reading; the reading does not name the cause. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The instrument, not an argument, resolves it.

Note the correction carried in the exemplar's `05` §5b and relevant here: "shape, not a name" governs a **missing** primitive, not the selection of atoms already held. In this solution the gap is not about a missing primitive in Γ. It is about an empirical name the figures do not contain. So the rule applies by a different route: the figures do not hold the name, so they cannot return it. That is still a limit, but it is a limit of content, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why "which driver is primary" is the source error

The headline inflation rate is a projection. Demand, supply, money and expectations can each produce a high reading. The AEA survey fact that most economists agree on money growth is a reading of a community, not a discriminator among sources. The quantity theory page records Friedman's own caveat that "The statistical connection itself, however, tells nothing about direction of influence", and the abandonment of money targeting after the relationship "proved to be rather unstable".

Under *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the correct response to several sources giving one reading is not to vote among schools. It is to ask what instrument would discriminate among them. The 2021 to 2023 surge debate (supply chains versus stimulus versus profits versus housing) is the live case: the reading is not in dispute, the source is.

## 5. "Some combination" is not a shape

A shape narrows. "Some combination" narrows nothing: it admits every slot and every episode. Under *Library of Babel*, a search that returns everything has not started. Under Fig 9 :55, a union must be unbundled before it is searched. So the phrase is a placeholder for the work, not the conclusion.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous inflation economists who agree with a story is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried against sub-index data, and let the ground settle it.

## 7. This reading is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So this file, and the audit it supports, are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle which driver of inflation is primary. No inflation series was measured.
