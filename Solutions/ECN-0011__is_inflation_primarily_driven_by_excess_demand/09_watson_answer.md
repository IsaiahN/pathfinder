## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that inflation is primarily driven by excess demand, supply disruptions, money and credit, expectations, or some combination. Option A: pick one driver as primary. Option B: refuse the aggregation, unbundle the union, and report the dominant driver as a gap left to the ground. What each leads to: A sends a self-scored verdict that hides the per-slot signal and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument requirement that would close the name.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot, where a slot is a price sub-index in a country in an episode. Arity: four candidate causes plus a compound, where the compound is a union of overlapping conditions rather than a fifth peer cause. Symmetry: the candidates are not interchangeable; demand and supply are opposed levers and expectations are reflexive. Scale: first order inside one sub-index and episode, second order across episodes and against the nominal anchor. Fig 9 :55 names it a bundled problem, not a list of separate ones.
- **Domain 1, measurement and definition.** Actors: statistical agencies and index constructors. Currency: index numbers and price levels. Anchor: the published index and the transaction prices under it, which do not move when an economist changes school. Question type: partition of measured universes. Dependency: first; no causal claim is readable before this link works.
- **Domain 2, causal attribution.** Actors: demand shocks, supply constraints, money and credit conditions, firms and workers. Currency: causal influence on the price path. Anchor: none inside the frame. Question type: union of overlapping conditions. Dependency: second; consumes D1's per-slot residual.
- **Domain 3, reflexive expectations.** Actors: households, firms, wage negotiators, central bank communication. Currency: beliefs that feed back into the prices they predict. Anchor: the realised path plus survey and market expectations series, which are instruments, not anchors. Question type: a cycle inside D2 with its own loop. Dependency: third; re-enters D2.
- **Domain 4, verification and publication.** Actors: researchers, editors, referees, data archives. Currency: residuals, corrections, replications. Anchor: reproducible data and a source-discriminating instrument, never agreement. Question type: partition of channels, but many channels share one evidence pool. Dependency: fourth, grading D1 through D3 from outside.

Strain test applied: the question lists four separate options while Fig 9 :55 describes overlapping conditions. Re-census resolves it: the list is a phrasing, the union is the domain structure. Different objects, no strain. The other apparent strain, Fig 1 :19 per slot versus a published headline rate, is likewise resolved: Fig 1 governs the residual used for search, the headline is an aggregate. No figure line was forced to name a driver.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical name is a gap. Inflation's residual is per sub-index, country and episode, so a headline average hides the live signal. Demand, supply, money and expectations are overlapping conditions with one functional group shape, not exclusive rivals; "some combination" restates the union. Expectations are a reflexive cycle whose sign decides amplification. No school may score itself, and agreement within a shared evidence pool cannot decide. The figures do not name a dominant driver, so reporting one would breach the canonical limit. This is a `general` shape result with `specified` sub-claims and no `grounded` component.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a preferred school or a prestige list instead of a shape and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable.
- **undoable:** yes. The four candidate stories are not withdrawn by this verdict; only their standing as a single aggregate answer is refused. A corrected per-slot decomposition can supersede the framing without touching any driver. Nothing has to be deleted from the record, and Fig 6 :42 notes that a reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An aggregate driver that predicts every sub-index residual as well as a decomposition against a non-updating anchor would break Fig 1; a demonstration that the four conditions are mutually exclusive would break Fig 9 :55; a measured negative expectations feedback sign would break Fig 12 :121; a source-discriminating instrument naming the active driver in an episode would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the shape stands as `candidate` and the name remains `gap`.
