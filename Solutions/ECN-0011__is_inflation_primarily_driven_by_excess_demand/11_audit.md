## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is `|Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot, where the slot is a price sub-index in a country in an episode. Arity: four candidate causes plus a compound. Symmetry: the candidates are not interchangeable; demand and supply are opposed, and expectations are reflexive. Scale: first order within a sub-index and episode, second order across episodes and against the nominal anchor. Structurally this is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, measurement.** Actors: statistical agencies and index constructors. Currency: index numbers. Anchor: the published index and its underlying transactions, which do not update on anyone's opinion. Partition of measured universes. Order: first.
- **Domain 2, causal attribution.** Actors: demand, supply, money and credit, firms and workers. Currency: causal influence. Anchor: none inside the frame. Union of overlapping conditions. Order: second.
- **Domain 3, reflexive expectations.** Actors: households, firms, negotiators, communication. Currency: beliefs feeding back into prices. Anchor: realised path and survey instruments; the instruments are not anchors. Cycle within D2. Order: third, re-entering D2.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: researchers, editors, archives. Currency: residuals and replications. Anchor: reproducible data and a source-discriminating instrument. Partition of channels, but shared pools. Order: fourth.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-57 and the per-slot rule from Fig 1 :19/:22, I derived the same boundaries from the anchor test in Fig 2 :29 and the cycle test in Fig 12 :121. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is low overlap within one framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the route level.
