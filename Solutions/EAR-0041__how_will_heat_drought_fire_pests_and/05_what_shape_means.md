## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0041 the shape is therefore not a forecast for a named stand and not a universal interaction coefficient. It is the set of constraints any such answer must satisfy: per-stand indexing, a union rather than a partition, residual-relative valence, ordered bonds and cycles, a threshold comparison on survival, a lossy cross-scale transform and a substitution branch. Those constraints narrow the answer without filling the slot for a particular stand.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer"*, and *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Vocabulary here came from reading the forest-dieback, bark-beetle, fire-ecology, fragmentation and threshold literatures.
- The same essay warns that *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the five-item question here; Fig 9 :55 confirms the union.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The residual was described in effect terms in `01` section 0 before any world frame was chosen.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar, not re-opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what a checker would expect at a stand before sending the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the shape plus the refuter, not a number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For a forest question the ground is a measured stand state over time, not a model's survival index.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming hydraulic failure as the mechanism by which drought kills a tree is an import from tree physiology, and it is sourced in `10_source_ledger.csv` accordingly.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried, not re-opened): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own variable list.

## 4. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming heat, water, fuel, host or edge. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Heat, water, carbon, fuel, host, microclimate and seed source are bindings from the forest-science frame onto terms already available in the world evidence; naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is manufacturing a new primitive by rearranging the five (Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*) and claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

The correction does **not** retract the metamerism point in section 5. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. The source question commits the metamerism error if it asks for one cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism directly: *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a stand with 40 percent crown loss is one reading. It is compatible with drought-driven hydraulic failure, carbon starvation, beetle attack, fire scorch, edge microclimate, or several of these in sequence. The *Forest dieback* page (opened) says precisely that *"identifying specific cause-effect relationships between dieback and the environment is a difficult process"* and lists multiple candidate causes. The present question asks how they interact, which is the right structural move; the wrong move would be to name one as *the* cause from the single reading.

What is owed is a better instrument: a source-discriminating observation at the stand, not a larger panel of people reading the same crown. This is the governing reason the email asks for a failure criterion and the packet names an instrument condition.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious forest scientists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried at a stand and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the forest question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Honest stop

The framework returns a shape and a refuter. It does not return the stand, the year, the coefficient or the name of the missing instrument. Those are reported as `gap` or as an instrument owed, in `01` section 6 and `07_the_tether_answer.md`. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README): a single dieback reading is not proof that the causes are the same, and a room full of agreeing models is not proof that the shape is true.
