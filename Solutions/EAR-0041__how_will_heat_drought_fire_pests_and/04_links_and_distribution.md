## 2. Wider literature by field

These are the fields a low-overlap check would draw on. The sources themselves are listed above and in `10_source_ledger.csv`; the grouping exists so that one evidence pool is not counted as several witnesses.

- **Forest pathology and ecophysiology.** Hydraulic failure versus carbon starvation as two mechanisms of drought and heat mortality; host weakening before beetle attack. Corroborates the union structure and the ordered bond, not a specific coefficient.
- **Entomology.** Native bark beetles as decomposers versus aggressive outbreak pests; warming shortening development time and extending the breeding season. Corroborates residual-relative valence.
- **Fire ecology.** Fire as renewal in adapted systems and as stand replacement in intolerant systems; short intervals converting forest to grass. Corroborates the valence rule and the habitat-substitution branch.
- **Landscape ecology and fragmentation.** Edge microclimate, isolation, SLOSS disagreement, rescue effect and extinction debt. Corroborates the habitat-enumeration rule and supplies a genuine internal disagreement that the union reading can hold without averaging.
- **Regime shifts and thresholds.** Alternative stable states, hysteresis, cloud-forest moisture failure and coral-reef analogues. Corroborates the comparison-bond and substitution findings.
- **Earth-system accounting.** WRI's forest-carbon ledger, regional sink-to-source flips and the caveat that average removals miss short-term disturbance. Corroborates per-slot indexing and channel decay.
- **Framework essays (maximal overlap with the figures).** The three essays opened this session govern the canonical reading of "shape", source discrimination and the simulation limit. They share the framework author's evidence pool and carry no independent witness weight.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a stand residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Their agreement then stops being evidence.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 9 :30-31 - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Ask what they would expect to see at a stand if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of a wrong forest forecast is high, so the check should come from a frame that does not share the author's sources.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-stand residual and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a stand they know; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a fresh mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer (and the method stage)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. The stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so each recipient writes down what would refute their own result before running it.
- **Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The two live type questions, the comparison-bond operand type at stand level and the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim, must be stated as open in the covering note. A recipient who can see that the author knows where the weak points are is being given a fair check, not a pitch.
