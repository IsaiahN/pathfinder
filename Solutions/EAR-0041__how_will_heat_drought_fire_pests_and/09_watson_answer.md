## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that five pressures, heat, drought, fire, pests and land conversion, interact to affect forest survival. Option A: treat them as five independent doses and return one interaction coefficient or one global survival score. Option B: treat them as a per-stand union of overlapping conditions with typed bonds, a threshold on survival, a lossy cross-scale transform and a substitution branch, and withhold the coefficient. What each leads to: A returns a number the figure set does not hold, aggregates the live stand residual into an average, and gives a standing sign to actors whose valence flips with the residual; B returns a checkable shape, eight refuters written in advance, and an instrument owed at the boundary, and refuses the coefficient as a `gap` rather than inventing it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: five named conditions plus one outcome, with unenumerated habitat terms; arity greater than one with overlap, so a **union compound**, not a partition. Symmetry: the bonds are ordered, sequence and production, and fire is a cycle; the symmetric `+` bond cannot express the interactions. Scale: xylem and leaf at hours, stand at seasons, landscape at decades. R is indexed per stand, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The sought-for shape was bound before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" and Fig 5 :65, so it is refusable like any other term.
- **D1, stand physiology.** Actors: trees, water column, carbon stores, stomata, xylem. Currency: hydraulic status and carbon balance. Anchor: measured stand state (live basal area, crown, regeneration). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **D2, disturbance propagation.** Actors: fire, beetles, pathogens, hosts, fuel. Currency: mortality and spread per stand. Anchor: observed mortality and burned area, separate from D1. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated, with one declined bond.
- **D3, habitat and land conversion.** Actors: edges, fragments, microclimate, seed sources, surrounding land use. Currency: contact and connectivity. Anchor: forest cover and edge microclimate over time. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **D4, verification and monitoring.** Actors: plots, remote sensing, models, managers. Currency: per-stand residual and bracket fidelity. Anchor: the ground, which does not update; the channel does. Verdict: `general` on the anchor rule, triangulated.
- **D5, regime and substitution.** Actors: feedback loops and alternative states. Currency: persistence and hysteresis. Anchor: long-term forest state versus the state that replaced it. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **Partition or union.** Union with compounds at the intersections, so one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** A reachable reading first, then vocabulary for what the conditions afford, then the objective that defines survival, then a route, then learning and carrying (Fig 3 :20). Dependency order D4 to D1 to D2 to D3 to D5.
- **Strain test.** An initial mapping of Fig 13's six physical terms onto the five stressors was re-censed out: Fig 13 governs the cross-kind rule and the closed-set limit, not the identity of ecological drivers. One live type question remains: the operand type of the comparison bond at stand level. The beetle-to-fire causal bond is conflicted in the world source and declined.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the coefficient does not. The five pressures are a per-stand union of overlapping conditions with residual-relative valence, ordered bonds and cycles, a threshold comparison on survival, a lossy cross-scale transform and a habitat-substitution branch. They are functional-group members as listed and a genuine isomer claim over a fixed stand-atom set, with a stated falsifier and a live fixed-atom qualifier. Survival belongs to forest-and-habitat, and the ground is the measured stand state while the monitoring channel is an instrument that can fail silently. One tempting causal bond, beetles to larger fires, is declined on the opened source. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The universal interaction coefficient and the named-stand forecast are a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A wrong union reading would over- or under-weight an intersection; a wrong valence reading would assign a fixed sign to fire or pests; a wrong substitution reading would promise recovery by single-stressor removal. The packet states these as refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger avoidable cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a recording of a conclusion instead of a shape and its refuter; Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The declined beetle-to-fire bond, the fixed-atom qualifier on the isomer claim and the operand-type strain on the comparison bond are removable or resolvable without touching the union, per-slot or valence findings. A superseding packet can be sent. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a refused term stays in the record while its standing to be composed over falls (Fig 6 :42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates hydraulic failure, carbon starvation and beetle attack from one crown reading would close the mechanism gap; a threshold instrument that names the comparison operands at stand level would clear the type strain; a factorial intervention that clears all five intersections at once would fire the union refuter; a restoration that recovers former feedbacks by single-stressor removal would fire the substitution refuter. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
