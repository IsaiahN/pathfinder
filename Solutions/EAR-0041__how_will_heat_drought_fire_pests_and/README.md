## Restatement in effect terms

The question asks what five conditions, heat, drought, fire, pests and land conversion, do to a forest when they act together. Stated as an effect: for each forest stand there is a gap between what the stand's current terms predict and what the ground returns (live basal area, canopy, regeneration, species composition). The five conditions are candidate terms that could close that gap. The decision is not whether each condition is bad in isolation, but which composition closes the residual at a given stand, at a given horizon, and what a manager could remove or introduce without substituting a different habitat. An answer would look like a shape: a per-stand union of conditions with named bonds, a threshold on survival, and a refuter. That shape is bound as an ordinary term before the derivation, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal email addresses of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, and no claim that a larger panel would settle a forest question.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words, all checked in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Prestige cannot settle an action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The legitimate ask is a budget to test the refuter, not an endorsement.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The two-stage offer therefore sends the residual and its refuter first and offers the source links only after a verdict is recorded.
