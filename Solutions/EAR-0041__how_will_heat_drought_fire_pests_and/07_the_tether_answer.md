# The Tether's answer on forest survival under five interacting pressures

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` at the coefficient and named-stand level. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a forecast. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No stand was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A stand's current terms predict persistence; the ground returns live basal area, crown, regeneration and composition. The gap is indexed per stand (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). Heat, drought, fire, pests and land conversion are candidate terms offered to close that gap. The question asks for their composition. The answer is a union of overlapping conditions, not one dose-response curve (Fig 9 :55). The sought-for shape, a per-stand union with typed bonds and a threshold, was written down before the run and bound as an ordinary term (Fig 5 :65).

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before searching.** The five conditions overlap. Heat and drought share hydraulic and carbon mechanisms; drought changes host susceptibility before beetles act; beetles and drought change fuel and snags; land conversion changes edge microclimate. One lever clears a single-condition region and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*).
2. **Read per stand, never as one forest score.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The same data set can give a sink in one region and a source in another; the pooled number hides the flip (WRI 2025, opened).
3. **Forest survival is forest-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Enumerate the habitat outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). Light, water, temperature, season and terrain are conditions; pollination, predation, competition and dependency are relations (Fig 11 :46-47).
4. **No standing sign.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fire renews a fire-adapted stand and destroys a fire-intolerant one. Beetles recycle dead wood at one residual and kill healthy trees at another. The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :50).
5. **Interactions are bonds, not ingredients.** Over a fixed stand-atom set the five conditions are isomers (Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*); as the question lists them they are a functional group (Fig 12 :123). Fire is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes (Fig 12 :121). The naive beetle-to-fire link is declined because the world source reports no effect on fire size or severity in most studies.
6. **Survival is a threshold comparison, not a stock.** Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Operators :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*. The comparison's operand type at stand level is a live question, named in `01` F7 and `11_audit.md`.
7. **Expect substitution at the tail.** Crossing a threshold can replace a forest with a grass or shrub habitat that has its own feedbacks. Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Restoration is introduction and permanent work (Fig 11 :61, :63), with two silent failures: what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69).
8. **Keep ground and channel separate.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. A saturated or pooled monitoring index is a dead channel while the forest keeps paying. Agreement among models sharing a pool is one witness, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :35).
9. **Costs do not add.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Description bits and verification actions are held apart. No single resilience score is legal (Fig 9 :60).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a forecast for a named stand, a peak-mortality year or a universal interaction coefficient. That is a `gap`, reported as a verdict and not as silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).
- Not that all five pressures are always harmful. Valence is relative to the residual.
- Not that beetles cause larger fires. The specific bond is conflicted in the source and declined.
- Not that a crossed forest can be restored by removing one stressor. Habitat substitution breaks that lever.
- Not that more monitoring finds the missing coefficient. Search inside one closure adds no atom (Fig 8 :32).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a stand measurement.
- Not a claim that the figure set names heat, drought or fire as primitives. Those are bindings from the forest-science frame with provenance owed, not derivations from the six-term substrate.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at the stand, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here the questions are:

- **Mechanism discriminator:** can a stand reading separate hydraulic failure from carbon starvation from beetle attack when all three yield the same crown-condition reading?
- **Threshold test:** does a comparison bond hold between delivered conditions and stand demand at a named horizon, or demonstrably not? What are the operands at that level?
- **Cascade test:** does removing the third-order drought-host-beetle cascade change the residual while the direct effect stays?
- **Substitution test:** after a state shift, does removing the original stressor restore the former feedbacks, or does the new habitat persist?
- **Channel test:** does the monitoring index move with the analyst's choice of pool? If so it is an instrument, not an anchor (Fig 2 :30).
- Standing refuters from `01` section 4: the union refuter, the habitat refuter, the per-slot refuter, the valence refuter, the cascade refuter, the threshold refuter, the substitution refuter and the instrument refuter.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five conditions and their bonds are held; the numerical interaction coefficient and the stand forecast are not. The framework can say the shape any candidate answer must have, what each domain's anchor is, and what instrument would close a slot. It cannot say which primitive or instrument is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README): one dieback reading is not one cause.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give one number for how heat, drought, fire, pests and land conversion will affect forest survival. It says the five are a union of overlapping conditions read per stand, not five doses of one pressure; that forest survival belongs to the forest and its habitat, so an edge or a lost seed source is part of the answer; that fire and pests have no fixed sign, because their valence depends on the residual being tracked; that their interactions are typed bonds and cycles, so an ingredient list cannot predict the outcome; that "survives" is a threshold comparison, not a stock of biomass; and that crossing the threshold often substitutes a different habitat, which cannot be undone by simply removing the original stressor. What it offers is the shape any valid answer must have, eight failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close the remaining gaps. The stand, the year and the coefficient stay open until the ground settles them.
