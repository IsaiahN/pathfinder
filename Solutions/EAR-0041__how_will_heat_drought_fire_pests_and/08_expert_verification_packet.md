# Expert verification packet - the Tether on forest survival under five pressures

**What this is:** a claim in a form a forest ecologist or global-change researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` at the coefficient and named-stand level. Not `grounded`.** No stand measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The five named pressures are a union of overlapping conditions read per forest stand, not five independent doses of one pressure. Heat and drought couple through hydraulic failure and carbon starvation; drought changes host susceptibility before beetles act; beetles and drought change fuel and snags before fire acts; land conversion changes the edge microclimate around a stand. Forest survival is a property of forest-and-habitat, and fire and pests have no context-free sign. Interactions are typed bonds and cycles over a shared stand-atom set, so the conditions are isomers in the strong form and a functional group in the weak form. "Survives" is a threshold comparison, not a stock, and crossing the threshold substitutes a habitat that cannot be restored by removing one stressor alone. The framework returns this shape, eight failure conditions written before the run, and the instrument questions that would close the gaps. It does not return a stand forecast or a universal interaction coefficient.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Union and per-slot indexing; checkable by a forest ecologist against stand data

- **Statement:** the five conditions overlap, and the residual must be read per stand; a global survival percentage destroys the signal that says which stand is live.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** take a region where the mean forest condition is flat. Does the mean hide stands that flipped from sink to source or from forest to grass? Does one intervention clear a single-condition region while the drought-plus-pest intersection persists?
- **Refuter (pre-registered):** a global forest metric predicts which stand flips and when at the accuracy of per-stand readings, or one intervention clears all five conditions and their intersections at a stand.
- **Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9, Fig 1 plus WRI 2025 regional flip data).

### C2 - Residual-relative valence and contact cascades; checkable by an entomologist or fire ecologist

- **Statement:** fire and pests have no standing sign, and the damage runs through higher-order contact, not the direct pressure alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :52 - *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** do bark beetles recycle dead wood in an undisturbed stand and kill healthy trees in an outbreak? Does fire renew a fire-adapted stand and replace a fire-intolerant one with grasses and forbs when intervals shorten? Does drought change host susceptibility three steps before the measured mortality? A specific tempting bond is **declined**: the opened bark-beetle page reports that *"most studies of wildfire after beetle outbreaks have found no effect of beetle-caused tree mortality on wildfire size or severity."*
- **Refuter (pre-registered):** a context-free sign for fire or pests holds across adapted and intolerant stands and across endemic and outbreak populations, or removing the third-order cascade leaves the stand residual unchanged.
- **Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus fire-ecology and entomology sources); the beetle-to-fire bond is conflicted and declined, not counted as corroboration.

### C3 - Threshold, substitution and ground-versus-channel; checkable by a global-change researcher or monitoring specialist

- **Statement:** survival is a comparison at a horizon; crossing a threshold substitutes a habitat; the ground does not decay while the monitoring channel does.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :87 - *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. Operators :51 - *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*. Fig 11 :66 - *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*.
- **What to check:** after a cloud forest is cleared, does moisture recirculation cease and natural reforestation fail because conditions are too dry (alternative-stable-state source, opened)? Does a pooled satellite index stop registering a stand flip while the stand itself keeps changing (WRI caveat, opened)? Does removing the original stressor restore the former feedbacks, or does the new state persist?
- **Refuter (pre-registered):** a monotonic stock with no comparison orders survival across gradual and abrupt regimes; a crossed stand is restored by single-stressor removal alone; or an instrument whose reading moves with the analyst's pool choice is used successfully as the non-updating anchor.
- **Status:** `general` on the comparison and anchor rules, `specified` on the stand predictions, triangulated (Fig 12, Operators, Fig 11, Fig 10 plus threshold and accounting sources). **Live type question:** the comparison bond is defined for two quantities, but at stand level the operands are composed stand states (Fig 12 :104); whether they count as quantities at that level needs an instrument, not an argument.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a forecast for a named stand, a peak-mortality year, or a universal interaction coefficient.
- Not that all five pressures are harmful in every stand.
- Not that beetles cause larger fires, or that any single pressure is the cause of a given dieback reading. Several causes give one reading (Hard Problem essay, opened).
- Not that a crossed forest can be recovered by subtracting the stressor.
- Not that agreement among vegetation models corroborates the shape; shared evidence pools are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a stand measurement.
- Not that the figure set names heat, drought or fire as primitives. Those are bindings from the forest-science frame with provenance owed.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at the stand, not a further argument. The Hard Problem essay: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. Can a stand reading separate hydraulic failure, carbon starvation and beetle attack when all three give the same crown reading?
2. Does a threshold comparison hold between delivered conditions and stand demand, and what are its operands at that level?
3. Does removing the third-order cascade change the residual while the direct effect stays?
4. After a state shift, does removing the original stressor restore the former feedbacks?
5. Does the monitoring index move with the analyst's pool choice?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55 and :57, Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 11 :49 and :66, Fig 12 :87, Fig 10 :24. Eight short passages.
2. Name one stand you know where the mean hid a flip, and say whether C1 survives.
3. Say whether fire or pests have a fixed sign in your system, and whether the cascade in C2 is three steps out. If the beetle-to-fire link is real in your data, that is a correction to a declined bond, not to the valence rule.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish the sources of a dieback reading, or say why none exists.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
