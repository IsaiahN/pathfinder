## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "accuracy is a per-slot residual against an observational anchor, with numerical and physical fidelity unbundled and a class of source-discriminating instrument owed" is a shape. "IllustrisTNG is accurate to 5 percent" is a name and a number. The shape survives a re-census; the number has no ground reading here.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay form of Fig 9 :54. "How accurate are simulations" is exactly such a bundle, and section 1.3 of `01` unbundles it into two conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here the engineering V&V field is such a frame for the verification/validation split; it is recorded as an import with its provenance, not as an astronomical measurement.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the category limit: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* A simulation's outputs similarly do not contain its subgrid generator: the same galaxy population can be produced by different feedback implementations, as Crain and van de Voort 2023 report. And: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The CGM observable is such an axis for the subgrid question.

The same essay adds: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* This governs how far Fig 13 may be used in `01`: its six terms are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list, and they are used here only for the composition rule and the honest stop.

## 4. Why the single accuracy number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

In this question, the stellar mass function is the green reading. Several feedback bonds can produce it. A single accuracy number computed against it names the source of a projection without an instrument that discriminates among sources. What is owed is a source-discriminating observable, not a tighter fit to the same curve. This is the essay form of F4 and F7 in `01`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the caution on closure: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* A simulation that matches every measured observable within error is still a projection, and its provenance, calibration targets and round-trip gap must be recorded rather than inferred from the match. The same essay defines the live limit: being *"below the union horizon"* is *"never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

The famous simulation collaborations in this field are prestige proxies. Their scientific standing entitles them to a budget to be tried, not to a verdict that their baryon physics is accurate. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A contact list selected for people who already agree with the shape claim manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* applies the same rule to groups: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (quoting Fig 1 :22 in that essay) and *"A group's agreement is never the verdict."* Ten simulation groups reading the same review are one opinion said ten times.

## 6. Where the framework stops, and the honest status of this folder

- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*
- *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*: LLM output from the figures is *"simulacra"*, and *"the solutions presented"* could *"never be verified"* if they were *"contrived to fit the formula."*
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not ground readings, not measurements of any code's error, and cannot settle QMGR2-0059. Every substantive claim is labelled `specified`; the structural claims are labelled `general`. Nothing is `grounded`.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (visible, not erased)

I first wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids saying anything specific about which component carries the residual. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*. Gas, stars and dust are not missing primitives; they are the slots the question already names, and so are ordinary terms available to the derivation. What is forbidden is naming *the* accurate code or *the* correct subgrid prescription as if the framework returned a name. The shape constrains which slots to measure and what instrument would discriminate a failure, not which collaboration wins.

The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted. The error is naming a source from a reading that several sources can produce, which is what a single accuracy number against a calibration curve does. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. Consequence for the outreach artifacts

The two-stage offer follows from the shape reading. Stage 1 hands an outside frame the per-slot residual and its refuter and asks which slot they would expect to be live. Stage 2 is opt-in only, because once a recipient holds the whole framework their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). Stage 3 offers the formula as a method, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* No stage asks for trust, agreement, or a vote of confidence in a named code.
