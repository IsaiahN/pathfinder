## What the wider literature contributes (secondary only)

These are corroboration, never the verdict. Overlap is recorded because many of them draw on the same evidence pool.

1. **Bodily sensation is ground-facing but underdetermined.** The interoception article maps body states to feeling states; the Emotion article shows that the concepts applied to those states vary by culture. Together they predict that B is real but that E is not read off B. Corroborates F2. These two Wikipedia articles share some sources, so treat them as one pool with two views, not two independent witnesses.
2. **Culture is a weighting and a habitat rather than a new organ.** Cross-cultural psychology reports that constructs assumed universal failed replication outside North America and Europe, and that the field now studies variance directly. Motivation Science is reported as claimed etic-universal but its global validity is not established. This predicts that culture changes priors and vocabulary, corroborating F1. It conflicts with no figure line.
3. **The relation is not a fixed display rule.** Emotional labor documents display rules and bodily regulation strategies that differ across settings and cultures, predicting an ordered, learned mapping. Corroborates F2 and F5. Single field.
4. **Animal behaviour is the low-overlap check.** Emotion in animals shows affect-like states in species with no human culture and no explanatory vocabulary, predicting that B and a primitive E exist without X. That separates the slots: culture modulates the binding and the explanation, not the bare capacity. Corroborates F1 and F3. Different evidence pool from the cultural psychology sources.
5. **Mathematics and engineering frame the residual as a prediction error.** The free energy principle casts perception as inference over a generative model with sensory prediction error, and cites variational approaches to culture. This predicts that a prior (the weighting w) and a body signal (sensory states) are composed rather than one reading off the other. Corroborates F2 structurally. It is a different field, but note the risk: importing it as the explanation for the Tether would be an adopted import with no source gate, which Fig 8 :50 warns looks identical to convergent derivation. It is used here only as corroboration.
