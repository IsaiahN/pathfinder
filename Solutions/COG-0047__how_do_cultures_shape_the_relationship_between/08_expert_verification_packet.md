# Expert verification packet — the Tether on culture, emotion, body and explanation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cultural psychologist or motivation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** No cross-cultural measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A culture is not a new kind of mind. It is a weighting of inherited prior against private history, plus a library of available emotion concepts and a habitat of actors and conditions in contact with the person. Two people with identical architecture can diverge because their histories and their weightings diverge. The underlying six-term capacity is universal, so cultural variation is arrangement and weight, not new primitives. The relation among bodily sensation, emotion and explanation is ordered: the body is ground-facing, emotion is a binding of the signal to a concept, and explanation is a composition above it. Which bond holds is not readable off the relata and is left open. The three pairwise links form a union compound that must be checked separately, and two cultures count as two witnesses only at low evidence overlap.

### C2 — cognition-facing; checkable by an emotion or interoception researcher

- **Statement:** the relation is ordered B to E to X; the body is ground-facing; emotion is a binding, not a readout; the bond is not determined by the operands.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75, "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." - **What to check:** in your own data, does holding the interoceptive signal constant while changing available emotion concepts change the reported emotion? Interoception research defines the signal as "the physiological status of all tissues in the body" feeding "subjective feeling states that are critical for human emotion and self-awareness." If the emotion changes with concepts, the binding claim stands; if it does not, the body fixes the mapping and the shape breaks.
- **Refuter:** an instrument showing the body-to-emotion mapping is an identity fixed by physiology alone, independent of concept availability and prior; or two persons with identical signals and identical concepts reliably producing different emotions, which breaks the determinacy of the binding.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a culture determines a particular emotion. Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that all cultures are independent witnesses; overlap is checked, not assumed.
- Not that the emotion and the bodily signal are identical, or that one reads straight off the other. The ∥ and ≡ item is open (Operators table :79).
- Not that culture installs a fixed answer. Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating experiment, not further agreement.

1. **Concept-availability test:** manipulate available emotion concepts while holding the interoceptive signal constant. Emotion change with concept availability confirms the binding; no change breaks it.
2. **Body-to-emotion identity test:** do identical signals plus identical concepts give the same emotion reliably? A reliable difference breaks determinacy; a concept-independent identity breaks the ordering.
3. **Low-overlap triangulation:** two samples shown to draw on different evidence sources. Agreement then counts twice; shared source counts once.
4. **Isomer test:** list the atoms each culture actually uses for B, E and X. Matching atom lists with different bond or w confirms the isomer reading; differing lists reduce it to a functional group.
