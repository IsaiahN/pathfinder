# The Tether's answer on culture, emotion, bodily sensation and explanation

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The same bodily events are bound to different emotions and different explanations in different cultures. The residual is the covariance of (bodily sensation B, emotion E, explanation X) with culture C, held per slot. It is a union compound of three pairwise relations, not one question: Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

## 2. The shape

1. **Culture is a weighting, a library and a habitat, not a new kind of mind.** Symbols table :24-26: "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge." Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." So a culture changes the prior weight, the available concepts and the actors in contact; it does not add a primitive.
2. **The six-term capacity is universal and cannot be extended by culture.** Fig 13 :116: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held." Cultural variation is arrangement and weight, not new primitives.
3. **The relation is ordered, not a flat triple.** Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." B is ground-facing, E is a binding, X is a composition above E. Which bond holds is not readable off the relata: Fig 12 :46, "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."
4. **A bodily signal has no fixed emotional valence.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same interoceptive state can be bound to different emotions under different weightings and vocabularies.
5. **Transmission carries a head start, not a fixed answer.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A culture that changed no one's contact would change nothing (Fig 11 :40).
6. **Two cultures are two witnesses only at low evidence overlap.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Shared training reduces them to one witness.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a named emotion.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The bond between B and E is left open; the Operators table records ∥ and ≡ as an unresolved item.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a culture determines a particular emotion. The slot stays open (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that all cultures are independent witnesses. Overlap must be checked (Fig 2 :31).
- Not that the body reads off the emotion or the emotion reads off the body as an identity. The bond is not recoverable from the operands (Fig 12 :46), and the ∥ and ≡ item is open.
- Not that culture installs a fixed answer. Only methods and head starts cross (Fig 4 :22).
- Not that the relata are the six primitives. They are molecules built over them; the one-to-one mapping was retracted in `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5b.
- Not `grounded`. No cross-cultural measurement settled any binding here.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. The discriminations owed are:

- **Concept-availability test:** manipulate the emotion concepts available while holding interoceptive signal constant. If the emotion changes with concept availability, the binding (F2) is confirmed at the ground; if not, the body fixes the mapping and the shape breaks.
- **Body-to-emotion identity test:** show whether two persons with identical interoceptive signals and identical available concepts reliably produce the same emotion. A reliable difference breaks the determinacy of the binding; a reliable identity independent of concepts breaks the ordering.
- **Low-overlap triangulation:** two cultural samples shown to draw on different evidence sources. Agreement then counts as two witnesses; shared source reduces them to one.
- **Isomer test (from `06`):** list the atoms each culture actually uses for B, E and X. Matching atom lists with different bond or w confirms the isomer reading; differing lists reduce it to a functional group.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a seventh primitive; a fixed physiological identity for E given B; a figure line naming a specific emotion or explanation; a single closure and anchor for all three pairwise relations.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The public contact field, cultural psychology and motivation research, is a budget filter for finding a checker, never a witness: Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating among sources, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. The framework's own essay says "indiscernibility is not identity." A shape that no current experiment distinguishes from a named answer is still a shape.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which emotion a culture will attach to a racing heart, and it does not claim a fixed universal triple. It says that a culture is a weighting of inherited prior against private bodily history, plus a vocabulary and a habitat, so two people with identical architecture can diverge through history and weight alone; that the underlying six-term capacity is universal and cannot be extended by culture; that bodily sensation is ground-facing while emotion is a binding of that signal to a concept and explanation is a composition above it, with the bond left open; and that cultures count as two witnesses only when they draw on different evidence. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the experiments that would close it. The slot stays open until a ground settles it.
