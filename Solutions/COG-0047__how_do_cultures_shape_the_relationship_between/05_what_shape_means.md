## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 states that the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of a result you can state before you can name its content. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "the relation is an ordered cross-kind composition with an open bond and a weighted prior" is a shape. "Culture makes people feel X" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the residual was stated as a covariance of three slots with culture, not as "how culture changes emotion," which already picks a frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- "How Intelligence Actually Works" (read directly): "The framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time." And the stopping rule: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."
- The same essay: "Diversity is a natural hedge," because "changes in diet, or having an assortment of different perspectives in a brainstorming session can prevent outcomes from drifting further than they would with groupthink." This is the framework's own reason for wanting low-overlap cultural frames rather than a single consensus.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For COG-0047 the ground would be a within-session cross-cultural experiment that manipulates concept availability while measuring interoception, emotion and explanation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A cross-cultural finding imported into the framework must say where it came from and which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. Why mapping the three relata straight onto primitives is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can give the same reading. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question the temptation is to read a bodily signal and name its source as a specific emotion, or to read a cultural practice and name its source as a specific primitive. That is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A derivation cannot certify that it named the right emotion for a culture.

## 5. Q: what does "shape" mean for this question?

**Answer.** It means the arrangement-class that any cultural answer must have, stated before any particular emotion, bodily label or explanation is named. Concretely, from the figure lines re-read this session:

- an ordered cross-kind composition, because Fig 13 :75 forbids same-kind direct joins;
- a ground-facing slot (the body) and a binding slot (emotion), because Fig 12 :46 says which bond holds is not recoverable from the operands and Fig 11 :49 says valence is relative to the residual;
- a weighting between inherited prior and private history, because the Symbols table :24-26 says two frames with identical architecture diverge through histories and w;
- a habitat to be enumerated, because Fig 11 :43 says the habitat is enumerated, never composed;
- a union compound of three pairwise relations, because Fig 9 :54 says to unbundle a union;
- an open slot, because Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself.

**Does the framework license naming a specific emotion or explanation here?** No. The six-term set names capacities, not the content of a culture's emotion vocabulary. Selecting a specific label is a binding from the cultural psychology frame, and it must be provenanced as such. Until a ground settles it, the slot stays open.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on selecting any term already in the library. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Gamma. The relata of this question (emotion, bodily sensation, explanation) are not primitives of the six-term set at all; they are compositions over it. So two distinct mistakes have to be kept apart:

- **Retracted:** mapping emotion to Gradient and explanation to Network one-to-one. These are molecules built from the six, not the six. That mapping names a frame first and the residual second, the apophenia the exemplar calls "echo without shadow." It is withdrawn.
- **Kept:** refusing to name which specific emotion or explanation a culture produces. That is not because the term is missing from Gamma but because the framework returns the arrangement-class, not the content. The Figure 9 :32 limit still governs, and the binding still needs provenance.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous cultural psychologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground, an experiment or a dataset settle it. The public contact field "cultural psychology and motivation research" is a filter for where to look, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
