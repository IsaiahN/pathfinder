- **Domain 3, explanatory frame.** Actors: causal attributions, narratives, models of self, display rules. Currency: the composition that binds E and B into an account. Anchor: observed behavior and the predictiveness of the attribution. Frame with a behavioral anchor.
- **Domain 4, cultural or historical frame.** Actors: many persons, a shared library, inherited priors, practices and language. Currency: transmission of methods and head starts across the membrane. Anchor: the same ground as its members, reached through their contact; no separate anchor. Population-scale frame, not a second ground.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception to vocabulary to objective to regulation to carrying across generations. Culture acts at the vocabulary-to-objective link, where Fig 3 says the instrument is missing. Unbundle the three pairwise relations before searching (Fig 9 :54).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the crowd-pleasing mappings do not. Culture is a weighting, a library and a habitat, so two people with identical architecture diverge through history and w, and no seventh primitive is introduced. The body is ground-facing, emotion is a binding of that signal to a concept and explanation is a second-order composition above it; which bond holds is not readable off the relata and stays open. The three pairwise relations are a union compound to be checked separately, and two cultures count as two witnesses only at low evidence overlap. The specific emotion/bodily bond is a `gap` because the Operators table records ∥ and ≡ as open. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded result. The one-to-one mapping of the relata onto the six primitives was retracted, not confirmed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named mapping instead of a generator, which Fig 4 :25 prices as "a recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The retracted one-to-one mappings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already omit them. An open slot can be closed by a measurement without withdrawing any primitive. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A concept-availability experiment that changes the emotion while holding the body signal constant would confirm the binding; a demonstration that the body-to-emotion mapping is a physiological identity independent of concepts would break the ordering; a derivation of a seventh primitive or a figure line naming a specific emotion would break the shape; an instrument that directly measures what counts as a good explanation across cultures would move the dependency break; and a resolved canonical reading of ∥ or ≡ would close the recorded `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
