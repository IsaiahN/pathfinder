# Expert verification packet: Are other cosmological parameters also in tension?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No new measurement or likelihood fit is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The disagreement between early-universe and late-universe inference should be reported as a vector of residuals indexed by parameter slot, not as a single yes or no about H0. The support of that vector includes at least H0 and structure growth (S8 or sigma8), with H0 the larger persistent residual; the slots share atoms (Omega_m, the sound horizon r_d, the model), so they form a union compound rather than a list of independent confirmations. Independence must be audited per pair of frames because two collaborations calibrating on the same atom are one witness. Any proposed mechanism must be scored against the whole vector, since a change that lowers H0 while shifting lensing or clustering has moved residual rather than explained it. The framework stops before naming a mechanism and offers the shape, pre-registered refuters and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1

- **Statement:** the governing residual is indexed per parameter slot, and aggregating slots destroys the signal that says where to look.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :28: *"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."* Supporting: Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
- **What to check:** is the parameter vector a legitimate set of object slots here, or are the slots only different parameterisations of one quantity?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an exact one-to-one map among all non-H0 slots and H0 under one model and one dataset, so no slot carries information the others do not; then per-slot indexing adds nothing and C1 is withdrawn.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable against published likelihoods

- **Statement:** structure growth is a distinct live slot sharing atoms with H0, so the two form a union compound with a nonempty intersection; their agreement is not two independent witnesses.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
- **What to check:** using a published covariance matrix, is there a nonzero persistent residual in S8 or sigma8 once H0 is fixed, and which calibration atoms do the relevant early and late chains actually share? The Abdalla et al. (2022) review and the Martinelli and Sapone (2026) null test are public starting points.
- **Refuter:** show that every non-H0 slot has zero persistent residual with shared atoms accounted for, or show that the ostensibly independent chains use disjoint calibration data and disjoint likelihoods. Then C2 narrows to a single-slot claim.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a physics-facing consequence

- **Statement:** an import (new physics or a calibration shift) must be judged against all slots with provenance, because a single lever clears the H0-only region and leaves the intersection standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*; Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** for one candidate mechanism, does a published joint fit report the effect on H0, S8, Omega_m, lensing and the sound horizon together, with the source of any new atom stated?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an import with a complete per-slot table that closes H0 while every other slot remains strictly inside its previous error budget. Then the intersection was not binding and C3 is withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a measurement. Nothing here is `grounded`; no new significance or fit was computed.
- Not a name for the mechanism. Early dark energy, extra Neff and modified gravity are bindings from the physics frame, not derivations from the figures.
- Not that every parameter is in tension. Slots with near-zero residuals are reported, not promoted.
- Not that the S8 slot is a second independent witness for the same cause. It is a second slot in a union.
- Not a final answer or a theory of everything. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not a claim that the simulated second seat corroborates the primary. It shares the same evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A per-slot likelihood table (H0, S8, Omega_m, r_d, age) under one model with explicit covariance.
2. An independence audit naming the shared calibration atoms for each pair of frames.
3. An assumption-isolating null test, for example the four channels of Martinelli and Sapone (2026).
4. A simultaneous, model-light determination of H0, S8 and r_d, which would test the tension itself.
5. A whole-vector import table for any candidate mechanism, with provenance and shadow test.

Standing pre-registered refuter from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: if every non-H0 slot has zero persistent residual with shared atoms accounted for, the "confined" option wins and this packet is withdrawn.

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54-55, Fig 2 :31 and Fig 6 :14, four short passages.
