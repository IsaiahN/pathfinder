## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, and stops at the limit of what the instruments distinguish, recording the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the constrained form of the answer you can state before you can name the mechanism: a per-parameter residual vector over a union compound, with independence audited per pair of frames and an import judged against the whole vector. `H0 in tension, others not` is a claim about the support of that vector. `Early dark energy is the answer` is a name, and the framework does not license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing and bootstrap. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest vocabulary is the published parameter list itself, read off the likelihoods rather than composed from theory.
- *Library of Babel* on bundle detection: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay's own version of Fig 9 :54, and it is the hinge of this question.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach email sends a description of the expected per-slot table, not the framework.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A source-discriminating measurement would name the mechanism, not a further argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). The import adds an atom; it does not get to rename the residual.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason with metamerism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It concludes: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Applied here: several mechanisms can yield the same upward shift in H0, so the reading cannot determine which mechanism is real. What is owed is a discriminator, not a preference.

So naming early dark energy, extra relativistic species or a calibration shift from the tension alone is the metamerism error. It is a binding from the physics frame onto the shape, to be provenanced as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* For this question the anchor is the likelihood under a stated model, plus instruments such as time-delay lensing and sound-horizon-free estimators. It is not the number of reviews that agree.

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The cosmology sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` share published data and review literature. Their agreement is one pool talking. Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."*

Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Adding another analysis of the same supernova anchors does not change contact. Adding an independent lensing channel does.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape here?** A per-slot residual vector with a union structure, an audited independence matrix, and a mechanism slot that stays open. Equivalently: the answer says *where* the residual lives and *what any candidate mechanism must do*, not *which mechanism is true*.

**Does the world evidence supply a name?** No. The Abdalla et al. (2022) review lists several candidate mechanisms; the Wang and Guo (2026) review reports that fixes shift other observables. That is exactly the source-indistinguishability the shape predicts, not an exception to it.

**Is this a ban on discussing mechanisms?** No. Binding a candidate to the shape is allowed if the provenance is stated. What is banned is presenting the binding as a derivation from the framework or as settled by agreement.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the per-slot shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a likelihood or an instrument, settle it.

**My own derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This file, `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**A correction to keep visible.** I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding any mention of a specific parameter slot. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive or mechanism. Parameter slots (H0, S8, Omega_m) are not missing primitives; they are already bound terms in the physics frame. Listing them in the table is a binding, not an invention and not a breach of the shape limit. The limit bites only when a mechanism is named as the answer. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
