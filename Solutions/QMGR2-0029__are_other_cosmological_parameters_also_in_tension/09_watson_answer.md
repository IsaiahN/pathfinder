## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the scope of the residual between early-universe and late-universe cosmological inference. Option A: the problem is confined to the expansion rate H0, all other slots being consistent once H0 is fixed. Option B: the residual has support on several parameter slots (H0, S8 or sigma8, Omega_m, the sound horizon r_d, the age), which share atoms and form a union compound. What each leads to: A licenses a single-lever fix and treats other slots as settled; B requires a per-slot table, an independence audit per pair of frames, and a mechanism scored against the whole vector. The figures decide which shape an answer must have. A world source may corroborate that shape but may not overrule it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not say which new physics is real. They say what an answer must look like. The residual is indexed per parameter slot, so the question is answered by a per-slot likelihood table, not by a single verdict on H0. The support of that vector includes at least H0 and structure growth, which are distinct terms sharing atoms, hence a union compound with a nonempty intersection. Independence is bought per pair of frames, not per paper. An import is judged against the whole vector with provenance, and the framework stops before naming a mechanism. So the honest answer to the question as asked is: concentrated in the expansion rate in the sense that H0 carries the largest persistent residual, but not confined to it in the sense that matters for a fix. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- cost if wrong: if the shape is wrong, a real single-slot problem is turned into a multi-slot research programme and effort is spent on a phantom intersection. That cost is bounded and locatable because the table and its refuter are written first. The larger avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework before the residual: Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Leading with the table and the refuter avoids it.
- undoable: yes. The shape can be narrowed to a single slot by the first refuter without withdrawing any primitive or touching the framework. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. Nothing in the derivation is irreversible.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot likelihood table showing zero persistent non-H0 residuals, an import that closes H0 while leaving every other slot inside its prior error, or a simultaneous model-light determination of H0, S8 and r_d showing all slots agree would reverse the verdict. A source-discriminating instrument naming one mechanism would move the mechanism slot from open to bound. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
