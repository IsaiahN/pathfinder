# The Tether's answer: Are other cosmological parameters also in tension, or is the problem confined to the expansion rate?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two inference frames, early universe and late universe, return parameter vectors that disagree. The question asks for the support of the disagreement across slots. In effect terms the residual is a vector over H0, structure growth (S8 or sigma8), Omega_m, the sound horizon r_d and the inferred age. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a list: the slots share atoms, so a lever that clears one can leave the intersection standing (Fig 9 :54-56).

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**D1, observational frames.** Report residuals per frame and audit independence per pair. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Two collaborations calibrating on the same sound horizon or the same supernova magnitude are one witness. Time-delay lensing is a genuinely different closure; Wong et al. (2019) describe their method as *"completely independent of both the supernovae and CMB analyses."* **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 2 plus Fig 7 :12-13).

**D2, parameter inference.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The live slots are H0 and structure growth, with H0 the larger persistent residual and other anomalies of lower mass. Significance ranks where to look, not what exists: Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 5 :59; empirical ranking from world sources only.**

**D3, proposed mechanisms.** An import moves the wall, but must be scored against the whole vector and carry provenance (Fig 6 :14, Fig 8 :50). A fix that lowers H0 while raising the lensing or clustering residual has moved residual, not explained a gap. The framework names no mechanism: Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 9.**

**D4, verification.** The anchor is the ground, not agreement. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* This simulated second seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. **Verdict: `general`, single-source for the seat rule.**

Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. Averaging them would recreate the aggregation error of F1 at the level of domains.

### The answer in one line

The problem is **not** confined to the expansion rate in the sense that matters for a fix: the residual has support on at least two covariant slots, so any candidate mechanism must be judged on the whole vector. It **is** concentrated in the expansion rate in the sense that H0 carries the largest persistent residual. Both halves have to be said, because collapsing them into one yes or no is exactly the aggregate the figures forbid.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a measurement. No new significance, no new fit and no new likelihood was computed here.
- Not a name for the mechanism. Early dark energy, extra relativistic species, modified gravity and calibration shifts are bindings from the physics frame, not derivations.
- Not that every parameter is in tension. Slots with a residual near zero stay near zero in the table; they are reported, not promoted.
- Not that the S8 slot is a second independent witness. It is a second slot in a union, sharing Omega_m and the model.
- Not a theory of everything and not a final answer. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not grounded. Nothing here is settled by the ground.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, 2026-08-28) says an instrument that reads a value *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

1. **Per-slot likelihood table:** compute H0, S8, Omega_m, r_d and age residuals under the same model and an explicit covariance matrix. If every non-H0 slot has zero persistent residual once H0 is fixed, the "confined" option wins.
2. **Independence audit:** name the calibration atoms shared by each pair of frames. If a slot has two genuinely disjoint chains, its residual is a real witness; if not, it is one witness counted twice.
3. **Assumption-isolating null test:** run the four-channel decomposition of Martinelli and Sapone (2026), or a sound-horizon-free estimator, to see which assumption carries the shift.
4. **Whole-vector import test:** for any candidate mechanism, show the effect on every slot with provenance. If one closes H0 and leaves all others inside their prior errors, the union reading is withdrawn.
5. **Standing refuter from section 4 of `01`:** a simultaneous model-free determination of H0, S8 and r_d that shows all slots agreeing would refute the tension itself.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Some slots have no discriminator yet; that is recorded as a stop, not converted into a claim of absence. The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list, so the report says what could have been concluded and what would reverse it. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The gap is enumerated qualitatively in `11_audit.md`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a cause of the Hubble tension. It says the question should be answered as a table of residuals, one row per cosmological parameter, because the disagreement signal is indexed per slot and vanishes into an average. On that reading the problem is both concentrated and not confined: H0 carries the largest persistent residual, but structure growth is a second live slot, and the two share atoms such as Omega_m and the sound horizon, so they form one union compound rather than two independent confirmations. Independence has to be bought per pair of frames, since two teams using the same calibration are one witness. Any proposed new physics must be scored against the whole table, because a fix that lowers H0 while shifting lensing or clustering has moved the residual rather than explained it. The framework returns that shape, its pre-registered refuters and the instruments that would close it, and stops before naming a mechanism. This is a simulated derivation, not a measurement.
