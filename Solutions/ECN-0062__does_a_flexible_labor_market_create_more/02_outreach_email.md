# Outreach draft: check the split before you decide about flexibility

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the findings in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are repaired. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One claim to check before you decide whether the flexibility question is well posed

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to read a theory first. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as posed:** does a flexible labor market create more jobs, or mainly shift risk from employers to workers? I think the either/or is malformed, and I wrote down what would refute that before running the derivation.
>
> **The output (`general` on the refusal, `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the empirical choice):** the two outcomes are not competing answers to one question. They are effects on different slots produced by the same lever. A count of jobs is an ordering; a transfer of risk is a directedness. The same flexibility arrangement can be a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so both can be true at once. The correct shape of an answer is two verdicts with two grounds, plus an explicit account of the intersection where the same lever moves both. A single national employment average is the wrong instrument, because a near-zero aggregate is compatible with a live risk residual on one contract type.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not claiming that flexibility has no effect on employment, nor that it always shifts risk. I am not reporting a country-level estimate. I am not asking you to accept a framework. The empirical choice stays open until measured per-slot outcomes settle it.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. One measured quantity that is simultaneously the job count and the risk incidence, so the two are one slot under two names.
> 2. Employment and risk residuals perfectly correlated across slots, so the average loses no signal.
> 3. A fixed valence of flexibility that does not depend on which residual is at issue.
> 4. A derivation from the framework alone that returns a country-specific employment effect with no imported data.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README, canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The world literature I actually read: see `10_source_ledger.csv` in the accompanying folder, with verified URLs and quotes.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the framework files. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, explicit

1. **Stage 1.** Send the split, the shape and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict **before** any framework material is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the local figure text, the canonical README and the source ledger. The figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

One-to-one only. No bulk send. No scraping personal addresses. Public institutional and opt-in channels from `03_public_contact_points.csv` only.
