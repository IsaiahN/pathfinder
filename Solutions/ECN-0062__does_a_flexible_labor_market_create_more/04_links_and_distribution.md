## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The aggregate employment claim is weak; the distributional claim is stronger.** Rodgers (2007) says the evidence does not support higher employment from flexibility but does support an effect on the distribution of employment among groups. The World Bank (2024) says there is no clear relationship between employment protection and unemployment rates, while firing costs reduce both hiring and firing. This is the world-side counterpart of the figure verdict that the residual must be read per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :22).

2. **The two halves of the question appear together in one source.** Eichhorst et al. (2019) state that shifting risk from employers to workers creates insecurity for workers "at the same time as it provides employers with greater flexibility." That is exactly the valence claim: the same arrangement is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :50).

3. **Contract form is the unbundling variable.** The ILO (2016) defines precariousness by who bears the risk, not by contract label, and reports wage penalties and lower benefit coverage. The World Bank (2024) reports that flexibility through temporary contracts can produce dual labor markets with lower productivity and higher wage inequality. So "flexibility" fails the MDL bargain unless the forms are separated (Fig 5 :43).

4. **The frames are not independent.** Rodgers, the OECD, the ILO and the World Bank draw on overlapping policy literature and EPL data. By Fig 2 :40 their agreement is one pool talking. The lower-overlap witnesses are Green and Leeves (worker well-being), Rubery et al. (employment relations), Bieber and Moggia (political philosophy) and Peetz (public employment-relations commentary). Agreement across those and the econometric work would carry more weight than agreement within it.

5. **No source conflicts with the figure verdict.** The conflict rule was not triggered. Where the literature is itself mixed, that is corroboration of the split, not a reason to average.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A reader who has absorbed the whole derivation before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the split claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."** A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not."** A long chain of agreeing labor economists is one long room, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters, (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure, (3) record that verdict, (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the split without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The stage-1 output here is a framing refusal and a shape, not a country estimate, so it is checkable without any new instrument. If a referee shows that the two outcomes are one slot, the whole packet is withdrawn.
