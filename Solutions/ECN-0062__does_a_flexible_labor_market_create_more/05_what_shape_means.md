## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it or measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: two slots instead of one either/or, each measured on its own ground, with the valence of flexibility left relative to the residual rather than fixed. A country-level point estimate would be a name, not a shape, and the framework cannot produce one without imported data.

The same essay names the exact defect in the question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is the either/or in this question, almost verbatim.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 says "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Here the effect terms are "count of jobs" and "who bears income variance", not the jargon word "flexibility".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach in `02_outreach_email.md` leads with what a referee would expect to see if the split were true.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." A shape is not manufactured by adding more labor economists who read the same EPL dataset.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." (the step is labelled SETTLE in the source; a comma replaces the source's dash here to honour the no-em-dash rule). For this question that means measured per-slot employment and income-risk outcomes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8). The world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are imports of that kind, and their overlap is declared.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Declaring that the two relevant slots are "jobs" and "risk incidence" is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; several compositions produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." What resolved it was a spectrometer, not an argument.

The world-side counterpart here: a national employment rate is a projection. It cannot say whether jobs were created by higher hiring on permanent contracts or by churn on temporary contracts. The source question, who bears the risk, is invisible in that reading. The same aggregate can arise from different contract mixtures (ILO 2016; World Bank 2024). What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, that is, per-contract and per-worker data, not another argument about the sign of flexibility.

Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." A pooled employment rate is a channel that can go stale; the ground underneath it does not.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does a correct answer look like before it is measured?**

- It has **two verdicts**, one for the employment slot and one for the risk-incidence slot, each with its own mode and its own ground. It does not average them (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").
- It states the **objective** before the instrument: is the aim a higher count, a fairer distribution of variance, or both with a stated trade rule (Fig 3)?
- It treats the valence of flexibility as **relative to the residual**, not as a fixed sign (Fig 11 :49).
- It **unbundles** flexibility by contract form and by the surrounding habitat, because a flat term that explains everything by saying nothing fails the bargain (Fig 5 :43 to :44).
- It keeps the empirical choice marked `gap` until a ground reading arrives, and it reports the route so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,").

**What is not licensed:** a country-level sign on employment, a universal claim that flexibility only shifts risk, or a ranking of expert opinions as a verdict. Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 6. Two consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A petition signed by famous economists gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not settle whether flexibility created jobs or moved risk. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest*: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." And the same article warns that simulated outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the labor-market claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
