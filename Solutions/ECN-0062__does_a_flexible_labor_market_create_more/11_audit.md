## 1. Independent domain census

Working from the residual line alone: "what changes on the ground when hiring, dismissal and contract rules are loosened, given that two effects are offered: a count of jobs and a transfer of risk from employer to worker."

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** Arity two or more: a level/count and a directed transfer. Symmetry: the count is an ordering (more or less); the transfer is directed (employer to worker), so the operands are not interchangeable. Scale: the count is naturally read at a national level, the transfer at the level of a contract or worker. That scale mismatch is itself a warning: a single instrument cannot span both without loss.
- **Domain A, the hirer.** Actors: firms. Currency: adjustment cost, hiring and separation. Anchor: payroll and vacancies. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain B, the bearer.** Actors: workers. Currency: income variance, tenure, benefits. Anchor: administrative income records. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain C, the match pool.** Actors: vacancies and seekers. Currency: flows and rates. Anchor: statistical agency counts. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain D, the rule set.** Actors: legislators and courts. Currency: statutory rules and EPL scores. Anchor: the law. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain E, the checking frame.** Actors: researchers. Currency: estimates. Anchor: data and reproducible estimates, never consensus. Nameable: yes.
- **Non-domain:** "flexibility" has no anchor of its own; it is a condition inside A to D. Its sign cannot be assigned without naming the residual.

**Partition or union?** Union. The same lever changes both the hiring decision and the contract form, so the two effects overlap. A single lever would clear the part where only one condition holds and leave the intersection standing. This was reached from the scale mismatch and the shared lever, before reading the primary's Fig 9 route.

**Dependency order.** Can we perceive the three quantities (yes) -> do we have terms for the contract forms (yes) -> what counts as progress (contested, and under-instrumented) -> identification strategy -> carry forward. The objective link is the break.

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. I reached it from the arity/scale mismatch and the shared lever; the primary reached it from Fig 9's union/partition split. Different starting points, same boundaries. That is weak internal agreement, not a witness.
