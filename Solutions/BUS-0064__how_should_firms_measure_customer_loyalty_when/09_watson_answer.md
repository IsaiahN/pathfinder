## QUESTION

How should firms measure customer loyalty when repeat purchases may reflect habit, switching costs, or a lack of alternatives?

Restated as an effect: the firm observes `o' = bought again` at customer slot `s` and time `t`, and wants a latent commitment that would survive a rival. At least four sources produce the same reading, so the residual is source ambiguity, not measurement noise. Option A: adopt a single loyalty score and treat it as the recovered state. Option B: refuse the scalar, keep the reading per slot, enumerate the sources, build a discriminator by varying conditions, and range low-overlap frames against a market anchor. A sends a reading that cannot comment on its source and rewards lock-in as commitment. B sends a checkable protocol, a pre-registered refuter, and an honest stop where instruments fail.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Symmetry: the collapse is symmetric in the sources, but the sources separate only under an ordered intervention, so the shape carries a comparison bond rather than a symmetric conjunction. Scale: per customer slot and per occasion, ordered in time; a firm aggregate discards the index. The Symbols table says of R: *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice"*. - **D1 Customer behavior (the reading).** Actors: the customer, the purchase occasion. Currency: repeat behavior. Anchor: the market, customer choice when an alternative is available. Admitted.
- **D2 Measurement instrument (the metric).** Actors: retention rate, share of wallet, NPS, surveys, experiments. Currency: discrimination between sources. Anchor: whether the metric tracks the latent state under intervention. Admitted. This is where the ambiguity lives.
- **D3 Competitive habitat (the sources).** Actors: rivals, alternatives, procedural/relational/financial switching costs, habit cues, convenience, availability. Currency: contact and switching. Anchor: customer choice; the habitat is read off the world. Admitted.
- **D4 Verification frame.** Actors: analysts, researchers, expert readers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted, with independence = 0 in this simulated run because all seats share one evidence pool.
- **X1 Loyalty as a directly readable trait.** No nameable anchor: nothing reads the trait without a behavior or a stated response. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Satisfaction as sole cause.** Already inside D3 as one habitat condition. Absorbed.
- **X3 Habit, lock-in and no-alternative as three separate problems.** They are overlapping conditions of one projection, not separately anchored problems. Union compound with its own loop.

**Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :55 says *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A customer can be habitual and locked in and have no alternative at once; a case in two conditions is categorically different (Fig 9 :57). **Order by dependency:** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: a repeat purchase is a lossy projection, so no scalar recovers its source. The residual must be kept per customer and per occasion, the four conditions treated as an overlapping union, the market used as a non-updating anchor, and a source-discriminating instrument built from the existing reading by varying the rival, the switching cost, the habit cue and the choice set. Behavioral, stated and experimental frames are ranged separately against the ground; verdicts are kept per slot and never averaged. Naming a specific loyalty metric is a `gap`, a verdict and not a silence. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The business pool corroborates the attitudinal/behavioral split but cannot decide the figure verdict; the low-overlap attribution source corroborates the instrument shape; the conflict rule was never triggered.

- **cost if wrong:** a firm buys a retention program aimed at lock-in while believing it bought commitment, and an expert's twenty minutes are spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and locatable, because the protocol, mode and refuters are recorded and a later reader can find the error.
- **undoable:** yes. If the claim fails no term is deleted; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers carry provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the seven pre-registered refuters in `01` section 3: a non-lossy reading; an aggregate that predicts individual switching under a rival at least as well as a per-slot measure; a metric invariant to the effort used to raise it; one intervention clearing the union; genuine indiscernibility at every resolution; an invariant valence for a standing driver list; or a scalar that recovers the source against the ground. A genuinely different evidence pool showing per-slot discrimination adds no predictive power over one aggregate would also reverse it.
