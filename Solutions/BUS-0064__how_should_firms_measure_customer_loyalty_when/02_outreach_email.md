# Outreach draft: check the measurement claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No follow-up sequence and no request for an endorsement.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter are understood well enough to state in your own words. If the stage-1 output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about loyalty metrics, and what would refute it

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about loyalty measurement. I am not asking you to read any framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a repeat purchase is a reading that cannot comment on its own source. Habit, switching cost or lock-in, and the absence of a viable alternative all produce the same observable, "bought again". So a single loyalty number, whether retention rate, share of wallet or a recommendation score, collapses four different states into one reading. The figure-derived shape is this: the residual must be kept per customer and per occasion; the four sources form a union of overlapping conditions, so one lever leaves the intersections standing; and what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument that varies the conditions, ranged against a market anchor that does not move when the metric is redefined. The framework returns a shape, not a name. It does not say which commercial metric to adopt.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that surveys are useless, that satisfaction is irrelevant, or that any named score is the answer. Scores are fine as filters for where to look. They are not witnesses for what a behavior means. I am also not claiming a universal percentage of loyal customers, and I am not reporting a measurement. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this was sent):**
> 1. a reading of repeat purchase that maps one-to-one onto its source across customers, so the behavior is not lossy;
> 2. a firm-level aggregate that predicts individual switching under a rival at least as well as a per-customer measure;
> 3. one intervention that removes commitment, habit, switching cost and no-alternative at the same time and leaves a single clean residue;
> 4. a scalar metric that recovers, against the ground, which source produced each repeat purchase.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source material is below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them. I am sending these only after your verdict on the paragraph above is recorded, so that a later look at the theory cannot quietly become part of the verdict.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The figures as metalaws (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is in the figures material. Run it on a measurement problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the output: the residual in effect terms, the shape claim, and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before any framework material is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local figure files, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**One-to-one only.** No list, no automation, no misleading subject line, and an implicit opt-out in the "no reply needed" line. Public institutional submission pages in `03` are preferred over any personal address.
