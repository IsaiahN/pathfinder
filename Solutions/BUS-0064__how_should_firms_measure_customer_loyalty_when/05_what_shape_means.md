## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the form of a measurement that would discriminate the source of a behavior, stated before we can name the specific commercial instrument. The shape constrains: it must be per slot, must vary conditions, must range low-overlap frames against a non-updating anchor, and must refuse a scalar recovery of the source. It does not fill: it does not say "use NPS" or "use a 10 percent weight for attitude".

## 2. The governing essay for this question: source, not projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened and read 2026-11-22) is the closest essay to this question. The author poses the problem as metamerism:

> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"

> "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"

> "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

> "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

That is the question in other words. A repeat purchase is the green reading. Commitment, habit, lock-in and no-alternative are the Yellow+Blue, RGB, CMYK and undiscovered schemes that can produce it. The instrument "bought again" cannot comment on which. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the color case; here the corresponding move is to vary the conditions so the sources stop being metameric.

The essay also supplies the honesty rule for this folder: a derivation that cannot discriminate its sources demonstrates its own inadequacy. So a loyalty metric that returns one number while claiming to have recovered commitment is the very failure the essay names.

## 3. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here the effect is "would the customer stay if a rival appeared", not "is the customer attached".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- The shape is reached when the set of instruments that could produce the reading is small and describable. That is the stop.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here: an intervention produces a source-specific prediction and the market pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a differing closure** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here: the four conditions are imported from marketing science into the measurement frame, and their provenance must be recorded, not treated as figure-derived.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The four sources are a visible choice from the business frame, not nature's own list.

## 5. Correction to my own §4 and to the audit's F1

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any metric. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Here the source of the behavior is genuinely missing from the measurement library, so the rule applies with full force: the framework can say "a discriminator is owed" but not "use net promoter score". Where a firm simply names a condition it already holds (switching cost, habit, availability), that is a provenanced binding, not a forbidden invention and not a wall-moving import (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

I also called the four sources "isomers" outright in the first draft of the audit. That is downgraded to a `specified` structural analogy, with the strain recorded as S1 in `01`: Fig 12's bonds are typed composition relations, whereas the four sources differ by causal history. Both corrections are kept visible in `01` section 5. Neither is silently erased.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous marketing academics who endorse a loyalty metric is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator against a ground, not a set of signatures.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of a loyalty metric and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. One consequence for this question

The practical consequence is that the correct answer to "how should firms measure loyalty" is a protocol, not a score. A score is a reading; the question asks about the source of the reading. By the essay's own rule, an instrument that cannot distinguish the four sources cannot claim to measure loyalty. It can claim to measure repeat purchase, and it should say so.
