## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- a shape: "intervention sits between the reading and the term, in its own currency, and makes the term separately correctable";
- a name: "the experiment that did this was Galileo's inclined plane in 1604".

The first constrains any answer. The second is a historian's claim that requires different evidence and a different ground.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *"closest"* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *The Library of Babel* on the stop condition: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing."* The question is the tether.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why §1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` states the residual as an effect before naming any figure.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *The Library of Babel* on the paradox: by the time you have the exact search terms, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as the answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

For this question the relevant name is a historical one: which experiment, which year, which person. No figure line can supply it, so the name must come from a historian's ground (archives, instruments, records), with provenance. F8 records this as a `gap`.

## 4. Why the chronology is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several paths produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

A historian's narrative can be fitted to any of several readings of "experiment changed science." What is owed is a discriminator: a source-discriminating instrument, an archive, a dated protocol, an independent reading. Until one is brought, the chronology is a projection, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. The three terms as the essays name them

*They're Scanning Books* states the triad directly, in a form that can be carried to this question without strain:

> **"Science is another: experiment is the walk, theory is the model, and the anomaly-chaser is the recognizer."**

Mapped in effect terms:

| essay term | question term | what it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| the walk | intervention | unguided, instrumented contact that samples what no hypothesis pointed at |
| the model | explanation | the term that can be violated, the φ that pays or fails |
| the recognizer | observation, as the reading that registers a violation | the o′ that decides whether the walk found something |

This is the same union the census found, reached from a different essay and with a different vocabulary. It is still the author's evidence pool, so it is a second rendering, not a second witness.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: *"Measurement doesn't reveal the value. It creates the value by collapsing the space of values."* And: *"Contact Is the Distinction Resolver."* That is F1 and F3 in the essay's terms: the intervention is not a passive look; contact resolves which distinction is being made.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the verification side: *"An experiment, an outcome, a measurement."* And: *"A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure"* (Fig 10 :72 quoted there). A protocol that does not change when the question changes is a recording, not a method.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read the rule as a blanket ban on naming any part of the answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ.

In this question, observation, intervention and explanation are all already in the reader's library. What is missing is the **bond structure between them**, and the chronology of that change. So:

- saying that intervention is a distinct currency and that the reading must not update is a shape; it is licensed by Fig 2, Fig 12 and formula step 1;
- saying which experiment first achieved this is a name; it is not licensed by any figure line and needs a historian's ground.

The correction is kept visible. It does not rescue the chronology; it narrows what the ban applies to. The same correction is carried in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and in `11_audit.md`.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: a famous experiment is a filter for which case to study (Fig 9 :60), not a witness for the structural claim. A list of famous experimentalists selected for likely agreement would be a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40-41). The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim and a failure condition written in advance.
