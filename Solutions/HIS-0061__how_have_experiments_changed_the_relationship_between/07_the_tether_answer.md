# The Tether's answer: what experiments changed

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the historical question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how experiments changed the relation among three terms. In effect terms: what was settled by comparing an account with what was noticed became settled by comparing an account with what happened under an act that the account did not choose. The residual is a change in a bond structure, not the arrival of a new faculty.

The three terms form a **union**, not a list: observation ∩ intervention is the produced reading, intervention ∩ explanation is the test of a term, and observation ∩ explanation is the source problem, where one reading has several possible sources (Fig 9 :54-57). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a single trend.

## 2. The shape, per domain

### D1, the experimental loop

1. **Observation became a per-slot residual ranged against an anchor that does not update.** Fig 2 :29 names a measurement and an experiment as anchors: *"does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The reading is not pure, and it does not have to be; it has to be separately correctable (Fig 2 :46). **Triangulated.**

### D2, the instrument lineage

2. **Intervention is a distinct currency, and instruments are sharpened from worse instruments.** Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* The two do not add. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Experiment changed the lineage of contact, not the isolated observer (Fig 11 :40). **Triangulated** for the currency rule and the instrument rule; the specific historical instances are `single-source` and unmeasured.

### D3, the explanation or mint frame

3. **Explanation became a settled term with a lineage.** Fig 5 :43 gives the bargain; Fig 13 :130 makes the lineage part of the term: *"That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* Provenance is what separates a convergent derivation from an adopted import (Fig 8 :50-52). **Single-source** on the figure side for the lineage-as-term claim; corroborated by the CONSORT and registration record.

### D4, the verification frame

4. **A verifier must not be able to reconstruct the claim.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Blinding, allocation concealment and independent replication are channel repairs on an anchor that does not decay (Fig 10 :24). **Triangulated** (Fig 10 and the history of the RCT).

### Across all four

5. **The before and after configurations are isomers over the same atom set.** Same atoms (belief, action, reading, residual, term, library, ground), different bonds. An ingredient list cannot name the route (Fig 12 :111). The order is part of the claim: anchor → instrument → intervention → observation → residual → settled term → carried method (Fig 3 :20). **Single-source** on the figure definition, with the falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that experiments made observation theory-free. Observation remains theory-laden and per-slot (F4).
- Not that there was one decisive experiment, or that any figure line returns a chronology. That is F8's declared `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind.
- Not that a famous experiment or a famous experimentalist has verdict weight. Credibility accrued is frame-internal (Fig 1 :26).
- Not that intervention and description are the same currency (Fig 12 :125-130).
- Not that the lineage is documentation appended to a result. It is part of the term.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement or archival check was performed here.

## 4. What would settle it

A historian's ground, not agreement. Specifically:

- **A source-discriminating record:** dated protocols, instruments and correspondence showing which bond changed first and where. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*
- **A currency discriminator:** a case in which a description cost and an action cost are actually compared in one ledger. If they are commensurable there, F2 falls.
- **The five standing falsifiers** in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6, written before this conclusion, any one of which withdraws the corresponding finding.
- **The live strain S3:** a statistical method that improves a verdict while leaving the anchor movable by the frame would break the channel-repair reading of blinding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures stop at the shape: a three-term union with a changed bond structure and an anchor that does not update. They do not name an experiment, a date, an author or a causal priority. The canonical rule returns *"a shape, not a name"* and stops at the limit of the available instruments, recording the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The essays in `05` are by the framework author and cannot independently certify this stop. Six outside sources were opened; the strongest, Bogen 2026 in the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, reaches the same union from a different tradition and is recorded as one witness, not several.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not claim that experiments made observation pure, and it does not return a chronology of famous experiments. It says that experiments changed the bonds among three terms that were already there. Observation became a per-slot residual ranged against a reading that does not move when the explainer pushes on it; intervention became an action priced in a currency that does not add to the cost of a description; explanation became a settled term whose lineage is part of the term, and whose checker must be unable to reconstruct it. The before and after arrangements are isomers: same atoms, different bonds, different substance. Which experiment did this first is a `gap` left to historians. What the derivation offers is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It is `specified`, ungrounded, and a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.
