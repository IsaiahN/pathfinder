# WATSON answer: how have experiments changed the relationship between observation, intervention and explanation?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This WATSON run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own rule it cannot settle the historical question it reports on. No measurement or archival check was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says changed when experiments entered the relation among observation, intervention and explanation. Option A: experiments made observation pure and thereby improved explanation. Option B: experiments changed the bonds among three terms already held, installing a non-updating anchor, a distinct action currency and a settled term with a lineage, while leaving the chronology unreached. What each leads to: A asserts a figure-refused reading and turns a currency into a verdict; B returns the shape any answer must have, with five refuters written in advance and a declared gap for the history.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency. Written before any figure line was chosen.

- **R (the residual).** The change in the coupling of three terms. Arity: three, a union rather than a list. Symmetry: ordered, because intervention precedes its observation and observation precedes the explanation that must answer to it. Scale: one run and one instrument lineage, joined by a membrane. Fig 9 :22 gives the describing words: *"arity · symmetry · scale"*.
- **Domain 1, the experimental loop.** Actors: experimenter, apparatus, object slot. Currency: actions spent and residual paid per slot. Anchor: o′, the reading that does not update on the account. Nameable: yes, Fig 2 :29.
- **Domain 2, the instrument lineage.** Actors: successive instruments and proto-instruments. Currency: residual from a worse instrument, sharpened. Anchor: whatever registers at any resolution. Nameable: yes, Fig 6 :34-35.
- **Domain 3, the explanation frame.** Actors: candidate terms φ, library Γ, bonds. Currency: bits for description and actions for settling. Anchor: the ground that settles. Nameable: yes, Fig 12 :125-126.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: historians, referees, replicators. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the documentary and experimental record. Nameable: yes, Fig 2 :45. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Union, not partition.** observation ∩ intervention is the produced reading; intervention ∩ explanation is the test of a term; observation ∩ explanation is the source problem. The overlaps are compounds with their own loops (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** anchor, then instrument returning something, then intervention, then observation, then per-slot residual, then term minted and settled, then method carried up the membrane.

Strain test applied: "experiment is an anchor" versus "experiment is an action" was re-censused into the response under intervention versus the procedure that elicits it (Fig 2 :29 and formula step 1, different quantities). "Theory precedes instrument" versus "instruments from worse instruments" was re-censused into the two opposed reachability instruments of formula step 7. One live narrow strain remains: statistics versus contact, where a statistical method may repair the channel without changing the ground (Fig 10 :24, Fig 11 :40).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Experiments changed the bonds among three terms already held. Observation became a per-slot residual ranged against a reading that does not update on the account. Intervention became an action priced in a currency that does not add to the price of a description, and instruments were sharpened from worse instruments already returning something. Explanation became a settled term whose lineage is part of the term and whose checker cannot reconstruct it. The before and after configurations are isomers over the same atom set. Which experiment did this first, and in what order, is a `gap`, reported as unreached rather than silence. Mode `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`, and a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures rather than a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is stated with five refuters written before the run and a declared gap, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a chronology or a famous-name list instead of the shape. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The chronology claim is not made, so there is nothing historical to retract. If a refuter fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the census is rerun without touching the other three domain verdicts. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Dated protocols, instruments and correspondence that discriminate which bond changed and where would name what the figures refuse to name; a case where the two currencies are compared in one ledger would break D2; an instrument built from a pure description with no prior instrument returning anything would break D2; a checker who reconstructs the claim being used as verifier would break D4; a statistical method that improves a verdict while leaving the anchor movable by the frame would break the narrow strain S3. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
