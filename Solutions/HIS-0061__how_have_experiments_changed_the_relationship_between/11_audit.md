**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **verifier's non-access**, which collapses if blinding is treated as etiquette rather than soundness;
- the **lineage as part of the term**, which collapses if the protocol is treated as an appendix to a result.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: intervention makes a separately correctable reading available, and the before and after configurations are isomers over the same atom set. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
