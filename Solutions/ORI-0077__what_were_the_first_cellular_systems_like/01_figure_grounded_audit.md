## 0. The residual under audit

The question is: *what were the first cellular systems like before the last universal common
ancestor?* Stated as an effect, with the two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, a name.** There was one discrete, reconstructable "first cell" with a definite anatomy,
  and the ground can in principle name it. Leads to: a verdict stated as if settled, a composite
  anatomy assembled from modern features, and outside readers asked to endorse a name before they
  have described a residual.
- **Option B, a shape.** There was a population in an unmaintained habitat, where selection ran
  before any maintainer existed; a cell is a composition across the six term kinds; heredity was
  initially exchanged sideways, so ancestry was web-like before it was tree-like; the transition to
  vertical heredity is a threshold, not an individual. Leads to: a checkable shape, a written
  refuter, and a slot kept open until a ground discriminates among candidate settings.

What would count as an answer: a constrained shape that any candidate must satisfy, plus the
instrument that would close it, plus the refusal of the name the framework cannot return. Bind that
as an ordinary term now, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its
own question, so the sought-for shape must be written down and allowed to accrue a residual like
anything else.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: a **union compound**, not one hard question. The
pre-LUCA interval bundles at least three overlapping conditions: the origin of a boundary, the
origin of energy coupling, and the origin of a hereditary carrier. West, Sojo, Pomiankowski and Lane
(2017) model all three in one feedback loop, so they overlap rather than partition (Fig 9 :54:
*"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*).
Symmetry: the transition is directional (horizontal to vertical transmission), so `→` and `⇒` apply,
not `+`. Scale: first order inside a protocell, second order at the population threshold, and a new
level again at LUCA.

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor.** A domain without a nameable anchor is not a
domain yet.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2. Empirical pre-LUCA frame | shared genes, ribosomes, lipid bilayers, FeS minerals, isotope ratios, microfossils | inference from shared features, molecular clocks, laboratory models | genomes, rocks and experiments; these do not move when a frame pushes on them | union of boundary, energy and heredity |

| 4. Verification / outreach frame | expert readers | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | checking arrangement |

**Partition or union.** Domains 1 and 2 are different closures: the figures decide legality of
composition, genomes and rocks decide what existed. Their overlap is a binding, not a shared atom, so
they are two frames whose union may carry a surplus (Fig 8 :29: *"low overlap: B does not draw on
A's evidence"*). Inside domain 2 the three conditions are a union, so they are unbundled before
search and kept as separate verdicts below. Domain 3 is a crossing of role, not a higher rung (Fig
13 :4-5: *"the two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward"*).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28);
refuse the discrete "first cell" term before minting a name (Formula step 2); settle the
first-order molecules before composing the second-order threshold (Fig 12 :104); only then ask what
LUCA is. Optimising a link above the break cannot rescue one below it (Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a
link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

## 3. Findings

### F1 - "First" is level-relative, so the question cannot name an absolute first (decisive)

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Fig 4 :33: **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."**

The framework's own recursiveness forbids a privileged stratum. Whatever we call the first cell is
an atom for whatever composes over it, and there is no bottom at which "first" becomes absolute.
The empirical record agrees in shape: FUCA is proposed as a non-cellular ancestor to LUCA, and
progenotes and an RNA world are proposed before FUCA (Wikipedia *First universal common ancestor*;
Wikipedia *Abiogenesis*). The regress is open, not closed.

**Refuter:** produce a ground reading that localises an absolute first entity with no predecessor
frame, or derive the six terms from a lower stratum so that a bottom exists. Until one fires, the
ordinal "first" is a shape (a level), not a name (an individual).

### F2 - A cell is a composition across the two kinds, not a new primitive

Fig 13 :74:

> **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

Fig 13 :3: **"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."**

The six are space, time, energy (states) and spectrum, gradient, network (structures), in lockstep.
A minimal cell reads as a composition: a boundary (space bonded with network), a directed energy
source (energy bonded with gradient), and an ordering that can be inherited (spectrum bonded with
network). Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* This is not an analogy imposed on
the biology: West et al. (2017) model a protocell as a fatty acid membrane in a geologically
sustained proton gradient, with FeS nanocrystals coupling the gradient to carbon fixation. The
figure supplies the legality of the composition; the experiment supplies whether it paid.

**Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five, or exhibit a cell-like system that composes
without one of them. Either would break the substrate claim and this finding with it.

### F3 - The pre-LUCA regime has no maintainer; selection ran without a seat

Fig 11 :36:

> **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."**

Fig 11 :14-15: **"nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept"**

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when
there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the
environment is the only selector."* The Darwinian threshold is exactly the name biology gives to
the arrival of effective vertical selection on individual entities (Wikipedia *Darwinian
threshold*). Before it, the habitat is pure contact; after it, there are genealogies.

This is the strongest figure-to-world alignment in the solution. It is also the one most likely to
be strained, because Fig 11's "maintained" panel is about a **seat** (an office that can act on the
habitat), whereas the Darwinian threshold is about **vertical heredity**. These are not the same
quantity, so I re-census: the figure governs the presence or absence of a maintainer; the threshold
governs the mode of transmission. They coincide in the pre-LUCA case because there is no maintainer
and transmission is horizontal, but the coincidence is empirical, not definitional. Marked
`specified` and triangulated for the no-seat claim, single-source for the identity of the two
transitions.

**Refuter:** show a maintainer acting on the pre-LUCA habitat, or show that vertical heredity can
arise while a seat is present in the Fig 11 sense. Either dissolves the alignment.

### F4 - Ancestry was web-like before it was tree-like; this is a transform, not a contradiction

Fig 10 :70:

> **"The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."**

Wikipedia *Last universal common ancestor* reports that the tree *"may have been more weblike or
netlike in its early phase and more treelike when it grew three-stemmed."* Kandler's pre-cells are
described as *"metabolizing, replicating loose entities ... no proper cytoplasmic membrane and no
stable chromosome, thus allowing frequent mutual exchange of genetic information"* (Wikipedia
*Pre-cell*), and in that scenario *"there was no almost complete ancestral 'first cell' or cell
stage"*. Woese proposed that *"no individual organism could be considered a LUCA"* and that the
genetic heritage of all modern organisms derived through horizontal gene transfer among an ancient
community (Wikipedia *LUCA*). Glansdorff's phrase is that LUCA *"was in a metabolically and
morphologically heterogeneous community, constantly shuffling around genetic material"*.

The figures do not require a single rooted first individual. A non-invertible transform from a web
to a tree is precisely what Fig 10 describes. The empirical disagreement about where the root lies
is a dispute inside domain 2; it does not move the shape.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that the earliest genealogy was a single clean tree with no horizontal
component, or show that the transform from web to tree is invertible. The first is a ground
finding; the second is a figure-internal derivation.

### F5 - The ground cannot yet name the setting; the metamerism is real

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Domain 2 contains a live empirical conflict, recorded here rather than resolved: Weiss et al. (2016)
infer an anaerobic, CO2-fixing, H2-dependent, thermophilic LUCA in a hydrothermal vent; Berkemer and
McGlynn argue the protein families were undersampled and infer a mesophile in a terrestrial hot
spring with a heterotrophic lifestyle; Martin et al. (2026) propose LUCA as the successor to
separate archaeal and bacterial ancestors with metallic catalysts filling enzyme gaps (all reported
in Wikipedia *LUCA*). These conflict about the setting and the metabolism, not about the existence of
a complex LUCA with precursors.

By the framework's rule, a conflict sends us back to the census and never flips the verdict. The
re-census: the shared genetic core is a **reading** that several different sources could produce.
Naming one source from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating
instrument, not a chosen setting. The shape survives; the name is withheld.

**Refuter:** an instrument or experiment that discriminates among these settings against the shared
genomic reading, with a published result. Agreement among reviews that share the same genomes is one
witness (Fig 2 :40), not three.

### F6 - Absence of fossils is silence, not proof of absence

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

Wikipedia *LUCA*: *"With no discovered fossil evidence of LUCA, it may precede the earliest known
life forms. However, the detailed biochemical similarity of all current life makes its existence
widely accepted by biologists."* Betts et al. (2018) write that establishing the early timescale is
*"challenging and mired in controversy because of the paucity of fossil evidence, the difficulty of
interpreting it, and dispute over the deepest branching relationships in the tree of life"*, and
conclude that *"the search for the earliest fossil evidence of life on Earth has created more heat
than light."*

Two instruments are opposed here and must not be read against each other (Fig 6 :31: *"Neither
answer tells you much about the other."*). Inward: sharpen genomic reconstruction and
microscopy from instruments already returning something. Outward: import from geochemistry and
laboratory protocell models, whose closures differ. Importing against a budget limit adds an atom
that was not needed; underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts.

**Refuter:** a positive fossil or experimental result that settles a pre-LUCA anatomy to a single
compositional type with no remaining residual. Absence continues to prove nothing.

### F7 - Reading below the break is a reading of nothing

Fig 3 :22-23:

> **"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."**

The pre-LUCA chain breaks at the boundary between perception and vocabulary: we can perceive shared
genes and isotope ratios, but we lack terms for the population-level entities that held them.
Detailed claims about the anatomy of a pre-cell are readings below that break. Fig 3 :27: *"A link
with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

This finding governs the honest stop. It does not license resignation: West et al. (2017) show a
laboratory-model route that is already returning something, so there is an edge to extend from (Fig
6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is
a search for one that is already there."*).

**Refuter:** an instrument that returns a reading of pre-LUCA anatomy at a resolution where none
exists now. Then the break moves and this finding is withdrawn.

### F8 - The three conditions are a union, so their verdicts stay separate

Fig 9 :54-58:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

West et al. (2017) couple membrane growth, FeS catalysis and proton gradient in one positive
feedback loop, so boundary, energy and heredity cannot be scored as independent yes/no items. But
they are not one verdict either: a case in two conditions is categorically different from a harder
case of one. The three sub-verdicts in §4 are kept separate and never averaged (Fig 1 :19: *"The
residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

**Refuter:** show that one of the three conditions is strictly composed from the other two under
the six-term set, so the union collapses to a partition.

### F9 - "Cellularization" is a route, not an event

Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

Kandler introduced *cellularization* for the successive improvements from pre-cells to cells
(Wikipedia *Pre-cell*), listing membrane, then genome stability, then walls and cytoskeleton. Fig
12 :48: *"A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching."* A single event ("the first
cell appeared") is the flat formula: it records the ingredient and loses the arrangement. The
route is the substance. Note the correction in `11_audit.md`: the listed steps are an empirical
proposal from Kandler, not figure-derived; the figure supplies the requirement that a route must be
writable.

**Refuter:** a ground reading showing the transition was a single unbonded event with no
intermediate arrangements.

### F10 - This is not a mint, and the name is parked

Fig 5 :26-28: *"and then: does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes? park
it keep the gap, look for an import"*. Formula step 3 gates a mint by a product: SUPPORT
`|R⁺_s| > 0`, REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)`, NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`.

For a "first cell" term: SUPPORT is positive (the gap between geochemistry and LUCA is real);
REACHABILITY fails because the six-term set yields a shape, not an individual; NOVELTY fails because
"cell", "protocell", "progenote" and "pre-cell" are already in the empirical library. So the term is
parked and the gap is kept. Fig 5 :30-31: *"nothing new here"* and *"the machinery worked; the
answer was already known"*

**Refuter:** a candidate that composes inside the closure, is novel relative to the held terms, and
pays its MDL bargain against the residual. Then it can be admitted and settled.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. From the Seat Map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design
instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Discrete first cell.** A ground reading identifies one discrete cellular individual as the
   unique first cell, with the transition to vertical heredity localised to that lineage rather than
   a population threshold (breaks F1, F4 and F9).
2. **A name from the six.** The six-term set plus the seven bonds derives a unique composition for
   the first cell, so the slot can be filled without an instrument (breaks F2, F5 and F10).
3. **Substrate failure.** One of the six is derived from the other five, or a cell-like system is
   shown to compose without one of them (breaks the substrate section of `THE_FORMULA.md`).
4. **A maintainer is found.** A seat is shown acting on the pre-LUCA habitat, or vertical heredity
   is shown to require a maintainer (breaks F3).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
A failed search for a refuter is not a confirmation: Fig 8 :39 (*"a search that finds nothing is
never a proof of absence"*) applies to refuters too.

## 8. Updated verdict

The shape survives all ten findings after re-census. Any first cellular system must be a
cross-kind composition of the six term kinds, arising in an unmaintained habitat where selection
ran before a seat existed, web-like before it was tree-like, with its three conditions kept as a
union, and its transition to effective vertical heredity a threshold rather than an individual.
LUCA is a settled molecule that becomes an atom for what composes over it, not a first entity. The
name of the setting, the anatomy and the ordinal "first" are withheld until a discriminating
instrument pays for them. Strains after re-census: **one live, the empirical identity of the no-seat
regime with the Darwinian threshold** (F3), named rather than hidden. Nothing is `grounded`.
