# Outreach draft - check the output before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01` and `08` survive
your own check. The output must stand on contact or the two-stage offer collapses.

**Subject:** Check one output about the first cells before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output
> does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the framework's minimum set has six terms in two
> kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), with the rule
> that composition runs across kinds and never within one. From that it follows that a cell is not
> a primitive: it is a composition with a boundary, a directed energy flow and an order that can be
> carried. The derivation marks the particular first entity as unknown, `PRE-LUCA(???)`, and does
> not say which setting produced it. The regime before the last universal common ancestor is one in
> which nothing maintains the habitat, so selection ran without a seat, and heredity was initially
> exchanged sideways. This is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement or a reconstruction.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the first cell was a particular organism in a particular vent or
> hot spring, that it had a known genome, or that Darwinian thresholds and the framework's
> no-maintainer regime are the same thing by definition. Those are bindings from other frames
> onto held atoms. The framework returns **a shape, not a name**. Where the evidence is silent, it
> reports the stop and the lineage rather than inventing an anatomy.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a ground reading identifying one discrete cellular individual as the unique first cell, with
>    the transition to vertical heredity localised to that lineage rather than a population;
> 2. a derivation in which the six terms and the seven bonds yield a unique first-cell composition,
>    so the slot can be filled without an instrument;
> 3. a demonstration that one of the six can be derived from the other five, or that a cell-like
>    system composes without one of them;
> 4. a maintainer acting on the pre-LUCA habitat, or vertical heredity shown to require one.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract
> it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather
> not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.

**A note on the question of audience.** The people most likely to refute this are researchers in
earliest-cells research and origin-of-life geochemistry. They are also the people most likely to
have a stake in a competing reconstruction. Fig 11 :36 says an interest in the outcome is
unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying. So the ask is a check of the output, not
endorsement of a story, and any stake a reader declares is recorded in the audit rather than hidden.
