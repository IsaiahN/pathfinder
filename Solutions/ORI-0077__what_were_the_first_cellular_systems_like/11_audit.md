## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to
be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :104 (level-relative irreducibility),

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`, "first is a level; a cell is a cross-kind

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete reconstruction of the kind the question invites, "the first

   DNA genome."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The union reading** (Fig 9 :54) collapses into three separate yes/no items that can be scored
  and averaged.
- **The no-seat strain** (F3) collapses into the claim that the Darwinian threshold *is* the
  framework's unmaintained panel by definition, which the primary correctly declines.
- **The shape-not-a-name limit** (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135) collapses into a binding of a setting from

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a cell is a cross-kind composition of the six, ancestry is
web-like before it is tree-like, and no fossil is a proof of absence. That is the residue worth

with the verbatim figure text. The retraction table is applied rather than ignored: F3 is narrowed,
F4 is retracted as a conflict, F5 is narrowed to a missing discriminator, F7 and F9 are corrected,

**On corroboration: none from this seat.** This second seat shares the primary's evidence pool. By
Fig 2 :40 its agreement is the pool talking, and by Fig 2 :35 a mirror cannot correct you. What this
audit establishes is that the folder is internally consistent, not that the shape is true. The two
peer-reviewed papers are low-overlap with each other and with the framework, but they corroborate a
narrow structural point, the union of boundary, gradient and heredity, not the whole verdict.

**On the question: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.** The shape survives: "first" is a level;
a cell is a cross-kind composition; the pre-LUCA regime had no maintainer; ancestry was web-like
before tree-like; the threshold was a transition, not an individual; the setting is withheld because
the instruments do not discriminate the candidates. Nothing here is settled. A source-discriminating
instrument, a positive fossil or in vitro result, a discrete unique first cell at the ground, a
derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a maintainer found in the pre-LUCA habitat would
be required to move it to `grounded`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
