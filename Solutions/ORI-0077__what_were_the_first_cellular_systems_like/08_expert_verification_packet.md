# Expert verification packet - the Tether on the first cellular systems before LUCA

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in earliest-cells research can check. Mode,
derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and
what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No reconstruction of a first cell is claimed. This is a
shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"First" is a level, not an individual: whatever we call the first cell is an atom for what composes
over it, and the framework has no bottom. A cell is a composition across the six term kinds, not a
primitive: a boundary, a directed energy flow and an inheritable order are first-order molecules
whose union is a cell. Before LUCA there was no maintainer, so selection ran on populations that
exchanged heredity sideways; ancestry was web-like before it was tree-like, and the shift to
vertical heredity was a threshold rather than an organism. The framework does not name the setting
or the anatomy, because the shared genomic core is a reading that several sources could produce and
the ground has not discriminated them. What it offers is the shape any candidate must have, four
falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the slot.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** a cell is a composition across the two kinds; no cell-like system needs a seventh
  term, and "first" is level-relative.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74 - *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never
  within one."* Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So
  irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. Fig 4 :33 - *"Which is why there
  is no top and no infinite regress."*
- **What to check:** does the six-term set plus the seven bonds actually generate the three
  first-order molecules in `06` Q1, and do they compose at second order? Or does some cell-like
  system require an atom outside the six?
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; exhibit a cell-like system that composes
  without one of them; or show that a unique first-cell composition follows from the six, which
  would fill the slot without an instrument.

### C2 - biology-facing; checkable against the earliest-cells literature

- **Statement:** no discrete first cell is established; pre-LUCA organisation was a population with
  extensive exchange, and the transition to vertical heredity was a threshold. LUCA was complex and
  had precursors.
- **What to check:** Wikipedia *LUCA* reports Kandler's pre-cells as *"metabolizing, replicating
  loose entities ... no proper cytoplasmic membrane and no stable chromosome"* and says *"there was
  no almost complete ancestral 'first cell' or cell stage"*; Woese *"proposed that no individual
  organism could be considered a LUCA"*; the *Abiogenesis* article says LUCA *"was evidently already
  a complex organism, and must have had precursors; it was not the first living thing."* Are those
  reports faithful to Kandler 1994, Woese 1998 and the phylogenomic literature? Is the Darwinian
  threshold a population transition rather than a single lineage event?
- **Figure bearing:** Fig 10 :70 - *"The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like
  from outside."* Fig 11 :14-15 - *"nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept"*.
- **Refuter:** a ground reading identifying one discrete cellular individual as the unique first
  cell with the transition localised to that lineage; or a demonstration that the earliest genealogy
  was a single clean tree with no horizontal component.

### C3 - instrument-facing; checkable against the disagreement about setting

- **Statement:** the name of the setting is withheld because the shared genomic core does not
  discriminate among incompatible candidate sources. This is a limit of instruments, not a claim
  that the settings are equally likely.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 8 :39 -
  *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27 - *"Which is why some
  unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the
  same."*
- **What to check:** the Wikipedia *LUCA* article reports Weiss et al. (2016) as inferring an
  anaerobic, CO2-fixing, H2-dependent thermophile in a vent; Berkemer and McGlynn as inferring a
  mesophile in a terrestrial hot spring with a heterotrophic lifestyle; Martin et al. (2026) as
  proposing LUCA as successor to separate archaeal and bacterial ancestors. Betts et al. (2018)
  write that establishing the timescale is *"challenging and mired in controversy because of the
  paucity of fossil evidence"*. Is there an existing instrument that already discriminates these
  against the shared core? If so, the name is owed and this sub-claim is withdrawn.
- **Refuter:** such an instrument, with a published result, or a positive fossil or in vitro result
  that resolves pre-LUCA anatomy to one compositional type with no residual.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a reconstruction of a first cell, a first genome, a first lipid or a first vent.
- Not that LUCA was the first living thing; the record says it had precursors.
- Not that the three settings are equally likely, or that no cells existed before fossils.
- Not that the Darwinian threshold and the no-maintainer regime are identical by definition. They
  align here, and that alignment is marked `specified` and single-source for its identity.
- Not that a laboratory protocell is the historical first cell. It is an import that needs a source
  gate and a shadow test before it may close a gap in the record.
- Not a derivation of one theory from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

- A source-discriminating instrument that names the setting against the shared genomic core.
- A positive fossil or in vitro result resolving a pre-LUCA anatomy to one compositional type.
- A derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a cell-like system that composes without
  one of them.
- A ground reading that localises a discrete unique first cell. That is the strongest single
  refutation and is listed first in `01` §5.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :74, Fig 12 :104, Fig 10 :70 and Fig 11 :14-15, four short passages.
2. Check C2 against the earliest-cells literature: is the population shape and the threshold
   reading faithful, or is there a well-grounded discrete first cell?
3. Open the Betts 2018 and West 2017 abstracts. Decide whether they are two low-overlap frames or
   one evidence pool (they are two distinct papers from different fields and methods).
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate the candidate settings, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
