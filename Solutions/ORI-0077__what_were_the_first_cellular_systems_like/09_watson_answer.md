## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between prebiotic chemistry and the complex LUCA recorded in shared
  genomes. Arity: a union of three overlapping conditions, boundary, energy coupling and heredity,
  since West et al. (2017) model all three in one feedback loop. Symmetry: the transition is
  directional (horizontal to vertical transmission), so sequence and production apply, not
  conjunction. Scale: first order inside a protocell, second order at the population threshold, a
  new level at LUCA. Fig 9 :54: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions,
  not a list of separate ones."*
- **Domain 1, framework-internal composition.** Actors: the six terms, seven bonds, negation.
  Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)` then settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus
  the ground for any measurement. Question type: partition (which compositions cross the two
  kinds).
- **Domain 2, empirical pre-LUCA frame.** Actors: shared genes, ribosomes, lipid bilayers, FeS
  minerals, isotope ratios, microfossils. Currency: inference from shared features, molecular
  clocks, laboratory models. Anchor: genomes, rocks and experiments, which do not move when a frame
  pushes on them. Question type: union of boundary, energy and heredity.
- **Domain 3, regime and threshold frame.** Actors: horizontally exchanging pre-cells, vertical
  heredity, LUCA. Currency: the transition from horizontal to vertical transmission. Anchor: which
  entities leave descendants. Question type: a crossing of role, not a step upward.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and
  residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one
  evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the discrete
"first cell" before minting a name (Formula step 2); settle each first-order molecule before the
second-order composition (Fig 12 :104); ask about LUCA last.

Strain test applied: the no-maintainer regime (Fig 11) and the Darwinian threshold govern different
quantities, the presence of a seat versus the mode of transmission. They coincide in this case
empirically, not definitionally. Recorded as a strain, not smoothed (see `01` F3).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. Any first cellular system must be a cross-kind composition of
the six term kinds, arising in an unmaintained habitat where selection ran before a seat existed,
web-like before it was tree-like, with boundary, energy and heredity kept as a union whose verdicts
are never averaged. The shift to vertical heredity is a threshold, not an individual, and "first" is
a level, not an ordinal. LUCA is a settled molecule that becomes an atom for what composes over it.
The setting, the anatomy and the name are withheld because the shared genomic core is a reading that
several sources could produce and no instrument here discriminates them. The three candidate
settings are not declared equally likely; the claim is about the state of the instruments. This is a
`specified`, ungrounded, candidate result with one named strain.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, records four refuters before
  the conclusion and keeps the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost
  is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a reconstruction instead of a shape.
  Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* It is
  avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The withheld name costs nothing to leave withheld, and no primitive has to be
  withdrawn if a refuter fires. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one; `07` and `08`
  already carry the refusals. A positive ground result would add a term rather than delete one, and
  would lower the reach safely (Fig 6 :42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the
  pre-LUCA interval that names the setting; a positive fossil or in vitro result resolving a
  pre-LUCA anatomy to one compositional type with no residual; a discrete unique first cell localised
  at the ground; a derivation of one of the six from the other five; a cell-like system composing
  without one of them; or a figure re-census that shows a maintainer was present. Until one of
  these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
