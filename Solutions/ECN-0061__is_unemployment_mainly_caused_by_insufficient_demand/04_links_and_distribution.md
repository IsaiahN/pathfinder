## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** The Nobel 2010 page, Cowen 2010, Fiveable and the Wikipedia EPL review are all economics. They partly agree and partly conflict, but they draw on the same conferences, citation chains and concepts. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not four, and their conflict is resolved by re-census rather than by picking a side (F9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).

- The Nobel 2010 page gives the demand diagnostic: *"If unemployment and vacancies move in opposite directions, then changes can be regarded as reflecting variations in the demand for labor which occur over a business cycle."* It also says that if both rise together, the natural explanation is a change in the performance of the labor market, weaker matching efficiency or faster structural change. That is the union read from inside economics, and it is why C1 and C2 need separate instruments.
- The same page says benefits *"bring about higher unemployment and longer search time"* while the insurance *"can facilitate efficient matching between unemployed workers and vacancies ('the right person in the right place')"*. Two valences, two residuals. F6 of the audit maps this to Fig 11 :49-51.
- Cowen 2010 is the sharpest challenge to the four-way taxonomy: the Mortensen and Pissarides model makes *"unemployment ... look like it is structural when it is not"* and *"deconstructs the traditional distinction between cyclical and structural unemployment"*. This **corroborates** the audit's union verdict rather than overturning it, but it is still one economics witness.
- Fiveable names wage stickiness as a cause in the Keynesian frame. Cowen says the search results do not require it. That is a genuine **conflict** inside the pool, returned to the census as re-census R1.
- The Wikipedia EPL review says the net effect of employment protection on unemployment is *"not identifiable a priori"* and records policy complementarity. It corroborates the union structure and the honest `gap`, not a verdict about any single institution.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each opened directly:

1. **Animal behaviour and foraging.** Tidy Ecology reports that across Kennedy and Gray's studies *"44 of 52 matching slopes fell below one"* and that *"The matching slope is a good summary and a poor diagnosis"*: perceptual limits, incomplete information, switching costs and unequal competitors all pull the same way. This corroborates Fig 3 :27 (the link with no instrument) and the effect-before-frame rule in Fig 9 :30, with no shared evidence pool with labor economics. It also warns against reading a single matching number as a cause.
2. **Mathematics and market design.** The AMS Feature Column states that a matching is stable when *"there is not a student and a school who would prefer to be matched with each other more than their current matches"* and that Gale and Shapley proved *"there is always a stable matching"*. Stability depends on the arrangement of preferences, not the count of participants. This corroborates Fig 12 :5 and :117: the bond carries information the ingredient list does not.
3. **Engineering and operations research.** The IIT Bombay NPTEL notes state that *"the analysis of delay normally focuses on delay that results when demand exceeds its capacity"* and that when arrival rate exceeds service rate *"the queue has a never ending growth"*. That is Fig 7 :37-39 in an unrelated field: demand pressure and service capacity are two different conditions, and the queue grows when the second cannot keep up with the first.
4. **Human cognition and expertise.** Trinh 2019 reports that experts *"suffer from the curse of knowledge and cognitive entrenchment, which prevents them from being able to adapt to changing situational demands"* and that reinforced schemas *"also tended to be more stable"*. This is the Fig 11 :49-51 valence flip and the Fig 12 :119 spent-catalyst reading at the individual stratum. Fig 4 :20 requires the anchor to be re-named at the new scale, so it corroborates the mechanism shape, not a labor-market measurement.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the union shape were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** This solution is an LLM-assisted simulated derivation and is labelled as such everywhere. Do not submit it as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude, with AI disclosure as the venue requires. An economics journal or conference will expect a real per-slot measurement, which this package does not contain.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
