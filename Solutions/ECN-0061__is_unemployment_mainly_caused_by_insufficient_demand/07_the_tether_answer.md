# The Tether's answer on the causes of unemployment

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No per-slot labor-market measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what workers and firms bet on and what the labor market actually returns stays positive in some worker-slots and not others, and which of four conditions is binding in each. R is indexed per worker and vacancy, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **which condition fails**, not about the level of the national rate.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is four conditions, not one hard question with four rival answers:

- **C1:** a vacancy exists and is reachable (demand).
- **C2:** the worker's library fits the vacancy (mismatch).
- **C3:** the wage comparison clears (wage adjustment).
- **C4:** the boundary between worker and job is crossable (institutions).

## 2. The shape

1. **This is a union, not a ranking.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A demand stimulus clears the region where only C1 holds and leaves the intersection where C2 still binds. "Mainly" is a question the frame cannot answer from inside.
2. **Demand failure is ground pressure falling, not a worker defect.** Fig 7 :39: *"the ground’s pressure on the room"*; Fig 7 :37: *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*; Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* The market is the anchor, named in Fig 2 :29.
3. **Mismatch is a bond and vocabulary failure.** Fig 3 :26 names the middle link that has neither terms for what a situation affords nor a way to say what progress would count as. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Two workers with the same atoms but different bonds are isomers (Fig 12 :117), so a résumé inventory cannot predict who stays unemployed.
4. **Wage rigidity is a bracket that stopped tracking the residual.** Fig 10 :26 names the decay modes: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* A wage threshold that does not update is a comparison that keeps returning the old answer.
5. **Institutions are walls; only import moves the wall.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* No amount of applying inside a closed occupation adds the missing licence or the missing contact.
6. **Any rule can change sign when the residual changes.** Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A benefit that secures income can lengthen search; a protection that steadies incumbency can close the door to outsiders.
7. **The unit of analysis is the slot, never the average.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A national rate is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
8. **The break is usually in the middle of the chain.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary/objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. Mismatch and wage comparison live there.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No per-slot measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a ranking. It does not say demand, mismatch, wages or institutions is the main cause. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about what percentage of unemployment is "structural" or "cyclical". Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that the standard categories are wrong. They are bindings from the economics frame; the claim is that binding alone does not settle which condition is active (Fig 11 :40).
- Not corroboration by agreement. The Nobel page, Cowen, Fiveable and the EPL review share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap sources from animal behaviour, mathematics, engineering and cognition corroborate the mechanism shape, not the labor-level fact.
- Not a theory of everything about labor markets. It stops where the instrument stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the reading is the unemployment rate; the candidate sources are a demand shortfall, a bond failure, a stale comparison, a closed boundary or several at once.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. one intervention restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue;
2. unemployment rising while vacancy creation and labor demand both hold high;
3. identical atoms and identical bonds yielding divergent persistence with no contact difference;
4. a wage change clearing the market with no employment or duration response;
5. reach renewed purely internally across a habitat change with no new atom;
6. an averaged statistic predicting prospectively which named worker fails next and on which condition.

A prospective study that measures C1 to C4 separately per worker, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before each spell of unemployment would move this from `specified` toward `grounded`. The Nobel 2010 page already names the two raw readings such a study would need: the unemployment and vacancy series, and whether they move in opposite directions or together.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources among four conditions, characterized in effect terms but not held. Naming a winner from an averaged reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The four conditions in section 2 are a shape; the fifth thing, which one binds in a named worker's case, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank the causes of unemployment, and it does not claim the final answer. It says that demand, mismatch, wage rigidity and institutions are not four rival explanations but four conditions in a union, each with its own failure mode and its own instrument, and the worker or vacancy slot, not a national average, is the unit of analysis. Demand failure is the market's pressure on a search going to zero; mismatch is a vocabulary and bond failure, so two people with the same résumé can still differ; wage rigidity is a comparison that stopped tracking the residual; institutions are walls that only contact with something outside can move. Any of these can change sign when the situation changes, so a benefit that secures income can also lengthen a search. What is offered is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which condition is "mainly" responsible stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
