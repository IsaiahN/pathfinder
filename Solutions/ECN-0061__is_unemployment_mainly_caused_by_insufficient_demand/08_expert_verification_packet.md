# Expert verification packet - the Tether on the causes of unemployment

**What this is:** a claim in a form a labor economist or empirically minded policy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No per-slot labor-market measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Demand, mismatch, wage rigidity and institutions are not four rival explanations from which one main cause is selected. They are four conditions in a union, and all four can be present in the same labor market. Each has a distinct failure mode and a distinct instrument: a vacancy exists and is reachable; the worker's skills compose with that vacancy's requirements; the wage paid tracks what the job is worth; and the boundary between the worker and the job is crossable. Which condition binds is a per-worker, per-phase measurement question, not a question a national unemployment rate can answer. Demand failure is the market's pressure on a search going to zero, mismatch is a vocabulary and bond failure, wage rigidity is a comparison whose bracket stopped tracking the residual, and an institution is a wall that only contact with a differing closure can move. Any of these can change sign when the situation changes. The claim is a shape that any candidate answer must have; it does not rank the four and does not name a winner.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9, 7 and 2

- **Statement:** the four candidates form a union, not a partition or a ranking; demand is a fall in the ground's pressure on the search room, and the market, not internal agreement, is the anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 7 :37 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*; Fig 7 :39 - *"the ground’s pressure on the room"*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same line names *"a market"* as an instance.
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** the Nobel Prize 2010 popular information states that if unemployment and vacancies move in opposite directions, changes reflect variations in labor demand over the cycle, but that if both rise together the natural explanation is a change in labor-market performance, weaker matching efficiency or faster structural change. Does that diagnostic not already require two conditions with different instruments, rather than one main cause? A "no" with a counterexample is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** one intervention restoring all four conditions in all four failure types and leaving no categorically different residue; or unemployment rising while vacancy creation per firm and measured labor demand both hold high and non-declining. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus two low-overlap checks

- **Statement:** mismatch is a bond and vocabulary failure; two workers with identical atoms but different bonds are isomers, so a skill inventory alone cannot predict persistence; wage rigidity is the comparison bond at the same middle link.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 3 :26 - *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."*
  - Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 12 :112 - *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*
  - Fig 10 :26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
- **What a researcher can check:** the AMS Feature Column on Gale-Shapley states that a matching is stable when no matched pair would prefer each other, and that a stable matching always exists. Is stability not a property of the arrangement of preferences rather than the headcount, as Fig 12 :5 claims? And does the Tidy Ecology finding that *"The matching slope is a good summary and a poor diagnosis"*, with 44 of 52 slopes below one in Kennedy and Gray's review, not show that one matching number cannot identify the mechanism? A "no" to both is a refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** two worker-slots with identical atoms and identical bonds whose persistence diverges with no ground-contact or import difference; or a wage change that clears the market at slot level with no employment or duration response. Either breaks C2.

### C3 - Structure, institutions and the valence flip

- **Statement:** institutions are closures that only import can move; the union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; a rule's valence is relative to the residual, so the same institution can help one worker and harm another.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
  - Fig 11 :50 - *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
  - Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **What a researcher can check:** the Wikipedia EPL review says employment protection *"reduces both job creation and job destruction, so that the net effects on average employment and unemployment are not identifiable a priori"*, and records that the effectiveness of one policy depends on another. The Nobel 2010 page says more generous benefits both lengthen search and can *"facilitate efficient matching between unemployed workers and vacancies"*. Are these not two valences of one rule rather than one effect? A documented single-valence institution would refute the claim.
- **Refuter:** reach renewed purely by internal composition with no new atom from a differing closure and still passing the ground test across a habitat change; or an institution whose measured valence is invariant across a documented change of residual; or one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types with nothing categorically different left over.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a theory of everything about labor markets, and no final or unique theory claim.
- Not a ranking. No claim about which of the four conditions is "mainly" responsible, and no claim about percentages of structural or cyclical unemployment.
- Not an average. No claim that a national rate predicts a named worker's outcome; that would be a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26).
- Not that demand, skills, wages or institutions are irrelevant. Each is a real binding onto a condition the figures govern; the claim is that a binding does not settle the ranking.
- Not that the economics literature independently corroborates this. The Nobel page, Cowen, Fiveable and the EPL review share one evidence pool. The low-overlap sources from animal behaviour, mathematics, engineering and cognition corroborate the mechanism shape, not the labor-level fact.
- Not that this packet, produced with an LLM, is submittable or publishable as authored work. Do not submit it as authored empirical or theoretical work. Use it privately as a checking instrument; a human researcher who runs the check owns and writes anything submitted, with AI disclosure as the venue requires.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-worker study that measures C1 to C4 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before each spell. Concretely:

1. **Pressure test:** do unemployment and vacancies move in opposite directions, or together? The Nobel 2010 page uses exactly this split to separate demand variations from matching performance.
2. **Fidelity test:** are the worker's reading of available jobs and the wage comparison both tracking the current situation, or are they stale, saturated or pooled (Fig 10 :26)?
3. **Bond test:** is the difference between two otherwise identical workers in the arrangement of their skills rather than the list of them (Fig 12 :117)?
4. **Import test:** did any new atom enter the worker's reach from a closure that differs, before the spell ended? If not, C4 is the binding condition.
5. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish a demand shortfall from a mismatch from a stale comparison from a closed boundary when all four produce the same unemployment reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55 and :57, Fig 7 :37 and :39, Fig 2 :29, Fig 3 :26, Fig 12 :5, :112 and :117, Fig 10 :26, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :32 and Fig 11 :50. Eleven short passages, about eight minutes.
2. Check C1 and C2 against your own knowledge of labor-market diagnostics: can one matching or unemployment number identify which condition binds? About five minutes.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate demand from mismatch from wage rigidity from institutions using observable worker-level data, or say why none exists. About five minutes.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
