# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no per-slot ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about the causes of unemployment before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about unemployment that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "demand, mismatch, wage rigidity and institutions" is not a menu from which one main cause is selected. It is a union of four conditions that overlap and can all be present in the same labor market. They are:
>
> 1. a vacancy exists and is reachable (demand);
> 2. the worker's skills compose with that vacancy's requirements (mismatch);
> 3. the wage comparison clears, meaning the wage paid tracks what the job is worth (wage adjustment);
> 4. the boundary between the worker and the job is crossable (institutions).
>
> The claim is that these are four different failure modes with four different instruments, not four degrees of one problem. A demand stimulus clears the region where only condition 1 fails and leaves the intersection where condition 2 still binds. Which condition binds is a per-worker, per-phase measurement question, not a question that can be settled by a national unemployment rate.
>
> The unit of analysis is the worker or vacancy slot, not an average. An averaged statistic hides exactly which slot is losing contact and on which condition.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can rank the four causes, that any one of them is the answer, that this is settled by the evidence, that the standard economic categories are wrong, or that this is the only account of unemployment. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. one intervention restores all four conditions in all four failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind;
> 2. unemployment rises while vacancy creation per firm and measured labor demand both hold high and non-declining;
> 3. two workers with identical measured skills and identical measured bonds diverge with no difference in contact or in anything newly available to them;
> 4. a wage change clears the market at the worker level with no change in employment or duration;
> 5. a worker renews their reach purely from inside their existing set of options, with nothing new entering from outside, and still passes the ground test across a change of circumstance;
> 6. an averaged unemployment statistic predicts prospectively and per worker which named person becomes unemployed next, and on which condition.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers: the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Four failure modes kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No ranking of the causes | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Per-slot, not an average | *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* (Fig 10 :59).
