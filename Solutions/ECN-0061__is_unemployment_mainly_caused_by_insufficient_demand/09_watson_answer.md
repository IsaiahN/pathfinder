# WATSON answer, ECN-0061

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Is unemployment mainly caused by insufficient demand, mismatches between workers and jobs, wages that do not adjust, or institutional barriers?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay positive in some worker-slots and not others, and which of four conditions is binding in each? R is indexed per worker and vacancy, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. The sought-for shape is a set of conditions with instruments, a dependency order, and a per-slot statement of which condition binds, bound as an ordinary term before the run.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a multi-place relation among worker, vacancy, wage, institution set and time; two-sided, carrying an ordered bond, a comparison bond for the wage and a disjunction over the four candidates. Symmetry: "unemployed" is a threshold comparison, and the matching is two-sided but not symmetric in anchor. Scale: worker loop, firm loop, market matching function, institutional frame; the membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** unemployment is a union of four conditions, demand, mismatch, wage adjustment and institutions, each with its own instrument and kept separate per domain, never averaged. Demand failure is ground pressure falling; mismatch is a bond and vocabulary failure; wage rigidity is a comparison bracket that stopped tracking the residual; institutions are walls that only import moves; any rule's valence can flip when the residual changes. The ranking implicit in "mainly" is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`. The economics pool corroborates the union once its own internal conflict is re-mapped; the two conflicts were returned to the census and did not flip the verdict. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism, not the labor-level ranking.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A worker failing C2 would be sent demand stimulus, or a worker failing C1 would be told to retrain, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs four. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) one intervention restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue, (2) unemployment rising while vacancy creation and labor demand both hold high, (3) identical atoms and bonds with divergent persistence and no contact difference, (4) a wage change clearing the market with no employment response, (5) reach renewed purely internally across a habitat change, or (6) an averaged statistic predicting prospectively which named worker fails and on which condition. A source-discriminating worker-level instrument that ranks the four conditions and pays at the ground would convert the `gap` to `grounded`. Agreement or prestige is not such an instrument.
