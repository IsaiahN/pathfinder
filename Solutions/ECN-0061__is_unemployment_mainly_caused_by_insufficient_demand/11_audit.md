## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among worker Γ, action a, market return o′, an institution set and time. It is two-sided, so a two-place reading (worker, unemployed or not) loses the vacancy and the wage. There is at least one ordered bond and one threshold comparison, since "binding condition" is a comparison among candidates and the wage itself is a threshold quantity.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the worker's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Unemployed" is a threshold (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is compared against a boundary rather than merely conjoined with another.
- **Scale:** the slot is a worker or vacancy, but "unemployment" as a phenomenon exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5), and the institution set operates at a third scale. The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, so the membrane's lossy transform and R_T apply (Fig 4 :30).

Both censuses arrive at five admitted domains. The difference is one of articulation only, not of boundaries.

**Dependency order:** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3). ## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's four-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *unemployment persists when the worker's link to a paying labor market fails in any of four ways, so maintain contact.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific worker situation. Result: *keep applying and keep learning new skills.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a worker failing C4 (a closed licence boundary) merely to apply harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Paying labor market" does not specify that realized employment, not the worker's own rating of their skills, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a worker to measure success with a quantity they produce (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the bond/isomer distinction. "Keep learning new skills" assumes the problem is an ingredient shortage. It has no place for Fig 12 :5 and :117, where the difference between two identical résumés is in the arrangement.
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep applying" has no place for the possibility that a benefit or a skill that helped now lengthens the search (Fig 11 :50, Fig 12 :119).
5. It loses the ranking `gap`. The abstraction is silent on which condition binds, and so is the concretisation. That silence is correct at this instrument boundary, but a reader of the one-liner will not know it was a recorded verdict rather than an omission.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
