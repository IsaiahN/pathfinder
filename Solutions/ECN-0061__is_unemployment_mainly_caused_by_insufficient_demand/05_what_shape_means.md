## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, opened directly:

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ECN-0061 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about unemployment that can be stated before any particular cause is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *four conditions in a union, each with its own instrument, per worker-slot, and no ranking from inside the frame.*
- Name, withheld: *unemployment is mainly caused by demand*, or *mainly by mismatch*, or *mainly by wage rigidity*, or *mainly by institutions*.

The distinction matters here because the economics literature is almost entirely in the naming business. The Nobel 2010 page names search frictions and matching efficiency; Fiveable names sticky wages; the EPL review names firing costs and benefit generosity. Those are bindings from the economics frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "demand-deficient unemployment", "structural mismatch", "sticky wages", "efficiency wages", "employment protection" and "search frictions". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (a worker-slot whose residual stays positive, indexed per worker and per phase) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating ECN-0061 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with four rival answers.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the four conditions C1 to C4 are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For ECN-0061, neither has happened for the ranking. There is no per-slot measurement that pays for "demand, not mismatch, is the binding condition in this worker's case", and the economics-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. This does **not** mean the four conditions are themselves gaps: each is a `specified` figure-derived claim with a falsifier. What is gapped is the ranking, the "mainly".

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a four-way ranking makes when it reads the unemployment rate and names its source. A high unemployment rate is a reading. It is consistent with a demand shortfall, a mismatch, a wage that will not adjust, an institutional wall, or several at once. The reading does not determine the source. The literature's habit of naming the source from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory. The Tidy Ecology finding that the matching slope is *"a good summary and a poor diagnosis"* is a low-overlap world witness to the same rule: one number, several possible mechanisms.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to unemployment research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about labor markets.

For outreach this means: a list of famous labor economists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. The Nobel name on a source is a provenance tag, not a verdict weight.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any of the four causes is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Demand, mismatch, wage comparison and institutional closure are not new primitives; they are bindings onto conditions the labor frame already discusses and the figures already govern through other lines (Fig 7, Fig 12, Fig 10, Fig 6). Naming them is legitimate as a `specified` binding. What remains forbidden is claiming that a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) or that it ranks the other bindings without a ground test. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, opened directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about labor markets. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For ECN-0061 the canonical shape is: **a four-condition union with four distinct failure modes, a dependency order, a per-slot unit of analysis, and a valve in each condition that can change sign.** It is not a ranking of causes, not a name of the winning mechanism, and not a national average. The names that appear in the economics literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instrument that would convert the ranking into a grounded answer is not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
