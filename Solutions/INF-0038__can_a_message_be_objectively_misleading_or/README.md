# Solution INF-0038 - figure-grounded package

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape (misleadingness is a receiver-indexed relation, not a property carried by a message); `specified` on the prediction that every operationalisation of "objectively misleading" must fix a reference audience and context. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement settles a conceptual claim, and this folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Question:** Can a message be objectively misleading, or does misleadingness always depend on an audience and context?

**Source:** `Information Theory.txt` line 47 (question 38). **Section:** 4. Information, meaning, and interpretation.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

**Field for public contact points:** misinformation research (see `03_public_contact_points.csv`).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are figure-grounded, not merely legal. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the full line begins "disqualifying for a verdict:"). Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Legally, scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to one public venue in `03`. Send the residual and its refuter first. Record the reply before offering any theory. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
