## The question restated as an effect

The word *must* asks about necessity at the threshold between chemistry and biology. The target property is not "a cell with parts" but the capacity that the field itself uses to mark the transition: a system that can undergo cumulative Darwinian evolution. NASA puts it as *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution"* (NASA Astrobiology, fetched 2026-11-23).

- Option A, "the first life had all three in their modern form": leads to the prediction that genes, metabolism and a membrane-bound compartment were simultaneously present at the threshold, and that removing any one removes life.
- Option B, "the first life had none of them, only chemistry": leads to the prediction that the transition involved no carried record, no spendable gradient and no individuated slot, so nothing could be selected per system.
- Option C, "some combination": leads to the prediction that the three are conditions of different kinds, in dependency order, none sufficient alone, and that their first forms were not their modern forms.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* stating which conditions must hold for a system's residual to stay indexed to a slot and to keep being selected across rounds, plus an instrument that would discriminate a failure of one condition from a failure of the others. That shape is the sought-for term; until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option C in `specified` mode, with the literal historical order declared a `gap`. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the origin-of-life question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of life's origin.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
