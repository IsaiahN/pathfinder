# The Tether's answer: must the first life have had genes, metabolism, a boundary, or some combination?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on each per-domain necessity claim, `gap` on the literal historical order, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is about a threshold, not about a cell's parts. The operational marker of the threshold is cumulative Darwinian evolution: NASA's working definition, *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution"*, is a marker and not a mechanism. The residual is the gap between what a proposed mechanism predicts about which conditions must be present and what the ground shows. The ground has not shown the transition: *"The transition from non-life to life has not been observed experimentally, but many proposals have been made for different stages of the process"* (Wikipedia, abiogenesis, fetched 2026-11-23).

R by arity, symmetry and scale: three conditions, not interchangeable, at a level crossing. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* So the answer is not a parts list; it is a union compound with its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **No single candidate suffices.** Fig 13 :33: *"Neither kind generates anything alone. Neither denotes anything alone. That is why they are two kinds rather than one list."* Fig 13 :29-30: *"A state with no structure has no magnitude: three of what, ordered how, varying across what?"* and *"A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes."* A record with nothing to spend it against, a gradient with no slot to accrue to, and a seam with nothing held inside are each empty. The "some combination" option is the figure-derived one; the none-of-them and one-only options are refused.

2. **A slot must be individuated before selection can be indexed to a system.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The requirement is boundary-shaped, not membrane-named: the Symbols table defines a slot as *"One object, region or entity being tracked"*, and Fig 10 :21 calls the membrane *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction."*

3. **A spendable gradient must be in contact with a ground that does not update.** Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The gradient can be environmental and pre-biological; Knowable Magazine reports core metabolic reactions reproduced with iron and nickel in place of 127 enzymes, while quoting Moran's caveat that *"we have not identified any conditions that allow an entire metabolism to emerge."*

4. **A carried method is required, and modern genes were not available at the origin.** Fig 3 :15: *"name what worked, verify it, and carry it to the next problem."* Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 5 :70: *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it."* Modern genes are settled molecules of a later stratum; by Fig 12 :104, *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it"*, they could not serve as atoms before they were settled. NASA's Joyce refused his own lab's RNA system as not life because of *"not enough information carrying capacity"*, which is the world-facing form of the same point.

5. **The dependency order is not the chronological order.** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Slot → gradient contact → carried record is the licensed order. The temporal order is a `gap`: no anchor exists, and several programs produce the same reading. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

6. **The three programs are isomers over the shared atom set.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first differ in which atom they settle first, not in which atoms they need. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a parts list. No figure line names genes, metabolism or a membrane as a primitive (Fig 13 :3, :134-135).
- Not a choice among RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first. They are isomers, not rival verdicts (Fig 12 :117).
- Not a chronology. The first form of each condition and the order of their bonds are withheld until a discriminator exists.
- Not "metabolism is life." NASA's own page says *"metabolism is not even life itself, just a way of turning organic compounds into energy, a baseline requirement for any living thing."*
- Not grounded. No measurement of the transition settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only account of life's origin. It is a generator of shape, not a final room (Fig 7 :35).

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the rule: an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer resolved that case. Here:

- **Discriminator:** an instrument that distinguishes a carried record from a spendable gradient and from an individuated seam at the threshold, at some resolution. Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
- **Threshold trace:** a ground reading, not a model, showing which bond was settled first. The current Hadean-timing result is a thermal model, not a trace.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a cumulative evolving system with only one shape-constraint; a modern gene present at the first transition; programs with disjoint atom sets; one mechanism with one anchor accounting for all three; or an import at the threshold sourced outside a first life.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The three shape-constraints are figure-derived and remain as candidates; their first forms and their order do not. The source literature confirms the stop from its own side: Barge asks *"We don't know what pieces of the first life came when. What came first? What came later? Was it before or after the cell emerged?"* The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner among RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first, and it does not hand back a parts list. It says that the question is a union of conditions: a system enters cumulative selection only when an individuated slot, a spendable gradient in contact with a ground that does not update, and a carried method are bonded together in that dependency order. No one of them is sufficient alone. Their first forms were not their modern forms, because a modern gene is a settled molecule of a later stratum and could not be an atom before it was settled. The three programs are isomers over the shared atom set, same atoms arranged differently, and the chronological order of the three bonds is withheld: no experiment has observed the transition and no instrument yet discriminates the sources that produce the same reading. What the derivation offers is the shape any answer must have, five failure conditions written in advance, and the name of the instrument that would close it. The threshold stays open until the ground settles it.
