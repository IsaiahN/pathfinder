## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: must the first life have had genes, metabolism, a boundary, or some combination? Option A: all three in modern form were present at the threshold. Option B: none were present, only chemistry. Option C: a union of conditions, none sufficient alone, with first forms that were not modern forms and an order left open. What each leads to: A sends a parts list that the figures do not return and that the experiment record refuses at least in the case of a modern genome; B fails the lockstep rule because an empty structure or an undirected record cannot be selected; C sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an honest `gap` on the chronology.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option C. The literal parts-list question is a `gap`: no figure line returns genes, metabolism or a boundary as a primitive, and no ground reading of the transition exists. The question in effect form has a figure-derived shape. A system enters cumulative selection only when an individuated slot, a spendable gradient in contact with a non-updating ground, and a carried method are bonded together in that dependency order. No one of them suffices alone. Their first forms were not their modern forms, because a modern gene is a settled molecule of a later stratum. RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first are isomers over the shared atom set, not mutually exclusive answers, and the chronological order of their bonds is unreachable at this budget. The result is `general` on the shape, `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `gap` on the order, and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a parts list or a program preference instead of a shape with its refuter. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its failure conditions.
- **undoable:** yes. The named positions are removable without touching the derivation: no program is selected, no modern form is asserted, and the chronology is left open. A later discriminator would close the `gap` without withdrawing the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates record, gradient and seam at the threshold would name the first form; a ground trace showing which bond settled first would close the chronology; a cumulative evolving system with one condition and no substitute would break the lockstep verdict; a modern gene shown settled at the first transition would break the later-stratum claim; programs with disjoint atom sets would break the isomer reading; and a threshold import sourced outside a first life would break the closure verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
