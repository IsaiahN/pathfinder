# Expert verification packet : the Tether on the first life's requirements

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origin-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the three sub-claims, `gap` on the literal order, not `grounded`.** No measurement of the transition is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "must the first life have had genes, metabolism, a boundary, or some combination?" is a union of conditions, not a parts list. A system enters cumulative selection only when a slot is individuated so a residual can be indexed to one system, a spendable directed gradient is ranged against a ground that does not update on the system's own report, and a carried term makes the next round cheaper. No one of the three suffices alone. Their first forms were not their modern forms, because modern genes and modern pathways are settled molecules of a later stratum. RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first are isomers over the shared atom set, not rival verdicts; their chronological order is a `gap` until a discriminating instrument exists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 10 and 13

- **Statement:** a slot must be individuated before a residual can be indexed to a system. The requirement is boundary-shaped, not membrane-named, and no single condition suffices.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 : *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 : *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 10 :21 : *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 13 :33 : *"Neither kind generates anything alone. Neither denotes anything alone. That is why they are two kinds rather than one list."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate cumulative Darwinian evolution with no individuated slot and no substitute for one; or produce a figure line that requires a lipid membrane specifically; or demonstrate that one of the three conditions alone is sufficient.

### C2 : ground-facing, checkable against the experiment and observation record

- **Statement:** a spendable gradient in contact with a non-updating ground is required, but a modern metabolic pathway is not. A carried record is required, but a modern gene was not available at the first transition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 : *"a difference that can be spent."* Fig 11 :40 : *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 5 :70 : *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it."* Fig 12 :104 : *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*
- **What to check:** does a pre-biological gradient in a vent or surface environment satisfy the spendable-gradient condition without a metabolic pathway? Knowable Magazine reports metal-catalysed reproduction of core reactions and quotes Moran's caveat that *"we have not identified any conditions that allow an entire metabolism to emerge."* Does the Lincoln and Joyce RNA system, which met the working definition yet was refused by Joyce for low information capacity, show that a carried record can exist without a modern gene? Check both against their primary sources.
- **Refuter:** show a cumulative evolving system with no spendable directed difference anywhere in system or habitat; or show a modern DNA gene present and settled at the first transition; or show that a spendable gradient whose ground updates on the system's own report still settles a verdict.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation with a live type question

- **Statement:** the three programs are isomers over a shared atom set (Fig 12 :117), and the licensed order is a dependency order, not a chronology (Fig 3 :20). The chronological order is `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 : *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 3 :20 : *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 9 :54 : *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 8 :39 : *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Live type question:** can a slot, a gradient and a record be written over one substrate at the threshold? Fig 12 :117 requires the same atom set; whether these three qualify as atoms at that level needs an instrument, not an argument. Named, not hidden.
- **Refuter:** show the programs have disjoint atom sets (kills the isomer claim, leaves the functional-group reading); or show one mechanism with one anchor accounts for all three (kills the union reading); or produce a ground reading, not a model, establishing the order (closes the `gap`).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a parts list, and not an assertion that the figures name genes, metabolism or a membrane.
- Not a choice among RNA-first, metabolism-first and membrane-first. They are isomers.
- Not a chronology. The first forms and the order of bonding are withheld.
- Not "metabolism is life." NASA's page says *"metabolism is not even life itself, just a way of turning organic compounds into energy."*
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only true account of life's origin.
- Not a ground reading. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
