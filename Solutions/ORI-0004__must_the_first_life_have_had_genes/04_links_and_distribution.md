## Four things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The list of traits is not a sufficient condition, and not even a necessary one trait by trait.** The NASA learning page says fire, crystals and computer algorithms have many traits of life, and that mules are alive without reproducing. That is the world-facing twin of Fig 12 :5, *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Use this when explaining why "does it have genes" cannot settle the question alone.
2. **Heredity and flux are treated as constitutive; compartmentalization as derivative.** Joyce's Q&A derives self-replication, mutability, heritability, variation and a flux of higher-energy starting materials from Darwinian evolution, then files *"ecological properties such as compartmentalization"* under adaptations. This corroborates D4 and D3 and narrows D2 to a seam rather than a membrane. It is one institutional evidence pool with the other NASA pages, not independent corroboration.
3. **The first forms need not be modern forms.** Knowable reports metal catalysts replacing 127 enzymes in core metabolic reactions, and NASA reports an RNA system that met the working definition yet was refused by Joyce as not life because its information capacity was too low. Both are ground-adjacent instances of Fig 12 :104, *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Moran's caveat that no entire metabolism has been reproduced is the honest stop on that claim.
4. **The order question is explicitly open and is a source-discrimination problem.** Barge's three questions (what came first, what came later, before or after the cell) and the Nature Communications model-dependent date show that no anchor exists for the order. *The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the instrument rule: several programs can create the same reading, "a system that evolves," so the reading cannot comment on the source. That is why the derivation returns a `gap`, not a program choice.

## Overlap declaration

- The four NASA pages are one institutional pool and count as one witness.
- Wikipedia abiogenesis and Wikipedia autopoiesis are secondary aggregators; they draw on overlapping primary literature with the science-journalism and Nature Communications sources, so they are not independent witnesses either.
- Knowable Magazine (science journalism) and Nature Communications (geodynamics) are genuinely lower overlap with the NASA pool. Their agreement with the figure-derived shape is weak corroboration, not a verdict.
- The two framework essays share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures completely. They are cited for the simulated-output caveat and the instrument rule, never as independent corroboration.
- No world source was found that conflicts with the figure verdict. Joyce's "compartmentalization is an adaptation" was the nearest case and was returned to the census, where it was re-mapped to a different object (see `01` retraction table). The verdict was not flipped.
