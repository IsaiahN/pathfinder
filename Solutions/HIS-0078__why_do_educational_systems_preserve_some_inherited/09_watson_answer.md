## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, why educational systems carry some inherited ideas forward as given while training students to question others. Option A: treat it as one question about the right balance between tradition and inquiry. Option B: treat it as a union of two conditions with different currencies and grounds, and derive each separately. What each leads to: A produces a single score that averages away the live per-idea signal and invites a virtue claim about preservation or questioning. B produces a per-slot rule, a head-start and convention account of preservation, a refusal and independence account of questioning, and an honest `gap` for the empirical allocation.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The differential treatment of inherited content. Arity: one slot per idea, not a global average. Symmetry: asymmetric, preservation transfers a head start across a scale boundary while questioning adds a unary refusal. Scale: one learner against a community across generations. Fig 9 :54 and :57 name a union of two conditions, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the learner's belief formation.** Actors: learner, teacher, belief b, weighting w. Currency: per-slot residual updated by contact. Anchor: the ground, what pays, which does not move when the learner thinks otherwise. Question type: partition boundary inside one agent, which held terms are load-bearing and which are owed a refusal.
- **Domain 2, the convention and shared-meaning layer.** Actors: community members reading each other against a shared backdrop. Currency: agreement about shared meaning. Anchor: the convention, a declared meaning with no truth value until tested. Question type: preserve for coordination, guard against deciding an outcome.
- **Domain 3, the verification layer.** Actors: claims, instruments, refuters, independent frames. Currency: settlement by what pays. Anchor: the pinned ground. Question type: correlations from a shared evidence pool are disqualifying.
- **Domain 4, the transmission membrane.** Actors: generations, methods, recordings, new learners. Currency: methods and head starts versus recordings and replays. Anchor: the round-trip gap R_T. Question type: what may cross between one agent and a population.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive a residual per slot, acquire a vocabulary of affordances, state an objective, plan which ideas to teach each way, then carry only methods up and send only head starts down. Refuse before mint. Describe the residual before choosing a frame.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical allocation is a gap. The question is a union of two conditions. Preservation is a head start across a membrane plus a convention that must never decide a verdict. Questioning is a refusal added to the record plus independence bought against an unmovable ground. Which idea gets which treatment is selected per slot by the residual that persists and does not trend down. The framework returns that shape, not the list of ideas or the fraction attributable to each cause. Status `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The one live strain is the boundary where a convention becomes a verdict, which needs an instrument in a specific institution.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes five refuters before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices by the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The two condition loops can be separated, the head-start and convention functions tested independently, and the refusal and independence mechanisms measured, without changing the shape claim. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A head-start test showing no cheaper search for preserved ideas, a measurement showing learners checking only a shared source are as reliable as those checking an independent ground, a system that carries a correction with no negation-type operation, or a per-slot list that does not track the ideas actually questioned, would each fire a refuter. A figure re-census that shows both conditions share one anchor and one currency would collapse the union. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
