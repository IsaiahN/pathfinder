## Residual in effect terms

A learner enters a system holding beliefs *"formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w"* (Symbols table). The system treats some inherited ideas as given and others as open to refusal. The residual R is the per-idea gap between what the inherited frame predicts and what the ground returns. It is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), never as one global score for tradition versus inquiry. The effect to explain is the differential itself: why one idea is carried forward as a head start and another becomes the target of a negation.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that education should preserve or question a given idea.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not supply an anchor. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)
2. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement as a verdict (Fig 2 :40; Seat Map rule on correlation).
3. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) Sending the whole framework before asking for a verdict spends the outside frame's independence.

Legally: public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that unsolicited bulk outreach creates.
