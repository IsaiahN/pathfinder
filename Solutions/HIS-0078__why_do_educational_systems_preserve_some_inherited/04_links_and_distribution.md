## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly

1. **The persistently useful idea is preserved, not the merely old one.** *Conceptual change*: *"some of the intuitive conceptions people hold cannot be changed because they are pervasive, persistent, and helpful in some contexts such as everyday life."* This is the independent-field version of F1 plus F5: load-bearing ideas stay. It does not prove the figure line; it corroborates it from a different evidence pool.
2. **Both conservatism and innovation are required.** *Kuhn* (SEP): *"normal science can succeed in making progress only if there is a strong commitment by the relevant scientific community to their shared theoretical beliefs, values, instruments and techniques, and even metaphysics"*, and *"an inculcation of that commitment is a key element in scientific training."* The same entry notes that revolutions *"involve a revision to existing scientific belief or practice"* and that *"Not all the achievements of the preceding period of normal science are preserved in a revolution."* This is a low-overlap witness for the union reading in F6.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its five refuters; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27 to :28, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :40, agreement about the world is worthless when frames share an evidence pool. A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One-to-one contact buys more independence than a list.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three local links, with the note that the figure text, not this summary, is the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the local figure text, formula, and usage rule. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter, states their own ground, and labels the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. It is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading, and cannot settle the question. Its value is that it is checkable and falsifiable.
