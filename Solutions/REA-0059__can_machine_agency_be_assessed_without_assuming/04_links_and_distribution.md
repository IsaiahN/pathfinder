## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Measurement science in AI.** Voudouris et al. (2026) is the closest published statement of the separator. Its distinction between capability (success as task demands vary) and propensity (selection as incentives vary) is a practical decomposition of the per-slot residual. Its warning that benchmarks and IRT summaries do not by themselves identify causal structure is the world-side twin of Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
2. **Agent-as-environment-coupling.** Gibson's affordance and Conant and Ashby's regulator theorem are two low-overlap statements of Fig 11 :39 from ecological psychology and cybernetics. Both make the unit of analysis the agent-and-environment pair. The good-regulator result is explicitly lossy (homomorphic, not isomorphic), which is the world-side shape of the round-trip gap R_T.
3. **Levels of description.** Dennett's physical / design / intentional stances and Marr's three levels are an analogue of level-relative irreducibility (Fig 12 :104), not an identity: the framework's levels are composition levels, not explanatory stances. Do not present them as the same claim.
4. **Observer-side bias.** Agent detection, the Heider-Simmel effect and Huebner's experimental-philosophy results show that attributing intentions to non-human shapes is cheap and common. That explains why Option B feels compulsory; it does not establish that machines lack internal states.
5. **Metric collapse.** Goodhart / Strathern and the JAIR governed-hybrid-agency map both warn that a score selection procedure can replace the target. The JAIR authors call their constructs *"integrative, falsifiable comparison targets rather than a validated universal architecture"*, which is exactly the mode discipline the first contact asks for.
6. **Gap literature.** The framework essays *The Hard Problem is About Source* and *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* are framework-side, maximal overlap, and carry no independent witness weight. They are used for the shape reading and the simulated-status warning only.
