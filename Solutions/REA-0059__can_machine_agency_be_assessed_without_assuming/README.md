## Question restated as an effect

The question asks whether an agency verdict can be reached while leaving the internal psychological question open. Option A says yes: assess the system by its contact with a habitat and a non-updating ground, index the verdict per slot, and read goals and plans as bond types in a description rather than as mental primitives. Option B says no: a genuine agency verdict must ascribe human-like desires or intentions, because that is the only instrument we have. A leads to a checkable, per-system, per-context measurement with a stated refuter and no prior commitment about phenomenology. B leads to a verdict whose anchor is the observer's own case, which the figures allow only when the question is constitutively about people, and which the cognitive-science record shows is a documented false-positive bias.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a separator between the capability question (answerable per system-and-habitat against a ground) and the source question (which internal state, if any, produced the behaviour), with the first settled and the second left as a declared open item. That shape is the sought-for form, written down and refutable; it is not the verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likely agreement with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree. Second, Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; agreement bought by sending the full theory first is the pool talking. Third, Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the architecture. Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public, opt-in institutional venues avoid the structural failure and the legal one together.

## Key verdict in one line

Yes, with a re-grounding and one declared gap: agency is assessable as capability of an agent-and-habitat against a non-updating ground, indexed per slot and read in bonds rather than mental primitives, while the question of what internal source, if any, produced the behaviour stays open because no figure line governs it and no available instrument settles it.
