## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the missing thing. It constrains; it does not fill. For REA-0059 the shape is: *a capability verdict that is agent-and-habitat relative, indexed per slot, anchored outside the frame, with goals and plans read as bonds, and an internal-source question held open*. That is a shape. "The machine has human-like intentions" or "the machine has none" are names, and both outrun what is held.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- The formula's Direction rule: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* I did this in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, before the findings.

Applied here: I described the residual in effect terms (a verdict whose anchor would otherwise be the observer's own case), then went looking for frames that predict its shape. Four were found: AI measurement science, ML theory, cybernetics and cognitive science. I did not pick the fields first and then map them onto the figure; the residual selected them (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the six makes the choice visible, not neutral.

**Consequence for REA-0059:** naming "intention" as the source of a machine's behaviour would be the second kind of move without the gates. The source question stays a shape-shaped hole until a discriminator exists. Note also that the shape itself does not include the word "agency" as a primitive; the word is the ordinary term under audit, not a term the framework supplies.

## 4. Why the direct answer is a source-naming error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* was opened this session. It gives the mechanism in one field. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* What resolved it was not an argument but *"the arrival of a better instrument"*: the spectrometer. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Apply to the question: an agency reading (a behaviour under a context) cannot comment on which internal source produced it. Asking "did the machine act from human-like desire or intention?" reads a source off a projection. The honest reply is not "no" but "this instrument cannot comment; a discriminator is owed." That is Fig 8 :39 *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* stated in measurement terms.

The Voudouris et al. paper (opened, low overlap) independently makes the same structural point without the framework's vocabulary: disposition measurement *"does not require a complete account of the internal mechanism realising that disposition"*, and identifying the causal basis is *"methodologically separable from completely identifying its internal mechanism"*. Note the limit of the corroboration: they are saying mechanism identification is not *necessary* for measurement, not that internal states don't exist. I do not upgrade their claim into a metaphysical verdict.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid describing goals and plans? Correction to my own reading

No. The canonical README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Bonds are already in the library: Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* So describing a system's route with →, ⋛ and − is a binding from the assessment frame onto held structure, not the invention of a new primitive. The framework says it returns a shape, not a name, for the *missing* thing. It does not forbid using the vocabulary it already holds.

**I initially overstated this rule in the draft of this section and the overstatement is preserved here rather than erased.** The narrower, correct form: shape-not-a-name governs the internal-source question, because no figure line supplies such a primitive and no instrument discriminates among candidate sources. It does not govern the use of already-held bonds to describe behaviour. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 12 + Operators table).

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* was opened this session. Its governing sentence: *"Prestige must* **never** *be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For REA-0059, an outreach campaign that collects famous AI researchers willing to agree that machines lack intentions (or have them) produces the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried against a ground, and let a failure count. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 8. One paragraph for the shape file

A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the missing primitive. For this question it is the separator: agency assessment is a per-slot capability claim about an agent-and-habitat, anchored outside the frame and described in bonds, while the internal-source question is held open because no figure line governs it and no instrument yet discriminates among candidate sources. Calling that open item "no intentions" or "real intentions" would name a primitive from a projection, which the metamerism essay forbids and Fig 13 :135 says cannot be done from inside. The essays are framework-side, maximal overlap, so they explain the discipline; they do not independently certify it.
