## 1. Independent domain census from the residual line alone

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The apparent predicate "has agency" is one-place in the question but the assessment needs at least system plus context plus a ground. So arity is at least three. Symmetry: the mental-state ascription is symmetric with the observer's own case, so it carries the disqualifying correlation; a ground reading is ordered, frame to ground, never negotiated. Scale: first order in one agent-habitat pair, second order at the checking room and the population of systems. Question type: union compound. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Domain 1, capability in habitat.** Actors: system, action, observation, habitat. Currency: per-slot residual, settlement. Anchor: a board or measurement that does not move when a frame changes its mind. Question type: partition of legitimate compositions (which bonds cross from state to structure and back). This is a domain.
- **Domain 2, internal source.** Actors: observer and observed. Currency: attributed states by analogy. Anchor: none that passes the anchor test; human judgment is right only for questions constitutively about people. Question type: not yet a domain; a proposed source. No verdict on existence is available.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: outside checkers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence from the primary is zero.
- **Domain 4, perceiver bias.** Actors: perceivers and ambiguous stimuli. Currency: false-positive cost asymmetry and reaction time. Anchor: signal-detection experiments. This is a domain with its own anchor, genuinely lower overlap with the framework.

Dependency order: refuse the one-place form before minting (formula step 2); enumerate the habitat before the verdict (Fig 11 :43-44); settle perception, vocabulary and objective before planning (Fig 3 :20); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 11 and Fig 2, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran it on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40 (capability of agent-and-habitat), Fig 2 :29/:33/:36-38 (anchor and its legitimacy condition), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot), Fig 12 :112 (bonds), Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134-135 (shape, not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand used across the folder: "agency is contact, not intention; assess capability per slot against a non-updating ground; leave the source open".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete assessment claim of the kind a reader might build: "this model is / is not agentic; its behaviour shows its intentions".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor test** itself is dropped when the shorthand is read as "just use behaviour", because a behaviour stream can be a mirror if the assessor's prior updates on it;
- **per-slot indexing** is dropped when the conclusion is collapsed into a single global label;
- the **bond distinction** is dropped when goals and plans are re-read as inner states, which is the flat-formula collapse of Fig 12 :5;
- the **source gap** is erased when the shape is renamed as a denial of internal states, which Fig 8 :39 forbids.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: capability belongs to the agent-and-habitat pair and a structure of bonds describes the route. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
