## What an answer would look like (bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term)

Before deriving, write down what the two options mean in effect terms, so the shape can accrue a residual like any other term (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

- **"Information conserved"** = the map from an earlier description to a later one is invertible, so the earlier description can be reconstructed from the later one. This is a claim about a bijective map.
- **"Information not conserved"** = the map discards something, so reconstruction is impossible and the discarded amount must be recorded as a gap (`R_T`).
- **What is being decided:** whether one of these applies to *all* physical processes, or whether the question bundles two different maps that require two different verdicts.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the conclusion.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list of people who already accept the framework contributes one pool, not independent checks.
3. **Do not hand the interiors to the checker.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's source text.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
