## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound and must be split. Across the framework's scale transform, information is not conserved: loss is definitional and `R_T` is the honest measure of what the coarser description cannot hold. In the six-term substrate, the conserved state is energy, not information. The loss is representational, not a physical destruction claim. For physical dynamics including quantum gravity, the figures give no verdict, so the honest status is `gap`, with `specified` world evidence leaning toward preservation. The verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. This is `general` for the two structural findings and `gap` for the physics finding, all ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separates the maps and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a filled name instead of a generator and a `gap`. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the split and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The `gap` is a recorded stop, not a withdrawn primitive. A source-discriminating instrument moves Domain 3 without requiring any earlier figure line to be retracted; a measured zero `R_T` would refute Domain 1 cleanly and leave Domain 2 standing. No atom has to be invented or deleted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A Planck-scale instrument that discriminates unitarity from objective collapse or remnant scenarios moves Domain 3 from `gap` to a verdict; a measured round trip with zero discarded detail refutes Domain 1; a derivation of conserved information from the six, or a seventh primitive, refutes Domain 2; a re-census showing the within-scale and cross-scale maps are one operator dissolves the whole split. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` with Domain 3 explicitly `gap`.
