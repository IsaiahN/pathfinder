# Expert verification packet — the Tether on information conservation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or information theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on C1 and C2, `gap` on C3. Not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is information conserved in all physical processes, including quantum gravity?" bundles two different maps under one word. Across a scale crossing, the framework's transform loses information by definition and the honest report is a measured round-trip gap `R_T`. In the six-term substrate, the conserved state is energy, not information, and no conserved-information primitive is derivable from the six. For the physical dynamics, including black-hole evaporation and quantum gravity, the figures give no verdict, so a `gap` is recorded rather than a name filled. The verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The framework returns a split and a stop: refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 4 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** the cross-scale description transform is non-invertible; the loss is definitional; the amount given up is `R_T`, measured by a round trip.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :28-29 "Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought back down will not match what was sent." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Symbols table :75-78 defines `R_T` as "the gap between what was sent up and what came back down".
- **What to check:** is the loss claim about a *description* transform (`T_A`/`T_E`) and not about physical dynamics? If it is about dynamics, the claim has been misidentified and should be refused.
- **Refuter:** a figure line making the abstraction/concretisation transform information-preserving as a law (`R_T = 0`), or a measured round trip with zero discarded detail.

### C2 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** in the minimum set, conservation attaches to energy. Information is not among the six and no composition of them yields a conserved-information primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :13-14 "conserved, finite, directionless alone" (energy). Fig 13 :3 "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds." Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Fig 13 :116 "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."
- **What to check:** can any of the six, or any composition of the seven bonds, be shown to carry conservation of information? The thermodynamic-law table in Fig 13 assigns energy to the second law, not information.
- **Refuter:** derive a conserved-information quantity from the six using only the seven bonds, or exhibit the figure line that makes information a seventh member.

### C3 — figure-silence; checkable by searching the figure text yourself

- **Statement:** no figure line governs whether unitary quantum gravity conserves fine-grained information. This is reported as `gap`, not as "no".
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** search the verbatim figure extract for any dynamics-level conservation law over information. There is none. World sources (Hawking 2005, Zhang/Corda/Cai 2025, Wikipedia reference article) lean toward preservation, but they belong to the physics frame and do not decide a figure verdict.
- **Refuter:** a figure line that states a dynamics-level conservation law, or a Planck-scale instrument that discriminates unitarity from objective collapse or remnant scenarios. Either moves C3 from `gap` to a verdict.
- **Strain, named not hidden:** the apparent conflict between C1 (loss) and the physics literature (unitary conservation) is resolved by re-census. C1 governs the description transform; unitarity governs dynamics within a frame. If a re-census shows they are one operator, C1 and C3 both fail.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "information is always conserved" and not "information is always destroyed".
- Not that the framework settles black-hole physics, unitarity, objective collapse or firewall questions.
- Not that the field's consensus is a measurement. It is `specified` world evidence.
- Not a seventh primitive, and not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument.
- Not that the two verdicts can be averaged into "partly conserved". Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." A single word carrying two operators is not one quantity with a compromise value.
- Not that the cross-scale loss is a physical destruction of information (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world").
