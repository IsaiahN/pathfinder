## Three findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The fine-grained / coarse-grained distinction is already the field's own split.** The Wikipedia reference article says unitarity preserves entropy and that apparent irreversibility comes from tracking only coarse-grained features. This corroborates the D1 / D3 census: the word "information" is being used for an invertible fine-grained dynamics map and for a lossy coarse-grained description map. Overlap warning: the reference article and the papers it cites draw on one evidence pool about the Page curve, so treat them as one witness on that point.
2. **Coarse graining discards information as a definitional, monotone quantity.** Mori and Nagasawa (2026) define a "relative entropy of coarse graining to measure discarded UV information" and prove its monotonicity. This is a low-overlap corroboration of Fig 4 :27 from statistical mechanics and RG, a field with no connection to the framework author. It corroborates D1 only, not D3.
3. **"Information is physical" is a thermodynamic claim, not a conservation law over all processes.** Bormashenko (2025) frames Landauer's principle as "the minimum energy necessary for the erasure of one bit of information." That supports the substrate finding (F3) that conservation in the framework attaches to energy: information has a cost measured in energy terms, and is not itself a primitive conserved state.

No world source in this folder was used to *overrule* a figure line. Where a source leans toward preservation in black-hole evaporation, it is recorded as `specified` world evidence while the figure verdict on the physics question remains `gap` (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. 1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A Stage 2 reader who has absorbed the theory has joined the frame being checked, so their agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
