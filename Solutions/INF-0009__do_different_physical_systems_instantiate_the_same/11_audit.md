## Independent domain census from the residual line alone

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. The residual line I worked from was: *what would have to be true for the phrase "same information" to be licensed across two systems with different carriers?*

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A two-place comparison relation, not a one-place property. Arity: two systems plus a slot, a ground and an observer. Symmetry splits the question immediately: a substitution identity is symmetric (Operators :69), a resemblance claim is symmetric in a weaker sense, a source reading is ordered from cause to projection (Fig 12 :73), and a per-slot residual is a comparison, not a join. Scale: four distinct levels, carrier, arrangement, lineage, measure, with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4 :27). A single verdict over all four would be an aggregation error.
- **D1 Carrier.** Actors: physical media, states of space, time and energy. Currency: energy and material cost. Anchor: the ground as measurement plus the energy budget. Admitted, with a live conflict from Thagard 2022.
- **D2 Arrangement.** Actors: atoms, seven bonds, negation, molecules. Currency: description length and which typed bond holds. Anchor: the bond, not recoverable from the operands (Fig 12 :46). Admitted.
- **D3 Lineage.** Actors: routes, provenance, instruments, readings. Currency: discriminating power. Anchor: the record of the route (Fig 13 :130). Admitted, with a `gap`.
- **D4 Measure.** Actors: sender, channel, receiver, slot, R. Currency: bits and actions, two currencies. Anchor: the per-slot residual against an immovable anchor (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D5 Verification.** Actors: frames and readers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **X1 Information itself.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet.

**Union.** Licensed sameness requires D4, D2 and D3 together; D1 varies within a reach; D5 checks from outside. One lever cannot clear all four (Fig 9 :55). **Dependency order:** anchor and slot, then bond, then carrier, then lineage, then outside verification. The question reverses this by naming the two systems first.

My census matches the primary's five domains plus the non-domain, and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5/:46/:117/:125 (bond, isomer, two currencies), Fig 13 :121/:128 (different routes, lineage), Fig 2 :29 (anchor) and Fig 9 :54 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: same arrangement plus same anchor plus same lineage equals same information; carrier varies within a reach and is priced; source is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim such as "these two systems running the same algorithm on different hardware carry the same information".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **level index** at which identity is claimed. Fig 12 :103 makes irreducibility a fact about a level; the shorthand drops the level and invites a cross-level identity claim;
- the **two-currency split** (Fig 12 :125). A concrete sameness sentence almost always quotes only bits and silently drops actions, which is exactly the one-accounting error the figure names;
- the **convergence versus import distinction** (Fig 8 :51). Two systems that reach one atom independently and two that adopted it from one source look identical in the contents; only the record separates them, and a concrete claim rarely carries the record.

One thing survives cleanly: a typed-bond difference with fixed atoms is a different substance, and that is checkable in chemistry. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for the whole bundle; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
