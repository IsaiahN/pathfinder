# WATSON answer, INF-0009

**Mode: `general` on the four-domain split, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on naming a source from a reading, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Do different physical systems instantiate the same information, or only structurally similar representations? Source: Information Theory.txt line 12, section 1 "What information is".

Restated as an effect: what would have to be true for the phrase "same information" to be licensed across two systems with different carriers? Option A predicts that a difference of carrier is not a difference of information and that an instrument checking sameness need only find the invariant. Option B predicts that resemblance is the strongest licensed claim. The derivation refuses the binary as a bundle of four questions with four anchors, and binds the sought-for shape before concluding: the conditions for licensed sameness, plus an instrument that would discriminate carrier, arrangement, source and measure.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** A two-place comparison relation indexed by slot. Arity: two systems plus a slot, a ground and an observer. Symmetry: identity and structural resemblance are symmetric and pass a substitution test (Operators :69); a source reading is ordered, running from cause to projection (Fig 12 :73). Scale: four levels, carrier, arrangement, lineage and measure, with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 Carrier and substrate.** Actors: space, time, energy states; physical media, spins, charges, ink, neurons. Currency: energy and material cost. Anchor: the ground as measurement plus the energy budget. Status admitted, with one named conflict.
- **D2 Structure and arrangement.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds, negation; atoms and molecules. Currency: description length and which typed bond holds. Anchor: the typed bond, not recoverable from the operands (Fig 12 :46). Status admitted.
- **D3 Source and lineage.** Actors: routes, provenance, instruments, readings. Currency: discriminating power. Anchor: the record of the route (Fig 13 :126 to :130). Status admitted, with a `gap` for naming a source from a reading.
- **D4 Measure and residual.** Actors: sender, channel, receiver, slot s, R. Currency: bits and actions, two currencies that do not add. Anchor: the per-slot residual against an immovable anchor (Fig 2 :29). Status admitted.
- **D5 Verification and observer.** Actors: frames, seats, expert readers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Status admitted.
- **X1 "Information itself" as a free-floating property.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet; it is the residual itself.

Union, not partition: licensed sameness requires D4 and D2 and D3 together, with D1 varying within a reach and D5 checking from outside. A single lever clears one condition and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). Dependency order: D4 then D2 then D1 then D3 then D5, because optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not (Fig 3 :19). Strain test: Fig 13 :121 concerns different routes to one composition, Fig 12 :117 concerns different compositions over one atom set, so the apparent contradiction is a domain misreading, not a strain.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated or single-source or gap)

**D4 Measure, status triangulated (Fig 1, Fig 2 and Symbols table, distinct roles).**

- Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." -> sameness is a per-slot claim, never a global average.
- Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." -> the anchor must be immovable when either comparing frame is pushed.
- Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." -> bits and actions must be reported separately.
- Mode `specified`. Refuter: identical per-slot residual reductions against two different anchors that still settle the same information at the ground.

**D2 Arrangement, status triangulated (Fig 12 and Operators table, with an external chemistry corroboration).**

- Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." -> the typed bond is part of the information, so an ingredient match is not an information match.
- Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." -> such systems are different substances, not similar copies.
- Operators :69 "substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No." -> identity has a test.
- Mode `general` for the bond rule, `specified` for systems. Refuter: derive the typed bond from its operands alone, or exhibit identical atoms, bonds and arrangement with different behaviour at the same level.

**D1 Carrier, status triangulated with one named conflict (Fig 13 and Fig 8 for the reach, Fig 12 and Landauer and Thagard for the price).**

- Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." -> different carriers can reach one term.
- Fig 8 :50 "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." -> convergence under differing closures is admitted.
- Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them" -> the reach is not cost-free.
- Mode `specified`. Refuter: two different carriers instantiating one term at identical description cost, identical action cost and zero round-trip gap. The conflict with Thagard 2022 is handled in WORLD below.

**D3 Source, status `gap` (Fig 13 and Fig 8, unreached not silence).**

- Fig 13 :128 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." -> the artefact does not carry its source.
- Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." -> name withheld.
- Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." -> `gap` is a verdict, not silence.
- Mode `gap`. Refuter: a source-discriminating instrument that makes a reading match entail a source match under change of illuminant and observer.

**D5 Verification, status triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10, anchor rule versus verifier non-access).**

- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" -> a consensus is not a witness.
- Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it" -> shared code is a convention, not a verdict about the world.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." -> first contact carries the residual and refuter, never the framework.
- Mode `general`. Refuter: two frames sharing one evidence pool producing independent world evidence.

**Union and method, status triangulated (Fig 9 and Fig 4).**

- Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." -> refuse the forced choice.
- Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" -> describe the residual before naming systems.
- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." -> send a generator, not a recording.

Triangulation summary: D2 and D5 are triangulated across low-overlap figure roles plus an external chemistry corroboration for D2. D4 is triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 2 and the Symbols table with SEP Information as a low-overlap external witness. D1 is triangulated across Fig 13 and Fig 8 with SEP Multiple Realizability and SEP Structural Realism, and carries one live conflict handled below. D3 is single-source at the figure level (Fig 13) and triangulated externally by the metamerism page and the source essay, but the missing instrument is not held, so the domain verdict is `gap`. No per-domain verdict was averaged with another.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The offered binary is refused as a bundle. Two physical systems instantiate the same information only when the same bonded arrangement is settled against the same ground-anchored per-slot residual with a lineage that survives a substitution test. Structural similarity is a functional-group relation, real but weaker than identity. Different carriers can reach one term, so carrier identity is not necessary for reach, but the reach is priced in actions and carries a nonzero round-trip gap. A reading match does not determine a source match; that domain stays a declared `gap`. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `general` on the split, `specified` on the four admitted domain verdicts, `gap` on D3, nowhere `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** if the bundle is forced into one binary, a system failing the lineage condition would be sold a measure instrument, and a system failing the arrangement condition would be told its Shannon entropy already settles the question. The deeper cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator and a verdict instead of a refuter. That cost is bounded and avoidable by leading with the four verdicts and R1 to R7.
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing; the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"*. The shape, the seven refuters and the lineage are recorded so a later reader can locate the error. No primitive has to be withdrawn to supersede a sent claim.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. R1 or R2 would break the arrangement verdict; R3, a source-discriminating instrument, would close the D3 gap; R4 would break the verification verdict; R5 would break the framework; R6 would break the two-currency rule; R7 would break the narrowed carrier verdict. A world source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot residual equality is fully predicted by a single measure with zero tolerance would also reverse the measure verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
