# Expert verification packet - the Tether on information instantiation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a philosopher of information, information theorist or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the four-domain split, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on naming a source from a reading, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle the whole question. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "do different physical systems instantiate the same information, or only structurally similar representations?" bundles four questions with four anchors, so neither offered option is correct as written. The unit of comparison is a per-slot residual against an anchor that does not move when the comparing frame is pushed. The bond carries the information the operands do not, so two systems with identical atoms but a different typed bond are isomers, different substances, not similar representations of the same information. Different physical carriers can nevertheless reach one term by convergent derivation, so substrate identity is not required for reach, but the reach is not free: bits price the description and actions price finding out whether it holds, and the two do not add. A match at the level of a reading does not determine a match at the level of the source. Two physical systems instantiate the same information only when arrangement, anchor and lineage all agree. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and chemistry-facing; checkable by reading Figure 12 and the chemistry record

- **Statement:** same atoms plus a different typed bond is a different substance, not a structurally similar representation of the same information.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :46 - *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*
- **What to check:** is the chemistry case faithful? Silver fulminate and silver cyanate share AgCNO composition and differ in properties; Wikipedia "Isomer" (opened 2026-11-23) confirms the Wöhler and Liebig case of 1826 to 1827 and Berzelius's 1830 naming. Does the figure's notion of typed bond cover the physical relation that actually makes the two systems behave differently?
- **Refuter:** derive which typed bond holds from the two operands alone with no record of the bond, or exhibit two systems with identical atoms, identical bonds and identical arrangement that still behave as different substances at the same level.

### C2 - information-theory facing; checkable against Shannon and algorithmic information

- **Statement:** a measure-level match is the weakest of the four senses of "same" and cannot by itself license the claim. Shannon's measure counts ingredients and is blind to the arrangement; the invariance theorem gives equality up to a constant, which is a bounded gap, not identity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 - *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"*. Fig 4 :30 - *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **What to check:** SEP Information (Adriaans and van Benthem, opened 2026-11-23) states that `0000011111` and `1001011100` carry the same Shannon information because they have the same symbol counts, and that Kolmogorov complexity assigned by two universal machines differs by a constant. Do those two facts establish that a measure match is a tolerance-bound equality rather than an identity? Thagard 2022 measures the action side in bits per joule.
- **Refuter:** exhibit one accounting in which bits and actions add to a single sameness figure with no residual and no tolerance, or two systems with different carriers that instantiate one term at identical description cost, identical action cost and zero round-trip gap.

### C3 - perception and instrumentation facing; checkable against metamerism and source discrimination

- **Statement:** a match at the level of a reading does not determine a match at the level of the source; naming a source from a reading is a declared `gap`, not a silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :128 - *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 13 :135 - *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **What to check:** Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)" (opened 2026-11-23): *"In colorimetry, metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions. Colors that match this way are called metamers."* The page records illuminant, observer and device metameric failure. The framework essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) states that an instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*. Is a reading-level match across two physical systems therefore insufficient for source identity?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument that reads the source rather than the projection, so that a reading match entails a source match under change of illuminant and observer. The spectrometer did this for colour, so the refuter is runnable.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that all universal computers carry literally identical information in every respect. Material and energy costs differ.
- Not that a reading match establishes a source match. That is the D3 gap.
- Not that a consensus of readers makes two representations the same (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that Shannon entropy, Kolmogorov complexity or semantic information is *the* definition this question asks about. Each is a binding from a world frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that the six terms or the seven bonds are nature's own list. Declaring them makes a choice visible, not neutral.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
