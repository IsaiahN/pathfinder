## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> "Same information" is licensed only when the same bonded arrangement is settled against the same ground-anchored per-slot residual, with a lineage that survives a substitution test. A reading match, a pattern resemblance, or a shared vocabulary is not enough.

That is a shape. "The information is the Shannon entropy of the source" is a name. "The information is the Kolmogorov complexity of the string" is another name. The shape does not choose between them, and F1 of the audit explains why choosing one early would collapse four conditions into one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* / *"rather than the cause"*. The effect here is: what would an instrument have to resolve for the equality claim to be settled? That question can be asked without first deciding whether "information" means entropy, complexity or semantic content.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the vocabulary closest to the question is provided by the figures themselves (bond, isomer, identity, per-slot residual, anchor, lineage). The world fields then supply the converging and conflicting terms: multiple realizability, structural realism, Shannon and algorithmic information, metamerism, energy cost. Recursing over these narrows the answer to the four-domain shape above.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question the six are a visible guess about the compression axes of any instantiated system. Naming Shannon entropy, Kolmogorov complexity or semantic information as *the* correct definition of the information in a system turns a visible choice into an invisible one. The shape does not forbid using those measures; it requires that each be declared as a binding from a world frame and be paid for in the right currency.

## 4. Why the source question is the one that stays a gap

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism directly:

> *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"*
> *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*
> *"the instrument detects green... it cannot comment on the source."*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)" (opened this session) is the independent engineering corroboration: *"In colorimetry, metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions. Colors that match this way are called metamers."* The same page records illuminant, observer and device metameric failure, where a match under one condition fails under another.

So when two physical systems give the same reading, there are at least three possibilities the reading cannot distinguish: one source seen twice; two sources that happen to be metameric under this instrument; or two sources related by a shared cause (Fig 2 "collapse 2: common cause", :18-21). Fig 13 :128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. The essay's own precedent is that the spectrometer settled the colour case *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a measure or a bond here? CORRECTION TO MY OWN READING

- The README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- The seven bonds and the six terms are **already held**. So saying "this difference is an isomer difference under a typed bond" is a **binding** from the chemistry frame onto structure already in the vocabulary. It is not the invention of a new primitive.
- What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh term, or claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- **I initially overstated the rule.** Naming Shannon entropy or Kolmogorov complexity is not banned outright. What is banned is treating the name as what the framework derived, rather than as a provenanced binding that still owes settlement. Triangulated (README + Fig 6 :21 + Fig 13 :135). This correction is kept visible, not erased, exactly as the exemplar keeps its own corrections.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that two systems "carry the same information" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, an experiment or a substitution test, settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional venues and no personal emails, and why the first contact asks for a refutation.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures.
