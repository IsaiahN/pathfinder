# The Tether's answer on information instantiation

**Mode: `general` on the four-domain split, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on naming a source from a reading, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question offers a binary: same information, or only structurally similar representations. The residual is that neither option says what "same" is indexed to, against which anchor, at which level, or at what cost. The contact is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* It has four domains with four anchors, and each keeps its own verdict.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **Measure (D4).** "Same information" in the measure sense means the same slot's residual is reduced in the same way against an anchor that does not move (Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*). A global equality over a system averages away the live slot (Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*). Bits and actions are two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*). Mode `specified`, triangulated.
2. **Arrangement (D2).** The bond carries the information the operands do not (Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*). Same atoms with a different typed bond is an isomer (Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*), not a similar representation of the same thing. Identity has a substitution test: *"two names, one referent"* (Operators :67). Mode `general` for the bond rule, `specified` for systems, triangulated.
3. **Carrier (D1).** Different physical carriers can reach one term: Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* World sources corroborate from low-overlap fields (multiple realizability, structural realism, Fodor's same information over many channels). But the reach is not cost-free: Thagard 2022 conflicts with a strong substrate-independence reading, and the conflict was returned to the census and narrowed the verdict to same term at a different price. Mode `specified`, triangulated with one named conflict.
4. **Source (D3).** A match at the level of a reading does not determine a match at the level of the source (Fig 13 :128 *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."*). Metamerism and the source essay corroborate. This is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached rather than silence (Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).
5. **Verification (D5).** Agreement inside one evidence pool is a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Mode `general`.

**Overall verdict.** The question's "or" is refused as a bundle. Different physical systems instantiate the same information only when the same bonded arrangement is settled against the same ground-anchored per-slot residual with a lineage that survives substitution. Structural similarity alone is a functional-group relation, real but weaker. Substrate difference is admitted in the reach and priced in the cost. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that all universal computers carry literally identical information in every respect. Energy and material cost differ (Fig 12 :125).
- Not that a reading match establishes a source match. That is the D3 gap.
- Not that a consensus of readers makes two representations the same (Fig 2 :35).
- Not that Shannon entropy, Kolmogorov complexity or semantic information is *the* definition of information. Each is a provenanced binding from a world frame, not a derivation.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles any per-slot residual here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. The source essay's precedent is that the spectrometer, not reasoning, resolved the metamerism case. Here:

- **Substitution test:** can S1 replace S2 at the stated level with nothing lost, and does the per-slot residual at the same anchor stay unchanged? (R1 and R2).
- **Source-discriminating instrument:** does a match survive change of illuminant, observer and channel? (R3).
- **Single-anchor check:** is the anchor immovable when either comparing frame is pushed? (Fig 2 :30).
- **Two-currency check:** are bits and actions reported separately, with a measured round-trip gap R_T? (Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*).
- **Standing framework falsifiers:** derive one of the six from the other five; derive a typed bond from its operands; exhibit an accounting where bits and actions add.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The question's own vocabulary is borrowed, so the effect was described before a frame was chosen (Fig 9 :30-31). The D3 source remains unnameable from a reading alone: Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether refuses the forced choice. "Same information" is a bundle of four questions with four anchors, not one binary. The unit of comparison is a per-slot residual against an anchor that does not move; the bond carries the information the atoms do not, so two systems with the same atoms and a different typed bond are isomers, different substances, not similar copies of one; different physical carriers can still reach one term by convergent derivation, but at a different price in actions and with a nonzero round-trip gap; and a match at the level of a reading does not settle a match at the level of the source, which stays a declared gap until a source-discriminating instrument exists. So two physical systems instantiate the same information only when arrangement, anchor and lineage all agree. Structural similarity is a real but weaker relation. What the framework offers is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The source stays open until the ground settles it.
