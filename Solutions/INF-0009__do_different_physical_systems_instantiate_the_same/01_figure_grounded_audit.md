## 0. The claim under audit

> **Question:** Do different physical systems instantiate the same information, or only structurally similar representations?
>
> **Option A:** different physical systems can instantiate the *same* information. The information is a pattern that is independent of its carrier.
> **Option B:** different physical systems produce only *structurally similar representations*. Resemblance is the strongest licensed claim; identity is never warranted.

Both options are kept. Neither is averaged with the other. The task of the audit is to find what each option would require at the ground, then decide which figure lines govern each requirement.

---

## 1. The residual, in effect terms

State R before choosing any figure line. Two systems S1 and S2 are given with different physical carriers. An observer asks whether `info(S1) = info(S2)` or only `info(S1) ~ info(S2)`. The residual is the gap between what the equality claim predicts and what an instrument can actually settle.

- **Arity.** The question is a two-place comparison relation indexed by a slot, not a one-place property of a system. R is measured per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). A global statement such as "these systems carry the same information" averages away the slot where the equality fails (Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*).
- **Symmetry.** Three of the candidate senses of "same" are symmetric and one is ordered. Identity (≡) is symmetric: *"substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No."* (Fig 12 :69). Structural similarity is symmetric in the same way (conjunction and identity bonds, Fig 12 :59, :69). A sample of a *source* is ordered: the route runs from cause to reading (Fig 12 :73, *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*), and an instrument reads the projection but *"cannot comment on the source"* (essay, Hard Problem is About Source). The measure itself is a per-slot error signal, which is a comparison (⋛) and not a join.
- **Scale.** The comparison can be made at four different levels, and the transform between them is lossy (Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*). The four levels are the carrier (states), the arrangement (bonds and molecules), the lineage (provenance), and the measure (per-slot residual against an anchor). These are not four spellings of one answer. They are four questions with four anchors.

The dependency order follows Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* The measure and its anchor must be settled before the arrangement, the arrangement before the carrier is allowed to vary, and the lineage before the verdict is offered to an outside frame.

## 4. Findings F1 to F12

### F1. The "or" is a union, not a choice (governing finding)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

Option A and Option B are not contradicting answers to one question. They are answers to different questions that the word "information" has bundled. A carrier-level identity question, an arrangement-level identity question, a source-level identity question, and a measure-level identity question are four conditions. You cannot settle them with one lever.

**Refuter for F1:** exhibit one anchor and one instrument that settles carrier identity, arrangement identity, source identity, and measure identity at once, with no residual left over. Until that is done the union reading stands.

### F2. "The bond is the rest of the information" (decisive for D2)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*

Two systems with the same atoms but a different typed bond do not carry the same information. The difference is not in the ingredients; it is in the relation, and that relation is *"not recoverable from the operands"*. This is the figure-grounded reason Option B has real content: resemblance at the level of ingredients is not identity at the level of the molecule.

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :47: *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*

**Refuter for F2:** derive which typed bond holds from the two operands alone, with no record of the bond. That would make the bond recoverable and collapse the finding.

### F3. Isomerism is the exact structural analogue, and it is not an analogy

Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :97: *"silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced"* structure into the notation.

This is a chemistry fact, not a metaphor imported into the framework. Wikipedia "Isomer" (opened 2026-11-23) confirms: *"isomers are molecules or polyatomic ions with an identical molecular formula... but distinct arrangements of atoms in space."* The same page records that Wöhler prepared silver cyanate in 1827 with the same elemental composition as Liebig's silver fulminate but distinct properties, which *"challenged the prevailing chemical understanding of the time, which held that chemical compounds could be distinct only when their elemental compositions differ"*, and that Berzelius introduced the term isomerism in 1830. This is a low-overlap corroboration for D2. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the full derivation.

**Refuter for F3:** exhibit two physical systems with identical atom sets, identical typed bonds, and identical arrangement that behave as different substances at the same level. Fig 12 :92 says the symmetry test would have to fail: *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."*

### F4. Identity has a test, and it is substitution with nothing lost

Operators table :67: *"two names, one referent"*; :69: *"substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No."* The identity bond ≡ is symmetric. This supplies the operational test for Option A that the question never states: two systems carry the same information in the ≡ sense if either can be substituted for the other, at the level under test, with nothing lost.

The world literature converges on the same test from a different field. SEP Semantic Conceptions of Information (opened 2026-11-23) quotes Evans: *"We want to be able to say that two informational states (states of different persons) embody the same information, provided that they result from the same initial informational event."* That is a provenance condition, not a resemblance condition. The same entry quotes Fodor: *"they explain why information (indeed, why the very same information ) can be transmitted over so many different kinds of channels."* That is a reach condition over differing carriers. Together they triangulate the figure-derived shape from a genuinely different evidence pool.

**Refuter for F4:** show a substitution that loses nothing at the stated level yet changes the settled per-slot residual at the ground.

### F5. Sameness of measure is blind to arrangement (strain named and located)

SEP Information (Adriaans and van Benthem, opened 2026-11-23): *"the string 0000011111 contains just as much Shannon information as the string 1001011100 because it has the same number of ones and zeros."*

This is not a conflict with F2. It is the **same failure described from outside the framework**. Shannon's measure counts ingredients; it does not price the bond. Fig 12 :42 prices the bond: `(k+1)·log₂(|atoms|+1) + (k−1)·log₂(|bonds|)`. The two strings have the same ingredient multiset, so a flat measure cannot tell them apart. Fig 12 :14: *"nothing downstream can tell them apart,"* and :15: *"and nothing signals that anything was lost."* This is the flat-formula collapse Fig 12 names.

It is also why an information-theoretic equality cannot by itself license "same information" in the sense the question asks. A **measure-level match** is the weakest of the four senses.

**Refuter for F5:** produce a measure that is invariant under a change of typed bond yet still predicts the behaviour that the bond change alters. If such a measure could be settled at the ground, the bond would be carrying no information and Fig 12 :5 would be wrong.

### F6. Different carriers can reach one term, but the reach is not cost-free (D1)

Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

So Option A is admitted at the **reach** level: two frames with different closures can converge on one term. SEP Structural Realism (opened 2026-11-23) states the world-side version: *"The structuralist solution to this problem is to take the metaphysical import of successful scientific theories to consist in their giving correct descriptions of the structure of the world, where this structure can be captured with different ontologies."* The same entry records Russell's claim that *"science can only describe the world up to isomorphism"*. OECS Multiple Realizability (Polger, opened 2026-11-23): *"Computer software can be implemented or instantiated on different kinds of hardware."*

But the admission is bounded. Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"*. Landauer's *"Information is physical"* (Physics Today, 1991, opened 2026-11-23) and Thagard 2022 (opened 2026-11-23, a genuine **conflict** source) insist that real information processing is energy-bound and materially specific. Thagard writes: *"there are important real-world cases where it fails"* of substrate independence.

**Conflict handling, not a verdict flip.** Thagard conflicts with a *strong* reading of Option A, the reading that carrier difference costs nothing. Per WATSON discipline the conflict sends the derivation back to the census. Re-census: D1's verdict is narrowed to a reach claim plus a cost claim. Different carriers can instantiate the same *term*; they do not instantiate it at the same *price*. The two-currency rule and a nonzero round-trip gap R_T carry exactly the difference Thagard measures (bits per joule, inferences per joule). The verdict is not flipped; it is made conditional. See `09_watson_answer.md` WORLD and `11_audit.md` strains.

**Refuter for F6:** exhibit two systems with different carriers that instantiate one term at identical description cost and identical action cost, with zero round-trip gap at every level. No source read provides such a case.

### F7. A reading does not determine a source (D3, declared gap)

Fig 13 :128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* Fig 13 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."*

The canonical figures README states the limit as *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The framework essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism: *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."* and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The instrument detects green but *"it cannot comment on the source."*

Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)" (opened 2026-11-23) is an independent, low-overlap corroboration: *"In colorimetry, metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions. Colors that match this way are called metamers."* It adds that *"different combinations of light across all wavelengths can produce an equivalent receptor response and the same tristimulus values or color sensation"*, and that a match can fail on change of illuminant or observer. So a reading-level match across two systems is exactly the phenomenon that does **not** establish source identity.

This domain ends in a declared `gap`, not a silence. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* What is missing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held.

**Refuter for F7:** exhibit an instrument that, under change of illuminant and observer, reads the source rather than the projection, so that a match at the reading entails a match at the source. The essay's own precedent is that this happened for colour with the spectrometer, *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, so the refuter is genuinely runnable, not decorative.

### F8. The anchor must not move between the two systems (D4)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*

Symbols table :36-40: R is *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it. It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

So "same information" in the measure sense means: the same slot's residual is reduced in the same way against the same immovable anchor. If the anchor differs between S1 and S2, equality has not been established; two frames have merely agreed about their own readings. Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

**Refuter for F8:** show a per-slot residual reduction that is identical against two different anchors and still settles the same information at the ground.

### F9. Agreement among converging readers is a convention, not a verdict (D5)

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Two experts who both say "same information" after reading the same sources are one witness wearing two names. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* The verification protocol in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` therefore asks for a refutation of the stage-1 shape before showing any theory.

**Refuter for F9:** exhibit a verification arrangement in which two frames sharing one evidence pool produce independent evidence about the world. By Fig 2 :31 this is close to a contradiction; that is the point of naming it in advance.

### F10. What crosses between systems is a method, not a recording

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

If "same information" is taken to mean that the exact representation of one system appears in the other, the framework says what actually transfers is a generator that can be reapplied, compressed to its essentials, and a head start that makes the next search cheaper. The copy of a representation is a recording of one occasion. This matters for the question because it splits "same information" into same *artefact* (a recording, which is not portable without loss) and same *generator* (a method, which is portable by Fig 4). Round trip gap R_T is nonzero (see `11_audit.md`).

**Refuter for F10:** exhibit a lossless copy of a representation crossing between two systems of different closure with zero round-trip gap. Fig 4 :27 says the transform loses information and that is the point.

### F11. Bits and actions are two currencies, so "same information" is ambiguous about price

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :127: *"The two readings come apart in both directions."*

Two systems can match in bits and differ in actions, or match in actions and differ in bits. SEP Information (Adriaans and van Benthem) records the invariance theorem: *"in the limit the complexity assigned to a dataset by two different universal Turing machines only differs by a constant."* That constant is the framework's R_T in another notation: equality up to a bounded gap, not identity. Thagard 2022 supplies the action side in bits per joule. So "same information" must always say which currency is being equated and what tolerance is allowed.

**Refuter for F11:** exhibit a single accounting in which bits and actions add to one figure for sameness. Fig 12 :129 names the error: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the Field Guide: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **R1 (against D2/D4 sufficiency).** Exhibit S1 and S2 with identical atom sets, identical typed bonds, and identical arrangement, settled against the same anchor, that yield different per-slot residuals.
2. **R2 (against D2 necessity).** Exhibit S1 and S2 with different bonded arrangements that nevertheless carry identical settled information, that is, the same residual on the same slot against the same anchor.
3. **R3 (against D3 gap).** Exhibit an instrument that reads the source rather than the projection, so that a reading match entails a source match under change of illuminant and observer.
4. **R4 (against D5).** Exhibit a single frame that certifies its own sameness verdict with no differing closure and no ground reading, and show the certificate is accepted.
5. **R5 (framework level).** Derive one of the six terms from the other five, or derive a typed bond from its operands alone.
6. **R6 (against the two-currency claim).** Exhibit one accounting in which bits and actions add to a single sameness figure with no residual and no tolerance.
7. **R7 (against the narrowed D1 verdict).** Exhibit two systems with different carriers that instantiate one term at identical description cost, identical action cost, and zero round-trip gap at every level.

If any fires, the corresponding domain verdict is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing in this folder may be labelled `grounded`.
