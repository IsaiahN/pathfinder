## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public submission portals, general institutional addresses, or opt-in communities.
