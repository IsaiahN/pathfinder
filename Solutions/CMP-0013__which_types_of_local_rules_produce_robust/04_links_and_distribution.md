## 2. Two facts from the architecture article that bear directly on this question

1. **"Three mappings offered and declined"** is the article's own precedent for refusing a mapping that resembles the framework without aligning with it. Bayesian updating was declined because *"there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism."* Naming "negative feedback" as the figure-given answer to this question has the same problem: the figure gives a closing-loop shape, and the control-theory name is an import. Cite this section when explaining why no rule type is asserted.
2. **The triangulation claim is a retrodiction.** The Bekenstein, Hawking, 't Hooft and Susskind entry is tagged *"a case the framework describes, arrived at independently by someone else"*, and the article says the claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* The CAS examples in this solution (ant foraging, boids, canalisation) are in the same position: corroboration at second strength, not results the framework produced.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** : *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the shape, the refuter, and the method. Do not send a list of rules as if it were knowledge.
- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** : *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the closing-loop shape were true before naming any rule type.
- **Seat Map :163** : *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** : *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Use the public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`; do not compile a personal list.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) only if asked, the formula as a method each person runs against their own ground.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape, and the pre-registered refuter. No theory, no links, no request for agreement. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the sources. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

**Stage 3, method only.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"*. The architecture article warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This packet is simulated and ungrounded, so it is offered for checking, not for belief. No measurement has been taken.
