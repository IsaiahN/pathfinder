## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The extract adds that the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** for this question is the narrowed form of a robust-rule answer that can be stated before a specific rule class can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. "A closing loop that damps its per-slot remainder in a named habitat" is a shape. "Negative feedback always wins" is a name, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27 to :28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the two-stage offer in one sentence.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* A refuter written before the run is the operational form of this, because it binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :55 in prose, and it is why `01` splits order formation from order invariance.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, a per-slot invariance measurement across a stated family of initial conditions is what would settle a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52). The attractor, canalisation, and swarm references are such frames. They carry part of the answer, but importing them must be attributed, not silently adopted.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question: declaring that local rules are the right level of description makes that choice visible, not neutral. A rule-only answer hides the habitat, and the hidden choice then does the work of an argument.

## 4. Why the source question matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here, a macro pattern (segregation, flocking, a stable attractor) is a projection. Several local rule sets can produce the same macro reading. Naming the rule from the pattern is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument that discriminates sources, specifically a per-slot metric that tracks which initial conditions land in which basin. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the same limit as *"indiscernibility is not identity"* and *"Contact is Distinction Resolver"*. Two rule sets indistinguishable at the macro projection are not thereby the same rule set; the lineage is what distinguishes them.

## 5. Q, answered

**What licenses a specific rule type as "the robust one"?** Nothing in the figures. The framework returns a shape. A specific type is licensed only by the ground settling a per-slot invariance measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question as posed commit the forbidden error?** Yes. "Which types of local rules produce robust collective behavior" asks for names, and the framework returns a shape. It also names the rule level and omits the habitat, which *How a Society of Agents Would Work* calls out: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The correct form is: which structural properties of a rule, in an enumerated habitat, give macro invariance across a stated family of initial conditions, and what measurement would license a name.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for the answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to endorse a rule list is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried.

**Variance and structure.** *How Intelligence Actually Works* says of diversity that *"it's a robust property that hardens an entire population against something that would affect them en masse if they were homogeneous."* *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds that the arrangement matters more than the members: *"The arrangement mattered more than the members, which is Figure 12's 'The arrangement is the substance.'"* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* So part of the shape answer is structural, not ingredient-based: which bond holds between local rules matters as much as which rules are present. `06_isomers_and_order.md` derives that part.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* From *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* and the output warning: *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figure text. They are **not** a test of which local rules are robust and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
