## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

From *The Library of Babel* (VERIFIED): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the numerical content. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *per-function residual slots, a union of overlapping conditions, no standing ordering, bonds carrying the arrangement, cascades to third order, and an asymmetric management frame*. A universal response curve with a fitted slope is not the shape; it is a name with a number attached.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (VERIFIED): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names this question's own structure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54-57 in essay language. Multiple functions are a bundle until unbundled.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (CARRIED): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here that means: once you can state the per-slot union shape, there is no further figure question to ask about a single global response.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a measured, non-updating function rate per slot (Fig 2 :29), read through an instrument that discriminates sources.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Trait ecology and network ecology are such frames. Their claims must arrive with provenance, not as framework outputs.

*They're Scanning Books* (CARRIED): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: even the list of functions is a visible choice. Declaring that a study measured 34 functions (Albert et al. 2026) makes that choice visible; it does not make the list nature's own.

## 4. Why a single index is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (VERIFIED) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

A multifunctionality index is exactly such a projection. One number can be produced by productivity rising and decomposition falling, or by all functions rising modestly, or by a different subset in a different habitat. The index cannot comment on the source. Bradford et al. 2014 is the field demonstration: the indices rose consistently while three of five processes were neutral or negative, and the authors called it a statistical fallacy analogous to Simpson's paradox. The essay's conclusion applies: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the better instrument is a per-function measurement with a source-discriminating design, not a more refined argument about the index.

Fig 10 :25 *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"* names the channel failure, and Fig 10 :24 says *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. The function rate is still there; the index has lost the route to it.

## 5. Q2 answered, and a correction to my own first reading

**Question:** does "shape, not a name" forbid saying which functions respond to diversity loss?

**First reading (now narrowed):** yes, the framework returns a shape, so no function can be named as the responsive one.

**Correction kept visible.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Ecosystem functions are not missing primitives; they are enumerated from the habitat (Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*). Naming the functions a study measured is legitimate enumeration, and naming a trait-based or network-based binding is a provenanced import, not the invention of a seventh term. What the framework actually forbids is (a) a standing cross-ecosystem list of robust and sensitive functions (Fig 11 :51) and (b) claiming that the index-derived name was figure-derived. My first reading overstated the rule; the narrowed version stands, and the overstatement is recorded here rather than erased.

**Answer:** you may name the function slots you measured and you may bind a trait or network frame to them with provenance. You may not return a universal ordering of functions or present the binding as a derivation. The universal response curve remains a `gap`.
