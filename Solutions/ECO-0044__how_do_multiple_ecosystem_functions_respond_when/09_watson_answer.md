## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the figures license about the response of several ecosystem functions to biodiversity decline. Option A: one multifunctionality response curve with one sign and one ordering of functions. Option B: per-function residual slots kept separate, a union of overlapping conditions, no standing sensitive-function list, bonds carrying the arrangement, and a declared gap for the universal curve. What each leads to: A gives a single cheap number that discards sign and ranking (Bradford et al. 2014 found the index rose while three of five processes were neutral or negative); B gives checkable per-domain verdicts and an instrument owed, at the cost of being harder to communicate. Verdict from the figures: B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: many, one slot per function, so R is a vector and never a scalar (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the decline to response relation is ordered, while a richness-index association is symmetric; + cannot choose an arrow that → or ⇒ carries. Scale: local plot and one trophic group at first order, across trophic levels and habitats through cascades at second order, across seasons and years through asynchrony at third. Fig 9 :22 calls this *"arity · symmetry · scale"*.
- **Domain 1, measurement and index frame.** Actors: multifunctionality indices, standardized function rates, meta-analyses. Currency: an index score or effect size. Anchor: the measured rate of each function, a flux or counter that does not move when the index is recomputed (Fig 2 :29). Question type: a per-slot partition that is often illegally fused into a scalar. Order: first.
- **Domain 2, ecological habitat.** Actors: species, functional groups, interactions, habitat conditions. Currency: matter and energy flux through contact. Anchor: the same per-function measured rate, read separately. Question type: union of overlapping conditions with third-order cascades. Order: second, depends on D1 slot definitions.
- **Domain 3, management and service frame.** Actors: managers, land users, stakeholders. Currency: delivered service and the capacity to refuse a verdict. Anchor: the same measured rate plus a ground that does not update on the service report. Question type: maintained habitat with asymmetric power. Order: third.
- **Not a domain: biodiversity as one x-axis.** Species richness is a count of ingredients with no distinct currency or anchor, so it is not admitted as a domain.
- **Partition or union.** D1, D2 and D3 overlap; the same measured rate is read, produced and valued in different frames. A single lever leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Dependency order.** Perceive each function, have terms for what it affords and what counts as progress, index by slot and refuse to average, enumerate habitat and cascades, build the union compound, then let management act. Effort above the break cannot rescue a link below it (Fig 3 :20).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's six physical terms were first tried as the list of ecosystem functions and strained badly. Re-census assigns Fig 11 to habitat enumeration and keeps Fig 13 for composition grammar only (strain S1 in `01` section 5). Five strains are named there, none hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The per-slot shape survives; the universal curve does not. Multiple ecosystem functions respond as separate residual slots with separate anchors, never as one averaged curve; their drivers form a union of overlapping conditions with third-order cascades; actor valence is relative to the function tracked, so no standing sensitive-function list is licensed; species counts name a state while interaction bonds carry the route, making same-species communities with different links isomers; the decline to response arrow is ordered while a pooled richness association is symmetric; and management adds contact but cannot subtract habitat, so a functioning state is permanent work. The universal response curve, threshold value and cross-ecosystem function ordering are a declared `gap`. D1 and D2 are `specified` and triangulated, D3 is `specified` and single-source on the framework side, the curve is `gap`. Nothing is `grounded`.
