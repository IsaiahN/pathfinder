## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the vector of gaps between predicted and observed function rates after a biodiversity decline. Arity: many, one per function, so R is indexed by slot and cannot be a scalar. Symmetry: the response is ordered, decline then function change; a richness-index correlation is symmetric and cannot choose the arrow. Scale: local plot and single trophic group at first order, cross-trophic and cross-habitat cascades at second and third order, seasonal and interannual asynchrony as a time channel.
- **Domain 1, measurement and index frame.** Actors: indices, rates, meta-analyses. Currency: scores and effect sizes. Anchor: the measured function rate read separately per slot, which does not update on an index. Question type: a per-slot partition that is often fused into a scalar.
- **Domain 2, ecological habitat.** Actors: species, groups, interactions, conditions, relations. Currency: matter and energy flux through contact. Anchor: the same per-function rate. Question type: union of overlapping conditions with cascades.
- **Domain 3, management and service frame.** Actors: managers and stakeholders. Currency: delivered service and the capacity to refuse. Anchor: the same rate plus a ground that does not update on the service report. Question type: maintained habitat with asymmetric power.
- **Not a domain: richness as one x-axis.** Count of ingredients, no distinct anchor, so not admitted.
- **Partition or union.** The three domains overlap on the same measured rate. Union, not partition. Within D2 the conditions overlap too.
- **Order by dependency.** Instrument then vocabulary of progress then per-slot indexing then habitat enumeration then union compound then management. The middle link, vocabulary and objective, is the one Fig 3 :27 says has no instrument.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. I derived the boundaries from Fig 1 (indexing) and Fig 10 (channel decay), whereas the primary's route in `01` section 2 names Fig 9 and Fig 11. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes inside one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one pool.
