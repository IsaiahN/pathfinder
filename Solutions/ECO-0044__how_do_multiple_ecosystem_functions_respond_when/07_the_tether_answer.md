# The Tether's answer on multiple ecosystem functions and biodiversity decline

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape claims, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the universal response curve. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An ecosystem loses species and interactions, and we want to know what happens to several function rates at once. The residual is not one number. It is a vector indexed by function slot s: R_s = |prediction_s - observation_s|. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a partition into resilient and fragile sets. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* it is a specific compound. The union is over species identities, interaction bonds, habitat conditions and cascades, with an unassignable shared fraction.

## 2. The shape

1. **Verdicts are per function and are never averaged.** Each function has its own anchor, the measured rate that does not move when an index is recomputed (Fig 2 :29). A global index near zero with one live function is a legal state. Bradford et al. 2014 is the field case: the indices rose while three of five processes were neutral or negative.
2. **There is no standing list of sensitive or robust functions.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A species, trait or group helps one function and can harm another. Hector and Bagchi 2007 found only 0.2 to 0.5 overlap among species sets affecting different functions.
3. **The response is a union of overlapping conditions.** Fig 9 :54-57 requires unbundling before searching. One lever clears the region where a single condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Soliveres et al. 2016 found on average three trophic groups influencing each service.
4. **The arrangement carries what the count does not.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Two communities with the same species list and different interactions are isomers (Fig 12 :117), and that difference is decisive in behaviour. Albert et al. 2026 found network structure often more influential than tree species richness.
5. **The causal direction is ordered; a pooled association is not.** The decline precedes the response. A symmetric richness correlation cannot choose that arrow (Operators table :34). An instrument that supplies the order is owed.
6. **Cascades reach third order.** Fig 11 :52-54: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* A predator loss can surface in nutrient dynamics far from the first contact.
7. **Management is asymmetric and permanent work.** Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :63: *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* A restored multifunctionality target is a maintained contact regime, not an equilibrium.
8. **The universal response curve is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* No figure line returns a slope, threshold diversity value or cross-ecosystem function ordering. A site-specific result is a binding from the world frame with provenance, not a figure-derived universal.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "biodiversity loss always reduces multifunctionality" as a universal verdict. The literature reports a generally positive index-level effect, but the figures license only the per-slot shape; the sign of any slot is a ground reading.
- Not "each function responds the same way". That is the aggregation error F1 refutes.
- Not a standing ranking of which functions fail first. Fig 11 :51 refuses it.
- Not that a multifunctionality index is useless. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* It is a filter for deciding where to look.
- Not that the six-term physical substrate is a list of ecosystem functions. Fig 11 enumerates the habitat; Fig 13 governs composition only.
- Not `grounded`. No function rate was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (VERIFIED): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. The spectrometer resolved that case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Per-slot measurement:** for each function, is the response sign and magnitude read separately, with the rate and its own uncertainty, rather than through a pooled index?
- **Source discrimination:** does the design distinguish a decline driven by species identity, by interaction arrangement, by habitat condition, or by a third-order cascade? A one-number reading cannot.
- **Order:** is there a before/after or exogenous-decline instrument, so the → is observed rather than assumed from a symmetric association?
- **Threshold test:** does a threshold-like collapse appear at a function slot, or only in the index? Bradford et al. 2014 shows the two can come apart.
- **Standing-list test:** does any candidate ordering of functions survive changing which function is tracked? Fig 11 :51 predicts it will not.
- **Instrument extension:** per Fig 6 :32-35, an instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something. The existing per-function rate measurements are the proto-instrument; the index is the coarsening to be tested against them.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set returns a kind of thing, not a curve. The ecological literature has many site-specific results, but none of them is a figure-derived universal, and a search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39). The framework stops at the limit of what its instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). The two domain verdicts that are `specified` are kept separate; the third is `single-source` on the framework side; the universal curve is `gap`.
