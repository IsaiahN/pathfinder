## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
2. **A pooled response is not a verdict.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single multifunctionality number cannot decide how several functions respond.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
