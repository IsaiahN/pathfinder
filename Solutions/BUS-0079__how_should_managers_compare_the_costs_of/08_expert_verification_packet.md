# Expert verification packet: the Tether on comparing costs across parties

**What this is:** a claim in a form a decision analyst or management accountant can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No firm measurement is claimed. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When the costs of quality failures, safety incidents, delays and prevention fall on different parties, the figures do not license a single additive total. The residual is indexed per party and per slot; the currencies and anchors differ; and the four classes form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. What survives is a per-party vector, each cost in its own currency ranged against its own non-updating anchor, overlap left unassigned, prevention recorded as a reapplicable method rather than a one-occasion replay, and a pre-registered failure condition. A local, scope-bounded conversion may be grounded with the right instrument; a universal rate is a declared gap. Budget decisions are threshold comparisons and progress is a subtraction, not a sum.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** a single additive total across parties is structurally refused. Report a per-party vector instead, with anchors named per line and overlap unassigned.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
  - Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
  - Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
  - Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."
  - Fig 9 :55-57 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."
- **What to check:** are the four classes a union in the reader's own setting, and do the party readings use different units and anchors? If they genuinely share one anchor and one unit, C1 does not bite there.
- **Refuter:** a figure line licensing a single additive total with a non-updating conversion anchor; or an empirical partition with assignable shared fraction and no cross-class overlap.

### C2 Framework-internal plus accounting-facing; checkable by a management accountant

- **Statement:** the cost-of-quality report is frame-internal and cannot score the frame that produces it. An allocation that decides a budget has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
  - Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
  - Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
  - Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
  - Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check:** does the reader's PAF report decide budgets while its own inputs come from the same ledger it scores? Are the party anchors (return, injury, stopped line, fine) read independently, or recomputed from the report?
- **Refuter:** a report whose allocation demonstrably decides no outcome, with party anchors still read independently and shown unmoved by it.

### C3 Framework-internal derivation; checkable by a decision analyst

- **Statement:** the comparison is a typed threshold relation, not a sum, and any local rate needs a source-discriminating instrument. The operand type of ⋛ across money and harm is a live strain, named rather than hidden.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"
  - Operators table :51 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"
  - Operators table :46 "progress is a subtraction, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot say how far along it is"
  - Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."
  - Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
- **What to check:** can a weighting scheme state a goal and a progress reading without comparison and subtraction terms? Does a single total return the same number for a fatality and a warranty claim, so that the reading cannot comment on the source?
- **Refuter:** a decision procedure using only conjunction that states its goal and progress checkably at the ground; or an instrument that discriminates the sources and grounds a local rate; or a figure re-census admitting ⋛ over a money quantity and a harm quantity at the same level, which would clear the type strain.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal dollar value of a safety incident, a universal cost-of-quality percentage, or a conversion rate for any firm.
- Not that cost of quality is unimportant, or that prevention always dominates. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :51).
- Not `grounded`. No party anchor was read and no firm was measured.
- Not that the figures name the conversion atom. They return a shape, and the shape-not-a-name rule governs missing primitives, so naming held terms such as money, time and injury counts is a provenanced binding, not a derivation (see `05` section 5b and `06` Q1).
- Not that the simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` is an independent witness. It shares the evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Party-anchor test:** can each party's cost be read at an instrument that does not update on the manager's report?
2. **Source-discrimination test:** can an instrument separate a fatality from a warranty claim where the total currently returns one number?
3. **Independence test:** are the four readings drawn from different evidence pools, or one ledger?
4. **Generator test:** is the prevention spend a reapplicable method or a replay of one occasion?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19/:22/:27, Fig 12 :125/:130, Fig 10 :47 and Fig 9 :55-57, seven short passages.
2. Check C1 and C2 against your own organization: are the four classes a union with different anchors, and does the report decide budgets?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a safety source from a warranty source in your cost report, or say why none is possible.
4. Name the live strain in C3 in your own terms (the operand type of comparison across money and harm).
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
