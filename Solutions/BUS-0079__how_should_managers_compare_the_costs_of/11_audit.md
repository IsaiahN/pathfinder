## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unread party-bearing cost behind a single management total. Arity: four named cost classes crossed with several parties, so a **union of overlapping conditions** (Fig 9 :55-57), not one quantity to minimise. Symmetry: the question's "compare" proposes a symmetric additive relation, but a money amount and an injury count are not interchangeable operands, so the applicable relation is an ordered threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87). Scale: per party and per slot (Fig 1 :19), with contact cascading outward (Fig 11 :52-54).
- **Domain 1, firm prevention and appraisal.** Actors: managers, quality engineers. Currency: money, tests, training, defect counts. Anchor: a test result, a specification, a warranty claim, a fine. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 2, customer quality failure.** Actors: customers. Currency: returns, lost time, harm. Anchor: the return, complaint or field injury. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 3, worker safety.** Actors: workers, unions, regulators. Currency: injuries, near misses, lives, capacity to succeed. Anchor: an actual injury or inspection. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 4, supplier and downstream delay.** Actors: suppliers, logistics. Currency: time, schedule, delay cost per unit time. Anchor: a delivery date, a stopped line. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 5, society and regulator.** Actors: regulators, communities. Currency: compliance, social cost, externalities. Anchor: a law, a fine, a published statistic. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 6, the accounting frame.** Actors: the manager, accountants. Currency: a declared common measure. Anchor: none of its own; the report is not a ground (Fig 1 :27, Fig 10 :18). Not a sixth ground.
- **Order by dependency.** Per-party perception, vocabulary for what each cost affords its bearer, per-party objective, planning, then learning. The break is between vocabulary and objective (Fig 3 :25-27). Refuse the universal total before minting the vector (Formula step 2); set the threshold after the party anchors are read (Fig 12 :87).
- **Question type:** union, not partition. Verdicts per domain, never averaged.

My census matches the primary's six domains, union reading and dependency order. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 12, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency chain. Different starting figures, same boundaries, so the agreement is weak but not circular inside the framework. It is still one evidence pool and therefore no independent witness.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22/:27 (per-slot residual and frame-internal quantities), Fig 2 :29/:40/:42 (non-updating anchor and independence), Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 12 :125/:130/:87 (two currencies and typed comparison).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: report a vector, keep anchors separate, unbundle the union, buy independence, pre-register a refuter.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete management decision: which prevention program to fund, and what one number to put in the board pack.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **typed bond** naming who bears each cost and under which obligation (Fig 12 :5); a vector of bare numbers loses it as easily as a total does;
- the **non-updating anchor test** (Fig 2 :29), because a board pack is generated from the same ledger it is meant to check;
- the **source-discriminating instrument** needed to ground a local rate (*The Hard Problem is About Source*), which the shorthand promises as an open gap but does not supply;
- the **union's unassignable shared fraction** (Fig 9 :57), because a budget decision pressures the manager to allocate overlap by convention, which Fig 10 :47 then promotes into a verdict.

One thing survives cleanly: the residual is per party, and summing across parties discards the only signal that says where to look. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
