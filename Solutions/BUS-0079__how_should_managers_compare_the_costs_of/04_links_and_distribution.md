## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the question

1. **The PAF total is the ordinary term under audit.** The quality.org page and the 2013 and 2008 quality-cost papers describe the same move: add prevention, appraisal, internal failure and external failure into one number and seek an optimum. The figures do not permit that move across parties because the units and anchors differ (F1) and the classes form a union with an unassignable shared fraction (F4). The literature's own admission that hidden external costs "remain uncalculated" is corroboration that the party-bearing remainder is missing from the total, not that the total should be widened until it contains everything.
2. **The cleanest low-overlap corroborator is decision science, not management accounting.** Walasek and Brown 2024 find no universal value scale and show that trade-offs between attributes tied to different covering values are not meaningful. That is the same structural verdict as Fig 12 :130, reached from human cognition and without the framework. The management accounting sources share the PAF evidence pool with each other and count as one witness, not several.
