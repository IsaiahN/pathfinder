## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the manager's question: how to compare the costs of quality failures, safety incidents, delays and prevention when those costs fall on different parties. Option A: produce one total with a common conversion rate, the ordinary cost-of-quality move. Option B: keep the verdicts per party, report a vector in separate currencies against separate anchors, leave overlap unassigned and declare the universal rate a gap. What each leads to: A decides a budget with a frame-internal number that cannot see the party-bearing costs, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a checkable shape, a typed comparison procedure, written refuters and an open slot until a local ground settles a rate.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Each party holds a cost reading in its own currency at its own anchor; the manager's accounting frame holds one declared total produced from its own report. Arity: four cost classes crossed with at least five parties, so a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55-57), not one cost to minimise. Symmetry: "compare" proposes a symmetric additive relation, while the relation that holds across parties is an ordered threshold comparison, so swapping who bears a cost changes the term (Fig 12 :87, Operators table :48-52). Scale: per party and per slot (Fig 1 :19), with contact cascading three steps out (Fig 11 :52-54).
- **Domain 1, firm prevention and appraisal.** Actors: managers, quality engineers, budget owners. Currency: money, tests, training hours, defect counts. Anchor: a test result, a specification, a warranty claim, a returned unit, a fine. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 2, customer quality failure.** Actors: customers and field users. Currency: performance, returns, lost time, harm. Anchor: the customer's actual return, complaint, field failure or injury. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 3, worker safety.** Actors: workers, unions, regulators, families. Currency: injuries, near misses, lost time, lives, capacity to succeed. Anchor: an actual injury, an inspection, an epidemiological reading, work-as-done. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 4, supplier and downstream delay.** Actors: suppliers, logistics, downstream production. Currency: time, schedule adherence, throughput, delay cost per unit time. Anchor: a delivery date, a stopped line, a contract penalty, the local resource's remaining time. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 5, society and regulator.** Actors: regulators, communities, insurers, taxpayers. Currency: compliance, social cost, reputation, externalities. Anchor: a law, a fine, a published statistic, an inspection. Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 6, the accounting and decision frame.** Actors: the manager, accountants, analysts, the cost-of-quality report. Currency: a declared common measure. Anchor: none of its own; it borrows D1 to D5. Its own report is not an anchor (Fig 1 :27, Fig 10 :18). A frame with no nameable anchor is not a sixth ground.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception at each party, then vocabulary for what each cost affords its bearer, then a per-party objective, then planning, then learn and carry. The break is between vocabulary and objective where the money vocabulary is rich and the others are absent (Fig 3 :25-27).
- **Question type:** union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-57). Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.

Strain test applied: Fig 12's "two currencies" are bits and actions in its own context; extending the no-add rule to money and harm is an analogue, so the business application is `specified` and single-source on the extension while the no-add rule itself is triangulated with Fig 1. The live type strain on the operand of ⋛ across money and harm is named, not smoothed (see `06` Q3 and `11`).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single total does not. The residual must stay indexed per party and per slot, in separate currencies against separate non-updating anchors. The four cost classes are a union with an unassignable shared fraction, so one lever leaves the intersections standing. The manager's own report is frame-internal and cannot score the frame that produces it; an allocation that decides a budget has stopped being a convention. Prevention is a reapplicable method, a failure replay is one occasion, and the comparison is a typed threshold relation with a subtraction for progress rather than a sum. No figure line names a universal conversion rate or a dollar value of an injury, so those are declared gaps of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as verdicts and not as silence. Mode: `specified` on the separators, `general` on the shape, ungrounded and candidate. The live type strain is the operand of ⋛ across money and harm.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a total instead of a vector, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong"). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters. A second cost, if the shape is wrong and a universal rate exists, is prolonged under-measurement of party-bearing harms because we told managers to keep them apart. That is why the local-rate gap is owed an instrument, not just a disclaimer.
- **undoable:** yes. A vector report can be superseded by a corrected one without withdrawing any held term; no primitive is lost. If a source-discriminating instrument grounds a local rate, it can be added with its scope and anchor declared. If a figure re-census admits ⋛ over money and harm at the same level, the type strain clears. If the four classes prove to partition, the union verdict is replaced. Each reversal is a promotion or a refusal in the record, not a silent rewrite, and `01` section 6 keeps the retracted readings visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at a specific firm showing whether one total returns the same number for a fatality and a warranty claim would ground or refuse a local rate; a measurement showing two currencies are one at the same anchor would break the no-add verdict; a demonstration that all party readings come from one instrument and still triangulate at maximum independence would empty the independence requirement; a prevention spend shown to be a replay rather than a method would collapse the generator distinction; a partition with assignable shared fraction would break the union verdict; an existing per-party progress instrument would collapse the broken-link diagnosis. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
