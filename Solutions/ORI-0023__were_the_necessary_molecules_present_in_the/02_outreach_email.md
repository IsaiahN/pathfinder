**Subject:** One output to check on prebiotic co-location, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "were the necessary molecules present in the same place at the same time?" is not one yes/no fact. It is a union of at least four conditions with different grounds: supply of each molecular class; spatial co-location; temporal coincidence; and concentration at reacting level. A lever that establishes supply leaves the co-location intersection standing. So the right unit of answer is four verdicts, not one.
>
> Two further constraints follow. "Same place" and "same time" are different quantities, adjacency is not duration, so they cannot be merged into one co-presence flag. And an ingredient list is not a chemical system: a recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself. The arrangement, which is where the process, surface or cycle sits, is the other half.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not saying the molecules were definitely co-located, and I am not saying they were not. I am not naming which molecules are necessary, because that is relative to which first step you are explaining. On the available record I cannot give you a dated locale with all required classes present at reacting concentration. That is a `gap`, an unreached result, not a "no". A search that finds nothing is not proof of absence.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. showing that adjacency and duration are one quantity under the six-term set;
> 2. showing that co-presence is a primitive, with no structure or process in the slot;
> 3. a dated measurement showing all required classes co-present in one locale and window, which would move the empirical verdict to grounded yes;
> 4. a discriminator showing one required class absent from every candidate locale, which would move it to grounded no;
> 5. evidence that the supposedly independent supply streams all draw on one dated source event, which would collapse the union into one condition.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
