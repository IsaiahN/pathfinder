# The Tether's answer on ORI-0023

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the empirical yes/no. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a Hadean result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether the molecules necessary for life's origin were present in the same place at the same time. In effect terms it asks for a locale L and a window W with all required classes at reacting concentration. The residual is that no such certificate is available and that the question as posed bundles four conditions with different anchors into one yes/no. Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The union is supply, co-location, coincidence and concentration, and it has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Four verdicts, not one.** Supply of each required class, spatial co-location, temporal coincidence, and concentration at reacting level are overlapping conditions with separate anchors. A lever that establishes one leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

2. **Same place and same time are different quantities.** Fig 13 :36: *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction"* Merging them into one co-presence flag would erase information and make one of them redundant (Fig 13 :36-37).

3. **Co-presence is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* A process, cycle, gradient or surface must sit in the slot. The laboratory record supplies candidates: evaporite flow and stream confluence (Patel et al. 2015), deliquescent moisture regulation (Campbell et al. 2019), surface hydrothermal pools (Rimmer and Shorttle 2019). The figures license a structure; they do not name one.

4. **An ingredient list is not a chemical system.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :47: *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself"* The arrangement is the other half. The question's real subject is the bonded arrangement, not the directory.

5. **One-pot and flow are isomers.** Over an overlapping molecular set they differ in arrangement and therefore in substance (Fig 12 :117). The functional-group reading (Fig 12 :123) is the fallback if the isomer claim fails. A falsifier is stated in `06`.

6. **The empirical answer is a `gap`, unreached at this budget.** The Hadean record cannot resolve a local locale and window. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* This is `gap`, not "no", and not an invitation to invent a date.

7. **The necessary set is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Which molecules are necessary depends on which first step is being explained, so the set must be re-enumerated for each proposal.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a dated locale, a named environment, or a yes. The laboratory co-production result is a shape for a locale, not a Hadean measurement.
- Not "no". Absence of a dated find is not proof of absence.
- Not a single co-presence flag. Adjacency and duration stay separate.
- Not a standing list of necessary molecules.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at a Hadean locale settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not a claim that the framework names the process in the slot. Flow chemistry, deliquescence and wet-dry cycling are provenanced imports, candidates with a source gate and a shadow test.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer resolved the colour case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Locale certificate:** is there a dated, single locale in which the required classes are spatially associated at the grain scale, with mineral and isotopic evidence that they were contemporary? That moves the verdict to `grounded` yes.
- **Discriminator:** is there evidence that a required activating agent was absent from every candidate locale, or that the streams now thought independent all draw on one source event? That moves the verdict to `grounded` no or collapses the union.
- **Concentration discriminator:** can the measured concentrations support the next step at the relevant temperature and pH, or does the dilute-ocean problem bite? Fig 11 :46: *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"* The molecular inventory alone is incomplete.
- **Instrument refinement (INWARD):** improve isotope and micro-scale spatial discrimination from readings already returning something (Fig 6 :32), rather than specifying a sensor for something that never registered (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **Standing figure falsifiers:** treat adjacency and duration as one quantity; admit a direct state-state bond; show a held atom list independent of the residual.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The six-term set and the habitat list are a visible guess at the compression axes (Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of a wrong yes about the Hadean is high, so independent checking is worth buying.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to "were the necessary molecules present in the same place at the same time?", because that question bundles four conditions with different grounds. Supply, spatial co-location, temporal coincidence and concentration must be settled separately; adjacency is not duration, so "same place" and "same time" are not one flag. Co-presence is not a primitive either: a process or surface must sit between space and time, and an ingredient list is not a chemical system because the arrangement is the other half of the information. One-pot and flow chemistry are isomers over a shared molecular set, with a stated falsifier. The laboratory record shows that co-production in one locale is chemically reachable as a shape; the Hadean rock record cannot show that it actually happened there. That is reported as an honest `gap`, with the instrument that would close it named, not as a "no" and not as a "yes". What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and a stop recorded so it can be checked.
