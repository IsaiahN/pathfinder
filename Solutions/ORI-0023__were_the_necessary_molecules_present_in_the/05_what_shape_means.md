## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the shape is therefore not a date, not a place name and not a list of molecules. It is a constraint set that any acceptable answer must satisfy: a union of four conditions; adjacency distinct from duration; a structure in the slot between space and time; the arrangement counted as part of the system; and a locale-and-window certificate as the anchor. The shape narrows what would count as an answer without supplying the answer.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: "co-location" is a borrowed term; the closer terms are adjacency, coincidence, concentration, flow, confluence and interface. Each narrows the search.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So we describe the effect (all classes at reacting concentration in one window) and then look for the frame whose closure predicts it, rather than picking sedimentology first.
- From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: a green-reading instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Applied here: a measurement showing molecules in one rock does not tell you which supply stream put them there, or whether they were contemporary. The spectrometer resolved the colour case; here a source-discriminating instrument is owed.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Applied here: the search stops when the locale-and-window certificate is in hand or when the instrument limit is stated honestly.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A dated locale with the required classes co-present at reacting concentration would settle the yes; a discriminator showing one class always absent would settle the no.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52) with a source gate and a shadow test. A laboratory flow chemistry result is a candidate import: it explains how co-production could happen but it does not by itself date a Hadean locale.

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* So a laboratory plausibility argument cannot validate the Hadean claim.

## 4. Why a single co-presence flag is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: metamerism. One reading, several possible sources, and the reading does not determine the source. Here, a positive detection of, say, amino acids and nucleobases in one formation is a projection with lossy information about whether they arrived together, when they arrived and whether they were concentrated enough to react. Several histories produce the same rock reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a story.

Fig 13 :36 governs the same point at the figure level: *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."* Collapsing space and time into one flag is a source-erasing move of the same kind.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the conditions and the structures?

**No, and here is the correction to my own first reading.** The figures README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. The conditions and structures named in this folder (flow chemistry, deliquescence, wet-dry cycling, mineral surfaces, the union split) are bindings from the chemistry and geology frames onto arrangements already expressible in the framework. Naming them is provenance work, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What would be forbidden is inventing a new substrate member or claiming the binding moves the closure wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*, and this one adds none).

I initially overstated the rule in `01` section 7's first draft by writing as if naming any filling were forbidden. Corrected and kept visible. The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: the discriminator is still missing, and that is the reason we cannot name the Hadean source, not a ban on naming held atoms.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A consensus of well known origin-of-life researchers agreeing that the ingredients were co-located is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a measurement that could come back either way.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, verbatim: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the Hadean claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Stopping rule for this question

The Library of Babel searcher stops at a general shape, not at a final book. Here the stop is: four conditions with separate verdicts, adjacency held distinct from duration, a structure in the slot, the arrangement counted, and the empirical result reported as `gap` with the instrument that would close it named. Anything beyond that (a named locale, a definite yes) is a name where the framework returns a shape, and is owed an import with provenance and a shadow test.
