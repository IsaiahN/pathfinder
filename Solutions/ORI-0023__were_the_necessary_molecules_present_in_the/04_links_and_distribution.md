## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **One-pot versus flow.** The Miller-Urey, Butlerow formose and Oró syntheses are compared inside the Patel paper. Patel et al. report that their own network cannot run in a single pot because of periodic reagent delivery, and move to a geochemical flow scheme. This is the isomer claim in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- **Concentration as a separate condition.** Campbell et al. and Longo and Damer both treat overdilution as a distinct obstacle, not a footnote to synthesis. That is the fourth condition of the union.
- **Dispute about the setting.** Russell (2021) contests the existence of the evaporating-pond setting; Rimmer and Shorttle (2019) argue for surface hydrothermal vents; Longo and Damer (2020) keep vents and hot springs as separate hypotheses with separate falsification conditions. The disagreement is evidence that the co-location condition has no settled ground, which is exactly the `gap` verdict. It is not a reason to pick a side.
- **The record limit.** Pizzarello and Shock (2010) and Rimmer and Shorttle (2019) independently state that the Hadean geological record cannot resolve local environments. This is the instrument limit, not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
