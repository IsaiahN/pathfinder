## The residual in effect terms

The question asks whether, on early Earth, the set of molecular classes required for life's origin (informational building blocks, protein building blocks, membrane-forming building blocks, plus activating agents and an energy or catalysis source) had overlapping spatial support and overlapping temporal support at reacting concentration in some locale. It is a conjunction, not a single property. Deciding it requires naming the required set, establishing spatial co-location, establishing temporal coincidence, and establishing concentration at reaction rates. An answer would look like: there existed a locale L and a window W in which all required classes were present together at concentrations and conditions permitting the next step; or no such L and W existed; or the available instruments cannot resolve L and W. That last form is bound as an ordinary term in `01` and carried through the folder.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Seat Map :163, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and asking them to trust the framework before they check the output spends the independence they contribute.
