## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Question as posed: *"Were the necessary molecules present in the same place at the same time?"*

Stated as an effect, the question asks whether there existed a locale L and a time window W on early Earth such that the molecular classes required for the next step (informational building blocks, protein building blocks, membrane-forming building blocks, and the activating agents or energy source that make them react) overlapped in space and in time at concentrations high enough for chemistry to proceed.

Three things are hidden in the ordinary wording and must be made ordinary terms before the search, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction":

1. **"Necessary molecules"** is not a standing list. It is relative to the residual, that is, relative to whatever next step is being explained (Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*). A different proposed first step changes the required set.
2. **"Same place"** is spatial adjacency. **"Same time"** is duration and ordering. These are different primitives (Fig 13 :36: *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction"*). They cannot be merged into one co-presence flag without losing information.
3. **"Present"** is ambiguous between trace detection and reacting concentration. The concentration condition is the one that makes the difference between a directory of molecules and a chemical system (Fig 12 :5, :47).

What an answer would look like, bound now: either a locale-and-window certificate with all required classes co-present at reacting concentration (a yes), or a discriminator showing that at least one required class was absent from every candidate locale or window (a no), or an honest report that the available instruments cannot resolve L and W at all (a `gap`, unreached rather than silence). The third form is a legitimate outcome (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).

R by arity, symmetry and scale:
- **Arity:** four or more conditions (supply of each class, spatial co-location, temporal coincidence, concentration), not two.
- **Symmetry:** the operands are not interchangeable. Adjacency and duration are distinct, and swapping them changes the claim.
- **Scale:** local. The question is about a locale and a window, not a global inventory. Global inventories answer a different question and are a category error here.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The question is a union of four conditions (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 9 :55-56 and :57 govern. Supply of each molecular class, spatial co-location, temporal coincidence, and concentration are overlapping conditions with different anchors (D1 laboratory, D2 rock record, D3 meteorites, an instrument for concentration). A lever that clears one leaves the intersections standing. The correct unit of answer is four verdicts, not one. The world literature behaves as if it were one question, which is why a paper can claim the ingredients "could have arisen at the same place and time" (a D1 result) while a geologist argues the required ingredients "simply weren't available" (a D2 result). Both can be right about their own condition. Triangulated: Fig 9 (union structure) plus Fig 2 :29 (distinct anchors) plus Fig 13 :35 (distinct primitives).

### F2: "Same place" and "same time" are not one condition (triangulated)

Fig 13 :36 and :36-37 govern. Adjacency is not duration. Treating co-location and coincidence as a single co-presence flag would make one of them redundant, which the figure forbids for two members that draw on different facts. A locale can satisfy one without the other: molecules delivered to one pool at different times, or delivered simultaneously to different pools. The question requires both, and each needs its own discriminator. Triangulated: Fig 13 and the world supply/delivery literature, which separates exogenous delivery (D3) from local concentration (D1).

### F3: Co-presence is not a primitive; a structure must sit in the slot (single-source on the figure, triangulated with the laboratory record)

Fig 13 :75 governs. Two states never bond directly. Space and time compose only through a structure. So "present in the same place at the same time" is not a term the six-term set can supply directly; it requires a process, cycle, gradient or surface in the slot. The laboratory record supplies exactly such structures: wet-dry cycling, evaporite flow and confluence of streams (Patel et al. 2015), deliquescent minerals regulating water activity (Campbell et al. 2019), surface hydrothermal vents providing a local pool with wet-dry edges (Rimmer and Shorttle 2019). This is the figure's shape, and the world sources instantiate it. It remains `specified`: the figures license a structure in the slot, not a name for it.

### F4: An ingredient list is not a chemical system (triangulated)

Fig 12 :5, :47 and :111 govern. Knowing that amino acids, sugars, nucleobases and lipids were each produced somewhere does not tell you they were in one place with the bonds and activation needed to react. "A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself." The missing half is the arrangement. The lab literature's own phrasing confirms it: Patel et al. describe the products being "produced and then blended together" in pools, and describe the spatial arrangement (rivulets, streams, confluences, pools) as part of the result, not as scenery. Reading only the molecule list and dropping the arrangement is the exact failure Fig 12 names.

### F5: The one-pot and flow readings are isomers over the same set (single-source on the figure, with a falsifier)

Fig 12 :117 governs. The Miller-Urey, formose and Oró syntheses are "one pot" constructions; Patel et al. report that their own network needs "periodic delivery" of HCN and H2S and sequential delivery of other reagents, so they move to a flow scheme with streams merging and products blending in pools. These share the molecular set but differ in arrangement and therefore in substance and in what they predict about co-location. They are isomers, not a milder and a stronger version of the same thing. Falsifier: if one of the two can be written without a molecule the other requires, the isomer claim fails and the weaker functional-group reading (Fig 12 :123) stands instead. This is worked out in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

### F6: The empirical "were they" is unreachable at this budget, reported as a gap (triangulated)

Fig 8 :39 and :40-41, Fig 13 :134. The Hadean rock record is largely destroyed. Pizzarello and Shock (2010): *"geological and biological processes of over four billion years have long eradicated any traces of early Earth's chemistry."* Rimmer and Shorttle (2019): *"This deficit of geological record over the key time period in Earth history poses a difficulty for validating the surface hydrothermal vent scenario."* Longo and Damer (2020): the identity of the environment where abiogenesis took place "remains a topic of debate." No dated locale certificate showing all classes co-present at reacting concentration is available. Absence of such a certificate is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39), and the limit is a budget and instrument limit, not a theorem (Fig 8 :40-41). So the empirical verdict is `gap`, unreached, not silence and not "no".

### F7: The system's drive manufactures a gradient, so plausibility is not evidence (single-source)

Fig 5 :47: *"When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient."* The origins field has a strong preference for a co-located answer, because a scattered answer does not lead anywhere. That preference is a shape held as a preference, not a term with a residual, so `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 cannot see it. Committing the discriminator before the run (section 5) is the discipline that makes it refusable.

### F8: The anchor is a measurement or a discriminating experiment, not agreement (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29, Fig 2 :35, Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). A consensus that the ingredients were co-located is a room reaching a conclusion without the ground. Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label... Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* The legitimate ask is for a measurement that could come back either way, not for endorsement.

### F9: The habitat must be enumerated, and it includes non-molecular conditions (triangulated)

Fig 11 :43, :46, :48. The list includes light, water, temperature, season and terrain, plus actors and relations. A molecular inventory alone is incomplete by construction. ## 4. The three route questions, kept distinct

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7: *"the set of kinds is closed; the arrangements over it are not."*

1. **SEARCHED, is it in here and unfound?** Has the rock record been swept at the resolution a locale certificate needs? Yes in part: zircons, vent precipitates and hot-spring sinters have been examined, and isotopic and morphological readings are already returning something that fails to resolve co-location (Dodd et al. 2017; Djokic et al. 2017, both read via the review sources). This is asked first because it is cheapest and both other routes assume its answer. More search at the same resolution adds no atom (Fig 6 :49-50).
2. **INWARD, is the representation adequate to hold it?** Improve the instrument from one already returning something (Fig 6 :32): source-discriminating isotope work on single mineral grains, micro-scale spatial association of organic and mineral phases, better dating of the smallest habitable niches. This sharpens in our own terms and does not cross into the lab frame.
3. **OUTWARD, IMPORT.** Characterise the residual in effect terms (a locale-and-window certificate) and find the frame whose closure predicts its shape: laboratory systems chemistry, meteoritics, planetary science. With a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does the imported scenario explain a gap in the rock record?). Debited against independence (Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*).

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and never read against one another. Importing a photochemical scenario against a rock-record budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim (union, distinct space and time, structure in the slot, ingredient list incomplete, empirical gap) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure re-census in which adjacency and duration are one quantity under the six-term set (breaks Fig 13 :36).
2. A composition of space and time with no intervening structure is admitted by the framework and cannot be refused (breaks Fig 13 :75).
3. A dated locale measurement shows all required classes co-present at reacting concentration in a single window. This moves the empirical verdict from `gap` to `grounded` yes and is a successful refutation of the gap claim.
4. A discriminator shows that at least one required class was absent from every candidate locale or window (for example, an activating agent demonstrably absent wherever nucleobases accumulated). This moves the verdict to `grounded` no.
5. Evidence that the supposed independent supply streams all draw on one dated source event collapses the union into one condition (breaks Fig 9 :55 and Fig 2 :31).

If any fires, the affected claim is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded here, not silently erased. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
