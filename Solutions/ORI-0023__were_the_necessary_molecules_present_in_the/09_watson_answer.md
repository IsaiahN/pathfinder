## VERDICT (from the figures)

The single-flag question is refused and replaced by a four-condition union with separate verdicts. Adjacency and duration are distinct quantities, co-presence is not a primitive, an ingredient list is not a chemical system, and one-pot versus flow is an isomer distinction with a stated falsifier. The laboratory record gives a `specified` shape for co-production in one locale; the Hadean record cannot supply a dated locale-and-window certificate, so the empirical verdict is `gap`, unreached at this budget, not no and not yes. Mode `specified` on the shape and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the union structure, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the four-way split and the refuters are sent with the claim. The real cost is importing a laboratory shape and calling it a Hadean fact, which is an import with no source gate and a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24). The opposite cost is treating a failed search as a no (Fig 8 :39). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter, and by buying independence in proportion to the cost (Fig 2 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. Each domain verdict is separate and revisable with a new instrument or a dated locale; no substrate primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A dated locale certificate with all required classes spatially associated, contemporary and concentrated would move the verdict to `grounded` yes; a discriminator showing one required class absent from every candidate locale, or all streams traced to one source event, would move it to no or collapse the union; a figure re-census that makes adjacency and duration one quantity, or admits a direct state-state bond, would break the shape claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
