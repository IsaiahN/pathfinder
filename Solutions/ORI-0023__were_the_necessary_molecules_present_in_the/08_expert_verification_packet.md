## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "were the necessary molecules present in the same place at the same time?" is a union of four conditions with different anchors: supply of each required class, spatial co-location, temporal coincidence, and concentration at reacting level. The right unit of answer is four verdicts, not one yes/no. Spatial adjacency and temporal duration are distinct quantities, so they cannot be collapsed into one co-presence flag. Co-presence itself is not a primitive, because a structure or process must sit between space and time; and an ingredient list is not a chemical system, because the arrangement carries the information the list omits. One-pot and flow accounts of co-production are isomers over a shared molecular set, with a stated falsifier. Laboratory work shows co-production in one locale is chemically reachable as a shape; the Hadean rock record cannot show it happened, so the empirical verdict is an honest `gap` with the discriminating instrument named.
