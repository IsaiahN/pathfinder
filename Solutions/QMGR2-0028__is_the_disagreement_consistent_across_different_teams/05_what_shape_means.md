## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README (local) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the three-part constraint: establish independence, test persistence, let the ground decide. It narrows what any answer must look like. It does not fill the slot with "new physics" or "shared systematics." Those are names, and the framework returns a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the existing vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Stopping is recognisable because "the finish line will also be the starting place."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the residual was stated as *shared evidence*, not as *Cepheid crowding* or *early dark energy*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The outreach email in `02` therefore asks what overlap a reader would need ruled out, rather than asking them to accept a diagnosis.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "This process isn't about reaching the truth. It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it." The shape stops where the instruments stop distinguishing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." A spectrometer, not a better argument, discriminated the sources of green. Here the analogue is a new independent probe, not another review.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mechanism relevant here: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." A 5-sigma H0 value is a projection. It does not comment on the source, which could be new physics, a coherent systematic, or correlated reuse of data. Naming the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. Independence is the framework's own version of source discrimination

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes the bridge explicit: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are 'one frame wearing two names' (Fig 8 :48-49)" and "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources (Fig 2 :40-41)." It also notes the three drift-stoppers: "A ground that both are corrected by, separately", "Separate experience", and "Claims in effect terms."

That is exactly the shape delivered in `07_the_tether_answer.md`. The essay corroborates the figure verdict but is not an independent witness: it is by the framework's author and shares the figure evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. The declamation that is refused

*They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: declaring "teams" or "pipelines" as independent does not make them independent. It makes the choice visible so it can be audited. A covariance matrix is the instrument that turns the visible choice into a checkable one.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A fold-in of famous names agreeing that the tension is real would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an instrument or a public checkable record, settle it.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the Hubble tension and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The validator, not this derivation, is what would settle it.
