| Hubble Tension Survey (arXiv abstract) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18443 | Wang, Lopez-Corredoira and Wei, submitted 2023-11-30, accepted in MNRAS. 216 H0 measurements from 2012 to 2022. | Same paper, full arXiv text | https://arxiv.org/html/2408.11031v4 | v4, 2024-09-02. States the core lies "not between early and late-time measurements, but between distance ladder measurements and all other H0 determinations." |
