# Outreach draft: check the residual before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. The field is analysis pipeline comparison research.

**Hard precondition:** the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 must be written down and dated before this is sent. If the recipient cannot be shown the refuter first, the two-stage offer collapses on contact (Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").

**Subject:** One check on whether the H0 disagreement survives an independence test

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow output I would like you to check against what you already know about H0 measurement pipelines. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If this output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` structural shape, `specified` on the cosmology reading, not `grounded`):** the question "is the H0 disagreement consistent across teams and pipelines?" is, in effect, an independence question before it is a persistence question. Two pipelines that share data, anchors, samples, photometry, or systematics are one evidence pool wearing two names. Their agreement, and their disagreement, is the pool talking, not two independent witnesses. Only after independence is established does it matter whether the residual persists and does not trend down. That persistence is what would owe a term, either a coherent systematic or new physics, and only a measurement settles which.
>
> What I am claiming is therefore a shape with three ordered parts: establish independence, test persistence, let the ground decide. I am *not* claiming that the tension is real, that it is an artifact, that new physics is owed, or that any particular team is at fault. I am not asking you to trust a framework. A "this fails because..." line is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What would refute the shape (written before this email):**
> 1. Two pipelines with demonstrably disjoint evidence are shown to have their values set by mutual updating or shared influence rather than shared evidence, so the diagnosis is coherent drift, not common cause.
> 2. A disagreement that persists and does not trend down across independent frames is fully explained by an already-held term, or a disagreement trends monotonically to zero with added data, making it a budget artifact.
> 3. A new, mutually independent measurement lands on one side and makes the residual vanish or flip.
>
> **One question for you, stage 1 only:** what overlap would you need to see ruled out before you would call two H0 pipelines independent? A covariance matrix, a shared-anchor audit, a sample-selection sensitivity test, something else?

**No personal emails, no receptiveness ranking, no bulk send.** The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public and opt-in. Correlation is a disqualifier for a verdict (Seat Map :163), so selecting recipients for likely agreement would manufacture the very thing that makes their answer worthless.
