**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

If a covariance audit shows disjoint anchors, software, hosts, and systematics, the reading flips to two frames and the independence verdict changes. The shorthand does not carry that discrimination.
- **The operand type of ⋛ at the meta scale.** Comparing two H0 numbers is well typed; comparing two whole pipelines is not, until a covariance definition makes a pipeline count as a quantity at that level.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: independence before persistence, and the ban on naming what is owed without a ground reading. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no new ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the checklist as identical to the figure law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
