# Expert verification packet: the Tether on H0 consistency across pipelines

**What this is:** a claim in a form an analyst of cosmological measurements can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the cosmology reading, not `grounded`.** No new measurement is claimed.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is the H0 disagreement consistent across different teams and analysis pipelines?" is an independence question before it is a persistence question. Two pipelines that share evidence are one frame wearing two names, so their agreement and their disagreement are the pool talking, not two witnesses. After independence is established, a residual that persists and does not trend down is owed a term, and a ground reading settles which term. The current teams look like isomers over a shared ladder, same atoms arranged differently, so their different H0 values do not by themselves establish independent disagreement. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 8

- **Statement:** independence between frames is the precondition for reading agreement or disagreement as evidence; a shared evidence pool collapses separate team labels into one frame.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Also Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." And Fig 2 :18-21: "they never talk, and still agree" and "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other."
- **Refuter:** exhibit two pipelines with demonstrably disjoint evidence whose values are nevertheless set by mutual updating or shared influence (Fig 2 :13-15, "no anchor; each updates on the others"), so the diagnosis is coherent drift rather than common cause; or show the independence condition cannot be operationalized for any real pair of analyses.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Formula step 2 and Figure 9

- **Statement:** a residual that persists and does not trend down across independent frames is owed a term, but the compound must be split before a verdict. Five sigma is a filter for effort, not a witness for new physics.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Formula step 2 (local `THE_FORMULA.md`) ranks by "the residual that PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN."
- **Refuter:** show a persistent residual that is fully explained by an already-held term so nothing is owed; or show a disagreement trending monotonically to zero with added data, so it was a budget artifact; or show all compound conditions reduce to one overlap parameter so no unbundling is owed.

### C3 - physics-facing; checkable by an analyst against the published record

- **Statement:** SH0ES and CCHP are isomers over a shared atom set, not two independent witnesses by virtue of being different teams. Their different H0 values can arise from different arrangements (selection, indicator, filter, statistic) of one ladder.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." (The last is the condition that would make them independent; the claimed overlap is the reason it is not met.)
- **What to check:** do the two teams actually share anchor (NGC 4258), software (DOLPHOT, stated by Riess et al. 2024), host galaxies, and SN samples? Is the ~2.5 km/s/Mpc H0 difference between CCHP JWST and the full HST sample attributable to selection, as that paper reports? If yes, the isomer reading stands. If the teams use disjoint anchors, disjoint software, disjoint hosts, and disjoint systematics, the isomer reading fails and they may be two frames.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate disjoint atom sets for the two pipelines, or show that the H0 differences remain after selection, anchor, and covariance differences are removed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the Hubble tension is real, an artifact, or new physics. Those are names; the framework returns a shape.
- Not that any team is wrong or at fault. Two arrangements of one ladder can each be internally faithful.
- Not that adding more agreeing reviews adds evidence (Fig 7 :12-13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not").
- Not a measurement, not a derivation of H0, and not `grounded`.
- Not that independence has been established for any real pair of pipelines. A covariance audit is owed.
- Not that ⋛ pools the two H0 values. It compares them (Operators table: "a threshold relation between two quantities").

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Overlap test:** a published covariance matrix and shared-anchor audit giving an effective overlap rho for a pair of pipelines. High rho means one frame under two names; low rho means corroboration is possible.
2. **Selection test:** a pre-specified, blinded reanalysis varying sample selection independently, separating arrangement effects from measurement effects.
3. **New frame:** an independent probe (standard sirens, strong lenses, cosmic chronometers, Gaia DR4) ranging against a ground neither team can move.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 3:** mutual updating instead of common cause; a persistent residual already fully explained; a ground reading that makes the residual vanish or flip.

2. Check C3 against your own knowledge: do SH0ES and CCHP share anchor, software, hosts, and SN samples? Is the H0 difference largely selection? A one-line yes or no with a source is enough.
