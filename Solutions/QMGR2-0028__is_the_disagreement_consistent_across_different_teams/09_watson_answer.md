## QUESTION

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The framework returns a shape, not a yes or no. Independence is the gate; persistence is the test; the ground decides what is owed. Team and pipeline labels do not by themselves establish independent frames. Pipelines sharing anchors, software, samples, or systematics are one evidence pool wearing two names, so neither their agreement nor their disagreement is evidence of a real tension. Only after disjoint closures are demonstrated does a residual that persists and does not trend down owe a term. The current teams are isomers over a shared ladder, same atoms arranged differently, which explains how different H0 values can exist without two independent witnesses. This is a `general` structural shape with a `specified` reading of the current record, ungrounded. No name (new physics or shared systematic) is asserted.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters, and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is treating correlated pipelines as independent witnesses and importing a wrong atom, priced at Fig 6 :24 as "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." A second cost is dismissing a real residual as an artifact because a shared-pool diagnosis was applied without a covariance audit. Both are avoidable by leading with the independence test and keeping every cut reversible (Fig 9 :41).
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is structural and survives correction of any particular slot reading. A covariance audit can change a verdict from "one pool" to "two frames" without withdrawing the shape. No primitive is withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A published covariance audit giving low effective overlap for two pipelines would change the C3 verdict to two frames; a persistent residual across those frames that no already-held term explains would owe a mint; a mutual-updating demonstration would change the diagnosis from common cause to coherent drift; and a new independent probe that makes the residual vanish or flip would confirm it was a budget artifact. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
