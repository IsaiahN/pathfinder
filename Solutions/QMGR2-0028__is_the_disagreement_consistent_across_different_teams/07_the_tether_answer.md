# The Tether's answer: is the disagreement consistent across teams and pipelines?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the cosmology reading, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The early-universe and nearby-universe routes to H0 differ by about 5 sigma. The question asks whether that difference survives repetition by different teams and analysis pipelines. In effect terms this is not one question. It is a union compound: a route difference must exist and a cross-route comparison must be made, and the comparison is contaminated by shared anchors, software, samples, and coherent systematics (Fig 9 :4-5, :54). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Independence comes first.** Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." A "different team" is not a different closure. Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Their agreement *and* their disagreement are the pool talking, not two witnesses (Fig 2 :18-21).
2. **Then persistence.** Formula step 2 ranks by "the residual that PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN." A disagreement is *consistent across independent frames* when it persists and does not trend down as more data and more independent routes arrive. That is the mark of a term owed, not a verdict about what the term is. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Five sigma is a filter for effort.
3. **Then the ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A consensus of teams is a convention if it is produced by influence or shared priors (Seat Map :163, local). The anchor is a measurement that no team can move.
4. **Split before you search.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Shared-anchor, shared-software, shared-sample, and coherent-systematic conditions each need their own cut, kept ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41).
5. **Two routes sharing atoms are isomers, not necessarily independent frames.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." SH0ES and CCHP overlap heavily in instruments, anchor, hosts, and software, and differ in arrangement. Different H0 values can therefore arise from different arrangements of one ladder rather than from two independent witnesses. The two values are compared with ⋛, never pooled.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the tension is real" and not "the tension is an artifact." Both are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that any team is at fault, and not that SH0ES or CCHP is wrong. They are checkable arrangements of a shared ladder.
- Not that adding more teams helps. Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."
- Not a derivation of H0, not a new measurement, and not `grounded`. No instrument was read.
- Not a claim that independence has been established for any real pair of pipelines here. That needs a covariance audit, which is the handoff to the ground.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Overlap test:** a published covariance matrix and shared-anchor audit that yields an effective overlap rho for a pair of pipelines. If rho is high, their comparison is one frame wearing two names; if low, they can corroborate (Sandler 2026 reports rho about 0.84 inflating 5.32 sigma to 6.5 sigma in one compilation).
- **Selection test:** a pre-specified, blinded reanalysis in which sample selection is varied independently, so the part of the disagreement due to arrangement is separated from the part due to measurement (CCHP's blinding procedure and Riess et al. 2024's selection accounting are first steps).
- **New frame:** a decisive independent probe (standard sirens, strong lenses, cosmic chronometers, Gaia DR4) that ranges against a ground neither team can move.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** mutual updating rather than common cause; a persistent residual fully explained by an already-held term; a ground reading that makes the residual vanish or flip.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the instruments stop distinguishing. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." The lineage is recorded so a later reader can see what was assumed, what was refused, and what would reverse it.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "consistent" or "not consistent" in the lump. It says the question is an independence question before it is a persistence question. Two pipelines that share data, anchors, samples, or software are one evidence pool wearing two names, so their agreement and their disagreement are the pool talking, not two witnesses. Only after independence is established does it matter whether the residual persists and does not trend down, and that persistence is what would owe a term, either a coherent systematic or new physics. The current teams mostly look like isomers over a shared ladder: same atoms, different arrangement, different H0. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments, covariance audits, blinded selections, and independent probes that would close it. It returns a shape, not a name, and the ground decides which name is owed.
