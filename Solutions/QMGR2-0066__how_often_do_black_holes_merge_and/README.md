## Question restated as an effect

Two effects are bundled in the one sentence. (1) A frequency effect: count coalescences per unit comoving volume per unit time and say how that count changes with redshift. The options lead to a number with a credible interval, settled by detectors. (2) A source effect: use the distribution of those events (masses, spins, eccentricity, redshift trend) to say which formation route produced them. The options lead to a channel claim, settled only by a discriminator that separates routes. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a rate with units, plus a map from observables to channels with an error rate. The rate term is ground-owned. The map term is the residual the figures bear on.

## What is deliberately not here
