## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 says the system can report what it lacks and that "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened and verified): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name the instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `RATE(???)ROUTE` is a shape. "The dynamical channel produced GW241011" is a name.
- "A discriminator must change value across channels, already register somewhere, and range against a detector anchor that does not move" is a shape. "Use spin" is a binding from the astrophysics frame onto an observable that already exists.
- "The rate is symmetric; the route is ordered" is a shape for the relation between the two halves. "The rate is 23.9 per Gpc^-3 yr^-1" is a ground reading and is not a shape at all.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary closest to the thing and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the closest held vocabulary is "count", "distribution", "channel", "imprint", "lineage". Each term narrows the space.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So do not ask "which channel?" first. Ask "what would have to differ in the data for two channels to be distinguishable?"
- *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (opened and verified): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A new discriminator is an import of a new axis, not a new label on the same mass-spin plane. Existing instruments improved from worse instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question that happens when the shape of the answer and the shape of the question coincide: a rate that does not discriminate and a distribution that does.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The LVK rate is such a payment: a detector counted events and a population model paid for the interval. A channel attribution for a single event is not yet a payment, because the collaborations themselves write "could be" and "tantalizing evidence".
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A formation mechanism imported from population synthesis into the figure vocabulary is an import. It must say where it came from and which gap it explains.

Everything else is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The governing essay: source, not reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened and verified) gives the mechanism for this exact question. Metamerism: an instrument detects green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The essay's conclusion: "This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source."

Apply it: the merger rate is the reading. Isolated evolution, dynamical encounter, gas assisted capture, primordial origin and hierarchical remerger are the sources. A more precise reading does not by itself identify the source. What is owed is a discriminator, which is the gravitational-wave equivalent of the spectrometer. The essay adds that the answer was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the better instruments are spin-orbit alignment, eccentricity measurement, mass-gap occupancy and redshift-trend fitting, all of which already return something.

This is triangulated with Fig 13 :126 and :129 at low overlap: one source is a philosophical essay about measurement, the other is a structural claim about composed terms and lineage. Their agreement is one evidence pool in the sense that both come from the framework's author, so it is weak corroboration; the figure line governs, the essay illustrates.

## 5. The answer to the shape question for QMGR2-0066

**What shape must any answer to this question have?** Three constraints:

1. **Two-condition shape.** The answer must separate a ground-owned count from a source attribution. They are a union with overlap, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57). A single number cannot be the whole answer.
2. **Symmetry-order shape.** The count is symmetric under exchange of events; the route is ordered. Any term that claims to derive route from count must name the typed bond that carries the order, or it has recorded only ingredients (Fig 12 :5, :111).
3. **Lineage shape.** The route is not in the artefact. It is in the lineage that survived into the artefact, so the answer must state which observables carry lineage and what their misclassification rate is (Fig 13 :126, :129).

**Does the framework select a channel?** No. Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README's "a shape, not a name" refuse it. Selecting dynamical over isolated for a given event requires an instrument and a ground payment, not a derivation. The figure set returns the constraints a channel answer must satisfy.

## 6. Correction to my own first reading

My first pass wanted to write "the framework cannot name a formation channel, full stop". That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is about a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Channel names and observable names such as spin and eccentricity are bindings from the astrophysics frame onto things that already exist in our instruments. Naming them is allowed as provenance. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as something the figures derived, or as an import that moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here).

A second correction: I initially treated the metamerism point as if it said the source is unknowable. It does not. The essay says the answer is "unreached until a better instrument exists", and that the instrument arrived. So the correct mode for the route half is `specified`, with an instrument owed and partly in hand, not `gap` and not `unreachable`. The `gap` in this solution is narrower: no figure line yields the numerical rate (section 5 of `01`).

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened and verified): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous gravitational-wave physicists who agree with the shape claim would be a prestige loop, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35, :40). The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let a detector and a discriminator settle it, and record the refutation if one is found.
