Symmetry: the four measures are not invariant under the same transformations, so identity is a substantive claim that needs evidence rather than a default. Scale: each domain is a level, so cross-domain claims are cross-level transforms and inherit a loss term.
- **Domain 1, physics.** Actors: bit, channel, memory. Currency: entropy, erasure cost. Anchor: a measurement or an experiment. Closure: physical theory.

- **Domain 4, society.** Actors: individuals, prices, institutions. Currency: dispersed knowledge. Anchor: a market.
- **Partition or union.** Union. There are overlaps at signal/carrier and at aboutness, so a single lever leaves intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual, name anchors, refuse the universal Q before minting, settle each currency, then compare at the next level.
