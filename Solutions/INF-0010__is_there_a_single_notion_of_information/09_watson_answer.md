## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether one quantity is the currency of *information* in all four domains. Option A: yes, one notion applies coherently, so the four measures are commensurable and the cross-domain transform is lossless or owes a loss term. Option B: one loop applies, but the currency and anchor change in each domain and the transform is lossy. What each leads to: A commits us to a single undefined anchor for four different grounds and to an unmeasured invertible bridge; B returns a checkable shape with four refuters and an open slot until an instrument settles it. The sought-for shape was written down before the run and bound as an ordinary term (Formula "Direction"), so it can fail.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

`R` by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four conditions, a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5). Symmetry: the measures called information have different symmetries (permutation-symmetric entropy; symmetric mutual information; directional aboutness; directional and local price signals), so a single notion would have to explain why these asymmetries are decoration. Scale: each domain is a level with its own currency, so cross-domain claims are cross-level transforms with a round-trip gap.
- **Domain 1, physics.** Actors: bit, channel, memory, erasure event. Currency: entropy in bits, thermodynamic cost. Anchor: a measurement or an experiment (Fig 2 :29). Closure: physical theory.
- **Domain 2, biology.** Actors: organism, environment, Markov blanket, regulatory network. Currency: variational free energy, surprisal. Anchor: a maintained viable state / an assay outcome. Closure: life science.
- **Domain 3, cognition.** Actors: agent, generative model, sensory states. Currency: prediction error, model evidence, information gain. Anchor: an answer for a person (Fig 2 :37). Closure: mind and brain.
- **Domain 4, society.** Actors: individuals, prices, institutions. Currency: dispersed knowledge carried by prices. Anchor: a market (Fig 2 :29). Closure: social and economic order.
- **Partition or union.** Union. The domains overlap at the signal/carrier seam and the aboutness seam; a single lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). Unbundle before search.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the universal Q before minting (Formula step 2); name each anchor; settle each currency against its own anchor; only then compare at the next level (Fig 12 :104).
- **Strain test.** Two strains found and re-censused: the Fig 10 convention lines were narrowed to the cross-domain seam, and the Fig 13 "not three readings of one quantity" line was downgraded to an analogy. See `01` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B survives: one kernel, four currencies, four anchors, lossy transforms, no universal quantity named. The four-domain census has nameable anchors in every domain. A shared definition is a convention (Fig 2 :41), declaring one does not ground it (Fig 10 :49), Shannon coding is a composition for model selection (Fig 5 :45), and the cross-domain bridge has a gap that must be measured (Fig 4 :30). The stronger isomer reading over the six-term set is a falsifiable hypothesis, not a result: its bindings are provenanced imports with an unmeasured `R_T`. Mode `general` on the kernel, `specified` on the classification, not `grounded`. Candidate, not accepted.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a universal definition as a verdict when it is only a convention. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal-quantity claim can be withdrawn without touching the four-domain derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already do this. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A commensurability result settling one quantity in all four domains by the same instrument type; a measured `R_T = 0` between two domain currencies; a single lever shrinking all four residuals at once; a source-discriminating instrument showing four readings come from one source; or a re-census admitting a fifth domain with a different anchor. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
