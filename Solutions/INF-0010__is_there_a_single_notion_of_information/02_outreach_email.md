# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four refuters in `01` section 4 are intact and unchanged. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about a single notion of information

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it or to trust me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** The word *information* is used in four closures with four different currencies: entropy and erasure cost in physics, free energy and surprisal in biology, prediction error and model evidence in cognition, and dispersed knowledge carried by prices in society. The question is not whether the word is used consistently. It is whether one quantity is the currency in all four, with one kind of anchor settling it in each, or whether what is shared is only the loop.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** One kernel runs in all four domains, but the actors, currency and anchor change at each one, and the transform between domains is lossy. What can be returned is a shape: one loop, four currencies, four anchors, a measurable round-trip gap. No universal quantity is named. This is a structural prediction, not a measurement.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that Shannon entropy *is* information in biology, that free energy *is* information in cognition, or that a price *is* information in the Shannon sense. Each such mapping is a binding from one frame onto another, and convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Until provenance and a round-trip gap are shown, such a mapping carries no evidentiary weight.
>
> **What would refute the output. I wrote these down before sending this:**
> 1. A quantity Q that is the currency in all four domains, with the same anchor type in each.
> 2. A lossless, invertible map between two domain currencies with a measured round-trip gap of zero.
> 3. One operation that shrinks the residual in all four domains at once and leaves no intersection standing.
> 4. A demonstration that the four notions are readings of one underlying fact, so their agreement is redundant.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures text with line numbers (local extract in this folder's framework): every `Fig N :line` above can be checked character by character.
> - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Information* (Adriaans and van Benthem, 2012, revised 2023): https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/information/
> - Landauer's principle (physics): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landauer%27s_principle
> - Free energy principle (cognition and biology): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_energy_principle
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the eight-step loop is in the figures. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
