## What the wider literature actually bears on the question

Five low-overlap fields were sampled, and each supplies a different witness:

1. **Philosophy of information (mathematics and logic).** The SEP entry is the strongest outside corroboration for the verdict: the leading definitions conflict, and the emerging picture is *not* one quantity but several sub-theories with transformations, explicitly compared to energy. It predicts Option B.
2. **Physics and thermodynamics.** Landauer ties a bit to a physical cost, which is a domain-specific anchor (a measurement), not a universal definition. It predicts a currency with an anchor, not a single cross-domain notion.
3. **Biology and cybernetics.** The good regulator theorem makes the model a *homomorphism* that *"can lose information about the entity that is modeled."* That is a domain-internal instance of the lossy-transform claim.
4. **Cognition and neuroscience.** Friston's own distinction between a principle and a process theory mirrors the framework's kernel/currency split, and the warning that the principle cannot be falsified as a principle is a direct caution against treating the unity claim as a ground reading.
5. **Society and economics.** Hayek's dispersed knowledge is a currency with a market as anchor, and his point that the knowledge *"never exists in concentrated or integrated form"* corroborates the per-slot residual rule against a single aggregate.

Overlap is declared rather than hidden. The four Substack essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement with the figures is one evidence pool and carries little independent weight. The five sources above are independent of that pool, but they overlap each other at one point: all five are surveys or encyclopedic summaries of established results, not new experiments. They corroborate the *shape* of the answer; they do not settle it.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the answer before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the sources above. Never present agreement from stage 2 as evidence for stage 1.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage 1 output survives. If any of the four refuters in `01` section 4 fires, the shape claim is withdrawn before contact, not after.
