## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and then stops where instruments cannot distinguish. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a shape is the narrowed form of an answer about information that can be stated before a universal quantity is named. **One kernel, four currencies, four anchors and a measurable cross-domain gap is a shape. "Information is entropy" is a name.** The first constrains any candidate answer; the second fills a slot by a binding from one frame and is not licensed by the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity and unbundling

From *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively. The essay warns that a question may be a bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is exactly the move in `01` F3: four conditions, unbundled before search.

Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual is described in effect terms (what each domain's measure does and what settles it), not by the word *information* imported from one domain into the others.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question: a ground reading in which one quantity is settled in all four domains by the same instrument type.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and shadow test, and with its round-trip gap measured (Fig 4 :30). A cross-domain binding such as "free energy is biological information" is such an import, and it carries no weight until provenance and gap are supplied.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A universal notion of information would be a new axis, not a new term inside one domain. The essay also warns that a declared set is a choice: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So even the claim that the loop is universal is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Record it as such.

## 4. The hard problem of source: why a shared reading is not a shared notion

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. A color instrument reads green; the source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Apply to this question: all four domains can return a number called *information*. That shared reading does not establish a shared source. What is owed is a discriminator that can tell whether the four readings come from one quantity or four, not a stipulation that they do. This is also why the F5 convention reading matters: a shared *meaning* is a convention (Fig 2 :41), and *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"* (Fig 10 :18).

## 5. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous people who agree that information is one thing would be the named collapse, not evidence. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it. That is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with refuters and asks for a failure, not an endorsement.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. No measurement was taken in this folder.

## 7. One consequence for this question

The shape that survives is modest and checkable: the loop is single, the currencies and anchors are four, the transforms are lossy, and the universal quantity is not named. The essays add two cautions that are part of the answer rather than footnotes: the four-domain census is a human's visible guess (so keep it revisable), and a shared reading is not a shared source (so do not name a quantity from a reading). What would turn the shape into a name is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this folder does not have.
