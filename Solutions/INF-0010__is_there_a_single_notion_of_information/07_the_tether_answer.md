# The Tether's answer on one notion of information

**Mode: `general` on the kernel, `specified` on the four-domain classification, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The word *information* has a working measure and a working anchor in each of four domains. The question asks whether one quantity is the currency in all four. Answering "yes" commits us to commensurability and a lossless bridge; answering "no" commits us to an account of what is nevertheless shared. Fig 9 :4-5 splits the question before answering it: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The loop is single.** Fig 4 :19-20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."*

2. **The currencies and anchors are four, not one.** Physics settles a bit against a measurement or an experiment; biology settles free energy against a viable state; cognition settles prediction error against an answer for a person (Fig 2 :37); society settles dispersed knowledge against a market (Fig 2 :29). The figure's own definition of an anchor, *"whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :29), gives no single anchor that is constitutive for all four.

3. **The four conditions are a union compound.** They overlap at the signal/carrier seam and at the aboutness seam, so they must be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54-57). A single lever leaves the intersections standing.

4. **The bridge is lossy, with a measurable gap.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip to be measured. A universal notion that transfers losslessly is exactly what the figure excludes unless `R_T = 0` is measured.

5. **A shared meaning is a convention, not a verdict.** Fig 2 :41 places *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* under "efficient for a convention". Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*. Stipulating one definition of information across the four domains authors a convention; it does not touch the ground.

6. **Shannon coding is a composition, not the universal quantity.** Fig 5 :45: *"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."*

7. **What is returned is a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The framework can say one loop, four currencies, four anchors and a lossy bridge. It cannot name a universal quantity, and this solution does not try.

8. **A stronger structural hypothesis is offered, not asserted.** Written over the six-term set, the four notions may be isomers (Fig 12 :117, *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*) or only members of a functional group (Fig 12 :124). The bindings are provenanced imports with an unmeasured gap, so this stays `specified`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the four domains are unrelated. They share a loop and a substrate; what they do not share, on this reading, is one currency.
- Not that *information* is meaningless or merely a word. It is a working term in each domain, with an anchor. The claim is about its scope, not its usefulness.
- Not that Shannon entropy, free energy, prediction error or price are the same thing under different names. Each such mapping is a binding with provenance, unmeasured here.
- Not a derivation of one domain's measure from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles the unity question.
- Not that the census is nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."*

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that settled it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Commensurability test:** exhibit one quantity settled in all four domains by the same instrument type, with the same anchor relation. That would promote Option A.
- **Round-trip test:** map one domain's currency into another and back; measure `R_T`. A measured zero breaks the lossy-bridge claim; a measured nonzero corroborates it without settling the word.
- **Single-lever test:** find an operation that shrinks the residual in all four domains at once and leaves no intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **Discriminator test:** build an instrument that tells whether four equal readings of *information* come from one source or four. The essays' metamerism case is the model.
- **Standing refuters from `01` section 4:** a universal Q with one anchor; a lossless invertible map; a single lever; redundant agreement among the four notions. Any one fires and the shape claim is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four-domain census is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's list. *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The transforms are lossy, so a bridge built by argument alone is dead reckoning (Fig 4 :30). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* This is a candidate answer, not an accepted one.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not say that information is one thing everywhere, and this answer does not claim a final theory. They say that the same loop runs at every scale, but that the actors, currency and anchor change from domain to domain, and that the transform between domains loses information. Physics settles a bit against a measurement, biology a free energy against a viable state, cognition a prediction error against an answer for a person, and society a dispersed knowledge against a market. So what is single across the four is a shape, not a quantity: one loop, four currencies, four anchors, a round-trip gap that has to be measured. A shared definition would be a convention, not a verdict, and declaring one would not ground it. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question stays open until a ground settles it.
