# Union, functional group and order in the complex-systems answer

**Mode: `general` on the structural derivation, `specified` on the family readings. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).**

## RESIDUAL

The question bundles two conditions (fine-scale unpredictability and coarse-scale stability) and asks for a selection of systems. Three questions are owed:

1. Is the bundle a union or a partition, and what follows for the search?
2. Do the candidate families form an identity, an isomer set or merely a functional group under Fig 12?
3. In what dependency order must the conditions be checked, so effort is not spent above a break?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: the two levels (fine outcomes, coarse statistic), the crossing between them, an external anchor, and the round-trip gap `R_T`. Bond vocabulary held: the seven bonds plus negation. No new primitive is available for this derivation; the families are bindings from the science frame, not new atoms.

Anchor per domain: an instrument for the fine/coarse pair; `R_T` for the membrane; the ground for the checking frame. Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54).

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Union or partition?

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :57-58: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

The two conditions are independent enough to come apart:

- self-averaging without chaos still gives a stable ledger (many i.i.d. trials with unknown single outcomes);
- chaos without finite ensemble statistics gives no stable mean (heavy-tailed distributions, where the opened law-of-large-numbers source reports non-convergence for Cauchy and Pareto with exponent below 1).

So the compound needs two loops and its own ground. A single lever that clears one condition leaves the intersection standing. **Verdict:** `triangulated`, Fig 9 plus the world source, low overlap.

**Falsifier:** if one condition entailed the other in all cases, the union reading is wrong and the unbundling instruction does not apply.

### Q2 - Identity, isomer or functional group?

Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

As the families are written, they share a motif but not an atom set: molecules and pressure, bets and percentage, nuclei and half-life, trajectories and climate bounds, avalanches and a power law. The atoms differ, so under Fig 12 they are **not** isomers. They are members of one **functional group**: *fine unpredictable → crossing → coarse predictable*. The group fixes the kind; the atoms say which member. Calling them one phenomenon is the flat-formula error (Fig 12 :14 *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"*).

The stronger claim is available but distinct: two systems written over the **same atom set** and differing only in the bond between fine and coarse would be Fig 12 isomers, with different statistical substance. This is a checkable structural claim, not yet a result. **Verdict:** `single-source` for the figure definitions; `specified` for the family reading.

**Falsifier:** if two families with the same atoms and the same bond yield different statistics, or two with different atoms and different bonds yield identical statistics, the functional-group/isomer distinction collapses here.

### Q3 - Cycle and attractor

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

A strange attractor is the cycle reading applied to a trajectory: the output re-enters as input, the folding keeps it in a bounded region, and the invariant measure is the coarse statistic. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes; a plain chain cannot. This is why a chaotic system with a closing, bounded loop (Lorenz, a driven pendulum) has statistics, while a merely unbounded amplifying chain does not.

**Verdict:** `single-source` (Fig 12), corroborated at low overlap by the opened chaos sources (SEP and Wikipedia describe strange attractors and finite predictability). The corroboration is in the field of nonlinear dynamics, not the framework, so it is a genuine low-overlap witness for the physics half only.

**Falsifier:** a system whose output re-enters with a positive, non-closing sign yet still has a bounded invariant measure.

### Q4 - Dependency order

Fig 3 :19 **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."** Fig 3 :20-21 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

Order for this question:

1. **Anchor** (Fig 2 :29, :33). Is there a counter that does not update? If not, stop: there is no prediction to classify.
2. **Membrane and round trip** (Fig 4 :21, :27, :30). Is there a lossy crossing, and has `R_T` been measured and bounded?
3. **Compressibility** (Fig 5 :18, :43). Does the statistic pay for itself as a description?
4. **Describe before frame** (Fig 9 :27-28). Only now pick a family vocabulary.
5. **Refuse the closed list** (Fig 13 :115-118). Search inside an unbounded arrangement space is not a route to completion.

A reading taken at step 5 without steps 1 to 3 is not a weak reading of the answer; by Fig 3 :22-23 it is *"a reading of nothing."*

**Verdict:** `triangulated`, Fig 3 plus Fig 2 plus Fig 4.

**Falsifier:** show that family classification can validly precede anchoring, and that a list can be closed before the membrane is checked.

## CONCLUSION

The bundle is a **union compound** with two loops and its own ground. The families form a **functional group**, not an identity; an isomer reading requires the same atom set and a different bond, and a cycle reading applies where the output re-enters and the loop closes. The dependency order is anchor, membrane with measured `R_T`, compressibility, describe-before-frame, refuse-the-list. This rescues the defensible part of the question (there is a stable shape any answer must have) without producing the closed catalogue the figures forbid.
