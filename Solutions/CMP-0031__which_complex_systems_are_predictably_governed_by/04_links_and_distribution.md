## Four findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The question in one sentence.** Wikipedia, *Chaos theory*: *"This does not mean that one cannot assert anything about events far in the future - only that some restrictions on the system are present. For example, we know that the temperature of the surface of the earth will not naturally reach 100 °C ... but we cannot predict exactly which day will have the hottest temperature of the year."* A stable bound with an unpredictable individual date. This is the question's property stated without framework vocabulary.
2. **The myth correction.** SEP, *Chaos*: *"Even for the Lorenz system there are regions in state space where uncertainties decrease, meaning forecasting the future is quite good in these regions even with chaotic dynamics"* and *"Hence, the idea that chaotic systems are unpredictable is a myth."* Chaos does not abolish prediction; it bounds the horizon and changes what can be predicted. SEP's ensemble example (*"On 30% of the model runs..."*) is an ensemble statistic, not a single-outcome claim.
3. **The boundary that a flat answer would miss.** Wikipedia, *Law of large numbers*: the average converges only under conditions. The heavy-tail limitation (Cauchy distribution, Pareto with exponent below 1) gives a family where no finite mean exists and the average does not settle. So "many trials" is not sufficient for a stable regularity.
4. **A candidate family that refuses to be guaranteed.** Wikipedia, *Self-organized criticality*: *"there is no known set of general characteristics that guarantee a system will display SOC."* This is why `01` classifies SOC as `specified` and ungrounded rather than as a figure-derived class.

None of these conflicts with the figure verdict. Where the world sources and the figures would disagree, the figures govern. The four framework essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement is high overlap and is not counted as corroboration.

- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A mass send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three conditions and the boundary failures; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
