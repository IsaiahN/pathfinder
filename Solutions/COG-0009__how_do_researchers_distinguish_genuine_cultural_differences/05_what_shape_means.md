## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The canonical README sets the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where the instruments stop discriminating. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for COG-0009 a **shape** is the form of a discrimination procedure that can be stated before a specific statistical test is chosen. It constrains: split the residual, measure the round trip, install a falsifiable check, keep the anchor outside the channel, buy independence, test per slot, use thresholds as filters, record the stop. It does not fill: it does not say "use scalar invariance" or "use anchoring vignettes." `A between-group score difference underdetermines its source at the instrument's resolution` is a shape. `This difference is a translation artifact` is a name, and naming it from the score is the error.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: use the terms you already have that are closest, then let recurring new terms that overlap with them narrow the space. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: begin with "difference," "same item," "same score," then let terms like "invariance," "DIF," "vignette equivalence" and "contact" arrive from the measurement frame, checking each against the residual.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". A residual described as "the Likert means differ" is sharpened into one instrument's terms. A residual described as "a reading that cannot identify its source" travels.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :54-55 in essay vocabulary, and it is the reason the four candidates are unbundled before a method is selected.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by the time the right vocabulary is held, the answer has been composed in the locating. Here the stop is when the instruments no longer discriminate source, and the lineage is what remains checkable.

## 3. What licenses a name (a specific method, a specific source assignment)

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A cutoff that a model passes is a gate, not a settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test. Naming scalar invariance, a vignette protocol, or a team-translation procedure is an import from psychometrics, survey methodology or translation studies. It carries a source and an accessed date in `10_source_ledger.csv`. It is not a figure verdict.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the provenance rule: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* For this question, the methodological vocabulary chosen (invariance, vignettes, DIF) is also a guess about which axes can discriminate the residual. Declaring them makes the guess visible, not neutral.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: a source named from a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or an undiscovered scheme can all produce the same reading. The essay states the general form: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the consequence: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Also: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

The cross-cultural case is the same structure. The score reads "group A higher than group B." Genuine construct difference, changed item meaning, shifted response scale and non-contacting sample can all produce that reading. Naming one from the score is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (an invariance constraint, a fixed vignette, a cognitive interview, an enumerated sampling frame), not a larger reading or a rhetorical preference. It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the color case.

This corroborates F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, and it supplies the reason the shape stops at a procedure rather than a verdict.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the artifact?

**No, with a condition.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the frame. The condition for naming a source here is that the instrument discriminates among candidate sources. A source named from the reading alone is forbidden; a source named after a discriminating check that could have failed is a `specified`, still ungrounded assignment, with the provenance of the check recorded. The same distinction is in the figures: Fig 8 :50-52 says convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents, so only the record of where each came from separates them.

## 6. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I first wrote that a discriminating instrument *identifies* the source. That overstated it. The essay's instrument example is a spectrometer, which narrows the source set by measuring a new axis; it does not deliver a name without a model of what the axis means. Re-census: the correct claim is that a discriminating instrument **reduces the set of sources consistent with the reading**, and the ground settles the remainder. The source assignment stays `candidate` until something pays for it. The §4 claim is narrowed, not retracted; the stronger version is recorded here so neither reading is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A well-known journal, a famous methodologist, or a large multi-country dataset can fund a check. None of them settles whether the difference was genuine. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues and no names, and why a reply is a conversation, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So all twelve files here are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any cultural claim and cannot settle the question they report on. No dataset was measured, no instrument was run, no expert has replied.
