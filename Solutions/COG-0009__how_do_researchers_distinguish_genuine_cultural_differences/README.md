## What is being decided, in effect terms

A researcher observes a score difference between two cultural groups under an instrument that is assumed to measure the same construct in the same way in both groups. Four candidate sources are on the table: a genuine difference in the construct; a translation artifact; a testing or response artifact; a sampling artifact. Each candidate leads to a different act: a substantive cultural claim, a repaired instrument, a corrected response format, or a re-contacted population. The residual is the observed difference that the prediction "same instrument, same meaning, same contact" fails to explain. An answer is a procedure that assigns the difference to a source with a stated failure mode, not a verdict pronounced from the size of the difference.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The figure-grounded reasons are in `01` F7 and `04`: a list selected for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Seat Map :163), a room does not upgrade a label, and a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
