## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A group difference `Δ` is observed on a score obtained with one instrument administered in more than one cultural group. The prediction that failed is: same construct, same instrument, same contact, therefore a difference in the score is a difference in the construct. The residual is `Δ` minus what that prediction can explain. It is a **reading**, not a source.

Candidates, with the act each one licenses:

| candidate | what it says produced `Δ` | act it licenses |
|---|---|---|
| G, genuine construct difference | the latent construct really differs | make a substantive cultural claim |
| T, translation artifact | the item does not carry the same meaning across languages | repair or re-adapt the instrument |
| S, testing or response artifact | the scale, mode, or response style shifted the reading | change format or correct response use |
| P, sampling artifact | the two samples do not contact the populations claimed | re-sample or re-weight |

Asking "is this difference real or an artifact?" treats G, T, S and P as two options. The figures say it is a union of overlapping conditions, so the honest question is which conditions bind and which compound remains after each lever is pulled (Fig 9 :54-55).

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): *a procedure that assigns `Δ` to a source or to a named compound, states the failure that would change the assignment, and records what the instrument could not discriminate.* This is the sought-for shape made refusable.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** one reading (`Δ`) with at least four candidate sources. A single score with multiple possible sources is a seam, not a single quantity.
- **Symmetry:** the candidates are **not** interchangeable. T, S and P are different bonds between the reading and the world, not permutations of one another. Swapping translation for sampling changes the remedy, so the relation is ordered, not symmetric (Fig 12 bond table).
- **Scale:** the instrument is a channel at one scale; cultural groups are frames at that scale; the construct is the ground the channel should range against. A step, not a lineage, until repeated administration is considered, at which point channel drift becomes a cycle-level concern (Fig 10 :24).

### 1b. One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

**Domain 1, the translation seam (T).** Actors: source-language item, target-language item, translators, adjudicators, bilingual reviewers. Currency: whether the description still carries the same meaning when it crosses. Anchor: a procedure that can fail, for example committee review, cognitive interviewing, or a measured round-trip semantic gap. This is a genuine domain: the anchor is nameable (a discriminating procedure), even though no perfect translation exists. Question type: a **seam** between two closures (Fig 10 :13-19), upstream of everything else.

**Domain 2, the response channel (S).** Actors: items, response scales, respondents, response styles, administration mode. Currency: the mapping from latent construct to observed responses (loadings, thresholds, intercepts, DIF). Anchor: an invariance constraint that can be rejected, plus an external criterion the channel does not author. Question type: a **partition** question at first (does the channel have the same form in both groups), becoming a **compound** when response style and translation interact.

**Domain 3, the contact habitat (P).** Actors: who was sampled, under what conditions, mode, setting, season. Currency: contact with the population the cultural claim is about. Anchor: an enumerated habitat and the population the inference targets. Question type: a **habitat** question, enumerated rather than composed (Fig 11 :43). It has a nameable anchor, so it is a domain.

**Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: methods, studies, reviewers, instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: a frame-independence question. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero and its agreement is not a witness.

### 1c. Partition or union

T, S and P overlap. A poorly translated item can also shift its intercept (T ∩ S); a convenience sample can also lack the language variant the item assumes (P ∩ T). A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds (Fig 9 :55). The residual is therefore a **union compound** with its own loop and its own ground, not four separate hard questions and not one question with four labels.

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Contact and translation first (perception and vocabulary). A reading taken at the mean-comparison step when the channel has not been checked is *"a reading of nothing"* (Fig 3 :22-23).
2. Invariance of the channel second (the objective: what would count as the same measurement).
3. Mean or relation comparison last (planning under the settled channel).
4. Verification runs alongside, but its verdict never substitutes for a ground (Fig 2 :45).

Upstream first. Effort spent on step 3 before step 1 arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19-20).

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1. The score difference is a reading, not a source (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
Fig 12 :95: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

A translation maps the item from one closure into another. The transform is lossy, so the target item carries the ingredient set (the words) without necessarily carrying the bond (the meaning). The score records the ingredients and cannot record the bond. Therefore two groups can score differently because the bond changed, with an identical written item, and nothing in the score announces it. By inspection of `Δ` alone, G and T are indiscernible. This is the metamerism point in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading cannot comment on its source. The remedy is a source-discriminating procedure and a measured gap, not a larger difference or a better argument. **Triangulated**: Fig 4 (lossy round trip) and Fig 12 (bond invisible in the notation) are low-overlap figures. World corroboration: Putnick and Bornstein (2016) write that *"different groups can apply different meanings to the same cognition or behavior."*

### F2. Split the union before testing (triangulated)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

Back-translation, response-style correction and re-sampling are three levers. Each clears the region where its own condition holds alone. The intersections (a translation that also shifts a threshold, a sample that also lacks the language variant) survive, because a second condition still binds. What remains is not a harder translation problem but a specific compound with its own loop. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged: an average of "probably translation" and "probably sampling" is not a diagnosis. **Triangulated** with Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and Fig 10 (different frames, different ground).

### F3. Test per item and per group, not on the pooled mean (single-source on the figure, corroborated)

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A scale mean pools many item slots. A single item whose meaning shifted can be cancelled by other items, so the aggregate looks invariant while a live per-item residual remains. Differential item functioning is a slot-level reading. The operational consequence: report item-level statistics and group-level constraints, not only a total-scale comparison. **Single-source** for the figure line (Fig 1), corroborated by the field's own DIF and partial-invariance practice (Avvisati et al., 2019; Putnick and Bornstein, 2016), which is an independent evidence pool.

### F4. Independence is bought with low-overlap methods, not with more of the same method (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"

Two self-report scales in the same language, filled by the same respondents under the same instructions, are one frame wearing two names. An anchoring vignette, an informant report, a behavioural measure, or a cognitive interview that observes how the respondent parsed the item draws on different evidence and can correct the reading. King's anchoring-vignette site states the mechanism directly: *"the new idea underlying anchoring vignettes is to measure directly, and then subtract off, the incomparable portion."* **Triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 7. Caveat named: the vignettes themselves must be equivalent across groups; otherwise the "anchor" is another frame (King et al., 2004, vignette-equivalence assumption).

### F5. A declared equivalence is not an established one (triangulated)

Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
Fig 10 :19: "install what can be violated"
Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"

Saying "we administered the same scale in both countries" is a declaration. It becomes a grounded claim only after a check that can fail is installed. An invariance constraint that cannot reject, a cutoff chosen after seeing the result, or an equivalence asserted because the study needs the comparison is a convention deciding an outcome. The figure-grounded form is: state the equality you are assuming, state what would falsify it, and run the falsifier before making the comparison. **Triangulated** across Fig 10's three lines and Fig 9 :60.

### F6. Fit thresholds are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A ΔCFI cutoff decides how much evidence to spend on a comparison, not whether the cultural difference is genuine. The ground (an external criterion, a corrected reading, a discriminating instrument) decides the verdict. This matters because the field's own literature disputes the cutoffs. Funder and Gardiner (2024) call *"the prohibitionist tone of discussions surrounding MI"* unhelpful; Fischer et al. (2025) call for integrating theoretical and empirical evidence rather than treating a statistical threshold as final. Both are consumed as **corroboration of the filter rule**, not as an instruction to abandon checking. A conflict about a cutoff is a budget argument, not a figure conflict.

### F7. Sampling is a contact problem, not a third kind of test bias (single-source, re-censused)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

A convenient online sample is a substituted habitat. It proves the instrument wires up in that habitat and says nothing about the population the cultural claim names. Increasing the sample size inside the same non-contacting habitat changes nothing about contact. The first act is to enumerate what is in contact with the residual (who, where, under what mode and condition), then decide whether the sample reaches it. **Single-source** (Fig 11); the field's sampling literature is a separate pool that corroborates but is not needed for the verdict.

### F8. The shape is a procedure, not a named test (triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

No figure line names CFA invariance, IRT DIF, anchoring vignettes, back-translation or cognitive interviewing as *the* sufficient test. The figures constrain the shape (split the residual, measure the round trip, install a falsifiable check, keep the anchor reachable, buy independence, test per slot) and stop. If no available instrument discriminates source, the honest report is `unreached`, with the lineage recorded. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The specific choice of method is an import from the measurement frame, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). **This is a gap in the figures, recorded as a gap, not filled by a world source.**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted or narrowed if any of the following holds:

1. **Reading determines source.** A demonstration that a between-group score difference identifies its own source at the instrument's resolution, with no measurable round-trip gap and no hidden bond (breaks F1, Fig 4 :30 and Fig 12 :95).
2. **Union collapses to a partition.** A single lever that clears every intersection, leaving no compound (breaks F2, Fig 9 :54-55).
3. **Correlated agreement admitted as witness.** A figure reading under which two methods sharing an evidence pool can corroborate the verdict (breaks F4, Fig 2 :40).
4. **A figure names a sufficient test.** A verbatim figure line that designates one specific statistical procedure as sufficient to certify genuineness (breaks F8 and turns the recorded `gap` into a verdict).
5. **The channel does not decay.** Evidence that repeated administration and translation never shift the mapping from construct to score (breaks F5, Fig 10 :24).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn or narrowed in this file with the retraction left visible. Until one is run, no part of this is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the refuter, not with a conclusion about a culture (F1, F8).
2. Unbundle T, S and P, and name the compound that survives each lever (F2).
3. Install a falsifiable check per domain before comparing means; report item-level and group-level results (F3, F5).
4. Buy independence with methods that draw on different evidence, and treat the vignette itself as a frame to be checked (F4).
5. Use cutoffs as budget filters, and record the ground that actually decides (F6).
6. Enumerate the habitat before trusting the sample (F7).
7. Say `gap` for the choice of a specific test, and record which method was imported from which frame (F8).

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What can be offered to another researcher is the method (split, bracket, falsify, record), not a recording of this derivation's conclusion.

### Round-trip gap R_T (summarised here, full treatment in `11_audit.md`)

- **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 12 :95 (bond invisible in notation), Fig 9 :54-55 (union), Fig 2 :31/:40 (independence).
- **Abstracted (T_A):** "separate reading from source with falsifiable checks and independent methods."
- **Brought back down (T_E):** the concrete field procedure (team translation with documented round trip; item-level invariance tests; vignettes, informants and behavioural criteria; enumerated sampling frame; preregistered falsifier).

**R_T is nonzero.** The shorthand drops (a) the per-slot requirement, (b) the union-compound requirement, (c) the channel-decay requirement, and (d) the independence parameter. One thing survives cleanly: a reading does not identify its source without a discriminating procedure. R_T is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints, not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: split the residual, measure the round trip, install a falsifiable check per domain, keep the anchor reachable and outside the channel, buy independence with low-overlap methods, test per slot, use thresholds as filters, and stop at the instrument's limit with the lineage recorded. The specific method is a `gap` filled by imports whose provenance must be shown. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; one earlier forced reading of Fig 13 is retracted above.
