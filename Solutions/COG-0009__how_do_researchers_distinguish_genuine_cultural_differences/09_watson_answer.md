## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what follows from observing a between-group score difference under an instrument assumed to measure the same construct in the same way in both groups. Option A: treat the difference as a genuine cultural difference. Option B: treat it as a translation, testing or sampling artifact. Option C: treat the difference as a reading whose source is underdetermined, and specify a procedure that discriminates sources with stated failure modes. What each leads to: A risks a substantive claim about a culture built on a changed item meaning; B risks discarding a real difference because a gate failed; C yields a shape, written falsifiers and an honest stop where instruments no longer discriminate. The figures decide C.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** A between-group score difference `Δ` that the prediction "same construct, same instrument, same contact" fails to explain. Arity: one reading, four candidate sources (G genuine, T translation, S response/testing, P sampling). Symmetry: the candidates are not interchangeable, they are different bonds over the same atoms; swapping T for P changes the remedy. Scale: a step at the instrument level; a cycle only when repeated administration and channel drift are included. The candidates overlap, so R is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), not four versions of one question.
- **Domain 1, the translation seam (T).** Actors: source item, target item, translators, adjudicators, bilingual reviewers. Currency: survival of meaning across the crossing. Anchor: a source-discriminating procedure that can fail, for example a documented round trip or cognitive interviewing. Question type: a seam between two closures, upstream of everything else.
- **Domain 2, the response channel (S).** Actors: items, scales, response styles, mode, administration. Currency: the mapping from construct to item response (loadings, thresholds, intercepts, DIF). Anchor: an invariance constraint that can be rejected plus an external criterion the channel does not author. Question type: partition at first, compound when T and S interact.
- **Domain 3, the contact habitat (P).** Actors: who was sampled, where, under what mode and condition. Currency: contact with the population the claim names. Anchor: an enumerated habitat and the target population. Question type: habitat and contact, enumerated rather than composed.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: studies, methods, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** contact and translation first, channel invariance second, comparison last; verification runs alongside and never supplies the verdict. A reading taken at the comparison step without the upstream checks is a reading of nothing with respect to source.

Strain test applied: an earlier attempt to use Fig 13's "spectrum = ordering" to license a psychometric scalar-invariance requirement governed a different quantity and was retracted (see `01` section 6). Fig 12 governs the arrangement of bonds instead. After re-census, no hidden strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A between-group score difference underdetermines its source at the instrument's resolution. The figure-governed procedure is: describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a method; split the union of translation, response and sampling conditions and name the compound that survives each lever; measure the round-trip gap across translation; install a check per domain that can fail; read per item and per group; keep the anchor reachable and outside the channel; buy independence with low-overlap methods; use thresholds as budget filters, never verdict witnesses; enumerate the habitat; and stop at the instrument's limit with the lineage recorded. No verbatim figure line names a sufficient statistical test, so method choice is a `gap` filled by imports whose provenance is shown. This is a `general` shape with a `specified` cross-cultural application, ungrounded and still a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or discarding a genuine difference because a cutoff failed. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by treating cutoffs as filters.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. A source assignment can be superseded when a discriminating instrument returns a different result; the method imports are recorded with URLs and dates and can be replaced; a compound can be re-split. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the claims that failed the figure check.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating check that could have failed and did not, on an axis outside the channel; a lever that clears every intersection (would break the union verdict); a figure line naming a specific sufficient test (would close the `gap`); a demonstration that a score difference determines its source with no round-trip gap; or evidence that repeated administration and translation never shift the construct-to-score mapping. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
