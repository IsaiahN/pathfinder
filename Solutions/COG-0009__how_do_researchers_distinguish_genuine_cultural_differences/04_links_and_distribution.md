## 2. What the wider literature bears on this question

Three findings are worth stating to a researcher because they sharpen the figure-derived shape without changing it.

1. **Equivalence has levels, and the level determines the claim.** Putnick and Bornstein (2016) and Avvisati et al. (2019) both set out the ladder: configural (same form), metric (same loadings), scalar (same intercepts), residual (same unique variances). Mean comparisons require scalar invariance; relational comparisons need less. This is a **budget and claim specification** (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*), not a figure verdict on genuineness.
2. **You can measure the incomparable portion instead of trying to talk it away.** King's vignettes and the cognitive-interviewing guidelines are concrete instantiations of Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* A vignette rating is a fixed reference presented to every respondent, so the variation in its rating is the channel, not the construct.
3. **The strict gate has a real critic.** Funder and Gardiner (2024) argue that invariance benchmarks can be arbitrary and that noninvariance can itself be a finding. This does **not** overturn the figures; it corroborates Fig 10 :53 and the filter rule. It does mean a researcher should not use this shape to prohibit a study before looking at the data. The figures constrain the procedure, not the publication decision.

One **conflict to record**: the field disagrees about how strict a gate should be. Per the WATSON rule, a conflict sends us back to the census, not to a new verdict. Re-census result: the disagreement is about a convention (a cutoff), while the figure verdict is about a structure (reading does not determine source). They are different quantities, so no figure strain is created. Funder and Gardiner are classified as corroborating the filter rule, not as conflicting with the figures. No world source was allowed to decide the verdict.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole derivation before a recipient has described their residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13** "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Ten studies using the same instrument are one study with ten letterheads.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter, with the request "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then, if invited, the three local figure paths; (5) the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that the user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. Two-stage offer

- **Stage 1**, before any theory: send the residual, the shape and the refuter. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the output without holding the derivation's interior.
- **Stage 2**, opt-in only: offer the three figure paths and the ledger. The figures are the authority, not the summary. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3**, offer the formula as a generator (Fig 4 :22). Each new problem still needs its own ground and its own pre-registered falsifier, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted it is a shape with a recorded `gap` on method choice and a nonzero round-trip gap. Both are visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 and `11_audit.md`. Repair means keeping those records, not erasing them.
