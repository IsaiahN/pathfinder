## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a between-group score difference that the "same instrument, same meaning, same contact" prediction fails to explain. Arity: one reading with four candidate sources, so a seam rather than a single quantity. Symmetry: the candidates are ordered, not symmetric; substituting sampling for translation changes the remedy. Scale: a step at the instrument level, a cycle once repeated administration and channel drift enter. The four overlap, so R is a **union compound** with its own ground.
- **Domain 1, translation seam.** Actors: source item, target item, translators, reviewers. Currency: survival of meaning across the crossing. Anchor: a procedure that can fail. Type: seam between closures.
- **Domain 2, response channel.** Actors: items, scales, respondents, response styles, mode. Currency: the construct-to-response mapping. Anchor: a rejectable invariance constraint plus an external criterion. Type: partition that becomes a compound when T and S interact.
- **Domain 3, contact habitat.** Actors: sample, conditions, mode, setting. Currency: contact with the target population. Anchor: an enumerated habitat. Type: habitat, enumerated.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: studies, methods, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence here is zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: contact and translation first, channel invariance second, comparison last; verification alongside but never as verdict (Fig 3, Fig 2 :45).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-57 and the isomer set from Fig 12, I derived the union from Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and the order from Fig 3 :20/:22. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :30, Fig 12 :95, Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 2 :31/:40, Fig 11 :43.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01`, `07` and `08`: a score difference does not determine its source, so split the residual, bracket the round trip, install falsifiable checks, buy independence and record the stop.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the field-facing procedure: team translation with documented gap, item-level invariance constraints and DIF, fixed vignettes with equivalence checks, cognitive interviewing, enumerated sampling frames, preregistered falsifiers.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot requirement** (Fig 1 :19/:22), easily dropped when the shorthand becomes "test invariance";
- the **union-compound requirement** (Fig 9 :54-57), easily dropped when the shorthand becomes "control for bias";
- the **channel-decay requirement** (Fig 10 :24), easily dropped when the shorthand becomes "the instrument was validated once";
- the **independence parameter** (Fig 2 :31), easily dropped when the shorthand becomes "use multiple methods" without specifying that they must draw on different evidence.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a reading does not identify its source without a discriminating procedure and a measured gap. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
