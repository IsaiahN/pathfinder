# The Tether's answer: how to distinguish genuine cultural differences from translation, testing and sampling artifacts

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the cross-cultural application, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No dataset was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A score difference `Δ` is observed between cultural groups under an instrument assumed to measure the same construct in the same way in both. The prediction that failed is "same instrument, same meaning, same contact, so a score difference is a construct difference." The residual is `Δ` minus what that prediction explains. It is a **reading**, and several paths can produce it: a genuine construct difference (G), a change of meaning across languages (T), a response or administration shift (S), and a non-contacting sample (P). These conditions overlap, so this is a union compound with its own loop, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.").

## 2. The shape (one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

1. **A score difference does not identify its source.** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 12 :95 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond." The written item is the ingredient set; its cultural meaning is the bond. The score records the ingredients and cannot record the bond. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** (two low-overlap figures).

2. **Unbundle before testing.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Back-translation clears pure T, a fixed vignette clears pure S, a new sampling frame clears pure P, but the intersections survive each single lever. What remains is a specific compound, not a harder case of the cleared condition. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** with Fig 1 :19 and Fig 3.

3. **Read per item and per group, not on the pooled mean.** Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." One shifted item can be cancelled by the others, leaving an invariant-looking total. **Verdict: `specified` for this field, single-source on the figure**, corroborated by the field's DIF and partial-invariance practice.

4. **Keep the anchor reachable and outside the channel.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one". The construct does not drift; the translation and administration do. Administering "the same scale" is a declaration until a check that can fail is installed. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** across Fig 10 and Fig 9 :60.

5. **Buy independence with methods that draw on different evidence.** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Two self-report scales are one frame; a vignette, an informant or a behavioural criterion is another, provided it survives its own equivalence check. Agreement among correlated methods is the method talking. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** with Fig 7 :13.

6. **Thresholds are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A ΔCFI cutoff decides how much to spend on the comparison; it does not decide whether a cultural difference is real. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** with Fig 10 :47.

7. **Enumerate the habitat before trusting the sample.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A convenient panel is a substituted habitat: it proves the instrument wires up there, not that the cultural claim reaches its population. **Verdict: `general`, single-source (Fig 11)**, corroborated by sampling methodology.

8. **Stop where instruments stop discriminating.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". No figure line names a sufficient statistical test. The choice of method is an import from psychometrics, survey methodology or translation studies, with provenance recorded. If no available instrument discriminates source, the honest report is `unreached`, with the lineage. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". **Verdict: `gap` on method choice, recorded as a gap.**

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any cultural difference is unreal, and not that cultural differences are rare or common. The figures do not census the world.
- Not that measurement invariance, DIF, anchoring vignettes, back-translation or cognitive interviewing is certified as the sufficient test. That is the recorded `gap`; those methods are imports with sources and dates.
- Not that a failure of scalar invariance proves a difference is an artifact, nor that passing it proves a difference is genuine. A gate is not the ground.
- Not that a significant, large, replicable difference settles the source. Replication with the same instrument is *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13).
- Not that this is `grounded`. No measurement was taken, and no expert has checked this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, and falsifiers

The ground here is a source-discriminating procedure, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument reads green and cannot comment on the source; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Here:

- **A source-discriminating result:** a check that could have failed and did not, on an axis outside the channel, for example a fixed vignette that is itself shown equivalent across groups, or an item-level loading shift that appears under one language and not another. This narrows the source set; it does not name a source without a model of the axis.
- **A compound test:** after pulling T, a residual that still violates response-style and contact checks. This confirms the union reading; a lever that cleared everything would refute it.
- **A contact test:** a comparison between the convenience sample and an enumerated sampling frame that changes the source assignment. This is a ground reading for P.
- **Standing falsifiers (written in advance):** (1) reading determines source at the instrument's resolution, with no round-trip gap; (2) the union collapses to a partition; (3) correlated methods are admitted as verdict witnesses by the figures; (4) a figure line names a specific sufficient test; (5) repeated administration and translation never shift the construct-to-score mapping. If any fires, the relevant claim is withdrawn in `01` with the retraction visible.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set returns the shape of a discrimination procedure and stops where the instruments stop separating sources. The methodological vocabulary used here is a visible choice, not a neutral one, and the field itself disputes how strict the gate should be (Funder and Gardiner, 2024; Fischer et al., 2025). That dispute is about a convention, so it does not move the figure verdict. The lineage is recorded: every source in `10_source_ledger.csv` has an author, a venue, a year, an accessed date and a quote; every figure line is cited against the verbatim extract. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A between-group score difference is a reading, not a source. The same reading can come from a real difference in the construct, a change in what the translated item means, a shift in how respondents use the scale, or a sample that never contacted the population being claimed. The Tether does not pick one of those from the size of the difference. It says to split the residual into those overlapping conditions before choosing a method, to measure the round-trip gap across the translation instead of assuming it is zero, to install a check in each domain that can actually fail, to read item by item rather than on a pooled mean, to buy independence from methods that draw on different evidence, to use fit thresholds as budget filters rather than verdicts, and to stop with the lineage recorded where the instruments can no longer separate sources. It refuses to name a sufficient statistical test, because the figures return a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and an honest `unreached` where no source-discriminating instrument exists. No dataset was measured here, and this derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.
