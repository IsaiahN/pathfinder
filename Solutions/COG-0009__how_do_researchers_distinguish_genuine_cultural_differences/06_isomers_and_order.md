# Union, order and analogues: the supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified` on the field application, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## RESIDUAL

The four candidate sources of a between-group score difference (genuine construct G, translation T, response/testing S, sampling P) are treated in the literature as competing "biases." Two questions are owed: (1) is their structure a partition or a union, and (2) in what order must they be tested for a reading at one step to mean anything? A third question is whether the engineering measurement-systems analogue is a real mapping of relations or only a resemblance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held for this derivation: a reading (the score difference), an item (the instrument's carrier), a group (the frame), a construct (the intended ground), a language/culture (the seam), a response process (the channel), and a sampling frame (the habitat). Bonds available: the seven plus negation. No new primitive is needed; the four candidates are different bonds over the same atoms, not four new atoms.

Order by dependency (Fig 3): contact and translation upstream, channel invariance next, comparison last. Verification runs alongside and never supplies the verdict (Fig 2 :45).

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound"

A pure translation error is cleared by re-adaptation. A pure response-style error is cleared by a fixed vignette or a format change. A pure contact error is cleared by re-sampling. But T ∩ S (a mistranslated item that also shifts its threshold), P ∩ T (a sample lacking the language variant the item assumes), and T ∩ S ∩ P all survive any single one of those levers, because the other condition still binds. The residue after one lever is smaller **and categorically different**: it is a compound, not a harder instance of the cleared condition. Treating the four as a partition would apply one lever to a two-condition case and declare success because the mean moved. **Ruling: union.** Triangulated across Fig 9 :54-57 and Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual): a compound must be read per slot, because averaging it is exactly how the live signal disappears.

### Q2. What is the order, and what breaks if it is reversed?

- Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
- Fig 3 :22: "Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."

Map the five links for this question: (1) perception: can the researcher tell what item was actually presented and to whom; (2) vocabulary: are there terms for what the item and scale afford in each group; (3) objective: what would count as the same measurement here; (4) planning: the comparison design; (5) learn and carry: which invariants survive to the next study. A mean comparison is link 4 or 5. If links 1 to 3 have not been checked, a significant difference measured at link 4 is a reading of nothing with respect to the source question. Reversing the order does not make the comparison weaker, it makes it non-assessable for this residual. **Ruling: order is part of the spec**, not a preference. Single-source for Fig 3, corroborated by Putnick and Bornstein (2016), who make invariance a prerequisite for mean comparison.

### Q3. Are G, T, S and P "isomers" under Fig 12?

- Fig 12 :95: "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."
- Fig 12 :99: "A bond costs what it could have been, and nothing more."

All four candidates share the same ingredient set: a group, an item, a score difference. What differs is the bond between the reading and the world: a construct difference (G), a meaning change across languages (T), a response-process shift (S), a contact failure (P). By Fig 12 they are not variants of one explanation; they are different substances whose notation (the score) cannot tell them apart, with the difference carried entirely by the bond. This is the exact structural reason a score difference cannot decide among them. Under Fig 12's "flat formula" collapse, writing all four as "bias" records the ingredients and drops the bond, and nothing downstream can tell them apart. The priced-bond rule adds the practical consequence: a vocabulary that has only the ingredient "bias" and no bond vocabulary cannot *state* which lever to pull, so the choice is made blind. **Ruling: yes, a genuine Fig 12 isomer set, single-source for the figure definition**, corroborated by the field's separate bias taxonomies (van de Vijver's construction/ item/ method bias families, as reported in the meeting report and the equivalence reviews).

### Q4. Is the engineering analogue a real mapping? (living-system test)

Manufacturing gauge R&R (ASQ, 2026; NIST/SEMATECH, 2012), read 2026-11-22:

- **Actors:** parts, operators, gauges, setups. **Currency:** variance components. **Anchor:** a homogeneous reference part assumed not to change during the study.
- Mapping to this question: parts map to the construct levels the instrument should distinguish; operators and gauges map to the language and response channel; part-to-part variation maps to a genuine difference; repeatability and reproducibility map to channel artifacts; the homogeneous reference part maps to a fixed vignette or an invariant item.
- The mapping is of **relations, not looks**. Both systems decompose total observed variation into a world component and a measurement-system component, and both require an experiment, not inspection of the total, to separate them. ASQ states that total variation combines repeatability, reproducibility and part-to-part variation; NIST states the purpose is to characterize instrument performance in terms of errors affecting measurements, with artifacts, operators and gauges as design factors. That is the same bond structure as Fig 4 :30 (measure the gap) and Fig 1 :22 (do not aggregate away the live slot).
- **Where the analogue stops:** a manufactured part has a reference value that can be measured more accurately with another instrument, so the anchor is physical and reachable. A cultural construct usually has no such second instrument; the vignette is a designed approximation and must itself pass an equivalence check (King et al., 2004). Claiming more than this for the analogue would be importing an anchor the frame does not have. **Ruling: valid relational analogue with a stated limit**, corroborating, not deciding.

## FALSIFIER, WRITTEN BEFORE THE RUN

The union/order/analogue derivation is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A lever is found that clears every intersection of T, S and P, leaving no compound (breaks Q1).
2. A mean comparison is shown to be assessable for source without prior contact and channel checks (breaks Q2, Fig 3 :22).
3. A notation is produced in which the four candidates share the same ingredients and the same bond yet remain different sources (breaks Q3, Fig 12 :95).
4. The engineering decomposition turns out to rest on resemblance rather than on the same part-to-part versus measurement-system structure (breaks Q4).

If any fires, the corresponding ruling is withdrawn in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` with the retraction left visible.

## CONCLUSION

The four candidates form a **union compound**, not a partition; a compound is a specific case in two or more conditions and has its own loop. The test order is **part of the specification**: contact and translation, then channel invariance, then comparison, with verification alongside but never as the verdict. Under Fig 12 the four are a genuine isomer set over a shared ingredient list, which is the structural reason the score cannot choose among them. The gauge R&R analogue maps relations (world variation versus channel variation) and stops where the physical reference part is replaced by a designed vignette that must itself be checked.
