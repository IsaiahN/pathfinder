# Expert verification packet: the Tether on genuine cultural differences versus artifacts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cross-cultural or measurement researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` shape, `specified` application, not `grounded`.** No dataset was measured. This is a shape and a procedure, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A between-group score difference is a reading, not a source. The same reading can arise from a genuine construct difference, a translation that changed the item's meaning, a response or testing shift, or a sample that does not contact the claimed population. These conditions overlap, so the residual is a union compound and must be unbundled before any single lever is trusted. A valid discrimination procedure: describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a method; measure the round-trip gap across translation; install a falsifiable check in each domain; test per item and per group; buy independence with methods drawing on different evidence; treat fit thresholds as budget filters, never verdict witnesses; enumerate the sampling habitat; and stop with the lineage recorded when instruments no longer discriminate source. The framework does not name a sufficient statistical test. That choice is a recorded `gap`, filled by imports whose provenance is shown.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 12
- **Statement:** a score difference cannot by itself identify its source, because the transform between language frames is lossy and the score records the item's ingredients but not the bond that carries its meaning.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 12 :95 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that a score difference determines its source at the instrument's resolution, with no measurable round-trip gap and no meaning the score fails to carry.

### C2. Field-facing; checkable by a measurement researcher against their own practice
- **Statement:** translation, response style and sampling form a union compound with surviving intersections, so one lever (back-translation, vignette correction, or re-sampling) clears only the pure region. Consequently checks must be item-level and group-level, not only on the pooled scale mean.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** does a translation shift ever show up as an intercept or threshold change (T ∩ S)? Does a convenience sample ever lack the language variant an item assumes (P ∩ T)? Does a scale-level invariance result ever hide a single shifted item?
- **Refuter:** a lever that clears every intersection and leaves no compound, or a demonstration that a pooled mean is assessable for source without item-level checks.

### C3. Framework-internal derivation with an explicit gap
- **Statement:** independence must be bought with methods drawing on different evidence; correlated methods are one witness; and fit thresholds are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict. No verbatim figure line names a specific sufficient statistical test, so method choice is a `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **Refuter:** a verbatim figure line naming one specific procedure as sufficient to certify genuineness; or a figure reading under which two methods sharing an evidence pool can corroborate the verdict.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that cultural differences are unreal, rare, or common. No world census was run.
- Not that scalar invariance, CFA, DIF, anchoring vignettes, team translation or cognitive interviewing is figure-certified. All are imports with sources and dates in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not that a failure of invariance proves an artifact, or that passing it proves genuineness. A gate is not the ground.
- Not that a large, significant, replicated difference settles the source. Replication with the same instrument compounds agreement, not evidence (Fig 7 :13).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, no dataset was opened for analysis, and no expert has replied.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A check that could have failed and did not, on an axis outside the channel, with the source set narrowed and the model of the axis stated.
2. A compound test showing which intersections survive after each lever.
3. A contact test against an enumerated sampling frame that changes the assignment.
4. The falsifiers in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 4, any one of which withdraws or narrows a claim.
