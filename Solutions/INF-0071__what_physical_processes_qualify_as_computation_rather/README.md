## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

A shape plus a route, not a decision procedure:

- a statement of the discriminator between "evolves according to rules" and "computes", in effect terms that a non-framework reader could check;
- a separation of the three candidate answers (intrinsic physical property / observer mapping / contact property) with a verdict per domain, never averaged;
- at least one instrument or observation that would decide between those candidates;
- a refusal of any claim to have settled the philosophical question itself.

Anything shorter, in particular a bare definition of computation, fails this binding and is refused by the pre-registered refuter in `01`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles the question it reports on.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.
3. Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." Agreement about a label is not a ground.
