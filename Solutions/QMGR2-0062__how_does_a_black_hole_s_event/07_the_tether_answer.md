**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A horizon is a classical causal boundary. Quantum field theory on that background radiates thermally, with entropy proportional to area. Semiclassical radiation appears thermal and information-free; quantum evolution is unitary. Asking "how does the horizon fit with quantum physics?" bundles at least three conditions: entropy and temperature, information and unitarity, smoothness and the interior. It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :5, :54). It has no anchor at the horizon (Fig 2 :29; Fig 6 :34). The objective link of the dependency chain is unfixed: what would count as a fit is still contested (Fig 3 :19).

## 2. The shape

1. **The horizon is a molecule over the six, not a primitive.** Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* A horizon composes containment (space) with the future direction of no return (time) through a boundary structure, with a spendable difference (surface gravity) and an ordering (area). Smoothness and discreteness are what the composition leaves, not members: Fig 13 :44 *"smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."*
2. **The question must be split before it is searched.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Score entropy, information and interior separately. A lever that clears one condition leaves the intersections standing.
3. **The firewall conflict is a refutation.** Fig 5 :53: **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"** and Operators :57: **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**. ## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a solution to the black hole information paradox. No choice among unitarity, statistical mechanics and locality is made here.
- Not a derivation of Hawking radiation, the Bekenstein-Hawking area law, holographic entropy, or any microstate model (fuzzball, firewall, condensate, remnant). Those are instance bindings from the physics frame, provenance as such, and ungrounded here.
- Not "the horizon is smooth" and not "the horizon is a firewall." Smoothness is a composition remainder; the firewall is a refutation of a term, not a substance.
- Not grounded. No instrument at a real horizon has been read. Analogue systems are substituted habitats (Fig 11 :70).
- Not a final theory. Fig 7 names the limit of no last room; a terminal account of the horizon is excluded by the framework's own rule.
