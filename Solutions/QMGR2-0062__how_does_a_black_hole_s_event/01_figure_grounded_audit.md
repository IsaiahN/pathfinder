## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.**

- **Arity:** at least three conditions, not one. The classical causal boundary (a null surface separating events that can send signals to infinity from those that cannot), the unitary evolution of quantum theory, and the semiclassical radiation that is supposed to mediate between them.
- **Symmetry:** the horizon bond is directed and threshold-like, not a symmetric join. Passage outward fails while passage inward succeeds. This matters because `⋛` comparison and `→` sequence are ordered bonds, while `+`, `∥`, `≡` are symmetric (Fig 12 bond table).
- **Scale:** first order at the horizon itself (a composite over the six held terms); semiclassical at Hawking's calculation (QFT on a fixed background); second order at the union where both theories meet and neither is background to the other.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the residual (Hawking's calculation and the paradox), acquire vocabulary (temperature, entropy, hair, information, firewall), state the objective (a unitary, information-preserving account), plan which frame to import, then learn and carry. Effort spent on a microscopic model before that link is fixed lands above the break.

**Strain test applied.** A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity and sends us back to the census. Two strains were found and are named rather than smoothed: (i) `⋛` is defined as a comparison between two quantities, but the interesting comparison here is between two settled theories, an operand-type question (see F6 and `06_isomers_and_order.md`); (ii) the word "horizon" names a global causal boundary in GR but a local stretched membrane in complementarity, two different objects that share a name.

So the strongest figure-grounded fact about this question is a limit on our own claim: the framework's contact with black-hole thermodynamics and holography is **retrodiction**, a case fitted after the fact. ### F2 - The horizon is a composition, not a primitive; smoothness and discreteness are remainders

Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

The event horizon is a null surface: a boundary built from where (space, containment of inside and outside) and when (time, the future direction of no return), with surface gravity as a spendable difference and area as an ordering. It is a molecule over the six, not a seventh atom. The firewall question ("is the horizon smooth or not?") asks whether a property that the framework says is a composition remainder is a primitive. Under Fig 13 :44 it is not: smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are what a composition leaves behind.

- **Mode:** `general` structural, triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 12 and the substrate section of `THE_FORMULA.md` (low overlap between the minimum-set argument and the bond argument).
- **Refuter:** derive the horizon, or smoothness, or discreteness as a seventh primitive from the other six; or show the framework must admit a direct state-state bond it cannot refuse.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat-reasoning discipline, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A seventh primitive, or the horizon itself, is derived from the other six; or a direct state-state bond at the horizon is admitted by the framework and cannot be refused (breaks F2).
2. A forward, novel prediction about horizon physics is derived from the six-term set before observation and is then confirmed at the ground (breaks F1, converts retrodiction to prediction).
3. An instrument discriminates candidate microstate accounts at a real horizon, so smoothness or discreteness is settled as a primitive rather than a remainder (breaks F2 and F5).
4. The three firewall postulates are shown mutually consistent, so no refusal is owed (breaks F4).
5. The three sub-questions are shown to share one ground and cannot be unbundled without losing the answer (breaks F3).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
