## What the wider literature contributes

1. **The retrodiction is confirmed from outside.** Wüthrich (2017) argues black hole thermodynamics lacks empirical confirmation and that Bekenstein's information-theoretic argument fails. Harlow (2023) treats the area law and holography as evidence and guiding constraints, not as measured horizon facts. This is low-overlap corroboration of F1: the framework is fitting known cases, not settling them.
2. **The union reading has an outside analogue.** AMPS (2012) exhibits three mutually incompatible postulates. This is the kind of compound Fig 9 :5 says to split rather than search. The figures decide the shape; the physics supplies the example.
3. **The absent anchor is corroborated.** The SEP entry documents that thermodynamic properties of black holes are theoretically robust but not directly observed, and that analogue systems are the nearest thing to a test. Under Fig 11 :70 a test harness proves wiring, never capability at a real horizon.
4. **A conflict is recorded, not hidden.** Harlow's mainstream account takes unitary evaporation as the likely resolution; Wüthrich challenges the epistemic basis of the entropy claim. They share an evidence pool only partly (physics review versus philosophy of physics), so their agreement about the paradox's seriousness is one witness, not two. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) optionally the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## Two-stage offer, stated plainly

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Do not send theory. The recipient checks the output against what they already know, without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links. Their verdict on stage 1 must already be recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they have absorbed the theory their agreement is expected under a shared evidence pool.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a written refuter per problem, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records what the output is and what would break it. The claim is deliberately modest, which is what lets it be checked in twenty minutes.
