## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the figure adds that the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form an answer must have before any microstate is named. The shape is: a cross-kind composite at the horizon; three separable sub-questions; a refusal owed where three held terms conflict; no anchor currently at the horizon; known cases fitted after the fact. "Horizon equals smooth classical null surface" is a name, not a shape. "Horizon equals fuzzball or firewall or condensate" is also a name. The shape stays marked `???` where the ground has not spoken.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For a horizon the available vocabulary is boundary, area, temperature, entropy, information, entanglement, smoothness. The figure's substrate adds space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network. The overlap narrows the shape without naming a microstate.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe what a candidate must do (carry unitarity, reproduce the thermal approximation outside, preserve the no-drama experience where it is observed) rather than what it is made of.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the packet sends effect descriptions and a refuter, not a model.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is earlier: the ground does not pay, so the shape stays open and the lineage is recorded.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming "the horizon is a condensate" is an import from the physics frame. It may be useful, but it adds no atom to the six-term closure and carries zero verdict weight until provenanced and settled.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."* The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one of its atoms as the answer to the horizon slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the microstate question is the source-discrimination error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: the Hawking spectrum and the area law are projections. They do not comment on the source. Several microscopic accounts can produce the same semiclassical reading. Naming a microstate from the thermality reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at a real horizon, not a better argument. Analogue systems do not supply it, since under Fig 11 :70 a test harness is a substituted habitat.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket ban on naming anything at the horizon. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network are all already held. So saying "a structure must sit between the states at a horizon" is not naming a missing primitive; it is reading a held atom from the six-term set. What remains wrong in the physics claims is different and is not retracted: naming a specific microstate from the thermality reading is still the metamerism error, because the reading cannot discriminate sources. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Citing Bekenstein, Hawking, 't Hooft or Susskind to make the horizon claim look settled is the named collapse. The figures themselves list those results as instances fitted after the fact (Fig 8 :56-57). The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried, not endorsement.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* The Architecture article adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* And from *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the horizon claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
