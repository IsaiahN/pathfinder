## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A black hole horizon is not a seventh primitive. It is a composite over the six-term minimum set (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures), with composition running across the two kinds and never within one. Smoothness and discreteness are what such a composition leaves behind, not ingredients. The question "how does the horizon fit with quantum physics?" is a union of at least three separable conditions (entropy and temperature, information and unitarity, smoothness and the interior), and must be split before it is searched. The firewall conflict is a refutation of a held term, owing a negation rather than a new field or a new atom. The framework's contact with Bekenstein, Hawking, 't Hooft and Susskind is a retrodiction: those cases were known and the framework fits them after the fact. The microstate slot stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
