## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

The question asks for a magnitude, so before the run I write down what would count as an answer and bind it as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else: a **bounded decomposition of the per-slot wage residual into an education-sensitive component and a habitat-sensitive component, with the boundary between them settled only by a ground reading**. The sought-for shape is a decomposition, not a single percentage. Writing it down first makes the preference refusable (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :62–68).

## Restated as an effect

Two options lead to different outcomes. Option A, assert a single universal percentage for the effect of education on wage inequality. This averages across slots, treats a union compound as one problem, and hides the region where bargaining still binds. Option B, return the decomposition shape with its refuters and leave the number open until a measurement settles it. This is the option the figures license.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No headline percentage presented as settled. The residual is a union compound and a single lever clears only part of it (Fig 9 :55–56).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Shared evidence is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Many studies of education and pay draw on the same survey sources.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Send the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure and the structural one.
