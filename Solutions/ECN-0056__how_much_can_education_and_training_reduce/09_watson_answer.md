# WATSON answer: how much can education and training reduce wage inequality when jobs and bargaining power are unevenly distributed?

**Mode: `specified` for the decomposition shape, `general` for the structural rule. Not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*) it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request for a magnitude. Option A: report a single universal percentage for the reduction of wage inequality due to education and training. Option B: return a union decomposition shape with an open boundary and four pre-registered refuters. What each leads to: A aggregates away the per-slot signal, treats a union compound as one problem, and invites a `grounded` verdict without a ground reading; B sends a checkable shape, keeps the habitat component's provenance visible, and leaves the number owed to a measurement. Figures decide; world sources only corroborate.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot gap between the wage distribution that would obtain if skill were the only active condition and the distribution actually observed. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54, :57–58), not one hard problem. Symmetry: the conditions are not interchangeable, because training acts on the worker and bargaining institutions act on the job; the bond is ordered rather than symmetric. Scale: first order inside a working life, second order at the distribution.
- **Domain 1, the worker (agent side).** Actors: learners, trainees, training providers. Currency: skill and credential, what a job affords. Anchor: a per-slot skill assessment. Question type: composition inside the worker frame.
- **Domain 2, the labour market habitat (structure side).** Actors: employers, unions, occupations, job queues. Currency: job access and bargaining power. Anchor: a measured wage distribution with a job structure (a market, Fig 2 :29). Question type: enumerated and imported with provenance, not composed from the worker frame.
- **Domain 3, the measurement and verification frame.** Actors: researchers, statistical agencies, instruments. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is low.
- **Domain 4, the lever frame (compound).** Actors: D1 and D2 in contact. Currency: the causal effect of a lever on the residual. Anchor: only D2's anchor can settle it. This is the overlap of D1 and D2, so it gets its own loop.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive jobs (1) → vocabulary, what jobs afford (2, where education acts) → objective, what counts as progress (3, loaded by the habitat) → planning (4) → learn and carry (5). A link that already works cannot be optimised to rescue one that does not (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test applied: the world finding that earnings rise with schooling strains Fig 11 :40 read literally against individual pay. Re-census resolves it: BLS and World Bank figures are per-slot readings inside a fixed habitat, while Fig 11 :40 governs the shape of the distribution when all agents improve and the slots do not change. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. The habitat result is an import with a source gate, not a figure derivation (Fig 6 :23). Both moves are recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The decomposition shape survives; the single number does not. Wage inequality is agent-and-habitat, so education and training reduce it only by changing contact with the job and bargaining structure. The residual is per slot, skill and bargaining form a union, and a training-only lever clears the skill-only region while leaving the intersection standing. Education is upstream-valid but not sufficient, and the boundary between the two components is left open until a ground reading closes it. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The labour-institutions side is a provenanced import, not a figure-derived primitive, and the overbroad sentence "education cannot reduce wage inequality" was refused in the audit and is refused here.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is a shape with four refuters rather than a number, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is handing policy makers one headline percentage for a union compound: that clears the skill-only region, leaves the intersections standing, and then declares the pile solved (Fig 9 :55–59). That cost is avoidable by leading with the decomposition and the refuters (Fig 2 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. The decomposition claim can be withdrawn or re-censused without touching the figures or removing any primitive. The habitat imports carry provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`, so a bad import can be replaced without rewriting the skill side. Nothing sent has to be declared permanently true.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot experiment or credible decomposition holding institutions fixed, showing whether the low-bargaining residual falls, stays flat, or rises; a contact test showing whether training changes reachable slots or only credentials; an intersection test showing whether training plus bargaining coverage moves what training alone left standing; or a demonstration that skill and bargaining have empty intersection, which would turn the union into a partition and kill F3. A derivation of the habitat component from inside the worker frame with no import would also change the verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
