## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22–25**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the decomposition shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27–28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source list.
- **Fig 2 :35**: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong percentage handed to policy makers, so independent checkers are worth the extra effort.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the decomposition shape and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before any theory is shown; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The checker tests the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded first and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem, and must write a refuter before running it (Formula "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1–3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As written here it is a shape with four refuters, not a number. If a number is attached before stage 1, the offer fails on contact (F2 and F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).
