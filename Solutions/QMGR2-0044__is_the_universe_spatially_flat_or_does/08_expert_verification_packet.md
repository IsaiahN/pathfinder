# Expert verification packet : the Tether on cosmic curvature

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on structure, `specified` on the per-domain separators, `gap` on the numeric value. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the empirical question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Space is a state in a six-term set; flatness and curvature are what compositions over that state leave behind, not seventh primitives. The curvature question is constitutively about an anchor, a measurement that must not change because of what any frame thinks, so only a ground reading can decide the value. A global fit near zero with one live curvature slot is a legal state, not an answer, and failure to resolve the slot is not proof of absence. The CMB-only preference for positive curvature and the combined CMB plus BAO consistency with flatness are two overlapping conditions, not two answers, while local curvature does not settle global topology. The framework returns the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument question that would close the gap. It does not return a number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 1

- **Statement:** curvature is a derivative, not a primitive; a near-zero global residual with a live per-slot residual is a legal state.

### C2 : cosmology-facing; checkable against the published readings

- **Statement:** the CMB-only curvature preference and the combined CMB plus BAO flatness fit are a union of overlapping conditions with an unassignable shared fraction, not two answers to one question; the anchor is the measurement, not agreement; global topology is a separate condition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55-56 : *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 2 :36 : *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :40 : *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 8 :50-52 : *"Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.
- **What to check:** do the CMB-only reduction (Di Valentino, Melchiorri and Silk 2019, *"preferring a positive curvature at more than 99% C.L."*) and the combined fit (Planck 2018 VI, *"good consistency with the standard spatially-flat 6-parameter Lambda-CDM cosmology"*; DESI 2024 VI, *"DESI BAO data alone are consistent with the standard flat Lambda-CDM cosmological model"*) use different data combinations and model assumptions? Does zero curvature settle whether space is a three-torus or simply connected?
- **Refuter:** show those reductions draw on identical evidence and identical model assumptions, and that curvature settles topology. Then the union reading collapses to a partition and this sub-claim fails.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A ground reading that resolves the sign and magnitude of `Omega_k` with an error bar excluding zero on one side, from an instrument whose response does not move with the model prior, with covariance and prior provenance recorded. That closes the declared `gap`.
2. A source-discriminating test of the lensing-amplitude excess that distinguishes systematics, new physics and statistical fluctuation (the candidate list in Di Valentino et al. 2019). That would name or refuse the source claim without a figure line being changed.
3. A topological search that settles whether space is simply connected, which is a separate condition from curvature.
