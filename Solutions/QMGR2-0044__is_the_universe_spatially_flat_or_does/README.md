## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that the universe is flat or curved.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **The question is constitutively about a measurement.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Asking for belief instead of a measurement abandons the only anchor the question has.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one together.

## The one-line result

The figures cannot return a value for the curvature parameter. They return a shape and a discipline: curvature is a derivative left by a composition over the space state, not a seventh primitive; the question is constitutively about a ground that must not update; a global fit near zero with a live curvature slot is a legal state, and failure to resolve that slot is not proof of absence. The numeric verdict is a declared `gap`, not a silence.
