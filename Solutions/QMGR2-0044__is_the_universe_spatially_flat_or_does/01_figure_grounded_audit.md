## 0. The question stated as a residual in effect terms

> Is the universe spatially flat, or does it have a small but measurable curvature?

Restated as an effect: an instrument returns a curvature parameter whose interval straddles zero, while a different reduction of the same sky returns a preference for positive curvature. What is being decided is not the meaning of the word flat, but which side of a threshold a measured quantity falls on and whether the instrument can resolve it.

- **Measured effect.** Planck 2018 plus BAO reports `Omega_K = 0.0007 +/- 0.0019`, consistent with flat; the CMB power spectra alone show a lensing amplitude above the base model prediction and, in one reduction, a preference for positive curvature above 99 percent confidence. The sign is unresolved and the magnitude sits at the edge of resolution.
- **R by arity.** One continuous parameter, but two measurement regimes that do not agree, so the *evidence question* is a union of overlapping conditions, not a two-option partition. At the level of the world, flat and curved are a partition (exactly one holds). At the level of what the instruments return, the question is bundled, and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57-58). It must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54).
- **R by symmetry.** Curvature is a scalar under the homogeneity and isotropy assumptions of the FLRW model. Its sign is decided by a threshold relation against zero, not by a conjunction of ingredients. That matters: the Operators table calls comparison *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* and warns *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* The question asks for a threshold decision, so a ground must supply the side of the threshold.
- **R by scale.** Cosmological horizon scale. In the six-term substrate, space is a state; curvature and flatness are what a composition over that state leaves behind, not a member of the set. Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*
- **The live slot.** Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* A global fit consistent with zero is exactly this: a near-zero global reading with one slot, the curvature slot, still live. It is not an inert state and not an answer.
- **What an answer would look like (ordinary term, bound before the run).** A number `Omega_k` with a sign, an error bar small enough to exclude zero on one side, obtained from at least one instrument whose response does not depend on the model prior being tested, plus a recorded account of which evidence streams were independent. This is the shape an answer must have; until it exists the slot stays open and the residual stays live.

**Anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* This question is constitutively about measurement. The anchor is the instrument and the data, never the agreement of readers and never a framework's preference. Anything that moves when priors or reputations push on it is not the anchor (Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*).

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 : The figures cannot return the value of `Omega_k` (decisive for the numeric question)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134-135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"** and **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**

The cosmological curvature parameter is an empirical quantity settled by instruments. The figures are a generator of the shape of an answer at any level; they contain no number, no dataset, and no measurement of the CMB. Running the loop here returns the *kind* of thing an answer must be (a signed, resolved, instrument-grounded value) and the discipline for obtaining it. It does not return the value. Marking the numeric question `gap` is the honest verdict, and it is a verdict rather than silence (Fig 13 :134). Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

- **Refuter:** a figure re-census that derives a specific numerical `Omega_k` from the six terms and their bonds would break this finding. Until that happens, any number in this folder would be an import with no source gate, forbidden by Fig 8 :50-52.

### F2 : Curvature is a derivative left by a composition, not a seventh primitive

Fig 13 :43-44: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."**

Fig 13 :124-125: **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

Flatness and curvature are properties of how space composes with the structures it can bond to. Spatial flatness is a smoothness/ordering result; curvature is a directedness result over the same space state. Neither is a new member of the six. This is a `general` structural claim, triangulated across Fig 13 :43-44, Fig 13 :124-125 and Fig 13 :115 (three separate passages with low internal overlap: primitive minimality, derivative leftovers, and closure). It does not say which sign the derivative takes in our world.

- **Refuter:** derive curvature or the continuum from the other five terms and show it is not a derivative (breaks Fig 13 :41, *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it"*), or show the six-term set admits a seventh member (breaks Fig 13 :115). Either would move this from `general` to refuted.

### F3 : A flat-within-error fit with a live curvature slot is a legal state, not an answer

Fig 1 :13: **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**

Fig 1 :20-21: **"a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one."**

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Symbols table :38-40: **"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."**

The global fit being consistent with `Omega_k = 0` does not retire the curvature slot. The six cosmological parameters are fitted jointly; the curvature slot is one slot among them. When a fit reports near-zero global residual, the figure explicitly preserves the live slot as a legal state. Conversely, quoting only the global fit and dropping the live slot is the aggregation error named in Fig 1 :22. This is why the phrases 'the universe is flat' and 'curvature is unmeasured' are not the same claim.

- **Refuter:** show that under the figures a global near-zero residual with a positive per-slot mass is *not* a legal state, or demonstrate that the curvature slot carries zero live mass at every resolution (in which case the slot retires and this finding narrows, but does not flip the shape).

### F4 : The evidence question is a union, and must be unbundled before a verdict

Fig 9 :4-5: **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54-58: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** and **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

The CMB-only curvature preference and the combined CMB+BAO flatness fit are two conditions, not two answers to one question. They use different data combinations, and the joint fit imports a model (six-parameter flat Lambda-CDM) that the CMB-only reduction does not hold fixed. That is a difference of closure, so the agreement or disagreement is about provenance as much as about geometry. Global topology is a third condition: zero curvature does not settle whether space is simply connected, and a three-torus can be flat and finite. Bundling all three into "flat or curved" is the failure Fig 9 :55 names.

- **Refuter:** show that the two reductions draw on identical evidence and identical model assumptions, in which case they are one condition and the union reading collapses to a partition. The source record (Planck 2018 VI; DESI 2024 VI; Di Valentino, Melchiorri and Silk 2019) shows they do not.

### F5 : Non-detection is not proof of absence, and depth and absence look the same from inside

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

Fig 6 :34-35: **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**

Fig 6 :37: **"It tells a question that nothing could ever answer apart from one that is merely hard. It is not a rule about theory."**

Curvature has registered at some resolution: it is a fitted parameter with an error bar, not a quantity that has never appeared. That makes this the "merely hard" case, not the impossible-in-principle case. The public reference notes that future experiments such as the SKA cannot distinguish flat, open and closed if the true curvature is below `10^-4`, while values above `10^-3` are distinguishable now. That is an instrument edge, not a verdict about the world. Depth (instrument not yet sharp enough) and genuine absence (truly flat) look identical from inside the current frame, and the honest report says so.

- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument whose response to curvature is identically zero at every resolution, in which case the question moves to the underivable-in-principle class and the remedy changes (Fig 8 :40-43). No such instrument is implied by the sources read.

### F6 : The anchor is the measurement, and a channel that depends on the prior is not the anchor

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 2 :30: **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."**

Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."**

Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."**

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Planck 2018 VI states that the CMB spectra prefer higher lensing amplitudes than base Lambda-CDM predicts at over two sigma, and that this pulls parameters away from the base model, "however, this is not supported by the lensing reconstruction or (in models that also change the background geometry) BAO data." That is the channel, not the ground: a reading whose value moves when the background-geometry model changes. The ground is what the instruments return under a stated, checkable model. Di Valentino, Melchiorri and Silk argue that a closed universe removes the internal discordance; that is a frame ranging against the same ground, not a new anchor. The disagreement between the two readings is to be recorded, not arbitrated by counting authors (Fig 2 :40).

- **Refuter:** show that the curvature inference is invariant under the model changes named in Planck 2018 VI and DESI 2024 VI. Then the channel objection narrows, while the `gap` on the numeric value stands.

### F8 : The two evidence streams are not two independent witnesses by default

Fig 2 :31-32: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**

Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Planck CMB and DESI BAO are different instruments, which is a genuine source of independence, but they are interpreted inside a shared model framework and often fit jointly with the same priors and the same acoustic-scale calibration. Two names in a combined likelihood are not automatically two witnesses (Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*). The record of which parts are shared and which are independent is the whole issue. Agreement between a CMB-only reduction and a combined fit is therefore evidence about the union, not a duplicate confirmation of flatness.

- **Refuter:** supply a full covariance and prior provenance showing the streams share no evidence and no model input, in which case their convergence is corroboration at high independence (Fig 8 :50), and the verdict on *this* point changes without changing the numeric `gap`.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

**S1, Fig 13 versus cosmological geometry.** At first pass, "a continuum was the seventh and was removed" seems to conflict with a universe whose space is described as flat or curved continuum geometry. Re-census: Fig 13 governs the *generating set of primitives*, while cosmology governs a *derived geometric property of the space state*. Different objects. Fig 12 :103-104: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* No strain after re-census. Recorded visibly because it was a real first-pass error.

**S2, Fig 1 live-slot legality versus "consistent with flat".** Apparent conflict between "a legal live slot" and a published conclusion that the data are consistent with flatness. Re-census: the figure line governs the *state of the residual*, not the truth of a model; the published phrase governs the *fit*. They describe different quantities and agree: a near-zero global fit with a live slot is exactly the figure's legal state. No strain.

**S3, world/world conflict, CMB-only closed preference versus combined-fit flatness.** This is a conflict between two readings that share the Planck CMB data, so by Fig 2 :40 their disagreement is not two independent witnesses speaking. It is returned to the union census of F4 and kept as evidence for the union verdict. The figure verdict is not flipped by it. Recorded as a live strain in the world sources, not hidden.

**S4, the threshold type of "measurable".** "Measurable curvature" is a threshold on an instrument's resolution (`10^-4` to `10^-3`), while "flat" is a threshold on the parameter at zero. Two thresholds, different currencies (instrument resolution versus parameter magnitude). Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Named, not summed.

**After re-census: one live world strain (S3), one named type distinction (S4), two dissolved (S1, S2).**

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Fig 10 and the derivation discipline, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* I commit to the following before writing the verdict.

The shape claim of this solution is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Primitive test.** A figure re-census derives curvature or the continuum from the other five terms as a member rather than a derivative, or admits a seventh primitive. That breaks Fig 13 :41, :43-44 and :115.
2. **Slot test.** The figures are shown not to permit a global near-zero residual with a live per-slot mass, or the curvature slot is shown to carry zero live mass at every resolution. That breaks Fig 1 :13 and :20-21.
3. **Anchor test.** The curvature question is shown not to be constitutively about a measurement that must not update. That breaks Fig 2 :36 and :45.
4. **Union test.** The CMB-only and combined reductions are shown to draw on identical evidence and identical model assumptions. That breaks the unbundling in F4 and moves the question to a partition.

Separately, an event that is **not** a refutation but an update: a ground reading that resolves `Omega_k` with a sign at a level above the instrument edge would close the declared `gap` and move the numeric question from `gap` to `grounded`. That is the ground doing its job, not the shape being wrong.

No part of this folder may be labelled `grounded` until such a reading exists and is recorded with provenance.
