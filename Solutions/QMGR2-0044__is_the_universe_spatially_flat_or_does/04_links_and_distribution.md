## What the world literature bears on

1. **The two readings share evidence.** Planck 2018 VI reports flatness in the combined analysis and simultaneously reports a lensing amplitude pull above two sigma that is not supported by lensing reconstruction or by BAO when the background geometry is allowed to change. Di Valentino, Melchiorri and Silk read the same spectra as preferring positive curvature above 99 percent confidence. DESI 2024 VI reports BAO alone consistent with flat Lambda-CDM. These are three readings, but the first two share the Planck data and the joint fits share model assumptions. By Fig 2 :40 they are not three witnesses; the record of what is shared is the point.
2. **The sign is not resolved.** The public reference states that measurements constrain curvature close to zero *"although they do not constrain its sign"*, which is the live-slot condition of Fig 1 :13 in world terms.
3. **There is a hard instrument edge.** The same reference says future experiments cannot distinguish flat, open and closed below `10^-4`, while above `10^-3` they can be distinguished now. This is a budget edge, not an underivability result (Fig 8 :40-43). It governs the honest stop.
4. **Curvature is intrinsically measurable.** Gauss's Theorema Egregium is the low-overlap corroborator: curvature is determined by measurements made on the surface, without an external embedding. That matches the figure requirement that the ground be reachable from inside the frame (Fig 10 :29: *"keep the anchor reachable"*), without making any figure line depend on the mathematics source.
5. **Topology is a separate condition.** A flat three-torus can be finite; local curvature does not settle global connectedness. This is the union unbundling of F4 in `01`, corroborated by the public reference, not derived from it.

None of these sources decides the figure verdict. They corroborate the shape claim and supply the world-side record the figures deliberately do not contain.

- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record that verdict; (3) only then the framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, output before theory.** Send the shape claim, its mode and its refuter. The recipient checks it against their own knowledge and records a verdict before seeing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure links and the world sources. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because sharing the material creates one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a generator. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"the output is only as good as the validator... An external validator, or ground, is always needed."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It does not survive if anyone silently imports a curvature number from a world source into the figure answer; the numeric verdict stays `gap` until a ground reading closes it.
