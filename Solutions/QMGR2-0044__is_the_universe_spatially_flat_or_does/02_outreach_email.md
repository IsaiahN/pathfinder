# Outreach draft : check the shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 0 and 5 survive your own check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about curvature, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow output I would like you to check against what you already know about cosmological curvature. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on structure, `gap` on the number):** under a six-term substrate, space is a state, not a primitive "spacetime" and not a primitive continuum. Flatness and curvature are what a composition over that state leaves behind, not additional members of the set. The curvature question is constitutively about an anchor, a measurement that must not change because of what any frame thinks, so only a ground reading can decide it. A global fit near zero with one live curvature slot is a legal state, not an answer, and failure to resolve that slot is not evidence of absence. The framework returns a shape, not a number, so I am not supplying a value for `Omega_k`.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the universe is flat, that it is closed, that a particular published best-fit number settles it, or that "flat" implies infinite extent. The CMB-only preference for positive curvature and the combined CMB plus BAO consistency with flatness are two conditions with an unassignable shared fraction, not two answers to one question, and global topology is a third condition that curvature alone does not fix. I am not asking you to take my word for the provenance of those two readings either; I am asking you to check the shape claim.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. derive curvature or the continuum from the other five terms as a member rather than a derivative, or admit a seventh primitive;
> 2. show that a global near-zero residual with a live per-slot mass is not a legal state under the figures, or that the curvature slot carries zero live mass at every resolution;
> 3. show that this question is not constitutively about a measurement that must not update;
> 4. show that the CMB-only and combined reductions draw on identical evidence and identical model assumptions, which would make them one condition rather than two.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. Please record your verdict on this stage before reading anything else I have written; once you have absorbed the framework your agreement stops being evidence. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file)
> - Figures README and THE FORMULA (local framework files)
> - Planck 2018 VI: https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209
> - DESI 2024 VI: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03002
> - Di Valentino, Melchiorri and Silk 2019: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.02087
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the framework files. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]
