## VERDICT (from the figures)

The numeric question is a declared `gap`: the figures cannot return a value or a sign for `Omega_k`, and importing a published best-fit number into the answer would be an import with no source gate. What survives is the shape. Curvature and flatness are derivative geometric properties, not seventh primitives. The question is constitutively about a non-updating measurement anchor, so only the ground can decide it. A global fit near zero with a live curvature slot is a legal state, not an answer, and non-detection is not proof of absence; at the current instrument edge near `10^-4`, depth and absence look identical from inside the frame. The CMB-only preference and the combined-fit flatness result are overlapping conditions requiring provenance, not two witnesses, and global topology is a separate condition. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. This is `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states four pre-registered refuters and the mode, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a number, or a request for agreement instead of the shape and its refuter; Fig 2 :42 prices that (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*) and it is avoidable by leading with stage 1.
- **undoable:** yes. The numeric gap is already declared, so no primitive is withdrawn and nothing must be retracted if an instrument settles the value. The shape claim stands either way. If one of the four refuters fires, the shape is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded visibly in `01` rather than erased.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument result that resolves the sign and magnitude of `Omega_k` at a stated confidence, with covariance and prior provenance, closes the `gap`. A source-discriminating test of the lensing excess would name or refuse the closed-universe source claim. A figure re-census that derives curvature as a primitive, or that admits a seventh member, would break the shape claim. Agreement among readers, prestige, or a pooled best-fit number is not such a ground.
