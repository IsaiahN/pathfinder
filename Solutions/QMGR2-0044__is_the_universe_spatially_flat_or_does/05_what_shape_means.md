## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, *"a shape, not a name."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* [carried]: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form any answer must take before the number is known: a signed curvature parameter, resolved against a threshold, read from an instrument whose response does not move with the model prior, with the evidence streams recorded. That is a constraint. "-0.0106" or "0.0007" are fillers, and neither is supplied by the framework. `SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar and `Omega_k = ???` here are the same kind of object: a marked slot, not an answer.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* [carried]: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as "a near-zero global fit with an unresolved curvature slot and two partially shared evidence streams", not as "a closed-universe crisis" or "a flatness triumph", which are borrowed cause-labels.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* [carried]: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` does exactly this: it states what would count as a failure of the shape claim, checkable by a cosmologist without reading the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping [carried]: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop comes earlier: at the instrument edge, with the slot still live and the lineage recorded.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For curvature, a ground reading is an instrument result that fixes the sign and magnitude at a stated confidence, with the model dependence recorded.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing a published best-fit number into the figure answer would be an import with no source gate, and by Fig 6 :24 *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*

*They're Scanning Books* [carried] sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Treating space as the state it is under the six-term set is a visible choice; treating flatness as a seventh member would turn a derivative into an invisible one (Fig 13 :43-44).

## 4. Why naming the source of a reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or *"a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered."* The essay's conclusion: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And the resolution is instrumental: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here, the lensing-amplitude excess is a reading. Naming its source as positive curvature, as in Di Valentino, Melchiorri and Silk 2019, is a source claim from a projection for which the published record itself lists several candidate sources: undetected systematics, new physics, or a statistical fluctuation ("Future measurements are needed to clarify", their words). The essay would call that a metamerism error unless a discriminator exists. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. This does **not** refute the closed-universe reading; it marks the reading as a source claim with no discriminator yet. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. Q : what shape does this question need? ANSWERED

**What licenses a particular value or sign?** Nothing in the framework. A value or sign is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the error the README forbids?** It would if it imported `Omega_K = 0.0007 +/- 0.0019` from Planck 2018 VI or the greater-than-99-percent preference from Di Valentino et al. into the figure answer as if the framework returned them. It does not; the numeric verdict is `gap`. What the folder does name is the *shape* (a signed threshold decision over a derivative), which is licensed by Fig 13 :43-44, Fig 1 :13 and Fig 2 :36.

## 5b. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming anything the figures did not hand over verbatim. The canonical phrase is about a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Space is already one of the six (Fig 13 :9-10), and curvature is a derivative left by compositions over it (Fig 13 :124-125). So discussing curvature, or naming it as a derivative property, is not forbidden; it is a binding of the word to a held atom and its compositions. What remains wrong would be:

- importing a numeric value as if the framework produced it (no source gate, Fig 8 :50-52);
- promoting curvature or the continuum to a seventh primitive (Fig 13 :115);
- naming the source of the lensing reading without a discriminator (section 4).

The correction is kept here rather than silently applied. It is the same overstatement the exemplar corrected in its own `05` section 5b, reached here independently from Fig 13 :43-44 and :124-125.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous cosmologists to agree that the universe is flat, or to agree with this framework, is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle the value.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, labelled as such, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of flatness and cannot settle anything it reports on.
