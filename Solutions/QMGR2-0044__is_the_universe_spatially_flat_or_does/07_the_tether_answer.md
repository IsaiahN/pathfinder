# The Tether's answer: flat, or measurably curved?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain separators, `gap` on the numeric value. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An instrument returns a curvature parameter whose interval straddles zero, while a different reduction of the same sky prefers positive curvature; a third condition, global topology, is unsettled by curvature alone. R is indexed per slot: the curvature slot is live even when the global fit is near zero (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22). The evidence question is a union of overlapping conditions, not a two-option partition (Fig 9 :54-58), so it is unbundled before it is searched.

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

**D1, cosmic measurement frame.** The anchor is the instrument and its data. Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* A reading whose value moves when the background-geometry model changes is the channel, not the ground (Fig 10 :24). The two readings share evidence and model assumptions, so they are not two independent witnesses (Fig 2 :40). Verdict: `gap` on the value; the ground requirement is `general` and triangulated (Fig 2 :29-30, :36, :45).

**D2, geometric theory frame.** In GR and FLRW, `k` is a free parameter of the composition; the density ratio decides its sign only in combination with a measured density. The theory frame returns a shape, not which sign nature chose. Inflation drives the ratio toward one, but no figure line selects a value. Verdict: `general` shape, value `gap`; triangulated (Fig 13 :74, :115; Fig 9 :32).

**D3, framework frame.** Curvature and flatness are derivatives left by compositions over the space state, not seventh primitives (Fig 13 :43-44, :124-125). A near-zero global fit with a live curvature slot is a legal state, not an answer (Fig 1 :13, :20-21). Non-detection is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39), and depth and genuine absence look the same from inside the frame (Fig 6 :27). The numeric question is unreached at this instrument budget, not underivable in principle (Fig 8 :40-43). Verdict: `general`, triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 13 and Fig 2.

**D4, verification frame.** Agreement among readers, simulated seats, or a crowd selected for receptiveness cannot supply a measurement. Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 7 :13, Fig 2 :35, :40).

No verdict is averaged across domains. Each one answers a different part of the question, and one lever clearing one domain leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57).

## 3. What the answer explicitly does not say

- Not "the universe is flat." The global fit being consistent with zero is a legal state with a live slot, not a settlement (Fig 1 :13).
- Not "the universe is closed." The greater-than-99-percent CMB-only preference is a source claim from a projection with several listed candidate causes and no discriminator yet (`05` section 4).
- Not a number. No `Omega_k` value is imported from a world source into the figure answer (Fig 9 :32; Fig 8 :50-52).
- Not "flat means infinite." Local geometry does not settle global topology; a flat three-torus can be finite.
- Not a derivation of curvature from the six terms. It is a derivative, and deriving it as a primitive would break Fig 13 :41 and :115.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, and no simulated derivation can settle an empirical parameter.

## 4. What would settle it, and the refuters

**The ground:** a measurement that resolves the sign and magnitude of `Omega_k` with an error bar that excludes zero on one side, from an instrument whose response does not move with the model prior being tested, with the covariance and prior provenance recorded. The public reference puts the current edge near `10^-4` for future experiments and notes that values above `10^-3` are distinguishable now. That is a budget edge, not an underivability result.

**Standing falsifiers of the shape claim (pre-registered in `01` section 5):**

1. Derive curvature or the continuum from the other five terms as a member rather than a derivative, or admit a seventh primitive (breaks Fig 13 :41, :43-44, :115).
2. Show that a global near-zero residual with a live per-slot mass is not a legal state, or that the curvature slot carries zero live mass at every resolution (breaks Fig 1 :13, :20-21).
3. Show that this question is not constitutively about a measurement that must not update (breaks Fig 2 :36, :45).
4. Show that the CMB-only and combined reductions draw on identical evidence and identical model assumptions (breaks the union unbundling of F4 in `01`).

An event that is an **update, not a refutation**: a ground reading that resolves the value closes the `gap` and moves the numeric question to `grounded`. The shape claim would stand.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The measurement chain works through perceiving the acoustic scale and naming the curvature term, and breaks at the objective link where no instrument resolves the sign near `10^-4` (Fig 3 :27; Fig 6 :37). The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* from *They're Scanning Books* [carried in `05`]; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"*: a fit consistent with zero is not a proof that the parameter is zero, and a preference for curvature is not a proof that it is nonzero.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether the universe is flat. It says what any answer to that question must look like. Space is a state in a six-term set, and flatness and curvature are what compositions over that state leave behind, not extra primitives. The question is constitutively about a measurement that must not change because of what anyone thinks, so only a ground reading can settle it; agreement among readers cannot. A global fit near zero with a live curvature slot is a legal state, not an answer, and failure to resolve that slot is not proof of absence. The CMB-only preference for positive curvature and the combined CMB plus BAO consistency with flatness are two overlapping conditions, not two answers, and local curvature does not settle global topology. What the framework offers is the shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument question that would close the gap. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
