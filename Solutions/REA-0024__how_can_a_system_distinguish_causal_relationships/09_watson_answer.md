## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the difference between a correlation that predicts well and a causal relationship. Option A: treat a high-scoring co-variation as causal and compose over it. Option B: refuse to compose over it until a change of contact with the ground has settled whether the effect responds to the cause. What each leads to: A gives a cheap plan that collapses when the co-variation came from a hidden shared source, and it spends the independence of the people checking the claim; B costs an intervention but yields a relation that can be composed over at the next level, with a written refuter and a recorded stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A persistent gap between the predicted effect of setting A and what the ground returns when A is set. Arity: two or more slots plus possible unmeasured common causes. Symmetry: co-variation is symmetric, production is ordered. Scale: one associative frame versus the contact with the ground where an intervention lands. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the associative or predictive frame.** Actors: variables A and B, the fitted predictor, held-out observations. Currency: predictive accuracy and compression. Anchor: none inside the frame; it borrows an anchor it does not touch. This is a frame but not a settled domain.
- **Domain 2, the intervention or habitat frame.** Actors: the setting act, the instrument, A as manipulated slot, B as outcome, and the unmeasured causes of both. Currency: change in B when A is set. Anchor: the experiment or measurement (Fig 2 :29). This is a genuine domain.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers, rival frames, the record. Currency: residuals and independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run seats 1 to 3 draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a union compound and get separate loops. D3 ranges both against the ground.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, vocabulary (symmetric and ordered bonds), objective (residual against the ground under a setting), planning (design the setting), learn and carry (settle, then compose). The chain breaks between vocabulary and objective, where no instrument exists.

Strain test applied: a predictive log can establish sequence but not production. These are two ordered bonds with different tests (Fig 12), so no strain survives re-census. The apparent conflict between "intervention settles it" and Pearl's "causation without manipulation" is resolved by reading intervention as a change of contact (Fig 10, Fig 11), not as a human act.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A well-predicting correlation and a causal relation are different bonds over the same atoms: co-variation is symmetric and frame-scored, causation is ordered production settled by a change of contact. Predictive score is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. Direction is invisible to internal checks, so the intervention prediction must be bound as an ordinary term before the run and read against the ground. More associative search adds no atom, and agreement among correlated predictors is one witness, not two. This is a `specified` procedure and a `general` shape. Whether any named real relation is causal is `gap` here and stays `candidate` until a ground reading settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** if the bond-vocabulary claim is false, a system with only symmetric co-occurrence could still choose correct interventions, and the whole shape would be an unnecessary restriction. If the contact claim is false, better models alone would settle causality and the intervention requirement would waste a budget. The cost is bounded and locatable because the packet states four falsifiers before the conclusion. The larger practical cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is delivered without naming any real variable pair, so no specific causal claim has to be withdrawn if a case fails. A committed prediction can be replaced by a better one, and an imported experimental frame can be swapped for an extended instrument. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already keep the unverified parts out of the claim.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An observational-only direction criterion that works across habitats; a co-variation stable under every intervention the ground can perform; a symmetric-only vocabulary that reliably picks correct targets; or an agent-only improvement that moves the ground would each retract a finding. A source-discriminating instrument that shows B responds to a broken-link setting of A would move the real-case row from `gap` to `grounded`. Prestige or headcount is not such an instrument.
