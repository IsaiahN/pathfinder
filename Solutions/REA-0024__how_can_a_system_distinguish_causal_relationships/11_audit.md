## 1. Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between the predicted effect of setting A and what the ground returns when A is set. Arity: at least two slots plus an unbounded set of possible common causes, so a **union compound** (Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either), not one hard question. Symmetry is the live discriminator: co-variation is invariant under swapping operands, production is not. Scale: one associative frame versus the contact where an instrument acts.
- **Domain 1, the associative frame.** Actors: variables, model, held-out rows. Currency: accuracy and compression. Anchor: none nameable inside the frame. This is a frame that borrows an anchor it never touches, so by the census rule it is not yet a settled domain. Question type: partition boundary (what the frame can write).
- **Domain 2, the intervention and habitat frame.** Actors: setting act, instrument, manipulated slot, outcome, unmeasured common causes. Currency: change under setting. Anchor: the experiment or measurement (Fig 2 :29). Question type: a change of contact, and a union with D1.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: readers and rival frames. Currency: residuals and independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence from domains 1 and 2 is zero.

Order by dependency: perception, then the bond vocabulary, then an objective stated against the ground, then the design of the setting, then settlement and carrying. Refuse the associative verdict before minting a causal term (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a checking frame (Fig 9 :27 to :28).

My boundaries match the primary's three domains and its union reading. **Different starting figures:** I derived the union from Fig 9's split and the direction limit from Fig 5, while the primary derived the bond distinction from Fig 12 and the contact requirement from Fig 10 and Fig 11. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular inside the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
