# Outreach draft : check the residual first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 F9 and the refuter in §4 are stated in the exact form you intend. If the output cannot survive the check, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One claim to check before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already
> know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking
> you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else
> to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A predictor can score very
> well on the data it was fitted on and still be wrong about what happens when
> someone sets the supposed cause. The effect I want to decide between two
> options: (1) compose over the relation and act on A expecting B to change,
> or (2) refuse to compose over it until a measurement shows whether B
> actually responds when A is set. The first gives a cheap plan that collapses
> if the co-variation came from a hidden shared source. The second costs an
> intervention but gives a relation the ground paid for.
>
> **The output, in four lines.** A co-variation is a symmetric relation; swap
> the two variables and nothing changes. A causal relation is an ordered one;
> the effect is absent before the cause fires. A score the model computes for
> itself cannot decide between them, because it is a quantity the frame both
> produces and uses to grade itself. Only a change of contact, an intervention
> whose result the ground returns, settles the direction in a particular case.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming.** I am not claiming this framework
> solves causal inference for any named real pair. It returns a shape and a
> route, not a verdict about a specific dataset. I am not claiming that any
> particular well-predicting variable is or is not a cause here. And I am not
> claiming that randomisation is the only admissible discriminator; an
> instrument extended from a worse one, or an import from a frame whose
> closure already predicts the shape, can do the same work.
>
> **What would refute the output.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a criterion computed from the observational joint distribution alone,
>    with no intervention, no committed prediction and no added assumption,
>    that uniquely identifies direction across habitats;
> 2. a well-predicting co-variation that stays stable under every intervention
>    the ground can perform in a habitat specified in advance;
> 3. a system with only symmetric co-occurrence in its vocabulary that still
>    picks the correct intervention target in a post-hoc habitat;
> 4. an agent-only improvement in discrimination that changes the ground
>    without changing contact.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails
> because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not
> engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the
> authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, verbatim with line numbers (local canonical extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The eight essays, canonical reading of "shape" and the simulation limit: `05_what_shape_means.md`
> - Independent causal-inference sources actually read: `09_watson_answer.md` §WORLD
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** write down what would refute your own
> result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot
> check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is looking for
> is not a term it holds. A reported failure is more useful to me than a
> reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

## Two-stage discipline

Stage 1 is the residual, the shape and the four refuters. Record the reader's verdict on stage 1 **before** showing any framework material. Stage 2 is opt-in only: if they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the reader has now seen the interior (Fig 2 :40). A third stage may offer the formula as a method for the reader's own checked problems.

Nothing in this folder sends anything. Hosting the packet and sending it is yours to do. Prefer a channel that lets a recipient decline contact.
