# Expert verification packet : the Tether on correlation versus causation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a causal-inference researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the procedure, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No dataset is decided here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A co-variation that predicts well and a causal relation are different bonds over the same two atoms. Co-variation is symmetric (swap the operands, nothing changes); causation is ordered production (the effect was absent before the cause fired). A predictive score is a quantity the frame produces and grades itself with, so it is a filter for the budget and not a witness for a verdict. The direction of the relation is not visible to checks that run inside the associative loop; it becomes checkable only when an intervention changes contact with the ground and a prediction committed in advance returns a residual. Independence between checking frames, not the number of agreeing predictors, decides whether agreement means anything. The framework returns this shape and this route. It does not name the variables in any real case and does not replace the established causal calculus.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading the Operators table and Figure 12

- **Statement:** the operator set contains both a symmetric co-occurrence bond and an ordered production bond, and the figure's tests separate them. A correlation is written with the first, a causal relation with the second. A flat ingredient list cannot carry the difference.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :57 *"both hold at once"* and :59 *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*
  - Fig 12 :77 *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* and :79 *"is there a B before A fires? No."*
  - Fig 12 :92 *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."*
  - Operators table :16 *"the only bond in a flat formula, which is why a formula records ingredients and not arrangement."*
- **Refuter:** a system whose relation vocabulary holds only symmetric co-occurrence, which nonetheless selects the correct intervention target in a habitat specified in advance with no added assumption. If that holds, the bond-vocabulary claim is false.

### C2 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 5, 9, 10 and 11

- **Statement:** a score produced by the frame for itself cannot be the verdict, and the direction of the question is not visible to internal checks. The discriminator must change contact with the ground through an intervention whose prediction was committed in advance.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
  - Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
  - Fig 5 :63 *"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"*
  - Fig 5 :65 *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*
  - Fig 10 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
  - Fig 11 :61 *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
- **Refuter:** a criterion computed from the observational joint distribution alone, with no intervention, no committed prediction and no imported assumption, that identifies direction across habitats. Or an agent-only improvement in discrimination that changes the ground without changing contact.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation with a world-facing check; checkable by reading Figures 7, 8 and 2

- **Statement:** more search inside the associative closure cannot add the intervention, and agreement among correlated predictors is not corroboration. Two routes that converge count only if they draw on different evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 8 :32 *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*
  - Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
  - Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **World-facing check:** Vandenbroucke, Broadbent and Pearce, *Int J Epidemiol* 2016, state independently that confidence rises when different data, investigators and methods converge, and warn against reducing causal evidence to one design.
- **Refuter:** a derivation of an intervention calculus purely from the observational distribution with no intervention and no extra assumption; or two frames using the same dataset and the same fitted model counted as two witnesses whose agreement survives a ground test.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict about any named real variable pair. That row is `gap` in `01` F9.
- Not a replacement for the do-calculus, potential outcomes, randomised trials, or any working method of causal inference. Those are the world sources, not the figures.
- Not the claim that only human manipulation counts. The figure requirement is a change of **contact** with the ground, which an unmanipulable cause can meet through an instrument.
- Not that temporal order establishes production. Sequence and production are different ordered bonds.
- Not that correlation is useless. It is a budget filter and a habitat clue, disqualified as a verdict only.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment was run in this folder.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Concretely:

1. Set A by an act whose link to the other causes of B is broken. Does B move as the committed prediction said?
2. Check the effect per slot rather than in a global average.
3. Run two independent routes (different instruments or different frames) and see whether they converge.
4. Apply the four falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. Any one that fires retracts the corresponding claim.
