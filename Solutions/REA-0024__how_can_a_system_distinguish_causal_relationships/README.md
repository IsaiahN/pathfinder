## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The figure-grounded reasons are recorded in `02_outreach_email.md` and `04_links_and_distribution.md`: a list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies a verdict, and a verifier that has already absorbed the theory cannot check it. The legitimate ask is for a refutation, not an endorsement. Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, which is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` to one venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv` at a time. Ask what the reader would count as a failure, record that verdict, and only then offer the sources. A reported failure is more useful than agreement.
