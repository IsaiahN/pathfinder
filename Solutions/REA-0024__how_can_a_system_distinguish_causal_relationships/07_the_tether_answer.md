# The Tether's answer: causal relation versus well-predicting correlation

**Mode: `specified` on the distinguishing procedure, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a decision about any real dataset. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system holds a relation between A and B that predicts well, yet cannot tell whether setting A would move B. Arity: two or more slots plus possible unmeasured common causes. Symmetry: co-variation is symmetric; production is ordered. Scale: one associative frame versus the contact with the ground where an intervention lands. Fig 9 treats this as a **union compound**, not one hard question: "predicts well" and "would change under a setting" are two conditions, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

## 2. The shape

1. **A correlation and a cause are different bonds over the same atoms.** A co-variation is `A + B`: both hold, and swapping the operands changes nothing (Fig 12 :57, :59). A causal relation is `A ⇒ B`: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*, with the test *"is there a B before A fires? No."* (Fig 12 :77, :79). Fig 12 :92: *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."* Over the shared atom set the two readings are isomers (Fig 12 :117).
2. **Predictive score is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A score that merely predicts well is the filter working correctly; it is not evidence of causation.
3. **The direction cannot be checked from inside the associative loop.** Fig 5 :63: *"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION."* The remedy is a discipline: bind the intervention prediction as an ordinary term before the run (Fig 5 :65) and let the ground return a residual against it.
4. **The discriminator changes contact.** Fig 10 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Enumerate the habitat, find what drives both A and B, then set A by an act that breaks the link to those other causes (Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*).
5. **More search inside the frame cannot add the intervention.** Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Formula step 7 separates SEARCHED (same closure, more visits), INWARD (extend the instrument) and OUTWARD (import the experimental frame). The intervention is the instrument extended from a worse one already returning a failing reading; it is not conjured from a description (Fig 6 :34).
6. **Agreement of correlated predictors is not corroboration.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Independence, not headcount, decides whether agreement means anything.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular real relation here is causal or non-causal. That row is `gap` in `01` F9 because no measurement was taken.
- Not that randomised trials are the only admissible discriminator. Any instrument that changes contact, or any import from a frame whose closure predicts the shape, can do the work.
- Not that a temporal order establishes production. Sequence and production are different ordered bonds (`06` Q3).
- Not that correlation is useless. It is a filter for the budget and a first habitat clue. It is disqualified as a verdict, not discarded.
- Not a complete method for causal inference. The figures give the shape of the distinction and the route to settle a case; the world literature supplies the actual calculus.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an experiment in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does B change when A is set by an act whose link to the other causes of B is broken? If yes, the production bond pays; if no, the co-variation was a shared-source reading.
- Is the effect present in the slot where the predictor was strongest, or only in an average? Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- Do two **independent** routes (different data, different instruments, different frames) converge? The epidemiology paper read for this solution states the triangulation rule in its own vocabulary.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: an observational-only direction criterion; a co-variation stable under every possible intervention; a symmetric-only vocabulary that still picks correct targets; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached in shape, unreached in any particular case.** The figures return *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). A search that finds no discriminator in a frame proves no absence (Fig 8 :39). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Nothing here maintains the ground; the ground does not decay, and the channel does (Fig 10 :24).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which variable in your dataset is a cause, and it does not replace the do-calculus or randomised trials. It says what any answer must look like. A well-predicting correlation is a symmetric relation: swap the two variables and nothing changes; it is also a score the model produces for itself, which is a budget filter and not a witness. A causal relation is an ordered production bond: the effect is absent before the cause fires, and the swap makes a different thing. A log can establish order but not production, and more fitting inside the same frame adds no new atom. What settles the direction is an intervention that changes contact with the ground, with a prediction committed in advance and a residual read against it. Two ways of checking that share one evidence pool are one witness, not two. The shape is delivered; the verdict on your dataset waits for the ground.
