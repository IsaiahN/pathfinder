## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not evidence.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
