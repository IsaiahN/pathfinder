## QUESTION

Option B: answer with a constraint-shape that any helpful disclosure must satisfy, with overload and misleading treated as separate failure domains. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a disclosure lets an evaluator range against the ground and what they need to judge reliability. Arity: four overlapping conditions (ground contact, structure, per-slot granularity, channel capacity), so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54, *"unbundle before you search again"*), not one hard question. Symmetry: not symmetric. Timing and direction matter: residual before frame, explanation after expectation violation. Scale: the same loop at the individual evaluator scale (per slot) and the population scale (rooms and institutions), with a lossy transform between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, verification frame.** Actors: evaluator, claim or system, disclosure artifact. Currency: residual `R` per slot, actions spent to check. Anchor: the ground, a measurement, record, outcome or board that does not move when a frame changes its mind (Fig 2 :29). Question type: which disclosures change contact.
- **Domain 2, disclosure-making frame.** Actors: discloser, metrics, explanations, labels, lineage. Currency: bits and actions, two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125). Anchor: the external ground for the claim; the disclosure alone has no anchor. Question type: what a disclosure must contain and what it costs to check.
- **Domain 3, institutional / population frame.** Actors: multiple evaluators, rankings, reputation systems. Currency: agreement, votes, credibility. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35). Question type: when more readers add no evidence.
- **Domain 4, verifier capacity (channel).** Actors: attention and working memory. Currency: cognitive load, bits arriving per unit time. Anchor: measured ground-contacted decision quality, reachable only through an instrument. Question type: where the interior optimum lies. Figures speak of it via channel imagery, so the figure verdict here is single-source.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (channel not saturated), then vocabulary (bonds, not ingredients), then objective (a violable test written before the verdict), then planning (range against ground), then learn and carry (methods, not recordings). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2).

Strain test applied: Fig 12 (structure matters, so disclose more structure) initially strains against Fig 10 (less is better, so disclose less). Re-census resolves it: Fig 12 governs what a disclosure must contain, Fig 10 governs what a channel can carry. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. Likewise Fig 1 (aggregate hides a live slot) and the world's inverted U in total volume govern distribution and total respectively, so both hold.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a ranked catalogue does not. Helpful disclosure changes contact with the ground, stays per-slot, carries bonds and lineage, respects two separate budgets of bits and checking actions, installs something violable, and is checkable from an independent evidence pool. Overwhelm is the channel-and-budget failure: volume rises while ground-contacting actions do not. Misleading is the declared-meaning failure: a label or an unread explanation supplies confidence instead of a discrimination. Perceived trust is not ground contact. The output is a shape, not a name, and the concrete formats remain open until a source-discriminating instrument settles them. This is `specified` and ungrounded; a controlled comparison would be needed to move any part of it to `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion, and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. A wrong shape here is costly because it would be used to design disclosure systems; the four refuters are cheap to run relative to that cost. By Fig 9 :60, filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict must stay separate, and this verdict is not handed over by a filter such as "more pages disclosed."
- **undoable:** yes. Each of the six constraints is separately refutable, and the two harms are separate domains, so one can be withdrawn without touching the others. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already strip the unsupported catalogue and keep the shape. No substrate term or figure line has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled comparison showing a self-scoring disclosure improving ground contact, a single score preserving every live slot, an unread explanation improving evaluation, or quality rising without bound as bits rise would each break the corresponding constraint. A closed, ground-settled enumeration of helpful kinds would break the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`.
