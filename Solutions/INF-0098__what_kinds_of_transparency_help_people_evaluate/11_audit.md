## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual in effect terms: a discloser chooses what to reveal so that another person can judge reliability; the gap is between what the disclosure lets the evaluator range against the ground and what they need to judge reliably.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four overlapping conditions (contact, structure, per-slot granularity, capacity), so a **union compound**, not a scalar. A single lever clears the singletons and leaves the intersections. Symmetry: not symmetric; timing and direction matter. Scale: individual evaluator per object slot, and population of evaluators, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, verification frame.** Actors: evaluator, claim, disclosure artifact. Currency: per-slot residual and checking actions. Anchor: a measurement or record that does not move when a frame changes its mind (Fig 2 :29). Question type: which disclosures change contact.
- **Domain 2, disclosure-making frame.** Actors: discloser, metrics, explanations, labels, lineage. Currency: bits and actions (two currencies). Anchor: the external ground for the claim; the document alone anchors nothing. Question type: what a disclosure contains and what it costs to check.
- **Domain 3, institutional frame.** Actors: readers, rankings, reputation. Currency: agreement and credibility. Anchor: ground, never agreement. Independence here is near zero in any single-pool deployment.
- **Domain 4, verifier capacity.** Actors: attention, working memory. Currency: cognitive load. Anchor: measured decision quality (an instrument). The figures reach it only by channel imagery, so its figure support is single-source.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a disclosure frame (Fig 9 :27); install the objective (a violable test) before expecting planning to work (Fig 3); refuse a misleading disclosure before minting a new one (Formula step 2); carry methods, not recordings, across scales (Fig 4 :22-25).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 10, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's two-currency rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries, so the agreement is not purely circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool, so it carries no independent weight.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-27, Fig 10 :18-28, Fig 12 :5 and :125-127, Fig 9 :32.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: helpful disclosure contacts the ground, stays per-slot, is structured, respects two budgets, is violable, and is independently checkable; overload is a bits/actions confusion; misleading is a declared meaning.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete decision a transparency team faces, for example which fields to publish in a model card and whether to add an explanation feature.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-condition independence requirement** collapses easily into "publish more fields," which raises bits without touching contact;
- the **exact violability test**, which the shorthand names but does not specify for any particular deployment;
- the **two-currency separation**, which deployment teams routinely merge into a single "transparency score," the exact error Fig 12 :125 forbids.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a disclosure that only reports frame-internal quantities cannot be the metric. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no controlled comparison exists in this run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the condensed formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
