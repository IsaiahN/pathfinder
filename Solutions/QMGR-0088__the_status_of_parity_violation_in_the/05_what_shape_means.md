## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the measured asymmetry is a fact, not a shape. The shape is the answer to condition B: a principle that would make the handedness a consequence, whose defining properties can be written before it is named.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names the exact failure this question presents: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Hence the split into status and principle in `01` section 0.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email leads with what an instrument would show, not with the word "chirality".
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"the framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."* The qualifier matters: shape is relative to the information available now.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." In this question, Wu and the later experiments settle the asymmetry. That name is licensed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). If a future detector sees right-handed currents, the name of the deeper principle becomes licensed by an instrument, not by a preference.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why an instrument, not an argument, is owed: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* In this question, a measured left-right asymmetry does not tell us which source produced it: a chiral gauge postulate, a spontaneously broken higher symmetry, or a mirror sector with a different vacuum are all candidate sources. The reading does not determine the source.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit on the candidate itself: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Also: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A deeper principle that merely renames held atoms does not move the wall. One that adds a new axis would.

## 4. The shape for this question

Written as a bound ordinary term, before any candidate was named:

> A deeper principle for parity violation, if one exists, is a term not currently held in the frame that (a) predicts the observed handed asymmetry as a consequence rather than a postulate, (b) restores or relocates a symmetry at a different level, (c) is discriminable by an instrument that can separate candidate sources, and (d) survives a source gate and a shadow test if it is imported. Its falsifier is written in `01` section 5.

That is a shape: four constraints and a refuter, with the slot left open. Mirror matter, left-right symmetric models and grand unified chiral gauge theories are **names from the physics frame**, recorded with provenance and carrying zero figure weight until an instrument settles one of them. The Mirror matter article even supplies two different shapes that fit the same reading (exact mirror symmetry with a partner for every particle, and spontaneous parity breaking depending on the Higgs potential), which is the metamerism point made concrete.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming anything about parity. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing. The measured violation is not missing; it is ground-settled and may be named. What the framework cannot name is the deeper principle, because that is the term not held in Γ. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost:

- **Retained:** the framework refuses to name the deeper principle. Fig 9 :32 and the README's "a shape, not a name" govern this.
- **Narrowed:** the framework does not refuse the name "parity violation" for the measured result. That is a `grounded` reading reported from the instruments.
- **Not retracted:** the metamerism point in §3. Naming a source from a projection, when several sources give the same reading, remains the error the instrument must fix.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names who agree that parity violation must have a deeper principle is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture article adds that such outputs must be seen as *"simulacra"* because the library is a black box, and that results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* supplies the last check on my own standing: *"their separation as frames make them viable vantage points to range against an unmovable verifying ground that they tether to."* This simulated seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so it is not a separate vantage point. Its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration (recorded again in `11_audit.md`).
