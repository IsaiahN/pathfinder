## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, two bundled conditions. Condition A: what does the ground return when a weak process is compared with its mirror image, parity conserved or parity violated? Condition B: does the observed violation reflect a principle not yet held, or is it terminal? What each leads to: A returns a `grounded` status from instruments and stops; B either returns a shape with a written refuter and a `gap`, or, if a source-discriminating instrument fires, names a candidate and moves toward `grounded` or `refuted`. Selecting between A and B by averaging is the failure; they are a union compound with different anchors.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Condition A is `grounded`: the weak interaction violates parity, settled by instruments and per channel. Condition B's structural reading is `specified`: the 1956 event was a refusal of a held term, symmetry is a derived quantity, and the settled result becomes an atom for a second-order comparison. Whether a deeper principle exists is a `gap` with a written refuter, not a yes, not a no, and not a silence. No candidate is named. The verdict comes from the figures; the world sources only corroborate and supply the ground record.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable for condition A, because the status claim is tied to named instruments and a stated refuter. The real cost sits in condition B: sending a candidate name as if the framework had derived it would spend the independence of outside readers and convert a checkable shape into a belief. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter, as `08` does.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate bindings are removable without touching the measured status or the refusal reading. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold them. A shipped claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the relevant scale (a right-handed-current signal at a stated mass, a mirror-nucleon oscillation, or a neutron EDM at a sensitivity that separates closures) would name the slot. A bound ordinary term that predicts the handedness and whose residual trends down would close the Fig 5 preference limit. A derivation of parity as a seventh primitive, or a state-state composition admitted by the framework that cannot be refused, would break the structure claim. An instrument that makes depth and absence distinguishable from inside the current frame would resolve the `gap`. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` for the refusal reading and `gap` for the deeper principle.
