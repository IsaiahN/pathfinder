## The question restated as an effect

Two conditions are bundled in one sentence, so they are split before either is searched (Fig 9 :54, Library of Babel: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?").

1. **Status.** What does the ground return when a weak process is compared with its mirror image? Option A: the rates match, parity is conserved, left and right are conventional labels. Option B: the rates differ, parity is violated, the weak interaction supplies an operational definition of left and right. Each option leads to a different instrument record.
2. **Deeper principle.** Is the observed violation a terminal fact, or a consequence of a term not yet held? Option A: it is a brute refusal with no further composer. Option B: it is a derivative of a higher-level symmetry broken at low energy. Each option leads to a different search: stop, or build a discriminator.

An answer would look like a two-part verdict with its own mode on each part, plus an instrument that would change the second part if it fired. That shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's Direction section.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no receptiveness ranking. Selecting readers for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40, and the project's Seat Map rule that high correlation disqualifies a verdict).
- No bulk-send plan, and no claim that agreement from a list would count as a check.
- No assertion that the Tether names the deeper principle. It returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).
- No claim that the simulated derivation is a measurement. "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened 2026-11-23).
