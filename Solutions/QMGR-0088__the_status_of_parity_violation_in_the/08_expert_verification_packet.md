# Expert verification packet - parity violation and the deeper-principle question

**What this is:** a claim in a form an experimentalist or phenomenologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Modes:** `grounded` on the measured status, `specified` on the refusal reading, `gap` on the existence of a deeper principle. No instrument is claimed for the last part.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Parity violation in the weak interaction is a ground-settled result, established by independent instruments from Co-60 beta decay onward. Its precise structural reading is that parity conservation was a held, predicting term that was refused in 1956, not a term that was missing, so what the record gained was a negation and what it lost was the standing of that term to be composed over. Symmetry is a derived quantity, not a primitive in the six-term substrate. Whether the violation reflects a deeper principle is not settled: the framework returns a shape, not a name, and from inside the current closure a deeper symmetry and genuine absence look the same. What is offered is the shape such a principle would have, written refuters, and the instrument classes that would close the question.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal and world-facing; checkable by reading Fig 2 and the experimental record

- **Statement:** the status of parity violation is settled by a measurement that does not move when a theory changes. It is `grounded`, and it is triangulated across different instruments.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Also Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."

### C2 - The structural reading; checkable by reading Fig 5, Fig 6 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** the 1956 event belongs in the REFUTED bin. Parity conservation was bound and predicting before the experiment. The record gained a refusing term; nothing was deleted; the reach fell because something was added. The violation is a negation, not a subtraction and not a missing force.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Fig 6 :42-43 "A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted." - **What to check:** was parity conservation actually a bound, predicting term before 1956 (so the bin is WRONG, not MISSING), and does the figure's reach rule describe what happened to its composability? The historical record in the Wu and Parity articles supports the first clause.
- **Refuter:** show that parity conservation was never bound and predicting in pre-1956 physics, so the residual was NOVEL rather than REFUTED; or derive parity as a seventh primitive of the six-term substrate; or show the framework admitting a state-state composition it cannot refuse. Any of these breaks the reading and the claim is withdrawn.

### C3 - The deeper-principle limit; checkable by reading Fig 5, Fig 8 and Fig 6

- **Statement:** whether the violation reflects a deeper principle is a `gap`, not a yes or a no. The sought-for principle is a preference held as a shape, unbound and predicting nothing, so the mint pipeline cannot see it. A frame cannot reach its own metaframe, and depth and absence look the same from inside.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :61-62 "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term." Fig 8 :34 "A frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Godel's second theorem: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency." Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :27 "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
- **What to check:** is the claimed limit a metaframe claim (underivable from inside) rather than a claim that no deeper principle exists? The mirror-matter and spontaneous-breaking proposals are live shapes precisely because the limit is not a prohibition.
- **Refuter:** an instrument that discriminates among sources at the relevant scale (a reproducible right-handed-current signal at a stated mass, a mirror-nucleon oscillation, or a neutron EDM at a sensitivity that separates closures); or a bound ordinary term that predicts the handedness as a consequence and whose residual trends down; or a demonstration that depth and absence are distinguishable from inside the current frame. Any of these closes or breaks the gap and the claim is revised in that direction.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that parity violation is unexplained. The measured status is `grounded`.
- Not that a deeper principle exists, and not that one does not. Fig 8 :39 forbids the second as a verdict from a null search.
- Not that mirror matter, left-right symmetry or a grand unified theory is the answer. They are candidate imports with recorded provenance and zero verdict weight until settled.
- Not a derivation of the weak interaction from the six-term substrate. The chiral gauge structure is a binding from the physics frame.
- Not `grounded` for the deeper-principle part. No instrument currently discriminates the candidate sources.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 names "the limit: no last room"; a terminal deeper principle would end the handoff.
- Not a ground reading. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding instrument classes are:

1. Direct searches for right-handed charged currents at a stated mass scale, or a positive signal with a reported significance and a controlled background.
2. Neutron-to-mirror-neutron oscillation or mirror-matter dark-matter searches that return a signal, or a null result with a sensitivity that covers the predicted range.
3. A neutron electric dipole moment at the sensitivity that separates candidate closures.
4. A bound ordinary term predicting the handedness as a consequence, with a residual that trends down under one of the above.
