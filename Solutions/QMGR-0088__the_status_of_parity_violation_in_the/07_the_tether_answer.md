## 5. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that parity violation is unexplained. The measured status is `grounded`; only the deeper-principle part is a `gap`.
- Not that a deeper principle exists, and not that one does not. "A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that mirror matter, a left-right symmetric model or any grand unified theory *is* the principle. Those are imports with provenance, `candidate` at best.
- Not a derivation of the weak interaction from the six-term substrate. The chiral gauge structure is a binding from the physics frame.
- Not `grounded` for domain 3. No instrument currently discriminates the candidate sources.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 names "the limit: no last room"; a terminal deeper principle would close the handoff.
