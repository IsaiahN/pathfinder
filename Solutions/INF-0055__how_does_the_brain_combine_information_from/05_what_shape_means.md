## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

From *The Library of Babel*, full text opened: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill in the numbers. It constrains; it does not fill.

For INF-0055 the shape is:

> conflict between senses is resolved by a decision about source structure (one cause or two) taken before fusion or segregation, with per-cue reliability setting the weights inside the fused regime.

That is a shape. "The brain runs a two-cause mixture model with prior p_common = 0.28 and sigma_V = 2.14 degrees" is a name with numbers attached, and those numbers belong to a particular experiment, not to the framework.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*, full text opened: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Start with the terms you have (cue, weight, conflict, disparity, event) and look for recurring terms that correlate with them (reliability, common cause, unity judgment, segregation). Recurse.
- The same essay on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly the Fig 9 union/partition point: the one-cause case and the two-cause case must be searched separately.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` is this rule applied: send the predicted regimes, not the theory.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A good model fit is a gate, not the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applied here: the label "Bayesian causal inference" is licensed by Körding et al. 2007 and Shams et al. 2005, who fitted and tested it. It is not licensed by the figures. The figures license the shape (decide union/partition, keep per-slot residuals, range against a ground). Where we borrow the algebra, the record says so.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The causal decision is a new dimension for a forced-fusion model; it is not a new coefficient inside one.

## 4. The source problem is the metamerism problem, and it is this question's sharpest essay link

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, full text opened, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*.

This maps directly onto multisensory conflict. The percept reports "one event at location X" or "two events". That reading does not determine the source. One physical event observed by two senses and two independent events that happen to align can produce the same percept. The *"spectrometer"* here is the experiment that discriminates source: a dual-report paradigm with causal structure manipulated independently of cue reliability (Körding et al. 2007). The essay's own formulation: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

So an answer that names a weight from a percept alone commits the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a larger model.

## 5. Q answered: what licenses "causal inference" as the name here?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. The specific variable C (one cause versus two), the inverse-variance weighting, and the fitted prior are bound from world sources, provenanced as such. Until a ground settles a particular instantiation, the number is the experimenter's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule when I wrote that the framework cannot name any specific mechanism. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Here the causal decision is an ordinary bound term from the world sources, not a new primitive the figures are being asked to invent. What remains wrong is calling that binding figure-derived. It is an import with a source gate, not a derivation. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach and status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous names who agree is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the experiment decide.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
