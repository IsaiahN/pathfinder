# The Tether's answer on conflicting senses

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive shape, `general` on the structural form, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a new result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two senses report incompatible estimates of one world. The percept that comes out is a projection: it does not say whether the cues came from one source or two. The question is how the brain gets from two conflicting readings to one percept or two. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The per-sense disagreement is the live signal, not noise to be averaged away.

## 2. The shape

1. **Conflict is a union of two conditions, not one hard average.** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. The one-cause case and the two-cause case need different rules.
2. **A decision about source structure comes first.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Each sense is a frame; the distal event and its causal structure are the anchor. If the cues share one cause, fuse them. If they have two causes, keep them separate. This is the brain's answer to Fig 2's collapse 2: *"so the agreement is the pool talking,"* (Fig 2 :20) *"not two frames confirming each other"* (Fig 2 :21).
3. **Inside the fused regime, weights track precision.** Degrade vision and the visual weight falls (Ernst and Banks 2002). The weakest individual cues give the largest multisensory gain (Meredith and Stein 1986; Stein and Stanford 2008). So there is no universal modality hierarchy: vision dominates spatial size, audition dominates temporal event count (Shams et al. 2000; Hirst et al. 2020). This part is world-sourced and `specified`, not figure-derived.
4. **The union has a surplus only because the closures differ.** Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* The blend is the collapse (Fig 7 :14 *"blending: novelty swamped to the mean"*).
5. **The combination is a priced bond, not a list of cues.** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The causal relation is the information the two estimates do not carry by themselves.
6. **Development and habitat are in the answer.** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*: multisensory integration develops with cortical input and cross-modal experience.

Per-domain verdicts, kept separate and never averaged:

| domain | verdict | status |

| D1 modality frames | keep per-slot residuals, never a global average | triangulated (Fig 1, Fig 2) |

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the brain literally runs Bayes, and not that any particular fitted prior is a law.
- Not that vision always wins, or that there is one fixed dominance hierarchy.
- Not that the figures derived the precision-weighting algebra. That is a provenanced import.
- Not a complete neural implementation. Körding et al. 2007 state: "A neural implementation of optimal causal inference will be an important step towards a complete neural theory of multisensory perception."
- Not `grounded`. No new measurement was taken, and the six-term physical substrate (Fig 13) does not govern perceptual frames (see audit F9).

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From the Hard Problem essay: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; an instrument that cannot distinguish cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. Here the discriminators are:

- **Dual-report with independent C manipulation:** vary causal structure and cue reliability separately; measure fusion versus segregation and the weights. Large-disparity, two-source trials that still force fusion would fire refuter 1.
- **Reliability test:** degrade one cue and check whether its weight falls. Flat weights fire refuter 2.
- **Unity judgment test:** ask whether the observer perceives one cause or two, and compare with the causal-inference prediction. Systematic misclassification fires refuter 3.
- **Neural test:** population recordings in multisensory areas during conflict, to see whether the decision variable is represented before the fused estimate.
- **Developmental test:** cross-modal experience versus single-modality rearing in the same circuit.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the available instruments stop discriminating source. Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The psychophysical paradigms are those instruments, improved from earlier ones; they are not the ground itself. The record of which figure line governs which domain, which transition was re-censused (F9), and which refuters are live is what makes the stop checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> When the senses conflict, the brain does not simply average them. The percept reports an estimate, but it does not say whether the two cues came from one event or two, so the system first has to decide that. If the cues share one cause, it fuses them with weights set by how reliable each cue is; if they have two causes, it keeps them separate. Degrading one sense lowers its weight, and the weakest cues show the biggest multisensory gain, so there is no fixed hierarchy in which vision always wins. The figures decide this shape: keep the per-sense disagreement alive, range each sense against one world, treat agreement from a shared source as one witness rather than two, and unbundle the one-cause and two-cause cases before searching. The algebra and the fitted numbers come from the psychophysics literature, are recorded as imports, and are not claimed as figure derivations. What would close it is a source-discriminating experiment that varies causal structure independently of reliability, not another argument.
