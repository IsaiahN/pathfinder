## 0. The claim under audit and the residual in effect terms

**Question as given:** "Causal set theory and whether causality alone can generate the structure of spacetime."

**Stated as an effect.** Let `O` be the causal order (the before/after relation over discrete events, a locally finite partial order). Let `G` be the full structure of spacetime (adjacency and dimension, the conformal geometry, the volume element, hence the metric). The question asks whether `O` alone suffices to generate `G`.

- **Option A (strong causal metric thesis):** `O` on its own generates `G`. The order is a complete generator; nothing outside it is owed.
- **Option B (order plus something):** `O` generates part of `G`, and a second quantity, the counting of elements (number, volume), must be supplied from outside the order itself. The field's own slogan *"Order + Number = Geometry"* (Sorkin, source F-SORKIN in the ledger) already gestures at B.

**What each leads to.** A makes the order a closed generator and would require the figures to permit one kind to produce another. B makes causality necessary but insufficient, and owes an imported magnitude. The two do not average into a middle answer: Fig 9 :54 says a union must be unbundled, not blended.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term).** An answer is a verdict on the composability claim: either the order is in `closure(O)` sufficient for `G`, or it is not and a missing atom with a specified effect (a magnitude that fixes volume, supplied per region) is owed. Bind that sought-for shape as `X = a quantity that supplies volume to the order`. It then accrues a residual like anything else and is refusable (Formula "Direction"). The framework cannot name `X` as a primitive; it can only give its effect.

## 2. Findings

### F1: Ordering does not produce adjacency (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 13 :42:

> **"Ordering does not produce adjacency; duration does not produce conservation; a quantity does not produce"**

And in the same line:

> **"There is no route."**

Fig 13 :41:

> **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**

Fig 13 :75:

> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Causality in a causal set is the order: duration, precedence, the ordered succession of differences. Space is adjacency (Fig 13 :10: *"near, far, between, contained"*). The figures say there is no route from the first to the second, and that a direct bond between the two states is not composable. So a claim that the order alone generates spatial structure fails at the composition rule before any physics is considered.

Triangulation status: Fig 13 :41-42 (indivisibility, no route) and Fig 13 :75 (cross-kind rule) are two figure lines with low overlap (one governs the primitives, one governs the bond rule), so **triangulated**. Refuter: derive adjacency from the order alone inside the six-term closure, or exhibit a state-state bond the framework admits and cannot refuse. Fig 13 :143: *"Indivisibility and minimality are claims and both are refutable: derive a member from the other five, or derive one of the established laws without a member the table says it needs."*

### F2: A structure with no state is empty (single-source, sharpened by re-census)

Fig 13 :29-30:

> **"A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes."**

Fig 13 :33:

> **"Neither kind generates anything alone. Neither denotes anything alone. That is why they are two kinds rather than one list."**

A bare partial order is a structure (network plus ordering). Without the events as states and their cardinality, it is an ordering over nothing. Causal set theory's own definition already includes the elements, but the question as posed asks whether *causality alone* (the order) can do the generating. Read strictly, that is structure without states. The framework's answer is that it cannot denote anything on its own.

Single-source (Fig 13 :29, :33) within the figures; the world source F-DRIBUS corroborates from mathematics (a discrete measure μ is needed alongside ≺). Refuter: generate any geometric magnitude from an order with no elements and no counting measure.

### F3: The continuum is not a primitive; this part of causal set theory is figure-supported (triangulated)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

Fig 13 :124:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

Causal set theory's stance that the continuum is an approximation and the discrete order is fundamental matches the figures: the continuum was tried as a seventh member and rejected because it is *a spectrum without gaps*, a derivative of members already held. Discreteness is likewise a derivative of the arrangement, not a seventh member. The framework therefore supports the *emergence* half of the causal set program, and refuses to treat the continuum as an atom.

Triangulated across Fig 13 :43-44 and :124 (different passages, one about the generating set, one about what compositions leave behind). Refuter: show the figures admit the continuum as a member of the minimum set, or derive a seventh member from the six.

### F4: The missing quantity is an import, not derivable from the order (triangulated)

Fig 6 :21:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 13 :115:

> **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

If the order is all you hold, number/volume cannot be composed out of it, because composition never adds an atom. The counting measure is supplied by a frame whose closure differs (the physics frame, which identifies element count with volume). This is the figure-grounded reading of *"Order + Number = Geometry"*: order (a held structure) plus number (an imported atom) compose into geometry. Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall."*

World corroboration is strong and low-overlap with the framework: F-SURYA (Surya, 2019) says discreteness *"supplies the missing conformal factor, or the missing (1/10)th of the metric, in d=4"*; F-DRIBUS (Dribus, 2013) says *"the only obstruction to the recovery of pseudo-Riemannian spacetime geometry from causal structure is a lack of appropriate data about scale; i.e., a missing conformal factor."*

Triangulated across Fig 6 :21, :23 and Fig 13 :115. Refuter: derive the counting measure from the order with no import and no added atom.

### F5: The magnitude must be per region, not a global average (single-source plus physics)

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22:

The volume must be assigned locally (per Alexandrov interval), matching both the local conformal factor and the causal set's local finiteness. Single-source within the figures (Fig 1), corroborated by the physics frame (the conformal factor is local). Refuter: show a global-only volume assignment recovers the local metric.

### F7: Strain: discreteness as axiom versus discreteness as derivative (named, re-censed)

Causal set theory takes local finiteness (discreteness) as a fundamental axiom (F-SURYA: *"local finiteness encodes an intrinsic discreteness"*). Fig 13 :124 says discreteness is *"what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* These pull in opposite directions.

Re-census resolves the location, not the metaphysics. The causet *elements* are states (fundamental in both readings); *discreteness* is a property of how those states are arranged (derivative in the figures). The two statements can be true at different levels. What remains genuinely open is whether nature's fundamental level is discrete at all: F-JAKSLAND (Jaksland and Linnemann, 2024) notes *"it arguably remains undecided whether spacetime is discrete, though there is (theoretical) evidence that it might be."* The figures cannot settle an empirical question with no registering instrument (Fig 6 :39: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."*).

Status: **strain, recorded, not hidden.** Sent to `Logs` via this file and to `11_audit.md` §5.
