# Expert verification packet: causality alone and spacetime structure

**What this is:** a claim in a form a causal set theorist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape; not `grounded`.** No measurement at the Planck scale or continuum limit is claimed.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The figures' minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), and composition runs across the two kinds and never within one. Causal precedence is an ordering, and ordering does not produce adjacency, so the strong claim that causality alone generates spacetime structure fails at the composition rule. What the order does supply is conformal geometry, and even that only over event-states and for a limited class of spacetimes; the volume element is a separate magnitude that composition inside the order's closure cannot add. The framework therefore agrees with the causal set program that the continuum is emergent rather than primitive, and refuses only the strong reading of "alone". It returns a shape (event-states, order as structure, an imported per-region magnitude, provenance) and written refuters, not a new derivation of geometry.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13
- **Statement:** ordering cannot produce adjacency, and a direct bond of two states is not composable. Causal order alone cannot generate spatial structure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :42 *"Ordering does not produce adjacency"* and *"There is no route."* Also Fig 13 :75 *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* And Fig 13 :41 *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."*
- **Refuter:** derive adjacency from the order alone inside the six-term set; or exhibit a admitted state-state bond the framework cannot refuse; or show one of the six is composed from the other five. Any fires the claim.

### C2: physics-facing; checkable against the causal set literature
- **Statement:** the order supplies the conformal geometry but not the volume; a counting magnitude is owed from outside the order. The field's own sources say the same.
- **Source, verbatim (figures):** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- **World check:** Sorkin (Einstein Online, 2006) *"the order carries 9/10 of the information and the number 1/10"*; Surya (Living Reviews in Relativity, 2019) discreteness *"supplies the missing conformal factor, or the missing (1/10)th of the metric, in d=4"*; Dribus (arXiv, 2013) *"the only obstruction to the recovery of pseudo-Riemannian spacetime geometry from causal structure is a lack of appropriate data about scale; i.e., a missing conformal factor."*
- **What to check:** are those quotes faithful, and is the missing conformal factor genuinely not derivable from the order? If a theorem shows order alone fixes volume at the relevant scale, C2 fails.
- **Refuter:** derive the counting measure from ≺ with no import and no added atom; or a measurement showing order alone recovers the full metric.

### C3: framework-internal plus philosophy-facing
- **Statement:** the continuum is a derivative and not a primitive, so discrete-first emergence is figure-supported; but a conflict from philosophy warns that order-only grounding fails for spacelike relations.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43-44 *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* Fig 13 :124 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*
- **World check (conflict):** Jaksland and Linnemann (Erkenntnis, 2024) *"the same continuum spacetime will be approximated by many different causets"* and, of the proposed spacelike-distance ground, *"the distance between every two spacelike separated events approximately vanishes."*
- **Refuter:** show the figures admit the continuum as a seventh member; or show that order-only grounding does recover spacelike distance without the failure reported in the philosophy source (that would remove the conflict, not the figure claim).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that causal set theory is false; the framework endorses discrete-first emergence.
- Not that the order is unnecessary; it is necessary, just insufficient.
- Not that the counting measure is derived by the framework, or that number is a new framework primitive.
- Not that the Hauptvermutung is proved; Surya reports *"there is no full proof of the conjecture."*
- Not that spacetime is discrete; that is an open empirical question.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Conformal-factor test:** an instrument or theorem discriminating the volume element at the order's scale. If the order alone fixes it, C2 reverses.
2. **Adjacency test:** a derivation of adjacency from ordering inside the six-term set. If it works, C1 reverses.
3. **Ground reading:** any Planck-scale or continuum-limit measurement of whether discreteness is fundamental. This would close the F7 strain.
4. **Proof of the Hauptvermutung:** would update the status report in C3's world check, without changing the figures' no-last-room limit.
