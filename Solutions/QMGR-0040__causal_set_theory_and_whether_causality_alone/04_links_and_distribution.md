## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The field's own answer is already "no, not alone."** Sorkin (Einstein Online, 2006): *"the order carries 9/10 of the information and the number 1/10. Together they add up to 10/10."* Surya (2019) and Dribus (2013) say the same thing in theorem language: the conformal factor is the missing tenth. This is the strongest external corroboration for the figure finding F4. Do not present it as a disagreement with causal set theory. The disagreement is only with the *strong* reading of "causality alone".
2. **A genuine conflict from philosophy.** Jaksland and Linnemann (2024) argue the grounding story fails for spacelike relations and that CST recovers only a subset of GR spacetimes (those that are future and past distinguishing, no closed timelike curves). This is the one source returned to the census rather than used as corroboration. It sharpens F1: a relation built only from the order has nothing to say about spacelike separation, which is the *absence* of order.
3. **The Hauptvermutung is open.** Surya: *"there is no full proof of the conjecture."* Do not describe the reconstruction as a theorem. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
4. **Discrete first is figure-consistent.** The figures removed the continuum as a seventh member (Fig 13 :43-44). This is the part of the causal set program the framework endorses, and it should lead the exchange so the recipient sees the agreement before the refusal.
