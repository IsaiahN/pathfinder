## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the three checkable sub-claims and the refuter; offer sources after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :35**: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement and do not select recipients for likely agreement (Fig 2 :40).

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three sub-claims and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which slot they would measure; (3) only then the Web of Life database and the three open-access papers, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim, its refuter and the per-slot question. Ask for a verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figure text and the sources above. Their agreement at this stage is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode (from `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" and "Mode").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. As currently drafted it does; the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are checkable by reading four short figure passages and one body of ecological literature. The field test itself has not been run, which is why the whole folder is `specified`, not `grounded`.
