## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the form of a checkable answer about a network response. It has four constraints: the residual stays indexed per partner and per function; the habitat is enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; valence is stated relative to the residual at each slot; and the objective, "the function was maintained", is stated in terms the system can be measured against. It does not contain a collapse threshold, a critical decline fraction, or the name of the species that will compensate. "The network is robust" is a name. "For this partner, in this habitat, these slots changed in this order through these contacts, and the function was or was not maintained by this measurement" is a shape.

The question as posed asks for a general behavior. That is why the answer is a `gap` at the level of a universal magnitude and a `specified` result at the level of the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *The Library of Babel* on bundling, opened this session: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is finding F1: the response is a union compound.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe "the pollinator that visited the declined plant was observed at another plant in the same week", not "the network exhibited compensatory rewiring".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly what stage 1 of the outreach sends.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The first draft named nestedness and connectance and then went looking for evidence. The audit reverses that: describe the residual (a union of service loss, turnover and valence change, indexed per slot) and only then choose a literature.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means a removal experiment with independent measurements of visitation, recruitment and persistence in a named habitat. Until then, "rewiring compensated the loss" is a candidate.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "rewiring" or "nestedness" inside this derivation is a binding from the ecology frame onto a description, not a derivation from the six-term substrate.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (carried from the exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." For this question the needed import is not another network metric; it is the dimension of a partner-specific, independently read outcome, which a presence-absence interaction matrix does not contain. The same essay adds: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six" and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Calling "network robustness" the outcome is a visible choice, not a neutral measurement.

## 4. The source problem in this field

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The same reading can come from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a stable visitation count after a partner declines is a projection. The source could be genuine partner switching by the same species, replacement by a different species that looks similar in the dataset, a change in abundance that keeps the visit count flat while pollen deposition falls, or a sampling artifact. Fig 11 :68-69 names the two silent failures in the habitat frame: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." What is owed is a discriminator that separates these sources, not a finer metric on the same matrix. Leimberger et al. 2023 supply a concrete version: persistence after removal may come from hummingbird mobility or energy-saving adaptations rather than partner switching, so a visitation-only instrument cannot comment on which source produced the reading.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (carried from the exemplar): "The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." A robustness curve that survives every check inside the same interaction dataset is still a projection of that dataset.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular mechanism as the response?** Nothing in the framework. The framework licenses the separator: per-slot residuals, an enumerated contact cascade, residual-relative valence, a stated objective, and an anchor that does not move when the network's own report changes. A particular mechanism is licensed only by a ground reading in a named habitat, with its provenance recorded.

**Does the field commit the error the essays forbid?** Often, but not necessarily. A before-and-after interaction matrix read twice, with no independent measure of function, is a projection whose source cannot be determined. Inferring "compensatory rewiring" from an unchanged nestedness value is the metamerism error. The correct form is an open source slot per habitat until an instrument discriminates. This is not a claim that no network compensates; it is a claim that the usual reading cannot say which source produced the pattern.

**Correction to my own preliminary reading.** I first treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any mechanism. On re-census against the canonical README it is narrower: the phrase governs a *missing* thing. Rewiring, generalist partners and functional groups are available bindings, not missing primitives. Naming them is not forbidden; it carries provenance and remains a `candidate` until the ground pays. This correction is kept here rather than erased, and it is applied in `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session), verbatim: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question: a famous ecologist's endorsement of a robustness result is a budget to try the separator, not evidence the separator is right. A list of agreeable readers is the named collapse. Fig 10 :59 (from the local figure extract): "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Conservation researchers, including this derivation's author, have an unavoidable interest in whether networks are resilient. The correct handling is to declare it, write the failure condition first, and let an outside reader refuse the shape.

## 7. Why this derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." The essay adds "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not an experiment, not a new analysis of Web of Life data, and not a reading of the ground. It cannot settle how mutualistic networks respond when a partner declines. The world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are secondary corroboration; all were opened or retrieved this session, the conflict rule returned the nestedness disagreement to the census, and no verdict was flipped by a world source.
