## 1. Independent domain census (constructed before reading the primary's conclusions)

I worked from the residual line only: "a partner declines or changes behavior; what does the network do next?" I applied the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: the event is one trigger but more than one condition, because the same decline removes a service, changes who competes, and alters signals read by others. **Union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), not one hard question. Symmetry: asymmetric; the effect of A on B is not the effect of B on A, so any symmetric pooled interaction strength loses the arrow. Scale: first order at direct contact, second and third order along contacts (Fig 11 :52).
- **Domain 1, the service slot.** Actors: declined partner and direct partners. Currency: the service, visitation and pollen transfer. Anchor: measured pollen deposition or fruit set at the slot. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: all species in contact directly and indirectly, plus conditions. Currency: persistence and contribution. Anchor: persistence of species and function in the ground. Two modes: unmaintained (no seat) and maintained (a seat that can introduce but not subtract).
- **Domain 3, the observation frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, interventions. Currency: predictions, residuals, agreement. Anchor: field data that must not update on the frame's own report.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the changed partner; acquire terms for what the remaining interactions afford; state the objective (what counts as the function maintained); plan; carry. Break expected between vocabulary and objective (Fig 3 :26-27).
- **Partition or union.** Union. One lever clears single-condition regions and leaves intersections.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. I reached it from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency chain, whereas the primary started from Fig 11's habitat rules and Fig 1's per-slot residual. Different starting figures, same boundaries, low overlap between routes. That is weak internal agreement, not corroboration: the underlying evidence pool is one.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what a coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :43-52 (enumeration, valence, cascade), Fig 11 :61-73 (asymmetry, habitat, ground), Fig 6 :22-23 (import), Fig 9 :60 and Fig 2 :29 (filters and anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: union compound; per-slot residual; cascade outward; valence relative to residual; rebonding adds no atom; metrics are filters; no universal magnitude.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field claim of the form "in this meadow, after this pollinator declined, these partners switched, pollen deposition was maintained, and the function persisted" or its negation.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which **source** produced a stable reading: same-species switching, species replacement, abundance effects, or sampling artifact (Fig 11 :68-69 names two silent habitat failures; *The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discriminator requirement);
- the **objective** of "function maintained", which has to be stated before the model (Fig 3 :26-27) and is absent from the shorthand;
- the **mode** of the verdict, which cannot become `grounded` without a field measurement (`THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule);
- the **hysteresis** condition, because restoring the stressor is not the same as reversing the response (Baruah and Wittmann 2024).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be enumerated outward from the residual, and valence cannot be read off a species list. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the folder's one-paragraph summary as identical to the figure derivation would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
