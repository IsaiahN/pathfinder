## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And from the same essay: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: `space - (structure) - energy`, with the structure slot open, plus "constancy is a symmetry of that composed term to be decided by the ground." `dark energy is a constant property of space` is a name, and it is the thing being re-censused.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* (opened 2026-11-23)
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here "constancy" was described as a symmetry, not as a substance, before any field was chosen.
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar-led): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* (opened 2026-11-23)
- *Library of Babel* on a question that will not resolve: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* (opened 2026-11-23). This is the essay-level source for `01` F4: the dark-energy question bundles smallness, constancy and coincidence.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means a measurement of w(z), not a further argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (exemplar-led) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming vacuum energy as *the* answer to the slot would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the record's own phrasing commits a source-naming error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several distinct sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered) can yield the same reading. From the essay: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: reading a cosmological constant off the expansion history does not comment on its source. A bare geometric Λ, a quantum vacuum contribution, and a slowly rolling field can be observationally degenerate at current precision. The equivalence is algebraic (Carroll 2001, opened 2026-11-23). What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. And since "property of space" is itself a source claim, the phrase is the metamerism error in ordinary language.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (exemplar-led): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q - what shape does "constant property of space" have under the essays?

**Answered without importing a new primitive.** The constraint set is:

1. Two states, space and energy, cannot bond directly (Fig 13 :75). A structure must sit between them. The slot is marked `???`.
2. "Constant" is time-translation symmetry of that composed term (Fig 13 :65; Fig 9 :21-22), not a substance and not a bond. No new operator is needed: the seven bonds include ⋛ comparison and → sequence, but a symmetry is a property of an arrangement, not one of the bonds (Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*).
3. Neither the structure nor the value of w is returned by the framework (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :133-135). The verdict on value is a `gap`.
4. The question is a bundle, so it must be split before it is searched (*Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23; Fig 9 :54, :59).

This is a shape: narrow enough to rule out "primitive property of space", open enough to admit Λ, quintessence or modified gravity as bindings, and silent on which is true until the ground settles it.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading of §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming vacuum energy. That was an overstatement, and it is preserved so the correction is visible. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Vacuum energy, quintessence fields and gradients are already atoms in the physics frame; naming one is a **binding** onto a held atom, a provenance-bearing act, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong is calling such a binding a derivation, or treating a reading of the expansion history as a reading of the source. Both claims are now recorded: the first is retracted, the second stands. This mirrors the exemplar's own §5b correction on Gradient/Spectrum.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (exemplar-led): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that Λ is constant is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against an independent measurement.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
