# The Tether's answer on dark energy as a constant property of space

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and union structure, `general` on the structural limit, `gap` on the empirical constant-versus-dynamical verdict. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The public question fuses three conditions: why the vacuum energy is so small, whether its density is constant in time, and why it is comparable to the matter density now. These have different anchors and form a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :59, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). At the same time, the phrase "a property of space" tries to bind energy (a state) directly to space (a state), which the substrate forbids at first order (Fig 13 :75).

## 2. The shape

1. **Dark energy is not a primitive property of space.** Energy and space are both states. Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* If dark energy composes at all, a structure sits between the two states. The slot stays marked `???`.
2. **"Constant" is a symmetry of that composed term, not a substance and not a bond.** It is time-translation invariance (Fig 13 :65; Fig 9 :21-22). The framework returns the shape of a symmetry-bearing term, not the value of w. No eighth bond or new primitive is needed (Fig 12 :112).
3. **The framework does not decide whether the symmetry holds.** That is the `gap`. Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The anchor is precision cosmology, not agreement about ΛCDM (Fig 2 :29, :35).
4. **The three bundled conditions must be unbundled before searching.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
5. **Constant and evolving formulations are isomers or a functional group over the shared atom set.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* They are compared as second-order arrangements of settled molecules (Fig 12 :104), not merged.
6. **The structure in the slot is not named by the framework.** Selecting vacuum energy, quintessence or modified gravity is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such (Fig 8 :51-52). The equivalence between a bare Λ and vacuum energy is algebraic (Carroll 2001), so distinguishing them is a provenance and instrument question, not a new composition.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that dark energy is constant, and not that it evolves. Both are ground questions that the figures leave open.
- Not a derivation of Λ, not a solution to the cosmological constant problem, and not a solution to the coincidence problem.
- Not that the metric or the expansion history names the source of dark energy. An instrument that reads the expansion *"cannot comment on the source"* (*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23).
- Not a theory of everything. No final room (Fig 7 :6).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a clean symmetry measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Symmetry test:** with controlled systematics and independent instruments, does w(z) equal -1 at all z, or is there a secure departure? The world sources currently conflict: Planck 2018 and Di Valentino et al. find no compelling evidence for evolution, DESI DR2 finds a 2.8 to 4.2σ preference, and Efstathiou 2025 plus Afroz and Mukherjee find the preference is sensitive to dataset combination. Because they reuse the same public data pools, they are one witness, not four (Fig 2 :40).
- **Consistency test:** does the distance-duality relation hold between Pantheon+ supernovae and DESI BAO? Afroz and Mukherjee report a violation that mimics evolving dark energy. This is an INWARD instrument move (Fig 6 :32), not an import.
- **Source test:** can any instrument distinguish a bare geometric Λ from a quantum vacuum contribution? None of the sources read supplies one. Until one exists, provenance is the only honest record (Fig 8 :51-52).
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a direct space-energy bond that cannot be refused; produce a figure line that returns a numerical w without an instrument.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached on the shape, unreached on the value.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*, exemplar-led); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The framework returns a shape and stops at the limit of what the instruments distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The current inability to decide between Λ and evolving dark energy is `unreachable at this budget`, not `underivable in principle` (Fig 8 :40-43); more independent search is the right instruction, not importing an atom.
