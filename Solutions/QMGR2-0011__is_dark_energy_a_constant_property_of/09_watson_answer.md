## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical position does not exist to be taken. "Dark energy is a constant property of space" is not composable as a primitive: space and energy are both states and the figures forbid a direct bond, so a structure must sit between them, and the framework leaves that slot open. "Constant" is a symmetry of the composed term, not a substance or a bond, and whether the symmetry holds is a `gap` handed to the ground. The question bundles three conditions that must be separated before search. Constant and evolving formulations are candidate isomers or a functional group over a shared atom set, compared only after each settles. The vacuum energy equivalence is a provenance distinction, not a new observation. This is a `specified` shape with a `gap` at its empirical core, not a `grounded` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim is mode-labelled, names its refuters before the conclusion, and keeps the slot open, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by presenting a figure verdict where there is none, or by letting a sigma-level filter hand them a conclusion (Fig 9 :60). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its gap.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings (vacuum energy, quintessence, modified gravity) are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A ground reading that closes the gap updates the empirical verdict while the category correction stands. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A systematics-controlled, independent measurement of w(z) returning -1 at all z or a secure departure would close the symmetry gap; a distance-duality test that confirms or removes the claimed violation would decide the INWARD route; a source-discriminating instrument would name the slot; deriving one of the six from the other five, or admitting a direct space-energy bond, would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
