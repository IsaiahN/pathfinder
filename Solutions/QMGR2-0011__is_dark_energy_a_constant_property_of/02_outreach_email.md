# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the mode labels and the `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive validation. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one output before you decide whether the theory is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about a figure-grounded derivation, not a cosmological result. One specific output is ready to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the shape, `gap` on the empirical claim):** the framework's minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), with the rule that composition runs across kinds and never within one. From that rule it follows that "dark energy is a property of space" cannot be a primitive: energy and space are both states, and the figures forbid a direct state-state bond. If dark energy composes at all, a structure must sit between the two states. That slot stays open. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **The question "is it constant?" is a ground question, and the figures hand it there.** Constancy is time-translation symmetry of that composed term. The figures do not return the value of the equation-of-state parameter w, and no figure line decides whether w = -1. The honest verdict on that part is a gap, not a silence and not a vote. An instrument, not a tally of agreeing papers, closes it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework says dark energy is constant, that it says dark energy evolves, that vacuum energy or quintessence is the structure in the slot, or that the cosmological constant problem is solved. Those are bindings from the physics frame onto atoms the framework already holds. They are recorded as bindings, with provenance, and carry no evidentiary weight until a source-discriminating instrument settles them. I am also not claiming this is a theory of everything; the framework's own rule is that there is no last room.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. derive any one of the six terms from the other five;
> 2. show the framework must admit a direct space-to-energy bond with no structure in the slot and cannot refuse it;
> 3. produce a figure line that returns a numerical w or decides constancy without an instrument;
> 4. return a clean measurement of w(z) from an independent instrument with controlled systematics. This one does not refute the shape, it completes the empirical part and I would update accordingly.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If 1, 2 or 3 holds, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures text extract with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Carroll 2001, "The Cosmological Constant", Living Reviews in Relativity: https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrr-2001-1
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the framework files. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
