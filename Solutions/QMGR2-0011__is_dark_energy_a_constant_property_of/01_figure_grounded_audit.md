## 0. The question in effect terms

> Is dark energy a constant property of space, as in the standard cosmological model?

Stated as an effect: something in the universe's energy budget makes expansion accelerate. The standard model (flat ΛCDM) represents it as a term Λ with equation of state w = -1 and an energy density that "does not change as the universe expands" (Carroll 2001, opened 2026-11-23). The question asks whether that density is time-independent (a genuine constant of space) or whether it evolves (dynamical dark energy, quintessence, a modified gravity term).

What each option leads to:

- **Option A, constant.** ρ_DE has time-translation symmetry; as the volume grows the total dark energy grows with it, and the acceleration history is fixed by one number. This is the ΛCDM reading.
- **Option B, not constant.** ρ_DE(z) evolves; the acceleration history carries at least one extra parameter (commonly w0 and wa), and a source must be named for the evolution.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): either (i) a ground reading that returns w(z) = -1 at every z with systematics controlled, or (ii) a ground reading that returns a statistically secure departure from -1 with systematics controlled, or (iii) the admission that no figure line governs the value and that the figures return only a shape. The pre-registered refuter in §4 makes this binding refusable.

**Initial residual, in effect terms.** Two things fail to resolve: (a) a category failure, since "energy as a property of space" is a state-state binding that the substrate does not license at first order; (b) a ground failure, since the current measurements do not yet return a settled value of w(z), and the-combination-of-datasets that appears to favour evolution is internally strained (DESI DR2 versus Efstathiou 2025 and Afroz and Mukherjee 2025/26). These are kept separate. Averaging them would be the "global R near zero with one live slot" error (Fig 1 :13, :19).

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

**R (residual) by arity, symmetry and scale.**

- *Arity:* two conditions, not one hard question. Condition 1 is the category question (is dark energy a property of space at all, and if so what composes). Condition 2 is the symmetry question (is the composed term constant in time). Fig 9 :59 calls this *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* and Fig 9 :54 says *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* This is a **union compound** with its own loop, not a single lever to be pulled harder.
- *Symmetry:* the operative symmetry is time-translation invariance. A constant density is invariant under a shift in cosmic time; an evolving density breaks it. The framework's own treatment of time ties it to this: Fig 13 :65 reads *"no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder"* when the time member is removed. Symmetry is therefore the right axis on which to describe R (Fig 9 :21-22, *"arity · symmetry · scale"*).
- *Scale:* cosmological, the whole expansion history H(z). A second, distinct scale is present in the vacuum-energy puzzle: the ~120 order-of-magnitude gap between the QFT estimate and the observed density (Carroll 2001). These two scales must not be pooled; Fig 2 :31 warns that *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor). No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.**

1. **Domain 1, framework-internal composition.** Actors: the six terms (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures), the seven bonds, negation. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ) and settlement against the ground. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement. Nameable anchor: yes. Question type: partition (what may compose across the two kinds).
2. **Domain 2, the physics and cosmology frame.** Actors: Λ, ρ_Λ, w(z), w0, wa, the scale factor a(t), H(z), vacuum energy, scalar fields, the metric. Currency: physical predictions (luminosity distances, CMB anisotropies, BAO scales, structure growth). Anchor: cosmological observation. Nameable anchor: yes. Question type: a binding from this frame onto atoms held by Domain 1, not a new member of the six.
3. **Domain 3, the metrology and instrument frame.** Actors: Planck, DESI, supernova compilations (Pantheon+, Union3, DESY5), Euclid, Rubin/LSST. Currency: constraint precision on w0 and wa, and control of systematics and dataset consistency. Anchor: the data. Nameable anchor: yes. This is the domain where the constancy question is actually settled, or not yet settled.
4. **Domain 4, the verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Nameable anchor: yes in principle, zero in practice in this simulated run, because all seats draw on one evidence pool. Declared rather than hidden (Fig 2 :40).

**Partition or union.** Condition 1 and condition 2 form a **union**, not a partition: they overlap at the composed term whose symmetry is in question. A single lever that clears the category question leaves the symmetry question binding, and vice versa (Fig 9 :55-58). The vacuum-energy smallness puzzle is a third, overlapping condition that is frequently bundled into the same headline; it is named and then set aside, because it asks "why so small" rather than "is it constant." Folding it in would be the undirected-union collapse (Fig 8 :6-7, *"collapse 5: the undirected union → interdisciplinarity, no mint"*).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3, lines 18-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance"*).** First refuse or repair the category ("dark energy is a property of space"), because a reading taken below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23). Then derive the shape of the composed term. Then ask the symmetry question of that term at the ground. Refusal before mint (Formula step 2): if "property of space" is a false term, it owes a refusal, not an explanation.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The phrase "a property of space" is a state-state binding the substrate forbids (decisive for the category)

Fig 13 :74-75:

> **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**
> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Energy is a state (Fig 13 :13-14: *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*); space is a state (Fig 13 :9-10: *"near, far, between, contained"*). "Dark energy is a property of space" attaches one state directly to another. Under the six-term set that is not composable at first order: a structure must sit in the slot. The framework returns the shape `space - (structure) - energy`, not the name of the structure (see F3). This is not a claim that Λ does not exist in physics; it is a claim about what the framework can compose, labelled `specified`.

**Refuter:** show the framework must admit a direct state-state bond (space to energy) with no intervening structure and cannot refuse it; or derive one of the six from the other five, breaking Fig 13 :41 *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."*

**Strain test:** someone might read "property of space" as merely locational, not compositional. Re-census: the question says dark energy is an *energy density of space*, so the energy member is explicit in the phrase itself. The strain is in the reader's paraphrase, not in the figure line. No strain after re-census.

### F2 - "Constant" is a symmetry of a composed term, and the figures do not return its value

The question's operative word is "constant." Constancy is time-translation symmetry. Domain 1 can say that a symmetry belongs to a composed term; it cannot say whether this particular term has it. Fig 13 :133-135:

> **"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."**
> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**
> **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

And Fig 9 :42: **"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."** So the verdict on constancy is `gap` for the figures, and the instrument, not a further argument, is what is owed. Fig 2 :29 supplies the anchor: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Cosmological data is that anchor here.

**Refuter:** a figure line that returns w = -1, or a derivation of w(z) from the six-term set without an instrument. Until one is shown, any claim that the figures settle constancy is an out-of-mode verdict.

### F3 - The slot stays open; naming vacuum energy or quintessence is a binding, not a derivation

The figures repeat the limit. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :138-139: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."** The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Important narrowing from the exemplar's correction: that phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Vacuum energy, scalar field and gradient are already atoms in the physics frame, not new members of the six, so naming one is a **binding** from Domain 2 onto a held atom of Domain 1, provenanced as such (Fig 8 :51-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"**). It is not a derivation and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**).

**Refuter:** exhibit a derivation of the structure slot from the six alone; or show that naming vacuum energy adds an atom to the framework's closure. Neither is available.

### F4 - The bundled question is a union, and unbundling is owed before searching

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :59: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** The public question fuses (i) the smallness puzzle, (ii) the constancy question, and implicitly (iii) the coincidence question (why now). These share a vocabulary but not an anchor: (i) is a QFT-vs-observation mismatch, (ii) is a measurement of w(z), (iii) is an anthropic or selection question. Treating them as one "dark energy problem" and searching harder is the mirror chain (Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).

**Refuter:** a single instrument or argument that settles smallness, constancy and coincidence at once. If none is produced, the union reading stands.

### F5 - The ground reading is live but strained; conflicting world sources are one pool, not two

This finding is world, not figure, and is recorded as secondary. Planck 2018 reports w0 = -1.03 ± 0.03, consistent with a cosmological constant. DESI DR2 reports a preference for evolving dark energy of 2.8 to 4.2 sigma depending on the supernova sample. Efstathiou 2025 finds *"no significant evidence in support of evolving dark energy"* and Afroz and Mukherjee find that accounting for a distance-duality inconsistency *"refutes the previous claim of evidence for non-cosmological constant dark energy from DESI DR2."* These sources reuse the same CMB, BAO and supernova data. By Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; the same applies to their disagreement. The honest report is a **strained, unresolved ground**, which is exactly the `gap` of F2, not a verdict imported from a preferred paper.

**Refuter:** a measurement with an independent instrument and controlled systematics that returns w(z) = -1 or a secure departure. That would close the gap at the ground.

### F6 - No last room; a final answer to the nature of dark energy is forbidden by the framework's own limit

### F8 - "Bare Λ" versus "vacuum energy" is a provenance distinction, not a content distinction

Carroll 2001 shows that the vacuum energy-momentum tensor is algebraically equivalent to a cosmological constant and that the two are used interchangeably. Fig 8 :51-52 applies: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."** So asking "is Λ a property of space or is it vacuum energy" is asking for the record of where the term came from, not for a new observable, unless a source-discriminating instrument exists. Koberinski 2021 argues from philosophy of physics that *"one should not accept the cosmological constant problem as standardly stated"*, which is a low-overlap corroboration of the need to re-census before naming a source.

**Refuter:** an instrument that distinguishes a bare geometric term from a quantum-vacuum contribution without assuming the answer. None is in the world sources read.
