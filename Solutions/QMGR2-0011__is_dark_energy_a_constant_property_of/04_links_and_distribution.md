## Two things in the world literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The ground is live and contested.** DESI DR2 finds a 2.8 to 4.2σ preference for evolving dark energy when combined with CMB and supernova data, while Efstathiou 2025 and Afroz and Mukherjee find that this preference is sensitive to dataset combination and consistency tests. Planck 2018 and Di Valentino et al. find no compelling evidence for evolution. These are not four independent witnesses: they reuse the same public CMB, BAO and supernova pools (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Report the dispute, do not pick a winner. This is the `gap` of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F2 and F5.
2. **The category is older than the cosmology.** Carroll 2001 shows that vacuum energy and a cosmological constant are algebraically interchangeable, so "a property of space" is a provenance question as much as a physical one (Fig 8 :51-52). Koberinski 2021, from philosophy of physics, argues that the problem is often posed badly. That is a genuinely low-overlap field relative to the figures and corroborates the need to re-census before naming a source.

## The two-stage offer

The proposition is: **(1)** validate the output against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on other problems.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It does, as a shape with a declared gap, provided the mode labels are kept. The moment someone edits it into "the figures show dark energy is constant" or "the figures show dark energy evolves", it fails (F2 and F3 of the audit).
