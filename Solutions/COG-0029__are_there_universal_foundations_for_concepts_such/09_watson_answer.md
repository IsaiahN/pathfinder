## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, between option A, that agency and causation have universal foundations, and option B, that they are substantially culturally learned. State what each leads to. A predicts convergent derivation at low inter-frame overlap, invariance under renaming, and a ground-tracked core independent of any one language. B predicts variation tracking cultural contact and naming, with no invariant that survives an independent ground test. A third possibility, recorded and checked first, is that the question is a union compound whose parts have different answers. The shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: a universal foundation is a constraint that survives convergent derivation at low overlap, not a name in a vocabulary; a culturally learned element is a change in shared meaning with no change in a ground prediction.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The observation that every human frame tracks an actor and an outcome, while cultures carve, name and assign responsibility differently. Arity: at least three sub-residuals (perceptual causation, agency ascription, cultural carving), so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: neither bond is symmetric; causation is directed production, agency is an actor/patient asymmetry with a goal. Scale: individual frame, cultural transmission, ground and habitat, research frame.
- **Domain 1, individual perceptual frame.** Actors: object slots s, percepts, beliefs b, priors w, actions a. Currency: per-slot residual R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| and prediction-then-correction. Anchor: the ground o', what actually happened. Nameable anchor: yes, a task or correction.
- **Domain 2, cultural transmission.** Actors: agents, shared library, language, conventions. Currency: methods versus recordings, head starts, shared meaning. Anchor: conditional. While a convention ranges against the ground it has one; when a culture only checks against itself there is none, and the frame becomes Fig 2's mutual-update collapse. Nameable anchor: conditional only. Not a domain that can settle a world question on its own.
- **Domain 3, ground and habitat.** Actors: habitat, sensorium, conditions, relations, selection. Currency: contact and what the ground kept. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Nameable anchor: yes, cross-cultural and comparative data.
- **Domain 4, research and verification.** Actors: researchers, instruments, cultures as frames. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: experiments, not researcher consensus. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Partition or union:** union. Split before searching (Fig 9 :54). A single lever clears the regions where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The universal/core question is answered at perception and vocabulary before the cultural-carving question is asked at carry. The chain breaks at the middle link, which has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test: Fig 13 :74 governs composition of the six primitives; Operators :52 and Fig 12 :77 govern bond types on composed terms. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. The remaining strain is the applicability of the bond types to regularity-based causal judgment, named in F3 and not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed receives no single answer. The figure-derived verdict is a split. The either/or is a union compound: causation and agency use different bonds, and the cultural carving is a third condition. At the universal end there is a `general` structural constraint, cross-kind composition plus convergence at low overlap, which any frame in this habitat must satisfy if its concepts are to track the ground. At the learned end there is a `specified` conventional layer of names, boundaries and responsibility attributions, which is a product of shared meaning and becomes world evidence only if it changes a ground prediction. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so it does not say which folk concept is universal. The specific bond mappings (production ⇒ for causation, comparison ⋛ plus actor slot for agency) are `specified` bindings, single-source and strained, not derivations. The empirical claim that a universal core exists in this population is `gap` at this budget: no low-contact ground task was run. Overall mode `specified`, status `candidate`, nothing `grounded`. The strongest single corroboration from low-overlap sources is the Berlin and Kay pattern of universal carve order plus cultural choice of cut.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished theory instead of a refutable shape. Fig 2 :42 - "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The strained bond bindings are removable without touching the F1 or F2 shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already mark them as bindings. A sent claim can be superseded. No substrate term or figure line has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A low-contact ground task with an effect description would move F9 from `gap` to a verdict. Low-contact populations with no actor/outcome distinction, a naming-only effect on the ground prediction, or a shared contact channel explaining convergence would break F2 or F4. Deriving one of the six from the other five would break the substrate. A task showing that swapping ⇒ for → or ⋛ for a non-comparative bond changes predictions would break F3. Prestige, fame and endorsement are not such instruments.
