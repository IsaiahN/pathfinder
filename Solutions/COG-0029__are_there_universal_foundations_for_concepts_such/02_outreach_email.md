**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the strained bond mappings in F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are either marked as bindings or removed. The stage-1 output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** A falsifiable split of the universal-or-learned question about agency and causation

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation of the question: are agency and causation universal foundations, or substantially culturally learned? I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the either/or is a union compound, so it should be split before it is searched. Human frames everywhere track an actor and an outcome, and cultures differ in how they carve, name and assign responsibility for those events. Those are two different quantities. The shared outline is the part that would have to survive convergent derivation at low contact between populations; the varying carving is a convention about shared meaning. A naming difference that changes no ground prediction is a convention, not a refutation of a universal core.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. A low-contact population, in full contact with the relevant habitat, that converges on no actor/outcome distinction in a ground task.
> 2. A naming-only manipulation that changes the ground prediction while the effect description is held fixed.
> 3. A demonstration that the apparent cross-cultural convergence comes from a shared contact channel, so the populations are one evidence pool.
> 4. No task on which the universal-core option and the learnable-convention option predict different effects. In that case I record a `gap` rather than an answer.
>
> If any of the first three holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework derives the folk concepts of agency and causation as primitives. It does not. It holds states and structures, and the mapping of "cause" onto production and "agency" onto a goal comparison is a binding from our frame, marked `specified` and provenanced as such. I am also not claiming that a universal core exists as an empirical fact here. I have not run the cross-cultural task. This is a shape and a route to a ground, not a result.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Local verbatim figure text (Fig 13, Fig 9, Fig 2): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md`
> - Full architecture, including the eight essays and the figures: `https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the architecture link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. The architecture's own caveat is that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Recording the verdict before theory

Stage 1 ends when the recipient names a failure condition or reports a residual. Record that verbatim, with the date, before sending any link. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. If a recipient sends a refutation after stage 2, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
