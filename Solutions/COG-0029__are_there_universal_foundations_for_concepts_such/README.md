## The question restated as an effect

The question offers two options. Option A (universal foundations) predicts a shared invariant core of agency and causation that any human frame lands on because the ground and the habitat require it, so names and cultures are surface variation. Option B (substantially culturally learned) predicts that the shared outline is a convention produced by common language and history, so the core is an artifact of one evidence pool. What an answer would look like: a statement of which sub-questions have a ground-tracked core, which have a conventional layer, and what measurement would separate them. Bound as an ordinary term before the run: "a universal foundation is a constraint that survives convergent derivation at low overlap, not a name in a vocabulary" (Formula step "Direction", Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Two researchers who read the same review are one witness, not two.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not this derivation.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap says LLM output is *"simulacra"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns a shape and a route to a ground, and records where it stops.
