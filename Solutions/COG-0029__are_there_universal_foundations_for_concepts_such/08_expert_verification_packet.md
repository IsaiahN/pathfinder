**What this is:** a claim in a form a cognitive scientist or philosophical psychologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No cross-cultural ground reading is claimed. This is a shape and a route to a task, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "universal foundations or culturally learned?" is a union compound and must be split before it is searched. Causation (production, Fig 12 :77) and agency (goal comparison, Operators :52) are different bond types, and the carving and naming of both is a third question about conventions. What may be universal is not a folk concept but a shape: any frame in contact with this habitat must land on a ground-tracked actor and outcome structure, and must do so by convergent derivation from frames with independent evidence. What is culturally learned is the carve, the name and the attribution of responsibility, which are agreements about shared meaning. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so it does not say which folk concept is universal. The empirical existence of the core is recorded as `gap` here; a low-contact ground task is required.

### C2 - framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figure 8, Figure 13 and Figure 9
- **Statement:** the universal is a cross-kind constraint plus a convergence criterion, not a named folk concept. Production ⇒ and comparison ⋛ are bindings onto held atoms, not derivations from the six-term set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74 - *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 8 :50 - *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **What to check:** does the substrate actually require a cross-kind composition between tracked states, and does convergence at low overlap support the universal reading without naming a concept? Are the bond mappings provenanced as bindings?
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; find low-contact populations that converge on no actor/outcome distinction in a ground task; or show a shared contact channel that accounts for the convergence. Any of these breaks the claim. A swapped bond that changes no prediction breaks only the bindings, not the shape.

### C3 - science-facing; checkable by a cross-cultural cognitive scientist against the literature
- **Statement:** a culture's carve, name and responsibility attribution are conventions about shared meaning. They become evidence about the world only if they change a ground prediction. Comparison must therefore use effect descriptions, not translated words.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :41 - *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Fig 10 :49 - *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 9 :30-31 - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- **What to check:** can a naming-only manipulation change a ground prediction while the effect description is held fixed? If yes, the layer is not only conventional. Do the cross-cultural attribution findings (Morris and Peng 1994; Choi, Nisbett and Norenzayan 1999; Ge 2025) show a shared core plus variable carving, which would corroborate the split?
- **Refuter:** a naming-only effect on the ground task; or a demonstration that all apparent cross-cultural variation is translation noise. The former breaks C3, the latter narrows the learned layer.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that all humans are born with an innate concept of cause. The universal is a constraint over held atoms plus a convergence criterion, not a folk name.
- Not that culture is irrelevant. Fig 2 :41 makes agreement about shared meaning the product, not a corruption.
- Not that the framework derives the folk concepts of agency and causation. The bond mappings are `specified` bindings with a named strain: production ⇒ requires a B that *"did not exist before"*, while regularity-based causal judgment often does not; comparison ⋛ is a threshold relation between quantities, while a goal is not obviously a threshold.
- Not that a universal core exists as an empirical fact here. F9 records that as `gap` at this budget.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settles the boundary.
- Not a theory of everything, and not the only framework. A frame cannot certify its own limit (Fig 8).

## 5. What would settle it

A low-contact, cross-cultural and comparative ground task that (a) samples populations with independent contact histories, (b) states what is to be observed in effect terms, not translated words, and (c) yields a residual that persists and does not trend down under the universal-core option and shrinks under the convention option. The public colour-term work (Berlin and Kay: universal order of carve, cultural choice of how many terms) is the nearest documented analogue, not a settlement. A source-discriminating instrument is required, because the same observed word use can come from different underlying carves.
