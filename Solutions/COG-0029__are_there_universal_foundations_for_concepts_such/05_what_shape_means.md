## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From The Library of Babel: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a universal core must survive convergent derivation at low overlap, and a convention is a change of shared meaning with no change in ground prediction" is a shape. "All humans possess an innate concept of cause" is a name, and a ground claim.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- The Library of Babel gives the bootstrapping method: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse.
- The same essay gives the split rule: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is F1 in `01`.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Decoding Tacit Knowledge: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- The Library of Babel on stopping: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The shape is the residue of a search, not a preview of a finished theory.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a low-contact ground task that discriminates the two options.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

They're Scanning Books sharpens the limit in a way that bears directly on "universal": *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"*. The same essay: *"Change the sensors, and not just the data changes, the shape of the compression changes: the categories, the primitives, what counts as similar to what."*

So "universal" can only mean universal **for frames with this sensorium and this habitat**, and even then as a visible guess that can be revised. It cannot mean universal for every possible mind. A snake's compression axes are not ours; the essay says as much. This is the honest boundary of Option A.

## 4. The source-discrimination problem

The Hard Problem is About Source gives the mechanism that matters for cross-cultural concept comparison. Metamerism: an instrument reads green, and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*

The resolution in the essay is instrumental, not argumentative: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: two cultures can produce the same observed word use from different underlying carves (a convergence of projection, not of source), and one culture can produce different word use from the same underlying carve. Word-level comparison is a spectrometer-free reading of green. What is owed is a source-discriminating task, which is F8 in `01`.

How Intelligence Actually Works gives the same point in its own image: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* And the standing caution: *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. Shape for THIS question: universals as constraint, culture as convention

The eight essays give both halves without choosing the question's either/or:

- **Universal side.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Unless you tie it to something all humans have. Something that does not change because culture changes. The priors and primitives are the thing you tie it to, but you grow the learning aligned by the ground and the architecture."* How a Society of Agents: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Same essay: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* These make the universal a property of frame-and-habitat, discovered at low overlap, not a famous name in a vocabulary.
- **Learned side.** Decoding Tacit Knowledge: *"Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge. Its members can hold very different mindsets; what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each other against."* How a Society of Agents: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* and *"Independent discoveries count most."* These make the cultural layer a convention with a real function, not an error.
- **The working shape.** A universal foundation is a constraint that survives convergent derivation at low overlap; a culturally learned element is a change in shared meaning with no change in a ground prediction. The answer is a split of the two, with an instrument specified for the boundary. That is a shape. It does not name which folk concept is universal.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (see §3 above and F3 in `01`)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any claim about universal foundations in this domain. That overstates the rule.

The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in the library. Network influence (Fig 13 :24) and the production bond (Fig 12 :77) are already held atoms. Naming them as candidate bindings is not inventing a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* the folk concepts of agency and causation, or that it names which bond a given culture uses. Those are bindings from a frame and must be provenanced as such.

The source-discrimination point in §4 is **not** retracted. Claiming that a cross-cultural word reading identifies the underlying carve is still the metamerism error. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers endorsements for the universal core is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to run the discriminating task.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The Tether Architecture page adds that LLM outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
