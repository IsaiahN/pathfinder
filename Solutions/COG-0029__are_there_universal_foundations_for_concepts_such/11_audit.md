Nameable anchor: conditional.
- **Domain 3, ground and habitat.** Actors: sensorium, conditions, relations, selection. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 4, research frame.** Actors: researchers and instruments. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: cross-cultural data, not consensus. In this simulated run independence is zero.

Order by dependency: settle perception and vocabulary before objective, refuse before minting (Formula step 2), settle a concept against the ground before it becomes an atom for comparison (Fig 12 :104), describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 13 :74 (cross-kind composition), Fig 8 :50 (convergence at maximum independence), Fig 2 :40-41 (valence of agreement), Fig 9 :30-32 (effect terms, shape not a name) and Fig 11 :36 (selection).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried through `07` and `09`: universal equals cross-kind constraint plus convergence at low overlap; learned equals convention about shared meaning; split the question; effect terms not words.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the folk question as posed: do all humans have an inborn concept of cause, or is the concept an invention of culture?

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **shape-not-a-name limit** (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135), which the folk question immediately violates by asking for a named concept;
- the **independence requirement**, which collapses into bare agreement when the question is asked as "do all humans agree";
- the **conditional anchor in D2**, which disappears in the folk framing, making the cultural option look like a verdict rather than a convention;
- the **bond strain in F3**, which is lost when both concepts are called by one word;
- the **`gap` status of the empirical claim**, which is easily read as a answered "no" when it is only "not run here".

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a structure must sit between tracked states if concepts compose at all, and convergence at low overlap is the test for a universal rather than consensus. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
