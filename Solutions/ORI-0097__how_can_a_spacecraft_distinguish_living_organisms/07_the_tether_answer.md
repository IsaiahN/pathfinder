# The Tether's answer: how a spacecraft can distinguish living from nonliving chemistry

**Mode: `specified` on the discrimination constraints, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §5), not a ground reading. No instrument was operated.

## 1. The residual it answers

A spacecraft returns readings. Living and abiotic chemistry can produce the same reading, so the map from source to observation is many to one. The residual is not "we lack a sensitive enough spectrometer"; it is "the current instrument closure returns readings that fail to discriminate among sources, and no agreement among frames using the same evidence can add the missing information". Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Fig 8 :50 "Only the record of where each came from separates them".

The question is a **union compound**: contamination, abiotic mimic, environmental context and positive structure are four conditions, not one (Fig 9 :54). The answer must be a procedure, not a single measurement.

## 2. The shape (one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate)

**Domain S, the sample and habitat.** Enumerate the habitat, never compose it. Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The abiotic background, contamination and environmental context are part of the habitat. Capability lives in the agent-and-habitat pair, so a clean spiked analogue proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :70). Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus the LDKB and the abiotic workshop, low overlap).

**Domain I, the instrument.** Sharpen an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve. Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34 *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Track the residual per slot. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Combine several instruments on different physical axes, with different preparations and different analysts. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 1, the Mars case).

**Domain C, the classification closure.** A single closure cannot certify the source from inside. Fig 9 :6 *"the verdict: is φ* in here at all?"* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* When no line closes the gap, report `unreachable at this budget`. Do not report `life`, and do not report `no life`. What the closure can offer is a shape: a regenerating catalyst (Fig 12 :119), a cycle whose output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121), a functional group that fixes the outcome (Fig 12 :123). These are candidate structural discriminators, not certificates. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 9).

**Domain V, verification.** Two instruments add reach only when they draw on different evidence. Fig 2 :31 *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* The ground is the sample and a known control; it must not move when pushed (Fig 2 :29). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42). Verdict: `specified` with a `general` core, triangulated (Fig 2, Fig 8, NASA expert, methods literature).

Verdicts are not averaged. A strong result in V (many independent lines) cannot rescue a broken link in I (no per-slot residual), because Fig 3 :19 says "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

## 3. The operational answer in five moves

1. **Describe the residual before choosing an instrument.** Fig 9 :27 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Say what would have to be observed for a biological source to be distinguished from an abiotic one in this particular environment.
2. **Unbundle the union.** Contamination, abiotic mimic, context, positive structure. A single lever clears only the region where one condition holds (Fig 9 :55). NASA's own expert gives the same order: rule out contamination, check habitability, rule out abiotic routes, then seek independent corroboration.
3. **Sharpen what already returns something.** Higher spatial resolution on an ambiguous spot, a second physical principle, isotopic or chiral axes, repeated measurements in time and in controls. Do not start from a description of a perfect life detector.
4. **Range independent instruments against the ground.** Different physical principles, different preparations, different analysts. If two instruments share a calibration library and a sample preparation, they are one frame with two names. Keep every cut ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :40).
5. **Report honestly.** `life`, `nonlife with this instrument`, or `unreachable at this budget`. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the canonical README: "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of biosignature molecules, instruments or mission designs. The framework returns a shape.
- Not a claim that any mission has found life, or that any reported signature is biological.
- Not a claim that current instruments can do this; that is a measurement question.
- Not a claim that unanimity among teams settles anything. Agreement compounds; evidence does not (Fig 7 :12).
- Not a claim that a CoLD level, a confidence threshold or a ⋛ comparison is a witness. Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not a derivation of a seventh primitive, and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- Not a ground reading. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. What would settle it, and what would reverse the answer

A ground reading, not more agreement. Specific checks:

- **Controlled halves:** a returned sample or analogue with known living and known abiotic halves, run through the same procedure. A correct classification at the ground settles the procedure for that target class.
- **Source-discriminating instrument:** a measurement that maps source to reading one to one across known mimics and contamination states. This fires refuter R1 and removes the multi-line requirement for that class.
- **Shared-pool discovery:** evidence that two supposedly independent instruments share calibration, preparation or analysis. This fires R2 and collapses the triangulation claim for that pair.
- **Ground reversal:** the controlled halves return the opposite classification (R4). The shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed.
- **Figure re-census:** a derivation of a seventh member, or an admitted state to state bond with no intervening structure (R5), breaks the substrate rule.

Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.

## 6. Cost, undoability and the honest stop

**Cost if wrong:** high, because a false positive or false negative in life detection affects planetary protection, public claims and the direction of future missions. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That is the figure-grounded reason to demand low-overlap instruments and a fixed anchor rather than a larger agreeing committee.

**Undoable:** largely. Keep the raw per-slot readings, the samples, the controls and the provenance of every binding. Keep cuts ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :40 to :41). A published claim is harder to undo, but a well provenanced correction is locatable: Fig 10 :62 "A ruling carries provenance like an atom."

**Honest stop:** the answer stops where the instruments stop discriminating, and it records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *Library of Babel*: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether". Here the tether is the sample, not the consensus. What remains outside is a seat's office, not the loop's.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A spacecraft cannot read life off a single measurement, because living and abiotic chemistry can produce the same molecule, the same mineral and the same ratio. The reading does not give the source. What the figures license is a shape for a discrimination procedure: track residuals per spot instead of averaging them into one bulk signature; unbundle contamination, abiotic mimics, environmental context and positive structure before searching; sharpen an instrument that already returns an unresolved reading rather than designing a perfect detector from a description of life; and range two or more genuinely independent instruments against a fixed ground, a sample and a control. The candidate discriminators are structural, a catalyst that survives its own use, a closing production cycle, a functional group that fixes the outcome, but none is a certificate and the framework does not derive which one is the biosignature. When no line closes the gap the honest report is `unreachable at this budget`, not `life` and not `no life`. This is a `specified` shape with refuters written in advance, not a ground reading and not a mission design.
