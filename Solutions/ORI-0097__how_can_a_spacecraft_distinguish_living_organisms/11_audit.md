## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the source-to-reading projection. Arity: at least three sources map to one reading, a biological source, an abiotic source and contamination from the instrument's own terrestrial origin, with environmental context as a fourth condition. That is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: the map is many to one, so exchanging living and abiotic sources can leave the reading unchanged; the discriminator must add independent axes. Scale: per slot, not a bulk average, and recursive across molecule, grain, structure, community and environment.
- **Domain S, sample and habitat.** Actors: sample, organics, minerals, isotopes, textures, abiotic background, contamination, context. Currency: contact, traces, gradients. Anchor: the sample plus a known abiotic control. Question type: union unbundling and habitat enumeration.
- **Domain I, instrument.** Actors: spectrometers, imagers, chromatographs, analyzers, calibration, signature library. Currency: reading per slot and residual. Anchor: an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve. Question type: sharpening, not specification from a description.
- **Domain C, classification closure.** Actors: held terms, bonds, closure, candidate term. Currency: composition and settlement. Anchor: the ground, never internal coherence. Question type: verdict, lookup, narrowing.
- **Domain V, verification.** Actors: independent teams, missions, sample return, review. Currency: independent lines and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run this domain's independence is limited and declared.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame; perception of slots, then vocabulary and objective, then planning, then learn and carry; refuse before mint; keep SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD as three separate questions.
