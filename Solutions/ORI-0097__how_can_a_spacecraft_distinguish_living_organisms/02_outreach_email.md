# Outreach draft: check the residual first, then decide about the shape

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` has been checked against the verbatim figure text in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The claim must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about life detection, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a spacecraft reading does not determine its source. Living chemistry and abiotic chemistry can produce the same molecule, the same mineral, the same ratio. The framework's own figure text says "Different routes can arrive at the same substance" and "Only the record of where each came from separates them." So the discrimination cannot be one perfect biosignature measurement. What it needs is a shape:
>
> 1. residuals tracked per object or per spot, not averaged into one bulk signature;
> 2. two or more instruments with genuinely different evidence bases, so their agreement is triangulation rather than one pool talking;
> 3. the union unbundled first: contamination, abiotic mimics, environmental context, and positive structure are four separate questions, not one;
> 4. instruments sharpened from something already returning an unresolved reading, not designed from a description of life;
> 5. an honest `unreachable at this budget` report when nothing closes the gap, rather than `life` or `no life`.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any mission has found life, that any particular molecule is *the* biosignature, that more agreeing teams means more evidence, or that a confidence level or threshold settles the case. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Cycle, catalyst and functional group are candidate structures, not certificates.
>
> **What would refute it, written before the run:**
> 1. a single reading that maps one-to-one to living versus nonliving across all known abiotic mimics and contamination states;
> 2. two supposedly independent instruments shown to share a calibration, preparation or analysis pool;
> 3. a source discriminator derived entirely inside one instrument's current signature library and settled at the ground;
> 4. a returned sample or controlled analogue that returns the opposite classification under the same procedure.
>
> If any holds, I retract or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Life Detection Knowledge Base, a public community tool: https://lifedetectionforum.com/
> - Community Report from the Biosignatures Standards of Evidence Workshop: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14293
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Distribution rules

- One-to-one only. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Public institutional or opt-in venues only (the CSV contains no personal emails).
- Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 **before** any theory is shown.
- A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: agreement about the world is worthless when the frames share an evidence pool).
- A refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source.
