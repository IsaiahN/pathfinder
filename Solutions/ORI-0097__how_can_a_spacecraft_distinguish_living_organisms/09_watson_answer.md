## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether licenses for the discrimination a spacecraft owes. A reading o' at a slot s is a projection: living chemistry, abiotic chemistry and terrestrial contamination can produce the same molecule, the same mineral, the same isotopic ratio and the same texture. The map from source to reading is many to one. The residual is not "we lack a sensitive enough spectrometer". It is "the current instrument closure returns readings that fail to discriminate among sources, and no agreement among frames drawing on the same evidence can add the missing information".

Two options were on the table. Option A: name one definitive biosignature or instrument and treat a positive detection as a verdict. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the specific binding. What each leads to: A hands a mission a name the framework cannot derive, spends the independence of the people asked to check it, and collapses the union of contamination, abiotic mimic, environmental context and positive structure into one threshold. B hands a mission a checkable procedure, pre-registered refuters, and an open slot that only a ground reading can close. The Tether decides for B, while declining to name the discriminator.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The contact failure between a source and a reading. Arity: at least three conditions operate on one slot, a biological source, an abiotic source and contamination from the instrument's own Earth origin, with environmental context as a fourth. By Fig 9 this is a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: exchanging living and abiotic sources can leave the reading unchanged, so the projection is many to one and a discriminator must add independent axes to break it; Fig 12 says symmetry decides whether an arrangement is a new thing. Scale: per slot, never a bulk average (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :28), and recursive across molecule, grain, cell-like structure, community and environment (Fig 12 :103).
- **Domain S, sample and habitat (contact).** Actors: the sample, organics, minerals, isotopes, textures, the abiotic background, contamination, context. Currency: physical and chemical contact, traces and gradients. Anchor: the sample itself plus a control of known abiotic origin. Question type: union unbundling and habitat enumeration.
- **Domain I, instrument frame (perception and measurement).** Actors: spectrometers, imagers, chromatographs, isotopic and chiral analyzers, calibrations, the signature library. Currency: reading o' per slot and residual R_s. Anchor: an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve. Question type: sharpening, not designing from a description.
- **Domain C, classification closure (chemistry and biology library).** Actors: held terms (molecule, ratio, structure, disequilibrium), bonds, closure(Gamma), candidate term. Currency: composition inside the closure and settlement against the ground. Anchor: the ground, never internal coherence. Question type: verdict, lookup and narrowing.
- **Domain V, verification and community frame.** Actors: independent instrument teams, other missions, sample return, peer review. Currency: independent lines of evidence and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation. In this simulated run every seat draws on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is limited and declared.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception of slots, then vocabulary and objective (what would count as progress for this slot), then planning the next probe, then learn and carry. Fig 3 :20 governs the order. The middle link, vocabulary and objective, is the usual break, and the CoLD scale and Standards of Evidence framework are instruments for that link rather than detectors.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a single measurement does not. A spacecraft distinguishes living from nonliving chemistry by range-finding several low-overlap instruments against a ground that does not move, per slot, after unbundling the union of contamination, abiotic mimic, environmental context and positive structure. It sharpens an instrument already returning an unresolved reading rather than designing a detector from a description of life. It treats a regenerating catalyst, a closing cycle and a functional group as candidate shapes, records the provenance of every binding from chemistry or biology, and holds bits and actions as two currencies. When nothing closes the residual it reports `unreachable at this budget`, not `life` and not `no life`. Verdicts by domain stay separate and are never averaged: S `general`, I `specified`, C `specified`, V `specified` with a `general` core. `Unreachable` remains available at every domain. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest figure-grounded point is Fig 13 :121 together with Fig 2 :31: the reading does not give the source, and only low-overlap instruments ranged against a fixed anchor can add that information.

- cost if wrong: high. A false positive or false negative in life detection affects planetary protection, public claims and the direction of future missions. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That is the figure-grounded reason to demand low-overlap instruments and a fixed anchor rather than a larger agreeing committee.
- undoable: largely. Keep the raw per-slot readings, the samples, the controls and the provenance of every binding, and keep cuts ranked and reversible. A published claim is harder to undo, but a well provenanced correction is locatable rather than only felt.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A single reading mapping one to one to living versus nonliving across known mimics would remove the multi-line requirement; a demonstration that two supposedly independent instruments share a calibration, preparation or analysis pool would collapse the triangulation claim; a controlled analogue with known living and abiotic halves returning the opposite classification would withdraw the shape; a derivation of a seventh member of the six-term set would break the substrate rule. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
