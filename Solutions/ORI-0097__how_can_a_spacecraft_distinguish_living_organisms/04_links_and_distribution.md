## What the wider literature adds to the derivation

1. **CoLD is a filter, not a witness.** The scale organizes communication about confidence. Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A level on a scale does not settle the source. Green et al. explicitly invite the community to "modify or supplant the scale".
2. **The field already unbundles the union.** The Standards of Evidence framework separates signal detection, contamination, mimics, context and complementarity. This is the same split the derivation reached from Fig 9 :54, arrived at independently in a different field, so it is corroboration at low overlap rather than a shared-pool echo.
3. **The abiotic background is a habitat, not noise.** The 2025 workshop and the LDKB treat false positives and false negatives as recorded arguments in a specific environment. This is Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* applied by the field itself.
4. **The hard case is documented, not hypothetical.** The Mars release gives a real instance where a compelling pattern of leopard-spot minerals could be biological or abiotic and the team said so. This is F2 and F11 at the ground.
5. **Colour metamerism gives the generic mechanism.** One receptor response, many spectra. Changing the illuminant, the observer or the instrument breaks the match (microscopy plus spectroscopy in the textile case). This is the engineering instruction behind INWARD in the audit.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Lead with the checkable shape and its refuter, not with the architecture.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* No bulk send selected for receptiveness.
- **Fig 7 :12** "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" More names on a list do not add an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) if they ask, offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms (one reading, several possible sources), the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. No theory, no request for trust, no request for agreement.

**Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious after recording a verdict, offer the three local framework files and the public life detection sources. Their stage 1 verdict is already on record, so later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, on request.** Offer the eight-step formula as a generator. Each user writes their own refuter before running it and states a mode. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. If a world source returns a refutation, it goes into `11_audit.md` with provenance before any further contact.
