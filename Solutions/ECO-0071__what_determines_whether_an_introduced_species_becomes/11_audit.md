## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from that line, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line
per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three-place: actor, habitat, tracked effect. Arity makes an
  actor-only answer incomplete by construction. Symmetry: none under a swap of tracked effect,
  because the same actor takes opposite signs. Scale: at least three orders, because the decisive
  effect in the cited DDT case arrives third. I read this as a **union compound**, not a partition:
  establishment, cascade reach and sign overlap, so a single lever cannot close all three.
- **Domain 1, the actor.** Actors: organism, traits, propagule number. Currency: establishment.
  Anchor: the ground, but only while in contact with a habitat. No standalone anchor, so this is a
  sub-loop rather than a full domain.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: residents, conditions, relations. Currency: contact cascade.
  Anchor: the ground as measured change in enumerated slots. Enumerated, never composed. Governing
  domain.
- **Domain 3, the seat.** Actors: the introducer. Currency: addition only. Anchor: the ground.
  Present in the maintained mode, absent in the unmaintained mode.
- **Domain 4, the verifier.** Actors: specialist readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor:
  the ground, never agreement. Independence zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: name the residual, enumerate the habitat, settle establishment, trace the
cascade per slot, judge sign. Refuse a held term before minting a replacement (Formula step 2).

My census matches the primary's three substantive domains and its union reading, but I reached them
by a different route: I started from the arity of the residual and Fig 9 :55, while the primary
started from Fig 11 :39 and the Fig 4 seed line. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low
overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still
one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what
came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on
the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40, :43-52, :61-65, :66-69;
   Fig 4 :34-35; Fig 1 :19; Fig 2 :29-30; Fig 9 :55.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "invasiveness
   is a relation among actor, habitat and residual; the transformation is a cascade; introduction
   is asymmetric; habitat is enumerated; keep slots separate".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete advice a reader would take away, namely "measure the
   contact graph, track effects per slot, enumerate before introducing, budget for permanent work".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the slot** at which the cascade becomes decisive. The shorthand says "per slot";
  the concrete advice needs an instrument that can tell which slot is live and which residual it
  feeds. Fig 1 :19 assumes the index exists; it does not supply the instrument.
- the **mapping refusal** (F5). The shorthand says "habitat is not niche"; a reader concretising it
  under pressure will reintroduce the niche vocabulary, because that is the available ecological
  language. The refusal has to be restated at each handoff.
- the **untested-forward status** of the capability and isolation claims. The canonical article says
  they fit known cases and have not been tested forward; the shorthand drops that qualifier and
  would otherwise read as a settled law.
- the **asymmetry narrowing**. The shorthand says "exclusion is not available"; the figure means
  exclusion is unavailable to the seat, not to nature. Without the narrowing the claim is false for
  beavers, earthworms and other unmaintained introducers.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a relation rather than a carried property, and a cascade
out to third order. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically,
because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped
constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set
R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
