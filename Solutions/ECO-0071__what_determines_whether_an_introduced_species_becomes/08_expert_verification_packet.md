# Expert verification packet: the Tether on invasion outcomes

**What this is:** a claim in a form an invasion ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters
are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No field measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route,
not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a
ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Invasiveness is not a property carried by an introduced species. It is a property of the actor
together with the habitat it enters and the effect being tracked, so the answerable question is
which (actor, habitat, effect) triples produce a transforming contact cascade. The transformation
is itself a cascade: enumeration must run outward until the effect that mattered is reached, which in
the DDT and eagles case was three steps out. The human introduction is an asymmetric move: an actor
can be added, the habitat cannot be subtracted, and a managed ecosystem is therefore permanent
work. Cane toads and kudzu are the same move as ecosystem engineering with the cascade left
unenumerated. The framework does not name species or traits; it gives the shape an answer must have,
refuters written in advance, and the measurement that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11 and 4

- **Statement:** capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone; an
  improvement that does not change contact changes nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what
  makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent
  alone."* and Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in
  isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it
  improves."* Low-overlap triangulation: Fig 4 :34-35 *"The level a method lands on remakes it. A
  seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide
  what it becomes."*
- **What to check:** are the two figure lines quoted accurately, and is the reading strained? Fig 4
  is about the membrane between scales; if that is a different quantity, the triangulation is weak
  and should be discounted.
- **Refuter:** show an actor whose establishment and cascade are invariant across habitats at equal
  propagule pressure. That makes capability a property of the agent alone.

### C2 : ecology-facing, checkable against the literature and against field data

- **Statement:** valence is relative to the residual, so there is no context-free ordering of
  "invasive species"; and the transformation is a cascade out to third order, not a first-order
  effect.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor
  to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing
  list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :52 *"And contact cascades. Scoping
  the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration,
  second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** is there a case where a species is harmful to every tracked residual in a
  habitat, or where the transformation is fully captured at first contact? Does the cane toad
  literature (Pettit et al. 2021; Jolly et al. 2015) show the predicted second- and third-order
  effects?
- **Refuter:** a context-free harm ordering, or a transformation with no detectable higher-order
  effects at any instrument resolution.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation with a management consequence

- **Statement:** introduction is available and exclusion is not; the disaster form of an
  introduction is an unenumerated cascade, so a managed ecosystem is permanent work.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :61-62 *"The seat’s power is asymmetric. Introduction is available;
  exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a
  fact about the world and does not consult you."* Fig 11 :65 *"Introducing an actor for its cascade
  is ecosystem engineering, after Jones, Lawton and Shachak, 1994. Cane toads and kudzu are the same
  move with the cascade unenumerated."*
- **What to check:** is the asymmetry a rule about who acts rather than about what is possible?
  (The canonical article notes that unmaintained nature also introduces actors, for example beavers
  and earthworms.) Is there a one-off exclusion that permanently restores a habitat?
- **Refuter:** a permanent restoration with no continuing work, or a genuine subtraction of a
  habitat. Cassey et al. 2018 provides secondary corroboration: *"only complete exclusion of an
  alien can guarantee no establishment."*

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of invasive species, and not a universal trait syndrome. The framework returns a
  shape, not a name.
- Not that the Tether habitat is the ecological niche. The canonical article declines that mapping;
  see section 7 of `05_what_shape_means.md`.
- Not that cane toads or kudzu are derived predictions. Fig 11 :65 names them as the same move with
  the cascade unenumerated, which is an instance, not a forward result (Fig 8 :56).
- Not a verdict from consensus. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror
  cannot correct you."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

The ground: a measurement of the enumerated contact graph and its cascade, per slot, against a
named residual. Not an argument and not a single pooled impact score. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is
indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Specifically:

1. The invariance test: hold actor and propagule pressure constant across habitats.
2. The residual test: track one effect, then reverse it; does the label flip without a physical change?
3. The cascade test: at what order does the transformation become detectable?
4. The exclusion test: does a one-off removal restore the system permanently?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, :49-52 and :61-65, five short passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know: was the transformation a first-order effect or a cascade?
3. Name one measurement that would distinguish a carried property from a contact-relative one, or
   say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
