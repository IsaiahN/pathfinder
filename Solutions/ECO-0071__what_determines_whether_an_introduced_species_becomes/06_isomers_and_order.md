# Supporting derivation: structure, order and analogues for the invasion question

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground
reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

The question bundles three conditions that are often treated as one: does the actor establish,
how far does its contact cascade reach, and relative to which residual is its effect harmful. This
supporting derivation asks (1) is the bundle a partition or a union, (2) what is the dependency
order of the loops, and (3) which structural analogues in Fig 12 actually apply.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held for this derivation: the introduced actor, residents, conditions, relations, the seat,
the ground and the residual. Bonds held: sequence, production, comparison and negation where a
refusal is owed. Fig 11 :45-47 gives the three enumeration classes: actors, conditions, relations.
No new primitive is needed; the question is one of arrangement, not of new kinds.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 : Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you
  search again."*
- Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of
  separate ones."*
- Fig 9 :56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the
  intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*
- Establishment, cascade reach and impact sign overlap. A lever that stops establishment does not
  address an established actor's cascade; a lever that reduces one tracked harm does not address
  establishment or a different residual. So the residual is a **union compound** with its own loop.
- Consequence: no single "invasiveness score" can close it. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and
  it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** show that the three conditions are independent and
  partition the cases, so one lever closes each region without intersections. If that holds, the
  union reading fails and the simpler partition stands.

### Q2 : What is the dependency order?

- Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the
  one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- Ordered loops for this question:
  1. **Name the residual.** Without a tracked effect, "invasive" has no truth value (Fig 11 :49-51).
  2. **Enumerate the habitat.** Actors, conditions, relations, outward until the cascade stops
     mattering (Fig 11 :43-48).
  3. **Settle establishment.** Does the actor persist and spread in this habitat? (the ground).
  4. **Trace the cascade, per slot.** First order, second order, third order (Fig 11 :52).
  5. **Judge sign relative to the residual** (Fig 11 :50).
- A link below a break cannot be diagnosed: Fig 3 :22-23 *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be
  diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak
  reading. It is a reading of nothing."* Measuring cascade before establishment is settled is a
  reading of nothing.

### Q3 : Which Fig 12 structures actually apply?

- **Isomer.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference
  is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Two introductions with the same actor
  and the same resident set can differ only in which relation is altered, and behave differently.
  That is a real isomer claim for invasion comparisons, but it needs an instrument to identify the
  bond; the figures do not name it.
- **Functional group.** Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the
  outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* "Introduced apex predator"
  is a functional group, not a species list. The qualifier (which predator, in which habitat) says
  which member. This is the closest structural reading of an "invasive guild".
- **Catalyst.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating
  parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Some introduced
  actors persist and keep producing the effect; others spend themselves. Fig 11 :58 records the
  maintained analogue: *"Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the
  impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is
  worked on rather than waited for."*
- **Cycle.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides
  whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Ecosystem engineering can close a
  feedback loop; a simple linear "introduction then impact" chain cannot represent it.
- The two modes are not the same object: unmaintained has no seat and *"what survives is what the
  ground kept"* (Fig 11 :56); maintained has a seat that introduces but still cannot author the
  habitat (Fig 11 :58-64). Conflating them is a change of domain, not a smaller instance of one.

### Q4 : What is the order of the cascade bond?

- Fig 11 :52: *"first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was
  three steps out."*
- Fig 13 :23: *"network"* and :24 *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*; network is a
  structure, one of the six, so the cascade is a composition over a held structure, not a new kind.
- The order is therefore: first-order direct contact, second-order effect on a directly affected
  actor's partner, third-order effect on the tracked residual. The transform between orders is
  lossy (Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*), so
  the round trip gap has to be measured, not assumed zero.
- **Falsifier:** an exogenous action at first order that regenerates at later orders with no
  attenuation or sign change would collapse the three orders into one, breaking the cascade claim.

## CONCLUSION

The question is a union compound whose loops must be ordered before they can be measured: name the
residual, enumerate the habitat, settle establishment, trace the cascade per slot, then judge sign.
The applicable structures are functional group and, where the bond can be identified, isomer;
catalyst and cycle are available for the maintained mode. The two modes stay separate. No new
primitive is required, and the only order claim with a direct figure line is the three-step cascade
in Fig 11 :52. Everything beyond that is a binding from the ecology frame, provenanced as such.
