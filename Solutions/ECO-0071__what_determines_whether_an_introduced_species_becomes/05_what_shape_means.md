## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a
shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish and records the
lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the
answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing,
because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the constraint any answer must satisfy before we can name the mechanism. For
this question the shape is:

> Invasiveness is a relation among an introduced actor, an enumerated habitat and a named residual.
> The transformation is a contact cascade measured per slot, not a property carried by the actor.

That is a shape. A named list of "invasive traits" is an attempted name, and Fig 11 :39 refuses the
agent-only question that would license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing and recurse by proximity. *"With
  each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary.
  It’s this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being
  sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are
  borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- From *The Library of Babel*: the search is steered by the question itself, which acts as a tether.
  *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present
  collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to
  turn next."*
- On stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search
  and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: the effect terms are establishment, cascade reach and sign relative to a tracked
effect. The frame chosen second (after describing those effects) is invasion ecology. Choosing the
frame first, then mapping traits onto it, would be the inversion Fig 9 :27-28 warns against.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays.
   A gate is not the ground."* Here that means a field measurement of the enumerated contact graph
   and its cascade, per slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. The ecology literature qualifies as such a frame; its agreement
   is useful because it was reached independently, not because it is quoted here.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The figure on the ground: Fig 11 :73 *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors
come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."* A habitat is not an anchor.
If you measure "the habitat" and treat it as the thing that settles the question, you have made a
moving frame into a ground and the drift becomes invisible (Fig 2 :33-34).

For this question the ground is not the invader's abundance alone (an instrument reading), and not
the habitat list alone (the field of contact). It is the measured cascade against the named
residual: the thing that can refuse the claim. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the
question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework say an answer must look like?** It must name the residual; enumerate
actors, conditions and relations outward until the cascade stops mattering; keep the residuals per
slot; keep valence relative to the residual; and treat the introduction as an asymmetric seat move
whose disaster form is an unenumerated cascade. It must not be a standing list of species, and it
must not be a single global impact score.

**Does the framework name the mechanism?** No. It does not say which species, which trait, or which
threshold. Those are bindings from the ecology frame or measurements from the ground, recorded as
such. Fig 11 :65 names cane toads and kudzu only as instances of the same move with the cascade
unenumerated, not as derivations.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*
(opened 2026-11-23): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found
in the architecture post."* It continues: *"However, the truly testable output would need to be
derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the canonical article says
such outputs *"must be seen as simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal
consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it
reports on.

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be
allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a
budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground
Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous invasion biologists who agree with the
shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be
tried and lets the ground decide.

## 7. Retraction: the habitat is not the ecological niche

From *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* (opened 2026-11-23), section "Three mappings
offered and declined": *"The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism’s
relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual
moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes."* The article
adds that the resemblance is real but the definitions do not align, *"which is why the figure says
enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."*

So my first reading, which wanted to use this ecological question to illustrate the niche mapping,
is withdrawn. The framework declines the mapping. What it offers to invasion research is a method
for enumerating contact and a warning about averages and unenumerated cascades, not a new niche
theory. This retraction is kept visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F5 and in the ledger.
