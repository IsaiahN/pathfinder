## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. All contact points are institutional, public and opt-in.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the Tether "habitat" is the ecological niche. The canonical article explicitly
  declines that mapping: the habitat is defined by contact with a residual that moves, and is
  "enumerated, never composed" rather than borrowed from ecology. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F5
  and `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7.

Three reasons from the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13 says *"same closure, handed along; agreement
   compounds, evidence does not"*. A list of agreeable recipients is one long room.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 says *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement
   about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting for
   receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make the agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can
   reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style
rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Standing of this derivation

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a
ground reading. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
The world literature is secondary corroboration only; it never overrules a figure line.
