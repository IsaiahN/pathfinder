## 0. The question in effect terms

> Can religious and spiritual beliefs be explained through general cognitive tendencies, or do institutions and histories do more explanatory work?

Stated as a residual R, not as a topic:

- **Observation O.** Across cultures and periods, religious and spiritual beliefs (a) recur in recognizable forms (supernatural agents, minimally counterintuitive concepts, afterlife ideas, ritual obligations) and (b) vary sharply in specific content, prevalence and institutional embedding.
- **Candidate A.** General cognitive tendencies (agency detection, folkpsychology, memory biases for minimally counterintuitive material) predict (a). They are panhuman and individual-scale.
- **Candidate B.** Institutions and histories (transmission chains, ritual specialists, texts, political and kinship structures, contingent events) predict (b). They are local and multi-generational.
- **R per slot s.** For each belief form in each population at each time, R is the gap between what a candidate predicts and what the record shows. A model that predicts recurrence but not local content leaves positive residual on the content slot; one that predicts local content but not recurrence leaves positive residual on the universal-form slot. R is never aggregated into one global number: Symbols :43 "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."

**Arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: two conditions, so a union compound rather than one hard question (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Symmetry: the two candidates are not interchangeable. They have different actors, different currency and different anchor (Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it."). Scale: A is individual and species-typical, B is population and historical; the membrane between them is a filter with a direction (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."). The question as posed ("A, or B does more work") assumes a partition and a single currency. The census below tests that assumption before any figure line is chosen.

## 1. Domain census before choosing a figure line

The rule: one line per domain with actors, currency and an anchor that can be named. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. Then partition or union, then dependency order (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance.").

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Cognitive frame** | individual minds, evolved detection and inference mechanisms (agency detection, folkpsychology, memory bias for minimally counterintuitive concepts) | which concepts are acquired, recalled and passed on | **yes**: cross-cultural laboratory and developmental studies, memory and recall experiments, independent of any particular institution | partition member |
| **D2 Institutional / historical frame** | ritual specialists, texts, kinship and political structures, transmission chains, contingent historical events | which variants persist and spread | **yes**: ethnographic, archaeological and historical records, cross-cultural databases, independent of the laboratory | partition member |
| **D3 The ground** | none. It is not a frame. Symbols :104: "Whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." | predictions that can be refused | **yes, itself**: a discriminating dataset or experiment that neither frame can move | settlement point |
| **D4 The union compound** | both sets of actors plus the typed relation between them | the predictive surplus neither frame has alone | **yes**: the same ground D3 | compound with its own loop |

**Partition or union?** Union. The two conditions overlap: institutions recruit cognitive biases, and cognitive biases are transmitted through institutions. Fig 9 :54 says "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A bundled problem is "usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones" (Fig 9 :55). A single lever that clears the region where only one condition holds leaves the intersection standing. The either/or question is therefore a malformed split, not a choice between two answers.

**Dependency order.**

1. Describe R in effect terms before selecting a frame (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." and Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").
2. Check whether each condition is reachable at all inside its own closure (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.").
3. Range each frame separately against the ground (Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.").
4. Only then test the union for surplus (Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone.").
5. Import from the other frame with provenance and a shadow test, debited against independence (Fig 6 :24, Fig 8 :51).

**Strain test, applied before conclusions.**

- *Initial strain.* My first pass mapped Fig 13's six terms (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network) directly onto "cognitive" and "institutional." That strains: the six are load-bearing terms in physics, and a belief is not a measured physical quantity in the same sense. Per the standing rule, a strained figure line governs another quantity, so re-census. Re-census result: the governing figures are Fig 12's bond rule (the relation carries information the operands do not) and Fig 11's agent-and-habitat rule. Different objects, no strain after re-census. No six-term binding is claimed in this solution.
- *Second strain.* Fig 13 :4 says "The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward," which could be misread as making cognition and institution equally ranked peers. Re-census: that line governs the six physical primitives, not two research frames. The correct rule for two frames is Fig 8 :45 plus Fig 2 :31 independence. Recorded as a resolved strain, not as a verdict that the two factors contribute equally.
- *Live strain.* Which typed bond holds between cognitive and institutional operands is not derivable from the figures. The Operators table supplies the tests but not the selection. This is named as a `gap` in F5, not smoothed over.

## 2. Findings, one per governing figure line

Every quote below is verbatim from `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. I quote before concluding. Mode is stated on each claim. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Triangulation means two figures with genuinely low overlap, not two paraphrases of one.

### F1. The either/or is a malformed split (D4, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"

Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 13 :136: "It can say the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again."

The question asks which of two candidates does "more" explanatory work on one bundled outcome. The figures answer: it is a union compound with its own loop, and the bundle must be unbundled before search. Refusing the either/or is not evasive. It is the figure-mandated first move. Triangulated across Fig 9 (leave/arrive) and Fig 13 (what a closure can report), which are different figure functions with low overlap. **Refuter:** a single-parameter model predicts both recurrence and local content on held-out cultures with no residual left on either slot. If that fires, the union claim falls and the either/or is restored as a legitimate question.

### F2. A cognitive-only account is an improvement to the agent in isolation (D1, `specified`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 11 :35, on the question "what is intelligence": "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."

Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

Reading: general cognitive tendencies are the **agent** in this analogy; institutions and histories are the **habitat**. Fig 11 says an agent-only improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. A cognitive frame that searches only inside its own library cannot derive the particular historical content any more than a closed set can add an atom. So the cognitive account is necessary for the recurrence slot and insufficient for the content slot. Triangulated across Fig 11 (habitat) and Fig 8 (closure idempotence), low overlap. **Refuter:** a cognitive-only model with no transmission or institutional parameters predicts the observed cross-cultural distribution of *specific* belief contents on held-out populations, with positive residual nowhere. If that fires, F2 is withdrawn and the cognitive frame owns both slots.

### F3. An institution/history-only account lacks the universal and cannot derive the recurrent form (D2, `specified`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Reading: the historical frame enumerates its actors from the world, it does not compose its way to a universal. It predicts which variants persisted here, given this history. It cannot derive why the variants have the recognizable forms they do (agents, counterintuitive beings, afterlife concepts) without importing that shape from the cognitive frame. And the habitat must not be confused with the ground: a list of institutions is not the thing that settles which explanation is right. Triangulated across Fig 11 (enumeration and the habitat/ground distinction) and Fig 6 (closure), low overlap. **Refuter:** a history-only model with no cognitive mechanism parameter predicts the cross-culturally recurrent belief forms from institutional variables alone on held-out cultures. If that fires, F3 is withdrawn.

### F4. Agreement between the two literatures is not a verdict (D3 verification, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not"

Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

Reading: cognitive scientists and historians often draw on the same ethnographic corpus. When they agree, that is one evidence pool talking, not two independent witnesses. A consensus narrative in which "both sides concede the other matters" is efficient for a convention and disqualified for a verdict. Independence has to be bought: Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 7, which are different figures (alignment and room chains). **Refuter:** show the two frames have disjoint evidence sources and that each independently predicts the residual the other leaves; then their convergence is "corroboration at maximum independence" (Fig 8 :50) and F4 is withdrawn as applied to that pair.

### F5. The explanation is a typed bond, not a list of factors (D4, `specified` for the shape, `gap` for the specific bond, triangulated for the shape)

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."

Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

Reading: "cognition plus culture" as a list of ingredients is the flat formula failure. The bond between them carries the information the operands do not. A causal claim such as "cognitive biases are recruited by institutions" has a different bond from "institutions select which cognitive variants are transmitted," and the two are not the same explanation even with the same ingredients (Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."). **Which bond actually holds is a gap:** no figure line selects among the seven for this domain, and the Operators table says "A relation that fails all seven is not an operator you are missing; it is a claim that has not been made yet" (Operators :68). The framework returns the shape (a typed cross-kind composition with a discriminable bond), not the name of the bond. Triangulated for the shape across Fig 12 and Fig 8. **Refuter for the shape:** show that the union of the two frames adds no predictive surplus over the better single frame on held-out data, which is the blend collapse of Fig 8 :12-15; then F5 falls.

### F6. The ground, not the debate, settles the weighting (D3, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"

That requires an anchor: a cross-cultural, longitudinal or experimental reading that neither literature can move. Until such an anchor is named and used, the honest report is `unreachable at this budget`, not a silence and not a false verdict. Triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 9. **Refuter:** name an instrument or dataset that returns a discriminating reading on this question and show that the union shape conflicts with it.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **Union refuter.** A single-parameter model predicts both recurrence and local content on held-out cultures, leaving no positive residual on either slot.
2. **Cognitive-only refuter.** A model with no transmission or institutional parameters predicts the cross-cultural distribution of specific belief contents on held-out populations.
3. **History-only refuter.** A model with no cognitive parameter predicts the recurrent belief forms from institutional variables alone on held-out cultures.
4. **Surplus refuter.** The union of cognitive and institutional frames adds no predictive surplus over the better single frame on held-out data (blend collapse, Fig 8 :12-15).
5. **Bond refuter.** A typed bond is demonstrated between the two operand sets that the figures' seven tests reject, or two different bonds are shown to be indistinguishable in their predictions and no instrument discriminates them (in which case the bond claim becomes `unreachable`, not false).
6. **Ground refuter.** A discriminating instrument or dataset returns a reading that contradicts the cross-kind composition shape.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn in this log rather than quietly softened. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Ask the question as a union compound and state the shape before any empirical weighting (F1, F5).
2. Treat the cognitive frame as the agent and the institutional frame as the habitat; require contact between them in any explanation (F2, F3).
3. Do not count agreement between the two literatures as corroboration unless their evidence pools are demonstrably disjoint (F4). This is also a contact rule for the outreach packet: bring a characterised residual, never the source (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
4. Name the bond only with provenance and a shadow test; leave it marked `???` until the ground settles it (F5, F7).
5. Use the public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, one to one, and ask for a refutation rather than assent (F8).
