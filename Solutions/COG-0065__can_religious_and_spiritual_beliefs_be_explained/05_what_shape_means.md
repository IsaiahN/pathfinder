## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: "a typed cross-kind composition of cognitive and institutional operands, with the bond left open until the ground discriminates it."
- Name: "agency detection causes religious belief," or "the priestly institution caused this tradition," or "cognition explains 60 percent and history 40 percent."

The first is a constraint any adequate answer must satisfy. The last three are claims that would have to be settled by a reading. The question asks for a winner. The framework returns the form the winner would have to have.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay warns: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is exactly the either/or question in COG-0065.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with a refuter rather than a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The same essay gives the paradox: "By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. Why "both factors matter" is the specific forbidden error here

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the mechanism. A written sentence is a lossy compression of the reasoning that produced it: "The corpus contains the problems evolution already solved and someone wrote down. It does not contain the capacity to solve new ones, because that capacity lives in the living process." Fig 12 :5 says "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

The common answer to this question, "cognition and culture interact," is exactly the flat formula failure. It records the ingredients and not the relation. Nothing downstream can tell "institutions recruit cognitive biases" from "institutions select which cognitive variants get transmitted," yet those are different explanations with different predictions. Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument-level version: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." Applied here: observing that a belief is both cognitively natural and historically shaped does not tell us which bond produced it. The observation is a projection. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a verdict.

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular bond in this question?

**Answered, no further question needed within the framework.** Nothing in the figures selects a bond among the seven for this domain. The Operators table defines the tests, and says "A relation that fails all seven is not an operator you are missing; it is a claim that has not been made yet." A bond is licensed only by the ground settling a candidate relation, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the bond is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The status is `gap`, and Fig 13 :134 says "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** The common debate answer does: it names a winner where the framework returns a shape for a union compound, and it treats agreement between two literatures as a verdict where Fig 2 :40 says "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The correct form remains: `COGNITIVE(???)INSTITUTIONAL`, with the relation marked unknown.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first draft of this file. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Cognitive mechanisms and institutional forms are both already held in the world frame; mentioning one is a **binding from the world frame**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in the common answer is calling that binding a settled cause without provenance, and treating "both matter" as a composition when it is an ingredient list. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a reading that a belief is both natural and traditional does not determine the source relation. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held candidates.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names on either side of this debate is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let a cross-cultural reading settle it.

**Habitat, not agent alone.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." *How a Society of Agents Would Work* repeats it: "A newborn agent is made capable by the surroundings it is born into, and the shared knowledge store is part of those surroundings." *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the drift warning: "Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice." An institution-only account that checks only against its own tradition is precisely that failure.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any claim about religious belief and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
