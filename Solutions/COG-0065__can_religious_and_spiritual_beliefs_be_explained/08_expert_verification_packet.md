# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cognition versus institutions in religion

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cognitive scientist of religion, historian or methodologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural claim, `specified` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No cross-cultural measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question of whether religious and spiritual beliefs are explained by general cognitive tendencies or by institutions and histories is a case in two conditions, not one harder question. Cognitive mechanisms supply the recurrent forms; institutions and histories determine which variants persist in a given place; capability here belongs to agent and habitat together, not to the agent alone. An adequate explanation is therefore a typed bond between the two operand sets, not a list in which both matter. Which bond holds is not given by the figures and is marked open until a reading that neither literature can move discriminates it. The figures also refuse the common move of counting agreement between the two literatures as corroboration when those literatures draw on the same evidence pool.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework internal; checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 11

- **Statement:** the either/or is a malformed split; the two conditions form a union compound, and the cognitive side is an agent in a habitat rather than a self-sufficient cause.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"; Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Also Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Refuter:** a single-parameter model predicts both recurrence and local content on held-out cultures with no residual on either slot; or a cognitive-only model with no transmission parameters predicts the specific cross-cultural distribution of belief contents.

### C2. Framework internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** the explanation is a typed bond over a shared atom set, not an ingredient list. As usually written the cognitive and institutional accounts are a **functional group** (different atoms); over the full shared set they are **isomers** under Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The comparative "more work" reads as a threshold comparison on an unmeasured quantity and is marked `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Also Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." And Operators :68 "A relation that fails all seven is not an operator you are missing; it is a claim that has not been made yet."
- **Refuter:** either account can be written without an atom the other requires (isomer claim fails, functional group stands); or a discriminating instrument returns the bond and it is one of the seven (the `gap` closes and the specific bond claim is then testable).

### C3. Verification arrangement; checkable by reading Figure 2 and Figure 7

- **Statement:** agreement between cognitive scientists and historians is not corroboration when they share an evidence pool. Independence is a quantity read twice: it governs both what agreement means and whether the union has surplus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Also Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."
- **Refuter:** show the two frames' evidence sources are disjoint and each independently predicts the residual the other leaves; then their convergence is corroboration and C3 is withdrawn as applied to that pair.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that cognition wins, or that institutions win, or that they contribute equally.
- Not a specific cognitive mechanism as *the* cause, and not a specific institution as *the* cause. Those are bindings from the world frame and need provenance.
- Not a numerical weighting of the two factors. That quantity has not been measured here.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment, dataset or field reading was run in this session.
- Not that the figures select the bond. They return the shape, not the name.
- Not a claim about the truth or falsity of any religious belief. The question is about explanations of beliefs, not their metaphysical status.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating reading that neither frame can move. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

1. **Bond test:** recruitment of cognitive biases by institutions versus institutional selection of cognitive variants, tested with held-out cultures and disjoint evidence pools.
2. **Union surplus test:** does the two-frame union beat the better single frame on held-out recurrence and content?
3. **Isomer test:** can both accounts be written over the same atom set, differing only in bond?
4. **Standing refuters:** the six conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, written before this conclusion.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :57, Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 12 :5 and :117, Fig 2 :40. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: do the cognitive and institutional literatures actually hold different atoms, or can both be written over the same set with different bonds?
3. Name one cross-cultural design with independent evidence pools that would discriminate the two bonds, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
