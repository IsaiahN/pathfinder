## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is refused. The question is a case in two conditions: cognitive tendencies supply the recurrent forms, institutions and histories determine which forms persist locally, and capability belongs to agent and habitat together. An adequate explanation is a typed bond over the shared atom set, not a list in which both factors matter. As usually written the two accounts are a functional group; over the full shared set they are isomers with a stated falsifier. The specific bond is a `gap`, the comparison of explanatory weights is a threshold on an unmeasured quantity, and consensus between the two literatures is not corroboration when their evidence pools overlap. This is a `general` structural claim and a `specified` shape, ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and the gap before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, priced by Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific bond and the weighting are left open, so no primitive or empirical claim has to be withdrawn if a reading arrives. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with the discriminating dataset attached. The only thing that would need retraction is the union shape itself, and the six pre-registered refuters say exactly what would fire it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A held-out cross-cultural design with disjoint evidence pools that discriminates the two candidate bonds would name the relation; a single-parameter model covering both recurrence and content, a cognitive-only model covering content, a history-only model covering recurrence, or a union with no surplus over the better single frame would break the shape; a bond the seven tests reject, or a discriminating instrument that contradicts the cross-kind composition, would close the remaining gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
