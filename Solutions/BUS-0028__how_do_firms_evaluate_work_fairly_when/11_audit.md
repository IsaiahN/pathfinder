## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: procedure not metric, index per person, two different conditions, second-order team score, no named formula.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete appraisal system: voice interviews, a correction path, per-person residuals, low-overlap peer and customer signals, a team score composed from settled person terms.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **definition of anchor**. Fig 2 :37 licenses human judgment, but the shorthand can be read as "whatever people happen to say", which Fig 2 :29 would reject. The procedure with a violation surface (Fig 10 :19) is what survives, not the raw opinion.
- the **type of ⋛'s operands** at team level. The Operators table restricts comparison to two quantities; after Fig 12 :104 demotes settled molecules to atoms, whether they count as quantities at that level needs an instrument.
- the **overlap of the two conditions**. The shorthand says "two conditions"; the concrete case is a union with an intersection where both fail at once (Fig 9 :55-58), which needs its own lever.

One thing survives cleanly: per-person indexing with a correction path and independent frames. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
