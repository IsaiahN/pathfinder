## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The README adds: "Indiscernibility is not identity."

From The Library of Babel (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for BUS-0028 a **shape** is the form a fair evaluation system must have, statable before any scoring formula is named. It constrains; it does not fill. "Evaluate per person, allow correction, keep frames independent" is a shape. "Use a 9-box grid with a 20 percent forced distribution" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Decoding Tacit Knowledge (via exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question: describe what a fair allocation would look like if the contribution were visible, then check whether the proposed procedure would produce it.
- Library of Babel on bundles: "perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the union compound in `01` section 1. The shape for a hard-to-measure output and the shape for a pooled team output are derived separately and only then composed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, a procedure is named only after an instrument discriminates the contribution it was designed for.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A customer outcome or a peer signal can be imported as the reading of a contribution, but its provenance and the gap it explains must be recorded.

They're Scanning Books (via exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So declaring "output equals value" does not make the metric neutral; it makes the choice visible and refusable.

## 4. Why the single-number answer is the specific forbidden error

The Hard Problem is About Source (opened) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several sources can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument.

A performance score is the same kind of reading. High score can come from high contribution, an easy assignment, a generous rater, or a shared narrative. Naming "contribution" from the score is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (independent frames, per-person accounting, a correction path), not a better-looking number. World corroboration: the idiosyncratic rater effect finding reported in `04` says most rating variance is the rater, not the ratee.

## 5. Q1 - Does the framework license a specific appraisal method?

**No.** The README's phrase governs a missing thing: "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." For BUS-0028 the missing thing is a discriminator that can separate contributions. The framework constrains it (per-slot, violable, independent, declared-stake) but cannot select among forced ranking, calibration committees, peer review or customer signals. Selecting one is a binding from the management frame onto the shape, provenanced as such, not a derivation. If someone claims the framework derived a specific method, that is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known HR academics who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the procedure and a ground to settle it.

**Agreement is not a second witness.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any fairness procedure and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any instrument at all. Corrected. The rule governs a missing primitive or a missing discriminator. It does not forbid using an instrument already held in the firm's library, provided its binding is recorded as a binding rather than presented as a derivation (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`, Q3). The original overstatement is preserved here rather than silently erased.
