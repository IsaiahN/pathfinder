## What is being decided, in effect terms

A firm must decide a reward, a rating, and a feedback message for a unit of work. The ground (the actual contribution of that work) does not register cleanly in the available readings, because (a) the output is hard to measure and (b) the output is pooled with other people's output. The two conditions form a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9). The options and what they lead to:

- **Option A:** treat the metric or rating as the anchor and refine it until it looks precise. This leads to sharpening on an axis that cannot carry the signal (Fig 6 :29), pooled readings that erase the per-person residual (Fig 1 :22, Fig 10 :26), and a convention that decides an outcome while calling itself a measurement (Fig 10 :47).
- **Option B:** treat the question as constitutively about people, so a maintained procedure with voice, error correction, per-person accounting, and independent low-overlap frames is the legitimate anchor; the metric is only an instrument (Fig 2 :36-38). This leads to a checkable shape with a written refuter and an honest stop where no reading exists.

An answer would look like: a constraint on the form a fair evaluation system must take, plus the conditions under which it fails, plus the instrument that would close the open slot. Bind that description as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction") so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

**What this folder is NOT:** a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on (see `05_what_shape_means.md`, section 7).
