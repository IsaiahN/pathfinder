## Wider literature that bears (read, secondary)

1. **Holmstrom 1982, contract theory.** Joint output as the only observable indicator makes individual shirking unidentifiable, which is the economics version of the pooled-reading failure (Fig 1 :22, Fig 10 :26). Predicts that a fair system must import information about individual inputs rather than rely on the aggregate.
2. **Holmstrom and Milgrom 1991, multitask incentives.** A single measure aggregates disparate aspects of performance and omits essential ones. Predicts the Fig 6 :29 failure of sharpening an axis that cannot carry the signal.
3. **Tyler 1988, procedural justice.** Voice, representation, honesty, ethicality, decision quality and error correction drive fairness judgments. Predicts the Fig 10 :19 requirement to install what can be violated.
4. **Organizational justice (Colquitt 2001 as reported).** Four factors: distributive, procedural, interpersonal, informational. Predicts that a fair evaluation cannot be reduced to one number; it needs a comparison, a process, an explanation and respectful treatment.
5. **Performance appraisal literature (Scullen et al. 2000; idiosyncratic rater effect).** More than half the variance in ratings is rater-specific, not ratee performance. Predicts Fig 2 :40: agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, and a single rater's score is mostly a reading of the rater.

These fields are low-overlap with the figures (economics, law and psychology versus a domain-agnostic metatheory), so their agreement is weak but genuine corroboration. They do not decide the verdict; the figures do.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the shape claim and its refuter. Send the architecture only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."** Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the claim were true, before showing the framework.
- **Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** The formula is a generator. Giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** A wrong fairness procedure is expensive and hard to undo, so spend on independent checkers, not on a larger agreeing audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their stage-1 verdict; (4) only then offer the three links, noting that the figures are the authority, not the summary.
