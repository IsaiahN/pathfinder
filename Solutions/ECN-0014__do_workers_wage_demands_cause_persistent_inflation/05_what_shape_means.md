## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit (figures README, local copy): the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name a magnitude. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0014 the shape is: a typed cycle, split into a union of conditions, measured per slot, with a named common cause and a named reversing instrument. The missing thing is not a number yet; it is the source-discriminating instrument that would let a number be meaningful.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (re-opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach file sends the refuter, not the conclusion.
- *Library of Babel* (carried) on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

For this question, describing the effect rather than the cause means saying: "if wages were the origin of persistence, then holding demand, expectations and supply fixed while shifting wage demands should move prices through the markup channel." Anyone with a natural experiment can check that, whatever school they belong to.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

**Correction to my own section 4 below (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 for the parallel case).** I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the use of the words wages, prices, markups and expectations. That was over-strong. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the frame. Wages, prices, markups and expectations are already held economic atoms. Naming them is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive and not a forbidden name. What remains forbidden is asserting that the framework itself returns a number or names a dominant link; that is a ground question.

## 4. Source discrimination: why a co-movement cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened) states the mechanism. An instrument reads a color and says it is green, but "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And the resolution: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Also: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here: the observed wage-price co-movement is the green reading. Wage push, price response and a shared demand-and-expectations source are three different sources of the same reading. A better instrument, not a better argument, is owed. This is the strongest parallel between the essay and Fig 2's collapse 2, and it is why F2 and F3 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are type claims rather than empirical preferences.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened) confirms the validator limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So this entire audit is a candidate, not a settlement.

## 5. Agreement, drift and the wage-inflation literature

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (re-opened) gives the social version of Fig 2. "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources." And the essay gives a navigator image that makes the same point as the figure: "Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark, and nobody can talk the landmark into moving."

A literature that reads the same national accounts wage series is one evidence pool wearing many names (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13). The fixed landmark here is the realized wage bargain and the realized price outcome, which do not change because a school of thought thinks otherwise.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous wage-inflation economists assembled to agree with the shape claim would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, by people whose public work touches the residual, and let an instrument or a natural experiment settle it. Receptiveness is never a selection criterion.

## 8. The shape, stated once

For ECN-0014 the figure-licensed shape is:

> A typed cycle (Fig 12 :121), not a one-way chain; a union of overlapping conditions to be split rather than searched (Fig 9 :4-5, :54); a residual indexed per slot rather than pooled into an average (Fig 1 :19, :22); a named common cause so the correlation is not mistaken for a source (Fig 2 :20); and a reversing instrument still owed, with the magnitude declared `gap` rather than filled (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134).

That is a shape, not an answer. It does not say whether wage demands or price responses dominate in any particular episode. It says what any admissible answer to that question must look like before it can be checked.
