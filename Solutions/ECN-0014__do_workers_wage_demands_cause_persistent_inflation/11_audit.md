## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as "The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold." Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set on correlation versus causation, bond order, cycles and slot indexing.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "it is a cycle and a union, not a chain; correlation cannot decide direction; the magnitude is a gap".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete economic answer "wages and prices form a loop; both arms are real; which dominates is owed to an instrument".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which collapses into an economy-wide average when the abstract form is applied casually;
- the **bond typing**, since "feedback" used loosely hides the difference between a symmetric conjunction and an ordered production;
- the **instrument requirement**, since "which dominates is owed to an instrument" can be read as "no answer is possible" rather than as a specific exogenous-shift design;
- the **domain separation**, since averaging D1 through D4 would erase the different anchors.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a cycle with a signed loop, split before search, with a common source named. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
