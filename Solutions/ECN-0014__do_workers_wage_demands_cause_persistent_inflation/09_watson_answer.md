## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the choice between (A) workers' wage demands cause persistent inflation and (B) wages mainly respond to prices and labor-market conditions. What each leads to: A sends a one-way wage-push account that mistakes a symmetric co-movement for an ordered origin and omits the common source; B sends a one-way response account that omits the real cost channel through unit labor costs and markups; both treat a union compound and a cycle as a single chain. A third possibility, the one derived here, is to refuse the either/or as a type error, split the union per subset and slot, measure each arm separately with the common source screened off, and declare the dominance question `gap` until an instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The co-movement of wages and prices with an unresolved direction. Arity: three arms, two ordered links plus at least one common source; the question names only two. Symmetry: the observation is a symmetric conjunction, so it cannot carry an ordered conclusion. Scale: micro bargain, meso contract and sector, macro index, and across time from impact to persistence. Slot indexed: which wages, which prices, which contract.
- **D1, wage setting.** Actors: workers, unions, firms, bargainers, expectation formers. Currency: nominal wage claims and settlements. Anchor: the realized wage bargain and wage expectation surveys. This is a domain because the anchor is nameable.
- **D2, price setting.** Actors: firms, price setters, input suppliers. Currency: prices, unit labor costs, markups. Anchor: realized price indices and firm-level prices. Nameable.
- **D3, common cause and expectations.** Actors: central bank, demand conditions, supply and import shocks, expectation formers. Currency: demand pressure, expectations, shock measures. Anchor: measured inflation outcomes and independent shock measures. Nameable, and omitted by the question.
- **D4, measurement and identification.** Actors: researchers, instruments, estimators. Currency: identification, residual, evidence pool. Anchor: published data plus exogenous variation. Nameable; this is the domain that decides direction.
- **Partition or union.** Union compound, not partition. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." An indexed contract under strong demand is in both conditions, a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :57-58).
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive per-slot claims, settlements and prices; build vocabulary for expectation, slack, markup and indexation; state what would refute each arm; choose an identification strategy; carry forward only methods. The middle link is instrument-poor (Fig 3 :25-27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Refuse the either/or. The figure-decided shape is a signed cycle over a union of overlapping conditions, measured per slot, with the common source named and screened off, persistence separated from pass-through, and the directional verdict bound to an exogenous instrument still owed. Both wage-to-price and price-to-wage arms are real; the claim that wage demands are the *origin* of persistence in general is a regularity to be tested with the persistent-and-flat rule, not a conclusion. The question of which arm dominates, where, and by how much is `gap`, unreached rather than unreachable, and reported as a verdict rather than silence. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. This is a `specified` and `general` result, ungrounded, candidate only.

- **cost if wrong:** the shape could over-constrain a genuinely one-way mechanism. The packet states this cost explicitly and makes it locatable: if a single chain mechanism works in every subset with no feedback and no common cause, C1 and the union reading fail and the shape is withdrawn. The practical cost of acting on the wrong arm in the meantime is a policy error applied to the wrong link, which is why Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is reduced by keeping the magnitude as `gap` instead of asserting a number.
- **undoable:** yes. The three checkable sub-claims are separable; if C3 fails, per-slot measurement is dropped while the cycle and common-source claims survive. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can be sent. The refuters are written before the conclusion so a reader can see exactly what would change the verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An exogenous wage shift with demand, expectations and supply held fixed that produces no price response would kill the wage-to-price arm; the mirror test would kill the price-to-wage arm; a single chain mechanism working in every subset with a shrinking dispute would kill the union and cycle reading; a direct pre-bargain wage-demand instrument would close the middle link; a measured R_T near zero would collapse the micro-macro distinction. Prestige, school authority or the number of agreeing papers is not such an instrument.
