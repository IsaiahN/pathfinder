# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package for ECN-0014

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain structural claims, `general` on the shape of the answer, `gap` on the empirical magnitude and on which link dominates in any given episode. Nothing here is `grounded`. Every substantive file is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Question:** Do workers' wage demands cause persistent inflation, or do wages mainly respond to prices and labor-market conditions?

**Source:** economics questions.txt line 21. Section 2, Inflation, unemployment, and price-setting.

**Registry status:** pending.

**Boundary.** Nothing in this folder is in the Tether repository. No repository file was read for the purpose of editing and no repository file was changed. Sources used were the canonical figure text at `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, the canonical usage rule at `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_SEAT_MAP_Field_Guide_amended.md`, the eight essays, and the public URLs listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. All 12 files in this folder were written here; the only file touched outside the folder is the required append to `Logs/completion_log.md`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the empirical question. A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency is not a measurement, and the figures forbid a frame from certifying its own contact with the ground (Fig 2 :35, Fig 10 :42).

Three reasons for the refusals, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not add an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Many readers of the same wage series are one frame wearing many names.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. Ask for the one thing a knowledgeable person can give in twenty minutes: a refutation of the shape claim. Record their verdict on stage 1 before offering the framework at stage 2, and never pool a later agreement as corroboration.
