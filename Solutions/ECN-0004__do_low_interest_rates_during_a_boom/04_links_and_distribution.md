## 1. World sources actually fetched

| source | URL | what was on the page that bears on this question |
|---|---|---|
| Austrian business cycle theory | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_business_cycle_theory | States that credit expansion "pushes the market rate of interest below the 'natural rate'"; records Sraffa's objection that Hayek "failed to define a single 'natural' rate of interest"; reports that "Empirical economic research findings are inconclusive, with different economic schools of thought arriving at different conclusions." |
| Debt deflation | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debt_deflation | Fisher's nine-link chain runs through distress selling, falling prices and bankruptcies; Bernanke's version has banks tightening credit so that "a credit crunch lowers investment and consumption, which leads to declining aggregate demand". A channel account, not an allocation account. |
| Neutral rate of interest | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_rate_of_interest | "It cannot be observed directly." Powell is quoted that "guiding policy by the stars ... has been quite challenging of late because our best estimates of the location of the stars have been changing significantly". This is a proto-instrument that returns something and fails to resolve. |
| Goodhart's law | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart%27s_law | "Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes." A measure used as a policy target stops being an anchor. The page also records the related Lucas critique. |
| Capital budgeting | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_budgeting | "Cash flows are discounted at the cost of capital to give the net present value"; "the firm would accept all projects with positive NPV"; projects are ranked against a hurdle and a budget. This is the per-project mechanism that a rate gap would have to pass through. |
| Outcome bias | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outcome_bias | "the outcome bias is an error made in evaluating the quality of a decision when the outcome of that decision is already known", with Baron and Hershey 1988 as the experimental source. Corroborates the retrodiction warning in F4 from a low-overlap field. |
| Ecological trap | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_trap | "If either the habitat quality or the cue changes so that one does not reliably indicate the other, organisms may be lured into poor-quality habitat." A shape analogue only: a formerly reliable signal is decoupled from what it used to indicate. Not economic evidence. |
| Simpson's paradox | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simpson%27s_paradox | "a trend appears in several groups of data but disappears or reverses when the groups are combined." Corroborates the per-slot versus aggregate separation in F2 from mathematics/statistics. |

## 5. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a full theory before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separation and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the separation were true before giving them the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :40** agreement within a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. A send selected for people who already favour one school produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong attribution of recession to the wrong mechanism, which is high, so prefer frames with separate experience.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the separation claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then, if they opt in, the three links and the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 6. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the separation claim and the pre-registered refuter. No theory, no links, no request for agreement or trust.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, offer the derivation and the three source links. Their stage 1 verdict is already on record and is never pooled with later agreement.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse on a problem with a real ground, with the instruction to write the refuter first and state the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage 1 output survives. The separation in `01` section 4 is the stage 1 output. If a refuter fires, stop and withdraw it before sending anything.
