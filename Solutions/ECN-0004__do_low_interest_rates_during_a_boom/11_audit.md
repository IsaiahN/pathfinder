## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, and Fig 4 :30 requires that it be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 9 :4-5 and :54-57 (union, split), Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor test), Fig 10 :24 (channel versus ground) and Fig 5 :43-44 (description-length bargain).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: split the union, index per project, require a non-updating comparator, keep channel separate from allocation, admit malinvestment only forward, report the gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a full economic account of a named boom and bust with estimated rates, project data and credit spreads.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The identity of the slots.** "Per project" is a schema without a list; the economic frame must decide whether a slot is a plant, a loan, a security or a firm, and the figures do not decide it.
- **The comparator's provenance.** "Non-updating" is a property claim that the shorthand does not supply. Model, vintage and revision history have to be added from the world, and adding them is a provenanced binding rather than a figure derivation.
- **The union boundary.** Which conditions are present in a given episode is an empirical enumeration (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed"), not something the union verdict itself returns.
- **The channel/allocation separation.** A concrete failure can be both at once, and the shorthand gives no rule for assigning a weight; the rule would have to come from an instrument.

One thing survives cleanly: a policy-moved rate cannot be the anchor that settles the policy question, and an average over projects cannot say which project was wrong. That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no economic measurement was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the full figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
