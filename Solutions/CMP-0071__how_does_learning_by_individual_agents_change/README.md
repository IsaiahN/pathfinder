**Question restated as an effect.** R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| on every agent and every object slot (Fig 1 :11, :19). An individual agent learns when it reduces that residual and admits a term into its own library Γ. The question asks what this does to the long-term behavior of the population the agents form. Option A: collective behavior is the aggregate of individual improvements, so more learning by more agents produces a proportionately better system, and pooled agreement among learners counts as evidence. Option B: collective behavior changes only through what crosses the membrane between the one-agent loop and the population loop, only when the change alters contact in the agent-and-habitat system, and only with a measured round-trip gap; pooled agreement is one evidence pool, not corroboration. What each leads to: A sends an unmeasured verdict and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B sends a checkable shape, written falsifiers and an open slot until an instrumented system settles it.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** A binding of four quantities: (1) the set of individual terms that become reapplicable methods and cross up, (2) the head starts that come down to later agents, (3) the change in agent-habitat contact, and (4) the round-trip gap R_T between them. Until that binding is written down, the sought-for shape is a preference no internal check can see (Fig 5 :62-64). It is written down in `01` section 4 as a pre-registered refuter, before the conclusion.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons. First, a room does not supply a ground: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13), and Seat Map :283 says *"A room does not upgrade the label."* Second, selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: Seat Map :163, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Third, sending the architecture before asking for a verdict converts a checker into a participant: Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one together.
