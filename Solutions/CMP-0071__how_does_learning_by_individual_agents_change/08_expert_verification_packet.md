# Expert verification packet - the Tether on individual learning and collective behavior

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in agent-based modeling or collective learning can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the figure laws, `specified` on the application, not `grounded`.** No agent-based model was run. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Individual learning does not change a collective system by summing learning curves. It changes it through a directed membrane: a learned success is a recording of one occasion, what crosses upward is a reapplicable method, and what crosses downward is a head start that makes the next agent's search cheaper. Separately, capability is a property of the agent and its habitat, so an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves. Residuals stay indexed per agent and per slot, independence rather than headcount decides whether the union of learners has surplus, and the cross-scale transform is lossy with a round-trip gap that must be measured. The framework does not name which long-term behavior will appear. It offers the shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the dials an instrumented system would measure.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 11

- **Statement:** the one-agent loop and the population loop are joined by a directed filter, not by aggregation. Only reapplicable methods cross up, only head starts come down, and an agent-only improvement at fixed contact cannot move the collective.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :23 - *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Fig 4 :24 - *"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."* Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled multi-agent system changes its long-term collective behavior while no method crosses, no head start descends, and contact with the ground is held fixed.

### C2 - measurement-facing; checkable by anyone who has run an ABM or a collective-learning experiment

- **Statement:** the collective signal must stay indexed per agent and per slot, and independence, not headcount, is the parameter that decides whether the union has surplus.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 6 :53 - *"searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names"*; Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 8 :45 - *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7: *"Distribute the search and keep the searchers independent."*
- **What to check:** in your own system, does a single aggregate learning curve hide a live agent or slot? Do agents with correlated evidence produce genuine new reach, or only agreement? Hong and Page 2004 and Lorenz et al. 2011 (both opened, see `10`) report the two sides of this from different fields.
- **Refuter:** averaged per-agent residuals preserve the live-slot signal where one agent changes the outcome; or a maximally correlated pool matches an independent pool on a ground measure at equal ability.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with an instrument owed

- **Statement:** the cross-scale transform is lossy and its round-trip gap R_T is the honest report of what was given up. The question is a union compound, so transport through the membrane and change of contact both have to hold. The specific outcome is a `gap`.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30 - *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- **Refuter:** a transform with identically zero round-trip gap on a named ground and a named code; or one intervention that restores both union conditions with no categorically different residue.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that more agents or more learning is better. Headcount is a budget filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that agreement among learners is corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that a learned term transfers as content. Only methods and head starts cross (Fig 4 :23-24).
- Not a name for the resulting collective behavior. Collective intelligence, shared error, resilience and brittleness are all possible branches of the union; which one is on a given system needs a ground reading.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, and the framework's own society-of-agents sketch says of itself *"Nothing here has been measured for a society of Tether agents."*
- Not that the framework's authorship or citation record is evidence. Prestige can fund an action to be tried and cannot settle it.

## 4. All seven pre-registered falsifiers

Written in `01` section 4 before this packet was composed. If any fires, the shape is wrong or must be narrowed.

1. Collective behavior changes with no method crossing, no head start descending and contact held fixed.
2. Averaging per-agent residuals preserves the live-slot guidance signal.
3. A maximally correlated evidence pool matches an independent pool on a ground measure.
4. An agent-only improvement at fixed contact moves a collective ground reading.
5. A cross-scale transform has an identically zero round-trip gap on a named ground and code.
6. One intervention restores both union conditions with no categorically different residue.
7. Library growth at fixed reach and fixed contact increases population reach.

## 6. What would settle it

An instrumented system, not a further argument. From the society-of-agents essay, the measurable dials are: how different the members are, how much of the problem must be done in sequence, how costly it is to stay in sync, and how many generations the group keeps building. A ground reading that varies those while holding the shape fixed would name the branch, which is the `gap` this packet refuses to fill by assertion.

## 7. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 4 :22-25 and Fig 11 :40. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own model or experiment: do correlated learners add reach, or only agreement?
3. Name one measurement that would give a nonzero R_T for your system, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. No agreement is requested and none will be pooled as corroboration.
