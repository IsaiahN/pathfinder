## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the outcome

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the next line says it cannot say which primitive is missing because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a shape is the set of constraints any account of individual-to-collective learning must satisfy. It is not a value of the collective outcome. Concretely here:

- a shape: individual learning reaches the collective only through a directed membrane, with a change of contact, with independence between frames, and with a measured round-trip gap;
- a name: this system will become collectively intelligent, will lock into shared error, will be more resilient, will be more brittle.

The first can be stated before any model is run. The second cannot, without a ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on method: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel*, on multiple searchers: *"Convergence towards the answer is faster with multiple helpers, but communication between them is paramount and would have to be virtually 1:1 to be effective."* This is the essay-level form of Fig 2 :31 and Fig 6 :53. Helpers help only while they remain separate vantage points.
- *Library of Babel*, on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For CMP-0071 that condition is not met, which is why the answer stops at a shape.

## 3. The framework's own society-of-agents sketch is directly about this question

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* is the closest thing in the corpus to a direct treatment of CMP-0071. It must be read carefully, because it is framework-internal and explicitly unbuilt.

Its central claims, quoted from the opened essay:

- *"What turns new agents into capable agents is not a smarter agent. It is being born into a group that has already learned things: generations of agents, sharing a place where knowledge is stored, sorted and passed on."*
- *"The group shares methods, never recordings."*
- *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."*
- *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."*
- *"What comes down to a newborn agent is a head start: candidates that make its search cheaper. It still has to test each one in its own world."*
- *"If every agent's w drifts to the average, the spread is gone and the gradients are spent."*
- *"None of this gives one number for 'how much smarter.' It gives the dials: how different the members are, how much of the problem must be done in sequence, how costly it is to stay in sync, and how many generations the group keeps building."*

The last quote is the cleanest statement of the `gap` in F9. The framework supplies dials, not a magnitude or a sign. It does not say whether a particular system's long-term behavior will improve or degrade; it says which quantities to measure to find out.

The essay also states its own limit, which must be preserved: *"This is a design sketch, and nothing in it has been built"* and *"Nothing here has been measured for a society of Tether agents."* This folder inherits that limit. It is a second simulated layer over an unbuilt sketch, not a measured result.

One mismatch worth recording. The essay says a room is one problem's work spread across many bodies, and inside a room everything can be shared, including recordings, while only general methods move up into the commons after the room closes. That distinction is not stated in this form in the figures, which say simply *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22). I use the figure rule as the authority and treat the room/commons distinction as an essay-level elaboration, debited against independence rather than presented as figure-derived. This is a strain between sources, not between figures, and it is kept visible.

## 4. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For CMP-0071 the ground is an instrumented agent-based model, a laboratory population, or a field system with a defined outcome, not the agreement of the agents.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world sources in `09` are used this way: they corroborate the shape from different fields, but none of them names the outcome of a specific system.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a verdict

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this field that means a well-cited simulation result is a budget to try a shape, not a verdict on it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is designing collective-learning systems on a false theory of how learning transfers, so independence is worth paying for.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, quoted verbatim from the opened essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures and the opened essays. They are **not** tests of the claim and are labelled as such everywhere. They cannot settle CMP-0071.

## 7. Consequence for outreach

The first contact must carry the shape and its falsifiers, never the architecture. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Seat Map :149: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* An expert in agent-based modeling who has absorbed the full framework before giving a verdict has joined the system being checked, and their later agreement is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Ask what they would count as a failure first. Record it. Then, if they are curious, offer the figures.
