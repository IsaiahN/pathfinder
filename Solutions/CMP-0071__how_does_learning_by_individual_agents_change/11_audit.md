## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold."* Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :22-25 (membrane), Fig 11 :39-40 (contact), Fig 1 :19/:22 (indexing), Fig 2 :31/:40 (independence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: individual learning reaches the collective through a directed membrane, a change of contact, independent frames and a measured gap; do not sum learning curves; the outcome is unnamed.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete ABM claim of the form "doubling the learning rate of agents doubled collective accuracy."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **which method crossed**, and whether it was compressed or was a recording (Fig 4 :23-25);
- **whether contact changed**, which the concrete claim never measures (Fig 11 :40);
- **whether the extra agents were independent**, which the concrete claim treats as headcount (Fig 6 :53).

Conversely one thing survives cleanly: a learned success is not transferable as content, so "more learning" cannot be the whole explanation. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
