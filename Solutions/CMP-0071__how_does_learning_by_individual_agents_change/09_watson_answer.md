## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the map from individual-agent learning to collective long-term behavior. Option A: the collective is the aggregate of individual improvements and pooled learner agreement is evidence. Option B: the collective changes only through a directed membrane, a change in agent-habitat contact, independent frames and a measured round-trip gap; pooled agreement is one evidence pool. What each leads to: A sends an unmeasured verdict and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B sends a checkable shape, seven refuters written in advance, and an open outcome until an instrumented system settles it. R is |Γ(b,a) − o′| per agent and per slot (Fig 1 :19), never a single aggregate.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Individual learning changes a collective system through a directed membrane, through a change in agent-habitat contact, and through independence between frames. It does not change the collective by summing learning curves, and pooled learner agreement is not evidence. The transform is lossy and its round-trip gap R_T is owed as a measurement. Residuals stay indexed per agent and slot. The question is a union compound, so a single lever aimed at individual learning leaves the contact condition binding. Which specific long-term behavior appears is a declared `gap`, unreached until an instrument reads it. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `general` on the laws, `specified` on the application, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** a wrong design rule for collective-learning systems. Teams would be scaled by headcount instead of independence, methods would be replaced by recorded successes, and contact would be held fixed while capability was claimed. The cost is bounded because the shape is stated with seven refuters and a provenance trail, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable part is spending an outside expert's independence by sending the architecture before their verdict (Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers: collective change at fixed contact with no crossing; averaged residuals preserving the live-slot signal; a correlated pool matching an independent pool; an agent-only improvement moving a collective ground reading; an identically zero round-trip gap on named ground and code; one intervention restoring both union conditions with no categorically different residue; library growth at fixed reach and contact increasing population reach. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that collective outcomes are fully predicted by averaged individual learning curves would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
