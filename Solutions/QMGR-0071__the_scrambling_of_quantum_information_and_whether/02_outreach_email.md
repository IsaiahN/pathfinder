**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about scrambling bounds, before any theory

> Hi [name],

> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Scrambling is not a primitive. It is what happens
> when the remainder left by one turn of a composed system re-enters the next turn and compounds
> through a chain that amplifies. The framework's own line puts it this way: *"a remainder
> compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards
> reappears above it as noise."* From that, a speed limit on scrambling is a constraint on how
> fast that remainder can compound per crossing. It prices the budget. It does not by itself
> settle what the scrambled state is.
>
> **The question has to be split.** "Universal speed limit" bundles three things: an upper bound
> on a growth exponent, a lower bound on a scrambling time, and a claim that one bound covers all
> systems. The framework's rule is that a case in three conditions is not a harder case of one,
> so these need separate loops. I am not offering a single number.
>
> **What survives the split, with mode attached:**
> 1. A bound is owed, because the compounding is finite: `general`, figure-derived.

>    are not derived by the framework. `specified`, ungrounded.

>    actors, the currency and the anchor change. The kernel form is retained. `general` on form,

## Two-stage offer, explicit

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the split and the refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure.
   Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the
   figures are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes
   its own refuter and states its own mode.

Stage 1 precedes stage 2 because a recipient who has absorbed the theory before being asked for a
verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).
