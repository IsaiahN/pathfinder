## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it
to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually
uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 13 :32 and :88 (derived chaos, remainder
   re-enters), Fig 9 :60 (filter rule), Fig 4 :20 and Fig 10 :68 (levels differ).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: scrambling is a bounded derived
   rate; a limit is a filter; split the union; universality in form not number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claims: λ bounded by 2πT/ħ, a minimum time
   dependent on entropy and temperature, a Lieb-Robinson velocity, one universal speed limit.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **operand type** of the comparison bond at second order (quantities versus settled molecules
  now atoms);
- the **class and window conditions** on exponential OTOC growth, which the shorthand "bounded
  rate" tends to drop;
- the **distinction between an exponent bound and a time bound**, which the single phrase "speed
  limit" collapses;
- the **import status** of the physics bounds, which the shorthand can make look like framework
  derivations.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: scrambling is a derived remainder-compounding rate, and a
limit on it prices the budget without handing a verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was
**not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the
enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the
metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
