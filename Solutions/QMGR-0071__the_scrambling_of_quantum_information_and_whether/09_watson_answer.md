## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the strong universal reading does not. Scrambling is a derived rate of residual
amplification, and a limit on it is a filter on the budget, not a witness for a verdict. The question
is a union compound, and its three conditions must be kept separate: the exponent bound and the time
bound are different compounds sharing a functional group, and universality holds in kernel form and
not as one numeric constant across frames with different actors, currency and anchor. Every bound is
conditional on the habitat. The two specific physics bounds are imports with provenance, not
derivations. The operand type of the comparison bond at second order remains a named strain. This is
a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest figure-grounded point is the filter rule: a bound
prices what a search may do, it does not decide what is true.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, splits the union and records
  refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is
  spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-number conclusion instead of a
  conditional shape. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That
  cost is avoidable by leading with the residual, the split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The strong quantifier is removable without touching the derivation;
  `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. The specific
  bindings remain as provenanced imports, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating experiment that shows
  the same conditional bound governing exponential and power-law regimes would settle the domain of
  validity; a derivation of one numeric bound in two frames with different anchors and no shared
  evidence would clear the kernel-form claim (and falsify Fig 4 :20); a formal result resolving the
  operand type of the comparison bond would close the live strain. Until one of these fires, the
  verdict stands as `candidate`.
