## Three things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The word "universal" is already contested.** The Scholarpedia review documents class and
   window dependence and many exceptions; the 2026 PRL report claims an exact bound "in every
   quantum system". These are not the same claim. The figures resolve it by splitting the union
   (Fig 9 :4-5): form universality survives, a single numeric constant does not follow.
2. **The 2026 result is a lower bound on time, not an upper bound on rate.** The two bounds are
   different compounds (Fig 12 :125, two currencies that do not add). Do not present them as one
   limit.
3. **Both the MSS bound and Lieb-Robinson are conditional on the habitat** (bounded clusters,
   local interactions, finite temperature). Fig 11 :39: capability is a property of agent and
   habitat, never of the agent alone. Drop the conditions and the claim changes.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :23-25**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full
  framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with
  the split and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient
  who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being
  checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
  Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat map**: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
  A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the three-way split; (2) ask what they would
count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures,
not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, split and pre-registered refuter. No theory. Ask what they would count as
   a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as
   corroboration (Fig 2 :40: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share
   an evidence pool).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and
   states their own mode. The article's own warning is that outputs *"could never be verified if
   the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the
question bundles three conditions (F3). Split before sending.
