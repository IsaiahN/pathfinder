## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks whether the scrambling of quantum information sets a **universal speed limit**. Unpacked as effects:

- **Scrambling** is the process by which information initially local to a few degrees of freedom becomes delocalized across a many-body system. It is diagnosed by the growth of an out-of-time-order correlator, by a Lyapunov-like exponent λ, and by a scrambling time t* after which the correlator saturates.
- A **speed limit** is a bound. In the literature this appears in two different forms: an **upper** bound on the growth exponent (maldacena/shenker/stanford, λ ≤ 2π k_B T / ħ) and a **lower** bound on the scrambling time (vikram/galitski/shou, 2026, entropy and temperature dependent).
- **Universal** is a quantifier over all systems, all scales and all frames.

Option A: scrambling sets one universal speed limit, so a single bound governs all systems. Option B: scrambling is a bounded rate of residual amplification, the bound is a filter on the budget, and universality holds only in the kernel form, not as one number across frames whose actors, currency and anchor differ. What each leads to: A hands a `general`-mode verdict to a claim whose three conditions have not been separated; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters and an open slot until the ground settles the numeric claim.

Per formula step "Direction", the sought-for shape is made an ordinary term before the run: **an answer would look like a bound on how fast a remainder can compound per crossing, stated in effect terms, with its domain of validity named.** That term is now refusable like any other.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1: Chaos and scrambling are derived, not primitive (decisive for the shape)

Fig 13 :32 (verbatim, from the extract):

> **"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."**

Fig 13 :88:

> **"The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."**

The canonical article's substrate section says the same: *"the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."* So scrambling is a **rate of residual amplification across crossings**, not a substance and not a primitive slot. A "speed limit" on scrambling is therefore a constraint on how fast the remainder can compound per turn. This is a `general` structural claim, triangulated across Fig 13 :32, :88 and the canonical article's substrate note. It does not depend on any QM/GR binding.

**Refuter:** show chaos is a member of the minimum set, or that the remainder does not re-enter the next turn. Either breaks the derivation.

### F2: A speed limit is a filter on the budget, not a witness for the verdict

Fig 9 :60:

> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

The seat map's summary is the same rule: *"Deadlines are filters, never verdicts."* The 2026 PRL result is a **lower bound on time** and the MSS result is an **upper bound on exponent**. Both price the budget (how fast or how soon). Neither tells you what the scrambled state is, whether unitarity is preserved at a specific contact, or which frame settles the question. Treating the existence of a bound as if it settled the universality question is exactly the named error.

**Refuter:** produce a derivation in which the bound alone entails a verdict about the state of the system, without a separate ground reading.

### F3: The question is a union and must be split before searching

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

The phrase "universal speed limit" bundles three conditions: an exponent bound, a time bound, and a quantifier over all systems. World sources confirm the split rather than resolve it: the Scholarpedia review on OTOCs states that exponential growth "can only appear in a well-defined class of systems, and in a particular time-window" and that "there are many known exceptions that yield the behavior of Eq. (12) non-universal," while the 2026 PRL result proves a minimum time "in every quantum system." A single lever (one bound) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). The residue after splitting is smaller and categorically different: a compound with its own loop.

**Refuter:** show the three conditions are one condition, so a single bound settles exponent, time and universality together.

### F4: Universality is in the kernel form, not in one number

Fig 4 :20:

> **"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."**

Fig 10 :68:

> **"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."**

Fig 13 :14: energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*, and :68: *"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate."* The invariant across scales is the loop and the cross-kind rule. The numeric content of a bound is expressed in the currency of a frame (temperature, entropy, Hilbert-space dimension, interaction range). So "universal" can be true of the form (remainder compounds; a crossing discards detail; finiteness bounds the counting) and false of a single constant across black holes, strange metals and cold-atom lattices. The world literature already shows different currencies: 2πT/ħ, entropy and temperature, Lieb-Robinson velocity. Agreement among them is convergence in form, not one number.

**Refuter:** exhibit one numeric bound with the same value and the same derivation in two frames with different anchors and no shared evidence pool.

### F5: The bound depends on the habitat, not on the system alone

Fig 11 :39:

> **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**

Fig 10 :24:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Scrambling is a property of system plus environment: the temperature, the entropy budget and the interaction graph are part of the habitat, not optional details. Sekino and Susskind's fast-scrambler conjecture is explicitly conditional on "interactions ... between bounded clusters of degrees of freedom," and the Lieb-Robinson bound is conditional on local interactions. The 2026 PRL result is conditional on entropy and temperature. So the honest form of any bound is conditional, and a claim that drops the conditions is a different claim.

**Refuter:** show the same bound with the same value for a system whose interaction range, entropy and temperature are changed without changing the bound.

### F6: The two bounds are charged in two currencies that do not add

Fig 12 :125:

> **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**

The verbatim continuation names them: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* The exponent bound is priced in rate (inverse time) and the time bound in time; the entropy and temperature dependence is a description price, the experimental OTOC measurement is an action price. Summing them into one "speed limit" number is the error the figure names, not the fix. This is why the parent question's singular "a universal speed limit" is already a compression that the census must undo.

**Refuter:** give a single additive measure that converts a rate bound, a time bound and an entropy budget without losing the type difference.

### F7: The universal claim, if read as one frame for all systems, is refused

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 8 :39:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

The framework returns a shape, not a name, and it cannot certify a terminal frame. A universal numeric speed limit would be a last room: a frame with no outside left to import from. Fig 7 names *"the limit: no last room"*. So the figures refuse the universal claim only in its strong (single constant, all frames) reading. They do not refuse the existence of bounds; indeed F2 and F5 require them. This distinction matters for the verdict: **refuse the quantifier, retain the bound.**

**Refuter:** derive a terminal frame in which no further import is possible from any scale. Until that is done, the strong universal reading is a preference held as a term, which formula step 2 says cannot be seen by the loop.

### F8: One live strain: the operands of the bound at second order

The Operators table defines ⋛ as a threshold relation between two quantities. At second order the compared objects would be settled molecules now serving as atoms. Fig 12 :104 permits the demotion:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Whether an atom counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument, not an argument. This is recorded as a strain, not smoothed over. It is the only strain found after re-census.

**Refuter (for the strain):** show that a settled molecule can serve as a quantity operand of ⋛ at the next level, or show that it cannot and that the comparison must be between bare quantities at every level.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the seat map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Chaos or scrambling is shown to be a primitive member of the minimum set, not a derived remainder-compounding rate (breaks Fig 13 :32).
2. A bound is shown to settle a verdict about the state of the system without a separate ground reading (breaks Fig 9 :60).
3. A single numeric bound is derived with the same value and derivation in two frames with different anchors and no shared evidence pool (breaks Fig 4 :20 and Fig 10 :68).
4. The three conditions (exponent bound, time bound, universality) are shown to be one condition (breaks Fig 9 :4-5).
5. The interaction range, entropy and temperature of a system are changed without changing the bound (breaks Fig 11 :39 and Fig 10 :24).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
