# Expert verification packet: the Tether on scrambling and a universal speed limit

**What this is:** a claim in a form a scrambling researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters
are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.**
No measurement at the relevant scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a
ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Scrambling is not a primitive. It is the rate at which a remainder left by one turn of a composed
system re-enters the next turn and compounds through an amplifying chain. A speed limit on scrambling
is therefore a constraint on that compounding, which prices the budget and does not by itself settle
what the scrambled state is. The question bundles three conditions, an exponent bound, a time bound
and a universality quantifier, and these need separate loops. The exponent and time bounds are
different compounds sharing a functional group, not one limit with two spellings. Universality
survives in the kernel form (the cross-kind loop and the bounded remainder) and fails in the strong
reading (one numeric constant across frames whose actors, currency and anchor differ). Any bound is
conditional on the habitat: interaction range, entropy and temperature are part of the claim.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13 and Figure 9

- **Statement:** scrambling is a derived rate of residual amplification, and any bound on it is a
  filter on the budget, not a witness for a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :32 *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos,
  and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the
  budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **Refuter:** derive chaos or scrambling as a primitive member of the minimum set; or show that a
  bound alone entails a verdict about the state of the system with no separate ground reading.

### C2: physics-facing; checkable by a researcher against the two bounds

- **Statement:** the MSS upper bound on λ and the 2026 lower bound on t* are different compounds
  sharing a functional group (a threshold on a rate). They are not one limit written two ways, and
  both are conditional on the system's habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The
  difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 11 :39 *"capability is
  a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not
  decay, the channel does"* .
- **What to check:** is it true that the two bounds use the same ingredients (time, energy, entropy
  as ordering) but different bonds and directions? Does changing interaction range, entropy or
  temperature change the bound? The Scholarpedia review documents class and window dependence and
  many exceptions to exponential OTOC growth; the 2026 PRL report claims an exact bound in every
  quantum system. Are these claims about the same quantity?
- **Refuter:** show a reversible transform derives one bound from the other with no extra assumption;
  or exhibit the same numeric bound with the same value when habitat conditions are changed.

### C3: framework-internal derivation with one live strain

- **Statement:** universality holds in kernel form and not as one constant across levels. The first
  order claim (scrambling inside one system) must be settled before the second-order claim (comparing
  bounds and quantifying over all systems). The operand type of the comparison bond at second order
  is still open.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Fig 10 :68
  *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."* Fig 12
  :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact
  about a level and never about the world"* (the extract continues *"and there is no bottom"*).
- **Refuter:** derive one numeric bound with the same value and derivation in two frames with
  different anchors and no shared evidence; or show that a settled molecule cannot serve as a
  quantity operand of the comparison bond at the second order. The second is the named strain, not
  a hidden one.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that scrambling lacks a bound. The figures require a bounded compounding rate.
- Not a new physics result, not a new derivation of 2πT/ħ, and not a derivation of the 2026 PRL bound.
  Those are imports with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not one universal number, not a theory of everything, not a terminal frame.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an OTOC measurement or an experiment.
- Not that the world literature is unanimous. The conflict on the quantifier is recorded and was
  resolved by re-census (split the union), not by suppressing it.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Regime discriminator:** an experiment distinguishing exponential from power-law OTOC growth
   across system classes, testing whether one conditional bound predicts both.
2. **Operand-type test:** a derivation or instrument resolving whether a settled molecule counts as
   a quantity operand of the comparison bond at second order.
3. **Universality test:** two frames with different anchors and no shared evidence deriving the same
   numeric bound. This would falsify the kernel-form claim.
4. **Habitat test:** change interaction range, entropy and temperature while holding the bound fixed.
   If the bound does not change, C2 fails.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :32, Fig 9 :60, Fig 12 :117 and Fig 4 :20, four short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: are the exponent bound and the time bound different
   quantities, and are their domains of validity different?
3. Name one experiment that would discriminate the regimes, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
