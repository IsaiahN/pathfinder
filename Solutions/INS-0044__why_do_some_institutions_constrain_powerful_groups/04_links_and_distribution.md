## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source material.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 7 :35** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A room of agreeable readers is one long room.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing any framework material; (4) only then the local framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 (residual first).** Send the shape claim, the four refuters and the explicit non-claims. No framework, no internal vocabulary beyond what is needed to state the effect.
2. **Stage 2 (opt-in theory).** Only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded, offer the figures and formula. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the reader now holds the interiors.
3. **Stage 3 (method reuse).** Offer the formula as a generator with a ground and a self-written refuter per problem. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method; it does not permit sending an answer disguised as one.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The F9 gap must stay open, the four domain verdicts must stay separate, and no country or reform may be named as the figure's answer. F1 to F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are the claims on trial; if a checker breaks one, the packet is corrected before it goes anywhere else.
