# Outreach draft: check one shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four domain verdicts in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are checked against the verbatim figure text and the F9 gap is kept explicit. The output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about why some institutions constrain powerful groups

> Hi [name],
>
> I work on institutional constraints on power. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The difference between an institution that constrains a powerful group and one that reproduces that group's advantage is not the content of the formal rules. Two systems can carry the same impartiality clause. The difference is whether the checking anchor stays pinned, whether the people doing the checking draw on separate evidence, and whether the channel from the check to the actual outcome stays reachable across a generation. Where the anchor updates on the group it measures, the drift is coherent and insiders experience it as normal operation. Where checkers share one evidence pool, their agreement is the pool talking, not two independent confirmations. Where contact is not changed, improving the people in office changes nothing.
>
> This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. It names conditions, not a country and not a reform.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That I can name which specific check will bind in a given country. That more transparency alone breaks a reproduction loop. That formal rules are irrelevant. That this is settled by anything I have run here. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and I do not hold the instrument that would close the remaining gap.
>
> **The refuter, written before this result was finalised.** The shape claim fails if any of the following holds:
> 1. A documented case where the check is pinned, can register and sanction violations, shows no coherent drift, and yet the group's advantage is reproduced at the same rate as in captured cases, with no hidden contact channel.
> 2. A documented case where checkers share the group's evidence pool and personnel, yet constraint outcomes match the low-overlap case, with no convergent independent derivation.
> 3. A controlled comparison in which formal rule content alone, with anchor status and contact held fixed, produces a sustained drop in intergenerational reproduction.
> 4. A case where improving only the character or training of officeholders, with contact unchanged, produces that drop.
>
> I wrote these down before the run. If any holds, I withdraw the affected claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Figures as metalaws (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures folder. Run it on a case you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
