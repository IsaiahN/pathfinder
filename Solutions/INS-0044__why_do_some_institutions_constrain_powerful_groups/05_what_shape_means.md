## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INS-0044 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name a case. The shape derived in `01` is: a check that stays pinned, checkers whose evidence pools do not coincide, and a channel to the ground that survives a generation. That shape constrains any candidate explanation; it does not fill in a country, a reform or a date. "The formal rule exists" is a name-like filler, not a shape, because it fits both outcomes.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on method: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* In this question the bootstrapped terms were anchor, update, evidence pool, channel, habitat and closure. Each narrowed the residual.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence "transparent institution" was refused as a description; "can the group's action be seen and can the check register a violation" is the effect form.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The four refuters in `02` and `08` are exactly such descriptions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*. Here the finish is the F9 gap: the point at which the shape is stated and the missing discriminating instrument is named in effect terms but not held.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the status of the vocabulary: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."* Mapping the figures onto institutional study is a visible choice. It is not a discovery of the social world's own list, and naming one institution as the answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "which reform works" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading can come from one light, a yellow/blue mix, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For INS-0044 the institutional analogue is direct. "Compliance improved" is a reading. It can come from a genuinely independent check, from a captured check that stopped looking, from a change in reporting rules, or from a change in the population being sampled. A national governance index cannot comment on the source. Naming "independent judiciary" or "electoral commission" as the cause of a good reading is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator that ranges the check against something the group cannot move, not another name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the warrant condition: *"indiscernibility is not identity"*. A reform that looks like a constraint at the resolution of an aggregate index may still be a reproduction loop; the lineage of the reading must be kept.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, verbatim:

> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A prestigious reform, a respected court, an internationally praised anti-corruption agency: each can be given a budget to be tried. None settles whether the check actually binds. The same essay names the failure: *"someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result."*

This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no famous-name list and no ranking by likely agreement. A hearing is a budget. Agreement is not a verdict. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; and :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the group form: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* A panel of like-minded institutional reformers is one opinion said ten times. The same essay's own discipline note applies: *"Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice"* (echoing Fig 2 :34's coherent drift).

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay's own demo note says the outputs generated by an LLM with the formula *"must be seen as simulacra"*; *Arrival of the Fittest* states the limit plainly: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of institutional theory. They cannot settle the question they report on. A ground reading, meaning a documented case with a pinned anchor, low-overlap checkers and a reachable channel measured per slot, is required before any part of this moves to `grounded`.

## 7. Correction carried forward

An earlier reading in this folder family sometimes treats "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any framework term. That is too strong, and the correction is preserved rather than erased. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Gradient and network are already among the six structures (Fig 13 :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*, :24 *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*). Naming them in a social binding is a choice of vocabulary, provenanced as such, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden here is naming a specific institution as the figure's answer, because the F9 instrument that would discriminate among institutions is exactly what is missing. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
