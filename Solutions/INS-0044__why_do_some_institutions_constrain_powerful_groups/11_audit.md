## Independent domain census (written before reading the primary's conclusions)

I redid the census from the residual line, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four-place, (check, group, action, generation). Two values of one slot quantity, not two questions. Symmetry: "binds" is a threshold comparison, because a sanction either exceeds the group's absorption capacity or does not; it is not a symmetric join of two ingredients. Scale: single action, institutional loop and generational chain, with a lossy transform between the first two and the third.
- **D1 Rule-and-check frame.** Actors: statute, court, auditor, budget. Currency: authority and compliance. Anchor: the actual outcome for the group. Admitted.
- **D2 Concentrated group.** Actors: wealth holders, firms, families, networks. Currency: spendable difference and access. Anchor: reproduction of its own closure. Admitted.
- **D3 Outside checker.** Actors: press, audit, watchdog, researcher. Currency: evidence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **D4 Contact field.** Actors: donors, schools, professions, family, media. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Rule count as cause.** No anchor that is not produced by the frame being graded. Not a domain.
- **X2 Culture as sole cause.** No anchor independent of D4. Absorbed.

Union, not partition: three conditions conjoined, with intersections that bind; one lever clears only the single-condition regions. Order by dependency: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry. The middle link is the least instrumented.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :25 and :34 (anchor), Fig 7 :12-13 and :36 (chain of mirrors), Fig 8 :48-49 (overlap), Fig 11 :39-40 (habitat), Fig 9 :57 and :60 (union and filters), Fig 12 :117 and :121 (isomer and cycle).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: constraint is pinned anchor plus separate evidence plus reachable channel; reproduction is a closed cycle; the two are isomers; the specific arrangement is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a claim an institutional researcher would act on: "this agency is independent therefore it constrains", or "this transparency reform will break reproduction".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **the per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19). The shorthand says "constraint"; the concrete claim needs a named check and a named group, otherwise the live slot is averaged away.
- **the evidence-overlap condition** (Fig 8 :48-49). "Separate agencies" is not "separate evidence"; the concrete claim cannot be made from an organigram.
- **the union structure** (Fig 9 :57). The shorthand's three conditions can be read as a checklist; the concrete case has intersections that no single lever clears.
- **the negation requirement** (Fig 12's operators amendment). A vocabulary with subtraction but no refusal can remove a benefit and cannot say an arrangement is wrong; the shorthand's "subtracting link" can be read as subtraction only. `06_isomers_and_order.md` records this explicitly.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a check that updates on the group it grades cannot serve as the anchor. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no institutional ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
