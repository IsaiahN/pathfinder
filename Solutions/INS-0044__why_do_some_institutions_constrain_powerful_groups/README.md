## The question as an effect

Two institutions can hold the same written constitution, the same formal checks and the same anti-capture statute, and still differ in outcome. In one, a powerful group's action is corrected by a check that binds; in the other, the group's advantage is reproduced across generations. The residual is not "which rules exist" but "what makes the check settle against the powerful instead of updating on them." An answer would be a shape: the conditions under which a check stays pinned, independent and reachable, together with a refuter written before the check. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, no scraped contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim, and no receptiveness column.
- No bulk send plan and no request for trust or agreement.

Three figure-grounded reasons, stated in full in `04_links_and_distribution.md`: a room does not upgrade the label (Fig 7 :35), high correlation disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40), and a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). Legally, unsolicited bulk outreach to personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, which is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
