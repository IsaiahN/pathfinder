# Expert verification packet: the Tether on institutional constraint versus elite reproduction

**What this is:** a claim in a form a political scientist, institutional economist or public-administration researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the institutional claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No institutional outcome is asserted as measured here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The difference between an institution that constrains a powerful group and one that reproduces that group's advantage across generations is not the content of the formal rules. It is whether (i) the checking anchor stays pinned and can register a violation, (ii) the checkers draw on separate evidence rather than one shared pool, and (iii) the channel from the check to the actual outcome stays reachable across a generation. Where the anchor updates on the group, the drift is coherent and insiders experience it as normal. Where checkers share a pool, their agreement is the pool talking. Reproduction is a cycle handing the same closure to the next cohort; constraint is a chain with a subtracting and refusing link inserted. The conditions form a union, so a single lever clears single-condition cases and leaves intersections standing. The specific arrangement that would bind in a given case is a declared gap, not a conclusion.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** the operative distinction is anchor status, not rule content. A check that updates on the group drifts coherently; a pinned check is ranged against by both parties. A rule that cannot be violated decides no outcome.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :25 *"pinned: it does not update on A or B"*; Fig 2 :34 *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*; Fig 10 :19 *"install what can be violated"*; Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*.
- **Refuter:** a documented case where the check cannot be rewritten, registers and sanctions violations and shows no coherent drift, yet advantage is reproduced at the same rate as in captured cases with no hidden contact channel; or a controlled comparison where formal rule content alone, with anchor and contact fixed, lowers reproduction.

### C2. Framework-internal and field-facing, checkable by an institutional researcher

- **Statement:** intergenerational reproduction is a chain of mirrors handing one closure along, and genuine checking requires low evidence overlap. Separate letterhead is a filter; separate evidence is the condition. Formal impartiality with a shared pool is one frame wearing two names.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :12-13 *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*; Fig 8 :48-49 *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus"* and *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*; Fig 10 :86 *"Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."*
- **What to check:** in two cases you know, do the oversight bodies draw on separate evidence and separate careers, or do they share personnel, donors and information feeds? Does the next cohort inherit the same closure, or is there a selection step against what the previous cohort's check surfaced?
- **Refuter:** a documented case where checkers share the group's evidence pool and personnel yet constraint outcomes match the low-overlap case, with no convergent independent derivation; or a case where the same closure is handed along and evidence nevertheless accretes and advantage falls without import from a differing frame.

### C3. Framework-internal derivation, with a habitat extension

- **Statement:** capability is agent-and-habitat, the channel decays rather than the ground, and the conditions form a union. Structurally, the two outcomes are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance; reproduction is a cycle whose sign has closed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39-40 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*; Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*; Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **Refuter:** a case where improving only officeholder character, with contact unchanged, produces a sustained fall in reproduction; or a single lever that demonstrably clears the anchor, overlap and channel conditions at once, dissolving the union; or a reproduction loop shown to close without re-entry of output as input.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and no final institutional form. The framework's own rule is "no last room" (Fig 7 :6).
- Not that formal rules are irrelevant. They are the thing that can be violated.
- Not that transparency alone works. It is the perception link, not the verdict (Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not that a named country, reform or agency is the figure's answer. That is the F9 gap; naming one would be a binding with no source gate.
- Not that an aggregate governance score measures the residual. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an institutional outcome.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Anchor:** a case meeting the pinned-anchor conditions with no change in reproduction, or a controlled rule-content effect with anchor and contact fixed.
2. **Overlap:** a case where shared evidence and personnel produce outcomes as strong as low-overlap checking, with no convergent derivation.
3. **Channel:** a case where contact-changing reform fails to move the residual, or character-only reform moves it.
4. **Instrument (closes the gap):** a discriminator that can report low overlap and channel reachability before outcomes settle them. Until one exists, the specific arrangement remains unreachable and is reported as such.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :25 and :34, Fig 10 :19 and :47, Fig 8 :48-49 (five short passages).
2. Check C2 against one case you know well: do the checks share evidence, and does the next cohort inherit the same closure?
