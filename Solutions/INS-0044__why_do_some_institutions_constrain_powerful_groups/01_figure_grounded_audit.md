## 0. The question and the residual, in effect terms

> Why do some institutions constrain powerful groups while others reproduce their advantages across generations?

Stated as an effect, with the slot kept local:

- Actors: an institutional check C (a court, an auditor, a regulator, an electoral commission, a press freedom rule, a budget office) and a powerful group G (a firm, a family, a faction, a network).
- Prediction Γ(b,a): the check will bind when G acts against the shared rule.
- Observation o': what actually happened to G's action.
- Residual R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|, indexed per check-slot and per group-slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).
- Two outcomes: in one set of slots R closes (the check binds, G is corrected); in another set R persists across generations (the check does not bind, G's advantage is reproduced).

The question is therefore not "which constitution is best" and not "do rules matter." Both outcomes are compatible with identical written rules. The residual is about **anchor status**: does the check settle the question without changing its answer because of what G thinks (Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*), or has the check become another frame that updates on G?

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (Formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66). The answer would look like this, written before the derivation: a constraint outcome requires (i) a check that cannot be rewritten by G and can register a violation, (ii) checkers whose evidence does not come only from G's pool, and (iii) a channel to the ground that stays reachable across a generation. Reproduction is the complementary state: an updating anchor, a shared evidence pool, and a channel that decays. If the run cannot derive those three conditions from the figures, the shape fails and is recorded as failed. This paragraph is the pre-commitment, not a conclusion drawn after the fact.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

R by arity, symmetry and scale (Fig 9 :21-22 *"R, described / arity · symmetry · scale"*):

- **Arity:** a four-place relation, (check, powerful group, action, generation). "Constraint" and "reproduction" are two values of the same per-slot quantity, not two different questions.
- **Symmetry:** the decisive relation is a threshold comparison (⋛, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, Operators table :48), not a symmetric join. Whether a check binds is about which side is larger (the check's sanction versus the group's capacity to absorb it), not about both merely being present.
- **Scale:** a single decision loop (one group action), an institutional loop (one check over many actions), and a generational chain (checks handed from one cohort to the next). The membrane between these scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).

One line per domain. A candidate with no nameable anchor is not yet a domain.

| id | domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | The institutional frame | branches, agencies, courts, statutes, budgets | formal authority and compliance | the ground, that is, what actually happens to G's action | admitted |
| D2 | The powerful group as a frame | firms, wealth holders, families, professional networks | money, access, appointments, information | its own closure and its reproduction across generations | admitted |
| D3 | The checking seat | auditors, judges, journalists, watchdogs, researchers, citizens | evidence and residuals | the ground, never agreement | admitted |
| D4 | The habitat and contact cascade | education, finance, media, professions, family, donors | contact | the ground, which does not decay | admitted |
| X1 | "Good rules" as a free-floating property | the statute on paper | rule count | none nameable, because the rule scores itself | not a domain yet, absorbed into D1 as a frame-internal quantity |
| X2 | "Culture" as sole cause | norms | custom | none independent of D4 | absorbed into D4 as a habitat condition |

**Partition or union.** This is a union, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-55 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"*, :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Constraint requires an unmoved anchor AND low-overlap checking AND a reachable channel. A single lever, for example more transparency, clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

**Order by dependency** (Fig 3 :20-21 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces"*):

1. perception: can G's action be seen at all?
2. vocabulary: are there terms for what a rule affords (conflict of interest, capture, revolving door)?
3. objective: can the institution say what would count as progress, namely that the check binds G?
4. planning: is there a route to that objective under incomplete information?
5. learn and carry: is what worked named, verified and carried to the next generation?

The middle link is where such chains usually break: Fig 3 :25-26 *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"*, and :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 3. Findings, with verbatim quotes and per-domain verdicts

### F1. The distinction is anchor status, not rule content (D1, triangulated)

Fig 2 :24-27 names the two panels:

> **"the anchor"** / **"pinned: it does not update on A or B"** / **"both range against it; it is not"** / **"a third party to the disagreement"**

Fig 2 :34 states the failure mode:

> **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**

Fig 2 :35:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

A check that updates on the group it measures is panel 1. The drift is coherent, so insiders experience it as normal operation. A check that is pinned is panel 3: both parties range against it and neither can move it. This is why two institutions with identical statutes diverge: the written rule is the same term, but in one case it is pinned and in the other it has become a frame.

Fig 10 :47 and :18-19 give the institutional form:

> **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
> **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
> **"install what can be violated"**

An impartiality clause that cannot be violated with consequence is a convention that has stopped being a convention and has become a constant the powerful authored. Fig 2 :30 adds the test: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*

**Verdict D1: `triangulated`** (Fig 2 anchor rule and Fig 10 maintainer rule are different figures with low overlap, one about independence, one about what a seat may author). **Mode `specified`** for institutions, `general` for the anchor rule. **Refuter:** a documented case where the check is demonstrably pinned (cannot be rewritten, can register and sanction a violation), the drift test is negative, and yet G's advantage is reproduced at the same rate as in captured systems, with no hidden contact channel. That would break F1.

### F2. "More rules" is a frame-internal quantity (method, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19 and :22:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :26-27:

> **"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"** / **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

A national count of anti-corruption bodies, laws passed or independence indices is produced by the frame it is meant to grade. Fig 9 :60:

> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

So a rule-count is a filter for where to look, not a witness for whether a check binds. Fig 3 :27 (*"A link with no instrument is not a link that works"*) makes the same point at the institutional level: a transparency statute with no instrument that can register a missed signal is a link nobody has looked at.

**Verdict method: `triangulated`** (Fig 1 and Fig 9). **Mode `general`.** **Refuter:** a showing that an aggregate formal-rule score predicts per-slot constraint outcomes with no residual loss, which would dissolve the slot-indexing rule.

### F3. Reproduction is a chain of mirrors handing one closure along (D2, triangulated)

Fig 7 :12-13:

> **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

Fig 7 :36:

> **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Intergenerational reproduction is not a single long consensus. It is generation after generation inheriting the same closure: the same schools, the same networks, the same routes into appointments. Agreement inside that chain compounds while evidence does not. Fig 7 :30-31 adds *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"*, and :37 *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* namely *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* and *"the ground's pressure on the room"*. A chain that books the next room like the last one stops travelling (Fig 7 :42).

**Verdict D2: `triangulated`** (Fig 7 room chain and Fig 6 closure are distinct figures). **Mode `specified`** for elite reproduction, `general` for the closure rule. **Refuter:** a case where the same closure is handed across generations and yet evidence accretes and the group's advantage falls without any import from a differing frame.

### F4. Independence is low overlap, not separate letterhead (D3, triangulated)

Fig 8 :48-49:

> **"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."**
> **"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."**

Fig 2 :31-32:

> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**
> **"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."**

Two agencies with different names but the same donors, the same expert pool and the same information feed are one frame wearing two names. Their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Conversely, Fig 8 :50 allows that *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Same conclusion reached separately is the good case; only the record separates it from adoption (Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*).

Fig 10 :86 and :93 sharpen the seat rule:

> **"Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."**
> **"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."**

And Fig 10 :42:

> **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."**

**Verdict D3: `triangulated`** (Fig 8 overlap, Fig 2 independence, Fig 10 seat rules; three distinct figures). **Mode `specified`.** **Refuter:** a case where a checker demonstrably shares the group's evidence pool and personnel yet constraint outcomes match the low-overlap case, with no convergent independent derivation.

### F5. Constraint is agent-and-habitat contact, not the virtue of officeholders (D4, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39-40:

> **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :61:

> **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."**

Fig 11 :67:

> **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."**

Changing the people in an office without changing what they are in contact with does not move the residual. Nor can a reform subtract the habitat: it can only introduce something and inherit the rest. Fig 11 :52 warns that contact cascades: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* A reform that enumerates only direct conflicts of interest misses the third-order effect, exactly as the DDT chain did.

Fig 10 :24 supplies the channel rule:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

So "the rule is still on the books" is not evidence that the ground is still reachable. Fig 10 :25-28 names the decay modes: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*, for which *"the output is not a verdict, it is / you have lost touch"*.

**Verdict D4: `triangulated`** (Fig 11 habitat and Fig 10 channel). **Mode `specified`.** **Refuter:** a case where improving only the character or training of officeholders, with contact structure unchanged, produces a sustained fall in intergenerational reproduction.

### F6. Stake cannot be removed, only declared (D3 refinement, single-source with a corroborating source)

Fig 10 :58-60:

> **"A seat with no stake at all does not exist."**
> **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."**
> **"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."**

Every checker has a stake: a budget, a career, a reputation. The rule is not "no interest." It is that nothing about which value the ground returns today serves the occupant, and that what cannot be removed is declared. Fig 10 :61 adds *"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."*

This governs how to read a formal independence guarantee. A court with life tenure has removed one reading-interest; a court whose members expect lucrative private work after leaving has not removed the standing stake. Both facts are admissible; the undeclared one runs unchallenged.

**Verdict: `single-source`** on the figure side (Fig 10), corroborated at low overlap by the regulatory-capture literature's *"revolving door"* finding (world source 4 in `09_watson_answer.md`). **Mode `specified`.**

### F7. The mechanism is a union of conditions, so one lever cannot settle it (method, triangulated)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :55-57 and :59:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

The leverage of unbundling is not only a smaller pile but a describable residue (Fig 9 :59 *"not that the pile is smaller, but that the residue is finally describable"*). For this question the residue after applying F1 to F5 is a compound, not a list: pinned anchor AND low-overlap checking AND reachable channel, with the intersections binding.

**Verdict method: `triangulated`** (Fig 9 and Fig 3). **Mode `general`.**

### F9. The `gap`, stated plainly

No figure line selects which institutional arrangement will bind in a particular case. That is not a silence; it is an unreachable verdict. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The missing thing is characterized in effect terms: an instrument that can tell, before the ground settles it, whether a given checker is genuinely low-overlap and whether its channel to the ground is still reachable. We can describe that instrument; we do not hold it. Naming a specific reform as the answer would be a binding from the political-science frame, provenanced as such, not a figure derivation.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before outreach (Fig 10's discipline and the seat-map rule *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"*). Written before the conclusion above was finalised.

The shape claim (constraint is decided by pinned anchor, low-overlap checking and reachable contact, not by formal rule content) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Pinned-anchor failure.** A documented case where the checking anchor cannot be rewritten by G, registers and sanctions violations, shows no coherent drift, and yet G's advantage is reproduced at the same rate as in captured cases, with no hidden contact channel.
2. **Capture-with-constraint failure.** A documented case where checkers share G's evidence pool and personnel yet constraint outcomes match the low-overlap case, with no convergent independent derivation.
3. **Formal-rule sufficiency.** A controlled comparison in which increasing formal rule content alone, with anchor status and contact structure held fixed, produces a sustained drop in intergenerational reproduction.
4. **Habitat failure.** A case where improving only officeholder character or training, with contact unchanged, produces that sustained drop.
5. **Union failure.** A case where a single lever clears both the anchor condition and the overlap condition simultaneously, so the compound dissolves into a single condition and the unbundling instruction is wrong.
6. **Shape-not-a-name breach in this folder.** If any file here names a specific country or reform as *the* figure-derived answer, that claim is withdrawn as a binding with no source gate.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the retraction is added below. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

2. Keep the four domain verdicts separate. Do not average "constraint" into a single national score (Fig 1 :19).
3. State mode on every substantive claim (Formula mode rule).
4. Present the union as a union. More transparency is a lever for the perception link, not a verdict for the whole chain (Fig 3 :19 *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

6. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, that is the residual, the census and the refuter, not a conclusion.
