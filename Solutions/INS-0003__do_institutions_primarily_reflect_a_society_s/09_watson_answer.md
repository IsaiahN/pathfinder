## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the options: institutions primarily reflect a society's existing balance of power (Option A), or institutions can independently reshape that balance (Option B). State what each leads to. A makes the rule a projection whose reference updates on the powerful, so it cannot ground or correct anything and cannot explain rules that outlive their authors. B makes the rule an uncaused cause, so it cannot explain cases where a rule simply tracked a shift that had already happened. The answer returned is a third object: a union of conditions with a four-condition separator, per-slot verdicts, a pre-registered refuter and a declared gap for the universal quantity.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: two conditions, rule at t and distribution at t+1, so a **union compound**, not one question with opposite answers (Fig 9 :57). Symmetry: reflection is a symmetric co-presence (Fig 12 :59); reshaping is an ordered production (Fig 12 :74, :75). Scale: one institution, a field, a whole society. Per slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain 1, the institutional frame.** Actors: rule makers, gatekeepers, enforcement, agenda setters. Currency: settled rules, compliance, precedence. Anchor: an outcome that does not move when the rule's beneficiaries praise it. Question type: mirror, or convention promoted to verdict.
- **Domain 2, the power and habitat frame.** Actors: organized interests, coalitions, capital, force, newcomers. Currency: influence, resources, who-gets-what. Anchor: material outcomes and reproduced contact, not opinion. The habitat is not the ground; it updates and is not authored by any seat.
- **Domain 3, the verification and outside frame.** Actors: researchers, journals, associations, expert readers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Domain 4, the universal "primarily" quantity.** No nameable actors, currency or anchor. **Not a domain**, declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Describe R first (Fig 8 :53); apply the anchor test before any reflect/reshape judgment (Fig 2 :29-30); refuse before mint (formula step 2); test for a differing closure before attributing reshaping (Fig 6 :23); settle per slot, then compose, then re-check the residual (Fig 1 :19, Fig 4 :30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is refused. Reflection is a symmetric reading; reshaping is an ordered production. A rule that mirrors the powerful is a mirror and cannot correct, and once its keeper benefits from the reading it has become a frame occupying the ground's position. Real reshaping requires contact with a differing closure, with provenance and a shadow test; an institution can introduce actors but cannot subtract its habitat, so "independently" fails and "merely reflect" fails too. The characteristic mechanism is quieter than either option: a convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention, silently. What the figures return is a four-condition separator applied per slot, with verdicts mirror / import-conduit / verdict-as-convention / gap, a pre-registered refuter and an instrument owed. This is `specified` on domains 1 to 3, `general` on the separator, `gap` on the universal quantity, and ungrounded throughout.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The separator states its refuter before the conclusion and keeps verdicts per slot, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is conceptual: accepting the question's either/or and averaging across institution types throws away the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22) and turns a filter into a witness (Fig 9 :60). That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter and the anchor test.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal "primarily" claim is withdrawn without touching the per-domain findings; the separator can be applied case by case. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a slot reclassified from mirror to import-conduit on new evidence is a normal revision, not a retraction of the framework.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An exogenous change in the rule with the distribution fixed, or the reverse, read by an instrument that does not consult the institution's own report, would select the arrow at that slot. A dated lineage showing which atom crossed from which differing frame would settle provenance. A case where a convention that decides an outcome remains checkable by an outside frame that cannot reconstruct the decision would clear the live type question. Any of the five failure conditions in `01` section 4 would narrow or withdraw the separator. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
