## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, read this session) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0003 the shape is: *a union of conditions, indexed per institutional slot, returning one of mirror / import-conduit / verdict-as-convention / gap, with a refuter and an instrument owed*. It is not a percentage and not a choice between the two offered options.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox that by then you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it." For this question, that means the separator is finished when the refuter can be stated crisply enough to be false.

## 3. The decisive essay passage for this question: agreement as drift

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session) contains the passage that most directly bears on "institutions reflect power":

> "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

That is Fig 2's mutual-update collapse in different words. An institution whose reference is the current balance, and whose balance is in turn read off the institution, is two clocks setting each other. The drift is coherent, so nobody notices. The same essay records the remedy: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Which is why the separator in `01` section 5 requires a reading of who-gets-what that does **not** update on what the institution says.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For institutions, that is a measured outcome at a slot, not a scholar's judgment that an institution "feels transformative".
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. In institutional terms, a dated account of which external actor, technology or coalition crossed the boundary and what gap it explained.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The same warning applies to declaring which institutional conditions the separator checks: the choice must be visible, not smuggled in as a universal.

## 5. The metamerism point

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The instrument "cannot comment on the source". And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: an observed institutional form (a court, a commission, a constitution) is a reading. Several different sources can produce the same reading: a coalition that captured the rule, an imported rule that survived its authors, or a convention that was silently promoted into a verdict. Naming the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the reason F7 and F11 in `01` demand an instrument and a lineage.

## 6. Correction to my own section 1 and section 4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming *any* institutional condition in the separator. That is wrong. The figures README's phrase governs **what a missing thing would have to look like**, and Fig 13 :133 ties it to what a system with a closure can report about *decidability*. A missing primitive is not the same as an already-available condition used as a binding.

- The conditions in the separator (a non-updating reading, a differing closure with provenance, a seat able to refuse) are all **held** resources, not invented seventh primitives. Naming them is a binding with provenance, which is permitted.
- What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* which specific institution occupies a slot, or that naming a condition adds an atom (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure").
- The metamerism point in section 5 is **not** retracted: the reading still does not determine the source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held conditions.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A roster of famous institutional theorists agreeing with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a slot with a real ground.

**This whole folder is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the institutional question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. What shape means for INS-0003, in one paragraph

The shape returned here is not "institutions mostly reflect" or "institutions mostly reshape". It is a union of conditions with a per-slot verdict, a pre-registered refuter, and an instrument owed: describe the residual, test the anchor, test for a differing closure with provenance, test for a seat able to refuse. The framework can say what any answer must look like and what would reverse it. It cannot say which institution in your society is doing what, because that requires the ground, and no ground reading was taken in this simulation.
