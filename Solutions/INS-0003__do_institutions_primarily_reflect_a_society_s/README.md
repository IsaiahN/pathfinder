## Restating the question as an effect

Two options are offered. Option A (reflect): the institution is a projection of the power distribution, so changing the institution without changing power is impossible. Option B (reshape): the institution is a generator, so it can change the distribution on its own. What each leads to: A treats the rule as a recording and cannot explain the observed cases where a rule outlives the coalition that wrote it; B treats the rule as an uncaused cause and cannot explain the cases where a rule simply tracked a shift that already happened. The answer this package returns is a **separator**, not a choice between A and B: the two are different mechanisms with different anchors, indexed per institutional slot, and a verdict about which is operating requires a non-updating reading of the outcome, a differing closure, and a seat able to refuse.

Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's "Direction" rule: an answer will have the form **a four-condition separator with per-slot verdicts and a stated refuter, not a percentage and not a single arrow**. That shape accrues a residual like any other term and is tested in `01` section 6.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle a question that requires measured institutional outcomes.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Seat Map :163).
2. **A verifier who holds the interiors cannot verify.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
