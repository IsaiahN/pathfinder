# The Tether's answer on institutions and power

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural separator, `gap` on the universal "primarily" quantity. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe a rule and a distribution of power moving together and are asked to choose which one caused the other, for institutions "in general". The residual is the unchosen direction and the unexamined scope. Arity: two conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :57: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Symmetry: reflection is symmetric co-presence, reshaping is ordered production (Fig 12 :59, :74, :75). Scale: a single institution, a field, and a society are three different anchors. Per slot, not averaged: Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## 2. The shape: one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

### Domain 1 - The institutional frame

An institution that only mirrors the balance is a mirror, and a mirror has no purchase on what it reflects. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Its reference updates with the powerful, so it cannot serve as an anchor: Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."

But institutions are not only mirrors. Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." A seat can add an actor and cannot subtract the habitat. That is real work that changes contact, so the passive reading of "reflect" is also wrong. The characteristic failure is a rule that begins as a shared meaning and is promoted into a decision about who wins: Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Because the keeper of that rule has a stake that survives the outcome (Fig 10 :61: "standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on"), nothing reports the promotion.

**D1 verdict:** `specified`. A slot is `mirror` when its reference updates on the powerful; `verdict-as-convention` when the rule fixes outcomes and its keeper benefits. An institution is neither a passive projection nor an uncaused cause.

### Domain 2 - The power and habitat frame

Power is a network relation, not a stock: Fig 13 :24: "that entities relate, and influence travels". It is habitat, not ground (Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."). Capability lives in the agent-and-habitat pair: Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Change of the wall requires a differing closure. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." So an institution cannot *independently* generate a new kind of actor from its own resources. Where it reshapes, it is a conduit: a newcomer, technology, or external coalition crosses with provenance and a shadow test. And because introduction is available while exclusion is not, a managed arrangement is permanent work rather than a solved state.

**D2 verdict:** `specified`. A slot is `import-conduit / reshaping` exactly when a new kind of actor or claim entered from a differing closure, with a dated lineage, and the resulting change paid at a non-updating reading of who-gets-what. Otherwise the observed change is recombination inside the closure.

### Domain 3 - The verification and outside frame

Agreement cannot decide the arrow. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." A scholar who absorbs the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked: Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

The world literature corroborates the mechanism shape from low-overlap fields, but does not decide it. Glaeser et al. (2004) find that human capital is more basic than institutions and that institutional improvement follows; de Waal (2010, 2022) shows power as a shifting coalition process in which even the strongest player faces erosion; Jost, Sapolsky and Nam (2018) show the cognitive mirror (people defend the institutions they depend on). Institutional-theory work (Garud, Hardy and Maguire 2007; Micelotta, Lounsbury and Greenwood 2017) names the same structural bind: those with power lack the motive to change, those with the motive lack the power. These are corroborators, not witnesses; the verdict comes from the figures.

**D3 verdict:** `general` structural, ungrounded. The separator must be checked against a ground by an outside frame that holds only the residual and the refuter. Their agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

### Domain 4 - The universal quantity

No figure line returns a global reflect/reshape coefficient or a universal direction. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

**D4 verdict:** `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind. Not unreachable in principle: with a per-slot ground reading and a differing closure, the separator can be applied case by case. What cannot be delivered is the one number the word "primarily" asks for.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that institutions are mere epiphenomena. They introduce actors and can change contact, with asymmetric power.
- Not that any institution can reshape power from its own resources. "The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs" (Fig 6 :23).
- Not that a correlation identifies a cause. "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that either offered option is a bond the figures define. Fig 12 :90: "A relation that fails all six is not a bond you are missing. It is a claim that has not been made yet."
- Not a global winner, not a percentage, not `grounded`. This package cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 4. The four-condition separator, restated so it can fire

Per slot, in dependency order (Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."):

1. **Describe the residual.** What failed to resolve here? Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."
2. **Anchor test.** Is there a reading of who-gets-what that does not update on what the powerful or the institution say? If no, call the slot `mirror` and stop.
3. **Differing closure and import test.** Did a new kind of actor, claim or instrument enter from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test? If no, call the change recombination, not reshaping.
4. **Seat and refusal test.** Is there a seat able to refuse a verdict, with no gain from a particular reading, and whose standing does not survive the outcome? If the rule decides outcomes and its keeper benefits, call it `verdict-as-convention`.
5. **Settle per slot; re-check R.** One of `mirror`, `import-conduit`, `verdict-as-convention`, `gap`. No averaging.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it, and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding questions are:

- An exogenous change in the rule with the distribution held fixed, or an exogenous shift in the distribution with the rule held fixed, read by an instrument that does not consult the institution's own report.
- A dated lineage for an apparent institutional innovation, showing which atom crossed from which differing frame and which gap it explained. Without it, "reshaping" is a recording presented as a derivation (Fig 4 :25).
- A case in which a convention that decides an outcome remains checkable by an outside frame that cannot reconstruct that decision. This is the live type question from `01` section 6.
- The standing falsifiers in `01` section 4, any one of which narrows or withdraws the separator.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The separator uses held conditions, not an invented seventh primitive, but naming those conditions makes the choice visible rather than neutral (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, carried from the exemplar: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."). No ground reading was taken, so the answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "institutions reflect power" or "institutions reshape power". It says the question bundles two different relations with different anchors: reflection is a symmetric reading, reshaping is an ordered production, and a correlation cannot choose between them. An institution that only mirrors the powerful is a mirror, and a mirror cannot correct; an institution that really changes the balance does so by carrying in something from a frame whose closure differs, never by generating it from its own resources, and it can introduce actors but cannot subtract its habitat. The characteristic failure is quieter than either option: a shared convention that decides who wins and whose keeper benefits from the reading has stopped being a convention, and nothing reports the promotion. What the framework returns is a four-condition separator applied per institution, with a refuter written in advance and an instrument owed: describe the residual, test for a non-updating reading, test for a differing closure with provenance, and test for a seat able to refuse. It returns no percentage and no named institution as the winner. That is a shape, not a verdict, and it is offered to be refuted.
