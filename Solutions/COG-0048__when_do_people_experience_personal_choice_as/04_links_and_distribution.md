## What the wider literature contributes, and where it conflicts

1. **Low-overlap corroboration for the anchor claim.** Moore 2016 and the Wikipedia entry on sense of agency come from cognitive neuroscience, not from the Tether framework. They establish that agency is a subjective experience with separable implicit and explicit measures, and that it can dissociate from causal facts. This corroborates treating first-person report as the object of study while refusing to treat it as a readout of the cause.
2. **Low-overlap corroboration for the bond claim.** SEP's relational autonomy literature and SDT's internalization theory arrive from philosophy and motivation psychology respectively. Internalization is the production bond; constitutive relational autonomy is the identity or conjunction bond. They are not two independent witnesses for the same claim because both answer an atomistic picture, but they are meaningfully different frames.
3. **A genuine conflict, handled by re-census.** Iyengar and Lepper 1999 read their results as limiting autonomy's relevance in interdependent cultures. Bao and Lam 2008 reply that freedom of choice is not autonomy. The conflict sends us back to the census, where "who authored the choice" and "does the person endorse it" are separate quantities (Fig 12 :46 and Symbols :24). The verdict is unchanged; the need for the bond/ingredient distinction is confirmed. The two papers share a question and part of a literature, so their disagreement is one pool arguing with itself.
