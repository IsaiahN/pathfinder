# The Tether's answer: how classifications reflect the interests of their makers

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain findings, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a separator, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is not "are classifications biased?" That averages away the signal. The residual is per slot: when a bin decides an outcome, the criteria that placed this person or this specimen in it trace to what the classifier was positioned to want, see, and reward, and the trace is usually invisible in the published category list. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

It is a union compound, not one hard problem: interest in the reading, absence of a non-updating anchor, aggregation across slots, and a convention that decides an outcome overlap. Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Verdicts are kept separate per domain in `01`, never averaged into a single answer.

## 2. The shape

1. **An interest in the reading is disqualifying; an interest in the outcome is unavoidable.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged." This is the direct answer to "reflect the interests": the interest is not a contaminant added to a neutral cut, it is the structural condition of a cut made from inside a frame. The figures do not demand impossible disinterest. They demand that the interest be declared so it can be challenged.

2. **A classifier with no non-updating anchor is a mirror.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." When a discipline validates its categories only against itself, the categories converge on whoever had the most influence. That convergence is what we call the maker's interest.

3. **The habitat must be enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." A cut made from the classifier's vocabulary imports the interest into the object; a cut made from an enumerated habitat keeps the interest outside, where it can be declared. The ground does not decay, the channel does (Fig 10 :24).

4. **The criteria and bonds carry what the category list cannot.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." So the interest is read from the lineage, not inferred from the nouns.

5. **Residuals stay per slot; aggregation hides the signal.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single number per person or group, used as a summary of a category, is the named failure. This is the structural form of Gould's "single series of worthiness" and of Blumenbach's five discrete varieties over a continuum.

6. **A naming that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.** Fig 2 :43: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Where a category determines rights, admission, diagnosis, or enumeration, it is a verdict about people and must be checked with independent frames against a ground that does not update on the registry.

7. **Keep every cut ranked and reversible.** Fig 9 :40-41: "a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up", "so keep every cut ranked and reversible". Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Category counts, coverage statistics, and the prestige of the classifier are filters for the budget, never witnesses for what the categories mean.

8. **No standing category survives a change of residual.** Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Linnaeus's ranking shifted between editions while Africanus stayed last; the categories are stable, the valence is not.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that every classification is mere ideology. That is a `general` verdict with no ground, and it fails Fig 5 :44: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."
- Not that no classification can track a real ground. The figures require an anchor; they do not deny anchors exist.
- Not that the figures name the correct taxonomy. They return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that a historical classifier's motives have been established. The claim is about the structure of the cut and the visibility of the interest, not about a person's psychology. Blumenbach's own warnings against discrete races are part of the record and are not erased.
- Not `grounded`. No classification was measured against a non-updating anchor in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A provenance record plus a discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source", and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The spectrometer settled the colour case because it discriminated sources. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Source test:** does the surviving classification record the criteria and bonds that produced each bin, or only the bin names? Without the record, several interests can yield the same list and no argument can separate them.
- **Slot test:** is the residual still indexed per person or specimen, or has it been pooled into a group average that cannot show who failed?
- **Anchor test:** is there anything the cut can be ranged against that does not change its answer because a classifier or a committee thinks otherwise?
- **Outcome test:** has a naming been allowed to decide an outcome while still being treated as a neutral convention? If so, Fig 2 :43 has fired.
- **Standing refuters from `01` §4:** the five pre-registered failure conditions, written before the conclusion. If any holds, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached in shape, unreached in name.** The separator is fully stated and checkable against records. Which classification is correct is not returned. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The derivation stops at the limit of what the available provenance records can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that all classifications are ideology, and it does not name the correct taxonomy. It says that an interest in the outcome is unavoidable for anyone who cuts a habitat into bins, while an interest in the reading is disqualifying for anyone who uses that cut as a verdict, so the interest must be declared or it runs unchallenged. A cut is checkable only when the habitat was enumerated rather than composed, the residuals stay indexed per person or specimen, the criteria and bonds are kept in the record, and the cut can be ranged against a ground that does not update on what the classifier thinks. Where a naming decides an outcome, it has stopped being a convention and must be checked as a verdict. What the framework returns is that separator, with five failure conditions written in advance. It does not say which categories are right. That stays open until a provenance record and a discriminating instrument settle it.
