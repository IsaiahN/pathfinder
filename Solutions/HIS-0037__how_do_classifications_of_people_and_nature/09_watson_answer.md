## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that classifications of people and nature reflect the interests of those who create them. Option A: assert the strong form that every classification is an expression of the maker's power. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the strong form. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union. Order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** When a bin decides an outcome, the criteria that placed this person or specimen in it trace to what the classifier was positioned to want, see and reward, and the trace is invisible in the published category list. Arity: several overlapping conditions. Symmetry: the "reflects" relation is not symmetric; the classifier acts on the habitat and the habitat does not classify back in the same currency. Scale: one slot through an institution's register to an infrastructure that decides life chances. Union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54, :57).
- **Domain 1, the maker's frame.** Actors: classifier, vocabulary, criteria, cut. Currency: predicates minted and credibility accrued. Anchor: none as first described, so this is a mirror, not a grounded domain. Union with D2 and D4.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: people, specimens, conditions, relations in contact with the residual. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground that would settle a category. The cut claims a partition; the habitat is a union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: outside readers, affected people, a ground maintainer. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: a ground that does not update on the classification. Independence decided by whether frames share evidence.
- **Domain 4, diffusion and use.** Actors: registries, censuses, clinics, laws. Currency: outcomes. Anchor: the consequence at the slot, which does not consult the registry. Compound with D1, because the same cut both describes and decides.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); enumerate the habitat before cutting it (Fig 11 :43); refuse an outcome-fixing convention before accepting a cut (Fig 2 :43); only then check an outside frame against a non-updating ground. A reading below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :20-23).

Strain test: Fig 1 :19 per-slot versus bins is not strained after re-census, because Fig 1 governs the residual and bins are the library. Fig 2 :40 versus Fig 8 :50 on agreement is not strained, because one governs verdict weight and the other provenance. One apparent strain in `01` F1, that interest in the reading would make all classification impossible, was narrowed by re-census to a seat condition: the line governs a verifier using a cut as a verdict, not the act of cutting.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the strong form does not. An interest in the reading is disqualifying when a cut is used as a verdict and must therefore be declared, because an undeclared interest runs unchallenged. A checkable cut enumerates its habitat, keeps residuals per slot, preserves its criteria and bonds in a provenance record, ranges against a ground that does not update, and is held reversible. A naming that decides an outcome is a verdict and must be checked with independent frames. No standing category list survives a change of residual. What is refused: "all classification is ideology", which is a `general`-mode verdict that fails the acceptance bargain; any claim that the figures name the correct taxonomy; and any psychological verdict about a named classifier's motives. This is `general` on structure and `specified` per domain, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not trivial. The packet is mode-labelled, carries five refuters written before the run, and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is re-enacting the defect: selecting checkers for likely agreement, or sending a finished taxonomy instead of the criterion, manufactures the very interest-in-the-reading this question is about (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The strong form is removable without touching the derivation; `07` and `08` already remove it. A provisional claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, no primitive has to be withdrawn, and the cut is held reversible by Fig 9 :41. A published historical error, by contrast, is not undoable at the slot, which is why Fig 2 :43 matters.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A provenance record plus a source-discriminating instrument showing that the interest in the reading did not determine the cut would break C1; a demonstration that averaged group numbers preserve the live per-slot signal would break Fig 1 :22; a classification derived with no cut would break Fig 11 :43; a naming that decides an outcome and still functions as a convention would break Fig 2 :43. A figure re-census that lets an interest in the reading serve as a verdict without declaration would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
