## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The claim that interests enter classifications is not a slogan from one tradition.** Bowker and Star (information infrastructure), Foucault (history of epistemes) and Barsalou (cognitive science) arrive at structurally similar shapes from low-overlap fields: a classification is a segmentation, it is tied to a grid or a goal, and its criteria are not recoverable from its list. Agreement across such different pools is weak corroboration for the shape, not for any particular historical case. Fig 8 :48: "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."
2. **The counterreading is also in the record.** Blumenbach, often listed as a founder of scientific race classification, explicitly warned that varieties "run into one another by insensible degrees" and wrote against the inferiority of Africans (Bhopal 2007). Linnaeus used "varieties", not "race", and held that skin colour was largely a product of climate (Linnean Society 2020). So the honest history is not "classifiers were simply prejudiced"; it is that a cut was made from an interested channel, then entrenchment did the rest. This is why the figures' answer is a separator with provenance, not a verdict on a person. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the figures before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before showing them the sources.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A mass send chosen for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is high, because the question is about interests in classification and selecting checkers for agreement would re-enact the defect.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, never a finished taxonomy.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Submission cautions

- HSS, 4S, ISKO and the SHPS special issue all use public portals. Do not substitute personal addresses found in author bios.
- Several journals charge an article processing charge or use double anonymized review. Check the current author guidance before preparing a simulated derivation; a paper that presents LLM output as a ground reading would violate both the figures' mode rule and ordinary journal standards.
- Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's to do. Prefer an opt-in download or a submission portal over an attachment pushed to a list.
