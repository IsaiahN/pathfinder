## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. No scraped contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Seat Map :163, Fig 2 :40).
- No bulk-send plan. The venues in `03` are public submission and contact channels, and the first contact is the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list of people chosen for likely agreement is a mirror.
2. **Interest in the reading is the defect under study.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." It would be self-refuting to select checkers by their agreement with the claim about interests.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Show the derivation only after the stage-1 verdict is recorded.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `02_outreach_email.md` to a public venue in `03`, one to one. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record that answer before offering any figure text. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
