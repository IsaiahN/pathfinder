## The residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot s. Here b is the worker's belief about what a task affords, a is the action on the task, Γ(b,a) is what the worker's library of routines predicts, and o' is what the task actually returns (time, quality, output, pay, continued employment). The slot s is the occupation-task pair. R is indexed per occupation and never averaged into an economy-wide number.

The three options in the question are three different operators on that slot, not three degrees of one effect:

| option | operator | figure warrant |
|---|---|---|
| automate existing tasks | subtraction, human operand removed | Operators table: *"A with B removed or absent"*; Fig 12 :112 *"Progress is a subtraction."* |
| expand what workers can do | production and composition inside the closure | Operators table: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*; Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* |
| create new forms of work | import of an atom from a closure that differs | Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent..."* |

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula, "Direction"): the answer would be an operator assignment per occupation, plus an instrument that distinguishes a subtraction from a production from an import at the ground. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that AI augments rather than automates.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade a label.** Seat Map: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A list of sympathetic labor economists would be a room, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact is the residual and its refuter, never the figure set.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
