## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among worker library Γ, action a and outcome o', indexed by slot and ordered by time. Three operands plus a time index; a two-place reading (tool, productivity) loses the action and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor does not update on the worker's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Primarily" is a threshold comparison across per-slot operators (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is compared rather than merely conjoined.
- **Scale:** the slot is a task within an occupation; the ranking of "primarily" exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the worker's step loop and the population's selection loop (Fig 4), so the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :77, :82 (production and subtraction), Fig 6 :14, :21 (import and idempotent mint), Fig 11 :49 (valence relative to residual) and Fig 10 :24 (ground versus channel).
- **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: AI changes work by subtraction, production or import; which one fires depends on the slot; read it per occupation against outcomes.
- **Concretisation (T_E):** the applied claim a reader is likely to carry away: "AI helps novices, replaces routine work, and creates new jobs, so measure exposure per occupation and train accordingly."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a slot failing C3 to train harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the instrument rule. "Measure exposure per occupation" is read as if exposure were the outcome, which permits a frame to score itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27) and ignores Fig 10 :24.
3. It loses the source gate on imports. "Creates new jobs" arrives as a name with no provenance and no shadow test, so convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :50-52).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Helps novices" has no place for the possibility that the same tool changes sign when the residual changes (Fig 11 :49-50).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: three operators are not one operator, and import is not mint. That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no task-level ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensation of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
