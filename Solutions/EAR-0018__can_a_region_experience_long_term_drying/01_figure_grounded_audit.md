## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The thing that failed is an inference, not a measurement:

> From "global mean precipitation is rising" a listener infers "rainfall here is rising." The slot does not comply. The residual is `R_s = |Gamma(global mean) - o'_s|` for the region s: what the aggregate predicted for that slot versus what the gauge actually returned. When R_s stays positive and does not trend down across years, the slot carries live mass (Fig 1 :20-21) even though the aggregate is small.

The question "can both be true?" is therefore two questions, not one:

1. **Structural:** is a positive aggregate trend and a negative slot trend a legal state at all? (Does the map from slots to mean forbid it?)
2. **Physical:** does the climate system actually produce such a state for a sustained period, and by what mechanism?

Per Fig 9 :4-5, disagreements that do not shrink with effort because two well-formed questions are bundled must be split, not searched. These two are kept separate below and their verdicts are never averaged.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** two objects. One aggregate over N regional slots, and one slot. The aggregate is a many-to-one map (a weighted sum), so information is lost by construction.
- **Symmetry:** the mean is a **symmetric** function of its slots. Under the `+` bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term (Fig 12 :92-93). Reordering the regions leaves the mean unchanged, so the mean cannot carry which region is which. This is the flat-formula failure: "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not" (Fig 12 :14).
- **Scale:** global (aggregate) versus regional (slot), crossing the membrane (Fig 4). The transform upward is abstraction, downward is concretisation, and it is lossy in both directions.

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor.**

- **Domain A, aggregation structure (mathematics and statistics).** Actors: the N slots, the mean operator, spatial variance. Currency: quantities of P (mm/day) and their trends (%/K). Anchor: the arithmetic identity that a weighted mean can rise while one component falls, and the water budget mass balance. This is a nameable anchor and the question is constitutively about it (Fig 2 :36), so this is a genuine domain.
- **Domain B, hydroclimate physics (sciences).** Actors: atmosphere, ocean, land surface, aerosols, circulation, moisture transport, convection. Currency: water flux and atmospheric energy flux. Anchor: gauges, satellites, reanalysis, CMIP ensembles, and the atmospheric energy constraint. Nameable.
- **Domain C, verification and outreach (the checking arrangement).** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground (observations and models), never agreement. Nameable. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence here is zero.

**Partition or union.**

- The aggregate/slot pair is a **partition**: two different quantities answering two different questions. A lever that clears the mean does not clear the slot.
- The mechanism that produces regional drying is a **union compound**: thermodynamics, dynamics, aerosols, land use and internal variability all bind. Per Fig 9 :54-58, a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; it is a specific compound with its own loop. It is unbundled in `06_isomers_and_order.md` and in the WORLD section, not collapsed into "climate change."

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).**

1. Settle Domain A (is the joint state legal?). If no, stop; no mechanism is needed.
2. Settle Domain B (does the system realise it, and how?). This consumes A's result.
3. Only then Domain C (who can check, and how to approach without spending their independence).

Effort spent at step 3 while step 1 is unexamined is effort above the break (Fig 3 :19-20).

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The question is answered by the slot rule, not by the aggregate (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :13:

> **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**

And Fig 1 :20-21:

> **"Support is satisfied when some slot carries positive mass, so a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one."**

A rising global mean with one persistently drying region is therefore a **legal state** under the framework's own bookkeeping, not a contradiction. The mean is the wrong object for the question about the region. This is triangulated across Fig 1 (residual bookkeeping) and Fig 12 (the flat formula loses arrangement, F3), two figures with low overlap.

**Refuter:** show that the mean is invertible to its slots, that is, that the aggregate trend determines the sign of every slot trend, or show a framework rule that forbids a positive aggregate trend coexisting with a negative slot residual. Then F1 falls.

### F2 - The residual must be read per region, or the signal vanishes (single-source within Fig 1, corroborated in kind)

Fig 1 :28:

> **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."**

The Symbols table defines `s` as "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." So "region" is literally one of the framework's named slots. The correct measurement question is not "is global rainfall up?" but "what is R_s for this region over this period?"

**Refuter:** a measurement protocol in which the global mean predicts the regional trend with no loss. Until then the slot residual is the only object that can answer the question.

### F3 - The mean is a flat formula: ingredients kept, arrangement lost (triangulated)

Fig 12 :5:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 12 :14-15:

> **"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"**
> **"nothing downstream can tell them apart"**

The global mean records the ingredients (total precipitation across regions) and drops the arrangement (which region received it). Under the `+` bond "k operands have k! spellings and one term" (Fig 12 :92-93); the many-to-one collapse is the same structure. Nothing downstream of the mean can tell a wet-north/dry-south world from a uniformly wetter world with the same total.

**Refuter:** exhibit an operator that recovers the arrangement from the mean without importing information from outside the aggregate. That would break the flat-formula result in Fig 12.

### F4 - The cross-scale transform is lossy and not reversible (triangulated)

Fig 4 :27:

> **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

Fig 4 :28-29:

> **"Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought back down will not match what was sent."**

Going up is averaging the slots into a global mean. Coming back down is asking what the mean implies for a region (downscaling). The round trip does not return the original, and the gap is not an error to be assumed away: Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The measured gap R_T is recorded in `11_audit.md`.

**Refuter:** a downscaling operator that reproduces each slot from the mean with zero gap. It would have to carry the lost arrangement, which contradicts F3.

### F5 - The global frame cannot derive a slot; a different frame must be imported (single-source, corroborated by the mechanism)

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

A frame that holds only the global mean cannot derive the regional trend. What must cross in is a frame whose closure contains moisture transport, circulation and the regional water budget. This is not a call for more searching inside the aggregate; it is the "OUTWARD" branch of formula step 7. The world sources in section 09 supply exactly that frame (energy budget, moisture budget, stationary waves).

**Refuter:** derive the regional trend from the global mean alone, with no imported transport variables.

### F6 - The two questions are a partition; the mechanism is a union (triangulated)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54-55:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

The question as posed is a partition of two objects (mean and slot), settled by F1. The physical cause of a dry slot is a union of conditions: thermodynamic moisture increase, circulation change, aerosol forcing, land use and internal variability. Asking "is it thermodynamics or dynamics?" is the wrong form; the compound is what binds. The world literature confirms this (Wills 2016, Dagan and Stier 2020, IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch8).

**Refuter:** show that one condition alone accounts for every persistent regional drying trend, so the compound is unnecessary.

### F7 - Residual first, frame second: the error is choosing the global frame first (single-source, corroborated)

Fig 9 :27-28:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

The residual is "a rising aggregate, a falling slot." If the global-mean frame is chosen before the residual is described, every answer will look like a confirmation of the mean. If the residual is described first, the required frame must predict a per-slot trend, and the aggregate is recognised as the wrong instrument. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A global-mean statistic is a filter for where to spend attention, not a witness for what happens in a region.

**Refuter:** a case where the residual is fully described by the aggregate alone and no per-slot description is possible.

### F8 - No line governs the physical magnitude; that is ground work, not a figure verdict (gap, declared)

The figures decide the **shape** (per-slot residuals, lossy aggregation, cross-frame import). They do not decide whether regional precipitation will fall by 5% or 20%, which regions, or over which decades. Those are empirical quantities with a nameable anchor (gauges, reanalysis, models). After a genuine re-census (section 6) no figure line governs them, so this is declared a **gap for this derivation**, not filled by a figure quote. The canonical usage rule permits this: the framework reports unreachable as a verdict rather than a silence, and returns a shape rather than a name. The empirical content in this folder is attributed to the world sources, never to the figures.

**Refuter:** a figure line that predicts a quantitative regional trend. None was found; the search is recorded here so the absence is visible.

### F9 - The checking frame must not be selected for agreement (triangulated)

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

For this question the cost of being wrong is high (water infrastructure, drought planning), so an outside frame is worth buying. But a list of readers chosen because they already accept the aggregate framing shares the frame's evidence pool, and their agreement is worthless. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The packet therefore leads with the residual and its refuter and withholds the framework until stage 2.

**Refuter:** a checking arrangement in which selecting for receptiveness adds independent evidence. None is supplied by the figures.

### F10 - The six-term set is an analogue here, not the mechanism (strain, named)

Fig 13 :75:

> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Fig 13 :76:

> **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

It is tempting to derive atmospheric moisture transport directly from the six-term substrate (a gradient of moisture over space, a network of transport, a flow). That would be a **strain**: Fig 13 governs the six primitives of the framework, while precipitation and evaporation are derived quantities in a physical frame. After re-census the line is retained only as a structural analogue (the mediate-by-a-structure rule and the space/time distinction of gradients), marked `specified`, never as a derivation of hydroclimate. The physical mechanism is witnessed by the world sources. This is the one live strain in the solution and it is recorded, not smoothed.

**Refuter:** a derivation of a regional precipitation trend from the six-term set alone, with no physics imported. That would promote F10 from analogue to mechanism.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion and before outreach)

SHAPE-EAR-0018 (bound in README section 2) is refused if any of the following holds:

1. The global mean is invertible to its slots, so the aggregate trend determines every slot trend (kills F1 to F4).
2. A framework rule forbids a positive aggregate residual coexisting with a negative slot residual (kills F1).
3. A downscaling operator returns each slot from the mean with zero round-trip gap R_T (kills F4).
4. A single physical condition, rather than a union, accounts for all persistent regional drying (kills F6).
5. A figure line is found that predicts a quantitative regional precipitation trend (fills the F8 gap and changes the mode claim).

If any fires, the answer is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. This refuter was written before the WORLD search and before the one-paragraph answer, per the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Answer "yes" to the structural question and keep the physical question separate. Do not let the global mean stand in for the region (F1 to F4).
2. Measure R_s per region, over a stated period, against the global trend (F2).
3. Unbundle the physical mechanism: energy constraint on the mean, moisture transport and circulation on the slot (F6, F8).
4. Approach regional-climate researchers through public opt-in venues with the residual and the refuter first; record their verdict before showing any framework material (F9).
5. Keep the six-term analogue labelled as an analogue (F10). Do not present the figures as a climate model.
