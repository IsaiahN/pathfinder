## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figures README adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0018 the shape is **SHAPE-EAR-0018** as bound in `README.md` section 2: a many-to-one aggregation is lossy and non-invertible, so the aggregate trend and the slot trend are different quantities and their joint sign is free; the mechanism that sets the slot lives in the transport structure between slots. That constrains the answer. It does not fill in which region, which decade, or which percentage.

A shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. "The mean is the wrong instrument for a slot" is a shape. "The Mediterranean will lose 20% of rainfall by 2080" is a name plus a number, and the figures do not supply either.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is "a rising aggregate, a falling slot," not "global warming" as a cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn’t possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you’d expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends a describable residual rather than the framework's private vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the finish line is the per-slot residual: once you are measuring R_s, there is nothing further the global mean can tell you about that region.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For EAR-0018, gauges, reanalysis and models settle the regional trend.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The energy-budget and moisture-transport sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are such imports: they explain the per-slot shape the figures predicted, and their provenance is recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"*.

So the six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Using it as an analogue for atmospheric composition (F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`) must be recorded as a choice, not presented as a derivation of climate physics.

## 4. Why "read the source off the reading" is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For EAR-0018 the global mean is exactly such a lossy reading. Many spatial arrangements of wet and dry regions give the same mean. Naming the regional source from the mean (inferring "this region got wetter" from "the mean rose") is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument (the per-slot gauge and the transport diagnosis), not an inference from the aggregate.

## 5. Consequences for this question

**The mean is a shape-level instrument, not a verdict-level one.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A global mean is useful for deciding where to spend attention (a global water-budget constraint), and useless as a witness for what happened in one region.

**The "shape, not a name" rule is narrowed correctly.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Regional precipitation is not a missing primitive of the framework; it is a derived quantity in a physical frame. So the rule here does not forbid talking about regions. It forbids claiming that the figures *name* a particular regional trend or supply a number. That distinction is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8 and F10.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A respected institution's agreement would give this claim a budget to be tried. It would not make the shape true.

**Two consequences for outreach.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Lead with the per-slot residual and its refuter; record the verdict before showing the framework; never pool later agreement as corroboration.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum of a derivation.

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. They are **not** tests of the climate claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the empirical question they report on. The empirical content in this folder is what the cited climate sources report, with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
