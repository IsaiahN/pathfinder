## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether two trends can be true at once: a global mean precipitation trend that is positive over a period, and a named region's precipitation trend that is persistently negative over the same period. Option A: treat the global mean as a prediction for the region and call the disagreement a paradox or an error. Option B: treat the mean and the slot as different objects and ask which mechanism sets the slot. What each leads to: A applies the wrong instrument to the region and misdirects water planning; B yields a checkable shape, a stated refuter, and a clear division of labour between the figures (shape) and the ground (number).

- **R (the residual).** The mismatch `R_s = |Gamma(global mean) - o'_s|` for one region s. Arity: two objects, one aggregate over N slots and one slot; the aggregate is a many-to-one map. Symmetry: the mean is a symmetric function of its slots, so reordering leaves it unchanged and the arrangement is lost. Scale: global versus regional, across the membrane. Fig 9 names the per-slot form the object of measurement, not the aggregate.
- **Domain A, aggregation structure (mathematics and statistics).** Actors: N slots, the weighted-mean operator, spatial variance. Currency: water quantities (mm/day) and trends (%/K). Anchor: the arithmetic identity that a mean can rise while a component falls, plus mass balance. This is a partition: mean and slot are different quantities. Nameable anchor, so a genuine domain.
- **Domain B, hydroclimate physics (sciences).** Actors: atmosphere, ocean, land surface, aerosols, circulation, moisture transport, convection. Currency: water flux and atmospheric energy flux. Anchor: gauges, satellites, reanalysis, CMIP ensembles, the energy constraint. This is a union compound: thermodynamics, dynamics, aerosols and land use all bind. It is unbundled, not averaged (Fig 9 :54-58).
- **Domain C, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle legality (A) before mechanism (B); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); record an outside verdict before showing any theory (Fig 10 :52).

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources are corroboration only. They do not overrule any figure line. Each was opened on 2026-11-22; author, venue, year and quote are recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

- **Field: climate assessment (physical basis).** Douville et al., IPCC AR6 WG1 Chapter 8, Cambridge University Press, 2021. "There is high confidence that global mean precipitation and evaporation increase with global warming, but the estimated rate is model-dependent (very likely range of 1-3% per 1C)." Predicts a rising mean with a small, energy-constrained rate; corroborates the figure verdict that the mean is a different object from the slot. Also: "the atmospheric energy budget" constrains the mean while regional changes depend on moisture transport. Overlap: distinct assessment venue, low overlap with the framework.
- **Field: climate assessment (impacts).** Caretta et al., IPCC AR6 WG2 Chapter 4, Cambridge University Press, 2022. The AR5 "wetter regions get wetter, drier regions get drier" concept "is assessed by AR6 WGI ... as too simplistic"; in most land regions even the sign of projected change is uncertain. Predicts that the aggregate slogan is not a local rule; corroborates F6. Overlap: same assessment family as the previous source, so these two are one pool, not two witnesses.
- **Field: atmospheric physics.** Held and Soden, Journal of Climate, 2006. Global-mean precipitation strength "does not scale with Clausius-Clapeyron"; sensitivity "on the order of 2% K-1 ... much weaker than CC scaling"; with fixed flow, wet regions get wetter and dry regions drier. Predicts a mean constrained more weakly than moisture; corroborates the two-object reading. Overlap: independent of the framework, but cited by the assessment sources above, so partly shared.
- **Field: atmospheric physics and scale.** Dagan and Stier, npj Climate and Atmospheric Science, 2020. "local precipitation changes, which are key for society and ecosystems, demonstrate a large spread in predictions by climate models, can be of both signs and have much larger magnitude than the global mean change." Gives the regional/large-scale break near 3000 km. Predicts precisely the isomeric divergence the figures describe; corroborates Fig 4. Overlap: cites Held and Soden, so partly shared.
- **Field: atmospheric water budget.** Dagan, Stier and Watson-Parris, Geophysical Research Letters, 2019. "for smaller scales ... changes in P could be much larger due to dynamical contributions"; closure scale near 3500-4000 km. Predicts the composition of the cross-scale gap; corroborates Fig 4 :31-32. Overlap: same authors and pool as Dagan and Stier 2020, so one witness.
- **Field: climate dynamics.** Wills et al. overview, Caltech Climate Dynamics Group, 2016. "These circulation changes lead to regional departures from the 'wet gets wetter, dry gets drier' idea"; thermodynamic scaling has the wrong sign over about one third of the globe. Predicts that a positive mean can coexist with a drying region; corroborates Fig 9 :54. Overlap: cites Held and Soden and Seager et al., partly shared.
- **Field: mathematics and statistics (low overlap).** Simpson's Paradox, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2021, substantive revision 2026. "an association between two variables in a population emerges, disappears or reverses when the population is divided into subpopulations." Predicts that an aggregate trend need not hold in a partition; corroborates Fig 1 :19 and :22 from a field with no connection to climate science or the framework. Overlap: genuinely low, so this is the strongest independent witness in the set.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Most climate sources share an evidence pool through Held and Soden and the IPCC assessments; they are counted as one witness with several facets, not several independent witnesses. The statistics source is the low-overlap corroborator.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Yes.** The joint state is legal and structurally expected: the residual is per slot, the mean is a lossy flat formula, equal-total spatial arrangements are isomers, and the cross-scale transform is not reversible. The global mean is a budget filter, not a verdict witness for a region. The physical mechanism is a union of energy-budget and moisture-transport conditions, corroborated by the climate sources, and "wet gets wetter, dry gets drier" is not a local law. The figures supply the shape and the refuters; which region dries, by how much and over what period is a ground question owned by gauges, reanalysis and models. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result from a simulated derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable for the structural claim, because the refuter is written in advance and the figure lines are quoted verbatim. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the framework before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. The cost of misusing the mean as a regional prediction is not abstract: it misdirects water and drought planning.
- **undoable:** yes. The structural verdict can be retracted by showing invertibility of the mean, a zero-gap downscaling operator, or a framework rule forbidding the joint state. No primitive is withdrawn and no source is silently discarded; the retraction log in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` keeps the earlier readings visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot gauge and reanalysis trends over a stated period; a moisture-budget decomposition separating thermodynamic from dynamic terms; a scale test against the roughly 3000-4000 km closure; or a figure re-census that finds a quantitative regional line. Until one of these fires, the structural verdict stands as `candidate` and the empirical claim stands as reported by its sources.
