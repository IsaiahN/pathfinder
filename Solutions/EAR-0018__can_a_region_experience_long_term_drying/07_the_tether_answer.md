# The Tether's answer to EAR-0018

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdict, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two objects. One is a global mean precipitation trend over a period, a weighted sum of many regional slots. The other is one slot, a named region, whose precipitation persistently falls over the same period. The residual is the mismatch produced by using the aggregate as a prediction for the slot. The Tether answers the structural part of this directly and hands the physical part to the ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The residual is per slot, never per aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The region is literally one of the framework's slots: the Symbols table defines `s` as "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." So the correct object for "is this region drying?" is R_s, not the global trend.
2. **The joint state is legal.** Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state" and Fig 1 :20-21 "Support is satisfied when some slot carries positive mass, so a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one." A rising mean and a persistently drying region can both be true. There is no contradiction to resolve; there is a frame error to refuse.
3. **The mean is a flat formula.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." The mean keeps the total and drops the arrangement. Two worlds with the same total but different spatial arrangement are Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." That is the structural form of the answer.
4. **The cross-scale transform is lossy.** Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Averaging up is abstraction; asking what the mean implies for a region is concretisation. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The gap is location, transport and divergence, and the literature puts its closure scale at a few thousand kilometres.
5. **The mechanism must be imported from a different closure.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The global frame cannot derive a slot. What crosses in is the energy-budget and moisture-transport frame, with provenance and a shadow test: it explains the per-slot shape. Its verdicts are secondary corroboration, not the figure verdict.
6. **The mechanism is a union, not one cause.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Thermodynamics, circulation, aerosols, land use and internal variability all bind. "Wet gets wetter, dry gets drier" is an aggregate-scale slogan, and current assessments call it too simplistic.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

The structural verdict is settled by the figure text. The physical question is settled by instruments:

- **Per-slot trends:** gauge and satellite records and reanalysis for the named region over a stated period, compared with the global trend over the same period. The anchor must not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Mechanism discrimination:** a moisture-budget diagnosis that separates the thermodynamic term (moisture increase, about 7% per K) from the dynamic term (circulation and moisture transport), and an energy-budget diagnosis for the global mean (about 1-3% per K). A reading that cannot distinguish sources is the metamerism error of `05_what_shape_means.md` section 4.
- **Scale test:** does the region lie below the roughly 3000-4000 km scale where divergence terms still operate? Above it, its trend should track the global mean; below it, no such expectation holds.
- **Standing falsifiers from section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:** invertibility of the mean, a zero-gap downscaling operator, a single-cause mechanism, or a figure line that predicts a quantitative regional trend.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is complete: a per-slot residual, a lossy aggregate, an isomeric arrangement and an imported transport frame. The number is not. The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." The empirical claim is left to the climate literature with its provenance recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. "Indiscernibility is not identity": a global mean that does not discriminate among spatial arrangements is not evidence that the arrangements are the same.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Yes, a region can dry while the global mean rises, and the reason is structural before it is physical. The framework measures residuals per object slot, not as a single aggregate, and aggregating across slots is exactly how a live regional signal disappears into an average. A global mean keeps the total water and drops the arrangement, so two worlds with the same total but different spatial arrangements are structurally isomers: the difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour. The cross-scale transform is lossy and not reversible, so the mean cannot be used as a downscaled prediction for a region. What the mean cannot supply must be imported from a different frame: the atmospheric energy budget constrains the global mean to roughly 1-3% per degree while moisture transport and circulation, governed by the water budget, set regional changes of either sign below a few thousand kilometres. That mechanism is a union of conditions, not the slogan "wet gets wetter, dry gets drier." The figures give the shape and the refuters, not the regional number; gauges, reanalysis and models settle that. This is a `specified` structural result from a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.
