## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13, :19, :22, Fig 12 :5, :94, :117 and Fig 4 :27, :30.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: the mean and the slot are different objects; the joint state is legal; equal-total arrangements are isomers; the cross-scale transform is lossy.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim: a named region dries while the mean rises; the mechanism is the energy and moisture budgets; the divergence scale is roughly 3000-4000 km.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an import:

- the **quantitative regional trend** (which region, how much, which decades), dropped by the figures and supplied only by the world sources;
- the **union structure** of the mechanism, which collapses if shorthand says merely "the mechanism";
- the **anchor requirement**, namely that the per-slot trend be read off a gauge or reanalysis that does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a positive aggregate residual and a negative slot residual are a legal state, and the mean cannot recover the arrangement. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no single ground reading was taken in this run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the figures as if they supplied the regional number would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
