## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry, scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. - **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the persistent asymmetry in method authority. Arity: at least five operands, method, anchor, community, habitat, transmission (and a sixth appears if closure is separated). Symmetry: authority is a relation between method and community relative to a ground, not an intrinsic property; the same method changes valence with the residual. Scale: method, field, institution. Question type: union compound (Fig 9 :54: unbundle before searching again).
- **Domain 1, method-to-ground contact.** Actors: method Γ, predictions, observations. Currency: per-slot residual and settlement. Anchor: the ground, a measurement or outcome. Union member: a necessary condition for the anchoring kind of authority.
- **Domain 2, community verification.** Actors: researchers, journals, verifier seats. Currency: independence, agreement, residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Union member: the site of the two-kinds confusion and the mirror collapse.
- **Domain 3, habitat and selection.** Actors: funders, instruments, curricula, problems, institutions. Currency: arrival and contact cascades. Anchor: the ground. Union member: the site of isolation by substitution.
- **Domain 4, transmission.** Actors: methods as generators, recordings, next researchers. Currency: reapplication versus replay. Anchor: ground contact on reapplication. Union member: the site of generator/recording confusion.
- **Domain 5, closure and import.** Actors: atoms(Γ), closure(Γ), peer frames. Currency: atoms, imports, provenance. Anchor: the ground via an imported primitive. Union member: the wall that can block any of the above.

Order by dependency: contact, then objective, then planning, then learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); closure is a wall, not a link. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :26-28); refuse before mint (formula step 2).
