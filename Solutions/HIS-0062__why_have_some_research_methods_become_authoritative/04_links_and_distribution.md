## The two-stage offer (stage 3 is a method, not a claim)

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim, and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three sources that actually bear (the figures README, one history-of-science case, one methods-hierarchy case). Their later agreement is never pooled as a witness.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the loop as a method to reuse. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 lists the five ways it fails. If one fires, stop and withdraw the claim rather than sending the links.
