# Outreach draft - check the shape claim before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 is written down and dated on your side. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown.

---

**Subject:** One claim about method authority that you can check in five minutes

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a historical question, not asking for a belief. The question is why some research methods become authoritative while others stay marginal. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the observed asymmetry is not one thing. It is a union of at least five conditions that have to be kept separate:
>
> 1. Does the method range against a ground that settles a question and does not move when the field pushes on it?
> 2. Is the community that endorses it made of independent frames, or is it a chain drawing on one shared pool, where agreement compounds and evidence does not?
> 3. Does the method's habitat (instruments, venues, training, funded problems) maintain contact with that ground, or was the habitat substituted?
> 4. Is the method transmitted as a reapplicable procedure, or only as a recording of past successes?
> 5. Does the field's closure already contain the atom the method carries, or would a differing frame have to be imported?
>
> The claim is that authority has **two different kinds**. One kind earns the right to settle a fact by staying in contact with a ground. The other coordinates shared meaning by convention, which is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict. Most of the history that looks like "the best method won" is the second kind doing the job of the first. This is a shape, not a name: it predicts which conditions bind, not which named method wins.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that a famous method is false, that a marginal method is true, that citation counts measure truth, or that agreement from people who share a literature is corroboration. Prestige can give a method a budget to be tried. It cannot settle the method.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. A method's authority persists after its ground contact is severed, its community is one shared pool, and no differing frame is admitted.
> 2. A method's capability increases while its habitat and its contact are held fixed.
> 3. High-overlap agreement drawn from one evidence pool predicts correct method choice above chance after overlap is removed.
> 4. A field admits a method with an atom absent from its closure and no import in the record.
> 5. A record of particular successes, with no reapplicable procedure, reliably generates new successes on a fresh occasion.
>
> If any one of those holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send three public links. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority. Your verdict on the five points above is recorded before you see any of that, and your later agreement is never counted as corroboration.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** it is a loop that starts from a residual in effect terms, refuses before it mints, keeps verdicts per domain, and asks what would move the result to `grounded`. Run it on a case you know well. Write down your own refuter before you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [your name]
