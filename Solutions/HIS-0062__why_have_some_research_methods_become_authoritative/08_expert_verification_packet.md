# Expert verification packet - the Tether on method authority

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian or sociologist of science can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the habitat and closure claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No controlled historical comparison is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The observed asymmetry in method authority is a union of conditions, not one question, and "authority" names two different things. Anchoring authority settles a fact because its reference point does not move when the field pushes on it; convention authority coordinates shared meaning, and is efficient at that until it is asked to decide a fact. Credibility and citation counts are quantities the field produces about itself, so they cannot be the ground. A chain of endorsers who share one evidence pool compounds agreement rather than evidence. A method's capability is a property of method and habitat, so an internal improvement that does not change contact changes nothing; only a reapplicable method crosses to the next generation, while a recording of past successes does not. A field's authoritative set is a closure, and a marginal method may carry an atom it lacks, which is why the wall moves only on a provenanced import from a differing frame. The framework returns this shape, not the name of a winning method.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7, and 1

- **Statement:** authority splits into two kinds. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :41, *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 2 :43, *"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Supporting: Fig 7 :13, *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- **What to check:** does the source text actually carry this valence distinction, and does the history-of-science record show cases where a convention was asked to decide a fact?
- **Refuter:** produce a case in which high-overlap agreement from one evidence pool predicts ground settlement above chance after overlap is removed, and where no convention has been promoted to a verdict.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 4

- **Statement:** capability is method-and-habitat; isolation substitutes a habitat; an internal improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. Only a reapplicable method crosses scales, not a recording of a success.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40, *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :66, *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 4 :23, *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*
- **What to check:** is this a claim about the figure's own terms, and does it survive the historian's counter-case of a method improved internally within a stable habitat?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a method whose capability at the ground increased while its habitat and its contact were held literally fixed; or a record of particular successes, with no reapplicable procedure, that reliably generated new ground-paid successes on a fresh occasion.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a world-facing test

- **Statement:** the authoritative set is a closure; a marginal method can hold an atom the field lacks, so the wall moves only on contact with a differing frame. This predicts that an import needs provenance and is debited against independence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :24, *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Fig 8 :45, *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
- **What to check:** is the closure claim about methods analogous to the figure's closure(Γ) in a way the historian accepts, or is it a metaphor stretched past its evidence? This is the weakest of the three and is offered for refutation first.
- **Refuter:** show a field admitting a method with an atom absent from its closure, derived entirely inside the field, with no differing-frame contact in the record.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named method is the winner, and not a ranking of methods.
- Not that consensus is worthless; it is efficient for a convention about shared meaning.
- Not that a famous method is false or a marginal method true.
- Not that more experts, a bigger survey, or a prestigious list would settle the question.
- Not `grounded`. No controlled historical comparison was run; no overlap between sources was partialled out; no instrument was built.
- Not a claim that the figures directly describe historiography. The figures are primary for the shape; the history-of-science sources are secondary corroboration only, and they overlap with each other.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating comparison. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. **Anchor test:** hold ground contact fixed and sever the community's independent frames; does authority persist?
2. **Habitat test:** hold habitat and contact fixed while improving the method internally; does capability move?
3. **Import test:** introduce a method with an atom absent from the field's closure, with provenance and a shadow test; does the wall move only then?
4. **Isomer test:** compare methods with the same ingredients and a different bond; do they diverge where the bond binds?
5. Any one of the five refuters in `01` §4 firing means withdrawal of the claim.
