## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For HIS-0062 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of the explanation we can state before we can name the winning method. It constrains: it says the outcome is a union of conditions, that authority has two kinds, that agreement is not an anchor, that a method's habitat must maintain contact, that only methods cross scales. It does not fill: it does not say "the randomized controlled trial won because X" or "ethnography stayed marginal because Y." Those are names drawn from the historical record, not shapes derived from the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a bootstrapping convergence process."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the census in `01` describes the residual in effect terms (what failed to resolve, which slot carried live mass) before choosing any figure line.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the operating rule for the outreach packet.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop comes when the five domains stop returning new residuals against the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, a name would be licensed by a controlled historical comparison showing that a specific method's authority tracked its ground contact after overlap was removed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "The RCT became authoritative because it carried an atom the previous field lacked" would be an import from the history-of-medicine frame. It needs provenance and a shadow test; it is not a derivation from the figures.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens why a reading does not determine a name: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can give the same reading. Here, a citation count or a consensus statement is the reading; "this method is truth-tracking" is a claim about the source. The reading does not settle it. By that essay, *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and what is owed is a better instrument.

## 4. Why "the best method won" is the specific forbidden error

The naive explanation names a winner and treats its authority as a verdict. That commits two errors at once.

- It reads a projection (consensus, prestige, citations) as if it determined the source (truth-tracking contact). This is the metamerism error in *The Hard Problem is About Source*.
- It scores the method with a quantity the field itself produces. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

The correct shape remains: authority is a compound, and the slot marked by which condition binds stays open until a ground or a provenanced import fills it.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 and §4

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any method in the answer. That overstates it. The README phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in Γ. The methods in HIS-0062 are not missing primitives; they are already in the historical record and in the world frame. So naming a method is not forbidden by that sentence.

What remains wrong, and is **not** retracted, is treating the name as a figure-derived verdict. A specific method, say the randomized controlled trial, is a binding from the history-of-medicine frame onto a pattern the figures describe (an anchor that does not move when pushed, a habitat that maintains contact). That binding must carry provenance and a shadow test. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost: naming is allowed, but a name with no provenance is the error.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read directly, 2026-11-23):

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: a famous method gets a budget (funding, chairs, journals, training pipelines). That is legitimate and it is not evidence that the method tracks the ground. A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that a method won is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give a candidate method a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

## 8. Consequences for this question

1. Deliver the shape (two kinds of authority, union of conditions, open binding slot), not a name of the winning method.
2. State the mode on every substantive claim: `general` for the structural split, `specified` for the habitat and closure claims, nothing `grounded`.
