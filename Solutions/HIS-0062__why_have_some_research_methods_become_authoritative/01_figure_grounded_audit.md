## 0. The residual in effect terms

R is the observed asymmetry itself: in a field at a time, method X is authoritative (its results settle disputes, it is funded, taught, cited as the standard) while method Y stays marginal, and this persists across cases where authority does not track ground contact. In the loop notation, R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot s, where Γ is the community's library of methods, b its inherited weighting, a its choice of method, and o' the outcome the ground returns. The puzzle is that a global R near zero (the field works by its own lights) coexists with live per-slot residuals (some methods are trusted beyond what they pay for at the ground). Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** at least five operands, a method, an anchor, a community of frames, a habitat, and a transmission channel. The residual is a union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **Symmetry:** authority is not a property a method carries. It is a relation between a method and a community ranging against a ground. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The same method is an anchor to one community and a marginal curiosity to another.
- **Scale:** individual method, community or field, institution or society. Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."* A verdict at one level is an instance at the next; it does not cross by being repeated.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term.** Before running, I write down what an answer would look like (formula step "Direction"). An answer here is a shape with four properties: it separates two kinds of authority; it names the anchor that settles each; it says which link a method chain stops at; and it states what instrument would move a method from marginal to authoritative. Any answer lacking these four is not the sought-for shape and is refused before it accrues credibility.

**Partition or union: union.** A method can fail D1 and still be authoritative through D2; it can pass D1 and stay marginal because D3 withdrawn its habitat or D4 transmitted it as a recording. **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception and vocabulary (D1: can the method register the world and name what it affords?) precede the objective (D2: can the community say what counts as progress?), which precedes planning (D3: is there a route the habitat permits?), which precedes learn and carry (D4: does a method cross up?). D5 is a wall that can block any link. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces."* A lever applied above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Authority comes in two kinds, and only one of them settles facts

Fig 2 :29-30:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."**

Fig 2 :39-41:

> **"Correlation has valence."**
> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
> **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**

Fig 2 :43: *"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

A method is authoritative in the **anchoring** sense when its output is settled by a ground that does not move when the community pushes on it (D1). A method is authoritative in the **convention** sense when it coordinates shared meaning, and agreement is the product rather than a corruption of it (D2). Both are real. They are not the same thing. The puzzle dissolves partly into a category mistake: a convention is asked to do a verdict's job. When that happens, Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47 (*"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*) say it has stopped being a convention without becoming a ground.

**Strain test.** Fig 2 :41 seems to praise agreement that Fig 2 :40 disqualifies. Re-census: :41 governs conventions about shared meaning (units, terminology, reporting formats), :40 governs verdicts about the world. Different quantities, low overlap within the same figure. Not strained after re-census. This is the re-census rule from the canonical README: a reading that makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

**Refuter.** Show a case where agreement about shared meaning changes an outcome at the ground while the anchor is held fixed and unmoved, and where no convention has been promoted into a verdict. If that holds, the two-kinds split collapses.

### F2 - Credibility is a frame-internal score, so it cannot be the ground of authority

Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 1 :26-27:

> **"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"**
> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."*

Prestige, citation count, and institutional standing are quantities the community produces about itself. They can fund a method's trial, but by Fig 1 :27 they cannot score it, and by Fig 10 :93 an institution that defends its own reputation has stopped being a ground. This is why the historical pattern includes authoritative methods later found wrong and marginal methods later vindicated: the score and the contact drifted apart.

**Refuter.** Produce a controlled comparison in which a method's credibility score predicts its ground settlement after all shared-evidence overlap is removed and the reputation of its institution is held constant.

### F3 - A mirror chain compounds agreement, not evidence

Fig 7 :12-13: **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

A method becomes authoritative inside a field by being handed along: taught, cited, reviewed by people trained on the same canon. Each handoff adds agreement. By Fig 7 :12-13 the chain can run a long way without adding evidence. This explains why a method can be almost universally endorsed inside a field and still fail at first contact with an independent ground. It also explains the replication crisis pattern (world source read 2026-11-23): papers that cannot be replicated are more likely to be cited, so the citation channel and the ground channel decouple.

**Refuter.** Show a chain of frames drawing on one evidence pool whose agreement, measured across handoffs, predicts independent ground settlement at above-chance rate after overlap is partialled out.

### F4 - Capability is agent-and-habitat, so improving a method in isolation changes nothing

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 11 :39-40:

> **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :66-67: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*

A method stays marginal when its habitat is substituted: the instruments it needs are absent, the problems it would illuminate are not funded, its practitioners are not trained, or its results are not admissible in the venues that decide. The method itself may be internally excellent. By Fig 11 :40 the improvement does not register because contact did not change. Conversely, an authoritative method can be carried by its habitat long after its ground contact has decayed, because the habitat is the field of contact and can maintain a reading that the ground no longer pays for.

**Refuter.** Demonstrate a method whose capability at the ground increases while its habitat (instruments, admissible venues, training, funded problems) is held literally fixed and its contact with the ground unchanged. That would break Fig 11 :40.

### F5 - Only methods cross up; a recording of a success produces repetition, not a new success

Fig 4 :22-23:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
> **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**

Fig 4 :25-26: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*

Some methods become authoritative because they are generators: they can be taught as a reappplicable procedure and handed to the next researcher as a head start. Others are known only through their landmark results, which travel as recordings. A recording can look like a method from a distance and fails on a new occasion. Fig 4 :30 adds the instrument: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

**Refuter.** Show a body of work transmitted as a record of particular results, with no reapplicable procedure, that reliably generates new ground-paid successes in a different setting without a head start being rebuilt.

### F6 - A marginal method may hold an atom the authoritative closure lacks

Fig 6 :21-23:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

A field's authoritative set is its closure(Γ), the methods composable from the primitives it already holds. A marginal method can be unreachable from inside, not because it is false but because it carries an atom the field lacks. By Fig 6 :23 the wall moves only on contact with a differing frame, and by Fig 8 :53 the residual must be described before the frame is chosen: *"Residual first, frame second."* The import needs a source gate and a shadow test, and it is debited against independence.

**Refuter.** Show an authoritative set admitting a method with a genuinely new atom, derived entirely inside its own closure, with no differing-frame contact and no import in the record.

### F7 - Describe the residual before you go looking for the method

Fig 9 :26-28:

> **"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*"**
> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

The field labels "method" is a filter, not a witness. If we start from a list of famous methods and ask why they won, any list will seem to fit. The residual has to be described in effect terms: what failed to resolve, which slot carried live mass, what an answer would look like. This is why the domain census precedes the figure quotes in this audit.

**Refuter.** Produce a derivation that starts from a preselected list of authoritative methods, reports a clean explanation of their authority, and survives a re-run in which the residual is described first and the list is assembled afterward from the habitat.

### F8 - An institution with a stake is not disqualified by having a stake, but by defending a reading

Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :61: *"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."* Fig 10 :65-66:

> **"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."**

Authoritative methods are often defended by institutions whose standing was earned on the earlier successes of those methods. By Fig 10 :61 that standing is a stake that survives the outcome. The failure is not having an interest but defending the reading rather than letting the ground range it. A clean publication record is provenance, not truth.

**Refuter.** Show a body that changes its method when the ground changes while its institutional standing, funding, and prior commitments are held constant and no new outside frame is admitted.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction and the exemplar's practice. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. Under a controlled comparison, a method's authority persists after its ground contact is severed, its community is a single shared pool, and no import from a differing closure is admitted (breaks F1, F3, F6).
2. A method's capability at the ground increases while its habitat (instruments, venues, training, funded problems) and its contact are held fixed (breaks F4, Fig 11 :40).
3. High-overlap agreement drawn from one evidence pool predicts correct method choice above chance after overlap is removed (breaks F3, Fig 2 :40).
4. A field admits a method carrying an atom absent from its closure with no differing-frame contact recorded (breaks F6, Fig 6 :23).
5. A video/record of particular successes, with no reapplicable procedure, generates new ground-paid successes on a fresh occasion (breaks F5, Fig 4 :25-26).

Until one of these is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Ask which link the method chain stops at before asking whether the method is good (Fig 3 :29-30: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*).
2. Keep the two kinds of authority separate in any write-up. Do not average them into a single score (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).
3. Treat prestige and citation counts as a budget the method gets to be tried, never as a verdict (Fig 1 :27; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."*).
4. Lead with the residual and the refuter when contacting anyone. Offer links only after their verdict is recorded (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
5. Report the shape and stop. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

**Step 7 discipline, kept distinct.** SEARCHED: is the method already inside the field's closure and merely unfound? The answer is more distributed, independent search (Fig 6 :49-50). INWARD: is the field's instrument adequate to hold it? Sharpen from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-34). OUTWARD: does another frame already hold the atom? Cast the residual in effect terms and import with provenance (Fig 6 :30, Fig 8 :45). Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 8 :40-43). No one of these is read against another.
