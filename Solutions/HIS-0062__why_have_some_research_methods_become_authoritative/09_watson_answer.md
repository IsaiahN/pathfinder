## QUESTION

Explain, in effect terms, the persistent asymmetry in which research methods are trusted, funded, taught, and taken to settle disputes while others remain marginal. Option A: authority tracks method quality (the best method won). Option B: authority is a union of conditions in which grounding, community verification, habitat, transmission, and closure each bind, and in which authority has two distinct kinds, one that settles facts and one that coordinates shared meaning. What each leads to: A predicts that more agreement means more evidence and that the authoritative set is truth-tracking; B predicts mirror chains with compounding agreement, two kinds of authority that are routinely confused, an open binding slot, and a stop at the limit of the available instruments.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Authority is not one property. It is a union of five conditions, and the word names two different things: an anchoring kind that settles facts because its reference does not move, and a convention kind that coordinates shared meaning and is efficient at that until it is asked to decide a fact. Credibility, prestige, and citations are frame-internal scores, so they can fund a method's trial but cannot settle it. A chain of endorsers drawing on one literature compounds agreement, not evidence. Capability is method-and-habitat, so internal improvement that does not change contact changes nothing; only a reapplicable method crosses scales, while a recording of successes does not. A field's authoritative set is a closure, and a marginal method may carry an atom it lacks, so the wall moves only through a provenanced import from a differing closure. The result is a `specified`, ungrounded shape with five pre-registered refuters. It does not name a winning method. The habitat clause (D3) and transmission claim (D4) are single-source and are recorded as such.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its refuters, and its provenance before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than silently compounding. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a residual (Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoided by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be withdrawn without discarding any figure: fire any of the five refuters in `01` §4, or show with a controlled comparison that ground contact does not discriminate authoritative from marginal methods. No primitive has to be retracted to reverse this answer.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled historical comparison that partials out shared evidence and tests anchor contact, a habitat test holding contact fixed, an import test with provenance and a shadow test, or an isomer test on methods sharing ingredients but not bonds. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or a longer list of agreeable readers is not such an instrument.
