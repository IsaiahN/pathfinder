# The Tether's answer on method authority

**Mode: `specified` on the habitat and closure claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical verdict. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Methods do not become authoritative one at a time. The asymmetry (trusted, funded, taught, settling disputes versus marginal) is a union compound of at least five conditions, not one hard question. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The naive answer, "the best method won," averages the conditions and throws away the live per-slot residual (Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **Authority has two kinds.** Anchoring authority settles a fact because its reference point does not move when the field pushes on it: Fig 2 :29-30, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Convention authority coordinates shared meaning, where agreement is the product: Fig 2 :41, *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* The historical puzzle is mostly a convention doing a verdict's job (Fig 2 :43).
2. **Credibility is not the ground.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Prestige gives a method a budget to be tried. It cannot settle it.
3. **A chain of endorsement is not a chain of evidence.** Fig 7 :13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Independent frames, not more frames, are what make agreement meaningful (Fig 2 :31).
4. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A marginal method often lacks the instruments, venues, training, or funded problems that would keep it in contact with a ground. Isolation substitutes a habitat (Fig 11 :66).
5. **Only methods cross scales.** Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* A method known only through its landmark results travels as a recording and fails on a new occasion.
6. **The authoritative set has a wall.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* A marginal method can carry an atom the field does not hold. Import requires provenance and is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24).
7. **The answer is a shape, not a champion.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* It names which conditions bind and what instrument would close each, not which named method won.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a famous method is false or a marginal method is true.
- Not that any specific method, randomized trial, cohort study, ethnography, or simulation, is *the* winner. Binding a name requires a ground comparison or a provenanced import, neither of which is supplied here.
- Not that consensus is worthless. It is efficient for a convention about shared meaning (Fig 2 :41). It is disqualifying for a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that more experts settle the question (Fig 7 :13).
- Not grounded. No controlled historical comparison was run, no overlap was partialled out, no instrument was built.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating comparison, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Anchor test:** hold ground contact fixed, sever the community's independent frames, and see whether authority persists. It should not, under F1 and F3.
- **Habitat test:** hold a method's habitat and contact literally fixed while improving it internally. By Fig 11 :40 its capability at the ground should not move.
- **Import test:** introduce a method with an atom absent from the field's closure, with provenance and a shadow test; check whether the field's wall moves only then (Fig 6 :23).
- **Isomer test:** compare two methods with the same ingredient set but a different bond (random allocation versus observed allocation). They should behave differently where the bond binds, even though their ingredient formula is identical (Fig 12 :117).
- **Standing refuters:** the five in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, all written before this answer.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return a shape. The specific bindings (which method in which field carried the atom, which habitat was substituted in which century) belong to the history-of-science frame and must be imported with provenance, or settled by a comparison at the ground. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that the output is only as good as the validator and that a metatheory cannot validate itself. This answer stops at the limit of what the figures distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning method. It says that "authority" is two different things: a method can settle a fact because its reference point does not move when the field pushes on it, or it can coordinate a field's shared meaning by convention, which is useful until the convention is asked to decide a fact. Credibility and citations are scores the field produces about itself, so they cannot be the ground. A chain of researchers who learned from the same literature compounds agreement, not evidence. A method stays marginal when its habitat (instruments, venues, training, funded problems) loses contact with the ground, and internal improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. A method known only through its famous results travels as a recording and cannot produce a new success; only a reapplicable method crosses to the next generation. And a field's authoritative set has a wall: a marginal method may carry an atom the field lacks, which is why it takes a provenanced import from a different frame to move it. What is offered is the shape any explanation must have, five refuters written in advance, and the comparisons that would close it. The binding slot stays open until the ground settles it.
