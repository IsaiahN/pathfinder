# The Tether's answer: the simplest system that can undergo Darwinian evolution

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the substrate and habitat mapping, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Simplest" is two questions, not one (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*): the substrate floor (S1) and the unit floor (S2). The answer keeps them separate. The figure-derived result is a **shape plus a refusal**: a shape for any Darwinian system, and a refusal to name the smallest physical unit.

## 2. The shape

1. **The unit is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone.** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The simplest system is the smallest pair that keeps contact, not the smallest molecule.
2. **Selection is between variants, and the residual is per variant.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A population average is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
3. **Reproduction closes a cycle; a chain cannot.** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Inheritance is the loop, and the persistent template is the catalyst.
4. **Both factors must be nonzero.** Fig 7 :37 *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*; :38 *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"*; :39 *"the ground's pressure on the room"*. Fidelity without pressure, or pressure without fidelity, is motion without cumulative evolution.
5. **Variation has two sources.** Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Ordinary variation is unbounded rearrangement over a closed set; genuinely new atoms require contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23).
6. **No maintainer is needed; the ground is the selector.** Fig 11 :56 *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* Fig 11 :36 *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."*
7. **The substrate floor is six terms, no seventh.** Fig 13 :3 *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* Fig 13 :48 *"And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."* This answers S1. `single-source` (framework-internal), `general`.
8. **The unit floor (S2) is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The figures do not name an RNA replicator, a hypercycle, a vesicle, or a clay crystal. Naming one is a binding from the chemistry frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the first evolving system is a named molecule. That is the `gap`, and a name would breach the F8 refuter.
- Not that the six terms are nature's own list. They are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (essay, read 2026-11-23), a visible and revisable choice.
- Not that selection alone creates novelty. It is *"non-innovative"* in the world's own mathematics (Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23).
- Not that a crystal or a repeating reaction is evolving. Repetition without a heritable difference and a ground that votes is not the loop.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement dates the first evolving system or discriminates among the candidate chemistries.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument or experiment, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Fidelity test:** does the system carry a selected difference across a handoff with h² measurably greater than zero, and is mutation nonzero but below the error threshold?
- **Pressure test:** does the ground change the relative abundance of variants, and does selection stop when the ground is suspended?
- **Cycle test:** does the output of one generation re-enter as the input to the next, with the template surviving its own copying?
- **Per-variant test:** can persistence be predicted per variant, rather than only by a population average?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a seventh; observe cumulative evolution with h² = 0 or S = 0; or find a governing figure line that names the physical unit.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops where the figures stop: at a shape and an open slot. A search that found no name is not a proof that no name is possible (Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A candidate chemistry that behaves like the shape is not thereby shown to be the first one.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a smallest first replicator. It says the question is two questions. The substrate floor is six kinds, with composition across kinds and never within one, and no seventh is admitted. The unit floor is open: the framework returns a shape, not a name. That shape is an agent-and-habitat pair, not an agent alone; selection between per-variant residuals with the ground voting; reproduction as a cycle where the output re-enters as input; and two nonzero factors, the fidelity of the handoff and the pressure of the ground. Variation comes from unbounded arrangements over a closed set plus imports from a differing closure, and no maintainer is needed because the environment is the only selector when no seat is occupied. What is offered is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the open slot. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
