## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question asks for a floor. Stated as an effect: what is the least arrangement in which a per-slot residual can vary, be inherited, and be sorted by a ground that does not move when any frame pushes on it? What fails to resolve at the outset is the word "simplest." It names two different quantities:

- **S1, substrate minimality.** How few kinds must any composing system have? The figures answer this directly at Fig 13.
- **S2, unit minimality.** How small can the evolving unit be, and which physical thing is it? The figures do not name one. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* and Fig 13 :135 says *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

A single answer that silently merges S1 and S2 would be a union treated as a partition (Fig 9 :54). This audit keeps them separate and marks S2 a `gap`.

The sought-for shape is made an ordinary term now, before the run, per Formula step "Direction": *write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else.* Bound shape: **an agent-and-habitat pair running a closed cycle, with per-slot residuals, bracket fidelity and ground pressure both nonzero, over the six-term substrate, with the physical unit left unnamed.** That bound term is what the refuter tests.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: at least three places (replicator slot, variant set, ground), so evolution is never a property of one entity alone. Symmetry: variation is undirected, selection is ordered. The ground votes and the replicators do not vote on the ground, so the governing bond is asymmetric (Fig 12 :74, the sequence test *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*). Scale: within-generation per-slot error, between-generation inheritance, population-level sorting, and habitat-level variation supply. The transform between the scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).

One line per domain. A domain needs actors, a currency, and a nameable anchor. Where there is no anchor, it is not a domain yet.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - Evolution is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone (decisive for framing)

Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

So the "simplest system" is not the smallest replicator. It is the smallest agent-and-habitat pair. A bare genome in a nutrient soup is not a Darwinian system until the soup is counted as the partner that settles which genome persists. This is the framework's own author's point in *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector."* (essay, read 2026-11-23).
**Mode `general`. Triangulated:** Fig 11 (habitat) and Fig 7 (selection between rooms), low overlap (one figure is about contact, the other about handoff). **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground and yields cumulative evolution without any change in contact.

### F2 - Selection is between variants, per slot, not within one and not on an average

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 7 :33: **"→ variation inside, selection between"**
Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

The unit of selection is a slot (Fig 1's `s`) carrying its own residual, and the ground votes between rooms that differ. A population-average fitness is a filter for budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*). World corroboration: the quasispecies account says *"selection does not act on single sequences, but on mutational "clouds" of closely related sequences"* and the replicator-equation account says it is *"non-innovative"* (both Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23). These agree on the shape from mathematics and chemistry, fields with low overlap with the framework.
**Mode `general`, mapping `specified`. Triangulated.** **Refuter:** a system whose cumulative evolution is fully predicted by a single global average with no per-slot indexing.

### F3 - Reproduction is a cycle bond, not a chain; a catalyst survives its own use

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

Darwinian evolution needs inheritance, which is the output re-entering as input. A linear reaction chain cannot close by construction, so it cannot be a replicator lineage. A template that survives its own copying is Fig 12's catalyst. World corroboration: *"a hypercycle is an abstract model of organization of self-replicating molecules connected in a cyclic, autocatalytic manner"* and *"each of them catalyses the creation of its successor, with the last molecule catalysing the first one"* (Hypercycle (chemistry), Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23). This is the same bond shape arrived at independently in chemistry.
**Mode `general`. Triangulated** (Fig 12 bond definition plus the chemistry account, low overlap). **Refuter:** an indefinitely evolving linear chain with no cyclic closure, or a template that is consumed by its own copying and still inherits.

### F4 - Both factors of progress must be nonzero: R = h² x S

Fig 7 :37: **"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"**
Fig 7 :38: **"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"**
Fig 7 :39: **"the ground's pressure on the room"**

Heredity without selection pressure, or pressure without heredity, produces motion without cumulative change. This gives the cleanest operational floor available from the figures: bracket fidelity h² > 0 and ground pressure S > 0. World corroboration: the quasispecies model has a fidelity/error-threshold bound, *"If the mutation rate is too high, exceeding what is known as the error threshold, the quasispecies will break down and be dispersed over the entire range of available sequences"* (Quasispecies model, Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23); the self-replication account notes *"Any self-replicating mechanism which does not make a perfect copy (mutation) will experience genetic variation"* (Self-replication, Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23). Note the subtlety: fidelity cannot be perfect either, or there is no variation. Both factors need a positive but bounded value.
**Mode `general`, the numeric form `specified` (the figures give the product, not measured values). Triangulated.** **Refuter:** cumulative Darwinian evolution observed with h² = 0 or S = 0, or with zero mutation.

### F5 - Variation has two sources, and they are not the same quantity

Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**
Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."**
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
Fig 13 :113: **"So the array is unbounded and the reach is not."**

Within the six-term substrate, arrangements are unbounded, so ordinary variation (mutation, recombination, compositional novelty) needs no new primitive. Genuinely new atoms, if the system requires them, must be imported from a habitat whose closure differs. The replicator equation's *"non-innovative"* character (world source, see F2) is the mathematical statement of the same limit: selection alone sorts what is already present. **Apparent strain resolved by re-census:** open-ended evolution seems to contradict idempotence. Re-census shows Fig 8 governs atoms and Fig 13 :115 governs arrangements. Different quantities, no strain.
**Mode `general`. Triangulated** (Fig 8, Fig 13, Fig 6, with the mathematics source as low-overlap corroboration). **Refuter:** new atoms generated by internal composition alone, with no differing closure.

### F6 - Darwinian evolution is the unmaintained mode, and needs no intelligence

Fig 11 :56: **"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."**
Fig 11 :36: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."**
Fig 11 :5: **"survival of the fittest"** is the figure's own label for panel 1.

So X2 ("intelligence as cause") is absorbed into D2/D3 as a seat, not admitted as a separate domain. This is also the framework author's explicit reading: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground"* and *"Natural selection is not the whole answer. It is a special case. The full answer is the loop itself."* (Arrival of the Fittest, read 2026-11-23). An important limit on that sentence: it is the author's interpretation, so it corroborates the figure reading but shares the figure's evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
**Mode `general`. Triangulated** (Fig 11 panel 1 plus the dependency/seat rule, different figures). **Refuter:** a lineage in which survival is decided by a frame's agreement rather than by the ground.

### F7 - The substrate floor: six terms, two kinds, no seventh

Fig 13 :3: **"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."**
Fig 13 :48: **"And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."**
Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**
Fig 13 :116: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever"**

This answers S1. Any composing system, including any evolving chemical system, assumes states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network). A seventh is ruled out; a missing member breaks a law (Fig 13 :48-71 gives the thermodynamic test table). This is a `general` structural claim, not a claim that a prebiotic soup literally contains a "spectrum" in the laboratory sense. **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five, or exhibit a Darwinian system that composes without one of the two kinds.
**Mode `general`. single-source** for the minimality table (framework-internal: Fig 13 and the Formula substrate section are one evidence pool, not two witnesses). Reported as single-source deliberately.

### F8 - Naming the smallest physical unit: `gap`

After a genuine census, no figure line names a chemical instantiation. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The world literature confirms why: *"Opinions differ, however, as to whether RNA constituted the first autonomous self-replicating system or was a derivative of a still-earlier system"* (RNA world, Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23); the minimal-genome account says *"it is thought what makes up the minimal genome will depend on the environmental conditions that the organism inhabits"* (Minimal genome, Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23). The floor of S2 is habitat-dependent and instrument-dependent, so a name would be a binding from a world frame, not a figure derivation. **This is an honest `gap`, and it is the answer to the literal question at the level the figures can reach.**
**Mode `general` for the limit, `specified` for the refusal to name.** **Refuter (would close the gap):** a figure line that names a specific chemical unit as the simplest evolving system. None was found.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map, quoted in the exemplar), the shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A system undergoes cumulative Darwinian evolution with bracket fidelity h² = 0 (no inheritance of the selected difference).
2. A system undergoes cumulative Darwinian evolution with ground pressure S = 0 (selection suspended, yet directional cumulative change continues).
3. A system undergoes cumulative Darwinian evolution fully predicted by a single global average, with no per-slot residual.
4. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five, or a seventh indivisible term is admitted.
5. A figure line is found that names a specific chemical unit as the simplest evolving system (this would flip F8 from `gap` to governed, and would require re-running the audit).

Until one fires, every substantive claim stays `candidate`. None was fired in this run.
