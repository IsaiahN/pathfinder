## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "What is the simplest system that can undergo Darwinian evolution?" Option A: answer with a named physical unit (an RNA replicator, a hypercycle, a vesicle, a clay crystal). Option B: split "simplest" into a substrate floor and a unit floor, give the figure-derived shape for any Darwinian system, and mark the unit floor as `gap`. What each leads to: A imports a name where the framework returns a shape, and smuggles in a habitat-dependent answer as if it were universal; B gives a checkable shape, five pre-registered refuters, and an open slot that a discriminating instrument could close.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unresolved gap between "minimum" as a substrate count and "minimum" as a physical unit. Arity: three places (variant, variant set, ground). Symmetry: variation is undirected, selection is ordered, so the governing relation is asymmetric (Fig 12 :74, the sequence test *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*). Scale: per-variant error, between-generation inheritance, population sorting, habitat variation supply. The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).
- **D1 Replicator loop.** Actors: slots, Γ, the cycle bond. Currency: per-slot residual and bracket fidelity h². Anchor: the ground. Admitted.
- **D2 Habitat and variation supply.** Actors: habitat, conditions, relations, imported atoms. Currency: contact and variation. Anchor: enumerated from contact; the ground settles. Admitted.
- **D3 Ground and selection.** Actors: the ground. Currency: what survives. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not update. Admitted.
- **D4 Substrate.** Actors: six terms in two kinds, seven bonds plus negation. Currency: composition and reach. Anchor: the figures. Admitted.
- **X1 Named chemical unit.** Actors: named molecules. Currency: chemical kinetics. Anchor: none from the figures. Not a domain yet: `gap`.
- **X2 Intelligence or designer as cause.** Absorbed into D2/D3 as a seat (Fig 11 :36). Not separate.

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D2/D3 are overlapping conditions; a single lever leaves the compound standing (Fig 9 :55). **Order by dependency.** Describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."*); refuse the "smallest entity" framing before minting; settle the first-order loop before the second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104).

**Strain test applied.** Fig 8 idempotence versus open-ended evolution: re-census separates atoms from arrangements (Fig 13 :115). No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures give a shape and a refusal. The substrate floor is six terms in two kinds, with no seventh, and that part of the question is answered at `general` mode. The unit floor is a `gap`: no figure line names an RNA replicator, a hypercycle, a vesicle or a clay crystal, and the framework's own rule returns a shape rather than a name. Between those, the figure-derived shape is firm: agent-and-habitat rather than agent alone; per-variant residuals with the ground voting between variants; reproduction as a cycle whose output re-enters as input with a catalyst that survives its own use; progress as the product of bracket fidelity and ground pressure; variation from unbounded arrangements plus imports from a differing closure; and no maintainer required, since in the unmaintained mode what survives is what the ground kept. All verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its five refuters and its lineage before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a name or a full theory before they have judged the shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added. The two floor questions can be separated again without withdrawing the shape, and `01` keeps the retraction of my own first "smallest entity" reading visible. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates which chemical system first crossed the fidelity threshold would close the X1 gap; a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a seventh indivisible term, would break D4; cumulative evolution with h² = 0 or S = 0, or full prediction by a population average, would break D1; a figure line naming the unit would flip X1 from `gap` to governed and require a re-run. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
