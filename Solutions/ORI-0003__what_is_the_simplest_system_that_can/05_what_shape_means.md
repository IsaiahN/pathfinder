## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (figures README). Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0003 this means: **"an agent-and-habitat pair running a closed cycle with per-slot residuals, nonzero fidelity and ground pressure, over six kinds, with the physical unit unnamed" is a shape. "An RNA replicator" is a name.** The shape constrains a candidate answer; it does not supply one.

The same essay diagnoses the original mistake: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the split between substrate minimality (S1) and unit minimality (S2) in §0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on recurring terms. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the email in `02` lists refuters in effect terms rather than a diagram of the framework.
- On stopping: *Library of Babel* says *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The same essay: *"you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author."* This is a warning for this solution: a shape derived from the essays about the essays is internal consistency, not a ground reading.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the status of the six themselves: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So Fig 13's floor is a visible, revisable choice, not nature's own list read off the world. Naming one chemical instantiation as *the* first evolving system would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

The same essay on imports: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* For this question, importing "RNA" into the slot is a new term in an existing space; importing an instrument that discriminates which chemical system actually crossed the threshold would be a new dimension.

## 4. The source problem: why a reading does not name its cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from the spectrometer, not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: observing that a chemical system persists and changes does not tell you which of several mechanisms (template copying, autocatalytic set, compartment selection, mineral surface) produced the reading. Naming RNA from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating experiment, not a name. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the caution: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. The special case of this question: natural selection is the unmaintained branch

*Arrival of the Fittest* (Substack) states the identity directly: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* And: *"Natural selection is not the whole answer. It is a special case. The full answer is the loop itself."*

This is the canonical reading of F1 and F6 in `01`. It also limits the answer: the "simplest system that can undergo Darwinian evolution" is a case of the loop, and the loop is the more general object. Asking which came first is asking for an origin of a lineage, which *How Intelligence Actually Works* frames as seed and ground: *"A ground with no seed gives you a scorched earth. A seed with no ground gives you unbounded generation with no resilience to harsh conditions and constraints."* And: *"The seed is not the recording of a tree, it is instead the encoding of something that will grow one."*

The same essay notes the cost of the real thing: *"Evolution pays the bill in bodies and lots of time."* A simulated derivation pays none of that bill, which is why it cannot settle the origin.

## 6. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any particular structure in the slot. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. For this question the open slot is not one of the six; it is the physical instantiation of the unit. The distinction matters: Fig 13's six are already named by the framework, while "the first replicator" is not. So the ban is correctly applied to S2 (chemical unit), not to S1 (substrate), and F7's naming of the six is not a violation. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach and for status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names who agree with a smallest-system story is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle which system first evolved, and they say so on every substantive claim.
