# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the simplest evolving system

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in origins of life, evolutionary theory or complex systems can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the substrate and habitat mapping, not `grounded`.** No measurement dates or identifies the first evolving system. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question splits into two. The substrate floor is figure-governed: any composing system needs six terms in two kinds, states and structures, with composition across kinds and never within one, and no seventh. The unit floor is not figure-governed and stays open: the framework returns a shape, not a name. The shape is an agent-and-habitat pair, not an agent alone; selection between variants with the residual indexed per variant rather than averaged; reproduction as a cycle whose output re-enters as input, with a template that survives its own use; and progress as the product of bracket fidelity and ground pressure, both nonzero. Variation comes from unbounded arrangements over a closed set plus imports from a differing closure. In the unmaintained case, nothing selects on a maintainer's behalf and what survives is what the ground kept. Five refuters are written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 1 and 7

- **Statement:** the minimal Darwinian system is agent-and-habitat, and selection is between variants, not within one and not on a population average. Progress needs two nonzero factors.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Also Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 7 :35 *"Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :37 *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*, :38 *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"*, :39 *"the ground's pressure on the room"*.
- **Refuter:** cumulative Darwinian evolution shown with zero inheritance of the selected difference, or zero selection pressure, or fully predicted by a population average with no per-variant residual. Also: an agent-only improvement that moves the ground.

### C2 - Structural; checkable by reading Figure 12 and comparing with the chemistry literature

- **Statement:** reproduction is a cycle bond, not a chain; the persistent template is a catalyst in the figure's sense; a chain cannot close by construction. The candidate chemistries discussed in the literature (RNA replicator, autocatalytic set, compartment-selecting protocell) are a **functional group** sharing this motif, not demonstrated **isomers** over one atom set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :124 *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **What to check:** does the candidate system close a loop, and does its template survive its own copying? Does a linear reaction network with no closure ever produce cumulative evolution? Are the candidates actually over a shared atom set?
- **Refuter:** an indefinitely evolving linear chain with no closure; a template consumed by its own copying that still inherits the difference; or a demonstration that two candidates share an atom set and differ only in bonds while remaining evolvable (which would promote the claim from functional group to isomer).

### C3 - Framework-internal limit; the one that decides whether the literal question was answered

- **Statement:** the substrate floor is six and no seventh, but the framework cannot name the smallest physical unit. That part of the question is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :3 *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* Fig 13 :48 *"And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*; Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a seventh indivisible term; or supply a figure line that names a specific chemical unit as the simplest evolving system (which would close the gap and require re-running the audit).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the first evolving system is RNA, a hypercycle, a vesicle, or a clay crystal.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument or experiment, not agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

1. **Fidelity:** is h² measurably greater than zero, with mutation nonzero but below the error threshold?
2. **Pressure:** does the ground change relative abundance, and does selection stop when it is suspended?
3. **Cycle:** does the output re-enter as input, with the template surviving its own copying?
4. **Per variant:** can persistence be predicted per variant rather than only by an average?
