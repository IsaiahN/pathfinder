## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **The two kinds of minimality are different.** The quasispecies and hypercycle accounts supply a fidelity bound and a cycle; the minimal-genome account says the floor depends on conditions. None names a universal smallest entity. Together they corroborate the split in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` from mathematics, chemistry and synthetic biology, three fields with low overlap with the framework.
2. **Selection is non-innovative on its own.** The replicator equation explicitly *"does not include mechanisms for mutation or the introduction of new strategies."* This is the world's version of Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* It corroborates the shape but does not decide it.
3. **Openness is a condition, not an ornament.** The dissipative-system account describes an open system exchanging energy and matter far from equilibrium, with autocatalytic reactions and reproducible steady states. This is the world-frame counterpart of Fig 11's habitat and Fig 8's import.
4. **All the above share an evidence pool at one remove.** They are secondary summaries (Wikipedia pages) of primary literature. Where they agree with the figures it is weak corroboration, not a second witness. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; here the agreement is across different fields but through the same kind of compiled source, so overlap is declared rather than hidden.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer the essays after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (as quoted in the tacit-knowledge essay). Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement Trap:** *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask anyone to endorse it.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so stage 3 gives away a method rather than an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) shape claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are authoritative; (4) if asked, the method with its own refuter requirement.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive. The claim in `02_outreach_email.md` is deliberately a shape with five refuters and no named chemical unit. If it is rewritten to name "the first replicator", it fails its own F8 refuter and must not be sent.
