## Wider literature that bears on the question

Three clusters, with overlap declared:

1. **Contemporary evolution and eco-evolutionary dynamics.** Yoshida 2003, Grant and Grant 2002, Phillips 2006, Bell 2017. These are the closest field witnesses for the claim that a response is contact- and slot-dependent. They share the contemporary-evolution literature pool and count as one witness for that pool, not four independent confirmations.
2. **Coevolution and context dependence.** Thompson 2010, Holland and Bronstein 2008, Yoder 2025. Different conceptual frames within ecology and evolution: geographic mosaics, conditional outcomes, and the evidential standard for calling an interaction coevolution. Partial overlap with cluster 1 through Thompson's own work.
3. **Low-overlap corroboration.** Nowak 2006 (mathematical biology / game theory) and Laland et al. 2016 (human cultural evolution and niche construction). These are the two genuinely different evidence pools, so their agreement with the closure and contact shape carries more weight than another ecology review would.

- **Fig 2 :40** — "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." No ranking by likely agreement, no bulk send, no pooling of later endorsements.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures and the primary literature, not this summary, are the authority.
