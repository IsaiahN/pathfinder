## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43/:49-51 (habitat, valence), Fig 6 :21/:23/:25 (closure, import, no genesis), Fig 9 :54 (union) and Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: interactions constrain by closing reach and enable by opening reach; valence is relative to the residual; capability is agent-and-habitat.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a named field case such as "predation enabled defence in population P in season k."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** of the residual, which a population-level "predation enabled defence" statement silently aggregates (Fig 1 :19);
- the **source gate and shadow test** on the named binding, without which "predation" is an adopted import rather than a convergent derivation (Fig 8 :50-52);
- the **response-source discriminator**, because one trait-change reading has several possible sources and the shorthand says "response" as if the source were known (Fig 3 :27).

A fourth, smaller loss is the **catalyst versus spent-part distinction** (Fig 12 :119): a pollinator that persists across uses and a consumer that depletes its resource are written with the same word in most ecology vocabularies.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the same interaction can take opposite valences against different residuals, and reach rather than genesis is the variable. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
