# The Tether's answer: how species interactions constrain or enable evolutionary responses

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a focal species-slot and a trait in a season, the inherited frame predicts a response and the interaction returns an observation that misses it. The question asks whether interactions fall into a constraining list and an enabling list. That is a false partition. The two lists are a union of overlapping conditions: which contact is enumerated, which slot the residual is indexed on, and which response atom is already reachable (Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Interactions are the habitat, enumerated not composed.** Fig 11 :43, "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :47 lists "relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency." The list for a particular residual is read off the world, including second and third order contact, not assembled from interaction categories.

2. **Valence is relative to the residual, not carried by the partner.** Fig 11 :49, "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :50, "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." A predator can constrain foraging and enable defence in the same season. There is no standing list of good and bad interactions (Fig 11 :51, "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual").

3. **Constraint in effect terms is closure; enablement is import across a differing closure.** Fig 6 :21, "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32, "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 6 :23, "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Constraint is not a force and enablement is not creation: Fig 6 :25, "An import adds no atom to the world." The partner changes which moves the focal lineage can reach.

4. **The residual is per slot, never a community mean.** Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A mean effect of interactions budgets the search; it does not decide the claim (Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict").

5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat, not intrinsic.** Fig 11 :40, "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A common-garden result is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66, "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another") and a microcosm proves wiring, never capability (Fig 11 :70, "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability").

6. **The recurring structures are cycles and catalysts.** Fig 12 :121, "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :119, "A part that survives its own use." A pollinator or niche constructor persists across its own use; a spent consumer does not. A flat interaction list cannot record the difference.

7. **Two regimes over a fixed atom set are isomers.** Fig 12 :117, "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Constraint and enablement readings of the same predator and the same prey, ranged against different traits, are the same atoms arranged differently. If a new atom is required, the relation is compound plus import, and its provenance must be shown.

8. **The framework returns a separator, not a name or a percentage.** Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135, "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." No figure line identifies which partner controls which trait, or returns a net sign or magnitude. That is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached and not as silence (Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence").

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named interaction is intrinsically constraining or enabling. Valence is residual-relative.
- Not that interactions create traits. Import changes reach, not the world's inventory.
- Not that an increase in adaptive capacity measured in isolation transfers to the field. Isolation substitutes a habitat.
- Not that a community-wide mean effect of interaction is a verdict.
- Not a mechanism for any particular species pair. That is a field claim with its own provenance.
- Not `grounded`. No selection gradient, common-garden result, mark-recapture estimate or reciprocal transplant was taken in this derivation.
- Not that the word "response" is settled. The source of a trait-change reading is still unresolved (see section 5).

## 5. What would settle it

The ground, not more agreement. Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The owed instrument is a discriminator that separates genetic adaptation, phenotypic plasticity and movement for the same trait in the same season. Without it, one reading has several possible sources, and the colour-instrument problem from *The Hard Problem is About Source* applies: "it cannot comment on the source."

Concrete checks for a named system:

1. **Slot check:** does the residual stay live at one species-slot when the community mean is near zero?
2. **Reach check:** was the new response atom already in the lineage's closure, or did it arrive with a partner whose closure differs? Record the provenance and the shadow test either way.
3. **Contact check:** does the effect survive removal of the partner in a common garden or exclusion, and does the common garden itself substitute a habitat with unchosen actors?
4. **Source check:** common garden, reciprocal transplant, mark-recapture or parental assignment that separates evolution, plasticity and movement.
5. **Round-trip check:** measure R_T between a microcosm or lab shorthand and the field case; do not assume the gap is zero (Fig 4 :30).

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: a valence-invariant interaction; a new atom produced by composition inside a fixed closure; an aggregate that loses no live slot; an isolation-only gain that survives return; an acyclic representation of frequency dependence with no loss; a common currency for bits and actions.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The enumeration stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something" (Fig 6 :32). The particular binding is a provenanced import from ecology, not a derivation. The record is kept so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Fig 13 :129, "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a list of constraining and enabling interactions. It says that the question's "or" is a union of overlapping conditions, and that the same predator or pollinator can constrain one trait and enable another depending on which residual is indexed. Constraint in effect terms is closure: no amount of search inside a lineage's current reach adds a new move. Enablement is contact with a differing frame that changes which moves are reachable, and the partner does not create the trait. Capability belongs to the agent and habitat together, so an improvement measured in isolation changes nothing unless it changes contact. The framework names no partner and returns no net percentage; it returns the separator, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument owed to distinguish genetic change from plasticity and movement. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
