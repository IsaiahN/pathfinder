## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135, "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question, the shape is the separator itself: enumerate the contact, index the residual at a slot, decide whether the response stayed inside the lineage's reach or arrived through contact with a differing frame, and name the measurement that would settle it. A list of "constraining interactions" and "enabling interactions" is a filled answer, and it is not the shape the framework returns.

Applied carefully: the shape is **not** "predation constrains and mutualism enables." That is a binding from the ecology frame, and by Fig 11 :49-51 its valence flips with the residual.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (opened): "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The method is proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on recurring new terms that overlap with it. Here the starting vocabulary is per-slot residual, contact, reach and import; the ecology terms (predation, pollination, competition, dependency) enter as bindings through proximity, not as primitives supplied by the framework.
- Fig 8 :53, "Residual first, frame second." And Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." A search that begins "which literature studies species interactions?" has already picked the frame.
- Fig 6 :30, "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what a reader would expect to see at the focal slot before sending any framework material.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stop is the point at which the separator can be applied to a named system without adding a new term.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." A measured selection gradient naming a partner's effect is a ground settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming pollination as the relevant relation for a plant's trait is an import from the ecology frame. It is legitimate only when its provenance and the gap it explains are recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (quote carried from the exemplar mapping, not re-opened this session): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six-term set is therefore a visible guess, not a discovered list of nature's categories. Naming one of its atoms as *the* cause of an evolutionary response turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source problem, and why it governs the word "response"

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The essay's resolution is historical and practical: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a change in a population's mean trait value is one green reading. Its source may be genetic evolution, phenotypic plasticity, immigration of individuals with a different mean, or a shifted age structure. The observation cannot comment on the source. Calling the reading "an evolutionary response" is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (common garden, reciprocal transplant, mark-recapture, parental assignment), not a name. This is the instrument debt in `01` F10 and the broken middle link in Fig 3 :27.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (quote carried from the exemplar mapping, not re-opened this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any partner or relation. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Predation, pollination, competition and dependency are already available bindings from the field, and Fig 11 :47 lists exactly those relations. Naming one is provenance, not a forbidden invention.

What remains forbidden is claiming the figure derived the name, or treating the named binding as an import that moves the wall when the atom was already held. Fig 6 :25, "An import adds no atom to the world." The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted: the reading still does not determine the source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Why the question's own vocabulary is part of the problem

The question asks how interactions "constrain or enable evolutionary responses." Three ordinary terms are loaded with unexamined structure:

- **"Constrain"** suggests a force. Fig 8 :32 makes closure definitional: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Constraint is the shape of a reach, not a push.
- **"Enable"** suggests creation. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." Enablement is access, not genesis.
- **"Responses"** suggests a single kind of change. Section 4 shows that one reading has several possible sources. The term must be split before it can be used as an anchor.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* and *Arrival of the Fittest* (quotes carried from the exemplar mapping, not re-opened this session) supply the practical analogue: room per problem, commons of methods only, and the ground under all. For this question, that means a separate room per focal slot, shared methods (enumerate, index, range against the ground, write the refuter), and no shared answer list.

## 7. Prestige is not action; this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (quote carried from the exemplar mapping, not re-opened this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers well-known ecologists to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the separator a budget to be tried and let a measurement settle it.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
