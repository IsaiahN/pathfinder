## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For focal slot `s`, trait `t`, season `k`, the inherited frame predicts `Γ(b,a)` and the interaction returns `o'`, leaving `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`. Arity: at least three slots (focal, partner, third-party cascade); the residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"). Symmetry: the interaction is a directed relation while its valence flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49). Scale: ecological time and evolutionary time are two scales with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4 :30). Type: union, not partition (Fig 9 :54).
- **Domain 1, the interaction habitat.** Actors: predator, prey, competitor, mutualist, parasite, pollinator, disperser, conspecific, and conditions of light, water, temperature and season. Currency: contact and fitness consequence. Anchor: what actually happens to the focal lineage, measured independently of any model. Nameable anchor exists, so this is a domain. Verdict `general` shape, `specified` per named system, triangulated (Fig 11 with Fig 1).
- **Domain 2, the response frame.** Actors: alleles, genotypes, regulatory and developmental pathways, plastic reaction norms, behaviour, dispersers. Currency: heritable variation, selection gradient, generation time. Anchor: a measurement surviving removal from the interaction (common garden, reciprocal transplant, mark-recapture). Nameable anchor exists, so this is a domain. Verdict `specified`, single-source on the figure side for the split of response kinds, world corroboration from Grant and Grant 2002 and Bell 2017.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: field ecologists, modellers, reviewers, archives. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is low and agreement is not a witness. Verdict `general` rule, triangulated (Fig 2 with Fig 7).
- **No fourth domain for "the interaction itself."** Without a focal residual, a relation is not yet a domain; it is a candidate binding in D1. This is the anchor test applied at census.
- **Union, not partition.** The three overlapping conditions are contact, slot-indexed residual, and current reach. A lever acting on only one leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-58).
- **Dependency order.** Name slot; state residual; enumerate habitat outward by cascade; refuse before mint; decide reach versus import per slot; settle first-order before composing second-order; measure the round trip (Fig 3 :20; Formula step 2; Fig 12 :104; Fig 4 :30).

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :50 (relations, not kinds) versus the mutualism-parasitism continuum in Holland and Bronstein and Thompson was dissolved by re-census. Fig 11 governs valence relative to a named residual; the continuum governs outcome distribution across contexts. Different objects, so no figure contradiction. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's partition is refused. The two lists are a union of overlapping conditions. Valence is relative to the residual, so the same partner can constrain one trait and enable another. Constraint is closure and reachability; enablement is contact with a differing closure that changes what the focal lineage can reach, and the partner does not create the trait. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so isolation-only improvements change nothing. The residual stays per slot, and a community mean is a budget filter, not a witness. Two regimes over a fixed atom set are isomers; where atoms change, the relation is compound plus import with provenance and a shadow test. The framework returns a separator with pre-registered refuters and an owed source discriminator, not a named partner and not a net percentage. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, provided the packet is sent with its mode and refuters in front of the links. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a name instead of a separator. Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is treating a community mean as a verdict and aiming fieldwork at the wrong slot. Both are avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A named binding can be withdrawn or re-provenanced without touching the separator, and a failed refuter narrows one domain rather than the whole shape. Nothing in the record has to be deleted; a refusal adds a term and the reach falls safely (Formula step 3). Nothing here has been sent to anyone, so no correction has to be propagated to a public audience.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator that separates genetic evolution, plasticity and movement would settle what "response" means in a named case; a measurement showing that a newly reachable atom arose inside a fixed closure with no contact, mutation or migration would break the import claim; an interaction with invariant valence across all enumerated habitats would break Fig 11 :49-51; a lab-to-field round trip with a measured zero gap would close one of the two debts. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
