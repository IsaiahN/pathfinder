## What is being decided, in effect terms

For a focal species-slot and a trait, the inherited frame predicts one response and the interaction returns an observation that misses it. The residual is that miss, indexed per slot. "Constrain" and "enable" are asked to name two lists of interactions. The derivation tests whether that is a partition of two kinds of interaction, or a union of overlapping conditions in which the same relation takes opposite valences against different residuals. What an answer would look like is written down and bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0, so the sought-for shape can accrue a residual like anything else (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Adding more readers raises the vocabulary and does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **Sending the interior before the verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
