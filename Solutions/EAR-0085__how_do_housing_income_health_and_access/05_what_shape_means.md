## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0085, the shape is: a per-slot distribution read through an enumerated habitat whose conditions have residual-relative valence, a union of overlapping conditions, and an anchored measurement channel. The name would be "housing is the binding factor in ward 7" or "income explains 40 percent of the variance." Neither is licensed here.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn’t possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you’d expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends effect descriptions, not jargon.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the direct warrant for treating housing, income, health and service access as a union rather than a list.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, that means a per-slot outcome measurement that discriminates which condition bound.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question, that means a discipline that already predicts the distribution shape (epidemiology, infrastructure engineering, ecology) brought in with its record shown, not an index adopted because it is convenient.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why a vulnerability index is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A social vulnerability index reads a composite score for a neighbourhood and cannot say which condition produced the harm in a named household. Several combinations of housing, income, health and service access yield the same score. Naming "housing" as the source from the score is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* An index is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27); it cannot validate the distribution it is used to explain.

## 5. Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming housing, income, health or services?

No, with a distinction that matters. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Housing, income, health and service access are not missing primitives; they are observed conditions and relations read off the world (Fig 11 :43-48, *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world"*). Naming them is **enumeration**, which the framework requires, not the invention of a new kind.

What is forbidden is (i) treating an enumerated condition as a standing "good" or "bad" actor when valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51), (ii) composing them into an index and calling the index the answer (Fig 12 :5), and (iii) naming which one binds in a given case before a source-discriminating instrument exists. The exemplar QMGR-0037 made the parallel correction for naming Gradient: a binding onto a held atom is not the invention of a new primitive, but claiming it moves the wall is wrong (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). The same discipline applies here.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected institutions to agree that "housing matters" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried against per-slot outcomes.

**Independence is bought, not assumed.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* applies the same rule to groups: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* It also warns that *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* when a group's votes are pooled. For EAR-0085 that means low-overlap readers, each ranged separately against the ground.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* The architecture article adds that such outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of who bears climate harm and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. What it can do is state the shape the answer must have, the conditions that must be kept separate, the anchor that would settle a claim, and the falsifiers written in advance.
