## Three things in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **The distributions are real and unequal.** IPCC AR6 WGII B.1 and B.2.4 report that the most vulnerable people and systems are disproportionately affected and that mortality from floods, droughts and storms was 15 times higher in highly vulnerable regions. This is the ground-level phenomenon the shape claim addresses. It is corroboration only; it does not decide which figure line governs.
2. **The four factors overlap, and the intersections matter.** IPCC Chapter 6 states that intersections of gender, class, race, age, ability and other drivers *"shape unique experiences of vulnerability and risk."* Mah et al. show that equal-income households can sit in distinct tiers. This is a world-level match for Fig 9 :55-57 (union, not list). One evidence pool, so one witness.
3. **Indices are useful filters and poor verdicts.** The opened synthesis records that weighting and aggregation choices affect an index's results and that some argue vulnerability *"cannot be categorized and captured fully in indexes."* Mah et al. report *"there is no gold standard."* This matches Fig 1 :27 (a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it produces) and Fig 9 :60 (filters for the budget, witnesses for the verdict).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is high, so seek low-overlap readers: engineers, epidemiologists, ecologists, local and Indigenous knowledge holders, each ranged separately against outcomes.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it before any theory is shown; (3) then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against a per-slot ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the framework that produced it. **(3)** Use the formula recursively on other problems.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first and never pooled later as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result and labels the mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Stage 1 here is a shape, not a measurement, and it is explicitly `specified`. Do not present it as a settled result about any real community.
