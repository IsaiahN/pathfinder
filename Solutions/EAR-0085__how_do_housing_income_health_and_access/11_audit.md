## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the unequal per-slot harm left after a climate shock. Arity: multi-place (bearer, hazard, action, outcome, time), so it carries a sequence bond and a comparison bond, not a symmetric one. Symmetry: bearing is a threshold relative to a baseline and to other slots, and it is asymmetric in time. Scale: household, community, city and region, with a lossy membrane between scales.
- **D1 Household capability loop.** Actors: household members, Γ, actions. Currency: actions and residual reduction. Anchor: realised outcome per household. Admitted.
- **D2 Habitat.** Actors: housing, income, health, service access as conditions and relations. Currency: contact and valence relative to the residual. Anchor: measured harm distribution; habitat updates, ground settles (Fig 11 :73). Admitted.
- **D3 Measurement frame.** Actors: surveys, sensors, indices, records. Currency: contact versus pooled readings. Anchor: the ground. Admitted.
- **D4 Union compound.** Actors: intersections of conditions. Currency: which condition binds. Anchor: per-slot outcome at the intersection. Admitted.
- **X1 Vulnerability as a free-floating property of a place.** No anchor independent of outcomes; frame-internal score (Fig 1 :24-27). Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Adaptive capacity as one stock.** Absorbed into D1 and D2.

Union, not partition. D1/D2 and D2/D4 overlap; D3 overlaps all three. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20-27), with the break expected at vocabulary or objective where there is no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: index per slot, enumerate habitat, do not compose, split the union, trace cascades, keep the anchor reachable, stop at the shape.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete deliverable a policy reader would want: a ranked list of which condition binds in a named place, with effect sizes and a budget recommendation.

**R_T is nonzero and large at the last step.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip down to a concrete answer:

- the **binding condition** (the shorthand refuses to name it; the concrete question demands it);
- the **effect size or weight** of each condition (the figures price structure, not coefficients; no instrument here measures them);
- the **intersection-specific lever** (Fig 9 :57 says the intersection is categorically different, but the figures do not say which lever clears it).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be enumerated, the distribution must remain per slot, and the union must be kept separate. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
