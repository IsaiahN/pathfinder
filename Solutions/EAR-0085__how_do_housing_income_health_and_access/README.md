**Source:** earth systems.txt line 113

**Section:** 9. Human infrastructure, society, and adaptation

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No per-slot ground measurement was taken in this run, so nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## Restatement as an effect

The question asks which slots (households, groups, places) carry the positive residual after a climate shock, and how four habitat conditions (housing, income, health, service access) bear on that distribution. "Who bears" is a per-slot distribution question, not an average. An answer would look like: an enumerated habitat whose elements are indexed per slot, a separation of overlapping conditions that can each fail independently, a characterisation of the anchor that settles a harm claim, and a source-discriminating instrument that would name which condition binds in a given case. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` §0, so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles a real question about who bears climate harm.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room does not become an anchor. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) Sending the framework before asking for a verdict spends the outside reader's independence.
3. *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60) Prestige, fame and institutional weight are contact-cost estimates only.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

This folder is a **simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. The source essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output is *"simulacra"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. It states what a figure-grounded derivation would predict and what would refute it.
