# The Tether's answer: who bears climate impacts

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a distribution, not a total. After the same hazard, some household slots carry a large positive residual (harm) and others a small one. Housing, income, health and service access are asked to explain that difference. The answer must be indexed per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19-22). The anchor is measured per-slot human outcome, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :29, :36-37). The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Read harm per bearer, never as a regional mean.** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A mean answers a different question and destroys the diagnostic.
2. **Housing, income, health and service access are habitat enumerated, not ingredients in an index.** Fig 11 :43, *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Their valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51): the same condition protects in one hazard and harms in another. A composite score is Fig 12's flat formula, which records ingredients and not arrangement (Fig 12 :5).
3. **Bearing is household-and-habitat, not a property of either alone.** Fig 11 :39-40. An intervention that changes the household without changing contact may change nothing.
4. **The four form a union whose intersections are categorically different cases.** Fig 9 :55-57. One lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. They are a functional group (Fig 12 :123), not a ranking.
5. **Material harm arrives through cascades several steps out.** Fig 11 :52-53 (DDT reached the eagles three steps out) and Fig 3 :20 (dependency order). A first-order exposure map misses the slots that matter most.
6. **The measurement channel decays even though the harm does not.** Fig 10 :24, :26. Pooled readings and saturated metrics produce *"you have lost touch"* (:28). Keep the anchor reachable (:29).
7. **Duration is a chain of handoffs with two factors.** Fig 7 :37-39. If bracket fidelity or ground pressure goes to zero, the household runs in place. Resources can be catalysts or spent parts (Fig 12 :119), and recovery can close or reverse as a cycle (:121).
8. **The binding condition is not named by the framework.** It returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32). Which of the four, or which intersection, actually produced the positive residual in a named slot is a declared `gap`. An instrument that reads the composite outcome cannot say what produced it; a source-discriminating instrument is owed.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that housing, income, health or services is *the* cause in any named place. No source-discriminating instrument was run.
- Not that a vulnerability index is useless. It is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60), not a witness for the verdict (Fig 1 :27).
- Not that the four factors form a standing list of good and bad attributes. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not that this is a settlement of any real community's experience. It is `specified` and ungrounded; no per-slot outcome measurement was taken.
- Not that agreement among researchers settles the distribution. Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a result *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the analogue. Here the question is: which condition, or which intersection, produced the positive residual in a named slot? The discriminating observations would include:

- A per-slot outcome series that distinguishes tenure, income, health status and service reach within the same hazard footprint, with no regional pooling.
- A natural experiment in which one condition changes while the others are held, with the per-slot distribution read before and after (tests F2 and F4).
- A cascade trace that follows service interruption through to health and income outcomes, rather than stopping at direct exposure (tests F3).
- A recovery series that measures bracket fidelity and ground pressure at each handoff (tests F7).
- Low-overlap readers: engineers, epidemiologists, ecologists, local and Indigenous knowledge holders, each ranged separately against the same per-slot outcomes (Fig 2 :42).

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: an aggregate that predicts per named household; an invariant factor valence; all harm first order; an agent-only intervention that moves the distribution; a single lever that clears all intersections; a full-distribution measurement with no pooling; a check settled by a shared evidence pool.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are *"read off the world"* (Fig 11 :48), not invented, and they are the right habitat to enumerate. But the source of a given slot's harm is below the current union horizon for this run: a composite reading does not discriminate among causes, and no new instrument was built here. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). The record stops where the instruments stop and keeps the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank housing, income, health and service access as causes of unequal climate harm, and it does not claim a settled answer for any community. It says that who bears is a per-slot distribution that must never be averaged into a regional mean; that the four factors are an enumerated habitat whose valence changes with the hazard and the outcome, not ingredients in a vulnerability score; that they overlap as a union whose intersections are different cases needing different levers; that much harm arrives through cascades several steps from direct exposure, and is lost from view when readings are pooled; and that the binding condition in a named place stays unnamed until a source-discriminating instrument exists. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
