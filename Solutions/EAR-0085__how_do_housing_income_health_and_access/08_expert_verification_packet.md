# Expert verification packet - the Tether on who bears climate impacts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a climate-vulnerability or equity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No per-slot outcome measurement is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Boundary.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on. The figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Who bears climate impacts is a per-slot distribution that must be read per household or group and never averaged into a regional mean. Housing, income, health and access to services are best treated as an enumerated habitat of conditions and relations whose valence is relative to the specific hazard and outcome, not as ingredients in a single vulnerability score. They overlap as a union whose intersections are categorically different cases, so a single lever clears only the single-condition regions. Material harm often arrives through cascades several steps out from direct contact, and the per-slot signal is lost when readings are pooled. The binding condition in a named place is not claimed; a source-discriminating instrument is owed. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and methodological; checkable by reading Figures 1, 10 and 11

- **Statement:** a distributional answer must remain per slot and habitat-enumerated, never an average and never a composed index.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings."* Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **Refuter:** an aggregate statistic that predicts prospectively and per named household who will bear the next shock with no per-slot reading; or a factor whose measured valence is invariant across hazards and outcomes; or a demonstration that the four factors compose into an index that retains the arrangement information an index normally loses.

### C2 - world-facing; checkable against vulnerability and equity literature and data

- **Statement:** the four conditions overlap as a union, the intersections are categorically different cases, and material harm arrives through cascades beyond first-order contact.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 11 :52-53 - the DDT cascade, *"first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 6 states *"Intersections between these drivers shape unique experiences of vulnerability and risk and the adaptive capacities of groups and individuals"* and gives the cascading-blackout example. Is the union reading faithful to what the field observes? Does a first-order exposure map miss slots whose harm came through service interruption?
- **Refuter:** a single intervention that reduces harm equally in all four-factor intersections with no categorically different residue; or a documented set of events in which all material harm is first-order and no cascade matters.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a world-facing check

- **Statement:** the measurement channel decays while the harm does not, and duration is a chain of handoffs with two factors that can each go to zero.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 10 :28 - *"you have lost touch."* Fig 7 :37-39 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* , *"the bracket’s fidelity across the handoff"* , *"the ground’s pressure on the room"*.
- **What to check:** do loss datasets that pool by region or count only insured and official losses systematically miss the same households a per-slot series would catch? Does recovery duration track the two factors?
- **Refuter:** a full-distribution measurement with no pooling or saturation across a major event whose verdict stands against the ground; or a case where harm duration stays high while both factors measure high and non-declining.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that housing, income, health or service access is the binding cause in any named place. That is a declared `gap`: the framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that a vulnerability index is invalid. It is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that this is settled by agreement. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- Not that the two IPCC sources are independent witnesses. They share one assessment evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot outcome measurement was taken in this run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a result *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the analogue; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

1. A per-slot outcome series that distinguishes tenure, income, health status and service reach inside the same hazard footprint, with no regional pooling.
2. A natural experiment changing one condition while the others are held, read per slot before and after.
3. A cascade trace from service interruption through to health and income outcomes.
4. A recovery series measuring bracket fidelity and ground pressure at each handoff.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 9 :55-57, Fig 10 :24 and :26, Fig 11 :43 and :49-51, Fig 7 :37-39. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own data or review: are the union and cascade readings faithful, or does one condition dominate?
3. Name one instrument or design that would discriminate which condition produced a named slot's harm, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record the verdict before reading any framework material.
