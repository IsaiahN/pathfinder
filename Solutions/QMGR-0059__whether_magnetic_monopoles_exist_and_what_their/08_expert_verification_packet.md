**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the inference, `general` on the epistemic rule, `gap` on physical existence.** No measurement at any scale is claimed to settle existence here.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The figures cannot decide whether an isolated magnetic charge exists; that is a ground question and the slot stays `???`. They can state the shape of any answer: a null search is a bound, not a refutation; GUT-scale monopoles are unreachable at the current collider budget, which calls for instruments rather than a new theory; and a confirmed detection with quantized charge would be an atom crossing from the ground into the GUT frame that predicted a heavy stable monopole, owing a new term to the physics library without adding a seventh substrate primitive. Charge quantization narrows the shape but does not prove it. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

- **Statement:** current searches set mass, flux and cross-section limits and report candidate events that do not resolve; they do not establish non-existence. GUT-scale monopoles near 10^16 GeV are beyond these colliders, so the case is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that monopoles exist, and not that they do not. Existence is `gap`.
- Not that charge quantization proves monopoles. It is consistent only.
- Not that null results refute grand unification. They bound the parameter space.
- Not which GUT is correct; the source record says there is no generally accepted GUT model.
- Not that a monopole is a new primitive, nor that electric and magnetic charge are Fig 12 isomers under the figure's own bond definition. That reading is strained and named in `06`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement.

1. **Detection test:** a detector event with quantized magnetic charge, with detector, date and charge quantum recorded. This closes existence and confirms the predicting frame.
2. **Bound test:** improved limits reaching the canonical GUT-monopole region. This pressures that prediction without proving absence.
3. **Charge test:** does the measured charge satisfy the Dirac condition relative to the elementary electric charge? If not, the shape fails.
