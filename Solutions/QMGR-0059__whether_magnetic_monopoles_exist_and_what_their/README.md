## Restatement as an effect

- Option A: an isolated magnetic charge exists. This owes a new term to the physics library, makes the observed electric charge quantization a consequence rather than a coincidence, and would be a ground-imported atom that confirms the GUT frame whose closure predicted it.
- Option B: no isolated magnetic charge exists. Charge quantization still needs an account, the GUT monopole prediction is either wrong or diluted beyond reach, and the null searches set bounds rather than proof of absence.
- What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a ground reading (a detector event with a quantized magnetic charge) that settles the existence slot, plus a recorded inference about which predicting frame that reading confirms.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a monopole claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for agreement produces a mirror, not a check.
2. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Physicists who read the same reviews are one pool talking.
3. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the framework's interiors before giving its verdict.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in and submission routes in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
