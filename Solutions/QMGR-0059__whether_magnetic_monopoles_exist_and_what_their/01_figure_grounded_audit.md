## 0. The residual in effect terms

R = is there an isolated magnetic charge in nature, and what would a yes or no settle about grand unification?

- **Option A, yes.** A source with nonzero divergence of B exists. A new term is owed to the physics library, electric charge quantization gains a reason, and the GUT frame whose closure predicted such an object is triangulated with the ground.
- **Option B, no.** No such source is available at any achieved resolution. Charge quantization still needs an account, the GUT monopole prediction must be revised or diluted, and the null searches set bounds, not proof of absence.
- **What an answer would look like, bound before the run (Formula "Direction"):** a detector reading carrying quantized magnetic charge, with provenance, plus a recorded inference naming which predicting frame that reading confirms. Until such a reading arrives, the existence slot stays open. This binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

The two halves of the question are not one hard problem. Existence is a ground question; implication is an inference question that depends on it. ## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- *Arity:* one candidate entity type (magnetic charge), but two coupled conditions: (1) existence in nature, (2) consequence for GUTs. The second consumes the first.
- *Symmetry:* electric and magnetic charge are related by a duality rotation of Maxwell's equations, yet the observed world breaks the symmetry, since every observed particle has the same magnetic-to-electric charge ratio (zero). The monopole is the entity that would restore the missing pole. This is a near-symmetry with a broken ground reading, not a symmetric join of two equals.
- *Scale:* quantum (Dirac quantization, 1931), field theoretic and topological (the 't Hooft-Polyakov soliton, GUT scale near 10^16 GeV), cosmological (relic monopole abundance, inflationary dilution), and experimental (collider and cosmic-ray flux and mass bounds).

**One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor.** A domain with no nameable anchor is not yet a domain.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1, theoretical and quantum frame | Dirac condition, Maxwell equations, compact U(1), Dirac string, topological soliton | internal consistency and the quantization prediction | observed electric charge quantization (a ground datum; constrains the shape but does not settle existence) |
| D2, experimental and search frame | MoEDAL trapping volumes and SQUID scans, IceCube and other cosmic arrays, earlier MACRO and accelerator searches | events, flux limits, mass limits, cross-section bounds | the detector reading itself (the ground for this question) |
| D3, cosmological frame | GUT phase transition, relic monopole abundance, inflation | predicted relic density and its dilution | cosmological observation, including the absence of monopole domination |
| D4, verification and outreach frame | expert readers | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement |

**Partition or union.** D1, D2 and D3 are separate closures with different actors and currencies, so they form a partition of frames. But they share one ground anchor (experiment and observation), so their agreement about the ground is one evidence pool, not three witnesses (Fig 2 :40). The question's two halves form a union compound: existence (D2) is upstream of implication (D1 and D3 read back through D2).

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** D1 sets the shape of magnetic charge and its quantization, then D3 derives the cosmological consequence, then D2 is the link that can settle existence, then the implication is inferred back into D1 and D3. The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance, and Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." More theory does not substitute for a detector reading.

Strain test applied: a line that only fits a domain by forcing it was sent back to the census, and the two such cases are recorded openly in §6.

## 3. Findings

### F1. Existence is a ground question, and the figures cannot close it (triangulated, `gap` on the content)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

A monopole's existence is not derivable from the closure of the GUT frame, from Dirac's argument, or from the six-term substrate. It is exactly the kind of question the anchor settles. The honest output of the figures on the existence slot is therefore `gap` / ground-undecided, not a guess and not silence. Three low-overlap figure lines (anchor definition, import boundary, unreachable-is-a-verdict) carry the same instruction, so the epistemic rule is `triangulated`; the content verdict is `gap` because no figure line governs physical existence.

World corroboration (secondary): Wikipedia's "Magnetic monopole" states "there is no known experimental or observational evidence that magnetic monopoles exist" and "whether monopoles exist remains an open question." This is the same shape, from a different domain.

**Refuter:** show a figure line that settles whether an entity exists in nature from closure alone, with no ground contact. If one exists, the `gap` verdict fails.

### F2. A null search bounds; it does not refute (triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

MoEDAL's published result is exactly this shape: "MMs with Dirac charges 1 g_D ≤ g ≤ 3 g_D and masses up to 75 GeV/c² were excluded by the analysis" and the result "significantly extends previous mass bounds." The 2017 MoEDAL search "did not find any magnetic monopoles, setting new limits on their production cross section." A bound is a legitimate ground reading. A verdict of non-existence is not licensed by it.

**Refuter:** a proof that a finite search over a closed frame establishes absence, which would contradict Fig 8 :32, "no search, however long, adds an atom."

### F3. Unreachable at this budget is different from underivable in principle (triangulated)

Fig 8 :40-42: "the remedy is more search, not another atom."

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

GUT-scale monopoles are expected at masses near 10^16 GeV, far beyond colliders, so a collider search of this kind is `unreachable-at-this-budget` for the canonical GUT monopole. That issues the instruction "more search, better instruments, and cosmic reach," not "import a new theory." Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it." Does not fire here: monopoles have registered as predictions inside theory frames, and candidate events have registered at instruments and failed to resolve (Milton 2006: "occasional short-lived positive evidence for such exotic particles reported"). There is an edge to extend from.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that no instrument at any resolution could register magnetic charge would move the case from unreachable-at-budget to underivable-in-principle, and would require a different instruction.

### F4. A detection would be an atom crossing back, and would owe a new term (single-source on the physics binding, triangulated on the inference)

Fig 8 :46: "Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."

Fig 5 :14: "mechanism broken: a new term is owed"

If a detector returns a quantized magnetic charge, two things follow in the framework. First, the physics library owes a new term (magnetic charge and current densities in the extended Maxwell equations), gated by the three guards of Fig 5. Second, the atom crosses from the ground into the GUT frame whose closure already predicted a heavy stable monopole, so that frame is triangulated with the ground at maximum independence. This is the sense in which existence would imply something about grand unification: it would confirm a specific predicted closure, not create a new primitive.

Caution from Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them," so the provenance of the detection and the prior prediction must both be recorded.

**Refuter:** a detection with no prior prediction from any GUT closure would be an unmotivated import, and the "confirms the predicting frame" claim would fail. Also refuted if the new term cannot be built from the existing library (REACHABILITY guard fails).

### F5. A monopole is a new term, not a seventh substrate primitive (triangulated)

Fig 13 :116-117: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."

Electric charge and magnetic charge are charge assignments, not new states or structures among the six (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network). A monopole would be a new molecule in the physics library built from held atoms, not a seventh member of the substrate. This matters because claiming a new primitive would break the closure argument and is not supported by the figure.

**Refuter:** derive magnetic charge as an irreducible seventh member, or derive one of the six without it. Either would break the claim.

### F6. Charge quantization constrains the shape but does not settle existence (triangulated on the rule, single-source on the physics)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Dirac showed that if any monopole exists, all electric charge is quantized; electric charge is in fact quantized, "which is consistent with (but does not prove) the existence of monopoles." The quantization datum is a ground fact that narrows the shape of any monopole (its charge must be quantized inversely to the elementary electric charge), but it cannot close the existence slot. Treating quantization as decisive would be reading a mirror as an anchor.

**Refuter:** a derivation in which electric charge quantization entails the existence of a magnetic monopole, with no ground reading. None of the sources read supplies one.

### F7. The broken link is the measurement, not the theory (triangulated)

Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

The theoretical chain from Dirac through 't Hooft and Polyakov to GUT monopoles is settled internally and keeps producing consistent predictions. The link that does not yet return a resolving reading is the experimental one. Effort spent above that link (more theoretical unification arguments) arrives at a step that cannot use it. Fig 3 :22-23: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."

**Refuter:** an experiment already returning a resolved monopole signal, which would move the break and invalidate this diagnosis.

S is refuted if any of the following holds:

5. Magnetic charge is shown to be an irreducible seventh substrate member (breaks F5).
6. Electric charge quantization is proved to entail monopole existence with no ground reading (breaks F6).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of the existence verdict may be labelled `grounded`.
