**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** send only after the stage-1 output below survives the reader's own check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the output does not hold up.

**Subject:** Check one claim about monopole searches before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (in effect terms):** does an isolated magnetic charge exist, and what would a yes settle about grand unification? The two halves are chained. Existence is a question for a detector; the implication for grand unification is an inference that depends on the detector reading.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the inference, `gap` on existence):** the framework cannot settle whether a monopole exists. It can only say what a closing reading would look like. Three points survive a check:
>
> 1. A null search is a bound, not a refutation. Nothing in a finite search over a non-sealed frame proves absence. A mass or flux limit is a legitimate result, and it is not a verdict of non-existence.
> 2. GUT-scale monopoles are unreachable at the current collider budget, not underivable in principle. That distinction matters: the remedy for a budget limit is more search and better instruments, not a new theory.
> 3. If a monopole is ever detected with the predicted quantized charge, the physics library owes a new term, and the GUT frame that predicted a heavy stable monopole would be confirmed by contact with the ground. That is a `specified` inference, not a measurement.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that monopoles exist, that they do not, that electric charge quantization proves them (it is consistent with them only), that current null results refute grand unification, or that a monopole would be a new fundamental primitive. The framework returns a shape and a reach, not a name and not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message was sent:**
> 1. a figure rule that settles physical existence from internal consistency alone, with no ground contact;
> 2. a proof that a finite null search establishes absence;
> 3. a demonstration that no instrument at any resolution could ever register magnetic charge;
> 4. a confirmed detection with no prior prediction from any GUT closure;
> 5. a derivation of magnetic charge as an irreducible seventh substrate primitive.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - MoEDAL Schwinger-mechanism search, Nature 602 (2022) 63: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11933
> - Milton, "Theoretical and Experimental Status of Magnetic Monopoles," Rep. Prog. Phys. 69 (2006) 1637: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0602040
>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
