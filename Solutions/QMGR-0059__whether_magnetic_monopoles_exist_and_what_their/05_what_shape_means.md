## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (read 2026-11-23): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: an isolated magnetic charge with quantized magnetic charge, such that its detection would confirm the GUT closure that predicted it, and such that null results are bounds.
- Not a shape, but a ground reading: "a monopole of mass X and Dirac charge g was observed on this date by this detector."
- Not a name: "the monopole is the X boson of SU(5)." The framework does not select a GUT model. The world source "Grand Unified Theory" says plainly "there is no generally accepted GUT model."

**The existence slot stays `???`.** There is no monopole detection in any source read here.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described in effect terms (a ground question chained to an inference question) before the GUT and experiment frames were named.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So the residual is stated as an effect: what would a detector have to return, and what would that confirm.
- From *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, note recurring terms that overlap with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The terms gained here were quantization, soliton, GUT closure, flux bound, mass bound, duality.
- From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A null detector reading and a candidate event are projections; neither determines the source without a discriminating instrument. The essay's own resolution: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." This is exactly the F3 distinction between unreachable-at-budget and underivable-in-principle.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A detector reading with quantized magnetic charge would settle existence.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and shadow test. A GUT prediction is a derivation inside the theory frame; it becomes knowledge about nature only when the ground pays.

The metamerism warning applies to candidate events. The 1975 and 1982 events, and the "occasional short-lived positive evidence" reported by Milton (2006), were readings whose source could not be determined. Calling them monopoles would be naming a source from a projection.

## 4. Why `gap` is the honest stop, not a failure

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A search that returns bounds has not settled existence. The framework can report that it lacks a ground reading without inventing one. That is the stop.

The eight-essay corpus gives the same discipline in a different vocabulary. *The Library of Babel*: "If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed." Here the question is neither; it is answerable by a ground we do not yet hold.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

A list of famous physicists who believe in monopoles gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether monopoles exist. The same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So this audit cannot validate its own shape claim; that requires a checker outside the frame, and in the last resort a detector.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a simulation of a derivation. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether monopoles exist and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to §1 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name the monopole" as if naming were forbidden outright. That overstates the rule. The canonical README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the missing thing is not a primitive at all: it is a ground reading. The correct statement is narrower. The framework cannot supply the reading; it can state the shape the reading must have. This correction does not change the `gap` verdict; it changes the reason, from "forbidden to name" to "no ground contact available."
