## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about comparability between two teams using different instruments and assumptions. Option A: make the teams agree on one protocol and one number, then call the readings comparable. Option B: keep the teams independent, range each separately against a reference that does not update, author a convention that makes the disagreement statable without deciding it, measure the round-trip gap, and carry the lineage with the number. A leads to a convention that has decided an outcome and to agreement that cannot corroborate itself. B leads to a checkable shape with written refuters and an open standard until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unlicensed substitution between two readings. Arity: four places, team A, team B, an anchor and a transform. Symmetry: swapping A and B must not change the verdict if the frames are independent and ranged against the same anchor; if it does, one frame has been privileged. Scale: within-team prediction against observation, and cross-team round trip. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, each team's instrument frame.** Actors: instrument, operator, local library Gamma_A, prediction. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: none inside the team, so this is a channel, not yet a domain; "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces" (Fig 1 :27).
- **Domain 2, the anchor or ground.** Actors: the reference, a measurement, experiment, counter, outcome or invariant of nature. Currency: the value returned. Anchor: itself, because "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor" (Fig 2 :30). This is the only domain with a nameable anchor.
- **Domain 3, the seam and convention.** Actors: units, protocols, common items, the ground-maintainer seat. Currency: shared meaning, not a verdict about the world. Anchor: the ground, which the convention makes statable but does not settle.
- **Domain 4, comparison and lineage.** Actors: the two readings, the transform and the record of each route. Currency: provenance. Anchor: the ground plus the record. This is where substitutability is licensed or refused.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Four conditions bundle: same referent, same ordering and unit, independent evidence, recorded route. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Order by dependency.** Instrument contact, convention, objective, anchor registration, transform and round trip (Fig 3 :20). Effort spent above a missing anchor cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test: common reference items look like overlap, but the item represents the anchor, not a frame's evidence. Fig 2 :46 permits the anchor to be shared while forbidding frames to move it. No strain after re-census. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a named universal standard does not. Comparability is a five-part arrangement: a reference neither team can move with each frame corrected separately; independence between teams' evidence until both verdicts are recorded; the smallest convention that makes the disagreement statable without deciding the value; a round trip with R_T reported; and a lineage that travels with the number. The question is a union of four conditions and must be unbundled before search. Tolerances and protocols are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict. Specific readings that failed: agreement as corroboration, a protocol that decides the outcome, equal numbers as identical measurements, and the attempt to name a standard from the figures. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a finished protocol, instead of a generator, a shape plus its refuter and round-trip method. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The failed readings are removable without touching the derivation: `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes the two readings would name the source; a round-trip test with zero gap across all distinguishable conditions would remove the R_T requirement; a reference that moves when pushed would fail the anchor test; a convention that decides an outcome and still detects disagreement would clear the Fig 10 failure; a derivation of a specific universal standard with source gate and shadow test would exceed the shape-not-a-name limit in a licensed way. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
