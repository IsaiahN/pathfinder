## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before relying on the primary's conclusions: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The unlicensed substitution between two readings. Arity four: team A, team B, anchor, transform. A two-place "do the numbers agree" framing already drops the anchor and the transform. Symmetry: swapping A and B must not change the verdict if both draw on disjoint evidence and range against the same anchor; if it does, one frame has been privileged. Scale: within-team prediction against observation, and cross-team round trip.
- **Domain 1, instrument frame.** Actors: instrument, operator, local library, prediction. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: none inside the frame. Not a checking domain yet, only a channel, because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.
- **Domain 2, anchor.** Actors: reference, measurement or invariant. Currency: the value returned. Anchor: itself, because it must not update on opinion. This is the only domain with a nameable anchor.
- **Domain 3, seam and convention.** Actors: units, protocol, common items, maintainer seat. Currency: shared meaning. Anchor: the ground, made statable but not decided by the convention.
- **Domain 4, comparison and lineage.** Actors: readings, transform, record of routes. Currency: provenance. Anchor: the ground plus the record.
- **Partition or union.** Union of four conditions, not a single hard question. A lever that fixes units leaves the referent, independence and lineage conditions standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Instrument contact, convention, objective, anchor registration, transform and round trip. Optimising the transform cannot rescue a missing anchor.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. I reached it by a different starting route: I began from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 1's per-slot rule, whereas the primary began from Fig 2's anchor rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries, low overlap within the framework. The agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round-trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :31, :35, :46 (anchor and independence), Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 10 :16, :47 (convention), Fig 12 :94-96 (record) and Fig 9 :60 (filters).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: non-updating anchor, disjoint evidence, convention that states but does not decide, measured R_T, lineage with the number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete regimen, for example a key comparison with a common transfer standard, a published degree of equivalence, a preregistered tolerance and a calibration chain recorded with each result.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- whether the common transfer standard is the anchor's representative or has quietly become the decision (Fig 10 :47);
- whether the two laboratories' evidence is truly disjoint or they share a calibration pool (Fig 2 :40);
- the actual size of the round-trip gap, which the shorthand names but cannot supply (Fig 4 :30-32);
- whether equal degrees of equivalence carry the same lineage and meaning, or whether the same number masks a different source (Fig 12 :95-96).

One thing survives cleanly: separate correction against a reference neither frame can move, with independence between the frames' evidence. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no real pair of teams was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
