# The Tether's answer on cross-team measurement comparability

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two teams, different instruments, different assumptions, each returns a number for what each claims is the same quantity. The wanted service is substitutability. The residual is the failure of that substitution to be licensed, indexed per measurand, time and scale. It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: same referent, same ordering and unit, independent evidence, and a recorded route are four conditions, and a lever that clears one leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-58). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **A reference that neither team can move.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :46: "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it." If the reference moves when a team pushes on it, it is not an anchor. Agreement between teams is not the anchor.

2. **Difference of instrument is the independence.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Keep the teams' evidence and verdicts disjoint until both stage-1 verdicts are recorded. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

3. **A convention that makes the disagreement statable, stopping before it decides.** Fig 10 :16-17: "the convention does not fix the bug it makes the bug statable". Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Agreement about a shared meaning is the product of a convention; agreement about the world is a different thing and must be settled by the ground (Fig 2 :40-41).

4. **A round trip with a measured gap.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 4 :31-32: "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." Carry a common item through both pipelines and back; report R_T as a result.

5. **The lineage travels with the number.** Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :95-96: "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond." Fig 13 :129: "It is recoverable from the lineage." Two equal numbers can be different substances.

6. **Describe the effect before choosing the standard.** Fig 9 :26-28: "R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ* describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Write down what decision the number must support and what would count as a failed substitution, then pick the reference.

7. **Protocols are filters, never witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one universal protocol, reference material or tolerance. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a claim that more teams, more reviews or more agreement establish comparability. Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."
- Not a claim that two equal numbers are the same measurement, or that equal scores under one label are comparable. Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance."
- Not a claim that a protocol or preregistered tolerance settles which value is correct. That is a convention that has decided an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,phi)` for any real pair of teams.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a recorded round trip, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. The corresponding questions here:

- **Discriminator test:** can an instrument distinguish an ordering (spectrum-like) from a directedness (gradient-like) in the two readings? If two teams report the same label but one ordering and the other directedness, they are different substances (Fig 13 :35-36).
- **Round-trip test:** send a common item through A, across to B and back. Report R_T. A zero gap across all distinguishable conditions would remove the need for the round-trip requirement.
- **Anchor test:** push on the reference. If it moves when a team pushes, it is not an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Substitution test:** put each reading into the downstream decision and look for a difference. "Substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No." is the identity test from the Operators table, and the burden is on showing nothing is lost.
- Standing refuters from `01` §4: a team self-scoring with a quantity it also produces; disjoint-evidence agreement that diverges under a ground-resolved perturbation; a convention that decides an outcome and still detects disagreement; identical readings with no provenance-bearing difference.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring a unit system makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a universal standard for cross-team comparability. It gives the shape any licensed substitution must have. Two instruments pointed at the same thing are the independence, not the defect, so keep the teams' evidence disjoint until both verdicts are recorded and range each separately against a reference neither can move. Author the smallest convention that makes the disagreement statable, and stop before it decides which value is correct. Carry a common item through both pipelines and back and report the round-trip gap rather than hiding it. Ship each number with its lineage, because equal values reached by different routes can be different measurements. Protocols and tolerances are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict. What the framework returns is this shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The specific reference is left open until the ground settles it.
