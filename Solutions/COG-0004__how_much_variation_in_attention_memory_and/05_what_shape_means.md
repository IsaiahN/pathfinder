## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the form an answer must have, stateable before you can name its content. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: three separate per-slot residuals, a union that must be unbundled before search, an agent-and-habitat pair rather than two buckets, a per-frame weighting that is learnable, and a number that is owed to a ground rather than supplied by the framework. A headline percentage is a number, not a shape, and it would fill the slot before the instrument exists.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." And the explicit instruction for this question: a problem that is "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one" must have each part searched individually.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what a recipient would expect to see per slot before showing any source.

## 3. What would license a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question the ground is a per-slot measurement with a stated sampling frame, instrument and date.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A cross-cultural experiment is such a frame relative to a twin study, but only if the two do not draw on the same evidence pool.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A "culture gene" would be a new term in the existing variance-decomposition space, not a new dimension, so it would not widen what can be perceived. It would also fail the source gate unless it explained a gap.

## 4. Why a single percentage is the specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB or CMYK can all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the source.

A measured difference in attention or recall is likewise a reading. Both a learned history and an inherited prior can produce it. The reading alone does not name its source. The question asks for the source share from the reading, which is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a coefficient.

*How a Society of Agents* makes the same point in the exact grammar of this question: "None of this gives one number for 'how much smarter.' It gives the dials: how different the members are, how much of the problem must be done in sequence, how costly it is to stay in sync, and how many generations the group keeps building." Replace "smarter" with "cultural" and the sentence is the answer's form: dials per slot, not one share.

## 5. What actually licenses a particular split

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular coefficient is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the coefficient is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The world sources are explicit that the number is not stable across instruments: classical designs give roughly 0.4 to 0.8, reviews about 0.5, within-family molecular methods roughly 0.10 to 0.20, and Trahan et al. 2014 say a working magnitude and measurement error are not well established. That is method-dependence, which is a source-discrimination problem, not a precision problem.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on any concrete number. The README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like". The subject is a primitive not held in the library. A heritability coefficient is not a missing primitive; it is a measured statistic from a different frame. So a number is not forbidden here. What is forbidden is presenting one as figure-derived, or pooling method-dependent estimates into a verdict. Both my earlier claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost:

- **Retained:** the reading cannot name the source of a projection. Metamerism stands.
- **Narrowed:** a number is not banned; it is owed to a ground, and the figures do not supply it.

This matches exemplar QMGR-0037's correction, where naming a held atom was found to be a binding rather than a prohibited invention. The parallel here is that a measured coefficient is a binding from the statistics frame, provenanced as such.

## 6. Three consequences for this question

**Culture is a back drop, not a variable to subtract.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge." It also reports Bartlett's result that each participant's retelling "mirrored his or her own culture". That is culture shaping the reading of memory itself, which is why a clean partition into cultural and biological inputs is suspect: the measuring act is inside the habitat.

**Inherited and taught are both head starts.** *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it." This is the essay's own answer to the nature/culture framing: both channels deliver a head start, and the cost was paid somewhere. Fig 4 :22 says "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Concretely, the transferable thing is a method, not a recording of the ancestor's answer.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action." Gathering famous names to agree with a percentage would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to let the claim be tried and let per-slot measurements settle it.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The Arrival of the Fittest article adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula", and says LLM simulations "must be seen as simulacra". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
