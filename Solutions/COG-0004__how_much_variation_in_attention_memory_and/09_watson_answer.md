## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request for one number apportioning variation in attention, memory and reasoning between cultural learning and biological differences. Option A: the headline share is well formed and the figures constrain it. Option B: the question bundles a union of capacities, the measured quantity is indexed per slot and never aggregated, and the two named sources are an agent-and-habitat pair whose interaction is not a separable remainder. Option A leads to a pooled percentage that hides a live slot and to two sources priced in different currencies. Option B leads to three separate per-slot verdicts, a shape, and an instrument programme.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (the residual).** The request for a source share. Arity: two or more related terms (a capacity, a learning history, an inherited prior, a habitat). Symmetry: ordered, since swapping culture and biology changes the claim. Scale: a within-population variance decomposition mixed with a generational transmission scale. Fig 9 :22 calls for arity, symmetry and scale before framing.
- **Domain D1, the measured capacity slots.** Actors: tasks and stimuli for attention, memory and reasoning. Currency: per-slot prediction error. Anchor: the task measurement. Without a task there is no slot.
- **Domain D2, the transmission frame.** Actors: learner, teachers, peers, artefacts, parents. Currency: transmitted practice with a per-frame weighting `w`. Anchor: a provenance record plus an outcome that shows whether the head start paid.
- **Domain D3, the biological frame.** Actors: genotypes, twins, adoptees, polygenic scores. Currency: covariance with genetic similarity. Anchor: registered relative outcomes, not agreement between studies.
- **Domain D4, the habitat.** Actors: conditions, institutions, language, tools, curricula, nutrition, peers. Currency: contact effects and cascades. Anchor: an intervention that changes contact with a before and after reading.
- **Domain D5, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers and the studies they check. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all five domains draw on one evidence pool, so D5 independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** The three capacities are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition of one trait; the biology/culture relation is a further compound because agent and habitat are in contact. The intersection gets its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 slot reading first, then D2 and D3 in parallel each ranged against its own ground, then D4 habitat enumeration, then D5 verification. Describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).

Strain test applied: an early reading took "inherited prior" to mean genes and tried to map `w` to heritability. Re-census showed `w` governs belief formation per frame and covers taught priors too, so the line governs a different quantity. No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures refuse the single aggregate percentage and supply the shape instead. `R` is per slot, so keep attention, memory and reasoning separate; they are a union of overlapping conditions and members of one functional group, not isomers. Biology and culture are an agent-and-habitat pair whose interaction is the substance, not a separable remainder. The nearest figure term, `w`, is a learnable per-frame weighting that permits identical architectures to diverge, which supports substantial cultural variation without a biological difference, but it is not a heritability coefficient. The numeric apportionment is `gap`: unreachable with the instruments held here, not zero and not silenced. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the falsifiable predictions, not `grounded` on the number.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a percentage instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The headline number was never asserted, so nothing must be withdrawn to correct it; a source-discriminating measurement can be added as a new binding from the statistics frame without touching the shape. A sent shape can be superseded if the standing falsifiers fire.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates the sources of the same slot reading, two single-source interventions with additive effects and no interaction, a figure line licensing a global apportionment, or evidence that `w` is fixed and genetics-only would reverse the relevant part of the verdict. Until one of those fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
