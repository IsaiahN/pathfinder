## 0. The residual, in effect terms

Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before choosing any figure line, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual.

**Question as posed:** produce one number, the proportion of variation in attention, memory and reasoning attributable to cultural learning rather than biological differences.

**What an answer would look like (bound term):** a partition of observed variance into at least two named sources, identical across the three capacities enough to be reported as one headline figure, with each source separately identifiable as "cultural" or "biological" and the remainder assignable to measurement error.

**Two readings and what each leads to:**

- **Option A.** The headline number is well defined and the figures can constrain it. This leads to a single reported percentage, a pooled verdict across three capacities, and a claim that a two-source partition is causally identifiable.
- **Option B.** The headline number is an aggregation that discards the per-slot signal; the three capacities are a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled; and "cultural" and "biological" name an agent-and-habitat pair whose interaction is the substance, not a leftover. This leads to three separate per-slot verdicts, a shape, and a list of instruments that could settle each slot.

The task of the census is to decide which reading the figures license, before any world source is consulted.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Two or more things are related in each claim: a capacity and its source; a learning history and an inherited prior; an agent and a habitat. One field with two readers is a seam (Fig 10 :13). This is not a one-term quantity.
- **Symmetry.** The two named sources are not interchangeable. Culture is transmitted horizontally, obliquely and vertically on a different timescale from genes (world source: Animal culture, section on transmission modes). Swapping them changes the claim, so the relation is ordered, not symmetric.
- **Scale.** The question mixes at least two scales: a within-population variance decomposition at the current population scale, and a generational transmission scale on which culture changes faster than genes. A head start crosses the membrane; a recording does not (Fig 4 :22).

### 1b. One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

A domain needs a nameable anchor. Where none can be named, it is not yet a domain.

- **Domain D1, the measured capacity slots.** Actors: the tasks and stimuli by which attention, memory and reasoning are read (cueing paradigms, recall tasks, reasoning batteries). Currency: per-slot prediction error, `R` indexed by object slot. Anchor: the task measurement, the only metric (Fig 1 :23). Without a task there is no slot and no residual.
- **Domain D2, the transmission frame.** Actors: learner, teachers, peers, artefacts, parents. Currency: what is learned and transmitted, with a per-frame weighting `w` between private history and inherited prior. Anchor: a provenance record of who learned what from whom, plus an outcome that shows whether the head start paid. The symbols table names `w` and says it is learnable from outcomes; that is a checkable anchor, so this is a domain.
- **Domain D3, the biological frame.** Actors: genotypes, alleles, polygenic scores, twins and adoptees. Currency: covariance between genetic similarity and phenotypic similarity. Anchor: a measurement or a registration of relatives' outcomes, not agreement between studies.
- **Domain D4, the habitat.** Actors: conditions, institutions, languages, tools, curricula, nutrition, peers. Currency: contact effects and cascades. Anchor: an intervention that changes contact and a before/after reading. `capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone` (Fig 11 :39).
- **Domain D5, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers and the studies they check. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all five domains draw on one evidence pool, so D5 independence is zero.

### 1c. Partition or union

The three named capacities are **not a partition of one trait**. They overlap: attention selection affects what is encoded into memory; working memory supports reasoning. A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). They are a **union of overlapping conditions**, with a compound at each intersection that needs its own loop. `And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again` (Fig 9 :54). The biology/culture relation is a further compound: the organism and its habitat are in contact, so the interaction is not a third independent bucket but the relation itself (Fig 12 :5).

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

`The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance` (Fig 3 :20).

1. Settle D1 first: is there a per-slot reading at all? A reading below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).
2. Then D2 and D3 in parallel, each ranged against its own ground separately (Fig 2 :45). Neither may update on the other's answer.
3. Then D4: enumerate the habitat by contact, never compose it (Fig 11 :43).
4. D5 last: a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42).

**Ordering of the figure search:** describe R first, frame second (Fig 8 :53). No frame was named from the world literature until this census was written.

## 3. Findings, with verbatim lines and refuters

### F1. The single headline number is the aggregation the figures forbid (decisive for the question's form)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Fig 1 :28: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."

The Symbols table defines `s` as one object, region or entity being tracked and says `R` is measured per slot, never as a single global number. The question asks for a global number across three capacities and across a population. That is exactly the operation Fig 1 warns destroys the signal. A reported "60 percent cultural, 40 percent biological" would be an average over slots, and a live slot, for example an attention effect with a strong cultural loading and a memory effect with none, would vanish into it.

**Mode:** `general` (structural), triangulated across Fig 1 and the Symbols table, low overlap because one is the agent loop and the other the notation sheet.

**Refuter:** show a figure line that licenses one global `R` over all slots for this question, or a measurement in which the per-slot residual and the pooled residual carry the same information about where to look. If either holds, F1 fails.

### F2. Three capacities are a union, not one variable

Fig 9 :4-5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The Library of Babel essay states the same instruction in effect terms: if a problem is a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one, search each individually and then combine. The cultural-variation literature corroborates the overlap: cultural differences are documented specifically in attention, perception and cognition as separate findings, and reasoning style varies across populations. None of that licenses adding the three into one score. Verdicts stay per capacity and are never averaged.

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 9 and the Library of Babel essay (different evidence pools, one framework and one literary analogue).

**Refuter:** exhibit a single instrument whose reading over attention, memory and reasoning is invariant to which of the three is measured, and that instrument has the same ground for all three. Without it, the union reading stands.

### F3. Biology and culture are an agent-and-habitat pair, not two buckets

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 11 :41: "The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not."

Fig 11 :61-62: "You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."

Fig 12 :5: "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

The question is framed as an apportionment, which presupposes that the two sources are separable operands whose contributions add. The figures say the capability lives in the contact between them, that the habitat cannot be subtracted, and that the bond carries information the operands do not. Assigning a share to the organism alone is the same error as assigning lift to the bird alone. `Fig 4 :19-20` gives the general rule: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The world literature agrees from outside the framework: the nature-versus-nurture entry says a strong dichotomy is generally considered to have limited relevance and that both contribute in an inextricable manner, and Hebb answered the "which contributes more" question with the area of a rectangle, its length or its width.

**Mode:** `general` (structural), triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 12 (habitat and bond figures have low overlap), with world corroboration from nature-versus-nurture and gene-environment correlation.

**Refuter:** exhibit an intervention that changes only the biological term while holding the habitat's contact exactly fixed, and a second that changes only the habitat while holding biology fixed, with effects that add to the observed variance without an interaction term. If both exist, the bucket model is viable and F3 fails. Gene-environment correlation predicts the refuter will fail, but that is a world prediction, not a figure verdict.

### F4. The framework does have a per-frame learning/inheritance weighting, and it is not a heritability estimate

Symbols table :20-21: the belief is "Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w."

Symbols table :23-25: "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."

This is the closest the figures come to the question's own vocabulary. It says divergence does not require different architecture: identical architecture plus different history and different `w` produces divergence. That is figure support for substantial cultural variation with no biological difference. But `w` is a per-frame parameter inside belief formation, not a population variance component, and "inherited prior" is not restricted to genes. How Intelligence Actually Works says the mapping "It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it." So `w` cannot be read as a percentage of genetic causation. Note also that `w_A + w_B = 1` is a normalisation inside one frame, not an empirical finding about human populations.

**Mode:** `specified` on the claim that identical architecture can diverge through history and weighting, `general` on the reading of `w`. Single-source for the notation (Symbols table), corroborated at low overlap by the essay sentence about inheritance and teaching.

**Refuter:** show `w` entering a population variance decomposition with a fixed cross-person value, or show that "inherited prior" in the symbols table can only mean genetic inheritance and cannot cover taught priors. Either removes this finding.

### F5. The framework returns a shape; the number is owed to a ground

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

Fig 13 :138-139: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." No figure line carries a population variance ratio. A search of all thirteen figures for "heritability", "percentage", "culture as a quantity" returns none that governs a ratio. After a genuine re-census (section 6), the honest report is `gap` on the number. `gap` is not an admission of failure: Fig 8 :39 forbids reading it as proof of absence, and Fig 13 :134 makes unreachability a verdict.

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 9 and Fig 8 (three figures with different offices: minimum set, leave/arrive, union surplus).

**Refuter:** produce a figure line that yields a numerical apportionment for a named population, with its sampling frame, instrument and date. Until one is produced, the numeric claim is `gap`.

### F6. Filters must not become witnesses

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not"

The heritability estimates in the world literature range from about 0.4 to 0.8 by twin and family design, around 0.5 in reviews, and roughly 0.10 to 0.20 by within-family molecular methods. The agreement among methodologically similar studies is one pool, not independent witnesses. Selecting a preferred percentage because many studies report it would let a filter hand us a verdict. The world sources are used below to check the shape, never to pick a number.

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 2 and Fig 7.

**Refuter:** show that two estimates drawn from disjoint instruments and disjoint populations converge on the same ratio and that the convergence is not explained by a shared model assumption. That would be corroboration at high independence and would reopen F5.

### F7. The 20-minute check claim survives, the headline number does not

Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

What can be sent to an outside frame is the shape: the residual is per slot, the capacities are a union, the sources are agent-and-habitat, the weighting is per frame and learned. What cannot be sent as a figure-derived result is a percentage. `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is built from the first list.

**Mode:** `specified` for the outreach rule, `general` for the shape it carries. Triangulated across Fig 10 and Fig 1.

**Refuter:** an outside expert who checks only the residual and shape returns a correct refutation that the figures do not license the per-slot rule. The refutation goes into `11_audit.md` with provenance and is re-evaluated by the auditor seat.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the Seat Map: `a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device`. Written before any verdict below was fixed.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line is produced that licenses a single global apportionment over slots for this question, with no per-slot remainder (breaks F1 and F2).
2. Capability is shown to be a property of the agent alone, separable from habitat contact, with an intervention that changes contact having no effect on the measured capacity (breaks F3, and contradicts Fig 11 :40).
3. `w` is shown to be fixed across frames and to refer only to genetic inheritance, so identical architecture cannot diverge through history (breaks F4, and contradicts the Symbols table :25).
4. A ground reading, not an agreement, returns a per-slot apportionment that is stable across instruments whose assumptions do not overlap (closes F5's `gap`; this would not refute the shape, it would settle the number and should be recorded as such).

If 1, 2 or 3 fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. Item 4 is a settlement condition, not a falsifier, and is kept separate so it cannot quietly become one.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Report the shape, not a percentage. State the mode on every claim.
2. Split the question into attention, memory and reasoning and give each its own loop, its own instrument and its own ground.
3. Replace "how much is culture versus biology" with per-slot questions about contact: what changes when the habitat changes, what changes when the inherited prior changes, and what is the interaction.
4. Use `w` as a per-frame hypothesis, not as a variance ratio. It is learnable from outcomes, so it can be tested.
5. Send the generator, not the conclusion. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Lead with the residual and its refuter; offer links only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
6. Keep the `gap` visible. Do not let the absence of a figure line be read as an answer of zero cultural variation or zero biological variation.

## 6. Re-census, strains and corrections

### 6a. Strain one, resolved: "inherited prior" as biology

An early reading took the Symbols table's "inherited prior" to mean biological inheritance and tried to map `w` to a heritability coefficient. **Retracted.** Re-census: `w` governs belief formation inside one frame; How Intelligence Actually Works says a mapping "It can be inherited or taught". The literal covers both channels. The line governs a per-frame weighting, not a population variance component. Different object, so no strain after re-census. Matches the canonical rule that a reading which makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

### 6b. Strain two, resolved: union versus two conditions

An early reading treated the three capacities as "conditions" in the Fig 9 sense and asked whether the biology/culture pair was the harder case. **Restrained and re-censed.** Fig 9 :57 governs two conditions binding one case. The three capacities are three slots with their own residuals; the biology/culture pair is an agent-and-habitat relation. Different objects, so no strain.

### 6c. Strain three, named and left open: two currencies

Fig 12 :125 says a composition has two currencies that do not add. The world literature prices heritability in bits of explained variance and cultural transmission in speed of spread and fidelity. Those are different units. This is not a defect in the derivation; it is the reason a single headline number is suspect. Recorded as a strain, not corrected.
