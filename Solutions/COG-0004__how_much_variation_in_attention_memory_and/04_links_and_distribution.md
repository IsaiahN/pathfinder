## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The number is method-dependent, not merely unknown.** Classical twin and family designs give roughly 0.4 to 0.8, reviews about 0.5, and within-family molecular methods roughly 0.10 to 0.20. Trahan et al. 2014 add that a working magnitude and measurement error "are not well established". That is why the shape claim refuses a headline number rather than choosing one. Use this to explain the refusal, not to nominate a favourite estimate.

2. **The two sources are entangled in the measurement.** Gene-environment correlation says environments are heritable because genotype influences the behaviours that evoke, select and modify them. Kong 2018 (cited in that page) reports that nurture has a genetic component. So a variance partition cannot be read as a causal separation of two independent inputs. This is world corroboration of Fig 11's agent-and-habitat line, not a replacement for it.

3. **Culture changes the reading, not just the amount.** Bartlett's retellings, reported in Decoding Tacit Knowledge, converged toward each listener's culture; cultural psychology documents differences in attention and reasoning style; the Flynn effect shows attention and memory scores rising over decades. These are per-slot, per-task effects. They support the union and per-slot shape and do not yield a single share.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone describes a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who absorbs the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see per slot before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in their own field and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a verdict on that alone.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the sources. Later agreement is never pooled as evidence (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives the recipient's check. If the refuter fires, stop and record the failure.
