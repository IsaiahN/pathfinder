# Expert verification packet: the Tether on culture versus biology in cognition

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cognitive scientist, behaviour geneticist or cross-cultural psychologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the falsifiable predictions, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the population scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A single headline number for the share of variation in attention, memory and reasoning attributable to cultural learning rather than biological differences is not a well-formed measurement target. The error signal is indexed per object slot and never aggregated, so a pooled share discards the live slot. The three capacities are a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before search, and they are members of one functional group rather than isomers of one substance. Biology and culture are an agent-and-habitat pair, not two independent buckets: capability belongs to the pair, the habitat cannot be subtracted, and the interaction carries information the two named sources do not. The framework's nearest term to the question is a per-frame weighting between private history and inherited prior, which is learnable from outcomes and permits identical architectures to diverge. That is a dial to measure, not a heritability coefficient. The percentage is `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** `R` is per slot, never a single global number. The question's headline percentage is an aggregation the framework forbids for this quantity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." - **Refuter:** show a figure line that licenses one global apportionment over slots for this question, with no per-slot remainder, or a measurement in which pooled and per-slot residuals carry the same information about where to look.

### C2. Cognition-facing; checkable by a cognitive scientist against the three capacities

- **Statement:** attention, memory and reasoning are a union of overlapping conditions, not one variable, and under Fig 12 they are members of a functional group rather than isomers. Each needs its own ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Contrast Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful? Attention is read by selection and cueing, memory by encoding and recall, reasoning by inference over propositions. Do these share an atom set, or only the substructure of a learner in contact with a habitat? If they share atoms and differ only in arrangement, the isomer claim succeeds.
- **Refuter:** exhibit one atom set and one bond arrangement that generates all three capacities with only the qualifier changing, with the three still distinguishable in behaviour; or exhibit one instrument whose reading is invariant across the three and whose ground is the same for all three.

### C3. Method-facing; checkable by a behaviour geneticist or cross-cultural researcher

- **Statement:** biology and culture are not separable additive buckets. A controlled task is a substituted habitat that proves wiring, not capability in the natural habitat, and the two source currencies are not commensurable until a ground makes them so.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- **What to check:** does gene-environment correlation break the independence of the two inputs? Is there an intervention that changes only biology with habitat contact fixed, and a reverse one, with additive effects and no interaction? Do classical (roughly 0.4 to 0.8) and within-family molecular (roughly 0.10 to 0.20) estimates differ in a way that points to source discrimination rather than sampling noise?
- **Refuter:** both single-source interventions exist with additive effects and no interaction, and both sources are returned in one currency under one declared, provenanced code. If so, the bucket model is viable and C3 fails.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a percentage. The figures do not carry one and this packet does not nominate one.
- Not that culture or biology is irrelevant. `gap` is neither zero nor a denial.
- Not that the world literature is wrong. Twin studies, cross-cultural findings and the Flynn effect are corroboration for variation and per-task effects, not for a share.
- Not that a laboratory harness measures culture directly. It is a substituted habitat.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the three capacities are isomers. They are a functional group under the figures' definition.
- Not that `w` is a heritability coefficient. It is a per-frame, learnable weighting that covers taught as well as inherited priors.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating measurement per slot. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument reads green but "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it by measurement, not argument.

1. **Source test:** can an instrument distinguish readings produced by different sources for the same slot? Without it, the source remains unnamed, as green does not name RGB or CMYK.
2. **Contact test:** does changing the habitat, with inherited priors held as fixed as the design permits, change the slot reading? And the reverse?
3. **Weighting test:** can `w` be estimated per frame from outcomes, and does identical architecture with different history produce the predicted divergence?
4. **Commensurability test:** can both sources be priced in one currency under a declared code with a provenanced conversion rule?
