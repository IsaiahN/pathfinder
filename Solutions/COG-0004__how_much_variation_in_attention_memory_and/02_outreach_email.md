# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the number

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used anywhere in this folder.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` has been read and the refuter is still standing in your own reading of it. If the refuter fires, there is nothing to send.

---

**Subject:** Would you check one claim about culture, biology and attention?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one structural claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, I am not asking you to agree, and I am not asking you to trust anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the test, not `grounded`):** a single headline number for "how much of attention, memory and reasoning is cultural rather than biological" is not a well-formed measurement target. Three things follow from the framework's own rules, and you can check each without reading the framework:
>
> 1. The error signal is indexed per object slot, never aggregated. A pooled percentage across three capacities is exactly the average that hides a live slot.
> 2. Attention, memory and reasoning overlap. A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either, so the three must be unbundled before anyone searches for a source.
> 3. Biology and culture are not two buckets. Capability belongs to the agent and its habitat together, and you cannot subtract the habitat. The interaction carries information the two named sources do not.
>
> The closest the framework comes to your vocabulary is a per-frame weighting `w` between private history and inherited prior. Two frames with identical architecture can diverge because their histories and their `w` diverge. That supports the intuition that culture matters without a genetic difference, but `w` is not a heritability coefficient and the framework does not return a number. Where a number would go, it reports a gap and names the instruments that could close it.
>
> **What would refute the claim, written down before I wrote the conclusion:**
> 1. A figure line that licenses one global apportionment over slots, with no per-slot remainder.
> 2. An intervention that changes only biology while holding habitat contact fixed, plus one that changes only habitat while holding biology fixed, with additive effects and no interaction.
> 3. Evidence that the per-frame weighting is fixed across people and refers only to genetic inheritance.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Before you answer, what would you count as a failure?** I would like your verdict on the claim above recorded before I show you any source. Your later agreement, if you have one, would not be evidence, so I will not pool it.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures text with line numbers (local extract): `C:\Users\Admin\Desktop\Project Pathfinder\FRAMEWORK\reference\03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (GitHub, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture and the eight essays: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures repository. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The author's own write-up warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula and that LLM simulations of it are not testable outputs. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Distribution discipline

- One recipient at a time. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Do not follow up to ask whether they agreed. Ask only whether the refuter fired.
- If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- A Track B reply is a conversation, never corroboration (`verdict_weight = 0` by definition in the master plan).
