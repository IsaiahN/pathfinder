# The Tether's answer: how much is cultural learning?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the falsifiable predictions, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a measurement route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for one share of variance in attention, memory and reasoning, attributed to cultural learning rather than biological differences. The two readings are: (A) a single population variance decomposition is well formed and the figures constrain the share; (B) the question bundles a union of capacities, the measured quantity is per slot and never aggregated, and culture and biology are an agent-and-habitat pair whose interaction is not a separable remainder. A leads to a headline percentage that hides a live slot. B leads to three separate verdicts, a shape and a list of instruments.

## 2. The shape

1. **One global percentage is the forbidden aggregation.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A pool across attention, memory and reasoning discards the only signal that says where to look.

2. **The three capacities are a union that must be unbundled.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Each capacity gets its own loop, its own instrument and its own ground. Under Fig 12 they are members of one functional group, not isomers.

3. **Culture and biology are an agent-and-habitat pair, not two buckets.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat. The bond carries information the two named sources do not (Fig 12 :5).

4. **The framework has a per-frame learning/inheritance weighting, and it is not a heritability coefficient.** Symbols table :23-25: "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge." That is figure support for substantial variation with no biological difference, but `w` is learnable, per frame and covers taught as well as inherited priors. It is a dial to measure, not a coefficient to report.

5. **The number is owed to a ground and the figures do not carry it.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The honest report on the percentage is `gap`: a named absence of an instrument, not an answer of zero for either source.

6. **What can be transmitted is a method, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The outreach offers the shape and the refuter first. Agreement with a conclusion is not evidence when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a percentage. No figure line yields one, and world estimates are method-dependent (roughly 0.4 to 0.8 by classical designs, about 0.5 in reviews, roughly 0.10 to 0.20 by within-family molecular methods).
- Not "culture does not matter" and not "biology does not matter". A `gap` is neither zero nor a denial.
- Not a claim that the world literature is wrong. Twin studies, cross-cultural studies and the Flynn effect are corroboration for the shape: variation exists, it is per task, and it changes when conditions change.
- Not that a controlled experiment measures culture directly. A harness is a substituted habitat and proves wiring, not capability in the natural habitat (Fig 11 :70).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this package settles the share.
- Not a derivation of the three capacities from the six-term substrate. They are functional-group members, not isomers.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Per slot:** does the same attention, memory or reasoning reading arise from different sources, and what instrument discriminates them? A reading alone cannot name its source.
- **Contact intervention:** when the habitat changes while inherited priors are held as fixed as the design permits, does the slot reading change? The reverse intervention is equally required. `An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves` (Fig 11 :40).
- **Weighting:** can `w` be estimated per frame from outcomes, and does identical architecture with different history produce the predicted divergence? Symbols table :25 makes that a falsifiable prediction.
- **Commensurability:** can both sources be priced in one currency under one declared code, with the conversion rule provenanced? Fig 12 :125 says bits and actions do not add until a ground makes them commensurable.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a figure line licensing a global apportionment; an agent-only capability separable from habitat; a fixed, genetics-only `w`. Any one withdraws the shape claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable at this budget.** The instruments exist in principle and some already return per-slot readings, but no single reading discriminates the source. The framework stops where the available instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *Indiscernibility is not identity*: two sources producing the same reading are not the same source. The world literature's disagreement across methods is a method-dependence result, which is exactly a source-discrimination problem.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a percentage for culture versus biology. It says that the error signal is indexed per object slot and never aggregated, so a single share across attention, memory and reasoning is the average that hides the live slot; that the three capacities are a union that must be unbundled; and that biology and culture are an agent-and-habitat pair, not two buckets, because capability belongs to the agent and its habitat together and the habitat cannot be subtracted. Its nearest term to the question is a per-frame weighting between private history and inherited prior, which is learnable from outcomes and allows identical architectures to diverge. That is a dial to measure, not a heritability coefficient. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, three refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap. The percentage stays open until a source-discriminating measurement settles it, and an open percentage is a verdict here, not a silence.
