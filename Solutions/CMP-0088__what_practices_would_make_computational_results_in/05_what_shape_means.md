## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". `Fig 9 :32` "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> A lineage whose atoms and bonds are recoverable, whose tolerance is declared before comparison, and which at least one frame with a different closure ranges against the same unmoving ground.

That is a shape. A single "reproducibility score of 87" is a filler.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens the distinction: "the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." A result that matches within tolerance is indiscernible, not identical. The same essay: "indiscernibility is not identity."

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." And on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is F8 in `01`: software, data and parameters searched separately, then the compound handled on its own.
- `Fig 6 :30` "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." `Fig 9 :30-31` "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." A manifest that says "we used version 4.2" travels; a manifest that says "the usual pipeline" does not.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the acceptance criterion written before the re-run, not the framework's vocabulary imposed on the reader.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." And: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." The shape includes the independence condition as a term, not as an afterthought.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place." By the time the vocabulary is precise enough to name the reproducible result, the analyst has reconstructed the route.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A badge, a checklist and an archived container are gates. A controlled comparison of two frames against the same ground is a settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** `Fig 6 :23` "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." An independent reimplementation is such contact. A same-stack re-run is not.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the status of the vocabulary itself: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The reproducibility vocabulary (atoms, bonds, tolerance, ground) is a visible choice, not nature's own list. It can be revised.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that makes lineage load bearing. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. The essay: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "It was proven wrong, not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Apply this here. A published number is a reading. It does not determine the source, which is the software stack, data revision, parameter setting and random stream that produced it. Several configurations can give the same number. What is owed is a discriminator (an independent implementation ranged against the same ground) and a record (the lineage), not an assertion that the number speaks for its own source.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point about artifacts: "very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." That is why `Fig 13 :130` says the record "is not documentation of the term. It is the term."

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape would a reproducible computational result have?** Three nested constraints, none of them a score:

1. **Recoverable lineage.** Atoms (versions, data identifier, parameter values, seeds, hardware) and typed bonds (the executable route). `Fig 13 :130`.
2. **Declared comparison.** ⋛ within a stated tolerance, with the failure condition written first. Operators table: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."
3. **Independent range.** At least one frame with a different closure against the same ground. `Fig 2 :35` "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

**Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming these three?** No, and I initially over-read it. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Lineage, tolerance and independence are compositions of terms already held (a record, a comparison bond, a second frame). Naming them is binding ordinary terms, not inventing a seventh metric. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework *settles* which tolerance or which implementation is correct; that is the ground's office.

### Correction to my own section 3 and 4

I wrote in an earlier pass that "shape, not a name" means the framework cannot say anything about what a reproducibility practice is. That was an overstatement, recorded here rather than erased. The narrowed reading: the framework can describe the shape of a practice set from terms it holds, and it cannot certify a particular number, tool or threshold as the right one. The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: a passing number still does not identify its source, and the reason is the missing discriminator plus the missing lineage, not a blanket ban on naming held atoms.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well known names to agree with a practice set produces a convention, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**Drift is silent.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "they drift together, so neither notices." Two teams that share a pipeline, a preprint and a reviewer pool can agree for a decade while moving away from the ground. This is why stage 1 of the outreach records a verdict before any theory is shown, and why later agreement is never pooled.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds: "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the practice set and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
