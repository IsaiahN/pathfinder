# The Tether's answer: reproducible computational results in complexity science

**Mode: `specified` on the practice set, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

---

## 1. The residual it answers

A computational result is published. A second party, changing software, data or parameter choices, tries to obtain the same result. The gap between the two is the residual `R`, measured per slot, never averaged. Arity three and overlapping, so it is a union compound (Fig 9 :55), not one hard question. Symmetry: comparison by ⋛ within a tolerance, not identity. Scale: one run, two frames, one channel between them.

The question is not "how do we get the same number again." It is "what recorded constraint lets an independent frame range against the same ground and discover where the first frame was wrong."

## 2. The shape

1. **The lineage is the proof.** `Fig 13 :128` "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Record atoms (exact software versions, data identifier and checksum, parameter values, seeds, hardware) and typed bonds (the executable route), plus the residual answered, the reasoning and the date (`Fig 10 :63`). A number is a reading; it does not comment on its source.
2. **Declare the tolerance before the comparison.** `Fig 4 :30` "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The bond is ⋛, not ≡. Say what counts as agreement and what would count as a failure before the re-run. ACM states the same: exact reproduction is not required, only agreement within an acceptable tolerance.
3. **Buy one independent range.** `Fig 2 :35` "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A second team with a different closure, ideally independently developed artifacts, ranges against the same ground. Same code, same data, same seed is repeatability and carries no verdict weight. `Fig 2 :42` "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
4. **Keep the anchor reachable and enumerate the habitat.** `Fig 10 :24` "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Archive versions, raw data, intermediate results and seeds. List everything in contact with the result outward until the cascade stops mattering (`Fig 11 :44`). Put the channel in a seat that does not author the results.
5. **Reproduce contact, not only the harness.** `Fig 11 :70` "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A benchmark proves the pipeline executes. It does not prove the result survives the conditions the claim was about.
6. **Report two prices.** `Fig 12 :125` "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Bits for the description, actions for the search. Report the sweep budget and parameter sensitivity, not only the chosen values. No single summed score.
7. **Build the middle link.** A parameter list is a dictionary; a runnable controller with a comparison is a grammar (`Fig 12 :111`). The chain is ordered by dependency (`Fig 3 :20`), and the middle link (what counts as progress) usually has no instrument (`Fig 3 :27`).
8. **Search before importing.** `Fig 6 :49` "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited." An unfound parameter regime is not evidence that a new primitive is missing.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a badge, checklist or score makes a result true. Those are filters for the budget (`Fig 9 :60`), not witnesses for the verdict.
- Not that bitwise identity across stacks is the target. The round trip is lossy, so identity confuses repeatability with reproduction.
- Not that same-stack re-runs are worthless. They find transcription errors and hardware faults. They are simply not corroboration.
- Not that synthetic benchmarks are forbidden. They are valid for wiring and invalid for capability.
- Not that the framework settles which tolerance or which implementation is right. That is the ground's office, and no measurement here was taken.
- Not that the vocabulary of lineage, tolerance and independence is nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."
- Not a theory of everything, and not the only possible practice set. There is no last room (`Fig 7 :6`).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A controlled comparison on a real complexity-science model, not a further argument. The five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 are the loss conditions:

1. **Lineage test.** Can an independent party decide provenance and decidability from the artifact alone? If yes, the lineage requirement falls.
2. **Independence test.** Does a same-stack re-run count as a verdict in a real journal's or community's standard? If yes, the independence requirement falls.
3. **Tolerance test.** Is bitwise identity across two stacks both achievable and the correct criterion? If yes, F2 falls.
4. **Habitat test.** Does a synthetic harness result transfer to the real system with no contact conditions reproduced? If yes, F5 falls.
5. **Currency test.** Does a single summed number over bits and actions predict reproducibility better than the two reported apart? If yes, F6 falls.

The ground here is a recorded outcome on a named system: a published model, a fixed dataset, a declared tolerance, an independent implementation. Agreement among readers is not that ground (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that cannot distinguish sources "demonstrates its own inadequacy").

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** No controlled comparison was run. The practice set stops at the limit of what the figures can constrain and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Two findings (F5 habitat, F6 currencies) are single-source within the figures and are marked as such; they are not dressed up as triangulated. The canonical rule: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." Indiscernibility is not identity. What this package offers is the shape any reproducible result must have, the refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close it. It does not settle the question.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a reproducibility score. It says that a computational result is reproducible only when its lineage is recoverable, its tolerance for agreement is declared before the comparison, and at least one frame with a different closure ranges against the same ground. Re-running the same code with the same seed is repeatability, not corroboration. A synthetic harness proves wiring, not capability. Description cost and settling cost must be reported separately, because they are in different units. The practices run in dependency order, and the link that usually breaks is the one nobody instruments: saying what would count as progress. What is offered is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the controlled tests that would settle it. Nothing here has been measured, and later agreement with the framework is never pooled as evidence.
