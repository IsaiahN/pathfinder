## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between a published result and a result under changed software, data or parameters. Arity: three overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not a partition into three isolated questions. Symmetry: a threshold comparison ⋛ with a declared tolerance, not identity ≡. Scale: one run within a frame, two frames ranged against one anchor, one channel between them.
- **Domain 1, original run.** Actors: code, data, parameters, seeds, hardware. Currency: `R` per slot against the dataset. Anchor: the named measurement the model was fit against. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 2, reproducing party.** Actors: a second team with different software or independently built artifacts. Currency: agreement within tolerance versus residual. Anchor: the same ground, never agreement with Domain 1. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 3, transmission channel.** Actors: artifact, lineage record, abstracted method, maintainer seat. Currency: bits for description and actions for settlement. Anchor: the ground, kept reachable through a channel that decays. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Partition or union.** Domains 1 and 2 are two frames against one anchor. Inside R, the three conditions form a union with intersections. Domain 3 is the channel, not a fourth condition.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective (the tolerance), planning, learn and carry. The middle link is the one without an instrument.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 13 and Fig 10 for D1, I derived from Fig 1's per-slot rule and Fig 12's arrangement rule. Where the primary used Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 8 for D2, I used Fig 9's residual-first rule and Fig 11's habitat enumeration. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the concrete run, exact code, data identifier and checksum, parameter values, seed, hardware and run order.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely a recoverable lineage, a declared tolerance, and one independent range against the same ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a second implementation in a different stack, with the same tolerance, asked to reproduce the original result.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **operand types**: a version number is a different kind of thing from a parameter value, and a data checksum is not a hyperparameter. The shorthand "record the configuration" drops that distinction;
- the **contact conditions**: the harness carried up a wiring result and brought down a capability claim unless the habitat was enumerated separately (Fig 11 :70);
- the **two currencies**: the shorthand "reproducible" compresses a description cost and a settling cost that the figures say do not add (Fig 12 :125-130).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure (the lineage) must sit between the published number and the ground, and the comparison is a threshold, not an identity. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no controlled re-run exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
