## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before using the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** One unobserved scalar inferred from several observed series, feeding a policy stance. Arity: many-to-one, so underdetermined. Symmetry: candidate sources are symmetric for observed output and asymmetric for capacity utilisation, vacancies, wage growth and inflation. Scale: aggregate, component and vintage are three levels with lossy transforms between. Question type: a **union compound**, because informative and imprecise can both hold (Fig 9 :57-58).
- **Domain 1, framework-internal metatheory.** Actors: figures, methods, Γ, R. Currency: composition, residual, shape. Anchor: the figures as metalaws for structure, the ground for any measurement. Partition: what an inferred reading can carry.
- **Domain 2, economic measurement.** Actors: GDP and vintages, capacity utilisation, unemployment and alternatives, vacancies, hours, wage growth, producer prices, productivity. Currency: published statistics. Anchor: outturn data and the revision record. Which series carry slack and how estimates revise.
- **Domain 3, latent-state estimation.** Actors: potential output, NAIRU, decompositions, filters. Currency: model inference. Anchor: prediction against Domain 2. One unobserved state with observationally equivalent drivers; union.
- **Domain 4, policy decision and verification.** Actors: central banks, fiscal authorities, surveillance bodies, expert readers. Currency: policy stance, agreement and residual. Anchor: outturn inflation and employment, never agreement. Triangulation, not negotiation.

Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat before selecting variables (Fig 11); read per slot before aggregating (Fig 1); describe the effect before naming the ceiling (Fig 9); unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9); triangulate before acting (Fig 2, Fig 8); settle at the ground before carrying a verdict (Fig 5).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-59 and world evidence, I derived it from Fig 9 :57-58 and the arity analysis (many-to-one map, several sources for one reading). Different starting points, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-34 (anchor must not update), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :27-30 (lossy transform), Fig 6 :27 (depth versus absence), Fig 9 :54-59 (union unbundling).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` §0 and `07`: an unobserved capacity ceiling cannot be the anchor; report a band; read per slot; triangulate; publish the revision; report `unreached`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy rule, for example "use the CBO real-time gap of x percent to set the stance now."

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **vintage and revision convention**, which the concrete rule must state and the shorthand leaves implicit;
- the **per-slot decomposition**, without which the band is still an aggregate and F2 is breached;
- the **independence condition on the corroborating readings**, which a concrete rule can quietly replace with a consensus of overlapping estimates;
- the **`unreached` status**, which a concrete rule tends to convert into a point decision as soon as a number is on the page.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: outturn inflation and employment are the anchor and the estimate is a reading. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no vintage-level computation was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
