## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (via exemplar mapping): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `CAPACITY(???)` is a shape: a sustainable ceiling with an unknown level and an unknown revision path.
- `Potential output = 29,443.0 billion chained 2017 dollars` is a name, and a vintage-specific one at that.

The shape also includes the behaviour of the thing: it must be reportable as a band, revisable without pretending the old value was wrong, readable per slot, and ranged against outturn data. That is more constraining than a point estimate and less than a theory of the economy.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (via exemplar mapping): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (via exemplar mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (via exemplar mapping): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the effect first (output grew, capacity utilisation and vacancies moved this way, inflation did or did not respond), then bind a capacity term with provenance. Do not start from the CBO series and ask what it means. That is the mapping-first inversion the figures forbid.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For potential output, no such instrument was run in this folder, so the number stays `candidate`.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The engineering and meteorological sources in `04_links_and_distribution.md` are exactly that: a different closure that predicts the shape of a latent state corrected by observations.

*They're Scanning Books* (via exemplar mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The choice of what counts as "capacity" is a visible guess. Turning it into a point value presented as measured makes the choice invisible.

## 4. Why a capacity estimate is the specific metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay adds that an instrument unable to distinguish the cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and that the answer is *"unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case.

The parallel is exact. An observed path of GDP plus a subdued inflation reading is consistent with at least two sources: substantial slack in an economy with stable capacity, or little slack in an economy whose capacity has fallen. The VoxEU column makes the same point in economic language when it notes the difficulty of distinguishing falls in demand from falls in supply and says *"alternative means of understanding slack are also crucial to ensure the robustness of the diagnosis."* The estimate reads the projection and names the source. What is owed is a better discriminator (independent slack readings), not a more confident name.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid publishing any potential output number?

**No, narrowed.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* thing, a primitive not held in Γ. It does not forbid reporting a value with a stated vintage, interval, and provenance as an ordinary term. What it forbids is presenting that value as the capacity ceiling itself, as if the framework had settled it. The honest form is: here is this institution's estimate for this vintage, with this band and this revision history, ranged against outturn data.

**Does the common policy use commit the forbidden error?** Yes, when the estimate is used as the anchor in a rule (Fig 2 :29-34) or when a point value is treated as decisive without a band (Fig 1 :22, Fig 9 :30-31). The correct form remains `CAPACITY(???)`, with the question mark replaced by a range rather than a name.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote §4 as if the framework forbade naming capacity at all. That overstates the rule. The forbidden move is naming the *source* of a lossy reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources, and assigning the reading the anchor position. Publishing a provenanced range is permitted. The metamerism point stands, but its reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on all numbers. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous forecasters who agree with a capacity estimate is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let outturn data settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLMs can *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any capacity estimate and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
