# Expert verification packet: how should AI systems combine neural learning with symbolic reasoning, search, memory, and tools?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a hybrid-AI researcher or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. One-to-one use only.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape and dependency order, `general` on the structural rules. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle which arrangement wins.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of overlapping conditions, so it must be unbundled before it is answered. The five named parts are ingredients; the answer is the typed bonds among them plus a ground. From the figures it follows that the residual stays indexed per object slot and the ground is the only metric; the roles sit in a dependency chain (instruments and tools, symbolic vocabulary, objective and comparison, search, learn and carry) with the unmeasured vocabulary-to-goal link the usual break; search visits the same closure and buys reach only by import or refusal; memory splits into a growing record and a fallible reach and needs negation; and tools are imports that change contact, making capability a property of agent and habitat. The whole is a typed cycle, not a linear stack. The slot for the winning architecture stays open, and falsifiers were written before this packet.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 3

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot and the question is a union; one aggregate benchmark and one lever are both insufficient.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores all five conditions with no categorically different residue; or a system whose global score is near zero while no live per-slot residual can be recovered by any instrument.

### C2 - framework-internal and engineering-facing; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 6 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** bonds are priced and typed; the arrangement, not the ingredient list, is the answer; search never adds an atom, and memory must be able to refuse.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Operators table on negation: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*.
- **What to check:** can a bond type be permuted between two roles without changing per-slot residuals or probe outcomes? Does any claimed capability gain come only from more search inside the same closure? Can the memory mark a held arrangement false rather than merely absent?
- **Refuter:** a bond permutation with no behavioural change; search that adds an atom to closure; or a library and a reach that grow together with no new atom and no refusal.

### C3 - habitat-facing; checkable against instruments and environment

- **Statement:** tools are imports and instruments; capability is agent-and-habitat, and a test harness is a substituted habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 6 :26 *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access. Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."*
- **What to check:** was the claimed gain an agent-only change, or did it alter contact through an instrument or import? Was the import provenanced with a source gate and a shadow test?
- **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground while habitat and contact are held fixed. Fig 11 :72 names this as the refutation of the capability claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named winning architecture, and not that neuro-symbolic integration type 6 is the answer.

- Not that search or parameter count increases reach by itself.
- Not that an aggregate benchmark scores the combination.
- Not that a self-reconstructing verifier is an outside seat (Fig 10 :42).
