## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the winning architecture. It constrains; it does not fill. "Five roles joined by typed bonds, with the residual kept per slot and the slot for the winning architecture left open" is a shape. "A neuro-symbolic transformer with module X" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the words you already have that are *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then let recurring new terms narrow the space. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* This is Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground"*) in search language.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: the final check asks *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly this question, and it is why F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` unbundles the five components instead of answering "how to combine them" as one problem.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and :31 *"rather than the cause"*. The effect here is per-slot residual reduction against a ground; the cause names (neural, symbolic) are borrowed engineering words.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :9-13). Naming a specific integration type from the neuro-symbolic survey would be such an import, and it would need provenance and a shadow test before it carried weight.

The survey itself refuses the name: *"there is no NeSy approach to-date that can truly meet the standard of Type 6"* (Wang et al., 2024, opened 2026-11-24), and the structured review says *"we do not believe there are enough results to draw definitive conclusions"* (Hamilton et al., 2024, opened 2026-11-24). Agreement between these papers and the framework is one evidence pool talking, not corroboration at independence.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument rule that governs this question: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* An instrument that reads one value *"cannot comment on the source"*; yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or an undiscovered scheme can all produce the same reading. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and the resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: two architectures can produce the same aggregate benchmark score while differing in which slot is live, whether a bond was priced, whether memory can refuse, and whether tools changed contact. The aggregate score is the reading; the source is the arrangement. What is owed is a per-slot, source-discriminating instrument, not an architecture name argued from a projection. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run:** a union of five role-bearing components, ordered by dependency, with (a) a typed and priced bond assigned between each adjacent pair, (b) the residual R readable per object slot, (c) search treated as SEARCHED with independent searchers and imports where the wall must move, (d) a memory whose record and reach are distinguished and whose symbolic vocabulary carries negation, (e) tools treated as instruments and imports with provenance, and (f) a ground and an outside checker that cannot author the claim.

**Does any world source fill the open slot?** No. The neuro-symbolic literature supplies integration *types*, not a settled winner, and each type is a candidate binding that would need a source gate. The honest form remains a shape with a declared `gap` for the specific architecture. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, as recorded in the exemplar's `05` and ledger this session by citation only: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous neuro-symbolic authors selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets a per-slot ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The same essay records that LLMs *"simulate an approximate answer/output"* while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the architecture question and cannot settle it.

**The stop is honest.** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage: what was unbundled, what bond was priced, what was imported, what was refused, and what would reverse the verdict. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README). Two architectures indiscernible on an aggregate benchmark are not thereby the same architecture.
