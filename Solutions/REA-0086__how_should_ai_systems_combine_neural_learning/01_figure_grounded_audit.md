## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question is not "which modules exist." It is "which typed relations among five components drive R down per slot, and what settles them." Symbols table: R is *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it."*

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** Arity: at least two operands per bond, indexed by object slot s. Symmetry: the bonds are not interchangeable; some are ordered (sequence →, production ⇒, subtraction −, comparison ⋛) and some symmetric (+, ∥, ≡), and negation is unary (Operators table). Scale: a single agent loop, a population of searchers across a membrane (Fig 4), and an environment that settles across time.
- **Currency.** Description length |φ| (bits) and actions (budget) are two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125). Any proposal that sums them into one score is the named error, not a score.
- **Anchor.** The ground: a measurement, benchmark outcome, or environment that does not update because a frame thinks otherwise (Fig 2 :29). Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*
- **What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run).** A set of typed bonds among five role-bearing components, a dependency order, a per-slot residual test, and a named ground that can fire against it. Anything that cannot be written in those terms is not yet an answer.

The five named parts are not the six substrate terms. Neural learning is a role in the controller (Γ, b, w, R), symbolic reasoning is the bond vocabulary, search is an operation over closure(Γ), memory is the record, and tools are instruments and imports. This is a binding from the engineering frame onto framework roles, not a derivation of new primitives. That distinction is recorded, not hidden.

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union of conditions, not one lever (structural, decisive)

Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The five components overlap. Symbolic reasoning can support search; memory can hold symbolic rules; tools can be selected by search. A single lever labelled "combine them" clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. So the honest answer is five loops with a dependency order, not one architecture name.

Mode `general` for the union rule, `specified` for applying it to these five. **Refuter:** one intervention that restores all five conditions at once and leaves no categorically different residue. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union rule plus Fig 3 dependency chain, different objects).

### F2 - The arrangement is the answer; an ingredient list records half of itself (structural, decisive)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*

Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*

Naming neural, symbolic, search, memory and tools names five ingredients. It does not say whether neural → symbolic (a pipeline, ordered), neural ∥ symbolic (either suffices), or symbolic ⇒ neural (production), nor which bond joins memory to search. Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*

Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a controlled swap of one bond type between two components that leaves every per-slot residual and every probe outcome unchanged. Then the bond carried nothing and Fig 12 fails here. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 bond price plus Fig 13 cross-kind rule).

### F3 - Composition crosses kinds; the five parts are roles, not new primitives (specified, ungrounded)

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*

Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."*

Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

Binding proposed here: neural learning acts on Γ, b and w (the controller), symbolic reasoning supplies the bond grammar (a structure), search is an operation on closure(Γ), memory is the record, and tools are imports from a differing closure. No seventh primitive is minted. This is a binding from the engineering frame onto held framework roles, not a derivation, so it carries `specified` mode and zero settlement weight until a ground pays for it.

Mode `general` for the cross-kind rule, `specified` for the binding. **Refuter:** a figure re-census that admits two same-kind terms bonding directly without an intervening opposite-kind term, or a demonstration that "neural" and "symbolic" must be members of the six-term set. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 substrate plus Operators table, different scopes).

**Strain, named:** Fig 13 :75 governs the six substrate terms. It must not be stretched to forbid an engineering pipeline between software modules. The claim made here is the narrower one that the framework's six are not augmented by five new primitives. After that re-census the strain dissolves.

### F4 - The ground is the only metric; internal agreement cannot score the combination

Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

A symbolic consistency check performed by the same system that produced the neural output is one frame grading itself. It cannot be the anchor. The anchor is an external measurement that does not move when the system changes its mind.

Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a system whose self-consistency score predicts ground outcomes across a changed environment with a genuinely different evidence pool. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 metric rule plus Fig 2 anchor rule).

### F5 - The residual stays indexed per slot; a global score hides where to look

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Fig 1 :13 (from the figure): *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*

A hybrid architecture reported by one benchmark number has thrown away the signal that says which component to change. The combination is good only if the residual is readable per slot.

Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a system whose global score is near zero while a live per-slot residual cannot be recovered by any instrument. That would show the framework's indexing rule is not load-bearing here. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 per-slot plus Formula step 1).

### F6 - Search is SEARCHED at the same reach, not a new atom, and independence is the parameter

Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."*

Fig 6 :50: *"Fails by spending budget on a region already swept. Distribute the search, and keep the searchers independent."*

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

Search changes the space visited, not the wall. To move the wall you need an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23). More searchers drawn from one evidence pool are one searcher wearing many names (Fig 6 :53). AlphaGo is the checkable instance: the search algorithm *"combines Monte Carlo simulation with value and policy networks"* (Silver et al., Nature 2016, opened 2026-11-24). That is corroboration from the same AI evidence pool, not a second witness.

Mode `general` for idempotence, `specified` for the engineering reading. **Refuter:** a composition inside a closed set that adds an atom, or two independent instruments that agree but demonstrably draw on one evidence pool while still predicting the ground. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 third question plus Fig 8 idempotence).

### F7 - Memory: the record grows, the reach can fall, and refusal needs negation

Fig 6 :42: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."*

Fig 5 :69: *"AMENDMENT · settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."*

Fig 5 :71: *"So a system can hold terms it purchased and cannot use,"*

Fig 5 :72: *"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*

Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."*

The symbolic part of the combination is what lets memory refuse, not just accumulate. Operators table :57: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Negation is unary and is not a bond (Fig 12 amendment), so it is not one of the seven priced relations. A memory with addition and subtraction but no negation can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say the arrangement is wrong.

Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a library that provably grows in reach with no new atom and no refusal, or a vocabulary with subtraction only that marks an arrangement false. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 record/reach plus Operators negation, different objects).

### F8 - Tools are imports and instruments; capability is agent-and-habitat

Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access. Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."*

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

Tools are outside the agent: they extend contact, and an import adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25). They must be provenanced as imports with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13, not quoted here as figure lines to avoid a strain on line boundaries). Instances: external memory is coupled to a network in Graves et al. 2014 (*"coupling them to external memory resources"*), and ReAct uses actions to *"interface with external sources"* (Yao et al., 2023). Both are same-pool corroboration.

Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground while habitat and contact are held fixed. The habitat figure says exactly that this would refute the capability claim (Fig 11 :72). **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 habitat plus Fig 6 instrument rule).

### F9 - Refuse before mint: the direction of the question is outside the loop

Fig 5 :61: *"BUT THE TERM MOST WORTH REFUSING MAY NOT BE IN THE LIBRARY AT ALL."*

Fig 5 :62: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."*

Fig 5 :63: *"residual, so nothing here can see it. Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"*

Fig 5 :65: *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."*

The sought-for shape here is "the right combination." If it is left as a preference, no check inside the loop can refuse it. The remedy is applied literally in §0 (write down what an answer would look like and bind it) and in §3 (pre-registered refuter, written before the conclusion).

Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a loop that certifies the direction of its own question without an externally bound term. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 5 direction limit plus Formula step 2 REFUTED branch).

### F10 - Verification is a seat, not a fifth module

Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*

Fig 10 :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*

A checker that is trained on the same data and shares the same benchmark as the generator is one frame, not an outside seat. Fig 2 :31-32 define independence as how little two frames draw on the same evidence. So the "combine" answer must name a seat that cannot author the claim and a channel that is kept reachable, or it has no way to know the combination failed.

Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a verifier that reconstructs the claim and still returns an independent ground verdict. By the figure that is impossible by construction, so a positive result would falsify the seat rule. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 10 verifier non-access plus Fig 2 independence).

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five, or a same-kind bond is admitted that cannot be refused (breaks Fig 13 :74-75).
2. A bond type can be swapped between two components with no change in any per-slot residual or probe outcome (breaks Fig 12 :46, :94).
3. An agent-only improvement moves the ground while habitat and contact are held fixed (breaks Fig 11 :40).
4. A library grows and reach grows with no new atom and no refusal (breaks Fig 5 :72 and Fig 6 :42).
5. Search inside a closed set adds an atom (breaks Fig 8 :32).
6. A single lever restores all five conditions with no categorically different residue (breaks Fig 9 union rule).
7. A self-reconstructing verifier returns a ground-valid independent verdict (breaks Fig 10 :42).

If any fires, the corresponding domain finding is withdrawn and the union is re-censused. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`. This is the *"design instrument"* the seat map requires; Fig 5 :65 supplies the discipline that makes it bindable.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Do not ship "neuro-symbolic architecture" as a single answer. Ship the unbundled union with a dependency order (F1).
2. State the bonds, not the ingredient list, and price them; a flat stack records half of itself (F2).
3. Keep the residual per slot, never as one benchmark scalar (F5).
4. Treat search as SEARCHED, distribute it, and keep searchers independent; use budget as a filter, not a witness (F6, F9).
5. Distinguish record from reach; give symbolic reasoning the unary refusal operator so memory can change reach (F7).
6. Treat tools as imports with a source gate, and build instruments from worse instruments already returning something (F8).
7. Install a checker that cannot reconstruct the claim and keep the ground reachable (F10).
8. Leave the named-architecture slot open. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual (F9). A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator (the union, the bonds, the refuter), not one concluded architecture.
