## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **There is no agreed winning integration type.** The neuro-symbolic survey adopts Kautz's six types and reports that *"there is no NeSy approach to-date that can truly meet the standard of Type 6"* (Wang et al., 2024, opened 2026-11-24). This matches the figure-derived `gap`: the framework returns a shape, and the world literature has not settled a name. Agreement between the two is one witness, not two, because the survey and the framework answer different questions at different scopes.
2. **The structured review refuses a verdict.** Hamilton et al. find *"we do not believe there are enough results to draw definitive conclusions"* and advocate *"a more methodical approach to the application of theories of reasoning"*. That corroborates the demand for a pre-registered refuter and a ground, not an architecture endorsement.
3. **Search combined with learned evaluation is a checkable instance.** AlphaGo combines Monte Carlo simulation with value and policy networks. It corroborates Fig 6 :49 (search visits the same tree more thoroughly) and Fig 4 :22 (a method that can be reapplied crosses scale). It does not corroborate any claim about which union of five components is best in general.
4. **Memory and tools are couplings, not new primitives.** Neural Turing Machines couple a network to external memory; ReAct lets actions interface with external sources. Both corroborate Fig 6 :26 (*"instruments extend what can be represented at all"*) and Fig 11 :40 (*"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing"*) at the instance level. Both are from the same AI evidence pool.
5. **The dual-process analogy is low overlap but only an analogy.** Evans (2008) distinguishes fast automatic from slow deliberative processing. The neuro-symbolic survey itself connects this to System 1 and System 2. Per Fig 9 :30-31, describe the effect rather than the cause: the analogy supports the shape of a two-role story, not a claim about how a machine must be built.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union shape and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the interiors before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the survey or the AlphaGo paper.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Venue prestige and publication count are budget filters, never evidence that the shape is right.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) stage 3, the formula as a reusable method, only on request.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded *before* any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22).
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` says the loop *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it, and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. It does not survive if the slot is named, if the residual is aggregated, or if search or memory is credited with moving a wall. Those defects are repaired in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md` before sending.
