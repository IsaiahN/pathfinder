## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no named architecture does. Unbundle the five components into a union of overlapping conditions, keep R indexed per object slot with the ground as the only metric, assign and price typed bonds, order roles by dependency with the vocabulary-to-goal link under an instrument, treat search as SEARCHED with independent searchers, split memory into a growing record and a fallible reach that can refuse, treat tools as imports and instruments that change contact, and close the loop as a cycle rather than a stack. Leave the winning name open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. Every domain verdict is kept separate and none is averaged. Mode `specified` for the domain findings, `general` for the structural rules, all ungrounded. The AI literature corroborates the mechanism shape and the `gap`; the low-overlap cognition source corroborates the two-role analogy only.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If bond pricing is not load-bearing, the bond-swap test fails and the arrangement claim is withdrawn. If capability is agent-only, the contact test fails and D5 is withdrawn. The larger practical cost is spending an expert's twenty minutes refuting a shape that was never the right shape by leading with a named architecture and a self-consistency score. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers in `01` §3, in particular (1) a substrate term derived from the other five or an unrefusable same-kind bond, (2) a bond permutation with no behavioural change, (3) an agent-only change that moves the ground, (4) record and reach growing together with no new atom and no refusal, (5) search adding an atom inside a closed set, (6) one lever restoring all five conditions, or (7) a self-reconstructing verifier returning an independent ground verdict. A genuinely low-overlap source with a source-discriminating instrument that names the slot would also reverse the `gap`. Agreement among hybrid-AI authors would not: Fig 2 :40 disqualifies it.
