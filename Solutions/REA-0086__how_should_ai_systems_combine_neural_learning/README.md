## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that one named architecture (for example "a neuro-symbolic transformer with module X") is the answer. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

Three figure reasons: Fig 2 :35 (a mirror cannot correct you), Fig 2 :40 (agreeing frames that share an evidence pool are one witness), and Fig 10 :42 (*"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
