# Movement and adaptation: isomers, union order, and a falsifier

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

"Move or adapt" could be read as two spellings of one response (isomers) or as two different operators in a union. The figures must decide which, and the order of the union must be stated so the loop runs per domain rather than on a blend.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are movement and adaptation isomers?

Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

For isomerism the two terms must share the same atoms and differ only in the bond. They do not:

- A range shift is a change of position of the whole organism, a relation of an organism to **space** over **time**. Fig 13 :76 "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."
- An adaptive change is a change in the distribution of a trait in a lineage, a relation of a population to **energy and conditions** through **selection**. In the figures' vocabulary it is selection running (Fig 11 :36), a change in the agent's relation to its habitat, not a change of location.

They share the state Time and little else. World corroboration: Nathan et al. 2008 measure movement in units of space and time with motion and navigation capacities; Hoffmann and Sgrò 2011 and Buskirk et al. 2012 measure adaptation in per-generation trait units (haldanes). Different units, different instruments, different grounds.

**Verdict: not isomers.** They are two conditions of a union (Fig 9 :54) with different currencies. The weaker structural relation is that of Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Both are members of the class "responses that change contact with a shifting habitat", qualified differently. The functional-group reading is `specified`, single-source for the figure definition, with world corroboration on the different units.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if movement and adaptation can be expressed in one currency with one instrument and one ground, so that a single rate subsumes them without residual, the not-isomer verdict fails and the union must be re-censused as one mechanism.

### Q2 - What is the order of the union?

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

Dependency order for this question:

1. **Perceive the shift.** Climate velocity is computed from a temporal rate and a spatial gradient. This is a condition in the habitat, not the ground (Fig 11 :73).
2. **Have terms for what the habitat affords.** Corridors, gradients, phenology of partners. Fig 3 :25-26 says this middle link has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments.
3. **State the objective.** Persistence of the realized niche, not a global average across species (Fig 1 :19).
4. **Plan the route.** Movement path or adaptive response, each with its own rate.
5. **Learn and carry.** Observed persistence settles which route worked; the record carries the lineage, not the success (Fig 4 :22).

The two mechanisms (movement, adaptation) sit at step 4 as parallel branches of a union, each with its own loop and its own ground. They are not composed with each other; they are compared at step 5 against the same anchor of observed persistence.

**Falsifier:** if one mechanism's output is the required input of the other (for example, if dispersal must precede adaptation for any adaptive response to count), the parallel-branch reading fails and the order becomes a dependency chain. This is testable: a population adapting in place with no immigration would refute a strict dependency.

### Q3 - What does recursion buy at the next level?

Fig 12 :103 "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."
Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."

A range shift that is settled against observation (Chen et al. 2011) becomes an atom for the next level: a community response. An adaptive change that is settled against a common-garden or heritability study becomes an atom for the next level: a lineage's response to subsequent warming. The community-level question (does the assemblage reassemble or dissolve) composes over these settled atoms rather than over the raw species. This is Fig 12 recursion, not a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall.

**Falsifier:** show that a settled range shift cannot serve as an atom for a community composition, so every community-level claim must re-derive each species' movement from scratch.

### Q4 - Why this matters for the outreach packet

The packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks an expert to check three sub-claims. Q1 licenses splitting the "how quickly" question into two checkable claims rather than one blurred one. Q2 licenses asking, for each species and habitat, which link the dependency chain stops at (Fig 3 :29-30 "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."). Q3 licenses the honest stop: we report the shape at this level and leave the next level's composition to a ground reading.

## CONCLUSION

Movement and adaptation are **not isomers**: they share too few atoms and have different currencies, instruments and grounds. They are **union conditions**, parallel branches ordered by dependency after perception and vocabulary, before settlement. Their union is not a blend (Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom"), and averaging them into one global rate destroys the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."). The falsifiers above are stated in advance. Nothing here is `grounded`.
