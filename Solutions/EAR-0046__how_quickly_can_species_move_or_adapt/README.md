## Question restated as an effect

A climate zone is a band of suitable conditions moving across the landscape at a rate set by the temporal rate of warming divided by the spatial gradient of the climate variable. A species persists if its realized niche can follow that moving band, either by moving in space or by changing its relationship to the conditions in place. The residual is the gap between the rate of the moving band and the rate of tracking. Option A: treat "how quickly" as one number and report a global average. Option B: unbundle the union, give a per-domain rate shape with separate grounds, and leave the number to measurement. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula step "Direction"): a **tracking capacity** for each species and habitat, decomposed into a dispersal-driven range-shift rate and an in-place adaptive rate, each compared against a local climate velocity, with the comparison settled by observed persistence rather than model agreement.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list selected for agreement is variation selected into one room.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" and Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Reachability and contact scores are filters for the budget, never witnesses.
3. Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
