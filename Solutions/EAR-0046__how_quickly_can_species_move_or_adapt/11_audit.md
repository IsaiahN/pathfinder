## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a moving climate band and a species' tracking capacity. Arity: at least three conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54), not one hard question. Symmetry: movement changes location; adaptation changes the species-to-condition relation; the two are not interchangeable and have different currencies (distance per time versus trait change per generation). Scale: individual, population, range, community, recursive across strata.
- **Domain 1, climate forcing / habitat conditions.** Actors: isotherms, gradients, topography, season. Currency: km per year. Anchor: none that settles persistence. Not a domain that issues a verdict; a condition enumerated from the world.
- **Domain 2, movement.** Actors: dispersers, propagules, corridors, barriers, partners. Currency: distance per time. Anchor: observed range boundaries through time. Ground-visible.
- **Domain 3, adaptation.** Actors: individuals, heritable variation, generation time, selection gradient. Currency: trait change per generation. Anchor: common-garden and heritability measurements. Ground-visible.
- **Domain 4, verification and management.** Actors: researchers, managers, monitoring datasets, models. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: observed persistence, never agreement. Ground-visible.

Order by dependency: perceive the shift, have terms for what the habitat affords, state the per-species objective, plan the parallel branches, settle against persistence (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.").

My census matches the primary's three verdict-issuing domains, its union reading, and its classification of forcing as a condition rather than a ground. I derived from Fig 9's union split and Fig 3's dependency order, whereas the primary derived from Fig 11's capability rule and Fig 2's anchor rule. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40, :52, :73 (capability, cascade, habitat versus ground), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot), Fig 6 :32 (instruments).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: tracking capacity is agent-and-habitat, the partner can be three steps out, adaptation is selection running, the ground is observed persistence, the number is an import.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete management answer, for example "assist this species to move at the velocity of climate change", or "report a global median range-shift rate as the answer to the question".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot requirement**: the concretisation "report a global median rate" collapses the per-species signal that Fig 1 :19 requires;
- the **habitat condition / ground distinction**: the concretisation "climate velocity decides persistence" silently promotes a condition to the anchor, which Fig 11 :73 forbids;
- the **substitution law**: the concretisation "assisted migration removes the climate risk" erases Fig 11 :66-68, including the two silent failure modes.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a species' realized tracking capacity is a property of the species and its habitat, and the binding relation can be several steps out. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
