## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 states the system can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands and cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can supply a number. It constrains: any acceptable answer must treat tracking capacity as agent-and-habitat, must enumerate contact cascades, must separate movement from adaptation, and must be settled by observed persistence. It does not fill: it does not say that a given species moves at 3.7 km per year or adapts at 0.02 haldanes.

- Shape: "a per-species tracking capacity, decomposed into a range-shift rate and an in-place adaptive rate, compared against a local climate velocity, settled by observed persistence."
- Name or number: "the boreal spruce tracks at 0.4 km per year." That is a measurement from a world frame, provenanced as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect terms are "band moves at rate set by rate over gradient", "species follows in space or changes relation in place", "partner three steps out can bind the rate", and "observed persistence decides". We describe those before picking any paper to cite, so any paper we pick will not automatically seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` line 41: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here the frames are climate velocity studies, movement ecology and evolutionary biology. Their numbers are admitted only with author, venue, year and method recorded.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. Why "how quickly" cannot be answered from the figures alone

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here several paths produce the same observed persistence: a species may track by moving, by adapting in place, by plasticity, or by a partner arriving. An observation of "the species is still there" cannot comment on which path ran. The instrument owed is one that discriminates the paths (common-garden trials, heritability estimates, telemetry, occurrence time series), not a further argument about the framework.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

### Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" might forbid reporting any number at all. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, and numbers from world instruments are not primitives. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework *derives* the number, or naming a source (movement versus adaptation) from a persistence reading alone. The metamerism point in §4 is not retracted; only the over-broad ban is narrowed.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well known names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let observed persistence settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
