# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before deciding whether the framework matters

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter survive the reader's own check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One shape claim about species tracking a shifting climate, with the refuter written in advance

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "how quickly can a species move or adapt as climate zones shift" is not one number. It is a union of conditions with separate grounds. The framework I am using says three things that are checkable without the theory:
>
> 1. Realized tracking capacity belongs to the species and its habitat together, not to the species alone. A disperser with no corridor and no required partner does not track; a slow mover in a steep, connected gradient may track with little movement.
> 2. The rate that matters can be set by a relation three steps out. A species can arrive in a newly suitable climate band and fail because its pollinator, prey, or competitor did not arrive. Direct-contact scoping is how DDT reached the eagles.
> 3. "Adapt" is selection running on heritable variation in a lineage, not a decision by the species, and a change that does not alter contact with the ground changes nothing however large it looks.
>
> The specific speed is **not** derived by the framework. It is an import from your field, with provenance and a shadow test. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have: a per-species tracking capacity, decomposed into a range-shift rate and an in-place adaptive rate, each compared against a local climate velocity, settled by observed persistence rather than model agreement.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. movement and adaptation share one closure and one ground, so a single global rate leaves no residual at the intersections;
> 2. a species-only improvement changes realized tracking while habitat contact is held identical;
> 3. a species tracks its band with no dependence on any other actor and no effect beyond first-order contact across the whole interval;
> 4. a plastic or genetic change alters persistence without changing contact with the ground.
>
> If any holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the material is below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

| No request for trust or prestige | Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." An ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask for endorsement. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask readers to state their own ground and refuter | Formula step "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. |

**Stage order.** Stage 1: the reader checks the shape claim and its refuter without holding the framework's interior. Stage 2, opt in only: if curious, they open the figure links. Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded **before** any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
