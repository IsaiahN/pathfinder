# The Tether's answer: how quickly can species move or adapt as climate zones shift?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the composition rules. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a number. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A climate band moves at a rate set by the temporal rate of change divided by the spatial gradient. A species persists if its realized niche follows, by moving in space or by changing its relation to the conditions in place. The residual is the gap between the band's rate and the species' tracking capacity. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55), so it gets one loop per domain and the verdicts stay separate.

## 2. The shape, per domain

### D2, movement / range shift (triangulated, `specified`)

1. **Tracking capacity belongs to the species and habitat together.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A disperser with no corridor and no required partner does not track; a slow mover in a steep connected gradient may track with little movement.
2. **The dispersule is a head start, not a recording.** Fig 4 :34 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." What the landscape receives is a propagule whose fate the landing conditions decide.
3. **Contact cascades can bind the rate.** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." A species may arrive and still fail because a partner did not. The rate that matters is the rate of the slowest required relation.

### D3, adaptation / plasticity (triangulated, `specified`)

4. **Adaptation is selection running, not a decision.** Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."
5. **Only contact-changing improvement counts.** Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A plastic shift that does not alter realized fitness in the new conditions is not a tracking mechanism.
6. **The currency is per-generation change, imported from the evolutionary frame.** Hoffmann and Sgrò 2011 (Nature), Gonzalez et al. 2013 (Phil Trans R Soc B) and Buskirk et al. 2012 (Ecology and Evolution) supply breeder's-equation response, evolutionary rescue limits, and haldane rates. The figures license the shape (contact); the world supplies the magnitude, provenanced as an import.

### D4, verification / management (triangulated, `specified`)

7. **The ground is observed persistence, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." Climate velocity is a habitat condition. Model agreement is a frame.
8. **Per species, never a global mean.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Chen et al. 2011 (Science) measure median shifts of 11.0 m per decade upward and 16.9 km per decade poleward, and report that rapid average shifts derive from a wide diversity of individual responses.
9. **Agreement from a shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Several velocity studies cite the same 2009 source; that is one pool talking about the existence of climate velocity.

### Cross-cutting gap (`general`)

10. **The number is not in the figures.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The numeric tracking rate is an OUTWARD import from empirical ecology (Formula step 7: "OUTWARD    IMPORT. Characterize R in effect terms, then find the frame whose closure predicts its shape."). The figures are not stretched to produce a speed.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number for any species, and not a global average speed.
- Not that climate velocity alone decides persistence. It is a habitat condition, not the ground.
- Not that "move" and "adapt" are isomers or one mechanism. They are union conditions with different currencies (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- Not that assisted migration is a clean fix. Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you" are the two silent failure modes. Ricciardi and Simberloff 2009 (Trends in Ecology and Evolution) supply the contingency and irreversibility.
- Not grounded. No measurement settles the per-domain magnitudes in this derivation.
- Not a claim that the framework derived Loarie's 0.42 km per year. That is a provenanced import.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument that discriminates the paths, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the questions are:

1. **Movement ground:** do occurrence time series show the range boundary tracking the isotherm velocity in the same habitat, per species? Chen et al. 2011 and the 2024 Nature mountain study are the existing readings; sharpen them, do not invent a sensor from a description (Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something.").
2. **Adaptation ground:** do common-garden and heritability studies show a trait response at a rate that closes the gap in the same generation count? Breeder's equation and haldane rates are the existing readings.
3. **Cascade ground:** does the tracked band contain the required partners at the required time, or is the limiter three steps out? This is the enumeration of Fig 11 :43-44.
4. **Standing falsifiers:** the seven in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**The shape is reached; the number is unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). The numeric rate is neither proved absent by the figures nor derived by them; it is held in world frames and imported with a source gate and a shadow test. The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes", making the choice visible rather than neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single speed for species tracking a shifting climate, because the question is a union, not one rate. It says that tracking capacity belongs to the species and its habitat together; that a dispersing propagule is a head start whose fate the landing conditions decide; that the binding partner can be three steps out in the contact cascade; that "adapt" is selection running on a lineage, with only contact-changing responses counting; and that the ground is observed persistence, read per species, never a global average or model agreement. The figures return that shape and leave the number to provenanced measurement from climate velocity, movement ecology and evolutionary biology. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
